This game is cooking my CPU, but I don’t know why…

Hey guys, this is a really strange issue and I’m not entirely sure how to address this.

Whenever I load into a new map, my CPU tempts go up to 85+ and stay there. I know a lot of people end up having high temps at map load due to shaders, but my temps don’t go down…

Until I die, or I do a screen reload by changing the Texture Quality from HIGH to MEDIUM and then back again. Then, my temps go back down while I’m on a map. Normal temps run about 50-55 C while playing.

The moment I load into a new map, we’re back up to 85-90C, once again until I die or reset the graphics.

Is there any insight into this?

Edit: I’ve already changed my CPU maximum processing power to 99%

Edit2: I possibly have resolved this…

I noticed that the folder was marked as Read-Only. I ended up following this https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1132053826 to change the Grinding Gear Games folder permissions to my user. Since then, map loads spike to 80-85 for a few seconds, but then the game is back down to 50-65 throughout the whole map.
