I already did star players on random teams, so let’s add a twist to it. Do you know any examples of excellent seasons by players on teams you would’ve never thought they’d play for?

For example, I’d probably go with Adrián Beltré in his lone seaosn with the Red Sox in 2010. He would then go on to play 8 years with the team he’s most known for, the Rangers, from 2011-2018.
