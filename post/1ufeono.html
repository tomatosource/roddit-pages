Javier Assad is one of the most clutch players of all time

As a brewers fan, I’ve noticed a shocking trend for years now that is tough to wrap my head around. Most people don’t see Javier Assad as elite, or close to it. Yet when runners get in scoring position, he becomes one of the best players in baseball history.

Javier Assad has now pitched 380 innings in his career. Although this may be a statistical aberration, the trend shows something meaningful.

Career era: 3.51
Career OPS against: .717

Now diving into the splits is where it gets interesting.

When Javier Assad is facing a batter with RISP, his OPS against drops to an astounding .504. This is over 390 plate appearances across 5 seasons.

When nobody is on base his OPS against is .771. When a runner is only on first his OPS against is .826 (283 plate appearances).

Have we ever seen this in the history of baseball? Such dramatic splits with RISP for a pitcher? What could be behind this? If we cannot find reasons together as a community, it begs the question: is Javier Assad the clutchest pitcher in MLB history?
