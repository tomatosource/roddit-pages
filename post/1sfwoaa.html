What a hypothetical Giannis trade would actually look like

I said this yesterday in daily discussion, but given speculation we’d show interest in Giannis, and Giannis making positive comments about the Celtics, I wanted to share.

Not that we want to do this, but technically Boston could make an offer for Giannis in the offseason that is competitive. Especially if Giannis uses his leverage to say Boston is a team he would re-sign with. You’re just not gonna like it.

It would be Vucevic sign-and-trade (starting salary around 25.7 mil) + White (30.3 mil) + a young asset like Walsh (2.4) or Hugo (3 mil) + our available picks which would be 58.4 mil out and match Giannis 58.4 salary while also keeping Boston under the 2nd apron.

Picks I think available be the guy we draft in 2026, 2027 1st, 2031 1st, 2033 1st, pick swaps in 2028 and 2030, plus 2nd round picks.

More than likely you’d see a 3rd team involved where White would be re-routed to. Over the offseason it sounds like the best offer we got for him was the 10th pick. So in the scenario you’d see the Bucks get a lotto pick + one or more of boston’s young prospects like Hugo + all our available future picks.

Boston would then have a line-up of Pritchard, Brown, Tatum, Giannis, Queta… or maybe they start Hauser/Baylor and bring Pritchard off the bench.

We currently project to about 185 mil on the books not including Vucevic. In the above scenario (Vucevic. + White + Walsh + picks for Giannis) you’re roughly looking at us having 213 mil on the books when the dust settles after filling out the remaining roster spots with cheap talent Thats over the 1st (209 mil) apron but under the 2nd (223 mil). You could tweak this idea slightly by giving up more salary in the trade. For instance, involving Hauser‘s 10 mil contract. At that point, Boston could in theory utilize the massive TPE + any leftover picks to try and add additional guard talent to make up for losing White. But I’m assuming in a scenario where Boston went all in on Giannis to become title favorites, they are going to just accept that going over the aprons is part of life

Some variation of that if Giannis really wanted to use his leverage and Boston wanted to go all-in on a 3rd superstar. I get that losing White sucks, but he’s the same age as Giannis and if you somehow come out of it while keeping Walsh or Hugo, maybe it makes sense to some people on paper.
