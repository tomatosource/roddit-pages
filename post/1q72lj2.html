Season Feedback From a Casual

Long time ARPG player, very much a casual who has kids and only so much time to play. My understanding is GGG want PoE 2 to appeal to players like myself in a way that PoE 1 doesn’t (although I do love PoE 1 from my earlier days, probs top 10 game ever made). I made several mistakes this season that are entirely on me, but I believe there are some systems in place that punish these mistakes in a way I don’t believe is intended:

1. Gold - I have bricked a character in 0.2.0 and 0.4.0 by making a jump to another build before I fully understood the costs involved (this is entirely a mistake by me) and then not being able to afford speccing back into a more appropriate build, and not being able to farm anything above act 3-4 to make gold. I believe there are many, many players out there like me that have made this mistake (way more than dozens!).

Omen of Bartering currently seems to be the appropriate mechanism for me to use in this situation, I had 200 or so ex at the time, but out of the 20 or so Omens I used more than half of them resulted in the item selling for less, and only 2-3 of them greatly increased the item value. My feedback here is to either make respecs cheaper, give players one free full respec, or buff omen’s of bartering.

2. Ground Loot - I am never going to make 5-10d an hour and I’m ok with that. ARPG’s should reward players with that dedication and drive, but currently ground loot means that unless you are using meta strategies or crafting, you really have no way to engage in the market in a meaningful way. Even if you buffed ground loot by 30-40%, high end players would still engage with the high end meta strategies, it won’t affect them, it will just mean the gap for casual players will be less.

3. Season Mechanic - Temple is not fun, it’s way too complicated, and very poorly executed for a casual to engage in. Abyss was absolutely unreal, but I get that not all new content should be aimed at casual players so perhaps a super complex (or convoluted) mechanic aimed at high end players is reasonable every 2nd or 3rd season.

4. Campaign - The issue with the campaign isn’t that it’s not fun, it’s that its not optimised and it feels that way. There are so many instances like this (Act 2, going to a location only to find it’s blocked and running back is just one example, there are at least 10 moments like this that would shave an hour of campaign combined. I’ll write them all out next season if it’s of use). The problem with the campaign is it just stifles a casual players ability to experience more classes and more builds. A friend of mine thinks that GGG doesn’t want campaign to be too quick or people will play the league less. If that is the case then what an insanely poor take, because if the campaign is easier people will make more alts and play waaaay longer.

There have been a bunch of things implemented that I really appreciate from a casual’s perspective and I’m excited to watch the game evolve. It does seem like sanity prevails at GGG pretty much 100% of the time, I just hope it doesn’t take too long to do so in some of these areas.
