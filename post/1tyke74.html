Temple is to PoE2 what Betrayal is to PoE1

Betrayal is notorious in PoE1 for being an overly complicated mechanic that is only worthwhile to players who spend a long time watching YouTube videos to learn the specific tricks involved. While a mechanic having depth is a celebrated idea in theory, it can go too far to the point of intimidating players to not wanting to invest that much time and energy into a learning period that will feel like homework rather than fun gameplay.

I worry that Temple has become the same thing, even after the nerfs. Like Betrayal, the Temple mechanic has the element of building up some persistent board that involves esoteric knowledge to set up “correctly” and which can be accidentally completely destroyed with one small mistake (e.g. accidentally clicking ‘remove all rivalries’). The snaking isn’t gone, just harder to maintain. What I’m seeing is that people who happened to bother learning how to build a snake last league are seeing huge benefits like the best experience per hour in the game. Meanwhile, anyone who is intimidated by the mechanic won’t want to go in there because they will get fear of misusing the finite resources (e.g. placeable rooms) so they just stockpile up the rooms and in the back of their mind they are thinking “maybe one day I will watch an hour long video and try temple but there’s no point to try until then”.

I think this is terrible design. I think Temple needs to be designed to have depth, but also not have such insane variance between 99th percentile “knowers” and 1st percentile “knowers”. There’s just too much of a difference on rewards between a noob at Temple and a pro at Temple. It’s good for a mechanic to have some significant difference on rewards between a noob and a pro at it, but that difference should be like 3x not something crazy like Temple has.

I hope GGG reworks this mechanic so that it doesn’t generate such enormous FOMO.
