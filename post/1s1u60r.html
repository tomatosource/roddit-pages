After almost a year and a half since PoE 2 launched, how do we feel about the pacing of the combat?

I remember having an argument with my friend where I was saying PoE 1 is an amazing game, but has degenerate combat.

Meaning, the combat is revolved around you having 1 ability that nukes the entire screen. You build a character that amplifies one specific ability and you just spam that one spell for the most part other than buffs/debuffs,totems, etc. The most efficient builds allow you to face tank tons of mechanics in the game, and instantly kill your screen with one button, you move around at the speed of light, etc.

It’s essentially a math game about maximizing all of your modifiers as much as possible.

Which, hey, I love math, and that shit is definitely really fun.

However, when they came out with PoE 2 and the whole idea was that it’s much more slow, methodical, challenging bosses, you’re going to be chaining your skills together, I was really pumped because I thought that was the biggest problem I had with PoE 1 so it’s like they were making this for me.

But I feel like it really isn’t that, is it?

There’s tons of builds in the game that just wipe the whole screen and you’re moving insanely fast and killing bosses ignoring most of their mechanics.

I feel like what they were going for with PoE 2 isn’t really exactly what’s going on you know? I’m not chaining together a bunch of skills to hit combos and learning boss fights, I’m usually still just killing them with one or maybe 2 abilities. It more-or-less just feels sort of the same as PoE 1 in a lot of ways.

I was thinking it would be something similar to Lost Ark bosses, I’m not sure if any of you have played that game but the bosses are so incredibly well done. The bosses have a fuck ton of life and your combat abilities are all very chunky, synergize with each other very well, etc. So you had to really learn all the mechanics of the fight and it’s very challenging.

But yeah I just don’t get that much in this game that much. The best builds are just super degenerate and play like PoE 1, but at that point, what’s the point of PoE 2? I thought they wanted the game to be way slower?

I dunno, what do you guys think? I’ve had a lot of convos with my friends about it and I still think the game is way too fast paced, I still get one shot by things so the best way to build it just to one shot them first, etc.
