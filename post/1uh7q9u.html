[Robb] The Celtics and Blazers have discussed a trade for Jaylen Brown, though a source says that Deni Avdija would not be on the table in any talks and it would be tough to pry away Donovan Clingan as well. Ultimately, any deal with Portland will likely center around draft capital.

All-Star Deni Avdija would not be on the table in any trade talks, league sources tell MassLive and it would be tough for Boston to pry away Donovan Clingan as well. The Blazers do not have strong center depth behind Clingan with Robert Williams set to hit free agency so trading Clingan for Brown would simply create another hole on the roster.

Ultimately, any deal with Portland will likely center around draft capital. The Blazers control the Bucks’ future first round picks from 2028-2030 and also have plenty of their own first-round draft capital. How deep they are willing to go into that pool of assets will be a major factor in whether the Celtics are willing to pull the trigger on a draft-heavy package.
