Last night’s Pacers vs Pistons game had 19 players with at least one steal (9 for the Pacers and 10 for the Pistons). This tied the NBA record set by the Sonics and Raptors on 1/15/1997. This dates back to the 1973-74 season when the NBA began tracking steals.

I haven’t seen anybody mention this so we were looking it up in the Pistons discord. After box score review this is the OG record and last night’s game tied the record for number of individuals with a steal from both teams combined.

Pretty neat little stat for a random January blowout.
