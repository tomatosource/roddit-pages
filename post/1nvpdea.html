PSA: If map boss HP pool is bothering you, refund some Map Boss passives from the Atlas.

So I’ve been cutting through T16 maps like butter (6 mods, 3 tablets). But the new map bosses have had insanely high HP for my build to deal with. I’m dying in roughly 1⁄10 boss fights and especially against bosses like Viper Napuatzi (you’ll find her in Vaal City maps).

The problem, for me at least, is that I have to go through multiple (like 4+) cycles of boss mechanics. I’m doing fine… doing fine… then bam I time a dodge poorly and get one shot. I’m actually okay with this when I’m fighting pinnacle bosses and such. But when I’m mapping? Nah no thanks.

Anyway I realized the problem was in my Atlas tree. I’ve fully unlocked the Map Boss tree up to Organized Forces which looks like a total of +5 map boss difficulty for me. I’m assuming that’s mostly just HP and their attack speed.

I brought it down to +2 difficulty and now it’s much more manageable for my build. And all I’ve lost was +10% waystone drop rate and +1 strongbox. Organized Forces isn’t relevant to me because it only applies to non-irradiated maps and I can irradiate any map now. So now I’m back to comfortably running area level 82 maps with bosses and one life.
