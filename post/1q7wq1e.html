The Vaal Temple is Ridiculous

Obvious late to the party here and I know there was controversy related to the Vaal Temple snaking that made 6000 divines a run or whatever.

I’ve been playing this game casually since the druid drop and dealt with the insane annoyance of being a new player with the limited in game descriptions and tips.

I’ve been making headway and wanted to try a Atziri fight so I’ve been spending time running maps and saving vaal temple crystals to try out a few hours of tinkering to get to her room.

Little known to me over the earlier part of my play through I had been connecting rooms together to reach her chamber when you actually have to chain paths.

Leading tonight to where I finally have the time and crystals to make the final push only to connect a barracks to a commander room right outside and it show it wouldn’t path. Ran to the key room inside and it also stated it wouldn’t path.

I understand there is a whole economy people worry about but I just want to play the game and not spend equal time researching optimal stuff or mechanics before I play after a 12 hour workday.

I’m putting the game down for awhile despite generally enjoying it but when this game is frustrating its frustrating bad.
