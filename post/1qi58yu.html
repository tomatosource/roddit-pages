The guys he trails consistently LeBron, Durant, Erving, Bird, and Kawhi for efficiency. Havlicek, Barry, Baylor, Pippen, and Pierce come up a lot on these boards.

Only 1 SF that we know of matched his career averages (18.4 PPG/5.4 RPG/4.4 APG/1.6 STL): Larry Bird. Barry almost certainly did so, Havlicek maybe.
