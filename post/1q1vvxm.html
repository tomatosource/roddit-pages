Unsure of what to do next.

This is the first PoE2 league where i actually played seriously. Before now, i mostly was just coming around to play around a bit in PoE2 and then quit while i waited for it to get into a better state.

So this is the first PoE2 league where i have made it to maps, and i went all the way to T15 maps.
So far, it hasn’t been too difficult. I am running T15 fully affixed maps with 3 tablets without too much issue.

BUT, i am not following a build guide. I am playing a Shaman wolf build. And now that i am running T15 maps, i am kind of at a loss of… whats next? Is it just grinding and slowly incrementally upgrading my gear for very expensive small improvements?
I haven’t really fought any of the endgame bosses yet. But still, the content just feels very repetitive and not very rewarding. There are only so many breaches and deliriums i can do before it just feels like i’m kinda getting nowhere and nothing is changing. Just farming currency is already getting boring.
