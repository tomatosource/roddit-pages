Simulating a theoretical expansion draft

Suppose the NBA added Seattle and Las Vegas teams for expansion. How would the draft go?

PLAYER PROTECTIONS:

In expansion drafts, each team protects 8 players, and can only lose 1 player. It’s a toss-up once you get to the 7th and 8th players but this is at least close to what each team would do. I didn’t check on outgoing free agents so for the purposes of this exercise all teams retain their players.

DRAFT:

Seattle gets the first pick, and then teams alternate from there (couldn’t find good source on this but initial Google searches said two team expansion would not be a snake draft). Obviously the initial move would be to tank a few years, but for this hypothetical I think it’s more fun to see how competitive a team could be. Once a team loses a player, their remaining players cannot be picked (i.e., if Jerami Grant gets picked, Ayton is off the board).
