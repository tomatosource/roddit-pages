The Bat knights need to be looked at

I don’t know what their in game name is, but they transform into a flock of bats (they are quite common in “Crypt”. Move over to you and slice with their scythe, now, I believe they received some changed to their AI and Cooldown adjustments, this in and of itself is not a problem. However, I have noticed in maps that have increased monster speed (or rares with Haste aura) and similar, it becomes really awkward.

Monster teleports as a swarm of bats onto you, it materialises, hits once or twice and then transforms back to a swarm of bats. Then it just, kind of loses track and hovers menacingly for a few seconds before it goes back to attacking. What I *think* has happened is that their “scythe swipe” has it’s old cooldown, whereas the “cloud of bats” has a reduced cooldown rate that results in the monster not having any way to either hit you or regain it’s hit box.

Proposed solution: Either make them unable to turn into swarm of bats when “Scythe swipe” is on cooldown or decrease the cooldown of “Scythe swipe” to match their transformation cooldown.

Sincerely, someone tired of waiting for the stupid rare to do it’s best ghosted Sirus impersonation.
