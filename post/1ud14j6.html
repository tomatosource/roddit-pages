TIL that the Tomb of the Fallen Knight encounter is bugged in three separate ways.

  1. You click on the Verisium behind the boss to start the encounter, the encounter doesn’t start but you can no longer interact with the exit. You are now stuck in the boss room, he will not spawn, and the rest of your map is bricked. I suspect this could because when I entered he was in his “dead” state (no idea what causes this) but I suspect it’s the case because…

  2. When I found the boss the next day, instead of laying in his dead animation on the floor he was kneeling in the room and did this “twitching” animation. I clicked the Verisium behind him and the boss encounter started, but when I killed him he dropped zero loot. This wasn’t a filter problem, the Verisium pile showed up that spawns from the clickable object that starts the boss fight, but the boss itself dropped zero items.

  3. Apparently sometimes even though you explode the tomb, you just cannot enter it. Just had this happen in my last map.

How are we now over three weeks and a special and rare encounter for the new League mechanic is bugged in three separate ways that prevent you from completely it or getting rewards? The League has been great, but this just blows my mind…
