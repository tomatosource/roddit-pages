Reminder: David Price was a Super 2 and on his 4th trip through arbitration when he got 19.75M

David Price had a total of 4 arbitration years, he is not a comparable example for Skubal who is only on his 3rd time through arbitration. Had he been a Super 2 he’s probably looking at 25M+ this year

Super 2 matters a ton for large arbitration salaries (Soto, Vlad, Price.. etc), name a massive record setting arbitration deal and it was probably a Super 2

Scherzer in 2014 is a good comparable and he ended up avoiding arbitration with a 15.25M salary coming off a Cy Young year. Or Arrieta in 2017 coming off his dominant season in 2015 and a good result in 2016 getting 15.6M.

MLBTR had his arbitration estimate at 17.8M, Tigers filed at 19M its a more than fair filing and the gap has more to do with Skubal making a point about arbitration that actually trying to win his filing.
