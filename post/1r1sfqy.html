I’ve ran around 400 of ritual maps this league. Here are my results.

For a few leagues I’ve been running rituals. This league I decided to keep statistics to determine my profit. I wrote in notepad every top tier (whittling, sinistral \ dextral annuls, sinistral \ dextral erasure) omen, that I’ve received in rituals.

My atlas setup:

Additional reroll.

Cheaper reroll.

4 altars.

Increased chance for omens.

My tablet setup.

1 x legendary ritual tablet (x 2 rerolls)

1 x “ritual altars allow rerolling 3 additional times”

1 x cheaper reroll, more tribute \ cheaper deffer.

With this setup, I had ~12.6 average rolls per map

(No remnants of power proc = 10 rerolls per map + 1 initial,
Remnants of power proc = 18 rerolls per map + 1 initial. (0.8 * 11) + (0.2 * 19) = 12.6 average rolls)

Total recorded maps: 397 (95% of them were lvl 80, 5% were used to to travel between nodes)

Total whittling received : 39

Other top omens received : 39

Whittling per map : ~1 per 10 maps

Other high tier omens per map : ~1 per 10 maps

If you decide to forgo 3 x additional reroll tablet due to its price, especially during league start, you will need to multiply those numbers by 2.6, since in that case, you will have 5 average rerolls per map, instead of 12.6, meaning, that you will get 1 whittling \ high tier omen per 26 maps.

Keep in mind, that I’ve encountered kinda dry streak during the end of the run. I’d say whittlings are more common, than that (around 1 in every 9 maps, or so).

Conclusions:

  • It’s essential to run 80+ lvl maps. More than half of your profit comes from top tier omens, and they are dropping from lvl 80+ maps only.

  • Omen droprate sucks. I’d say, that I spent around 70-80% of my league running rituals, and I havent got enough whittlings to complete single high end craft. This is laughable, imo.

  • It is not economically feasible to buy “3 x reroll tablets” during league start. I remember seeing those tablets for 10+ divines, while whittling costed 2d, and other top omens costed 3-4. You wont be able to break even.

  • Odds of crafting such tablet yourself are low due to low suffix weight. I’d say you need around ~150-200 chaos orb on average to get it. I craft those tablets myself, and once spent around 900 orbs to get single tablet.

  • Most of maps are bad for rituals. Monster spawns are tied to ritual area. Narrow area = less spawns, less tribute. I missed 2 high tier omens, due to not being able to deffer it.

  • King of the mists (ritual pinnacle boss) - sucks too. After developers massacred ingenuity (legendary belt), there is simple too little incentive to farm\buy splinters and run it. All his legendary drops are bad, and chance of getting high tier omens are miniscule.

tl:dr

Ritual system needs rework.
