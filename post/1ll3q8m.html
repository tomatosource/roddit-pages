Cooper Flagg gave us a big shoutout last night

When he was asked who his top five All-time players are, a question often asked to incoming players, Flagg gave us his list:

  1. Larry Bird
  2. Tatum
  3. MJ
  4. Lebron
  5. Kobe

Per Cooper: he grew up watching Larry. Tatum is his favorite player. MJ is the Goat. Lebron and Kobe are legends.

The fact that the 80s Celtics is what this kid modeled his game after, and he happens to turn into one of the most complete american prospects in NBA history, is just an amazing story line for us Celtics fans.

Seriously, this will be the only time a Number 1 pick says his favorite player is Larry Bird.
