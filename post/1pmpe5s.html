Greatest MLB seasons where a player did not lead the league in WAR

There has been a lot of recent discussions on how Judge’s recent MVP wins overshadowed one of the greatest two way seasons ever (Ohtani), one of the greatest seasons by a shortstop ever (Witt), and one of the greatest seasons by a catcher (Raleigh). The runner-ups likely would have won MVP in most other seasons, but Judge had a historic season in all 3 seasons and lead the league in WAR. This got me thinking, what are some other seasons where a player had a historic year but did not lead the league in WAR because they were bested by another player having a historically great season? One season that came to mind was Ted William’s 1957 seasons where he had 9.7 bWAR, hitting .388/.526/.731, but finished behind Mickey Mantle’s 11.3 bWAR. Another that came to mind was Lou Gehrig’s 1927 season where he hit .373/.474/.765 with 47 home runs, 52 doubles, and 11.9 bWAR but finished behind Babe Ruth’s 12.6 bWAR.
