PoE2 may really want more RAM for stable mapping

Not sure if this helps anyone, but I kept getting freezes/crashes in PoE2 during mapping ever since the hotfixes of 0.5.

For context, my specs:

  • i9-9900K
  • RTX 2080 Ti
  • NVMe SSD
  • 16 GB RAM (DDR4 2666 MHz)

During gameplay I was consistently running into issues like:

  • freezing during map transitions or Atlas usage
  • SSD usage pegging at 100% during freezes
  • sudden stutters followed by crashes

What I observed:

  • Fresh boot: ~8–9 /35GB committed
  • Menu: ~20⁄35 GB committed
  • During maps: climbs to ~30⁄35 GB and then stabilizes

All while having nothing running in the background like browser tabs, spotify etc

On a 16 GB RAM system this basically forces heavy pagefile usage, and that’s exactly when the freezes happen (SSD 100%, game stalls).

After seeing this, I’m pretty convinced PoE2 can easily push past what 16 GB comfortably handles.
Not saying 32 GB is “required”, but it seems like a pretty big stability upgrade if you’re hitting similar issues like me. I’m at least considering upgrading now if issues keep persisting.
Just sharing data in case it helps someone.
