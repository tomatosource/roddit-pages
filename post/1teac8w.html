Here’s something that could create a lot more flexibility and build variety: Passive tree “gateways”

Hear me out.

You know how POE1 atlas tree has “gateways” you can take, that let you traverse to other side of tree at cost of couple points?

What if… we had a similar system in POE2? Where taking a “gateway” as a point could allow you to move quickly to the other side of passive tree, to get something there?

At the moment, a lot (and i do mean a lot) of builds go vroom all around the outer edge. Hell, This is what Kripp suggests doing on the Spiritwalker. This lets you get a lot of jewel slots but also reach stuff on the opposite end of the tree.

Yes, it would be powerful, but at the moment it feels rather restrictive. See, in POE1 you have a lot of options - you have impossible escapes, you have cluster jewels, you have flesh/flame, thread of hope, let alone quite a few items that allocate specific keystones. Basically you have options - expensive or inefficient options, but options.

And in poe2, if you want something not near your starting zone - well, better pack your sandwiches because you’re going on a journey exile. A journey - all - across - the - outer - rim.

POE2’s passive tree is already more restrictive than POE1 (lots of kiss/curse nodes, no life, etc). Can we at least get some better pathing options? Please?
