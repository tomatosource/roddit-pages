You can “divine” single resistance on items using fluxes

Not sure if everyone knows this already but I initially thought when you use fluxes each resistance has a roll and by swapping between them you will keep getting the same roll for each type.

Nope, you can swap back and forth until you hit max roll on your desired resistance, I just did it on my rings to cover my missing 2% lightning res.

Important note: Do not do this on items with more than 1 resistance or you will end up with 70%+ lightning/cold/fire items that you can’t switch back to multiple resistances.

On items like
15% all resist
40% fire

You can switch freely until you hit the max roll
