As an Invoker Monk 4 Nerfs I’m thankful for

The nerf to HOWA has given me more freedom in my choice of gear. And crafting gloves is fun again. Rare gloves can be really good now.

That lessens the blow of Grim Feast being removed, because I can now get way more ES from my gloves. I’m also glad Grin Feast is gone because on kill buffs like that are inconsistent, you can’t always have it. For example you can’t use it at all vs the arbiter. Any success you had otherwise lets you down in this case.

Ingenuity nerf also allows for more interesting choices. Having high value 3 charm belts has been fun. The 3 charm slots are a total game changer. Crafting belts also became a fun side hobby.

The nerf to herald of lightning and ice. You feel it at first, but it’s actually made combat feel way more involved. My choices as to how I move around are more impactful. Obviously, since I’m not one tapping and deleting mobs off screen with how it was last season.

I’ve made critiques lately but I also want to point out some honestly really positive changes they’ve made through just proper nerfs that all directly affected my build.
