Untold Stories From a Co-founder of GGG

Greetings fellow Exiles!

25 years ago, through a series of incredible coincidences, I met Chris Wilson while playing Diablo 2.  Our immediate friendship evolved into a brotherhood, spanning the vast distance between New Zealand and California.

A few years after we met, Chris and I began laying the groundwork for the company that would become Grinding Gear Games, and the game that would become Path of Exile.  I have increasingly felt an imperative to tell the details of that story to the community.

Last week, with the help of the incredible YouTube content creator Gavryn, I finally had a chance to get it all on record.  I am very pleased with the result of our lengthy conversation, and with his visual editorial storytelling.  If you’re ever wondered how one of your favorite games came to be, you’ll likely enjoy the video.

Here is the YouTube link:

https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=SC16Xj_jwp4&t=3342s&pp=ygUUZ2F2cnluIHBhdGggb2YgZXhpbGXSBwkJzQkBhyohjO8%3D

I will watch this thread in the days ahead, and answer any reasonable questions, as time allows.  From the bottom of my heart, thank you all for more than a decade of loyalty and support.

Sincerely,

  Brian Weissman
