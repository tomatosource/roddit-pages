PoE2 is a gorgeous, engaging, difficult, unique arpg… it’s just not fun

I feel like they are nailing all of the metrics that we know they are measuring or that matter to them -

  • Is it really difficult?
  • Is it punishing?
  • Is there the constant fear of death?
  • Loot should be scarce
  • Trading should suck
  • We don’t want players to have OP stuff easily
  • Ailments and mitigation should matter
  • We want to slow the game down
  • Everything should be about combos
  • Boss fights should be grueling
  • You should be punished for your mistakes

I’m sure theres many more… it’s just that, well, my concern is that they’ve forgotten to measure the most important thing. “Is it fun?”

I realise this is purely a subjective take, but, as badly as I want to love poe2, and trust me I am grinding and forcing myself thru because I know poe1 (and even perhaps poe2) continues to show so much potential, but… it’s just not a fun game right now.

I don’t really remember having fun yet. I kept thinking “it’ll be fun when I get stronger” but you don’t really get stronger. “It’ll be fun when I get loot”… you don’t really get loot. It’ll be fun when I unlock the atlas… most nodes don’t even make a difference. It’ll be fun when I juice content… well unless you get 3 towers overlapping, everything is way under-tuned to feel like poo. It’ll be fun when I roll another character… well you are still weak, and you have a long campaign (20 hours on avg for me) to get thru. It’ll be fun when I….. fill in the gap. In most games, eventually, you reach the fun at some point… but for me, I’m starting to think that the “fun” metric just isn’t something they’re necessarily concerned with or balancing around. More like a pleasant byproduct if it happens, rather than something you strategically pivot around.

This isn’t a doomsday post, I’m not RAHHH Rage Quit Poe2 !!1!1, its just my own observation and something I hope PoE2 learns to fix.
