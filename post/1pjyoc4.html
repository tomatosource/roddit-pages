Why is pack size not dependant of the enemy type?

On the topic of the decrease pack size coming up there is one thing I don’t really understand.

We all know this tomb room in act 2 that is so damn satisfying to chain explode, and I really understand the appeal of screen wide clear when such thing happen, and at the same time I understand the philosophy of reducing the general pack size to make the game less cluttered and more interesting.

I know an upcoming passive atlas tree will make us choose, But… Why do we have to choose between the 2?

What if we could have more swarmy, low effectiveness monster come as a group of 20~30 mobs at once and the player must deal with this and right after, the next pack is composed of 3 more tough enemies?

This way, the game improves on gameplay diversity, it keeps the increased build viability because some skills will be better at the few tough enemies and other at the huge pack sizes, players who like screen-cleargasm will have them and people who want more engaging combat will have it too.

Thought?
