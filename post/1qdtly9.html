Trial of Sekhema is terrible in party play

What genius at GGG decided it was a good idea that honour damage in the trial should be shared by all the group and yet each player have their own separate honour pool?

Playing coop through PoE2 revealed some mind boggling bad decisions in the game that you never encounter in solo play, but that has to be the worst one I’ve seen yet. It’s especially horrible if you play a life+armour character with someone who plays pure ES as your honour pool is much, much lower and yet everytime they take damage you take the equivalent of the fully unmitigated honour loss, which absolutely decimates you.

On top of that, if the party leader dies, the other party members cannot continue the trial.

I do actually enjoy Trial of Sekhema quite a bit in solo, but in coop it’s kind of a joke unfortunately, and the game in general is full of decisions that seem to discourage or just hamper party play. It’s not a priority for the game but it’s really a shame.
