my biggest gripe with this game as a new player

poe2 is my first ever arpg, so i’m not entirely sure if this problem occurs in other entries of this genre, but why does crafting items feel completely pointless? theres a whole bunch of orbs and omens that can influence modifiers on a weapon/ equipment piece, but yet it feels like you could always just resort to buying an item off the marketplace requiring way less resources and providing better stats. unless you’re abnormally rich and can afford wasting your currency on equipment with seemingly abysmal odds of rolling good mods, trading with other players just makes way more sense. the satisfaction of crafting a piece that actually upgrades your build is so addictive, yet it almost never happens since the whole crafting mechanic feels like a slot machine. am i just crafting wrong or is trading with other people actually the way to go?
