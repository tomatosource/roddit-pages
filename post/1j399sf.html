Do you like orb pick up skills in POE2 ?

We have mana, ES, armor, blood orb from blood mage, and dark monk skill (into the breach).

There is no such feature in POE1, I believe it is borrowed from Diablo series (life potion drop from mobs)

I personally really don’t like them because

  1. already too many things on screen, some of them even hard to see.

  2. it’s totally random and add uncertainty to game (not a bad thing though)

  3. you have to deliberately move to pick them up which is very annoying

IMO at least make them easier to catch from a distance, or adding support gems that can magnet them further away.
