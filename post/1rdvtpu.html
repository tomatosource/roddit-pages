No Locks - 2 Theories

Hey all,

you might have noticed a few posts about locks stopping to drop. It seems that something has changed. But while some players do not see any difference and still drop locks, others run dry for such a long time that it is very unlikely to be related to just bad rng.

I happened to be one of those lock starving people (sad temple makes sad noises post). And I have been thinking for a while… I seemed to have noticed that especially two room types did not yield any locks. or in some cases no medallions at all: Elite Legions and Spy Masters. Other rooms just worked fine, but they of cource did not drop locks as frequently as required. I was really starving out and my temple destabilized - luckily just a bit and nothing too important or time consuming.

However, suddenly, my Elite Legions provided stuff again. And one run later, my spy masters startet do drop again - in a manner I was used to. Then it hit me…

Maybe, they added a hidden malus system and I have currently two theories.

Theory 1 (I believe this is less likely): There is a chance that the medallions you can use to add modifiers to your temple are cursed and lead to a random room to provide less medallions or even no medallions at all. So if you are unlucky, you might just deprive yourself from locks alltogether. Reasons this could be true: those medallions are active for 10 rounds. And this seems something which might fit with the description of the buffs. Similar to waystone modifiers. Adds rare monsters but gives them more crit hit chance. And if you have to endure any kind of starvation for 10 rounds it feels like not rng anymore. Reasons against this theory: it would be weird to add a hidden modifier on something like medallions, or not to mention it in patch notes. So this theory may be weak.

Theory 2 (and much more likely): They added… or there always had been… a death penalty for temples. I always wondered… you are heavily punished for dying in all the game. You die in your map: you lose the portal, all special content of the map and a large chunk of xp. You die to whatever, you are punished. However, if you die in temple, nothing happens except losing xp. But as you gain xp like there is no tomorrow, noone would really care about that. So my theory is that if you die in the temple, you will not drop medallions for a while. And more specific: if you die in a specific room, you will receive less or maybe even no medallions from that room AT ALL. Maybe just for a few rounds, maybe for 10 rounds. I don’t know. Reasons for this theory: it would explain, why you suddenly dont get locks anymore. You died to often. And it explains why some people do not experience it. They dont die.

Of course I understand, that noone is ever interested in trying this out. If the theory is right and you die in spymaster and are deprived of locks for 10 rounds as punishment, your temple is kind of doomed. And if you also die in Elite Legion at the same time? Goodnight then.

HOWEVER, if we could all keep a bit of track on ocassional unwanted deaths, and see in what rooms we died and see if anything is dropping from these rooms in the subsequent temple runs, we might unveil a new mechanic. Or maybe someone is brave enough to die on purpose. Smithys would be not as painful…

Anyways, what do you think about these theories?
