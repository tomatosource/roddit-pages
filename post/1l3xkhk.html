If the Pacers win this year, it will be the first time in NBA history a championship team’s direct path to a ring crossed through two 64+ win teams.

This has never been accomplished before, although Orlando came closest in 2009.

There are four years with multiple 64+ win teams:

1996 - The 72-win Bulls beat the 64-win Sonics in the Finals.

1997 - The 69-win Bulls beat the 64-win Jazz in the Finals.

2009 - The Magic beat the 66-win Cavs in the Eastern Conference Finals but lost to the 65-win Lakers in the Finals.

2016 - The Cavs beat the 73-win Warriors in the Finals but never would have seen both them and the 67-win Spurs who lost to OKC in the second round (who lost to the Warriors in the ECF).
