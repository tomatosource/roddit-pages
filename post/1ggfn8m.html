It was a very strange night last night in the NBA. Anomalies I caught:

The Boston Celtics (great) lost a basketball game.

Jaylen Brown took 30 shots (a lot!), but only had 25 points (not enough) and only made 11 of those field goals (not enough).

The Washington Wizards (bad) won a basketball game — by double figures, no less.

No further comment.

The Detroit Pistons (bad) won a basketball game — by double figures, no less.

But Jalen Duren (Pistons) fouled out in ten minutes.

Cade Cunningham took 8 three pointers, and made four of them. Through his first 3 seasons, Cade took about 5 threes per game, and made about 32% of them.

Bronny James scored his first NBA points

Charlotte (bad) beat Toronto (mediocre). Brooklyn (mediocre) beat Memphis (decent). Chicago (mediocre) beat Orlando (good).

Charlotte’s bench scored 69 points, led by 27 from Tre Mann and 25 from Cody Martin (on just 11 shots!). Charlotte’s starting lineup scored 69 points.

Zach Edey (Memphis, 7’4” and strong) had just 2 rebounds in 19 minutes of action.

Cam Thomas (Brooklyn) scored 19 points despite making just 4 field goals.

Franz Vagner (Orlando) scored just 7.

Josh Giddey (Chicago) shoots 4 threes. He makes all 4. Giddey is roughly a 30% career three point shooter.

Grady Dick (Toronto) scored 30 points.

KAT (Karl Anthony-Towns) scored 44 points — his Knicks high - in a win.

KAT added 13 rebounds as well.

Bam Adabayo was absolutely dominated by KAT. Bam finished with 11 points, 4 assists, 3 rebounds, and zero blocks. Jimmy Butler took only 9 shots and scored just 15.

Mikal Bridges’ outside shot is looking different (improved) from his offseason workout videos and first Knicks game. The release is faster and has less of a hitch.

Wemby is silent in a rivalry game, badly outplayed in a loss to OKC and Chet Holmgren.

Wemby takes just 5 shots and scores just 6 in a loss to OKC.

Zion loses his mojo in a blowout loss to a Steph Curry-less Golden State team.
