The only player ever with 1250 steals, blocks, and 3’s was Clifford Robinson, a 1x All-Star whose career began in the 1980s. LeBron and Durant are close, but not likely to join him.

Clifford Robinson is mostly known to Blazer fans for his contributions in the 90s, in part for his defensive versatility and in part for his “why’s that big guy playing on the perimeter so much?” game which in retrospect was very ahead of its time. Not a lot stands out about his career statistically, but he retired with over 1250 steals, blocks, and 3’s, something no other player has ever done. Dirk is the only other retired player who was close (40 steals short).

Currently LeBron is about 130 blocks short and seems unlikely to get there. Durant is about 120 steals and 30 blocks short and is maybe 50-50 for getting there.
