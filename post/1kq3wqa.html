Best and Worst

NOTE: This is a light-hearted post, please don’t take mine, or anyone else’s funny opinions to heart and feel the need to ‘correct’ them.

BEST/WORST DIALOGUE:

BEST

Honourable Mention:

Anything Count Geonor says (Does the voice actor give you guys Matt Berry vibes?)

Winner:

Witch: “At least he died do something he loved…or some such nonsense”

WORST

Honourable Mention:

“You will die…BY MY HAND”…76 times in a row

Loser:

“I am a GOD”

(Cue Tyrael Lannister quote: anyone who has to declare himself a God, is no God, also, didn’t we rescue your sad ass in Act 1?)

BEST/WORST MUSIC:

BEST

Honourable Mention:

Utzaal, Pan pipes, ominous, terrific stuff

Winner:

Ogham Manor, grinding organ music, haunting, foreboding, perfectly matching the quest.

WORST (Look, the music is all pretty amazing, so I struggled a bit here)

Honourable Mention:

Dreadnought: I dunno, just seemed it didn’t fit the theme of the level.

Loser:

Ignagduk, the Bog Witch battle music…ugh

BEST/WORST BOSS BATTLE: (the battle with the most satisfying gameplay, not just the bestest/coolest)

BEST

Honourable Mention:

Zarokh, the Temporal Boss

Winner:

Count Geonor, HE READS POETRY

WORST

Honourable Mentions:

The Crowbell, not one, two looong invulnerable phases so he can die in a tiny pit…

Ignagduk, the Bog Witch battle, Just…weird, is she riding a broomstick?

Xyclucian, the Chimera, Sit DOWN will you!

Loser:

Bahlak, yep, the tornado bird…

BEST/WORST Quest

BEST

Honourable Mention:

Cemetery of the Eternals, Lachan, Draven, Asinia, all in one quest?

Winner:

Ogham Manor: Best Bosses (Candlemass is peak creativity), best music, best dialogue!

WORST

Honourable Mention:

Matlan Waterways, a linear path punctuated by levers, who could ask for more?

Loser:

The Ardura Caravan:

Talk to Asala, walk 10 feet, talk to Asala again, use the map to send the entire caravan to the gates. leave the caravan, talk to Asala again who has mysteriously teleported to be ahead of you after you left her on the caravan. Couldn’t this have been one line of dialogue?
