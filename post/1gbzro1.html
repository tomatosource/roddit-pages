Denver Nuggets Miss spacing provided by KCP

His replacement Christian Braun is a hesitant three point shooter and a bad free throw shooter. He shot 38.4 on 2 attempts a game last season and 35.4 on 1.3 attempts a game a season prior. He also shot just 69.4% from the line last season. Data

The lack of being a legit three point threat who can shoot when guarded is valuable. This makes teams close out and spaces the floor. Christian Braun is not respected in that way. Which decreases the spacing on the floor for Jokic to do his work.
