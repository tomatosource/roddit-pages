[BasketNews] When asked about what could have been if he and Brunson had stayed on the same team, Doncic acknowledged that their duo would have been very good : “We have improved a lot every year. We are not the players we were before. It would have been very good, for sure,”

​

​

​

Doncic revealed that he did not watch much of the NBA Finals, but said he sent Brunson a message after his title win, noting that he was impressed with his performance in leading the Knicks to their first championship in over 50 years.

​

“Well, I barely watched anything,” Doncic said. “But I think he played very well. I sent him a message, and he must be very happy.”

​

​

When asked about what could have been if he and Brunson had stayed on the same team, Doncic acknowledged that their duo would have been very good, noting that both players have improved a lot since their last playoff run together with the Dallas Mavericks.

​

“We have improved a lot every year. We are not the players we were before. It would have been very good, for sure,” Doncic said.
