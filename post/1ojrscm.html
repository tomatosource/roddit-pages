To break down the process:

Step 1: perfect aug/annul till +5 cold while the wand is blue
Step 2: perfect homo regal, 99% of the time delete the affix given using whittles/sinistral annul if needed
Step 3: perfect homo ex slam till t1 cast speed/t1 crit dmg, whittle suffixes into prefixes and annul them till both t1 cast speed/crit dmg are on the suffixes
Step 4: perfect homo ex slam till t1 spell dmg or t1 cold dmg
Step 5: whittle for t1 gain as cold
Step 6: desecrate the missing mod between spell dmg/cold dmg
Step 7: lock -&gt; sanctify
