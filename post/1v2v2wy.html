After years of bouncing off PoE at maps, I finally “get” endgame at 88… now I don’t know what to do with myself

Okay so this is a weirdly emotional post for a video game but here we go.

I’ve started PoE since 0.1, multiple leagues, and every single time I hit maps (or sometimes before) my brain just goes “nope” and I quit. Too many systems, too much info, felt like homework. Same story in Poe1, got to maps once or twice over the years and just… didn’t click.

This league it finally clicked. I’m 88 on a Spirit Walker and for the first time I actually understand what I’m doing in the endgame instead of flailing around clicking random things now that end game feels more guided. For context, I either get to play around 15 mins in the morning or in the evening or maybe 2 hours during a weekend.

So now I’m stuck at a weird crossroads and want to hear from people who’ve been here: Do I ride this high and keep pushing deeper into PoE2’s endgame while it’s clicking? Or try to carry this momentum into PoE1 since I’ve never actually “gotten” that one either?
