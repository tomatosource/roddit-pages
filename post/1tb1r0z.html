I am kind of loving GGG’s new approach to expanded ascendancy design (Spirt Walker)

I hope they continue to toy with more flavorful and interesting takes on Ascendancies that really make them feel like a transformation of your class. We saw this with things like the Abyssal Lich and it sounds like they have hinted at a similar hidden interaction with the Chalupa Monk in the Breach rework, but the one that I really like is Spirit Walker getting extra options enabled after allocating a specific combination of points.

This is really cool and opens up the possibility for a lot of other ‘conditional’ class design directions that could fill gaps or ‘twists’ for certain classes. They could even expand it and have different nodes appear or change function if you have certain Keystones allocated along with certain Ascendancy skills.

What are some other things they could do with existing classes like they are doing with Spirit Walker?
