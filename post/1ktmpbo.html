If the Pacers win, this year’s NBA Finals will have the most challenging travel logistics for fans and media in the last 25+ years.

I evaluated the relative travel difficulty of getting to, from, and between NBA Finals game locations since the 1999-2000 season.

I then compared those travel logistics to a hypothetical matchup between OKC/MIN and the Pacers.

Interestingly, this year’s potential OKC/MIN vs. Pacers matchup will be the first Finals in nearly 20 years with no direct flights between the two locations. The last such series was in 2007 between the Spurs and Cavs, and ranks 24th out of the 25 Finals I analyzed. Only this year’s potential matchup would rank as more difficult for travel logistics.

Welcome to the Flyover State Finals. Hope you have an airport lounge pass.
