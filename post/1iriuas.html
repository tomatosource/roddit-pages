Inside the NBA and last night’s All Star “Game” perfectly encapsulated Adam Silver’s NBA

Please keep in mind I’m really petty when reading this.

The advertisement/commentary/events to basketball ratio was disgusting. Fans know the ASG isn’t competitive, but the fans of first time all stars really got screwed last night. Tuning in to watch Sengun or Cade, only to see them barely play, while listening to a washed comedian and 4 guys who don’t watch the NBA provide commentary on the best of the best. From the comments about SGA’s shoes to telling the bigs to stop shooting to the break honoring COMMENTATORS like they died but who in fact still have jobs next year (!!!) during an ASG was Groundhog Day level of insanity.

I was frustrating when I realized Ant decided not to play, like Lebron and Giannis, but by the time the circus had ended I’m glad he wasn’t there to have insults thrown at his game or his shoes. I mentioned it in a few threads already, but it’s absurd these guys (+Draymond) are allowed to comment so negatively so often on the league like this. Shaq and Barkley don’t know the players’ names, Ernie can’t control anyone, and Kenny has phoned it in. They actually made me enjoy Candace Parker and Reggie. Do you know how fucked up that is? Charles said Ant was regressing — he’s been the best volume three point shooter in the league this year!

It started well before last year’s playoffs, but that was the turning point for me. The way that network shit on Karl-Anthony Towns and Rudy Gobert (who had just won DPOY for the 4th time) during multiple playoff series was horrific for the watching experience, even if you don’t like one or both of those maligned players. Draymond being allowed to be at the table after being suspended for choking one of the players THAT SEASON is the epitome of what is wrong with coverage and Silver’s NBA. I don’t care if you hate Rudy Gobert — that’s just so insane that it was allowed to happen. He was yelling at Wolves fans to go home, who quite literally haven’t seen their team win anything but the recent ownership sale.

Back to the plot. This mentality was on full tilt last night — roasting the best of the best while they play, forcing them to take unnecessary breaks, not caring/knowing how to pronounce Sengun, Wembanyama, or Antetokounmpo, the Mr Beast stunt, the lack of self-awareness, the advertisements plastered everywhere like one big soccer kit.

Am I in the right here? Or am I just an angry Wolves fan who wishes small market teams weren’t the butt of the joke? Everything last night felt like a microcosm of Silver’s play to advertisers over actual consumers and players.
