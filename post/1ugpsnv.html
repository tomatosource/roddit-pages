Isn’t this Joe’s fault?

I feel like I’m stating the obvious but I haven’t heard anyone bring this up on any podcasts.

Brad Stevens spent his entire post-season interview talking about the C’s style of play and why it does not work, especially in the playoffs. This is a total reversal of what we saw from the Jaylen-led Celtics all season.

The entire year we saw this version of the Celtics win with tough defense, easy buckets (+mid-range jumpers) in close games. As soon as Tatum came back, especially in the playoffs, Joe was back to his old tricks, firing up bad shots, allowing Philly to get into games they shouldn’t have been in, and showing no ability to make adjustments in-game or game to game.

But instead of going after Joe or making a decision to bring in someone with more discipline, Brad looks to blow up the team for an alt-injured Giannis, just to take the keys out of Joe’s hands, signifying he doesn’t trust Joe to make schematic adjustments, so he has to go over his head to make personnel changes.

Jaylen plays exactly the way Brad is envisioning and what it takes to win in this league. A high-volume three point shooter who knows when an easy two is needed to slow a run or simply get a bucket when things get tight.

We just watched the Finals, where Mitch Johnson put on a Joe Mazz clinic. He didn’t call timeouts to stop runs, didn’t run sets to settle down his team, and continued to let them chuck threes even after going ice cold. This was Joe against the Knicks in ‘25 and he doesn’t seem to want to change.

Joe is not disciplined enough in the big games and spends the regular season playing a completely different style than what wins in today’s playoffs.
