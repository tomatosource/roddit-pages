I believe spear abilities would benefit from a change

Instead of having this rapid assault ability stick a spear warhead in on the last strike, I believe this ability should be separated into two different abilities:

1). New rapid assault that only swings 3 times (because any more than that is just too much animation lock) and the 3rd swing sticks out with more reach and more damage. Leave the bleed on it if you want, but taking bleed off of it and making rake better might be a better idea.

2). The part of rapid assault that sticks a warhead into the enemy would be placed on an entirely new ability that you throw a spear at a distance to accomplish this. Each warhead spear would have a finite number of charges (something like 5) and would regenerate on a fixed timer.
