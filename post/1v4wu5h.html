2025 Pacers: ideal blueprint for contending in the new era?

After watching the Hali-less Pacers be the toughest obstacle (IMO) on the road to Banner 18, then following it up with a Finals run, I’ve always considered them as an ideal sustainable blueprint for this new CBA era.

They had a good/deep roster filled with complementary role players who consistently played within their system of high-pace, high-movement offense. Their defense was not elite but good enough with switchable players to hold its own.

As always, the key is overall fit, being on the same page, and most importantly… health!!

I think our C’s are actually being built in a similar manner. Different personnel and strengths, but similar with the emphasis on spacing and 3ball filled with great value role players.

The Joe system is established, whether you like it or not. The underrated player development is excellent and neverending. Great value contracts are being signed. We have our franchise guy.

Player $$ valuation is steadily adapting to the restrictions of the new CBA. After getting off of PG’s contract, I would be perfectly fine with the 1 supermaxer-led team moving forward.

Of course, acquiring expensive talent is always great to have, if the opportunity arises, but since this new era doesn’t seem to favor long-term core runs, I wouldn’t mind the safer and more sustainable route of not having multiple max guys.
