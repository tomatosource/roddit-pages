Centers not named Zubac the C’s could target

Looking at our roster, the most glaring issue seems to be big man depth. While Queta has dons a great job, when he’s off the court we have almost bo big man presence. Recently, Luka Garza has been benched and we’ve been running ultra small woth Minott playing C in sone lineups. We’ve even brought up Amari Williams a few times to eat minutes.

Garza, Tillman, and Boucher are now end of bench guys. Once Queta goes out we lose a lot at the rim.

I dont think the goal necessarily needs to be a starting caliber C. Lots of really great teams in recent year’s have had a guy like Queta starting. So, instead of looking for a star level C, here are a few guys who could provide a shot blocking or rebounding presence in the 15 minutes where we rest Queta and still need a big.

Brook Lopez - Old as fuck but with a lot of great experience, Lopez is still shooting and blocking shots at a decent rate. This Clippers roster is dogshit and im not holding this year’s stats against him. There are scenarios of a trade where he could come back with Bogs, CP3, or Jones. Lopez’s deal is pretty small, and has a team option for next year, we either we like what we see or move on.

Da’Ron Holmes - Holmes is a guy i really liked last year in the draft. Hes a versatile defender, great size, and has the potential to be a 3 point shooter. Denver has Jokic and a proven backup in Valanciunas at C, and a handful of really good guys taking minutes at PF (Gordon, Johnson, Watson), so there hasn’t been much chance for the young big man to play. Hes averaging around 20 points, 7 rebounds and 4 assists in thr G League.

Jusuf Nurkic - Nurkic isnt the guy he used to be, but hes still one of the best rebounders in the league. Hes also a good passer. I know this isnt a sexy move, any time ive mentioned Nurkic in the past, people jump in and talk about how he’s not good. I agree he’s not that 30 minute guy l, but he can absolutely screen and rebound for 15 minutes a night. He also brings some edge.

Charles Bassey - Bassey flirted with the Celtics this summer playing for our summer league team. He looked a cut above the competition and then left just as weirdly as he arrived. He then signed with Atlanta but didnt make the main roster. Most recently, he spent a 10 day injury exception contract in Memphis. The 10 days came and went and as far as I know, Bassey is sitting at home not doing anything. He dunks, he blocks, he’s strong as an ox. Im not sure whats going on but im pretty confident he could give us 10 - 15 big man minutes on the boards.
