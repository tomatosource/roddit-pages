I think PoE2 passive tree needs masteries. (or something like it)

I recently played Mirage League in PoE1. I came back to PoE2 to de-rust a bit before 0.5 and realized spending passive points feels very unexciting and a bit aimless.

I think PoE2 passive tree needs to have some more interesting, build defining clusters to give a leveling character some goals and a better sense of progression.

It probably cant be weapon masteries because skills are already bound to weapons and that would make weapon clusters mandatory, but more universal ones would do the trick imo (stun mastery, ailment mastery, defence mastery etc.)

I remember Mark saying in one of the Q&As that he didnt want to add masteries to PoE2 because they are overused in PoE1, but honestly the passive tree needs some sort of a kick, right now it’s just a bit too bland.

If anyone has other ideas, feel free to share
