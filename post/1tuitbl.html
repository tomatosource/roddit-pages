Trials of Sekhemas should not be an RNG-laden debuff waterfall that’s required for character progression

Forcing players to bang their heads against RNG debuffs (many of which are not avoidable) and then go through a long chain of obstacles just to unlock core character progression is bad design.

Some people may like the types of games where you focus on dodging and restarting failed runs, so if they want to lock certain loot behind that, that’s fine. But forcing every single player to go through this when it is not the core game play style of path of exile is poor decision making.

And the fact that they have all the mob types increasing in difficulty as you progress through the run is also time-wasting. You could have a careful and clean first couple floors only to lose it all on the third floor, plus on the aoe-laden 3rd boss with slowing effects (in a trial where you need to avoid damage!).

And this would be fine if it was a challenge run for really rewarding loot, but making it required to unlock character power is not.
