Am I the only casual player loving PoE 2 more than ever?

As someone who dives deep into every league and leagues start but doesn’t have all deep mechanics memorized and gets hyped over a few Divines like it’s a jackpot, i really enjoy it at the moment. This season (0.4) is straight fire for me! With 0.3 mechanics now core, it’s never been easier: juice maps with three tablets, farm/sell essences, smash Chaos recipes, loot Expeditions… Endless casual-friendly ways to stack currency without hardcore profit-crafting.

0.3 was fantastic, but 0.4 levels it up (Temple aside maybe). Abysses on every map? Hell yes, I love ‘em! Other mechanics on the side, feels so smooth and rewarding. Room for tweaks always, but GGG’s direction is making PoE 2 a blast for casuals like us.
