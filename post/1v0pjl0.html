Pro basketball teams from New York State won 6 titles between 1951 and 1976, but managed to win only one title between 1977 and 2026

Crazy to me as a New York basketball fan how good my dad’s generation had it as kids, as well as his father’s. Especially compared to the last 50 years. New York never came close to dominating basketball like the Celtics or Lakers, but I’d say 6 titles in 26 years is very good:

1951: Rochester Royals (NBA)
1955: Syracuse Nationals (NBA)
1970: New York Knicks (NBA)
1973: New York Knicks (NBA)
1974: New York Nets (ABA)
1976: New York Nets (ABA)

4 titles for NYC teams in a 7 year span is great (even if it was in two leagues). The 1970s were easily New York’s best basketball era. Those Knicks were special as everyone knows, but people really forget how big it was having Dr. J in New York. He is one of the all time greats, and by far the greatest New York basketball player of all time. The ABA was only a sliver behind the NBA, and Dr. J dominated its most competitive era (the years directly before the merger), winning the last ABA title.

New York State teams made the NBA Finals SIX years in a row from 1950 to 1955, winning 2, including the only NYC vs. NYC matchup in a professional basketball championship series - between the Rochester Royals and New York Knicks in 1951. The Knicks did lose 3 finals in a row from 1951 to 1953. From 1951 to 1976, New York teams made the Finals 12 times. There was a 14 year drought in titles and finals appearances from 1956 to 1970, when the Knicks won their first championship.

Compare all that to one title in half a century… My uncle is 55 and just witnessed his first New York championship that he can remember. Words cannot express how happy I am that the Knicks finally won.

Before 2026, we had reached the NBA Finals 2x since our last professional basketball title (1994, 1999). The Nets reached the Finals in back-to-back years in 2002 and 2003 when they were the New Jersey Nets.
