What if Jaylen and Jayson consistently drive to the basket throughout the entire game next season?

We’re already seeing Jaylen Brown fully lean into attacking the rim this season, and it’s completely changed the dynamic of the Celtics’ offense. He’s drawing more attention, making quicker reads, and opening things up for everyone else in a way we didn’t consistently see before.

Now imagine if Jayson Tatum takes that same leap next season.

Less settling for step-back 3s… more downhill pressure, more midrange control, more “attack, attack, attack.” It’s kind of funny because not too long ago, we were all bracing ourselves every time JT pulled up for a random midrange jumper 😅 but now it actually feels like there’s a real opportunity for him to weaponize that area properly.

If both of them fully commit to this style and start living at the line… say 10 free throws a game each… how do you even game plan against that?

You can’t just sag off, you can’t just load up, and you definitely can’t send soft help. These aren’t just slashers, they’re elite scorers who can also make the right pass when the defense collapses.

At that point, you’re basically choosing how you want to lose:

• Let them get downhill and live at the line
• Overhelp and give up open shots everywhere
• Or try to play them straight up and get cooked anyway

If this version of JB is real… and JT adds that same relentless pressure… the ceiling of this team gets scary fast.
