Mark Hendrickson: The last athlete to play in the NBA & MLB

Dick Groat, Dick Ricketts, Cotton Nash, Ron Reed, Steve Hamilton, Dave DeBusschere, Gene Conley, Howie Schultz, Danny Ainge & Mark Hendrickson are the only athletes to play in the NBA & MLB.

Mark Hendrickson is the most recent athlete to play in both the MLB & NBA.

Hendrickson, a 6 foot 9 lefty, played basketball & baseball at Washington State University.

After graduating, Hendrickson was drafted in the 2nd round in 1996 by the 76ers then drafted in the 20th round in 1997 by the Toronto Blue Jays.

From 1996 to 2000, Hendrickson averaged 13 minutes in 114 NBA career games with 4 different teams.

From 2002 to 2011, Hendrickson complied a 58-74 record in the MLB with a 5.03 ERA and 666 strikeouts

Utah Jazz CEO Danny Ainge played mostly 2nd base for the Toronto Blue Jays while playing basketball at BYU.

Gene Conley is the only athlete to win championships in the MLB and NBA.

Conley won the 1957 World Series with the Milwaukee Braves as a teammate of Hank Aaron then won 3 consecutive NBA championships with Bill Russell.

Groat became an 8x All-star and won a MVP & World Series with Pittsburgh in 1960 after playing with the Fort Wayne Pistons in 1952.

DeBusschere pitched 102 innings for the Chicago White Soxes in 1962 & 1962 before becoming an 8x All-Star in the NBA and winning 2 championships with the Knicks.
