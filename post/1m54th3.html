Adrian Dantley: The ultimate free throw merchant before “free throw merchant” was a term

Adrian Dantley has always been a fascinating player to me. In all the discussions about the great scorers in NBA history, his name rarely comes up – but he’s responsible for some of the greatest high volume + high efficiency scoring seasons ever in the league.

To illustrate – if you filter the highest TS% seasons for 20+ ppg (min. 65 games), the top 20 is littered with names like Jokic, Curry, Barkley, Stoudemire, McHale, Embiid, Reggie Miller, Lebron, Giannis, KD, and KAT. But then there’s the man himself, Adrian Dantley, coming at #12 with 65.2% TS on 30.6 ppg (!!!) in 1983-84. It’s particularly impressive given the low efficiency era he was playing in. As a result, his TS+ (true shooting adjusted for league average) was a ridiculous 120 in 1983-84. For reference, Steph’s 2015-16 season was TS+ of 124, and Jokic’s 2022-23 was 121, so Dantley’s 1983-84 was almost as ridiculous as some of the all time great efficiency seasons, relative to the league. Some other seasons of his included 27.4 ppg on 66.1% TS in 1982, 27.5 ppg on 63.1% TS in 1981, and 29.7 ppg on 62.9% TS in 1985. All his stats are here.

The thing that really fascinates me is how exactly he was so efficient. He wasn’t a 3 point shooter. And he was an efficient scorer from the field (54% career FG), but that doesn’t fully explain it either. The real reason is that he was a fucking free throw merchant. In fact, one of the great free throw merchants this league has ever seen.

From the 5 seasons of 1981-1985, he averaged over 10 free throws a game in all of them (except for 9.9 in 1984). That includes an astounding 12 FT (!!!) a game in 1983. He was truly elite at getting to the line. And that, combined with being an ~80% FT shooter, meant he stacked up points like crazy without too many shot attempts. From what I understand, he scored primarily from the post as a 6’5” SF, and was really good at drawing fouls.

It’s funny looking back on this given all the discussion these days about free throw merchants, foul baiting, etc. with respect to guys like SGA and Embiid. Adrian Dantley’s career was well before I was born, but I’m curious how people responded to his free throw volume back then.

Anyways, I just wanted to make this post to highlight a very interesting player from the 1980s who’s discussed relatively little given how fantastic his numbers were and how interestingly high his free throw count was. I know he had trouble getting his team to the playoffs, was an apparent black hole on offense and weak on defense, and had a poor locker room reputation – which makes his career relatively forgotten. But he’s still definitely an intriguing player.
