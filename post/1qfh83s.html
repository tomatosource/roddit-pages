Estimated Luxury Tax Payments for the 2026 Season

With Kyle Tucker and Bo Bichette signing contracts with unprecedented AAVs, I wanted to go out and calculate just how much each team will be receiving as a result of the luxury tax payments being made by teams that are above the threshold.

Using Fangraph’s estimated luxury tax payroll for 2026 and excel. I was able to calculate the payment each team will be recevieng as a result of the penalties. Percentages in the middle columns were obtained from the MLB glossary on the CBT based on escalating amounts. Under the current agreement, the total amount of the penalties are put into a pool and distributed to all teams under the threshold. In 2026, Fangraphs estimates that 22 teams will be under the threshold and will receive luxury tax payments from the Dodgers, Mets, Blue Jays, Phillies, Braves, Yankees, Padres, and Red Sox.

After completing the table and determining the percentages based on how many years a team has been over the threshold, we reach a total calculated luxury tax payments for the 2026 season to be ~\(362.7m. When distributed to 22 teams, that comes to \~\)16.5m per team.

According to this, the Dodgers total payroll including luxury tax penalties will be \(571m, over \)157m more than the Mets. To put that into additional perpective, if the Atlanta Braves (whose financial statements are publically available) were to run a payroll this high in 2026, I’ve calculated their estimated OIBDA (operating income before depreciation and amortization) to be -$166m:

https://preview.redd.it/te32kuhymxdg1.png?width=748&format=png&auto=webp&s=92a94a8679383c0ddff342cccf60a38042eebd9e

This projection is also assuming the most optimistic revenue estimate for 2026 I could find of \(750m and stagnant non-payroll baseball operating costs, costs related to the Battery, and SG&A (which is very conservative as costs increase YoY, so the losses would actually end up being greater). The \)797m in baseball operating costs was obtained by taking 2024 non-payroll operating costs (Fangraphs payroll of \(278m in 2024 subtracted from the \)504m) and adding in the $571m estimated Dodgers payroll.

Just wanted to provide some actual numbers and estimates based on what is publically available.
