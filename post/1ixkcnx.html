Crafting / orb / economy idea

Not sure if any similar idea has ever been floated, but the basic idea is this:

Non corrupted gear gains xp as you wear it / kill with it (maybe capped by your own rate of lvls gained).

Introduce an orb of extraction, which mirrors / extracts an affix from your gear. The extraction is annotated with a quality value, which is based on rng, the xp of the underlying item, and the level of the affix. If the quality is too low, the extraction simply fails. After extracting, regardless of success, the underlying gear disappears.

E.g. a ring with +200 mana affix and 40 hours worth of xp might have a 50% chance of successfully being extracted, conditional on the orb selecting that affix. If you are unlucky it targets light radius 😃

The orb may now be applied to a new item (of sufficient lvl) to overwrite a random affix, or simply sold.

Benefits:

Much more targeted crafting.

Using non endgame gear with a single high tier good roll affix can be rewarding (levelling affixes can be an income source).

A way to flush good items out of the economy and keep farming rewarding (massive).

Reduce the wealth disparity between full time farmers (who run the same perfect gears for 1000 hours and don’t benefit as much from this) and the average player.

More complexity:

At xp breakpoints, item quality may increase beyond 20

Items can have mods like: 10% increased chance of extraction, or 50% increased item xp gain (applies to all equipped items; could also be a passive)

Add a div cost based on affix tier for extraction. Too many divs in the market :) Cost is paid after successful extraction.
