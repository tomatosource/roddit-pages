Lebron James is the only player in NBA history to have played a full season at both Shooting Guard and Center.

Yes that’s right. No other player in NBA history has been listed on Basketball Reference as both a Shooting Guard in one season, Center in another season. Lebron is the only player with that label. He was listed at SG in his rookie year and C in his 20th season. Not trying to cherrypick but I find it funny that no other player has managed to play a full season of both positions over the course of their careers.

Who do you think will be the next player to be listed at both SG/C on Basketball Reference?
