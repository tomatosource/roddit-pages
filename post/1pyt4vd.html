My version of endgame

So I don’t know If Im alone in this feeling but to me the endgame for PoE2(and PoE1 for that matter) is hit t15 maps do a few of them and then just make another build and go again. I have very little desire to finish all the pinnacle bosses or hit lvl 100. I just find fun in pushing new fun looking builds to t15 and optimizing them up to that point to see how fast I can get them to that point and then going again with something different and using the currency earned in the process to fund the next character. And with that in mind I think the endgame is perfect…. But like I say I have almost zero interest in pushing pinnacles….Does anyone else feel this way with these games or is it just me.
