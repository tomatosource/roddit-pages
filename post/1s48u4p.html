Recent discourse around Jaylen Brown is nonsense

As a Sixers fan I was rolling my eyes seeing people call Jaylen Brown a hypocrite and foul-baiter/flopper. He absolutely foul-baited during the win yesterday. But that’s because he had to - he was trying to make a statement saying “THIS is how you want me to play? THIS is what you want to see?”. Brown is one of the most talented paint players in the league, no way he needs to sell contact. His midrange and finishing at the rim are elite, and he’s delivered against great defenses. If anything, he should be MORE vocal about the foul baiting problem. I would even talk about this explicitly in press conferences, mentioning teams and names.

Even as a Sixers fan I was glad to see the Celtics make a statement last night. Whatever team you root for, you just can’t respect flopping and foul baiting. Jaylen Brown is more worthy of the MVP than SGA.
