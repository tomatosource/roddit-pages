Instead of watching a 10 minute video for one PathOfExile2 tip, how about we share our own tips in this post?

My one tip is before doing some content like Expedition or Breach, I always summon a portal for a quick exit.

This also helps with replenishing my potions and sometimes it kind of pauses the monsters when I teleport back so I can curse the area or cast something situational.

Or sometimes it’s just a straight up escape. I might not get out of a situation alive if I return to the map.
