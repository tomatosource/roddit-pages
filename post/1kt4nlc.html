For those players who strictly play PoE2, you should still watch the PoE1 livestream on June 5th to claim a free MTX.

Every time GGG does a livestream for a league or expansion, they give away free microtransactions. For PoE1 it tends to be a \(10-\)30 portal or back attachment.

You may be able to use this microtransaction immediately in PoE2. If not, it will definitely be available in the future.

https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3783838

Be sure to connect your Path of Exile account to your Twitch.tv account!

  1. While you’re logged in to your Twitch.TV account, login to your PoE account at PathOfExile.com. Go to your account page and then to manage account located on the right side of the page. Scroll down to Social Connections and underneath will be a section for Twitch. Click the Connect button.

  2. Verify the accounts are connected by visiting the connections page on Twitch. Scroll all the way down to Other Connections and if Path of Exile is listed there, you’re linked!

Useful information about Path of Exile 1:

  • Every new league has a set of challenges(typically 40) that you may complete for some microtransactions. These will all work in Path of Exile 2 eventually! Here are some examples of past challenge rewards:

  • Settlers of Kalguur

  • Lake of Kalandra

  • Sentinel

  • For everyone commenting about not wanting to play PoE1 due to no pause and no WASD: Jonathan has stated these is coming in the future. Pausing may actually come this patch(no guarantees.) WASD will come once they have completed rigging all of the microtransactions to the new character models.
