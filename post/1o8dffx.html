Just wanted to share some of my enjoyment and learnings

Hi folks,

I’m by no means any sort of POE2 pro or super knowledgeable about the game - I am a 40’something casual lol but, I enjoy the game.

I tried POE1, but felt like I got to the party too late and there were so many mechanics and leagues, I was kinda lost … so when POE2 was announced and released, I’ve been dipping my toe in and out – but having fun.

One problem I have with games (D&D or POE, in general) – I find I always come up with an idea/build, have fun at first and then get fixated on a new “flashy” idea (this is my ADHD) and restart … or I get decision paralysis, trying to think of the “exact right build” … and it’s stopped me from fully enjoying the game.

Anyways, with that out of the way – I just wanted to share for any folks who experience the same or similar. I decided to start 4 playthroughs/characters at once. I don’t really have any idea of where to go with the builds, so I might mess them up – but I’m less concerned about being the most powerful and just want to have fun and relax.

So I’ve created:

- A Wind/Lightning Monk who uses Lightning Spear and Tempest Flurry

- Fire warrior using a 2H Hammer and Fire staff, only focused on that sort damage/spells

- Mercenary using Rake and shield and either a bow or crossbow (haven’t figured that out yet)

And last but not least, a Ice Sorceress – with Ice Nova and Contagion (to start)

The Ice Sorceress is the one I want to share more about. I’ve never really been a “mage” type of person, but to battle indecison and decision paralysis – making all these characters, I want to level them all, keep them themed and try my hand at them.

So playing this Ice Sorceress, I hadn’t really tried Ice Nova or Contagion, I was mostly focused on Ice and Cold spells, but I know there’s some enemies that are 100% resistant to a damage, so the Chaos was only to counter that. But I’ve got to say, I’m super surprised at just how much I’m enjoying this build so far. The character is somewhat mobile, but I start with Contagion, cast it – then jump in the middle of a pack, use Ice Nova and then the enemies are freezing, breaking and spreading the contagion. I’m clearing packs and levelling up so fast! It feels incredible!

I think I also got SUPER LUCKY, I had an ice staff drop, Level 1 with 40% increase to Cold Damage – and then when I got to level 10 I had a 2 socket Ice Staff with 21% increased spell damage, 58+ max mana, +2 to all Cold Spell Skills, 16% increased Crit spell damage bonus, 12% mana regen and 5% light radius.

Again, I’m not a pro and I don’t always know what’s good or what isn’t – but all the leanings in to Cold for these staffs… it’s been awesome!

Anyways, if you’ve made it to the end – thanks for reading – and if you’re like me, I hope you found something in what I shared helpful.

Happy questing folks!

Ps - Oh, and I hope I’m not coming across like I’ve come up something or anything original, I’m sure there’s other people who have paired Ice Nova and Contagion – but this was a first for me and it’s been so much fun.
