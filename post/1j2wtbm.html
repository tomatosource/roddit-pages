I figured out how to “use” the Currency Exchange, and I love it.

Figured I would post this since I see more negative feedback than positive, but I love the Currency Exchange.

I say “use” since it operates similar to the stock market in a way, you can undercut the going rate to sell orders fast, or if you can wait you can put in orders that are over the “market rate” in hopes the cheap supply is bought and your orders are filled.

Also, if you want to buy something at a discount you can put that order in and wait for it to fill, if you can wait.

I’m sure there is the opportunity to flip currency here, but whatever. I just like it quite a bit, and it makes liquidating stuff from all the different game modes easy.

For the record, I do think gear should remain the way it is now.
