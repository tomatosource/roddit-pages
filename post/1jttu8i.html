I am done with this game

I was having a blast in 0.10. Leveled my home brewed character to 96. Did the end game without the need for cookie cutter guides or trading. I was happy to finally have found an ARPG that fit me. After playing D4 where every mob dropped full inventory worth of items, POE2 felt like a fresh breath of air. A good balance of loot, difficulty and depth. And things were only to get better. Or so I thought.

I am nearing the end of Act 3 and honestly after killing Viper Napuatzi, getting one shitty rare drop, killing 4 next rare monsters and getting only magic loot as a reward I am done. It is not even the difficulty and slower pace that is the problem. I managed to adapt to the pace. The problem is the lack loot. I got more exalts in 0.10 than I got augment orbs this expansion. Rare monsters that are like mini bosses now drop jack shit. What is the point of of clearing huge maps if all you have to show for are few rares and a handful of magic items if you are lucky. I feel even if only items dropped for your class it would still be too few.

If this is GGG’s vision of their game I want no part of it. Which is a shame, because I really enjoyed it. I will give it another go during full release or if I see a substantial change. Until then, I will vote the only way I can - my time and money.

/rant
