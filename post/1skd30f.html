What I Hope will happen with temple

I hope, and I mean HOPE, is that the temple will be tweaked and changed with Functionality and recraftability in mind. Currently there is 2, maybe 3 camps in mind that I can think of.

1: Infinite temple.

2: Finite temple and boss rush.

3: I don’t care I don’t play temple.

The main thing that all of these camps have a problem with is functionality of making or remaking the temple.

How I always figured it could be fixed is 3 layers, one of which will piss off the Infinity temple camp.

• Step 1: refreshing tiles utilizing energy crystals as the currency to refresh. This can be added around act 4 when you have Doryani in the temple.

Function: 3 energy to refresh the tilesets giving different tiles to use. If you’ve used all your room tiles, it will refresh with all movement directional tiles and if you haven’t used all your rooms, it will refresh 1 room tile to use per refresh.

&gt;What this does: This makes energy be a currency for more fluid and intuitive temple crafting to fix both for infinite and finite temple enjoyers which is the primary problem with the temple, the crafting and recrafting process after destruction or breaking of chains.

• Step 2: Tradable energy crystals, also introduced by Doryani after you’ve beaten the architect once. You gain access to converting your energy at a ratio of say, 2:1 or 3:1 of untradable energy for tradable, fungible use for all parties involved. People who ignore temple can now have a monetary transaction that can benefit them even if they don’t run temple while allowing for much more open use of energy and rerolling of tiles for step 1 for temple enjoyers as a whole.

• Step 3: remove the locking feature of the temple and limit the destroyed rooms, but in exchange add an attempts limit. 20 seems like a nice round number. Change the locking medallion to additional attempts +1 (max 5 until resetting the temple) and if you don’t visit Atziri, oh well. It still blows up.

This is if you want intentionally or unintentionally to hurt the infinite Temple enjoyers fun, which in my personal opinion step 2 and 1 allay much of the problems that removing the infinite nature of the temple currently has and should be removed for the health of the game overall. This is my own personal opinion showing, but all content should have an end. It should not eternally go on but NEEDS an endpoint and rebuild for it to be healthy for the game.

Suggestions are welcome and I’m sure more than one person will have an issue somewhere.. I can definitely see step 3 getting hate. But step 1 and 2, no matter the form the temple may take would benefit from both of these steps.
