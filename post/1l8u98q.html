[Barlowe] There’s a growing belief that the Washington Wizards could trade Jordan Poole this summer

Source

Khaman Maluach continues to gain traction as a potential fit in Washington. The rationale is straightforward — Alex Sarr prefers to operate on the perimeter and hasn’t shown a consistent willingness to mix it up inside. That opens the door for a more traditional rim presence, and Maluach brings the type of length, mobility, and defensive instincts that align with Washington’s recent draft habits.

Another name worth watching is Oklahoma’s Jeremiah Fears. I had Fears mocked to Washington at No. 6 in my latest projection, and the freshman lead guard has reportedly drawn real interest from the Wizards’ front office. His shiftiness, scoring burst, and upside as a playmaker make him an intriguing option in that range.

Also worth noting — around the league, there’s growing belief that Jordan Poole could be moved, either on draft night or later this summer. That potential shake-up adds another layer to Washington’s draft strategy and could open up a path for a new backcourt face.
