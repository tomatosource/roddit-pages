Why does mouse aiming/targeting remain awful after so many patches?

Let’s nevermind the fact that it constantly chooses the wrong target to lock onto for a minute. It doesn’t even ground target correctly! When I press a spell, it should lock that spell onto the point in space where I was aiming at the time I pressed it. Instead, if my character moves, the ground-targeted AoE also moves. As a result, for example, when I target a corpse with Unearth while running past, my character will shoot the Unearth slightly to the side of the corpse because I was moving.

The game isn’t even choosing the target at the time of the cast - it’s waiting until the completion of the cast. It’s crazy to expect me to keep my mouse held perfectly over something for 0.2s while running, especially when we also use our mouse to run.

Bonus points because Unearth snaps to enemies, meaning it’ll ignore corpse targeting if there are enemies nearby. Thanks for deciding what I want for me, Microsof– err, GGG.
