sharing my expedition farming strategy: ~4 divines per T15 map

This is a mid-end strategy, it’s not close to high end atziri temple’s, but I am enjoying it.

Strategy: sell expedition runic splinters to high end atziri temple farmers

What I like about it: consistent decent earnings without needing a super rare drop to carry the yield. It’s consistent because logbook drop is consistent and runic splinter drop is consistent, removing ultra rare drops from the process.

Yield: ~12 divines per expedition logbook, works out to ~4-5 divines per T15 map.

Steps

  1. run expedition precursor tablets.

  2. explosives can go wherever, but always take increased quantity of logbook drops.

  3. in T15 maps with irradiation, you’ll get 1 logbook every ~2-3 maps. More if you’re juicing a lot. So this strategy works out to a steady drip of ~4-5 divines per map.

  4. When you run the logbooks, explosive placement has a dramatic effect on yield. Runic splinters almost never come from monsters regardless of juice. Runic splinters come from excavated chests.

  5. When opening a logbook map, Dannig gives you a choice of which to run. Prioritize additional chest markers and explosive radius, as the goal is to maximize number of excavated chests you get.

  6. When you open the logbook map, run around entire thing (annoying but crucial), find all sources of increased quantity/rarity for excavated chests.

  7. Place explosives to maximize chest quantity/rarity, and number of chests. Completely ignore monster rarity/quantity/etc., it has no effect on yield at all. The yield comes from (number of chests * chest rarity * chest quantity).

  8. For your atlas passives, for low-to-mid-endgame players, the runic splinters are too valuable to use yourself. Nevertheless, run a single T1 olroth (50 splinters ~= 12 divs) so you have 4 expedition atlas passives. Place them into Detailed Records so your logbooks drop lvl 81 with 4 modifiers - that’s crucial to maximize odds of getting a great logbook mod like “13 additional chest markers”.

Just ran two logbooks now, &gt;100 runic splinters, sold for ~4.8 splinters/divine, yield was 27 divines = 13.5 divine per logbook = 4.5 divines per map farming logbooks.
