With their 42nd win on the season, the Toronto Raptors will finish above 0.500 for the first time since 2021⁄22.

That was Scottie Barnes’ rookie season where they finished 6th with a 48-34 record that ultimately lost in 6 games to the Harden-Embiid 76ers. It’s been a while since they’ve had a record above 0.500 but they achieved that while making history at that with their 31-0 run.

Where this Raptors team will end up in the standings - and their ultimate W/L record - remains to be seen but getting the tiebreaker over the Magic secures them a far better chance of getting a playoff seed proper rather than being stuck in the play-in for a chance at the postseason.
