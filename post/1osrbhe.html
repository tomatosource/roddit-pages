What was the most story line heavy season?

This post on the subreddit earlier about Ichiro breaking George Sisler’s single season hits record reminded me of 2004 overall and how many storylines there were throughout the season, and I can’t recall there being any particular season that has so many concurrent storylines and narratives throughout. Off the top of my head we have:

  • Ichiro chasing and beating George Sisler’s single season hits record
  • The Red Sox in their quest to end their World Series drought that was already reaching a boiling point when ARod and Jason Varitek had their fight and with the Red Sox trading Nomar Garciapparra like a week later.
  • At least three Barry Bonds talking points and storylines: Passing 700 home runs and his continued chase of Babe Ruth and Hank Aaron, the BALCO stuff still lurking in the background, and he being intentionally walked so often.
  • The last season of the Expos in Montreal.

I’m sure there are other contenders for most story line heavy season, so list ‘em out, or maybe I forgot stuff from 2004.
