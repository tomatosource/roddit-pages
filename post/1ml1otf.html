The late 90s early 00s were a wild time

Was there just something in the air in the NBA during this period. It feels like a whole chunk of legends from this era were either doing crazy stuff on the court, or were in a court room. If this sub was pearl clutching like it does with Ja Morant, I think the pearls would explode if they had to deal with this list in real time in this era.
