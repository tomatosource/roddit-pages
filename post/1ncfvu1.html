Poe2 campaign appreciation thread

I personally think the campaign is the greatest part of the game and probably will be for some time and that isn’t because the endgame seems lacking right now but because i think the campaign is genuinely a great experience.. the pacing, the visuals and the story are all top notch, if they keep up this level of quality with the next two acts it will be perfect.

Something feels off when going from the campaign and into maps though, it’s like going from a carefully curated experience into something that feels rushed and cobbled together, honestly the endgame should just be more acts with the same quality but harder.😂
