Favorite Playoff Moment From The Game-Losing Team?

I’ve asked this same question before, but I didn’t really specify enough with the title.

So, again, what is your favorite postseason moment from a team that would still drop said game? It doesn’t even matter whether they took the series or not, they just had to have lost the game in which this play took place.

For example, my favorite of all time is from our most recent Fall Classic. In Game 7, a hobbled Bo Bichette hit a 3-run bomb that lipped the 2nd deck in left-center field, and fell back into the bleachers. This sent the entire Rogers Centre (bar the away fans) into a borderline mosh pit, and their 45,000 fans started jumping like mad and roaring like wild tigers once it landed. Unfortunately, the Jays would go on to drop the game 5-4 in 11 innings, as the Dodgers repeated as champions. But even despite the loss, this homer should be heralded as one of the greatest World Series moments in modern baseball history.
