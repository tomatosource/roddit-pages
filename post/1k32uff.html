Add “sold items history” to trade website

Ignoring the hundreds of issues with the POE2 trade system, if they wish to stick with the current system, one thing desperately needed is the ability to see a history of purchased items, not just the ones currently listed.

If for example I wanna sell a rare Yugioh card from my childhood on eBay, I don’t base my listing price on the other current listings. Literally survivorship bias, those are the ones left listed because no one has bought them. I base the price of the card on the sold cards, so that I can see what people are actually willing to buy.

Why doesn’t POE2 have this? It would help so much, because instead of only seeing stupidly inflated prices on the site, we would see what people are actually happy to spend on items similar to what we’re selling.

Please, if we’re going to use such an archaic “friction” trade system, at least let us use it more accurately by adding this “sold items history” feature.
