Mouse Controls - A rant (Again)

Since my last post wasn’t received well, I’ll try again and clearly list my problems with the mouse controls:

  1. It’s not user-friendly. The move command and auto-attack are on the same button, and the move command can’t be removed from left-click. This means that when kiting mobs, a misclick results in moving toward them. The only way to prevent this is to shift-click, which adds unnecessary inputs and inherently punishes the player by stopping their movement.
  2. Changing the function of other skills to “move only” (right-click) removes one of your spell slots, putting mouse players at a disadvantage.
  3. In a game where mouse controls are encouraged and presented to the player as a control scheme, there should be proper customization.
  4. Players often just say “play WASD,” but that ignores the fact that the devs should be accommodating player preferences.
  5. The original Path of Exile has been out since October 23, 2013—over 12 years. The developers should have had the controls figured out by now.
  6. In a game with a playstyle very similar to other mouse-control-based games (e.g., League of Legends, Dota, Albion), they should emulate those controls so players from those games can adjust more easily. Many new players would appreciate this.

What I want from the devs:

  1. Allow players to remove movement from left-click.
  2. Change the skill system so that assigning “move only” to right-click doesn’t consume a skill slot.

That’s it.
