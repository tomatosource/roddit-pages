Stevens and Belichick

Something I have been thinking about for a while now.

Remember when Belichick and Brad were hanging out a few years ago, and Brad had a sort of 😍 look on his face when talking to Belichick?

At the time, the Cs were already on the doorway to a championship, but getting one isn’t really the hard part when you have a franchise like the Celtics. The hard part is sustaining success.

In the early 2000s, Theo Epstein was running the Sox and as a native Massachusetts resident, it wasn’t out of place when he started rocking Pats gear…but it was curious when he started incorporating some visits to Foxboro, approaching the media and player dealings in a Belichickian way.

The fact is, having had this coach/GM monolith an hour outside of town, having a cerebral coach -&gt; President/GM, having a coach like Joe Mazulla from the area, you start to see exactly how the Celtics have molded themselves in the early (best) part of the Pats dynasty.

Next man up. Do your job. No piece is greater than the sum.

When the season started, I was hoping for 2008 Pats results. A scrappy team that gelled, made some noise but was never a contender without their “star”. Now I see the Celtics as not only a contender, but also this machine that can survive, and thrive, simply through the process of team building.

It’s awesome. And I think a piece of this is how Belichick impacted the world of coaching and approaching the roster.
