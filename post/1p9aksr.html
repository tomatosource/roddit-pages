BSOD on loading screen POE 2 - Critical_Process_Died 0xc01 Possible FIX

I quite love this game, and in my 1000+ hours in Poe 2, I’ve had a lot of fun. But starting at 600 hours, this is patch 0.2 somewhere,when i opened the game, I had a CRITICAL_PROCESS_DIED problem. So I changed the SSD, changed the motherboard, changed the processor. But nothing helped.

I saw many solutions on the forums, but only one thing helped, it was to disable ENGINE MULTITHREADING. So here’s the fix.

  1. C:\Users\User\Documents\My Games\Path of Exile 2

  2. poe2_production_Config.ini - open it, change “engine_multithreading_mode=enabled” to =disabled.

  3. After that, you can open the game without crashes, and u can enable back multithreading in settings.

If the error appears again, just do the steps again. If that didn’t helped, then check your Ram, GPU, and your SSD.
