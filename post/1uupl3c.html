Why dont GGG just add convocation to the game ?

For people who dont know convocation is a skill from poe1 that teleports all your minions to you and gives them a small life regen effect. This helps keep them alive a little but is really important to re-poistion your minions.

Little things like projectile shields you can move past and teleport your minions inside or even just having an option for when you outrun your minions.

Currently in poe2 this skill does not exist but there is a method to teleport all your minions to you. You can un-summon then re-summon them all and it sets the location next to the player. The way you do this is by weapon set1 having your minions and weapon set2 having none or a different type. So if you weapon swap then back it re-summons all your minions placing them next to you and also fully healing them. One person tracked and they used weapon swap over 200 times in one map and no skills at all, this seems like a clear bad game design to me.

There is alot of minions in poe2 that are balanced around being slow, so the builds using them just do this setup and weapon swap over and over to skip the movement downside and also make them super tanky as it fully heals them every time.

This has been going on since 0.1 so why would you not just add a skill to teleport your minions instead of making a player weapon swap 2 times to get the exact same effect ?
