I hope the new 0.5 endgame brings more Exalt-sinks into the game

  • In 0.1 we had Exalt:Divine exchange ratio reaching 2000-3000+ Exalts for 1 divine.

  • In 0.2 and 0.3 it was very similar often reaching 1500+ Exalts for 1 divine.

Currently in 0.4 due to the current iteration of the Vaal temple (which is not surviving 0.5) printing thousands of divines combined with most temple players hiding exalts from their filters we have a “healthy” range of exchange ratios of 150:1 to 300:1 of exalt:divine.

However when temple likely gets significantly changed in 0.5 and the game is no longer flooded with thousands of divines from temple we will once again see exalt : divine ratios explode like in all previous leagues.

The entire game economy centers around basic concepts of supply and demand so I hope 0.5 endgame introduces more exalt-sinks (that increase demand for exalts which helps prevent massively inflated exalt:divine ratios) and that exalts have more new uses in late game than just exalt slamming waystones and some items.
