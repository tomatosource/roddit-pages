Luka Doncic is hosting the entire Los Angeles Lakers roster in Slovenia for a four-day minicamp in August, a source close to the Lakers told ESPN. The itinerary will include workouts, golf and some Ljubljana tourism and bonding ahead of training camp.

“And it appears Doncic is taking his leadership role seriously as he is set to host the entire team for a minicamp in Slovenia next month before the start of training camp, via Dave McMenamin of ESPN.

Considering how many new players the Lakers added this offseason, this is a great idea by Doncic to get to know each other and begin the chemistry-building process for the upcoming season. The Lakers recently had a minicamp with a few players in Las Vegas during Summer League, but Doncic was not in attendance as he has been back in Slovenia spending time with his daughters all offseason.”
