Lustrum of Parity: Not only is there no repeat champions in the last 5 years; The last 5 NBA Champions have not made it past the second round the following year

The 2015-18 NBA Finals saw a quadrology between the Warriors and the Cavaliers. Some said it was boring seeing the same two teams over and again. So from 2019-24 there have been 6 unique NBA champions.

Ironically though, the ratings were way better during the Steph-LeBron meetings.

Of the 6 Champions from 2019-24, 5 have not made it past the second round in their title defense year. With a loss against the Knicks tonight, the Celtics could be 6th:

Championship Year Team Following Year Playoff Result
2019 Toronto Raptors 2020 Eliminated in the Conference Semifinals by the Boston Celtics, 3-4
2020 Los Angeles Lakers 2021 Eliminated in the First Round by the Phoenix Suns, 2-4

2022 Golden State Warriors 2023 Eliminated in the Conference Semifinals by the Los Angeles Lakers, 2-4

If you believe in patterns, it goes lose in 7 - lose in 6 - lose in 7 - lose in 6 - lose in 7 - so the next item would be lose in 6. Which would mean the Knicks should get it done tonight.
