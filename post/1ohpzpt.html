Ohtani and Judge finished 1-2 in total bases in ‘24 & ‘25, the first repeat pair since Stan Musial and Bill Nicholson, 1943-44

Bear with me, I wanted a way to quantify how rarely two hitters are so dominant simultaneously. Normally there are a higher number of players vying for a top 2 finish so you get a variety in the leaderboards. A super trio took shape in the late 1950s when Aaron, Mays, and Banks made up the top 3 in 1957, 1958 and 1960, each time a different order.

If you are not familiar Nicholson, he shined brightest for the Cubs during WWII including leading the team in homers on the way to the misbegotten 1945 World Series.

Anyway prior to Musial and him, you have to go back to Jimmie Foxx and Chuck Klein, 1932-33.

Ohtani is the first to lead his league in TB 3 years in a row since Jim Rice, 1977-79.

By another measure, this will (likely) be the first time the same players win MVP two years in a row. The best comparison there might be Yogi Berra and Roy Campanella winning 3 MVP awards each in the span of 1951 to 1955, surely to the dismay of many fans outside New York.
