TIL: Crossbows have a hidden Reload keybind (PC)

Playing crossbow for the first time, really wishing there was an “omni”-reload key.

Turns out, there is! It is just hidden in the ini-file. And it works! I’m playing HCSSF and so far has worked reliably.

Navigate to:

%USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\Path of Exile 2\

Open this ini file:

poe2_production_Config.ini

Find:

reload_crossbow=82

&nbsp;

Note: There are two entries, one for click-to-move and another for WASD.

By default, it should be “0”, meaning unbound. “82” is the keycode for the “R” key.

&nbsp;

Find the keycode you need here:

https://www.toptal.com/developers/keycode

Hope this helps anyone!

&nbsp;

BONUS: There is also

cycle_crossbow_ammo=0

, but I haven’t tried it. If you do, let me know in the comments if/how it works!
