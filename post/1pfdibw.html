Here are some of the notes where there are changes which I believe should include Electrocute:

Spear of Solaris: Removed Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory for this Skill on Heavy Stunning an enemy. Now generates 1 Glory when you inflict Freeze, Shock or Ignite on an enemy.

Thunder storm also is shown on the expansion’s landing page to give the Drench debuff, where enemies are more easily shocked and frozen.

These have no mention of Electrocute, making builds who want to leverage it as a way to build up against enemies, to interact with these skills, unable to do so. I haven’t found any other cases yet, but I just wanted to share this input before it becomes too late to include it.
