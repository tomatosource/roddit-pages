I think Simon’s is genuinely a perfect long term fit and allow me to explain why

We have been here before where we trade for a guy who’s one player in a different/non-winning situation, they come to Boston, and we are able to turn them into something or fix their mistakes. Brad did it with Derrick, nobody saw white becoming such an elite shooter and when he became that he became one of the most valuable players in the league.

What’s Simon’s issue? From the Portland side, a lot of this is redundancy and lack of room, they’ve got sharpe and scoot who they’d like to start getting more minutes so they don’t need a third slightly older but still young guy in Simon’s, 2 guard development projects is plenty.

Outside of that, it’s defense, to this point Simon’s has not been an effective defender. I will say right here right now, I am very bullish on him defensively in Boston. For the first time in his career he will be the smallest guy on the court for his team, before landing in Boston he was always the 2 guard, whether it’s next to dame or scoot, he’s always been miscast as a 2 guard not playing with a good guard defender next to him.

Now he’s next to Derrick white, who is an elite defender, who is long and strong and can comfortably begin to take the bigger stronger guard defensive matchup into his 30s where some of his quickness begins to go and his strength increases. Simon’s is very quick, he will now be able to leverage his speed and relatively good size for taking point guard matchups, this allows us to put him in a position to succeed defensively

Do I expect him to be a good switch defender? Probably not, which is fine, I’m not suggesting we get better or stay as good defensively from this.

Do I expect our offensive to receive a pretty significant supercharge? Yeah, I do, Simon’s and white are gonna get up like 20 3’s a game and shoot 38+% on them, we are gonna be raining hellfire offensively, I love jrue but Simon’s is twice the shooter he was.

In closing, I’m very bullish on Simon’s for this team moving forward and I think we absolutely keep him long term.
