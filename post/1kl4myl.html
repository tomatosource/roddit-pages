##When was the last time each team selected 1st overall?

  • Utah never selected 1st overall as an NBA team, but selected Jim McDaniels 1st overall in the 1971 ABA draft.
  • Washington selected 1st overall four times, as the Chicago Packers and Zephyrs they took Walt Bellamy in 1961 and Bill McGill in 1962, then as the Wizards they took Kwame Brown in 2001 and John Wall in 2010. They also selected 1st overall
  • Charlotte selected 1st overall once, Larry Johnson in 1991.
  • New Orleans selected 1st overall twice, Anthony Davis in 2012 and Zion Williamson in 2019.
  • Philadelphia selected 1st overall four times, Doug Collins in 1973, Allen Iverson in 1996, Ben Simmons in 2016, and Markelle Fultz in 2017.
  • Brooklyn selected 1st overall twice, Derrick Coleman in 1990 and Kenyon Martin in 2000. As the New York Nets of the ABA they also selected Lew Alcindor 1st overall in 1969 and Bob Lanier in 1970.
  • Toronto selected 1st overall once, Andrea Bargnani in 2006.
  • San Antonio selected 1st overall three times, David Robinson in 1987, Tim Duncan in 1997, and Victor Wembanyama in 2023.
  • Phoenix selected 1st overall once, DeAndre Ayton in 2018.
  • Portland selected 1st overall four times, LaRue Martin in 1972, Bill Walton in 1974, Mychal Thompson in 1978, and Greg Oden in 2007.
  • Dallas selected 1st overall once, Mark Aguirre in 1981.
  • Chicago selected 1st overall twice, Elton Brand in 1999 and Derrick Rose in 2008.
  • Sacramento selected 1st overall five times, as the Rochester/Cincinnati Royals they took Sihugo Green in 1956, Rod Hundley in 1957, Bob Boozer in 1959, and Oscar Robertson in 1960, then as they Kings they took Pervis Ellison in 1989.
  • Atlanta selected 1st overall four times, as the Milwaukee Hawks they took Mark Workman in 1952 and Dick Ricketts in 1955, then as the Atlanta Hawks they took David Thompson in 1975 and Zaccharie Risacher in 2024.
