LeBron James Has More Playoff Series Wins (42) Than His Age (41) Again.

James reclaimed his lead over time by securing his 42nd series win at age 41 after eliminating the Houston Rockets. The last time he held this edge was in 2023 at age 38, before a two-year postseason lull allowed his age to briefly catch up. This milestone further separates him from Derek Fisher and Robert Horry as the only players to ever maintain more series victories than years lived into their 40s.
