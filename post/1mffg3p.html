Thunder winning the 76ers 117-115 with Paul George’s dagger three plus and-one on 19 Jan 2019, and a glimpse of how good the 76ers were back then (especially Ben Simmons)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIQI3o3BkOw

Coming across this highlight recap just now and watching it recalls a lot of memories about this 76ers team. It is a great up and coming team with Embiid, Simmons, Butler and Redick as the outside threat. Simmons was a beast back then in both ends, running the floor and is willing to go to the post and work while dictating the offensive pace of the Sixers.

Jimmy Butler is the same Jimmy we know, taking over down the stretch in the fourth with a big three, an and-one layup and a critical steal before foulling PG in the 3+1 attempt.

Meanwhile, Westbrook + PG was a fun duo to watch too with a great support from Schroder. What a fun time to be before Thunder going for a rebuild while the 76ers making the infamous “Tobias Harris over me” decision and the injuries of the two stars demolishing their future.
