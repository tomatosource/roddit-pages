Skills being interrupted is one of the biggest downsides of using combos

Unlike poe1 I decided when poe2 released to not make a science out of it and instead just decide what type of char I want to play and just go for it.

Wanted to play chronomancer, and I ended up using cold spells. Both my clear skill and my single target skill have cooldowns, which works better than I first expected.

My single target skill is eye of winter, which can be extremely strong if all the stars are aligned.

To get the maximum out of it I have to cast 3 other spells before (call me insane). Eye of winter itself has a default 10 second cooldown. So if I cast 3 spells before just to get interrupted while casting the final spell of the combo, the entire combo is useless and I have to wait for 10 seconds because the skill still goes on cooldown.

I believe it would be much better and more widely accepted if interrupted skills only go on cooldown for 25% - 50% of their CD duration. Another option would be to add passives or supports that prevent them from being interrupted, but I feel like having to invest into it would make if feel even worse playing.

No idea if GGG’s ever gonna read this but I just felt like sharing my thoughts about it.

PS. I am aware that you can invest into stun threshold, but even with around 10 points into it (and I really don’t want to use more for that) it still happens very frequently.
