Wishlist from a POE 1&2 noob

Just to preface, the POE series are basically my first ARPGs, I’ve played D3 but my playtime was so short that its negligible

Context: Recently picked up POE 1 after fate of the vaal fell off for me and I’ve been really enjoying it, after playing for a bit i’ve found areas of both games that I think could be taken from each other to create one cohesive and “good” product

Things from POE1 that I want in POE2 (not listed in any priority):

  1. More Tries on maps

SUCH a blessing to have 6 tries on maps in poe1, which is why poe2 should 100% have this same mechanic because bullshit deaths happen way more frequently in 2 than in 1

  1. Zero to no degen mobs in campaign

Playing through degen mobs in poe2 is so indescribably annoying because it takes incredibly long to set up any meaningful defenses that in campaign you just take deaths from degens like a dog and die

  1. Faster Acts

OH MY GOD PLEASE I literally blasted through like 3 acts in 4 hours in poe1 whereas act3 in poe2 alone usually takes me a day or two of gameplay

3.5. More power earlier

this is like a complimentary point to #3 because i think i wouldnt mind as much about the time it takes to do acts if i had a good build online faster,

in poe1 i was already blasting through acts with a barely finished build by the time i got my hands on my main skill cyclone

  1. Utility flasks

im not a fan of the charm system being substitute for utility flasks at all, utility flasks in poe1 have singlehandedly fixed my defenses and helped me significantly with my damage

  1. Better defenses in midgame

i died so rarely in acts 6-10 in and early maps in poe1 because i had easy access to decent defenses

Things I want from POE2 in POE1:

  1. checkpoints, especially for maps

i was seriously ungrateful for these in poe2 but when you die to some bs over and over in poe1 and you have to do those tedious runbacks you really start missing checkpoints

  1. the skill gem previews

just a nice qol for noobs

  1. WASD

wasd singlehandedly carries poe2 for me. makes it so much more pleasant to play over a long period of time than mouse clicking to move + precision of wasd movement allows for easier time dodging stuff

  1. enemy attack clarity

I find it incredibly difficult to see attacks in poe1 to the point that i frequently die to slams or nuke attacks from enemies whereas poe2 enemies articulate big hits much better

i might update this if i come up with anything else but this is it for now. feel free to give your own suggestions on what you think should be added
