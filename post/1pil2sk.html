Still no love for life and MOM?

This league I leveled 1 character with pure life + block, 3 with life + MOM and the rest 6 or so were mainly ES or CI, in HC abyss league.

  • The first 4 characters needed nearly double the investment to become half as good as my ES ones.
  • Each semi-juiced T15 felt like a gamble with those characters and their eHP, while the ES characters could do fully juiced maps with delirium and felt good.
  • ES characters felt like they had some defensive upgrades to choose from in the end game, while the life and MOM characters mostly felt like a dead end and extremely hard to scale after a certain point.
  • In the end, except two that died from the Arbiter and Simulacrum 3, most of the ES characters survived - the others died in maps.

This time around I don’t know if I’ll bother with life (and certainly not with MOM) - maybe with a blood mage, but that’s about it. Haven’t tried Veil + Kaoms yet but I hear it’s even more likely to get one-shot. The gap seems quite large - I am missing something or does pure life and life + MOM warrant some kind of boost?
