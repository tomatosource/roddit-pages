What are the Clippers doing?

On paper it seems fair, when you just look at the history or stats for each player.
But I truly don’t get this NBA fascination with old and “semi-washed” players.
Last year Clippers were one of the best defenses in the league, although, they still got the guys that defended last year, and they still have the coaching staff, but these very old limited players on the defensive end pulled in this year will weaken this, but for them, hopefully not much.
