[Dua] Multiple sources indicate that long time equipment manager, David Jovanovic, aka “Big Shot” has been let go by the Pelicans after 38 years

Jovanovic has served as the equipment manager since 1988 when the Charlotte expansion franchise was introduced. The move has completely shocked people.

Big Shot was universally beloved- by former players, coaches, current staffers, people who hadn’t been associated with the organization in years. After 38 years with the franchise. His dismissal has left people inside and outside the building in disbelief.
