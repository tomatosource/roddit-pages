In 2017 during Blake Griffin’s free agency meeting, Clippers employees wore shirts during the meeting with the faces of Blake Griffin, Barack Obama, JFK, and Martin Luther King Jr on them. He would proceed to be traded halfway into the first season of his five year deal.

As part of the elaborate pitch the Clippers organization used in luring Griffin back, they had these shirts made and wore them during the meeting to show how he was the face of the franchise. They also did a “mock retirement” of Blake’s number, with the PA announcer referring to Blake as a Clipper for life. He wound up being traded to the Detroit Pistons not even a full season later for Tobias Harris, Avery Bradley, and a first round pick.
