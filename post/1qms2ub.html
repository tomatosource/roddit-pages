Which MLB ballpark has the least enjoyable bullpen setup?

The worst is clearly Tropicana Field, the last remaining park without an enclosed bullpen. The second worst would clearly be Sutter Health Park, where there’s barely any shade in front of lawn seating.

Other than those two, the worst is a real discussion. I’d say Chase Field, where they’re basically as far from the action as they could reasonably be and they look pretty cramped.

The best in my opinion is Coors Field, which has both a nature reserve and the added bonus of being pretty isolated from hostile fans if you’re the road team.
