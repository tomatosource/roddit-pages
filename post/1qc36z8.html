Tiers in this game are really un-intuitive to me.

  • For mods on items, tier 1 is the best tier. The higher the tier goes, the worse it gets. Every individual mod also has an individual amount of different tiers. Some have tier 1-3, others tier 1-10, etc. There is zero ingame indicator about that range, btw. You could roll a tier 4 mod and have no way to know based on ingame information if that’s tier 4 out of 4, or tier 4 out of 13.

  • For unidentified item drops, tier 5 is the best tier. The higher the tier goes, the better it gets. (Also: Why 5? That’s so random). Identifying a tier 5 item can also roll bad tiers. I’ve heard there are some mods and tier ranges that are cut from high tier numbers, but yet again: zero ingame manual / explanation for concrete information.

  • For waystones, tier 1 is the lowest level zone. The higher the tier goes, the more difficult/reward/better it gets. Waystones can roll up to tier 16⁄17 (?).

  • For uncut gems, tier 1 is the lowest and 20 the highest.

  • For league bosses (Breach, Delirium, Ritual), tier 1 is the easiest and tier 3 is the most difficult version.

Isn’t there a more elegant way to design and unify tier numbers? Especially one that doesn’t, by design, makes it mandatory to use 3rd party sources like poe2db and craftofexile somewhere outside the game to get crucial information?
