Completionist bossing temple idea

https://preview.redd.it/4muros6gfqcg1.png?width=1029&format=png&auto=webp&s=649d7be940384880594947f6f580ae505e80bae7

(Click in for image) I was playing around with the planner, with the goal of a “completionist” temple. Normally the minmaxed juiced temples will block you off from ever running architect & atziri, and loot-wise they are not worth killing in comparison, but I wondered if I could get to experience everything, i.e. still run the bosses, while getting “ok” juice. A bonus would be sometimes having access to limb modification.

Here is the layout I ended at. There are 11 spymasters to hopefully sustain at least 2 locks for the main snakes. There are 3 golem works and commanders each squeezed in, which is right before diminishing returns starts, and 4 corruption chambers in the remaining space. The armouries are like the glue between the different chain types and are tricky to fit into such compact spaces without causing loops. The extra flesh surgeon and corruption chamber mini chains are just nice to have if possible, but I expect them to destabilise most of the time.

All that empty space is to avoid loops with the paths, and hopefully that one path tile leading to the architect destabilises each time, forcing him to always respawn in the only spot that leaves him unconnected. This is the part I’m most unsure about. Other than that, the space beside the paths could maybe be used to toss in random extra rooms or architect reward rooms without looping.

The layout would use some techs which I think can fit in the unfilled space as it’s being built. I reckon it will be harder to set up/run and give less loot than just doing even a no-tech temple, so it’s more for the experience/fun factor.

Looking to hear your thoughts, or for people to point out issues!
