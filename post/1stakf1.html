Are we watching the best version of Tatum right now?

I know this gets said a lot, but it really feels like Jayson Tatum has hit another level this season.

It’s not just the scoring. We’ve always known he can get 30 on any given night. It’s the decision-making, the patience, and how he’s controlling the game even when his shot isn’t falling. The playmaking jump has been huge, and you can see defenses scrambling because he’s making the right read almost every time.

What’s impressed me most is how he picks his spots now. Early in his career, it felt like he’d settle for tough shots too often. Now he’s dictating matchups, getting to his spots, and trusting his teammates when the double comes.

Also gotta give him credit defensively. When he’s locked in, he’s impacting both ends in a way that puts him in that true superstar tier.

Feels like we’re past the “can he be the guy?” stage. He is the guy.

Is this his peak, or do you think he still has another leap coming?
