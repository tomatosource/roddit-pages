PSA: Holding down Alt while unveiling Desecrate skips the animation & Ctrl + Alt on Ange pulls up your Shop

Have seen a lot of players and even streamers not know that you can hold down Alt to skip the Unveil animation for Desecrate mods.

You can also access your Trade Shop instantly by press Alt + Ctrl at the same time. This trick works for other vendors which have three options as well like Doryani.
