Celtics Prospects for Still Advancing in the Cup

Make no mistake–the Celtics earned the Cup predicament they are in by not holding the lead against the Hawks a few weeks ago. Everything is a consequence of that squandered opportunity, which if we had won would have made all of the following moot.

But the Celtics received next to no help at all from anyone today. The Hawks have won Group C, leaving the Celtics to attempt to secure the conference’s one wildcard spot. Wins by the Knicks, Pistons and Magic left us with very few remaining paths.

In order to advance, two things need to happen on Tuesday. One is easy to explain: The Magic “must” defeat the Knicks. There’s a path if the Knicks win by nearly 40 points, but that’s probably not realistic.

--

The second path involves the Bucks / Pistons game.

If MIL wins by 6+, the Celtics advance!

If MIL wins by 5 exactly, the Celtics probably advance! (They could be eliminated if both teams score over 140 points, but I won’t explore this scenario).

If MIL wins by 1-4 points, the Celtics are eliminated.

If DET wins by 7+ points, the Celtics advance!

If DET wins by 6 exactly, the Celtics probably advance! (They could be eliminated if both teams score over 140 points, but I won’t explore this scenario).

If DET wins by 1-5 points, the Celtics are eliminated.

--

So TLDR: ORL probably has to win (agt NYK), and either MIL or DET (who play each other) have to win by more than a few points.

--

So it just comes down to scoreboard watching two games, and it’s very possible that we can catch a couple of breaks. Hauser’s 3 may end up doing a lot of work for us.
