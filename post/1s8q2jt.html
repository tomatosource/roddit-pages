SGA is averaging 6.5 PPG on 67% TS in clutch time this season, that is the most clutch PPG ever averaged by a player since play-by-play tracking began in 1996.

Source: https://www.nba.com/stats/players/clutch-traditional

Note: The NBA defines “clutch time” as the last 5 minutes of a game within a 5-point margin.
