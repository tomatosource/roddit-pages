Web Tool] Campaign Step-by-Step Guide

So I saw a post by u/Vulcaiiin yesterday morning here: Guide

I had an idea to iterate on his work so I could make it easier for myself and my sister and her friends who are just starting to play POE2 this league. Something we could have up on the 2nd screen and quickly toggle through the steps without having to scroll and look too hard at what’s going on.

So I threw this together… it’s nothing fancy and has some weird quirks, but I had my sister use it last night for her test playthrough to act 3 and she said it worked great for her so I thought what the heck I’ll share it maybe someone will enjoy it.

Step by Step Campaign Walkthrough Guide:

https://beargrump.nerdcub.workers.dev

Update 12⁄11: Now Mobile Friendly, you need that 2nd monitor for Path of Building :) Should now work well with Mobile Browsers and Tablets.

https://preview.redd.it/e09yctzdkm6g1.png?width=1973&format=png&auto=webp&s=50c08b6dcd59317c59e726ff51d72a41bb69f7e3

It’s pretty self explanatory but as you complete a step click “COMPLETE” or “Space Bar” to jump to the Next step in the Act. It will give small notes/tips for the particular step that you are on. When you complete the Act just click the drop down on the top and pick a new Act.

Let me know if you see any issues, concerns, problems, bugs, or changes I should make/add to the NOTES section please!

I hope this helps someone, there have been so many tools/posts here that have helped me. Hope everyone has a great league start!

-BearGrump / EndofLine
