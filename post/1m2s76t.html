Summer League

Jordan Walsh

Probably our best player in Summer League. Affects both sides of the ball. Shooting mechanics has improved a lot. Can get to the rim but has trouble finishing.

Baylor Schierman

His role in SL is so far from his role in the team. Can’t creat his own shot. Is not our Hauser replacement. Can handle the ball and pass. Probably more cut out to be a combo guard than a SF.

Hugo Gonzales

Already great at rotating on defense. Does ok in help defense, and should improve with more game reps. Plays hard and has high bball IQ. Cuts hard and has a knack at getting to the rim. Needs to improve finishing around the rim. His release seems to be slow as he tends to get blocked a lot. He will get minutes this season and may jump Schierman in the rotation.

Amari Williams

Passing ability is obvious. Strong and can carve out space in the post. Would be a serviceable back up if he improves his footwork. Rushes his shots around the rim but also hustles for rebounds.

Max Shulga

For his age, I don’t see him as an NBA player. His shot is flat, even on free throws. Decision making seems to be slow. There are fast breaks that would have been easy buckets had he passed the ball 1 second early. But he is probably the best ball handler in the team

Kenneth Lofton Jr.

Skilled but too slow for a PF and too undersized for a Center. Everyone knows this by now. If he can develop a consistent 3-pt shot, he can be a poor man’s PJ Tucker.
