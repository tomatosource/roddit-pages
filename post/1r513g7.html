Might be time to make All-Star just one day

The arena has been empty so far over the course of Friday and Saturday. Clearly what’s being charged just isn’t worth it relative to the product you’re getting each night, so my solution would be to make it all one day. Celeb game and Rising Stars early in the day, 3-point contest & dunk contest in the afternoon, and then the All-Star Game in the evening. At least that way, when you charge a high ticket price, people get their money’s worth for the whole day and we don’t have to see flashing lights in the arena each night where people are not sitting. Will it ever happen? Probably not, but that’s my solution.
