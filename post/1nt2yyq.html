Dybantsa vs Boozer

Just a thought experiment but say we end up with the chance to draft either Dybantsa or Boozer, who do you think we’d end up picking? Dybantsa would obviously be a fan favorite as a Boston kid and could learn from the Jays to become an absolute two-way superstar. However, it’d likely take AJ a while to reach that level and that’s where I think Boozer becomes a better option. He’s basically young Al Horford with old Al’s shooting and would be a big boost, even as a rookie, to our weak front court. His ability to knockdown open threes and switch on the perimeter defensively is so valuable to a team trying to win now.

So which path would you think the Celtics would pursue? Personally, I lean heavily towards Cam. Maximizing the Jays window should be the main priority and Boozer seems like he’ll follow in the footsteps of the other recent Duke forwards (Tatum, Zion, Banchero, Flagg).
