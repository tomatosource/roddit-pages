My new theory is that Jaylen is frustrated with his contract situation. This dates back to the 2023 off-season.

Something I think a lot of us forgot about were the Jaylen contract discussions back in 2023. Jaylen was coming off the best season of his career until that point (and I still think it is). But the Miami series caused a bunch of public discourse, and some internal discussions within the front office.

The team and Jaylen had preliminary talks on the extension and then paused for a period. Jaylen did go overseas, but the problem was the player option.

The team did not give a guy who was 26 and coming off a 2nd team all nba season a fifth year player option. Guys usually decline it, but it is a big safety blanket. And the team wouldn’t give it up.

Contrast that to Tatum. I will never concede that Tatum shouldn’t have won the 2024 ECF MVP. It was a miscarriage of justice on par with the OJ trial. But Jaylen was a deserving FMVP, put Luka on an island, and made the shots that kind of ended the series in game 3.

But Tatum got the 5th year option and the photo shoot the instant he could sign. No questions asked. I’m sure that stung Jaylen. I think that’s human nature.

I also think the team has just continued to show they believe in a hobbled Tatum more than they do Jaylen. I read this stat today:

Jaylen and Tatum played 6 playoff games together this year. Tatum played more minutes in all 6 games.

The guy coming off a blown Achilles is getting more minutes than your first team all nba wing? This is an aside but this drives me nuts. You can argue about who YOU think is more important. The team has shown us and by extension Jaylen for a decade who they think is more important.

Now we head into another off-season with Jaylen eligible for an extension. He’s coming off an all nba season. 29 PPG 7 boards. He’s got a finals mvp, an ECFMVP, and he’s a home grown superstar. It’s early, but we still don’t have any idea if the Celtics are going to offer the extension. Plus he’s in trade talks.

No matter how much you spin it, that’s gotta be annoying. I’m sure that’s where the Tmac comments stem from. And if that’s the case, I don’t blame Jaylen for being frustrated. It’s gotta be challenging to have your work constantly questioned by your employer when most outside observers think you’re elite at what you do. While the team still has some questions.
