Reid is unlikely to be the only marquee free agent returning to Minnesota.

There is a growing belief that Julius Randle will be next in striking a long-term agreement to sign with the Timberwolves as well.

Those highly anticipated returns of Reid and Randle, however, have increasingly painted Alexander-Walker as the odd free agent out in Minnesota.

The Clippers, Hawks and Magic have all continued to be mentioned as possible destinations for the scrappy 3-and-D guard. Saturday’s emergence of Detroit as a potential bidder for Alexander-Walker follows our publisher Marc Stein’s reporting all week about Sacramento’s serious interest in signing Schröder away from the Pistons.

The Pistons, to be clear, are indeed expected to come to terms with Malik Beasley on a new multiyear agreement that keeps the sharpshooter in Detroit.
