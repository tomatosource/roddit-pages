Making the Early Acts a slog

I really don’t understand this. I was excited to play after all of the announced changes, even running LA deadeye. I just don’t understand the design choices behind making the early game acts such a slog. In PoE 1 I already felt decent on the Beach, but even halfway through Act 1 in PoE2 I legitimately felt weaker levelling up.

I’ve put thousands of hours into PoE 1 and several hundred in PoE2, but I’m not going to bother doing the “Hey you MIGHT enjoy the endgame changes after you waste several hours going through a slog of a campaign with white mobs that take several attacks to clear”.

I understand the fact they want the games to feel different, but being slow and weak isnt a good feeling, especially if you’re trying to sell me on playing a game long term.
