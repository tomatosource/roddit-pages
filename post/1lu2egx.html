Dying with lvl 99 in HC SSF in a hillarious way

Hey guys

I just died with my lvl 99 ritualist in hc ssf in pretty much the stupidiest way and wanted it to share it with you. My gear was absolutely cracked and I felt pretty much invincible at this point. I had more than 16k ehp, maxed resis, decent damage and more.

This was my gear: https://maxroll.gg/poe2/planner/6xbkg03y

I had a chill run in vaul foundry, rushed through the map and killed the last rare pack before entering the boss arena. Unfortunately I didnt notice that the last rare pack had the shroud walker modifier on it (I was wearing headhunter belt). Usually I immediately unequip and re equip my headhunter when I get shroud walker modifer because I imagined this being the only way I could die. There wasnt a single mob anymore between me and the boss so I did not get teleported before entering the boss arena. When the bossfight started, I got instantly teleported to the boss which stood in the lava. I can now confirm the lava will oneshot you always in a milisecond no matter how much hp you have.

I am pretty sad since I really wanted to get to 100 this season. I am especially sad about my rite of passage golden charm and my adorned diamond with 86% increase. I have an insane amount of gear in my stash and its no problem to gear a new character but I am pretty sure I wont see this two items ever again,

It was an amazing run with a shocking end I did not expect but thats the life of hardcore! Hope you had an entertaining read and stay safe! :)
