The worst part is that this NBA All-Star format COULD HAVE BEEN AMAZING.

The format and concept were actually great. The players were actually playing to win (at first). It would have worked exceptionally well if not for the absolutely atrocious over-producing of this event.

The Good:

  • Single elimination AND short games actually does create the environment where players DO want to play (relatively) hard and try to win
  • Awesome opportunity for the rising stars to get some shine
  • Even a chance for the G-leaguers to get some shine
  • The players actually WERE treating the game more seriously, until the ridiculous interruptions began
  • The musical performances and intros were actually dope
  • Reggie Miller was solid.

The Bad

  • Draymond, omfg. STFU crying about and trashing the format the entire time. NEVER, and I mean NEVER should Draymond serve as an analyst at NBA events. OF COURSE something needed to change, the All-Star game has been absolute trash for the last decade+
  • Kevin Hart is obviously awful, obnoxious, and should also never be involved in an NBA event again
  • Kenny Smith is so bad (no one cares about your ITS OVER call man, we really don’t)
  • LET THE PLAYERS PLAY. Stop trying to distract from the actual product, the greatest players in the world competing in the game we all love
  • The HUGE interruption to honor the TNT crew in the MIDDLE OF A SHORT GAME???? Let the players play the game, and do that extra dumb shit afterwards in the studio if you must
  • NEVER, EVER let “Mr. Beast” on an NBA court again. This dude yelling in the kids ear while he was trying to shoot. Props to Dame for trying to get the idiot out of the way.
  • NEVER let commentators/comedians voices come through the PA system. Again, it DISTRACTS from the product

FOCUS ON THE PLAYERS. FOCUS ON THE QUALITY OF THESE AMAZING ATHLETES. FOCUS ON THE IN-GAME ACTION. STOP TALKING ABOUT RANDOM UNRELATED SHIT WHILE THE GAME IS GOING ON. BRING IN PEOPLE WHO CARE ABOUT THE CRAFTSMANSHIP OF THE GAME.

This truly had the potential to be a FANTASTIC format; the over-production of this event outright destroyed it. Perhaps to the point it will never be able to recover.
