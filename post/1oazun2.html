How to stream all of your team’s games for the 2025-26 season (National and Local, including how to avoid blackouts)

Some people are still confused about where to stream the games this season with the new TV deal. Consider this a one stop shop for how to legally stream all of the games starting in 2025-26 (yes all of your pirate and highlights jokes are very original and incredibly hilarious, but this is for people who just genuinely want their question answered).

This post will be ignoring any sort of regional TV provider deals and is specifically aimed at just pointing out what streaming services you will need; handle accordingly for your region if your cable/tv provider gives access to your local team’s games:

National TV Games

To watch national tv games this year, they will be on the following services:

  • Peacock (Mondays) - All Peacock exclusive games can be viewed via a Peacock subscription
    • Peacock will also air the second game of (Tuesday) double headers
  • NBC (Tuesdays) - All NBC games (alongside Peacock exclusive games) can be viewed via a Peacock subscription
    • NBC will also air (Sunday) night games after the NFL season concludes
  • ESPN (Wednesdays) - All ABC and ESPN games can be accessed via the ESPN Unlimited subscription tier of their new streaming service. The ESPN Select tier (formerly known as ESPN+) will not give that access
    • ABC will air Christmas Day games (simulcasted on ESPN), and ABC games will air on (Saturdays) after the College Football season and (Sundays) after the NFL season.
  • Prime Video (Fridays) - All Prime Video games can be accessed via an Amazon Prime subscription
    • Prime Video will also have games on (Saturdays) following the conclusion of the College Football season and (Thursdays) following the conclusion of the NFL regular season

Out of Market Local Broadcasts

As always, the majority of NBA games for out of market NBA teams will all be accessed via NBA LeaguePass subscription. Local blackouts will still apply for these games if you are in the region of a team, these can be checked via your zip code on the LeaguePass subscription page.

  • Beginning this season, Prime Video will be the exclusive way to subscribe to LeaguePass via another paid TV service (no Youtube TV or other alternatives). All LeaguePass subscribers are able to link their LeaguePass directly to Prime Video without needing to buy your subscription through that service. This can put all of those LeaguePass games on Prime without having to switch back and forth
  • LeaguePass subscription tiers:
    • LeaguePass - All games with commercials and broadcasts from both teams’ perspectives
    • LeaguePass Premium - All games with direct feed of the arena jumbotron (no commercials), as well as both teams’ perspectives
    • Team Pass - If you only want to follow one team, you can buy the Team Pass (with commercials); Regional Blackouts still apply (do not buy if you are in a blackout area for the team)
    • Student tier - a student discount for LeaguePass if you use a university email
  • Update (10/13/25) - NBA TV will also be airing 60 LeaguePass games throughout the year, though these should not be exclusive to NBA TV and should still be available in market on LeaguePass

In Market Local Broadcasts

If you live in market of an NBA team, you will need to use the Regional Sports Network’s method that has the rights to your local team in order to view games inaccessible with LeaguePass. I’ve grouped them below into different categories by team (as many share the same provider):

  • FanDuel Sports Network - Many teams throughout the league all fall under FanDuel Sports Network (formerly Bally Sports, formerly Fox Sports). To watch these games in your local network, you will need to purchase a FanDuel Sports Network subscription. You can either subscribe to FanDuel Sports Network directly or through Prime Video (which is home to many of the national broadcasts this year). You can check your zip code and reach all links to ways to watch here. Availability for these subscriptions will be based on your zip code, and the teams under FanDuel Sports Network are as follows:
    • Atlanta Hawks - FanDuel Sports Network Southeast
    • Charlotte Hornets - FanDuel Sports Network Southeast
    • Cleveland Cavaliers - FanDuel Sports Network Ohio
    • Detroit Pistons - FanDuel Sports Network Detroit
    • Indiana Pacers - FanDuel Sports Network Indiana
    • Los Angeles Clippers - Unfortunately for Clippers fans, ClipperVision is not being renewed this season. You will instead need to purchase FanDuel Sports Network SoCal to watch your local games.
    • Memphis Grizzlies - FanDuel Sports Network Southeast
    • Miami Heat - FanDuel Sports Network Sun
    • Milwaukee Bucks - FanDuel Sports Network Wisconsin
    • Minnesota Timberwolves - FanDuel Sports Network North
    • Oklahoma City Thunder - FanDuel Sports Network Oklahoma
    • Orlando Magic - FanDuel Sports Network Florida
    • San Antonio Spurs - FanDuel Sports Network Southwest
  • NBC Sports Regional Networks - A few teams have their local games broadcast directly through Peacock/NBC, which can be added in as an add on to your Peacock subscription if you live in market based on zip code.
    • Boston Celtics - NBC Sports Boston
    • Golden State Warriors - NBC Sports Bay Area
    • Philadelphia 76ers - NBC Sports Philadelphia
    • Sacramento Kings - NBC Sports California
  • NBA LeaguePass Add Ons - For the following teams, an offer of local games is available directly through LeaguePass for your local games:
    • Dallas Mavericks
      • MavsTV - You can purchase a MavsTV subscription to watch local in market Mavs games, which you can link to your LeaguePass ID to receive all local games.
    • Los Angeles Lakers
      • Spectrum Sportsnet+ - You can purchase a Spectrum SportsNet+ subscription to receive Lakers games if you live within market, which can be linked to your LeaguePass subscription.
    • New Orleans Pelicans
      • Pelicans+ - You can buy a Pelicans+ subscription to watch local Pelicans games, which you can link to your LeaguePass subscription.
    • Phoenix Suns
      • Suns+ - Suns fans living local in Phoenix can subscribe to Suns+ to watch their local games, which they can link to their LeaguePass subscription.
    • Portland Trail Blazers
      • BlazerVision - Fans in the Portland area can subscribe to BlazerVision for their local broadcasts and link their subscription to LeaguePass to receive all local games in and out of market
    • Utah Jazz
      • Jazz+ - Jazz fans, you will find your local games through the Jazz+ service if you live within market (Utah, southeastern Idaho, and western Wyoming). You can link your Jazz+ subscription to LeaguePass to receive all local games through the NBA app.
  • Individual Regional Sports Networks - The following teams have individual local RSNs that are not linked to any of the previous bulk groups
    • Brooklyn Nets - You will need the Yes Network or bulk subscription with MSG Networks via the Gotham Sports App
    • Chicago Bulls - Bulls fans will need CHSN to stream their local games, which they can get through CHSN directly
    • Denver Nuggets - You will need an Altitude+ subscription to receive local Nuggets games if you live in market.
    • Houston Rockets - Unfortunately there is no streaming service available through the Space City Home Network for Rockets games. You will need DirecTV, Fubo, or any of the other various local cable services to receive local games.
    • New York Knicks - You will need the MSG Networks or bulk subscription with Yes Network via the Gotham Sports App
    • Toronto Raptors - Raptors fans, your schedule is technically split into two sets of national games. For 41, you will need Sportsnet+. For the other 41, you will need TSN. This obviously covers the entirety of your season separately from every other fanbase, so follow accordingly (both of these appear to be available as Prime Video channels as well, if you would like to link LeaguePass all through one place)
    • Washington Wizards - Wizards fans will need the Monumental Sports Network for local games

Final Round Up

Of course there are other methods out there to watch the games as stated before, but for people who just want the straightforward answer hopefully this will make it easier. Any fans of these teams, feel free to correct anything that I may have missed. Local fans of these teams would know the setup of how to watch games better than what I could Google. It is also possible that having a cable service (Youtube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo, etc.) gives you access to these services without needing a subscription, as well as the teams who have in recent years offered their games over the air like the Phoenix Suns. This is not meant to be all encompassing, but just a simplified version of how each team can get all of their games to stream online without a cable subscription (except for Houston fans, sorry).
