Catalyst Rerolling

I don’t know if this was brought up, but if you are like me and struggle to craft anything decent for div, try Catalyst rerolling. Find one of 6 (usually) catalyst that are under 15 exalt (flesh, adaptive, xoph, uul-netol, chayula, carapace), reroll 3 into the reforging bench. 1 neural catalyst pays for 10 rerolls. 15x3=45. 1 neural = 470 exalt. 2 neural and you’ve doubled your investment. If you have like 500 exalt to start, thats the best. Repeat until you have the div you want or run out of gold. In, maybe a day worth of playing, I’ve made like 200 div. Just look out cause cheap catalysts do fluctuate depending on the time of day. Less that 15 exalt is where you wanna be.
