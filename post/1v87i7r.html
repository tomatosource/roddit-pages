Just found another one of the rare 10-rune Expedition rewards.

So far I already hit:

  • Uhtred’s Boon: +10 Spirit
  • +1 Passive Skill Point from reroll

Now I have the option between:

  • 1x Aldur’s Legacy
  • 1x Medved’s Boon
  • 1x Vorana’s Boon

What would you guys take here?

Would you go for Aldur’s Legacy for the chance at a big legacy item, or keep taking Medved/Vorana boons?

Update: I ended up choosing Aldur’s Legacy and used it for the immunity to slows boot augment.

Honestly, it was definitely worth it for the quality of life. Being melee and constantly Shattering Palm-ing into crowds, not having to worry about slows is a huge benefit lol. The +Spirit and +passive point boons were obviously great, but this slow immunity feels like one of those upgrades you don’t fully appreciate until you play without it.
