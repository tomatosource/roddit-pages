Why Not Combine ED and Contagion?

Does anyone genuinely enjoy casting contagion the way it’s done in poe2?

At least in poe1 you could scale its AOE to a comfortable area, while in poe2 you have to support it with unleash to make it almost bearable to use and a lot of the instances in endgame it doesn’t work as mobs swarm you and ED doesn’t connect to one of the contagion-marked ones. Also, you need to spend a whole support slot of ED on chain support which is god awful.

Given the way its played it requires you to focus on cast speed to get a smooth four-spell click rotation, when cast speed shouldn’t ever be a focus on a DOT build.

Why won’t GGG just give ED the contagion mechanism? As a chaos witch you already have to cast despair and cast dark effigy (twice if you use overabundance) constantly. At some point in mapping I just give up on ED/contagion and just cast despair/dark effigy which is enough to clear the whole screen assuming the effigy isn’t insta-destroyed with how fragile it is.

It won’t make or break the character, all it’ll do is take a spell off a very long rotation and actually make it smooth to play (Despair&gt;ED&gt;Effigy) rather than a 4-spell rotation that’s extremely clunky. This is compared to meta builds that have 1 spell/button faceroll experience.

End of rant
