Parrying was a blast in campaign but it’s kind of useless in endgame outside of bossing

Title. Souls/Elden Ring Parry Veteran/enthusiast here who loves the mechanic and had a pretty good time with it even pre-hotfix at Dawn of the Hunt launch. I know pretty much which attacks on most monsters at this point are parry-able and where I need to be (which a lot of the time is right under them.) It made bosses way more fun for me because learning attack patterns becomes more meaningful than just knowing when to dodge. On top of being able to stun them, you also get a ton of buffs on skills by consuming a parry charge, and disengage just absolutely DELETES health.

My problem is that this particular style of play just breaks into a million pieces once you hit endgame. It’s not worth the 1-2 secs I have to stay still (because that’s how long most monster attacks take) because the risk of me dying from something I am BODYBLOCKED from being able to effectively parry is just too much. On most slam attacks you need to be literally touching before it lands, and if there’s another monster of the same kind behind it (as there often is in endgame packs) their slam will proc no matter what I do. It doesn’t matter if I parry + disengage the faster mobs in front of me because something, somewhere can still spam AOE. No amount of armor/ES can salvage this, and I’ve tried.

On EVERY crypt/tomb map that I’ve had in endgame on any tier so far is an absolute nightmare unless I just spam glacial lance and other projectiles that penetrate, because my build is based around alternating melee/projectile and using parry mechanics. If I see there are certain kinds of packs that can AOE me from offscreen I don’t even bother parrying.

It feels like no matter what you’re funneled into a build that can kill shit offscreen, but honestly I find spamming the same +10% projectile skills into elemental sundering/primal strikes to be insanely dull. Kind of disappointed that this is the most pain-free way of map-clearing in endgame that I’ve found so far. At least Last Epoch updates today, because idk how much longer I can slog through like this.
