Bennedict Mathurin extension?

Figured this was worth bringing up coming off of his hot 27-point game off the bench.

He’s extension eligible this summer and honestly I have no idea how we handle his extension because I figure he wants to be a full-time starter moving forward.

I don’t have the exact numbers, but the starting lineup of Hali/Nemby/Mathurin/Siakam/Turner had a really good net rating this season. But, the one lineup that had a better one was with Nesmith in place of Mathurin.

To me, drafting Mathurin was a bit of an interesting choice by the front office. He was drafted at #6 overall in 2022 (fun fact - that was our highest draft pick in over 30 years and the first time we had a pick inside the top 10 in over 30 years). There’s a good chance they picked BPA and ran with it.

That being said, it’s clear that his style of bucket-getting is a stark contrast to the team ball that the Pacers are currently known for. On the flip side though, Mathurin’s one of the only guys on our roster that we ask to go get a bucket/free throw consistently.

He’s had some inconsistent time in the playoffs but he has shown up when called upon and he’s still only 22. I’m just wondering out loud how his trajectory would’ve been if he had been drafted to a team like Detroit where he would’ve had the green light immediately to shoot the ball as he pleased.

Anyways, I’ve heard the ~20M range is the going rate for a premium sixth man in today’s rising cap. That number is a tough stomach for me personally but I get it.

All of the trades I’ve been running thru my head are kind of mediocre anyways. Mathurin + Toppin gets us in the 25M range. That’s Trey Murphy money, but I’ve always figured that we wouldn’t want to give up first rounders for Trey. That being said, we’re in the fucking Finals so who knows.

What do you think?
