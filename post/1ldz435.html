PoE 2 endgame should NOT be infinite. Thoughts?

Just wanted to share a take. The current endgame in PoE 2 gives me the same vibe as when I finish the Atlas in PoE 1 - and that vibe usually kills my motivation to keep going. It feels like I’ve already “finished” the game, and that sense of progression just disappears.

Playing PoE 1 again lately really reminded me how much I dislike the idea of an infinite map system. I miss the feeling of pushing through the Atlas, of having a clear goal to work toward. That structure gave me a reason to log back in every night.

Endless content might sound good on paper, but for me, it makes the game feel aimless way too soon.
