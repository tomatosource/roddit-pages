Crafting Process:

  1. Frac a max roll target mod, in this case Cold Res

  2. Chaos spam for 2nd res, then Dextral Perf Exalt for 3rd res, raw annul and repeat until trip t1

  3. Perf exalt and rotate whittle, abyss essence, and swearing until you have 1 target mod and a 2nd that’s not entirely useless for the build (Armour in this case)

  4. Desecrate for final target mod

  5. Div slam til you’re happy with it

  6. Vaal or no Baals
