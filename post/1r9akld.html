Is it crazy to say that Amen Thompson shouldn’t get a $252M Max extension contract?

So Amen Thompson is eligible to sign a max extension for 5 years/ $252 million this summer. I’m just thinking out loud here but is it crazy to say that Amen shouldn’t get that kind of contract?

I feel like Amen being projected as a superstar has kind of faded. Don’t get me wrong he’s still a great player but in a era where 3s are valued extensively, especially for player growth, he’s shooting 20.5% from 3 on 1.7 attempts…

His past years aren’t great either, last year was 27% on 1.3 attempts & his rookie year was 14% on barely 1 attempt.

His FG attempts are up this year but the percentage is the worst with 50% and while he’s averaging 17/5/7 this year I just don’t think it’d be fiscally responsible especially in this cap environment for the Rockets to give him a max level contract. Unless you really project him to become a Giannis level guy that is but I’m willing to hedge against that
