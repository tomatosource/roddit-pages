Being punished for trying to focus activities such as the temples in endgame punishes the player for seemingly no reason

Hey everyone. I’ve been following the sub for a few days and have yet to see many people bring this up. This isn’t just another criticism of the league mechanic but more a criticism of how the endgame operates fundamentally after seeing the implementation of temples. There is definitely a chance this is fixed or honed in on in 0.5, but just in case it isn’t I’m making this.

I am someone who started with Path of Exile 2 and worked my way back to the original, which definitely seems like the minority in the community (In fact Path of Exile 2 is my first ever ARPG and I’ve racked up a solid 500 hours over all patches), and one thing I absolutely love about PoE1 is the fact that if I like a league mechanic I can just focus that and do it over and over and over. PoE2 both encourages against this by punishing the player for not focusing on multiple mechanics, but also the devs don’t seem to fundamentally grasp this concept, especially with this latest league and arguably with abyss too.

The new league mechanic in my opinion is solid once you really get into it. It’s a bit buggy, a bit unintuitive but surprisingly I managed to grapple onto it relatively quick and nailed down how it works, and the bugs I can mostly avoid. The issue is I can’t.. do the league mechanic lol. I am essentially forced to run 5 regular maps every single time I want to run one temple which feels entirely unintuitive and it just makes me not want to do the mechanic at all, which sucks because I actually do like the mechanic. Now this critique I have actually seen from people like DM and on this Subreddit + the forums, but my main complaint here is core philosophy. Based on the two league mechanics so far as well as the changes made mid league in 0.3 I’m not really sold on the idea that the devs understand what’s wrong, or that if they do they plan on changing things. Why can’t I just prioritize breach or deli or ritual 100% and make as much currency or close to as much as someone who prioritizes abyss or temples or expedition? Why can’t I just run temples over and over?

Path of Exile 1 (based on my limited experimentation with mapping over there) seemed to grasp this quite well, granted it has been out for.. substantially longer, but I’m not sure why this philosophy was dumped almost entirely for Path of Exile 2 in favor of “run every mechanic” and I hope that changes. Now my point isn’t “just copy Path of Exile 1’s endgame”, I do like that Path of Exile 2 is different, but I’m genuinely astounded at the lack of one of the philosophies that makes mapping in Path of Exile 1 so damn rewarding and so fun.
