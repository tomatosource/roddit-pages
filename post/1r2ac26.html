Some fine boots I crafted

https://preview.redd.it/oo3etc42nxig1.png?width=880&format=png&auto=webp&s=01c20a445ba0d7331ac0c4d87426cdea59d69326

  1. Get a pair of 2 soc, iLvl82 Sekhema Sandals

  2. Perf aug/annul until you get 2 ideal affixes (e.g. energy shield/35%MS/T1 res)

  3. Perf regal and pray to get a good affix

  4. Divine up, desecrate, and fracture -&gt; got T1 max ES fractured

  5. At this point, perf exalt and pray for a good affix, which I didn’t, so I had to annul down to affix

  6. Chaos spam until you get 1 good affix, then perf exalt +/- omen of greater exaltation until you get 4 desirable affixes. This took probably 1000+ chaos and 200+ greater exalt.

  7. From step 6 i got T1 increased ES, T1 MS, T1 lite res, T1 cold res. Then use omen of dex crystallisation and use essence of horror to get 60% increased effect of socketed items, which removed T1 cold res.

  8. Finish the last suffix with desecration. I settled with cold/chaos res

  9. Muster up courage to vaal it, and got 3rd soc.
