Gathering Storm is an enormously fun skill and I’m sad I can’t socket three copies anymore

(Allow me to ramble about my favourite skill.)

Gathering Storm does everything all at once.

Single Target

lvl 20 Hammer of the Gods: 1529% damage
lvl 20 Gathering Storm (perfect dash + shockwave): 1014% + 435% = 1449% damage

94% of HotG’s damage ratio without having to build glory is eyebrow-raising. It’s split across a couple hits, so not as great for ailments, but you can use gathering storm back-to-back-to-back.

(I was surprised to find qs and 2h maces do actually have similar weapon damage at the same trade price. No giant’s blood of course.)

And there’s more:

  • The built-in shock from shocked ground
  • Loads of high-damage supports (momentum, window of opportunity, concentrated effect, ambrosia)
  • Like other slow skills, benefits a lot from warcries and other empowers
  • Built-in phys to ele
  • Like other skills with flat added attack time, it scales fast with gem level (e.g. at level 30, HoTG is 2119% while GS totals 2761%)

Clear

It’s got AoE for days. Both the dash and the shockwaves scale with AoE mods. The dash AoE is incredibly generous, hitting several meters ahead of where your character stops and a couple meters our to either side, and even a bit behind where you start.

Heralds and Armour Explosion can proc off the shockwaves, reaching further out.

And it’s inherently a travel skill; you don’t have to sit still, or even really slow down on average, to attack. You can cancel the recovery animation using movement input (though I haven’t pinned down what determines which direction causes that cancel).

QoL

Gathering Storm is by far the easiest Perfect Timing skill to use. The animation and audio cue are very clear, you move during the windup which makes it far easier to not get hit, and the window is quite generous. In 0.2 I’d run it with Window of Opportunity and essentially never miss the timing unless I got unexpectedly slowed.

Flat added attack time means it doesn’t scale much with attack speed, but that’s not really a downside. You can take the %increased damage, %reduced attack speed notables with abandon, and you’ve still got plenty of scaling axes to invest in given quarterstaves’ high base crit. It also makes itemisation easier (one less weapon suffix you need). And even at gem level 31 I was never even close to having mana issues.

It enables ailment mechanics easily, with its enormous single hits and shocked ground. Herald of Thunder and Innervate have basically perfect uptime. You can convert the lightning damage or change the ailment it applies without losing your shock, and you can consume the shocks from shocked ground every second or so.

There’s some perfect-timing-exclusive supports:

  • Old: Perfection (stacking global more damage), Window of Opportunity
  • New: WoO grants a 15% ev to deflection buff, ‘Perfect Endurance’ (endurance charge on perfect timing)

And it just feels nice. It’s crunchy and explosive. You can control the direction of both dashes independently, re-aiming in between (to e.g. do the wind-up and main dash in the same direction). It instantly one-shots rares (especially ones surrounded by minions) almost as soon as you get the skill.

Let me socket three copies :(

In 0.2, I ended up with:

  • Gathering Storm with damage supports for single target
  • Gathering Storm with AoE supports for clear (also Stomping Ground proccing Herald of Thunder is a neat way to light stun enemies while you’re winding up)
  • Gathering Storm with shock effect, exposure, and duration for boss setup
  • No other active skills

Won’t be able to do that anymore in 0.3.

(I’ll still be starting with a qs titan, though. See if avatar of fire + the new warcry stuff goes anywhere. Maybe Veil of the Night and armour to ele if I want to suffer…)
