Introducing the Atlas of the Azmerian sea

In the Atlas of the Azmerian sea, maps have completely been reworked. Waystone no longer open portals to one instance of a map, instead it will open portals to an instance of a dangerous island filled with multiple instance of different zones all connected to eachother. Every mechanics can spawn in every zone, but zone will sometime come with modifier for mechanics. In this island, you must hunt the dangerous island seer that is located at the top of the tower, but to access it, you have to kill the bosses holding the keys that are located around the island. Slaying the island seer will allow you to peer into the telescope and choose your next destination, dropping a map of a connected island in the process. All the islands are themed differently and completing an island will grant you atlas point.

Since this is a bit different from the usual map system, there is a few key element to note:

  • Number of revive per instance has been increased to 10 per island dropping to 3 in higher island

  • The number of zone in the island can from one map type to another or by rarity

  • As long as the island is still active, you can still come back to previous area in the island

I had fun thinking about it and just wanted to share. I don’t think it’s perfect, I just made this for fun. Hope you enjoyed.
