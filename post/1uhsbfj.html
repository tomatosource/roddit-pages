Spectre spirit cost feel random

So, I just hit lvl 96 on my Minion Master and completed most Atlas trees. Overall I really like GGG’s take on minion masters, which are more interactive than I first expected due to command skills.

Now, there however is one thing that irritates the hell out of me: Spectre Spirit costs.

You have a slow spectre that can barely reach a target? 90+ spirit cost.

You have trashmobs that the game throws at you in groups of 5? 55 Spirit cost for one of them, same as for the bigger, faster and far more powerful unit for the same cost.

Looking at PoE2 Ninja most players tend to converge towards the same 2 or 3 types of spectres. I’ve tried them - and I can see why. Spectre costs are vastly unbalanced and especially “trash mobs” are severely overpriced given their performance.

Honestly? The idea to be able to have any enemy ingame as a minion is so great and has so much potential (especially if more enemies are introduced that have buff effects, etc.), I know there are higher priorities, but damn - it’s really a shame that spectre costs are so random and unfitting. That being said, more options got get +movement speed on minions would already make (non-wasp) melee spectres somewhat more viable.
