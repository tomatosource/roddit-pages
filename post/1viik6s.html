SOME NOTES:

Lich: I was aiming to eventually get an Atziri’s Communion for CoC setup but ended up spending the divs on alts and helping friends. Could automate Thrashing Vines with CoC and change the spirit investment but manual casting feels fine. CoC Thunderstorm is very comfy but hard to get consistent high magnitude shocks without self casting with a dedicated lightning staff weapon swap like this build has. Great for mapping/mobbing but lowers the damage ceiling a good amount.

MA: The Trinity amulet was from the previous MA build I played aka Trinity Whirling Assault but works fine for this setup. Able to mostly balance the damage for Trinity between flat elemental damage on gear and some gain as extra damage. The gloves are a bit lackluster in terms of what you can get on Fists of Stone but they hit all the big ones IMO, very serviceable. Flicker Strike got me killed more than anything but it is fun.

Gemling: While I love this build, the whole attack speed stacking interaction to get instant perfect timed Perfect Strikes makes it weird. If basically anything lowers your attack speed, Perfect Strike no longer works, and same goes for anything that increases attack speed too much like speed shrine or Headhunter speed buff. Attack speed should be right around .5 - .4 to keep the Perfect Strike interaction working. An annoying balancing act and some mobs like Expedition mosquitos with temp bubble or hinder on hit are actual Satan. Very fun and ez 1 button build otherwise but be aware it is easy to brick.
