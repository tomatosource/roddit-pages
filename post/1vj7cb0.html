Predictions for National Opening Week games and the Christmas Games

This is what I think the NBA National games will be for opening week and the Christmas Games

Tuesday October 20th (opening night on NBC)
7:00 76ers vs Knicks (Knicks get their championship rings and Lebron and Jaylen Brown’s debut in 76ers uniforms)
9:30 Spurs vs Thunder (two best teams in the West and a rematch of the 7 game WCF series)

Wednesday October 21st (ESPN)
7:00 Raptors vs Heat (Kawhi’s first game back in a Raptors jersey and Giannis’s debut in a Heat jersey)
9:30 Lakers vs Mavs (Luka goes up against his former squad to open the season and Cooper Flagg’s sophomore season)

Thursday October 22nd (ESPN)
7:30 Nuggets vs Timberwolves (Jokic vs ANT, Lamelo’s debut in a Timberwolves jersey, rematch of the 6 game opening round series that resulted in the Wolves upsetting the Nuggets)
10:00 Rockets vs Warriors (KD vs Steph, Durant once again returns to the Bay to take on the team he won two titles with)

Friday October 23rd (Prime)
7:30 Celtics vs Knicks (rivalry matchup)
10:00 Spurs vs Lakers (Wemby vs Luka in a matchup of two of the best players in the NBA

Christmas Games (all on ABC)
12:00 Timberwolves vs Heat (ANT-MAN and Lamelo vs Giannis)
2:30 Spurs vs Knicks (Finals Rematch)
5:00 76ers vs Celtics (New chapter in this rivalry as Jaylen Brown returns to Boston to take on his former team and Lebron’s first game in this rivalry)
8:00 Warriors vs Lakers (Steph vs Luka)
10:30 Thunder vs Nuggets (SGA vs Jokic, battle of the last two MVP winners)
