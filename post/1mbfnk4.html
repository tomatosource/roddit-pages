How good was Charles Barkley’s defense? Was he a better defender than Larry Bird?

Recently, i stumbled across this video, where Chuck says he is a better rebounder and, probably, defender than Bird. Is it true? How good was Chuck as a defender?

I never had the opportunity to watch them play live, as i was born later than their careers and it’s very difficult to evaluate defense through highlights, as they only show the good parts and can be missleading.

In the video, Chuck says he is a better rebounder and it’s obviously true. He is one of the best rebounder of all times, despite his shorter height.

  • 19th for TRB and RPG in regular season
  • 15th for TRB in the playoffs
  • 5th for RPG in the playoff

He averaged 11.7 RPG and 7.7 of them where DRB, which is incredible. Since rebounds are part of the defense, this goes to him.

Larry bird is much far off, but mostly because of his shorter career, in fact he averaged 10 RPG, which is great, as well.

Steals and Blocks are pretty much even. None of them was a known blocker and stats are even. Bird is 41th in SPG and has a higher “per game”, but lower total, in fact Barkley places 28th. I’d say the difference is not very significant.

Here comes the hard part. Counting stats are only a limited part of the defense and i’m missing the eye-test.

Bird was known as an elite defender, he is probably one of the most complete players of all time and has 3 All-Defensive 2nd team nomination; while, Chuck has none. Despite being considered a good defender in the post and considering himself a better defender than Bird he has 0 nomination to All-Defensive teams.

Last but not least, i heard dubious opinions on the internet that said Chuck was, in fact, a bad defender. I never really gave weight to them, but here i am, asking for “truth”. How good was his defense?
