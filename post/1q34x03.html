Defender Reputations in the NBA

Why does it feel as though if a player at some point in their career was a great defender, that title sticks with them throughout their career even if they’ve declined on that end of the floor?

For me the 2 that come to mind are Kawhi and Giannis, and Mikal Bridges to a lesser extent. At their peaks they were the best defenders in the league. But a simple eye test, along with injuries and age catching up to them, its clear that they’ve taken a step back on that end of the court. Kawhi isn’t gonna lockdown the other teams best player anymore and Giannis is nowhere near the defensive menace he was during his MVP days. Yet they’re both still highly praised for their defense.

Seems as though once a player is given the label as a good defender it sticks with them no matter what. Is it a reputation thing? Cognitive bias?
