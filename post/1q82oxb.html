Teach me about arbitrators

I’ve always known about arbitration, but I never fully dove into how it worked until recently. And while I get it, I’m more fascinated, for whatever reason, by arbitrators as a career choice. I understand they’re often attorneys, former judges, etc., but are there people who dedicate their lives to sports arbitration?

I find it interesting that a neutral arbitrator still needs extensive knowledge of baseball statistics to properly rule a hearing, while it may not be their full-time gig. But that raises another question: are they just checking out Baseball Reference once they find out about an upcoming hearing? Is it all done during the actual hearing? Are they die-hard fans of a team who have to follow jurisdiction over fandom?

Who are these people, what is their official title, and are they, in fact, scrolling Baseball Reference at any point during the case? Couldn’t they just screenshare the player’s page on a big monitor and be like “hm, yea nah this guy is cheeks, for sure not worth that much. The team wins the case.”
