Xesht difficulty opinions

I dont know if this is the kind of sentiment people express about games often, but does anyone think Xesht actually needs a difficulty buff? Im not trying to make it sound like im super good at the game or something (i’m not at all) but I feel like Orloth, King in the Mist, Simulacrum, and Arbiter have enough going on that there are lots of ways to die, but the only real threat from Xesht is the occasional bad timing with double frost/electric hand summon at the same time as the quadruple fist slams in phase 2. Otherwise, all of his attacks have like a 2 second wind-up, pursuit is pointless and you should just walk into it immediately and roll sideways to avoid the rising slash, you can just walk in a circle around the frost hand or walk away from the electric hand, and even entering phase 2 gives you a significant amount of time to stand still and burst him, etc. Due to wrist issues I often play with only my left hand, moving with WASD and adjusting mouse with the same hand if I need to, but when fighting Xesht i dont even necessarily need to move the mouse whereas I cant get away with that on any other pinnacle content. To be fair, it’s nice to have pinnacle content i can farm one handed, but it does seem kinda weird. I feel like this is gonna be an unpopular opinion, but I’m curious to hear other people’s input.
