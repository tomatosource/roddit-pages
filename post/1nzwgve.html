Tip for rolling waystones

You can still reveal desecrated mods on corrupted waystones. In addition, corruption doesn’t seem to be able to remove an unrevealed descerated mod (tested on ~40 waystones or so). EDIT: It does seem like corruption can remove the unrevealed desecrated mod, but the chance for that seems significantly lower.

This means you should always corrupt before revealing, waystones are generally more valuable with the abyss mod, and you don’t risk losing the mod when you corrupt it.
