Repeating History

Milwaukee Bucks:

  • Won the championship in 2021
  • Bounced in the second round in 2022 after being the favorites
  • Embarrassed in the first round by the 8th seed
  • Trade a core piece of their team for Dame Lillard
  • Core piece, known as Jrue Holiday, ends up winning a championship the same year he’s traded to the Celtics. Meanwhile, the Dame & Giannis duo is currently failing

Boston Celtics

  • Won the championship in 2024
  • Bounced in the second round in 2025 after being the favorites
  • Embarrassed in the first round by the 7th seed
  • What’s next?????

Now I’m not a conspiracy theorist, but my mom taught me if you see one person burn their hand on the stove, don’t be the dumbass to follow him up to see if you get a different result.

“But Jaylen Brown is gonna win a ring in Milwaukee!”

DUH. The point is you don’t move core pieces for something shiny when you are 2 years removed from a championship. Especially after surpassing any and every expectation regarding the “gap year”.

This team doesn’t need to swap out stars. This team needs its young guys to develop. It needs to stop neglecting the point guard position and find a playmaker. Stop focusing on floor spacing bigs and get us a guy who can rebound and defend (damn…makes me want RW3 back on the team). Most of all, we need to learn how to switch our playstyle and stop trying to push one agenda.

The three point shooting is nice…..when we’re shooting the ball well. You’re down almost 20 and you claw your way back down 1. That is not the time to chuck up a 3 after a great defensive possession. The opponent is already on their heels. This is where you slow the game down to a safe, efficient, and comfortable pace. Attack the basket, get the opponent into foul trouble, take advantage of what the game is giving you! Exactly what the Knicks did to us in 2025.

I love this team. But we keep driving our car over the same big ass pothole (if you live in MA, you know exactly what I’m talking about) and it’s not working.
