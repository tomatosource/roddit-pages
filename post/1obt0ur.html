Delve may or may not be coming, but if it is…

If Delve IS coming…

What if it’s purely on the atlas?

There’s hundreds of pitchforks I can imagine coming for even daring to say it but.. what if?

Example: Currently, the Isle of Kin is destroyed and desolate. It’s original purpose destroyed by the Abyssals and most likely erupted out of the Delve entrance forcing Niko to go abroad, most likely with a 20-years upgraded device that might just have all in one functionality now.

What if, Delve acts as the “Underground” to the atlas’ “Overworld”. You find Sulphite above which forms the entrance, and based on Biomes of already cleared maps… you go to the “Underground” portions of that map, Say Desert biome has a “Maraketh burial city” that you can then delve into for a certain period and depth, that depends on the tiering of map you are using to find resonators that socket Runes into rather than Fossils, and even might be a new source of Rune generation for the ever-growing rune issue people seem to have.

We’ve already seen examples where they’ve used this for campaign, so what’s to stop them from doing exactly this in the atlas? It ties in atlas progression you’ll already have to do to gather Sulphite and ties it directly into your delving progress which then fuels the two in an endless perpetual motion machine as long as you’re wanting to Delve.

Admittedly it doesn’t have the choose-your-own-adventure feel of delve but eventually even in poe1 you typically need to map to delve, so what exactly is the difference? Your boss nodes are easily applicable based on tiering of maps used and you can even put special bosses in citadel areas.
