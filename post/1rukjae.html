Devs can you please be a bit more careful?

I started playing PoE2 something like a week or two after it launched into early access.

Had spent several days worth of playtime theorycrafting which class and spec would be the best choice for the build, and then made a completely new character and raised it, then spent countless more hours theorycrafting the best potential build without spending a nation’s worth of gdp in orbs and was getting ready to create an actual guide video for the build.
