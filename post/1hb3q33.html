Great Midrange Shooters with Poor 3% - A follow-on from an old post about how form impacts range

So I was wondering this in relation to players like D-Wade and Derozan, and did some snooping on the sub and found an old post about how guys with one-motion, perhaps lower release points have far extended range than guys with high release or two-motion jumpshots of yesteryear. They used the example of Steph having the most range (while conceding his midrange is also just so good cause he’s just the best natural shootever ever, to which I’d also add that if Steph is taking a midrange it’s probably not contested as teams are so worried about the 3 they’ll give up middies and even allow him a fairly clear runway to the rim, then you get the late rotations and his high off-glass finishes) vs CP3 who kind of hoists the ball up around or behind his head for his shot, and is a 36% shooter which is about average.

They then mentioned guys like Kobe and MJ as being guys with high elevation, high release point jumpers that prohibit range but allowed them to be elite mid-range, with others being KG, and exceptions being Dirk (some debatable inclusions too are Jokic and Luka. The former has fluctuated wildly over his career but the trend last 3 years has been upwards and this year on fire, and ofc for a big is elite, and Luka who takes a lot of tough contested threes on high volume. I thought Joel had more of this time of shot but looking at it his shot seems to be a fairly smooth one motion from what I can tell) I thought about this for a while as it explained guys like Kobe and T-Mac having some ridiculous 3pt shooting runs and even an entire season being a (for the time) scorching percentage on high volume, again relative to the time.

But then this got me thinking though about guys that currently break this mould in a big way, as we have guys like Kawhi in particular being elite from 3% and midrange whilst his jumper looking a LOT like MJ’s, possibly an even higher release point, less in elevation perhaps but certainly his upper body, while being for his career % being at 39% and only really being brought down meaningfully by early years in SA and his one year of TOR, which was still 37% which is respectable for someone with such an incredibly high release point typically associated (as mentioned) as well as PG13, KD, Booker and Middleton as efficient jumpshooters from both ranges obviously on good to great efficiency.

Do we have any other hypotheses for why guys with such famously good middies like the aforementioned D-Wade , Derozan and Butler are genuinely bad from 3? Is it a mechanical thing, just a touch thing, a confidence thing? Maybe a preference for the pull-up or spins and fades not blending well into 3pt range?
