Moral Victories

So many teams in the NBA somehow come away with the feeling of having a moral victory by playing the Celtics close forgetting two really important things. “We might have lost but that was the best game we’ve played all year, we had a really close game against the defending champions and best team in the league.”

We play down to our competition a lot AND we don’t have Kristaps Porzingis… There are no positive takeaways any team should have from playing us close right now or even beating us. It’s still the NBA where on a hot night vs a cold team, you’re probably going to win, but our .824 win percentage without Porzingis comes from us still having the best roster in basketball AND having a pretty easy schedule so far. This is not really the Boston Celtics.

The KP erasure happening across the league is criminal. I was afraid this would happen even after he was absolutely dominant in the finals. Can’t wait for him to return to the starting lineup and for people to see the real Celtics.

The Celtics are a AAA tier team with KP. We’re SSS tier with him, like historic stuff levels of greatness.
