The 2024-25 season is on pace to be the only season in NBA history where 3 teams have at least a +9.0 net rating

Thunder (+12.7), Cavaliers (+10.1) and Celtics (+9.1) are on pace to make this year the only season in NBA history where 3 teams finish with at least a +9.0 net rating

The Thunder and Cavaliers are on pace for their best seasons in franchise history by win total and SRS, and although the Celtics have taken a step back in the win column on pace for their 16th best season in terms of win percentage, their SRS (which factors in point differential and strength of schedule) is 4th only trailing their 2023-24, 2007-08 and 1985-86 seasons.

By the numbers, this is one of the best regular seasons of all time for the top end of the league. And if all 3 of these teams hit 60 wins and there are eight teams with 50+ wins, the last time that happened was 2008-09
