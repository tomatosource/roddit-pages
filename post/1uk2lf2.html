Two big reasons why Runic Ward is amazing

Ngl, I am addicted to Runic Ward now. I have always been a big fan of Recovery based builds, like Mana stacking regen/remnants etc. Runic ward is like this, but now you can also do both!

For me, there are two very powerful Runes in particular that I have made excellent use of for ward builds:

  1. Rune of Equinox (caster weapons): Your mana regen applies to runic ward regen too, but you have 40% less mana regen

  2. Rune of Annihilation (martial weapons): spend 5% of your runic ward to add that amount as flat phys to weapon attacks

#2 is an amazing way to further buff low level uniques. The Lorrata spear is pretty popular this season with being able to buff it a bit with runeforge. People are typically playing Original sin version, but Runic ward scaling is an alternative, or an addition. But you can also use this for loads of weapons, typically ones that won’t need Iron runes.
-&gt; The rune is also not local to attacks made with the weapon, and will add damage to attacks with their own bases like Repulsion, Caltrops and the new Conductive runes!
-&gt; The Runic Infusion support gem will add another 5% of max ward as flat phys, but at the cost of 20% max ward per cast

#1 can be used in caster weapons, which includes sceptres. This can work well with Spear+sceptre combo, so being able to use #2 as well in a Lorrata or Tangletongue spear, for example. Sceptre can roll max mana and mana regen, and with a stoic that has discipline aura + eldritch battery to convert the ES into Mana, you can get a good +500 max mana just from a sceptre.

For use with #1, building around 50% mana before life is extremely effective, as it can in effect double your hit pool recovery. In a situation where you are down to 0 life and you take, for example, 1000 dmg, with 50% mana before life you would take 500 to ward, 500 to mana. If you had 500 mana regen p/s, with your mana regen *also* applying to ward, you will recover 500 mana and 500 ward in one second, therefore fully recovering the 1000 dmg.

-&gt; Tldr with Rune of Equinox, 50% mana before life will effectively double your hit pool recovery for dmg taken beyond your Life pool.

Two main problems with Runic ward builds are Stun and ailment threshold, as it is also with max mana builds. Without stacking life or ES with % of ES as Stun /ailment threshold, your base is low, while not having dmg reduction like armour.

-&gt; Dream Fragments is an amazing solution for ailments when used on max mana/runic ward build. You get % max mana, mana regen, and Immune to chill and freeze, which is forking amazing. Ignite becomes far less of an issue because of high recovery, but you can supplement this with charm.

-&gt; Flesh crucible: Unwavering Stance fixes stun threshold as it just straight up makes you immune to stuns. But this comes at the cost of not being able to dodge/sprint. Pros/cons, it prevents you from being sprint stunned, because you can’t sprint! This really slows you down out of combat. Mageblood with at least one quicksilver legacy helps a lot. Disengage is better than dodge roll, as it moves you through enemies, and it also a travel skill, so I have just started using it on all builds like this. On any crit build, it is a pretty great skill on weapon swap, as you can use weapon swap points for shock magnitude, getting ‘guaranteed’ shocks with Shocking Leap support. Wolf on weapon swap is also good for the movement speed and/or pounce (and shock weapon swap). Pounce and disengage don’t share a cd, so you can even use both if you can.

(I feel spoiled after playing a build with immune to chill/freeze/stun, and kind of don’t want to ever play without it again.)

But what about CI?
Being able to stack Runic ward + Mana, and overflow both with their respective remnant skill sounds great for CI. However, you cannot use Olroths flask with CI, as you have to have an amount of life to recover for flask to be used, so you lose out on the insane Guard amount stacked on top of your hit pools. I am unaware if there is a way around this. But chaos res cap is much more easily obtained with Void flux, and adding life pool on top of Ward/Mana is just added gains.

Uhtreds Chalice mana flask is amazing, as well.
