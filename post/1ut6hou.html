Looking for a NBA team to root for

I will start this post by cutting right to the chase: I am looking for a NBA team to root for and raise my son to root for and I am giving every NBA team a chance to convince me and my family to join their fandom.

And now here is a little of my backstory…..

I was born in St. Louis, MO in 1993 and was raised as a fan of everything St Louis. Sports have played a big role in my life and while I am avid St. Louis Cardinals and Blues fan, I became a Baltimore Ravens fan last summer after trying to reach out to every NFL team last summer and hearing their best pitches for why i should join their fandom and raise my now 1.5 year old son to root for them too (the Ravens did the best job at convincing my family to join their “flock”), Now, I have the goal of trying to find an NBA team to officially root for here on out! While I don’t personally have an baseketball talent whatsoever, I can and have appreciated many great basketball players and teams as just a sports fan. I was a little too young to remember watching Michael Jordan, but players like Kobe Bryant, Lebron James, Steph Curry, Wemby, and countless others have made me tune in at times. Coming out of St. Louis, MO and residing in Central IL has made me also somewhat follow and root for players like Jayson Tatum and Shaun Livingston. As I think about and try to be the very best father I can be to my son now and into the future, I know sports can play a role in that to a great extent. In just his 1.5 years of life, my wife and I have taken our son to several sporting events and of varying sports (5 baseball games, 1 NFL game, 3 Hockey games, and countless high school sporting events as we are both high school teachers). Seeing the communication I got from NFL teams like last year, I would be remiss if I did not try this task again this summer with NBA teams as well! As an added bonus, being a High School science teacher in central Illinois of now 10 + years, I have used sports as a means of connecting with my students and having an NBA base to be apart of would certainly help that as well

All that being said, I sent a message similar to this to basically every team in some kind of fashion (Email, live chat, etc.) and i am hopefully it has reached the right people within each organization with this request and question (to this point, i have heard from 10 teams). I figured opening it up to others and various NBA fans could make for a fun addition to the arguements that could be made to join their fandom and raise my son to be a fan of.
