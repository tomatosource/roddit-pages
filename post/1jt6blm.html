Let’s show some love to Jaylen Brown tonight for playing through his injuries and giving this team his all.

Jaylen Brown is playing in tonight’s game against the Wizards despite a debilitating left leg injury. He is doing this because he needs to play in at least three more games this season in order to qualify for the awards to meet the new 65-game limit, including All-NBA considerations. I think it’s noble that he would even play at all at this stage in the season, and given the current state of the NBA where star players are typically rested for inconsequential games like this one. He’s taking a big risk playing through injury so I applaud him for his ability to power through and play through it like a trooper. Most players would sit this game out at this stage of the season, but Jaylen Brown isn’t like most players.
