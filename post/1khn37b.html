Acolyte of Chalupa mana leech

TLDR: How can I cause significant mana degen on my attack based character?

Okay, so I just respecced my invoker because I really wanted to try out energy shield leech.

But it is really really bad.

For background, when you leech, you recover some resource over time. When that resource is full, the leech ends.

The problem is that Acolytes energy shield leech, is it based off how much mana you’re leeching. Even at very low investment (two rings, no talents) you’ll recover the cost of your attack in like 0.05 seconds and hit full mana, which means the energy shield leeched is jack shit.

I’ve found two solutions so far, and I don’t really like either. One is anointing the node so 20% of my leech is instant (I’ll try this once I farm, costs about 170ex and takes up your anoint), and the other is Atziris gloves that makes leech from crits instant.

My question is, is there another way?

I want my mana to have a substantial and consistent drain on it, so that either it doesn’t fill, or doesn’t stay full. There is a body armour that drains your mana into health, but it isn’t really viable endgame to give up 800 ES for that.

Is there something like Arcmage for attacks, so I can spend obscene amounts of mana?
