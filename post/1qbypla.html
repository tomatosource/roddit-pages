Crafting is by far the best currency farm in the game

It makes everything else obsolete. Once you have banked a bit of currency and really learn the crafting in POE2 you can make a ridiculous amount of currency with the odds of hitting a good craft. I’ll use 100 divines as an example. Obviously you can start with less but let’s take 100 dives. You can turn 100 divines into 1000 divines in a 3 hour crafting session consistently. That’s 300 divines an hour. There’s not a whole lot of luck to this it’s just a number game and understanding the crafting mechanics and what best currency to use depending on prefixes, suffixes and base items. I try other farms but I just always go back to crafting because nothing comes close to it (except a giga pumped temple of course, that most people don’t have.)
