Their last home playoff win was on May 26, 2008 in Game 4 of the Eastern Conference Finals.

Since then, their drought:

- 2008 ECF: lost the last two games

- 2009 playoffs: Swept 0-4 by the Cavs

- 2016 playoffs: Swept 0-4 by the Cavs

- 2019 playoffs: Swept 0-4 by the Bucks

- 2025 playoffs: Did not win a home game

Over 6500 days since then. 17 years and nearly 11 months. What a road it’s been for Detroit
