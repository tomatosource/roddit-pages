Michael Jordan’s Son, Marcus, Reportedly Told Police to ‘Play Some Mariah Carey Up in This B*tch’ During Arrest

Source: https://www.complex.com/music/a/jose-martinez/marcus-jordan-demanded-offers-play-mariah-carey-police-say

A new police report reveals the extent of Marcus Jordan’s behavior following his arrest on DUI and cocaine charges earlier this week.

According to the report, obtained by TMZ Sports, Marcus is described as being “loud, profane and belligerent” throughout his time in police custody. Jordan was also allegedly fidgeting “constantly,” talking “incessantly” and getting “agitated and confrontational when given commands or asked questions.”

The report alleges Jordan “aggressively and repeatedly” pulled away from officers and refused to take a breathalyzer test.

Marcus even allegedly demanded a change in music at the police facility, telling officers, “Can’t you play some holiday music or some s***?,” before adding, “Play some Mariah Carey up in this bitch.”

Jordan was arrested early Tuesday morning after his Lamborghini SUV got stuck on train tracks in Florida. Soon after arriving at the scene, officers learned that his vehicle was suspected of fleeing a nearby traffic stop. Officers claim Marcus had “bloodshot eyes” and appeared “confused where he was located.” There was also an alleged strong odor of alcohol.

After he was asked to exit his vehicle, bodycam footage shows Marcus seemingly trying to talk his way out of the situation by bringing up that he is the son of Michael Jordan.

Marcus was charged with DUI property damage or personal injury, resisting officer without violence and possession of cocaine. He was released that same day on $4,000 bond.
