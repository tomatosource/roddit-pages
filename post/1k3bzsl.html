Why do you play games?

Personally, I play to have fun and generally challenge myself. I started gaming in the era of being able to buy booklets covering different starts, and secret areas. I couldn’t beat twilight princess without the booklet, it gave me the knowledge of what to do, but it wasn’t some magical power up that instantly made me bet it. I still had to struggle and eventually did end up beating it.

I don’t want to be spoon fed power. I don’t want to play through a campaign where every boss interaction is a trivial because the baseline power is high enough. I don’t want to be rewarded for being bad.

My last post here got some traction because I was giga unlucky with loot throughout the campaign and starting maps, I was frustrated and wanted to share my experience.

I was still having fun exploring their world and had a strong sense of accomplishment when I beat their bosses by my own means. Not following a build guide, but playing the game on my own terms.

Through that post I saw a lot of different comments, but what stood out the most is all the people looking for the easy way out.

Why even play a game if it requires 0 effort? If it’s a full second windup on a boss that’s 5 times your characters size, you should die.

I appreciate what GGG is trying to do with POE2, even with all the negatives. Especially after trying the new LE season which seems like they have brought the baseline power so high because of all the backlash on POE2.

Now, this is all my OPINION of which everyone is allowed. There’s no progress without discourse.

Stay sane exiles.
