Blown Away by First MLB Game at Fenway and Want to Try Some Other Stadiums

Went to Fenway for a Boston Red Sox game last month (May 1) and it was one of the best experiences of my life.

We had quite good seats close to Boston’s dugout. I was entertained from the time I stepped onto Lansdowne street until we left.

I’m not a huge sports fan (I have seen lots of live NHL games though), but I just can’t get over the sense of camaraderie that there was in the stands. I’d literally never even seen a Red Sox game on TV or even been to Boston and it felt like “welcome to the family” or maybe even “you’ve always been part of this family.” (sorry to be sappy)

*May include spoilers*
My highlights:

- The game itself. 1000x better than on TV.

- the guys serving the food in the stands, sometimes they used those funny yelling voices, I absolutely loved it and was surprised by how constantly they worked the stands (I didn’t even order anything)

- Sweet Caroline, the ba-ba-ba and SO GOOD SOO GOOD. I get chills even thinking about it

- Take me out to the ball game - we actually welled up (cheesy but true)

- This is a bit negative, but it was still entertaining and funny and educational, how everyone around us would yell TRASHCAN or CHEATER when certain Astros players came near us

- the wave that went around like 4-5 times. I’ve never been part of a successfully excuted wave.

- The number 1 top thing, and you all are going to think I was so naive after this but OMG when they brought out the CLOSER!!!! Chappy. I heard someone say, here he comes. Then the big song/video/whole place went dark. I don’t think I’ve ever experienced anything like it. Then WHAP WHAP WHAP the game is over. It was magical. Naive part: I did not know that bringing out a closer was a normal thing, or even what a closer was before this. So to me it felt very, very special. Actually, I feel like it would have been special even if I had known.

less exciting but still:
- the walk in, the historic architecture, the deep history vibe
- Seeing the old school manual change of the scores, including scores from the other MLB games going on that night! That was pretty cool.
- the rich guy that came in half way through the game dressed like an old Boston prep and just joined in with everyone. I know it’s a stupid little thing but even this added to the experience.

Two questions:

I would like to check out a couple other stadiums. Could any others compare to this, in your experience?

Red Sox fans, is the experience at Fenway always this good, given good weather, seats, and Boston wins?

TLDR: Amazing experience at my first MLB game, do any other stadiums offer an immersive experience that can compare to it?
