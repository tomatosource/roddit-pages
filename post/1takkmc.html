You guys are trying to re-invent the wheel with roster building and construction

This team has a great core in tatum and brown. History has shown that this team wins competitive basketball and his championship caliber. So blowing up that proven core who have had close to a decade of chemistry is insane, because Giannis is not guaranteed. Look at what happened in Milwaukee with him and dame for example.

The main issue with this team is COACHING. Living and dying by the three is not the right move to be doing with this team. We have two athletic wings in their primes. They should be attacking the basket whenever they can and either going up with it or kicking it out to a shooter or passing it to someone in the paint instead. Not jacking up a shit ton of threes. It is GAMEPLANS that have been holding us back, not Tatum or brown. You don’t trade assets that are doing great for you! You only trade the ones holding you back. Therefore, I propose doing simple changes. As in changing up the gameplan so that it is more balanced. Look at all the teams now in the playoffs, they all have one thing in common: THE ABILITY TO SCORE EFFICIENTLY ACROSS THREE LEVELS OF OFFENSE. For us, we only have threes. For them, they can mix and match. It is that simple: get a new coach who SPECIALIZES in this type of freelance 3-level offense.

This video explains it best: video

I would also suggest minor alterations as in getting a true big man in the paint who can score and rim protect, and a true point guard who looks to pass first, push the pace, and distribute the offense rather than just scoring.

DO NOT BRING IN GIANNIS. He is on the wrong side of 30, has already shown wear and tear, has been unavailable, etc. You get maybe 1-2 years before he completely falls off, because he is just starting that decline. We definitely do not want a 2013 Nets, or a 2021-2022 Lakers shitshow. Because the lakers had that exact logic.

The superteam era is over and has been over for years. The teams that do win are the ones who have All-NBA cores, and surround them with role players specifically designed for their needs. We did this already in 2024, we very well could do it again. It has worked. You don’t win in today’s NBA by trading away all your depth and role players for a generational talent. The CBA penalizes that. Not even mentioning the chemistry issues between three ball dominant players (like 2021-2022 lakers again). Instead, you win by surrounding your core (Tatum and Brown) with good depth and role players. That is how it’s done.

Giannis is a big name (literally and mentaphorically lol). I would say if this was possible 3-4 years ago I would say to pull the trigger. But it’s too risky to do. Because if we don’t win with Giannis, we just completely gutted our roster and will be doomed to losing seasons and presumably have no draft picks to rely on because we traded them away. Why break up this duo now in their prime, especially when they already won a title and proved they can do it? Let’s keep this core together, and look to 2024 as inspiration of how to replicate it. Just get a big man in the paint to establish presence and get easy buckets and defend. And get a true point guard. We already have the bench which is great. And change the gameplan either with a new coach or a serious talk to Joe demanding he changes it. It’s all we have to do.

Don’t over complicate this, don’t blow up what we built. Sometimes simpler is more. And that is this time.
