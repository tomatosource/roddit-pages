Fubgun’s LA deadeye the further beyond version looking for feedback.

As many of you know our deadeye godfather Fubgun has left us almost two months ago. But I stayed to study the blade bow&arrow and tried to see how much further I can push this build beyond what he left off. I now have 1.3 mil DPS on the tooltip without shrines and HH and this build can easily handle multiplayer 66% delirium maps while without sacrificing on movement speed or rarity.

EDIT: Adding ninja link https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/1188d

Below are the changes, the part of the build I don’t mention is exactly the same as his latest guide:

  1. level 100. Who can say no to more passive points? Minus the suffocating depression to grind the last few levels ofc lol.
  2. Ice shot. Ice shot does more single target damage on average 40% more than lightning arrows but losing on AOE due to missing chain. However, in high delirium maps what’s slowing down your clearing speed are those tanky rares/delirium bosses which require more single target DPS.
  3. Trinity setup. Trinity with Dialla’s Desire costs 90 spirit and I achieve that by having T1 spirit on armor(60) + 30 spirit corruption on the viper cap. The most reliable way I find to maintain trinity resonance is by having roughly equal lightning and fire damage (flat and extra damage combined) and using embitter on herald of lighting to add cold resonance.
  4. The bow:

https://preview.redd.it/m9e1ly6w112g1.png?width=969&format=png&auto=webp&s=bbc6a96b5376dd80a3cf7a40b8098c6b3d68dd14

In order to make trinity work I have to forgo physical damage on the bow. T1 phy + T1 flat phy = massive amount of phy damage which gets converted into a single element and becomes too dominant. Besides, the portion of unconverted phy damage gains no bonus from elemental damage buff. Because phy damage is no longer relevant the best choice of bow becomes ironwood which has the fastest attack speed. It’s also much cheaper to make so you can play around with corruption to get the 4th socket.

  1. The quiver:

https://preview.redd.it/311o9w67312g1.png?width=711&format=png&auto=webp&s=cdb22eca251da462a0702244d55eca27077689a6

Got a nice damage corruption on the quiver. The quivers with higher damage corruption on the market are either order of magnitude more expensive than this one (I got it for 700 div) or with lower rolls on the affixes so they balance out.

  1. +4 proj amulet:

https://preview.redd.it/6c7eivm0412g1.png?width=582&format=png&auto=webp&s=d0f3db64591091810fd4f44a3646f7b4077e3cf9

This nice little +4 proj amulet is sitting on a mountain of bricked amulets (40+). Literally just crafted it yesterday.

  1. Darkness Enthroned belt for movement speed:

https://preview.redd.it/jt6d11kh412g1.png?width=848&format=png&auto=webp&s=bd0f0ee6077a87091303c436a7acb2c030eb61cd

I swapped HH for this belt not only because I was traumatized by shroud walker teleporting me into a sea of 3 FPS abyssal overrun with Kosis as cheery on top but also because I believe they add about the same value to the build. I can take off 22% movement speed worth of investment on the passive tree to invest them into damage which is huge. I mainly swapped Vaal Pact from nothing to Walker of the Wilds for more ATD notable passives.

That’s all, looking for feedback to take this further if possible. I can’t afford giga diminishing returns like spending mirrors to get a 5 5 extra damage ATD
