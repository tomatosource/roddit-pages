I like combo

I’m not gonna bother with the “Unpopular opinion” in the title because I don’t know if it’s a popular opinion or not, but I like combo. Last league I’ve played warrior and it felt so good using multiple abilities that synergize well together. I threw a hammer and used a warcry after that to make it explode, that was usually enough to clear most small packs. If I needed more, I usually followed with some fissure and rolling slam. And when there was a tough ennemy I could get my earthquake for a big hit and similarly when there was a big pack I could use stampede on my fissure for a lot of damage. Like most of the time I used my hammer and warcry but I liked having option to get my big guns depending on the situation. It felt natural and I want that experience with more classes.
