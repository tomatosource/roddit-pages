I feel like the NBA is shaping up to be much more fun this year

The Wolves are going to be cinema with LaMelo and Ant together, I’m probably equally as excited for their antics as I am what they do on the court.

The Heat last year were really hard to watch because they sprinted up and down the court to get as many transition buckets as possible, but they were a garbage transition team so it was ugly. Now with Giannis they’re going to be fun as hell.

The Blazers with Ja and Dame in the same backcourt are going to be comedy af.

The Lakers finally have a roster with some shot creation outside of Luka and Reaves, they’ll be fun too.

The Sixers are loaded as all hell and Tyrese Maxey already made them really fun to watch.

The Leprechauns traded their finals MVP for Wayoff P

I know we’re just a few days into free agency but god damn am I excited for the season to start. There are lots of teams that I would never think to watch regularly but I definitely will this year.
