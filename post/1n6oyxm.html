If you ever wonder why bad skills that don’t work well for ARPG gameplay make it into ARPGs: It’s me, they’re all for me

I swear I’m not intentionally being contrarian or hipster, but I’ve realized over years of playing PoE and a slew of other ARPGs that I’ve got a sickness in me, where I get instantly bored with efficient, effective skills that work right and love skills that are, to some extent, at odds with my goals of killing everything at breakneck speeds in an ARPG.

Channeled spells you actually have to aim a bit like Storm Burst in PoE or Arcane Torrent in Diablo 3.

Melee skills that lock you down because they’re slow or channeled, like Flurry in Last Epoch or Blade Flurry or Lacerate of Butchering in PoE or that bone spike melee thing in Grim Dawn–but not GOOD channeled melee, like Cyclone, because it’s too effective and cuz I think melee spinning looks very silly.

Just plain awkward stuff that does a thing okay but makes it more complicated, like Charged Dash or Dominating Blow.

I think the closest thing I’ve ever enjoyed to an efficient or effective build in any ARPG was running a Curse-based Harvest build in Last Epoch (where I cosplayed melee while curses cleared everything but bosses) and a attack-really-slow-but-instantly-gib-everything Sunder build in PoE, and both of those were just ‘decent’, not great or outstanding.

So, you know, those skills aren’t worthless! There are literally DOZENS OF US out there enjoying them. That’s worth it, right?
