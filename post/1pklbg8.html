POE 2 helped me break out of my gaming burnout

First time poster here! On the release day of the new update for Path of Exile 2 I wanted to share a quick personal story of my own.

I went through some pretty heavy gaming burnout over the summer and honestly thought about giving up the hobby altogether because nothing felt fun anymore. One night I forced myself to play anything despite the rut, so I fired up POE2 and decided to play the huntress class.

Long story short, the build I was following got nuked by patch changes and I had to figure out my own way to make it work. Weirdly enough, that process ended up being the most fun I’ve had in months and it actually pulled me out of my burnout.

I figured others might relate to the burnout part, so I made a little video talking about my experience if anyone wants more context. Totally fine to ignore, just sharing in case anyone else is in the same spot:

YouTube link: https://youtu.be/Wq8yELkZKx0

Hope this helps someone who’s been stuck in the same loop.
