The Milwaukee Bucks are without Giannis, and are the worst team in the NBA when he’s off the floor according to on-off metrics

Giannis Antetokounmpo picked up a groin injury during the game against the Cavs a few days ago, which ultimately led to the Bucks losing and falling to 8-7 and the 11th seed in the East. They have lost 3 of their last 4 games, with Giannis missing 2 of these losses, including a loss to the Hornets without Lamelo and Brandon Miller.

The Bucks are +7.6 per 100 possessions with Giannis on the floor and –12.6 when he’s off, with their offensive rating dropping as low as 104.9 without him on the floor, an On/Off swing of 20.2 lol. Lineups specifically with Gary Trent Jr are specifically bad for the Bucks early this season, with and without Giannis. Offensively they go from playing like one of the best teams in the league to the worst when he is off.
