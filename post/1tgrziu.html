Microtransaction Portal Setting Request

I would like to be able to select favorite portals and an option for “Random Favorites” for which portal is selected when I use a portal. I have received a couple portals in mystery boxes I don’t particularly care for, but would like to see more than 1 portal type at a time when I open a map.

Currently we can select “Use one random variation for every portal” (Use 1 portal MTX for each available portal chosen at random from those you own) or “Use random variations for all portals” (Uses a randomized portal MTX for each portal created for your map individually from portals you own).

I would love to have the option to set favorite portals and “Use 1 random variation from your favorites for every portal” and “Use random variations from your favorites for all portals.” Without this I may forgo more mystery box purchases as I don’t particularly like having portals I am not a fan of show up or having to only select 1 portal at a time.

Has this ever been discussed by GGG? I can’t believe I am the first to have this idea.
