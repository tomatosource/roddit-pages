[Friedell-NYT] Inside ‘Chase South,’ the Warriors’ secret weapon for staying competitive (their own Las Vegas Training Facility)

SRC:https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7481097/2026/07/31/warriors-las-vegas-nba-summer-league-secret-weapon/

Inside the Las Vegas Basketball Center, pictures of Curry, Draymond Green, Steve Kerr and other current and former team staffers, players and coaches adorn the walls. Warriors and Golden State Valkyries banners hang from the ceiling. Two of the actual courts the Warriors played on over the last few seasons make up part of the Las Vegas facility’s five full-sized courts. A weight room sits in the back of the building. Longtime Warriors public relations maestro Raymond Ridder and his staff have a dedicated workspace. So does the team chef, who puts together made-to-order meals while players get their work in on the courts nearby.

The Warriors believe the large, once-nondescript building has become a secret weapon beyond the strong summer play of rookie Lendeborg, the scoring of LJ Cryer and the rest of the young talent on their 2026 NBA Summer League-winning team.

In the NBA arms race for the best facilities that seems to get more competitive each year, the Warriors’ decision to pour money into a facility in Las Vegas that was a go-kart track just a few years ago is a reminder of team owner Joe Lacob’s investment in the pursuit of every possible competitive edge.

“I don’t know what the other 29 teams are doing,” Harris said. “I’ve told people that will ever listen, ‘No,’ just doesn’t come out of his mouth. (He’s asking) ‘what can we do to be better? What can we do on the margins to do something that’s a little bit different, gives us an advantage?‘”
