New Player Gripes

I’ll admit, that going into this, I didn’t expect a Dark Souls style ARPG. I read nothing about it, no guides either, I just dived right in. After the Act 2 boss I realised that the bosses in particular just sap all the fun out of the game for me, and it’s weird because I feel like it’d be such an easy problem to fix with a few minor tweaks. Which presumably means the devs don’t even see any problems and this just might not be the game for me. Either way, here are the minor tweaks I think would completely transform the game into something better:

For the love of God, let me zoom out further. If you’re going to commit to being a Dark Souls game and want me to react to telegraphing, I need to be able to see the bosses visual cues as a ranged character. If my character can fire at something and hit it, I should be able to see it. Multiple bosses have left me relying on their audio cues and basically just counting in my head before dodging. Failing that, add telegraph markers on the ground like Last Epoch.

Reduce boss health pool by 50% so the fights don’t take so long that players lose focus out of pure boredom and die because they were just zoning out and going cross-eyed.

Increase movement speed by 100%. Even outside of bosses the fact my character practically walks everywhere just adds to the tedium. My most frustrating moments in this game weren’t even deaths, they were walking into a dead end on a map and then groaning as I have to backtrack at a snail’s pace.

Increase dodge distance by a small amount, and dodge’s refresh rate by a small amount. Most of my deaths have less to do with how quickly I react and more to do with my sluggish character trying to walk away from an explosion.

Also, just completely rework or remove the mercenary slowing down to fire, this entire gimmick with this class is at odds with the sort of bullet-hell twitch-reflexes the game wants you to have on bosses, you can literally get in one shot between dodging so your limited DPS draws out these already boring fights even further. Oh yeah, and maybe try mixing up the resistances of bosses too, because almost every boss in act 2 being fire resistant made my fire-focused mercenary feel even more dire.
