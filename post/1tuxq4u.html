Chris Paul - the best player-mentor in recent history?

We just had a heavy weight Western Conference Finals that may be a preview of years to come. In this same year, Chris Paul has retired. But his imprint is still felt on many teams as a player-mentor! He took SGA under his wing. Aside from on court skills, he learned discipline, nutrition, and training from CP3 https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/chris-really-first-person-show-065304273.html

On the flip side, he also mentored the young Spurs specifically Wemby and Castle - where to position for lobs, how to read the pick and roll, how to be a professional.

We can also go back a few years to when he was on the Suns and took a young team to the Finals. Devin Booker is now asked to play “Point Book” more and he learned a lot from CP3.

It’s unfortunate how Paul’s career ended this year, but his legacy is still in the league’s playoffs even now.
