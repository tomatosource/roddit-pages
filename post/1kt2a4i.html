Unironically, if they changed the name of MVP to Player of the Season, there would be a lot less debate on who should win it

More often than not the best player in the league doesn’t win MVP, and that’s ok, because MVP is about your season in its totality (team performance, stats, big games, etc.)

At least this is how it has historically been voted on. BUT the issue come with the name given to the award: MOST VALUABLE PLAYER. How the award is voted on does not accurately represent the name it is given. If the award was called Player of the Season, there would be a lot less debate from fans on who the true deserving players are while also acknowledging that there might be another player who individually is better but did not have the SEASON necessary to achieve the award.

What do y’all think?
