I liked lategame more when there was difficulty/friction

So initially for those who haven’t played then if you died in a map it was gone. And a mapdrop wasn’t guaranteed on the last rare kill.

Which made it so map-sustain was something that was challenging and engaging. You cared about sustaining maps, so it made you really care about not dieing in maps. But since you weren’t guaranteed a map either, you also were encouraged to juice as much as possible within reason.

Overall it was quite an engaging experience to me. Yeah it was occasionally pretty punishing (looking at you purple thingies of death), but I felt like my choices and gameplay mattered. Currently to me none of the things matter as much. Mapsustain is a total non-issue, since you’re guaranteed a mapdrop, even if you die. So because of that dieing doesn’t really matter as much, and in my opinion my choices and gameplay don’t matter much.

Just my 2 cents as feedback
