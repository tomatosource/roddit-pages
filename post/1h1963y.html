NBA agent Bernie Lee: “The All Star game is about everything but the players and the players who participate in it realize this pretty quickly when they look over and realize Guy Fieri was given better seats then their parents.”

Full tweet from NBA agent Bernie Lee

1st of all no sentence should start “Stephen A makes a good point”. 2nd of all the all star game is not about basketball and is in no way tied to any aspect of the history of the game. In fact its greatest main legacy was a group of players refusing to play to advance

their rights as workers for themselves and for future generations. The All Star game is about everything but the players and the players who participate in it realize this pretty quickly when they look over and realize Guy Fieri was given better seats then their parents.

That tweet was in response to the following tweet from Yahoo Sports NBA reporter Vinny Goodwill:

Stephen A makes a point I did on Monday’s Good Word… All stars play harder during the summer at Lifetime fitness than they do at the All Star Game. It’s a disgrace, it starts at the top and it’s a flagrant disrespect to the history of the game
