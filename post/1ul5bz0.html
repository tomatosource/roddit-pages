Is Paul Skenes pitching injured? Or are teams adjusted?

In his first two seasons, Skenes pitched 55 games. In those 55 games, he gave up 4+ runs a total of 5 times. Once his rookie year, four times last year.

Rookie year, he had 7 games with 0 runs allowed. Year two, he had 14 games (out of 32 starts).

So far this year, in 17 starts, he has four games with 0 runs allowed.

ER allowed in 2024, 29. In 2025, 41. In 2026, 32.

So his effectiveness, especially from the start of the season, has seemingly fallen off. He’s given up an earned run in 8 straight starts. The longest stretch of his career.

What’s different about right now vs previously?

Edit: Just want to add that I know baseball is baseball and any given game something crazy can happen. But players who establish a baseline tend to play at that baseline unless something else is going on.

So when I see the “worst” stretch of Skenes’s career (even if it’s still elite compared to others), it has me wondering what’s up.

Did he change his pitch mix? Did he have a sore lat last month and has thrown through it? Is his normal catcher injured? Dm

When Verlander had a down year in 2013, he ended up needing core muscle surgery in the off season. When he had a down year in 2014, it’s because of the core muscle surgery prior to the season. Not saying Skenes is injured, just demonstrating that usually a story behind trends like this.
