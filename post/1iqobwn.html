Endgame is too disconnected from campaign

Playing the campaign, every area felt rewarding to clear through, and it felt like most build types were usable. Struggling with a boss? It’s okay, respawn right before it, learn its moveset more, and try again. Fail, learn, improve, succeed. It had a brilliant gameplay loop, you’re constantly levelling, unlocking new skills, accessing new things. Your gameplay style changes in terms of your build, but also you the player have to learn and adapt.

Endgame is absolutely nothing like that. The basegame rewards you for learning and methodical gameplay. The endgame has literally nothing like that, and no slower gameplay. It rewards only the builds that 1-tap everything off screen, and gives no opportunities for learning. Deaths are typically instant, random and with no way of identifying what you did wrong, other than existing.

To me, it’s really strange how the whole campaign teaches new players that it’s okay to make mistakes so long as you learn from them, and then put you into the endgame which follows an entirely contradictory design philosophy. It just leads to frustration and being let down, there needs to be more consistency between the two, whether that’s making the campaign more like the endgame experience, or making the endgame more like the campaign experience.

There’s plenty of other issues in the game, but for me after 170 hours, the endgame is just so, so disappointing compared to the campaign, devoid of any actual fun and engaging gameplay that the campaign had, and just reliant on fueling people’s genuine gambling addictions.
