Cavs vs Celtics play style

Watching a couple of Cavs games and offensive breakdowns, I see a lot of resemblances to the Celtics offense (slot cuts opening up the corner three, a lot of relocation and movement, extra passes etc. )

What I don’t get is why (except for the obvious anti Boston bias) people are gushing over the Cavs offense and scheme while saying the Celtics are ‘ruining basketball’ and ‘boring to watch’. Are people blind with hate? Can’t they see that they play off of the same concepts?

I have no ill will towards the Cavs atm, I just hate the hypocrisy around the discourse about their offense.
