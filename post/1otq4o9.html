[Defector] The Kings Are An Abomination

Link: https://defector.com/the-kings-are-an-abomination

Defector’s Patrick Redford lays bare what many Kings fans have seen coming for years now, and has culminated in whatever it is we see taking the court each night this season.

Organizational dysfunction has worsened for years and can be tied back to Kings owner Vivek Ranadive and the mix of sycophants and machiavellian execs he surrounds himself with.

As Redford states here, the question with the Kings is not so much “What is their vision?” As it is “Are they able to fundamentally understand the reality of the mess they’ve constructed?”
