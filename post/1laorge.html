Where would each Finals team’s Championship run rank in terms of difficulty all time?

With the NBA Finals at 2-1 Pacers I decided to calculate how difficult each team’s Championship run would rank historically if they were to win it all. If you don’t care to hear how this was calculated just go to the graph. The way this was calculated was by using what is called SRS which is used to measure how good a team is by comparing its Net Rating to its strength of schedule, so essentially a 60 win team in a harder conference will have a higher SRS than a 60 win team in an easier conference due to the difference in competition. After that the SRS of every team that a Championship team faced in the Playoffs is added together to get what I called “Total SRS” and then it was divided by the SRS of the Championship Team. The reason why we divide it is so a team that was far better than the rest of the league isn’t given the same amount of credit difficulty wise compared to a team that was far more even with the rest of the league. This list only goes back to 1984 since that was the first year there were 16 teams in the playoffs.
