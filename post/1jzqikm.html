Th current trade and loot makes it difficult to get engaged

The whole game is balanced around trading. You just don’t get enough crafting currency to match the amount of randomisation and bad options involved.

The only way to get decent gear is to trade, and the trading system is not even in the game client! What year are we in.

I love a lot about the game, hell I dare say I’m their target audience when it comes to all the other mechanics. I tried PoE and hated how I was steam rolling enemies, I love how combat is much slower and large packs of mobs are scary.

But this trade thing is just genuinely unpleasant. They need to make SSF have a significant loot increase so I can ignore trade or add an auction house. Gonna try LE later in the week as they have done something like that with their merchants guild and circle of fortune thing.

I bounced off it initially as it didn’t have WASD but they added that for the latest patch. Even then I don’t think the combat will measure up to PoE 2 but being able to avoid the trade system is worth it.

Which is such a shame, the bosses are so dope. Bro Count Geonor with his bloody poems was so fucking peak and you had that bloody fire priest thing in the temple, the game really knows how to leave an impression.

Edit and TLDR

Calling trade mandatory was hyperbolic. I think to summarise what I’m trying to say in three points:

  • The amount do crafting currency dropped compared to the probability of getting something useful out of it is way of balance
  • I want SSF to be an option, not a challenge mode. I respect it will never be as optimal as trade but I want it to have its own benefits so it’s at least viable as an alternative
  • If not that then I want quality of life improvements to trade so you have an auction house you can buy directly without messaging other humans
