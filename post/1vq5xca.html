The Jaylen Brown trade makes a ton of sense

The decision to trade Jaylen Brown makes a ton of sense, and here’s why.

With the ascent of the Spurs and the Thunder, there are very few paths to championship contention. Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown alone won’t do it - and building out our depth won’t either. The Spurs and OKC both have superstar talent AND depth.

The Celtics front office are asking a very concrete question: what is a viable path for us to win a championship? When framed like that, the answer should be obvious: Jokic. There are very few players who can push the needle, and he is one of them.

Everyone has the same complaint about the trade: why trade for PG, when it is only a 1 year difference? But what does that extra year get you? The Paul George contract lines up perfectly with Jokic, giving you the optionality to pursue him. If you have JB, you simply can’t do it. Optionally is not the vague notion of being flexible that everyone assumes. It is a concrete plan to acquire Jokic.

When viewed in this light, it makes a ton of sense. You get rid of JB not because PG is better, but because it’s the only viable path to championship contention.
