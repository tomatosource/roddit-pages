Trial of Sekhemas and Chaos also need Atlas Tree support and a Secondary Mapping Mechanic.

* I’m not trying to suggest this is the entirety of what they need, they also need gameplay and itemisation changes, but they will also need Atlas Tree support. *

Jonathan commented that GGG view the Trials as an Endgame mode, no different to Breach, Abyss, Ritual etc. I foresee that even if they make changes to which playing them feels both fun and rewarding, players will prioritise other mechanics simply because they have a “Tree”.

I believe they need two things.

Secondary Mapping Mechanic accessed with Tablets

Think of this like the Vaal circles for Vaal Temple….Or in PoE, the in-map mini Ascendancy Trial to get an offering to the goddess.

For the Trial of Chaos, I’d bring back the “Ultimatum” system from PoE 1, a short 3 round mini in map mechanic, which drops an Inscribed Ultimatum on completion and a Vaal orb.

For Trial of Sekhemas, I’d do something a little different, I’d bring back the Mirage mechanic we just had in PoE 1. But don’t add mechanics, it’s just a small mini-dungeon where you free the Djinn and are rewarded with a Djinn Barya and a single Relic. Essentially a cross between Mirage and the in-map trial from PoE 1.

This way players have guaranteed ways to interact with the Trials whenever they want and they can play them like any other Endgame mode.

Atlas Tree Support

I find it odd that Sanctum (Sekhemas) is essentially a roguelite, and would be perfect to have an Atlas Tree which doubles down on this, yet we don’t have Atlas Tree support….Across either game.

The Multi-choice node seems like it would work really well here.

For example, needing to choose between starting a run with a boon, or starting with an affliction but increased rarity etc.

Without Atlas Tree support, I feel like people will still avoid them in the Future if they don’t have one, simply because other mechanics do. People will view it at face value as an “Inferior” mechanic, because it doesn’t have one.
