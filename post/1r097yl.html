Wisp Change

I thought of a Wisp change that might make them not so annoying. I know it goes against the design of the encounters, but I think we can all agree how aggravating following Wisps can be.

The change I think could work is we catch the Wisps and THEY follow US, and the first rare we encounter after grabbing them, the wisps will detach from us and go into that rare. That way we don’t have to go backtracking the way we just came, and we can just keep moving in the direction we choose to go.
