My biggest request for .5 is Ascendency

While I’m hoping for Templar, my biggest request is changes to ascension.

What I enjoy most is making new characters and trying out different builds. I’ll usually take characters to T15 and get level 90+ and then try something else.

I don’t mind the campaign, I actually enjoy it, most of the power jumps come before maps, and then it’s just RNG on finding gear or currency.

The thing that sucks is doing the trials. I usually just buy a carry to unlock all 4 and never look back. it’s such a bummer to break up the cadence of leveling to do this side quest, that is not really in line with how the rest of the game is built.

I love warrior, and I like building so I can take hits and shrug them off.

but now I have to tediously run around avoiding trivial hits because it’s a shame to my honor?

additionally, the ascendency is a huge part of character identity. it’s often times the entire core of a build, even if it’s a meme build.

to lock the characters identity behind an absurdly long and extremely tedious dungeon is annoying to say the least.

my thought it is to allow unlocking the ascension behind the same length as the campaign trials. the first floor or the first 4 trial things. the cool items can be gained by continuing on if you want, as an additional challenge

what are your thoughts?
