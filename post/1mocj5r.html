Alternate Class discussions

So at first it seemed like the class spread was gonna be 8 men 4 women based on character models… in concordance with the idea that each ‘character’ model would be getting two classes which left us thinking that Sorc and Witch were alternates despite being voiced by different people and both being on the execution block…

However with the Huntress release, it’s clear that Huntress IS the alternative profession of the same character, voice and all, and the class emblems beneath each character are all similar in association with the release classes symbols above them which indicates to me that Sorc and Witch are NOT alternates of one another, and are, in fact, going to have new archetypes released for them.

Which leaves me wondering… which of the upcoming ‘classes’ do we think are going to be paired with which of the release ‘characters’? I’ll be honest when I admit NONE of them sound like ‘alternative’ life paths of two magically inclined characters… but the greyed out class emblems beneath the characters feel like our most solid evidence of what’s coming for whom thus far.

Thoughts?
