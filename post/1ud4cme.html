[The Athletic] The Timberwolves are trading Julius Randle to Brooklyn, sources say

From Shams:

The Minnesota Timberwolves have traded Julius Randle to the Brooklyn Nets as part of a three-team trade with the Chicago Bulls.

The Bulls will acquire Nic Claxton from the Nets as part of the deal.

Minnesota will also send the No. 28 pick to the Nets while getting the No. 33 pick back in return.
