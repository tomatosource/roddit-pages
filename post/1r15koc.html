YouTube TV Sports Bundle - Celtics games

https://deadline.com/2026/02/youtube-tv-sports-bundle-price-channel-lineup-1236713095/

YouTube TV is launching a sports bundle (~$20 cheaper than general base plan).

The bundle will cost \(64.99 a month, or \)54.99 a month for the first year for new subscribers

It includes local affiliates of national broadcast networks like ABC, CBS, The CW, Fox, NBC and PBS. Also included are ESPN; ESPN News; ESPN2; ESPNU; ACC Network; SEC Network; Big Ten Network; FS1; FS2; NBA TV; Golf; NBC Regional Sports Networks; USA; NBCSN; CBS Sports Network; PickleBall TV; T2; TUDN; TBS; TNT; truTV; and NFL Network. Access to ESPN Unlimited will be added this fall.

As per this list, NBC Boston should be covered if you live in Greater Boston. Not a whole lot cheaper, but $20 cheaper ain’t too shabby for someone like me who only watches sports on live TV.
