Jaylen Brown is shooting under 40% from the field and around 20% from 3 in his last 10 games. Both games that he missed in the month of January we won, and in both games JT took the most shots on the team and had a good game. Once he returned, the power struggle that he manufactured in his own head returns and we start to have problems again.

January is as far back as I went simply out of laziness, but I’d be willing to bet pretty good money that this pattern continues throughout a solid amount of the season. JB takes the most shots = bad loss and nobody else getting involved, JT takes the most shots = high scoring game for him while also creating for others and opening up the offense for team success.

Looking at their efficiency and playstyles and just general overall skill levels, it seems totally absurd to me that having JB take the most shots is even an option in any game that JT is healthy, but it with 100% certainly ABSOLUTELY SHOULD NOT be happening in HALF our games. Every game JB gets his little Kobe chip on his shoulder we lose embarrassingly, and when he resigns himself to being the role player and complementary piece that he actually is in real life where the rest of us live he shoots much more efficiently, has better stats overall, and we win games. But he doesn’t get to pretend he’s a superstar and “take over” the team, so he doesn’t do that.

What’s the solution to this? It used to just be “well that’s just JB” and we lived with it cuz it helped more than hurting, but ever since he got his little sympathy fMVP and decided he owns the team his play has been detrimental to our team. At this point I kinda want him gone, unless he learns his place and chills tf out real quick.
