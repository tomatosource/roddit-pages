NBA teams to be consecutive 1st seed and how they performed

As the Thunder sit atop the Western Conference standings for the third year in a row, it got me thinking about which other teams in history have done this. Since conferences were introduced, there have been 7 other teams to achieve it:

  1. 2015-2017 Warriors

  2. 2008-2010 Lakers

  3. 1996-1998 Bulls

  4. 1982-1990 Lakers

  5. 1984-1988 Celtics

  6. 1980-1982 Celtics

  7. 1972-1976 Celtics

All 7 teams won at least one championship during their streaks. Six of the seven won more than once, three of those seven made the Conference Finals in every season of the streak, and one won the title every single season. In their 9 seasons as the No. 1 seed, the 80s Lakers failed to reach the Conference Finals twice. The 80s Celtics would have qualified for more than one championship run but dropped to the third seed in 1983.

With the Spurs hot on their heels, we’ll see if the Thunder can become the 8th team to finish as the top seed in their conference three consecutive times. And if they do, history says another title is on the way.
