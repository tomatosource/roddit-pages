[OC] The NBA Belt: Who’s got it now and how we got here

Many veteran members of this sub probably know all about the NBA Championship Belt. The Belt is an unofficial fan-created concept that applies a boxing or WWE-type system to the NBA where a team wins the Belt if it defeats the team that previously owns it. For instance, if the Indiana Pacers are the owners of the Belt going into a game against the OKC Thunder where the Thunder end up winning, the Thunder then steal the Belt and are the team to beat for any team that aspires to wear it next.

There are some traditional rules for the Belt. Notably that the winner of the NBA Finals every season starts the next season off with the Belt. This makes enough sense to why I can see how it made its way into the Belt lore, but I think it’s dumb. For instance, let’s say the Thunder, a good team, own the Belt before the last game of their season against, say, the Washington Wizards. Now let’s say the Wizards win this game because OKC sits all their players. I believe that the Wizards should get to keep the Belt all the way until they lose their first game next season, even if they don’t make the playoffs.

Now, if the Belt does end the regular season held by a playoff team, the championship-winning team will naturally hold the Belt for the whole offseason anyway, but there have been 14 teams who entered the offseason with the Belt despite not winning the Finals, and for those 14 teams, taking away that accomplishment because of something mostly out of their control feels wrong. So, I’ll be letting these 14 teams keep the Belt through the offseason for each of their respective years.

Another technicality that doesn’t really have an answer is when to start. I’ve seen some people start all the way back in 1946 when the New York Knicks won the very first BAA game against the Toronto Huskies. However, I’m a firm believer in the fact that the NBA didn’t actually start until the NBL/BAA merger in 1949 and that it’s a travesty that the NBA doesn’t include NBL statistics in their official stats. Therefore, I will be starting these statistics in the 1949-50 NBA season, where, because someone has to start with it, I gave it to the LA Lakers who did win the BAA championship the previous year.

Lastly, I’m only counting regular season, play-in, and playoff games. I think technically if we count preseason, Summer League, or other exhibition games, the Belt currently resides in some European country thanks to some Summer League loss by an NBA team. As much fun as that is, it’s pretty hard to track a multi-league Belt, but by any means if someone wants to attempt it, it would be awesome.

“Belt Champions” every year

Now, before you Clippers, Pacers, Nets, and Timberwolves fans go celebrate your first championships, I want to go over a certain issue that comes up if you think about the Belt as much as I did while making this post. How do we determine who won the NBA Belt Championship for each season?

It’s a little bit of a dumb question. In some people’s eyes, there shouldn’t be a Belt champion for a season, just whoever owns the Belt at any given time is the reigning champion until they lose. I kind of agree with this sentiment, but because I find the alternative viewpoint interesting, I will indulge in its debate.

If we do try and find a Belt champion for each season, there are two ways to do it. The first one is simple: whoever owns the Belt going into the offseason is the champion. I don’t really like this way. You see teams like the 2022 Sacramento Kings (who weren’t very good), who get lucky and win a meaningless game at the end of the season to become Belt champions in this scenario. If you like that idea, that’s fine by me, but I prefer the second way: the Belt champion for any year is whoever owns the Belt for the most games. I think this way makes a lot more sense and it actually rewards good teams still without going to the NBA champion every year. However, good teams still do get a boost because the more playoff games you play, the higher chance you have of winning the Belt. Here’s a table with every Belt champion under this definition:
