To the new players just reaching maps:

Speed is your friend in solo maps! Don’t spend too much time trying to juice your maps and towers. Just throw any tablet that has rarity or quantity of items found. Don’t stop for unidentified rares that aren’t t3-t5. Quality items aren’t really worth salvaging as the quality currency is a very volatile market. Always put delirium on your maps, even if it’s only 1 distilled ire. I find that pushing for citadels is one of the best ways to make currency solo. You’ll find a lot of unique maps along the way, and some of those can give you (if you’re lucky) multiple divines worth of drops.

But most importantly, try to join groups. I believe the rarity and quantity bonus from groups is multiplicative. You’ll get far more currency than playing solo. Try to join at the very least short allocation, but permanent is ideal if you’re a bit slow or laggy.
