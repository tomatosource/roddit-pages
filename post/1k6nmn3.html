Is POE 2 Designed for Just One Playthrough?

Is it just me, or did GGG design Path of Exile 2 and its systems with just one playthrough in mind?

A lot of the mechanics feel immersive the first time, but they become tedious or annoying on repeat runs. It almost feels like POE 2 is meant to be a one-and-done story experience rather than something you play over and over.

Why would they go in that direction?
