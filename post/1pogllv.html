[BasketNews] Michael Jordan “regrets” losing his relationship with Scottie Pippen

Given how events have unfolded between them over the years, Michael Jordan feels deep remorse over his sudden fallout with Scottie Pippen.

Chicago Bulls longtime chronicler Sam Smith dropped the revelation in his recent interview with HoopsHype’s Raul Barrigon.

“Phil [Jackson] was very sympathetic toward [Pippen], despite whatever things he said about him. We talked about that in the book. He said, ‘I feel like Scottie’s had bad advice.’ And the touching thing, though, was that when I asked him about how Michael reacted, he said Michael was really hurt by it, and not angry,” Smith stated. “He regrets losing this relationship.

“I wouldn’t have seen that from Michael, that Michael would feel the things that Scottie has said about him that have been critical or interpreted as really negative; instead of getting angry, he just felt a sense of loss.”

As of today, both sides remain distant from each other. But Smith confessed that Jordan was completely devastated about his estrangement from Pippen, given the bond and success they shared during their glory days.

“Michael really appreciated Scottie. In his Hall of Fame speech, he credited one player. He didn’t bring up any of the other teammates as much as he liked him. He liked John Paxson and other players, but he brought up Scottie specifically,” Smith added.

“It’s kind of a tender moment where it shows Michael’s sort of soft side, and that really his reaction toward Pippen was a feeling of loss.”
