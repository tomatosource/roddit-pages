Review of 0.5 from top 20 HCSSF player

Going to give a brief review of 0.5 from the perspective of an HCSSF player who enjoys long-term goals, friction and challenge over zoom. This will be focused on negative areas I think can be improved. We all know endgame is much better than it was and there’s infinite positive things about the patch.

I am playing monkey build which I went into completely blind on a whim at league launch because it looked fun. I have currently completed all challenges, got all atlas points and killed all content in the game.

Character - https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds/runesofaldurhcssf/character/policecar-7449/APETAMER_?i=1&search=class%3DSpirit%2BWalker

  • Gameplay - When mapping and on certain bosses I feel like you’re always getting ‘nudged’ around, blocked and pushed by mobs and it feels really jank and almost low budget. This feels particularly bad on things like ritual, I don’t know how this could be fixed - some sort of collision mechanic maybe but that probably isn’t even possible at this stage.
  • Balance and player power level - I killed all content in the game in under 3 seconds on my first attempt of them on the 4th day of the league, no risk of dying, don’t know the mechanics, they look cool for 3 seconds but felt super flaccid. There are many other builds doing the same. Currently I have nothing to farm for, I have no reason to reroll a new build, I have no reason to improve my gear further. I walk around with one hand on my mouse in juiced t16s and everything dies almost instantly. In my opinion this should not be achievable power levels in HCSSF after a few days and you could never reach this level of power in poe1 even after weeks of farming. I feel we desperately need more aspirational content or player power needs lowering by 10x so the current pinnacles feel like real, dangerous and epic fights. I understand catering the game to newer / casual players is important, but don’t completely forget about the vets / blasters.
  • Crafting - You get very good 6 t1-t3 mod items very easily by just picking up a a few decent bases - perf trans - aug - ess - abyss - slam twice with greater exalt. It then goes from easy upgrades very quickly to days of farming to improve maybe one affix on one piece of gear. This also then makes essences worthless pretty fast in comparison to poe1, I don’t know the solution.
  • Current atlas - I think this is a first iteration (and its still decent) and will be changed significantly over the next 12 months however, I feel the whole rogue exile / whisp / summoning circle is not fun. These things should be very small elements of endgame not every map has 4 rogue exiles, 5 whisps and 10 bosses spawning everywhere, it feels too much and becomes annoying after a while. We at least need a way to ‘block’ these mechanics. Also whisps move too slow and half the time don’t go where they should go.
  • Other quickfire issues:
    • Speccing into the banded (belt) wombgifts on the breach tree does not seem to be working, I still get 10x more currency and signet (ring) wombgifts
    • Verisium is borderline worthless on ssf
    • Gold is borderline worthless on ssf past lvl 90
    • Expedition in general feels bad on ssf - if you arent using rune ward in your build 95% of the mechanic is worthless, I ignore anything below 5 socket rune nodes
    • Many UI/UX things e.g having a massive rogue exile / boss health bar blocking all buffs
    • Performance in juiced maps is rough even on 5080 (sub 60 fps)

Overall I still enjoyed my time in 0.5 and hope you are too, see you in 1.0.
