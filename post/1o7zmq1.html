How to Make Multiplayer More Rewarding for Party Members

One of the worst aspects of being a party member is that you don’t get to progress on your map like the leader does. Could the devs not introduce a “skip waystone” that drops for party members (excluding the party leader) at the end of each map? It could take the form of something you insert into a map in your own game and at least get trash/empty nodes out of the way. There’s very little incentive to play with friends after the story mode is finished.
