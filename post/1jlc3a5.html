Please give us at least two portals on juiced maps - one portal feels pointless

With all the extra mechanics, giving us only one portal on high maps feels completely disconnected from how the game will play. It punishes players for mistakes, crashes, disconnects, or even just the occasional misstep, and removes a lot of the incentive to go hard on mapping.

&nbsp;

Even just two portals would be a huge QoL improvement and make the system feel a lot less punishing, without fully reverting the changes.

&nbsp;

And honestly, the extra portals on lower-tier maps wasn’t what we were asking for. Nobody was struggling with white or yellow maps. The real issue is when you’re running fully juiced red maps — that’s where the portal limit becomes unnecessarily brutal.

&nbsp;

Anyone else feel like one portal on high-investment maps makes the whole risk-reward structure kind of fall apart?
