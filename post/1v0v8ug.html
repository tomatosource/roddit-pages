2nd Apron stays undefeated

Lu Dort was just traded by the Thunder for a couple of 2nd round picks. Dort was their starter and an all defensive guy. He brought a lot of identity to the Thunder and they pretty much just salary dumped him. This comes after trading away two depth pieces in Isaiah Joe and Aaron Wiggins, both of whom played over 20 minutes a game for them the last two years.

You look at other past champions like the Nuggets and they have also been hit pretty bad. MPJ, KCP, Bruce Brown, and now most likely Peyton Watson all gone from their championship teams. Plus they had to give picks away to give away those guys.

Warriors are a different team completely from the 2022 Championship team now except for Curry, Dray and Kerr. They’re also stuck with an aging core and Jimmy Butler’s albatross contract.

The 2020 Lakers and 2021 Bucks are literally a completely different team now from the roster to the coaching. Not one of their players from their championship team is still with them now.

Meanwhile Knicks didn’t even get a chance to run it back with the same core. They had let their 6th Man, Mitch Robinson go, Ironically he ended up with us.

The turnaround in the NBA is insane. No team that won a championship is in the same spot. Everybody literally got so much worse, some more than others.

This parity era is insane. The cost of winning a championship is being collected even years after.

Seeing this all makes me feel a bit better about trading JB and losing everybody else. At the very least at least we have Brad guiding us through this.
