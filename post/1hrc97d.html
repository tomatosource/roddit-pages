Some New Years thoughts after watching yesterday’s game - us Cs fans have a lot to be grateful for in 2025!

I wasn’t able to watch the game yesterday so I just watched it recorded this morning. As I was watching while cozied up on the couch with a mug of tea, I couldn’t help but notice all the little things that make that make being a Celtics fan right now so awesome.

Obviously the team is great and we’re on top right now, but every part of the organization, broadcast, fanbase, etc, there’s just so many little things that we might take for granted but are things that other fanbases don’t get. Taking in a Cs game is just such a positive and pleasant experience, and even on frustrating losses (cough this last week or two cough), there’s so much about the fan experience that makes it not even that bad. I made a little list of wholesome, funny, and just generally awesome things I was noticing while watching.

  • A hilarious coach and a broadcast team who loves that about him. I love how different the coach’s interviews are depending on whether Drew, Abby, or Scal does them. All awesome, all in very different ways.
  • The Scal and Drew combo that’s grown into such an easy listen. They honestly just sound like two best buds taking in a game together. A little light-hearted ribbing isn’t just funny but also shows how well they get along. Listening to our home broadcast feed is just such a more pleasant experience than national broadcasts. Whether Scal’s goading Drew into giving us more “useless information” and loving every second of it, or Drew sliding in a joke about the Houston strip clubs, these guys are awesome together.
  • Scal has grown on me so much over the years. On one hand, he’s genuinely gotten way better since he started, but on the other I’ve just grown to appreciate his personality more. Five years ago I would’ve cringed at him talking about he thinks country music is strange so he plays it at pickup runs to throw (subtext black) players off their rhymth for the competitive advantage, only to be surprised when some black guys actually like country. I mean that is just the most likeable form of ignorance possible lmao.
  • Abby’s golden retriever energy. Whether it’s looking like a little girl on Christmas while showing off the new bench seats or constantly gassing up the guys in on-court interviews, she brings the absolute best of fan energy to her work and none of the worst. She was my celebrity crush from day 1 so many seasons ago, and she’s only grown on me since then. Also love hearing her poke fun back at Scal these days like when they were talking about New Year resolutions, it’s funny because it’s so accurate! Also worth noting that at this point, only Eddie Paladino has been a part of our Celtics experience for longer than her, since she started the same year Brad did. Crazy how little she’s aged!
  • Speaking of Eddie, I love his work on the PA, how he does each player’s names differently, how he pronounces “Scottie Bahnes,” and how the deepest layers of my subconscious associate his voice with that feeling of excitement you get when you’re at the Garden and they do the pre-game intros.
  • Seeing Deuce at so many games and Horford’s kid behind the baseline basically every single night. Side note - Horford’s fam seems to have inherited the spot Marcus’s girl used to always sit at?
  • Watching every one of our guys play their roles with so much personality, roles which may or may not have anything to do with their size/position. Jaylen just imposing his will on the game through sheer strength and power, Tatum manipulating the defense like a puppetmaster, Pritchard’s nonstop energy, KP grinning on the bench for basically 48 minutes straight, or White getting visibly uncomfortable with compliments on his play from Abby, it’s a joy to see these guys be able to lean into their unique strengths and talents so freely. I think that’s a credit to them as a group, and to Joe as coach for not only letting guys be themselves, but pushing them to maximize the best of themselves

I know that’s a lot of fan-girl-esque rambling, but I think it’s good to sometimes take a moment to appreciate how good we have it as Celtics fans. We are living in the good old days everybody, and I hope we can enjoy it now and remember it for the future. Wouldn’t want to be a fan of any other team, Cs for life yall!
