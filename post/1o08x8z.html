First Endgame Bow Craft

I made my first end-game bow for my Iceshot / Whirlwind Deadeye. I had a Gemini bow that was decent to good but spent some hard earned cash to make this to fill my bow slot with something nice – probably for the rest of the league for this character. I really wanted to try having a +2 arrows on a good base and that meant gambling for prefixes or gambling for crit. I found a good starter for the latter case for “cheap”.

I do not need any more adrenaline this week

I started by finding a T1 phys damage and 2 bow additional arrows magic bow for 40 div (currently 75 div to buy another on market). I had been trying to recombine t1 phys or t1 crit with 2 additional arrows on a warmonger for a while now without success – mostly by recombining bad bases on warmonger ilvl82 bows with some t4/5 that give a decent chance to combine then combine individual bases. I had hit a decent stat combo on a cavalierly bow earlier in the league but the craft for that one went sour and I decided to not over invest in a worse base.

I then put t3 flat phys (rolled almost perfect) because I had 60 divs to my name at this point and I’m not going for mirror quality or anything. Then I gambled a fracture and hit the t3 flat phys out of the 3 options. I was really hoping to not hit this as I wanted a safer suffix or prefix side for applying essences. But it was a slight blessing as it made no low roll affixes that I wanted to keep available for chaos orb to replace. However this left me with no good way to get +4 attack so I had to put a desecration and a random mod next to +2 arrows and pray I didn’t strip the arrows off. Luckily I hit the desecration and was left with a junk dex suffix, and empty prefix, and the rest of the mods I wanted to keep.

It was important I left the prefix open at this point because it allowed me to chaos orb back and forth to try and hit crit chance or a good prefix. I thought I had potentially bricked it with a hit on accuracy but luckily it was 1 tier below the +4 attack ilvl. After 3 or 4 perfect chaos orbs I hit t2 phys + accuracy which given the rest of the bow was phys was just fine in my book.

Finally I rolled ancient jaw bone + omen of light a couple times and hit t3 crit. I wanted t2+ but I was running out of divs and the odds as far as I could tell were ~1⁄15 (hard to estimate with jaw bone) of landing and I didn’t have 130 more divine to burn on trying. So I settled with this craft which came out to around 80 div total cost for me. Similar bows on market are going for 110-150 div so seems like I got a good deal for my heart palpitations this evening.

Debating if I should divine to try and get the crit chance up… probably should. I’m not going to vaal this unless I make something similar later on because I can’t afford to make anything to this level again for a while.
