Combo Count feels like a really cool mechanic that’s entirely wasted by not working with weapon swapping.

Mainly just the title. The idea of building combo and then consuming it in a back and forth flowing style of gameplay is extremely fun in concept, but is lost entirely by the fact that it only works if you stick to the same weapon. The concept of using a spear or quarterstaff to stack up a big hit then swinging with a Mace or a large Falling Thunder slam is just not permitted by GGG.

It can be argued, “Oh well then you’d just use fast attacking weapons to stack it up.” But, yes, that’s exactly the point. It encourages mixing up playstyles, and utilizing weapon swapping for combo based gameplay to have a much wider variety of playstyles. Instead Combo hard locks you to one weapon, and is instantly lost the second you swap, disincentivizing you to be creative or to mix up potential combinations of equipment and playstyles.

As someone who loves PoE 1, but hates the melee combat in it, I really want PoE 2 to have a more active playstyle and exciting combinations of equipment. However as is, there’s not a lot of reason to branch out, and instead we’re forced back into one or two button builds again.
