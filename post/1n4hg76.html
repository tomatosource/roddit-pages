Started as a Mercenary with a Crossbow: By level 11, I somehow became a Warhammer using Necromancer.

Figured I’d try the free weekend - normally don’t do Early Access, but I have been excited for this game for awhile.

It all started when I got an insane Energy Shield 47 robe at level 2. I gambled on a wooden headpiece, hoping for more Energy Shield… and saw that gave me +1 Minions Skills. Scoffing, I was about to dismiss gambling as foolish when I realized I had just sold some Scepter that gave Skeleton Warriors… I was like: “This can’t possibly work, can it?”

Turns out, my gauntlets had a random +7 Intelligence on them, so I was able to buy it back and equip it. Also happened to have a Yellow Smith Hammer I was about to sell… so what the heck.

With all my skills as Archery, I equipped a Mace+Scepter and just started left clicking with my 5 Skeleton Warriors backing me up.

A few levels later, I have a mix of Mace Skills + Occult Skills and am blowing up everything. Crossbow long forgotten. Got an upgraded Scepter, summoning 7 level 5 Skeleton Warriors that lay waste to everything, with me spreading Contagion, Infernal Cry, and Rolling Slamming around like a some kind of Drunk, Hillbilly Necromancer.

Where has this game been all my life?
