My first mirror-tier craft - Mana Caster Ring

Link

I have a friend who’s doing the Spark Comet build and was curious about how to craft the rarity-caster ring. I did this craft earlier in the league and decided to show them the process. I happened to get very lucky and hit T1 mana for the last mod.

🔨 Crafting Steps

  1. Get a ring with Rarity & All Res / Cast Speed
    • You can make a lot of currency using perfect Alt/Aug, and selling the misses but it’s quite a bit of work. I did this earlier in the league, but not for this craft.
    • Buying rings with both mods is not cost-effective. (20-50 div for all-res/cast speed)
    • It’s cheaper to buy ones with 1 mod (~1-2 div) and perfect Aug (~12%)
    • Or buy ones with 1 bricked mod (~100 ex) and Annul → perfect Aug (~6%).
  2. Perfect Essence of Opulence
  3. Catalyst to 20%
    • Use Caster to hit Cast Speed
    • Use Fire/Cold catalyst to hit All Res
    • You have a good chance of hitting T1 Fire/Cold Res. It’s worth quite a bit but not as valuable
    • I don’t recommend t use the lightning catalyst. Spark often uses Choir of the Storm and doesn’t need more lightning Res
  4. Perfect Exalted Orb
    • Omen of Catalysing Exaltation
    • Omen of Dextral Exaltation
    • Hit T1 Cast Speed/T1 All Res (???%)
  5. Preserved Collarbone
    • Blackblood Omen (Kurgal)
  6. Catalyst to 20% for Mana
    • Life is better for my build, but I picked mana since it can sell for a lot more
  7. Perfect Exalted Orb
    • Omen of Catalysing Exaltation
    • Hit Mana (???%)
    • Hit T1 Mana (20%)
  8. Divine to near perfect
    • 3 off perfect rarity, all the rest perfect
    • I hit this within 20 div which was pretty good
  9. Unveil Spell Damage while on full ES
    • Omen of Abyssal Echoes to reroll low rolls
    • There are only 3 Kurgal mods you always get spell damage
    • 2 off perfect on the first try. I decided not to reroll or retry

⚠️ RNG Notes

You need to get lucky on steps 4 and 7.

  • I started with 4 rings that all bricked at step 4
  • 2 hit decent mods, but not what I was looking for
  • 2 bricked and were annulled
  • 1 annul bricked
  • The last ring was a survivor

In the end, it’s not quite perfect, but it’s close enough for me and my budget. There is only 1 similar ring on the market for 4 mirrors.

The total crafting cost was ~100 div. However, I was very lucky.

💡 Bonus Crafting Advice

In these kinds of threads, I often see people asking what to do if you have no currency and want to start crafting. Here’s a craft that requires no startup capital and has a ~100x return on investment.

  1. Buy normal amulets with ilvl 10
    • Currently they are ~1 ex per base, so basically free
    • You can gamble at lvl 10 if you literally have no currency, are rerolling, or at league start
  2. Trans / Aug / Alch / Exalt them to see 4 suffixes
    • Don’t Alch if the Trans/Aug hit something valuable
  3. The odds of +1 skills (melee/proj/caster/minion) is 12.8% so you have a ~60% chance of hitting at least one of them

The initial cost is basically nothing, and current prices are rather high

  • ~1 ex (minion)
  • ~40 ex (proj)
  • ~300 ex (melee)
  • ~4 div (caster)

Unfortunately, this will probably ruin the profitability of the craft but shrug. If you have currency and are leveling a new character, please keep in mind that these are available and help keep up the demand.
