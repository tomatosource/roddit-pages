Point thresholds and the love of round numbers

I was looking at the frequency of point totals in NBA boxscores, and I noticed that there are distinct peaks around 50 and 60 points.

Image: https://imgur.com/a/OKA6g2T

Specifically, since 1990-1991 season, there have been 45 games in which a player has scored 49 points, and 98 games in which a player has scored 50 points. Similarly, there have been 5 games in which a player has scored 59 points, and 17 games in which players have scored 60 points.

This increase isn’t necessarily due to an odd/even point total, since the frequency of point totals decrease steadily leading up to 50 points, and similarly in the lead-up to 60 points.

We all know players like to target whole numbers (see the 50⁄50 Ohtani chase for instance), but its cool to see it in visual form.
