Has Sorceress always been this incredible?

I’ve played thousands of hours of ARPGs, played all of the Diablos for hundreds of hours (except 1), Last Epoch for a few hundred, POE1 for 600, Grim Dawn, Torchlight etc etc you get the idea.

I’ve played all classes in most of these games. But for some reason with POE1, I mostly always stuck with Ranger, Duelist, and Marauder. Never really as a caster.

Only played POE2 at launch as Monk (which was still super fun). For some reason, I always kind of feared typical casters in PoE1&2.

Yesterday I rolled a Sorceress for 0.4, and what the hell? Is this what I’ve been missing the whole time? I flew through Act 1 flawlessly much faster than I recall doing previously and I had an incredible time doing it. So fun, so strong, honestly feeling OP. One boss into Act 2 so far in and everything has been so smooth. Granted I’ve been lucky with some rares, lot’s of spell and cold dmg stakced on my wand and focus etc.

Now I know you might hate this next sentence, but I was kind of minorly hyped for Paladin on D4, but why would I want to touch that now, when I have this incredble looking and feeling experience on POE2. I’m committing deep to this league.

TL:DR, fairly seasoned ARPG player, feared casters on POE, now shredding with one and delighted.
