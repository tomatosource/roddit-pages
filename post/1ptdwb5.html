Suggest me some songs to create organ-style for my pro team

I’ve always loved the creativity and ambience that organists bring to baseball games, and kind of consider myself a weird scholar of stadium noise. When I got the job as our team’s DJ/audio operator, I knew exactly what I wanted to do, but didn’t have the talent to play live. So what I’ve done is preproduce everything at home, rather than learning a few songs to pointlessly play on a keyboard (No one in the crowd can see me and would be able to tell, anyway)((I also definitely would NOT have space in our box for a setup like that))

My library during a game is up to over 300 tracks that I’ve arranged/produced myself, and I’d love to continue to grow it with suggestions from the Reddit audience. If you can think of any tunes that have the melody or harmony to work on a stadium organ, go ahead and drop whatever you can come up with. I’m not necessarily going to be creating any on-demand tonight, but I’ll put them on my list of tracks to try out before next season starts. Any genre, and I mean any.
