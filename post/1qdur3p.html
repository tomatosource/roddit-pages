Team USA has just three roster spots left: a reliever and two hitters, including likely a catcher. Who do you want to see them add?

To recap:

SP: Skenes, Skubal, Webb, Ryan, McLean, Boyd, Holmes, Kershaw

RP: Miller, Bednar, Speier, Whitlock, Jax, Cleavinger

INF: Witt Jr., Henderson, Turang, Harper, Clement, Bregman

OF: Judge, Buxton, Carroll, Crow-Armstrong

C: Raleigh, Smith

DH: Schwarber

What they need:

  1. They have the the standard eight starting pitchers and 14 pitchers total. The minimum for the WBC is 15, so they should be adding one more reliever.

  2. In 2023 they took three catchers: Raleigh, Smith, Higashioka. Expect the same this year.

  3. Another outfielder or infielder, but I’d expect an outfielder because the infield is already pretty crowded.

My picks:

  1. Trevor Megill as the final reliever, the best righty American reliever not on the team.

  2. Patrick Bailey as the catcher. Higashioka was used as a defensive replacement in 2023, and if they plan to do the same with their third catcher in 2026, nobody fits the bill better than Bailey.

  3. Mike Trout. Although he may not have earned it from his 2025 performance, he can be trusted to show up in the big moment and this is probably his final opportunity to play for his country.

Who would you pick to fill the last three spots? Would you do something creative like taking 16 pitchers or only two catchers?
