Idea for a better infinite atlas in POE2

https://preview.redd.it/oa2wr54tleug1.jpg?width=1455&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=aed86c95ae6d96c38cf240b61f304d0c283de9f7

My Idea is that people in the future travel from biome to biome not from map to map. This way it still could be that huge infinite atlas but we wouldn’t have to do little map by little map.

Instead if we are not at an Biome that is good for our build or interests us in the layout we can skip that and keep going.

At the same time biomes should get stronger and harder the further they are away from the start point. But also ofc more rewarding.

That way you dont have to “spare maps/biomes” for later.

And there is still good space to put all the citadels and special maps and such in between.

Just a quick flash in my brain.
