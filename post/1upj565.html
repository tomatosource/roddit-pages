The Tatum/Brown Duo peaked in ‘24

2022, Finals: Lost to Golden State. Had a 2-1 lead.
2023, ECF: Lost to #8 seed Heat. Started series with 0-3 deficit.
2024, Finals: Won, dismantling Mavs. Masterclass in roster construction that is mathematically impossible to replicate under today’s strict second apron rules. Examples that are now banned include Holiday trade via salary aggregation, Horford via MLE, Porzingis trade via future picks (now frozen). This is peak of the current Tatum / Brown dynasty, and it unfortunately cannot be duplicated per the current rules.
2025, Second Round: Lost to Knicks. They blew 20 point second half leads, in both games 1 and 2, at home, and never recovered. This was the final shot with the 2024 roster as they were deep in the luxury tax at this stage.
2026, Second Round: Lost to Sixers. They blew 3-1 series lead. I know Tatum was recovering but that is part of the journey.

While Tatum and Brown are two of the league best, it is becoming clear that they do not have what it takes to make it to next level, as demonstrated by their ceiling they hit in the playoffs. Stevens chose to take a new path to see if he can unlock another peak with Tatum as clear #1 and surround with more depth. He obviously “lost the deal” with the Sixers, but who knows if he was able to extract anything better on the market. The longer he waited would have potentially yielded worse results. Everyone is jaded by the Kawhi and Lonzo ball haul, but that is fool’s gold to compare to. People want Brad Stevens’s head but actually have no clue what other teams would have truly given up for a top 15 player with a top 5 salary that produces only on high volume.

Also, the Sixers will literally be having the same predicament in two years max when they realize they cannot keep all of Embiid, Maxey, Brown, and likely Edgecomb on supermax deals (unless they want to role with Lower Merion High unpaid interns to fill out the bench).

I feel like many people are not seeing this in terms of the good of the franchise and are taking the bait from the fake news - believing that this is a political thing with Brown’s twitch platforms, or that Ownership is cutting costs like John Henry, or some other conspiracy to be outraged. I know it is a huge loss giving up your homegrown favorite, but you ultimately need to decide if you want to root for your boys or root for the team you rep. We need to give up the personal grief and move on for the good of banner 19 and maximizing Tatum’s window!!!!
