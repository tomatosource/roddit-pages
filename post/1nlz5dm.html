Combo shouldn’t reset when swapping weapons

For how much they want to create a playstyle where you combo together a bunch of different skills, and for how they tie every ability to specific weapons, and with how easy they’ve thankfully made it to swap weapons, using multiple weapon types is absolutely going to be the name of the game for a lot of builds in order to have access to more skills.

But comboing together a bunch of different abilities is not able to keep the actual “combo” mechanic going? Come on…

There’s so much of the current design that completely does not support branching out to explore combos, even on the skill tree.
