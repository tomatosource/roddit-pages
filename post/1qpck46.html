The Dunc’d On podcast’s mock trade deadline has us trading 2 first round picks and Simons for Okongwu. How would we feel about that?

Every year the Dunc’d On podcast does a mock trade deadline where they pretend to be the teams and basically run through a simulation to see what might happen. It’s not necessarily meant to be 100% accurate, it just allows listeners to go through some possibilities of things that might happen.

Kevin Pelton was the Celtics this year and he said he thinks Okongwu would fit perfectly with our core. The final terms were:

Hawks receive: Simons + 2026 1st + 2031 1st

Celtics receive: Okongwu + Luke Kennard + 2029 CLE 2nd

Personally, I would be thrilled with this. Okongwu is on a great contract, fits with the play-style of the team, and is just 25 years old. I’m not so sure the Hawks would be interested. The Dunc’d On guys’ logic was that Atlanta is having a disappointing season and will likely be focused on building out their team around Jalen Johnson using the NO/MIL picks that they own. The 2031 Boston first would have some upside. Boston would likely have to use a second round pick to dump Kennard, who would be pretty useless with Hauser on the team.

If the Hawks were open to doing something like this, how interested would you be as a Celtics fan?
