Place of the Druid in ARPGs like POE2

I’ve been thinking about how ARPGs/MMORPG handle hybrid classes, and the Druid sits in a weird spot across most games. In practice, ARPGs rarely manage to balance all three/more in a meaningful way from my point of view.

POE2 doubles down on class identity, but the Druid fantasy is basically 4 identities duct-taped together:

  1. big tanky animal form (probably bear)
  2. Speedy/slasher form (probably wolf)
  3. Might have Human form who’s probably gonna be a caster based
  4. Fourth one (cf the datamined infos for whose who did not saw)

If 2 or more are meant to be used at once, it risks turning into a rotation that feels like a chore. If you pick one or the other, the “Druid” label starts to lose meaning.

A few things I’m wondering:

- Do you actually want to swap forms mid-combat, or should each form play like its own build?
- Can a hybrid even compete in POE2’s scaling model without feeling strictly worse than a pure caster or pure melee?

Curious to get your opinion before we see the Path of Exile 2 interpretation :)
