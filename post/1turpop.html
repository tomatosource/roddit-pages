The New Endgame Feels Great

I fall into the casual player category, and I have to say the new Endgame feels amazing. I usually play through to the endgame, start clearing T15s and stop playing because figuring out what to focus on starts feeling like a chore. Now, every hour feels like I’m making progress towards an attainable goal, it’s awesome.

Side note, the ps5 whisper bug ended up being a good thing for me. I usually pay to get my 4th ascendancy, but not being able to whisper forced me to actually try it and I absolutely dominated the last trial of suck my ass. I hope you all are enjoying it too!
