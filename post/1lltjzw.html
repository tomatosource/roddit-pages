Financial Outlook For The 2026-2027 Season If We Are Able To Dump The Simons Contract And Get A TPE

This is mostly an exercise to show we can still build a potentially title contending roster around White/Brown/Tatum, at least from a financial standpoint, and stay under the 2nd apron.

Next year, we are projected to be about 33mil below the tax. We still need a starting center, a starting guard, and a back-up center. If we outbid the TMLE to get Kornet, maybe that can be 21mil for 3 years or something, (so take 7mil off that 33mil).

That puts you at 26mil below the tax to fit one of those starters in. Then we are an extra 27ish mil below the 2nd apron. If we are able to get very little money from a Simons deal, we’d end up with 2 20+mil TPE’s, (one for Simons + one for KP).

In terms of just money our rotation could be:

Starters:

  • White: 30.4mil
  • Simons TPE: ~20mil
  • Brown: 57.1mil
  • Tatum: 58.5mil
  • Porzingis TPE: ~20mil

Bench:

  • PP: 7.8mil
  • Hugo Gonzalez: 3mil
  • Scheierman: 2.8mil
  • Hauser: 10.9mil
  • Kornet: 7mil

Deep Bench:

  • Walsh: 2.4mil
  • Queta: 2.7mil

2026-2027 Roster Total: 222.6mil

2026-2027 2nd Apron: 228.6mil

As you can see, we have 2 roster spots to fill and about 6mil below the 2nd apron to do so.

However, we can cut and replace Walsh/Queta for 2nd Round picks and give them cheaper contracts, (Williams/Shulga/Peterson/Norris?).

One of those final roster spots will go to the 2026 1st and that value is dependent upon where we fall in the draft.

If we think Scheierman can step up for Hauser, then there’s an extra 10mil to be moved to fill out the roster.
