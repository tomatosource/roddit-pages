The Sacramento Kings have claimed the title for “biggest loss margin” for the 2025⁄26 season with a 41 point loss to the Memphis Hustle

No Ja Morant.

No Jaren Jackson Jr.

No Scottie Pippen Jr.

No Brandon Clarke.

And more injuries to the Grizzlies!

Sacramento was on a back-to-back and they lacked Sabonis but that’s not excuse for what is the Kings most embarrassing performance of the season (so far) against a severely undermanned Grizzlies team, especially with Keegan “Untouchable for Pascal Siakam” Murray returning to their lineup. This blowout surpasses the Nuggets previous record for biggest blowout for the season via a 40-point win vs. the Timberwolves.

Keegan went 1-6 from 3 in his season debut, in case you were wondering.
