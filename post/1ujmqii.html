What is the best end-game farm, and why is it Ritual?

Ritual is my best friend this league.

  1. Does not require high gear IRR because there is no reliance on ground drops.

  2. Does not require city maps or head of the king for the highest possible divine spent to earned ratio (See: Scorp’s Farming Analytics Spreadsheet).

  3. Does not rely on rng to start farming as there is no need to fish for grand mirror spawns or anomaly maps.

  4. Minimal prep as you do not have to stack delirium to 200%.

  5. Less rippy than 200% delirium alternatives like breach and abyss.

  6. You can take your time clearing the map instead of playing hot potato with the mirror gauge.

  7. You get to gamble 10+ times per map, if that’s your poison. If you don’t hit the jackpot (Mageblood, HH, etc.), it’s still profitable and gives a bunch of mini dopamine hits.

  8. If you like to craft your own stuff, you can avoid market struggles by purchasing omens with tribute and keeping the one’s you plan on using.

  9. If you are not min-maxing with perfectly rolled or higher value tablets, the start up cost is relatively low compared to others.
