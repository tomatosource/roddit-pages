The Orlando Magic have won the Southeast Divison with a record of 41-41. The first team to win a division without a winning record since the merger.

The winner of Tuesday’s play-in game between the Magic and Hawks will go on to take on the Boston Celtics in the first round of the playoffs. The loser will host the victor of the No. 9 versus No. 10 game, featuring the Chicago Bulls and Miami Heat, on Friday night. Whomever comes away victorious in that contest will match up against the Cleveland Cavaliers in the opening round.

The Magic finished the regular season 41-41. It’s the fourth time in franchise history they ended with .500 record. The other years were 1992-93, 1997-98, and 1999-00.

“With everything we’ve gone through – with the bodies being down, with guys being in and out of lineups and that adversity that we’ve hit this year – for this group to get to .500 says a lot about their character, their care factor, the coaching staff and what they’ve done to keep these guys prepared each and every single night,” Mosley said. “But again, we’re going to have to put that behind us in this moment and move onto Tuesday and make sure we are ready to take care of business.”
