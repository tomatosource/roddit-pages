My review of the campaign acts

Here is my subjective review of the campaign so far:

Act I: 9⁄10

A nearly perfect start to the campaign, its areas feel very coherent - much more than any other act - and thematically blend into an atmospheric journey. It also has some of the best soundtracks in the game.

Worst map: If I HAD to choose one, I’d choose Red Vale.

Best map: For some reason, Hunting Grounds.

Act II: 7⁄10

The caravan is a great touch, and choosing where to go is nice even if it’s of little consequence. Its areas initially have some variation (except Lost City/Buried Shrines and Mastodon Badlands/Bone Pits) but towards the end they get rather repetitive. Overall a nice and atmospheric depiction of a desert area but especially the latter half lacks variation and has some needlessly large areas.

Worst map: Path of Mourning

Best map: Titan Grotto

Act III: 5⁄10

I really enjoy act III up to the part before you enter Jiquani’s. The marsh, the jungle, the venom crypts, the infested barrens, the village and chimera is a great variety of uniquely themed areas. Most of them could be a little smaller but they feel good overall.

However it all goes downhill after that. Areas become repetitive and feel flat. Geographic elements are nearly absent - it’s all ruins and corridors. Utzaal is well crafted but its design is uninteresting. The only map that I like in the latter half of act 3 is Aggorat - huge, but varied and filled with lore. Again some needlessly large maps, that feel even larger due to lack of variation.

Worst map: Channels - probably my least favorite map in the game.

Best map: Infested Barrens

Act IV: 8⁄10

The islands have very interesting themes and great variety. I think there’s a missed chance for many pirate-themed areas, like naval battles, coves and so on. Choosing where to go is also a big plus.

Worst map: Nearly tied with Excavation, I’ll choose Whakapanu Island, which is not much of a tropical island, and is mostly a huge flat maze-grid with narrow paths.

Best map: Solitary Confinement - phenomenally atmospheric map, perhaps my favorite map in the game and one of the very few with interactive environmental elements.

Interlude: 8⁄10

Again, choosing where to go is a big plus. Much like act 1 there is a certain coherence between the maps of each interlude which is great to experience, especially in the vaal and Clearfel mini-acts (although the former transitions to a cold climate very abruptly). It seems we also haven’t seen the last of the Clearfel act, so here’s to hoping for a Castlevania tribute!!

Worst map: Sel Khari Sanctuary perhaps? I am on the fence with this.

Best map: Kriar Village
