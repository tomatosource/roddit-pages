[MassLive] NBA announces Celtics Summer League schedule

The Celtics will hit the court for at least five games in Las Vegas next month when NBA Summer League begins with all 30 teams taking part.

The Celtics have not officially announced their roster just yet but it is expected that 2026 draft picks Chris Cenac Jr. and Dillon Mitchell will take part along with recent picks Hugo Gonzalez, Amari Williams and Max Shulga.

Summer League will begin in Las Vegas on July 9 but the Celtics will not have their opener until Friday July 10. Here are the four scheduled games for Boston in Las Vegas. Boston will also play at least one more game after July 15, which will be announced at a later date.

Friday, July 10: Celtics vs. Raptors, 9 p.m. ET, ESPN

Sunday, July 12: Celtics vs. Hornets, 5 p.m. ET, ESPN2

Monday, July 13: Celtics vs. Hawks, 6 p.m. ET, Prime

Wednesday, July 15: Celtics vs. Kings, 8 p.m. ET, ESPN2

Celtics assistant coach Amile Jefferson will be the head coach of the Summer League team, a team source confirmed to MassLive.

Boston will have plenty of intrigue in Summer League as there are a lot of roster spots to fight for in Boston. The team currently has three open two-way slots available with second-round pick Dillon Mitchell expected to take one of those spots.

Boston’s Summer League team will practice at the Auerbach Center over the next two weeks before departing for Las Vegas ahead of the team’s opener on July 10.
