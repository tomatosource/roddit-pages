From 2007-2008 (Tim Duncan’s age 31 season) until the end of his career, Duncan had fewer MVP votes than teammate Tony Parker. Parker finished with 450 points (from 1st to 5th place votes) while Duncan had 117

If you change it to since 08-09, the margin would be 450-92

Parker had two top six finishes in that time, peaking at fifth in 2012, a year where he also got four first place votes.

Duncan is an all-time great, so that fact will probably be poorly received, but that wasn’t the intention. It does show that Duncan took a step back in the second half of his career, and that there was a distinct period (2012-2014) where Parker was genuinely, strongly considered the best player on the Spurs. Because of Kawhi’s finals MVP and Duncan’s overall legacy, that has been partially lost to time.
