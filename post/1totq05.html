How many teams have us as their number 1 rival?

With the knicks in the finals, I’ve been kinda thinking about where they rank in terms of our rivals. For me, the highest I could put them is 3 to be honest. The lakers will always be number 1 but I would put either the heat or sixers over them, some fans may say both. However, on the contrary, I’m assuming a large portion of their fanbase ranks us as their number 1 rival. I think you could argue all 4 of those teams may rank us at number 1. We may not be number 1 for CLE, MKE, or DET but even those fans despise us as well. So how many teams have us as their number 1 rival?

Also, this question extends deeper than you think if you’ve never interacted with nba fans outside of boston. I live in charlotte and am active in Hornets circles. The hornets fans absolutely despise us and believe theres some sort of rivalry. I’ve even seen some hawks, wizards, and even nets fans rank us as a big “rival.”
