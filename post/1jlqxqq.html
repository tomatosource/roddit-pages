Reconsider the Decision to Release the New Patch on Standard Simultaneously

Dear GGG Team,

First of all, I’d like to express my gratitude for the incredible work you’ve been doing with Path of Exile 2 . The game continues to evolve in exciting ways, and the community is buzzing with anticipation for the upcoming patch. However, there’s one aspect of the recent announcement that has caused some concern among players, including myself, and I wanted to share my thoughts on it.

In the past, new patches were initially released on the Challenge League, allowing players to experience the content in a competitive environment before being transferred to Standard. This approach had several benefits:

Balancing and Testing : By releasing patches first on the League, the community could help identify potential balance issues, bugs, or exploits in a controlled environment. This ensured that when the patch eventually made its way to Standard, it was more polished and stable.

Fresh Content for Standard Players : When patches were transferred to Standard after the League ended, it provided a fresh and exciting experience for players who primarily play on Standard. This kept the game feeling dynamic and rewarding for those who didn’t participate in the League.

Clear Progression Between Leagues and Standard : The separation between League and Standard content created a natural progression. Players knew they could look forward to new mechanics, items, and systems arriving on Standard after the League concluded, giving them a reason to continue playing long-term.

The decision to release the new patch on Standard simultaneously with the League seems to undermine these benefits. It risks diluting the excitement and longevity of the game for Standard players, while also potentially introducing untested changes into a more permanent environment. Additionally, it may reduce the incentive for players to join the League, as they won’t feel like they’re missing out on anything by staying on Standard.

I understand that this change might have been made with good intentions, perhaps to streamline updates or make the game more accessible to a wider audience. However, I believe the traditional approach served both the community and the game itself better in the long run.

Would it be possible to reconsider this decision and return to the previous model of releasing patches on the League first, then transferring them to Standard? The community values the care and attention you put into maintaining the integrity of Path of Exile , and this approach has always felt like a key part of that philosophy.

Thank you for taking the time to read this, and thank you again for everything you do to make Path of Exile such an amazing experience. I hope this feedback is helpful, and I’m confident that whatever decisions you make moving forward will continue to reflect your dedication to the game and its players.

Best regards.
