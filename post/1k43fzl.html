My personal opinion of the game

I haven’t played POE1 because I always thought it was too complex and you needed too many hours to actually understand it, I wasn’t interested to be honest. I started playing POE2 like 2 weeks before it changed to the new season/league. At first it was too much fun i couldn’t stop playing it, then the first downside came. After doing Act 3 you have to repeat the same damn thing but more difficult, same bosses, same maps, same everything. I kept playing because I wanted to see the endgame content and thought that was the principal content of the game, where it would lock me in for hours to understand the crafting and building of a character. So I did, it was hard asf but I did (remember I didn’t understand anything of the game) played barely 5-10 HS of endgame and then new season. I said fine I guess I’ll make a new character but I won’t have to do the same campaign all over again ¡WRONG! same campaign, same everything. I’m not going to talk explicitly about the downsides of the new patch because I haven’t played much of endgame before it, so what I can say is that it’s repetitive, tiring and not rewarding. Builds feel weaker, drops feel empty, and most of my interest is mostly gone.

So this is my opinion having 90hs on each characters (One Invoker and one Lich):

•Game is fun for the first run, then it becomes tedious

•Its poorly rewarding

•Dont feel like there’s a way to get better equipment than trading

•Dissapointing

Tbh I enjoyed it a lot and if I had told my opinion a week before now, I would have said it was one of the best games that i’ve played. I know there’s much to come in the next updates but for now I’ll give it a rest till I see something interesting that motivates me to go back. I really hope for something to change in the future because I think there’s a chance for it to be good.

Any recommendations for a similar game? I play on Xbox series S 😔😔
