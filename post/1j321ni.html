Prophecy Complete

Months ago, a youtuber theorized that it would be possible to hit 10 million DPS with shockburst, at the time he was at 2.5m or so. It was an interesting build design for gemling xbow so I leaned into it. Today I have finally made that build a reality and have done some significant improvements to the original design. I asked all around in this reddit, and poe builds, and the discords and did not get much theorycrafting help, so I mostly figured it out all on my own, but I am proud to present the FULL BIS configuration of gemling xbow. It can still be slightly improved with an additional 3 mirrors, but this is the general end result.

https://preview.redd.it/c985kxobilme1.png?width=856&format=png&auto=webp&s=d9b0e3f1caf3dbce8ec6415d1793a682cdc77084

https://preview.redd.it/hvxq8hpcilme1.png?width=930&format=png&auto=webp&s=eb13daf2c8ef4be475ecda132c172ae92c266ab8

With a couple more skill points I could get to 1500 shields, and with a 5% int 4% extra lightning against the darkness time lost diamond, it would be around 1950 intelligence. It was actually an additional bit of effort to reduce the attack speed to stay at 30.3 so that it doesn’t glitch out, so this tooltip is neither inflated by attack speed nor crit chance/crit damage.
