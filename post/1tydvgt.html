Can we get a megathread to list each league mechanic and what it gives you in SSF?

I am lvl 95 in SC SSF, here is a list off the top of my head

  • Do Abyss to get:
    • Abyss crafting currency
    • General currency
  • Do Ritual to get:
    • Specific Unique items
    • Omens
  • Do Breach to get:
    • Amulets, rings, belts with minion stuff
    • Breach rings
    • Catalysts for jewelry quality
  • Do Temple to get:
    • Vaal currency
    • Irradiated tablets
    • General Currency
    • Vaal orbs
    • Atziri special supports
    • Double corruption
    • Mechanical limbs (lost on death)
    • Lineage gems
  • Do Delirium to get:
    • Amulet instilling
    • Very Large Radius on a timeless jewel
    • Emotion crafting for gems
    • Double instilled amulets
  • Do Sekhema to get:
    • Ascendancy
    • Gems
    • Time-lost gems
  • Do Trial of Chaos to get:
    • Ascendancy
    • Vaal orbs
    • Soul cores
  • Do Expedition to get:
    • Runestones
    • +1 permanent level unlock
  • Do specific biomes to get:
    • Cities: Fractured orbs Juiced 4 tablet maps
    • Forest: Lineage supports
    • Swamp: Target farm chaos/exalts
    • Desert: Target farm regals/alchs
    • Mountain: Target farm tablets/runes
    • Grass: Target farm perfect currencies
    • Water: Currency
  • Do Fortress to get:
    • Atlas passive pointsWhich gives: essences, wisps, strongboxes, shrines, rogue exiles, +2 mods on tablets, and many others
    • +1 permanent level from Martyr
  • Enable Jado to get:
    • More exceptional items and unique strongboxes
  • Enable Doriany to get:
    • More revives and juice
  • Enable Hilda to get:
    • More rares and uniques
