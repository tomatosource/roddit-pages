Wind Ward removal a mistake?

One of the beautiful thing about PoE2 compared to PoE1 and other A-RPG is the tremendous amount of viable HC ascendancies (especially in league start), having a lot of them with some defensive layers.

It’s rare in A-RPG to have this, and Wind Ward for Deadeye was perfect conceptually as a defensive layer (but way too strong I agree, it needed a nerf), but removing it making Deadeye almost impossible to play on league start.

It was so cool, what about bringing it back with a nerf version like 1.5%/2% instead of 3% per stack.

I know that the game is never balanced around HC (and will never for obvious reasons), but even for SC I think having a nerfed version would be ok.

I’m pretty sure it will never comeback but please don’t wipe defensive options entirely on ascendancies next time, nerf them, because you’re reducing the pool of possible builds which is a bummer.

Would love to hear other PoE2 players opinion on this (not specifically Wind Ward) HC or SC.
