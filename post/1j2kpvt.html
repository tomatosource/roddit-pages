This game has one of the best controller rumbles in the genre

I was playing Diablo IV, and the force feedback there sucks major ass. In POE2, the vibration of the controller gives me so much information. An example: controller vibrates when Plasma Blast is fully charged (has a 2 sec cast time). In a game where there is a bazillion things happen on screen, any non-visual information is super important.

I do have 2 major gripe with the controller:

  1. picking up specific things off the floor
  2. target lock. Diablo does this MUCH better.
