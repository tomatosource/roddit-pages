Would a crafting tool make PoE2 easier for beginners?

Hi everyone,

I’d like to make a small suggestion regarding crafting in PoE2.

From my perspective, the crafting system feels quite different compared to PoE1 – in PoE2 you can reach BiS items much faster if you already have the right base and resources. While that’s exciting, it can also feel overwhelming for beginners who don’t fully understand the process.

My idea would be a tool or simulator where players could select a base item, the prefixes and suffixes they want, and then receive a step-by-step guide on how to craft it. For example: “use X currency first, then Y, then Z,” until the result is achieved.

I think such a feature could make crafting more approachable and enjoyable, especially for newer players who want to learn the system without feeling lost.

Thanks for reading, and have a great day!
