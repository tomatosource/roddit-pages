Some thoughts on skill designs in PoE1 vs PoE2

So i know many people here have a spectrum of feelings torwards the skill design in PoE2, and for me, i sort of somewhere in the middle. I enjoy the more tailored designs and cool custom-made animations, and i like that there are all these cool options for skills that synergies with eachother.

But what i feel is, especially compared to PoE1, is that GGG is just too focused on “intended gameplay”. In PoE1 you can make so many cool builds and take advantage of some insane combos of skills and items to make something truely remarkable.

In PoE2 i feel like the cool builds and interactions people show are not thanks to GGG’s design of the game more so it is despite GGG’s design and intent. Players fight to break the game and do something creative with their tools but it seems GGG keeps on knee-capping these creations trying to only allow you to be creative under strict conditions. I agree balance is hard and there are some stuff that definitely need to be nerfed, but the devs keep on trying to nerf things by killing creative interactions, and by designing skills around cooldowns and resources, and limiting the way you can utilize them outside the intended way.

Kudos to GGG for making 2 amazing games and i will say i’m truely enjoying playing PoE2. But i also feel like trying to come up with interesting stuff is very limited in a sort of frustrating way.
