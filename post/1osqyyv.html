I made a mistake, and it cost me over 70 fps.

This game is poorly optimized and overly CPU dependent. Now that I’ve got that out of my system, I have to admit most of my issues in getting to run the game well are my fault.

System info Processor: 9950x3d Video card: 5090 rtx

My video card was in slot 2, rather than slot 1. This is a much bigger deal than I thought.

I’m in a weird situation where I am running an AI server and have bifurcated my slot 1 to a few 3090s. I had to do this because my slot 1 is the only one that could split into 4 lanes, and I thought having my main gaming card in slot 2 shouldn’t be that big of a deal.

I was wrong. Just because its a full sized, pcie 4 x16 slot doesnt matter, it matters how the lanes are connected. And in my case, slot 1 is the only one where the CPU lanes and the GPU are connected. (I may be saying that wrong)

Before I made this change, with all of the best FPS hacks I could find and implement, I could get an unstable 75 fps which also pinned my processor and video card at max usage. Now I’m easily able to get the 120fps with room to spare (my monitor is 4k @ 120hz)
