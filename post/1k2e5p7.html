The Game Doesn’t Let You Feel The fantasy.

PoE2 gives you deep systems Presence, Consumption, Conditionals be it supports or passives but the game doesn’t show you when they’re working.

You fulfill a condition. You consume an effect. You trigger a passive, but there’s no sensory feedback, there’s just more backend math.

If developers replaced every conditional effect with flat stats, nothing about the gameplay would change, because most of it is not felt, nor seen by the player.

The only stats that are felt are the simple, streamlined ones everyone’s used to “Damage”, “AoE”, “Speed”. So when a skill has a static attack speed, it immediately feels worse because 1 of the 3 ways a player senses power is gone.

Wearing The Deepest Tower Spiritbone Crown? Enemies in your presence are considered “low life.”

Where’s the pressure? The aura? The tension? Are they really considered low life? How much do I need to hug them?

Gain “x” when surrounded.

Surrounded by what? Who? How many? No HUD, no flash, no sound cue.

Execute, Concoct, Consume Bleed/Freeze/Rage for X bonus, Projectile attack forks after melee attack, Hitting with a projectile extends your melee range.

The condition is met, but your skill looks and sounds the same. You don’t know it happened unless you compare the damage numbers.

Bloodhound’s Mark, Voltaic Mark, Freezing Mark, Snipers mark, they all feel the same, my character points a finger and that’s it?

A tiny sigil appears, No buildup to give unique flavour. Barely visible payoff. No fantasy.

The tech is already there:

  • Momentum Support adds a green afterglow to your character, why don’t more supports get this sort of simple care added to them, why are supports like ambrosia/concoct added if they’re not “finished” even?
  • Flame wall changes the texture of projectiles passing through it, why don’t we have more subtle differences when cold is infused with lightning, vice versa?
  • Heralds apply clear weapon VFX matching their ailment. So why do support gems like Concoct or Ambrosia which consume your flasks for an added benefit, have no visual effect at all?
  • Rage gives your character a “rage glow” yet berserk aura doesn’t upgrade this, even though it’s literally an aura that upgrades rage? Why is it just backend math?
  • We already have enemy glow if you have things like “Killing palm”, “Boneshatter” equipped, why not continue with this? HUD shows if you’re surrounded, maybe plays a sound etc.

None of these effects have to be over the top, there already are plenty examples of skills leaving a mark on the game world making you feel what you ascended, what you specced into, what aura you’re using, think “Into the breach”, “Temporal Rift”, and yes even “Caltrop Trail”.

Yet there’s things like “Trinity” that change nothing, just add something to the corner of your screen, while things like “Elemental Conflux” or “Unbound avatar” actually show what you have, what your character has become, some of the effects can even just be there on your HUD.

“Wildwood persistence” buffs my recovery per 5% hp missing, why not have my “life bar” fill up with minimalistic red wildwood energy like it implies in the art?
