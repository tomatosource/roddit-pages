Coaches Corner - Rookie of the Year (1993)

Baseball is over, and we all are trying to figure out what to do with ourselves at 7pm every night. As a lifelong baseball fan and a baseball dad/coach for the last 12 years, baseball has been my life. Especially as I watch my son enjoy the game. Something we have been doing is watching our favorite baseball movies. Now that he is older he is noticing the terrible baseball play in some of them. Got me thinking maybe I can watch these movies and leave critiques strictly on the baseball play of the actors. If there is interest I’ll keep going with this. As well as a huge baseball fan, I’m a movie nut. So this combines two of my favorite things. 

SYNOPSIS

So the first movie I watched was the 1993 classic Rookie of the Year directed by Daniel Stern. Rookie of the Year is about a 12 year old little leaguer that wears jeans during his games Henry Rowengartner. Henry breaks his arm. The tendons heal a little tight, and he is suddenly able to throw a 101 mph fastball. His terrible mom lets her boyfriend of 3 months become Henry Rule-Inverters agent and he signs with the Cubs. 50 year old Gary Busey, who looks like he has never thrown a baseball in his life, is an aging teammate, that gets called on to mentor the young boy. But really, I think he just wants to bang Rosenburgers awful mom. Henry eventually gains confidence and has some success on the mound. The team starts selling out games and winning games because we all know the impact 1 relief pitcher can have on a baseball team. Damn, maybe the Pirates should sign a relief stud to inspire the boys. I wonder if they have tried scouting the bleachers? Anyway, Gardenhoser gets overwhelmed and tells his piece of shit mom I just want to be a kid again, and after the year is over, he is done with baseball. Oh yeah, it’s implied in the last shot of the movie that the Cubs won the world series because after Henry robs a homerun in his little league game, he flashes the saddest world series ring ever to the camera. Henry is wearing jeans when he made the play. Have some respect for the game Hank. I guess it’s not your fault, your mom sucked and you didn’t have a dad. 

BASEBALL PLAY 

https://imgur.com/a/T1ZS71A

The first “Big League” action of the movie you see 50 year old Busey with possibly the worst wind up and throw I have ever seen. I dont know if it wasnt in the budget to get him a private coach or even a little league pitcher to play catch with him for a week before they shot. But it was obvious Daniel Stern knew there was an issue because Busey’s second pitch they film him from the letters up. Gary Busey never gets better, and he never stops looking 50. Maybe that’s why he started focusing on banging the shitty mom instead. There is a scene where a tubby fellow in the stands throws back a homerun ball hit by the visiting team. But it fails to clear the fence. He would have been a better pitcher than Busey. You can tell he was trying to look bad throwing for the movie. But he clearly played ball before. Throwing a baseball is not a natural motion at all. It’s always the biggest giveaway in any baseball movie. Ever see a t-baller throw the ball? Awkward and clumsy. Watch that same kid at 8 if he keeps playing. Much more smooth, natural, and athletic looking. It takes time for your body to get used to that motion. Can’t fake that. Go and touch grass and get those reps Gary, two thumbs down for you. 

https://imgur.com/a/0p46Wjr

In this same game, there is a double off the wall. The right fielder plays it nicely and throws it in for a play at third. Everyone is where they should be. AARP is backing up third base. The shortstop moves in to take the cut. But when then we switch to a close up of the relay and he fakes it. It’s quick but he just bangs his throwing hand on the outside of his glove to simulate catching the ball. Then a quick wide shot of the relay being performed. That’s just lazy directing. Do better, Daniel Stern. But that’s not the biggest issue with this sequence. The ball beats the runner to the bag. But the third basemen pauses and does this huge sweeping tag instead of dropping a quick tag into the hands sliding in. Tagging 101 let the ball get to you and drop the tag straight down. Keep this cute shit for movies…wait. Pretty bad sequence 2 out of 5 stars.

https://imgur.com/a/SkHzq13

In a game against the Dodgers that is played in Comiskey Park for some reason, the Dodgers actually had a major league pitcher, or retired pitcher. Tim Stoddard is the infamous belly itcher that brushes Hank back. What really gets funny is when Stoddard tries to pick Henry off at first and over throws the ball. Henry takes off for two and the right fielder fields the ball and crow hops to make that long throw to second. That throw is maybe 150 feet feet. You should be able to make that throw from your knees. But also a crow hop is useless. All your energy is moving up instead of towards your target. Try a pro step next time. One quick shuffle towards your targfet then release the ball. All that momentum is moving towards your target. Also you get the added benefit of not looking like a jack ass. Perfect Game Rating for this sequence is 4. 

https://imgur.com/a/ehGGvDv

The last play we are going to look at is a 4-6-3 double play. Second base does a good job at fielding the ball we dont really get to see him well. But he gets low, and stays low as he feeds short. But then short comes across the bag to turn two and looks like he is throwing a grenade to first base. He probably had a 15 degree arm angle and gets the weakest throw off. His arm action was so slow. He needs a shorter quicker arm if he wants to turn 2. Shawon Dunston is rolling over in his grave. That throw might work in 11u travel. But not in the big leagues. -25 points for the 25 MPH velo from 90 feet away..  

The Good 

Henry is the one that gets the most screen time playing baseball, and he looks competent. Sure he has that weird hitch in his throwing motion. But he looks athletic and comfortable out there. Daniel Stern gets a lot of the details, right. Every time Henry gets a runner on first base he pitches from the stretch. He also remembers to check the runner. Tiny detail but it’s one that matters. I noticed they showed his fastball grip twice. Once he went 2 seam the next time it looked like he went 4 seam. Hey I’m just happy he wasn’t whole handing the ball. 

Heddo the power hitter on the Mets that looked like Ogre from Revenge of the Nerds had a decent enough swing. I bet he is a Co-ed Tuesday Night Softball demon. 

Rocket giving Henry the “half two” mound visit speech was perfection. All you are trying to do is get the kid or pitcher to get out of their head. Personally, I liked to crack a joke or offer them a piece of gum and just walk back to the dugout. Leaving them wondering wtf just happened and not worrying about the situation they are in. Anything to get them out of their own head. 

The first baseball action we saw was on the little league field. You see a kid pitching and another kid swinging. Daniel Stern did a good job, he got kids who could actually play. Kid actors are one of my biggest pet peeves. Let’s be honest, they suck half the time. These kids didn’t have lines, but they looked like ball players. There is a scene where Henry breaks his arm. The bully teammate hits the ball and Henry slips on a baseball trying to track it down to impress some girl goalie. The bully had a solid swing, above average for that age. He is probably about my age now, 44ish. He probably hits excellent fungo. An underrated skill. Great job kids. 

Daniel Stern takes BP as pitching coach Brigma. It’s a moment put in for laughs. But Stern has a solid swing. Also, you can tell the balls he fouled off went straight up into the top of the turtle. Making the moment look that much more real. I wonder how many takes that took to complete? 

 FINAL THOUGHTS 

The movie was just as much fun as when I saw it at a sneak preview a week before its national release in 1993. The kid acting which is usually mediocre at best. Is really good. The movie was fun, it still made me laugh. I had a funky buttloving good time. As far as the baseball play that was mediocre. Henry held his own. But the supporting cast failed to carry their weight on the field, 6.5 out of 10. This score is based purely on the quality of the baseball played on screen.
