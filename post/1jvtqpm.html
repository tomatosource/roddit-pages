Can we stop talking about loot in campaign and talk about map loot?

Beating a dead horse here, but what are we doing here? End game changes are extremely well done, some changes and bug fixes needed for sure but a complete 180 experience on towers, unique maps actually fun (specifically the gold explosion one), etc. Map sustain could be a little better, constantly getting terrible mods I just don’t want to run (mainly Temporal Chains) that provide no additional benefit besides more Waystones. Having said that the loot and currency are just non-existent.

Take one look at Phrecia league idol strats and compare them to this atlas tree. The rates on targeted mechanics are terrible. Running 80%+ rarity maps with quant, 3-5 man party scaling, churning T15 maps with juiced towers with more rarity and there is just nothing to be had. Took me over 100,000 monster kills (campaign and maps) before seeing my first divine, haven’t seen a single greater jeweller’s orb yet. What is the point of running maps? Why would I feel I need to get stronger and continue grinding if nothing is around the corner.

I don’t get why there is so much focus on the campaign loot. It should be better don’t get me wrong, but the campaign is meant to be done with in less than a day for most, then move into maps where you spend the next 2-400+ hours of the league. You do it every league and move on to maps. I don’t understand why there is a such a delicate approach to loot in a temporary game mode. If this was a permanent thing like an MMO I would understand but this won’t be around come a few months, let us have FUN playing this patch you guys have worked so hard on.

I got 350 hours out of the previous patch, 370 hours out of Phrecia, I don’t see myself getting past 150 (currently ~100 hours in) I appreciate all the fixes and work they have done post launch to support the game, but the core of this game is the loot and the high replayability of maps with unique strats.
