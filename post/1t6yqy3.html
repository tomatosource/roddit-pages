PoE2 Is Missing One of the Most Addictive Parts of PoE1 — Player-Controlled Mapping

GGG really needs to let players freely choose which maps they run before 1.0 launches.

One of the biggest reasons mapping in PoE1 feels so good long-term is player agency. You are not just grinding random content — you are building your own endgame experience.

Want to spam Crimson Temple for Apothecary cards? You can.

Want to chain Jungle Valley because the layout feels smooth? You can.

Want to focus on bosses, fast layouts, mechanics, div card farming, or specific strategies? You can.

That freedom is a HUGE part of what made PoE1’s endgame replayable for thousands of hours.

In PoE2 right now, the atlas feels much more restrictive and RNG-driven. Instead of choosing content you enjoy, you often feel forced into whatever the atlas decides to generate. That removes a massive layer of player expression and long-term motivation.

The current system creates several problems:

- You can’t consistently farm layouts you enjoy

- Building specialized strategies is harder

- Burnout happens faster because players lose control over their gameplay loop

- Bad layouts feel much worse when you’re forced into them repeatedly

And honestly, one of the BEST feelings in PoE1 is finally setting up your atlas exactly how YOU want it. That ownership over your endgame is core to the PoE identity.

People often say “randomness keeps things fresh,” but PoE1 already solved this years ago:

You can choose your maps, AND still have randomness through crafting, scarabs, mechanics, modifiers, altars, league content, etc.

Player choice and replayability are not opposites. PoE1 proves they actually reinforce each other.

If GGG wants PoE2 to have the same longevity as PoE1, giving players more control over map selection is incredibly important. The endgame should feel like a sandbox you shape yourself — not a path you’re forced down.

This is something that should absolutely be implemented before 1.0.

Edit: A lot of people seem to think I’m asking for everyone to be forced into running the same map hundreds of times. That’s not really my point.

What I’m asking for is player choice.

Right now, PoE2 often feels like you have no choice at all and are instead forced into running layouts you may actively dislike. With map choice, players who enjoy variety can still run many different maps while avoiding the ones they hate, while players who enjoy optimizing or spamming a favorite layout can do that too.

The important part is that the player decides.

I’ve also seen people say that they like the current PoE2 system and that players who want more map choice should just play PoE1 instead. I understand that perspective, but I don’t think wanting PoE2 to adopt some proven strengths from PoE1 is unreasonable.

I already enjoy PoE2 a lot, which is exactly why I care about giving feedback on it. And when PoE1 already solved some of these problems years ago, it feels strange not to build on that success in the sequel.
