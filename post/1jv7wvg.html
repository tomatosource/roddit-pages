Victor Wembanyama will lead the league in Blocks Per Game, despite not playing in the usual required minimum of 58 games.

Victor Wembanyama still leads the league in total blocks this season with 176. Despite playing only 46 games

https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2025_totals.html#totals_stats::blk

Right now he doesn’t lead the in BPG, Walker Kessler does with 2.4 a game.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2025_per_game.html#per_game_stats::blk_per_g

Normally a player has to play in 70% of their team’s games (58 games over an 82 game season) to qualify for the BPG leaderboard.

However since 2013-14 if I a player fails to make it too the 58 game threshold, they can still qualify for the Blocks Title (or any other season title) if their total block numbers divided by the 58 games, was enough to give them the best Per Game Average.

https://www.nba.com/stats/help/statminimums

Wembanyama’s 176 (total blocks) / 58 (required games) = 3,03 BPG, more than enough to give him the Blocks Title for the second season in a row.

This happened in 2020-21 when Myles Turner lead the league in BPG despite only playing in 47 of his team 72 games.
