Rugby League has the World Club Challenge. What would this look like in baseball?

Rugby league has two ‘major’ leagues, albeit nowhere near the kind of money you get in baseball. However, the more prestigious and better paid league is the NRL in Australia, while the Super League in the UK plays second fiddle. Internationally, outside of club sides, there’s Australia in a class of it’s own, then New Zealand firmly placed second, then the Pacific Islands sides, England beneath them,… and then the rest.

The two champions of the leagues meet in the World Club Challenge, which is surprisingly even, even when both leagues were taking it seriously. The team that comes first in their league, wins the championship, and then the WCC is considered to have won the triple crown of rugby league.

What would this look like in baseball? Obviously, it’d be NPB vs MLB. Hawks vs Dodgers. The Club Challenge is often expected to be won by the NRL by default, but the English clubs actually win a fair amount of games. The Dodgers lost to an NPB team at the beginning of the year, too.

Or even Japan vs some of the other Asian pro leagues would be interesting.
