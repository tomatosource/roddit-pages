In 2006 at the age of 36 Takashi Saito made his debut in MLB. From the ages of 36 to 39 he racked up 10 WAR (2.05 ERA) as a reliever. He also pitched professionally until he was 45.

He had two more decent seasons at age 40 and 41 before his age finally caught up to him in MLB at 42. He then went back to Japan and kept going till age caught up to him there 45. He was excellent and one of those guys who if he had come earlier could have had a chance to put together a really elite (HOF?) relief career.

Also interesting about Saito, he started during most of his career in Japan, and actually had pretty mixed results for a lot of it.

He was another great late career Japanese pitcher, like Hiroki Kuroda who also put together a great MLB career from 33-39.
