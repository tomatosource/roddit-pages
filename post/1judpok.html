Fascinating Bucks/Pistons Scenario’s for the 5⁄6 seeding

  • Magic #’s = 3 for the Bucks and 5 for the Pistons, both teams control their own destiny but the Bucks have an easier way of controlling it.

  • if the Bucks lose to either the Timberwolves OR the Pelicans then it guarantees that at-least 1 of the final 2 games between the Bucks/Pistons will matter for their 5⁄6 seeding.

  • if the Pistons beat the Knicks then that also guarantees that the final 2 games between the Bucks/Pistons will matter for their 5⁄6 seeding.

  • if the Bucks beat BOTH the Timberwolves and Pelicans AND the Pistons lose to the Knicks beforehand, then those final 2 games won’t matter and the Bucks would clinch the 5 while the Pistons would clinch the 6.

  • the Pistons still control their own destiny because if they finish 3-0 (46-36) then that guarantees that the Bucks would finish no better than 2-2 (46-36) and the Pistons would win the tiebreaker due to tying the H2H (2-2) with the Bucks, tying the division record (7-9) with the Bucks, and winning the conference record (31-21) compared to 29-23) over the Bucks.

  • no matter what each teams record is heading into the final 2 games, the Bucks would only need to win 1 of those final 2 games and the Pistons would need to win both of those games.

  • remember, the Pistons must avoid having 3 games go against them for those final 2 games to matter (Pistons going 0-1 vs Knicks, Bucks going 2-0 vs the Timberwolves and Pelicans), or none of these games would have any meaning.
