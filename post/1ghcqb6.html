“So the 2020 lottery – when the Wolves won the No. 1 pick that became Anthony Edwards – was a peak pandemic affair in Secaucus done mostly remotely. (And it was in August – weird!)

I was the only reporter there. Afterward, I saw what I was and am pretty sure was the No. 1 envelope and Wolves team placard Mark Tatum unveiled on the televised lottery show. It was just laying around. I asked an NBA staffer if anyone was taking it. They said no. I asked, “Umm, then, can I grab it?” They said yes. I’ve had it since and jokingly threatened to return it in exchange for inside information.

Went to Minny last week on a guy’s trip (saw Wolves-Raps), and decided to bring it as a gift. ANT later signed it. It now belongs to the Wolves. And so ends the 2020 lottery placard saga.”
