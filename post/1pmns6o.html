The premise

As an avid baseball fan and especially a fan of ballparks and their uniqueness, I was intrigued by u/TechnoBabbles Magnetile Stadium from this post. While this stadium has some design flaws that make it questionable from an architectural standpoint (lack of seating capacity, luxury seating or a wraparound concourse), the playing field seems at least realistic to the sport.

However, noticing this led me to another question: although the field looks at least geographically correct, are the park dimensions conducive to a Major League ballfield?

To begin

Here is a layout of the field, alongside what I interpret to be the infield:

The interpretation of where the infield lies is based upon an anomalous amount of non-red tiles (66.6%) in this area of a total field with only 42.4% non-red tiles overall

Using this to calculate field dimensions

Bases are generally, in infields, laid an equal distance away from the grass on either side. This includes home plate. The distance between two bases is 90 feet. For that reason, for the sake of this field, a total length of two tiles is 90 feet (see diagram). For the sake of measuring, one tile is 45 feet.

2 tiles = 90 ft, 1 tile = 45 ft

Now, simply by multiplying, we can determine field dimensions to the corners:

3.5(45) = 157.5ft to the LF corner

5.5(45) = 247.5ft to the RF corner

Next, calculating dimensions to center field is slightly harder. However, basing off the famous Pythagorean Theorem, we can simply multiply the number of squares of the diagonal by the square root of 2 to get a distance in “tiles”, then multiply that by 45 to get a length in feet.

(5.5)(1.414)(45) = 350ft to CF

What does this tell us?

u/TechnoBabbles Magnetile Stadium is almost unplayable at the MLB level. Even considering that the walls are 45 feet tall all around, these tiny dimensions would make for absurd games. Considering the calculated dimensions and the fact that the left field power alley is even more sucked in, while this park could somewhat limit homers it would not be surprising to see batting averages in the .500s across the league at this stadium.
