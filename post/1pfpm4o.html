The NBA will soon have to develop a “loan” system

Before I start this post, I’m not saying that I want this to happen. I’m just saying that I predict this will happen soon.

Edit: I’m also not saying this system will/should replace the current system. It would just be an option that teams would consider when trading their star/trading for a star.

With the current trade rumours around Giannis, it’s clear the value of superstar players is massively inflated compared to earlier eras of the NBA. There are essentially no teams that can realistically provide the trade assets that a team selling their top-5 player are after, while also preserving any chance of contending in the near future. Trading for Giannis will clear out essentially all of a team’s young players and draft picks, leaving them thin on the ground in terms of developing depth and future moves. It also limits their window, as a player like Giannis likely won’t age as well as a less athletically-gifted player. On the other hand, the Bucks (and most teams trading their star) don’t really want a player in their prime, as they likely won’t be in a state to compete (see Davis and the Mavs). So buyers don’t have enough young talent to trade and compete, and sellers don’t have use for older players who aren’t as valuable. A handful of players are literally too valuable to trade

So, what is the solution? A loan system. Players on a multi-year contract could spend a period of that contract playing for a different team, in exchange for a permanent transfer of assets from the buyer. For example, Giannis might go to the spurs for 2 years (if he was contracted for at least 3) in exchange for a smaller number of picks and young players. The Spurs get a significant benefit of 2 years of a top 3 player before they (likely) start to decline, while keeping enough draft capital to make other moves. The Bucks get assets to develop while Giannis is on loan, which allows them to tank for a few years, develop a young core and in 2 years decide what they want to do, compete with a returning Giannis OR trade him again for a smaller yield that he is worth now. Both teams get a meaningful benefit that is appropriate for their current situations, while also allowing superstars to be moved more readily during periods where a team might want to stop contending and rebuild instead of doing as the bucks, Celtics and Sixers have done recently and tried to constantly compete.

There might be rules against this style of trade, but with the way that star value is going I don’t see another way to facilitate players like Giannis, SGA, Jokic, Luka (under non-Nico Harrison conditions) and Wemby moving in their primes.
