Biggest team needs next year

Taking the JT injury into account and not necessarily listing specific players you might want on the team next year, what are the biggest overarching needs for this roster? For me the clear answer has to be an influx of athleticism and size at the four and five especially but all across the roster. This team was sneakily at an athletic disadvantage against most teams in the league this year, and it was really noticeable in the playoffs where orlando was bigger and more athletic almost everywhere, and the knicks killed them on the glass at times by being bigger and quicker to loose balls.
