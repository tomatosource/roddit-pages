Opinion: Rarity Applying to Currency Isnt the Problem

IIR seems like a hot topic right now, and I wanted to throw out a conflicting opinion against the popular dreamcore video.

In the video, he makes a lot of very valid points, but I wanted to say I really do not want GGG to roll back the rarity applying to currency, because I 100% disagree that that is the problem, or even a problem.

Rarity on gear is the problem, full stop. Nothing else is busted about the way rarity works, and that’s a hill I’ll die on.

Here is my stance in more detail.

The dreamcore video talks about how in PoE1, rarity isn’t a problem because it doesn’t affect currency, so rarity builds are really just about farming uniques. This is a valid conclusion I suppose, but I do not agree. He’s essentially arguing that rarity isn’t a problem in PoE1 because it’s not that impactful. Which is a true statement. But I think it’s missing the forest for the trees.

Farming currency in PoE1 often turns into a massive chase for wild meme strategies because rarity doesn’t affect valuable currency drops. It’s ALL about pack size and item quantity, literally just juicing killing as many things as possible. PoE1’s affliction league spammed extra projectiles on abysses to spawn dozens of rares, this last league we got abyssal hoards and insane pack size alva farming for the same reason.

PoE1 has found a place for this behavior, and I’m not saying it’s inherently bad or even ruining the game in any way. But I don’t think that necessarily means it’s a healthy state either.

And especially, I would argue that rolling back to that behavior would be a very bad state for PoE2.

Do you want to have the only valid farming method for decent currency being pack size? Do you want to ultimately have to only farm currency on builds that can clear dozens of monsters per millisecond across the entire screen? Aren’t some folks already tired of getting swarmed by so many monsters in PoE2 as it stands today?

This is why. Because there is no other vector for improving the quality of drops from monsters, so you simply have to kill as many monsters as fast as possible.

So back to my core thesis. Rarity applying to currency is the only thing that makes bosses potentially exciting. The only thing that makes a 5 or 6+ mod rare monster more exciting for loot than a weaker rare mob.

Rarity scaling currency allows for harder and more powerful enemies to drop better currency. That’s a good thing.

The real problem is that with it applying on gear (and especially, with the gear IIR being an entirely separate multiplier from map/monster IIR) that forces builds who want any good currency to fit it into their build, always. If you eliminated IIR from gear overnight, I do not believe anyone would be concerned anymore about rarity applying to currency. In fact, I can’t imagine why anyone would think that’s a problem if the only source of IIR came from content difficulty rather than gear.

So that’s my stance on it. I understand some people disagree, but I wanted to hopefully also give GGG some visibility that it’s not the only opinion out there. I really really hope they don’t roll back this change in PoE2. I’d 100% prefer that sources of rarity on gear were either removed or dramatically reduced.
