The starting first basemen in the World Series will be representing their places of birth in the World Series, but not in the World Baseball Classic

Freddie Freeman was born in Fountain Valley, CA (part of the Greater Los Angeles metro area), but plays for Canada in the World Baseball Classic to honor his Canadian parents, who are both from the Jays home province of Ontario. Freeman has dual-citizenship.

Vladimir Guerrero Jr. was born in Canada, but plays for the Dominican Republic in the World Baseball Classic, both due to his heritage and the fact that his mother moved him to DR at a young age and mostly raised him. Vlad has dual-citizenship.

As an additional fact - the Blue Jays have 3 players that grew up in Southern California, the Dodgers have 4. Vlad is the only Canadian-born player in this series, which makes it kind of a fun fact that the SoCal-born and raised Freddie Freeman is Team Canada’s only representative in this series.
