Map Objectives

So yesterday during the interview the devs mentioned how internally they tested objectives like “clear the ritual” and people didn’t like it because “well I hate ritual I don’t want to be forced to do that”.

What I’m wondering is if they tried making the map completion be “clear an objective on the map” as in ANY of the objectives currently on the map. This could be thinks like “All rares”, “Map boss”, “All Essences”, “All rogue exiles”, or “All strongboxes” as some examples.

I think these types of objectives are only a problem when it’s the only way you can finish the map because you feel forced to do content you don’t enjoy. This also potentially brings back all the rush x mechanic strategies from PoE 1. Especially later on in the endgame when you don’t really care about killing all the rares because your already drowning in T15 waystones and don’t need the guaranteed drop.

They already added the map objective list into the top right for 0.2 and I just think this is something they might want to try testing internally for 0.3.

Idk maybe I’m cooked this is just something I’ve been thinking about since yesterday so I thought I’d share.
