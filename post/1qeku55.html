The Little Contender That Could

At 25-15 with the 2nd best net rating in basketball you would think there would be a parade of “here’s why the Celtics can win the title” stuff on both this website and elsewhere. And yet as far as I can tell Celtics fans are completely convinced and still searching for reasons why this is either fake or not gonna work in the playoffs.

One thing I have learned from listening to Brad Stevens and watching how he has both coached and built teams is that hyper-focusing on weaknesses is just a fruitless process in terms evaluating teams/players. It’s more about what players CAN do instead of what they CANT do. If you watch any press conference from any era of his tenure here you’ll see how he stresses that, you can also see it in how he coaches and leads.

Basketball is a pretty simple game. The object is to put the ball in the hoop more times than your opponent. How you go about that goal is up to you. I think of it similarly to a game like Chess, there an almost infinite number of ways to win with a fixed personnel grouping. We know how a pawn moves, a knight moves, a bishop moves, and how the King/Queen can move. Though unlike Chess your move sets are drastically more fluid because the game is about the pieces on the court. In Chess while there are an astronomical number of ways to win, you are limited in strategy based on how the pieces are allowed to move. In Basketball the pieces simply do whatever you know their skillsets allow them to do. There’s no One Size Fits All perfect solution to the essential problem: “How do we outscore the opponent”.

So instead of hyper focusing on what Cs might be missing why are we not focusing on what they have and how that has led them to being so good despite missing $60M in salary for every game this year.

The frontcourt is a hot topic of discussion for Cs fans. It’s such an issue among fans that many are willing to surrender multiple draft picks to “fix” it. But what are we fixing?

So traditionally Centers are for protecting the paint and grabbing boards. The Celtics rank #2 in the league in protecting the rim, #2 in points in the paint allowed, and #8th in Total Reb%. Not only do these areas not need fixing but they have been among the best in the league. Their defensive REB% has been top 6 in the league for the better part of almost 2 months now.

How they’ve done so is a mix of traditional center play and stretching the abilities on a versatile piece on the board to historical heights.

Derrick White, a guard, would traditionally be a guy we use solely for perimeter defense, though in this system is our main rim protector. In Chess White would be restricted to his move set, only allowed to help the Celtics on the perimeter but since this is basketball the Celtics can use him however they see fit. His hyper-awareness and timing on defense allows him to materialize instantly whenever and wherever there’s a problem.

So while Queta and Garza might not be traditional rim protectors(Queta is basically pretty good in this area), it doesn’t matter we have Derrick White. But it’s not just White, Jordan Walsh uses his 7’3 wingspan and freaky athleticism at the rim defensively, we’ve seen Minott make an impact at the rim defensively. Even at times when JB wants to he has historically shown he can chip in and provide some secondary rim protection. The Celtics are smart as a unit and talented enough on defense to just get enough resistance to equal an elite rim protection squad.

The Celtics outscore teams by +7 with Queta as the 5 and outscore teams by +7 with Garza as the 5. To me that reads as a pretty good frontcourt pairing. Both find themselves in the top 100 of EPM and both have spent time on the top of the Celtics on/off net rating list. So these guys both aren’t costing us games but they are also adding to winning. Their offensive rebounding is essential to the Celtics possession math. The Celtics, as a team, are a top 5 offensive rebounding unit and top 5 in net shot attempts. And their ability to screen is a godsend. Garza, built like the Kingpin, uses that WIDE frame to set incredible screens. He has particularly good chemistry with Jaylen Brown and I thought that during JBs 50 point explosion Garza was almost essential to the matchup hunting math. He got JB 1on1s with LAC’s big men almost automatically after every screen. Are they perfect bigs? No of course not but perfect doesn’t have to be the enemy of good, and good is just fine.

So not only are we figuring out solutions to problems that pop up, we still have a bunch of insanely good things going for us. The Celtics have a personnel grouping of elite isolation talent + a collection of “Leave Me and I’ll Beat You” shooters that puts defenders between a rock and a hard place. It’s the same combination that has fueled Mazzulla Ball for the last 3 seasons. Simons, as we saw vs Miami, is capable of winning games off his shooting/scoring talent. Payton has been winning us games at the 3pt line since last year and this year has begun cooking anybody who wants it in the mid-range with his absurd handle and feathery shooting touch. Sam “40% from 3” Hauser is back on his bullshit and slipping screens into barely open triples. This isn’t even counting White who has had a inconsistent year shooting but we know in his more comfortable off ball role next to Tatum that he is a guy that can and will win games at the line. All of that next to Jaylen Brown who is doing his best SGA/DWade impression is an impossible task for defenses and it’s why the Celtics offense is one of the best we’ve ever seen. They are currently 21st all time in relative offensive efficiency, which is how much better compared to the league average offense in that given season.

As I said earlier the goal of the sport is to outscore the other team. The Celtics at the moment are out scoring other teams by 7pts per 100, #2 in the league and Jayson Tatum hasn’t played. They have the best point differential and the best offense in the conference and have been among the top of the league vs teams over .500. So I say again why are we focusing on what they can’t do and why don’t we focus on what they can do? If you have a weakness as a team and haven’t had your best player in the lineup + have been winning at an extremely high level, it stands to reason that either that weakness has been oversold or you fixed the weakness already. Either outcome is good for the team because you will only get better once Tatum returns.

Tatum is not only a top 3-5 player but he’s just straight up the best 4 in the sport in terms of versatility and impact. The Debbie downers will say he won’t be good when he returns and to that I say “maybe he won’t” , but again a lot of the same people thought we would all be watching Cam Boozer highlights right now instead of watching Ant Simons win us a 19pt road comeback. So maybe let’s just let this team find its own path to glory?
