Upscalers and overall performance with older gen cpu’s in PoE 2 (9070 xt vs 5070 ti)

So long story short, in the past week I’ve had the chance to test 5070 ti using different upscalers while waiting for my new 9070 xt to arrive. For reference, my cpu is 5700x3d and I’m playing on 1080p. I might go for 1440p relatively soon.

I’ve noticed FSR being so bad it’s unusable in this game, so for AMD card’s you would probably want to use default upscaling (NIS). For me DLSS4 is about as good as NIS, if not even better.

I originally decided to go with 9070 xt because of something called cpu overhead in Nvidia’s 50 series cards, which make them perform worse with weaker cpu’s like mine. It should make cpu bound games perform better on 9070 xt compared to 5070 ti.

I thought this would be true for PoE 2 as well since it’s a cpu bound game, but while testing upscalers the past week, I’ve noticed DLSS4 being so good, the performance boost from that probably exceeds the cpu overhead. During endgame mapping blasting mobs with my cluster grenade mortar cannon build and native upscaling (NIS) my fps averages about 120, with DLSS4 it averages about 160.

PoE 2 is one of the few games that I play that support DLSS4 while not supporting the latest FSR4, forcing me to use NIS instead. It’s kind of hard to decide if it’s worth paying such a big price premium for a 5070 ti just to play PoE 2 with a superior upscaler.

So my question for anyone who has a 9070 xt and is using an older gen cpu, possibly a 5700x3d or 5800x3d, how has your performance been in PoE 2 now in the past months now that the drivers have matured?
