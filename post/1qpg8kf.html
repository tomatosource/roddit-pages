[Charania] The Milwaukee Bucks are willing to wait until the offseason to trade Giannis Antetokounmpo if their price of a blue-chip young talent and/or a surplus of draft picks is not met.

Source: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/47755088/sources-giannis-antetokounmpo-ready-bucks-trade-amid-offers

Multiple teams have received a sense that the Bucks are more open than ever on Antetokounmpo offers between now and the deadline, league sources said. However, Milwaukee has indicated to interested teams that the organization is not in a rush to complete a move and is willing to navigate Antetokounmpo’s future in the offseason if its believed price point of a blue-chip young talent and/or a surplus of draft picks isn’t met, sources said. By waiting until the summer, the Bucks could also see which teams are able to offer more appealing draft picks in June.

Because next season essentially serves as an expiring year on his contract, it increases his ability to position where he wants to be dealt. Any team willing to pay the steep price in both players and draft capital to acquire him would want to know whether he is committed for the long term. If traded in the offseason, Antetokounmpo would have to wait six months from the point of the deal to sign the supermax extension.

Milwaukee has tried to operate as a buyer in the trade market but has not moved forward with a transaction due to the uncertainty around Antetokounmpo’s future and the team’s limited mobility to make a major move. Forwards Bobby Portis and Kyle Kuzma are the main names the Bucks have discussed in trade talks while keeping their one tradeable first-round pick (2031 or 2032) off the table unless it is moved for a no-brainer superstar, according to sources. At the draft in June, the Bucks will have their pick that night plus the 2031 and 2033 selections to move in trades.
