Kevin Gausman is the first pitcher in MLB history to retire 16+ straight batters twice in the same postseason

Gausman is the first pitcher to do a 16 batter streak twice in the same postseason. Unfortunately for him, he took a loss in the first game and is on the hook for a loss in tonight’s game.

Pitcher Game Streak Length IP FIP K
1. Kevin Gausman LAD@TOR Oct 25, 2025 17 5.2 1.37 5
2. Kevin Gausman SEA@TOR Oct 12, 2025 16 5.1 1.26 5

Only two pitchers have ever had 2 15+ consecutive batter retired streaks at all in the postseason.

Johnny Cueto had a 15 batter and 19 batter streak in the 2015 playoffs (the 15 batter streak coming in game 2 of the World Series, where he pitched a 2-hitter). His Royals won the WS.

Don Gullett had a 16 and 17 batter streak, but they were split over 2 seasons. One in 1975, one in 1976. The Reds won the WS both of those years.

Source: Most consecutive plate appearances with an out by a pitcher in postseason history, in one game
