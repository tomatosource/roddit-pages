Breaking down how good Catha’s Blessing for Spirit Walker is for Companion’s Damage

For those that never played Tactician, you wouldn’t know how Added Damage to minions actually works in PoE 2.

Catha’s Blessing gives 60% of Main Hand weapon as Added Damage to your Companions.

How good is it? It depends.

Let’s look at how good it is on 3 different minions: Skeletal Warrior, Skeletal Reaver and Skeletal Brute.

Reaver has the highest Attacks per Second (1.2) and Brute has the Lowest (.57).

Meanwhile, Brute has the highest Base Damage and Reaver the lowest.

Let’s assume that your weapon grants 100 Physical Added Damage to them (The “Juicy Spear”). Let’s assume 3 scenarios. Gem level 1, Gem level 20 and Gem level 40. Yes, I know it’s technically not possible to have Gem Level 1 of all of them, but PoB has the numbers.

Brute has:

Gem Level 1: 2.2

Gem Level 20: 151.1

Gem Level 40: 3070.1

You will notice how hard the damage scales with level. It’s 68.6x from 1 to 20 and then 20.3x from 20 to 40. But, what about the weapon?

For Brute, it works like this:

Gem Level 1: 26.4

Gem Level 20: 64.5

Gem Level 40: 102.2

You will notice how the damage barely scales with level. It’s 2.44x from 1 to 20 and then 1.58x from 20 to 40. That means that the weapon is this much more damage:

Gem Level 1: 1182.4%

Gem Level 20: 42.7%

Gem Level 40: 3.3%

As you can see, it goes from very OP to absolutely useless. Of course, this is worst case scenario. Let’s look, instead, at Warrior.

Gem Level 1: 0.8 / 26.4 / 3350%

Gem Level 20: 37.7 / 29.3 / 77.7%

Gem Level 40: 810.5 / 90.9 / 11.2%

Still very bad. But what about the Reaver?

Gem Level 1: 0.5 / 26.4 / 5025%

Gem Level 20: 24.7 / 27.8 / 112.9%

Gem Level 40: 445.3 / 82.3 / 18.5%

Still quite bad, but much better. Now, the biggest issue is that you will be sacrificing at least 2 Minion Levels if you are wielding a weapon. So, is it worth it? At a shitty 100 Added Damage, never. 2 Minion Levels would beat it on every gem level past 20.

Bottom line is, Low Damage + High Base Attack Speed = Good. High Damage + Low Attack Speed = Bad.

But, of course, if you are taking that node, you aren’t going for the “Juicy Spear”. You will go for a 2H Mace. You WILL need to go Giants Blood, so you can wield a Scepter on your off hand.

There will be 2 Maces we will be able to use. Early/Mid/Late game Mace and our GG Mace.

Our Early/Mid/Late is Chober Chaber. Why? Two reasons. First is that it can have +3 to Minions Level. Second is that it’s level 33, which means it can be upgraded. Upgrading gives a significant amount of damage. We don’t know how much, but it’s around 80 to 130, based on what we saw (Hrimnor’s/Amor Mandragora/Double Vision). Once upgraded, with a Vaal Orb to add a third Socket to add Increased Damage, at 20% Quality, it has around 481 Damage. Let’s call it the “Juicy Hammer”. It will require 180 Str and 300 Int to use it with Giant’s Blood though. This Hammer is so much better than the “Juicy Spear” it’s not even funny.

Another option, if the 3 Gem Levels don’t give you enough, is a Marohi Erqi. You will need 717 Str to use it though. Good luck with that.

The best Mace to use is an Ironwood Greathammer. It “only” needs 3 T1 Physical and T1 Reduced Attribute Requirements. It also needs to be Exceptional and Sanctified. That way it gives around 900 Damage. It also “only” requires 315 Str.

Finally, keep in mind that Boss have HIGH DAMAGE and LOW ATTACK SPEED. So this node will be quite terrible for them.

TL;DR: If your weapon is a “Juicy Spear” and your gem level is low enough, it’s worth it (off handing a Scepter). If you have enough gem level, it’s worthless unless you have a REAL weapon.
