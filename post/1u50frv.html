A Game Designers Thoughts on the Current Endgame

Hey, I want to preface this post by saying that I’m relatively new to PoE 2 (1000h) and have played every league and I absolutely love the game. It’s up there with my favorite games of all time. It’s also totally possible that I just am not understanding certain mechanics regarding the endgame so I’m happy for anyone to point out if I’m doing something wrong and that’s what’s leading me to feel the way I am about some of the content. Also if anyone is curious I’ve worked on Predecessor (Omeda Studios) for 6 years, so the genre is very different but some principles still apply.

I’ll just hop right into some of the content that I have issues with, if I don’t mention some content its because I haven’t engaged enough with it to form an opinion or I don’t have issue with it at all.

Minor Content

My opinion is that in general, regardless of the type of content a player is encountering, they should be happy that they have come across that content and want to do it. This doesn’t include players that are hyper-optimizing their play obviously as I know metas will always form and some content will be less valuable than others.

Strongboxes

Outside of Researchers and Cartographers boxes, I’m not really sure why I should open the other box types. Unless I am using a T16 Waystone that is Irradiated or the box has + to level, I don’t think I can get level 82 bases for rerolling, which makes almost all of these boxes pointless to open.

My suggestion would be to make magic/rare boxes be treated like magic/rare enemies and just spawn everything at +1⁄2 so when a player is in a level 80 map they will get a little pocket of 81⁄82 enemies making the box automatically worth doing for the bases alone. I’d also have the box itself just drop items at +1⁄2 of the area level respectively.

Essences

Outside of the Greater Essence of Opulence, and the corrupted essences, it seems like the rest of the essences are pretty pointless once you leave the first tiers of Waystones and are looking for higher tier mods. Also I’m a bit confused as to why some of the perfect essences seem worse than their greater counterparts. As is, I’m regretting putting points into the essence nodes in the atlas.

Here I would suggest changing the essence system to function similarly to the standard greater/perfect currencies. Have the lesser essence guarantee a certain modifier with the higher tiers of essence increasing the minimum tier of that modifier, and make perfect essences WAY more rare than they currently are as they would be competing with the top tier standard currencies. The goal would be to create the same awesome feeling that you get when you drop a Perfect Exalted or a Perfect Chaos Orb.

Furthermore I think there could be an interaction here with Strongboxes where the player could apply essences to the boxes which would then guarantee (or increase the weighting) of a certain class of mods on every item that comes out of the box. For example, using an Essence of Flames would add “Monsters Deal x% of Damage as Extra Fire” to all monsters spawned by the box, and every weapon coming out of the box would have a higher chance of having flat fire damage on it.

Spirits

I think the concept of spirits is really cool and I was excited to play with them when Dawn of the Hunt dropped, but I think they’ve fell pretty flat. I love the +50% movement speed that they gave the spirits in this latest patch but I think it doesn’t really solve the core issue with spirits.

I think the core issue is that the player has no agency over this type of content outside of the Hilda Atlas Passive that directs them to other content a little better than the baseline. The problem however is that a player might be running through a map and encounter a spirit that has targeted some thing they missed or go off in a random direction that the player wasn’t going to go. I think this makes the mechanic actually feel negative for the player as it can now feel like its a missed opportunity so they either have to waste time back tracking or just lose out on the content entirely.

I think maybe the player should be able to collect the spirits as they encounter them and the player themselves would become possessed making all enemies they encounter take on the properties of the spirits they are holding. You could then give the player a button to release all the spirits making them target the nearest piece of compatible content, like a box or a boss.

Summoning Circles

As is, due to the time it takes for the bosses to spawn in, and the poor rewards that bosses give, I don’t really see a reason to engage with this mechanic.

Maybe the player could open an inventory window on the circle and modify it to make it harder while dropping additional rewards.

Rogue Exiles

I love them, great minor content, cool concept, tons of rewards. I just don’t like that they can drop corrupted gear. Inventory space is already very cramped, if it’s an issue that they drop too much, I’d rather encounter less of them and have no gear corruption.

Major Content

I might have a misunderstanding in general about the major content in PoE 2, so I’ll lead with this and am happy for people to explain it to me if I’m getting it wrong.

My opinion is that in any given game in general, the player should be incentivized to engage with all of the content the game has to offer. Not forced, mind you, just incentivized.

It seems like the way PoE 2 is set up, players should target one specific type of content and just do that. Whether its the meta content that all the creators are targeting, or the players preferred content, it seems like you should generally use 2⁄3 tablets of the same type + irradiated if necessary, and hard focus one piece of content.

I thought GGG were attempting to fix this before the latest hotfix to Hilda’s Ancient Inscriptions Passive (was +25% to tablet modifiers per type of tablet slotted, now +8%) but with the latest change running 3 different tablet types (+24%) now is only slightly better on average than Jado’s “Partial Translations” Passive (+0-40% to tablet modifiers, +20% average).

I’m the type of player that really likes to try to engage with all the content and so I enjoyed feeling like I had a passive node that was tailored to my playstyle of content mixing, to which I now feel incredibly discouraged. It was awesome to uncover a city and get to run all content with 4 tablets.

I have a feeling that incentivizing players to target grind one piece of content increases the likelihood of burnout and eventually churn till next league.

Radiation

This could just me a me issue, but I don’t understand why irradiated tablets need to exist. It seems to be a gating mechanic to force players to use an expensive T16 Waystone if they want to get an extra tablet slot but I think there muse be a better way to allow players to experience more content per map while still gating higher leveled loot. I also don’t understand the difference between where map level 79 vs 80 vs 81 is important outside of farming bases and the obvious XP difference.

Abyss

I love the abyssal content, both the map content and crafting. I think there might be a bit of a missed opportunity here though. It doesn’t seem like there is a good path to level 100 in PoE 2 (maybe I’m missing something here) and I think Abyss could be that path. I think its cool that when you jump into an Abyssal Depths it increases the area level by 1. Maybe there could be some way to keep going down the Abyss and every level down would increase the area level by 1, up to a point, with the specific purpose to be the way to level to 100.

I know that the grind to 100 is supposed to be ludicrous. I play a lot of D2R and I felt like they struck a really good compromise with the terrorized monsters up to 96, would like to see something like that here.

Rituals

I think this is a great mechanic, only one small issue that I have is the massive pool of uniques in the shop that are bad. I’ve been playing for 1000h and still get anxiety when hitting the reroll button that I might be leaving something good on the table. Maybe just reduce the pool to a set number of uniques so that I can quickly learn which ones are worth skipping.

Delirium

I think I preferred a mix of old and new Delirium. I don’t like being pushed down rails where if I backtrack to TP to a summoning circle I just lose my Delirium. I do however really enjoy the multi-map, charge up to 200% mechanic, and love the concept of Grand Mirrors, very cool.

Breach

I’ve loved the evolution of Breaches both mechanically and thematically, no complaints there.

Vaal Temple

My main complaint with Vaal Temple is that I don’t think a player should have to use an external tool to know which rooms can connect to other rooms. It’s very counter intuitive. If it’s necessary to gate this for balance purposes, then maybe a legend with symbols where matching symbols can connect rooms or something would be nice.

Overseer

I feel like I must be missing something here but bosses feel extremely lackluster in rewards and I’ve tried to increase it by running some Overseer Tablets with Hlida’s “Soul Eater” Passive to increase their effectiveness after clearing the whole map first but they just feel bad. It feels like a chore that must be done to map complete and not the highlight of the map or even a desirable piece of content.

Conclusion

I’d like to thank GGG for making one of the best games I’ve ever played! I’d love to know some of this communities thoughts on my post and definitely feel free to let me know where I’m just misunderstanding mechanics as I’m sure that’s the case.
