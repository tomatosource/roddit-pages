Current one shot design of end game bosses completely ruins Hardcore

I love to play PoE2 and I only ever play HC but knowing I have to fight a boss like Arbiter of Ash with frequent fast reaction one shot mechanics that I can’t mitigate at all just completely ruins my vibe to the point I really am thinking what is the point in playing?

&nbsp;

One shot mechanics like this should be locked to stuff like Uber bosses. Not locking a large chunk of regular endgame content behind them like Arbiter of Ash now does.

&nbsp;

I think the only way I’d stand like more than a 5% chance of beating Arbiter 1st try is basically tailoring my entire character to nuking bosses ultra fast… the exact thing the devs said they don’t want us doing and a play-style I don’t enjoy.

&nbsp;

For the love of god GGG, think of the hardcore players ;(
