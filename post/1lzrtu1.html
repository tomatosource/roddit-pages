Reshuffling all the NBA team nicknames

  • New Orleans Jazz - bringing the music back to the Big Easy
  • Utah Raptors - in tribute to the state’s rich paleontological heritage
  • Toronto Timberwolves - a nod to the Algonquin wolf native to Eastern Canada
  • Minnesota Lakers - the land of 10,000 lakes
  • Los Angeles Magic - an ode to the greatest point guard in the game’s history
  • Orlando Wizards - for all the wizardry happening at the nearby Disney World
  • Washington 76ers - the perfect nickname for the city named after George Washington
  • Philadelphia Warriors - classic name for an NBA pioneer
  • Golden State Nuggets - keeping the gold rush going
  • Denver Trail Blazers - for all the hikers across the Rocky Mountains
  • Portland Pelicans - Oregon teams and water bird mascots
  • Chicago Grizzlies - to go picnic basket hunting with the Bears and the Cubs
  • Memphis Kings - remembering two all the Kings associated with the city: Elvis and MLK and BB King and Jerry the King
  • Sacramento Hornets - borrowing Sac State’s longtime mascot
  • Charlotte Thunder - at least they’re not the Bobcats
  • Oklahoma City Bulls - rodeo yeehaw
  • San Antonio Suns - for all the shootouts at high noon
  • Phoenix Hawks - keeping the birds in the valley
  • Atlanta Heat - Hotlanta
  • Miami Clippers - built on the back of the Carnival Cruises empire
  • Los Angeles Cavaliers - a nod to Zorro’s roots in Pueblo de Los Ángeles
  • Cleveland Bucks - for Ohio’s state animal, the white-tailed deer
  • Milwaukee Pacers - for the Milwaukee Mile, the oldest operating motor speedway in the world
  • Indiana Pistons - perfect team name for the home of the Indy 500
  • Detroit Rockets - named for the Rocket 88, the first ever muscle car
  • Houston Mavericks - for the noted political maverick Sam Houston
  • Dallas Spurs - just keeping with the city’s cowboy theme
  • Boston Knickerbockers - why not another Boston team named after a piece of clothing
  • New York Nets - bring back the red white and blue basketballs while you’re at it
  • Brooklyn Celtics - a tribute to the wonderful 2015 film directed by John Crowley and written by Nick Hornby
