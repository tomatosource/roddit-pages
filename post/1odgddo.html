All of the top 10 longest shots are WRONG [Analysis]

Graphic of updated shot distances: https://imgur.com/a/CRUXJTb

Preface

This quest started when Jokić made a 3⁄4 court shot against the Kings last year and I saw it was officially recorded at 66ft. Some fans immediately pulled out an image of court dimensions and did some Pythagorean theorem and saw this was incorrect. My calculations had it around 72-73 ft, which immediately made me skeptical of Baron Davis’s legendary, record-holding 89 ft shot because there was no way he was 16ft further back based on the video. Thus, I decided to build a little desktop app that let me click anywhere on a court and figure out the shot distance. What I found had me up until 5 am, feeling like I had just uncovered a vast conspiracy.

All of the top 10 longest shots are wrong.

Turns out, bad record keeping on long shot distance is pervasive throughout NBA history. Some shots don’t adjust for vertical distance off the center line, some don’t account for the backboard being 4ft off the baseline, most don’t factor in that the center of the hoop is another foot forward from that, some are just bad spotting of where the shot was.

Methodology

I went through the video of each shot and recorded the furthest point forward the shooter was still touching the ground when the ball was released, AKA, their leading foot. This is the standard for shot distance. This means people that ran and leapt forward can get a bit of an advantage via their air distance compared to, say, Planinić, who just water polo’d it.

The end point for a shot is the center of the net. The distance is just Pythagorean theorem.

Limitations

My estimates of a shooter’s front foot placement are not perfect. I went through videos frame by frame and referenced whatever marking on the court that I could. I estimate these should be within ±1 ft of the true distance.

Norm Van Lier. I could not find ANY videos or images of this shot. I barely found anything describing the actual shot. I checked Internet Archive, If you have them, please let me see them. The only account I have is from NBA.com: “Van Lier, standing in the Bulls’ foul lane, picked up a loose ball and heaved it in the other direction”. I put him about in the middle of the foul lane. This shot is recorded as 84 ft but does not appear to factor in the hoop being 5ft off the baseline. It’s probably 76-80 ft depending on distance from center line as well.

I only checked the official top 10 shots plus Jokić’s shot. Others are probably wrong, which means some of the latter entries on this list might not be in the real top 10.
