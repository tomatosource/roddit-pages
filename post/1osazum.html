Does it make sense for GGG to update POE1 and POE2 at the same time?

As titled.

The goalposts with Poe2 have been consistently moved, delayed, etc. Now we’re up to not every class when the game launches, and essentially no real timeline at all. Druid was teased as being almost ready when early access released, and then the design philosophy for the game changed and (my guess) the druid was too slow and methodical for the way the game changed. Ergo it seems like Poe2 moved speed wise to be more inline with POE1. On top of that, we get bombarded with features from POE1 that are “needed” in 2, and things like Abyss come in. That’s great, but from a game design perspective it doesn’t make sense to me to make two copies of the same game; and keep updating both games. In my view, POE2’s goal posts have moved so far off target that I genuinely can’t tell if there’s a clear direction they are going forward in. When you get a class that’s touted as nearly finished at early access launch, and your product doesn’t align with the current plan when you go to implement it and you have to have 8ish months+ of delay just to get that back on track I can’t help but imagine that time pushed every other thing back as well. We still have spells and abilities in the game that are very much suffering from the effect of the shift from “slow methodical combo driven” to screen clears, to the point where some of these just aren’t even viable for much of anything.

It really seems like we’re not going to go for a full launch until some time in 2027 or longer, and we know we’re not guaranteed all classes at launch. It seems like based off what I’ve seen that having both games being consistently updated is muddying the waters between them, and making it hard for GGG to keep a hold of a specific design philosophy and it’s exacerbating already lengthy delays.
