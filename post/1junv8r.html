Approx. 69% of the league was born after Pop was hired as head coach of the Spurs

Based on the Malone news there were some posts about the longest tenured head coaches. Pop was hired as head coach of the Spurs in 1996.

Looking at player bios sorted by age on NBA.com: https://www.nba.com/stats/players/bio?dir=D&sort=AGE

You can see that 171 players were 28 or older (i.e. meaning they were born in 1996 or earlier). With 565 active players listed, that means roughly 394 of them were born in 1997 or later.

This is roughly 69.7 (~70%) of the league. Insane longevity from Pop to be around this long.
