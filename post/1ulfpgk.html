Just want to share how I made this ring

https://preview.redd.it/ez815jh0xsah1.jpg?width=662&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=a7f599879656c9a248d8be9c5349ce53246f63e1

Estimated cost 300~500 divs.

  1. Buy fracture base or fracture one yourself
  2. Slam breach essence, then add atk catalyst to 40%
  3. Spam chaos until you get one t1 flat you wish for
  4. Slam 1 perfect Ex with omen for prefix, if you hit other t1 flat you want then proceed to desecrate. Otherwise spam whittling with greater chaos until hit t1. If it hit undesired t1 prefix that has the same mod lvl as your first flat t1 then 50⁄50 anul. If failed return to step 3. If you hit the T1 res suffix that has mod lvl 82, you could use breach essence with omen of destral crystalization to target that suffix and make it into lvl1 mod, then spam whittling again.
  5. After having 2 flat t1 mods, you just need to slam desecrate with omen guarantee prefix, ideally use the ancient collarbone.
  6. Slam a perfect exalt with greater exalt, if one of the 2 suffixes hit any T1 ele res, you slam it with the swift alloy (no omen needed here cuz all affixes are full), it’s 50⁄50 for you to remove undesired suffix and retain the t1 res. If you didn’t hit any t1 res then anul with omen for suffix, then slam perfect exalt again fishing for any t1 res, then 50⁄50 with the alloy.
  7. Slam the void flux to change the t1 res into t1 chaos res.
  8. Reveal the desecrated prefix with abyssal echo, spam omen of light until you get the last t1 flat, then it’s done.
