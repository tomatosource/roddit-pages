I have a question about Hinekoras Lock, its rarity and its usefulness…

Ok hi everyone. As the Title states I wanna talk about Hinekoras Lock.

The question is - is pricetag and rarity justifiable by its effect. Easy answer: Yes! But … is it?

The Lock is a fair deal, the 1% get toys to foresee one crafting outcome to make Mirror Tier Items (Or Mirror² Tier lul) and the average joe gets alot of Divs if one is lucky enough to find a Lock.

Let just pretend Locks drop more often and the pricetag would be 50Div. What’s the difference? Mirror Tier Items are crafted more “easily” or more cheap and the average joe gets 50Div now. Would the average joe now consider using a lock for his own crafts on not mirror tier gear. Probably not.

Ok what if the pricetag is 20 Div. Mirror Tier Items are basically free for the 1% and the average joe only gets 20 div. But more frequent than the 500 Div drop in a lifetime. And with a pricetag of 20Div maybe the average joe considers to use one lock to foresee one crafting outcome. And probably is broke afterwards.

Hinekoras Lock is strong. We do not need to discuss that. But is it for the average joe or just the 1%? Would you rather have toys to tinker with or a chase item drop which makes you rich but you’ll probably never encounter? Have you ever used a lock and what did you use it for? What would be a good price FOR YOU to consider using a lock?

I never used one and probably wouldn’t have considered using one for my crafts. Maybe for Weapon corruption but this wouldn’t result in Mirror Tier Items if I look what I craft.

This is not a post about current state with locks = bad … It’s more like “what if…” I’m really curious.
