Favorite NBA Passer? Entertainment Wise.

As the title says, it doesn’t matter if they had the most assists or were statistically great.
I’m asking based on purely the entertainment factor.

I personally love seeing assists or passes more than a 3-pointer, not saying 3-pointers are terrible though.
My first exposure to flashy passes was Magic Johnson, because my dad told me that when I asked if he knew any NBA players. He said Magic was good at passing, but I didn’t know “that” kind of passing back then.

Then I learned about Jason Williams, John Stockton, and Steve Nash. Then current ones like Steph Curry, Nikola Jokic, Lamelo Ball, and, surprisingly, Lebron James. I think Tyrese Haliburton does passes too.

Magic is still my OG fav, though honestly, anyone who can do flashy passes or assists is automatically in my favorite list.

Edit: Wanted to add Jason Kidd and Mark Jackson
