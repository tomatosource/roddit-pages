Diablo4 Ex-Pat

This game is awesome. If you’re a D4 player looking for a bit of intellectualism in your ARPGs this is the place. Playing Mercenary class and it’s about the coolest ARPG mechanic I’ve run across. Looking forward to trying other classes. Also, taking a ton of time with campaign as there are an incredible number of ways to affect your character. By no means a power player but wanted to drop a note for fellow bored D4 players. Suspect it will take multiple play through to really understand and am not anywhere close to the endgame.
