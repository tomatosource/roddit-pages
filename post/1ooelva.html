What’s your best tank-focused trade ideas?

For those who still are holding out hope for a deep playoff run, this thread isn’t for you.

I’m more interested in getting ideas from the fans here who think 35 wins and a possible 8th seed/1st round exit is a bad position to be in. This team is still probably too good to really bottom out.

Goals of tank trades:

- Set the team up to fail so they can maximize their chance at a top 5 pick
- Pick up blue chip prospects they can focus on developing this season
- Potentially shed additional long-term salary

A couple ideas of my own, but I’d love to hear yours:

Spurs: Prior to the draft Dylan Harper was the one guy people here seemed to reluctantly admit was worth moving Brown for. Castle as well, but at this point the Spurs would probably keep Castle over Brown even if they were paid the same. Harper is a fairly “can’t miss” prospect. He’s been coming off the bench (even though D’Aaaron Fox has been out), but he’s looked terrific early on. 14 points, 3.8 assists, 4 rebounds with 50%/36%/83% shooting in 23mpg. Per-36 of 21.6 points, 6.2 rebounds, 5.9 assists. He’s out weeks with an injury.

In theory the Spurs could immediately contend with Fox, Castle, Brown and Wemby. Spurs fans hate this idea, though. They see Harper as untouchable and don’t want Brown’s contract. But there’s a couple ways it could work. One would include Vassell (27 mil) but given the Spurs likely already see Harper as untouchable, hard to see that happening. Harper + Barnes expiring (19 mil) + Keldon Johnson (17 mil) also could work financially. If Keldon Johnson is also seen as too valuable, they also have a 13 mil Kelly Olynyk expiring contract they could throw into the mix. You’d probably just buy out Olynyk and Barnes.

Super unlikely, though. Spurs are far better off just building around Harper, Castle and Wemby

Rockets: They still need a starting guard with Fred VanVleet injured. Many like Bill Simmons have discussed trades centered on Derrick White for Reed Sheppard (last year’s #3 pick). I imagine White’s trade value is dropping now that he’s getting exposed early in this season as someone who can’t be a #2 option, but he proved the last few years that he can be a perfect 4th option on a team like the Rockets alongside Durant, Sengun and Thompson.

My favorite iteration of this deal would involve both White (28 mil) and Simons (27 mil) going to the Rockets with us taking back the injured VanVleet (could end up our starting PG next season), Sheppard (21 year old we could spend the season developing) and Jabari Smith Jr (22 year old who just signed an extension). Smith Jr has a poison pill contract that makes moving him complicated, but this is totally doable if a 3rd team is involved and Rockets move a guy like Capela to a team with a trade exception. That would actually shave 10 mil of Boston’s books this season since Smith’s contract pays him 12.3 mil this year. Rockets would have White, Simons, Thompson, Durant and Sengun as their starters. This trade can’t be done until December 15th.

Anyone have interesting ideas you’d consider? Cooper Flagg hasn’t exactly lit the world on fire yet. Is it time to try and get Nico to take a player who better fits their Kyrie/Davis/Klay window?
