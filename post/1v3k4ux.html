[Scotto] The Washington Wizards intend to hold extension talks with star big man Anthony Davis. Golden State has attempted to trade for Davis, but Washington has rebuffed their inquiries to this point.

Source: https://www.hoopshype.com/story/sports/nba/2026/07/22/nba-intel-anthony-davis-trae-young-jonathan-kuminga-benn-mathurin-cam-johnson-miles-bridges/91007110007/

While re-signing star guard Trae Young was the biggest splash for the Washington Wizards this offseason, several items remain on their checklist. 

With fellow star Anthony Davis extension-eligible August 6th, the Wizards plan to have extension talks with the 33-year-old, 10-time All-Star forward, league sources told HoopsHype. 

The Golden State Warriors have expressed trade interest in Davis. Yet, Washington has rebuffed such inquiries to this point, putting a major wrench in Golden State’s dream scenario of pairing Davis with free agent target LeBron James in the Bay Area.
