I just did my first Simulacrum and it was so much fun.

Went in blind, no tips, no YouTube guides, just pure excitement and a wand in hand.

Some waves took place in really tight spaces, which made things tough but thrilling. The loot was solid, and the eerie voiceovers added a haunting touch.

I was able to finish all waves but died a few times as after resurrection I kept respawning over 20 baddies!

Way more fun than running maps. Can’t wait to jump into another one.

Good job GGG.
