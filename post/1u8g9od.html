Just did the math… Now that omen of corruption is gone, demands for locks are about to hit the stratosphere

Realized recently that with omen of corruption gone, there’s 1 less potentially good outcome from corruption/sanctification. Before, corruption with an omen of corruption had a different outcome to corruption without one. This means with each lock, you can get either a good sanctification, 1⁄3 for another soc with an omen, or 1⁄4 for another soc without an omen. Now that the 1⁄3 is gone, you have to use way more locks to guarantee a good corruption/sanctification. Here is the math for a bow/spear (these are the most popular for high end crafting right right now)

Previous math:

soc with omen: 1⁄3 = .333

soc without omen: 1⁄4 = .25

+4 -&gt; +5 sanc: (4x1.2-4.5)/((1.2 - .8)4) = .3⁄1.6 = .1875

Chance of getting a good sanc/corruption: 1 - (1-.333)(1-.25)(1-.1875) = .593 or 59.3% chance

95% confidence interval: (1-.593)^n =.05

n = ln(.05)/ln(1-.593) = 3.33 locks

New math with removal omen of corruption:

soc without omen: 1⁄4 = .25

+4 -&gt; +5 sanc: (4x1.2-4.5)/((1.2 - .8)4) = .3⁄1.6 = .1875

Chance of getting a good sanc/corruption: 1 - (1-.25)(1-.1875) = .39 or 39% chance

95% confidence interval: (1-.39)^n =.05 n = ln(.05)/ln(1-.593) = 6.06 locks

TLDR: so on average last league, it took 3.33 locks to guarantee a good corruption/sanctification 95% of the time on a spear or bow (slightly less for wand if you are going for +5 -&gt; +6). This league, it takes 6.06 locks to do the same
