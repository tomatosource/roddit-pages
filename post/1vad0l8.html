Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame players who were drafted by an NBA team and never played for any other NBA franchise.

Player Team that drafted him NBA career
Bill Russell Boston Celtics* 13 seasons (1956–1969)
Sam Jones Boston Celtics 12 seasons (1957–1969)
John Havlicek Boston Celtics 16 seasons (1962–1978)
Larry Bird Boston Celtics 13 seasons (1979–1992)
Isiah Thomas Detroit Pistons 13 seasons (1981–1994)
Magic Johnson Los Angeles Lakers 13 seasons (1979–1991, 1996)
David Robinson San Antonio Spurs 14 seasons (1989–2003)
John Stockton Utah Jazz 19 seasons (1984–2003)
Reggie Miller Indiana Pacers 18 seasons (1987–2005)
Tim Duncan San Antonio Spurs 19 seasons (1997–2016)
Kobe Bryant Los Angeles Lakers** 20 seasons (1996–2016)
Dirk Nowitzki Dallas Mavericks** 21 seasons (1998–2019)

Important draft-day exceptions

  • Bill Russell was officially drafted by the St. Louis Hawks, but his rights were traded to the Celtics on draft day before he played an NBA game. He spent his entire NBA career with Boston.
  • Kobe Bryant was officially drafted by the Charlotte Hornets, but was traded to the Lakers before his rookie season and never played for Charlotte.
  • Dirk Nowitzki was officially drafted by the Milwaukee Bucks, then immediately traded to Dallas before playing an NBA game.
