Made a little 3-5 min game for y’all, ahead of game 7! I’ve been a lurker here for years and finally found a way to contribute.

Hey all, 

I made a silly little thing called FLOP or FOUL and figured this community might get a kick out of it.

https://happydistractionsstudio.itch.io/flop-or-foul

It’s a short, dumb, hopefully funny loop with juicy buttons, confetti, a smug ref, and a streak counter that quietly rewards you for accepting that justice does not exist. Built in Three.js with the full crunchy PSX look vertex wobble, low-res pixels, fog, the works.

I’d love if you gave it a spin and told me what made you laugh, or what felt off. I’m still tweaking the flops, so any “make it worse” ideas are very welcome.

Thanks for taking a look!
