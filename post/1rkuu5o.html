Optional campaign skip per season should be implemented

This is coming from someone who praised the developers for how well made the campaign was, and felt the need to tell my friends about how PoE 2 campaign mogs every other ARPG I’ve played in my life.

That being said, if you’ve fully completed the campaign for that specific season you should be able to make a new character without going through it all over again.

I’m okay with seasonal resets and starting from scratch to do the campaign again. This makes sense and is totally balanced, but it transitions from balanced to tedious when it comes to alts or just making another character within that season.

The only reason I’ve not leveled a monk is because I don’t want to slog through the mandatory spirit upgrades, or the backtracking which has lost its charm the 50th time around.

There really isn’t a 2nd or a 3rd reason. That’s it. The only obstacle between me and continuing to play the game is the fact I’m just over doing the campaign 500 times, especially considering the Act 1-3 cruel I’ve been through during the first season.

Just let me keep those spirit upgrades and mandatory powerups from the campaign on my second character, and let me level however I want.

I’m open to it, but I’m so far not understanding how this quality of life change would have any downside whatsoever,, or lead to someone playing less that season, because it would lead to people like me playing more than they otherwise would have.
