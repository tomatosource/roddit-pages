GGG unhappy with trigger skills - but it points to an underlying issue with GGGs POE 2 philosophy

So, I am basing GGG being unhappy with trigger skills (CoC, CoA, etc) on their interview with ZiggyD, where Jonathan expressed discontent with the trigger skills and them being a tad overpowered.

On the surface, I can get behind this, after looking at poe.ninja and seeing a bajillion comet spam builds that all more or less look the same.

However, I think there is a deeper reason why trigger skills are so heavily used.

The POE 2 devs are REALLY high on a combo system for the game - as in, using moves to set up other moves. Sometimes, these moves flow well together, like some of the monk skills for instance.

But other times, and I would argue MOST other times, it’s clunky, time consuming, and hard to manage when in the thick of a tough fight, especially in situations where you need about 4 or more different skills to to maximize damage output and also still have to focus on skill placement with a moving boss (looking at you, infusions).

To put it another way - a few years prior to POE 2, Mageblood was added to the game because players were tired of playing flask piano. Now in POE 2, we’ve moved on to playing “skill” piano.

The trigger meta gems help alleviate this by automating some of the processes to make it feel a lot smoother. That’s not a trigger gem issue - that’s a design issue.

It’s one thing to have skills feel clunky in Act 1 and most of Act 2. But by mid act 3, things should be smoothing out, and by the time you reach act 4, it should be more of a slow process of ramping up rather than still dealing with clunky skills and combos.

I sincerely hope GGG revisits all the skills, makes the ones intended to combo to have smoother transitions, and gives new ways to make the gameplay less combo-reliant, so that players feel less pressured to turn to trigger skill gems to fix the problems of poor game deisgn.

(For those who need a specific example, level a witch or sorc with Ice Nova. I was ok with the clunky nature in Act 1. Once Frost Bomb was online, it got better, but even with snap and Siphon Elements, suddenly things started feeling worse in act 3 and 4. It always was clunky due to having to wait on charges - something ok for early acts, but not when you are trying to ramp something up).
