Bricked Spirit skills (and some equipment)

This has been a problem since .2 for me personally but i saw threads about it at launch too.

If you are unaware sometimes it seems that spirit skills that trigger other spells (CoC, CoEA, CoMD) can get bricked and say that it is missing a supported skill even if you have one equipped. The same thing happens with some equipment, my stormweaver has a completely bricked Adonia’s Ego that i can no longer use due to this bug and caused me to restart a whole new char since that wand was a ton of divs and cant be easily replicated .

After loosing my 4th lvl 21 CoMD (9d each) i started doing some testing and i can reliably get the gems to brick. For me any time i take out my Grand Spectrum Emerald (+2% Spirit) and than socket it back in it bricks my CoMD skill gem and there is no saving it. This makes it incredibly frustrating to play with my passive tree to try different things. Thankfully the gem isnt bricked for other people so i can still sell it to get my divs back, but still very frustrating. This bug still bricks the skill if its activated or not, even if you deactivate all your spirit gems as long as its in your skill list it bricks… i THINK what is happening is when i unequip the jewel there is a single frame on my spirit counter where it reads 0/0 than pops back up to 807⁄807 and the brick is occurring during that single frame, could be wrong but thats what ive seen…

All this to say that i now (and you should too) unequip any trigger skills anytime i mess with my passive tree.
