0.3 is great but the game has core issue with making any kind of melee engagement extremely punishing/unfun and abyss only emphasizes this

I think GGG made many great decisions while tweaking stuff for 0.3 - Act 4 is masterpiece (good luck with A5 and A6 as it’s gona be hard to deliver something better than A4 currently), crafting is more engaging, drops are better, passive tree is more interesting/fun, sprint is great (and I do not mind it’s clunkiness), support tier levels are great.

But one thing that bothers me very much is how punishing is going toe-to-toe with enemies and engage in short-range melee combat, especially in endgame and with abyss league mechanic.

Getting anywhere near mobs is asking for trouble - you either kill it from half-screen afar or blast whole screen with AOE.

That’s also why Lightning element is so op as it’s core mechanic allows to clear screens faster (shock damage + jumping between targets/AOE). On the other hand any kind of CC like freez/stun is… useless - I can freeze/stun rare mobs in half a second but it still does not give me any opening because there is so much shit spawned around that still need to incorporate hit and run tactics. Generally you should not stop moving at any time as there is so much stuff spawned on ground, so there is no time to just land few melee hits. Also the visibility is a huge issue - most of the time I can’t tell what exactly killed me. On top of this we can get interrupted/min-stun on hit which very often leads to super-unfun feeling where even hit and run mechanic can backfire if you get hit and iterrupted by some trash mob.

And abyss mechanic just emphasizes those issues - there are so many deadly AOEs/Auras combined with amount of mobs and their speed, particle effects, lazer-beams, exploding bombs etc.

I have no idea how it could be fixed with the current game design but at least it would be great that if we CC rare it should disable their aoe’s/affixes (or clear all AOE dmg effetcts in a radius around CC’ed mob) to give a window for engaging in melee combat. Or give to melee characters some significant bonus to damage resistance on hard CC depending on distance between character and cc’ed enemy.

But it would probably also make range classes life even easier. For me personally freeze/stun is a great opening for landing damage and it’s working very well for most bosses (there are some that still spawns too much stuff) but with clearing map/abyss it all seems to be usless.z
