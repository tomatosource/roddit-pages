Performance : can we talk about it ?

I’m on xbox series x, and I keep running into the same wall every league. I’ll start fresh, progress through the grind, make it to juiced maps… and then I end up walking away. Not because I’m bored, but because the game performance just falls apart ,sometimes dipping to 15 FPS right in the middle of the action. It really kills the experience.

And yes, I know it’s still technically “early access,” but after two leagues in, it feels fair to expect performance to be in a more stable place. Especially since other parts of the game (like the shop) seem to function flawlessly.

I feel like optimization has become one of the biggest problems in modern gaming. If we, as players, don’t give consistent feedback about it, it often gets pushed aside in favor of other priorities. That’s why I think it’s important for the community to keep talking about performance issues and not just accept them as “normal.”

I love the core of this game, and that’s why I want to see it actually run smoothly on the hardware it’s advertised for. Performance matters and I really hope the devs can make it a priority moving forward.
