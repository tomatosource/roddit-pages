Multiplayer is lacking something

I tried group play for the first time the last few days. Campaign, mapping, trials of chaos and sekhema.

Out of all of these the trial of chaos felt the best. With campaign coming in second place.

Trial of the Sekhema felt the worst because only the leader profits from the relic effects. This makes it borderline unplayable in group play, or at least not fun.

Mapping is the most complex to analyze. It feels alright to jump in with a friend and help them for a few maps. It feels a bit lackluster in some areas like rituals where only the host can buy items. But the other mechanics feel alright. The biggest gripe I have with it is that there is no shared sense of progression and exploration because only the host can see the atlas. The other person feels too much like a blind passenger being taken on a run. That makes it feel flat very soon. Another problem is that the open map design that feels great in single player gives players too much opportunity to separate in multiplayer.

Easy quick fixes to mitigate the biggest problems would be to at least let the guest use their own relics in the trial of the sekhema and to allow the guest to at least see the atlas map in mapping so the two players can at least strategize together where to go next.

In order to make mapping together big time fun we need much more shared agency. One idea would be to create new limited size shared atlases that a team of players can complete in a day or in a week with a big final reward at the end. Just 10 to 50 maps with a meaningful final encounter at the end. Maybe have a 10 map wing for each league mechanic and a pinnacle boss fight at the end of each wing. After doing all wings you fight the arbiter (or a special boss). Make the maps inside this special multiplayer atlas a much higher percentage of more linear maps like crypt or bluff or bloodwood, to help guiderail players into playing together. Entry could be handled via a group waystone that “contains” the shared atlas. You find the item and share it with a friend. Sharing “breaks” the waystone in two and once broken it can no longer be traded. Each player engages with the group atlas by putting their half into the map device. The waystones can be taken out again and reinserted later so that you can break up your collaborative play into many sessions spread over many days. The group keystone only break once final boss has been defeated and the reward collected, or if the maximum number of allowed deaths has been reached.

TLDR Just a quick review of my personal multiplayer experience and some ideas to improve it.
