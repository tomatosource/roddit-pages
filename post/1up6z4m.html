While the trade return was underwhelming, this team can be a regular season behemoth.

I’m firmly in two camps: the piece (Paul George) that came back in the trade devoids me of excitement and it doesn’t really matter because this team still has the skeleton of last year’s winning machine.

&nbsp;

We can’t take anyone seriously who thinks that our team will fall to the play-in but I’m almost leaning into the other extreme. I wouldn’t be shocked if this team maintains its streak of being a top 2 seed in the East with a defense that threatens to be #1 league-wide. We were top 5 last season and are replacing Vucevic minutes and some of Garza ’s minutes with Mitchell Robinson. Tatum is one of the best off-ball defenders in the league and can cover up any possible mistakes from the overzealous young wings.

&nbsp;

Your mileage may vary on the offense, especially as it pertains to the playoffs, but hopefully at the press conference later today our president Brad Stevens lets us know how the perimeter-oriented Paul George helps us with rim pressure. I doubt we get any hint on the next roster move this soon but we know Podcast P is going to be a lightning rod for his entire Celtics tenure.
