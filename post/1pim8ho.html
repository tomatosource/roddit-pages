MS penalties on base items suck

In Tris recent interview with game director Jonathan Rogers, Tri asked abouut the hidden MS penalty on Body Armour (and Shields) and why that is higher on Str items. Jonathan said that he likes the fantasy of a heavy plated knight being slower, but tanky as hell, but I see 3 major problems with that power fantasy:

  1. Mace and Xbow already are the most immobile feeling weapon types(Xbow doesn’t have any movement skills on their own). Adding the increased movement penalty of Str items on top of that doesn’t help with that feeling whatsoever
  2. Life based builds do offer a lot let defensive than just going into ES for a way bigger hitpool and similar recovery. Being very tanky, but immobile is just a fantasy and has nothing to do with the implementation of Armour right now. Especially in comparison to current ES balance
  3. The cool fantasy element of heavy and tanky knighs IMO is the fact they are stationary, can’t be pushed around and can’t be stopped in their movement either, but that also isn’t the case as you will still experience plenty of body block by minions and knockback like every other character as well

My personal solution(s): Introduce PC pushiness and let it sclae with stats like Strength or remove the increased MS penalty for Str based Body Armours and Shields
