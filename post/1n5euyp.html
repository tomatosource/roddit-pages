Frost sorc feels amazing

With all the doom and gloom I keep hearing about sorc I wanted to share my positive experience.

I’m currently in act 4 and am freezing and clearing mobs quite fast. It’s very enjoyable to watch them pop after a big freeze. To me almost all the frost skills seem useful and mixing is quite fun.

The main clear comes from Ice nova and snap that generates frost infusion making ice nova stronger. I run increased aoe, more freeze buildup and echo as supports on the nova. Sometimes I also use frost bolt to use the novas from further away and block mobs and projectiles with frost wall.

For single target I just use frost darts, frost bomb and elemental weakness. I don’t even need the infusion on frost darts to do good damage. Just make sure the target is either chilled or frozen (usually by frost bomb that also provides important elemental exposure).

In the tree I have focused on more aoe, increased cooldown recovery, increased elemental damage, increased energy shield, increased elemental exposure and increased mana recovery. Oh and increased frost damage of course.

I also use chronomancers sands of time skill that gives so much more aoe and cast speed although in fluctuating intervals. I love it.

The most difficult part for me has been some of the act 4 bosses with a lot of frost resistance even though elemental weakness and elemental exposure do help a lot.

I hope this helps some of you struggling sorcs. Do note that this is not a counter argument to any issues that spells are facing. I just wanted to share my positive experience and some tips.

I don’t currently have pob but I’ll try answer if there are any questions.
