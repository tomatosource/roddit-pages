Is picking up rare items outdated ?

Hi all,

I’m relatively inexperienced in PoE but farmed 0.1 a lot and now 0.3.

0.1 had very low deterministic crafting, so even in late end game I picked up high tier items on the ground, because good tiers on key affixes + 1 or 2 lucky exalts could net a nice profit.

For example, I picked up ALL wands on the ground. If I got a combination of some nice affixes like a &gt;+4 spells, T1 mana (it was the everlasting gaze amulet meta), cast speed, %spell damage etc. even if the item was not perfect it could still make some profit. If it didn’t, I combined 3 to get a new one and try again. I particularly liked all kind of rings to do this kind of stuff.

Now I don’t see the point of trying to improve these rares  ? Realistically, these “not so perfect” items, good for characters not in late end game, are achieved through crafting quite easily so that’s a lot of work for not so much gain. Even “good rolls” I got are now outshined by more controlled crafting from ground up. 

So, should I still pick up rares ? Is my old method outdated ? Or should I adjust it to keep it relevant ?Thanks !
