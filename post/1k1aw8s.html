Boss Feedback - Arbiter of Ash: If GGG wants a slower and more methodical gameplay, this Boss is unironically the worst offender against their own vision.

So yeah, I’ve finally beat the boss, took me about 50 minutes to get the hang of it, and I literally had to remove survivability to gain more damage so the fight wouldn’t last long before he does the fast movements around the arena, making it hell for characters lacking MS and outright burst damage.

I haven’t had troubles with other bosses up to this point, since I think this is the only boss that has MULTIPLE one shot mechanics, that is incredibly unfriendly to people who build defenses and play slower.

I feel like what they should have done is make it so that his one shots just deal incredibly high amounts of damage, but not the ridiculous amount that instantly kills your character outright, and also, they should honestly keep the Camera zoomed out in this instance, (Not sure why the camera zooms out at first then zooms back in, for cinematic effect I think?).

Anyways, atleast I got a pretty good unique out of it.
