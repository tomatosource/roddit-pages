Alert: ED/Contagion is great this league

I’ve enjoyed playing ED/Contagion for years but it always fell off endgame. I remember sitting there for 10 minutes on Drox waiting for him to die. But in Poe2, in this league, it’s great. The fact that you can get so many extra gem levels on ED (which is where it gets most of its power) and Dark Effigy is just enough for that extra damage you want sometimes makes it feel great. Lich is a great ascendancy too with a lot of options on how you want to use it. I see most people going full ES and taking advantage of the no HP while you have ES node, but I’m doing armor/ES with a shield and doing fine. In any case it’s not set in stone how to use the ascendancy which is refreshing.

Beat the arbitrator last night and never felt like the damage was low. It’s a fun and different play style too where you do have to land the ED but once you do you know everything is going to die. Really great for Abyss and you can clear the second wave pretty often with the same contagion you had on the first. Very satisfying to just watch a bunch of mobs die.

It’s not as fast as the fastest Meta build, but it’s fun to play and I felt like the progression was the most steady I’ve ever had for a build in path of exile. My power level kept up slightly ahead of the mobs throughout without any hiccups.

Just an alert for anyone that’s enjoyed ED/Contagion in the past and is looking to start a new build. Highly recommended!
