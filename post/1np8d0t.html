Guaranteed Minion+3, Spirit + 47 amulet, cheapest 100% crafting method here!

    • Perfect Essence of Enhancement - to remove that suffix and generate prefix (20-30%) increased global defences (3 Ex)
  3. Add a Amanamu prefix desecrated modifier. We will 100% get Minions deal (15–25)% increased Damage if you’ve Hit Recently
    • Omen of Sinistral Necromancy - (9 Ex)
    • Omen of the Liege - (Almost free)
    • Preserved Collarbone (6 Ex)
  4. Check your amulet, it now has 3 prefix, and no suffix
    • +47 spirit
    • (20-30%) increased global defences (3 Ex)
    • Minions deal (15–25)% increased Damage if you’ve Hit Recently
  5. This means the amulet currently only have the “damage”, “minion”, “defense” tags.
  6. Use homogenising greater exalt x2
    • Omen of Homogenising Exaltation (95 Ex)
    • Omen of Greater Exaltation (2 Ex)
    • Greater Exalted Orb (3 Ex)

    • Amulet cost (15 Ex)

    • Omen of Sinistral Necromancy (9 Ex)
    • Omen of the Liege (Free)
