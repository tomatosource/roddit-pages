The Knicks lost last night because of the Brunson minutes, specifically because he and KAT can’t play together

Despite white hot shooting for the Pacers in the 1st quarter of game 4, where the Knicks truly lost this game was in the 3rd quarter. In the second quarter, the same lineup that saved the Knicks in game 3 fully brought New York back into game 4. The one major change being OG on the floor instead of McBride.

In the second quarter, the Knicks were +7 with Shamet-Wright-Hart-Anunoby-Towns on the floor. This lineup in 12 playoff minutes (albeit very small sample size) has a NET rating of +29.2

This isn’t even the best Knicks lineup we have seen in the playoffs either. Brunson-McBride-Anunoby-Robinson-Hart/Bridges have actually been better lineups in much larger sample sizes.

So what do you think Thibodeau did in the 3rd quarter? He ran nothing close to these combinations of players, instead opting to give us 12 minutes with little more creativity than this busted ass Knicks starting lineup that is currently -7.3 in 321 playoff minutes. To take it a step further, Brunson himself was a -10 from when he checked in at the 4:53 mark in the 2nd to when he checked out at the beginning of the 4th.

Trailing by 15 in the 4th, Thibs once again put his Shamet-Wright-Hart-Anunoby-Towns… for a whole 2 minutes. And then subbed Brunson right back in the game. The Knicks made a valiant effort, but just couldn’t come up with enough stops down the stretch to pull out another win.

Three Knicks finished the game with a positive +/-:

Wright +11 Shamet +6 Towns +3

For the Knicks to be successful, it’s becoming clear that Brunson and KAT can’t share the floor for long stretches of games. Opening and closing with them is fine, they are easily the two best offensive players the Knicks have. But the two of them are just too easy for a team like Indiana to exploit on the defensive end.
