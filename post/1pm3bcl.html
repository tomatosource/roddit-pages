The state of the game balance as duo party

I’m playing duo with a friend and we’re fairly experience players in the genre and the specific game.
We’re both able to finish the whole game in around 6hrs of relaxed play.

We typically oneshot bosses and fly through campaign.

However, if we play duo, the campaign becomes insanely harder. It’s not even fair. It’s scaled to the point where even with very good play and synchronization and combos, you finish mana flask charges and the boss is still at half hp.

As a comparison, the same boss in single player has around 350k hp, while in duo it has around 1 million hp.

How are you expecting us to deal 500k hp in act 3?

It took us 12hrs to finish campaign, struggling, using all of our resources, farming and overleveling.

The game is totally unabalanced for duo play. There’s not much synergy you can do to overcome such hp scaling.

Furthermore, the temple of vaal just doesn’t work in party. The endgame is totally lackluster for party play. Not to mention the trial of sekhemas where my sorcerer friend with 12k honour can lose 1k to some damage and I instantly lose the trial because I don’t have that much energy shield as I’m an armour or evasion character.

The game is punishing group play and this might be a very big reason why it feels unnaccessible to a lot of new players.

Imagine now new players wanting to play with friends and thinking this insane campaign scaling is expected and normal. It’s not. It’s hard even for experienced players.

I’m writing it here because it’s a very big problem for me and my friend but also for the growth of the game.

I would love to see some attention to this post.

Thank you

-—-

EDIT:

Furthemore, you also gain less loot as duo players. You get 50% extra loot, but you have to share it by 2. So 150% loot becomes 75%.
