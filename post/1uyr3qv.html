Denver is seen as an increasingly likely landing spot for former Grizzlies and Kings coach Dave Joerger.

The Stein Line first reported in late June that Joerger had interviewed with the Nuggets in their search for a more experienced top assistant to head coach David Adelman.

Paris Basketball, whose front office is headed by former Timberwolves lead executive David Kahn, was seen as the top threat to Denver after so openly chasing Joerger to be its next head coach, but a BasketNews report from Europe suggested that the French club has struggled in its bid to lure Joerger away from the NBA.

I’m told that Paris Basketball has also pursued former Raptors and Suns coach Jay Triano for its head coaching vacancy after notable recent stints there for Memphis’ Tuomas Iisalo and Chicago’s Tiago Splitter. Triano, though, committed instead to join Portland as the top assistant to new Trail Blazers coach Micah Nori.
