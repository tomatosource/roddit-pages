Crossbow loading/reloading - the more I think about it the more it seems unintended to work the way it does.

Think of it like this, you have two separate ammo types, you empty one clip, switch to the other and empty that one, then when you switch back you load in the empty clip, and then immediately re-load a new clip, and then it occurs AGAIN, when you switch to the 2nd ammo type again, and forever and so on…

After your first two clips of each, this is how switching ammo goes the rest of the time:

shoot - load - reload - shoot - load - reload - shoot - load - reload

Why the heck do you load empty clips upon switching ammo types? Doesn’t it make more sense to go:

shoot - load - shoot - load - shoot - load

Just give us a full clip every time we switch. It’s essentially the same as naturally reloading a full clip anyways. I get that the mercenary isn’t on the intelligence side of the tree but c’mon… this just seems odd, no?

I can’t help but think it’s not supposed to work that way because it just seems kind of counter-intuitive to purposefully design it to behave like it currently is.

What do you guys think?
