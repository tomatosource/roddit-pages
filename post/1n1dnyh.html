The implications of the new Witch Hunter change are hilarious

If you didn’t hear, witch hunter gets 100 weapon set skill points, which is 80% of a skill tree. So you can allocate effectively and entire second tree for your offhand. This is hilarious to me.

You’re a fast rare mob and you see this exile. He’s got a crossbow. You run at him thinking melee range might be the way to go. And he fuckin infinite mass punches you into the stratosphere… Pulls out his crossbow and keeps going.
