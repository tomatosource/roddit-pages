The implications of the new Witch Hunter change are hilarious

If you didn’t hear, witch hunter gets 100 weapon set skill points, which is 80% of a skill tree. So you can allocate effectively and entire second tree for your offhand. This is hilarious to me.

Picture this:

You’re a fast rare mob and you see this exile. He’s got a crossbow. You run at him thinking melee range might be the way to go. You get into melee while he’s attacking your minions… and he puts away his crossbow. His fist clenches. And he fuckin infinite mass punches you into the stratosphere… Pulls out his crossbow and keeps going.

Or you’re fighting a spear guy, he break distance, pulls a two handed mace out of nowhere and drops a goddamn hammer of the gods on you.

Edit: the question is, will witch hunter still be mid?
