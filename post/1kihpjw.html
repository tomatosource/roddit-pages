Does melee “strike” skills feel mechanically awkward?

I’ve tried to make some home brew melee strike builds for fun. Thorns Armor break, Fangs of Ice, and Boneshatter to be specific. They are okay, nothing great, but strike skill in this game feels awkward mechanically, at least in my opinion.

I’m not sure how to explain it but maybe it has to do with the animation, but a forward hitting strike (especially one that moves your character forward during the attack) feel super awkward. Often times my character just attacks air, even when surrounded. It’s really funny with Armor Break cause rather than that “break noise” you literally get a whiff noise.

There’s also times if I click a mob to hit I’m locked to this forward animation that occasionally makes me hit past them. I know that if you click close enough you can strike without moving (this is only with Strike), but when clicking a mob, it feels like there’s a delay on where the mob is and when you click sometimes making your character attack forward and just hit air. Very fast moving mobs seems to be just the bane of these kinds of strikes - i detest those stupid machines that fire a bow at you and keep moving.

The funny thing is, with default attack mace strike, because it’s a wide swing not a forward poke, this problem doesn’t seems to exist.

Am I the crazy one here? Does any other Strike sufferer notice the same?
