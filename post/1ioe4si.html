How do you keep grinding?

Hi,

I played PoE2 for approximately 150hrs. I am running a chronomancer:

  • farming t15 maps with moderate to low stress.
  • my gear is worth a few divines and I have like 7 divines left (I suspect that the only way to upgrade is to completely revamp my gear and that would cost me 50d or more)
  • I got tired of farming fragments and I simply bought citadel fragments, breach stones and killed the bosses. I still need to kill the ritual and the expedition one.
  • farmed some sekhemas and chaos trials, killed these bosses as well.

My chrono is relatively good. However, I need to actually be careful while playing (positioning, knowing which monsters can get me off screen etc.). I am exploding frost walls while clearing and I am using CoC comet for bosses. Being able to freeze the boss, go back in time for mana reset and to slow the hell out of the enemies proved to be rather safe for most things. I can’t play mindlessly though.

So my problem is: Getting to citadels is not fun at all. I am not really bothered by random spawns. Instead I am bored AF trying to reach the damn citadel (wired paths, towers to run, one accidental death being a huge let down). There are very little things that add friction which keep piling. ( for example a map containing a bridge has some damn pots blocking it. If I don’t see the damn pots the monsters can swarm me)

Farming splinters or simulacrum sprinters on my own is just a chore: find towers, add tablets, run map, get a few splinters. Since my chrono is actually a slow paced character, it can’t explode the whole screen in 1 sec. I ended up spending some money getting the atlas points to increase the farming speed.

Trials are fun in a way. I really linked them as a concept. I don’t get why they have to be so tedious. You can run sekhema for like 1 hr and with a little bit of luck you get nothing.

I thought about getting a new character, but I am waiting for the reset. I don’t want it to catch me with a lvl 60 char.

So, how do you guys keep grinding?? Randomness + friction + punishment + unreliable way of progressing + a relatively slow build. I am a casual, so I play a 1-2 hrs a day a few times a week. Sometimes I play longer on the weekends but that’s rare.

LE: I had one citadel drop 2 most expensive fragments. I bought the other two and when I entered the boss arena, I had some unknown disconnect issue and lost the map. :(. My hard earned exalts.
