Unhinged Expectations

Alright everyone, 0.5 is getting close and the community is buzzing with theories, expectations, and wild predictions. But there are a few things I’ve noticed that I think are worth pointing out.

Yes, it’s been around 6 months since the last patch, and expectations have naturally grown. We also know this is officially a “big patch.” That said, I think it’s important not to let expectations spiral out of control. If we set the bar unrealistically high, even a very strong patch could end up feeling disappointing.

Looking at what people are expecting, I keep seeing multiple new classes, entirely new league mechanics, and even new acts being thrown around.

Now let’s take a step back and look at what we’ve actually been shown.

  • The first teaser clearly points toward a new endgame system.
  • The second and third teasers look like updates or reworks of existing mechanics (Expedition, Delirium, etc.). (To be followed with Breach prob tonight)

So here’s the key question:
If a new class were coming, wouldn’t that be the thing they tease instead of a reworked Expedition mechanic? Almost certainly yes.

Same logic applies to new acts.
If we were getting a new act, would they show Delirium… or tease the new act itself?

Exactly.

TL;DR:
We are being teased with a new endgame and reworked mechanics.
We are not being teased with a new class or new acts.

And that’s completely fine. This can still be a massive patch. Reworking old systems, tying them together with story, and overhauling the endgame is already a huge amount of work.

I’ll go even further: we might not even get a brand-new mechanic. If there was one, it likely would have been teased already instead of focusing on Expedition.

My expectation for 0.5:

  • New endgame system
  • Reworked existing mechanics
  • Temple adjustments and integration
  • Maybe 1–2 new ascendancies

Still big. Just not in the way people are overhyping it.

PS: I’d be very happy to eat my words.
