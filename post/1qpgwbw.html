The UX is terrible and needs a dedicated UX/UI team to work on it.

Pretty much what the title says. The UI in this game is SO opaque in so many areas and it fees like the documentation on how certain aspects of the game work is severely lacking. It feels like a lot of it is technical debt by the team who just dropped down quick internal solutions so they could roll out a playtest product and those quick solutions have turned in to a permanent answer incorporated in the live product. Like the Block and Rage bars are basically hidden UI elements. The Block/Stun bars are very difficult to notice and once you are aware of them, the block bar has no numerical feed back and the rage bar’s numbers require you to mouse over the mana orb. Then different unique have different symbols in the corner of their name plates. like all the ‘Vaal’ unique have this little face demon thingy, but the difference from a standard unique is so tiny it’s almost unnoticeable.

I noticed last night that despite wearing 35% move speed boots my move speed was sitting somewhere around 29%. My roommate goes oh it’s because armour has a movement speed penalty, but that’s not written ANYWHERE on the armour and the in game info box on armour doesn’t say it either. He just replied ‘oh it was that way in Path 1 so most people just know that now’. 🤯😤

Many spells and attacks don’t have proper tooltips, like god help me if I want to see what kind of damage my minions are doing, and I know it’s a beta but I also know that ‘temporary’ solutions have a tendency to become permanent. I know there’s a lot of back end math but I shouldn’t need to reference a third-party site and a community wiki to fully understand how the damage calculation works for a given spell. (and even then still have most of that be speculation)

To me it feels like GGG doesn’t really respect players time and expect you to learn how everything works through trial and error rather than creating clear systems and proper documentation on those systems. Many aspects of this game’s interface feel like you’re playing a kafka-esque version of Where’s Waldo, trying to spot the single difference the UI hud or notice one specific keyword in a description (“more” vs “extra” vs “increased” comes screaming to mind) and given the single use nature of most of the resources in this game, learning through trial and error becomes very expensive in terms of in-game currency and real life time played.

Beyond just the ‘old man yells at cloud’ nature of this post my questions are: 1) What are the odds GGG will address any of the issues with the UI? 2) Does this game support any sort of 3rd party ui mods that could provide this information better?
