I wanted so bad for the Jays to win, but what I got out of these playoffs is worth way more

I’ve been a Jays fan since I was a kid. I was 10 when Joe Carter hit the home run in 1993. I remember so much from that night. Who I was with watching, what my Mom made for dinner that night (lasagna), everything pretty much.

Fast forward to 2025. I’m a father of 3 teenage boys who are at the point in their life where their time with friends is extremely important, so we see them less and less around the house. They know how much the Jays mean to me, so they all decided to watch Game 1 against NY with me. We had a blast!

Then Game 2, a pal I hadn’t seen in a year messaged me out of the blue asking me to come by to watch.

Then another old friend for Game 3, then my boys again for Game 4.

This trend continued for every single game. Even my wife was getting into the games!

Throughout the playoffs, I got to watch 7 games with my sons, ended up seeing 4 friends that I haven’t seen in a long time, and over a dozen other current friends and acquaintances. We shared great food, cold beers, cheered together and ultimately cried together. I wanted so bad for my sons to experience the same joy I did in 1993, but the quality time I spent with friends and family is something I’ll never forget.

Thank you Blue Jays and Dodgers for an unforgettable World Series

But mostly, thank you Blue Jays for providing some of the most exciting playoff baseball I’ve ever seen for 3 straight rounds and getting so many families and friends together throughout Canada. That was so much fun to watch.
