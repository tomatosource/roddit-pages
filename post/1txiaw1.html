My 4th Trial Ascendancy Ruined me. Warrior Rant.

Running a Titan build with Sunder and the 4th Ascendancy ruined the whole game for me.

Was doing pretty great and went on an Escape Trial of the Sekhemas. My Sunder did not traverse over the stairs. I got interrupted and body blocked, ending my run.

That just made me realize: a whole week of grinding for an average gameplay experience.

The staircase interaction was just the thing that finally made it click for me. It feels like I’m constantly fighting the game instead of the monsters. Tight corridors, terrain, body blocking, attack animations, having to stand still, mechanics that seem designed around not being in melee range in the first place…

Am I the only one who feels like melee is just miserable compared to everything else?
