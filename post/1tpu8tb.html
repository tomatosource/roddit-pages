I know people will get confused by it, so here’s a recap of the league mechanics

GGG has repeatedly called this league a crafting league, but I think a lot of people are going to be confused by the different types of crafting that got added in so I wanted to write up a quick guide.

Here’s what I mean:

  • “Runecrafting” refers to “Remnant” encounters, the thing you’ll actually do in maps. You Runecraft by aligning a series of Runeshapes based on the existing Runeshapes and the remaining available slots. In practice, you will walk up to the encounter, pick the reward you want from the list, then kill some monsters. These monsters can drop Verisium, and these can exist within Expedition chains and encounters.

“Runeforging” is a new crafting bench type thing, where you drop an item in the slot and hit the button. This one interface will do the following.

  • The first, main use for runeforging is to add Ward to armor, which just costs Verisium. For bases above 55, you gain even more Ward from Runeforging, but at the expense of the other defensive values on the armor.
  • The second use case for Runeforging is if you add a low-level unique armor or weapon to the bench. This will bring the base type of the unique up, and from patch notes, it seems it’ll be brought up to level 55. It also seems that this will require an additional resource, but they didn’t share specifics.
  • The third use case for runeforging is specific to certain uniques, almost entirely Ezomyte or Kalguuran uniques. This changes them in some bespoke way. Some get certain parts reforged (Serle’s Grit had one modifier they showed with multiple versions of it you could re-roll), or changed more dramatically than simply adding ward (Brass Dome got a ton of Ward instead of a ton of Armour). Worth mentioning that high level unique armor without a specific interaction will simply get Ward at the expense of their base defenses, just like any other item.

And then the misc. section:

  • Alloys can drop, and they function like Perfect Essences.
  • Certain socketables that are going to function as crafting modifiers. We have examples of new mod pools, +1 suffix, +1 jewel socket, etc. These won’t be able to be removed or replaced.
  • Some stuff that will enhance existing runes. Because why not.
  • Fluxes for res swapping (and maybe for de-leveling gems? just got added to the item filter),
  • The rune that destroys a unique to gain a modifier.

I was getting confused, so wanted to write it all out. Hope it helps someone!
