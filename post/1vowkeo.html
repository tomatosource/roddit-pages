Christian Yelich Is Officially Having a Negative WAR Season

The Brewers primary DH who has played 85 games this year is batting .229, slugging .369, with an OPS of .680.

He has 8HR, 36BB, and 101Ks. Of his 75 hits 47 are singles. He has completely lost his power, and the Brewers have all but fully acknowledged he only plays now because he’s the biggest contract on the team.

The team with the best record in the NL is starting an objective, factual, and unequivocal anchor on their lineup because he’s a veteran who’s expensive. And with two more seasons before the mutual option that Milwaukee will obviously not take, it is likely to continue.

While the Brewers offense as a whole continues to be a problem, that will likely cause another early postseason exit, starting Yelich is nothing short of malpractice at this point.
