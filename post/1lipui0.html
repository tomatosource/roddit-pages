The NBA Draft: Who I think the Celtics could pick

Hi! I generally make these posts before the draft to help people understand prospects around the Celtics draft range/guys they could draft.

Potential needs are as follows: (obviously depending on trades that will be coming)

A shot blocking center/shooting big

A backup guard who can defend

A versatile wing

Name: Noah Penda F (Le Mans Sarthe)

Draft age: 20.45

Measurements: 6’7.25 without shoes 6’11.5 Wingspan 242lbs

Stats: 10.2⁄5.5⁄2.7⁄2.1 stocks 1.6 TO 27.3 MPG

Splits: 44.7⁄32.2⁄66.7 54.2 TS

Pluses: Penda is a wing who has positional versatility to defend multiple positons. Nice feel for the game makes high quality reads with the ball in his hands 1.62 A/TO ratio big time glue guy. High motor and always makes an impact. Good at drawing fouls with a 42.3% FTr. Great at cutting for easy baskets. Decent stroke off catch and shoot 36% from 3. Takes a steady diet of 3PT attempts. Wears the number 93 so he wouldn’t need to worry about a number.

Minuses: Inconsistent scorer especially in the half court. Confident in his dribbling ability, but not very efficient at all in ISO opportunities. Very poor FT shooter could potentially limit his upside as a shooter.

Comparisons: Nic Batum/younger Toumani Camara

Name: Kam Jones G (Marquette)

Draft age: 23.32

Measurements: 6’4.5 in shoes 6’6 wingspan 202 lbs

Stats: 19.2⁄5.9⁄4.5⁄1.7 stocks 1.9 TO 33.8 MPG

Splits: 48.3⁄31.1⁄64.8 55.1 TS

Pluses: He is a nice defender who will pressure the ball. Can finish well in the paint and at the rim. Despite the poor shooting from deep shoots 36.6% for his career from 3. 3.17 A/TO ratio this year as a primary playmaker. Relocates well nice as an offball scorer. (Forced into primary scorer this season)

Minuses: An average athlete to below average at the nba level which can affect his defense and offensive ability. Extremely low FTr at 16% for a player that drives as much as him. Forces bad shots from 3. Poor FT shooting under 70% for his career brings questions about long term shot ability.

Comparison: Donte Divincenzo/Andrew Nembhard

Film: https://youtu.be/c-2Rm9jTvtY?si=ClHw3ZousBax26Hr

Name: Ryan Kalkbrenner (Creighton)

Draft age: 23.42

Measurements: 7’2.25 in shoes 7’6 wingspan 257lbs

Stats: 19.2⁄8.7⁄1.5⁄3.2 stocks 1.5 TO 34.5 MPG

Splits: 65.3⁄34.4⁄68.1 68.4 TS

Pluses: Big bodied shot blocking big. Developing shooter. Very good screener. High floor as a backup center at the least. Elite in drop coverage. Uber efficient in the pick and roll. Good hands. Plays a lot of minutes. Elite efficiency in the post. Low fouls.

Minuses: Poor lateral movement can only play drop coverage gets cooked on the perimeter. Old prospect makes his ceiling a lot lower. Pretty poor rebounder when you factor in his size. Poor passer he’s constantly doubled and has extremely low assists.

Comparison: Zach Edey/Luke Kornet

Film: https://youtu.be/hYo-Lxc1iQY?si=mO3MAte7s2XCC_5e

Name: Ben Saraf (Ratiopharm Ulm)

Draft age: 19.18

Measurements: 6’6 barefoot 6’8.75 wingspan 199lbs

Stats: 12.3⁄4.2⁄2.8⁄1.4 stocks 2.7 TO 23.6 MPG

Splits: 45.1⁄29.6⁄75.4 52.5 TS

Pluses: High feel player with elite playmaker potential. Great at driving and manipulating the pick and roll. He has a nice first step and changes speeds well. Solid on ball defender for his position. Very good in the midrange and floater range.

Minuses: He is very left hand dominate. He needs to add weight to be able to function better as a driver at the next level only 31.7 FTr. Poor shooter from distance and very bad off the dribble from 3. Extremely foul prone due to bad gambles. High turnovers.

Comparison: Goran Dragic/Tj McConnell

Film: https://youtu.be/jAk8O523veo?si=z7k2vAWeoa6GHUGn

Name: Rasheer Fleming (Saint Joseph’s)

Draft age: 20.95

Measurements: 6’9.5 in shoes 7’5.25 wingspan 232lbs

Stats: 14.7⁄8.5⁄1.3⁄2.9 stocks 1.5 TO 31.3 MPG

Splits: 53.1/39/74.3 63.8 TS

Pluses: Elite wingspan and defensive potential. Guards all 5 positions. Elite shooter this past year on high volume. Efficient finishing around the rim. Ideal 3 and D role player. Great rebounder for his position crashes offensive glass hard. Great at cutting.

Minuses: Older prospect limits his ceiling. Basically no ability to create shots for himself extremely poor ballhandler. Played against low level competition. He shot poorly from 3 prior to this year. Needs to develop his defense mostly to be more disciplined.

Comparison: DFS/Jaden McDaniels

Film: https://youtu.be/kmvFnZ9ilnk?si=70w_aJ2r8NOmLN8m

Name: Bogoljub Markovic (Mega Basket)

Draft age: 19.94

Measurements: 6’10.5 barefoot 6’11.5 wingspan 213.2lbs

Stats: 13.9⁄6.9⁄2.6⁄1.7 stocks 2.7 TO 29.2 MPG

Splits: 54.6⁄38.8⁄76.5 64.2 TS

Pluses: Great floor spacer and elite at catching and shooting from 3. Moves well for his size. Able to attack close outs with decent ball handling for his size. Effective as a roller and a popper. Has nice touch in the paint.

Minuses: Most likely will be a below average defender. Cannot block shots. Poor wingspan for his position. Turnover prone when pressured in isolation. More likely a catch and shoot player versus a big that can win in an isolation. Bad lateral movement.

Comparison: Kelly Olynyk/Davis Bertans
