Small QoL changes?

While the next patch will be out “soon,” what are some easily implemented changes you’d like to see in the subsequent patch? The kind of changes that could be implemented without any concern about balance, art/thematics, or “vision, and are presumably not technically challenging (near-zero chance of causing bugs).

There are a two on my mind:

  1. Improve contrast for mod tier text and lines.

Maybe just me, but the dark bronze color on translucent background for mod tier text and the dividing lines between mod tiers is often really difficult to read. A lighter font color (say, gold instead of bronze, and boldface text and dividing lines) would be really helpful.

  1. Simulacrum and simulacrumbs auto-send to Delirium tab.

Simulacrum and simulacrumbs (splinters) currently get sent to fragment affinity tabs. Why? There’s a space for these in the Delirium tab, so that’s where they should go.

Any other tiny QoL changes you think could be implemented easily?

Edited to add from others:

  1. Improving the weapon set color contrast in the passive tree.

  2. Improving passive tree searched mode visibility (the faint blinking white border is often difficult to see).

  3. Auto-collect stackables. [FWIW, as nice as it’d be, this probably goes against the GGG philosophy of having too little friction.]
