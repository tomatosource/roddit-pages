Small QoL changes?

While the next patch will be out “soon,” what are some easily implemented changes you’d like to see in the subsequent patch? The kind of changes that could be implemented without any concern about balance, art/thematics, or “vision, and are presumably not technically challenging (near-zero chance of causing bugs).

There are a two on my mind:

  1. Improve contrast for mod tier text and lines.

Maybe just me, but the dark bronze color on translucent background for mod tier text and the dividing lines between mod tiers is often really difficult to read. A lighter font color (say, gold instead of bronze, and boldface text and dividing lines) would be really helpful.

  1. Simulacrum and simulacrumbs auto-send to Delirium tab.

Simulacrum and simulacrumbs (splinters) currently get sent to fragment affinity tabs. Why? There’s a space for these in the Delirium tab, so that’s where they should go.

Any other tiny QoL changes you think could be implemented easily?

Edited to add from others:

  1. Improving the weapon set color contrast in the passive tree.

  2. Improving passive tree searched mode visibility (the faint blinking white border is often difficult to see). [this will be addressed in 2.1… hope they give it a nice fix!]

  3. Auto-collect stackables. [FWIW, as nice as it’d be, this probably goes against the GGG philosophy of having too little friction.]

  4. Improve visibility of objects within blast radius of expedition charges, and improve visibility of circular blast radius indicator.

  5. Drop-at-end splinter mechanic for all splinters (for example, for delirium, including for emotional waystones)

  6. Apply distilled emotions the same way currency can be applied, just by right clicking the emotion and clicking the waystone to apply (why is there a separate distillation window?).

  7. Boss icon revealed from the start of a map.

  8. Ctrl + click to send a currency from inventory to the Alva window (as is possible in PoE 1 Settlers league).

  9. Have a customizable favorites tab in the currency exchange.

  10. Fix symbol overlap in minimap (for example, when rare monster symbols and ritual symbols are both far away, only the ritual symbol will show, with the rare monster symbol being invisible).

  11. Speed up the recombination animation.

  12. Show mod ilvl.

  13. Option to toggle off clickable keywords (helpful in expedition and simulacrum for example).

  14. Reduce overlap between stash/portal/initiation window in simulacrum (the stash especially is often hidden).

  15. Indicate suffixes and prefixes when holding alt (as is already done with the trade site).

  16. Icon indicating which weapon set is currently active.

And don’t leave the console folks behind!
