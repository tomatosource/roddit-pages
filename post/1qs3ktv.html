I’m worried for next league…

I keep seeing all these posts where people can’t believe how many divines and mirrors they were able to get.

I think people are going to be in for a reality check next league. The economy is so out of whack right now and people’s dopamine receptors are fried.

Don’t get me wrong, this league is a blast, but no one should expect this going forward or you’re in for major disappointment.

EDIT: I agree with a lot of you that this league’s economy was a great time and really fun. My point is more of a psychological one than anything. We’re all ARPG players so we have the same “number go up, brain happy” dopamine rush. Even though less divs will bring down prices of everything essentially making the same economy, I think it’ll still feel bad to a lot of players getting less drops. And I really don’t see any world in which the loot from temple doesn’t get nerfed.
