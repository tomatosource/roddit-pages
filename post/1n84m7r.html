Why nerf incindiary rounds/frostfire support?

This us a wider topic for the games balancing. I’m having a blast, but only because I’m playing something slightly off meta that still does the job.

If i was playing ignite I wouldn’t be. Ignite has been terrible since the release of the game and the pre release for 0.3 looked so promising, offering a buff for incidniary shot and frostfire support. It had so much potential…then they nerfed incindiary rounds max damage from 600% to 300% and frostfire magnitude from 100% to 25%.

That’s a 60% damage loss on a build nobody played in 0.1 till now and now, nobody will play again.

You’re meant to feel strong in this game. That build could barely do act 4 with a high elemental crossbow and I tried experimenting with high phys dps. It’s just a shame, please allow for more diversity.
