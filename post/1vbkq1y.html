The way that this sub and r/nba talk about this man ridiculous whether you like love or hate the trade, some of you guys are embarrassing. The way some Celtics fans talk about Jaylen Brown you’d think he’s been a plague on this franchise instead of one of the biggest reasons Banner 18 is hanging.
