Did GGG ever give context on changing Arc to be a projectile skill?

In 0.3.0 Arc changed from being a beam to being a projectile, but in my relatively quick searching I can’t see any commentary from GGG about why they decided to do that.

I ask this mainly because I feel like the change gutted Arc’s identity and fantasy. I’ve always thought Chain Lightning was a really cool spell concept and visual, going back to Diablo II’s Sorceress, various tabletop RPG iterations on the spell, and some awesome portrayals in film. PoE 1’s Arc is a really nice take on the classic spell, and to me it feels like it really delivered on the fantasy chain lightning promises, with a satisfyingly visceral feel to how it propagates through packs. The original version of PoE 2’s Arc was essentially the exact same spell, and it even fixed my only problem with the skill: that the visuals looked quite dated compared to the more recent lightning spells.

I’ve tried messing around with Arc a few times since the 0.3.0 change, but I’ve found the skill very disappointing in its current state. Power-wise I think it’s fine, but now it feels like I’m just playing a spell version of Lightning Spear, and it really doesn’t scratch the chain lightning itch that it used to. The projectile visuals don’t impress me as much as the chaining beam used to, either.

Anyone else feel a similar way?
