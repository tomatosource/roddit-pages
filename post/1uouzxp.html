I played Conductive Runes so you don’t have to - Build feedback

Hey guys,

The least played skill of all new skills is arguably Conductive Runes(as of this moment, I’m one of two players on SC trade playing the skill for more than utility, for the past week I was the sole one). I’d like to share my thoughts on it to provide insight to anyone considering playing the skill.

The skill is problematic to play around to scale because of several reasons:

  • It is very gem level sensitive. Early on, ~2 levels can double the damage of the skill. However, it is neither a melee nor a projectile skill, so most conventional levels are not available. Prism of Belief is also not available for runic skills.
  • It does not scale with your weapon damage at all, similar to Herald skills
  • It fires relatively small hazards in a very wide area, spread across randomly. Area of effect increases the cone in which they spawn, so you can hit very far and I mean 2 screens away far, but also less reliably against any single enemy.
  • The damage variance can be staggering; there will be times where you use the skill 5 times in front of an enemy and no hazards hit and there will be times where 5 runes overlap on a single cast and a boss explodes

It is also a very buggy skill:

  • Rearm chance does not work. Neither the support nor the rearm chance on tree, an equivalent of about 50% more reliable damage is just not working.
  • Delayed Reaction(70% less duration) also does not affect the skill’s duration, so you can’t really play around less duration either.

Some other interesting tidbits:

  • I ran Shaman to hopefully run around Ward Brass Dome which is insane for recovery with the Olroth’s Resolve flask, however I found the lack of mitigation too problematic. A lot of bonded modifiers for Shaman are insane for ward skills, in particular the recharging rune(25 flat ward regen), but there’s other clever means of restoring ward regularly such as Animus Exchange. Personally, I went 2 runemastered Mjolners for a ton of added levels and the lightning damage % implicit from one of the three Mjolner Runeforged variants.
  • Hazard AoE (present in bottom right of the tree) does not increase the cone area - it’s a very good stat
  • As a ward build, you need mitigation elsewhere than gear. I chose a high % phys as ele and stacking maximum elemental res with Lightning Coil, but feel free to also explore mind over matter/damage taken before mana approach.
  • For whatever reason, evasion/Energy shield have highest base ward on rares.
  • You can level with the skill pretty decently with a Widowhail bow and an Asphyxia’s Wrath quiver and a few Ward runes.
  • Despite the wide cone, if the cone is limited by a wall, the runes spread out in front of you - as long as they can squeeze into the cone.

My recommendation is to run the skill as either a standalone with Concentrated Area or go completely the other direction and stack AoE and offscreen enemies with cascading corpse explosions or/and heralds, while getting another setup for single target. For example, you can run MoM as Acolyte of Chayula’s and use Acolyte of Chayula’s chaos tornado for single target.
