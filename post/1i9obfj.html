Saturday NBA Quadruple-header of national tv games

Pretty cool thing the NBA is doing today, on the first non-football weekend day of the season.

Pacers vs Spurs in Paris at noon (ESPN)

Nuggets @ Wolves at 3 (ABC/ESPN/ESPN+)

Celtics @ Mavs at 5:30 (ABC/ESPN/ESPN+)

Lakers @ Warriors at 8 (ABC/ESPN/ESPN+)

You’ve got Wemby in France, rematches of the best playoff series last year (DEN-MIN) & the Finals, then LeBron vs Steph. Will be interesting to see how the ratings perform for these games! With no football going on, the NBA is taking a huge swing with these matchups. Should be a great day of hoops!
