I lost a Mirror due to a bug, literally.

There I was in a fully juiced T15 map. Was a few minutes into the map when I walked up to one of the new Verisium Remnants events. Upon killing the surrounding mobs, the tablet became visible and it was a NINE rune tablet. I was like, “hell ya, I’m getting a div for sure”.

Well, fun fact, one of the rewards from this event can actually be a Mirror. And somehow, I was lucky enough to hit it. It was the only option available.

At this point, I’m sweating my ass off clearing through what felt like 20+ waves of elites. The entire time, each wave had one of the mobs that is invulnerable until you kill every other mob attached to it.

Fast forward to the last wave and the only two mobs left are the big invulnerable elite, and one minion attached to it (pictured below). Except this minion had a special affix. Completely untargetable and unkillable. Which in turn meant that the main elite could also not be killed.

I cleared the entire map, including all events. Logged out / back in. Even tried dying once (I get one extra portal from my map champion person). I eventually killed the last boss with still no luck.

Eventually, I slammed the next key and gg.

The first mirror I’ve ever seen, and it’s gone due to a bug, literally.

Will I ever see another?
