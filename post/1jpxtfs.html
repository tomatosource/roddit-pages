The meaningful combat conundrum

I just ended watching the other day’s Tavern Talk with Jonathan, Mark, DM and Ghazzy and in absolute honesty I liked very much the way the questions and answers went but nevertheless I have developed this lingering feeling of perplexity about the devs not knowing in many cases what the solution to core problems of the game are. Let me explain myself.

In the context of reaching end game, if I understood correctly the end goal is to make the game slower or at least non-blast-through to get the sensation of every action matters, every decision mid combat is meaningful and purpose is the ultimate way to achieve success in the game although with a good power up through the endgame this dilutes as more power means more clearing speed but still this feels a hell of a good game to me when It is achieved, however, the speed of monsters is a problem and the measure of the maps is still a problem.

I personally think for the combat to be more meaningful you need more tools at your disposal to use in the different situations the game feels like is punishing you for following this ideal “combo oriented combat matters”, for example:

  • In the topic of monster speed, having sufficient AoE skills, auras, banners or adding a modifiable evade mechanic which puts down an AoE that slows monsters down, with enough downside for it to be only used in the most extreme situations. For this to work the norm should be that every monster has a similar speed to the player excluding a certain fast type of monster or a really dangerous one, but it is not okay to open a breach and have every monster on top of you almost instantly. I don’t want the usual freeze all screen solution and I hope the new pin down mechanic would make this function viable.
  • In the topic of bleeds not going through shields, implement a skill that sucks up monster energy shield, maybe with upsides like monster ES gets converted into mana, or downsides as it has X seconds cast or is channeling, which would’nt be a problem if you are able to strategize the combat by not having every freaking monster rush at you at mach 3 speed.
  • A bit overhaul on the evade mechanic, by adding gems that can get this evade mechanic some passives not related to movement like HP regen, almost all ailment cleansing, chance to get frenzy/power charges, etc… I don’t know really, but being the core of the survivability it deserves a bit more of love, and if you don’t use blink at least you have other ways to boost evade.

I love the game and I love the direction it intends to go (I’m in love with the ritualist) but If I am being overly simplistic or seeing problems where they aren’t please let me know. What do you think? I hope this small brainstorming of mine helps!
