Without trying to be a comedian, what do you think would be a good name for the Las Vegas NBA team?

Also add a color way that you’d like to see the team use.

&nbsp;

For me, I’ll try to stay away from the casino life. Corny ass names like Aces or Jacks or whatever. I’d have taken the Kings but that’s no longer available.

I’ll try to include all of Nevada in the thought process, since that’s where most of the loyal fans will be from. I’ll probably go with something like Las Vegas Oasis.

Then for the colorway, stay away from the blue-red combo. That’s way too overdone in the league. I for one am happy that the Wolves finally went back to their blue-green jerseys. I was advocating for the Mavericks to go do that, or any team in the league really, just so we can have some fresh color combo jerseys.

The Sonics will probably be back with their green-yellow, which is nice. I’m also happy that the Rockets went back to red-yellow. There’s also way too many red-black teams. Doesn’t help that Raptors went away from their purple-red and went red-black also.

For the Las Vegas Oasis, I’m going with Orange. Just orange, kinda like how the Celtics are just green. Keep it simple, keep it clean. Maybe orange-green if they’re gonna do a combo. There’s just not a lot of orange jerseys (main ones, not the gimmick jerseys) in the league. Just the Suns and Knicks use the color orange, and both as just their secondary color. I just want to see something new. Not a new shade of blue or red.

How about you?
