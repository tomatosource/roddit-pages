Whirling Assault strange mechanics

On the original Monk showcase demo, we can see that the Whirling Assault can be move around with WASD, and it’s also showcase on the recent ‘Breachlord Skill effects’ as well.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6-NcZw_Rh1Q

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xrBO3M9WKgo

The problem so far is that: It’s clunky and often or 50% of the time, it really missed the mark, while still heavily draining your mana.

Then I tried (and compare) the traditional mouse move - to point and click on where you want the Whirling Assault should direct (without touching the WASD) and surprisingly, it HITS the mobs way, way better than with the WASD.

Not entirely sure if this is just a coincidence on my part, or if the WASD mechanic might have been disrupting the ‘continuous’ attack pattern damage? (thus it tends to oftentimes missed the targets) but as of now, Whirling Assault is serviceable as long as I’m doing the point-and-click mouse approach (downside is that it’s now a linear approach - but works better).

PS: Curious if GGG would reconsider the behavior of the Whirling Assault be closer or similar to the contact-damage mechanics of the Whirling Slash (Spear), as these two shares some similarities except the WS seems to execute well in comparison.
