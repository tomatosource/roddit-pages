The game feels way better and I wanna make builds but I can’t bring myself to do the campaign again.

I’m not going to repeat what everyone has been saying. I agree with it. The new end game is fun, the build opportunities are great and I haven’t been this excited to play an ARPG in a long time.

However I’m an old fart and I’ve been playing ARPGs since Diablo 1. When D2 introduced seasonal play, it was game changing for the genre, but one thing they had going for themselves is that after your first campaign runthrough in a season, you could get rushed to the end game.

It was part of the economy. Some people were rushers, you paid them, got dumped into the end game and got power leveled in groups. It opened the door for easier experimentation and it kept seasons going far longer than a 2-4 week period. A single person could fund multiple builds.

In PoE 1, the campaign wasn’t too long. If you were twinked up it was tolerable. Still a far cry from the D2 experience and it showed. Not many people played multiple builds per season but if you really wanted to, it wasn’t so bad.

In PoE 2, I genuinely hate every single second of my life doing the campaign. I’m slow, I’m sluggish, the areas are long, I’m missing out on building wealth and taking part in the economy for WAY too many hours… I just can’t justify doing the campaign again. I know some tryhards will say “well if you micro every action you take and twink perfectly you can get it done in X hours where X is still awful”…c’mon. This will never be everyone’s experience.

I WANT to make a new build, but this is a stop gap/hurdle I can’t get over and I can’t be the only one. I’m sure many people enjoy the campaign and if that’s your thing, great! But I really think having a way to power through the campaign and early end game leveling would incentivize people to make more builds per season.
