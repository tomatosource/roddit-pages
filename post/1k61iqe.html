It’s unconventional, but the Cavs’ Ty Jerome legitimately has a case for the most lethal midrange game in the NBA. For most it would be a bad shot, but it happens partially on the strength of his floater: this year he took that shot at the highest rate in the league and made 57% of them

He’s also ridiculous in the mid-range in general, converting 58% of his attempts (also 100th percentile, along with his floater). Because of his lack of blow-by athleticism, bball index also put him in the 2nd percentile in shot quality (he takes a lot of heavily contested off-balance floaters), and as the number one overall shotmaker in the league.
