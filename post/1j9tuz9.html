Infernelementalist - A Multi-Barrier Invocation Manual-Bomber Build

I’ve had a blast making and playing this build, and wanted to share it for the like-minded and the generally tech-curious exile.

Check it out!

https://maxroll.gg/poe2/planner/vycss0el

Disclaimer: You might want to play this build if, like me, you actually enjoy the micro of having a lot of buttons to press. Very roughly, it actually feels a bit like fighting game.

The tagline: This build is triple elements, but focuses cold. Crit and spirit stacking, and uses quadruple Barrier Invocation.

Pros:
- Capable of clearing all content (and quickly!)
- Very strong offensive/defensive
- Incredibly fast paced and fluid (you essentially have no cast times, and have high mobility)
- Is visually stunning
- Is mechanically complex / Many button presses (if you do like this)
- Doesn’t rely on any gimmicks that might get nerfed, or expensive uniques

Cons:
- Ideally, it is a later-into-league build, as it uses several unique jewels for highest efficiency, and uses the most expensive anoint in the game.
- Many button presses (if you don’t like this)
