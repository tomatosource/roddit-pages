More Tank Talk: The 2006-2007 Celtics had one of the Best tank seasons of all time.

This team sported net negative of -3.7

This is one of the smallest Net Negatives of any team, ever, who won less than 30 games.

Let me rephrase that. This was one of, if not the most, competitive tanking team of all time.

I distinctly remember watching every game that season. We literally almost won 50 games that came down to the final minute or so in the 4th quarter.

Our young players played so well, and competed so hard, they all raised their values so much, that we traded them all for 2 Hall of Famers in the off season, kept the best ones for us (Rondo/Tony Allen) and won a ring immediately.

The 2006-2007 season was incredibly successful.

Tanking is fine, Talking about Tanking is fine. Sometimes you have to tank. We should know, we have what is considered the best tanking season of all time outside of when maybe Spurs got Duncan.
