If the Pacers win tonight, 2025 will set the record for the fewest number of 2-2 ties in the best-of-7 playoff era

Since the conference finals, I’ve had this lingering thought about this year’s playoff matchups being unusually lopsided. So while bored at work I started counting the number of 2+ game leads and it led me to something I found interesting.

So in 2002-03, the NBA officially switched their first round format from a best-of-5 to a best-of-7, matching the later rounds. That change guaranteed a fourth game in every playoff matchup. Since then, every postseason has seen at least 3 series go to a tie after the first four games.

This year however, with 14 of 15 series already concluded, 12 have seen their 4th game result in either a sweep or in one team going up with three wins to one.

The current mark for the fewest was set in 2007 then matched in 2023 when we only got 3 occurences, which notably all happened before the Finals. On the other hand, the years 2005, 2006, 2021 and 2022 each got a series tied at 2 for a record 8 times.

Here’s the 2025 recap after 4 games for each series before tonight

ROUND 1

Oklahoma City v Memphis: Thunder win 4-0

Cleveland v Miami: Cavs win 4-0

Houston v Golden State: Warriors lead 3-1

Boston v Orlando: Celtics lead 3-1

LA Lakers v Minnesota: Wolves lead 3-1

New York v Detroit: Knicks lead 3-1

Denver v LA Clippers: series tied 2-2

Indiana v Milwaukee: Pacers lead 3-1

ROUND 2

Oklahoma City v Denver: series tied 2-2

Cleveland v Indiana: Pacers lead 3-1

Minnesota v Golden State: Wolves lead 3-1

Boston v New York: Knicks lead 3-1

CONFERENCE FINALS

Oklahoma City v Minnesota: Thunder lead 3-1

New Yor v Indiana: Pacers lead 3-1

I realise it probably doesn’t really answer my question and there must be some better advanced stats you can look at to find the true answer but I thought it was worth sharing.

Side note: it’s also interesting how both series that were tied at 2 were the only ones involving Denver.
