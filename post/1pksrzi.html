A word of caution about Chonk’s Darkness

Since the per level maximum values of Darkness got buffed alongside Flames of Chayula while Ghost Dance was nerfed, some people may get baited into playing the Acolyte of Chayula monk using Darkness as their defensive layer.

As was just tested with several grounds degens, Darkness is still bugged towards damage over time:
DoTs damage your ES while fully bypassing your Darkness.
Once ES reaches zero, your entire Darkness gets reserved for 5 seconds and you start taking HP damage.

I personally tried to play ES/EV hybrid Darkness Chonk with ED/Cont this league and was heavily annoyed, especially considering the amount of ground degens in Abyss encounters.

This bug was reported to GGG and discussed by the community several times during last league (and I remember seeing people talking about it before). A GGG employe aknowledged it on the forum, however, it seems to still remain in the game and there were no mentions of this bug in the 0.4 patch notes.
