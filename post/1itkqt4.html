What about an asynchronous race in the future?

Just the idea of having a race but making it so that you have 24hours (or some other amount of time) to submit a run. It could be just 1 run and whatever happens happens, or it could be maybe unlimited attempts and your best time gets recorded.

I’m just thinking about this because I work the weekends so I can’t even attempt at this race coming up. But there could be many other reasons why people can’t be there exactly at the moment the race starts. I would definitely not rank high, but other people could, if only the strict schedule wouldn’t get in the way of their attempt.

This event would definitely be different, given that the pressure of performing right there and then is gone, so I am not saying it is a better, or worse, version of it. Just a different kind of event, and one where more people would be able to participate.

No participation reward this time is rough, and this is maybe because people that can’t make it to the event would just be left out. But if you make it asynchronous, then GGG could make it so that there is a goal to achieve like “Beat Act 3 in under 3 hours and get an MTX” or even just make it that by submitting a run regardless of time you get a nice little MTX as a reward.

Opinions on this?
