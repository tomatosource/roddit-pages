For the too many 3s crowds

It drives me nuts with ppl talking about how Cs only shoot 3s, or when 3s are not falling why not drive to the hoop. What those people maybe fail to realize this is simply due to the roster construction.

This team is not built to play like the 90s ball, physical drives, midrange, post up etc. The team is full of unathletic, undersize guards, and shooters. Thus, the reason why they gotta play to their strength.

Looking at our core, besides JT who we can argue can get to the hoops will or post up, we don’t have anyone else on the team who can play that way. Jaylen does a good job with middies by when he tries driving its almost a 50-50 it will be a turnover. PP is also pretty good with getting to around FT line besides that no one else. Just shooters.

And those blaming Mazulla, he did show he can also mix it up with the right personal. 2024 championship with Porziguis, i believe he averaged an insane point per possession due to his post up or mismatch hunting.

Looking at remaining teams core players, most have either really efficient mid range guys, scoring bigs, or athletic/quick/skilled guards who can get to the hoop.

OG, Brunson, KAT, Embiid, Maxey, Wemby, Fox, Castle, Ant, Randle, McDaniels, Jokic, Murray etc

My point is, with this roster shooting 3s really gives us the best chance of winning. I do understand you cant be one dimensional, but thats something Brad will have to address with his roster moves.
