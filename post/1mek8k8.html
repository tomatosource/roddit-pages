My (bad) suggestion to fix Life on Warrior side of the tree

There is a big issue with life scaling as well as Armour in the game. I know people want life nodes back on the tree and an improvement to the Armour formula (me included). However there are possible reasons why they don’t want to do this and if they are not willing to do this for whatever reason, one way that they could potentially alleviate the life problem without it affecting the overall ‘balance’ of the game too much is to add a Keystone like this:

Thick Skin:

  • 30% increased maximum life if you have no life modifiers on equipped Body armour.
  • You have no Evasion or Energy Shield.
