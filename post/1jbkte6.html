Dallas Mavericks’ Two Way players Kessler Edwards and Brandon Williams have 3 and 6 games remaining on their contract. Eventually if no one return’s from injury the Mavericks will be down to only 6 healthy players.

So the injury curse or the “Nico curse” continues for Dallas. Tonight Dante Exum suffered another injury blow; he has a broken hand and will be out for the season. Earlier the Mavericks signed Kessler Edwards and Brandon Williams to two-way contracts, but they are soon going to run into the limit and each have 3 and 6 games remaining on their contracts respectively. The Mavericks can’t sign another two-way player or any other player until April 10th due to cap restrictions, which means that once these two players run into their limit and if no-one returns, the Mavericks will only have 6 active/healthy players remaining. This is a historically unfortunate situation for an NBA team.
