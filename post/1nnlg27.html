Finally admitting… Acolyte isn’t sustainable.

Let me preface, I don’t get many characters to endgame, 1 per season and blast that one is something I am fine with and tend to do.

In 0.1⁄0.1.1 I picked Monk and went Acolyte. This is my main all patch.

In 0.2⁄0.2.1 I did the same as above.

In 0.3 I did the same as above.

It’s time to admit it… You can’t sustain playing this ascendancy all season, it’s identity isn’t here.

Leading up to 0.1 many looked at this tree and felt “sweet a dark monk, chaos based, this will be cool”. Only to discover what ended up being a fairly weak choice overall. Darkness left A LOT to be desired and that hasn’t changed unfortunately. You could make the class work, we always feel like a weaker Invoker however.

In comes 0.2 and there’s a couple minor changes to the class, nothing ground breaking but you could see an interesting direction begining to take shape. Darkness hadn’t changed but the allowance of a notable to leech mana from ALL forms of damage made this stands out. We were a black hole for mana, always topped off. Add that to the ability to leech mana to ES, few interesting items later and you have a self healing monk who could rarely die. Add CI to the mix with around 3k ES at the very least and you were set. Anything more and your sustained life was insane.

Then comes 0.3 with its revamp to Ascendancys that didn’t perform…. Somehow we managed to get a nerf. On paper the class looked to get a big buff but at the end of the day, it became less fun and lost its direction. All mana leech was broken out, elemental was now a passive tree notable, chaos shifted to another tree in the ascendancy. Darkness wasn’t touched. Illusion that doesn’t scale well was included. ES leech became unraveling where darkness helps you build shock, chill, and ignite. Volatility for free was added. Into the breech was butchered.

While I feel like adding ele mana leech to the core tree is a good move, keeping the original in Acolyte would have been great, save some passive points and keep the “all leech” for Acolyte would have been fine. Keeping the ES leech would have also been great as that line was a cornerstone in the class fantasy, using essence of another to sustain yourself, that’s pure chaos monk. Breech went from 3 flames then 3 flames with more on the ground, double time, double effect, to a much weaker version. Sure you can pick your flame, but red and blue aren’t really choices at the end of the day and not having double time is very unfun for a skill that was already a little bit of a chore.

So what needs to happen? Well here’s some thoughts I have that could fix this class.

  1. Remove unraveling. While it’s a decent idea, it’s not so useful overall. Replace it with either ES Leech or an upgrade to the prior volitility node where we halve the detonation timer and double the buff timer. Effect does not need buffed, just the timer for when it expires. Optionally an auto explosion at X stacks so we don’t have to manually detonate it and it can be used to sustain itself in a fight if needed. Leave the prior node as is for volatility, it’s unique and interesting.

  2. Change the breech skill. The current design with picking your own flame I like, but we should have at least a longer active buff for picking one. The effectiveness doesn’t need to change but an increased timer at least. Also make Red and Blue more viable options, no one uses them as they don’t really contribute to high end gameplay. Maybe make them have lightning and cold modifiers similar to the purples Chaos. This would make them feel like valid choices based on your play style.

  3. Change Mantra of Destruction. Yes this is a core skill but I think a base change then a following with darkness could work. As it stands, this is a 8 point build, one hit spend. Only two skills can actually use it in the monks tree to do anything and of those, only flicker can make actual use of it with stacking flames from it. Make this a buff for 5 seconds with a limit on damage given to it in that time, so say 10 stacks of purple flame enhanced by the into the breech skills.

  4. Darkness……… Yes the skills that no one uses other than to try and make it work then quickly respect and regret your choice. Darkness is a cool thought, a skill that covers you for damage above mana and es and health, it can Regen in combat and grants you a ton of extra damage…. At a cost. The cost is spirit. While darkness gives a ton of damage, it doesn’t hold a flame to the loss of a spirit skill. So for this, remove the spirit block first and foremost, the loss is so much greater than the gain currently that it is honestly not a choice. Turn darkness into a buff that you activate like Meditate, except, darkness provides you with ES leech and damage. It will also enhance the strength of your flames, this includes those generated by Mantra. Second node in it is a further buff enhancement to uptime and cool down reduction. Possibly add chaos exposure or penetration.

I feel like everything else is fine as is within the tree. That being said, there’s 3 additional items that I feel are needed.

  1. Allow Sinister QStaff to roll with chaos damage as well as the element damage. Through the tree we have “increased” and “extra” chaos damage but with no flat damage, we rely on buffs or uniques to make that damage exist. This would give us the ability to deal chaos damage innately and help remove us from being elemental focused as that really doesn’t matter for an Acolyte like chaos does.

2 & 3. We have two abilities not yet added. One of these should be a chaos strike and the other a chaos ranged attack like we have for cold and lightning. This gives agency to the Acolyte and allows us deal our damage instead. Sure we can get uniques to turn others to chaos damage still, but we won’t have to.

These changes should be enough to bring Acolyte into the fold and be a useful option. It leans into our use of chaos over elements, it differentiates us from invoker, who is an element master, and it frees us from being required to look at darkness as a meme.

I hope these changes land somewhere, while they do not need to be my design, something similar where we lean into chaos would give this variation of monk agency, usefulness, and identity to itself. Until something is done however, I am leaving Acolyte for Invoker. The struggle of acolyte is just not worth it unfortunately when I can be an invoker and play at a higher level.
