[Savage] Magic announced that guard Jalen Suggs is expected to play in the team’s season opener against the Miami Heat

After shootaround on Wednesday, the Magic announced that guard Jalen Suggs is expected to play in the team’s season opener against the Miami Heat at Kia Center at 7 p.m. ET.

Suggs worked relentlessly with Orlando’s training staff to make a return on opening night a possibility. The 24-year-old guard’s 2024-25 season was cut short after suffering a trochlea injury in his left knee.

“I’m super happy for him,” said Magic forward Franz Wagner. “We definitely need Jalen. His energy, (his) defense, (and ability) to make big plays. (He’s) one of our most important players on the team.”
