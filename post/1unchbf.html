Paolo Banchero trade value in the aftermath of the Jaylen Brown trade?

Paolo Banchero is a great young prospect, he was a top 3 pick and averages a lot of points, and made an All Star team.

However, kinda like Jaylen Brown, he has the horrible coincidence that his team always plays worse when he’s on the court. We just saw how the league now seems to care about how much better your team plays when you’re on the court, especially when you’re on a max contract. Even when adjusting for teammates and doing all the other fancy advanced stats stuff, it doesn’t seem to rate Paolo near a max player level
