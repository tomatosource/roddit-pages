Eurobasket Day 6 Recap + NBA Player Stats

Heyo, unfortunately I did not feel well enough to stay up and write the recap, so I missed yesterday.

What you missed was France getting upset by Israel 82:69 making Group D very interesting. Otherwise favorites dispatched off underdogs relatively quickly with Greece, Poland, Spain, Italy and Slovenia all getting the wins done.

Today we are back with Group A and B and a matchup that could decide the Top 2 in Lithuania vs Finland or set us up for a three way tiebreaker with Germany. But Step by Step:

Group A:
Estonia vs Turkey
Turkey is a really good team and they showed it once against vs Estonia. There isnt much to say here. Sengun once again was insane earning man of the match and being the first player since stat collection to have 20/5/5 in 3 consecutive games are Eurobasket. Game was decided in the first half afterwards it felt more like a friendly match. Both teams shot well from the 3 but thats about it. Turkey Serbia could be interesting on wednesday.
84:64 Turkey

NBA Player Stats:
Sengun: 21/8/5 with 1 Turnover
Bona: 12/4/0 with 2 Turnovers

Portugal vs Latvia
After a good first game vs the disappointing Czech Team Portugal has lost 2 Games in a row vs the big dogs. Latvia being inbetween those team just had an absolute thriller with Serbia and was trying to prove that it wasnt a fluke, And they absolutely did in the second quarter, winning 27:7. With a 22 Points Lead they safely won that. Most blocks in the game was done by Latvian Laser David Bertans showing nice defense.
78:62 Latvia

NBA Player Stats:
Porzingis: 21/9/3 with 5 Turnovers
Queta: 16/7/1 with 2 Turnover

Serbia vs Czech Republic
Its official: Bogdanovic will not be playing in the Eurobasket. A big blow for Serbia unlike the Czech Republic who were absolutely demolished with a 5:27 First Quarter. Going 1⁄11 from three cause they couldnt get under the basket vs Jokic Unlike another team later in this recap, Serbia showed Mercy making the game INCREDIBLE boring. Jokic only played 18 Minutes and Milutinov was the men of the match. Czechia are now eliminated from the tournament.
82:60 Serbia

NBA Player Stats:
Jokic: 6/7/4 with 2 Turnovers
Jovic: 10/7/0 with 3Turnovers
Vukcevic: 5/5/2 with 3 Turnovers
Krejci: 7/6/0 with 1 Turnover

Group B:
Sweden vs Montenegro
Two days ago I said that Montenegro needs to be careful about Sweden because while Sweden is not contender or anything they have been playing well for their standards, while Montenegro was not. Still the Balkan Boys were favorites and in a physical first half soared to a 6 Point Lead. Sweden wasnt going down without a fight though and managed to get into the lead at the end of the third quarter. In the fourth it was Kyle Allman with 10 Points in 2 Minutes bringing back the lead to Montenegro which they managed to hold onto and win the game. Vucevic was once again outstanding here, Larsson also played well. Sweden now rely on either Montengro losing to Great Britain or beating Lithuania in order to advance which probably wont happen.
87:81 Montenegro

NBA Player Stats:
Vucevic: 28/15/4 with 1 Turnover 2 Steals and 3 Blocks.
Larsson: 28/7/4 with 2 Turnover

Germany vs Great Britain
Yeah sometimes sport write fairytails and in one of them the heroic British basketball players after going down 0:3 and facing the current World Champions get a miracle win. Yeah I know would be a fun story. Unfortunately Britain is completely outmatched by Germany as evident by German 12th Man Leon Kratzer getting his first minutes in the second quarter with Germany leading by 25. The only question was how much of a beatdown this would be. On a positive note for the NBA followers and especially for Magic Fans Tristan Da Silva took a bit more of an offensive role today and was doing quite well. Overall just an absolute destruction by merciless Germans.
120:57 Germany

NBA Player Stats:
Tristan Da Silva: 25/5/2 with 1 Turnover
Franz: 18/1/10 no Turnover
Schröder: 19/2/5 with 4 Turnovers

Finland vs Lithuania
This game was something that could decide a lot in Group B. If Finland wins they and Germany are playing for first with the loser being confirmed second place. If Lithuania win there is a chance at a three way tie only if Finland somehow upsets Germany. This was an absolutely thrilling game with Lithuania seemingly coming out on Top as they went up by 15 with 8 Minutes left into the game. However Finland has Markkanen (which name I am always inclined to write wrong) and apparently they also have Olivier Nkhamhoua who made two big threes in a row to put the game back in reach. With 1:40 Left it was only a 5 Point Game and this was closer than most Lithuanian fans would have liked but in the end they got it done, making a three way tiebreaker possible now. MVP was again Jokubaitis for Lithuania but he unfortunately also got injured.
However given that after this game Gernany are +80 Points on Finland and +100 on Lithuania they are most likely confirmed first if I understand the tiebreak rules correct the points differential matters most.
81:78 Lithuania

NBA Player Stats:
Markkanen: 19/11/6 with 3 Turnovers
Valanciunas: 8/5/2 with 4 Turnovers

Short thing: Germany, Lithuania and Finland are already save in the Round of 16 from Group B. Meanwhile Turkey, Latvia and Serbia are save from Group A.
Czech Republic is the only team eliminated so far.
