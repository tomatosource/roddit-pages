The All-Star weekend could really go without Reggie Miller’s commentary for a change

I am probably in the minority, but I am growing tired of Reggie Miller being the main voice of every year’s All-Star weekend. His commentary is dry, repetitive and he just spends most of his time hyping up plays of his favourite players. He really likes the veteran stars, such as LeBron, Curry, Durant. I understand that he has been the main ASW commentator for a very long time, but there are so many good commentators across the entire league, specially local ones.
