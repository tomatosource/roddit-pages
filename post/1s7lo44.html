0.5 features wishlist

0.5 will be a lot of endgame activities, which mean high risk – high reward stuff. Also, GGG will probably mess up some mechanics and rewards (which is fine, it is in tha nature of this genre and it adds fun to the game) and people will be stupidly rich as in the temple in the last update. We need a lot of stuff for managing our currency and improvements on many other aspects of the game.

Here’s my take on what could be imporved in the game:

 

Major improvements:

  • Currency Wallet; an additional stash just like inventory, personal stash or guild stash that will be always accessible. You can Access all your currencies in the map, in campaign, in your hideout trading with ange, etc. This stash will not be shared with your regular inventory. May be a microtransaction.
  • Keyboard and mouse co-exist with controller
  • Once you have 2 characters over level 80, when levelling a new character you gain 20% extra XP bonus -or- your items have 50% reduced attribute requirements bonus
  • Ability to see on chat window what stats were changed on an item when it’s rerolled with divine orb, vaal orb, etc.

 

Minor improvements:

  • More checkpoints in maps (This was improved but recently feels rolled back)
  • Graphics transparency adjustment for special effects for spells and attacks
  • Option to keep item details always visible (instead of needing to hold down a modifier key to see tiers)
  • For PS5 dualsense controller; option to open different menus depending on where you click on the touch pad. E.g: clicking top right opens character screen, top left opens skills screen, etc.
  • Ability to reforge in bulk
  • Auto transfer the reforged item to inventory
  • Recombinator yields much better results and is actually useful for common player
  • All the number value entries in the game behave the same way (e.g. when changing  values in Ange’s window up-down arrows change values by 10, left-right arrows change values by 1, when changing values somewhere else, its different)
  • Ability to see your current amount of crystals

 

Ange;

  • Ange remembers the last item you search or sub category you were browsing when window is closed
  • Option to directly transfer currency and items to your stash (we have auto transfer from stash to Ange, but no auto transfer to stash)
  • Option to add some trades as favorite trades and see their current exchange rates every time you open Ange window (developed for POE 1, will probably come to POE 2)
  • When trading with Ange the system automatically checks your stash for tradeable items.  Currently you have to open your stash and browse each tab so that tradeable item or currency information can be “cached”
  • Option to convert currency automatically when buying stuff from Ange or trade site (e.g. an item is sold for 80 Chaos orbs but you have no chaos orbs but a few hundred exalted orbs, Ange converts the price to exalted orbs based on current exchange rate)

 

Atlas map;

  • An advanced search function with Biomes, unique map types and map modifiers
  • A more advanced bookmarking function with more save slots
  • Ultra zoom out and fast scrolling
  • Atlas remembers the location when window is closed
  • Ability to put more than 1 waystone to the maps to make them harder with more rewards, we already have 4 slots for waystones but can use 1.
  • Towers show where the closest citadel is
  • Unique maps that actually drop proper usable loot, that will make you happy
