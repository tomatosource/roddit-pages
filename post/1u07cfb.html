Atlas questlines are awesome. But Infinite is still bad.

Ggg masked the problem of the infinite random atlas quite nicely in the early stages of the endgame by simply not making it random and confiding you into a finite pre planned area.

But once you are done with the first areas and finish each questline you have to search on the inifnte atlas again, and it’s just as bad as past leagues. I wanted to do the arbiters again to quickly gain my remaining atlas points. But you have to find a new fortress to get the fragments.

I have 6 breachstones and 2 audience with the king in my stash and can’t use them. I spent 2 hours mapping straight in one direction on the atlas from tower to tower to find anything. Nothing.

I dont mind traveling 10-15 maps to the area of a questline if i can see it and know where to go and how long it will take. But mapping randomly without working towards your goals is boring, demoralising and makes me quit.
