Kyle Farmer is currently batting .333, but he has on OBP of .286.

I’m not sure I have ever seen that before. It’s possible because Batting Average is calculated by dividing the number of hits by the number of ABs, but OBP is calculated by taking Hits, Walks, and HBP divided by ABs, Walks, HBP, and Sacrifice Flies. I’ve never seen it before, and I assumed there was an error in the box score, but it was correct. Thought I would share because I learned something today.
