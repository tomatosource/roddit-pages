A playoff concept I like a lot: the ultimate loser

I’m not sure if I picked this up from somewhere or it just came to me, but at the end of the playoffs I love seeing who the ultimate loser is. What this means to me is that this is the the team that just lost all the way down the board. Take the 2025 NBA playoffs for example. A lot of people would claim that Denver was either the 2nd or 3rd best team that year, as they pushed OKC, the eventual champs, to 7. As they lost to OKC, you can make claims about them vs OKC’s next opponents, Minnesota and Indiana. Who you cannot say this about is 2025’s ultimate loser: the Orlando Magic. Orlando lost to Boston in the first round, who themselves lost in the second round to New York, who lost in the conference finals to Indiana, who lost in the finals to OKC. There can only be one ultimate loser every year following this chain of whoever beat you lost in the next round. Here are the ultimate losers for the last 10 playoffs:

  • 2026 - Houston Rockets
  • 2025 - Orlando Magic
  • 2024 - Los Angeles Lakers
  • 2023 - Brooklyn Nets
  • 2022 - Toronto Raptors
  • 2021 - Memphis Grizzlies
  • 2020 - Brooklyn Nets
  • 2019 - San Antonio Spurs
  • 2018 - Miami Heat
  • 2017 - Atlanta Hawks

Congrats to the Nets for being the only repeat ultimate losers these last 10 years. We are in a true era of parity
