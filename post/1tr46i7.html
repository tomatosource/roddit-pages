The way GGG has handled this league launch reminds me of the excitement of midnight launches past

If you’re old enough to remember lining up at midnight at GameStop to get your hands on halo 2 or 3 or whatever game you were hyped for you may know that gaming feels so different now. Yes I’m older and grumpier, but GGG creates and hypes their content in a way that brings me back to those days and feelings.

Their passion and talent as well as fearless compulsion to try new things and push the envelope reminds me of when games were more exciting and novel. When new games were doing something new instead of slightly varying a ubiquitous successful formula.

I truly appreciate their talent and passion as developers and it makes me passionate and excited about their games. We are lucky to have them.
