The seven different NBA champions of the parity era reflect the seven deadly sins

We have reached an unprecedented time in NBA history where we will have seven different champions in a row. For an organization whose history is shaped by dynasties and basketball empires, this is a gigantic shift. Each new champion has had a folly that has kept them from repeating as champions.

I believe every one of the defending champions have showcased one of the seven deadly sins in why they didn’t go back-to-back.

Toronto Raptors - Envy

City and franchise desperately wanted their MVP player Kawhi Leonard back. He obviously chose Los Angeles, leaving the fans and team alike envious of their former centerpiece player. Even to this day as they transition into a new, fun era with exciting players that they wonder what if Leonard never left them.

Los Angeles Lakers - Greed

This is straightforward. Lakers had an amazing title winning team and then wanted more, messing up their stellar bench and defensive mentality that helped them win in the Bubble. This then continued a year after where they got even greedier by trading their entire depth away for Russell Westbrook.

Milwaukee Bucks - Gluttony

Giannis famously went through a Chikfila drive through the day after winning the championship to eat 50 nuggets on livestream. It all went down from there.

Golden State Warriors - Wrath

Draymond punched out his own teammate Jordan Poole in practice and it all went crashing down from there. He is a very wrathful fella.

Denver Nuggets - Sloth

They won the title and then went into sloth mode, throwing out big time contracts to players that have hurt them ever since. GM and coach were ousted after failing to repeat last year and before this season‘s playoffs.

Boston Celtics - Pride

A team that was outwardly confident the entire season. Their pride saw them drop two straight 20 point leads before falling apart in game four with the loss of their star player Tatum.

Pacers/Knicks/Wolves/Thunder - Lust

Based off my theory, the next champion will succumb to lust in the 2025-26 season.
