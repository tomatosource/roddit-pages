Am I progressing way too slowly?

This is my first PoE2 league where I’ve actually reached and played the real endgame, and I feel like I’m progressing extremely slowly compared to what I see other people doing.
I keep seeing people talking about heavily juiced maps, city maps, expensive farming strategies, making lots of divines, etc., while most of the content I’m doing still feels pretty mediocre. I’ll get 1–2 div here and there, but nothing that feels like the kind of endgame farming people talk about.

Part of the problem is that I definitely wasted a lot of time early in the league because I simply didn’t know how the endgame progression worked. For example, I didn’t realize I should prioritize Arbiter of Ash and Divinity, so I spent a lot of time just running random maps with a partially completed atlas.
Once I learned that, I started focusing more on progressing properly, but now I feel like I’m stuck doing master quests and trying to finish the atlas points for the different mechanics.

I also still haven’t seen a single city map, despite seeing people talk about them constantly here.
As far as actual farming goes, the main strategy I’m currently doing is pretty simple: I use 3 Irradiated tablets, run the maps, and sell the tablets I get. It makes some money, but it doesn’t feel anywhere close to what other endgame players are doing. And it’s very slow because I have to do all of the content.

So I guess my questions are:
- Am I actually doing something wrong, or is this normal for someone’s first time reaching endgame?

- What progression should I be prioritizing to get from where I am now to the properly juiced endgame?

- Should I focus on finishing all my atlas/mechanic points before worrying about serious farming?

- How are people actually getting access to things like city maps and the more profitable endgame strategies?

- Is the 3× Irradiated tablet strategy reasonable for now, or am I wasting my time/currency?

I’m having a lot of fun with the league, so this isn’t really a complaint. I mostly feel like there’s a layer of endgame progression/knowledge that I’m missing, and I’m trying to figure out what experienced players are doing differently.
