Pritchard and Tatum chemistry

I’m excited to see Tatum and Pritchard get to run actions again together next season. I personally expect to see Pritchard start next season. He and Tatum have historically played extremely well together, running screening actions together with either as the ball handler. Pritchards on ball creation was fantastic this season, but he didnt get as many oppurtunities for offball actions. Pritchard and Jaylen did not coexist well together on the court this past season, which i believe led to Pritchards increased production when returned to thw bench. I dont believe this was Jaylens fault either, but they were both iso heavy players and Jaylen does not run pick and pops or pick and rolls as well as Tatum, making it difficult for them to effectively play off of each others gravity.

Pritchard may not necessarily have to ‘improve’ on last season to be a much more effective player on the court. I could see his efficiency and stats go up this season simply because he will be playing more often with a player that fits with his playstyle better, offering more catch and shoot oppurtunities in addition to his on ball creation from last season
