End of season LEBRON rankings

LEBRON (Luck-adjusted player Estimate using a Box prior Regularized ON-off) is a stat that was invented in 2010 and has highly correlated with MVP in the last 16 years, getting it wrong twice. These were: 2011, Derrick Rose– 3.8, 11th, Chris Paul was 1st, 6.8; and 2023 Joel Embiid– 5.5, 4th, Nikola Jokic was 1st, 6.7.

Here are the top 10 LEBRON according to NBARAPM:

  1. Victor Wembanyama (7.4)
  2. Nikola Jokic (6.7)
  3. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (6.4)
  4. Kawhi Leonard (5.7)
  5. Cade Cunningham (5.3)
  6. Luka Doncic (5.3)
  7. Chet Holmgren (4.8)
  8. Derrick White (4.7)
  9. Giannis Antetokounmpo (4.3)
  10. Jalen Duren (4.0)

Victor Wembanyama recorded the 5th highest LEBRON ever, after LeBron (8.8, 2010) and Giannis (8.3, 2020), and just behind Giannis (7.4, 2019) and LeBron (7.4, 2013).

Source nbarapm.com - an NBA stats website
