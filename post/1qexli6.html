Temple Building Tips Exchange

Currently building a temple, with tech, and wanted to share some tips and tricks that may not be as well known or documented.

i also want to learn tips from others that I might not know yet

  • go slow. probably my biggest mistake, especially early on, thinking I know what I’m doing is placing down a piece too fast. Use the web simulator if you wanna see the game rules in advance: https://sulozor.github.io/#/atziri-temple
  • figure Out where you want your architect early. You probably give a rats ass about this when you start. Suddenly you’re 20 temples in and kicking yourself.
  • you can place medallions (the rooms and “locks”) before you close — but you can only place the rooms each round before you open your temple. So you can either lock and then run or if you want to play fast and loose (or need a lock to drop) you can do it any time before you close the temple.
  • SUB NOTE: there was a bug where leaving would auto close temple. This seems fixed just be mindful there’s not no risk.
  • more rooms isn’t always better. Destabilization goes up the more rooms you have. Also they’re often worthless early on. Though you can use them as bait early on if you don’t have locks.
  • light radius isn’t just a meme. Don’t need to go all in but it saves a ton of time if you wanna just run a quick(er) map.
  • you probably Need a tighter loot filter than you’re used to using. This does end up dropping lower tier currencies, but I’d especially mention a ton of value comes from Magic Tier 4⁄5 high value base items and sapphires etc. early on.
  • grand projects are worth it. Don’t be shy. Yes you can just leave the map when you find the Vaal beacon. I’m still over sustaining maps.
  • Prepare in bulk (but don’t go insane). running t15 6mod maps is fastest i find . I get ~2.x (sometimes 3) crystals / 2 min (including time in hideout) with maps ranging from 15s to 2.5m.
  • Theres typically a beacon right at the entrance or immediately by the boss. my mental logic is if you get the boss one, kill the boss for the waystones. Otherwise bail immediately.
  • If you’re using grand project (you should be) using even a crappy tablet is a great way to ensure you’re running a previously attempted/old map as they’ll be greyed out.
