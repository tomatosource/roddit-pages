[Game Thread] Spain vs. Greece in Group C of EuroBasket 2025. Spain must win in order to qualify to the next round, the round of 16.

The game will begin in about 20 minutes from now.

Because of their 69-83 loss to Georgia earlier on, a loss will send Spain home (same amount of points with better differential, but in case of a tie of 2 teams or more, the differential between the two teams is the one that counts, so Spain’s differential is -14 vs. Georgia.

Spain has 6 points, Georgia got 7. A loss = 1 points, a win = 2 points, so that’s why Spain must win (losing in case of a tie in points, losing on a tie-breaker).

Spain or Greece will face France in the next round (4 place in group C will face 1st place in group D).

Thanks to the correction by u/Ok-Pie4219

If Spain wins their match they move to 8 Points and so does Greece. That would cause a tiebreaker between Spain, Greece and Bosnia. Spain would actually win that tiebreaker and Greece would face France since that would mean 1. Italy 2. Spain 3. Bosnia 4. Greece.

On a Greece win we will have:

France vs Georgia
Slovenia vs Italy
Israel vs Greece

On a Spain win we will have:
France vs Greece
Slovenia vs Spain
Israel vs Italy

Group C is already decided with 1. France 2. Poland (on a loss they win the tiebreaker with Slovenia and Israel) 3. Slovenia and 4. Israel
Bosnia is also locked in third no matter what, so Bosnia vs Poland is locked.

Also, at the moment, Italy will face Israel in the round.
