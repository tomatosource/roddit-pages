Why do we consider insane currency inflation in every league to be normal?

Wouldn’t it benefit the game in terms of player retention if currency didn’t skyrocket into space?
To achieve that, we need more ways to sink currency.

Chaos Orbs and Orbs of Annulment are used relatively often, but with Exalted Orbs it’s basically just throwing two per map after an Alchemy Orb and that’s it (especially after the introduction of Tier 2 and Tier 3 currency). Divine Orbs barely leave the economy at all.

So here’s my proposal: allow players to combine 10 Tier 1 Exalted Orbs into a Tier 2 Exalted Orb, and then further into Tier 3. This would act as a currency sink and smooth out inflation.

I haven’t figured out what to do with Divine Orbs yet.
