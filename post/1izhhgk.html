Vorana + From Nothing Jewel Combo: A Guide To This Lesser-Known Trick

Despite its immense potential, the Vorana jewel remains one of the most misunderstood/avoided jewels in POE2. I haven’t seen a detailed post here yet on how to maximise its potential—so here I am, with yet another unique jewel guide.

Why Vorana is an Absolute Game-Changer

This jewel is an absolute must-have for stat stackers, because all Vorana jewels give your keystones this Circular Teachings property.

The Vorana transforms keystones, which are build-defining passive nodes like Chaos Innoculation, Acrobatics etc, which have a fancy gold rim in your tree. There are just 22 of them.

The jewel gave me an instant +4,000 Energy Shield and with less life I just switched to CI and after that I was able to overcome Simulacrum T4 after being hard-stuck. It also allowed me to swap from Sapphire jewels to Emeralds, which increased my hideout Stormwave DPS to 1.6M+.

https://preview.redd.it/wgbbjj7yyole1.png?width=1783&format=png&auto=webp&s=0765c224bf3de92f13bc3b39c60095652fd19e65

Apart from Keystones, the Vorana Jewel also changes the abilities of your nodes. If you’re lucky, some notables will transform into +15 stats nodes with +40% increased damage—an insane boost for stackers. Other nodes give abilities like +40% energy shield.

But while some may be already aware of this, many players stop there, because while the notables are great, they’re too far away and not worth the investment on weak nodes, just to path to them.

How the From Nothing jewel changes everything.

The From Nothing jewel gives you access to these 5 notables without pathing to them

The From Nothing jewel allows you to connect to any node within its radius, making pathing a non-issue. When combined with the Vorana jewel and placed within the radius, a perfect stat roll can get you:

  • +75 total stats (+15x5)
  • +200% increased damage (+40%x5 - fire/cold/physical damage etc)
  • All this for just 5 passive points!

Where to place the From Nothing jewel?

The most popular slot is at the Dance With Death keystone which is at the bottom right. You can search on the trade site for the jewel with the property “Passives in radius of Dance with Death…” or use this link.

  • Dance with death has access to the most transformed notables (5) within Vorana’s radius.
  • Most builds skip the original notables in this area, so you lose nothing by modifying them.
  • There are hundreds of listings of Dance With Death jewels at just 1-2 Divs, so it’s one of the most underpriced jewels.

The second most popular slot is at the Whispers of Doom keystone (Near Pure Power), you can use this link to search for it. The From Nothing jewel for that goes at about 4-5 Divs.

How to Buy a Known Configuration Vorana Jewel?

The players on The Forbidden Trove discord (invite link) are the more hardcore traders and half of them who post have figured out this combination too. So they will take a screenshot in the #jewels-wts-poe2 group and you’ll often see screenshots like the above, accompanied with numbers and a description of their properties like this:

  1. 10% Cast Speed / 10% Chance to Pierce an Enemy
  2. 40% Cold damage / 15 dex
  3. 40% Fire Damage / 15 Int
  4. 40% Cold damage / 15 dex
  5. 40% Energy shield / 10% lightning res

These numbers refer to the 5 node transformations within reach of the From Nothing jewel because those are the ones that matter.

FAQ:

1. Tell me more about the Vorana jewel.

The Heroic Tragedy Timeless Jewel comes in three versions:

  • Vorana - Strength’s inherent bonus is 1% increased Energy Shield per 2 Strength instead
  • Olroth - Intelligence’s inherent bonus is 1% increased Evasion Rating per 2 Intelligence instead
  • Medved - Dexterity’s inherent bonus is 1% increased Armour per 2 Dexterity instead

So when people talk about the Vorana jewel, it’s really the Heroic Tragedy Timeless Jewel with the Vorana spec as it’s the only one worth its price. The jewels start from 40D+ on the market and Voranas are priced at 1-3 Divs more because all it takes is just 1-3 Divines to randomise Medved/Olroth’s to Voranas.

2. What does the seed number in the XXX Songworthy Deeds mean?

Each Vorana jewel has a random seed number specified before the Songworthy Deeds phrase, that determines how your passive tree is transformed. You can use Divine Obs to re-roll the seed and it’s supposed to be somewhat of a “Divine Sink”, for players wanted extreme build optimisation.

It’s important to know that when you divine it, you risk changing a Vorana to a Medved or Olroth. You’ll have to divine it till it goes back to a Vorana. So on average, you’re spending 3 divines to get a re-roll on a Vorona, not 1 divine.

3. Should I corrupt the jewel?

No, there is zero value in doing that and it will prevent rerolls and the market value of it will plummet.

4. Is the jewel seed tied to the players? Does it mean each player will get a different result for the same jewel?

This is not true. Perhaps it worked that way in POE1 that’s why some people think that (but I never played that), but in POE2, the jewel’s transformations stay the same even when you trade it with other players.

5. What are some other spots for the From Nothing jewel?

The From Nothing jewel affects the radiance around keystones, and different keystones have different prices. On Discord, 80% of players use the jewel that affects the Dance with Death keystone (1-2D). About 15% the Whispers of Doom (4-5d) that is at the top near pure power, and 5% at the other misc spots.

If you have a high-end build, another way to use the From Nothing jewel is to buy the Resonance keystone (150D). I did this so I could remove spaghettification from my amulet anoint and get Pure Power instead. It was a slight upgrade for me, but not a cost-effective one because I now miss out on the way cheaper Vorana benefits.
