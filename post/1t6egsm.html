In Endgame, Normal and Magic Gear Item drops are 99% better than even Tiered Rare Gear Drops

  • In endgame if you drop a Normal piece of gear of a high ilvl you can craft on it or sell it easily, both of which are very good.

  • In endgame if you drop a Magic piece of gear and it has 1 or 2 good modifiers it’s very good because you can craft on it, or sell it easily to someone else who will craft it.

  • But if in endgame you drop a Rare piece of gear, even if it’s T5 (the rarest tier), 99% of the time it will be worthless and too full of useless mixes of affixes that you can’t even fully fix with crafting and most of the time can’t sell to anyone either as its not craftable or has too much of a mix of affixes where nobody can use it.

It feels bad that as the games drop system upgrades your item drops it upgrades items from Normal _&gt; Magic _&gt; Rare, where the “rarest” outcome is objectively the worst and most worthless one:

Rare Gear item drops are the weakest rarity of gear to drop, even Tiered rares at their higher tier are most of the time a worthless mish-mash of affixes and most players in endgame just hide almost all tiered ground drops and you’d rather drop a tiered magic item every single time.

Now imagine they just “nerf” rarity so that it only affects Gear like in PoE 1. But rarity reduces your white drops and your magic drops and increases your rare drops. It’s actively making your gear drops worse. Even now no rarity or negative rarity farming is one of the most “profitable” things to do in the endgame because white/Magic items are so valuable and rares largely useless. Outside of buffing currency drops, rarity is actually bad for you.

So if rarity in 0.5 only affects gear drops that will practically be the same as removing rarity from the game, as adding rarity to your character is making your Item drops significantly worse in almost every case because it upgrades your normal and magic items to rare items, making rarity detrimental if it didn’t affect currency (outside of Unique item farming)

They really need to fix the value of rare gear drops and tiered rares. The fact that white and magic item drops are 100x better than any rare gear drop on average is weird.
