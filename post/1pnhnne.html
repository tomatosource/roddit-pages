My analogy for PoE2 skill design.

Just sharing my opinion, hearing that spell totem, a ubiquitously simple and easy setup to use in PoE1, requires 3!!! Power charges to use, just simply broke my brain and made me feel alienated from the game as a 10000 + hour PoE1 player.

In PoE2, almost every skill requires multiple resources, buffs to be consumed, or synergies to function properly. Sure, you can use the ‘unbuffed’ version in most cases, but unlocking the full power of each skill is gated behind the usage of other skills, supports, and so on.

It feels like this to me:

In PoE2, the developers carefully hide a golden nugget inside a pile of manure- and say to players, hey- spend some time digging through the crap, and you’ll be able to uncover an awesome prize. And then they hand you a shovel and let you choose which pile of manure and which shovel to use. All these skills look great, sound great, and CAN be good, once you’ve meticulously balanced several resources and spent hours upon hours slowly grinding through the campaign and early maps. But the problem and the solution are both pre-determined for you, so the digging isn’t fun. It’s just chores.

Whereas in PoE1, it felt more like the developers handed players a bucket of bronze or silver nuggets, and said, these are pretty decent as they are, but if you really want, you can come up with a way to turn them into gold. Basically, PoE 1 skills have a decent baseline, and the player gets to choose which way they want to go about turning their bronze into gold. There is no pile of manure because the majority of skills feel perfectly fine to play, even at early levels, and without needing to satisfy several built in requirements.
