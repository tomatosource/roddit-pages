Which championship team has the closest to perfect roster construction?

Now, ik THE answer is the 2017 warriors, they were pretty much perfect in most respects. But besides them who had the perfect roster construction?

I feel like 2 teams come to mind.

The 2014 spurs and the 2024 Celtics.

The 2014 spurs were so solid in every single aspect, they had defense, passing, rebounding. They had 4 players who shot 40% from 3. They had it all.

And the 2024 Celtics starting 5 and bench didn’t feel like it had a flaw. Super star player? Check. The best second option in the last 5 years? Check. 2 all defensive guards who shot 40% from 3? Check. A spacing big man who’s a decent shot blocker? Check.

These are two teams who, may not have won multiple chips (one due to the second apron, the other cause the main piece was getting way older and Steph rose). They felt like the most solid team in every aspect.
