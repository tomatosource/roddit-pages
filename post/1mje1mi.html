The WNBA is rolling out a sweeping “no-bag” policy tonight as officials aim to stop a recent surge in games being disrupted by sex toys thrown from the stands.

In response to multiple incidents, the league is now barring all bags from the arena to prevent fans from smuggling inappropriate items onto the floor.

Over the past week, three separate games featured sex toys delivered from the audience onto the court, most notably during a Sparks‑Fever contest in Los Angeles where a green toy landed dangerously close to Indiana guard Sophie Cunningham. The Fever star had previously begged fans to stop such behavior on social media.

The no-bag policy is being introduced midseason — an unusually drastic leap for a major sports league — but it comes amid mounting pressure following arrests and repeated safety compromises. One fan was arrested after a sex toy was thrown during a Dream‑Valkyries game and faces disorderly conduct and trespassing charges.
