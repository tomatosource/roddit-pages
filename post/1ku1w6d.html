Haliburton vs Brunson is one of the greatest PG duels in a long while

I’m trying to stretch my memory so feel free to correct me. Almost all playoff teams have a competent PG that can go off any given night, but I feel like I haven’t seen two healthy franchise superstar PGs dueling at their highest level in a long long time. Granted, contenders led by superstar PGs are historically a rare breed, but Haliburton vs Brunson fits that bill exactly.

And if you think about it…. Finals is gonna be Shai/Brunson/Haliburton (no disrespect to Conley). That level of quality at 1 hasn’t been a common sight for a long long time.

PS. I’m seeing people making this out to be a comparison between Brunson and Hali. My point was more broadly the quality of play at 1, in this series and the Finals. I honestly can’t remember seeing this many All NBA PGs in the playoffs!
