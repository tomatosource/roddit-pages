The last time a team from either conference made the Finals in back-to-back years was the 2018 and 2019 Warriors

We’ve had 7 consecutive years without a team making back-to-back Finals appearances. In addition to having 8 straight years of unique NBA champions.

In that time only two teams, the Heat and Celtics, have been to the Finals more than once (both 2x). In the 7 years so far of the 2020s decade, 12 different NBA teams out of the 30 have made the Finals. In the 2010s decade, only 9 different teams made the Finals.

In the 2010s, the Warriors made five straight Finals (2015-19), the Cavs and Heat both led by LeBron went to 4 straight each (2011-14 and 2015-18), and the Spurs had to back-to-back appearances in 2013-14.

It’s been so hard to establish a dynasty since Golden State. And it makes the four straight Finals appearances between the same two teams (Warriors and Cavs from 2015-2018) that much more impressive. We may never see anything close to that again.
