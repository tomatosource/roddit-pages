Stay the Course vs Retool vs Rebuild? Which would you prefer?

I feel like most trade ideas I’ve come across in general come down to one of three concepts (which duh lol) and while we wait for next week’s draft and free agency to begin, which general option do you prefer?

  • Stay the Course - Have a core of Jays/White and thread the needle to get under the 2nd apron this upcoming season (trade Jrue, Sam, KP), see what you’ve got with whoever you acquire & the youth/draft picks we have, and try to make more moves for the season after when JT is back and we have a bit more space to add better players unless Brad manages to hit multiple home runs with picks, development of guys, etc.
  • Retool - Keep White, Trade JB whether this season or next, (in addition to trading Jrue, KP at some point, etc.), acquire picks, see what you have with the guys you draft, etc. The season JT is back, assuming with all the cap space from JB and everyone being gone, perhaps you can create more of a feasible team around him. Bide time until free agency of the 2027-2028 season and try to get a big name someone like Giannis (assuming he stays this season), Jokic, etc. or any of the younger up and comings (one of the Thompson bros, etc) who could be at a star level and available to pair up with JT.
  • Rebuild - Trade JB, Jrue, KP, and Sam for picks and whatever else you can get. Keep White & Pritchard. Play the young guys, your draft picks, see what you’ve got. Hopefully get a high draft pick next season and get AJ or Boozer or whoever for a fresh start for when JT is back.

I’ve seen quite a few people have interest in rebuilding which is interesting to me because would JT want to be a part of a rebuild? Or are they operating under the assumption he won’t be completely back to his old self for a few more seasons?
