I’m really gonna miss LeBron

Born in 1988, MJ was a GOD to me and I eagerly worshipped every thing he did. Mike WAS basketball for me. As an average sized white guy I was elated when the grown ups would call me baby Kerr at the pickup runs.

Then it was AI and Kobe, not sure who came first but they were my new heroes (not that I took down the MJ posters or stop watching the VHS docs every single day).

Then when I was around 15 or 16 Lebron entered the league. He was the new exciting thing, but I was still a Kobe dude.

Fast forward to today and Bron has been my goat for awhile. Arguments for MJ aside, he’s my goat and that’s after coming up worshipping the dude.

Watching him tonight I was equally enamored with his second excellent playoff performance in a row as I was prematurely nostalgic for when we won’t have him around anymore.

Who’s next?

SGA does whatever he wants on the offensive end and at an extremely efficient clip. And he’s a really good leader and dude. Meh.

Jokic is one of the single most talented players of all time. Physically, mentally…offensively. Cool.

Wemby is an alien. Maybe he ends up the greatest two way player ever. Love him and love watching him. Doesn’t move me like Bron, Steph, KD etc. ever did.

Am I just a romantic millennial or a nostalgic casual or does anyone relate to this? I am starting to feel sad about Steph, KD, and specifically LeBron going away.

I don’t have any sort or emotional connection to any of the new generation of stars and I’m scared about that.
