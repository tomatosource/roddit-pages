Am I wrong to hate towers as much as I do?

Before I even begin I’d like to give a blanket positive statement. I absolutely LOVE 90% of this game and I really think that in time it can be the greatest of its genre. They do a lot of things very well and I really do appreciate the work and the passion they have put into the game. Now to the 10% that drives me crazy. Crafting does absolutely suck right now BUT I tend to give them a pass on that because after a league or an update or two more crafting will HOPEFULLY be available. My biggest issue is also my biggest concern. TOWERS ARE ABSOLUTELY TERRIBLE. It feels like two different games. I’m lucky and in a position where I can play 3-6 hours a day and I feel like I spend 60-70% of that time prepping map nodes and towers. When I’ve ran a few good maps and my good nodes dwindle down I don’t say “ man that was really enjoyable I can’t wait to keep mapping and be a lot goblin”. I instead am thinking “ Shit man after two or three more maps I’m gonna have to spend so much time running through bad maps, running through towers , and attempting terrible maps so they won’t get juice when I activate a tower.” It takes away alot from the game. If you ask any efficient POE player they will tell you if you aren’t mapping and then stashing efficiently then you are losing a lot of currency/ items. But to run efficiently, you have to path to towers, make sure the maps around towers are good and good for breach , ( I know breach isn’t the only mechanic at the moment but I’m speaking from the point of view of a player that is above average and wants to farm the best loot and get the most currency), and then activate your towers in a way that doesn’t allow them to hit on several bad map nodes . Also before I get the “ well you don’t have to play like that and don’t have to be super efficient.” Id have to say that this sucks for new and more casual players too. New players get to the end game and for them it may take several days to run bad nodes and get towers amd then also get scammed horribly in trade chat ( entirely different discussion but if you’re a “ buying all divs for 80 ex.” guy In trade chat, your mother is a big ol hoe. The reason the towers worry me is it seems kind of foundational and every league it stays in, I really think it’s gonna turn more people off of the endgame and the endgame was what was so extremely enjoyable about poe1. I know it isn’t poe1 but it’s a feature everyone wanted and is looking forward to that other games just don’t have. If you disagree that’s totally fine!. We all enjoy what we enjoy
