A General Overview of the Celtics Game 7 History in the Jays era

Alright lets quit moping and look at the track record in our blessed 9 years of Jayson and Jaylen’s partnership

Since the 2017-18 Season, the Celtics have gone 3-2 in Home game 7s

G7 vs Bucks: W (112-96)

^(G7 vs Cavs: L (87-79))

G7 vs Bucks: W (109-81)

G7 vs Sixers: W (112-88)

^(G7 vs Heat: L (103-84)*)

^(*Tatum blown ankle)

  • Average margin of victory: ~22.7 points
  • Average points allowed: ~88.3 points
  • Average points scored: ~111.0 points

In losses to Miami & Cleveland:

  • Average points scored: ~81.5 points
  • Average margin of loss: ~ -13.5 points
  • Average points allowed: ~95.0 points

Since the 2017-18 Season, the Celtics have gone 5-2 in game 7s overall:

G7 vs Bucks: W (112-96)

^(G7 vs Cavs: L (87-79))

N G7 vs Raptors: W (92-87)*

G7 vs Bucks: W (109-81)

G7 @ Heat: W (100-96)

G7 vs Sixers: W (112-88)

^(G7 vs Heat: L (103-84)**)

  • Average points scored: ~98.3 points
  • Average points allowed: ~91.1 points

^(*Neutral Court NBA bubble)
^(**Tatum blown ankle)

This post is not here to sit and proclaim that everything is okay and that what we watched last night was acceptable because we’re good in Game 7 scenarios, but its worth mentioning that the Jayson and Jaylen led core traditionally does very well in Game 7s.

If you want you can go ahead and calculate the shooting splits for all those games, but just at a glance they looked solid to me. I just don’t have time to cope that hard.

Do I think we can lose? Absolutely. If you fuck around and come out with no fire whatsoever that Sixers team will probably whoop our ass. But I would be a moron to say I don’t have utmost faith that Jayson and Jaylen aren’t gonna play their best game of the season on Sunday. Barring that the Sixers don’t completely melt down like they did in a certain other Game 7 held at TD Garden.. you will have to fight tooth and nail for this game. Coaches will have to game-plan for a desperate 6ers team accordingly.

My personal opinion is that the Celtics win in a gritty showing. 10-12 point game in the 4th and pull away late. But if we dominate them from 0-48 I also wouldn’t be surprised.

I would be stunned if we lost Sunday, more-so if we don’t even put up a fight.
