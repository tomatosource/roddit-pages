Shrodinger’s Strongbox

How do you proceed with a map once all rares and mechanics are finished?

I was playing with a buddy, and we had cleared all known content off the map (expedition, ritual, boss, whatever). I went to TP out and he asked ‘where am I going? We need to clear the map.’

His argument was that we could potentially find a high end strongbox or find currency still and we shouldn’t leave until there’s only a few mobs left just in case.

My argument was that it doesn’t matter how much of the map is left. Once all the main elements are cleared, we can leave. We’ll be killing more content that gives off better possibility of loot in the next map and find strongboxes there in the way to clearing that map as well as progressing towards citadels faster. Either way, full clear/content clear, you’re giving up some value (as long as your map sustain is fine).

So do you full clear? Or head out once the main content in each map is complete?
