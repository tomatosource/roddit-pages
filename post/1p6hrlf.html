[Video] RTX 5060 Ti 16GB – Path of Exile 2 T15 performance at 3440x1440 ultrawide

Recorded a short ~5 minute T15 map to show how the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB performs in Path of Exile 2 at 3440x1440 ultrawide.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0OENEz7elY

The goal of the video is mainly to show GPU performance at this resolution, not to be some min-maxed showcase. The first seconds are a bit more stuttery because shaders are still compiling, after that it smooths out.

I only have around 100 hours in PoE 2, so I’m still learning the game and the systems. If you notice anything obviously off with my setup, keybinds, routing or general gameplay, feel free to point it out. I’m fine with feedback, both on performance settings and on how I’m playing.

Specs:

  • GPU: RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB GDDR7
  • CPU: i5-12400F
  • RAM: 32 GB
  • OS: Windows 11
  • Storage: 2x 500 GB SSD
  • Monitor: 34” 3440x1440, 180 Hz (VRR)

If anyone is considering this card for PoE 2 at 3440x1440, this should give a rough idea of what to expect in a T15 map.
