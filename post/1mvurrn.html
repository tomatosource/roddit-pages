Wanna thank everyone of you fine redditors who made the “Auction House” a reality in POE 2

I am in tears, when I first started playing POE 1 many years ago in 2013 the forum was filled with so much toxicity due to the controversy surrounding what was then Diablo 3’s Real Money Auction House that if you mentioned the idea of some sort of automated trade system in POE you would be abused and ran out of town and so really I stopped visiting the forums and started focusing more on reddit.

Over the years I would see threads on this sub asking for Auction House and it would get many positive feedback, if not for you all showing the devs that this was a feature most people wanted I do not believe this would have been possible.

Part of the reason it was also held back was because Hide Outs was one of the ways GGG made money And you guys here made the suggestion of doing the Auction House trade system where you would still teleport to the player’s hide out but it would just be automated so they still get to show off their Hide Outs.

The days of scammers and bots posting fake price fixing listings and not responding is over now if they wanna post their god tier item for 1 chaos they can absolutely do so and someone will just outright buy it.

You guys made what was only a dream a total reality can’t thank you beautiful humans enough.
