Mace skills’ long post attack animation result in required animation cancelling for optimum play and it feels off

Currently, if you play maces, the optimal thing to do after nearly every skill is to animation cancel the end bit of the animation after the attack has landed already. Be it leap slam or boneshatter, you are nearly always better off cancelling the end bit of the animation with a roll or something. My question is, does something like this have a place in the game?

I’m not really debating the long windup times here, think of that what you will, but I get the premise that mace skills should be high impact and require commitment. But I don’t think you would find many people who like animations continuing for so long after the attack landed already. I don’t think GGG would be particularly keen on animation cancelling being such a regular part of gamplay, seeing their focus on immersion and such, but currently it is required for optimal gameplay and it feels kind of janky.
