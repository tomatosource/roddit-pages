Giveaway threads are not good for this subreddit, and here is why

I messaged the mods and they recommended opening a discussion thread, so here we go.

Having individual threads for giveaways is bad/unhealthy for this subreddit because:

  • It adds no value to vast majority of the readers, who want to play with what they earned. People come here for content (discussion, info, memes etc.).
  • They are artificially up-ranked and clutter the front page, due to how they work (asking people to comment etc.).
  • It signals to people that players are quitting, giving the vibes that the league is over.
  • The posters basically get an easy way to farm karma. This doesn’t hurt anyone, but it feels wrong.
  • At the end of the day, very very few people end up getting any value (i.e. one person per thread) out of these.

I think there should be an alternative place where these giveaways should go. Could be either a single sticky/mega-thread, or some sort of third party website/tool dedicated to this. What do you think?
