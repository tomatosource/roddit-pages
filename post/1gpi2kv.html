Isn’t it kind of surprising that neither LeBron, Durant nor Curry have won an MVP award after the age of 28?

LeBron James won his last MVP at age 28, Curry was also 28 and Durant was 25.

Lebron, Durant and Curry are three of the players with the greatest longevity in history of the NBA. Even in their mid to late 30s, they are performing at a level few could reach at that age in NBA history.

So it seems kind of ironic that just those three longevity guys didn’t win an MVP award for being the best player in a season after age 25-28.
