[OC] Payton Pritchard’s Officially Arrived

I wanted to post this here as I thought it a relevant topic due to taking a look at Payton Pritchard’s growth on both ends of the floor. I recently made a video that discussed some of the stats and ways that he’s elevated his game so far this season.

The craziest thing to me so far about Pritchard’s start so far, is that his efficiency even with the larger role has been absurd. He ranks in the 99th percentile on a per possession scoring out of pick and roll, the 93rd percentile scoring out of isolation and he’s knocked down 47% of his threes off the catch. Then on top of all that, teams have to account for him creating for others out of pick and roll and isolation as well.

Another thing I noticed was that the Celtics (on a smaller sample) are finding some newer ways to get Pritchard looks from three. Last year including the playoffs, he took just 17 shot attempts coming off screens. He’s already taken 16 threes off of screens so far this year and to this point has made 12 of them. Shooting 75% from deep off movement is probably not sustainable, but it does show he can be incorporated more shooting off movement. Then Pritchard’s also knocked down some threes out of pick and pop as well.

His defense doesn’t get a ton of credit nationally, but Pritchard really competes on that end as well. So far this year on a per possession basis, he’s really impressed on post ups and in isolation. It really speaks to both him and the Celtics that even players teams might perceive as mismatches, they can still hold their own on that end of the floor.

I went into some other elements within the video, but was wondering how everyone was feeling about this recent stretch from Pritchard. The current contract looks like an absolute steal, and will be a huge boost with bigger extensions about to kick in. While he’s looking like the Sixth Man of the Year favorite (and a MIP candidate), I’m interested to see how things play out, but having someone take this kind of leap to add more fire power to a defending champ seems pretty rare and it will be a lot of fun to see everything unfold this season.
