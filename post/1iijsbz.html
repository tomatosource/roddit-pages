[Ben Cafardo] “Hubie Brown’s Final NBA Broadcast: Colleagues Pay Tribute to Legend Ahead of Feb. 9 Farewell”

Final Broadcast Details:

  • Date/Game: Feb. 9, 2025 – Bucks vs. 76ers (2 p.m. ET, ABC).

  • Location: Milwaukee, where Brown began his NBA career as a Bucks assistant in 1972.

  • Partner: Reunites with Hall of Fame commentator Mike Breen (2006 Finals duo).

Colleagues’ Tributes:

  • Mike Breen: “His preparation is unmatched… He makes every broadcast better.”

  • Dave Pasch (ESPN): “He treats me like family. There’s no ego—just love for the game.”

  • ESPN President Burke Magnus: “We’re sending him off in style… His impact is timeless.”

Career Highlights:

  • Coaching: 2x NBA Coach of the Year (1978 Hawks, 2004 Grizzlies).

  • Broadcasting: 50+ years across ESPN/TNT/ABC; famed for simplifying complex plays.

Personal Resilience:

  • Lost wife Claire (63 years) and son Brendan (ex-Knicks broadcaster) in 2024.

  • Continued working despite grief: “This final game is humbling… a culmination of my life’s work.”

Hubie Brown, 91, caps his legendary 50-year NBA career with a farewell broadcast in Milwaukee, where his journey began. Colleagues laud his mentorship, preparation, and enduring passion. Tributes during the game will highlight his coaching/broadcasting legacy, bridging eras from Kareem to Wembanyama.
