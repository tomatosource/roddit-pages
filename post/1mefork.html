It looks like LeBron James was working out at the Clippers practice facility. But the reason why is because Klutch now uses the Honey Training Center.

When the Clippers moved to the Intuit Dome, they no longer needed the practice facility in Playa Vista. So it is now used by Klutch Sports Group, and they simply haven’t swapped out the branding yet. This probably isn’t as big of a deal as it looks. LeBron is just getting in a workout at his agency’s office.
