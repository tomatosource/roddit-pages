A Point Against Dale Murphy’s Induction Using The MVPs

Hello baseball fans. With the eras committee debating who should get in, I became more interested in Dale Murphy’s case. I never saw Dale Murphy much less heard of him. The more I looked into his career the more I was puzzled. Overall, I came away with the idea that Murphy should not be voted into the hall, and part of that is due to his MVP wins.

Simply put, the MVPs overestimated Murphy’s value those years.

Initially I thought Murphy won those MVPs as a catcher, but not he not play catcher in the two years he won, he was an outfielder.

In his first MVP season, his overall war value was not even in the top five. It was Gary Carter who as a catcher had the highest value according to war.

His second season to win the mvp is much more justifiable as he did have the third highest war but honestly it’s not by a lot. You could easily make the case for the other top highest six players because of how close they were.

Even his 7.7 war campaign still isn’t the highest of that season, although he should have gotten more votes. In fact his 7.7 season is his highest season value ever.

The mvp awards make him look better than what he was.

That isn’t to say he didn’t have a great peak, he did, but it’s not as dominating as the mvp awards made him seem to be.
