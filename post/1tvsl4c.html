Which team do you think would be the best fit for Kawhi Leonard if he gets traded?

Kawhi is entering the final year of his contract and there are reports that both sides are not happy with the current situation. Plus the Clippers don’t want to risk losing him for nothing like they did with PG. Obviously at his age, his trade value is no longer that high anymore and a contender would not have to give up a whole lot to get him as the 2nd or 3rd option. With his contract being up soon, he likely would only re-sign if he’s traded to a contending team. As for the Clippers they likely would only consider offers that included multiple 1st round draft picks as they would head into a full rebuilding mode if Kawhi is gone.
