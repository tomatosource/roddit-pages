Banning prep players + Shortening draft (again) and eliminating Low A

3 major changes to the draft and player development could come with a new CBA. The first is banning high school players from getting signed straight out of high school (forcing them to go to a 4 year college or juco). Then there’s likely going to be another shortening of the draft (down to 10 rounds) in addition to eliminating LOW A (after having already eliminated short season A ball).

I don’t like any of these changes, forcing players to go to college against their will (especially the elite talents) is wrong.

Shortening the draft again reeks of veteran players hoping to stay in the league longer by eliminating much of their competition. I don’t think that will work in most cases, hopefully the independent leagues can fill the gap.

Eliminating the short season A ball level has already wreaked havoc on development, eliminating Low A full season ball will create a bigger obstacle for organizations.
