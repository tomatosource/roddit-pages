3 step by step crafting process (amulet, armour, gloves)

First of all, english is not my native language so I apologize for any grammatical errors.

Second of all, when using a fracturing orb you can desecrate a modifier before so you have 1⁄3 chances to fracture the target modifier instead of 1⁄4. For this you can use the bone if less than 4 modifiers or a essence of the abyss. Also you can target the essence to avoid the targeted modifier by using a omen of dextral/sinistral crystallisation. Those also work with the essence of the abyss.

Now, let’s begin.

You can use any base.

The item lvl should be at least lvl 80 if you want T1 Maximum energy shield or 77 if you want % increased evasion rating. For any else 75 works just fine.

1-Buy a base with +3 to lvl of all melee skills.
2-Get up to 4 modifiers and fracture the item.
3-Use 2 orbs of annulment to get down to 2 modifiers.
4-Use either chaos orb or greater chaos orb. Because the current price the greater worked fine for me. Go for whatever you want between Maximum energy shield, % increased evasion rating or % increased maximum shield. I suggest to go for Maximum energy shield but if you don’t have enough chaos you can settle for the other 2.
What about armour? I don’t think is worth it but if is what you need…go ahead.
5-Add another modifier and use perfect essence of enhancement. You have 50⁄50 to target the dummy modifier or the searched one. If you lose then you have to go back to step 3,
6-When desecrating I want to be more sure about my chances so I always use a omen of abyssal echoes, is just cheaper than fail and needing an extra omen of light. You want to use 2 things with your preserved collaborne:
Omen of sinistral necromancy and a omen of the liege. Use a preserved collarbone and select “Minions deal % increased…blabla” We need this for it’s “Damage” tier
7-I can’t say for sure if using only 1 omen of homogenising exaltation and 1 perfect exalt with omen of greater exaltation it works but you can test it. Otherwise use 1 omen of homogenising exaltation and 1 perfect exalt( it can be a greater one if you don’t mind worse chances). You should get %increased critical damage bonus
8-Repeat step 8, this time you should get %increased critical hit chance.
9-Use an orb of annulment and a omen of light to remove the “Minions deal…”
10-I suggest you to use and ancient collarbone instead of preserved for better chances but do as you please. Desecrate, use the omen of abyssal echoes and go for the missing prefix. In my case I went for %increased maximum energy shield.
11-You can repeat step 9 as you wish until you get the prefix you want.

This is a tricky one, you have the cheaper/safest option and the better but risky one.
1-Get a base lvl 82. Any defensive prefix works perfect but for higher numbers I would avoid the one with Maximum life. You also want at least 1 resistance or not…and you can do the same as with the amulet steps 2 to 4. In my case I will save that step for the salvation step. Because in the current economy it was cheaper.
2.1- Cheaper option. Use a greater essence to add any T3 resistance. Guarantee, cheaper, safest, but will you settle for a T3 modifier when it could be T1?
2.2- More expensive and risky option. Use an omen of homogenising coronation and a perfect regal orb. You have 3⁄6 chances to get a second resistance, you can also get reduced ignite duration on you, faster start of energy shield recharge or gain deflection. Is that the end? Not yet.

Salvation step. Fracture the item and hope it hits the defensive modifier or the resistance. Use 2 orb of annulment and rerroll the last modifier.

If you went for the cheaper option or you got lucky with the risky one you should have 1 defensive prefix and 2 resistance suffix.
3.1-Desecrate your item with a preserved rib, we are going for “Increased spirit reservation” So no need to use an ancestral one.
4.1-Use an omen of homogenising exaltation, a perfect exalt and omen of greater exaltation to get the other 2 defensive prefix. If you get the maximum life you can settle with that or craft another armour.
5.1-Reveal your desecrated modifier. If is not the one you wanted you can use an orb of annulment and omen of light until you get the one you want, in my case after 5 tries I got 11% Increased Spirit Reservation. Perfect for my monk to have 242 spirit.

If you had to save your armour now you should have 1 fractured modifier and whatever modifier you settle down during the chaos party. I have at least 2 scenarios in mind.
First, you got a resistance:
3.2-Same as step 4.1 but you would also need omen of sinistral exaltation to add the 2 missing prefix.
4.2-Another omen of homogenising exaltation and a perfect exalt to add the second resistance, you can fail again, in which case you will need to annul and chaos again.
5.2-Desecrate and reveal the last suffix.

Second scenario, you, like me, got a second defensive modifier.
3.3-Desecrate and reveal a resistance dummy modifier.
4.3-Omen of homogenising exaltation and a perfect exalt to add the third defensive modifier.
5.3-Omen of homogenising exaltation, a perfect exalt and omen of greater exaltation to get 2 resistances. Here, sadly for me I got a T4 and T3 resistance but because the prefix were so good I settled down for this.
6.3-Annul and omen of light to remove the dummy resistance.
7.3-Desecrate and reveal the last suffix to your satisfaction.

Last one, the gloves, I hope my instructions are clear enough for you to follow…otherwise I wrote a lot for nothing.
Sadly for this I never actually crafted the gloves because I’m done with the season but I have the theory.
I already saw 2 videos with:
3 flat damage to attacks prefixes and
Increased attack speed
Increased effect of socketed items and T2 Increased critical damage, which is cheaper.
BUT I want +2 to melee skills and leech % physical attack damage as mana, so here we go.

1-Buy a exceptional base with 2 sockets for extra DPS or settle with 1 socket because to be honest, exceptional bases for gloves are a bit expensive right now.
2-Chaos until you get leech % physical attack damage as mana. This should be cheaper in case you end fracturing the wrong modifier.
3-Up to 4 modifiers and fracture, 2 orbs of annulment down to 2 modifiers.
4-Chaos until you get +2 to melee skills.
5-Add a dummy prefix using an exalt and omen of sinistral exaltation.
6-Use an essence of horror using omen of sinistral crystallisation.
7-Desecrate and reveal # to accuracy rating, we don’t want it to appear as normal modifier in the next step.
8-Omen of homogenising exaltation, a perfect exalt and omen of greater exaltation to get 2 flat damage to attacks.
9-Annul and omen of light to remove the accuracy rating.
10-Ancient rib and omen of abyssal echoes to try to get third flat and that is it :D

I hope any of this procedures helps you to get the item that you want, any questions I will gladly answer them. Cheers.
