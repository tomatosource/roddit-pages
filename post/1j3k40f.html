With both AD and Kyrie down for the season, Luka starting to look great on a Lakers team in the middle of a six-game win streak, and the most depressed fans in the league, is this the worst season any team has ever had the year after losing in the Finals?

The 2012 Thunder are a good example as they ditched Harden, but they were still competitive for several years with Durant and Westbrook. I also don’t think the fans were nearly as bummed out as the Mavs fans are right now.
