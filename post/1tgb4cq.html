Druid using Amor Mandragora

I remember this unique item being sold as a good starting weapon. Of course, since it’s a level 1 item, its power drops off quickly.

Now, bear with me for a moment.

The shaman spends 5 rage when using any ability. Meanwhile, Bear Form generates endurance charges when spending rage.

There is an interaction, where using a slam skill, being Ancestral Cry my choose, and the supports:
Jagged Ground I, to consume endurance charges
Bramblesman, to keep triggering Rageforged I or II
And of course, Rageforged to spend rage resulting in more endurance charges.

The idea behind this was to constantly trigger the passives that interact with endurance charges consumption just by walking, it worked…okay.
It would work better to be able to do this while using Rampage but you can’t generate rage while doing that.
And the other major problem is that it consumes rage faster than it generates. No rage, no endurance charges after a few seconds.

Now, for Amor Mandragora I already have a few ideas to having spells hitting the enemies, aka generating rage for me.
Thunderstorm, Spell Totem, Cast on something.

Problem is, and I hope they fix this, (but since season 1 you lose combo when having the skills in different weapon sets) when you leave the bear form you lose the Ancestral Cry effect (this doesn’t happens with walking calamity)

So what alternatives do we have to spend rage?
Furious slam it feels slow.
Fury of the mountain is a channeling skill so you would have to spam it burning your mana too.
Walking Calamity with Rageforged?
Wolf or mace skills?

I want to take advantage of that 9 rage per hit with an spell generation. While also putting in use that rage.
