Pathfinder changes look dope as hell

I was reading through the patch notes and saw this change to pathfinder :

  • Traveller’s Wisdom now requires Path Seeker. Traveller’s Wisdom now instead grants Attribute Passive Skills offer additional Options:
  • 5% increased Damage
  • 5% increased Global Defences
  • 5% increased Cost Efficiency

Am I smoking or does this seem really really good? A ton of builds in poe2 use a lot of travel nodes, like A LOT, so being able to stack up global defences while also effortlessly solving mana issues seems crazy to me. that paired with now being able to jump around the passive tree could lead to some incredible tech. Im really hyped to see what people can do with new pathfinder, im praying it can see the light of day honestly. (At 40 attribute nodes which isn’t unheard of for builds, you get 200% increased global defences)
