Hello there exiles. You may remember me from my video of that Demon Bear with the molten fissures, a very simple build, that I enjoyed for not even a day. I quickly jumped on the wolf wagon, but soon realized 50div are not enough to do top content with my friend, and making few dives here and there wasn’t really cutting it. So I decided to learn how to craft. I had 8divs on the 31st.

Now this is not going to be a post of, here is my strategy, copy it and now compete with the rest of thousand of you who had same idea. I will teach you to come up with your own.

Here are a few very, very important tools, which are actually the key to my success:

  1. Recombinator is your most important tool.
  2. Omen of Recombination is your second.
  3. Omen of Catalysing Exaltation (the one that consume quality to infuence a tag)
  4. Perfect Essences
  5. Omen of Dextral/Sinistral Crystallisation (removes suffix/prefix with next Essense)

Ok, so now you may ask, ok mr EmptyFolder, what next? Well, we go to poe.ninja and we search for a Breach ring that is popilar with a build you somewhat understand. Sort by highest DPS or EHP to find the pro players and their rings, so we can get an idea what is BIS Craft, so we can try and make something similar. Another thing you can use is, when chosing the build in poe.ninja is to click on Rare Ring on the left side, then it will list you the most used affixes for that class. Easy data mining!

Next is the “engineering part”, how do you pull this off? Open Currency Market and learn what Perfect Essenses do. They remove a random affix to 100% place a very powerfull mod. Global Defenses on Amulets, Intelligence on Amulets, Mana on rings.
When you Recombine two breach rings, often with high odds (20%-30%) you will end up with 2x T1 affixes, best case scenario. Odds go down significantly with the weight of the tier affix you’re trying to combine, but remember that not all classes need 50 Spirit with a 3 Skill Amulet, maybe 40 is good too (Tier 3)
Next we use Omen of Dextral Exaltation to slam any suffix, then we target remove it with a Perfect Essense + one of the Omens of Crystallisation.
Now the end result will be 3⁄6 prefixes done.
Next we want to slam some crazy resistance, crit or speed. Say no more. Buy these pricey quality things from Breach, buy like 32 of them and put that ring to 40%. Here is the lucky part, where you shouldn’t be discouraged if you fail on your first try. Use Omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Omen of Greater Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation + a cheap Perfect Exalt, close your eyes and prey. If you hit something great, which is very likely all you need to do now is Desecrate and unveil your last mod.

I hope this gives you an idea and inspiration to craft and get out of the slums. There are few more trinks I can teach you, how to fix your craft, if it has potential, but went sideways, how to price your items, but this post is getting too lengthy for me.
