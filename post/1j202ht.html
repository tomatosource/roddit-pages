63 years ago, Wilt Chamberlain scored 100 points (36-63 FG, 28-32 FT) against the Knicks, the most points scored in an NBA game

#On this exact date, March 2nd, 1962, Wilt scored 100 points.

The box score of the game. Philadelphia Warriors 169-147 New York Knicks

Wilt went an incredible 28-32 FT (87.5%), a huge anomaly for a career 51% FT shooter. 4,124 people were in attendance at Hershey, PA. He also had 25 rebounds, which was his average. This game occurred in Wilt’s 1961-62 season where he averaged 50 ppg and 48.5 minutes per game.

Aside from the most points scored in a single game, Wilt set several other records in this game:

-Most field goals in a game with 36

-Most field goal attempts in a game with 63

-Most free throws in a game with 28

-Most field goal attempts in a quarter with 21

-Most points in a half with 59
