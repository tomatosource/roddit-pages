Tonight marks just the 2nd time this century that 2 teams will face off for the 12th time in a single season

Its not uncommon for 2 teams in the same conference to play 4 regular season games and go to 7 games in the playoffs (Sixers and Celtics just did) which would be 11 total games. But because the Spurs and Thunder faced off in the IST as well, game 7 tonight will be the 12th time they’ll face off

The same thing happened last year between the Rockets and Warriors and before that it happened in 1995 with the Spurs and Rockets back when there were less teams in the league which meant more matchups (These two faced off 6 times in the regular season). The 1962 Celtics faced the Warriors 19 times for the same reason
