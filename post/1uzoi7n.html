Crafting on this game is not just a supplementary feature but a game on its own

The more I think about it, I have not ever seen a crafting like this, crafting is mostly a simple supplementary feature on most games, on this game it’s something else, it’s like a poker game, it’s a good alternative to slot machines. I guess for some, just crafting stuff and never map can be an enjoyable experience.
