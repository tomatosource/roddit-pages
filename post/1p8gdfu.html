Throwback to one of my favorite interviews where JB asks Paul about the wheelchair

Credit: KG’s podcast\/show, KG Certified

I first got into the Celtics during the KG era, so suffice it to say, this interview (and this whole episode) brought me so much joy when it first aired. But it popped up on my feed the other day, so I wanted to share it.

Awesome for so many reasons:

  • KG & Paul’s friendship is just so fun to watch. (Their podcast is great, too.) But it’s also surreal to see them talking to the two guys drafted from the picks we got by trading them.
  • JB even asking about this in the first place LMAO
  • Marcus so clearly trying to hold it together the whole time. (Kudos to JT for being able to haha)
  • KG rolling with it and fanning the flames, and as such, he & Paul’s back and forth.
  • JB poking JT’s foot like they don’t believe Paul when he explains why the wheelchair came out

I love how Paul is still so involved with the team and close to the current guys. I don’t know how common it is, aside from the Spurs, maybe the Lakers?, for old players to still be involved with their current teams? But it’s what makes the Celtics so great.

I’d love KG to pop up at a game. I don’t think he’s gone to any T-Wolves games either though, to be fair. Maybe just not his thing?
