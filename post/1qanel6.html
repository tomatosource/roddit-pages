Easy maxed out temple layout with an Russian and None variant.

Hey guys idk if anyone has made a layout like this before but i would like to share it anyways since i spent some time figuring this one out my self, i’m calling it Qcumbers Temple.

People usually say this Architect placement is pretty bad, but i made it work and its one the easy placement to get as well.

It has 3 main chains which are very easy to do as long as you do the long spy chain at the top that’s in the outside perimeter first. Followed bye the middle one then the final part is the bottom chain.

THE Pros? you don’t have to pray to whichever deity you worship to get that 1 single Room to progress your temple by a single step, You have enough locks? Well lucky you!! You have 3 chains to choose from!!

THE Cons? You better have them damn locks.

Basically it uses all the available spaces with the down side of it having 4 Ends “technically 3”. But by the time you reach the final chain which splits 2 times you will be able to sustain it with Locks without a problem and you can always just fall back to 3 if you find your self short.
