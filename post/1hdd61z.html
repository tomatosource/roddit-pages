Does Pritchard’s breakout make Jrue’s extension feel a just bit regrettable?

To be clear, Jrue has been a slam dunk addition for the Celtics overall- his on-ball defense and off-ball game offense were crucial to last year’s title run. But I can’t be the only one who got a little nervous after they signed him to 4 years/\(135M including a player option in 2027-28 for \)37.2M when he’s 37. I also understand that his offensive role has been very different since joining Boston as an overqualified fifth option, often relegated to spotting up in the corners, playing the dunker spot, and pushing the break. Still, the Memphis game definitely highlighted the fact that while he’s always been a solid 3-point shooter for his career, he probably overachieved with his outlier 60% corner 3P% last year, and that 33% that he’s at from 3 this season has kinda been a due regression to the mean. I love Jrue, but I don’t know if he’s featured enough on this current team to live up to that dollar amount.

Contrast his performance to Pritchard this season, who really took advantage of the additional opportunities given to him with KP out of the lineup for the early part of the season. He’s setting career highs pretty much across the board without being sloppier with the increased usage and still bringing the feisty defense. His size will probably always keep him from being what Jrue is on D, but his shooting to this point has been extremely valuable. He’s been awesome to watch this year, and his 4 years/$30M extension is looking like the best value contract in the league right now.

I don’t think it ever had to be one or the other, and I do think Jrue’s shooting will pick back up, but I wonder if the front office would offer him the same money if they knew PP would take the leap that he has this season.
