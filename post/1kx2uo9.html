Short (thankfully0 feedback on Mapping from a newish player

OK, I think having beaten the Arbiter and all the other Pinnacle bosses some multiple times at higher levels, I can offer a short bit of feedback.

Mostly, I think the 0.2.0g system (to date this feedback) is in a pretty good space. (Yes, I do not have POE end game as a reference, so apologies for that).

However, the sheer number of maps that you need to do to gain access to various pinnacle challenges is way out of kilter (ignoring trade of course).

To do Xesht, I might need 5-6 good maps, all close by around some towers.

To die horribly in a Simulacrum, I might need to do 5-6 maps around appropriately configured towers.

To slaughter poor old King in the Mist, I can get lucky in 1 map, but 3-4 would generally guarantee an Audience.

Olroth needs a bit more work, or luck, but, again, you’d be unlucky not to see him after 10 maps.

And, yes, I recognise that the Arbiter is THE pinnacle boss, but without trade, how many maps do you need to run to collect all three fragments?

Well, I’m scrolling around my map, and I think I can get 1 in about 40 maps if I head thataway =&gt;

Then another 60 maps thisaway &lt;=.

I can’t even SEE the third citadel!

Further, the game is telling me to form an area of maps influenced by towers, which obviously are all clumped together, BUT to get to the Citadels, I have to run loooong lines of maps, and these are all going to be plain maps, unless I really want to spend a considerable investment in time filling up every tower, and completing all the maps in these areas, (and gaining multiple mats for the other pinnacle bosses), before I get ONE fragment!

Yes, I read that they will cluster citadels in the new patch, but will that be retrospective to existing maps?

How about instead of randomly appearing in the Atlas, we can influence maps to change a normal map into a citadel? That would fit in with the current mapping process of working with towers to create areas to farm.
