​

While most of the mainstream media’s attention remains fixed on traditional destinations, the Washington Wizards are team who could quietly position themselves as a compelling wildcard in the sweepstakes for LeBron James.

The gravity of Washington’s pitch could center around Anthony Davis. It is no secret around the league that Davis and James remain incredibly close friends. However, Davis has been noticeably non-committal publicly regarding his long-term desire to stay in Washington. Compounded by a desire to keep their star happy and anchored in D.C., the Wizards organization is feeling the pressure to make a major splash.

Enter Trae Young. Sources on Young’s side tell me that the Wizards point guard has been exceptionally vocal and adamant behind closed doors, trying to convince Davis to fully commit to the franchise. Young’s pitch is simple yet aggressive: he firmly believes that with the talent already on hand—and the potential additions they can make—the Wizards can genuinely disrupt the hierarchy of an increasingly competitive Eastern Conference. If Washington can use their asset pool to orchestrate a monumental re-pairing of James and Davis alongside Young, the Eastern Conference landscape would shift overnight.

James spoke glowingly of the pieces that the Wizards have on their roster on his Mind the Game podcast stating: “I love the young kid they have from France (Alex Sarr), love the kid [Kyshawn] George and also the rookie kid they got from Texas.. [Tre] Johnson).”

The Wizards who possess the No. 1 overall pick in next month’s draft have been guided by General manager Will Dawkins and President of Monumental Basketball Michael Winger. That team received high praise from James earlier this year when he said: “You know they have some pieces. And I think for the first time in a few years we can say that Washington has grabbed some pieces”
