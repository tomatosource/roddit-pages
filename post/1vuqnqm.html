[CHRISTIAN CLARK] Klay Thompson dealt with Mavericks’ chaos gracefully. Now, his time in Dallas is over

Link: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7447503/2026/08/21/klay-thompson-mavericks-buyout-reflection-luka-trade/?unlocked_article_code=1.7FA.Rqej.UtrBTld30zK6&source=emp_shared_article&smid=url-share-ta

In April, once the Dallas Mavericks’ 26-56 regular season mercifully came to an end, Klay Thompson was asked a question he considered a “hard-hitter.”

Are you anticipating being here next season?

“I’m not sure. I’m under contract, so I do,” Thompson said.

Then Thompson paused and let out a laugh.

“But I’ve definitely learned in my time in Dallas (that) things can change on a dime.”

Thompson arrived in Dallas two years ago thinking that he’d spend his mid-30s feasting on the open looks Luka Dončić created. To say the situation around him changed quickly would be putting it mildly.
