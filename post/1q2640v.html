Offseason Post: Any Little League Rules Experts Out There

I was talking to my daughter about softball, and she was asking me about my little league experience. Way back when – in the 20th century, mid-to-late-80s – I played little league, in what was 3 divisions as we called it: T-Ball, minors and majors. Majors was 9-12, the Little League World Series age.

Anyway, when I was 8, I was asked to move from minors to majors, and I said no, because I just didn’t want to. I liked my team and where I was.

They then forced me to leave my team which won first place in the first half of the season, and put me on the last place team. Why? Because that was “the national Little League rule” back then. We won the second half of the season, because that’s the effect a good 8 year old can have on a little league team. I never played another season after that.

Does anyone know what that bullshit rule was?
