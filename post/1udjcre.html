Final Draft Thoughts: 2 Guard, 2 Wings, 2 Bigs I’d be happy with at #27

Here is my final attempt at organizing my thoughts on the draft about who we should be going after. I would be happy with all of these picks, and totally expect Brad to pick someone I’ve barely researched. I like all these guys I’m listing and would be ok with any of them. I will say though, despite our need for big men, there doesn’t seem to be a big in our #27 range with a ceiling higher than Queta. I know folks don’t want to hear that, but there just isn’t a guy here. There is a small chance Chris Cenac or Jaden Quaintance drop though, but I don’t expect it.

&nbsp;

Guards

&nbsp;

Maleek Thomas - G 6’5’’

Tough shot maker. Willing to sacrifice to fit. Gets wherever he wants to go with a live dribble.

&nbsp;

Sergio De Larrea - G/F 6’6’’

Smart guard with good size. Has PG skills, can run slow and methodical pick and roll. Solid 3 point shooter.

&nbsp;

Wings

&nbsp;

Isaiah Evans - G/F 6’6’’

Creative and versatile shooter. Can pull up from anywhere. Willing to sacrifice.

&nbsp;

Dailyn Swain - F 6’7’’

Bulldozer attacking the rim in transition. Attacks driving lanes in half court. Coachable and shown willingness to grow.

&nbsp;

Bigs

&nbsp;

Zuby Ejiofor - PF/C 6’9’’

Hustle beast who plays bigger than he is. Solid passer for a big man. Switchable defender.

&nbsp;

Tarris Reed - C 6’11’’

Traditional ground and pound big. Higher floor than other guys in this range. Solid passer for a big.

&nbsp;

PICK #40 BONUS ROUND

&nbsp;

Jaden Bradley - G 6’4’’

Plays hard D and passes the ball. Strong frame for a guard. Can play on and off ball.

&nbsp;

Alex Karaban - F 6’8’’

3 point shooter with good volume. NBA ready day 1. Championship poised and experience.

&nbsp;

Baba Miller - PF/C 7’0’’

Tantalizing measurables. Strong passer for a big man. High ceiling.
