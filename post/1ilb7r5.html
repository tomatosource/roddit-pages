In the last 172 minutes of basketball they have played, the Lakers have trailed for only 66 total seconds

Beginning when the Lakers took the lead with 4:51 remaining in the second quarter against the Knicks, the Lakers have only trailed in a game for 66 total seconds in the three and a half games since then.

The Lakers never trailed again in that game in NY, ultimately winning by 16. Their next game against the Clippers they trailed for a total of 51 seconds, with the Clippers last lead coming at 8-7 less than two minutes into the first quarter, with the Lakers ultimately winning by 25. Their next game after that was against the Warriors, and the Lakers never trailed once for this entire game. In their most recent game the Pacers briefly led once, at 5-4, but only for 15 seconds before the Lakers tied it, and then eventually took the lead for good just over one minute into the game.

It should be noted that the Knicks and Clippers games were road games for the Lakers, and the Lakers didn’t have Anthony Davis or Luka for any of these games, and did not have LeBron for this most recent game. This is a pretty impressive stretch for this shorthanded Laker team.
