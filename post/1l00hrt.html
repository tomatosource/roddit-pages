Most negative “subreddits” from last year

A recent post was made on this subreddit titled “Most Negative Fanbases in the NBA.” Mavericks and Celtics fans topped the list. But I believe that post was misleading and had temporal sampling bias — it reflected the most negative subreddits, not necessarily the fanbases overall. Mavericks fans topped the list due to the Luka trade, and Celtics fans came in 2nd largely because of the Tatum injury.

Here were the most negative subreddits from last year:

  1. Memphis Grizzlies – 48.4% of comments had negative sentiment
  2. Cleveland Cavaliers – 44.6%
  3. New Orleans Pelicans – 43.6%
  4. Brooklyn Nets – 42.7%
  5. Milwaukee Bucks – 41.3%
  6. Utah Jazz – 41.2%
  7. Philadelphia 76ers – 41.2%
  8. Minnesota Timberwolves – 40.6%
  9. Houston Rockets – 40.2%
  10. Charlotte Hornets – 40.2%

Interesting to see how this shifts year to year depending on what a franchise is going through.
