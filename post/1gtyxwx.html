After 14 games, the Portland Trail Blazers - who are on a 3 game winning streak and 5-5 in their last 10 games - have a better record than the Milwaukee Bucks led by Giannis Antetokounmpo and Damian Lillard

While the Bucks have yet to play their 14th game, their 4-9 record makes it mathematically impossible for them to match the Portland Trail Blazers 6-8 record even if they do win and thus will possess a worse record than the Blazers to start this season.

While some of the Bucks current struggles can be blamed on injuries, the Blazers are not exactly healthy in their own right; Deandre Ayton and Anfernee Simons are out due to a finger injury and illness respectively.

As a result of the Damian Lillard trade in 2023, the Portland Trail Blazers possess control of the Milwaukee Bucks first round picks from 2028 thru 2030*

They are one of five teams who possess control of the Buck’s firsts from 2025 to 2030:

  • Nets: 2025 (Pelicans get it if it’s Top 4)
  • Pelicans: 2026 via a swap; may get 2025⁄27 picks if protections kick in
  • Hawks: 2027 (Pelicans get it if it’s Top 4)
  • Blazers: 2028 via swap, 2029 (May be swapped with the Wizards), 2030 via swap
  • Wizards: 2029 if the Bucks pick is the second most favorable pick owned by Portland between the Bucks, Celtics and Blazers 2029 firsts
