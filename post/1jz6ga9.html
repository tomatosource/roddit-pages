In 10 seasons, Devin Booker has had 7 coaches and is about to have his 8th.

then you have Igor Kokoskov who made history as the first nba coach born and raised outside of the US to be a head coach.

Then the Suns seemingly find their guy, Monty Williams. You have the good vibe bubble run, then a finals run and they look like the best team in the league in 2022…until they don’t, Then he’s fired in 2023 and becomes the worst coach for any team in 20 years with the Pistons, still cashing checks to this day(on the real, he suffered a family tragedy, I don’t want to rag on him).
