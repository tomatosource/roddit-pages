It shouldn’t be good for a team that a player didn’t make all-nba (re: supermax)

Jaren Jackson Jr missing all-nba is pretty good for the Grizzlies all things considered. his contract would have been huge and that would have kneecapped them for the future with him and Ja on the team.

The supermax seems like it was a bad idea generally because I don’t even really think it convinces players to stay long term with the team that drafted them. The solution that the additional money from the supermax shouldn’t count against the cap probably has some flaws to it, but it’s better than the lack of anything there is now. Especially when you combine it with the second apron.

This Thunder team is phenomenal, they drafted and/or developed their players. They will be kneecapped by that in a few years and be forced to break up. The Celtics did a great job getting the best player in their respective drafts in back to back years at pick 3 and developing them into a championship team. Now it looks like they probably should trade Jaylen Brown due to his salary.

edit: somehow it seems this has been taken to mean feeling bad for the small market/cheaper owners? if so, no. on the contrary, the second apron was created for those owners. this is about how it’s dumb to have to root against your guy as a fan
