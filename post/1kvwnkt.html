A plea to the networks/NBA: stop the quarter break player interviews

I can’t believe these are still happening in the playoffs. Usually it’s a team’s 2nd or 3rd banana or a bench player having a hot game, but last night we had Jalen Brunson talking to Allie LaForce when the Knicks were currently losing and down 0-2 in the series.

These do absolutely nothing for the fans, other than annoy us that our team’s guys aren’t resting or talking to teammates/coaches on the bench. And I’m positive that the players hate it.

(I know this is the not an unpopular take, but my God Shammgod I don’t know why we have this.)
