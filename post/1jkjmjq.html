Few ideas that could make the atlas and mapping WAY BETTER

  • Boss in all maps
  • Kill the map boss to complete it
  • Bosses and rare monsters appear on the map upon entry.
  • Towers contain a stronger version of a random boss with better loot.
  • Towers have a small chance to spawn a magical blacksmith NPC who will offer you 3 deterministic crafting options in exchange for gold. Choose only one.
  • Upon defeating a tower boss, there is a small chance to open a portal that teleports the player to another tower in a different region of the Atlas, previously covered by fog. The player can continue progression from the new point or return to the previous one at any time.
  • Significant layout improvements, minimizing the number of corridors that could cause excessive backtracking.
  • Rework of the Atlas passives into a model three times larger, where players have more points (perhaps 60⁄70 points) and three times more passive options to allocate, directly influencing and modifying their Atlas. For example: “Completed maps have a 10% chance to connect with another incomplete and already revealed map.”

NEW TABLETS

I thought of new tablets that could change the progression dynamics in the Atlas and give players more control over how they progress. Many new strategies could emerge from this.

  • Chaotic Tablet: Generates random content in 2-9 maps in the area, which can include any endgame mechanics. The player only discovers the content upon entering the map.
  • Safe Passage Tablet: 3-12 maps within range allow free passage without needing to complete them. (The map does not count as completed unless you do so.)
  • Long Path to the Winner Tablet: Doubles the reach of 2-5 Towers within this tower’s area of influence.
  • Worm Hole Tablet: Upon completion, 1 map within the tower’s range will directly connect to a randomly revealed but still incomplete map.
  • Rush Tablet: 1-3 maps within range will count as completed twice in your Atlas progression upon being finished.
  • A Second Chance Tablet: Upon dying in 2-6 maps in the area, gain a second chance and respawn at the last checkpoint. Consumes 1 portal to revive. (not available in hardcore)
  • Vaal Architect Tablet: Transforms 1-2 incomplete maps in the area into towers.
  • The Real Lord Tablet: 2-6 maps within range will spawn 1 additional boss.

These are just a few ideas that I believe would make Atlas progression and map exploration much more interesting and fun, allowing players greater control by choosing more versatile strategies. Of course, other ideas could also be great to complement these.
