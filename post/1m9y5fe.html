Note: TS+ is a stat that adjusts a player’s True Shooting Percentage wrelative to the league average for that season. TS+ = 100 means the player’s TS% is exactly league average. TS+ &gt; 100 means the player is better than average. TS+ &lt; 100 means below average. 120 TS+ means you were 20% better than league average, 90 TS+ means you were 10% below league average.
