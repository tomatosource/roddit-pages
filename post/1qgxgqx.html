22 Days till Ps & Cs

Baseball, at its absolute core, refuses to be rushed.

Over time, most sports have been optimized, and baseball followed suit.

It didn’t need to.

It says, or at least used to say,

No…Take your time.

Stand there and motherfuck your glove for the 14th time.

Let that stranger 60 feet, 6 inches away stare you down like you’re the only two left on earth.

Let the crowd fade until the only noise is your heartbeat. Find your breath and you’ll find yourself, I promise…

Then give me 96 up and in, or lace a 2-2 curve through the 4-hole.

Better yet, foul off 11 straight because you can, and because we’re getting into that damn bullpen early tonight.

This is the sport where the defense has the ball. Better, the only one where failure is the default, the expectation…celebrated for its reality, its humanity.

Here, you get 162 chances to fall in love, fall apart, and fall in love again.

This thing keeps score like a novelist and like a nerd with his spreadsheet…uniquely for everyone. More importantly, uniquely for you. However you want it.

It democratically immortalizes September call-ups and April no-hitters alike. You remember playing catch with dad in the backyard before dinner? 5’6” guys beat 6’7” guys then hug at next year’s all-star game.

How are you still dreaming in October when the dog days had you 15 games down and out in July?

You can tell me baseball is boring, but you can’t tell me it’s empty.

Me?

I’ll watch nothing happen for three hours…

because when you actually pay attention, more often than not…you’ll see something perfect.

And what somehow adds to that perfection, the universe will barely remember it.
