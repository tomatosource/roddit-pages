[Smith] Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones wants billionaire Michael Dell to help pay for new Spurs arena

Molly Smith:

Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones wants the world’s sixth-richest person to help pay for the Spurs’ planned \(1.3 billion downtown arena. Jones has asked Austin billionaire Michael Dell to consider paying some — if not all — of the city’s pledged \)489 million contribution to the project.

“I think it’s only fair,” Jones said of her request to Dell. “Normal folks in the community have routinely asked, be it at public comment or at other forums, ‘Why can’t we ask them (billionaires) to pay more.’ So, I in fact did that.”
