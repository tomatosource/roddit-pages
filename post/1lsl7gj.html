Why I prefer PoE 2 over PoE 1

The only reason for me is that when you get a good item drop in PoE 2, you can instantly use that item with no hassle. However in PoE 1, no matter how good the item is, if the gem slots on it are trash, you have to spend a shitload of resources to get it to 6-link. After you spent a shitload of resources to get a 6-link with the right colors and then you get an item with better stats, it can be an extremely unpleasant experience. I’ve tried to enjoy PoE 1 many times but the sole fact that you can’t use an item with good stats right away just puts me off. While PoE 2 might lack in endgame when compared to PoE 1, the skill system in PoE 2 is far superior and I believe the game will be much better than in predecessor given enough time for development and polish.
