Hoops Family Feud

If there was a tournament between families, who would take it? Rules can be made up as we go. I’m thinking 5v5, rosters can be filled out by siblings, cousins, parents, kids, etc. The arbitrary criteria of “family” can be something like “if someone drew a family tree the max distance between any two players is at most 4 connections.”

Lots of good candidates between Antetokounmpos, Thompson twins, Currys, Jameses, Wagners.. I might take the Holidays with 3 NBA caliber players and a sister who played at UCLA. I’m sure they could find a good 5th. 

We can even open it up to any time period so we can take the ‘12 Gasols vs the ‘17 Lopezes. Projecting into the future, maybe if Carlos can knock down open jumpers as an old head the ‘30 Boozers will be dominant.

Edit: I was thinking no time machines. Can’t just cherry pick each player’s prime but instead have to pick them all from a single year.
