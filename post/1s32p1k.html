Rush Campaign

​

Something that id really like to see in 0.5 is a “Rush Campaign”. My essential idea is once you’ve beaten the campaign 1-3 times. The next time you make a new character you have the option to pick a different kind of “campaign” where instead of starting at Act 1 that characters story will start from the end of all Acts you’ll be level 1 youll spawn in an area that has a chest with all the skill books and various stat items. The story is your character got amnesia from losing in a fight against whoever but the hooded one/doriyani is going to help you get back to full power. You have to go through 6 maps kill the bosses in each one, EXP gain in this area is drastically boosted based on level, the enemies scale with you, by the time you kill the 6th boss youll be level 65 ready to do whatever endgame content they release.

I dont hate the campaign but the best thing about this game is experimenting and all the various combinations you can do with different classes and ascendancys. I played 250 hours during 0.4 maybe more or less I know others have played more. Im not super efficient or a min maxer when it comes to the campaign . I doubt most players are running the campaign super effectively to finish it in 3-4 hours. I made alot of characters and for each character I made i had various hours just doing the same campaign.

I like the campaign dont get me wrong but it would be nice to think of a possible build and then get to make a new character and see how it runs without having to go through hours of campaign to try a build that may or may not pan out the way you want it to
