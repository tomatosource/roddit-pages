This team is statistically a very good team when Queta is on the court…and a tanking-level team when he isn’t. Amazing progress from him.

According to NBA.com’s On/Off numbers, the Celtics are a +18.3 when Queta is on the court this season. They are -5.1 when Garza is on the court, and -10.2 when Tillman is. That’s a +22.8 rating over Garza and +27.9 for Tillman.

So if you had to ask the two players this current team could ill-afford to lose to injury, the answers are Jaylen and Queta. As someone who has been a Queta doubter before, this is an impressive leap from him.
