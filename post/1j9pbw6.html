Guide to the Battle for the 4 seed in the East.

The Bucks, Pacers, and Pistons all currently sit exactly 18.5 games behind the Cavs with a month left in the regular season. The Bucks and Pacers are both 36-28 (.563), while Pistons sit at 37-29 (.561).

Remaining strength of schedule difficulty:

- Pistons: 7th. They have to play the Thunder twice, as well as the Cavs, Knicks and Grizzlies. However they do get to play the Wizards tomorrow, as well as the Raptors, Mavs and Pelicans later on. In my opinion their schedule is actually slightly easier than their rankings show. It seems to me that the strength of schedule rankings don’t take into account situations like the Mavericks becoming awful over night, making the Pistons schedule easier

- Bucks: 8th. They have to play several of the better Western Conference teams including the Thunder, Nuggets, Lakers and Warriors. Their easiest game remaining is likely the 76ers, but the bulk of their games are against moderate difficulty teams like the Suns, Hawks and Heat. .

- Pacers: 20th. The Pacers schedule still have to play the Cavs twice as well as the Thunder, Lakers and Nuggets, but they also have several theoretically easy games against the Wizards and Nets twice each, as well as the Hornets, Jazz, and 76ers.

Remaining Matchups:

- Sat 3⁄15: Pacers @ Bucks

- Fri 4⁄11: Bucks @ Pistons

- Sun 4⁄13: Pistons @ Bucks

Tie Breakers:

1. Head to Head:

- The Pacers have the head to head tie breaker over the Pistons as they won 3 of their 4 matchups this season

- The Bucks may have the head to head tie breaker over the Pacers, as they lead the season series 2-1, however, if the Pacers win Saturday, then the series becomes tied.

- The Bucks will likely hold the tie breaker over the Pistons as they lead the series 2-0, and the Pistons would have to win both of the remaining matchups to tie the series.

2. Division Records:

- Since all 3 teams are in the same division the division records tie breaker could come into play.

- As it currently stand the Pacers have the best division record at 8-5, followed by Milwaukee at 6-7. So far, the Pistons have struggled in the Central division with a 4-9 division record.

- With only 3 division games remaining the Pistons cannot pass the Pacers in the Division tie breaker, but the Bucks can move up or down.

- It’s worth noting that the Pacers still have to play the Cavs twice which could harm their division record.

3. Conference Win-Loss record:

- The Bucks currently have the best conference record as they are 26-19 against the East. The Pistons are just behind in that regard at 25-20, and the Pacers are 21-20.

- It’s unlikely that the tie breaker has to go this far.

- It’s worth noting the Pacers will likely improve their conference record as they have 5 remaining games against the bottom 3 teams in the conference.

Conclusion:

I personally think that my Pacers are in the best position to win the 4 seed as they have the easiest remaining schedule. However, Bucks fans should feel happy knowing that the 4 seed is very much theirs to lose. If they take care of Business and win their remaining head to head matchups with the Pacers and Pistons they will have a significant leg up, and they tie breaker over both the Pistons and Pacers. For the Pistons the path to the 4 seed while difficult, is very clear. Hang around with the Pacers and Bucks for the next few weeks, and beat the Bucks twice to end the season.
