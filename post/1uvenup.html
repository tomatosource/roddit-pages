Depleted Mana Rune deleted from Storage

GGG bestowed upon me great luck and had the Quest Item “Depleted Mana Rune” drop from my first fight with Uthred.
Not having any Characters anywhere near 10K Mana, I opted to store the item safely in the Reforging Bench as you can’t store Quest Items in your Chest.
A few weeks later (today) I decide to redecorate my Hideout a bit and ended up moving the Workbench around, which consequently deleted the Depleted Mana Rune…

So, if you have it stored anywhere outside your Character’s inventory, I would greatly suggest you to tread with caution. Don’t make my mistake ):
