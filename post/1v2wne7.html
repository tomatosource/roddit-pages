Who was the Best Player who was Never the Best in the League

The first name that comes to mind is Kevin Durant. Neither during his statistical peak from 2011-14 nor during his Warriors championship years (2017 & 2018) did the consensus rank him over LeBron James.

Some others would be

- Karl Malone: despite the MVPs, nobody had him over Jordan

- Kevin Garnett: Even at his peak, Kobe & Tim Duncan were still ahead
