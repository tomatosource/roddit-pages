Another warning about MLB app free trial. Incorrectly charged $237 with no option to refund.

I saw posts here about the a MLB app charging users for the free trial and thought it was just user error. Recently was doing some yard work and just wanted to just listen to a game and saw it was possible via the app.

Installed and opened the app, saw I needed a subscription to listen and that there was a free 7 day trial, so why not.

Signed up for the free trial and then enjoyed the game.

Next day, I see a charge for $237 on my credit card from MLB.

Check the app and it signed me up for the top tier plan on an annual renewal. No free trial and my only option was to cancel my auto renew for April 2027.

Reached out to Google to have the payment refunded and they said I can’t, to just reach out to the developer and provide my GPA order # and email for the account.

Frustrated, I did that too. Initially the dev responded asking for the GPA/email again. That was nearly a week ago and no refund has been processed and they are not responding to any followup emails.

Just another word of warning, don’t trust the free trial on this app and be prepared for them to incorrectly charge you for using the app.
