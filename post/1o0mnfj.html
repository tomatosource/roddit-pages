Is it useful for uncut gems to be in such short supply during the campaign?

Is there a balancing reason for this? Seems like it just forces people to play meta builds or the same ones they’ve already played and not experiment with anything. I was constantly running out of support gems at league start, and ended up changing my ascendancy because I couldn’t get enough support gems to change my build. Even while leveling a character at this point it’s a significant expense (15 ex) to buy uncut skill gems at campaign levels.

Seems like we should at least be able to buy skill gems with gold from vendors—if they want this to be a constraint during the campaign, they could price them high enough that it would be, without making it unduly restrictive to people who want to try stuff out.
