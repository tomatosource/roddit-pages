PoE1’s campaign feels amazing in comparison

Quit the PoE2 campaign after act 1, was just not having fun. Started a new character in PoE1 just to get a sense of progression, and holy shit that felt amazing in comparison. Even without movement speed boots I feel fast. There is actual rare gear that drops. I can deal with white mobs that surround me. I can look forward to meaningful support gems. There are impactful nodes on the passive tree that I can work towards.

After 7k hours in PoE1 and running the campaign over 250 times (estimate), I dreaded creating a new char over the last few leagues. Now, it feels like a breath of fresh air. That tells me a lot about the state of PoE2, I will skip this game for while
