MLB expansion draft scenario

Fun little game here as expansion has been in the news, albeit for other sports.

What if there was an MLB expansion draft this offseason?

Looking for a list from each team of what 15 players each team would protect.

Teams would also be able to protect an additional three after the first round and another three after the second round of the expansion draft so 21 players total could be protected.

All players with MLB experience or players on the 40 man roster would be eligible for protection and selection. Current free agents are not eligible.

Other players would need three years of minor league experience or four years for international signings to be eligible for selection or protection.

While I know that this isn’t happening this offseason or anytime soon, I thought it might be a fun exercise.
