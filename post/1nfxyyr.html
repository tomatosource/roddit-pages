Don’t scam yourself: a simple regex to filter price in async trade

Since some players started to put a lot of items with fixed prices (like waystones) and one with an extra digit to scam the buyer that buy everthing, here is a simple regex that you can use to avoid this trap (I saved in my exile exchante):

~(?:price|b/o)\s+(1[0-9]|2[0-9]|30)\s+ex

This one is filtering for 10 to 30ex, you can change the numbers to fit the purschase that you want (I case of doubt, put this in ChatGPT and ask to change to range that you like)
