POE 2 Rarity Issue?

Yeah I don’t have any graphs or in depth statistics or anything of the sort - but just wanted to pop in and say this:

I switched from using increased quantity tabs and opted instead for increased rare monster tabs.

Whatever they did to the item quantity - seems to be completely nullified by just simply increasing the number of rare monsters so yeah.

Simply put: 3x Rare Monster % Tabs, 3 Paranoia Slams, a desecration modifier that adds rare monster %, as well as just a high base rare monster % on the waystone = good drops. Again. Or something along those lines.

My Rarity% from gear totals out to 162% if this was of any use to anyone.

Seems to be working just fine for me - maybe even better than before on account of how small the maps are now, kinda just a bunch of rares all stacked into perfectly AOE sized groups.

Hope this helps my fellow Exiles.

Edit: Forgot to mention the Atlas passives! Stack AS MUCH Rare Monster%, Magic Monster%, and Pack Size as reasonably possible into your Atlas builds!!!!

Also grab as many “Waystone Modifier Effect” nodes as possible again within reason of course. This will quite exponentially increase your rare monster percentage

There’s also “Unstable Energies” notable skill which can have a ridiculously high impact on your percentage increases.

Also two more notables, “Remnants of Power” for 20% chance to double your tablet modifiers, and “Enigmatic Intensification” which is basically a deluxe version of “Waystone Modifier Effect”. Which will add an extra 9% boost to map modifiers - assuming you are using 3 tablets that is.

I was just running a map earlier that had 339% rare monster%, and that was without Unstable Energies or Remnants of Power having been active. If they would’ve been active, I easily could’ve been sitting around 500⁄600% total rare monster%.

I’m telling you - this strategy is just insane.
