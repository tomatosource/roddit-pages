120% Druid Predictions

Last post was about how the third Druid form is 110% a Treant and this post contains even more speculations on that topic with even higher degree of unreasonable certainty.

As all classes Druid will have 3 ascendancies, which potentially can be themed around Bear, Wolf and Plants. The most interesting to speculate about is the plants one, as there is 0 information about it.

I believe this plant theme is going to include thorns, poison and minion focus. Thorns and poison fit the plant theme perfectly and there is some thorns support in that region of the tree. Regarding the minions I believe that it’s unlikely that wolves are the only minions the Druid can summon, as it makes sense that Druid is going to be a high spirit class where you combine cast on gems + minions.

All these extra minions are going to be plant based (little treants and maybe these flower creatures). Here are few minion ideas of mine:

❁ Poison Mage Treant (similar to frost mage for Witch) which shoots poison and creates these poison puddles

❁ Thorned Treant some thick minion which scales from thorns damage

❁ Flower Ent which takes Spell Gems and casts them (like Warrior’s Ancestral Warrior Totem)

❁ Flower Ent Summoner which can create little flower totems with thorn surfaces

What do you think about these minion ideas? Would you like to see any of it in the game?
Please feel free to suggest other minion ideas, the more speculative the better, as maybe GGG will see this and implement it, even if this original speculation is way off.
