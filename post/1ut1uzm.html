PSA- Horror Story Aldur’s Legacy

- Running normal expedition maps

- Found a 10 runes! Aldur’s LEGACY, I thought, a cool 300 divs or something. So I can finally buy the +3 prism for my build! Yay!

- Build a nice chain of explosions. The map is looking JUICY. Lot of raw divs. Couple of exceptionals.

- Remember reading some posts about people losing out of 10 runes because of bug/glitches etc. So I PICK UP THE ALDURS LEGACY AND PUT IT IN MY INVENTORY.

- Continue through the map blasting everything.

- Uh oh stuttering starts. But I think. HEY I HAVE THE BIG TICKET ITEM IN MY INVENTORY SO ITS WHATEVER.

- Few seconds of freeze. My ping climbs up to 100k.

- Disconnection. I thought ok whatever.

- Log back in. The map portal is gone. I thought ok whatever.

- Open inventory AND THERE’S NOTHING THERE. ITS REVERTED TO PRE MAP STATE.

- Curse the gods, old and new.

TLDR- Portal out as soon as you pick your big ticket item in expedition maps. Shit has more bugs than a hoarder’s house.
