Should we have more drops restricted to low/mid level areas?..

Look at lesser/regular runes, or low level gem stones. They have some worth cause they aren’t overabundant in the late league. It’s good for economy and useful for people who are stuck on low(er) levels for whatever reason.

let’s say you’re trying to get your weak ass homebrew build off the ground. Or don’t care for the rat race and wanna play at your own pace. Or just new to the game and completely lost. Wouldn’t it be nice to be able to run campaign or T1-5 maps and still drop something you can eventually sell to people who are blasting though T16 and can’t bother with going back to farm some mid level shit?.. Division of labor, so to speak.

So what else can be divided into low/mid/high tiers and spread between campaign, low/mid maps and high tier maps/bosses?..

I think uniques. They already have hidden rarity tiers. What if all common uniques would only drop in campaign. It would immediately raise the price of all semi-useful items that are currently worth 1 chance shard. And all the build enabling uniques or good leveling items that are currently sell for few ex would cost divines.

Then theres mid level uniques. Let’s say they would only drop in T1-10 maps. No self-respecting endgame blaster would ever farm those maps. But there are plenty of people who live there and simply can’t handle higher tiers. Let them farm those mid level uniques for profit. And again, scarcity will push prices up.

And rare uniques could only drop in the very late game, restrictred to T15+ maps and pinnacle bosses. Or not. I don’t really care. Let them drop anywhere, cause they are so rare that most of us will never see them in the wild anyway.

…

What do you think of this idea?

And is there anything else that could be restricted to lower levels?
