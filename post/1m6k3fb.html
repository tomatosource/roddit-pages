I actually like the new CBA rules

I like it because it brings back the focus on team building rather than star building, something we honestly been missing since the 1980s and 90s

But it also prevents the likely hood of a dynasty happening like back in the 90s and 80s so to me it literally solved 2 problems within the NBA I always notice

  1. The lack of parity
  2. Team focus rather than individual focused

The new CBA could of been prevented if didn’t need to be like this but 2 players in specifically cause this shift to happen the way it did and those 2 names are

Kevin Durant and Lebron James

Something really bothered me and open my eyes to why the NBA is the way it is now and it’s just a quote that Durant said “the goal is longevity, some people quit and go play baseball” Then LeBron just laughs……

And that’s the problem right there

You got 2 of the greatest players of this generation telling you that individualism determines greatness rather than winning

It’s this same self absorbed mindset that make kids of this generation fans of players rather than teams and the actual game of basketball

Now imagine yourself as the CEO of the NBA What is more profitable in the long run?

Promoting team culture and focusing on team play style rather than a single individual

Or

Focus on finding that “next Jordan” and riding him to make money off and hoping that someone else will come

And I don’t blame the NBA for picking the 1st option

Look at what LeBron did 1st

He left Cleveland then took an all star with him to Miami (which again at the time was a major deal but looking back at it, it wasn’t as bad as Kevin Durant). This right here again wasnt the biggest factor of the player movement era of the 2010s but it was a major influence on players.

Now Kevin Durant……this right here is what truly killed the nba of what it used to be

You had a organically made team in the warriors Facing LeBron and the cavs which was perfect for the league. This is exactly what the league was missing those rivalry matchups that we remember back in the 80s and 90s. This was our magic vs bird moment, both 1-1 in the finals against each other, it was going to be the most exciting time since the 90s and 80s.

You know what Durant did…..he looked at that and said “nah ima do my own thing” and ruined basketball forever.

Instead of him toughing it out after a 4-3 lost to the warriors that he was up 3-1 against. He did the worst thing imaginable not only in sports but just as a Man.

He joined the team that beat him and not as a 2nd option 3rd bench whatever he was a MVP caliber player that was fully capable of winning decides to do that.

I don’t think yall understand like how rare that happens in sports or even in life.

But these are just 2 moments You got Durant moving to the nets and then the suns now the rockets And Lebron to the lakers

But this is the exact reason Lebron and Durant team hopping made the NBA look at a different perspective when it comes to player empowerment

And I just got back to that interview right there

As a commissioner trying to make money can you really rely on these 2 guys to be the image you want for your league?

People who is more focused on individual success rather than team success

I think they notice it’s just not a winning formula anymore and they gotta move on.

But that’s just my perspective on it
