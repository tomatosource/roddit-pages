I built Letterboxd for NBA games

A bit of background

Hey everyone, so like a lot of you, I’ve been watching basketball my entire life. For as long as I can remember, it’s been a part of my daily routine. Every now and then after watching an amazing game, or sometimes a terrible one, I would think to myself that game was a 10⁄10. It felt intense and had me engaged the entire time. I would have all of these thoughts on the game and yet they would disappear in group chats or social media not even 24 hours later.

I felt like there was something missing in the NBA community and that’s when the idea of Hoopsify hit me.

I wanted a platform where fans like me could rate games, leave thoughts, and even rate individual player performances. By doing this, it automatically creates a personal collection of games throughout the season.

I really wanted to build something that brought fans together and this felt like the right way to do it. It took me a while to build but I think it’s finally ready for its first intro to the public.

What you can do

  1. Browse games by date
  2. Log what you watched (full game, partial, rewatch)
  3. Rate the game and individual players
  4. See community POTG and Weakest Link based on real user votes
  5. Follow friends and see their takes in your feed

Launched today and it’s totally free to use. Just create an account and it’s all yours to explore. I would love to hear what r/nba thinks, this community would have the most valuable feedback I could receive. It’s totally empty at the moment so feel free to go in and rate, log, and comment as you wish!

It’s still very much a work in progress but I hope you enjoy. I’ll be reading any and all comments so if you have any feedback or thoughts please let me know!

Try it here → https://www.hoopsify.com
