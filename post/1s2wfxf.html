Several newly discovered unofficial stats added to Basketball Reference — most notable is Wilt Chamberlain blocking 446 shots in his final season (1972-73)

The best way to learn about this update is check out Mike Lynch’s blog post here: https://www.sports-reference.com/blog/2026/03/several-dozen-newly-discovered-unofficial-totals-added-to-basketball-reference/

To recap: with some help, we were able to track down and add some “unofficial” stats to Basketball Reference.

  • Wilt Chamberlain had 446 blocks in 1972-73, which is the second-most in a season on record, trailing only Mark Eaton’s 456 in 1984-85.
    • No other player 36 or older has even managed 200 blocks shots in a season, let alone 400+.
  • Bob McAdoo, who won the Rookie of the Year in 1972-73, blocked 216 shots.
  • McAdoo’s teammate in 1972-73, Elmore Smith, replaced Chamberlain with the Lakers in 1973-74. The Lakers 1973-74 media guide mentions that the NBA had a ‘new statistical category’ in 1972-73 and that Smith blocked 300 shots.

We also uncovered a handful of other stats from the 1970-71, 1971-72, and 1972-73 Portland Trail Blazers teams. Newspaper clippings can be found in the blog post.
