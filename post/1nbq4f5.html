Does anyone buy Mark Cuban’s Uncle Dennis logic?

I listened to that entire podcast between Mark Cuban and Pablo Torre: https://youtu.be/NwXcEG5BA6U

Cuban’s argument seems to be that it’s more likely Ballmer knew nothing and this was just a case of Uncle Dennis going to Aspiration, (who was still a month away from announcing the 300 mil clippers sponsorship but maybe Dennis knew it was coming) and telling them “hey if you don’t also pay my guy 48 million dollars directly I’m gonna make sure he doesn’t sign an extension, leaves the clippers, and your future sponsorship will be worthless!”… and that Ballmer was never informed any of that was happening. Curious if anyone actually buys that logic that a team sponsor would be so concerned about a player leaving that they’d pay 50 million in bribe money to make sure he stays. To the sponsor isn’t the clippers brand value just valuable as a NBA team with or without Kawhi around?

Then if you actually subscribe to Cuban’s logic other aspects just seem even dumber. Like the 7 sources of Pablo all saying that it was common knowledge the whole thing was salary cap circumvention and the owners admitting it to them. Cuban argues the owners would have just said that to just shut them up instead of admit the money was something for more insidious. Wouldn’t it have just been way easier for the owners to tell the employees “yeah we decided to pay Kawhi 50 Mil to ensure he’d stay a clipper to protect our sponsorship value”? How is that worse than “Steve ballmer forced us to do it”?

Then the timing of it all seems even dumber if subscribe to Cuban’s logic. Ballmer invested 50 million into aspiration in September 2021. That was a month after kawhi signed his 4 year extension (August), 3 months before Kawhi sets up his llc (November). You can imagine it being Ballmer, who now had a great ass kissing perk-heavy relationship with Kawhi saying “hey if you sign this extension in a couple months Ill get you a no show sponsorship deal… you have my multi-billionaire word…. I just gotta get the money to them. It’s gonna take a few weeks. Then you’ll set up an LLC and they will give you the money just give me time to get it sorted”… it makes less senses that this sponsor, who wasn’t even a sponsor until September 2021, was talking to uncle Dennis a month prior and saying “No Kawhi don’t leave! Sign the extension! If you don’t our 300 mil investment into the clippers next month will be worthless! Sign it. Then let’s wait 3 months and you can set up an LLC for us to wire you the money next year” and then kawhi being like “ok I trust you guys. I’ll sign the extension!”
