Vinnie Johnson doesn’t get talked about much when people talk about the Bad Boys Pistons.

He was a key player in that back-to-back run but nowadays when people talked about the Bad Boy Pistons, they don’t seem to talk about Vinnie aka The Microwave much.

He was very critical imo. I mean, when the starters rested, it was literally throw Vinnie the ball and watch him go to work. 12ppg over two runs isn’t attention-catching but very solid considering the context.

The best thing about him was that even though he was there just for offense and was good at it, he never messed up the team’s chemistry or anything. Which was interesting considering what he was tasked to do was pretty individualistic.
