Trial of Chaos, probably best early-/mid-game farm

Didn’t really realize it until now, but once you can run them reliably with lvl 40’ish and have the currency exchange, it’s a guaranteed ~25 exalts (50 with 7 stages) for the soul cores plus items that may actually be useful.

Wish I had realized it back then, but maybe there are some new players who can take advantage of this.
