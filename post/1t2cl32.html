We need more rim pressure

It’s not necessarily a problem that we take a lot of threes, and we had great looks today, it’s that our shots are so far from the basket. Even in the regular season we had highest average shot distance in the league. Near the bottom in paint points. Constant ISO midrange from JB was even more frustrating in the clutch, it felt like without Tatum nobody could get to the rim consistently. Biggest change for me needs to be a new center and make some move around Pritchard/ brown/ white to get a rim-attacking playmaker. Brown doesn’t quite have the burst to get there anymore. In our title season we were three point dependent but scored much more around the rim
