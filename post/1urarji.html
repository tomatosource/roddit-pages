Brad’s Blueprint for a Post-Jaylen Future

I’m sure we’ve all litigated the Jaylen Brown trade enough for a lifetime at this point, but regardless of how you feel, it’s time we start looking toward the future without Jaylen Brown. As we know, Paul George is on the books for \(54MM this year, with a \)57MM player option for next season, making him an expiring in the 2027-2028 league year. Of course, many have suggested using his large expiring salary + a boatload of picks to land a big fish like Luka, Jokic, or Ant next offseason. But while that’s certainly fun to dream about, I doubt such an unlikely scenario is plan A for the C’s front office.

My read is that Brad Stevens is looking to construct a team not too dissimilar to the 2024 squad, which wouldn’t be possible with Jaylen’s 35% max on the books. That 2024 team was bolstered by three elite role players in White, Holiday, and Porzingis, all of whom were making around half of Jaylen’s 2026 salary. That comment from Brad’s presser about tying up so much of the cap and usage rate on two players was telling. All the analytics talk has spawned a million jokes, but the idea that the Celtics’ ideal lineup looks something like Tatum + 4 Derrick Whites probably isn’t too far off. If Brad and Zarren can construct a new crop of high-level role players around Tatum, we may have a contending roster again sooner rather than later.

It’s crucial to remember that other teams will be feeling the second apron squeeze too; we’ve already benefitted by stealing Mitchell Robinson from the Knicks. Pretty soon, teams like OKC and even San Antonio will have to make some hard decisions, and the “optionality” Brad talked about in his presser has positioned us to benefit. Having the full MLE + George’s expiring next offseason means we could be well-positioned to snag several contributors. I would keep an especially close eye on OKC; with JDub and Chet on rookie maxes and Cason Wallace up for an extension, it’s only a matter of time before at least one of them ends up a cap casualty.

Of the three, I think Wallace makes a ton of sense for us as a younger Jrue Holiday replacement with some offensive upside. Chet is maybe the player most similar to Porzingis physically, but I’d bet he’s the one OKC elects to hold onto next to SGA. Besides, if we can’t land a truly dominant inside presence, it may prove more advantageous to maintain a cost-effective platoon of bigs like we currently have with Robinson and Queta. Whether a core of Pritchard/Wallace/White/Tatum is capable of beating the best of the West remains to be seen, but regardless, I believe turning Jaylen’s impending $70 million salary slot into 2-3 elite role players may be our best path back to contention under the new apron rules, and I think Brad agrees.
