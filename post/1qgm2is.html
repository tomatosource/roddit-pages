Most times in an MLB career going between two specific teams?

While looking into the earlier post about palindromic careers, I found out about Casey Lawrence’s propensity for jumping between the Blue Jays and Mariners. In particular, he has directly gone from one to the other a total of three times.

  1. In 2017, Lawrence was DFA’ed by the Jays and claimed on waivers by the Mariners. He played for a couple of years and then went off to Japan.

  2. Last year, after having played for the Jays again in 2022 and for the Cardinals in 2023, he was called up by the Mariners, shortly thereafter DFA’ed on April 26, and then claimed on waivers by the Jays.

  3. After a single appearance on April 29, he was promptly DFA’ed by the Jays, returning once again to the Mariners who called him up on May 20.

All in all, looking at his career progression, he has 6 stints with the two teams, having directly gone from one to the other a total of 3 times. The same goes for Harold Baines, who jumped between the White Sox and O’s 3 times.

My question is, is 3 the record for the most times an MLB player has gone directly between two specific teams? I’m allowing for any means of movement between the two teams, including trades, waiver claims, free agent signings, etc. so long as the player doesn’t make any other major league appearances in between (minor league stints allowed.)

Similar question, what’s the record if you only look at one direction, i.e. going only from team A to team B? Bobo Newsom has two such instances with two different pairs of teams, having gone twice from the St. Louis Browns to the Washington Senators, as well as from the Senators to the Philadelphia Athletics. However, I don’t know of any instance of a player going from one specific team to another 3 or more times.
