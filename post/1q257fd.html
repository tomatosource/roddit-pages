[ESPN] People in the league believe Steve Ballmer’s good reputation with his fellow owners and his $150B net worth are important factors to consider in the Aspiration investigation: “You have to be careful getting into a [legal] fight with someone like Ballmer. You have to have the evidence.”

Source: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/47476203/nba-intel-lebron-trade-season-thunder-wemby-expansion-top-2026-storylines

The interest in the Clippers across the league is more off the floor than on it. The Aspiration-Kawhi Leonard investigation has been going on behind the scenes for months. The NBA is outsourcing the investigation, which is standard procedure, to New York law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz, and the billable hours are mounting.

No announced timetable has been announced, but with the Clippers hosting All-Star Weekend in February, a worldwide spotlight will be on the situation if it is not resolved by then. It will be an early 2026 story, however it plays out.

Clippers owner Steve Ballmer has earned a good reputation with fellow owners, and his net worth is around $150 billion. When speaking to people in the league, these are important factors to consider.

“You have to be careful getting into a [legal] fight with someone like Ballmer. You have to have the evidence,” one rival team president said. (Ballmer and the Clippers have strongly denied salary cap circumvention allegations that came out after reporting by the “Pablo Torre Finds Out” podcast.)
