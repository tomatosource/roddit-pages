No team has beaten OKC back-to-back in the playoffs since Mavericks in 2023-2024. Spurs have to be the first since in order to avoid elimination… OKC is 9-0 following a loss

Here is an interesting statistic to highlight how OKC makes adjustments and responds to losses in these past 2 postseasons and what the spurs must do now to win the series.

OKC since their loss to the mavericks in 2023-2024 has not lost 2 games in a row and have been dominant every game following a loss. They are 9-0 following a loss.

Here are the series they have played since losing 4-2 to Dallas when they dropped game 2&3 in a row as well as 5&6 to them. They have had home court advantage in all of these series.

2024-2025 1st Round Memphis vs OKC:

OKC sweeps to win the series 4-0.

2024-2025 2nd Round Denver vs OKC:

OKC loses game 1 and wins game 2 to tie 1-1.
OKC loses game 3 and wins game 4 to tie 2-2.
OKC loses game 6 and wins game 7 to win the series 4-3.

2024-2025 Western Conference Finals Minnesota vs OKC:

OKC loses game 3 and wins game 4 and 5 to win the series 4-1.

2024-2025 Finals Indiana vs OKC:

OKC loses game 1 and wins game 2 to tie 1-1.
OKC loses game 3 and wins game 4 to tie 2-2.
OKC loses game 6 and wins game 7 to win the championship 4-3.

2025-2026 1st Round Suns vs OKC:

OKC sweeps to win the series 4-0.

2025-2026 2nd Round Lakers vs OKC:

OKC sweeps to win the series 4-0.

2025-2026 Western Conference Finals Spurs vs OKC

OKC loses game 1 and wins game 2 to tie 1-1.
OKC loses game 4 and wins game 5 to go up 3-2.

To advance to the finals the spurs now must be the first since the Luka + Kyrie Mavericks to win two against the thunder in a row in the playoffs and knock them out by winning a Game 6 at home and a Game 7 on the road to hand them their first back to back loss.
