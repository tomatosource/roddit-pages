Bad Timing

The Celtics sale really came at the worst possible time. not only did it come as the team was fully stocked as much as any team in the league or more but it came after rules changed recently that allowed private equity to own a large chunks of NBA teams.

this the buyer that the Celtics agreed to was the by far and away the most dependent on private equity of any sports team in America. history. (I’m not sure about soccer/futbol although I think they may allow some private equity as well.).

in fact at one point there were articles coming out that the NBA had to actually get them to make some tweaks because the local Boston guy that everyone was so excited about, technically owned less than 15% of the team which was less than the private equity firms.

my understanding is he had to go back and tweak with these percentages to meet league rules. the point is, while people were celebrating a local owner the fact is that they sold the team to private equity effectively. private equity firms do not care if the Celtics win. 3 titles in a row. they would rather lose in the first round of the playoffs and make a hundred million dollars then win another championship and lose 100 million. that might sound obvious but some fans seem to be holding on to this hope that if we win a championship the worst will be spared. I think the success of the team this playoff run is probably completely irrelevant to the coming moves.

Despite the face of the new ownership group technically being a local guy, we should expect an ownership group that is uniquely cold and indifferent as it comes to caring about the Celtics as anything but a vehicle for further profit.
