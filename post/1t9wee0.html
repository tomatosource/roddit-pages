After a very good 2021 season Aaron Judge gambled on himself and turned down a 7 year 216 million dollar extension and it paid off. What are some other extreme examples of players declining extensions for better or for worse?

Aaron Judge finished the 2021 season with 6 WAR, and had established himself as one of the best players in baseball. However he wasn’t even close to the Judge we know now. He went from one of the best players in the league to one of the best hitters in the history of baseball. He turned down a 7 year 216 million dollar extension after 2021 despite having an extensive injury history. The gamble paid off as he went on to break the AL home run record and sign a 9 year 360 million dollar contract, which insanely has somehow become a bargain deal for the Yankees as he has cemented himself as one of the best hitters in baseball history.

Has any other player gambled on themselves and have it pay off as much as it did for Judge? On the other hand what are some of the worst cases of players gambling on themselves and losing millions?
