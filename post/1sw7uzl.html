Casual new player - my experience as melee [Rant]

Casual, boomer. Play 1-2 hours after work and on weekends. Basic boomer gear; don’t know how to Div farm yet. I understand at least 4 mod crafting. Not following a build guide.

This is my first season. I played a caster and easily killed normal arbiter. Range/casters are usually better for learning. Then I tried melee titan build, and I thought I accidentally played a different game. Just starting blue T15 maps and my experience was so different, frustrating and negative compared to range.

My map clear seems OK but bossing can be long fights. More likely my own skill/tree/itemization issue but I’m working on it. TEMPLE is terrible as melee. 75% of my deaths from temple…

Here are my biggest complaints:

  1. Animation lock: Many melee skills lock you into an animation but can be extremely punishing in game with mob mechanic bloat.
  2. Mob one-shot melee attacks: Excessive mob melee attacks that can easily almost kill you. Excessive dodge-rolling; I develop dodge-roll fatigue. Greatly reduce up-time on bosses compared to range.
  3. There’s no life: I struggle to obtain &gt; 2500 life. This feels awful especially with the affix bloat.
  4. Low defensive layering: My impression is that there’s no “fun” defensive layering besides that standard armor, life, res, armor apply to elemental.
  5. Attack speed stacking required: I’m playing a sunder, stun/bleed, hammer build. Default attack speeds feel awful. Pathing to attack speed on tree feels bad. Default attack speed can get you killed mid animation. Certain mobs mini-stun mid animation (like in temple).
  6. Excessive anti-melee mechanics: Excessive use of anti-melee mob skills like temporal shield, volatile plants, temporal chains, over-tuned white ranged mobs, high mob densities. Even the mana drain ring feels bad when required to constantly dodge melee attacks. Abyss spawns can be a nightmare.
  7. Environmental obstructions: Map is FULL of tiny obstructions that make melee movement feel awful. Little stuff like fences, logs, boxes, ledges. Many maps have too many narrow areas and choke-points. Mechanics like abyss fissures can block attacks like Sunder.
  8. Attribute requirements: It appears Giant’s Blood is mandatory. Pathing to attribute nodes in Merc tree is extremely unsatisfying. Just to be able to equip weapons. Please tell me if there’s a trick to giant’s blood.
  9. Active block: not fun. period.
