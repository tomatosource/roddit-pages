James Dolan on the second apron - “There’s certain things in the NBA that you’d have to be suicidal to do.. One of them is called the second apron. We cannot go into the second apron. I’ll write as big of a check as possible, but I can’t write a check that goes into the second apron.”

Knicks owner James Dolan was asked if the Knicks could keep their championship team together and his response was no because of the second apron. I think this is the first time an owner has come out and spoken so negatively about the second apron. Us fans know it has been handicapping teams moves the last few years but now ownership are publicly disparaging it.
