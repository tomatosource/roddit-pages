Inadequate prices for items from NPC in Acts 1-2

I’ll say it right away: I suggest cutting prices at least 2 times in Acts 1-2.

Now to the point. I started leveling up my 1 character. At the start of the game you have no items, no currency. Nothing to earn money from, which means that trading with players is closed to you. So… trading with NPCs remains. But even here, trading is closed to you. In Act 1, accessories cost about 1-1.5k. Items (magic) cost about 300-700 (or even more). And now you have no gold to buy items, no normal equipment.

To fight bosses you need resistances. But rings are too expensive. In the entire 1st act you can earn gold only for 2-3 rings by PICKING UP EVERYTHING (lol, in the entire first act I only came across ONE ring for sale at Una’s). Over the course of 2 acts, I teleport to the city every few minutes and sell things to merchants to earn at least some crumbs of gold. I literally pick up EVERYTHING.

I’ll make a reservation right away: I sometimes buy items with sockets, but if they are not more expensive than 500 gold. I also sometimes bought a pair of boots and gloves.

And now to a more illustrative example: in the middle of Act 2, I saw armor from a merchant for 6.5k gold. I only had 4k gold. The armor was better than mine. But while we are farming, we will level up. And by increasing the level, we update the merchant’s assortment. While I was walking around the map and collecting everything I get almost 6k. But I also got a lvl up. I came to the merchant and I see NEW armor, which costs 8.4k. That is I need to farm 2k gold again, while I will 100% get a new level and the assortment will be updated again.

And now to the most “delicious”: normal items on the marketplace are quite expensive. For starting levels and especially for those who are just starting first character. And here I am with 4 regals, 2 exalts and 1 alchemy, I can’t buy anything to improve my character.

Awesome! No loot. What rare drops is 90% not for my class, it is impossible to sell it, because it is garbage. There is no currency to normally craft at least starting equipment, and buying items in the NPC store is too expensive. In Act 2, I still walk with 5-10% resistances, because nothing drops for me with resistances.

No resistances, no currency, no equipment, no damage (as I remember in PoE1 we could already wear 3-4 link items in Act 2). Awesome!

Why not give players the opportunity to buy cheap things in Acts 1-2? Considering that they WILL DEFINITELY buy equipment from NPCs out of necessity and try to improve it.
