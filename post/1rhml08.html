As a fan, what do you want?

I read all the posts and comments and some agree with and some leave me scratching my head. So I’ll ask this, knowing that the Celtics just can’t possibly be the odds on favorite to win the championship every season what are you looking for as a fan? Some context to go along with our rants and raves. For example:

Come playoff time I want to feel confident that we can play with the best of the best and beat them if everyone does their job. Knowing that on paper you should destroy anyone is fun when it happens, but I don’t need that. I want to believe we aren’t dreamers and have a real shot at the championship.

In the regular season I want to be entertained. I want the guys to play hard and smart. I want the same from the coaches. I want them to be good dudes both outwardly and to each other. Good vibes. I don’t want to buy a title and I want to see draft picks, undrafted free agents and unproven NBA free agents come here and flourish in this system and become legit contributors to a deep playoff run, hopefully ending in a conference final, an NBA final, and a lord willing, a championship.

What about you?
