Tatum is poised to break into the top 30 NBA career playoff points this post-season

Tatum currently sits at #37 all time with 2836 playoff points. An additional 145 points this post-season would push him to #30. 280 points would potentially lift him to #25 depending on Kawhi, who is currently at 3084 points. Right now, Tatum is 344 points behind Pierce, who is #24 all time with 3180. It’d be a fun story line if the Celtics go the distance and Tatum overcomes Pierce during the finals or something like that. Tatum has more career playoff points than Bill Russell, Charles, KG, Jokic, Giannis, Ewing, and Stockton. He has scored nearly 1000 points more in the playoffs than Kyrie (lol). He’s got a good shot at cracking the top 15 or top 10 if his early career success continues.
