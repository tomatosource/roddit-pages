Grant Williams just pledged $1.5M to Tennessee! What a legend.

​Just saw some amazing news about Grant Williams. He’s pledging a massive $1.5 million back to the University of Tennessee, which is just incredible.

He’s spreading the love, too, with some of the money going to the Men’s Basketball Excellence Fund and the softball program, and another big chunk setting up a scholarship endowment for the business school.

It’s awesome to see a former student-athlete give back in such a big way. He’s calling Tennessee his “home,” and this really shows how much he means it. Love to see it.
