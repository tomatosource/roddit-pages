The reason why we have an U25 list always is because usually people consider the ages 26 - 34 years old as a Player’s peak/prime confidence interval.

So, if you win an MVP or FMVP U25, it means you got it before entering your prime. I already made an MVP list past few years.

Now, I made one for FMVPs.

From 1999 - 2025, Only 4 players won Finals MVP under 25 years old: Kawhi, Parker, Wade, and Duncan.

It happened back to back in 2006 (Wade) and 2007 (Parker)
