2025 MLB World Series Schedule and Live Stream Official Channels

The Los Angeles Dodgers will battle the Toronto Blue Jays for the 2025 MLB World Series crown — a matchup that’s pure baseball history in the making.

Toronto earned their ticket to the Fall Classic in stunning fashion — George Springer launched a go-ahead three-run homer in the seventh inning of Game 7 to lift the Jays over the Seattle Mariners 4-3.

It’s been 32 years since Toronto’s last World Series appearance — back when they claimed back-to-back titles in 1992 & 1993, defeating the Braves and Phillies. 🇨🇦🔥

Meanwhile, the Dodgers return to familiar October territory, hungry to add another trophy to their legendary postseason legacy. Expect elite pitching, electric bats, and maybe a few walk-offs along the way.
