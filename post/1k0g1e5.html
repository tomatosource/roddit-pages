PoE2 Feedback After Some Time Played – Feels Restrictive and Sluggish

After spending a decent amount of time in PoE2, I wanted to share some feedback and see if others feel the same way. Overall, I’m still playing and trying to enjoy it, but certain aspects are really dragging down the experience for me:

&nbsp;

  • Maps feel quite empty. There’s often too little density, and the lack of consistent rewards makes them feel unrewarding.
  • Movement and combat feel slow. Compared to PoE1, everything feels a bit clunky and less responsive. This might be a personal issue due to comparing it to PoE1.
  • Atlas passives take too long to acquire. Progression through the tree feels sluggish, and it makes early to mid mapping feel unrewarding.
  • Expedition vs Delirium tree progression seems unbalanced. Getting Expedition points is fairly easy, while Delirium requires full Simulacrum clears to even start progressing the tree. Feels a bit unfair considering how much harder Delirium content is.
  • Crafting feels worse than ever. It’s almost entirely RNG. Recombination is a nice addition, but combining desired mods and high tiers gives you a &lt;3% success chance. That doesn’t feel good. Fracture orbs are a step into the right direction for a more deterministic approach to crafting.
  • Wisps often lead you on bad routes and don’t provide enough rewards to justify following them, especially compared to other league mechanics.
  • The dopamine loop is missing. There’s not enough “wow” factor, loot explosions, or satisfying moments.
  • Everything feels overly restrictive. It seems like every mechanic has to come with a downside, and it just stacks up to create an exhausting experience.
  • EU servers have been a mess. Disconnects on a daily basis.
  • The only reason I’m having some fun is because I’m playing a one-button LS Amazon build that deletes everything. If I weren’t, I probably wouldn’t stick around.

&nbsp;

Anyone else feeling the same way? I really want to love this game, but it feels like too many systems are built around slowing you down and punishing you for trying to play efficiently.
