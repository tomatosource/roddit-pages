Gem Studded (Gemling Notable) is Not Working as Written

I can confirm it doesn’t work as the description in game says it does. It is a bug or it’s worded incorrectly.

Gem Studded says:

“For each colour of Socketed Support Gem that is most numerous, gain:

Red: Hits against you have no Critical Damage Bonus.
Blue: Skills have 30% less cost.
Green: 40% less Movement Speed Penalty from using Skills while Moving.”

Previously: you could attain all bonuses by maintaining the same quantity of socketed support gems of each color. For example, 5 red supports, 5 green supports, and 5 blue supports socketed in your skill sheet would mean you get all three bonuses.

Today, after testing:
I have 16/16/16 (16 support gems of each color socketed) across both weapon sets and I do not receive the bonus of green or red, only blue. I only LOSE the blue gem buff of -mana cost when I go down to 13⁄16 blue, even with red and green still at 16 socketed supports each. To be clear, I have the blue support gem buff at 14⁄16 blue supports socketed even with 16⁄16 red and 16⁄16 green supports also socketed (which is the the opposite of how this is supposed to work).

Possible explanations (requires more testing):

(1) supports socketed into weapons don’t count (which is NOT how it used to work and that isn’t explained anywhere, and/or

(2) the weapon set you’re not using don’t count towards Gem Studded (which, again, isn’t how it used to work and isn’t explained anywhere as not valid).

Hopefully this gets patched soon as it makes my gemling much worse off.
