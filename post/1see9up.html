Simulacrum needs some love

I’ll start by saying I love doing Simulacrum. It’s non stop action. When your build is not completely online it can be really challenging. Even when your build is online, it does require attention. You cannot be doing other things, it might cost u a run (I’m sure ppl will point to afk simu builds and tell me I’m wrong, but let’s ignore those).

But I find that a few changes in the recent patches, along with previous design choices make for some odd things in simu.

  1. Value of distill emotions - since we don’t instill maps with emotions anymore (which was a great change), there is no real purpose to this currency other than anoint. Breach, ritual and expedition currency have crafting purpose. Deli needs that too.

  2. Lack of XP - I understand and agreed with the change to not gain or lose XP during Pinnacle encounters when it was made. But now, after grinding simu, I feel it’s unfair to bucket simu in with the other Pinnacle encounters. Rest of those are simply just a single boss fight. Your XP gain is very limited. But in simu, 15 waves is generally a 15 minute run. I feel like simu has been unfairly bucketed in with those other encounters. Simu should give XP.

3 Build problems - simu monsters have a quirk, they don’t leave corpses. So certain archetypes just can’t complete this content. I’ve personally experienced this with ice attack builds that rely on the Herald of Ice. I’m not sure, but I assume other archetypes, like raise dead type builds or wyvern builds that consume corpses for charges have issues with this (please correct me if I’m wrong). Would love it if more archetypes had access to this content. I’m not asking to make anything easier, I think the difficulty is at a great spot, but I would love to farm this next season too, but don’t want to exclude any type of builds I might want to do because of this quirk.

What do you all think about Simulacrum? Is it liked? Hated? Needs changes?
