[Guillory] The Houston Rockets aren’t looking to the future. They want to compete for a championship now.

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6825731/2025/11/21/reed-sheppard-rockets-improvement-experience/?source=user_shared_article Reed Sheppard’s improvement helps Rockets’ NBA title chances grow

Sheppard is slowly finding his way and turning into the player Houston hoped he would become. His recent production has provided a glimpse into just how much potential he has.

However, the Rockets aren’t looking to the future. They want to compete for a championship now, and a key part of that puzzle is Sheppard learning from his experiences over the next few months so he’ll be ready for his first real taste of the postseason.

—

The piece in question is mostly about the evolution of Reed Sheppard as the season has gone on, but I thought this small tidbit here might be worth posting. If the Rockets’ mindset is “win-now” then they’ll likely be buyers at the deadline. Potentially using the 2027 pick from Brooklyn and/or Phoenix to get a piece they believe might swing the odds in their favor in a matchup against OKC and/or Denver.
