Following this three-team deal with Boston and Atlanta, Brooklyn currently possesses the 8th, 19th, 22nd, 26th, and 27th picks in tomorrow’s NBA draft, with makes up one-sixth of the entire first round.

Reporting has been that the Nets are actively looking to trade up in the draft, and they are well-equipped to do just that tomorrow night.
