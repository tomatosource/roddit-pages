I hope a dev reads this, I’m blown away.

I finally had a chance to dig into this game and I have to say from a console player perspective this is heaven.

The controls feel amazing and the pacing and movement in combat is so much fun.

Twin stick shooter style ranged comabt !in this kind of game !

It feels fantastic, bringing me back to the early xbox days and some of those twin stick shooters.

Then it has good fair combat that feels like I’m playing am excellent well tuned Hades style roguelike , rolling, disengaging, parrying…its so much fun.

I then decided to create a party to see how multi-player was and within 5 minutes of putting up my group I had 3 other players roaming around with me taking on the story quest and areas, and everything scaled…flawless.

I just wanna say as someone who played Diablo 2 on his Playstation as a teen with my buddies while we stayed up late thank you for capturing the way that felt in a modern package.

I’ve tried all the other ARPGs, even poe 1 and nothing feels this good, since titan quest ive been waiting for the next modernization of the genre and I’m soooooo happy this studio has the product, because I know from poe 1 where this can go and I am 100000% here for the ride.
