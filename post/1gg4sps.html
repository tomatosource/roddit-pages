This is our first chance, as a fan base, to prove we aren’t going to overreact to individual losses like last year

Admittedly, I was in the negative camp last year. Too many years of choking and underperformance for me to remain positive during certain games last year. However, Ive reformed myself this year and I believe the rest of the sub should as well. We are the defending champs and the best team in the league. Regular season games against the Indiana Pacers before Halloween simply don’t matter as much as they did in years past. We didn’t really come to play today and the Pacers had a point to prove after we swept them last season. Despite this, we still somehow almost won (without KP) and are now 4-1 on the season. For us this year, its about getting a top 3 seed, playing our best come playoffs, and most importantly, staying healthy. If we are healthy come playoffs, idgaf where we finish, we’ll run through the east. In years past I wouldn’t subscribe to this logic because the celtics had notoriously choked in the playoffs frequently. However, now that they’ve proved they can win the title, I don’t think we should freak out on them for dropping a game against the pacers in october after winning 4 straight. Now if this sparks a losing streak, then that could be a problem but otherwise, we have more important things to worry about.
