[Murphy] Jakob Poeltl is back. He will be on a minutes restriction.

Source: https://bsky.app/profile/blakemurphy.bsky.social/post/3memkxamko22j

Poeltl is on an unspecified minutes restriction. Darko joked it’s capped at 48.

Team has talked about being smart with the long-run in mind as they build him back up. For his part, Poeltl told us on the weekend that his goal is to play free and not think about it when he’s out there.

The Raptors center will play his first minutes since an early exit from the Brooklyn Nets on December 21st (He played 6 minutes in that game). He’ll be on a minutes restriction but for the first time in over a month, the Raptors will have their starting 5 available for a game.
