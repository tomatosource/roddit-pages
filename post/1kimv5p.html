A few QOL changes I would like to see

First of all, I love the new item drop rates in .2g. It makes it much easier for a casual player like myself to enjoy the game, trying out exalts on items I found as well as trading for items.

One thing I would like to see though is a way to bypass the campaign on second and subsequent character. Once you finish the campaign, it would be nice to start a new character at level 65 (or some high level) and you completely outfit the character from your stash. You would have to go back and complete the bonus quests if you want get those benefits, but at least it will save you from having to run a new class through the entire campaign again. I love the campaign, but doing it every character you start is tedious.

I would also like to see a quest list where I can easily see what has been completed. for some reason, looking at the maps for crossed out skulls just doesn’t do it for me. D2 had a pretty easy quest list you could look at to see what has been completed or not completed.

The last change is the past and present. It feels like a pain to have to figure out how to go from the past to the present and back again is not intuitive. Enabling the Ziggurat waypoint would help, but taking a waypoint and then the time portal to get back to your stash is also a pain.

Love the game and the number of positive changes already made since it was opened up, but just giving a few thoughts that would improve it for me. What are everyone’s thoughts?
