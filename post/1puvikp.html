A New NBA role-type: The off the bench Superstar

Thought of this after seeing the way teams have handled Zion, Embiid and Wemby recently

We all know there’s a class of superstars who struggle with heavy NBA workloads - Embiid and Zion are two of the classic cases from the past 10 years - and lately we’ve seen the how the pace and intensity of the game becoming increasingly difficult for top players. In the past, the top player who can’t handle the workload were basically expected to play a normal workload, with teams hoping they could sustain, and if they couldn’t, they’d break down, then are no longer considered top superstars in the league. But watching the Pelicans have success with Zion coming off the bench has me wondering if teams will find a compromise by trying a new role for injury-prone superstar level players.

Teams can acknowledge that some players can’t handle 30+ minutes for 82 games a year, and start bringing these guys off the bench for limited, but high intensity minutes. Clearly its going well with Zion these past couple games. The logic is also sound - 20-25 minutes of Z is better than none at all. Personally I think the 76ers should do this with Embiid, let him cook for 20 minutes, or save him until crunch time. It also allows more time for Maxey and Embiid to run possessions through them. Though I wouldn’t put Wemby in the same class as those two injury-wise, his impact in the Cup games coming off the bench showed their a clear blueprint for success.

Soccer often has some of their best players stay on the bench until after halftime, and then unleashes them so they can stay fresh for the entirety of their playing. I think we could start to see a similar strategy in the NBA, if the players can find a way to keep their rhythm and adapt to the unusual role.

Curious what others think – any other players you think could/should fit this mold? Had this in mind for true top flight talent - like MVP or All NBA caliber players who struggle with health

Edit: Meant this as a true blue Superstar who comes off the bench, not someone who is a super star at coming off the bench
