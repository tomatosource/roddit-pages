To Whom It May Concern: My Breakup Letter to the Dallas Mavericks

It’s done. At 11:23am on March 6, 2025, I canceled my season tickets and effectively broke up with the Dallas Mavericks.

I know my protest is futile. I know my seats will easily be resold to someone else. I know that my money, my passion, and my history of loving this team means absolutely nothing to Patrick Dumont, Miriam Adleson, or Nico Harrison.

I know this is going to hurt me more than it hurts them – but I did it.

My heart bled for this team a minimum of 82 nights a year for the better part of the last 30 seasons. I worked my ass off for every single ticket. I made sacrifices to be in the building as often as life allowed. I was truly ALL-IN. I cherished every single moment. It was magical. And I never took a single moment for granted.

But this isn’t the organization that I grew up learning to love. This isn’t the organization that loved me back.

My heart is broken. But this isn’t Mark Cuban’s Dallas Mavericks anymore.

And for that reason, I’m out.

Here is the text from the email:

Hi Spencer,

First off, I would like to thank you for being the best season ticket rep I have ever had. In addition to our time with the Mavs, I am 20+ year STH with the Dallas Cowboys and a STH for the Dallas Wings. Your service has been impeccable and nothing that I am about to say has anything to do with you. None of the people in your role deserve what has happened or the position that you’ve been put in.

Regarding next season and beyond: I do not consent to a renewal for the 2025-26 season. I want to finish out the last 5 games of our package this season and any potential playoff games and then, regretfully, we will have to be done. Let me know what needs to be done to make this happen.If any of my payments have been applied to next season, I am entitled to a refund.If I need to make a partial payment to cover the next 5 games, I am happy to do so.

In the unlikely event that we host playoff games, I will opt in for those as well. I will not abandon the players that are also suffering through this horrible moment in professional sports history.

Here is why I am canceling:

In one month, the Adlesons have used their pawn, Nico Harrison, to unravel my lifetime of Dallas Mavericks fandom and destroyed this once illustrious and enviable franchise from the inside out.

I will not pretend to know their motive although, at times, it seems like cruelty and shows of power has been the only point.

Patrick Dumont is a man that knows nothing about basketball. The way he has continued to disparage and slander Luka’s name after the trade has been disgusting, disgraceful, and an insult to basketball fans worldwide; not to mention the entire city of Dallas.

The Dallas Mavericks are nothing more than Dumont’s toy; a plaything gifted to him by his billionaire mother in law in an effort to keep him busy and away from the family’s real money plays.

Dumont and Harrison have teamed up to annihilate what was once one of the most respected and modeled after professional sports franchises on the planet.

I am heartbroken to send this email. I worked hard for these seats and it cuts deep to have to let them go. But I can no longer financially support a franchise that doesn’t support its players and doesn’t care about its fan base.

Thank you again for your assistance this season. I wish you the best and hope we all get to see Luka lift a trophy in Los Angeles this summer.

With regret,

Former Dallas Mavericks Season Ticket Holder
