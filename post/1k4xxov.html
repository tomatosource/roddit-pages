Campaign feedback

Hello, I’m an old d2 fart and I’ll be using some old references so excuse me for that.

I haven’t seen this mentioned anywhere so here are my 2 cents on the campaign and how it would be better for me personally. For me a poe1 ssf leaguestart is 10hrs and the equivalent takes 25hrs. Now I don’t mind the campaign taking a little while longer to progress through and that is not the point of the post. There is a critical lack of “farming zones” I recall hearing somewhere how in poe1 the a3 docks before piety were designed for players to farm in preparation for the upcoming bossfight. It doesn’t feel like there is quite anything like that in poe2 currently (I know you have some things like that like the graves that drop you a random blue ring in a1 but why on earth only on the first try, resetting a zone for a second 30 mana 4 res ring wouldnt be that broken would it?)

I’m quite an experienced player and that’s my bias aswell as maybe my downfall as I dont really like playing the one capable build on the weapon I’m using, I end up trying most if not all skills as they unlock as I play through the campaign and I dont use trade to upgrade my gear until I’m at maps.

A3 is my favorite act! (was on release aswell) Bosses are great and tough and I don’t mind farming a bit extra on some of the bigger zones. Maybe there is a bias of me getting my build going with the higher tier support gems/better gear/ascendancy but a3 and after I quite like the campaign. Acts 1 and 2 is where I’m lost and I think my biggest problem is not having a good sidezone to farm before hard encounters. For me personally the 3 main points of friction are the:

-A1 graveyard boss (if you dont have a cookiecutter tool to deal with the proj reflect shield guys on some builds makes clearing the catacombs a right pain)

-A1 actboss (Candlemass runs could in theory be nice but I tilt after a couple full blue drops in a row)

-A2 actboss (last zone is a pain to farm on most builds, even with a well clearing ability the maggot lair area template makes it pretty much impossible to get a full pack or 2 affected by your attacks)

tldr. I need a buff for zones before hard encounters early campaign or dedicated farming zones please
