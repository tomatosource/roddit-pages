Mychal and Klay Thompson, between the two of them, have a combined 25 seasons played in the NBA. The 2026-27 season will be the first time either of them has played in the Eastern Conference.

Mychal:
Portland - 7 seasons (8 counting injured years)
Lakers - 4.5 seasons
Spurs - .5 season

Klay:
Warriors - 11 seasons (13 counting injured years)
Mavs - 2 seasons

This fun fact dawned on me yesterday. Pretty crazy they’ve never left the Western Conference.
