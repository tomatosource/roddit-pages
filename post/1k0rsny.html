My farewall to 0.2 with hopes of 0.3 going better and my take on the endgame of 0.2.

I find the current end-game to be useless and boring. Currently a lvl 96 amazon lightning spear - current gear is around 25-30 divine worth - farmed around a total of 70 div in currency and i lost my will to play further. I no longer find fun in farming and even sometimes, i had a habit of opening POE2 as soon as i start my PC but then Alt+F4 after opening POE2 as i lost all my motivation to play.

Chase uniques are gutted/nerfed that they are not worth farming for. Most of the new added uniques are build defining and not a power spike dopamine inducing chase item that the are no longer worth to farm for / chase after. Endgame is stale and really not fun. Farming maps/bosses for 2-4 hour straight, you often ask yourself “is this fun, what am i farming for ?”

Combo style gameplay is fine but boring as there is no uniqueness in it, i feel like it’s just a bunch of combos pre-defined by developers. There is no hype or that feeling of playing your own build, the feeling finding a bunch of skills/aura/items you can combo to take your builds to new heights is gone with pre-defined combos. Most of the gameplay now feels like dev-suggested or tutorial-suggested builds and combos.
