[Holmes] Dennis Robertson gave a series of verbal requests that the Clippers would need to agree to before Kawhi would consider signing there

These included:

Forcing the Clippers to acquire Paul George (Even though it meant parting with then rookie SGA who the Clippers were quite high on)

The ability to live in San Diego and commute to LA via private Helicopter

To skip media obligations and community service

Market Kawhi individually

Bring some of his own people into the organization

Establish a strict protocol on how to communicate with Kawhi and that it would only be done on his terms

The Clippers also fired then broadcaster and former Spurs champion Bruce Bowen who made inflammatory remarks against Kawhi during his scandal with the Spurs in 2018 due to fear that he would refuse to sign there otherwise

Its also said that Dennis asked the Clippers for a part ownership of the franchise, private jet, full paid house, and guaranteed off court money. However, since these were against the CBA, the Clippers declined. He asked the Raptors and Lakers for this and possibly the Spurs too who also declined
