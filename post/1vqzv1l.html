[Raptors] The Toronto Raptors sign guard Malachi Smith

Source: https://www.nba.com/raptors/news/raptors-sign-smith

” The Toronto Raptors announced Monday they have signed guard Malachi Smith. Per team policy, financial terms of the deal were not disclosed.

Smith, 6-foot-4, 205 pounds, averaged 8.3 points, 3.4 rebounds, 3.3 assists and 23.9 minutes in 15 games (four starts) with Brooklyn last season. He shot .485 (48-99) from the field, including .435 (20-46) from three-point range, and scored in double figures six times with one double-double. Smith recorded a season-high 19 points, 10 assists and eight rebounds Apr. 10 at Milwaukee. He also appeared in 39 games (18 starts) with Long Island in the NBA G League, averaging 14.4 points, 5.7 assists, 4.6 rebounds, 1.4 steals and 28.7 minutes. “
