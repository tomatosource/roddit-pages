Do you think the Wizards end up last in offensive and defensive rating?

The Wizards are currently 28th on offense and 30th on defense. Raptors/Jazz/Wizards have been at the bottom on defense and Wizards are 30th now after the Spurs game, giving up nearly 140 in regulation.

Wizards are starting two rookies in Bub Carrington and Alex Sarr, another rookie in Kyshawn George is a full time rotation member, and a sophomore in Bilal Coulibaly is one of their featured players. Net rating absolutely hates Bub, one of the worst players in the league so far in terms of net impact, even though I personally think if you watch the games, he’s shown some very nice flashes. Sarr is bottom of the league level offensively, sub-5th percentile but his defense is already pretty solid and should only continue to improve. He has his issues at times on switches, but he’s got very good instincts in the paint. Kyshawn’s defense also grades out pretty well for a rookie, he’s versatile on that end with his size/length and slides his feet well.

Bilal is the Wizards’ best two way player IMO and only going to continue to improve.

Kispert was in a big shooting slump to begin the season and is starting to break out but can’t guard a chair. Kuzma has been way off since preseason, just returned from a groin injury having missed five games. Shooting 36% with awful individual defensive numbers and more TO’s than AST’s. JV is who he is, he’s giving the Wiz a strong inside presence and rebounder and someone you can throw the ball too and depend on to score in the half court. He and Sarr didn’t work as a duo, lack of speed and spacing, and JV only playing 20 minutes a game right now off the bench; his defensive numbers from various metrics range from decent to absolute bottom of the league.

Poole isn’t a team elevator but his individual numbers are pretty good. He’s not the most efficient but has had big games and is shooting well from 3. His individual defensive numbers are better than Kuzma but not great. The one thing the Wiz like to do is try to press defensively and speed teams up and force turnovers so they can get out in transition. They’re overmatched in the half court and look to use their youth to their advantage to create pressure defensively. Poole is averaging over 2 steals a game.

Brogdon is due to return soon from his wrist injury and we’ll see him take a pretty big share, most likely, early on, of the Wiz lead ball handling role. That will help and wouldn’t be shocked to see at least Brogdon remain this season on the Wiz as veteran presence, but of course just as likely wouldn’t be surprised if he’s moved.

The Bobcats that won 7 games were 30th on both ends. That’s the team that has the worst net rating in recent memory as well at over -15. The Wizards are over -12 net rating currently. Their net rating is worse by two points compared to those Process Sixer teams. They currently have a bottom 5 net rating in NBA history and bottom 5 defense in NBA history. It’s all early, so not reading too much into those numbers, but Valanciunas is very likely to be moved and the Wizards offense has a good likelihood of being worse, maybe significantly worse, on offense, in the second half of the season without JV.

The Wiz are gonna make a run at finishing 30th on both ends this season. You think they’re up to doing it?
