My incredible SSF-Story

This is my story, wich i really wanted to share as i dont have any friends in this game!

So, yesterday i decided to start a fresh character in SSF. I still had around 400k gold, a couple exalts, 2 divs from my first char, but not much else.

I wanted to try the tangletongue, as it seemed fun and my other char was boring. I knew it would be a gambleeto get the tangletongue so i prepared to play a build with spears if i dont get it.

Once i was Level 30, i started to gamble with alva, and i had 1 white Base and 1 Chance orb.

The chance orb failed me greatly, and i had 41 rolls left with alva.

I spent all the rolls down to 6 left, no tangletongue in sight. I never get lucky with those so i was already sad. And then, baaaaamm!!! Tangletongue in SSF!! I was so damn Happy!

The crit rate you ask? 11.3% :S

Well, i remembered to Qual and socket it. Then i knew the crit Chance could go up with a vaal. And then IT Hit me. I had to 2 divs, maybe they could reroll my crit Chance. I wasnt Sure so i Just tried, AS divs dont have alot of use in SSF Till very late Game.

First div, and yes! Crit reroll, to 14.5%!! Nice. Lets try the second div! baaaaamm! 18.75%!!!!

Holy, this is now usable :) i was so Happy.

The vaal didnt Change much.

I have my tangletongue!! Thanks for Reading all! :)
