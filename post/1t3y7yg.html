Stay the course

I understand that everyone is pissed off and maybe even a little embarrassed that they blew a 3–1 lead to Philly. It’s a failure and an epic collapse, no doubt. However, let’s not overreact.

This whole season was about a “gap” year while Tatum gets healthy, where they reset the books and got under the tax/aprons to push the chips back in the middle in 2026-27. You can’t lose sight of that because the team outperformed expectations and Tatum came back. The roster was closer to the middler most expected, not a major surprise.

If it were me, I’d use the resources they have (trade exception, MLE) to get a legit starting center and another scoring guard. Go into next season with something like this:

Bigs: Vet starter, Queta, Garza
Wings: Tatum, Brown, Hugo, Baylor
Guards: White, Pritchard, vet addition

You can fill the roster out with Harper, Walsh, etc. My guess is Hauser is gone to make room for the new additions. Just don’t panic!
