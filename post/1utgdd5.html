Comet (Cast on variants) need to die

Every season, since 0.1, I will play a certain character, beat all the content with it and when at the end of the day ask people which secondary build I should go for in terms of being the best / strongest, it always ends up being some sort of Comet Build.

Having played many variants of Comet builds, this ability, the way it functions and how it outclasses almost everything else in pure damage, range and clear speed is just hilarious and a bit bland at this point.

IMO Comet scaling needs to be turned way down and we should encourage more unique builds hitting very hard to reach points to reach this level of dominance.
