POE2 Rubberband lag fix ps5

I hope everyone is doing good. After finally buying poe2 I was having soo much fun solo leveling. I made it to maps where things started getting funky. My screen would freeze, I would appear somewhere else dead. This got even worse when I was in a group. Completely unplayable. Looking at the stats in options it appeared my latency went from 30 to 1000 ms. After countless threads, forums and suggestions I found the solution that fixed it permanently.

Ps5 by default is auto 5.0ghz and 2.5ghz. For some reason it was bouncing back and fourth between the two. Possibly connection instability? Maybe shitty router? I changed it to stay at 5ghz and the ps5 kept dropping wifi Same thing with 2.5ghz. My network provider does not allow me to access my router settings so my only option was to hook up LAN cable. So I did and have not had an issue since. Hopefully this points everyone having an issue into the right direction.
