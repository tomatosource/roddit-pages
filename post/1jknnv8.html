Controller Support is barely acceptable in PoE2 and lacks feedback about it

Hi everyone, i’ve a bit of a rant to share about controller in PoE2, I understand most of you must be playing with K&M so i get it if you don’t have interest in the subject so bear with me here!

It’s already bugged and limited in PoE1 but at the very least the mapping makes sense, but PoE2 mapping do not match with PoE1’s which makes hopping between games an extremely frustrating experience; yet they explained how making us hopping between games is one of their main objectives.

In addition, some bindings conflit with each others: the best exemple is when you trade for a socketed gear, confirm trade and select sockets are the same bind (long press on A/cross) : why would they not make the “confirm trade” binding consistent between games, and why are they making it overlap with another important command ?

Controller support comes out looking like another rushed feature which will probably see minimal improvement over the year because I sincerly doubt they consider it a priority, I think it’s not acceptable especially because it’s a crucial feature when it comes to accessibility.

I’ve seen very little feedback about it, i’m sure i missed some but I believe it’s an overlooked issue and I’d really like devs to adress it at some point
