Feature/Bug to Always Show Tier Levels with a Controller

I’ve been using a controller and always liked to see the tier level of attributes on an item. On a controller you have to hold down the left thumbstick.

The implementation makes it that if you release the thumbstick the tier levels (attributes) go away, which is bothersome when you are browsing a bunch of items. Even worse when you are doing it from your stash, it puts the focus on the stash search box.

The real solution, as many have stated, would be a toggle to always display these tier levels.

Until then, I noticed that if you grab an orb and long press the left thumbstick on an item, the tier levels stay on even when you stop pressing the thumbstick. It even shows them when you move from item to item.

Bug? Feature? Thought I’d share so other controller players could benefit from it.
