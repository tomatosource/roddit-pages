Mapping currency yields in 0.3 are absolutely abysmal

FYI, this is from my experience as a solo mapper on a very fast-clear max MS / ~140% player rarity, multi-mirror DE setup running juiced maps until I hit L99 this week. Mapping is not my main source of currency generation and this run was more of a data test of sorts.

Utilizing absolutely the most optimal juice available (7% quant tablets [with Visions where it makes sense], T16 corrupted waystones with chaotic rarity omen + paranoia instills or a 7-mod T15 corruption with chaotic rarity + paranoia instills), I’ve come to the conclusion that anything that’s short of a cleansed 3-tower area feels like a complete waste of time. And this is with the best juice that many players simply can’t afford (e.g., a chaotic rarity + paranoia instill runs around 40-50ex / map, and this is the gross cost before corruption and potentially ending with T14 or rarity bricks; 7% tablets now run anywhere from 1⁄3-1D), so the average yields for most players are probably even lower. Tiered rares are for the most part worthless with the new crafting changes. Maybe you can find something to craft on but it’ll always usually end up worse than what you can get from slamming fresh bases with perfect augs and transmutes. Breach is also worthless IMO after the Soul Eater node was removed [previously an extra monster mod on Breach rares that scaled quant and rarity] – Breach rares seem to have their quant and rarity completely decoupled from map effects and rarely drop divs or annuls. You’re honestly better off skipping Breach completely and running more maps / hour to focus on map rares and Deli unique boss spawns.

The quantity and rarity scaling in cleansed areas is extremely unbalanced and results in extreme Atlas RNG. Based on my testing, a 2-tower cleansed area yields about as much as a 3–4 tower non-cleansed area—which already isn’t impressive. The worst part, though, is that even if you manage to find 3 towers in a corrupted area to cleanse, the chances that all—or even most—benefit from the full 3-tower overlap are very slim.

I know mapping overhauls are coming in 0.4 but I’m really surprised GGG didn’t address this with any sort of a temporary band-aid fix in 0.3 [increased frequency of towers or corrupted areas, increased non-corrupted scaling, etc.].
