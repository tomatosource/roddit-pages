The less content I consume, the more fun I have.

So about a week ago, I made it a point to not watch as much poe2 content on YouTube as I normally do. I’m referring more to class guides, build guides, or different showcases on what classes can do, etc. Videos about game changes or patch notes I’ll still watch just to keep up on the state of the game. I just felt like I was falling into a trap of relying on these guides too much, instead of just naturally discovering things that I liked about the game, or the character I was playing. Don’t get me wrong, guides are perfectly fine for what they are, just don’t make the same mistake I did by blindly following them without understanding why the guide made the decisions it did. Apologies if I’m beating a dead horse here, but I just thought this sentiment would be useful to others.
