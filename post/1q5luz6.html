Why play melee?

Genuinely what is the advantage you get for playing what is ostensibly the more risky archetype? I have a 94 Oracle Wolf this league. I have done okay, but man is everything just twice as hard for no benefit.

I do less damage than my BMage did last league with the same investment. I am also less tanky. I take longer to do all content since I have to be far more careful of getting off screened and avoiding degens. Pinnacles are all way more difficult since I actually have to engage with all the mechanics instead of ignoring half of them and spamming spark while I can’t even see the boss. Legit why even have a melee class if they don’t do anything better than ranged?
