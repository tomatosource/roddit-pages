LeBron is close to breaking one more record: the all-time field goals made record

A few years ago LeBron became the all-time leading scorer in NBA history, but he still was behind one final record: field goals made. He is finally closing in on passing Kareem for getting the most buckets in NBA history

  1. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar: 15,837

  2. LeBron James: 15,819

  3. Karl Malone: 13,528

LeBron is only 19 baskets away from setting this record. The last five games he’s average 7.2 field goals. This would mean we can expect LeBron to break Kareem’s record on Thursday March 5th in Denver.
