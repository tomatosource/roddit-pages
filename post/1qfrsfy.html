Drives seem to have little impact on Free Throw Attempts and Free Throw Differential

There have been quite a few complaints about inconsistent and/or biased officiating from coaches and players this season. One of the responses is that teams who drive more get more fouls than teams who don’t.

That makes sense, but doesn’t seem to match the stats. Some teams don’t drive a lot but get a ton of free throw attempts. Some teams drive a lot and get comparatively very few free throw attempts.

For example, the Clippers drive more than the Lakers but 3.4 less FTA per game and their Free Throw Differential is 3 less.

https://preview.redd.it/y28zhdr4qzdg1.png?width=1688&format=png&auto=webp&s=73b70aec56e6ab5d98d3066c16d08ee6d0873b84

Caveats:

  • It isn’t clear to me if a drive is counted if a foul is called or if it “goes away” like with FGA
  • A better comparison would be fouls received while driving, but I don’t see that data available
    • Or maybe even better: fouls, FTA, and FTA differential by shot distance
  • Some stats (like 3PA) do have a stronger correlation, but without knowing how many 3PA were removed because of fouls that became FTA, it’s hard to make that comparison.
