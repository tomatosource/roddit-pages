Wyvern not what I expected. Rend spam.

nearing the end of the campaign, and Wyvern is not what I expected. Thought it was going to be a fire blasting ranged caster so I could oil up enemies and burn them, but Rend is just…so much better but also more boring. Rend, devour, rend, rend, rend, devour. Its just far better.

Maybe its the lack of skills. We have Rend, the very strong screen clearing melee swipe, Rolling magma, the very underwhelming and practically useless basic spell, Wing blast is okay at stunning? Devour, which is just a requirement setup of Rend, with not a lot of use otherwise. Then we have a huge gap until oil barrage, which I do like, its cool and fun. Then flame breath, which is cool, but feels rather slow and I feel vulnerable during use.

So to recap.

1 Spamming melee skill (which seems to be the best skill for the wyvern?)

1 Usless skill in rolling magma

2 very situational setup skills that you dont need with proper builds in devour and wing blast.

2 actually fun skills in oil barrage and flame breath

I feel like there are a lot of missing skills for this (and the wolf tbh). No fireball esc skill with an explosion similar to explosive shot. An on the ground fire breath would be good similar to the channel fire skill. Devour corpses to empower spells? Seems we got the outline of 3 classes rather than anything fully fleshed out.
