Slowly becoming one of my all time favorite games

I started midway through last season, playing FGKorbyns Hollow Palm build. Made it to end game with a lot of help from friends.

This season I am doing it completely solo and let me tell you the experience is unlike any other I’ve had in gaming. Specifically the level of theorycrafting itches a hard to reach part of my brain that has always yearned to be itched. One tink can change your whole build, and going the route of crafting through trial of error as opposed to saving currency and buying what I need off the market, really has me hooked this season.

And it’s still being actively developed, it’s still in early access. How insane is that?! Sorry for the ramblings I just figured here is the best place to share a common awe of something as “simple” as a video game 💚
