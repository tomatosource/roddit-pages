Most recent MLB game with no existing video footage

This is question which came to mind during the season, but I saved asking here until the offseason:

How far back in time would we have to go to come across a regular-season MLB game for which video footage no longer exists? Not just games not broadcast on TV but games for which no local TV affiliates saved any footage they might have shot for the local news.

I think this question was spurred by seeing the clip which pops up here from time to time of Lenny Randle of the Rangers laying down a bunt with the sole purpose of blowing up the Cleveland pitcher on his way down to first. It was in 1974, and the footage shared is from the archives of the DFW ABC affiliate. Are all Rangers home games in that archive, or was that particular clip from that particular game preserved while footage from less noteworthy games was discarded?

My instinct is that, for each game shown on TV over the last 30+ years (so practically every MLB game), there’s preserved footage lying around somewhere. If so, I think the answer might be a game in the 1980s.
