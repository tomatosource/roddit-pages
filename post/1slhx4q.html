Suggestion: Omen crafting bench

Since PoE2 does not use the crafting bench, which is a shame, I suggest bringing it back. Crafting is way too cluttered already with all those Omens etc.

The way I envision it is that the bench has access to all the Omens and currency in your stash, so that an Omen kinda functions like a one time craft at the bench. Omens can be activated within the crafting bench and don’t need to dragged into the inventory. The crafting bench can automate currency uses like “Repeat X times Orb of Augementation -&gt; Orb of Annulment Until T1 Mana”. Maybe it also has some kind of customizable, modular interface, where you only show the crafting materials you need for your current craft.

What are your thoughts on that?
