I wish they would move the tablet customization options to the Atlas and let scarabs substitute tablets

To be honest, I like the options that tablets add to maps across different league mechanics, but tablet management, price-checking, and everything else creates so many inconveniences (I’m not going to explain in details we all know how they work).

At the same time, I really struggle to name even one or two nodes that stick out to me on the Atlas tree for each league mechanic. I can remember the Amanamu node in Abyss, a Temple node that lets you choose Atziri drops, the catalyst picker in Breach, and a couple of remnant nodes in Expedition. But for instance I have no idea what the nodes in Delirium or Ritual even are, and I’ve been farming all of these mechanics.

This is my general critique of all the Atlas reworks: the nodes feel relatively inconsequential. You just passively take them. Sure, they improve your farm, but the changes feel marginal and most of them don’t stand out. The same goes for the main Atlas, I think I’m even more of a detractor of that one.

It seems logical to me that league Atlases should dictate *how* we play, featuring more multiple choice nodes that reflect the mechanics we currently find on tablets. Doing this would also allow devs to gate the most OP strategies behind increased difficulty rather than gating the OP tablets by market value. As an example, let’s look at the +3 reroll in Ritual. Obviously that’s OP, so why not put it on a node that makes Ritual significantly harder? Right now, we can manipulate very little of the juicing in PoE2, and the most difficult content in the end is just a league mechanic + 200% Deli, Expedition with 6 extra map mods, or Azmeri shenanigans. I wish we could have 2–3 mechanics through the Atlas that scale alongside character power, leaving scarabs to complement these strats with basics modifiers like pack size, effectiveness, and rarity. I don’t think I need to explain the perks of scarabs, especially compared to the headache of tablet management.

What would be the drawback of doing this? Keep in mind I never played PoE1, but just a quick look at how scarabs work makes me think they would be a much better system.
