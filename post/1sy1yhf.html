Ideas for inner turmoil

Inner turmoil is an interesting ascendancy option for chayula monk, allowing you to stack volatility by applying elemental ailments to enemies and avoiding all damage from the volatility detonation.

What’s fun about this is the added dimension to gameplay. Playstyle adapts to stack as much volatility as you can by applying elemental ailments with your attacks, and it’s satisfying when you can deal damage while also stacking volatility.

What doesn’t work at the moment?

  1. Big investment for small payoffs. Unless you’re cheesing the mechanic, it can take a long time to build up a lot of volatility, and it’s easy for that investment to go to waste if it pops in between packs or phases.

  2. The payoff could be more interesting. More chaos damage is nice, but you also get more chaos damage from into the breach and/or mantra of darkness, usually for less effort.

  3. It feels like a builder spender in practice. In other words, build volatility to explode it and get the extra damage for 10s. This feels kind of like a combat system from WoW or other MMOs, which can get a little boring after a bit of time goes by.

  4. It’s a little too hard to stack volatility in single target encounters. There are nice options for this (eg a support gem that gives you volatility on crit), but it still feels like inner turmoil alone should be more useful in single target encounters.

What would make it more interesting?

  1. Modify inner turmoil to cause each stack of volatility to provide increased darkness and/or increased global defenses. You could also make the damage buff from exploding volatility cause reduced darkness and/or global defenses. This would make for a fun blend of gameplay - you can rely on extra defense when building volatility and then be a glass cannon when it pops.

  2. Modify the amount of volatility granted for causing elemental ailments to be based on the power of the monster (similar to other skills). Getting 1 volatility for freezing a boss feels so underwhelming. I’d guess that gaining 30 volatility for applying an ailment to a boss, 10 for a rare, and 1 otherwise would work well.

  3. Delay the start of the damage buff until your next attack (similar to staggering palm). Now you don’t have to be mad when the stack explodes in between monster packs.

That’s all. I hope this can potentially help a chayula rework

in 0.5 or at least start a productive discussion.
