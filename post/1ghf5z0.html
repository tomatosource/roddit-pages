[Goldsberry] “The Efficiency Landscape. What Jumps Out?”

https://x.com/kirkgoldsberry/status/1852346509259362388

We’re only about 10 days into the season so it’s very early, but there are some surprising highlights:

Warriors #1 Net rating - With big roster changes, uncertainty with Kuminga, and Steph getting injured in the 3rd game, there’s been a big question mark on the team but they’ve been off to a good start.

Thunder #23 Offensive rating #1 Defensive rating - They were expected to have a strong start, and have had that, being undefeated so far, but surprisingly they have dropped significantly in offensive rating, from 3rd last season to 23rd.

Kings #5 Net rating - The Kings were another question mark as they added a great offensive player with DeRozan, but it was unclear if another sub-par shooter with spotty defense would translate well but the Kings have looked decent with 2 close losses to strong teams and 2 easy wins versus weak ones.

Knicks #25 Defensive rating #3 Offensive rating - Knicks saw big roster changes from their starting lineup, the team has struggled defensively without MitchRob but have had a steller offense despite the panic from their brutal first game of the season.

Cavs #1 Offensive rating #Net rating - With mostly the same roster, there are lukewarm expectations, and the Cavs have had a somewhat easy schedule, but have faced 5 teams in 8 days, 3 of which being weaker teams, but still saw a win vs the Knicks and a blowout vs the Lakers. They have had noticeably better coaching, their bigs have been gelling well, and with the absence of Strus, they have faired well with Dean Wade in the starting spot. There have also been minor adjustments with Merril, and Ty Jerome getting more time, and LeVert, Niang, Craig Porter Jr., and Okoro getting less.
