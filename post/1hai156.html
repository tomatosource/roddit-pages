Box Office Staff saved the day after being sold fake tickets

I purchased 4 tickets to the 12⁄7 game vs. the Grizzlies for myself, my stepdad, nephew, and brother in law. This was a thank you gift for all 3 of them being an integral part of my wedding last month. I utilized Capital One Entertainment to get these seats, as I redeemed some credit card points towards the tickets. Capital One Entertainment uses Vivid Seats as their ticket broker- and I had assumed this was no concern to use as I did the same thing for game 1 of the NBA Finals this year and had no trouble at all using my tickets once purchased from COE.

My party and I traveled from southern Rhode Island and arrived ~50 mins before tipoff and when going through the point of entry, we were denied access and told to see the Box Office. From there is when I met Alex at the box office, and he explained to me that the season ticket holder was hacked and someone was fraudulently selling their seats on resale sites. I showed him all correspondence/confirmation I had from Capital One, and forwarded him the ticket link they gave me (Securemypass.com) is the link they used, which Alex told me is not a valid form of ticket, as it must be Ticketmaster (good to know for the future!). Alex then let us in on his own discretion. Not only did he allow us in, which was enormously generous and kind- he has helped me in my complaint I filed with COE directly, as he helped provide the proof I need to let COE know they indeed sold me fraudulent tickets.

Alex was able to turn what could have been a very stressful and disappointing evening into a night of family fun. I am grateful for this kindness, as this was my nephew’s first ever professional game of any sort. Needless to say, he is hooked on the Celtics now and can’t wait for me to take him again.

Kudos to Alex and the entire Celtics staff, it’s great to see how amazing of a staff the Celtics have beyond the product on the court.
