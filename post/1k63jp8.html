Level 89 and still 0 Divine Orbs…

Level 89, and I still haven’t seen a single Divine Orb this league. No Perfect Jeweller’s Orbs either, and just one Greater Jeweller’s Orb. Not sure if I’m unlucky or just invisible to loot drops.

As soon as I finished Act 3 and unlocked currency exchange, I started reforging and flipping. I got my first divine orb at about level 55.

But I keep thinking: What if I didn’t know how to trade or craft? I would’ve walked into maps with 5 Exalts to my name, hit a wall, and quit the game somewhere along the way—because honestly, without that extra income, it feels unplayable.
