Hot take: Maybe we should give Jayson Tatum and this team time to acclimate before saying the season is over

I was at the game last night, and for sure that 4th quarter was gross. There was way too much ISO-ball in the 4th quarter, they were missing a lot of open shots.

But guys, I don’t know what to tell some of you, but this team had zero shot without him. None. Zero. Their shot at the Finals was him coming back, and when he did, we expected there to be growing pains. There have been. They’re still 6-1 when Tatum and Brown play a full game.

I think the team was gassed last night, and that’s on them, because that Memphis game shouldn’t have been so close. If you want to sit there and call that game concerning, by all means, go ahead. We’ve seen this team struggle a ton against physical switching defenses in 2023 vs the Heat and 2025 vs the Knicks. I didn’t hate some of the shots they got, but the DWhite threes were awful looks.

I’m going to give this team time though. They’ve got a few days off now, hopefully that gives them fresh legs. Not every team is as good on defense as the Ant-less Wolves, tbh.

And give Tatum a break man. He just came back from a TORN ACHILLES!!! We knew he wasn’t going to be gangbusters right away. And yet every game at halftime, I’m seeing fans say he’s cooked. Come on man.
