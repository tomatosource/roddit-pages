Trading is almost mandatory

Without a doubt, the best way to gear up in this game is via trade. Even online build guides just tell you to buy gear.

This approach has unfortunately removed all the in-game loot excitement. I never actually expect to find anything worthwhile in game.

It has also diminished crafting in the game. It is completely foolish to spend a divine orb gambling in game, when you can take that divine to trade and get a guaranteed success.

I just want to find loot. I don’t want to spend my game time scrolling websites. And yes, I could just play SSF, but this game can be totally punishing depending on your drops (or lack thereof).
