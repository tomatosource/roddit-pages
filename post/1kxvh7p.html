What’s the purpose of EXP penalty for dying in the end game?

I’m struggling to keep my enjoyment of the game up when an off-screen 1-shot can undo a whole map’s worth of progress. Especially when, up until the moment I die, I’ve been cruising through the map without issue.

I’m sure it’s a matter of “git gud,” but my skills aside, what does this accomplish in the game’s design? It slows down leveling, but relative monster level of map tiers should be able to do that. It punishes you for leaning more into DPS over defenses, but just having to respawn at the last checkpoint is already punishing, as is the loss of waystone/modifiers if you run out of portals.

It feels like it’s designed to make you feel “stuck” until you change your build. But it seems unlikely to me that this is the intent. What am I missing?
