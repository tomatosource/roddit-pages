How I farmed over 100div without any triple tower setups (I’m lazy)

I’ll try to keep this as short as possible (again, cause I’m lazy) - but this is a ‘guide’ on how I made over 100div (prob closer to 150d) without spending time on setting up some crazy 3-4 tower setups with quantity tablets bla bla bla.

For reference here is a link to my character on poeninja. I invested almost all of the currency I made into it - it’s nowhere near to be min-maxed but its good enough to farm everything very comfortably while walking on a treadmill.

So the main way I farmed my divs was crafting. Yes, I know, its gambling not crafting, but still with the right choices and strategies you can make some easy buck without investing into any whittling omens etc.

The second source of income were fragments. With random tower setups with breach+ritual+delirium i managed to farm a LOT of breachstones (30ex), simulacrums (90ex) and audiences (40ex). To be frank I think the most money here came from breachstones - with some random tablets (I usually TRY to target quant/rarity but i’ve never 3to1 any tablets or stuff like that) I was getting somewhere between 50-100 breach splinters a map, so in best case scenario it was 3 maps for 30ex.

Back to crafting though. I crafted 2 types of items:

  1. Sceptres - this was my main source of income (still is but probably won’t be anymore xdd)

Pick 2 white base types - Omen Sceptre and Stoic Sceptre. These are the base types with the best auras for LS characters. Use attribute essence. If you hit &gt;20 str/int, use augment orb hoping for %spirit or hybrid %spirit/mana. Even if you don’t get them, use greater attribute essence hoping for another &gt;20 roll on the second attribute. Then spam 3x exalted orb (I used a lot of double ex omens to lower the cost).

For example: 25 strength + 25 intelligence + 70% spirit sceptre currently sells for around 10d. I think I made like 7-10 of them, one of them I used for my character.

If you get around 30-40% spirit with good attributes thats somewhere between 30-50ex.

For other ‘bricks’ I usually listed them for 10ex, if they didn’t sell I’d recombine them hoping for something good (like double attribute for 20-30% chance) and if that succeeded I just spammed exalts again and sold if it hit spirit.

  1. Evasion/Energy Shield body armours

The process here is similar but a little bit different (probably there is a better way to do it - don’t know cause I don’t really care). Pick up white Sleek Jacket and Falconer’s Jacket bases. Use defence essence hoping for a high flat or % roll for es+evasion. If you get two bases recombine them (I usually got like 20-40% chance to succeed). If you hit the recombine, close your eyes and slam exalted orb 4 times hoping for some hybrid mod on prefix and some useful suffixes (which arent that hard to hit).

I think I made maybe 3-4 decent armours like that, and they sold for somewhere between 4-10div.

Tl;dr:
Everyone plays LS. Everyone uses Fubgun’s or Ben’s POB. Everyone needs a sceptre and body armour and those are easiest to craft with minimal investment of currency and time. Spam essences and recombobulators and hope for the best.
