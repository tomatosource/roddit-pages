[Feedback] Unchangeable Character Quest Rewards Should Go

Have you ever…

  • picked up the 10% lightning res quest reward and then decide you want to try doryani?
  • gotten to level 90+, are min maxing your gear, and realize 5 attr is worth more than 6% res?
  • found your mana regen is doing great now, but you’re getting stunned and shocked more than you’d like?

In all of these cases your choices are to

  1. Skip all the rewards until you know for sure what you want
  2. ROLL A NEW CHARACTER
