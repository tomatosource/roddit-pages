Finished my first “league” of PoE2

Guess the ability icon

So, small context is that I’ve played poe1 since 2013. Loved it, fell out of love with it in recent years, took a big step back.

Due to life circumstances, Aldur is my first proper run of a character since the Beta for PoE2 started and I wanted to immortalize it with my impressions about the current state of the game.

Leveling - I didn’t go completely blind into a character, but I also didn’t read any in-depth guides. Although from what I gather( at least for the Witch) it’s a bit of a situation of Level with Build X, endgame with build Y. That’s what it was for me. Leveling with Spark & Arc, only to switch to my funny haha bird build as early as level 70 (Bought my staff). The acts and interlude are great, really high quality of level design, graphics, voice acting, narrative. The downside is that it’s LONG. As I suffer from the disease of employment, it took me a week or so to get to mapping. It immediately put me off of trying to make any alts.

Gameplay - The WASD movement is refreshing, my right hand is forever grateful for it in extended play sessions. The mechanics are explained in depth and there’s plenty of visual and audio queues for certain “oh shit” things. Obviously, I have taken into account that the game is not fully release so bugs will not be mentioned. Coming from PoE1, experiencing the different version of mechanics I was very used to, like Delulurium, Beach and Ay-boss, I feel like they belong quite well in the form they are in PoE2, not so belonging to PoE1. The slower pace and actual ability to see an attack you can dodge is great. And for some major bosses, it doesn’t seem to be just a dps check, but actual skill (Except arbiter of Ash, who decided in phase 2 to combine his slam with the “stand in the circle” moves 4 times in a row to up my death counter)

Goals - My goal for the league was to play a stupid build in endgame. As soon as I did a whole t4 map, I said to myself “I’m going to do the 8 challenges”. That was my only goal. Unlike in the first game, where I’d hoard most of my currency and just convert the pebbles into shinier rocks, I found myself slamming them on gear, looking for my next upgrade through rares, crafting and gamba. My two biggest purchases were my staff for 1 div and an ammy for 7 div. 8 div spent, rest of the gear was found or self-crafted, and I am fairly positive my best rares were from transmute-augment-regal-ex spam crafting. That didn’t stop me from trying to smack gems with preserved craniums or deleting every utility and heavy belt bases with chance orbs. In the end, I actually quite liked how the challenges were composed, since I wasn’t forced to do EVERYTHING. For example, I barely interacted with breach and expedition, but I’ll give them some love next time.

TL;DR: Game is chill. Not as stressful as the first one. Easier (for now). Go do your challenges
