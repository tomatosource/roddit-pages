As a filthy casual, 0.4 has been the greatest patch so FAR!

This patch from the beginning has had so much controversy, to abyss mapping leading up to exploits for temple building and it was all absolutely divine, but for a casual dad it has created the kindest community in game I have ever experienced. I’m not sure many are aware but top end players are asking for other players to join their temple runs and they pay you! Last night I was told I would be paid 10 divines to join a temple group, which I was skeptical but why not. I spent the most enjoyable hour scouring around like a rodent collecting the so called trash drops in the run, for me it was mountains of treasure, exalts, vaals, chaos orbs, linage gems, uniques all for the taking. After the run finished, they honored the agreement and paid out the 10 divine plus ANOTHER 10 divine just because and they threw in a headhunter! Like what?? That was more then my networth for the past 2 patches combined! (I missed out on 0.1). For myself, the next time I see someone on global chat struggling with something I’m going be kind and help them out, maybe even throw a divine or 2 their way.
