Miguel Rojas is a great example of why the Golden At-Bat is a terrible idea.

I know it might be beaten to death by this point, but Miguel Rojas’ game-tying run in the 9th likely would not have happened if the absurd Golden At-Bat were implemented. You heard it in the broadcast if you were watching on Fox. There was basically zero expectation for Rojas to be productive in the at-bat and the announcers were already looking forward to Ohtani. If there were a golden At-Bat, what better time to swap Ohtani in and fly out to center? The game gets fundamentally changed, and maybe the Blue Jays win, maybe Shohei gets the game tying home run and Rojas just doesn’t get his chance. One of the most beautiful things about baseball is that everyone gets their chance to contribute, and I think the Golden At-Bat ruins that and we could not have had a clearer example of why right here.
