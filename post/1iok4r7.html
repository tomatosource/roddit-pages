Paul George shows up once every 2 weeks

One of the clearest signs that a veteran star just doesn’t have it anymore is poor play with the occasional high scoring game

A quick look at Paul George’s highest scoring games, in chronological order:

29 pts (11⁄12 vs Knicks)

29 pts (12⁄3 vs Hornets)

33 pts (12⁄16 vs Hornets again, followed 4 days later by 5 points vs CHO)

30 pts (1⁄1 vs Kings)

26 pts (1⁄15 vs Knicks)

30 pts (1⁄24 vs Cavs)

Since his outburst versus the Cavs he missed 5 games and hasn’t scored about 14 points in the other 6. He scored 2 points in a loss to the Nets last night. It’s clear that George has fallen well off from the 20+ ppg scorer he was for a over a decade. He’s now averaging 16.1 pts shooting 43/36/82 as the “third option” on a 20-34 team. All while getting paid $49 million on the first year of a 4 year max. People clowned the Clippers for not keeping Paul for at least the short term, but that decision is looking smarter and smarter everyday.
