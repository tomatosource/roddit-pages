The only team in the East with a Top 10 offense AND defense is… The 11th seed Boston Celtics

Source: NBA.com

Quite hilariously, despite being dreadful in the clutch and all the players they let go or are missing, the numbers have the Celtics as pretty good team in a season that’s still kind of impossible to predict. They could get Tatum back and trade for a Center to help Queta and maybe have as good a chance as anyone in the East, or they could have one or 2 injuries and bottom out.

For those curious, East teams in the Top 10 for OffRTG and DefRTG include:

Offense: NYK 3rd, PHI 5th, CLE 7th, BOS 9th, TOR 10th.

Defense: DET 3rd, ATL 5th, BOS 8th, MIA 9th.
