[Tim Rohan] Jayson Tatum says he found out about the Jaylen Brown trade like everyone else and was “unaware” of the move: “I wasn’t warned, I kind of just saw the news like everyone else. I was completely shocked.”

Jayson Tatum declares his love for Jaylen Brown and talks about the trade:

“Let’s be honest, it’s weird. You play on a team with a guy for 9 years. I was lucky enough to go to the Finals with him twice and win the NBA.

We pushed each other to become the players we are today… it just makes me appreciate all the time I had by his side.

But the NBA is weird, right? It’s business, we ended abruptly. For me, this trade doesn’t mean we failed.”

Tatum also adds, revealing that he wasn’t warned beforehand that Brown would be traded:

“I wasn’t warned, I kind of just saw the news like everyone else. I was completely shocked.”
