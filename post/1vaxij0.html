The four teams that have hit the fewest home runs this season are all currently in the playoffs.

The bottom of the Team Home Runs leaderboard is:

30. Milwaukee Brewers: 98 home runs (67-41, 1st place in NL Central)

29. Boston Red Sox: 102 home runs (56-51, 2nd AL Wild Card)

28. Arizona Diamondbacks: 104 home runs (57-52, 2nd NL Wild Card)

27. Cleveland Guardians: 106 home runs (56-54, 3rd AL Wild Card)

At the same time, the teams with the most (Nationals, 159) and third most home runs (Astros, 151) are currently out of the playoffs.

With all of that said, it is important to note that it is currently July and we still have two months of baseball left to play before the playoffs begin.

Edit: Also, Tampa Bay is tied with Toronto for the 5th fewest home runs in baseball, at 107. Tampa currently leads the AL East and has the best record overall in the AL. No word yet on where Toronto is in the standings.
