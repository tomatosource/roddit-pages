The only players in NBA history to be top-5 in Win Shares for 2 franchises: Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, LeBron James, Chris Paul and … Rudy Gobert

Just a random little fun fact.

Rudy Gobert passed Sam Mitchell for 5th on the all-time WS leaderboard for the Minnesota Timberwolves, behind KG, KAT, Kevin Love and Wally Szczerbiak. He is also 3rd on the all-time WS list for the Utah Jazz behind Karl Malone and John Stockton.

The rest of the list:

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, 2nd all-time for the Bucks behind Giannis, 3rd all-time for the Lakers behind Kobe and Magic.

LeBron James, 1st all-time for the Cavs, 2nd all-time for Heat behind Wade.

Chris Paul, 1st all-time for the Pelicans and Clippers.

’* Julius Erving is also here if the ABA counts, which it usually doesn’t for NBA records. He is 2nd all-time for the Sixers, and 3rd all-time for the Nets including ABA.

source : basketball reference https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/
