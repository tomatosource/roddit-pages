Spurs record 7th 60-win season since 2000, most in NBA over that span

With their 115-102 win over the Sixers, the Spurs reached 60 wins for the first time since the 2016-17 season

It is the 7th time since 2000 that San Antonio has won 60+ games in a season, the most in the NBA over that span

No other team has reached 5, and 14 teams have not recorded a single 60-win season since 2000
