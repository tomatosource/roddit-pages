Transactions tied to Kevin Durant have netted a total of eight first-round draft picks since 2023

Kevin Durant to Phoenix Suns (2023 Trade): The Brooklyn Nets received 4 unprotected first-round picks from the Phoenix Suns:

  • 2023 first-round pick (conveyed as #21 to the Nets, used to draft Noah Clowney)
  • 2025 first-round pick
  • 2027 first-round pick
  • 2029 first-round pick

Mikal Bridges to New York Knicks (2024 Trade, asset acquired in the Durant trade): The Brooklyn Nets (who acquired Mikal Bridges from the Suns in the Durant trade) later traded Bridges to the New York Knicks for 4 unprotected first-round picks:

  • 2025 first-round pick
  • 2027 first-round pick
  • 2029 first-round pick
  • 2031 first-round pick

Additionally, two first-round pick swaps were also involved:

  • A 2028 first-round pick swap from the Suns (in the Durant trade).
  • A 2028 first-round pick swap from the Knicks (in the Bridges trade).
