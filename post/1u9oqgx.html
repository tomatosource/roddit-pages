PSA: A new fractured jewel scam has been running rampant

Currently AFAIK there is no way to tell whether a fractured modifier was from either emotion crafting or it was natural rolled at least on base jewel. For that reason people have been buying jewels with one modifier that they want and slam the emotion and sell if their fracture attempt hits the crafted modifier for profit.

These jewels with fractured crafted modifier is a brick as you can not use any of the emotion craftings on it as it would exceed the maximum crafted modifier limit. I have tried some methods but could not really differentiate these from a normal fractured jewel including using “NOT crafted modifier” trade filter, holding ALT (there is no C it shows T1).

One of the scammed jewels look like this when holding ALT, impossible to tell

For now it is best that you fracture your own jewels if you want to make your 5 affixes jewel following Conner method.
