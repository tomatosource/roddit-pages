Exilecon announcement predictions

So… We know that GGG wants to organize exilecons only when they have something significant to announce.

They said so, and we’ve seen it in practice: the first one was to announce PoE2 (and PoE Mobile), and the second was to announce that PoE2 was now a full game playing differently, and not an expansion anymore.

Up until today, the obvious theory for the one this November was for the release date the next month. While the December release is now confirmed, and we even have swords confirmed with footage… There doesn’t seem a lot left to announce, especially not warranting a whole convention.

So, what could be announced there? Is there something huge that is hidden but coming? Or is it simply a change of purpose in the exilecons?
