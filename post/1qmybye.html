5 Draft Prospects for the Celtics

I love the draft and I think about it year round! Earlier in the season when we were most likely picking late lottory I did one of these posts, which I had a lot of fun making. Now that we’ve been killing it and moving to the near top of the eastern conference, our draft options have changed dramatically. So here are 5 guys I’d be hyped to see us draft.

&nbsp;

Tounde Yessoufou - 6’5 G/F 19 years

Stats at Baylor: 17.7ppg, 5.9rpg, 1.7apg, 2.1spg on 49/30/77 shooting

I wrote about Yessoufou when we had a much lower draft pick and not much has changed. Dude is built like a tank and on offense likes to take it to the rim. He thrives playing through contact and getting to the line. He’s not so much a crafty ball handler as he is a transition guy and a cutter. He’s also not a great 3 point shooter, but his ft shooting shows it’s probably more a matter of focus than an inability to shoot. Defensively he’s another hustle beast like Gonzalez. He has a great motor, and while he’s a little undersized for his position he’s a positive rebounder.

Player comp: Lu Dort, Marcus Smart

&nbsp;

Hannes Steinbeck - 6’11’’ PF/C 19 years

Stats at Washington: 18ppg, 11.1rpg, 1.6apg, .9bpg on 66/33/76 shooting

Steinbeck is a high floor near 7 footer. He has an NBA ready body and can most likely come in and contribute his rookie year. Offensively he has solid hands around the basket finishing plays are a roll man. It’s been low volume but Steinbach can potentially hit an occasional 3. He’s also lauded as a solid high post passer, so he doesn’t have those stone hands you’re worried about your big men. Defensively he’s a positional guy. He’s not going to block a ton of shots but he has good energy and positioning and makes things difficult for offensive players. He’s also a really good rebounder. This is the pick if the C’s are looking for a 3rd C by the time the draft rolls around.

Player comp: Jacob Poeltl, Luke Kornet

&nbsp;

Henri Veesaar - 7’0’’ PF/C 21 years

Stats at Arizona: 16.8ppg, 8.8rpg, 2apg, 1.2bpg on 63/47/61 shooting

Veesaar is another solid backup big man option. He’s a solid low volume 3 point shooter. He also has some off the dribble skill, and just knows where to be to take advantage of defenses. He’s a really good fit next to elite scorers cough Brown cough cough Tatum. He’s really solid as a trailer in transition from 3. Defensively he’s very mobile and can cover ground, but he’s not the strongest and can get bumped off his spot. He’s also not always the most focused and can give up rebounds. He is a decent shot blocker though!

Player comp: Kel’el Ware, Myles Turner

&nbsp;

Alex Karaban - 6’8’’ F 23 years

Stats at UCONN: 13.8ppg, 5.4rpg, 1.9apg on 48/42/85 shooting

Karaban is a guy I’ve been looking at for 3 years at this point and I can’t imagine a more natural fit into a Joe Mazzula/Brad Stevens run franchise. Offensively he’s a guy who makes a quick read to either shoot it or move the ball to the next guy and relocate. He’s not going to change much at the NBA level. He’s a good team passer and a high % shooter. Defensively he’s big enough and smart enough but he’s not the fastest guy. He’s not significantly different from Hauser physical profile wise, so there’s hope he can be solid defensively. Karaban is also an older prospect with 2 NCAA championships, so plenty of big moment experience. He’s also from New England, going to high school in MA and NH, and has played 4 years at UCONN, idek if he’s even left New England. If Karaban is the pick, I’d hope to trade back for him, as drafting him in the early/mid 20’s would be a reach for a guy with his age and ceiling.

Player comp: Sam Hauser, Omri Casspi

&nbsp;

Brayden Burries - 6’4’’ G 20 years

Stats at Arizona: 14.6ppg, 4.4rpg, 2.8apg on 50/36/76 shooting

Burries projects as a combo guard at the NBA level. He’s a heady offensive scorer who can use his combination of speed and herky jerky movements to get shots off. He’s considered a 3 level scorer for Arizona who can score in bunches. Even with his smallish stature, he has good intensity on D. On top of his great physical tools for a combo guard, he’s just a smart player. He’s also able to create his own shot off the dribble. We have some really great bench players right now, but we have a scenario of going into next season with PP as our starting PG, and Anfernee Simons has either been traded or signed elsewhere. Our bench is already struggling for shot creation, and Burries is a guy who can create his own shot off a broken play. Defensively at 6’4’ he’s always going to give up something, but he’s a solid defender with good effort. He might be too reliant on his pullup game, taking some deep 2’s instead of positioning for a 3 or working the ball around for a better shot.

Player comp: Anfernee Simons, Malcolm Brogdon

&nbsp;

Those are the guys I like in this range, is there anyone standing out to you?
