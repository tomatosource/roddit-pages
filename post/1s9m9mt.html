My theories for 0.5 content (act 5, blight, 3rd trial )

Reminder : This is just a fun speculation post dont take it too serious

What we know:

- Endgame will get a major rework. My theory is, that the endgame rework for poe1 were also something like a test, if people like the changes. I expect poe2 endgame to go much in this direction of poe1s rework but with its own few twists. It would also explain the long waiting time for 0.5 better, so they have time to actually use some of poe1s feedback for poe2s rework

The rest is basically unknown at this point, but there a few things , you can speculate about based on the previous patches.

New Class? - I think no. Druid was released last patch and last patch was less popular and played than 0.3 . Jonathan also stated a lot of times that adding classes is way more difficult and time consuming than adding pure content. The goal for 1.0 isnt anymore to have all the announced classes introduced into the game, so they def also put campaign and endgame upwards in the priority list for 1.0 . Also for many people that skipped 0.4 druid still be basically a new class

New Act(s)? - I think there is a high chance that we get act 5. Based on a few statements of jonathan it seems like their approach with poe2 leagues is that every second league is supposed to be a major patch that is adding a lot of content ( when talking about 0.2 he said that the league was planned to be smaller so league 3 would be massive and would reintroduce a lot of players ( which worked). Based on that and its timing 0.4 def felt very similar to 0.2 ). Acts are also one of the big priorities of GGG. Jonathan stated that a lot of times they also need to finish the campaign based on their own goals to be able to release 1.0 . Adding an act also just is a big content drop as enemies,bosses, maps all can be further used in endgame. Its also a good selling point for more casual players.

The only thing that could speak against act 5 being part of the patch is that there is a possibility that they want to use the interludes as long as they can and that their plan is to drop both act5 and 6 with 1.0. So I would say the chances are like 60% we get act 5

Third Trial: (trial of the ancestors)

This one is almost guaranteed. It neither was ready for 0.3 or 0.4 and its something people are actively waiting for. Also its needed to polish act 4 more as its acitvely missing content from that act. It also adds automatically a new layer of content for the endgame. So I would say thats almost a 100%

New Ascendencies and abilities:

As I think 0.5 will be the most important patch of poe2, I think another major focus of it will be to maximize the value of existing content. This means that all missing ascendencies will be added. I also can see the probability that we dont get ascendencies but all the missing abilitiesfor the existing weapon types. I dont think both will be part of the patch at the same time and I can imagine that one of them will be a major addition for 0.6

League Mechanic: Blight

I think a new interpretation of blight will be the the league mechanic. Its popular and relatively simple in concept. I am sure they will add a poe2 twist to it , but I feel like a tower defense like mechanic is something they could add relatively easy with exisiting content.

What will poe 2s 1.0 timeline be ( theory ):

I think 0.5 will be the most important and biggest update for poe 2 because

The waiting time is relatively long and therefore shifts the schedule of all later leagues as well. 0.4 came out in december. With 0.5 being announced for early may , thats longer than the originally planned 4 months schedule. This means if a 4 month schedule is continued afterwards we only will get one more league this year for poe2. This means for me this patch has to be huge because its meant to be a huge stepping stone for 1.0. With current schedules 0.6 would arrive september 2026 and 0.7 january 2027

So my theory for a timeline is:

0.5 : Major patch. The biggest of poe2s EA phase. It will rework endgame completely and add a lot of content that is needed for a later 1.0 launch

0.6 : smaller scale patch focused on balancing , polishing the game, increasing game stability, and maximizing the existing content: All ascendencies, all missing abilities for classes/ weapon types

1.0 : I think 1.0 will add act 6 and one class ( maybe two for a bigger hype moment ). With my timeline theory 1.0 will come out early 2027 ( either january or february)

With exilecone happening this year I expect that the 1.0 release date will be announced there. I can either imagine it being almost a suprisingly shadowdrop ( like december release ) or and thats the most likely situation them revealing that 0.7 is 1.0 and is coming out early 2027

Thanks for reading. Let me know what you think ! :)
