Light Radius and the Deep Dark

Since light radius is in the mod pool, can it have a meaningful mechanical purpose that can affect gameplay? The Maori god of darkness and evil is Whiro, surely he could inspire mechanics.

We have low light areas. What about true darkness? Abyss and Delve come to mind. Some maps are crypts and dungeons. Light Radius could weaken evil but vulnerable monsters like undead or abyssals. It could actually be a desirable, niche stat for delvers or have a niche mechanic in the TOTA afterlife.
