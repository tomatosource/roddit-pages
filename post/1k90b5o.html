Recombinator - How would you improve it?

I enjoy the recombination, I’ve made the majority of my (still quite modest) wealth from it.

However, I think it could be better.

I’d love to hear what you would do to improve it!

Lets try and keep this positive, I’m sure some of you hate the recombinator and think it needs a whole new mechanic, this thread probably isn’t for you.

To start things off - I think the stats that are added by the recombination should be fractured. Currently if you recombine two high tier stats you have to slam exalts to try and get complimentary stats, if you don’t hit and need to chaos you run the risk of removing the lines you’ve just spent time and probably 16-20 rares to get to. If the lines you combined are fractured you can then chaos the additional lines of they don’t work for you without risking bricking the item.

It would also allow you to recombine further - if you were so inclined you could spend time making a bunch of bows with flat phys and % phys, if they were all fractured you could then go again and pick two more attributes and the fractured lines would stay too. It would be expensive and time consuming but it would be an actual way to craft mirror level items!

Looking forward to hearing other people’s ideas too!
