Wishlist for 0.5

  1. Big mobility improvements: sprint less clunky and punishing when hit; unbind sprint from dodge roll; more cheap and viable mobility skills; boots have implicit MS like belts got charm slots, more MS for everyone?

  2. Layout improvements: less little nooks, crannies and objects like urns to get stuck in; less doors or auto-open option for doors when next to them; make areas smaller (especially in campaign)

  3. Atlas device shows the spot you looked at last, not the last map you cleared; Waystones and Tablets being accessible from Atlas device

  4. Endgame overhaul with much better ability to scale loot and difficulty on the top end (see PoE1 T16 & T17 maps and Void Maps), big atlas tree improvements. Make it much bigger and give us more options for customization

  5. Crafting bench for more deterministic crafting and currency sinks (especially exalts)

  6. Inventory Management improvements. Stuff like sorting by equipment types (rings, weapons etc.), integrated regex functions (i.e. show all waystones with 80+ rarity); looking for keywords such as “rarity” on tablets not being awful

  7. Map bosses actually dropping exciting loot?

  8. mobs bodyblocking (and damaging) before they can be damaged finally improved

  9. bosses talk less and have less phases of invulnerability (especially while they attack you). Maybe nerf bosses in campaign (both HP and damage). 50% of my deaths this entire league was during 2,5 days of campaign and I played a whole month. Also make Arbiter of Ash less unfair, maybe?

  10. Party play loot should stay the same regardless of how many people are nearby

  11. Giga juicing Vaal Temple with 50+ hours playtime for setup not going core

  12. Fix for having to search for single white mobs hidden in the ground to finish stuff like strongboxes or rituals

  13. more currency drops in campaign, so you can actually craft some gear. I think I dropped like 6 Regal Orbs during the entire campaign and ran around with a magic quiver in act 4 - what the f?

  14. Currency exchange: take currency from stash, not inventory; autofill of numbers doesn’t mess you up constantly or suggests numbers that are way off market ratios

  15. more power from passive tree and less from equipment

Did I miss anything else?
