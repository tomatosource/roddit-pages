Thoughts on Jay Huff?

HoopGrids reported that the Celtics are very interested in him (I can’t post the Twitter link).

I don’t really put any validity into Hoop Grids’ reporting, but Jay absolutely seems like the type of center Brad would be interested in. I see you guys positing various centers who can’t shoot, and that has never been Brad’s MO.

Brad has spent assets for 3 centers ever: KP, Al, and Vuc. Now, Vuc was somewhat just as a tax measure, but still. That is a skill Brad obviously values in a center. I’m not really counting a 2nd round pick on Amari as much of an asset. He was drafted with the 46th pick and gets paid nothing.

We got Queta for absolutely nothing and pay him nothing, so I wouldn’t really count him.

Jay is on a tiny contract ($2.67m) and turns 29 this year. He is one of the better rim protectors in the league (-7.1% at the rim this year) and has some signs that he can shoot. Career 35.1% on 10.7 attempts per 100 possessions (that is *very* high for a center - KP is at 8.4 for his career). Career 82.7% from the line and his form looks fine. His advanced analytics weren’t great this year for Indiana, but nobody was going to have good advanced stats on that team. Neemie didn’t either before this year, and was excellent by multiple stats this year. I think if you put any competent center in our lineup they’re going to have good advanced stats.
