Rick Carlisle is the 13th winningest playoff coach all time

Carlisle will enter his fifth conference finals with a total of 79 playoff wins (49%) under his belt, essily the most experienced and accomplished playoff coach still in this post season. (Thib = 46-51, not too shabby either but still behind)

I made a post about Reggie Miller the other day… Turns out I also have a passing interest in Rick Carlisle as a coach. I remember him as, and I think quite a few will agree, the mastermind of arguably the most “deserved” championship since Pistons in 2004, with the Mavs beating the Heatles (after beating the Thunder!) using a highly effective defensive scheme that clogged the lane and forced Lebron to take uncomfortable Js. It was a helluva roster yes, but I don’t think it should discredit Carlisle.

Just thought of putting this out there and learn a few things more about coaching. Imo the Pacers are in very good hands. I hope Carlisle’s experience will guide those boys to greater success.
