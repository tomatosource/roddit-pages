Legacy of Phrecia league got me thinking…

Hey GGG,

With Legacy of Phrecia coming in a couple days, i decided to get back on POE1 and readjust myself to it and within an hour of playing, I felt strongly enough to come and drop a post to see if others have also experienced the same feelings, and hopefully if this makes it’s way to GGG maybe someone will listen. “copium”

I understand that GGG doesnt want players to feel that POE2 is just a reboot with a new layer of paint on the outside, but I feel that they have gone so far in the opposite direction that the game is loosing alot of the look and feel that POE1 gave us. Some of the best parts of POE1 were just removed for the sake of being removed, and i think its a big mistake. I will list the things that come to my mind as the glarring changes, but please feel free to add anything else in the comments and hopefully they see this if it gets upvoted.

For instance, the new style of crafting is fine, but their vision of finding some “good item” and finishing the craft is so far fethced for 95%+ of players, and by implementing this style of crafting it just makes every other part of custimization in the game completely one dimensional. If they reintroduced Alteration and Scourings and made Essences drop more and have a better effect, and lastly the crafting bench, so that players could add a 6th mod on it would give back the feeling of crafting and owning an item tailored for you and specifically for your build. Lastly, for heaven’s sake just use the old tier system for affixes, its just confusing af now for absolutely no reason.

Secondly, as much as I want to comment on the end game in POE2, I know that it was just implemented as a temporary thing

POE1 in itself was an amazing game, to the point if GGG simply made the graphics better, implemented WASD movement, made all new classes and ascendancies, and a new end game grind, while leaving the rest of the game, modes, crafting, mapping system, ascendancy system the same and just released that game. Well it would be a masterpiece just like POE1.

With that being said, GGG we all support you and cant wait for what you bring out in the next major update for POE2, and hopefully that next update will feel more nostalgic like POE1.1 versus completely differemt and new but not necessarily better. The fix would be easy enough, just bring more game elements (Harvest, Crafting bench, POE1 Crafting, Scarabs) from POE1 into POE2, then the game will have more nostalgic vibes.

Thanks alot!

hopefully someone from GGG can weigh in and these topics can get discussed.
