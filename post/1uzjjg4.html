Heres what i did to craft this base.

  1. get a 30% sleek jacket base (buy or quality it yourself). i used a ilvl 81 base here as i didnt need T1 ele res.

  2. hit the fracture on T1 defences. (dont forget to div it up like i did) then chaos spam for another T1 defense line. i would avoid hybrid evasion%/life. that line is ass.

  3. slam on the last defence line with perf exalt.

  4. desecrate for T1 life regen to block. then add one more suffix and whittle till you hit T1 deflect.

  5. add on medved tending rune at this step and continue whittling for reduced crit dmg bonus.

  6. omen of light jail for the last line that you want. i wanted chaos res or es recharge rate.

originally, i wanted to whittle for chaos res and then omen of light jail till i hit the crit dmg reduction. but whatever works.
