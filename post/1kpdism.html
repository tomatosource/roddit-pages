Designing for Fun, Not Just for Loot!

I’ve been thinking about how certain mechanics in Path of Exile 2—and many other games—aren’t actually fun on their own.

The only reason players engage with them is for the rewards. To me, that’s a sign of poor design.

Take the Trial of the Sekhemas, for example. Would anyone genuinely enjoy running it if there were no rewards or ascendancy points at the end? Probably not.

Contrast that with parts of the game that are fun without needing extra incentives: blasting through hordes of mobs, running Breach encounters, or fighting well-designed bosses. These experiences are engaging in their own right.

TL;DR: If a game mechanic isn’t fun without rewards, it’s probably bad design. Good mechanics should be enjoyable even with no loot at the end.
