Mo Dakhil on the Miami Heat front office: “Maybe make some moves to get some assets back, and then be ready for when the Giannis [or the next big name] trade comes. But they’re not even preparing for that. They assume everyone wants Tyler Herro.”

Source: https://open.spotify.com/episode/2LPQELL1rPJwIzGuX2JlFL?si=ELNXloU4T5uyO8DzzWu97w&t=3153&pi=G0rXaGL5RPuvw

On the Miami Heat front office:

“We’ve done the ‘Miami Heat are interested in X and are going to make a major move’ and I get it: They were in it for Giannis up until Milwaukee said they’re not going to trade him and all that, but we kill the Bulls for being on the treadmill of mediocrity. How long are we going to allow Miami to get away with this as well?”

“… They’ve been a Play-In team for 3 years. They’re in the Play-In range now, so it’s going to be 4 straight years at this point.”

“I’m tired of this stuff of them saying ‘We’re Whale-Hunters. We’re gonna go get a guy’ and then they never do.”

“And part of it is that they might not have the means to do it… Maybe make some moves to get some assets back, and then be ready for when the Giannis [or the next big name] trade comes. But they’re not even preparing for that. They assume everyone wants Tyler Herro.”
