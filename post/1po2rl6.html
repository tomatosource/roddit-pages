Staggering Palm got reworked but without actually fixing anything

Staggering Palm gives you a ~7.5 second buff whenever you stun an enemy with it.
This on its own is still clunky and janky because melee collision sometimes simply prevents you from hitting a moving target at all, even if you have 100% accuracy and the target is primed for stun.

But now GGG tried to fix and rework it, with almost 0 effect.

Staggering Palm now grants its buff if a primed enemy is stunned or killed while you are dashing to them

This straight up doesn’t work. Staggering palms projectiles fire before the melee attack hits when you already have the buff: resulting in you stunning the target with the projectile, not the melee part and therefore STILL not extending the buff. The skill prevents itself from working and this hasn’t changed that at all.

Now also quickly Dashes and Strikes additional valid targets.

This is complete RNG. There can be 10 primed targets on top of each other and you only get a 7.5 second buff. Some other times the mobs are 50 miles away from each other and it randomly dashes to them like flicker strike would. (Why?)

Projectiles now deal 61-204% of Attack Damage at Gem levels 3-20

A big damage buff to a skill that is barely scalable with any projectile nodes as it can neither fork nor benefit from more projectiles. It straight up just doesnt shotgun and there is no way to fix that.

Added Nova Projectiles II, causing Supported skills to gain 6 Additional Projectiles, deal 50% less Damage and applying a Cooldown Time of 8 seconds to Supported Skills.

With Nova projectiles now completely unusable on the skill, its not even desirable to use as a supportive skill to your kit for blind/daze debuffs.

Why is GGG so reluctant to make non “main” skills viable?
And why in the hell was Hollow palm nerfed even though it was already underperforming on top of all of that?
