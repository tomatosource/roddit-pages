For many years, Ed Porray was thought to be the only major leaguer born at sea – but research has revealed a second one, Red Fisher!

TAke a look, y’all: Red Fisher on Baseball Reference.

He was thought to have been born in Pittsburgh in 1887, but is now listed as being born in the Atlantic Ocean (presumably on a boat, but it isn’t specified) in 1881. Here’s an archived version if you wish to compare. So next time you’re doing Immaculate Grid and you have some combination of Played for BAL/Born Outside US/Played LF or RF, consider Red Fisher.

I found this a few days ago while browsing through the latest SABR Biographical Research Committee newsletter, but I waited to post about it here until Baseball Reference updated, because it’s way more exciting to link to Baseball Reference than to a .pdf.
