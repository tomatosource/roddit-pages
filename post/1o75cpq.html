What are the benefits of the infinite atlas vs having maps drop on waystones

Some people seem to be blindly defending the new system vs poe1’s, and I have to ask why.

I first started poe2 4 weeks back. The new Atlas system is bad, just to be blunt, its clunky and gives no benefit over POE1.

Ive seen multiple posts of people complaining, and many people blindly defending, well it looks like the devs have already acknowledged that their precursor tablet/tower system was terrible, and have essentially reverted to the poe1 scarab system, so I would ask people defending poe2 for the sake of it to bear that in mind.

You have to scroll for ever to find nodes, and often lose them.

Counter reply is “you can bookmark them”, yes you can, but what is the benefit of this system in the first place.

Also needing dozens of bookmarks to simply replicate the ease that having maps drop on waystone provides is just stupid, why make the system so much harder for the sake of it.

We could make many other systems more convoluted, non user friendly etc, and the reply could always be “just use a notepad”, but if thats your defending argument you have to acknowledge the system is not optimised properly.

So what benefits does the current system hold over simply finding rare maps as waystones? I can’t think of any, and since so many people seem intent on claiming the poe2 atlas isnt terrible, there must be a miriad of reasons why the atlas taking ages to load, needing bookmarks to navigate, it being less streamlined and organised, it being less malleable for the user etc are somehow worth it.

Imagine if we could simply sell our castaways rather than need to use 3rd party apps to do so?

If people don’t have any decent arguments as to why the new system is good, they have to acknowledge reverting to POE1 is the better solution, which the devs have already started with the tablet change.
