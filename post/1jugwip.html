Suggestion - Orbs of Alchemy should drop frequently during the campaign

Title. As it currently stands, crafting through the campaign with anything but Transmutes/Augs and an occasional regal feels pretty terrible. Using an exalt on a level 24 item will never not feel like a waste, when theres the option to just trade that exalt for a much better item.

It kind of feels bad to spend any exalts at all in the campaign, when really I should be saving them to gear for maps. Regals also feels bad to use on anything but jewels, 3 affixes on a piece of gear is pretty weak for how rare they are in campaign.

One bandaid solution is already ready— orbs of alchemy. Alching a solid base gives you at least a solid chance of making something useful, and if the affixes are bad you can sell or reforge. This at least incentivizes players to keep an eye out for good bases, rather than just trashing everything but rares that drop with good mods.

The crafting system is already pretty limited, and being disincentivized so heavily from interacting with it for the bulk of the campaign doesn’t help. I can’t even imagine playing SSF in this game currently, just seems like “hope you get lucky or you will do no damage” as a game mode.
