Today marks the halfway point of the offseason

December 21 is exactly halfway between November 1 (the date of World Series Game 7) and February 10 (when pitchers and catchers report for most clubs).

So as of today, not only will the days start getting longer for those of us in the Northern Hemisphere, but the light at the end of the tunnel will start to grow brighter and brighter. Before you know it, players will be showing up to camp in the best shape of their lives, guys will have made some changes to their swing, other guys will have been working on a new pitch, for some reason nobody will ever say they’ve been working on their defense or baserunning, and the WBC will be right around the corner.

We’re halfway home folks.
