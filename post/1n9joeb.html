For the 3rd time in 4 years, an OKC Thunder rookie has suffered a major injury that will preclude them from participating in the upcoming season: Thomas Sorber sustained an ACL tear

Today, it was announced that rookie center Thomas Sorber suffered an ACL tear in his right knee during an offseason workout. This marks the 3rd such significant offseason injury for an OKC rookie in the past four seasons:

  • 2022⁄23: Honest “Chet” Abe suffered a Lisfranc fracture when guarding LeBron at Pro-Am.
  • 2023⁄24: No notable injuries prior to the season starting.
  • 2024⁄25: Prior to the draft, Nikola Topic suffered an ACL tear in the middle of a game. He’s an NBA champion despite never playing a minute for the team.
  • 2025:26: Sorber’s aforementioned ACL tear.
