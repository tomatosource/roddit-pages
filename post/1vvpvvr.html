LeBron has changed teams for the 4 most recent World Cup Summers.

2010 — The Decision. LeBron leaves the Cavs to sign with the Heat.

2014 — Homecoming. LeBron returns to Cleveland.

2018 — LA-Bron. LeBron heads to California.

2022 — Winter World Cup in Qatar. LeBron remains on the Lakers.

2026 — The Last Chance. LeBron announces he is signing with the Sixers a week after the World Cup final.
