Navigating the atlas remains cumbersome as well as tablet management

Let me preface this by saying I genuinely enjoy running map content. I find something to like in almost every league mechanic: the updated Abyss clouds is good, the loot change and the direction pointing changes in ritual are good, even the changes in breach are nice and expedition feels less brainy.

My issue isn’t with the maps themselves, but with the logistics of getting there: navigating the Atlas, managing tablets, and dealing with the Vaal city meta. I know the common counterarguments: “You don’t need to juice,” “Alch & go is fine,” or “If you don’t like it, don’t do it.” You’re not wrong, but I have this nagging feeling that the current system is forcing suboptimal playstyles if you don’t like all the tediousness connected to it. The min-maxing feels tedious rather than smooth, and if enough people share this sentiment, maybe it’s worth discussing a potential change

1. Navigating the Atlas is cumbersome.

This is an old tale in PoE2, but it feels worse now. If you want to get from point A to point B without wasting time, you often can’t. Last league Grand Project was accessible enough that mapping felt fluid. I loved citadel farming and hunting towers with minimal travel nodes. Now, Grand Project is prohibitively expensive (5-6 Divs a pop), making it unsustainable for most players.

With the 0.5 update, the issue has escalated. We have so many areas of interest: Ritual ares, Breach hives, Vaal cities, Corrupted Nexus, Citadels, Anomaly maps. The Atlas feels cluttered. These options only really work if you want a jack-of-all-trades experience. If you want to take your time, you can spend some time in a breach hive, then clear a nexus, then do a citadel boss and so on… but if you want to focus on a specific strategy, most of these nodes are just noise. I don’t want to clear a breach hive or a cleansed area if I’m hunting for citadels, I just want to get there in the minor time possible. Also different strats require a completely different set of tablets so that I don’t loadout for so I need to constantly swap in and out if I want to play like that. The meta is typically run 10 things of the same time due to the tablet design.

2. Tablet management is a chore.

Sure, I could “alch & go” with tablets too, slotting 3 random ones, but it feels bad. If you try to actually build a strategy around your tablets, you have to go through an incredibly tedious sorting procedure. It’s so cumbersome that it makes me want to quit mapping entirely. It’s reached a point where I feel pushed toward Expedition just to avoid the FOMO of not having the “perfect” setup of tablet, just to scroll endlessly in the sea to the correct set of rumors. Every juicing strategy currently feels like it’s bound by administrative chores before the actual content even begins.

3. The Vaal City meta feels like a regression.

I recall Jonathan saying they disliked the old 3-4 tower setups because players felt forced into one “best” way to play. Yet, here we are again. With the 4th tablet rule and biomes favoring Vaal cities, anything else feels suboptimal. Why place tablets in random areas when you can stack four in a Vaal city? It forces you to path through ten maps you don’t care about just to reach your juice. It’s the same old mechanic, and I don’t understand why we’re back here.

4. Biome manipulation is too restrictive.

I rarely bother checking a map’s biome because I can’t manipulate it. What’s the point of choosing what a mountain biome does if I can’t effectively select it? We need to be able to navigate toward specific biomes, but right now, I just have to pick whatever is in front of me. The tablets that allow you to change biomes are as rare as Grand Project, which keeps us in the same loop of frustration.

My tiny suggestion (which is preached by many)

Give towers the inherent property of terramorphing and/or Grand Project.

  • Want to farm a specific area? Path to the tower, terramorph the biome to fit your strat, and clear the zone.
  • Want to chase specific points on the Atlas? Use the Grand Project property to get in and out quickly.

Making the towers meaningful tools for navigation would fix so much of the friction. As it stands, I also really hope they consider nerfing the incentive to run exclusively Vaal cities.
