What makes the Cavs so good this season compared to before?

The Cavs had a 48-34 record last season and finished 4th in the east. Now they have a 55-10 record and are comfortably 1st.

Usually, if a team makes that big of a jump from one season to another, it has to do with one of the following reasons:

  • they made a blockbuster trade or some really good trades
  • a key player or some of their players made big improvements in their game
  • they had big injury problems in the years before and are now healthy

For the Cavs, none of these things really seem to be the case, right? They didn’t make a big trade, they had some injury concerns last season (Mitchell and Garland played around 60 games last year, Mobley around 50, Allen around 80) but nothing too crazy that explains that big of a difference in team performance.

And when I looked up the boxscore stats, it doesn’t seem like one of their key players made a big jump in his development compared to the year before. Like for example, Mitchell was 26.6⁄5.1⁄6.1 last year and is 24.2⁄4.3⁄4.8 now. Darius Garland was 18.0/2.7⁄6.5 last year and is 21.0/2.6⁄6.7 now. Jarret Allen was 16.5⁄10.5⁄2.7 last year and is 13.6⁄10.3⁄1.9 now. Evan Mobley was 15.7⁄9.4⁄3.1 last year and is 18.6⁄9.3⁄3.1 now.

So with no big trades and no major improvements (at least when looking at basic boxscore stats) of their key players, why are the Cavs all of a sudden playing a historically good season?
