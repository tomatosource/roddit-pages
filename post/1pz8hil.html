Joe Kelly’s comment on players retiring had me wondering. Who are some players who might fit a narrower definition of retirement? Guys that had legit stuff to still offer, but walked away.

For context, here was Joe Kelly’s quote:

“Retiring is like something that my grandmother did,” he said. “I’m sorry all you people out there watching this that work a real job. You guys deserve to retire; athletes don’t. We just stop playing. Let’s cancel the word ‘retirement.’”

I think I understand his sentiment, and to a certain degree I agree with him. It’s funny to me when young guys no team is interested in announce they are choosing to retire. On the other hand I feel like even people who “work real jobs” aren’t usually as productive at the end of their career, and I would still consider that retiring. So for a guy like Kelly who maybe doesn’t have the same market he once had but stuck around and contributed longer than most I would consider this a retirement.

But if we narrow the definition of retirement to guys who still had plenty to offer and multiple interested teams, who are guys that you would say would fit that narrower definition of retirement?
