Path of Exile is too grindy

I’m fairly new to the whole arpg / dungeon crawler genre. Only other similar games I’ve played before were Might Quest for Epic Loot and Minecraft dungeons. Both of which I thoroughly enjoyed for being fun to play and fairly easy to understand. But I was ready for something more complex.

I got both Diablo 4 and Path of Exile 2 as part of the sales and decided to try PoE 2 first which I deeply regret. The size of the skill tree alone is completely overwhelming. Build guides are also hard to understand and when trying to follow them still wasn’t doing enough damage or getting killed easily. I got 20 hours in and felt progress was extremely slow for the time I put in and still don’t really understand most of the items / stats.

I then try Diablo 4 and instantly started enjoying it. I’ve now put 30 hours in and already have a good understanding of the skill tree and gems / codexes better than I did in Path of Exile 2.

It’s less overwhelming in Diablo and I feel like Path of Exile has too much information and a grind. My sorcerer on Diablo 4 is already doing 2k crit attacks with my chain lightning spell. I can melt bosses with my alt and nearly ready for torment difficulty.

Path of Exile on other hand, still stuck doing 30 damage and mobs are health sponges.

I may give Path of Exile another go, but first I’m probably going to put 500 hours or more into Diablo 4. It’s a much more fun game and easier to pick up and understand. Build guides are also easier to follow due to having less moving parts.
