Most, if not all media, is currently boasting betting ads. The people who tell you who’s good/bad at basketball, are the ones that profit from you being WRONG.

(Dear diary,)

The people telling you who’s going to win and lose. The people who “analyse the statistics”. From the yappers to the stats nerds, more than ever, they’re all interrupting the message for some betting odds. To tell you where to place the bets. To give you the latest promo info.

They profit from you being WRONG. The wronger you are, the more money their advertisers make.

I don’t care if you’re Chuck spouting the name of a betting agency, or you’re Jason Timpf analysing a specific play.

My hot take; Whether you know what you’re talking about or not, if you promote gambling, you’ve last all basketball analysis credibility.

Whenever we discuss basketball on here, and bring in ANY media funded by betting agencies, they ought to be immediately disqualified from having an opinion in the first place.

This sucks because there’s good ones out there. It’s just all tainted.

Curious to hear what everyone else thinks about this.

.edit: after some further discourse;

  • Betting agencies benefit in funding the discourse around NBA basketball, because 90% of basketball opinions that could “make you money” are wrong.
  • None of this is necessarily about conspiracy theories and people being paid to say the wrong thing, even though that is a possibility too.
  • Saying “this team has an outside chance to win” in the same line as “their odds are [x] and [y]” is morally reprehensible as it turns JOURNALISM into a straight gambling ad

Basically I’m arguing you lose ALL journalistic integrity the second you promote gambling.
