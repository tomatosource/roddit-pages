Moment of KUDOS for Jeff Fassero, and his last pro baseball team, the 2007-08 Mayos de Navojoa.

Fassero was born in 1963 and began his pro baseball career in 1984 for St. Louis. Remarkably, a former teammate of his is still playing!

He wasn’t able to make the majors until 1991, when he was already 28. He made it count, posting a 2.64 ERA in his first four seasons over 429 innings. Remarkably, he became the last active major league player from the 1991 Expos team, ending his career after 2006 with SF at age 43.

However he got the itch again and a year later went down to Mexico to play for 2007-08 Mayos de Navojoa. His teammates on that team included:

  1. Mike Hampton
  • Isidro Márquez,  who later had the Best Pro Baseball Season Ever for a 55+ year old

• • Orlando Lara, who is still playing! Played for Veracruz next to Derek Rodriguez 2025
