The Detroit Pistons are now the first team in NBA history to triple their win total in a single season

The short-handed Pistons beat the east-leading Cavs to earn their 42nd win of the season after finishing 14-68 last year, something no other team has accomplished in the history of the league. The win also secures their first winning season since 2015-16.

In other news, JB Bickerstaff is currently not the front-runner to win coach of the year.
