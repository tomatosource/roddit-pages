Patch notes use wrong current damage numbers for Arc

The patch notes use the wrong current damage numbers for Arc. In the “Skill Changes” portion of the patch notes it reads:

Now deals 2-39 damage at Gem level 5 (previously 5-31), scaling up to 20-386 damage at gem level 20 (previously 49-277).

Also:

It no longer causes explosions against shocked enemies and is no longer a payoff skill.

But the damage numbers the patch notes cite are the current damage numbers for the pulse that Arc releases against shocked enemies. The current damage numbers for Arc itself at lvl 20 are 82-462.

This should be rechecked.
