Excerpts from Nikola Jokić’s interview with X&O’s Chat

Nikola Jokic on if he could imagine himself playing somewhere other than Denver

“I wouldn’t like to imagine that.” “Even if we never win anything else after this, an organic title, it means more to me than anything.” “I really found peace here. My two kids were born here. Everyone’s here. Peace, home, I found my life here. And I like life here. I don’t feel the need, I don’t have the urge to. We built something here, together as a team.”

Nikola Jokic on what motivates him

“Winning motivates me. Playing well motivates me. A good routine motivates me. A good weight room motivates me.” “Good company motivates me. A good drink motivates me”

Nikola Jokic on how long he plans on playing professional basketball

“I want to play as long as I can.” “As long as I’m creating an advantage and I can play at the highest level.”

Nikola Jokic on if he’d ever play professionally in Europe again

“No.” “Nothing against the EuroLeage, Europe, or anything, it’s just that I wouldn’t.”

Nikola Jokic explaining why he sometimes doesn’t shoot the ball in a game

“I’ve had those moments and I don’t know why. Maybe it’s some kind of stubbornness. The day just comes…and I just didn’t want to, I literally didn’t want to. I make the extra pass, like “I don’t want it”.”
