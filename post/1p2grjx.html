Two potential HOF case narratives to watch out for in 2026.

Ahead of the beginning of the season, I’d like to bring up two things that are pretty likely to happen in 2026 that could tremendously boost the Hall of Fame cases of two players, but which aren’t being talked about much (at least from my anecdotal perspective).

First is with Aaron Judge. To clarify, I think he’s already solidified his eventual enshrinement, I just want to bring up another accolade he can add to his ledger. Last year, Judge reached the 50 home run club for the fourth time, and in doing so, he became one of just three players to get 50 home runs in four separate seasons (the others are Mark McGwire and Sammy Sosa). With how proficient he’s continued to be in terms of his home run power, I think there’s a very real chance that he could become the sole player to reach the mark five times (barring his toe being okay, of course). Additionally, if he gets even just 50, he’ll be 82 away from reaching the 500 home run mark, with five seasons on his current contract (after next season) to get there, which is plenty of time.

Second is Kenley Jansen. As much as people like to roll their eyes at saves, the fact is that Jansen is only three saves away from surpassing HOFer Lee Smith to reach third all-time in the stat, only behind Mariano Rivera and Trevor Hoffman. On top of that, if Jansen keeps up his current pace of at least 25 per season (the only times he has gone below that figure were in his first two seasons and in COVID year), he’ll reach 500 saves, a feat that only Mo and Hoffman have reached.

Thoughts? Anything I missed?
