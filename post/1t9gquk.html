Skill Gem and Support gem scarcity makes swapping from a speed campaign build to your main build feel terrible.

With all the races and realization that I’ll probably be playing this as long as I’ve played POE1 I started working on my campaign times so it doesn’t take me years like it did in POE1 to get sub 6hr clears.

Unlike POE1 though there’s no gem vendor. I can gamble and get some basic SSF gear, buy waystones and 3:1 to go up tiers even, but there’s no way to bypass the waiting to drop skill and support gems so that I can swap my build.

Obviously it’s known people like to swap builds, the gold cost is fair at this point in my opinion. This gem scarcity roadblock makes it so you have to keep playing a campaign build and increases the cost of swapping as you’ll be higher leveled.

I don’t see a reason to keep it this way. I think a system like the waystone one where you can buy gems at the tier you’ve dropped them or character level dictates should be available. Maybe cap it at some arbitrary level so that in the end the best is still drop only but give us a way to buy gems and experiment without needing to have cleared 10s of maps to find them.
