I just want to point out that there are no two-handed Spears.

One-handed, wooden shafts with a point metal tip, used at short range with a shield, or thrown, are Javelins. Spears are two handed, used to keep opponents at range, and aren’t typically thrown.

GGG is out here calling a turtle a tortoise, a wyvern a dragon, etc. It’s literally unplayable.
