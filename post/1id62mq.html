After starting 10-15, the Pacers have won 15 of their last 20 games.

The Pacers are tied with the Rockets and Cavs for the second best record in their last 20 games at 15-5 (Thunder are first at 17-3). In this stretch they’ve secured away game wins against top teams like the Cavs, Celtics, and post-new years Pistons. A lot of this can be ascribed to defensive improvements. In their first 25 games, the Pacers were among the worst defensive teams in the league, ranking 6th worst in Defensive Rating at 118.1. But in their last 20 games, they’ve been the 8th best defense in the league in Defensive Rating at 112.7. The return of Andrew Nembhard has been a big help, Thomas Bryant is no longer a traffic cone, and Jarace Walker looks like he’s taking a leap. Only question mark seems to be rebounding, but the Pacers are looking dangerous if they keep this up.
