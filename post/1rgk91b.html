On March 16, the Atlanta Hawks will be hosting “Magic City Night”, an event to celebrate the city’s famous strip club.

A bit surprising that in a league with so many kid-friendly promotions, the Hawks will be honoring a strip club.

But there’s a reason behind it: The Hawks principal owner Jami Gertz is also a filmmaker who produced a 5-part documentary about the strip club.

The full details of the promotion are not available, but the Hawks plan to have T.I. Perform at half time and DJ Esco will be in charge of pre-game music.
