As someone who is just not interested in crafting, I really hope 0.4 focuses on the other half of endgame: Maps and the Atlas.

Don’t misunderstand the title. I love the changes to crafting and the addition of the market in 0.3. This isn’t a complaint post at all. I’m genuinely stoked for everyone who’s having fun crafting right now. Crafting just isn’t my thing.

Mapping isn’t terrible by any means. It’s fun enough to do for a few days after you finish the campaign, but it gets stale fairly quickly. The Atlas is pretty uninteresting to me. It doesn’t give me the same excitement as the passive tree when I get more points. I hope they can find some way to make it more interesting.

The variety of maps is much better now, but I definitely hope they add even more. Expedition is really the only somewhat unique mechanic in mapping. All the others are just killing big packs basically. I do hope they can add more to each of the mechanics to give them more identity and make them more fun.

Overall I think 0.3 has been a great success. I definitely had way more playtime in this league over the others so far. But now that crafting is in a good place and the market has been added, I hope that 0.4 sees some interesting changes to mapping.
