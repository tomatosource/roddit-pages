Mike Trout is Walking at a Historic Pace

Mike Trout currently has 32 walks in 30 games played, over one walk per game. (Or 32 walks in 31 team games.) He is walking 22.9 percent of the time, which is more than anyone since Barry Bonds. He is on pace for 167 walks, which is the most among non-Bonds players since Babe Ruth in 1923. This incredible walk rate will probably go down a bit, but this awesome walk pace has been a major part of Trout’s bounce back season. His .436 OBP is right in line with his prime OBP’s from 2016-2019, despite a batting average of only .248. He is walking more than he has struck out, which is a major change from his very strikeout heavy last 5 years. Let’s hope Trout stays healthy, and Keeps on walking to first base.
