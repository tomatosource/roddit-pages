Huge change to Sekhemas no one is talking about

From the patch notes:

Relic modifier “Light-footed” can no longer roll. Relic modifier “Acrobatic” now grants “Traps deal (7-5)% reduced Damage” (previously 15-12%). Relic modifier “Limber” now grants “Traps deal (10-8)% reduced Damage” (previously 15-12%).

This means that 100% trap reduction is no longer possible, making no hit runs much more difficult. Temporalis will spike hard the patch.
