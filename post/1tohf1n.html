Zach Lowe reads out the Draft Credits proposal gaining traction around the league

https://open.spotify.com/episode/4YEiFWtpPRyXxeADvTrI32?si=m3euRuLVSZy–DnZ31ctUA&t=5135

transcript copy-and-pasted from spotify below. Zach is talking to John Hollinger, for context.

tl;dr

  • Vorkunov from The Athletic reported yesterday: “A draft credit system is seen as the leading contender to take effect when this* runs out, according to league and team sources, and is favored by a number of team executives. The league will begin to study it closely once this reform passes.”
  • *meaning the 3-2-1 lottery reform proposal, which will likely pass this week, and is scheduled to end after 3 seasons/2029 draft
  • draft credit proposal was written by the Celtics, shared to Zach by other front offices
  • teams get 100 credits every season. credits can roll over each year. you can trade draft credits. each playoff series you win deducts 20 draft credits
  • you bid the credits on draft slots. you can bid for multiple draft slots within each draft.
  • late April, every team secretly sends a bid for the draft slot(s) they are interested in. early May, the league publishes these bids
  • “The top four highest bidders for that pick are put into a lottery amongst themselves, with probability proportional to the number of credits each team bid toward that slot.”
  • “The winner of this mini lottery gets the first pick, gets charged halfway between their own bid and the bid of the lowest bidder, and is removed out of the pool for the second pick.”
  • “The number 5 bidder is added to the three non winners and another lottery is held. And we do that all the way down until the end.”
  • “Teams may bid zero and just pick at the end of the first round, and the second round operates the same way.”
