Minor league towns with a clear favorite MLB team that hosts a rival MLB team’s affiliate

I always knew this was a reality for some MiLB towns but I experienced it in person for the first time a few years ago, and it was weird.

The Nationals’ High-A affiliate is the Wilmington Blue Rocks. Wilmington, DE is bona fide Phillies territory, and that’s the MLB team you see represented most among the crowd.

I once went to a game when they played Jersey Shore, the Phillies’ High-A team. I sorta kinda thought the crowd, Phillies fans, might acknowledge that fact at least a little bit and I’d hear some claps/cheers for that team of Phillies prospects.

Lol nope. Didn’t matter. And I get it. It’s not “the Phillies,” and affiliates can change. Minor league crowds are also generally more casual than MLB crowds, as a rule. And the people are rooting for the local team irrespective of who’s actually wearing the jerseys.

It was just kinda funny to witness. Are there any other situations like that in the minors? Have you seen it personally with current or past affiliates? Lol do you wish your local team would change its affiliation?
