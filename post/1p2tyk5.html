Why you should use Trinity.

Filling Trinity gauges is not as hard as you think you generally just need to have roughly equal amount of fire and lightning damage then use Cold Attunement and Embitter on herald of lighting to fill up the cold gauge. I have about 90% uptime of all 3 gauges during mapping.

The cons:

Need to free up 90 spirit(with Dialla’s Desire). I use a 30 spirit enchantment helmet + 60 spirit body armor. If you can’t find 30 spirit helmet may need to get rid of one of the defensive persistents like Wind Dancer.

The pros:

  1. Compared my own top end Trinity to non Trinity builds, the former generally do 30% more damage when gauges are filled.
  2. Huge QoL upgrade in crafting weapons. You no longer need to chase merciless and T1 flat phy because all you want is one flat lightning and one flat fire. The third prefix can be anything conducive to your build(+ elemental damage or + quiver bonus for bow). You no longer need to chase lvl82 perfect base. Lvl81 can hit all the mods you want(minus +2 bolt for crossbow). Getting a exceptional base become dirt cheap.
