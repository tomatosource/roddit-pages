Necropolis League would be a better League in PoE 2 than PoE 1.

I know many didn’t enjoy the League, specifically the crafting system. But I really do feel like it would tie in with PoE 2, far better than in PoE 1.

Because PoE 1 already has such a wide array of crafting options, it resulted in a League where these different mechanics were unfortunately, disregarded. The ‘Borrowed Power’, was too strong.

But it feels like the design concept would work far better in PoE 2, possibly because of a lack of crafting options available.

Essences would suffer a bit, but items like Catalysts, Breach Rings and systems like Rogue Exiles, Wisps, Strongboxes etc, wouldn’t be impacted.
