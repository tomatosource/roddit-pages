One of the best “next man up” situations and total team rallies in NBA history

  • This team lost its only 2 All-star Veterans to season ending injuries during the regular season
  • They still made it to the Conference Finals game 7 (but lost in a close one)

Here’s the points / rebounds / assists averages in the playoffs for the guys that played:

  1. 18.5 / 4.4 / 2.7 (19 year old)
  2. 18 / 4.8 / 1.4 (21 year old)
  3. 16.5 / 5.3 / 5.7 (23 year old)
  4. 15.7 / 8.3 / 3.3
  5. 12.4 / 5.4 / 1.1
  6. 9.8 / 3.7 / 5.3
  7. 6 / 6.2 / 1

This team was so young for a Conference Finals team, and just look at how much they all shared responsibilities.

No one averaged more than 19 points or 9 rebounds or 6 assists and they still made it 1 game away from the Finals.

They didnt play more than 7 guys because their top 2 All-stars were out and didn’t have much else to work with.

This is one of the best examples of a total team next man up effort I’ve ever seen.

After thinking about this more, I almost feel like this is one of the best coaching jobs in NBA playoff history (where a coach doesn’t win at least).
