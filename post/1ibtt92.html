Mentality, Performance, and Coaching Balance

4th quarter performances have been our biggest struggles. Even in games we won, 4th quarters were where we were getting beat. We are consistently bad in 4th quarters even though the coaching response from Joe has been different multiple times. And on top of this, this season isn’t the only times we’ve been bad in the 4th, as pre-2023-24 we’ve seen this too. The bad 4th quarter scare has been so ingrained in each player’s head that the self-fulfilling prophecy just works its way out to reality every game. If they THINK they’re going to struggle in the 4th then they WILL struggle in the 4th. So then they’re nervous and tense which consequently leads to bad shots, turnovers, stiff defense, etc. Joe being an elite coach simply can’t fix players being nervous. It’s not that Joe won’t change or he has no backup plan, it’s that the players get anxious when opponents score on them in the 4th (especially if they go on a run) and then they get scared. “Oh my god, this (mid team) is actually catching up to us, we can’t lose this. omg omg omg….”

If we want to fix this issue, then Joe’s coaching needs to consist of as much strategic training as mental training. Aside from reviewing film, Joe needs to know what each players’ true feelings are when they play. If I were the Celtic’s head coach, I’d probably want to do 1 on 1 talks with my players after almost every game to gauge their psyche and how they feel about the game. On top of that, there should be a LOT of clutch time drilling happening. Set the clock to 12 minutes and divide the players into 2 groups, one group needs to score X amount of points and the other group needs to hold them under that. Or better yet, do this with even less time to simulate clutch time.

I’m tired of hearing how we’re not serious or not trying or not motivated or not committed. I think the players are 100% trying, but just aren’t getting the results, and that leads to decreased morale. The players are all too aware that they’re win-lose-win-lose, but the nervousness is clearly visible even in games they win. No we are not gonna fire Joe, as there would be no better coaches at the moment to replace him. No we are not going to do away with Brown because if he leaves we’re going sub .500 real quick.

Let’s have faith in the team. LET’S GO CELTICS!
