Fan arrested and banned for life after running onto court to take selfie with Victor Wembanyama

https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/04/sport/fan-victor-wembanyama-selfie-nba-finals

A fan was arrested and banned for life after running onto the court and taking a selfie video with Victor Wembanyama during Game 1 of the NBA Finals on Wednesday.

The bizarre moment occurred nearly midway through the fourth quarter, with the New York Knicks leading 92-86. The fan ran onto the court as the San Antonio Spurs were on offense, before stopping next to Wembanyama and attempting to record a selfie video with the San Antonio star.

“The individual who entered the court area during Game 1 of The Finals was arrested and will be banned for life from all NBA arenas,” an NBA spokesperson said.

“A second individual will also receive a lifetime ban for his role in the incident.”
