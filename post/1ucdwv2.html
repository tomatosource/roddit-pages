OKC’s guard depth put into perspective

With the Aaron Wiggins trade, I was thinking about how deep OKC had to be at the guard position for this to be a steal for any other team but them.

The 2 starters at PG and SG are obviously SGA and Jalen Williams, 2 all nba players in their own right.

Then they go:

Cason Wallace (who realistically should be starting next year over Dort, so he’s kinda half a rotation piece half a starter)
Jared McCain
Alex Caruso
Isaiah Joe
Ajay Mitchell

So even without Wallace who should be starting next year, they’re still 7 guards deep. Wiggins obviously dealt with an injury, but before that he was putting up very good numbers on good efficiency, and he didn’t really get minutes in the playoffs this year. It just puts Okc’s guard depth into insane perspective.
