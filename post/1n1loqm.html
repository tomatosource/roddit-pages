This year’s 3 point shooting is better than last year’s 3 point shooting

  • Out
    • Holiday: 4.9 3PA at 35%
    • Tatum: 10.1 3PA at 34%
    • Horford: 5.2 3PA at 36%
    • KP: 6.2 3PA at 41% (42 games)
    • ALL FOUR COMBINED: 575 / 1598 for 35.9%
  • In
    • Simons: 8.5 3PA on 36%
    • Pritchard: 7.8 3PA on 41%
    • White: 9.1 3PA on 38%
    • Hauser: 6.7 3PA on 42%

Simons is a better shooter than Holiday, Tatum and Horford. Then you give increased 3PAs to our already elite shooters and we are bound to hit a ton of 3s this year if we can generate good looks.

We might get the chance to run a small ball line up of Pritchard / Simons / White / Brown / Hauser, depending on what our opponents do, and that will be really fun to see if we can pull that off.
