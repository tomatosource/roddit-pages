The Magic have alternated between wins and losses for 14 straight games. The NBA record for longest such streak is 16 games, by the 1977-78 Buffalo Braves

Dating back to December 18th, the Magic had gone L-W-L-W-L-W-L-W-L-W-L-W-L-W. And with a win today, the streak has ended at 14.

https://preview.redd.it/ebbyrmq50ldg1.png?width=565&format=png&auto=webp&s=c51329dccf5e6a756c1a3bd61adcfa92ee8996e1

We looked up what the longest such streaks are in NBA history, and here’s the list!

  • 16 games by the 1977-78 Buffalo Braves (games 8-23)
  • 15 games by the 1966-67 Lakers (games 17-31)
  • 15 games by the 1997-98 Knicks (games 23-37)
  • 15 games by the 2016-17 Pacers (games 57-71)
  • 14 games by the 2025-26 Magic (games 27-40)
  • 14 games by the 2008-09 Bucks (games 30-43)

Notes:

  • This is regular season games only*
  • This is within a single season only* so no overlapping season streaks
