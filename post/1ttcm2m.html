An underdiscussed aspect of the Knicks/Spurs 2026 NBA Finals: This will be the 4th pair of black head coaches in the Finals. Mike Brown is responsible for 2 of those four pairings.

It’s 2017, in case you’re wondering what the 2nd Mike Brown head coaching bout was. Even though Steve Kerr was the head coach of the Warriors, back issues led to Mike Brown taking over in interim, ironically against his former team in the Cavaliers.

For reference, these are the four times it’s happened in NBA history:

1975: Alvin “Al” Attles (Warriors) vs. K.C. Jones (Bullets); Warriors swept 4-0

2017: Mike Brown (Warriors, interim) vs. Tyronn Lue (Cavaliers); Warriors win 4-1

2024: Joe Mazzulla (Celtics) vs. Jason Kidd (Mavericks); Celtics won 4-1

2026: Mitch Johnson (Spurs) vs. Mike Brown (Knickerbockers); series TBD
