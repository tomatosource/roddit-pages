[NBA] Anthony Edwards, Jalen Duren named NBA Players of the Week

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/nba-players-week-anthony-edwards-jalen-duren

Minnesota Timberwolves guard Anthony Edwards and Detroit Pistons Center Jalen Duren have been named NBA Players of the Week for Week 19 of the 2025-26 season.

Anthony Edwards (MIN): 28.7 PPG; 3.3 RPG; 5.0 APG; 3-0 record (3rd career POTW)

Jalen Duren (DET): 25.8 PPG; 13.8 RPG; 1.3 BPG; 3-1 record (1st career POTW)
