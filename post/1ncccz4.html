GGG - get rid of the pre-requisite for Deadly Boss Maps.

A boss map being gated behind an interaction in another map is fairly cool on paper, but not having any indication of what map its in, how far out you can find them, how many you need to find, and then having this feeling of maybe you missed it in some weird corner of the HUGE maps that you didn’t explore but then having to consider going back into an already completed map to make sure you didn’t miss it - yeah, all of that feels really frustrating as a player.

If you’re not going to remove the pre-requisite, at least give some indication as to WHERE you find them, like an icon on the map itself that let’s you know it’s at least somewhere in there?

I’m a POE1 vet with 5k+ hours, and this has to be one of the worst implemented mechanics for newer players I’ve ever seen
