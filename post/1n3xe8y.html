Prediction: 2030s Centers (Wemby Era) will be very unique and diverse

I am very excited in 2030s where Wemby and his peers (within his age range) reach their primes. As, the Centers today are more untraditional. (Not Back to the Basket Type Only Centers)

Wemby - We all know he is a generational talent. He is a multiple DPOY winner candidate. And his offensive arsenal and sjills are insane. Especially at 7’3-7-4” size.

Chet - He is like a potential rival for Wemby. As chet share similar archetype (Very Tall Skinny Lengthy Center). Though, he is slightly smaller, and his offensive skills are below Wemby. He is still a very good player. And he even won a chip already as the third option.

Sengun - One of the most unique Centers in the history of Nba. With his slomo big man moves. But very effective. He is only around 6’10-6’11”. But he is probably one of the most gifted offensive Centers in the league. Remember, he was the best player from #2 Rockets last year. Imagine, if he reach his prime.

Mobley - DPOY winner. One of the best Rim Protectors in the league. The Defensive Anchor of #1 Cavs last season already. And he will only get better.

Alex Sarr - One of the Fastest 7’0 Centers in the league. And he can shoot the lights out from 3. His Fast Face Up Game makes him looks like a Wing/Forward playing Center.

Now, there will still be Back to the Basket Big Men like Zach Edey and Donovan Clingan. And ofc, future draftees too.

But overall, Do you think Wemby will have a tough competition once Jokic, Embiid, AD enters father time?

OR Wemby will dominate his peer centers in same range with his age?
