Combo Builds Are Close. But they need more love.

TL;DR at the bottom.
I want to like combo-heavy builds. In fact, I do — especially in the campaign where enemy scaling, tempo, and skill slot limitations push you into engaging play. But once you hit late game, that feeling fades. The incentives fall off, and spamming one high-efficiency skill just ends up being the better option.

And I’m fine with this. One-button builds should exist. The fact that they can become optimal fits within PoE’s design vision and offers important player agency.

Many combo interactions just don’t feel as satisfying as they should. The impact is muted, the conditions are too severe, or the reward isn’t there. And while I know that giving an inch can sometimes make the community take a mile, I’d argue this is one of those places where you should open things up, especially considering this is EARLY ACCESS.

Let Spear use some Quarterstaff skills.

They’re both polearms — one’s blunt, one’s sharp. They’re already similar archetypes, and most of their non-projectile skills feel thematically compatible. Locking something like Flicker Strike behind Quarterstaff only feels unnecessarily limiting, so do things like Ice strike, Tempest flurry just to mention a few.

For instance, Ice Strike on a spear would open up entirely new creative combos with things like Glacial Lance. Ice Strike already has what spear ele melee lacks: damage and gap closing. It would immediately improve the combo archetype without touching any numbers, why? Because spear is 1h, QS is 2h, modifiers are there and it’ll make sense.

Why Combo Play Falls Off Late Game:

1. Hyper-specific NICHE Conditions: Many skills want to be used in very niche cases (e.g. parry-only, only worth with frenzy charge etc) and don’t let you improvise mid combat.

2. No Clear Payoff: Combos often offer the same result as spamming a good 4–5 link, but with more effort and risk, less satisfaction.

3. Aura/Scaling: Lategame scaling favours aura stacking and efficient one-button damage loops not combo-loops.

4. Mobs: Swarms everywhere, with no “solo target” scenarios to really show off a combo string, aside from bosses which is fine and you can work with that, as long as we have satisfying, short, quick combo-loops for packs!

5. Monsters Attack Too Frequently: There’s no window for a counter-combo if enemies never stop swinging, add in the mini-stuns and there’s no wonder everyone wants to melt the screen with 1 button, ideally ranged.

6. Lack of Sensory Feedback: Even when combo effects do land, there’s no visual/audio candy, the payback isn’t there, you don’t see your combo supports/passives working, they’re just math, numbers what most players do not see, nor feel.

What Would Fix It:

1. Multifaceted Triggers: Combos should not hinge on the most niche scenarios that might, or might not present themselves, skills should either be very strong in a niche case, and decent over-all, or have more than one trigger. Example:

Fangs of Frost retains it’s counter-parry identity but gains another conditional: If target is already frozen, consume the freeze play a ice cracking effect and make the mob explode violently. Now you can build it around both things, only freeze consumption, only parrying, great player agency no?

Cross-Class Skill Access: Let overlapping weapon types (like spear + quarterstaff) share non-projectile skills where it makes sense. Let us experiment, let us show you with the tools that are present what the players want to do.

Skill Movement Buffs: For example, let Primal Strikes function like Quarterstaff skills: a short dash towards the enemy on the first hit. This would naturally support short combos like Lightning Spear -&gt; Primal Strikes, giving both mapping and bossing flow, we need the combos to feel snappy and great, not slow, so the player can react to a possibility, promoting mechanical skill not spam.

Balance by satisfaction, not just hidden numbers: Exiles will gravitate toward what’s fun and expressive and cool not just OP, I know I would.

Monster Modifiers Control: Don’t let high-speed spammy monsters gain speed modifiers. Save that for enemies with big telegraphed attacks.

Better FX/SFX for Combo Triggers: Make supports that consume freeze or trigger secondary effects feel punchy and satisfying when they go off, If my projectile attack is going to fork because I hit someone with a melee attack, give me a loud DING, a green-ish fork icon appear for a split second on top of my character, anything! If my next melee attack has more range cause I hit someone with a ranged attack, make my weapon have an extended glow or anything that fits the themes! If I’m supporting with freeze consumption, make the enemy crack loudly as if threw a rock at a frozen lake, if I’m supporting with extra fire damage against an ignited target, make my projectiles/weapon glow red when hitting them, etc!

if you’re carrying a buckler with parry give us some form of indication via sfx/vfx what cannot be parried, or what can be parried either is fine, and those who like the idea of parrying will do it, to top it all off make the parry mechanic more satisfying even if you don’t follow up with counter-parry.

Bonuse ideas: A playstyle centered around detonating spears (or any other detonatable skill) would work, and most likely be a viable build choice if you gave us a support that makes slam/melee attacks detonate if they touch the detonatable object, should also provide you with information as to why we aren’t using designated detonator skills(static attack speed…), e.g detonator support for elemental sundering would open up a explosive spear+storm lance build possibility = 3 button combo, which both creates a consumption/detonation condition, and consumes the existing ailments only to rewrite them via detonating the spears, satisfying in theory, and satisfying in reality (probably).

To ensure that some combo-playstyles are not at the mercy of how high your damage is (Leap slam kills/stuns enemies before you get to boneshatter) Add in support that reduces your damage, increases stun buildup but you cannot heavy stun with that skill, should make it so that leap slam will always leave enemies in a sweetspot, that you can then boneshatter.

TL;DR: combo play isn’t bad — it’s under-supported. We don’t need huge changes, just a few careful unlocks and some love to the feel of these builds. Because currently, attempting a combo-heavy build just feel limited, niche and unsatisfying in most cases, it’s far easier to build around 1 button with gear, supports, auras, passives, because they’re not as conditional, this is especially true once you reach mapping.
