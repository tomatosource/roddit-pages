Celtics Practice Day 1 (Offseason Fanfic)

Pritchard is leading a high intensity drill.

Pritchard: (blowing whistle) “Alright! Every layup must be textbook! I need perfection.” (demonstrates layup perfectly)

Tatum: (on facetime) “Hold on… my kid just learned to clap. Look at this!” (shows phone to everyone)

Jaylen: (calm Morpheus tone) “These drills aren’t real and Pritchard is just the illusion of the disciple”

Mazulla: (consulting with a real cowboy and trying to lasso a basketball)

Pritchard: (facepalms)

White: (brought his dog to practice) “No rules says he cant play!” (laughs at his own joke and hopes others laughed too)

Hauser: (giving rebounding tips to Hugo) “Getting rebounds is like fighting hay, except hay fights back” (Hugo nods knowingly)

Brad: (now clearly overweight, eating popcorn in a comfortable balcony seat)

Pritchard: (measuring the distance between cones) “If these cones are 37 inches apart we will get too sloppy, if they are 35” apart we will get too tight.” (dead serious)

Tatum: (facing camera and talking) “Basketball is cool, Championships are cool, but have you ever seen your Kid eat a Cheerio for the first time?” (big smile)

Mazulla: (somehow now on top of the basketball stanchion) “This view changes everything.”

Jaylen: (running un-instructed suicides alone) “The real suicide is running without purpose.”

Tatum: (serious, inspecting the bleachers) “Hey we need to do something about these sharp edges.”

White: (facing camera and talking) “Some players try to live up to Kobe or MJ. I’m just trying to impress my dog.” (hopeful)

Mazulla: (after practice to reporters) “Practice is like making spaghetti, too much sauce and it’s a mess. Not enough sauce? Dry noodles.” (every reporter starts writing this down)

Pritchard: (after practice to reporters) “My practice doesn’t end, I even use the games as practice.”

Tatum: (after practice to reporters, holding his son in his arms) “We’re headed home to build a block tower!”
