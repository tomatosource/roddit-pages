Here’s how to make a +12 Wand (and a +11 wand giveaway)

Shoutout to u/Moist_v_Lipwig for theorising this.

As I have basically run out of currency, I thought I’d create a full crafting guide from base to end product for anyone rich (and dumb) enough to try to make this thing a reality.

  1. Buy a magic wand base with 2 sockets and +5 of whatever spell level of your choosing (preferably without mana as its prefix)
  2. It essentially doesn’t matter what you slam on this wand as long as you add the +1 spell alloy and desecrate suffix but heres how i get it to that state:
    1. Use a Greater Essence of Seeking to add critical hit chance for spells and turn the item rare
    2. Socket Astrid’s Creativity
    3. Activate an Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation and use a Celestial Alloy to add +1 spells
    4. Use an Omen of Sinistral Necromancy and an Ancient Jawbone to add a suffix desecrate
    5. Use an Omen of Greater Exaltation and slam a Perfect exalt orb to fill prefixes with something useful
    6. Please don’t minmax this any further than this, most of your attempts will brick anyway
  3. Now for the fun part: We socket a Thrud’s Might and start the process of desecrating all your currency away
  4. You are looking for the modifier “(15—20)% increased Explicit Fire/Cold/Lightning/Chaos Modifier magnitudes” with the same element as your +level to spells. Now this modifier will absolutely despise you and always show up for the wrong element
  5. In the off-chance you do ever get this modifier, divine it to 20% to turn the +5 into a +6, add quality and add a Perfect Jewellers Orb
  6. Vaal Orb time. We are looking for a 3rd socket. So far i’ve had only 5 wands get a third socket out of i don’t know how many attempts:
  7. Double Corruption time. Grab an Architect’s Orb slam it on there and enjoy your new inventory space.
  8. Should you not be blessed with your weapon having vanished out of existence you may now look at your weapon. Did you hit +1 level to your element of choice? If yes then youre only a 50⁄50 away from the +12.
  9. Use a Yaomac’s Orb of Sacrifice and pray it doesn’t hit the +1 to spells, +5 to spells or the Modifier Magnitude.
  10. If all 3 modifiers are still there, socket a Hedgewitch Assandra’s Rune of Wisdom as well as a Legacy of Lifesprig for another +3 to all spells.
  11. You’ve done it. Congrats on the +12 wand

If my math is somewhat correct this should “only” take around 225 attempts starting at the corruption stage, however i sadly do not have the currency to finish it.

One quick side note:

A +12/+11 wand is essentially the same as a +10 wand due to spell level limitations. I don’t want to give anyone false hope that they will craft some insanely sought after item. This craft is purely an achievement and this league may be the last time this is possible as I strongly expect GGG to change how accessible spell levels are in the future.

Good luck to anyone trying this craft.

Also thank you everyone for making this community as great as it is, as a thank you I’ll give away one of my +11 wands (either cold or lightning, your pick) to some random comment in here. &lt;3

EDIT: I’ll choose the giveaway winner Friday at around 18:00 GMT

EDIT2: This will sadly not work for physical wands as the modifier magnitude only goes up to 15%. Does mean its still possible on staffs tho!
