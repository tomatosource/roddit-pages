In POE1 you do 4 maps, and you have a full temple. In POE2, it takes ~60 maps to see Architect, and ~90 more maps to get to Atziri. So ~150 maps for one full temple run.

You get 1 beacon per map. It takes 6 beacons to get one “chance” to put down some rooms and paths for your temple. Lets be optimistic and say that it would take you ~10 temples to path to Architect from scratch. So that’s 60 maps.

Then after you get the key room spawned (and reset part of your temple), you now need to path toward the key room, and then also towards atziri room. Id say on average that’s 10-15 more temples. Lets say 10 to be optimistic again. So that’s 60 more maps (6 beacons per temple, times 10).

So in total you need to run 120 maps (if being optimistic) just for ONE chance to fight Atziri. (But realistically, its probably closer to ~150 maps).

And that’s a CURRENT LEAGUE MECHANIC that is present in every map, and is supposed to be easy to access because its newly added.

Lets compare this to incursion in POE1, where even without any atlas investment (outside of forcing Alva), it takes like 3-4 maps to get a full temple done. Not “once chance to put rooms”, but a full, completed temple.

Even if they were to buff a lot of loot drops in the temple, they desperately need to make it easier/faster to move through the temple.

Perhaps instead of offering 6 rooms to place, it would offer 4 paths/4rooms, and you can only place 6 total (but you can choose which)? Because as it is, doing 120 maps just for one “finished” temple run is a bit insane imo.
