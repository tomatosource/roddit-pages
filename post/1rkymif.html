[Bloomberg] Ex-Morgan Stanley Adviser Guilty of Defrauding NBA Players

Src: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-03/ex-morgan-stanley-adviser-convicted-of-defrauding-nba-players?srnd=homepage-americas

A former Morgan Stanley investment adviser was convicted of defrauding Portland Trail Blazers guard Jrue Holiday and former players Chandler Parsons and Courtney Lee out of millions of dollars.

Darryl Cohen was found guilty by a jury in Manhattan on two counts Tuesday following a monthlong trial. He faces a maximum sentence of 20 years in prison for persuading his clients to buy so-called viatical life insurance polices at massive markups and make other fraudulent payments.

policies were bought from a broker and then sold to Cohen’s clients at three or four times the purchase price. For instance, one policy bought for around \(465,000 was marked up to \)1.6 million. Another, purchased for \(620,000, was sold for more than \)2.5 million.

Cohen was also charged with transferring without authorization \(500,000 from two of the players’ accounts to a nonprofit amateur basketball organization started by an associate of his, and \)300,000 from Parsons’ accounts to pay off a former professional baseball player who was a disgruntled client.

According to the defense, at least some of the transfers Cohen made were on behalf of Parsons, who they claim was trying to hide his involvement with a sports agency he set up in violation of NBA rules.

Parsons knew when he established the agency that the punishment could be “severe,” Michael Bloch, an attorney for Cohen, told the jury during his closing argument. “Chandler did it anyway and by necessity had to lie to a lot of people.”
