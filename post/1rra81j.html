So glad I came back and Loving it

I had been away from POE for a while. I would return every so often over the years and I had POE2 purchased but didn’t really spend much time on it.

Then Diablo2 enhanced released so I bought that for nostalgia and played for an hour which reminded me why POE was so amazing…so I spent more time in POE2. At first it was a little slow…I thought.

And as I have leveled and used the in-game trading system and navigated some bosses and now almost 40, damn it’s so good and there is so much great stuff in this. I have a ton to learn but the creativity and I am seeing and the enjoyment I am having is just awesome. Best money I have spent in a long time. Looking forward to a later game spec and realizing how bad I screwed it up but so far I feel pretty good just fumbling around.

Another 300-400 hours to come.

I’m sure someone is dissatisfied with something but I’m constantly saying to myself “oh I see, ok that’s awesome!”
