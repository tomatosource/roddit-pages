Spencer Jones posts on LinkedIn about re-signing with the Denver Nuggets after signing an offer sheet with the OKC Thunder: “The offer sheet was a business decision. It ensured I ended up in the right situation w/ what I felt was the right compensation regardless of what happened next.”

https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7487522204054646784/

It’s official. I’m staying a Denver Nugget.

Plot twist. 

Yes, I signed an offer sheet with Oklahoma City and yes, Denver matched it.

I know some people are confused, and some fans are even upset. How could I sign with a rival?

I get it. But I also need people to understand something.

This is a business. Front offices treat it that way every day and players have to do the same.

I went into free agency with clear priorities: a team that fits my skillset, a championship culture, and an organization I believe in. Both Denver and OKC checked those boxes. Either way, I was landing somewhere that made sense.

The offer sheet was a business decision. It ensured I ended up in the right situation w/ what I felt was the right compensation regardless of what happened next.

And along the way, I made a little history.

The largest fully guaranteed offer sheet ever signed by an undrafted player converted from a two-way contract.

From a two-way with no safety net to this. I don’t take that lightly.

Now I get to keep building with the team that bet on me from the start.

Back to work!
