Let’s take a look at 1H Maces.

I see a lot of people mentioning how after the Giant’s Blood nerfs 1H Maces will be very popular, but I disagree and even go so far a to say GB was the least of 1H Mace problems.

TLDR: 1H Maces trade damage for better Attack Speed, however in general Mace skills don’t benefit from it because of fixed attack timers, payoff mechanics or Valour lock-out. No amount of nerfs to GB will make people switch from 2H to 1H Maces, because it’s a skill design problem.

I want to clarify right away: Attack Speed isn’t completely useless on Warrior, if anything it makes skills smoother and just feels good to play with some. However Mace skills are generally designed in a way where they gain almost no benefit from it.

Right out of the way: Warcries. They’re very important buffs for your melee skills, however they’re designed to buff a limited number of attacks. That pushes you to focus on a few very strong hits instead of rapid strikes.

Let’s look at, probably, most popular skills for a generic Warrior:

  • Boneshatter is a stun payoff skill. It can’t generate stun buildup by itself, but deals a lot of damage in a good AoE on enemies primed for stun. Since enemies grow resistant to stun for some time, you want to scale damage and AoE instead.
  • Leap Slam is a travel stun skill. Since it has a total attack time, Attack Speed has almost no effect on it. Amount of stun buildup caused depends on damage you deal, so again you want to scale damage, AoE and stun buildup.
  • HotG is a Valour skill. You can’t use it unless you have enough Valour, which means Attack Speed is pointless. Moreover it’s a huge singular hit, so it synergizes well with Bleed. You want to scale damage and Bleed.

Maybe those specific skills are just designed for a “big boom” and synergize far better with 2H Mace, so let’s look for other ones:

  • Earthquake. Initial hit is weak, but creates Jagged Ground. 4 seconds later it explodes, dealing good damage in a decent radius. Nuance is that you can’t place Jagged Ground atop of another one, so your DPS is limited by reductions to skill duration, not your Attack Speed.
  • Sunder was a payoff skill with added attack time, but according to patchnotes GGG removed both payoff and fixed time. Maybe it will be able to scale with Attack Speed? We will need to see, the base skill speed is only 35%.
  • Rolling Slam is a combo skill with added attack time. Previously it was 1 second, patchnotes changed it to 1.5 seconds and removed debuff to base Attack Speed. In other words this skill doesn’t care about Attack Speed at all now.
  • Supercharged Slam is a charge skill and it’s technically affected by Attack Speed, as in the animation of the hit itself is being performed faster. But it doesn’t affect charge time at all and that’s the only improtant metric of the skill. It counts only the amount of time you’ve spent charging up and cannot be sped up.
  • Earthshatter is one of the few that potentially can scale with Attack Speed. Might be worth dropping Upheaval support even. But you still need to blowup the spikes and now all Warcries have a cooldown. Spikes now interact with Detonate, so maybe it will solve this.
  • Stampede has a cooldown. That’s pretty much it.

That’s not all skills, but you see the trend: Mace skills in general don’t benefit from higher Attack Speed and care far more for bigger per hit damage, which gives 2H Mace a clear advantage over 1H. Mana costs also don’t care how much damage you deal, it only counts how many times you used a skill.

Of course 2H should be a better offensive option, but currently gap is way too big and GGG either needs to do something about 1H Maces specifcially to make them more competitive or rebalance Mace skills to make them more flexible.
