[NBA Communications] The All-Tournament Team for the Emirates NBA Cup: Jalen Brunson, Luka Dončić, De’Aaron Fox, Shai Gilgeous Alexander, Karl-Anthony Towns

The NBA announced today the All-Tournament Team for the Emirates NBA Cup 2025:

Jalen Brunson, New York Knicks

Luka Dončić, Los Angeles Lakers

De’Aaron Fox, San Antonio Spurs

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Oklahoma City Thunder

Karl-Anthony Towns, New York Knicks

The All-Tournament Team was selected by a media panel of 20 voters. Selections were based on player performance in both Group Play and the Knockout Rounds, with players chosen regardless of position.
