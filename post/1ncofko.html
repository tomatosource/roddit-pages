DOTs feel way too overtuned right now

I wanted to bring up my current experience with DOT effects in PoE2. Right now, outside of a life flask or the occasional charm proc, there aren’t really meaningful options to deal with them. Life regen investment feels almost worthless compared to the damage-over-time values we’re seeing.

Ground-based DOTs make the problem even more punishing. They force you into constant movement while still chunking you down at a rate that regen or mitigation simply can’t keep up with. Even with good positioning, you’re still dying to the amount of damage and spam these effects can produce.

The result is that DOTs don’t feel dangerous in a “fun” way, they just feel oppressive as there’s little to no counterplay other than flasking or relying on a specific charm to proc.

EDIT:
Yeah, I get that there are technically items, passives and modifiers out there, but most of them are scattered, inconsistent, and in practice don’t scale anywhere near enough to the endgame.

And on top of that, the payoff just isn’t worth it. You’re almost always better off investing into anything else for more reliable defense or offense.

Also people don’t seem to understand that DOTs are not only ignite, bleed or poison, it’s also the ground effects that monsters produce, that’s the real problem.
