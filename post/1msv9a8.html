Who is the LEAST talented/gifted best NBA player we have ever seen?

I absolutely love the discussions here on who the most gifted, talented, most explosive etc. player is. But what if we turn the question around. Who is the least talented/gifted best NBA player we have ever seen?

Of course talent, is a bit complex. But by talent I mean raw physical gifts (speed, agility, vertical, coordination) or natural skill (touch, handle, shooting ability that seems innate).

So what does it mean that a player is the least talented, it means they weren’t “natural freaks,” but became great through obsessive work ethic, intelligence, and maximizing what they had.
