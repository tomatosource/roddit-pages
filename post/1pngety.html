The Lakers are 9-0 in “clutch games” despite numerous injuries. Last season’s best clutch team was the Celtics who were 24-11 in those games.

Despite missing LeBron for most of this season, missing Luka for a handful of games, missing Reaves for a handful of games, and missing Smart for half the season, the Lakers are on track to be the greatest team in “clutch games” in recent NBA history.

Only 5 of 30 NBA teams (including the Lakers) have less than 5 losses in clutch games. The Lakers have 0. A truly remarkable stat that underscores the historic pace the Lakers are currently on.

And if past is prologue, the regression opposing fanbases are waiting on may not be as grand as they hope.

The 2024 Mavs led by Luka were 2nd in clutch games winning %, going 23-9 in the regular season (and then continuing their dominance that postseason). The only team better in clutch games that season? The 2024 Lakers, who went 24-9 in clutch games, led by Luka’s teammates LeBron and Reaves.

Impressive stuff from a core still learning to play with each other - and which has had limited time this season fully healthy anyway.
