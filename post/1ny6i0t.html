Had a bit of a ride with this one.
Saw a couple of YouTube videos (from Belton and Master T) about crafting 2 other versions of a spirit & +3 spell skills solar amulet, but wanted rarity + other mods, plus skipped fracturing sort-of accidentally.

Crafting process:

  1. Buy a fractured 50 spirit solar amulet (+4 random modifiers) thinking it’s better than betting on fracturing, but forgot the items had to be magic to add rarity with an essence lol (cost 12 divs instead of 3 divs)

Suffixes:
2a. Adjust the plan and annul x3 to keep the spirit + 1 other modifier
2b. Annul again since I wasn’t sure if chaos sticks to suffix/prefix shuffling based on the replaced modifier in poe2, but don’t do this, it’s random, just wasted an annul
2c. Dextral Exaltation omen + exalt

  1. Chaos spam to hit T1/T2 rarity (~2⁄177 chance), hit T2 in 36 and be happy, realize it’s a prefix and be sad
    3a. Consider continuing like this and go for a crit amulet after all, but if I want to keep the rarity later there’s a 33% chance to remove the +3 all spell skills with the essence, after which it’s very unlikely to exalt that again, and 33% chance to remove the crit bonus, after which it’s extremely unlikely to exalt/desecrate it again, plus there’d have to be at least one omen of light used
  2. Say F it and continue chaos, hit T1 rarity suffix in 2 chaos slams lol
  3. Omen of the Blackblooded (Kurgal) + Sinistral Necromancy omen + Preserved Collarbone - need to get the “invoked spells” modifier, the only one with only a caster tag - Kurgal only has 3 prefixes so I think it’s 100% chance to get this
  4. Homogenizing Exaltation omen + Dextral Exaltation omen + Greater Exaltation omen + perfect exalt for T1 +3 spell skills & T1 cast speed

Prefixes:

  1. Perfect Essence of Enhancement + Sinistral Crystallization omen which removes the desecration (the only non-fractured prefix) + adds global defences - got a perfect 30%, but the happiness was short-lived
  2. Aim for T3-T1 %ES or T2-T1 Spell Damage - too likely to hit flat ES / % armour / % evasion with a simple homogenized perfect exalt (2⁄3 chance), so replace the original quality with caster catalyst (“Sibilant”) and use Catalyzing Exaltation omen + Homogenizing Exaltation omen + perfect exalt (side note - if anyone knows how much the catalyzing omen changes weights, feel free to mention) - hit evasion anyway…
  3. Consider using a prefix annulling omen - back to step 7 if it’d remove the global defences, or step 8 if the evasion would be removed. Try to prefix desecrate because omen of light is cheaper than the prefix annulling omen (I’d have to use omen of light annul if the global defences was hit, then back to step 7), hit the defences…
  4. Reveal the desecration because what the heck, get lucky and get T2 spell damage, saving at least 4 divs
  5. Use Perfect Essence of Enhancement + Sinistral Crystallization omen again, 50⁄50 to hit the evasion mod, and it hit it luckily

The initial item mix-up might have saved me multiple fracturing attempts (5-6 div each), or maybe not, but I got essentially what I wanted, of course needs some divining.
Also might be worth redoing the prefixed to get the %ES/flat ES which seem to be high-priced.
