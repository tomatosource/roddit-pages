Josh Hart this morning with a tribute to Isaiah Hartenstein:

“You don’t really know the true ability or unselfishness of a player until you are teammates. In LA with the Clippers, he was solid. But he got to New York and played a style and brand of basketball that Thibs loves. And then his personality – he was a great locker room guy for us. Unselfish. Didn’t care for the spotlight. Did all the dirty work things. And won us a playoff game. I think he had 10 offensive rebounds. And didn’t care about scoring. He was like, ‘I’m going to dominate the glass.’ So when he’s your teammate, you love him. Now that he’s not your teammate, he can go smoke one.”
