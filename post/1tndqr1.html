The biggest mistake GGG make with Crafting is the lack of an overarching system in the game to assist players with all the different permutations of Crafting.

It doesn’t matter whether Recombination exists, or not.

It doesn’t matter whether crafting method ‘x’ is nerfed and now ‘y’ is stronger.

It doesn’t matter that we’re now limited to 1 “Crafted” modifier and 1 “Desecrated” modifier.

The problem is that there’s no where in the game, that a player can go to, where they can have the different permutations of each different crafting currency explained to them.

Each League mechanic having it’s own purpose is great, and needed. But there needs to be a system in the game where these different crafting currencies can come together and a player can understand their outcomes.

When the Devs say the PoE 1 “Crafting Bench” was complex for new players, what I imagine this complexity was/is, would be the fact that there’s all these different currencies required to imprint the modifiers. You open it up and there’s dozens of different modifiers and loads of different currency requirements.

If there was a “Crafting Bench”, which was empty by default. Which had space for just one thing, a piece of currency, this would be the perfect way to demonstrate how they work for players right? Breaking down the complexity.

You insert the currency click the “Show example outcome” button, and an item would roll with that piece of currency, demonstrating what it was doing, which you could click over and over again until a player fully understands.

The item on the left would show the item before using the currency, and the item on the right would clearly highlight the change on the item as a result of using the currency.

This would work for every type of currency in the game, from your basic exalt, to the more complex meta crafting runes.

There needs to be an overarching system where players can go to in their hideout in Endgame, or in Towns during the Campaign, where they can learn about Crafting in their own time and at their own pace.

&gt; I find the new “Meta Crafting” rune for Chronomancy in my Map

&gt; After completing my Map, I take it to my hideout and place it in the “Crafting Bench”

&gt; The different modifiers are shown to me for this one specific currency.

“Modifier 1 (Range) - T1-T5”

“Modifier 2 (Range) - T1-T3”

etc

&gt; I can then click the “Show crafting outcome” button and it performs the Craft, showing what the item looked like before the Craft and then after the Craft, highlighting the Modifier which was added, and the one which was taken away if so…..This can be done over and over again so the player fully understands what is going in.

Crafting will never be “Approachable” unless players have a place in game which is an all encompassing system, which exists for the sole purpose of explaining what each currency can do, visually.

Jonathan made a really intriguing point about Exceptional bases and Fracturing Orbs being on the Atlas tree now, and he said “Players can now be aware that they exist, by clicking on them and reading the tooltip”…..Well, why isn’t this done for crafting also?

Why would I want to apply a random chronomancy mod, if I don’t know what they are?
