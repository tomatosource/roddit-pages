Death XP penalty

Yes, the REAL answer is that we don’t NEED need an XP penalty at all, but it seems this is some ‘friction’ that isn’t going to change.

Yes, we have the Omen, but:

  • Why create a penalty, then add a method the reduce that penalty, pretty much forcing evert 90+ player to have one active at all times
  • It sometimes doesn’t work, and YES, it was on, it’s the first thing I check
  • If you have just hit a new level, for a short while, there is no real XP penalty, which seems a clunky design.
  • XP loss at high levels is a massive amount of lost time

So, IF we have to have an XP penalty, instead of knocking xx% of a player’s total, why not reduce future XP gain?

For a certain amount of XP, you are at 50% XP gain. Future deaths reduce this to 25% and so on.

So, we satisfy the requirements to prevent players getting to 100 without regard to dying again and again, but doesn’t take away hours and hours of hard work.
