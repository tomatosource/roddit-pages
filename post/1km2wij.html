Alternate campaign path like last epoch

In Last Epoch, it’s great that on your second playthrough of the season, you have the option to skip a lot of the campaign. That’s a huge quality-of-life improvement. But it would be even better if we could skip specific acts for alt characters especially given how tedious the early game can be.

Right now, having to go through Acts 1–3 and then repeat Acts 1–3 again on Cruel difficulty makes rolling a new character feel like a chore. For players like me who don’t have a lot of time each day, this process can take days or even weeks. And it’s not just me casual gamers make up 41% of the player base in most games on pc and console, so it affects a majority of players.

Adding more campaign skip options would help in two big ways:

  1. It would encourage more people to keep playing, which is good for overall player retention.
  2. It would boost early-game trading by giving value to low-tier rares that would otherwise be ignored later in the season.
