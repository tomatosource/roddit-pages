The Knicks were 6-2 in playoff games they trailed by double-digits. And OG Anunoby had an effective field goal percentage of 67.2%, the highest mark for a player with at least 150 field goal attempts in NBA playoff history.

Knicks’ opponents shot just 123-for-355 (34.6%) on wide-open 3-pointers, the fifth lowest playoff mark among the 51 teams whose opponents attempted at least 200 wide-open 3s in the 13 seasons of tracking data.

The Knicks opponents also shot just 4-for-19 (21%) on clutch three pointers and just 10-for-21 (48%) on clutch free throws as they went 6-3 in games that were within five points in the last five minutes.
