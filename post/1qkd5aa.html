50-70d profit from an hour of crafting breach tablets

Hi Everyone,

Newish to PoE2 and a lot of people helped me so I thought I might offer something back. This is definitely not new information, but I thought it might help someone that isn’t aware of the method.

I’ve been crafting breach tablets the last few days and it’s a very simple way to make 40-50 div from an hour of crafting, which for those like me that aren’t quite temple savvy of geared out I think is quite good.

The method is ultra simple:

  1. Buy 20 breach tablets (10 uses) and move them to a stash tab

  2. Buy 20-40 div of chaos orbs and move them to your inventory

  3. Set up a regex, or just type in stash search “Clasped “Additional Breach” - this will show any tablet that has the clasped hand mod AND either the chance to spawn additional breach or chance to spawn three additional breaches. These are all you are looking for.

  4. How you use the chaos orbs is up to you, I found that by doing 2 rows of 3 I was able to spam a lot of chaos orbs without worrying about doubling up on ones that hit (do one row by doing down 3 tablets, then the next row by going up, this gives the animation time to finish and the regex outline to become clear).

  5. Sell anything that hits, even the 1 clasped go for 2-3 div.

The tablets will sell, it might take 12 hours and maybe some price reductions, but they will eventually sell. I’d say good hauls netted me about 90-100 div from an hour, bad ones maybe 40-50.

I hope this ultimately lowers the cost of the tablets because I enjoy running the tablets more than crafting, but I’ve made 200-300 div from a few hours of crafting which has helped my build significantly.
