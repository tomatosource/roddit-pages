What franchises will retire the old guards’ jerseys?

Saw on another post talking about the remaining old guards in. the league. Some of them are forwards and big men though ;) ;)

Now the question is who will retire their respective jerseys, and if possible even a statue?

The oldies:

  • LeBron James
  • Kevin Durant
  • Steph Curry
  • James Harden

  • Paul George
  • Jimmy Butler

  • Kawhi Leonard
  • Draymond Green
  • Damian Lillard
  • Klay Thompson
  • Kevin Love
  • Al Horford
  • Mike Conley
  • Chris Paul
  • Russell Westbrook

Explanations:

  • LeBron James - pretty much self-explanatory. He won a title with each franchises. Shortest stint in Miami but that’s where he won multiple. No more explanation for the Cavs, y’all know that’s a statue-worthy stint. With the Lakers, he won one and then later on that’s the team he was with when he was breaking all-time records, most notably the all-time points record. Wade got a statue in Miami, LeBron deserves one, 2 MVPs 2 titles 2 Finals MVPs, though because of only a short stint, it’s just a maybe. I think what the Heat can do is – you know how Kobe has 3 statues in LA, one as number 8, one as number 24 and one as girl dad to commemorate Gigi as well – Wade has one separate statue, while they would do another statue featuring the Big 3 standing side by side.

  • Kevin Durant - did most of his work in OKC, won MVP there. Did a lot of winning in GSW, back-to-back Finals MVP.

  • Stephen Curry - you already know. The Warriors went from a low-tier franchise to a global brand.

  • James Harden - showed flashes in OKC already but did most of his work in Houston, won MVP, got into WCF multiple times. Sent a lot of Houston girls into college.

  • DeMar DeRozan - I don’t think he’s done enough, he’s a B-tier star. A-tier stars are First Team All-NBA level, S-class are MVPs. Or he’s been there enough like Reggie Miller was with the Pacers. But the Raptors might give him one for his service and for being the pawn to unlock their championship potential as he was the main piece in the Kawhi trade. Also, Reggie Miller was most famous for his choke gesture on Spike Lee, DeMar and Lowry were famous for their chokejobs in Toronto.

  • Paul George - it’s a maybe with the Pacers. Went to two ECFs, but uhh just 7 years with the franchise and no ring nor finals appearance, idk.

  • Jimmy Butler - he’s in the same tier as DeMar and PG but his run with the Heat pushed him further. Clutch, memorable playoff moments, carrying them to two unlikely finals appearances. That’s jersey-worthy imo.

  • Kyrie Irving - dude went nuts in Game 5 and hit a crazy shot in Game 7. He’s also a B-tier star imo but those playoff moments elevate him. Cavs will still be a loser franchise if not for Kyrie’s step-up games.

  • Kawhi Leonard - just spent one year in Toronto. But he did what he was supposed to do. Hard to deny when he won Finals MVP as the main carry. Thankfully he will return to Toronto to pad up his stats there a little, cement his legacy. A one-year stint makes it debatable but even a three-year stint with a ring, that’s solid jersey-worthy case.

  • Draymond Green - one of the pillars of the GSW dynasty

  • Damian Lillard - just one WCF, also a B-tier star, had some memorable playoff moments in the first round. But stayed long enough with the franchise to be all-time leader in points, among other stats.

  • Klay Thompson - one of the pillars of the GSW dynasty

  • Kevin Love - part of the core of the 2016 team. Stayed when Kyrie and LeBron left. I think if he hadn’t stayed this will be debatable, but since he did, he probably gets it. Did his best individual work in Minnesota but didn’t win a single playoff game there so the Cavs is his best chance

  • Al Horford - B-tier star in Atlanta, got deep in the playoffs once, got swept, idk if that’s enough. Boston has a history of giving jersey honors to role players, but those are role players were part of the dynasty, idk if Al winning just one gets his jersey retired.

  • Mike Conley - didn’t win the big one but got to WCF once and got swept. Though he stayed long enough. Core of Grit n Grind era along with ZBo and Marc. All three will probably get their jerseys retired in Memphis. The culture shift from low-tier to contenders, and they did it long enough, I think that warrants some recognition.

  • Chris Paul - technically never played for the Pelicans but he gets his jersey retired there as a New Orleans Hornet. Turned a bottom-tier franchise into a contender for a few years, MVP runner up while with the franchise. I think he gets recognized.

  • Russell Westbrook - stayed when KD left. MVP, crazy triple double seasons, all in OKC. I know people say it’s overrated but that’s still a historic run. No one’s ever done it since Oscar then Russ did it three straight years. That clutch shot against Denver to cap off his MVP season. I don’t think he necessarily deserves a statue, but they probably build him one – along with SGA – just to spite KD.
