“Unique Crafting”

I haven’t seen it discussed much, but I really like where GGG is going with uniques. Not just how some of these new uniques are really cool and worth “building around”. This post is specifically around how they are leaning into the origins/family of uniques and how the various factions in Wreaclast are able to change/enhance them.

Lost in all the discussions about 0.4 were all the interesting things that you could do with Vaal uniques after corrupting or double corrupting vaal uniques. This patch looks like Runic ward and Kalguuran uniques are going to see some really crazy interactions and they are made very different than vaal corruption.

This makes me excited to see what they eventually do with things like Breach or even Karui ancestral type of uniques.

Also, from a meta/systems perspective it allows them to give us interesting uniques that can become godly or even terrible uniques that can become useful in niche situations. It opens a ton of doors having sort of a 2 or 3 hurdle system in place and also adds a ton of flavor from a lore perspective.
