My takeaways from the cavs game

Celtics opened the game strong and obviously defending home court has become an issue. In a game of runs, we opened the game with an avalanche. 5 minutes of pure domination, reminiscent of what we saw against dallas in the finals. Please don’t fall for the narrative this was a blown lead (ik, maybe I’m coping). A run this early in a game is hypothetically the easiest to come back from. The other 43 minutes was great basketball and Mitchell torching us because we refused to put any of our great defenders on him. This will likely change in the playoffs and in a 7 game series. We have another tough game against Denver and LA looming. Holiday’s mallet finer might be an issue for us as well. We need to play good basketball down the stretch and prioritize staying healthy for playoffs. We’re losing tough games but also getting ready for the playoffs at the same time. Time to see what this team is truly made of this postseason.
