The Spurs join the 40-20 club in Victor Wembanyama’s third season

With today’s win over the Sacramento Kings, the San Antonio Spurs improve to 40–16, officially joining the 40–20 club alongside OKC and the Detroit Pistons.

The 40–20 rule by Phil Jackson says that true championship contenders reach 40 wins before suffering 20 losses. Since 1980, only four teams have gone on to win the title without meeting this benchmark. A crazy 90% success rate.

For a team so young, the Spurs appear way ahead of schedule 👀
