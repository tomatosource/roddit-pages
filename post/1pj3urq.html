What other ways could there be to minimise fomo for rare mobs?

Would like to start by acknowledging that the rare mob map icons ‘solved’ this in one way, but I believe it was partially reversed because the goal is to get us more immersed in the environment rather than worsen the mini map staring most of us already do 😅

For us to not stare at the mini map, the clues for going in the right direction generally need to be in the environment itself

Some examples:

PoE1 players would know to follow the main path and look out for the old sign post down the worn path when looking for Alira.

PoE2 players would know to follow the stone path in Vaal City to get to Viper boss.

When the goal is singular like a boss or quest giver, it is easier to put clues into the environment.

But the problem comes when the goal is the somewhat randomly placed Rares in the entire map. I’m happy enough with the upcoming compromise, but wondered if someone out there might have an elegant solution. It’s an interesting question to tackle.

What if we just had a rare mob count like we do monsters remaining? If you knew you had 12⁄26, 8⁄26, 5⁄26, or 3⁄26 rare monsters remaining but not necessarily know where they are, would you feel bad moving on to the next map?
