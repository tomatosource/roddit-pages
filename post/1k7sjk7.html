Parry seems meh cause its not effective vs AOE and its slow compared to other options. Spells just go through is my understanding, (havent played woth it much myself but read all the negativity).

Aka, you hold shield and release the exact moment you are hit. It fails with bad timing. Failing means it goes on cooldown. Succeeding resets cooldown. You can stack the damage bonus from parrys in succession. Aka parry 5x in a row gets damage doubled 5x, each compounding and multiplicative. Missing at any point removes the saved stacks.
