Which Weapon/Class Combo Are You MOST Excited For?

While it’s not confirmed for 0.5, I do reckon a new weapon + class combo is nearly guaranteed. They’ve been cooking the endgame overhaul for multiple patches now, the Druid was also in the oven for awhile, and we know they said it was the TOUGHEST weapon/class to design yet. Combined with the nearly 6-month window between 0.4 and 0.5 and GGG saying this would be a “pretty huge” update, I reckon we’ll be getting the endgame overhaul, a new league, plenty of balance, and…a new weapon + class combo.

So what remains?

  • Duelist + Swords (2H + 1H), supposed to be focused on elemental damage types.
  • Marauder + Axes (2H + 1H), supposed to be focused on throwing axex and bleeding.
  • Templar + Flails (?????, holy/light)
  • Shadow + Daggers + Traps, daggers will be poison/chaos, traps a variety of things.

Which are you most hyped for? From info on POE2DB and dev interviews, we know a tad about each, but EVERYTHING is subject to change. How do you think each weapon will shape out?

Honestly, I am most excited for Axes, but I reckon Swords are next given statements from the devs across multiple interviews and patch cycles. It sounds like Axes might be a little bit similar to Spears, with thrown and melee variants to skills…I remain pretty excited to see how they’ll be designing ALL this stuff though, as they’re basically all melee weapons. Perhaps this’ll ALSO be mixed for a bunch of encounter balance around melee characters (hopium), such as a change-up or complete rewrite of how some rare monster modifiers function.
