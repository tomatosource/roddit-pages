Missing Luke Kornet

The Cs are missing Luke Kornet more than anything else right now. Without queta they end up going small and sometimes it does not work out for them. No big bench depth is a killer and Luke gave everything you needed from him. Rebounds, okay D, big a solid presence on the floor.

If the Celtics were able to keep Kornet last year I think they would have a legit shot at going to the finals with this roster the way it’s been playing lately.
