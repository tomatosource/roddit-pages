Under Masai Ujiri, the Toronto Raptors had the 5th highest win percentage from 2013-2025. This period includes the franchise winning their first ever championship, and the first major championship Toronto won since the Blue Jays in 1993.

That figure is taking into account some of the more recent seasons where the Raptors haven’t been nearly as successful/were actively tanking like this most recent season. It goes to show the contrast with how the Raptors were run before and after Masai - as well as the major concern for the franchise’s direction going forward.

The championship year in particular cannot be overstated how much it means for Toronto and Canada as a whole. Toronto has not had the best of luck as a sport city ever since the Jays repeat, to put it mildly, and it meant the world to be on top of the world in 2019.

Thanks for everything, Masai Ujiri.
