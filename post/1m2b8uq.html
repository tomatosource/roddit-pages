NBA megastar takes part in the European Championship

Serbia can count on Nikola Jokic for the European Basketball Championship. According to SPORT1, the NBA superstar has confirmed his participation with Svetislav Pesic.July 17, 2025 • 2:30 p.m.

It was a long-awaited decision, but now the decision has been made. Three-time NBA MVP Nikola Jokic will play for Serbia at the upcoming European Basketball Championship.

Jokic, who is currently in China for promotional activities, has confirmed his participation in the tournament to national coach Svetislav Pesic, according to exclusive SPORT1 information.

He is the second absolute megastar, after Luka Doncic, to represent his country at the European Championships. The official squad announcement is still pending. However, it is already certain that Coach Pesic will be able to rely on his Olympic squad for the European Championships. All fit players have confirmed their participation in the upcoming tournament.

The Serbs will likely be among the German team’s main rivals again at the European Championships. At last year’s Olympic Games, Germany lost to this squad in the bronze medal match.

German fans can look forward to Jokic

In addition to Jokic, another Serbian NBA star, Bogdan Bogdanovic, will also be competing for his country. The Serbs will meet at the end of July for their European Championship preparations—also featuring superstar Jokic.

The Denver Nuggets star’s commitment is also good news for German basketball fans. Serbia will compete with Jokic in the Supercup in Munich as part of their European Championship preparations.

At the traditional preparatory tournament of the German Basketball Association on August 15 and 16, for the first time in the SAP Garden, Germany, Serbia, Turkey and the Czech Republic will compete.

Just a few days later, on August 27, the European Championship begins. The group matches will take place in Latvia, Finland, Cyprus, and Poland. The final round will take place in Riga, Latvia.
