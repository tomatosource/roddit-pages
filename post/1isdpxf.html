At this point, I’m more likely to win a Reddit competition for gear upgrades, than to have any of it drop or get crafted.

Every single piece of gear I have, I bought from the trade site.

Whilst I get the carrot of the stick concept of loot drops and the rarer the item, the bigger the dopamine hit is if it drops. However, this is early access. Nothing is going to be carried forward. What’s the harm in massively increasing crafting materials just for early access?

The larger the sample size of players playing overpowered builds, the better the data for balancing the full game. This is the time when things should be tested.

If I enter a Reddit giveaway for something like an Astramentis, I have a much higher chance of winning that Reddit giveaway than I do of actually being able to get one to drop (or affording one).

Update: many people saying increasing the crafting materials would mess up the economy and stuff. How about a one-off, free pack of non-tradable crafting mats?
