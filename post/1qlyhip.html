Worst Playoff Bullpen Move Of All Time?

Alright, another Q&A about bullpen moves. So, every manager goes to a certain bullpen arm because they trust that he’ll save the day and get some clutch outs. But time and time again, this decision they make absolutely backfires, resulting in a meltdown, and most times, an embarrassing loss. So, what do you believe is the absolute worst bullpen decision in postseason history, and why?

For me, it’s insanely difficult to choose a top pick. But one of the most nonsensical I’ve ever seen is Dave Martinez going with Patrick Corbin in Game 3 of the 2019 NLDS. It was a one-run game, and Anibal Sanchez was doing fine through 5. So, does he stick with Sanchez and likely get out of the inning without trouble? Nope. He goes to another starter, in Patrick Corbin. Granted, Corbin was solid during the Regular Season, but he didn’t appear once out of the bullpen for them. So, Corbin comes in, blows the lead, give up 4 runs on 2 doubles, and with 2 of his runners on, his replacement in Suero gives up a dagger 3-run bomb to Justin Turner, giving him 6 earned runs in 2 thirds of an inning pitched. It’s almost surreal to think that, not only did they not drop the series to LA, but went on to win their first World Series in franchise history after that. Especially considering Corbin pulled off a total 180 out of the pen in Game 7, going 3 scoreless and warning the win. Still, this was probably their lowest point since starting 19-31.
