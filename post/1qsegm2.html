The Fellowship of the Ring as Baseball Players

On a recent episode of the Effectively Wild podcast, they were asked which baseball positions the 9 members of the Fellowship would play. (The hosts were separating pitcher and DH, so technically a 10th person would have to be on the team - they wondered if Elrond or Eowyn or Treebeard could be a a good pitcher). But I’m sticking to the 9 Fellowship members. My list and some reasoning below.

Catcher - Gimli

Pitcher/DH - Legolas

1st Base - Gandalf

2nd Base - Pippin

Short Stop - Merry

3rd Base - Sam

Right Field - Boromir

Center Field - Aragorn

Left Field - Frodo

Gimli is the most obvious for catcher. Catcher is where you stick the short and squatty guys (Yogi Berra, Roy Campanella, Ivan Rodriguez) .

Meg and Ben put Legolas in Center, taking advantage of his range and athleticism. I would actually use him as a two-way player. Losing his defense would hurt; but I bet Legolas would have Maddux-esque control on the mound and be a great hitter. Plus he and Gimli would have a great pitcher/catcher chemistry.

Gandalf could have been a decent third-baseman or corner outfielder in his younger years; but at 2000 years old, his range ain’t what it used to be. So he gets stuck at first. Still a good hitter though.

There’s no getting around it - the Hobbits are going to be bad defensively. They lack athleticism, throwing ability, range, etc… Frodo needs to be hidden in left field. Merry and Pippen as your up the middle defense isn’t great, but maybe their chemistry makes up for some of their lack of ability. Sam lacks the foundation to be a good third baseman, but of all the Hobbits, he seems like he would put in a the most effort to get better - like Wade Boggs putting in the work and winning a few Gold Gloves later in his career.

Aragorn would be a great all-around player - Joe Dimaggio might be a good comp.

Boromir lacks the range to be a centerfielder, but probably has a cannon for an arm so we’ll put him in rightfield.
