What I wish the third ascendancy to be like

Yea, many here probably won’t like these ideas tooooo much because the purposefully go against glass cannon builds, but, well, that’s the whole point. :)

  • We have the trial of Sekhema which from my experience vastly benefits evasion ranged builds.
  • We have the chaos trial which is somewhat more balanced, occasionally, but as with so much of POE2’s content insta-kill builds make it all much simpler.
  • Then we have the yet unknown third ascendancy which I would like to throw some speculative fun towards.

Personally I love tanky builds and so I would love a trial that rewards at least decent level of tankiness.

I envision the following trial setup:

  • Several corridor map segments like a simple maze with the boss in the centre.
    • Each corridor is a third of the walking length of a average map.
    • The fastest route to the boss is always four corridors.
    • Each completed corridor adds to the rewards from the boss though.
    • And the ascendancy levels are tied to how many corridors you take before reaching the boss. (16 corridors for max ascendancy)
  • You see all paths and also their mods before you set off and can plan the route you want to take
    • there are some extra paths/shortcuts which are visible, optional and their details hidden.
    • there are also paths that are invisible until you stumble over them.
  • At the end of each segment you can either pick the pre-planned next segment, or if available, take a more risky route.
  • The risky routes come with strong benefits but also with great rewards.
    • See the list of possible mods from this below.
  • The more corridors you clear before reaching the boss the better the boss rewards get on defeating the boss.
  • Each corridor is populated with different monster types (Which most certainly cannot ever be the River Hag!)

Mods and benefits:

These are the mods the special risky routes can have:

  • When you kill enemies you are shocked based on the overkill damage
  • You cannot deal more damage in one hit than your combined value of Life + mana + energy shield
  • Monsters cannot take more damage from one hit than 49% of their maximum life
  • Monsters deal extra damage of a random damage type based on the percent value of player resistances missing to max cap (90%)
  • Monsters gain 1% increased attack speed up to 50% each time you evade their hits.
  • Monsters gain your attack and spell damage at 30% of it’s value. 

And these could be rewards they give you when you complete them:

  • The boss drops one extra unique item
  • The boss drops 100% increase quantity of items
  • The boss drops 100% increased rarity of items
  • The boss drops only items of Tier 2 or better
  • The boss spawns a ventors contraption strongbox on death
  • The boss is resurrected after death, dropping loot on both deaths
  • The boss drops three extra random currency items in stacks of three

That’s it. :)
