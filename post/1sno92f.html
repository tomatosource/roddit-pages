Today Kareem Abdul Jabber’s turns 79

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar’s

 career is marked by unprecedented longevity and statistical dominance, while his personal life is defined by deep scholarly interests and resilient health battles. 

Professional and Academic Achievements

Abdul-Jabbar holds several records that stood for decades, and he remains the only player in NBA history to win six regular-season MVP awards. 

https://preview.redd.it/ljukj2kgxnvg1.png?width=128&format=png&auto=webp&s=8a07cba5650f3609abdef47fc293d6d95d8b0808

  • NBA Championships: Won six titles (1971 with Milwaukee; 1980, 1982, 1985, 1987, 1988 with Los Angeles).
  • Scoring Legacy: Held the NBA all-time scoring record (38,387 points) for 39 years until 2023. He still holds the records for most field goals made (15,837) and career wins (1,074).
  • All-Star Selections: Earned a record 19 All-Star selections over 20 seasons.
  • College Dominance: Led UCLA to three consecutive NCAA championships (1967–1969) and is the only player ever named NCAA Tournament Most Outstanding Player three times.
  • Academic and Literary: Earned a B.A. in History from UCLA in 1969. He has since authored 17 books and was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016.  Wikipedia +8

Personal Life and Family

His personal life includes a transition in faith, a large family, and significant medical hurdles. 

  • Family: Married  Janice Brown  (who took the name  Habiba ) in 1971; they divorced in 1978. He has five children:
    • Habiba ,  Kareem Jr. , and  Sultana  (with  Habiba Sr. ).
    • Amir  (with  Cheryl Pistono ), who is an orthopedic surgeon.
    • Adam  (with  Julye Olds ).
  • Religion: Converted to Sunni Islam in 1971, adopting the name Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, which means “noble, powerful servant”.
  • Health History:
    • Cancer: Diagnosed with chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) in 2008 and prostate cancer in 2010. He manages his CML through targeted medication.
    • Heart and Surgery: Underwent quadruple coronary bypass surgery in 2015 and was diagnosed with atrial fibrillation (AFib) in 2021.
    • Recent Injury: Underwent successful hip surgery in late 2023 following a fall.
  • Interests: A lifelong practitioner of yoga and martial arts, having trained in Jeet Kune Do under Bruce Lee. He is also an avid coin collector and served on the Citizens Coinage Advisory Committee.
