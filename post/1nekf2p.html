Hornets cancel sports betting theme night with former guard Terry Rozier under federal investigation for gambling

“ The Charlotte Hornets have canceled a sports betting theme night with former guard Terry Rozier linked to a federal gambling investigation. The team was slated to host the promotion March 17, the day Rozier and the Miami Heat were in town. But the team canceled the promotion Wednesday, per the South Florida Sun Sentinel.

Rozier, who played for the Hornets from 2019 to 2024, is under investigation by federal authorities for his actions during a game in the 2022-23 NBA season when he was still a member of the Hornets. Prior to a game against the New Orleans Pelicans that season, a bettor placed nearly $14,000 on unders involving Rozier’s statistics. Rozier left the game after just nine minutes due to a foot injury, causing all the under bets to win, per documents acquired by ESPN.”
