So during the Interlude when we go back to Ogham you have to go to Holten to stop the Thane and his Wife and some of the freaky shit going on there had me literally stop for like 30 mins to let it sink in. Here are the letters you read/hear in order. TL;DR at the bottom

PRISON

Dear Oswin. The Red Than and I are thrilled with your attention to detail. The Scarlet Bathing was an exquisite moment that we shall not soon forget. That you were able to acquire so many heretical younglings in such a short time… well we cannot thank you enough. The whispers grow deeper. He sees us. He sees you. We shall know unfathomable prosperity by his grace! The Red Thane, the Pale Bride, and their loyal hound. A hound most faithful. A hound most strong. I must away, for tonight I shall dance once more. Join us to feast. Look upon me as you will. Elswyth.

WULFRIC’S ROOM 1st FLOOR

My dearest Elswyth. His Voice grows clearer by the day. I can feel his presence. He thirsts so deeply for this world. I know that we shall be rewarded beyond measure upon his arrival. I am so proud. The night we committed to Him is still vivid in my mind. You gave so much so willingly. On the cusp between he and I, you fell to and fro. Your flesh pale white against the moon. I am eternally yours. As we are his. Your Wulfric.

ELSWYTH’S ROOM 2nd FLOOR

My valiant Wulfric. You drive me forward. You give me purpose. I am but dust without you… and Him. But we must have more. My skin grows tired. My heart grows heavy. My brow wrinkles and my bosom betrays me. I must have what was promised. I must have his desire! Oswin will prepare the Scarlet Bathing. In a fountain of fatality, the lifeblood of youth shall make me whole. There you and your hound must take your renewed Pale Bride, as to please the watching Master. I cannot wait. Yours, Elswyth.

TL;DR

Warden Oswin captured children and killed them so that Lady Elswyth could bathe in their blood to remain young. She then had a 3 some with her husband and Oswin in a sex ritual where either The King Of The Mists (or some other dark/chaos god) possessed Wulfric.
