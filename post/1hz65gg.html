Interesting 80s vs 2020s contrast – play style vs results

Was wondering how much the evolution of better players and also play style (three point heavy, more freedom for offensive players) has shown up in the results/stats over the decades, particularly the “analytics” idea that shooting 3s is just going to give you way more points overall. Not trying to make this a “stop shooting threes” post, but rather it seems the fact that people shoot 10x the number of threes they did in the 80s isn’t really translating to a big difference in points between now and then.

Here’s Statmuse with some of the stats by decade

Interesting to note:

-Despite the 2020s seeing an average of 35 3PA per game compared to 3.5 3PA per game in the 80s (so 10 times the volume), and looser dribbling, teams in the 2020s score only 3 PPG more than teams in the 80s, although teams in the 2020s are doing it with 2-3 fewer possessions per game. If they had the same number of possessions per game in 2020s, the difference in PPG scored would probably be around 5-6 PPG.

So, taking 35 threes per game compared to 3.5 threes per game (at a higher %, too) has only yielded a small net gain in PPG scored over the 1980s decade. It’s interesting that the play style has changed so much but the results haven’t shown the same dramatic change. There’s likely other variables impacting this lack of significant change (rules/officiating changing like zone defense).
