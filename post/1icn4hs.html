Refusing the hot hand

Has anyone else noticed that the team doesn’t really focus on what is working and tries to force something else to work even if it isn’t?

Against Houston, Payton Pritchard was 5⁄6 from 3. He was hitting everything. Porzingis was 3⁄4 from 3. Also hitting everything.

In the 3rd quarter we went 4⁄9 from 3 with Tatum missing 2 3s and Brown missing 1.

In the 4th quarter we went 3⁄8 with Jaylen Brown and Jrue Holiday both missing 3 and 2 threes respectively. Pritchard was 2⁄2 while Holiday was 1⁄3.

Jaylen Brown was 1⁄6 from the field in the 4th quarter.

I know that Porzingis was in foul trouble, but there were ample opportunities to get him the ball and we simply didn’t.

Tatum and Brown should never have more 3pt attempts than Payton Pritchard. There’s absolutely no reason for it and Porzingis has a scoring efficiency of 1.424 compared to Tatum and Brown who are 1.350 and 1.224 respectively.

I’ve been saying it over and over again, but the offense should flow more through Porzingis, he’s a better pure shooter than either of the Jays and more consistent from the free throw line. Make teams adjust to Porzingis and Pritchard just like they had to adjust to Porzingis and Hauser last year. It’ll make things easier for the Jays.
