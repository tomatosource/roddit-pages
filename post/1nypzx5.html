Rare insightful comment:

AnyJamesBookerFans 186 points 5 months ago

While Ron Boone’s iron man streak falls ~150 games short of A.C. Green’s, Boone has the distinction of never missing a game in his entire career.

Boone played a total of 1,041 games across the ABA and NBA for his entire career and did not miss a single one of those games due to injury, DNP, etc. And across all games where they recorded minutes played, the fewest minutes he played was 3, and there are only a dozen or so games where he played less than 10 minutes.

And if that’s not impressive enough, his streak supposedly started from the womb, as Boone played in every game in college and, he claims, every game in HS and middle school.

Like everything I read on Reddit, I assumed the comments were wrong, so I asked myself:

  • Was this really a Mickey Mouse streak?
  • Did A.C. Green really do this?
  • Why did people keep bring up celibacy in that thread?
  • Whose Ironman streak is really the most impressive?

Methodology

I used this table on Wiki as my reference. I used the start and end dates as my guide for pulling the game logs from basketball-reference. Not all players had full game logs, and some of the older players had game logs but the times were always whole minutes and were not accurate down to the second. If ABA games were to be included, Ron Boone would have 1,041 consecutive games played with 379 games from his time in the NBA, so /u/AnyJamesBookerFans was right.

Once I had all of the data, I realized that I don’t really know anything about data analysis or statistics, so I used Python to make a bunch of plots and output summary statistics.
