Charania on the Butler situation: “There’s not really an end yet in sight. The Heat have been engaged in conversations with multiple teams… But they’re not close to a deal as of yet.”

Shams Charania on First Take this morning (1:30 mark on the linked video)

There’s not really an end yet in sight. The Heat have been engaged in conversations with multiple teams, one of which being the Phoenix Suns, from what I’m told. But they’re not close to a deal as of yet. They obviously have an asking price that they’ve laid out to teams, including the Suns, of players who they think can help, draft assets, and no long-term contracts. That contract of Bradley Beal has really been a non-starter in talks. They’re not any closer to an end, and before that point it’s going to be a little ugly.
