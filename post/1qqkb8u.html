List of every NBA coach by career win percentage

Becoming an NBA coach is no easy feat. We’ve all heard about how difficult it is to make it into the NBA. But it is much harder to become an NBA head coach. There are approximately 450 players in the league each year. There are only 30 head coaching spots available each year. The number is actually much less when you consider NBA staples like Erik Spoelstra, Steve Kerr and Joe Mazzulla are unlikely to ever get moved out of their role as the head coach of their respective teams.

Usually it’s a former NBA player with a high basketball iq or a former college player / college coach that has grinded their way through the system and has dedicated their life to the game of basketball that gets to become an NBA head coach. As we’ve passed the midway point of the season and the dust starts to settle, we can clearly see which coaches have our teams playoff-bound and which coaches are leading as tank commanders so that our team has a high chance of getting a top pick like Darryn Peterson, AJ Dybantsa or Cameron Boozer in this year’s upcoming draft.

Here’s a list of the career coaching records (as of January 29, 2026) of all 30 head coaches. They will either lead our team to the promise land of holding the Larry O’Brien trophy in June or they are the man that we trust for the job of tank commander to land us one of those coveted young future superstars.
