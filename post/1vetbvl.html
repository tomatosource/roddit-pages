Golden State Valkyries change court this week from team colors to sponsor colors. NBA trial run?

Pic of the court

If you didnt know, the valkryies colors are violet, gold, black and white - and for two games they are playing on a court painted in chase bank blue.

In a release, the Valkyries said the new hardwood is the team’s “first-ever custom court and the only co-branded partner court to be revealed in the WNBA this season thus far. The court brings the partnership with Chase Freedom to life beyond the jersey, celebrating the fan energy driving the growth of women’s basketball.”

Chase Freedom is offering fans 20% off at the Golden State Shop and will donate $1,000 to the Golden State Community Foundation for every shot made during the two games.
