Temple Tip

I recently decided it was time for me to start building a decent temple and wanted to share a tip that other noobs may not know. Here goes… if you have max medallions don’t remove them to make room for new ones that drop. Wait until you finish your run and use existing medallions before you close the temple. Then you can go back and pick up the medallions you left behind.
