Would you like a race/roguelike game mode in the game?

Ive been doing a lot of ssfhc lately and ive realised how fun the game can be at scrappy low levels, as well as the clean/weight vision. Once you nail it its SUPER rewarding, its just not what ppl want when they get to endgame where its often all about juice and clutter on screen.

That said, imagine the following:

One off characters for races. Basically you log in and in addition to having normal game modes, you have a race/roguelike mode (or ruthless).

Originally i imagined it like a character that is isolated, and doesnt share the stash/progression with others. Its even more fun when its hc. But basically its campaign/race focused so it has ladder and leaderboards.

Like getting super tight builds and individual runs with just enough vendor and drop rng to make it fun for replaying.

Will that regal hit, balancing offense with defence etc.

I think campaign/races are a super fun part of the game, and lately there was some marketing done around it which was very fun and cool as a break from usual endgame grind. But its basically a mode that only pros play, because its very hard to incentivise non racers to play - its obscure.

If there was some built in ladder ranking, just seeing your name in there with a personal record you could try to top would be so rewarding and get us so hyped to tighten up and minmax our campaign runs.

Not to mention its fantastic practice for next ladder reset.
