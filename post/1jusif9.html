The Player is FASTER than mobs in Souls games, and they aren’t too easy

The vast majority of mobs in Soulsborne games are much slower than the player and almost all the combat (and many of the bosses) are completely optional. They are known for having some of the most engaging gameplay and challenge of any genre.

I love the challenge those games can provide, but it isn’t derived from forcing the player to engage, but challenging them to see if they are good enough to do so. It is less about “can I avoid this challenge” and more “I hope I can rise up to this challenge.”

Why are the DEVs insisting that if the player was faster there would be no challenge and no engagement? The whole point of the game is to engage with the mobs, we WANT to do so. /endrant
