This experience felt unnecessarily punishing

I just died to a corrupted boss on the first Corrupted Nexus on my new character.

What can I say? It spawned exactlywhere the normal boss’ loot shower ended up, I didn’t have the wits to press Z to hide loot, so I missed a tell for a oneshot and got oneshotted. Happens.

Apparently if you die to the corrupted boss, the map is still cleansed of corruption and you get the map completion. Except you don’t get credit towards Doryani’s quest, so now I have to leg it all the way to the other side of the Atlas for the next Nexus.

This feel more frustrating than wiping for a hour on Geonor on my very first character, more frustrating than dying to a moment of distraction on the fourth floor of Sekhema. It feels… So unnecessarily punishing, I mean it honestly feels bordering on vindictive. I lose exp, I lose a map attempt, why design it so I also lose a ton of time and the opportunity to get Atlas points earlier because of it? Just let me redo the encounter! It would be the equivalent of forcing me to redo the whole Juggernaut zones because I wiped at Jamanra! Actually, that would still be less time wasted.
