Two points stand out to me. First, as oft-discussed, the Spurs are extraordinarily young. They have the three youngest starters in the conference finals and 4 of the 5 youngest.

Second, Harden is essentially an age outlier for an NBA conference final starter. The mean age is 26.9 with a SD of 3.8. Harden is more than 2 SDs older than the average starter in the conference finals.

You can knock Harden for his defensive effort in the ECF. But you have to credit him for being the only starter among the older generation of superstars playing in the conference finals while the rest - Lebron, Steph, KD, Westbrook, Kawhi, Giannis, etc. - are watching from home. As Draymond said, making him the primary defender against Brunson seems ill-advised.
