Crafted my Dream Amulet. Now I teach you.

Total cost of craft: 700-800div

After I learned to craft and made 2000div in a day. Now I teach you., I think it was my turn to send myself into the big boy pants and craft myself a beast. This amulet is 100% deterministic craft. It does involve luck of course, but luck only makes your cost of craft less, the steps are the same:
Will include some tips for these crafts at the end

  1. Fracture a +3
  2. Chaos spam untill you get T1 Crit chance or Bonus, for me Bonus is what I’m after
  3. Add Speed Quality to target the Cast Speed
  4. Use Omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation to consume quality and add a suffix. This took 6 or 7 attempts
  5. If you miss, Annul and try again, if you hit the Crit suffix, go back to step 2
  6. Once we hit this the Suffixes are done, now we work on the prefixes
  7. We want 2x T1 mana, nothing else, so we add Mana Quality
  8. Use omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Omen of Sinistral Exaltation + Greater Exaltation and hit 2x Prefixes. This step took 7 times and I think I got lucky
  9. If you miss with step 8, use Omen of Sinistral Annulment (32div yikes) and then Sinistral Exaltation + Essence of Abyss (here we save 31div on the 2nd prefix deletion)
  10. Repeat untill 2x T1 mana
  11. Unveil T1 Spirit (this took 20-some tries)

Now I know you want me to do with this, and I will after I rock it around for a bit and enjoy the fruits of my labor. Been trying to craft this for 2-3 days.

My tips for you are:

  1. Do not be afraid to learn. The league is very diverse in builds, which makes the most important part of profit crafting easy - your bricks will sell to recoup investment.
  2. Use Crystallisation Omen + Essence of Abyss to target a preffix/suffix to remove cheap. It is basically desecration but in the form of an Essence
  3. Whatever affix you fracture, work on that side (preffix or suffix) first as if your gamble fails, you can always go back to chaos spamming
  4. Don’t sleep on recombinator. Recombining T1 Flat Mana + T1 Life or Rarity Prefix is 30%
  5. Always craft in bulk and you’ll profit! Not 1 item, 5 to 10 of the same is a good start.
