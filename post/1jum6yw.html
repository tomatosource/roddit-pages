No vendor for uncut gems makes swapping skills/builds a miserable experience while leveling

Title.

In A2 and wanted to swap from elemental spears to bleed spear on my huntress

I have no uncut skill gems.

I have no uncut supports.

I have no uncut spirit gems.

I have a slightly underleveled Rake with Brutality, but that’s it.

So if I actually want to try to swap I have to go farm a bunch or just keep leveling with the build I don’t want to use and hope I get lucky with uncut gem drops.

Meanwhile in PoE1 I can walk to a vendor, buy the gems, spend a very brief amount of time leveling them a bit, and then swap right over.

This gem system was supposed to be more flexible than PoE1 but it feels far, far more restrictive. The only upside is being able to ignore socket colors and not being stuck with that 1 bad 4-link for 15 levels because it has the correct links/colors and you can’t get rid of it. Otherwise I cannot think of how this gem system is in any way an actual improvement over PoE1’s system.
