Critical issue with core loot-drop design that no one is talking about. (Fix this GGG)

We all know that loot is scarce in PoE2. But do you know what isn’t scarce? Vendor inventories each time you level up. And this is a problem.

I’ve made a habit of going back to every vendor in every act each time I level up and buying the good stuff, then trading it for big exalts.

Each time I level, its like I get a free couple dozen alchs and over a hundred bases/transmutes/augmentation orbs. Just browse the NPC’s inventory and boom, look at all those fresh already identified drops! And the craziest part? If the items don’t roll well, I simply don’t buy them, so there’s no cost! (Unlike crafting off the ground which requires you to find a base AND the currency which is then lost on use, whether the roll is good or not.)

The biggest problem with this is getting loot from killing monsters is 100x more fun than searching vendors for loot. Vendor loot should be for the very unlucky streaks where you haven’t found an upgrade for a slot in a long time, but currently, nearly every slot of gear I have is filled with vendor items or traded items (which they probably bought from vendors.)
