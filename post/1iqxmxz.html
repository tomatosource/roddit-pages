Is Jaylen Brown’s sneaker brand, 741, inspired by Severance (S1E6)?

I was curious about the name of Jaylen Brown’s sneaker company, 741, and had a theory: Could it be inspired by a scene from Severance (Apple TV+)?

In Season 1, Episode 6, there’s a pivotal moment where Dylan’s son is counting to 1000, and the scene opens with him saying: “…741…742…743…”

It’s an oddly specific number to choose as a brand name, and the timing lines up—Jaylen announced 741 in 2023, not long after Severance premiered in 2022. While the name could have another meaning, I found this connection interesting and wanted to see if anyone else had thoughts on it.

Does anyone know if Jaylen has mentioned the origin of 741? Or does this seem like a reach?
