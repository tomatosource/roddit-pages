2025-2026 Top 50 Free Agent Prediction Contest

As MLB offseason begins, we are returning the MLB Free Agent Prediction Contest. Using the 2026 FanGraphs Top 50 Free Agents list we have created our competition to find out who is the best Free Agent predictor.

#RULES

  • Choose the correct signing team for each of the Top 50 MLB Free Agents and you will receive one point for a highest possible score of 50 out of 50 points.
  • In the case of a tiebreaker being needed, the tiebreaker will go to the predictor with the highest correct signing. Continued tiebreakers will flow down the rankings until a winner is chosen. In the extremely rare case of an identical submission, we will use the earlier time/date submission.
  • There is an option for “MLB Unsigned on Opening Day” which will include all players not signed to an MLB club by 1st pitch on Opening Day. This will include players who hold out beyond Opening Day, retire, or who choose to play internationally. MLB Opening Day currently is March 25th.
  • You do not have to choose the terms of the deal, just the correct team.
  • You must include your Reddit username to get credit for your submission. MAKE SURE YOUR REDDIT USERNAME IS SPELLED CORRECTLY!
  • If you make multiple submissions before the deadline, only the latest submission will be accepted.

#DEADLINE

  • All Submissions must be made by Nov 23rd at 12AM PT (midnight between Saturday/Sunday). This allows for all non-tendered to be completed.
  • Any player who signs or accepts their Qualifying Offer before the Nov 18th deadline will be removed from the competition. This will ensure that if you vote early, you will not be penalized for doing so.
  • All submissions will be made public shortly after in a Google Sheet for the world to see, so be prepared to defend yourself if necessary.
