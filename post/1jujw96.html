With Malone’s firing, the New York Knicks’ Tom Thibodeau is now currently the FOURTH longest-tenured head coach in the NBA. He was only hired on July 30, 2020.

The more specific list goes as :

# Coach Team Hire Date (m/d/y)
1 Gregg Popovich Spurs 12/10/96
2 Erik Spoelstra Heat 04/28/08
3 Steve Kerr Warriors 05/19/14
4 Tom Thibodeau Knicks 07/30/20
5 Billy Donovan Bulls 09/22/20
6 Tyronn Lue Clippers 10/20/20
7 Mark Daigneault Thunder 11/11/20

It’s interesting seeing just how crazy coach turnover has been lately - both this season and in the past few years. And considering Coach Pop’s unfortunate health issues as of late…there is a considerable chance that Coach Spo (or Kerr!) can end up becoming the longest-tenured coach in the entire league come next season too…
