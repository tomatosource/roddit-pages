Who do you think is this generation’s Ewing/Webber/Baylor–a player just a step below some contemporary all-time greats in talent, but who won’t finish with the accolades to be remembered as such?

Title, basically.

Patrick Ewing is responsible for some great Knicks seasons and deep playoff runs, but falls significantly behind contemporaries like Robinson, Hakeem, and Shaq in all-time discussions today due to a lack of awards and championships (never finished higher than fourth in MVP voting, never made an All-Defensive First Team, only made one All-NBA first team). You could argue that his greater level of postseason success put him about on par with someone like David Robinson until Robinson’s 1999 and 2003 championships as a second option, but that’s not really the perception.

Likewise, Chris Webber was leading some incredible Kings teams and making First or Second Team All-NBA during the greatest era of power forwards over names like Duncan, Garnett, and Nowitzki, but dodgy performances in the clutch and the relative shortness of his peak make it so that his overall career isn’t regarded nearly as favorably.

Elgin Baylor was a bona fide ‘60s superstar, very much considered to be in the same air as Russell, Wilt, West, and Robertson, but his inability to secure an MVP or title has caused him to slip while those other names remain somewhere in the top twenty of most all-time lists. He’s also just not as enduring narratively or statistically as that crowd (Russell led the greatest dynasty ever; Wilt’s records will never be broken; West was basically proto-Jordan; Robertson racked up a billion triple doubles and won with Kareem post-prime).

Who do you think is this generation’s version of that player?
