Best use for 2nd item set… Light Radius?

I never really used a second item set until recently. I just had some buff me stuff there but I always forgot to cast it and actually never used it at all. Until I had an idea… what if I could just greatly increase my vision radius with a mouse button?

So I tried it and buffed my light radius as much as possible with my 2nd Item set and the respective skills in the tree…

So I got…

  1. Guiding Palm of the eye unique scepter… Usually 25% increased light radius, got a corrupted version with 31%.

  2. Threaded light woven focus. It has 20% light radius but I got a corrupted version with 24%.

You could ofc. have a 2handed weapon like some unique or rare item, but they all dont add up as much light radius as these two together.

And in the skill tree I happened to be close enough to the nodes:

  1. Illuminated Crown (20% increased Light Radius)

  2. Chakra of Sight (20% increased Light Radius)

So all together it is about 95% increased Light Radius…. I bound the weapon swat to one of my mouse buttons just to press it ocassionally with my thumb.

and maaaaaan… this makes life so much easier. almost 100% Light radius means almost 4 times the visible Area…. the difference is just HUGE! You rarly miss anything on the map anymore…. Both uniques are super cheap. So even if you cannot spec into the 2nd weapon set skill points for the additional 40% light radius, you still should get 45% increased light radius for basically free…

Try it out, and you will be enlightened!

Oh, and this is when I realized that light radius does not only give you more vision, but it actually makes everything brighter…
