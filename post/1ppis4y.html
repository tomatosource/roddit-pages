Immortal Demon Bear Tech with infinite charges

Basically, the bonded modifier on Saqawal’s rune of the Sky reads “8% chance to gain an additional charge when you gain a charge” this comboed with Jagged ground on ancestral cry and rage forged on walking calmaity creates a pretty fun one-button build for the bear that is very hard to kill. I have tested it on t16 maps and most pinicale bosses, and it’s pretty strong.

I made a quick showcase for anyone looking for a simple-to-play bear build. Only barrier to entry is owning the fury of the king Talasmin, as it makes generating glory for the ancestral cry SO much easier.
