Suggestion about Endgame disenchanting

Hi all, i would like to hear your opinion about late game disenchanting.

In the early game, seeing your first piece of yellow gear is really quite amazing, and for someone that is unprepared - sometimes a call to pivot their character entirely . And that is great.

Later if u know that this piece is useless, or maybe after a long deliberation between two pieces, you might start disenchanting some, going back to town, inhaling the atmosphere, watching other players in town, maybe even browsing shop. All while getting those juicy and scarce regal/transmute shards. And that is great too

In later stages of compaign, you still mainly do

the same, even though regal and aug are not as scarce anymore, and drops are much more frequent. Because the time between city visits is short, your build is mainly formed (so u are fast), and questing structure is formed in a way that frequently pulls you to town. So you have time to still collect and then disenchant your loot.

But during the endgame this process becomes much much more tedious. The maps are starting to be juiced. And there are a lot of yellow drops. I still really like my t1 regals. As they are still very useful but the maping that as i suppose everyone is starting to reaaaally zoom through, becomes a real swamp. I kill a big pack of mobs, and drop a butt tonn of equipment, some of which i would like to disenchant for those juicy double regal shards. But to do that - i have to pick it up, tp to hideout dis, sort, stash, repeat, repeat, repeat, repeat. Then next pack of mobs. Sometimes i get distracted, and, as im not in a map, it gets blown. That is very frustrating.

So guys, how do you think about adding some possibility to disenchant items right in a map during endgame? I think like an endgame unlock, or even better: a special currency: “scroll of disenchantment” that might only sell for gold, and never drop, think about it:

  1. another gold sink beside respec, trade filpping, and a very rare unique strongbox

  2. mapping becomes much smoother

  3. maybe even some unexplored design space with disenchantment

And also, while i was writing this, i thought about real fast trade league guys, that might say “don’t bother, just download a loot filter, and skip everything”. But i think, that it s pretty lame for your loot to just rot, and simple regals are very useful for tablets, maps, jewels, and etc.
