Thoughts on trial of the sekhemas from someone who plays (and completes) alpt of games especially Build making games.

For the most part POE2 has been decent its not amazing and I would not pay for it like its a full priced game tbh but it is pretty solid for me right now as ive been in a bit of a gaming dry spell.

My biggest pet peeve in every game is when the devs throw something in the game that does not align with the meat and bones of the game. Example is your playing a action game hack and slash, all of a sudden, you must solve this sudoku to access special powers that are required to beat the game…. DAFUQ???

Thats how I feel about this trial. Temple of chaos is annoying but it MAKES SENSE. O also notice hardly anyone plays these trials because they are so frustrating to others so that’s something. Whenever a situation like this arises I always think its some dev or 2 that either tried to make a puzzle/platformer game at one point and it failed HARD so they try to bring some puzzle to every project they touch.

I know this trial nonsense isn’t exactly a puzzle but it truly feels like the shit doesnt fit lol. What’s also weird is the HUGE gap on difficulty from trials 1-3 and 4. For some reason the 4th trial os filled with 200% more ranged enemies who shoot fast projectiles so u just gotta eat shit and let ur honor dwindle lol

Anyway overall I konda enjoy the game hut it has alot of useless pain points. Its pain points for the sake of pain points and not a sense of accomplishment or w/e. Done the trial on other tools and I never felt it was because I did something really good. It always was luck of the draw lol
