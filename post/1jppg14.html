Giannis didn’t understand why Budenholzer didn’t get a video from the Bucks

He got an April Fools ‘welcome back’ social media post but no video. Giannis was asked about it in the postgame:

No, it’s not hard. They had like this video on social media. It’s like 30 minutes, he was in the dark and the flames were coming out. Did you guys watch that video? You’ve never seen that video? (to Bucks Content Team) Find it. And cut this interview and put the video in and then go back to the interview. But no, they had an incredible video. I thought they were going to play a video for him and they didn’t, which was surprising to me. I felt like they should. We were three times No. 1 team in the NBA, two times No. 4 team in the NBA, won a championship, five times in the playoffs, two times in the Eastern Conference Finals, one time in the Finals, so a lot of winning games, so I felt like it would be a two-minute, three-minute - okay maybe not two minutes, I’m exaggerating - maybe 45 seconds, I don’t like him that much. (smiles) Maybe like a 30-second video of him being the coach.
