Biggest beef with the game

Overall I’ve really been enjoying the game. I’ve got some friends I play with and we all enjoy it.

My one problem I would really like them to fix for this next patch is multiplayer.

  1. Party invites aren’t getting to the other folks if they login after we’ve started a party so we have to create a whole new party.

  2. The cost benefit of difficulty vs reward of being in a party is way out of wack. At endgame it’s much more efficient in every way to just play by yourself so there is no real benefit of playing with friends.

  3. There is a massive latency issue between geos. One of our friends is in Australia, we are in the US. Whoever isn’t hosting is fighting a massive lag battle to the point that some content is not playable.

  4. We are constantly dealing with disconnects. We were trying to do an ascendency run last night. 4 runs were ruined by connectivity issues in a row.

If they could iron this out the game would be much more enjoyable.
