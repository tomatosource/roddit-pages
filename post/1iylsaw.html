Armor problem solution

I think that Armor should be more effective against big hits instead of small hits as it is right now.

  • If that was the case, It would make a lot more sense for warriors to invest into armor breaking, because it would reward single big mace hits.
  • Investing into life would make a lot more sense, since it is already helps to absorb a lot of small hits, but armor would allow not to get single taped by bosses.
  • From class fantasy stand point it makes a lot more sense that hulking ironclad warrior could withstand enormous hits, while robes are better absorbing magical sparks, AOEs and similar things.
  • Physical damage should be more effective against energy shield to make it more comparable to armor.

I may be over simplifying, but it just makes no sense what we have now and flipping it around seem to make Warriors so much more viable. Even if it was flipped I feel evasion probably still going to be more effective, so that’s not saying it would solve much. But what are your thoughts?
