The 2019 Toronto Raptors Playoff Run

Throughout the years, and as recently as today, I have seen people mention that the 2019 Raptors would easily lose to a healthy GSW squad in the Finals. I do not agree with this opinion and wanted to add my reasons why here.

The Rockets took 5 games from the Steph/Durant Warriors in 2 series and had injuries of their own (including no CP3 in the Game 7 where they missed 27 3s in row). The Rockets in either year were not as deep as the 2019 Raptors.

The Raptors went 2-0 against GSW in the regular season in 18-19. This included a win against a fully healthy GSW roster by 20 points, without Kawhi or Marc Gasol.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/201812120GSW.html

They had a team full of 3+D players at every position for almost all 48 minutes a game, and this is without OG in the playoffs. They had an all time defensive team with a plethora of 3 point shooting, playmaking and size. They have generally been underrated as one of the better champions of the era… they were underdogs vs the Bucks, Sixers and a Durant-less Warriors going into those series!

Some other facts about this team:

  • 1 of only 5 champions ever to beat 3 50+ win teams in a championship run.
  • Hit the only game 7 buzzer beater in history.
  • 1 of 3 teams since 2000 to win 3 games on the road in the finals
  • Bucks and Warriors were #1 and #2 in offensive rating that season and the Raptors held them to more than 12 points less than their average in their series. Held Giannis and Curry to 43% and 41% shooting respectively.
  • Easily a top 5 all time playoff defence in terms of Playoff Defensive Rating by a champion.
  • Kawhi and Siakam combined to tie with Jordan and Pippen for the 2nd most points scored by a duo in a playoff run (Kawhi with 3rd most ever for 1 player)

GSW was a top heavy team who had to risk their depth to make a big 4. Their 5th best player was a washed up Igoudala, followed by Livingston and Looney. When you build a team like that you need to assess the risk of what happens if your best players get hurt due to having to take on more of the work load. Raptors had OG injured and Kawhi on one leg but enough depth to make up for that.

They followed this season up with a 60 win pace season (53-19 in COVID shortened season) after losing Kawhi and Green while having the 4th hardest schedule in the league. The 2022 team was ranked 1st in OPP Pts/G and 4th in Net Rating.

Am I saying the Raptors would 100% win? No. But this would an evenly matched series considering both teams strengths/weaknesses and an argument could be made for either team. The 9 man rotation of Kawhi, Lowry, Siakam, FVV, Gasol, Ibaka, Green, OG and Powell is one of the most well rounded rosters you will ever see and is a championship threat in any season.
