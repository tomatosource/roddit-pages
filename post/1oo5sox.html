D White offence discussion

Love D White, he’s been fantastic since he joined the team and was a big part of the championship run. I don’t believe he can be the best caliber of himself with the current iteration of the team. I didn’t realise just how much he benefited from the gravity of Tatum, with teams now putting stronger defenders on D White and having a greater responsibility on offence his efficiency is way down and the eye test hurts.

D White can positively contribute to any contending team, when he is the 3-4th best player in a squad your team is just that much better because of his versatility, the Cs right now don’t have that luxury.

I think it’s something to monitor and it bodes questions in my head as in, would you trade whilst his value is high get a few picks and pray the tank works?
