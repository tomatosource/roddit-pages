This is the only team in the league that loses in the way it does.

Not a doomer post, more of an observation. I still think the Celtics should win this series in 5⁄6, but that’s besides the point. I have a problem with the way this team loses games, and it’s in its own unique way that other teams don’t experience.

The Celtics gave that game away, plain and simple. I’ve watched every game of the playoffs this season and no other team has choked away a lead in this same fashion. I can’t think of another team, or another top 2 superstars on a team that crashes and burns due to their own stupidity and refusal to attack the basket. Are these guys fucking pawns that only listen to what Joe tells them? You see the lead dissipating, and everyone bricking 3’s, and you as a top player in the league (JB is a finals MVP, Tatum is a perennial first team All NBA guy), decide not to attack the fucking basket or draw fouls? Congrats to Tatum for having a good series against an injured Magic team that we were supposed to win in 4⁄5 regardless, but these sort of games are why everyone dogpiles when he loses. We don’t need to see his defensive rebound numbers and screen efficiency either. Our superstar was unable to buy one fucking bucket in the entire 4th and OT. This is just as much on the two guys who have way too much experience to be doing this, as it is on Joe.

I know the simple way of looking at the game is the Knicks shot like 45% from 3 compared to our 25%, and that may be the only answer, but it doesn’t absolve the Celtics from giving away that fucking game. If your math equation gameplan isn’t working, maybe try getting to the rim or drawing some fucking FTs. At least go out fighting instead of losing in the most bitchmade way possible. We lost the Magic game last series in virtually the same way too. One of Tatum’s signature 5-19 type of performances.

Anyways, I still expect the Celtics to win the next game, and I really hope this is a fucking wake up call for them to add some more variety to their offense when the 3’s aren’t falling.
