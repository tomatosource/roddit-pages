Atlas Tree needs more Choice Nodes

I’m going to start off by saying that the revamp to the tree is absolutely wonderful and I love it personally. GGG made HUGE strides in this tree to make it feel good and I could see my progress and my drops get better (quality and quantity) as it filled. You really notice it from your first X points to the points from the first arbiter and every kill after as you finish it off.

What I am pointing to explicitly is the amount of choice nodes on the tree. When you look at the core tree (not league, I think they are fine with their size), you only have a handful of choices. You do feel the choices when you select them, an example of this is that I wanted essence early on and I could feel the amount of essences I would get per map. I recently moved to shrines and again, I see the amount of shrines increase substantially. This is great work and is miles ahead of where we were.

The issue, which I see, is that we do not have enough choice nodes in the existing tree. For a ~300 point tree, I would expect around 30-40 choice nodes in order to hone your experience. Want more waystones, sacrifice effectiveness. Want more rarity, sacrifice quantity. Things like that, things to give you real choice and agency to your core experience.

A note on the league trees, I have seen people not happy that you can spec to them all. My argument is that with 5 league trees that’s pretty simple to do, as the game expands and more leagues get released, we will see people specializing in a few instead of all. If you have 20 league mechanics plus a core tree, people will not, unless you are a streamer or have a ton of time to blast, max all 20 of these and specifically pick the ones they want to focus (min/max or for fun).

What do you all think? Does GGG need to expand the amount of choice nodes in the core tree line me? Something else?
