Comprehensive endgame feedback list after 500 hours

Not fun

  • Traveling to citadels
  • Setting up towers
  • Lack of inventory space, you just get so much stuff, even with the strictest endgame lootfilters
  • Lack of tooltip DPS options
  • Website marketplace instead of in-game
  • Lack of QoL inventory sorting, such as deposit/withdraw all, sort inventory and “combine all waystones into highest tier possible”
  • One-shot or get one-shot state of game when fighting against T4 pinnacle bosses
  • Lack of build variety, most builds revolve around HoWa, Astramentis, Ingenuity, Temporalis/Morior and Atziri in some capacity and that’s not because of lack of the community’s imagination
  • Vaal factory/sun temple
  • Rituals & delurium events
  • Respec cost

Fun

  • Every time a divine dropped it yelled “oh my gosh” from the Skrillex sample
  • Gamble
  • Cook maps
  • Variety of classes

How to make fun?

  • Bring citadels closer to each other
  • Less towers, higher rewarding tablets and bigger tower range
  • Learn basic game coding, if Sherwood Dungeon could do inventory sorting back in 2005, there’s no excuse to miss out on it in 2025, same thing goes for the Marketplace, the Grand Exchange was added in 2007 people
  • Better balancing of pinnacle bosses, uniques and player defences/offences
