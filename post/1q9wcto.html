Damage shouldn’t be the best defense

It’s not right for a character who kills bosses in a couple of seconds to be better at bossing than a character who has invested ten times the currency in both defense and offence but kills bosses in 10 seconds or more (which is the cap for most builds).

At the same time the trend with 1-shot mechanics e.g. Arbiter pushes people to not bother with defense and skip everything the boss is designed to throw at them instead by burning it down in a few seconds.

Got both kinds of characters, and frankly, the only reason I even create the second kind of character is to combat boredom, because with the first kind of character I don’t see much of the game’s mechanics.

Defenses, besides ES, are just not good enough, even if armour and deflection-stacking evasion are in a relatively good place compared to poe 1. Furthermore, there are no efficient ways to defend against some of the things the game is throwing at you besides a few flat damage reductions (most of which are for hits and do not work against e.g. physical degen).

It’s been like this forever in PoE, and I hoped 2 would change that but this was not the case. Does GGG even have any intention of ever changing this? Personally I don’t enjoy playing the same few builds on every league.

Because some of you misunderstood my post: I am not saying there’s something wrong with burning the bosses down fast. I am saying that characters and builds that can’t do that, should be able to survive them.
