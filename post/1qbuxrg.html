0.4 Crafting feels like overcomplicated gambling simulator

Combined with current inflated prices due to Atziri Temple its such a drag to craft something as semi casual.

Basically it goes down to(more or less):
Buy a base(can be hundreds of divs if its a good base)-&gt;spends dozens of divs on rng perf augment+annul spamming-&gt;buy a fracture orb for 30 div and pray for 33% success-&gt;rng chaos spam for who knows how many divs-&gt;sell a kidney and spam whittles/annulments untill rng hits for remaining affixes

I feel like im throwing hundreds of divines worth of currency for slot machine affix simulator. At this point even 20 years old Diablo 2 crafting feels better than this for me. In there you get random rare item with some add-ons affixes on top of it so you dont need 50 step plan of rng rolling.

And I actually enjoyed crafting back in 0.3. I get that homogenising omens were too strong but at least I felt that i’m actually “crafting” something instead of this rng slog fest.
