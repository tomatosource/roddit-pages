Armour “estimation” only confuses people

It’s bad case of information simplification, because we see many people on reddit come here and be confused why they die when they have 90% damage reduction when in fact it depends on size of the hit, the bigger the hit is, the harder it is to reduce it’s damage and why armour builds are prone to get oneshot.

I believe there is no way around it and you have to explain fully the armour in some simple manner. I saw this on few websites and I think it’s great to help people understand armour and should be in the game.

  • To mitigate 33.3% you need Armour equal to 5 times the Damage
  • To mitigate 50% you need Armour equal to 10 times the Damage
  • To mitigate 66.6% you need Armour equal to 20 times the Damage
  • To mitigate 75% you need Armour equal to 30 times the Damage
  • To mitigate 90% you need Armour equal to 90 times the Damage
