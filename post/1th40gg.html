For those excited about Facebreaker and any potential implications with Hollow Palm Technique…some info/warning.

https://preview.redd.it/haouukoo1z1h1.png?width=835&format=png&auto=webp&s=2f784617fc02882f78af92916b016c27a142f061

https://preview.redd.it/hvfrqk9s1z1h1.png?width=905&format=png&auto=webp&s=e6c40330de167d1a1119bc0945d8be8a525ee6f9

There are a few things to take note of:

  • The implicit/scaling physical damage of Facebreaker will not work on Quarterstaff attacks. Facebreaker is only treated as a weapon for damage calculation when using Mace skills. This “physical damage” is not the same as “Adds _____ physical damage to attacks” If it were written as adds/added it would work but it doesn’t.
  • Hollow Palm Technique relies on ES/EV hybrid, Pure ES or Pure EV gear for scaling it’s attack speed and critical chance. These types of armour items cannot roll Strength making it difficult to scale strength. Replacing any gear slot for an Strength based armour will not only harm your defense but gimp your attack between the two scaling vectors.
  • The ES/EV gear and Quarterstaff skill gems also have high requirements of Int & Dex at highest tier bases to equip. Scaling strength while satisfying Int/Dex + losing out on significant flat Strength from armour slots is possible but not recommended.
  • The Scaling attack speed and crit from Hollow Palm technique is specifically state to affect “unarmed attacks that would use your quarterstaff’s damage” meaning these modifiers will not apply to Mace skills.

On the positive side:

  • The 1% more unarmed damage from the gloves will work on Quarterstaff attacks with Hollow Palm
  • The Melee Strike Range from the gloves while unarmed will work on Quarterstaff attacks with Hollow Palm
  • The +1 to Armour per Strength still works. However, to maximize Hollow Palm technique, we won’t have any other source of armour which will limit how effective this is and making further armour scaling ill advised.
  • You can use both Mace & Quarterstaff skills while unarmed
  • The martial artist glove node will convert this low base armour glove into a solid ES/EV glove base for scaling Hollow Palm

What remains to be seen is whether or not the martial artist glove node may modify Facebreakers in a significant way out of left field like changing strength to dex, armour to evasion, etc. Without knowing that, I’d say this combination isn’t nearly as good as some people hope it will be.
