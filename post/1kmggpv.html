The Pacers have once again made the Eastern Conference Finals for the 10th time in franchise history - and the 10th time in just 32 years (since 1994). Their appearances came in 1994, 1995, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2004, 2013, 2014, 2024, and now 2025. The lone breakthrough to the Finals came in 2000, where they fell to the Shaq and Kobe Lakers.

Overall, the Pacers’ 10 Conference Finals appearances are tied for the most playoff semi-final appearances without a championship, alongside the Phoenix Suns. This isn’t a record just in the NBA, but across the Big 4 American sports leagues (NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL).
