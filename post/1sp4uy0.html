Does anyone else really wish Infernalist would get the Druid treatment?

I want demon form to be something I can switch in and out of smoothly, and keep as long as I want. Hell, I want different demon forms, that you can fluidly switch between. I’d even love to do a hybrid build with a Talisman on swap and switch between demon and and beast forms. The gameplay on Druid is so fluid, and demon form is just so clunky.

I get it, it’s power at a price, it literally burns your life-force away, etc etc. But it’s clunky. And Blood Mage is the cares-about-life ascendancy; it’s weird that Infernalist turns around and does a bunch of that too, between demon form and the life-reservation nodes.

I liked Infernalist, until I saw Druid. Now I know it can be so much better.

For a potential example, imagine the demon form node was changed to read something like

You assume X-demon form while your main-hand weapon is a Wand.
You assume Y-demon form while your main-hand weapon is a Staff.
You assume Z-demon form while your main-hand weapon is a Sceptre.

Now wands, staves, and sceptres work like talismans, and you can mix those, or a human or talisman form, as you like the same way you can with talismans themselves.

This is just one idea, probably a pretty maximalist one they aren’t going to do. They could also just do

You assume demon form while your main-hand weapon is a Sceptre, Staff, or Wand.

And it doesn’t have to be weapon-based, though I think it’s a good idea. The point is less this specific thing, and more just making demon form more fluidly and seamlessly a part of the rest of the game.
