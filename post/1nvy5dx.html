Boss life is actually fine, the issue is having only 1 portal in maps

The buffs to boss life are a great change and just make the game better. We can finally see some of their mechanics and have a real engaging fight, which was what they advertised poe2 to be.

The issue is that we are heavily incentivized to run 6 mod maps that only have 1 portal, which doesn’t give players a chance to learn the boss mechanics. This leads to frustration as most people are used to one shoting bosses and don’t even know their mechanics yet.

Maybe GGG could find a middle ground where they give us more respawn attempts, but only once we already entered the boss room. Maybe also reduce the XP penalty when dying to a map boss. I’m curious to see your suggestions!
