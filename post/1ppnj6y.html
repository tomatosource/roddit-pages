Confusing decisions from ggg

honestly some of the decisions made about this game are truly mind boggling. Someone with about 10 hours and any common sense could tell you that something needs changing.

Why is every unique completely useless on arrival unless it has a game breaking line. Why are we not making uniques somewhat comparable to rares u would obtain at the same time? Every unique that’s added is just a disappointment because the art department always makes an item look amazing only to be utilized by genuinely 0 players, I get that depending on the league can change the use case of uniques but there are a large portion of them which are just stat sticks that are infinitely worse than any comparable rare u can get at the same time. The worse part is that they sometimes buff these items by like 10-20% as if that makes them usable when in some cases they genuinely need a 5x increase in stats.

Why not just balance uniques aggressively stop with these 5-10% changes just go wild if it makes a shitty unique broken for a league so be it. I can understand not wanting to nerf mid league but why not just buff especially on these under utilized uniques. It was excusable before, but as we are at 0.4 I think it’s time we got changes.

Additionally how is the new league mechanic so undercooked in terms of rewards, it’s not as if any testing needed to even be done to tell you if it’s useless or not when it’s magnitudes weaker than where it should be. they haven’t just missed the mark in terms of rewards, they haven’t even aimed at the board. it just leaves me with the question of how has this even happened and makes me lose faith in the dev team, where I genuinely think 90% of this reddit could have done better balancing of rewards
