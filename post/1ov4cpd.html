Delirium fog fix

The problem isn’t the fog - it’s the repetition. If I could run 3 normal maps for every 1 delirium map, I’d actually think the the fog was pretty cool.

Fix: introduce a mechanic to delirium maps: if you complete the map, the benefits and difficulty scaling of the delirium on that map are distilled into a precursor tablet. The tablet will also increase the chances of having distilled vials drop from your normal maps. The higher the difficulty of your initial delirium map, the higher chances of getting rare distilled vials on your follow up normal maps.

Note: the distilled precursor tablet has no effect if you run it on a delirium map.

So after completing a delirium map you are incentivized to run some normal maps bc you get the same delirium benefit but you also have increased chances to find a distilled vials that can make your next delirium map more rewarding.

This has a secondary benefit of fixing regular delirium precursor tablets, which have no use once you start putting delirium on your maps.
