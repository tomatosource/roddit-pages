Druid Passive Tree Starting Area Notables

Paused throughout the ZiggyD gameplay video to find out what the notables in the starting area of the Druid were (*subject to changes)

First Row (Left to Right)

Guardian of the Wilds - 3% Increased Skill Speed, 16% Increased Damage, +5 Strength and Intelligence

Grounded in the Earth - 16% Increased Skill Effect Duration, 16% Increased Stun Threshold

Bastion of the Forest - 15% Increased Armour, +15% of Armour also applies to Elemental Damage, 15% faster start of Energy Shield Recharge, +5 Strength and Intelligence

Second Row (Left to Right)

Resilient Soul - 20% Increased Life Regeneration Rate, 5% of Damage taken Recouped as Life

Radial Force - 10% Increased Area of Effect, 12% Increased Immobilisation buildup

Sturdy Mind - +30 to maximum Mana, 14% Increased Mana Regeneration Rate

Third Row (Left to Right, missing rightmost defensive notable)

Primal Spirit - 10% Increased Critical Hit Chance while Shapeshifted, 8% Increased Skill Speed While Shapeshifted

Harbinger of Disaster - 10% Increased Critical Damage Bonus, 10% Increased Damage, 10% Increased Area Damage
