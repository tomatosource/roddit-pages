Back in the ol’ days, we watched the biggest superstars participate in the NBA Dunk Contest. Michael Jordan won the contest in 1986 and 1987. During those two seasons, he averaged 37.1 points and 35.0 points per game, respectively.

Suffice it to say, those days are gone. The 4 participants in this year’s dunk contest have averaged a combined sum of 18.5 points per game so far this year.

Jaxson Hayes (LAL): 17.1 minutes, 6.9 points per game

Jase Richardson (ORL): 12.1 minutes, 5.1 points per game

Carter Bryant (SA): 9.3 minutes, 3.4 points per game

Keshad Johnson (MIA): 7.6 minutes, 3.1 points per game

On the bright side, you can grab tickets to this year’s dunk contest at a very reasonable price of only $1,000 per seat !
