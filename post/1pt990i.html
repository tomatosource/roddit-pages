The Light Stun chain-lock is the least fun I’ve had in any ARPG

Seriously, I need to talk about this.

I can deal with hard fights. I can deal with one-shots if I mess up. But Light Stun? It feels downright unfair in a way nothing else in the game does.

The problem isn’t getting stunned once. That’s fine. The problem is the chain-stun lock that follows. It doesn’t matter how tanky you are or how well you’ve built your character (especially as melee) the first Light Stun almost always leads to 10 more in a row. You’re just frozen in place, watching your health bar vanish, completely unable to play the game.

It creates this feel-bad moment where your agency is entirely removed. You don’t get to react, you don’t get to use a potion, you don’t get to dash out. You just stand there and die.

I know the technical solutions exist: stacking stun avoidance, stun recovery, etc. but that’s not my point. My point is: why is a mechanic this frustrating so central and punishing?

It feels like it directly fights against the core fun of an ARPG: constant action and reaction. Getting locked down for several seconds straight is the opposite of that.

Does anyone else feel this way? Or am I just building wrong and this is a “me” issue?

Suggestion: What if there was a very brief immunity window after being Light Stunned? Just a second or two where you can’t be re-stunned by the same type of hit. It would keep the mechanic’s interrupt power but remove the utterly miserable chain-stun death loop.
