My league goal was to find a mageblood. It’s not going great…

Just felt like sharing how my season’s been going, since this is by far my most played season of PoE2, to the point where I’ve more than doubled my total hours play.

First off, I fucking love this game. This season especially has been incredible, if for no other reason than they seem to have fixed a major issue that caused me to freeze/crash every other map last time I played.

I decided to set myself the league goal of *finding* a mageblood. It’ll be neat, I thought, a long term goal that is relatively achievable. So to max my odds, I opted to farm rituals. Initially with the cheap setup, and later on switched to the giga expensive juicer setup.

At this point, I believe I used at least 100 heads of the king. My atlas is a sprawling landmass of cities that have been juiced dry, and no mageblood to be found.

I’ve found countless purple omens, 3 omens of chance, 2 headhunters, at least 3 rathpits and 3 kalandra’s touch, but no mageblood. The goal has kept me going this far, but it’s starting to get really damn depressing. I haven’t grinded this hard since farming Dried Lake for a full set of Voll’s devotion div cards years ago in Poe1.

I’ve managed to do some cool shit this league in the mean time, like push a characrer further than I’ve ever done in my history of playing PoE (lvl 98 on my abyssal lich!!), crafted a 400+ div spear, as well as a set of six 5 mod jewels (very fun to craft, would recommend) and found 2 freaking headhunters, but my bounty still eludes me. Could I have bought the mageblood several times over by now? Oh absolutely, but that would defeat the purpose of having a goal!

So now as my excitement for the game is starting to dwindle, I wanna make one last push to try and get it. Maybe this weekend will be my final attempts…

all I’m asking of you dear readers is this: pray for me, brothers and sisters! Drip feed me your rng through the ether like goku collecting energy from all living things to fuel the ultimate RNG spirit bomb. I will push through the boredom one last time to try and complete my league goal: finding a stupid pixelated belt!
