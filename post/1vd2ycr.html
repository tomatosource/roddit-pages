Stormweaver’s Elemental Storm & Multiplying Squalls are minor changes from feeling great

Elemental Storm has suffered from irrelevance since release, and its a shame with how cool it looks. With crit being basically the major way to scale damage in poe2, and this skill requiring a crit strike but having 0 interaction with crit itself is silly. It can stay not critting, but some sort of increased damage or more damage based on crit chance or damage would be great. And its cooldown being reduced. As is its just elemental archon fodder with arbiters ignition.

Crazy that the ascendancy named *Storm*weaver has Multiplying Squalls which doesnt increase the limit of Tornado, an iconic storm skill. With Taming in poe2, tornado has become much stronger as a primary option in a build, and its a shame that stormweaver doesnt have the option to lean into its limit stacking with this skill. Tornado getting an elemental tag, or Multiplying Squalls becoming spell limit (unfortunate for attacks or things like eventide petals) or just generic limit (could break something I’m sure). Would be great.

Stormweaver has an identity, with crazy infusion and remnant generation or non-damaging ailment scaling, but the elemental storm path has always been its weakest.

Anyway if you couldnt tell I want to play limit stacking stormweaver with tornado next league, but dont want the whole bonded mod storm limit body armour rune.
