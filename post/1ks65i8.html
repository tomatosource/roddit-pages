In January 2024, the Knicks starting lineup of Jalen Brunson, Donte Divincenzo, OG Anunoby, Julius Randle, and Isaiah Hartenstein went 9-1 in the games they played together. Three of those players are no longer on the Knicks, but all 5 are in the Conference Finals this year

It’s a travesty that we never got to see this lineup play for more than 10 games before injuries struck. Of course, I’m glad they found success elsewhere, and no Knicks fan can argue against our team’s results this playoffs, their first ECF in 25 years. But it remains a huge what if.

It’s also a travesty that Leon Rose didn’t even finish top 3 in Executive of the Year, neither last season nor this season. 3 of those players- Brunson, Divincenzo, and Hartenstein- were free agent signings, with OG being acquired through a trade for RJ Barrett and Immanuel Quickley. IQ was a draft pick made under Leon. He’s clearly got an eye for talent and roster construction.

Also sort of related: former Knick Obi Toppin is on the Pacers, so that makes every remaining team this playoffs have a current or former Knick. Toppin was also drafted under Leon
