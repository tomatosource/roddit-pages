In a 4 year span Mike Brown was fired by the 61-win Cavs, 5 games into the season by the Lakers, and then again by the Cavs

The 2010 Cavs were the first team in NBA history to win 60+ games in consecutive seasons and not make the finals, with Mike Brown being fired in 2010 after the Cavs with prime LeBron James lost in the 2nd round to the geriatric, decrepit Celtics.

In 2012 after pushing one of the oldest teams in the League, the LA Lakers, to play a motion heavy offense and going 0-8 in the preseason and starting the year 1-4, Mike Brown was fired.

In 2013 Mike Brown was rehired by the Cavs. The Cavs then fired him within a year after a season in which players fought in the locker room and the team won 33 games.

Mike Brown now coaches the fully healthy New York Knicks, who are down 28 at the half against the Dallas Mavericks missing Gafford, AD, D’lo, PJ Washington, Lively, Kyrie, and the ghost of Dante Exum.
