[Eurohoops] Alex Antetokounmpo joins Greek club Promitheas for next season

Alex Antetokounmpo has officially signed with Promitheas Vikos Cola for the 2026–27 season, the Greek club announced Saturday.

The forward began last season with Aris before heading across the Atlantic once more. That pivotal move culminated in his official NBA debut with the Milwaukee Bucks, where he logged six appearances, averaging 3.2 points and one rebound per game.
