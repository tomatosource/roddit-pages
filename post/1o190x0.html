Should Exceptional Bases Stay?

As title, on the one hand I love having one more thing to chase. A 4 sockets corrupted bow just looks oh so good.

On the other hand, it puts me off crafting anything that is not an exceptional base (aside from jewelleries) because the material cost is high to begin with, especially if you’re slamming perfect trans, homos, hysteria, horror, light, etc

If exceptional bases weren’t a thing, a craftable rare or magic off the ground is more attractive.

Maybe an orb with a chance to exceptional or poof an item is a halfway house? You can slam white bases with it to start crafting or you can slam a craftable magic/rare off the floor before you commit to the other materials.
