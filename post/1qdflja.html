Freeze mechanic feels inconsistent with its description?

I’ve been testing Freeze in PoE 2 and I’m noticing some behaviors that seem inconsistent with its description (unable to move or act / Action Speed = 0).

A few reproducible examples:

  • Residual movement while frozen: If an enemy is running, charging, or leaping toward the player and gets frozen mid-action, it becomes visually frozen but can still slide forward due to momentum and collide with / displace the player (no damage, but physical interaction still happens).
  • Persistent rotating attacks: Enemies with rotating blades or similar AoE attacks stop moving when frozen, but the rotating hitboxes continue to apply effects like Bleeding.
  • Channeled or continuous spells: If an enemy is channeling a continuous attack (e.g. flame breath) and gets frozen, the enemy model stops but the spell continues dealing damage and DoTs.

From the description, I would expect Freeze to interrupt ongoing actions and prevent any enemy-bound effects from continuing.
