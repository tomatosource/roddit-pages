Feedback on passive tree - particularly warrior Kiss/Curse nodes

I preface this by saying im a big PoE2/GGG fan and especially of jonathan and mark, I really dont want anyone using this feedback as an excuse to flame them, they clearly put inordinate amounts of time and effort and passion into this game. But i think this is currently one of my biggest gripes with the game at this time, and while it doesnt stop me from enjoying it i’d like to give feedback hoping for change.

During TalkativeTri’s video today Jonathon basically said they didnt feel like they had recieved a ton of negative feedback around the situation with the warrior tree and made a comment around wanting to adress complaints around a percieved imbalance by adding more downsides or kiss/curse nodes to the rest of the tree. I think this would be a big mistake, and this is me providing some feedback.

My anecdotal experience from watching numerous streams and spending a lot of time observing chat ingame is that these nodes are universally poorly recieved by the community, and that this was the case at release and has only gotten more negative/widespread since then. People havnt adjusted their expectations, people havnt accomodated to the change, it remains something that people emphatically do not enjoy.

I know for a fact that there are a number people who do not play warrior specifically because of them. I worry that of they became widespread not only would it be generally poorly recieved but that some people may even stop playing all together. I think most people are supportive of keystones having downsides, infact i’d argue they are necessary - key stones should massively alter the game mechanics and that requires changes and balancing. But me grabbing a little flat damage or aoe shouldnt.

I think part of the problem is that not only are they more frequent on the warrior tree, they often affect life/defenses and attack speed. Life generally doesnt feel great right now, particulalrly next to ES, and armour is also not incredible so these defensive nerfs hurt. Attack speed is always a tier 1 mod for melee, it doesnt matter if your big, slow, unga bunga slam wipes a entire screen, it feels garbage if its slow, regardless of damage, and the attack speed nerfs on the tree are intense - this is only worse when spellcasters and ranged attacks also wipe whole screens with bonkers action speed. So two of the most pressured parts of a warrior build are being made worse with almost every notable.

This feels awful, especially with the tree already feeling so weak. Jonathan points out the upsides are much numerically larger to compensate which isnt wrong but I posit that, from a psychological perspective, it doesnt matter - no matter how wonderful a meal is, if I know the chef spat in it while cooking I dont want to eat it. If a keystone existed that nerfed all nodes/notables by 50% but removed the downside i bet it would be taken like 99% of the time by 99% of the player base playing in the warrior area. So i would advocate GGG reconsider and look for further feedback around these nodes and if not remove them altogether than reduce the frequency and severity of the negatives. In general i think the tree feels weak, but at the very least i shouldnt feel like my character ‘feels’ worse after grabbing a notable cluster even if technically my numbers are better.
