Random crossbow reload is bad design

IMO the two nodes Reusable Ammunition and Efficient Loading are badly designed.

Crossbows are meant to be the gun archetype. The part that makes them feel like that are:

  • They fire instantly on click.
  • You plan around their reload.

However the two most powerful reload nodes in the tree completely break that fantasy.

&nbsp;

In the case of shotguns, it feels great to time your clicks around the reload time of the crossbow. But when you have those two nodes allocated, you can’t react or plan around that timing. It leads to the optimal gameplay being “hold down right click”.

Alternatively you could time your clicks early to try to hedge on the timing by “pre-clicking”. But that feels terrible because you lose the feeling of the instant feedback on click. It just becomes an inconsistent bow.

In the case of anything with a larger clip it’s the same. Instead of holding it down for a fixed time and planning around the reload, the optimal play is just to hold it down and ignore the reload.

&nbsp;

The biggest problem is most builds can’t afford to skip those nodes. Those two nodes give the equivalent of 200% (+ 30% for small nodes) effective reload speed, and the only other reload nodes in the tree give 70% for 3 nodes. You’re basically forced to take the random reload to be optimal.

I’m sure some people like those nodes, but to me it really removes what makes crossbows unique and enjoyable.
