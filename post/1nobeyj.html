So, I reached lvl 90 on ssf arc sorc

And today is d4 s10, so I take a poe2 break. As a noob in the ggg universe I think as follows:

  1. I really liked the game this league, 0.1 and 0.2 was a chore, but now my knowledge is better.
  2. I hated campaign before, now I really enjoyed it, perhaps not the grassy interlude map. I got a +3 to skills staff right after Viper and from there it was a breeze.
  3. I had good feeling of progression and some wow-moment with ground loot and vendor loot
  4. Too much visual clutter that makes you vulnerable for all the Dot damage on ground. I like to see what kills me
  5. Abyss, medium rewarding, the big monster is too overturned, it’s harder to kill than end of map boss. And again, the combination of darkness and green is awful
  6. Ssf needs more love. Campaign was really good item wise, but I have hardly upgraded my items since t6 maps.
  7. There is good crafting system now, but the crafting currency is to scarse for a ssf player. I now have 35 normal exalts, and 1-2 of most omens. Don’t have more than 4 of the perfect essences, even if I have “increased chance to contain essences” so I ha ent used omens for crafting
  8. Very important, my game is actually playable now, I can do both rituals and breaches without lag-death, that’s really nice!
  9. The endgame with mapping and towers is kind of boring, skills and itemization is awsome

Well, I’ll be back to poe2 when I get bored of d4, see you in a couple of weeks. Then I will start trading and learn crafting, I look forward to that! Stay sane
