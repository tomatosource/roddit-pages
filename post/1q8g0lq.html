Jayson Tatum’s shot selection moving forward

Do you guys think he’ll keep improving on his shot selection when he returns? JB has gotten to the point where he’s so much more deliberate about getting to his spots and you see the level up. I think Tatum was starting to do that more last year, but he would still take questionable low percentage shots at times. I’m not even worried about his athleticism because he was never the most hyper athletic player anyway. I hope he’s been using his time away to focus on his recovery, but also polish his shot diet. He really just needs to develop close mid range shots from 10-15ft and make them half the time. If he does that, the JB/JT tandem will be beyond insane and unguardable.
