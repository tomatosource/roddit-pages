The Celtics are 15th in 3P% (36.6%) despite being 1st in 3PA (50.5). They shot 16-54 from 3 (29.6%) in tonight’s loss against the Pacers.

The Celtics have built a reputation of being the best 3pt shooting team. Payton Pritchard recently claimed that they shouldn’t be taking less threes because they’re “the best at doing it”.

However, this doesn’t seem to be the case this season as they are 15th in 3P% (36.6) despite being 1st in 3PA (50.5), taking about 7 more threes than the Bulls who are shooting the 2nd most 3PA (43.7). Is this just a shooting slump they can climb out of or should they reconsider the amount of threes they’re taking?
