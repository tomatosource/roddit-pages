Hyped for the tribal theme Act 4

Kamil’s interview and really got me thinking about what we’re getting in 0.3.0. He mentioned the new boss music being tribal multiple times, with heavy drums, layered vocals, and even dynamic shifts depending on the phase of the fight. It sounds like these bosses are deeply rooted in some kind of ancestral theme, which lines up surprisingly well with what we saw in the PoE2 art book. The part on Act 4 describes the Karui Archipelago as a group of islands filled with diverse tribes and new Kalguuran settlers. There’s one boss in particular that stood out to me. Covered in bone armor and war paint, it looked unlike anything else we’ve seen so far in the game.

We already know 0.3 is adding a new act. And with all this tribal buildup in both music and concept art, I’m really hyped for the tribal theme, and hoping we’ll see mechanics that reflect that setting. Maybe spirit-infused fights or trials tied to ancestral themes. Would be cool to explore those island biomes in a more dynamic way too.

DarthM also called 0.3 probably the biggest update the game has had. That might be a bold claim since there are only 2 major patch for POE2 so far, but after everything we’ve seen and heard lately, I can’t help but feel excited.
