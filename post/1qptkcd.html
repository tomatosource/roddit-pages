Dear diary, I leeched a vaal temple for the first time…

and wow, I have never felt so insignificant and so poor. I was in awe yet disgusted, as I felt like an ant trailing a god while my eyes and ears were overloaded with the brightest lights and most orgasmic amount of tinks I have ever heard in my life. I thought “how many hours of gameplay would it take me to ascend to such godhood?”, but then I briefly remembered the story of Icarus, the feeling of the spring breeze and smell of grass, and my already diminishing hairline.

Anyways, that is all..

-sincerely

a new poe player
