Amen, Ausar Thompson to Consider Playing for Jamaica Basketball in Olympics, FIBA

Source: https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25199233-amen-ausar-thompson-consider-playing-jamaica-basketball-olympics-fiba

The twins have only just started the passport process and there is a long way to go, especially with FIBA rules in the way that limit naturalized players (these may change shortly as multiple nations, including Jamaica, Nigeria and the Philippines are pushing for a shift). But both are interested, and could be joined by multiple other current NBA players (Norman Powell, Nick Richards, Isaiah Stewart, and Josh Minott) if they do.
