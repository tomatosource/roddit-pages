With his historic defense, how good of a bat would Patrick Bailey need to accrue MVP-level WAR?

Patrick Bailey was worth 32 FRV last season, an unbelievable amount for someone who only caught a thousand innings.

Extended to a full 162 games and 1400 innings caught, he could have been worth 45 FRV last season.

Unfortunately for Bailey, he doesn’t quite know how to hit baseballs. He had a 70 wRC+ with a sub .300 xwOBA last season, and he was 1st percentile in batting run value on savant.

Despite being the single worst batter in baseball, he was worth 3.2 fWAR: someone you’d absolutely want on your team.

My question is this: if Bailey was able to maintain his defensive ability over 162 games, how good of a bat would he need to be worth 8 fWAR?

Edit: I think I’ve solved my own question.

Assuming 162 games, 1458 innings caught, 2025 Bailey would be worth…:

23.3 replacement runs

49.9 defensive runs

Which is roughly 73 runs altogether over a full season, ignoring the offense. With a league average bat (0 offensive RAA) and assuming 10 runs per WAR, Bailey would have been worth 7.3 fWAR.

Now we need to find what batting he needs to translate to 7 RAA over 162 games to get him to 8 fWAR.

Going back to 2025, Willy Adames was worth 7.1 runs over 686 PA’s. He had a 108 wRC+ with a .225/.318/.421 slashline.

There’s the answer! Patrick Bailey would need a roughly 110 wRC+ or a .230/.320/.420 slashline over a full season to be worth MVP-level WAR, assuming indestructible knees.
