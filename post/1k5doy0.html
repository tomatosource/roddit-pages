Skills that need a total overhaul

So as we know right now many skills aren’t worth using as they would take immense effort to make work at the same level as a basic lighting spear or lightning arrow build.

I’m here to ask what skills are immensely underpowered or simply require way to much setup to make work and should just get an overhaul/rework. I’ve dabbled in most of the classes getting to at least early maps but have predominantly played Mercenary and the specific offender I have in mind with this post is… Ice Shards. For this skill to work properly requires the “ice mines” shot on the ground to grow for one full second before reaching max damage… not happening, likely monsters step on this before this timeframe and even if you can get this you’ll likely only hit 3-5 monsters with a full clip. Not only is weird kiting necessary but the damage is not all that great with terrible AOE.

I know the intention from Devs is combo combat and maybe this can work with glacial bolt but at that point why even bother, just blast the ice wall with frag rounds for immediate kills instead of some unreliable kiting. Skill needs a total rework

What other skills in your opinion should be totally overhauled?
