Warrior Q… again

I’ve got a titan warrior build. I’m level 85, 2k life (low, I know), resistances maxed to 76%. Amour at 81% (spent 5-8 divs or something to get this). I even jacked up spirit via an amulet so I can get three spirit gems running, providing various health/heal buffs etc running, which got me from T9 to T10 very easily. Hulking Form was also a massive help (underrated point tbh). Damage is mostly fine (21K dual, 34k single hand mace) and can deal with pretty much everything - if, that is, I get enough time. What I can’t deal with is some hits. I don’t know what type of damage they are doing, but some mobs (not all) just nuke me into the ground before I can effectively close with them. One of the worst things to deal with are the essence bosses, especially if they are multiple essences. They destroy me, even though I have resistances maxed: ice and fire damage from these hit really, really hard.

I spent a decent sum for the level I’m at. I’ve done the things I’m supposed to i.e. max resists. I’ve tried to increase life, but to get gear that gives high life while also maxing resist is beyond what I have. I’ve tried to put passives into life stats. Yet, I get basically mowed down by trash white mobs. What is the point of this? How do I fix this?

(BTW, I have tried a blood mage. It was a total cakewalk compared to warrior, so easy it was boring, so I went back to my warrior. A ranger also was easy. This is because with any ranged character, you can avoid practically ALL melee hits, and just skate around (thus avoiding many ranged hits too) while still dealing damage. In my opinion, warriors need a big buff to general tankiness)
