What actually makes a player a “journeyman” in the NBA?

Been talking about this with people lately and I feel like everyone has a different definition, so I’m curious where you all draw the line.

For example:

  • Clear case: Rajon Rondo – 9 different teams, played key roles on multiple rosters, moved around a lot later in his career.
  • Iffy case: Shaquille O’Neal – Obviously had an illustrious career in LA, but bounced around a lot (4 teams) in his last few seasons, but was a superstar for most of his career. Does that late-career hopping make him a journeyman or not?

Two questions for you:

  1. What’s your minimum criteria for a player to be considered a journeyman? (Number of teams? Years in the league? Role?)
  2. What’s a successful journeyman career in your eyes? Is it just getting a ring, or can multiple deep playoff runs or Finals appearances be enough without a championship?

Would love to hear examples you think are right on the borderline too.
