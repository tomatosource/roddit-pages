Praise where praise is due

Heat fan here. As I await the day where our rivalry is brought back from the ashes, I have to give credit where credit is due. Brad Stevens has built an impressive well oiled machine in your team this year and going forward. There’s been plenty of things that have contributed to a very competitive rivalry between us in the past but I think the three main things that have stuck out to me are: coaching, player acquisition and player development. When most organizations may overlook, our front offices capitalize on finding underrated player value. Players like Queta, Scheierman, Walsh, González stand out to me the most. It’s honestly impressive the value that Mazzulla and your front office have brought out of them this year. With the looming return of Tatum and the addition of Vuc, I’m very confident to say yall are top tier contenders this year, if you weren’t already before. In the past three years I have been very impressed with Joe Mazzulla and I already recognize him as one of the best in the league, if not the best. The discipline and effort that he gets from each and every player on y’all’s team is remarkable. I will never forget where I was May 27th in 2023 when Derrick White forced Game 7 with the incredible buzzer beater put back. Plays like that do not go unnoticed and it’s great to know at least one team in this rivalry is serious about winning.
