Will we ever see another 100+ points+rebounds+assists (PRA) game?

The stat is pretty simple: take a player’s points, rebounds, and assists and add them all together. There have been a total of nine 100+ PRA games in NBA history. Unsurprisingly, Wilt Chamberlain has eight of them. Here are those ridiculous stat lines:

100/25/2 (127) 78/43/1 (122) 73/36/0 (109) 68/34/2 (104) 58/42/4 (104) 62/37/3 (102) 56/45/0 (101) 59/35/6 (100)

Those were all done between 1960-1968.

Elgin Baylor has the other one, which he did on December 8, 1961. That stat line was:

63/31/7 (101)

Amazingly, the Baylor game was against Wilt, where Wilt put up his second highest PRA ever (78/43/1). Imagine being at that game. The Lakers won, 151-147. Jerry West also put up 32/11/10. Wilt and Baylor took a combined 117 shots between the two of them. Oh, and this a few months before Wilt’s 100 point game, so he also set the (at the time) all-time single game scoring record here.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/196112080PHW.html

Anyway, the next highest non-Wilt instances I could find were:

Baylor again, 71/25/1 (97) Michael Jordan, 69/18/6 (93) Joel Embiid, 70/18/5 (93) Luka Doncic, 73/10/7 (90) Donovan Mitchell, 71/8/11 (90) David Robinson, 71/14/5 (90)

I think that’s everyone over 90, but let me know if I missed anyone.

So will we ever see 100 again?
