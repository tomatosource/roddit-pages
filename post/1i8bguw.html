r/bostonceltics will be banning direct twitter/x links moving forward

Hey All,

Effective immediately, the mods will be removing the ability to provide direct links to twitter/X from the sub. In an effort to be fair about this, we provided the community the opportunity to vote on this decision. From the votes cast, the community overwhelmingly chose to remove direct access.

https://preview.redd.it/g6xag8smmsee1.png?width=903&format=png&auto=webp&s=934b504a5c7f932919a37593927e3d4c48e21f8a

What does this mean going forward?

  • The automod will remove any direct links to Twitter/X
  • The automod will allow photos/screengrabs of tweets that use the flair “News” or “Highlight”

The mod team recognizes that the NBA, our Boston Celtics, and several beat writers still use Twitter/X exclusively. So the sub will allow photos/screenshots of tweets in order to continue to allow updates from those sources. We believe this is a fair compromise on the matter, and is a way to mitigate ‘censorship’ from these sources. Other sources such as BlueSky, mirror links, or other sites should still be accessible via direct link if this is the direction a user chooses to use.

We recognize some of the concerns about allowing screenshots, and that these photos could be altered/faked to spread disinformation. While we will work to pick these out and remove them on our own, we also expect the community to report any screengrabs that have been altered so they can be removed. The mod team will monitor if, and how often, this may occur before considering a full ban. For now we consider this the best compromise.

We appreciate the community feedback and participation. The mod team did not think it was fair to make a unilateral decision on this, and your participation was greatly appreciated. We will continue to monitor this decision and its impact on the sub over the next few weeks to determine if additional changes are necessary.

We’ve turned the comments off on this post in order to avoid it acting as an opportunity for people to get into a political argument. But if you have concerns we encourage you to message the mod team.

Thank you,
Boston Celtics Mod Team
