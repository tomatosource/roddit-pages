I enjoy early game more than endgame

Might be just me, but I really enjoy the game during the first story run, where dodging and stuff actually seems to matter. It’s like I’m playing isometric dark souls.

But then I hit maps and the game turns into vampire survivor…I want my skill comboes to be relevant even at the end rather than end up using one skill to just melt the whole map and then get oneshot by a random thing I can’t see.

The pacing changes so drastically around the final chapters, it’s quite a shame.

No other boss was as fun as the first act boss for me, and it’s a shame we move from the corrupted medieval european vibes (reminded me of Berserk a bit) and back to desert and jungle as is par for this genre.
