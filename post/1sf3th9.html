You’re Neemias Queta’s agent. What is your goal for your client this offseason?

To not mislead anyone i want to preface this little post by stating that Queta has a team option, is NOT a free agent this summer and will be a Celtic next season barring some unforeseen blockbuster.

&nbsp;

That being said, real free agency in today’s NBA is basically dead. The majority of players seem to get extended. I imagine both sides would want to get a deal done this summer. I think it’s likely he gets a 10-15 million dollar raise for next season (not to mention the additional years)

Or what if people in Queta’s circle/family convince him to wait until the following summer to test the market? It’s a gamble we don’t see often but I thought to mention it.

There could be a wacky middle ground where his agent pushes for a short term immediate payday like 2 years 40 million, kicking the team option down the road by a year
