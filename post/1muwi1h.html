What moments in specific games could have potentially changed a player’s or teams’ legacy if the result went the other way?

2012 ECF Game 6- Imagine if Lebron does not go god mode and the Celtics win. Lebron gets ripped even more than in 2011. His legacy might have been done. That game changed everything for him. Lebron probably does not become the Lebron we knew if they lost that game 6.

2013 finals game 6- Ray Allen misses the shot. I don’t think Lebron gets in the GOAT convo, even if we assume he still gets the 2016 and 2020 rings. Lebron would go on to be 3-7 in finals. Lebron also would never have even repeated as a champion. Lebron still goes down as a Mount Rushmore player, but he wouldn’t be top 2. I can see him ending up as #3 or 4 all-time but not top 2. Duncan, in the meanwhile, gets a legacy boost for having a 6-0 finals. Duncan would be ranked higher, possibly in the top 5. Duncan may have also won Finals MVP. Why did you bench him Pop?

2016 game 7- Imagine if Kyrie misses the shot and the Warriors find a way to win. I feel like it would be seen as Steph’s league. Even though Steph was bad in the finals and Draymond would likely win Finals MVP, I feel like this would have become Steph’s league. Next would be, does KD join the Warriors? If KD still joins, then I don’t see any other change, other than Lebron having one less ring. So, Lebron would not be in the GOAT convo in that case. Lebron may end up being #3 or 4, but not the GOAT. However, if KD does not join, then Lebron likely wins in 2017 and 2018, which would give him an even better GOAT argument.

1989 FR game 5- What if MJ misses the shot? This would not be seen as the all-time performance. The Cavs may stick together, so who knows what happens? MJ would have lost in the first round in four out of five seasons, so I wonder how that would be perceived.

1988 Finals Game 6- The phantom foul is not called on Laimbeer, and the Pistons go on to win. Isiah’s performance (25 points in the third quarter while on a sprained ankle) may be a top 5 performance all time instead of being lost in the mix. Pistons, not the Bulls, become the first team since Bill Russell’s Celtics to 3-peat. Isiah in particular gets a legacy boost.

1994 Finals Game 6- Hakeem Jon Stark gets off the shot before Hakeem gets the block. If it goes in, the Knicks get their ring. Ewings’ legacy takes a boost. Starks shot would probably be considered an all-time shot instead of being known as the disaster i game 7.

2002 WCF Game 4- Robert Horry misses the buzzer beater. Kings go on to win their first and only ring. Legacy boost for Chris Webber.

2019 ECSF Game 7- Kawhi misses the buzzer beater. Toronto does not get its one ring. Does Embid get his first ring or does Giannis get two rings?

2021 Game 7- KD’s toe is not on the line. KD gets his third ring and first without the Warriors. Big legacy boost for KD.

Any more you can think of?
