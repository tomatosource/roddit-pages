There has been one team in the last 50 years to win a ring as a non top 3 seed, the 1995 houston rockets

Sometimes you hear the narrative that seeding doesn’t matter. In the NBA really good teams have really good really regular season records. The vast majority of the NBA champions are the no. 1 seed. Slightly fewer are 2 seeds. There are a few 3 seeds.

Other than than 95 rockets who were the 6th seed and the 69 celtics who were a 4 seed there has been no champion with greater than a 3 seed.
