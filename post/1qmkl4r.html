Trade deadline is about 10 days away, this is around the time when stuff really starts to go down; where are we all at? What do we want?

Just wanted to do a sub check in and see where everyone’s heads are at as far as the trade deadline is concerned. It’s right around the corner and this is around the time when things actually start to happen so I figured we could have one last chance to predict, submit our final wish lists, or just speculate league wide.

Personally I still believe we have to do something if we want to actually have a shot at winning a title. I’m not certain just going after a backup will be enough, Queta/garza has been a nice surprise but im not convinced either will hold up the same come playoff time. A huge part of our championship team was having no weak links, and I think we’re leaving a potential weak link open by standing pat.

My ideal scenario, like I’m sure many others, is Zubac. He’s big, strong, great defensive big and great rebounder, sets awesome screens, smart enough to make decisions with the ball, can score a little bit in the paint, and is on a great contract. Not sure if the clips are gonna play ball but I’d absolutely do Simon’s and 2 firsts for him, 2 firsts sound scary but he would literally shore up the center position for the remainder of the jays prime because he’s the same age as them.

Lesser ideal scenario would be Claxton, but I’d only do one first for him I think. I don’t trust him like i trust Zubac, he’s got some things about him that are a little hazy. Free throw shooting all of a sudden isn’t a problem anymore but who knows, that could become an issue again. The other glaring problem with him is he’s neither super tall nor super strong; I feel like you have to be 1 of the 2 to hold up defensively as a 5 through entire playoff runs. He’s still very good, amazing switch defender, really good passer, capable ball handler, and solid lob threat.

-I’ve floated sabonis(deep down I still kinda fuck with the idea but yall are haters so I’ll stay in the closet on this one) but most people don’t like the idea which is fair.

-JJJ is another option but he’s even worse than sabonis financially and will be making 50 mill a year over the next 4 years(only way this would happen is if Brad was out on Derrick or thinks he’s gotta sell high, I don’t think that’s the case tho)

-assuming we don’t stand pat, the final decent scenario is just getting a solid backup by using our first this year(which is very expendable bc of the pels 2nd we also own)

-good backup names for me are Jaylin Williams, Jalen smith, yves missi, rob williams(wouldn’t do the first but I miss him), nick richards, oso

One last scenario I’ll propose as a weird future flier idea that I’ve liked for awhile, it’s basically standing pat for this year and gearing up for the future but if the mavs found themselves basically out on lively because of the health stuff I wonder if they’d consider giving him up for our first this year plus like Baylor or something, just an idea I kinda liked as a low ish risk high reward

What do we think? Anything stand out to you guys?
