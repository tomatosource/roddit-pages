T7 vs T15 Rarity Farm

https://preview.redd.it/mngr4xnzcewf1.png?width=708&format=png&auto=webp&s=04c140b3231a5b367a1d91a42bedfa7a8326ac20

Tested some t7 farming tonight and compared it to t15 farm. Ran 20 maps of each.

Waystone setup:
-6 mod -&gt; chaotic rarity (no desecration)
-1x paranoia + 2x fear

Tablet setup:
-3x Rare Monsters (total 109% increased for each run)
-1x irradiation tablet, 2x breach tablet*
*I did not run chance for extra breaches, but I had the Crumbling Walls node on–ended up with 36 breaches in the t7 maps and 33 breaches in the t15 maps

Character setup:
-200% character rarity
-Solo (no leech, no aura bot)

Atlas setup:
-standard rarity farming atlas
-local knowledge OFF, Remnants of Power and Unstable Energies both ON*
*Overall, most of the runs ended at around 180-200% in both map rarity and rare monsters w/ 4% quant from the tree

Map clear method:
-Cleared every rare and boss on each map and completed all breaches and abysses

Result:
-T7: 299 exalt, 65 chaos, 1 annul, 6 divine, 3 orb of chance, 1 greater exalt (!)
-T15: 197 exalt, 42 chaos, 1 annul, 7 divine, 5 orb of chance, 7 greater exalt, 1 perfect exalt, 1 greater chaos
-T7 maps found roughly 50% more exalts and chaos orbs with 1 fewer divine.
-T15 maps notably had a perfect exalt drop

Comments:
To start off, sample size is small. Farham will probably release a video soon with a better dataset and controls (see notes on breach # and atlas procs above).

With that aside, I think the findings support the idea that you can “block” the greater/perfect exalt/chaos with the lower tier waystone. The question is… was I just lucky with the perfect exalt drop? Hard to say with the small sample size and potential for variance. I will add that it took me about 1hr 15min to clear the 20 t15 maps and very close to 1hr clear the 20 t7 maps. Maybe t7s can be a viable strategy if you go fast given how close the divine drops were.
