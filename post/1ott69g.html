Un-juiced Maps should drop Divine Orbs 25% of the time

I believe regular 6 (or even 5 affixed) maps, undistilled and not overrun, should drop one Divine every four Maps on average. Not Divine equivalent- I mean a raw Divine. Oh, and I also mean with 0% Rarity on your gear. I have a couple reasons why, so let me know what you guys think:

1.“This late into the League”

I hear this phrase all the time, and it’s really a shame this is something that can discourage people to play even if they missed the start of the League. Anyone starting the League late has to deal with astronomical trade prices when starting to min/max their character, and that’s just an unfortunate fact. What really rubs salt in the wound though is that mapping itself, which is supposed to be THE endgame activity, isn’t even recommended to gain currency “this late into the League”. You’ll be directed to Sekhemas/Chaos Trials to earn currency which really sucks if you’ve leveled up your character all the way up just to be told you can’t even participate in the endgame while you farm Trials for 40 hours first. No one who started the League Day 1 has to do that, so neither should Late starters. Mapping itself should give you some Divines even when you’re not fully maxed out enough to start juicing.

  1. The Meta

Another reason is because not everyone plays meta builds. If you decide to have fun by building your own character, you should still be able to progress even if it’s at a slower pace. It’s harder shoving the “mandatory” 150% Rarity on your gear when your build needs those affixes (the same goes for negative rarity when you need those ring slots). I think getting one Divine every 4 maps for non-meta builds would help them participate in crafting, and help put items up for other non-meta players. 70% of the player base played the same 3 Ascendancies, but having currency still drop, but at a slower pace, would encourage people to play other things while GGG figures out balance.
