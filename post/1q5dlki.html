GGG please fix abyss loot

Abyss loot has been broken since the start of 0.4.

Every time you enter an Abyss map, the game seems to use the map seed to pick one of three special Abyss rare types, and then only that type spawns for the entire map. No variety, no randomness. This leads to some really bad outcomes:

1) Serpent-type rares (self-revive once)
These are generally the best outcome. They mostly drop currency and actually feel rewarding to fight.

2) Centaur-type rares inside the Amanamu void
These are the only Abyss monsters that can drop Abyss currency.
However, if you kill them inside the Amanamu mist, they only drop Omen of Liege.

This is terrible design:

  • You get punished for interacting with the mechanic the intended way (stepping into the mist to fight them).
  • The “optimal” strategy becomes kiting them out of the mist, which:
    • Is buggy as hell (they sometimes die while still protected).
    • Encourages passive, wait-and-kite gameplay.
    • Completely screws melee builds.

3) Centaur-type rares with Naruto kage bunshin clones
These mainly drop gold, making them the worst outcome.
Ironically, in my experience they’re also the most dangerous Abyss rares.

So the hardest enemies drop the worst loot. Again, bad design.

TL;DR:

  • Abyss loot is effectively deterministic per map due to seed-locked rare types.
  • The system is counter-intuitive, punishing players for engaging with the mechanic as designed.
  • It promotes passive gameplay and disproportionately hurts melee.
  • The most dangerous Abyssal rares dropping gold is just bad balance.
