Builds that play nice with a controller

So first just to get it over with. I love playing with keyboard and mouse. Playing with a controller is mostly to get less repetitive stress on my body, + it can be quite chill.

So with that out of the way there are some builds that play well on the controller and some that are a giant pain to pull of due to inferior control.

The typical builds that work great are builds that rely on just attacking in a straight line in front of your character. These can be most monk builds (that I’ve played), builds like self exploding gas arrows, grenades etc..

Builds that to me feel like a giant hassle on a controller are builds that require you to cast effects in a more strategic way. Like if you want the enemy or your summons to cross a Flame Wall and not just cast it directly on top of where the closest enemy stood at the time of casting. I’m not saying such builds are completely unplayable on a controller, they just require a lot more effort then what they do when you got access to a mouse. And I rather not feel like I’m battling against the controller when I play, I want a smooth experience.

So can anyone recommend me which builds they like to play on a controller? Doesn’t matter if I’ve already mentioned them, or even If I mention that I don’t think they feel great on controller. I’m open to all controller build opinions.
