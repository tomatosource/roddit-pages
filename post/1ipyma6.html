By my calculations, at maximum you can have exactly 126 minions. PoB included.

Disclaimer: This is a theorycraft build with the sole goal of having the most number of minions possible. I don’t even own PoE2.

tl;dr: Stack spirit and life (Infernalist can convert life with Beidat’s Will).
Use some reservation reducers like Lord of Horrors, Bones of Ullr, and Matsya Quarterstaff.

124 Skeletal Warriors (Clerics will work too)
1* Infernal Hound.

Edit: I got it up to 127 Skeleton Warriors (+3) by swapping to a SW tree cluster with less minion bonuses. So the new total is 128 minions. New PoB link

Note: PoB has some bugs like: not reducing the helm attribute requirement with the rune, and gem supports not adding their +5 Int required.
