Thoughts on the team before the all-star break

Tell me if any of my takes are inaccurate

Peyton Pritchard - Happy that he was able to step up in Tatum’s absence. I was curious to see if he would go back to the bench when Tatum returns but that question was answered when Ant got traded. Imma need him to show up in the playoffs though he usually disappears then outside of half court bombs.

Derrick White - Derrick is beginning to feel more and more like Marcus Smart everyday. He has SO many ups and downs , but my guy is so valuable defensively. I expect him to be a little more consistent when Tatum gets back but other than that I’m willing to live with all that he offers.

Nemmias Queta - I’m SO glad Joe gave Queta a shot this year , although it was slim pickens to be honest. Unless I’m forgetting someone I feel like he is our best Rebounder since since Jared Sullinger. He is a major upside defensively and I feel as if he earned his starting spot.

Baylor Scheierman- He’s continuing to gradually grow from last year. Think he will be a solid rotation player. Idk why I thought he was shorter. Turn out he’s a whole 6’6. He puts in effort on the defensive end and is probably the best shooter out of our young guys.

Jordan Walsh - it was looking spooky for the first couple of years but admittedly he was playing behind two all NBA forwards. I was nervous he would be our next Romeo Langford or James Young but not only is his on ball defense elite he’s a decent shooter as well. I’m looking forward to the evolution of his game.

Jaylen Brown - So JB has been my favorite Celtic since Kyrie left.He was my favorite because of the whole underdog thing he had going on. He would always put in work in the off season and come back with something new added to his game. He would also show more passion than Tatum in my opinion. Him playing at this level is awesome. I could see the scoring average being a thing but the efficiency is shocking. At this point I’ve come to terms with his turnover issues since he produces so much on both ends.

Hugo Gonzalez - He is a MANIAC on defense. He seems to be flying everywhere and is making his presence felt on that end. I think Walsh is a better on ball defender but Hugo takes the cake for off ball in my opinion. Can’t wait to see him continue to grow.

Luka Garza - The man with the forever green light from the top of the key. Luka is a SOLID player. He’s a menace on the boards and can spread the floor. I’m not sure where he fits with the recent trade and the potential return of JT ,but hope he can still makes an impact.

Sam Hauser - SNIPER and doesn’t have as many lows as Derrick. He does ok on defense like but isn’t much of a rebounder. I’m ok with that when Tatum comes back or if Joe decides to do the double big thing.

Nicola Vučević - Glad to have him on the team. I felt we should have traded for him years ago but glad to get him for a low price. He brings everything Horford brought on offense but lacks a lot on defense. He is really good on the boards so that will be awesome. Excited to see how he pans out.

Anfernee Simmons - Solid scorer off the bench and I loved him attacking the basket. I low-key wanted to bring him back for a lower price but trading him away for a bigger need and to get under the tax appron will be worth it. I have PTSD from the “super small ball” lineups consisting of Carson Edwards, Marcus Smart , Kemba Walker, and insert small guard and Daniel Theis , so I’m not too mad to move on from a smaller guard.

Josh Minnott - I was a bigger fan of him at the beginning of the year before the other young guys showed the can do everything he can do . He was a good shooter and I liked him on guards and lighter Forwards but he would get pushed around EASILY once you put a stronger guy on him. His rebounding would suffer as well. I think he deserves a spot in this league as a forward and not a big.

Everybody Else - I was excited when we got Xavier Tillman. I thought he could be a smaller horford without the shot blocking but he could not shoot well enough to stay on the floor it seems. He seemed like a good locker room guy so I’ll miss having him on the team.Amari Williams is a big body but still needs some time in the oven.I thought Chris Boucher would be our starter coming into this year based on his experience but unfortunately for him our young guys all exceeded expectations. Haven’t seen Max on the court and Ron Harper Jr. looks solid.
