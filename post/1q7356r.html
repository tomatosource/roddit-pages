In contrast to the Hawks turning Luka Doncic into Corey Kispert & CJ McCollum, the Washington Wizards turned Bradley Beal into Trae Young, Bilal Coulibaly, 4 unprotected swaps & a Warriors 2030 1st. Among many other smaller assets along the way.

Here’s the quick rundown:

  • Bradley Beal gets traded in a 3-teamer that nets them Chris Paul (And some other players but they’re there to match salaries), 4 Suns swaps in even years from 2024-2030 & additional draft assets via Suns 2nd round picks
    • The Wizards would use some of these 2nds to move up from 8th to 7th to select Bilal Coulibaly
  • The Wizards would then flip Chris Paul for Jordan Poole, Ryan Rollins, Patrick Baldwin Jr., a 2027 2nd and a Top 20 protected 2030 1st from Golden State.
  • Jordan Poole would moved be with Bey & the 40th pick in the 2025 draft for CJ McCollum, Kelly Olynyk & a future 2nd in a 3-teamer involving the Rockets
    • Olynyk was later moved for Malaki Branham, Blake Wesley and a 2026 second-round pick
  • Just today, Corey Kispert & CJ McCollum were moved with no draft capital for 2x All-Star and 2nd biggest menace to New York ^((Second only to Spider-Man)) Trae Young

Not bad for a guy who became untradable 6 months after moving to Arizona. I’m sure there’s some trade exceptions I missed along the way (And they did mess up prematurely moving on from Ryan Rollins who is balling out for the Bucks) but the Beal trade has been a remarkably successful trade tree thus far for the former Baltimore sports team.
