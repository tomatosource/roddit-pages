An easy and less time-consuming way to make a so-so amount of currencies.

So I am not a big fan of rarity in gears.

But I am also a big fan of decorating my hideout and inviting people over.

I also work. I have to go to work from 7am to 6pm.

So I sell white bases.

And here is what I made in 2 hours selling my white bases.

I know it’s not a lot. But it is stable, and truly low effort. I play only 3 hours every working day, and weekend I play the whole 16 hours. So far, here is my gear:

https://preview.redd.it/6dw5eikay29g1.png?width=1329&format=png&auto=webp&s=2fe3eff812b979e5559c2af7904bde9442c83989

Hyrri’s ire I bought at 39 divs. The Ingenuity belt was 5 divs. My breach ring was 10 divs. The rest was 2-3 divs each. My LA dps in hideout is around 120k and 45% crit chance.

https://preview.redd.it/0h8kdaru039g1.png?width=596&format=png&auto=webp&s=fb44bb255418b004f8e92e20fff29c99d10de77d

I think the most effort I ever put in farming was customing my filter. You can easily do that with poe2filter online.

I also sell my tablets because I don’t put them in my maps except ones with increased quantity or more mobs.

Here is the lists of (white base) things that I sell, I figure it might be beneficial to someone who also wants to sell white bases but do not know what to sell:

Gold rings, gold amulets, amythest rings, prismatic rings, furry & spiny tailsmans, quivers, obliterator bows, lvl80 energy shield items, gold and thawing charms, level 5 support gems, tablets, white belts, exceptional bases.

The only caveat is sometimes the market fluctuate. So your things might not sell. Usually I wake up, check if they got taken. If they don’t within 8 hours, I lower the prices or just clear them off the selves. And then I go to work. And play in the evening. Rinse and repeat.

I sometimes look through the market to see if I miss out on any white bases worth selling.
