First time trading!

So I’m relatively new to the game and just destroyed the boss in act 3 and moved to act 4. (Proud of that).

The thing about trading is that most guides are misleading! You can trade (BUY) from other players before act 4 but you can’t SELL! You can understand how bad I felt when I found that out at the end of act 3.

So here I go and do my first trade:
my first trade was me looking for a wand as I needed to do more damage for my chaos witch build, I got into the trade page and all the good wands I liked costs around 50 exalted orbs (very expensive!!) I found a good wand that costs only 1 Divine orb which I happen to have 1 of. and I instantly bought it (VERY GOOD DEAL).

After a bit of careful research I found out about the currency exchange and how much I was overpaying for that amazing (not worth it) wand. So you can imagine how my day was ruined when I figured out that my only Divine orb was around 380 exalted orbs which would have been more than enough to upgrade my humble and fantastic build.

So if anyone is looking for a financial advice in this game you know where not to go!
