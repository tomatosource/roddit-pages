Kentavious Caldwell-Pope’s 3 year 66 million contract is quickly becoming one of the worst non-max contracts in the NBA. He is averaging 8.6 points on 41 FG% and 30% from 3 this season on the Orlando Magic. Did Denver dodge a bullet by not re-signing him?

So last summer KCP opted out of his player option and the Magic outbid the Nuggets to sign to to a 3 year 66 million dollar contract, and many at the time criticized Denver for being cheap. The Orlando Magic signed him for his shooting, but he is averaging just 8 points on 41% from the field, 30% from three and 54 TS% and has been disappointing. Other than some max contracts like Embiid/PG he is quickly becoming one of the worst contract in the NBA, and the Magic would probably even have to attach a pick to offload him. Now some say his decline is because he is not getting the “Jokic looks” but that is not true, as he was averaging close to 40% from three on various different teams before. Denver didn’t resign him because they thought 22 million a year was too expensive for him and they may have been right. However, he would still help them out a lot right now and fit better there with his defense and spacing, both areas which they struggle in. Did Denver make the right move by not re-signing him, or would he have still moved the needle for them right now?
