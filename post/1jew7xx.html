Ascendancy - illustrations

Hi,

I wanted to share my thoughts and hear your opinion. I’m really impressed by how much attention to detail GGG puts into their work. The assets are amazing—rich in detail and forming a cohesive whole. However, in some cases, the character class illustrations seem like a step backward compared to PoE1.

Take a look at the comparison below. In PoE2, in my opinion, the proportions of the characters’ silhouettes are worse, the illustrations appear less sharp, and the colors are overly uniform and too dark. On the plus side, the backgrounds are now more detailed.

I’m not an art critic, but there are a few aspects that, in my opinion, could be improved in the future…
