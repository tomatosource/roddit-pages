Resolutions to Movement Speed problems.

So I was thinking the other day, boots aren’t really boots without movement speed.
It’s not like we have quicksilver flasks to compensate for the lack of movement speed.
Also, with the upcoming changes to filters being able to track stats on unidentified items on the ground the options below make the most sense to me. Since most boots that don’t have MS will be filtered out immediately.

Here are a few resolutions I thought of:

Option 1.
All boots drop with 10% MS Implicit and you can increase that further with runes.
Lesser MS Rune: 10% MS
Normal MS Rune: 20% MS
Greater MS Rune: 30% MS
40% Total. Only one MS rune per boots.

Option 2.
All boots drop with MS Implicit based on Item Level:
1-30 = 20% MS
31-60= 30% MS
61-??=40% MS

Option 3.
Runes only.
Lesser MS Rune: 20% MS
Normal MS Rune: 30% MS
Greater MS Rune: 40% MS

Option 4.
Tie Movement Speed to how far you are in the campaign to make the campaign feel more rewarding and give that feeling that your character itself is growing stronger.
Or make the MS rewards quests?
20% MS Act 1. 30% MS Act 3 or 4. 40% MS end of Act 6.
Also make it tied to your account so once you complete this once, it’s the same for all new characters.
(Quality of life.)

Clearly options 1, 2 and 4 sound better than 3.
But I think we can all agree that boots without MS aren’t really boots at all.
Would be way more interesting to dedicate that prefix to other stats.
