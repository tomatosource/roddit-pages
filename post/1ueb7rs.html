Darryn Peterson is from Canton and has several Akron connections, the city with the most disproportionately high amount of NBA talent from there (5 Hall of Famers + more)

Darryn Peterson was born and raised in Canton, Ohio, which is about 20 miles south of Akron. Peterson spent his first two years in high school at an Akron-area private school, his older brother played football at a big-time high school program in Akron, and their father played for the Akron Zips. Most fans know that Akron is where two modern legends were born, but here’s a fuller list of talented players born in Ohio’s 5th-largest city (population around 190k).

  • LeBron James - future HOF-er

  • Steph Curry - future HOF-er

  • Nate Thurmond - HOF center, 60s/70s

  • Gus Johnson - HOF forward, 60s/70s

  • Bobby Jones - HOF forward, 70s/80s

  • Chuck Noble - 1x All-Star

  • Steve Johnson - 1x All-Star

  • Chuck Share - 1950 #1 overall draft pick

  • Alvin Robertson - 4x All-Star, 1986 DPOTY, from nearby Barberton

  • Ed Sadowski - elite pre-NBA center in 1940s

I’ll add that Thurmond and Johnson were teammates for two years at Akron’s Central High School, the only time that two future HOF-ers played together in high school. Also, the 4 largest cities in Ohio–Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, and Toledo–collectively are the birthplaces of zero Hall of Famers.
