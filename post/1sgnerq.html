The dire need of a manifesto from GGG and why it should be published before the league trailer.

PoE2 is having a directional choice to be made soon - community is split between people who think that the game should be gravitated towards what PoE1 experience is, and people who think opposite of that. (+ a minority who likes the in-between state of now).

0.5 will give a strong indication of what direction GGG wants to see the game going and most likely the last moment to make any drastic pacing changes, since the endgame is getting a huge rework/upgrade.

GGG in my opinion, needs to take the stance on what direction they want to take and a manifesto would be a great “tool” to share it and make it clear, what the current vision is. If changes get presented during trailer, it will ruin the expectations many have at the moment (and everyone expects something different, so noone will be satisfied). With clear statement it will be easier for people to at least grown used to the idea that developers try to achieve here.

So, well… GGG, please just set the tone for the next patch with some kind of clear message of what this game is supposed to be.
