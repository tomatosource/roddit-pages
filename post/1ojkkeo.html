Easy passive income strategy

Hey all, I know many of you simply think that the rich will always be rich and the rest will need to pray for rare divine “tinks” to upgrade their gear. This is not the case, strategy helps a ton. I am done profiteering for this season and want to share a simple strategy that still works right now.

For this strategy, you’ll want to at least have 10 div but can get started with less. We all know that blood mage is arguably the most powerful build this season and this build really only has one absolute requirement to get working. An undying hate with tecrod base.

Undying hate unidentified or non tecrod base is 2 divine orbs. Tecrod base is 7. What people don’t factor is that a non corrupted undying hate will change its entire base with a divine orb. So let’s say we buy 5 unid undying hate for 10 div. There’s a 1⁄3 chance of it identifying as tecrod. Let’s say we’re unlucky and we id only one tecrod. You sell the tecrod base for 7 divines or 5 div profit right away. Now you have 5 divines to slam the other bases for a 1⁄3 shot at hitting tecrod. Since the cost was 2 and sale is 7, your odds of hitting at least a 2d profit is pretty good. For the sake of argument we’ll do the profit on these odds.

2d unid hits tecrod on id = 5d profit 2d unid hits non T, 1 divine slam hits tec =4d profit 2d unid takes 2 div to hit tecrod = 3d profit 2d unid takes 3 div = 2d profit 2d unid takes 4 divs (statistically unlikely) 1d

Here you have invested 10 divs to start, then an additional 9 on rolling. You now have 35 divs worth of jewels. I usually never take more than 2 divs to hit tecrod and usually hit tecrod with unid often. For the sake of argument, say I buy 6 for 12. At 1⁄3, 2 hit immediately. Now I have 14 divs. The profit of 2 divs goes into the 4 additional non tec base for another 7. Say I take 2 divs each of the other 4, I now have a total of 12 initial investment, and 8 more to roll them. 20 divs for 42 divs earned. Not much gambling or bricks and is purely passive income.

Idk how this is still so profitable but early in the league a non tec base was about 6d and tecrod was 40d. I was making about 500 divs a day doing this. Only thing holding me back was lack of gold.

I’d love to teach everyone how to craft BM amulets and wands as they are simple but that learning curve is steep. This is simple and literally anyone with 3+ divs can try this. I’ve been sharing this strategy a lot lately so get it while you can. Just make sure it isn’t corrupted
