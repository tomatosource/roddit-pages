Sprinting is good, but needs some work

I like sprint to navigate the area. Its agood improvement there.

But the issues are plentiful. First having to roll and then sprint just feels very weird. This also means if you accidentally press your roll key too long you start sprinting which either causes you to go into a slow animation which you can cancel with another roll or you get hit and drop to the ground. I died quiet a bit because i accidentally rolled and then sprinted. Im just used to pressing that key longer. And its really hard to fight muscle memory.

Another issue with sprint is the Bumping into obstacles. That just feels weird. It also looks and feels clunky.

Sprinting control is also just wild. Your turning radius should be way steeper. Like a normal person would sprint. We are not running at 60 KMH. If we would then, that turn radius would make sense.
