Great win, but need to clean up the offense

We had a great win but the reality is that we really struggled half the game playing Celtics ball…

The first quarter was a mess, the Cavs just played worse. Our percentages were abysmal, we kept losing rebounds that should have been ours, and our offense looked lazy. We played great defense, and the Cavs just couldn’t hit anything, but it felt more like them playing bad than us playing well.

Pritchard was a great swap and was well timed as he gave us back that spring in our step and we managed fine after.

Once again, we basically lost a 20 point lead for no apparent reason. Thankfully we rallied back.

I will say, that the celtics had a long run of trying to buy time with leads towards ends of games, and it would always look like a race of if we can lose the lead faster than time running out. Here they played well until the end and didn’t stall.
