[Ohm Youngmisuk] A Rivalry, a ‘Bromance,’ and a Failed Reunion: Steph, KD, and LeBron Reunite for All-Star Weekend

Steph Curry on LeBron James’ 2008 NCAA Sweet 16 visit: “Even Davidson, the fan base, we all wore red ‘Witness’ T-shirts… [LeBron] gifted me a signed Cavaliers No. 23 jersey with the message ‘The Best Player in N.C.’ I still got it at my dad’s house in Charlotte.”

LeBron on their rivalry: “When you’re in a dogfight… you’re going to dislike that person. He disliked me, I disliked him because we were both fighting for… the Larry O’Brien Trophy.”

Curry on their evolved relationship: “There’s always respect. I didn’t like the player. The person’s always great. You just hate when he has a jersey on wearing No. 23.”

Steve Kerr on Durant’s rejection of a Warriors reunion: “Why would [KD] want to face all that B.S. again?… He took so much s— for [joining Golden State in 2016]. It’s like he still gets criticized.”

Jimmy Butler on pairing with Curry: “They say opposites attract… I don’t think I could be a better complement to him and vice versa.”

Curry on Butler’s impact: “Every possession just doesn’t feel as hard… This has a potential to be really, really fun.”

Golden State’s failed pursuits of LeBron and Durant gave way to Jimmy Butler’s arrival, revitalizing Curry and the Warriors (3-1 since the trade). Meanwhile, the trio of Curry, LeBron, and Durant—once bitter Finals rivals—embrace their “precious” All-Star reunion, acknowledging their careers’ twilight. As LeBron noted: “It’s coming to an end soon.”
