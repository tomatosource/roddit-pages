Trading situation

Hey everyone, before getting into the topic, I just want to say that I’m loving PoE2! I loved PoE1, and this one is not disappointing, except for one thing that most people seem to agree on: trading.

I just want to share my negative points and some solutions I came up with. I’d love to hear your thoughts on whether these would help or just bring more downsides.

1- No instant buyout. I know this is a common complaint, but my biggest issue with it is that for someone who works and only has around 2 hours to play per day, it’s really hard to be active in the market during peak hours. And it’s not just about buying but also selling. My stashes are full because I’m online so little that buyers rarely even see my listings.

2- Bots. This will always be a problem, but there are way too many of them. And it’s not just bots—people intentionally listing items for a lower price just to negotiate in-game is extremely frustrating.

My solution for this would be a “honor system”, where there’s a correlation between whispers and actual sales at the listed price. Players with a higher score would appear first in the trade market. I’m not sure if this would help, but it’s the only solution I could think of.

Let me know what you think, would this actually help, or would it make trading even worse?
