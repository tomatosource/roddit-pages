Is Volant Quiver underrated?

Since most people take the projectile speed converts to damage node on deadeye, I was playing around with PoB and was wondering if the arrow speed, which is basically 20-30% increased damage, is better than the critical hit chance from Visceral Quiver. I’m not too sure about the math exactly… has anyone looked into it?

Edit: I looked further into it on PoB, copying Fubgun’s build, and it does look worse for sure (-300 dps, 45% crit to 40% crit), so it’s definitely worse for crit builds. But for people using Primed Quiver / not playing crit, I checked and I believe Volant Quiver is much better when you’re not running crit (around -6k lost using the prime quiver). Funnily enough, if you look at the price of a magic i82 T1 Bow skills damage / +2 projectile, volant quiver is around 1d whereas Primed Quiver is around 3d.
