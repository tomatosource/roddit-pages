Pounce is the ideal skill design for PoE 2

Pounce is a complicated skill. It has 12 different gem tags which I think is the record. The skill does a bunch of stuff. You can use it as a gap closer and to move around. It applies a socketed mark with increased effect which is of course very useful. Non minion werewolf will use it with freezing mark to gain more damage. The built in predator mark will let you summon wolves. And the mark itself makes enemies take more damage from minions not scaling with gem level, so it’s useful on a minion build weapon swap just for damage. It’s a skill with lots of components, and because of that it can it can be used with lots of other skills.

And that’s how skills in PoE 2 should be. Complex with lots of components thereby enabling lots of interactions. Able to be used in lots of different skill sets. They shouldn’t combo just one way. For example people have rightfully complained the other werewolf skills feel on rails. Because they are designed in a straightforward simple manner. Lunar assault freezes. Shred makes ice fragments. Cross slash detonates them.

I want to see existing skills get additional effects. Stuff like the final strike of shred could inflict a powerful bleed. Many expected werewolf to involve bleed. This would give it another use and let it combo with bleed skills. Volcano could erupt jagged ground at max stages giving a purpose to channeling. Entangle could be flammable and ignite attached enemies. Whirling slash picks up spears from spearfield and swirls them around you. Wind blast shoots picks up ground effects for an elemental wave.

With more complicated skills you open up more builds. Plus you can end up layering combos. With the volcano example you could create jagged ground while slamming the volcano and then erupt it with the next cast. You can get more combos for the same amount of skills.
