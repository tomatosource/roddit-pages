How do you suppose GGG will handle the Templar, character wise?

POE 1 Templar

I’ve played POE1 for a while, and the two classes that stood out to me, story-wise, were the Muradar and the Templar. The Templar, both gameplay-wise and his in-game lines, were my favorite of the bunch. In the first game, the Templar was both resolute in faith and in spirit, although his faith did waver in certain times, especially after learning what his order really was and the truth behind his faith; his resolve remained absolute, and he still ventured forth to make things right. His reactions to certain scenes and dialogues made him feel the most human out of the bunch, and his piety was sincere. I hope the writers realize this and make him the polar opposite of the Mercenary, who is my current favorite character of the bunch, mainly because we share similar views, considering his criticism of religion. The Templar, in my honest opinion, is a great positive representation of a religious person in all of gaming. I hope most of the elements of his character from the previous game make it over to this one, but I welcome some evolution of his personality. Also, please bring back Kevin Harty, who voiced the Templar in the first game, because he really did knock it out of the park with his performance.
