… becoming a Celtics fan?

Hey y’all!

I’m a former Mavs fan. I grew up in Dallas and was a huge fan during the Dirk era, but I just can’t bring myself to root for that team anymore. Ownership has changed and made a lot of decisions that have left me feeling completely disillusioned from the franchise, and it just doesn’t feel like the team I grew up with. I can handle bad basketball! I won’t, however, support an evil front office that probably ends up leaving Dallas for Vegas eventually.

My question is: would Celtics fans consider this fair or would it come across as bandwagoning/unearned? Curious what y’all think!
