I’ve calmed down from yesterday, but I still think losing this series would be a massive disappointment.

I don’t want to write the obituary yet because we probably still are the more talented group, we’re not playing to our standard, and we have won in this situation before. But….

It would be a huge disappointment to go down in this series, especially after how good we played all season. I think the hardest thing to understand is how everyone played above expectations during the RS and then this happened. Even though Derrick shot poorly during the season, he was still great. The young guys, Queta, LUKA, and RHJ. Jaylen is going to make first team all-NBA, and Payton didn’t miss a beat when he went into the starting lineup. Plus Tatum came back and the team was 13-3 when he played? It felt so good. But this series has has been a total 180.

As I look back at these 6 games, has anyone played at or above expectations besides Tatum and Payton? We are asking way too much of a guy coming off an Achilles tear, and a 5’9 slow white guy.

The big rotation has been comical. Queta has been, by the numbers, really good when he’s been out there. The problem is he can’t say out there. His minutes per game have dropped by 5 in the playoffs, because he’s averaging 7.2 (!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!) fouls per 36. It’s almost inconceivable that he’s doing that.

Vuch has had some solid moments as well, but those 0 resistance post possessions embiid had in game 5 were a joke.

Hauser has been horrible. We are getting killed in his minutes (net rating of -7.6) and dominating when he’s off the floor (+13.3) and his minutes are basically split.

Derrick…. You know what, it’s not even worth typing out how bad he’s been on offense. But here are his numbers:

8/3/3 on 31/26/89 and a -28.2 on/off. You can make your own conclusions.

And honestly, I have been going off on Jaylen today.
I think he deserves it. I’ve been so frustrated by him in this series, and i probably have gone overboard by getting so annoyed, but He has been terrible for 3 games. If you want to be “the best two way player in the league” you can’t leave your running mate hanging in the first round. I’m not one of those people who believes every possession is do or die. In the middle of a game, sometimes taking a possession off is the best thing to do. But Jaylens defensive effort was poor in game 6. The VJ dunk where Jaylen didn’t even bother to get square, the multiple back cuts by Obrue, the ORBs to Obrue, it was terrible. Not to mention the turnover problem.

This heat is what Tatum has taken for his whole career. It is part of being the guy. But he can fix it all tomorrow with a great performance which he is 100% capable of.

I also have to throw Joe into this. I’m not super bothered by the shorter rotation, but I think he was awful in games 2 and 5.

The Celtics were up 13 in the first quarter of game 2. After Tatum dominated the first Q of game 1, the Sixers never got close as Tatum played the whole quarter. But in game 2, after a 7/4/2 +13 8 minutes for Tatum, Joe says “let’s switch it up” and lets Jaylen run for the last 4 minutes. From that point on the Sixers outscored the Celtics by 27. He also planted Vuch and Queta under the free thrown line on ball screens. You can 100% do that to rondo, but why Joe thought he could do that to Maxey is beyond me. I know he’s hurt his finger, but Maxey is a 37% 3 point shooter in 9 attempts a game.

Then in game 5, Tatum who is coming an Achilles, played 24 straight minutes in the 2nd and 3rd. He was cooked by the 4th.

But again, the important thing to remember is that it can all be forgiven if you can pull this one off.

I don’t know what the adjustments are. I would probably just move Hauser to the bench and use him as a break glass option, let Baylor and walsh run around, and if you need scoring early, move quickly off of Derrick and to Pritchard.

Everyone always says Tatum at the 5 is a cheat code. But that only works when you’re not playing a post threat. If we somehow see Cleveland or OKC later? Play him 20 minutes a night at center. Not when he’s trying to guard probably the best post up player since Shaq.

I don’t hate the Knicks the way a lot of you do. For me, that hate is reserved for Giannis/Jimmy/Embiid. Until yesterday, I felt like we buried all those demons. I think embiid is a good guy, but he is such a brutal flopper, i hope he never gets by us. Even if we get smashed by the Knicks. I’d sleep easy knowing we never let embiid know peace.

Anyways I’m off my phone until Sunday. Go C’s.
