Just good, honest, .500 basketball.

Look, I see a lotta folks complaining about the Celtics being “mid” this year, like .500 is some kinda disease you gotta call a doctor for. But I’m here to tell you something: there is dignity in good, honest, .500 basketball.

You clock in, you clock out. You win one, you lose one. No shortcuts. No superteams. Just blue-collar hoops.

These boys don’t show up tryin’ to impress the Twitter analysts or pad some MVP narrative. They lace up every night like a guy lacing up his boots at 5:30am, coffee in one hand, back pain in the other. They get on the court, they do the job. Nothing more, nothing less.

People want 60 wins. People want dominance. Well, must be nice to live in that fantasy world. Some of us live right here, grindin’ out 41 and 41. Punchin’ the clock. Breakin’ even. Paying hoops taxes.

We don’t tank. We don’t soar. We’re just there. Reliable. Like a 2004 Ford F-150 that leaks a little oil but starts every morning if you treat her right.

You know what you’re getting: a couple great wins that make you believe… and a couple ugly losses that remind you not to.

That’s not failure. That’s balance. That’s union basketball.

And honestly? I respect the hell out of it.

Give me that good, honest .500 ball over some bandwagon nonsense any day.

Go Celtics.
