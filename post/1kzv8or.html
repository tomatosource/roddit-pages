Which NBA team has the best all-time starting five made up only of players they drafted?

I was thinking about an interesting hypothetical:

If each current NBA team could put together their best possible starting five using only players they originally drafted, regardless of whether those players ever actually played for them, which team would have the strongest lineup?

For example, the Lakers could pick Magic Johnson, Jerry West, Elgin Baylor, James Worthy, and Mychal Thompson (all drafted by them, even if some didn’t stay long). The 76ers c would have Joel Embiid, Allen Iverson, Charles Barkley, Hal Greer, and Dolph Schayes. Warriors have Steph Curry, Klay Thompson, Draymond Green, Rick Barry, and Nate Thurmond.

It’s interesting because you realise which teams really missed out by trading a player on draft night (Charlotte) and which teams were reliant on trades and haven’t drafted that well (Brooklyn).
