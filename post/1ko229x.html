If Denver wins Game 7, it’ll be the first time since 1981 that both teams in a conference finals series will be seeded #4 or lower (Denver is #4, Minnesota is #6).

The last time it happened was in 1981, when 26 year old Moses Malone averaged 27 & 15 for the series to lead the #6 seed Houston Rockets over 24 year old Reggie King (22 & 11 series average) and his #5 seed Kansas City Kings.

Houston would then lose 4-2 in the 1981 Finals to 24 year old Larry Bird (15/15/7 series average) and his #1 seed Boston Celtics.

Malone averaged 22 & 16 in the 1981 Finals loss, and won his only championship two seasons later, in 1983, when he paired with Julius Erving in Philadelphia to sweep Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, and their West-leading Los Angeles Lakers.
