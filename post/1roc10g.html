Chaos spamming has got to go

Chaos spamming is one of the most popular ways of obtaining T1 mods on fractured bases. This process requires you to spam, sometimes thousands of times, on a base with fractured mod in order to get second desired T1.

This is not good. Very boring, mind numbing spam. Crafting 2-3 items makes your finger sore from clicking left mouse button. Sound effect is pure torture, so much that whenever I do chaos spam, I turn off in game sounds.

More than that, sometimes, you roll over the mod that you are looking for. It is very very painful when that happens. Imagine already spending 600 chaos orbs only to realize that you just removed the mod that you were looking for and now you need to start again. This is just not a good design.

We need alternatives. I don’t believe GGG intended for people to sit in soundless hideout and click 3k times on items in order to get you craft started.
