Revisiting offseason projections

Watching and seeing all these videos on YT of content creators praising this team made me look back at some of the things they said in the offseason.

The main one I look at is from Sporting Logically. If you don’t know who that is he’s a YouTuber that does NBA reaction and content (duh lol). He was adamant that Boston was going to be a bottom 5 team this year and he couldn’t even picture them winning 35 games.

It’s crazy that I was thought I was probably too optimistic thinking this team would be a 45-48 win team. Fast forward 65 games in and they need 5 wins to get to 48. All of people were saying we will “find out” what kind of coach Mazz is. We did, he’s a pretty dang good one. Idk what the rest of the season will be like but I’m happy the whole team is doing what I have been seeing since I became a fan in 2006. They truly embody Celtics Pride
