Max Muncy, Mookie Betts and Clayton Kershaw are the only players to play TWO 18-innings World Series games

Muncy and Kershaw played for the Dodgers, while Betts played for the Red Sox in Game 3 of the 2018 World Series.

The three of them played their second 18-innings game tonight for the Dodgers in Game 3 of the 2025 World Series against the Toronto Blue Jays.

EDIT: Kiké Hernández also played both games, making FOUR players who played two 18-innings games in the World Series. Titles can’t be edited unfortunately.
