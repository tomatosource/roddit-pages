Last night, VJ Edgecombe became the first rookie guard since Jared McCain to drop 30 points and 10 assists in regulation (10th since 2000)

Imagine if they were on the same team!

List of other players to accomplish this feat: Andrew Nembhard, Luka Doncic, Trae Young (6x), Ben Simmons, Steph Curry (5x), Russel Westbrook, Jamaal Tinsley, and Steve Francis (2x).

Austin Reaves (42 mins against the Nuggets’ backups in April), Victor Oladipo (49 mins), John Wall (51 mins), and Raymond Felton (46 mins) have done it in OT.
