Hear me out: essences should be the base crafting currency and disenchanting should provide an essence shards

Playing in trade league atm cause I couldn’t bring myself to play SSF with the current state of affairs. I have been trying to rely on crafting a little more because I hate trading and being in control of your items sounds more fun to me than RNGing or trading. Completely gave up after I bought with 2 ex something I would need 100x or even more resources to craft myself. Trading is not just more efficient than crafting, it is entire universes of efficieny more effective. We are talking about sending an email vs delivering messages with a pidegon scale of difference.

I would like to propose something to bridge the gap a little bit. I think the answer is in essences. They are or could be the first step to a bit more deterministic crafting and should be the cornerstone of basic crafting. I come from D4 (yeah i know we have all done mystakes) and once I heard Raxx commenting on their crafting system “you want to do deterministic crafting first to give everyone a chance to have functional items and then RNG the last part, the one that transform your item to godlike tier” (back then it was completely flipped with how tempering and masterworking functioned). In POE2 I feel it’s kinda the same. “Determinism” is gated beyond large amount of resources and hours and hours of playtime. Transmute into aug into regal doesn’t cut it at the beginning of mapping. You are trying to solve specific problems and you have only generic solutions. It feels like shit cause it is way too much RNG and you don’t have the resources to sustain it. But also it’s literally identifying something on the ground with extra steps. Also this means that to have realistically decent chances of having something useful you have to transmute/aug/regal + reforging every base you find which is super tedious in the long run and requires also large inventory capacity and management. I’m here to blast not to play Amazon wearhouse simulator. The more time I have to spend in hideout alt-leftclicking currency the more I want to deal with chinese pricefixers.

So, why not showering us in essence? Also why not giving us essences shards when we disenchant items. Blue items can give lesser essences shards and yellows greater ones. Not sure if they should replace transmute and regal shard, maybe they can be an additive. What I’m trying to say is that essences should be our base crafting system and the means to obtain them should adjust. I’m at tier8 maps now, I have hundreds of blue orbs and probably less than 50 essences combined. Yesterday I used my single lesser movement essence to try to craft some boots. That was my only shot, it gave 10% and was the end of my deterministic crafting. Not sure when I’ll see a lesser movement essence back again and pretty sure I’ll never see a greater ones in my entire playthrough. It really shouldn’t be like that. They tried to pump up essences this season but they are still too pushed towards the end with corruption and so on. Early in the endgame you need cheap tools to try to craft your stuff and at the moment we have none beside RNG.
