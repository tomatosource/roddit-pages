It’s February 27th and the All Star Break has passed. There’s currently only a 3 game gap between the East and West record (159-153). The Detroit Pistons currently have the best record in the league. What will it take for people to acknowledge that the West isn’t substantially better than the East?

As we get closer to award season for the NBA and the regular season starts winding down, I’m seeing more and more people try to argue cases for who should be where on the rankings because a West star plays in a “way harder conference”. And I just do not see how people are arguing this still this deep into the season. I feel like people are just refusing to open their eyes to the parity that is seen not just league wide, but between the conferences.

There is currently only a 3 game gap between the East and West with the West being up 159-153 currently. This has been a trend holding for most of the season too. So why do people think the West is way better when none of the numbers support that? Here’s some stats to debunk this argument:

#Summary

People are so adamant about how much tougher the West is currently than the East. What is that based on? It can’t be based on the numbers, because the numbers say you’re wrong.

  • Is it just because Curry on the back of the jersey is better than Adebayo?

  • Is it because the Spurs have a few games of a gap as the 2 seed over Boston despite the fact that Boston actually has a better Net Rating?

  • Is it because the Grizzlies traded Jaren Jackson Jr. but just picked up Taj Gibson to close the 5 game gap between them and their 11th seed peers in the Bucks while Giannis is in street clothes?

  • Is it because the 6th seed Lakers are only 1.5 games up on the 6th seed Sixers?

  • Are all the west fanbases just trying to ride OKC’s coattails with their outlandish net rating that nobody else in the conference is even close to?

If I’m missing something besides arguing about the name on the back of the jersey, let me know.
