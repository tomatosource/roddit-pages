Reasons they collapsed

This truly stings. I know I was pissed a few days ago about them collapsing and I was thinking irrationally but I’ve realized more and more why it happened and how we can prevent this from happening again

  1. The 76ers getting Embiid back was a huge reason for their success. Remember even though Embiid is hurt he can still be that 2023 MVP. He played like it last night.

  2. The Simons for Vucevic trade: If we had Simons we would’ve won this game. Simons scoring output would’ve helped us way more than whatever Vucevic was doing the last 6 games. Clearly this is a top 2 worst decision Brad Stevens has made. Thankfully Vucevic will be gone after this year.

  3. Threes: Look I’m not gonna be an idiot and say fire Mazzulla but they need to switch up this “Live and Die by the 3” philosophy because it’s cost us 2 playoff series. You can’t be reliant on the 3 EVERY GAME when you don’t have the pieces to do so.

  4. Obviously Tatum being injured didn’t help. But I’m fine with it because we don’t want him going down with a more severe injury. Protect his career and help him recover for next season

  5. Depth didn’t show up: Only FIVE players scored in Game 7. That’s not a recipe for winning a playoff game. Other guys have to show up and produce scoring.

  6. Just them not finishing the series earlier than they should. They should’ve won Game 5 and ended it there and they didn’t. This led to game 6, the Tatum injury, and everything that happened last night.

It’s gonna suck, it’s gonna hurt, but the team will use this as a learning experience and move on. We ride and die with our duo just like we did in previous years.

Go C’s!
