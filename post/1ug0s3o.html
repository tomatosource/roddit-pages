Poe2 Disciple of Varashta Build

Hey, just wanted to share this build because I was honestly surprised by how much fun it turned out to be.

I’m playing Disciple of Varashta, originally planning to go for the Raven Flock setup, but while leveling this ascendancy really got me hooked. It’s a really fun skill with minions, even though you’re not actually using traditional minions just the Djinns.

I also wanted to share it because I was really surprised by some of the nodes. There were a bunch I never even considered before, but after playing around with them they suddenly made total sense. It was honestly kind of mind-blowing how well everything came together.

After getting some cool jewels and the 40% increased Area bonus, I decided to go for crit, and it felt really good. I honestly feel like it’s a bit overtuned, but overall the Archon of Undead + Puppeteer scaling is just insane. With the Dominion node, you also have basically 100% uptime.

I normally play around the Dex/Str side of the tree, but this was a fucking cool build to try. The defenses are also surprisingly solid.

tldr: really impressive what GGG can cook. This build completely changed how I look at some of these nodes.
