Notable occurences and numbers from last season

— Steph Curry was the highest paid player, making 55,7 millions. He has been the highest paid player since the 17-18 season.

For comparison, the highest adjusted salary for a single season would be Michael Jordan in 97-98, making 33,1 millions (around 65m today). Joe Fulks made \(8000 in 46-47, which would be around \)116 000 today.

— Steph Curry also became the first player to record 4000 three pointers (on 42,3% efficiency). James Harden is the only other player over 3000, with 3175.

— Jason Tatum signed the highest contract in NBA history (so far), with 5 years for 314m.

— Lebron James passed Kobe Bryant’s record of 14 481 career missed field goals. There are only 9 other players with more than 12 000, with the only active one being Russell Westbrook.

He also passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar’s record of most points made in the regular season (38 387) and is not at 42 184 and counting. There are only 6 other players above 30 000, with Kevin Durant being the only active one (30 571).

— Lebron James became the first player to record a triple double in his 22nd season. He is also the oldest player to score 40 points in a game, and the oldest player to record 3 consecutive triple doubles. He recorded 10 triple doubles last season.

Lebron also became the oldest player to lead both teams in points, rebounds, and assists (32/14/10), on october 26, 2024 against the Sacramento Kings.

— Victor Wembanyama became the third player to record multiple 5x5s (he has 2). The only other players are Andrei Kirilenko (3 times), and Hakeem Olajuwon (6 times).

Victor is also the youngest player to record a 5x5, as well as the fastest in 30 minutes 55 seconds.

Victor also came close to a quadruple double against the Denver Nuggets on april 2, 2024. He had a statline of 23 points, 15 rebounds, 8 assists and 9 blocks.

— Nikola Jokic passed Magic Johnson to become third in career triple doubles. He now has 164. Oscar Robertson has 181 and Russell Westbrook 203. Jokic recorded 34 triple doubles last season.

He also joins Westbrook and Robertson as the third player to average a triple double for a season, with 29.6 points, 12.7 rebounds and 10.2 assists.

— Jokic had the highest scoring triple double in NBA history with 61/10/10 against the Minnesota Timberwolves on april 1, 2025.

He is also the first player to record 30 points, 20 rebounds and 20 assists in a single game, against the Phoenix Suns on march 7, 2025. He actually had a statline of 31/21/22 and 3 steals.

— Chris Paul became the first player to start in all 82 games for his team, in his 20th season or later.

He also now ranks second in regular season assists (12 499) behind John Stockton (15 806). There are only 5 other players with more than 10 000, with Lebron being the only active one (11 584).
