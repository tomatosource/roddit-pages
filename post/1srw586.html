More slots for equipped gems

I think the current limit (9) for equipped gems should be increased or removed altogether.

There are many skills I’d like to use for crowd control, combo, escaping, ranged attack, apply debuffs and so on, but I can’t because there are no available slots.

yes, I know there is the unset ring, but it’s hard to give up implicits from other rings, specially if you’re playing builds that have tremendous affix pressure (like Titan mace for ex). In addition, a pair of unset only gives us 2 additional slots.

Many poe2 skills are designed to pair or combo with other skills and many of them are left unused because the marginal utility of a escaping skill, for the sake of argumentation, is much lower than the attack one.

I think the only limiting factor should be the human ability to manage that amount of skill options at a given time
