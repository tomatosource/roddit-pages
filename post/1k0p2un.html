Celtics villains of the 2000s and their ranks

As someone who started watching this team in Tatum’s rookie season and just returned watching last season after trying to catch up with the league in Joe’s first year after like 4 years of absence from watching , I want to ask who do you think are the Celtics villains of the 2000s?

Based on what I’ve read here before and my own experience of watching the Celtics , here’s my list :

  1. Kyrie Irving : Ah yes the man who sold us hope when he first arrived to replace IT who then snaked the fanbase and talked too much about guarding Giannis in 2019 only for him to stunk the place out in our elimination game and jumped ship to team up with KD in Brooklyn only for us to end the pairing in Joe’s first season and also humiliating and booing Kyrie in front of the TD Garden crowd satisfies us who got betrayed

  2. Kendrick Perkins : A media soldout who tried to start a narrative that the Jays failed and they should be separated only for him to get banned from the duckboat celebration which led to him flipping back to his “praise” for us in order for him to get accepted back with us , even Scal called him out

  3. Rick Pitino : I know I wasn’t even born yet when he was coaching us, but from what I’ve read so far it was our worst period before the turn of the millenium , older fans please correct me if I’m wrong

4. Grant Williams : What he did to Tatum was out of nowhere, yes I knew he had that goofiness in him, but doing that to your best friend yeah that was shocking considering how close he was despite Deuce not liking him which hurts me and other Celtics fans . At least he ain’t talking shit about our team unlike Kyrie after Grant departed

  1. Ime Udoka : After a torrid start where we choked against the Knicks at MSG, Derrick White’s arrival transformed the team and we finished the season strong at 51-31 , swept aside Kyrie and KD’s Nets , took down Giannis’s Bucks in 7, fought hardly against Jimmy’s Heat in 7 before unfortunately losing to the Warriors in 6. At that point most of us were optimistic with what the future would had look under Ime considering the praise for him by our current players, but that unspeakable offence nearly derailed our season which led to Joe’s interim appointment before getting promoted due to Will Hardy’s appointment by the Jazz. And the rest is history

6. Doc Rivers : This is debatable since he won us banner 17 in 2008. But my God the number of blown leads in the playoffs with us not only with others (Clippers and Sixers) is kinda baffling , even Rasheed Wallace called him out for his lack of adjustments . 2009 probably is forgivable with KG out in the playoffs , 2010 was it due to Perk’s ACL that it went downhill? And how about 2012?

How would you rank these 6 from 1-6 ? And who else can you add here

My rank : Kyrie &gt; Perk &gt; Ime &gt; Pitino

Perk and Pitino is probably interchangeable depending on how long you’ve followed the team

Do let me know what you think
