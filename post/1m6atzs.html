Mavs (2011), Bulls (96-98) were the ultimate unc winners

With the LA Uncs exciting NBA fans all over, I looked abit into the oldest championship winning teams in history. This is what I found.

Measured by the aggregate age of the core of a team, the legendary Bulls core was among the oldest winners recorded; but the Mavericks in 2011 were the oldest ever to win a championship. Against some of the greatest young talents this league has ever seen, no less. Sorta reinforced my belief that this remains the most “deserved” championship since the turn of the century.

Not trying to make any point, just some trivia I guess. Father time is inevitable, but maybe the Clippers can put up a good fight. Sight to see.
