I know there are points where a boss is going to transition to another stage, and you can’t do any more damage until it changes… so I was waiting for him to take his next form. But he didn’t transform. He didn’t die, had no health in his health bar, and didn’t transform. So, essentially, he was immortal and kept on attacking me until I died.
