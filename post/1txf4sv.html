0.5 Crafting not for casual players

I’m a father of two, I play between 0-2 hours a day. I played 0.3 last and man, what a blast the crafting was. I literally came on, ran a few maps for gold, checked the market, bought a few bases, did some deterministic crafting as per POE2DB and just waiting in anticipation for my crafts to sell. I was addicted.

I skipped 0.4 as it was over summer and didn’t have time.

Then came 0.5. Massive overhaul of endgame, so I was keen to jump in (im still enjoying the playing/levelling part).

I needed some currency as my drops have been poor. So I took up crafting, again. What a mess. Cant have more than 2 crafts? No more homogenizing omens? As a result, bases are ridiculously priced and only obtainable by the mega rich. Deterministic is out of the question unless you hit 2 x gambles, thus, not deterministic anymore. Mats to reduce risk in crafting are stupidly expensive. Might be the start of the league only?

Im not ranting (maybe I am), but for casual players, crafting was a fun way of making currency as we play way too little to farm currency drops.

Crafting for me this season will therefore only start when I have built up enough currency, when prices drop, or never.

What’s your take on this?

PS. I think if they took away the ‘only one craft’ OR brought back Homogenizing omens (either or), crafting would still have been for casuals to a certain extent.

Edit: No hate. I love the game. Im really interested on the community’s take on this.
