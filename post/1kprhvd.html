The game is almost very well balanced, except for a few enemies.

I’ve found that in general, most enemies and rare mods (yes, including volatile plants and crags) can get to the point where they’re annoying, but are all manageable.

Except for three specific types, and they all share the same pattern: Diretusk Boars: while their damage isn’t too high, their attacks are way too fast for how much they can stun. For melee/slow attack builds this is basically a build canceller.

Beyond/Vaal arcers: excessively fast attacks, insanely high damage, no way to avoid them actively or passively except for just killing them faster than they can notice you.

Lightning storm rare mod: similar to arc, it cannot be avoided passively, and it comes out way too fast (and usually in situations where visibility isn’t very good) for active dodge to be any use. One of these spawns in a sekhema trial and unless you oneshot it, you’ll likely lose way too much honor for one enemy.

In general the other enemies tend to have a good way to avoid their attacks or deal with the amount of damage, stun or speed they can put out, but for these it’s near impossible to work around in lower end builds and likely able to brick your maps.
