Interesting fact: The last player to lead their team to a finals before their 5th season was Lebron in 2007

When you look at the franchise guys to lead their teams to the finals for the first time, they all did it in their year 5 season or later in this era.

2025: Haliburton - Year 5

2025: SGA - Year 7

2024: Luka - Year 6

2023: Jokic - Year 8

2022: Tatum - Year 5

2021: Booker - Year 6

2021: Giannis - Year 8

2015: Curry - Year 6

2012: KD - Year 5

2009: Dwight - Year 5

This might seem obvious since players and teams need to develop but it’s surprising there are no outliers until Lebron in 07 and Wade in 06, who made it and won in his 3rd year which is insane.

This also doesnt bode well for teams that might want to “two timeline” their roster like Philly and Dallas with their new draft picks. Theres a good chance AD and Embiid will be well out of their prime by the time Flagg and whoever Philly drafts will be ready to contend. The last team that was able to successfully do this was the Spurs in 1999.
