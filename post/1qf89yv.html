Artificial Boss Difficulty

I have over a couple hundred hours at this point (still relative newb ik). One of the things that wears me down every league without fail is how poorly designed the bosses are. So many bosses have move pools where nearly every attack is dodge or die. This artificial difficulty is NOT FUN. I can write off bosses being in op in maps bc you’re intentionally increasing the difficulty for more reward. But the fact that it’s so prevalent in the campaign just shows incredibly poorly design choices. Now you have to slog through 4 acts of bosses that will throw one shots at you one after the other. I’m going through act 4 for the second time this patch and fighting benedictus has me seriously doubting the choices being made in this game. The strategy used and suggested by people for this is literally melt the boss so you don’t have to deal with it. Once again, in maps sure why not you’re going to speed anyways. But in the campaign? Come on. I can’t even use a slower burn build if I wanted to. Really limits the build possibilities which I resent because endless possible builds is what drew me to this game in the first place.
