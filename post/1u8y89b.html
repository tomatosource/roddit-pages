Limited supporter packs & potential alternatives

Is there any reason other than using fomo to fuel purchases that league supporter packs have to be limited? A very common issue people have with the supporter packs is the price and it’s hard to argue. They are pretty steep coming in at CA$89.68 for the second tier of a bundle which comes with armour.

Would it not be a very pro-consumer solution to add supporter packs back into the store after league ends, removing the titles and coins from pack? This way people will know you did not purchase the supporter pack during the league. While also removing the “incentive” or “added bonus” of getting the coin value from the purchase.

Or perhaps a system similar to Warframes Prime Access can be added. A system where previous supporter packs are locked into a vault after the end of league. With one pack being re-released with each new league with the same caveat as above.
