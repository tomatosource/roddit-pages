Has there ever been an ~endless or ascension / descension mode?

First time player reaching the endgame here. Been having a lot of fun going deep after bouncing off of 0.1 when the campaign looped on itself.

One thing I’m missing though is the experience of the first Diablo where you have an instance that just gets harder and harder (and more rewarding) the deeper you go.

I like the freedom of choice the current mapping system gives, but sometimes I just want to forget about waystones and tablets and mod combinations and all that noise for a while. Even if I spend time prepping a bunch so I can quickly get through one map after another, it’s still ~immersion-breaking?

It would be nice to just say, “give me a map that flows into another, and another, and another, and every time bump the item rarity, monster rarity, monster effectiveness, etc. every time, until it gets so crushing that I either have to run or die”. Diablo 4 has the pit, which is close I guess but not quite right, because each level is disjointed. It’s not the experience of Diablo where you start with basic bitch mobs and things just get worse and worse and worse in one cohesive run.

So, yeah, have they ever experimented with this?
