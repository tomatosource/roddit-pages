Maron Trudge Update

Prior to this season I made a post about “Maron Trudge” aka a hybrid of Mike Trout and Aaron Judge if they swapped places Freaky Friday style after the 2020 season. After another historic offensive season by Aaron Judge, combined with a clearly declining Trout, the comparison is even starker.

“Maron Trudge” is now fresh off of his 6th MVP, just one behind Barry Bonds, entering his age 34 season (and an astonishing 10 top-2 finishes in 15 total seasons). He now has 115.8 career WAR, good for 18th place in baseball history, just ahead of Lou Gehrig and just behind A-Rod (and Kid Nichols). He has 551 home runs and is well on track to break Bonds’s record. He adds his first ever batting title to his nearly never ending list of accomplishments. (Though he still has yet to win a World Series.)

Meanwhile, “Aike Jout” the injury-plagued alter ego of this Freaky Friday swap, isn’t doing so well, though he played more games and had more at bats than in any season since 2017. He now has 34 career WAR going into his age 34 season and an aesthetically pleasing but disappointing 222 career HR.

For all those interested, I will continue updating on Maron Trudge and his quest to become the greatest baseball player ever at the end of each season. I will do so even if “all those” ends up just being me.
