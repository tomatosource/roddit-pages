Future Celtics? Trades, Draft, Signings

While we are all heartbroken to see the championship core broken up maybe thinking about acquisitions we’d like to see would be a positive/exciting exercise. Who are some players you’d like to see join the squad? Either to keep us afloat for a season without JT, or who we can develop for when he’s back, and who will help us be the most feared in the east. And who do you think would be a bad idea to add?

I’ll start. To take over the starting center role I think Myles Turner is the perfect fit. Granted it depends on how far Indiana makes it in their post season run(hoping to see a Minnesota/Indiana finals myself). He’s got offense, and defense. Also Walker Kessler is on an expiring rookie deal. I think Utah would want Sam Hauser, and some draft compensation.

As much as I don’t want to see former Celtics on the Lakers I’ve seen seen a few mock trades that send Jrue Holliday, and Porzingis to the Lakers for Austin Reaves, and some other expiring contracts that I don’t hate.

As for mistakes I think trying to go after a superstar would be a bad idea, and just put us in the same salary issues we’re currently in. Maybe bringing in some chemistry issues when JT does return. So all the Giannis, and KD rumors can get out of here. And while Jaylen Brown would get the most value in return it would be a bad idea to get rid of him too. The JT/JB duo has been proven in the post season.
