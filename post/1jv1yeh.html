Per Adam Mares: Calvin Booth hired a shooting coach named Mike Penberthy, and wanted to rebuild the Nuggets organizationally. Michael Malone initially responded saying “No, that’s a Coach…I hire coaches”. Some players who started working with Penberthy, like Zeke Nnaji, had to go outside the building for most of their workouts.

Adam also theorized that Malone saw Penberthy as a potential spy for Booth, and gave that some credence (due to their tension and it being a GM hire for the coaching area).
