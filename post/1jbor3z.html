Why are people shocked that Jokic took 17 Free-Throws if JJ Redick’s entire game plan is to front him with multiple smaller players and get “physical”?

So Denver played the undermanned Lakers tonight and barely won. Jokic had 28 points on 17 free throw attempts which is unusually high for him, and some people thought it was an unfair whistle. But since the previous game JJ Redick’s gameplan has basically been to double/triple team him on & off ball as well as front him with smaller players and make those smaller players use “physicality” because they can get away with contact more easily than big defenders on Jokic. Guys like Vando/Knecht obviously can’t guard Jokic 1 on 1, but what JJ Reddick is doing is basically putting them out as bodies to be as physical as possible with Jokic until they inevitably get called for a foul or run into foul trouble. Do people really expect someone like Vanderbilt to be successfully able to guard Jokic 1 on 1 in the post without fouling?

If this is the strategy JJ Redick will be using against Jokic refs won’t be perfect so fouls are going to be called on the Lakers as well as some “non-calls”, but overall this gameplan will obviously result in Jokic shooting more free-throws.
