Do Brown and Tatum have synergy?

I think the argument has been that clearly they can win together. But the advanced lineup stats tell an interesting story.

Here are net ratings per 100 possessions from Cleaning the Glass:

Season JB on, JT on JB on, JT off JT on, JB off
25-26 playoff +1.9 -18.3 +21.4
25-26 reg season* +16.2 +4.8 +1.6
24-25 playoff +13.6 -0.4 +11.9
24-25 reg season +8.6 +10.5 +11.7
23-24 playoff +5.4 +8.0 +21.0
23-24 reg season +7.8 +8.4 +15.3
22-23 playoff +5.6 -12.4 +2.1
22-23 reg season +5.2 +3.4 +13.0

*The 25-26 regular season was a small sample after Tatum returned.

What I observe is that Brown has struggled without Tatum in the playoffs, except during the 23-24 team which was the loaded title team; even then, Tatum-on/Brown-off lineups were much stronger.

Jaylen clearly showed he can lead a team to a pretty good regular season result this year, and that he can lead both a weak support cast (25-26 regular season) and good supporting casts (JB on/JT off in the 23-24 playoff year and the 23-24⁄24-25 regular seasons).

But putting the two of them together on the floor is worse than just Tatum without Brown, and sometimes worse than Brown without Tatum as well. The problem is that lineups with Brown are worse on defense (we’ve had some top perimeter defenders as alternatives), and not enough better on offense when Tatum is on the floor, since one of them becomes a spot-up shooter at that point.

I really like Brown as a player and appreciate how much he’s improved pretty much every year, but his skillset really shows best when he’s the #1 option on offense, and that conflicts with his fit with Tatum.
