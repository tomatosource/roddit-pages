Clippers Extend Active NBA Record

Not sure if anyone’s posted this yesterday, but with the Clippers getting their 42nd Win, they’ve now had 14 consecutive winning seasons dating back to 2012. It’s the longest active streak in the NBA. Unfortunately, they’ve had zero titles to show it, but considering they were the worst franchise in NBA history for a long time, 14 straight winning season is a massive come-up.

For reference, the all-time record for most winning seasons in a row is the Spurs with 22.
