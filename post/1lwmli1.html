5 More Anfernee Simons Trades

Here are 5 Anfernee Simons trades in response to the reports that the Celtics are looking to move the big contract. Feel free to tear me to pieces over them.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Lauri Markannen

Celtics get big man talent. Lauri isn’t a #1 guy but playing off Brown and Tatum, in a system that moves the ball he’d thrive. Hauser is an unfortunate casualty but Markennen is a different tier. They give up 2 1sts which will most likely be 22 - 28 range

Jazz get - Anfernee Simons, Jordan Walsh, Sam Hauser, Georges Niang, and 2 future 1st round picks

The Jazz are in the process of flipping older guys for picks and eating salary. Simons, Hauser, and Niang can all be flipped again between now and the deadline for 2nd round picks. Simons and Niang can’t be traded until September.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Bogdan Bogdanovic, Kris Dunn

The Celtics pretty massively cut down on salary for the upcoming season without committing to the future. Both of these guys have team options for next year. If they stay and Boston likes them they could extend for lesser salary the following year.

Clippers get - Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman

The Clippers upgrade their shooting and get a guy who has given Nikola Jokic fits in the past. Both guys are on 1 year deals so they get the short term depth they’re looking for, without tying their hands for the following season.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Josh Green, Max Christie, Olivier-Maxence Prosper

This one is a little messier for Boston as none of these guys are on 1 year deals. Josh Green is a guy i’ve liked for a while and think he’d be a good fit in Bosotn. He’s a high energy defender who can hit 3’s at a solid clip although low volume. He’s like a prime Torrie Craig. Max Christie is a hard sell with his 3 year deal. He’s an ok bench player and it’s a low value contract. OMP is actually someone Brad was looking at in his draft. They brought him in for a late workout. Also, the second Dallas took him, Brad immediately started flipping picks and moving back to take Jordan Walsh.

Hornets get - Klay Thompson, Dwight Powell, 3 2nd round picks

The Hornets take on some expiring or near expiring deals to load up on 2nd round picks. Klay Thompson is also a great mentor with a lot of experience. Charlotte has a bunch of young guys and they need to see what a winner mentality looks like.

Dallas gets - Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman, JD Davison

Dallas gets out of next year’s Klay Thompson contract, while maintaining a shooter and picking up a big who has given Nikola Jokic fits in the past. JD Davison’s salary is non guaranteed so he’d likely get waived. Dallas’s big motivation here is opening up roster spots. They have guys they’re talking to in FA.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Jaden Ivey, Tobias Harris

The rehab season in Boston gord into full effect with Jaden Ivey, a player i’ve been interested in for a long time. If Brown and Tatum are both out for the start of the season, Ivey could come in and make the case as the most explosive scorer the Celtics have opening night. The Celtics also pick up expiring contract Tobias Harris who can eat Brown/Tatum minutes this upcoming season and provide guidance for younger players, which is something he’s excelled at in the back half of his career.

Pistons get - Anfernee Simons, Sam Hauser, future 1st

Detroit moves on from Ivey who has shown star potential but is hindered playing next to the ball dominant Cade Cunningham. Despite Ivey looking like a star they’ve even taken to benching him at times, and have honestly found some success without him. They pick up Simons and Hauser which is a lot of shooting firepower, which Detroit is clearly looking for. They have a lot of guys they want on the floor who are either bad, or not volume 3 point shooters.
