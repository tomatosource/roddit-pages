Warbringer basic mace attack kills everything

King meets Warbringer

That is King of the mist, dead. Killed by just basic mace attack. Ok that is not completely true, its basic mace, wind dancer and frost wall. Mana? barely needed. No sunder, no earthshatter, no orbs, no crying karens, none of what we see on the youtube. A little backstory, my friends are all on either lich, titan or lightning spears amazons, so lets do something diff. I can’t watch another lightning spear dance.

Total cost : 5 div.

The mechanics is simply whack, whack, whack, put ice wall and hope he hits you back so your winddancer will activate + trample toe. Nah, you don’t need level +7 melee mace on warbringer. Get the hammer of faith, come on guys, spear look so silly on him.

Here is one thing we can’t get rid off in using Warbringer, the shield, i tried. Get that up to 80% on svalinn, get a trample toe and for ascendancy up full shield (turtle charm) and get the other up on armour breaking.

About the wind dancer on Warbringer:
You don’t need poison+corrosion for winddancer armour explosion clearing mechanics, your ascendancy Anvil easily breaks them. Envenom is sufficient and i used heavy swing skill on it. Yeah that works. Up the 40% melee you see cause winddancer is a melee attack.

About the basic mace attack:

Brutality heft, martial tempo, close combat and…..exploit weakness. The latter is for lazy ppl like me that didn’t want to switch skills. Basically it “resets” the armour explosion that wind dancer dealt and you break it again and repeat.

That’s it. Kills anything.

Just one missing piece of info, you will need that Hero jewel 2550. Thats where 1 div was spent.
