Various QoL suggestions but no backup from the community

While reviewing QoL suggestions for the game, I noticed a few things in the community. Although people mean well and are benevolent toward the gaming experience and make various suggestions, a picture emerges of what I would call “pseudo-GGG representatives”.

There are people here who always speak on behalf of GGG when it comes to QoL issues: that it’s their game design decision, that’s how it should be, that it’s not technically feasible, that their in-game shop argues against it, that nothing will change, that this issue was sealed by a manifesto at the time, and then they basically bring a pessimistic attitude to the table. Most of the time, these people don’t comment from their own point of view or give their opinion on the topic, but always refer to GGG.

Just share what you think about it and not what GGG thinks about it; you don’t work there — unless you really do, in which case it’s a different story. I would say that GGG is interested in our ideas, but they would only be taken seriously if we addressed them unanimously as a community. For people who make QoL suggestions, it’s really demotivating to debate with these pseudo-GGG representatives.

Hardly anywhere do you read, “That’s an interesting suggestion, but from my point of view, the following speaks against or in favor of it.”

I have read the craziest arguments against the QoL suggestion “auto sorting” and it’s always the same old clichés.

Even with the topic of “auto currency pick up” – which I am an advocate of – there are more advantages for the player, ultimately more fun, time savings, and the certainty that you won’t leave anything valuable behind because it’s so tedious to pick it up yourself, even though that’s part of the core gameplay principle of POE and nothing should be left behind.

The list of suggestions could go on, of course.

I think there is also a lack of openness to change.
