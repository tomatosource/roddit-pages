New Specialists

Just wanted to get your opinion on these four very interesting minor leaguers. Who is worth taking up a roster spot in the majors:

Dandy Redditte - This pitcher cannot throw strikes. BUT, he has an incredible pickoff move. It’s so deceptive that the lead baserunner, every single time, without fail, will feel secure enough to take a lead, and get immediately picked off. His arm is incredibly weak, though. After just one pickoff, he has to come out of the game.

Rocky Thunderson - Insanely fast. He can steal 2nd base on any pitch. 100% of the time. He can’t hit worth shit and has no plate discipline though, so he can only be used as a pinch runner. Cannot play defense. He won’t steal 3rd either. When asked why, he said “Rocky doesn’t do that.”

Mario Ravenna - The clutchest closer of all time. His ERA throughout the first 8.2 innings of any game is infinite. He has never once succeeded in getting an out. EXCEPT, when it comes to getting the last out of the ninth. Then, he has a WHIP of 0.

Jett Cutler - Due to a chronic shoulder injury, he cannot swing the bat. Or play defense. Or run. He’s got a hell of a bunt, though. He knows exactly where to place it so that every other runner advances, guaranteed. He’ll be thrown out at first every time though.
