Dandy Redditte - This pitcher cannot throw strikes. BUT, he has an incredible pickoff move. Mario Ravenna - The clutchest closer of all time. His ERA throughout the first 8.2 innings of any game is infinite. He has never once succeeded in getting an out. EXCEPT, when it comes to getting the last out of the ninth. Then, he has a WHIP of 0.
