ES feels like “blue life” because it doesn’t behave like a defense layer

ES is one of three main “defenses” in PoE2, but most people would agree that right now it feels more like a different color of life. There are a few reasons for this:

  1. Armour and evasion multiply your effective life pool, making it efficient to stack both life and armour/evasion. ES is instead additive, so with enough of it you dont care about your life
  2. When ES runs out, during the recharge time you effectively have no defenses regardless of how much you stacked. So you never want to run out of ES, making it even less interactive with life.
  3. ES works on basically every damage type (especially after CI), whereas armour/evasion is limited to hit damage

The consequence is that ES feels like a replacement to life, rather than a defense on top of it. To be clear, this is independent of the current issue with the power level of ES. If they nerf ES, the above will still be true, just less easy to achieve.

Here is my proposal for an ES rework that aligns it more with other defenses:

  • 50% of damage from all Hits is taken from your ES before life
  • ES has a constant regeneration rate of 5% of max ES, without any delay

To scale ES, you keep the following modifiers:

  • % increased maximum ES
  • Regenerate an additional % of max ES
  • Additional % of damage taken from ES before life [premium stat]

This addresses all issues above:

  1. ES now multiplies life (at a base of 2x, but scalable)
  2. You effectively always have some ES, and have two avenues to ensure you can repeatedly take damage
  3. ES no longer interacts with damage over time like armour/evasion, that is the job of life

Of course, many changes to passives/items are required as a result of this change. I propose below changes to some of the main ES related keystones:

  • Chaos inoculation: “All damage is taken from ES before life. Your maximum life is 1” –&gt; re enable blue life but only with this keystone, and take away the broken chaos immunity
  • Mind over matter: “While having ES, damage taken by ES is instead taken from Mana first” –&gt; MoM still works with hybrid at 50% base (scalable), but also with CI
  • Eldritch battery: “Energy Shield from items instead adds Mana. You have 1 ES” –&gt; Large increase to mana pool, enables MoM but also armour/evasion items with ES give mana now
  • Zealoths Oath: “Your life regen applies to ES. Base ES regen is 0%” –&gt; Much higher downside, but enables a playstyle with less max ES and higher regen for constant 50% less damage

I think this change would make for a much more balance mechanic, that actually works as defense and interacts cleaner with armour (makes ES last longer) and evasion (gives more time to regen ES). Happy to discuss any other ideas on the topic!
