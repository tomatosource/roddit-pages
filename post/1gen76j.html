Jason Tatum Substitutions Data - Why Tatum Playing the FULL First Quarter Is Unique This Season

Jason Tatum has played all 12 minutes of the first quarter 3 of 4 games this season. As people on the broadcast and r/bostonceltics have noted, this is different than last year. I wanted to understand what was Jason Tatum’s substitution pattern during the 2023-2024 season?

I used my 2023-2024 NBA play-by-play dataset to find all instances where Tatum was either subbed in or out. Please note that this does not include when Tatum starts a quarter.

https://preview.redd.it/7dxqkd76gmxd1.png?width=1028&format=png&auto=webp&s=93e4262c42cc2f8a65d5413d182269a0e26e58f3

When visualized, there is a clear pattern in the first quarter: Tatum subs out at Q1 6:00 and subs in at Q1 2:00. There is also a strong pattern in the third and fourth quarters where Tatum subs out at Q3 4:00 and subs in at Q4 7:00. The weakest pattern is in the second quarter where Tatum subs out at Q2 7:00 and subs in at Q2 4:00.

This substitution pattern lets Tatum get brief rest in the first quarter before leading the second unit. Let’s compare this with Brown’s substitution pattern.

https://preview.redd.it/w88jyvaghmxd1.png?width=1028&format=png&auto=webp&s=24f7dc312e3c0d14e360b3e9e7e1d490d2ef264f

Brown’s substitution pattern is more consistent than Tatum’s. The strongest pattern is Brown subs out at Q1 2:00 and subs in at Q2 8:00. The other strong pattern is Brown subs out at Q3 4:00 and subs in at Q4 6:00.

When comparing the two substitution patterns, you can see that Brown and Tatum’s minutes are staggered. Tatum rests while Brown pushes through the back-half of the first quarter, and when Brown needs rest, Tatum subs back in with the second unit to finish the quarter. Tatum gets early rest to be Brown’s reliever.

Beyond the first half, both visualizations show more varied substitutions times in the second half. This is likely because substitutions are made based on winning the game, giving players more rest, or trying out new lineups. The end of a basketball game is far less predictable, which is also why it’s so exciting to watch!

What matters most is not the substitution pattern itself, but what it says about a players role. This change in Tatum’s role might be minor for now, but it could also be the first indication of Celtic energy shifting once again.
