I wish every ascendancy was like the new Martial Artist monk ascendancy

I think this game gets a lot of feedback thats negative, and theres some merit to it as Im not happy about some aspects of the game either. BUT I wanted to give a positive example of what I want when I saw one. The new monk class Martial Artist looks great so far.

My problem with a lot of the ascendancies in PoE2 was that even tho they are flavorful, they seem very weak. They seem so weak in fact, that a lot of the time Im not even excited to ascend during the campaign. In PoE1 I wanna get those ascendancies as soon as possible, because they feel like proper upgrades for my build. In PoE2, they feel so weak sometimes they are even just sidegrades, and I might as well wait until much later to do the trials and get my ascension when I need them, not because I want them. It gets to a point where even the flavor of an ascendancy is lost if the ascendancy is bad enough. Like, what even is Smith of Kitava bro.

I feel like this is kinda bad, and I wanna point to a good example where this doesnt seems to be the case. The new monk ascendancy, Martial Artist is not only full of flavor, but seems strong. Properly strong. Like, I saw some people say its overtuned, but I honestly think every ascendancy should be on this level. I just know that when Ill be leveling a monk in 0.5, I will try to rush into the Act 2 trial and get my ascendancy as soon as possible because its both very cool and a good powerspike for my character. Thats I think the feeling every player should have. Being excited is fun.

Now, there will most likely be some broken stuff with it, and Im not good enough PoE2 player to see what it is yet. Balance will be done Im sure and Im fine with that. But overall, I so hope that the new monk ascendancy will remain similar to this power level, and other ascendancies will be brought up to the same level of power. Thats my feedback. I want the ascendancies to not only be flavorful, but exciting as well and this new class does just that. The team did a good job, and I wanted to say that I appreciate the good job they did and hope well get more of this in the future. Hopefully this post wont make people mad lmao
