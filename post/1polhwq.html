GGG, take your month back please.

I think GGG really needs to take a serious look at stepping back from this tight schedule they’ve committed to. If an extra month allows them to get the endgame changes that we were due for example, or to really nail the league mechanic, I’d much prefer it.

The current cadence, in my opinion, is causing league mechanics to suffer and seems to have caused the endgame/balancing to miss the update again. The league mechanic isn’t even a bad idea, it’s tuning is just completely off the mark, and it’s not user friendly at all.

The large majority of players aren’t playing from league launch to league end, they are playing closer to launch so shouldn’t the league mechanic that we engage with be driving the hype instead of killing it. By the time fixes roll out it is likely too late for a lot of people. If people don’t like the flavor of the mechanic then maybe with the time they took back, GGG could have got the endgame changes across the line, or perhaps worked on the skill parity, or perhaps some other forms of balance. What is there to look forward to in this league if you aren’t playing the new ascendancy(they cooked with those)?

I think they need to cook a bit longer between games personally.
