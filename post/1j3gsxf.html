Who is more cooked? (Dallas/Philly/Phoenix edition)

My case for each for the most cooked franchise going forward. Sound off in comments:

Mavericks- Kyrie Irving has a \(40 Million dollar player option next year coming off a torn ACL. He is 33 years old and has a long history of lower body injuries along with his 31 year old, often injured max salary teammate in Anthony Davis. If Kyrie takes his option thats a \)40 million dead cap hit, if he declines it you have to either bring him back on likely a multi year deal that won’t be cheap or let him walk and fully send the fanbase into open warfare. Dallas doesn’t have pick control from 2027-2030.

Sixers- Joel Embiid is 31, Paul George is 33, both are on hugely expensive long term deals that are functionally untradeable. There is a chance they will lose their lottery pick to the Thunder in what most people think is the best draft class in many years at least in the lottery. Maxey and McCain are both good young players, but will they want to be in Philly for a team that basically can’t add blue chip talent around them for the next 3 years?

Suns- Kevin Durant appears to be headed for the exit in the offseason, but what exactly can Phoenix get for an expiring, 36 year old KD on a $50 Million Dollar deal? Bradley Beal is arguably the leagues worst contract, again functionally untradeable. Booker has been upset all year but it seems like the Suns have no interest in moving on from him. While the Suns do have some picks, it is likely that all of them will be later firsts through a convoluted series of swaps. They dont control their own pick until 2032.

Curious to hear your thoughts: I lean Mavericks or Suns because of lack of pick control but I think there are good arguments for all 3.
