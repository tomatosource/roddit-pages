Uniques and their power level generic uniques vs build defining

Now that we are getting more uniques as stated in the new article I think now is the best time to state my grievance with how uniques work in poe2.

Too often uniques with very interesting build defining effects are relegated to weak early game uniques that you throw away by the time you get to level 20. Not only are their base stats weak because they come from an early game base item but their effects are too narrow to use as twink unique because they focus too hard on the build defining effect so most people don’t even try using them because they don’t fit with their build.

Imo build defining uniques should almost Always be uniques that are made to be strong and viable end game uniques. This will encourage a variety of builds that are viable through these uniques instead of feeling frustrated that you want to use this build defining unique but it’s just too weak or the stats is just too small.

Generic uniques with very generic effects that only changes stuff like resistance or attributes or generic increase to physical damage or projectile damage are fine when used as twink unique or early game uniques you are expected to replace because they don’t encourage new builds and if there are too many of them or they are too strong they encourage boring builds where you equip 3-4 of them on literally all build just because they are generically strong in stats. These generically powerful uniques are the source of the fear for developers that don’t want you to slap on 3-5 uniques and call it a build but build defining uniques with specific effects don’t have this problem.

uniques like splinter of Loretta is a big example of this. It’s laughable weak, little to no base stats and the some of the unique effect doesn’t help it deal any relevant damage like (2-3)-(6-8) physical damage or 20% increase ranged. However the actual unique effect of always poisoning on hit and letting any amount of poison stack is a genuinely interesting effect that could support a new poison spear build something that doesn’t currently exist(or if it does it doesn’t use this unique lol)

Unique designs like this should be pushed more while uniques like deavata wind unique spear as an example is generic enough to be relegated to being a twink unique or a weak unique you use in act 1-2 and replace soon after because of its generic effect that works on a variety of builds. Hell, tangletongue is looking pretty generic as well with its basic forking crits ability 👀 I would rather live in a world where SkySliver is the unique of choice for lightning spear rather than tangle tongue
