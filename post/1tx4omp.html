New Elemental Nodes

For anyone looking for the new Elemental Nodes, here are all 40.

West End Jewel Socket:

  • 4 Points: Stormkeeper (20% Lightning Damage, 30% Reduced Shock on You, 15% Shock Magnitude)

  • 4 Points: Flamekeeper (20% Fire Damage, 30% Reduced Ignite on You, 15% Ignite Magnitude)

Middle Druid Jewel Socket:

  • 9 Points: Rhythm of Ice (20% Cold Damage, 6% Cast Speed per recent Spell Echoed up to 30%) and Rhythm of Fire (20% Fire Damage, 5% chance for Slam Skills to cause an additional Aftershock)

Northwest End Jewel Socket:

  • 4 Points: Arsonist (20% Fire Damage, 10% Cast Speed while Ignited, 5% reduced Movement Speed Penalty for Fire Skills)

  • 4 Points: Flurry (20% Cold Damage, 10% Cast Speed while Chilled, 5% reduced Movement Speed Penalty for Cold Skills)

Middle Sorc Jewel Socket:

  • 7 Points: Lightning Storm (6% of Lightning Damage as Extra Cold, Unaffected by Shocked Ground) and Ice Storm (6% of Cold Damage as Extra Lightning, Unaffected by Chilled Ground)

Northeast End Jewel Socket:

  • 4 Points: Brain Storm (20% Lightning Damage, 15% Mana Cost Efficiency)

  • 4 Points: Brain Freeze (20% Cold Damage, -8% Cold Res on Frozen Enemies)

Other Changes:

  • Empowering Infusions and Everlasting Infusions swapped, meaning Empowering’s minors now give a chance to save infusions instead of damage.

  • Sudden Escalation has moved from the left side to the right side. It now requires allocating the Critical Chance instead of having the option for Critical Bonus or Critical Chance.

  • Convalescence has moved above the Archon cluster instead of below. This puts it in range of From Nothing for Eldritch Battery.
