0.3 Review as a new-ish player

Repost since this sub hates me for some reason. TL:DR - it’s a masterpiece with flaws. Here’s the long version:

The Good

• ⁠Best core gameplay and combat in the genre • ⁠Actual, real challenge in an ARPG • ⁠Buildcraft, class and skill design (though I think the number of viable core skills is quite low) • ⁠Great campaign • ⁠Amazing visuals and sound • ⁠I’m a weirdo who actually loves the emphasis on combo gameplay (1-ability builds have been a long-time criticism of mine in other ARPGs)

The Bad (Some nitpicks)

WORST THING: Rarity on gear - I should be equipping the most powerful gear. Not gear that helps me find the most powerful gear. Gaining power is what should increase my ability to find better things. Argue with the wall.

Item/RNG Tuning - The new in-game trade is a godsend but trade is still way, WAY too overpowered. I had to purchase nearly every meaningful upgrade. (Or purchase a base and crafting items to craft it). I don’t know the perfect solution but good items simply do not drop especially in endgame. Just hunting divines until you can buy what you want as the only real strategy is not it and does not fit the ARPG fantasy imo. The madly fluctuating economy affecting the things you’re able to reasonably obtain depending on how long the season has been running also feels bad. Please god give us something like what last epoch does. (Can’t trade but way higher drop rates or vice versa)

Inventory - No amount of nostalgia will make this good. It’s ridiculously restrictive. After picking up core currencies in a map I have room for maybe 2-3 items. Ridiculous. Is storage QoL only considered good game design when we have to pay for it?

Co-op - Feels extremely bad and in most scenarios I feel very punished for playing with a friend. The monster scaling is too harsh. Why is it 10x easier to run Sekhema (or anything) solo? Why does the slightest level difference result in massive penalties to my power? I was 3 or 4 levels higher than a friend and went from being able to annihilate T15s solo to dying and feeling super weak in a T10??? Idk but it’s rough.

Smaller stuff

  • Zero-death waystones do not belong in a game with an insane lack of visual clarity. It is simply too punishing. Should have one retry minimum.
  • Mana costs in general are still too high to the point that it feels bad to scale skills.
  • Juicing waystones is very tedious and repetitive. Can we get multi-use waystones or something? Idk but it feels like a minigame practically.
  • Endgame is obsessed with slowing you. Temporal chains/chilled ground was on like, 8⁄10 of my waystones. It’s not fun. Please get rid of it.
  • Not being able to use cast-on-x until the campaign is essentially done kinda blows.
  • Please transfer some power out of gear and into the passive tree.
  • Buildcraft could be made even more flexible imo (really struggled to make a melee/cast-on-x build work. Am I stupid for trying? Yes. But I don’t think it would be bad for weird stuff to be more viable, plus I heard POE1 excels at that)
  • Skins are insultingly expensive. I get the game will be free but one skin will never be worth 1 - 4 Expedition 33’s. Again, argue with the wall.
  • Things like Breach and Delirium demanding extremely fast screen-clearing builds while the game’s design encourages thoughtful combo-builds does NOT go well together.
  • Delirium Fog
  • +Skill Levels is a problem. It’s kind of boring and way too essential. This is a nitpick but I think this stat could use a rework
  • Really need the devs to get over their lightning fetish and give some love to the other elements.
  • Many ascendancies are kinda boring and just provide strong scaling instead of genuinely interesting and creative passives

I know it seems like there’s more bad than good here but that’s just because what I love about the game is more broad and the things that bother me are more specific. At the end of the day I’m basically ruined for all other ARPGs and look forward to 0.4.

Anyway, roast away in the comments.
