Count Geonor is still the absolute BEST moment in the ENTIRE game

You’re level 14~16, you have just enough different skills that your “build” combos are coming together. You’re on an epic fight against a gothic-horror fallen king turned werewolf. The music is an absolute BANGER. Geonor trash-talking you (more like sulking in his own werewolf-ness) while he fades into an icy mist. A fight with very punishing, yet easy to dodge attacks. The lunar beams (bleed beams on the later stage) easily freeze you, but they’re easy to dodge and only punish you when you greed a few more hits instead of rolling.

Act 2, act 3, even act 4. There’s no other fight as fun and intense as this one. I go out of my way to not overlevel every time I fight Geonor, because it’s just way too good of a fight to steamroll it.
