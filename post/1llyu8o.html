What If The NBA Had A Hometown Cup

Inspired by the olympics and world cup, I created a league that has each NBA team fielding a hometown roster of 12 players. Rules are based on birthplace because I didn’t want to do a deep dive on how each player grew up and make a subjective decision so I kept it simple. I tried to divide the country as best as possible. Also, the starting 5s are not something I really thought about, but I thought it was better to visually look at
