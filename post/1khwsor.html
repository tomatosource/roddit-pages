It is also true that they ended the game with a timeout in their pocket and no designed play, just a Tatum (who was 4-14 with multiple misses at the rim) dribbling into traffic.

We can root for this team, and criticize them when they choke away back to back 20 point leads. We can say Joe had one of his worst games. Wasting two fouls on Robinson just to get him out of the game and not calling not calling time out after the Brunson free throws clearly did not work out.

I think the biggest point is that even if they had survived both comebacks and won, Indiana and OKC both look better than Boston right now. They are healthier, hungrier and just giving more effort when you watch them play.

My biggest complaint about the Celtics through two games is not even the shooting threes when they aren’t falling, that is annoying AF, but they were missing good looks during the fourth and you would have expected a couple of those to fall and the lead to be double digits with under three minutes (29 points total in the 4th quarter through 2 games). It is actually that they are not matching the Knicks intensity late in these games. They are getting outhustled, outrebounded, and outworked when the games in on the line. Watching the Thunder last night reminded me of the Udoka Celtics those guys are playing at 11 on defense from opening tip. One more fourth quarter stop in each game would have also been the difference.
