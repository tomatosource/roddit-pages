I’m very excited for 0.3 except for mapping itself

The Atlas of Worlds is really cool and visually appealing to me, but it’s not something I am eager to explore and interact with again to the extent I wish it was. By now everyone has given their ideas and thoughts on the matter, but my hype for 0.3 gives me an urge to share mine as well.

The most obvious is to move any juicing and map node league mechanic manipulation from towers to the Atlas tree and Waystones. I think this is really important to get rid of the term “travel nodes”.

  • Everything related to precursor tablets moved to the Atlas Tree
  • No League mechanics or bosses on the atlas map. You’d still have corrupted/cleansed and similar
  • Tower still reveal an area, but no straight up juicing of maps are related to them
  • Maybe there can be different types of towers so you can keep cool effects like accessing all maps in range. That could be a sky tower and the atlas tree could have a node for increasing the chance to find sky towers as an example

The importance is to make exploring the Atlas exciting and rewarding. For this I think we need some map nodes that are only accessible by the owner of the Atlas. That way, you could make some unique maps that are extremely rare, but giving ultimate rewards. In short I’d want to see the same pop off or excitement one might get for finding a mirror as finding a map node on their atlas.

There could also be unique maps that you’re excited to invite your friends to do, but I just don’t want to see selling of access to unique maps become meta. I’d love to see them add way more unique maps in general, ranging from rare to basically impossible to find.

I think it’s enough to have player agency and juicing restricted to the atlas tree and waystones(with potentially scarab-like items), and have the atlas map be about Exploration, RNG, RPG elements like the quest to cleanse corrupted nexus and biome/map preferences.

Let me know what your thoughts are. I think it’s good that they are acknowledging the pain points with towers and juicing even if they couldn’t get changes done in time for 0.3. I’ll be looking to probably play Stormweaver combining the elements, this is just filling time untill we get more info to base our theorycrafting on.
