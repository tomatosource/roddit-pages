[ESPN]: Payton Pritchard is among the 30 most inconsistent players this season

I thought this was actually in interesting piece of analysis in the ESPN playoff preview: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/48447222/nba-playoffs-2026-preview-teams-postseason

Pritchard has posted “23 good games and 17 bad ones (plus-37 average efforts),” making him one of the 30 most inconsistent players and an important X factor for any playoff series.

This passes the eye test for sure with Pritchard. If he is on, the team is probably unstoppable. If he has a couple stinkers at a bad time, we could see a repeat of last year.

“Boston’s biggest loss of the season was to the visiting Hornets on March 4, when Pritchard had his worst game.”
