Walsh IN…. Scheierman OUT

What did we see in the 2025 NBA Playoffs? Physical Basketball

With Tatum out. We need a SF to take minutes. So who is that going to be?

Watching Baylor Scheierman in the 2025 regular season he had moments. Moments of hot shooting, became a fan favorite for a span of 2 weeks. But never took the torch away from Hauser. Given his shooting form is atrocious compared to Hauser….

Jordan Walsh has been a disappointment so far considering he has not cracked the rotation in 2 years. Plays physical, inconsistent shooter, dumb fouls, average to above average defense. But what really is he? No one knows.

Here are my theories: Walsh = Aaron Nesmith try-hard Scheirmann = Hauser wannabee

Going back to my main question - what did 2025 NBA playoff basketball tell us - You can’t play 5 out against well-rounded NBA teams.

2025 Playoff Celtics got dominated by Towns/Brunson. Playing 5 out caused us to be stagnant… pressure skyrocketed… every 3 in the 4th quarter had us slamming our coffee tables. That offense is not sustainable.

So who would you rather have: a Nesmith try-hard or a Hauser wannabee..

You need to be physical in the NBA if you want to be a SF… end of story.
