Why do people think shooting development is just something that happens easily

We’ve all heard the ”wait till he gets a jumper” or the “his jumper in this offseason open gym workout looks smoother“ shit ad nauseam.

I can’t name you specific players this applies to rn but damn near every fanbase has said this about one of their guys this decade.

However, I’d say the most annoying thing is when a guy’s percentages look better so everyone starts victory lapping…then you check the volume and he’s barely shooting at all lmao.

Shooting, more often than not, is a “you have it or you don’t” typa thing. There’s a reason why people use Kawhi or Kidd as their references for shitty shooters who became good ones: they’re the only major success stories lmao.
