[HCSSF] Level 1-90 in less than 15 hours

Hey guys

I made a post here yesterday about the loss of my lvl 99 and was amazed about the positive reaction. I really have no clue why there are so many prejucides against redditusers but I always enjoy the interaction with all of you guys!

Anyway, today I would like to share with you what I did to get over my hillarious death asap. I had almost 26 days played time on my character. Thats a little bit more than 600 hours. Lets not talk about if this is a healthy amount of played time because it definitely isnt and I am totally aware of that. I just say it to give you guys an Idea how potentially frustrated I could be about this one little mistake which costed me almost four weeks of pure played time. I hope this could help to give a better understanding to the appeal of the hardcore mode.

First some quick notes to my run: This isnt by any means a speedrun. It is a run under insanely specific cirumstances which cant be done with a new char in a new economy. The purpose of this is to showcase how you can play hardcore without losing your sanity.

What did I do right after I lost my lvl 99?

first: freaking out and running around in my room like a headless chicken
second: tell my girlfriend that everything is okay when she rushed in my room
third: having dinner and realising that I dont want to eat anything
fourth: saying to myself this was an insane run and with all the stuff I accumulated in my stash I am really looking forward to a nice boosted campaign run
fifth: realising that I keep all my atlas progress and all atlas points for my next char. As soon as I hit endgame I can immediately start blasting again because of my juiced atlas and almost 1000 t15 and 150 t16 waystones in stash

With this in mind I started a new char on the same evening and managed to die again with lvl 41 against viper napuazi. Quick rant: She is my absolute nemesis and already killed my second char this season. I hate her with a passion. I went to bed this evening with no char left and two more deaths in my pocket and a lot of lost ressources during that process.

Btw if anyone is wondering, I have currently holidays and a lot of time to spare in general. So I started again next day with a fresh char. My target was to get past the campaign asap using all my ressources to boost that process up. I played the exact same build since I already was very familiar with it. I was able to finish act 3 normal in 5 hours and 50 minutes and cruel act 3 with 10 hours and 3 minutes played time. I finished the campaign with lvl 63. At this point I could already wear some very solid gearpieces.
I did one tier 5 waystone to get to lvl 65 because this was a threshold for some great items I had in the stash.
After that I followed up with two tier 10 waystones. I juiced a lot of maps with xp on my atlas and I was lucky enough to have a ten breach run in one of the two maps. I went on the first map from lvl 65 to 73 and on the second map from 73 to 80. Fifteen lvls in about 40 minutes felt so good. Both maps were juiced with 53% xp increase from tablets.

After that I maxed 40 t15 waystones with exalts and corrupted them. Out of this 40 corrupted t15 waystones I got six with prefixes only. some of them were delirious aswell. I used all of them for maps with 65%-78% xp increase from tablets. This was enough to get me to lvl 88.9. For the finish I used a t16 map and two more t15 waystones with two prefixes and a suffix. That means I needed 12 waystones in total to get from lvl 63 to lvl 90. And thats the story how I hit lvl 90 in less than 15 hours.
