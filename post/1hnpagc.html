Memphis became just the 3rd team in NBA history to score 155+ points, but have no players score 25+ points

Source: Literally us, Basketball Reference.

Box score to last night’s game: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/202412260MEM.html

Here are the other two times in NBA history that it happened:

  • Nuggets vs. Blazers in 1983
  • Knicks vs. Nationals in 1960

Also, it happened two other times in the ABA:

  • Nuggets vs. Squires in 1976
  • Cougars vs. Chaparrals in 1970

Another fun note: all those teams had a player score at least 22 points, except the Grizzlies last night, whose leading scorer had just 21.
