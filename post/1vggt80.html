Elemental Conflux vs Trinity

Let’s see we have low life already enabled with other spirit gems:
- It’s quite difficult to get 250 affinity on trinity, most I can get with a spark/comet/arc/frostwall build is 200 affinity from the cold/ice damage
- Elemental Conflux provides a decent amount of damage 2⁄3 of the time.

- Ideally in Spark/COC comet setups? I’ve tried using living bomb but it barely provides any fire affinity.
- I know Trinity is an easy gem to slot in Atziri’s communion to get the low-life tech enabled, but you don’t get the max bonuses of trinity itself (difficult to maintain 250 affinity, 15% skill speed).
- Elemental conflux is very random, it can roll a non-damaging element for you 2-3 times in a row, thus not providing any significant elemental damage.
