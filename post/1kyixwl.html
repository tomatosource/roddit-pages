The NBA Gambling Takeover

In the past 5ish years gambling has taken over the NBA. The local networks are owned by Bally’s and FanDuel, constant advertising, analysts reminding people at every break of what the current odds are for the game. I am tired of it. I just want to watch elite hoopers and talk hoops.

I understand people are wasting a huge amount of money on this, so I get why this is happening. The thing I cannot stand is constantly hearing from friends, family, social media posts. and even NBA MEDIA MEMBERS about how this or that player let them down or how they lost them X amount of money. No, YOU lost that money. You chose to gamble on something with huge amounts of variables then they want to cry about losing the money. It is not the players fault, it is not the team’s fault, it is your fault.

I have gotten to the point that I wish sports betting was illegal because I am just so tired of the negative effects it has had on the sport. That said, if you gamble on sports, do you, but please understand that if you do not win it is nobody’s fault but yours. Please stop blaming anyone else besides yourself for gambling away your own money.

This isn’t indicative of anything on this sub, as it doesn’t really happen on this sub. I just needed to get it off my chest because I am tired of hearing it elsewhere.
