[Stein/Fischer] We continue to hear that the Clippers prefer to re-sign Bennedict Mathurin if they can but have not ruled out participating in a sign-and-trade that lands Mathurin elsewhere.

There was a time in May, around the annual draft combine in Chicago, that the LA Clippers were giving some thought to renouncing the rights to restricted free agent Bennedict Mathurin to possibly function as a cap space team this summer.

That was then.

We continue to hear now that the Clippers, while still obviously waiting optimistically to complete their agreed-upon trade to ship Kawhi Leonard back to Toronto, prefer to re-sign Mathurin if they can.

The Clippers — unlike Detroit’s stance with restricted free agent big man Jalen Duren — have not ruled out participating in a sign-and-trade that lands Mathurin elsewhere. Word is they would consider that outcome as well.

Yet the Clippers’ preferred outcome, we hear, is striking their own deal with Mathurin.
