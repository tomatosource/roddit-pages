PSA: You can scale Mana Regeneration from “Soulless Form”

The ability reads “No inherent Mana Regeneration. Regenerate Mana equal to 6% of Max Life per second.” I stupidly assumed that any mana regeneration scaling doesn’t apply at all. That’s wrong, turns out it sets your base mana regen to 6% of life and then any “% Increased Mana Regeneration” scales on top of that, making this ability really really useful.
