Who should win the AL and NL Rookie of the Year Award tomorrow?

My picks:

  • Nick Kurtz in the AL
  • Cade Horton in the NL

For me Nick Kurtz is a no-brainer. He led all rookies in WAR (4.6) wOBA (.419) and wRC+ (170!), while hitting 30+HR and slugging .600+. He should be a unanimous selection.

In the NL, the race is between Drake Baldwin and Cade Horton. Baldwin was 4th in WAR (3.1) among rookie hitters, 7th in wRC+ (125) and 7th in wOBA (.351). Horton only had 22 starts and pitched only 118 innings yet accumulated 2.2 WAR (tied for 5th) and a sub 3.00 ERA (2.67). The case could be made that he was the best starting pitcher in MLB in the 2nd half (where he had a 1.03 ERA). So my pick goes to Horton.

What do you guys think? Who should be honored with the Rookie of the Year award for 2025?
