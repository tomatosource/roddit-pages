Predicting the NBA champion by the 30 game mark

I decided to check all NBA champions (since 1949-50) records in their first 30 games

Zero teams started worse than 17-13. The 1946-47 Warriors and 1947-48 Bullets started 16-14, but technically that was the BAA, and is so long ago, I decided not to include it as part of this data set.

From 1940-50, 9 teams finished between 17-13 and 19-11 and won the NBA championship. These teams are:

  • 1950-51 Rochester Royals (19-11)
  • 1954-55 Syracuse Nationals (17-13)
  • 1977-78 Washington Bullets (18-12)
  • 1989-90 Detroit Pistons (19-11)
  • 2002-03 San Antonio Spurs (19-11)
  • 2003-04 Detroit Pistons (17-13)
  • 2005-06 Miami Heat (17-13)
  • 2020-21 Milwaukee Bucks (17-13)
  • 2022-23 Denver Nuggets (19-11)

The majority of these teams had the benefit of facing weak competition in the finals. The 2023 Heat, 2021 Suns, 2003 Nets, 1978 Sonics were not some juggernaut team on a warpath. The 2004 Lakers were dysfunctional and the 04 Pistons break a lot of conventional metrics. In 1955, the Nationals beat the Fort Wayne Pistons, which were the 2 best teams in the league at 43-29 (49 win team in an 82 game season). and the 1951 Royals beat a 36-30 Knicks team (equivalent of a 45 win team). The best team these teams faced were probably the 2006 Mavericks or 1990 Trail Blazers. All 9 teams were top 3 seeds in their conference by the end of the year, which I only see as possible in the East this season.

Teams with 17-19 wins in their first 30 are closer to dark horse contenders that ended up with a favorable path/matchup, and are unlikely champions, but not impossible under the right circumstances.

All 76 of these champions averaged out to 22.37 wins out of their first 30 games.

28 teams (36.8%) finished 20-22 wins. 39 teams (51.3%) finished 23+ wins. This means over 88% of championship teams in the last 76 seasons started at least 20-10.

If we group them as “Favorites” “Contenders” and “Dark Horses” this is where we would currently stand after 30 games:

Favorites (23-7 or better):

  • Oklahoma City Thunder (26-4)
  • Detroit Pistons (24-6)
  • San Antonio Spurs (23-7)

Contenders (20-10, 21-9, 22-8 teams):

  • Denver Nuggets (22-8)
  • New York Knicks (21-9)
  • Minnesota Timberwolves (20-10)
  • Los Angeles Lakers (20-10)
  • Houston Rockets (if they beat Pacers tonight)

Dark Horses (17-13, 18-12, 19-11 teams):

  • Houston Rockets (if they lose to Pacers tonight)
  • Boston Celtics (19-11)
  • Orlando Magic (17-13)
  • Toronto Raptors (17-13)
  • Phoenix Suns (17-13)

Every other team started 16-14 or worse through their first 30 games, thus eliminating them from championship contention.

Teams newly eliminated from the 20 game checkpoint include the Miami Heat, Cleveland Cavaliers, Atlanta Hawks, Philadelphia 76ers, and Golden State Warriors.

Raptors, Magic and Suns have fallen from contenders to dark horses. Spurs have jumped up to tier 1, Lakers and Rockets have fallen to tier 2⁄3.

I’ll revise the list again at the 41 game mark
