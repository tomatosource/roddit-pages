The league not calling traveling has triggered the slow death of smaller non-star guards/wings and is causing the Wagnerization of the league…

Smaller players (5’11-6’3) have always been a fixture of the league because of 3 skills they bring to the table:

  1. Ball retention + ball handling
  2. Higher ball skills (passing/shooting etc)
  3. Screen navigation

The Franz Wagners of today’s game…

This newer generation of wings (6’6-6’10) are all very skilled (can shoot, can pass, can finish) and closed the skill-gap to the smaller guards.

With tall wings, you can switch everything and not worry too much about screen navigation.

Their only weakness used to be their easily-strippable handle in the post/through crowded lanes. And the league’s abject lack of enforcement on traveling has made the Wagners the most valuable player-type in the league.

What the league needs to do

  1. Start enforcing pivot foot travels: EVERY wing/big in the post takes extra tiny steps to reposition and get a better angle in the post. It makes them unguardable.

  2. Be stricter about ball-palming: It’s horrendous to watch the stars just take wacky dribbles by fully palming the ball. A gather step is now 3-4 steps long because they’re cupping the ball with one hand, and that needs to stop.

  3. Fucking call travels on DHOs: Bigs and wings get complete immunity from moving multiple steps on a dead dribble, that needs to stop.

All I ask for is to be able to see a small guard succeed in 2035.
