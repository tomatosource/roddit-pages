Solution to Charges (builder spender)

So with PoE 2 in its current state, the three charge types basically have no passive benefits and no real identity. Meanwhile, some skills still need specific charges to function, which just makes the whole system feel clunky. Most of the community seems to hate interacting with charges altogether.

My suggestion? Instead of deleting charges or ditching the builder–spender idea, just merge everything into one unified charge type. It’d be way easier for all parts of the passive tree to generate, and it would actually make charge-spending skills feel good to use again.
