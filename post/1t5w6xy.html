Browse the gemcutting menu without an uncut gem

With tomorrow’s announcement and the new release coming up, I wanted to share this for anyone who might find it useful: https://poe2gems.com/

I made the site to provide a quick way to browse the gemcutting menu outside the game, without needing an uncut gem or jumping between menus.

Features:

  • browse skill gems and support gems in your browser
  • search/filter gems quickly
  • view gem details and skill preview videos without being in-game
  • useful on desktop or mobile
  • no ads

I do my best to get things updated as soon as possible after each patch, which isn’t too bad thanks to the great open source tools built by other community members.
