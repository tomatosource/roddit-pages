My precious 1125 dps crossbow

https://preview.redd.it/4fhigiv77ypf1.png?width=900&format=png&auto=webp&s=fc254c3adcd069afc2204b77f61e3355a9c01eed

Browsing crossbow market with 1100 dps and they are all very pricey for 80+ divines and not to my liking so decided to craft 1 myself.

Rule 10: Step 1: Find the crossbow with T1 increased physical damage not corrupted. I used search “increased physical damage min 170% max 179%), and empty prefix set maximum to 1 (Because magic ones are like 5 div each). Found 1 with only 1 divine with 5 affix total. Yolo fracture, hit T1 increased physical damage first try. Off to good start.

Step 2: Annul down 1 mod. Chaos spam 1200 times, hit T2 adds physical damage 5-7 times, but not happy with it. Tried greater chaos spam. Hit T1 add physical damage max roll after 10 divine worth of greater chaos spam.

Step 3: Ancient Jawbone + Omen of sinistral necromancy to fill the prefix (No unveil yet).

Step 4: Exalt to get 1 random suffix. Then perfect essence of battle + Omen of dextral crystallisation to guarrantee remove the random suffix.

Step 5: Exalt to get another random suffix. Then perfect essence of haste + perfect essence of battle + Omen of dextral crystallisation spam until hit +6 all skills and chance to gain onslaught.

Step 6: Omen of homo + Greater exalt hoping to hit leech as mana, or mana per enemy killed. Missed. Hit leech as life instead.

Step 7: Unveil, didnt get hybrid increased physical damage and accuracy. Had to use omen of light, ancient jawbone, abyssal echoes 3 times until hit T1 said hybrid.

Step 8: Omen of whittling + Greater chaos spam 4 times and get mana per enemies killed.

Total cost: probably 60-70 divine. Very happy with the result. This league crafting is awesome.
