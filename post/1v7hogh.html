Phenomenal ARPG, but the juice is not worth the squeeze.

POE2 is unmatched in its genre in my opinion. Credit where it’s due, incredible ARPG. But juicing needs tuning imo.

TLDR: Juicing maps is not rewarding enough (outside of mechanics like ritual etc).

I ran 50+ city maps with 4 rarity/effectiveness tablets, 200% delirium, full atlas tree, crafted waystones either 8-mod or T16 with ~95% gear rarity. Averaged about 1-2 divs per map (yes there are other rewards, but div drops are the obvious measuring stick) - on cleansed maps I averaged 1 fracturing orb per 10-20 maps, if that?

And then I’ll add 0 juice to some T15 travel maps and get average like 0-1 divs per map, like what am I even doing all of this for?

Yes there are mechanics like ritual/expedition that are more lucrative in certain ways and I think those are in decent spots, but why does it take SO much juice to get a map’s actual, baseline floor loot feeling like it’s actually valuable? I mean by the time I’m strong enough to do any content that’s really churning out a satisfying amount of divs, gaining more power is useless anyway??

Sure it would just shift the economy but there’s still SSF and I could at least gather my own crafting materials for once.

Are there more ways I could be inline with the meta? Probably, but I really don’t think I should have to leverage every single strategy and metric just to finally start seeing more than 2 divs per map.

And if the problem is that I don’t have enough gear rarity, then that’s just another reason why gear rarity is the worst mechanic in the game.

Anyway, there’s the rant.
