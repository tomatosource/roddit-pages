The case for Bobby Abreu.

This one has always really bothered me. I’m going to present to you 2 players. They debuted in the same season, played the same position, and had the following accomplishments. Neither has been linked to PED use.

Player A: debuted in 1996, broke out in 1998 with 7.4 bWAR, and put up consistently great offensive numbers for the next decade. Highest bWAR totals of 7.4, 7.1, 6.0, 5.7, 5.6. Finished with 59.5 bWAR in 16 seasons and 2147 games.

Player B: debuted in 1996, broke out in 1998 with 6.4 bWAR, and put up consistently great offensive numbers for the next decade. Highest bWAR totals of 6.6, 6.4, 6.2, 6.1, 5.8. Finished with 60.2 bWAR in 18 seasons and 2425 games.

Player A is Vladimir Guerrero who made the HoF in his 2nd year of eligibility with 92.9% of the vote.

Player B is Bobby Abreu who barely even stayed on the HoF ballot with 5.5% of the vote in his 1st year of eligibility. His support has slowly increased but he’s still losing votes to far worse players like Jimmy Rollins or Omar Vizquel and he’s not anywhere close to HoF election.

On a surface level, Guerrero’s accomplishments are much more impressive. He won an MVP, and was a 9x all-star and 8x silver slugger. He had a sexy .318 batting average and could hit a ball to the moon.

Abreu, criminally, was just a 2x all-star and 1x silver slugger and 1x gold glove. His batting average was .291 and he didn’t have Guerrero’s raw power or bad-ball hitting ability. Abreu, however, was the Venezuelan god of walks, walking 1476 times in his career, giving him a robust .395 lifetime OBP, and stole 400 bases while getting caught only 128 times. Guerrero, despite having a reputation for being a 5-tool player, was a horrendous base stealer (181 SB vs 94 CS) and was not a good fielder outside of his A+ arm.

Abreu was criminally underrated and underappreciated during his career, and he’s still underrated today when voters judge him through the flawed lenses of how he was seen during his career (only a 2x all-star despite 7 straight seasons of at least 5 bWAR).

Abreu is a HoFer in my book.
