Pack size reduction was a necessary change

As someone who has killed every pinnacle boss at every difficulty every league, this pack size change was much needed. I know most people in this sub are wondering who asked for pack size reduction but remember that most casual players are probably not browsing this subreddit. By decreasing the pack size and buffing the loot it allows casual players to have a much better endgame experience while not making loot feel like absolute shit. It also allows GGG to add more ways to scale pack size for the super endgame players in the future to reach similar pack size to previous leagues while adding 40% more loot. Another bonus is your computer won’t be heating up your entire house while doing juiced maps (maybe this is a con considering its winter)
