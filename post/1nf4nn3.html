PSA - Giannis plays Alperen Sengun today at 2pm EST in the EuroBasket semifinal between Greece and Türkiye, international basketball at its finest!

If you’re a fan of the NBA and want to see some high level basketball do not miss the game today where Greece takes on Türkiye.

Both of these NBA stars seek their first ever medals in international competition, a win today would cement at least the silver medal and would be a huge addition to their resume.

Both of these countries are former basketball titans that seek a return to glory.

Other notable players for NBA fans:

Türkiye:

  • Shane Larkin, U of Miami legend, Knicks and Celtics legend. He is the star point guard who’s been playing in Türkiye for years at this point and has great chemistry with the team.

  • Cedi Osman, Cavs legend he has been a perfect complement to Senguns game cutting and hitting open shots and playing great defense

  • Adem Bona, 76ers center this year who is a defensive stud

  • Furkan Korkmaz, former 76ers player who routinely takes and makes crazy 3 pointers

Greece:

  • Tyler Dorsey, former Hawks draft pick and Mavs player and absolute gunner off the dribble from 3

  • Kostas antetokounmpo, playing like he could legit be a center in the NBA making crazy blocks left and right

  • Alexandros Samodurov, a possible future NBA draft pick, a big wing who can shoot

  • Kostas Papanikolaou, Rockets legend and one of the longest tenured Greek players
