Wishlist for the future

With 0.5 coming out and GGG addressing everything from balance to drops to skills, I thought it would be good for the community to define some things they would like to see come to the game in the future. So please add to the list hopefully GGG may see this and consider so of them.

​

Personally things I would love to see:

​

  1. Uber versions of bosses with additional drop pools.

  2. Truly scalable content (delve) something where no matter how meta your build is you can always try to scale further.

  3. A permanent longtime challenge reward designed to be completed over multiple seasons for longer time players (hideout/costume/totem) but designed to challenges like ‘kill arbiter of divinity 2000 times’, ‘complete 15000 Delirious maps’

  4. More build defining uniques - too many uniques currently don’t feel unique they just feel like a rare item with a stat boost somewhere.

  5. Review and potential re-design of older ascendancies, we know that some ascendancies were designed with a slower more impactful gameplay in mind which has changed throughout early access.

  6. A review on mace design, with content getting easier having mace still be so slow just feels awful endgame compared to other weapon types.

​
