Let’s share tips about new things in 0.5

(will try to update the post)

  • loot filter sounds are not working for now, both default Neversink and custom ones
  • if you got stuck in infinite loading that doesn’t go away when you restart the game, restart again and click the house button (log in town)
  • Rune encounters are a good source of runes, artificer orbs, whetstones and scraps even in Act 1, so I wouldn’t bother with hauling quality and socket items to town for salvaging
  • check rune encounters in general, they can give you Lesser Jeweller pretty early in Act 2, also Exalts and Regals are abundant. Support Gems too, also Spirit gems - the only realiable source of upgrading them in the acts that I know of
  • whenever you see Farrow in the map, go to him, he may be standing next to an Ancient Beacon, and you need a couple of them to enter his new location in Act 3. Btw, are these beacons shared across the acts?
  • the entrance to Act 3 Azak bog is now in Matlan Waterways, so as waypoint. In my layout the final lever was right past this waypoint
  • don’t skip Shark in Act 4, quest rewards now include permanent stats (choice of armour/evasion/es stats, equivalent in power to some notables)
