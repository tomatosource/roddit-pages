There should be a “players inducted for non-playing reasons” category for HOF players

Fernando Valenzuela not only didn’t get enough votes to get elected this year, he got so few votes that he and the steroid guys won’t even be on the ballot the next time this committee comes around.

Now, to be sure, Fernando isn’t, IMO, a HOF player. But as far as overall importance, I think he is a HOF member. The problem is that as we all know, statistics are an incredibly important part of baseball and the HOF, and many arguments go “well player X is clearly better than player Y, so player X should be in”, and so I think there’s a hesitancy to put someone like Valenzuela in because then there’d be more reason to have a guy better than Valenzuela but still clearly not HOF-worthy stats-wise to be in.

(While more intangible bits do certainly play a role, usually they only make a difference if the player in question was a lot closer to HOF-worthy than someone like Valenzuela was. There’s some guys who were borderline but who got over the hump because they then were good announcers or coaches or had a particularly historic moment, for example, or they played so long ago they literally helped create baseball.)

But anyway, I think there should be a “playing contributors” category to allow important parts of baseball history in even if they are not strictly HOF players. Guys like Valenzeula, Curt Flood, maybe Hideo Nomo, etc.
