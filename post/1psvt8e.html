Druid skills are quietly boosting a lot of other classes

One thing I don’t see talked about enough is how much Druid skill gems are helping non-Druid builds.

Warrior leveling feels way smoother now. Wolf skills as a bridge before Shield Wall fixes a long-standing pain point, and it actually speeds up early progression quite a bit for people racing to maps.

Plant-tagged gems are another sleeper hit. They open up some interesting CoC setups, especially for Blood Mage, which suddenly has new toys to play with instead of recycling the same old patterns.

Wolfpack is also surprisingly flexible. It’s an easy damage boost for summoner-style builds or anything that benefits from extra allies on the field.

And Oil Barrage? Niche, but some builds are already using it as a dedicated boss tool with decent results.

Overall, Druid didn’t just add a new class but also expanded the sandbox for everyone else.
