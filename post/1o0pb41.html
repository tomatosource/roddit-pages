Item Rarity

Just finished watching a couple takes on Dreamcore’s item rarity vid. Has GGG ever commented on making item rarity a stat attached to character instead of gear?

A system where you could do a side mechanic to increase a character’s “divine insight” or “worldy experience” or whatever to boost up magic find over the duration of a league sounds like it’d be a huge improvement over the current system of shoehorning in magic find to the detriment of your build. I’m all for having somewhat less effective gear rolls in the pool to maintain a dam on power creep and to keep those good rolls giving dopamine hits, but it doesn’t feel item rarity belongs in this arena to me.
