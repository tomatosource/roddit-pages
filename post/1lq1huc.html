NBA players will forgo ~$480 million from the escrow fund set aside to ensure the revenue split between the players and the league laid out in the CBA.

Source: https://bsky.app/profile/levakabas.bsky.social/post/3lsynuumbqc2z

The CBA (this one and others back to the 90s) calls for players and owners to split “basketball related income” (BRI) at a specified rate - 51% goes to players, the rest to ownership. To make this work, players don’t actually get their full salaries during the season - a chunk is left in an escrow fund, and then distributed to the players after BRI is determined. When BRI is lower than expected, salaries take a hit (after the fact) - this year, it was quite a bit lower, and players will be losing out on almost 10% of their salaries by not “retaining” the money they left in the escrow fund.
