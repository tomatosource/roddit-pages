Triple Flat Damage Refined Breach Ring Update (Unfinished Craft, Follow-up)

Yesterday I posted a triple T1 flat damage refined breach ring craft and wanted to follow up today with the big-vaals version of the craft, which pushes flat damage to the highest possible outside of corruption/sanctification or a +1 Prefix Dusk Ring.

The main difference for this craft is upping the quality of the ring from 65% to 75%, which requires using the new Vaal infusors currency. I’ve only finished the prefixes on this ring, so I will only cover up to there. This craft has taken me over a week of on-and-off playing.

Crafting Steps:

  1. Buy uncorrupted/unfractured/unsanctified iLvl 81+ Refined Breach Ring (55div)

  2. Use Essence of Breach to add +20 to maximum quality

  3. Spam Reaver Catalyst to 65% quality (~15Div)

  4. Spam Vaal Catalyzing Infusor to add 75% quality* (~25div when you successfully hit 75% quality, 15Div on average before corruption & brick).

*I cannot emphasize enough how brutal step 4 is. Each increased % over 65% quality has a higher likelihood to corrput the ring. Allegedly you are going to brick your ring 9⁄10 times to get to 75%. I was unlucky and bricked 18 rings before finally hitting 75% uncorrupted, and each attempt costs about 90div before you brick. I easily spent 1500 divs to get to this point.

  1. Annul down to 1 mod, chaos spam for any T1 flat damage.

  2. Divine T1 flat damage to perfect.

  3. Perfect Exalt + Omen of Greater Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation to add 2 suffixes.

  4. Preserved Collarbone to add unrevealed desecration to prep for fracture.

  5. Fracture perfectly divined T1 flat damage (1⁄3 hit chance)

  6. Chaos spam for second T1 flat damage

  7. Omen of Sinistral Necromancy + Ancient Collarbone for last T1 flat damage.

  8. Divine Prefixes in case suffixes roll well.

The Result of the craft thus far:

181.5 flat damage Refined Breach Ring. Highest unsanctified flat damage on trade.

This ring is 1 off perfectly rolled prefixes (Cold Damage)

When I finish up the suffixes I will target Rarity & Attack Speed using Swift Alloy, and ideally T1 All Res or T1 Chaos Res.
