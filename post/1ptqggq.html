Pure life (no es) is in rough spot.

Firstly, I’m going to say that it is NOT impossible to play pure life build, either armour, block-based, or evasion-based.

BUT, compared to energy shield, it’s so much harder and expensive to gear for, and it’s just plain worse than pure ES or hybrid.

It makes me worried for the future of the game when it’s so easy to make a viable es character and the complete opposite for life. As an ES, all you need to do is pick gear with high ES and pick passives that synergize with ES, and you have so many damn good options. CI, Eldritch battery, mind over matter. You can make a giant lifepool that will recharge quickly and there are also many fantastic skills like convalescence, ghost dance, etc.

What does life get? Life leech, some regen. and 3 passives that increase life by 11 percent ( 5 is from gem enthusiast )

I don’t understand why there can’t be some more life on the passive tree. As a sorceress or a monk, I’m not going to go to that side of a tree to pick life when I have ES available right there. They don’t want life to be auto-included for every build, but isn’t ES pretty much already that?
