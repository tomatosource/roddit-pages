If you had to move White to Houston, what would you want?

This was something that was discussed a lot in podcasts over the Summer, but most of you understandably weren’t ready to entertain discussion on it because you still thought this was a contender.

Now that you’re seeing what this team truly is (35 win team at most), is there a deal with Houston for Derrick White that would intrigue you?

Here’s my thinking on this. Boston probably still is slightly too good to really bottom out. Bottoming out, imo, is by far our best path towards retooling by landing a top 5 pick. I’d also love to pick up a blue chip prospect or two to spend this season developing, because as is I just don’t think we have a single youngster on this roster who qualifies. Hugo is interesting, but realistically he’s a 28th pick that might have a bench player ceiling.

White wasn’t a particularly great shooter in San Antonio, but as a 4th option he flourished in Boston. It’s early, but if these first 3 games are any indication, he’s going to struggle being a 2nd option and given he’s already 31 years old, his trade value probably peaked over the Summer.

If it’s true that guys post achilles tear take 2 full season to get back to “feeling normal”, we might not be seeing Tatum approaching 100% until 2027-28 when White will be 33 years old.

After VanVleet’s injury, people like Bill Simmons talked about how Houston was now desperate for a starting PG and pointed to people like Reed Sheppard (last year’s #3 pick who is only 21 years old) as a “project” that they’d likely move in a package if they could get a player like White.

I don’t know if Sheppard will ever pan out, but he’s exactly that type of “raw young high ceiling” guy that makes sense to spend a season developing during a tank year. Who knows what he might look like in year 3. Those leaps happen quickly. He had a strong preseason and actually put up 29 points, 6 rebounds, 6 assists, 4 steals and 3 blocks in their final preseason game, but that’s only preseason. So far through the first two regular season games, (both losses), he hasn’t yet solidified himself as a starter. Actually saw his minutes drop from 28 to 20 in game 2. Houston lost both games so in theory they could still be itching to add a Derrick White level talent while their Durant window is still open.

What might Sheppard look like in 2027-28 as a 23 year old? These leaps happen sooner than people realize. We gave up the Sengun pick to bring back Horford and by year 3, Sengun had leaped into a 20 and 10 all-star level center. It’s not unthinkable that a 3rd year Sheppard + the additional assets acquired from moving White could be better than White next season. Especially if you factor in the massive value add of tanking for a top pick. But clearly it would have to be more than just Sheppard for it to work financially and make sense for Boston. How much more?

Houston has tradeable firsts. As part of the deal maybe you take back VanVleet (25 mil with a player option next season) since he’s not hurting the tank job this season. VanVleet + Sheppard (10.6) already exceeds White’s salary. But you know, maybe you extend the deal to be White + Simons (27 mil) to send out 55 mil and take back someone else who intrigues you like Jabari Smith + multiple future firsts.

TL;DR: Just throwing it out there as a hypothetical. If Boston were ready to pull the tank switch and move White for building blocks, picks and a fast pass to the top of the lotto, what would it an acceptable trade return from Houston for Derrick White?
