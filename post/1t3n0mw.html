Jokic and Giannis have nearly identical basketball resumes. Why is there such a huge gap in perception and all-time ranking between the two?

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/a/antetgi01.html

Giannis - 1x Championship, 1x Finals MVP, 2x MVP, 1x DPOY, 9x All-NBA, 10x All-Star, 5x All-Defense, 1x MIP, various other accolades (1x ASG MVP, 1x NBA Cup, 1x NBA Cup MVP)

Jokic - 1x Championship, 1x Finals MVP, 3x MVP, 7x All-NBA, 8x All-Star

Both rose to superstardom at the same time and peaked in the exact same era. Both have had to carry flawed supporting casts, both only made it out of the second round twice, both only have one ring that involved a good amount of luck. Jokic gives you historic offensive impact but is a negative on defense, Giannis is a wrecking ball on both ends of the floor and is an all time great defender. They’re basically even. Even if you favor one over the other, it’s a tiny difference.

Why do so many people, especially Bill Simmons, Zach Lowe, the Ringer folk, etc. as well as people on here rank Jokic so much higher? I’ve seen hot takes where people rank Jokic above Bird, Magic, Hakeem, Shaq, etc. while Giannis feels almost forgotten in all time convos.
