Crafting is fun and exciting!

I’ve been playing POE1 on and off since 2013 and I still struggle with crafting. I do simple crafts but not final products. I find it too complicated to start.

In POE2 it seems different. My friend and I have been heavily crafting the past few days and we can’t stop. We end up doing crafts for both our own use and to sell. This is our first time doing profit crafting. Discovering new crafts is fun. RNG gamble adds to the rush when you hit something good.

I like the crafting system based on omens, desecration and the economics of it. The instant buyout is truly a godsend. Crafting is simple enough to start, but at the same time allows deeper more complex crafts.

Even if you don’t have money yet or can’t low-mid crafts, you still have access to perfect transmute and augment bases. Some of the bases really sell very well.

Feedback wise, I still find the use of omens somewhat clunky. I rather have access to crafting table with proper UI. The crafting options will then appear based on the currency or mats that you have in stash. Maybe. Just ideas that allows newer players to learn.
