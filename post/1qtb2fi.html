Insane Team Comebacks?

Like I said yesterday, every team has its ups and downs throughout the seasons. Some teams start off horribly and then claw their way back to a playoff spot, and some teams start off excellent and then fall apart to end the season. Yesterday, we focused on the latter. Today, we’re focusing on the former.

So, with that said, what do you think was the most impressive turnaround by a team to finish their season, and why?

There’s several good picks for me. But the 2011 Cardinals going from 10 1⁄2 games out of a playoff spot in August, to not only clinching a Wild Card spot, but going on to win the World Series as a massive underdog, is probably my #1.

Btw, you don’t have to pick teams that won the title that year. It could be any insane turnaround that helped a once-struggling team finish strong.
