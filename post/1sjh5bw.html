Does PoE2 Feel Too Soulslike?

First of all, I really love PoE2. I prefer this one over PoE1, and I especially like the improvements in the graphics and animations.

However, even though it’s a hack-and-slash game, the Soulslike elements feel a bit too strong, making progressing through each act feel unnecessarily tedious.

I don’t mind that regular enemies are somewhat tough. In fact, that allows me to appreciate their animations and designs more deeply, making combat feel more immersive.

The problem lies with the boss fights. The essence of a hack-and-slash game should be taking down enemies one after another. The fast-paced, exhilarating nature of that gameplay doesn’t seem to mesh well with the tense, intricate boss battles expected of a Souls-like game.

In Diablo-style games, I believe a good tempo and flow are more important than intense tension.

Of course, I get it. I’ve tried increasing my defense and adjusting my damage output. But that’s not the core of the issue.

It’s not that I dislike the difficulty itself. I just want to play a little more relaxed and carefree.

I’d like to hear your opinions. Whether you agree or disagree is fine, but please refrain from replying with comments like “then don’t play it” or “just play PoE 1.”
