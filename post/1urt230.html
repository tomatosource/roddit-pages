Is Walker Kessler missing his first name?

Not a basketball take, don’t have strong Kessler opinions, hope he blocks a million shots for the Lakers. But “Walker Kessler” is not a complete name and I need backup on this.

I don’t even care what word goes in front, I just need one (1) first name before “Walker” or this whole thing reads like someone hit ctrl+z on his birth certificate.
