Is Walker Kessler missing his first name?

Not a basketball take, don’t have strong Kessler opinions, hope he blocks a million shots for the Lakers. But “Walker Kessler” is not a complete name and I need backup on this.

“Walker” reads like a middle name. “Kessler” reads like a last name. Put them together and my brain just sits there waiting for a first name that never loads.

I looked it up hoping for closure. His middle name is Ross. Doesn’t help, the problem was never the middle name. Then I found out his brother is named Houston. Houston Kessler. This family just does this to people.

I don’t even care what word goes in front, I just need one (1) first name before “Walker” or this whole thing reads like someone hit ctrl+z on his birth certificate.

Say it out loud: **Andrew Walker Kessler**. Doesn’t that scan better? Am I the only one who sees this or is this an actual glitch in the matrix.
