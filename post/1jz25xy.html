Pulling off “Combos” FEELS GREAT.

The first few dozen times. Eventually it becomes repetitive and the novelty wears off.

I got to endgame on my warrior. After several maps of doing LEAP SLAM &gt; BONESHATTER &gt; LEAP SLAM &gt; BONESHATTER &gt; LEAP SLAM &gt; BONESHATTER &gt; LEAP SLAM &gt; BONESHATTER &gt; * encounters rare* WARCRY&gt; HAMMER OF THE GOD’S &gt; PERFECT STRIKE &gt; REPEAT.

Even with 2 attack speed clusters, attack speed supports, 18% attack speed on my weapon, and 30% movement speed on my boots and DESPITE boneshatter clearing packs, and executing rares and bosses with a several button combo, it still ends up feeling kind of monotonous eventually. I think this is compounded by the pauses between animations and wind-ups.

I used to play World Of Warcraft ALOT. In POE2 it feels like having high network latency in WoW, where you press a button and nothing happens for 500MS and then your ability finally goes off. Of course there are delays between attacks in WoW (GCD) but the initial button press happens instantly. There are also abilities that you can use simultaneously with other skills like interupts and some cooldowns. In POE2 there is a delay before everything (except potions) and not after. I hesitate to make this comparison (please forgive me) but D4 Barbarian, TRY IT OUT. You are leaping, shouting, and weaving attacks, and using your Ultimate (comboing) and it feels fluid, snappy, and responsive.

Anyway, back to my initial point; The “thrill” of pulling off the combo for the 1000th time isn’t inherently more exciting or rewarding than pressing 1 button for the 1000th time. Particularly if the “combo” feels sluggish to pull off. Given both options end up being repetative, then spamming one fast responsive button ends up becoming preferable.

This is even worse in multiplayer by the way. You are trying to execute a 5 second combo, while your friend just spams one button and kills the enemies before you finish a couple animations.
