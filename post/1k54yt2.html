Where’s the Juice?

I’m running 92% rarity on my gear. I even found a 4-tower overlap and stacked 12 tablets in those towers. Most of the tablets rolled additional rarity, bringing the total to 92% rarity and 34% quantity on those maps. I’m running maps with 90%–130% rarity, then I pop Distilled Greed for another 24%. With full atlas map effect bonuses, my tooltip shows up to 500% rarity inside the map.

I’ve completed all T4 bosses. I’ve done the setup. I’ve done the grind. And what’s the result?

Maybe 20 exalts worth of loot per map. Seriously?

Let’s be real: it takes the average player 3–5 gaming sessions just to find the towers overlap, stack the tablets, roll the maps, and run them. That’s a lot of time and planning. And for what? Nothing valuable or exciting, no dopamine detected.

Now compare that to PoE1:

  • I don’t need to search for some random tower overlaps.
  • I don’t need to fish for one specific map prefix.
  • I can roll any map as hard as I want, as long as I have the currency.
  • I can sink 1–2 divines into every single map and make it a loot pinata.
  • I can spend 50 divs and have days’ worth of maps, each paying out 2–3 divines reliably.

In PoE1, challenge and rewards scale with investment, effort and knowladge. In PoE2 right now, investment feels like a gamble, a time sink with zero guarantee of fun or profit.

I’m not even asking for insane loot explosions, but at least give players some consistency for all the prep we do.

Rant over. If anyone’s cracked the code and actually found a good strat that feels rewarding, I’m all ears, because right now, this is making me want to log out for good.
