On A Team That Needs Rebounding And Paint Protection…Payton Pritchard Is Doing Both?

I keep on seeing people upset about our starting lineup, but the fact of the matter is our starting lineup is actually doing pretty darn good. They are currently a +26 +/- in 3 games, good for 2nd among all lineups that have been played at all this year.

The main guy I see people wanting to bench is Pritchard, but he’s actually one guy doing the exact things that we need.

PP’s Rebounding

Team Rebounds Per Game Leaders:

  1. Queta
  2. PP

Team DReb% leaders:

  1. Boucher
  2. Queta
  3. Tillman
  4. Scheierman
  5. PP

Our biggest weakness right now is rebounding and PP is doing way more than his fair share.

PP’s Paint Protection

I thought this was fascinating and it’s what actually sparked me to make this post.

Defending Field Goals Within 6 Feet of the Basket:

  • PP has been defending 3.3 shots per game within 6 feet of the basket
  • Opponents are shooting 20.7% worse on those shots defended by PP

For reference, that is currently top 18 in the entire NBA. Someone like Jalen Duren is defending 3.7 such shots per game and opponents are shooting 21.8% worse. He’s an actual center.

Low Sample Size Theater

Obviously, this is low sample size theater, but I mostly care about trends right now. It’s something to keep an eye on and the trend here is fantastic for PP. These are the things Jrue Holiday was doing for us. He was rebounding, he was defending the paint, and he was defending the perimeter. If we are going to contend and do so cheapy, this is the kind of thing we need to be on the lookout for.

PP won’t keep up those paint protection and rebounding numbers, but can he get into the ballpark of where Jrue was last year? If so, that will be huge for us long-term.

Don’t bench this dude. He’s giving us what we need, even though he’s cold from 3.

TL;DR

Fast PP is playing like Big PP.
