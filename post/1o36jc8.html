So I went to watch the Knick’s last night….

Wow. First time ever watching an NBA game (yes I appreciate pre season!) and I was completely blown away.

Having grown up in the UK this was my first time watching American sports - I’ve never witnessed anything like it.

The music, crowd, dancing - everything was on another level - even the T shirt cannons looked like military grade anti aircraft guns 🤣.

No one at all appeared drunk and I saw no fights and at no point had anyone trying screaming or swearing at me (pretty common in uk premier league).

Was this experience normal or did I get lucky?

I bought a ticket last minute and it was my highlight of my holiday here.
