What is your team’s far and away worst decision ever made?

For the pistons it has to be the Darko pick over Carmelo. The Pistons were already a good team and had the opportunity to add someone great. Carmelo Anthony was considered the easy pick but for some reason Dumars felt the need to roll the dice on Darko. Carmelo could’ve turned the Pistons into a dynasty and been the best player on an already excellent team. Darko was just a waste of a pick. Had the Pistons drafted Carmelo then the window would’ve stayed open for far longer. Instead, they came up short a few times and then entered a very long, very bad rebuild that didn’t end until Cade.
