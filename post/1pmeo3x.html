Act 2 & 3 Map sizes need reduced by a minimum of 20% across the board

GGG, you’ve made a beautiful game in PoE 2. It’s extremely fun and well polished and it has great potential for replayability in the future. The campaign is, and i feel confident saying this and speaking for the average player, really well done and engaging.

There’s just one problem, and that is Acts 2 and 3 and the purposeless map size and maze-like layouts. It is the only friction point for me in the campaign and its a big one. Every single time I board that caravan for the first time in a league, all i can think is “here we fucking go again”. Then i get to the Ziggurat camp and think “i can just quit, I don’t HAVE to play this.” THEN i get to what I would say is the worst campaign zone in the entire game, Jiquani’s Machinarium/Sanctum, and I have to heavily resist the urge to alt+f4.

I’m kindly asking you, GGG, to please make Act 2 and 3 less grueling somehow. There are many good solutions proposed by the player base, and if you’re dead set on not reducing map size, then please allow some sort of map markers for quest objectives to reduce the amount of aimlessness and frustrating searching.

-Signed, an average player.
