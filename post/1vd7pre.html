An Underrated Impact of Losing Jaylen Brown: Rim Pressure

Ever since the Jaylen trade, most of the conversation has been about replacing his scoring. But I keep coming back to something else.

The Celtics finished the 2025–26 season last in the league in free throw attempts (18.7 per game).

What makes that weird is they weren’t a team that never attacked the basket. They averaged 48.3 drives per game (around league average) and actually shot **51.3% on those drives**, which was one of the best marks in the NBA.

So they got into the paint. They finished well. They just… never seemed to get to the line.

That’s why I think losing Jaylen could end up hurting in ways that don’t immediately show up in the box score.

I’m not saying he was SGA or Luka when it came to drawing fouls. He wasn’t. But he was one of the few guys on the roster who could consistently get downhill and put pressure on the defense. Even if it didn’t always end in free throws, defenses had to account for it.

Maybe this is more about Mazzulla’s system than personnel. Maybe the roster just isn’t built to play through contact. Or maybe it’s a little of both.

Either way, if the Celtics were already dead last in free throw attempts *with* Jaylen, I’m really interested to see how they generate easier points now that he’s gone.

Am I overthinking this, or does anyone else think this could become a bigger issue than replacing his scoring?
