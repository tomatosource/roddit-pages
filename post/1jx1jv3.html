First time playing through the campaign and I am impressed.

Just wanted to give my two cents:

Coming from PoE to PoE2 and I had the time to start the campaign. Not following any guides and just doing a Witch minion build to see the mechanics from a distance and learn all the boss patterns by observing. Nearing the end of Act 3 as Infernalist and it has been smooth sailing so far.

What I like:

  • Difficulty level is good, I die sometimes but not too often.
  • The game is absolutely gorgeous, story is great.
  • Enemy and character mechanics are great, I love the WASD movement option so much.
  • The ascendancy experience is way better, both the trial of the Sekhemas and Chaos is done really well.
  • Enjoying the mini bosses and rares that are actually dangerous.

What I don’t like:

  • Maps are slightly too big and mob density is kind of low.
  • Crafting currency drops (exalts, regals) is way too low. I love the crafting mechanics but I have trouble getting good results. Still I get by with checking the shops every level and crafting normal items in hopefully good magic items.
  • Socketed weapons/armour drop way too late in the game, I am drowning in runes but no place to put them.
  • Skill system is a lot better, would love some more support skill options in the future.
  • The league mechanic is kinda meh, sometimes the spirit didn’t even spawn and the rewards are also quite lackluster.
