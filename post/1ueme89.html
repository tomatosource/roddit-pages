Most likely Jaylen /Giannis explanation

Pure speculation but I think this is the most likely explanation given everything that went down in terms of trade offers / rumors:

Brown has basically said to Stevens, Hey I kinda enjoyed this having my own team thing, if you guys have a good trade offer that you like and gets me my own team, I’m cool with it. If not, this is still a good situation. I don’t to blow up a good thing purely to get my own team. JB is a smart guy and probably realizes that they’re aren’t many situations out there that are going to be better than the Celtics.

Stevens takes this to heart, rightly sees Giannis as a moderate upgrade over Brown, and offers Brown for Giannis with a few picks attached. Milwaukee wants more, Brad turns them down. He’s not actively trying to get rid of Brown, so he’s not willing to overpay for Giannis - once the offer isn’t worth it, the C’s are out. Milwaukee moves on. Other teams start publicizing their trade offers for Brown on the off chance it helps push him out of Boston. The Celtics listen because that’s what you do when you receive significant trade offers.

Basically the whole thing is a gray area / value proposition. Brown has given his blessing to be traded if the right opportunity comes up, but hasn’t explicitly asked out. Brad is doing his due diligence on offers for Brown but isn’t actively looking to get rid of him. Stevens is also protecting Brown by not acknowledging his openness to being trading, and creating more media controversy. Think this explains the ambiguous press statements Brad has made and some of the strange reporting around the Celtics trade offers. Basically this is a piece of NBA business that’s a little hard for the fans to understand and not meant for the media to clearly explain well, so things get kinda weird.

My guess is Brown sticks in Boston, because after Giannis its hard to imagine a trade that drastically improves the roster and makes trading a player of Brown’s caliber worth it. At the end of the day think that’s Brad’s number 1 decision making priority.
