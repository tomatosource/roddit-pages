Molten Vault, Mire, Forge, Flotsam etc. are terrible maps and need reworking. Breach/Ritual spawns little to no mobs, Abyss pits fail to generate, cramped narrow passageways everywhere causes mobs and players to constantly get stuck.

Look at some of the passageways on Mire for example. They are the width of one person. Mobs always get stuck in them, league mechanics can’t spawn mobs so Rituals end very quickly giving you virtually no juice to defer stuff with. Molten Vault is so bad that if you get “overrun with abyssal” to spawn on it, it will spawn only a tiny handful of pits because the terrain is so restrictive that there aren’t enough suitable open locations to spawn pits. Many large mob types glitch out on these maps because they’re too big to fit in the narrow passageways. They simply stand there at the end of the passageway trying to pathfind towards you (for example those giant rhinoceros mobs, giant skeletons, giant abominations). Spectres such as the Powered Zealot cannot cast their sparks properly, the projectiles all get stuck in one spot creating this star-like pattern that doesn’t hit anything.

My enjoyment of the game goes up tenfold whenever I get maps such as Savannah, Steppe or semi-open maps like Backwash or Rustbowl. GGG should use a minimum width specification for playable terrain. Slick and Epitaph are good examples of indoor maps where there’s a lot of passageways but they have enough width to allow you to move around and dodge stuff and for league mechanics to spawn mobs. Personally I find these maps to still be fun, even if the layout isn’t as ideal as say Savannah.

There is still a bug atm. where the Immured Fury (very big mob that drops a fracturing orb) fails to spawn (GGG is aware). It just so happens that the map I got this bug on was Mire. Coincidence? Maybe, but I have a theory that the reason it doesn’t spawn on some layouts is because the mob is too large for these crappy restrictive tilesets, so the game simply refuses to generate it.
