For the fun of it: who’d be your favorite (not the best or the greatest) starting 5, when, and why?

SG: Late stage MJ. I know he’s an obvious choice, but I grew up in Chicago in the 90s, and I fucking love MJ. The swagger, the style, the iconic fade-away jumpers… Never gets old to watch him play.

PG: Pre-injury Derrick Rose. I know his game overlaps with MJ’s, but the sheer athleticism, speed, and passion D-Rose displayed before his injury were electric to watch. Explosive.

PF: Prime Charles Barkley. Just great energy on and off the court. Great playmaker and all-around player. And strong.

SF: Jimmy Butler. I know it’s mostly Bulls at this point, but watching Butler blossom from a no-name rookie into an all star and dpoy and mvp contender was uplifting to watch. Gutsy all-around player.

C: Young Shaq. As a kid, he was my favorite player not named Michael Jordan, even before the Lakers, when he was on the Magic. He was lean at that time and could run.
