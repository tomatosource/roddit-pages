QoL Request: Parity for 3rd Ascendancy Trial

TLDR: GGG please make 10 trial Ultimatums spawn at level 65.

When GGG originally announced the different ascendancy trials, they said the point of making different trials was to give the player more options on how they ascend. Right now, the 3rd ascendancy for Sekhema starts at a level 60 zone, and the 3rd ascendancy for Chaos starts at a level 75 zone. I’m someone who prefers ascending through Trial of Chaos and not Sekhema. I would like to have my 3rd ascendancy done a good bit before level 75.

Patch 0.2.0f tried to fix this by lowering the level requirement of 10 room trials of Chaos from 75 to 65. All this seemed to do was allow a level 65 character to enter a level 75 Ultimatum. 10 room Ultimatums still do not spawn below level 75. By keeping the level of the zones far apart, players are still heavily encouraged to run their 3rd ascendancy through the Trial of Sekhemas, going against GGG’s original design philosophy. I hope GGG fixes this, but it looks like they think they already did, so that sucks.

ASA rise up!!!
