End game mapping needs to encourage group play more

I’m new to the path of exile genre and I’ve enjoyed my time.thus far, though am becoming frustrated in the end game because I’m playing with friends and running / improving their atlas while mine stays at the beginning. The more I play with friends, the further into their Atlas we progress the less incentive and alure there is for them to assist me with mine because my Atlas is crap. Then when I’m on by myself, I’m not motivated to play my own maps because my Atlas is crap and the time I’ve already spent assisting others with theirs is overwhelming for me to invest again into my own Atlas…

When Atlas points are awarded for the individual who started the map, they should be awarded to everyone in the party so everyone’s Atlas progresses at the same pace and do not have to reinvest time already spent to achieve the same outcome.
