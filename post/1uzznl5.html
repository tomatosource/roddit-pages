New WOWY RAPM from DARKO creator provides scoreboard based estimates of player impact going back all the way to 1980. Estimates for players such as Michael Jordan, Larry Bird, Magic Johnson are included

https://preview.redd.it/edu22lp3b0eh1.png?width=1414&format=png&auto=webp&s=79fa71ae828ecb401b0c6a3b6d4b8f9ea99a7002

Most popular impact metrics are limited to the play-by-play era due to their reliance on plus-minus to capture a player’s non-boxscore value. However, with the use of WOWY (With or Without You), a stat that looks at the final score margin for a team in games a player played compared to games that player missed, it is possible to go back beyond 1997.

https://www.darko.app/wowy?rating=adjusted
