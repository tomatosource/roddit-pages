I think we’ll fight Chayula

With the new teaser, i think the next boss might be Chayula. I think we’ll have 4 pinnacles + arbiter of ash and they will govern progress in the atlas as a whole, kinda like Eater/Exarch, Shaper/Elder, Maven and Originator now do in POE 1.

Also, to the person who commented the “Chocolate pyre” was the teaser for the breach teaser, i don’t think so. Before that one we had kelly posting the Tree.

The teasers of teasers were: Spider, Birds, Tree and now Chocolate Bonfire. While it did have correspondence to the lore presented, we must also remember that Ritual has a wicker theme and many traditional festivals/ceremonies involve the burning of wickermen or similar effigies.

Expedition(Spider), Delirium(Bird/Tangmazu), Breach(Tree) and Ritual.

So we’ll fight the person behind the Nameless King, Willy Wonka.

Jokes aside:

People are dreading or hoping for the Genesis tree to come for 0.5, i doubt we’ll receive anything similar to the Genesis Tree. Chayula and the Monastery in Poe 1 3.27 were shown to be quite ambivalent and ruthless, we won’t be harnessing THEIR power the same way the Godslayer did, if at all.

I think we CAN expect a new class, GGG has promised a huge expansion. This next expansion may be a culmination of many things left from other leagues, more than the endgame promised for 0.4.

The druid presents a clearer direction for what GGG wants melee to feel like, maybe we were supposed to receive a new class in 0.3 but Druid and the reception of 0.2 shifted priorities. This is why I think 0.5, despite having a full endgame rework and possibly a new league mechanic may also come with a new class.
