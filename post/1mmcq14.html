Who would win an nba series if it were teams made up of one position only (So PG vs SG vs SF vs PF vs C)

Imagine 5 teams in a best of 7 series, each made up of players who have played the same position

So (PG vs SG vs SF vs PF vs C)
Note, I’m surprised this hasn’t been asked before because I’ve had this convo a thousand times with mates (If it has been asked, I couldn’t find). IMO, I’ve always said a team made up of PFs, maybe SFs because they got a combo of size + skill in both defence & offence, but I’m curious to see what y’all think.
