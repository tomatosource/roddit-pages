The endgame and its inherent problem.

After playing the endgame for a month now it’s clear that it has one massive problem - the setup time to actually get to content that delivers anything worth getting. Between having to path continuously towards areas that are far off, rolling waystones that require a lot of currency to do so and then somehow aquire the tablets to make those zones “work” it really is an interest killer. All that on top of having to complete all your atlas’s points before anything decent will even drop (well over 100 maps in itself). I just find myself now in a position where I’m so bored of tying to find areas that work (city’s, areas with mirrors or delrium etc) all the while the teams of party players are miles and miles ahead in terms of gear currency and materials, sorry to sound like a sourpuss I’m sure I do but with this game once you get to mapping you realise that if your not either extremely lucky with RNG - which some players clearly are, or working in a team to drop tons of high value loot you are just going backwards in the economy.

Again, sorry for the rant just frustrated trying to get to the chase items while Grand expos are bricking for me and decent drops are non existent.
