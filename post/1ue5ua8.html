FIX: Poe2 0.5 game freezes every 30 min during maps

  1. Clear the cache in %APPDATA% for vulkan and dx. (Win+R &gt; %APPDATA% &gt; Path of Exile 2 &gt; delete files in ShaderCacheD3D12 & ShaderCacheVulkan)
  2. Nvidia control panel &gt; Manage 3D settings &gt; Shader cache size = 100GB
  3. Switch to VULKAN, leave dlss as it is (forget about dx12 until they fix it)
  4. Turn off TRIPLE-BUFFERING (removes that choppy screen lag)
  5. Turn on Nvidia Reflex (idk why it works but it removed some choppiness for me)
  6. Restart the game. (esp if you have changed the upscale mode or image quality)
  7. Wait till Vulkan loads in shaders, you can track it by opening options and checking SHADER on performance metric. (works better if you have SSD, otherwise, unlucky)
  8. ONCE the shaders have LOADED, you can now enjoy an even higher frame rate than dx12, esp during action. (Shaders will load much faster with time, dont worry)
  9. Be patient.

3060 rtx laptop, AMD Ryzen 7, 16 ram, ssd. Win10
Shadows + GI + low
textures - medium
dlss (balanced) - 80fps avg
