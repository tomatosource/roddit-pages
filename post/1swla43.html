I solved the armor/evasion/energy shield problem

We can all agree that ES is way too strong right now, especially compared to armour and evasion builds, but why is that?

Aside of one of the most obvious answer “the ES nodes just give too much”, the other obvious answer is “there’s just not enough life to make use of armour and evasion”. We can see this easily by just looking at the fact that ES is like a second, bigger life pool, unrelated to normal hp, meanwhile armour and evasion scale off of your actual hp.

This results in ES being unborhered by havig too little hp, meanwhile armour and evasion are getting randomly one-shot.

Now, we already know GGG’s stance on adding life on the tree, and that it would make those points mandatory and thus useless and wasted.

THE SOLUTION:

What if, instead of giving life on the tree, we just got more hp per level we get? Let’s say, instead of getting 12 extra hp each time we level up, we got 15? This would result in getting something along the lines of ~300 extra life to work with when reching level 90(taking into account campaign bonuses)

This would disproportionately buff armour and evasion, while mildly buffing es(and not even buffing it by that much sine many ES builds just run CI anyways). This, paired with some nerfs to, specifically, max ES nodes, in my opinion would be a great way to balance defeces across the board.

What do you think? Am I still sane? What would you do to balance defences?
