D4, Poe2, or what other ARPG?

Good evening. I find myself in a phase of reflection before starting a new venture, to explain. I already played one season on poe2 (the previous one), I brought my character to the maximum level, and I loved trading. Until one day I realized that there was no point in trying to get stronger because I was already strong enough to do all the content the game had to offer, and that’s when something broke. I quickly lost the drive to log in and finish the last few things. I don’t understand what I should do at this point. What’s the point of accumulating resources to make better items? Did I miss something in the endgame activities, or is that all there is?

Driven by this question, I was approaching the world of D4 (and no, this is not a provocative post), but at least there is something to do in the semi-competitive endgame that pushes you to want to do better than others. I think I read that there is, right?

At this point, the question that comes to mind is, yes, but what do you want from us? I would simply like to share my thoughts, hoping that I have missed something about the endgame of Poe. Have you ever had these thoughts? Where do you find the spirit to continue playing ARPGs?
