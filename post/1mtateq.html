Arvydas Sabonis and Yao Ming - Peak to peak, who’s the better center?

I thought it’d be interesting to compare these two venerated HoF centers, both with elite skills but relatively short NBA career due to injuries and age.

With these two, stats don’t paint the full picture of their excellence in their prime. Especially Sabonis whose best years were in Europe. Nonetheless my vote goes to Sabonis, although the edge is slight.

In my view:

Shooting: Both were exceptional shooters but Sabonis could shoot from 3. Yao hardly ever tried. But he never needed to and 3 shooting bigs were neither a necessity nor norm until this past decade.

Passing: No contest. Yao was a decent passer but Sabonis was considered THE Jokic predecessor.

Post game: Neither were Hakeem but I’d give this to Yao. Lighter on the feet and quicker to pivot. Although more athletic, Sabonis was iirc slightly slower and less agile in the paint.

Defense: This is abit of a toss up imo. Sabonis is generally considered a better rim and help defender but from my own memory Yao was a formidable presence too. His reputation as a prime Shaq stopper was probably more vibe than truth but still something to consider.

Matchups: I added this PostScript but it’s logical to consider that Yao Ming proved his cred against much tougher opponents. On paper Sabonis is a more complete player but Yao’s prime was proven against the likes of Shaq, Duncan and KG. This is why while I picked Sabonis in this comparison, imo the edge is smaller than most people think.

Happy to hear your analyses.
