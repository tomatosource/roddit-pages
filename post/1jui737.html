i know this isnt an airport, but i want to give my final thoughts

i wanted to enjoy 0.2 so badly, but i just got shit luck on drops and it took away my enjoyment. when checking trade i see garbage items for sale at insane high prices, so i am not the only one with this problem apparently. the new supports added nothing since i was lacking support slots. i was looking forward to a new meta, sadly the mobs were buffed as well. i dont want to be forced into a meta created by the devs. yet this is exactly what happened and i will no longer participate.

made a warrior, played shitty characters before, it was really really rough. i had no way of capping my resists, the gear pieces i found were a mixed bag of armour, energy shield and evasion so i couldnt focus on any sort of defence. i ended up being a glass canon and killed viper on cruel before her poison phase, struggled throught the campaign till doriyani’s lair and the mobs there were just too bad to handle. the constant lightning spam and grenades under my ass just got me every single time… i played 8 different builds in 0.1 and none of them were meta, i have around 2000 hours in poe 1 and 600 in poe 2. but at some point the amount of hours doesnt matter, i dont want to grind the same area in campaign to find a ring with life and 3 resistances. i dont want to pick up every item with sockets so i can finally salvage enough to afford 1 socket on an item to get 10% resistance just to progress a tiny bit. there simply was not enough anti stun, recovery or defence on the tree to make it enjoyable.
i am all for slow methodical gameplay, i love playing slow slamming builds that do mega chunks of damage, but the mobs were too fast, too tanky to handle. i invested all my gold into rerolling, gambling, optimizing and buying through trade optimizing my build but it just couldnt be done. yes my build sucked, funny thing is, i have played the same build before and it even got buffed and cruised through the campaign. i couldnt afford a greater jewellers orb to get the main interaction going and i am too tired to make another character.
