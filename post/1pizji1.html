My expectation and plan before 0.4.0 (not a literally guide)

Been following all the 0.4.0 previews, and honestly this patch feels pretty different from the last few. With density going down and the new efficiency stuff kicking in, I doubt we’ll see the usual “clearspeed meta.” It really looks like a patch where actual DPS and actual defenses matter again. Not a bad thing, just… different.

Class-wise, I think Pathfinder and Druid are going to come out swinging unless GGG decides to troll us. Invoker, Tactician and Ritualist feel like the Tier 0 group. Blood Mage and Titan seem fine but not giga-tier. Blood Mage losing the bleed-support is kind of funny. That whole “budget powerhouse” identity might not survive depending on the new curse gem drop rates. Hard to call it cheap if you can’t get the right gem.

Loot-wise, bossing looks juiced across the board. Abyss and other bosses got their drops buffed, especially abyss farming looks crazy good, Abyss currency will probably spike. Mists are basically done unless distilled emotions somehow becomes valuable again, and grave robbing is still the good, comfy low-cost option. Also: double-corrupts mean a lot of uniques get way better. There’s no universe where I’m risking a mirror-tier rare in that machine.

Shield builds getting hit is probably the funniest part. Block enjoyers are finally going to feel what melee players felt for months. Balance in all things, I guess.

As for my own league start: I’m going Blood Mage with Demon and Ember Fusillade up to like T15. Not touching Bone Cage because it’s too crowded. My early goal is pretty simple: farm the Trial of the Sekhemas for Ascendancy III and get that first chunk of starter currency. After that it’s flexible. Maybe some Ascendancy IV carries (money’s great, gameplay’s coma-inducing), maybe boss carries, maybe digging for fragments, maybe just live in Abyss zones. Depends on my mood and how the league mechanic actually feels — double-corrupt alone makes unique-based builds look spicy.

Overall, 0.4.0 feels like one of those patches where the whole ecosystem shifts at once: builds, loot, bossing, and the economy all move together. If you’re new, honestly this seems like a great league to hop in. If you’re a vet… well, nothing new: adapt, suffer a bit, then print currency.
