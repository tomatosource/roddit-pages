I feel like we keep missing the point

Edit: because apparently this is still needed. I’m not defending the trade, it was a bad trade and I don’t think anyone here is going to defend it. I am just pointing out the most important part of the press conference and the why they did the trade.

I also feel like I haven’t seen this talked enough about so, if I just missed it and am just rehashing it, I apologize on the rant.

USAGE. At the end of the day it comes down to usage between JB and Tatum. It’s not just the 70% cap, it’s looking at potentially 65%+ of our offensive possessions going through two guys. It makes it really easy for defenses to tee off on us.

Stevens flat out said having that much money AND that much usage locked into two guys makes it hard to stay at a championship level, and that they need more ways to attack. The flexibility was, yes about future trades and cap, but also offensively by having a complimentary piece like PG on the team. Tatum and Brown are both high usage alphas, and the nba today isn’t built for a team built like that.

I think if a player like Haliburton was available we would go into the second apron because of what he brings as a passer and playmaker and how that would bring balance to the team.

Everyone wants to argue the cap sheet because it’s the easy, visible number. Nobody wants to talk about the usage problem because it means admitting our offense had a structural flaw even while we were winning.
