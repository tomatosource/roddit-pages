Can we get some love for the hideout music player?

First up, in case you weren’t aware, you can buy a “hideout music player” MTX under “account” in the shop. It lets you select which track from the game you want to play while inside your hideout (provided it’s available), both for yourself and any players who visit.

Second, I’m not saying this is anywhere near the top in the list of issues to address in either game but it would be greatly appreciated by the music lovers. The “problem” is the hideout music player is missing a lot of tracks you can hear in the game. Compounding that, Kamil’s published work on youtube can often have a different title there than it does on the music player. e.g. from POE1, this track is called “Admiral Valerius” on youtube, but it’s called “Fortune Seekers” on the music player. This track is called “Fortune Seekers” on youtube but I’m unable to locate it on the music player (I think it was just the music in Kingsmarch?).

Kamil’s work across these games is fantastic and I’m personally willing to hunt through all the tracks to find the one I remember hearing, but it’s harder than it needs to be and it’s disappointing when it’s outright missing.
