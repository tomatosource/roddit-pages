[BasketNews] Russell Westbrook has not considered leaving the NBA, with reports linking the former MVP to Hapoel Tel Aviv described as “laughably false.”

Recent rumors linking Russell Westbrook with a move to Hapoel Tel Aviv appear to be wide of the mark, with multiple NBA insiders indicating that the former MVP has no plans to leave the league.

According to NBA insider Marc Stein, a source close to the situation strongly dismissed suggestions that Westbrook has been discussing a move to the EuroLeague.

“One source close to the process told The Stein Line that a recent report suggesting that Russell Westbrook was in talks to sign abroad with Hapoel Tel Aviv was ‘laughably false,’” Stein reported.

Another prominent NBA insider Brett Siegel also pushed back against the reports.

“Amid rumors surrounding Russell Westbrook potentially discussing a deal with Israel’s Hapoel Tel Aviv, I do not believe that to be true,” Siegel reported. “I’ve asked multiple people with different teams around the NBA if they’ve heard such and nobody has heard about this.”
