Loot Disappears If You Happen To Died Right After Killing A Boss. Why??

A few days ago I killed the boss of act 1 (wolf man) but simultaneously, he killed me as well. I watched the Hooded One appear and proceed with the dialog. After the dialogue was complete, the bosses loot dropped. I had no other option but to resurrect at nearest checkpoint. Upon coming back to the corpse of the boss, I noticed that all of the loot had disappeared.

Surely this is a bug, correct?

Usually after killing a boss, you can teleport to town (completely leaving the zone) to empty your bags and then teleport right back to the bosses corpse. You can then pick up the rest of the loot that you left behind.

Today, I was fighting the first boss of act 2. The centaur. I defeated the boss, (it was pretty easy) but then I got swarmed by multiple hyena and centaur. I was surrounded and since this game uses collision detection technology, I wasn’t able to roll through the mob and so I died.

Upon resurrecting and returning to the bosses corpse, all of the loot was gone.

If it is a deliberate mechanic, please fix it because it is a terrible idea and is more frustrating than fun.

At the very least, give us the option to fight the boss again so that we are able to retrieve the loot.
