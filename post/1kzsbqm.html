TIL: Half of the Delirium mechanic is still bugged and straight up doesn’t work

The Delirium mechanic - Specifically the Delirium Mirror, not the Distilled Emotions; is still bugged. When doing the Delirium mechanic with the Delirium Mirror, it should generate and drop additional reward types alongside the standard Distilled Emotions. However, this functionality is currently broken. The mirror neither drops extra Distilled Emotions nor any other reward types; in effect, it simply doesn’t work.

Furthermore, the Atlas passive point that is supposed to grant “additional reward types” for Delirium are also non-functional. They do nothing.

https://imgur.com/a/aFTfDd1

Likewise, the Delirium Tablet modifiers that state a “% chance for additional reward types” are bugged. These modifiers also do nothing.

https://imgur.com/a/qngPJXA

The reason this issue has largely flown under the radar is that most players don’t even play or use the Delirium Mirror mechanic. Current endgame mapping strategies prioritize Instilled Emotions for juicing, and those maps never include a Delirium Mirror. As a result, very few players are actively engaging with this part of the Delirium mechanic/content and are unaware of the bugs or the fact they’re getting a lot less loot than they should.

TL;DR: The Delirium mechanic is currently buged / broken. Half of the rewards tied to the delirium mirror simply do not function. This severely undermines an already underwhelming mechanic (Referencing the mirror; not using distilled emotions on maps), making it even less appealing for players to engage with this content that people already don’t play since the rewards are bad (and bugged).
