What would “losing the Jaylen Brown” trade look like to you?

In the press conference, Brad didn’t get into any timelines or specifics on when he thinks the Celtics will be in title contention again. Regardless, only one team can win each year, so every trade the other 29 teams made can’t be deemed a failure just because they didn’t win a title.

With that in mind, what would “losing” the Jaylen Brown trade look like to you?

  1. A Sixers/Heat ECF in 2027. Unmitigated disaster for Brad and Bill.
  2. A Sixers title in ‘27, ‘28 or ‘29 without the Celtics also winning one.
  3. Missing the playoffs in ‘27.
  4. A bottom 20 offense in the NBA in ‘27.
  5. Not making the ECF again in any of ‘27, ‘28 or ‘29.
  6. Brown outdueling Tatum in a playoff series.
