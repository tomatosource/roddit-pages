Flashback: First Nba MVP Races

Did you know that Nba started giving MVP award in 1956? From 1956 - 1957, there are 3 players fighting for MVP (2⁄3 share the name Bob)

1956

Bob Pettit (MVP) - 25.7 ppg, 16.2 rpg, 2.6 apg

Paul Arizin - 24.2 ppg, 7.5 rpg, 2.6 apg

Bob Cousy - 18.8 ppg, 6.8 rpg, 8.9 apg

1957

Bob Cousy (MVP) - 20.6 ppg, 4.8 rpg, 7.5 apg

Bob Pettit - 24.7 ppg, 14.6 rpg, 1.9 apg

Paul Arizin - 25.6 ppg, 7.9 rpg, 2.1 apg

—

This is before Russell and Wilt come along. (Russell was a rookie in 1957) (Wilt was drafted in 1959)

The best player title that time was between The two Bobs (Pettit and Cousy) and Arizin.

Bob Cousy is a little bit more known even now because he started the flashy passes playstyle. (He is called the Magician)

However, Bob Pettit was considered the better player between two Bobs. He won the First ever Nba MVP in 1956. And even in 1957, some fans said Pettit should have won over Cousy. Cousy only beat Petitt by 2 Voting pts. (23 Votes to 21 Votes)

Paul Arizin was never an MVP. But he is always 2nd or 3rd in his prime.

Final Verdict:

From 1955 -1957. I think my final verdict is Bob Pettit is the best player. And take note Pettit also won an MVP in 1959 when Russell was already around. Though in 60s, it is all about Wilt and Russell with Oscar.
