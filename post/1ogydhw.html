SSF: Missing crafting material, certain types just don’t drop

At first i was really enjoying my first SSF character in POE2 (never did one in POE1).

But then i realized, that the game is not taking SSF into any account regarding loot. For me this means:
I’m an infernalist at lvl96 and to carry on, the gear needs to be high end crafted and skills need their appropriate linegae supports.

I ran 400 maps and there never droped a preserved collarbone (really: not a single one and i checked the filter). I got nearly a hundred jaws and a hundred ribs, but no collarbone for crafting any ring/amulet. I spice my maps as hard as possible. I run ritual within every map. Things like omen of whittling or that thing to reroll a desecration just don’t appear from me. Neither as a drop, nor as a ritual option. I never got a Uul-Netol’s Embrace although running Esht about 90 times. And i never got a talisman of eeshta, although i maxed out Azmiri Spirits in the atlas and maps and run them several hundred times.

I really think after investing over 350 hours in this SSF char, the game SHOULD reward you at least with the basic things you need to progress when your time investment and your juicyness of maps is high enough. But it isn’t.

The game feels like a very stubborn slot machine, not like a modern game. Every map i run i just draw blanks. Nothing is helpful to get the progress going.

I wonder, if this is meant to be that way or is there something i do wrong? I really can’t get any motivation up right now and this is very unpleasant.
