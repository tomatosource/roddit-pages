Summoning Circle Bug (and cleansed areas)

Let me preface this by saying this is just want has been working for me for the past 2 or so weeks as I’ve been working through the cleansed areas I’d amassed.

[LONG READ] The issue was that even with the map bosses are powerful selected on blood on the stone, I’d be lucky if the summoning circle charges didn’t bug out after the first or second use. If clear most of the map until I found the runes and the summoning circle and then do them all in one go- if it would let me. It was very frustrating seeing all those charges go to waste and the 5% chance for a fracturing orb to drop from summoned bosses with modifiers felt much much lower than that.

​​For me, I was getting (on average) 1 fracturing orb from the summoned bosses every 4-5 maps once I ‘fixed’ it.

Fracturing orbs from cleansed monsters were more random but scaled in frequency as I had increased my item rarity. (Cleansed monsters are the ‘fettered x’ monsters).

I am using Hilda with an irradiated, abyss and overseer tablet. The irradiated tablet has the unique bosses have an additional rare mod and all three an additional summoning circle plus any mix of rarity and effectiveness I could get. That puts minimum 4 summoning circles on the map (well 1 plus the activation runes) and quite a few more if the atlas nodes proc.

I also try to do this on 200% deli cleansed areas because simulacrum splinters drop like candy and the beast and construct bosses quite frequently drop the expensive emotions.

Also, I have abyssal depths chance on the abyss tablet which sends me down unda quite often and the abyssal depths is littered with amanamu monsters.

It’s quite rare for me to leave a map without a fracturing orb, omens of light, those expensive emotions, or a stack of abyssal echoes or simulacrum splinters.

THE KEY, and I promise it’s not as stupid as it sounds, IS TO FIND THE SUMMONING CIRCLE FIRST, KILL THE SUMMONED BOSS, THEN TRAVEL BACK AND KILL THE BOSS FOR EACH ACTIVATION RUNE YOU FIND. Yes. Every time. Even if it’s a linear map and you find all the activation runes first, or even worse if you have multiple activation runes right beside each other. You have to port back.

There’s something about stacking the charges that bricks it. And it doesn’t completely remove the bug, but it went from bugging out after the first one every single map to like once every 8-10 maps and usually on like the last charge.

Now it’s pretty late in the league and it’s likely GGG will fix this in the next big patch, but if you want to make use of your cleared areas, that’s how I’d recommend you go about it.
