[sport24] Giannis Antetokounmpo: “I love everyone, I thank everyone for their support, because without it nothing would be possible. It is my greatest achievement, to represent the National Team, your country, 12 million people and reach a success, a medal.”

In detail, what Giannis Antetokounmpo said to FIBA: “ I have competed in nine competitions with the National Team. I was there from day one, we had our ups and downs. Today was the day! I love everyone, I thank everyone for their support, because without it nothing would be possible. It is my greatest achievement, to represent the National Team, your country, 12 million people and reach a success, a medal.

“I’ve experience everything in my life. I don’t talk much, I like my actions to speak. I’m not fake, I don’t like being famous, I like basketball and the crowd. For me it’s a relief because for me it was for my country, the team and I finally made it “
