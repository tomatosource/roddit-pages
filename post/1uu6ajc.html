You Can Keep Rerolling Limb Modification Options at the Flesh Surgeon.

I’ve friends who spam Temple, but they didn’t know you can reroll your limb modification options as much as you want, so I figured I’d share this info for the 17 people who still run Temple this league.

When you’re presented with the choice at the Flesh Surgeon, don’t make a selection and instead click on the X at the bottom. Your character will get off the table, and you’ll be able to get on the table again, which rerolls the available options, making it a lot easier to get the rolls and values you want.

You can also do the same if you have Flesh Surgeon IV. The game counts you used an attempt only when you modify the limb, so you can keep getting on/off the table as much as you want, as long as you don’t pick anything.

As an extra bit of info, here’s the list of all the limb modifications you can currently get and their values: Transcendent Limb - PoE2DB, Path of Exile Wiki us.
