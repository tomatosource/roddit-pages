Neemias Queta proved me wrong.

So like a lot of you, I was a Queta hate’a. To play on this team as a non shooting big he always had the Rob/Luke profile. Athletic, protect the rim, tall I guess.

But he just looked like he hadn’t played much basketball. Dumb fouls, bad screens, and all the things you can’t do as a bench big. I do agree that it’s hard to get in a rhythm for him, and walsh, as they both haven’t played enough to establish anything.

Eye test, he looks great. Really enjoyed some of his moments to start the year. Then today, I ended up on his BReff page today.

.194 WS per 48. By far the most out of our starters.

Highest Reb% on the team

2 highest FTR out of starters

62% TS

second highest PER among starters.

Overall probably leads the team in pleasant surprise’ness so far. I get we’re all thinking we need another big, preferably one who can shoot, but Queta really proved me wrong.

I also think he’ll fit really well in the Tatum Derrick PnR as a vertical spacer. Really excited to keep seeing him grow.
