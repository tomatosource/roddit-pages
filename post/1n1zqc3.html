Players Who Will Make the Jump Into Consistent Rotation Pieces in 2025-26

Jared Butler (PHX)

With the point guard role being wide open, I think Jared Butler was a great sneaky pick up. While a fan of Gilespie, I think Jared Butler could eventually take that starting role. He had a very good quiet end of season with philly last year and has the ability to play on and off ball

Johnny Juzang (MIN)

With the departure of NAW and need for shooting on the wing off the bench Johnny Juzang could solidify himself as a real rotational piece. While he is behind Terrence Shannon Jr. as well as probably Jaylen Clark. I expect him to get playing time especially in place of injuries (guys like Divicenzo and Randle) often missing time. His spot will move up in the rotation. He may not be the best defender, but he does have the physical tools to become a decent wing defender standing at 6’5 with a 6’ 11 wingspan while shooting 37% from deep. 

Vit Krejci (ATL) 

A 6’8 guard/forward who sees the floor well and pushes the pace as well as a developing 3 point shot. While he needs to get stronger and the defensive metrics don’t favor him too heavily, he has shown flashes of being a good on-ball defender with quick hands and feet.
