Btw, this are the steps I followed:

You unlock these points two at a time. First Two Points: Slay a map boss in an area level of 79 or higher while the waystone has at least 6 modifiers. Any tier 15 waystone will reach level 79.

Points 3 and 4: As for the first two points, but you require level 80 or higher. This requires a tier 16 waystone (corruption exclusive tier), or a tier 15 waystone that has been boosted by Irradiation (from RNG, a Precursor Tablet or ) or by certain Atlas Corruption effects.

Points 5 and 6: Slay an Anomaly Map Boss in an area level 79 or higher with at least 6 modifiers.

Points 7 and 8: Slay an Citadel Map Boss in an area level 79 or higher with at least 6 modifiers. This will award one of the basic Crisis fragments.

Points 9 and 10: Slay the Arbiter of Ash in either its basic or Uber version.
