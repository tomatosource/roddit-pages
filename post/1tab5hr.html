The weight of runes

Much is made of GGG’s (especially Chris) long-time argument in favor of item weight. Having actual “physical” objects to manipulate in the game makes them feel more real, creating more emotional attachment and a sense of value. Jonathan again put this argument forward in multiple 0.5 discussions, further arguing that it does a better job communicating to players that the option even exists, as it won’t get lost in a list on a crafting bench.

I can’t help but feel like they’re missing a fundamental part of that dynamic though. It’s one thing to have a single tabs of runes that are relevant for most of my game play where I can peruse my options to supplement my gear. The fantasy idea of having a crafting area where I store all my tools and resources is fun. I see I need to get more life, or strength, or lightning resist. I head over to my crafting area and see what I have to do that – or what I need to get.

But in PoE 2, GGG isn’t leaning into this aspect of the fantasy. Instead, it starts from the place of “itemize all the things!” and then jams then into your inventory as best they can. Having to micromanage thousands of tiny objects is not fun. It just isn’t. And with 0.5, it feels as though we’re already jumping the shark on that front. Every new league is going to be awash with new crafting currency. More than 160(?) new runes incoming? It already feels tedious.

How many variations of grew circular objects with colored icons do we need? At what point does the fantasy break down? Am I supposed to learn what all 160 are called and what they do so I can get excited when they drop? I’m 99% sure I won’t. After a week, most will fall off my filter and I’ll just go buy what I need.

At least PoE 1 had the core crafting centralized by the crafting function itself. I could go my crafting bench and sort through my options. Having to flip through multiple tabs dozens upon dozens of “runes”, many of which I have none of, with no visual indicators of what they do is a strict downgrade to the crafting experience.

I don’t know that benches are the solution. But I do know that I’m very much not looking forward to the weight of hundreds of new crafting objects, most of which I won’t have when I need them or which won’t be worth looting because the mere act of managing them won’t be worth the effort.

I love that GGG is leaning into complex crafting systems and leagues with crafting identities. I wish they’d spend more time thinking about the UX of it all.
