This might be the first game where I don’t like any of the armors in the microtransaction shop.

Was browsing thru the store looking at the stuff within, and it occurred to me that I’m not a fan of any of the armors. They’re too….”out there” for my tastes. Usually in games with an in game store I’ll see something that I’d probably buy, but I’ve never until now had no desire to buy any of it due to none of it appealing to me. Some of the gem effects look cool but that’s about it for me.

Luckily the armors in game look great so it’s not one of those situations where all the drip is locked behind your credit card. And I do appreciate that the “wacky” armors are in the storefront for those who want it rather than it being in game and clashing with the overall vibe.

Also please note that just because I don’t like them, doesn’t mean I’m saying they’re bad. There’s definitely an audience for it otherwise it wouldn’t be there.
