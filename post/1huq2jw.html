Mazzulla or Spoelstra

Let me preface this by saying the timing of this question has nothing to do with the Celtics mini “slump” of late. Instead I’m daydreaming due to the Heat recently coming to a crossroads of sorts as a franchise.

The Question: If you could, would you swap out Mazzulla for Spoelstra?

12 months ago I would have said yes of course. Then, Mazzulla won a title and coached very well along the way. He has a lot of talent at his disposal, but he also has a system, he has a style, and he has a culture. He’s also only 36 and should keep getting better and better.

Still, Spoelstra is Spoelstra. One of the 15 best coaches of all time. A guy you trust to lead the team from era to era, whether you have the best roster in the league or not.

I’ll probably get killed here for this, but I think I would still make the swap. Curious what others think.
