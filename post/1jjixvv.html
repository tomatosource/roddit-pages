Giannis seems to have unlocked a midrange game, with career high FG% from 10ft to the 3pt line this season. He’s on pace to sneak into top 5-10 in total pts of all time.

Shooting percentages for 2024-2025:

  • 3-10ft: 42.5% (second to last season, where he shot 45.2%)
  • 10-16ft: 47.9% (previous high was 42.3% in 2014-2015)
  • 16-3pt: 44.5% (previous high was 43.1% in 2021-2022)

His 0-3ft FG% is average this season (76.5%) compared to his career. His 3pt % is low (19.1%), but he’s taking a career low 4% of his shots from 3.

His TS% is about average (62%) for his allstar career.

I think Giannis is realizing that his “long 2s” are worth more than his 3s. For instance, the PPS based on his last 3 season 16-3pt avg and 3% is:

  • 16-3pt -&gt; 0.402 FG% -&gt; 0.804PPS
  • 3pt -&gt; 0.263 3PT% -&gt; 0.789PPS

However, those midranges probably make it easier for him to get to the line or get a rebound, which probably makes those shots even better than what the 0.01 difference in PPS indicates.

I think with Giannis’s length, he can become a solid 2 and D shooter in old age, and can probably run up into the 5-10 scoring spot all time.

  • Harden: 20962 pts (#11)
