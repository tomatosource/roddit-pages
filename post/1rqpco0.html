Campaign is too long on either PoE1 or PoE2 and this made it so I only ever played one league for each

Basically this.

I don’t get why 2 has to have even longer campaign. I would have played a lot more PoE1 if the campaign wasn’t such a slog. I figured that 2 should be in better shape regarding this - it’s EA after all. But no, apparently 2 is even longer than 1.

I just don’t get it. Who enjoys this? Completing once - maybe, but repeated completions - fuck no.

After all that’s how it was for me on PoE1 - I played a league, got to T14 maps, had an amazing experience doing that, and then, on the next league, I struggled to get past the campaign due to extreme boredom and realizing that the dopamine would not hit for another 15 hours.

It’s not like this for other ARPGs either. PoE2 act speedrun is 4 hours! D2 speedrun is 40 minutes, D4 is 3 hours and it’s skippable (getting a char to endgame takes maybe 3-4 hours), Last Epoch speedrun is around 2 hours.

I sort of wish to go back to Diablo 4, even though the game is ass, objectively speaking, but at least it doesn’t require me to work full time for a week to get to the fun parts. At least it respects my time and I can level multiple chars to check out the builds (something that I can never see myself doing in PoE as leveling is such a slog).
