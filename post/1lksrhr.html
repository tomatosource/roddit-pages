Ace Bailey refusing workouts, making him fall in the lottery, then being drafted by a team he did not prefer is not new. Ask Steph Curry

Back in 2009, Blake Griffin was the no-questions-asked top pick by the LA Clippers. The assistant GM of Memphis, Kenny Williamson, invited Curry for a workout so he can convince his team to pick him number 2. Curry’s team declined. They received an invite from number three pick holder OKC Thunder, which Curry’s team also declined.

They accepted the workout invite from Sacramento along with Tyreke Evans who had a more impressive workout. Then they declined invites from the Wolves, who had the 5th and 6th picks, and the Warriors who had the seventh pick.

Curry and his team wanted him to fall to eight to play with the Knicks.

But even without a workout, the Warriors picked him seventh. And the rest is history.

I wonder how Steph, declining workouts left and right to fall to his preferred destination in New York at number 8 only to be drafted by a team that only had one playoff appearance in 15 seasons at the time, would be talked about if social media and hot take media exists back then. Maybe he would be mocked like Ace is being mocked today.
