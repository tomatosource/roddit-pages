In 0.5, The Temple needs to just fully destabilize every 10 or so runs

Obviously adjust rewards and all that to make it worth running but it’s really close to being a great mechanic. However there’s no need or reason that one should devote so much time and effort in to “maintaining” a temple. It should operate more like the roguelike they clearly intended it to be. Let us get lucky and build a OP temple and see loot we can get before it eventually all collapses, and in the same vein some runs will naturally end up being total duds, a simple concept every good roguelike follows.
