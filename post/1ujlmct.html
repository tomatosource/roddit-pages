How much rarity on gear is enough or even too much?

As I’m slowly progressing my char, I’m adding rarity on gear. When I started mapping early on after campaign I had less than 50% on gear. I was often seeing raw divs drops, even in lower tier blue maps. Then after some time I was running 15 tier waystones with around 75% rarity on gear, waystones were always 6 mod alch+exalt and go, whatever mods - divs were common, omens of light were common.

Then I got around 120% rarity and that’s when I started noticing worse currency drops, less raw divs, less omens.

Now I pushed it to 164% on gear, 8 mod maps with at least 30 rarity and 15 effectiveness on them, and raw divs are very rare, I haven’t seen a single omen of light in many hours of abyss runs, whereas when I had less than 100% rarity omens of light were very common drops in less juiced maps compared to what I’m running now.

First i assumed I need more effectiveness of monsters: I swapped all possible atlas nodes in favor of effectiveness, started running waystones which have more effectiveness. Now I’m seeing bit more currency drops but of lowest tier, with occasional chaos and exalts here and there, and a div every few maps, and a LOT of useless equipment of all possible tiers.

Then I switched back to rarity on atlas and waystones: nothing really changed except I’m seeing less equipment and less low tier currency…

I have a weird feeling that after certain threshold rarity on gear starts messing with the loot in a weird way. Like it actually makes the loot worse.

Anyone else have the same experience?
Maybe I’m doing something wrong?
Should I be stacking effectiveness or rarity on atlas and waystones?
