Let’s talk about Azmeri

It is THE juice. From my experience it is the most rewarding (better than expedition, breach and ritual) if you do it properly. The only thing that compares is T4 sim. Unfortunately, some negative posts about it here just made me neglect it until recently where I just gave it a shot and now want to share the knowledge :)

Here’s the jist, pump an area with “rarity from map bosses”, use one azmeri tablet unique, one irradiation unique that allow you to do the map twice.

Take all the Azmeri nodes on the tree and on the map just do the color you’re after (blue for int items, yellow for dex items and red for str items).

My only gripe with it is that I experienced red possessed bosses most of the time (around 4 of 5) meaning str items which ofc is not up there in the meta. Curious to know about your experience if that is just RNG for me.

Blast away folks. And sorry if you die :D
