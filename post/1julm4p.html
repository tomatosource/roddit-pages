Trading Michael Porter Jr is likely the next move for Denver in the offseason.

So I watch a lot of Denver games and if you have been paying attention closely you would know they would be falling apart. I predicted earlier in a post this week that this offseason after they fall early in the playoffs, Malone would be fired(it already happened), and that they would also trade MPJ.

Their defense was the main issue, and it looked catastrophic. They do have personnel issues, but at some point this season it was no longer about personnel on defense, and it was obvious that they were completely lost defense: not giving effort, not communicating, and out of sync with the coaching staff. With that being said, they still have multiple bad defenders in their rotation. Obviously Jokic has limitations on that end, but their other personnel is also bad too and the first player who has fallen off a cliff defensively for them was MPJ.

There were rumors that he was going to be traded before the trade deadline, but he didn’t because they probably wanted to run it back last time with this core.

However now in the summer they will probably look to move him right away. He does have a 35-40 million dollar contract, so it will be hard for them to make a “TRUE UPGADE” but he is still averaging 18ppg on elite efficiency, and they can probably move him for a better defensive player but downgrade offensively like Jaden McDaniels or Andrew Wiggins .
