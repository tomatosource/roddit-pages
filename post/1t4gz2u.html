So I gave Lord of hatred a try I really did.

I can honestly say this, D4 has gotten much better, blizzard has looked at the feedback and brought much improvement to the game that was severely lacking. But there’s just one problem… POE2 is just significantly better. Don’t get me wrong the new expansion has a decent story, some great visuals, MUCH better boss battles that were needed, but I just couldn’t scratch the itch that I felt when playing it and I think I know why. POE2’s combat and detail is excellent; I would say it’s even perfect. It’s so good that no matter what ARPG I try (epoch, lost ark, D4) I stop playing them because it is just nowhere as good as POE2.

The amount of detail and work put into POE2 shines most when you play other games and you compare the two, and when you do. it’s almost night and day. This isn’t even including the INSANE amount of customization POE2 offers that is leagues beyond anything else, it’s just…staggering really. At first, I didn’t understand what people meant when they said POE2 ruined this or that for me but after trying to play D4 again, I can completely relate. My hype for the endgame revamp just shot up 110% after trying to give D4 another shot.
