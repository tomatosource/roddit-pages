[NBA] Sacramento Kings center Maxime Raynaud and Philadelphia 76ers guard VJ Edgecombe have been named the Kia NBA Western and Eastern Conference Rookies of the Month, respectively, for games played in March.

Raynaud averaged 17.9 points (.595 FG%, .784 FT%), 8.5 rebounds, 1.8 assists and 33.0 minutes per game in 15 games (all starts) in March. He recorded six 20-point performances, including two 30-point games, and led all rookies with six double-doubles.

In 13 games played throughout the month, Edgecombe averaged 18.9 points, 5.8 rebounds, 4.3 assists, and 1.2 steals in 32.6 minutes. He scored 20-plus points six times, including a pair of 30-plus points games. Edgecombe also produced three double-doubles in his 13 contests, two of which came in consecutive games on March 19 (at Sacramento) and March 21 (at Utah).
