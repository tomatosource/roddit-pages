The leveling experience still needs some work, especially around drops

The first time leveling in a league can be a bit of a hassle, but it’s usually forgotten a few weeks in because having gold or currency helps tremendously when leveling the following characters (or people just play one character). But over the last few weeks I’ve been playing HCSSF just to try a few builds. I’ve gotten to maps a every time now, and I see this constant pattern around drops, especially gem drops.

It actually seems that some level ranges or areas are particularly affected. Keep in mind that I’m doing all side content each time. What I’m regularly seeing is:

  • I’m constantly starving for support gems, especially between Act 3 and the Interludes. In one run I actually got more Lesser Jeweler Orbs than support gems until I got to maps. In one of my runs I got a level 10 skill gem (at level 36), cut it to Bloodhound’s Mark, and only when I was level 42 was I able to put Mark for Death into it. Six levels without a single support gem drop.

  • Skill gems can be fairly rare too. Again, from Act 3 through the interludes it becomes especially bad. I find myself upgrading some skills directly from level 8 to 10 and similar, only having one skill at the appropriate level.

  • Currency is another problem too, especially Regal Orbs. I’m constantly going back to town to disenchant everything. Transmutation/Alteration Orbs are all right at some point, but I’m often at 0 Regals/Exalts, so sometimes I’m carrying a piece of gear for 15–20 levels. In one run I used the same crossbow from level 36 to the early 70s. I’m gambling, checking vendors on every level-up, crafting when I can. I have to say, abyssal mods have been a lifesaver when it comes to this.

Reaching endgame even like this is obviously doable, as I’m managing with very off-meta self-crafted builds, but sometimes the experience doesn’t feel smooth enough due to gems and currency being fairly rare. It’s somehow so consistent in this level 30–50 range, where many builds have several active skills and you want to upgrade or socket supports, and suddenly you have to arrange some sort of priority mode.

Nothing new with this; I think we’ve all read similar posts during 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3. It’s just a recurring issue.
