You should be able to pick up the spear of Solaris.

Spear of Solaris sucks. It does terrible damage and has no real place in the game. But what if you could pick it up and use it?

Thematically, it makes sense. Solaris throws it down in a beam of fire and you pick it up and use it. And mechanically, giving players a damage as extra fire + spreading burning ground on hit buff to spear attacks would make the skill super worth using.

Being able to pick up the spear turns a useless glory skill into something actually good while giving huntress one more fire themed skill that she can use outside of explosive spear.

Edit: when I say pick it up, I don’t mean actually replacing your main hand weapon like energy blade, I mean that it would buff you while being themed as you picking up the weapon
