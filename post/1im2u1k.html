Celtics players declining once traded

We need to talk about how the Celtics keep getting away with trading players for value at the right time before their decline or injury. Trades like Pierce and KG for the Brooklyn package, IT for Kyrie, etc. Look at what happened to players the Celtics have let go the past few years, players like Marcus Smart, Grant Williams, Robert Williams, and Malcolm Brogdon, These players were let go at the right time for great deals before declining or succumbing to injuries. Are the Celtics management just good at maximizing talent just like the Heat, Thunder, etc. or are other teams just that unlucky when trading with the Celtics.
