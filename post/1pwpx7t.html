Odd Pet Peeves?

Alright, as baseball fans, we all have things that kinda piss us off. But which of these things for you is the most random?

For me, there’s several, but here’s a few examples: pitchers getting a save, but giving up a run or more, ground rule doubles, runners getting held at third on plays they definitely could’ve scored, etc. And probably the weirdest of all is seeing the ball hit the ground before the wall. I’d much rather see the outfielder pick it up before it hits the wall, or have it hit the wall first.
