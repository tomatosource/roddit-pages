My first decent craft albeit at the end of the league

This is my first time playing Path of Exile 2, and I had a lot of fun!

I started with my Stormweaver and went with a Frostbolt spam build since it felt comfy while leveling and was cheaper than the popular lightning mage.
I already had a decent wand before this with all tier 3⁄2 and some tier 1 mods.

I got bored and had some spare divines since I had nothing else to do other than farm boring maps lol.
I looked for rare wands with fractured +5 to Cold Spell Skills, and they were surprisingly cheap. I sniped my base for 20 chaos orbs.
The base had 4 other mods, which I annulled down to one.

Then I chaos spammed and just prayed for any tier of spell damage since that helps you later get cast speed and crit chance/crit damage mods using Omen of Homogenizing Exaltation + Greater Exalt + Omen of Dextral Necromancy.

If anyone reading this is new, I highly recommend craftofexile.com to see how modifiers work, their tags, and the probability to hit them.

Anyway, I got lucky and rolled a tier 1 spell damage % (106%), then divined it to 118%.
Now this was a solid base since spell damage has both the caster and damage tags.
That guarantees cast speed and one of crit chance% or crit bonus dmg%.

I used Omen of Homogenizing Exaltation + Omen of Greater Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation + Perfect exalted orb and prayed to hit crit chance% instead of crit bonus dmg% since it’s useless for my build.
Got giga lucky and rolled almost max tier 1 cast speed and crit chance.

Now that my suffixes were done, the craft got much easier.
I used Omen of Homogenizing Exaltation + Perfect Exalted Orb, praying to hit any “gain damage as extra (fire/light/cold)” mod, and luckily got Gain 30% of Damage as Extra Fire Damage.

With 5 mods done, I desecrated with Ancient Jawbone (not really needed but I had all T1 mods, so why not). Then I used an Omen of Abyssal Echoes to get a second roll.
Got trash mods on the first roll, but echoes came in clutch and I got Gain 29% of Damage as Lightning Damage.

I’m too scared to vaal it for a second socket, but I’m really happy overall.

This craft has a good amount of RNG, but as long as you start with a fractured base, you can almost guarantee 4 out of 6 mods easily for a wand like this.

Sorry if I broke any rules or typed a huge wall of text — it’s my first time posting here!

I will try to vaal it later once I farm some confidence lmao

Edit 1 - All this cost me around 8 divs including all the materials and the ancient jawbone + perfect exalted orbs!
