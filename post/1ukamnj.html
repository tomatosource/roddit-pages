Why LeBron could sign with every team.

Atlanta Hawks
They traded Trae Young away, so LeBron shows up mostly to check out Jalen Johnson in person. Comes away impressed, gives a small nod of approval, doesn’t say much else.

Boston Celtics
He signs during the exact week they’re trying to move Jaylen Brown for the third time this offseason. Walks into his own introductory presser and finds out he’s been mentioned in trade packages too, next to two second-round picks.

Brooklyn Nets
Joins right after they land Julius Randle out of the big Wolves-Hornets trade chain. He finally gets to be a teammate with randle, the only member of the Lakers former young core he never played with.

Charlotte Hornets
The Bojangles thing is real, so he takes a minimum deal purely for the cafeteria access. Gets there and realizes they shipped LaMelo to Minnesota and Bridges to Phoenix while he wasn’t paying attention. Orders a Cajun Filet Biscuit, appoints himself point guard on the spot.

Source for his love of Bojangles: https://www.wbtv.com/2026/06/30/lebron-james-loves-bojangles-could-it-be-enough-lure-him-charlotte-hornets/

Chicago Bulls
Signs hoping to finally pass Jordan’s ring count, looks up at the rafters, has a small crisis about it like he does every ten years or so. Asks for a locker near the statue instead of one with the team.

Cleveland Cavaliers
Comes home for a third stint, second time being the guy who came back. Nobody’s surprised. The moving company has his address on speed dial at this point. He hopes to help cursed Harden and Mitchell win a ring. He’s also confused how the East became good when he wasn’t watching.

Dallas Mavericks
Signs to keep Kyrie company as he works back from surgery, immediately gets looped into a group chat trying to make sense of the Luka trade. Nobody in the chat ever lands on an answer that satisfies anybody.

Denver Nuggets
He’s always had a soft spot for chicken nuggets. Shows up expecting some, gets served Jokić’s homemade burek instead, and somehow becomes loyal to it for the rest of the year.

Detroit Pistons
Takes the President of Basketball Ops job again, except this time the roster’s actually good, so his one real move is extending himself and sending Cade a one-line text: “you’re the guy now.”

Golden State Warriors
Signs for the MLE to be near Steph, his longest-running rival and, at this point, closest thing to a regular golf partner. They play more rounds together than actual games.

Houston Rockets
Signs a year after Houston trades for Durant, and the two spend most of shootaround arguing about who’s the better all-time scorer. Sengün has to get between them more than once.

Indiana Pacers
This one’s an apology tour, he’s knocked them out of the playoffs enough times over the years that he figures he owes them one. Also wants to know what happened to their 5th pick. He’s happy to play with Haliburton.

LA Clippers
Signs to get out from under the Laker-greats comparisons, gets compared to Laker greats within the first week anyway, asks for a trade before the season even starts.

LA Lakers
Been there done that.

Memphis Grizzlies
Lebron would not sign with the grizzlies.

Miami Heat
Heads back for a second stint to join Giannis. Shows up already scouting how to guard prime Giannis, then remembers that’s his teammate now.

Milwaukee Bucks
Signs right after the Giannis trade sends Milwaukee into a full youth movement, Herro, Ware, Jakučionis and company. Ends up the accidental elder statesman of a roster that got a lot younger overnight.

Minnesota Timberwolves
Joins a backcourt that now has Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball together, and spends most of camp trying to get a word in between the two of them talking trash to each other. He doesn’t enjoy the -40 degree weather.

New Orleans Pelicans
Signs mostly for the Smoothie King access, and won’t answer a single postgame question that doesn’t somehow circle back to mango flavor.

New York Knicks
Signs with the defending champions. Shows up to his presser in an Anunoby jersey and asks, pretty sincerely, if there’s a roster spot open or if he’s just there for the parade.

Oklahoma City Thunder
Signs to chase a real dynasty, but they’ve only got the one ring so far, so he figures there’s room to help stack a couple more. Shai lets him know gently that he’s the sixth man now. He takes it in stride, mostly.

Orlando Magic
Back to Central Florida to mentor Banchero, leads them to another second-round exit, spends his exit interview telling everyone to shoot more threes and questions why they’re so bad at 3s.

Philadelphia 76ers
Signs because he genuinely still believes in the process at this point, which has now outlasted a couple of presidential terms. At least they have edgecombe and Maxey now, he says.

Phoenix Suns
Signs right as they land Miles Bridges from Charlotte, gives him a pep talk about second chances, and starts drawing up plays to get him 30 a night almost immediately.

Portland Trail Blazers
Signs mainly to be closer to Nike headquarters so he can walk over on off days and negotiate royalties on shoes that don’t exist yet.

Sacramento Kings
He’s the King, they’re the Kings, it was always going to happen eventually.

San Antonio Spurs
Signs to learn from Wemby, who just led the Spurs to the finals. He helps them with their blowing lead problem. Realizes within a week the roles have flipped and becomes his full-time hype man instead.

Toronto Raptors
Signs just to check in on them after Kawhi’s return. Finds out they’re doing fine, leaves reassured, still a little confused about the metric system. He apologizes for the LeBronto era.

Utah Jazz
Signs, discovers there’s no actual jazz in Utah and asks for a trade to New Orleans almost immediately.

Washington Wizards
Signs with a Wizards roster that now has Trae Young, Anthony Davis, and rookie AJ Dybantsa. Splits his time between basketball and running for mayor, somehow racks up more votes than the team gets wins.

Seattle SuperSonics
LeBron won’t sign with this team because it doesn’t exist yet. Maybe they’ll get KD though.
