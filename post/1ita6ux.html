Which teams have overachieved and underachieved the most this season? A look at the numbers

The following is the ranking of all teams in terms of the difference between their preseason over/under total (listed by Sports Illustrated prior to opening night), and their projected win total over the course of a full 82 game season.

OVERACHIEVING

CLEVELAND: + 18.3 wins (expected 48.5, on pace for 66.8)

Coach Kenny Atkinson is the favorite for ‘Coach of the Year’ for a reason.

DETROIT: + 17.7 wins (expected 25.5, on pace for 43.2)

Funny enough, the guy he replaced J.B. Bickerstaff is nipping at his heels in Detroit.

PORTLAND: + 13.8 wins (expected 20.5, on pace for 34.3)

Coach Chauncey Billups was considered a dead man walking, but did he earn a call from the governor?

L.A. CLIPPERS: + 11.6 wins (expected 35.5, on pace for 47.1)

Even more unexpected since Kawhi Leonard has only played 15 games.

BROOKLYN: + 10.9 wins (expected 19.5, on pace for 30.4)

Good for them? Or bad for them?

OKLAHOMA CITY: + 9.3 wins (expected 57.5, on pace for 66.8)

Hard to overachieve on an expected # 1 seed but they’ve done it.

MEMPHIS: + 8.2 wins (expected 46.5, on pace for 54.7)

Back to guns blazing, fastest paced offense in the league.

HOUSTON: + 8.2 wins (expected 42.5, on pace for 50.7)

Winning with youth and a great defense.

L.A. LAKERS: + 8.0 wins (expected 42.5, on pace for 50.5)

Winning with oldies and a mediocre defense.

CHICAGO: + 4.3 wins (expected 28.5, on pace for 32.8)

Ditto the Brooklyn comment. Did they wait too long to trade Zach LaVine?

DENVER: + 3.2 wins (expected 50.5, on pace for 53.7)

Never doubt the reigning MVP.

ATLANTA: + 2.3 wins (expected 36.5, on pace for 38.8)

This sounds good, but the line was probably too low to begin with.

N.Y. KNICKS: + 1.2 wins (expected 53.5, on pace for 54.7)

This listed over/under had been listed after the KAT trade.

SAN ANTONIO: + 0.8 wins (expected 35.5, on pace for 36.3)

Opposite of Atlanta, this preseason over/under was generous, so beating it is impressive.

INDIANA: - 0.1 wins (expected 46.5, on pace for 46.4)

About as close as you can get.

BOSTON: - 0.4 wins (expected 58.5, on pace for 58.1)

Cleveland’s success may be overshadowing another great year.

GOLDEN STATE: - 1.8 wins (expected 43.5, on pace for 41.7)

It’s possible that the Warriors may end up overachieving with their new big-headed addition.

TORONTO: - 3.2 wins (expected 28.5, on pace for 25.3)

Not sure what the plan was… or is…

MILWAUKEE: - 4.6 wins (expected 49.5, on pace for 44.9)

They stumbled out of the gates with that 2-8 start but have recovered OK.

SACRAMENTO: - 4.8 wins (expected 46.5, on pace for 41.7)

Their advanced stats have been better than their W-L record.

MIAMI: - 4.8 wins (expected 43.5, on pace for 38.7)

Sagging without Jimmy B.

DALLAS: - 5.6 wins (expected 49.5, on pace for 43.9)

Guessing a Luka Doncic trade was not on any books or bingo cards.

WASHINGTON: - 5.8 wins (expected 19.5, on pace for 13.7)

Tank masters.

MINNESOTA: - 6.1 wins (expected 51.5, on pace for 45.4)

Similar to Dallas, their over/under may have been inflated after a good playoffs.

UTAH: - 7.8 wins (expected 27.5, on pace for 19.7)

Finally embracing the darkness.

ORLANDO: - 8.0 wins (expected 47.5, on pace for 39.5)

KCP didn’t help much – their offense has sagged from 22nd to 29th this year.

PHOENIX: - 9.0 wins (expected 48.5, on pace for 39.5)

A fairly shocking number given the relative health of KD/Booker.

CHARLOTTE: - 11.0 wins (expected 31.5, on pace for 20.5)

The bottom teams are somewhat injury related, including Brandon Miller…

PHILADELPHIA: - 19.1 wins (expected 49.5, on pace for 30.4)

And Joel Embiid…

NEW ORLEANS: - 26.1 wins (expected 45.5, on pace for 19.4)

And Zion Williamson (and half their team.)

UNDERACHIEVING
