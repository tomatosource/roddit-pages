Most Injured Teams by Value Missed ($M): Dallas (37.4), Indiana (37), New Orleans (31.8), Oklahoma City (31.5), Miami (28.2)

Calculated using Spotrac’s Injury Tracker for players’ games missed and their Average Annual Value (AAV) for contracts.

They do have their own stat calculating contract value missed, but

  • this accounts for days missed rather than games (giving an inaccurate view on impacted games), and
  • only looks at how much players are being paid this season (so players like Nesmith, Braun and Jalen Williams are underrepresented since their extensions haven’t kicked in yet)

Instead, I took the maximum available AAV on the books for each player, and divided it by 82 games. Then for each game a player missed through injury, their value for that missed game is summed. Here are the results:

Three Talking Points:

  • No shock that the Mavs and Pacers sit atop the list, with a decently sized gap above 3rd place. Dallas’ record is quite impressive in the West, given the circumstances
  • The Wolves have been freakishly blessed when it comes to health to this point. Does that bode well or rather spell trouble, seeing as though they’re already behind their competition?
  • I was surprised by the Nuggets being 27th on Spotrac’s rank considering all the talk about their injuries, but even with this adjusted measure they barely make it out of the bottom 10
