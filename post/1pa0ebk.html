As someone who only played PoE 1 for 40~ hours…

… and with +700 hours in PoE 2, I’m really surprised Path of Exile lore is deeper than the Diablo universe.

Like most of you, I think the best Diablo game is D2. I have +2000 hours in D4, but it’s just because of the living situation I’ve been in for the past three years and playing ARPGs as a pastime activity is pretty fulfilling for me.

However, I’ve loved PoE 2 since launch, and my love for the game is growing bigger and bigger with each league. After watching the teaser with Atziri for the upcoming league, my interest in PoE lore has grown stronger.

After watching a couple of videos about the Beast, Atziri, and the Vaal civilization, I must say that I’m really impressed by how PoE lore is deeply connected with philosophy, mythology, ancient history, and theology.

It’s very interesting for me also because my favourite act in PoE 2 is Act 3. I love going back in time to see the ancient civilization, their technology, and to witness the cataclysm. It reminds me of Graham Hancock.

That’s pretty much it. I guess this post has become an appreciation post the more I wrote. What do you think? I’m sure there are many more like me who didn’t play PoE 1 that much and came from the Diablo universe and fell in love with PoE 2. I wonder how much you care about PoE’s lore.
