Can we have some simply, clear method to indicate when we are “low life” or “low mana”?

EDIT:

THIS POST IS ABOUT THE LACK OF A SIMPLE, CLEAR, IN GAME INDICATION OF WHEN YOU ARE ON “LOW LIFE”

I KNOW HOW TO GET TO LOW LIFE ALREADY!

  • Portal back to my hideout
  • Work out what 35% of my life is (OK, I only do that once now that my gear is pretty set) by literally using a calculator
  • Equip a set-aside-for-this-purpose Soul Tether to drain ES
  • Swap out a support gem for a set-aside-for-this-purpose Life Tap support
  • Use that skill to drain life down to below the calculated value
  • Re-equip my belt, which as it gives me Sanctified Gound, immediately starts recovering life
  • Quickly cast Convalescence to gain ES to lock the life in place
  • Wait too long go over by 3 health, and have to restart the whole process again.

Now, I accept all that as part of the price of gaining the benefits of Low Life (I mean, apart from the obvious offset of Low Life being that you have, umm, low life).

I know there are things you can do, I know I am on low life when my DPS jumps up, for example, or my speed goes up.

The point is, it should be something clearly indicated, it is a key trigger for many effects yet is not actually directly indicated in any way!
