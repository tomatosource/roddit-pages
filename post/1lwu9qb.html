Surprisingly Immersive gameplay

I had previously looked into POE1 but thought it was too arcadey and fast paced with constant explosions. Imagine my surprise when I tried POE2 the other day and it’s slow and immersive.

It reminds me of a souls like arpg which I didn’t know I would like. I think the monsters move a bit too fast for how heavy I feel as a character but the general gameplay and interactions feel way more intentional. I enjoy that I can bait a monster to attack and roll behind for a counter. I like that normal mobs can still feel threatening if I let them overwhelm me. Fights feel more meaningful overall and keep me engaged. Anyway I’m just shocked at the games pace and am enjoying it.
