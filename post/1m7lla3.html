Georges Niang can surpass Porzingis on the all-time Celtic list in 14 playoff games

In 32 games as a starter throughout his career, Georges Niang has averaged 13.1 points, 4.3 rebounds, 1.9 assists in 28.6 mpg on 48%/46%/96% shooting. Assuming he gets starter minutes by default perhaps his numbers here hold to that on our little borderline lotto/play-in team next season.

Kristaps Porzingis career Celtic playoff stats: 171 points, 82 rebounds, 16 assists

Gordon Hayward career Celtic playoff stats: 140 points, 50 rebounds, 36 assists

If, and that’s a BIG if, Boston makes the playoffs, Georges Niang would need just 10 games to surpass Gordon on the all-time Celtic playoff scoring list and basically tie Porzingis after 13 games.

If our Tatumless Celtics can defy all expectations and claw our ways to a couple back-to-back 7 game playoff series before being eliminated (like the Garnettless Celtics in 2009 that beat the Bulls in 7 before being eliminated by the Magic in 7), Georges Niang can have a legendary run.

Interestingly, Big Baby Glen Davis averaged 16 points during that 2009 run and finished with 221 points JUST in those playoffs. Clearly, Niang would have a LONG way to go to reach the heights of the entirety of Baby’s Celtic career, but Hayward and Porzingis are very much in reach.
