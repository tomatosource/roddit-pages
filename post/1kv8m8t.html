Michael Jordan played all 82 games in each of the ‘96, ‘97, ‘98 Three-peat title seasons. 304 consecutive games in total.

It’s borderline unheard of in today’s era of load management, but starting with his age 32 season, MJ didn’t miss a single game for those 3 straight championship seasons.

He also didn’t miss a single playoff game in that run either - 58 of them. In total, he played in 304 consecutive games during the Three-peat, and averaged over 38 minutes/game.

For his career, he played 9 seasons of 82 full games, the last being his final age 41 season with the Wizards. He also never missed a single playoff game in his career.

A different era, but the “Iron Mike” nickname could also applied to another Michael in the 90s. Pretty incredible compared to today’s era, where the playoffs seem more and more like a war of attrition.
