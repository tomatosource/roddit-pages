PoE 2 is much more enjoyable when taken slowly, but why is Delirium still a timed mechanic?

I am finding PoE 2 to be much more enjoyable when going slowly without the mindset of “I need to rush through this to get to the endgame” or “I need to complete my atlas ASAP”. Do a couple of zones, take a bit of a break, then go again. To me it feels the game was designed to be much more relaxed in that sense, especially compared to PoE 1 where you have to zoom all the time or be left behind. When I approach PoE 2 with that mindset it doesn’t bother me that zones are large for example.

With that then why is Delirium a timed mechanic? Every time I see a deli mirror I panic and start going to the end of the map as fast as possible, skipping everything and often lots of mobs as well and even then I barely make it to the end of the timer. Just why? It seems completely out of place compared to everything else that lets you take as much time as you want.
