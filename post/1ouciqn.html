Today is the greatest day of my entire life since the 2011 nba championship

I’ve been a Mavericks fan since I was 8. I grew up in rural Kansas where we got the local Mavericks games every game until OKC became a team and then it changed to that.

I watched every single Mavericks game from 2004-2008 and then i watched every playoff game they ever did after that.

When they lost the championship in 2006 I fell to my knees and cried and begged for mercy. I couldn’t fathom how they lost that. I was diabolical the next days in school

When they had their revenge season in 2011and Dirk whooped everyone including Kobe and LeBron, I cried the exact equivalent but this time? This time it was joy. It was because they won the championship.

Fast forward to 2024 and that mavs team was the best one since 2011 and Luka was the best player in the world. He was so clutch and so fun to watch. I think if he were fully healthy they would have won the 24 chip. I cried again and I couldn’t believe they had gotten this far and fallen once again. It felt really personal this time. For me

Fast forward to Feb 1st 2025. I fell to my knees crying again. This time? Because Luka was traded. I cried and fell to my knees in front of my wife and children. I had to make them understand that passion is something you need to have in life. Finding that passion and moving forward in life when that passion gets tortured.

Fast forward to today. November 11th, 2025. The very man who decided to trade Luka is getting what he deserves. And that is being fired and not having a meaningful job within the Mavericks organization. This is the greatest moment of my life right now. Words can not describe how much I wanted this to happen and I’m up and springing with tears of joy today.

This all brings me to my next point. Whenever you do something that a lot of people would have a problem with, you need to understand how it affects other people. It put millions of people in torturous nights, sleepless nights, explaining to their children why Luka isn’t on the team anymore only for the children to go to bed crying and wondering why this has to happen to their fav team and player. It’s hurtful.
