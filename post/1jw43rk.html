Despite having a record of just 40 - 40, the Orlando Magic have clinched first place in the South East division.

As it stands:

  1. Magic: 40-40

  2. Hawks: 37-42

  3. Heat: 36 - 44

  4. Hornets: 19 - 61

  5. Wizards: 17-63

Saw this and just thought Damn this division is ass. How many times has the first place in the division ended at .500 or below?

For those thinking the Hawks still have a chance at winning the division, they don’t. It goes like this:

  1. compare head to head, magic are currently winning 2 to 1 but there is another game to play which could make it 2 -2

Then

  1. compare divisional record and the magic are 12-3 and the hawks are 9-6 and there aren’t enough divisional games for the hawks to catch up

Edit:

I just checked and in 18⁄19 the magic won the southeast with a record of 42-40. 20⁄21 the hawks won the SE division with only 41 wins in a 72 game season, Celtics in 11⁄12 with only 39 wins (in a 66 game season though, which comes out to 48 wins if converted to a 82 game season). I couldn’t be bothered to go back further than 2002 and back then there were only 4 divisions so I’d be surprised if there was anyone with a worse record than the 41 win hawks. Interestingly enough almost every time a team had won the conference and had less than 45 wins, it was a South East division team.

TLDR The magic have a chance to break a record if they don’t win their next two games!
