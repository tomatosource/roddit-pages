Paul George’s Fit with Star Players

Let me get the obvious negative about George out of the way: He’s frequently injured so none of this matters if he doesn’t stay healthy. What happened with the fit in Philly and will it be different with the Celtics?

I saw a post on X by Kalshi Hoops, using data from databallr, stating that Paul George’s per-75 possession stats with Maxey off the floor were massive: 29.8 points, 6.1 assists, 65% TS, 47% on three-pointers. I’m not so familiar with databallr but I took a look at Cleaning the Glass lineup data (which is similar to other public data but omits garbage time). Here are the interactions between Maxey, George, and Embiid, who were the three top on-off players on the 76ers (Embiid +9.3, Maxey +7.5, George +4.6). These are net ratings per 100 possessions for the lineup over the regular season.

Maxey + George + Embiid: +3.4 over 919 possessions.

Maxey + George - Embiid: -0.3 over 821 (read as Maxey and George on, Embiid off).

Maxey + Embiid - George: +11.4 over 1061.

Embiid + George - Maxey: +8.4 over 227 (quite a small sample).

Maxey - George - Embiid: -1.4 over 2660.

George - Maxey - Embiid: +5.7 over 380.

Embiid - George - Maxey: -7.4 over 274.

The conclusion seems to be that Maxey and Embiid work together (very well), and George and Embiid work very well, but George and Maxey don’t work well, even when Embiid is on the floor with them.

Interesting, in the playoffs, Maxey without George was a -24 net on 174 possessions, and Maxey + George was “only” -5.

Does this mean that George doesn’t fit well with star perimeter players? Let’s go back to 23-24 when he was on the Clippers team with Kawhi Leonard and James Harden.

George + Kawhi + Harden: +10.6 over 2503.

George + Kawhi - Harden: +18.7 over 777.

George + Harden - Kawhi: +8.8 over 1028.

Kawhi + Harden - George: -2.2 over 701.

Paul George actually led that team in +/-, which is probably why Morey took that big bet on him. So I think at least two seasons ago, he was able to coexist with other ball-dominant perimeter players.
