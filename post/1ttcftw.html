The crafting nerf along with the Greater/Perfect Transmutation and Augmentation orb drop rate nerf is really rough on early mapping/gear progression in general.

After mapping to T15 with a grenade build, I have dropped way lesser transmute and augment orb than 0.4. It felt like the base drop rate in early mapping has lowered to compensate the atlas tree, to the point of loot level of pre buff 0.1.

While I agree that the essence crafting was very strong and required some adjustment, nerfing the drop rate for greater/perfect transmute and augment felt unnecessary. A huge part of gear progression since 0.3 has been using greater/perfect orb on white basses but this has become unnecessary expensive for early mapping. The removal of combinator just escalated this issue even more. Very limited amount of crafting provided by the runes of aldur to solve this issue other than adding ward.

If GGG is unsatisfied with the amount of EHP late game player has, nerfing crafting will just contribute to this problem even harder no matter how much you buff the base item defence value and runic ward. Good rolled items will just be expensive and out of reach of an average player while the atlas tree has just raised the ceiling even higher.
