In memory of my brother.

I’m not sure if this allowed but, this is something i just want to say.

This is my very first ARPG. I was an avid gamer through my life, and my younger brother introduced me to Path Of Exile 2.

He started off by showing me flicker strike and how he always wanted to make it “come online and work” he struggled horrendously to get it to work perfectly where all he had to do was use flicker strike and nothing more. He eventually settled on Warrior and Lightning Arrow Deadeye.

I gave him all the currency I had. It wasn’t much, but he was able to clear t15 maps with ease and chase that dopamine hit of dropping rare gear. I would hear him squeal over the mic when a rare item dropped, and he would rant and rave about how amazing the community is as people would always reach out and help him.

Not so long ago, he died in his sleep after a 9 hour long path of exile session with me. Those last 9 hours i had with him were no doubt the most precious memories i have. I have tried to get back into it within the last few days, but I haven’t been able to bring myself to it. I still log in to his account (this is me on his account right now) just to try and immerse myself in his life and interests and be in his space and see what surrounded him to have him feel so much joy.

I miss him very dearly, I just want to take every opportunity I can to talk about my little brother and his absolute adoration for this game. I log on to his steam account to try and complete the flicker strike build for him and I am hoping one day I can succeed and get the gear that he needs so he can fly through maps and drop divine orbs wherever he may be right now.

To all the flicker strike enjoyers out there, just know that one of you loved it so much that he made it his absolute goal to succeed in the build. And to all those who helped him find joy in this amazing game, I hope every single one of you are living incredible lives filled with love and joy.

Rest in Peace, N.

Edit; Thank you, everyone, so much for your kind words. It’s times like this i truly have faith in humanity. I appreciate you all so much.
