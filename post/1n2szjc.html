‘Twas the night before 0.3

‘Twas the night before 0.3 and all throughout the forum,
All were abuzz about the new features laid before them.
The league starters were considered, and the builds crafted with care,
As dreams of new content lingered fresh in the night air.
&nbsp;

Each exile sat eager with anticipation in their eyes,
While GGG prepared for them a most wondrous surprise.
Jonathan and Mark did their very best to prepare,
The free trial was going to be a burden to bear.

&nbsp;

Quickly to the patch notes I read again in awe,
Seeing each new feature made me giggle and guffaw!
For everything was set to become a most joyus romp,
“Friday could not come soon enough!” I said with a stomp!

&nbsp;

I sighed and I stumbled as I looked towards my bed,
Knowing that a good nights rest would be best for my head.
For clarity and energy could carry me and make me stronger,
Making my exile pursue the new act that much longer.

&nbsp;

With a yawn and a nightcap I turned towards YouTube,
To watch the creators ponder, craft, and collude.
Their excitement brought to me such a broad and happy smile,
My own mind started thinking once more of my precious Exile.

&nbsp;

Will I wield a bow, a staff, or maybe even just my fists?
Or will I just go with the meta, and copy from the lists…
Not being strong and viable is something to fear,
Perhaps it’s just that much easier to go with what’s S-Tier.

&nbsp;

No! I say, with a confident boast!
I will make my own build and I will be the Exile with the most!
For all the bits and bobs I find while I play,
Can now be sold in my hideout and put on display.

&nbsp;

Trade will boom and I will profit,
Map after map, I refuse to stop it.
My stash will grow and my riches will bloom,
And with that though I return my eyes to the room.

&nbsp;

Comfort fills my heart, and sleep has finally struck,
I’m ready to close my eyes and into my bed I tuck.
My slumber finally comes and my dreams play true,
And from the bottom of my heart, I hope the very same for you.

&nbsp;

With my last breath I do whisper without sight,
“I wish you fortune in this league, and a very good night.”
