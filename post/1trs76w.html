New league is awesome! My take on my first hours into the new league

Heading to bed now, after a really good and long session and it’s been a blast! Can’t wait to come back soon and continue. Right now I’m in act 3 and just completed trial of chaos with my monk invoker talisman wolf build. (SSF)(PS5)

I played since the League began and for like 7-8 hours. I won’t comment on endgame since I haven’t made it there yet! Here are my own personal good things and bad things :)

The good -

- New league mechanic is really cool and a chance to choose what currency/ loot to get and loads of currency is great!

- Rearranging campaign along with tons of improvements/ streamlining/ small changes here and there is really refreshing and makes going through campaign fun, quicker and more enjoyable. I noticed many areas with changes throughout so far in my run. I hope more things like this continues to be added :)

- 0 crashes, and 0 game breaking bugs.

- Updated Rune tab is really great and easy to navigate.

- New league areas are quick and enjoyable to run through.( ruins/ vaults idk)

- Challenges yay ! The portal mtx you get after killing geonor is awesome and continuously completing challenges throughout campaign is rewarding.

- Might just be luck but the few abyss areas in act 2 felt more rewarding, felt like I got desecration currency each time which I liked.

- Not sure if any changes was made or not but geonor and act 2 final boss felt tough and very rewarding the music as always in these battles just feel very epic. I don’t think any changes were made idk but they are really great bosses and fights

- UI and text- generally in tabs and advanced info on skills looks way more crisp and easier to read.

- Runic ward on armor is great! Adding another life source means less one shots and makes the game feel more fair. The sound when Runic ward is activated is punchy and noticeable and If I die after that it doesn’t feel unfair but I blame myself not the game.

- Lots of verisium so far yay! It doesn’t feel like you are bombarded with it but given enough to use when your build needs extra defence and in preparation for tough fights like end of act bosses .

- league encounters are tough but rewarding

- looking forward to endgame and being able to put 3 abyss in my maps ( love me some abyss 🤩)

The bad-

- The countdown shown on ps5 was wrong and showed an hour behind the official one on the website. I did get in at the right time, but was a real bummer listening to my friends on PC counting down the seconds and I thought I had to wait another hour.

- One timeout after I was let in and had to go back in queue ( although i still got in quickly)

- Minor bugs like getting stuck different places ( just a few times) trial of chaos fx and an issue with mob spawning with a vaal temple chest, I assume it spawned enemies out of bounds since no enemy was anywhere but had to reload to checkpoint to respawn enemies so I could claim the chest.

- I still think drop rates of small essences in early game is too low. I think upping small essences early game would encourage more crafting without being too op.

- Finding rune/ recipes in maps. Perhaps this is because I played the campaign many times already but most objectives feel intuitive and somewhat easy to find, especially now with more ways to help players navigate the maps. The runes however ( the area quests ones ) should be given the same kind of love. Hints / trails or something to help find them. I found myself several times only missing these after clearing the map area objectives and had to go clear most of the map which felt a little frustrating.

- After doing the first ascendency trial when I enter my passive tree it says I have two points to use but I don’t . After the second trial it says I have 4 points to use when I enter my passive tree, which I don’t ( I already used them all)

- Trials in general, I feel like these could be optimized a lot and made more fun. Maybe remove some of the worst punishments, add more interesting fun ones and remove honour system . I really like the idea of a rogue lite experience like trial of sekhemas but I think most would agree they are not the most engaging, fun or interesting content available in game. With some updates and reworks I think they could be a proper alternative to mapping meanwhile being challenging and fun to complete to gain your ascendency points.

- Basic transmutes and augmentation orbs. I suggest increasing the shards given from disenchanting blue items. I think generally the drop rates are fine with these but especially for ssf I think making it more worthwhile gathering trash blue items to disenchant would be really good. I don’t believe most players are gathering blue items to disenchant since its a bit of a chore imo. For players wanting to craft on many white items it should be more worthwhile doing the work of collecting and tp’ing back with a lot of trash blue items. I don’t think increasing the disenchant reward in basic transmutes and augmentation by 100% would hurt the economy. It would simply reward players more who do the extra work of disenchanting everything and give them and ssf players particularly, more options going forward to craft items.

Disclaimer these are all my own personal opinions and feedback( very early into the new league )

I understand and respect if anyone has different opinions than mine but also hope you will respect my opinions but feel free to express your opinions or data gathered. Hope everyone has a good league and good job to GGG on a a great launch.
