Hunting for nexus feels worse than running X maps.

Having not seen this sentiment stated anywhere before I thought I would make a post about it.

To progress in maps we now go hunting for the corrupted nexus. In theory this sounds more interesting, but imo this drags out progression even further. I’m only at tier 4, but I had to search these things out pretty far away from the starting position. In most maps I just run from rare to rare to travel closer to the next nexus. Luckily all rares are shown from the start now, which is a plus.
