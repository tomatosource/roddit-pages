Women’s Baseball League Naming Question and Discussion

Not sure about you all, but I for one have heard of and am excited for the Women’s Pro Baseball League. One thing I’ve noticed is that none of the teams actually names yet, other than their respective city: San Francisco, Los Angeles, Boston, and New York. 

The baseball historian inside of me hopes that they won’t do the tired “fans pick the name” thing. That inner historian, which I really should more fully embrace, wants those names to grow organically. For instance, many or most Giants fans know they weren’t called the Giants at first, but were the New York Gothams until their manager called them the Giants after a big win against Philly. Well, that’s the story from 1885 at least, whether it’s true or not.

I think it’d be cool if they were to play the season and then encourage both players and fans to figure it out for themselves. If the SF team asked me to name them, I’d very much like to suggest the SF Furies, which has a ring to it and ties in with the Golden State Valkyries. The Furies are the Roman deities of vengeance. Perhaps that would stick. Or get rejected because of “furries”. Just my two cents. What about you?
