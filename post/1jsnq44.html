I don’t think I want methodical gameplay for my ARPGs

Not to say it’s a bad thing, it’s just, I look back on playing through things, and I just kind of burn out at the thought.

Funnily enough it was me quitting out and loading up Vampire survivors that reminded me. I like feeling powerful. I enjoy laying waste to hundreds of enemies, I love having a character built with 20 layers of defense that is basically immortal against all but a select few pieces of content.

I have fun being able to just grab gear off the ground, slap a res mode at the bench and go, and then properly craft my gear once I’m at maps, or just buying something from someone else who’s done that work for me.

I like moving fast, blasting my way through at a brisk pace, and having trade offs being in me choosing to take something instead of something else, not because I chose something.

This isn’t to knock poe2 though, or maybe it is. Maybe it’s just me realizing that I don’t think poe2 will be the game for me. Which is alright. If nothing changes, it’s not like I’m not absolutely spoiled for other options. Poe2 can be the game poe 2 players enjoy. I just suppose I’m not one of them
