PSA: Your other characters can still finish and loot a map if you die with no portals left

Sorry if this was known before, not sure if it’s intended or a bug, but I just randomly learned this.

If you have more than one endgame character and die with no portals left, your other characters can still do the map, pick up any loot, do the mechanics…

So if you drop that Mirror and die - just login with your alt

Hopefully this is helpful to at least one person
