Acolyte of chayula (rework suggestion)

I love the theme behind acolyte of chayula. I however don’t feel the fantasy of utilizing chaos with this ascendancy to be satisfactory. The identity of what chaos damage looks like for this ascendancy I feel can be based on how the needler weapon in the Halo franchise works. A super combine effect of accumulating chaos energy upon reaching a certain threshold should result in an explosion (this can either apply on oneself or enemies). This will distinguish chaos energy amongst the other types of elemental counterparts. (not sure if a mechanic like this already exists in game)
