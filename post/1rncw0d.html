[Karalis] Celtics announce Vucevic’s surgery on his right ring finger and say he’ll be reevaluated in 3 to 4 weeks.

Nikola Vucevic today underwent successful ORIF surgery to stabilize a fracture in his right ring finger.

The surgery was performed at New England Baptist Hospital by Dr. Herve Kimball, assisted by Celtics Team Physician Dr. Tony Schena.

He will be reevaluated in 3-4 weeks and further updates will be provided as appropriate.
