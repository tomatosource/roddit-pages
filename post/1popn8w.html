The Druid Is My New Favorite Class

Wyvern’s rend consumes a power charge to grant 50% damage gained as lightning: Globally

Bhaitair’s Vengeance gives 1% damage as cold per 1 rage when you freeze a target: Globally

Putting Bhaitair’s on Rend with freezing supports and some kind of power charge gen like lingering illusion means you get 50% as lightning plus however much rage you have as extra cold. I have 72…

Not counting savage fury for 30% more, freezing mark on pounce for 30% as extra cold, walking calamity for 30% as extra fire or ferocious roar boosting attacks empowered by socketed skills by 80+% percent

The only downside is actually doing the combo, and remembering to refresh the buffs.

Just some fun interactions I’ve seen floating around I thought I’d put together
