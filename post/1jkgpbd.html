This was a really high quality regular season

With the season winding down and only a few weeks left, it felt appropriate to look back on the season, and man, this one felt like a good one.

  1. Tons of quality games. Thunder v Cavs back in January, the Lakers Nuggets game, that Lakers Warriors game on Christmas, so many fun ones. Even the cup was pretty good.

  2. Storylines went off. We got Bronny making his debut, we got Wemby’s continued rise before his blood clot, we got Luka on the Lakers, the rise of the Cavs, the Pistons becoming good again, the MVP battle, all kinds of stuff. And speaking of…

  3. One of the closest MVP races in recent memory. Shai and Jokic are putting together such quality seasons, it is so hard to pick which one should win. I’ve stopped debating at this point, and am just glad to be getting these insane runs from both players.

For all the talk and negativity about “what’s wrong with the NBA?”, this season has been a blessing. Really good one.
