Best Spectre after Hours of Playtime

It That Hates

Found in Breaches

Support gems: Faster cooldowns, faster attack speed, Muster, Infernal Legion

These things are sick. They have fast movement and rarely get “stuck”.

They have decent enough health that having infernal legion on them deals intense damage.

They have an aura that applies whither, pairing super well with chaos damage.

They have a laser beam that tears reality, hindering and pulling everything aside from unique bosses into the ground. Also decent chaos damage.

They have these chaos spikes that easily move over the map scape and hit for massive chaos damage.

I run 5 at a time and at least 6 “other minions” so they get as much muster benefit as possible. Can even use a couple clerics to increase their regen to stay alive longer.

Pair with passives that reduce the CD on revives and all the minion passives you can stomach and they shred through map clear and pinnacle bosses with relative ease. Also curse to lower resistances.

Use the command skill to group them all up for infernal legion damage x5. I’m hitting around 20k dmg just on the infernal legion alone.

To top it off the visual clarity of wtf is going on is vastly better than Vaal Guard builds.

Why is this one not talked about more XD
