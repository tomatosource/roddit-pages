GGG, I don’t even need a new league mechanic or a new act in 0.5, just spend all your resources to fix the end game and performance issues.

This league is the perfect example of spreading your resources too thin, they kind of delivered on the druid class (which I assume is the primary focus) but the Vaal Temple is clearly a rushed attempt that lacked sufficient internal testing and iteration. Like too many things in PoE2, I can see the potential but it’s not there yet. I’m constantly reminded that this is a AA studio with a limited budget and manpower, working on two games simultaneously.

That’s why I believe the next patch should focus solely on end game content and performance. One addresses retention for veteran players, while the other solves the horrible first impressions for newcomers (especially with free weekend launches, one crash is often enough for people to quit the game and never come back).

All of them experienced crashes or freezes with varying frequency. Despite this, four of them pushed through to T15 end game. None have returned this league, and I can’t really blame them.

Just focus on fixing your game, GGG. Everything else can wait.
