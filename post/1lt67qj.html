Bird in Games 5-7 of 1981 ECF: 27/13/5 on 55%TS..while adding 4.7 offensive rebounds, 3.3 steals and 2.0 blocks per game

The Celtics were down 3-1 against the Sixers led by 1981 NBA MVP Julius Erving. They came back and won the next 3 games by a total of 5 points.

  • Game 5: 111-109
  • Game 6: 100-98
  • Game 7: 91-90

Bird averaged 26.7 points, 12.7 rebounds, 4.7 assists per game on those 3 games. He shot 46% from the field, 100% from threes (just 1⁄1) and 91% from the free throw line.

That’s pretty good numbers.

But when watching the tape, what stood out to me was, he was EVERYWHERE, doing everything all at once. Imagine TJ McConnell in this year’s Finals, outrebounding Hartenstein, being the first man on the floor on loose balls, intercepting inbounds and outlet passes. But your MVP is doing that.

Almost 5 rebounds a game for a guy who they said cannot jump. Add 2 blocks per game. More than 3 steals per game – mostly interceptions, outthinking the opposing team, reading their next move – by a guy who they said cannot run. It looked like a Chess Grandmaster just completely controlling all 64 squares of the basketball court. I think this is a testament that hustle and effort goes hand-in-hand with basketball IQ. We saw similar with Jokic when they won the 2023 Championship. They say his weakness is defense. But we’ve seen him poking balls out of drivers and big men posting him up, that would lead to a fastbreak point for Jamal or AG.
