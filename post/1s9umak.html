Why are the PoE2 Passive Tree starting locations still restricted?

I’ve played a lot of PoE1, and in that game, the starting locations make perfect sense because every class shares the exact same tree. However, I’m finding the logic for PoE2 much harder to follow.

In PoE2, the tree is different for each class. Because of this, keeping the starting locations limited doesn’t really make sense anymore. This limitation effectively makes the opposite side of the tree redundant for the absolute majority of classes and builds. If I want to play a Witch with crossbows and armor, for example, the Mercenary side of the tree is basically unreachable unless I sacrifice everything else just to get there.

This predefined pathing also heavily limits leveling. It’s strange that the game doesn’t limit our weapon or skill gem choices at the start, yet we aren’t allowed to choose a different path on the tree to support those choices. When GGG mentioned class-specific trees, I thought it would mean more variability—like a Warrior being able to start from the Ranger side to avoid “wasted” nodes—but instead, I feel forced to level with specific gear and defenses just because they are the only things nearby. The only alternative is buying specific leveling gear just to avoid suffering with the limited passive skills available on your path.

Additionally, the actual shape of the tree doesn’t seem to make sense with these restrictions. Why do we have those early Energy Shield and Spell Damage nodes if no one actually takes them? (Yes, I know about Pathfinder, but that’s an outlier).

If this limitation is just an Early Access thing to avoid spending extra resources on balancing right now, I understand. But in the long term, it doesn’t make sense to me. Am I missing something?

Does anyone else feel like the current design contradicts the freedom PoE2 offers in other areas?
