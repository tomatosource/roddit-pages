Act 4 is a love letter to PoE 1’s Act 1

Apologies if this has been discussed before - I got to 0.3 a little after it came out so I missed the initial discussion on the new act, but what immediately struck me was how much inspiration it takes from Act 1 of PoE1.

Now, obviously there are aesthetic similarities - a cold, coastal area with Karui influence (Act 1 being the site of Kaom’s ill-fated attempt to establish a Karui empire on the mainland and the Act 4 archipelago being their homeland) but quite a few of the islands are directly inspired by 1’s first act.

Similarities include:

  • An Eternal Empire prison overran by undead (both prisons)

  • A bay of crashed ships inhabited by undead sailors (The Ship Graveyard, Kedge Bay) with a ghostly pirate boss (Captains Fairgaves and Hartlin). Kedge Bay’s soundtrack even includes the starting motif of The Coast.

  • A cavern ruled by a siren luring the unwary to their deaths (Merveil’s Caverns and Whakapanu Island).

  • A rocky area filled with carrion birds that spit blood/flesh projectiles (The Climb, Shrike Island).

Act 1 of PoE1 was even the place you found Navali, back when Prophecy was still a thing, although this is a stretch.

I just think it’s awesome to see the themes in 1’s first act expanded upon so heavily.
