The OKC Thunder are the first team since 1989 to record 45+ blocks and 65+ steals in any 5-game span

The Thunder have recorded 69 steals and 45 blocks in their first three games this season. The last team to have 65+ steals and 45+ blocks was the 1988-89 San Antonio Spurs. This stat isn’t just for the first 5 games of a season. It’s any 5 consecutive games of a season.

If we cherry pick a little more and set the bar at 69+ steals and 45+ blocks, the Thunder are just the 3rd team in NBA history to accomplish that.

Source: Stathead
