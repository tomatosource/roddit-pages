Friendly Reminder - Did you receive your T-Shirt and/or Hoodie?

Hey all - Just a friendly PSA to others out there about bundles and physical goods. With the initial POE2 bundle releases, I purchased a bundle that included clothing. Months went by and I forgot I selected the clothing instead of the virtual points. I randomly sent a email to support a week or two ago inquiring which option I did indeed select, and it appears they had a problem on their end which resulted in me receiving extra online currency instead of my clothes. Two quick emails later my items are on the way and they let me keep the free points. Shoutout to the support team for an easy and hassle free fix. I am unsure if they are going out of their way to fix every order that had this issue however.

Just a reminder to someone who may have forgotten like me and they need a memory jumpstart!
