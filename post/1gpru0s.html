“I don’t even care if we win the NBA Cup”

Shut up loser. This isn’t 7th grade. Being apathetic and not caring about something doesn’t make you cool. It makes you look like you think you’re cool. I’m a diehard Celtics fan and I want them to win everything. I want them to go 82-0 (or 80-2, whatever). I want them to go 16-0 in the playoffs. And I want them to take home the NBA Cup.

“But it’s nothing compared to a championship!”

No fucking shit. Who do you think you’re arguing against? Who’s the person out there saying this trophy means as much as the Larry O’Brian? Seriously who? A couple dumbass Lakers fans? Who cares? Of course it’s not a championship, everyone knows that. That isn’t a novel piece of insight. We know.

And just because it’s only two years old doesn’t automatically make it lame. Everything had to be new at some point. This tournament isn’t going anywhere, so in another 30 years there’s gonna be another 30 winners and it’ll be cool to look back and see who won. If a random dude, let’s say LaMelo Ball, has a great career and he’s on the border to make the Hall of Fame, it could help his case if we look back and see that he led his team to an NBA Cup. What’s wrong with that? It’s just another accolade like the ECF MVP, of which Tatum and Brown each have one. One more accolade doesn’t cheapen anything. It’s just fun

And if you still need a reason to care about the NBA Cup, the Celtics players themselves said they care. Joe Mazzulla cares. We know he values any opportunity for the Celtics to practice under duress. A single-elimination tournament is perfect for that reason alone.

In conclusion, let’s fucking win this thing! And then win the real championship!
