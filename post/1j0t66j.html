Amazing day in the NBA today: A quick recap, Six games are won in the last minute of play with two game winners in the last 10 seconds, one going to overtime. Cavs & Celtics doesn’t disappoint on national TV with the two stars dueling & putting up 40 each. Incredible games all day, stars performing.

Celtics vs. Cavs on national TV doesn’t disappoint with a 20 point comeback from the 1 seed and a close game from halftime going to the minute, a 40 point duel between their two biggest stars, and the reigning finals MVP also adding 37 points.

Pacers vs. Heat has the game winning shot to make it 123-120 with 40 seconds to go and Heat win on a steal, 125-120.

Raptors vs. Bulls tank off goes to overtime, with the Bulls winning 125-115 - Lonzo making two threes in OT, a great moment in his comeback year.

Knicks vs. Grizzlies goes to the buzzer, a three from OG Anunoby winning it with 5 seconds to go.

Twolves vs. Jazz is another winner with 9 seconds to go, dunk from Walker Kessler and the Jazz get their 15th win of the season.

Lakers vs. Clippers also is close from start to finish, the teams not separated by more than 10 all night. Lakers win 106-102. Zubac has a 27⁄16 & only 2 misses Moses Malone statline.

Even some of the blowout wins weren’t business as usual: The Nuggets end the Pistons 8-game win streak, close game until they run away with it in the 3rd. Suns beat the Pelicans in weird fashion, Bol Bol, Royce O’Neale and Nick Richards the leading scorers in a game with KD & Booker playing 35 minutes. Killian “Worst Basketball Player of All Time” Hayes also had his first game with more than 15 points since 2023.

It’s a very good time to watch basketball even as a neutral, whether it’s regular season or if you’re waiting for the playoffs. Most teams are playing hard as hell and trying to win for seeding in a tough West where the #2-4 seeds now are all tied at 21 losses & #6-9 are all tied at 32 wins. The East is worse but maybe just as chaotic, with the two juggernauts up top and teams who look incredible one day and horrible the next. The media has got plenty to talk about tomorrow.
