[ESPN] Taylor Swift’s courtside Cavs ECF seat goes up for bidding

Source: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/48985479/cleveland-cavaliers-taylor-swift-seat-auction-bid-eastern-conference-finals

According to listings on The Realest, the courtside seat that superstar singer/songwriter Taylor Swift sat in during Game 3 of the Eastern Conference finals between the New York Knicks and the Cleveland Cavaliers has been listed for auction.

The website also has 5 other listings for courtside ECF Cavs seats for auction. As of this writing, here are the highest bids for each seat that a celebrity sat in from the ECF (with about a week left to go before sale)

  • Taylor Swift: $6000
  • Travis Kelce: $525
  • Ben Stiller: $385
  • Kylie Jenner: $280
  • Machine Gun Kelly: $265
  • Timothée Chalamet: $106

Also 2 player seats previously sold from a regular season Cavs game:

  • LeBron James: $1905
  • Luka Doncic: $905

Source: https://therealest.com/category/stadiumarenacourse-items?partners=Cleveland+Cavaliers
