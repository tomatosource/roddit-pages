One week of the NBA has passed. Who do you see as the Celtics biggest threat in the Eastern Conference?

The Pacers played us amazingly last night. They’re not sparkling defensively (and weren’t last year) but they’re probably one of 4-5 teams in the entire league that can compete with our scoring prowess any given game. They should be a lot better than their 2-3 record right now, imo. If the Bucks keep playing as is, the Sixers are still injured through the season, and the Knicks don’t suffer any injuries I think they’ll be a top 3 seed in the East.

However… I think the Cs biggest threat in the East is definitely the Cleveland Cavaliers. They’re 5-0 right now with wins over the Knicks (though not as convincing as our win over them) and a 24 point beat down of a Lakers team that seems improved from last year. With Kenny Atkinson, starting 5 is playing very well offensively in particular, while their defense also is very improved from last year. If they keep this up and don’t get injured, they’ll be a very tough out in the playoffs and could definitely make an ECF run.

I’d also have to put the Orlando Magic up there. Paolo Banchero seems to be the next face of the NBA and has been playing at a first team all-NBA level so far. The Magic’s defense is also good, one of their strengths from last year. If Paolo keeps playing like this, he could very well be in the MVP conversation.

Thoughts?
