Underrated defence or peaking at the right time?

Despite the lowest opponents’ PPG (107.2)*, the Celtics should be seen as the best, or at least one of the top defensive team in the league. While there are some general acknowledgments of our good two-way players, implying defensive prowess, the conversations around DPOY and all-defensive teams have completely glossed over the C’s. Some advanced stats point to KP having great defensive numbers (eg lowest opponents’ FG%) in his limited minutes, but he obviously won’t have enough games to be eligible for any awards. Even more nuanced analysis like Thinking Basketball’s defensive awards “The Thinkies” only make a passing mention of Horford.

So why is the best defensive team not getting any individual defensive award recognition? One theory is that they have regressed slightly- it could be said Jrue has lost a step and DWhite had a bit of a slump part of the year. Another perspective is that everyone just plays team defense and no one stands out. Or it could be that the team is holding back to peak in the playoffs, which I could understand to some extent, but doesn’t resonate with their existing good defensive rating.

So do you think anyone will get all-defense from the C’s? Who should, but likely to be robbed?

*[after the Magic at 105.5 who are an anomaly with a losing season and negative plus/minus]
