The Pacers’ 13-game losing streak is the 2nd longest in NBA history by a team coming off a Finals appearance — the NBA record is 17 straight losses

There have been 6 teams (including the Pacers) that had a losing streak of 10+ games in the season immediately following a Finals appearance. Two of those teams had multiple 10+ game losing streaks that season, so their are 8 total streaks.

The longest losing streak in the season immediately following a Finals appearance is 17 by the 1964-65 San Francisco Warriors. The Pacers 13-game streak is the 2nd longest.

Below are the 6 teams and streaks:

  • 2025-26 Pacers - 13 games
  • 2019-20 Warriors - 10 games
  • 2018-19 Cavaliers - 12 games and 10 games
  • 1976-77 Suns - 12 games
  • 1974-75 Bucks - 11 games
  • 1964-65 Warriors - 17 games and 11 games

Source: us, Basketball Reference.
