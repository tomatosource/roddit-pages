What is a new crafting loop?

Assuming that simple essences, greater essences, fracturing orbs, all omens and expedition artifacts are common enough and somewhat spammable. Things that come to my mind:

  1. Pick all good bases of the needed type
  2. If the base is white, essence it to magic. Put aside if the mod is bad, greater essence if it’s good. If 2nd mod is good, leave for further recombinating
  3. if 2nd mod is bad, put in other tab and later recombine with other bricks with 1 good mod
  4. If the base is magic, aug-regal-exalt it fishing for good mods. If in 4 mods none are good, put aside; if 1 or more is worthy of fracturing, use a Fracturing orb
  5. If the base is rare with a good mod, use a Fracturing orb (annul/omen to 4 mods first). If none of the mods are good, fish for good ones with exalts, if didn’t hit any, put aside
  6. If fractured a good mod, try to exalt/chaos/annul more good mods, with omens or not. If fracturing didn’t hit a good mod, put the item with bricks from step 3 to recombine later or try to omen chaos/annul bad suffixes/prefixes
  7. Here I’m uncertain because while it is said that we now choose the mods we want to keep, are other mods added in the mix? Is there a chance to add a new mod? If junk mods affect the recombination, we need to annul/omen annul bad mods as much as possible on everything we put in the recombinator
  8. Slam together everything we have, first trying to get items with 2 good mods, then slam 2-mods to get 4-mods and so on. Testing required to see what is best: is it come down to selecting 5-6 good mods and praying, or is it about trying to get 3 good prefixes/suffixes and omen craft (gamble) the rest
  9. 3:1 everything we put aside or brick to recuperate some of the bases

Please correct me or suggest other good approaches. It still looks tedious, but at least there is some agency now
