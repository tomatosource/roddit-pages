Flames of the Dreamer, a Prophecy of Leech

I bring this vision forth to the Council of 13.

The Dreamers Great Glorious Guidance has foretold that Leech shall now be limited to the maximum of all sources rather than its cumulation, praise be to the Void Illusion.

But alas, I have been troubled by dreams of despair. For what now will the Flames of Chayula do in the face of the Dreamers wrath? If I am to leech the vitality of my foes in volumes greater than the Dreamers flames would offer, will the Flames forsake me their offerings? They are themselves, by definition, Leeching vigor unto me.
