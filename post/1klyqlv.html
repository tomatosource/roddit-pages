Tactician Rework Suggestions

I’m going to submit a suggestion for a rework of the tactician ascendancy and I was hoping to get the communities feedback to help fibe tweak it before I do.

This ascention feels like it should specialize in banners, totems, and companions. Especially the mercenary companion that has yet to be released.

https://poe2db.tw/us/Summon_Companion

1: Team Effort: Enemies killed while in the pressence of you, your companions, or objects you create count as your kills. OR Team Effort: All enemies killed count as your kills.

Team Effort allows allies to generate valour for you and allows your totems to get your herald on kill effects.

1a: Cannons Ready!: 20% totem skill speed, totems prioritize the last enemy you hit.

A bit less totem skill speed but you aren’t required to attack with them.

1b: Stratagems Banner: Gain a special equip slot for a ring. Banners also grants 5% of the special equiped rings effects.

A way for the tactician to stratagize a bit.

2: Suppressing Fire: Projectiles pin. Seems fine

2a: Right Where We Want Them: Pinned enemies can’t take actions.

I think it is fine but I have seen a lot of posts about it needing to be nerfed.

3: Keep Up: Companions are at least as fast as you, humanoid companions gain am additional +10% movement speed.

There might be support gems or a passive buff to cover this later. So I’m on the fence.

3a: Passive companion skills are 50% more effective.

If Titans can get 50% for all small passive skills. I don’t think it’s to powerful to grant 50% to all of a single catagory.

4: Solid Plan: -50% cost of persistant buffs

Fine as is.

5: Unleash Hell!: Skill Supporting Fire

This one needs a lot. I made a few versions but this one feels like it has the most synergy with the rest of the ascention.

Supporting Fire: Banner, Buff, Companion, Minion, Persistant, Totem

Summons a hwach totem squad which is placed in a hidden position to provide tactical support. You can raise a banner to signal the hwach totem squad to Fire a volley of arrows on command.

• No valour gained from kills with this skill. • 10% more damage accuracy, and critical chance magnitude per valour. • Counts as a creature with 50% damage and 100% accuracy for its level. • Has a base 15% crit chance. (Might need to be adjusted. • AoE size is 4.5 and increases with aura increase effects. (Banners) • Gains any aura effects that are effecting you. • Does not count against any banner, companion, or totem limits.
