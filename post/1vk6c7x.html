New player farming advice

Hey guys, I’m a relatively new player here and just looking for some advice and ways to improve my farming strategies.

What are some of your guys farming strategies that work for you?

I usually run 4 deli tablets on 200% deli city maps with the following;
+% sim splinters
+% increased rare monsters
+% fracturing mirrors on 1⁄4 of the tablets

With max monster rarity waystones, with this strat I’m able to get about 3 sims per map plus a ton of raw drops, it’s fun, fast and rewarding.

For travel maps I usually run:
3 Abyss tablets with;
+% abyssal modifiers
+% rare monsters
Sometimes I throw a “abyss spawns 2 extra rare monsters” in there.

Split increased monster rarity and item rarity waystones. This strat seems to pull about an omen of light every map or every two maps.

OR

3 breach tablets with;
+% hive blood
+% wombgifts

Any waystones work, I wouldn’t say this is NOT a very lucrative strategy but turning in wombgifts does earn at least some currency rather than just boss rushing.

If I need a grand mirror to setup a new 200% deli city zone I put a +30% chance of unique monster spawn deli tablet in my travel setup.

I’m wondering how you guys feel about ritual, I’ve been hearing mixed reviews about how many omens you actually get and it seems like the setup requires 20 div tablets along with the cheap unique tablet. It doesn’t seem like this can be more profitable.

I honestly haven’t even got a chance to do any expeditions yet which is probably a mistake. I’ve just gotten kind of burned out from learning the game and sort of checked out before getting deep into ritual and expedition. This is my second season and I started super late last season so there’s still so much to absorb.
