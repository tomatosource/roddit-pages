0.4 endgame wishlist?

What are some things you would like to see get added to the 0.4 endgame adjustments? Personally I would like to see the following:

  1. Removal of rarity in gear and is instead baked on to the maps on top of the system we already have (like 10% base quant and rarity for each map tier).
