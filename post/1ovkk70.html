0.4 endgame wishlist?

What are some things you would like to see get added to the 0.4 endgame adjustments? Personally I would like to see the following:

  1. Removal of rarity in gear and is instead baked on to the maps on top of the system we already have (like 10% base quant and rarity for each map tier).
  2. More meaningful Atlas passives.
  3. Phrecia idols on top of the Atlas we will have. Instead of replacing the Atlas why not just make idols available on top of it and make them craftable? Async trade solves the big problem with the idols back in Phrecia.
  4. Scaling down group content loot. Will definitely be needed especially if idols will be implemented so that people will not be priced out of profitable idols by these 6 man giga juicing teams that print out 20 mirrors daily.
  5. Bosses being able to drop uncorrupted T16 maps. This actually makes bossing somewhat interesting and makes the overseer tablets useful.
