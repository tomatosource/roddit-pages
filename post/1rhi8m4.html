‘Twas the night before Tatum.

’Twas the night before tip-off, and all through the Garden,
Not a fan could sit still, not even the hardened.

The jerseys were hung in the lockers with care,
In hopes that their forward soon would be there;
The rookies were nestled, laced up in their shoes,
While highlights of daggers replayed on the news.

When out on the parquet there rose such a chatter,
I sprang from my seat to see what was the matter.
Away to the tunnel I flew like a flash,
Past banners and memories, parquet and splash.

The lights on the court in a green-tinted glow
Gave the luster of legend to hardwood below,
When what to my wondering eyes should appear,
But number zero, back healthy this year.

With a smooth, easy stride, so fluid to chase’em.
I knew in a moment it must be Jayson Tatum.
More rapid than fast breaks his footwork it came,
And he whistled and shouted and called out each name:

“Now Brown! Now Pritchard! Gonzalez, White!
On Queta! On Hauser! Let’s hoop tonight!
To the top of the East! To the front of the race!
Now dash away, dash away—give ’em no space!”

As defenders before a step-back must fly,
When met with a crossover, watch ankles comply,
So up from the elbow he rose with a flick—
A swish through the net—clean, silent, and quick.

And then, in a twinkling, I heard from the floor
The roar of a crowd like a lion’s great roar.
As I drew in my breath and was turning around,
Through smoke and through spotlight he bounded with pound.

He was dressed all in green, from his shorts to his sleeve,
And the Garden witnessed what it longed to believe;
His eyes—how they focused! His jumper—how pure!
His comeback was certain, decisive, and sure.

A wink to the bench and a nod to the stands
Had the fate of the conference back in his hands;
And I heard him exclaim, as the buzzer gave light—

“Happy hooping to all, and to all a green night.” 🍀
