The longer I play, the more impressed I am with this current patch.

Just a little shoutout to GGG for the current Patch. For me, its the best league by far. All these secrets are already really cool additions for us players / the community to find. But I think what really shines is the build diversity and the seemingly endless possibilities of Item and skill combinations.

We are still early into the league and obviously the current meta is mostly shaped by what people league started. But we are starting to see new and cool builds pop up everywhere.

What made me make this post is just one example of an item that shows how insane this patch really is:

The new Unique 2 handed Mace Twisted Empyrean is an Expedition Boss Unique: https://poe2db.tw/Twisted_Empyrean

The Design is already great: decent damage, good Crit and a unique spin on Melee by building a Mace around Mana Stacking. Awesome. It also comes with a pretty awesome skill attached.

But then there is Runeforging. After exploring Exepedition Areas, you can unlock new crafting Knowledge. This one unique Items then has 4 different upgraded versions. Thats 5 Uniques in 1. Thats crazy.

Idk. I personally think thats insane game design and so much work for a single Item that probably will allways be kind of niche. But its so incredibly cool that they put this much work in seemingly every detail of the game.

I was following the reveal stream and the Teasers throught the pre-season launch time and there was lots of cool stuff already. And it already looked like a gigantic patch.

But with every day I play, I explore so many more things and all I can think about is “I want to make a build around this immediately”.

Honestly outstanding work from GGG.
