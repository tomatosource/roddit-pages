So much to consider…

This morning I started out with 5 divs and about 200 exalts. I put in about 3 hrs of total play time today and ended with 16 divs and 1100 exalts.

​

This is when I realized how much of the game I was missing in the previous leagues I played. I’m getting drops I’ve never seen before, so I know GGG cooked when they worked on this league, but it’s not all them.

​

My build is light years better than any other build of I’ve cooked up. This is probably my least homebrewed build and it shows. I didn’t follow a guide, but I did a couple of searches on what works when I wanted to increase my power level. My Ice Strike MA obliterates everything, but even better my defense is so good. Not dying really has kept me engaged compared to other builds I’ve done in previous league. When you can go through 5 or 6 maps in under 30 mins, it doesn’t feel like a chore to play the game. Normally, i would’ve died enough times that I woukd bail on the game. This league everything dies, except me(almost everything and I still die a little bit)!

​

The endgame having it’s own storyline for each of the mechanics is such a blessing. No more mindlessly mapping to some location and or tablet setup, everything is straight forward and I can engage with what I want, when I want. This league was my first time fighting Arbitor and Kulemek(?).

​

Anyway, I’m still only playing this game maybe 1 - 2 hours a day just because I have other interest and responsibilities. But this league is so damn good and so much fun, I can’t imagine how they are going to make even better in 1.0…oh wait, we still don’t have “swords”, so at least there’s still something to look forward to.
