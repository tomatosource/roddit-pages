Not a huge fan of the emphasis on magical martial attacks.

I more or less get it on the monk, as they are a half int “class.” However, I feel like spear and bow skills are over saturated with magically infused elemental attacks. Where is the martial fantasy? Why are 90% of skills of the spell blade archetype? Do tornados really fit the spear and shield fantasy. There should be more weapon skills that deal pure phys in creative ways that fit their respective archetypes. I actually think rolling slam is the best skill in this regard. I just think it would be cooler to have a skill that does something interesting by swinging metal with the option to add elemental effects by other means: other skills, items, passive nodes, gear, etc…

TLDR: Dislike that every weapon skill set archetype is “spell-blade”
