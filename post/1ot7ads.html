Oladipo, once an All NBA, All Defensive player, is now in the G League

With the Wisconsin Herd apparently

I believe in celebrating player achievements and Oladipo is a trove for that. In his breakout season in 2017-18, he led the league in steals and was selected for both All NBA (3rd) and All Defensive (1st) honors. All star. MIP. Sky was the fking limit.

I had high hopes for him to become the next Heat surprise….but it wasn’t meant to be, with his career once again derailed by injuries 😔 hard to see him coming back meaningfully at this age and with his physical conditios, but stranger things have happened. Let’s wish him all the best.

PS. Oladipo’s career peak was imo the seven game series vs Lebron’s Cavs in the 2018 playoffs. Although Lebron ultimately triumphed, Oladipo led Pacers across ALL metrics in the epic series, including a G6 tripple double masterclass. The series also featured an up and coming player called Sabonis.
