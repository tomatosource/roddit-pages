Unique Enemy visibility/health bars, and the location of our buffs/debuffs desperately need some really simple changes.

Visibility is a pretty substantial hurdle in high tier content, and it forces us to trivialize aspects of the game (even moreso than we might otherwise) to compensate for the inability to position ourselves and/or target enemies, simply because there is too much visible clutter on the screen, for various reasons.

A huge aspect of this problem is the (lack of) visibility for unique enemies, and a side-effect that causes us to lose even more important information when one is present.

Unique Enemies are given a large, persistent health bar at the top of the screen. For whatever reason, this means they do not have health bars above their heads (when the option is enabled).

This means they essentially disappear in a cluttered screen, especially if they are small-sized, like Rogue Exiles.

This large health bar also covers up a big portion of our buffs, literally obscuring crucial information. Meanwhile, the entire bottom of the screen (where our health globe, charms, flasks, etc live) is completely empty.

There are two things that can be done, relatively easily (I assume) that would alleviate these problems almost completely:

  1. Give Unique Enemies floating health bars, just like normal, magic and rare enemies have. Put a little border and a star on it to distinguish from other enemies.

  2. Move our buffs/debuffs to the bottom left side, next to the health globe. It already doesn’t make any sense to put this information opposite literally every other piece of visible info for our character, and goes from silly to downright obstructive when covered by a boss’s health bar.

That’s it. Now we can always see our buffs and where the enemies are on the screen, things we can’t do now under some really easily achievable conditions.
