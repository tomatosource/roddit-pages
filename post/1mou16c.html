My Top 15 NBA Players Heading Into The 2025-26 Season

I know everyone has been waiting with bated breath for this one. I’ve written two of the most resoundingly popular posts of the summer on this subreddit, with some people calling them “funnier than I thought it would be” and “not the worst post I’ve seen”. Now, some might use their newfound fame to live lavishly, but I’ve been in the lab cooking. So, I present to you — my controversy-free, universally accepted list of the best players in the league right now.

CAVEATS:

My rubric will weigh three categories, in order of value to me. The most valuable is a player’s performance in the 2024-25 season. The second most valuable (also the second least valuable) will be the player’s trajectory and performance prior to last season (this is so I can justify my favourite players being listed higher than your favourite players), and lastly I will put the least value on their projected growth - two in George Bush is worth one in the small hands or whatever (I don’t know politics).

I went back and forth on including players likely to miss next season, but I’ve decided to exclude them. That means Tatum, Hali, and Dame won’t be on this list (Dame probably wouldn’t have made the list anyway if I’m being honest). I’ll factor health and injury into this, but only so much as I think it will impact a given player’s performance.

As always, my final caveat is that I do zero research, I’m very stupid, and I am technically a redditor - all signs pointing to this lacking in any real insight or knowledge.

With that said, strap in, get your fingers warmed up, and get ready to type out your paragraph pointing out the various flaws in my analysis, my inherent bias, and how I’m disrespecting your favourite player (you’re probably right, and I did it on purpose to spite you).

THE LIST:

1. Nikola Jokic

Infamous Serbian Mobster, horse-racer, and occasional basketball megastar Nikola Jokic has held the top-slot for the majority of the NBA fandom for a while now. I really, truly believe it’s closer than people think between him and the other tall Balkan player, but given playoff performances and the chart-breaking boxscores and advanced stats Jokic puts up routinely, I’ve got to hand the throne to him. This has gotta be my least controversial pick right?

2. Giannis Antetokounmpo

The Greek Freak, the Madman of Athens, the Rhodes-Block - Does anyone else feel like this guy is getting somehow underrated? Elite defense, unstoppable offense, the most dominant player since prime Bron - let’s not forget the guy might not be known for his playmaking but he’s no slouch there either. If he could stay healthy in the playoffs I think there’s a real chance I put him over Jokic on his defensive strength alone.

3. Luka Doncic

Now, this might seem like Lakers bias shining through. You might think “SGA has finished higher in MVP voting the last 3 seasons, including the one where he WON it. Oh, he also won a championship AND a finals MVP - how can you possibly rank Luka higher???” There’s are all very good points fictional commenter, but allow me to interject - the difference between Jokic and Luka’s offense talent and everyone else’s is still bigger than people seem to think. I think luka is a better 3 level scorer, and a better 3 point shooter than SGA (yes, Shai shot a slightly higher 3P% last year on much less volume, but Luka definitely edges him out here) - and I think his playmaking is top class. His defensive effort sucks, and so does his perimeter defense - but when he’s actually putting in effort his help defense and rim protection is serviceable, and he’s actually got enough IQ to get in the lanes and make good decisions. He’s also a pretty good defensive rebounder, and get’s a lot of steals by reading passes and having active hands. Also, he’s a Laker baby! MVP!

4. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

If you wanted to put SGA second I would be willing to hear the argument. Hell, you could make a case for him being first even. Offensive juggernaut, and his defense is somehow both underrated and overrated simultaneously - let’s break that down. He’s an ELITE off ball defender, maybe the best guard off ball in the league. His IQ, hand speed, and verticality make him great at getting steals, blocks, and helping other defenders. He’s a bit lacking as an on-ball defender, if only for his lack of lateral speed and his (relatively) smaller stature- but make no mistake, for his size the man is strong as hell. So, why does he get underrated? It’s hard to really determine value when you’re on a team of such incredibly elite defenders. He’s the Dhaulagiri to the Thunder’s Himalayan range (shoutout to all my mountaineer homies). Will he continue to climb, or has he reached his apex? Either way, he’s already proven his place in the upper echelon of players in this decade.

5. Anthony Davis

For all the media coverage and hubbub of Luka having a revenge tour this season, and how disrespected he was - I’ve heard people (flight mostly) call Davis a literal bag of potato chips in the context of that trade. The man is a perennial DPOY candidate, gives you 25 and 12 a night (and a lot very nice moral support when he’s in Street Clothes), and is incredibly underrated as a passer. He can shoot well, and he’s able to be both the primary or secondary option, completely unselfishly, whenever needed. He’s now got a golden opportunity on a Dallas team that will desperately need him to show up, to prove to the world that he’s more than just the punchline of a meme for Nico. Or, alternatively, get injured again and make me look like a fool.

6. Anthony Edwards

Wolves fans, I know what you’re thinking “Hell NawHe can’t do dis”. I hear you, Anthony “Special Ed” Edwards is not only one of the most athletic players in the league, he’s also turned himself into one of the best 3 point shooters in the league, in just a season. He’s one of the best scorers in the league, and is a very strong on-ball defender. However, and this will come as a shock to many of you, his IQ is lacking defensively, and offensively at times. He has a bad tendency to get lost off the ball on defense, or throw it away on offense - if he can learn when to pull out offensively, and where to shoot, as well as protect himself on the other end, he’ll help make Minnesota’s defense impregnable.

7. Stephen Curry

Now LeBron fans (myself included) will likely see this as a direct and personal insult to the King. Well, you’re wrong. Let’s look at the things Curry is still the greatest in the league at - Off ball offense (maneuvering screens, relocating, catch and shoot, gravity), shooting (lead the league in 3’s made per game, FT percentage), Engine (I don’t have numbers to back this up but look how much he runs in a game, this guy does marathons nightly). I know I said the gulf between Luka and Jokic vs everyone else offensively is massive but the next man up is Curry, and then it’s another chasm.

8. Joel Embiid (yes, really)

The Processed cheese, the Hospital Hooper, the Kneed for Speed - This man is by far the hardest to put on a ranking like this. All signs point to him having another disappointing, health addled, mobility scooter of a year. But, if he’s healthy? He has an argument for the best player in the league. So how does that even out to 8? Idk, ask the Ringer. I do think even if he’s at 75% he is good enough and multi-talented enough to be a top 10 guy in the league still, and there’s a naive little boy in me, who still believes in Santa and magic and Embiid’s knees being filled with enough corticosteroids to still be a top level player. Plus, it will be really sad if he sucks again.

9. Victor Wembanyama

Wemb-MD, The Thrombosis with the mostest, Slenderhomme - This guy is already the best defender in the league, and is only getting better. He’s already got an insane bag, his post game is constantly expanding, he can shoot the three (over basically any person ever), and he’s 21?? If you don’t think he’s a top 10 player yet because 107 year old Pop decides to only play him 30 minutes a night then you’re going to be in for a rude awakening this season. It’s very, very likely that he climbs higher up this list as the season progresses, and if you think he won’t be able to pull it off in the playoffs, I have to remind you - he’s still going to be the same height there.

10. Kevin Durant

LeBron Fans, I promise i haven’t forgotten your spiritual God. Yes, KD has been the butt of Matt Ishbia’s cruel, sick prank on Phoenix fans. Yes, he’s kind of a weirdo loser in desperate need of a PR team. BUT, he’s also still one of the greatest shooters in the league. He’s still a good defender, with enough length to defend the rim well, and enough speed to stay in front of most players. He’s not elite on that end of the floor, but he is elite on the other end. He’s also elite on twitter, or X (probably the only X KD has ever had). When the Rockets in the WCF, I’m sure everyone will say how he took the easiest path, and even though they’ll be right he’ll still be great.

11. Jalen Brunson

Jalen Brunson has drawn comparisons to Kobe Bryant. Which, if you think about it, makes sense. Both are great clutch players, tough midrange shooters, both have a reputation as chuckers that’s likely overstated (brunson shoot’s the 3 very well and is over .600 TS%), both are great defenders- wait, no that’s not right. But what Brunson lacks on defense, he.. well he doesn’t make up for it with his playmaking, but he’s pretty good at passing! For his size, he’s very good at driving to the bucket, and he has the clutch gene imprinted in his RNA.

12, Devin Booker

This is the one I know is gonna catch me some heat. I welcome it. If you’ve sold your stock on D-Book, then I’ll buy it up right now. He is born and bread to be the key to the offense, not share the floor with two other scorers. Yes, he’s had a disappointing last few seasons in the league. Yes, his shot quality dropped significantly last year, yes, his defensive game still has room for improvement. But even at his worst, he’s an elite, 3 level scorer. He’s also a sickeningly underrated playmaker and passer, and has just been handed back the key’s to the Ferrari. Will he go full Bueler? only time will tell.

13. Cade Cunningham

Pistons fans rejoice, you’ve found your messiah. Cade is another in a long line of tall, multitool combo guards. But he separates himself from the chaff with his elite playmaking, high level scoring, and offensive versatility, especially for someone so lacking in athleticism. He’s like a worse, eastern conference Luka - right down to the defense. The Pistons have a real chance to be a top 3 seed in the East this season and Cade has a big, empty stage to climb the ranks further, but it feels a little too early to put him any higher than this.

14. Donovan Mitchell

Donovan Mitchell may very well be the most underrated player (or at least, underrated amongst the top 30) in the entire league. What’s a guy gotta do to get a little respect? Donny Dangerfield decides to be a team player, to shoot a little less and get Mobley and Garland more involved - “he’s not even a 25 ppg guy”. one of the best passing shooting guards in the league? “The guy needs to distribute more” (a comment I would never make). Another post season of him averaging nearly 30 PPG? “Playoff choker smh”. No respect these days.

15. LeBron James

I know, I know. LeBron fans - now is your chance to tell me how I’m dumb and stupid and pee my pants and don’t know ball and am probably a Jordan stan and low IQ and a Luka fanboy and a hater. Ya got me. Dead to rights. But, LeBron James is still an elite, top level player in this league. 15th best in the NBA is not some notch against him, if anything it’s a testament to how unbelievable he is at his age. 27/7/7 may look a little more like 25/8/7 now, but the safest bet you can make in all of sports is that this guy is giving you at least 10 every single night. He also took on a massive defensive role for the Lakers in the absence of AD, essentially anchoring the defense for a Jaxson Hayes. I know people will have him higher than this, and I think on any given night he can be the best guy on the court- I just think most nights he won’t be, and that he’s more of a “the best second option in the league” guy now then a championship level first option. I know, the longevity argument is in shambles now - he’s only 15th at 41!

So, there you have it. My top 15 players in the league. Feel free to sing my praises, offer me gifts of money and jewels, or convince Bill Simmons to hire me (I know you’re reading this Zach Lowe).

I look forward to hearing any well thought-out, considered criticism you have for me, I’m sure every comment will be civil and respectful!
