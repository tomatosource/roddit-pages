PSA possible bug in PoB currently, Fractured Modifiers are disregarded upon auto-importing items.

Noticed this when PoB showed less total Spirit than what have in-game, using the latest stable release v0.8.0. It’s quite easy to miss if it only shows up in your DPS calculations.

I tested other items I have equipped by manually re-importing the pieces. Confirmed the Fractured affix was missing in PoB on all my items that should have one. (I tried w/ the option checked to “Delete Items First upon import”; and also with the option de-selected.)

Tested on Amulet w/ +50 Spirit.
Tested on Breach Ring w/ T3 +# to # Physical Damage to Attacks.
Tested on Gauntlets w/ T1 +# to # Lightning Damage to Attacks.

Manually importing w/ copy/paste works fine. I have submitted a bug report. https://github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding-PoE2/issues/1154
