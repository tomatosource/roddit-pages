Aaron Nesmith - Truly Underrated

Now I know we all overreact to a playoff game or series, but Aaron Nesmith, even after exploding for 8 threes (30 points) in game 1 verse the Knicks with 6 clutch 4yh quarter threes was huge!

But I’ve felt he’s been truly under the radar since last years Pacers playoff run. He does get credit for his defense, as he should, but as an overall “3 and D” big, strong 2-way wing i feel like he’s been a huge steal for the Pacers.

He was a lottery pick in 2020 to Boston (14th pick, last lottery pick lol) and spent 2 years there doing nothing! He played 2 years in college at Vanderbilt and in his sophomore season averaged 24.0 points a game on 51%FG, 52%FG3, and 83%FT! Add his 6’5⁄6’6 size and strength on defense and you have a lottery pick!

Indiana essentially got a lottery pick and expiring contracts for the aging Brogdon who has only declined since then. That’s a steal of a trade in itself.

He went from Gleaguer and Celtics bench guy (playing 12-13 minutes a game in about half of Bostons games his 2 years with them). He arrived in Indiana as a 23 year old nobody knew much about, earned the starting Small Forward position with his elite defense and solid 3pt shot, as well as accepting his role as the 4/5th option in the offense!

This past season, 2024-25, though he had a severe injury came back and played 45 games (averaging 25.0 minutes as he worked his way back from injury. No his numbers dont jump out at you, but in 25 minutes averaged:

12.0ppg 4.0rpg 1.2apg w/0.8spg ON 51%FG 43%FG3 91%FT

That’s what we call a “50/40/90 season”. No he didn’t ay that 66 games to be eligible, but when you’re that effective on offense and a plus defender that can guard 1-4 positions, you’d expect he’d be more of a commodity. Guys similar to him but established like Harrison Barnes, Dorian Finney Smith, Royce O’Neal, etc all make \(15-\)20 million a year, and then there’s OG Annunoby making a max (no im not saying Nesmith is on OGs level)… but he’s 25 years young!

In the 2025 Playoffs Nesmith has gotten more minutes and been even better than the regular season! In the 15 playoff games he’s averaged 29.5 minutes starting at SF and guarding the opponents best offensive guard/wing, and averaging;

14.3ppg 5.7rpg 1.2apg w/0.9spg 0.9bpg ON 50%FG 50%FG3 & 90%FT

He’s at 50/50/90 in the playoffs with a 127 offensive rating and a 113 defensive rating… that’s HUGE! His Box Plus/Minus is +1.9 on offense and +1.1 on defense, with a total +3.0 BPM!

After Haliburtons +5.4 BPM, Nesmith is 2nd best on the Pacers at +3.0, with Siakam at +2.7 and Turner +2.2! His defensive rating is also tied with Kyle’s Turner for the best on the team in the playoffs at 113!!

Now please understand. Im not saying he’s better or more valuable than Siakam or Turner, im just pointing out his impact on the team and games so far and suggests he plays his role to near perfection!

So it really surprises me to look at the Pacers current/future CAP Space and contracts, with Obi Toppin, a decent bench spark making \(15 mil a year (\)45 million 3 years left), Nembhard starts his 3 year \(59.5 million deal this upcoming season, and Nesmith has \)22 million over 2 years following this summer!! $11 mil a year!! Exactly the same that TJ Mcconnel will make!

That’s a steal! He could easily be making twice that like Nembhard, or at least as much/more than Toppin a very one dimensional 20 minute max bench guy!

No he’s not OG Annunoby, and yes he’s a product of playing with Haliburton at PG and a 5 guy lineup who can all stretch the floor! He does get open looks as a result, but shooting over 50%FG/45%FG3/90%FT this past season and playoffs while being the primary defender on Brunson or DMitchel, etc, he’s a steal at that contract!

I know it’s a half rant, and I know Siakam/Turner are “better” than him overall, the metrics do show he’s been the Pacers 2nd best player this playoffs! And on a contract less than the MLE id call that a bargain!!
