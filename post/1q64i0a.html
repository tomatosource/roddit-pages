It’s my first season that very I’m invested in crafting, I’m proud of my first “Big” craft.

Edit: added the steps.

I found a white two sockets Spiny Talisman.

Used a perfect transmutation and perfect augmentation. Hit +6 melee and and 166% phys.

Then, added a greater essence of abrasion for physical attack damage.

Activated a omen of sinistral necromancy(prefix) and used a preserved jawbone for a 4th hidden affix.

With a 4th affix, I could use a fracturing orb and hit 166% phys

Used a omen of sinistral crystallisation and used a perfect essence of abrasion. Crushed the hidden prefix for extra damage as physical.

Activated a omen of dextral necromancy(suffix) and a omen of liege. Used a preserved jawbone to desacrate a suffix with a guaranteed amanamu’s mod for attack speed.

For the last suffix, I used a perfect exalt orb and hit t1 reduced requirements.

Used a omen of dextral crystallisation with a perfect essence of haste. Crushed the t1 requirements for onslaught.

All in all, it’s a bit messy, but I was very very lucky with the RNG.
