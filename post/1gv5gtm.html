Norm Powell has been this good for a while.

Norm Powell has surprised some folks with his play this season. But rather than surprise, his start should be validation of just how good a shooter and scorer he really is.

Norm is shooting even better than his average right now but his finishing is below his average. Think we’ll see both revert to the mean a bit, but playing with Harden and in a featured scoring role with Kawhi down, we’re seeing Norm Powell really have his breakout at 31.

He has such fantastic balance as a shooter, whether that be on the move or in the spot-up and no matter the contest. Great balance, great touch, and repeatable stroke.

Norm is one of the best shooters in the league, maybe the best shooter that doesn’t get called one of the best shooters. Everyone knows about dudes like Kennard but Norm hasn’t always received that level of attention; he’s absolutely an elite shooter. He moves so well without the basket to find gaps in the defense for kick outs and he doesn’t need much space to fire. He’s dynamic and can shoot from all locations around the floor from 3 but is incredibly good at the corners and very strong at the elbow. Over 50% from the corners last season, over 50% again right now. And doing this all on 8.6 3 point attempts per-36. If you run him off the line, he has the dribble pull up and has the floater he’s really comfortable with in his arsenal.

This man is a professional scorer and shotmaker. He’s always had this in him. He’s just got opportunity now…both individually and the opportunity to play with a playmaker like James Harden. He has also notably matured as an all-around scorer and player and really grown into the leadership role he’s been thrust in. It’s not just a hot start. Norm Powell is a beast.
