I just beat the campaign/interlude on HCSSF having never played the game before

I beat all the acts+ interlude and even got into mapping only to die randomly to some Vaal Temple boss 😅

Anyways I play mostly HCSSF in PoE 1 & I had no clue how PoE 2 worked or what any of the bosses did so it was a learning experience the whole way through for me and I died multiple times and kept rerolling until I figured it out.

I did it using Shield Wall (shout out to Ben, I copied his whole set up) and it was a good time and I had a couple thoughts about the experience:

  1. Going from clicking to move to WASD movement in PoE is like the equivalent of going from a single monitor to a dual monitor set up for the first time, you never knew what you were missing out on until you actually did it and now you never want to go back.

  2. PoE 2 abilities and game play feels more intentional, idk how else to really describe it but every action and every attack feels like there’s weight to it and it could’ve just been because of the HC mode but I genuinely felt like every move meant something and I wasn’t just mindlessly blasting through each map.

  3. The campaign is too long, but it makes sense since the end game isn’t finished yet. They need to dramatically reduce the size of Act 3 and they also need to fix the ascendancy point challenges and reduce the size of those too, doing multiple floors of that sekhram trials was so mind numbingly boring lol.

There’s a lot more things I can get into, but overall I’d give my PoE 2 experience this league a solid 8⁄10.

This game has a lot of promise, and I hate that I was apart of the hate train for so long without actually trying it out because I think if they can fix end game and campaign a bit more this game might over take PoE 1 for me.
