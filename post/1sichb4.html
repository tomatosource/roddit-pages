Victor Wembanyama is set to become the 4th DPOY with the highest scoring average and second DPOY in history after Hakeem Olajuwon in 1993 to average 25ppg and not win MVP

With his 40 piece against Dallas last night, Wemby is now averaging exactly 25ppg, and he is set to become the 4th player to average 25ppg while winning DPOY.

The list include :

- Michael Jordan : 35ppg, won DPOY and MVP in 1988

- Giannis Antetokounmpo : 29.5ppg, won DPOY and MVP in 2020

- Hakeem Olajuwon : 27.3ppg, won DPOY and MVP in 1994

- Hakeem Olajuwon : 26.1ppg, won DPOY and finish second in MVP behind Charles Barkley in 1993

It’s worth mentioning that Hakeem and Jordan were averaging 40 minutes a game during those seasons, while Giannis and Wemby were averaging 30 minutes per game.

The next best scorer as a DPOY is David Robinson with 23.2ppg in 1992.
