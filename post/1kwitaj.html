Each try at pinnacle bosses takes hours of grind

I am probably late to the party, but I didn’t even bother with this season up untill last week.

Now, after already being over with this season, I saw Fubgun video on how endgame is bad in his opinion. And while I agree with what he said, for me the main problem is that each try at pinnacle bosses takes hours of grind. That, in my opinion, is the main thing that needs to be addressed.

I want to play more, I want to farm pinnacle bosses for the off chance of dropping exciting loot. But I need literally hours and hours of grind, just for one attempt. And, let’s be honest, we need several.

I understand that all games place grinding within their mechanics too keep players playing for longer. But as of now, it is so predatory, that they are achieving exactly the opposite.

My suggestion would be to significantly drop how long it takes for each try at pinnacle bosses:
Xesht: drop to 100 splinters.
Kosis: drop to 50 splinters.
King: 1 audience on average per 6 rituals (roughly 1 audience per 2 maps with ritual).
Olroth: 1 logbook drop per expedition and improve his appearance rate on 80+ maps.
Sekhemas: less trials per floor.
Chaos: 2 instead of 3 fragments.
Citadels: all 3 of them per “screen” on atlas tree.

And before someone says this is too kind, for each boss, you first have to take it to +4 (for relevant loot) then you have to farm it several times. Let’s say 10 times on average. If it takes 2 maps to farm a try, each map roughly 2 minutes, plus the boss another 2 minutes, then that’s an hour and a half for 15 runs. Since there are 7 pinnacle bosses, that’s more than 10 hours of screen play. And considering loot drop rates and randomness of rolls, I’m probably underestimating saying 10 runs would suffice.
