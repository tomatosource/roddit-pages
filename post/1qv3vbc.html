Heroic Tragedy is the best Divine sink

Until this league, I was always hesitant to actually use divine orbs. I would occasionally use it to improve my weapon but it only served as trading currency for me. But, man, this has changed!

Now, one of my daily entertainments is to re-roll these jewels with the excess divine from the temple. The combination of Oracle ascendency and this jewel is so exciting to explore for my build.

As an Oracle, you would want to take as many keystone notables as possible. But sometimes the downside is too big. With these jewels, i can simply transform the keystone to something entirely amazing. For example you can give up 300 hp from 150 str in exchange for %75 increased maximum energy shield!! Then you can simply pick multiple notables in the radius that give ridiculous bonuses. My favorites so far are:

- 40% increased maximum energy shield

- 40% increased melee damage

- 10% increased attack speed

My current roll gave me 6 of the notable nodes above!

There was also a roll with 5x%40 increase fire damage and 1 of the %40 increase elemental damage. But unfortunately i don’t have much fire in my wolf build.

A tip for re-rolling with divines: buy two uncorrupted jewels. Divine them until at least one of them is usable. Then you should only divine the other jewel and replace only if there is an improvement. This way, a bad roll doesn’t break your build and you can afford to continue sinking the divines until you get the best combination.
