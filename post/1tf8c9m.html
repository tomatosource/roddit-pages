The Washington Nationals have won a game to get to .500 after failing 15 consecutive attempts to do so, a streak lasting more than two years

The streak dates back to May 14, 2024, in the first game of a doubleheader against the White Sox. They then immediately lost the second game to fall back under .500.

(Source: Mark Zuckerman, Nats beat reporter)
