Basic Mace, the last thing bosses see

Kills map bosses even faster with just basic mace.
Here is an idea that I tested by upgrading the one mace kills everything build with thorns. You can see how it can easily eliminate these map bosses with nothing but a mace attack in the video. This build however is more expensive than the basic version.

Even the best maces have lousy criticals compared to Spears like Tangletongue. The damage stacking that is possible on Warrior require some amount of juggling with stats, skills and mana. The solution is to use basic Mace + Thorns. Thorns is automatic and has 25% critical base on BriarPatch, we just need to move it’s damage around.

But how do you build successful thorns on Warbringer without a spear? As this build demonstrates it can work with some investment, particularly on a Stellar amulet to maintain the ability to use Giant blood and creatively re-purpose Barbs and Quill burst support gems.

Warbringer is the most powerful character with Shield, make use of it. Remember every block chance converts to damage in Offensive Stance and Lay Siege. Drop that oversized Titan WWE and Blacksmith barista class now!

First some house rules:
Spears are for the Amazonian ladies, stick to your maces.
Single mace sucks. This is weak, never use them.
2H mace on Warbringer is hard on stats, get the Hammer of faith and a Stellar amulet for attribs.

Wind Dancer + Quill Burst + Ingenuity + Armour Explosion = Treat Wind dancer as your 2nd attack
Basic Mace Attack + Barbs + Exploit Weakness = Synergizes with Wind Dancer
Block with Svalinn to above 84%
Scavenged Plating = free armour + thorns stacking (wearing a low armour 23% death mantle body armour can easily reach 40% in seconds). Those thorn hits break armour too.

There is no need for trampletoe boot nor herald spirit skill.

Hammer of Faith is the most powerful mace in the current league. Even if you have a mace with 1000 flat-phy, choose Hammer of faith instead. The godly powers it randomly grant the user far exceeds any extra damage other maces bring to the table.
