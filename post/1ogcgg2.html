Big wand value

So, after having made some good sales from a bow and quiver (approx 500d) a week or so ago, i had decided to make a wand with an exceptional 2 socket dueling wand base i82, made this beauty.

Steps:
Buy base
Perf trans/aug, see if it hits t1 extra lightning or +5 lightning spell skills - miss
Make base rare, annul to 1 mod, chaos spam for T1 extra lightning or +5 (hit +5 within 30c)
Add two mods, fracture the +5 to ensure hitting desired prefixes/suffs
Perf Ex, Greater Exaltation + perf Homog spam for T1 cast and crit damage, annul when t2 hits or crit chance hits (sometimes double annul bc it hits the t1 &gt;&lt;)
Perf ex, homog + greater exaltation prefix for +extra ele damage, get a mana mod and accidentally annul without sin annul omen, repeat previous step for t1 cd/cs again.
Regain cd/cs finally, go again for double t1 +extra ele% damage, hit.
Jawbone reroll spam with omens of light until hitting the last t1 ele%
Divine spam until near perfect rolls (just couldnt hit the last 1%!)

Finally decided to yolo vaal a week later, hit the third socket
