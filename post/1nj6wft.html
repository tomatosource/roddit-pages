Atlas screen rework

There are 3 main problems with the atlas screen as I see it.

  1. its infinite and after a few days gets very hard to manage logistically.
  2. its all very homogenous and the cool little bits broken up by a unique map or something are very small part of it.
  3. managing and min maxing tower for loot isnt very fun.

My idea: towers are no longer random encounters on the atlas screen, but now are doorways to a new “zone” tablets dont just buff an aoe around a tower they now influence the random generation of the zone.

for example:
you might roll a tablet that generates a “Lush” area with lots of jungle themed maps and make unique maps of that type more likely to spawn, as well as being able to have fun mods like:
“fire modifiers on your waystones used in area have 100% increased effect”
or “extra monster packs on your waystones are converted to Beasts”

This would let GGG curate the generation of the Atlas to be more fun as you could have guaranteed features in each zone naturally generate to have a fun beginning, middle, ending type of thing going on, and have a fun pacing of rolling enough waystones to synergize with the zone after rolling the initial tablet and seeing what you got. it would make a gaming session very fun and you would have clear goals.

side comment: the current view of the atlas screen is so zoomed out it doesn’t feel like you are IN an area, I get that its a map, but I think a more zoomed in view that made each screen look different rather than a big homogenous view of everything would be cool. so rather than 2 maps in the mountainous zone you get like 6-7. and traveling across the mountains is more of a vibe.

TLDR:
make the atlas reveal in chunks
Towers are doors to the next chunk
Revealing a chunk is like opening a loot box of maps and cool stuff.
tablets influence the generation of the next chunk
