The jokes about PG ruining the vibes are funny, but this team desperately needs a wing that can shoot and defend. Kelly Oubre won’t keep shooting 44% on 3PA, and the Sixers other rotation wings (Dominic Barlow, Jabari Walker, and Trendon Watford) are ignored beyond the three-point line. The Sixers are being forced to play three guard lineups massive minutes just to have functional NBA players out there. These small lineups and their lack of rim protection have resulted in the Sixers being 25th in DEF RTG so far. They’ll likely have to continue playing small even after PG and Barlow return because four of the Sixers’ seven best players are guards, but McCain’s return will also be huge in allowing VJ and Maxey to have a more sustainable workload.
