Future of the Roster - Path to banner 19

This season is almost certainly a retooling year, so the team is at a bit of a major crossroad for the first time in awhile. There’s a few paths/philosphies of how to move forward past this season. This list kind of goes through the least to most drastic changes.

Option A:

Keep the roster together. Derrick White, Jaylen Brown, and Jayson Tatum are all here for good. PP, Hauser, Simons also all stay. Explore some fringe trades/signings to address the big spot and fill in any holes in the roster. Bank on some of the younger guys stepping up.

Option B:

Keep the trio together. White, Brown, Tatum stay. Everyone else is on the market to be moved, and a drastic overhaul of the roster takes place. Ideally this would land us a solid center, and some better depth.

Option C:

Break up the core (short term). One/both of White or Brown gets moved. This could be for an upgrade at that spot, or maybe to land multiple good players.

Option D:

Break up the core (long term). One/both of White or Brown gets moved. This time it’d be for a bunch of picks and some promising young talent. This would eyeing a completely new chapter of the Tatum era.

Which option would you pick?
