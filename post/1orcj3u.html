On July 3, 1983, the Rangers defeated the A’s 16-4 (F/15) to set the record for the most lopsided extra innings win in MLB history.

The Rangers led 4-2 heading to the bottom of the 9th, but Oakland scored 2 in the frame to send the game to extras.

The score remained deadlocked at 4 until the 15th inning, when the Rangers poured on 12 runs to put the game well out of reach. Oakland went three-and-out with two strikeouts and a flyout to seal the Texas win.
