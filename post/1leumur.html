Please, don’t let PoE2 boss fights become like PoE1.

I’m not a hater of PoE1, in fact I bought the last Kirac track and I’m playing through. I think is the best ARPG of the market and will remain like that at least until PoE2 gains some traction.

But today I’m advancing more than ever before in the early endgame, with the most meta build of the league, and the difference between both games is absurd.

In PoE2 every boss fight was a challenge. Arguably hard, but still feels good to beat them.

In PoE1 I’m spamming 2 skills from afar, I don’t even see the boss, sometimes I don’t even see the HP bar of the objective, I just know it’s dying somewhere. This is T13-T15 maps with all sorts of buffs, Maven encounters, etc.

I know there are a lot of critics regarding PoE2’s direction, but I also know most people here don’t want PoE1 with better graphics.
