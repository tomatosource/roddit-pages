The Ringer Made NBA Finals Predictions. None Survived the First Round.

Two weeks ago, on April 16, the Ringer published article about the NBA Playoffs.

https://www.theringer.com/2026/04/16/nba/nba-playoffs-bracket-2026-preview-predictions-series-matchups

The article contains numerous predictions, one prediction that I would like to focus on is NBA Finals predictions. Here are those predictions:

Beck: Nuggets over Celtics

Pina: Nuggets over Celtics in 7

Parker: Thunder over Celtics in 7

Dollinger: Celtics over Thunder

Levy-Rubinett: Nuggets over Pistons

Chau: Nuggets over Celtics

Other three main personalities of the independent news organization, the Ringer, namely Bill Simmons, Zach Lowe and Joe House stated their predictions in podcasts:

Joe House: Nuggets vs. Knicks

Bill Simmons:

(1) Preseason prediction: Denver over Cleveland

He does not have a certain pick during the season.

(2) One clear statement on a recent podcast: “Denver to make the Finals, Denver to play the Celtics, Denver to play the Knicks”

(3) He also mentions Celtics multiple times as a finals candidate in various recent episodes. (not surprisingly)

Zach Lowe:

(1) In October: OKC vs. Knicks

(then he changed his prediction)

(2) Before the playoffs: OKC vs. Celtics
