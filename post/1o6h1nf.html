Potential rarity nerf suggestion

First, let’s bring in to context what rarity does or it’s potential purpose outside of it’s obvious loot-based benefits.

it’s meant to take an affix slot that has no tangible benefit to defense, offense, recovery.

it’s meant to affect the equipment in a similar way that light radius and movement speed work to “benefit” in some fashion that while it isn’t meant to directly help in a fight, it helps in either the before/after of a fight or during in the unique case of movement speed. Light radius is meant to help in the detection of enemies, rarity is meant to increase the quality of the loot dropped and movement speed.. well if you get out of the way you don’t get hit. Movement speed is the closest to a defensive affix in this type.

With that said, I feel the rarity issue has a pretty simple nerf format it can follow, namely removal of double affixes on all equipment on top of a severe value cut in all aspects.

Examples:

remove Rarity Suffixes from Armour. Make Rarity compete with the holy trinity of Flat/%/Hybrid and in certain cases movement speed. Make it compete at a third of its value at maximum, 8%. That’s 32%, potentially up to 9-10 if max corrupted/sanctified if memory serves.

remove Rarity from the Prefix slot on Jewelry. Make it compete with skill levels, cast speed, resistance, and other suffix stats at 10% maximum. 12% sanctified/corrupted. Reduce Gold rings to 3-5% and Gold necklace 6-12%. This puts it at around 66ish% from jewelry, 78% for ritualist and probably closer to 86-90% with ingenuity.

What this does is open up the suffix slots for the alternative deflection/armour as elemental/resistances/less durations that are potentially lost to fit the suffix easier in armours while making Rarity have to compete with the holy trinity at less than half its current value, making it much less attractive as a whole. You’re gaining far less from your armour.

This means you’re able to get more defense or damage from your jewelry while also making rarity compete at less than half its current value and easier for players in general on pretty much any build to get fairly equivalent levels of it with obvious points to minmax that don’t have such wild amounts that it can fluctuate between.

There is also an obvious cost to the min max via armour that if the build can or so chooses, they can benefit. But it requires significant investment and cutting defensive power to hit over 100 without sockets used or other forms of rarity existing.

I believe rarity should exist, and it’s function should be viewed as similar to Challenge rules such as the Sekhama Unique relics. Where I differ is that the level and FoMo levels that it exists should be drastically cut down and redefined to the point where you need to think about adding it onto gear rather than the obvious conclusion.

I actually think I’m being a bit kind to rarity with my numbers, since half the problem is Human Avarice. We want Tinks, we see people getting more tinks, we want to level the “tink” playing field so we say “remove X so he gets less tinks” and throw all of this on a stat.. when it’s not the stat making the decision. It’s us.

I don’t think it should be removed without actual testing and nerfing from GGG, and view anyone saying to remove it as “GGG save me from myself” personally.

Now with all the words said, what do you all think? I’m guessing a number will still say remove it but I love discussions of this type regardless.
