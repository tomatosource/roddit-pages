Anyone else pleasantly surprised with Torey Craig?

I’m not gonna lie I haven’t seen a ton of Craig before he signed in Boston so I, like I’m sure a lot of people, kinda just considered him to be a sorta washed 3&d bench player

He actually plays with a lot of composure, he’s not doing anything crazy or moving super fast but he’s basically making the right read every time on offense whether it be a quick pass or shot, seems to be a fast processor which is something I certainly did not expect

On defense he’s been completely serviceable so far, like I said he’s not moving super fast or anything but he’s supplementing that with pretty solid iq and making the most of his pivoting on drives/defending around screens
