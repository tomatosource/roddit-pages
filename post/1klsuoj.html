Horking Shout - Warcry “Make It Rain” Support Gem

I wanted to share a fun idea for a Warcry Support Gem that drops additional gold piles upon enemy death. The more enemies within the vicinity, the more gold piles that are dropped by detonating corpses. I would love to get in the middle of a monster pack that showers you with Gold when enemies die. This will be the “Make It Rain” Support Gem. Thoughts?

Horking Shout

  • Support Gem
  • Warcry, Duration
  • Supports Warcry skills, causing them to increase quantity of Gold piles dropped by slain enemies depending on counted monster power within range. Lasts for 3 seconds.
