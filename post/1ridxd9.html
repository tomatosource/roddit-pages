Jalen Brunson is on pace to be the first Knick ever to average 25+ PPG in 3 full consecutive seasons on the roster

Interesting stat shared on the broadcast during the game today. Jalen Brunson is on pace to be the first Knick to ever average 25+ PPG in 3 consecutive seasons. Very surprising stat given that there are some all time scorers that have put on a Knick jersey (Ewing, Anthony, King, McAdoo). But also an indication that many of them were Knicks for such a brief period of time. Brunson continues to cement himself as one of the all time great Knicks.

Jalen Brunson last 3 seasons:

  • 2023-24 - 28.7 PPG

  • 2024-25 - 26.0 PPG

  • 2025-26 - 26.7 PPG

Bob McAdoo also technically pulled the same feat, but he did not start his first season or end his last season with the Knicks.

Source: ESPN broadcast
