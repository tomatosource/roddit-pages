Sweet baby jesus Grand Expeditions are now insane

Jumped on at lunch, allocated my mechanic points to the new area somewhat randomly, and did a gran expedition I had ready with three irradiated tablets.

Exchanged all my Verisium for Liquid before going.

This is the hardest content now by a mile. Like, take 200% delirium and multiply it by five. Rerolling runes is good as you tend to get better rewards on the ones you do use liquid on, but my god, the modifiers on them are bat shit insane now.

I have a strong build Grim Pillars, not game breaking, but everything so far has been easy, including Bodach and Arbiter, over in seconds.

It took me some 15 minutes just to clear three Runes at one point.

Mad loot explosions but, be careful out there exiles. This is some mad sauce.

Someone feel free to tell me which bad modifiers I got. It was just a quick one at lunch but I felt obliged to warn people …
