I should not still be playing, yet i am.

Calling myself a casual would be a stretch of the definition, but thats because i play a lot league start then quit.

I never play past the first 1-1.5 weeks, i have my fun when the leagues launch and then move on, normally i put in 80-100 hours a league, playing SSF to extend my playtime, because by that point even in SSF i have everything done, my builds reasonably min maxed with everything being one shot and breezing through the game, and all that is left to do is just chase upgrades that wont really affect my gameplay and repeat the same content over and over again.

This goes for both poe 1 and poe 2, i hit that point of having done everything and just leave.

But like, theres something about the current state of poe 2 endgame, that is just so addicting, and i think its specifically chasing the boss drops and overall rare unique drops.

Spending days farming out a taming felt so good, every day i got 1 of the rings after a 14 hour session and that was enough to satisfy me to keep going, so i kept going.

Every single day im dropping and crafting items that can be potential upgrades, because even if its effectively just the same item, vaal orbs are just so fun in poe 2 and hitting an extra socket makes it an upgrade.

And that just feels good, i don’t know, having these attainable goals with clear paths just feels so good, im half farming atziri and expedition right now, and knowing that give it a couple days and im going to have an atziris communion and olroths flask for my new build is so exciting, hell i could also drop the depleted rune and go for the 10k mana goal, thats exciting.

im 180 hours in, ive done every boss multiple times, ive finished all the quests, i have 3 giga endgame characters done, but im still playing, there hasnt been a single league in poe 1 history where i have played this much, something about the current endgame loop poe 2 has just feels so much more addicting and better to me, and it cant just be a honeymoon thing because ive went through plenty of complete endgame changes in poe 1 that have been amazing, yet none of them have felt as addicting as this current poe 2 league.

I think they absolutely cooked here, the only thing i wish was better was balance, theres too many builds that just one shot bosses with shit gear and then too many that struggle just clearing maps let alone bosses with good gear, the bosses are really cool but it sucks that either you one shot them and dont get to enjoy the fights or you self torture yourself with a horrible build on purpose, ubers wouldnt solve this problem because then the bad builds would be even more fucked, imo they need to bring the top down and the bottom up to a new middle, i dont think they should just bring the bottom up to the top and add ubers.

But they did say balance is going to be their major focus now for the next 6 months till 1.0 launch, so im sure they can nail that, i dont mind people one shotting bosses, i just feel like that should be something you do with mirror tier gear, and then a strong build with strong gear should still at least have like a 40-60 second fight, im talking about the pinnacles btw i dont care if a map boss gets one shot by those either.

Eitherway, 10⁄10 league for me.

Most fun ive had in path of exile in the last 10 years, and im still having fun.
