Josh Giddey had the 12th game in NBA history in which a player had a triple-double and hit the game-winner at the buzzer

The last such game was Luka Dončić in the bubble playoffs. The most recent in the regular season was Russell Westbrook in Feb. 2018.

Most Points in Game With Triple-Double and GW Buzzer-Beater:

  1. Russell Westbrook — 50 (Thunder vs. Nuggets on April 9, 2017)
  2. Luka Dončić — 43 (Mavs vs. Clippers on August 23, 2020)
  3. LeBron James — 37 (Cavs vs. T-Wolves on February 7, 2018)
  4. Magic Johnson — 34 (Lakers vs. Suns on March 26, 1989)
  5. Hakeem Olajuwon — 31 (Rockets vs. Mavs on April 13, 1996)
  6. LeBron James — 30 (Heat vs. Pacers on May 22, 2013)
  7. Josh Giddey — 25 (Thursday night)
  8. Elgin Baylor — 23 (Lakers vs. Hawks on March 25, 1964)

Giddey is also the first player in NBA history to score 25+ points and hit a buzzer-beater from beyond half court — the only controversy here would be where Devin Harris actually released his crazy shot from…officially 43 feet (Video)
