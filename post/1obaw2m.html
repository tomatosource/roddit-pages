The Blue Jay batters in the ALCS have the lowest K% of a team in a series in the last 10 years

Through 6 games, the Blue Jays batters have struck out in 14.5% of their PAs (34⁄235), which is the lowest percentage in any multi-game series in the last 10 years. This edges out themselves in the ALDS, at 14.9%, and this also edges out the 2016 Blue Jays, at 14.8%. For reference, the average strikeout percentage in the 2025 postseason for all other teams is 25.9%.

The Jays’ BABIP so far in the ALCS is .273, which is on par with the overall MLB average BABIP in the postseason of .277, so they also don’t seem to be getting penalized by putting the ball in play (even when it is fielded properly).

We are getting to witness a brand of baseball that has been mostly extinct for the last decade.
