This “xyz team has no chance to beat OKC or San Antonio” argument is so dumb: we just watched it happen. Teams should try and go for it.

On the latest Zach Lowe pod he was discussing the Lakers’ offseason moves and basically called it a lateral move, and said they now have no flexibility and aren’t good enough to beat OKC or the Spurs. The implication here being “why did they do this.”

Last year, nobody thought the Knicks would win. There are definitely people who said they could make the finals, but no single person outside of NY was like “this team can beat OKC.” And, the Knicks traded ALL their picks and flexibility for their roster. Mike Brown at best was considered a lateral hire from Thibs, and many probably thought he was a worse coach.

Guess what, that team just won the title.

I think these media people just saying shit like “so and so team can’t beat OKC or Spurs so they should just pack it in,” are so stupid. Media members also talk about how good the talent level is in the league. The fact that we’ve had 8 champions in 8 years proves this point. I honestly think that there’s at least a half dozen teams that can win the title in any given year, and possibly more. The PACERS were 1 win away from beating OKC last year, and nobody even thought they were a contender in the East, let alone a championship contender. And yet, they may have won the title if Haliburton didn’t get hurt. That was a team that had 1 star and a bunch of role players.

So why can’t Philly who now has 2.5 stars (Embiid is the .5) and a rising star, and a bunch of role players not win. Why can’t the Lakers get hot for 4 weeks in the West and make the finals. The players are all pretty good now, it isn’t like the 80s/90s when the bench/role guys on most teams were mostly shit.

I just really hate this discourse of XYZ team “can’t beat OKC or San Antonio.” Maybe it’s not likely, but we’ve seen repeatedly in the playoffs that unexpected teams are winning it, and the behemoths have been taken down or challenged every single year.

So, I applaud teams who go for it. I may not love the Lakers moves, but fuck it, they’re assembling an OK roster, Luka is a top 5 guy, why not. Philly: why not. Detroit, why not. Etc.
