Watch out! Your persistent buffs might be doing nothing

I found out that there maybe a bug adding a hard cap on how many persistent buff you can have at the same time. My character is a Gemling Legion with Essence of Virture. I have equipped 10 presistent buffs listed as following:

Impurity (Palm of the Dreamer)

Enteral Rage (Absent Amulet)

Virtuous Barrier (ascendancy)

Mana Remnants

Arctic Armour

Trinity (Atziri’s Communion)

Wind Dancer

Charge Regulation

Cast on Elemental Ailment

Berserk

For the 7 of the spirit gem I equipped (excluding the one from gear and ascendancy), they all shown as active in the UI. However, only 4 of them works. Depending on the order of the spirit gem, the last three spirit gem has no effect at all (still reversing spirit, not showing in buff bar).

Just a heads up you might be running around the map with some of your persistent buff not working at all. Did someone notice a similar issue?
