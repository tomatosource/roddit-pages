I’m heartbroken too, but I’d like to highlight the 2028 swap-best clippers pick in a vacuum

Just read this article by Luke Adams (https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2026/07/details-on-2028-pick-involved-in-celtics-sixers-trade) and it explains the 2028 swap-best clippers pick involved in the JB trade. Yes, BOS could have gotten more value overall but this pick is essentially the best 2028 first round pick of LAC or PHI. Clippers are currently the 5th lowest championship odds and I do not think it is a stretch to say that it isn’t going to change much after 2027.

It segues into the FO’s (and subsequently, fans’) discussions about how the CBA is really stupid and the 2024 Celtics were the NBA’s guinea pig team to study it on (Thanks CJ McCollum). The name of the game is bargain contract guys, rookie contract guys, and rookie extension guys playing above their pay grade alongside one supermax player and some other good role players/MLE guys. You can be mad at the overall putrid trade return and also understand how the CBA is the primary culprit at the same time (not mutually exclusive). I have faith in this front office’s ability to draft well using at worst a mid FRP and realistically a top 5 pick to build around JT.

Smaller idea: what if the clippers want their pick back and we can leverage that to get some talent from LAC for the 2028 pick and very little in addition to it?
