What makes profitable crafts profitable?

Hey there, Exiles! I want to get a little bit technical into this topic.

What in your experience are the things that make a craft profitable?

Here’s what I was thinking - in theory, each and every single one of us can make calculations and figure out what is the chance to get specific mods of the specific tiers that we desire when crafting an item. In turn, we can calculate what is going to be the cost ON AVERAGE (the variability/rng part will do its thing, but the closest that we can get to the realistic potential returns is to calculate the averages) when crafting that said item.

So, we can take the cost of the base item on which we’re crafting, add the costs of the crafts on average and maybe a small margin for profit and try to sell it for that.

But in my experience, that’s not what I have found to be the case (not all the times I mean). Sometimes, I have found crafts that seems to net twice the return of the investment or so. And in my experience, I have no idea why because if anyone does the calculations, they should be able to see that the item is overpriced and they’d be better of crafting one for themselves.

What do you think are the factors that makes something more or less profitable? When have you found things to be profitable and what’s your takeaway from it?
