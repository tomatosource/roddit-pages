Saw a video on youtube behind a membership paywall for a “craft”. I really dislike that this sort of general information is being kept behind a paywall, so I’m posting it here for anyone who finds it useful.

Steps:

  1. Buy fireflower.
  2. Apply Xoph’s Catalysts until the item reaches 20% fire quality.
  3. Use an Omen of Corruption, then corrupt the item and hope for the +1.

Why this works:

  • The catalyst increases fire modifiers by 20%.
  • That pushes the potential +5 to Fire Skills corruption up to +6 (5 × 1.2 from catalyst = 6).
