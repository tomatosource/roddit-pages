Major QOL from POE1 missing that I never see people talking about

In POE1 campaign, whenever you start getting close to a point of interest (aka exit to the next zone or a waypoint), it appears on the edge of the minimap, letting you know that you are in fact going the right way, guiding you, reducing backtracking substantially and speeding up your campaign.

POE2-only Andies might not know this but the range at which this can happen is sometimes multiple screens away.

It’s kind of funny to me that I’ve literally never seen this mentioned by anyone and yet it is arguably one of the most impactful mechanics that affects campaign completion time.
