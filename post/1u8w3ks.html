[Fischer] The three-team trade concept making the social media rounds in which the Clippers join a three-team Giannis construction with the Bucks and Celtics and take on Brown while surrendering the No. 5 pick is “not a real conversation.” Clippers not operating like team willing trade No. 5 pick.

Source: https://open.substack.com/pub/marcstein/p/the-nbas-latest-and-greatest-draft?r=nuq3a&utm_medium=ios

As I discussed on my weekly Bleacher Report video stream Tuesday, I’ve been told emphatically that a three-team trade concept making the social media rounds in which the Clippers join a three-team Giannis construction with the Bucks and Celtics and take on Brown while surrendering the No. 5 overall pick is not a real conversation.

The Clippers, to this point, certainly aren’t moving like a team that has interest in surrendering No. 5 for a veteran. They have been deep in draft workouts with almost every prospect that is regarded to be part of the tier behind the foursome almost universally to go 1-2-3-4: AJ Dybantsa, Darryn Peterson, Cam Boozer and Caleb Wilson.
