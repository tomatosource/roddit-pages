Honest question: why does GGG have it out for frenzy charges?

I love huntress and spear gameplay. I actually like combo gameplay. I like moving in and out of melee range, I like doing backflips. I even really, really like parry (see my post history). But I honestly don’t understand what GGG is doing with frenzy charge generation.

I watched a monk showcase yesterday and it blew me away. It’s a night and day difference. You can essentially have infinite power charges in endgame and you can just spam skills that require charges. There’s also so many different ways to generate power charges, the vast majority (if not all) of which don’t significantly impact or alter your character’s gearing or gameplay. There’s also easy access to charge generation during boss fights. And GGG actually added even more power charge generation tech for 0.5 with the martial artist ascendancy, with chance to gain charges on gloves and bell culling.

Meanwhile, for frenzy charges, your options are:

  1. Be Deadeye

  2. Be Ritualist AND sacrifice your helmet, body armor and belt (lol)

  3. Parry

  4. Get 1 charge every 6 seconds via combat frenzy. Nerfed two leagues ago. Doesn’t help with bosses

  5. Get 1 charge per culling an enemy. Doesn’t help with bosses

  6. Take resonance, hamstring your primary skill by slotting in endurance charge tech. Doesn’t help with bosses (and potentially hurts it because parry no longer generates frenzy charges)

At this point, I just don’t understand the decision making here. You can’t even argue that it’s to limit spear power, because the damage monks are getting with skills like falling thunder and flicker strike is legitimately crazy. 0.5 is probably going to be my first league start not as a huntress, because MA (and monk in general) just looks so damn fun. Feels bad for spears man.

Anyways, /rant
