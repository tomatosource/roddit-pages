The 0-6 Pelicans Do Not Own Their 1st Round Pick

Sitting here bored at work thinking about how the worst team in the NBA does not currently own their 1st round pick.

It’s not like they dealt it in the past for a star in hopes of contending or are even remotely competitive. They’re the worst team in the league and willingly gave up their 1st round pick this year to Atlanta (in a loaded draft class) just to move up a few spots in last year’s draft.

I hear people in the media discuss this from time-to-time, but I don’t think it’s a big enough story. I’ve been a huge fan of sports for the last decade, and it’s genuinely hard for me to think of another example of such absurd incompetence. The Luka trade, Mookie trade, is there anything else?
