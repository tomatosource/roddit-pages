‘Get a better deal’ | Former Mavs majority owner Mark Cuban breaks silence on Luka Doncic trade

Link to article: https://www.wfaa.com/article/sports/nba/mavericks/mark-cuban-breaks-silence-luka-doncic-trade-dallas-mavericks-full-interview/287-23216eb6-d128-443d-9185-1694a1cd5cc1

Key quotes:

It all started when WFAA asked Mark Cuban who his son’s favorite Mav was. “It was Luka Doncic,” Cuban said. “But now it’s Max Christie.” And how did his son react to his favorite player being dealt to the Lakers? “About like I did,” Cuban said with a laugh.

“If the Mavs are going to trade Luka, that’s one thing,” Cuban said. “Just get a better deal. I still firmly believe if we had gotten four unprotected number ones and Anthony Davis and Max Christie, this would be a different conversation.”
