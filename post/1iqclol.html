The most ideal POE2 game has viable block/parry, armor, melee, actual weapon swapping, and uniques with stats scale to end game.

I know they are working on acts 4-6, and a better end game, and more classes, but what’s mentioned in the title would absolute change the entire game for everybody in a good way.

  • Right now, blocking isn’t viable as it’s time not spent doing damage, adding a parry option that maybe contributes to stun meter/break armor/exposure or other ailments would be awesome and thematic, and having enemy have attacks worth blocking would go a long way instead of just rolling through everything and hoping for the best or just getting 30k energy shield on everybody.

  • Melee is slow. Reward them with tankiness, big hits , tanking big hits with armor, and tanking for the team.

  • Running a mercenary swapping between things like a crossbow or dagger depending on the situation would be sick. This game has extreme potential to set itself apart instead of running two wands or two crossbow. Make them situational, viable.

  • 99% of uniques in the game are useless and the 1z are tired to certain endgame bosses. I don’t wanna hear jabber about “leveling” uniques when you spend 15 minutes at that level tops and HUNDREDS of hours in the end game. It’s a huge waste of creativity, fun, resources, cosmetics, and mechanics that could be the root of people discovering really fun mechanics and mixups.
