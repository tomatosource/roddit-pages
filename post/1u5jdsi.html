Some “hidden” mechanics are not fun (Abyss)

I just found out that people farm abyss in a specific way and that some items only drop if you do it that way. Normally i am not watching videos of how to farm things because i am not trying to be extremly efficent and i dont mind to make slow progress. But now i found out that some abyss items for example only drop if you kill monsters in a specific way.

The amunamu void monsters drop items depending on how to kill them as far as i understood. For players like me that dont watch guide videos or optimize farming videos this is kinda shit. The game never says “oh try to kite them” or hits me that killing enemies in a specific way also changes their loot. I always prefered and loved to stand on the abyss whole and spam my spells there and see how they die before they crawl out.

Now i am thinking i played abyss wrong the whole time and that i am missing out on items i could have gotten. It kinda makes abyss no fun for me anymore.

I know the people here on reddit are more hardcore players, up to news, more in the scene and generally more invested. But think about all the players that dont read every patch note and dont watch every video about the game. This is not a good design. Sure maybe they dont need omen of light and who cares if they drop it. But i dont think this is a good approach.

I now wonder how many more endgame “strats” i am not knowing and doing wrong by just simply clearing mobs as fast as i can.

Edit: some extra thoughts. This is a difficult topic for me, in general I think secrets and things to discover are a very cool thing. Maybe this felt so bad for me, because it made me feel like I am clearing wrong and never even thought about a specific way to do it. I think secrets in general are cool, like the annoints in the passive tree that are hidden, or the armor you can craft out of rings (idk if this is a secret). Maybe currency items just feel a bit out of place. I am not exactly sure how to feel about this whole thing.
