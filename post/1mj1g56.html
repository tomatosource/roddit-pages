What do people think the idea of a sprint button?

Just like how the dogderoll has been added as a fundamental mechanic into poe2, I feel like more general action game mechanics can be added.

Why? Just moving your character around should feel fun, atm its extremly one dimensional. Movement should be dynamic and fun. Right now i cant even think about playing poe 2 without a dogderoll it added such another dimension to the game.

So how would one implement it without ruining the game? There are many ways and how its done can be many different ways. But my suggestion would be somthing along the lines of you press down the sprint button and you character unsheats their wepons then starts the sprint animation, its a stacking buff which starts with 1% MS and goes up to 15% max after some time. Unsheating has the normal wepon swap time, when you try to attack while sprinting your character stops sprinting and goes back to normal and then swaps back to their wepons then attacks.

Note that i dont necessary just want the sprint to be somthing that makes us play the game faster, but rather add another dimension to just moving around. Moving should be fun and dynamic, there are whole games which are just based on running.
