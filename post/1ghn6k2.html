After tonight’s loss to the Lakers, Gradey Dick leads the 2023 draft class in PPG with 20.8 PPG on 61.1 TS% throughout his first 6 games

After one of the more disappointing starts to a rookie season of a lottery pick in recent memory, Gradey Dick has been having a shocking step up in his sophomore season. While part of his recent scoring uptick has been the benefit of injuries to the Raptors #1 and #2 in Scottie Barnes and Immanuel Quickley, the ways in which Gradey has been able to score outside of corner 3s has been a paradigm shift, and all the more impressive that he has been able to do so without Scottie or Quickley drawing the defense’s attention due to their respective absences.

In a “too early to draw serious conclusions” sample size of 6 games, Gradey Dick is currently averaging 20.8 points, 2.3 rebounds, 1.8 assists (1.2 turnovers) on 48.9⁄37.2⁄89.5 splits, lending itself to a 61.1 true shooting percentage. His 37% 3-point shooting is on roughly 7 3s a game. For some context, Gradey’s rookie season saw him boast a true shooting % of 54.6.

Among sophomores, Chet Holmgrem leads at 22.5 PPG but he was a part of the 2022 draft class; he missed his 1st year due to an offseason injury.
