Which less heralded/younger Celtic are you the most interested in tracking this season?

Wanna know which young/unheralded guy(s) you all have on your radars in the season ahead? Summer League is behind us and each of the young C’s showed some interesting flashes. I’m a big fan of Hugo Gonzalez but know he’s raw offensively in the half court. Jordan Walsh has come along with his handle and playing with more controlled aggression. He’s still a bit of a firecracker, but he shot it well in Vegas.

Josh Minott and Luka Garza both come from the Wolves to Boston. They’re very different players. Minott is a fantastic athlete and has some really interesting passing ability. Garza hasn’t shot it great at the NBA level yet but has done so in G-League and is a skilled offensive player.

JD Davison has pretty much answered all questions at the G-League level. It’s now or never for the 22 year old with the big league club. No better season to see whether or not he’s someone who can be a contributor. He’s certainly improved his feel for the game substantially since he entered the league.

Summer League only means so much but this season the Celtics have quite a lot of unproven guys who are likely to get minutes. Who are the ones you’re most interested in and keeping tabs on throughout the year and have the most belief can be part of the next Celtics winner?
