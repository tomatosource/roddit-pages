Am I Crazy For Getting Bored Once I Get To Maps?

In my eyes POE2’s peak is standard act 3. You get 4 ascendancy points, access to decent gear, and the bosses and mobs are difficult enough to still make you think about what you are doing.

But once I finish cruel and start mapping, I get really really bored. At this point I’ve been using the same 1-3 abilities for a while now, and the game feels kinda easy since my character is so strong.

I think the big issue is that your build really settles once you hit the late 60s. By then you’ll have most of the gear you need, and your passive tree should have all the most important nodes. That means you have about 20-40 levels of essentially the same thing. It almost feels like diminishing returns to me.
