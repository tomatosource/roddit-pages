Madeline from the 2018 video game Celeste would make the NBA and possibly be the GOAT.

I recently played through Celeste and got curious about the player character’s athletic abilities.

Celeste, being a platformer, gives Madeline a big vertical. According to fans, she should be 150cm/5ft tall, which is short, even for women. She is 2 tiles high and has a 3 tile vertical, 1 tile being 75cm/2’6” which means she has a 225cm/88.5” vertical. She runs around 6 tiles per second giving her a running speed of about 4.5mps/10mph which is not incredibly fast but not too exploitable, although she can go to full speed instantly, which could be great for drives to the rim and perimeter defense, also she cannot ever get tired from running. In one of the levels she is shown to throw a grown man multiple feet into the air and forward, probably enough to hit a 3 pointer, so she could have excellent arm strength for shooting. In official in game art she is shown to have arms long enough to touch her kneecaps standing, giving her a wingspan of around 190cm/6’3.

I haven’t mentioned her dash, but it’s shown to clearly be a power given to her by Celeste Mountain, however, she can jump that high before reaching it, so her athletic superpowers should stay with her in the normal world.

OVERVIEW:
Height: 150cm/5ft
Wingspan: ~190cm/6’3
Speed: ~4.5mps/10mph
Acceleration Time: 0 seconds
Vertical: 225cm/88.5”

Madeline from the 2018 video game Celeste would be unstoppable inside the paint on both defense and offense, is quick enough to be a good enough dribbler and perimeter defender and has the strength to control a shot at least from the 3 point line, and never gets tired, making her not need a backup.
Note: If a sports organization built an arena on Celeste Mountain, she could use her dashing abilities to dunk from 3 uncontested, making her an instant win at home.

It’s funny to think that what is depicted as an anxious and unimposing girl, who, at first, struggles to face challenges, could become the GOAT of a sport without much training.
