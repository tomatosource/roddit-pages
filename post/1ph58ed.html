After the 0.4.0 Stream, I’m Honestly Sold on the Druid Design

Before watching the 0.4.0 stream, I was pretty reserved about the Druid. Let’s be real, in a lot of ARPGs, Druids easily become cookie-cutter classes: either the shapeshifting feels useless, or everything just boils down to stacking raw numbers. But after seeing the showcase, my first reaction was: this version of the Druid has officially passed the bar for me, and not just barely.

What impressed me the most isn’t just the raw power, but how clearly the three forms are differentiated by design.Wolf form is all about speed, fast pacing, and satisfying hits.Bear form is clearly built for face-tanking and survivability.Dragon form fully commits to spell burst and sustained damage.

This kind of role separation is way more thoughtful than the usual “same gameplay, different skin” shapeshifting you see in many games. More importantly, it finally feels like the Druid isn’t locked
