[OC] The Most Aggressive Base Stealers of All Time

Using Retrosheet play-by-play data through 2025, I defined a base stealing opportunity as any time a player had an open base in front of them for a play. For example, if I hit a leadoff single, and the next three batters strike out to end the inning, that would go down as three opportunities to steal second base.

Omar Moreno is by far the most aggressive stealer of second base ever. He stole 343 bases from 1978 to 1982, although not that efficiently. It’s funny to see how despite being so aggressive to steal second base, he was not aggressive at all in stealing third base. Vince Coleman and Billy Hamilton go down as the most aggressive base stealers of all time, and what really boosted their numbers was how aggressive they were in stealing third base. They were the only players to attempt to steal third in 15% of their opportunities. What could have been for Hamilton if he had a better career OBP than .292, because was very efficient at stealing as well.

The winner here isn’t Rickey Henderson’s record-breaking season, but it’s still second place. The previously mentioned Omar Moreno shows up again. The kicker here is that Moreno shockingly attempted to steal second base 70% of the time in 1980, yet he only attempted to steal third 5% of the time. Lou Brock’s second greatest-of-all-time season is similar with a 67%/4% split. 2011 Rajai Davis is the only player ever to attempt to steal third base at a 30% rate in a season.
