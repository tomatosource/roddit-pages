Tonight, the OKC Thunder aim to tie the best 25 game start in NBA history.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_best_records.html

That record belongs to the 2015-16 Warriors, who started 24-0 before losing to MIL.

Let’s assume they join the 15-16 Dubs in the 24-1 club. In order to break their 73-9 mark, they would have to go 50-7 over their final 58 games.

That’s an 87.7% winning percentage. Over a full 82 game regular season, that translates to a 72 win pace (71.92 to be exact). They have to be the 95-96 Bulls from here on out to break the record.

If they regress to last year’s 68 win pace, they go 47-10 and fall short.
