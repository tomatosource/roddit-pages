Platooning Walsh and Hauser

Typically, a baseball concept. Red Sox fans will understand: splitting time between two players at the same position, like Left Field, based on their handedness (L/R) or another factor. Matchup is the basketball phrase for platooning, but it has other broader meanings than “two players sharing time”.

Walsh (defense) and Hauser (offense). A diametrically talent opposed pair of players who play the same position: Wing. One provides exceptional shooting and acceptable defense. The other provides exceptional defense and acceptable offense.

Who plays more is based not on “talent”, it is about matchup and situation and team needs. Who is first off the bench? Depends on the score.

Celtics ahead, Walsh is in. If the Celtics are trailing, Hauser’s playing.

Hugo and Baylor might be another platoon-pair. Queta and Robinson definitely are, though fouls are really their key factor.
