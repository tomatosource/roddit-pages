PoE2 Constant Crashing - and how I fixed the issue

TL;DR: Add key folders to your antivirus scan exclusions.

I just recently tried getting back to PoE2, just to find the game literally unplayable due to crashes, pc freezes and missing textures. It was so bad I couldn’t get to Clearfell on a new character before crashing. I tried reinstalls, verify integrity on Steam, different graphics options, DX12, Vulkan, GPU Driver updates, deleting cache folders etc. and nothing seemed to work.

Looking closer into this I noticed that Antimalware Service Executable was always having large CPU usage and disk reads as the game started becoming unstable. I added the following folders to Antivirus Microsoft Defender exclusions list (Windows Security &gt; Virus & Threat Protection &gt; Exclusions &gt; Add or remove exclusions):

  • {drive}:\Users\{username}\AppData\Local\NVIDIA (Shader caches are located here)
  • {drive}:\Users\{username}\AppData\Roaming\Path of Exile 2 (PoE2 specific cache stuff is located here)
  • {Path of Exile 2 game folder}

This solved the crashes for me and loading times are now almost instant when they used to be over 20 seconds at the minimum.

I couldn’t find any posts suggesting these steps when looking for a solution to this problem, so hopefully this will help someone.
