Duncan Robinson: Helping Isaiah Stewart Fight Is ‘Like Helping Bill Nye With A Science Project’

Duncan Robinson had a pretty funny analogy on the Young Man and the Three podcast when he spoke about the big Pistons Hornets brawl last month.

“It didn’t look like I was down for the cause,” Robinson said. “I learned my lesson– next time I’m going to grab someone, at least fake it, kind of get in the mix. I felt like it was like helping Bill Nye with a science project, like he’s got it taken care of, you know what I mean? There was nothing that I was going to bring to that situation that he wasn’t already handling.”

There’s more from the interview in the article posted here, and the full Beef Stew / Duncan interview is set to drop on the Young Man and the Three YT channel tomorrow morning.
