Tyrese Haliburton is having one of the weirdest seasons in recent history. He either looks amazing or awful, rarely in between. (Stats included)

Earlier in the season it was pointed out that Haliburton has a huge discrepancy in play when you compare his home games vs. away games, but something else I’ve noticed as of late is that he hardly ever seems to have mediocre/okay games - it’s either a very efficient, low turnover game, or it’s a god awful 3-14 stinker where it looks like he’s legitimately scared to score. It’s completely baffling and I don’t think I have seen another star level player in recent history do this.

As of late, he has been playing very well on the road too with some good road wins against Boston, Miami and Brooklyn last night - all games where he shot well too. I did a deep dive into his game log this season and he has only 3 total games shooting in the 30% range from 3. That’s kind of baffling. He has 18 games where he shot 40% or better, and 16 games shooting less than 30% from 3. Which goes along with the eye test and general vibe of this season where he has either been terrific, or pretty bad.

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/halibty01/gamelog/2025/

Further context: The Pacers are 13-5 when Haliburton shoots 40% or better from 3. And they are 4-13 when he shoots worse than 30% from 3. I know this isn’t some sort of novel, groundbreaking observation (hey Einstein, of course the team does better when their star shoots better) but the difference feels stark here. You can tell by midway through the 1Q if Haliburton is Good Hali or Bad Hali that night, and it rarely changes later on in the game.

On the season, he’s averaging 18.5-3.7-8.8 on 44/36/85 shooting (only 1.8 TOs a game which is extremely impressive). His numbers have improved greatly after that god awful start he had to the season, but he still isn’t back to where he was at his peak of the 23-24 season, but he has shown in multiple games that he’s still clearly capable of it. He seems like a good guy and he’s fun to watch when he’s on, so I’m hoping he gets back. I’m not sure if there is still a lingering injury that’s hampering him, but I think he’s due for another elite stretch of like 24-4-11 while shooting efficiently and not turning the ball over. But maybe the only obstacle is Tyrese himself.
