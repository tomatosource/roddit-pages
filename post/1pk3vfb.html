Low-key moves to get Celtics back into Contention?

I’ve seen a lot of infatuation with Zubac and Claxton but I’m not sure I see them as the answers. They’re good but with JB/JT/White we will always be short on cap space. I get they are big names but I highly doubt we will go after either of them. With Tatum back, we are likely another big, and another reliable guard/wing away from being contenders. Probably wouldn’t be the favorite but could have a real shot. This is also assuming experience continues to shape our role players into productive playoff contributors.

I do not think however that any of the pieces we get need to be big splashes like Zu and Claxton would be. I think just finding a usable cheaper big would be very beneficial for us (a la Kornet), while using any money we have left over on one more reliable rotation guard/wing for the playoffs. Maybe Simons fits this mold? Maybe we get him back next year at a good price? But I do think we have one too many defensive wings and another guy who can get a bucket when our offense is ice cold would be huge.

Not sure Simons would net us back someone who fits the criteria but we got DWhite essentially for scraps and a first. You never know. So my question is, who are your ideal candidates that could push this team over the top?
