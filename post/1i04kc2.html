Patience over panic. Let the season play out.

This post is for those who overreact to every loss and close win. Not for the fans who have patience.

Yes, this team hasn’t been playing up to their standard or what we expect of them, but why are some of you acting like they’re not allowed to struggle? Are they somehow immune to it?

Here are my observations so far this season:

  1. They haven’t been shooting well from 3. (Can’t imagine that will last all season)

  2. Effort and defense have been inconsistent.

  3. Rebounding needs improvement.

  4. Some role players aren’t performing at last year’s level.

  5. They’ve rarely had a fully healthy roster. (Yay to finally being healthy)

For whatever reason, they’re struggling right now. It happens. There could be many reasons for it, but this team is too talented not to figure it out. It’s still early in the season. Let’s wait until after the break before freaking out and acting like the team has gone to shit. They’re still 2nd in the East and 3rd in the league.

Last year they had a lot to prove. To the league, to fans, and to themselves. They played with a chip on their shoulder all season, and it showed. Expecting them to replicate that same dominance isn’t realistic. Their record at this point last year wasn’t much different from this year. Sure, it’d be nice to see them crush teams like last year, but just because they’re not doesn’t mean it’s time to panic.

Struggles and bumps in the road aren’t the end of the world. They could actually help them come playoff time. This team has always risen above adversity, and I believe they will continue to do just that.
