Herald of Blood has no effect on Delirium mobs

Since delirium mobs do not leave any corpses behind on death Herald of Blood does not function on them at all. means that players who build around bleed and relay a lot on the Herald of Blood for the clear speed will struggle doing deli maps and simulacrums so bear that in mind.

Doing the content is possible but you will notice the difference by a big margin, and you might struggle doing content you could have done easily with your regular bleed setup.
