Crafting gives me the biggest dopamine hit in this game

I primarily play HCSSF and sometimes I’ll craft some really insane stuff that’s worth 50+ divs in trade league just by doing basic recomb/essence/omen crafts.

Playing SSF forced me to slow down and learn every mechanic and made me learn where to source mats, what bases to save and how to use the least amount of mats to get the best result and it’s honestly been the biggest dopamine hit I’ve ever gotten playing this game when I craft something really good.

I took that crafting knowledge to trade this league and was able to farm up a mirror in divs by just turning into a hideout warrior, it felt so satisfying consistently making gear and flipping profit passively that it ended up ruining any other farm strat in the game for me.

For those of you reading this who are either too broke in trade league because you don’t know what to grind/how to grind, you should challenge yourself and join SSF league.

Force yourself to craft your own gear, save mats and learn how to optimize a build and I guarantee your entire perspective on this game will change by the time you make it to maps.

You’ll get addicted quick to crafting, and you’ll realize how high those market prices are when you can craft it for a fraction of the cost and it might even motivate you to turn into a hideout warrior in future league launches once you see how fun and easy crafting is.
