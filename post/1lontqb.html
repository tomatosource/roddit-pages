Reinvigorated myself for POE 2 Campaign

So having done what I wanted to with my LS Deadeye, I wanted to try something new.

So, despite the seeming overwhelming complexity, I decided to set myself a goal, a philosophy perhaps.

I would not use a single build guide; I will utilise what I have learnt by following them in the past but head off into the wilderness alone.

My goal is to recreate a Diabo 2 Necromancer Minion build. using curses to depower enemies while minions do the killing. This is not about one-shotting T4 Arbiter, I am being realistic, but simply to play through the whole game and see how much understanding I can unlock by reading everything with no base assumptions about what works, and what doesn’t.

It will take a lot longer, but I truly want to understand how to create builds, and not just be spoon-fed.

I am using search terms to find support gems, adding what makes sense, for example: I discovered that I had made my curse cost too much, searched for and found the support that reduced cost.

Simple, basic stuff, seeing a problem, and solving it from first principles is so much more satisfying than parroting someone else’s work.

I did allow myself to use the assets in my stash though, gawd dammit I AM going to use those ‘levelling uniques’!
