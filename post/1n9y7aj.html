Basically the title. Saw a short clip of a guy explaining it, tried it myself and my FPS increased substantially.

Setup your general chat to a long, random number, logout, log back in. Do the same for trade, and hopefully, like me, you’ll see an increase in your FPS.

Your mileage may vary, I’ve managed to get around 20-30 extra FPS.
