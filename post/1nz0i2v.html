I made a Spreadsheet for Twilight Reliquary Drops (and opened 1,400 myself)

Hello y’all! A few days ago, I made a post about my reliquary vault keys, and now can happily say I’ve opened them all.

In addition, I created a spreadsheet and google form so that people can input the results of what they get from Reliquary Vaults to add to the list. I 100% have not dropped every item possible from them, and my sample size isn’t large enough to calculate the exact odds, however if enough people pool their experiences and correctly enter their uniques into the google form, we can try to roughly accumulate the odds of certain items to appear. With this spreadsheet, you can also determine if you want to try your odds with some keys due to them being dirt cheap as of this post (like 5 ex per key) I’d 100% say they’re worth trying out. I personally will be entering over 10,000 keys into it over the league, but I hope you all do some, too, &lt;3

Some “rules” regarding it:

  1. When entering your info, say you drop a Pillar of the Caged God and want to enter it. DO NOT enter “Pillar of the False God, Long Quarterstaff” because the spreadsheet will read it as a new item. In order for it to work properly, you have to just do the unique item name, not the subtype.

  2. Spell it like I have in the Spreadsheet. Capitalize the first letter of the name, don’t capitalize articles except for that, etc. Otherwise the same problem will arise where the spreadsheet doesn’t recognize it and lists it as a new item

  3. Please, Please, PLEASE, DO NOT ONLY LIST ONE OR TWO ITEMS, ESPECIALLY IF IT’S JUST A HEADHUNTER. That will dramatically screw up the percentages and make it seem misleading, which may cause people to spend money believe they have a good chance to make it back, when they very well may not.

  4. I shouldn’t have to mention this, and I don’t think it’ll matter for a bit, but please don’t fabricate results. I can see when people do that and very easily purge everything they’ve added to the sheet. It wastes both our time. Please don’t, lol.
