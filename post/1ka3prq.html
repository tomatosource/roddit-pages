Timberwolves trivia: Before Anthony Edwards, the only player in franchise history to score 40 points in a playoff game was Sam Cassell in 2004, his only full season with the club.

Sam Cassell was known for his intelligent, clutch play without being flashy, including winning 2 rings with Houston in his first 2 seasons. His first season with Minnesota was in 2004, and they were a hot team going into the playoffs with him, Kevin Garnett, and Latrell Sprewell. Even though the other two were bigger scorers, Cassell set the franchise scoring record with 40 points in a game that year, which he did twice, before injuring himself doing his big balls dance, unable to play effectively against the Lakers in the WCF. He missed a ton of games the next year before getting traded to the Clippers.

No one else in Timberwolves history scored 40 points in a playoff game other than Sam Cassell–not KG, not Love, not KAT, not Sprewell–before Anthony Edwards.
