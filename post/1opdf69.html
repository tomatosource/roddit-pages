Kenny Smith signs with ESPN in a New Multi-Year Broadcasting Agreement

NBA legend Kenny Smith has signed his first deal with ESPN in a new multi-year agreement to join the network’s basketball programming. Smith will primarily contribute to ESPN’s “First Take” program while also serving as an analyst for select NBA games. His debut appearance under the new deal will be a special guest spot on NBA Countdown on November 5th. This marks Smith’s expansion into ESPN’s basketball coverage after his long tenure with Turner Sports/TNT’s “Inside the NBA.“​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
