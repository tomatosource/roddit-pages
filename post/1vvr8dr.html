My friend thinks my craft is mirror tier, but I said it’s missing 5% quality. Does it come down to build cost, or perfection?

Process is the same for sinister staff, which I think most monks use. I prefer AOE for fullscreen whirling assault. This was my process. Hopefully it helps someone.

If you have unlimited resources, Start with a 30% base by using vaal weapon infusers. It’s supposedly a 10% chance to get there without the item corrupting, so I found this cost prohibitive and skipped.

-Buy 3socket bases with T1 crit chance on them (make sure ilvl81). Divine until happy, add 2 mods and unrevealed desecration. Fracture and start over until the 1⁄3 hits (fracture will never select unrevealed desecration). The low ilvl of this affix means leaving it unfractured would prevent you from using omen of whittling during the craft. It also has a high range of variance, so locking it at a high roll after divining is preferable.
-Annul and chaos spam until T1 elemental.
-Perfect exalt with omens of sinistral and greater exaltation. You now have 3 prefixes and a fractured suffix.
-Omen of whittling until you convert the other two prefixes into T1 elements. I used perfect chaos.

Now suffixes. We started with prefixes because they won’t get picked up by whittles later. Armour crafts are usually done suffix–&gt;prefix by similar logic. You have to land +5 melee first because no other suffix is ilvl81 ergo it’s safe from whittling. This part is extra expensive–I remember one day I logged in, threw 1000div at the item, and literally nothing had changed.
-Pexalt+greater to add 2 suffixes. Dextral annul, dextral erasure, whittle etc whatever is cheapest based on market. Rinse repeat.
-After finally landing melee skills, you can safely whittle the third suffix to infinity. Take crit damage or attack speed–whichever comes first. Attack speed is technically a more lucky pull based on statistical frequency.
-Socket Serle’s Triumph, add desecration. Abyssal echoes and omen of light until you find T1 of the missing suffix.
