Can we stop with the monsters shooting homing long-reaching death beams?

I’m just going to rant here. My apologies in advance.

It used to be those god awful sun priests that would snipe you from somewhere off the screen and kill you in a couple seconds. That was done away with. Great!

Except that they now replaced them with these Twilight Order Priests. The “New Tomorrow” guys from Act 4. Their beam isn’t as far reaching, but there’s more of them and come with an escort.

Seriously, can we cut that stuff out? If you’re melee and you’re in a pack of mobs, you don’t always have time to get to those guys. Once they cast their thing, you can’t avoid it. They’ll lock onto you and will kill you pretty quickly. You can try to roll and back out, if the mobs or terrain allow you to, but that’s not always guaranteed.

Most of my deaths on juiced up maps are from those guys. The question that comes to mind is : “Why?” Wasn’t it pretty clear that this kind of stuff wasn’t good? Why nerf it on the priests…to then add it again on another freaking mob in a new season? Why are we so lazer-focused (pun intended) on these annoying and gratuitously punishing mechanics? Why not just focus on making content that is going to be a good experience? User feedback was pretty clear on that one. And that’s the whole point of a beta. To gather user feedback.

I don’t know. I wish things were different.
