I feel like both PoE subs go through the exact same cycle every few updates

  • If it’s not a W update, genuine concerns are raised, but a lot of people react in a very toxic/unhealthy manner
  • Sub is flooded with unhinged hate posts for a few days. Bad actors swarm in looking for some good drama to sink their teeth into. Streamers start to weigh in, usually with more measured takes
  • GGG comes in with an unpopular response that sounds a bit out of touch
  • Fan vitriol hits a fever pitch
  • GGG finally acquiesces and does something to put out the fires
  • People start to calm down. A lot of the bad actors lose interest and stop posting
  • Posts shaming all the bad actors and praising GGG for responding start appearing 3-5 days in
  • Sub goes back to mostly being game-related posts by week 2 or 3

This has pretty much been the pattern since 3.15.
