POE2 DPS Meter - A tool to measure your in-game DPS against bosses!

Hello exiles!

After a few months of on-and-off development, I am finally releasing POE2 DPS Meter! This tool takes advantage of the damage numbers on boss life-bars to determine how much DPS you are dealing.

Check it out here!

This tool can track your overall DPS (since starting a recording), your recent DPS (4-second window)

It also saves previous recordings so you can compare different values (this is useful for swapping items or support gems, or trying new combos).

Let me know what you think! If you happen to give it a shot, please leave a comment or github issue with any bugs or other problems you run into. I am also happy to hear feedback about new features. Thank you!

Disclaimers:

  • This is not affiliated with GGG in any way. It does not directly interact with the game in any way. It’s just aggregating some information that’s already available to you.
  • The damage numbers only count up when dealing damage to life (not energy shield), so be mindful of that.
  • It will probably break when fighting two bosses, or if there is any other text near the damage number.
  • This is my first release like this, and I am not really a front-end developer. I imagine some things will be wonky on different machines (as I could only test on a handful).
