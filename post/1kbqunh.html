Trade vs. Crafting

Gearing from trade is relatively deterministic. Depending on market availability you can generally find an item with 4-6 of the mods you want. If your bankroll is large enough you can essentially get almost exactly what you want.

The trade website is basically a crafting bench. You add the mods and ranges you want, get a bunch of outcomes, click “craft”(whisper) on the best outcome, and your item is delivered.

While it may not be the perfect item, you know exactly what you are getting.

Why is this acceptable but some similar determinism in crafting is unacceptable?
