More love for low-click builds

I’ve been playing PoE for years and really miss the more passive builds in PoE1 that didn’t require constant clicking, like auto triggers or passive AoE setups.

In PoE2, it feels like the only low-click option is minions, and even they need a lot of manual input. As someone pushing 40 and working as a programmer, I try to keep my hands healthy, I don’t want to feel like I need an energy drink just to play.

I recently played Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor and loved how chill but still engaging it was. I’d love to see PoE2 include more build options like that. Maybe not bleeding edge dps, but something fun without all the hand strain.
