I think they need to really focus on getting Uniques overhauled and up to par for next league.

I’m farming T15 maps and STILL haven’t gotten a single decent unique drop. The low level uniques that I get aren’t even good for leveling a new character. They’ll be like a level 11 item with 34 armor, +20 maximum life, 10% Cold Resist, and a unique affix like “Enemies that hit you have a 100% chance to kill you”.

The uniques that actually have decent effects are still unfortunately stuck on terrible item bases with zero defensive/offensive stats. I’ve never been more bored of unique items in my life. Even Diablo 1 did it better.

For myself personally, unique collecting is one of the most fun and exciting parts of PoE1, especially as a SSF player. Getting a bunch of cool uniques and using them to spark an idea for a second character build a few weeks into a league is what I do every time in PoE1.

While so few uniques exist in the game, it would make sense for the majority of those uniques to actually be usable.
