Possibly the craziest NBA butterfly effect since Game 6 Klay against OKC: if Josh Giddey doesn’t hit his half court buzzer beater against the Luka/Lebron Lakers on March 27th, Cooper Flagg is a Chicago Bull instead of a Dallas Maverick.

The Bulls & Mavs ended the season with the same record and therefore, the 11th pick came down to a coin flip. The Mavs won the coin flip so they received the 11th pick and the Bulls the 12th pick.

Had Giddey not hit that laughably low percentage probability half court buzzer beater, the Bulls would have lost to the Lakers 117-116 on March 27th and ended up with a 38-44 record and outright possession of the 11th pick, which would in turn become the no. 1 overall pick.

Add in the fact that the shot came against the Luka/Lebron Lakers, of all teams, a team that also had to necessarily exist in order for the domino effect that resulted in last night’s draft lottery to occur and whewww the basketball gods are some darkly comedic, clever mfs. 😂
