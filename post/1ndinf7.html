Rising Tempest tech for more damage on any elemental build

Unsure if it has been mentioned before but tri-elemental skills, such as Snap or Elemental Weakness work for all 3 procs of the Rising Tempest support (12% more elemental damage per different element skill use). After a single, not triple, blind cast of Snap in hideout (without any targets, so no infusions or procs) my tooltip goes from 61239 to 83286 (which doesn’t happen without the support socketed), being exactly 1.36x damage multiplier for 4 seconds which refreshes on subsequent casts. To compare this, 1.35x Considered Casting lowers your cast speed by 15% while 1.25x Controlled Destruction removes your crits, so the support is very strong, especially after consolidation of elemental curses into single EW which many builds happen to weave in their single target rotation anyway. This might be unintended and leave us sooner or later but for now you can play with it and you can do it on any elemental build, not only casters. Staves roll up to 52% increased cast speed, wand+shield combo up to 67% for some extra offhand QoL (base cast time of Snap is super fast anyway, just 0.5s - I managed to get it under 0.2s on my Infernalist). Haven’t tested if triggers proc the support too, you guys can try it out.
