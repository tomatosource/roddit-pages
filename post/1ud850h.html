Giannis Antetokounmpo leaves Milwaukee as the Bucks’ all-time leader in points, rebounds, assists, and blocks

Giannis Antetokounmpo leaves Milwaukee as the Bucks’ all-time leader in all four major statistical categories: points, rebounds, assists, and blocks.

He and Kevin Garnett with the Timberwolves are the only players in NBA history to lead a franchise in all four categories.

Bucks all-time points leaders:
1.) Giannis Antetokounmpo — 21,531 PTS
2.) Kareem Abdul-Jabbar — 14,211 PTS
3.) Khris Middleton — 12,586 PTS

Bucks all-time rebounds leaders:
1.) Giannis Antetokounmpo — 8,882 REB
2.) Kareem Abdul-Jabbar — 7,161 REB
3.) Bob Dandridge — 4,497 REB

Bucks all-time assists leaders:
1.) Giannis Antetokounmpo — 4,484 AST
2.) Paul Pressey — 3,272 AST
3.) Khris Middleton — 2,990 AST

Bucks all-time blocks leaders:
1.) Giannis Antetokounmpo — 1,088 BLK
2.) Brook Lopez — 990 BLK
3.) Alton Lister — 804 BLK

Timberwolves all-time points leaders:
1.) Kevin Garnett — 19,201 PTS
2.) Karl-Anthony Towns — 13,121 PTS
3.) Anthony Edwards — 10,854 PTS

Timberwolves all-time rebounds leaders:
1.) Kevin Garnett — 10,718 REB
2.) Karl-Anthony Towns — 6,216 REB
3.) Kevin Love — 4,453 REB

Timberwolves all-time assists leaders:
1.) Kevin Garnett — 4,216 AST
2.) Ricky Rubio — 3,424 AST

Timberwolves all-time blocks leaders:
