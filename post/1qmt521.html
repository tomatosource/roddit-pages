Game feedback - bricked temple

First of all: this is the best ARPG I have played. Having said that, I’ve almost quit this game because of how punishing it can be in many situations (death, penalty, crafting, etc.). It just makes you feel like sh*t whenever you make a mistake.

This time I will be unfortunately be quitting the game. Maybe I will come back in future leagues to check it out, but will hardly put the same time that I had been putting so far.

Long story short: I bricked my temple after I had been working on it for over a week. I didnt realize dynamo connected to traumaturge, made a loop and poof: 70% of my progress gone. It feels outrageous to have a simple undo button, and that alone can make all that effort go away.

The temple was the only thing keeping me this far in the league just because how fun it was to build it. I had so much currency that I didnt even bother with the loot anymore. I genuinely see this as an ultimate disrespect to the player base time. What is the point on allowing such a mechanic? Make me learn from it? After 1 week playing heavily, putting the effort to build it? No thanks, I will move on to games that punishment feels adequate and makes you want to improve (Fromsoftware Im looking at you). One thing is when you choose to play Hardcore and stuff like that happens… you signed up for that. On this case, I didnt sign up for this.

I do hope GGG changes its mindset and approach towards this game, no other ARPG comes close in terms of overall quality, but I value my time too much to play anymore.

I hope the rest of you enjoy your time playing and pray something like what happened to me, doesnt happen to you.
