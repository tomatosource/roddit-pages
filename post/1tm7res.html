A training / damage test dummy would be really nice

I often find myself just running bad maps in order to try something out. And often with the need to go to the boss just to see the numbers.

Often I am happy when I find a chunky rare monster with life regeneration so that I can judge how effective my hits are based on where I get the damage bar relative to the text with attack type A compared to Attack type B after the rare has fully regenerated.

So I wish we could just test more efficiently with some basic settings. With a test dummy some might know if you played the game Hades, for example.

Here a visualisation of how I imagine it, very roughly:

https://preview.redd.it/t0b2pmuk323h1.png?width=1536&format=png&auto=webp&s=99c584b492005a9cafb938e9f40a372081bf42aa

  • A hideout addition.
  • With selectable monster types (Including packs)
  • Settings for the “map” mods of the monsters
  • A boss health bar to look at
  • A set of damage info on the side

Perhaps also an option for them attacking you to test defences against certain damage types would be awesome. Like a test: can I tank a hit from the Mighty Silverfist, fully juiced?

This would make my build-crafting sooooooo much more fun instead of wasting maps for just comparing numbers.

Please?

It is also a business opportunity with lots of cosmetics that could be attached to this. :)
