You DO have a voice, even if it doesn’t feel like it

I know that a lot of people are frustrated by what they perceive as a disconnect between the devs and the player base, and the decisions the devs make with the game. Well, there are a few ways that you have a voice, as a player, that go beyond the forum posts and reddit threads.

For starters, you have your Steam review. This is a surprisingly powerful tool that you all have. When a game drops down many percentage points in the Steam score, companies pay attention. And even if you think that GGG doesn’t, (they assuredly do) you can bet that their parent company DOES. If you’re unhappy enough that you think the issues you are experiencing matter to future potential players, then you can always change your review score.

You can also not log in. I know this one feels bad, because you WANT to play the game, you WANT to enjoy the game and be part of the community, and you WANT to feel rewarded for playing. There’s also a sunk cost at play here. But if this weekend shows a massive drop in player numbers, that might communicate something to the devs that a reddit post won’t.

And then finally, you have the way in which you participate in their monetization. Maybe next league, if you come back again, you dont buy a supporter pack. Or a cosmetic, or whatever. Maybe you wait until the END of a league to buy anything, as a reward to the devs for a league you enjoyed instead of a pre-league hype purchase. And if they end up fumbling the league as badly as this one, maybe you dont give them ANY money for it.

These things all feel small to us, but en masse, actually accomplish a lot.

Im sorry to everybody that put in a lot of time to the league mechanic only to have it taken away from you today. That’s a shitty feeling.
