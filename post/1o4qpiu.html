NBC Sports Will Deploy a Pair of Dedicated Analysts ‘On the Bench’ for Peacock NBA Monday Games

NBC Sports is aiming to bring NBA fans closer to the hardcourt action than ever before with its new “On the Bench” production element exclusively for games streaming on Peacock NBA Monday. NBC will dedicate one analyst to each team and position that analyst courtside with their team, allowing each commentator to cover the game through the lens of the team they’re assigned.
