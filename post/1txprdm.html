How is alt spamming worse than buying hundreds of bases?

Let me preface this by saying that crafting is one of, if not my favourite, part of PoE 1

The reasoning for not having scouring orbs is usually given as not wanting to bring back alt spamming: sitting in your hideout and spamming hundreds to thousands of alteration orbs to roll the affixes you want. And I agree, this kinda sucks.

But let me counter that by asking how it’s better to instead have to buy hundreds of bases? If I want to roll +3 spell skills on an ilvl 82 amulet, that will take on average 177 perfects augs (assuming the suffix is open). So then instead of just buying one base and rolling it until I succeed, I’m about to spend the next 40-60 minutes buying at least 200 magic solar amulets with an open suffix.

Maybe it’s just me, but this is much worse than even alt spamming.

Edit: And even once I succeed at that roll, fracturing it is a 1⁄3. So odds are that after I go through all this, I’m going to have to do it again.
