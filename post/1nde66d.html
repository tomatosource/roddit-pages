Mapping feel lifeless and random with no soul.

This is my opinion of course, I love this game and I want to see it succeed.

It doesnt really feel like were cleansing the land in endgame, its just bunch of random mobs auto generated, there is no thematic dungeon crawling feel or sense of adventure in any of the maps, just random selection of mobs that for some reason just decide to team up and hang around lifelessly. Unlike in campaign going through the crypts in act one for a good example, thematically the enemies are fitting, they arent placed almost robotically and in giant packs filling the whole zone, player power feels balanced at this stage also.

First playthrough of act 1 I thought this is it, the best arpg in the making until I reached maps and it turns into a mess. I was so disappointed to see breach, ritual etc reused loot pinata minigames from poe 1. Thats the best they could come up with? Thats why I barely make it one week into each major seasonal update because endgame has no soul. I click map, bunch of randomly teamed up enemies fill the zone, do loot pinata minigame, they all die in one shot, get no loot anyway, teleport out and repeat for the next map.

There needs to be thematic objectives, more handcrafted enemy placements, better atlas layout, less but tougher more rewarding enemies.

Like instead of singular maps why not start in a specific biome and have zones that lead to other multiple zones like in campaign? You can leave anytime you want and start a new one but throw in dynamic pop up events and objectives, add increasing reward and difficulty the further you go, add random enterable dungeons found in the zones with unique conditions and modifiers which may have multiple depths which increase difficulty and reward, throw in dual boss encounters, invasions, ambushes, make things like breach appear more immersively not from stepping on a circle in every map. Its also ok for maps to be handcrafted entirely, it should feel like an actual adventure with risk, surprise and occasional downtime like small camps, wandering merchants, etc, not slay the rare enemies on every wonky generated map.

In an ARPG like this theres endless creativity for the endgame its just sad to see it in this state and I really cant see it improving much in the direction it’s going in if they dont rework it from the ground up.
