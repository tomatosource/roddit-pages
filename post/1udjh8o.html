Not Getting Giannis Was the Best Thing to Happen

I swear you guys are living in the past. We wouldn’t have gotten Giannis from 2018-2022. We would have been getting a 32 year old Giannis who is just starting to show wear and tear, a Giannis who hasn’t had a healthy postseason run since 2022, a Giannis who hasn’t played more than 65 games in 4 out of the last 8 seasons, a Giannis who consistently gets figured out, and more. The fact many of you were willing to break up a duo with a proven history of winning and pulling off deep playoff runs, for a hypothetical duo that may or may do that, is bonkers.

If this were 4-5 years ago yes I would have traded for Giannis, but we’re not living in 2021. It’s a whole different world and context than before. Our team would have been worse off with Giannis. Would have been, at best, two years before the whole thing comes off the wheels. And that’s being generous by the way. There is no guarantee that Giannis and Tatum would have clicked since they are both ball dominant players used to being the faces of their franchises, just look at what happened with dame and giannis. Meanwhile Tatum and Brown have proven for years they can play together successfully.

Not only that, Giannis would have made us rely on the 3 ball even more than what we already are relying on. The Bucks have been top 5 in 3PM for 7 of the past 8 years. Giannis doesn’t shoot many 3s, and because he spends all his time scoring inside, that means the other 4 guys on the court need to be outside.

To explain it really simply, your role players have to be where your star players aren’t. You can’t have everyone standing around next to each other. If we were to have traded for Giannis, we would have had to also trade Queta and try to bring in a starting big man who can shoot 3s. Unfortunately, that big man is likely to be expensive, which means we also have to trade Hauser as a minimum to free up some money.

The value of Tatum and Brown is that they can do literally anything. They’re the jack-of-all trades, while Giannis is the master of one. They can play with floor-spacing big men like Al Horford and Kristaps Porzingis, but they can also play with rim-running big men like Neemias Queta and Rob Williams III. When the big man is inside, the Jays are outside. When the big man is outside the Jays are inside. They make everyone look good. That means Boston can focus on finding good players and not worry too much about how they’re going to fit next to the Jays.

With giannis we would lose this luxury and be forced to consider how they would fit with Giannis on the floor. They would need to have some semblance of shooting threes, which basically eliminates majority of the big men. And would need to shoot way more threes than usual, which basically discounts their sample size of shooting threes.

Horford is a really good example of what I’m talking about. When he went to play for the 76ers, they hated him and thought he was a terrible 3P shooter. He came back to Boston and shot even worse, and we loved him for how good his shooting was. So he was shooting less and shooting worse for us, but Philly fans were complaining and asking “why didn’t he shoot this well for us?”. This is because Philadelphia had Joel Embiid and Ben Simmons, who really needed someone spend 100% of their time on the 3P line. We had Tatum and Brown, who only needed someone to shoot the occasional 3.

Interior players actually increase your team-wide reliance on 3s. Giannis would have done that.
