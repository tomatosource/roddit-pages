QOL suggestion regarding death

Death in this game in the mapping stage is IMMENSELY punishing. Honestly, I’m cool with this though, it makes everything feel a bit more tense.

With that said, I just fought Pirasha the forgotten prisoner on a lvl 81 delerious map. A tough boss fight to be sure, but I was doing quite well. Kept the poison cloud away, went in to fight during openings and wasn’t being greedy. I’m an invoker with around 3k life and with grim feat stacked around 10k ES.

I randomly got one shot by her. No slam, no poison, just a hit, and I’m dead. With how punishing death is, this feels TERRIBLE.

I think without having multiple attempts at maps like POE1, it’s time to include a post death information log. What killed me, what damage source, how much, etc. I was blindsided and straight punished and without going back and reviewing a shadowplay video, I have no feedback on how to prevent that from happening again.

Anyway, end of rant. I just think having the information on why you died would balance how punishing death is a bit without taking away from how punishing it is. I don’t mind dying when I know it’s my fault, I know I have not good enough gear, etc. But I was full life mostly full ES, getting one shot in that scenario just creates frustration when you have no clue why.
