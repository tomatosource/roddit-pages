What is the NBA equivalent of ‘Arson Judge’?

Yes that’s right. I said Arson Judge, not Aaron Judge. Those who don’t watch baseball, Arson Judge is a hypothetical MLB player. Back in late-2022, a news reporter tweeted with a typo where Aaron Judge was headed to the Giants. He typed Arson Judge instead because he was in such a rush. Then the reporter deleted and tweeted this with the correct name. However, Aaron Judge returned to the Yankees instead of Giants. This culminated in fans creating a joke player named Arson Judge.
