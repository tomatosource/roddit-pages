Winning Basketball Is Not Boring

I keep seeing the word “boring” being used to describe the Celtics’ style of basketball.

Nearly every time I get the chance to watch the Celtics, both this year and last year, I find it some of the most beautiful basketball I’ve seen in ages. The ball flow is immaculate. Watching someone drive, kick the ball out, and then effortlessly move it around the floor until someone has a good shot (often a three) or a chance to drive again and score. It’s glorious. It’s sweet. It makes me so happy and when I watch other teams play each other it often feels like I’m watching mid level college teams. They have no flow, at least not compared to what I’m used to seeing from the C’s.

I don’t know what they are looking for, but if their team was this good they wouldn’t find it boring. If a more popular team was moving the ball with this kind of ease and sinking their shots with anything close to our efficiency, they’d be heralded as the future of the sport, even if a more team-oriented approach might make it harder to (most likely inaccurately) crown the next Jordan or LeBron.

It seems too easy to just trot out the old “they hate us ‘cause they ain’t us” but at this point what else can we conclude? Winning is why we play the game in the first place. I’ll take Russell a thousand times… a million times over Chamberlain, and I’ll take these guys and the effortless beauty of their brand of winning Basketball over a more “exciting” team that is devoted to hero-ball and having a single superstar trying to become the next GOAT.

Can I have a “hell yeah”?
