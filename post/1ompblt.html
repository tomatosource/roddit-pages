I became a baseball fan this world series. I cant believe i’ve been missing this my entire life!

That world series was awesome. Highs, lows, exciting finishes, big moments, went down to the wire of game 7 in overtime.

Im from LA and have been here my entire life but ive never been a baseball fan. Sure id root for the Dodgers in the sense of “oh cool hope the home team does well”, but I never went to the games or watched on TV. I kinda always had the mindset “this is so boring, the pitchers takes 10 minutes to pitch the damn ball.”

Boy was I wrong, if this is how baseball is, consider me a freaking fan. I never appreciated nor realized how much of a chess game baseball is. The communication between the pitcher and catcher, the pitching line up changes and technique based on who is hitting the ball, I never realized baseball had strategy outside of “I hit ball and run around.”

Now I’ve read comments on here last night regarding how this is bad for the sport for the Dodgers to be the Tom Brady era Patriots of the MLB and I cant claim to know the inner workings of the MLB or baseball, but all I know is im a fan today and I plan to watch this upcoming season and try to go to at least 3 or 4 dodgers games with my family this next season.

My 2 other friends became fans as well and theyre more “NFL only” than me, but this world series felt more exciting than a couple recent super bowls and like the last 5 NBA finals.

Like is the MLB just bad at marketing the sport or have we been living under a rock?
