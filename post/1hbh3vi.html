Is it time to consider that the league partnering with gambling operations is contributing to the decline in fan interest?

I remember an old movie, “The Last Boyscout” where Bruce Willies is pitted against a shady football owner who wants gambling legalized. His big villain speech talked about how the game was dying and partnering with gambling operations was how they could squeeze the last bit of profit.

I think partnering with gambling hurts the sport in two ways. Those of us who know how destructive it can be to individuals and families don’t want it normalized for our kids, so if we get routine betting segments during games, we are less likely to tune in.

It also ruins the credibility of the competitive product. If the NBA earns more money based on their share of gambling revenue, they have a perverse incentive to manipulate results in order to increase profits. In fact, some would say they have a responsibility to do so.

In that environment, the wild swings in team performance, individual performance, in how games are called etc. all look extremely suspect. As a fan, I don’t feel that players or owners need more money, and I find the promotion of gambling within the sport extremely distasteful. I wonder if anyone else out there feels the same way and that has something to do with why people are abandoning the sport.
