The NBA should start tracking game winning shots.

A game winning shot doesn’t have to be a buzzer beater, although it can be. It can be defined as any shot that wins the game, regardless of time left on the clock. If a team is down by two with 30 seconds left, and someone makes a 3 with 15 seconds left, and the opponent doesn’t score in the 15 remaining seconds, that is a game winning shot. It just seems like this is a statistic worth keeping track of. It would be interesting to see who got the most.
