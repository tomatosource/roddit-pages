Volatile plants is way too common of a mod and awful to play against as melee

This mod shows up 5+ times per map in my endgame maps and it feels terrible to play against as a melee character.

- First, it deals chaos damage which is the hardest res to cap (I’m SSF and chaos res gear basically doesn’t exist). Most melee characters aren’t CI (unless they are certain monk builds) and can’t safely ignore chaos dmg.
- Second, it’s an annoying zone control mechanic that disproportionately affects melee characters that can’t deal damage while kiting the orbs. If you need to stop moving in close range and wind up a slow attack to deal damage the orbs will detonate on you.
- The orbs spawn too quickly after detonating, giving you only a very small window to deal damage.
- The orbs persist even after killing or stunning/freezing the rare.
- The orbs are often hard to see when there are a lot of other enemies and effects on screen. I often don’t notice the mod until it’s too late.
- The mod is way too common and I swear is on about 50% of rares in my maps.

GGG, please make this mod less terrible to deal with as melee. Make it less common, make the balls dissipate after stunning or killing the mob, and give a longer window between the balls spawning where you can actually deal damage.
