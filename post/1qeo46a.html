Derrick White is averaging 18/4/5 with the worst TS% (53%) of the 46 players averaging 18+ PPG. He’s also been one of the 5 most valuable players in the league this season according to two of the best advanced metrics: EPM and LEBRON.

A lot of people thought that White’s excellent impact metrics would crater now that he’s no longer the 4th or 5th option on a juggernaut. But they’ve actually improved this season. His EPM is +4.2 after hovering between +2.7 and +3.8 over the past 5 seasons. He also has a career high in LEBRON.

Despite his worst shooting season of his career, White continues to excel at every other aspect of the game: playmaking, defense, and ball security.

EPM and LEBRON are impact metrics that use box score stats, tracking data, and adjusted on/off to measure a player’s impact per 100 possessions. They each have a cumulative version, which takes playing time int account, that measures total impact.

Top 5 in EPM Wins:

  1. Shai, 11.2
  2. Jokic, 9.0
  3. Mitchell, 7.1
  4. Maxey, 7.0
  5. White, 6.1

Top 5 in LEBRON Wins:

  1. SGA, 7.1
  2. Cade, 5.9
  3. Jokic, 5.8
  4. White, 5.6
  5. Maxey, 5.5

Some of this is playing time: White is 18th in the league in minutes played. He’s only 16th in the league in the rate version of EPM and 8th in LEBRON. This puts his cumulative stats ahead of guys like Giannis and Wemby, who have better rate stats.
