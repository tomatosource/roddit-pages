Why completely delete the identity of charges?

Power, Frenzy, Endurance, one for Strength, one for Dex, one for Int, one for damage, one for speed, and one for tankiness. Some skills consume them to empower themselves or bypass cooldowns. Its a simple system, it works. They charges were unique and had flavorful ways to generate them (blood rage, ice bite, PC on crit, Endurance on stun etc.)

So why change it all?! In PoE2 we basically just got 3 colours, they do nothing on their own and more often than not require like 2-3 seconds of set up to generate ONE charge. And then the skills that do use them don’t use them to bypass a cooldown to get a powerful effect more often, its more like you need to perform a lengthy set up to get one good use of a skill, with a chance for more based on your support gem setup, which feels terrible.

Here’s an Idea: If you wanted this whole system of generating charges for skills, and don’t actually want charges to have another identity or use case, then don’t make them distinct, just call them “empower charges” or something, since that’s all they do anyway! Rn we have green balls, red balls, blue balls, which empower red, green and blue skills respectively. Why not just tie them together into a single mechanic that serves to empower specific skills across the board, then certain archetypes could also have different ways of generation for “empower charges” from the tree and specific supports. This encourages experimentation as a neat side effect, in addition to not being so damn messy.

The system for charges we have in place right now just has 3 flavours of ice cream, all of which fulfil the same purpose, but depending on which skill you want to empower with them and which class you play, gives you extremely limited options to generate each, meaning that we have a system that discourages experimentation and only seems to serve the purpose of trying to chokehold players into specifc playstyles and predetermined combos. Though that seems to have been the singular goal in the making of the game.

Do you share my opinion?
