Kawhi Leonard is the 2nd player in franchise history to score 200+ points over a 5-game span, joining Bob McAdoo (1974-75)

Source (via Stathead)

Kawhi Leonard has scored 202 points over his last 5 games. (List of box scores)

The franchise record is 224 points by Bob McAdoo, from 1975-03-18 to 1975-03-26. (List of box scores)

If curious, the NBA record for most points over a 5-game span is 351, done by Wilt Chamberlain in 1962. And yes, that includes his 100-point game.
