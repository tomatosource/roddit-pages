Summary of Tris “POE2 Changes needed” video

Tri recently released a collaboration video featuring various content creators and community members discussing the most critical changes needed for Path of Exile 2 Patch 0.4.

I wanted to make a breakdown of the requests. I don’t necessarily agree on everything, but thought it would be a good discussion.

Mapping & Atlas Systems

  • Streamlined Map Preparation: The process to “juice” a map (vertebrae, tablets, etc.) is currently too tedious. The setup time needs to be drastically reduced. — Darth Microtransaction
  • Atlas Tree Rework: The current Atlas tree feels inferior to PoE1’s in terms of player agency. It needs a rework to allow players to better control their activities. — Jazarus
  • Terraforming Mechanics: The Atlas tree should be able to “terraform” the Atlas—changing terrain types, biomes, and boss spawns—rather than just providing minor stat tweaks. — Tri
  • Improved Map Layouts: Maps need more structure and landmarks (similar to the “Augury” map) to guide players, rather than feeling like a generic “fog of war.” — Palsteron
  • Delete “The Well”: A specific request to remove the mandatory clicking interaction with the Well in town. — Jazarus

Loot, Economy & Rarity

  • Rarity System Overhaul: Currently, running ~150% rarity feels mandatory for currency efficiency. Rarity needs to be removed or decoupled from currency drops so players aren’t forced to build for it. — Miso
  • Divine Orb Sink: The economy is suffering from inflation. A sink for Divine Orbs (similar to the crafting bench in PoE1) is needed to balance the market for casual players. — GhazzyTV
  • Buff Boss Uniques: Bosses are currently too easy, and their Unique drops are weak compared to crafted gear. Boss loot needs to be significantly buffed to make bossing exciting. — FilterBlade/NeverSink

Combat Balance & Character Power

  • Movement Speed Nerfs: Movement and attack speed need to be toned down to prevent specific builds (like Deadeye) from dominating solely because “speed is king.” — Off Meta Michael
  • Shift Power to the Tree: Character power is currently too reliant on gear. Power should be redistributed into the Passive Tree and Support Gems to make progression smoother. — LocalIdentity
  • Buff Left-Side Tree: Reduce drawbacks on 2-Handed and Mace nodes to make Strength-based archetypes more viable. — Community Member
  • Revert Boss HP Nerfs: Recent nerfs to boss HP (32m -&gt; 14m) went too far; top-end challenges are needed to test finished builds. — Farham
  • Fill Missing Archetypes: Add missing skill support, such as Cold/Lightning attacks for Maces or a movement skill for Crossbows. — OmegaSybers

UX, QoL & Performance

  • Reduce Visual Clutter: Remove excessive ground effects to improve gameplay readability. — Off Meta Michael
  • Performance Optimization: Stability and crash fixes are the #1 priority, as performance issues are the main driver for players quitting. — Community, *Sir Edward V , blazeworks tv & K*altorrak
  • Bulk Juice Application: Applying Delirium or other juice to maps currently requires hundreds of clicks. A bulk application system is needed to save players’ wrists. — KingKongor
  • Delirium Visual Update: The constant grayscale fog in Delirium maps is exhausting. The fog should be limited to patches around mirrors or introduced via an animation so the map retains its color. — Lex D
  • Hybrid Input: Enable seamless switching between Controller and Mouse/Keyboard without having to relog or change settings. — Bruno

New Content Requests

  • Trial of the Ancestors: Add TOTA as the 3rd Ascension trial, including the return of Tattoos for passive tree customization. — Dreamcore
  • Aspirational Content: Add Tier 17-style maps or similar high-end content to justify min-maxing characters. — LocalIdentity
  • Missing Weapons & Classes: Add Swords, the remaining Ascendancy classes, and more uncut gems in the campaign. — Community Member

BTW, amazing response from Blazeworks tv!
