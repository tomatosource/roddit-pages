Tips for End Game Farming

Hi All - I wanted to put together a list of tips for endgame farming. List is mainly for myself, but I figured why not build and share with community. These tips are intended for level 95+ juiced farming as I am triple socket boots and gloves on meta junkie LA deadeye. Here is what i have so far:

  • Do not backtrack for exalts, splinters, vaal orbs, and other 1ex currency during/after a breach. I do not fully recommend disabling them, as these are basically pennies on the ground, but it is not worth your time to go out of your way to pick them up.
  • Disable all T5 Weapon and Armor Rares in your filter. A-C Tier T4-T5 Magics can be worth a check with your Scrolls of Wisdom, but Rares themselves are so unlikely to be good that it is not worth the pickup. Crafting is just too good this season.
  • Skip Blacksmith, Armourer, and Arcane Strongboxes. They are never worth the time. Only exception to this is if they are right next to a Ritual Altar, in which case you can double up and no reason not to.
  • Spend minimal to no time checking non-currency items in Ritual Alters. I would hover over anything that looks like it could be a Unique, and personally I check for Gemini/Warmonger Bows, but chance for item to be T1-T2 really kills efficiency. 9.9 times out of 10, you should just check currency and reroll. Bases can be bought on market and will almost always be better than what you could find in a Ritual Altar.
  • Fully clear Abyssal Depths. You get most of the good loot from the chests in the main room, but elites and other boxes regularly drop high value currency items and omens.

Please add any time in comments to help build out this list. Also open to discuss if anyone disagrees with the points listed above.
