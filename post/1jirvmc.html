Kawhi and the clippers

As i watched the clippers game last night it really stands out how good kawhi truly is. The man is borderline automatic from mid range, he’s so calm and clutch. He made a few great plays on defense. I hate the duality of his situation. The clippers could absolutely go on a deep playoff run and maybe even compete for the chip because of kawhi. And the clippers will absolutely not go on a deep run and compete for a chip because of kawhi. He’s so damn good, what a shame that he has the injury bug so bad. As a raps fan I’m truly grateful for what he did for us. I’m sure clippers fans are just in state of purgatory with him.
