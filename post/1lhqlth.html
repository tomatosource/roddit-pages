After PoE2, i can’t play any PoE anymore.

I played PoE2 on launch, and fell in love with it. Beautiful graphics, satisfying gameplay, fun leveling in acts: the standart tale. The thing is: i disliked a lot of its core aspects, such as endgame structure, how “slower” the builds felt, less build variety, and i also missed my favorite contents, such as blight.

So i went back to PoE1. Except i didnt. After playing PoE2, going back to the first game felt HORRIBLE. It felt clunky and ugly. Suddently, the game i played for years felt like a PS1 game. It is not responsive and i kept clicking things by accident after being accustomed to only using directional movement. The acts were brutally boring, trying alternative leveling builds felt like a death sentence, and i couldn’t get to my loved endgame before dropping the game.

So now i have no game to play. PoE2 feels incomplete and kinda slow. PoE1 feels clunky and frustrating.

May god help me.
