Consuming Questions just feels so bad… This needs fixed.

As an Acolyte, we don’t have much going for us in all honesty. We need a rework very very badly.

A lot of people talk about Darkness and while I fully agree, that needs a look, Consuming Questions is also ripe for a fix.

The idea is that all damage is leeched for mana then this skill recharges your ES and due to that, you drop recharge. That’s fine and I’m happy with that, the issue is that your leech does not send over capped mana to your shield.

To fix this you need MoM or skills that send damage to your Mana, you can do that, but without a high pool then you are out of luck. Eldritch Battery removes your ES so that just makes it useless in that regard.

While acolyte doesn’t have mana problems in almost all cases, we should get around 15-25% over capped mana back as ES. I understand not 100%, especially given how fast we are, we would never drop our ES then. Currently I can go into a fight, hit, and maybe get 10-80 ES back if my mana isn’t capped.

As I said, with damage to mana this opens that gate a bit more but overall that solution isn’t the best when you regain mana so fast.

The best way to go would be to get ES from Leech as 100% of the regained mana when under cap. Anything over cap should be 15-25%. This keeps friction in the mix and makes you build to take some mana damage but it also makes this skill useful if you don’t have points to invest to take damage as mana.

Darkness…. That’s another topic…..
