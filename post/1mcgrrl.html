Olroth Heroic Tragedy Jewel makes Acrobatics Viable

We probably all agree that the 75% less evasion that comes with Acrobatic is a massive blow, which often encourages players to instead opt for the unique body armor with one-time acrobatic effect instead. But, there is a secret way! Place an Olroth Heroic Tragedy Jewel in the jewel slot near CI keystone and pick the keystone. It gives you 80-100% increased evasion assuming you have 160-200 intelligence, which is a massive help and makes acrobatic viable. There are not many useful notables in that area anyway. So, the number doesn’t matter much. You can finally have an evasion/energy shield body armor + acrobatics! Just make sure you get mana on some gears/rings. I’ve been having so much fun with this recently.
