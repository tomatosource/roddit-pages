LVL 96 HCSSF Review - Gear/Crafting/Monster Tuning Solid Shape

Retiring my 96 Titan on HCSSF and wanted to give feedback on the state of the game.

First, play HCSSF if you want a challenge and old school gaming grind feel. I can see why people who want to zoom won’t like it but for a fun time sink, its worth it. I did a post stating to try it a while ago, after playing to 96, I can confirm its one of the funnier ARPG leagues I have done(despite the lack of end game content even)

Second, gear/crafting is in really solid shape. I think stripping the game to the basics and then building forward from there was the right call, I look forward to the small additions they are going to make. You can check out my Titan(BtheG) on POE 2 Ninja, I got lucky at 91 with an Astramentis from Alva but other then that it was mostly earned through “Crafting” / Expedition and I am happy where I ended up. I love POE 1, but thinking about going back to that bloated crafting system always intimidates me each season.

Third, monster tuning is in great shape. Outside a couple of bosses(which you can avoid/overlevel for), if you are dying to one shots - you are either undergeared/underleveled/overjuiced the zone. Every close call I had was because I pushed it to the limit.

Fixes

1)Level 20 uncut skill gem drops way to low. I farmed a ton of iiq/iir(50⁄150) level 81+ zones and somehow didn’t get a single one. I think these should be able to drop from level 80+ zones, with the rarity of drop rate I don’t think they will be to plentiful. I had better luck with them dropping on trade league, so I might have been insanely unlucky.

  1. Perfect Jeweler Orbs/Greater Jeweler- Seem slightly low? I only got 1 perfect/12 greater , not sure if it should be much higher drop rate but some method of gambling to get a six link after you put in the time would be nice. I never hit a 6 link vaal.

  2. Armour Scraps - I was still having to farm these at 96 by scrapping quality gear, this was annoying please just up drop rate in higher tier maps instead of transmutes maybe?

TLDR- HCSSF in great shape, monsters don’t kill you to easily, gear/crafting baseline solid, 2 minor high level gem fixes.

Thanks for your time. Have a great day.
