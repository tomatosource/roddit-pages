No one is talking about it, but leaguestarting in a party is absolutely miserable

One thing I haven’t seen discussed much around here is how horrible playing in a party at leaguestart is, and I feel like the topic deserves its own discussion.

TLDR at the end.

My brother and I have played thousands of hours of POE1 (I’m close to 13000 for example) and it’s become a ritual for us to group up for a leaguestart and play together. We don’t do party-oriented builds or anything, just the build each of us wants to play, but we go through the campaign and sometimes early maps together regardless.

We know that playing in a group without optimizing for group play means we are slower than if we both bolted through the campaign on our own, but we know the game well enough for the increased difficulty to not matter much and we enjoy playing and chatting together on these occasions.

In comes POE2 : like 1, POE2 is harder when you are playing in a group without optimizing for it, and we are not as experienced or knowledgeable about the game, so we struggle a bit more, but that is ok.

It also happens that my brother played Spear Huntress, which sucked, and I played Minions Lich, which sucked, so the campaign was a real struggle, painful and unfun.

But let’s put all that aside for a bit, because this is all circumstantial, builds were broken, monsters too hard, whatever. That’s not what I want to adress here.

My main point is : key mechanics of group play make absolutely zero fucking sense and ruin the fun of playing with your friends.

  • Having to wait for your friend to resurrect you and being unable to play for a very long time because you died, and he is surrounded by 150 monsters, is really, really annoying. It makes sense for bosses, but just for normal questing? No way. This has got to change for sure.

  • Collisions with other players characters are super annoying too. Who thought this was a good idea? This isn’t DotA, I don’t wanna be bodyblocked by my buddy, what the hell?

In general, I don’t understand why these efforts are being taken to ensure that nobody enjoys playing in a group. The game is already more difficult if you play with several people (unless you use support builds and the like), so I really don’t see the point of keeping these annoying features which ruin the fun.

My brother already quit the game saturday. I wanted to see the endgame, so I pushed on a little further. But these things need to go. Hope Ziz sees this and adresses those concerns with Mark/Jonathan.

TLDR : playing in a party is annoying as shit because of characters collisions and not being able to resurrect on your own when you die
