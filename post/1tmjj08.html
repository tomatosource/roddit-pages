I’m going over the trades from the 2018 era Marlins firesale and I am in awe of how terrible they are

JT Realmuto: February 7, 2019: Traded by the Miami Marlins to the Philadelphia Phillies for Will Stewart (minors), Jorge Alfaro, Sixto Sánchez and international bonus slot money.

Giancarlo Stanton: December 11, 2017: Traded by the Miami Marlins to the New York Yankees for Starlin Castro, José Devers and Jorge Guzmán.

Christian Yelich: January 25, 2018: Traded by the Miami Marlins to the Milwaukee Brewers for Lewis Brinson, Isan Díaz, Monte Harrison and Jordan Yamamoto.

Like…that’s three borderline HOF caliber players for jack shit. Nothing resembling a middling-good MLB player in the lot. And I’m leaving off lesser guys like Justin Bour, Adeiny Hechavarria and Dee Gordon who were also traded for peanuts.

The only exception is Marcell Ozuna, who yielded Sandy Alcantara and Zac Gallen (flipped for Jazz Chisholm, also a decent trade). But even so, taking all that as a group it’s still pretty bad and has crippled the franchise to this day.

Boy, I sure hope someone got fired for that blunder
