The Darkness and how to approach buffing it

How it works:

  • Spirit disabled as a drawback;
  • Scales with levels (10 base Darkness per level, boosted to 12 with both small nodes);
  • Second Notable allows for (capped) Chaos resistance scaling in ratio of 1% per 1%.
  • Capped max value ceiling at 2310 on 90% Chaos Resistance (2145 at 75%);
  • Takes damage before Life and Energy Shield;
  • Every damage instance taken on Darkness is put on a separate internal 5s timer, allowing for mid-combat recharge.

Why we cannot uncap it?

  • Because with mid-combat recharge, it would mean literal invulnerability at some point.

Why does it cost all the Spirit?

  • Because GGG sees it thematically fitting that way. (that is the only argumentation from GGG in that regard that I was able to find)

Why does it feel bad?

  • It’s very dependent and drawback packed:

- 4 Asc points to make it work + 2 additional ones for [Chayula’s Gift] to make it optimal;
- Needs external Chaos Resistance sources (which also is disabling CI entirely);
- The Spirit disabling once again (we are getting new Amulet base which will allow for Spirit ability though).

What do players (mostly) want:

  • Disconnecting the Spirit loss, or limiting its consumption to the innate 100 Spirit. (Knowing GGG it would come with changes to how its reservation work and we do not want that)

How can we make it better (realistically)?

  • Merge the [Chayula’s Gift] with [The Deepening Shadows] and add a new Notable in the former’s place;
  • Increase the ceiling cap closer to ~2500 (it is still within the safe value, not making it too much)
  • Add a new interaction on top - Ex. Increase your Evasion by % of currently Unreserved Darkness, Double the value when no Darkness is Reserved. (opens up new builds synergy which suits the Darkness nicely - and possibly thematically as you cover yourself in the Darkness, which increase your elusiveness - easy to build the lore on top of that)

May the Chonk be with You.
