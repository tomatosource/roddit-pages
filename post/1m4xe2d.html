Spirit breakpoints needs fixing

The fact that there are 3 hard breakpoints (30, 60, 100) for all spirit gems except minion ones - creates unnecessary breakpoints. This creates number of problems, i.e. T1 roll on armour is being far superior compared to T2.

Potential solution can be is that you still can equip gems that have requirement of X spirit, but if you don’t have enough spirit you lose some efficiency of the gem.

Another solution can be is to introduce many more pieces of gear that add 10-30 spirit to even out requirements for spirit gems.
