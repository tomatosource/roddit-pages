There is no way Holten Estate was tested on melee characters.

This map is an absolute horrendous experience for slow moving melee characters:

  1. The mobs are all from the set of the stagger heavy, hazard ground heavy, and machine gun heavy monsters from the end of Act 1. This wouldn’t be inherently terrible if not for the fact that…
  2. The map is comprised of a series of the smallest hallways yet. Fissures and AoE slams are constantly cut off by walls and odd tile collision. Monsters swarm and body block you into corners and deadends. Walking into a hallway will have monsters from multiple small angles spamming long range projectiles at you immediately that can light stun you into immediate death.
  3. The camera due to the above is awful. There are several parts of the map where walls completely obscure your character and approaching enemies.
  4. The checkpoints are at the start of every floor of the estate. If you die you have to restart the absolute slog of slowly poking through the clusterfuck of a map all over again.

The act 2⁄3 interludes were fine, but the act 1 interlude was awful. Don’t even get me started on the overuse of the corn/wheat field. Whoever thought that this was good to add as an endgame map (Razed Fields) is a joke.
