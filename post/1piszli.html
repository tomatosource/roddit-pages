Power Physical Lich

Let’s discuss the Physical Spell Lich (Bone Storm/Bone Cage) build.

While the Lich’s Ascendancy skills themselves saw no changes in this patch, there are several significant alterations related to Power Charges that could make the Lich substantially more powerful:

  • Power Charge Generation: We now have a new Lineage, Guatelitzi’s Ablation, which states: ‘Offerings from Supported Skills grant you a Power Charge every 4 seconds.’ If we combine Bone Cage + Combat Frenzy + Resonance, the amount of generated Charges could be quite plentiful. The Ascendancy skill ‘Price of Power’ could potentially grant 40% more damage with sustained Power Charges.
  • Tornado: Since both Bone Storm and Bone Cage have a high base Critical Strike Chance of 15%, we can easily reach a 90%+ Critical Strike Chance cap. Utilizing Cast on Crit was difficult in the previous patch because we lacked a high-damage physical spell to effectively optimize the damage output. Tornado might be a suitable choice for this purpose.
  • Spell Totem: With the Lich build having an abundant supply of Power Charges, using a Spell Totem seems relatively straightforward. Spell Totems generally work better with Channeling Spells, so I believe using Bone Storm would be a fitting synergy.

We do not yet have the new Passive Tree to confirm the existence of new Power Charge generating mechanics, but based on the current information, the Physical Spell Lich promises to be a very promising build.
