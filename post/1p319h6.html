Swords and the Duelist Class are Confirmed to be next after the Druid

Jonathan and Mark mentioned in the ZiggyD interview that Swords and the Duelist will be coming after the Druid, here are the direct quotes from the interview:

ZiggyD:

“Do you have a vision for who will be next after the Druid?”

GGG Jonathan:

“I’ve always said that it would be criminal to go into the full release without having swords”

GGG Mark:

“Yeah, It’s a bit criminal to get this far without any swords”

GGG Jonathan:

“So that would be an obvious candidate”

ZiggyD:

“People can infer that it’s some sort of duelist or something, most likely.”

GGG Jonathan:

(Laughing) “Yeah”

Source: 5:24-5:55

This is as close to an official confirmation that Duelist and Swords are the next class and weapons coming to Path of Exile 2 after the Druid (In Path of Exile 1, the Sword is the Duelist connected weapon).
