Lamelo Ball is averaging the most FGA per 36 minutes since Wilt Chamberlain in 1963

Lamelo is having quite the historic season with field goal attempts. He is averaging the most FGA per game this season with 24.5. To put things into perspective Kobe only put up more than 24.5 one season and he would play 40 minutes per game in his prime. Lamelo only plays 33.8 minutes per game.

  3. Lamelo Ball 2024-2025 - 26.2

  6. George Garvin 1981-82 - 25.39
  7. Freeman Williams 1980-81 - 25.16
  8. Michael Jordan 1986-87 - 25.01
