Our Commentators

I am just curious if I am alone in not being able to stand Scal & Drew. I didn’t grow up in Boston and the local NHL broadcasts were so different - the commentators approached the game more objectively. They called what they saw, not what they wanted to see, and referred to the team by their name rather than “we.” They had extra insights into the home team since they commentated nearly every game and had exclusive player interviews, but a fan of the opposing team could listen to the on-ice commentary and might not know which team the crew is from.

For a long time I didn’t get to watch many Celtics games other than the national games, but now I have League Pass. I find watching the Celtics’ local broadcasts infuriating because of the persistent bias, to the point where I often mute it. To be fair it is similar with a decent amount of other local broadcast teams in the NBA, but the Cs’ crew is a step beyond. Scal complains about every call against the Celtics no matter how obvious it is. I desperately wish we had better commentators.
