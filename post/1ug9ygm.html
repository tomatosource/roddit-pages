If you have Gem Enthusiast allocated, you should know …

The text for Gem Enthusiast says:

5% increased maximum life if you have at least 10 red support gems socketed

5% increased maximum mana if you have at least 10 blue support gems socketed

5% increased movement speed if you have at least 10 green support gems socketed

What it doesn’t say is that it needs to be 10 DIFFERENT gems.

Edit: I have been corrected. It’s actually a weapon set issue, if you have gems socketed into implicit skills granted by one weapon set, they will not be counted when the other set is active.

I was doing an audit of my tree and character sheet and noticed that I wasn’t getting the move speed bonus even though my skill sheet said I had 11 green gems. After I allocated a few more random green gems into Raise Shield it activated properly.

Maybe GGG didn’t update this text after they changed to allow multiple copies of the same support in different skills.
