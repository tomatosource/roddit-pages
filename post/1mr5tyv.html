Unpopular Take on ACT 4 Release

Honestly, Act 4 alone, does not give me any hype and could even feel not much different than having no new act at all.

Duplicating acts in Cruel mode, breaks immersion and makes the game tedious, it is a grind we’ve already done for two seasons.

In my view, resources would be better spent on endgame content and a new class release. Act 4 will only be one or two hours out of many anyway.

Of course, I would take if this ACT 4 or nothing, but if it is the case I am not hyped for it, as it won’t be a major driver for my experience.
