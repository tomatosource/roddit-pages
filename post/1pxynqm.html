How much should we consider postseason / All-Star performance when evaluating HoF candidates?

We routinely use regular season stats when evaluating Hall of Fame candidates, but usually not postseason. Many people feel that baseball is such a team sport that you shouldn’t penalize an Ernie Banks or Ken Griffey Jr. or Ryne Sandberg for not getting to a postseason.

On the other hand there are some bubble candidates who got in — Jack Morris and Bill Mazeroski for example — pretty much for what they did in the World Series.

And while people dismiss the All-Star Game as just an exhibition game, when people are talking about Hall of Fame candidates they will often say he was an eight-time All-Star or he never was an All-Star as a point in favor or against.

So does this stuff matter or not, when you’re evaluating candidates?
