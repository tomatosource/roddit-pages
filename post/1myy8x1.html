Knight Captain Mercenary concept

This is just going to be a me musing about a build concept I came up with at release. It doesn’t really work, but it feels like the kind of thing that should with what I understand to be their vision for poe2. That’s mostly why I wanted to make the post.

Here’s the pitch: A Mercenary rejects the otherworldly powers offered at every turn, instead looking to assert the dominance of the common person. The weak standing against the strong on their own terms, by their own might.

Thus, a minor knighthood order is formed and any willing to take up arms and shun the demonic, the godly, or other such super-natural entities is welcome.

Mechanically, we’re looking for combined arms of human power and technology. Foot soldiers, crossbow men, maybe some notable figures with specific styles, ballistas, banners, grenades, a mount. That type of stuff.

Most of this is present, and some parts work pretty well. Lots of holes tho.

We’re splitting the damage 3 ways with this build, between minions, totems, and the player. We can somewhat combat this with generalist AoE buffs like banners and by focusing the player and the totems on utility. Ripwire and the new grenade totem for instance, as well as armor break, stun grenades, and possibly oil slick and the new high velocity rounds from the player.

Some things are much harder to fix tho.

The soldiers available are either ghosts (spectres) and currently terrible or skeletons. Hopefully the patch will at least make the spectre options not terrible. Still, it’d be really nice thematically if we could have human followers, even if it were just a cosmetic.

The mount is of low usefulness compared to its cost for this build. Too low even for such a heavy flavor focus in my experience from last patch.

Your utility is still based on your damage.

Fair amount of travel to get to any decent quantity of minion nodes on the tree. Another “we’ll see” with the upcoming changes.

And the big one, a minion build with no sceptre or even shield. That one really stings with the way minions are setup in poe2. It’s a massive loss of both spirit and minion buffs, enough to pretty much just snuff the concept.

What would it take to make functional then? Not too much.

A simple MTX to remove the spectre effect or a total reskin for skeletons to return them to life would be nice thematically, but mechanically all it really needs is one handed crossbows. There’s other ways to solve the problem, and I still don’t like having to hold a sceptre, but one handed crossbows hit multiple areas at once.

They would mean lower damage, which does hurt this kind of utility, but not enough to make it non-functional. For non-utility based uses tho, the lower damage would be very meaningful, so hopefully that would be enough to not make too many waves.

They would also mean you could use a sceptre or shield in the off hand, allowing you to maintain the spirit and buffs from those items. Absolutely vital to any minion build.

Not that any of this is going to be added on my account, but imo both the thematic and mechanical cores of this concept fit the game and I’d love to see something like them. Crossbows have so much utility – it’s fun to lean into the armor breaking, pinning, and bannering supportive style without playing multiplayer. Maybe one day.
