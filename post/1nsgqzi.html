Record Prediction

50 wins

The consensus appears to be .500 record, give or take. 50-32 is well above that, and I’ll tell you why.

Tatum. Dude is playing for real by early 2026, maybe over half of the season.

Even if Tatum wasn’t playing, I think this team is built for 45 wins. They have a phenomenal, fanatical coach, the East is garbage, and every single player will try their asses off because they each have something to prove in their own way. Jaylen Brown as a number one option - Derrick White as a number two option - Pritchard being a go-to scoring option - they have all achieved their respective place through sheer effort, and it will be incredible to see their leap this year. Everyone else is fighting for a place in the league and will play their asses off. Plus, knowing that Tatum is coming back not just early, but strong, there’s going to be some excitement surrounding the team.

tl;dr- Tatum’s early return plus “intangibles” - energy and effort - is going to push the C’s to 50 wins in a very weak Eastern Conference
