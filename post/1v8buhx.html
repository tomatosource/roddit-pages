The Nats offense is a having a monster July

The Nationals offense has been a story all year with their shocking overperformance. While you probably know that they lead the MLB in runs scored, you probably don’t realize that they’ve found a whole new level in July. This month, the nats rank: 2nd in R, 2nd in RBI, 1st in HR, 1st (tied) in SB, 2nd in BB%, 3rd in K%, 1st in AVG, 1st in OBP, 1st in SLG, 1st in ISO, 1st in OPS, 1st in wRC+, and 1st in fangraphs offensive runs above average. Due to being the best team in the MLB at baserunning, getting on base, and hitting for power they have a ridiculous lead of 50.5 to 20.3 over the second-place padres in runs above average this month.

Earlier in the season, the nats had the underlying metrics of a top 4-6ish offense and relied on LOB luck to lead the MLB in runs. Many people myself included expected them to regress to the 10th best offense or so, but instead the opposite has happened and now the underlying metrics have them as by far the best offense this season.
