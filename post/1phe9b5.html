Who’s a random/obscure player that is a hero to you because they were nice to you when you were a kid?

When I was young, my family took a trip to Arizona to follow the Cubs around Spring Training for a few days. As a non-Chicagoan who didn’t get up to Wrigley very often, this was a huge deal for ten year old me. I spent the weeks leading up to the trip trying to decide which cards of mine I wanted Kerry Wood, Derrek Lee, etc. to sign. I just assumed that I’d be able to waltz on over to like Nomar on some backfield and get a picture. I was pumped. That unique pumped you usually only get as a kid.

Well obviously there were tons of other ten year old me’s there running around trying to meet the players. I didn’t know where to stand and even when I did, I couldn’t make my way to the front of the crowd. I still remember staring at the blank ball I had brought along to get signed as we were packing up the hotel on our last day. It was just straight up dejection. Again, the unique dejection you usually only get as a kid.

Before heading to the airport, we made one last gasp attempt to meet a player by driving to the complex and hanging out in the back where guys were running drills. It’s been 20 years but I swear I can still vividly remember Jason Dubois finishing up, grabbing his stuff off the bench and walking towards my younger brother and I along the fence. He came over and talked to us for a minute or two. Signed our baseballs and then walked away. I’m not even kidding, it was like talking to Batman. He felt completely larger than life to us.

Anyway, Jason Dubois (210 career ABs, -.8 WAR) will forever be a hero in my family. I remember my brother and I wanting to invite him to Thanksgiving
