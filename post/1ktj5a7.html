My very important thoughts so far

I don’t think it’s anything that hasn’t been said before and I’m just some random dude sharing his irrelevant opinions.

I’d like to say right off the bat that I think this will become the greatest ARPG of all time once it’s fully finished and polished.

Campaign is currently a little tedious and mobs are too oppressive but I think once the new acts come in and they hopefully make area levels smaller, it will be much better. I like going from boss to boss and I thought all the boss fights were fair. If we’re going to be playing this campaign every 3 months, it does desperately need to be smoothed out and shortened. I think it took me somewhere between 20-30 hours.

Also wanted to mention graphics. I’m playing on the Xbox Series S and the game currently looks really bad graphics-wise. If they can optimize graphics for the Series S close to D4 levels, that would be amazing. Also some random bugs and crashes that I’m sure will get smoothed out.

I understand that they haven’t put much work into the endgame but I’m not enjoying mapping and I dislike the tower system. It feels like a slog. I was running delirious 6-mod maps juiced by 3-4 towers and just didn’t find it very rewarding or fun. Moved onto Sekhema and ToC farming for now.

Some random thoughts:

Would love it if they got rid of scrolls of wisdom for good.

And finally, I think it would be amazing if they expanded the currency exchange into an instant buyout trade system.

Overall, really looking forward to see what GGG does with the game over the coming months and years.
