  1. Brandon Jennings with 55 against the Knicks on 11/14/2009 (20 years and 52 days)

  2. LeBron James with 56 against the Raptors on 3/20/2005 (20 years and 80 days)

  3. Devin Booker with 70 against the Celtics on 3/24/2017 (20 years and 145 days)

  4. Victor Wembanyama with 50 against the Wizards on 11/13/2024 (20 years and 314 days)
