Revisiting the Desmond Bane trade with the Jrue Holiday trade in mind

Desmond Bane is a very good offensive player that Orlando overpaid slightly for and honestly most people like that move for Orlando. He was traded for the following:

  • 4 FRP, 1 FRP Swap
  • 2 Salary Fillers

Bane has great stats and is essentially worth these picks. Banes averages for the last 2 seasons:

  • 20.9 PPG 5.4 REB 5.4 AST on .475/.387/.883

Now lets quickly shift to Simons averages for the last 2 seasons

  • 20.6 PPG 3.1 REB 5.1 AST on .428/.372/.909

Simons is really not that far off from Bane. Yes, his 2 Point FG is a lot lower, but consider that Bane got to play next to JJJ/Ja Morant most of the season and in general a much better team, and Simons was the Focus of opponents defense on a pretty bad team. Simons is more of a deep threat, while Bane is a little bigger and can score in paint a little easier.

I believe the disparity in how the market viewed these players is wild.

Bane, required 5 FRP to get.

Simons came with 2 free second round picks. And we are talking a couple percetnage points on 2point shots and a couple of rebounds difference.

I bet next season they both have very similar stats if not Simons outplaying Bane offensively in the Mazulla ball system.

TLDR: We got a Bane-esq type player + picks for a lot lot cheaper than what Orlando had to pay
