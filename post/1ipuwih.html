[Nick DePaula] Bucks’ Andre Jackson Jr. Signs Shoe Deal With Adidas Ahead of NBA All-Star Dunk Contest

“BREAKING: Bucks high flier Andre Jackson Jr. has signed a shoe deal with Adidas ahead of the Slam Dunk Contest. Look for @andrejackson111 to support Adidas’ lifetime athlete Damian Lillard’s signature series and lace up this ‘Outer Limits’ Dame 9 in the Dunk Contest.”

Jackson, known for his athleticism, will debut the Adidas partnership during All-Star Weekend. Teammate Damian Lillard—an Adidas cornerstone—endorsed the move by retweeting DePaula’s announcement. The “Outer Limits” Dame 9 pays homage to Lillard’s 2014 game-winner against Houston.
