Who’s stands tall as the best true little man of all time?

Average height of an American male is 5 ft 9 in so to objectively be considered “little” you have to be below that height…so who was the best player of all time who was BELOW average in height? So 5 ft 8 in and under is the assignment.

I believe Muggsy Bogues, Earl Boykins and Spud Webb are the strongest contenders. All had remarkably long careers (14, 13, 12 seasons respectively) and put up similar numbers as you can see by the graphic and its more than fair to say that they all were athletic freaks of nature..especially Spud and Muggsy. IMO, it’s a toss up between those two but I had to include Earl cuz he absolutely deserves to be mentioned.

Spud beat Dominique head to head to win the 1986 Dunk Contest at 5’6 (listed as 5’7 but was originally listed as 5’6 which means he is actually 5’6) weighing only 133 lbs as a rook…likely the most impressive feat of athleticism in NBA history, up there with VC’s 2000 dunk contest performance. His vert was an outrageous 42-46 inches (depending on source). He was the best scorer of the three and averaged the most pts per game in a season and for his career and arguably had the most well rounded overall game…his overall numbers do reflect as much.

Muggsy had a 44-48 in vert (depending on source) and could allegedly dunk at 5’3 but with a lot of difficulty due to his small hands…absolute freak. Earl and Spud were superior scorers and shooters than Muggsy and both put up multiple seasons averaging in the teens in ppg but Muggsy averaged a double double in pts and ast his best season and nearly did it in two other seasons as well so he was undoubtedly the best pure PG with much superior court vision and passing ability out of the three..he led the league in Assist to Turnover ratio multiple times and was a better defender..consistently amongst the league leaders in steals. He also has the highest VORP (value over replacement) at 17.9 which is insane and played 14 seasons to Spud’s 12. And again, he was 5’3

Who stands tall as the best little man of all time?

My vote goes to Muggsy but I can’t argue with anyone who believes it’s Spud. I believe Spud doesn’t get enough credit and is more underrated in general than Muggsy. Muggsy being cartoonishly small and the shortest of all time is likely why he’s more known than Spud imo.

Honorable mention : Isaiah Thomas would win hands down but unfortunately for him he stands at 5’9 tall so he’s not below average in height and at that point you almost have to bring AI into the discussion cuz I honestly believe he is more like 5’10.5 so it quickly becomes a slippery slope…so they don’t count
