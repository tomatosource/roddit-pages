Am I the only one that doesn’t like how skill gems are handled in POE 2?

After hearing the Cohh interview with Jonathan, he mentioned that he didn’t think anyone disliked how gems are socketed into a separate page compared to how they were socketed into gear in POE 1.

I hate basically everything about how POE 2 handles skill gems and I”m curious to know how others feel.

Key issues I have:

  • Being able to level gems without them being tied to a drop table in higher level areas.
  • Being able to use the same support multiple times if I want.
  • Having essentially infinite 6 links feels like you’re taking away the identify of my build. I like having my build being defined by the 1 6 link skill I wanna use.
  • I don’t like Skill Gems / Supports dropping as uncut gems. It’s just kinda boring when the same repetitive item drops. I like having individual gems drop. This also prevents gems from dropping with quality.
  • I enjoying using Jeweller’s, Chromes, and Fusings to “finalize” my gear. There’s also a level of mastery involved with socketing gear (like using the 3-4 socket bench crafting to get 4 off colors) that no longer exists with the current system. Also no ability to have things like white sockets which is a missed element of “coolness” to have on gear.
  • 6 Link being tied to the gem rather than the gear just feels worse to me. We’re way more locked into the skills we have now once they’re 6 Linked. In POE 1 I can just swap some gems around if I wanna change up my build. Now I need to invest multiple divines to get a new 6 Link for every skill gem i wanna try. And going to my previous point, I enjoy having to socket my gear, even 6 Linking.
  • I like how socket colors are defined by the base items stats (Dex = more likely for green etc). There’s an extra level of consideration to make when looking at gear because of this.
  • Stat requirements on gems are WAY too high right now.
  • The UI for selecting support gems feels really clunky.
  • I just like them better on gear.
