The statistically most entertaining games of the 2024-25 season

There’s this cool website called inpredictable that takes every game of the season and let’s you dive deep into how it stacks up against other games. It has three main metrics that it uses to grade games. All of them are based on numbers, so there’s no judgement used when deciding which games are higher up.

Excitement

The first is the Excitement. For this one, it takes the win probability graph of the game and finds the game where that graph wavered the most. A game with high excitement is likely a game where both teams had relatively high win probabilities at several points in the game. The top 5 games by Excitement are below (the results are spoiler tagged in case you want to go back and watch).

  1. April 1, 2025 - Timberwolves @ Nuggets

!The Timberwolves win this one in double overtime 140-139 despite a Nikola Jokic 61-point triple double. At the end of regulation, Jokic hits a couple free throws to tie it. At the end of 1OT, Jokic hits a floater to tie it. And then at the end of 2OT, Westbrook commits a foul with no time on the clock. Nickeil Alexander-Walker hits 2 of 3 free throws to win the game.!&lt;

  1. March 12, 2025 - Knicks @ Trail Blazers

!Mikal Bridges hits a game-winning three in OT giving the Knicks the win 114-113.!&lt;

  1. December 6, 2024 - Lakers @ Hawks

!Trae Young hits the game-winning three in OT with 8 seconds to go after his shot is blocked by Max Christie at the end of regulation. Hawks win 134-132. !&lt;

  1. January 6, 2025 - Heat @ Kings

!Keon Ellis goes 1 of 2 from the line with no time remaining to keep the game tied at the end of 1OT. Then in 2OT, some DeMar DeRozan heroics help the Kings win the game 123-118.!&lt;

  1. October 27, 2024 - 76ers @ Pacers

!Tyrese Haliburton sinks a 3 to tie the game and send it to OT. Then in OT, the 76ers pull away thanks to some Tyrese Maxey free throws.!&lt;

Tension

The next way they grade games is based on Tension. A game with high Tension is a game where the win probability never shifts greatly in either direction until the end of the game. High tension games are always close for the entire game. Here are the top 5 games by Tension:

  1. November 6, 2024 - Pistons @ Hornets

!The largest lead for either team in this one is 9 points. It ends with Brandon Miller putting back a Grant Williams miss at the buzzer to win it for the Hornets 108-107.!&lt;

  1. November 16, 2024 - Raptors @ Celtics

!The largest lead for either team was 10. Jayson Tatum hits a game-winning three at the buzzer to win in OT. Celtics win 126-123.!&lt;

  1. April 3, 2025 - Grizzlies @ Heat

!The largest lead for any team is 9. Ja Morant hits a game-winning shot at the buzzer to give Memphis the win 110-108.!&lt;

  1. March 8, 2025 - Pistons @ Warriors

!Draymond Green hits a three late in the 4th to go up 2, and then some Curry free throws ice the game. Largest lead for either team is 9. Warriors win 115-110.!&lt;

  1. December 9, 2024 - Knicks @ Raptors

!Largest lead for either team is 11. Karl-Anthony Towns hits two buckets in the final minute to give New York the win.!&lt;

Comeback

The last metric Inpredictable used is Comeback. This is exactly what it sounds like. The games where the winning team had the lowest win probability at some point earlier in the game. Here’s what that top 5 looks like:

  1. February 24, 2025 - Timberwolves @ Thunder

!The Timberwolves are down as much as 24 in the 4th quarter before they end up coming back and winning in overtime. Timberwolves win 131-128.!&lt;

  1. May 21, 2025 - Pacers @ Knicks

!In Game 1 of the ECF, the Pacers are down 14 with less than 3 minutes left but come back to win thanks to a miraculous Tyrese Haliburton shot to send the game to overtime. The Pacers end up winning in OT 138-135.!&lt;

  1. December 27, 2024 - Timberwolves @ Rockets

!The Timberwolves are down 12 with 3 minutes left but come back to beat the Rockets 113-112.!&lt;

  1. April 8, 2025 - Timberwolves @ Bucks

!The Bucks are down 24 in the 4th quarter but come back to win by 7. Bucks win 110-103.!&lt;

  1. May 6, 2025 - Pacers @ Cavaliers

!In Game 2 of the Second Round, The Pacers are down 7 with less than a minute to go, but once again Tyrese Haliburton leads the comeback and hits a game-winning shot. Pacers in 120-119.!&lt;

Number 7 on this list is also a Pacers playoff game: Game 5 against the Bucks. &gt;!Tyrese Haliburton hits another game winner in this one after the Bucks go up by 7 with 40 seconds to go. That means that of the top 7 most improbably wins this season, 3 are playoff games where the Pacers come back to win thanks to a Tyrese Haliburton game winner.!&lt;

If you want to dig around on this website, I highly suggest doing so, it’s pretty neat and there are a ton of features that you can play around with. Here’s the link again: inpredictable.com
