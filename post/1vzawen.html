Jeanie Buss and her attorney, Adam Streisand, have filed a petition to block her siblings’ attempted sale of the family’s Lakers shares.

A court hearing is scheduled for November 5, according to a filing that was publicly processed today.

Jeanie Buss is not only calling for the family’s vote of the Lakers shares to be deemed void, but for co-trustees Joey and Janie Buss be removed for “breach of fiduciary duty.” She calls for her siblings to be liable “for loss and damages” as well.

“Their conduct is not driven by financial necessity but by animosity toward Jeanie and in willful disregard for the (Trust), this Court’s 2017 Order, their fiduciary duties, and Dr. Buss’s express intent,” the petition reads.
