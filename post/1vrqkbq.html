We really need Templates

0.5 introduced Atlas Masters to the game, which, in addition to the massive overhaul to the endgame and atlas, contributed to this league being the absolute best when it comes to mapping. However, the best thing Atlas Masters did in my opinion, wasn’t introducing different ways to buff/interact with your maps, but it essentially added a quick-swap template setup to the game in a basic form.

When you want to play Jado, Hilda, or Doryani, you can have their 4 nodes that best fit what you want to do pre-set and you just click once on their icon and boom, you have those buffs affecting your maps. This system is extremely good and should be expanded on into other endgame systems. Think about how many juicing methods exist now and the different setups each require. You need different tablets + tree setup for Grand Expeditions, another setup for Ritual, another for City Delirium, another for boss rushing, etc. you get the idea. If you are someone who wants to continually play different strats and various setups, it’s tedious to swap tree, tablets, waystones etc each time, there has to be a more convenient way to do it and I really think Templates are the way to go.

The basic idea would be that you click a button or preset that you previously setup, and this allows you to swap to a “Grand Expeditions” template, or a “Gold Rota” template, or whoever you choose to name them. You store tablets and waystones relevant to that template within it and with one click, you change atlas keystone choices, atlas master, tablets, and waystone storage to that setup and you’re ready to go, you just have to setup the templates once and replenish them with tablets + waystones every once in a while (personally not a fan of tablets as they are now, especially them having roll ranges that you need to divine, but that’s a different topic).

Templates could be expanded into other aspects of the game as well, for example, Gemling has so many different build possibilities this league and say you want to play multiple styles, it would be really cool if you could swap a template and you go from a spark coc gemling to an ice shot one, your tree, gear, jewels, skills, all swap, even ascendancy maybe. This might be a bit ambitious and GGG probably has reservations against it, but I thought I’d bring it up regardless.

What do you think? Do you see any possible downsides or reasons for GGG to not implement a template system into the game in the future?
