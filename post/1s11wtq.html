II) First Ideas

Before testing the real effect of The Nameless One, I went through a few initial observations and hypotheses:

  1. It can be applied to almost any item type - weapons, armour, and off-hand items.
  2. It cannot be used on items with max quality, which immediately made me think it might be some kind of universal quality modifier.
  3. Based on that, my first theory was that it acts like a combined version of: Armourer’s Scrap + Blacksmith’s Whetstone + Arcanist’s Etcher. Basically, a “one-size-fits-all” quality currency (except for jewelry).

III) What actually happens

After testing, the effect was interesting: it provides a flat +6% quality to any of the applicable item types.

IV) Conclusion

At first glance, it looks like a simple utility item. But depending on how it’s balanced and obtained, this could become one of the most flexible crafting tools in PoE2 in the upcoming future.
