Trials should be focused on buffs, not debuffs

Trial of the Sekhemas and Trial of Chaos both apply a rogue-like formula. Other rogue-like games however, such as Hades, have never frustrated me like trials.

In a lot of rogue-like games, in each encounter you gain a buff of your choice, then you get to fight stronger enemies. It gives you a feeling of progress. However, trials in POE2 are designed to punish you instead of empower you. They keep assigning you new debuffs, weaken your character, and force you to take a cautious approach merely to survive among the white mobs that you wouldn’t even care in any other cases.

This is a huge mistake, GGG. You want the trials to be challenging? Maybe just give the enemies some buffs in each room, and stop adding debuffs like “you have no armour”, “you can’t see next room” or “a bomb will follow you and explode when you are stuck among mobs”.
