Mixed Wishlist after 168 Hours

Hello,

We all know there are a lot of issues with the game, and I’d like to offer some possible solutions for a few of them:

  1. Make blue items drop identified. This would make the process of pick → base → ID → augment → regal → recombine about 50% less painful.
  2. Tiered rares were GGG’s solution in POE1 for dealing with the flood of item drops, but it’s still not working—too RNG, mostly useless. Make tiered rare items drop smarter. For example, boots should always have movement speed, wands should be more likely to roll +level of spells or cast speed, etc.
  3. Map layouts ruin mechanics like Expedition and Ritual. They should spawn in open areas, not in tight corners or narrow corridors.
  4. Make Expedition cable longer—should be simple.
  5. Bring back old Ritual unique monsters into Ritual encounters. Also, make Ritual item rewards more useful.
  6. Add a Wisp node on the Atlas: “Possessed monsters start with 100 Empowerment.” When wisps make short trips to nearby rares, they don’t get enough empowerment. This, along with loot buffs for the Wisp mechanic, would help a lot.
  7. Make unique maps scale with towers and Atlas progression. Boss-only unique maps are a joke without tablets or Atlas investment.
  8. 168 hours played this league, still no Essence map. Make it less rare.
  9. Fractured Lake rewards are terrible. Let it drop better rings and amulets.
  10. Wands without +4/+5 and amulets without +3 are useless. Don’t bother making unique caster weapons without +spell levels. OR, better yet, scale down power: make wands cap at +2 like in POE1, amulets +1 max, and distribute skill level scaling more evenly from 1–23 instead of stacking 1–30.
  11. Campaign skippers unite. Make quests mandatory once per league for our first character. After that, just let us clear zones and progress. Nobody enjoys:
  • Gathering 3 scraps from Red Vale
  • Porting to town
  • Getting the spikes
  • Slamming the runes
  • Talking to the musician girl
  • Porting back to town
  • Going to Tangle
  • Talking to the musician again so she can sing the roots away… Just let us skip quests. This is a fair middle ground.

12 .You said gold would balance trading. It hasn’t. Make all items tradeable in-game, but at a much higher cost. Right now, trade gives too much power and too many headaches.

  1. You mentioned SSF getting bonuses. If not now, when? It’s the perfect time to boost SSF with some extra rarity or quantity.

  2. Purple flower rare monster modifier—please nerf it.

  3. Recombination success chance—double it. It eats bases; at least give one back.

  4. Unique tablets are way too rare. I’ve found just one in 168 hours. Please buff the drop rate.

  5. Only require 50% of rares for map completion. While exploring or heading toward towers, it takes way too long to set everything up.

Thank you for this game—despite all its issues. It’s raised the bar so high that I can’t enjoy other ARPGs anymore, even if they have better endgame or crafting systems. The core of this game is just so good… I can’t bear the animations, graphics, sounds, and effects in other games anymore.
