The Lakers are not true contenders no matter which way you look at it.

The Lakers can add Luka Dončić, prime Michael Jordan, and a resurrected Wilt Chamberlain, and they still wouldn’t be real contenders. This team is built on delusion, nostalgia, and the desperate hope that superstar names alone can carry them past teams that are simply better. Denver already humiliated them, and nothing about this roster suggests that would change. Nikola Jokić plays the Lakers like it’s a casual Tuesday scrimmage, and they have zero answers for his dominance. Meanwhile, Oklahoma City is younger, faster, and hungrier, with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander already outplaying LeBron in key moments and Chet Holmgren set to expose LA’s interior defense for the joke that it is. But let’s pretend for a second that the Lakers somehow survive the West—Boston is sitting in the Finals, fully prepared to embarrass them like it’s 2008 all over again. The Celtics are deeper, tougher, and far better coached, with the defensive versatility to make life miserable for Luka and LeBron. The Lakers aren’t built for a championship run—they’re built for highlight reels, media hype, and a second-round exit at best.
