Major questions:

What does this team need to (again) get over the hump? Do you see a big trade or do we just add a vet and try to run it back?

I think the major questions going into next year are:

  1. Coaching. We’ve completely collapsed in three of the 4 playoffs under Joe. I don’t think he’s a bad coach, but the only “intense” series we’ve won with him at the helm was the 7 game series against the sixers in 23. Im not calling for his firing, but think he needs to rethink his play style (not just the 3s, but our coverages, too) going into next year. Also needs to get a little more fight out of the guys.

  2. The Jays: I don’t think you can trade one of them. The stakes to do so are just so high, and the last thing the team needs is more role players. The question is whether you give one of them up for another star. Again, not sure you can pull the trigger given their success over the last 10 years. So then the question become (re)building around them. Who can you add that will unlock them as a tandem like KP and holiday?

  3. The center position: thoughts on what to do? Queta improved so much but I’m not sure he can ever be a starting center on a championship team. But who’s out there for us to get (still so mad Al left, think we win in 5-6 with him in this squad).

There’s a lot more, including shopping Hauser (necessary maybe). But my worry is just that there are some huge changes we have to make to compete with a team like okc or even San Antonio at this point.
