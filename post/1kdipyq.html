0.2.0g actually smoothed out a few rough edges

After the delayed rollout, I wasn’t expecting much right away—but some of the 0.2.0g changes ended up making a bigger difference than I thought.

Loot, for starters, feels less like background noise. Rare drops from bosses are more consistent now, and I’ve actually seen a few early T5 magic items with solid rolls—nothing crazy, but enough to build around instead of vendor instantly. The bump in currency drops is subtle, but you notice it over a few maps. Even the chests feel a little less disappointing.

Corruption Core also flows better now. Cutting the passive grind from 15 down to 6 makes it feel like part of the playthrough instead of something you have to go out of your way to finish. The visual mist changes help too, especially if you’re not trying to backtrack through half a zone just to find the entrance. Portal level pacing is also less janky—I’m not constantly overleveled for no reason anymore.

It’s not a flashy patch, and it doesn’t solve every problem, but it hits a few things the community’s been asking for—loot that matters, systems that don’t get in your way. There were also some tweaks to Trials, Expedition, and Delirium rewards that I haven’t fully tested yet, but the direction overall feels solid. Curious what others are seeing so far.
