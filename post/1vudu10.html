SSF Experience: Why it’s worth attempting

My Goals

  • learn the new end game on my own
  • force myself into basic crafting
  • not relying on trade or get distracted by soft core fomo

Key takeaways

  • With a beginner friendly build such as Varashta Djinns, you are going to have a good time. Even if you dont have 10⁄10 gear, mods, jewels or +40 levels to your attack or spell.
  • Having trade available, you avoid lots of tools and mechanics. Instead, you solve lack of gear or currency with divines you barely have.
  • Crafting is not as intimidating if you ignore the top level chaos spamming, sanctifying, quadruple corrupt and whittling. As you probably dont have the currency for it anyway in SSF.
  • SSF lets you eat smaller junks, instead of that endlessly deep void of information and mechanics the game as a whole throws at you.

Examples of learnings

Anoint / Instilling

Of course I wanted “Dominion” on my amulet. It requires rather high tiers of emotions.
So I started running Delirium. In the back of your head, you kinda know that there is recombinator.
But on soft core you kinda dont use it. Instead you would farm divines and buy whatever you need.
Even if your low level emotions pile up in your stash.
So I started combining. Until I got the ones I need.
During this, I also realised, I shouldnt have picket the one Delirium Passive node that says “20 chance for emotions to be ancient ones” (as it feels more like 70% and I didnt need them at all).
On top, my higher tier filter started to hide low level emotions, so watch out.

Crafting

You learn to settle with things - even if its not perfect. You may have 3 mods you really want on your gear. And thats fairly achievable. Maybe you need a few attempts, but you get there. Actually using desecration, essences and maybe an alloy here and there gets you to a decent spot.

Taking on gear or jewellery one by one really lets you learn bit by bit without being overwhelming.
I looked up Foci for example. It can have up to +2 levels to minions skills.
“Easiest craft of my life” - was my first thought. As its an anamamamamama-mod (or something) and I knew there is a currency to force this kind of mod onto an item.
I quickly learned this just works on weapons or jewellery…

So I started slamming 3 mods on Foci, desecrate a suffix and visited the well of souls for some gambling every now and then. The +1 would eventually show up. And a +2 the day after. Feels good.

Farming

There are moments of regret when something of high value drops but its useless for your build.
I still put it into the stash, maybe for a different build later.

You dont care about divines. How Relaxing.
And in general, the currency drops very consistently. Enough to fulfill the needs of your own build.

Milestones

I like that SSF creates a journey of many smaller milestones. Whether its completing the Atlas tree, target a certain piece of gear or a specific currency. It feels less like a grind to get rich and afford BIS equipment.

Next, I want to see what I can get out of the genesis tree. Maybe a Lineage support. Who knows.
