I thought POE2 was actually going to have a good endgame but turns out I might be wrong.

So I had a lot of fun doing the temple for the past few days. I would say temple is miles better than the previous waystone mapping. Mapping was never truely satisfying to me for the following reasons.

  1. There is a heavy entry fee to pay. The cost for good waystone and tablet is extra bad because of group play people can afford much higher price of these. And since drops are probabilistic and when you die everythign is lost, it means you have to constently worry about whether you are even making a profit at all when doing map. Which is probably one of the worst feeling you can have when playing a grindy game.
  2. Mapping has almost no progression. In fact, it is the opposite where the earning margin can get lower and lower because more people start using that specific type of tablet.

Temple fixed all above problems for me. It is not perfect, the mechanism can be simplified or better explained. The reward can be tuned down to be more balanced. But those aren’t important. Here is what I liked about temple:

  1. Temple doesn’t have entry fee except for the time you spent doing mapping. After they made three map per temple the entry cost seems to be on the sweet spot to me.
  2. The death penalty is reasonably where you will lose exp and waste time running the long chain, so it is still annoying enough to make combat and avoiding death meaningful, but not too harsh.
  3. You have actual progression. It just feels satisfying that the loots you are getting now will only get better in the future when you build your temple slowly. Also it just adds another dimension to endgame progression. I already finished my character gear progression two days ago but I still kept playing just because I wanted to finish my temple progression.
  4. Everyone will get different room order so everyone’s layout is a little different. This is just a cool bonus but I had a feeling that I was making my unique thing and was pround of being able to design and make a snake myself.

But no, turns out able to make long chain of rooms might be unintended. The nerf to locking mechanism will probably make the progression to long snakes several times longer if even possible. Which mean practically only the true no lifers can have the hope of building good snakes now. Right now it seems to me that GGG thinks temple should be people just slap down some random rooms that will mostly disappear every time. And the whole progression system of snaking was just by accident instead.

So I wasn’t even that mad about a mid league nerf and mechanism change (don’t get me wrong it’s still very bad to do such nerf mid league). But I am more sad about this fun progression system might be gone forever.
