Of the 190 teams to win a pennant in the 20th century, only seven of them had no Hall of Fame players on the roster

  • 1944 St. Louis Browns - lost in six to the St. Louis Cardinals who had Enos Slaughter and Stan Musial. Most worthy HOFer: Vern Stephens

  • 1945 Chicago Cubs - lost in seven to the Detroit Tigers who had Hank Greenberg and Hal Newhouser. Most worthy HOFer: Stan Hack

  • 1981 Los Angeles Dodgers - won in six against the New York Yankees who had Goose Gossage, Reggie Jackson, and Dave Winfield. Manager Tommy Lasorda elected. Most worthy HOFer: Ron Cey

  • 1988 Los Angeles Dodgers - won in five against the Oakland Athletics who had Dennis Eckersley and Dave Parker. Manager Tommy Lasorda elected. Does not count Don Sutton as he was not on the postseason roster. Most worthy HOFer: Orel Hershiser

  • 1989 San Francisco Giants - lost in four to the Oakland Athletics who had Eckersley, Parker, and Rickey Henderson. Most worthy HOFers: Will Clark and Rick Reuschel

  • 1993 Philadelphia Phillies - lost in six to the Toronto Blue Jays who had Roberto Alomar, Henderson, Paul Molitor, and Jack Morris. Most worthy HOFer: Curt Schilling

  • 1997 Florida Marlins - won in seven against the Cleveland Indians who had Jim Thome. Manager Jim Leyland elected. Most worthy HOFers: Kevin Brown and Gary Sheffield

This makes up less than 4% of all teams to win a pennant from 1903 to 1999. To put things in perspective, there are five teams without a HOFer in the 2000s alone.
