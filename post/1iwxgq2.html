Darkness Monk Impending Doom Build.

Build Link: Acolyte of Frozen Doom - PoE 2 Character Planner

Currently at Level 94.

Goal was to find a way to make Darkness work as a Mechanic, despite unpopularity. I went through many different skills and gear options. Finally found my stride with Despair + Impending Doom + Cursed Ground. Orb of Storms to Spread Wither, Contagion to add Freeze, Frost Wall for crowd control and Proximal Tangibility counter. Using lightening Warp as a utility Cull, but I rarely use it, just like having the cull as an option, and have all those empty slots anyway

Defense is EB/MoM/CI and stacking Mana over 5200+ (10k plus if you’re rich and can afford Dream Fragments and Ingenuity). Survivability and DPS scale with Mana, it’s your only pool.

Currently full clearing Juiced T16 Instilled Breach Maps in under 20 min.

Pros: Insane Clear and Survivability.

Cons: Boss Fights, you have high-Alpha Strike, but boss fights drag a bit. Can usually take them out by relying on flames and regen to fill mana instead of burning through flask. Ration it to fully utilize the flask blasts. I suspect this problem will disappear with Dream Fragments

Notes and Known issues:

  • Utility Belt isn’t good. Implicit isn’t just wasted, it cuts ongoing regen amount from unique flask by 20%. Want to replace with Fine or Linen for either more Mana Regen or auto-refill.
  • Need to replace an Emerald Gem. It is a legacy from when I was still using Quarterstaff, but it’s corrupted to have +8% lightning resist, and removing it puts me under cap.
  • Reduced Bleeding mod on Armour is kind of a waste on a high-regen build, need one with either another resistance, Mana Regen, or Attributes.
  • Gloves need to be ES only. A lot of base Mana on the table there.
  • Eva/ES boots are on purpose. Movement Speed and Flat Mana are required, and a single Defense mod has more benefit with the EVA/ES item than just ES.
  • Tried Spell Cascade and Spell Echo. Build works better without. Spell Echo with less cast speed is worse than Arcane Surge or Arcane Tempo. Because it makes it harder to control Mana Costs. Spell Cascade cuts damage by a flat 30%, and leaves curse ring out of position, hurting secondary Orb, DoT and Wall DPS.
  • Tried Envenom / Bursting Plague in place of Supercritical/Ambush. It boosts Clear DPS, but AoE and Damage on clear is already more than good enough. The poison is nice, but without the passives to support it, the effect on Single Target is less than upping crit chance/damage.

Current Test:

  • Will lose some Mana with loss of Soul Core slot
  • Will be able to swap Ambush for Minion Pact and its 40% more multiplier.
  • Will be able to run Soul Offering for another 65%-ish more multiplier buff.
    • Wording on Soul Offering says it’s not an aura, but a buff that lasts as long as the spire “exists”.
  • Will have Minions to help run interference, mostly disposable, probably run Last Gasp to ensure they are alive for buff and some debuff on hit effects
  • Ablation, Danse Macabre, Meat Shield, Elemental Army, Minion Mastery on the Spire to Max effect and up-time.
  • I need to scrape together 10-20 more Strength.
