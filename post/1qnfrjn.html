Philosophy of class balance

Whats the actual reason GGG is keeping classes nerfed while ignoring some archetype?

Blood Mage and Pathfinder are best this season in terms of clear speed, Blood Mage was insane since the launch, why they didn’t care about it, but for example;

- they nerfed all mana stacking sources to make it impossible for stormweaver to be viable (salute mahuxotl), whats the point of using stormweaver now instead of blood mage if you can do everything just better as blood mage? ultimate stormweaver is a blood mage now, with covenant robes as being “blood mage” that way solves all of your mana problems and makes stormweaver usable again, yea it has mana regen soul core with most gimmicky way you can generate mana, i am pretty sure they will nerf it next season so that won’t be an option either, they just hate stormweaver is what i think about this situation
- they nerfed caged god to ground “because” it was being stronger than every other weapon if you build around it, now its bad even if you build around it
- they deleted possibility of barrier invocation loop, which required insane amount of calculation and adjustment and probably hardest build to minmax due to this
- lightning spear is dead, like why? because it had good clear speed? blood mage is still better and untouched
- minion builds are dead, it was already a boring class and making it weaker didn’t change anything
- gemling legionnarie was good in past, was allowing you to tinker about your attributes, now its weak enough that you don’t even need to care
- invoker was once good boss killer, now its the one of few classes that i consider as hard to clear juiced bosses, removal of grim feast already made class very open to random stun lock deaths (occasionally) because its still a melee class (get a ranged ability is not a valid argument in my opinion because thats just an artificial layer of defense, in that case we should say get a melee ability to deal more damage for ranged classes)

im not saying these classes are unusable, they all have a gimmick to use them properly, yet they are still losing to single spark spam blood mage or poisonburst pathfinder, meanwhile other classes are trying to buff up themselves every other minute or need to do a combo then and so on

i want to understand what the philosophy behind this, whats the reason of killing a class and never returning back to it?
