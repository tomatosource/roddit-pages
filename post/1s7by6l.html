I would kill for a ‘league’ that was left alone for the rest of the game’s life cycle on release

I know this is highly unlikely. I just wanted to voice it because it makes me sad that it’s not a thing, even though I know it’s feedback that likely no one wants to hear.

I love the game but I really dislike not having a stable game with continuity that I can pick up and come back to over much longer periods of time than the seasonal three month cycles. Standard doesn’t really fulfil this role because free passive resets don’t really make up for updates that fundamentally change your build.

It actually makes me quite upset that this doesn’t exist because it’s my favourite game ever in pretty much every other way otherwise and this single thing curdles my joy.

It’s not that I don’t like the seasonal content, or don’t enjoy making new characters, it would just be nice to opt in and out in a meaningful way.

I’ll still continue to play it anyway because no other game on the market currently comes close to it in terms of overall quality and it’s my favourite genre but I’m pretty sure this aspect of it will always make me sad.
