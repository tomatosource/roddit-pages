Looking back at 0.1 with 1.0 in mind

When 1.0 releases we will have a huge influx of new players. So took a trip down memory lane and though back om when I was a new player in 0.1 and contemplated all the “mistakes” I did and how GGG can minimize the impacts of such mistakes.

I did go in quite blind and I picked Ranger/Pathfinder with poison skills. It didn’t go great but when I switched to Gas arrow and minions with Infernal Legion it started to go ok. Back then you could not change Ascendency, but that has luckily been changed.

So here are my list of things that GGG can improve to make the campaign experience a bit easier for new players:

  1. Make skill gem and support gem more abundant to incentivize experimentation when it comes to builds. Either make is so you can buy or farm these gems or just make them drop more.

  2. More ways to craft a better weapon. Since a good weapon will make or break any build, we should get more (blank) runes, (blank) essences and crafting materials during the campaign, or show the players where to farm these consistently.

  3. Remove the gamble limit in campaign. If you get stuck at a boss, it is usually because your weapon is bad. And this limit is removed in the endgame anyway.

  4. Teach the player how the trial of Sekhemas works, maybe by what relics should be prioritized. I had a rough time my first time around in the first ascendency trail. Now days it is a breeze but it would be nice that the UI showed a summary of all your relics. So when you are choosing your relics at the start of the run you get some type of indication that you probably should prioritize honour-resistance. Act 2 bosses could even drop relics or you could get them as quest rewards together with some tips from NPCs.

  5. Lower the levels of the Chaos trails. You find this trail around lv 30-34 but it is a lv 38 area. Just make it lv 33 and replace the tornado bird.

  6. Lower the skill level requirements. Personally I think all skill gems should be available at character lv 41 (skill gem lv 11) and spirit gems at lv 46 (skill gem lv 12).

  7. Guaranteed resistance rings. Add a guaranteed Sapphire ring to the first quest since you would like to have one for Geonor. And that quest needs something more than a support gem anyway. I know that there is an guaranteed elemental ring in Act 2, but it may not be clear to the player that the Topaz ring is the best choice most of the times. So either the player could say something in line with “Oh lightning would be good if I need to fight Jamanra again” or there could just be another guaranteed Topaz ring. Viper is another rough fight, but I’m not sure if a Amethyst Ring with 10% chaos res will save new players from her wrath.

Conclusion

My goal is not to trivialize the campaign but instead give the player ways to grind for better items if they get stuck. If a player gets stuck on Geonor, Sekhemas, Jamanra, Viper or Tavakai I want there to be actions to take, like lets farm some currency and craft some better gear. And If all my 7 suggestions are to be added, the game will probably be to easy, but a couple of them would probably make the experience for new players a lot more pleasant.

Wild bonus idea:

Improve the chances to get bases for your build. You are more or less just interested in 10% of all weapon bases and 1⁄6 of all armour bases, it would be nice to have a way to narrow it down. I was thinking of adding a smuggler NPC that has a “Genesis tree”-like system where you can influence what bases will show the other vendors. But maybe this should be a league mechanic with Smuggler-coins and a thief guild etc etc.
