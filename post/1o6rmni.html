My idea to avoid exalted orb worthlessness…

So, we see this crazy devaluation of exalts after a couple weeks.

We need an exalt sink.

Chaos orbs are useful and basically see no devaluation.

My idea is to add another omen similar to the catalysing omen.

This omen allows ‘crafting quality’ to armor and weapons up to 100%

Then, when adding the next affix you increase the likelihood to add the top tier of available affixes with your next exalt and then the omen is consumed.

This used in conjunction with the new tiers of exalts could be powerful but certainly not game breaking. You could blow 400 ex crafting a piece of gear.

Thoughts? I would think this would solve the issue of a basically useless currency post week 2.
