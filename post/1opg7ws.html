[Charania] Utah’s Walker Kessler is undergoing season-ending left shoulder surgery for a torn labrum, sources tell ESPN.

While a brutal development for Kessler, this is very interesting for the Celtics.

Now, he’s likely going to be looking for either a smaller deal, or a shorter deal. He could be back in the mix for the Celtics as they look for their Center this offseason.
