So, I think it’s time to add the “Orb of Horizon” to the game. You might ask why — well, there are a few reasons.

For those who don’t know what the Orb of Horizon is: it basically lets you re-roll your maps, like in PoE 1. But in PoE 1, maps are chained to tiers, while in PoE 2, maps aren’t tier-locked. So, some changes would need to be made. My suggestions are something like this:

  1. It fits well into the Atlas tree.
  2. It should cost something meaningful so it can’t be easily abused (especially by try-hards).
  3. There are, at most, five good map layouts in the game. This solves that issue and gives GGG time to improve layouts over time. Players who don’t like certain layouts (like Vaal Seepage) can skip those maps and play their own way.
  4. It also helps with the unique map problem. In 0.2, there are actually many good unique maps, but we rarely see them. Some don’t even show up. We could have, for example, a 5% chance for maps to be converted into unique maps (not including Untainted Paradise — that would be too OP, or at least have it at a much lower chance, like 1%, or maybe depending on biomes). This would open up new playstyles, and I think more variety in playstyle is good for the game right now.

Also, I don’t think GGG needs to wait until 0.3 to add them. Just like they added new runes, they can introduce this now and forget about fixing bad layouts for the time being. Instead, focus on Acts 4, 5, and 6, as well as polishing all the classes. Then, focus on balancing at those stages. Over time, map layouts can be improved naturally — like Augury, which is already much better than in 0.1.

Rate up guys so GGG do its sooner than later
