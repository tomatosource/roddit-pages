Feels like the POE 1 campaign is only meaningfully shorter if you know exactly what to do

I have about 350 hours in each game. I played 4 characters in POE 1, then another 4 in POE 2 once it released. I finally went back to POE 1 in the new league to see how things felt after all my POE 2 time.

I’m 4 hours into my POE 1 campaign and I’m still in Act 2. Which is to say, roughly on par with POE 2, which only has 4 acts and a few interludes. I would describe my play style as “methodical by default, but willing to cut corners when I know my next objective.” POE 2 tends to reward this mindset. You want to do *most* things, but you can occasionally rush through a map where the side content isn’t all that important.

By contrast, POE 1 seems to require a bunch of hyper-specific, “skip this but NOT that” pathing. Do that quest for the skill point, but skip that one because you don’t need the skill it rewards, etc.” For me specifically, this basically means I’m picking between “generally doing everything” and “alt-tabbing to wikis constantly to double check I’m pursuing the worthwhile quest.” Neither one is all that fast in the aggregate. And this is for a game I’ve played for three hundred and fifty hours AKA more than 90% of any games I’ve ever played.

I get that POE 1 grants mobility skills very quickly, something that POE 2 doesn’t really offer. Still, I find myself leap slamming, shield charging, and frost blinking toward dead ends and underwhelming rewards.

If the conventional wisdom was “POE 1 is a *bit* shorter” or “POE 1 gets shorter if you play the game for literally thousands of hours,” I would understand. But the narrative seems to be closer to ‘POE 1 campaign is *way* shorter for most players,” and so far, I’m not seeing it. What am I missing?
