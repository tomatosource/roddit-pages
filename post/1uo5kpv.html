0% vs 100% gear rarity from 68 juiced maps

EDIT:

TLDR: (oversimplified) 100% rarity on gear resulted in ~20-30% more currency

All maps were from the same strat with very similar setups. 200% deli city maps with 18-20 map mods. Tablets were Irradiate, Breach, Abyss and Overseer. All of them had +2 map mods on suffix suffix and 2 prefixes with % increased magic monsters/% increased monster rarity/% monster effectiveness. 2nd suffix was additional strongbox/shrine if possible, else a random mod.

Rarity setup had exactly 100% item rarity from gear and 2nd setup had 0%. I also used Rite Of Passage for both setups that gives 15% rarity for 1 second, which might have ruined the data a bit, but it should have minimal effect.

There are also some data mixed into this from tile loot (chests and expedition remnants) that’s not affected by gear rarity, estimate is about 5-10%.

0% rarity setup had 9x T16 6 mod maps and 100% rarity had 10x T16 with 6 mods maps. All other maps were T15s with 8 mods.

I took track of monsters killed for each map with map device mtx. Both setups have very similar amount of monsters killed.

White monsters Magic monsters Rare monsters Unique monsters

diff +9% +3.7% -3.96%

Data is focused mainly on Divine, Chaos and Vaal orb drops. I didn’t pick up exalts and other currencies don’t have enough sample size (you can still check them in complete table screenshots).
