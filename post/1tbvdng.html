Could a Offering / Citaqualotl’s Thesis build / utility work?

For those unfamiliar it is an Ancient Augment (limited to 1) that has the following effect on gloves:

Gloves: One of your Persistent Minions revives when an Offering expires

I’ve been wondering for a while now how to use this and the one thing i came up with that might actually work is combining 0 skill duration (Hulking Form Titan limited i think) and the Heartbound Loop unique ring to trigger the “300 Physical Damage taken on Minion Death” effect to then charge Lightning Coil’s Valako’s Charge skill to deal damage. I’m not sure however if the charging would be fast enough or if the cooldown recovery (main Valako’s Charge bottleneck if charging isn’t a problem) could be increased to a point where the build is viable (through a good Undying Hate for instance).

One question that comes to mind is - can this bonus get doubled through darkness enthroned to instead revive two minions? I’d guess it won’t since the “One” part isn’t highlighted.

If there are problems with using it offensively could it at least be used defensively instead? Like through the other effect it has on body armour:

Body Armours: You Recoup 50% of Damage taken by your Offerings as Life

If i understand it correctly you could stack life on the offering to then damage it with Infernal Legion support and get 50% * 20⁄30% of its life back and if placed into Atziri’s Splendour with the “This item gains bonuses from Socketed Soul Cores as though it was also Gloves” mod it would effectively act as an infinite life flask. as long as it expired before it got killed by the support or by enemies. Combined with Essence Harvest it could also recoup 8% max mana or recover 2% life with Gravedigger passive.
