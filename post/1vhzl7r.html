[OC] What are the tallest, shortest, oldest, youngest, and most common starting lineups in NBA history (plus other superlatives)?

What is the tallest starting five ever put out by an NBA team? What about the shortest? The oldest? Youngest?

Some of these questions are easy Googles, but there is usually no methodology connected to the answers you’ll find. I took it upon myself to use Basketball Reference’s starting lineup data to find answers to these questions.

It was actually a long, arduous process, but I was able to index every starting lineup ever put out by an NBA team since 1983-84 along with the average height, weight, age, total career scoring, assist, rebounding, steal, and block output, and the total number of games that each starting lineup took the court together.

Basketball Reference doesn’t have data for starters before 1983-84, so bear that in mind with these results. I also do not have data for play-in and NBA Cup Final games, but I’m banking on them not holding any of these records considering the tiny share of the total games that these oddities actually are.

With those caveats out of the way, here are some of my findings:
