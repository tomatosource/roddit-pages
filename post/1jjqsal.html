NBA Owners Set To Vote On Proposed European League

https://www.sportico.com/leagues/basketball/2025/nba-european-league-plans-vote-1234844674/

It’s a “semi-open” proposal, the person said, meaning there would be up to four spots for the best teams in the EuroLeague—the continent’s top circuit with teams like Real Madrid in Spain and Greece’s Olympiacos—to compete in the NBA’s league the following year. The franchises in places like London or Paris could sell for at least $500 million, according to the proposal

This has been rumored for the past year or so. NBA sees the growth in the international game and realized “Hey! Instead of the players and fans coming overseas, what if WE go to them!”
