Giannis Antetokounmpo has been ELITE in the Midrange this Season

The midrange has largely been phased out of the league except for a select few players who are so elite you just can’t take it away from them, and Giannis this season is matching those players in efficiency and volume. What seemed to be a new development to atleast an above-average jumpshot, as the season has progressed he has solidified himself among the league’s best

He takes 4.3 midrange shots and makes 2 a game, good for 5th in volume. SGA and Booker make 2.2 for reference. In terms of efficiency, in the top 30 players for Midrange Field Goals Made, only 3 players are shooting better than him: Devin Booker, Shai Gilgeous Alexander, and Kevin Durant.

In the 15-19 ft range where he takes 81% of his jumpshots, he’s second in volume to Demar Derozan and shooting better than everyone other than Kevin Durant.

He is straight up more efficient on higher volume than notable names like Joel Embiid, Nikola Jokic, Kawhi Leonard, Jalen Brunson, Brandon Ingram, Paolo Banchero, Lebron, and Jayson Tatum. What an awesome development.

Also Kevin Durant is insane we have “elite” midrange weapons like Shai, Derozan, Booker teetering on 50% and their efficiency goes down farther out at 15-19 ft, and then Durant is just by himself 56% midrange, 60% 15-19 ft. The man deserves so many flowers.
