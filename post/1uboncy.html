About pinnacle content

Loving 0.5 so far, everything feels super improved but one thing that still feels nagging at me is how unrewarding pinnacle content is.

​

At the moment, all of the pinnacle content is a breeze for a reasonably tuned build, besides 200% deli/simulacrum in the latest patch. The discrepancy in difficulty between mechanics is odd but more importantly, the loop of hunting down breach hives or invitations to go do the mechanic to get a key where then you delete the boss and get literally 1ex and some random crappy rares feels extremely unrewarding.

​

At this point, youre better off selling the key for divines unless RNGesus smiles at you. It would be nice if the currency value of the pinnacle encounters dropped in proportion to the value of the entrance fee to avoid this feeling (simulacrum seems fine here). It would be nice if breach etc pinnacle difficulty was buffed as well. Currently the run up breach to xesht is a complete joke as well
