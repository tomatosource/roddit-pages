League mechanic question and anecdotes

Just a thread to have a useful and helpful discussion to make running the temple more intuitive and fruitful.

Some things I’ve noticed from my limited experience running it this far -

  • never place tokens found during a run before you close the current run, your newly added rooms can be erased, this happened to me multiple times.
  • the champion and research (dunno what it’s called but the icon with vials) rooms compete with each other for upgrades.
  • the hidden currency chest from architect (the boss that is required to kill for atziri) actually drops divs.
  • tier 3 service rooms drop itemized currency which give the same service. In case you’re worried about getting a Vaal orb from t3 corruption.

One thing I was wondering, what use does increasing the crystal capacity give? You can’t see while running maps if you are storing more than 6? Can you get and place additional rooms? I’m at 18 and I see no difference..
