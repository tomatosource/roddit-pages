Please let us display ’stun threshold’ meter above the character

I don’t mind having to build into stun threshold as a melee character. But when I’m actively blocking, trying to keep an eye on enemy positioning, and reacting to telegraphed movements it’d be great to have the option to display stun threshold buildup above my characters head.

It would make it so much easier to prevent getting clobbered due to losing track of some incoming move because I darted my eyes away or because I didn’t dart my eyes away and now I’m crumpled on the floor.

Less of an issue @ endgame but hot damn I’m taking a few hits and it nearly fills. Fine, but give me a tool to keep a closer eye on it.

This is especially brutal on my Huntress when utilizing parry multiple times in a row. (Yes, I use parry and I like it!)
