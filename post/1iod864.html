If they want us to be excited about ground drops

This *might* be a controversial opinion but

If they want us to be excited about ground drops, maybe we shouldn’t have to identify everything that drops with a scroll of wisdom (or as most people probably do, carry it unidentified until they get back to the hideout and use the NPC to identify everything).

It’s kinda hard to be excited about a non-descript rare item that I lug around the map for 10 minutes and identify at my hideout only to realize its affixes are awful.

If items weren’t unidentified on drop, the loot filter system could possibly even be updated to allow filtering based on affixes, so you can tie satisfying sound and visual effects to when a rare with desirable affixes drop. Maybe then people would be excited about ground drops?
