Please let us equip 2 Shields at the same time (Walking fortress style)

Currently shields can only be equipped in the off-hand slot, which means you can’t have a main-hand shield or equip 2 shields at the same time or a shield + a different offhand. Please GGG change this to allow us to equip a shield in either equipment slot.

The character fantasy of an absolute tank that’s a moving fortress, constantly advancing forward as waves of enemies crash upon them and break on their shield is really grimdark and fits the poe2 universe narratively perfectly.

Besides allowing a cool moving fortress concept, being able to equip 2 shields at the same time could give rise to new in-game builds that utilize a rare + unique shield in combination, or two unique shields at the same time, or a shield in the main hand with another piece of equipment in the offhand.

There’s already a few skills in the game that primarily revolve around using only a shield:

  • Resonating shield

  • shield charge

  • shield wall

  • (even thorns)

Being able to equip a shield into the main hand and offhand would potentially give new ways to play these underutilized skills or find new cool combinations of unique shields, alongside it being a very in-universe build concept of a grimdark unbreakable moving fortress.
