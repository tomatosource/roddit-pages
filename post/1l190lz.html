Cleveland, Dallas, Memphis, Philly, and Minnesota To Receive Classic Jersey For 2025-26 Season

Source

We can leak exclusive information on which NBA teams will receive an Classic Edition Jersey for the upcoming 2025-26 NBA season. In total, five teams will receive an new Classic Edition Jersey with the old Nike Futura logo.

In the still ongoing 2024-25 NBA Season a total of 4 teams received an Classic Edition Jersey - the Golden State Warriors, Memphis Grizzlies, Sacramento Kings, and the Toronto Raptors got the honor and received an Classic Edition Jersey in 2024.

The Memphis Grizzlies receive two Classic Jerseys in two following seasons. The four other teams who receive a 2025-26 Classic Edition jersey are the Cleveland Cavaliers, Dallas Mavericks, Minnesota Timberwolves & Philadelphia 76ers.
