In 6 out of the last 7 seasons, the team that eliminated Jimmy Butler in the playoffs went on to win the championship

2019: Butler’s 76ers lose to the Raptors in round 2. Raptors win the chip.

2020: Butler’s Heat lose to the Lakers in the finals.

2021: Butler’s Heat lose to the Bucks in the first round. Bucks win the chip.

2022: Butler’s Heat lose to the Celtics in the ECF, Celtics lose to Warriors in NBA finals.

2023: Butler’s Heat lose to the Nuggets in the finals.

2024: Butler’s Heat lose to the Celtics in the first round (although Butler doesn’t play in the series). Celtics win the chip.
