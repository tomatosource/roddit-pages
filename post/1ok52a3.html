Best players in the NBA per ESPN’s new “net points” stat: 1-Wemby, 2-Reaves, 3-Giannis, 4-Lauri, 5-SGA

Fun new stat from Dean Oliver, one of the pioneers of basketball analytics. He explains the methodology here: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/44093220/introducing-net-points-latest-nba-metric-amazing-early-findings

In my experience it’s one of the better stats at assessing impact when the sample size is small. Some players that have interesting results:

  • #2) Austin Reaves - he’s looking like he might actually be a viable #1 option star
  • #8) Jaime Jaquez - looking like an an incredible player who should be in line for a $150m+ extension this offseason. From a Lakers fan standpoint, it’s absolutely tragic that there’s an LA Mexican in the NBA, and he’s not on the Lakers (the Lakers picked Jalen Hood-Schifino 1 spot ahead of him)
  • #13) Cedric Coward - A rookie being top 15 in a metric like this is super impressive. I’d probably take him top 5 in a redraft, he’s tracking for clear superstar upside

Source: https://espnanalytics.com/nba-net-pts
