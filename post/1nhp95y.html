now that EuroBasket is over, it’s time to vote on the hypothetical USA vs. WORLD series (Game 1 of 7)

The United States has won the last five gold medals in men’s basketball, clearly re-establishing itself as the premier basketball powerhouse in the world. But at the same time, the U.S. does have an advantage in terms of its population. With over 340 million residents, it’s usually the most populated country in the field.

Would the U.S. still be # 1 if we accounted for that disparity? There’s no easy way to answer that, but there is a fun way. A hypothetical 7-game series – with the other countries playing against a portion of the U.S. that matches their population.

Game 1 will begin with that classic rivalry – SERBIA (population 6.6M) against the state of INDIANA (population 6.9M). (Note: for the “Indiana” roster, we’re only including players who were born in the state – not ones who played high school or college there. Why? It’s just easier for me to search basketball-reference that way.)

Check out the rosters and vote on a winner in the comments below! The team with the most votes “wins” Game 1.

######TEAM SERBIA

PG: Aleksa Avramovic, Vasilije Micic

SG: Bogdan Bogdanovic

SF: Marko Guduric, Ognjen Dobric

PF: Filip Petrusev, Nikola Jovic

C: Nikola Jokic

team strategy: Team Serbia only has a few NBA players, but they have cohesion and chemistry as the unit that pushed the full Team USA to the brink in the Paris Olympics. Of course, a lot of that has to do with Nikola Jokic down low. The big man is used to carrying any team to competitiveness. For Team Serbia to win this opening matchup, they’ll have to run the offense through him.

######TEAM INDIANA

PG: Darius Garland, Craig Porter Jr.

SG: Desmond Bane, Jaden Ivey

SF: Eric Gordon, Gary Harris

PF: Michael Porter Jr., Gordon Hayward

C: Mason Plumlee, Trayce Jackson-Davis

team strategy: There are only 12 players in the NBA who were born in the state of Indiana. Unfortunately, not many are big men. The team will move veteran Mason Plumlee into the starting lineup because he’s a little bigger than Trayce Jackson-Davis and has some familiarity against Nikola Jokic after playing years together in Denver. But to win, this undersized team will need to go “bombs away” offensively, with Darius Garland, Desmond Bane, and Michael Porter Jr. firing from deep. The retired Gordon Hayward is here mostly to max out our nostalgia and Indiana-ness.
