My suggestion to GGG for Alt Leveling

Just thought of this suddenly so probably will have a bunch of things that i didnt consider and downsides as well. But thought i share it on the slim chance that people collaborate to make it better or GGG sees it and gives them at least an idea to consider.

I dont think the campaign in itself is tediously long if you just do the main quest. The problem is that you need to do many of the side content to get the permanent bonuses (i.e. skill points, resistances, etc). And they are usually quite tedious with you having to scour the maps looking for drops or items (e.g. final letter, clan relics, etc) or clear a separate map entirely (e.g. act 4, spirit bonuses). So what if instead of having all characters do all of these everytime, you ulitise the ‘Interludes’ to provide account wide progress for that league.

GGG already stated that the ‘Interludes’ are going away once all the acts are released. So instead of scrapping what is decent content, why not repurpose it for something else. Each of the interludes already are themed/located on the acts so it can still make sense in-game.

The idea is that after you do an Act, you can talk to the “head” of that act and help them on another problem. You get transferred to the interlude and once you complete the quest, you talk to the NPC again and you now have all the buffs you acquired from that act unlocked for all of your characters.

We know this is already possible since you dont immediately have access to the salvage table/reforging on alts. And this is a reasonable compromise with what GGG wants (people playing through the campaign) and what the many of the player base wants (better alt progression).

Let me know what you think. Im genuinely trying to contribute and help the game be even better. So please be civil and constructive.

Tldr: Use interludes to unlock account wide bonuses for alt progression.
