What is the obsession with the NBA’s ratings?

I am trying to wrap my head around this genuinely pathological obsession ordinary people on social media have with the NBA’s ratings.

Where does this concern come from? Do you have a stake in ownership of these teams, and lower ratings results in you losing money? Do you have a bet with your coworkers regarding the rating? What is the purpose of autopsy reports about the NBA’s future?

The NBA’s popularity around the world has never been higher. The popularity of the league on social media has never been higher. When there are trends on social media among sports fans, you can guarantee that 9 times out of 10, the trend was started by NBA fans.

So if the NBA and their fans are the ones constantly moving sports culture forward, why is this the one sports league being singled out for low ratings, in an era where more and more people are cutting cable and opting other services to watch their games?
