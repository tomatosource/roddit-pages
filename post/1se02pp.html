What former Celtic would you like to see come back?

Man, there’s a bunch of former Celtics I’d love to see back. Smart, Grant, Jrue, Timelord… those dudes were just Celtic to the core. The toughness, the IQ, the little winning plays, I still miss all of that.

But honestly, the guy I REALLY wanna see back is Anfernee Simons. The scoring, the shot creation, the fit next to our core… it just feels right. And if we win the Finals, I could actually see him taking a little pay cut to come back. It wouldn’t shock me at all if he looked around and said “Yh, I wanna run it back with these guys.”

If that happened, our already nasty offense would be straight up insane.
