The only AL batter with 90 BB & under 80 K in the last 23 years is Marco Scutaro

As a Blue Jay in 2009, Marco walked a career-high 90 times (33 above his second-best) and struck out 75 times, on the way to a 5.4 WAR season. It is the only example of an AL hitter walking 90 times while striking out under 80 times since Mariners 1b John Olerud in 2001 and 2002.

The only NL player since Scutaro was Albert Pujols, who had 103 walks and 76 strikeouts in 2010, the third year in a row he achieved it. Alex Bregman was very close four times, an honorary achiever.
