GGG please give pathfinder real identity

The new league has made Pathfinder lose a lot of identity in my opinion. How?

  • Skill speed/moving ascendancy passives nerfed
  • Poison ascendancy passive nerfed
  • Traveller’s Wisdom (travel node re-purpose) ascendancy passive indirectly nerfed
  • Unique jewels “Split personality” (allow starting from EVERY starting point of the tree, compared to Pathfinder who has to spend 2 points to be able to start from only warrior or sorceress) makes this section of the tree feel useless

For reference, pathfinder is so much more than just poison builds. Last league, I created my unique build and played exclusively shield wall quadra defence pathfinder (1.8K hp, 6.4K ES, 90% armour, 60% evasion, 27% block). Obviously shield wall was nerfed, so this league I’m doing a different damage skill (caltrops), but I cannot get triple defence anywhere close this league! Almost all hybrid nodes like “Gain X armour per Y ES on (gear item)” have been nerfed this league. And amulets have removed the “% increased global defence”, because “defence” tag has been reworked into “armour, evasion and energy shield”. There’s no longer value is trying to stack all three defence types anymore, as if there was even much before but at least it was doable in 0.4

(0.4 build link to demonstrate what was possible before)

Why do off meta creative challenge builds have to get caught up in nerfs and made not viable anymore?

I genuinely believe the whole Traveller’s Wisdom section of Pathfinder, which was my favourite last league, now needs a complete rework. The increased armour, evasion and energy shield travel nodes have lost so much value now, and likewise, the “extra starting points on passive tree” is literally a worse version of the unique jewels. The only advantage is that they don’t require currency. I would literally rather play any other ascendancy and use the unique jewel, than play the Pathfinder ascendancy anymore.
