Tejay Antone came back this year after his 3rd Tommy John surgery at age 32. He is now one inning away from matching his career high, and has been the best pitcher in the Reds bullpen

Tejay Antone making it all the way back to the majors after his third Tommy John surgery was a nice story. But since then he has quietly been the best pitcher in the Reds bullpen. At 32 years old and with a fastball that now sits at 94 mph instead of 100 mph, he has an ERA of 2.10, and ERA+ of 204, and has been worth 1.2 bWAR, all of which lead all Reds relievers. He has also now thrown 34.1 IP, which is only one off of his career high from his rookie year. (And he also literally wrote a book about recovering from Tommy John surgery).
