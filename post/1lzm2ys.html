Fun Interaction Last Night

Nothing deep here—just a fun little interaction from my session last night. So, I’m new to the game, with about 50 hours at launch and about 35 hours in the last week or so. I’m finally dipping my toes into trading, selling and buying maybe ten items total. I was skeptical since I play on PS5/Portal, but it’s been smooth!

So, last night, as I was gearing up to beat the act 3 boss, I needed some more max HP. I bought a couple items, then realized 2⁄3 were from the same guy. I didn’t realize it until I received a whisper saying, “Welcome back lol.” I ended up beating the boss, so I made sure to message him, thanking him for the items and informing him of my conquest. Look, most people probably couldn’t care less. He got his exalted orb and moved on. Instead, he responded with a heartfelt congratulations, which was, idk, just cool.

That’s basically the story (I know, a real nail biter), but it was just a fun example of kinda the MMO-lite characteristics of the game, which is probably my favorite genre (ARPGs, MMO-lites).

On a side note, I was fine taking my sweet time in Norma difficulty, but I definitely want to speed up cruel difficulty 😂
