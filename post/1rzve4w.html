[Serious] What would you expect Ty Jerome’s value to be on the trade market this Summer?

Source: https://www.espn.com/nba/player/_/id/4065733/ty-jerome

He’s averaging 19.9 PPG, 2.9 REB, and 5.8 AST on 48% shooting, but he’s only played 14 games after an injury kept him out for a majority of this season. He was healthy with Cleveland last season, but that was the first time in his career he’s played over 50+ games.

However, even though it’s a small sample size, he’s currently 7th in the league in EPM which is better than Chet Holmgren, Stephen Curry, Donovan Mitchell, and more which is incredible.

But he also had a chance in the playoffs with Cleveland last season and averaged 11.7 PPG on 40.2% FG and was routinely targeted on defense.

——

So… understanding his hypothetical value is very difficult. Teams will be trying to trade for him with the Cleveland stint in mind, but definitely wouldn’t pay the price of a player averaging 20 PPG/6 APG this season since he’s playing alongside majority two-way and 10-day players absorbing all the usage. Memphis might try to sell him as if he’s a guy who’s just emerging into a star role, but I’m not sure any team in the league would pay a star (or even starter) price for him.

He’s 28-years old, has a player option after next season, and, keep in mind, the Grizzlies just started tanking and currently rostering Scottie Pippen Jr., Walter Clayton, Ja Morant, Cam Spencer, Jaylen Wells, and will likely be bringing in a guard like Keaton Wagler, Mikel Brown Jr., or Brayden Burries in the Summer.
