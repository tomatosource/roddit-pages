What are some of your wishlists for 0.3?

Personally I’d like:

  • A balance pass on spell scaling, remove the higher tier of +level mods on gear and add more other type of scaling. Having one mod be as strong as all other combined is just bad
  • A stats similar to damage over time multiplier do we can properly play DOT builds
  • Instant buy/sell stash, you put items in there with a price (also cost gold) and other can buy your items without your input
  • Something similar to scarabs in PoE 1
  • Ascendancy balance change and new ascendancies
  • More campaign
  • No more running tower, just get the tablet slots when you get there
  • No more weapon locking for skills

EDIT: Somehow I forgot the thing that bugs me the most:

  • I hate the current Atlas tree and would like a tree where you run maps to unlock mechanics, not buy keys to unlock mechanic trees. So basically similar to the PoE 1 atlas tree
