From cookies to crucibles: my first real steps into Path of Exile 2 after Last Epoch

Hey fellas, So I’ve been living in Last Epoch for a while now. A game that basically treats you like a loving grandma would. Cookies, comfort, a loot filter built in, stash tabs for pocket in-game change, crafting bench on speed dial. Pure quality of life bliss.

Then I decided to walk into Path of Exile 2… and it felt like being dropped into boot camp by a drill sergeant who also stole my lunch money. Suddenly, resistances mattered, loot was stingy, and bosses actually yelled at me before destroying me. The culture shock was real.

Instead of doing a “which is better” rant, I put together a cinematic video sharing:

  • Quality of Life vs Friction (cookies vs drill sergeant)
  • Combat Philosophy (power fantasy vs Soulslike bosses)
  • Build Systems (skill trees vs gem links)
  • Loot & Crafting (confetti vs stingy, deterministic vs gambling)
  • Endgame & Progression (monoliths vs the Atlas)

Both games are certainly evolving fast. LE with the upcoming Orobyss expansion + console launch, and PoE2 with patch 0.3.1’s endgame overhaul. So the timing felt perfect to compare the philosophies from both games.

Video here if you want to hop on the full ride: https://youtu.be/ALkxF75cS4I

But honestly, I would love to hear from you all. If you’ve swapped between the two, what was your biggest “wait… wtf?!” moment? If you only play one, what would convince you to try the other, and what are you most puzzled by losing if you make the switch?
