0.5 hype made me log in again, loved every second of it.

After a long break during 0.4 having levelled a druid, reached insane levels of power and farmed temple to the max I decided to return and play a few builds I hadn’t tried before and it resulted in probably the most fun I’ve had since 0.1.

What made this so fun was that I had already farmed up thousands of divines, had a stash full of twink gear and end game items and I felt absolutely no pressure to participate in the economy. No rarity requirements, no speed requirements and no pressure to fit in to the meta.

First toon was a Merc, a character I just didn’t vibe with before. Took him to end game, proved my concept build and learned what combinations work should I ever choose Merc as a league start.

Second toon I decided to go pure lightning monk. First bits of campaign were rough but I persisted with some twink gear like tabula, boots and helm. I’ve pushed through to level 92 - with a bit of research and never once looking at a guide - I ended up putting together a rather potent elemental expression map clearer with flicker strike for bosses. Power charges are a bit of an issue and my defences aren’t min maxed but overall I am extremely happy with how much my game knowledge has improved since 0.1.

I have spent thousands of divines on gear, crafts, experimenting and I regret nothing. Those divines would have just been sitting in standard with no use come 0.5. No economy and meta pressure really allowed me to gear up way smoother without worrying about rarity or divines per hour. Just focused purely on the build and character development.

I highly recommend trying the same before 0.5 if you have a lot of currency that’s just going to disappear in two weeks. It’s a fresh way to play and the lack of pressure makes the whole journey a blast.

I can post the monk build if people are interested, it’s not perfect by all means but I am very pleased with what I managed on my own and I have some more ideas to further improve it.
