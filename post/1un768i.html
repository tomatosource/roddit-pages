Bitter Dead would be a S-tier skill if they just bug fixed it. Instead I feel forced to reroll.

Hey all, I have a 96 Bitter Dead Gemling. For the vast majority of content, the build and skill is great. The projectiles auto-home, phase through most obstacles, have insane base crit chance, and very high freeze buildup.

For those who don’t know however, for some monsters Bitter Dead’s hitbox is completely broken. Instead of exploding, the balls instead fly up to the target and sit for 5-10 seconds and do no damage before manually exploding. This is made worse by the fact that there seems to be an upper limit on the raw total of balls that can be active at a time, meaning that spam casting = balls respawning before they can self-expire and explode.

You can live with this in most content, but it just so happens the worst offenders are the content with the most juice: delirium and expedition. Essentially every rare expedition monster has the hitbox bug with bitter dead, and the same is true for delirium mirror rares.

For expedition especially, this makes remnant encounters incredibly tedious. These rares already have cycling invulnerability, 3 health bars, crit resistance, evasion, life regen, etc. Now throw on the fact that bitter dead will just continually start doing zdps because the balls just start sitting stationary in front of gigajuiced rare instead of exploding. It can make single expedition remnants take 5-10 minutes to clear if it is juiced enough.

Tl;dr — GGG please fix. This has been bugged for a month and there are already dozens of bug reports about it. Otherwise this would be a contender for my favorite skill in the game.
