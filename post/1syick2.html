Scaling Plague Bearer/Bursting Plague

This league I tried to make my own build using two fairly unpopular things: Gemling and Murkshaft, my boss solve was a heaping helping of Plague Bearer due to its seemingly nice progression at high gem levels, which is one thing I really like about Legionnaire (the other is the Crit defense from Gem Studded). Overall it was a lot of fun but I ran into some trouble in the very late game.

While I was able to clear most content including T15s and T3 bosses, and shined in simulacrums and juiced temples, I struggled to figure out how to scale endgame damage and I wasn’t quite sure how to put everything into PoB to check what was working. I never felt like the kind of build that could confidently do Uber Arbiter damage numbers so I never tried. Although my equipment doesn’t have a lot of value, I did save currency late - but I didn’t spend much of it as I struggled to select the right equipment and support gems in late endgame when those decisions really start to matter. I don’t like how fast I turned away from the challenge of finishing the final improvements to this character, I should have asked for help at the time but I thought I would ask for advice now so others could learn from my folly.

My week 4 build used Poisonburst in conjunction with Bursting Fen Toad for clear (pretty effective and safe), and was designed to rely on Poisonburst/Plague Bearer for bossing which usually resulted in a quick victory via large stacks of Poison DOT as long as I could dodge mechanics and one shots.

https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds/vaal/character/spacegostal-5767/Gisborough?i=0&timemachine=week-4

My week 5 build used different support gems, including an attempt to apply a substantial bleed to bosses with Plague Bearer, Wither with rain of arrows and incorporating Admixture onto Poisonburst… however I didn’t really notice much of a damage boost here, I don’t think most of these were hitting fast or hard enough to apply their ailments. Shortly after this happened I started messing around with Cospri’s Will to apply Wither and then dropped the character altogether in week 6 when I couldn’t figure that out.

https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds/vaal/character/spacegostal-5767/Gisborough?i=0&timemachine=week-5

In retrospect, my brainstormed list of things that I should have done to squeeze more bossing damage out of this is as follows:

  • Be a Pathfinder
  • Lineage gems for plus levels… uthred, dialla series and also uul-netol’s embrace, also obviously 21⁄23 on my main skill gems
  • Better anoint for amulet such as Spiral into Insanity or Dark Entries
  • Better Darkness Enthroned or rare belt or Headhunter? (was mainly using this self-found DE for defensive reasons to stack Damage from Mana before Life or resists but that seems to have been a waste of belt slot)
  • Better jewels including a non scuffed Heart of Well; always struggled to identify which emerald or sapphire affixes were good for PB - for example, should I have tried to scale critical hit% even though PB has a low base chance?
  • Some actual effective way of quickly applying Wither stacks (maybe get spirit on body/amulet for curse aura… or withered wand in second weapon slot for chaos bolt?)
  • Higher level murkshaft with a useful corrupt
  • I concluded that Flat Damage rings would NOT help me to scale Plague Bearer; but would they have?
  • Snakebite gloves for another high magnitude stack of Poison?
  • Corrupted armour pieces for extra rune slots
  • Stronger bow (believe obliterator bow is correct base, but I have several wasted/low-level affixes)
  • Skipping the Increased Damage When Companion is in Presence or related tree nodes, instead using those skill points for something else? Note: Beast companions were selected also for defensive aura and applying shock

Am I on the right track with this, did I just give up a bit too early? Or was this about as good as it would have gotten with the combined downsides of Gemling, squishy EV defense and off meta skills?
