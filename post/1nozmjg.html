Bone Blast - gimmick, pointless or has a use?

So i recently did a playthrough on my bone/phys bloodmage.

I tried to use all of the bone skills in the arsenal: bone cage, bonestorm, bone blast and reap (with occasional detonate dead).

What surprised me was bone cage, which seemed to have started as a defensive area control skill, is actually very strong for different purposes, both for damage, aoe, clear and pin.
Paired with crit and nodes for armor break one cast just instantly breaks armor of monsters and locks them down.

Reap is an odd one as it seems to do different things - cull, crit weakness and aoe.

But then theres the weird one: bone blast.
It comes on a wand only which already is gimmicky. The skill itself has good cast speed but has a massive activation delay, requiring a support gem for faster activation.
On top of that drawback, the skill ALSO has lower damage numbers compared to both bone cage and reap.
The aoe is also quite small, again smaller than both reap and bone cage.

So what exactly is this skill for?

It has some kind of high bleed % which im assuming is the key, but how does that scale and play out?

Only cool synergy ive noticed is like bone cage + bone blast.
Cage pins, so the baddies cant move out of the bone blast. Ive also added the bone shrapnel support to have it explode.
But the blast could easily be replaced by either reap or more of bone cage.

Am i missing anything here?
