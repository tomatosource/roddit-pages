[NBA.com] Luka Dončić, Immanuel Quickley named NBA Players of the Week

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/nba-players-week-luka-doncic-immanuel-quickley

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Dončić and Toronto Raptors guard Immanuel Quickley have been named the NBA Players of the Week for Week 14 of the 2025-26 season.

Luka Dončić (LAL): 34.3 PPG - 10.7 RPG - 9.7 APG - 2-1 team record

Immanuel Quickley (TOR): 25.3 PPG - 6.8 RPG - 61.1 FG% - 4-0 team record
