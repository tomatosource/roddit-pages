Primary skills should not have hard limiters directly integrated. Any payoffs or limiters should come from support gem choices.

Far too many main skills are by design support skills for something else or one half of a rigid and limited option of combinations.

I like the mechanics of dealing more damage efficiently by combining things in a smart and fun way and the way they are limited and combined right now is not nearly as much fun and smart as they could be.

I would rather suggest to make most main skills deal direct damage, but with a across the board lower damage starting point.

Some just feel like wasted and by that forced potential you HAVE TO utilise somehow.

Take Sunder as an example.

There completely remove the extra condition of dealing increased damage against fully armour broken targets and sundering the armour. That entire part of the skill should be in a support gem, not hardcoded into the main skill.

Clean up the main skill, put the second part in a support gem, change the effect to be worth it on the support and voila: It no longer feels like missing out on a core feature of the skill.

Or Killing palm.

There leave in the first part of damage and culling. Then extract the power charge logic into a support gem that also has the ability that the supported skill deals 3% more damage per power charge consumed in the past 8 seconds or whatever.

The important part is to keep the main skill cleaner.

Or Fireball.

Just let it be a fireball. NOT one that also explodes at the end to create an unavoidable screen clutter mess for those who prefer visual clarity.

The explosion with projectiles at the end could easily be a support gem that is like: “Supports spell projectile skills making them burst into 10 smaller projectiles when hitting an enemy or a wall.

-———————–

TL;DR;

Main skills need to stay cleaner in what they do and how because they otherwise feel too much like they are forcing limited build variety.

Too many skills right now feel like they have a support gem “merged” into them that we cannot get rid of.
