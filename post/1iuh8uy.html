3 Quality of life requests for 0.20 patch to improve group dynamics

I know we’re a month away, but here are some things I feel would immensely improve gameplay for all players.

1. Add the ability to toggle colors options for teammates particle effects and spells.

One of my least favorite things about playing with friends is the sheer amount of visual noise going on in the game. This gets outright despicable with multiple players in a party. It would be A M A Z I N G to be able to tweak the coloration of a party member’s spells and effects. For example, instead of fire being red and orange, I’d like to make my teammates look purple/blueish in hue. Instead of poison effects being a uniform green, I could change the color effects to bright pink, etc etc. The amount of player deaths related solely to confusing “Is that friendly poison/shock/fire, or enemy poison/shock/fire?” would drop so much.

2. Add outlines to enemy spells/effects that are “clipped” by terrain.

This is a bit of an edge case, but nothing sucks worse than when you are in tight quarters and you get 1-shot by a really obnoxiously placed burst spell. This only really happens in indoor maps, but in cave/fungus levels you can often die because the mountainous terrain obstructs your vision just enough that you cannot see the spell being cast at your feet, especially if you are in a tight hallway or corridor. Being able to see a small outlined hue around the effect that shows through those terrain edges would help a lot.

3. Add the ability to toggle colors options for teammates minions.

Similar to 1, but playing with multiple witches can be… overbearing on the screen. Being able to color-code their minions would help prevent confusion, especially when you’re running through rituals and there are so many enemies on the screen that not being able to identify friend or foe can result in untimely deaths. this also relates to having minions casting spells, where it can be tough to determine whose inferno skeleton mages are whose, and which particles are friendly vs. enemy
