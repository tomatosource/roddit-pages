OKC has the chance to make history in the Western Conference tonight for the first time since the 1970’s.

OKC currently sits at 49 wins while the next best teams have 39 wins (Denver) or 38 (Lakers/Grizzlies). Denver does not play tonight, so if OKC win their game against the Rockets, they will officially hit 50 wins before any team in their conference even has 40 wins. They will be the first team to accomplish this feat since the 2004-05 Miami Heat, and the first in the west since the 1977-78 Trail Blazers.

Here are some other Western conference teams who have gotten close to this achievement; the teams listed here’s next best conference rival had 42 wins or less:

  • March 6, 2009, the Lakers reached 50 wins while the 2-seed spurs had 41 wins and DEN + HOU had 40. (Won the finals)

  • March 14, 1988, the Lakers reached 50 wins while the 2-seed Mavs had 42 wins. (Won the finals)

  • March 12, 1987, the Lakers reached 50 wins while the 2-seed Mavs had 40 wins. (Won the finals)

  • March 13, 1986, the Lakers reached 50 wins while the 2-seed Rockets had 41 wins. (Lost in the WCF)

  • February 28, 1978, the Portland Trail Blazers reached 50 wins while no other team in the West had 40. (Lost in 2nd round, MVP Bill Walton notably got hurt in that series and missed 4 of the games)

  • March 18, 1976, the Golden State Warriors reached 50 wins while no other team in the west had 40 (in fact, 2nd place and 3rd place had only 35 wins); the warriors won 56 games before another Western team had 40 (Lost in the WCF)

  • February 13, 1971, the Milwaukee Bucks (west at the time) reached 50 wins while no other team in the west had 40. (Won the finals)

Basketball reference doesn’t have week-by-week standings before the 1970-71 season, but here is a similar exercise for the East.

  • March 11, 2024, the Celtics reached 50 wins while the Bucks and Cavs had 42 and 41 wins respectively. (Won the finals)
  • February 25, 2020, the Bucks reached 50 wins while the Raptors and Celtics had 42 and 40 wins respectively. (Lost in 2nd round, Bubble season)
  • March 9, 2015, the Hawks reached 50 wins while the Cavs had 40 wins. (Swept in ECF)
  • March 15, 2013, the Heat reached 50 wins while the Pacers had 40 wins. (Won the finals)
  • March 12, 2006, the Pistons reached 50 wins while the Heat had 41 wins. (Lost in ECF to the Heat)
  • March 15, 2005, the Heat reached 50 wins while the Pistons had 39 wins. (Lost in ECF to the Pistons)
  • February 27, 1996, the Bulls reached 50 wins while the Magic had 40 wins. (Won the finals)
  • March 6, 1992, the Bulls reached 50 wins while the Cavs had 38 wins. (Won the finals)
  • March 18, 1990, the Pistons reached 50 wins while the Bulls had 41 wins. (Won the finals)
  • March 16, 1984, the Celtics reached 50 wins while the 76ers had 41 wins. (Won the finals)
  • March 1, 1983, the 76ers reached 50 wins while the Celtics had 42 wins. (Won the finals)

Overall, the five teams who have accomplished this feat since 1971

  • won the finals twice
  • lost in conference finals twice
  • lost in the 2nd round once, but their best player also got hurt.

A more interesting data point would be looking at the 18 teams who achieved the feat or came close.

  • 11 won the finals (61%)
  • 0 lost the finals
  • 5 lost in the conference finals (28%)
  • 2 lost in the 2nd round (11%, both times have notable asterisks)
  • 0 lost in the first round

It’s a pretty small sample, but since OKC is basically already in this group barring a 4-5 game losing streak, essentially they have a 60% chance of winning the finals; however, another way of looking at it is that if OKC makes the finals, they will win it since 0 teams in this group have lost the finals.
