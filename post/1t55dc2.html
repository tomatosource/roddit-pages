The Timberwolves are the best watch in basketball

This is just an appreciation post for what I think is the best watch currently in basketball.

They play hard on both ends.

Defensively that combine all time great defenders in Gobert and McDaniels along with just shear effort. Not a signle guy takes a possession off even their superstar ant.

Offensively man its so fun. Great actions. Good spacing varied line ups.

Watching Naz Reid cook bigs with his handle and shooting is awesome.

Watching tj Shannon or McDaniels get down hill is awesome.

Watching ant just go Micheal Jordan on them is so sick.

They even got classic bruising power forward Randle who when he gets the mismatch is just surgical.

They might not be the best team but man are they so fun.
