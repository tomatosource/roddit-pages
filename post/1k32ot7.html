I am having a lot of fun

Let me preface this, I am not the typical aRPG player. I dislike “grind” games because I already have to grind in the real world, so I mostly play games for their story, gameplay and immersion.

As mentioned above, PoE1 was not a game I would have ever tried. But, I decided to play it and enjoyed it religiously for a good 60-70 hours while playing through the story and being impressed by the tasteful writing and amalgamation of different real-life cultures and motifs. I completed the campaign, did a bit of endgame and put the game down to continue on my backlog. I was very impressed with the experience. Needless to say, I was excited when they announced a sequel.

Currently, I am playing with 3 of my friends and we are all having a blast. We are casuals, and we understand the frustration from the community at the moment, and in many ways we feel it too. We do wish there were more “wow” moments where crazy unique items drop. I think I’ve gotten 5 unique items across my 45 hours. But, paying attention to the story and the lore has been so satisfying. We have been having a great time getting stronger and diving deeper into the story. I enjoyed how simple the story began with the Count’s tyranny, and how it evolved into chasing the Beast through the Vastiri Desert and then reaching Utzaal and meeting Doryani. The time travel aspect was done really well so far. I cannot wait to see where the story goes. All the environments have been a delight to see, though some were undoubtedly annoying (fuck poison and those jungle maps). They have done such a great job to immerse players in the world, from the character designs, the visuals, environmental designs, dialogue, etc.

What I am trying to highlight in this post is some positivity within all of the negativity. All of the criticism is valid, and Early Access is the time to fix the quirks and what doesn’t work, so let’s be respectful in that endeavor. Thank you GGG for your passion, and my friends and I can’t wait to see what’s in store for us in Act 4, 5 and 6.
