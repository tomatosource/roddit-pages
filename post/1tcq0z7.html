TV networks should show pitch overlay replays to help the audience understand the nuances of pitching

I’m referring to pitch overlays like the ones PitchingNinja posts

As a hardcore fan and player growing up, I find myself needing to explain why certain pitches are so nasty to more casual fans. Often times they don’t understand why a hard splitter that runs straight into the ground, or a sweeper that ends up 10 inches off the plate, can look like a meaty fastball to the hitter out of the hand.

This Altuve vs Ohtani AB is a great example of how overlays can change perception so much. Creators were quick to mock Altuve for swinging at a pitch that far outside, but PN’s overlays show that Ohtani actually hit the tunnel he’d established, and this was just a very nasty pitch.

It’d be great if casuals could see this, and even help hardcore fans understand more about the game they love
