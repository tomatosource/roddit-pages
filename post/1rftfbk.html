Kon Knueppel has officially passed Keegan Murray for most three-pointers made in a season by a rookie in NBA history

Keegan Murray set the record in 2023 with 206 three-pointers. In tonight’s game against the Indiana Pacers, Kon Knueppel passes the record by scoring 207 three-pointers.

Keegan Murray set the record 77 games in the NBA season. With Kon Knueppel breaking the record, there is still 22 games left in the NBA season.
