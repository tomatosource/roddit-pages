Which Michael Jordan achievement is more difficult to break? 10 scoring titles or 6 Finals MVPs?

Both are gonna be extremely difficult to break.

Somebody getting 11 scoring titles requires all time elite scoring ability with a good level of necessary selfishness for 11 seasons.

Somebody getting 7 Finals MVPs requires not only your team to be clearly championship worthy but for you to be the clear best player on that title winning team for 7 seasons. In todays era it’s seems harder than ever to build a dynastic team which makes me lean towards this achievement.
