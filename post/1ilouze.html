Of the 11 NBA players to play 20 Seasons, Chris Paul is the only one under 6’5

This longevity by an undersized NBA player is absolutely unprecedented and I’ve never heard it talked about.

CP3 was measured 5’11 3/4” barefoot at the combine. Below are the heights of the eleven players to play 20 NBA seasons:

Player Height Seasons Played
Kareem Abdul Jabbar 7’2 20

Kevin Garnett 6’11 21
Lebron James 6’9 22
Udonis Haslem 6’7 20
Kobe Bryant 6’6 20
Vince Carter 6’6 22
Jamal Crawford 6’5 20
Chris Paul 6’0 20

Legendary durability from a guy handling the ball and getting bullied by larger players for 20 years. Also, despite his reputation, CP3 is top 40 in playoff minutes (5,442) which is basically identical to Draymond Green’s playoff minutes (5,447)
