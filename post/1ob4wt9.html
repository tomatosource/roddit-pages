Small feedback on occult skills

GGG should have a look at improving the damage of chaos dots skills, ED and contagion play extremely slow compared to many other meta skills. Yes, they scale decently but they feel underwhelming often.

Also, bonestorm does a good damage but perhaps adding auto release on maximum projectile stacks would make it feel much more nicer to play or if they are going to want us timing the casting at least add a perfect timing window that gives us an extra something for the effort.

Doedres was an amazing addition, but it needs work. It makes no sense that we can only make it crit with bloodmage.a few ideas for it: Make it a skill gem so it has its base crit and quality. (Or just fix it so it has base crit) Have it link, like a rune, into a curse skill or vice versa. This could open a whole new world of builds, let it have its int scaling as is but have it do something new per curse it uses; for example, with vulnerability it now does physical damage and breaks armour. With elemental weakness it rotates between cold, fire and light and does tri elemental damage. Etc.

Also, not occult per se, but staves are underpowered and they really need some work. It makes no sense that we lose so much from using them vs wand and focus.
