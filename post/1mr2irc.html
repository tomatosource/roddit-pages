Who is your all time Celtics 5 that you think would beat any other combination?

Inspired from the First to the Floor podcast(definitely go listen; they just did an episode where they snake drafted their all time Celtics 5 man lineups)

Basically which all time Celtics 5 man lineup do you think beats any other combo someone could possibly throw at you? Also pick your coach and if you want to add explanation/how they’d play feel free, all the better

For the rules, you can only use Celtics as they were in Boston, meaning just because we had shaq in 2012, doesn’t mean you can pick him and have prime shaq.

In terms of the era we are playing in, best way I can think to do it is your lineup and coach get an entire summer to train, and they’re aware they’re playing against all these other Celtic lineups the following seasons. You can put the setting as modern day with modern rules, but they’ve got all summer to adjust.

No picking guys based on small sample sizes, gotta have at least one full year of sample size. So no “game 1 porzingis” or something like that. If I think of more rules I’ll add them but that seems good.

My team:

‘24 Jrue holiday-Jaylen brown-Larry bird-Jayson Tatum-‘08 Kevin Garnett

I struggled with this for a long time, guard and center specifically were difficult for me. I ended up going with Jrue just because you NEED role players on great teams, if I go Jrue there I’m getting an absolute beast of a defender and elite shooter/IQ guy. I just think he’s more seamless with the other 4 guys than anyone else. I felt okay going KG at the 5 simply because I don’t think we have any 5s in Celtics history who were big burly scoring 5s who could dunk him under the basket like shaq or wilt or something. Jaylen brown is also probably controversial, it was between him and pierce but I went brown because I value the better switch defense on guards and play finishing vs pierces shot creation/playmaking, bird and Tatum should have that handled

Who is beating my team?
