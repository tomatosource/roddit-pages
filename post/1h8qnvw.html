All-Celtics 3-Point Contest!!!

I don’t know how the rest of the league would feel, but put an all-Celtics 3-point contest out there during All Star Saturday. 8 rotation players, widely regarded as the greatest 3-point shooting full rotation of all time, putting up record-shattering numbers from 3. And then see them all battle each other for who is the best deep shooter on the team. The concept writes itself.

Heck, if the league wants to have a real 3-point contest too, ban the Celtics from entering (for just this season), have the contest in its normal spot, and do the all-Celtics one earlier in the evening, as a replacement for things like the Curry / Ionescu battle from the recent past. If you need to, cut the second round and just advance the top two Celtics so that it saves time.

Hauser or Porzingis? White or Brown? Horford or Pritchard? Tatum or Holiday? Super entertaining to see who will get hot.

This is the unique team for it, and this is the unique year for it. Even fans who hate us will end up rooting for or against somebody in our lineup.
