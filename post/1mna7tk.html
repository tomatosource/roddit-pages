[SDNA] Giannis Antetokounmpo can’t play for Greece in friendly games due to unpaid insurance

From the article

Greece’s superstar Giannis Antetokounmpo has reportedly missed the first few exhibition games for EuroBasket 2025 with his national team due to unpaid insurance.

According to Greek outlet SDNA, Giannis’ absence from Greece’s first EuroBasket warm-up games is due to unpaid insurance.

“Giannis’ appearance is just one payment away — a payment that would ensure insurance coverage in case of an accident or bad moment,” SDNA reports.

“When will that happen? The national team coach wasn’t even sure if Giannis would play on August 14 in Thessaloniki in the next friendly against Montenegro.”
