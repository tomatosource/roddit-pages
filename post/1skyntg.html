Trading and Looting

Even when currency drops, the joy is muted because you’re ultimately just trading it away. There’s none of that exhilaration you’re supposed to feel in a loot-based game—the anticipation that seeing an item on the ground might dramatically change your build.

Spending time searching and comparing items on trading sites is nothing but a chore. I feel stressed by the constant pressure to find the most efficient items.

That said, SSF isn’t fun either, because drop rates and quality are adjusted with trading in mind.

I understand that there are people who enjoy SSF. I also understand that there are people who enjoy trading. That’s why we need to create a new system while keeping SSF intact.

I believe what’s needed right now is a mechanism—like a faction system—where both the trading faction and the non-trading faction can coexist without feeling at a disadvantage.
