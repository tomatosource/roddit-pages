Lategame it is more exciting to pick up Normal / Magic items than rare ones

The reason is simple: It is EXTREMELY expensive to remove bad modifiers. In return, it makes more sense to have pick up a normal / magic items with 2 decent stats, than a rare one with 3 good stats and 1 bad one.

Unless you are a perfectionist, that Essence Upgrade does just fine. +T3 Res is better than +T2 Lifereg for most builds.

Heck, even without essence for that 1 Chaos Orb to re-roll a stat you get 30 Greater Regals right now - without the danger of losing a good stat.

One Abyss is not the current league, it will be more expensive - but right now (unless you go for level 40 Min) that significantly improves the chances for a 4th good stat.

For the last two: “Get something of the same type” are moderately affordable (which is a good thing!)

TL;DR: Rare items tend to drop with bad tiers, even at high levels. Removing bad stats is significantly more expensive than adding “decent” ones to a base.

To add to that - rares dropping unidentified is core to PoE, but it is also why no rare item drop on the ground will ever release dopamine and why most player don’t bother picking them up once they finalized mid maps.

With the auction house being more available ingame, for just one Exalt or Vaal you’ll likely get to buy a levling armour that’s better than the next 50 rare items you loot from the ground.

I’d recommend some drastic changes

  1. Let items drop identified. Identifying is free anyways. Allow players to actually get some dopamine from ground loot.
  2. Significantly improve loot quality, starting ad mid-map levels, to allow them to compete with mid-level crafting
  3. Increase the frequency of Annulment Orb drops
