Skill level requirements feel restraining

While I appreciate the design and mechanical depth of the PoE2 campaign, the early game leveling flow creates a frustrating disconnect. Being forced to slog all the way into late act 4/interludes using skills I don’t want to play just to unlock my actual build at level 58 is incredibly draining.

A massive part of the ARPG appeal is the power fantasy and mechanical identity of your chosen archetype. When you have to spend the first several acts playing an entirely different, unappealing playstyle just to reach the “real” game, the campaign stops feeling fun and starts feeling like a tax you have to pay before you’re allowed to have fun. The leveling experience would feel significantly smoother if build enabling gems were accessible earlier, allowing us to get a feel for our their actual end game builds from the start.

Don’t get me wrong, when I have a build in mind that uses some of the early skills, I have a great time. The problem is when want to play something that requires a skill at a higher level it makes the campaign so difficult to get through and leaves a bad taste in my mouth.

I know it’s not POE1 and people hate the when the two are compared but still, in that game you get unlock all the skills by at least level 45ish, which depending on how fast you are could be within 5 hours of starting the league. Of course that game has it’s own issues with leveling, but most of the time you are able to play the skill you want to by that point, even if it is jank as hell lol.

Let me know if I’m over reacting a bit, maybe I’m just burned out, idk.
