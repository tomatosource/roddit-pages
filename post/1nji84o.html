You should be having moderate expectations regarding the crafting system, especially if you are new to path of exile

There are threads here which ask for even more crafting power and my only thought is the following:

I hate to break it for you but, don’t get used to the crafting we have right now in this game. I know we love it, I am also having a blast, but looking at PoE1 we can expect that the power we have right now may be reverted, at least in some extent.

We went from throwing rocks on items which brick 99% of the time to crafting the most insane gear deterministically for almost every slot. That’s some harvest crafting level.

I think most can agree, that this what we have right now is too powerful. A fruit, too sweet, that it spoils our taste. I think GGG will know too and hopefully they will meet the middle ground between 0.2 and 0.3 crafting.

In the meantime enjoy your crafts but don’t get to comfortable.

Stay sane exiles
