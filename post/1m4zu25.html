🏀 [FR] I launched a Substack to break down NBA tactics in French – for fans who want to understand the game, not just watch it

Hey r/nba,

I recently started a French-language Substack called Étudiant du Jeu (“Student of the Game”) focused on NBA tactics and breakdowns. The goal is to make the game more understandable for French-speaking fans — whether you’re new to basketball or already deep into Xs & Os.

✅ What you’ll find:

•	Breakdown of common NBA actions (Step-Up Screens, Spain PnR, Ghost Screens…)
•	Diagrams and simplified visuals
•	A growing glossary of technical terms (hedge, drop, switch, ICE…)
•	Translation of key tactical concepts, explained clearly in French
•	Occasional video examples from real NBA games

I’m building this as a learning tool for curious fans, players, coaches — anyone who wants to go deeper than just highlights.

🔗 You can check it out here: etudiantdujeu.substack.com

Even if you don’t speak French, you might enjoy the diagrams or find it useful to share with French-speaking friends or teammates. Would love any feedback or topic ideas — and thanks in advance for the support!
