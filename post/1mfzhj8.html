Whirling Assault - How to fix it

I play a Chayula build that uses Culminatiom with Falling Thunder to spank pinnacles. Recently, I learned that Whirling Assault generates combo. I did some testing and found the following:

  • Whirling assault with Practiced Combo will generate about 12 combo per use if all hits land and practiced combo is slotted.

  • The ability is useful for generating power charges with Volls Protector (one use usually caps your charges with a high crit chance).

  • The damage is abysmal but it’s a setup skill.

Although it trimmed about 5 seconds off of a Xesht Kill, I found it very clunky to use. Not being able to cancel it into Ice Strike or redirect its trajectory makes it awful hard to use. It also has a significant “backswing” of sorts that makes timing Mantra of Destruction into Infernal Cry very difficult.

My feedback would be that it should allow you to move around like Rolling Slam and be cancelable with any action, not just dodge roll. If these changes were implemented I could see it being a premiere enabler for something like Culmination (don’t sleep on it, it’s very very good support).
