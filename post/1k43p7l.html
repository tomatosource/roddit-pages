Game Freezing, Lag, and Texture Issues After Season 2 – Anyone Else?

Ever since the launch of Season 2, I’ve been experiencing a lot of issues with the game—freezing, lag spikes, and what looks like texture corruption. One example is the ground not loading properly; sometimes it appears as a patchwork of colored squares. It usually fixes itself after a minute, but the problem is consistent.

At first, I thought it might be my GPU, but I’m running a 3080 TI and haven’t had any issues with other games. I’m playing through Steam and have already verified the game files, but that didn’t help.

The most frustrating part is when I’m in the middle of a boss fight, and the game suddenly freezes. The screen goes white, and I get the “POE 2 Steam Not Responding” message. If I choose to wait, the game usually comes back after a brief time out—but by then, I’m usually dead. It’s happening too often and really killing the experience.

Has anyone else run into this? If so, how did you fix it?

I’m considering uninstalling and reinstalling the game to see if that helps. If it doesn’t, I may just stop playing altogether, which sucks because I really enjoy the game—but the constant crashing is just not worth it when other games are running flawlessly. I had no issues like this in season 1 so im at a loss of what happened.
