Adam Silver, the ultimate capitalist sellout.

Today he basically said: “If you can’t afford NBA games, just watch highlights.”

Think about that. The commissioner of the NBA is openly admitting the league is no longer about the full game, the experience, or the fans who can’t pay. It’s about quick clips they can monetize.

This is the same guy who:

  1. Brought in jersey sponsors (remember when jerseys were sacred?)

  2. Put ads on the court

  3. Turned the NBA Finals into a corporate presentation, “brought to you by” whoever’s paying the most.

And now he shrugs at fans priced out of tickets or subscriptions. Why? Because the real customers aren’t fans anymore, it’s the sponsors.

Basketball has become a highlight league in the eyes of its own commissioner. The game takes a backseat to branding, engagement metrics, and ad dollars.

Are we even surprised?
