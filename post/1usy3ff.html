Could the Washington Wizards actually win 40 games for the first time in almost 10 years?

I’ll caveat that even I’m a little bit skeptical, because, well…the Wizards have been the laughing stock of the league for what feels like anything in recent memory..

But I’m looking at their roster, and for the first time (in almost a decade), I’m actually somewhat excited about it.

Starting Five:

  • (PG) - Trae Young
  • (SG) - AJ Dybantsa
  • (SF) - Bilal Coulibaly
  • (PF) - Anthony Davis
  • © - Alex Sarr

Bench

  • (G) - Bub Carrington
  • (G) - Tre Johnson
  • (F) - Kyshawn George
  • (F) - Julian Champagnie
  • © - De’Andre Ayton

If they coach them the right way (which is a very big IF), this could and should be a semi-decent defensive squad (with the exception of Trae young).

Lots of ifs, but IF Trae and AD both manage to play even 65+ games this season, I could easily see this team sneaking in as a 7th or 8th seed, or at worst, a potential play-in team.

Their bench feels eerily similar to their starting lineup last season (which in this case, is a massive plus).

And it feels like they’ve got 4 guys in their starting 5 that could be legit 15-20 PPG guys in Trae, AJ, AD and Sarr.

AD and Sarr both play solid defense, Dybantsa I believe will make a difference on the defensive end as well. And I think this is the moment where we see Bilal turn into a 3-and-D kind of player.

If nothing else, we should win at least 25 games. 40’s probably too bold…but hell…I’m a Wizards fan..cut me some slack.
