The 48 million that Kawhi got has been bugging me…

It coincides with the 50 million that Ballmer put in Aspiration, but I found out that it also coincides with how much money Kawhi lost from not resigning with the Raptors.

In 2019 if Kawhi resigned with the Raptors the max he could have received from them was a 5 year 190-million-dollar contract. The max the Clippers and Lakers could offer him was only 4 years 141 million dollars due to not having Kawhi’s bird rights. 190-141 = 49 million.

It may just be a coincidence since Kawhi ultimately only signed a 3 year 103-million-dollar deal with the Clippers, but I thought it was just an interesting nugget. This entire scandal has been riddled with coincidences, so I thought I’d add one of my own.
