Prime Dynasty Spurs vs Prime Dynasty Warriors — Who Wins?

We never really got to see peak Duncan, Parker, and Ginóbili go head-to-head with peak Curry, Klay, and Draymond.

In the early 2010s the Warriors were too young to really challenge the Spurs. From 2013 to 2014 San Antonio was still at full strength with Duncan dominating the paint, Parker slicing up defenses, Ginóbili leading the bench, and Kawhi emerging as a Finals MVP-level player. They handled the Warriors in the 2013 playoffs and kept winning most regular season matchups.

By the time Golden State hit its championship prime from 2015 to 2019, the Spurs Big Three was aging out. Duncan retired in 2016, Parker’s quickness was gone, and Ginóbili was in his final seasons. The Warriors became a juggernaut, but most of their playoff battles against the Spurs were against the Kawhi and LaMarcus Aldridge version, not the classic Spurs core in its prime.

So the real question is this: if we took the 2014 Spurs at their absolute peak (the “beautiful game” Finals team) and put them against the 2017 Warriors at their absolute peak (the KD version), who would take a seven game series? Could San Antonio’s ball movement, post scoring, and elite defense slow down the Warriors’ shooting and pace? Or would Golden State’s two-way firepower and ability to blow games open in a few minutes prove too much for even prime Popovich basketball?
