Execute III works with Essence Drain DoT (well half of it does)

I was playing around with support gems for my Essence Drain Lich and I noticed that although the text of Execute III specfies both of its bonuses only affect Hits, the second line still seems to affect the DoT from Essence Drain while you are on Low Life.

From https://poe2db.tw/us/Execute_III

Supported Skills deal 30% more Damage with Hits against enemies that are on Low Life.

Supported Skills deal 30% more Damage with Hits while you are on Low Life.

When I added the gem to support my ED the DoT damage in the expanded window went up by 30%. Initially I was unsure whether this was a display bug or not so I equipped a level 1 ED and went to the Monkey in Interlude III Howling Caverns to test.

After disabling my Withering Presence and Blasphemy my level 1 unsupported ED did ~14.5k damage (sometimes a bit over 15k if I procced a single stack of Withered).

When I added Execute III to my Essence Drain the damage went up to 18-19k, about 30% above what it was doing previously.

Interestingly enough, after I got the Monkey down to low life this number stayed the same so the line that gives 30% more based on the target’s life doesn’t seem to work.
