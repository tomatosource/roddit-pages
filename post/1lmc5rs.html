An Exercise in Manifestation

I don’t know about all of you, but I’ve noticed a big change in the way Jayson Tatum is talked about, both online and in the media. A lot of people seem to have had a bit of a “Come to Jesus” moment following his injury, and it feels like Tatum (and the Boston Celtics more generally) being out of commission for the most immediate future has dialed down a lot of the bias we were seeing these last few years and especially after the championship run.

With that in mind, I’d like to manifest the following: JT’s injury is unique. It wasn’t on his dominant foot, he’s young, he got surgery immediately afterward, and he’s someone who still feels like he has a lot to show the league. I think that he’s going to make a full recovery, or something so close to it that the work he puts into coming back more than makes up any impact the injury might have had. I think his best years are yet to come, that we’re going to see something truly special from him in 2027 and the years that follow, and that people are going to have a newfound appreciation for him after realizing that this generation almost lost one of his greatest players. He’s going to further cement his place on the Celtics Mount Rushmore, and he’s going to retire after a career so long and accomplished that no one can deny him a spot in Top 25 of all time. We’re going to see some of the greatest basketball ever played on the biggest stages the NBA has to offer.

I really believe there are special times in store for this city, this team, and these fans.
