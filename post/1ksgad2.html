POE2 needs more generic transitional uniques.

If the devs want to have an environment with less access to early deterministic crafting, and a more Diablo II style itemization experience, then I think they need to introduce more transitional uniques. Not necessarily items that could be BIS, but items available from lvl30 onward that function to fill basic gearing gaps. Things like:

A set of 20ms res boots, Phys Damage/IAS gloves, A selection of res/stat jewelry items, Some generic Phys damage based weapons, Cast speed rings

Not necessarily things that couldn’t be outclassed later obviously, but things that you’d swap out of gladly if you were even just a little lucky early on. I think these kinds of things would also encourage a more positive attitude with players being in the campaign longer than POE1. It may even encourage new and experienced players to farm campaign areas and not feel so pressured to rush for items that are actually useable longer term, and reusable for future characters.

It’d also cut down on the need to spend so much time on the trade site. If these things are fairly common, then they won’t be crazy expensive to trade anyway, and players who don’t enjoy engaging with trade as it is, or those who wanna dip their toes into SSF have some clear, and helpful early targets with less early RNG.
