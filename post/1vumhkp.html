  3. Lore is still going to remain a minority owner.
  4. The article explicitly indicates that all of this was done this way so they could all continue working together.
  5. So because of 1/2/3 that means when they say 4.5 billion they mean the total valuation of the team. The actual amounts being bought and sold are less than that amount being exchanged.
