My Biggest Wish for the Endless Atlas Map

What bugs me most about the Endless Atlas map is that it looks pretty much the same no matter where you are.

Why not make each biome much bigger so that each one covers about one screen when fully zoomed out? Wherever you are on the Atlas, you can see pretty much all biomes on your screen at the same time.

This would not only help with orientation, but people would also be excited to find their favourite biome when it’s rare and contains 20+ maps instead of only a handful.

They create so many beautiful maps that the variety of maps for larger biomes wouldn’t be a problem.

I hope they consider this for the future.
