Kicked out of Burning Monolith

After spending quite some time with getting the three fragments to open the Burning Monolith I was ready to face the Arbiter. I just leveled to 92 so I couldn’t lose any exp - I knew I was going to fail a couple of times but since you now get to do the first Pinnacle encounter with unlimited tries I didn’t matter.

But after the third failed attempt got disconnected. When I logged back in the portals were still up so I was quite relieved.

But the portals couldn’t be used since I had failed to kill him at the first try - even though the new change states that you get unlimited tries.

So be very aware of this, especially if you’re in Europe like me. Servers are way too unreliable atm, and I think the above applies to any Pinnacle boss on the first try (after that you only get one try so it doesn’t really matter)
