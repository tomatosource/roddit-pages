Looking back at the 2008 finals as an older person

So I was 16 when the Celtics won the title back in 2008. Grew up in Mass, huge Celtics fan. The Lakers had the best player in the world in Kobe and I always had in my mind that the Celtics were the underdogs. That the Lakers were this power house of a team. Now going back and watching it as an adult I see what hot garbage the Lakers had to work with. Kobe, Pau, Fisher, Odom were a great core but after that you had Bynum who was great but young, and we all know how he turned out. Ariza who was young and didn’t get a lot of playing time. Luke Walton you knew what you would get from him. But the other players who got a lot of the playing time: Farmar, Radmonovic, Vujacic, all became a bunch of bums when they left the lakers. I feel like it goes to show that Phil Jackson got the most out of mediocre players and Kobe made everyone around him better. The Celtics bench was deeper and overall better.
