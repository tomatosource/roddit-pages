Bestiary: Custom Hideout Encounters with Rewards for Guests

Hello everyone,

I’ve been thinking about an idea that could be really fun to play: custom beast encounters.

Players could capture beasts on maps, preserving their level and modifiers—perhaps even rare modifiers like Spirit-Touched. The exact capture mechanic wouldn’t matter much; something similar to the system in PoE 1 would work perfectly well.

Players could then use their captured beasts to create custom encounters in their hideouts. They might choose to fight several beasts simultaneously, face them one by one, or add packs of normal, magic, rare, and unique monsters. These encounters could take place in a standard arena or in customizable arenas with decorations.

Other players could access the encounter through a portal in the host’s hideout. The creator could provide a reward by putting up some currency, while guests could inspect the reward, the beasts and their modifiers before entering. Additional encounter modifiers and mechanics could make the fights even more interesting.

It would be great fun to hunt for powerful, high-tier beasts, build challenging encounters for hideout guests, announce them to other players, and watch people risk their lives for your Divines. Each encounter could also display statistics, such as the number of attempts, victories, and deaths.

There would naturally need to be some restrictions—for example, each player could claim the reward only once.

This could be a fun alternative to traditional giveaways. I give away almost all my currency in a month of every league, and a mechanic like this would make the experience much more entertaining and memorable.
