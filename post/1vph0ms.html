0.5.5 / 1.0 Balance/QoL Wishlist

Listed in no particular order. I am satisfied with the foundations of progression and the endgame loop, but the current balance chasm presents a real risk to sustained enjoyment. If I missed anything or you strongly disagree with any of the points go for it in the comments, I won’t be editing this post.

Outdated Ascendancies

Spirit Walker, Martial Artist, Oracle, the adjustments to Chronomancer and Gemling - GGG is more than capable of making interesting ascendancies with unique power. Please revisit the older ascendancies and adjust/reinvent them as necessary to give people a reason to play them beyond “let’s try something different.”

Skills and Advanced Thaumatury

Gemling’s Advanced Thaumaturgy has shown that many skills only require subtle (or sometimes obvious) adjustments to enter the realm of generic viability. I like the power that the ascendancy brings, but I would prefer it to be rooted in enabling certain niches instead of elevating a skill from mediocre to uniquely viable.

Scaling Vectors

Crit rules and it’s boring. There are exceptions to the rule but many system have obvious deficiencies with respect to scaling: damaging ailments? Scale the hit. CoC? Scale crit freqeuncy and hit saturation to drop comets from the sky. Generator/spender patterns are rather one dimensional. +Spell level affixes are in the same vein. In short: either provide more options for scaling vectors or vectors which are multi-dimensional.

CoC/CoA

Beating a dead horse with this one, but GGG should reevaluate how tightly they have coupled spell balance to the existence of CoC/CoA etc. Meta-skills have their place in build diversity and alternative scaling vectors, but at this rate GGG is entrenching the self-cast-problem that exists in PoE1 today in PoE2 systems.

Chasing Cities + Grand Mirror

If there is one thing to address for the endgame, this would be the one. There is the broader issue of needing to chase points-of-interest on the Atlas which make content overly restrictive and devolve into funnel strategies, so ideally GGG would produce a more holostic solution. The sentiment is not too different from the current PoE1 league where the voyages are interesting but processing individual charts is tedious.

Skill Tiering

I wouldn’t mind seeing some of the skill unlocks be compressed so players have earlier access to skill diversity or bring builds online earlier. I would prefer all skills are available by the start of act 4 (easy lore tie-in by bringing Doryani to the present - you have personal access to the gem guy, surely he has all the gems).

Basic Rune Volume

I’m fine without the crafting bench, but the bench did permit sinks for early game currency to solve problems. As it stands the only use for my trans/augs/alchs/chaos in early progression is to click gear. I have to pray I drop the runes I need. Interludes Ferryman and the current league mechanic provides some relief on rune pressure but that is an artificial solution.

Divine Sink

I focus on HCSSF so I have less skin in the game here, but I can appreciate the issue. As I understand it GGG has counterbalanced this by strictly partitioning rewards between league mechanics, but perhaps an additional limited use for Divines is necessary to alleviate the inflation pressure.

Trial of Chaos

GGG has already spoken to this in recent interviews so nothing much to add here. I personally find PoE2’s ideas for Chaos more interesting than PoE1’s Ultimatum (again, not everybody needs to absolutely love the mechanic), it’s just that execution and scaling have suffered over the lifetime of EA development.

Ascending

I think Trial of Sekhemas is fine, there just needs to be a reasonable alternative for 3rd/4th ascendancy (especially for the 4th ascendancy). It’s a bit of a tricky problem because even PoE1 players get this wrong when comparing it to Lab: PoE1 ascension has been solved by power creep. If its difficulty had been preserved through the years the complaints today would be endless. Ascending should have challenges and rewards, but they should be relative and diverse.

Life vs ES, Passive Tree etc.

Another dead horse topic. Max hit for life is still a concern. Life on the tree will probably only move goalposts or create new problems (every life node you click will be less offensive power, the number of viable builds will dwindle). We understand that there is a problem, but the solution is not obvious and requires broader adjustments to game systems and the passive skill tree.

I’ve been typing away for awhile now so I’ll probably stop there. Naming problems is easy, coming up with solutions that are net-positive is hard. Really hoping GGG sticks the landing for 1.0. Also swords please, I’m tired of the only good melee skills require a shield or turning me into an animal. I do understand GGG likely wants to have swords/axes/etc. transcend PoE1 strike skills but it’s cruel seeing Geonor get all the cool shit.
