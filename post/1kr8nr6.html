Path of Exile 2 account BREACHED - 1 Month later

Hello everybody, after being informed by GGG that nothing can be done on their end I have no other option other than to at least inform all of you here that play Path of Exile 2. This was all due to GGG not offering any form of direct 2FA and still allowing users to log in via the client.
