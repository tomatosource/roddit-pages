RIP Nellie Ball 🌹🌹🌹🌹🌹

Don Nelson was a 5-time NBA Champ in the late 60’s and early 70’s. Undoubtedly a contributing factor to the dynasty, leading to his number 19 being retired by the C’s.

As a coach, he’s the second winningest in league history and 3-time coach of the year. He coached 5 different teams, including the 1994 Team USA “Dream Team II”, and always had an eye for talent.

He also pioneered the aforementioned “Nellie Ball”, a style of play that involved fast paced & high scoring offenses. All of this and more lead to his Naismith HOF induction in 2012 and the earning of the Chuck Daly Lifetime Achievement Award.

Boston lost a legend today. Rest in Peace to Donald Arvid Nelson. ☘️
