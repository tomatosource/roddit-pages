The Boston Celtics have brought in 6 different All-Star Point Guards over the last 20 seasons.

Among their starting PG’s who were All Stars, and those who made it during and after their Celtics career are:

  • Rajon Rondo (2006-2015) was a 4× All-Star with Boston
  • Isaiah Thomas (2015-2017) was a 2× All-Star with Boston
  • Kyrie Irving (2017-2019) is an 8× All-Star; 1x with Boston
  • Kemba Walker (2019-2021) was a 4× All-Star; 1x with Boston
  • Jrue Holiday (2023-2025) was a 2× All-Star (2013, 2023)

Edit: Since others have pointed out Jeff Teague, Jameer Nelson among some of those missed, Mike Conley should not count (these guys were washed). It should be 5 All-Star starting Point Guards over 20 years. Points above are still notable and valid.

They’ve also had other award-winning point guards like Pritchard (6MOTY), Brogdon (6MOTY) and Smart (DPOY) in this time, not to mention Derrick White. Boston has basically always had an All-Star quality guard on their roster since 2006.
