Tactician - watch how I do it and minions

This ascendancy node provides 25% of your main hand weapons damage to allies in your presence.

This sounds like It should be amazing for minions on paper; however, in practice, it’s just not good.

Taking a look at possibly thebest possible candidate for this, skeletal reavers.

Even with a literally mirror tier 2 handed mace (and getting the attributes to wear it with giants blood) Trenchtimber and the +2 gem level from gemling just strictly overperforms it in damage. This isn’t taking into account how you don’t need 600 strength or a mirror tier mace and the minion survivability and spirit cost benefits you get from 4 extra minion levels.

Just to put it further into perspective, even a titan with just the 50% more small passive node effect and a trenchtimber almost performs as well as the tactician in damage in the best possible scenario for the tactician

When you look at almost any other minion, this problem is even more apparent, reavers have a much higher attack speed than most other minions.
