Ritual Sacrifice on Self QoL

As one of the 3 people playing a non-zoo Huntress this league, I wanted to say that I really appreciate the changes to Ritual Sacrifice since it was introduced way back when. However, there’s one small thing about it that has been driving me a little bonkers.

When you use it on yourself to gain a random modifier, there’s no indication of what modifier you actually got if it isn’t one of the abilities with a fancy visual effect. And unless I’m looking in the wrong place, it’s not listed on your character screen, either.

I know that it likely pulls from the same code as Headhunter, which doesn’t show individual modifiers (for reasons that I definitely understand), but if it were possible, I would love a tweak to self-cast Ritual Sacrifice that lets you see what random modifier you obtained.
