Hi,

Im not even sure what exactly Im asking here Im just confused and wanted some feedback.

Im pretty new to this genre. I had plenty of fun during the campaign: finding new skill gems, seeing new bosses, progressing through the story, etc.

I dont want to talk down about the game the game itself is awesome. Im just wondering if I misunderstood the genre and maybe its simply not for me. I usually enjoy MMOs and Souls-like games, so maybe Im approaching PoE2 with the wrong expectations.

I bought the early access because the game looked amazing.
