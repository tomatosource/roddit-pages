Tonight’s Padres-Mariners game was the 25th game in MLB history in which a team scored 3 or more runs on 1 or fewer hits.

The last time it occurred was on April 4, 2016, when the Rangers scored three runs despite being one-hit by the Mariners.

In fact, tonight marked the first time a team had one or fewer hits, scored three or more runs, and lost since the Brewers were defeated by the Tigers 4-3 on May 25, 1988.
