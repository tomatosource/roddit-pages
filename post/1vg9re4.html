Exiles who’ve come into a ton of currency, I have a question for you.

I recently dropped a Voice Sapphire and was very tempted to ID it.

But I actually decided not to, because I had this thought: if I miraculously hit the gamba and got something absolutely insane, why would I then want to keep playing?

I understand hitting 1 Mirror and using it to massively improve your build. That’s both a huge amount of progression and you still have a ton of chase left.

But once you’re talking about 6+ Mirrors, you’ve basically won the league. Your build is juiced beyond belief, and there’s arguably nothing left to chase.
So that’s what I’m wondering:

Why keep playing once you’ve reached the point where you simply can’t meaningfully make your character more powerful?

Isn’t the chase itself the whole point?

I do understand for those who like making build guides and using the currency to experiment before the next league. So I’m mainly asking those who juice their builds and carry on playing the same build every day.
