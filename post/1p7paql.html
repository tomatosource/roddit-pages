Druid Ascendancy Skill Concept: Primal Inheritance

Primal Inheritance: is a shapeshift-focused Ascendancy node that introduces a new mechanic called “Essence Capture” (similar to Tame Beast), allowing the Druid to convert creature attacks and abilities into shapeshifting skill gems.

- Essence Capture: Conjure Azmeri wisps to engulf a creature for a duration. If you defeat the creature while it is engulfed in wisps, the creature’s attacks and abilities will be transformed into skill gems.

  • Using these abilities transforms you into that creature to perform the action.
  • The gained skill gems inherit their base level from “Essence Capture” at the time of capture.
  • The gained skill gems can also be socketed with support gems like any normal skill.

(Note: Modifiers will NOT be inherited, only native attacks and abilities)
