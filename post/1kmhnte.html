20h in complete ARPG noob - overwhelmed but kind of loving it?

Just hit level 40 with my Huntress and finished off the Bog Witch. This is my first ARPG ever, and PoE2 has been… a lot. But in a good way?

I’ve never played a game that lets you straight-up parry with a shield (unless bosses are in SA mode, I guess). I used that to chip away at the Chapter 1 boss—died a bunch, but it felt earned. The combat honestly reminds me of Dark Souls in a good way. Boss fights are rough, but super satisfying once you get them down. I also didn’t expect to enjoy the pet-catching thing, but it kind of works? Feels like Pokemon and provides some extra targets. Of course, I’m still struggling in some areas. Mobs in this area are wiping me out, and I have no clue what stats I should be focusing on to survive. The talent tree looks amazing but also terrifying. I keep hearing that “you need a build for endgame,” but I’m just hoping I don’t accidentally brick my character before I even get there. Still, despite the confusion and getting my face kicked in, I’m kinda hooked. There’s something really satisfying about figuring stuff out bit by bit. If any other newer players are out there—how did you approach the early game? Anything you wish you knew sooner?
