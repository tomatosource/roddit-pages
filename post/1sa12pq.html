Let me hear some of your favorite forgotten individuals performances.

I’m not talking about a grant or Kelly game 7, but a random regular season or a non close out playoff game. Could either be a loss or a win.

Couple contenders from me:

Marcus Smart Game 6 2023 ECSF Vs PHI: game is remembered for Tatum, but Marcus won them that game. 22-5-7 and totally kept us afloat till JTs knockout.

Evan Turner’s 10-11-12 game against the worst Knicks team maybe ever in 2015: Turner had 3 TD’s in like a month. One of my all time favorite Celtics and I remember loving his game so much. Couldn’t shoot couldn’t really defend, wasn’t a great athlete but he was a fun player.

Antoine Walkers first game back in Boston: 24-10-2 in a win against Utah. That was when I really first started to get into it, and I was so excited to have him back. Never really got to see it, but loved having him and Paul.

Tatum’s second half against PHI in game 4 of the 2023 ECSF: the most LeBron like performance of anyone I’ve ever seen. 1-9 in the first half, 8 for 11 in the second half for 22 points. He was everywhere had some insane defensive plays and I still Think we win a title if Jaylen doesn’t help off harden for kinda no reason.

Jaylen last year Vs the Knicks in game 5: Jaylen didn’t quit for one second even when everything was lost. Gave me a more confidence in what was going to happen next.

Jaylens first game against LeBron in 2017: looked so athletic and powerful. Had 19-5-2 in a 6 point loss. So much potential and he exceeded it.

Just a few really fun games we forget about, but man have they been fun.
