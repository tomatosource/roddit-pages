Embiid take

The Sixers without Embiid are a backcourt dominant offense running their sets through Maxey and Edgecombe and to a lesser degree George and I think this has given us problems because of the first two guys quickness. When or If Embiid shows up the offense will start flowing to a great degree through him, which will slow them down and make them play in a more deliberate manner which I believe will feed into our strengths. Not sure if I’m articulating this effectively but despite Embiid being an elite player I think his presence on the court will detract from the players that we have had some difficulty guarding so far in this series, anyone else see it this way?
