The Indiana Pacers winning the finals would be the lowest regular season record and Net Rating since the 1994-95 Houston Rockets

1994-95 Rockets went 47-35 and had the 11th best Net Rating in the NBA that season

Every champion from 1996-2024 was at least 52-30 and top 8 in net rating

The Indiana Pacers finished the season at 50-32 and had the 12th best net rating

Would the Pacers winning the finals give them an argument for being the biggest underdog to win in NBA history? Especially with the context of them going up against a 68-14 Thunder team who just posted a historic net rating and have the MVP?

History is being made with one of the greatest seasons of all time from the Thunder, or one of the most unlikely champions from the Pacers. Going to be great to see a series ending with a historic year either way.
