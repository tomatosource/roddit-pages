Luka Doncic becomes the fastest Laker to make 100 three-pointers

With his 3rd triple of the first half against the Houston Rockets, Luka Doncic has reached a century of three-pointers made for the Lakers. He has done it in 27 games since his debut on February 10, the fastest ever in franchise history.

After a poor shooting start to his Lakers career, Doncic has made 41% of his threes over the past 20 games, averaging 30/8/8 in that span on 45/41/82 shooting splits.
