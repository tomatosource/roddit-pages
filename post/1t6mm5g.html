I believe base game content should have priority over just pumping end game.

As you saw in the stream, there’s no swords, no other weapon types and no duelist, and skill tree is still where it is.

I do know, that people who are fine playing with their spells or whatever their preferred melee weapon want more content, thus there’s more end game stuff now with the upcoming patch, nevertheless they should finally add to the game the VERY BASICS of any game, aka weapon types.

At this moment we’re still missing a lot of weapons, weapons that were featured A LONG TIME AGO, yet they’re still not added to the game. With more weapons come more skills, thus more builds. Poe is known specifically for build freedom, and end game content variety, and sure I am happy about more content and more things to do, but as a melee only guy, who HATES slow melee builds (hammers), and who pretty much only played quaterstaves in poe2, I’m just plain bored and uninterested in playing this game, no matter how much more end game content they add.
