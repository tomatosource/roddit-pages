If the Celtics want Giannis and the Bucks want to re-route Jaylen elsewhere, the Portland Trail Blazers are the perfect third team

The Blazers have the 2029 unprotected Bucks pick and 2028⁄2030 Bucks swaps. They were a playoff team this year but really struggled offensively. They have salary filler in Jerami Grant and Shaedon Sharpe. And honestly, I went on their reddit, and their whole fanbase seems into the idea of getting Jaylen for those picks.

I think that’s the framework if Brad wants to make the Giannis move, and he’s already made two trades with Joe Cronin before (three if you count salary dumping Banton). Plus, the Blazers are well below the tax for next year (about $30 million), so they can acquire JB and have no issues staying under for their cheap owner.

I can also see the Pelicans too, but I think Portland is most likely.
