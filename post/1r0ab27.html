[The Oklahoman] Jalen Williams to return from hamstring injury for Thunder vs Lakers

https://www.oklahoman.com/story/sports/nba/thunder/2026/02/08/jalen-williams-injury-update-okc-thunder-star-hamstring-strain-will-play-vs-lakers/88582739007/

OKC Thunder star Jalen Williams will make his return from injury during a road game against the Los Angeles Lakers at 9 p.m. CST Monday.

Williams has missed the past 10 games due to a right hamstring strain. The All-NBA forward is averaging 16.8 points, 5.6 assists and 4.8 rebounds through 24 games this season on 47% shooting from the field (30% from deep).
