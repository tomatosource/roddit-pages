Skills in this game seem intentionally weak

Everyone has already mentioned how auto attack is better than most skills DPS wise, but one thing I’m starting to realize about POE2 is that skills are intentionally nerfed below their base level and the game expects you to pick up a bunch of nodes on the tree and stats from gear to make the skill go back to being playable. I’m not talking about just damage numbers. I’m talking about the actual viability and animation of the skill. The reason auto attack is so strong is because it’s hard to weaken that too much. The only real way to do it is to lower attack speed or add some absurd animation, which would make the game painfully and obviously bad on the tutorial stage.

I’m playing merc, and most other skills like grenades and any crossbow skill is basically not playable until you obtain a good amount of crossbow reload speed, detonation time reduction, and cooldown reduction. It feels like the skills are bad until you make them good, which requires you to invest heavily into making them just barely usable again.

And that’s not fun. Skills should be usable from the start and only get better with time. Not completely useless and become just usable as you go on. Getting reload speed on the tree doesn’t feel like a buff. It feels like I’m going to baseline of what I should be. In POE1, take frostbolt or freezing pulse for example. They can carry you through campaign pretty easily and they’re extremely powerful and useful skills on their own. But as you go, you might get greater multiple projectiles to make them clear better or more damage or faster cast speed, but their base cast speed is never bad and they don’t have ridiculous requirements to get working. When you get buffs to them, you feel those buffs and feel a sense of constant progression.

In POE2, Scattershot support not only reduces damage but ALSO attack speed. For explosive grenade, which already attacks at 75% of base, that means the animation is absolutely terrible to shoot as part of your combos because it takes longer than reloading your crossbow just to shoot it and then you add onto that the additional 20% less speed from scattershot and a few 8% reduced attack speed here and there from the tree, and you have a really bad feeling skill. Not to mention the detonation time of 3 seconds and cooldown of 5. Which again, feels like you have to work to get back to baseline.

Skills should feel good from the beginning and feel better as you go. Instead they feel bad and go back to baseline as you go.
