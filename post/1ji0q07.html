Looking for Help to Improve Exile Companion – Send Your Item Photos!

Hey everyone,

As some of you may know, I’m the author of the Exile Companion app, designed to help console players quickly check item prices:

I’m constantly working to improve the app, and right now I’m planning to introduce an auto-crop feature that will skip the manual cropping step. However, to make this happen, I need to train a machine learning model, which requires a diverse dataset of item photos — and that’s where I need your help!

If you’re interested in supporting the app, I’d be incredibly grateful if you could send me 2-3 photos of different items. Ideally, I need:
✅ Items of various rarities
✅ Different item types (weapons, armor, jewels, etc.)
✅ Photos taken from different angles, distances, and in different lighting conditions
✅ Photos in different languages

The more variety, the better!

I previously posted this request, but I believe the timing wasn’t ideal as only a few people sent photos. So I’m posting this again and hoping for better engagement this time.

Additionally, many people understandably sent mostly rare items, but I’d really appreciate it if you could also snap photos of magic, unique, and normal items to improve identification accuracy across all item types.

Once I gather enough photos, I’ll start labeling them, training the model, and working on implementing the feature — so it may take some time.

I really appreciate your help with this! Thank you in advance for your time and support, and I hope this request isn’t breaking any subreddit rules — I’m just trying to make the app even better for the community ❤️
