Injuries are often a big talking point during the season, so now the chapter is closed let’s have a look at who truly was hit the hardest.
Injury impact can be viewed in many ways. I’ve looked at four criteria for teams across the league. Sparing you the detailed methodology/caveats, here’s a quick breakdown:

  • Games Missed
    • The simplest and most obvious. The total number of games players missed due to injury
  • Salaries Lost
    • The salary per game lost by a player sitting due to injury. For an approximation of player value, the biggest contract a player has on the books was used
  • Player Impact Missed
    • EPM (Estimated Plus-Minus) contributions lost based on possessions missed
  • Wins Missed
    • Total wins forgone based on a player’s LEBRON WAR (Wins Above Replacement) and games missed

Five Takeaways:

  • Despite missing the least amount of games in the league, Boston ranks 1st for impact lost, almost solely off the back of Tatum. Great to see him back so soon
  • Oklahoma City are insane. Being top 5 in every most injured category while having the best record in the league has frankly been underappreciated
  • Memphis have had a rough go at it for sure. 200 more games of manpower missed than the runner-up is ridiculous
  • San Antonio, New York and Minnesota have been some of the healthiest playoff teams all year. Does that factor into your predictions at all, as more teams are ramping up?
  • It’s Terry Rozier. Technically on leave, but perhaps we can account for it under brain injuries
