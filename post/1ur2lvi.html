[Gozlan] Following the John Collins sign-and-trade being made official, the LA Clippers could now choose to expand the Kawhi Leonard trade in order to acquire Peyton Watson OR Cameron Johnson.

Source: https://open.substack.com/pub/yossigozlan/p/kawhi-leonard-trade-raptors-clippers?r=nuq3a&utm_medium=ios

The Clippers officially signed and traded Collins to the Pistons, creating a \(17 million trade exception. The next way they could acquire Peyton Watson is by expanding the Leonard trade. They could take back up to \)63,125,000 for Leonard’s \(50.3 million (125 percent expanded trade exception + \)250,000).

If they acquire Dick into the Collins trade exception, then they could trade Leonard for Ingram’s \(40 million and still take back an additional \)23.1 million. That’s where they could fit Watson on a \(23,125,000 starting salary on a contract that could total \)99.4 million over four years. They could alternatively acquire Cameron Johnson, whose $23.1 million salary would fit into the remainder of the trade exception.
