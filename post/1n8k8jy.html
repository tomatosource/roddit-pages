Trial of Sekhema should be one floor per ascendancy with longer floors

The first Sekhema trial is a walk in the park, from the rooms themselves to the very boss. It’s short and if you know what you are doing you can get it done in 5 minutes.

But after you beat the first boss, the whole trial extends both its difficulty and worse, length. The length itself wouldn’t be much of a problem by itself if not for the honor system, which is a layer of artificial difficulty that nullifies the relic diversity, forcing you to hoard honor resistance relics or, with some classes/builds, skip it entirely.

This is an unnecessary punishment that is bound to change ahead in time, when the “meta” shifts towards doing the Sekhema’s only for the first ascendancy and all the other three in Chaos or Ancestors (assuming they are arena based like in PoE1).

Given that GGG won’t renounce to the honor system, I think the best solution would be:

  • 1 ascendancy = 1 floor.
  • Buff the trial so each floor is longer, adding more rooms for boon/debuff diversity.
  • Maybe add an “Enriched” version like in PoE1 with more than just 1 floor, for those who want bigger rewards or even make money by carrying other players.
