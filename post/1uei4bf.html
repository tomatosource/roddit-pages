Delirium “Hot/Cold” mechanic needs severe tweaking or removal. It objectively does not take map layouts into account.

Even though it’s just been added, the Deli ‘Hot/Cold’ system needs some serious reworking. So many layouts are winding with stubby dead ends or paths that lead away from the boss encounter to wrap around to get towards the boss. The mechanic just doesn’t align with the way the maps are built.

This culminates in the bar not being reactive to the map boss location in relation to your relative position on said map. In some cases, having the bar get “warmer” as you move away from the boss room. Like what I tested here (Fortunately, this was a deli fog map, and not a mirror deli) https://streamable.com/52h62h

With more map layouts added over time, it’s only going to exacerbate the issue.

EDIT: I’m specifically talking about the Hot/Cold mechanic and how it works in deli mirror maps, no fog maps where it’s redundant.
