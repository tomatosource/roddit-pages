5x NBA All-Star Wesley Unseld was born and raised in Louisville, Kentucky.

Unseld was best known for his outlet passing and rebounding.

5x All-Star Kevin Love credits his outlet passing to his godfather, Wes Unseld. Kevin Love’s father, Stan was Unseld’s teammate and a close friend.

Unseld (1969) and Wilt Chamberlain (1960) are the only players in NBA history to win the Rookie of the Year & MVP in the same season.

Unseld won a championship with the Bullets in 1978 and was named Finals MVP. Unseld led the Bullets to the NBA Finals in 1971, 1975 & 1979.

Unseld averaged 10 points, 14 rebounds and nearly 4 assists during his 13 year Hall-of-Fame career with the Bullets.

Despite being a 6 foot 7 center, Unseld finished Top 5 in total rebounds from 1969-76.
