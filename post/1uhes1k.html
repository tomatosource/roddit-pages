Who made the most of their time in the NBA?

Total Career Earnings (via Spotrac) divided by Total Career Minutes Played (via Basketball Reference) 

Applying my strict baseline requirement, the player must have a minimum of 6 years of contract history.

Here is the official top 50 adjusted for inflation:

| Rank | Player | Inflation-Adjusted $/Minute | Era Peak / Structural Context |

| **1** | Shaquille O’Neal | **\(21,410\*\* | \*\*Highest Historical Leap.\*\* His \)120M Lakers contract and subsequent extensions are worth nearly double in today’s dollars. |

| **2** | Jonathan Isaac | **$19,820** | Modern max-level extensions paired with massive chunks of time missed due to knee injuries. |
