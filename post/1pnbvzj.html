My thoughts on Infinite Atlas

Why not make the infinite atlas into continents?

Let’s say each continent has 80 maps, and you can travel to a new continent after clearing 40 maps. each continent has travel nodes North, East, South, West. if you travel south, there wouldn’t be a North travel node since that’s the continent you came from

There should still be an incentive to clear a continent, maybe the people of the continent are happy that you made it safe for them, so they reward you.

Right now the infinite Atlas is never going to work in my opinion, because after 2-3 days of mapping the atlas gets so big, and i lose all sense of direction and it just turns into a big headache. if it was all split into continents this issue would be fixed, and i also feel like it would bring back the feeling of exploration
