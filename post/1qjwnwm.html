Hey guys!

Please meet the EasyExile Trade Extension!

- for Chrome -&gt; LINK
- for Firefox -&gt; LINK

For PoE 2 it now supports 2 features:

⭐Bulk Sellers finder
it gathers and shows all the sellers with more than 1 items within current query. It’s especially handy for buying Waystones, bases to craft or Uniques to gamble on

https://preview.redd.it/2plrco66zweg1.png?width=952&format=png&auto=webp&s=96482f0ce0a2472f961942461ceb270eff26f9e4

⭐Modifier highlight
this lets you put any keyword to search for within item’s data (also the one that is only visible on hover) - for example if you look for Sorcerer’s, as in T1 Staff affix, instead of Essence that also gives you +5 to all Spells. This comes in handy for certain crafting projects

https://preview.redd.it/kugk80jvzweg1.png?width=1014&format=png&auto=webp&s=894f6cad97e08d6cea36852313b519b64b3fb5e9
