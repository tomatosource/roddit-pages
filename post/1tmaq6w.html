Absent Amulets: Deepish Dive

Let me preface this by saying that I do believe these items are incredibly powerful and open up many possibilities. The bases remove an implicit, a prefix and a suffix while awarding you a spirit reservation skill that does not cost additional spirit to activate. The amulets include all skills at 30, 60 and 100 reservation. We have no reason to believe additional spirit costs of supports in these skills will be “free” like in Cold, Fire and Lightning resistance aura sceptres since other sceptres do not all have this “feature” though who knows.

First let’s consider cases where they are slam dunk items:

  • Kaom’s Heart has the modifier “You have no spirit”
  • Acolyte of Chayula’s Darkness abilities also “Replace your spirit”

In these two cases, you can now get spirit reservation skills where you otherwise couldn’t. This creates a ton of new options for these two edge cases. There maybe be other niche cases like Invoker’s who take the spirit node but need an additional aura.

Another positive case is in situations where your build is interested in stacking or being extremely greedy for spirit. These amulets should be able to still roll the 50ish spirit as a prefix that other amulets can, resulting in 80-150 spirit in your amulet slot which was previously capped at around 65 if you combine implicit and affix. Increases the maximize pseudo-spirit of amulets by 85.

  • Minion builds can run auras without sacrificing spirit usage
  • Totem builds utilizing the totem keystone can fit auras without losing totems
  • Abyssal Lich’s converting skeletal minions into key build offense/defense
  • Other builds with extremely greedy spirit usage, especially if you cannot fit sceptres.

In comparison to a max spirit Solar Amulet: Some people seem to be considering these amulets mandatory or best in slot for all builds. However, considering that a spirit implicit and modifier can give you 65 spirit, this would mean that the 30 reservation tier is relatively bad and the 60 spirit reservation is better but still potentially not worth the drawbacks. The only case I can see you using one of these are possibly niche cases where you cannot meet attribute requirements of the gem. Afaik, skills awarded by equipment bypass any attribute requirements.

In the case of 100 tier reservation skills: You have to consider the following; If you take the spirit prefix, this leaves you with ONE prefix available. If you don’t take a spirit prefix, this is essentially only netting you 35 spirit over a max roll solar amulet. Both have 2 available prefixes in this case, but the Absent amulet trades that 35 spirit for a suffix slot. Consider whether 35 spirit is worth whatever suffix you’d be sacrificing. It is incredibly powerful in either case if you can afford the trade-offs.

Ask yourself if your build really needs/can support this item. While strong, the opportunity cost isn’t minor. Especially for builds that run hybrid defenses who would want say 50%es and 50%ev from their amulet or those who are stacking life or mana that want both flat and % increased, those that utilize uniques for load-bearing core aspects of their build functionality in the amulet slot and those who struggle to meet resistances due to rune/unique/affix demands in other slots. Additionally, with the new double instilled delirium amulets there is additional competition to consider in this slot.

Finally, we have every reason to believe that meta options in the 100 spirit reservation category may be prohibitively expensive for many players. They’ve stated these to be more rare and we don’t know how common they will ultimately be.
