Semi-Realistic Potential Center Trades/Signings Going Forward Into the Offseason

Considering that after missing Giannis, our main concern is our center rotation, here are some plausible moves we could make to improve it:

- White to MIN for Gobert/Naz Reid
- White and several assets to Utah for Walker Kessler in a S&T (Unrealistic but most ideal)
- Sign one or two Porzingis, Timelord, Mitchell Robinson, Nurkic?

Despite this sub’s opinion seeming largely negative in terms of a potential Gobert addition, I think it (coupled with the Wolves’ interest in White) is the most realistic trade we could make to actually improve our current roster for next season.

Gobert would provide a defense presence that we missed after losing Horford and Porzingis to some extent, albeit without the offensive impact. Another positive would be getting Queta into a backup role, limiting his minutes and fouls while making him one of the better backup bigs in the league.

Due to all of the centers we have a shot to get other than Porzingis being offensively mediocre to outright bad, we likely need to use the draft to get more shooters or a stretch big if one falls to use (unlikely). If white is traded, a defensive guard would be nice. That said, it is highly unlikely barring a trade to move up that we get any real impact out of the draft.
