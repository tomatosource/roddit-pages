The Memphis Grizzlies will be actively looking for ways to upgrade their roster this offseason, but they are not intending to trade for Phoenix Suns star Kevin Durant, a source told The Commercial Appeal.

Memphis was interested in Durant at the trade deadline in February and contemplated an offer, according to the source. However, the Grizzlies haven’t engaged in trade talks this offseason with the Suns for Durant.

The Grizzlies traded Desmond Bane on June 15 and created more roster flexibility. That move has led to rumors and reports, ranging from a potential rebuild to interest in Durant, who is expected to be traded from the Suns this offseason.

Ja Morant and Jaren Jackson Jr. remain franchise cornerstones and key pieces for the franchise to build around.

With cap space and some of the best draft capital in the league, the Grizzlies could still make several moves aimed at improving the roster.
