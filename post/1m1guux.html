Tower change suggestion

Towers being linked so closely (literally) to juice is unfun and tedious.

The tablet slot should be removed from towers, tablets should be reworked into PoE2 scarabs.

Towers should be more rare, provide a large map reveal upon completion, and have a high % tablet drop rate which also off sets the increased consumption of these items. That way, you can better control the juice/content on your maps and towers are exciting.

Relatively minor change that would make endgame 20x more enjoyable for me.
