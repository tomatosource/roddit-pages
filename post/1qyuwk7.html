What is the one trade your favorite team should have made, that would have put them over the top?

In the 1998 off-season, the Montreal Expos were shopping reigning Cy Young winner Pedro Martinez. Their first phone call was to the Cleveland Indians.

Montreal’s price? Bartolo Colon and Jaret Wright. Colon was seen as Cleveland’s best young arm, and Wright had just helped the Tribe make a deep playoff run, including making a QS in G7 of the ‘97 World Series. Cleveland said no.

Montreal would trade Pedro to Boston, of course, for two pitchers: Carl Pavano and Tony Armas

Cleveland would roll with Bartolo, who amassed really good, but not quite ace numbers in the following 4 1⁄2 seasons with Cleveland (71-38, 3.92 ERA, 22.9 WAR). Jaret Wright, however, was a bust. Couldn’t stay healthy, made just 22 starts in the 3 seasons 2000-02, never posted an ERA lower than 4.70 after his rookie year, and amassed just 2 WAR in six seasons with Cleveland

Ironically the Indians would trade Bartolo to the Expos in 2002, and for a really great return: Cliff Lee, Grady Sizemore, Brandon Phillips, and Lee Stevens.

Hindsight’s 20⁄20 of course, but it’s not like Cleveland didn’t know how good Pedro was at that time. He had just won the Cy Young for crying out loud. I think today teams gladly give up two of their top pitching prospects for a known ace. I mean look at what Texas gave up for McKenzie Gore, or what the Tigers want for Tarik Skubal.

Hard to think, though, that the Tribe, with that offense, would not have won a World Series if they’d have pulled the trigger on a Pedro trade. Pedro even pitched the Red Sox past the Indians in Game 5 of the 1999 ALDS, 6 no-hit innings in relief, for 3-2 series win after the Sox fell behind 0-2.

Hell, Manny and Pedro ended up being good friends on the Red Sox, maybe Cleveland is able to retain their core guys (Manny and Thome) a little better if the front office had shown a more killer instinct and given the best team never.to win a championship a true ace.
