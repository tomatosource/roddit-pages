Wisps are underwhelming.

I like Wisps as an idea, but the implementation is just lacking at the moment.

  1. Wisps often force players to backtrack. I assume that around 40% of all my Wisps go to areas where I already cleared the majority if not all monster packs, meaning that not only does following them waste my time, but also the reward becomes much worse due to less possessed enemies killed. Ignoring Wisps that force backtracking is just the best course of action.
  2. Rare Wisps feel too rare. During my 100 hours of mapping I haven’t found a single Wisp that upgrades rare monsters to unique bosses. I also found just one Wisp that revives a rare monster ~10 times each time with a new Wisp influence. Are there any other rare Wisps? I have no idea, but most of them seem to work exactly the same and the rare ones should be more common to give us some more variety.
  3. They are too slow. I’m playing a Warrior- the slowest class in the game- and Wisps still tend to be way too slow way too often or stuck on 1 or 2 mobs left in a monster pack.
  4. The rewards just don’t feel good enough. I’ve got a single Rite of Passage in early maps and this made me spec into Wisps heavily, only to not drop anything meaningful from them for the rest of my playthrough. Monsters and map bosses uber-juiced by Wisps that witnessed killing hundreds of possessed monsters do feel much stronger, but rewards just don’t seem to scale well enough to justify it.

That’s it. It’s a decent mechanic that just needs some help in order to be great imo.
