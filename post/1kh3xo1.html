Treasure Jewelry (Item version of Greed Shrine)

As the devs already added Greed Shrine to the game, I would like to propose a new common jewelry similar to the Gold Amulet/Ring, but instead of increasing rarity they would have a gold find mod and found randomly throughout the world. They could even add a unique amulet that is the PoE2 version of Blade Of Ali Baba from D2…

Treasure Amulet

  • Common
  • (75-100)% Increased Quantity of Gold Dropped by Slain Enemies

Treasure Ring

  • Common
  • (25-50)% Increased Quantity of Gold Dropped by Slain Enemies

Amulet of Covetousness

  • Unique
  • (150-300)% Increased Quantity of Gold Dropped by Slain Enemies
  • Other Mods

As Gold is the main currency to perform several actions in PoE2, I think it would be fun to have the option to build a gold find character, especially when starting a new campaign where gold is relatively scarce. Who doesn’t want extra gold in their pockets?
