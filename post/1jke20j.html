Do you prefer MnK or Controller?

Since I’ve spent most of my time on an xbox controller, I find that’s easiest for me to play on, but I like how convenient a mouse is for doing everything else in the game. Since I’m on PC, I will often log out and log back in with MnK to respec my character if I’m changing a lot of nodes, or if I’m going through and cleaning my stash. It’s so much faster. I wish they would make it easier to switch back and forth, but I sort of get why you can only have the 1 input at a time.
