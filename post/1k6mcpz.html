Mechanical Complexity and Early Endgame feedback

After playing PoE1 as a new player recently, the biggest turnoff was mechanics getting introduced during campaign.

A lot of these mechanics felt unrewarding early but as a new player it’s not clear which ones are related to the campaign and which are league mechanics. I worry that PoE2 will head in the same direction leading to a poor new player experience.

Azmeri Spirits is a perfect example of this. It immediately adds noticeable difficulty to the area while not getting a feeling of reward even if you do manage to clear early on. By the end of Act 1, I found myself just skipping them because they were high risk, no reward.

As an aside, mechanics that have single rewards each time (Strongboxes, Spirits, etc) need to have a noticeable reward every time. Rogue Exiles show this perfectly with a loot shower. Getting an increase in item quantity or rarity is a lot less obvious per instance, while difficulty is obvious. Strongboxes should adjust their suffixes to things like “Drops [1-4] Rares” “Drops [1-2] uniques” “Drops [1-5] Exalts or higher”. Make it worth rolling on these boxes with an immediate effect. It’s obvious this mechanic is just ported directly from PoE1 without a second thought because “Items Drop Identified” and “Items have extra sockets” are both at least useful in PoE1 while basically useless here.

I’d like to see all mechanics that aren’t part of the actual story, or story driven side quests, be kept until end game. They can be introduced appropriately. E.g have someone tell you to watch out for Rogue Exiles that may be roaming around the atlas, or hearing tales of extremely powerful chests that have extra effects.

In addition to this, the quests that talk about Breach, Ritual etc in end game are a solid start, but should be expanded upon. Once you hit high enough tier maps, have the Ritual quest pop up to talk to someone that then tells you to watch out for invitations. Basically something that hints that these mechanics get more rewarding at higher tiers. It also reminds players that may have skipped them earlier to try them again because they can see a hint of better rewards.

If the tower system is being left in, I’d like to see a better method of getting tablets. Currently getting tablets of the type you want to use requires running those mechanics and hoping for a tablet to drop. How about instead having only one type of tablet drop (plus uniques), and we choose what to upgrade that tablet with. Have one of the NPCs give you the option to add +1 lvl, breach, delirium, etc to your tablet. That will let players target their ideal mechanics more easily while still maintaining the tower system.

The introduction to towers should also be a little better, let Doryani give you the choice between a 1/3/6 mod map to run your first tower with. Should let new players see the increase in difficulty (They’ll see less revives on more difficult maps) while also letting veteran players start blasting straight away. Imagine getting to hit your first tower, instantly run it with 6 mods and then slam 3 tablets of your choice into it without having to go through the effort of trading.

As for the Atlas itself, GGG should lean into the idea with a compass system. As players increase in tiers, or path towards certain mechanics on the Atlas Tree, give us the ability to know what direction to look for various mechanics.

“A Rogue Exile was spotted West of your last map location”

“A Citadel was rumored to be North of your last map location”

A major turn off has been getting to a point where there’s nothing you can spot on the map. No unique maps, no Citadels, no Corruption poking out of the fog. A compass system will give players a direction to travel, and should allow them to target mechanics of their choice. It would also let GGG add zones of interest down the line. Imagine having an area of the map which has nothing but unique maps across the whole screen. Or Rogue Exiles have set up an encampment somewhere and you get to fight 20 of them in one map. There’s a ton of possibilities that a compass system would add while still maintaining a balance between players feeling like they made progress and GGG keeping things scarce.

The last point I want to touch on is Ascendancies. While I don’t have solutions for Chaos, Sekhemas could at least give players the option to run as an ascendancy trial, or a loot trial. Loot trials can remain in the current format, while Ascendancy ones should be rebalanced to not require Relics at all and only give the ascendancy. Also have them be infinitely repeatable if you fail. Have them be say 3rd floor only for 3rd ascendancy, or 4th only for 4th. Maybe this won’t matter once we have the 3rd trial type added to the story, hopefully that happens sooner rather than later.
