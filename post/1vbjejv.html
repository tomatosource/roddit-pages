Making Sense of Item Price

A case in point, a Perfect Iron Rune goes for 700X. around 1.6D. All it does is bumping %defence another 2% over the greater version. In concrete terms, that’s maybe +13 sheet ES on a 160ES chest piece with flat ES roll. The odds of that 13ES making a difference in surviving are minuscule. Meanwhile a masterwork rune runs around 400X and it does the same thing.

Sure there is a parallel between this and people buying designer bags in real life for thousands of dollars simply because they are rich. But then there is the conspicuous consumption and flaunting wealth in real life. The aspect of which is absent in POE2.

Am I missing something when it comes to min-maxing? I can understand if there is an argument about reaching certain breakpoints for some stats. But most of POE2 is pretty linear in terms of scaling.
