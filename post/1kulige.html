In the conference finals, teams that lost their first two home games have a 0-11 record, losing all 11 series. In 4 out of those 11 instances, the team that won the series went on to win the NBA Finals. In the last 6 instances, the team that won the first two road games in CF, lost in the NBA finals

Source. I used CTRL+F to go through the Conference Finals, going through all 60.
I also used Bbref to confirm the data.
11 times in the conference finals a team lost its first two home games - 0-11 record, losing all 11.
That’s out of 60 times a team went 2-0 in a series in the conference finals (whether first 2 wins were at home or not)

1977 WCF: Trail Blazers went 2-0 vs. the Lakers, winning the series 4-0.
Trail Blazers went to win the NBA championship (4-2 vs. 76ers).

1995 WCF: Rockets went 2-0 vs. the Spurs, winning the series 4-2.
Rockets went to win the NBA championship (4-0 vs. Magic)

2001 WCF: Lakers went 2-0 vs. the Spurs, winning the series 4-0.
Lakers went to win the championship (4-1 vs. 76ers)

2003 ECF: Nets went 2-0 vs. the Pistons, winning the series 4-0.
Nets lost the NBA Finals to the Spurs (4-2)

2005 WCF: Spurs went 2-0 vs. the Suns, winning the series 4-1.
The Spurs went on to win the NBA championship (4-3 vs. the Pistons)

2010 ECF: The Celtics went 2-0 vs. the Magic, winning the series 4-2.
The Celtics lost the NBA Finals to the Lakers (4-3).

2015 ECF: The Cavaliers went 2-0 vs. the Hawks, winning the series 4-0.
Cavaliers lost the NBA Finals to the Warriors (4-2)

2017 ECF: The Cavaliers went 2-0 vs. the Celtics, winning the series 4-1.
Cavaliers lost the NBA Finals to the Warriors (4-1)

2020 ECF: The Heat went 2-0 vs. the Celtics, winning the series 4-2.
Fun fact: the Miami Heat won their first game in Boston by 3 points margin, in overtime, and winning the 2nd game by 5 points margin, in regulation. The Pacers did the same to the Knicks in 2025 ECF
The Heat lost the NBA Finals to the Lakers (4-2)

2023 ECF: The Heat went 2-0 vs. the Celtics, winning the series 4-3.
The Heat lost the NBA Finals to the Nuggets (4-1)

2024 WCF: The Mavericks went 2-0 vs. the Timberwolves, winning the series 4-1.
The Mavericks lost the NBA Finals to the Celtics (4-1).
