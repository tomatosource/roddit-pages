6th place in MVP: The Gary Payton Award (A History)

In honour of NBA fans deciding that finishing 6th in MVP is one of the greatest accomplishments you can achieve in an NBA season, I dedicated my time to listing all 6th place finishes to see who is the best 6th man of all time.

1980-81 was the first season that the MVP was decided by the media so I started from there.
