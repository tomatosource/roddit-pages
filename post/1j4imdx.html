400 hours in and my Cold Sorc is finished - How much did it fell into the Meta?

TLDR: I made a Spirit-Stacking Cold Damage Eye of Winter Sorc. Skip for PoB + Video.

My friends and I started and decided to play blind for the most part - no looking for builds, no researching the meta, and so on. I’d start with a Stormweaver and focus on Boss/Single target damage, our two supports were set in stone when we saw the item and ascendancy previews: a Witchhunter culler and a Chronomancer with Merit of Badge. Finally a Ranger for clearing.

I decided to go with Force of Will + Shaper of Storms Cold Sorceress, for that sweet double shock. For my main skill I chose Eye of Winter to add Critical Weakness to the Party, and initially Cast on Freeze but changed that after the nerf to Cast on Critical. My first unique was The Everlasting Gaze out of necessity, I really wanted Mana Regen. After having it I tested if it worked with Eldritch Battery and to my surprise, it did.

My wife’s build was something we planned before the game’s release - Merit of Service was one of the first uniques showcased, and once we saw what Chronomancer could do, it was an easy combo. We went for the 78% Block Aura + Enfeeble/Temporal Chains/Conductivity + Chronomancer AoE slow. Because supports tend to cost a lot in PoE 1, we usually gear them up first, so our first Divs went for the support characters.

Oh and one thing: I always yolo vaal

  1. We saw Alpha’s Howl existed, tested it, and it was pretty much a must-have for her build due to how big it increased the presence range.
  2. She saw Threaded Light and wondered if it would be interesting since I had high spirit. There was no PoB but we tried it anyway and yeah, the combo of Threaded Light and wand outperformed a Staff.
  3. The next step was to buy an Alpha’s Howl, and change the chest to +Spirit. I started with a 12% spell damage Threaded Light and had about 300% increased damage from it. Around this time, PoB2 was released and I saw that the wand’s spell damage was outclassed by other sources of spell damage.
  4. Around this time I ended up with my BiS Wand. I yolo vaaled it in hopes of +Cold level, but got Crit Chance. Very rarely something better shows up in PoB and it’s 1-2 mirror when it does.
  5. Realized I could have Sigil of Power on the offhand, but the weapon swapping was clumsy due to low cast speed, so had to work on that.
  6. At this point I decided to include Elemental Conflux. I bought an Against the Darkness and decided to use it on the Whispers of Doom cluster. I bought a cheap one at the time and rerolled to 12. Was surprised to see that even with Mana Flare, the Fire conflux doesn’t do anything. In fact, only the Lightning one is significant.
  7. I learned about Soul Offering. The only remaining source of spirit would be from a corruption, so I went to poedb and looked for which items could be corrupted with Spirit. I ended up buying an Alpha’s Howl with +24 Spirit for surprisingly cheap (I guess no one uses those?)
  8. Looked for days for a Megalomaniac. Just as I was about to buy one with Barbaric Strength which had a floor of 100 Divs, someone posted one with Barbaric Strength and Throatseeker for 50 Div, both rated as my highest DPS annoints. Giving 22% more damage, that was my best purchase by far.
  9. Saved money for Dream Fragments and a Breech Ring with Mana Regen. Reached 3000 mana regen, ditched Frost Bomb in favor of Thin Ice because it was doing more harm than good breaking the Frost Walls.

Final build:

https://pobb.in/85KtcIjMIjbF

Eye of Winter, Frost Wall, CoC - Comet. 363 Spirit. ~900% increased spell damage.

Buffs: Archmage - Elemental Conflux - CoC - Grim Feast.

Voltaic Mark for up to 78% Shock. Soul Offering with Danse Macabre and two/three Skeletal Warriors (I tried Clerics first but Warriors just seem tankier and Clerics don’t heal each other enough).

Showcase, all bosses are T4 and the last Zarokh has The Desperate Alliance:

https://reddit.com/link/1j4imdx/video/435zlpa7pyme1/player

I did not stick with Mana Tempest because bosses usually die before I cast it once I’m finishing setting things up, sometimes they die just for the Frost Wall. My Mana Regen milestones that I can remember were 300 &gt; 700 &gt; 1100 &gt; 1700 &gt; 2100 &gt; 3200. I got super happy once I crossed 900 because I saw someone in January with that much and it seemed impossible at the time.

Now that my build is pretty much done and any further upgrade would be beyond expensive - I’d like some C&C, and also, how much did it converge into the Meta? Our goal was to play the game theorycrafting on our own. I’m curious about how many of my choices were the same everyone made or not, other than the fact I’ve already learned most people use Spark as the main skill.
