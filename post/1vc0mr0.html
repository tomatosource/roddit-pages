Who do you see as the comparables to Payton Pritchard?

This is a tough one, because Pritchard spent his first 4 years clawing for a role on a great team (years 2-4 were the Finals, ECF, Championship contending window). He saw his FGA increase to 10.8 in 2024-25 and averaged 14.3ppg. Last year that increased to 13.8FGA and 17ppg.

I think some people have a perception of him as something of a TJ McConnell/Patty Mills/Donte DiVincenzo who maybe already has an inflated role relative to his talents.

They point out he’s 6’1 195. But he clearly has elite scoring talent and has proven in small sample sizes he can still score with heightened role. In 10 games without either Tatum or Brown last season he saw his FGA increase to 20 and he averaged 25.2ppg on 48.5%/44% shooting. Team went 8-2.

So on the extreme end you have people questioning if he could be like (5’9 185lb) Isaiah Thomas with an increased role. In his 3 year stretch with Boston he saw his FGA increase from 13.6 (19ppg) to 16.9 (22.2ppg) to 19.4 (28.9ppg)

Obviously people have brought up 6’2 190 Jalen Brunson who went from averaging 12.8 fga in Luka’s offense (16.3 ppg) to becoming the driving force of the Knicks offense (17.6-21.4fga = 24-28.7ppg)

I wonder if it’s more of a middle ground? How about 6’0 197lb Fred VanVleet who had a three year stretch where his FGA increased to 16.1-16.9 and averaged 19.3-20.3ppg.

Maybe the most direct comp is actually 6’1, 190lb Terry Rozier. We saw a glimpse of him having a green light in 2017-18 playoffs where he averaged 14 fga and 16.5 ppg (extremely similar to Pritchard’s stats this season), but the next year saw himself struggle to have a role seeing his FGA drop to 8.4 and his points drop to 9ppg. He immediately saw his role increase in Charlotte (14.9fga/18ppg) followed by a half decade of him averaging 16-19fga and 19-23ppg.

I’ve been wondering for a while what Pritchard would look like if giving the Rozier-esque green light on a shitty team. How do you compare those two as players? With Brown gone it does seem like Pritchard could see his FGA increase from 13.8 into the 15-17 range, right?
