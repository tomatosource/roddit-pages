New record - Mavs 27 FT in a quarter

In the last quarter of their comeback win against the Grizzlies tonight, the Mavs shot an NBA record 27 FT in the 4th quarter.
Only four of them were from intentional fouls.

It was also the greatest FT differential in a single quarter in the history of the game being 24 (27-3).
