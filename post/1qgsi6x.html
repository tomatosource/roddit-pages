Sekhemas finally pays off!!!!

Hey Exiles,

I’m just a casual player here – logging in about 4 hours a day after work, nothing crazy. This past weekend, I finally had two full days off, so I went all-in grinding Trials of Sekhemas (the one content I actually vibe with, since it’s quick and rewarding without eating my whole schedule) and threw in some boss carry services on the side to scrape together extra divs for my build. But last run? Total disaster. I took a fews hit and 3 out of 5 carried folks dropped dead . Wiped the trial anyway, lost 30 divines on the spot, and I was tilted AF.

Then, as I’m waiting those eternal 2 seconds for loot in salty silence, BAM – out pops Incense Relic. That satisfying “tink” from my filter sound hit like music to my ears, and I looted it faster than a league-start Divine Orb.

Yelled in chat to the two survivors: “Run’s free, my bad guys GL and have fun” Checked prices quick – cheapest was 12,500 Divine. Listed mine at 11,999, and it sold in under 3 hours. I have like 9 stash full of Divines orb. Trading 1 for a Mirror and grab a dream weapon (fubgun’s one).

Before this, my build was not strong enough. I dont like mapping cus 1 portal mechanics, getting one shot and all progress gone in a blink. Hated it. Temple of Atziri? Pass – no time to build out my temple properly with my limited playtime; it’s too much commitment for a casual like me. Trials of Sekhemas is my jam though – efficient farm, no fluff, and apparently, lottery-level drops if RNG smiles.

Shoutout to GGG’s chaos – you got me hooked again. Fellow carry mains: What’s your best tip for smooth Trials of Sekhemas carries? Wildest fail-to-win story? Share below.
