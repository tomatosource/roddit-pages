My newbie experience To PoEs (Gemling 75lvl)

I adore this game and if you want the negative feedback, ask about it in the comments or correct me. I was looking for an engaging long-term grind-fest online game after one of my all-time favorites - Trove got really unhealthy to play.

The problem was not only devs pushing p2w monetisation strategies, killing the game further, but also game mechanics like insane difficulty and rarity curve, stamina pauses restoring when you jump, and numerous bugs (The most annoying being screen flick). Of course in the end I decided to quit, still enjoying Trove’s OST and experiences it gave me. After all, a dead game makes me feel dead.

I’m a casual player and I really enjoy looters like warframe, but there’s just not alot of luck involved. I like making unique builds and investing into characters I like. I want to showcase my luck. That’s why I also play Genshin alot, trying to compete for the #1 for my favorite characters no one ever uses. There isn’t much creativity when it comes to singular characters outside of team comps, though I really enjoy min-maxing and artifact farming.

I want it to be like that one Adventure Time episode with infinite dungeon train. For some reason Path of Exile (1) trailers/gameplay showcases didn’t resonate with me unlike actual streams and gaming videos and I’m thankful they did not, knowing now that the game is dying because of the primary focus on sequel nowadays. Maybe I too, would negative review bomb the PoE2 page because of that.

I remember trying out different a-rpg’s and they all were too simple and creativity-sucking, but it ended shortly because of the miserable experiences. But Minecraft Dungeons is lowkey insane?? It can be challeging, you can make cool builds with different playstyles and the game is extremely dynamic and fun.

But it’s expensive. And it’s offline.

Yeah, these two downsides were enough to generate a void inside me. So, I started looking for more online a-rpgs, but with my love to scrolling negative steam reviews I realized that they are all dead.

Some years passed, I stopped looking for those, after all I have quite alot of unfinished games.

But then steam sends me sales news and I click on PoE2 and I watch trailers (omg), I can use different weapons of different classes (OMG?), I can grindfest AND the negative reviews made it for me. You mean I get a developing game with alot of luck involved and my PC freaking EXPLODES?
Sign me in, Sign me up??

I choose the class without listening to outside opinions, despite every review saying otherwise. I grabbed Mercenary. I like ice and ice powers. There was a single ice skill showcase and Ranger one didn’t have it. I would’ve picked Ranger..

I’m really glad I picked Mercenary after all. I had zero idea about ascendancies and Gemling?? Gemling is perfectly my playstyle.

The passive tree seemed really easy, and when I refunded skills for the first time I understood that I really have to stack damage while progging. Crossbow offered me 3 obvious playstyles:

  • Ice Wall Explosions
  • Grenade Explosion Arrow
  • Thunder Manifestation God forbid I play Lightning and Ice wall targeting enemies instead of the GROUND I AIM AT is kinda annoying &lt;3. I can change the build when I want, I knew that. But because I like more skills than less (Flame arrow, Poison grenade, Fire grenade, Oil grenade &gt; Ice wall, Shrapnel shot) I took the fire path. Okay, but poison grenade is insanely powerful and mandatory because of armor break support skills, double poison and synergy with mercenary passive branch and it having insane stagger damage when exploding like.. Anyways, I got used to grenades and burst, but why did I change it? Act 2 final boss. It simply has too much hp I get tired of dodging and waste all my flasks. The first time I used charms. I don’t remember what skills I had and I was leaning towards 2 support gems Gemling passive. Frustrated, I killed mahomajamastra or whatever, I couldn’t care less for the story and I’m not sorry, It’s kinda stupid :sob:

Act 3 was a lightwork, but kinda too long imo. The town is mildly confusing. Passed it, got my support gem passive, it was even better than I imagined.

And got teleported to act 1 after a random npc befriended a maniac on rocket shoes from the past. I just skipped all the story and got confused, but also kinda happy because I can destroy act1 bosses with new skills.

But I also had to trade for new gear, because the money for GAMBLE and drops are not enough compared to endgame. The trading seemed easy because it’s the same as warframe and I was okay with it.

Until I sent 67 requests and no one responded. Warframe has a community-made utility and site to trade, so they can set the status from online to offline. In PoE2 it feels like I’m begging people to throw away their maps and trade with me. The problem is both players, not bothering to set stashes to non-public and the stupid system that requires trading and makes it miserable on purpose (I’ll stand on that trading is a necessary part of the game).

I had to revamp my build because of the optional boss. Realized I was dying to any move because I was full evasion and AoE attacks ignore it until you get a skill that halves evasion?.. Anyways Backjaw couldn’t do anything to me with armor and it was a great idea to change the defensive stat.

In the endgame I got around setting skills to different weapons, but there’s a bug that crashes the game when you right click on crossbow base shot.? But no matter how I tried I couldn’t beat ascension 3. The reason was actually spinny fireball traps exploding with quite undredictable AoE. Endgame is actually alot more fun than story because I enjoy looting rare enemies the most. I’m still not getting loot compared to what I can buy, and I got around trading pretty well. I traded for jewels and trial relics to finally beat it and finally.. I have Gemling’s adaptive capability. I can use spells, curses, cool spirit abilities, WHATEVER. Quicky got myself Mage’s fire resistance shred rune and blink. And dreamed of blink. It is even better than I imagined. Why do int users get such cool sick stuff.? But I have it too now. I’ve achieved Jack of all trades and now couldn’t be happier. Gonna try out more skills today to think about possible builds in the future, but I wanna invest in this game and gonna play it for a really long time.
