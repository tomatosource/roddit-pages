Elephant in the room… would we have any interest in AD upon a potential Dallas firesale at the deadline?

Let me preface this by acknowledging the reasons why we would be against this

-2019 was annoying, he and his people made it public that Boston wasn’t an option, wasn’t cool

-he’s 32, which in its own isn’t the end of the world because Derrick is also like 31-32 but AD comes with more uncertainty

-this trade is essentially impossible without including Derrick white in it. I love Derrick white, you love Derrick white, we all love Derrick white

-injury concerns, nothing crazy significant like embiid but just very consistently injured in some minor way.

Now let’s move on to the reasons it might be worth considering:

-AD, when he plays, is still a beast. Still an elite post scorer/rebounder/defender/top 15-20 player.

-32 is on the older side but not old old, we could still expect him to produce at an elite level for another 3-5 years as long as he maintains relative health if we look at other similarly aged stars career trajectories as comparison

-we desperately need big man help. Guys that’s out championship lynchpin right there, gotta get a high end big on the roster, and the market isn’t exactly overflowing with them.

-our starting 5 would be fucking disgusting when healthy. Pritchard-brown-Hauser-Tatum-AD is the best 5 in the league, you’re surrounding 3 top 15 ish players(one top 5 player) with two 40% 3pt shooters who are both good defenders. We would be a monster to beat in the playoffs

The package would basically have to be around white, only way to do it really

Imo it would be Simon’s-white for AD and that’s it, im not interested in adding picks and I’ve sent some feelers to mavs fans and they seem relatively happy with basically just white in return.

I’ll finish by saying im in no way all in on this idea or even necessarily convinced it’s the right move, I just think it’s worth discussing. I’d like to hear from you guys because im 50⁄50 on it
