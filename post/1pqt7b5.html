[SBJ] NBA opens the door wider to private equity. The NBA is now allowing investment firms to own equity in up to eight teams, increasing the previous cap of five

The NBA is allowing investment firms to purchase equity in as many as eight franchises, sources told SBJ, elevating the previous league limit of five and enabling the private equity company Arctos Partners to increase its team investments to six.

The league’s amendment became effective Dec. 3, and, on Wednesday, Monumental Sports & Entertainment announced Arctos’ investment in the Wizards, the fifth known NBA team to strike a deal with the firm.

Sources said the Grizzlies also are part of the Arctos portfolio. Robert Pera has been the control owner since 2012, when he bought the team for $377 million. Arctos’ involvement in the Grizzlies has not been previously reported. The Grizzlies did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

Arctos, which declined comment, also owns shares of the 76ers, Jazz, Kings and Warriors.

Its stakes in both MSE and the Grizzlies have not been disclosed.

NBA rules limit a single fund to acquiring up to a maximum 20% stake in any one team, and teams can sell up to 30% of their equity to institutional investors in the aggregate.

Arctos is the only private equity firm to own stakes in teams in all five major professional North American men’s leagues and also has brands across Formula One, NASCAR and European soccer, including Liverpool and Paris-St. Germain. The Financial Times has reported that KKR is in talks to acquire Arctos.
