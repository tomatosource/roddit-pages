Trae Young is having the weirdest season in the NBA

However, he is leading the league in assists at 12.2 and if he keeps this up, he will be the first player to average 12 assists a game since John Stockton in 1995. His last 8 games the divide has gotten even larger as he’s averaging 16.1 ppg on 38.5⁄24.5 splits but a crazy 14 assists per game. And he’s consistently having these low scoring on bad efficiency but high assist games too
