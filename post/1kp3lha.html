Manifest weapon

Could we see some adjustments to this basically useless ascendancy node ?

It doesn’t scale with gem level, aside from gaining sockets, this also means things like +minion levels do not scale it at all It doesn’t work with half the recommended gems, because again it doesn’t have a minion tag. It’s ai is wonky and if a monster moves it whiffs constantly.

The whole thing is a bloody joke. It’s one thing to release it like this, but we are over a month in, could we see this currently useless thing get some love.
