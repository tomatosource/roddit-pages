GGG took delirium away, so I made a build that makes it so I still cant see anything. Indigon Firestorm. (No CoC)

https://reddit.com/link/1pzc6u6/video/5skzias8oaag1/player

Ninja: https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/Kerrigan-7588/character/Storm_Of_The_Hawk

There are probably much better options to run Indigon with, but I wanted to play firestorm this league, and Indigon also caught my eye because of Quipolatl’s Thesis. Kills Xesht in 3-5 seconds depending on firestorm RNG. No issues with overrun maps, but I do play them slow since mana siphon exists.

This build is surprisingly tanky. I have only died twice since switching to it around lvl 85. I switch to dream fragments on maps I think I will be frozen, but I can run all mods.

Was pretty annoying to get the mana balance just right. It’s easy to let the mana costs scale out of control. Quipolatl’s Thesis is the main driver here. It Allows me to scale my mana regen to 20k+, even with 2 keystones that reduce regen by 50%. 2nd weapon set is just to get higher shocks with Orb of Storms, which also help make sure I have all infusions.

I think the tree can be much better, but I liked the idea of firestorm being as big as possible, even if that means fewer hits.
