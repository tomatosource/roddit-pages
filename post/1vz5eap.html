I played the duelist today at gamescom

Hi everyone! Just wanted to share a few quick impressions: the Duelist is super fun. I enjoyed playing him a lot more than the other melee classes, and the parry mechanic was probably my favorite part, especially during boss fights.

Gameplay recording was allowed at the event, but videos and screenshots are still under embargo until tomorrow. I also spotted Zizaran at the media booth, so I’m pretty sure you’ll get a much more extensive look at the Duelist and all of his skills very soon.
