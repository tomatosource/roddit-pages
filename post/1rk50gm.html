Sick and tired of re-equipping HH in temple? Here’s a workaround:

all temple fans know the situation where a golemworks or a smithy monster doesn’t activate because you have some buff from the Headhunter belt that makes you invisible to them.

the solution is simple: summon something that’s not invisible for the monsters to activate.

I have a second weapon set for crystal farm which I don’t use for fighting so I equipped the Wolfpack spirit gem for the second set. if a monster doesn’t activate, I swap sets and summon the wolves for a second before swapping back and the wolves activate the “sleeping” monster.

you can use any minion for this to work. if you don’t have the spirit to spare, but have an empty skillslot, you can use one of the witch skills where you summon zombies from dead monsters.

I haven’t tested it but skills which summon walls that the enemies can attack like bone walls, might also do the trick.

tldr: summon minions or something the inactive monster can attack!

good luck & happy temple farming everyone!!!
