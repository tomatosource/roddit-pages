Celtics On-Ball Defense so far: DFG% vs FG%

Inspired by a r/NBA post about Kawhi Leonard, I went ahead and checked out how the Celtics are doing this season in-terms of on-ball defense. Essentially, the lower the DIFF%, the better the player’s defensive impact is directly on the shooter.

https://www.nba.com/stats/players/defense-dash-overall?PerMode=Totals&TeamID=1610612738&dir=A&sort=PCT_PLUSMINUS

Filtered by at least 10 games played, Jordan Walsh leads the team at -6.8% followed by Neemy at -6.7%.

Minott, Scheierman, JB (this is impressive given the load he is carrying on offense) and X are in tier 2.

Only Luka Garza and PP are just average. Even Anfernee Simons is passable on defense just in terms of on-ball defense.

At the very least, we have a team full of good defenders, and so far it has translated into the #3 defense in the NBA in terms of opponents’ PPG, only behind OKC and Houston.
