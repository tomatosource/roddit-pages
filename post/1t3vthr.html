I think wisps are a popular contender for a mechanic that needs some work. Personally I am happy with how they have seemingly adjusted drop rates and how often they spawn but wisp pathing is still a huge sticking point. What changes would you like to see, if any?

This would allow you to stack multiple wisps on your character at once, juice desired rares or bosses at will, and just look kinda cool. Thoughts?
