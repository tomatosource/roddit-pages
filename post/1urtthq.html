If LeBron James decides to join the Miami Heat, he could join Nicolas Batum and Grant Hill as the only players to play 5+ seasons with 3 different franchises.

Nicolas Batum:

  • 7 seasons with the Blazers

  • 5 seasons with the Hornets

  • 5.5 seasons with the Clippers

Grant Hill:

  • 6 seasons with the Pistons

  • 7 seasons with the Magic (missed 1 full season)

  • 5 seasons with the Suns

LeBron James:

  • 11 seasons with the Cavaliers

  • 4 seasons with the Heat

  • 8 seasons with the Lakers

-

Jason Kidd and Dikembe Mutombo just barely miss the cutoff, as Kidd was traded midway through the season to the Suns in 1997, and Mutombo to the Sixers in 2001.

Jason Kidd:

  • 6.5 seasons with the Nets

  • 4.5 seasons with the Suns

  • 7 seasons with the Mavericks

Dikembe Mutombo:

  • 5 seasons with the Nuggets

  • 4.5 seasons with the Hawks

  • 5 seasons with the Rockets
