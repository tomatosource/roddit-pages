Can OKC get under the 2nd apron in 2027 to prolong their window?

The longer they can avoid the 2nd apron the longer they can prolong this Celtics situation where they need to break up the team

Since Chet/JDub are still on their rookie deals next season they will be way below the 2nd apron, with a team payroll of $177m with all their current guys under contract. So i’m not worried about that. I think OKC wants to delay entering the 2nd apron as long as possible so they can keep their core guys together. How far can that go? In 2027, i’m assuming Chet and JDub will both get max extensions, which are 25% of the cap, but raise to 30% if either makes All NBA next season.

To project their 2027 cap i’m going to make a few assumptions

  1. JDub makes All NBA next season but Chet doesn’t

  2. They have 5 total FRP in 2025 and 2026, i’m going to assume they only draft two first rounders and trade the others for future picks

  3. Jaylin Williams, Ajay Mitchell and Ousmane Dieng don’t get new deals

The FRP salary is impossible to project without knowing the draft slot so lets just assume $5m which is like mid first round territory

The 2nd Apron threshold for 2027 is projected to be be $228,608,000. So they would be $23 million over with 14 guys on the roster.

Now Hartenstein and Dort are both on team options which makes it very easy to duck the 2nd apron if they wanted to.

They could do any combination of declining one of their options and letting them walk, declining their options and signing them to new long term deals that pay less in year 1 (unlikely), or picking options up and trading them. I think the most realistic path would be declining Hartenstein + trading Joe for no salary back which would get them roughly $16m under with 12 guys on the roster. This would add room to have drafted a guy in a previous year or just fill out the roster with vet mins and still be the 2nd apron and prolong their window
