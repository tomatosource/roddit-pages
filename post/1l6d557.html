The Last Lament - theorycrafting

Now that the Last Lament is available, it’s time to ask the question: How to create a fun to play build around it?

It seems any Crossbow skill that only loads 1 bolt could be a good candidate to fully enjoy the fact we don’t have to worry about reloading, but we also have to somehow get a proper life regeneration (likely through leech) to be able to keep on shooting.

But how to get the sufficient amount of leech to keep on shooting?

Life From Death seems decent with its 3% of max HP restored per kill.

Mercenary & Goring Node may be a good option - this node may be too far away for other classes to consider.

Amazon & Mystic Harvest seem to be a good candidate here thanks to leeching from both physical and elemental damage. Explosive Shot and Glacial Bolt would deal huge AoE damage, so combined with a couple items with leech she should

Smith of Kitava & Leather Bindings may seem strange, but he has it pretty close to life leech nodes and will regenerate 100+HP per second.

What are your thoughts?
