New Offline/Online Trade Idea

Okay so before I begin I will state I have only read a select few of the offline trading posts before writing this but I do believe this to be a different idea that GGG may actually consider implementing. I’ve also read some of their posts dating back to 2017 about why fully offline trade is not available and honestly I fully agree with them.

The fact of bots, trade scrapers and other aspects just being automated to check markets and instantly buy an item and having the currency transactions back and forth be done in the background is something I would hate to see. It would ruin the market and honestly as nice as it would be to have divines just be there when I log in, it would also ruin it for everyone trying to actually find good gear that didn’t use automated bots.

So the idea is this, make an offline trade that requires online input. I honestly love the fact you need to verify the currency or item before it allows you to confirm the transaction, it’s brilliant so keep it. But say I wanted to make a transaction while not being in the game then it would be treated the same way just through the trade site (an app for your phone would be the most optimistic) and you do the exact same steps.

  • You login to the trade site with either your phone or your computer (already available) and you search for listings

  • After finding the item you want you (as you already do) click direct message. The end user would get a notification and see someone’s interested. This is where an app on your phone would be ideal because you could technically be offline, recieve a notification via the app and then login wherever you are and be directed to the trade site

  • The end user agrees to the sale and both of you are directed to another page that looks the same as the game, your currency is available on the right hand side (or under or wherever) without any clutter from your stash and you add your divine. The end user is more simplistic where it shows the item and they need to select it (or drag it over to the trade box) to confirm they want to sell the item.

  • Both users then click the opposite trades items and currency to validate it’s a good transaction (negating scams, low balling or accidental clicks and presses.)

  • Only after both parties have completed the steps above then a button or ability to click confirm becomes available and the items are directed to your stash, or a virtual stash which could be limited to a number of items that would be next to your stash tabs next time you login.

Honestly I think this is along the lines of what GGG wants for their trade and I’m completely for the ability to keep things fair and away from using tech to do so. You could add in things such as a captcha after both have validated the items and before a confirm button was shown to even further negate bots in this. It wouldn’t take much, hell a second web page really. The ability to show the exact item with a highlight and its location in your stash tab is already implemented. The ability to login to the trade website externally is the only default and it shows online vs afk vs offline, so really it’s adding notifications and a segregated place to fill transactions is the only difference. Well and maybe an app that which would really kick things off with fully offline notifications and access to the trade site for simple logins and trades. Hell even enable biometrics for further security!

Let me know your thoughts!
