The Meta-Level Problem with the Atlas

Another issue apart from the much lamented fact how tedious it is to play and that you have to basically play for a few hours before you can finally “play the game” is also how painfully inefficient it is, or better, how the Atlas forces you to forgo rewards in order to set it up.

Every time we path to a new tower we are traveling through maps that then become completed and can’t be influenced by said unlocked tower. Add to that that we need at least two of them, we are basically gimping ourselves before we can even apply the juice. This is from the perspective of the average PoE enjoyer akin to deleting some scarabs and a handful maps before we even open one map, it’s just giving me physical pain.

Also the gap between “alch and go” and Fubgun is SO BIG that it’s just not justifiable to run a map raw and alched for the sake of procrea- progressing the path. If we are to have a tedious system (with pseudo hardcore elements) then the reward curve has to go from good to great and not from nothing to everything.

I am really, really struggling to find anything good about this Atlas, I am sorry. From the fact that I have a mini loading screen every time I open it, to the non-0% chance for a bricked opening where it loads in corrupted and unusable forcing me to close and open the window again, from the manual navigation to a node instead of just putting in a map in an clicking open it is in every facet I can think of bad. Or let’s at least say worse than we know it it could be. The unlimited sense of progression is also null because there is nothing interesting happening the further you go, it’s just repeating the same things over and over.

To end on a good note: The new map layouts are very much improved compared to 0.1 and the Sprint makes the general mapping much better so that’s a W.

But I have to say the whole thing is not in for an improvement, it’s due for a re-imagining.
