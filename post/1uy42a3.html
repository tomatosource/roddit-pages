Basic Beginner Cheat Sheet for Understanding Crafting Omens

I notice in every crafting related thread there’s always a lot of people showing frustration at the complexity and not understanding a lot of the terminology being used. It’s a jumble of sinistral this and desecrated that and can be pretty confusing. I’m not listing everything here as there’s a lot of omens that are rarely used and some only in very niche circumstances. The ones below you’ll see mentioned in crafting conversations a lot and for those struggling with this stuff you’ll see that they are actually very simple.

First things first. If you’re on PC hover over an item with your mouse and hit alt and it will display all Prefixes and Suffixes on the item. If you have a desecrated mod the Tier text on the right will be the Green.

https://preview.redd.it/jpaom76ajldh1.png?width=775&format=png&auto=webp&s=ee220dd2011b7cccc9ca212058f911ea8ded1316

By default items can have 3 Prefixes and 3 Suffixes. You don’t need to know much more about it other than that all possible base mods are either a prefix or a suffix. You can check what these are on poe2db for any item. These are the possible base mods for an INT Helmet

https://preview.redd.it/z9oar3tnjldh1.png?width=1498&format=png&auto=webp&s=7a9ac171ffb39762e5b5a5a068b36a70c03c4843

Generally speaking an item is made up of 3 of each. There are ways of accessing other pools of mods but it’s beyond the scope of this right now.

Covering the handfull of Omens that are regularly discussed is pretty simple. They are just a way of targeting the usage of basic currency. For the most part we’re really just talking about Exalted Orbs, Chaos Orbs and Annulment Orbs

One thing to keep in mind and credit to u/melankoholisti who mentioned this in another conversation earlier most of the Omens are telling us what they do by their name

Sinistral is an adjective meaning on the left side
Dextral is an adjective meaning on the right side

Prefixes first on the left, Suffixes on the right. See above mod screenshot

So now we know that Sinistral omens are related to Prefixes and Dextral to Suffixes

Now it’s just a matter of matching them up against their currency

Exalted Orb Augments a Rare item with a new random modifier.
Target Prefix Omen of Sinistral Exaltation Your next Exalted Orb will add only prefix modifiers.
Target Suffix Omen of Dextral Exaltation Your next Exalted Orb will add only suffix modifiers.
Chaos Orb Removes a random modifier and augments a Rare item with a new random modifier
Target Prefix Omen of Sinistral Erasure While this item is active in your inventory your next Chaos Orb will remove only prefix modifiers
Target Suffix Omen of Dextral Erasure While this item is active in your inventory your next Chaos Orb will remove only prefix modifiers
Target lowest level mod Omen of Whittling While this item is active in your inventory your next Chaos Orb will remove the lowest level modifier
Orb of Annulment Removes a random modifier from an item
Target Prefix Omen of Sinistral Annulment While this item is active in your inventory your next Orb of Annulment will remove only prefix modifiers
Target Suffix Omen of Dextral Annulment While this item is active in your inventory your next Orb of Annulment will remove only suffix modifiers
Target Desecrated Mod Omen of Light While this item is active in your inventory your next Orb of Annulment will remove only Desecrated modifiers

I hope this helps someone. Realising that most of these things are just ways of targeting specific mods or vacant mods on a piece of gear. I will add that the Chaos orb Omen descriptions are confusing to newer players and a bit of a trap. It’s pretty easy to think erasure would be related to an annulment when it’s actually just related to the first stage of using a Chaos orb which is a 2 step process of removing a modifier and then adding one.

Most of this really boils down to,
No omen = use currency on random prefix or suffix
Then it’s just the variations of targeting them and of course the Omen of Light for annuling a desecrated mod only

The nuance of crafting comes down to knowing when to use these of course along with how to use things like fracturing and is something best learned through practice and other tutorials.
