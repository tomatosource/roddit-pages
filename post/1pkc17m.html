A guaranteed good MVP take every day for the rest of the season

There’s been so many posts about SGA, Luka, and Jokic and the MVP race. I know this is one more, but my take has the benefit of barring some catastrophic injury it will be correct every hour of every day until the award is given.

First, I grew up in Denver and am unapologetically a Nuggets and Jokic homer. There. My loyalties and biases.

All three of these guys (and some others like Giannis) are very good players! But barring major injury or like a really bad case of gout or something, the Thunder are going to win a crapton of games. 70 give or take a few. When you are as good as SGA and your team wins 70+ games you win MVP and we don’t need to argue, get mad about it, or overcomplicate it.

On March 3 if you are wondering if SGA is going to win the MVP the answer is yes.

January 12? Yes.

April 16? Yes.

After he has a bad game? Yes.

If you think Luka is really good? Still yes for SGA.

If you don’t love the aesthetics of his game? Yes he will still win.

If the mods delete this shitpost that is nonetheless 100% gospel truth? Yes.

If he gets caught in a gambling scandal? Yes, because there are no gambling scandals look over there! Wowww so neat.

If SGA is dead? Yes if he played at least 65 games before it happened.

You don’t need to ask, you don’t need to write an essay. If you are tempted, just come here and remind yourself that SGA will win and that is fine.

Now you can enjoy watching basketball (except for those of you who aren’t into that sort of thing, I hope you enjoy your box scores).
