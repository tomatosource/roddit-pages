Sprinting is great! The mechanic has room for more growth/customization.

Overall sprinting has had a great first impression. Sometimes I get tripped up and eat dirt unexpectedly, but usually I kinda feel like it’s my fault, and the risk-reward in combat is awesome.

One direction I’d love to see options unfold for is to push into the fantasy of a berserker. I’d love to see a Spirit Gem or Keystone that prevents you from getting knocked down by enemies you’re sprinting directly at. Maybe otherwise some nodes that give degrees of a cone where that effect would take place.

There’s been a lot of times as Warrior where I’ve been charging heroically into the enemy and I eat a crossbow bolt and tumble into the ground, and while I can say “Okay yeah, that’s how the mechanic works - I should have expected that”, I’d LOVE to see ways to turn a barbaric charge into a fearsome engagement tool!
