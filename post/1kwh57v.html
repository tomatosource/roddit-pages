The VanVleet cap-clearing rumor mirrors the Van Exel rumor from 20 years ago

I saw one of those fake “insider” rumors floating around Facebook that said the Celtics might trade Jaylen Brown to the Rockets for Fred VanVleet, #10 pick, and “other draft compensation” with the idea that Boston would then decline VanVleet’s $44.9M team option for 2025–26 and create instant cap relief.

Obviously nonsense for a bunch of reasons—but what was mildly interesting about this fake rumor is that it unintentionally echoes some deep-cut Celtics history involving a different “Van”… Nick Van Exel.

Back in 2005, the Celtics were spinning their wheels in mediocrity. They had just won 45 games, bowed out in the first round after re-acquiring Antoine Walker (who briefly wore #88 before pressuring rookie Al Jefferson into switching from #8 to #7). Danny Ainge, meanwhile, was reportedly very high on Chris Paul going into the draft and explored a blockbuster deal: Paul Pierce to Portland for the #3 pick (which Ainge would have used on CP3) and Nick Van Exel’s contract.

Van Exel was making about the same salary as Pierce, but the final year of his deal was *non-guaranteed*—effectively functioning like a team option. The idea was to trade for Van Exel, immediately waive him, and wipe his salary off the books, giving Boston instant cap relief to spend on free agents and begin a rebuild around Chris Paul, Al Jefferson, and other youth.

The trade allegedly collapsed because Pierce “squashed” it. I’ve never seen it reported exactly how he pulled that off given I don’t believe he had a no-trade clause, but apparently he didn’t want to play in Portland and the deal died. Instead, Boston went into a two-year freefall (33 wins, then 24) before landing a highly valuable Top 5 pick that set off the chain of events that landed Ray and KG.

So admittedly a couple weeks ago as I started personally considering big-picture moves to set up a 2025-26 tank job, I also keyed in on that VanVleet 44 mil team option and thought to myself, “Hmm, maybe that acts like the Van Exel “instant cap relief” contract 20 years ago and can be a key trade asset if the Rockets want to pair Udoka and Brown together and give up something juicy like Amen Thompson?”

I’m not terribly surprised to see some of these bogus twitter/Facebook rumors coming up with similar concepts since they basically do the same thing we do (come up with logical ideas based on available info), but in their case they pretend to have inside information.

But in this case, I’ve seen it reported that whoever trades for VanVleet must first decide to pick up his team option. This would suggest that it’s no longer possible to trade and THEN decide (the instant-expiring contract). Possibly in the past 20 years the path-not-taken rebuild strategy Boston almost took has subsequently been killed via a CBA update closing the loophole?
