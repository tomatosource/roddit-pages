Issues with the current socketable system, and a possible solution.

With the mid-patch introduction of all the new socketable items, I’ve been really thinking about the design purpose of having 112 different socketables dropping in the game. It can be a hassle to acquire exactly what item you want at best, and extremely frustrating at worst such as in an SSF environment.

My issues with the socketable system are as follows:

  • Lesser Runes become vanishingly rare once everyone is in maps and they cease dropping. Getting your hands on Lessers to level new characters becomes a challenge, and later in leagues they become inordinately expensive due to the nature of their drops.

  • There are so many socketables of each type that finding the exact one you want is a massive uphill battle in SSF, and the artificial scarcity via RNG drops of each type is an issue even in trade.

  • The currency exchange UI is incredibly cluttered and not user-friendly. There are 3 different tabs in the exchange for the 3 types of socketable items: Runes, Soul Cores, and Talismans. Each tab has quite a few different items, but bar memorizing every single one by name, you can’t tell what each one does without hovering over each one manually and reading the effect.

  • The entire system seems to be trying to reinvent the wheel, and actively attempting to make a departure from an already established system that works: the crafting bench.

With all that said, I have been thinking a lot as to what some solutions to a few of these issues could be, without deviating too much from the design space of content exclusive drops. I think I’ve pegged down a solid system for managing socketables, at least in my opinion:

A pseudo-bench that uses both universal and content-exclusive currency to runecraft on items.

Let me explain: The idea here is to entirely replace the current dropping of socketable items into simply 3 new baseline currency types that can be exchanged to craft socketable mods on items.

These 3 new currencies could be called something similar to ‘Unetched Rune’, ‘Chaos Seed’ and ‘Spirit Essence’, I think you can guess what types of socketables each one refers to. Runes can drop anywhere, Chaos Seeds in the Trials of Chaos, and Spirit Essence from Wisped rares.

Lesser Runecrafts might cost 3 Unetched Rune, normals 5, and Greaters 10. Baseline Soul Core crafts could be set up similarly with base ones costing 3, and the more powerful ones costing more. GGG of course sets the drop rate for each socketable, so translating each one’s drop weighting to a cost relative to their previous rarity makes sense.
