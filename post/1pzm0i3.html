Armour ES is actually good, zealoths oath and barkskin make a fun and unique build!

Take a look at what i made defensively in the planner here to appreciate what i made: https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/iamwillpower-5983/character/GrizzlyBearGrills

So during the campaign, no idea what i wanted to play eventually as a druid i saw barkskin and though that is cool, if i can get hit and regain my energy shield somehow i get armour out of my energy shield and my health pool gets damaged less because there is more armour on the next hit.

So i went to work with it and started playing some homebrew jank decided to go with life regen, life leech and zealoths oath together with tecrod for life regen per life spent and blood magic to not deal with mana.

This build was focussed on testing how well it performed in maps. I had a great testing subject right beside me, my friend with 8.000 energy shield vs my own 2500 hp and 3500 ES and 5K base armour. Barkskin gives me another 992 base armour, which also benefits from all the increases to armour.

That base armour goes pretty hard with barkskin, armour per rage, damage taken from companions and 11% block from my talisman.

Energy shield was winning on a few occasions, but my leech and regen being around 900/second or maybe even more was actually competing with my friends survivability.

I would need more armour on my gear to get more out of barkskin but it definitely is viable for Endgame. The big scary oneshots from bosses will hurt, as per design, but regular mapping with a party of 3, doing crit mods, extra damage, elemental pen, lowered resistances and such. The usual suspects when a character falls over from 3 abyss rares, it could actually take it really well.

But ofcourse the real build breaker mod for me is less recovery because blood magic eats my health and i need to regain that health and more before my next attack to keep my energy shield full.

Pounce was really helpful for this, since i can put down consecrated ground, my wolves break armour on bosses and rares from extracting shred impales.

I was regenerating alot of life, but not enough, so i took the arcane surge gives more life regen node and 10% chance to gain arcane surge on crit. The 100% crit ascendancy and on top of all that i could freely take a bunch of conditional damage nodes.

Single target is nothing crazy, but mapping feels really good and the character really has a unique feel to it. Sprinting, tripping and becoming tankier every hit you take was pretty fun to see as a side effect!

Sadly the belt armour did not count towards barkskin which would have given me another 300 armour, i havent been rich enough to buy a helmet with enough armour to make use of the 1 ES per 8 armour node so i went with a pure ES helmet.
