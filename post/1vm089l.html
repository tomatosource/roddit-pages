My personal list of changes that I would love to see in 1.0

It’s getting near the end of the league for me, and I wanted to share some of the pain points, improvements, and general QoL additions that I really hope make it into the next version of the game. I’m not going to touch on some of the bigger ones that have been discussed to death here, but some smaller ones that I think would go a long way to making the game even more enjoyable than it already is, for new and experienced players both.

This is long, feel free to skip, also feel free to debate or educate me on any shortcuts or ways around the issues I am about to list.

MAPPING AND COMBAT

- Omen of Light tutorial of some kind. Many new players will not be aware of the way the Amanamu Void works, and will miss out on some very valuable currency. If GGG is intent on having this mechanic exist, which I am personally fine with, I think it would be great to have an NPC guide you to your first Omen of Light on the battlefield, explaining how this works and maybe even adding some story to explain WHY this mechanic acts how it does.

- While we are on the topic of Abyss rares, I want to discuss Shade Walker. PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE make the ground effect around the Shade Walker rare more visible. It is almost impossible to see when there are a lot of monsters (and even when there aren’t many, it isn’t super obvious), and this effect drains my life faster than just about anything in the game as a melee character.

- Delirium. I would love if the maps stayed delirious permanently after the boss is killed. I sometimes miss stuff on the way to the boss, and I hate that timer counts down when I have to backtrack after killing it.

- Rogue Exiles. Cool idea, but really annoying in execution. Please make them more visible in a crowd, and also PLEASE give them a cooldown on their dodge roll or make them engage at a closer distance so big dumb melee characters like myself don’t have to chase these stupid things all over the map.

- Summoning circles seem too half-baked to me. Click pillars and spawn random boss? They have to be able to flesh this out a bit more interestingly than this.

- Azmeri spirits, again, cool idea but annoying in execution. Instead of the wisp flying all over, why not just make the tablet affix read “25% for all rares to be inhabited by an Azmeri Wisp”, or something like that. Then you start the map and the rares all already possessed. I dunno, I’m bad at ideas, but this can be greatly improved upon.

- Strongboxes. please let me spend currency from my stash to exalt/vaal etc. Same thing with Essences, or anything else that asks for currency during mapping.

- Shorten boss animations in mapping. The mermaid boss is awesome in the campaign, but not so much in maps. I know many of you run builds that probably kill her in .5 seconds, but there are probably a lot of bad players like me that have to wait through a long annoying invulnerable phase every time. There are others as well, like the monster that has to keep running through doors and eventually pick up the giant bell, who incidentally seems to sometimes be a shortened version and sometimes not? I dunno

- Clean up the map detail a bit. I love the visual detail in this game, I think it’s beautiful, but there are still too many times where I am getting stopped by some small puddle on the ground or something like that. Also, there are too many times where a building or a tree still obscures half my screen even when I am standing near it and it should disappear.

- I would love a short period of maybe 1s where I can walk through enemies that are spawning beneath me, like when you are fighting the waves of runestone monsters or strongbox monsters etc. I am oftentimes blocked before the enemies even fully spawn. I don’t mind body blocking in general, just a grace period when monsters are first spawning.

GEAR

- Not a ton to say here because things will probably continue getting overhauled with the new version, but one thing in particular that I would love is if Exceptionals and Tier 5 gear were made more rare and also more desirable. Exceptionals should probably have something like a quality range of 30-40% instead of 21%-30% to compete with extra socket Exceptionals. Tier 5 gear should have an even smaller pool of affixes and values that can roll. Make them exciting when they drop.

QOL STUFF

- Please let me transfer currency from the currency exchange directly to my stash.

- Please give me an option for even smaller UI elements such as monster/boss health bar, and the giant ugly boxes around Strongboxes and that stupid Verisium Sentry for example. I have a big screen.

- Larger item pick up radius. If I can see an item drop on my screen, I should be able to click on it to pick it up. I’m not going to run all the way back to pick up an exalted orb, but if I could click on it while moving on that would be great. Also, I wish I could pick up items mid-skill animation. I know this doesn’t make sense from a real-life standpoint, but this is a video game.

- Shop stuff. I want to be able to pretty much do everything in batch. Batch list an inventory of stuff on the market, like 25 tablets that are 2div each, or something like that. I would like to be able to batch price change stuff that is already listed. I love that we can trade ingame now, but please don’t stop there.

Anyway that’s about all I got. If you got this far, thanks for reading exiles.
