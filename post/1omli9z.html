Remove one portal limit in maps POE2

Hi,

I know many of you hardcore players will most likely disagree with this take.
I wish POE2 would not have a one limit portal to maps in the end game. Why ? Not because I don’t like punishing mechanics, but because this mechanics particularly is aweful to play with.

Not everyone here has a godlike PC but still would like to play the game. But even though I have a i710700k and a 3070Ti, the game in late mapping a bloody powerpoint.
