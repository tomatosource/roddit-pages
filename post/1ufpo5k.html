Mana Siphon shouldn’t appear on invulnerable Expedition mobs

This has probably been and is, still a problem, feels worth to bring it up again.

With Expedition becoming one of the main endgame farming strategies, this interaction feels especially frustrating. You can’t damage the invulnerable Expedition mobs that chase you, and because they use ranged attacks they constantly try to reposition. That often causes them to walk directly into a Mana Siphon on you, making the encounter much more annoying than it needs to be.

Normally I wouldn’t mind this on random rares or even unique monsters, but these invulnerable Expedition mobs are different because you have no way to deal with them other than waiting for them to fk off(so never) or kill you.

Also, the new serpent/shrimp-looking enemies are awesome. They really remind me of the Harbingers from PoE 1, and I genuinely like their design. I just don’t think Mana Siphon is a good fit when it’s attached to an invulnerable enemy.

Hopefully GGG takes another look at this interaction. Thanks.
