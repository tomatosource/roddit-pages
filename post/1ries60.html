[The Sporting News] The Knicks’ Mahamed Diawara just set the NBA record for most 3-point attempts per minute in a game, shooting 13 times in 14 minutes.

In tonight’s 114 - 89 victory over the San Antonio Spurs, the New York Knick’s Mohamed Diawara shot 13 three-pointers in just 14 minutes which is an NBA record for most three-point attempts per minute in a game (with a minimum of 10 minutes played)

Diawara hit 4 of those shots from distance and finished the game with 14 pts in 14 minutes, 4 rebounds, and finished +18 for the night.
