Mavericks tried to buy out Stars AAC ownership for $110, Stars countersuit claims

“In the counter lawsuit filed by the Stars on Oct. 28, the team alleges that the Mavericks’ owners sent a letter and $110 in cash to the Stars owners, claiming, per the teams’ 1999 agreement, to purchase the Stars’ stake in the arena because the team relocated its team’s headquarters to Frisco.”

“The Stars deny that they have declined to invest in improvements to the AAC. Instead, in the statement, they claim that they have tried to renovate the arena for the last five years, but the Mavericks have refused to participate. “

The Dallas Stars have wholly denied the Mavericks claims. They have rebuked breaching the ownership agreement and denied not wanting to pay for renovations. This is likely going to be a long dispute but in the mean time, fire Nico and sell the team.
