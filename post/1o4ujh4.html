The biggest mechanical buff GGG can give to the game is to allow skills to push mobs out of the way if a spawning object skill or movement skill connects with their hit box

Skills like stampede, frozen locus and other back jumping skills don’t want their movement stopped by an enemy getting in the way. Push them out the way.

Skills like shield wall, frozen locus, frost wall and glacial bolt would be much better if they pushed back mobs that happened to be on their spawn point rather than breaking the object.

While we are at it, I don’t want my dodge roll to be stopped by a pot. Break that pot!
