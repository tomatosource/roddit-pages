Who will be the 2024-2025 NBA Rookie Of The Year?

This class has been very underwhelming since McCain went out for the year.

Leading PPG at 11.8 is Jaylen Wells, Memphis.

Next 5 leading candidates:

  • Alex Sarr, Washington (11.6)
  • Stephon Castle, San Antonio (11.6)
  • Zaccharie Risaacher, Atlanta (10.5)
  • Dalton Knecht, LAL (9.3)
  • Zach Edey, Memphis (9.3)

None of the leading candidates are above 27MPG

None of the leading candidates are above 4.0 APG (Castle is at 3.6)

Only 2 of the leading candidates are above 4.0 RPG (Edey 7.3, Sarr 6.7)

Some notable past ROY winners who scored under 12 PPG:

  • Malcolm Brogdon - 2016-17 (10.2)
  • Jason Kidd - 1994-95 (11.7)

Traditionally the ROY winner have shown to at least average 15PPG and 30MPG

This draft class is certainly one to forget this season.
