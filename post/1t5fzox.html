Best “forgotten” World Series?

Obviously there are some WS that stand up as some of the greatest ever played - 1960, 1986, 2001, 2011, among others - but what are the ones that don’t get talked about, for one reason or another, but were exciting?

I was thinking about the 2015 World Series. It only went 5 games, but it was much, much closer than it sounds. 3 of the 5 games were close. The Royals had 8th or 9th inning comebacks in games 1, 4, and 5. The Mets were this close 🤏 to having won a boring WS in 5 games. It breaks my heart, but objectively speaking, it was a thrilling series that nobody ever mentions anymore.
