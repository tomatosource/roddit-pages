My first Level 100 + League review

https://preview.redd.it/w8renahwyyfg1.png?width=2559&format=png&auto=webp&s=efd6abb3c99657edaea03587a8166edc359e98d7

Well, did it. I got my first Level 100 Character. Without the Temple it would probably not have happened. And i don’t think i will have the patience to ever do it again.

I played a Bloodmage with Ember Fussilade/Fireball as Chaos (Blackflame Covenant) as a pure Life build (Bloodmagic).

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/FlowJow-2847/character/WeaponizedBodyFluids

My best piece of equipment is the Wand in the screenshot and was crafted from an unfinished version with 4 stats i bought. And i dropped a foiled Headhunter from a Reliquary Key. Never even had normal one before and i’m playing PoE rather casually on and off since 2014.

Overall i loved the league. Being rich opened up a lot of possibilities to experiment and craft on my own. I think i made around 2500-3000 Divs throughout the league with everything combined. My Temple wasn’t really optimized so i didn’t drop 100s of divs per run. Profits were kinda equally divided between Temple, crafting and Ritual.

But i also have some bad things to say.

- I am not happy with the bricking (looping) mechanic. At Lvl 99.7 i made a mistake, that looped my Temple and it killed 95% of all drops and 80% of my Exp gain. I would have not made the mistake, if connections from rooms from Medallions would properly show the connection they produce. I almost gave up at that point and had to take almost a week off, because i was so angry. If that had happened at ANY other point before, i would have quit the league without looking back.

- Shroud walker triggers way too often, was annoying when i wanted to collect drops and occasionally bugged me out of the surgeon table, placing me inside mobs. I couldn’t move anymore and not use the table afterwards. The only thing i could do i quickly logout, so i wouldn’t die.

- I really hate Temporal chains. It is the worst mod in the game. In PoE1 it wasn’t that bad, because you could mitigate it with Quicksilver flasks and/or Movement skills. I’d love to see changes to reduce impact or straight up remove it from the game.

Despite all that, even with all the frustration it caused, it was the most fun i ever had in the game. But i need a long break now.
