What are some of your favorite baseball superstitions/rituals?

I don’t think it’s any secret baseball, and its players, are a superstitious bunch. I’m gonna be taking some of my nephews to a game this season, and I’d love to have a list in my head to help teach them and instill some magic in the game.

So what are your favorites? From the more obvious, to the more obscure and team-specific. Things like:

  1. When a pitcher has a no-hitter, nobody says the words “no-hitter” and use things like “No-no” instead.

  2. Don’t step on the foul lines

  3. (this one is a bit more obscure) “Twosies”. On a 2 ball, 2-strike count with 2 outs, you take off your hat and shake it like a beggar would to say “give me something good to hit” (if your team is at the plate) or “give me a good call, ump” (if your team is pitching).

  4. The Rally Cap: turning your hat upside down in the late innings to spark a comeback.

  5. Ignoring a player in the dugout after they hit their first career homer.
