Quick Tutorial: Setting Up Your Shop & Using The New Merchant Tabs

I noticed a lot of people asking about this in chat, and honestly it took me over an hour to figure out myself. So here’s a simple step-by-step guide to help you get your shop running in PoE2.

How to set up your shop:

Step 1: Set up merchant tabs

  • Go to the PoE2 website: https://pathofexile2.com/my-account/overview
  • Convert your premium stash tabs into Merchant Tabs, or buy them in the store.
  • Shortcut: Press M in-game.

https://preview.redd.it/mew7lj1u77mf1.jpg?width=1531&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=7eda4630e1db76aba361340a169b8721acaddba9

Step 2: You need to go to your hideout to use your shop.

  • You can do this by simply typing /hideout in chat.
  • In Standard, if you already have a character that completed the campaign, you can use your shop immediately.
  • In the new League, if you’re fresh, you’ll unlock a hideout around Act 4 after the first few quests.

Step 3: Find Ange.

If you completed the campaign before, Ange should already be in your hideout, just click on her to manage your shop. I’m playing in standard and I did not have to complete any quests to see ange.

https://preview.redd.it/5hix5fo1a7mf1.jpg?width=855&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=7fcc024cee356ef778b22fd66aa9e12abb724e64

Step 4: PROFIT!!!

After you sell your stuff, there’s a nifty tab that displays all your past sales.

https://preview.redd.it/gxbgcnq0a7mf1.jpg?width=1538&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=57681509cc1bd580b19d0e71fdd6173b35ac62d1

That’s it!

FAQ:

Q1: How do you buy items?

Use the POE2 trade website (https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2/search/poe2/Standard) and filter by “Instant Buyout” at the top right and click on “Visit Hideout” on an item’s listing to go to a players store.

Q2: How do I access the in game market browser?

With the menu on the right of your flasks, or simply use the forward slash hotkey “/” in-game.

Q3: How do I get to Act 4?

If you’ve already completed the campaign, click on your waypoint then go to Act 3. Following the quest markers and NPC prompts to advance into Act 4.

First Thoughts:

  • Standard trade issues still exist: price-fixers are still around, and many won’t switch to Instant Buyout.
