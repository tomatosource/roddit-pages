Some people are really overrating how much Miami lost in the short term.

Lots of people are claiming the team is a play in despite just getting a straight up major roster upgrade. Even saying that the Heat’s roster is equivalent to the Bucks W/O Giannis. So let’s look at some numbers.

Bucks W/O Giannis last year: 15-31 for a sub 30 win pace

Heat W/O Herro (our main loss): 29-20 for a near 50 win pace

Herro was injured most of the season and had some major fit issues with the team when he did play, while also just not being right for almost the entire season. This pace would have put the Heat at the 5 seed last year, something they were only 3 games outside of to begin with.

Beyond Herro the Heat lost 2 real bench players. Both Jaquez and Ware are real NBA players and will be missed. However both skillsets are covered by the trade itself. Jaquez was such a massive windfall because he was the one guy who would get to the rim for Miami, with the team struggling mightily when he was out (granted, against basically the best opponents possible, but still). As good of a season as Jaquez had, he has nowhere near the rim presence of Giannis who is still putting up some of the best rim numbers in NBA history. Ware’s offensive rebounding will also be missed, but again, Giannis (and the corpse of Portis) cover for this. Ware’s OREB are better and Giannis is not the rebounder I think he was 4 years ago, but he’s still damn good.

The team absolutely has holes and need to retain Powell for their offense, but it’s going to roll out as a top 5 defensive team with an offense based around Giannis, who dragged a horrific Bucks team to a near .500 record with a blazingly good 125 offensive rating when he was on the floor.
