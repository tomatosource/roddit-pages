How far will the current old-guard get in the career scoring/assists leaderboards?

It’s off-season time - here’s a few fun questions to chew on:

1) Will LeBron pass Chris Paul for #2 in total career regular season assists?

Chris Paul - 12,552

Jason Kidd - 12,091

LeBron James - 12,016

Barring injury, LeBron passing Kidd is a safe bet. Passing CP3 is a tougher ask - Assuming LeBron plays 65 games in his (presumably) final season, he’d have to average over 8 dimes per game to break CP3’s record.

Verdict: Probably not, but LeBron is already #2 all-time in total (regular season + playoffs) assists anyways

2) Will Kevin Durant pass Karl Malone for #3 all-time in total career regular season points?

Karl Malone - 36,928

Kobe Bryant - 33,643

Kevin Durant - 32,597

Barring injury, KD passing Kobe this year is a safe bet. KD’s been extremely reliable during the twilight phase of his career - he’s averaged 72 games/season for the past 3 seasons. He continues to be a scoring machine as well. The only season he’s ever averaged less than 25PPG was his rookie season, and even then he averaged over 20PPG.

I’m taking a conservative route here - assuming 20PPG and 65 games/season, that’s 1300 points/season. If KD could keep that level of production, he could pass Malone in ~3.5 seasons. KD turns 38 at the start of next season, so he’d be ~41 if that were to occur.

Verdict: Barring injury, this seems probable

3) Will James Harden join LeBron James as the only other player with 30K points and 10K assists in the regular season?

James Harden total points (regular season) - 29,339

Isiah Thomas total assists (regular season) - 9,061

James Harden total assists (regular season) - 8,873

Points? Easily. Barring injuries, Harden will join the 30K club this season.

Assists? Tougher ask. Barring injures, Harden will join the 9K club and pass Zeke this season, which will put him inside the top-10 all time for career regular season assists. Getting another 1000 dimes on-top of that will take some time.

Like KD, Harden has been remarkably durable during the twilight of his career, averaging 74 games/season across his last 3 seasons. If I take the conservative route and mark him down for 6 assists/game and 65 games per season, that’s 390 assists/season. That means he’ll break the 10K club in 3 seasons. He turns 37 before the season starts, which means he has a chance to get into this club before he turns 40.

Verdict: Barring injury, this also seems probable

4) Can James Harden retire inside the top 5 career regular-season scoring list?

Kobe Bryant - 33,643

James Harden - 29,339

KD is realistically passing Kobe, which means Kobe will now be #5 all time. Harden is currently a hair over 4300 points from passing Kobe.

Simple calculation - 20PPG at 65 games per season is 1300 points/season. Barring injury, he can pass Kobe in ~3.5 seasons of productive play. If I guess, he’ll hit this around age 40.

Verdict: Barring injury, probable.
