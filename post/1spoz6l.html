Inevitable criticals from Forced Outcome: I did the maths.

So I worked out the expected hit damage while taking Forced Outcome in the Oracle ascendancy and how it compares to normal. Firstly, define

x=pre crit-calculation hit dmg,

y=bonus crit dmg (y&gt;=1),

z=crit chance (0&lt;z&lt;=1).

Basically, the normal expected hit dmg is calculated: D=x*(1+y*z), whereas taking Forced Outcome means your expected hit dmg is: D=x*(1+y*0.7^(1/z)/0.7).

So, you could also think of it as while taking forced outcome, your ‘effective crit chance’ is 0.7^(1/z)/0.7 instead of z. Therefore, effective crit chance with Forced Outcome is only greater than normal crit given that your normal crit chance is greater than about 16.55% (this is the minimum amount of crit chance for which Forced Outcome will be a buff to expected hit dmg, less crit than this means Forced Outcome is a nerf). Furthermore, the comparative advantage is maximised at a normal crit chance of 50%, at which point Forced Outcome effective crit is 70%.

Next I wanted to know, given that I’m using Forced Outcome, what is the comparative value (in terms of increase to expected hit damage) of % crit chance passive nodes vs % bonus crit dmg passive nodes (i.e., what stat to prioritise based on current stats). So considering the expected damage function D=x*(1+y*0.7^(1/z)/0.7), taking a ratio of partial derivatives with respect to z and y, we find a conversion of ln(1⁄0.7)*y/z^2, that is to say:

A +1% increase to your crit chance is worth roughly a +(0.3567*y/z^2)% increase in your bonus crit dmg, where of course z and y are your current crit chance and bonus crit dmg, respectively.

However, here we should note that when you see % increases to crit chance on the passive skill tree, this is referring to adding that percentage of your base crit chance. Let’s label base crit chance = b. (0&lt;b&lt;1). Then taking this scaling into account, the above statement would similarly be:

A +1% crit chance increase (on passive skill tree) is worth roughly a +(0.3567*b*y/z^2)% increase in your bonus crit dmg, where z and y are your current crit chance and bonus crit dmg, respectively.

So for example, I had a look at my oracle character and I have (excuse the bad stats) crit chance z=0.1367 (or 13.67%), and bonus crit dmg y=1.7 (or 170%) and base weapon crit b=0.08 (or 8%). So using the above formula, in terms of expected hit dmg, every +1% crit chance on the passive is worth about +2.596% increase to my bonus critical dmg.
