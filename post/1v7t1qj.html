Why almost every non-life build needs a stun charm.

I’ve seen so many videos of people die and lose their maps due to “lag” and “character not responding”, which are almost always stun locks.

Some of these are initiated by a real lag/stutter. Some are not caused by internet or performance issues at all. However, players thought the death was caused by these issues because their characters don’t respond. The real culprit is that they get stun locked so they can’t move or act. Also stun removes previous player commands so the character stops moving until further commands.

I have 1700 life, 14k ES, 30% unconditional damage reduction, all 4 res capped. I have 20% stun threshold from ES, and 82% increased stun threshold. This sounds like pretty solid stun protection right?

Guess what? The deli rares and expedition mobs stun me dozens of times per map. The big sword attack and poison rune explosion almost always stun.

Stun is extremely dangerous because things like poison rune with high frequency hits and stun buildup can stun your character to death.

Get a blue stun charm. You will still feel the stun as it removes your previous commands, but you can move immediately after being stunned. This will save your life.

Just try it and see.
