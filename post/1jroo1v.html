Huntress… underwhelming

So huntress… Parry is incredibly clunky, requiring you to get ganked by mobs in order to parry just one, disengage has a tiny hit box so trying to use it to generate frenzy charges will just miss 70% of the time. Parry is also a lot harder to use on enemies that dont have any melee based skills, like mages/archers and projectile crabs. Along with the stun threshold nerfs in this patch, getting hit 4⁄5 times while trying to parry ends up with a 3 second heavy stun that you cant attack/dodge in even from trash mobs, usually just being a death sentence

The first 3 rows of skills deal next to no damage even with frenzy charges, let alone without. In more bad news for huntress, perpetual charge support gem can’t be applied to spear skills right now, so all the skills that revolve around having frenzy charges to do more than 15 damage (all of them), has no way to maintain the charges through support gems. The class itself might become better when it starts picking up ascendencies but in the state its in now, its going to be a lot more effort than its worth to push this through trials.

Needs some serious (re)work putting into it to make it somewhat viable
