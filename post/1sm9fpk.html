Thinking about switching to Solo Self-Found as a new player — good idea or not?

Hi everyone,

I’m currently playing a CoC Blood Mage and a Poison Burst Pathfinder. I started about three weeks ago, and both characters are now around level 68, running Tier 1–3 maps.

That said, I’m not really enjoying it as much as I expected. A lot of my gear is bought, and it kind of takes away from the experience for me. I mostly went this route because the season is ending soon, and I didn’t want my effort to feel wasted.

Now I’m considering switching to Standard Solo Self-Found. I feel like it might be more rewarding to progress entirely on my own—finding or crafting all my own gear, learning the systems better, and really understanding the game as I go.

Has anyone here made a similar switch or felt the same way? Do you think SSF is a good idea for a relatively new player, or is it easy to hit walls and stall progression? What are the biggest downsides of going Solo Self-Found?
