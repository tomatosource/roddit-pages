Taiki Sekine of the Yokohama DeNA BayStars is currently participating in the Mexican Winter League. He has released his game-day schedule, which shows just how demanding the season is. It’s an extremely tough and packed schedule.

After finishing a game, he arrived at his hotel at midnight. He managed to get less than an hour of sleep before leaving the hotel at 3:00 a.m. He checked his luggage at the airport and boarded a flight at 6:00 a.m., arriving at Ciudad Obregón Airport after crossing a one-hour time difference.

He then took a nap at his apartment until 1:00 p.m., bought lunch, and headed to the home stadium of the Yaquis de Obregón, the team he belongs to. Batting practice began at 3:00 p.m., followed by a game that started at 7:10 p.m. After the game, he had dinner, including a bean-based soup, and finally returned to his apartment at 11:55 p.m.

There are also times when he has to get his sleep during a 12-hour bus ride.
