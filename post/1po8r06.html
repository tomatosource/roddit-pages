[Fischer] The Toronto Raptors have expressed interest in acquiring Domantas Sabonis and Anthony Davis.

Source: https://open.substack.com/pub/marcstein/p/the-many-many-layers-to-the-nbas?r=nuq3a&utm_medium=ios

The business Toronto is ultimately willing to conduct before the February trade buzzer will likely come down to how good the 16-11 Raptors still are as that deadline draws closer.

Toronto, though, has sent out some early signals that it hopes to upgrade its frontcourt. Sources with knowledge of the Raptors’ thinking have identified them as a team with interest in Domantas Sabonis as well as Davis.

I’ll add that I think it’s a wise forecast to project the Raptors to come calling on Antetokounmpo, too, if the Bucks eventually do reach the point over the next six weeks that it’s time to answer those Giannis calls.

Sources say that the Raptors, to be clear, do hope to retain Jakob Poeltl as part of any in-season dealings they undertake rather than part with their long-tenured center. Yet we’ll have to see how the up-and-down Raptors, whose offense has faded without the injured RJ Barrett, hold up in the East before we know for sure if they are truly prepared to follow up last February’s acquisition of Brandon Ingram with another win-now splurge.
