PSA: The “grand project” tablet allow you to rapidly generating new temples without exploiting the campaign.

    1. Find a tower (preferably with little water close to it so you can as many maps as possible).
    1. Clear it while using a “grand project” tablet. This will let you run any map in the range of the tower, without having to path or clear bosses.
    1. Go into the maps and find the vaal altar as quick as possible (high MS or blink helps a lot here). Clear it and exit the map. Important you can use any waystones for this. There’s no need to juice them or run hard ones. We just want to be as quick as possible.
    1. Once you cleared all the maps you can usually path to a new tower in just a few maps.
    1. Rinse and repeat.

On many maps it’s not really hard to find the altar in like 20-30 seconds if you’re quick, which lets you consistently make new temples in under 5 minutes. Not as good as the exploit, but it’ll survive the patch. Enjoy.
