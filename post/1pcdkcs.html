The 3rd place Rockets played their 1st B2B of the season last night, with no travel, and still lost to the 11th place Jazz 133-125. The NBA’s scheduling imbalance is on full display.

The 13-win Rockets were the only team in the league to not have played a back-to-back until last night, the game after beating the 6-win Jazz by 28 points Sunday night. The Jazz took the lead for good in the 2nd Q, led by as much as 21, and were up by double digits most of the way until the Rockets rallied to cut the lead to 1 pt in the 4th Q on a three by KD with 5:12 remaining. Then the Jazz made 2 straight threes by Markkanen and George and the Jazz lead was at least 2 possessions until the final minute, when Sengun cut the lead to 3 pts with 52.7 seconds remaining. 6 ftas by George (5-6) and 2 by Markkanen (2-2) on fouls in the last 37 seconds were enough to hold off the Rockets.

The Rockets’ cake schedule continues with their 1st B2B including travel being in their 26th game, on the road in Sacramento after playing in Denver the night before. By that time, the Warriors will have played 6 B2Bs, 5 of which included travel.

The league should start the season earlier and end it later so they can eliminate this sort of huge imbalance in scheduling between teams and not only level the imbalance, but likely reduce injuries as well. It doesn’t make sense that they can’t do a better job of ending up with more fairness in scheduling back-to-backs.
