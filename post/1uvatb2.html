The transition from SSF to Trade league is a big surprise

For the context, this was my first SSF league and I have enjoyed playing SSF a lot - my own pace, my own challenges. I really like the idea playing around new equipment found and playing all content to achieve certain goals. I do not like delirium, but I had to farm it and it feels great to accomplish the goal.

My plan for this league was getting a new Nightfall shield and a Mjolnier, but after countless farm of Rituals and high-level content I still haven’t obtained a Mjolnier (and I need two 🫩)

I got a bit burned out farming it, so I decided to switch to a trade league. Mjolnier is a 1d unique, yet I had to chase it for two months…

But here’s the interesting part. Apparently when you migrate to Trade league, you lose your stash savings AND ATLAS PROCESS.
I am about to drop the league, as I cannot imagine going through all the quests from zero. The only thing what followed the migration was my nickname, level and gear.

I figured out to create new characters in SSF, getting to the city and emptying the stash and migrating new character to Trade (had to do it 10 times; apparently Druid is the fastest one to kill first boss), but I am totally devastated starting the atlas and quests from scratch. It was funny to open map device and getting a Tier 1 waystone as a quest reward, having a gear able to close all the existing content.

Perhaps I will make a giveaway thread soon 🫩
