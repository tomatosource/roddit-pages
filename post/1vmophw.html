[Price] Bob Iger on Jeanie Buss: “We have every intention to honor the agreement (Jeanie as the Lakers governor for 5 years) that was made between Mark and Jeanie. If things change, they’ll change, but we’re going into this with enormous respect and appreciation for who she is & what she represents”

Source: https://imgur.com/a/5bFOYGB

From Khobi Price on X who did an exclusive interview with Bob Iger:

When Walter purchased the majority ownership of the Lakers from the Buss family for a valuation of a then-record $10 billion, the league announced that former majority owner Jeanie Buss would remain the team’s Governor for a period of at least five years.

Iger told the Post he and Kushner intend to honor the agreement made between Walter and Buss. 

“We have enormous respect and appreciation for Jeanie, her father and what they’ve contributed to this franchise,” Iger said. “And we have every intention to honor the agreement that was made between Mark and Jeanie. If things change, they’ll change, but we’re going into this with enormous respect and appreciation for who she is and what she represents in the organization.”
