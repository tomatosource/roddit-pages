It is infuriating how sometimes crafting just doesn’t work, you can’t roll mods that you need at all, you waste your currency and end up selling unfinished base, even though chances were on your side.

And sometimes everything just RNGs into what you were planning to do.

This ring is not mirror tier by any means, but a pretty solid mid tier craft. I spend like 5-10 divs crafting it, although average price would be much higher.

  1. Look through my “for recomb” tab from farming T3 Xesht and find 2 rings. One with T1 cold damage and 1 with T1 cast speed. Showed them into recombinator. 16% chance. Applied omen of recombination, got a good base to start with.

  2. Apply Neural catalyst for 40% quality - this is the most expensive part of the craft. Omen of cat exalt + Perfect exalt to get T4 mana - unlucky I guess, but it will do.

  3. Kurgal omen + prefix abyss omen desecrate. Reveal - I think this guarantees %Spell damage on full energy shield.

  4. Max Tul’s catalyst to get 40% cold modifiers + omen of cat exaltation + omen of greater exaltation + Perfect exalt - lo and behold T1 Cold res + T2 ele res as if I was using Omen of homogenizing exaltation.

  5. Added Tul’s catalyst to 40% quality as it improves both res and cold damage. Alternative is to add caster quality and it would improve cast speed and spell damage, but I think higher res would sell easier.

  6. No baals and no double baals for corruption.

I wish every craft would be so polite and do exactly what I want to do with T1 and T2 rolls. SSF would be so much easier.
