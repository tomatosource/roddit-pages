Dylan Harper is a future superstar and core member of the Spurs next dynasty.

I hear a lot of people saying Spurs missed on Kon Knueppel but I don’t think they understand the true potential of Dylan Harper. He is first in highest fg% in drives in the entire league and becoming a good three point shooter as time goes by. He is quick and strong which makes him a capable defender up for any matchup. His season stats look like this:

22.4 mpg

11.8 ppg

3.3 rpg

3.9 apg

0.8 spg

0.3 bpg

1.4 tov

50.8% - FG%

34.5% - 3PT%

74.3% - FT%

57.7% - TS%

Obviously his stats are not impressive but he has been playing less and meaningful minutes in a contending team. Spurs are deep and are a system which makes players stats spread out. Also he has been getting better and better throughout the season. In his last 20 games he is averaging 13 ppg on 59% fg and 51% 3pt. He will be one of the best two-way players for years to come if he is injury free and keeps working hard.
