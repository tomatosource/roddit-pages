Am I the only one who wants PoE2 to stay simple and not repeat PoE1’s complexity overload?

Hey everyone,

I’ve been playing PoE1 since 2013, and honestly, I really want PoE2 to succeed. I kinda like what I’ve seen so far — it feels like it’s going back to the roots a bit.

But man… something changed around 2019. In my opinion, Breach was peak PoE. After that, things started getting way too complex — tons of old league mechanics, way too many currencies, atlas systems on top of atlas systems, super complicated crafting, and just an overload of skill and support gems.

Back in the day, crafting with alts, regals, and exalts was exciting and simple. You didn’t need a spreadsheet and 10 guides just to make a decent build.

PoE2 feels like it might be going back to that simpler, more grounded feeling — and I really hope when they add new content, they don’t make the same mistake as PoE1 by piling on endless mechanics. Not everything needs to go core, and not every system needs another layer.

Anyone else feel the same way?

Edit: After reading through the comments, I get both sides now. I still think PoE1 got a bit overloaded over time, but I also understand that the simplicity I miss can be seen as a lack of content for others. Guess it’s really about finding that sweet spot between depth and accessibility.

Thanks to everyone who shared their thoughts — it’s been great reading all the perspectives! ❤️
