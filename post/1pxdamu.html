Story about Robinson Cano’s teeth

Robinson Cano was my favorite player when I was a kid. I vividly remember the way that he spit his dip juice one the field and I remember thinking how cool it was. He didn’t even open his jaw. When he was a rookie, and during many of his early years on the Yankees he had a small gap between his two front upper incisors. He just forced the spit out the front of his teeth through that gap with his tongue and it looked so casual and nonchalant to me as a kid because he could spit AND smile at the same time.

I tried hundreds of times to do this on the little league field but I’d just get spit all over my chin and shirt because I didn’t have teeth like that. I also routinely tried with sunflower seed shells which also obviously didn’t work because I was stupid.

I stopped wearing my retainer as an adult and I now have a gap significant wider than Robbie cano’s ever was and I can spit like an all star now. I also wasted all the money my parents spent on braces.

Cano doesn’t have that gap anymore and I wonder if he can’t spit that way now.
