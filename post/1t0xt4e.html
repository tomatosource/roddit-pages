To the team, trust each other

I can list a myriad of reasons for the struggles, loose dribbling, poor shot making, uninspired defense, inadequate spacing and off ball movement etc etc, however…

The thing screaming at me most through the TV from the last 2 games is something I’ve seen many times over in defeat from the JT/JB core over the years.

The entire team is playing like they do not trust eachother. Players getting the ball behave like they know they won’t get the ball back if they swing it, you can blame JB or JT since they are the primary initiators but it’s about everybody.

More of the same on defense, players are hesitant to commit to a contest because they don’t trust that someone will pickup another assignment.

If this is the last game of the season please play it trusting each other , maybe the shots won’t fall but you and everyone else knows the best brand of Celtics basketball is when the ball is flying around, players getting multiple touches on the same possession if that’s what it takes. Trust that if you’re open you’ll get the ball back, trust that the open guy will hit the shot.

Please if this is the end of the season or a launch point at a title run, trust each other.
