Thoughts on Baylor

I will lead off with I’m super happy with the team so far and thrilled to see a bunch of the unknowns/tbd guys actually hitting on their potential and showing they can contribute on a good team. Walsh was a guy I wanted us to draft and then I’ll be honest I thought the dream was dead in preseason. But now he’s doing exactly what I thought he was capable of and I love it. Queta taking another step forward is huge as well (still hopefully a backup on champ team). Hugo does still need time but absolutely love what I’ve seen so far! Minott ebbs and flows for me but still has a place.

With all that said, I’m worried Baylor is not a hit and it might be time to move on after the season. I do want to give him the year because it has taken others time to finally blossom so I am willing to see. His offense is there to help - shooting has been much much better than I expected. The defense though to me has been worse than Garza. He helps down on basically every drive but the problem is he is then too unathletic and unskilled to get back to his man and defend. He has fouled several three point shooters, and gets blown by when he’s one on one. You don’t have to be Walsh athletic to defend but he defends like he has Walsh’s athleticism. His IQ on defense also worries me. He defends every guy the same, helps off anyone he’s defending. He could have Steph or Drummond on the three point line and he’ll help down on a drive on both and close out the same on both.

Again I am hoping in the long term I end up wrong on him but him being the oldest of the bunch I fear his improvement won’t be what we have seen from others. And in the end I don’t think his shooting numbers can be relied on to stay as high as they are so if that dips and you add the defense, it gets non playable fast. Here’s to hoping this post comes back to bite me when he’s helping us lift the Larry O’B!

Cheers! 🍀
