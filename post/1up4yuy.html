Laying out what we would have given up for Giannis vs what we got by trading Jaylen

I could miss some stuff, but I basically wanted to have an overview of what we currently have vs what the Celtics would have if they did the rumored trade package for Giannis. This is assuming the Bucks were actually going to trade him to us, and not just using as leverage in talks with the Heat because we couldn’t find a third team for Jaylen.

I assume Celtics FO looked at like this:

Option A: Trade for Giannis no matter what

  1. Giannis, currently with 2 years left but we would have signed him to an extension where he’d be on the Celtics until end of 2030-31

  2. Jaylen Brown would be on the Bucks or likely some Western Conference team

Option B: Trade Jaylen to the 76ers

  1. Paul George without an extension, on the books until end of 2027-28

  2. Gain 2 first round picks

  3. Gain 2 second round picks

  4. Keep Hugo

  5. Keep Schierman

  6. Keep 2-3 first round picks

  7. Keep whatever seconds

  8. Jaylen Brown plays for a top 2 or 3 franchise rival, the 76ers

Option C: Wait to trade Jaylen for a better package

Team chemistry goes way down the longer he stays on the team.

If they wanted to trade him before training camp, this PG deal may be the best offer. It could have gone away, or they could have dumped him elsewhere and Celtics would have just dealt with that team.

Waiting until the trade deadline or next offseason I believe would fetch the best package for Jaylen, but do the most harm internally to our team and Jaylen’s relationship with fans and teammates.

Overall I think the package we got could have been better but is okay. It was smart not to let it drag on, but they could have let the market build for Jaylen more.

And the worst part is that they made the 76ers better, and sent a player whose jersey should be retired to play for the enemy
