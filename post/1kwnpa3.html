Let us swap resistances

Upgrading gear in this game is frustrating and tedious because we are all gearing around resistance caps. If you want to upgrade just a single gear piece, your pool of available choices is narrowed exponentially by restricting to the specific res you need on the gear. By the time your gear is more than 1div per slot, upgrading anything requires upgrading multiple pieces at once, which is expensive and dramatically slows down the upgrade cadence.

I think POE1 had a system like this though I never played, but please let us change the element of resistance on gear pieces. Ideally for a fixed currency cost, but I’ll even take a chance of bricking the item as long as it’s deterministic or semi-deterministic.
