Future QOL that was nice to see

I just decided to post this here because I think GGG reads reddit more than their own forum lol, at least they react to some things faster from reddit… which is strange.

Anyway, GGG recently wrote that their team plays (all their free time?) in poe2, but I don’t believe it. There’s so much in the game that could be improved with just one patch, which would greatly enhance the QOL, and a player from GGG simply couldn’t miss out on it, so I don’t believe they’re playing their game.

So let’s get started.

GENERAL:

A) Market:

- We have various orbs such as divine orb, exalted orb, etc. which FOR SOME REASON have different dimensionality in 20⁄10 pieces in a stack, others have 30, others have 40, what the fuck? Why not make the dimensionality of all orbs in a stack 50?

This would greatly simplify the players life. In the context of the market, this means that you would have to spend less time acting like a monkey and transferring 5,000 exalted rob 20-piece stacks into your chest, or 10-piece stacks for divine orb in the late stages of the game. It would also look much more organized.

- Why is there still no button to send the received currency directly to the chest? Why do I have to act like a monkey and put it in my backpack first, then in the chest? This is completely absurd.

- When working with the market, if you want to sell a group of products (for example, abyss), after listing each product, you need to go back to that tab and select the one you want (in some tabs, you also need to scroll down) - is this really necessary? Can’t you remember my selection so that I can sell products from a specific tab faster and not have to click around? I think you can, and you should.

B) Mapping:

- Anyone who has been actively playing, periodically comes to look at the masters of the atlas some passive skills… and forgets to switch back to the desired one. Why not add them directly to the map selection field?

https://preview.redd.it/fj86xvdfdmch1.jpg?width=586&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=8da3cefe814fb0bad9f63cdce7d2e8c64935b5e1

- With the addition of the expedition as we know it now, it is often necessary to switch between sets of tablets (for passage maps, for fat maps, for regular maps, etc.), and it is also tiring to insert and remove them over time. Can we have 1(2?) additional space for the tablet set? Where you can select the desired option, choose the desired atlas master in the same window, and start the map?

- In the 0.5 preview, they showed us how cool it is to use map search, but they forgot to mention that you have to go through (cache) all your maps to do it. At the end of the game, I have about 50 screen space, and I wonder if I should go through them every time to find a couple of maps with simmulacrum that I missed. GGG, are you serious? Can’t u save cash of map space for every player and update it?

It would also be a nice QOL to increase the number of notes to 30 (or more), as it is currently too limited.

It would also be nice to have the ability to take notes while we’re spreading the fog from the Grand Mirror, because otherwise you might not be able to find it quickly.

Another good solution would be to not save the player’s current location in the basic content areas (temple, ritual, etc.), as we only visit them to perform a few actions and then return to the atlas. However, sometimes there are no notes about where we are, and when we enter Kaer Tarth, we have to search for our location again.

CONTENT:

- Not on all maps after killing (actually only on 1-3 it works normally) boss points where the rest of the content is located. To make it appear you need to open (somewhere) not yet opened checkpoint and/or scout a certain % of the map to see all the additional content. Why this is not done on most maps automatically after killing the boss (even if I did a speedrun to him) - personally for me is a mystery. Except that if GGG wants me to lick every corner in search of a breach icon that takes up 1x1 square and can be literally anywhere.

- There’s also a problem with boss loot due to their animations, as some bosses drop loot immediately, allowing you to continue playing. However, some bosses have animations that last for 2-3-4 seconds before they start dropping loot. This can be frustrating, as killing a boss takes only 1 second, but you have to wait 2-3-4 seconds for the loot to drop. Why not make it so that the loot drops immediately, regardless of the animation?

A) Ritual:

- I don’t know if any of the GGGs are farming the ritual, but I’m sure they’re not. The ritual is a low-mid game content where you can’t boost your loot with 200% delirium, character boost, etc. This puts certain restrictions on the amount of currency you can farm in a given amount of time. One way to farm is by depositing smaller items (low-cost omens) over and over again. However, you need to choose a new item each time you deposit it.

Personally, I start every league with a real one, and I would really like to see a “Deffer all previously postponed items” button or something like that.

This is in the basic version, in the advanced version (although I don’t think this will ever happen) I would like to have a built-in omen filter in the ritual where it would be possible to mark those omens that are needed and that will be postponed automatically. That is, it would be cool to have a button where you could set up the omens that you would like to postpone for the current stage of the game, and if they appear, you don’t need to select them manually - you just click on the button, and you select the omens for the future deffer.

This will allow you to raise the farm of the ritual to a higher level and increase its attractiveness, which will make other aspects of the game more attractive as well. Believe me, rolling 10-20-30-40 items on EVERY ritual 10-20 times (the number of rerolls) is very stifling and tiring.

B) Abyss:

- The abyss is a good mechanic, except that it takes me 2-3 seconds to kill the monsters that come out of the hole, and the game makes me wait the same amount of time for the chest to appear.

Is it possible to make the reward drop from the last monster killed in a specific hole immediately after it is killed? Or is it better to make it drop all at once from the last closed hole? Both options are acceptable, and it is better than spending an extra 40-60 seconds on the map waiting for a chest that often doesn’t drop anything good.

C) Breach:

I’m not a fan of breach, and I haven’t farmed it much, so I don’t have much to say except that you have to lick every corner of the map to find the other breach points. It’s more of a problem that they don’t reveal themselves after the boss is killed.

I can also add that wombgifts take up a lot of space, making it easier to just buy them instead of constantly spreading them out. I think a good solution would be to add them in stacks of 50, depending on the level. And it’s also possible to add them in batches to the genesis tree to receive the entire reward at once (for example, 10 batches of catalysts at once).

D) Simmulacrum:

Not much to say here, good reworking, exept some problems with Grand Mirror placement, but it mostly bug i think. And a 500+ run for voicec for me with no loot :(

E) Expedition:

This is a great rework of the expedition, and I hope it will remain the same in the future (stack and rune accumulation). However, there is still something to add.

- It’s a mystery to me why the route on the minimap is not saved when you start an expedition. What is the hidden reason for this? Often, when the area where you’re placing charges is large, it’s easy to get lost and have to search for your current position. Why not add a complete route to the map with flashing charge points to indicate your current location? This seems like an obvious solution (assuming you’re playing the game).

- Also a great solution (again if the expedition is planned to be saved in its current form) would be to add a strip of runes (for example at the bottom where the ritual icon is usually) that are already in the pool for saving to the next waves. This would speed up the expedition, as sometimes you have to go back and remember which runes are already selected.
