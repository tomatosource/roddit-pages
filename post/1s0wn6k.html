I think the Jayson Tatum criticism on this sub and r/nba is too reactionary game to game and it drives me nuts.

I’ve been posting way too much and I’m sorry but I’m bored and I gotta do this. I’ll start by saying two things:

  1. Jayson Tatum is my favorite Celtic ever. I have so much respect for him as a person, a player, and as a guy who has shown up in every must win game except for like maybe 3 in 8 years.

  2. As much as I wish he was, even at his very peak, he was still slightly below the Giannis/Embiid/jokic class before the title, and even last year he was behind the Shai/Luka/Jokic class before he got hurt.

There is plenty to criticize Tatum for. He sometimes looks lethargic when he plays, he’s had some god awful shooting performances, and I think the team’s reliance on him in 2022 and 2023 was more than he was capable of handling.

With all that being said, I still can’t believe how much disrespect he gets here and on the NBA sub. I didn’t spend time on NBA Reddit until last spring. My twitter account got hacked and I was kind of sick of that environment anyways. I appreciate the long-form post people do here, even if you do get a lot of the “not reading all that” comments. Overall though, I have been mostly under the impression that conversions here are a little more nuanced than the guys just trying to go viral on twitter.

Then, Tatum came back a couple weeks ago. Most of the commentary has been positive, but that god awful game Friday brought everyone back out of the woodwork.

A lot of moaning about the shot selection, I saw a ton of “I wouldn’t want him taking the last shot” discourse, and just general negativity about him as a player.

Like I said, he is not an MVP level player even at his best, but the complaints about him as a shooter or scorer are just like factually incorrect when looking at the numbers. And yes, I understand you have to “watch the games”. But eyes get clouded by bias, when numbers don’t.

Tatum for his career, has a 58.2% True shooting percentage. True shooting isn’t an advanced number at all, it’s just division while accounting for 3’s and free throws. In my opinion, it’s the best overall indicator of efficiency. Over the past 5 years, Tatum has been well above league average while also placing in the top 10 in scoring. For reference, Jaylen’s true shooting this year is 57.4%, and has crept back up from 56.8% since Tatum came back.

Until Shai and oddly enough Austin reeves, it was almost impossible to be more efficient as a perimeter player on that volume.

That’s what irks me so much about “shot selection”. There really isn’t much more Tatum could have done in the past to be more efficient as a shooter. Maybe you could argue it would be to get off the ball more and move it, and that’s fine. But I think a team that has been a top 5 offense since he made his first all-nba first team is still pretty good.

The next point I have to make is about the late game shooting. I get way too mad about this and I’m sorry already for what I’m going to say. *jayson Tatum is a historically good shooter at the end of games”. The stat flew around last year, but he is 22⁄58 in the last 24 seconds to tie or go ahead. The second most efficient shooter on more than 50 attempts *since they started tracking the stat*.

That just blows my mind that he can be that good, have the 50 point game 7, 46 in Milwaukee, 50 on the nets, 50 vs the wizards, insane 4th against Philly , insane 4th in a game 7 vs Miami, and he’s still a bad clutch player? It just makes no sense.

I’m old enough to understand success is a function of expectations. That’s why this year has been so fun. But I think that’s probably why he gets the hate he does, but man does he get way too much of it for being that good and that successful.
