Endgame is really nice now … but the “traveling” is still soo boring.

I’m having fun with the endgame in poe2 for the first time but what really sucks the fun out of it for me is the “traveling” to bosses/areas. Often I run a couple of maps and then just quit the game. It takes up so much time and im already not a slow build.

So this got me thinking of two ideas on how to fix this:

First is simple: Just +1 range so you can travel 2 nodes far. Half the pain. The 10% Doryani Node just doesnt cut it. Maybe as a reward for completing all atlas nodes.

Second Idea: What if there were “Travel Maps”. Those maps could simply reveal the mini map and/or the location of the map boss but have no extra content. These could be as easy as corrupted white maps to have some cost and agency to it. Any other ideas?
