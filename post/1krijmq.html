What I wish I knew earlier, at 400 hours in as someone who never played an ARPG before.

The importance of using the trade site to mitigate the vagaries of random loot drops.

The importance of being in the new league… I have a level 91 Warrior and the items I want in standard cost 30 div, but cost 1 div in league. This is fairly consistent across items, a 30x difference in cost.

The use of meta builds… I am not saying that everyone should follow the meta, but I have put more hours into this game than I’ve put into almost anything else. I think, in hindsight, I would have preferred to take a Lightning Spear Huntress to level 95 in order to farm currency for the creation of my own builds. There were long periods of struggle that would have been a lot more fun and less of a chore if I’d had an excess of currency to make experimentation painless.

Other things are probably minor, like the doing towers first to boost maps, like using delirium for anoints, like buying currency through the exchange and not through the trade site, like currency being a lot more than just exalts.

Other than that, huge thanks to the Global chat for making me buy the correct 3 stash tabs (currency, quad premium, and waystones.

What else is there I should have known earlier?
