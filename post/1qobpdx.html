Luka Dončić recently became the 5th player to score 14,000+ points before turning 27

He also crossed the 4,000 assists mark. He’s currently at 14,000+ points, 4,100+ rebounds and 4,000+ assists.

He still has an entire month left before his birthday, on the 28th of February, so he could look to pass Carmelo Anthony and finish as 3rd on the list.

The other 4 with 14k points before turning 27 are LeBron James (17,460), Kevin Durant (15,537), Carmelo Anthony (14,681) and Kobe Bryant (14,034).

Here are a few more stats for each of them:

Player: Games: MPG: PPG: Points:
LeBron James 630 40.1 27.7 17,460
Kevin Durant 569 38.0 27.3 15,537
Carmelo Anthony 591 36.4 24.8 14,681
Luka Dončić 487 35.0 29.0 14,127
Kobe Bryant 627 35.0 22.4 14,034

3 other players came within 650 points or less:

Tracy McGrady (13,570), Jayson Tatum (13,406) and Wilt Chamberlain (13,355).

Source: https://www.statmuse.com/nba/ask/most-points-by-nba-player-before-age-27
