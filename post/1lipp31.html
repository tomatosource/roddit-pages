Unholy Might DoT Compensation Suggestion/Discussion

We can agree the Lich ascendancy is supposed to be the chaos specialist and Unholy Might not applying to chaos DoTs is disappointing at best?

Some suggestions to Necromantic Conduit to make up for the missing DoT dmg, in no particular order:

  1. Let Necromantic Conduit also unlock the missing reviving chaos skeletal sorcerer that fires chaos hits. This ensures you consistently have an ally in presence to leverage unholy might. We have the other 3 elemental mages, this is a perfect spot to get a chaos mage. Let it trigger profane ritual where it dies.

  2. Contagious reanimated corpses have no expiration timer.

  3. Contagion on reanimated corpses remember the bonus power of Contagion as they died.

  4. Just add a (non-crittable?) chaos hit to spells that DoT, making them similar to Essence Drain but equal to 30% of the total DoT.

  5. Give +X chaos skill levels while not low on mana.

  6. etc, etc
