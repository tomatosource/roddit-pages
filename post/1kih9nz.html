Delve lovers, do you feel Atlas in POE2 less appealing?

I love Delve, and Altas in 2 basically combine Delve and old atlas, which are two of my favorite systems, and I am so excited when it was announced, thinking of it will be a huge success.

However after playing two seasons I feel somewhat dissapointed, few seasons:

  1. Lack of progressive feeling

In Delve you dive deeper into underground, the deeper you go you encounter not just increasing difficulty, but also more monsters, bosses and loots. In Atlas you just wander aimlessly hoping to find some point of interest, map nodes themselves do not “upgrade” when you travel further.

  1. Pace is too slow

Delve you have to follow the cart and is quite fast paced, you basically don’t have to look at mini map or think, just go straight forward. You can complete a node pretty fast.

  1. Tower juicing

Many people’s main complaint is tower. I don’t like it either. too much pain, too less gain. and it makes the pace even slower. I wish tower is only for revealing large (much larger than current radius) area. and tablets can be added into any map node (we have 4 slots for map activation right?), that way we can juice wherever we want. (tablet drop rate maybe be pretty low to compesate that)

  1. Clunky layout

I really like its visual, it’s beautiful and more realistic. however it’s hard to track all your branches, even after newly updated pin function. I strongly think it needs a “mini map mode” that looks exactly like Delve to quickly track all your explore progress.

  1. Too few content

Delve is a side content, you gather minerals from altas, spend them to delve, It’s a fun mini game that switching between map to experience different vibe and game pace. In POE2 you just got Atlas… and maybe sekhemas if you like it. and the POE2 atlas itself is not having enough of content yet. There is not much excitement beside finding a stronghold. I believe it is not a problem over time, we will get more eventually.

IMO I like the current concept and visuals of Altas, but slow, lack of accomplishment and progression really hurt the joy of playing. Do you have any idea to make them feel better?
