My main wish for Parry/Block: Please let us display the stun bar above our character in 0.5

As one of the 4 or so Active Parry enjoyers in the game, one of my main pieces of feedback is that the stun bar is super difficult to monitor during gameplay.

&nbsp;

For example, here is a crude showcase for a fight where i get rather unlucky with Evasion, and “don’t realize” that my stun bar is filling up very fast.
Try to focus on the boss attacks, and you will see that the stun bar just kinda blends in with the UI ornamentation.

But since getting heavy stunned is often a death sentence it is a rather important piece of information - especially in the campaign before you can gear and dedicate weapon passive points for stun threshold, and just keep the button pressed through entire breaches between attacks/casts without worrying.

&nbsp;

This problem is further exasperated by the inconsistency in how much stun you accumulate:

  • Monster attacks can vary a lot on damage and stun application, and these don’t always correspond to each other
  • Evasion characters don’t get feedback when they evade an attack and thus stun buildup while actively blocking/parrying, so buildup speed can be very inconsistent, especially when you don’t have super high evasion

&nbsp;

So yeah, among all of my smaller wishes for the mechanic, seeing the stun bar above my character is probably the most important one.
