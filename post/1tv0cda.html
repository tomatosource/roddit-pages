Ranking the last 20 NBA Finals in terms of enjoyability

  1. 2013 Heat vs Spurs: Epic 7 game series, Ray Allen shot was legendary. Game 7 was very close too.

  2. 2016 Cavs vs Warriors: Cavs came back from 3-1 vs a 73 win team. I put this 2nd because it had a lot of blowouts.

  3. 2010 Lakers vs Celtics: Very exciting 7 game series, Lakers get their revenge for 08

  4. 2025 Thunder vs Pacers: Was a very entertaining series but Haliburton’s injury ruined game 7

  5. 2021 Suns vs Bucks: Suns blow a 2-0 lead after Giannis goes god mode

  6. 2011 Heat vs Mavericks: Very close games, Mavericks won in 6 after LeBron kept choking

  7. 2022 Warriors vs Celtics: Fun 6 game series, Curry went God mode to come back from 2-1 and win 3 straight

  8. 2006 Heat vs Mavericks: A lot of free throws but had some very entertaining games, Heat came back from a 13 point deficit with 6 minutes left to avoid going down 0-3.

  9. 2008 Lakers vs Celtics: Was a fun 6 game series but game 6 was a boring blowout, no games came down to the wire really.

  10. 2015 Cavs vs Warriors: Was entertaining to see LeBron carry the Cavs to 2 wins, but no Kyrie or Love ruined the enjoyability.

  11. Raptors vs Warriors 2019: Entertaining 6 game series, but all the injuries to the Warriors made it less enjoyable.

  12. 2020 Finals: 6 game series but the Lakers controlled the whole series and blew the Heat out in game 6, would’ve been more interesting if Bam and Dragic weren’t injured.

  13. 2012 Heat vs Thunder: Another 5 game series, had some close games KD vs LeBron was interesting.

  14. 2017 Cavs vs Warriors: Warriors dominated the Cavs in 5 but it was interesting to see LeBron and Kyrie vs Curry and KD.

  15. 2009 Lakers vs Magic: Was a closer series than most people remember, but still Lakers won in 5.

  16. 2007 Cavs vs Spurs: Boring sweep but had close games so I put it higher

  17. Heat vs Nuggets 2023: Boring 5 game series, Heat didn’t stand a chance

  18. 2014 Spurs vs Heat: Spurs won by a record margin

  19. 2024 Mavericks vs Celtics: Very boring series, Mavs didn’t have a chance.

  20. 2018 Cavs vs Warriors: Boring sweep, Cavs didn’t stand a chance.
