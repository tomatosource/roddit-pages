Way too early 2026 draft prospects you guys are looking at for the Celtics?

Hey first post in this community.

The 2026 draft is almost an entire calendar year away but with this year most likely being about finding out what we have in the younger guys, I thought it’d be a fun experiment to see if you guys had any current prospects you hope are on the C’s radar and who might realistically end up as options next draft night.

Personally, I am hoping for a shot at Alex Condon F/C from Florida. Many expect him to be a first round level player and he’s a part of arguably the best frontcourt in college basketball with Thomas Haugh. I think he’s a good passer for a big man, mobile, plays inside and out for Florida. Having a stretch big is almost necessary in today’s game so that really intrigues me about him but he does have to show that he can be that kind of consistent threat night in and out. While not the biggest frame, he’s very solid at 6’11” - 225lbs and I don’t believe has much of an injury history which has been a notable problem for our Centers for a while now.

On the negative side, while he plays hard defense, I saw him get into a decent amount of foul trouble this year by falling for guard fakes or not having good hand discipline. Also, ball security while dribbling isn’t great, pretty common for most big men.

I’m expecting the Frontcourt to be the weakest part of the team next season and for us to be picking somewhere in the 14-20 range. If Condon were to fall here, I think he could be a worthwhile pick, though I know NBA teams tend to draft for BPA instead of need, so anything could happen. What do you guys think and do you have any players you’ll be watching out for this season?
