Now that the Druid is upon us… What’s up with claws?

Seeing the teaser for the upcoming Last Druid patch got me wondering again…

Claws don’t currently have a tab in the skill window, so I wonder if claws will be associated with Druid/Primal?

I mean I know Claws are DEX/INT in POE 1, but thematically (especially with shapeshifting) it won’t be the biggest reach to think Claws might become a STR/INT weapon associated with Druids. I’ve seen many games where Druid classes have claws as one of their weapons of choice. Although I will admit that STR is a bit of an odd attribute for a weapon like claws.

Has there been any official mention of what they’ll do with claws in POE2? I’ve always felt like in POE 1 (even though I like claws), they were the weapon type with the least “identity”. The almost never felt much different from daggers, and shared the exact same skill options.

Either way, I hope the lack of claws in the skill window does not mean claws are not planned for POE2. I think they can really do some cool stuff with them in POE2.
