Sekhemas doesn’t consider “damage taken from mana before life” from sources other than Mind over Matter

Super niche oversight but it’s a problem for me none the less.

If you have a mana stacker that takes mind over matter then your mana pool will be considered in your maximum honour calculation. But if you instead decide to stack other sources of % damage taken from mana before life, even up to 100%, your mana is not at all considered for your honour.

You shouldn’t even need to be at 100% in order to have it contribute to your honour. It should add in the amount of mana you would lose before you die. For example at 1k life and 9k mana. 90% should still give you your full mana bar as honour. If you had 50% then your honour should be 2k since you’d lose 1k mana before you die.

Would be nice if this was fixed. Have a hankering to run sekhemas with my mana stacker now that expedition is bricked and I can’t really do it.
