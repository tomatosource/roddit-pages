More campaign gear should come from boss fights and rares, not the vendor

Every leveling build guide reminds you to “check the vendors every level”

Why? Because 75% of your gear upgrades come form them, 20% from drops, with maybe 5% early crafting.

But…surely that ratio is backwards?

I mean, that’s the point of the game isn’t it? Kill bosses for loot.

Killing the campaign bosses should lead to a once-off first time loot that makes you smile. By making this the first-time kill only you avoid any early farming strat abuses (if that is even a concern).

Killing the Rust Monster, or Lachlan, and especially Geonor should be really exciting moments where you are anticipating some upgrades.

I just think it would be better from a gameplay POV to have the upgrades BE the gear that drops, not a means to BUY the gear, most of the time.

There are even a couple of locations that have an inventory full of rares!
