Mercenary lore

The crossbow is the best thing that’s ever happened to the game, but I’m disappointed by the mercenary’s character. He doesn’t feel cynical and greedy, but rather like Scrooge McDuck from the cartoon. He’s been given a literal RIVER OF GOLD (of which he can’t take even a small portion), but he still worries about every coin. He constantly wants to escape, but for some reason keeps getting into fights with semi-divine enemies. It’s stupid. Is it just me, or do others feel the same way?

I’m ready to buy a lore MTX where the mercenary is a reckless daredevil looking for a fight, or a cold-blooded war veteran seeking redemption, or a soldier confident in the perfection of his weapon and eager to test it, or anything else, as long as it has minimal logic.
