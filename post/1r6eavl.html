Crits and Coc are too OP (long post)

Not a new topic but it’s wild how many top-tier builds are CoC, especially comet.

I didn’t play CoC in endgame until this league in poe2, and I only recently began appreciating the positive feedback loop between average hit damage scaling and CoC.

Now that CoC gains energy based on % of enemy’s ailment threshold dealt by your crits, it creates an additional positive feedback loop (superlinear damage scaling) where bigger hits -&gt; more crits with bigger hits

So if you selfcast crit for 100 dmg, you’ll proc Coc every 5 hits, and that proc crit does ~100 dmg. But if you get your selfcast damage up to 1k, you proc CoC every single hit, and proc hit does ~1k dmg, an increase of 20x Coc dmg from 100 damage/5 hits to 1k damage/1 hit

Actually the positive feedback loop is even bigger than that because non-crits don’t energize CoC.

And this entire CoC positive feedback loop stacks with nearly every other single form of spell damage scaling: increased, more, cast speed, gained damage, spell levels.

The root issue seems to be that it’s difficult to creatively mix and match damage types when they all stack together multiplicatively, it means you should just get a balanced amount of all of them, including crit, including CoC.

My starter suggestion for how to increase endgame build diversity away from crits is to add a family of new support/lineage gems that greatly boost damage but prevent crits.

For example… some of these will be OP or trash, they are just concept support gems:

  • 100% more cast speed, can’t crit

  • 100% of damage taken by monsters from supported skills converted to damage over time over 2 seconds, can’t crit

  • arcane surge caused by this skill grants 200% more mana regen. can’t crit from any source during arcane surge

  • target can be affected by double the number of poisons, can’t crit

  • meta support gem: all other linked supports receive +50% increased effect, can’t crit

  • 200% more magnitude of damaging ailments caused by this skill, but crit bonus does not Contribute to ailment magnitude (so you could still technically crit but it doesn’t do extra damage for ailments)

… you get the idea. a family of support gems to increase endgame build diversity by opening new player power alternatives where crits are disabled.

tldr CoC is so fun but there’s more to poe2 life than CoC, or there should be
