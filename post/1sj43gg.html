Circle of Fortune style mode

I want to propose a dedicated mode for SSF that focuses on the quality and quantity of drops. Even with asynchronous trade, the constant need to price-check and browse markets is a massive burden for those who have no interest in trading.

Currently, rare drops feel unrewarding and the crafting system is clearly balanced around the assumption that players can just buy what they need. To me, the core appeal of an ARPG is the satisfaction of slaying monsters with gear you actually found or built yourself.

I’d love to see a system similar to Last Epoch’s Circle of Fortune integrated into SSF. Increasing drop rates in exchange for being permanently locked out of trade would make the self-found journey much more viable. Both trade-heavy and self-sufficient playstyles deserve to be rewarding. I know balancing this is difficult, but it’s a direction that would make the game much more enjoyable for a significant part of the community. However, it might not be very fun for SSF players who enjoy low drop rates…

In that case, it might be a good idea to create a new “NEW ssf” mode to distinguish it from the existing ssf.
