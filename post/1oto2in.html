[TheSteinLine] Daniel Gafford Would Receive Trade Interest From Around League

Center Daniel Gafford would receive trade interest from around the league should the Dallas Mavericks decide to move him, sources told Marc Stein of The Stein Line.

The Mavs have not yet entertained the idea of trading Gafford despite the team starting 3-7 on the year.

Gafford signed a three-year, $54 million deal this offseason. It is the max that the Mavs could have offered while keeping him trade eligible this year.
