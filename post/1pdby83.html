Can someone explain how Lou Whitaker isn’t just a HOF snub, but isn’t even making the committee ballot? Is there an off the field factor at play?

I don’t understand this at all. He’s one of the biggest HOF snubs of all time, and it’s odd he hasn’t made it considering Alan Trammell made it from the committee and their careers go hand in hand.

The last time Whitaker was on the committee ballot was 2019, when he got 6 of the 16 votes. He needed 12. Okay, 6 is a place to start. He did better than Bob Mattingly and Dale Murphy, who got less than 4 votes that year.

But he hasn’t been invited back since then, while Mattingly and Murphy have, multiple times! This year Carlos Delgado and made the ballot and Lou didn’t. No disrespect to Delgado, but there is no sense in this at all.

This all seems to be underreported. I understand that certain guys are snubbed from the Hall, but I don’t get how a guy like this can be snubbed from the ballot entirely, while:

  1. His career partner, whose name is synonymous with his, got in

  2. He showed progress the last time he was on the ballot

  3. Worse players (who did worse on the same past ballot) keep getting put on the ballot

Is there something I’m missing?
