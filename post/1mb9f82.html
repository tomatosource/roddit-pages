My Perspective After Engaging Heavily With Almost Every Mechanic

Still sane, Exiles? I might not be.

This is general specific feedback for GGG. I don’t know if they’ll see it but if they do, I have a pretty large sample size of engaging in mapping and pinnacle mechanics and want to share my experience.

I have a good build that can easily take on all content. I’m not even playing LS or any of the meta builds. Just a homebrew I cooked up myself. I am running 165% rarity on gear as well.

I know it’s early access. So please take this feedback as constructive from someone who LOVES your games. I am really looking forward to what you guys cook up!

Just as an overall feedback note: I am a working husband and father and I don’t have time nor do I want to have time to grind all day every day to get a little bit of decent loot here and there. I think most people share this sentiment. I don’t care that streamers who live on this game grind tons of loot. most people can’t and you should tailor your game to the working person who uses it as a fun past time.

Finally, I will not be comparing to POE1. It’s a different game and I like that. I like both games, but I don’t want this game to be a POE1 remake. I’ll just play POE1 if I want to play POE1.

MAPPING

After doing hundreds (maybe even thousands) of maps, I can safely say that mapping as a whole feel very unrewarding and leaning on unfun.

Without the tower setups, it feels like a slog. It kinda just feels like a character tester for me at this point. Engaging in mapping without tower juicing (alch and go) is bad.

Ok mapping feels bad if you arent using towers, cool, lets talk about towers. Towers take too long and are too tedious for the reward you gain from setting them up. Either increase the reward of setting up towers significantly, Allow for a larger radius and increased tablets/tablet effects, or just remove them entirely.

It’s a fun idea to setup your favorite mechanic and exciting to see all that quant/rarity on maps. Then receiving some bubble gum currency, high tier currency every once and awhile, and gear thats mediocre at best is demoralizing and makes me not want to engage with mapping at all.

SPECIFIC MECHANICS FEEDBACK

(Note: Trials of Choas is the only mechanic I have not played very heavily because I personally find it extremely boring)

  1. Trials of Sekhemas (9⁄10)
    • Ive done around 400 Trials and made around 2000 divines this league. This mechanic feels REALLY good to me. In general ToS is very rewarding, even without juicing relic quant to 100000%
    • You get useful jewels, some cool uniques, and some good chase items (vase/incense relics) to go after that make you want to keep running them.
    • The ATD jewels are really fun to go after because they can take any build to the next level. It feels well worth it to farm them. I was very excited when I got a jewel that was perfect for my build, and it really didn’t take a ton of farming to get it.
    • While this mechanic is a little time consuming, the only feedback I have for this is more chase uniques would be cool, and maybe cut down the size of some of the rooms and fix obvious buggy rooms as others have mentioned before im sure.
    • I know a lot of people really hate the ToS because of how hard they are for new players, so maybe reduce the difficulty a little for lower level trials so people arent looking to just get an ascendancy carry because they hate it.
    • Fun note: I actually got most of my raw divine drops this way (around 100).
  2. Breach 7⁄10
    • I’ve run hundreds of juicy breach maps and breachstones. This mechanic is really fun and can be quite rewarding, especially when juiced right with deli and quant/rarity. The breach rings are fun to craft and in general it’s just a fun mapping mechanic.
    • Expand on the content more. More bosses, more breach types, and please more chase items especially for Zesht. The amulets specifically arent that good and I end up vendoring them all. The amulets specifically just don’t compete at all with regular rare amulets. So pump those up a bit and maybe add some other item types/uniques.
    • Add some more catalysts. Rarity, spirit, all ele res, tainted catalysts for corrupted items, and other catalysts would be really fun to go after and i’d play the hell out of breach just to farm some of those.
  3. Expedition (6⁄10)
    • Ive run about 100 juicy expedition maps and around 50 logbooks. Expedition feels pretty good to me. The expedition currency farming for crafting and gambling is a lot of fun. The chests and monsters are pretty rewarding too. SUPER RIPPY if you arent careful though. Maybe a little TOO rippy, but that’s just me.
    • The gambling mechanic with the vendors is just bad though. I have cycled through their inventories over 1000 times and have yet to gamble ANY good items to make using the vendors worth it. It’s just bad in my experience.
    • Increase the number of crafts by at least 50%, and make getting decent modifiers a little easier and this will feel much better. Love the idea though.
    • Things I would add
      • When you go to gamble an item, it would be really fun if you could choose from a few different craft options varying in price depending on how good the modifier on the craft is. This would GREATLY incentivize farming Expedition currency.
      • Imagine you find a good base to gamble and you get to select from 3 different crafting options and you find that really good t1 modifier craft but it costs 100 artifacts. So, you leave it, go farm some currency for a while and come back to hit that juicy craft.
      • I think something like that would feel really fun and rewarding, id love to hear others thoughts on this too.
  4. Delirium/Simulacrum (4.5-5⁄10
    • Delirium is a fun mechanic in general I think, but its definitely got some things that make me not want to engage at all with it.
    • Delirium fog is bad. It looks terrible, you guys made a beautiful game and the delirium fog just ruins it man haha. Just allow us to toggle off that fog so we can appreciate the pretty colors.
    • I think instilling waystones is a good idea. I think it should stay, but just get rid of the fog. Also allow us to instill tablets for some extra layers. Also PLEASE add corruption emotions so we can instill amulets after corrupting.
    • The delirium map mechanic doesnt feel rewarding enough. Nuff said.
    • Simulacrum also needs a little buff to loot. Some more waves, bosses, and chase items as well would really round this mechanic out and be more fun to run.
    • I think thats all from me on that, if I missed anything or you guys have some more ideas I’d love to hear what you think.
  5. Ritual (3⁄10)
    • GGG, please please please increase omen drop chances. I want to engage in your crafting! its fun! But the omen drop chance is so abysmally low. Even after running around 50-100 juiced rituals and farming the King, I only got 2 whittlings. TWO! I think it’s fine if they are rare, but because they are sooo essential for crafting good items, they need to be more common. I think if you buffed the drop rate by another 6x, that might be the sweet spot.
    • The omens are so rare and you have to use so many of them to craft a good modifier that they MUST be more common
    • Ritual in general is fun, its just extremely unrewarding.
    • Overall; buff drops, add more chase items, add more ritual alter types, and just expand on the content a little more.
  6. Essence (2⁄10) Bad bad bad
    • The essences are too much of a gamble when crafting. FAR to random on the modifier pool to make grinding them even remotely worth it.
    • I’ve done over 100 juiced essence maps, I got maybe 50 greater and ZERO corrupted essences. Corrupted essences are far too rare.
    • Its fun breaking out a juiced monster to fight and getting a special crafting drop, it’s just not worth the squeeze.
    • Overhaul the essences to have tiers, so that they have a better chance to craft a good modifier the greater the essence is.
  7. Strongboxes (6-7⁄10)
    • Ive run around 50 juiced strongbox maps. Pretty good overall. Just add some more variety and increase chance for unique boxes as they are too rare.
    • Maybe add some boss boxes as well with special chase items. Make juicing them a little more impactful as well. They are almost there, but just need a little more love IMO.
  8. Wisps womp womp (2-3⁄10)
    • I’ve run around 75-100 juiced wisp maps with possessed bosses. Its a really fun idea. Juicing up a mob to drop unique socket currency and items, along with a buffed drop rate is fun.
    • HOWEVER, talismans are too rare to be worth running this in maps. Add a few more chase uniques. Special wisps (Hare/Fox) are a little too rare as well IMO.
    • Make the wisps possess monsters instantly or something like that please. Waiting for the monster to be possessed then getting killed and losing the map doesn’t feel good.
    • Again, a little more tender care to this mechanic and it will go hard.
  9. Rogue Exiles (0/10)
    • Ill keep this short. Fun idea. Too rare, too few, and needs more in the loot department. Never worth juicing in maps or picking in atlas tree. Don’t have too much specific feedback because everything about it needs improved.
  10. Bossing/Citadels (6-7⁄10
    • Done lots of boss maps because I love fighting the bosses in this game.
    • They should absolutely stay very challenging, but should be a little more rewarding for the risk involved. Bossing as its own mechanic should be just as rewarding if not more than running a specific map mechanic.
    • Generally they drop lots of high tier rares and thats good. More currency, socketables, uniques, etc should drop from them. This would make it feel GREAT.
    • Citadels are in the same boat. I don’t really have more to say on those as they feel fine otherwise to me.

OTHER FEEDBACK

  1. Atlas Tree
    • More points, expand the specific mechanic trees more to add more layers to juicing mechanics.
  2. Defences
    • This has been beat to death but make armor and life better defensive layers. Improve evasion to be less of a coin flip and more of a dice roll on impact.
  3. Fix server issues please
  4. TRADE
    • Please make an Auction house. You can keep the old trade system too, but there needs to be instant trade options that allow for trade while the players are offline.
    • But but….bots…china….meaningful trade. Blah blah blah. I don’t care. I just don’t. Other games have successfully implemented Auction houses. Don’t listen to the doomer players please. Most of us want this badly. It would be a HUGE improvement to the game.
    • Maybe add a gold cost for the auction house trading so bots are less of a problem.
