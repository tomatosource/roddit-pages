Killing fodder mobs shouldn’t be “Tactical”

I’m fine with bosses requiring some thought and action, in fact i LOVE it, poe2 is one of the greats when it comes to boss design no doubt, but the essence of an ARPG imo is the ability to steam roll through fodder without much a struggle till you reach the boss, resulting in a pleasing sequence. But in this game for some reason, i face too much resistance from the average white mob than any arpg out there, resulting in faster than usual mapping fatigue, which is almost directly ruining the whole experience for me.

Probably a hot take, but fodder kill time should be cut by 50% at least.

EDIT: THIS IS IN CONTEXT OF THE OVERALL VISION OF GGG FOR POE2 AS A WHOLE, NOT THE CURRENT LEAGUE. “you’re not playing the broken 1 button skills” ONLY PROVES MY POINT.
