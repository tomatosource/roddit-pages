[CHART] Not only has Lebron’s 3-point volume increased over the years - he’s shown a significant improvement in becoming an elite shooter

See chart here - https://imgur.com/a/eEiFInq

LeBron’s three-point shooting has come a long way over his career. Early on, he was a low-volume, low-percentage shooter, struggling to crack league average. But during his Miami years (2011-14), he took a big leap, with 2012-13 standing out as a peak. His return to Cleveland saw steady improvement in both volume and efficiency, but it’s with the Lakers that he’s taken the most threes of his career. While his efficiency has fluctuated, his recent seasons show he’s consistently above league average. The 2023-24 and 2024-25 seasons suggest he’s still evolving as a shooter, taking and making more threes than ever. At 40 years old, it’s wild to see him still expanding his game like this.
