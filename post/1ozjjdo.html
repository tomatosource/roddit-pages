In 1994 Kirby Puckett led the AL in RBI with 112, but only had 139 hits. Lowest amount of hits with 110+ RBI by a long shot, right?

What was his RISP 🤣, “only” 20 homers - meaning it wasn’t a crazy amount where he was racking up a ton of RBI from them unless most of them were multi base homers, even then he must’ve just been cashing in with RISP. He batted .317 but that RISP gotta be .360+.

For reference in 1986 he had 223 hits and 31 homers with just 96 RBI. 16 less RBI with 84 more hits and 11 more homers. Baseball stats are funny sometimes.
