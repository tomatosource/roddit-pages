Ethical Guide for Temple Farming

I kind of don’t want to write this because I’ve been making bank on Temple this league, but I’ve seen enough hate that I felt there needed to be a post defending my favorite mechanic. Also, I want to make clear - this is a guide on the intended way of playing it. No exploits, no weird snaking strat that gets messed up by a single misclick. I’m going to repeat this a bunch because it’s the main theme: Temple is meant to be easy come easy go. If you get a medallion, use it. If you mess up, oh well, the whole thing gets shaken up each run. Every strategy that has “broken” the Temple is because it pushes it away from this direction into stacking bonuses higher than they’re meant to be.

And up top I want to say that just because this explanation is long doesn’t mean the mechanic is hard to understand. The game teaches you everything, and the most intuitive way of playing (room go in highlighted spot) is generally the correct thing to do. If you’re intimidated by the wall of text below, just close the guide and go play. Or even better, keep ignoring Temple so that those of us who like it can keep profiting.

#TL;DR

First off, here’s the extremely short version if you’re just scared/overwhelmed by the grid and don’t want to mess it up: For tile placement, always start by putting down all your paths to get where you want to go (Atziri, Architect, corner room, whatever). Then put the other rooms. Sometimes the other rooms can bridge the final tile between your path and Atziri/Architect. T3 rooms are much better than T2 rooms.

Each Aztiri kill gives 6 passive points, so do that at first (and you’ll also learn the mechanics). Focus on Temple tablets and you can usually do a Temple run every other map at least.

As an overarching strategy, everything in the Temple is easy come, easy go. You don’t have a ton of control over room types, so it’s not worth agonizing over, and yes your best rooms will get deleted, that’s just how it goes sometimes. It’s messy on purpose, so just embrace it. Slam your medallions, put rooms in the highlighted green spots, and then kill the monsters.

Also, some of the info on the poe wiki is a bit oudated (still mentions snaking) but for the most part, for specific questions, just check the wiki or poe2db.

#Room Placement: The “auto-connect” system explained:

The most confusing part of Temple is when you go to place a room and the game won’t let you, or you’re placing them and the game “randomly” puts an extra connection between it and the adjacent room. There is a method to the madness! Think of the rooms as having three tiers: Paths, Content, and Restricted. There’s an “auto-connect” mechanic where rooms will form connections when placed next to each other, and it makes a lot more sense when you think of them in these tiers because higher tier rooms will always auto-connect to lower tier rooms.

  • Paths: These connect to each other exactly as you’d expect. If the game was only placing paths, it’d be completely intuitive. Because they’re the only room with multiple connections, you need to use these to build the backbone of your structure. I’ve found the most success with a “central highway”. Important: Everything else auto-connects to Paths; the orientation of Path tiles only matters for placing more Paths. Think about it - no other type of tile even has an orientation.

  • Content rooms are the other main type of tile you place. They only auto-connect, as they have no set openings. However, they have rules on what I’ll call a “valid tile” to auto-connect to. All Path tiles are valid, as is any type of other Content that they synergize with (highlighted bright green when you go to place). You can chain together several rooms in this manner without Paths getting involved at all, which makes for very dense rewards.

  • Restricted rooms are the tiles you place from the Architect’s chambers after defeating them. I include Atziri’s Chambers in this tier as well. These will auto-connect to anything that gets placed near them. Think of them as destination tiles, or if they were paths they’d always be the 4-way crossroad path, it’s just hidden.

So, a quick tip is to place your paths to build a long highway to get to your destination, but if you’re a single tile short of your goal, you can use any of the Content rooms to bridge the gap. The Restricted rooms will always auto-connect, and the content room will connect to any side of the path tile.

Room synergies:

  • The most valuable rooms depend on the economy, but at the time of writing, most of the Temple Currencies are &gt;80 ex and keeping pace with inflation, so these are pretty easy wins to go for. You also don’t have a ton of control over your room choices (you place all 6 each time), so the easy strategy is to just work on getting as many T3 currency generating rooms as possible.

  • There are no bad rooms. Ones that don’t directly give currency can still drop medallions, or they give bigger boosts to rarity/quantity in the Temple. You always get to place all 6 of your rooms each time, so you might as well run them.

  • Because Content rooms chain off of each other when they synergize, and because T3 is worth much much more than T2 for the valuable rooms types, the game encourages you to build clusters of these content nodes as branches off of a main path.

  • If you’ve got a basically clean slate with just a blank straight path down the middle and rooms that don’t synergize, place them off the main path with two spaces between them, that way you can freely place neighbors that upgrade them.

  • T4 rooms aren’t that valuable most of the time. Rooms that give specific currency items won’t get extra of those items for being T4, not that I’ve found anyway, so compared to T3 rooms they’re pretty much just stat boosts, while T3 vs. T2 is usually a bigger step up (a currency item, for example). In general I don’t make a lot of T4 rooms. Still, if you’ve got the option to make one of these, check the actual stat the room provides.

  • Generators are weird, but just know that they boost some additional rooms when you place them along a longer path. They “power” a portion of the path and certain rooms benefit from this.

  • Barracks are another weird room type where they actually have 3 variants: Vanilla, Transcendent (synthflesh lab), and Legion (spymaster). If you see your barracks change color but not upgrade to another tier level, that’s what’s happening.

  • Spymasters boost each other without being connected to each other.

  • Sealed Vaults: If I’ve got a large Temple and I get to place a Vault, I tend to not open the Vault and just keep the rarity boost. You risk the Vault getting destabilized without opening it, but the stat boost is more valuable. Note as well that the description of the room says it has content based on the neighboring rooms, so even though it doesn’t get a highlighted adjacency bonus, it may still be worth considering adjacency when placing.

Macro strategy Tips:

  • There are three general ways to make money in Temple: 1. Kill Atziri ASAP, she drops the most loot. 2. Place rooms that drop currency items at T3, the currency items themselves are valuable. 3. Maximize your stat boosts across the Temple as a whole and farm it for big drops. The only tradeoff between these is that killing Atziri destabilizes more rooms than normal, which is counterproductive if you’re investing in placing “good” rooms for one of the other purposes.

  • I would recommend starting by killing Atziri. Not only does she give passive points, but it genuinely is profitable. Even if you’re doing other strategies, I’d say it’s still worth pathing towards this to at least give yourself the option of killing her when you want or your best rooms get destabilized. If you’ve got a very profitable temple setup, still keep in mind that the single most profitable room is always Atziri’s, so if you’ve got a ton of great rooms that are boosting drops, it’s also going to be boosting Atziri’s loot.

  • Medallions are plentiful, use them or lose them. I will generally only consider sitting on one or two of them between runs, otherwise everything must go. They all start with a different letter, so I just call them that letter in my head. The most valuable ones are the ones that upgrade room tier (Q), lock them from destabilization (J), and re-roll available rooms cards (P). It’s not worth agonizing over room types to angle for specific medallions.

  • Don’t sit on the one that adds map mods (Z) or the one that lets you place an extra Restricted Room after defeating the Architect (E). The latter works by just throwing it on the Architect and triggers the next time you beat him.

Last Tips/FAQs:

  • The Temple is pretty forgiving when it comes to death, but just like in maps challenging either Atziri or the Architect will cause the area to lose all additional content. Basically, always do these two rooms last, and maybe skip the Architect if you’re planning to run Aztiri in the same run.

  • For the Atlas, the Temple area is all City maps, so they benefit from the best Atlas bonuses like the extra tablet slot.

  • As far as tablets go, the best suffix you can get is the one that adds chance for an extra crystal (currently &gt;1div for that modifier), unless there’s some strategy around the getting rare chests that I’m just not aware of.

  • Area level of the temple apparently factors in area level of the maps used to generate crystals (from the patch notes). So an irradiated tablet or running T16s may be useful.

  • The Temple passive tree is pretty straightforward. I would recommend grabbing “Offerings to the Queen” early for a chance for big currency items during mapping, and also Stolen Authority for more medallions. The others I hope you can evaluate on your own, but IMO they’re all good. It’s worth mentioning that the terraforming room will limit some of your lineage support gem drops I guess, at least based on this post.
