Other unbeatable LeBron records: Player of the month and Player of the week

Hi,

It’s pretty much impossible for any current player to catch either of those records. LeBron has won 40 player of the month awards and 68 PotW awards.

Note: It’s impossible to win PotM or PotW in both conferences, so the following data is based on what’s actually achievable - winning these awards in the conference LeBron was playing for. So when I say there were 24 PotWs for LeBron to win, there were 48 to win if you include the other conference.

An NBA RS is usually 24 weeks, meaning LeBron was the player of the week for a rounded 3 seasons. An NBA RS is 6 months, so LeBron was the player of the month for a rounded 7 whole seasons.

To put into perspective how insane and unreachable this is;

Next up in the PotW all-time category are Kobe and KD, having achieved this feat 33 and 32 times respectively. Take both of them together and Bron still has won the award more times than 2 Top15 players with long careers and overall good longevity. Players like MJ, Steph, Giannis, Shaq, Duncan, or Harden are in the low to mid 20s.

PotM is even crazier imo. Kobe and Jordan are next up at 17 and 16 respectively, making both combined still 7 whole awards short of reaching LeBron. I was too lazy to count each PotM individually, but let’s just say there’ve been 6 PotM per season since 1980 when the award was first introduced, leaving us with ~44 seasons or 264 PotM all time. This means that LeBron won 15% of the entirety of PotM awards. In 21 full seasons played, LeBron won 32% !!! Of the available PotMs during his active career. Seriously wtf?!

Another crazy stat: from 2009 to 2018 Bron won the PotM 52% of the time over a decade of NBA Basketball (31⁄60). It’s honestly an unparalleled prime combined with an even more unparalleled longevity channeled into a single NBA player. This also means that Bron achieved almost twice as many PotMs as Kobe or MJ, and more than twice as many as KD (15) in less than half of Brons career!

From my research I concluded that it’s not too hard to win PotM consistently if we’re talking about a Superstar at their peak - which is why KD, Kobe and Harden racked up a lot of them over a 5 year span or so. With Bron the difference is he’s been getting them for the entirety of his numerous primes and the only player comparable to that would probably be MJ, who’s been getting the award on a great pace for a while as well. But when it comes to superstar longevity and health, it’s just no contest really. They’re not the most coveted awards but they paint a really good picture of how stupidly long LBJ has been the best or among the very best players in the league.

Note that the non rounded numbers favor LeBron a bit more since I assumed full seasons every year rather than check for shortened lockout seasons, so all these %s are a bit higher in reality.
