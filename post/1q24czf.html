I’m on my first PoE2 character (last played PoE 1 around 10 years ago). I’m playing freezing werewolf and finding it pretty fun so far. My elemental resists are around 50 each (seems like enough so far with all the freezing I do) and dps feels decent enough. Chaos resist seems pretty damn rare right now, but haven’t felt like it’s needed so far.

I have tried crafting and sometimes have a little success, but I feel like my best items have come from the Vaal Temple crafting bench upgrades. The benches make me reluctant to use much currency. I’ve also tried gambling with very little success, but maybe I just need to do it more. I kind of just gamble for one piece at a time, and usually just end up thinking I should have saved that gold for end game gambling.

I only have around 35 Regals right now (with a fair amount of disenchanting) and even less Exalted. I recently started looking at prices on the trade site and I can get a better weapon for 1x Exalted than what I’m likely to craft with Augmentation, Regal, and 2x Exalts. I haven’t traded for anything yet, but it seems to me that it’s both the least time consuming and cheapest way to upgrade?
