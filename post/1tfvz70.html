In every decade thus far of the 21st century, every year ending in a 5 or 6 has had a Western Conference point guard whose name starts with S win back-to-back MVPs.

In addition to the stat above, in all three cases thus far said MVP made back-to-back Conference Finals appearances, as well as secure the best overall record in the league in their first MVP run.

Steve Nash won MVP in 2005 and 2006, with the Suns having had the best overall record in 2005.

Stephen Curry won MVP in 2015 and 2016, with the Warriors having had the best overall record in 2015 (and then broke the best ever regular season record in 2016).

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander won MVP in 2025 and 2026, with the Thunder having had the best overall record in 2025 (and also in 2026).
