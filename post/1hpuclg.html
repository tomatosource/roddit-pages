The Case on why Tyler Herro should be an All-Star this season

This season, Tyler Herro has been putting up career highs in every category. Not only has his role on the team expanded in the scoring and playmaking department, but he has also became more concise and efficient in just about everything. In years past, Tyler Herro was a microwave scorer who would go 1⁄7 throughout 3 quarters, but score 10-15pts in a quarter. It is why he won 6MOTY in 2021-2022 and has been a valuable piece for the Heat.

However, this season, Tyler Herro has completely changed everything with his game. He has nearly completely removed the mid range shot from his game, now making up less than 10% of his total shots. Nearly all his shots now come from the 3Pt Line and the Paint area, where he is also having career highs in efficiency. As a result, his splits are 47/41/86. His TS% is also a career best 62%, above many notable guards in the league.

Tyler Herro has also expanded his range, now shooting deep 3s from way behind the line at a relatively high clip and efficiency. In the Bam Adebayo dribble handoff plays, whenever a player goes under the screen, he steps back and shoots a deep 3. As a result, teams are forced to play higher on him, allowing him to drive into the paint for his highest paint scoring clip in his career.

When you watch Tyler Herro play, you can just see how teams are reacting to his new and improved game. With the rest of the Miami Heat might as well being a black hole on offense (the second best player, Bam, is averaging 15 while Butler is injured), teams are doubling and even tripling teaming Tyler Herro off the jump.

However, perhaps the most compelling argument for his case to become an all star and most improved player is how he compares against his peers thought to be a tier ahead of him. So far into the year, his splits are better than the likes of Devin Booker, Anthony Edwards, Tyrese Maxey, De’Aaron Fox, and Donovan Mitchell.

The big argument against Tyler Herro making the 2024-2025 All Star Team is how stacked the guards are in the east. Big names like Trae Young, Donovan Mitchell, Jalen Brunson, LaMelo Ball, Tyrese Maxey, and Damian Lillard are likely to compete for all star selections. Several of these players have had significantly more team success, which may play a factor in voting when it comes down to it. However, looking at the raw numbers and advanced stats, Tyler Herro might be near the best of the bunch in the batch.

With all the players that realistically might make the all star team listed, I believe LaMelo will miss it based off missing too many games. Tyler Herro has been objectively better than the likes of Tyrese Maxey and arguably Donovon Mitchell, while being on par with the likes of Jalen Brunson and Damian Lillard.

If All Star selections were made today, I believe Tyler Herro would make it in over LaMelo Ball, Darius Garland, Damian Lillard, and Tyrese Maxey. I think the selection made would be Jalen Brunson and Donovon Mitchell starting based off team success, with Cade Cunningham Tyler Herro and Trae Young off the bench. His impact, stats, and advanced stats bring him high enough to compete with even the best guards in the east, and should be rewarded for his play.
