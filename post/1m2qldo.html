Not for Casual Gamers

Hey guys. I’ve played Seasons 1 and 2 now as a casual dad gamer, only playing 1-2 hours a day at most. Playing on PS5 Pro. Here’s some things they could do/add to help us casual gamers and open the game up to more people:

  1. Add an in-game auction house or trading function. Us console players can’t even trade now (well we can but it’s not good to do that with a controller).

  2. Make crafting stuff way better and worth while. It’s almost worthless to craft anything now unless you are a hardcore gamer. The average player will never be able to craft anything worthwhile…just the super rich ones.

  3. Make those trials waaaaaaaaaaaay waaaaaay waaay more doable for the average person. As it is now no casual player will ever be able to complete them unless they get a hardcore player to do it. Make the boss fights easier and more forgiving. In 2 seasons I can only get 4 ascend points. It takes like 30 mins to even try then u die once and it’s over…very bad decision, will lose tons of potential players alone by not changing this. Don’t understand the logic here.

  4. Make campaign skippable if already completed, like Diablo 4 does. I can barely get into T15 maps each season. The campaign takes a really long time…it’s fun the first time but not the second. Let us skip it.

  5. The unique items are all garbage. I’ve collected over 100 and have never even used one. Either just get rid of them completely or make them all useable. This will require them to redo all unique items to make them all on the same level. All uniques need to be considered using not just insta trash.

That’s all for now lol. I hope they actually change a few of these so the game can be opened up to more people.
