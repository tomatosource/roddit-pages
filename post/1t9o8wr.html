Are unique specific skills good design?

I think the idea is fun as the exception to the rule, but they’ve gone hard with new uniques that have specific skills in the announcement video.

To me it seems like a path of further reduced skill flexibility. Like we already have the more restrictive attitude towards skills and weapon types, and this just goes a step further and allows ggg to not only control the weapon type but also the stats. Wouldn’t it be cooler to have these skills as just generic skills that we can choose which items to use with it? Maybe the unique they go with is best in slot for them still but this design means the door isn’t even open to try some janky alternative.
