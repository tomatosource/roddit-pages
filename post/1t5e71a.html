3 Paths for this Offseason

There are 3 paths facing the Celtics this offseason after a disappointing first round exit. Run it back and make 1 - 2 more attempts with the championship core (Brown, Tatum, White, Prichard, Hauser, Queta), trade JB and assets for the one big name on the market (Freak), or, combine White and assets for a moderate upgrade.

A lot of this is going to come out as word salad, but I love using this space to organize my thoughts and explore options.

And just to be clear, im not advocating for any one scenario, im just workshopping what each would look like.

&nbsp;

Scenario 1 - Trade JB

I had a much longer version of this post typed out but I’m practicing being concise.That being said, I love JB, and think he’s an amazing person on and off the court. I would be sad to see him go.

Quickly looking across the league, the only real “Upgrade” the Celtics could trade for is Giannis. There are other guys like Donovan Mitchell, Bam Adebayo, Kevin Durant, Kawhi Leonard, or Anthony Davis, who are feasible but don’t really upgrade the team past Brown.

A trade for Giannis would also need to involve a 3rd team. It’s doubtful Milwaukee would be interested in trading a disgruntled Giannis for a disgruntled Brown. One potential partner would be San Antonio. I won’t map out the entire trade but the bones would be Dylan Harper and picks from both teams to Milwaukee, Brown to San Antonio, Giannis to Boston. A million other pieces could move around here but that would be the bones of it.

Looking at fit in Boston, it would pretty much be solid. Giannis, when healthy, has some of the biggest gravity in the league. He sucks defenders in and opens up the 3. Boston has the shooting to capitalize on that.

Lineup after JB trade -

White - Pritchard

Hauser - Sheierman - Harper

Tatum - Gonzalez

Antetokoumpo - Walsh

Queta - Garza - A. Williams

MLE FA and pick #40

Scenario 2 - Derrick White + Misc -

We can combine White and Hauser to get us to 41.48m. This puts us in the area of Kevin Durant, OG Anunoby, Michael Porter Jr, Kyrie Irving, and then a bunch of guys I don’t like even more than Kyrie Irving. White + a small contract like Scheierman/Gonzalez/Garza puts us at about 34m, which would be in the Mikal Bridges, Julius Randle, Tyler Herro range. This is a bunch of high paid severely flawed players I’m not super into.

Again I had a longer version of this typed out, but for your sake I’m going to highlight my thoughts.

Of the options, I feel like Kevin Durant would be the only significant upgrade to the roster. Houston’s biggest issues are shooting, lack of PG depth, and Kevin Durant being a toxic asshole. They essentially solve all 3 issues by shipping KD out for one of the best defensive players in the league, and both players are shooters which Houston desperately needs. Houston gets to rebalance their roster and lean into Sengun and Thompson who they hope will blossom offensively. Again, I won’t go into the finer details of what a trade for KD would look like. I would say, I’m sure Houston is eager to move on after some of the behind the scenes toxicity. Durant is also 38 years old. I have no idea what the trade would look like and won’t bother trying to construct a whole trade.

KD has been super healthy these last 3 seasons but the results have been meh. He actually played 78 games last season and got injured going into the post season. The big question is, can KD accept being a super 3rd option? Because of the age and experience of this Houston team, KD took on the #1 scoring option by default. He may be more willing to take on that 3rd option role with Tatum and Brown being right in their primes.

Roster after KD trade -

Pritchard

Brown - Scheierman - Harper

Kevin Durant - Gonzalez

Tatum - Walsh

Queta - Garza - A. Williams

MLE FA, pick #40

Scenario 3 - RUN IT BACK

The 3rd and final option, which is the most likely (and I’m not even mad at that) is we run it back, add somebody with the MLE, make our draft picks, and see where we are after 2026.

One fun note about the salary cap, a bunch of teams are about to need to desperately shed salary, mainly the Knicks, Cavs, and Magic in the east, and the Kings (lol), Nuggets, and Thunder out West. Within the next few seasons all these teams will need to make some Holiday/Porzingis/Horford level decisions.

The MLE is right around 15m for next season, which the Celtics will have access to. A bunch of teams are suddenly not going to be free agent buyers, and some guys who are looking to sign with contenders may jump on the 15m MLE.

I also want to be clear, NOBODY here is the answer to Embiid. The answer to Embiid is to hit your shots. There isn’t an MLE C thats going to lock down Embiid. If we run into the 76ers in the playoffs again, you need to outscore and outperform Embiid as a team, there is no 1 to 1 solution.

Here are a few FA’s who could be available for the 15m MLE -

Nikola Vucevic - C

Kristaps Porzingis - C

Kobe White - G

Benedict Mathruin - F

Anfernee Simons? (Unsure if we can re-sign) - G

Tobias Harris - F

Collin Sexton - G

CJ McCollum - G

DeAndre Ayton - C

Kelly Oubre - F

John Collins - PF

Let if be known, this isn’t an all inclusive list. This is a list of guys who I THINK might actually bite for 15m, and who I THINK would be a reasonable fit.

Boston also owns the #27 and #40 picks in the draft, and have proven they’re a team where going as an UDFA could get them to the main roster. With the 27th pick there are guys I like who are either big wings, or are 4’s who can flex to 5’s who should be available. Allen Graves, Morez Johnson, or Koa Peat could be available. With the 40th pick there are projected to be shooters and PG’s available. Guys like Alex Karaban, Tyler Tanner, and Ebuka Okorie could be available.

Roster after RUN IT BACK scenario -

White - Pritchard - Tyler Tanner

Brown - Scheierman - Harper

Tobias Harris - Hauser - Gonzalez

Tatum - Walsh - Morez Johnson

Queta - Garza - A. Williams
