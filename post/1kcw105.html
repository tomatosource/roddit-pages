The delirium fog aesthetic needs to go.

I know they tried to make it less bad this league. It didn’t work. It’s still terrible. It would be cool if it were an occasional mechanic that happens in a map every once in a while. But the fact that every map you do is going to be the same ugly washed out black and white with the same annoying music once you’re juicing maps is a tragedy. They made all of these beautiful varied environments with their own music and ambience, and they just completely destroy it all with this one mechanic. I don’t care about the lore or the feel they’re trying to go for with delirium. This is unacceptable. It’s a disservice even to themselves and the work their team has put into making the game.
