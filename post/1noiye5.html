The #1 What If

What if we reach the following benchmarks?

  • Top 4 seed
  • Jaylen averages at least 28/6/6
  • Jaylen efficiency is at least 48/37/80
  • Jaylen is getting top 5 MVP votes

If we reach these 4 benchmarks what does it actually change anything about our team?

  • Tatum will still come back and we will still run the offense through him?
  • Will Jaylen become too valuable to keep as a #2 option?
    • Parked on the wing while Tatum runs PNR
  • Do we make Jaylen the #1 option for a few years while Tatum slowly retakes it? Will that create more problems are we back in 2019 deciding who’s the top dog again?

So what does Jaylen balling out actually accomplish for us this year?

I want all 4 of the benchmarks to be hit, but Im not sure that if that happens that will somehow change anything about us going forwards.

Other, less important benchmarks, seemingly would have greater effect on our future. Like a younger player breaking out.

The #1 What If is Can Jaylen be a #1 Option, lead a team deep in the playoffs, be an MVP candidate.

If that happens what would you do?
