Bill Simmons’ list of ~ 16 things that have made this one of the wildest NBA seasons in recent history.

  1. San Antonio went from 34 wins to one of the title favorites.

  2. Wemby became arguably the face of the league and the hottest ticket to see

  3. Bam Adebayo had an 83 point game, surpassing Kobe

  4. The Clippers had an aspiration scandal, started out 6-19, traded James Harden, and are now making a playoff push. And the scandal has not been resolved. They also waived Chris Paul.

  5. Charlotte has had the best net rating for 40 games (almost half the season) after going 4-14

  6. Kevin Durant had a crazy burner scandal at the All-Star game, and the team has not been the same since.

  7. Jason Tatum blew out his Achilles in May. This was supposed to be a Celtics gap year, they are #2 in the East, he’s already back and just had 32 points.

  8. The Lakers were an absolute mess and now look like contenders.

  9. After Stephen A. Smith called Mat Ishbia the worst owner in the league for their trades, the Suns are going to be in the playoffs.

  10. OKC became the villains of the league.

  11. The Wizards have Trae Yound and Anthony Davis

  12. We’ve got an expansion plan out of nowhere including the return of the SuperSonics.

  13. Everyone has turned on “Inside The NBA”, a show that we all loved.

  14. Zion Williamson now plays in every game.

  15. The Giannis saga.

  16. Ja Morant is the last guy left in Memphis except everyone has basically forgotten that he is even in the league.

  17. The Nets had 5 first round picks and not one of them will make an all-rookie team.

What else do you guys think should be on the list?
