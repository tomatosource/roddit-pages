13 year old Mohamed Dabone is dominating youth leagues in Europe at a level rarely seen before for his age, is drawing comparisons to Giannis and Wemby, and might be the next generational prospect…if he’s actually 13

First i’ll let you watch some highlights of Dabone to get yourself familiar with him: https://streamable.com/l1p8se

The 13 year old Burkino Faso born prospect measures 6’10 with a 7’4+ wingspan and shows incredible athleticism for a literal child. His speed, quickness, vertical burst, and fluidity at that age is incredible to see. And it’s not just physical attributes, he shows top tier awareness, making timely defensive rotations and cuts that most high school aged prospects don’t make. He has a soft touch inside the paint, and is making good strides with his jump shot. He has a combination of size and skill that has never been seen before at that age. And it has not just been hypothetical, he has been producing. Here he is dropping 21 points in his Adidas Next Gen U18 debut, at the age of TWELVE. He also won Finals MVP of the U16 La Orotava tournament when he was 12, dropping 31 points and 19 rebounds in the title clinching game. I just want to re-iterate one more time, TWELVE years old.

People who scout U17 talent are calling him one of the best prospects ever at that age group. It cannot be understated how unprecedented it is for a 13 year old to be as developed, skilled, and productive he is. NBA Draft Room’s early scouting report goes as far as to say “if he was indeed born in 2011 he’s the most advanced prospect I’ve ever seen for his age”.

And that’s the thing. IF he was indeed born in 2011. This has unfortunately been a conversation surrounding Dabone since he burst onto the scene in 2023. The birthdate listed for Dabone on the official EuroLeague website is Oct 21st, 2011. But on the website for FIBA 3x3, Dabone is listed as 17 years old currently (no birthdate given). But, this website looks poorly put together, and even gets his nationality incorrect, listing it as Togo instead of Burkina Faso. And unfortunately, there’s the traditionally held bias against African-born prospects when it comes to believing their ages. Serge Ibaka has spoken about this at length in the past. Dabone was born in Burkina Faso in a time of political instability, which has given people another reason to doubt his age. And finally, just watching Dabone play, it is hard to believe someone so athletic and fluid can be 13. Kids his age are usually awkward and clumsy and growing into their bodies, while Dabone plays like he could walk into a pro league tomorrow.

Regardless, if Dabone is 17, he would still be a good prospect, but not the Wembenyama level prospect some are thinking he is. But at 13? He is something we’ve never seen before. We don’t know what his real age is, but personally, I will choose to believe the 2011 date until proven otherwise.

Dabone is currently eligible for the 2030 NBA Draft, so it’ll be a long time before we get to see him in the NBA. But I thought this was an interesting international story that was unfolding and probably won’t be mainstream for another few years.
