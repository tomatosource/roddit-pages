What bugs me most about the Brown trade

Yes, the trade sucks for a lot of reasons. I loved Jaylen Brown. Watching him evolve over the last decade, seeing how he embraced Boston, and watching him and Tatum finally get over the hump and win a championship together was an incredible ride. It just had a really lousy ending. The lack of explanation from the organization is baffling. It makes no sense that they allowed Jaylen to be the first person to publicly address the trade, and on his own stream no less.

What really pisses me off, though, is the timing of this trade. From everything we can tell, this came down to the organization’s belief that you can’t pay both Brown and Tatum and still remain a legitimate contender. If that was truly the prevailing belief, then why not make this move last year? Make 2025 a bridge year, take your lumps, and position yourself for an incredibly valuable pick in what was supposed to be a deep draft. Financially, nothing fundamental has changed over the last year. If they already knew they weren’t going to keep Brown long-term, or believed his contract would eventually become an albatross, then move on from it sooner. That strategy would have made far more sense than whatever this is.

I still hope there’s another shoe to drop, but at this point that probably feels more like coping than anything else. This appears to be the team they’re taking into 2026-27, and I’ve come to accept that.
