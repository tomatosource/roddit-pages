Blame this trade on Adam Silver and the CBA

My evidence is as follows.

To this point, Brad Stephens has been a good GM. He drafts well. He usually trades well. He is good at finding underused talent like White, Garza, Harper, and Queta. What are the chances he suddenly because as stupid as this trade makes him look?

Most of the best teams in the playoffs had one star player with deep teams. The Knicks, Spurs, and OKC had deep teams. Only the Knicks have two stars but neither is a supermax and one took less money. The Knicks are deep and they have two stars. That’s not likely to happen often and it will be very difficult to match.

Playoff teams with two stars Celtics, Cavs, Lakers, and Nuggets are all struggling to improve year to year. The Celtics and Nuggets lost in the playoffs in upsets. It’s a small sample size but that’s 50%.

The Celtics had the best duo in the NBA. That used to be the golden ticket for championships. However, after the new CBA, it doesn’t work that way. Depth is becoming important.

The market for Jaylen was soft. I think this is because GMs with playoff aspirations have reached the same conclusion as Brad. Two max contract stars (not to mention two supermax contract stars) destroys team flexibility. Detroit with Cunningham and Brown would run into the same salary cap restrictions as Brown and Tatum. Any team with a star would struggle finding depth and flexibility if they added Brown.

So, despite the soft market due to the overly punitive CBA, Brad had to let one of the Celtics stars go. He had to trade for pennies on the dollar. I think the Celtics thought this trade was time sensitive. Eventually this new understanding for building winning teams under this CBA would be common knowledge and the market would get softer.

I think we all kind of new the CBA destroyed the Celtics championship window. It was such a big change, teams would have to be built differently to win. The Celtics were built to win under the old rules with the softer cap. We hoped Brad would figure it out anyway. He may have figured it out but it’s a tough pill to swallow.

I was so angry when I heard about this trade.

I am sure a lot of people feel similarly. We should be angry. Teams should be able to keep their best players and contend for championships. It’s a joy to watch players go from rookies to champions. We get attached to these players and they get attached to us. The NBA forced the Celtics to trade one of their best players. We are rightfully furious. They took Jaylen away from us.
