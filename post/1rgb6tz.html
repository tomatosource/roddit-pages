Baseball WBC vs. FIFA World Cup Parallels

England (soccer) = Venezuela (baseball). Always have extremely stacked team, fall embarrassingly short of expectations.

Brazil (soccer) = USA (baseball). Almost always the most talented team on paper, always a heavy favorite, almost never reaches its full potential.

France (soccer) = Japan (baseball). The most consistent team, always a top contender, consistently performs well with rare exceptions.

Germany (soccer) = DR (baseball). Always scary, always top contender, mostly peforms well with some hiccups here and there.

Belgium (soccer) = PR (baseball). Tiny nation with a couple of great talents that can make a run deep into the tournament.

Croatia (soccer) = Mexico (baseball). Overlooked underdog that has surprised people by making deep runs lately.
