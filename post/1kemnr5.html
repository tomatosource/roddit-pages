Teams that won a 7-game series are 3-17 in their next series when they play against a team that swept in the previous round (since 1970).

The only teams since 1970 that did win a series when they were this rest-disadvantaged?

2013 Miami Heat over San Antonio Spurs in the Finals.

1998 Chicago Bulls over Utah Jazz in the Finals

2009 Orlando Magic over Cleveland Cavaliers in the Conference Finals

Oddly enough, all 3 contained either Jordan or LeBron. 2 of these (Miami and Chicago) had the better team winning the series, so there has only been one case since 1970 of the underdogs winning a series while this rest-disadvantaged.

The team that played the 7-game series on average wins 1.35 games in this type of series. Most of this is likely due to the sweeping team usually being a better team.
