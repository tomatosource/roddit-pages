Guys, relax

People are treating this loss like it’s the end of the world. Yeah losing sucks. Losing to the Knicks and their rabid, obnoxious, egomaniac fans makes it suck more. But there’s still takeaways from this game:

A loss can be expected against a good team after we’ve been on a hot streak. Don’t let a rough game distract you from the fact that the league is on notice now that we’re fully healthy again.

They can gloat over this win all they want, but a two possession win with a rough shooting night from JT and with JB resting isn’t something they should be comfortable with, because we know that’s how we’d feel if it were the other way around.

A rematch would fare better for the Celtics this year because they don’t have the pressure of winning back to back. The Knicks may be the media darling, but they should feel pressure because their boogeyman isn’t going to be in their way this time.

The Jays have eliminated Prime Giannis, the Nets superteam, and the motherfucking Heat after being eliminated by them in years before. They’re not going to let the Knicks take them out two years in a row.

Take all this in and go to bed. Still a lot of basketball to be played. Go Cs
