SSF saved the game for me

Been a long time player of PoE 1 and 2 but in my case it’s always been about blindly following build guides. I did that to avoid soft-bricking my characters which may happen if you don’t know what you’re doing (I didn’t) and out of laziness.

Around a week ago I rolled my first SSF character and it’s been an amazing journey. Yes, I did follow a build guide but quite early on I had to start improvising and working around the limitations of my gear. Learned a lot about skills, supports and interactions.
Each gear upgrade felt great, each respec resulting in a smoother experience was very satisfying. Trade trivializes so many aspects of the game I wasn’t even aware.

And in my case SSF removed another factor that put me off - the minmax and wealth race. I don’t feel like I’m lagging behind anyone any more.

I’m sure that with the right mindset you can have similar experience in trade league but maybe there are people like me with performance anxiety. If that’s you - try SSF, it might boost your fun with the game like it did for me.
