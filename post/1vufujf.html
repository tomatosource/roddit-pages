60+ Druid MTX escape the mystery box

The waiting period is over. GGG moved more than 60 Last of the Druids cosmetics into direct sale on August 16, including armour sets, portals, level-up effects and other items usable in both games. Players can now choose individual cosmetics without rolling the box. The community question is whether guaranteed access changes what people buy, or merely when they buy it. Official announcement

ExileCon Qualifier #3 brings a free Coliseum drop

Qualifier #3 ran Thursday, with Jiluo and Giuygiuyg already confirmed for the November finale. Watching 30 minutes of the official broadcast or any eligible Path of Exile 2 Twitch stream unlocks the Coliseum Helmet Attachment. Progress remains available until Friday, August 21 at 2:00 p.m. PDT. Qualifier and drop details

Wraeclast’s artists enter the spotlight

GGG published its first collection of standout entries from the current Path of Exile fan-art competition. Entrants have until September 2 at 10:00 p.m. PDT, must submit work-in-progress evidence and cannot enter AI-generated artwork. The early field already ranges from traditional illustration to sculpture and textiles. Current highlights | Competition rules

Deleted a character by mistake? There is a direct recovery route

GGG Support confirmed that Path of Exile 2 characters can be restored through the account character page. For anyone who has ever deleted the wrong exile during a cleanup, this is a small detail with potentially enormous consequences. Support confirmation | Character restoration page
