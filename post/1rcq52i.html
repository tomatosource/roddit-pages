I don’t want to be a hater but Johnnie LaRossa, a contestant on the upcoming season of The Bachelorette, had one of the worst baseball careers I’ve ever seen

So if you watch a lot of trash dating reality TV shows - and you should be - a common casting choice is get pro athletes because they tend to be very physically fit, and sometimes even hot. They also often have the very specific type of attention seeking mental illness that is required to be a reality show contestant. This normally takes the form of what ABC/The Bachelor franchise specifically likes to call “Former NFL Player” for guys that spent a few months on practice squads.

When the new cast for The Bachelorette dropped this morning with a “Former Professional Baseball player” I of course immediately went to bbref and found this king:

https://www.baseball-reference.com/register/player.fcgi?id=laross000joh

Normally out of these I expect a guy who was taken in like the 34th round, played one year in rookie ball, and flamed out. This guy didn’t get drafted, because he was awful in college. And then he was awful in independent ball. Then, somehow, in 2021 he wound up with a minor league deal with the Brewers. I cannot fathom why - going through his social media he wasn’t clearly working with a pitching lab or anything that would give him any sort of diamond-in-the-rough style hype. Did they just need bodies post-pandemic? And then he went back to indy ball where he sucked for another couple of years.

There isn’t anything super notable here, I just don’t think I’ve ever seen anyone grind it out for as long as this dude has without being good ONCE.

He is currently doing cosmic ball, which is a Bananas-style baseball show, and good for him. He is also doing a ‘Carnivore Diet’ which should shoot off every red flag imaginable.
