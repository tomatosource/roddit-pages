POE2 Understanding Progression

Now before I start I will add I am no expert or even a accomplished vet in this game or the first. I’m actually still quite very new to the game and understanding everything. But I have learnt alot and done tons of research on moba, maxroll, pob and poe2 ninja. But aside from all I’m still learning and this is what I feel progressing is based on for myself;

  1. No longer is currency like exalts and chaos orbs the apex of loot for running maps, divines will always be amazing to find as it helps when you trade them for 900 ex as of the market recently. But gold… Just straight piles of gold have been something I farm like crazy lately. I get divs less frequent but enough where I have to farm maps for 110k of gold just to convert them sometimes so I can craft.

  2. T15 and T16 seem to be a good benchmark, but once you run those and can do decent with them it’s hard to tell if your still progressing. Well, I finally got past the strict filter and got to the very strict filter from neversinkdevs filters and lemme tell ya that felt great to see the progression.

  3. Hoarding items, I’m terrible for it, I love crafting, even if it’s just with reforging and not even with recombination machine. But see play long enough you get a lot of gear, tons you wanna keep but how do you choose? Well it was lvl79 is all gone and now it’s lvl80 haha only keeping lvl81 and up for thing. Belts and jewelry are a bit different but hey maybe I haven’t progressed there yet in the game.

Now these are just benchmarks I’ve hit for myself but it’s something I’ve realized lately in this season as I’ve put in a lot of work into making a single build work, there’s only so much you can read and understand before learning yourself. Crafting is still a trial and error that is a currency dump with testing and many failures but that’s the game. I could be wrong in these benchmarks but maybe some may feel the same haha
