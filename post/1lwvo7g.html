Some hopes for 0.3

Five months of work is no small thing, specially considering 0.2 brought up some heavy stuff like 100 support gems and 100 uniques.

Considering this, and assuming the PoE1 league didn’t drag many resources (which I’d assume considering is far from their biggest update), my “realistic” hopes are:

  • Act 4, last trial of ascendancy, no more Act 3 Cruel.

  • New class and weapon, 5 total new ascendancies if we count the already existing classes OR full ascendancies for the new cass.

  • Actual meaningful league content.

  • Minimal endgame tweaking/rework in the right direction. Mainly because these things take time and they can’t be developing new stuff while reworking old stuff consistently.

  • More active skill gems, less uniques and support gems.

  • Deeper crafting options, since this has been consistent feedback since 0.1. Hopefully less gambling based, more consistent.

My more hopeful, non realistic expectations are:

  • Two new classes since they are not developing one at a time and Druid seemed to be very advanced in showcases.

  • A means for traversing faster, possibly a Quicksilver flask.

  • Trials of the Ancestors.
