The Seattle Mariners are officially the closest they have ever been to a championship

This season is the M’s 4th appearance in the ALCS. They previously had never won more than 2 games in the series, never coming within 5 wins of winning the World Series. With tonight’s win making it a 3-2 series, that has finally changed.

For comparison, 25 of the other 29 teams have come within 0 wins of a championship (bc they won the World Series, obviously). These are the other 4:

Milwaukee Brewers: 1 win away, coming in their lone WS appearance in 1982

Tampa Bay Rays: 2 wins away in their 4-2 loss to the Dodgers in 2020. Their other WS appearance (2008) ended 4-1 vs the Phillies

San Diego Padres: 3 wins away, coming in the 1984 World Series. They made the series again in 1998, but got swept by the Yankees

Colorado Rockies: 4 wins away, the only other team to never win a WS game. They got swept by the Red Sox in their lone WS appearance in 2007
