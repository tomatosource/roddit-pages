The primary difference between watching baseball in Spanish vs English

In English, “pitch” is a small word, so if there are 250 pitches in a game, you don’t notice the word being said 250 times. But in Spanish lanciamento if a 4⁄5 syllable word said 250 times a game. We need a shorter word. How about “El lans”?
