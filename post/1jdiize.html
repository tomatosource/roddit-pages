Would this be a solution to the armor issue?

Been thinking about this lately, but it’s been on my mind for several years of PoE1. I get the idea behind non-linear scaling (is it inverse quadratic or something? I’ve seen all the charts, but not sure) in order to help with many small hits (swarm) but not make it too OP on big hits (rares/bosses). However, this has always been kinda meh because trash dies instantly anyway unless you get unlucky with some kind of hard cc and it does nothing against bosses.

So I was thinking, why not make it flat % based with a lower cap and expand the cap via nodes on the ‘armor side’ of the tree.

You can tweak it so it’s still not entirely linear. So for example:

1k armor = 20%

2k armor = 25%

5k armor = 30%

10k armor = 40%

20k armor = 50%

^ this is a random example

Cap it at 50%. Rebalance some armor nodes to provide +max% armor mitigation. You’d have to play with the numbers, but I don’t see why it’s not doable - convince me (mathematically) otherwise. This way on the high end you could end up with something like 60-70% at 30-40-50k armor or whatever and have it be an actual percentage.

It might fix melee.

The idea is that you a) don’t make lower amounts of armor completely useless, i.e. hybrid gear (armor/es), but also don’t make armor stacking punishing because it’s a poo poo defensive layer.

I dunno .. what are your thoughts? I really just hope GGG doesn’t keep the same ‘ol formula for PoE2 cause it just doesn’t work.
