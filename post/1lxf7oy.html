Most fans including recent players get 60s & 70s basketball completely wrong — I broke down an hour of Willis Reed gametape to show how the game used to be played and how to appreciate that era

A while back I saw Al Harrington on Gil’s Arena say he would’ve busted Willis Reed’s you know what if they played in the same era. A lot of fans think this same way and I’ve heard half a dozen active or recent NBA players talk about that era the exact same way. It’s like a recurring trend that isn’t just a jab at Reed but rather a dismissal of that entire era as unskilled or inferior.

I’ve been collecting film for something like 15 years now so I dug in and spent a few months organizing some rare film and breaking it down for fans that may wonder about that era and actually want to try and figure out how to fairly judge players like Reed. From his matchups with Bill Russell and Kareem to how much more or less physical the rules allowed the game to be in the ‘60s and ‘70s. Even though this focuses on Reed this is as comprehensive a film breakdown I’m aware of of that era, showing what made Reed and players like him great, and why so many misconceptions about his era exist.

Whether you end up agreeing or not with what I tried to cover, I’d love to hear what you guys think after having more context. Enjoy!
