[Charania] The NBA has cancelled the Atlanta Hawks’ Magic City game promotion on March 16.

Source: https://www.espn.com/contributor/shams-charania/f64fa213e2e97

The NBA has cancelled the Atlanta Hawks’ Magic City game promotion on March 16.

——

#UPDATE:

Statement from NBA commissioner Adam Silver: “When we became aware of the Atlanta Hawks’ scheduled promotion, we reached out to Hawks leadership to better understand their plans and rationale.  While we appreciate the team’s perspective and their desire to move forward, we have heard significant concerns from a broad array of league stakeholders, including fans, partners and employees.  I believe canceling this promotion is the right decision for the broader NBA community.”
