Monk Starting Experience

After getting hyped by the Hollow Form announcement, I thought I’d refresh myself on the Monk levelling experience by doing some HC SSF. I had forgotten just how bad Act 1 Monk (or perhaps I should say Quarterstaff) is.

This video from FGKorbyn21 gives a guide (written up here)… and while he makes it look easy, I’m pretty sure that the average player that’s logging back into POE2 for the first time in months to give the new thing a try is going to bounce of this so hard. Comparing it to the Talisman - bear and wolf come out of the box with a basic skill set that just works.

This video from Path of Evening appeared on my feed the next day and really reflected my feelings (for both staves and spears) and offers some pretty sane sounding recommendations:

  • switch Falling Thunder out for Ice Strike.
  • just buff the spear damage

I’m guessing there are plenty of other ways to skin these cats. I was wondering whether giving Quarterstaff basic attack some kind of Power Charge generation would make sense/be overpowered (every 3rd strike on the same target has an X% chance to gain a power charge, for example).

I just really hope that GGG do something. If the obvious starting point for both of the 2 ‘shiny new things’ (Monk and Hunter ascendancies) then the new/returning player experience is going to be so bad.

If something doesn’t come, what’s your best advice to players (less experienced than the hardcore) who do want to get into Monk? Use a bow until act 2? Pick up some strength and level with a Talisman? Spells? (Contagion has been a favourite in the past, Entangle is also pretty strong and perhaps fits the RP of Monk better).
