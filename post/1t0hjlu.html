We have reverted to 2023 Celtics ball “movement” since the 3rd Quarter of Game 5

I am the biggest optimist of the Celtics, and it is so easy to overreact on a game to game basis. But oh my god the hesitation of everyone other than Brown and Tatum is absolutely killing our flow.

Derrick White struggles continue and it makes every offensive possession stagnant when there’s no serious 3rd option threat. Along with that, all the players that stepped up all year with no Tatum look physically shook and defer to the Jays on literally every possession.

The starting lineup in Game 7 has to include Pritchard, this isn’t the regular season, you need the best players on the floor. If Derrick white is not gonna shoot, and least Pritchard can create space and make Phillies defense second guess helping and doubling.

I can live with Game 2 when the Celtics shoot horribly and Philly is lights out, we can expect that every once in a while. But what pisses me off is the lack of aggression, passion, urgency, heart, and confidence. The team overachieved all year, it would be a damn shame to go out like this.

Oubre and Embiid deserve Oscar’s, but that’s besides the point. The whole Philly team is playing with house money and Boston looks so lackadaisical and outplayed on both sides.

It’s not an over-exaggeration to say game 7 will dictate the future of this squad. I’ve got faith but damn this team raising my blood pressure.
