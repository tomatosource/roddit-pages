The tatum-brown celtics regular season performance is completely based on if they over or under perform expectations the year before

So I’m not sure if anyone has picked up on this but, at least in my opinion, the celtics regular season performance in the tatum-brown era is completely based on if they over or under performed expectations the season prior. This is kind of long but it needs to be because this biyearly halfassing has gone all the way back to 2017. You guys could totally disagree with me but I know this at least plays a part. I’ve been tracking this since tatum was drafted and let me break it down:

2017-2018: Tatum joins the team and officially starts the timeline. They significantly overperform and go all the way to game 7 of the conference finals, losing out to only lebron james. Although they lost, this season was hailed as an unmitigated success due to the injuries. OVERexpectations

2018-2019: The team sucks ass and completely half asses the regular season, losing to garbage teams whenever possible. Kyrie famously proclaims that their regular season struggles don’t matter because nobody was beating them 4 out of 7 times. Team underperforms title level expectations as they’re embarrassed in the second round. UNDERexpectations

2019-2020: the team is turned over to Tatum and Brown. They improve on the previous season and they go all the way to the conference finals. Fun team to watch. The season was considered successful because both tatum and brown emerged and they went further than the year before. OVERexpectations

2020-2021: This team was just flat out terrible. Injuries and covid did ravage this team but they still sucked. Knocked out in the 1st round and take a massive step back. UNDERexpectations

2021-2022: This team started poor but ended up being very good. They vastly overperform expectations by simply out trying teams in the regular season. They make it all the way to the finals, the franchise’s first since 2010. Huge success of a season. OVERexpectations

2022-2023: This team was absolutely the biggest culprit of the half assing. This team half-assed it all the way until they were down 3-0 in the conference finals. Then, they turn it on to come back from 3-0 down just to lose in hilarious fashion at home for game 7. This team was notorious for blown leads, losing to shit teams, and extending series. UNDERexpectations

2023-2024: With new players and a point to prove, the celtics turn into a buzzsaw and shred anyone and everyone. Was the best team in the whole league and wasn’t even close. They finally breakthrough and win the title. Massive success of a season as banner 18 is finally secured. OVERexpectations

2024-2025: Although it may seem like i’m kicking the team when they’re down, they’ve been doing this shit all year. They’ve halfassed most games this season. The only difference is that at the beginning of the season, they managed to win these games. Now, they’re losing them. As a quick aside, they remind me of the Brown, Bell, Roethlisberger steelers teams who would put up poor effort most games and simply out talent teams at the end to save the day.

I’m in no way blaming any individuals, tatum/brown, or the coaching staff. It just seems like there is a certain lack of urgency from within the organization. Like let’s be honest, most of their injuries this season are completely made up. They feel that they’ll win the title as long as they’re healthy and they’re treating the regular season as such.
