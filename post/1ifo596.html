With today’s upset win vs. the Timberwolves, the Washington Wizards break their 17 game losing streak & hold on the slimmest of chances to go 0.500 by the season’s end. They would have to never lose their remaining games to do so.

With a record of 7-41, the Wizards are on an all-time worst pace - they are on pace to win 12 games and that’s rounding up based on their current win percentage - but they do still have an oppirtunity to do one of the funniest things ever.

By winning all remaining 34 games, the Washington Wizards can technically go 0.500 at the end of the season. This would not only be near unprecedented for a team with a record this bad this late into the season but a 35 game win streak counting tonight’s game would also beat the current record for all-time win streaks of 33 (It was the 72 Lakers).

Will Washington pull off this record-breaking streak? Probably not, especially with Alex Sarr’s absence but the dream for such still lives on for another day.
