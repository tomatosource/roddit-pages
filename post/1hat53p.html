As of tonight, 3 of the Top 4 teams in the NBA lottery feature notable current/former injuries to their players. The 4th and worst of them all is the Washington Wizards.

Something I thought was interesting, irrespective of which had the most or least difficult schedules up to this point, is that 3of the bottom 4 in the standings/top 4 in the lottery are teams which either are currently banged up or had prior lost games to open the season. Notably, the Pelicans lost Dejounte Murray on opening night amongst the many players who have been in & out of the lineup including All-Star Zion Williamson. Other players in their rotation such as Herb Jones and Brandon Ingram have been in and out of the lineup due to their own respective injuries.

The Utah Jazz’s own All-Star in Lauri Markannen has been in and out of the Jazz’s lineups along with sophomore Taylor Hendricks and former 6-Man of the Year Jordan Clarkson set to miss his 8th straight game due to plantar fasciitis.

The Toronto Raptors just lost Scottie Barnes in tonight’s sole game, with Scottie himself being one of many Raptors players who were either injured out of the gate or got injured early on; Immanuel Quickley went down in the Raptors home opener and Bruce Brown has yet to play a single game as of early December.

Then there’s the Washington Wizards.

While the Washington Wizards have not been 100% injury free - Alex Sarr is currently dealing with back soreness for example - relative to these teams (or even other lottery teams dealing with major injuries like the 76ers) and who are the main rotational players for the Wizards, the only notable rotational players who have missed significant games are the oft-injured Malcom Brogdon and a Kyle Kuzma who has not been playing to their expectations. Even if they were, it should go without saying that Kuzma is not on the same level as Murray, Zion, Markannen or Barnes in terms of talent or importance to their team’s success when they play.

The 2024⁄25 Washington Wizards possess not only the worst record in the league at 3-19 but one of the worst net ratings in NBA history at -15.7 in spite of their health relative to the other on-court hospital clubs.
