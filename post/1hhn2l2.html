Home Court 3pt line

I’m fascinated by the idea of each franchise setting their home court 3pt line like how baseball teams set their own outfield home run walls / lines.

Each team locks in its home court before the season. Minimum college line, no maximum or no line at all.

For example, Milwaukee could set a very far line that only Dame can reliability hit. Or a team can go old school with no three point lines at all.

Upsides:

  1. Lots of different offensive or defensive styles available. Is it worth having a post up team with physical defense with no three point lines at all?

  2. Championship teams have to be able to play multiple styles so they can win on the road.

  3. Road game matchups much more interesting. Can Mavs or Celtics or Warriors win at Utah’s no-three court?

What’s the downside? Seems fun.
