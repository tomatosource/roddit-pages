Noob player here. I’m having so much fun.

I played PoE 1 once and followed a build guide, and really enjoyed it… but i didnt feel like I had enough agency myself. Fast forward to now – PoE 2 is insane. I’m doing a hybrid lightning frost sorceress, and im speccing (if that’s the correct term?) heavily into infusion points and exposure with shields, elemental damage and frost/lightning penetration.

I feel like I don’t even need a loot filter now. I’m getting just enough currency to feel like it’s rewarding, and I’m trying to focus on building resists, but it’s more challenging to do that than say Last Epoch.

I’ve just gotten to Act 3, and I’m having so much fun. I’m guessing if it’s anything like the first game I’ll hit a wall trying to use my own build, but if I’m correct it only costs money to re spec so it seems fine?

The boss fights at this stage seem really well made, like if I don’t actually learn what to avoid i will die… which has happened a few times.

Honestly, I think I’m going to end up spending hundreds of hours in this, and the game isn’t even 1.0. I bought a lot of tabs in PoE1, and it seems like even those have been migrated over. Blizzard would have absolutely charged me twice.

As a gamer since the early 90s, I’m super proud that this came from my home town in Auckland, and I’ll gladly end up spending more money to support GGG.
