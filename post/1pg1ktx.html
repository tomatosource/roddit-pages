GGG, if you want us to combo, please give us more skill slots!

In general I don’t mind the combo gameplay GGG is pushing for (as long as it’s not too clunky), however I find on pretty much every build I play that I run out of skill slots to do all the interesting things I want to do. Particularly when you utilize weapon swap and have different spirit reservations on your swap. Since swapping sets doesn’t give us two separate sets of skill slots, we end up eating up a bunch of extra slots for spirit buffs and either can’t fit in the skills to combo with or have to drop some spirit buffs.

Unset rings are a thing but imo are rarely worth giving up ring res implicits/unique rings for the extra slots. It’s a bad tradeoff to be forced to make just to play a combo-heavy build.

Please GGG, consider giving us a few extra skill slots.
