Anthony Edwards’ career-long promise to a child with cancer lasted 4 games

This heartwarming post drew big attention on this subreddit a few days ago, where Anthony Edwards receives an orange wristband from a young fan with cancer. He gave a promise to the kid, saying, “I’ll wear this for the rest of my career on my left arm band just for you.” To his credit, Edwards was seen wearing the wristband for the next four games, but no wristband was in sight for the fifth game in Washington DC yesterday. The highlight videos on ESPN’s website feature many close ups of Edwards without the bracelet.
