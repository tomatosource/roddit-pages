Is Mazzulla&gt;Udoka Now Certain?

When Ime was suspended and ultimately released, I thought the Celtics made a rash decision that mortgaged their future success on how a 37-year old with no professional coaching experience and limited D2 college coaching experience would lead a group of players that had just gotten to the finals and seemed to love/respect Ime. I’ll admit that I myself was a bit frustrated at times during Joe’s first season especially when we fell 0-3 vs Miami in the ECF. That might’ve been the lowest point of that year when the team looked like they gave up on him before rallying back 3 straight wins and being a Tatum ankle turn away from the finals. I again was frustrated at the manner we lost the first two games against the Knicks last year in what was a series we should’ve taken in 5 and I attribute that in part to Mazzulla allowing the team to take as many threes as they did. He certainly silenced any critics by winning the title the year before but with the team we had last year and seeing how Indiana pushed OKC to 7, I feel like we should’ve repeated last year.

Now that we are close to 4 years since that shock, it seems like the general feeling around the league is that Mazzulla clears Udoka especially with how the Celtics over achieved this season without Tatum and losing key pieces to a championship team while the Rockets appear to be imploding.

What is everyone’s general feelings about Mazzulla and 4 years out, do you believe we are better off with him as coach vs Udoka? Are there certain strengths and weaknesses one coach has over the other?
