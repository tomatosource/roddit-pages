  • Only 4 NBA champions in the last 45 seasons were not in the 40-20 club.

  • Neither the Pacers or the Knicks made the 40-20 club this season.

  • Only 7 other teams in history have made the 80-20 club, and out of those teams, only the 2016 Warriors did not win the championship.

The current 80-20 club:

  • 2025 OKC (80-18)
  • 2017 GSW (80-15, finished 83-16)
  • 2016 GSW (80-11, finished 88-18)
  • 2015 GSW (80-18, finished 83-20)
  • 1997 CHI (80-15, finished 84-17)
  • 1996 CHI (80-11, finished 87-13)
  • 1986 BOS (80-16, finished 82-18)
  • 1972 LAL (80-16, finished 81-16)
