An Examination of Why the Unique Economy Crashed in 0.3 and How to Fix It

As we exit 0.3, it has become an unmistakable fact that unique items… are trash. There were complaints about how wobbly the unique pool was in 0.2, but 0.3 has seen the market crash out.

Uniques that used to cost divines now cost chaos or exalts. Orbs of chance are barely worth picking up. No one uses unique weapons, and former chase items are all but forgotten. 

What Happened?

Well, the system was always bad. The idea of suboptimal items with unique effects always depended on the relative value of the effect to the opportunity cost of the lost item. Which they rarely matched.

Then in 0.3, deterministic crafting made formerly high divine items dirt cheap. Every unique’s value is decided by the power of its unique effect vs the opportunity cost of a rare item’s superior statline.

Better rares became categorically cheaper and easier to obtain, so the trade-off for uniques became categorically more punishing.

This can be shown when you look at unique jewels like Heart of the Well and unique Charms like Rite of Passage. You can’t craft a better one, so they’re still divine valued. Unique belts also fared okay, because the relative value of a crafted belt is lower than any other slot. Unique Value only crashed in the slots with easy crafting.

A second factor is that GGG often used nerfs to specific items as a way to control the power of builds. Hands of W&A and Tangletongue are two examples of previously meta-viable items that were gutted to reduce the power of a specific build (Stat Stacker/Crit Lightning Spear). Others like Infernoclasp and Deepest Tower were removed entirely.

0.2.1 was meant to increase the number of Chase Uniques by adding 9 and buffing over 15, but only Kaom’s Heart, Headhunter, and Palm of the Dreamer are still meta. An around 90% failure rate. No wonder the deflation of uniques continued. If anything, it got worse. Once rare amulets like Astramentis had their drop-rate normalized, their value also fell off a cliff.

Another thing that’s harmed many uniques is changing mechanics. Chernobog’s Pillar is the poster child of this. When Block was changed, its extra damage was cut by 1/3rd. Many uniques lost out when “Armour Applies to Elemental Damage” and “Deflection” were added to the game. Few Uniques received any of the two powerful stats that make certain rares way stronger.

Indeed, many uniques were subtly harmed by the shift in 0.3. To account for crafting being stronger, certain runes like Iron Rune were weakened. In rares, deterministic crafting more then accounted for the difference. Any unique that wasn’t buffed actually became weaker as a result. Morior Incivus was hurt worse by this than anything else.

The current situation demands a new approach and I hope GGG is discussing this internally. But, as a player this is my feedback to them. 

MUGA

Make Uniques great again. Make them exciting again. When I see that light shoot up and a unique drop, I want to feel like I won something big.

That means more unique rarity. I shouldn’t be getting 3 uniques per map. But, it also should mean better stats. I shouldn’t consider uniques trash-drops. Any unique that’s less valuable than 10 exalts, 2 weeks into a season, is a failed item. I am willing to abide by an across-the-board increase in rarity if the value of the average unique was significantly raised.

Let’s focus on stats. They don’t have to match the best-in-slot numbers. They *shouldn’t* match best-in-slot numbers. But, there’s a difference between “sub-optimal” and “wasted slot”. A unique billed as one of the game’s most valuable drops, Coming Calamity, gives ONE-QUARTER the defenses of a one-exalt armour. You are trading most of your body-armour slot to use it. If this is supposed to fulfill the fantasy, you have to buff it. You simply have to buff it.

The buffs don’t have to be raw stats. I can see why low-level uniques might ruin the campaign if they were over-buffed. In their case, you can at least give them supplementary stats. An item like Crown of Eyes has potential to be endgame viable, because of its fantastic effect. But, it already has over 100% increased ES and if it dropped in campaign, it could be wildly unbalanced for an Act 4 character. 

Do you want to make it more viable? Buff the attribute gain to 20-25 and give it +15% Cold and Lightning Resistance. You would instantly make it way more playable. 

Most characters get a maximum of 60-66 item modifiers they can give their build. Most uniques trade raw stats AND modifier slots for their unique effects. Some of those, especially resistances, are basically a tax that a finished build has to fill. If the game lets more of those low level uniques fill your modifier taxes, they’d be much better.

It also wouldn’t break the campaign, because ultimately the value of filling more of your modifier taxes matters more in the late game when deterministic crafting and trading increases the value of every mod slot.

Painter’s Servant is a unique item I want to use for Trinity Builds, but it basically offers no defenses. At least give the damn things +10 all Element Resist. It would free up a ring modifier, making the opportunity cost smaller.

One way to buff many uniques is to figure out what they’re being replaced for and give them *something* back. I tried to run Snakebite on my Poison Pathfinder, but gave up because I could get more value from cheap gloves with AS and extra physical damage. If you gave them a modest amount of either, they could become viable again for me.

The Skill Level Problem

Which brings us to the entire class of items that are all being oppressed by a single stat. Weapons, Wands, Foci, Quivers, Damage-Boosting Amulets, Scepters. With few exceptions, the +Skill Level stat makes them worse than rares. Sure, I can have your unique effect… Or I could give my skills flat 10-20% exponentially increasing DPS per level. 

I consider the unique system’s lack of +Level Modifiers a unique problem. Gem levels are so core to scaling endgame characters, they represent a third tradeoff uniques require besides stats and mod-slots.

Look at poor Hammer of Faith. They gave it so much extra damage in 0.2.1, but no one started using it. Because, why bother when you can get a hammer with *just as much damage and +6 to Skill Level* for 10 exalts. That +6 Skill Level will nearly double your DPS. Will Hammer of Faith’s shrine buffs?

Ditto for every unique wand in the game. The *core* The determining factor of a wand’s value is +skill levels. Any wand without +skill levels is worth nothing, and none of the unique wands give you more than +3 flat spell levels. Every Unique Focus (aside from the hilariously overtuned Rathpike Globe) has completely fallen out. Threaded Light and Effigy of Cruelty could be cool endgame tools. Trade ES for unique ways to do damage? Too bad you’re also giving up +3 Spell Levels.

Amulets do a little better, but you still feel the missing gem levels on them. Choir of the Storm completely died in the meta when people realized +Skill Level was everything. All it would take to make it a viable item is a “+1-2 Lightning Spell Level”. Because otherwise you’re trading crit pen and lightning storm for a whopping 30%-40% flat DPS from a single affix. 

I also felt this hard when I wanted to experiment with cool minion builds. An Alpha’s Howl Evasion based summoner? Trash. Wear a +2 Helmet. Trading defense/attack for Minion defense using Carrion Call? Trash. Use a +2 Foci. Gimping your own damage & Defense for minions using Leer Cast? Trash. A +2 rare helmet literally buffs their DPS way more. 

God, look at this thing. Chobber Chabber. Throw some minion or attack levels on there. Give the damage a solid buff. Suddenly, an entire badass lead-from-the-front Warlord build could be viable. Use Tactician or Titan for it. God, even thinking about it makes me so mad. Why is this just in the game taunting me when it’s so garbage!?

I want to point at boots, which are their own problem. If +Skill Level is the core mechanic in other items that is treated like another modifier, MS is the core mechanic of boots that’s treated like a modifier.

The fact that +MS is a mod for boots and not a core part of the item is already basically a major design flaw. Everyone tiers boots by their MS anyway. The stats on your boots barely effect their value if they lack MS. Any unique boots that don’t have 30% MS are already forcing the player into a major trade off. 

The designers put so much work into giving the game Shankgonne, EXPLODING CANNON BOOTS. If they merely said “You can’t sprint and the stats are bad”, that would be an interesting play choice. But, by giving it an MS bonus of 0%, they removed the item from endgame play. 

To Conclude

Alright, this is running long. But, I hope I’ve a sufficiently in-depth overview of the abysmal state of unique items. The game is desperate for another unique overhaul like 0.2.1, but this time GGG seriously needs to look at all these cool items that they’re paying talented artists to create and ask what it would take to give them a meta use. There’s a world of difference between “Interesting Tradeoff” and “Easily Replaced with Mathematically Superior Rare”. 

I want to be excited by drops besides currency. I want to monkey around with viable off-meta builds. Fix uniques.
