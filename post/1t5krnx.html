“The roster is built to shoot 3s, what else are we supposed to do?”

Yes, ​if the roster is built from mostly shooters who don’t have the strength or athleticism to get downhill and finish through traffic, ​​then ​you should​ take a lot of threes​. That’s fine. The Celtics should take a lot of threes. ​The problem is ​too many EARLY SHOT CLOCK THREES. People seem unable to make that distinction.

Joe’s basketball philosophy: if we take more shots then them, we give ourselves the best chance to win. If we take more threes than them, we give ourselves the best chance to win. It’s math. ​That simple idea dictates his entire approach: minimize turnovers (horrible execution btw), maximize offensive rebounds, maximize threes, and maximize total possessions so the “math advantage” becomes more pronounced. That’s why he has the team shooting threes in transition, shooting threes off the first screening action, shooting threes directly ​off OREBS, and so on. He wants a faster pace and more possessions​. ​He’s trying to ratchet up the​ total ​3-pointers attempted by any means necessary.

So all the fans complaining about “too many threes,” including myself, aren’t upset that they ran their offense and it happened to end with a 3-pointer instead of a middy or layup. That would’ve been fine. We’re upset because Joe thinks he outsmarted the game of basketball. He’s not even trying to ​create the best shots, he’s just trying to create the most shots. In his mind, some enormous gap in 3pt FGA is enough to completely overwhelm any other ​considerations​, and he wants to achieve that gap by any means necessary.

But the means do matter. The method of creating a shot influences the likelihood of the shot going in, even if it makes no difference to your math equation​. It makes a difference on the court, and that’s why the Celtics are a 2 seed not playing in the second round. ​You can’t take shortcuts to juice the numbers.​ The Celtics should rely heavily on the 3pt line, that’s what they’re built to do, but they shouldn’t rely on shortcuts. They should actually use the shot c​lock, run multiple actions, ​work the ball side to side, drive and ​kick multiple times, and THEN shoot a three if they have to. Quality over quantity.

So the “too many threes” people aren’t really complaining about too many threes. It’s too many low quality, early shot clock, “we’re only doing this to juice the total 3pt FGA at the end of the game” type threes. ​Joe keeps prioritizing quantity over quality, and it doesn’t work.
