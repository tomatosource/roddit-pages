Brandon Ingram’s 6 year gap between All-Star appearances is tied for 2nd longest time between All-Star appearances over the past 3 decades

Brandon Ingram: 2020, 2026 (6 year gap)

That gap is tied with Manu Ginobli, who had his 2 All-Star appearances in 2005 and 2011.

Only Jrue Holiday has a longer gap between appearances. His appearances were in 2013 and 2023, marking a 10 year gap - albeit Jrue racked up his fair share of other accolades over that timespan such as several All-Defense nods and winning with the Bucks in 2021.

Congratulations to Brandon Ingram on making his 2nd All-Star appearance as a reserve!
