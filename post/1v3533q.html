FINALLY Did the 4th Ascendancy in SSF!

HOOOLLLYY What a battle against Zarokh! Especially with only a 411DPS Phys bow lmao. I’ve done my own 4th plenty of times in Trade League, but this is my first time doing it in SSF. I failed Trial of Chaos 1hr before this due to a poor dodge roll, and dude’s sunder attack got past 73% evasion, twice. I was so mad bc I was doing so much damage. Decided, F it, I’ll do Sekhema’s. I was devasted bc I was 2 rooms from Zarokh. Well, this time with about 4500 honour, and the “Pledge to the Powerful” (50% less Honour lost in Boss Rooms), I was able to kill him. My DPS was pretty bad, so it took a few minutes. I even ran out of Mana potion charges and quickly slotted my Basic Attack bc he had like 5% HP lmaoo. Swapped it back to Poisonburst when I noticed my Mana was fully recharged. Holy shit, I am so hyped to get the Archon of Chayula tornado that should boost my single target DPS. Yup, that’s all guys, thanks for reading lol I am ecstatic right now. Back to grinding the Atlas and the League Mechanics a second time lol. Also, I HIGHLY recommend everyone try SSF once in their Poe2 career. It is so much better than Trade League, after inflation gets bad and upgrades become too expensive, of course. It forces you to learn how to do basic crafting and how to understand valuable gear bases and overall is just a better experience than Trade League, in my opinion.
