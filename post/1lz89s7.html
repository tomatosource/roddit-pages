LeBron James’ stats in the postseason vs teams with the lowest ranked defensive rating in the league (featuring MJ, Shaq, and other all-time greats)

I was randomly arguing with people on this sub and came across what I thought was a pretty interesting little tidbit; LeBron played a number 1 rated defense in the postseason every year he was a member of the Heat, and he won all 4 series. So I did some digging about how he played generally against the best defense the league had to offer. All of these teams had ranked 1st in terms of defensive rating. Here were LeBron’s stats against them in the playoffs.

Team faced (Series) PPG RPG APG FG% TS% Series Result for LeBron
Celtics (2008 ECSF) 26.7 6.4 7.6 35.5% 48.0% 3-4 Loss
Magic (2009 ECF) 38.5 8.3 8.0 48.1% 59.1% 2-4 Loss
Bulls (2011 ECF) 25.8 7.8 6.6 44.7% 56.9% 4-1 Win
Celtics (2012 ECF) 33.6 11.0 3.9 52.7% 58.7% 4-3 Win
Pacers (2013 ECF) 29.0 7.3 5.3 51.0% 60.9% 4-3 Win
Pacers (2014 ECF) 22.8 6.3 5.5 55.9% 63.7% 4-2 Win
Celtics (2018 ECF) 33.6 9.0 8.4 52.4% 61.0% 4-3 Win

Now, I didn’t adjust for pace because, frankly, I didn’t know how to balance pace with games played, but in terms of per game stats, here’s what I found:

LeBron averaged 30.1 PPG, 8.1 RPG, 6.4 APG while shooting 48.5 FG% and 57.6 TS% with a 5-2 series record (25-20 games record).

I wanted to see how this stacked up against other all-time greats. I didn’t want to make a bunch of graphs, so here is just the summation of what I found for these other players.

Michael Jordan played 3 number 1 rated defenses in the playoffs: the ‘86 Celtics, the ‘93 Knicks, and the ‘97 Heat. Overall, he averaged 33.9 PPG, 6.8 RPG, 5.1 APG while shooting 42.1 FG% and 52.0 TS% with a 2-1 series record (8-6 games).

Shaquille O’Neal played 6 number 1 rated defenses in the playoffs: the ‘96 Bulls, the ‘99 Spurs, the 2001 Spurs, the 2002 Nets, the 2004 Spurs, and the 2007 Bulls. He averaged 26.6 PPG, 12.7 RPG, 2.4 APG while shooting 58.0 FG% and 57.5 TS% with a 2-4 series record (9-16 games record). All but 1 of these series, wins and losses, were sweeps, interestingly enough.

Larry Bird played a number 1 rated defense once in the postseason: the ‘84 Knicks, a series the Celtics won 4-3. Bird averaged 30.4 PPG, 10.6 RPG, 7.1 APG shooting 58.5 FG% and 64.9 TS%. (unreal)

Magic Johnson played 3 top-rated defenses in the postseason, winning all 3 (games record of 11-6); the ‘80 76ers, the ‘88 Jazz, and the ‘90 Rockets. Magic averaged 19.8 PPG, 7.5 RPG, 10.5 APG shooting 52.0 FG% and 59.7 TS%.

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar played 5 number 1 defenses: the ‘70 Knicks, the ‘74 Bulls, the ‘79 SuperSonics, the ‘80 76ers, and the ‘88 Jazz. Before I continue, I just want to make it known that the 7 game series against the Jazz in his second to last year in the league seriously drops his career average. If you exclude that series, he averaged 32.7 PPG, 15.6 RPG, 4.1 APG shooting 56.7 FG%, with a 2-2 series record (9-10 games record). That’s unbelievable. Including the Jazz series, his career averages against top defenses are 26.2 PPG, 13.2 RPG, 3.5 APG shooting 54.6 FG% and 58 TS% with a series record of 3-2 and a games record of 13-13.

As far as I can tell, at least when comparing him to other all-time greats, LeBron has played more number 1 rated defenses than any player I could find. Even when comparing him to legends, his stat profile and winning record stand out, because of his high level of production, the remarkable level of consistency that he has maintained overtime, and the fact that he has 5 such wins while no other player has more than 3 (although, for what it’s worth, the player I’m most impressed with is Kareem, given the unreal stats he was putting up in the 70’s). If someone could convert these to per100 stats, I’d be really interested to see how these profiles change.
