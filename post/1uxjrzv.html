Finally made top grenades crit crossbow

Took too much currency but critical damage bonus mod worth it i guess (+15% more damage for me).

Crafting steps:

  1. Buy bunch of ilvl 81 bases (82 is bad - Load 2 additional bolts in suffixes may brick whittling later)

  2. Quality to 25-26% - do nothing for ele crossbows but crafting byproducts sells better.

  3. Aug and annul until T2 % phys, T1 flat phys or T1 crit. Phys crossbows go to independent profit crafting pool.

  4. Fracture crit (4.99% oopsie)

  5. Chaos spam T1 Fire or T1 Lightning.

  6. Whittle second T1 flat ele. T1 phys hybrid, T1 % ele or T1 flat cold may brick whittling, so can go to step 5 multiple times.

  7. Whittle T1 % ele - chances to success ~5 to 1 with T1 phys hybrid bricking it again (go to step 5).

  8. Perfect exalt + dextral annul until +5 to projectiles skills. Took ~80 attempts.

  9. All 5 mods are ilvl 81 now, can whittle anything you want for last suffix. My target was Crit bonus, took ~60 whittles with perfect chaos.

  10. Divine until you’re fine.

  11. Slap +1 suffix rune, desecrate guaranteed chance to activate grenades second time (its more damage amplifier for grenades builds). Used omen of light 10 times trying to get 25%, settled for 24%.

  12. Enjoy big booms.
