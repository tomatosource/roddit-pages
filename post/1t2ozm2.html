The bigger issues standing in the way of another ship in the Js era

During the Bird era Larry was surrounded by HOFers at PG, Center, and PF that team was stacked and they still lost 2 out of 3 to a stacked Laker team in the finals. This group and even the one that beat Dallas in the finals a couple of years ago does not come close to that talent pool. Combine that with what’s lurking out west with OKC and the Spurs who are young AND have put together juggernaut teams that barring injury will probably be the favs to win the next few titles and I just dont see a move that puts us at their talent level except for getting really lucky in the draft and picking up a sleeper late first or 2nd rounder like a Joker or Dray level player to round out the team. We are great and the Js run has been historic but there isn’t a path for us to match the elite teams talent levels by trading our way to it we are gonna need to get real lucky and have have some complimentary pieces fall into our laps.
