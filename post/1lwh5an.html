Observations:

  • TIM DUNCAN +10000: By far the most all-time. LeBron for his whole career is +8659; even he’s not breaking this record. The next closest Spur is Manu Ginobili (+6429).
  • CHRIS PAUL is the only leader for two teams (Clippers and Pelicans/Hornets).
  • MICHAEL JORDAN only played two seasons for the Bulls in this timeframe. He went 161-43 and won both championships, playing every game. Luol Deng is 2nd at +1529; he actually leads Jordan in the regular season (+1496 to +1416).
  • IMMANUEL QUICKLEY is the only bench player to lead a team (LOL Knicks, but seriously, why didn’t he play more?). Jalen Brunson is only 44 behind; he should pass him immediately barring catastrophe.
  • MARCIN GORTAT is the only player on this list without a significant NBA award, unless you count his player of the week in Dec. 2015. Quickley made all-Rookie and Christie made several all-Defense teams.
  • Charlotte was actually quite respectable before bolting to New Orleans. They made 5 playoffs in 6 seasons from 1996-97 to 2001-02, winning 3 playoff series with a revolving cast of characters (the LJ/Mourning squads only won 1 series in 2 appearances). Since their 2004-05 rebirth, they’ve only made 3 playoffs, losing in the first round each time. The player with the highest plus-minus since 04-05 is Jeremy Lamb (+229).
