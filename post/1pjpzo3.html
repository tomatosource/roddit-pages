Aside from Wemby, People might be underrating other Spurs Young Core

Spurs fans, and even other NBA fans knows and consider the Spurs is Wemby’s Team now and in the future.

But people might be underrating their other young core players too.

Fox is good. But obviously, he will not fit the timeline in the future imo.

Spurs might trade Fox and Vassell (toegther with picks) in the Future for a veteran star. Who knows.

But I think the Spurs young core is not just all about Wemby. Spurs did a great job scouting and drafting Castle and Harper.
