Ancestral Bond should be the baseline mechanic of totems instead of charges.

I loved totems in PoE 1. Probably my favorite build archetype. But in their current state in PoE 2, I would never consider using them. The whole point of totems is that I want the totems to do everything for me. They’re literally just stationary turret minions.

So, instead of having to generate charges so you can SOMETIMES have a totem, why can’t the totems work exactly like minions? You reserve spirit to increase how many totems you can summon. So, just take the Ancestral Bond keystone and make it the baseline mechanic for totems. No charges.

If you can have a minion build where your minions do everything for you, why can’t I have a totem build like that? Why make the use of totems so painful? I hate the feeling of having to mix 2 different playstyles, so that 1 of them can work. Apologies if people have talked about this here before.
