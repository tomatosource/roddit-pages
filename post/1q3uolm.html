What to do with Anfernee Simons

I think a month ago most everyone in this sub was very much ok with a trade that sent Simons out for a big. He hadn’t really shown anything other than the occasional 20 point game. I’m not sure if it’s just me, but what I’m seeing from him now has made me reconsider whether trading him is the right option.

He currently has the 3rd highest net rating on the team. He is one of the league leaders in +/- since the beginning of December. And he’s shooting 38.5% from 3, which is a number I think he still can improve on by a good amount.

But that’s not why I’m making this post. For as good as the numbers look, they don’t tell the whole story. Defensively he has made tremendous strides. Multiple times a game I find myself saying that he just made a great play on that end. He’s actually rebounding now too and not being lazy at all. And he’s spacing the floor better and being a more willing passer on offense as well. These were all the complaints about his game coming in, and suddenly they are turning into strengths.

He’s 26 years old and just beginning to enter his prime. I could be wrong, but I think he’s a great fit for this team and I think that playing next to Tatum especially would help him take another step on offense. Am I alone in thinking this?
