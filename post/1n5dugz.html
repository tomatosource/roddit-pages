Crafting has gotten better, but it still has a ways to go

I love crafting in PoE. I have over 6k hours in PoE 1, and my favourite part of that game is the crafting. I’ll usually play a league until I make a few absurd crafts for my end build, then quit. I end up usually with 1-3 self-made mirror-tier items by the end of a league.

I didn’t have super high hopes for PoE 2 crafting going into 0.3, but the introduction of perfect essences, perfect orbs, and desecrated crafting have bumped it up from a 2⁄10 to a respectable 5⁄10

I’ll start with the good:

  • Perfect transmute and aug let you more easily fish for a good 1 mod magic item to start a craft

  • Essences going from magic to rare let you then get a decent 2 mod item

  • Desecrated crafting gives you another affix you can reroll infinitely. It also helps a ton that the omens for abyssal crafting are much more accessible.

  • Perfect essences are almost metacrafting. Given they augment predictably, they can be used to manipulate either prefixes or suffixes reliably.

  • Fractures are cheap enough that you can work on a fractured base item pretty much as often as you want

  • All this together, the barrier of entry for crafting has gone down quite a bit. So far, I’ve managed to make a decent 450 DPS Gemini bow with onslaught and +4 attack skills for under 2 divines. This almost feels like a PoE 1 craft.

It still is far from perfect, however.

  • Bases are still annoying to get. The first step in many crafts (getting a perfect mod on a magic) is very RNG, and there’s no real technique to it - you slam the perfect orbs and pray, then throw away the item when it’s done. On one hand, this does maintain the value of good bases, but it also means you waste a lot of time buying them. Async trading makes this better, but it’s still awful.

  • That ties into this complaint: no good way to craft magic items. We’re starting to have some ways of manipulating rares, but magics are still one and done for the most part.

  • Omens in general are still way too rare. There’s not a single sinistral annulment omen available in 0.3 trade yet, and we’re on day 3 of the league.

  • Still very limited metacrafting options. We can manipulate prefixes/suffixes semi-deterministically with perfect essences, but only for the handful of mods they give. We can reroll one mod, but only for the desecrated modpool. Once you leave these designated areas, you’re back to just rerolling without any way of fixing the odds (minus sinistral/dextral omens, which are way too rare for 99.99% of the playerbase to use).

  • All this means is that, while early and (some) midgame crafting has an appropriate barrier of entry, most midgame and almost all high tier crafting is still disproportionately expensive.

So while it’s obviously still not there yet, I think the additions this patch are definitely a step in the right direction. It’s still not quite at the point where I’ll want to carry out some ambitious project yet, though.
