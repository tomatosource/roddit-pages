What do you make of these recent comments by Brown and Tatum?

Jaylen Brown obviously deserves a ton of credit. He’s playing the best basketball of his life. He’s added elements of his game that just weren’t there in previous seasons. He was never this elite from mid range. He’s flourishing as the top option

He and the team have exceeded many reasonable expectations. Not even Kobe could do this in his 3 seasons between Shaq and Pau.

Brown has made several remarks that essentially say he believes he’s the best two-way player in the league, he believes he doesn‘t get enough respect, the league has some conspiracy against him, etc

Then today the comments “I’ve sacrificed over the years for us to be a championship-caliber team. Now we’re seeing what I was sacrificing. Being at the helm, and us being 2nd in the east, versus last year we finished 2nd in the east. It’s almost been no drop off with five players that are essentially gone”

It’s hard not to hear comments like that and not come away thinking it comes across like he’s saying JT was holding him back and the team is just as good without him…

That’s actually an opinion that has been held for years by Tatum‘s haters. The idea that Brown was the team’s real best player, Tatum was holding him back, etc.

It would be easier to brush that off if Tatum also wasn‘t on a podcast today saying he thinks about whether he should return daily. He mentioned the team is playing so well and has fear about ruining what is working.

What do you make of that ? Personally this season feels 100% like the 94 Bulls where Jordan retired, Pippen played the best ball of his life (went from a 57 to 55 win team), made 1st team all-nba, and started talking about how the team didn’t need Jordan to four-peat. Pippen, historically underrated, certainly deserved credit. Many so-called Superstars fail to ever lead a team to 50 wins, but he proved he could do it. Pippen was helped a bit that season by guys like Horace Grant stepping up and the addition of young Toni Kukoc

It feels exactly like that to me. Brown leading the team to 50+ wins, 1st team all-nba. Likewise he’s getting huge help from guys we had zero expectations of. Queta looks like a real starting center. Walsh, Garza, and Hugo are making a real impact. Pritchard and White are both stepping up.

In prior years when Tatum played without Brown the team remained at 60+ win team, but when Brown played without Tatum they were basically .500. That suggests, to me, that this season is less about Brown, secret superstar, having been held back and more about Brown having taken a leap this season while Joe has found gold in swapping old/injured (Horford, Jrue, Zingis) for young/energy (Queta, Walsh, Hugo, Simons)

With Pippen, he fell short in the playoffs that year and followed it up with a very disappointing/humbling season until Jordan returned and they started dominating again.

i really would prefer we fast-forward through the “Brown gets humbled, realizes he needs Tatum“ portion of this and Tatum returns this season for a deep playoff run. The team has a new system that is working with this new roster. I can only see Tatum slotting in and helping.

TL;DR - The comments by Brown suggesting this year is proof he was being held back, and comments from Tatum saying he isn‘t sure he should return given how good they are playing has me a bit bummed. Whats your thoughts?
