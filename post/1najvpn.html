Feedback: Game is amazing, but current map layouts suck

Played 80+ hours this league, and I’m done.
Had an AMAZING time, wish I could keep going, but gotta get back to real life.

The only two reasons I enjoyed it so much were:

  1. Abyss League
  2. New crafting currencies

GGG, please keep following this formula, adding new stuff like this makes leagues feel fresh, and I feel like POE2 is headed in the right direction with the current crafting state.

Crafting

The new currency ranking system makes acquiring valuable white bases actually feel rewarding. It’s a solid currency sink for the great/perfect basic currencies (augments, transmutes, etc.).

What really stood out is how interconnected everything feels now. The great/perfect basics tie into league-specific currencies you can combine for stronger crafts, with their own ranking system and purpose (Rituals, Abyss, Essences). Even Breach catalysts with the new omen of catalyzing have a proper role in crafting.

  • Abyss desecrating is absolutely INSANE. Probably should be nerfed and limited to desecrating-specific mods only.
  • Overall, crafting feels like it’s heading in the right direction. From picking up exceptional bases &gt; magic/rare &gt; fracturing &gt; targeting prefixes/suffixes &gt; building a 6-mod item… it’s semi-deterministic, streamlined, and just FUN.

Endgame

Progression? Honestly, it sucks. GGG already said they’ll address it, so I won’t waste too many words on towers/citadels. Just hoping next league feels better.

Mapping

This NEEDS to be addressed ASAP. Current map layouts? AWFUL, Like 99% of them.

Expedition, Breach, Ritual: All of these are circle-based mechanics. But the maps? Narrow corridors everywhere. It just doesn’t fit. Playing those mechanics in tunnels feels terrible: backtracking, broken flow, clunky design. Just a bad time…

When you get a Breach/Expedition/Ritual in the perfect open spot vs. stuck in a cramped corridor… the difference is night and day.

It’s one of the fundamental pillars of an ARPG: THE GRIND
99% of players spend the majority of their time mapping.

If map layouts were actually amazing, the game would feel 10000% better. I honestly wouldn’t even care for towers, or chilled ground. I would look forward to every new map and continue the grind.
Currently every map feels like a maze, we had the same issue in POE1 lab and why players hated the lab, because the layout sucked.

Every new map I open, I’m look forward for the Abyss, because that mechanic doesn’t care about map layout.
But honestly, I’ve skipped so many Rituals, Expeditions and Breaches just because I know I’ll have a bad time running them. I don’t even bother backtracking anymore.

And since mapping is what I do 99% of the time in endgame, that really kills the fun.
