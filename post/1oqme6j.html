This is my first league, and third character. crafting got me hooked. after making nearly a dozen wands, I finally hit most of what I wanted. what do y’all think?

rule10:

(1) find a wand with +5 skills, and a lower-tier wand with % spell dmg (not hybrid mana)

(2) recombine wands, so it takes the higher tier base and snags the lower tier % spell dmg - your odds should be sitting close to 50%

(3) with these two tags, you greater exalt (or perfect), omen dextral exalt, omen of homogenize exalt, and omen of greater exalt - this gives you 2⁄3 good tags. it can hit crit hit for spells, crit dmg bonus, and cast speed. after making a dozen of these, i realized cast speed must be higher weight because i hit it every time i do this.

(4) now that you have your suf filled, and an undesirable pre, you omen of the abyss with sinistral crystalization to add abyss mod and remove the undesirable pre.

(5) Use a jawbone to desecrate. Before you do this, consider an omen of liege or whatever kind of desecrate mod you are targeting… don’t reveal.

(6) use a homogenize exalt, omen of greater exalt and a greater exalt (or perfect) to get 2 pre. This can hit gain % ele like mine, and or % spell dmg.

(7) Once you have two pre, you then reveal the desecrate for something good.
