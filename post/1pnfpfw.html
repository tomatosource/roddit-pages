Bahlak (tornado bird boss) needs adjustments.

This boss has been pretty unpopular since it released, with flaws in both its number balance and its design. That being said, I think it has a design flaw that goes beyond personal taste and starts to raise questions of “How did this make it into the game?”

That design flaw is in the tornado attack. Not the core concept of the boss becoming invulnerable and spinning itself into a whirlwind (as controversial as that attack is), but the fact that the tornado lingers after the boss finishes the attack.

This disproportionately punishes certain builds (melee) by often preventing them from dealing damage until the tornado expires.
The tornado is both large and opaque, and the boss really likes to hide inside of it. This makes the boss nearly impossible to see during the tornado despite it still being able to use other attacks, which is a big no-no in a fight that often requires the player to position and move very specifically to avoid dying.

It’s a fundamentally unfair mechanic that forces you to approach the boss as a pure stat check because you can’t dodge attacks you can’t see.

The *Vision* of players having proper boss fights falls apart when interacting with their mechanics is not only inefficient, but significantly more punishing than trying to just blow it up before it does anything. This will probably always be the case to some degree, but bosses with no visibility (or other design flaws like super long invulnerable attacks) will accelerate it in the opposite direction much faster.
