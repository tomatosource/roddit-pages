When did Dame stop yammin on people?

Damian Lillard is his for like 3-5 season or so used to dunk on people a lot more than some may realize. And they were pretty nasty dunks. I mean dude was in the dunk contest 2014 I believe. Then all of a sudden he just didn’t dunk like that anymore and became one of the greatest shooters of all time.

When and why did that happen? I don’t recall him having a major injury around that time that zapped his athleticism. His Achilles tear was recent. It’s always intriguing to me when I see highlights of young Dame and he’s just yammin on people because I sometimes forget how athletic he was. And he’s widely known for his shooting so I bet a lot of young fans who didn’t watch him back then don’t even know he used to get up like that.
