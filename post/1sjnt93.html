All NBA Games Ranked on Importance

You can celebrate, u/AlmostHereButNot, as this is the final part of this series. I will have 3 tiers. One is game doesn’t matter for either team. Another is the game matters for one team and the last group are games that matter to both teams.

Games that Don’t matter for either team

Wizards at Cavaliers

Suns at Thunder

Pelicans at Timberwolves

Games that matter for one team

Magic at Celtics

Potential playoff series preview and Celtics fans don’t want that. The Celtics have clinched the 2 seed so this game doesn’t matter for them but the Magic need to win this game to have a chance at getting a top 6 seed. They need to win and the Raptors to lose to get it.

Pistons at Pacers

The Pacers want to lose this game so that they have the second best odds to get the number one overall pick while the Pistons have already clinched the one seed in the Eastern Conference so this game doesn’t matter to them.

Hornets at Knicks

This game is meaningless for the Knicks as they are stick as the 3 seed in the Eastern Conference but this game matters for the Hornets. If they win this game or the Heat lose, they clinch the 9 seed in the play-in tournament.

Grizzlies at Rockets

This game is meaningless to the Rockets but the Grizzlies want to lose this game at stay at the 6th best chance to get the number one overall pick.

Nuggets at Spurs

This game is meaningless for the Spurs but the Nuggets if they don’t want to face OKC in round 2 needs to win this game or hope that the Lakers lose. The Nuggets are resting most of their starters so maybe they don’t really care.

Warriors at Clippers

The Clippers need to win this game and the Trail Blazers to lose to get the 8th seed in the Western Conference play-in tournament but this game is meaningless to the Warriors because they are stuck at the 10 seed in the play-in tournament.

Games that matter for both teams

Hawks at Heat (Game of the Day)

If the Hawks want the 5 seed they either need to win this game or hope that the Raptors and Magic win. The Heat, in order to get the 9 seed, need to win and for the Hornets to lose. The Heat are way better at home than on the road.

Bucks at 76ers

The 76ers need to win and for the Magic and Raptors to lose to get the 6 seed in the Eastern Conference while the Bucks want to lose so that they have the highest chance possible to get the number one overall pick.

Nets at Raptors

The Raptors need to win to get a top 6 seed and a Magic and Hawks lose to get the 5th seed while the Nets want to lose and the Pacers to win so that they get a chance for a higher draft pick.

Bulls at Mavericks

The Mavericks look to keep the 6 best lottery odds while the Bulls want to keep the 9 best lottery odds to get the number one overall pick.

Jazz at Lakers

The Lakers want the 3 seed in the Western Conference while the Jazz want to have the 4th best lottery odds so they are hoping that they lose and the Kings win.

Kings at Trail Blazers

The Trail Blazers want the 8 seed in the Western Conference play-in if they win or the Clippers lose. The Kings want to lose and the Jazz to win so that they have the 4th best odds to get the number one overall pick.
