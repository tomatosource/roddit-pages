The big problem isn’t that the game is too fast. It’s that being slow is unrewarding and nerfing speed is not the solution.

This whole game was meant to make combat feel rewarding and exciting. But the whole gameplay loop of path of exile one, and currently 2, relies on the fact that killing more monsters is better. Until this very basic fact is changed, none of the discussion around speed is worth a damn.

If I got the loot I was getting for most of the league but had to fight at act 1 speeds, I’d have quit already. If GGG want us to stop running at 100 extra movement speed, give us a reason to gear for tankiness instead. Make prefixes on boots more valuable and incentivize us to use them.
