This has been by far my luckiest league ever, here are my drops

- Garukan’s Resolve on my first (quest) Sacred Reservoir

- Rakiata’s Flow on my second (first after quest) Jade Isles

- THREE Kalandra’s Touch, all from Ritual rewards

- 200 Divine Orb drops
