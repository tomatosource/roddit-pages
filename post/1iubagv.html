Jokic stats in the clutch this year: 56.7 FG%, 54.5 3P%, 1st in FGM, 3rd in assists, 1st in rebounds, and 5th in total points.

These are some pretty absurd stats for clutch time, which is defined by the NBA as when there is a 5 point game or less with under 5 minutes to go. And if you watch his games he can pretty much get a very high percentage shot in the paint any time in the clutch unless he is doubled, and that shot is far more likely to go in than jumpers by guards like SGA or Curry. And even though his shots are not flashy, he is probably the front-runner for Clutch player of the year award.
