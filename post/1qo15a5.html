GGG should try to add skills/supports that allow for more interactions between existing things as well.

There’s a lot of discussion about how it would be great if more variety could be available for builds, with more skills, more build-defining nodes, less downsides to notables, etc. But I think, without adding too much, a lot more variety and fun could be made available by allowing/creating interactions between these preexisting skills/things.

A really simple example of what I’m talking about: I was trying to plan a cold-focused Titan build. However, if you want to go with a shield and mace, then I’m pretty sure the only real way you could do this would be Polcirkeln and doing some stuff with that.

But there could be some interactions here, that don’t add too much new, but reuse the existing skills in a different way. What if we could somehow empower Earthshatter and similar skills (like Shield Wall) to instead create an ice-infused spike rather than a regular rock spike? Just a small change, but it open new avenues for builds.

For example, I came up with the following skill as a proof of concept of how this could work:

Glacial Cry
.
Perform a Warcry, Empowering subsequent Melee Attacks and Attacks that create destructible structures if there are enemies nearby. Chilled Enemies in the warcry’s area cause Ice Fragments to be created around them and cause additional Ice Fragments to be created when they die.
.
Empowers one Attack per 10 enemy Power in range, counting up to 50 Power.
Empowered Attacks Gain (30-49)% of damage as extra Cold damage.
Empowered Attacks that create destructible structures Convert 70% of damage into Cold damage and cause Freeze buildup as if dealing 50% more Cold damage.
Empowered Attacks have 25% reduced Attack speed.
Triggers Ice Fragments on Chilled enemies in range and again on death.
.
Effects from Quality
Empowered Attacks Gain (0-10)% of damage as extra Cold damage.

Maybe its good, maybe its horrible, maybe its OP, idk. But it’s just an idea for relatively small changes that can reuse preexisting skills and effects but in new ways.

Sticking with warcries, another example could be one that empowers attacks making them arc bolts of lightning on hit, and also converting any Jagged Ground they create into Shocked Ground.

Or, just making Blackflame Covenant work with all skills, not just spells.

Idk, I just thought that this whole thing was perhaps important to mention. I think the game is pretty good, and I think embracing this more can help make it even better.
