The most recent team with 25 CG was the 1997 Expos

The most a team has had since 2010 is 18 by the 2011 Phillies; last team with 10 was the 2016 Giants; and the most this decade is 6 by the 2022 Marlins.

Anyway what I really want to talk about is that Pedro Martínez won the 1997 NL Cy Young Award with 13 CG and a 1.90 ERA (219 ERA+) but did not lead the Expos in shutouts. That honor belonged to Carlos Pérez who had 5 to Pedro’s 4, and a 3.88 ERA. Worth nothing this was only Carlos’s second season, too, which means he had more shutouts through 51 starts (6) than Max Scherzer has in his career (5).

Pérez was traded to the Dodgers with Hiram Bocachica and Mark Grudzielanek in July 1998 for Ted Lilly and Vlad’s brother Wilton Guerrero, among others. He last pitched in 2000, retiring with 8 shutouts, which would be the 3rd most among active pitchers.

Speaking of brothers, Carlos’s older brother (by 14 years) Pascual pitched 11 seasons, 4 shutouts, one All-Star Game, but is best remembered for missing a start for the Braves in 1982 because he ran out of gas circling I-285, Atlanta’s perimeter, trying to get to the stadium. The next day, he pitched 9 scoreless innings before allowing a 10th inning home run to (not that) Brian Giles, then gave way to Steve Bedrosian, who retired one batter to become the pitcher of record and after the Braves scored twice in the bottom of the 10th got the win.
