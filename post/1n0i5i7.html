Anyone else noticed how the new bosses might play differently in 0.3.0?

Has anyone been paying attention to what Kamil said about the “tribal” music and those boss concept arts with the tribal theme? Pair that with the teases from the 0.3.0 previews and livestream, plus from some folks from the Gamescom Boss Rush… it really feels like the new bosses are shifting away from being pure HP sponges.

I’m starting to think we’re going to see more interactive mechanics tied to the arena itself. Stuff like lighting or extinguishing campfires mid-fight, triggering totems that give buffs or debuffs, or even multi-phase encounters where the boss behavior changes depending on how you interact with the environment.

If they can pull it off, maybe this could break away from the old “chip the health bar down” style? And it could make fights feel more like a mix of puzzle-solving and execution? Honestly, if it was real I’d be pretty excited for that direction.
