Hugo Gonzales is the one, he definitely deserves more playing time

Even in this young stage of his career where he has yet to develop confidence in his scoring, he *still* impacts every possession he plays on both ends of the floor

Offensively, he has terrific spacing which makes things nice and smooth for scorers, and he often looks to get the offensive rebound. He also has a good zip on his passes and he doesn’t seem very prone to turnovers. Defensively he constantly gets to the right spot and has an uncanny ability to switch, guard different positions, and recover for terrific plays. His abilities and tenacity on every play are why he leads all rookies in plus/minus by a long shot and also is 2nd or 3rd on the team despite averaging only 15 minutes. Even in this game you look down and he somehow has +19 in just 13 minutes, the only guy who had a higher +/- than Anfernee Simons.

And on top of all this the most important thing of all is that “he’s only 19!!!!” With Tatum out we should remember not to lose sight of this year as a valuable year for player development, and among all of our young guys Hugo Gonzales is taking the clear lead for long-term value in my opinion
