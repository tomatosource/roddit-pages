Now that we are a quarter of the season in, which players have surprised you the most so far?

I thought he might struggle with the transition from the team that drafted him, with which he won multiple champions to a team that sometimes felt like a rival in the Western Conference, the Dallas Mavericks. But he has been really good so far. His defense is solid, his shooting is okay for the most part, including the games where the shots simply aren’t falling so well and he seems to enjoy being in Dallas.
