Pitchers You Forgot Were Once Late-Inning Relievers?

You’ve got two types of pitchers in baseball: starters and relievers. But sometimes, pitchers we usually see as either starters or early relievers have that one season, or even a couple seasons, where they emerged as a late-game weapon. So, which of these do you think was the most random, and why?

For example, Drew Smyly was once a set-up man for the Tigers, and he had a dominant 2013 season with them, going 6-0, striking out 81 batters, and racking up a 2.37 ERA, in 76.0 IP. I would’ve gone with John Smoltz’s two years as the Braves closer, but I feel those were a lot more well-known.
