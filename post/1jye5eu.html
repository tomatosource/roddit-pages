Ogham’s Unique Strongbox waystone trick

If you find an Ogham’s Unique Strongbox on one of your maps, leave it until you have killed all other rares on said map, then come back to the strongbox and do it. The rare inside will count as the map completion kill on the the first kill and will drop the corresponding waystone.

The funny part about this is that all subsequent kills of said rare will also drop a corresponding waystone.

Happy farming, exiles!
