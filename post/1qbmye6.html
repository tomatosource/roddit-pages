Chaos slamming as endgame crafting feels bad

This is the first league where it feels very easy to reach a point where the only realistic way to upgrade your items is through heavy currency crafting, especially Chaos slams.

Example of crafting a physical weapon:

  1. Buy a level 82 base with T1 phys%.
  2. Make it rare.
  3. Desecrate.
  4. Divine until max T1 phys%.
  5. Fracture to hit T1 phys%. If it fails, go back to step 1.
  6. Annul until only two affixes remain.
  7. Chaos spam until you hit T1 added phys.

The problematic part is step 7. It can easily take 1000 Chaos orbs to hit the desired affix. Every single orb requires you to stop and read the item. After hundreds of attempts, everything starts to blur together. You have to actively focus just to avoid falling asleep or accidentally overwriting something important.

To be clear, Chaos slamming is not the only way to reach the desired affixes on this base. Similar results can be achieved with combinations of Orbs of Augmentation and Orbs of Annulment. However, those approaches lead to the same core issue: a long sequence of repetitive clicks with very little actual decision making.

This kind of crafting feels insanely mind numbing and I think it needs to change. Since the outcome is already deterministic in the long run, maybe there is room for a better system. Instead of spamming 1000 Chaos orbs, there could be a very rare item or currency, roughly as rare as naturally dropping 1000 Chaos orbs, that lets you choose a single T1 mod. The cost would be similar, but the process would be far less exhausting and error prone.

Curious what others think. Do you enjoy this kind of crafting loop, or does it feel like unnecessary friction?
