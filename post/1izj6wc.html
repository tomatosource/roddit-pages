Ways to give more player agency in the atlas without reworking the system

Full disclosure; I like the current system. I enjoy the variety, more than I enjoy having an entirely deterministic. I realize however, that this is not everyone who plays this game. LUCKILY, we already have a system baked into the atlas that can solve this!

Idea: we have 4 slots on a map node with which to place things to modify the map. Currently, all that we are waystones. A much simpler solution to overhauling the whole system is to add a currency which alters the biome or map of a selected node on the atlas. Also add a currency which marks the map as “completed” but moves all the modifiers and content to the nearest connecting node.

I think this creates a very good middle ground. People like me who just like to alch, and go can continue to do so. For people who like to farm a few specific maps and a few specific content can just add currency to the atlas and set up a nice cluster of maps with everything they want and then run them.

TLDR ; Add currency to change the selectec atlas node map/biome, and currency to mark node as completed & move content to a neighboring node.
