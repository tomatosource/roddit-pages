Best Defenders to Never Make an NBA All-Defensive Team

Defense has always been my favorite aspect of basketball, and the subject of all the best defenders throughout NBA history that were never selected for an All-Defensive Team is a topic of discussion that I’ve been very interested in lately. Here’s a group of 10 players that I came up with listed in chronological order from their dates of birth…

- Ron Harper

- Kendall Gill

- Shawn Kemp

- Rasheed Wallace

- Kenyon Martin

- Shawn Marion

- Jermaine O’Neal

- PJ Tucker

- Kyle Lowry

- Aaron Gordon

Out of that group, who do you think was the best defender of the bunch? And are there any other names that come to your mind when thinking about players that were exceptional defenders but never received an All-Defense selection? I’m looking forward to hearing your thoughts.
