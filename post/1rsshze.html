Bam Adebayo is the only player in the top 20 all time scoring games to not be named on an All NBA Team.

Of the 20 highest scoring games of all time in NBA history, there have been 13 players (Wilt has 8 in the top 20).

  • Wilt - 10 All NBA (7x 1st team, 3x 2nd team)
  • Kobe - 15 All NBA (11x 1st team, 2x 2nd team, 2x 3rd team)
  • Luka - 5 All NBA (5x 1st team)
  • David Thompson - 2 All NBA (2x 1st team)
  • Damian Lillard - 7 All NBA (1x 1st team, 4x 2nd team. 2x 3rd team)
  • Donovan Mitchell - 2 All NBA (1x 1st team, 1x 2nd team)
  • David Robinson - 8 All NBA (4x 1st team, 2x 2nd team, 2x 3rd team)
  • Elgin Baylor - 10 All NBA (10x 1st team)
  • Joel Embiid - 5 All NBA (1x 1st team, 4x 2nd team)
  • Devin Booker - 2 All NBA (1x 1st team, 1x 3rd team)
  • Michael Jordan - 11 All NBA (10x 1st team, 1x 2nd team)
  • Pete Maravich - 4 All NBA (2x 1st team, 2x 2nd team)

Bam is the only player who has no All NBA selections in his career. About 100 media people vote on the All NBA and he has never really had a chance any year, his best was probably 2021 or 2022 but even then there were 4-5 players who didn’t get selected that had more votes and a better chance (also note that in 2024 they started being positionless).

2025: 1 single vote for 2nd team
2024: 1 vote for 2nd team, 4 votes for 3rd team
2023: 9 votes for 3rd team
2022: 4 votes for 2nd team, 16 votes for 3rd team
2021: 5 votes for 2nd team, 17 votes for 3rd team
2020: 26 votes for 3rd team

This was just pretty interesting to me that he was the only player in the list. Bam is still a good player and a very good defensive player but very interesting to take note and analyze. Regardless if you think him scoring 83 points was good or not he does have the record and should be congratulated. So good job Bam!
