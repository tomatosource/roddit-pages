[OC] How often do the defending champions open the season facing the team they beat in the Finals or Conference Finals? How often does a team ruin Ring Night for the home fans? Not nearly as often as you would think.

I was curious how the potential storylines for Ring Night and how the NBA has historically handled it, and there were a few things I found that were kinda interesting.

For starters, opening the season hosting Ring Night at home wasn’t even really tradition until the 2000s if you can believe it. So many defending champions actually started their season on the road without getting to hang a banner up. It is also historically very rare to have the defending champions play the team they beat in the Finals or even the Conference Finals to open the year. The defending champions currently sit with a 63-16 record (79.7%) winning by an average of 8 points.

Cheat Sheet for Confusing Franchise Names

  • 1948 Baltimore Bullets are not the Baltimore Bullets that became the Washington Wizards. The Washington Wizards franchise lineage begins in 1964; the 1948 Baltimore Bullets are their own franchise.
  • The 1949 Washington Capitols are also not the Washington Wizards
  • Rochester Royals = Cincinnati Royals = Sacramento Kings
  • Syracuse Nationals = Philadelphia 76ers
  • Philadelphia Warriors = Golden State Warriors
  • New Orleans Jazz = Utah Jazz
  • Seattle SuperSonics history is separated from Oklahoma City Thunder history for the purpose of the chart
  • Chicago Stags are not the Chicago Bulls
  • Buffalo Braves = Los Angeles Clippers
