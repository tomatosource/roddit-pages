Thoughts on Homogenizing Omen alternatives

I think Homogenizing Omen puts too much power in to the hands of crafters (ignoring for price/rarity). It allows for items to be stacked along a major axis (Attack/Defence/Resistance), making for targetable and deterministic-ish crafting. However. the idea of targetable or modifier manipulating options for craft does seem appreciable, Homogenizing just isolates the modifiers TOO MUCH.

As an alternative, I would suggest a selection of Omens to replace Homogenizing. Omens that rather than force modifiers to a specific range (Homogenizing) would prevent a certain set of modifiers. An example would be an Omen that prevents Attack/Caster mods, Defence/Resistance or prevents Mana/Life mods. I would lean towards a pairing of blocks that are general a suffix/prefix. And then of course make it so only one of these Omens can be used at once.

I think it would allow crafting to deprioritize certain undesirable mods without making the crafts deterministic.

Just a thought, not a hill to die on. What do you think?
