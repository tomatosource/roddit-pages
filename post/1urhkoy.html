PSA for Grand Exped: Avoid Propagating Oath Rune

If you’ve ever encountered a grand remnant that spawns immortal mobs that keep spawning endless waves of mobs, and then get no loot at the end, this is all because of Oath Rune.

After patch 0.5.4.b, Oath Rune is just a massive time waster, continuously spawning mobs that do not drop loot and creating immortal enemies till you kill these waves. I’ve had Power empowered Oath Runes that have spawned at least 200 rare monsters. The monsters also spawn fairly scattered because of sheer lack of space on screen so it takes forever to kill these things.

Avoid propagating Oath Rune. It can be a bit tricky, because every Perfect Chaos recipe has Oath Rune, but an early Oath Rune can easily add 10 minutes to a grand expedition for 0 reward.
