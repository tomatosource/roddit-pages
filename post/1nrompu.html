Pleeeeease let us set the price of a whole tab for instant trade

Don’t get me wrong, I LOVE instant trade and it’s probably the biggest QOL improvement to either POE, ever

The one thing I am really missing is the ability to set the price on a whole tab

Like my whole trade strat is to have a tab for stuff I think might be worth, and just set the price on the whole thing to 2 div. Once that tab fills up, I lower the price of that tab to 1 div and start filling another 2 div tab. Rinse and repeat, cutting the price of the oldest tab in half each time you fill another, until it’s down to however low you think isn’t worth your time, then just shred whatever’s left for the shards and then you have a fresh, empty tab.

Apart from that I have one separate tab for individually-priced, high-value items that I know are big money.

I like this method because I can’t possibly keep track of every build that’s popular and what gear they use. Instead of dicking around trying to price each item, just toss whatever looks like it might be valuable into the tab and lower the price until it sells.

Will you get the absolute most value out of each and every item? Nah, probably not. BUT you save all the time that you would spend fuckin around researching prices so you can just go crush more maps instead.

However, trying to use this method while needing to set the price on every single item is so far beyond tedious. Even if you’re just setting everything in the tab to the same price, it’s sooo many clicks just doing the same thing.

I’ve given up on doing this with 1 and 2-slot items because it’s just awful.

GGG pls let us set the price on a whole Merchant Tab &lt;3
