Bane traded for four 1sts. If that’s the going rate, should the Celtics in Derrick White?

Bane is about to turn 27, White is about to turn 31. So there’s a meaningful age gap, but not enormous.

Bane is signed for four more years, White for three more years but he’s cheaper.

The Grizzlies just received four 1sts and two role players for Bane.

Could White fetch three firsts on the open market? Or two plus a younger role player that the Celtics could develop?

My entire thesis is how do the Celtics continue building long term around Tatum and Brown. Those two are the cornerstones. The surrounding pieces are going to have to change, and the Celtics will have to get some cost-controlled pieces in there.

I love Derrick White and think there’s an argument that he’s been the third best player on this team over the last two years – whenever the game got a little unstructured, he could control the temperature when the ball was in his hands. Great defense, ball handle, and three point shot.

But he’ll be in his age 32 season with just two years left on his deal once Tatum is back, and if you assume it takes Tatum a year to get his sea legs back, that’s the age 33 season.

Jrue is the easy guy to think about letting go, he’s 35.

Many people want to trade KP, I’m in the minority opposed to it. Health issues yes, but he’s a unicorn.

IMO, D-White is the true question of the offseason.
