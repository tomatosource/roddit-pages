It’s time to vote on the hypothetical USA vs. WORLD series – Game 2 (of 7)

The United States has won the last five gold medals in men’s basketball, clearly re-establishing itself as the premier basketball powerhouse in the world. But at the same time, the U.S. does have an advantage in terms of its population. With over 340 million residents, it’s usually the most populated country in the field.

Would the U.S. still be # 1 if we accounted for that disparity? There’s no easy way to answer that, but there is a fun way. A hypothetical 7 game series – with the other countries playing against a portion of the U.S. that matches their population. The winners of each game will be voted by the fine folks here.

In Game 1, the world struck first, as Team Serbia and Nikola Jokic roughed up an undersized Team Indiana.

In Game 2, the country of Greece (population 10.4 million) will go to war with the state of Michigan (population 10.1 million). It’s the birthplace of democracy vs. the birthplace of the assembly line! Greece vs. car Grease! Let’s get it on! (Note: for the “Michigan” roster, we’re just including players who were born in the state – not ones who played high school or college there.)

Which team wins this matchup? Study the rosters and vote in the comments below!

######TEAM GREECE

PG: Nick Calathes, Kosta Sloukas

SG: Tyler Dorsey, Vassilis Toliopoulos

SF: Kostas Papanikolaou

PF: Giannis Antetokounmpo, Kostas Mitoglou

C: Georgis Papagiannis

team strategy: Like Serbia in our first game, Team Greece is going to have to rely on its superstar Giannis Antetokounmpo. Around him, he’ll have a supporting cast of his two naturalized guards (Nick Calathes and Tyler Dorsey), his two brothers, and a bunch of dudes named Kostas. Is it fair for the team to have naturalized guards when the U.S. states can’t? I don’t know – take it up with the U.N.

######TEAM MICHIGAN

PG: Ryan Rollins, Monte Morris

SG: Devin Booker, Jaden Hardy

SF: Miles Bridges, Emoni Bates

PF: Draymond Green, Kyle Kuzma

C: Isaiah Jackson, Xavier Tillman Sr.

team strategy: The state of Michigan has a storied basketball history with Hall of Famers like Magic Johnson and Chris Webber, but this current edition feels more limited than expected. They do have an elite defender in Draymond Green to battle against Antetokounmpo. Offensively, Devin Booker will have to carry the load. Whether the team has enough may depend on what you think of their (empty calorie?) scorers Miles Bridges and Kyle Kuzma, and their talented but unproven youngsters like Ryan Rollins and Isaiah Jackson.
