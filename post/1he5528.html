Here’s some data showing that the most efficient offenses aren’t the most prolific 3P shooters - except for the Celtics

I was messing around with NBA stats and wanted to check a widely held assumption that, given the era we are in, the best offenses are all heavy 3P shooters. To do this I looked at the % of each team’s FGA that are 3PA and compared that to their points per possession.

When you look at the data mapped out, outside of the Celtics - who seem to be playing a completely different game from everyone else - 7 of the 8 highest efficiency offenses don’t rank amongst the highest 3P shooters (Cavs, Knicks, Grizzlies, Mavericks, Kings, Thunder, Nuggets). Interestingly, the higher 3P share does seem to separate the mid teams from the bad teams, but when you look at the best teams, the trend reverses.

I also had a look at how 3P share correlates with PPP and the relationship is weak (0.28) and basically disappears when you remove the outlier Celtics (0.09). Also for those interested, PPP unsurprisingly has a strong correlation with win% (0.8).

It is pretty mad just how much the Celtics skew in this metric, with 56% of their shots being threes, way ahead of anyone else (2nd place Bulls are at 48%).
