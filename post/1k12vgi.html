Here’s my POE2 0.2 journey as an average gamer.

As the average POE player, I struggled but finished the campaign before nerf, started grinding endgame, nothing drops which made progression even slower, now being gated outside of pinnacle bosses before I learn how to juice map loots and have better gear. /played 125-ish hours in. /deaths is 200+ times.

Sorry no TL;DR version, as it will just be another rant post :s

For the league start:

I hyped up this game for my friends, we had 6 people the day 0.2 launched (2 of 6 with POE & ARPG experience), 3 of us finished the campaign, the guy with no APRG knowledge was fighting bosses like he’s playing Monster Hunter on max difficulty, die 30+ times each boss to learn boss mechanics before asking for help. And we couldn’t help him either early on (until we over leveled a bit) because if any of us joined the boss would have even more HP to kill. The other 3 either quit a few days in and falling behind too much, or didn’t bother to learn anything just waiting for us to boost him, they soon quit at the early endgame grind because they don’t know how the game works lol.

I learned as I was playing through the game, that most of the things you get from act 1-3 will have no use in the later stage of the game, upgrading your gear seemed to be the only way to be “stronger” early on without many skill gem levels / sockets, and passive points; the amount of gold I struggled in the beginning was laughable upon reaching endgame; the lower level skill gems have no use later either… So if new players at the first 3 acts could get more useful gear drops and uncut gems, they will be able to test around before getting stuck at a boss for hours then quit the game.

They were simply left with no resources to fix their noob mistakes.

For endgame:

Couldn’t catch up with the currency inflation speed, which almost made trade upgrade pointless - I spent a lot of hours playing each day, didn’t get a piece of gear that is better than what I have at the moment or worth selling for a lot of currency (it’s also a huge learning curve for new players too, to know what’s worth more than other pieces, while you’re farming and need to save time for inventory management).

The few exalted orbs I farmed can only buy me a &lt;10% or &lt;5% improvement from trade. It’s just a bad loop for normal players.

I have dropped 2 Divine orbs, 0 greater jeweler’s orb, 1 perfect jeweler’s orb from unique gambling strongbox- in 70 hours of mapping. Other than the drop rate being too low, I think one of the reasons is that the game did not teach me how to “juice” the maps for more loot.

The dilemma I was facing as a new player experiencing the endgame:

  1. More prefixes on waystones = better, fewer = no loot.
  2. More suffixes = more difficult and dangerous, fewer = no waystones.
  3. If I want to progress the endgame quests faster (do 15 corrupt nexus), I had to use easier waystones (often blues or 4-mod rare ones) which means no loot.
  4. If I want more loot I need to use much harder waystones to clear very slowly.
  5. *If I die in the process, I loose experience which set me back for the build progression.*

Along the way I learned how to avoid bad map types and bad modifiers (temporal chains yuck), how delirium and breach work, how to progress the atlas tree etc. But ALL FROM YOUTUBE, not in game. I have to watch multiple extensive videos to learn how things work which could be explained while playing. It’s already bad enough having to copy a build in order to play properly, it’s a bit too much that you have to watch how other people play the game first or you jeopardies your own progress by going slower and slower and eventually brick the game and quit.

If this was 2001, I could just sit in front of my PC and grind t12 maps to level 100 before starting to find those 15 nexus maps, there would be no such thing as “brick the game”. But those days are in the past, and we definitely need something that’s more modern paced.
