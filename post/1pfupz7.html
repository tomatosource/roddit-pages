I am wondering if people would have thought of him as a better pitcher had he played in a different era. I think pitching at the same time as Clemens, Maddux, Johnson, and Pedro Martinez skewed people’s expectations for what a great pitcher should look like. Pedro could be argued to be even higher just based on how good he was on a per start/inning basis (all the pitchers ahead of him by career WAR made more starts and are way ahead in terms of innings pitched). Do you think that Mussina would have more recognition for being a great pitcher if he pitched in a different era?
