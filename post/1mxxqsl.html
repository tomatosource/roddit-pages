Let’s cook up some endgame tower ideas for GGG

Just some brainstorming on a rainy weekend. We know from the interview they’re still working and are not set on how to solve the issues with mapping in its current state.

Here are my suggestions. Trying to keep it simple and in line with the current Atlas. No complete redesign.

Biomes

Embrace biomes. Make them much larger. I’m thinking 5-10x biome size.
One might assume this is bad for variety but I think it’s actually the opposite. Having a different set of maps available to you every once in a while is better than feeling like every other map you run is one you don’t like.

Additionally with over 20 new maps added (and more to come) the variety within each biome is much larger.

At the same time this also allows players to target certain biomes and run the layouts they prefer. More on this below.

Towers

Always generate a set amount of towers within each biome. Juice from towers (and tablets) affects every map within the biome. This gets rid of the overlaying tower range hunting.

Possible big endgame notable: “Your biomes generate with an additional tower”..?

Generally, having an explorer section on the Atlas tree that influences Atlas generation is something I’d like to see. Reduced chance of finding x biome / citadel, more/less corruption, anomalies etc., “Your biomes generate with more maps”..

Irradiated fragments

Killing the boss in an irradiated map drops an irradiated fragment. These fragments can be put in together with a waystone on a tower to increase its irradiation level.

You can get a total of four irradiation levels on each tower you run. Three from irradiated fragments, one from putting a T16 waystone. Of course this will increase the difficulty of the tower by raising the monster level.

On completing an irradiated tower the irradiation affects all maps within the biome, increasing their explicit modifier magnitudes by x%.

Additionally completing an irradiated tower allows you jump to another tower within the same biome or the nearest tower within each adjacent biome.

Essentially this means you can quickly explore the Atlas by tower rushing but this time without having to run low juice boring maps in between. You will be hunting your preferred biomes and map layouts to juice them as you like.
At the same time it’s not ‘free’ since you have to run irradiated boss maps for the fragments.

This will naturally become more available to you as you progress through endgame, become more powerful to face irradiated bosses and collect their fragments.

(I think each tower map should have a boss you have to fight before activating the tower and each irradiated fragment should add an additional unique monster or rogue exile to the fight but that would require redesigning the tower maps to have a boss arena)

The Atlas

We need a simplified map. With the suggestions above a simplified biome map comes to mind. Far more zoomed out. Showing biomes, their type and towers within them. Also allowing you to do the irradiated tower hopping.

Switching to the ‘real’ Atlas map, the one we know, is what you do to run the maps. Since biomes are much larger each of them will feel like its own little mini Atlas from PoE1.

There should be a reward for completing it entirely.

What are your thoughts? Would you like to see something like irradiated fragments? Share your own ideas and thoughts on how to make mapping feel better in PoE2.
