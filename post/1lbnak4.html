Top 100 NBA Tier List

What do you think of my all time top 100 tier list? I’m sure conversation will be civil lol. This was a pretty fun little exrercise. I learned a lot about NBA history especially some of the old guys.

Rough criteria 

  • 53⁄54 (98%) of the players with 6+ All-NBA/ABA seasons
  • 60⁄67 (89%) of the players with 8+ All Star seasons  
  • 49⁄54 (91%) of the players with 120+ career WS
  • 58⁄66 (88%) of the players with 0.65 career MVP vote shares 
  • 56⁄60 (93%) of the players with 2100 career playoff points 
  • 17⁄22 (77%) of the players with 7+ All-Defense seasons 
  • I try to weight all basketball played NBA, ABA, Olympics, College, etc.
  • I try to weigh overall importance to the history of basketball, so if a player does something meaningful not on the hardwood (e.g. legendary broadcaster, breaking color barriers, etc.) that’s more impressive than an extra All-NBA selection imo
  • Winning solves all. Gotta do it in the playoffs to really move up the list
