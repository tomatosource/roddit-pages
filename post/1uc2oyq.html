In the 90’s the #1 seed from each Conference met in the NBA Finals 4 times. Since 2000, this has happened only 3 times.

Since 1990, the #1 seed has met a total of 7 times in the NBA Finals.

  1. 1991 - Bulls vs. Blazers
  2. 1996 – Bulls vs. SuperSonics
  3. 1997 – Bulls vs. Jazz
  4. 1998 – Bulls vs. Jazz
  5. 2000 – Pacers vs. Lakers
  6. 2008 – Celtics vs. Lakers
  7. 2016 – Cavaliers vs. Warriors

Interestingly, these look to follow a trend of being highly rated/classic Finals looking back. Since 2000 however, this doesn’t seem to be as common.
