The returning champion hasn’t made it out of the second round since 2019.

In 2019 the defending champion Warriors lost in the finals to the Toronto Raptors. The Raptors would be bounced in round 2 the following season, and since then no other champion has made it past the second round.

2019 - Warriors lose in finals; Raptors win championship

2020 - Raptors lose to Celtics in round 2; Lakers win championship

2021 - Lakers lose to Suns in round 1; Bucks win championship

2022 - Bucks lose to Celtics in round 2; Warriors win championship

2023 - Warriors lose to Lakers in round 2; Nuggets win championship

2024 - Nuggets lose to Timberwolves in round 2; Celtics win championship

2025 - Celtics down 3-1 to Knicks in round 2; champions TBD

Of course Boston still has a chance to complete the 3-1 comeback this round, but if they don’t it will be the 6th straight year the returning league champion hasn’t made it back to their conference finals.

Additionally, none of the teams to eliminate the returning champs since 2020 have gone on to win themselves, and only 2⁄5 made it to the finals. Playoff parity has been incredible in the 2020s so far after a 2010s that was predominantly owned by a handful of teams.
