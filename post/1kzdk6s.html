Since Dawn of the Hunt, poor performance on my Xbox Series X

Before the big patch, I did run into the occasional issue with broken builds messing up the game, but overall it ran pretty well. Since the big update though, I’ve been having way more problems—glitched graphics, missing assets, and just straight-up unplayable visuals. I think most of you know that moment when you juice up a T15 map and the whole screen just goes completely insane. It’s practically impossible to play when that happens.

On top of that, I often get stuck on loading screens when entering maps. I have to restart the game to even get back in, which is incredibly frustrating and really breaks the flow.

Unfortunately, nothing has improved over the past few weeks, even after taking a break and coming back. At this point, I think I have to hang up the game for now—I just don’t have the patience anymore, even though I really enjoy the core gameplay loop. It’s a shame, I love this game…
