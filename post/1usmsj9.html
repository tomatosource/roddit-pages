If the Charlotte Hornets win the 2026 Summer League, they will become the NBA’s first “back-to-back champions” since 2018

They would also (by extension) become the first repeat champions in this time period. It’s also interesting to note that no team has won both a summer league championship and an NBA season championship in this time period either.
