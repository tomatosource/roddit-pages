So I am doing my 4th and trial currently lvl 84. I bought a Major boon from the merchant because I had never received one prior and was curious.

“Gain 2 Sacred water when you take Damage from an Enemy Hit”

This seems insanely weak. I could be wrong but that’s not where it ends. Not only do you have to do the one thing you don’t want to be doing (getting hit and losing honour) but it is completely overcasted by my affliction “Leaking Waterskin” which causes me to:

“Lose 20 Sacred Water when you take Damage from an Enemy Hit”

I can see how maybe someone has a build to gain insane amounts of sacred water and regen all their honour at a fountain, but why is this still a major boon at all? It’s very backwards and has little to no rewards unless you are missing like 4 sacred water for a merchant purchase for a shop you know you will receive and even then you can just kill monsters and get 3x-5x more per kill.
