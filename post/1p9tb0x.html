Who are the best/worst pair of ROY winners?

I was looking at ROY winners by year and was impressed by a lot of the pairs. Ichiro/Pujols, Carew/Seaver, Allen/Oliva

Which is the best pair? Which is the worst? Which is the most “unbalanced”, where one guy went on to become a legend and the other is mostly forgotten?

My picks:

Best: Ichiro/Pujols in 2001. Just two of the most iconic players of the 21st century and both owned the league for a solid decade

Worst: Coghlan/Bailey in 2009. A couple of older rookies at 24 and 25 and neither made a lasting mark in the league. Bailey was a great closer for the A’s for a few years and Coghlan did have a mini resurgence in Chicago and won a title there.

Unbalanced: Jeter/Hollandsworth in 1996. The captain became one of the faces of the league and I admittedly didn’t know who hollandsworth was before looking at all the winners. To make matters worse the runner up in the NL was Édgar Rentería who had a solid career and a couple of iconic postseason moments on the path to two World Series titles.
