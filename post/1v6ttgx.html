Better off selling Aldur’s

I’ve been giving expedition another shot because I do actually enjoy it. I know I can make better currency with my other strategies but I’ve gotten bored of them… so the last few days I’ve tried aldur’s 5 times. Here are the results:

  1. 5 GE with the best thing being one with guaranteed 6 runs remnants. The rest were +1 7 remnant or range or something. I barely broke even.

  2. 5 GE with the best being a single with guaranteed 5 runs remnant and then a bunch with +1 7 or 8 remnant. Luckily I got another aldur’s so I made a little.

  3. 6 GE with the best being two with guaranteed 6. Unfortunately the layout of one was total trash but the other I got another Aldur’s. Didn’t make much currency.

  4. Goofed up and used it on a logbook with 6 rumors but 2 of them were bosses so I got 4 GE with… 1 having guaranteed 5 rune remnants. Would have made more had I sold the aldur’s.

  5. 5 GE with no guaranteed 5, 6, 7, etc remnant. Nothing really good came from this. Would have made more selling the aldur’s. I actually made more from one of the maps running between GE when a Dialia’s support gem dropped.

For the first 3 I was using tablets with effectiveness, monster rarity, and rare monsters. The waystones I was using were 70% effectiveness T15 and T16. I’m running jado. The RNG aspect of aldur’s is beyond frustrating. I’ve made significantly more currency doing ritual or breach without having to decipher rumors and try to plan the perfect path.

I also know how to propagate runes and typically get at least 4-5 purple and opulence before the bigger runes…just not in aldur’s. I do fine in normal GE which is what I’ve decided to runs The rng gods just aren’t giving me anything good when I use aldur’s. I’ll have a good line up of 5⁄6 rune remnants and then a 7⁄8 on the other side of the map that I can’t reach unless I skip all the 5⁄6.

Anyone else feel this way?
