I come from the future playing the December league. Props for not falling into 0.3’s design pitfalls now on the Druid design!

Playing Druid in 0.4 is absolutely a blast!

At first, I was worried that Nature Infusions would be yet another remnant resource only generated with skills locked behind specific base types, or that we’d have to manually move close to them. I’m glad it’s not necessary to spend any valuable passive points on remnant pick-up radius nodes. Having them automatically collected makes the gameplay so much less of a chore!

Then, I saw Trailwild being available for ALL Druid ascendancy classes. Increased movement speed and skill speed are such important stats (for both power and gameplay flow) that it not being only available for 1 ascendancy class actually helps to balance the overall balance. If only one asc class had access to it, would the ascendancy class choice even be an actual choice, ‘ight?

And talking about gameplay flow, now that I’m in the middle of winter, I was fully prepared to slowly ice glide everywhere. I’m an un-cha ined grizzly Druid wanting to claw and paw! Thankfully, the total rework of chil led gr ounds and tem poral cha ins didn’t force me into a winter hibernation! Sleep is something for the 1% of Chonk players, or whatever the 1% of Chronis do in the time it takes for Eye of Winter to become ready.
