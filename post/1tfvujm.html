Catha’s Balance build; am i missing something?

So The Catha’s Balance makes your companions deal 60% of your weapon as added damage and i was very excited to make a build with 3 badass companions (boss, bear spirit and another beast) while still dealing some dmg myself but i found several issues that made me think its a terrible idea:

  1. You start at Huntress path, meaning your only defensive nodes available are evasion and deflection, wich means you must go dexterity in order to equip evasion items wich is kind of mandatory considering all the companions nodes are close to them aswell. This is bad if you want to use a 2h mace or a high dmg weapon bc you are far from armour & strength nodes (see point 2).

  2. Both dex weapons are kind of 1 handed weapons (spear & bow) wich is bad bc you want as much dmg as possible in your weapon for your companions, they do not benefit from weapon’s base attack speed as far as im aware, ideally you want a 2h mace or similar. Bow has a considerable part of its dmg coming from the quiver and spear’s have high base attack speed.

  3. Most of the companion nodes require you to be in the companion’s presence in order to gain extra defenses & dmg wich makes ranged weapons fairly useless.

  4. There’s one very strong node that makes your life leech also apply to your companions and also the fact that the bear also breaks armour and has life leech itself apparently, wich makes it very important to be full physical dmg.

  5. The only other 2h option would be quarterstaff, but again they have high base attack speed and lower dmg aswell to make up for the high base crit chance, on top of that you would have to invest in int points, and only go for physical dmg skills while ignoring extra physical dmg bonuses since you want to take nodes that are usefull for both you and your companions.

  6. I may be wrong but i dont think you can equip idols on weapons that are not a sceptre, wich means losing the bonuses from Idolatry while also getting penalties for equiping regular runes instead.

All of this makes me think that its not viable to make a build that lets you fight alongside your companions and not just basically be a zookeeper bot that just watch as the minions do all the work. Whenever i theorycraft a build i try not to rely on uniques, lineage supports nor time lost jewells in order to get the build online. Am i missing something here?
