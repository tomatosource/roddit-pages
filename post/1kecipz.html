Volatile plants, specifically from Smothering Rituals, seem different from before patch 0.2.0g

I’m saying “seem different” because there’s a chance I’m imagining things, but I am fairly certain that this is an undocumented change. I’ll list some of the glaring changes I noticed and maybe you fellow exiles could tell me if I’m going crazy or if you have experienced something similar.

  • The orbs are bigger, which would be great for visual clarity, but the visuals are not indicative of the actual “hitbox” of the orb needed to trigger the explosion when in close proximity to them. You actually need to take a step into the “bloom” of the orb to trigger the explosion, which I feel wasn’t a thing before.

  • They also seemingly trigger on terrain collision now? This is especially bad since there is certain terrain in the game that fits in perfectly with the traversable ground, which is made worse by delirium fog, particle effects etc.

  • In Smothering Rituals these orbs are spawning at an annoying pace, like sure it’s manageable but it’s just not fun. A poorly timed attempt to detonate the orbs by walking through them can cause deaths from a random light stun, or from the orbs exploding on terrain collision as I previously mentioned. Four to five orbs can pile on top and shotgun you, and unless you have Chaos Innocation it will destroy your ES pool even with high chaos res.

Please let me know if I missed the part of the patch notes where they mentioned this change, or if I have just gone insane. Maybe I just randomly started sucking at the Smothering Rituals, that could be a possibility too.
