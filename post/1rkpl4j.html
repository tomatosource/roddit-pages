Even a huge big like Drummond could be a good fit, maybe with not much dribbling. I don’t really think McCain has the pop to create by himself against most defenders (nothing wrong with that, few do) so I am curious - what type of shots did he even get this season in Philly? And did he get a more steady diet of DHOs at the start of last season?
