Grip of Kulemak rings can get absolutely bonkers. A specially for minions.

Fellow minion (and non only minion) players I highly suggest taking a look at those rings as they are capable of resolving couple issues minion builds tend to struggle with otherwise. Also given how limited minion options for scaling are this thing may be one of the few non standard ways to scale our builds.

Mana Regeneration - solved by on kill within presence effect which would otherwise require us to wear grave binds and some source of mana on kill.

Spirit which can obtained via Increased Spirit Total 10% (this is quite insane a specially for Infernalists =&gt; it’s the highest increase spirit source in the game). Even for non Infernalists it’s a 40-50 (or 70-75 if you roll both Inc Spirit and 20-25 spirit if you have more than 200 stat affix) on addition to potential cast/attack speed/mana recovery/other useful affix.

Cast Speed and Attack speed for US AND OUR MINIONS.

Cast speed per spirit (this is great for Djinn Minion Builds).

Onslaught/Arcane Surge on Minion Death.

Any many other https://poe2db.tw/us/Grip_of_Kulemak#GripofKulemakModifiers
