Multiple-time champions from 2019-2026

This is an 8-year span of parity where no franchise has won multiple championships.

There are, however, players who have won more than one championship in this era.

Here is the list:

  • Danny Green - Raptors 2019, Lakers 2020
  • Kentavious Caldwell-Pope - Lakers 2020, Nuggets 2023
  • Jrue Holiday - Bucks 2021, Celtics 2024
  • Alex Caruso - Lakers 2020, Thunder 2025
  • OG Anunoby - Raptors 2019, Knicks 2026

Let me know if I missed anyone.

One team that didn’t have a multiple-time winner in their roster during that span was the Warriors 2022. But those guys are not first-time winners either.

Siakam had a shot at 2025. Kornet had a shot in the past finals, 2026.

LeBron James, Jaylen Brown and Nick Nurse all can join this list if they do something special in the upcoming season.
