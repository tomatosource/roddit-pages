Started a 2nd Char today and something major occurred to me immediately:

So my litch is lvl 92 and I’m enjoying the endgame but I always get the itch to play something different so I start a 2nd character. Obviously having done the campaign on my 1st character I have all the gear currency runes etc I need and can basically twink level my character pretty quickly - apart from one thing, this “thing” is the reason every new character is such a pain and makes you absolutely hate the campaign - level maps.

Why on earth don’t GGG just allow all campaign maps to be visible on a 2nd character (or when you get to endgame) ? It would make the whole process so much easier like - 1000% easier ! I know people will say “can’t you remember the map layouts??” Hell no I can’t and I’ve levelled 15 characters since release.

Running round trying to find exits or poi’s is so unnecessary on a 2nd character, I don’t need to look for loot or chests I just need to blast a bit, kill the bosses and get to endgame where I can easily make up any missing XP in mapping.

Please GGG consider making maps fully visible for 2nd characters - I can use the currency exchange in my hideout but not see where I’m going…….
