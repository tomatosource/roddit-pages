Carl Braun is one of the first tall Point Guard in NBA History

Before Oscar Robertson, Carl Braun is one of the first tall Point Guard.

Listed at 6’5, He started his career as a Shooting Guard in the 1947-48 season as a Knicks. Back then shooting guard height was typically smaller than it is now. There was 6’0 and 6’1 SG. Paul Arizin was shorter than Braun with him listed at 6’4 and play at Small Forward.

During the 1956-57 season, Braun switch to the Point Guard position. While there is no article i can found on why he switch position, it seems that the Knicks have drafted a SG and so Braun became the team PG.

The Knicks previously have All-Star PG Gene Shue but was traded early that season. Dick McGuire was also an All-Star PG that the Knick have but this season he only play 16 minute, presumably off the bench and he will join the Piston the next season. This season they traded for All-Star and 4x champion PG Slater Martin before trading him to the Hawks after 13 games. All 3 PG will still be an All-Star after they leave the Knicks.

Carl Braun would have his career high assist per game the next season with 5 APG. He would finish his career still playing PG and winning a champion with Boston.

According to the NBA he is credited for coining the term swish for the sound of basketball going through the net.
