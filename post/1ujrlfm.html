Opinion on JB situation from a basketball fan who’s moving to Boston

I’m moving to Boston so I’m going to become a Celtics fan regardless of the outcome. I’m from a non-NBA market, so the title that Jaylen Brown helped bring doesn’t mean much to me (I also don’t think he deserved finals mvp - Tatum’s offensive gravity was entirely discounted from the conversation, but different topic)… I’m starting from scratch with the Celtics.

With that said, the Celtics have a very likeable team… with one glaring exception.

JB has regularly been one of my least favourite stars in the league. Sure, his play style and impact has something to do with it (while I think it would be exaggerating to call him a ball stopper, I do think he suffers from possession-ender-itis).

But his off court conduct has always been eye rolling.

  • The pseudo intellectual nonsense
  • How he externalizes internal frustrations - publicly litigating your organization over and over again is not good leadership
  • He has repeatedly prioritized his personal narratives over team focus

This isn’t even delving into the individual comments he’s made that deserve specific criticism (most fall into one of the above buckets), just the directional issues.

Tldr; I’m excited to arrive in Boston and become a huge Celtics fan either way. For both on court and off court reasons, I hope JB is gone.
