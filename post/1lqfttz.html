King Of The Mists is disgusting

King of the Mist is literally the worst boss in the game. Orloth and Xesht are fine, the fights are challenging but fair (well, kind of, since both of them have at least one completely bullshit attack), but King of the Mist is just a joke. DOT-dealing blobs, exploding purple plants, poisonous exploding frogs, movement-blocking curses, and my personal favorite — a tornado that often blocks your view of the boss. Who the hell designed this? Either you have a build that can one-shoot the boss down fast, or you’ll just get flooded with effects and in 90% of cases won’t even know what killed you. And even if you somehow manage to kill him, in 50% of cases some exploding frog will waddle over and blow you up after the boss is dead. I don’t know, this boss is just a bad joke.
