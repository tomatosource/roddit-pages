Build Idea: The Unholy Duo (Minions+Support)

Just a Disclaimer: I am heavily spitballing with this idea and I just want to know if some of you guys can estimate of we are cooking or if we are cooked. :D

First of all: We are a duo and have played 0.1.0 and really pushed ourselves. I have played a Cursebot+Rarity bot and know how to build an Infernalist support character which can boost damage significantly. It goes without saying that my partner has played spark in 0.1.0.

Now we come to the fun part, my partner (gaming husband lol) has displayed interest in playing minions this coming patch. And I thought that the normal cursebot might not be as strong (especially when they nerf the curs effectiveness on magic, rare and unique mobs) instead, I want to buff his minions. This is where the Infernelist comes into play:

Bricks and Mortar of the idea:

What my Friend will need:

  • The new passive note: Blackflame Covenant. This Keystone converts Fire Damage to Chaos Damage, with this damage contributing to ignite chance and magnitude, and ignites inflicted with Fire Skills dealing Chaos Damage.

Bonus: -Any form of Unholy Might for minions (Especially after seeing that new awakened Support)

What my support brings to the table:

-50% Block with the unique shield

-All Damage counts as ignite magnitude

-Withering Presence + Wither selfcast for Single Target

-Temp Chains, Enfeeble as fat Blashemy, (Chaos Curse as self Cast for single target)

~ 50% Damage as extra Fire damage (chaos for him)

~ 2-3% Life regen (He plans on playing Lich)

And some other minor fun stuff from the passive tree. I may use the Yix amulet for more damage but that might be a stretch now since I don‘t know how much presence area I need) In this idea, I will use the Sceptre from the Arbiter of Ash and use Bloodmagic to make my Infernal Bullshit 0 (which made it count as high and low in 0.1.0, could not find changes/fixes to that), thus giving my allies the extra damage to fire all the time. In the End, I should give at least more than 50% of damage as extra fire (thus chaos damage) to all of his minions.

What I know: My support will be fairly tanky even though I use Life, but I will recoup a lot and take some of my damage as fire damage. The damage I give is nothing to laugh at, since it scales well with the damage of my carry. 50% Block on minions will probably be very good.

What I would like to know: Based on what you read, would poison make sense if he is running with me since it would stack, other than ignite? Is there an easy way to make ignites stack? Have you guys any idea of what minions to use when it comes to our (very roughly) planned playstyle? In general, does it sound like we have something on our hands here or are we cooked?
