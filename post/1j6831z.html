Timberwolves Win 106-104 vs. Heat Despite No Player Topping 15 Points

Maybe this isn’t so rare but I can’t remember the last time I saw an NBA team win with no single player topping 15. Maybe some stat wizard can check this for us? Like this seriously might be one of the most balanced NBA games ever played, with all 7 Timberwolves who eclipsed 13 minutes scoring between 12 and 15 points (on between 9 and 13 FGA). Ant had an off night and we tried to throw the game like 4 separate times, but Mike Conley came through with 15, tying for the team lead with DDV and Naz Reid.
