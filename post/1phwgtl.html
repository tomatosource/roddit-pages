3 Problems with Shields I Hope are Fixed By 0.5

Problem 1: Stun Threshold

While it’s not an issue deep in the late game when you can just one shot bosses with shield wall and chain explosions from resonating shield to screen clear, for the other 90% of the game it feels like you can only keep your shield raised for a few seconds in most combats before getting heavy stunned. It feels like even when stacking armor, resistances, health, and stun threshold, the duration you can keep your shield raised is super low. It’s so bad that I often find myself facing away from hits in combat because I’d rather take the damage and keep channeling than accumulate the stun threshold. This is thematically the exact opposite of how using your shield should feel.

Additionally, while you have 100% block chance against hits in front of you while your shield is raised, you are essentially de-incentivized from building additional passive block chance because you gain less benefit from raising your shield. Building block chance currently makes you no better at blocking from the front while your shield is raised. This anti-synergy for building block chance on builds that center around raising your shield feels like a mechanical and thematic failure.

Solution: A way to fix this would be inspired by a change they made last patch where while your shield is raised you can’t evade attacks and instead your evasion gives you a change to not accumulate stun threshold from a hit. I think block chance should get a similar treatment where while your shield is raised, hits against you from the front check against your passive block chance and don’t accumulate stun if you would passively block the attack anyway.

Problem 2: Shield Defenses Passive Nodes

The passive nodes that grant additional defenses based on your equipped shield’s defenses seem to be tuned very low. There seems to be no reason to pick one of these nodes since they always add less defenses than any % defense increasing nodes. They also don’t add damage to shield skills either because they don’t actually increase the defenses of your equipped shield, just the defenses you get from it.

Solution: Either increase the scaling of these nodes to bring them in line with % defense increasing nodes or change them to actually increase the defenses OF your equipped shield not just from your equipped shield so that you at least get an offensive benefit for picking them.

Problem 3: Lack of a basic shield striking skill

There is no basic/default skill that just allows you to attack with your shield.

Solution: Make equipping a shield either grant you raise shield as well as a default attack skill like “Shield Strike” that allows you to swing your shield to deal damage in front of you, or make quickly pressing and releasing the raise shield skill perform its shield bash effect while holding it down causes you raise your shield as normal. If you perform a block during the shield bash animation, it could just deal more damage or stun damage, rather than being the only way to trigger the bash as it is now.
