I can’t put it to words, but PoE 2 feels so addicting to play

I’m a die hard PoE 1 fan that recently moved over to PoE 2 and off the bat I was ready to list out everything I hated from the map sizes, lack of end game, awkward movement/collison, etc.

Yet despite all of my complaints I can’t seem to keep myself from logging in lol, idk what GGG did but the game just feels so satisfying and fun to play and it’s hard for me to figure out what makes it so addicting but it just is.

If anyone is on the fence about trying out PoE 2, I feel like this is the perfect league to jump onboard.
