PoE 2 Needs Auto-Swapping between Controller & Mouse/Keyboard

For 1.0 I really hope that GGG adds auto-swapping between mouse/keyboard and controller.

After spending the last couple weeks testing build ideas and running campaign I decided to try playing the game on my xbox controller. I was very surprised how AMAZING the combat felt on controller, GGG really did a excellent job at this and my first thought was that I want to continue playing on controller from now on. It felt that great.

It was not long after I realized how difficult inventory management is on controller (maybe I am just bad at controller and not used to all the inputs). It was so difficult for me that it basically cancelled out the excitement I had for actually playing the game and blasting monsters on controller.

The obvious solution to this for me was the thought of adding that auto-swapping feature; I think some other games have this feature but maybe the implementation for it is impossible at this point of development for PoE 2? I would not know.

Anyways, that’s about it. Just hoping this feature gets added. Maybe some other people have thought the same?
