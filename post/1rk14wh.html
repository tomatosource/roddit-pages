[NBA PR] San Antonio Spurs guard Dylan Harper and Charlotte Hornets guard-forward Kon Knueppel have been named the Kia NBA Western and Eastern Conference Rookies of the Month, respectively, for games played in February.

NBA Communications:

San Antonio Spurs guard Dylan Harper and Charlotte Hornets guard-forward Kon Knueppel have been named the Kia NBA Western and Eastern Conference Rookies of the Month, respectively, for games played in February.

February stats:

Dylan Harper (Spurs): 12.5 PPG; 4.9 APG; 55.4 FG%

Kon Knueppel (Hornets): 21.5 PPG; 5.7 RPG; 48.5 3FG%

Note: Kon Knueppel is the first player to win Rookie of the Month 4 months in a row since Paolo Banchero in 2022-23, and is on track to be the first to win for all 5 months since Luka Doncic in 2018-19.
