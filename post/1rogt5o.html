Can People Stop Proposing MLB Expansion Into Puerto Rico ITs beyond a Bad Idea

As a Puerto Rican and an Avid Baseball Fan I see this way too often and it just annoys me. Sure it sounds great if you don’t think about but Lets run by why this is such a stupid Idea

  1. Its Fucking In a debt crisis and these billionaire owners wouldn’t even entertain the idea fronting the bill for a new stadium

  2. Its Current Infrastructure is inadequate. Hiram Bithorn stadium in San Juan Makes Fenway Park look massive

  3. MLB Season Takes Place during Peak Hurricane season. They don’t play Caribbean Ball in the Winter just to avoid competing with the MLB its bec its the Dry season and much safer. the MLB already has 2 Florida teams they constantly have to worry about rescheduling you think they want an Island one too

  4. Locals cant afford it. Median Income is under $30K Local fans don’t the the fiscal capacity to afford 81 games of baseball

So to summarize No gov’t capacity to fund a stadium, current infrastructure is lacking, weather is a nightmare and locals cant afford to watch.
