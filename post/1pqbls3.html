Quick campaign tip for leveling and ssf

In Act 4 there is a guaranteed Abyssal Depths right before Heart of the Tribe. You can repeatedly farm this for tons of currency, omen, XP and other drops, then blast through the interludes. The Abyssal Depths will generally have tons of rare monsters and 5-8 rare chests. To get to it quickly, take the heart of the tribe waypoint then walk backwards to the previous zone and look for a wide open area with the abyss icon on the minimap.
