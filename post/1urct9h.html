Who are some of the the most “efficient” passers of all time?

I know a lot of people used to love the A/T ratio stat to show how good a passer a player is. The problem is that it doesn’t separate the difference between a pass leading to a turnover or a different type of turnover (traveling, out of bounds, stolen ball, etc). So I thought to isolate specifically turnovers from bad passes (IE: pass out of bounds, inaccurate pass to a defender, etc)

So I started looking through some players to see who has comparable A/BP (assist to bad pass ratios) and it’s really interesting just how good Paul is compared to some other playmakers in NBA history. Data is only available from 1997 on so any player that played pre-1997, their data after 1997 will be taken. (IE: John Stockton from 1997-2003) . For some reason, bref not loading up certain guys so I’ll upload when they are like Steve Nash. The list consists of mostly people who have lead the league in assists or are constantly up there. Also contains some top 50 all time playmakers.If there’s any particular name you want, let me know and I can grab it. One name I wish I could grab data for was Muggsy Bogues who I believe would be at the top of the list or near the top if it was available. In his last 3.5 years before retirement (97-01), he posted a 5.4 A/BP.
