NBA games that have a similar legendary quality to 1998 Finals Game 6?

I’m young (22) and a new-ish fan. Last night I took a break from an extremely stressful study session to smoke, unwind, and watch some basketball. I went to YouTube and watched the entire game via that popular upload that I’m sure people here already know (the one that alleges to be shot in 4k). From what I can tell, this was the same film that was used in The Last Dance. I wasn’t alive in the 90s, and I have a tendency to be very interested in the past and attracted to vintage aesthetics.

I’m sure the weed was a factor, but watching this game felt fucking electrifying. I mean, it seriously felt like I was watching a movie. Hell, I felt like I was THERE in the Delta Center with these people. It all felt mythical, a “life imitates art” moment where the sequence of events feels more like a script. As someone who never got to truly witness this era, MJ doesn’t even feel like a real man who actually exists. And honestly, the rough nature of this game (some pretty terrible basketball actually) and the fact that its stars are very past their primes, kinda adds to the story.

Do any other iconic games come close to this energy?
