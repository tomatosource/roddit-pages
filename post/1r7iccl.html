How long does it take to go from a bottom 5 record in the NBA to a playoff team? Only about 3 seasons

Since we’re in Tank/Draft/Lottery discourse season, wanted to look into how long it takes teams to go from the bottom to the playoffs in recent years. Looked at how many years it took to make the playoffs after a team ended the season with a bottom 5 record in the NBA (NOT top 5 pick in the draft, going off record). Used following time frames just for recent simplicity, occasionally there will be a team that might have a season or two in the bottom 5 before a time frame and there are some extreme recent droughts if you expanded the view to last 20 years or so. For a simple and easier to compare cut-off though, we’re going 4 years before and 4 years after the 2019 odds change.

2019-2022

All 20 teams in the bottom 5 from 2019-2022 have since made the playoffs so it was a good range to start the analysis. On average it took 2.7 seasons to make the playoffs (if you include play-in as the playoffs, it becomes an average of 2.4 seasons). Some teams have been in the bottom 5 multiple times in this timeframe, so if you trim it down to only the first time a team appears it cuts the list from 20 teams to 13 teams and the average becomes 2.85 seasons after their first bottom 5 appearance. Only 3 teams took more than 3 seasons in the 2019 Cavaliers (4 seasons, 3 if you count the play-in), 2020 Pistons (5 seasons), and 2021 Rockets (4 seasons).

2015-2018

Since I used the 4 seasons since the lottery changed, also looked at 2015-18 for the last 4 seasons with the previous lottery odds. When looking at all 20 teams (including the repeats) the average was 3.05 seasons, when you trim it down to the first time a team appears in that timeframe it becomes 10 unique teams with an average of 3.7 seasons. 4 teams took more than 3 seasons in this time frame from their first appearance with the 2015 Knicks (6 seasons), 2015 Lakers (5 seasons), 2015 Magic (4 seasons), and the 2016 Suns (5 seasons).

2023-2025

Didn’t include 2023-2025 teams since the time frame is too recent. However, many of the 2023 teams are on pace to make the playoffs or play-in this season. If you wanted to know how the 2023-2025 bottom 5 teams are progressing

2023 Bottom 5 (first recent bottom 5 appearance, next playoff appearance)

  • Pistons (first appearing 2020, made playoffs 2025)

  • Spurs (first appearing 2023, on pace to make playoffs in 2026)

  • Rockets (first appearing 2021, made playoffs 2025)

  • Hornets (first appearing 2023, on pace to make play-in for 2026)

  • Trailblazers (first appearing 2023, on pace to make play-in for 2026)

2024 Bottom 5

  • Pistons (first appearing 2020, made playoffs 2025)

  • Wizards (first appearing 2024, not on pace to make playoffs yet)

  • Trailblazers (first appearing 2023, on pace to make play-in for 2026)

  • Hornets (first appearing 2023, on pace to make play-in for 2026)

  • Spurs (first appearing 2023, on pace to make playoffs in 2026)

2025 Bottom 5

  • Jazz (first appearing 2025, not on pace to make playoffs yet)

  • Wizards (first appearing 2024, not on pace to make playoffs yet)

  • Hornets (first appearing 2023, on pace to make play-in for 2026)

  • Pelicans (first appearing 2025, not on pace to make playoffs yet)

  • 76ers (first appearing 2025, on pace to make playoffs in 2026)

Conclusion

Obviously, a lot of factors are involved to become a competitor again that aren’t really factored in here such as big trades or superstar free agent leaving/joining. With all of the takes about tanking being so detrimental or a need to change the draft/lottery, I found it interesting that if a team bottoms out they are back in the playoff mix relatively quickly averaging just under 3 seasons. Although the sample of the new odds is still rather small, it appears the turnaround to become a playoff competitor has gradually sped up since the odds change
