Being a die hard fan of Warbringer (the unpopular cousin of Titan), I have discounted thorns away so many times and tried every build imaginable or seen in youtube with some rules. No spears, no jumping jack, no crying karens and no orbs! Just wanted a warrior that can stand and fight with macho weapons and not end up a half mage or son of wonder woman.

The problem with warrior is really weak critical combination for normal attack. With just one hand, abusing the melee+7 is also not as strong as a Titan with +14. The latter had the strength and health to afford holding two. One thing that has high critical on warrior….Thorns.

https://preview.redd.it/7pl2gtquegef1.png?width=1920&format=png&auto=webp&s=5b47a8cae5c1f983f4b167da2ec819657b95f5ee

Balancing health is the hardest when playing thorns, the pale helmet and briar boot are must have. I anoint Vengeance on amulet for the leech which is pivotal to survival. Hammer of faith for those extra phys + random shrine buffs + elemental protection. The key is probably Maligaro fine bracers in this build.

32k thorns damage? + Hammer of Faith random resistance blessing

Basically it’s all luck.
