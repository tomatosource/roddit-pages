Hauser needs to be replaced by Walsh in the starting lineup, and this team can’t have Luka Garza as their primary backup in the playoffs

There’s going to be a lot of debate over the next few weeks about Simons. I will leave that decision at the deadline for Brad, and you should all too.

However, it got swept under the rug yesterday because of the win, but this starting lineup can’t have Hauser in it anymore. They get off to terrible starts every game with him in it, they can’t defend or rebound. If they don’t put up points, they get blown off the court. Walsh has been playing well and needs to be re-inserted.

Also, I like what Garza bring to the table on offense. However, he is one of the five worst defenders I’ve ever seen wear a Celtics jersey. I’m not exaggerating, if he’s the Celtics’ primary backup in the playoffs, they are going to get KILLED on defense. Zero shot at guarding any real teams, they will constantly run pick and rolls at him.

Whether or not Simons is moved for a big, or Hauser, or whoever, they have to find a way to make Garza their 3rd center. That’s his ideal role, not as primary backup.
