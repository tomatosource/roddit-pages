The last Knives Out Mistery “Wake up dead man” features the infamous 11 to 13 Diamondbacks-Cubs from this year but messed up the timeline of the game (minor spoilers)

Spoiler: &gt;!the murder of the movie happened on Good Friday 2025, same day as the D-Backs-Cubs game. Benoit Blanc and his crew watch a synced-up version of the game with the camera feed of the murder scene to try to find a clue and move the plot forward.!&lt;

The movie features a video tape of the game showing a Ian Happ single off Corbin Burnes, Nico Hoerner in the box and this Carson Kelly home run in that order. The video tape has a timestamp and around 50 seconds elapsed from the Ian Happ single to Carson Kelly touching homeplate. However, the Ian Happ single happened in the 1st inning while Carson Kelly homered in the 2nd inning so more than 20 minutes between the 2 events in reality.

The movie also features an at-bat between Kyle Tucker and Merrill Kelly in the game on Sunday 2 days after, in the scene right before, and Drew Smyly inducing a flyout from Willi Castro and a glimpse of José Miranda in a game on August 7, 2024 when the character of Samson Holt is introduced.
