Why Magic Find or Item rarity on gear is a mistake in ARPG.

Hello Everyone,

Over the years I’ve seen plenty of people expressing how much they like or dislike magic find as a stat but rarely do they ever go in the details of why. This is what I want to do in this post and maybe spark a discussion.

Disclaimer: The problems I have with magic find cannot be balanced. it’s not a numbers problem so please don’t say oh but it soft caps at 100% etc. it doesn’t address my points.

  1. Let’s start with the biggest gripe I have with the stat : it doesn’t need to exist.

having items found tied to the difficulty of the content you are doing is a much better system and most games already do that. Usually people will express that magic find makes you think of a trade-off between character power and magic find.

In my experience it either creates a situation where the hardest content in the game is ignored in favor of just putting more mf on gear.

For example, the white tower map meta in poe 1 where people played MF tornado shot in white tier maps as it enabled them to equip every increased item quantity uniques.

Or it severely limits build variety where the few build who can afford to put the most Magic find on their gear while clearing the highest difficulty content are severely more efficient than the rest.

A good example of this in poe 2 is any build who is using unique rings for example is severely punished by the fact that they are losing up to 50% rarity for each ring slot.

Building you character power is already trade off after trade off, do I want to be tankier, faster or deadlier here? you ask yourself that question every time you choose your affixes on an item. Now this is my personal bias but I just don’t find making my character weaker to drop more loot in easier content an interesting trade off.

2. My second problem with the stat is that the games loot has to be balanced around it.

While I understand that it doesn’t represent everybody in favour of magic find. I’ve seen so many people talk about how they would be poor without magic find and that they already don’t find enough loot but they fail to realize that the baseline game would simply be balanced to drop more loot if magic find didn’t exist.

GGG for example has to balance around the fact that some ritualist can reach some stupid level of item rarity (which is what they did by introducing the soft cap).

Just look at Poe 1, due to how overpowered loot conversions were they ended up nerfing the base quantity of all league monsters, then they removed the good loot conversions and now we are just clicking boxes because monsters drop nothing.

That principle can also be applied to player magic find, if people can equip gear enabling them to drop 10x more loot, they’ll just make monsters drop baseline 7x less loot or something so that the stats feels like it does something but now you are just forced to run it because otherwise monsters will drop nothing. (just making it clear i’m not saying this is the current situation in poe 2 or 1 but i’m giving an example of how having the stat on gear in the game is a net negative).

3. While i don’t personally care too much about this sentiment, i’ve seen a lot of discourse about the FOMO (Fear of missing out) it causes.

Once I start thinking about it, magic find has to be the stat giving the worst FOMO feeling possible. it doesn’t give direct feedback of how impactful it is. so it means anything that happens CAN be attributed to it.

Let’s say you see somebody on a stream drop 10 divines from a single monster. the guy happens to have 40% item rarity. You have no proof it did anything but come on let’s be honest some of you would be thinking “I wonder if he would have dropped 6 divs instead of 10 if he had 0% item rarity” or “ Man if he has more rarity he would have dropped 20 divs from that mob.”

While I don’t think player FOMO is something that should be taking priority I believe player perception matter and it just seems to be another negative for the stat when I personally don’t feel like it brings a single positive.

I could probably rant for hours about the different implementations of Magic find in different games but I wanted to address the more fundamental problems I have with it.

For the people that took the time to read and engage. Thank you! may we all get the game we hope for.
