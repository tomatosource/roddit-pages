Not having any form of colorblind fixing is taking the enjoyment out of this amazing game.

Love it, been grinding for hours since I bought it during free weekend.
but this is really ruining any type of ‘fun’ experience for me after campaign/late game maps.

I have pretty severe Red-Green colorblindness and this game is HORRID at visual clarity.

just in general its already pretty bad at visual clarity but that is more a thing of the genre then it is its Devs.

But PLEASE implement some colorblind filters within the game itself, it isn’t a large or big thing to do and can be pretty much automated for 90% of it and having it makes a world of difference.

to put into perspective how bad it is.

the green forest maps i’ll walk into hills or walls that i visually can’t see, i have to check my minimap or just dodge in the opposite direction.

same goes for brown, grey, red, colored maps.

especially the realm in the Crux of Nothingness is insanely bad.

everything is the same color and just finding a path i can walk to, even if it is a dead end is already a challenge, its very hard to impossible for me to clearly make out floor from wall.

this issue started of small in the campaign but is now growing to be such a pain that it is actively diminishing my enjoyment of playing the game.

even tho the game itself is really fun and incredible.

next time you play, put this thought in your head for 5 minutes.

Red-Green, Black-Grey, Brown-Green, Brown-Red, Blue-Purple.

whenever one of these combination of colors show up next to each other in game, imagine not being able to clearly see that distinction of going from one color to the next…

it is everywhere and it is MADDENING XD

game looks amazing and plays amazing, but its also so softly tinted with dark colors this becomes a legit issue for people like me.
