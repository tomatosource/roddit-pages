My thoughts as a smaller content creator on the current exploit

As someone who genuinely loves this game and cares deeply about the long-term health of the league, I’ve been pretty disappointed watching how the current exploit is being handled by parts of the content community.

Seeing large content creators promote it as if it were a legitimate strategy feels discouraging. I understand the pressure to create engaging content, but highlighting something that clearly harms the economy and pacing of the season feels short-sighted and extremely selfish. Once an exploit like this becomes widespread, it doesn’t just affect the top end, it impacts everyone’s experience.

The reality is that most people who abuse it will likely reach their goals very quickly and then lose motivation to keep playing. That kind of progression might feel exciting in the moment, but it tends to undermine the longevity that makes a league fun and rewarding in the first place.

I don’t think this comes from malice, but I do think it’s worth reflecting on the influence large creators have on player behavior, just looking at the top played characters every season. I hope we can move toward promoting gameplay and strategies that keep the game healthy and engaging for the whole community, because that’s what will keep people invested long term.

This game is far away from perfect but it gives a lot to all of us, as I can see every single season in my smaller community. This feels like a step too far and in this case the wording: „content creators destroy games“ is where it’s fits perfectly.
