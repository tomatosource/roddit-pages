Boston can make a competitive Giannis offer without including Brown/Tatum

I posted a version of this a month ago, but most weren’t ready to talk about it.

Giannis is only under contract for 1 season. We’ve seen this numerous times before with other superstars. When there’s an expiring contract involved, the superstar has leverage to dictate where he goes, because only a team with some assurance of re-signing the player is going to give up major talent. The one exception was the Raptors in 2019 who decided “fuck it” and gave up DeMar Derozan, Jakob Poetl and a top 20 protected pick that ended up 29th for Kawhi and Danny Green. To be straight, that would be like offering White (instead of DeRozan) + Hauser (instead of Poetl) + the #27 pick (instead of #29).

So this idea that you’d have to give up a 29 year old superstar like Jaylen coming off a 1st team all-nba season + picks when JB has 3 years left on his deal is just kinda ridiculous.

Yes if Giannis makes it clear Boston is one of several teams he will sign long term with like the Rockets, Knicks, Blazers, etc, you’d have to compete with those teams but none of those teams are giving up a 1st team all-nba player and picks either.

Boston could make a competitive offer for Giannis in the offseason that is competitive. Especially if Giannis uses his leverage to say Boston is a team he would re-sign with.

My guess? It would be Vucevic sign-and-trade (starting salary around 25.7 mil) + White (30.3 mil) + a young asset like Walsh (2.4), Baylor (2.7) or Hugo (3 mil) + our available picks which would be 58.4 mil out and match Giannis 58.4 salary while also keeping Boston under the 2nd apron.

Picks I think available would be the #27th pick in this draft, 2027 1st, 2031 1st, 2033 1st, pick swaps in 2028 and 2030, plus 2nd round picks.

Note, the flattening of draft odds makes unprotected picks even more valuable, imo, because now even a play-in team has a shot of landing a top pick and we saw what happens when a player like Giannis spends a season injured. No telling with an unprotected 2033 Boston pick means when Giannis is 38 and Tatum/Brown (currently) are both only signed through 2028-29.

More than likely you’d see a 3rd team involved where White would be re-routed to. Over the offseason it sounds like the best offer we got for him was the 10th pick. So in the scenario you’d see the Bucks get a lotto pick + one or more of boston’s young prospects like Hugo + all our available future picks.

Boston would then have a line-up of Pritchard, Brown, Tatum, Giannis, Queta… or maybe they start Hauser/Baylor and bring Pritchard off the bench.

We currently project to about 185 mil on the books not including Vucevic. In the above scenario (Vucevic. + White + Walsh + picks for Giannis) you’re roughly looking at us having 213 mil on the books when the dust settles after filling out the remaining roster spots with cheap talent. That’s over the 1st (209 mil) apron but under the 2nd (223 mil). You could tweak this idea slightly by giving up more salary in the trade. For instance, involving Hauser‘s 10 mil contract. At that point, Boston could in theory utilize the massive TPE + any leftover picks to try and add additional guard talent to make up for losing White. But I’m assuming in a scenario where Boston went all in on Giannis to become title favorites, they are going to just accept that going over the aprons is part of life

Some variation of that if Giannis really wanted to use his leverage and Boston wanted to go all-in on a 3rd superstar. I get that losing White sucks, but he’s the same age as Giannis and if you somehow come out of it while keeping Walsh or Hugo, maybe it makes sense on paper.
