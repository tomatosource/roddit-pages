Source: Sirius XM NBA Radio

“One of the hard things for an organization is when a player and his agent are pushing, saying ‘let us talk to the other 29 teams’ and as the GM, you have to play it coy while thinking ‘Well, I know what they think about him, which is not what you guys think it is,’ but you have to let them go out and go through the process themselves and see if they can drum up some interest that the franchise hasn’t been able to.”
