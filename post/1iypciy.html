How I’d Rework Maps

Currently I don’t necessarily think maps are in that bad of a spot. There are a good amount of maps, I would think more to come, and most aren’t garbage. I also really like the biome idea. What I don’t like is being forced to play a specific map you don’t like.

Here’s what I’d love to see. Instead of having a set map layout, let us select from a number of maps from that biome. The map location is on a mountain? Let me pick from the 6 different mountain maps. Grass? Same thing. It would still force people to play different maps, but also there should be a map within each biome that a person can prefer.
