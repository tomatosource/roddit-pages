Thoughts on PoE 2 coming from PoE 1’s league

What is does well;

Art + ambience: Biggest reason to play

Music + sound: feeds into the experience

Smooth animations: “looks” powerful

What it’s struggling with;

Passive tree - feels boring, and anything interesting is hit heavy handed with nerfs. Lack of defensive options for non-ES characters forces everyone to play ES endgame.

Slow progression - campaign feels very artificially stretched out with map sizes, and terrain layouts. Sprint is “okay”, but still not enough.

Pre-Made combos - takes away most player innovation, and when something is discovered, uber nerf.

Where it’s completely failing;

Combat Fantasy: unless you get great rng AND follow a cookie cutter fotm, you will have a bad time. You rarely feel a power fantasy until WAY later in the game, where the game is lacking massively.

Build Diversity: In an effort to create engaging combos, they’ve instead narrowed down the available choices to play. A true combo play-style would be

“this attack slams on the ground.” Another could read “does thing, and can have magnified effect if ground is slammed.” And then “Ground slams cause this move hinder”.

The smaller, the better. Large pieces engaging with large pieces only allows those things to interact with each other. A truly fun combo be would finding something you can make your own because of all the little elements that get altered based on how you play.

Endgame Towers/Maps: It’s just Delve with extra steps. The UI is clunky, and is too easy to get lost in. In order for them to pull off something like this they should really take more inspiration from “Gauntlet Legends 64”.

Tedious Gameplay: Long and boring maps. Slow and over paced combat.

If you want to do slower, more engaging combat, GREAT! However, you have to include elements beyond just combat then. Look at Elden Ring, or Dark Souls. Part of the “methodical” gameplay is you’re also engaging with a unique terrain, so the lulls of combat feel appropriately paced.

As it is right now is just a marathon of mob engagement. Nothing really else.

Mob Swarming: if you want methodical combat, then “swarm” mechanics do not make sense to keep introducing as they will continue to lead people to do “strongest hits that hit the most” gameplay with preferably little to no “combo” engagement.

Take away:

The game feels like it’s being developed to be 2 totally different things stitched together taking just about the worst parts of each.

PoE 1 is brilliant, and it’s brilliant because it’s exactly what it is. A fast paced ARPG with a great power fantasy. PoE 2 is slower paced, but is building itself as though it were PoE 1. PoE 2 has great bosses and awesome mob encounters, but you rarely ever feel like you are truly the strongest in the room. It’s room temperature in its decision making, and is actually going backwards on ideas they innovated. “Updates” are league based, but should be weekly with radical changes happening regularly as this is “Test Beta”.

The game is being handled like it’s cooked, and currently it is feeling far from. It feels like a PoE copy if it were made by Blizzard.
