PSA to Ancestral Warrior Totem Users: You can no longer JUST stack levels to scale it

If you look at the poe2db page for this gem, it no longer shows flat damage or attack time, and does not show the damage scaling in the table below. It just shows 100% in 3 columns, which probably means 100% of your min/max damage and attack speed. This means that totems no longer uses their own weapon, but uses your one.

This means you cant just get high gem levels on your weapons and ignore damage stats to deal damage.

This is a little bit speculative but i dont see a better explanation.

Poe2DB page: https://poe2db.tw/us/Ancestral_Warrior_Totem

Edit: Shockwave totem still has those lines, so you can use that instead, but keep in mind it lost quite a bit of damage from changes.
