How to Easily Find the Immured Fury

Short Version: If you are entering an Immured Fury map, use ritual tablets to add Azmeri Spirits. Save one of the wisps without letting it activate until you have killed all the other rares. Then, when you have killed them all, the wisp will lead you to the Immured Fury.

For new players: If you cleanse a corruption and one of the cleansed map says it contains the Immured Fury, this mob has a special boss that will drop a fracturing orb. Fracturing orbs tend to sell for about 30 divs each. It only spawns if you run over a special crystal formation, and they can be hard to find.

Edit: Not ritual tablets, any precursor tablets that add spirits.
