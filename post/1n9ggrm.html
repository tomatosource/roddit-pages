The league should move the 2026 All-Star Game from the Intuit Dome

First, there is precedent for the league moving the All-Star Game inside of a year. They moved the 2017 game from Charlotte for political reasons. So it’s doable.

While other punishments for the Clippers are discussed—forfeiting draft picks, fines, suspensions, voided contracts, etc.—I just don’t know how you can go through with Ballmer’s big celebratory All-Star Game at his brand new arena.

Besides, of everything we know about Ballmer—and what it would look like the punish him—I imagine he cares way more about losing the game than he does about being fined millions of dollars.

But even beyond a Ballmer-specific punishment, who the hell wants to have an All-Star Weekend celebrating the Clippers this year? Wouldn’t it feel embarrassing for the league? Isn’t it just rubbing dirt in the eyes of the other owners?

If for some reason there’s not enough time to move cities entirely, let the Lakers host it in their building.
