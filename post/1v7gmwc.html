Horrible Performance on a 5070ti

I just installed the game and performance was horrible both in the main menu and in the tutorial zone.

GPU is a 5070ti, CPU is a Ryzen 9800. Surely my hardware is capable of running this game at 60 fps.

After googling I tried messing around with in-game settings, deleting the shader cache, and messing around with nvidia control panel settings (including setting the shader cache to 100 GB). Nothing has fixed it, although at minimum settings I’m closer to 20 fps than 10. I also updated my drivers.

I can’t think of anything that would be causing this. I bought this computer so that I could play poe 1 with decent loading screens and I now have no issues with that game even running juiced endgame strats.

I’ll include a screenshot of my performance graph in case it’s helpful. I notice the GPU latency is very high but am not sure how to fix that. Would really appreciate any help for me and for future people having this problem (I read the previous reddit threads I could find but nothing they suggested fixed it).

EDIT: [FIXED] The issue was that I was inadvertently using the 9800’s integrated graphics card rather than the 5070. I just plugged my monitor into a different display port and it fixed everything.
