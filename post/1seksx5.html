Gameplay overhaul / The vision?

Combat should be redesigned around two main parts:

  1. Non-boss encounters (White, Magic, and Rare enemies)
  • Combat should feel fast-paced and explosive, similar to POE1.
  • Most enemies should die very quickly, enabling satisfying “screen-clearing” gameplay
  • The goal is to create high-speed, dopamine gaming
  1. Boss encounters (souls-like overhaul)
  • The style will be more in line with GGG’s POE2 “vision.”
  • Bosses should not be consistently damageable. Instead:
    • They have extremely high damage reduction or temporary invulnerability during most of the fight.
  • Players must interact with boss mechanics to create damage opportunities:
    • Bosses must be stunned/incapacitated, creating a short vulnerability window / damage reduction of 0% period (there already is a short timer that quickly depletes right when a boss is stunned)
    • During this window:
      • Damage reduction is removed OR
      • Invulnerability is lifted, allowing full damage.
  • This creates a two-phase loop:
    1. Build toward a stun (sprinting, tanking, or dodge rolling from boss mechanics, positioning, skill usage)
    2. Capitalize on the damage window

Overall thoughts…

  • Reward builds that can efficiently build stun or control bosses (This’ll help breathe life into some of those lesser used skills or give some other’s a better purpose)
  • Preserve the importance of single-target damage, but tie it to execution during vulnerability windows.
  • Encourage more engaged, mechanical gameplay during boss fights while keeping mapping fast and satisfying.
  • Reward the AOE clear builds with non-boss encounters (but kinda nerf them during bossing)
  • Right now everything dies instantly, bosses included (which I find to be the best part of the game… I find the arbiter fight to be the best fight, because of the mechanics and its fun having that challenge)
