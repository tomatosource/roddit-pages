I leveled all my Alts to level 90 with the same build in less than 10 hours (build included)

Hey folks,

I like experimenting with alt leveling builds and just leveled my last char (an unascended Warrior) to level 90 in 8 hours and 26 minutes. Since there might be some altoholics here, I thought I’d share my build.

# Some important notes in advance:

  • You need a certain amount of currency to be able to afford this build
  • It’s pretty unlikely that you are able to pull this build off in SSF
  • While play time is shorter than usual, setting things up and trading takes time as well
  • The more you invest, the faster you will most likely become (duh!)
  • I dont consider myself a speedrunner at all and actual speedrunners can go way faster than I did

# The build

The build is a Gas Arrow build (transitioned from Lighting Arrow at level 28) and can be leveled (!) with any class and ascendancy. Some of them contribute more to the build, some less, some not at all. In fact, both the Warrior ascendancies contribute nothing to the build (except for 15% inc more life on the which is whatever) which is why I didn’t even ascend at all. You can pick whatever ascendancy skills you prefer (or need later for your actual build) - it will not impact wether or not you will be able to reach level 90 (but it can make it easier, of course).
