Feedback Compilation from a First time ARPG player and First time PoE player.

I have at this point 400hrs in the game, 5 characters and hundreds of divines worth spark archmage that 1 shots any boss in the game. So I have experienced everything in the game, these are MY opinion so here I go:

  1. I don’t like that targeting projectile seems to not go where I click, it’s like slightly below or slightly above.
  2. I dislike trading in this game and it’s extremely tedious even after learning of price fixers and never whispering ultra cheap items. The longer the “leagues” go, the less likely people responds for cheaper items because it’s not worth it for them.
  3. I love the gem system, but I would love the ability to craft uncut gems into lower or higher level.
  4. Corrupting gems are not cool. At end game there is only 1 outcome you want which is +1 level. But this could have been made interesting. Such as making quality not +3% but 10% and or exceeding the maximum 5 support limit and now you can put 6 support. After perfect Jeweller Orbs, the extra slot becomes a useless possibility which is sad.
  5. 3 to 1 is extremely tedious if you have a lot of item to 3 to 1. I like that it’s an option, but I would love a batch kind of way to do this.
  6. When crafting/putting item in/out of stash the server not being able to handle the speed is quite annoying.
  7. Currency stacks seems arbitrarily low, I wish exalt is a 50 stack for example.
  8. Greater essences feels too rare, in 400 hours I only see 2 drops.
  9. I like the currency drop buff that they did before.
  10. The tedious trading makes me want to play SSF, but the gambling “crafting” makes me want to play trade.
  11. The speed of end game vs campaign is jarring. I feel like the current end game system have scarred all players for life because if you ever slow it down to the pace of campaign where you need combinations of skill, people will hate it now since they experienced the end game tempest flurry speed. But skill combinations have always been the highlight of PoE2 not 1 button build based on all the livestreams ever showcased before.
  12. I wish crafting is less gambly, getting a white base, hitting it once with essence of electricity and throw it out if it hits something I don’t want feels bad.
  13. I would love for all of the information that I ever need to be available in the game. I dislike having to open browser to find something about the game.
  14. I wish filter is built into the game and does not require downloading from neversink/filterblade.
  15. Maces feels too slow, their impact feels good but in end game enemies are moving at mach 3 speed.
  16. Summons lags behind us and gets stuck on stuff a lot. I wish they walk with me, not follow me after a certain range.
  17. A lot of uniques are garbage, I mean the vast majority.
  18. Omens are too rare.
  19. The end game atlas mapping becomes ridiculous at a certain point, the map is so big I can’t possibly remember my super juiced area and have to check 1 by 1.
  20. The end game atlas mapping is super tedious with a lot of garbage maps like Mire, Vaal Factory and so on that I don’t even want to even touch once. But I need to in order to make my tower juicing actually impactful.
  21. Bluff is an amazing map design. Sandspit too (courtesy to the commenter that reminded me).
  22. I dislike that at some point, breach sometimes stops summoning monster on a specific side instead of always summoning monster whenever the area grows. This is so painful especially if breach is at the corner of a map that it stops spawning and it becomes impossible to keep it going even after clearing everything on every side.
  23. When blinking it feels like you’re stock moving a specific direction for like a fraction of a second that makes it feel kinda janky.
  24. Lightning spells are way way way better than any other spell combination due to archmage. Archmage needs to be tuned.
  25. Love the amount of unique bosses in the game but a lot of them are annoyingly bugged, like the steaming spring snake lady that 1 shots you before she even spawns.
  26. Burial bog can summon enemies outside of boss arena while fighting the boss, thereby locking you from finishing the boss fight and bricking the map.
  27. A lot of island in the map have no connecting nodes and they are just there, unclearable.
  28. I wish Bluff can be a non tower map too.
  29. SSF should have an incentive for being SSF and transfer should be prevented from SSF so that the advantage stays in SSF.
  30. 99.9% of builds will never see more than 1 charm slot and this feels wrong. At end game charm slot should be 3, having this empty locked spot in your inventory feels bad and ALL belts unique or not unique should have the ability to have 3 charm slot. On that note, charm slot should be an implicit.
  31. I don’t like the Scroll of wisdom mechanic but I understand that you don’t want identified items to drop to the point where filters can check their stat. I think we should have a button next to the portal button that auto identifies your item. This allows you to keep items unidentified for the purpose of selling if you want but does not require this useless cheap resource of scroll of wisdom that takes up inventory spot and needs to be replenished again and again.
  32. Giant’s Blood downside is too brutal.
  33. Mace passive tree seems to have downsides attached to it everywhere which makes no sense in the context of every other class.
  34. There is no way to focus in Life as main defense, ES is too good and eventually full mana MoM is insanely broken.
  35. Armor sucks, a lot of enemies armor break you so easily that makes it worthless.
  36. If Tempest Flurry speed is intended, it gets stuck too often.
  37. I personally hate the charge system of flicker strike, I wish it doesn’t use charge and just teleports.
  38. Map stash don’t contain precursor tablet which feels bad.
  39. Arbiter can be slowed down to the point where his one shot mechanic is literally undodgable. Please fix this.
  40. Simulacrum enemies have instant hit 0.0000001 seconds after they spawn which is ridiculous.
  41. Ritual tablet feels so rare that I can’t focus on it.
  42. I wish I can pick the map I want to juice when I put in my tablet in towers.
  43. I wish there is a filter or search function in the atlas map, example I put in “ritual” in the search, it shows a drop down list of all map that contains ritual, if I click, the map goes there.
  44. The breachstone area is boring and uninspiring with only 1 map layout.
  45. I HATE the simulacrum fog in instilled map. Every map with the same music, black and white look that makes some enemy barely visible is extremely boring. But I need it to giga juice my maps.
  46. The strongbox is still worthless to run because of the drop, the enemy spawn speed is buffed but it’s still too slow.
  47. Belt affixes are still really bad, there is no chance it can ever compete with Ingenuity.
  48. Because group play exist and it’s incredibly strong to the point where people constantly asks for leechers in global chat, why not allow people to just simply 4x the difficulty of any map run they want even if they are alone if they are confident with their power? These leechers meta is really tiring, it does not seem intended.

I understand that I’ll get lynched but yeah these are my feedback for now, I might add more later on but that’s my 2 cents.
