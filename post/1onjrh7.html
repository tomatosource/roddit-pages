My Map Juice Strategy for those too afraid to do 0 revive maps

I use this way to get most of the juice while keeping at least 1 revive per map.

The key thing here is the volume of maps you get vs the ones you use. For Tablets:

  • 2 of the following
  • Rarity of Items Found in Map (at least 24-25)
  • Quantity of Waystones found in Map (at least 40)

Can also go for quantity of items found in map and its better, but its also quite a bit more expensive.

For instills we’re going for Envy, we want those waystones to drop!

For using currency I go with the classic thing uve seen in a million videos already:

  • Exalt until 1 revive left, chaotic rarity and chaos orb it.
  • You CAN do a Vaal afterwards, and it will juice the map more, issue is it can also brick the map or put it to 0 revives, the risk is there, but the rewards are more, so your choice!

THIS IS IT FOR THE MAPS WE RUN! But here is where it gets fun(and a bit rng).

Once you get a comfortable amount of maps you want to run, the rest of the excess of maps we have(and its why we run envy instilled maps!) we will juice with the purpose to sell!

So we do the following juice steps:

  • Exalt until 0 revives left
  • Chaotic Quantity + Chaos Orb
  • Preserved Vertebrae with Sinistral Necromancy turned on(What we want is Overrun by Abyssals FIRST AND FOREMOST, if we do not get it, Abyss has Additional pits is good, or Natural rare monsters extra abyssal modifier)
  • Then you Instill with Guilt
  • Then you Vaal and pray it gets to T16. T16 overrun abyssal maps with high packsize go for quite a few divines, even T15 sell for enough to recoup cost plus a bit more. You sell T16 and T15 overrun. Run the bricked maps or sell them for a bit to recoup some of the lost currency, your choice.

Note: If you want to spend more, you can also do Omen of Abyssal Echoes.

With this strategy you can farm safely while selling the tons of maps ull be inevitably getting/crafting to people who can run them, the cost per map is relatively… low? Medium? I’ve been making way more than what I’ve been spending for sure. I used this while I got strong enough and been making quite a few div.

As with everything, 100⁄150 IIR on your character is recommended if you want better currency drops off the ground.

I am not anywhere near qualified enough or knowledgeable enough to make a guide but this is what ive been doing and its been working so I wanted to share.

Or you can forget all of this and do Sekhemas with high Quantity of Relics and open the spectrum chests to sell, and sell the against the darkness jewels unidentified by running the corresponding relic, its a lot more boring but it makes bank as well.
