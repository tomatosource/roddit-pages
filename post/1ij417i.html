Getting Under The 2nd Apron With Minimal Dumping

So, my math could be wrong, so it’d be cool if someone that’s good at it could check it, but I do think there’s a way to get under the 2nd apron by only dumping 2 more players…as long as those 2 players are Scheierman and Tillman.

  • Amount over the 2nd apron: $3,625,628
  • Salaries of Tillman + Scheierman: $4,732,011
  • Difference: $1,106,383
  • Rookie minimum: \(1,157,153 or \)14,112 per game (very important)
  • Roster spots to be filled: 3

Scheierman + Tillman Math

Let’s say we did dump Scheierman + Tillman and wanted to replace them and Springer with those rookie min deals now: each one would cost \(437,472, (\)14,112 * 31 games remaining) and for all 3 that would be \(1,312,416, which would *still* mean we are **over the 2nd apron by about \)200,000.**

We Can Wait 2 Weeks To Fill Out The Roster

However, you can wait an extra 2 weeks to sign those players. which would put them at \(381,024 per player (\)14,112 * 27 games) or \(1,143,072 for all 3 players, which would *still* mean we are **over the 2nd apron by about \)40,000.**

But Squim, we are still OVER THE 2ND APRON, YA DUMMY!!! Yes, yes we are. It’s because there’s another wrinkle I haven’t explained: 10-day contracts.

10-Day Contracts

With 10-Day contracts you can sign a player for 10 days or for 3 games, whichever comes first. It’s important that you can only have 2 players on 10-day contracts at a time. So, we sign one guy 2 weeks after we traded for Springer and 2 players for 3 games AND we reset the 2 weeks we can go without filling out our roster spots.

However, the thing we have to watch out for is that we have to have our playoff roster set by March 1st and the players need to be on the team by then. So we don’t actually get a full 2 weeks, more like 2 games…and that’s all we need to get under the 2nd apron.

Now the math becomes \(381,024 (guy to replace Springer) + \)28,112 * 25 games (10-day contract included) = \(1,086,624, which puts us about **\)20,000ish below the 2nd apron!!!!!**

Conclusion

If we really wanted to, we could get under the 2nd apron by moving Scheierman + Tillman, but keeping Walsh + Queta. You can thread the needle to get about 20k under the 2nd apron, as long as you use 2 10-day contracts for a 3 game stretch. You also have to replace them with rookie min contracts, (Peterson, Davison, and Watson?).

This would save us from having our 2032 1st locked out of trades.

Tillman is currently at the end of the bench not playing and Scheierman is tearing it up in the G-League. Would it be worth it? Idk, but just continuing to throw out things that technically could happen.
