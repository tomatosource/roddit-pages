Insanely profitable Ritual strat (NOT EXPENSIVE)

I’m a relatively new player and just wanted to share a very consistent and profitable strategy for farming Rituals. I’d say you need around 20-30 divs to start, but you might already have some of the rituals in your inventory if you’ve been playing for a while.

CORE IDEA: make deferring cost 0 tribute and use it only for rerolls, use expensive tablets efficiently

This make it so you can keep deferring every single item, not just important omens, over and over until they reach their lowest price. I’m picking up chaotic omens, crystallization omens, amelioration, uniques etc.

THE STRAT:

  1. Use Head of the King to put Rite of the Nameless on 6 maps
  2. Do your best to put first four Rites on regular maps, and last two Rites on city maps (not required, but helps a lot)
  3. Last two maps are always the juiciest and give the most tribute
  4. Use following tablets (IMPORTANT):
    • %Deferring cost decreased (at least 28%) + chance of omens (ideally 60%+) ≈ 13 div
    • %Deferring cost decreased (at least 28%) + chance of omens (ideally 60%+) ≈ 13 div
    • Freedom of Faith (10% tribute cost, don’t bother with anything higher) ≈ 1 div
    • Rerolling favours X additional times (ONLY ON THE LAST TWO CITY MAPS) ≈ 24 div
  5. Make sure %Deferring cost decreased is at least 56% combined. This makes every defer cost exactly 0 tribute.
  6. Use T15 waystones on non-city maps (with 30%+ pack size if you can craft it yourself, don’t buy)
  7. Use T16 waystones on last two city maps (with 30%+ pack size if you can alch it randomly, don’t buy)
  8. Always rush to the boss first so that he keeps spawning in subsequent rituals
  9. Keep deferring items EVERY SINGLE REROLL because the cost will be 0. This way you will get 24k omens down to 800-900 tribute.
  10. If you don’t have any tribute left after the last reroll, and you have a ton of stuff to pull out - run a basic setup with 3 or 4 %increased tribute tablets on a separate map.

THE GOAL:

You only need enough tribute to keep up with reroll attempts (have 5k tribute for 4 rerolls, 7k for 6 rerolls etc, you want 1-2k extra if possible). Make sure to defer chaotic omens because in the end you’ll be able to get them out for 60-90 tribute each. This will sustain your waystone crafts plus you can sell them as well.

TIPS:

  • If you get proliferating bonus that grants %increased number of favors or tribute, try to run that map first
  • Get 30%+ pack size on waystones whenever you can.
  • Monster effectives and pack size are good to have on Ritual tablets (don’t overpay, it’s not mandatory).
  • Always use rerolling tablet on the last 2 city maps. It’s the most expensive one, and it can last you for 5 ritual runs.
  • Don’t put all 6 Rites on city maps, it’s a waste. With this strat you can use one city for at least 3 separate runs.

This is probably nothing for many of you, but I thought beginners like myself might find it helpful. It’s the only strategy that actually made me good money. The usual stacking of 3 rerolls tablets is terrible IMO because you need fortune to start and a lot of the times you can barely break even.

EDIT: ‘Reroll favours X additional times’ tablet is not necessary until you can afford it. The whole point is to make deferring cost 0 so you can get everything out of the ritual for cheap. The last tablet can be random, something you might already have in stash.
