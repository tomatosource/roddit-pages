Valanciunas: This is the time he will speak on Tuesday in Vilnius

THANOS FERONAS 

07.07.2025 

This is the time when the face of the day in European basketball, Jonas Valanciunas, will speak at a conference in Vilnius.

All of basketball Europe is awaiting the outcome of the Jonas Valanciunas case, and the Lithuanian center will speak tomorrow (8⁄7, 17:30) at a conference in Vilnius.

This is the B8 Summit, which will feature basketball figures such as Giannis Sfairopoulos, Ibon Navarro, Vincent Collette and Kostas Hadjichristos.

On Tuesday afternoon, Valanciunas will speak, taking questions, and understandably, interest is focused on whether he will reveal his future.

The Denver Nuggets want to keep the player in their squad , with Panathinaikos AKTOR pushing to sign him,  having even brought the player to Greece a few days ago.

In 13 seasons in the NBA, Valanciunas averaged 13.1 points (58% on two-pointers, 34% on three-pointers, 79% on shooting), 9.3 rebounds and 1.4 assists per 25.1 minutes of participation in 937 regular season games, while he has earned a total of $145,756,944.
