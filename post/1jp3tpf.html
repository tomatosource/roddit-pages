Remember if you see a new unique that grants a skill you only get 4 support gems instead of 5. Don’t plan a build without remembering that.

Unless something is changed you can never get a level 20 skill on an item which means the skill can’t unlock the 5th support gem slot.

With what looks like death’s oath coming and most likely more uniques with skills I thought I would mention this for anyone who didn’t know.
