PSA: Quipolatl’s Thesis enables 100k+ mana regen with Indigon + Archmage

TLDR: Stack mana and mana regen, then use Quipolatl’s Thesis + Indigon + Archmage + Sacrifice of Mind for:

  • 10k+ mana pool
  • 100k+ mana regen
  • 1500%+ increased spell damage
  • 280%+ extra damage as lightning

Core Details

As I’m now done with the league, I decided to share what I think is currently the most underrated interaction in the game. It all begins with the following line on Quipolatl’s Thesis:

Gloves: Each Runic Inscription from your Curse Skills causes you to Regenerate Mana per second equal to 10% of that Skill’s Mana Cost

Now, on first look this doesn’t look like much. A Runic Inscription is the animation that is shown during the Curse Activation Delay. By default, this is 1.5 seconds. So this means that on casting a curse you get 15% of the mana cost back over the next 1.5 seconds. Nothing crazy, until we begin scaling it. Let’s do some sample math as to how much percentage of the mana cost we get back.

  1. First, we can actually increase the activation delay to 3.9s by using the supposed drawback of the Whispers of Doom keystone (doubles activation delay) as well as the Ritualisitic Curse support gem (30% more delay). Mana regenerated per cast: 39%
  2. Next, we will be stacking mana and mana regen on this build, so let’s assume a conservative 400% increased mana regen for an even 5x multiplier. Mana regenerated per cast: 195%
  3. Next, we can use Spell Cascade (+2), Unleash (+2) and Spell Echo (+1) as support gems to get a total of +5 runic inscriptions per cast. Mana regenerated per cast: 1170%
  4. On top of that, we can make use of Arcane Surge for 20% more mana regen. Mana regenerated per cast: 1404%
  5. Further, Arcane Blossom passive notable gives us 15% increased mana recovery rate. Mana regenerated per cast: 1614% Edit: Mana Recovery Rate works differently than in PoE 1, so this is not a more multiplier.
  6. Finally, Essence of Horror gives 60% increased effect of Socketed Items (you could go even higher with Darkness Enthroned). Mana regenerated per cast: 2246%

Now, the above is already enough to get 20k+ mana regen easily, but we are just getting started. To truly raise this interaction to ridiculous levels, we make use of Indigon’s line:

5% increased Cost of Skills for each 200 total Mana Spent Recently

The funny part of Quipolatl’s Thesis is that the mana regen it grants is based on the skill cost. As we spend more mana and our mana costs go up, so does our regen. Thus completely negating Indigon’s downside and actually turning it into an upside, as it allows us to ramp up our spent mana to also absurd amounts. This allows us to literally spend 20k+ mana recently for ridiculous 1500%+ increased spell damage.

But wait, how do you actually spend so much mana, even with Indigon? Doesn’t it have an awfully long ramp time? That would be true, if it wasn’t for the final part of the combo: Archmage (+ Eldritch Battery). Archmage not only gives us 280% extra lightning damage on a 7k mana build, it also comes with the supposed drawback of adding 6% Base Mana Cost, which Eldritch Battery then doubles. This makes our Curse cost ~1500 mana at rest and even a single cast is enough to instantly get to 10k+ mana regen. Cast it several times and you get 100k+ mana regen.

In fact, we have so much mana regen, that we can (and should) easily use the Sacrifice of Mind keystone:

Mana Recovery from Regeneration Overflows maximum Mana
50% less Mana Regeneration Rate

At our levels of regen the downside is irrelevant. The upside is very important though, as it gives us both effectively 50% more “hp”, as well as allows us to cast spells with a cost of up to 150% of our maximum mana, which allows us to ramp Indigon further.

Some additional mentions and interactions

  • The above setup is very flexible and allows us to use any kind of spell.
  • I recommend to use Cast on Crit and Cast on Elemental Ailment, with one of those triggering your second curse, for even more mana regen.
  • Use mana cost efficiency and the Efficiency II support to tweak your mana costs until they feel right.
  • You can use Mana Flare (25% of 10k mana means a 2500 base damage spell with 1s CD).
  • You can use Wildsurge Incantation for 50% less cost of plant and storm skills.
  • You can use Ritualists Mind Phylacteries ascendency to have permanent Charm uptime. I use it to have permanent Stun and Slow immunity, as well as a gold charm.

Example Build

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/KontraMagic-3614/character/Larcanalia https://pobb.in/Opxn6Ws3_FEd

This build is far from fully pushed to the limit (I didn’t even bother with an anoint!). I did not make use of all possible interactions to maximise mana regen, as I felt it was already enough. As is, I already did all content in the game with it - killed all bosses (including Uber Arbiter) in seconds without seeing their mechanics, cleared an abyssal overrun map with the increased difficulty per previous abyss modifier, etc.

Final warning

As you rely on your high mana pool and regen for survivability, you will occasionally get killed by Mana Siphoner rares. So for all the power of this interaction, I wouldn’t recommend it for hardcore.
