Review from someone try this game 20 times

TLDR: I like the game, but somehow I kinda hate it at the same time.

I’ve played both Path of Exile and Path of Exile 2 , but I never made it past Act 4 in POE and Act 3 in POE2 before. This time, I finally beat Act 4 in PoE 2. I like the game, but to be honest, I still don’t know what I’m doing.

Because of that, I decided to keep things simple: focus on one build only, I use youtube and reddit to find a guide. Once I understand more of the game, I’ll start testing and playing in a way that feels comfortable to me. Thanks to the Druid class, I can finally blow stuff up and actually feel like I do something.

Pros and Cons after finish ACT 4

Pros:

  • Great art style
  • Tons of customization
  • Skills exploding all over the screen feels awesome

Cons:

  • Because there’s so much customization, it can be extremely confusing
  • Skills exploding everywhere also makes it hard to see what’s going on
  • Sometimes I can’t even see my own character
  • I hate having to fight all the mobs again after dying. It’s really annoying.
  • The map design is kind of annoying. It’s so tight and so big that it takes quite a while to reach the quest area.

I’m still very new and trying to figure this game out. Hopefully one day I’ll reach that point where I’m teleporting around and everything just dies instantly, like those crazy videos where people blink nonstop and the screen clears itself.

The only tip I can give to new players like me is to stack as much elemental resistance as possible, because it really helps prevent dying instantly. Also, remember you can hold ‘Space bar’ to run, I didn’t figure that out until near the end of Act 2 last season, and I felt really dumb.
