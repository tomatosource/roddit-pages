Temple lock sustain tips

Just wanted to give some tips to help with lock sustain. Doing this I basically never went below 5 locks. But you lose some juice with this strategy.

What I did was

  1. Always let 1 room destabilize. I used the fubgun version of the temple so this was easy. You usually get a good room every roll (commander &gt; generator &gt; treasure vault &gt; alchemy lab lvl 1) so it’s not a big deal to lose this room. But you don’t get the extra juice from levelling this room.

2 when running the temple only lock your main chain. Your secondary chain leave unlocked until you’re done the temple.

3 don’t pick up any medallions. Even the destabilization medallions until you are maxxed out ie 7 total medallions are available (one to lock your secondary chain and rest to fill out your stock). Once temple is complete lock the secondary chain and grab the medallions. This means a short run back to grab the medallions so use the checkpoints.

4 Let your solitary room destabilize. Rinse and repeat.

Can’t prove this works, other than my own experience, but a lot of people said they stop getting as many locks once their temple is complete. So what changes? You start locking at least 2 chains and maybe even 3. So no rooms are destabilized. My guess is that the game gives medallions based on how many rooms destabilize and or are locked. And possibly how many medallions you pick up in the run. Good luck and hopefully people can give feedback if this also works for them. But like I said. When I followed these steps I never went below 5 locks with both chains prelocked. I even dropped 6 medallions in one run.
