Every Lakers championship winning coach won in their first season with the team

John Kundla was the Lakers coach in the 1948-49 season. They won that year and went on to win 4 more

Bill Sharman took over as head coach for the 1971-72 season. They won the championship that year.

Paul Westhead took over in the middle of the 1979-80 season and won a championship

Pat Riley took over in the middle of 1981-82 and won that year. He went on to win 3 more titles in the 80’s

Phil Jackson became the Lakers HC in 1999-00 when they won their first of 5 with him

Frank Vogel took over as HC in 2019-20 and won a championship with the Lakers

Every other Lakers HC has failed to win a title. In fact, the only other coaches who have made the finals are Mike Dunleavy (1990-91, first year as HC), Joe Mullaney (1969-70, first year as HC), Butch van Breda Kolff (1967-68, first year as HC) and Fred Schaus (1961-62, 2nd year as HC. In his first season, the Lakers lost in the Western Division Finals to the Hawks 105-103 in a game 7)

JJ Redick is entering his 2nd season as HC
