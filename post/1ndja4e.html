Loved the new coffee break feature

Basically the title, sometimes you’re playing and really wish you could go and grab a coffee but the game is so good so you don’t wanna pause and go. Glad GGG implemented this new feature where your game freezes for a good 20 seconds, followed by the 15 seconds of “cogs” now I have the perfect moment to get up and go grab a coffee!

Better yet, when things get back to normal, your mini-map is reset, so you got to explore it all again =)
