How I shut up and learnt to craft

My current wand is mid-high tier, but anything on sale that is better, is waaau beyond my budget (100 Div).

So, I stopped pretending like it was all too hard, and set about learning.

Started by buying three magic +5 chaos wands for 2 Div, and one for 8 chaos, which was an lucky swoop.

Two were failures, mostly because I messed up one and prefect Alactritied the +5 away as I didn’t have the omen active…oops.

The one I stuck with though went like this.

Started +5 Chaos and Max mana, two suffixes.

Alacrity Cast Speed.

Remembered the sinistral omen and replaced +mana with mana efficiency using perfect Alactrity.

Jawboned on +mana (seems so weird having just removed it!)

Turned on homo and greater exaltation and prefect exalted.

T3 chaos and T3 spell, which I was disappointed with (I needed have been)

Omen of light annulled that +mana (again!)

Jawboned again and got well, this:

https://preview.redd.it/ykae21ee1k2g1.png?width=867&format=png&auto=webp&s=a00251196955093ccc3c95c72cfd9d75b7d1a6ab

Closest on sale was 300 Div.

My cost, about 38 Div.

I didn’t want to risk the Vaal, someone else with deeper pockets can try that!

I hope they don’t nerf crafting for the next league!
