PSA: Controller UI calculates your spirit incorrectly and tells you that auras are active even when they are not

Just figured this out after 70+ hours on this character, I’m so irritated. The controller UI can underestimate the true spirit cost of your gems, and uses that calculation on the “Toggle Auras” UI, so then it allows you to turn on a bunch of extra auras (it sets the text to “On” and puts the spinning aura animation over the icon), even though they’re not actually being turned on. There are no errors or warnings about this anywhere, except that the buffs don’t appear in your status bar.

I randomly switched to the mouse+keyboard UI and was so confused when it told me three of my very important auras weren’t active. Googled it and found this reddit thread. This is not my first time discovering a game-breaking UI bug just because I happened to check something on M+K.

I suppose the lesson is to just not play with a controller? GGG pls.
