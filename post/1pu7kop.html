So Voll’s Protector is bugged?

I think other people have mentioned Voll’s Protector which has a 25% chance to grant 1 power charge on crit, and can only generate 1 charge per cast/attack. And I assumed that might be a bug or intended in some dumb way. But now I actually found it it’s not that simple.

What actually happens is you can only generate 1 power charge per attack/cast until you spend that power charge. So you have to constantly spend the power charges if you want say Firestorm/Thunderstorm to constantly generate more then 1 charge.

This bug is so annoying lmao. I’m trying to find out the best attack/spell for generating charges fast with this dumb unique. So far seems permafrost bolts just because they attack so fast.
