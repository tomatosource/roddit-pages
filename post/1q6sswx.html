[Sports Business Journal] Peacock adding enhancements that may allow fans to mute announcers, control crowd noise

Source: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/01/05/peacock-becomes-first-sports-streamer-with-full-dolby-audio-video-enhancements/

Peacock is the first sports streamer to integrate new Dolby products to enhance the sights and sounds of the games as the NBCUniversal property broadcasts NBA, MLB, NFL Sunday Night Football and the FIFA men’s World Cup in 2026 and beyond.

The announcement, made this evening at CES in Las Vegas, means Peacock will use Dolby’s Vision and Atmos products, with the intention of upgrading to Vision 2 and AC-4 when they launch later this year. Fans are expected to experience more vivid and balanced colors, immersive sound and clearer dialogue, even when there’s loud background or ambient noise.
