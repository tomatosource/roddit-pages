JB workin’

Jaylen Brown is hitting his free throws, grabbing boards and locking down on D. Tatum is feasting on defenders in iso and shredding down low. D White is an everywhere at once on both sides of the ball and Big Al is playing like it’s 2008. The Detroit game last night was a test, but it woke up the team and tested the mettle they lacked so much over the past few seasons when giving up big leads. If the Two Jays keep setting the tone, and White, Holliday, and Big Al keep pestering on the perimeter and paint this team is going to be SCARY good when Milk and Cookies are back on the floor together. I am loving this.
