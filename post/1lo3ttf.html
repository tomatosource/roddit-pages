This is part #1 of my Mirror craft item collection

The highlight of my collection is highly likely the highest ES focus (500+ ES) on 0.2 league from hitting a vaal and getting the 2 socket corrupt.

For anyone into mirror-tier item crafting you’ll know that crafting 3x ES Mirror-Tier Focuses is extremely annoying and hard because prefix ES ilvl’s are low compared to the various spell damage % T1s and so you have an absurdly high chance to brick the craft or rely on multiple prefix annuls 33%’s that are almost guaranteed to send you back to the beginning of the craft. As such probably one of the best craft of the league for me is the 2 socket corrupted focus shown 1st.

The breach ring craft was a joint project with a buddy of mine, nothing too special as most mirror ring crafts look very similar. Its a pure lightning rarity and stat ring for LS deadeye/amazons.

The ES helmet was a map drop with T1 ES prefix + T1 chaos res and then took a metric ton of whittles and dextrals to finish up the craft, but nothing risky to craft here, just whittles and dextrals (a sinestral here and there) - monetarily expensive, a few hundred divs but nothing too crazy.

The boots were the easiest craft due to the good fracture on the ES and with 35% MS already on the base I bought, it was just a matter of crafting the 1 ES prefix and the 3 suffixes which is a lot easier than it sounds, just required a few hundred divines of omens and then it just depends how lucky you get if you hit it in 200 divs or 800.

And then a couple hundred divines spent redevining all these items to hit good affix values and that’s that.

EDIT: I have 3 more crazy mirror crafts that are like these that I’m currently using on my other character including another crazy focus craft that i’ll post in Part 2 eventually
