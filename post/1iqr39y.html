Hardcore Group Found and Group Found

Hey,
I wanted to grasp the community’s opinion of this.

So in original Hardcore WoW with the add-on, not after official release, you could, at the start of the game, type in your teammates names and lock your group to the selected few.

Groups had to log in together, be together in the same zone, or get disqualified.
The add-on would check this by having a timer, tracking these things. Party members were allowed to trade with each other, but only with each other.

This made the whole experience a bit easier in HC WoW, but it should be fine in Poe due to enemy scaling in parties.

So to summarize, what I am asking is a mode/league with the following features.:

  • Set party members at the start of the game
  • Have a reasonable timer to make sure the selected group logs in together (3 minutes for example) if not, all registered party members get disqualified.
  • Do the same for zoning. If any party member progresses alone in a zone, disqualify the entire group.
  • If any group member dies, the full group dies.
  • Trading is allowed within the selected starting group members and only them.
  • Groups have to enter arenas/content together or get disqualified.

I personally think, this is a fun way to play the game together in a group while having restrictions, that yes, you could set yourself, but it would be fun, to have its own league as well.

I am curious to see what people think.
