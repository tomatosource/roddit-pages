Jokić currently leads the league in Assists and Rebounds per game. This would be the first time he has led the league in any major per game statistic.

Nikola Jokić is one of the greatest players of all time, last year he averaged a 30 point triple double but one thing Jokić has somehow never done is lead the league in any major per game statistics ie. points, rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks.

Last year Jokić was 3rd in points per game behind Shai and Giannis, 2nd in assists per game behind Trae Young, 3rd in rebounds per game being Sabonis and KAT, and 2nd in steals behind Dyson Daniels.

This year Jokić is currently 5th in points and 30th in steals (only a .4 SPG drop) but he is 1st in assists at 11 per game with a 1.6 per game lead over 2nd place Cade Cunningham and he is 1st in rebounds with 12.1 but this is only a 0.3 rpg led over 2nd place KAT.

If Jokić can maintain this for the rest of the season, which isn’t guaranteed as the season is still young, he will become the only player with both an assist and rebounding title. No player, not even Wilt has ever done that.
