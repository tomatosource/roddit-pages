Fast XP isn’t always a good thing (my experience)

This isn’t a complaint post, just sharing a personal experience and a bit of advice.

This league I made four characters and genuinely enjoyed leveling and progressing with all of them.

Today, at level 67, I was just chilling in base and reading global chat. I saw someone looking for a leecher for Temple runs and thought, “Why not, let’s see what that’s like.”

After about 15–20 minutes of stacked Temple run… I was level 91.

And honestly, that completely killed my enjoyment of progression and working toward things on my own.

That was definitely my first and last time doing that. I’m not blaming anyone , it was my choice, and this is just my personal experience. But I really wouldn’t recommend it to others, especially newer players who haven’t experienced normal progression yet.

See you next league, exiles.
