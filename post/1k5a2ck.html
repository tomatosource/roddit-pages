I’ve really enjoyed Dawn of the Hunt

I’ve been having a blast with this league and am now feeling comfortably into the latter part of end game. I’ve been more or less solo self found with a few caveats (e.g. things from friends who are also following the same rules are allowed) and I think it’s made the game a lot more enjoyable for me.

Campaign was more enjoyable than 0.1 and I’m not sure why because I don’t think I did anything differently. Act 2 is a bit of a problem. The boss feels like a difficulty spike that’s a little excessive. Probably needs less hp. Act 3 was fine, especially now with new checkpoints. Viper was a beast but the changes made her a lot easier.

Maps were more challenging at first because my gear wasn’t very good, but it was fine. I just spent a little more time in early maps to find upgrades and essences/exalts for crafting and it worked out. Only problem I ran in to is that respec costs can be a little much when your not blasting T15s with ease, so I did accidentally brick my build because I wanted to test a cool idea that was completely awful and left myself without the gold to fix it, but I manage to get enough gold leveling my second character to recover.

The things I have found to work well are the bosses, rituals, essences, unique maps are cool, and I’m getting enough rare items to not starve of gold so long as I plan around having a reserve. Gear progression has actually felt really good, and I have really enjoyed not feeling pressure to trade, which means I can slam currency on pieces that are kind of good already in the hopes of getting another decent modifier.

Really like the new towers, they feel a lot better to run now and I’m actually holding good maps for them instead of running junk ones like I used to.

Rogue exiles are super cool too, I wish I saw them more and that they were a little scarier.

Some things I think need work would be the atlas passives. I don’t feel like they do much. Personally, I would rather get fewer passives with more impact than many with little impact. I kind of wish the rogue exiles passives would make them more frequent than they are currently, but with increasingly better floor for loot and substantial challenge increases. Currently most of them die incidentally while I’m clearing mobs in search of the exile.

Essences are cool, but I also kind of want a way to make them more exciting/challenging on the tree. As it stands, they die in one combo.

Omens, these are still not well balanced. If the good omens were less rare crafting wouldn’t feel so bad. Audience with the King took me probably 50+ rituals to find then an additionally 30-40 to finish deferring. I haven’t seen a second.

Boss fragments in general are a little rare, so I can’t comment on the others. I would ideally like to see more boss fights.

Currency: I’m not going to dwell on this much because I think it’s bugged. But largely the currency that is most painful is greater and perfect jewellers orbs. Exalted need to be more common during lower levels but I haven’t had issue with later on. Chaos orbs weirdly in a good spot.

Overall though, I have been enjoying the patch and am looking forward to seeing what comes next.
