Its time to face reality. The knicks would have smoked us.

All year I wanted to believe in the Celtics and was majorly impressed by their season and Mazzulla’s coaching job to get us to the 2 seed. Watching JB was awesome and I hope brad stevens can keep him and Tatum on the roster.

But in the back of my head I kept asking myself. “The knicks look better than last year and the Celtics look worse. Why should I believe we wouldn’t lose a rematch?”

I think the first round has pretty much proven this. The fashion we lost to Philly has led fans to seriously question Mazzulla’s coaching which is a positive. We’ve had some incredibly embarrassing playoff losses under him.

But overall I don’t expect major roster moves for 2026-27 and am keeping my hopes up for 2027-28. I think they’ll want to stay under the first apron again for the repeater tax so unless we’re taking in less salary I can’t see anything crazy happening.

TLDR: I’m shocked that the Celtics seemed to “trick” the general public two years in a row to be heavy favorites to beat the knicks even though they truly didn’t deserve it after last year’s playoffs. Go C’s, very hopeful for the ‘28 season. In Brad I trust.
