If you’re a new player and you want currency heres a farm that netted me 1 divine (at the moment almost 400 exalts) in 10 minutes at lvl 2 in act1.

Disclaimer: Only works in the current league Runes of Aldur.

Whether you’re a late starter or a newcomer, you probably want some nice pieces of loot while leveling. People who are playing alts want to get them going asap, so they need some items only lvl 1 characters can use, this is what we’re farming.

Disclaimer 2: You do need around a thousand gold for this, or if you want exalts you can try asking in global for anyone to trade the divines(s) with you in person.

Step 1: Go to the first zone and find and help Farrow.

Step 2: Find the Mud Burrow on the right side of the map and clear it until you find the current league event mechanic (Verisium remnant aka blue stone thingy)

It should only offer you 2 options: Skill Gem and Skill Support Gem. Take the SKILL GEM option. Not the support.

Step 3: Finish the event and loot the stone. Run to a checkpoint and teleport to the start. Leave the area and CTRL Left click on the entrance to select a fresh instance and enter it to repeat this as many times as you’d like.

Step 4: Go to the starting town and in your browser go to https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2 and type in literally anything into the search bar while you have “INSTANT BUYOUT” selected in the top right. It doesn’t matter what item it is, you just want to borrow their hideout.

Step 5: You should be teleported next to a shopkeeper NPC called Ange. Talk to her, click on Currency Exchange, CTRL + Left click your Skill Gem in your inventory, and select Divines at the left side. At the moment I’m writing this I sold 3 for 1 divine. for 1000 gold.

Step 6: Either spend your divine as it is on whatever you want or ask in Global if someone could exchange your divine for exalts (make sure you ask for around ~50 less exalts than it is worth so people don’t just find you annoying and actually get something out of the trade)

Obviously this farm will get worse the more people do it so I’m advising you to check the price on these gems before doing this.
