Could Simons threaten for 6th man of the Year?

Still a good chance we move Simons, but if he sticks around does he have a shot to work up the 6th man of the Year rankings?

Just checked Vegas odds and he’s 14th behind a bunch of guys: Naz Reid, Jamie Jaquez Jr, Anthony Black, Keldon Johnson, Reed Sheppard, Collin Gillespie, Dylan Harper, Nickeil Alexander-Walker, Ajay Mitchell, Santi Aldama, Isaiah Stewart, De’Andre Hunter, Quentin Grimes.

But for the season he’s averaging 13.5 points, 2.3 rebounds, 2.4 assists with 43.3%/39.6%/88% shooting in 23.8mpg

Pritchard last season was 14.3 points, 3.8 rebounds, 3.5 assists with 47.2%/40.7%/84.5% in 28.4mpg

During Brogdon’s 6th man season, he averaged 14.9 points, 4.2 rebounds, 3.7 assists with 48%/44%/87% shooting in 26mpg

Simons would need to see his numbers creep up, but he’s had some good games lately and if he gets more comfortable in that role and Boston continues having momentum, maybe he’ll have a chance. Tiny sample size, but over past 4 games he’s averaged 19ppg/26mpg. Interested to see if that keeps trending up.
