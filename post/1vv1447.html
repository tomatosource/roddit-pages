How many unique all nba first team players there were in each decade since 1960

I wanted to focus on all nba first team players since that’s the top collection of talent and I was interested to see if there is any correlation between eras.

1960s:

14 unique first team players

1970s:

24 unique first team players

1980s:

18 unique first team players

1990s:

22 unique first team players

2000s:

16 unique a first team players

2010s:

20 unique first team players

2020s (still in progress):

15 unique first team players:

I was originally interested in this because at first I thought the 2020s had a lot of the same guys be all nba first team. But when I saw 15 different unique players I was surprised. Then I remembered the 2020 all nba teams where LeBron AD and Harden all got one and dones.

So I wanted to see how this correlates throughout nba history, and the results aren’t particularly shocking.

The lowest unique amount of players happening in the 60s is not shocking at all. Not only was there a limited sample size due to lack of teams, it means it was arguably one of the more diverse eras, since in a league of 8 teams 14 different players made a first team.

The jump from 14 to 24 also isn’t too shocking, but even then, there weren’t 30 teams like today, by the end of the 70s there were still only 22. Not as big of a ratio as the 60s, but it’s still the era with the most unique first team players by pure numbers.

2000s having (basically) the second lowest is interesting. There are 29-30 teams in the nba at this point but only 16 unique players who made first team. Having the lowest diversity by far.

Overall, I thought it was interesting to see and layout.
