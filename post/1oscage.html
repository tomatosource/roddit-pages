The affliction: “Monsters remove 3% of your Life, Mana, and Energy Shield on Hit”is still bugged

This has been true since POE 2 was launched, if you have this seemingly mild affliction, there are circumstances that will instantly kill you, whilst leaving you with honour:

  • The Urnwalker’s cinder attack
  • The 2nd floor fire boss can leave a cinder ground effect AFTER death, so even if you have: “You cannot be Damaged until you complete the next room”, you will still die
  • Urnwalkers can have a missile sky attack that tracks you, even between platform levels and cannot be outrun, dealing massive damage. (This particular attack is randomly assigned to Urnwalkers, and is just ridiculous)

This cannot, and should not be the case. Sekhemas are tough enough already, this is simply unfair and unfun.

Yes, I farm Sekhemas regularly, I have avoided this affliction, but today wanted to test it to see if this had been fixed. no. it hasn’t
