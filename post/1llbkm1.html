The Rasheer Fleming Camp

I’m ecstatic there’s a subgroup here who also love Fleming as a prospect & hopefully soon to be Celtic!

I saw Fleming as a lottery pick candidate based off his performance at St. Joseph’s, as well as his high ceiling.

I’ve noticed a group of fans who would prefer a Center selected at 32.

Let me first say, Raynaud and Kalkbrenner are solid second round Center prospects.

My counter is, their lack of offense and defensive physicality, and their limited athleticism. Lesser athletic centers can and have become successful in spite of these limitations. Though on that the list of centers, we see usually either, elite skill paired with sound fundamentals, or strongmen paired with aggression. I’m not sure either have demonstrated such in their four years.

Now to Fleming:

Rasheer Fleming, 6’8”, has a 7’5” wingspan, almost equal to Kalkbrenner, and 5” more than Raynaud.

Fleming is exceptional athlete with an aggressive demeanor.

He can play SF/PF and in a small ball lineup has the size and length for stretch five. He can space the floor, 39% from three, and averaged 8.5 TRB a game.

Although not a consensus take: He has one of the highest ceilings in the entire class.

As a four and stretch five, a position of need for the Celtics, he could be a plug and play while having a runway to develop while Tatum recovers.

Fleming is a Bam Adebayo/Kawhi Leonard hybrid. ***(in play style and strengths).

Let’s see Rasheer Fleming in the Celtics green tonight!
