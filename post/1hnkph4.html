Notable players who will not be eligible for awards (MVP, All-NBA, etc.) due to missed games this season

To be eligible for MVP, DPOY, MIP, All-NBA and All-Defense, players must play at least 65 games. Technically, they have to play at least 20 minutes in 63 games and at least 15 minutes in the remaining 2 games (according to this article) but for the purpose of this post I will simply look at total games played/games missed. Basically, if you miss 18 games in the season, you’re out.
