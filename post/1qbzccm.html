[150 Breach maps, 37d/hr] - Anyone can make 100s of divs; find what works for you

  • Yes temple has made economy real inflated, but just trying to show that there are plenty of ways to make 100s of divs easily, even if you’re pretty new to the game.
  • Ran 150 maps w triple breach tabs, focusing on clear breaches + crystals only.
  • ~37div/hour just w breach drops; 56div/hour if I sell everything
  • Am running ~20% total rarity (couldnt care less)
  • Cleared breaches in around 3-3.5min on average (sometimes 1 min, sometimes 6 min w multiple breaches). Blink helped a ton. Some maps I killed the boss if its closeby, some just portal out after breach ends.
  • Focused on open layouts for better pathing and clasp hands spawn &gt; Rustbowl, savannah, steaming springs, penitentiary, fortress. Somehow Cenotes always hits multiple breaches for me its weirdly good. Avoid molten/hidden grotto/sun temp at all costs.
  • Zero optimization in strat - Maps - Alch, exalt, chaotic rarity, done; Tablets just look for additional breach and clasp hands mods on tablets and just run them. Chance for 3 additional breaches is nice when it hits but in my experience too wildly inconsistent unless you get it on all 3 tabs. Mostly self sustained other than buying some tabs (max 1 div each) when I ran low. On avg 60% of all my map had 2 breaches, and a small handful had 4.
  • Splinters have shot up considerably since I started, and so will all other non temple currency. Do a little research and just put some time into a strat that works for your build
  • The other non breach loot were just drops along the way. Didnt bother with any other content if its not in my way
