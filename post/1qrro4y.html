From riches to rags.

A few hours ago I was at more than 7,000 Divine Orbs. I had just sold a self-crafted Time-lost Emerald for 1500d, things were looking good on the way to Temporalis. Then, in a fit of something I can’t really explain, I proceeded to create 4 more of the same or very similar time-lost emeralds. To do that I bought countless Omens of Light, used about 2,000 Divines on Rerolls and overall deleted all but 50 Divines in the process. 3 of the Time-lost at least got max rolls in the end, on one I had to give up. Sadly I somehow doubt they’ll sell at all and if they do, I’ll not make back more than maybe 3,000 divines. I’m scared to vaal them, even though that could, if insanely lucky, prove to be the ultimate gamble and get me back more than invested.

While it does feel pretty bad, and I do contemplate quitting this league and maybe the game itself, I don’t share this to produce pity, but to remind you, that when you’re in a fit of crafting rage, trying to get a perfect roll, stop for a second and think. Maybe settle, maybe just leave it and cut your losses, everything is better than deleting what took you weeks to aquire.

Well, thanks for reading. If you have similar experiences to make me feel better, these will be appreciated.
