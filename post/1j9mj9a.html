Waystones Map is so Cluttered, here’s a thought;

If anyone remembers playing one of the OG ARPG’s, Gauntlet Legends, then you may remember the “portal room”. Basically, each biome had 5 or so different levels in it, but were unlocked as you progressed. So you could do Level 1 of the forest biome, and also Level 1 of the Castle biome, etc…

How this could be useful for PoE2 is that they could use this similar concept, and allow players more choice in the “biomes” while also clearing up a TON of the clutter.

Example; Towers juice “biomes”. Depending on the waystone level certain layouts are available/unavailable. After so many cleared waystones a center portal is opened for the unique map/citadels, etc…

How this helps the player’s experience is that they now have greater control over the map type they want to do, and it would also GREATLY reduce the clutter that is the endgame mapping system.
