#Core Differences from Fubgun’s LA Deadeye

  • This build let’s us use cheaper/crappy, off-meta “Gain #% damage as Extra” mods we want because Embitter Support turns all sources of Gain as extra damage into Gain as extra COLD damage.

  • https://poe2db.tw/us/Embitter

  • My Bow’s 3x “5% added extra of all elements” is giving me 45% extra cold damage.

  • My Against the Darkness is giving me 56% extra cold damage.

  • My Heart of the Well is giving me 24% extra cold damage.

  • Material Solidification is giving me 8% extra cold damage.

  • Note, Embitter calculations do not work in Path of Building 2.

  • Embitter is applied to LA, Rods, HoT, and HoI for maximum cold damage.
  • AoE scaling is pushed about as hard as I reasonably can.
  • AoE scaling also improves the DPS of Lightning Rods by maximizing Rod burst overlaps.
  • Freeze scaling lets me insta-freeze nearly anything in the game, making this a very effective defensive layer.
  • Need spirit on chest and amulet in order to fit Herald of Ice with all of your other auras. 180 Spirit minimum. Precision Support I / II are not necessary.
  • We keep some lightning damage to make sure we get the bonus damage from shock, and can still activate our Herald of Thunder.
