In my ~30 years of gaming, I have never hated anything with the passionate fervor with which I despise Proximal Tangibility.

I’m a pretty level headed guy, usually. Not the type to get all bent out of shape over a tough video game mechanic.

Proximal Tangibility is different.

It has me absolutely seething every single time. Literally screaming obscenities at my monitor. Threats. Promises.

Now, I’m not opposed to its functionality. It doesn’t get me killed or leave me with unkillable rares.

It annoys the everliving @#%! out of me.

The “this enemy has to be damaged in close range” thing is fine. It’s a pain in the ass for some builds, but that’s what map/rare mods are for. That’s fine. Keep that.

Just do it better. It is implemented so, so, SO poorly, it makes me want to rip my eyelashes out.

What’s the range? Like half a meter? It feels like I have to be literally inside the enemy to do them damage. That’s ridiculous. This shit should be a PRESENCE modifier and be 3-4m, not “now kiss.”

There are like, 5 other modifiers that looks sort of like Proximal Tangibility, which to be fair, basically doesn’t look any different than a regular monster.

This should be one of the MOST OBVIOUS MODS in the entire list.

It doesn’t even get special colored text?

What game does GGG think people are playing where we have anywhere close to the fraction of a second it takes to read a list of modifiers? They have not given us a game where we have time to read things. They have given us a game that kills us in 0.0025 seconds if we stop paying attention to the 5000 things happening at once.

Where’s the equalizer? Where is the “can’t be damaged in close range” one that fucks over the other builds in the same capacity? We don’t think its challenging to make the tanky characters step back and try to kill something at range? We’d rather force the squishy characters to go inside the body of rare enemies instead?

It’s just…. Who is in charge here?

TL;DR

Proximal Tangibility needs a larger radius in which you can do damage, and needs to be one of the easiest mods to notice on a rare enemy. They should be glowing pink with a circle around them or something, not “kinda gray? maybe? I dunno, maybe try and read it?” and if you wanna mess up ranged characters SO BAD, why not give melee a taste too? Share the love.
