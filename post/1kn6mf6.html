Breach - go the whole distance!

This might be a unpopular opinion, or some may think I’m ungrateful. But with the resent change to splinter drops, I wish that they would just go the whole distance and drop all breach loot at the end, the same way it already works in the breach pinnacle encounter.

I do appreciate GGG’s effort but currently it doesn’t really accomplish anything. If someone has a good argument why it’s better to limit it to splinters only, feel free to share!
