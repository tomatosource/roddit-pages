Breach - go the whole distance!

This might be a unpopular opinion, or some may think I’m ungrateful. But with the resent change to splinter drops, I wish that they would just go the whole distance and drop all breach loot at the end, the same way it already works in the breach pinnacle encounter.

Although the current change is a great step in the right direction. I still have to go back to pick up all the other loot. Before I had the splinter drop icons on the mini-map to roughly indicate areas of interest. Now I feel like it’s easier to miss things and I have to search more.

I do appreciate GGG’s effort but currently it doesn’t really accomplish anything (Edit: for me personally because I still need to backtrack to pick up/check items and currency and could pick up the splinters at the same time without much effort). If someone has a good argument why it’s better to limit it to splinters only, feel free to share!
