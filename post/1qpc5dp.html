Why did Amari Williams drop so low in the draft?

Strengths:

  • Williams has elite physical tools with a towering frame, absurd length (7-6 wingspan, 9-3 reach), and strong build at 255 pounds.

  • High-IQ passer from the top of the key and low block; averaged 3.2 assists per game and consistently found cutters with impressive timing and angles.

  • Comfortable initiating actions in five-out schemes despite being a non-shooter; projects as a high-post facilitator for second units.

  • Very effective as a finisher in the dunker spot and around the rim (64% FG in halfcourt); uses length and touch well off his left hand.

  • Aggressive offensive rebounder with long arms and good instincts (2.1 OREB per game).

  • Solid post-up scorer at the college level with a go-to left-hand hook and ability to draw fouls (5.1 FTA per game).

  • Reliable interior presence in drop coverage; blocked 2.1 shots per game and lowered opponents’ rim FG% by 6.6% when on court.

  • Smart and active defender who uses length and hand activity to contain guards backing into the paint.

There is also a list of his weaknesses. Like not explosive vertically or quick laterally. Can’t shoot other than FTs. Age factor (a little older).

Still, players his size + length + IQ don’t grow on trees.

As far as I’m concerned, he is a 1st round pick type talent.
