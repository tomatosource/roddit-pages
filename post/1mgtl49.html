[Spoiler] New GGG Interview Heavily Implies new Campaign Maps are coming in 0.3

DarthMicrotransactions (a content creator) was recently invited by GGG to visit their offices in New Zealand and interview GGG developers about their work. There were some comments by GGG employees from the newest environmental artist interviews that heavily hint at new campaign content coming in 0.3

The following is a transcript from the interview with GGG environmental artists that heavily hints that new tile-set(s) for endgame maps was sourced from new campaign content (Bolded parts added for emphasis)

Darth:

“How about keeping variety, I mean you take a lot of campaign maps and turn them into endgame maps, how do you manage to make the maps looks different while also being the same?”

Fabian (GGG dev):

“I’m always thinking about endgame and how I’m always looking at tilesets and what can I take from one and make it feel like its something different, so with the new maps that will be coming in the next patch (0.3) hopefully people enjoy that, but the main thing I look at is I just try to pick 1 element of a whole tileset and try to accentuate that element or take small sections of an area in campaign and try to use that small section to make the entire map (…)”

Blake (GGG dev): “Is there anything you can think of that is like in campaign and the endgame map is like using the same art but retextured in such a different way that you’d think is a completely different map.”

Darth: “That happened to me when you were showing me that one map”

Fabian (GGG dev): “Yeah there is that one map that is coming which I can’t talk about (laughter)”

Blake (GGG dev): “That we can’t talk about (laughter)”

While nothing is directly confirmed, it is very heavily implied and hinted that they are refering to new endgame map(s) that is/are sourced from new campaign map(s) or tileset(s)
