Let me start by saying I stopped playing after Act I, I was just so incredibly bored. So perhaps it gets better later on.

  1. Passives have too many downside. I hardly noticed any difference in damage when leveling up.
  2. Support gems have too many downsides. Or if they don’t have a downside, the don’t increase the damage that much. Hardly noticed any increase in damage. Remember when in PoE1, you would plug ancestral call to your skill and you would instantly do a shitton more damage?
  3. No loot or currency. I tried SSF for the first time after 2500 hours in PoE1. I found ONE usable weapon, my main skill did 88 dps when I tried to fight Geonor.
