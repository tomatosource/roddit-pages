Ron Teasley turned 99 years old today. Teasley is one of two known living people who played in the Negro Leagues when it was considered professional leagues (1920-1948)

Teasley played 2 games for the New York Cubans in the Negro National League in 1948, just before the NNL folded. He never played in the MLB.

The other living player is 101-year-old Bill Greason who played in 8 games for the Birmingham Black Barons in the Negro American League in 1948. Unlike Teasley, Greason did get a cup of coffee in the majors, playing 3 games with the Cardinals in 1954.
