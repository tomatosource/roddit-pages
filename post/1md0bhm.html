Upcoming class (druid)

We all know druid is the favorite guess for next class within the community, and we all know it will involve shape-shifting. Bear tuck’n’roll is firmly trapped in my head and I smile every time my brain decided to replay it like a goddamn reaction gif.

But I can’t stop thinking about the fungal druid boss, and like…can we get some of that?

The occult magic type is a split between chaos DoT and undead minions. So can primal be a split between shape-shifting animal forms and fungal magic?

I’m imagining implanting fungus in enemies to explode or sap their resistances, mushrooms that sit and arm themselves like mines that explode with enemy proximity, clouds of spores that poison enemies, gimme a mushroomancer is what I’m saying.

We have a precedent with the aforementioned boss, he’s even in act 1 for shaper’s sake. What do you folks think? Or are we all deep in the hopium and shadow’s gonna come outta fukkin nowhere?
