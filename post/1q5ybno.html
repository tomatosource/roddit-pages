A dice baseball game my family has played for years finally turned into an app

For years, my family has played a simple dice-based baseball game at restaurants while waiting for food.

No phones. Just dice, rules scribbled on napkins, and a lot of debate over whether something was a double or an out. It became one of those small traditions that kept baseball fun, especially for a kid who loves the game.

Over winter break, my second grader asked if we could turn that same dice game into a real app. So we spent part of the break doing just that. We talked through innings, outs, walks, strikeouts, and what makes baseball feel fair even when luck is involved.

One thing I didn’t expect was how much he thought about baseball itself. When should a game end? What makes a comeback exciting? Why does batting last matter? He even came up with an idea to pause the game occasionally and encourage players to step away from the phone for a short stretch, his version of a modern 7th inning stretch.

We ended up finishing the app, but honestly the best part was seeing how baseball rules translate outside the field and how much of the game is really about rhythm, patience, and shared moments.

I’m curious if anyone else has family baseball traditions like this, dice games, scorekeeping games, road trip games, anything that keeps the game alive away from the field.
