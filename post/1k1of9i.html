‘I was in a dark place’: How Ty Jerome emerged from the shadows and became the Cavs’ beacon

This is a pretty awesome article about Jerome and his story and connection to Koby Altman and how he almost became a Cav multiple times before finally getting the chance last season. I’ll include some of my favorite nuggets

Cleveland had two picks that year — Nos. 5 and 26. With now-president of basketball operations Koby Altman in charge, Jerome was one of the many players who came to town for a pre-draft visit and dinner. Jerome didn’t conduct a workout. His agent believed he would already be off the board at 26. Altman, a born-and-bred New Yorker, spent two years (2010-12) as an assistant coach at Columbia University before joining the Cavs’ front office. That’s where Altman first met Jerome’s father, Mark, the Harlem native who learned the game on the New York asphalt, coached Ty from a young age and ran the Riverside Church AAU program on the Upper West Side — about two blocks from the Columbia campus.

Surgery isn’t new to Jerome. But getting through it wasn’t easy. He drew on experience. He leaned on friends and family. He spent hours on the phone with teammates, past and present, including Curry, who was plagued by early-career ankle problems that required the creation of specialized support braces that he has worn for 10 years. Jerome received some from the personal Curry collection. He’s used them all year.
