Early Game Monk

Until level 14 and Level 3 Skill gems, Monk (quarterstaff) feels like garbage.

I know there are other weapons to use than Qstaff as monk, but Qstaff shouldn’t suck either. The opening skill selection is terrible at early levels, there is nothing in there that qualifies as a Main skill, not even the t3 gem skills are even Main skills. only at t5 do you finally get a skill that replaces basic auto attack.

Monk is also the ONLY Class that does not have access to increased Damage as a first passive. Sure, attack speed is thematic, but it does not serve the same function as damage especially not the first 10% damage increase. Not even close.

I really hope they buff Monk and Qstaff (low level experience) in 0.5
