Idea for DoT characters

My preferred character is The Witch, and this season I tried to push through to end game using a mostly SSF with currency trade, and occasional purchase (e.g. The Atziri was a bargain for 17%)

Builds - EdictWitch - Path of Exile 2 - poe.ninja

This is her; she can handle most content, but things like Breach are…tough.

Note that the Ninja page lists 45k DPS&lt; but it’s only 33k, (and I have no idea why it says 100% Fire!).

However, unless an ED lich DPS is massive, it takes…time… to kill mobs. While their health drains, the fastest mobs still close and run into her, which will block her from casting. I die 99% to super-fast coked up white mobs, that all die AFTER they have killed me. Pyrrhic victory indeed.

So I rolled a Deadeye…

OMG

So we have two characters, each does XXX DPS.

The mobs that approach you have &lt;XXX health.

So, the Lich casta her spells, and the mobs all start to die.

The Deadeye fires, and the mobs…die…where they stand...over there!

Everything is not only easier, but also trivial.

How are you dedicated high-level ED/C players doing Breach, or other high-intensity combat?

So, why not have monsters SLOWED when they are affected by any DoT, to give those characters a fighting chance?
