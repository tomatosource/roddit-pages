PSA: If you’re juicing Ritual, DON’T pick “Area is overrun by the Abyssals” desecrated waystone modifier

So this just happened to me, I wanted to warn others before they burn maps the way I just did.

I was fully juicing Ritual maps, had invested heavily into crafting high-quality waystones with pack size, and on one of my maps I desecrated a waystone and picked “Abysses have (14–18) additional Pits. Area is overrun by the Abyssal.”

To my shock, this completely removed Rituals from the map.

It was a boss map, the boss was still there, and allowed me to “Complete” the map

No Ritual altars, nothing.

This was on a map that was in the overlap of 3 towers, with a ~40 exalt investment into the waystone and ~3div investment into Tablets. and I basically lost it all because this modifier apparently disables Ritual entirely.

Feels really bad, and I think this should either be clarified in the text or changed so it doesn’t nuke Ritual encounters.

Just wanted to get this out there before anyone else loses a juiced Ritual map this way.
