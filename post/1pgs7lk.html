Warrior remains without a single efficient “main skill”.

One of the main reasons why people play LA Deadeye so much its because Lightning Arrow itself is a main skill. The warrior doesn’t have a single viable non-clunky clear skill.

(If you consider the slow motion MOVEMENT SKILL Leap Slam a decent main skill, this post is not for you).

I get it, I get it, devs want this to be a combo based game, and I’m sort of ok with this, but I don’t want to press a 3-4 button combo to clear a pack of white mobs, I’m fine doing it for a rare, magic pack or bosses, but I can’t understand why did they gut so many of the skills and made most of them situational or combo reliant only.

- A mage will have their main clear (Spark, Arc, Fireball, Ember Fusillade, etc)

- A ranged attack class will have their main clear (LA, Galvanic Shards, Tornado Shot, etc)

- A monk will have their main clear (Charged Staff, Ice strike, Tempest Flurry, etc)

- A summoner will have their minions

- A warrior has nothing. Unless you use a slam, fissure, warcry combo you don’t have a main clear skill.

Why can’t they unfuck Sunder for example? Why does the warrior need to be lifted by an invisible crane that takes 3s to then perform the attack?

Why can’t rolling slam (most useless skill ever) be turned into an average hitting Ground Slam?
