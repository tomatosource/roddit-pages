In 4 playoff runs as a Knick, Jalen Brunson has averaged 27.8, 32.4, 29.4 and 28.4 PPG. All higher than Larry Bird’s best ever postseason run as a Celtic (27.5).

Brunson still gets talked about like a guy who happens to score a lot, not a real playoff scorer. Four postseasons in New York and he’s scoring with the greats.

317 players have appeared in 55+ playoff games for a single team. Only four have averaged more with one franchise than Brunson’s 29.4 with the Knicks:

Jordan 33.4 with the Bulls

Iverson 30.6 with the Sixers

LeBron 30.1 with the Cavs

Kareem 29.7 with the Bucks

Since he got to New York he’s first in playoff points and second in playoff wins, and the Knicks have gotten out of the first round every year he’s been here.

Not bad for the 33rd pick. At some point we’re going to have to say it out loud: he’s on his way to being one of the best playoff scorers who ever did it.
