Surprising Player Comparison – 8 Year Runs for 2 All-Time Greats

This is an interesting player comparison that I stumbled on while perusing BBREF.  Two all-time great players who go about business very differently.

The below is a comparison of the peak 8-year stretches for these two players. Interested in the blinded opinions from the crowd.

Question 1: Who was the better scorer?

To minimize era effects, I added rate and possession statistics, as well as comparisons to the top of the league (seasons as league leader, seasons in league Top 5 for each category).

Unblinded, I think nearly 100% of NBA fans would have said player B is the better scorer prior to looking at the statistics. A majority would probably guess B has better overall peak as well. I was in both of these majorities.  However, I left the exercise thinking it’s roughly a wash on scoring, and I would probably lean toward Player A’s overall 8-year stretch.
