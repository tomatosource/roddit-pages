The difference between a good build and bad build is far too large

I was cruising as a varashta, speeding through t15s and melting bosses. Decided to try chrono ed/c with black flame covenant with flameblast incinerate.

The difference was just night and day. I had to spend 2d for a castaway for enough gold to respec since I bricked my character. Niche / mid tier builds need to be brought WAYYYY up imo. Hopefully it’ll happen after the big release. There’s also a huge lack of nodes for ignite on the sorc tree.

Also, the lack of fluid feeling spell rotations for spells other than lightning spells is outrageous. There needs to be a couple of just straightforward spells that interact well with each other out of the box

For example, lightning you have orb of storm spark and mana tempest and you clear the screen, but for ice what do you have? Frost bolt and nova? Nova and ice bomb? Those combos just don’t feel fluid or good to play.

Needs to be 2 or 3 spells / attacks for all spell types that just work fluidly together imo. I’m optimistic that will come once th have the game released.

But yea rant over, jusy a little fed up of being shoehorned into the same spells and such
