The Phillies now have 4 comeback wins after being down to their final strike. The single season record is 5.

The Phillies have done this on back to back nights, and 4 times total this season:

  • Jun 24: Down by 1 w/2 outs in the 9th, Both Schwarber and Hill have 2 strike counts (Walk and HR respectively)
  • Jun 23: Down by 2 w/2 outs in the 9th, Turner has a 2 strike count (Hits a single)
  • Apr 30: Down by 1 w/2 outs in the 9th, Schwarber has a 2 strike count (Hits a double)
  • Apr 1: Down by 2 w/2 outs in the 9th, Bohm and Sosa have 2 strike counts (Walk and 2-RBI Single)

The all-time MLB record for comebacks when down to the last strike is the 1991 Tigers, who have 5*.

For reference, we are just under halfway through the season.

Incidentally, I can’t find any record of anybody realizing that the 1991 Tigers are the only MLB team to have come back 5 times in a season after being down to their final strike. So if the Phillies do tie or break this record somebody needs to give them a nickname or highlight it on their 2026 team wikipedia page or something.

-—

*: As a big caveat, this only counts seasons where we have pitch sequence data, which is most games from 1988 onwards and all of them from 2000 onwards. We have a few more decades of data for comebacks down to their last out, and the record for that would be 8 wins by the 1991 White Sox. Interestingly, the 2026 White Sox already have 5 such comeback wins down to the last out, but none of them were down to their last strike. The White Sox don’t get deep in the count for their miracle comebacks.
