State of Melee Warrior?

So how do you evaluate the new changes as fellow melee warrior enthusiasts?

I’m pretty torn. It seems, there are quite a few nerfs with some buffs here and there. I’ll try to summarize, what I read:

Buffs:

  • Getting 100% Hit chance with less Accuracy seems to get easier.

  • All skills damaged buffed in higher Skilllevel (so melee skill level scaling will still be the way to go), if not stated otherwise (see Perfect strike)

  • easier to get elemental exposure (Frostbomb…)

  • reduced attribute requirements

  • mana cost of skills reduced, especially in high skill levels.

Nerfs:

  • BLOCK… wtf? Max Block Chance is 50% and it’s harder to scale (reduced numbers on incresed Block chance affixes), also the “1% increased damage per 1% block chance” nodes took a massive hit with this.

  • Giant’s Blood will be easier to achieve attribute wise but halfs the amount of life gained per strength

  • increases to aoe in the passive skill tree will be reduced by around 25%. That hurts, man.

  • whats up with armour? There will be affixes and nodes to apply armour on elemental hits, but after or before resistances? And I didn’t need better Armour for elemental damage, but for big physical hits…

  • elemental weakness aka Flammability curse is dead for warriors. Low Lvl Skills won’t apply the effect on hight Lvl mobs.

  • Perfect Strike took a substantial damage nerf, tho got more range and the hitbox got fixed.

  • Will the changes to sunder make it single damage viable? (No more 100% crit chance, but some eleviated armourbreak shenanigans, didn’t quite get it)

  • Deepest Tower and Infernoclasp taken out of the game? Thats two big ones. How will we scale endgame damage? Crit? Thats a gamble on slow hitting skills.

  • Smith of Kitava nerfed (less life regen, less fire res and no max fire res on body armour) With infernoclasp out and the bodyarmour node gone it will be much harder to get to 90% ele res.

  • Banners got buffed massively. Will it help with endgame single target damage?

What did I miss? How do you view these changes for melee warrior? Will you still try and play it this league?
