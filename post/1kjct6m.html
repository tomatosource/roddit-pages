Endgame feels empty for new player

Hello,

I’ve started PoE1 multiple times throughout the years because I was really liking the idea of complex char build system. yet I’ve never even completed campaign because I’ve never enjoyed char controls.

Now with wasd in PoE2 I finally could enjoy the game, learning about all the systems, crafting and char building. I didn’t want to build by a guide so I’ve rerolled char 6times before building somewhat strong build myself.

The whole rerolling, campaigning and learning details about the game was extremely fun and I was looking forward to endgame that everyone in PoE1 was praising all this time.

But the shock I got into in endgame in PoE2 was huge. There is no purpose. No real progression. No depth. Just running boring maps again and again. Every map feels identical. Rare mobs are extremely far apart sometimes not even seen on big map. I keep playing because I’m proud I made usable char without single guide, but playing the game is soulless. 370exalted orbs without need to use them. My build is working fine, I don’t need to make any more currency, I don’t need any more skills or skill slots even tho these are the only bit of dopamine I still get when I add one more gem support slot, then I realize there are no more good support gems to slot in and I just scroll repeatedly through the list of them.

Is this what I experience common or did I just had too high expectations for the endgame? Will this just get better with time and patches or began again every league.
