#1 Overall Picks by Conference: The gap is insane

Here’s every #1 pick in the last 20 years that each conference has gotten

West

  • Greg Oden

  • Blake Griffin

  • Anthony Davis

  • Karl Anthony Towns

  • Deandre Ayton

  • Zion Williamson

  • Anthony Edwards

  • Victor Wembanyama

  • Cooper Flagg

East

  • Andrea Bargnani

  • Derrick Rose

  • John Wall

  • Kyrie Irving

  • Anthony Bennett

  • Andrew Wiggins

  • Ben Simmons

  • Markelle Fultz

  • Cade Cunningham

  • Paolo Banchero

  • Zaccharie Risacher

When the West gets the 1OA there’s a good chance there’s a franchise changing guy like AD, Wemby, or Flagg available. Zion/Oden were also generational but disappointed because of injury/Moriah Mills. Meanwhile when the East gets 1OA they have to pick through a pile of doodoo, and most of the little elite talent get their career wrecked by injury (Wall/Rose). Cade is the only exception, assuming (and hoping) he stays healthy. The talent gap between conferences is partly related to this, just blind luck in what years that conference gets the top pick.
