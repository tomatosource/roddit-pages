What existing class are you hoping to be re-worked just like the Gemling in the future?

They really did great with the overhaul of the Gemling (we are definitely expecting a nerf, but we can - for now - safely that they can’t heavily nerf it that hard as this is the only league that it has been playable again since 0.1).

My take, and as always a Monk main - Chonk and Voker.

Martial Artist proves that having a defensive ascendancy node doesn’t actually hurt the game WHILE still having the Spirit points (same with the WitchHunter). Hopefully GGG could reconsider this stance for the Chonk.

Chonk overall is decent, it could be given further tweaks or better yet - for the narrative - make the Chonk really feel like the Acolyte of Chayula, a class that primarily specializes in Chaos / Chaos melee.

Invoker right now (or ever since 0.1) is of 2 things only: Crit Ele, and more Spirits, but the current state of the game already proves that even having a decent spirit is fine to completely enjoy the whole game, my personal take for the Voker would be:

  1. Monk already have a passive node for “do a melee damage will increased the spell damage”, but it doesn’t seem to hold that much for now. What I was thinking instead is having the Invoker (by the very essence of the ascendancy name itself) invoke the right of converting all ice / lightning spells melee scale damage as its unique ascendancy specialty, this way, the Voker would have further arsenals to try and a more potential, expansive build outcome since it only specializes in Ice or Lightning only (the challenge definitely is the damage conversion, on how the devs could make it effective converting to melee scale).

  2. Another thing that I am considering with the Voker again is that - let one of the final ascendancy point focus-boost the prime element that you are aiming to go with, instead of having all different elements be the attack / power buff, this way the scaling would be more sensible and the ascendancy point would make better sense for the general aspect of the Invoker (since the dude as mentioned before only specializes on either ice or lightning).

Lastly, and this is a very personal take: WE NEED MORE WIND SKILLS, esp a wind strike skill the likes of Ice Strike, and the Vaulting Impact improvement or reworked, I think Vaulting Impact would benefit if it’s a 2 or 3 slam animation (even with a reduced damage) than just 1 - I’ve been trying to cooked this skill since 0.1 and having 1 slam for it still feels too underwhelming even if the intention is to combo it. Also, HERALD OF WIND please? (maybe like, a successful stun or successful armour break will trigger a wind nova around the player? Just a random idea).
