All the WBC news you may have missed today

Since the Winter Meetings began today, reporters had an opportunity to speak to execs, managers and players about World Baseball Classic participation. Here’s a rundown, since MLB refuses to do anything like this officially despite the success of the 2023 WBC, anyways:

  1. Byron Buxton is on the 35-man preliminary roster for the USA
  2. Aroldis Chapman is on the 35-man preliminary roster for GB
  3. Geraldo Perdomo says he will play for the DR
  4. Junior Caminero was officially announced to play for the DR
  5. Roki Sasaki says he will play for JPN
  6. Harry Ford says he will play for GB
  7. Shohei Ohtani may or may not pitch for JPN, but he will hit
  8. Garrett Crochet (USA) and Roman Anthony (USA/ITA) aren’t expected to play

Some other tidbits: 17 year old Joseph Contreras is on the 35-man preliminary roster for BRA, Miguel Cienfuegos and Logan Allen are in talks with PAN, Alan Rangel says he will play for MEX, Yu Chang wants to play for TWN
