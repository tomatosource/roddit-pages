Couple of notes.

Trout’s age 33 season is ongoing reasonably well so far. He needs 0.9 WAR to get to 16th (highly likely) and 4 WAR to get to 14th (possible if not necessarily likely.) To get to 13th would require 8.5 WAR (practically impossible.)

#1 hitter by fwar through age ~24, ~25, and ~26 is Ty Cobb.

The only pitcher to have more fwar by age 32 than Trout is Walter Johnson. (But fwar is not a great measure for pitchers who played 100 years ago.)

Trout’s age 28 season was the Covid-shortened 2020. Simply prorating Trout’s 2020 to a 162 game season would give him an extra 4.1 WAR.

In this hypothetical scenario, Trout’s rank would be

~28: 2nd

~29: 4th

~30: 4th

~31: 7th

~32: 12th

~33: 14th
