Final Take: JT ain’t Lebron(Ragebait)

Hi guys,

I’ve uploaded 2 very divisive takes over the past few days, and by now a lot of you probably hate me, some might agree with me.

1st take: JB for Giannis
2nd take: JB = Zach Lavine?

I just want to say I’m okay with the hate, since at the end of the day, I know we all want the best for the Celtics, and that’s to win another title.

With that being said, here’s my final take. The reason I’m so supportive of trading JB for Giannis is because JT ain’t a Lebron or a Jordan, meaning he’s not a top 2~3 player currently or a top 15 player of all-time. I consider him more of a top 40~50 player of all time which is an incredible accomplishment, considering the legends that are up there with him.

That being said, if JT were as good as Lebron, I’d be fine with having JB as the 1B of the team, b/c I would know that JT alone would guarantee a 70+% chance at a finals, with a solid chance to win it. But I see JT as more of a Pippen or Dwade level player. Top 40 all time sure, but looking at nba history, 80% of all titles are won with top 15 all time players as your 1A, not top 40.

So what’s your best bet at a title when you have a top 40 all time player on your squad? It’s pretty simple, you bring in a top 15 player to the team and make that top 40 player your 1B. You have pippen on your squad? You bring in Jordan. You have Dwade on your squad? You bring in Lebron. You have Paul Pierce on your squad? You bring in KG. You have JT on your squad? You bring in Giannis.

With JB’s trade value at an all time high, and Giannis possibly available, I don’t see why we shouldn’t pull the trigger for a shot at an all time team. The risk is there, but sometimes you have to risk it for the biscuit, rather than be satisfied with JT/JB.

Thanks for the engaging comments as always guys. Hope the celtics win the next one regardless of what happens. In Brad I trust
