The Aberration is better to farm than I expected.

Been looking for a boss to farm that doesn’t need a massive upfront investment. I ended up trying The Aberration (the expedition space crab). Invites were just under 0.5d when I bought them, so I picked up 50 invites for 22 div just to see if it was worth doing.

I have a pretty mid build (Gas grenade pathfinder), and the boss was still really quick. I averaged just under 2 minutes per run including loading screens, so the 50 invites took 1 hour 28 mins.

I converted all of the ores into uniques to try for good rolls. I might have hit one decent Duality that’s worth around 10-20 div, but it hasn’t sold yet, so I haven’t included it in the profit. Everything else rolled pretty badly. I probably would’ve made another 1–2 div just selling the ores instead, but it wasnt going to add much.

  • Cost: 22 div
  • Revenue: 88 div
  • Profit: 66 div
  • Profit per invite: 1.32 div
  • Approx. div/hour: 45 div

It’s definitely a drop pool where a few items do the heavy lifting, but even with that in mind the overall profit felt very solid. At the current invite price I’d definitely recommend giving it a try if you’re looking for a farm that doesn’t require a big upfront cost.

I’ll probably run a larger sample at some point, but first I want to test a few other bosses and compare the results. There are 100% better things out there but it was very solid.
