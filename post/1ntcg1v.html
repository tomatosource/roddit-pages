Do the Celtics currently have the single worst “farm system” in the NBA?

I mean this as a genuine question. Obviously being the most successful team over the last 5 years is the main culprit as Brad traded any notable prospects for veterans and we’ve exclusively had selections deep in the draft.

But presently, if every NBA team had their prospect pool ranked in the same way many publications rank MLB “farm systems”, would Boston rank dead last?

That’s not to say someone like Hugo Gonzalez can’t surpass expectations, but if you’re looking at every team’s top players under 25, Hugo being a late 1st round pick with expected “rotation” player potential (I read his best case scenario is someone like Bruce Bowen) likely isn’t even having him currently crack the Top 100 “under-25” players.

Then beyond Hugo, if you’re trying to figure Boston’s top 3 current prospects, I suppose the #2 rated guy in our “farm system” is Jordan Walsh - a 2nd rounder who has showed next to nothing through two seasons and looks just as likely to be out of the league in a couple years than playing rotation minutes.

I struggle to even figure out who our 3rd rated prospect even is. Our 2nd rounder, 23 year old Amari Williams?

Legitimately, is there a single team with a lower rated Top 3? Even the Lakers, who don’t have much in terms of developing youth, almost certainly rank ahead of us with 24 year old Dalton Knecht and 20 year old Bronny James showing more so far than any guy we have.

And then there are many teams with several guys with top-tier potential. Again, that’s not to say they will not all bust, but you can basically pick any team at random and go “oh yeah I’d 100% rather be spending this season developing any one of Bilal Coulibaly, Bub Carriginton, Alex Sarr, etc over every single prospect on our roster and it’s not even remotely close.”

It’s semi a bummer, because we’ve lost some major talent this Summer without making any tangible addition to our prospect pool. If Tatum doesn’t return, you’re looking at season best spent developing youth, but no amount of developmental minutes is going to help a guy on a trajectory to playing in Mongolia in a few years to suddenly being an impactful player on an NBA contender. Without a single guy with star potential to develop, it does feel like a waste of a season.

Can you name a team with a weaker prospect pool?
