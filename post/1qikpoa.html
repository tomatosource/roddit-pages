Victor Wembanyamama has a message to the All-Star players, “..if they don’t play hard, I’ll do it without them.”

Source: https://www.basketballnetwork.net/latest-news/victor-wembanyama-vows-to-force-nba-stars-into-a-high-intensity-all-star-battle

Victor Wembanyama when asked about what it means to be part of the All-Stars:

“I want to push the great players of this sport to play in the All-Star game just as hard as I will. We’ll see how it goes, But if they don’t play hard, I’ll do it without them.”
