As a newcomer I really enjoying the game

I hesitated very long before purchasing the early access of this game, I was casually playing Diablo IV but I was getting bored, the “crafting” is very limited to put slots on equipment and put gems in them. The gameplay is about the same, no matter the class, the is no interaction or very little interaction bringing combat to life. In POE2 this is the contrary, movements, interaction with the decor like hidding behind a pilar, sprinting, rolling all those thing are as much important than actually casting spell and using abilities. Of course the experience in Diablo IV is more casual, and the story is more developed but the sensations in Diablo IV compared to POE2 are completely different. In fact I think both game are not comparable or similar. Both are ARPG like but Diablo seems a lot more static and simplistic. I was really afraid to be disappointed by POE2 and I must admit it is quite the opposite. This game is challenging, combats are intense and dynamic, gear crafting possibilities are phenomenal and so is the skilltree. I love it ❤️
