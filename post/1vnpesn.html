Mana b4 life melee

Discovered mana before life this league with the gemling arc totem build, tried to incorporate the Alkem Eira shield into the build but couldn’t make it work as I wanted.

After feeling like my sanity was slipping away while chasing voices I decided to take a break and pursue an idea that came to me during those long and boring simulacrum sessions: build a mana before life melee char.

Leveled a new gemling with a bow and block quiver to partially keep with the theme of char, it was surprisingly effective. Switched to Cadiro in early maps.

Gear on the char is partially repurposed from the arc totem build.

early map gear

current gear

Atlas tree is full int, jewels, crit and some notables for block.

Body armour: started with Cloak of Defiance before switching to a Morior I had used on the arc build once I started using the unique rings to compensate the mana before life affix on the cloak. A full ES body armour would give more mana and allow for the desecration that frees some spirit or mana before life. I the morior and preffered having some armour/evasion/deflection.

Rings: I used Dream Fragments and mirrored it in the later variants, rare rings would be better (more mana, rarity/flat damage/different resists) but I was lazy and didn’t feel like crafting one/s (you need the breach desecrate that gives 12% mana before life).

Jewels are what I had in the stash except the 5 affix ones which I crafted. The idea would be to replace all of the 4 affix with some more mana before life jewels and attack speed in order to liberate some atlas points and try to get pain attunement.

Helmet: vertex with equipment has no attribute req is needed.

One of the big hurdles was getting stunned so got Unwavering stance.

  • movement speed: shield charge (massive distance bonus on gemling)
  • no dodge roll: leap slam to jump out of danger

Belt: Since I had a mageblood (ms/rarity/all resists) from the arc build I used it to partially compensate the lower mobility (I’m used to sprinting a lot between packs and dodge rolling) and handle a good portion of my resists. Without one you’d definitely need resists on gloves/boots/ring

Gloves: wanted an excuse to use a the jewel socket rune so threw it on a pair of mid gloves I had around.

Shield: Alkem Eira set1 and Redblade set2

Skill:

  • Perfect strike: play with attack speed to get it “on click” for ease of use, makes life much easier.
  • Sunder: backup damage if PS on click is “broken”. Tried it for map clear but found it didn’t freeze enough to trigger good clear with herald of ice so switched full time to perfect strike. Have significantly improved the damage since then so maybe sunder could also work.
  • Thaumaturgic generator + charge regulation for added crit chance and armor/ev. In order to increase the probability of power charges (crit chance) I gave up on shield charge and leap slam (and died a few times because I did :D)
  • Blasphemy: ele weakness + temp chain (I use it to get to low life too)
  • Cast on ailement: played around with it as a host for differnt spirit supports and with detonate dead. Herald of Ash is potentially a better use for the skill slot.

Tested the char against arbiter of divinity, simulacrum and t15/16 deli maps:

  • Bosses are easy, you can tank most/all of their hits, you freeze them and kill them very fast. Arbiter fight before being low life and having ele weakness in blasphemy: https://imgur.com/a/rMrkq3k .
  • Sim is hard but doable with a not so slight chance you’ll get swarmed/slowed and die. Temp chains greatly help with control, don’t try it without as I did and lost half a bar of progress.
  • Hard maps are OK, blowing up packs with a perfect strike is quite satisfying. Slows/chills that “break” the on click perfect strike pose problems so some care is needed. Too high rage poses a problem too if like me you don’t get any/enough life regen. The weapon swap handles that by cutting the max rage (berserk is deactivated on it) and having some life regen runes in the shield)
