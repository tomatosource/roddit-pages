Mets take season series against Braves

The Mets have been letting their fans down this year but don’t tell that to the Braves. Despite their near three week difference in the standings the Mets have beaten the Braves for the 7th time in 11 games. With 2 games left the Mets have clinched the season series and unlike against most other teams (the Yankees are another exception so far but only with one series) they seem to be earning their money against a franchise that has historically gotten the better of them.

I wonder what Mets and Braves fans are thinking.
