We desperately need more skill slots in 0.5

With more skills being added to the game and more combo pieces, movement abilities, and spirit gems being given to players, we desperately need more skill slots. I tend to play more combo-heavy builds like quarterstaff, spears, and talismans, and every single build I play runs out of skill slots fairly quickly. Builds that require 2-3 skills for a main combo, another 1-2 for charge generation or ailment application, a buff skill like a warcry/barrage, a movement skill like pounce, a debuff skill like marks or curses end up with no room for spirit buffs, especially if you’re playing a class that gets bonus spirit or reservation efficiency like tactician or invoker.

GGG, I really enjoy the high skill expression of combo builds and the way I can multiply my damage output by executing a multi-step combo properly, but we absolutely need more slots to be able to equip the required skills!

Unset rings are NOT a good solution. Not only do they make you miss out on a huge chunk of res from ring implicit, which is already very tight for most builds considering the suffix pressure, but the prevent you from using interesting unique rings and don’t do enough to solve the skill slot issue.
