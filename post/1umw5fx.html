On-death effects: Sometimes harmless, sometimes a one-shot

Hey guys,

Is it just me, or are these on-death explosion effects actually terrible design? Lately, I’ve been seeing an insane amount of them in my maps.

https://preview.redd.it/iibc9kc2a4bh1.png?width=1506&format=png&auto=webp&s=29c4898512ecea0778ffd7897f42b2178f04e225

The biggest issue is that sometimes these effects do almost zero damage, but other times they completely one-shot you. And as far as I know, there is absolutely no way to tell which one is harmless and which one is lethal.

Especially in grand expeditions when these bombs combine with reflect chill, it becomes impossible to dodge everything. You end up just randomly moving to a less crowded spot, and if you guess wrong, you instantly die. Honestly, these things are responsible for about 90% of my deaths lately.

Wouldn’t the game be so much more playable if they just changed the visual intensity based on the damage? For example, making the ring thicker, brighter, or having higher flames for the high-damage ones. At least that way players would have an idea of the incoming damage. What do you guys think?
