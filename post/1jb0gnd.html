“Quick” Money Maker For New Players

Playing POE2 for a while now, played A LOT of POE1. Endgame POE2, I have always thought experience is for maps (haven’t had much luck there in currency) and trials are for currency. I know the market is ALWAYS going to fluctuate but as of the game right now, 1 level 80 Trial of Chaos will get you 3 soul cores (cheapest being 22 exalt right now) and 1 fate piece (cheapest being 120ish exalt).

Means half an hourish, will get you 200 exalt, if you complete it. Trials of Chaos can be difficult, especially when you just hit that level 80 mark, but usually Inscribed Ultimatums are a dime a dozen. 200 exalt can do a lot for your build at that level. I’m just saying, if you’re not going to use the fates or the soul cores, Alva is right there.

When I got into endgame, it felt impossible to make money. When you can run trials, do so. Floor is 200 exalt (right now). Ceiling is like 4.5 divine. (One soul of Azcapa and one Deadly Fate.)
