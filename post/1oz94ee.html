Translations of the top ten most liked comments on a Japanese Yahoo Sports article discussing the participation of Japanese Dodgers players in the WBC

Here’s the article: https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/e964320c92e8dfecb4472e16341f7f6904947768/comments

User 1:

As a Japanese Dodgers fan, I would like all Dodgers players to refrain from participating in this year’s WBC.
It’s been a mentally and physically tough season and playoffs not only for Shohei Ohtani and Yoshinobu Yamamoto, but for everyone as well, so moving the start of the 2026 season up by a month seems like nothing but a risk…
It’s a difficult decision after being so impressed with the previous WBC, but as a fan, I would like to see them perform well this season without any injuries.

User 2:
Honestly, the team’s true feeling is that they don’t want players to participate in the WBC. For Ohtani, if he plays only as a hitter, the strain on his shoulder and elbow might be less, but starting the season earlier could make him more fatigued by the playoffs. At the very least, I hope Yamamoto gets enough rest. In Japan, choosing not to play in the WBC shouldn’t be seen negatively—it should be recognized as a legitimate choice for the players.

User 3:
Of course, from the perspective of a manager on the ground, the truth is they don’t want the players to participate in the WBC. All three Japanese players are pitchers, after all. Some say Ohtani could play as DH only, but even then, he wouldn’t be able to pitch during that period, which is a clear disadvantage. It makes sense that the Dodgers are cautious about sending players to the WBC.

User 4:
Exactly. The previous WBC was amazing—hard to imagine surpassing that. Ohtani had a fantastic season, but it’s clear he got fatigued more easily. Yamamoto was overused in the playoffs, and even just maintaining the rotation and fighting through the postseason next year will be tough. Sasaki has a critical season ahead. Right now, only these players can aim for a three-peat. I want them to do it in a situation where they can give their all.

User 5:
In 2023, COVID restrictions eased and many global events resumed. Players had more freedom, so participating must have been fun. But for 2026, I wonder. Ohtani got injured after participating, and many players lost form once the season started. I hope it’s exciting, but it’s complicated.

User 6:
Even without WBC, Ohtani bears twice the workload of an average MLB player, both hitting and pitching. Teammates often talk about how extreme it is. Seeing young two-way talents in the majors having to focus on just one role shows how intense it is. Please, to protect this global treasure, I hope Ohtani doesn’t participate.

User 7:
Ohtani worked full-time in the last WBC but got injured that year. Fatigue probably played a part, but the WBC peaks in March, meaning players need to be in top condition earlier than the regular season. The physical burden is definitely higher than normal. I agree with Manager Roberts’ stance that Ohtani and Yamamoto should ideally skip it.

User 8:
If Ohtani were on a team like the Angels, far from the playoffs, motivation and stamina for the WBC would be higher. But this World Series was extremely tough, so even as an Ohtani fan, I want him to take the offseason slow. Especially for Yamamoto, I feel he absolutely shouldn’t participate. That said, I admire players like Betts, Smith, Freeman, who played in the playoffs and still participated in the WBC.

User 9:
Considering Ohtani and Yamamoto give fans joy and inspiration all season, I want them to focus first on staying in perfect shape for the MLB season. There’s also the dream of a three-peat. I also can’t imagine Ohtani playing under Manager Iba in Japan.

User 10:
At the very least, the Dodgers should clearly indicate that Yamamoto and Sasaki are not allowed to participate. Yamamoto has thrown so much that fatigue is inevitable, and playing could risk injury. Sasaki is coming off an injury and aims to return as a starter next year, so non-participation is essential. Ohtani might play DH only if he really wants. Honestly, all Japanese MLB players could just skip the WBC.

What do American fans think?
