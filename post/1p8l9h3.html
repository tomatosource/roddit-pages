Your Ideal Pitcher’s Duel?

I’ve asked this question before, but I feel it’s been long enough to reignite this discussion.

What kind of pitcher’s duel would be absolutely perfect in your mind?

I’d like you to describe the kind of stats and performance you’d like to see from both pitchers, but if you don’t feel like it, you can just give me an example of a past game that meets the criteria. And by the way, it’s anything goes.

For example, my perfect pitcher’s duel, which I now call a “Duel For The Ages” is comprised of 4 crucial details:

-7+ IP

-3 or fewer runs allowed

-100+ pitches thrown

-6+ strikeouts

And that goes for both starters.
