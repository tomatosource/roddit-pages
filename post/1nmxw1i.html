The crafting options have definitely improved. The accessability and intuition on how to craft good results? Not so much.

I am still hoping that PoE2 tries to make crafting good gear much more intuitive to understand in comparison to PoE1, especially when it comes to the current reliance on 3rd party tools outside the game.

Two examples:

  1. Omen of Whittling removes the “lowest level modifier.”

Even if you didn’t fall in the seemingly common pitfall of thinking that this removes the lowest (worst) mod tier and instead knew that the “level” relates to the minimum item level for that tier of this specific mod to roll, there is no way for you to know that minimum item levels of your item’s mods without looking them up on 3rd party websites outside the game.

The highlighting is a good step in the right direction, but doesn’t help you plan the craft when Whittling is the step after the current one.

  1. The new Abyss omens like “guarantee a random Amanamu modifier” are impossible to evaluate ingame without knowing what an Amanamu modifier is. This is stated nowhere ingame, and there are multiple omens/modifier lists like this from Abyss alone (3). Yet again, you have to dig through 3rd party databases on the web.

Please expand your design philosophy of having important stuff (like we’ve seen with the ingame trading UI) in the game, not on 3rd party websites, to also include everything crafting-related.

The Early Access is the best time to implement fundamental intuition for crafting that the inevitable content creep over the coming years with more and more crafting options and items can be build upon.
