Last MVP to play all 82 games in a season: Kobe Bryant

In the 2007-08 season, Kobe Bean Bryant appeared in every regular season game.

Interview from the 2015 “Grantland Basketball Hour” with Jalen Rose & Bill Simmons w/ guest host Kobe Bryant (42:36 mark)

Bill Simmons: question: “Kobe, you only won one MVP award?”

Kobe: “Yeah”

Simmons: “Why?”

Kobe: “Well because the media votes on it… listen, it was never a mission of mine to win a bunch of MVPs; it was to win a lot championships. With that being said, does it bother me? Yeah, it bothers me.”

Simmons: “At least you won more finals MVPs”

Kobe: “That’s the thing that matters the most, winning those things. But you do want to win as many as you can”

Rest in peace Black Mamba.
