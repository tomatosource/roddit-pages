Some improvement and QOL improvements for Atlas

I’ve been playing since 0.1, and while I’ve played poe1 before, I’ve never managed to complete a character to reach mapping and have been quitting before the campaign (I mean 10 acts is waaay too long too). I’ve also played other games like Last epoch , and those games do some things better which I think PoE2 could benefit from.

Here are the changes I think will help:

  1. Rarity on Items is bad. We should not be forced to forsake power from gear to farm when we can’t make up for the power through passive tree yet. Also, rarity by itself doesn’t help, we need quantity which we can only get through tablets. LE has the corruption mechanic where increasing it on a map increases drops, rarity and difficulty. For PoE2, what I propose is completing a corruption nexus or adding any content like breach, ritual etc, scale both quantity and item rarity by default. To this end, a new tablet which adds corruption, with a chance to turn it into a nexus would be great and helps the players to corrupt a juiced map

  2. 10 pc xp loss is too much of a penalty when we reach maps. It’s very rare to hit 100 on off meta classes. Instead, let’s borrow the corpse run mechanic from diablo 2 or souls games . When you die, you loose half of accumulated xp, you have to go the place you died and retrieve your corpse to recover the xp. For maps with 0 revives and trials, the corpse spawns at the waypoint but you only get 25 pc xp. New omens could be added which modify this such as omens which make your corpse spawns at the portal or hideout when you die.

  3. Allow chance orbs to be used on tablets and strongboxes. Chancing tablets clhas a chance to convert them to unique tablets, chancing strongboxes allows to change them to unique strongboxes. To compensate for the increased availability of the unique tablets or strongboxes, give the chancing a fail chance. For e.g, chancing could reduce number of uses, provide bad rolls or even destroy the tablet. Strongboxes have a chance to have their tier reduced or modifiers bricked or even opened with basic drops.

  4. Disenchanting high tier currency and socketables, essences will give lower tier counterparts. For e.g Disenchanting a perfect essence gives 1 to 3 or 1 to 2 greater essence of same type, Disenchanting runes gives 1 to 3 or 1 to 2 lesser runes and so on.
