Act 3 is the most beautiful thing I’ve ever witnessed

I feel like I started at a good time after the nerfs, the game feels good.

Apart from that, Act 3 feels like real life lore and I’m all for it, it’s really amazing. Some neat things I’ve enjoyed about it (mostly finding relations to the Aztecs):

  • The advanced water canals and getting surprised by the little fish “oh look what a cute little fiSH IT HAS LEGS AND IT’S CRAWLING TOWARDS ME HOLY FUCK KILL IT!”
  • The vivid red colors (aztecs invented red dyes), I find them so beautiful
  • The torture chambers (clicking a cadaver and it slices in half, guts getting pulled out through machines, etc.)
  • The sun part where the Vaal citizens are burning themselves (young aztecs were submitted to high temperatures “training” to endure them)

I love EVERYTHING about Act 3. EVERYTHING. Playing through it has me in complete awe, thank you GGG!
