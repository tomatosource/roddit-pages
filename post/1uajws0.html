The Milwaukee Brewers have lost the first game of a series for the first time since May 4

Milwaukee had won the first game of 12 consecutive series until tonight’s 2-3 loss to Atlanta. The previous time they lost Game 1 of a series was Monday, May 4 against St. Louis. They are now 19-6 on the season in Game 1s.

Jacob Misiorowski takes his first loss since April 19 against Miami.
