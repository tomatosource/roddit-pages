Would a guy prefer a jersey with his own name on it or the team’s star player’s name?

I want to buy my boyfriend a jersey from his favorite team for his birthday. I’m thinking of either getting it personalized with his name or getting one with the name of the team’s star player on it.

I know absolutely nothing about basketball, and since it’s a surprise, I can’t ask him what he would prefer. I just wrote down in my notes app what his favorite team is and the name of the guy he always mentions (Wembanyama). So I’d love to get some input! Generally speaking, what do guys tend to prefer? He doesn’t own a jersey yet, so this would be his first one. Also he plays basketball three times a week with friends.

Edit: Thank you guys for saving me from not only my own embarrassment, but also from potentially subjecting my boyfriend to a lifetime of embarrassment. Before posting this, I genuinely thought a jersey with his own name on it would be super cool.

After reading the comments, I now realize that would have been a spectacularly bad decision. So, going with the popular vote: Wembanyama it is!
