SUPER late PSA You can add/modify rooms to temple mid temple

I just tried this out today after finishing up the first loop of my temple. I’m now dropping enough medallions that I was wondering if I could use them before I close temple to save slots for later use like rerolls/locks/rooms that aren’t quite next in line. Mid temple i had locks drop back to back but already had 6 medallions. 2 of them were a room upgrade, the other was a golem works. You have to tp out of the temple, but don’t close the temple like the spymaster trick to lock them. There you use the room upgrade, or place the room, then reenter the temple to finish and picked up the 2 locks. I’m not sure if this is common knowledge, but it has saved me quite a bit of time with efficient use of medallion slots. I don’t recall seeing this in any temple tech videos
