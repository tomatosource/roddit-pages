Players are expected to use combo playstyle, yet monsters ignore this entirely.

I don’t mind combo focused gameplay where we set up pay offs with different abilities for different situations. The disconnect comes when mobs start to be able to reliably one and two tap you and combo gameplay falls apart.

Why should we have to use 2 cycles of 2 or 3 abilities to kill a monster when they have the ability to delete us from off screen with a projectile we never saw fired in the first place?

Why can monsters auto cast their modifier abilities regardless of their animation status?

Why do monsters get essentially free attack and movement actions that are uninterruptible except for hard cc?

If the game is designed around the thought that combat should be meaningful then it makes no sense to only pay attention to the player side of the equation and leave massive differences between player and monster capabilities unchecked.
