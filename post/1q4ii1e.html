Wouldn’t it be easier to just itemize the crystals?

Amidst all the discourse surrounding the temple, there seems to be a constant pain point: casual players have a hard time engaging with the mechanic.
Imo this is mainly due to two factors:

  1. Before killing the architect you have no way of storing multiple temple runs, therefore having to choose between engaging with the temple then and there or permanently losing a potentially valuable resource. Potentially here is the keyword, since you will be seeing returns from your early runs quite a bit down the line. When posed with this choice most people simply opted out of the mechanic altogether.

  2. Players who don’t want to setup/run the temple have no other way to engage with its economy. Juiced temples also require pretty strong characters to run, which might not scale at the same rate as the temple itself. This is not how the rest of the game works. If you can’t be bothered or your build is too weak to run ultra-juiced mechanics/T3s you can always sell your tablets/waystones/splinters for fairly good profits.

Since the trove that you find in maps still feels like shit we might as well drop the crystals directly, thus giving weaker players an easy source of income while providing wealthier players with a faster way to bring their temple up to speed or to further crash the economy. This would also make it consistent with most out-of-map mechanics like TotA, Heist, the trials etc.
