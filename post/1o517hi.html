My Seasons Currency Into a Bone Wand Craft

https://preview.redd.it/6tq1uz9etquf1.png?width=1112&format=png&auto=webp&s=14238c59bd81e730f692d7745303ffabe80b02a0

I wanted to dump all my currency from the season into an endgame item. this is how it turned out.

Step 1 (3 Divine)
buy Exceptional Bone wand base. requirements level 81 and has 2 sockets

Step 2 (20 Divine)
Chaos spam until T1 Spell Physical Damage. this takes about 700 chaos orbs on average.

step 3 (36 Divines)
homogenizing + Dextral Exalt + perfect exalt = +5 Physical Spell Levels
this should be a 1 out of 3-4

i missed 7 times…. this hurt and cost 36 Divines to retry multiple times

Step 4 (1.5 divine)
homogenizing + Dextral Exalt + Greater Exalt + perfect exalt

you can get 2 of these 3. Cast speed, Crit chance and crit damage. cast is very likely but not a guarantee.

Step 5 (this is the lucky part)
Fracture and hope for Spell Physical Damage. I’m not sure you could continue the craft without the fracture

this craft is junk without % increased spell damage.

step 6 (1.5 divine)
homogenizing + Sinistral Exalt + Greater Exalt + perfect exalt

high chance to miss % increased spell damage. i hit first time. if you miss and you do have the fracture you can sin annul and try again this costs about 12 divine. i got lucky and hit first try.

so yah… this was my seasons currency(~65 Divine) put into one craft and I got pretty lucky. the item would have been worthless without the fracture and the lucky rolls after.
