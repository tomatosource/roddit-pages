PoE2 is the first gaming experience that makes me want to open my wallet

Since I started gaming in my childhood, never I thought like buying or seeking in-game cosmetics was worth it. I’ve always thought that it’s a real waste of time and money, even if I understand people that do like having a sick outfit.
