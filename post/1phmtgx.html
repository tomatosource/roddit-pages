PoE2 is the first gaming experience that makes me want to open my wallet

Since I started gaming in my childhood, never I thought like buying or seeking in-game cosmetics was worth it. I’ve always thought that it’s a real waste of time and money, even if I understand people that do like having a sick outfit. PoE2 does something different to me, but I mean overall PoE. These games are so much fun, worth it so much of my time that that I feel ashamed to play such a masterpiece for straight hundreds of hours and not giving a single penny for it. And there’s more!
