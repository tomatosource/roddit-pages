[Gutierrez] BREAKING: Brandon Clarke’s death ruled accidental overdose of cocaine, heroin. The medical examiner also listed as a significant contributing condition the “combined effects of multiple prescription medications.”

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7355567/2026/08/07/brandon-clarke-death-overdose-cocaine-heroin/?unlocked_article_code=1.3lA.b3r2.NrDxpnFdvPis&source=athletic_user_shared_gift_article_twitter&smid=tw-share-ta

Memphis Grizzlies forward Brandon Clarke’s death has been ruled an accidental overdose of heroin and cocaine, according to a report released Friday by the L.A. County Medical Examiner.

Clarke, a beloved teammate who spent his entire NBA career with the Memphis Grizzlies, died May 11 at a residence in Southern California. The 29-year-old had been found unresponsive in his bedroom, according to the coroner’s report. The medical examiner also listed as a significant contributing condition the “combined effects of multiple prescription medications.” Those prescriptions were not named in the report.
