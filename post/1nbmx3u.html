Heavybelt scams on trade

Lately I’ve been seeing shops flooded with heavy white belts all listed at “10 ex.” But mixed in between them, some are actually priced at 10 div.

It tricks buyers into thinking, “Cool, there are so many belts for 10 ex, I’ll grab another one,” and then — boom — you just spent 10 div or more without realizing it.

Feels like a scammy way of catching people who aren’t double-checking prices.
