Jonathan Kuminga is out of the Golden State Warriors’ rotation due to an ankle sprain. The fourth-year forward has emerged as a key part of Steve Kerr’s rotation this season, so his absence is a significant blow to the Warriors’ chances of turning their season around.

According to The Athletic’s Anthony Slater, Kuminga will likely be out of the rotation for multiple weeks due to the severity of the sprain. Slater also noted how the explosive forward hasn’t progressed to doing any work on the court at the moment. Kuminga’s injury was sustained during the Jan. 4 loss to the Memphis Grizzlies.

Kuminga has played in 32 games this season, averaging 16.8 points, 5 rebounds and 2.2 assists. He shoots 45.9% from the field and 34.5% from three-point range. His absence will undoubtedly be a blow to the Warriors. However, they hope he can return closer to or just after the All-Star break.
