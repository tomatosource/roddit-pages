Ownership and the tax

This is just a one year gap year, right?

(Of course I don’t expect anyone to know for sure what Bill & the rest of ownership’s mindset is, but I mean in terms of what you think realistically as it pertains to the team’s goal of being competitive, too.)

I keep seeing people mention the luxury tax and if ownership wants to get out of it, then they have to be out of it for two straight seasons at least.

The CBA isn’t exactly a fun read, but I thought with the luxury tax there aren’t any basketball specific penalties?

I can’t see wasting a year of Jayson’s prime by continuing to barely spend anything in the ‘26-‘27 season. If the reports (and Jayson’s own comments) are accurate and his plan is to come back this season (which I think makes sense to dust off rust and for him to feel more mentally comfortable on the court again), then he should be more than ready next season.

But we also know team owners don’t care about anything more than their money, regardless of what they say publicly.
