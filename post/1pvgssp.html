I know it’s not going to work in Endgame but leveling Druid as hybrid wolf / plants has been fun

I started the league late so I’m only in Act 2 on my first character. Wanted to water plants but also used a talisman in weapon set 2 mainly for mobility in the beginning. I kept thinking I would ditch it but somewhere along the way I realized that it’s actually fun to do both even though I don’t know if there are any build guides for this (a sign that it’s suboptimal). I normally hate the combo stuff (Amazon parry mechanic was some of the least fun I have had in this game) but running around with a wolfpack that does zDPS but distracts enemies for me, pouncing on mobs, watering my plants, and then also freezing and shredding elites and bosses has worked well in the early game. I’m sure I’ll hit a wall soon and have to specialize more but I just felt like posting to share that I was enjoying this gameplay even if it’s off meta to try to do the jack of two trades type of build.
