New Deadeye tech for glory generation makes her better than tactician for banners.

Pob: https://pobb.in/ugM83MnLcpAu

Video of me yapping: https://youtu.be/3mHYe3noLTQ

Banners were typically played on tac before because he had banner specific buffs and a damage boosting aura thanks to ‘watch how I do it’. He also has glory generation for banners, which is really hard to get normally. However, I believe that Deadeye is actually the best bannerbot this league.

Here’s why:
Quipolatl’s thesis, when socketed in a helmet, gives ‘A random Skill that requires Glory generates 15% of its maximum Glory when your Mark Activates’. This might not sound like a ton, but it can be boosted by darkness enthroned, giving you up to 33% of glory. Deadeye just so happens to have a way of automating marks through called shots, which automatically marks enemies, and when they activate, mark new enemies. As long as the person you support has crit investment, they will always proc the mark, giving you 30% glory every time. It does go on a 5 second cooldown every 3 procs (?), but that’s a banner every 5 seconds, which is much higher than a tactician who needs to wait 15-25 seconds.

Of course, this still means that we need to get the last few percent of glory, which is actually really easy. The node ‘glorious anticipation’ gives you 1 glory every 2 seconds per unique or rare monster in your presence. With % increased glory generation (which you get a ton of, thanks to the banner small nodes), you can get 2-3 glory per second in walled maps, and like 10 per second in open maps.

The rest of the setup is up to you. You can get even more banner uptime with fury of the king, which generates an additional 3 glory per second, or companions for permanent haste aura (10% movement speed).

This still means that tactician is better if you want to use all three banners, since he gets glory on every banner, but for this league specifically, that’s not actually super useful. The meta build this league is comet, meaning war banner’s martial specific buffs are not super useful to a huge amount of the playerbase, and dread banner just isn’t very good in general. Movement speed is universally beneficial (except blink temporalis or lightning warp but that’s incredibly niche).

I also avoided a few uniques that are generally taken on aurabot setups. Fixation of yix, for example, only gives heavily diluted stats, and halves your presence radius, which makes glorious anticipation worse. I also avoided evergrasping ring because making all enemies do 20% more damage as chaos makes content incredibly rippy unless everyone has CI. Gain as is also pretty inefficient for casters, who drown in it with wand prefixes.
