In 19-20 season Westbrook had his 3rd best career scoring season. Despite having his lowest 3PT% shooting season since his sophomore year, he had a career high in FG%, FGA and eFG%.

Westbrook’s one season playing in Houston was probably the biggest anomaly in his career. Whilst his rebounds and assists dropped noticeably, his overall scoring efficiency was his best since his MVP season.

He also had a career high in both 2PA and 2P%, which is an insane anomaly.

The two main factors in Houston was Harden, and utilizing Westbrook as a point center, which allowed them to space the floor to a degree.
