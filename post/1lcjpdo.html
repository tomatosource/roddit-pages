Cursed idea for Light Radius

Light radius already controls how far out your minimap gets filled out, iirc. GGG could go one step farther with this.

Increasing light radius should increase how far out you can zoom. This would allow melee characters to ignore light radius, while ranged characters have to stack it. This would solve the problem of “ranged characters are always better than melee”.

For a truly cursed and vomit-inducing implementation, you could automatically zoom in and out based on your character’s light radius changing due to health gain and loss.
