Aesthetics over fun

When I play this game and look at some of the game design decisions, this is the first thing that pops in my mind.

Take into consideration the choice to limit skills to one weapon.

In POE 1, I have played Sword Flicker, Axe Flicker, Dagger Flicker CoC, Claw Flicker, Dual Wield Flicker with two swords, mace and shield flicker and every single one of them was a different flavor of the skill with so much variation between each version. And now in POE 2 I can only play Quarterstaff Flicker. How is this good for the game? It ’s a lazy cop out because they did not want to do animations for multiple weapons. In the interview they just waved this question away saying oh people just go for whichever version does most damage, and this is such a shallow and false answer I was shocked at hearing it. Using different weapons provides so much different utility, especially when you take into account the uniques. Imagine they release something like Starforge and only a handful of skills can use it?

Lets look into Parry next? Did they test this skill in the endgame? I find it very very hard to believe that they did. The devs said they spent two weeks getting this to feel good? Really? I consider that two weeks of wasted time. Yes aesthetically it looks cool, it is even usable when there are 2 or 3 mobs on the screen and they are in front of you, but in maps you get mobbed from all sides how in the world do we use active parry for that? Why couldn’t they just have made it a toggle like retaliate skills in POE 1. Again this decision screams of focusing on aesthetics over fun.

There are many many such instances I have come across but these are the ones that stand out to me.
