Joe Mauer vs. Buster Posey who was the more valuable overall player in both primes and whole careers?

Both MVPs, both HoFers despite playing under 2000 games.

In both their primes: Joe Mauer hit .327 with a 139 OPS+ for 8 years (6.5 WAR per 162 games). Buster Posey hit .309 with a 136 OPS+ for 8 years too (5.9 WAR per 162). Posey was a much better defender and won 3 titles to Joe’s 0. Does that make Posey better despite Mauer’s 3 batting titles and 3 (earned?) gold gloves?

Mauer was a very solid defender for a stretch and ran into a far superior Yankees team in 3⁄4 of his playoff appearances, failing to win a single game against them (0-7).

Posey on the other hand was the back(stop)bone of the San Fran dynasty but it’s worth noting he only had a .667 OPS in the playoffs and batted .216 across the 3 World Series with just 14 hits and 16 games (still some big moments).

Mauer also played 500 more games but has a 124 OPS+ to Posey’s 129. They both struck out and grounded into double plays at similar rates. Joe’s RISP is .331 and I couldn’t find Posey’s but he was decently clutch in the regular season.

So who’s better?
