Minion Focused builds need more active skills

I’ve seen a lot of builds that want to do minions and they turn into basically a person with a shield and a sceptre that sits back and maybe throws out a curse or two. I feel like there needs to be more active skills that support this kind of play style to make it work. Like having buff spells, or spells that play off having minions. A thought would be something like a chain lightning spell that gains strength the more it chains and is able to chain off minions, or maybe a chaos totem that can be placed down that passively heals minions and helps them stay up.

This also kind of goes into the scaling as well with spectres because a lot of spectres need to have keywords added to take advantage of support gems. Even if a minion primarily does fire damage through spells, it lacks the descriptor to take advantage of the +1 fire skill level support gem. Those same keywords could also impact how a spell skill works, so for the “chaos chaining lightning skill” it might gain the benefits from the keywords on the minions so that it does added elemental, physical, or chaos damage.

Also, not complaining about the playstyle of just having a shield and sceptre with letting the spectres do all the work. I just feel like there is no real good support for strict minions in the campaign. It all turns into comboing with chaos and the minions are there for the ride, or just having the minions be incidental and the character is really focused into something else.
