I don’t want atlas towers to be stronger, I want other mechanics to rely on

tl;dr : some want (overlapping) towers to be stronger and more rewarding, but no one likes to search for them. I think they should be left as something that we don’t worry much about, and juicing should come from stuff we don’t hate engaging with.

Some people are pointing out that overlapping towers is the only option we have to really boost the loot of maps, and that it feels too weak for the amount of effort and tedium it takes. It’s been suggested that they should be buffed or brought back to their .1 level of power. Given that it is fairly easy to reach a point where you’re blasting maps and this is the only way to sort of keep progressing, that sentiment is very understandable.

But wasn’t the goal in this patch specifically to make it so it wouldn’t feel worth it to chase 3+ towers overlapping ? Even when it was strong, I didn’t ever want to do that and I probably never will. I’m quite happy with it not being worth doing. The way I can easily get to a tower and instantly create a big spot full of maps with multiple fun little events feels really good to me, it’s enough. Really so far one of my gripes has been that since I’m looking for nexus/citadels/unique maps, I’m not clearing all these areas I’ve built up on the way.

But then yes, once you get to a point where this level of content is pretty trivial and you want to ramp up, there’s not much to do. So you do that, or you reroll, or you quit. But looking for overlapping towers is never going to be an interesting solution to that. I’d much rather keep them as something that I just do without worrying too much as a part of progressing/traveling through the atlas. What we need to go beyond the basic mapping we have is proper ways to scale up the content.

Juicing should be about increasing the difficulty, the cost or both of mapping to scale up the challenge and the rewards, to keep going to that next level, to give you both reasons and means to improve your character or to come up with another one that can take on even more challenge (or just blast faster). Overlapping towers is a way to slightly boost rewards in exchange for pretty much nothing, with the only limitation being you need to find a perfect spot on the map first. I simply don’t think stronger towers will keep anyone playing longer. But new sets of challenges and rewards will. Whether it be more impactful atlas nodes, scarabs or something new GGG comes up with, that’s what I’m hoping for.
