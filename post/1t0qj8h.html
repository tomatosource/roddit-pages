A few thoughts on Bosses having to One shot the player to be at all threatening

I’ve been thinking about how i think in the one Zizaran Interview in 0.2, Ziz did mention that he would like to be able to invest in defenses and that that is mostly irrelevant when the only threatening stuff the boss throws at you is one shots with very very few setups being able to survive the corridors for example.

This therefore encourages the Player to just fully invest into damage going kinda glass cannon.

The Reason this has to be the case is that with for example Leach in the game player health can shoot up to 100% in milliseconds. So i’ve been thinking that maybe a system could be implemented that if you take a Hit that you are not supposed to take something big idc it reserves your live so the balance team can make a fight that is not completely reliant on one shots as you would not be able to regen over that cap. Alternatively it could also be a Debuff that increases the Damage you take but i think Visually your Health being locked or reserved for either a long time or the whole fight would be more pleasing to my Brain.

In my very Personal Opinion that could be a neat option to make building more tanky actually matter while still retaining that you are very unlikely to be completely invincible.
