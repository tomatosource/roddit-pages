New POE 2 player, not sure what the endgame is or what to aim for in this game, is there even an endgame?

What actually is the endgame in Path of Exile 2? Had a debate about it and I’m curious how other players see it.

I had a long debate with my boyfriend about the endgame in Path of Exile 2 and I’m curious how other players think about it, because I’m struggling to understand the “purpose” of the loop.

For context, I’m coming from games like Lost Ark where the endgame structure feels clearer to me — you grind to get stronger so you can access the next tier of raids or harder structured content.

In POE2, the loop feels different to me and that’s what the debate was about.

My question was basically:

If the gameplay loop is **running the same dungeons/maps repeatedly to RNG gear upgrades**, what is the actual endpoint or goal of that progression?

For example, my thought process was something like this:

* You grind dungeons/maps

* You get stronger gear through RNG

* You clear faster

* You finish your build

But then what?

If the answer is just **“keep grinding for better gear”**, then what is the purpose of that gear? What does it ultimately lead to?

I understand that getting stronger is part of ARPGs, but it feels strange to me if the loop is essentially:

&gt; grind &gt; get stronger &gt; clear faster &gt; grind again

without a clear “climax” or final purpose to the progression.

At one point in the discussion I described it like this: it feels like a paradox where you repeat the same process just to become better at repeating the same process.

So my genuine question to people who play POE or understand the genre better:

What do you personally consider the “endgame” in POE/POE2?

Basically I’m trying to understand what motivates players once their build is strong and they can clear content quickly. What’s the end goal that keeps people engaged?

Curious to hear how other people think about it.
