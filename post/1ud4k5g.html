East and Western Conference isolation until NBA Cup concept

What if each season the East only play other East teams and the West only play other West teams leading up to the NBA cup. This each conference only play each other until the best of each conference meet in the NBA Cup.

After the Cup then it’s like regular programming but I thought it could be cool to see how different styles would develop due to this. What do you all think?
