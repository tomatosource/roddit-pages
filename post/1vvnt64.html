MLB on a little league field

Watching the LLWS and they advertised the Braves and Brewers will be playing the Little League Classic tomorrow. It will be played at the Bowman Field and not actually at the little league complex, but it made me think. Assuming you could figure out a way to make it safe for everyone, who would have the advantage if two MLB teams played on a little league field: offense or defense.

The field would be significantly shorter so pop ups would likely be home runs for them, but a pitcher throwing in the 90s is going to feel like 120+ from a little league mound. Not to mention some pitchers will be releasing the ball a quarter of the distance to home plate.

Would a catcher be able to throw out a runner stealing? Would a triple even be possible? Would breaking balls even have time to do anything?
