Undo and reset buttons are needed for the temple, at least on softcore

I am writing this after having created a loop for the second time because of my brain lag. Once its clicked - gg, its over, hours and hours of progress lost and then you need to spend even more time to reset it to start over. This feels very bad and demotivating. I don’t think I have the willpower to go 3rd time and thats the only thing that kept me playing.

Resetting the field to start over also can take hours. Farming 60 crystals for like 40 minutes only to open close the temple and watch 1 tile at a time get deleted is unfun. So the reset button will just save all that time. On the contrary, it is funny that when the loop blows up, it deletes 10+ tiles! and leaves your temple with islands, both of which i believe must be a bug.

On the side note, something feels off with the temple, it takes too long to setup to be worth doing, i feel like a casual player wouldnt even have the time to even start any snake, thats how rng heavy and tume consuming it is. The grid is too large too. Once u get to 50 rooms, which again takes days to setup, running it takes like a full simulacrum run. And unless you build the temple the snake way, you get no rewards.

Need overhaul imo. Smaller scope, better minimum rewards, much smaller maximum rewards, faster progressing or less rng heavy. Keep the slowburn dopamine when making progress though.

p.s. Would be also nice to show connections when placing a room via a medallion, just like when placing a room normally.
