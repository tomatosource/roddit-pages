PSA: Monster buffs for spectres makes them more dangerous for players.

When GGG buffs or nerfs a skill that impacts players, it often impacts monsters in much the same way, which then makes them more or less dangerous to players. We may gain these buffs using the monsters as spectres, but this will make them more dangerous to us as players.

Just highlighting this due to the nature of campaign and the variance of player durability depending on build, movement speed, melee vs ranged and other factors that may put you in a bad position at certain points during the campaign. A few of these changes will make end game a little more dangerous.

Patch notes - https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3883495

Adjusted the following monsters (and subsequently their spectre variant):

Frost Wraith: Lowered Spectre Cost, Reduced Cooldown of some skills.

Lightning Wraith: Lowered Spectre Cost.

Vile Vulture: Improved Monster AI, Lowered Spectre Cost. - already dangerous

Faridun Impaler: Improved Monster AI, Lowered Spectre Cost. - Mini boss before Perennial King’s 1st fight, can skip.

Bramble Hulk: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills.

Skeletal Reaper: Improved Monster AI, Rebalanced monster power resulting in a lower Spectre Cost.

Cultist Archer: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills. - already dangerous - The chaos spore volley was on an 8 second cd and spammed. Typically encountered 1 at a time outside of rituals in Freythorn, but I’m guessing you will see more than 1 volley at a time now. Rituals will be more dangerous if you are not chaos res capped when mapping.

Blackstrider: Rebalanced monster power resulting in a lower Spectre Cost, Improved Monster AI, Increased the Web Mortar skill damage, Reduced Cooldown of some skills. - already dangerous - Their web mortar already does a ton of damage and slows. This can be killed at range or simply run past in most cases.

Mud Simulacrum: Slam damage is now more consistent, Reduced Cooldown of some skills. - Burrower’s Lair can be avoided. - a strong slam that does significant damage at low level.

Werewolf Prowler: Slightly increased attack damage. - Likely the most dangerous on the list due to the level you encounter them. These swarm you. Would suggest clearing these out before the Witch fight for the flasks in Grelwood.

Ancient Ezomyte: Adjusted the Lightning Arrow skill to correctly be Attack damage.

Brimstone Crab: Reduced Cooldown of some skills.

Mar Acolyte: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills.

Quadrilla: Improved Monster AI. - These rush you from off screen.

Iron Sharpshooter: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills. - Slow

Iron Thaumaturgist: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills.

Fungal Proliferator: Improved Monster AI, Reduced Cooldown of some skills. - Slow but their Vomit leaves a ground degen that does significant damage and blends in to the floor.

Vaal Guards - The type that throw Oil and Explosive Grenades now have an 11 second Cooldown on their Oil Grenade (previously 2 seconds). - This is a buff to the frequency of their explosive grenade spam. - These are typically killed very quickly, so I wouldn’t be too worried here.
