IMO: Endgame Mapping is a HUGE step up from 0.1. What do you think?

Got to T15s yesterday and my mapping experience has been significantly smoother this time around.

Atlas point progression feels fine, the towers are actually pretty neat now with the added mechanics and spawn rate, the map juicing is really good now with stacked towers and being being able to overlap 9 tablets to create a super juice area. Waystone sustain is a non-issue. Dying doesn’t feel totally bad. Overall the experience has been really good for me.

Just want to acknowledge the step in the right direction here because positive feedback is just as important as negative feedback.

That being said, my two main points of negative feedback is that:

  1. My loot feels kinda shit compared to before. It almost feels like 0.1 loot before the buffs to it. I’m not really getting that much currency or good loot drops. Curious to hear what others have been experiencing.

  2. Recombination is cool but the success rate for trying to combine more than 2-3 affixs is really bad and feels almost not worth trying at all. I already had to hit the rolls on the items first so it feels like a last ditch recycling effort. I would like to be able to reasonably craft an item with like 3-4 of my primary needed affixs(even if not highest tier rolls). I understand why they don’t want to super buff this but I feel like a small buff to base success chance would feel great for most players. Curious what data they pull from this.

Other than that I’m having a blast in mapping. It’s not perfect of course and def still room to improve but this endgame overhaul is a huge W overall. Would love to hear others thoughts and experiences.
