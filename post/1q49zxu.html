Bots in Trade

I’ve noticed a lot of people talk about issues with bots in trade. I don’t personally play trade but I feel like this could easily be mitigated by a captcha type system with items from the game. Obviously people could get around it but it would definitely slow them down. Click all of the whatever necklaces for example. Another one could be click these monsters. It would be annoying to have to do it but it would be nice to actually get items you want at low prices.
