The entire Halls Of The Dead section is phenomenal.

The visuals, the sound effects and the ambience are spot on. The narrative, characters and voice lines were very interesting. The combat is really exciting with the spirits coming out to fight you and the little mini-arenas. The soundtrack is fantastic in this entire section. Even the voices that play while you’re going through the fights…

And Yama, I love this monkey lol. His design, voice lines, his moves. The soundtrack for that fight is a banger (Kamil uploaded it on Youtube).

That whole island from start to finish is brilliant and sticks in my memory as a high point. Third time playing through it and I wish I was playing a less zoomy character so I could enjoy it more. Warrior was perfect the first time around.

Well done GGG, this is really high quality for an ARPG.
