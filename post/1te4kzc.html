How do you feel about the new atlas passive system?

I’m really excited to play around with this at league start. There’s just something that’s really neat about completing maps and assigning points that I really enjoy. Exploring all the nodes and understanding the changes has me really excited.

However I am worried about the full allocation aspect of it.

I understand and appreciate the logic of wanting to add passives that don’t make us constantly swap around points, like we had with the “biome specialization” of the previous atlas tree. However I’m not a huge fan of feeling like my atlas tree is basically the same as everybody else’s, and I wasn’t particularly reassured by the “masteries” shown in the reveal.

From the reveal (and we haven’t seen the full thing to my knowledge), they showed that one of the masteries was a node that looked like a keystone might on the passive skill tree. And going over the rest of the tree, there appears to be 7 of those on the main tree, with each of the league mechanics having one except breach which has three, abyss which has three, and expedition I’m not sure I’ve seen (maybe that’s just the league mechanic, so it doesn’t have one? idk).

Now, we don’t yet know if we will be able to get a multi-choice option on other nodes, or if the top of the tree maybe has a ton of them or something. But assuming these are the only multi-choice options, and with the reveal showing two as being “25% chance to give shrine buff to me or boss” and “Boss maps have essence, rogue or summoning circle”, I can’t help but feel like this tree isn’t really giving me much agency to influence the endgame content. I’m also worried that with so few options in each league mechanic, there’s going to be one option that just beats out the rest so handily that there basically isn’t a choice for me to make really.

On a more positive note though, the atlas masters seem like a fun addition, and I’m looking forward to running 6 mod maps with +1 revive from homeboy Doryani, if nothing else. This system does seem like it does actually give rather meaningful options to choose from.

So how about it? How are you feeling about the new atlas passive system?
