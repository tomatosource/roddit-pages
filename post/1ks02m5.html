- Necromantic Talisman is a keystone that I haven’t really used.

“All bonuses from Equipped Amulet apply to your Minions instead of you”

Ok… so what amulets would do something.

- Fireflower : “Take 100 fire damage when you ignite an enemy”

Ok… So minions need to ignite something and they take damage… great.

Bone Offering - “shield absorbs all damage from the next Hit each minion takes, then explodes”

Bone Offering explosion damage at Level 30 = 2426 to 3639 Physical Damage.

This is a Minion gem that scales with minion damage which causes each of our minions to have a giant physical shield explosion whenever they ignite an enemy.

30 minions * 3k = 100,000 “base” unscaled shotgun damage? Sounds fun.

The goal is… scale bone offering… have a ton of minions… cast bone offering to give all minions a big shield… get all the minions to ignite the target in order to pop their own shield with fireflower.

Funny thing is when investigating Bone Offering it used to have a 0-40% increased physical damage from quality. With the new % increased quality sockets… i see why this was removed… GGG nerfed it already?

So in comes the issues of minions igniting enemies…

Infernal Legion - Minions Ignite enemies within a radius

Then you use Firestorm which consumes ignites on the target (this will wipe ignite forcing infernal legion to reapply the ignite to the target = minion igniting the target = take damage = exploding its shield)

Or you can go further and do Contagion with Drain Ailments (which consumes ignites) which you can also load up your Unearth minions to spread contagion which wipes ignites loop.

So as of right now

We have minions running around with infernal legion, we use bone offering to shield ALL of our minions with a high damage shield…

We have Cast on Minion death using firestorm to help with ignite wipes… we are casting contagion once to get our unearth infection running… spam unearth and bone offering…

Some stuff that would be better….

Even with Bringer of Flame and Seething Body ascendancy nodes it doesn’t seem like you could get minions to ignite bosses.

The best thing would be each hit from each minion having the ability to ignite because that would just pop their own shield each attack (although considering the new ignite rules that just doesn’t seem possible, especially on bosses)

This then leads me to think that doing infernal legion and wiping ignites is the only viable bossing method of damage application.
