Miami’s offer vs Boston’s

Based on recent discussion it appears the Miami offer is something like:

Tyler Herro (68th best player in the league according to Ringer Rankings)
Jamie Jaquez (80th best player in the league according to Ringer Rankings)
Kelel Ware (promising young, but not ranked)
13th pick in this draft
2030 1st
2032 1st

By comparison, Boston could offer

Derrick White (35th best player in the league according to Ringer Rankings)
Payton Pritchard (70th best player in the league according to Ringer Rankings)
Sam Hauser (solid rotation player/borderline starter, but not ranked)
Hugo Gonzalez (Not worth a lotto pick, but promising young who probably is worth a 1st)
27th pick in this draft
2027 1st
2030 1st
2032 1st

Note that White arguably more valuable than any single asset Miami is offering. Boston allegedly was offered the 10th pick for White last season so this is a key asset that could be re-routed elsewhere for more youth. While Boston’s 2nd best asset in the trade, Pritchard, was ranked basically dead even with Tyler Herro. My guess is the Bucks could get substantial youth/pick assets for White, a late lotto pick for Pritchard, and a late 1st for Hauser.
