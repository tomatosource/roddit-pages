Pearleascent Amulet

Belton just put out a video on the Pearlescent Amulet in PoE2 (he got it from Jason), and honestly, it looks kind of nuts.
The fact that it gives +all resistances as an implicit on an amulet ist huge. Early game, this basically patches your defenses in one go. Even later on, it frees up suffixes on gear that would normally be reserved for resists.

On one side, this is amazing QoL – on the other side, is it really better then +All Attributes e.g.?

So, I’m curious:

  • Do you think the Pearlescent Amulet is too strong, or just a clever utility piece?
  • Will it become a must-have for leveling and mapping?
  • Could this make other amulets less relevant?
  • Will this be relevant this season? Or will it take some time to arrive in the community.
