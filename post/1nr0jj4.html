How to deterministically craft a +3 to all projectile skills amulet

Edit: More accurate title: How to get a entry level +3 proj amulet with recombinator and good suffixes

Hey guys, trade links and cost breakdown shared below, hope you like it!

Trade links for the amulets mentioned

+3 proj base (no fractures)

Donor base (trash prefix)

Items and Cost (from my pov):

Clean (unfractured) +3 solar amulet base - 7 div
Clean donor base - 1 div
Preserved collarbone - 1
Perfect essence of enhancement - 1
Omen of sinistral crystallisation - 1
Omen of sinistral necromancy - 1 Omen of homogenising exaltation - 3 (or 4, refer to note below step 9)
Perfect Exalted Orb - 2 (or 3, refer to step 9)
Omen of Light - 1 (optional but recommended, refer to note below step 6)
Orb of Annulment - 1
Carapace catalysts - 40 (or 60, note below step 9)
Omen of Dextral Exaltation - 2
Omen of Catalysing Exaltation - 1 (or 2, note below step 9)

Step 1:

Grab 2 amulets, one will be the base and one will be the donor

Both amulets must be at least ilvl 75 (Would recommend closer to 81 for higher possible tiers on other affixes)

The base amulet must contain +3 to all projectile skills

The donor amulet must have a T8, T9 or T10 prefix (lowest tier roll of any modifier, like accuracy rating, energy shield etc)

Ensure that neither amulet has any fractured mods or it could brick the recombine as shown below

https://preview.redd.it/ei5x59ma8irf1.png?width=348&format=png&auto=webp&s=cf59ed53a7803dbfadca7e306d1f646e4b0ea7ae

Step 2:

Recombine them (always use an omen of recombination to get better odds as the bases are quite expensive and the omen does not cost anything in comparision - the base odds should already be above 50%)

https://preview.redd.it/a86blyu78irf1.png?width=1179&format=png&auto=webp&s=77780f2ac07d011f3ca1a5d39cbfd6de84b927f3

Step 3:

Use an Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation + Perfect Essence of Enhancement to get (20-30) % increased global defences in place of the low tier roll from the donor amulet. At this stage of the craft, we have only 1 tag, defense

Step 4:

Use an Omen of Sinistral Necromancy + Omen of the Liege + Preserved Collarbone to ensure that your desecrated modifier will reveal only a prefix, and that the revealed prefix will only be an Amanamu modifier

Step 5:

Head to the Well of Souls and use an Omen of Abyssal Echoes to ensure that you hit “Minions deal % increased damage if you’ve hit recently”

The reason we’re after this modifier is because it has a minion tag and a damage tag. We’re after the damage tag here and we don’t really care about the minion tag

At this stage, we have DEFENSE tag, MINION tag, DAMAGE tag.

Step 6:

Grab a Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Homogenising Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation to guarantee T1 or T2 Increased Critical Damage Bonus, which provides us with critical tag and damage tag

Note: You cannot use an Omen of Greater Exaltation to reduce the cost as at Step 6 of the craft, there is only 1 modifer in the suffix pool that can roll due to the omen of homogenising exaltation.

Step 7:

Repeat Step 6 by grabbing a Perfect Exalted Orb + Omen of Homogenising Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation to guarantee T1 or T2 “Increased Critical Hit Chance”

This completes all our suffixes (+3 proj, 2 x Crit)

Step 8:

Omen of Light + Orb of Annulment to get rid of the minion tag (Note: You may choose to keep this if you are specced into the node which states that increases and decreases to minion damage affect you, but I personally think it’s not the best tag if you’re looking to sell the amulet)

Step 9:

Use Carapace Catalyst to add Defense Quality to the amulet up to 20%

Then use an Omen of Homogenising Exaltation + Omen Of Greater Exaltation + Omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Greater/Perfect Exalted Orb (depending on budget, self craft or for sale etc)

Note: I am not fully sure about how the catalysing and homogenising omens work together, so I personally repeated step 9 twice without using the Omen of Greater Exaltation as I didn’t want to hit “% increased spell damage” or any other undesirable modifiers

Step 10:

Quality back up to 20% and you have a result like the one below

https://preview.redd.it/ze89g9ko6irf1.png?width=446&format=png&auto=webp&s=e6dea6c2036e9a8547d35482da1f2f662a800486

Hope you guys find this useful, this is my first craft where I’ve done everything from scratch, if there’s a more efficient way for doing any of these steps, I’d love to hear your feedback
