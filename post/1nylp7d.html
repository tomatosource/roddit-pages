At 20% quality, gem has 10% increased reservation efficiency.
Normal cost is 100 spirit, with 10% efficiency I’d have expected it to cost 90, leaving me with 10 spirit leftover.

I was certain 20% quality CoC only reserved 90 last two leagues and that the change of wording was to simplify searching (so we don’t worry about having to put -10 in maximum for a 10% reduced reservation or better search, for example).

Turns out there was a formula change, so now 10% efficiency of 100 means you save 9 spirit instead of 10.

Plenty ways to get that 1 extra spirit. I’ve opted for 23% gem quality so not to disrupt the rest of my gear.
