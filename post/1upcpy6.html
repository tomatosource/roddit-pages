Brad Stevens Press Conference

Brad and Bill’s press conference 3 main takeaways, curious for opinions and thoughts. At the end of the day, believe in the front office, be skeptical of ownership.

  1. Brad is objectively pissed and while everyone says he didn’t fully answer anything besides using the word “optionality”, he was clearly forced to make this trade. His tone, his attitude and his demeanor indicates that this trade was not his decision and ultimately he had to make the best deal that was on the table at the time. Which brings me to point 2.

  2. Bill Chisholm is acting like a bitch, everything he said in his first response essentially threw the blame back on Brad for making this trade now (the question this whole time has been ‘why now’). Ownership is clearly too cheap to commit to a JB extension this summer and forced Brad to make a deal to avoid a tough conversation. Forget the fact he has 3 years left on the existing and this could have been dealt with later.

  3. Brad Stevens is objectively the best exec in the league, and he somehow managed to turn a forced hand into an open door for the Celtics, shedding JBs contract and the potential extension opens the door for a big FA signing in 2-3 years, while maintaining the same core as last year around Tatum + a few new signings (Mitch, Conley)
