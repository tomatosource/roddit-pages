We need better Alchemists. Potions are lame.

So, we got rid of the utility flasks from PoE 1. They added some interesting utility. We may have less flask slots in this game but that doesn’t mean those two flasks have to be, underwhelming.

The current pool of mods for potions are just lame. Half the time all I ever want is for the potion to heal more numerically and have more times I can use my flask.

Restore instantly? kinda nice. Taking some of my other resource to replenish another? meh. Regain flask charges? Good for bosses I guess when you don’t have mobs to replenish them.

I mean, imagine a flask with a + movement speed for X number of seconds. Invulnerable to damage for X amount of seconds. Increase resistances by X amount. After using this potion send out a shockwave that stuns nearby enemies. Short teleport/blink after using a potion? All kinds of crazy things you could do that is either just some sort of buff or another gameplay mechanic.

No rare flasks or Unique flasks either.
