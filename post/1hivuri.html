Draymond Green set two NBA records against the Grizzlies with worst +/- for a player with 0 points, 0 rebounds, and 0 assists at -42 and doing so in less than 20 minutes.

“Green did not manage to score a single point. He didn’t dish out a single assist. And he didn’t pull down a single rebound. Across 19 minutes, the only positive statistic he managed to generate was a single steal. Within those minutes, the Warriors were outscored by 42 points. 

Among players who posted no points, rebounds or assists, that minus-42 plus-minus is a new NBA record. In fact, no player in the play-by-play era had ever been a minus-42 in fewer than 20 minutes before Green did so on Thursday.”
