Which Teammate had the Best One-Year Run in the Jays Era?

Here are my rankings. This is all subjective and just for fun. Curious to see the discussion.

1. 2023-24 Jrue Holiday
Probably a hot take, but I thought Jrue was the third best player in the championship run and the best teammate the Jays ever had. He always showed up in the big games and was the long-needed calming presence in clutch time.

2. 2021-22 Al Horford
I heavily considered 2018 Al here but 2022 Horford’s playoff defense was more iconic.

3. 2017-18 Kyrie Irving
Pre-drama. Led us to 55 wins. Incredible efficiency and high volume scoring season. I’m not penalizing his injury and missing the playoffs for this list but maybe I should. I agree with the consensus that we definitely make the finals if he is healthy.

4. 2024-25 Derrick White
I think this was his best season offensively and he was great on defense. Hopefully he gets fully back to this level of shooting.

5. 2023-24 Kristaps Porzingis
A fully healthy KP playoff run would probably make him number 1 on this list, he was unreal on the court this year. I still wouldn’t disagree if someone wanted him higher up on it.

6. 2019-20 Marcus Smart
Lots of debate around which Smart season to go with. I think this is an incredibly underrated Marcus season. His defense and athleticism peaked here and so did his shooting. He also bought into more of a bench role that suited him well. I wouldn’t really disagree putting his 2022 season here though. 

7. 2021-22 Robert Williams
Unreal rim protection and lob season and also (sadly) his healthiest season.

8. 2019-20 Kemba Walker
Maybe should be a bit higher on this list because he was truly excellent before the all-star break but I just remember so many bricks in the bubble.

9. 2024-25 Payton Pritchard
Great 6-MOY winning season. I didn’t count last year’s Pritchard just due to Tatum missing most of it.

10. 2022-23 Malcolm Brogdon
A forgotten 6-MOY winning season, shame he hurt his elbow in the playoffs because his offense was hyper-efficient in the regular season and we really needed him to step up vs Miami.

Honorable mentions: ‘24 Kornet, ‘18 Morris, ‘22 Grant, ‘20 Hayward, ‘18 Baynes… who else?

Notes
I thought it’d be more interesting to only include a player once on this list, so just their best year was selected.
Considered both the Regular Season and Playoffs.
Only included players who played with both Tatum and Brown (no 2016-17 IT).
