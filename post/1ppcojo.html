I cant enjoy this game and I don’t know why

I am a casual player. I started playing PoE1 on Settlers, and played every PoE2 league since launch.

Yesterday I died randomly in Act 3 and… I just quit. ALT+F4, right click, uninstall. I didn’t even rage quit. I wasn’t even bored. I just didn’t feel anything.

There is something wrong with this game and I quite put my finger on it, specially because this is one of those games that is so fun to think about, but not so much while playing.

I am not that annoyed by the creativity issues people have, saying that builds are shoehorned or something… And also, I do enjoy the visuals, the combat mechanics… but literally everything else has a “but…” that sucks so much.

Again, I can’t quite put my finger on it, because I don’t think its one thing. Its the skills, the progression, the campain, the endgame, the ascendencies… Every aspect of the game is cool in principle but has one or more icky parts that put me off.

Maybe the worst part for me is the skill progression. I mean most “good” skills are level 1 skills. Other than that, its combo setups, generators, stupid ultimates with long cast times and/or cooldowns… Then once you get your main combo, you just spam it to infinity. And here is the weird thing: I dont care for spammy one button gameplay… its just that everything in the game is you preparing to do what you want to do instead of doing it. This is such a complex issue I cant even start to explain it, everything seems off to me.

And then the combo interfaces are almost always resource management. Combo, rage, ground effects, infusions, charges… And most skills dont work well if you dont combo. And its not even like you need to do a MMO like rotation, combo setups are very finicky and inconsistent.

It never feels like I’m getting stronger, even when I am. The game feels more like a puzzle than a ARPG, and not in the good way. PoE1 is about optimization, the puzzle solution is making you stronger. PoE2 feels like you need to solve the puzzle so that your 3-5 buttons in your rotation dont suck as much. I dont think this is bad in principle, but I dont think I like it. At least not with this execution.

And again, that is my biggest pain point. Every singel mechanic in this game has something like this. And its all designed. Its like the game is chopping off your balls and then you need to cull enemies with elemental ailments to have chance to get half a ball back. It feels like a pay to win game with no cash shop.
