Please GGG, make Trial of Chaos less annoying.

Those elevators and walls between “rooms” are so slow and time-wasting. They add nothing to the gameplay, just frustration, especially in party play.

When my friend and I decide to do Ultimatums, the encounter itself is nothing in terms of difficulty compared to the elevators, we literally have to sit up in our chairs just to manage going up together.

The elevator itself is so small that if we had a third friend join, we wouldn’t even fit. One person would have to pull the lever then micromanage it for themselves.

Why do I say micromanage?

That button in the middle of the elevator cannot be triggered for the first 2~3 seconds after the elevator arrives. You have to wait for it to visually rise up. Sometimes the elevator knocks one of us off when it starts going up, and sometimes we cannot trigger it at all, then we both have to leave the elevator, then micromanage stepping onto it at the same exact time. This thing is the most annoying part of the whole run. It is not the bosses, not the monsters, and not the mechanics.

Then there is the bonus dead-end before the walls. If you take the wrong turn after the elevator, you waste five seconds of your life running the wrong way, turning around, and backtracking to the correct path, where you get to wait another five seconds for the wall animation to finish. GGG needs to either completely remove these elevators and walls or significantly speed up the animations, make the platforms bigger, shrink the zone size, and remove dead-ends because the entire place is just unnecessarily HUGE just to get from one room to another.

The best solution for the elevators would be to remove them completely. It looks cool, yeah, but that is the only pro. The same goes for those moving walls. It is just wasting time. Let them go, or make them not block player movement. Whoever wants to see the animation can stay and watch, and whoever has seen it 1000 times can just pass through.

The Mechanics

The relief when I hear the Trialmaster say “Slay everything!” twice in a run is easily more valuable than a tink at the end. The other mechanics need work:

1. Follow the Statue

This mechanic is too slow, and there is too little space to count as “following.” The solution is easy: If you go behind it, it should move slow, but if you are ahead of it, make it faster.

To avoid just running past the whole encounter without killing anything, make it slow down when a monster is present in its range (as if you were behind it), make the range at least 50% bigger, and speed up the statue’s animation a bit.

Also, make the path to the end a bit wider. On some layouts, the space is so narrow that there is literally no room to dodge the mechanics and monsters together if you picked lightning storms or any of the projectile modifiers.

There is one more “Why?” thing here: at the very end of some layouts, more monsters will come down on the elevators, but they disappear halfway down because the statue fell into its place.

To be honest, I don’t know how they actually managed to fit 4 or 5 of them onto that tiny elevator at once, I guess the monsters figured out something that we haven’t.

2. Gather 3 stones/keys to proceed

I don’t know how to improve this one entirely, but maybe give the player Invulnerability when socketing in the stones. It has happened multiple times that I died while socketing them in because a random mob spawned / jumped behind me from offscreen and gave me a few slaps because they have time for that too since socketing the stone in its place takes seconds meanwhile the player is “stunned” by the animation.

Some of these stones are gated behind elevators, which is another reason to change them. If those don’t get changed, at least keep the suffering part (the elevators) as a “reward” after completing a room, not mid run with a DoT on you as the cherry on top.

Interestingly, there is a rare layout where you have to pull levers and walls emerge on one side and descend on the other so you can pass through to get the key. That part actually feels good to play. It is like a little minigame from PoE 1 Labyrinth.

3. The ~2 minute AFK room

This should be the worst mechanic out of everything here (except elevators), monsters take painfully long time to spawn in but the whole thing just missing one element.

Make rare monsters have 1-2 more modifiers here but cut down the timer like in the Sekhema hourglass trial if you kill them. I don’t know how that didn’t make sense to whoever designed these rooms. I’m sure they were made by a different person.

4. Stand in the 3 circles

The main problem here is that these circles are almost invisible. If you pick a modifier like the growing rings that explode or lightning storm tier 2, the actual objective gets completely buried under the other animations. You literally have to just wander around aimlessly until monsters start spawning just to find where the circles actually are. This 100% needs a visual fix.

Additionally, there should be a risk-reward speedup option. That stone structure already sitting in the middle of the arena should have levers you can pull. If you pull all the levers, all the circle monsters spawn instantly in the arena, additionally raising the difficulty significantly. If you choose not to pull all of them, it could just slightly increase the passive spawn rate and difficulty from the actual circles.

This gives an option for every type of player: If you are doing ultimatum just for the loot with an existing build, you pull all the levers. If you are still progressing and want to take it slow, you can just stand in the circles, assuming you can actually find them, and if you want to speed it up a little just pull 1 or 2 levers but risk dying, or pull all of the levers after completing 2 circles so the monsters from the third one spawn all at once.

Thoughts

I just want the mechanics to feel reasonable, consistent, and above all, to give players more options.

Right now, so much of the game feels unnecessarily restricted, even the UI settings barely give us anything to customize, and that same rigid design is bleeding into the game mechanics. By introducing things like the lever system for the circles, etc, GGG would be giving us choices. Stronger builds could opt into higher risk for faster clears, while developing characters could take it slow.

I tried to offer actual, realistic solutions and additional ideas for these issues because the foundation for these QoL fixes already exists elsewhere in the game.

For example, the Sekhema trial proves that GGG knows how to make a survival timer feel engaging by letting players speed it up through aggressive play by killing the rare monsters. Bringing that same logic to Ultimatum’s survival encounters, or adjusting the pacing of the statues and elevators, would make the entire run feel much more smoother and enjoyable.

Hire me.
