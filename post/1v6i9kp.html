Denver Nuggets offseason has been terrible

Lost in all of the big drama this offseason, the Denver Nuggets have genuinely had the worst offseason so far

As of this point, they added Marvin Bagley, Alpha Diallo and re-signed Tyus Jones. They waived Jonas Valancunus, Lost Tim Hardaway Jr and David Roddy. They drafted two second rounders, Trevon Brazile and Bryce Hopkins

The nuggets currently have a payroll of 211 million, 216 million with unlikely incentives (around 5 million under the second apron) and have yet to bring back Spencer Jones, Peyton Watson, or Bruce Brown.

According to Bobby Marks, the nuggets have only paid 53 million in tax penalties since 2003. Matching the spencer jones contract will bring their penalty payment from 36 million to 69 million for this upcoming season.

If they chose to bring back Peyton Watson on a contract around 23 million without making a move, as well as bring back spencer jones, their luxury tax payment will go to 240 million. So they would have to pay close to 500 million for the roster in order to bring back Peyton Watson and Spencer Jones

They can most likely keep Peyton Watson if the are able to dump one of Cam Johnson or Christian Braun. Either way, they will lose a rotational player

there is a legit scenario where the nuggets lose 4 out of their top 9 in minutes per game from last season
