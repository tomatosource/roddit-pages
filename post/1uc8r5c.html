On May 18, 1929 the Phillies beat the Dodgers. The winning pitcher, Ray Moss, allowed 10 earned runs in 5.2 innings. Approximately seven weeks later, on July 4, 1929 the Dodgers beat the Phillies. The winning pitcher, Les Sweetland, allowed 9 earned runs in 5.1 innings pitched.

Only three other times in MLB history has a winning pitcher allowed 9+ earned runs while failing to pitch 6 innings:

Rube Walberg (PHA@CHW 8/21/1931) - 5.1 IP, 9 ER
Bartolo Colon (CLE@CHW 5/17/1999) - 5.2 IP, 9 ER
Dan Haren (OAK@TOR 8/21/2006) - 5.2 IP, 9 ER

Interestingly, the White Sox lost two of those games and the A’s won two of those. The away team also won all 5 games.
