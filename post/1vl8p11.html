Aldur’s Saga

  • ~15 total: ~10 purchased, ~5 self-dropped in maps

Notes

  • poison is OP — was ripping maps day 1 and missing loot (even died to a GE with a boon). fixed with megalomaniac with toxic tolerance; was on ~40% chaos res before that.
  • rumours: GEs can appear more than once per rumour. had sagas with 3 GE rumours produce 6 GEs. worth using aldur saga on 3+ GE rumours imo
  • aldur saga mods: lucky is ass, all my boons came from at least 5/6/7 runes
  • rerolling remnants: i was liquid verisium-ing mostly everything that isn’t perf chaos/perf ex/divs with no good socketed runes (opulent, power)
  • loot: rune combos matter to maximising loot (opulent, power, death, time, oath + bond)
  • logbook sustain: oversustained on travel maps — target tall red flags there.
  • time management: if GE had no good remnants (&lt;1 div), no early opulent, and no 5/6/7 runes, i placed all detonators and exited map
  • xp is pretty good, got 8% at 99
  • logout method is OP if you get into tricky situations (ES dropping below half, since I was running 1 portal)

Overall

  • pretty fun grind, think i broke even on loot (mind you i ripped way too many maps on first day of running it)
