Since the NBA Draft Lottery started in 1985, the Spurs have had Top 8 odds six times. In those six years, they have gotten the #1 pick three times, the #2 pick once, the #3 pick once, and the #4 pick once. They moved up in the lottery in every single one of those years.

With the Mavs jumping up to #1 and stealing the headlines yesterday, it felt like the Spurs jump up to #2 was flying under the radar when it is arguably just as ridiculous. Since the lottery’s inception, the Spurs have been the luckiest NBA team in my opinion, and that trend continued yesterday. When they have tanked, they have been awarded a generational big man at #1 50% of the time, and they have moved up in the lottery every single year.

Spurs draft lottery history:

1987: David Robinson drafted 1st overall (4th best odds, jumped up to #1 pick)

1989: Sean Elliot drafted 3rd overall (4th best odds, jumped up to #3)

1997: Tim Duncan drafted 1st overall (3rd best odds, jumped up to #1)

2023: Victor Wembanyama drafted 1st overall (3rd best odds, jumped up to #1)

2024: Stephon Castle drafted 4th overall (5th best odds, jumped up to #4)

2025: Awarded #2 pick (8th best odds, jumped up to #2 overall)

EDIT: I’m not saying the NBA Draft Lottery is rigged. I’m just pointing out that San Antonio has been very lucky with their lottery results in the past.
