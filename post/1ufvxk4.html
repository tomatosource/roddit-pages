Amick on the Sacramento Kings offseason: “Toronto still has some interest [in Sabonis], but there are similar obstacles to that kind of a deal”…whether through a trade or release, the Kings are expected to end their partnership with DeMar…Zach LaVine is ‘not leaving that money on the table’.

Appearing on Sactown Sports’ The Carmichael Dave Show with Jason Ross, The Athletic‘s Sam Amick shared the latest on Sacramento’s efforts to move veteran contracts, including potential trades involving DeMar DeRozan and Domantas Sabonis.

On Sabonis

Charlotte’s recent acquisition of Naz Reid in the LaMelo Ball-based blockbuster deal with Minnesota may have complicated a potential pathway to a Sabonis deal.

“Toronto still has some interest, but there are similar obstacles to that kind of a deal,” Amick said.

According to Amick, Sacramento’s successful 2026 NBA Draft has strengthened the organization’s belief in its young core, making veteran salary relief a priority moving forward.

On DeMar

One veteran who appears unlikely to remain in Sacramento long term is DeMar DeRozan.

Amick said the expectation has always been that the partnership would likely end after two seasons, whether through a trade or release.

Earlier this week, HoopsHype’s Michael Scotto reported that the Kings are expected to waive-and-stretch DeRozan this offseason. DeRozan’s \(25.7 million expiring contract is only guaranteed for \)10 million, which can be stretched over the next three seasons.

“The DeRozan thing, that was always kind of unofficially the plan,” Amick said. “I do expect that to happen, whether it’s trade or getting cut.”

On LaVine

The veteran guard has a player option worth approximately $49 million and must decide by June 29 whether to exercise it.

While speculation continues regarding possible destinations, Amick doesn’t see LaVine walking away from that money.

“He’s not leaving that money on the table,” Amick said.

Other Kings tidbits

Beyond Sabonis, DeRozan and LaVine, Amick also pointed to Malik Monk as another player who could generate interest around the league.

Oh and in case you’re wondering: The trade exception Charlotte opened up isn’t big enough to absorb LaVine or Sabonis. It could absorb DeMar but all signs suggest that he’s getting waived and stretched if a deal doesn’t materialize.
