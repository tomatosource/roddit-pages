AI summary of the article:

I built a FairSalary model to estimate what players should be worth based on production, advanced impact, usage, efficiency, availability, and role.

For this board, I used both the 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons, then normalized the values to a projected 2026-27 salary cap.

Biggest takeaways

  • Austin Reaves looks like the strongest non-superstar contract case in the class. I have him as a near-max offensive starter, ideally around 5 years, $190M, with an expected range of 5 years, \(200M to \)220M.
  • Jalen Duren’s model value is massive, but the his archetype and playoff translation make me cautious about a full max. Ideal range: \(37M to \)41M per year.
  • Harden and LeBron still grade like stars, but they should be short-term contracts because of age.
  • Trae Young is still hard to price. His creation is elite, but he needs a specific roster build. Most likely outcome is an opt-in around $49M.
  • The best value pockets are club-option bigs, two-way wings, defensive guards, and short veteran deals.
  • Biggest overpay risks are expensive player options, one-way scoring guards, older names, and long-term money for players without secure playoff roles.
