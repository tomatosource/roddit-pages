1+ million dps raw incinerate build

video

If anyone just really likes incinerate style skills and is looking for a high dps build that actually does damage with incinerate and not “cast on” skills let me know and I’ll write a guide on everything i know about incinerate in its current form.

Build in the video is done with a crit blood mage.

https://reddit.com/link/1ispd0j/video/ksccf0sz6zje1/player

This build takes advantage of the fact that incinerate can crit if given a base chance to crit with blood witch ascendancy or by using critical weakness. Since you can only have 1 ignite the biggest ignite overwrites smaller ones so you don’t need a ton of crit to keep your big ignite going but some is useful so if you’re just using critical weakness your crit ignite will often wear off without a ton of cast speed which is just a waste imo.

This uses a 5 link incinerate with considered casting, mobility+ momentum, and searing flame, the last gem i switch between inspiration for lower costs and concentrated effect for more damage.

The other important parts of the build are miligaro’s virtuosity for a flat +300 crit damage and stacking a crap load of aliment magnitude with crits through time lost jewels since they’re currently rolling higher than they should, up to 20% magnitude per notable (will probably be fixed down to 10% in a later major patch).

It uses a sacred flame shrine scepter in the offhand for the extra spirit to run double curses with blasphemy (not incredibly important) because added fire damage does contribute to incinerate ignite magnitude since ignite is based on your total fire damage.

It does not use black flame since it makes you damage based on the targets chaos resistance so you miss out on a ton of free fire exposure and a ring slot.

Incinerate damage is based on the calculated hit damage but it doesn’t count as a hit or a crit even if the damage is calculated that way. So any increased damage with hits (eg: intense flames) does increase the ignite damage, but anything that procs from hits like repulsion (knock back on aoe hits) don’t trigger. Life remnants will also only proc on kills since you don’t technically crit anything.

This build was fairly cheap (~6d including the perfect jewelers?) but can be started for less. Time lost diamonds with 18%+ magnitude of ailments and a large radius are fairly rare and their price can vary wildly. You don’t need the sacred flame shrine scepter, you could use a focus with damage and + levels which does more damage overall but pumps up the life/mana cost. I got a prism of belief for like 100 ex a while ago, they’re currently 1d. Other than that most of the gear is simply hp/mana/resists/magic find.
