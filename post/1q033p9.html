QOL tech for CoC mana users. (That don’t want to use life cost to cast).

So the Quipolatl’s Thesis tech that WarZealot posted few days ago here is pretty interesting.

Basically hes making his curses cost a ton of mana while Quipolatl’s glove gem is replenishing a percentage back (which you can scale as WarZealot shows).

He makes his entire build revolve around a helmet that scales of mana cost. But I tried to figure out how to take advantage of the tech to gain some much needed regen for my CoC Oracle without ripping up my entire build idea, so I only borrowed some of the tech.

The small thing I did which fixed all my mana issues immediately was to put Ritualistic Curse + Spell cascade + Burning Inscription on a curse + Whisper of Doom Keystone and Quipolatl’s Thesis rune in Glove. Thats it.

I put one Curse inside CoEA, and made one Curse which I could hardcast on bosses with the aforementioned support gems.

Ofc you still need great regen and mana on rest of gear and passives like you did before but this small tech investment is really worth it. It 5x’ed my Comet damage on bosses.

My build: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/150fe
