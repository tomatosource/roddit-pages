Casual gamer with a family here - What am I doing wrong when it comes to POE2?

I really like the ARPG genre. I rolled into the genre when Diablo 3 was released and I eventually went into POE1. I couldn’t get into it. Not because the gameplay was boring or anything, but because the game had an immense learning curve. At the time, it was too much for me to handle. I didn’t understand what I was doing and why I was doing it.

Nowadays, I have a family and have about an hour to an hour and a half play time per day. POE2 is much more accessible than POE1, thank god for that. However, here is generally how my experience with POE2 goes now:

Act 1: Best act, really looking forward to developing the character. I feel powerful and crave more power.

Act 2: Decent, some okay boss fights. I continue to develop the character. Some areas are dragged out a bit, but generally it’s alright.

Act 3: Fatigue sets in and I start to get bored with the game. My power doesn’t increase as much (or at least it feels like that) and enemies hit a lot harder, especially near the end. It’s just a drag.

Act 4: Great act, but because of the fatigue that set in in act 3, I can’t appreciate act 4 for what it really is. I just want to get through it, but my motivation to develop my character is pretty much gone.

Why do I get burnt out when I reach the endgame? I love the genre, but the experience is always the same when it comes to POE2. What am I doing wrong?
