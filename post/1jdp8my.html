Map Completion Suggestion

Hey Everyone,

Something that came to my mind and I’d like honest feedback is a change for map completion. Instead of having to kill every rare, would it be better/easier to reach a “gate”? You know how during campaign you go from zone to zone.

Essentially, you start at Point A and have to make it to Point B of the map to be considered “complete”. That way if you want to complete a bad layout you’re not forced to kill every rare on the map. You’re not forced to backtrack if you missed a rare at the start of the map.

It’ll also feel more like you’re traversing the World Map as you will be literally going from zone to zone.
