Warrior would feel so much better if you were able to move during their attacks

Ive never played a warrior in poe2 before. I was trying to cook up a mjolner build, but I noticed something very weird. Mace Strike can not be used during movement. It forces you to stand still.

Why is it that my crossbow character with this massive crossbow can move during the attack, but my warrior is forced to stand still? If they put a heavy movement speed penalty on mace attacks, something like 80-90% it would basically feel the same on low levels, but in high maps you could scale your attack to have reduced movement speed penalty and make the gameplay insanely smooth. Reducing the clunkyness of mace skills by a lot.

This applies for many of the mace skills and currently I just cant be bothered to start up a warrior because of this.
