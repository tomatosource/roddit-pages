Ritual tablet setup 0.3.1

Before the recent changes i could have gotten 4,6, or 8 pages of rituals with 2-3 tower setups that werent hard to find with next to no cost for rerolling pages or deferring items, but now…

I feel like Ritual potential has been too much negatively affected by the recent tower and tablet changes and i want to hear other peoples opinion about it because i cant seem to find a good and worthy combination of tablets to use.

Right now you are OBLIGATED to use a maximum of 2 tablets tops to influence the ritual in some shape or form because you have to use an irradiated tablet no matter what to have access to the purple omens and i feel like that is too much of a downside, on top of having to use fully juiced waystones. And because of this you cant reliably use tablets that offer more rerolls because you dont have enough tablets to stack enough cost reduction on top of it to actually use all the rerolls, plus cost reduction for deferring. If you use only cost reduction for deferring, you’re left with only 2 pages of ritual so you have to run so many more maps in order to find something. If you use cost reduction for reroll, you have a chance to not have enough tribute to defer, so on and so forth.

What strategies have you guys come up with? This is the only game mode that i like because it doesnt require increased rarity on your items and before it was a pretty steady stream of currency by selling omens.
