Celtics center Neemias Queta on 1st Portugal’s EuroBasket win since 2007: “I wasn’t sure what basketball was in 2007”

Full article

Portugal defeated Czechia in the opening EuroBasket 2025 game 62-50, while the Boston Celtics center put up a performance of 23 points (11⁄15 FG), 18 rebounds, 4 blocks, and 2 steals.

For Portugal, it was the first EuroBasket win since 2007. It’s also its first appearance since 2011.

“2007… I wasn’t sure what basketball was,” Queta said after the game. “I hadn’t picked the basketball yet. So I wasn’t watching that team. I wasn’t watching what was going on.”
