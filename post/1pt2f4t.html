Revisiting the KAT trade - how do neutrals see it?

Here are the details of the trade from right before the start of the 2024-2025 season:

Knicks received:

Karl-Anthony Towns

Draft rights to James Nnaji

Timberwolves received:

Julius Randle

Donte DiVincenzo

Keita Bates-Diop

2025 Detroit top-13 protected 1st

Hornets received:

Buncha stuff

Additional details: The Detroit pick Minnesota received at the time was viewed as one that would not convey and turn into a 2nd. Instead, Detroit became a playoff team and Minnesota received the 17th pick and selected C Joan Beringer.

In the first year following the trade, both teams found playoff success, each reaching their respective conference finals.

From what I can tell, both Knicks and Wolves fans feel okay to good about the trade overall but not without some frustrations with the main players of the trade (KAT, Randle, and Divo).

With one and nearly a half seasons, how do neutrals see this trade?
