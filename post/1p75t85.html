LeBron James has finally surpassed Michael Jordan (In career fouls)

With his first 20 point game of his record breaking 23rd season going for 25/6/6 LeBron James has now finally committed more fouls in his career than Michael Jordan who he played 493 more games than.

Going into tonight LeBron and Jordan were tied at 120th on the all time foul list with 2,783 fouls but after a 3 foul performance against the Clippers LeBron passed Jordan pushing him to 121st and passing Darryl Dawkins who was next on the list.

There are 5 other active players ahead of LeBron in career fouls and it is the unexpected group of Chris Paul (56th), Russell Westbrook (57th), Kyle Lowry (61st), Brook Lopez (93rd), and James Harden (98th)
