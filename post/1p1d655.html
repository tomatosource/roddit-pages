the top 5 players in FT attempts this season, and the percentage of their shots taken within 10 feet of the basket:

  1. Luka Doncic 12.5 FTA, 25% of all FG attempts inside 10 feet

  2. Antetokounmpo 10.2 FTA, 78.2%

  3. Austin Reaves 9.8 FTA, 38.5%

  4. Paolo Banchero 9.3 FTA, 49.4%

  5. Gilgeous-Alexander 9.1 FTA, 33.6%

Additionally, Luka has only taken 9.3% of his shots from 3 feet or less this year. A career low mark and despite that he is somehow leading the NBA in free throw attempts

I had always been told FT attempts were supposed to be a stat related to driving more, but that does not seem to be the case for Luka.
