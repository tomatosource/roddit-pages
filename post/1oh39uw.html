The Last Flame Relic Sekhemas challenge

I am a big Sekhemas fan and wrote the Sekhema’s guide a while back.

I mention that up front, because I am about to say something possibly controversial.

The “you have 1 honour” challenge presented by The Last Flame relic, is ridiculous

YES, people do pass it, but ONLY by using the most OP screen-clearing build and cheesing their way through Gauntlet doors using Blink and Ice Wall.

YES, it should be challenging, I am not suggesting that Temporalis should be easily obtainable.

HOWEVER, gating an item to a few builds that can clear monsters off screen with no effort, AND gating behind an incredibly rare Relic drop is a double whammy of the worst kind.

Imagine someone really good at farming Sekhemas, who knows the ins and outs of all the rooms, can clear all the Unique relic challenges.

  • All rooms hidden, no problem
  • You cannot gain boons? Easy peasy
  • Cannot restore honour? Pfft, please.
  • Harder Zarokh? He dies.

Then they get this rare relic drop: Wait…1 honour?

So, any hit, from anything, at any time, is instant loss?

With so many rooms designed to send fast attacks at you from offscreen?

The only way to have any chance at this room is to obliterate any monsters at range, be they on a different platform level, above or below you.

I am perfectly capable of getting through Gauntlets without getting hit, and consider myself quite handy at

clearing Sekhemas, but something will usually get a hit in, simply because my ED lich can’t clear monsters a screen and a half away.

PLEASE I AM NOT ASKING FOR TEMPORALIS TO BE SUPER EASY TO GET

But can we make it something that any decent build, with enough skill, can achieve.

AT least make the Last Flame relic itself a common drop, so you can try this unbelievably difficult feat yourself.

I mean, we have The Peacemaker’s Draught, a daft relic that only seems to exist to kinda practice 1 honour runs, but not really as having no defence is NO THE SAME as having 1 honour!
