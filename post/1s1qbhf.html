Kraken owner purchases majority stake in Arena prior to NBA expansion vote

[Chris Daniels] Ahead of this weeks Board of Governors vote, the ownership group of the Seattle Kraken have reorganized into “One Roof Sports and Entertainment” and purchased the remaining operator/investor stake in Climate Pledge Arena. Surest sign yet they will be the primary bidder for the new NBA expansion franchise.
