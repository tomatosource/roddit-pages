The Arbiter of Ash is poorly designed

I’m writing this off of my first arbiter kill. I haven’t watched anything about the fight before going in to it actively attempting to avoid spoilers for its since I was haven’t a blast since 0.1 though I haven’t reached more then t10 in 0.1

after finishing the fight I’d like to offer my critique of the fight and why I believe it is one of the least enjoyable (and interesting) bosses in POE 1 and 2.

since its relevant to the gameplay id say I’m playing a cold cast on freeze cast on crit chronomancer.
after breezing through t15 and doing citadels I 1 for 1 for the fragment I was missing assuming the fight would be overall similarly difficult and since I’ve had a total of 0 deaths on the citadels I went to the fight excited.

while I don’t know the death count I had prior to the fight after dying a multitude of times I’ve checked and was at 85. I won after the number reached 124.

so why was the fight so miserable for me?
the way I see it there are multiple options:

  1. I was too weak for the fight
  2. I am simply bad at the game/fight
  3. the fight is just bad

I want to go through each of the options in depth so I’ll start with the first

  1. Was I just too weak?
    at the time of entering the fight my defensive stats were as follows
    1955 HP, 2953 ES, 76% lightning fire and cold resists, 0 chaos, 2% damage recoup as life
    2167 armor, 410 evasion and 0 block chance since I don’t use a shield , 20%MS
    calculating my EDPS is rather complicated due to the synergies in the build and conditional modifiers but according to the tooltip in game my Frost bomb has an average damage of 15092 occurring twice due to spell echo my Frost wall does 3591 average my cold snap 35980. my Comet that is cast on crit has 20078 average and the cast on freeze one has 16893 average. my frostbite was reducing 51% cold res and I penetrate an additional 25%

so these are my stats heading into the fight. previous to the fight I just steamrolled count Geonor and Doryani dying to them a total of 0 times. those two alongside the third citadel boss (which I couldn’t find) are the closest thing I had to the Arbiter of ash in terms of difficulty to which I gained access, steamrolling them would lead one to draw the conclusion he is strong enough to take on the next challenge which is the Arbiter himself.

so while it may be debatable if I was under geared for the fight or not it is evident that the game did not give me any reason to believe this was the case.

  1. Am I simply bad at the game
    while I’m far removed from the peak of the player base POE 1 or 2 have I would consider myself average. In POE 1 I have managed to do every end game boss at least on their base difficulty. and I finished the campaign with this character with a total of 13 deaths. while I can’t entirely prove that skill issue did not play a part in my struggle with the fight I think this information at least suggests that I’m not as bad as to suggest it is the main problem here

  2. the fight has a ton of issues and in its current form just bad
    this is obviously my claim here and so ill try to prove it. after reaching 100 deaths total 30~ deaths to arbiter I’ve deiced to write down what kills me and track it below is the result
    beam - 1
    flaming gales - 7
    falling flame seed - 0
    falling flame seed combo - 11
    sword combo - 1 (I attribute the death to the no regen arena but still)
    sword slash - 0
    flying slam - 1
    bullet hell - 0
    sword beam - 3
    this is the exact number of deaths from each move since 100 deaths if I were to add what I recall from 85 its one more sword slam and about a 50 50 split between flaming gales and flaming seed combo.
    so if we look at my main cause of death its rather evident that the 3 highest on the list have a commonalty you get 1 shot by them. these moves were definitively the worst part of the fight for four main reasons:

  3. 1 shots are not fun

  4. Inability to DPS during them (you can dps during sword beam the other two are the issue)

  5. Chaining of them one after the other

  6. clarity and visibility

  7. one shots aren’t fun
    when you fight a boss you usually do appreciate the idea that your stats matter and have meaning however I died a total of 2 times to arbiter due to non one shoting moves which means that for the majority of my deaths my defensive stats, abilities and items were IRELEVANT. It didn’t matter if I have Convalescence or Temporal rift available it didn’t matter how good my potions and charms were all my attempts to make myself tankier were vain.

  8. my build relies on frost bombs detonating frost walls to crit and freeze leading to additional damage with the comets. frost bomb is a slow skill and during the fight my orbs duration was 3 seconds meaning it takes 3 seconds before it deals damage. during the flaming gales ability the arbiter is above the arena and cannot be damaged, during the falling flame seed combo ability he has a tendency to blink around the arena meaning despite it theoretically being possible to hit him I was unable to do so.

  9. chaining abilities (more annoyingly one shots)
    in my attempts to beat Arbiter at least 7 of my 50~ attempts were after the attack right after he finished one of the one shoting moves he went over to another 3 times I’ve had him chain 3 together to completely drive me insane. chaining them together meant that for long stretches of time I was unable to do any damage and the entire fight experience was attempting to avoid these abilities.

  10. understanding what is happening and reaction time
    I don’t think I’m inventing the wheel when I say that the worst offender for awful visibility is the Flaming Gales ability, it can be incredibly difficult to understand where is the safe zone and the incredibly short window to act all but guarantees failure if you moved even a step in the wrong direction. essentially you have to get this flawlessly since you don’t have enough time to reposition if you didn’t get it in time. with the flame seed combo the problem in my opinions at least and what led to many of my deaths was either not seeing the location of the next seed therefore wasting time looking for it and ending up outside or and this is is worse in my opinion assuming there isn’t a next one since I cant see one and the arbiter is attacking me as usual. lastly with the sword beam ability it is unclear to me at least where in the zone am I supposed to be safe each time I stuck to the side of the circle and each time I was evaporated by the laser.

to compare these abilities to better designed ones id suggest the eater of worlds in POE 1 as an example, during the ability inescapable doom it is crucial for the player to know the location of the spheres or be almost certainly killed by the boss’s slam, as such the spheres are bright purple in contrast to the bluish grey of the arena and the camera zooms out to allow a better view that includes most of the arena the time frame given to the player is also enough so the player doesn’t require blinks or dashes to get to each of them and does not require optimized pathing to survive.

as for my final gripe with the fight as mentioned briefly earlier I attribute my death to the sword combo to the regeneration disabling arena. in my opinion not being able to restore health energy shield and or mana are all toxic debuffs that only serve to frustrate players. the majority of builds require mana to deal damage and at times also defend themselves those that don’t usually rely on health or ES as cost instead even without their use for defense the resources we are given in the game have regenerative qualities for a reason, WE NEED THEM and if we lose them we CANT FUNCTION more often then not. the modifiers of mana siphoner and prevents recovery above 50% both are limitied firstly in range and secondly in effect due to this fact, so why is it ok suddenly to remove the limitations when designing bosses? the maven also the effect to block regeneration but at least its avoidable in her fight.

and lastly I took a potshot at The Arbiter of Ash for being uninteresting I don’t have an issue with the character’s design I find the way it reminds me of Maligaro’s face rather nice even its simply how little content his character is currently given. To contrast this with Poe 1 The Maven is a fight you get to only after seeing her and interacting with her and her envoy many times, you know her by then and you know her thought, opinions and ideas by the time you fight her. similarly with Eater of Worlds and Searing Exarch both have envoys prior to them as well as descriptions by the maven to give them depth. with Sirus we get the stories from the journals of the conquerors (and used to Zana), shaper and elder also had Zana telling you their story and you would see them and hear from them while collecting the fragments. on the other hand what or who is The Arbiter of Ash? truthfully I don’t know I assume we will get some answers by the time 1.0 rolls around but what’s truly lacking most to me is a connection between him and the citadels, sure they give you access to him but why? how are they related? we don’t hear or see anything to give us an idea closest we get to any information on him is the mysterious obelisk outside the arena which tells almost nothing at all.
