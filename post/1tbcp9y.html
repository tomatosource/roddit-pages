Only 40% of players who played 3.27 also played 3.28. I think we vastly underestimate the “Casual” audiences interest and desire for an ARPG “Endgame”.

I was staggered to hear this when Jonathan revealed it to Chris in their recent interview.

Only 40% of players for Mirage played Keepers.

10-15% new players who haven’t interacted with the game before.

Then the rest from players who have previously engaged with the game in a past League.

The reason this number is staggering, is that there is no “New Campaign” or anything like that, which is what you would expect to appeal to “Casuals”. Updates/Leagues for PoE are almost entirely “Endgame” focused. Which tells me that the demand for “Endgame” is truly huge and that we severely underestimate just how strong the appeal is to a wider and more “Casual” audience.

When we look at PoE’s Seasonal numbers of Steam for instance, I think a lot of us are mistaken into believing that because the numbers are usually very similar and generally show slight growth year upon year, that we expect league to league retention to be closer to 80% rather than the 40% Jonathan revealed.

It’s pretty obvious based on these numbers that PoE is a far more popular and financially successful IP than we all thought, and that “Endgame” is also a key focus for more “Casual” players than many claim.
