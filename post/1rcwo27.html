I want to tell you a story about a sad temple making sad noises. I once build up this pretty and wonderful temple and had locks in abundance. Like… I had so many locks that I could lock 2 to 4 rooms, have 5 locks in my stash and still leave a lock or two on the floor. Those were great times. However, things changed, starting yesterday evening. I started, as usual, with 2 locked rooms before closing temple and with 5 locks in stash. And run temple after temple. But locks would not drop like they used to. One, maybe two locks, but thats all. All 11 spymasters, which have so generously provided me with abundand locks, suddently stopped dropping locks. During 10+ runs I may have gotten 2 or 3 locks total from the 11 spy masters. Most other locks came from completely random rooms, like smithys. To be honest, I believe that during my last 10 runs I dropped more locks from smithys than from any other rooms. Even my Elite Legions, where locks droped quite frequently during better times, left me alone - disapointed. What changed: I got an abundand number of room cards and architect reroll cards and temple buff cards, which were all… useless. I just needed some locks and one room: the corruption chamber. For the last really difficult part, the sacrifice tech.

My last run started without locks. Not a single one left in my stash, no locked rooms at the beginning of the run, as I was so used to. And finally, the corruption chamber dropped, not as a placement room, but as a medallion. But this time. Not even a single lock. Not in spymasters, not in legions, not even im my so trusted smithys, which - during the last 2 days - were the only ones keeping my temple alive for one or two more breaths…

I wanted to post a conspiracy theory, that maybe someone with godly power over the game has changed the drop ratios for locks - as suggested in other posts. But maybe I am just terribly unlucky and this and the other reports are just pure imagination. And my report got stuck somewhere in the nirvana…

Anyways, I am about to press the “CLOSE TEMPLE” button and see what will happen to my hard work. I will just hope that RNGod has mercy with my main chain and leaves it as whole as possible, as to not start again with any smithy tech…
