I was watching back Celtics playoffs games and noticed that comparing 2022 to 2024, everything seemed worse. To start, this may just be a YouTube issue, but 2022 is posted in 1080p 60fps, and 2024 being in 1080p 30fps, and looking more like 720p. The microphones also placed in the stadium to capture stadium noises also seemed much better in 2022, being very clear and quite immersive. 2024 on the other hand, seemed muffled and you could hear echoing/out of sync microphones across the stadium. Another note, the announcers to me sounded louder more recently, which I’m not a fan of.

Is this something else other people have picked up on?? Hopefully, I’m just being nit-picky or the two videos I have attached are bad examples, but from what I remember these issues came across my mind often in the last 2 years of NBA on ESPN/ABC, TD Garden or not.
