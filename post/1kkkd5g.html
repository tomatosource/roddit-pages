What’s Your Celtics Playoff Hype Song?

I find the gym really boring, so for exercise, I just go to the park and have a shoot-around basically every day. I always bring my headphones and listen to music.

During last year’s playoff run, Atarashii Gakko! had just put out AG! Calling, and this song (“Maji Yoroshiku”) just screamed “NBA Basketball” to me lol I’m not even entirely sure why.

I guess it’s the horns and bass parts and the jazz and funk influences? The chorus lyrics, too, are just “TD Garden vibes” to me: energy, support, gratitude.

Anyway, it became kind of my little Celtics playoff anthem. I’d play it while doing fast-breaks before leaving the court to hype myself up for the upcoming game.

https://youtu.be/JyKHxFFBHpM?si=4FadVTm_85AqZ_qX

However far-fetched, it’d be incredible to hear it played during a game at the Garden.

What’s your C’s playoff run hype song? What song would you want to hear blasting from the speakers at the Garden as the Celtics send these Knicks packing?
