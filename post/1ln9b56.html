Crossbows still feel clunky, unresponsive and ultimately not as good as bows to use

  1. You character always moves with crossbow lowered and when you press shoot the time to raise the crossbow and shoot is constant and no amount of attack speed will change that. Bows are different, attack speed directly correlates to how fast your character can ready the bow and shoot,
  2. When you are shooting the crossbow there is a speed limit on how fast you can rotate your character and that speed limit is rather low. It’s unresponsive and doesn’t feel good to use especially when you are surrounded. Again no such issue for bows.
  3. The need to reload. The existence of reload is supposed to balance for crossbow’s higher firepower which is arguably subpar to bows.
  4. The fact that crossbows can’t be used while mounted while bows can and the fact that there is giant blood for two hand melee but no such thing for two hand ranged (crossbows)
