The latest head coaching hire (Mike Brown) has more career wins as an NBA head coach than the previous 11 hires did combined

The New York Knicks waited a minute or two before finalizing their next head coach hire: Mike Brown. Whatever you may think of Brown as a coach, he at least has a long resume. He got his first head coaching job back in 2005 and has been a head coach in 11 seasons since: compiling a total record of 454-304.

With the hire, the Knicks broke a trend that had developed over the last few offseasons. Perhaps inspired by the success of Oklahoma City and Boston, most teams have been hiring younger assistants instead of the experienced, “retread” candidates like Brown. In fact, 9 of the 11 coaches hired or promoted prior to Mike Brown had never been a head coach in the NBA before (Jordan Ott, Mitch Johnson, David Adelman, Tuomas Iiasalo, Doug Christie, JJ Redick, Brian Keefe, Charles Lee, Jordi Fernandez).

Amidst that group, two experienced head coaches were hired last offseason: Kenny Atkinson (CLE) and J.B. Bickerstaff (DET). Neither Atkinson (118-190) nor Bickerstaff (255-290) had a great W-L record as a head coach prior. Even combined, they hadn’t won as many games as Mike Brown. Of course, those two old retreads fared just fine in 2024-25 anyway. Kenny Atkinson won Coach of the Year, and Bickerstaff finished # 2 in the voting behind him.
