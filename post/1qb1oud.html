NBA ANNOUNCES GAME SCHEDULE ADJUSTMENTS

NEW YORK, Jan. 12, 2026 The NBA announced today the Miami Heat at Chicago Bulls game that was postponed on Jan. 8 has been rescheduled for Jan 29 at 8 p.m. ET.

Additionally, the Chicago at Miami game, previously scheduled for Jan. 30, has been rescheduled to Jan. 31 at 8 p.m. ET.
