Rite of Passage Golden Charm

I love POE 2, but sometimes the utter lack of documentation and dependence on the community to figure things out and provide documentation instead is astonishing. That being said, the community usually does the necessary work and things don’t go unexplained for too long. But, with the Rite of Passage Golden Charm, there are so few of them available that community information on exactly how the charm works is still unclear after nearly half-a-year.

I know, for example, that one rare or unique monster procs one charm, meaning that multiple charms cannot be triggered by one kill. But it’s still very unclear if multiple rare and unique kills in a short span of time can trigger 3 charms and get all of their effects enabled at the same time.

I’ve read a few people saying that only one of these charms can be active at once, and the second one doesn’t trigger until the first one has expired, but I’ve also seen a video where the streamer says that 3 can be active simultaneously if you’re able to kill 3 rares in quick succession.

I really haven’t gotten any definitive answer on this, and spending a couple of hundred divs on different versions of this charm just to experiment with them and see if they can be active at the same times really isn’t feasible or sensible.

Has anyone actually run 2 or 3 of these at once, and can multiple kills actually proc more than of them at the same time? It seems to make sense that more than one kill could trigger more than one charm, but I’ve heard more than one person say this doesn’t work.

Yeah, we’re far enough into the league where I have excess currency to be a little extravagant with, but not enough where I want to spend that much without even knowing how or if things work.
