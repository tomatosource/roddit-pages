Visual and pathing clutter in maps

I feel like this issue isn’t often mentioned, but I find it to be totally unfun.

Specifically, maps with a lot of trees (Bloodwood, Creek, some other ones), or like Decay that is both dark and have random untraversable terrain elements. These things make it very hard to see where my character is going, and also very hard to predict what pathing my character will take (I’m using mouse & keyboard).

Extra credit for this issue is with Ritual, if it spawns on top of a densely cluttered area. At least in normal mapping I can just run out to a more open area to see what I’m doing and dealing with. I’m mostly skipping rituals in this kind of maps, especially if it’s the one with chaos explosion balls.

I feel like this is such a weird design choice. Yes the first ten seconds in a map like this, you get a feeling of “immersion” and sure, GGG cooked on the graphics. But after that first ten seconds, and for every map like this ever since, I don’t want to see and deal with all this clutter. The experience difference between these maps and maps like Sandspit or Bluff is MASSIVE.

Anyways, just a point I think people might have forgotten about/neglected to mention after all the other issues. I’ve stopped playing for now.
