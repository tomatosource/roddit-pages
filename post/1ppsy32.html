[QoL / Accessibility] We really need an option for an “Overhead Rage Bar” alongside Life, ES, and Mana

The game currently has great options to display Life, Energy Shield, and Mana bars directly above our character. For many of us, this is super helpful for keeping focus on the action during combat.

However, for classes that use the Rage mechanic (like Druid and Warrior), Rage can be just as important to track depending on the build.

The issue is that right now, checking our current Rage usually means looking away from the character to the bottom-right corner. In a game where positioning and dodging are so critical, breaking eye contact with the center of the screen can be distracting. Also, constantly shifting focus between the center and the corner can get physically tiring after a while.

It would be amazing if we could get a UI option to display a Rage Bar above the character, similar to how Life/ES and Mana are handled. It could be positioned either between the Life/Energy Shield and Mana bars, or simply below the Mana bar.

This would be a huge Quality of Life and Accessibility improvement for Rage-based builds, making the experience much smoother.
