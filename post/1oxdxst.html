Gameplay and Endgame changes I would like to see after playing 300 hours of 0.3.0 in SSF and what improvements I really enjoyed

Obligatory character

Changes I would like to see

  • There is a massive lack of build diversity somewhat due to lack of skills and due to lack of support on the tree. Many skills just straight up have numbers that are so low they do not make sense.

  • Atlas tree rework. It is extremely boring in its current state just due to lack of interesting choices. Mechanic trees are extremely not fun. The most fun node is on the least fun mechanic (wisp spawn chance after killing wisp buffed mob).

  • Delirium rewards rework. Fishing for a megalomaniac is pretty much the only reason to do a Simulacrum after you have enough liquid emotions to sustain enchanting maps. The rewards for Delirium are boring in every way. Also megalomaniac was the only chase jewel I did not get multiple of (besides Arbiter jewel because getting fragments was boring, I killed him once).

  • Wisps are a terrible mechanic. Would be way more fun if they attached to you and gave you more loot the more damage you did to an enemy as it was possessing them. Killing an enemy 400 times and 400 wisps spawning is fun. Everything else about them, not so much

  • Ritual is a lot better in PoE 2 than PoE 1 but min/maxing it (clearing all rituals before actually starting any ritual) is not fun. Also, there just needs to be more ritual monsters on a baseline. 300 favor in a juiced map is not cool.

  • Shouldn’t have to be said but smaller maps. Playing a character other than deadeye feels ungodly slow

  • Less fog of war on the atlas and more nodes with cool shit/improve nodes like Kalandra Lake/Enzomyte stones nodes. I eventually just found myself pathing to every overran abyss node and the occasional seer. A lot of people dislike this map system but it has a shit load of potential. Every node should be interesting.

  • Some maps are truly awful with the lack of movement skills. I found myself rolling 90% of the endgame because it autopaths you around dogshit terrain blocks

  • Being able to fight Arbiter as much as you want is great and it makes it feel very Soulslike. I would honestly love to see all bosses buffed with unlimited attempts (and no effect on loot)

  • More chase uniques

All in all, this was a great patch. I was able to get trade tier gear within a reasonable amount of time. If I was not investing a ton of currency in a character I never ended up making, I would have borderline perfect gear. Getting a 4 socket Gemini xbow in SSF was really sick. Abyss is awesome, so much better than PoE 1. Definitely need more abyss mods though because all of those lich omens suck ass. Also, more perfect and corrupted essences would be cool. Pretty boring that the only perfect essence to use on gloves gives gold or on rings, like recoup or whatever it was.

Seriously looking forward to next patch!
