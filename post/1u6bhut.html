Don’t sleep on “lush isle” expedition encounters. Insane exp for leveling alts. 2-3 maps 71-86+

Lush isle in expeditions comes with buff totems that increases exp with 200% and they stack.

T15 maps seems to spawn like 5+ of these and combined with 3x irradiated tablets with exp and pack size on the map yuo can get some insane exp per h from these.

Strategy: rush as many exp buffs as possible, the go for as many rune word encounters as possible, more runes == more monster waves and exp.

Had a friend run me thru 2 maps and I went from 71—83 in first map, 83&gt;86 in second map.
