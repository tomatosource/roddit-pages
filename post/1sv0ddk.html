The 2025⁄26 Houston Rockets - who choked a 6-point lead with less 30 seconds to go - are now 1-8 in OT this season after tonight’s embarrassing loss.

After a monumental choke job with 25 seconds left and up 6, the Houston Rockets were sent to overtime where they fall once more in overtime in what was almost too predictable a fashion.
