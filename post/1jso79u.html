Reducing non-boss Monster Damage and Movement Speed by 20% will not hurt the game - it will probably make it better

I just wanna preface that I am the type of player that enjoy the slower pace of PoE2. This patch may be a little slower than the start of 1.0, but I it hasn’t been a big issue for me personally.

However! The recent discourse around this patch made me recall playing chronomancer back in 1.1. The ascendancy has a node that reduce monster movement speed in your presence by 20%, and I remember the experience made me feel that “this is how the game should be played”. Fighting huge packs of monsters can often be stressful and annoying. And now we get more easily light stunned, frozen etc, so I can understand people are frustrated.

A flat 20% nerf to monster damage and movement speed will go a long way to make it feel better for a lot of people, and it’s not significant enough to ruin the experience for those that enjoy PoE2 for the more engaging gameplay. In fact, having more time to engage with the monsters will likely make the combat more meaningful.

This is to me the best ARPG game I’ve ever played so I trust GGG to make the right call in the end. I never much liked PoE1 or the Diablo games, but PoE2 feels like a fresh take on the genre that I didn’t know I needed.

Take care everybody!
