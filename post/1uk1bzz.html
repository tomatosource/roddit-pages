Item Design Philosophy in 0.5

Hello everyone,

I want to give a bit of feedback about a concern of mine about the design of some of the new items in 0.5, and the future direction of item design in poe2.

These are by no means major problems, but they could become major, if this philosophy is further pursued in the evolution of poe2.

Trarthan Cannons:

Okay, this is the least problematic item I wanna discuss. The idea to introduce grenade throwers to poe2 is VERY fun, and I love the idea behind them. The execution was just…frankly terrible. The whole draw of this new base is that they cannot shoot traditional bolts, and are only for grenades. Problem is: Normal crossbows can also do that. And shoot normal bolts.

The supposed draw of the Cannon base type is that it can roll special grenade related mods that normal crossbows cannot access. The most important one here being “Grenade Skills fire 1-2 additional projectiles”. Issue is, this stat takes a suffix slot, and is VERY rare. With how tedious weapon crafting is, it’s basically impossible to get a good weapon with decent damage prefixes, levels of attack skills (which is mandatory), AND grenade related mods, like additional grenades fired.

To make this problem worse, there is already a normal crossbow base (Siege Crossbow and its lower level equivalents), which has +1 grenades fired as an implicit modifier. So not only does that base type NOT have a downside like not being able to fire bolts, you also get an explicit mod essentially for free as an implicit.

So the Trathan Cannon straight up gets outshined by the Siege Crossbow in the archetype that the cannon is designed for. The exception here being mirror tier weapons with absolute perfect mods, but do we really need a base type that is JUST for mirror tier items?

This is a relatively easy fix imo. Take the implicit from the Siege Crossbow, give it to the cannon. Or give it another good implicit, so we can actually use the sick grenade thrower.

The Taming:

Ok, this one is a way bigger issue. I hate this items existence. It interacts with only 4 skills in the game at the moment. Whirling Slash, Twister, Whirlwind Lance and Tornado. And it is straight up mandatory to use for these skills now. Would you “sacrifice” a ring slot to get 150% more damage on your skill? Yes, I think I would! This is such a non-decision that it pidgeonholes these skills into using the ring. That is some Diablo 3 esque design that isn’t healthy for an ARPG in the long term imo. And you don’t even really sacrifice the slot, because the other stats on taming aren’t even that bad.

It also takes away all the potential interactions that these wind skills normally have. Usually you need to create elemental ground effects for the wind skills to soak up. Taming just entirely gets rid of that. The synergy is so obviously insane that it gets rid of any nuance in build making around these skills. Equip taming, equip decent spear, there, tri-ele destruction!

I’m not saying every strong build in the game needs to be locked away behind some super obscure interaction, that you need a gaming PhD for to figure out. But this is just way too simple.

Voices:

This is my biggest gripe, and the reason for writing this post. This item in my opinion fundamentally breaks the rules of PoE in an insanely unfun way. It is just pure power for money. No ifs or buts.

Now, powerful items are allowed to exist in the game, I have absolutely no problem with that. BUT: Every other powerful item in the game comes with an opportunity cost. Voices does not.

Example: Headhunter is an insanely powerful chase item in the game, we all know that by now. Yet it takes up your belt slot. Sure that is not that big a cost for how much you get out of it. But still, you lose the opportunity to use another belt. A rare with better life and a lot of res. A Mageblood. An Ingenuity. A Darkness Enthroned. You get it. You have to make a decision to use the Headhunter, and there is competition for it.

For Voices that is not the case. Use 1 Jewel socket, get 2 Jewel sockets. At worst. It has NEGATIVE opportunity cost. Rare jewels being as powerful as they are (especially with Adorned in the mix) is an issue all by itself, but Voices completely breaks the equation. There is no thought process, no decisionmaking, no conceivable reason to not use this item.

This is also not faithful to the spirit of the spiritual predecessor, the Voices Cluster Jewel in PoE1. Yes, that item is very powerful, and unlocks a lot of potential for highend builds. BUT it also has an opportunity cost. You could instead use a normal Large Cluster Jewel with stats and/or notables. Voices is a tradeoff. The Poe2 Voices doesn’t do that.

I really hope Voices gets redesigned in the future, or straight up cut from the game. It doesn’t need to exist, and it shouldn’t exist. And I certainly hope it doesn’t set the standard for chase items in future leagues to come.

I always want to make a decision to use an item or not. Voices is not a decision. It’s a foregone conclusion for those that can afford it.

That’s about it. I love this game, and I really want it to succeed in the long term. Don’t take my critisism for hate.

Cheers

And PS: Yes I know you can easily clear all the content poe2 has to offer without a voices. That’s not what I mean when I say it is mandatory. There is just no LOGICAL reason not to use it.
