Off Day Thoughts: Replacing Baylor & Walsh

Obviously with the season right around the corner, I’m assuming our roster is our roster for now, and the team still likes the upside of Baylor and Walsh.

But, I don’t, and this is my post, not Brad’s! Here’s my list of guys I’d like to consider replacing them with:

  1. Garrison Matthews

Garrison may certainly make the Knicks roster, but he has told them he will refuse a G League assignment. If he misses the roster at cut time, he is shooting the lights out this preseason and is a legit NBA rotation player.

  1. Delon Wright

Very unsexy veteran player, almost no upside, and just got cut after smashing his head on the floor in a preseason game. However, I think he provides something this team could use in very, very limited minutes. He’s a tall, fundamentally sound playmaker who can defend a bit. I think there’s value in that deep in our guard rotation.

  1. Drew Timme

More of a fun add than anything else. I’ve enjoyed watching him play going way back to his early Gonzaga years, and I would very much enjoy watching him play in some low stakes games for the Celtics. The upside might be limited, but it’s limited with Baylor and Walsh, too.

  1. Olivier Sarr

Unlikely to ever be an NBA rotation guy, and he’s coming off an achilles (healthy now). So this is probably a dumb take. But there is some upside as a rim running big and he flashed a ton in the G League for the Thunder, just had no hope of breaking that rotation.

  1. Dalano Banton

Always loved the guy. Can’t shoot the 3 ball to save his life and is not a highly efficient player, but there’s just something about his uniqueness that always piques my interest. Extremely tall playmaker, that, like Delon Wright, could be a nice minutes spell for our tiny backcourt.

  1. Lonnie Walker

I’ve just seen him ball out way too many times to think he should be wasting away at Maccabi Tel Aviv. I won’t pretend to understand the contract issues at play here, I just like pure bucket getters.

Who am I missing? Or are you pro-Baylor and Walsh?
