How many all stars were on each 60 win team in NBA history

There are a total of 94 60 win teams in NBA history. 92 of them have had all star selections during the season (1999 didn’t have an all star game due to the lockout season, Spurs and Jazz had a 60 game win% in this season). I was curious as to how many all stars were on each team that achieved 60 wins. I’ll link the document but if you’re lazy and don’t want to, I’ll put the percentages here.

I’m also including early 60s teams, 2012 lockout season, and the 2 covid seasons that achieved a 60 game win %

Percentage of 60 win teams that had 1 to 4 all stars on them:

1 all star - 15 teams, 16%

2 all stars - 42 teams, 46%

3 all stars - 28 teams, 30%

4 all stars - 7 teams, 8%

So on average, if a team achieves 60 wins in the regular season, they’ll have 2 all stars on them.

Now for some interesting things. Out of the 15 teams that achieved 60 wins with 1 all star, the Spurs did it 5 times. Accounting for 1/3rd of all teams that had 60 wins with 1 all star on them. 1995, 2003, 2012, 2014, and 2017.

In the 70s, 9 teams achieved 60+ wins. Out of the 9 teams, 6 of them had 3 or more all stars on them, except for the Kareem Bucks which from 1971-1973 achieved 60+ wins 3 times in a row with 2 all stars.

Whereas an inverse, in the 90s, 19 teams achieved 60+ wins, while 16 of them had 2 or less all stars on them. The ‘90 Lakers, ‘91 Blazers, and the ‘98 Lakers being the outliers with 3+.

Another thing from the 90s, for 3 years in a row from 1996-1998, 3 teams achieved 60 wins in each season. When in 1998, 4 different teams got to 60 wins. The Bulls, Lakers, Supersonics, and Jazz.

The Celtics have the most 60 win seasons in NBA history at 17. They achieved 15 of those seasons while having 3 or more all stars, the only 2 outliers being 2024 and 2025 when they had 2.

Whereas the inverse, the Spurs have the 3rd most 60 win seasons at 10 (by win%), and have never had a 60 win team with 3 or more all stars. They’ve always had 2 or less.
