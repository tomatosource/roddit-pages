PSA: Each abyss crack in the abyss questline is tied to a faction, if you force a different faction via the Atlas Passives the map will fail to spawn an Abyss

Balance of Power in the Abyss passive tree lets you force a specific faction. As far as I can tell, each directional crack (North/South/East) is also tied to a specific faction.

If the faction you set on the passive tree is mismatched from the one the directional crack is forcing, you will get no Abyss and mistakenly think you’re bricked. You should set Balance of Power to Any Faction whilst completing the Atlas Abyss questline
