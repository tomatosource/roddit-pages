10 More Realisitic Jrue & KP Trades

All these trades work according to Fanso.org trade machine. Are they realistic? Idk the worth of picks are hard to evaluate imo. Let me know your thoughts below

General Philosophy:

  1. Keep JT, JB, White, PP
  2. Get off Jrue’s contract even if it costs 1st + 2nd (or more?)
  3. Get cheaper assets from a contender for KP
  4. No 3 way trades those are nuts

Admittedly, some of these require a bit of delusion from the opposing GM about their chances, but I’ve seen crazier things.

  1. The warriors get a high-risk high-reward starting lineup for 3 role players.
    • BOS gives: Kristaps Porzingis, 2025 #32 pick 
    • GSW gives: Moses Moody, Buddy Hield, Brandon Podziemski 
    • NET: -6M
  2. Luka gets a center and the Celtics get a starting PF.
    • BOS gives: Kristaps Porzingis
    • LAL gives: Rui Hachimura, Maxi Kleber, 2026 1st round 
    • NET: -6M 
  3. HOU frees up minutes for their young wings and gets a champion + picks back.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday, 2025 #28 pick, 2031 2nd round pick (via HOU)
    • HOU gives: Dillion Brooks, Jock Landale
  4. DET gets 3 picks for an expiring vet.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday, 2025 #28 pick, 2026 2nd round (via 4 possible teams), 2031 2nd round pick (via HOU)
    • DET gives: Tobias Harris 
  5. LAC gets a starter for 2 role players.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday 
    • LAC gives: Derrick Jones Jr. and Bogdon Bogdonovic 
  6. NOP gets 2 picks for an expiring vet.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday, 2025 #28 pick, 2026 2nd round (via 4 possible teams) 
    • NOP gives: CJ McCollum 
  7. DAL exchanges frontcourt depth for backcourt defense & playmaking.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday, 2026 2nd (via 4 possible teams) , 2027 1st 
    • DAL gives: Klay Thompson, Daniel Gafford, Brandon Williams 
  8. A whole new offensive dynamic to a sneaky exciting team when healthy.
    • BOS gives: Kristaps Porzingis 
    • ATL gives: Terrance Mann, Gorges Niang, 2027 2nd round (via CLE)
    • NET: -7M
  9. BRK gets two picks now and more when they trade KP at the deadline.
    • BOS gives: Kristaps Prozingis, 2026 2nd round (via 4 possible teams), 2031 2nd round pick (via HOU)
    • BRK gives: Nic Claxton
    • NET: -5M 
  10. BRK gets 3 picks for a role player.
    • BOS gives: Jrue Holiday, 2025 #28 pick, 2026 2nd round (via 4 possible teams), 2031 2nd round pick (via HOU)
    • BRK gives: Nic Claxton
