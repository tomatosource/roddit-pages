At age 22, Wemby joins the list of youngest Player to lead their team to the NBA FINALS.

Wemby joins an exclusive list of youngest players to lead their team to the NBA Finals, matching Lebron, Shaq, Duncan and Magic Johnson to do so before their age 23 season. (Since 1980)

  • Shaq (22) led the Magic to the finals in his 3rd season. Losing 0-4
  • Duncan (22) led the Spurs to the finals in his 2nd season. Winning 4-1
  • Lebron (22) led the Cavs to the finals in his 4th season. Losing 0-4
  • Magic Johnson (20, 22) reached the NBA Finals in his 1st season (age 20), but was not the best player on the team. By his 3rd season, most viewed Magic as the best player on the Lakers. Winning 4-2

Age 23 seasons:

  • Hakeem (23) led the Rockets to the finals in his 2nd season. Losing 2-4.
  • Dwight (23) led the Magic to the finals in his 5th season. Losing 1-4.
  • Durant (23) led the Thunder to the finals in his 5th season. Losing 1-4.
  • Tatum (23) led the Celtics to the finals in his 5th season. Losing 2-4.

Other notables:

  • Bird (24) led the Celtics to the finals in his 2nd season. Winning 4-2.
  • Wade (24) led the Heat to the finals in his 3rd season. Winning 4-2.
  • Kobe (21-23) would win 3 straight Finals but was not the best player on his team.

Of the 8 to do so prior to their age 24 season, 4 were big men (Hakeem, Shaq, Duncan & Dwight). Hakeem, Shaq and Dwight would all lose to greater teams or player, while only Duncan was victorious.

Most of the players on this list would lose to better teams or players (at the time) and in fact usually lost to a more experienced legend on this list. The notable exceptions was Duncan. (Magic beat an aging but still incredible Julius led 76ers team.)

Wemby has a chance to repeat history and like Tim Duncan, lead the Spurs to the title over a NY base team.
