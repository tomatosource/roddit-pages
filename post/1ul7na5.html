Abyssal Wasting synergystic effects: Grip of Kulemak is strong!

I did some testing to answer questions I had about Grip of Kulemak effects that I couldn’t find any info on, first the good news and then I’ll explain how I found this info:

  1. Does “Increased Magnitude of Abyssal Wasting” also apply to the -% resistance debuff? - ✅Yes!

  2. Does the resistance debuff stack with multiple Kulemak rings? - ✅Yes!

  3. Is the resistance debuff scaled by enemy rarity? - ❎ No!

  4. Can the resistance debuff be increased by Ingenuity? - ✅Yes!

This can get really crazy when you consider that Ingenuity is hitting both the increased magnitude AND the resist debuff. The best Kulemak ring I have right now is 87% increased magnitude and -15% fire resistance (along with max rolled gain fire damage), which makes the resist debuff 28%. If I apply a 37% corrupted ingenuity, that turns 87% into 119%, and -15% into -20%, for a total of -43% resistance. If I equip a Kalandra’s Touch on the other ring slot, I am approaching almost -100% debuffed resistance. This is also not considering the fact I get about 20% gained fire damage from each Grip of Kulemak as well.

(Unfortunate note that fire catalyst does not affect the resistance debuff - but it does affect the gain damage mod.)

To confirm this I went into Trials of Chaos with 4 different Grip of Kulemak rings - one with -10% fire res, then -13%, -15%, and then -17% (-10% with 70% increased magnitude). I used Uxmal the Bitchlord as my control, testing with each ring how his resistance icon changed. The 15% ring made his resistance icon disappear entirely, meaning neither resistant nor vulnerable, so this confirmed his base resistance to fire is 15%. The 10% and 13% rings did not change the grey resistant icon, the 17% ring changed it to red. The 13% ring combined with ingenuity changed it to red. The 10% and 13% equipped in each ring slot together changed the icon red.
