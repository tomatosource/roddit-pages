Delirium Fog Suggestion - why don’t we just have the Mirror Shards instead?

https://preview.redd.it/01865ruciuuf1.png?width=2532&format=png&auto=webp&s=d2a7ae9faefbb7922e311d662ec016c7e0c73487

I, like everyone and their Rhoa, have grown tired of being in perma-Delirium fog as part of endgame mapping.

Currently in 0.3, it is a vital part of juicing and it completely butchers the variety of colours in the maps.

I’m seeing daily posts of people calling for the removal of Delirium fog, or an option to toggle it off, due to it completely ruining the beautiful visuals of the game. (I’ve also seen a few suggest an outline/border/vignette of the fog instead, which is a nice idea)

I am yet, however, to actually see anyone suggest using the glass/mirror effect that we see in the Simulacrum realm instead, so I thought I’d hop on here and actually post about it to put it out there and see what people thought? I think it would be cool to still have a deli effect, but to keep the colours of all maps, and I cant help but feel like having these mirrors intertwined into the terrain would be the best of both worlds.

I like the idea that Deli brings a unique feel to maps, and while I wouldn’t mind the fog being toggleable/removed, I do think it is important that we have things that keep variety to the feel of mapping.

Realistically, I do see GGG addressing or tweaking the juicing process, and making it so that instilling maps maybe isn’t a necessary part of this (as there are a gazillion steps currently), and so I’d like to see instilling keep a cool and unique feel to the maps.

The music can go, though.
