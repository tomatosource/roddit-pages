Why do instances fully reset mobs and loot on death in Campaign when its not like this in endgame?

There are several issues that can make the campaign feel too long, but for the SC players who might die here and there in the campaign, one of these issues is the fact that once you die your instance reset its mobs, so you have to push through all your progress again in hopes you can make it through this time, and also if you get any meaningful drop and fail to grab it before you die then you lose it.

And this semi-hardcore mechanic could make sense as some thoughtful game design, except for the fact that in maps it doesnt work like that!

In maps if i die, i go back to the last checkpoint (which btw the maps definitely dont have enough of) but all the map stays the same! I dont need to slog through the mobs again to reach the same point i died in, and if i missed any loot i can pick it up when i come back.

And i know, in 0.1 all endgame maps were single-revives, so it kinda did work like campaign. But now that you can revive in maps and its behaviour is totally different than the campaign, it no longer makes sense to keep the campaign death toll with its outdated gameplay design.

GGG please reconsider removing this death reset in the campaign, its not really fun and it clashes with the endgame experience.

If i happen to have like a very strong essence mob or hard ritual or something that i dont want to die to accidentally, so i want first to finish my map, and if i die to the boss once then poof. No more extra stuff. Like why?
