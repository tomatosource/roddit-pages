Hey,

Beside bookmark link item, I got tired of constantly switching between PoB and the trade site just to check upgrades or missing uniques, so this brings your build info right into the browser ,

What it does:

  • Import your Pobb, PoB (link or code) and see your unique gear.
  • Compare items on trade instantly with what you’re using
  • “Anti-spam” tracker (adds a 2-min cooldown for people you’ve already messaged)
  • Bookmarks: Save search URL

Still pretty early, so I’m mainly looking for feedback or ideas on what would actually make this more useful.
