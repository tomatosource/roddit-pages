Trivia: The whole 1980s Decade Finals feature Magic Johnsons’ Lakers and/or Bird’s Celtics

1980 - Showtime Lakers won against Sixers

1981 - Bird’s Celtics won against Rockets

1982 - Showtime Lakers won against Sixers

1983 - Showtime Lakers lost against Sixers (Moses joined Dr. J)

1984 - Bird’s Celtics beat Showtime Lakers

1985 - Showtime Lakers beat Bird’s Celtics

1986 - Bird’s Celtics beat Rockets (Hakeem/Ralph)

1987 - Showtime Lakers beat Bird’s Celtics

1988 - Showtime Lakers beat Bad Boy Pistons

1989 - Showtime Lakers lost against Bad Boy Pistons

—-

Magic Johnson and Larry Bird only played against each other in the Finals 3 times in this decade.

The other 7 years, either one of them made the finals, but not both of them.

Some teams that beat them are Moses/Dr. J Sixers, and Bad Boy Pistons.

Rockets and Sixers kinda stop the frequest Lakers/Celtics matchup in the finals in Early 1980s.
