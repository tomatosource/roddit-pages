Other than Steph, are there any active players who will most likely retire having only played for one team?

Was thinking about this in response to now-deleted speculation that Anthony Edwards may want out of Minnesota eventually (pure conjecture.)

That got me thinking about how players don’t stick with one team for their whole careers anymore like Dirk, Duncan, Bryant etc. I thought Giannis would be in that category after winning with the Bucks in 2021 and even made a post about in the Bucks sub at the time, but that theory has obviously been proven incorrect now.

I say “other than Steph” because he’s approaching 40 and the vast majority of people think he will retire with the Dubs. The only other team I can imagine him voluntarily leaving for is Charlotte because he grew up there.

However, I feel like if any modern superstar (aside from Jokic since we sometimes talk about foreigners not being seduced by the allure of big markets as much as American-born ones, coupled with Jokic’s general indifference towards being famous and his dislike of pomp) would stick with his original team, it would indeed be Ant.

Ant, much like KG before him, self-identifies as loving Minnesota and its residents. Despite growing up in Atlanta, Ant has grown to be very comfortable in Minnesota and views it as a second home. His business associates have said that he’s actually uncomfortable in unfamiliar places and situations (eg. He was initially hesitant to play in the 2023 FIBA World Championship because it was being held outside the U.S. and Ant had never left the country up to that point.)

There are only a handful of other teams for whom I can imagine him playing, mainly his hometown Hawks. He doesn’t seem swept up by the allure of bigger cities.

Anything can happen, but I don’t see Ant leaving in the near future. KG left also, but the Wolves were also a lot worse then as opposed to now where they’re a consistent playoff team.

What say you?
