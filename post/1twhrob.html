It feels bad that uncut gems limit exploration during campaign

The main problem is that skill selecting is currently tied to skill leveling. Imagine this:

You have a “gem cutting” bench at each base:

* It allows you to cut gems for a gold price

* You unlock gems to cut based on level, like now

* These cut gems are always at the level they were unlocked (e.g. Tier IV skills will always cut at level 4). Critically, the moment they are unlocked they are always of “good” level for progression

While killing mobs, you loot instead “upgrade books”:

* They upgrade any equipped gem to the level of the book (like now, but without the full book)

* You can always go to the bench, take a new gem, and upgrade to the current level

With this small change you can now test every skill while leveling, without having an insane surplus of uncut gems for the ones that are following a guide.
