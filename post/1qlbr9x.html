My mindset on Res Cap was wrong - 75% is not really 4x less damage taken. It’s a baseline

I always thought… 80% res has to be so good, because if 75% 4 times is less damage taken and 80% is already 5 times less taken and 84% is already 6 times less taken and 90% is oh my god just 10% damage taken that must be so op… but we all know that endgame is ballanced around having 75% res. But even though I know that and I heard that many times… It never clicked to me what it actually means.

You should view that res as not 4 times less of something. But simply think of that in a way that you basically have no resistances and anything above 75% is actual resistance.

So with that mindset if you take 500 damage with 75% res, 80% res. reduces that to 400, so think of it like 20% res. And that way you will not think of youself as invincible tank just because you are taking “5/6/7 times less damage”. With that approach 90% simply is 60% resitance - that 500 damage is reduced to 200.

Ofc anything that has penetrates or reduces your resistances gets you absolutelly demolished, so for that reason don’t think of it that you simply take a bit more damage because took away 10 res from 75%. That 500 damage with 65% res turns to 700. Ah wait.. that is not I am taking 10% more damage, I am taking 40% more damage. That’s why if something reduces your max res and also penetrates… you might be easilly be taking double damage just like that.

To someone who gets number this might be no brainer. But I to me it was just always that mindset that made me think of how 80 res must be amazing. But in reality it’s like if 80% sale goes from 100\( to 20\), but the value of the item was 30$ anyway.

I wrote this after I died two times in a row in maps because I thought 84% res will make me invincible.
