How Much Did The East’s Best Defense Impact Our Offense?

Not much for in-depth analysis here, but just wanted to point out that both the Magic and Celtics are slow teams and that was responsible for most of the low scoring on our end.

Fun fact:

  • Magic had the best DRtg in the east: 109.1
  • Celtics ORtg on the season: 119.5
  • ORtg in Game 1 vs. the best defense in the East: 118.4
  • We only had 87 possessions in Game 1, but scored 103 points

The reason we didn’t score a lot of points was largely due to the pace both teams play with. Magic and Celtics are the slowest paced teams in the NBA

TL;DR: We scored a little less per possession, but not that much less.
