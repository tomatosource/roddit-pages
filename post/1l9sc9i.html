Adding a banner bot to a 450% rarity party is equivalent to ~700% rarity in terms of Divines per minute

It’s been a second since I took probability, but I think this is right. Note that percentages and completion times are assumed, but can be varied in the formulas. Feel free to check the math.

Let’s assume:

Base chance for a Divine Orb to drop: 10% per map

A party includes a 450% increased item rarity bot

A banner bot doubles movement speed, cutting map time from 2 minutes to 1 minute

We’re comparing setups by how many maps (and how much time) it takes to reach a 90% chance of getting at least one Divine

Step-by-step:

  1. Drop chance with 450% rarity: Effective drop chance = 10% × (1 + 4.5) = 55%

  2. Maps to reach 90% chance of 1 Divine: 1 - (1 - 0.55)^n ≥ 0.90 → n ≈ 3 maps

  3. Time to complete 3 maps:

Without banner bot: 3 × 2 min = 6 minutes

With banner bot: 3 × 1 min = 3 minutes

  1. What drop chance gives a 90% probability in 1.5 maps? 1 - (1 - p)^1.5 ≥ 0.90 → p ≈ 79.8%

  2. What rarity gives a 79.8% drop chance per map? 79.8% = 10% × (1 + X/100) → X ≈ 698%
