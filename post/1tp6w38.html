My thoughts on POE2 after Diablo 3 and 4

Veteran POE1 player here, been playing Poe since open beta, cleared t16 maps and uber bosses in 1. Haven’t played in few years. Recently picked up Diablo 3 on my PS5 and enjoyed the game. Fast gameplay, reminded me of poe a lot, but i basically cleared it pretty fast and it became repetitive. Got me D4, beat the campaign, it became super super repetitive, got me the expansion pack and havent even finished it.

Instead I got into POE2 early access. Im amazed how much I love this game compared to D4. It’s more much more challenging, better animations and sound effects, builds are crazy, good items are actually rare. What I disliked in diablo 4 is that your build basically never changes, gameplay feels the same on all levels.

Etc etc, so Im in act 3 now in POE 2, playing ice shot ranger, loving it. In 2 days we get the reset, are you all planning to play in a new league or anyone playing the eternal? Is the skill tree going to be totally different? Cheers
