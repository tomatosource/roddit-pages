The 2018 draft is the 6th draft class in history to produce multiple Finals MVPs

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Jalen Brunson both hail from the 2018 draft and have won the last 2 finals MVPs. The other draft classes to produce multiple FMVPs are…

2003: LeBron James, Dwyane Wade

1998: Dirk Nowitzki, Paul Pierce

1997: Tim Duncan, Chauncey Billups

1984: Hakeem Olajuwon, Michael Jordan

1969: Lew Alcindor, JoJo White

This also makes Brunson and SGA the 2nd draft class to produce finals MVPs that won it in back to back years after the ‘84 class when Hakeem and MJ won it in 8 consecutive years

And the 2018 class still has Luka Doncic, who I think still has a pretty good chance at winning FMVP before his career is done, and no draft class has ever produced three FMVPs
