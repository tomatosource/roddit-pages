poe2db.tw is down and shows fundamental flaw in UX design

I am looking at a spear with great prefixes and 1 open prefix, +4 to projectiles and 2 trash suffixes. I would wanna desecrate the prefix and then whittle the suffixes.

Currently I can see that whittle will target one of the trash suffixes, but I have no idea about the second one. Without poe2db(or craftofexile or similar resources) there is no way to actually plan your crafts. And I really don’t want to even start this craft if this suffix overlaps +4 proj.

This is a fundamental flaw in GGGs design philosophy where they delegate important in-game information to 3rd party services. Don’t get me wrong, poe2db is amazing, but it is still a 3rd party that have their own services and their own problems. And this is not even starting that 3rd party can just provide straight wring information (looking at you fextralife wiki that says that rakiata can be dropped anywhere in the endgame).
