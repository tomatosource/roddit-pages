Draymond Green: “Yaxel has been f—ing lying,”. “We’re not a team that’s big on rookie hazing, but we can pick that back up,”

SRC: https://www.sfchronicle.com/sports/warriors/article/draymond-green-says-warriors-lendeborg-22326748.php

Green initially called Lendeborg “a great pick.” And then …

“Yaxel has been f—ing lying,” Green said. “I was very happy to expose the rookie on the internet. This guy texts me and I text back within 30 seconds: ‘Welcome to the squad, bro, I’m happy you’re on the squad.’

“This f—ing guy — and of course it’s the Michigan way — this guy says, ‘I texted Draymond and I’m still waiting on a response back.’ You f—ing liar, Yaxel. How dare you come out and lie like that? I’m gonna provide the receipts.

“You text me at 1 a.m. or 1:01 a.m., I text you back the next minute boom-boom-boom. Now how the hell are you going to say Draymond didn’t text me back? So I had to expose the young fella a little bit. Your credibility is everything, don’t you come in here lying.”

Green didn’t sound angry, but it also didn’t sound like he was joking. He suggested Lendeborg might face some “hazing” from his new Warriors teammates.

“We’re not a team that’s big on rookie hazing, but we can pick that back up,” Green said. “I’ve been part of some popcorn and tires missing, we can bring those back if you want to be lying. Tread lightly, young fella, let’s make your rookie year easy.

“If you turn up the pressure on yourself it can get pretty intense — all of a sudden you’re on a 100-mile drive to Best Buy, a particular Best Buy, to get a PlayStation 5.”
