With their outing against the Brewers, the Dodgers have set a new franchise record for consecutive scoreless bullpen innings with 36

Kyle Hurt’s scoreless inning in the 7th was the 34th consecutive inning, breaking the franchise’s previous record. A scoreless 8th from Tanner Scott brought it to 35, followed by a scoreless 9th from Jonathan Hernandez for 36.

In terms of league records (please correct me if I’m wrong), the 2016 Royals 41 1/3rd is the 21st century record, while the 1966 Royals is the longest streak I’ve been able to find (44), so every scoreless inning from here on in would bring the Dodgers bullpen to the brink of history
