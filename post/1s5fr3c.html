New “Upcoming Milestones and Leaderboard Movement” page on Baseball Reference

There is a new page on Baseball Reference that lists players who will realistically reach a specific milestone or pass someone on a leaderboard in their next 3 games.

Link: https://www.baseball-reference.com/friv/upcoming-milestones.shtml

For Milestones, you can see which players are approaching 1,000 career strikeouts, 2,000 career total bases, 400 career doubles, etc.

For Leaderboards, you can see which players are on the verge of passing other greats on all-time lists like wins, walks, games pitched, etc.

Today, for example, here are a couple of things to watch for!

  • José Ramírez is 1 double away from 400 in his career
  • Chris Sale is 2 strikeouts away from tying Bob Feller for 31st place on the all-time list
