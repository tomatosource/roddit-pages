I very much hate being in an open bidding war we might lose.

I’ll preface this by saying I really hope we get Giannis. I’m sure those of you who don’t want this trade are fine people but I think you are either too young or like not watched this team play to think this is a bad move. Or you just care way more about watching Jaylen than you do about winning.

However…….

I do not like being in an open bidding war where you’re telling your second best player you want him gone for the second time in the past 5 off seasons.

Historically, the team has been ruthless. They traded max two years after winning finals MVP, sent Pierce packing when the time was right, and got Isaiah out of here when it was time. But all those took place without an open bidding war. IF this goes south and they take Miami’s offer, I have no idea how you bring brown back. It’s been pretty poorly managed from the start.

That’s why it feels like he’s gone either way. He’s dealt with this before but I can’t imagine Jaylen would be back after this.
