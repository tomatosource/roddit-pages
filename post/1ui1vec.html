Much needed QoL - Show sold items in market search

The thing is, searching for an item you have sounds nice in theory you see these lovely high prices, but things actually never sell, just because the item is listed at that price it doesn’t mean people will actually buy it, rather the opposite, the fact that it’s still listed shows people aren’t buying it.

Think of when you wanna sell things online on platforms, if you want to list the price you search not by similar listed items, but by similar items that have actually sold. I think having this in PoE2 would really benefit the overall experience for both buyers and sellers.

The only downside would be that yeah, technologically implementing this would probably be quite challenging and require a lot of resources. So maybe it could be that items only stay in the sale history for the last 24 hours? If it were permanent it’d fry their storage way too hard, but maybe a last 24 hours sale history could be useful.

And I’m sure there’s probably plugins that do this but it’s 2026 and relying on plugins for this sort of stuff is what alienates playerbases and pushes people away, it’s not 2008.

Edit: Point considered too that people could effectively scam and rig this with insider trading from each other to make it look like an item is being bought at a lower value. So I guess it’d need careful consideration in implementation
