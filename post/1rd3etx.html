Something that’s missing from the Skubal WBC discussion today

It’s annoying that he’s bailing on the team after one easy game, but he’s not harming the roster construction by doing that, at all.

WBC teams have a thing called a DPP (designated pitcher pool) with up to six pitchers. They aren’t on the roster but can be added after pool play. The USA has five on their list, all relievers.

Basically my point is that Skubal won’t be wasting a roster spot after he leaves. We’ll add a reliever and ride it out with the rest of the rotation for the knockouts. We don’t need nine starting pitchers for three games.

Thought it was worth noting since a lot of people are saying he’s selfishly harming the roster construction of the team. 2 starts &gt; 1 start but 1 start &gt; no starts without a doubt.
