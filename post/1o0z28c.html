Crafting mana ring

I’m planning to craft a ring with t1 mana, %mana, double iir, mana regen and all ele res. Curious what the best was going about this would be. I was thinking of 2 options here

  1. Start with double rarity base, catalyze exalt mana/mana Regen, try to fracture suffix rarity, then homog the other stat and dextral crystallize %mana. Then desecrate and reroll with light for allres

  2. Start with rarity + allres base, catalyze flat mana, try to fracture allres, then catalyze (+ homog? Not sure) mana regen. If any of the catalyze steps fail, can try to crystallize %mana in to replace the dud. Desecrate and light reroll for suffix rarity

  3. Start with il81 base with rarity prefix fracture and preferably mana regen suffix. Whittle suffix for rarity and all res. Exalt and crystallize prefix for %mana, then desecrate and light reroll for flat mana

For options 1 and 2, catalyzing still leaves it open to roll duds, and flat mana has a bunch of tiers even if I use greater/perfect exalts. Fractures can also miss, meaning the essence step can be quite rng too. The bases themselves are probably relatively cheap, but I would probably need quite a number to land everything up to the desecration step. Finally, the desecration rerolling itself is also quite rng heavy. All res has a slightly lower weight than rarity so I’m inclined to think the 2nd option would be cheaper.

Option 3 is technically fully deterministic (I believe), but also would probably cost a stupid amount of currency so it’s probably not worth it.

Is there a better method, and/or is there a cheaper method if I’m okay with settling for say t2 mana/mana regen and one t2 rarity affix?
