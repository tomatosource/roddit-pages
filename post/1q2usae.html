PSA: “Life Regeneration Is Applied To Energy Shield Instead” Converts % of max life to % of max ES regen

https://preview.redd.it/lpo3f495z4bg1.png?width=1061&format=png&auto=webp&s=e31d975767268553137263c80eee232058dbbc35

This replaced mod on Soul Tether (not sure of any other sources) doesn’t just transfer the life regen into ES regen, it replaces the life keyword in all regen mods to ES. Edit: it only seems to convert “Regenerate x% of Max Life” into “Regenerate x% of Max ES” it doesnt convert regen rate as far as my testing has confirmed

ie. the ‘Regenerate 5% of maximum life per second while in Consecrated Ground’ becomes ‘Regenerate 5% of maximum Energy Shield per second while in Consecrated Ground’

This is very big for ES builds and provides a better version of Zealots Oath while keeping natural ES Recharging.

Edit: PoB doesnt reflect this, but I’ve confirmed in-game that this is how it works
