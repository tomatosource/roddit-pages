After beating the Raptors and ending his double digit scoring streak LeBron now is tied for the second most regular season wins at 1,014

LeBron’s at least 10 point streak ended tonight but in addition to all of that with the win tonight LeBron tied Robert Parish for the second most regular season wins in NBA history at 1,014.

Robert Parish is the current holder of the record for most regular season NBA games played and due to both him being a great player on great teams like the Bird Celtics and one year on the Jordan Bulls he racked up 1,014 regular season wins a total LeBron now matches. First place is the obvious answer after LeBron, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar with a staggering 1,074 regular wins wins which is basically impossible for LeBron to pass this year since he’s not going to win 61⁄61 games this year but if he plays again next year he would likely pass Kareem.

Source: https://www.statmuse.com/nba/ask/most-career-wins-by-an-nba-player-in-the-regular-season
