They wanted difficulty, we got tedium instead

I get what they were trying to accomplish. This vision that they spoke of, and what Chris had been trying with ruthless. They aimed for souls like difficulty. Slower pacing, challenging content. That would have been awesome.

Instead, we got a tedious version of PoE that does not respect our time. This iteration is not difficult. It’s boring. A slower pace can work if the payoff is there to make it worth your while. Needing your whole kit just to destroy white monsters is not it. Dark Souls makes learning to fight a specific monster or boss fun, and satisfying. PoE just does not.

Furthermore, not getting the right loot is not a fun way to increase difficulty. It’s bad design.

Reduce monster speed, reduce map sizes, increase currency drop rates significantly and add deterministic crafting. Then, work on some really awesome monster mechanics that will actually challenge us in a meaningful way.
