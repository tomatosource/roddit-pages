There’s only 1 year in NBA history where 2 players from the same team were both top 3 in mvp voting

In 1972, Jerry West and Wilt Chamberlain who were both on the Lakers were top 3 in MVP voting. Jerry West was 2nd in MVP voting and Wilt Chamberlain was 3rd. The lakers that year put up a franchise record in the regular season of 69-13.

Kareem Abdul Jabbar won MVP that year while leading the league in scoring. Wilt led the league in rebounds and Jerry West led the league in assists.

There are a couple of Kareem and Magic, Shaq and Kobe, and even a close McHale and Bird year where one of them is top 3 and the other is 4th or 5th in mvp voting. But none where they’re both top 3.

Realistically I don’t think this can happen again for 2 reasons. 1 because the cap makes it extremely difficult to have 2 mvp level players on the team at one time. And 2 because you don’t really win championships anymore with 2 stars and some role players anymore.

And come on, if Shaq and Kobe couldn’t do it I don’t think it’ll happen again.
