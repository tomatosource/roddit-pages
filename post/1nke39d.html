5 Anfernee Simons Trades

Here are 5 Anfernee Simons trades to love/hate/cry about

Chicago Gets - Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman, top 46 protected 2nd

Chicago is in a weird state where they could go a lot of different directions. The only long term money they have on the books is Josh Giddey and Patrick Williams. Looking at their roster up and down, hey could use a volume shooter more than most teams. Getting Simons on a 1 year deal to feel out his chemistry with Giddey and potentially sign him to a smaller multi year deal would be a decent use of Zach Collins’ expiring deal. Xavier Tillman is a low minutes warm body to replace Collins. The pick is top 46 protected because I meant to type top 45 and when I went to fix it i typed top 46 again and said fuck it.

Boston Gets - Zach Collins, Ayo Dosunmu

Dosunmu is someone who I’ve liked since his draft night. He fits into a team mold better than Simons does. Chicago weirdly haven’t extended him yet and you’d imagine if they were interested in paying him they would have at this point. Collins’ career has been pretty up and down. He’s had some massive injuries, and the hope was always that he’d be able to add a 3 point shot. When he’s healthy he’s a decent backup big who plays with some edge.

Net savings - - $4.6m

Chicago gets - Anfernee Simons

Same logic applies here, Chicago has a few non shooters locked up long term, and could use a good look at a volume shooter.

Boston gets - Nikola Vucevic

Vuc is old and doesn’t play D, we know that. But Simons really doesn’t play defense either. Vuc would probably be a 1 and done in Boston. He’s a guy a lot of contenders might want to add to their bench for a playoff run, so maybe he gets flipped again at the trade deadline to the highest bidder. He can actually shoot the 3 from the C position and even at his age he’s still an elite rebounder. He’d be our best big man by a pretty large mile immediately.

Net savings - - $6.2m

Utah gets - Anfernee Simons, Jordan Walsh or Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman, future 2nd

Utah probably doesn’t care what players come back, if they can get a 2nd round pick for Nurkic. Simons is probably the best player they could hope to get back in any Nurkic deal. They know Boston saves a shit ton of money on this deal so they’ll want an additional asset. Walsh’s stock is probably at an all time low, so it would most likely get a 2nd round pick squeezed here.

Boston gets - Jusuf Nurkic

This would be nothing but a cap savings move. Nurkic showed he’s pretty much washed at the end of his Phoenix run. He’s still a beast offensive rebounder and we’re gonna miss a lot of shots this year lol. He’s also an underrated passer as a big man.

Net savings - - $10.8m

Pacers get - Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman

The Pacers are without Hali just like we’re without Tatum for at least the next season. The issue for the Pacers is they still have a pretty competitive roster that just made the finals, and they need a stop gap guy at PG. No, Simons isnt Haliburton, but he can fill into Hali’s scoring role a bit easier than anyone they currently have. Its also a 1 season contract where they can either resign him to a smaller deal later, or just let him walk with Haliburton back. They paid big money for Siakam and will want to compete.

Celtics get - Obi Toppin, Jarace Walker

Initially, this is a cap saving move short term, but these contracts extend 2-3 years so it doesn’t just clean the books like Simons current contract would. Toppin is a playable NBA PF who is a cut above guys like Minot and Boucher that we’re going to end up playing next season. He doesnt protect the rim but he is aggressive offensively and can hit 3’s at a decent clip. Walker was a top 10 pick that hasn’t really panned out in Indiana, and could use a fresh start on a team looking to grow guys this year.

Net savings - -$9.5m

Cavaliers get - Anfernee Simons, Sam Hauser, Payton Pritchard (maybe Picks idk)

The Cavaliers are in a similar place as the Celtics where they have max guys (Mitchell and Mobley) and a handful of bigger contracts (Garland, Allen, Hunter, Strus) and they’re going to break due to salary cap issues. They’ve already seemed to take a step back from Garland with Mitchell being the franchise face. They could replace Garland with 3 shooters to compete for this upcoming season and be a deeper team, while also getting out in front of the cap issues theyre about to face. Simons 27m flat falls off after this season, so instead of 40m+ for Garland the following year, you’re at less than 20m for Hauser + Pritchard.

Celtics get - Darius Garland

The Celtics pick up Darius Garland who is undoubtedly the best player of the bunch. I feel like the Celtics could get a better version of Garland. But where he is at now hes already All Star level. The Celtics consolidate some of their salaries into a star level player.

Net savings -$5.4m

I get this one is gonna be unpopular but have you ever made a list of 4 trades? I’d be laughed out of the mock trade community!
