The longevity of the 2007 Top 5 is insane

While confirming that Lebron has outlasted the entire 2003, 04, 05, and 06 draft classes, which is freaking hysterical, I realized something else entirely…

The first pick in 2007 Greg Oden retired in 2014, his career only lasting 105 total games.

Picks #2-5 from that draft are ALL still playing, entering year 20, and not for nothing. Kevin Durant has stayed elite for nearly two decades, Al Horford aged into one of the league’s most dependable veteran big men, Mike Conley is still a productive starting point guard, and Jeff Green went from a young scoring forward into a versatile veteran role player who stayed valuable across many different teams.

I believe this is the first time in NBA history that even two of the top 5 picks reach 20 year careers. This one went 4⁄5. Absurd longevity and I hope we look back upon this class fondly.
