Does WAR account for the strikeout when accounting for value from a defensive perspective

I’ve had this discussion a lot, and firmly believe that while defensive value is important, offense outweighs that defensive value drastically.

Here is what I would say…say you have two players with offensive stat lines and 3 WAR, just say third base as an example

Player A: 240/310/360 with gold glove level Defense

Player B: 260/340/460 with below average defense but above average offense.

WAR, in this hypothetical would say they generate the same value, but I think most people would want player B.

So that brings me to my point. Does defensive WAR account for the strikeout? On offense you make 27 outs. The batter is directly involved in all 27 of those outs.

On defense, and I don’t have exactly averages, there are strikeout, meaning you had about 7-8 outs a game that the defense has no impact on whatsoever. Yes, I get that bad defense can cause “extra outs” to occur, but again there are a large number of at bats that the ball is never even in play and defense literally plays no part

So my question is if defensive WAR, which I fully admit I think is often overvalued, accounts for this?
