Drew Hanlan obliterates Brandon Jennings: ‘Maybe if your trainer would talked s–t to you, you wouldn’t have shot 37% from the field & 23% from 3 in the playoffs while never winning s–t’

It’s nearly off season and I’d like to get a head start on the endless NBA drama posts by highlighting this ongoing feud between the Chinese/Compton legend Brandon ‘55 point debut’ Jennings and wannabe trainer to the stars Drew ‘I think I’m Tim Grover’ Hanlan.

According to the New York Post (don’t judge me) the conflict began when Jennings criticized Hanlen’s motivational techniques, particularly his use of trash talk and group chats among clients, labeling them as “weird and stupid” and using that old boomer NBA argument of ‘the GOATS would never!’

Jennings: “I’m reading this article on how Drew Hanlen puts his guys in ‘Text Chain’ to tell them they won’t do this or this guy is better and etc. weird and stupid I said this earlier in the year to much buddy buddy when it comes to that guy. I’m sorry but Robmac (skills trainer Rob McClanaghan) would never have Drose, KD, Westbrook, Klove, and etc all in text chain saying who can’t win MVP!!!! GTFOH!!!!”

Hanlen didn’t respond.

That apparently didn’t sit well with Jennings, who re-tweeted his original post.

“Why u so quiet now you groupie ass trainer @DrewHanlen where is the group text chain now!!!!” Jennings wrote Tuesday. “U the biggest basketball groupie trainer ever.”

Hanlan went full ether with his response: “I’m getting two of my clients (Haliburton & Holmgren) ready for the finals so don’t have time to waste on your dumbass takes. Side note: Maybe if your trainer would talked s–t to you, you wouldn’t have shot 37% from the field & 23% from 3 in the playoffs while never winning s–t.”

I know /r/NBA doesn’t love Hanlan but as a jaded Bucks fan I found the response highlighting Jennings’ efficiency issues (career avgs of 34/41/79) pretty funny.
