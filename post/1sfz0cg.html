Untouchables in the Giannis trade talks

I’ve been in hearing a lot of different opinions of where we should draw the ‘uncrossable’ line in the sand for a Giannis trade, and was curious how this community feels about even discussing one of our beloved Jays.

As much as I like to consider myself an nba-head, I’ve spent most of my past 3 years over r/SeveranceAppleTVPlus (which has beared fruit in the form of a former #1 all-time post. Feels pretty damn good not gonna lie. It’s funny, when I thought of the post, I was just taking a shower, and didn’t think anything of it. I shared it before going to bed, thinking id wake up to 100-200 upvotes maybe. The resulting outpour of support was nothing short of incredible. I really would recommend to anyone to take the time to get deep in the content, and find your own way to #1).

Back to Giannis - any combination of firsts, pritchard, d-white, etc would be hard to swallow, but I feel like we’d simply have to do it.

It gets a lot harder once we get to the Jays. I love Tatum, but as good as he’s looked, the achilles scares me. Brown’s advanced numbers are underwhelming, and this might be a perfect time to sell high.

So the question is… would you consider trading either of the Jays for Giannis?

If no, who would you trade either for? Obviously SGA or Wemby are no brainers, but what about Luka? Luka’s advanced and basic stats are through the roof. I’ve never seen him this motivated, and he’s really ramped up on the defensive side of the ball. What argument is there for this team’s expected outcome to not improve after either a Tatum or Brown for Luka swap?

Should we be emotionally tied to these players til the better in? Or let the spread sheets dictate our fates?
