The Mets have just thrown the 4th combined 3-pitch inning in MLB history, and the first since 2022.

Jonathan Pintaro started the top of the 8th inning with a Ty France groundout, before getting pulled for Nate Lavender. Lavender would then draw a flyout by Jackson Merrill and a popout by Luis Campusano.

Per Baseball Almanac, this is the first 3-pitch inning of the season, and the 4th instance of a 3-pitch inning being split by two pitchers.

  • The first instance came on September 30th, 2000, when Mike Mussina started the 7th inning for the Orioles in Baltimore, recording one out on one pitch, before being replaced by B.J. Ryan who finished the last two.

  • The second came on September 9th, 2012, where the Rangers took on the Rays in St. Pete, this time in the 8th inning. Same as before, Martin Perez would record the first out, and Yoshinori Tateyama the final two.

  • The third and most recent occurrence came on September 27th, 2022, where the Cubs took on the Phillies in Chicago. As with all of these instances, Brandon Hughes would start the 9th inning with one out, before getting pulled for Manuel Rodríguez to finish off the final two.
