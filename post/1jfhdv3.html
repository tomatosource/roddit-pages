The Suns are now the 10th seed in the West

With their win over the Bulls and the Mavericks loss to the Pacers, the Suns have officially rejoined the play-in as they currently hold the tiebreaker over the Mavericks.

The big question remaining: can they hold onto it, and can the trailblazers make a play for the 10th seed. The Suns only have 1 game against a team outside the play-in in either conference (the Spurs) while the Trailblazers have games against Toronto, Spurs, Jazz (as well as the 10th seed Bulls). The Mavs meanwhile may see Anthony Davis return, and their schedule is about the same level of difficulty as the Trailblazers.
