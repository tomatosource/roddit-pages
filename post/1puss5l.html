Sorceress ascendancies have no nodes which explicitly buff fire and ignite, despite having Stormweaver which explicitly buff chill and shock. This is confusing for new players.

This is mainly a clarity/new player issue, not a balance issue. Imagine you’re a completely new player to Path of Exile 2 and think, “I want to play the most generic archetype that almost every fantasy RPG has, a fire mage.” You look at Sorceress and see her description: 

“The elements are mine to control. I know why lightning strikes, why fire burns, and why cold freezes.” 

This strongly implies fire should fit perfectly. But then it’s time to choose your ascendancy. Based on the names, art, and maybe some of the nodes you might think that Stormweaver is the cold and lightning one, Chronomancer is the time one, and Discliple is the minion one… so which one is best for fire? For a new player, this answer isn’t obvious. It IS obvious for cold and lightning, but not for fire.

“That’s what Infernalist is for.” But how is a new player supposed to know to pick the Witch instead, and if we actually take a look at the Infernalist nodes the only ones that actually have buffs for ignite are Bringer of Flame and Loyal Hellhound, both of which are more minion-based nodes.
