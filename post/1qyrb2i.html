Compilation of Outstanding POE 2 Balance Issues

POE 2 still has considerable balance issues. Outside of comparative class damage, which I will not get into here, I would to cover some of the design flaws that have continued for some time.

Mobility

Sprint: Absolutely terrible. The only thing worse than Sprint is not having Sprint. The knockdown needs to be effected by Stun Threshold at the very minimum. Does anyone actually think that logging in and out to avoid the knockdown is good gameplay?

Itemization Issues on Gear:

  • (-) requirements has no purpose since GGG lowered gear and gem attribute requirements. Nobody wants this stat. If it needs to exist for niche builds, move it to a rune/soul core; though they already exist.
  • Mana rolls (on Wands and Staves). You literally roll mana 3x (or more!) on staffs and wands. Nobody wants a mana stat. Mana is broken on weapons; this is an offensive slot and mana isn’t really an offensive stat. We don’t want it.
  • T1 Spellpower/Attack power are perhaps just too rare. This could be addressed if +mana is retuned, as it should be.
  • Spirit: This stat is frequently the end-all, be all stat on gear. Suggestion would be +attribute catalysts, which are virtually worthless, have Spirit added as one of the +stat outcomes. Ditto For the Perfect Essence of Infinite to add % Spirit (at 25% rate), which would make it very powerful affix and allow more flexibility with spirit requirements. Attribute Catalyst would effect Spirit on amulets (and, in some exceptions, on Rings)
  • Attributes: Similar to Mana, we don’t want attributes on our weapons either. Strength, Int, Dex, this is an *offensive* slot and these add virtually nothing on weapons. Instead of Attributes, I’d suggest changing weapon suffixes to Area Damage, Area of Effect, and possibly +1 additional chain/+fork modifiers to open up new build possibilities.
  • Light Radius/Mana recovery/Accuracy hybrid stat: Scrap it. If you want Light Radius to be a thing, make it a pure Light Radius stat and design notables around Light radius. I understand this stat has a niche use; perhaps a soul core?
  • Soul Cores: A lot of soul cores are just useless with no use-case. Make them do interesting , perhaps niche things, like the “Light Radius” suggestion above.
  • Physical damage as thorns: Scrap it. It’s already a rune. If you need to buff the rune of Greater Rune of Tithing to keep Thorns viable, go ahead, but I would suggest just making it into a standard rune to prevent gating issues.
  • Uniques tuned very poorly. While low level uniques don’t need to have perfect affixes, they need to have at least *competitive* affixes, which means spell skills and spell damage at the absolute minimum. Higher, more rare uniques need to have proper tuning.

Atlas Map/Nodes

  • The Map passives are terrible. Specific suggestions are problematic nodes like “Cataclysm Survivors” and “Corrupted Infusion”. I would suggest “Cataclysm Survivors” have a chance for Rogue Exiles to drop exceptional corrupted gear (aka, extra socket built in + whatever Corruption yields.) For Corrupted Infusion, the solution seems obvious: +1 Map corruptions get an additional +1, for level 17 tier Waystones.
  • Wisps: Such a frustrating mechanic. A gimme solution is wisps simply target the map boss. This prevents the issue of wisps despawning, and lets you know in advance the pathing they will take to the map boss. Season of the Hunt would likely need to be changed to double the quantity of wisp drops.
  • Expedition: I suggest Olroth drops, rarely, a new currency item that cleansed Corruption and all-corruption affixes. Currently, his drop pool is very limited, with only the Diamond and the flask being sought after.
  • increase the chance of successful combining using the Recombinator slightly. The omen is fairly rare, but the demand for it is low because the utility is low. Instead of 20 divines, it goes for ~20 exalts.
  • Simulacrum: Suggest mob density reduced to make more builds, specifically those without screen wipes, competitive.
  • Melting Maelstrom mana recovery doubled to 620 to increase viability.
  • Map connections: Increased from 5 to 6. Bugs with broken map connections fixed.
  • Problematic maps adjusted: Hidden Grotto, Excavation, Molten Vault spring to mind. We hate this maps, for pathing and density issues.
  • The Nameless Seer always offers at least a tier 2 unique.

Currency

  • Regal Orbs serve no purpose. Their function can be easily baked into Exalts, with the drop rates simply combined. AKA: Exalts can move an item from Magic to Rare. This helps create more demand for Exalts.
  • Subsequently, Exalts need to have a greater end-game purpose to keep demand high. It is no good to have a Divine Orb going for 2000 exalts. Fate of the Vaal was unique in the fact that divines were severely devalued which kept the ratio in check. The inverse would also work. Use FotV’s learnings to improve the economy going forward. The only suggestion I can put forward is a stat to increase the power of unique item modifiers by 1% per exalt spent. Uniques would subsequently be 20% stronger with 20 Exalt investment. This would keep demand for exalts reasonably higher.
  • Lesser/Greater Jeweller’s Orb: Allow them to be transmutable to the next rank at whatever you deem appropriate. I’d suggest 10 Lessers –&gt;1 Greater and 10 Greater–&gt; 1 Perfect as a starting point, or 3 to 1 but *not* guaranteed to upgrade at like a 33% chance to upgrade for that dopamine hit.
  • As noted above, a new currency item is added: Orb of Purity (suggested name) which cleanses Corruption and all Corruption effects. I would suggest this drops from Olroth.
  • A different idea is an Orb to randomly improve the tier of an item affix by 1 level. (Orb of Attunement?)
  • Omen of Refreshment changed to trigger on *low mana* instead of low life (which makes absolutely no sense with Omen of Resurgence in play.) Omen of Resurgence changed to only restore HP on low life to clearly differentiate the two.
  • Omen of Catalyzing Exaltation success rate seems to be far too low to be close to worthwhile.

Assorted Complaints

  • There is no companion node to Eldritch Battery to improve ailment/stun threshold based on Maximum Mana. This needs to be a new Keystone in the passive tree.
  • Enemy Nameplates need to show nameplates of monsters . The game’s visual effects completely obscure enemies and affixes on the ground. There should be an option to turn off allied spell effects.
  • Cast on Crit is perhaps too-dominant in game. I would suggest opening up “Heralds” spell effects to casters and severely curtailing the dominance of Cast on Crit. This would improve the slower leveling of casters as an added bonus and the sudden power spike (or, more accurately, the correct functioning of the class) with spirit gem level 14.
  • There needs to be an option to search for +1 corrupted gems on the trade site.
  • Types of armor should not have associated movement speed penalties. Scrap this dumb idea. Nobody likes the terrible gameplay of removing armor to move faster, as you see with speed racers.
  • Freeze and Fire have very little identity (Fire with one notable exception in the game of Rolling Ignites.) Fire should probably roll ignites baseline, to be very strong single target. Frozen enemies should probably damage nearby enemies on shattering. Herald of Ice would need a small tweak with this change. I would suggest baking in Pocirkeln’s effect into Herald of Ice and revamping Pocirkeln’s effect. An obvious tweak would be to increase the radius or the area damage of Shattered Enemies.
  • Group play is perhaps too dominant compared to solo play. This is a difficulty balance to strike.
