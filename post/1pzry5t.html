Overdesigned skills part 3 - stats

TLDR: If you shapeshift into a wolf, you are doing the same thing as everyone else, as there is no room for anything else or anything interesting. also hybrids virtually don’t exist. This is a problem with many other skills as well, though the new ones are especially bad.

-———

I posted my feedback about skills several times already, and I focused on the new ones. Let’s now illustrate my point on stats.

in general, my concern was that skills are overdesigned, have too many very specific effects that naturally combine with other skills, significantly reducing player choice and the “feeling” you get from being specific and personal when choosing/homebrewing a build. There are many skills, but a lot of them are tied together very strongly, which means you are not choosing some skills from a lot options, you are choosing packages of skills from a very few options.

I specifically chose Shred as the worst example, it creates very specific fragments that interact only with other wolf skills, making it essentially a package you can’t avoid.

Week 2:

Shred -&gt; the entire package of Pounce, Lunar Assault, Lunar Blessing, is all over 93%. herald of ice is 90%. the only skill out of the entire wolf package that does combine with something else is Lunar Assult, and that is because it is more versatile, freezing stuff instead of doing something very specific that only interacts with the rest of the wolf.

Of course, because this is all connected with a weapon, only 1 type of weapon, even though the skill itself is better, it is only combined with bear skills as a 1-off.

This, in its entirity, means 1 thing. If you want to shapeshift into a wolf, not only you are using the same other skills as everyone else, you are ALSO doing the same rotation because of how the skills chain from one to another. there is virtually no room for anything else.

There was a lot of talk druid is supposed to be this caster/melee hybrid, and how it is supported on the tree and skills design.

Well that’s simply not happening at all. the only non shapeshift skill that people who shapeshift use at all is around 5-8% Thunderstorm to help with ailments. There is no other frequently used active spell shifters use. This is, to me, obvious because if you shapeshift you are already doing a very specific multi-skill rotation, and casting a spell that is not a part of that. hence no one is doing it.

with Bear the situation is better, but only to a point.

Bears are a walking calamity. if you shapeshift to a bear, you are overwhelmingly using Walking Calamity.

Shapeshifting in general is 2 builds. ice wolf with its package, walking calamity bear with its package (slam, ramapge, roar). the variations in terms of skills are cosmetic and rare to say the least.

Now, this is a problem with other skills as well, though druid is really bad at this, worse than the rest of the game, and the rest of the game is also pretty bad at this.

other notable package is the poison arrows (poisonburst arrow, toxic growth, gas arrow, vine arrow to a point). or the spell primal package of Enangle thungerstorm and vines. and more.

Not all skills are this bad, though, there are examples of great skills that fit into various builds that don’t necessarily require others to exist, or function on their own: Spark, Comet, Orb of storms, whirling assault (that is often combined with charged staff, but that is more about how terribly designed THAT skill is, rather than WA).

This lack of potential goes even further. Here the novelty of druid plays a role, but just for future observation: Shred is played on druid only. 87% of all builds that use shred are shamans or oracles. Whirling Assult, a much better designed skill (even though it has its problems) is much more versatile, as only 56% of builds using it are monks, and Titan is even the second most played ascendancy with it. much much better stats, even considering people are sleeping on Acolyte of Chayula.
