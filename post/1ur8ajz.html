Legacy of Runeseeker’s Call not working as intended on wands

I fell for this earlier today when trying to craft a +5 suffix wand and a friend of mine just tried this on a freshly bought wand as well since he’s done with the league anyway and had some spare currency and we both came to the same conclusion: As of today (can’t comment on anything earlier since we haven’t tried before and there are items on trade that prove this worked at some point) the “Legacy of Runeseeker’s Call” seems to be bugged/ not working as intended. It adds its effect “visually” to the item when combined with “20-30% (read: 25%+) increased effect of socketed augment items” to turn Serle’s Triumph from +1 to Suffix modifiers allowed to +2 to Suffix modifiers allowed but, under no circumstances that we could think of, lets you add a 5th suffix modifier to the item. I clicked thousands of exalts to try and add a 5th suffix just in case it was some weird interaction that wouldn’t allow me to add one unless I hit the “5th” slot, tried adding, re-rolling and removing all sorts of modifiers, using greater exalt omens on 3 suffixes and so on. Forced suffix desecration attempts always resulted in a removal of one of the 4 existing suffixes, never adding a 5th. I made another thread with poor wording and explanation earlier about my failed wand craft that gained very little to no traction and I hope this will reach more people to prevent them from attempting this very craft at the moment.
