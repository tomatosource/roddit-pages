If you’re going to game 5, do all of us who can’t be there a big favour

Be fucking LOUD.

Be loud whether we’re up 3 or down 23.

Be loud for Jaylen. Loud for Al Horford. For D White. For KP, for Jrue, for Payton, for Sam, for Luke, for Neemi, for Baylor, for JD, for Jordan, for X, for TC and for Joe.

Most of all, be loud for Jayson.

So if it does end in game 5 at TD garden, don’t let this season and potentially this era of Celtic basketball end on a whimper. They deserve better than that.

These are our Boston Celtics. They deserve to know it.
