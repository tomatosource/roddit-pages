Early campaign warrior feedback

It’s early access and GGG is asking for feedback. I recently watched a friend of mine pick up the game and play for the first time. He chose warrior. He understood the combo of rolling slam to set up stuns for boneshatter. But the awkward nature of rolling slam made the combo really hard for him.

I taught him that by spamming boneshatter he can cancel the second swing in rolling slam and actually get the combo off, but it made me think of 1.0 and all the new people who will be picking up th game. Rolling slam is just the most awkward, unwieldy ability. And having it be the starting ability for warriors just sucks.

They either need to rework the ability or do a new one that can serve the same function in prepping the stun for boneshatter. The good thing is that pulling off the boneshatter combo feels amazing when it works, but getting it to work is so frustrating. My suggestion would be to have rolling slam broken into two parts each requiring a separate button push. First time you press it, it causes one slam which is perfect for boneshatter. And if you decide you want to uselessly slam the ground just past the pack of mobs then you can press it again and it will continue with second slam just like it functions now.
