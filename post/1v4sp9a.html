Ritual Farming Guide: Head of the King Strategy

How Head of the King Works

Head of the King adds six connected Ritual maps to your Atlas.

Moving Head of the King to another location does not change the rewards already rolled for each step.

Each step has four predetermined reward options:

  • A-1
  • B-1
  • C-1
  • D-1

After you select one reward, the next set is rolled:

  • A-2
  • B-2
  • C-2
  • D-2

This continues until all six maps have been chosen.

Changing the location can reveal a larger selection of the available rewards, but it does not reroll or alter them.

For example, one position may show:

  • 2× Chaos Orbs
  • Greater Exalted Orb
  • Omen of Sinistral Exaltation

After moving Head of the King, the fourth option—such as 4× Chaos Orbs—may become visible. The rewards themselves remain the same.

General Farming Strategy

Use Head of the King at all times, both while travelling between cities and while farming cities.

Ritual Waystone Setup

Use:

  • Any corrupted Tier 15 six-mod Waystone

Ritual Tablet Setup

Use:

  • 1× Unique Ritual Tablet
  • 2× Omen and Deferring Favours Tablets with at least 25% reduced deferring cost

Master Setup Jado

  • Unexpected Missions
  • Partial Translations

The remaining two choices are flexible and do not significantly affect the strategy.

Farming Cities

When a nearby tower can cover both the city and its outskirts, use Grand Project.

Place Head of the King so that:

  • The first three maps are outside the city
  • The final three maps are inside the city

First Three Non-City Maps

Use the following tablets:

  • 1× Unique Ritual Tablet
  • 2× Omen and Deferring Favours Tablets with at least 25% reduced deferring cost

Final Three City Maps

Use the following tablets:

  • 1× Unique Ritual Tablet
  • 2× Omen and Deferring Favours Tablets with at least 25% reduced deferring cost
  • 1× Tablet with three additional rerolls and reduced reroll cost

Run the three non-city maps first, then continue into the three city maps to complete the Head of the King chain. This allows you to run the city 3 times for even greater rewards!

Atlas Tree Setup

Mountain Mastery: Tablets

This allows you to craft your own tablets, reducing the overall cost of the strategy.

Evolving Throngs: Swarm

Increased pack size gives you more monsters and therefore more Tribute.

This makes it easier to reroll and purchase deferred Omens.

Swamp Mastery: Pack Size

More pack size means more monsters, which generally results in more Tribute.

Grass Mastery: Effectiveness

Increased effectiveness improves your map modifiers and contributes to greater Tribute generation.

Nemesis Rising: Effectiveness

This provides additional modifier effectiveness and further improves your Tribute generation.

Theory behind the setup

Why 2x defer

  • With two deferring-cost tablets, deferring favours costs zero Tribute.
  • This allows you to run almost any corrupted Tier 15 six-mod Waystone because your Tribute is only needed for rerolls. It significantly reduces the cost of each run and makes the strategy much smoother.

Why 2x Omens

  • Omens is the most reliable source of income when doing rituals Why Unique Ritual tablet? - Allows for additional rerolls at the cost of nothing. Tablet is very cheap.

Why Farm Ritual Outside Cities

  • Cities are great, but rerolling the Ritual shop is the most important part of this strategy.
  • Only running Ritual inside cities limits how often you can reroll and creates more downtime from buying new tablets, changing Masters and repositioning your setup.
  • With this method, you can buy a large supply of Omen and Deferring Favours Tablets and continue running maps with minimal interruption.

Why I Think This Setup Is Superior

A lot of farming strategies become less profitable once content creators start promoting them and more players begin competing in the same market.

This setup is less dependent on a single expensive drop or temporary market trend. Instead, it focuses on consistent Omen income, low running costs and smooth Ritual management.

When choosing tablet suffixes, one suffix is effectively locked:

  • Increased chance for Omens

Omens are the main source of reliable income, which leaves one suffix available.

The question is whether that final suffix should increase Tribute, reduce reroll costs or reduce deferring costs.

Option 1: Monsters sacrificed at Ritual Altars in your maps grant 18–30% increased Tribute

This sounds strong because more Tribute means more purchasing power.

However, increased Tribute does not guarantee that you will have enough to defer every valuable Omen that appears.

Why take the risk of becoming short on Tribute when you can instead reduce the cost of deferring to zero?

More Tribute is useful, but zero-cost deferring removes the problem entirely.

Option 2: Rerolling Favours at Ritual Altars in your maps costs 20–30% reduced Tribute

This is also useful, but rerolling is rarely the most expensive part of a Ritual window.

Buying or deferring valuable Omens generally requires more Tribute than performing the reroll itself.

Reducing reroll cost saves some Tribute, but it does not solve the main issue: keeping valuable rewards available without draining your Tribute.

Why Reduced Deferring Cost Wins

Reduced deferring cost gives you the most consistent result.

With two tablets providing at least 25% reduced deferring cost each:

  • Deferring costs zero Tribute.
  • Valuable Omens can always be kept.
  • You do not need to rely on unusually high Tribute generation.
  • Most of your Tribute can be reserved for rerolls and purchases.
  • The strategy becomes safer and more consistent.

The other suffixes improve your Tribute economy.

Reduced deferring cost removes one of the largest Tribute expenses completely.

TL;DR: Deferring Is King

More Tribute is good.

Cheaper rerolls are good.

But making every deferred reward cost zero is better.

What Favour Rewards Should You Look For When Connecting Your 6 Maps?

Prioritise:

  • Omens
  • Valuable currency
  • Mageblood
  • Headhunter
  • Increased Amount of Favours

What Should You Defer?

That is mostly up to you.

Because deferring costs zero Tribute, you can defer anything that looks useful or valuable.

Purple Omens

I personally defer:

  • Omen of Whittling
  • Omen of Sinistral Erasure
  • Omen of Dextral Erasure
  • Omen of Sinistral Annulment
  • Omen of Dextral Annulment
  • Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation
  • Omen of Dextral Crystallisation

I also defer:

  • Headhunter
