Jonathan Kuminga is demanding a salary in free agency exceeding $20 million annually

The Lakers have had a strong offseason, continuing to build around Doncic and Reaves while adding young talent they believe fits their long-term vision. However, one of their top priorities remains landing Kuminga, who has been at the top of their wish list for much of the offseason.

For weeks, the Lakers and Kuminga appeared to be a natural fit. However, negotiations have become increasingly complicated as the young forward continues to seek a lucrative contract.

Jonathan Kuminga is demanding a salary in free agency exceeding $20 million annually, per The Athletic. Kuminga prefers a sign-and-trade from the Hawks over leaving outright, simply due to the added financial implications. The Cavaliers and Lakers are the finalists for Kuminga

Sources say that the Lakers would still like to sign Kuminga and make him their starting small forward, provided it is on their terms.

“The lakers would like to sign him provided that the 23-year-old is ultimately willing to accept a contract structure on the Lakers’ terms…“ Fischer wrote

The Cleveland Cavaliers remain the Lakers’ biggest competition in the race for Kuminga. Cleveland recently added another wing by signing former first-round pick Mario Hezonja. Kuminga remains one of the Lakers’ top offseason targets, but if they cannot find common ground with the Hawks on a sign-and-trade framework, acquiring him will be an uphill battle. With the Cavaliers still actively pursuing Kuminga, Los Angeles could be in danger of missing out on one of its top priorities.

Kuminga finished the 2025-2026 season with the Hawks averaging 12 points, 5 rebounds and 2 assists per game.
