Additional context provided by Underdog:

  • Klay suffered torn Achilles while recovering from torn ACL
  • KD is only player who averaged more PPG post-injury
  • Kobe played 6 games post-recovery then fractured his knee
  • Billups only played 47 total games post-recovery
  • Dominique scored 30 PTS in first game back; Avg. 29.9 PPG that season

Providing some more context to Underdog’s context re: Klay & KD:

  • The average games played that they list post-recovery for Klay includes his return season, which was a half season only. In the 3 full seasons that he’s played since that season, he’s averaged 72.7 games per season (29.9 mins per game).

  • Same goes for KD. In the 4 full seasons he’s played since his return in the 20-21 season where he returned from injury halfway through the season, he’s averaged 59.8 games per season (36.7 mins per game).
