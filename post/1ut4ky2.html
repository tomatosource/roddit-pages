Mana Siphon is OP

For context, my build has over 11k mana with 50% mana regeneration per second with capped resistances and 40% armor with no life, ES or ward. The build is very tanky except for the mana siphon mod which can kill in 2 seconds.

Most of my deaths to mana siphon are completely unavoidable with no counterplay. Sometimes the mod does not visual show up until you’re dead. There are some monsters that are bugged and show an incorrect location for the effect. Sometimes more than one mana siphoned rare attack you leaving no safe zone. Then there are also many mechanics where monsters spawn on top of you which can instantly kill an otherwise unkillable build.

Mana siphon wouldn’t be so op if it was just taking a base amount of maximum mana per second or reducing mana regeneration rate by a significant amount. But instead it prevent mana from regeneration at all. This pairs horribly with the new Stars Answer skill pared with archmage as you can’t turn it off and therefore die instantly when effected by mana siphon.

So I ask, please nerf this mod or add an atlas passive that allows you to disable a monster mod from appearing in your maps.
