I have a well-equipped character and 1,000 Divs without min-maxing a darn thing

This is my Abyssal Lich:

Builds - GodCthulhu - Path of Exile 2 - poe.ninja

Every day there’s another post on how to ‘farm Divs’ that involves varying degrees of preparation and sunk cost.

This specific combination of these tablets with these modifiers, in this type of Biome.

I never did any of that, the most I did was:

Try and get T16’s, without worrying about what the mods were, or T15’s with high waypoint or Item Rarity

Glance at the tablets in my stash, grab any that have one decent mod.

Never bought a tablet except Visions or Gran Project, never bought a waystone.

I just did…whatever I felt like, couple of Sekhemas, Temples on filler maps between GE’s, breach, Ritual, Citadels, Bosses of all types everything except Delirium, because I don’t like Delirium never have.

I only did just enough to complete the Atlas, the starter Delirious areas is the only one I have, and it’s not finished.

I had these “Big Ticket” lucky drops only:

Mageblood, sold and bought the one I have.

Mana Rune after a few dozen runs, which was used to upgrade my gear to what I have now.

So, yes, a couple of lucky drops, invested into my gear, that’s it.

The entire rest of my gear and currency is from simply playing the game as I described, saving up, and buying upgrades.

What’s the point of my post?

Simply that IMHO, the time spent ‘prepping’ for “efficient” runs is not mandatory, do it if that’s how you like to play, don’t feel obligated to if you don’t.

In the end, the time I spend in game is more enjoyable for me, if I don’t bother much with activities not involved in killing monsters.

If you do, perfect, you will undoubtedly be richer than me, but don’t feel you HAVE to only run 4 city Delirious maps and then make a post about: “burning out”

Just play the game you want the rewards are there.
