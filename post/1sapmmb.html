I expected this game to be way more souls-like, and was mislead.

I bought this game on release and forever will be feeling mislead by the devs and partially by community. We finished the game with my friend, even the high tier maps. Like ok yes, it was fun. But it was gnawing at me 24⁄7, this game was promised to be more difficult and souls like, but it wasn’t at all. I remember the devs saying so. Instead its souls like for the first 3 hours and then it’s same loop as poe1. I ignored the feelings at first, but now that I’m playing Final Fantasy Stranger of Paradise, my ass is lit on fire. Poe2 is not a bad game, but the mismatch of what was promised and what it’s becoming makes me mad. Stranger of Paradise or Nioh 2 is what poe2 should have aimed to be, because Stranger of Paradise and Nioh also have random loot drops. That’s what mix of souls and arpg should be, I understand that this game will never fail, but the direction they are taking is definitely not helping. I just hope they will change course and introduce systems that make game harder and more souls-like. Where your skill is first to your loot.
