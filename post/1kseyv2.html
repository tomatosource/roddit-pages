GGG, Talk to a Chonk Main – 30 Seconds Could Fix an Entire Ascendancy

I need the communities help for this one.

GGG has always valued player and content creator feedback, just look at how creators like Zizaran, Ghazzy, and Fubgun have helped shape balance changes over time. We’ve seen updates to ascendancies and playstyles based on this kind of input, and it’s clear the developers do listen. That’s why I want to talk about one of the most neglected ascendancies: Acolyte of Chayula (a.k.a. Chonk).

Despite having a dedicated playerbase, Chonk remains one of the least-played ascendancies in the game, and the reasons are clear to those who have tried to make it work. Compared to Invoker, it has major struggles. Invoker has strong quarterstaff abilities, bonus Spirit, crit scaling, and can easily run powerful cold or lightning builds with flashy clears. In contrast, Chonk suffers from:

  • Only two chaos-based abilities in the quarterstaff tree
  • A core passive that removes Spirit, hurting clear speed
  • Mantra, a chaos buff that only empowers one singular attack after 10 combo points
  • Hand of Chayula, a melee unarmed skill that’s very difficult to scale effectively

Thematically, Chonk is a chaos monk, a cool concept like a Breachlord-style edgelord dipping into the void. But mechanically, it’s in rough shape. Despite this, the Chonk community is passionate and incredibly creative. I’ve spent countless hours and divines experimenting and learning from theorycrafters way more talented than me (Korihorr, Squish). They know what needs to change to make the class desirable and even popular.

It’s disappointing that the community seems to have largely given up on this ascendancy instead of pushing feedback. If anyone at GGG just spent 30 seconds talking with a Chonk main, they’d understand the problems and the solutions.

How can I get visibility with GGG to make that conversation happen? Even just a small dialogue could lead to meaningful improvements.

Please, help me get visibility to this. I just want to play a melee chaos build, I know many of you do also. There are so many cool build concepts and would love for chonk to become a popular played ascendancy. Look at the latest MTX for Breachlord build, it might actually be the coolest MTX I have ever seen GGG release.
