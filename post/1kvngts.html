Feature Idea: “Boss Practice Mode” - No Permadeath, loot, or other rewards. Must be unlocked.

Title pretty much says it all - interested in people’s thoughts on the idea.

It started after I ripped against Silverfist in act 3 cruel and just decided to farm him 20 times on the same character that just ripped in order to “learn” the fight for that kind of character, against that boss. It was nice to have a no-stakes way to practice a boss encounter.

I decided to take the idea forward on other hardcore characters and now I find myself making the same characters twice - once in softcore, and again in hardcore, just to “practice” certain endgame encounters before I do them in hardcore. Yes, it’s tedious.

It makes no sense to go into a pinnacle boss fight blind in hardcore - hell, even some of the regular map bosses can be dangerous if you don’t understand them - but I hate leveling up characters in softcore, and then also doing all the endgame stuff necessary to say, get to Xesht, or any other endgame boss.

It seems like I could have the best of both worlds if there was just some kind of “practice mode” where I could simulate a no-stakes boss encounter where there’s no loot, xp, progress, or any reward at all - and if I die, I wouldn’t lose my hardcore character.

It would mean I wouldn’t have make characters in softcore just to try out a new boss I’ve never fought before.

Put it behind some sort of grindy unlock or something, I dunno - maybe you have to activate the boss fight at least once just to unlock it in each league, or something else - I haven’t thought that far ahead. The idea is just to keep me in hardcore and pursuing content on the character I want to be playing and pursuing all the content with instead of avoiding it, or making an un-fun redundant character in softcore.

I bet there’s a lot of hardcore players who just never ever even attempt a lot of endgame bosses because it’s basically certain they’re going to get one-shot to a mechanic they’re seeing for the first time and won’t immediately know how to avoid. I don’t think most people are going to gamble their character on something like that.

There’s also benefits for xp-loss avoidance in softcore as well, not to mention you can ‘proof of concept’ a build without grinding/spending absurd amounts of time/orbs for an attempt.

What do you guys think? Good idea? Problematic?
