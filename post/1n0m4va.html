Ranking the Greatest NBA Journeymen

Last week, I asked what makes a journeyman in the NBA, and the consensus seemed to be:

  • Doesn’t stick with one team for long (rarely more than 3 years).
  • Solid but unspectacular role player, not a star.
  • Career “success” usually defined by longevity or being on winning teams, not individual accolades.

So, using that rough criteria, here’s my interim ranking of some NBA journeymen

Tier 1: The quintessential Journeymen

- Jeff Green

- Ish Smith

- Robert Horry

- Trevor Ariza

Tier 2: Career-long Movers

- Enes Kanter

- Dennis Schroder

- PJ Tucker

- Patrick Beverley

- Jae Crowder

Tier 3: Current Active Journeymen
- Taj Gibson

- Garrett Temple

- Reggie Jackson

- Marcus Morris

Curious what you think — who’s missing from this list, and who’s overrated as a “journeyman”? - And what other info/stats should be included when looking at who qualifies as an NBA Journeyman (looking for quantitative criteria for a ranking in a final blog post).
