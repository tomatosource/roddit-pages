The NBA should bring back a version of the “Legends” game for all-star weekend to add some excitement

For those who don’t know, the NBA had a “Legends” game during all-star weekend from 1984 to 1993, which was a completely insane spectacle. It was retired players, spanning different generations (some of them approaching 60-years-old) playing against each other in a full court, five on five game. Somewhere, there’s footage of Bob Cousy scoring at 58.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/allstar/legends.html

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lsz3wLZfwSo (highlights from original 1984 game)

We got to learn that Pete Maravich, turned gospel, could absolutely annihilate his competition a few years post retirement. Turns out Oscar Robertson, approaching 50, could definitely still hoop. George Gervin was still a level above.

Somehow the five on five full court concept lasted eight years before someone had a serious injury, but Norm Nixon and David Thompson both going down caused the league to cancel the event, and they never went back to it.

But…with the increased popularity of 3x3 half court basketball right now across different platforms (big 3, olympics etc), why couldn’t we pay some old guys to have something like that? I think seeing some of the old legends go up against each other, even as a shell of their former selves, would be a much better product than a lot of what the all-star weekend has going on right now
