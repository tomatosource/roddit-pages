Movement in combination with the terrain in this game is absolute ass

Let me preface this by saying that i understand that more depth can be created in the movement space and that terrain being a factor in gameplay can add value.

IF worked out properly. Right now the biggest enemy i face in most maps is the clunkyness of movement.

Being stuck in every nook. The horendous roll into sprint animation. The half forward, half backward roll out of nowhere.

Theres a sidestep/backstep even? I cant reproduce it consistenty. It just happens sometimes.

Its just clunky allaround. One of the pillars of a good game is the ability to transfer input to smooth gameplay and have the character go where the player intents it to go. It just doenst.

Theres way to much friction in this.

Moving in this game sucks. A lot.
