From the latest Ziz Interview - GGG wants Death Recap and is testing it

Didn’t see many people talking about this yet, but I know Death Recap has been a long requested feature that people have wanted and thought GGG was just being stubborn about, but in the recent interview with Ziz, Jonathan and Mark talk about how they not only see the value in it, but want it to be more than just “X damage killed you” and the want it to break down your death in ways that let you better understand where your defenses failed. They said they have an internal version that they use to track things that are out of band or bugged, but it’s very much not ready to show to the player (especially on the UI side.

Direct timestamp to the part of the interview here

Sidenote - as someone who has always been fascinated by game dev, I love that they do so many of these talks. It’s also refreshing over the last couple years to hear how many times they admit “this idea didn’t work” or “we like this, but players don’t so it has to change”. It also humanizes the challenge of making changes in the wake of a lot of comments along the lines of “Why don’t GGG just fix this/make it better?”

Anyways, I think the whole interview is worth a listen, but wanted to link that specific bit since I know it’s something people have wanted for a long time.
