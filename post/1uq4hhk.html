In 1961, on December 8th Wilt Chamberlain put up the most points in a loss in nba history

The game went to triple OT after regulation ended at 109-109. The first OT both teams scored 12 points, the second OT both teams scored 12 points again, in the third OT the lakers put up 18 to the Warriors 14, ending in a score of 151-147 in a win for the lakers.

Elgin Baylor and Jerry West combine for 95 points, with Baylor going off for 63/31/7, 23⁄55 from the field and 17⁄24 from the ft line.

And West going off for 32/10/11, a triple double, on 10⁄23 from the field and 12⁄14 from the ft line.

Wilt Chamberlain went off for a staggering 78/43/1. On 31⁄62 from the field and 16⁄31 from the ft line. His next best teammate Paul Arizin scored 19 points.
