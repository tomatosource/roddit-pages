The 2001 Mariners have the highest team WAR ever recorded. The 1927 Yankees have Ruth, Gehrig, and a +376 run differential. Who actually wins a 7-game series?

This one keeps me up. Let me lay out both sides.
1927 Yankees:

110-44, swept Pittsburgh
Ruth at 60, Gehrig at 175 RBI
Earle Combs, Bob Meusel, Tony Lazzeri hitting 6th, 7th, 8th
Wilcy Moore inventing the closer role before anyone named it
+376 run differential

2001 Mariners:

116-46, tied the all-time wins record
Highest team WAR ever produced, ahead of ‘27 Yankees, ‘98 Yankees, anyone
Led the majors in runs scored AND fewest runs allowed
Ichiro swept MVP/ROY/batting title/steals as a rookie
Boone set the AL RBI record for second basemen
8 All-Stars at their own home park

Numbers say Seattle. Pedigree says New York. Pitching context says it’s not as lopsided as the rings make it look, Moyer at 80mph against modern eyes, but Garcia took the ERA title and Sasaki tied the AL save mark.
I’m leaning Yankees in 6. Convince me I’m wrong before I run the sim.
