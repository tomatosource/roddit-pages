When you’re up by 20, put in the subs

Yeah, I know people will hate this because it’s “not the way the NBA works,” but seriously, when we get up by 20 we need to put in the subs for 5 mins, or until the lead is cut down to 12.

Our starters get complacent, it’s OBVIOUS. They slack on D, then play ineffective slo-mo ISO ball, which gives 4/5ths of the other team a chance to basically rest on every defensive possession.

Let the team down by twenty try to mount a comeback against Scheirman and Walsh flying all around and Queta crashing the boards.

Our guys rest and the other team has to exert extra energy. If they hold the lead, great. If the lead dwindles, you got a rested group to come back in.
