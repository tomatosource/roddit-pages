An Idea for Balancing Solo and Party Play: Equalize Rewards

Hi everyone. I’d like to discuss an idea for balancing solo and party gameplay.

The core problem (which many will agree with):
Currently, party farming provides a huge advantage in the quantity and rarity of loot. This makes solo play inherently less efficient for farming, especially at high levels. Simply “nerfing” these bonuses is not the solution, as it would kill the motivation to play together.

The proposed solution principle:
We need to fight the cause, not the symptoms (the bonus loot). We should equalize the potential reward, but change the conditions for obtaining it.

Concrete proposal:

  • The solo player gets tools to increase difficulty and rewards to the level of a full party (a virtual party, analogous to the /players 8 command from D2(maybe implemented through scarabs) map mods, etc.). This would provide a goal for end-game builds and a challenge. For lore: imagine something like your character being observed by the Maven on the map (virtual players 8).
  • Group play, in order to preserve high rewards (which would now be equal to the solo potential), would “pay” with speed. Content in a group should become objectively slower to complete.

How to implement this?
Introduce map/area mods or mechanics that are only active in a group: “Players have -5% to all Resistances per player in the party”, “Damage chains between players”, “Monsters’ attacks always chain between PLAYERS (not minions)”.

This would create a natural “handicap” for groups: yes, you are stronger and safer together, but the pure speed-clearing of a map will be slower than that of an optimized solo build with the same reward.

The result: Solo gains access to top-tier loot through increased difficulty, while party play retains its social aspect and safety but loses speed as the price for efficient group farming.

Currently, in PoE 2, there are no mods that hinder party play while providing a reward. For example, imagine a scarab with the properties: +20% extra rarity, but damage is dealt first to the player with the lowest EHP. Would a player in a multi-person group want to take this mod? Definitely yes. Would a solo player lose anything? Definitely not.

What do you think about this approach? Would it kill the desire to play in a party altogether, or, on the contrary, would it make the balance healthier?
