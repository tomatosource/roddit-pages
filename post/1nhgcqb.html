A part of stun threshold should apply to sprint interruption

The new sprint mechanic is good and GGG wanted to punish player for abusing it by making the character trip when getting attacked.

This punishment is the reason for the majority of my deaths this season. Sprint to a pack of mob, stop sprinting half a second to late. A 50 dmg projectile hits me. Trip, die.

This punishment should still exist but be avoidable in order not to lose a full map for a stupid mistake. I propose applying a % of stun threshold to damages required for the character to trip. Characters with high stun threshold would be less likely to trip, but abusing it to rush into high damage hits would still get punished.

Any thoughts?
