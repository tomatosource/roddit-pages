Fire infusion on firestorm in not worth ?

For firestorm, the patch indicates “Fire Infusion causes the storm to double in size and create three times as many bolts”. After doing some quick math, it seems that tripling the number of bolts and doubling the storm radius actually decreases your single-target DPS.

Let’s take an example with a 20% quality Firestorm and no modifiers to the area of effect, giving us a 1.2m radius for each bolt and 2.8m for the storm.

  • The area covered by a bolt is: π × 1.2² = 4.52m²
  • For the storm without Fire Infusion: π × 2.8² = 24.63m²
  • With Fire Infusion, the storm radius doubles to 5.6m, so the area becomes: π × 5.6² = 96.52m²

Now let’s calculate the coverage of the bolts:

  • Without Fire Infusion: 4.52 ÷ 24.63 ≈ 18.75% of the storm’s area is covered by one bolt.
  • With Fire Infusion: 4.52 ÷ 96.52 ≈ 4.78% of the storm’s area is covered by one bolt. With three bolts (assuming no overlap), that’s about 14.34%.

If I understood correctly, this would result in lower single-target DPS (23-24% less), regardless of whether you use lightning or cold infusions.

Edit : I guess it depends on if the fire infusion doubles the radius or the area. In this case it would be 53% more damage. We’ll have the answer very soon.
