Offseason Changes

Obviously, we all know about the report that Shams had this week that roster changes are coming after this postseason run for the Celtics. However, I am truly wondering how big those changes could be. I think we’re all in consensus that Tatum, Brown, White, and Pritchard are likely not going anywhere. But say the Celtics were to win the title this year, is it out of the realm of possibility that KP and Jrue stay as well?

My thought process is that Sam Hauser is probably gone after the year between his pay and decreased role. But what if Horford retires? His money would also be off of the books, that clears around $20 million. Would ownership bite the bullet on a decreased tax bill to keep the core together and go for the three-peat?
