Heads up for those who use the Rhoa mount (crash, CTD)

If you happen to be crashing frequently and consistently then make sure you’re not using the Rhoa mount before fiddling with your settings or troubleshooting drivers and anything else.

I personally copied a fairly popular blood mage / ice shot / bleed bow build that uses the Rhoa, and noticed I was crashing a lot. This is likely due to a weapon swap bug exclusive to the Rhoa, or just the Rhoa mount itself. If you notice your character essentially clipping into the Rhoa instead of riding it properly, then mounting the Rhoa and firing your bow will 100% cause a crash.

I have no idea if there is a fix, except just never using the dumb mount. This is just what I have been able to gather.
