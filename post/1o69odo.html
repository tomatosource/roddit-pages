The “infused spell” idea is great, if only…

If only the player could gain infusion charges immediately after conditions are met instead of jumping into the swarm of monsters to pick them up.

Why on earth do I need to run into the middle of the monsters’ pack, in melee range, as a spell caster, to pick up the infusion charge to kill that same pack? Or how about that crazy Soul Eater, Hasted, Invulnerability Aura rare mob, am I supposed to sit next to him to pick up the infusion charge, instead of kiting him as a normal human being?

I know that GGG values the moment-to-moment combat and stuff, and there are also increased pickup range affixes, but isn’t this too much? I started leveling an elemental caster yesterday, put aside all the godawful numbers on the elemental spells, the entire infused/enhanced gameplay is just exhaustion, and it killed all the motivation to play for me.

Call me lazy or tell me to gid gud, whatever. But this is just too much.
