Just a noob who Decided to craft a glove for my twister build and after spending maybe 50 divines I was able to hit this craft. I Am not sure if this is better than my previous gloves but it surely looks nice.

Craftting steps if I remember was:

1.) Buy gloves of your choice with 2 augmentable sockets.

2.) if it wasn’t rare make sure to alch it

3.) Orb of annulment your glove until there is only one modifier

4.) Socket a Kolr’s hunt and a second socket of your choice but for me I used Astrid’s Creativity

5.) Chaos spam till you get +2 projectiles

6.) Once you get + 2 projectiles make sure to slam a perfect exalted orb with omen of greater exaltation ( hope that you get flat damage or surpassing chance of projectiles to save you from a headache.)

7.) Desecrate suffixes with abyssal echoes if you dont have a good suffix or Desecrate prefixes if you dont have a good prefix.

8.) OPTIONAL Fracture orb and hope you hit +2 projectiles ( I only did this when i had atleast 3 modifiers that were good so that its a good fracture regardless)

NOTE - my crafting guide might not be the best and I was pretty lucky with my rolls.
