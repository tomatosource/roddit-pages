Best Playoff Bullpen Decision Of All Time?

Alright, we did the worst yesterday. Now, let’s do the polar opposite. What do you think is the absolute best, smartest, and/or most rewarding bullpen decision ever made in the playoffs, and why?

For me, this is surprisingly easier to choose. MadBum in Game 7 is my pick for this. Even though he wasn’t brought in to face a lefty, he was still damn near unhittable in his 5 starts before. So, Bochy going to his ace in the 5th inning, out of the pen, and with a one-run lead, has to be the ballsiest and smartest bullpen move ever made, especially considering he was on 2 days rest, and pitched 5 scoreless on the road for the longest save in Postseason history.

But there are a lot of other good picks, whether they’re starters or relievers. In fact, my pick for strictly relievers would probably go to Joe Maddon bringing in Chapman for an 8-out save. Once again, it was a 3-2 game, in a World Series game said team had to win, and said manager brought out their star lefty to face a righty. But either way, said lefty still delivered a masterpiece out of the pen, and their team would go on to win the series thanks to their efforts.
