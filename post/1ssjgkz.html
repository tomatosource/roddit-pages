Usually a rogue-like game/mode is very addictive, but not for the Trial of Sekhemas&Chaos, what went wrong?

I have played a lot of rogue-like/rogue-lite, Skul, Hades, Vampire Survivors, tiny rogues, etc you name it, usually they are very addictive to me which makes me want to play rounds after rounds and sinking countless hours into it.

However in PoE2 the two trial that are inspired by rogue-like, just aren’t that fun, most people even hate it and don’t want to engage with it at all.

From my personal perspective, the main reason I dislike the trials is the sense of negative feedback.

Usually in many other rogue-like, after you clear each stage you are able to acquire a new weapon/item, add a new ability, upgrade your skills, so on, although the content is getting more and more challenging, but at the same, you are constantly feeling stronger, stage after stage, which makes people feel fun and want to keep going deeper. However in PoE2, especially in the Trial of Chaos, what you are getting after you have completed a stage is to pick a downside, getting annoying afflictions, you are fked, you are getting shittier and shittier lol, all you can do is to pray that the next downside is relatively not as terrible. I think most of the players want a challenging content, they don’t mind the trials to be hard, or even harder than the current version, but these regressive approach will lead to a sense of a negative feedback, which accumulate stage after stage, and just make people feel annoyed, stressed instead of challenging and fun. Interestingly, Ultimatum in PoE1 works and people love it, I think because it is way more fast paced which lead to my second reason.

The second reason is that the punishment for dying in the trials is just too high. I understand that you die you get no loot, but these Trials are a relatively long section, once you die you not only get nothing, you can’t ascend, but also it makes you feel like you have wasted a lot of time. In other rogue-like, even when you are dying you are progressing, because the exp you earn, the stuff that you collect can be used to upgrade your passives, your abilities, your stats, which makes you feel like you have a higher chance of winning in the next round. We have relics in Trial of Sekhemas, but I think they aren’t interesting enough, I hope they can provide more powers, some exclusive abilities instead of just stacking honour resist etc.

What about you guys? What do you guys think went wrong and what could GGG do to improve the experience?
