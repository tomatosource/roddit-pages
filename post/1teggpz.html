Jealousy

It’s been a few weeks since the Celtics have been eliminated. Seeing the remaining teams, in both conferences, I am so jealous of the way they play the game. I grew up watching the NBA in the late 2010’s and loved seeing players go at it in the post and the painted area, with a few 3’s in between. Watching the Pistons play today made me so jealous. I was watching today, and the 3’s they could’ve shot but made the play inside when it wasn’t going for them was great to see.

Again, I’m just sad, sad the Celtics are gone and wishing we could drive the ball to the hoop more and play the inside game.
