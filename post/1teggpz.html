Jealousy

It’s been a few weeks since the Celtics have been eliminated. Seeing the remaining teams, in both conferences, I am so jealous of the way they play the game. Again, I’m just sad, sad the Celtics are gone and wishing we could drive the ball to the hoop more and play the inside game.
