Friendly Reminder: The 76ers haven’t beaten the Celtics in a playoff series since 1982, 43 years ago.

If you want some positivity back in your day look no further than this post.

After 1982, the Celtics and 76ers have gone head to head in the playoffs for 6 series, with the Celtics winning all of them.

I was thinking about Kyrie’s comments today, about that weird KD/AD super team and it reminded me of Marcus Morris going “lll. 0” with his hands in Embiid’s face. Well, the answer is I can’t remember because it was around a decade before I was born.

1985 - Boston, 4-1

2002 - Boston, 3-2

2012 - Boston, 4-3

2018 - Boston, 4-1, ‘fetti

2020 - Boston, 4-0

2023 - Boston, 4-3

Go Celtics.
