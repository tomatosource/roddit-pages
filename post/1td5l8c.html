Each League I can’t decide beetween Trade or SFF, so far I went SFF every time

My experience with SFF is that I am loving it, I can get deep into T15’s and have tons of fun, but at some point I hit the wall with my character power progression. Once you are like “This is my build” you realise you start facing a lot of frustration. You don’t find runes/soul cores/idols you actually want and deep endgame becomes unrewarding and frustrating. Majority stuff becomes worthless to me, yet simple things like Exalts are extremly valuable coz I don’t think I ever stockpiled more then 100, Divines are worthless to me and I “waste” them so fairly low value actions like rerolling some items or tablets.

Each league I am thinking, “man, I hear the trade is so good in this game, especially since the automation of it since 0.3”.

Yet I am affraid that trade will have negative impact on everything before that point of my SFF wall. Ofc… I will just have to try Trade at some point and make my opinion on which one I prefer.

Anybody here had similar experience? Perhaps you started SFF and later decided give Trade a shot? What was your experience?
