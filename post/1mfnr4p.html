Release act 4, 5, and 6 with 1.0

Am i the only one, who wishes, that act 4, 5 and 6 would release with 1.0?

One of the most magical parts of POE2 has been experiencing Acts 1, 2, and 3 for the first time. That sense of discovery, new bosses with its cool voicelines and music, artstyle, it all felt truly unique.

Now, with the plan to release Acts 4, 5, and 6 in slices before 1.0, I’m worried that magic will be lost. By the time the full release arrives, many of us will have already played through these acts multiple times. Instead of feeling fresh and exciting, it will start to feel repetitive.

Personally, I’d love to experience Acts 4–6 for the first time as part of a complete journey when the game officially launches.

What do you think?
