The amount of care that goes into the sound design, and voice acting is top tier.

There are just so many moments during the campaign that stand out even after countless runs. A few of my favorites that still hit even now as I’m making my way through:

The entirety of Lettis’s lines pleading with the Executioner before that chilling scream.

The discontent, rage, and sadness in Azarian’s lines followed by his last words being “She didn’t.. want me..” still gives me a lump in my throat.

The sound the gate makes as it opens in the Ziggurat gives me little goosebumps every dang time.

The anticipation the distant thumps give when I enter the Molten Vault.

Just wanted to give a bit of praise to the people who craft these details. Didn’t even touch on the skill sounds or phenomenal art but they all add up to create the most atmospheric campaign I’ve played in an arpg. So thank you!
