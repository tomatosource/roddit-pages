Pseudo-MoM and Trial of the Sekhemas

I built a manastacker with Eldritch Battery and 100% damage taken from mana before life with Cloak of Defiance and the tree. I didn’t want to allocate Mind over Matter because Sacrifice of Mind was already cutting my regen in half. Turns out, while with MoM you get your max honour in Trial of the Sekhemas based on your mana as well your life (and es if you didn’t convert it), it’s not the case if you just simply take all damage to mana instead of life, thereby circumventing the drawback of MoM by instead using other parts of your power budget (more skill points, chest piece and a rune)

What I would like to see happen is to take a percentage of our mana into account based on how much of our damage we take from mana before life (up to 100%, since the stat can go above 100% but does nothing past that point)
