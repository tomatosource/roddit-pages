Steph Curry to the Celtics

I would like to start by saying there is an extremely small chance Steph curry does not retire as Warrior. However, in the event Dunleavy blows up the roster, anything is on the table.

Going full rebuild in similar fashion as Danny Ainge did back in the day is probably the best move for the sake of Golden State’s future. They have shown no capability of surrounding steph with a contending team and the value of draymond, Jimmy, and Steph gets lower by the day given their age. An expiring contract like PGs and the picks obtained in the Jaylen brown trade would be great assets for a rebuilding team. Steph curry in this offense is quite literally a perfect fit. He’s still playing at an all nba level, and the shooting isn’t gonna decline much with age. If he can stay healthy, and that is a big if given his injury history the past few years, I would say he still has 2-3 high level seasons left in him.

GSW ownership signing off on trading a franchise icon and a cash cow is a tough sell, but it is their best path to contention in the future. Steph is also a free agent next summer, but I doubt he would ever willingly play somewhere else and PG will opt into his final year with Boston anyways. He could easily enhance his legacy and put a contender over the top, but he has shown no signs that’s a priority for him. It’s almost a guarantee he signs his extension next month as well, so a full blown golden state rebuild seems like the only possible route. Interestingly enough, the Warriors have kept almost all of their future picks, maybe for a reason.

Who knows, maybe this is one of the possibilities Brad had in the back of his mind when he kept mentioning optionally in the JB trade presser.
