Heat keeps Terry Rozier on roster past Wednesday deadline, guaranteeing full salary for season

Article by Anthony Chiang

While Miami Heat guard Terry Rozier has been away from the team and is not expected back this season in the wake of his Oct. 23 arrest stemming from a federal investigation into illegal gambling, the Heat has still opted to guarantee Rozier’s full $26.6 million salary for this season.

With only \(24.9 million of that \)26.6 million salary initially guaranteed, the Heat had an opportunity to save $1.7 million by waiving Rozier before Wednesday’s 5 p.m. league-wide deadline to release players before all contracts become fully guaranteed.

But the Heat decided to keep Rozier on its roster past that deadline and guaranteed his full $26.6 million for this season.

Why?

The biggest advantage of keeping Rozier on the roster beyond Wednesday is to have his large expiring salary available to use in a trade prior to the NBA’s Feb. 5 trade deadline. Not only could Rozier’s $26.6 million salary help balance the salary-cap math in a trade, but it also could be attractive to a team because it’s an expiring salary that will come off the books this upcoming offseason.
