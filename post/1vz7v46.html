I feel onboarding to the crafting system is not very beginner friendly. There’s too much risk involved if you aren’t sure what you’re doing, and it just ends up making more sense to just save crafting currency to buy gear from the trade site

and that’s…not great. I want to play the game and get gear, not earn currency to browse a trade site to purchase gear.
