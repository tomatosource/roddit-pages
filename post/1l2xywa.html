Towers are hidden in the mist, tower hop mindfully

I’ve been tower hopping looking for 4 tower set ups for 6 man farming for my guild. I’m not the only one doing this (and there are guildies who are better at setting up maps than I am) and I wanted to share this tidbit of wisdom.

Towers can be hidden, so you have one tower poking out of the mist, and it could have another tower in the vicinity that is hidden.

I’ve started really thinking about how I approach towers. The 3 tower set up turned into a 4 tower set up, and I could easily have bricked it. Because the 3 tower set up wasn’t the best, it could have way better overlap and there was a lake adjacent.

I get it, for a lot of us setting up maps is tedious. It feels a lot better to make it its own mini game, and I’ve found the key to this is setting them up for a group.

It means we can take turns. So we can always run a 6 man group with an aura and a banner bot, and always be running a bare minimum of 49 quant on the node. We’re trying to get to the point where 49 is only for 4-5 man groups and have higher quant for 6 man crews.

As a group we get so much more productivity out of the work that goes into setup. It becomes an art. I run corrupted waystones while pathing to farm more waystones which I always use an omen on to increase the number of prefixes.

We’ve dropped 3 mirrors so far. It isn’t just luck. It’s consistency and efficiency.

Mind you we consider ourselves a “try hard” guild. But it doesn’t feel like we’re trying hard cuz we’re joking most of the time.

I didn’t have anyone to talk game with in the first league. Anyway, apologies for the rant…

TL:DR some of your towers are hidden in the fog of war, not based on distance, but some random roll. Keep this in mind when looking for tower setups.
