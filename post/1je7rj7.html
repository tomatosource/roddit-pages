Please for the sanity of the following leagues, fix the drop rate of everything.

I think we can all say that the economy has moved destructively fast, and it’s important to note a few things:

Divine Orb on release was pretty tame, mostly because there was no use for them. Important to note that Divines rose quite significantly once people started obtaining Astramentis, Ingenuity, and Temporalis. The price of the Divine Orb is locked between these high-cost uniques.

With a patch, they increased the drop rate of Exalted Orbs. This skyrocketed everything else. I think we all know now that it’s not that everything is gaining value, it’s that the Exalted Orb is tanking, hard. This made the balance of the currency extremely out of sync and essentially made it impossible for newer players to afford anything.

GGG will not balance the game around SSF. I think this is fine, but SSF progression is extremely sparse. You will most likely not see a Greater or Perfect Jewellers Orb, Divine Orb, Omen of Anything, and very few of things you actually need that you can’t salvage like Gem upgrades, Greater Essences, Chance Orbs, Chaos Orbs, etc. even all the way up to T15 maps.

What is the harm of increasing the drop rates for things other than Exalted Orbs? Thing is, we DO have decent crafting options, maybe not as many as we’d like, but the sheer lack of non-Exalted Orb currency in general loot makes crafting extremely limited.

GGG needs to find a balance between currencies to a point where they think it’s fair, because realistically currency will sit at whatever price they want based on its drop rate. The only things that will hyper-inflate will be end-game uniques such as Astramentis and Temporalis. All craftable gear will stay in line with the cost to make them.

So why not buff Divine Orbs so hard that they drop to 100ex? As well as every other currency being buffed to be able to maintain a surplus for crafting.

Shifting the economy to stop the hyper-inflation of currency will bring so many more players back to the game than another crafting method. Just bring in more currency to craft with.
