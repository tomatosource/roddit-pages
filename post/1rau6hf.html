In retrospect, did the Heat get more value from trading an aging, disgruntled Jimmy Butler than the Mavericks did for trading prime Luka Doncic?

In exchange for Doncic, the Mavs received:

- Anthony Davis* (20.2 points, 10.8 rebounds, 1.8 blocks in 29 games played)

- Max Christie (12.5 points, 3.7 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 82 games played)

- 2029 1st round pick (Unprotected)

*Davis subsequently traded to Wizards for:

- Marvin Bagley

- A.J. Johnson

- Khris Middleton

- 2026 1st round draft pick (via OKC, likely pick 29 or 30)

- 2030 1st round draft pick (via Golden State, top-20 protected)

- 2026 2nd round draft pick

- 2027 2nd round draft pick

- 2029 2nd round draft pick

In exchange for Bulter, the Heat received:

- Andrew Wiggins (16.7 points, 4.8 rebounds, 2.9 assists, 1.2 steals and 1 block in 68 games)

- Davion Mitchell (9.5 points, 6.4 assists, 1.2 steals in 77 games)

- Kyle Anderson*

- 2025 1st round pick (top-10 protected, conveyed and used to select Kasparus Jakucionis)

*Anderson subsequently traded in a package for Norman Powell

The Mavs definitely got more than the Heat did in terms of pick quantity, but Miami getting decent production out of most of the acquired players and Davis being a lemon has made the deals seem a lot closer in value than I thought when both trades were made.
