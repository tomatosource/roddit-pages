The Celtics are the Mecca of basketball

This is an appreciation post. This team, to me, is the most well managed team in sports. Better than any team across the major leagues in North America. The Celtics are so well oiled, and it starts from the top down. The ownership, while *understandably* cutting costs to go under the 2nd apron, still maintaind a close eye on getting quality contracts. Then we have Brad - absolute unit as the GM. Dumped two major contracts off the books. Got some solid picks and players out of it. Made incredible trades to improve the team each year. Then there’s the coaching staff, starting with Mazzulla, is absolutely incredible at leading a pack of lions and winners. Then the players. The players have unreal chemistry, and work so well together, even if the combinations and lineups vary from game to game.

We have an innate ability to find and develop homegrown talent (e.g. JT, JB, Hugo, Pritchard, Hauser) and finding hidden gems that thrive in our system (Queta, KP, DWhite).

And we have an insane player dev system. We just develop players and keep them good and working so well.

We are so consistent and have been in the 2020s. Through 2026, we have 4 conf. finals appearances, 2 finals appearances, and a title. Tied with the Heat for most Finals appearances this decade. This is unmatched in the NBA right now.
