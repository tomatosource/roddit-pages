Where’s the hype for the built-in build following system? This is one of the coolest things I’ve ever seen any studio do for their community.

In case anyone missed it in the reveal stream: Here’s the relevant segment.

TL;DW, there is a new filetype that will be supported by the game itself that basically imports full builds (including notes about items, skills, even item slots) into the game itself, acting as sort of a built-in guide that grows with your character.

I am pretty sure that this will be something you can “export” somehow with ease, maybe through POB2 or poe.ninja somehow.

I think this is a spectacular thing to see. I know most people who’ve been around the PoE block a few times are very much accustomed to following builds with as little as a basic PoB, but for new players this is going to be a huge game changer.

There’s a lot of disappointment surrounding this reveal, but I think this aspect of 0.5 needs to be celebrated! I can’t wait to see what our favorite build creators, and everyone else too, is able to put together.
