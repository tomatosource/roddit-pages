PoE Farming Situation

I’m a recent D2 convert to PoE 2 and I’ve noticed a massive gap in the gameplay from my perspective (hardcore only player):

In D2, you used to be able to grind areas/acts for gear to be able to proceed through the game and ultimately the hangup would be the lack of endgame

With PoE2, the grind of certain areas is literally non-existent, or feels terrible… So you’re essentially forced to push forward through the game to enhance your gear and power. This sucks because not only does it not feel good to farm items prior to end game, but the chances of getting one shot through the campaign are also high…

I sure wish PoE2 would incorporate even 40% of the replayability of the campaign in terms of farming compared to D2….
