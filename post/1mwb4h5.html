Crafting in 0.3

I think people are sleeping on how impactful change of introducing Greater and Perfect crafting orbs is.

In announcement video Aug and Transmute limits mods to 70+ and Regal + Exalt + Chaos limits to mods to 50+.

THIS IS HUGE.

  1. If you crafting from an empty base you are guaranteed to have at least 2 T1 or T2 mods with magic currency. This alone will significantly increase number of available good crafting bases for future crafting.
  2. Reccombinator got buffed because of that too. One main issue with reccombinator was that you never had enough good bases with good mods to choose from. Well now you will have significantly more.
  3. Rare currency applies 50+ level mods. Meaning that all of your mods going to be at least T4, but in majority of cases T3+. Again, huge for self-crafting. Yeah, you not going to craft mirror-tier items and yes there still going to be a lot of waisted bases. But it is miles better than what we have now.
  4. A lot of bad mods are cut off at lover level tiers. Light radius? level 30 max, you never going to get it again while using any greater crafting currency. Reduced attribute requirement? - Capped at level 60, not going to get it with magic currency and odds of getting it with rare are significantly reduced.

Is it a perfect crafting system? No. Does it give much more agency and ability to craft? Hell yeah. The only thing that we need to see now is how rare those currencies are. If Greater Transmute and Aug will be as rare as omens are then this all doesn’t make any difference to 98% of player base, but if they are, lets say 3x rare than regular version - then crafting just got significant boost, probably the biggest since start of PoE 2.
