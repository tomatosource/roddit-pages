Temple building hidden lessons learned (the hard way)

Temple has so many hidden rules, I just wish there is a simple place that has quick condensed tips like below:

  • When you are almost done with spymaster chain, DO NOT UPGRADE the level of last spymaster!! – if you do it you will not get enough room locks in future.
  • If you lock any of the room before clicking the “run temple” button, the drop rate of room locks will decrease significantly. Not sure whether locking during the run has impact though
  • Do not trust the in-game hint of NOT connecting rooms. Even if you are confident, if you are about to drop a room that in graph theory (not temple) that can loop your chain, run the simulation in Sulo first

There are so many people making youtube videos about temple, I cannot watch all of them for hours then find out the above rule. But you can also imagine learning the above the hard way
