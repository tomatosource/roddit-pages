Millions of Salary Paid per Win Share for All Stars Drafted 2011-14

I wanted to measure just how far availability matters and decided to use career salary earned / career win shares to get a proxy for it.

Limiting the range to the top 3-4 all star caliber players in the draft and keeping the draft range relatively tight going with 2011-14 here to avoid wonkiness due to salary inflation.

[Rank] [Player] [Draft] [Millions / WS]

  1. Jokic 2014 $1.95M / WS

  2. Gobert 2013 $2.25M / WS

  3. Giannis 2014 $2.35M / WS

  4. Butler 2011 $2.52M / WS

  5. Dame 2012 $2.78M / WS

  6. AD 2012 $2.83M / WS

  7. Draymond 2012 $3.13M / WS

  8. Kawhi 2011 $3.17M / WS

  9. Kyrie 2011 $3.42M / WS

  10. Embiid 2014 $4.10M / WS

  11. CJ McCollum 2013 $4.69M / WS

  12. Klay 2011 $4.94M / WS

  13. Beal 2012 $5.43M / WS

  14. Lavine 2014 $5.62M / WS

Some notables are:

  1. On availability: Kawhi being worse than Draymond on this metric but this is partly due to Draymond’s lower salary as well, as well as Klay and Kyrie generally being low.

  2. However, AD despite having an injury track record still manages to be near the top of the list.
