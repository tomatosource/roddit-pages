I love Combos - but only a specific type of combo.

I have like 500 hours in POE and it’s one of my favorite games ever. I’ve been playing a lot of POE2 with my girlfriend and something I’ve been noticing a lot is that I really don’t like the “X Charge” generation mechanics or the fact that some moves require you to expend charges to make them good. This reminds me a lot of how D4 started out where you had mana generators and mana expenders. I hated that.

I recently started a warrior and noticed I don’t have to do any of that shit to make an effective build, but I’m still doing lots of awesome combos with cool interactions. Getting enemies to the right stun threshold before unleashing with hone shatter feels amazing both in groups and on bosses, and saving my big charge moves for when the boss is down also feels rewarding. The most important part is that while this does rely on a “state” being active, it doesn’t rely on a “resource” being expended. I was also pleasantly surprised that stuff like the shock totems have support gems that enable their own combos. This is wonderful.

If there were more ways to generate these kinds of charges I wouldn’t mind, but the fact that a lot of these charge generators rely on end game skills or horrible horrible execute-style moves (which incentivizes you to NOT KILL an enemy and NOT DO to much damage) means I kind of hate them and avoid them like the plague.

More combos should be interactions with other abilities instead of just “shit move. If you have a charge, it becomes a good move”
