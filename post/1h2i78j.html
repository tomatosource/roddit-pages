Today’s Celtics Cup Prospects and Rooting Guide

There’s a TLDR at the bottom, if people want to skip there.

If the Celtics lose today in Chicago, we are officially eliminated.  Therefore, since nothing about the Cup means much to us if it doesn’t involve the Celtics, all scenarios below presume that the Celtics win today.

--

The remaining Cup games are today and Tuesday. I’ll only discuss today’s games other than giving general context.

If ATL beats CLE, the Celtics cannot win Group C, and therefore must compete for the 1 wildcard slot with three other teams: Tuesday’s [MIL at DET] loser, Tuesday’s [ORL at NYK] loser, and Cleveland. No, our head to head tiebreaker with Cleveland won’t help us in this circumstance.

Point differential will decide who gets the wildcard, so the Celtics will need to crush the Bulls to have a good chance to get that spot. ORL plays at BKN today, NYK at CHA and DET at IND, and since any of these three (ORL, NYK and DET) could be our competition for that spot, we should hope that as many of them lose today as possible, and by as many points as possible. It might not end up mattering, but rooting for these outcomes cannot hurt us and could help us.

If CLE beats ATL, the then 3-1 Celtics will win Group C. At this juncture, point differential still matters, since we have some chance of hosting our quarterfinal game, which means we’d have to be one of the top 2 seeds among the 3 Group winners (decided by point differential). So while it’s less important for us to crush the Bulls, it is still meaningful to give us the best odds, which would come from hosting our upcoming opponent.

Again, we should root for ORL, NYK and DET each to lose today, by as many points as possible, since each of them could also be our competition for one of the top 2 seeds (and thus the quarterfinals home game).

--

The rest of the story will come on Tuesday, where today’s results will determine who our rooting interests are on that night, and which games matter.

As a spoiler alert, if ATL wins today we’ll probably be rooting for Orlando (over NYK) and Milwaukee (over DET), as well as hoping WAS can stay close to CLE on Tuesday. If CLE wins today, we’ll probably be doing the opposite–rooting for NYK and DET (and the CLE/WAS game won’t matter to us). Reasons for this will just complicate this post, as well as why I’m saying “probably” instead of “definitely.” It will all be sorted out by today’s results.

--

TLDR for today’s games: We are rooting for the Celtics to beat CHI in a blowout. Points will definitely matter to us in some way.

We are rooting for CLE to beat ATL. Priority: High. Point differential of this game is irrelevant to us if CLE wins. [If ATL wins, we hope it is a blowout.]

We are rooting for CHA to blow out NYK. Priority: Probably low.
We are rooting for BKN to blow out ORL. Priority: Probably low.

We are rooting for IND to blow out DET. Priority: Probably low.

The TOR / MIA game doesn’t matter to us at all.
