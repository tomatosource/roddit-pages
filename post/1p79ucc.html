Druid, Controller and my hopes.

This is going to sound sacrilege to most of you PC players but I recently started using my controller instead of my mouse/keyboard and it completely changed the vibe while playing Path of Exile 2. I was absolutely blown away. I thought for sure inventory management would be a nightmare with a controller but GGG nailed it with their control scheme/design!

I’m really looking forward to playing the Druid and I hope it has some builds that play well with a controller. The Bear build they showcased in 2023 specifically looked like it could be pretty comfy just trampling everything on your screen.

Anyone else feel the same about controller after trying it? Does anyone know when they are going to make controller and mouse/keyboard hot swappable without going to login screen?
