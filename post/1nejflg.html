An new angle regarding the ramifications for a potentially light punishment for the Clippers for presumably circumventing the cap to pay Kawhi:

Earlier in the offseason, the Milwaukee Bucks and Phoenix Suns both waived and stretched max players—Damian Lillard and Bradley Beal—taking on the maximum allowable dead cap hits for the foreseeable future.

If Clippers get a relative slap on the wrist, both teams would be highly incentivized to circumvent the cap in a similar fashion to work around the ~$20m of dead cap space they inherited from enacting the waive-and-stretch provision.
