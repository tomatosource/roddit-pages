The NBA Cup shouldn’t be structured as East vs. West

There is no real reason that the NBA cup has to follow the same East vs. West playoff/finals structure other than limiting travel, but given that the group stage schedule is already part of the regular season this only really matters for a couple of elimination matches. The NBA should actually use this opportunity to scramble up all 30 teams into 6 truly random groups. It would be refreshing to see two East or two West teams compete in a “final” for something and would promote the possibility of a big local rivalry game being elevated to NBA cup final status
