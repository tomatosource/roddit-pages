[The Athletic] Adam Silver and Mark Tatum were NOT in attendance in Washington for the briefing to Congressional staff members about the league’s ongoing illegal betting scandal. The league representatives claim they did not clear Rozier, but simply could not prove he committed any wrongdoing.

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6781280/2025/11/05/nba-officials-congress-briefing-gambling/?source=user_shared_article

Members of the NBA’s front office, but not commissioner Adam Silver, are in Washington on Wednesday to brief Congressional staff members about the league’s ongoing illegal betting scandal, two sources briefed on the meeting told The Athletic.

Lawmakers, most if not all of whom are not on Capitol Hill due to the ongoing federal government shutdown, are not attending the briefing.

The meeting with the U.S. House of Representatives Energy and Commerce Committee staffers began at 3 p.m. and ended before 4. Attorneys for both the league and the committee, as well as a consultant representing the NBA, are involved in today’s briefing, said a congressional source, who also said deputy NBA commissioner Mark Tatum was not at the briefing, either.

While the NBA can respond to the Senate’s inquiry in writing, if the answers do not satisfy lawmakers they could, when the government re-opens, compel Silver to testify. The league has said it didn’t “clear” Rozier of wrongdoing, but rather simply wasn’t able to prove he broke league rules or committed federal crimes, in part because it does not have subpoena power like the federal government does.
