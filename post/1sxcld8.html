Anyone else find the Temple to be infinity more satisfying than the campaign or end game?

Now I know for the overall proper game they are going to nerf the hell out of the temple because it was too rewarding for the stingy POE devs.

But I honestly cannot think of a time where the effort and the reward felt like they were balanced perfectly.

I mean we definitely have people maximizing the temple now, but my first go through was one of the most rewarding times when they finally killed Atziri, and my temple would have been considered a mess by the maximizing standards.

It actually felt REWARDING to play by actually just PLAYING the Temple, in multiple ways.

First, it was FANTASTIC to use for XP and gold. If you found yourself lagging during the campaign, a temple run would get you caught up.

Second, if you had enough rooms, killing Atziri was ALWAYS rewarding - You never felt like you went through all the effort to clear the temple and kill the final boss and then you ended up with jack shit. I know that some of the devs feel like coming away with nothing makes the rewards feel that much bigger but in this case given the time spent in the temple, it would have felt so empty to get so little.

The temple just seems to reward effort. Effort spent in building the temple, effort spent in killing the boss - to me it’s by far the most fun mechanic I’ve ever seen in either POE game.

This isn’t like original harvest where it was just something you could abuse to get overpowered.

Low key, I almost wish they could split off the original temple into its own game because I would play the hell out of that. It’s just fun and well-conceived from start to finish, and I’m going to miss them ruining it for the sake of not hurting the main game.

Anyone else feel similarly?
