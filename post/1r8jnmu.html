With Kyrie out, how Much of Their Careers Have Kyrie, Kawhi, Simmons, AD, and Zion Actually Missed?

There’s always debate from nba fasn about “injury prone” vs. “load management” vs. “unlucky.” I only looked at regular season games (no playoffs), and counted total possible games since entering the league.

TLDR

• Kyrie and Kawhi have missed about the same share of their careers (~1⁄3).
• AD has missed a lot but way less than Kyrie/Kawhi in percentage terms (you don’t hear plainclothes kyrie)
• Simmons and Zion are in a different tier of no-show entirely.
• Zion’s number is honestly kind of wild

EDIT (with the older / retired players added):

  • Rose has played 700+ games and it still feels like we lost half his career.
  • Oden’s number is honestly almost unfathomable.

Kyrie Irving

  • Games played: ~779
  • Possible games: ~1,148
  • Missed: ~369
  • Unavailable: ~32% of career

Kawhi Leonard

  • Games played: ~774
  • Possible games: ~1,148
  • Missed: ~374
  • Unavailable: ~33% of career (And that includes a full missed season.)

Ben Simmons

  • Games played: ~332
  • Possible games: ~656
  • Missed: ~324
  • Unavailable: ~49% of career

nearly HALF of his career regular-season games!!

Anthony Davis

  • Games played: ~777
  • Possible games: ~1,000+ (approx depending on season counting)
  • Missed: ~176
  • Unavailable: ~23% of career

About 1 in 4 games ngl interestingly lower than the public perception.

Zion Williamson

  • Games played: ~214
  • Possible games: ~492
  • Missed: ~278
  • Unavailable: ~56% of career

Has played in less than half of his potential regular season games. For how young he is this is honestly insane.

Joel Embiid

  • Career games played: ~433
  • Seasons active: 2014–15 through 2024–25 (11 seasons)
  • Possible games: 902
  • Missed: ~469
  • Unavailable: ~52%

Has missed over half of his possible career games. Still has an MVP. Pretty wild contrast.

Kristaps Porzingis

  • Career games played: ~450
  • Seasons active: 2015–16 through 2024–25 (10 seasons)
  • Possible games: 820
  • Missed: ~370
  • Unavailable: ~45%

Has missed almost half his career. Feel like not really talked about.

And for good measure some historic injured players who famously remember for their injuries to compare to:

Greg Oden

  • Career games played: 105
  • Seasons active: 2007–08 through 2013–14 (7 seasons)
  • Possible games: 7 × 82 = 574
  • Missed: 469
  • Unavailable: ~82% of career

One of the most extreme injury cases ever for a #1 pick.

Derrick Rose

  • Career games played: ~723
  • Seasons active: 2008–09 through 2024–25 (17 seasons)
  • Possible games: 17 × 82 = 1,394
  • Missed: ~671
  • Unavailable: ~48% of career

Missed nearly half his career (2012 ACL tear completely changed everything)

Brandon Roy

  • Career games played: 326
  • Seasons active: 2006–07 through 2012–13 (7 seasons)
  • Possible games: 574
  • Missed: 248
  • Unavailable: ~43%

Smh what could have been
