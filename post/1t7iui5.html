Keep the J’s together

I personally think it would be ridiculous to break the J’s up. We have two of the best wing players in the NBA on the same team that play well together and typically have huge success with each other. They both are number one options and have grown together since being drafted in back to back years.

I’m sorry but Giannis is NOT the answer and wouldn’t

Be the move. We had a solid roster this season and we are just missing a couple pieces most of all additional bench scoring spark plug players that make instant impact and size would probably be the biggest area that needs to be addressed and we don’t need to get a marquee name Center to do it.

I think a player like Kelel Ware from the Heat, Jaylin Williams from OKC, Walker Kessler from Utah, or maybe even Isaiah Jackson or Jay Huff would be extremely impactful and addressed the lack of size that cost us the series against the Sixers. They thought that Vucevic would be the answer and it was overly apparent that he cannot play defense against scoring bigs. That’s what Al Horford and Kornet did for us SO WELL that nobody really mentioned about after they were gone.

Anyway, that’s just my personal take on the matter
