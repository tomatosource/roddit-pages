Why are NBA fans/media so invested in all time rankings? Genuine question no disrespect intended.

I’m primarily a hockey and soccer fan, but I’ve been recently getting into watching basketball more. I’ve noticed that within the basketball fandom, a far larger amount of discussion seems to be dedicated to how players rank relative to each other. I’m not saying hockey or soccer fans don’t compare players (arguing who is better is a time honored tradition of sports fans everywhere), but here it feels like you could ask any reasonably invested fan to rattle off the top 15 players of all time and they’d be able to do it right away, and have very strong opinions about the rankings of each. It feels like any post on here with enough comments someone will be arguing with someone else about who is rated higher than who on an all time list. Even in general phrases like “top 10 peak oat” or “top 5 midrange shooter oat” seem very common compared to how often I see them in other sports discussion. Even the media seems to get in on doing rankings a quite a bit more than equivalent hockey media. Not saying that this is a bad thing, tbh I kinda enjoy the discussions (even though I really don’t know enough to have a real opinion), just thought it was an interesting cultural difference. Also obviously this is all anecdotal, so I may be way off base.
Anyways, have any of y’all seen the same difference? Any thoughts as to why it might be the case?
