Tyrese Haliburton calls Kingston Flemings ‘unbelievable’ after NCAA tournament game

Here’s a fascinating one for the Pacers. In an interview with For The Win, Tyrese Haliburton was asked which guards in this year’s tournament he’s been watching most closely. He singled out Houston’s Kingston Flemings without hesitation.

“I really like Kingston Flemings. I’m a big fan of his. I know that he’s going to get drafted high, as he should, because I think he’s unbelievable.”

As a reminder: Indiana holds its own pick this year but loses it if it falls between 5 and 9 — right where Flemings is widely projected to land.

I’d assume Indiana would pick Dybantsa, Peterson or Boozer if they end up 1-3. But if the Pacers end up at 4, though? Probably Caleb Wilson but still I wanted to share the conversation.
