NBA Media is catching on that Cs are a Super Team and spoiler: They don’t like it

The last thing the NBA media wants is a Boston NBA dynasty. Boston is a boring team to cover. The players are all stand up guys, most of them are husbands and/or fathers. It’s like having a team filled with Kawhi Leonards.

Brad Stevens put together a super team and he did it pretty organically albeit with the help of the Bucs.

Boston has always been kind of a media punching bag because they were really good but couldn’t get over the hump. And where is sports media located? LA, NY, Miami, and even a lot from PA… Hmm.. what city do these cities hate most?

It’s no surprise that we were going to get venom, but what is surprising, hilarious, and even disgusting is that this is coming from the same people who said that Boston was not a great team and only made it to the finals because of injuries. The same people that said Boston would be defeated by the Mavs in 5 because we weren’t used to real playoff basketball and the juggernaut West. The same people who spent all offseason talking up the Knicks only to have the Knicks blown out by the Celtics… they’re only now realizing what the Celtics are… A super team even without Porzingis, but with Porzingis… stop it…

These same people are now saying the league needs to change the rules to protect the product… because they don’t want to cover Boston winning multiple championships as people tune out of their drama focused coverage of Lebron playing with his son or Ja Morant getting in trouble. No one cares about these storylines with the Celtics blowing teams out.
