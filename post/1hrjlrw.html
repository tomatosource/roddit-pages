Biggest “free throw merchants” of the season so far

As SGA rises in the MVP voting, I wanted to see if he was really relying on FTs as much as people say he is (no shade, but he literally pops up when you google free throw merchant lol). I copied the stat totals of the top 100 scorers of the season so far into Excel to find these percentages.

Here are the players, of the top 100 scorers this season, who are scoring the largest percentage of their points from the line:

  1. James Harden - 29.96% of his points this season are from FTs
  2. Trae Young - 26.5%
  3. Devin Booker - 26.2%
  4. Ja Morant - 25.71%
  5. Damian Lillard - 25.17%
  6. Anthony Davis - 24.60%
  7. Deni Avdija - 24.48%
  8. Cam Thomas - 24.04%
  9. Lauri Markkanen - 23.33%
  10. Bennedict Mathurin - 23.08%

Notable players just outside the top 10 were Julius Randle, DeMar DeRozan, SGA, and Jalen Brunson, who took spots 11-14, respectively. Of the top 100 scorers so far this season, 20% of them score at least 20% of their points from the FT line.

It’s also important to note that Joel Embiid was not on the list I used; he is not in the top 100 scorers due to his injuries. If he was on the list he’d be in first place, as 31.4% of his 261 points scored so far this season have been from the line.
