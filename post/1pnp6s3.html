Disagreement with the class fantasy

I’m not sure if I’m the only one or if I have an unpopular opinion.

I’m playing the druid in wolf form, and beyond the combos, mechanics, and interactions, it feels like I’m playing a melee mage or sorcerer. Almost all of the werewolf’s abilities are ice-based; its main attack is a sweeping freeze. Its entire mechanic revolves around that element.

Something similar happens to me with the other classes. Although the warrior could be considered 50% physical and 50% fire damage, in most cases the class’s fantasy feel is “another way to do magic, but with a bow or a mace.”

While I did expect some interaction with the elements with the druid—it’s ultimately an intelligence and strength class—I thought the werewolf might be more associated with bleeding or even poison (rage). A more “physical” aspect, the strength side.

I want to clarify that this isn’t a complaint or anything like that regarding its gameplay mechanics, interactions, or combos. In that sense, I think it’s fun, but I believe there could be great potential in creating diversity in the classes while maintaining the “more traditional” class fantasy archetypes. Almost everything revolves around magic and the elements, but instead of a staff, it’s a mace, spear, or bow. I think the druid, being str/int, makes a lot of sense with its magic focus, but it seems to be 100% of the class. I only briefly tried the bear form, but it seems like more of the same, but with fire.

Anyway, I really enjoy the game and appreciate the variety of existing builds; I’m only referring to the “visual” aspect, or rather, the class fantasy, where they all seem to be elementalists or mages with bows, spears, maces, and crossbows.
