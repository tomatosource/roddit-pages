Khris Middleton: the most underrated star of the late 2010s early 2020s.

hello, my case is simple. Khris Middleton was one of those careers I always really like where he’s just a traveling nobody for a WHILE, and then he just finds himself on the Bucks and he’s an all-star level player and incredible second option to Giannis.
