Denver Nuggets HC Michael Malone on the Minnesota Timberwolves: “I think they’ve approached this game like it’s a rivalry, and I think we’ve approached these games like it’s just another game sometimes.”

Ahead of tomorrow’s WCSF rematch, Malone is implying the Timberwolves play harder against the Nuggets, while they see them as just another team.

Minnesota has beaten Denver in their previous three games this season by an average of over 17 points.
