Aaron Boone is on pace to be the ejection GOAT.

Bobby Cox, rest his soul, currently holds the record for most lifetime ejections - 162 as a manager and 1 as a player for a total of 163. He was ejected from 3.593% of his managed games. And record or not, his reputation precedes him. Even with plenty of professional success, he’ll always be remembered fondly as the guy who threw himself in front of every argument and got fined so often that he started paying a retainer.

We all know Aaron Boone as the biggest hothead manager currently in the game, but I don’t see much talk about how he’s actually on pace to beat Bobby. He’s at 48 ejections in 1245 managed games - a rate of 3.855%. Plus two as a player to Cox’s one.

What’s more is that Boone is doing this is an era where it should be harder to be ejected. Replays and challenges should be ending most arguments before they begin, but the guy still finds things to argue about. Even with ABS, he’s right on pace - 2 ejections in the 51 games so far this year.

He’s been ejected for doing absolutely nothing. He was ejected in his MLB debut.

The only thing holding him back is age. He started managing at 45 compared to Cox’s 37, and he’d have to go until 71 to break the record at his current pace. But that’s not unheard of. Cox himself retired at 69. Dusty Baker just retired at 74 and Bruce Bochy at 70.

He’s got 18 seasons to go, but it really doesn’t seem like the Yankees are going to get rid of him anytime soon. They’ve already amassed the longest championship drought in team history, so if they haven’t fired him by now I have to assume that he’s never going to get fired so long as they keep making the postseason.

And even if he does get bounced from the Yankees… if he gets a manager gig with a less successful team I have to imagine that’s even more opportunity to get ejected.
