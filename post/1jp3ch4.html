There is no solution for partial skill damage conversion

Currently elemental attack skills convert 20-80% of damage to an element. There is no way to convert the full amount. This is amplified even worse for skills that attack fast and deal low damage. If you have something like rapid shot that converts 60% and deals low base damage fast, the rest 40% is basically just ignored by the monster armor and you deal 40% less damage using the skill.

Only option currently available is avatar of fire which is on the other side of the tree to not play a scuffed skill.

I hope we will get more conversion items/nodes in the new patch.
