As a casual who is at end game, thanks GGG. I am having a great time and I am excited to see the progression of the game.

In the midst of all the *ahem*, complaints, by people who never had to drop trade annis in D2 /s, I just want to say I am loving the game. Currently get 1-3 hours in a day on average during the week. I am level 88 rn, and I have been running T15 maps. I have made a few alts to try out using lower level uniques to make life easier. I have bumbled my way through passives, skills, supports, maps, crafting and trade.

So far this has been the closest to feeling the old D2 style of Holy Grail runs (mostly playing solo, but selling the occasional good drop), and even the repetition of the maps isn’t really draining on me (at least no more than gaming takes on me after a few hours mentally at 36).

It’s EA, and sure ping is annoying, and a bunch of QOL features would be amazing to have. But honestly, this is probably one of the most stable EA’s I have played, with the most content to boot. I am just excited to start seeing content drizzle in as we approach a full release. I haven’t picked up an ARPG (even D2R I dropped after about 60 hours) in a long time, and I am fully enjoying my time with it.
