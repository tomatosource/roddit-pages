Is anyone straight up enjoying the dumpster fires our most hated rivals are becoming? Some glorious schadenfreude? Celtics won banner 18 last year. Lakers and Bucks are mid. Heat are a dumpster. Sixers are a dumpster fire.

  • Lakers continue to be a mid team while wasting AD’s prime and LeBron’s last few years. Lakers FO has been asscheeks since Kobe’s twilight; they took that solid 2020⁄2021 team and let all their role players go (for Westbrook lmfao…). But with LA’s lifestyle/Hollywood/weather advantage, every 5-10 years some disgruntled star forces their way to LA. Fuck the Lakers. Fuck LeBron.

  • Heat have disintegrated with Jimmy Butler’s bullshittery and Pat Riley staying put and not putting up with said bullshittery. Tbf, Pat never got Jimmy the help he needed the last few years whereas the Celtics got Jrue/KP, Bucks got Dame, Pacers got Siakam, Cavs got Mitchell, Knicks got Brunson/KAT/Bridges/OG. Hope Pat sends his ass to the Wizards or the Jazz. Fuck the Heat. Fuck Jimmy Butler.

  • Bucks are not that bad, but this iteration isn’t competing for a ring. Giannis is still amazing, and Dame is still good. But their team needs quite a few pieces to become true contenders. And if any of the rumors of Beal going to the Bucks are true ….. my goodness, they’ll be so awful with Beal. And Doc Rivers knows how to lose playoff games. Fuck the Bucks. Fuck Giannis.

  • Sixers ….. I almost feel a little bad. They’ve had some rough years with their roster underperforming in the playoffs (lmfao max contracts to Simmons and Tobias, while letting Butler go). Embiid is an incredibly dominant player when healthy, but he’s a mega playoff choker who gasses out at the end of series. And injury prone 7’ 300 lb dudes with constant leg injuries don’t magically get healthier …. And lmfao @ Paul George’s contract. Fuck the Sixers. Fuck Embiid.

  • Knicks made huge moves to make a team specifically to beat us, but I ain’t worried. Thibs is gonna have these guys all gassed out, and their defense is hot garbage. Brunson is great, but we can shut his ass down. KAT is great, but usually pretty shit come playoff time (albeit, he’s on a better spaced team now). Not worried. Celtics in 5. Fuck the Knicks.

  • Cavaliers are the only team I’m worried about. They’re extremely well coached and a really balanced team. Three elite All-Star calibre players in Mitchell, Mobley and Allen. And Mitchell especially wrecks us; he averages 30/5/5 vs Celtics in his career. But their playoff experience is still lacking, and I think Celtics will just outlast them. Celtics in 7.

  • The other teams in the East don’t worry me, except a healthy Magic and Hawks have a good shot to upset some teams. Fuck the Hawks especially. Nothing but love for the Magic; their team looks pretty good.

  • I’d talk shit about the Mavs, but they just have had a ton of bad luck with injuries. If they get healthy, they can absolutely make a deep playoff run again with that squad. Those guys are really well built and well coached, with a great 1-2 punch in Luka/Kyrie. As long as Kyrie doesn’t play the Celtics lmfao. Fuck Kyrie. Fuck Luka. Fuck Klay.

  • Loved slapping the Warriors by 40+ again this season. Let’s make that a yearly tradition. Warriors are mid af; team is dogwater around Steph. Fuck Kerr. Fuck Draymond. Steph is cool though; good dude.

I gotta say, I feel like we just won twice.
