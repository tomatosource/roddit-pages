Shower Thought: What If Delve Gets Added to Abyss?

I really like how remnants got added to expedition instead of being their own league mechanic.

That got me thinking: what if we get fewer but overall deeper league mechanics over time? For example, instead of making Delve its own league, the depth mechanic gets added to Abyss? Like, Abyssal Depths go deeper and deeper and every floor down, you fight harder monsters, drop better loot, and the Vessel of Kulemak appears with more and more mods?

And what if we eventually see items, skills, and support gems from Abyssals, the Eldritch (breach), etc. like we have Kalguuran items, skills, and support gems today?

I think really fleshing out each enemy faction and adding more to each league mechanic could be a cool way to have our cake and eat it too: more end-game content without more atlas bloat.
