Members of the Bill Simmons’ “42 Club” in the First Quarter of the 2024-25 NBA Season: Jokic, Giannis, Luka, Tatum and LaMelo Ball

BS theorized the “42 Club”, that if an NBA player’s Points + Rebounds + Assists per game is equal to or more than 42, the player gets into the club, because he is having a magnificent season. I know he used it more for a playoff run. But in this exercise, we will use it for the regular season.

I thought 20/5/5 is a sexy stat and that’s a pretty good average so I will also include a 30 club. And somewhere between the good and the truly elite, we will also include a 36 club for the better, great players today.

&nbsp;

He doesn’t make the 30 club but I included Evan Mobley in the list as he missed it only by the thinnest of margins. He was pretty much a dunk or a layup away from qualifying. Another two who narrowly missed the cut were Tyrese Haliburton and Darius Garland who both scored 29.4. Below, I also added a list of players who would have made it had they played enough games. 15 is the number of minimum games played to qualify.
