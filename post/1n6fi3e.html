A Rather Nasty Tech For Bleed Builds.

Everyone is quickly learning about the super broken Bloodgulp and Fleshpierce tameable beasts in the interludes whom give powerful auras.

Bloodgulp in particular gives a 40% Phys aura that STACKS with other phys auras. So double Phys Bloodgulp is 80% Global Physical damage.

You can than slot the new Lineage support gem Brutus’s Brain to make it deal no damage but become immortal. Thus just basically turning it into a PoE 1 style super aura.

HOWEVER it still attacks even tho it cant deal damage, and those attacks can still proc. Thus you can slot in cheeky gems like oh say… Incision and Rapid Attacks 3.

Thus making it so you can get an immortal pet that offers you insane buffs on top of a quickly stacking free More Bleed Magnitude multiplier.

The result is hilariously broken.

Honestly its so broken I recommend taking the haste aura from Fleshpierce because you simply don’t need all that damage.

Happy Hunting exiles.
