My First Spellweaver Build - Not a CoC/CoA Build

First, I’m no PoE2 expert by any means, I just do my own thing and see how far I can make it. I don’t follow guides and I didn’t want to do the meta CoC/CoA one shot screen clear builds I always see so this is what I came up with. I use an Aura Double Curse with Spell Totem and a ton of other spells because I think it’s fun to have variety and all the spells are pretty cool. For whatever reason, Poe Ninja doesn’t show the Spell Totem so the damage numbers are way off but I can clear most of the content pretty easily. My temple is semi juiced and I’ve cleared several of the T3 bosses (just not an instant kill like Cast On builds). I’m sure my build could be better as far as the passive tree goes and gear/support gems but it’s fun for me and working so far. Feel free to offer any constructive feedback on mechanics I might be overlooking or missing out on, because like I said, I’m no expert.
