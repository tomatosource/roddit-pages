Player collision with other players needs to go

I don’t think anyone at ggg plays this game coop or even bothers testing it hence the number of broken things every season with coop - e.g. this league the temples seem to break randomly with no one able to interact with it in coop- but the collision aspect is the worst part. I challenge anyone here to play the game and level from 1 with 4-6 friends and tell me its not a total nightmare. Every act and nearly every zone is full of tiny narrow hallways that dont allow you to dodge anywhere but backwards or forwards, which completely falls apart when playing with other players.

Playing with 2 people, while still annoying, is at least tolerable in that you can generally have one player play somewhat melee and the other ranged, but you constantly bump into each other and get movement locked otherwise! I have no idea how anyone could play with bigger coop and not feel its a total nightmare from ggg. Which comes down to, why even support it? It feels awful to play and if you dont want to fix it then why pretend its a 6 player game? Cant revive in maps, league functions only for one player (if at all), collision nightmares making it hard for multiple people to even fight in most areas, I mean who is this for? The collision for players into players needs to be outright removed and admit that collision was a failed experiment in terms of coop play.

Note I dont mind collision vs monsters.
