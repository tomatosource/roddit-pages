A “Decent” Center Isn’t Enough

If you spend enough time going through this subreddit you’ll see the biggest concern for this offseason is getting a big man, suggestions like Wendell Carter Jr and other players of the sort.

Now if your following the western conference finals you’ll also know that this isn’t anywhere near enough. Wemby just had a 41 and 24 game and its only his 3rd season. Asides from him look at our competitors in the east; Duren, KAT, Mobley, Bam. Then in the west of course Jokic, Chet, Sengun.

Now that doesn’t mean that we have to make it happen this offseason, but for the long term future, if we want to have a shot at winning the championship, were gonna need a damn good big man because who we face in the finals will almost certainly have an elite big man.
