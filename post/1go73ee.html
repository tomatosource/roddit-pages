How much do you think Trey Murphy’s impending return will help the New Orleans Pelicans while Zion is out?

We all saw the news yesterday. Zion, Out. Murphy, In. Murphy set to return tomorrow and play his first game of the season for the Pels after suffering a hamstring strain in training camp, against the Nets.

Zion suffered a right hamstring strain of his own. Same leg as the playoffs and he has suffered right hamstring strains pretty consistently over the years. Considered week to week.

I think Murphy is a starter in this league and a fantastic role player, a true two way guy, who also has a little bit of creation game in him nowadays too, but of course you can’t expect the world from him right away. But his mere presence and defensive acumen and versatility I think will make a massive difference for the Pels as he gets his rhythm back, in these next few weeks without five of their top players.

I think Murphy+Ingram with their current role player crop is maybe enough to scrap a few wins, like tomorrow against the Nets, before hopefully Hawkins and McCollum return in the week after this upcoming one. They’ll have to do it on the defensive end, but Murphy is obviously someone who can aid with that, and he can shoot. CJ was expected to be re-evaluated after two weeks and we’re approaching that date. Hawkins a few days ago reported to be out 1-2 weeks with a back strain.

What do you all think? How much do you think TM3’s return helps the Pels in these next several games?
