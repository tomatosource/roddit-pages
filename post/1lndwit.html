Every potential pick the Pelicans traded on draft night featured a team who’s starting point guard tore their Achilles this past season

This isn’t really meant to be another “shit on the Pelicans” post, but rather an interesting observation that I haven’t really seen mentioned.

During the first round of the 2025 NBA draft, the Pelicans traded pick 23 and their own (unprotected) 26 first with Milwaukee swap rights to the Hawks to move up to 13. The 23 pick was acquired from the Pacers shortly before Haliburton tore his Achilles.

I just thought it was interesting that the three teams who’s picks are involved in this deal all have a starting PG that tore their Achilles this past season:

Pelicans - DeJounte Murray Bucks - Damian Lillard Pacers - Tyrese Haliburton

I know that makes the trade even dumber— trust me I know. But pretty crazy coincidence nonetheless. DJM could return shortly after the New Year, while it’s unlikely we see minutes from Lillard and Haliburton this upcoming season.

Edit: to add, the Pelicans acquired the Pacers 27 first from the Raptors in the Brandon Ingram trade. The Pelicans anticipated they’d want to move up for Queen in this draft, so prior to game 7, they made a deal with the Pacers where they swapped Indy’s 27 first for their first this year: pick 23. While the trade with the Hawks was objectively awful, it’s really hard to fault the FO for this one (as much as I want to shit on them even more than I already have), as the Pacers were looking like they’d be an easy top 3 seed next year coming off of this finals appearance. Save the “Haliburton had a calf strain and the Pels should have known something catastrophic could happen” for somebody else. No one has a crystal ball.
