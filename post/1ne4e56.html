My experience with spells in campaign

After reading some of the criticisms of the new elemental infusion changes I thought I would roll a Bloodmage and see for myself what the changes felt like.

Long story short I can’t fathom how these changes made it through to the patch.

I tried to use the ice spells, so projected ice novas into snap etc and for the life of me I could not make it work. DPS tooltip at level 50 for ice nova was around 1200 (essentially zDPS) and whilst crits could occasionally one shot white mobs the general gameplay was just spam nova into groups until freeze, snap, run and pick up an infusion and repeat. Snap felt atrocious. 4 second cool down essentially guarantees inconsistent damage between mobs and a frustrating gameplay loop.

I laughed the first time I used frost darts on a frozen white mob and its health bar barely even moved.

Now I’ve seen the videos of the frostbolt BM and it looks awesome, but to get to that level of power you ideally need at least 2 uniques, so power is locked until endgame essentially.

I also tried fireball (again, I know it’s a meta endgame build) but I find something so unsatisfying with the fireball in this game. There’s a scale with fireballs in videogames, imo, ranging from super Mario’s fire bubbles to Elden Rings giantsflame. Somehow PoE2 fireball feels worse than Mario’s. Where is the power fantasy? Let me throw huge fireballs which explode into packs!

So after all of that I switched to bone cage and storm. For the same gear I was using for my ice spells my tooltip went up 2k DPS and this was with a +2 ice spell wand so totally useless for phys. All white mobs were a one shot, bonestorm tooltip was 9k. No longer need to deal with running around after infusions on the floor. Improved gear and now tooltip is 6k on cage - absolute night and day difference in gameplay.

I refuse to believe that GGG did a balance pass of the spells. Unless I’m missing something a good 80% of sorc spells are borderline useless, at least in the campaign.

Am I missing something here, or was everyone just as disappointed with spells in the campaign?
