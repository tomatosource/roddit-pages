LeBron’s 15th Best Season

Inspired by this video about Hank Aaron: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_3aAw5iY4k&embeds_referring_euri=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.reddit.com%2F&embeds_referring_origin=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.reddit.com&source_ve_path=MjM4NTE I went looking for LeBron James’s 15th best season. (Not an actual season, but a combination of his 15th best season statelines)

The result? 15th best season LeBron has:

26.4 PPG (2017)

7.4 RPG (2005)

6.8 APG (2023)

1.3 SPG (2019)

0.6 BPG (2019)

.493 FG% (2020)

.335 3P% (2006)

.731 FT% (2018)

25.6 PER (2019)

70 Games played (2025)

A 26.4⁄7.4/.6.8 line will have you competing for MVPs plenty of years. Jokic was the only player this year with more in each category (Giannis came close). PER isn’t the best stat, but it would have 15th best season LeBron as the 5th best player this past year, behind Jokic, SGA, Giannis, and surprisingly Anthony Davis. Perhaps the most impressive is the 70 games played, which LeBron just did this year at age 40.
