This game should do something about all the micromanaging

This league has been one of my most played, and I enjoyed Druid a lot. It probably has been my first league where I spent a lot of time in the endgame and overall had a blast. Having said that, one of the bigger problems I have is all the micro-management required. I am talking about things like atlas nodes, which are mandatory if you don’t want to miss out on things For instance Local Knowledge. It just has to go. I hate having to remap every time I see a Water/Swamp biome or potentially miss out on a lot of currency drops. Basically every guide I looked at makes it a point to have this node for certain biomes. I can’t tell how many times I ran juiced maps where I forgot to spec in or spec out of local knowledge.

The other example is the Ritual. Basically if you want to properly farm you have to remap nodes that focuses on deferrals to hunt for omens and then next run remap to spawn more monsters to buy deffered items. I think the choices should affect what type of omens you get. If 90% of guides have always same nodes selected, then design should be revised.

As someone who plays on ps5, I am already at big disadvantage when it comes to price checks or to do anything in general takes many times longer than on pc. Especially when it comes finding tablets with relevant mods. It is just such an exhausting experience. I think the whole mods system should be simplified and just have labels with categories and tiers. If they made a stash where you could filter tablets based on a combination of your desired mods I would throw money at the screen. I even saw some streamers use ReGex to filter out desired mods. I mean, come on… This can’t be intended experience.

I know people will say you don’t have to do any of it and they would be right, but the problem is that you are missing out on so many rewards that it takes all fun out of it if you don’t do it. I can run 100 maps fully modded and get less rewards than someone with one, surgical precision juiced map. If you want to get anything decent you have to do so much prep work before you can play the game. And I agree you should invest in prepwork to get rewards, but I wish it was made a bit easier.

Just my 2 cents. The game is great and I can’t way to see what’s next.
