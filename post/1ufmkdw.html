Now that the final piece of Paul George Clippers trade draft capital has been utilized, here’s everything the deal has become.

In exchange for Paul George, the Oklahoma City Thunder received…

  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander:
    • 2x MVP, 1X NBA Champion, 4x NBA All-Star, 4x All-NBA 1st Team
  • Danilo Gallinari
    • Sign and traded to the Hawks for a 2025 1st; that pick was used to select Brooks Barnhizer
  • Heat 2021 first-round pick
    • Used to select Tre Mann 18th overall who was traded for Gordon Hayward. Oops.
  • Clippers 2022 first-round pick
    • Conveyed at 12th overall & was used to select future All-NBA and future NBA champion #2 option Jalen Williams
  • Clippers 2023 first-round pick swap
    • Did not convey due to the Clippers having a better record
  • Heat 2023 first-round pick (Lottery-protected)
    • Heat eventually made a trade to reacquire this pick & used it on Jaime Jacquez Jr.; the Thunder received a 2025 lottery-protected Heat 1st
    • Conveyed 15th overall in 2025 & was used to draft Thomas Sorber
  • Clippers 2024 first-round pick:
    • Traded along with a Pistons 1st & Wizards”1st” to select Ousmane Dieng 12th overall
      • Dieng would be moved with a 2029 second for Mason Plumlee (Waived) & eventually found his way on to the Bucks as a pending RFA
  • Clippers 2025 first-round pick swap
    • Conveyed, the swap moved the Thunder from 30th to 24th*
  • Clippers 2026 first-round pick
    • Conveyed 12th overall & was used to select Aday Mara

TL;DR - Paul George was turned into SGA, Jalen Williams, Thomas Sorber, Aday Mara & Brooks Barnhizer.

I didn’t see a post discussing this but I thought it was interesting now that “The worst trade of all time™” is fully completed.

Sort of.

There is technically one more piece in this deal that’s yet to be turned into a player. That 2025 1st round swap? After the Thunder moved up as a result, they moved the 24th pick in the draft to the Sacramento Kings for a 2027 Spurs 1st; the Kings used that pick to select Nique Clifford in the 2025 NBA Draft.
