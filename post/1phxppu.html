The one mechanic which leads to both one button builds, +gem level problems, and others.

There is a core game design problem which is leading to many of the current complaints about the game. Skill numerical scaling is either linear or exponential. This leads to A)things which stack a single statistic like +gem level or str stacking being incredibly powerful ways to scale damage and B) Along with the point above it being better to focus on multiplicative scaling a single skill rather than multiple skill layering or “combos”. C) in non hardcore it also leads to maximizing DPS and clear speed over all other mechanics due to the lack of needing defenses or time to deal that damage. D) it exacerbates currency scaling as the league progresses due to limiting the only way to add damage to the best builds by increasing the top end of tier 1 numbers on items with the exact same affixes. Rather than having different affix combos be better for different multi skill builds.

Skills with a scale curve which initially increases but the falls off with diminishing returns encourages builds to layer skills in order to increase their damage. I use the term layering instead of combo because combo implies that you use one skill in order to buff another skill in order to buff a third skill etc. But skill layering is more like using 1 skill to deal damage simultaneously with another skill. Some of the druid skills are more akin to layering, putting down a large AoE DOT and then entering it to deal melee damage (assuming that they can deal similar damage numerically). Vs combing using a skill to deal very little damage and gain charges, then spending those charges to deal massive damage.

Layering then requires damage to be dealt through various delivery methods, passive, periodic, active, situational etc. which the game currently has limited options for. If damage scaling has an initial increase and then falls off in more of a bell curve you CAN still try to stack stats into a single ability it just doesn’t become the default way of increasing damage. Some builds can find success by “layering” different damage sources near the peak of their damage curves with build synergies.
