Today is the 20th anniversary of the Malice at the Palace (Pacers/Pistons brawl)

On 19 November 2004, the Indiana Pacers were playing the Detroit Pistons in Detroit in their first meeting since a contentious playoff series in May of the same year. With less than a minute left in the game and the Pacers on their way to a comfortable win, Ron Arttest fouled Ben Wallace on a layup attempt. Wallace shoved Artest, causing most of both rosters to get tangled up in an effort to separate Artest and Wallace. Artest removed himself from the confrontation by lying on the scorer’s table. While he was lying there, a Pistons fan threw a drink at him. Artest charged into the stands and attacked the wrong fan. All hell broke loose.
