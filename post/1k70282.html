Everything I know about Aftershock

Hello Exiles!

I’ve started playing warriors this league, and last night, I’ve done some labbing to truly figure out the best way to optimize my heavy stun punish skills (hammer of the god/sunder). It is then when I’ve truly understood how good aftershock can be, so here’s everything I’ve found out for my fellow slam enjoyers!

What is aftershock? The best way to explain it is that it is basically warrior’s version of crit. Aftershock is a follow-up slam that does the same amount of damage as your original slam. This is effectively a 200% damage when it hits, just like crit in most traditional games. This is especially great for warriors who use resolute technique, since this is the most efficient way to do the big boy damage we’ve always wanted. Let me explain.

In our passive tree, there are only 2 nodes that grants BASE aftershock chance, Split the Earth and Mace aftershock chance. Those who are familiar with how good base crit chance increases are will understand how huge of a boost this is, since any support gems we add later can MULTIPLICATIVELY add onto this chance. With 4 skill point investment, you can get +24% damage boost and 18% aftershock chance!

So, what support gem options do we have to capitalize on this “meager” 18% chance? The most notable ones are Reverberate and Aftershock. Aftershock provides an additive 25% chance, which is basically a 25% damage boost. Using Aftershock alone raises your aftershock chance to 43%, that’s almost half success-rate!

Reverberate is where it gets really interesting. Reverberate multiplicatively increases your aftershock chance based on skill duration. Since Hammer of the God and Sunder are long-lasting skills, they increase the aftershock chance quite significantly. Using Sunder, aftershock and reverberate, I was able to achieve a 95% chance aftershock! That is almost guaranteed!

https://preview.redd.it/63d4e4r4wtwe1.png?width=664&format=png&auto=webp&s=487ec7cfd55d375b09043f7e4d6b70b0a52b2f62

I currently favor Sunder for 2 reasons:

- The skill duration is “longer”, so it yields a higher aftershock chance than HoTG, which is 82% vs our 95%. Despite the longer duration, sunder hitbox comes out much sooner, so you’re actually less likely to miss your skill.

- The skill doesn’t require you to “last hit” heavy stun like Hammer of the God does. This means you don’t have to worry about getting the last hit when you’re fighting pinnacles with your friends! You can use ancestral totems with their own sunder and let them carry your boss fight damage then you swoop in for the finishing blow with your stronger sunder!

My other support gems on sunder are fist of war, rageforged, hour-glass. These are all approximately 30-35% damage amp each, and total to almost 2.3x damage to Sunder! With the 95% after shock chance, we’re doing almost 4.6x (2.3 * 2) damage with Sunder. But we’re not done yet!

https://preview.redd.it/1t9ss9fbvtwe1.png?width=736&format=png&auto=webp&s=a6965ed256097162f06d640774023ceaa2a943cf

When the boss is heavy-stunned, seismic cry guarantees an aftershock, this is independent of the additional aftershock we make ourselves, so using seismic cry can give you a total of 2 aftershocks, which is basically whopping 6.9x (2.3 * 3) damage on heavy stunned enemies! I’ve done almost 11mil burst damage on pinnacles. There are other ways to continue adding damage, such as infernal cry.

This jump in damage is what finally allowed me to solo tier 4 breach boss as a warrior. Hope you found this informational and happy aftershocking!

Note: I don’t think tremor is good, the 35% base damage reduction is too big of a loss and makes our aftershocks less valuable. Also, the boss is going to move out of the way before you can land all 8 aftershocks.
