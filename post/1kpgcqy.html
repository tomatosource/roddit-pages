Spurs style rebuild?

In the 95-96 season, the spurs had 59-23 and were 4th overall and made it to the second round losing 4-2 to the Utah jazz. They entered the 96-97 season without their star when Robinson injured his back, and subsequently his foot, and chuck Pearson, their 3 point specialist, also out with a back injury…

Pop took over that season (presumably, to lose), and they finished with an abysmal 20-53, putting them at third to last (just above the grizzlies and us :/)

Anyway, they got the first pick, picked up tim duncan, and went on to win 5 more rings.

The spurs 100% hold the title for fastest rebuild period!

I think (and would love some opinionated pushback) that they’re going to try to do something similar…

  1. Go below the cap: Probably let go of holiday, let horford retire, maybe restructure porzingis to stay under the cap (because selling him will be expensive). Hopefully, keep white as bang for buck, I think he’s one of the best “deals” in the league.

1.b may be a little drastic… But trade brown.

  1. Develop younger players: start houser and Pritchard, maybe let kornett out for a bit

  2. Lose a lot

  3. Get up a high pick

Then 2027: Tatum comes back healthy, KP hopefully back to 100%, a new high pick, and hopefully a much more manageable salaried squad to win for a few years.
