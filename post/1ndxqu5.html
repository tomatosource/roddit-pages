Having played POE2/POE1 since launch, I feel like these are the 3 most important things that could be improved on

1. Endgame Play

Right now, the campaign is fun but only lasts ~20 hours, after which the gameplay changes. You’ll be pushed into making a build that can clear as fast as possible. If your goal is to push the higher maps you’ll be optimizing as much as possible which leads to using the strongest skill in the meta. You can use the not as strong skills, but the gap between them is far too high at the moment.

2. Skill Balance

At the moment, most classes gravitate toward one or two viable “clear-speed” skills, which makes the game feel repetitive. Suggestions:

  • Make more skills viable for endgame by tuning damage, AoE, and resource costs so niche builds can survive map-clearing
  • Revisit support gem and passive synergies to help weaker skills scale better without power-creeping the strongest.

3. Gameplay Pacing

The game currently emphasizes screen-clearing speed and massive mob density, which can be overwhelming and even cause performance issues. A slower, more tactical pace might be more enjoyable:

  • Reduce mob density while increasing individual enemy threat, making fights feel deliberate rather than spammy.
  • Reward positioning and skill usage instead of raw clear speed.
  • Consider an optional “slower mode” or experimental league that emphasizes smaller packs with harder mobs, giving players a different style of ARPG experience.

Those are my thoughts, lmk what you guys think about 0.3. Overall great game cant wait to see where it goes from here! Thanks GGG
