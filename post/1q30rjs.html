The Azmerit Spirit mechanic.

I’d like to share the major issues I have with the Azmerit Spirit mechanic.

Why doesn’t the Azmerit Spirit attach to the player and then apply itself to the next rare enemy I encounter? You could even imagine an Atlas Tree node that allows Azmerit Spirits to stack on the player and be forced to stay with you until a boss, for example. That’s just a suggestion based on the problem I’m encountering, but in its current state, the system really doesn’t work well.

I just ran a map where I had an Azmerit Spirit near me and a rare enemy about one screen away (I could clearly see its minions). So I ran toward the Spirit to trigger it on that rare enemy.
NOPE. The game decided I had to run to the other side of the map, even though there was a valid rare enemy clearly within range. I then tried to lure the rare enemy toward the Spirit, but every time I got close, the Spirit moved even farther away. In the end, a stray poison arrow accidentally killed the rare enemy.

At that point, I simply gave up on that Azmerit Spirit because it was too annoying and a massive waste of time for a potential good drop. I honestly have zero motivation to interact with this mechanic when the system itself forces such a time loss.

WHY are Azmerit Spirits so frustrating to use?

I genuinely believe that since the very first day this mechanic was introduced, many players have complained about being forced to chase them around just to benefit from them. Can we realistically hope for a rework?
