Cross-platform MTX restrictions are ruining the fun of swapping between PC and couch gaming (PC & PS5)

Hey fellow Exiles,

I just wanted to share some frustrating issues I’ve hit recently when trying to enjoy Path of Exile across both PC and PlayStation (PS). I’m hoping this sparks some discussion or maybe even grabs GGG’s attention for some possible improvements.

Here’s what’s happening:

I linked my PC-created character to my PSN account, excited about switching smoothly between couch gaming and my usual PC setup. Unfortunately, I quickly ran into the reality of MTX and stash tabs being locked to the platform they were purchased on.

The main problems I’m dealing with:

  1. Can’t exit maps on PlayStation:
    • Every time I try to leave a map on PS, it tries sending me back to my PC-purchased hideout (Dreadnaught Hideout). Since that hideout isn’t available on PS due to MTX restrictions, I’m stuck.
    • The only workaround I’ve found is begging for an invite from someone in general chat to teleport out, which feels pretty awkward. And since you can’t warp directly to town from maps, it’s become a major gameplay roadblock.
  2. Sorting items is a massive hassle:
    • On PS, my purchased stash tabs are “remove-only.” This means I’m forced to stash items in the basic PS tabs (which don’t show up when I switch back to PC). So later, I have to log onto my PC, manually retrieve the items from the PS tabs, and reorganize everything.
    • Even worse, every time I switch back to PS, all my stash tab affinities and public trade prices reset completely. Each session starts with me spending about 10-15 minutes fixing everything all over again.

Support Response:

When I reached out to GGG Support, they simply redirected me to their existing post ( https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3586288 ) explaining the MTX restrictions, without addressing any of the actual gameplay problems this causes.

Why this matters to me:

I’ve invested a lot into this game over the years, buying early access packs and MTX frequently. The current system doesn’t just discourage cross-platform play—it actively makes it feel like a punishment. I bought EA twice, but also buying duplicate stash tabs on PlayStation wouldn’t solve the hassle of sorting items for trade primarily managed on PC.

Community thoughts?

Has anyone else experienced similar frustration with cross-platform MTX and stash issues? Do you have any tips or tricks to mitigate these issues, or do we just need GGG to reconsider their policy?

Thanks everyone—would love your thoughts!
