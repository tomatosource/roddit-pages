Feedback from my experience in 0.2

0.2 just isn’t as fun as 0.1. The recent loot update was a nice change but it didn’t fix the issues I am experiencing. I just feel like there is a huge lack of build diversity in this game. Every build is lightning spear or lightning based so it all looks the same. Most of the Cold skills don’t really feel that good to play especially when compared to the lightning skills.. There are almost no fire skills in this game. To make it even worse all the fire skills that are in the game suck and need huge buffs.

I also believe that some of the skills in this game should be buffed and nerfed during the leagues. Lightning Spear for example is so over tuned that it makes every other build feel worse than they actually are. Then there are skills that feel almost useless like incendiary shot which would love buff mid league. Or Even fangs of frost, Why do you need to parry to get the buff on fangs of frost? Parrying doesn’t even work after Act 1.

Another issue Im having is that there is no reason to fight pinnacle bosses. The loot they drop is just boring. Fighting pinnacles was fun when Ingenuity was good. But now it seems like there is no point in fighting bosses because most of their loot isn’t good.

The campaign should be a one and done every league. If I finish the campaign in 0.3 with one character, I should not have to replay the campaign with a new class if I don’t want to. I should be able to create a new character that starts at level 60 with all the skill points unassigned. This will allow for more experimentation with classes and builds and a lack of fatigue from playing through the campaign 2 times every new character.

Crafting still sucks.
