Jaylen would have been traded a year ago if Tatum didn’t get hurt

I was revisiting u/GoatmontWaters post from last offseason which accurately predicted the JB trade and the reasons for doing so. In that thread, u/Sagebeing made an interesting point. The Celtics had 3 possible options:

  1. Hope that the JB/JT combo (making 70% of the cap) is so good that the associated players on the remaining 30% doesn’t even matter
  2. JB/JT combo is great but not a juggernaut. Celtics have to nail the margins and maybe trade D White for assets to load up on cheap young talent that pushes the team over the top
  3. Trade JB and build around Tatum
  • *We are here*

As my post title suggests, I think Jaylen would have been traded last offseason if Jayson didn’t tear his achilles. Us losing to the Knicks was proof that Option #1 wasn’t true. The JB/JT combo alone was not enough to win a chip. I believe the Celtics would have pivoted to Option #3 after losing that series and have traded Jaylen Brown. However, JT tears his achilles and that put the front office in a very difficult spot.

As we now know, his recovery was fantastic and set a new precedent for those coming back from an achilles tear. But during last summer, the Celtics front office didn’t know if he would be the same player ever again. None of us did. They could not in good conscience trade away JB (Option #3) and risk Tatum coming back as a shell of himself. They let Jaylen take the reins for a full season to see if he had to capability of being the guy you build around in Option #3, therefore trading Jayson instead during this summer, but given JT’s miraculous recovery and Jaylen’s clear ceiling to his game, they were able to pivot back to Option #3 this summer.

Just thought this was an interesting wrinkle in the JB trade saga. I really think we would have seen this last summer if JT was healthy.
