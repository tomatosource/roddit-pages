My thoughts about infinite atlas hotfix

Main problem of infinite atlas is that more you play harder it becomes to maintain, you can’t easily save areas and just come back, at certain point it is just bunch of saved locations with unrelated names, it is hard especially on console where i play.
My solution would be changing every node into some sort of city. You enter node(city) and you fall into different UI where you have nodes and maps inside it. So basically you have maps in maps. It is just fastest way to fix main issue, of course GGG cooked some fantastic rework with deeper changes for us(i hope). Can’t wait for thursday.
