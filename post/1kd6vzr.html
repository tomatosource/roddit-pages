GGG could you please update us on plans for character defensive layers.

Now that the item drama is mostly fixed let’s talk about something in the core system that still feels unintended. At end game ES is still such a massively superior choice to other defense types. The core defense philosophy of POE should result in 3 different feelings for gameplay. ES for dipping into and out of combat. Evasion for dodging most damage but then reacting quickly when you do take a hit. And armour for a slow methodical ticking down of incoming damage vs your rate of recovery from all sources. Right now due to lack of additional defensive layers armor and evasion feel the same; Ignoring most small damage and then dying to a one shot.

Part of the reason act 1 feels so good is because the defensive layers feel different and result in player deaths feeling “deserved” (I didn’t mitigate ENOUGH damage so I ran out of potions and died, vs I just took a 10k hit that my defenses could do nothing about) what are your plans to bring the core defense of the game back into part of the fun for character building. How can we achieve 3 distinct defensive options which play differently, can be mixed and matched AND all feel viable at all stages of the game?
