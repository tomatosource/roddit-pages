Rui Hachimura vs the Portland Trail Blazers tonight: 28/4/1 on 10⁄15 FG (66.7%), 2⁄4 3PT (50%), 6⁄6 FT (100%)

Didn’t see a post for Rui tonight and I thought his efforts deserved highlighting. Outside of Luka and AR, Rui has been our most consistent third option. He is averaging a career-high 16.9 PPG on elite efficiency (59.3% FG, 45.5% 3PT). He has not shot below 50% from the field in any game this season, having played all 8 games for the Lakers. His three-point shooting has been elite, midrange has been absolute money, and his paint finishing has been excellent.

From the eye test, it seems like he has more of a green light to get to his favorite midrange spots, and it has paid huge dividends for us—this shot is one of our go-to bail-out shots and he has been automatic when he takes them. Tonight, he and Ayton exploited Portland’s drop coverage relentlessly by hitting jumper after jumper. On a night when playmaking was scarce, having him comfortably get to his spots and shoot high-percentage middies saved us.

He has some obvious flaws as a player: not able to defend down, not a great rebounder for his size, and has some moves that are too telegraphed. But he is currently one of the best scoring role players in the league and is a proven playoff performer. Some of us Lakers fans like to put him in funny trade scenarios, but judging from his play early in the season, I would love for him to stay on the team long-term.
