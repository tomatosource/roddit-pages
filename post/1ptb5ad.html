Michael Porter Jr’s last 17 games: 27.3 PPG, 7.2 RPG, 3.4 APG on 50/42/83 shooting, 64.5 TS%.

The nets are 8-9 in his last 17 games played. The cohort of Nets fans who want to tank are quite eager to trade him and prevent more winning.

For those who are not watching the nets, MPJ has been labelled as an empty calories scorer who’s only playing well due to a high usage rate. There is potentially evidence to the contrary.

On the season, MPJ has a -2.5 Net rating on-court, and a +8.4 On-off rating.

People have compared his increase in stats to Mikal Bridges when he was on the nets. Bridges had a 24 game stretch where he averaged 28PPG on about 61% TS.

Bridges had a +0.8 Net rating on court, and a -0.6 On-off rating. The nets team surrounding him was definitely a bit better than the current nets team.

What is his current value as a player? He can definitely be compared to guys like Markannen since he has such low on-ball time and has great off-ball awareness on offense.

Most of his scoring comes from DHO actions with Nic Claxton, or cutting backdoor when the defense overplays the DHO. Using this action, Claxton is averaging a career-high 4.6 assists in 23 games with MPJ. Jordi’s system is making great use of both players and boosting their value.

Another tidbit: He is one of the best rim finishers in the league, with a staggering 86.5% FG% from 0-3 feet.
