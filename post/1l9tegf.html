I feel our best path this summer is to dump Jrue/Sam, and actually extend KP. I broke down how it would play out in the description

So we basically need to dump 30 mill this summer to get under the second apron. Let’s get that out of the way and operate under the assumption that our working goal is to get under that number.

First order of business; extend KP for 3 years 15-20 mill a year, third year team option. This is essentially cost savings because that contract would kick in next summer and shave 10-15 mill off his current deal, if you trade him you likely have to take back salary in that deal; why not simply keep porzingis and just have his salary at a lower number? I decided to buy low on him here, he could very easily outplay this contract.

Next: trade Hauser before/at the draft while his contract is only 2 mill into a TPE for a higher draft pick, we would likely need to attach one or two of our own but regardless, our goal should be to use him and our picks to move up as far as we can and draft a real difference maker. I’ll give 3 different scenarios that achieve this.

Scenario 1: Hauser and pick 32 in exchange for pick 22 and a future 2nd from Atlanta

Scenario 2: Hauser and picks 28&32 in exchange for pick 14 from San Antonio

Scenario 3: Hauser and pick 32 in exchange for pick 20 from Miami

Each of those 3 trades moves us up in the draft and shaves Hauser’s 10 mill off our books. The next move is very simple; trade jrue somewhere that can take him and only send back 10 mill. This actually becomes simple if you do a 3 team deal and use a TPE to accept the outgoing player. I’ll give 3 scenarios for this too.

Scenario 1:

-Celtics acquire Cam Christie, Kobe brown, and pick 30 from the clippers.

-Clippers get Jrue and dalen terry into their TPE from Chicago

-Bulls get bogdan into their TPE, as well as eubanks and a 2031 2nd swap with Boston(just to make the trade work)

Scenario 2:

-Celtics acquire Keon Ellis and mo gueye

-hawks acquire Malik monk and valunciannis into their 2 TPEs

-kings acquire Jrue, pick 22 from hawks, and pick 28 from Boston

Scenario 3:

-Celtics acquire max Christie and pick 27 from the nets

-nets acquire PJ Washington and Caleb Martin into TPE and cap space from Dallas

-mavs acquire Jrue, Maxwell Lewis, and picks 28⁄32 from Boston

So each of these trades get us under the apron and I think work well for each team. The last step that sort of comes in the wrong order is to select using our draft pick(s)- names I like are coward/sorber/penda/raynaud/traore/kalkbrenner/fleming
