After drafting Tim Duncan in 1997, the Spurs didnt have a single lottery pick for over two decades, until Devin Vassell was selected 11th overall in 2020.

The San Antonio Spurs didnt have a lottery pick for 22 consecutive seasons from 1998 to 2019, the longest in NBA history. Spurs’ highest draft pick during this span was James Anderson ,selected at 20th in 2010. The Spurs made the playoffs in all those 22 seasons (tied for the longest playoff streak in NBA history) and won five championships, nearly a quarter of potential NBA titles in that period.

The Spurs also achieved atleast 50 wins in 18 consecutive seasons, the longest streak in NBA history. If not for the shortened 1998-99 season (which the spurs had 0.74 winning percentage, 60-win pace), the streak wouldve extended to 20.

The 2013-14 Spurs that beat Miami in the finals is tied for the second fewest lottery picks (1) on a championship-winning team. Three other Spurs title teams also rank among the top 10 of this list. In the final season of the Spurs’ playoff and no-lottery streaks, the 2018-19 Raptors won the title with zero lottery picks, led by Kawhi Leonard - who was traded from the Spurs the year prior.
