GGG needs to ask nvidia/amd for performance help at this point

Cyberpunk was an utter mess until cdpr went joint collaboration with nvidia, implemented bleeding edge stuff into dlss via pathtracing and optimization went through the roof.

You can load into the game within 5s of starting it and it will pop you into the city within another 2s. No stutter, no shader issues you can fast travel again and it will take a few seconds at best. Game runs butter smooth.

You change dlss setting in PoE2 from quality to balanced and the game implodes for the next 20s… This is not okay.

PoE2 needs the same treatment there is no way with the amount of planned content added, the amount of hotfixes and patches needed in the future to even address the performance of the game just by sheer ggg devs alone.

I can’t imagine how terrible the mapping feels on console let alone on lower end devices and how many players literally drop the game because of gamebreaking issues.

I don’t see how at the end of year this game will be running smooth without an intervention like this.
