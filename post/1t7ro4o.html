The Sixers are on the verge of becoming the first team in NBA history to come back from a 3–1 deficit and then lose 4 straight the next round.

In other words, Philly could be the first team to win 3 straight and then lose 4 straight in the playoffs (similar to losing a series after being up 3-0).

The 2015 Rockets came close, as they came back from 3–1 to beat the Clippers, then lost 4-1 to the Warriors in the WCF (losing the first 3 games).

But as of today, no team has ever:

  • Went down 1–3
  • Won the series 4–3
  • Then immediately lost the next series 0–4
