Looking for Unique-heavy builds

Hi fellow exiles! I’m looking for builds in POE2 that uses as many Unique items as possible. Preferably at least 5, including Charms. Bonus if they also interact with passive Keystones or Unique Jewels.

Firstly, I understand that Rares are much better than most Uniques in most cases. I also understand that the game is still very new, and there are hundreds of Uniques to be released (especially for unreleased weapons such as Flails).

That said, I really enjoy trying to make a build work using as many Uniques as possible. This also works for me because in POE1, I personally disliked crafting, especially Harvest-crafting. So farming currency and specific content to obtain Uniques became my preferred style of gameplay.
While other players theorycrafted using POB and craftofexile.com, I was browsing through every Unique item, thinking about how they can interact with each other to enable a build.

Some of the Unique-heavy buiilds I have enjoyed in POE1 over the years:

  • Spider Minions Guardian: Arakaali’s Fang + Aegis Aurora + Memory Vault + Shavronne’s Wrappings + Astramentis + Dream Fragments + Shaper’s Touch + Rainbowstride
  • Reverse KB Chieftain: The Three Dragons + Empire’s Grasp + Eye of Innocence + Quill Rain + Skirmish + Kaom’s Heart + Kaom’s Roots
    • Builds like this are only possible in POE (out of all ARPGs, IMO) and what I love about POE1.
    • I enjoyed this one immensely even though it didn’t do good DPS.

  • Flame Golem Necromancer: Clayshaper + Victario’s Charity + The Vertex + Skin of the Loyal + Presence of Chayula + Rainbowstride
  • Molten Strike Juggernaut: Grelwood Shank + Lioneye’s Remorse + Devoto’s Devotion + Loreweave + Xoph’s Blood + Tombfist
  • Wander Pathfinder: Piscator’s Vigil + The Tempest’s Binding + Queen of the Forest + Bisco’s Leash + Atziri’s Step + Bisco’s Collar
  • Freezing Pulse Hierophant: The Annihilating Light + Viridi’s Veil + The Pandemonius + Anathema + Soul Mantle + Coward’s Legacy
  • Cyclone Slayer: Starforge + Devoto’s Devotion + Daresso’s Salute + Kaom’s Heart + Tombfist + The Retch

Most of these also uses several Unique Jewels and Flasks as well. Got all of them to Red maps and Lv 90+. Understandably, many of them couldn’t do Uber-content, but I didn’t enjoy Uber content anyway.

The way I see it, we have an entire passive tree with (mostly) fixed modifiers, and Keystones are literally game-changing. They make me wish I can allocate more of them. When we allocate a Keystone, we are giving up a Notable that gives Life or Resistances. IMO, Uniques are an extension of that; we choose to equip a Unique with a special modifier over a Rare with Life or Resistances.

Thank you for reading all of that, and thank you in advance for any builds you can share.
Even if you don’t have a build to share, feel free to discuss any combination of Unique items that made you want to make a new build around it.
