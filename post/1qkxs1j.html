5 Mock Trades to pick apart

The trade deadline is soon so I figured I’d take a shot at filtering some of the currently available players through my brain and seeing if I actually liked the trades. I’m ngl, some of these trades even I don’t super like. I did my best to view the trades through the perspective of what other teams are trying to accomplish. I know I’m guaranteed to get some folks who just read the trades, think i’m advocating for the trades, and will DM me telling me to kms. I honestly wouldn’t do some of these.

As I continued, I realized that spending big assets on a C doesn’t help us if you’re just taking minutes away from Queta who has been a solid starter. Like it would need to be an All Star level C to make sense spending multiple major assets and benching Queta.

&nbsp;

Magic get - Xavier Tillman, Chris Boucher, future 2nd

Celtics get - Goga Bitadze

Origionally, I didn’t like this for us, but the more I looked at the trade market, the more value I saw in having a 3rd playable C. He does overlap with Queta quite a bit, but he’s great insurance in case Queta goes down or gets into foul trouble. He’s a solid screener so he keeps up with Queta and Garza. He’s a better defender than Garza, but not as mobile as Queta. He’s more of a positional defender and rebounder than Queta. He’d be a situational player but I think he solidifies our C rotation with really low investment. That being said, I don’t see him being able to play next to Queta at all, and only situationally next to Garza.

&nbsp;

Clippers get - Anfernee Simons, Xavier Tillman, 2 future 1sts

Celtics get - Ivica Zubac, Bogdan Bogdanovic

Here’s my question about Zubac. If given the same amount of minutes, how much more impactful is Zubac over Queta? And then that leads me to, is this production worth flushing 2 1st round picks for? I just don’t think it is. Even for 1 pick, you’re losing your best trade asset to bench Queta. They won’t play the floor together as neither can shoot. With Zubac playing 28 - 30 minutes, that leaves 18 available C minutes. There are going to be times where you want Garza in for shooting, and other times you just want to go small, especially at the end of games. We’d be losing 1-2 first round picks and smushing Queta out of the rotation. This is another one I really don’t like.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Jaren Jackson Jr., John Konchar

Grizzlies get - Khris Middleton, Anfernee Simons, Jordan Walsh, future 2 future 1sts (Celtics)

Wizards get - Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Brandon Clarke, Sam Hauser, Xavier Tillman/Chris Boucher, 2026 1st (Boston), future 2nd (Boston), future 2nd (Memphis)

Some people I’m sure will hate this trade, but i really like it. Celtics consolidate assets to get on of the greatest players of all time John Konchar, while also picking up Jaren Jackson Jr. I like Jackson’s fit on the C’s about a billion times better than anyone else on the trade market, because he could actually play next to Queta, and shift to C for smaller lineups. I also expect Joe could iron out some of his standing around and not being engaged on rebounding. Memphis picks up 2 firsts as well as clearing their dead salaries after this season, giving them a lot of freedom. They also pick up Jordan Walsh, following a path of Boston trading defensive players to Memphis. Finally, Washington does exactly what they want to do, picking up unwanted salary for draft picks. Hauser is easily tradeable again in the offseason or next year’s trade deadline, and everyone else is on a short 2 year deal.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Daniel Gafford, Al Horford, Moses Moody

Mavericks get - Jonathan Kuminga, DeAnthony Melton, Gary Payton Jr.

Warriors get - Anfernee Simons, Miles Bridges

Hornets get - Klay Thompson, Buddy Hield, Chis Boucher, 2026 1st (Celtics), future 2nd (Warriors)

So Celtics get big man depth in abundance, and also a cost controlled Gafford. Same issue across the board with these big man trades where I don’t really like the idea of the C’s giving up a draft asset for a redundant big man. Like again, what ground does Gafford cover that Queta doesn’t? Al Hoford returning is fun although I know theres a vocal contingent here whose opinion is “let Al burn”, which I don’t really get. We had a fire sale this summer and Tatum is injured. Horford didn’t want to spend his potential final season on a Tatumless C’s which i get. Celtics also pick up bench guard Moses Moody. So Celtics can go C’s by committee with Queta/Gafford being able to start, Al and Garza being situational, with Al being able to play some PF if we want to go double big and any point.

Mavericks are one of the few teams to register interest in Kuminga, and they get him here for spare parts and no huge commitment.

Warriors get 2 guys who can play 25+ minutes a night, which they definitely need. They’ve recently expressed interest in Bridges. They’re essentially consolidating guys who aren’t playing much for guys who can play starter level minutes. They also don’t end up having to give up multiple 1sts for Bridges.Essentially, the Warriors make the C’s pay the first and the only big asset the surrender is Kuminga (who they hate).

Hornets use their cap space to absorb Thompson, whose salary next season is a team option. They might even extend him, as I see them being interested in his championship experience on these younger players who seem to lack leadership. They get a first from the C’s, and a second from the Warriors, picking up what they’re looking for in a Bridges trade.

&nbsp;

Celtics get - Coby White, Jalen Smith

Bulls get - Anfernee Simons, Chris Boucher/Xavier Tillman, 2-3 2nd round picks

Trying to mock a trade with the Bulls is always a shit show because they hold on to guys for way to long and as of right now, they’re selling low on a bunch of guys. Coby White is a great player, but he’s expiring. Whoever is trading for him either is trading for the right to pay him his next contract. Most teams could wait for free agency. They’re in the same boat with Jalen Smith and Ayo Dosunmu. I honestly love all 3 of these players. I don’t see the Celtics bidding against a huge market for any of these players. This only really makes sense if the Celtics intend to give Coby White a contract and see him as a PG who can play next to Brown and Tatum. (I actually do).

I would simulataneously be suprised and not suprised if White wasn’t moved by the deadline.

&nbsp;

So, like I said at the start, I fully expect a bunch of people to not read the logic, or my thoughts on these, and to just poke fun at the highlighted trades themselves. For those who read, my challenge is, how would you change/balance these to make them more palletable for you? Some you may just absolutely hate which is fair, but if there are any on the line, how would you adjust?
