The side of Jayson Tatum (a lot of) people don’t see.

So I want to start off saying that a lot of people tend to hate Jayson Tatum because he is corny? He is cringe? No aura?

Now look I get it, he isn’t the flashiest superstar and he played for Boston & Duke so I get that. But he actually is one of the best human beings in the NBA and lemme explain why.

So first of all, Tatum is 1 of the most hardworking guys in the NBA, just look at his Achilles recovery process. Gets injured Mid-May (a month after the reg szn finished) and usually players like to take the 12 months to recover, not Tatum. He got his surgery done earlier than anyone in history and was back in the gym by 25 days, back walking after 3 months, back on the court after 5 months, back doing 1v1’s by 7 months and 5v5’s by 8 months. He put in the hard work to come back to help his team win and now look at him.

Next thing is that he works on his game every year. Since he came in, he has drastically improved on the defensive end, and is Rebounding has also been elite (might be the best Rebounding wing) and his Passing is also top notch. Even though since 2023 his numbers and efficiency has gone down (Scoring 30 to about 27 a night) he has arguably been better in both years since 23 he has become the leagues most complete player.

Next how as a human he is so good because he is a great dad, always working for Deuce bringing him to every game. Also has no Rape or Wife beating allegations (alr a lot of the league don’t have either but yk). He also has foundations and does a lot of things for the kids and his community. He also doesn’t get into fights on the court or beef with other players (which is why he is so respected in the NBA with many stars being close with him). Also has good relationships with Tyrese Haliburton, Bam, D Mitch, Harden, Embiid, MPJ, Dray, Bron, Dejoune, Dame, KD, Past and present teammates on the Celtics and Team USA, Ja, JJ, Paolo, Coop & pretty much just most of Duke. Also never complains about teammates and coaches as well as being very loyal.

Now this last part is my favourite part. Since coming into the league he has always been up for the challenge, In his rookie year going B4B with 2018 LeBron in the conference finals also dunking on him. But he is such a team guy because when he went down his first thoughts were This isn’t legit this is what I have heard from JT himself) Am I gon walk? Am I gon Play again? Are they gonna trade me? Am I gon be the same? He was thinking about his team first before anything. Then he stayed with the team while injured and was at every game and practice (either warming up or rehabbing on the side). Then when it came to returning he wasn’t sceptical of getting re injured, he was about messing up team chemistry, just shows he is a team first player and is willing to buy in to any role they need him to.

Bit of a dear diary but I kinda js wanted to share with everyone and see what they would say.
