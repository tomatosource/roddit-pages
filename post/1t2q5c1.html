No Teardown Needed, Just a Retool

I never contribute to these threads but guys let’s not overreact. Some of this chatter is crazy af.

If you remember this was supposed to be a punt year, so the fact that they were even the No. 2 seed and made the playoffs is pretty remarkable given the talent that we lost and Tatum missing most of the year. Technically yes we blew a 3–1 lead, but the series changed once Embiid came back. This is also a significantly more talented 76ers team than in years prior especially with the additions of Edgecombe and Paul George.

The Jays are our team and Joe Mazzulla is our coach. There are not magically better options out there. Giannis is not the answer and there is no head coach on the market that I would prefer over Mazzulla.

The only thing we need to do is trade White and Pritchard for a legitimate 3/D player or draft or develop someone who complements the Jays. We lost the series because of our inability to make open shots. If we hit 2 or 3 more 3 pointers per game this series would have been over. This is not a teardown. It is a retool.
