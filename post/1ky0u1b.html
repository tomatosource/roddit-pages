How big is your Shock?

Found a simple support gem combo I haven’t heard anyone talk about:
Overcharge+Commiserate in your shock applying skill, but it has to hit multiple times, and ideally only twice.

Overcharge increases shock magnitude by 50% but applies shock to you.
Commiserate adds 30% increased magnitude of an ailment if you have that ailment when inflicting it.
So, if you have a skill that hits multiple times, the first application of shock will Overcharge and shock you; the second application will remove the shock from you and increase the shock further.

E.G. In my current build, I am using this combo in Frost Bomb+Spell echo with sources of lightning damage gain. So, I get exactly two hits.

The skill universal supports are Overcharge+Commiserate+Conduction+Ambrosia (if your build can afford the flask sacrifice).
