Thread: I like that the Bigs/Centers are back to being valuable in Modern Nba

Did you remember the time (Mid 2010s) where Heatles and Steph/Klay GSW kinda temporary killed the Big Men/Centers? Especially the slow footed ones like Jahil Okafor or Roy Hibbert?

But now, We have Joker who is arguably the best player in the world. We have Wemby who is considered the new generational player. And he is only 21.

And I am not only talking about superstars. Even all star level players like KAT, Sengun and Sabonis are thriving in 2020s era.

Even young players like Duren, Mobley, Chet, and Kalkbrenner are showing that they are very solid even in pace and space game.

Ofc, someone will say:

“It is never a bad thing to have tall height, it was just that those Big Men in Mid 2010s are unskilled or slow footed”.

But, that’s the point. I love that it cycled back to NBA Centers being in Superstars or All NBA level, or All Star level categories this decade.
