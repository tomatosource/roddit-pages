GGG, I don’t think huge, sprawling map “exploration” in the campaign has the effect you intended, because this isn’t a 3D PoV game

Every map is just going up and down and left and right until I discover the next zone or some objective. This is not “exploration” and it’s not fun.

I could find Ignakduk in the first 5 minutes, or wander that blasted Bog for half an hour. That fills me with anxiety. Even after doing every act like 10 times, I cannot be 100% sure where to go, and it’s always so stressful knowing I might just waste the next 30 minutes of my life.

Here’s the bottom line: This isn’t a 3D game

I can’t just look into the distance and see grand landmarks like some big ritual in the Azak Bog, some big Ziggurat Apex in The Drowned City, or the Ogham Mansion from the farm fields or hunting grounds.

There is no “informed” exploration, there’s just scanning like a blind critter in the wheat fields. Top-down games don’t lend well to this kind of gameplay loop, we wanna kill stuff and get rewards. LE, Diablo and even PoE1 did this so much better, what in the world happened? Sure, the lore and world building is great, but not with 10 times more wasted dead-ends than real objectives.
