The Toronto Raptors turned Pascal Siakam, Otto Porter Jr. and Gradey Dick into Kawhi Leonard and Ja’Kobe Walter.

January 17th, 2024: Pascal Siakam is traded for 3 first round picks, Bruce Brown, Kira Lewis Jr and Jordan Nwora. One of these firsts is later rerouted to the Jazz with Otto Porter Jr. for Ochai Agbaji and Kelly Olynyk. Another is used in the 2024 Draft to select Ja’Kobe Walter.

February 6th 2025: Kelly Olynyk and Bruce Brown are traded to the New Orleans Pelicans with the Pacers Top 4 protected 2026 1st for Brandon Ingram who is penned to a 2+1 extension (3rd year is a player option)

Today: Brandon Ingram is traded with Gradey D1ck and two firsts, two seconds and a first-round swap to the Los Angeles Clippers for former Raptors Finals MVP Kawhi Leonard.

What a wild ride the Raptors rebuild around Scottie Barnes has been.
