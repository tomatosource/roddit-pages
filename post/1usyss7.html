Snapping your head and flailing your arms when you get hit is an unnatural motion and should be an automatic no-call by the referees.

After seeing those AJ Dybantsa Summer League highlights, I went to watch some NFL hit highlights as well as clips of guys getting knocked out in MMA and even a lady colliding with a cyclist. In no examples does anyone flail their arms or snap their head back. When people get trucked in the NFL their arms stay down. Likewise when people get KO’d in mid-air in MMA (like Fedor Emelianenko versus Arlovski), they just go limp.

If you’re trying to score or stop someone from scoring in basketball legit, you absorb the hit and keep your arms up or keep your hold on the ball to try to score. You don’t turn into a rag doll. If the refs just refuse to make a call for a player when they do that, they probably would stop doing it pretty quickly.

If anybody has some clips of people getting hit in the NFL or knocked out in boxing or MMA and actually snapping their head or flailing their arms, go ahead and share them, I’d like to analyze more.
