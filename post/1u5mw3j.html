Don’t buy fractured jewels

Wanted to make some jewels so I went and bought a fractured attack crit bonus emerald, after a bit of chaos spamming wanted to hit the +1 suffix but it said I couldn’t add another crafted mod, so after a bit I realised that the crit bonus was a fractured crafted mod so basically the jewel is dead (-30d).

​

THEN, I went to buy another with no crafted filter on, but guess what? That didn’t work (definitely bugged) and got scammed again (-30d).

​

So I just looked for a fractured mod that can’t be crafted (spear crit bonus) and all went fine.

​

TLDR; some fractured jewels are crafted so you won’t be able to add +1 suffix or increased effect.
