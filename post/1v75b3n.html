PSA: Usage Rate only attributes a percent of possessions that ends with said player, it’s not indicative of how much they control the ball

I still see people constantly misusing the usage rate stat. Especially a big conversation I see regarding Philly’s roster.

Usage rate calculates how much of a team possession ends with that specific player. Possessions can only END with a shot, free throw attempt, or turnover.

It doesn’t take anything else into account, such as how much a player plays on ball vs off, if they are a playmaker, time spent holding the ball, or anything else that would tell us a player is reliant on the ball to be effective.

Like all stats, it has its place where it can be used. But it is not a tool to analyze how players fit together alone.
