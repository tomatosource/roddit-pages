Why is 1968 SUCH a crazy outlier year for pitching?

Inspired by this post highlighting Bob Gibson and how that year was instrumental in the mound being lowered to encourage more offense, but why was it so crazy even compared to other years prior?

In 1968, 6 SP had &lt;2.00 ERAs. The average ERA among the top 10 was 1.86. But in 1967, the ERA leader was Phil Niekro with a 1.87. The average of the top 10 in 1968 was lower than the #1 pitcher by ERA the year prior. Even excluding Gibson’s 1.12, the average of 2-10 was 1.94, which still clears every pitcher after Niekro.

So what the heck happened in 1968 specifically to cause bats to die and pitchers to dominate, even moreso than prior years of pitcher dominance?

Edit: Over the last 100 years, among pitchers who’ve made 15+ starts, there have been 51 instances of a Sub 2.00 ERA. 6 of those occurred in one season, 13% of the total.

Edit 2: Seeing a lot of “the mound was still 15” at that point” comments. Yeah, I know that, but why was 1968 an extreme outlier even compared to 1967 and 1966? It was 15” then too.
