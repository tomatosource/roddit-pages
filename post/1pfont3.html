Got an email this morning from Xsolla that I bought Poe2 early access key.. but I already did a year ago and never used Xsolla?

It showed up in my purchase history on my path of exile account too. The key showed up and is unused. What the hell happened? Should I be concerned? Why did someone buy a key for my poe account? How does Xsolla have my email when I’ve never used it? Has anyone had a similar thing happen to them? I’m worried about the security concerns here or if there was just a mistake on Xsolla’s system.

edit:

PSA: I think I figured out what happened.

When you buy EA key from the poe 2 website it is done via Xsolla. For some reason they had my paypal saved as a payment method probably from the last time I bought something via the shop, and my paypal does not require login for that link because it was associated with my email address. I believe someone logged into my poe account on the website and purchased the key.

What I suggest EVERYONE to do:

  1. Disable auto-login in your paypal for email address associations

  2. Delete all of your saved payment methods in poe website, do not save any payment method on there because poe’s security is terrible, they don’t even have an option for 2fa.
