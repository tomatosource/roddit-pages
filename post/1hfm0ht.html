A good start IS important to winning a title: Since 2007, 16 of the last 18 NBA champions had a top 3 (or tied for top 3) record in the league only looking at every team’s first 25 games of the season.

Here are those teams record after 25 games and where it ranked among every other team:

  • 2024 BOS: 20-5 (Tied-1)
  • 2023 DEN: 15-10 (7)
  • 2022 GSW: 21-4 (Tied-1)
  • 2021 MIL: 16-9 (Tied-5)
  • 2020 LAL: 22-3 (Tied-1)
  • 2019 TOR: 20-5 (1)
  • 2018 GSW: 19-6 (3)
  • 2017 GSW: 21-4 (1)
  • 2016 CLE: 18-7 (3)
  • 2015 GSW: 22-3 (1)
  • 2014 SAS: 20-5 (3)
  • 2013 MIA: 19-6 (Tied-2)
  • 2012 MIA: 19-6 (Tied-2)
  • 2011 DAL: 20-5 (3)
  • 2010 LAL: 21-4 (1)
  • 2009 LAL: 21-4 (Tied-2)
  • 2008 BOS: 22-3 (1)
  • 2007 SAS: 19-6 (1)

Of those 18 teams, 16 of them started 18-7 or better. Only the 2021 Bucks and 2023 Nuggets had worse starts to the season after 25 games.

The top 3 teams after 25 games thisyear are the Cavs, Thunder, and Celtics, and those are also the only 3 teams to start 18-7 or better this year.
