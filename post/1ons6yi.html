PSA: If you get stunned, you cannot block or evade!

This isn’t really mentioned much in the tooltips so I figured I’d shout it out. Getting stun protection is actually a good thing and more than just to avoid the annoyance. Think of it this way, if you get stunned 50% of the time from regular mobs and get stunned for half a second, then really your block chance and evasion rating mostly all goes to waste!

I learned this after trying to crack the code for surviving simulacrum. I fixed the stun issue and completed the trial from there! Hopefully this helps someone out there that has survivability issues.
