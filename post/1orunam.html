In the 2024 NBA Finals, the Mavericks did not lose to the Celtics because their defense wasn’t good enough

The entire premise of trading Luka less than a year after making the finals was that with him as your star player, your defense was inherently SO bad that it was impossible to win a championship. Defense wins championships and all that.

But in the 2024 Finals, the Mavericks held the Celtics to a 111.0 offensive rating, which was TWELVE points lower than their regular season offensive rating of 123.2

The Mavericks held the Celtics offense relatively in check throughout that finals. They did not lose that series because of their defense. Dallas lost that series because they had a 108.4 offensive rating which was 9 points below their regular season average. The OFFENSE was the reason Dallas lost to Boston, not the defense.

Among all the other reasons the Luka trade was stupid, I never see this one mentioned. The entire underlying logic of the trade was a version of the 2024 NBA finals that didn’t actually happen in real life.
