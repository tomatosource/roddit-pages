Permanent invulnerability with Tame beast

If you tame a beast with Undying Will (Abyss mod) and support it with Brutus’ Brain, you can create a beast that is unkillable and provides an infinite invulnerability buff. You can apply an Abyss mod to a beast through the new Abyssal modifier that grants all rares in the map one Abyss mod.
