Trial of Chaos needs work. Affixes are annoying/boring with low room for error or counter play. Player choice feels too restricted with no “bad luck” reroll mechanics. Lots of running/delays between combat makes it feel like a slog.

As titled. Trials of Chaos should be something you look forward to running because it’s fun but it feels like a slog with little room for counter-play, overly punishing room affixes, and ample downtime between combat due to running and random trial master “freeze the game” role-play.

Change the room affixes so they at least feel fair, or at best feel fun to choose. One option here is to have the downside apply to BOTH player and monsters so it doesn’t feel shitty having to constantly re-position due to traps, etc, and not be able to fight mobs. Plus it makes it feel FUN to lure mobs into a trap meant for YOU! Also special hidden reward for pacifist runs??

Bosses:

Some of the bosses have really punishing moves that can melt you with little to no choreography. The bird dude’s air blasts that go around a circle, the chimera’s fire breath both hurt a ton and are really hard to dodge. For how difficult those boss moves are to read, they do WAY too much damage. Also the invulnerability phases feel unfair for how exposed the boss is, totally breaking immersion. The invuln. phases need to be shortened.

Please change the Trial of Chaos so it feels more fair and fun to play. It should be something you look forward to playing because it in itself is fun, not just because you get two ascendency points from it (which are fun)
