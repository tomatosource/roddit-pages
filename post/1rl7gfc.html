Nolan Ryan famously played for all four of the 1961⁄62 expansion teams (Mets, Angels, Astros, and Rangers). Those four teams coincidentally won their World Series championships in the exact same order Ryan played for them.

Ryan played for the Mets first from 1966 to 1971 with them winning in 1969 (Ryan’s only championship) and 1986, then for the Angels from 1972 to 1979 with them winning in 2002, then for the Astros from 1980 to 1988 with them winning in 2017 (cheating aside) and 2022, then finally for the Rangers from 1989 to 1993 with them winning in 2023.
