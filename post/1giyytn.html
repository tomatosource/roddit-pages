14-Year Point Guard DJ Augustin has retired from the NBA

Augustin made his announcement earlier today on Instagram that he’s hanging it up. He last played in 21-22 for the Rockets and Lakers and played for 11 total NBA teams. Never a star or core piece but always a solid backup point guard that could shoot and quick enough to get to the rim for an easy layup. My favorite moment has to be him hitting that big shot against Toronto in 2019 to put the Magic up 1-0. Overall a solid career and congrats to him. Let me know what y’all think about him!
