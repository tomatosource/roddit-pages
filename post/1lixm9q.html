[O.C] How to pretend you know about the draft 2025

Hey all, another year another draft. We only have two days to care about the draft. You want to look cool, you want to look fresh. You want to look hot, but your issue is that you don’t know anything about anyone of these players. Don’t worry. I got you.

Rule 1: Anyone over 20 is a waste of a pick. Everyone over 21 is practically dead.

Fun fact, every player who has ever been older than 21 in the draft has been bad. All of them, ever. So Derik Queen who turned 20 in december? dead to you. VJ Edgecomb who turns 20 next month? great pick. Your favorite guy in the draft.

Rule 2: Say “Of course the Thunder get the steal of the draft” after they pick.

It doesn’t matter who they pick. you are likely to be right. The Thunder pick twice in the first round, at picks 15 and then again at 24. People will likely forget you said it at pick 24. Other words to use are “that’s a Thunder-y pick for sure” “watch this guy be an all-star in a few years,” “Presti really did his homework,” and “probably the next J Dub.” Fun fact, if you ever find yourself in a time machine to the 2010s, you could say the same about every Spurs pick.

Rule 3: Ace Bailey is your least favorite player in this draft. You hate his mentality.

Every year there’s a guy we all hate. It was Brandon Miller in that draft where Brandon Miller was there. It was basically everyone in last year’s draft and this year, it’s Ace Bailey. He has a terrible mentality and hasn’t worked hard for anything in his life apparently. He’s a selfish ball hog who cares only about himself. Why? I don’t know, but he probably is. You’re happy every time a team doesn’t pick Ace Bailey.

Rule 4: Pick a random player over 6’5. this is your favorite player(besides Cooper Flagg). This is your guy now.

Cooper Flagg is your lord and savior. He is the next face of the league. He is Scottie Pippen mixed with Kevin Durant. He is our lord and savior. The only downside of Cooper Flagg is that you can’t be the steal of the draft at the first pick. So go to any mock draft. scroll down. pick a guy, make sure he’s pretty tall, not 20 years or older, and this is your guy. So yeah everyone knows Cooper Flagg is good but now you’re the guy who thinks Egor Demin is good or Carter Bryant is your guy. Or Thomas Sorber.

Rule 5: Develop either a deep love or a deep hate for Jerimiah Fears in the next two days.

He is either the next big thing or the most obvious bust in the draft. And you will fight anyone who disagrees with you. There is no in-between here.

Rule 6: Describe players in pop culture references. No one will question them

The references don’t even have to make sense. You can full-on Family Guy this thing. As long as there’s a movie in there someone will like it and think it was inciteful. “Man Khaman Maluach is the *Shrek 2* of Big men.” This is Bill Simmons legacy.

Rule 7: Have Fun

That’s what the draft is really all about. just getting together and watching something that probably is about as exciting as a big excel sheet with a bunch of names of guys you don’t know on it.
