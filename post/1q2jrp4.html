The Worst Patch in PoE History

I am not a doomsayer, I never have been. Every league in PoE1/PoE2 where there are naysayers, I always look at the bright side. I think GGG has always been mostly fair with balancing/patches. However, this is a disaster.

AND NOT BECAUSE OF THE LOOT!

For the first time since the launch of early access, GGG introduced a mechanic with immense depth to the end game. A mechanic that with careful planning, decision making, and a lot of effort could be very rewarding, too rewarding. However, people didn’t mindlessly spend days, tens to hundreds of hours building these temples solely to walk around and pick up loot. It was because the design and scaling of the temple was immensely rewarding and fun.

Ignoring the consequences of the interactions of a mechanic with such depth and scaling for weeks led ultimately to massive inflation in the economy, but these things happen in PoE. That is why we have leagues. Returning to the office AFTER ignoring the consequences of the immense reward scaling of a league mechanic to completely remove the tens to hundreds of hours of time that thousands of players invested into the mechanic IS NOT OKAY.

Completely locking people out of continuing to progress on their temples, or even engage in the building progress for the sake of “balance” is a massive oversight. If you nerfed the loot, some people would have complained and some may have quit. The same thing may have happened if you nerfed other aspects of the temple. Fundamentally destroying the league mechanic and all of the progress that (Mostly casual players at this point who weren’t able to complete their temples and are suffering the hardest) made is an objectively horrible decision this late into a league.

This is not only going to cost PoE2 player retention in the short term, but likely a lot of loyal players who otherwise would have continued to get excited and buy supporter packs for each coming league. The destruction of progress, the hours time wasted… this is not about the loot. This is a matter of respecting people’s time and efforts. The people that support and fund PoE2.

This is the worst patch in PoE history.
