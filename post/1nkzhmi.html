The big questions that Cuban & Ballmer still haven’t answered

As Pablo Torre keeps revealing the paper trail between the Clippers and Aspiration, the “team Ballmer” arguments keep getting more technical. But they still haven’t addressed three major questions and this completely tanks their argumentation IMO:

  1. Why would Aspiration pay Kawhi for a no-show deal? And why pay him much more than any other celebrity?
  2. Ballmer, Clippers and Aspiration were heavily linked as we now know (investments, carbon credits and sponsoring). Why has the Kawhi endorsement deal never come up in what must have been hundreds of conversations, not to mention due diligence?
  3. Why didn’t ballmer tell us about Wongs investment, or the carbon credits purchase, when he went on ESPN? If he was really innocent, why didn’t he come clean then?

Am I missing something or is their denial getting more ridiculous by the day?
