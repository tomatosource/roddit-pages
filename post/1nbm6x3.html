Just my thoughts on Warrior

I’ve made multiple Warriors since release, and no matter how much I play the class (got close to 600 hours in the class so far) I noticed I just still haven’t found a skill/build that genuinely feels smooth and satisfying.

Yes Warrior has big AoE’s, big damage, survivability feels alright this patch, but I’m in this constant search of finding that one ability/build that feels satisfying to me.

Stampede got close, but there’s this windup and moments of standing still for a split second, it feels a bit clunky and would love for the class to feel smoother, less sluggish.

I feel like I’m chasing the goal of finding fun playing the class and never really get there.
