[Murray] The LA Clippers would likely prioritize good players over copious draft capital in a Kawhi Leonard trade— with a potential package from Minnesota involving Rudy Gobert, Julius Randle, the 28th pick, and an unprotected 2033 first-round pick seen as a potentially optimal.

Source: https://youtu.be/_pNlDDUPCOk?si=R5F10GgolIHjs0D9

Around the 34:00 minute mark:

“I don’t think the Clippers should be interested in taking back those current Warriors [Draymond Green or Jimmy Butler] or in Andrew Wiggins’ case, former Warriors champion.

The Timberwolves [trade] is probably the best of those … I’m sure Minnesota would love to pair McDaniels, who works out with Kawhi, with Anthony Edwards. That allows them to look at next season and say ‘we can take another leap here’ whereas the Clippers would get the better players out of all the potential trade packages. It’s not the best draft capital, but it’s the better players and guys whose deals will be up soon enough where you could move on from them— Randle has a 2027 player option. Even though Rudy Gobert is old, Rudy Gobert is in incredible shape and you know what you’re getting out of Rudy Gobert. He’s a walking top-10 defense by himself and he also has a 2027 player option.”
