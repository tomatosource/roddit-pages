Turkish club Anadolu Efes signs Cole Swider for 2025-26 season

Anadolu Efes has officially signed American forward Cole Swider for the 2025-26 season. The 26-year-old brings NBA and G League experience to the Turkish club.

Swider spent parts of three seasons in the NBA, playing for teams including the Los Angeles Lakers, Miami Heat, Detroit Pistons, and Toronto Raptors. Last season with the Raptors, he averaged 7.4 points and 3.4 rebounds in eight games.

In the G League, Swider was a standout performer, averaging 21.1 points on 44.3% three-point shooting along with 6.2 rebounds in 13 games for the South Bay Lakers. His shooting and scoring ability make him a valuable perimeter threat.

A product of Villanova and Syracuse University, Swider is expected to add floor spacing and outside shooting to Anadolu Efes’ roster as they prepare for the upcoming season.
