Caitlin Cooper Spoiled my Podcast Listening

I learned of Caitlin through her being on the Lowe Post podcast over the last couple of years. Then we got a big dose of her during the very fun Pacers finals run. Incredibly smart, in-depth X&O’s analysis while also factoring in the players as people and their tendencies. I was spoiled by that high quality basketball coverage. So now when I’m listening to my usual NBA podcasts there is a lot to be desired.

This is what I hear in every episode I’m listening to:

“Team A. Team A made some moves in the off season. We’ll see how Team A uses this roster in the regular season next year to make some noise and figure themselves out before going into the playoffs. They’ll finish with a record that adds up to 82 games.”

“Can you believe Team B?!? What are they thinking?!? I just don’t know what they’re doing. Can you believe this front office?!”

Anyone with access to a team’s roster page and half a brain could come up with these takes.

Anyway, shoutout Caitlin. Wish we had more like her.
