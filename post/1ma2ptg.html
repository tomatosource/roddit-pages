“I heard a lot of say Paolo is inefficient, he shoots a lot of mid-range jumpers, he’s not a #1 guy, how can you pay him like a number one guy? I don’t know what you are seeing, but this dude is fucking awesome. I am more than OK paying him a max. I don’t know if he is going to be a first-team All-NBA player at any point, I would probably lean towards there’s a season where he cracks it … but I know watched this dude in the playoffs against elite defenses with nothing around him – no shooting, no help, almost nothing reliable plus or minus a Franz Wagner game … he just shows up and shows up and makes a fair share of really hard mid-range jump shots. He has also shown me he is a good playmaker as a passer and a screener. I think the more talent around him, the more he will tap into those parts of his game. Give me all the Banchero stock. That dude is ready for everything.”
