“I heard a lot of say Paolo is inefficient, he shoots a lot of mid-range jumpers, he’s not a #1 guy, how can you pay him like a number one guy? I don’t know what you are seeing, but this dude is fucking awesome. I am more than OK paying him a max. He has also shown me he is a good playmaker as a passer and a screener. I think the more talent around him, the more he will tap into those parts of his game. Give me all the Banchero stock. That dude is ready for everything.”
