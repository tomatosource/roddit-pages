Munetaka Murakami drastically different splits in the 1st game of a series vs the rest of the series

This was brought up on the Mariners broadcast when they played the White Sox how Murakami has played significantly better in the 1st game of a series than he has in the rest of the series

PA HR AB/HR BA OBP SLG OPS BB% K%
1st Game 61 9 5.2 .383 .525 .979 1.503 23.0 13.1
Rest of series 112 6 15.8 .158 .277 .347 .624 14.3 46.4

This could just be simple small sample variance, or it could be teams adjusting to him through the series, but the near .900 point drop in OPS and the 3.5x increase in K% is crazy
