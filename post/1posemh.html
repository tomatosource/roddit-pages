Why Players Are Leaving in POE2

POE2 has too many design issues pushing players away. Here are the some of them i summary from the posts:

  1. Early game feels bad: before your build comes online, you just chip away at enemies. No satisfaction.
  2. “Souls-like” but cannot win with skill : heavy monster CC, one shot and weird rare affixes mean you get pinned and shredded before your build is ready. Imagine an Mana Drain Mob push you to a dead end…
  3. Maps are too big: too much pointless walking.
  4. Ascendancy is too RNG and too time-consuming: if you have a job/family, you might get 1–2 attempts a day. Fail, and try again tomorrow, way too punishing. (I was enjoying the league until I lost all progress twice on the third Ascendancy and quit.) So many people are selling Ascendancy 4… Ascendancy should be a milestone you reach through normal play or reasonable build.
  5. This league’s machanism experience is rough, enough said.
  6. Campaign is too long: getting one character online takes nearly a week. POE’s selling point is build variety, look at your passive tree, but this kills the appeal of rolling multiple builds.
  7. Not enough endgame content.

I love this game. I play since POE1 beta. Honestly saying, I felt really bad everytime when they are nerfing everything. You are trying to make the game Hard, not the player’s experience.
Thank you anyone for reading this :)
