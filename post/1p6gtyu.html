Is negative rarity farming by design?

Curious what others think:

Did GGG ever actually intend for negative rarity to be the best way to farm exceptional bases (especially via items such as Ventor’s)?

A lot of this feels like a side effect of crafting being so strong that almost all “normal” bases are basically worthless outside of jewelry — with maybe a few exceptions for meta weapons, since corrupted/sanctified T1 crafts can still sell for a ton. (e.g., a 3-socket corrupted meta bow is basically identical to a mirrored 3-socket version.) Even then, your real investment in those items that you find in the wild is minimal unless you get lucky with some initial augs or you hit the lottery and they drop with low-weight T1s. Personally, I’ve maybe found two or three Merciless weapons with an additional meta T1 roll across what feels like a million hours between 0.1 and 0.3.

Which brings us to the current meta: for solo players in particular, negative rarity absolutely crushes “normal” rarity-based currency farming for profit. Honestly, even outside of super-efficient 6-man groups (the full 6-player bonus really matters — the insane scaling from 5 to 6 makes no sense but it exists), fast solo negative rarity farming still seems best.

We’ll have to see what 0.4 changes, of course, but it raises the question: should GGG make drop odds of exceptional bases scale positively with player rarity, assuming gear rarity is staying?
