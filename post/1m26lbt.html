Giannis (2021 -2025): Best Stretch of Non MVP/Non Runner Up Straight Seasons

Giannis won MVPs in 2019 and 2020. However, after that, he didn’t finish higher than 3rd in MVP Race.

In 2021 - 2025 stretch. Not only he did not win MVP, he didn’t finish as runner up in any of these seasons. However, he is putting up MVP like numbers.

Giannis Antetokounmpo (2021-2025)

2025: 30.4 ppg, 11.9 rpg, 6.5 apg (3rd in MVP Race)

2024: 30.4 ppg, 11.5 rpg, 6.5 ppg (4th in MVP Race)

2023: 31.1 ppg, 11.8 rpg, 5.7 apg (3rd in MVP Race)

2022: 29.9 ppg, 11.6 rpg, 5.8 apg (3rd in MVP Race)

2021: 28.1 ppg, 11.0 rpg, 5.9 apg (4th in MVP Race)

Is it safe to say that Giannis had the best 5 year stretch in 21st century in terms of non mvp/non mvp runner up nba seasons?
