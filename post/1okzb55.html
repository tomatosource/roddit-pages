Teams Clinching WS at Home vs. on the Road

It feels like it is rare for a team to clinch the World Series at home. Off the top of my head, 2024 LAD, 2023 TEX, 2021 ATL, 2019 WSH, 2018 BOS, 2017 HOU, 2016 CHC, 2015 KCR, 2014 SFG, all won on the road.

If the Blue Jays win game 6 or 7 they join 2022 HOU by clinching at home.

Is it more common to win on the road the way the series is laid out or just a trend over the last decade or so?
