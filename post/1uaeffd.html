In the first Knicks Championship parade in over 53 years, James Dolan and NYC politicians got 13 minutes of podium mic time. All Knicks players combined got 2 minutes of podium mic time, including 0 minutes for KAT, OG, and Alvarado

I remember when Championship parades used to have end of the bench guys going crazy. Those were so fun. For example, Steve Kerr once had a legendary moment during a Bulls parade. Aaron Wiggins was also funny last year.

Instead, this year we had to listen to Dolan and a bunch of politicians larp as Knicks fans. Here was my approximate count:

  • James Dolan: 1:12 minutes

  • Zohran Mamdani (NYC Mayor): 8:17 minutes

  • Jumaane Williams (NYC Public Advocate): 1:25 minutes

  • Mark Williams LeVine (NYC Comptroller): 1:15 minutes

  • Julie Menin (NYC Council Speaker): 0:47 seconds

  • Jalen Brunson: 2:07 minutes

  • All other Knicks players: ZERO
