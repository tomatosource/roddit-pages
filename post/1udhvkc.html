Gameday Thread: The 2026 NBA Draft

Today’s the day, folks.

As a big recruiting and college basketball fan, draft day is one of my favorite days of the year.

Last year, we struck potential gold at the end of the round by selecting Hugo Gonzalez, who quickly turned into a fan favorite and productive rookie.

We’re in a similar boat this year, picking at the end of the round, looking for a diamond in the rough.

Some names that could go in the 20-30 range:

Allen Graves
Isaiah Evans
Dailyn Swain
Chris Cenac
Tarris Reed
Henri Veesaar
Labaron Philon
Koa Peat
Josh Jefferson
Maleek Thomas
Zuby Ejiofor
Alex Karaban

Lots of other names, as well.

This draft in particular is very deep. The top 4 is guaranteed, but 5-15 could be any number of players. This will lead to a few guys slipping further than expected, and I think we’ll see some surprising names on the list when the Celtics are on the clock.

Anyways, happy draft day. Let’s get it.
