An explosion in Offensive Rebounding is reshaping offense. Oreb% is up over 5% in the past 6 years and the Oreb% leader is over 13% higher this year vs 2020

There was recently a great post about how possessions have become the emphasis for coaching staff and their willingness to foul more to try and force turnovers and gain possessions. With this great Quote from Stan Van Gundy

The currency of the NBA now and even when you listen to the coaches is possessions, getting more shots than the other team. That has become much more of a big deal than efficiency with with coaches. It’s getting more shots.

On the other side of the ball we’ve seen just as drastic a change with teams placing increasing emphasis on gaining more possessions off the glass.

This Data is based on the top 8 from each conference and their Offensive rebounding %. I did this to try and get a cleaner sample with just teams that are competitive and actively trying to win.

There are 21 teams averaging a higher Oreb% that the league leading Celtics did in 2020.

Houston remains the poster child for how Oboards can create good offense but it seems clear that this emphasis is not just limited to them.
