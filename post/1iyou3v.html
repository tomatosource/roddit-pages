[Update] PoE 2 Companion – Improvements Based on Your Feedback!

Hey Exiles,

Thanks to all your feedback and testing, I’ve just released version 1.1.0 of Path of Exile 2 Companion, addressing some of the most common complaints and making the app smoother to use!

🔹 What’s New in v1.1.0:

  • 📸 Now you can select images from the gallery, not just take photos
  • ✂ Improved cropping – replaced pinch-to-resize with corner dragging for more precise selection
  • 🔄 Better image processing – works correctly in both portrait & landscape
  • 🛠 Fix for incorrect cropping on some Android devices

This update doesn’t add new features, but it fixes major usability issues and makes the app work better for those who had trouble before. If you had issues in the past, please try again and let me know if this helps!
