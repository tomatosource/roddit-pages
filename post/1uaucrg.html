Week 3 Market Recap: Divines rising from last week (+48%), Breach Catalysts surging, and Lineage supports split. Are you holding depreciating currency?

Hey Exiles,

Week 3 of Runes of Aldur is wrapping up, and the Currency Exchange has been absolutely wild. If you’ve been holding onto Exalted Orbs hoping to convert them to Divines later, you’re bleeding purchasing power every single day.

Here is the TL;DR of the major market swings between June 13 and June 19:

📈 What’s Pumping:

  • Divines are skyrocketing: We went from 133.5 Ex per Div on Monday to 197.4 Ex per Div by Thursday close (+48% in a week).
  • Breach Catalysts: Refined Sibilant Catalyst surged from 0.44 to 1.6 Div (+262%). Skittering Catalysts doubled as well.
  • Mid-tier Essences: Perfect Essence of Haste went from 0.04 to 0.19 Div (+332%) as mid-tier crafting demand catches up.
  • Runes & Lineage: Kolr’s Hunt almost doubled to 1.85 Div, and Breachlord’s Rift climbed 130% to 6.8 Div.

📉 What’s Crashing:

  • Ritual Omens: Omen of Amelioration down 63%. Favour farming supply has officially caught up to early-league demand.
  • Lineage Splits: Not all Lineage supports are winners right now. Breachlord’s Amalgam gave back 74% (down from 6.5 to 1.7 Div) and Brutus’ Brain fell 63%.
  • Abyss Drops: Preserved Rib crashed 75% as Abyss output flooded the market.

What are your predictions for Week 4? Are Divines going to break the 300 Ex barrier and stay there, or are we finally due for a correction? Let’s discuss!
