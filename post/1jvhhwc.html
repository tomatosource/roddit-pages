Notable Matchups and Playoff Implications for April 9th

WIth four games remaining, multiple seed possibilities are still in play. Here are the notable matchups with playoff seeding implications:

#2 Boston Celtics vs. #7 Orlando Magic | 7:00 p.m. ET

  • Orlando clinches Southeast Division with win.
  • Orlando is up two games in the East as the 7-seed over Atlanta.
  • Boston is locked into the 2-seed in the East.

#3 Los Angeles Lakers vs #10 Dallas Mavericks | 7:30 p.m. ET

  • Los Angeles can clinch the Playoffs with a win.
  • The Lakers can clinch the Pacific Division with a win AND losses by the Warriors and Clippers.
  • The Lakers are 1-game up on 4-7 seeds in the West.
  • Dallas is locked into the Play-in Tournament with a win OR Phoenix loss.
  • Dallas is 1 GB of 9-seed Sacramento in the West.

#10 Miami Heat vs #9 Chicago Bulls | 8:00 p.m. ET

  • Chicago and Miami have the same record at 36-43.
  • Both teams are 1 GB of 8-seed Atlanta in the East.

#13 San Antonio Spurs vs #6 Golden State Warriors | 9:00 p.m. ET

  • Golden State, the 6-seed, is tied with 4-seed LAC, 5-seed DEN, and 7-seed MEM.
  • Golden State is 1-game up on 8-seed Minnesota in the West.

#1 Oklahoma City Thunder vs #11 Phoenix Suns | 9:00 p.m. ET

  • Oklahoma City can clinch homecourt through the Playoffs with a win.
  • Phoenix is eliminated with a loss OR Dallas win.
  • Phoenix is currently 3 GB of 10-seed Dallas in the West.

#5 Denver Nuggets vs #9 Sacramento Kings | 9:00 p.m. ET

  • Denver, the 5-seed, is tied with 4-seed LAC, 6-seed GSW, and 7-seed MEM.
  • Denver is 1 GB of 3-seed LAL and 1-game up on 8-seed MIN in the West.
  • Sacramento is 1-game up on 10-seed Dallas.

#2 Houston Rockets vs #4 Los Angeles Clippers | 9:00 p.m. ET

  • Houston is locked into the 2-seed in the West.
  • The Clippers, the 4-seed, are tied with 5-seed DEN, 6-seed GSW, and 7-seed MEM.
  • The Clippers are 1-game up on 8-seed MIN.
