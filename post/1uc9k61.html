I made the biggest mistake

This is my first time playing POE2, second time playing POE in general. Today I learned about the biggest mistake I’ll ever make.

​

While leveling and doing ascendancy trials I came across a unique relic from the final boss of Trial of the Sekhemas. At the time I was leveling SSF and intended to go to trade later.

​

This relic was the Last Flame, currently valued at 2300 divine orbs. I know it was because it was distinctly an incense relic and I remember looking at the “Sets honour to one” line of text and thinking I’d never be able to do it. I didn’t think any of these things were valuable. I got rid of it for inventory space in the room with all the chests to pick up more jewels. I didn’t even think about it. Was just excited to get my last points and a few gems.

​

Flash forward to today where a friend was talking about the insane value of certain relics. I see the exact relic I threw away at this insane price and I am losing my mind over the fact I got rid of it and there’s no item restore.

​

This will haunt me for days at minimum. I ran trials seven times after hearing it and of course I will likely never see it again. I googled the drop rate and saw 1 in 650 and want to tear my hair out.

​

That’s it, but if you’ve made a costly mistake in this league it’s probably not as big as this one.

​

Adios, last flame.
