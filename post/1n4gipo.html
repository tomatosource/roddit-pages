Group A:

The Czech and the Spanish Team truly show that legacy means absolutely nothing as once again Czechia was just disappointing. Not much to say here, they are just not good enough. Serbia vs Latvia
So in yesterdays recap I did not wrote that Bogdanovic got injured because we had no further information at that point. Well its still uncertain but he missed this game and probably will miss the group stage. Still even without him Serbia was still favorite vs Latvia and the Unicorn. It wasnt easy however as at halftime Serbia only had a 4 Point Lead. Jokic was a monster and Serbia overall wasnt horrible (while apart form Jokic also not being really good) but Latvia kept being on their tails. With a minute to go Serbia had to take a timeout as Latvia has closed the gap to just 2 Points. In the last minute Jokic shot 2 more Free Throws to secure a narrow win as afterwardsa neither team was able to score despite attempts from Porzingis and David Bertans. Serbia survive a scare from well playing Latvians but this was far from the most convincing game without Bogdanovic.
84:80 Serbia

Group D:

Iceland vs Belgium
Iceland versus Belgium was another game for 5th place as neither team is expected to win against anyone else in the group. However this game was surprisingly enjoyable so much that I actually watched a lot of it. Even with the quality lacking a bit and no NBA players on either team, both teams clearly tried their hardest to secure a win at the Eurobasket and sometimes thats what matters to make basketball enjoyable. At first it seemed like Iceland had the advantage but the Belgian Team came flying in the fourth quarter pulling out a comeback win thanks to 8 4th Quarter Points from Emmanuel Lecomte.
71:64 Belgium.

France vs Slovenia
France is a young core that in 5 Years probably has Serbia status. For now its the first real test for them facing of vs he legend of Luka Doncic and whoever else is on the court for Slovenia at the time. Well Luka was very much only stoppable with fouls shooting a lot Freethrows and having 28 Points in three quarters keeping Slovenia at a narrow 2 Point Lead. Atleast Edo muric was also playing well on both offense and defense. The problems for Slovenia however are always the 4th Quarter as the best Slovenians have to play a lot more minutes than their opponents. Clearly getting tired to fresh French players, the last Quarter was all France securing a win vs Luka. Since both teams are very offense heavy (with Wemby and Gobert out) this was also one of the highest scoring games of the tournament so far.
103-95 France

Awards: After skipping it yesterday, today I feel like it again, so heres my awards.

Player of the Day: Nikola Jokic
Yeah we know it. Jokic is him. 39 Points out of Serbias 84, just show how good he is and why Serbia is so much better with him. Without him they just lose this game. Ill be naming a few more Standouts in the honorable mentions.

HM: Theis (9⁄9 from the field, Monster block) and Sylvain Francisco (32/7/5 in 26 Minutes vs Slovenia).
Other than that Luka, Sengun and Markannen had their usual good to very good games in Fiba.

Match of the Day: Serbia vs Latvia
Serbia getting pushed to their limits wasnt on my Bingo card today but they did. I dont think this was the best game quality wise but the upset looming and Jokic having a monster game aswell as the tension make it my pick. But all the Honorable Mentions have their arguments aswell.

HM: Slovenia vs France, Poland vs Israel and Belgium vs Iceland.

Disappointment of the day: Numerous.
I dont really want to mention one team here. With Czech Republic, Portugal and Montenegro several teams could claim that spot.

Positive Surprise of the day: Sweden
Sweden so far playedvery well at the Eurobasket for what was expected and them dismanteling Great Britain was a positive surprise. Montenegro gotta be careful vs them if they want to reach the next round.

HM: Sylvain Francisco
In order to be a medal contender without Gobert und Wemby France needed someone to step up. Surprisingly it isnt one of the numerous NBA player but Kaunas Point Guard Francisco going head to head with Luka and not losing. With that France is eyeing a medal.

Game to watch tomorrow: Georgia vs Greece
I dont think tomorrow has the most interesting games. There should be a lot of favorites beating on lower tier opponents. Slovenia vs Belgium, Israel vs France and Georgia vs Greece are the most interesting to me. Out of these Georgia has the most potential to upset, so i picked them.
