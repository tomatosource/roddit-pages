Why do the celtics always struggle around the new year?

The celtics struggles this year aren’t new for this time of year. For whatever reason, for the last few seasons, they’ve struggled in december/january. For reference, in 22-23, they lost 11 games in december and january, including a disastrous stretch where they lost 5⁄6. Last year, 7 of their 18 total losses came in those two months alone with another on february 1st. I’ll skip 2022 and 2021 cause those teams were just ass in general at that time and the data would be heavily skewed. They lost 10 games in those two months in 2020. Now, they’ve lost 5 games in december alone. It isn’t a coincidence that december and January tend to be rough months for the celtics as the data proves it. I personally think its because they start to coast during this time. They usually start and end the season hot but struggle during the middle. Just my thoughts.

There’s an argument to be made that this is just sheer coincidence and im trying to cope with our poor play but the percentage of losses show an even worse story.
