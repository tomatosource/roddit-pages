I love the team but hated watching them this year

I have been a long Celtics fan (Bird stole the ball!), and last year was amazing. But this year, while I love these players, I hated watching them play.

Maybe I’m just getting older, but when I watch a team like Golden State and the movement of the ball, with players cutting…and actual offense. That’s basketball.

Just watching this team stand at the 3pt line was not fun. Giving the ball to JT with 10 on the clock and he just iso’s for a 3….or when JT is out, JB then feels he has to be the man, and he takes a ISO 3!

Why can’t JB be an inside, perimeter guy? Why must the offense be all about the 3? It really turned me off.
