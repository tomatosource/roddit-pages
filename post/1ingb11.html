The damage that bosses do has a very strange distribution and GGG should adjust that

tl;dr: Boss damage is weird most of the times: Slow predictable moves deal no damage while fast unpredictable moves one shot you. It shouldn’t be like that.

I love most of the trash mobs in this game because there are a lot of enemies that stand out in the crowd and as the game progresses you learn to assess them and dodge the right moves. While there are exceptions, most of them are pretty well done. But I can’t say the same about the bosses. As much as I love the bosses in POE2, after 600 hours of play, I noticed a few bosses that just aren’t fair or well designed

I gathered most of my experiences on a really tanky Warbringer build, so maybe my experience could be different compared to glass cannons that just die to every arrow in the game so please be gentle

I think the boss damage itself is adequate most of the time, but I have a problem with the allocation of the damage of the different abilities of the bosses. In my opinion, the moves that can do the most damage should be those that can be most easily predicted and prevented. But at the moment it’s the other way around. To clarify the point, a few good and a few bad examples:

Examples for good allocation:

The Executioner - his guilotine move and his fire-explosion-axe swing possibly one shots you, its an animation with an audio signal, a visible area and a quote that warns you. These moves should deal a lot of damage

Count Geonor - his disappearing and re-appearing in the air do drop down follows the same principle: Audio Clue, visible area, a lot of damage as it should be

Draven, the eternal Praetor: His usual swings dont hurt as much as the long Swing to the ground with projectiles to the side - good

Zalmarath, the Living Colossus: The Move where he rams his blade in the middle of the arena to blast the whole place is a predictable move and therefore it should punish you if he hits you with it

Jamanra, the Abomination: In his first phase he summons the Winds, that obviously should hurt you - as it does. In his second phase he summons tornadoes and portals that shoot a lot of fire projectiles - they hurt you a lot as well while his other moves hurt, but not so much

Examples for bad allocation:

Rudja, the Dread Engineer: She has a move, where she just runs at you really fast and punches you with her weapon. Why does this move deal the most damage? Its the hardest to avoid - but not as bad as some examples below

Tor Gul, the Defiler: He has a move in which he just slaps his open hand on the ground. This move takes him maybe half a second, there is no announcement, no audio clue but it is the only move that can one shot me. Why should it be this one and not the other ones with a longer animation and obviously more power put into?

Lachlann of Endless Lament: He has a lot of predictable moves that he announces. But the move that deals the most damage is the one where he just slaps his weapon in front of him and then behind him. To be fair: he announces it, but once he started to announce it he already killed everything in front of him. You have to react in 0.3 seconds. Why does this specific move deal so much damage and not his other abilities that are way easier to dodge?

Mighty Silverfist: A different kind of problem. His Slam of the pillar deals a LOT of damage. The Move where he swings the pillar three times into the slam onto the ground should deal a lot of damage. His simple slam onto the ground shouldnt.

The Rust King: Nothing of it really hurts, not even the moves that get announced or that have a long animation. They SHOULD hurt, but they just dont. There are a lot of bosses that have cool moves, even some that are predictable, but none of these moves really hurt - like they should. For example the mentioned rust king or the wandering druid.

Special Case: Viper Napuatzi. This girl made me angry a LOT. I like every move but her flying into bombardement of the map. It feels extremely random and punishes a lot harder then it should. Im not sure if this is something that changes if you play alone, but me and my buddy cant find a save spot sometimes. The timing of the rune-explosions are a little bit off the animation and there is no pattern to guarantee you to at leat have a save spot from it. Kinda annoying

Overall i like most of the bosses are good designed and they certainly are a fun challenge but they have to finetune some numbers in my opinion. Please send me home with a blow if I deserve it. But standing in front of a boss as a warrior feels extremely bad as described.

Now I’m also interested in your opinion, have you had similar experiences?
