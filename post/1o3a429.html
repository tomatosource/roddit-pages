I think there is a bug of maps that were generated after the latest patch 0.3.1 or 0.3.1b.

I tested several maps now that were generated before 0.3.1 and Maps after and the discrepency cant be explained with simple RNG. The maps had very similar Quantity, Rarity, Rare Monster Numbers and Pack Size. And the older Maps definitely drop more stuff. I cant explain it with anything else than either a bug or its intended by GGG.
