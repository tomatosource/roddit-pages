Where does Giannis’ MVP-DPOY season (2019-20) rank historically?

Giannis is only one of three players with imo the ultimate individual honor of winning the MVP and DPOY in the same season, joining the legendary MJ and Olajuwon. This most of us know. But I’m not too sure where to place it historically.

I’m a Giannis fan myself and I have no problem calling it one of the greatest performances of all time. To reiterate: it’s my personal view that MVP + DPOY - both, not either or - is an amazing feat and much more significant than regular MVP awards.

Having said that:

  1. It happened during the shortened COVID season, which arguably reduced the level of competition and challenge.

  2. The subsequent postseason was highly disappointing and he missed some critical games vs the Heat due to injury. If we call it one of the best regular seasons of all time, necessarily the playoffs would be one of the worst upsets of all time.

Just a bit of thought exercise as the season winds up again. Happy to hear your thoughts and learn a few things~
