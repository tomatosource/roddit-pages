Seven years later, the Phoenix Suns finally landed Dillon (not MarShon) Brooks

https://www.nba.com/news/fallout-bizarre-trevor-ariza-dillon-marshon-brooks-trade

In 2018, the Suns, Grizzlies, and Wizards agreed on a three-way trade that would have sent Trevor Ariza to the Wizards, Kelly Oubre Jr. to the Grizzlies, and Austin Rivers and Brooks to the Suns.

The trade, however, fell through due to miscommunication between the Grizzlies and Suns over which Brooks they were trading. Grizzlies thought they were trading MarShon Brooks. Suns thought that they were getting Dillon.

Congratulations to the Suns for finally getting their man.
