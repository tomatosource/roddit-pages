Roller Coaster Of A Season

I thought we were going to be a play-in team. I didn’t think JB had this in him, and Neemias was just a third-string center. Even though I didn’t have high expectations, I still watched all the preseason games and our first two losses, and for me that was it.

I thought we’d be worse than a play-in team. Of course, it was just a two-game sample size, there was no way we’d lose three in a row… then I searched “Celtics” on Google, and BOOM, we lost to the Pistons, who are somehow the number one seed now. That’s when I thought I’d have a full Celtics withdrawal and just deal with it.

Then my birthday, the 22nd vs the Pacers, I figured, why not treat myself and watch a game. JB drops 31, and that Spanish rookie had me like, “Wow, he even looks like my friend andhis name is Nikko”

Fast forward after New Year, my YouTube feed is full of Celtics highlights. We’re 21–9 if you take out the first 3 big Ls (21–12 overall now). Then JB absolutely cooked the Clippers, and from there I was locked in and haven’t missed a game since.

On the eye test, I don’t buy the on/off numbers for Jaylen. It just doesn’t feel like that.

Now that JT is back, I predicted he’d average 18/10/6, and I’m almost right. Back-to-back triple-doubles (almost, curse you Payton 😂).

JT is already like 85–90% back. His body might not look 100%, but his basketball IQ is much better than last year. I had the same feeling heading into the 2024 playoffs when we won it all.

We’re winning this whole thing. JB and JT are going to cement themselves as the guys. We won before with vets who’ve done it before, like Jrue, now they’re winning it as vets with a younger supporting cast.

JT will finally get his MVP and just pick up where he left. 4-2 victory at home againts the Thunders.
