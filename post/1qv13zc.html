Rolled a Rampage bear this league.

Got some luck crafting items this league and realized I wanted to hit a perfect maximum spirit achievable on a scepter. After hundreds of failed attempts where I never even managed to hit 250+, tonight I’ve hit this. Many thanks to many members of global 6666 for witnessing my journey to this monstrosity.

I present you - Sorrow Smasher. Crafting process as per rules:

  1. Grab ilvl82, 2 socketed scepter on trade
  2. Roll with aug / perfect annuls until t1 spirit and a usable suffix (in case you brick). Usable suffixes are mana regen, reduced attributes or presence area of effect.
  3. Regal and prefix desecrate, don’t reveal.
  4. Divine t1 mod to 65%.
  5. Fracture hoping to hit 65% spirit . If you fail, try to unveil t1/2/3 hybrid on prefix, slam perfect exalts, vaal and sell your brick.
  6. If you managed to hit 65%, roll with greater chaos orbs until t1 hybrid.
  7. Vision craftSlam other mods.
  8. Vaal, get a socket
  9. Orb, hit perfect 25% spirit.

PS: 258 is a total overkill on a rampage druid as you get enough spirit for herald of ice + fire with basic scepter with some rolls and I’ve found Charge Regulation not necessary.
