Big shoutout to Kamil, the music in PoE2 is incredible

The music in this game is on another level.

Usually I end up turning off music in most games pretty much as soon as I open the settings, aside from Souls games and such.

With PoE2 though, I’ve played the entire game so far with the music on and it’s been amazing. Every track fits the tone of the area or fight perfectly and makes the whole experience feel even more immersive. The Kingsmarch music is my favorite and I even listen to it while working.

Huge respect to Kamil and the audio team.
