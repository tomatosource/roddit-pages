New node places in the upper half of the tree visualized

Because I am already planning theoretical builds on far too little information, I checked out and compared the images we have of the new passive tree and coloured all the new nodes green to see and perhaps even identify.

I am happy to report that I have identified I think five new Minion related clusters. More if I’d count those three at the top middle individually. (They seem to be minion nodes split by elemental types.)

In any case while I did not do the whole tree, I think it is very interesting to just get a feel for how much new nodes we are getting all over. It is really quite something.

  • Green = Completely new
  • Purple = Slightly moved in a way that matters for node travel /access
