Shohei Ohtani is the 12th player in MLB history to hit 3 home runs in a single playoff game

The list is now Babe Ruth, Bob Robertson, Reggie Jackson, George Brett, Adam Kennedy, Adrian Beltre, Albert Pujols, Pablo Sandoval, Jose Altuve, Kike Hernandez, Chris Taylor, and Shohei Ohtani. The Dodgers have the last 3 such games with Taylor doing it in 2021, and Hernandez in 2017, and now have the most players to have done it.
