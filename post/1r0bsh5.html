“Matching” the Zubac/JJJ offers

Setting aside the tax/aprons, it’s still tough to see how the Cs might have matched IND’s offer for Zubac, or UTA’s offer for JJJ -even before they acquired Vuc.

LAC receives: IND’s 2026 first-round pick, protected from picks Nos. 1-4 and 10-30 and a 2029 unprotected Pacers first-rounder. If the pick in 2026 does not convey it converts to an unprotected first in 2031. Assuming it doesn’t convey, the Celtics simply don’t have two unprotected picks available past 2027. Further out is less projectable/offers higher potential upside. IND also traded Mathurin, who needs to get paid but is good. Had the Cs offered 2026 1st + NOP 2nd and 2031 unprotected plus Hauser, I think it falls short. Add Gonzalez and LAC have something to think about, but they still go with the IND offer, I believe.

MEM receives: most favorable of Utah’s, Minnesota’s and Cleveland’s pick in 2027, the Lakers’ 2027 pick (top-four protected) and Phoenix’s 2031 first, and a few players go each way. The NOP 2nd, the Cs 2027 1st, the Cs 2031 first doesn’t top that offer. Again, add Gonzalez, maybe add Hauser (or move him for a 1st to add to the deal), it’s still probably not enough.

tl,dr - they don’t have the assets to top those offers.

There will be other, lesser bigs available in the off-season. Ideally, the Cs grab one - hopefully a lesser player but a better fit - who costs less. And costs something BOS can pony up.
