We should see a list of recent sales, not current offers in the currency exchange

Let’s use a simple example, Faded Crisis Fragment

Now if I want to sell one, I see this:

https://i.redd.it/d2sww37l8dcf1.gif

I want to buy one, I see this:

https://i.redd.it/y6a2k11q8dcf1.gif

So that’s a ratio of 3:1.

So what I am seeing are actual offers for the sales.

And here are all the offers:

https://i.redd.it/4p89z5xy9dcf1.gif

So we can see that price fixing is endemic, FCF are worth more than that, and for sure people will sell at a lower price than they should.

This either needs to be an auction system or:

What I should see here instead is previous actual sales of that item, along with the current listings.

By ONLY showing current listings, the system encourages under-pricing. Isn’t the whole point of the exchange to minimise that?

Yes, of course, careful research of the BUY listings and the SELL listings will reveal the fact that there is under-price fixing at both ends.

So, what is a FCF worth?

I dunno? maybe 30? I am encouraged to see at 35, and to buy at 13, but gee golly, would seeing that the last 10 sales were all around 32 chaos be a simpler, better idea?

Sure, very beginning of the season we can’t do this, but that happens regardless, you’ll simply see “no stock” anyway, replace that with “no sales history” instead
