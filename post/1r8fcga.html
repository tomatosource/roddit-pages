If March 1 is Tatum’s Return

I don’t know any better than anyone else does, of course. It still isn’t a guarantee that he returns at all. But let’s hypothesize that it is March 1, because NBC has changed their schedule.

That is 11 days from now. What would be the signs we would be seeing (other than the obvious injury report of him as “available” the day before) that would serve as confirmers that he is on this timetable? What would be the signs (again, other than the injury report the day before) that he is NOT on this timetable?

Do we have a sense of the remaining thresholds for strength and basketball activity, beyond just “he needs to go 5-on-5 unscripted at least once”? If so, when would this have to happen, and do we predict that it would be public news, or just a surprise one day that he is cleared? The team is out West until 2 days before February 1st–are there team practices during this stretch that could serve as Tatum’s 5-on-5 unscripted? Is there a chance that Tatum doesn’t go on this trip or leaves in the middle, so that he has more time with the Maine team?
