300 hours in: endgame is fun, but the setup loop is exhausting

After around 300 hours, I still enjoy PoE2, but the endgame mapping loop feels too setup-heavy.

  1. Right now, the optimal strategy feels too narrow:
  2. Find a city biome.
  3. Use a Grand Mirror effect.
  4. Push the area to 200% Delirious.
  5. Search for the right tablets and maps.
  6. Run a few maps.
  7. Repeat.

It feels similar to the old 3-tower triangulation system, just hidden behind a different process.

The main issue is that city biomes feel like the best option for almost everything: Abyss, Breach, Ritual, etc. With Atlas bonuses, four tablets, and master effects from Jado/Doryani, cities seem to scale much harder than other setups.

The preparation also has too much friction. You need regex for tablets, regex for maps, stash searching, trade searching, Atlas searching, and bookmarks. At some point, it feels like I spend more time preparing content than actually playing it.

Atlas search also needs work. It only seems to search visible or loaded areas, which makes it hard to track cities, Grand Mirrors, ancient modifiers, or future farming zones. On top of that, 20 bookmarks is nowhere near enough after hundreds of hours. I would love to have hundreds of pins, or at least a much higher limit.

The reward gap is also huge. In my experience:

  • Normal maps: around 0–1 divines per map.
  • 200% Delirious maps: around 3–8 divines per map.

Party play seems even stronger. With one friend, we were getting around 10–20 divines per map, so I wonder how high this scales in a full party.

TL;DR:

Endgame is rewarding, but the best strategy feels too centralized around city biomes, Grand Mirrors, tablets, and 200% Delirium.

The game would feel much better with less setup friction, better Atlas search, more bookmarks, and more viable alternatives to the current city-biome strategy.

##########

Edit: Thanks for all the discussion. Just to clarify, my point is not that endgame rewards are bad. The rewards are actually very strong.

The issue is the ratio between setup time and actual gameplay. City biomes, Grand Mirrors, tablets, Delirium prep, regex, bookmarks and Atlas searching all stack into a loop where the optimal strategy can feel more administrative than fun.

The goal is not to nerf high-end farming. The goal is to make accessing and preparing it feel less exhausting, while also giving players more viable alternatives to the current city-biome setup.
