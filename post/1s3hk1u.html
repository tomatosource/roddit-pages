Tatum’s role

I know we aren’t supposed to be expecting much from a guy who tore his Achilles less than a year ago, and I’m obviously still happy he’s back. But with all the discourse about his struggles, I’m just wondering why aren’t we using him as a point forward more? He seemed super capable in the Washington game where he had 7 assists and not terrible shooting, for instance, and it’s not like he’s turning the ball over much.

I don’t know, expecting Tatum to play the same instead of further exploring what I believe has been an under-explored part of his game even before the injury, which is his playmaking. I think it’s kinda crazy that he’s having games going 3-15 from the field alongside (and this is the way worse part imo) 0 assists.

Also will add that him having 8.9 rebounds per game in his return is genuinely extremely impressive. That was something I thought would take a major hit, I was expecting maybe 7 rebounds per game at best coming back.

I don’t know. Thoughts?
