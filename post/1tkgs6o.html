Another Problem for Melee is the lack of “Chase-Affixes” on gear.

This is something that frustrates me the most when it comes to Melee.

When you look at the Affix Pool of Melee weapons or overall Item Affixes for Melee playstyles, there is a big lack of chase-Affixes on gear that are specific to Melee.

With Projectiles, You can find % Surpassing chance to fire an additional Arrow, Projectile Speed, Pierce, Chain, Fork, chance for more projectiles while moving, Bonus gained from Quivers.

There are lots of different ways to scale Clearspeed, Single Target Damage, QOL. You can actually choose from different Affix Pools to uniquely scale your build/skill.

Then you look at Melee Weapons and their Affix Pool and you just get stuff like stun buildup, stun duration and… well thats kinda of it.

The Desecrated Modpool offers at least some interesting Modifiers, but IMO these should be much more available through the basic affix pool and also on other items, but there should also be alot more.

Why cant I get AOE on a weapon, or Chance for Aftershock/Fissures, Where is Chain/Fork on Melee weapons when there are Melee skills that create Projectiles. Why is there no surpassing chance for more Projectiles on Melee Weapons? Where are War Cry modifiers like War Cry CDR etc?

Where are literally any Melee specific Affixes in the basic Affix Pool that are unique to melee weapons that allow for more interesting and unique ways to scale a build?

I understand that each Archtype should also come with their own pool of affixes. But melee feels so bland and straight forward (just get damage, duh), where as other archtypes have so many tools to play with.
