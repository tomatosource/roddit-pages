I started a fresh SSF journey in PoE2 after playing PoE1 again for some time

After me and my friends were done with our first foray into PoE2 we started a private league in PoE1 and played for a couple of weeks, and also Legacy of Phrecia for a week or so. Contentwise, nothing beats PoE1.

But then I started a fresh Ranger playthrough in PoE2 last night and I had to force myself to stop playing and go to bed. PoE2 is just such a massively superior game in its early game and gameplay feel. When I boot up PoE1 after a long break I get a dozen stutters in the first zone and the early gameplay is as clunky as it’s ever been. But PoE2 is just so smooth. No stutters, awesome gameplay, progression feels meaningful right from level 1.

My feelings toward PoE2 were a little harsh in the beginning, but my expectations were sky high too. Now after going back and forth between the two games it is clear they have the start of something special in PoE2. All I can hope for is they keep innovating with cool systems that make PoE2 an even more unique game.
