Map and tower mechanic is not fun

There, I said it…

Nobody I know likes this mechanic, the difficulty in finding +1 level now, the time wasted with spamming countless maps, just needs a full rework.

The entire mechanic just isn’t fun.

Delirium juicing, that’s okay and deterministic.

My simple proposal. Link map difficulty and rarity.

More damage and harder map, more rarity and quant.

More exalt slams & vaals, more modifiers which increase both somewhat deterministically.

Add in t16 maps as recombination for base, vaal to t17 (remove the stupid random +1 to maps which is so rare, it’s pointless).

Give us some semi deterministic way of working maps and boosting without the excessive rng. This game has enough RNG with loot, “crafting”, corruption, boss drops & everything else.

We don’t need the main ‘end game system’ to also have full rng mechanics.

Towers should be flavour, not part of the map setup mechanic, where you setup the type of maps you want - not included loot boosts.

So towers are to buff and improve rituals, bosses, breach or delerium - not base rarity or quantity. Those are tired to character rarity and map rarity.
