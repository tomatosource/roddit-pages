Before 1.0 PoE2 melee needs to feel like weapon combat, not spellcasting with extra steps.

I’m loving what they cooked with 0.5, but I still feel there is a way to go when it comes to core gameplay design.

And, as the title says, I’m mainly talking about melee. I think one of the core issues with melee in PoE2 is that a lot of melee skills don’t really feel like melee skills. They often feel more like spells that happen to require a specific weapon type.

That might sound like a small distinction, but I think it matters a lot for gameplay and build diversity. When a melee skill is basically a fixed package of animation, weapon requirement, element, damage profile and archetype, it ends up locking the player into a very narrow identity. The weapon becomes less of a weapon and more of a casting focus for a predefined “melee spell”.

This also makes many melee skills feel less “melée” than they should. Instead of building around the physical identity of a weapon, you are often building around a skill fantasy that has already been mostly decided for you. A mace skill is not just a mace attack; it is often a specific archetype with a specific effect attached to it. Same for spears and it will be the same, I guess, for the rest of the mele rooster. The result is that melee feels constrained, not just mechanically, but creatively.

By contrast, I think crossbows are much closer to what a better system could look like. Crossbows have a more interesting distinction between types of attacks, like bolts and grenades, with skill effects layered on top. The weapon type provides a mechanical base, and the skills modify or expand that base. That feels more flexible and more distinctive.

Melee could bénéfit from the same approach.

Instead of having melee skills function as self-contained “spells”, weapons could have basic attack categories tied to their physical nature. For example:

Maces could focus on crushing attacks. Swords could have thrusting and slashing attacks. Spears could have thrusting, sweeping or throwing attacks. Axes could focus on chopping and heavy cutting attacks.

Then, each weapon type and sub-type could have latent effects associated with those attack categories. Maces could naturally interact with armour break or stun. Axes could lean into bleeding or heavy wounds. Spears could interact with armour penetration, reach, impale-like mechanics or projectile conversion when thrown. Swords could be more versatile, with access to both cutting and thrusting interactions.

On top of that, a thrd layer could come from the weapon itself: implicits, special modifiers, or unique item effects. A particular mace could change how crushing attacks behave. A unique spear could ad fire conversion to thrown attacks. A sword implicit could make thrusting attacks interact differently with armour or critical hits.

Then the actual skill gems would come in as modifiers or archetype layers, rather than completely defining the attack from the start. This is where the current fantasy elements could still exist: elemental effects, chaos, poison, explosions, shockwaves, frost, fire, etc. But they would be layered on top of a more grounded weapon/attack system.

So you could have something like an earthquake-style mace attack, but with a freezing effect depending on the skill and weapon. Or a thrown spear attack that creates a fire explosion. Or an axe-based slashing attack that applies poison or chaos damage depending on the build. The important part is that the mele identity would come first, and the fantasy effect would come second. The idea would be that skills gem have a defining add-on effect that can be applied to any melee weapons. I feel bow should also have this kind of system.

To me, this would make melee feel more like melee again. It would also open up build freedom, because players would be combining weapon behaviour, attack type, item implicits and skill effects, instead of choosing from a list of mostly predefined melee-spell archetypes.

A lot of people say melee in PoE2 feels weak, clunky or not engaging enough. I think part of the reason is that it often does not feel like true weapon-based combat. It feels like Diablo 4-style melee, where many attacks are basically spells with a sword or mace animation attached.

PoE2 has a chance to do something much more interesting. Melee weapons could have their own mechanical identity, and skills could enhance that identity instead of replacing it. What do you think about that Exiles ?

On a first afterthought, it could become an horror to create and balance, so maybe subtype could pe scrapped for just some specific implicit per item type.

TL:DR : melee should work as the crossbow, base archetypal attacks with innate effects tied to the item type, and add a layer of classical skill gem tropes : elemental attack/effect, chaos,bleed explosion etc…
