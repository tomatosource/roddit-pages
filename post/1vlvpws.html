Which players currently with a team are closest to entering the team’s top 12 all time players on Basketball Reference (measured by Win Shares)? Which players are realistically approaching all time best player?

Each team page on Basketball Reference has a list of headshots for the “All-Time Top 12 Players” as measured by the Win Shares they had with the team. Regardless of opinions on the accuracy in rating them, I was just genuinely curious how close some players are to actually getting on that page for each team. And who is actually on a path to be the best player for each team (by win shares).

Example, Jayson Tatum is not on the list currently for the Celtics. But if you pull the all time Win Shares for each team (which I did via Stathead), he’s actually 13th right now and a few games away from having his face on the list for the foreseeable future. Which makes sense, Tatum is clearly one of the greatest Celtics of all time.

Couple notes:

  • The Thunder page currently mixes Thunder and SuperSonics. I only measured after the relocation because I was curious how close Shai is to the top player. For the sake of this chart, KD is the all time leader and loses his rookie season. On Basketball Reference, Gary Payton is the leader.

  • For the players that “have a chance” at being the best, I’m just looking at guys decently high in the top 12. Yes, Caleb Wilson can hypothetically beat Michael Jordan’s all time win shares for the Bulls. I’m not including those types of choices.

  • “In range” of the top spot is a relative term. If you’re 8th place you’re in range, but the leader might have an insane gap.

  • This list is only people who are currently still with the team. For example, Mitchell Robinson is actually 15th for the Knicks right now. Decent chance he would have made the list (if Brunson didn’t bump him off), but he’s not on the Knicks anymore. So move down to the next closest player (in this case, Brunson).
