Pacers vs. Thunder last 4 matchups

Before you downvote and berate me, I understand that not every player on both teams played in each of the previous four games. But the principle players as well the fundamental nature of the two teams have been consistent over those four games, IMO.

The two teams have split the last 4 matchups. Each team has gone 1-1 at home. The margin is less than 1 point per game, with the Thunder outscoring the Pacers 475-472.

I think most interestingly, all four games have played out in eerily similar fashion. In each of the four games, the Pacers have jumped out to double digit first half leads. OKC has fought back every time, twice coming all the way back to take control, and twice coming up short with Indiana pulling away late.

All of the games have been high scoring with neither team held under 111 in any game, and both averaging nearly 120 per game.

I have no idea if any of this will mean anything when the Finals start, but going off of recent history, this should be a fun, high scoring, competitive series… and I’m looking forward to it.
