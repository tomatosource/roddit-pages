## 🚀 Performance Fix Found for 0.3.1 (Multi-GPU Conflict)

Hello, fellow Exiles,

If you followed what was going on the week before the tower patch (0.3.1), it became apparent that I was suddenly struggling for performance, falling as low as 15 fps in my hideout—very unplayable.

-–

### **TL;DR (The Solution)**

* **The Problem:** It seems the game has trouble with multi-GPU setups.

* **The Fix:** I removed my secondary GPU (an Intel Arc A310 Eco used strictly for encoding) and used DDU to clean up the system.

* **Action for Others:** If you don’t have a second GPU to remove, look for onboard graphics, deactivate it/them, use DDU to remove all traces of any other GPU from your system, and hopefully enjoy the performance you had before 0.3.1 hit.

* **Result:** I am back to peak performance.

-–

### **Detailed Symptoms Before the Fix**

Before this, my high-end setup was “flatlining.” My GPU and CPU were barely being used, with sometimes a spike just over 20% for my CPU. My GPU never went over 55%, even with all game settings set to maximum quality.

* **VRAM/Cache:** The shader cache was **always loading**. Although I had 24GB of VRAM, the client never went above 3GB.

* **Usage:** CPU and GPU usage was constantly fluctuating between 20 and 50%.

* **Time Metrics:** In-game, my CPU time never got under 6ms—mostly 12 to 20ms! Same fluctuation for the GPU.

* **Temporary Fixes:** I tried reinstalling the game, fine-tuning settings, and DDU on everything. At one point, unplugging my second monitor worked for a time, but it was not very pleasant.

* **Streaming Context:** I could stream smoothly before, so I blocked all system updates when things worked. The only thing that changed was around the time of that patch, and I assume it was on GGG’s side.

### **The Confirmed Fix & Post-Metrics**

Today, as I was shifting priorities and decided to stop streaming, I removed my Arc GPU, did a cleanup with DDU, and just decided to launch a map for fun, just to see… and:

* Shaders were not loading forever; sometimes just a few here and there in maps.

* Gameplay was buttery smooth again (no sluggish client/input delay leading to frustrating deaths, even when immune to stun/freeze).

**Post-Fix KPI:**

* **VRAM:** Almost double the amount is now used.

* **CPU Time:** Almost a quarter of what it used to be.

* **GPU Time:** Close to half of what it was.

From the OS point of view, my CPU, GPU, and memory are quite far from struggling—I have a big margin. I could be streaming or doing shenanigans again without fearing for stalls or crashes. (Keep in mind the screenshot relates the performance with max quality settings on again, and it is buttery smooth again.)

I can exclude software issues from my system, as I froze it before the start of the League and the second GPU was already here.
