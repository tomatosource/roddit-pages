I can’t see myself leveling another character, and act 5 and 6 will make it worse

Hi,

Patch 0.3 is great — don’t get me wrong. I really love the sprint function, the market, and the in-game trade site. The changes and the overall direction of the game are awesome.

I just finished Act 4 in about 8 hours of gameplay. I’m playing a Mercenary with a crossbow and some grenade skills (a build I came up with myself, not following anyone). Jamanra and the Act 4 bosses were really tough — each one took me at least 30 minutes to kill (multiple attempts). I often died because my mana flask ran out, and that was usually the end of the fight. Most of my attempts left the boss with less than 20% HP.

Act 4 itself is great, but it feels way too long. I honestly can’t see myself leveling another character this league, even with good leveling gear. The main campaign is fine, but only once per league.

For context:

  • In 0.1, I played Flicker Invoker.
  • In 0.2, I leveled an ED Witch and then played Chayula Flicker.
  • Now in 0.3, I’m trying Mercenary.

But I’m worried Mercenary might not be for me in the long run — not because of the gameplay itself, but because I just can’t bring myself to run through the campaign again (I didn’t even finish the three interludes).

And honestly, when GGG eventually introduces Acts 5 and 6, this problem will get even worse. I really think they should consider adding some kind of quick leveling system or campaign skip for alternative characters.

Do you have the same experience as me?
