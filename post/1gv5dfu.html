Antonio Daniels on injured stars playing in the Olympics: “I’ve seen Greg Popovich tell Tony Parker and Manu Ginobili ‘You’re not playing in the Olympics so you can be ready for preseason, training camp and the regular season.’”

Audio from NBA Sirius XM NBA Radio

“I’ve seen Greg Popovich tell Tony Parker and Manu Ginobili ‘You’re not playing in the Olympics so you can be ready for preseason, training camp and the regular season.’ I’ve seen it” …

“Should no star play for Team USA ever again? No, that’s not a discussion. Should stars that are injured going into the Olympics play for Team USA knowing that it could impact the start of their regular season? No they shouldn’t.”

Antonio Daniels is a former player who played with the San Antonio Spurs from 1998 to 2002.
