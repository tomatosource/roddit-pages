Remove skill level requirement while keeping the tutorial

I understand the intention of locking skills behind levels : it helps avoid overwhelming new players early on. But this approach significantly limits build flexibility during the campaign where you should be experimenting.

It also implicitly creates a hierarchy between skills, where those unlocked later tend to feel “stronger” or more important, which can constrain experimentation.

Removing level requirements would allow players to explore builds more freely from the start. It would also encourage better balance across all skills, especially since they are meant to serve as simple foundations for support gem interactions.

A good compromise could be to keep the current “skill selection by level” interface as a recommendation system, similar to how support gems are presented, guiding players without restricting their choices.

All in all, I feel like POE1 had something “more” about fun AND viable custom builds.
