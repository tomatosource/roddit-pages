I hope future league mechanics have an undo option / something to prevent “bricking” your progression

The temple will most likely get a change in 0.5, so let’s talk about league mechanics in general:

Whether you liked 0.4’s temple or not, I think we can all agree that the worst feeling it provided was the option to brick 100h hours of progression with 1 (!) singular, non-reversable click.

I don’t think any future league mechanic should ever create the situation where that effort- and time-heavy progression can be ruined by accident. ​

As a minimum, similar league mechanics (that require building/investing something) should always have an undo option. Or simply not offer any action that could totally brick it.
