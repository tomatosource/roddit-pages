How important is / can a single bench player be to a championship run?

Like when the star player finally sits, you think you have a chance, then this bench player comes out of nowhere and maintains the lead.

Obviously, minutes would play a part. Here are some examples.

Do the Warriors win in 2022 without Jordan Poole? Honestly, I don’t think so.

Or like a Harrison Barnes instead of Andrew Wiggins (not a bench player but I was shocked at how much he came through in the finals)

Alex Caruso for the Thunder this past year. Does he make or break the chip? Probably not. But I do think they may lose a couple more games if it weren’t for him. What do you guys think?

Payton Pritchard for the Celtics. I think he would make 0 difference to be honest.

Rondo for the Lakers. Honestly I think he had a bigger impact than thought. They lose a game or two without him

Shaun Livingston for the Warriors - I straight up don’t know if they win the chip without him

Nick Young for the Warriors - man I remember the Cavs bringing it real close and Nick Young hitting 2 back to back long threes. It was ridiculous!

What do you guys think?

Anyone I missed? Any bench player a team you think straight up would not win without? Any other surprisingly impactful bench players in the playoffs?
