The Celtics can still make the finals this season without JT

Obviously I’d love for him to return this season but if that isn’t how things turn out I still believe we can win the East this year.

Celtics are tied for 2nd place with the Knicks. Before this season started the projections had us being a play-in team. I know the East is weak but regardless of that fact this team is still exceeding expectations by a lot.

JB is playing like an MVP, Joe is probably having his best season as a coach in his career, the young guys know their roles and are developing well, PP has been doing his thing. White needs to pick it up offensively but aside from that I’d say this team is very good considering the fact that we lost our starting SG, SF, and both C’s.

We’ve made it this far without a legit starting C on the team. Queta has taken a huge leap from last season but I still think he isn’t a starting caliber C on a playoff team. I don’t see why the Celtics shouldn’t be buyers at this deadline regardless of whether or not JT comes back. Go out and trade for Zubac, Missi, or even JJJ if he’s available. The only thing that would change my mind about this is if the Knicks and/or Pistons traded for Giannis (depending on the price).

Celtics shouldn’t waste an MVP year from JB. Winning the East would be an incredible accomplishment for this team considering how “bad” NBA media said we were going to be last offseason and if we make it to the finals then anything can happen. Imagine buying at the deadline, making the finals, and still having JT waiting to return.

The Knicks seem like a worse team (especially defensively) compared to last season. The Pistons are a great team but they aren’t unbeatable. We lost to them by only 1 point despite White making 1 FG the entire game and our second leading scorer dropping less than 20 pts.

The only thing is if we made the finals or even just the ECF I truly don’t think Tatum would be willing to sit those games out. I think he’d force his way back.
