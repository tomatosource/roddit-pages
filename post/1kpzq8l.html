Armour: Do Something Already

Why not something simple? Just double the effects of all tree nodes. Instead of the salutary 12% per point bump it to 24%, doubling effect of EV/ES. That is a lot easier than tweaking effectiveness curve, as was done in league 1. That turned out to be an unintended stealth nerf as well. Mobs’ defense got buffed along with it, which impacted those on the left side of the tree who already need help. Simpler yet, leave tree values the same but double base Armour value on all gear.

What’s gonna happen? Everyone piles into the left side of the tree? Unlikely, players congregated on the right and top side because it’s easier to scale ES. Poe2ninja has lots of players in the 10k+ ES. It is hard to get above 4000 life unless you play a bloodmage. Sure, ES has a recharge timer, but life Regen doesn’t matter if you get one-shotted. One-shotting is a leading cause of death as it is.

There are several other ideas as well. How about letting armour defend against ground effect. Stack enough armour and you can live through burning ground maps. How about a keystone that buffs life but half all defenses?

It’s an EA, how bad could it be? In a way, league two already wrecks game balance to heck already? Around half players are using some form of spears or another. You want a tanky spearazon? Play Lich. You want to one shot bosses? Play BM rake or Deadeye WFS. Heck, even stat stacking merc is playing with a spear.
