Tired of softcore leagues where death is a minor inconvenience and trade chat looks like Wall Street bought on Wish?
Join Requiem_HC — a private hardcore league built on chaos, kindness, and questionable build choices.

What to Expect

  • Hardcore Mode “Death is permanent. Cowards respawn. Heroes do not.”
  • Teamwork Through Tragedy Two half-dead exiles still make one functioning party.
  • Experimental Builds We don’t follow meta — we chase it with a rusty dagger and a dream.
  • Lab Assistance Even if we hate traps. Especially if we hate traps.
  • Silent Trade Chat, Loud Discord We communicate in death screenshots, flask emojis, and character names.
  • Guild Stash of Regrets Take what you need. Leave what you forgot you picked up.
  • New Player Friendly Initiation involves telling your trauma story about THAT boss.
  • Comedy Over Caution We don’t ask “Can I survive this?” — we ask “Will it be funny if I don’t?”
  • No Flaming, Just Accountability Even if your friend chose no evasion in Sekhema while it’s your only defense.
  • Generosity & Camaraderie Come for the challenge. Stay for the camaraderie.

Our Vision

This is not a guild, but a private hardcore league focused on meaningful progression, survival, and a shared experience. Trade, compete, or cooperate – but do it where it matters.

Who We’re Looking For

  • 18+ active players
  • Preferably with Discord & willing to join voice chat
  • Hardcore enjoyers who want clean, fair, competitive play
  • No bots, no carries, no drama – just game
