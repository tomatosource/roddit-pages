I love the new mechanic hubs but they are a huge headache for bossing

I love the hubs, and I was really excited for them when Jonathan said you’d be able to jump into a hub and use your fragments back-to-back to farm bosses.

I assumed the flow would be: enter the hub, insert a fragment, kill the boss, return to the hub, insert another fragment, kill the boss again, and repeat.

That’s not how it currently works, and it makes farming bosses much more annoying than it needs to be.

Right now, you have to open the hub from your map device, enter the hub, insert a fragment, kill the boss, return to the hub, leave to your hideout, open the hub again, insert another fragment, and repeat the whole process.

This gets tiresome very quickly and is something that should be addressed/changed imo.
