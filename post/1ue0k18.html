With their 2nd trade back from #16, the Memphis Grizzlies acquired 5 additional 2nd round picks for moving back 5 spots

Since Shams didn’t share the full details, here it is for each transaction:

  • The Grizzlies traded the 16th pick to the OKC Thunder for the 17th pick and 2 future 2nd round picks; OKC use the 16th pick to select Bennett Stirtz
  • Subsequently, the Grizzlies traded the 17th pick to the Detroit Pistons for 3 future second round picks; Detroit used the 17th pick to select Ebuka Okorie
  • The 21st pick was used to select Karin Lopez

Memphis got as many seconds as spots they moved back in the draft along with Lopez.
