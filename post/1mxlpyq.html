Consume-gating makes skills feel bad - or why Fireball isn’t a skill anymore

In 0.3, as it stands, infusions are yet another source of an extra thing to do that gates skills.
Infusions continues the trend of skills being gated by something else to be interesting. And that interesting thing being a single use consume.
Admittedly, most of the infusion upgrades to skills are just supplementary - a bit of extra damage, a ground effect, a extra thing that can chain etc.. - with the exception of Fireball.

A brief history of Fireball in PoE2:

  • In 0.1, Fireball was a skill only unlocked in Act 3. It was a projectile that exploded on impact and split into multiple projectiles that shotgun. If supported with multiple projectiles, both the first projectile and the nova on impact got more projectiles. It was pretty awesome when combined with wildshard support. Great skill that cost a lot of mana to spend but you could cast away and felt good. Felt like a late game powerful skill worthy of being one of the later skills to unlock.
  • In 0.2, Fireball got nerfed, the nova’s projectile count is no longer modifiable and cannot shotgun. Still get a nova on impact, which made it okay for clearing. Bad for single target as damage was low in the first place.
  • In 0.3, Fireball no longer creates a nova on impact. It just fires one projectile. It’s the same spell as Firebolt, just with bigger damage numbers. (unless you have a fire infusion to consume!)

If you have a fire infusion, you can consume that infusion and, for one cast only, Fireball will go back to being the skill it was originally - nova on impact (fixed projectile count, though) and shotgunning.

The orignal skill has been gated by a single-use conditional.
The default is now a duplicate of Firebolt, a skill you get in the very first area that’s designed to be a basic attack spell and is supposed to be uninteresting.

I’m using Fireball as the main example here, but the problem exists on a bunch of skills, mainly fire and cold spells.

  • Living bomb: In 0,3 this spell literally does nothing until you kill the target already or use another skill to damage it enough. The skill is entirely gated by some other interaction. It is not a skill, it’s an effect.
  • Flameblast: a 15s cooldown spell that you have to channel for a few seconds (get stun it goes back on CD) and it centres from yourself unless you have a solar orb down. So its gated by a CD and by a solar orb being down somewhere. Only use when 15s has elasped and orb is on target.
  • Solar Orb: supplementary spell to ignite things real close and be a target for flameblast, not a main skill.
  • Ember Fusillade: an actual main skill!
  • Firebolt: an actual main skill, but has awful numbers and is replaced entirely by Fireball.
  • Incinerate: In 0,3 you literally cannot cast this without spending mana on other spells first (mana specifically; so any infernalists (the fire spell ascedancy) that go infernal flame to replace mana can’t ever use it).
  • Flame Wall: supplementary spell to add projectile damage and ignite things. Not a main skill, cast it and recast it at end of duration.
  • Ice Nova: useless damage, centres from self without astral projection support, bad aoe so needs aoe and/or cascade support - lose 2⁄3 links on a skill with bad numbers, great at freezing things though (freeze in one cast, requires another 3⁄4 casts to kill normal/magic monsters).
  • Cold Snap: literally cannot be cast unless the monster is frozen, (which it consumes), or there’s a frostbolt, which it consumes. If you use Cold Snap as main skill you never get to freeze enemies. In 0,3 its becoming Snap, which is still unusable unless the enemey has an ailment and it consumes that ailment to now do less damage. It makes a remnant though, so you can have another thing to consume!
  • Frostbolt: bad damage without the explosion at end of flight (snakepit unintended interaction) is just a target for cold snap.
  • Frost Bomb: Pre 0,3 you could get the CD and duration down enough to cast this at the end of its duration. In 0,3 it now has a 6s CD and worse damage, so can’t be a main skill.
  • Frost wall: Literally doesn’t do damage, or anything, until its hit by something else and destroyed. Not a skil, is an effect of another skill.
  • Comet: Huge damage, insanely long cast time that cant be shorted, never self-cast and is just used as the triggered spell in Cast-on-X builds.

So so many of the spells and skills in PoE2 feel like they are gated as actual skills by requiring other interactions.
A skill needs to be its own thing by default. Not some thing you get to do after doing something else.
Sure, add an effect to a skill that bumps the numbers, or puts a ground effect down, or makes it larger/faster if there’s a condition met, but conditions that get consumed for a single cast need to only slightly change a skill not define it.

As a stop-gap, I really wouldn’t mind if things like infusions/charge consumption, ailment consumption gave a buff that lasted for like 5s that powered up all the things that would benefit from the consumption - that at least means its not consume thing to use skill once.
Honestly though, I think thats a bandaid for this bad skill design.

Single-use consumptions of ailments/infusions/conditions on skills make the skills bad (or not even usable) without the condition.
The skill needs to exist, as an actual skill, without the conditions/consumptions. The conditions need to enhance it, not define it.

Please make Fireball an actual skill, please.
