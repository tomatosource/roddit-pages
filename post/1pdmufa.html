How many prime Barry Bonds clones would it take to make the 2025 Colorado Rockies WS Champions?

Rules:

  • Every Barry Bonds clone is from a different season (so no repeats of 73 homers or .609 OBP)

  • Barry Bonds does not magically know how to play any new positions on the diamond

  • Barry Bonds can not make the Rockies clubhouse morale any worse than it currently is, or get into fights with one another

  • We’re starting on opening day 2025, not skipping straight to the playoffs or theorizing a 7-game series against the Dodgers/Blue Jays. So fatigue/injury will come into play

For some context, the entirety of the 2025 Colorado Rockies roster accumulated a whopping -3 WAR depending on Fangraphs or BBRef. The Phillies led the league with 51 total WAR accumulated, and the Dodgers had 49 WAR. So going strictly by numbers, we would need at least the top 5 Barry Bonds seasons to catch up, although I expect the real number would be higher to account for defensive woes, injury, and the Rockies god-awful pitching

#EDIT: Food for thought, Barry Bonds batted 0.498 with a 1.774 OPS at Coors between 2002 and 2004.
