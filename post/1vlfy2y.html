The Opening Week of the 2026-27 NBA Regular Season will feature nine national games

The first four days of the 2026-27 NBA regular season will feature nine national games across NBC/Peacock, ESPN and Prime Video.

Tuesday, October 20th 2026 - Tip-off Tripleheader on NBC/Peacock

  • Boston Celtics @ Detroit Pistons at 3:00 PM ET on NBC/Peacock
  • Philadelphia 76ers @ New York Knicks at 7:00 PM ET on NBC/Peacock
  • Oklahoma City Thunder @ San Antonio Spurs at 9:30 PM ET on NBC/Peacock

Wednesday, October 21st 2026 - ESPN Opening Week Doubleheaders

  • Minnesota Timberwolves @ Miami Heat at 7:30 PM ET on ESPN
  • Golden State Warriors @ Los Angeles Lakers at 10:00 PM ET on ESPN

Thursday, October 22nd 2026 - ESPN Opening Week Doubleheaders

  • Cleveland Cavaliers @ Philadelphia 76ers at 7:30 PM ET on ESPN
  • Denver Nuggets @ Oklahoma City Thunder at 9:30 PM ET on ESPN

Friday, October 23rd 2026 - Prime Video First Games

  • New York Knicks @ Boston Celtics at 7:00 PM ET on Prime Video
  • Houston Rockets vs. San Antonio Spurs at 9:30 PM ET on Prime Video
    • HOU vs SAS will be at the Moody Center in Austin, TX.

Prime Video will present Friday night doubleheaders throughout the 2026-27 NBA regular season, beginning on October 23rd.
