New bug that potentially bricks your characters

Due to a recent change you have to pick up the first djinn barya from balbala and show it to Zarka in order to unlock The Trial Of Sekhemas.

Was leveling my twink characters with a buddy yesterday and we killed balbala and I said “hey we’ll just do this later let’s blast the campaign” so I picked up the quest item and immediately dropped it. We got to level 70 and decided to get our ascendency points now and tried teleporting to The Trial Of Sekhemas and are greeted with a messaging saying we don’t have the required quest state to enter.

Ive tried going back in cruel mode and in normal mode to kill her again but she does not drop the barya and the quest state says talk to Zarka and show her the barya in order to gain the ability to teleport there.

I still have Trial of Chaos ability to get my ascendency points but i’m locked out of Sekhema without switching characters so if you wanted to farm it goodluck.
