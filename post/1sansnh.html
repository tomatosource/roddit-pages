If Doc Rivers is a Hall of Famer, why isn’t Danny Ainge?

They had very comparable careers as players. Danny played longer and averaged more points. Doc averaged more assists. Both were all-stars one time. Of course Danny won two titles and Doc won zero.

Danny was a decent head coach for a short time before moving into the Celtics front office role, where he excelled. He built the ‘08 title team with Paul Pierce as his only meaningful asset, and then seamlessly built the team that would eventually win the ‘24 title. Loads of credit to Brad Stevens but Danny Ainge laid the foundation for that one.

Doc Rivers, on the other hand, was a good coach with a young Orlando team. He masterfully led the Ubuntu Celtics to one title and then lost a series to the Lakers that he should have won. After leaving the Celtics he has been disappointing - to be kind - pretty much everywhere. He blew multiple 3-1 playoff leads with the Clippers and was pretty bad with the Sixers and now the Bucks.

So what am I missing? Why has Doc Rivers earned a Hall of Fame nod but Danny Ainge hasn’t?
