The best upgrades in PoE2 aren’t always the ones that give me the biggest damage increase

I’ve noticed that the gear upgrades I remember most aren’t usually the ones where a number simply gets bigger.

It’s the item that suddenly lets me solve a problem my character has been dealing with for hours.

Maybe I can finally stop playing terrified around a certain type of encounter. Maybe I have enough sustain that I can stay aggressive instead of constantly backing off. Maybe one stat fixes something awkward enough that I can change another piece of gear and the whole build starts fitting together differently.

Those moments feel much better than replacing a weapon and watching my damage go up by 12%.

I think that’s one reason gearing is at its best when your character still has obvious weaknesses. Every drop has a chance to change how you actually play instead of just making the same playstyle more efficient.

Once I’m only comparing slightly better versions of things I already have, I lose some of that excitement.

Anyone else value the “this fixes my character” upgrades more than the pure damage ones?
