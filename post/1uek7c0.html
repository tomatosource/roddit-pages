Feedback about Endgame

Since this is the endgame patch, I wanted to provide specific feedback about the endgame.

What is everyone else’s thoughts on the endgame specifically?

  • Having to hunt for grand mirrors to get the 200% deli bonus is effectively the same as tower searching, but even more frustrating since there’s literally zero agency.
  • Combine that with how cities are the best layouts for almost every farm and it creates massive FOMO
  • Rolling/Buying tablets is extremely frustrating compared to scarabs from poe1 (and i’ve barely played poe1). I just want to apply the bonuses I want without having to chaos spam or spend a bunch of gold buying them from others. Too much time is spent outside maps.
  • Gold prices this league feel way worse. I’m constantly running out if I do even just a small amount of crafting/trading currencies,etc. I feel like if i’ve done hundreds of maps, I should effectively have infinite gold at this point.
  • Towers without the tablet feel really bad and unrewarding.
  • The atlas masters are really fun, we just need more of them with more variety.
  • 1 or even 2 portal maps feels really bad without a death recap. I died on 3 maps in a row in ritual because of some ranged green boss ability, but have no idea what it is or how I can mitigate it. I personally feel like death recap will likely resolve the 1 portal issue, but it’s still very punishing. If the map is going to kill me, having 6 lives vs 1 isn’t really going to make a difference
  • Liquidating drops is really expensive gold wise and not having a tool like wealthyexile means I I generally only liquidate really expensive stuff and have no idea how much my tabs are worth.
  • Endgame loot drops still feel terrible. I’ve picked up hundreds of T5 rare items and I sold 1 of them for 5 div, that was my best drop. T5 magic is fine since some of the bases are very valuable.
  • Rarity on gear as a mandatory stat still feels bad and means i’m either dropping defense or offense just so my drops don’t suck. 0 rarity is better this league, but still 100 rarity is far superior
  • Why do chill and temporal chains still exist as waystone mods?
  • Dying to a Boss shouldn’t remove all other content. The original reason for making this change no longer applies IMO (since completing a map before required killing all the rares). Monsters in maps should not reset/reroll if you die.
  • Failing a map and having to rerun it feels ultra bad when the loot is gone. I think taking away unique mods on the map is fair, (shouldn’t be able to repeat farm gold maps for example), but I should still be able to apply tablets). My guess is that this would allow players to pick one layout and infinitely farm it. I’m sure there’s a solution that works.
  • Knowledge is power is fine as long as all the information about the game can be found in the game. I really hate that I have to use external sites to discover hidden information about the game.
  • Performance is still probably the #1 issue most people face in the endgame.
