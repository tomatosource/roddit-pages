Composure is what really impresses with these Pacers.

Reasons why Pacers are 2-1: Haliburton, defense, ball movement, strong bench…all very much talked about. But I’m just in awe of how calm and composed they are, no matter what happens. Now I can’t say I remember every single moment in the finals so far, but this is like the least frustrated/emotional finals team I’ve seen in years. From the starters to the bench, no fuss, no complaints, work the ball, chase the lead, focus on the next possession or the next game, W or L.

I guess what I’m saying is, Pacers’ mental conditioning is no joke. Credit ro Carlise and the veterans in the team like Siakam and Turner for setting the tone. Composure enables perseverance and maybe that’s a considerable part of why Pacers are ahead against a team that is objectively better.
