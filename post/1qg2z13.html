Trying my own build experiment

I don’t really make my own builds. Don’t have the time or knowledge for it. However, I got a bit tired of stomping with my bear, so thought I’d try something.

I wanted to make a tanky, melee Witchhunter, ideally using bleed. I soon found out things are just… missing from the game. Obviously, weapons, and thematically, it makes more sense to have bleed builds using sharp weapons, and maces are more for stunning. Regardless, I pushed on.

My current build is lvl 52 and I’m having a great time. Had to switch to a spear and use Rake, but it still fits my initial idea. I’ve got a massively inefficient, convoluted build: I’m using pin to generate frenzy charges, while also immobilizing everything, getting more damage on immobilized enemies. Using leap slam to stunning groups, generating endurance charges. Herald of blood and rake gives great clear. Also quickly breaking armour of everything.

However, on bosses I struggle. My bleed doesn’t hit hard enough. Luckily, I’m somewhat tanky already, so I manage working through the mechanics, but it is not quite comfortable. I more and more realize I probably have to flip the entire build, ditch pin and everything associated with it, no more frenzy charges, just go for a ton of crit and spear damage, and let rake do all the work. No more fun interactions, just boost one skill to the max and let that do all the work…

I know, that is how it has always been, and sometimes combos are annoying to use for sure, but still…I was having solid fun working through the problems, using different mechanics etc. only to now go back to basic crit stacking. Or, I ditch bleed altogether and use basic hammer skills, but I just don’t like that playstyle.

No regrets there, I had my fun and this was just a cool experiment for me, but I am a bit sad that I’ll end up basic crit-stacking again.
