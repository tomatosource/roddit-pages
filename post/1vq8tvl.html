In 2013 Steph Curry led the Warriors past a 57 win Nuggets team with their All Star David Lee missing the series due to injuiry. In game 1 of the following round Curry drops 44 and 11 assists against a top 3 defense featuring Kawhi Leonard and Tim Duncan.

His performance against the Spurs was the most points any player scored against the Spurs in the entirety of the 2013 playoffs. With Lebron coming in second with his 37 point game performance against the Spurs in the finals.
