Current State of Endgame League Mechanics

TLDR: Delirium feels good, Expedition feels good, Ritual feels bug-I mean good, Breach is dead

I would like to preface this by saying this is just my opinion as someone who has put about 1200 hours into POE2 across several level 80-98s in 0.1 and a level 85ish in 0.2. A lot of the community is outraged due to several factors so I thought I’d take the time to offer some positive comments while also highlighting some issues.

Following Zizaran’s interview and the response post from GGG regarding upcoming changes in reference to Delirium, I feel like they missed a glaring league mechanic that needs to be retouched. Rather than dragging on some lengthy paragraph after paragraph post, I thought it better to provide a somewhat simple evaluation of the current endgame mechanics in their current (and post change) state.

Delirium: 9⁄10 (I can see clearly now/10)

  • Improved Visuals look fantastic. It still provides great balance of ambiance and clarity
  • Added monster density to maps is fantastic and makes it feel great to engage with
  • It was currently a bit too short in terms of duration which didn’t provide enough emotions
  • The proposed change to duration should fix any issue with this mechanic imo
  • Doesn’t require any additional time commitment when clearing maps
  • Splinters/Emotion drop rates seem fine overall. It should increase after change which is good

I would say that overall, after the upcoming change, Delirium is in an excellent state. I’m not sure if you are still unable to earn emotions if you use an instilled waystone on a Delirium map (was the case in prior league) but if so, this should be addressed to allow for increased splinter drops while still gaining emotions. Instilling serves a double duty between annointing and waystones which overall makes Delirium feel great to engage with.

Expedition: 8⁄10 (.7% chance/10)

  • Expedition feels much better to engage with/is more rewarding this time around
  • Currencies felt relatively useless previously due to shops being poor in terms of gear
  • Recombinator may have its flaws in calculations at times but gives expedition a clear purpose
  • It seems that overall monster density/spawns have been increased slightly
  • It does not require a large additional time commitment when clearing maps
  • Crafting/Shop currency drop rates seem fine but an increase of about 15% wouldn’t hurt.
  • The shops are still underwhelming and should offer higher tier base items as the players level increase

I would say that overall, with the potential of recombinators making expedition currency more relevant, Expedition seems to be in a good state. It wouldn’t hurt for minor increase in monsters and currency. Obviously the recombinator still needs some tweaks but I’d say I’m optimistic about it. The shops still need some improvement to make them feel better in terms of providing equipment upgrades late game.

Ritual: 8⁄10 (Bug/10)

  • The change of monster spawns being more gradual has made combat much less frantic and messy
  • It appears on average, the area given during Rituals has increased by quite a bit and that feels great
  • There are much less instances of getting stuck on terrain or obstacles due to lack of space
  • Some of the automated mechanics are a bit overtuned. (Volatile plants spawning orbs too fast, etc)
  • The change in monster spawn rates has increased the time commitment required significantly
  • The change in Omen availability/rates in shops feels great even if most of the time it’s cheap ones
  • The amount of tribute earned on average has increased and makes interacting with shop better
  • I can’t speak to Audience spawn rates, but hopefully they are more attainable without bugs

Ignoring obvious impact of the unintended interaction/bug abuse, Ritual seems to be in a good place overall. It feels more weighty and like a battle of attrition vs waves of unrelenting monsters. However, the time it takes to complete the 3⁄4 rituals can sometimes feel like too much for its rewards. I believe this needs some adjusting to reduce the completion time required by about 15% without changing the rate at which monsters spawn or the tribute earned.

Breach: 2⁄10 (Look how they massacred my boy/10)

  • Some changes appear to have been made visually and screen clarity feels better during Breach
  • My Breaches have about 5-10% the overall amount of monsters they did previously
  • My Breaches last about 10 seconds on average, which is not a large time commitment I guess
  • My Breaches spawn 1-2 hands, which contain nothing in them, which does not feel rewarding
  • I have earned on average, .25 splinters per Breach that I have engaged with. Xesht who?
  • I have not seen a single Breach Ring this patch. While I know they wanted less, this is awful.
  • The time before closing of a Breach has been greatly impacted by the decrease in monster density/fuel
  • Should adopt a Delirium style, all Splinters drop at the end when breach closes
  • Catalysts have been relatively non-existent for me

In my opinion, if they just reverted Breach to the exact way it was at the end of last league while keeping the increased visual clarity and toning down the Breach rings by 35% and Splinters by 10-15% from their values last league it would be perfectly in line with other league mechanics. As currently implemented, Breach feels terrible, its over before it even starts, has less monsters than Expedition does, and doesn’t feel rewarding at all. You get nothing at all from it most of the time. I don’t see how anyone engaging with endgame could look at Breach in its current state and think it feels good.

Quick rundown of the “random” in map events

Azmeri wisps don’t feel rewarding enough for the time commitment and the fact that the completely derail my intended progress around map layouts. 99% of the time it seems like they didn’t give any additional drops compared to just killing the rare as normal. They have questionable pathing at times and I’m forced to backtrack to pick up loot because if I stop, the wisp is gone. Additionally, most of the talismans are completely unusable when compared to greater runes. Some need their values tweaked and some are just dead.

Rogue Exiles feel amazing and super rewarding. In my opinion, a sleeper pick for one the best overall changes to the league. After the HP nerf, they feel much better to fight. They have unique combat styles/personalities and seem to be one of the more consistent sources of Uniques.

Strongboxes are….better? They are being hard carried by the Unique strongboxes. On average, engaging with a normal strongbox is much faster and feels better. However, they are still somewhat lackluster if you ignore the unique ones. I’m not sure what the fix is here, but they definitely are heading in a better direction these days.

Essences are in a great state in my opinion. They feel good to engage with, provide sufficient rewards and now with additional ways to interact with them via Vaal orbs are overall the prototype for a successful in map random event.

Shrines are shrines. Maybe add a few new ones to spice things up. Remove the Gold Shrine, no one wants it. They are meh, but fine. I think the shrine section of the Atlas passive tree could be better utilized to provide something else that people would actually take. I don’t think anyone is investing points here.

Feel free to share your thoughts below. It’s clear that we as a community strive for the game to improve and I hope that through constructive feedback and communication we can do just that. Cheers
