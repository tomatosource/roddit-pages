Day 2 prospects - Stretch big?

Obviously right now we have a gaping hole on the interior. But we also have the #2 pick in the second round.

Tomorrow I’m expecting us to pick one of the three remaining top stretch bigs: Maxime Raynaud, Ryan Kalkbrenner, or Rasheer Fleming. All of them could have gone in the first round, and we’re very fortunate to be able to land one of them. My quick thoughts:

Fleming - The smallest of the 3 at 6’9”, but huge wingspan. Also the best shooter of the 3. Some scouts question his feel for the game and athleticism, but he plays with great motor and hustle.

Kalkbrenner - A monster defending the rim. He’s huge, and has great timing. Not agile enough to be a great perimeter defender, but his length compensates a bit. Probably the weakest shooter of the 3, but his game feel is great.

Raynaud - Great rebounder and offensive feel. Underrated passer. Not much of a rim protector, but his solid agility should help him stay in front of guys on defense. Despite being legitimately over 7’, may be more of a 4 than a 5.

Any thoughts? Or other prospects to be looking out for that have flown under my radar? Feels like we’re in a great position to get a potential rotation big with our next pick.
