My Proposed Solution to Towers in Endgame

TL;DR: Towers generate an item containing their modifiers from tablets than you can apply to craft Waystones capped at equivalent to 2 or 3 towers applied allowing you to bring the “juice” while freely exploring the Atlas.

Before I start, I’d just like to highlight a few points related to towers that don’t necessarily feel that bad on their own.

  • Previously, towers felt unrewarding and chore-like. Now that towers contain bosses, league mechanics, etc. I feel that the actual gameplay during a tower map is close enough to a regular map that they feel fine in terms of completing them on an individual basis and even feel better than playing certain map layouts like Mire or certain Vaal maps.
  • Towers as a means to reveal and expand your Atlas function as intended and conceptually make sense. Pathing to a tower to reveal more area when looking for specific things like more citadels, etc. can sometimes feel bad but this is more tied to my next point.

The problem with Towers is that they provide so much “juice” to looting/clearing maps that it creates a feeling of FOMO or feels bad when you are completing maps that aren’t enhanced by them. People often think:

  • Doing a map with no active towers? “Man I’d get more loot if I had a tower”
  • Doing a map with an active tower? “Man I’d get more loot if this was double stacked towers”
  • Doing a map with double towers? “Man if only I could get a third tower in this node I’d have more loot”
  • I really want to go find citadels “But if I leave this area I lose 2 or 3 towers going on, I’ll get way less loot while looking around”
  • I really don’t want to do these bad layouts “But I can’t give up juice from 3 towers”

The Atlas is meant to encourage and revolve around exploration, but the tower mechanic encourages you to camp up in areas for hours or days on end and systematically clear every node.

My biggest take away from listening to various players, streamers, podcasts, etc. is that people really like the modifiers provided from tablets when completing towers. They don’t want this “juice” taken from them. However, the FOMO of tower-brain makes mapping feel bad when it should be making it more exciting. Exploration feels awful when you are simply looking around for the next giga tower overlap.

Here is my proposed solution:

When you complete a tower, rather than the modifiers being applied to all or various maps in the surrounding area, instead you extract an item from where you activate the tower. This item will be referred to as a “Beacon” from here on and it will:

  • Function as a method to additionally craft onto Waystones
  • Contain a number of uses equal to the amount of maps the tower would have applied its effects to
  • Apply all league mechanics the tower would have applied
  • Apply all modifiers the tower would have applied such as pack size, rarity, etc.

This item will function as a means to store the “juice” from the tower and apply it to your Waystone.

  • If the Beacon was generated from a 6 mod Waystone aka 3 tablets applied to the tower, it will only be applicable to 6 mod Waystones, etc.
  • A Beacon cannot be applied to the same Waystone more than once.
  • No more than 2 or 3(undecided) Beacons can be applied to a single Waystone.

This will eliminate the feeling of FOMO since all maps can be run with equivalent amount of tablet modifiers. It may even increase willingness or desire to run towers to generate better and better beacons. You will also be able to freely traverse the Atlas without feeling bad about leaving certain “juiced” areas behind. Instead of looking for tower overlap, you just focus on crafting or looting the best tablets and only “need” to hit towers when you run of uses or find better tablets.

Feel free to discuss your thoughts and let me know if you feel like there is an oversight of an issue with Towers that I haven’t acknowledged. Cheers.
