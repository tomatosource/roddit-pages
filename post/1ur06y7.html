Idk why we couldn’t wait one more year

The part of the JB trade that has baffled me the most is why now? What I haven’t seen discussed enough is remembering Tatum is fresh off an Achilles injury and has yet to log a full season.

The thing JB has consistently provided is durability and high performance. It would allow us to ease Tatum back in more regularly next season and be able to sit games out if needed.

Now we traded JB for a guy who historically hardly plays and can’t carry the weight as a number one option. If JT is out for Achilles maintenance for any extended stretch our roster starts to crumple.

By making this trade the front office is banking on someone fresh off an Achilles to give us 70+ games a season at 35+ minutes a night. Idk how sustainable this is long term for JT.

Idk, I just don’t see why we had to make this move now and couldn’t wait one more season to see how this all plays out with JT’s full return from injury.
