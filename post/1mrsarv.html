NBA Rivalries…

The rivalry week schedule came out and I dont get it, hardly any actual rivalries, so I ask the question who do you view as your teams Top 3 rivals?

Pacers rivals:

  1. New York Knicks - This one is old and new. This starts with our franchise player Reggie Miller, and goes to today where we’ve knocked them out of 2 consecutive playoff competitions. We hate them, they hate us.

  2. Detroit Pistons - Again this one has a history, there was a Malice in a Palace, and last season with the Pistons looking relevant again, the games were chippy as hell. Even if Indiana did win 3-1, and 9-3 over the last 3 seasons.

  3. Milwaukee Bucks - Everyone seems to think this is a big deal, but it’s a short-term thing. The Bucks can’t handle that Indiana became better, since they’re the bigger market, with the Superstar player, but it is a rivalry, it is a long way off the first 2 teams though. This is the place holder rivalry, it could just as easy be Chicago in a season or 2, as we don’t like them, but they suck right now.
