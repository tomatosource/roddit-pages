CJ McCollum is one of four players to have averaged at least 20 points per game for 10 straight seasons. Will he ever make an All-Star game?

CJ McCollum is one of four active players (along LeBron, AD and DeRozan) to have averaged at least 20 points per season for the last 10 straight seasons (eight if you consider Curry/KD/Kawhi/Lillard who have missed an entire season or the majority of one since 2015⁄16).

He put up a career high of 24.3 points after arriving to the Pelicans midway through 2021, yet his teams (and being shadowed by Dame early on in Portland) have not been able to put together an impressive enough season to get him over the hump for an ASG appearance.

So now that begs the question, does he have the most impressive career for a non All-Star ever? Jamal Murray has the playoff success, but CJ’s numbers have been more consistent.

He also has suffered from playing in the West his whole career. The West is stacked, especially at the guard positions. Had he had similar stats for an East team he would likely have made at least one All Star by now.

But now at 34, if the Wizards get their act together in the next 2 seasons, is it still possible? Mike Conley got his first nod in his mid 30’s in the West, so who knows.
