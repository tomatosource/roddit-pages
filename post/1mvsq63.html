While we haven’t tried them yet, Interludes seem like an interesting end game mechanic.

I actually like the idea of interludes. I think a series of maps with their own little mechanics, bosses, connected by a small side quest or story sounds like fun. We will have to see how they operate within the actual game at release, but I feel like perhaps GGG should consider expanding upon this system.

Maybe there could be a way to do interludes once all of the acts were added and they can add additional ones. Even if it is just the equivalent of doing a handful of maps I think there are people who would enjoy something with even a loose narrative structure compared to just running random maps.

PoE1 did have the Atlas Memories and then the Secrets of the Atlas stuff, but they were still ultimately random maps that ended in a unique map. This seems like a real advancement on that system. Maybe you could unlock these different side stories as you progress the Atlas, and players who want some more narrative or structure can go and complete them and others can just ignore them and keep on blasting.
