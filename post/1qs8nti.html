Anthony Edwards has raised his scoring average every season he’s been in the league

Edwards is scoring 29.3 ppg this season, with a career high in both FG% (49.6%) and 3P% (41.3%).

The NBA is honestly too stacked with talent. Edwards is a young, efficient offensive superstar that plays good defense but he doesn’t get nearly enough attention.

He still is a tier below guys like Shai, Luka, Jokic and Giannis but he’s definitely on the trajectory of being a tier 1 superstar. And he’s only 24!

The pool of players born in 2001 is crazy- Edwards, Cade, Barnes, Jalen Johnson, Deni, JDubb, Mobley, LaMelo and Wagner. Crazy talent there.
