Crafting feels extremely frustrating - especially with the recombinator

I know I might be in the minority here, but I am just so extremely frustrated by the crafting experience. I just have to vent.

Context: I run a Deadeye crossbow build and have a good enough endgame build so that my next weapon upgrade costs upwards of 50 divines. But I have only 25. Sure, I can go farm, but I decided to try crafting. I had set my loot filter to pick up Desolate Crossbows, so I had about 50 level 80+ items lying around in a dump tab. Since I only have 25 div I don’t want to spend a lot of currency on crafting. It simply makes no sense to pay 5 div for a whittling if you only have 25. Oh and I didn’t use fracturing orbs because I only found 2 of them in 250 hours of gameplay, which feels insanely low. Maybe I am doing it wrong, but I just wouldn’t know how to do it better without spending insane amounts of currency.

There are so many different elements of frustration that all add up to a terrible experience:

  • You can’t sort the stash tab, so you have to manually sort the items by item level. This is extremely tedious with a controller.
  • I want the items sorted by item level, because it feels bad to reforge two 82 items with an 81 item and have the result be an 81 item. I just don’t like that feeling. It’s very annoying to have to pay attention to that.
  • The first step is to augment and regal all blue items. That step is the only one that doesn’t feel bad, because it is very cheap and goes quick.
  • Then you have to scan all items individually, identify good affixes and exalt those items that don’t have good affixes on them. This is very tedious and costs hundreds of exalts. It’s not very expensive, but just feels bad to spend so many exalts without even getting anything for it. It feels much, much better to spend 100 exalts on one good item than to spend them on 50 items creating junk affixes.
  • I got around 10 or so decent affixes that are worth the recombinator process. But since you can’t mark them in the stash tab you have to remember where they are, or put them off to a corner or something like that. Handling the items like that is tedious and it feels really bad to get so few viable affixes from this whole process. It would feel much, much better if I could spend 10 or 20 exalts for a max tier affix of choice.
  • Needless to say the recombinator odds are extremely low when trying to combine T9 affixes. But I don’t have a choice because anything less would not yield me a weapon that is better than the one I already have.
  • It feels extremely bad to see your items vanish in the recombinator after spending so much tedious effort to find a handful of good affixes.
  • Only one of all the recombinations was successful.
  • Now I have an item with two good affixes. But the only thing I can do with it is to exalt slam it. I did that and the item turned out shit. Of course it did, because the chance to get 4 perfect exalts in a row is minuscule.
  • I also used 4-5 chaos orbs on one item. It felt really bad because of how expensive they are, and when chaos orb destroyed my tier 10 physical damage affix the feeling was horrible.
  • Then you reforge the remaining items and repeat the annoying process multiple times until no items are left.

All in all I spent about 2 hours, spent hundreds of exalts and got nothing out of the process except a really bad feeling. The company got the money I paid for the stash tab to collect the items. But I got nothing.

Rant over. Maybe this post turns out to be useless, but it feels good to get this off my chest. If others feel the same frustration maybe that will put some pressure on them to adjust the system.

Looking back on this process my experience was so tremendously bad, that I don’t even understand what kind of experience the designers were aiming for. This system is so extremely unfun that it has no business of being in a video game, imho. There are three different layers of RNG that compound. There is the RNG to find a good affix, then there is the recombinator RNG and then there is the RNG of exalting afterwards. The chances of crafting a good item this way are astronomically low. Even if the recombinator odds were 100%, it would still be astronomically low.

Currently I am thinking I should completely ignore all crafting in the future, pick up nothing but currency and just buy what I want from the trade site. But I don’t like that idea. I want to be able to craft. I enjoy crafting in most other games and I think it’s an important part of an ARPG - at least for me. I really wish they would rework the system somehow so that it feels fun and rewarding. It would be nice to be able to craft a decent item with an investment of a few hours of game time in terms of effort and currency cost.
