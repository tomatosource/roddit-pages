Does Glove Size Actually Affect Pitching Mechanics?

Tried throwing a bullpen with a slightly heavier 12” glove instead of my usual 11.75”, and it unexpectedly tightened up my glove-side and kept me from flying open early. Didn’t add velocity, but my line to the plate felt cleaner and more controlled.
