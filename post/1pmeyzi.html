Build your own baseball team.

Here’s a fun exercise.

You have to build a 26 man and 40 man roster for a new MLB team. Rules:
You can pick 1 contract from each team for 26 man roster.
You can pick 2 contracts from the rest of 4 teams for 40 man roster.
You can pick 6 minor league talents with 0 or very little MLB experience.
Maybe 25 innings for pitchers and 80 PAs for batters(5 starts/25 games at 3.2 PAs, randomly chose 3.2).

Budget-200 million cap and 150 million floor. You can build outside this as well but building inside limits will be fun ig.

Give a name to your team and select a city you feel deserving of an MLB team.
