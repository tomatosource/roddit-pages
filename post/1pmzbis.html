Not dropping loot from the platform in Maps is intended, and is not a bug (or a problem)

Ive seen a thread/comment pop up quite a bit over the last 2 days asking the following and not many people have explained it - “The league mechanic was super rewarding in the campaign and stopped being rewarding in Maps, is it bugged?”

In the campaign, the seasonal league mechanic has a guaranteed drop depending on what area you are in. This can be anything from an exalted orb to a chance orb to a support gem. This is to give you a reason to interact with the mechanic, and to smooth out progression for those who do.

GGG had to give you the guaranteed drop after clearing each platform, because that was the only way to do it.

That platform was never intended to drop loot in Maps, its purely a way to build your temple since their goal is that you interact with the temple once ever half a dozen Maps for your rewards (and thats fine.)

The platform doesnt need buffs, since its not the mechanic. The temple needs buffs though, major ones.
