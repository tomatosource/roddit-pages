Mageblood has showed me the true power of Ghost Dance

I finally got a Mageblood from Ritual and rolled Diamond, Jade, Bismuth, Bismuth on a CI monk with 7000 ES and about 69% Evasion (23,000). I was concerned about switching from my Satchel for my ES recovery, but after my Evasion jumped to 78%, Ghost Dance went into overdrive and, when I did get hit (which wasn’t very often) I almost immediately recovered all of my lost ES.

On top of that, the doubles have me critting almost all the time and I suddenly don’t need elemental resistances on any other gear. This is a magical, special belt that’s going to cost me a lot of divs in respeccing the rest of my set up to maximize around it, but it’ll be a good hurt.
