I called it quits

After 600h of playtime I just left the arena and won’t come back. More than I was forced to grind and farm for never dropping items and currency I was farming fun, that thing drops so seldom that I expected it in the same mystery box as the never to be seen mirror of freaking kalandra. There is a difference between difficult and grindy. If I wanted to work I would apply for a job and at least would get paid better. It’s a build game, so let me be build! Shut down the trade website and offer a proper forging system, copy Nioh 2 if your creativity got lost somewhere in that mini time stop high frustrating Zarok gimmick. All I did last month was pressing one button while running through maps and trials hoping for a huge drop that never came, just to avoid grinding a simulacrum or a breachstone but being able to buy whatever gives me entry to a boss flight. But even if I got it, what would have changed? Nothing! PoE2 is a never ending, boring and unrewarding grind with only one guaranteed drop: frustration! Even though it hurts: Goodbye my lover, goodbye me friend, I thought you have been the one for me …
