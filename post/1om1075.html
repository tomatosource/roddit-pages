Starting late - My Experience

So I started 4 days ago, before this league I played during release only, I skipped 0.2, you might have seen my other thread asking about it(I saw someone ask to share my experience) and I decided to share my experience 4 days into it and just starting T15 Maps with Blood Mage - tho my tactic to farm currency will be Sekhemas Jewels, not overjuiced T15 maps, but we’ll see, I am unsure what the best path forward is, but ill stick to Sekhemas for now and see how profitable that ends up being - anyone know the best Relic stats for profitable Sekhemas? I have no clue… either way!

THE KEY THING TO A LATE START IS TO HAVE A PLAN OF ACTION.

The start was far smoother than what I had expected, my plan was to keep as many of my low level uncut skill and spirit gems as possible (1-3-5-7 for skill - anything for spirit) and use the Exalts I get from that to get good leveling gear and get started in maps.

Overall the amount of profit I made selling low level uncut gems was near 1k exalts give or take, more than enough for gearing.

Starting maps I made sure I had 100 Item Rarity on my character and started the climb, I saw my first divine tink at Waystone tier 2, then I had about 3 more tinks in my climb to 15, which I used to buy better gear for my character.

Funny story is that the first ritual tablet I got entering the endgame(from the quest you’re given), was a Quant - increased tribute from monsters tablet (I made it), which ran for around 60 chaos, about 2.2div.

I saw 3 Sealed Vaults heading out into the atlas during my climb to T15, ofc I took the opportunity to do them and Ataluis Bloodletting dropped which was a surprise to me, but needless to say it was a huge boost in damage and I am glad I did the Sealed Vaults instead of selling the idols.

Overall I feel extremely squishy and very glass cannony(only 2800hp in t15 maps is dumb), I am not actively dying during T15 maps, but I have to be very careful about where I stand and what I am doing/actively dodging, I am not sure how to improve that without sacrificing my damage, probably annoint Fast Metabolism, but we’ll see.

Day 4 finished the climb to t15, in my stash I have: 2 Anoint (Drops) - 2 Divine (Drops) - 1.1k Exalt (selling stuff) - 86 Chaos (selling stuff) - 2 Fracture Orb (drops) and a bunch of other small misc stuff like an omen worth 120 exalt and such.

Overall, I had a plan and my luck has been relatively decent, but starting late was VERY good, starting late gave me an easy way to have a good Exalt Base going into maps to gear myself where I would otherwise wouldn’t have that, and they really buffed drops a LOT in maps, I just did Alch and Go until T15 and got a whole bunch of stuff that I will reinvest into my gear. I am wondering how to go about getting my Item Rarity to 150 while upgrading the rest of my stats.
