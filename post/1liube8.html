Hali’s injury last night is the worst by-context injury in NBA history

I genuinely can’t think of an injury that when considering the context is worse in the history of the league. I mean I don’t know if it’s recency bias or what, but this just feels like it’s the worst one when you combine all the factors that led to this moment. Game 7 of the NBA finals, you’ve been the leader of a team who have had one of the most magical playoff runs in any sport, you’ve been Mr. Clutch the entire time, you started the game on fire, your team is far less equipped to return, this is the moment, the opportunity, and your achilles ruptures in the first quarter. It’s heartbreaking.

I posed this to my brother and he said the only one he could think of was the KD and Klay two-fer, but those guys were already NBA champions. Does any other injury come close?
