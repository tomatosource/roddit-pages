Emotionally preparing myself for the end of this season

I’ve been watching Boston sports for 30+ years. I’ve seen everything. Leads mean nothing in the grand scheme of a playoff run (and you can take that both ways).

But after these last two games, I’ve decided to start the mourning process. In my mind, we’ve lost and we’ve done it in the most embarrassing and frustrating of ways. How can the process that won us a championship a year ago suddenly lead to such utter mental collapse?

We have an incredible team on paper when the shots start falling. What makes this hard to watch is this team should be able to find other ways to win. We just beat a Magic team that neutralized the three and forced physicality and paint presence. And now we’re being beaten by Mikal Bridges?

I watch a lot of games, read the articles, check this Reddit, I will bleed green until the day I die. But last night I saw a team that completely forgot how to win and that mental hurdle seems too great to overcome in a few short days. In order to protect my sanity, I’m going to start the funeral and look ahead to next season. It hurts to see them come this far only to beat themselves at the finish line.

TLDR: I’m sad and preparing for them to blow this.

(And for the “touch grass” and “it’s just a game” folks: I can be sad about something I invest time in. My feelings are valid even if you don’t share them/understand them.)
