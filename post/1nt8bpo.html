Sekhemas feedback

(Note: I am the author if the Sekhemas Guide):

Guide to Sekhemas Ascedencies : r/PathOfExile2

So while I do find Sekhemas to be not as bad as it is often regarded, I feel there are a number of aspects that lean too heavily in the “unfair” category that could be tweaked.

I am not proposing solutions here, that’s not my role, and any decision I might make will draw a rebuttal as to the efficacy of that approach.

Instead, I will simply list what I think are areas that, if fixed., would greatly improve the fairness of Sekhemas trials.

In no particular order:

  • The use of off-map long-range attackers.

There are many attackers, especially in floor 3 and 4, that will cast fast projectiles at you from off screen. Your first indication of this is series of bolts heading your way. Not only are you often on a narrow walkway and can’t avoid the, but you also can’t retaliate until you close the distance.

  • The amount of honour loss is wildly unpredicatable.

Given any decent amount of honour resistance, mot attacks will inflict only a small amount of honour loss.

Then, suddenly, one particular attack will drop honour by a significant amount. Often these are ground effects, certain traps, and urnwalker’s cinder spray.

  • The use of level changes in floor 3 and 4 is insidious

I chose the word: “Insidious” on purpose. The way isometric games handle levels has always been a compromise, you cannot have too large a difference, or too steep a slope unless you align the map in such a way that the slope is across the POV, and not along it. There is also the fact that some skills are very difficult to aim at an enemy on a higher platform, but easier on a lower, as you can see the lower platform from further away. If the enemy is both closer to your fixed POV, and higher, you may not be able to see that platform, let alone target an enemy.

Yet this is exactly how levels are designed in Sekhemas floor 3 and 4. Chalce rooms in floor 4 for example often have narrow winding stairways that you need to ascend to even see the targeted rare, yet are under fire from off-screen enemies with broad AoE effects as you do so.

Only a very experienced player knows to head around the other way (Which isn’t always possible), or to draw enemies to one side of a raised platform, then run around and ascend on the other side (again, not all platforms even have two stairways.)

There are Hourglass rooms in floor 4 in which a rare can appear on a higher, rear platform, and fire at you on the nearer, lower platform with no way to see where the attacker is, and no way to retaliate.

  • There are far too many underground pop-up monsters, especially in floor 3.

Hourglass rooms in floor 3 are simply best avoided, you are often in slowing sand, in a very small area, beset with enemies you cannot engage until you approach them, at which point half a dozen will emerge and attack at once. No wonder Melee characters find Sekhemas tough, but in these rooms, ranged attackers are forced into the same proximity to kill enemies.

  • All honour loss after completing a room should be prevented

Perfect example: there is a boon that prevents any damage or honour loss until the next room is completed. You can take this, complete the toom, and suffer honour loss immediately after from a boss AoE!

  • The “Take 5% damage from any hit” affliction needs to go.

Both Unwalkers and the floor 2 bosses inflict damage that insta kills any character with this infliction
