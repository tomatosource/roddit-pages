Survival tip: Omen of Resurgence

If you are dying in maps or other content I would recommend buying a stack of 10 of these and just leaving them active in your inventory. Its a quality investment for the time it should take you to run through these.

Please keep in mind if you use other omen’s such as amelioration these could prevent them from taking effect.
