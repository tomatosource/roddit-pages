Expecting to see a lot of Banton

This signing is very suspicious. The first thing I thought when I saw this was Banton is a dry run for our healthy players to adjust for Tatum’s return.

Banton and Tatum are both 6’8 205 lbs. Visually and positionally Banton can be utilized the same way they want Tatum to be when he first returns. So it makes sense to want our healthy players to first adjust to a new player coming in at the forward position and changing the lineup and minutes of everyone *before* Tatum actually returns.

This way when Tatum returns, everyone is already used to the adjustment because they adjusted with Banton.

This is very smart if this turns out to be true. If we see Banton getting 20 mins a night in a pattern that we would expect Tatum to play when he returns then I fully expect Tatum to join the team after Banton’s 10 day expires.
