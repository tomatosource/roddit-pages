Despite the unrelenting mediocrity of our major league team, Mariners farm is in great shape. Worth noting one of those top 10 guys, Colt Emerson, is already up.

There are 30 prospects listed as OF in the top 100. The Dodgers have 6 (20%) of them.

The AL East has the least prospects (14), the NL West and both Centrals are tied at 18 for the most. I was hoping counting that would reveal some kind of fun discrepancy but nah, they’re relatively well spread out among divisions.

I’m surprised to see the Mets this low, but I guess they just graduated a bunch of guys, it makes sense.
