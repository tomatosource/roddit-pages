[Feedback] GGG… Particles and lingering effects are ruining PoE 2’s performance, here’s why

I’ve been playing a Grim Pillar build, and one thing became very obvious:
The biggest performance killer is the massive amount of particles and visual effects generated.

Here’s some screenshots during my gameplay…

When clearing packs (or even bosses), the build creates an enormous amount of effects.
As they accumulate, FPS steadily drops, even after the enemies are already dead.
If I stop moving and simply wait for 20–30 seconds, the effects eventually disappear and performance immediately returns.

The last two screenshots from a boss fight show this perfectly:
During the fight: 23 FPS
After the effects disappear: 192 FPS

That’s a gain of 169 FPS without changing settings, leaving the area, or reducing monster count.
The only difference is that the visual effects are gone.

This makes it feel less like a hardware issue and more like particle and effect accumulation.
The game spends an enormous amount of resources rendering or processing lingering effects.
The moment the totems shoot out their pillars and I explode them, my graphic’s card goes nuts.

PoE 2 looks fantastic, but when builds start generating hundreds of overlapping effects, performance will collapse.
The fact that standing still for a short time restores FPS suggests that particles and visual effects may not be clearing efficiently or need a massive overhaul.
The screenshots make the issue pretty clear.

This was tested on:
CPU: i7 14700k
GPU: RTX 5060 Ti 16GB
RAM: 32GB

Having 23 FPS during a boss fight of mapping is not fun.
And I will not play a different build to ‘fix’ it because I do like the playstyle of the build.
It should be optimised more.

Please look into this GGG, it’s a massive issue and I assume it will fix a lot of performace issues people are having.
