Wade Boggs is so underrated compared to the other great contact hitters. His first 8 years in the league he hit .352 and averaged nearly 220 hits per 162 games with a 8 WAR. All while playing a very good third base.

Somehow never finished better than top 4 in MVP but wow he was insane from 1983-1991 (averaged 205 hits and hit .345 over 9 seasons). With a .436 OBP (+91 OBPba).

And unlike a lot of the greatest contact hitters like Gwynn, Suzuki, Carew, Puckett, etc he could draw walks and his OBP was even higher weighted than his BA relative to the average league difference. In Ichiro’s prime his average was .331 with a .376 OBP (+45 OBPba).

Boggs led the league in OBP more times (7) than he has batting titles (6).

I’d have to say he’s better than Gwynn. Not just because of WAR, but because he played a much harder position and he played it well. As a pure hitter it’s close too. Gwynn never struck out and always put pressure on the defense but somehow Boggs hit even better than Gwynn in the clutch: Boggs hit .374 with RISP while Gwynn hit .351. Boggs hit .489 with the bases loaded to Gwynn’s .444.

Both unfathomable. The best average hitter of our past generation/this generation is Altuve who hit .331 from 2014-2018. I can’t find his RISP stats but I’d be shocked if it was above .350.
