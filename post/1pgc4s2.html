OK, Scoring Question

I’ve thought about this and I can’t come up with an answer at least one that makes sense to me:

You’re pitching. The first batter you face hits a can of corn grounder to the 3rd baseman. 3rd baseman overthrows the first baseman and the runner heads for second. The catcher backing up the play picks up the ball, fires it toward second, overthrows everyone and the ball goes into left field. The runner rounds third and the left fielder picks up the ball and fires it into the stands, run scores. The pitcher then proceeds to retire the next 27 batters in a row(meaning he throws a no hitter) but the team is shut out 1-0. Why is the pitcher saddled with a loss?

Better yet, the first batter, 0-2 pitch, the batter swings and misses but the catcher misplays the pitch resulting in a passed ball. The runner heads to first but the catcher throws the ball up the right field line into the corner and the runner circles the bases and scores a run. The pitcher retires the next 27 batters on 27 pitches but his team fails to score a run(pitcher doesn’t bat because of the DH) and they lose 1-0. The pitcher is saddled with the loss. This makes no sense. Should it be a team loss instead but the pitcher doesn’t wear it because the run scored through no fault of his own?
