We’re so lucky. 💚

Imagine a world in which the 76ers or Lakers got either (or both) of them. 🤢

With all of the weight and expectations and vitriol (from our local media and some fans alike), it isn’t easy playing here: JT isn’t “tough” enough/can’t handle pressure, doesn’t have what “it” takes to win, JB can’t go left and always in trade rumors, “they can’t play together,” media always pitting them against each other, etc.

And yet these guys embrace being here. I think they value being a part of something special by being members of the Celtics and writing their own place in it’s history. They are always hard at work trying to get better each and every off-season. And now we have yet another 50 win season. 5 years in a row.

As JB said last May when everything looked bleak: “I know Boston… it looks gloomy right now, obviously with JT being down, and us ending the year, but it’s a lot to look forward to. I want the city to feel excited about that. This is not the end.”

I couldn’t have imagined how awesome and fun the team would be to watch this season after how it all ended last year.

With these two guys healthy (Basketball Gods please let us have a fully healthy post-season for once 🙏🏻), I like our chances against anyone.
