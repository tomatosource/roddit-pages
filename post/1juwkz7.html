[King] KP would love to see JB rest in final wk of regular season

Kristaps Porzingis said he appreciates Jaylen Brown’s heart and believes Jaylen will do what he needs to do to be ready for the playoffs. That said, sounds like Porzingis wouldn’t mind seeing Brown take some games off during the final week of the season.

“He’s a tough dude,” Porzingis said. “He always preaches his warrior mindset and he lives by it, but to what extent do we need that right now? Maybe he needs to take care of it and make sure he’s going to be ready for the most important moments.”
