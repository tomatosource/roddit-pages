[Feedback] Unfun requirements and impacts of levelled item skills

Good day exiles, as it is balance time for ggg I’d like to throw an issue into the hat:
Levelled skills attached to items.

The bad:

  • Getting a sub lvl 20 skill on your endgame unique is needlessly punishing. At lvl 19 or below you lose scaling and a support gem slot. It creates artificial rarity as lvl 18⁄19 items are much less valuable.
  • Level requirements for item skills create very weird and unfun situations. You can drop a great levelling unique that you can only use in the endgame (where its useless). The quintessential example is a +3 skills lifesprig. The attached skills is not at all the point of the unique but does fuck up its level requirements.
  • Using a weapon skill as a main skill is a SLOG when trying to use it during levelling as it adds another layer to itemization. The issues of gaining additional support gems repeats multiple times and its just frustration.

The good:

  • Its free to use, makes my brain tickle with new build ideas.
  • Unique build defining skills are just plain amazing.

Am I the only one annoyed by this? Am I still sane?
