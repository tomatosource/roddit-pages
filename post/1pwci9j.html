First time poe

Always loved ARPGs, in the past 2⁄3 years I tried D4 and hated it, also played Grim Dawn and loved it. But damn, Poe2 Druid is just the most fun I’ve had in this genre.

I was a bit apprehensive at first because I read so many posts about being difficult and complicated compared to other games in the same genre but i definitely feel like poe2 is in the perfect spot, not trivial but also not extremely difficult. The WASD movement is also something I never knew I needed until now and the graphics are incredible.
