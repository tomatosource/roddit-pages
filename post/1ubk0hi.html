Huge tip for Amanamu farm.(Omen of light, Omen of Abyssal Echoes)

As players have gotten stronger and acquired their HH and Magebloods. Stopping to attack/cast when amanamu void monster spawns can be tricky as they die in a few hits.

​

What I learned is that - right about as the Abyss monsters are going to ‘pop’ out stand next to the crack and just NUKE it, before you even see them come out.

​

The swirly shade covering them registers only once they are fully out. Havent gotten a single - Omen of the Liege since I started doing this.

To clarify for anyone doing this farm if abyss monsters are dropping - Omen of putrefication, Omen of Sinistral Necromancy, Omen of Light, Omen of Abyssal Echoes. You are doing it correctly.
