Jalen Brunson has the best playoff winning percentage of any star PG against 60-win teams in NBA history

After closing out the Spurs in 5 games to bring the Knicks their first title since 1973, Brunson is now 3-0 against 60-win teams in his playoff career. A perfect record, and more 60-win team scalps than players like Magic, Kyrie, Westbrook, Nash, Kidd, Iverson, SGA, CP3, Lillard, or Isiah ever managed in their entire careers.

Brunson’s three:

2022 Suns (64-18) — won 4-3 in WCSF with Dallas

2025 Celtics (61-21) — won 4-2 in ECSF with the Knicks

2026 Spurs (62-20) — won 4-1 in the NBA Finals

And then, Steph Curry also has a perfect winning percentage against 60-win teams at 2-0:

2017 Spurs (61-21) — swept 4-0 in WCF (Kawhi injured in Game 1)
