NBA announces nominees for the NBA Sportsmanship Award, players “who best represent the ideals of sportsmanship on the court”: Bam Adebayo, Derrick White, Al Horford, Harrison Barnes, T.J. McConnell, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

The NBA announced the finalists for the 2025-26 NBA Sportsmanship Award, which recognizes the player who best represents the ideals of sportsmanship on the court.

The players selected as finalists are below (listed alphabetically by last name):

Bam Adebayo, Miami Heat

Harrison Barnes, San Antonio Spurs

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Oklahoma City Thunder

Al Horford, Golden State Warriors

T.J. McConnell, Indiana Pacers

Derrick White, Boston Celtics
