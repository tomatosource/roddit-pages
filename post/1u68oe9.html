PSA: Runeforging Gloves with Cadigan’s Effigy and Jewel will DELETE the Jewel

I just found out the hard way that runeforging a pair of gloves with Cadigan’s Effigy (adds jewel socket to gloves) will delete the jewel.

https://preview.redd.it/a01axryu4e7h1.png?width=1172&format=png&auto=webp&s=378000fe0c94e3a1aaef125d56911029c3665977

Here is a screenshot from my character on PoE Ninja of the jewel that was socketed:

https://preview.redd.it/sh9dl3185e7h1.png?width=540&format=png&auto=webp&s=0e996ebaac9833d33d73e8aa3966e5b852250f36

It looks like this has been reported a few times previously on the official forums, but no-one seemed to include a bug report number from the few I checked so I created a new report with Bug Report ID: #35948768.
