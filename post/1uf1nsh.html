PSA: Do not bulk buy the new Liquid Verisium from Farrow!

There’s a “stock limit” (similar to gambling) of Liquid Verisium with Farrow. And if you bulk buy (via ctrl-shift-leftclick or whatever the hotkey is) it’ll consume all of your ore (potentially millions) but instead of giving you the appropriate amount of Liquid Verisium you’ll only instead get a maximum of 100.

A friend of mine just lost his entire stockpile of ore.
