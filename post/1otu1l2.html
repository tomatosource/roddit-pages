TIL an MLB game was forfeited because a team showed up with only three baseballs.

In 1895, the Louisville Colonels managed to lose a game in one of the most avoidable ways possible: they ran out of baseballs by the third inning.

Back then, teams had to bring their own supply, and Louisville’s owner was famously cheap. He sent the team to Brooklyn with just three new balls. Early-era baseballs didn’t last long. After a few hard hits and rough bounces, all three were beaten up and unusable.

When the umpires asked for a fresh ball, Louisville didn’t have one. With no playable ball available, the umpires called the game and awarded Brooklyn a forfeit win.

It’s still one of the only times in major-league history that a game ended because a team literally didn’t bring enough baseballs.
