Influenced Modifiers Return

Hello exiles! I have played a lot of the original Path of Exile and now about 100 hours into the sequel I have a list of things I would like to see added to build upon this experience. Influenced gear would be a great addition to current endgame content. For new PoE 2 folks, influenced gear includes weapons and armor that can drop from specific bosses or in specific areas that rolls at least one prefix/suffix modifier from a list unique to that type of influence. In example, enemies in the iron citadel and Ezomyte city biome would have a chance to drop gear with Ezomyte influenced mods. That makes those biomes more exciting to encounter because it is the only place you can get that type of loot. They also have neat cosmetic art!

Influenced mods are typically more than just stat bumps. They can be build-enabling by changing or improving specific interactions. They are also themed around a specific idea like an element or boss abilities. Here are some examples:

Ezomyte Weapons

  • Gain 6% of physical damage as additional cold damage
  • Your cold damage can inflict bleeding. Your cold damage counts towards the magnitude of bleeding you inflict
  • Leech 4% of cold damage as life

Ezomyte Armor

  • Take 8% of physical damage as cold damage
  • You have 100% increased freeze threshold
  • Regenerate 1.5% of your max life per second while chilled

Faridun Weapons

  • Gain 6% of physical damage as additional fire damage
  • Break armor on hit equal to 5% of fire damage dealt
  • 20% increased damage with hits and ailments against burning enemies

Faridun Armor

  • Take 8% of physical damage as fire damage
  • Drop burning ground when hit, lasting 2 seconds. Burning ground ignites nearby enemies for base fire damage per second equal to 30% of your max life. Internal 2 second cooldown
  • 10% increased movement speed while ignited (could be boots only)

Vaal Weapons

  • Gain 6% of physical damage as additional lightning damage
  • 10% increased magnitude and duration of elemental ailments you inflict on hits with this weapon
  • Leech 4% of lightning damage as energy shield

Vaal Armor

  • Take 8% of physical damage as lightning damage
  • 10% increased effect of buffs on you
  • Regenerate 1.5% of energy shield per second while shocked

Lastly, we could improve upon influence by adding enchantments to waystones, such as “This waystone is influenced by the iron citadel.” That would allow us to double-influence items and get some crazy combinations, like putting that waystone in a Faridun node for weapons that leech cold as life AND break armor with fire. What do you think? Hopefully the design possibilities get you excited as well.
