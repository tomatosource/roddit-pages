“Fun” fact: The movement speed penalty on body armours is both multiplicative and additive

Was confused looking at the values of my MS, so I took down some data points, with a dex body armour, not equipped/equipped ms%:

16, 12.5

26, 22.2

41, 36.8

51, 46.5

56, 51.3

66, 61

69, 63.9

Instead of figuring it out myself, might as well make use of AI for easy questions like this, and yeah, it’s just (.97x)-3

So for a strength armour, you get a 5% multiplicative penalty and a flat -5% ms. 3% for my dex example.
