What’s an NBA stat that sounds crazy without context?

My pick is that Shaq ”only” averages 23.7 PPG in his career.

Shaquille O’Neal averaging 23 points per game for his career sounds unimpressive compared to modern superstars, but the statistic is misleading without context. Shaq played for 19 seasons, including several late-career years with the Phoenix Suns, Cleveland Cavaliers, and Boston Celtics when he was far past his prime, which lowered his overall average. During his peak, especially from 1999 to 2003 with the Los Angeles Lakers, he averaged around 28–30 points per game on extremely high efficiency in one of the slowest, lowest-scoring eras in NBA history. His dominance was even greater in the playoffs, where he led the Lakers to three straight championships and averaged 38 points and 17 rebounds in the 2000 Finals. The raw career average ignores longevity, era, and postseason performance, making the number sound far less impressive than Shaq’s actual impact.
