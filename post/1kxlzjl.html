Four Knicks starters now lead the postseason in total minutes played. KAT only needs to play 13 more minutes to join the top 5 in total minutes played.

https://www.statmuse.com/nba/ask/nba-players-with-most-total-minutes-played-this-playoff

Three Knicks players surpassed the 600 total minute mark last night in a losing effort. Bridges leads the entire postseason in total minutes played, with OG trailing him by just a few minutes. Brunson trails behind for third, and while Hart isn’t technically a starter anymore, he still played 36 minutes tonight to Robinson only played 19 minutes. Rounding out the top four players to lead the postseason in total minutes played.

KAT, having had a knee to knee injury late in the game and remained on the court to the end, is the only Knick outside the top five in most minutes played this postseason. Both him and OG seemed banged up at the end of the game. And with a win or die game 5 back home in just a couple days, not much rest for this already heavy minute starting lineup.

On the opposite side, the entire Pacers starting five has also started to show up on the top 25 list for total minutes played. The caveat, being no one has crossed the 500 minute mark. Haliburton leads and barely misses with 494 total minutes played.
