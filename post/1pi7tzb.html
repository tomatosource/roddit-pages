Building out the next team

Feel like some core pieces of next years roster is starting to slowly come into place.

I do still hope we find a way to duck the tax with a Simons trade because despite finding some cheaper but valuable rotation players for next years squad, we’ll still have a few amount of holes to fill and we’ll need the full-MLE, Bi-annual exception, etc.

As of right now I feel like next years roster currently looks like:

G Pritchard | Open Spot | Open Spot

G White | Open Spot | Gonzalez

F Brown | Hauser | Scheierman

F Tatum | Walsh | Minot

C Open Spot | Queta | Open Spot

Two-Way Williams | Shulga

My hope is we have moved Simons and a future pick or two for a starting caliber center like a Zubac or Claxton who would also help us duck the tax.

If we can do that, then going into next year we need to find a scorer off the bench, a back up PG and hopefully we can hit on our first round pick.

Before anyone says, why aren’t we starting Queta. I love him but I think he’d be great off the bench.

Second unit which already has Walsh, Queta and Hauser will be awesome and get so much better once we add to it.

Still have Gonzalez developing. I can see him taking a year 3 leap like Walsh.
