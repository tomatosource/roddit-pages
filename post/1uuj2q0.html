Length: 121 players

Why It Stops at 121

The remaining 461 players are stranded, most of them because their names start with a letter that nothing ends with.

Permanently stranded letters (nothing ends with them):

  • J: 99 players start with J. Zero names end in J. Every single Jalen, Jayson, Jordan, Joel, Josh, Julius is a dead end. The J-army can only be the *beginning* of a chain, never the continuation.
  • B: 30 players start with B. Zero names end in B. Brandon, Brook, Buddy, Bogdan, all dead ends if you need to continue.
  • V: 5 players start with V. Zero names end in V.
  • Q: 3 players start with Q. Zero names end in Q.

J alone locks out 99 of 582 players from the backbone. The most common first initial in the NBA is the most useless letter for name chains.
