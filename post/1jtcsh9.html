We are not suppose to be playing how you want us too.

“While the primary role of the class is incentivising a ranged/melee hybrid playstyle, we do think we can improve on this type of gameplay”

“a lot of the power of the class is locked behind the Parry mechanic, and some players do not wish to Parry.”

Path of Exile has forever been a showcase to how putting a sword on a witch or running spells on a duellist can be fun and effective. Since the introduction of PoE2, there has been so many systems and interactions that have felt forced upon players, designing an entire ascendancy (Huntress) around constantly using Parry and jumping back and forth to produce ranged and melee damage output, and then being surprised that if a player doesn’t interact with ONE of those mechanics the entire character feels lackluster.

Why would players Parry? Every interaction with mobs is in a group or pack, and they already showcased the new ‘Heavy Stun’ which would remove all the evasion from the new ‘evasion based ascendancy’ just to gain a frenzy charge? Speaking of Frenzy charge, why is every Huntress (spear) skill just better on Deadeye?

Don’t tell us how to play the game. That isn’t what PoE is about. If a character\build decision is too obvious, there is a problem.
