“a lot of the power of the class is locked behind the Parry mechanic, and some players do not wish to Parry.”

Path of Exile has forever been a showcase to how putting a sword on a witch or running spells on a duellist can be fun and effective. Why would players Parry? Every interaction with mobs is in a group or pack, and they already showcased the new ‘Heavy Stun’ which would remove all the evasion from the new ‘evasion based ascendancy’ just to gain a frenzy charge? Speaking of Frenzy charge, why is every Huntress (spear) skill just better on Deadeye?

Don’t tell us how to play the game. That isn’t what PoE is about. If a character\build decision is too obvious, there is a problem.
