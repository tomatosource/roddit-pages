Breach is in a weird state or am I missing something?

After running a lot of Breach, I’m struggling to understand how hiveblood scaling is supposed to work.

With 3 Hiveblood tablets, an unstable breach gives me roughly 1.5k hiveblood, and I can get several unstable breaches in a single map.

A few observations:

  • A Breach Hive gives me roughly 2.2k hiveblood with 3 hiveblood tablets
  • The Hive takes much longer to complete.
  • The gameplay is considerably less engaging than unstable breaches
  • It blocks unstable breaches from spawning so you only get 1 hive each map
  • Most importantly, nothing seems to meaningfully scale Hiveblood rewards.

For example, I ran a Hive with:

  • The breachstone modifier that adds additional Hive waves
  • The unique Breach tablet that adds +5 waves
  • 2 increased hiveblood tablets

This pushed the hive all the way up to 22 waves, and only netted me 2.6k hiveblood. I’ve also noticed that when vruun spawns at the end of an unstable breach, the hiveblood reward doesn’t seem different either.

If all of this is intended, then the reward structure seems backwards. Nothing compares to just doing 3 unstable breaches in a map, especially since you can often do 2 of them at the same time, and in city maps you can use 4 tablets to get 4 unstable all with increased hiveblood. There’s something to say about fortresses since they give a ton of wombgifts but still the time doesn’t seem worth it for the hiveblood.
