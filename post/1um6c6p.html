10 Years of collecting Jaylen Brown jerseys. Thought it might be a good time to share.

The Association, Icon, and Statement, plus a wrong color of JB’s first All-Star game’s jersey

City edition jerseys from 2017-2021

City edition jerseys from 2022-2026. Couldn’t find the 2022-23 authentic one it’s so rare to find.

In 2016, I came to Boston to pursue my master’s degree. I went to my first NBA game at TD Garden on opening night of the 2016–17 season, which also happened to be Jaylen’s NBA debut.

Since then, it’s become a tradition for me to buy the authentic jerseys of the Jays every season.

As a Celtics fan living in China, you have no idea how difficult it is to get authentic Jaylen Brown jerseys. Somehow, I’ve managed to build this collection over the years. Every jersey brings back memories of a different season.

I hope No. 7 will one day be hanging in the rafters. It will be retired by the Celtics, right?

As for whether I’ll start collecting his Sixers jerseys…I’m not sure yet. We’ll see.
