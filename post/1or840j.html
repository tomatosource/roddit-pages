My thoughts about abyssal lich

I kind of gave up on physical witch with reap in favour of chaos damage with zero minions and it worked out truly great in the end. But then I dropped a few invitations, killed Kulemak and got the opportunity to transition (what are their pronouns?) to abyssal lich. The result? Kind of the same damage and other stuff, squeezed maybe 5% more dps after changing armour to one with more spirit.

But then something I noticed: no point in switching from focus to scepter for more skeletons to buff my damage, because focus with its +chaos/+spell/+3 gave a lot more damage than additional skeletons. Stats on scepter are worthless on abyssal lich.

Wouldn’t it make sense to kind of bind the spell damage to the minion damage and minion skill level as well? Now abyssal lich kind of feels like I could as well play normal lich without minions for same result.
