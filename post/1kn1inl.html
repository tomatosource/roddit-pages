PSA: FIX!! To anyone who has had freezes with the map being reset after (DX12)!!

Wipe the inside of your dx12 shadercache folder inside %appdata% pathofexile2

Go into nvidia settings, manage 3d settings, global settings

Change shader cache size from driver default to 10GB

0 freezes, 0 crashes, 0 map resets after and i used to have them every hour or so…

very common for us x3d cpu enjoyers ive noticed! Hope it helps, helped a few dudes in global chat and it worked for them
