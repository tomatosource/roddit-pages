On September 10, 1963, 42-year-old Stan Musial was awake most of the night for the 5AM birth of his first grandchild - about 16 hours later, he hit his 474th career home run on the first pitch he saw against the Chicago Cubs, becoming the first grandfather to hit a home run in the majors

His next at bat, he also hit an RBI single, and then had a flyout to RF before being pulled in the 5th inning of an 8-0 Cardinals victory, which featured Bob Gibson both throwing a shutout and hitting a 3-run home run in the 2nd inning.

Musial played 16 games as a grandfather with .341 BA, 2 HR, 7 RBI, and .348/.500/.848 splits before retiring after 1963.

I thought maybe someone would like a non-Donora related Musial fact.

Also, another family-related anomaly happened on September 10, 1963, which was posted on Reddit in the past so I’m just putting it here since it was years ago.

In the top of the 8th of the Giants/Mets game, the only all-brother half inning in MLB history happened. Jesus Alou, Matty Alou and Felipe Alou all batted back to back to back. Both Jesus and Matty were pinch hitters, replacing the SS and P respectively in a game they trailed by 3. Nothing came of it though, Jesus grounded out, Matty struck out and Felipe grounded out in a game they ultimately lost 4-2
