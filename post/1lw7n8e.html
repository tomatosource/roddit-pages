[SPOILER] ACT 4 Information and Content - Official Source

[ACT 4 Spoilers Ahead]

The 420$ Liberator of Wraeclast supporter pack includes a physical Artbook that includes some key information on Act 4, which is theorized to be released with 0.3 based on recent GGG comments about cruel going away “sooner than you think”.

I’ve attached pictures of some of the content in the book under the Act 4 chapter and summarized some of its other content. This content is highly likely within Act 4 since its an official product released by GGG and shows the artwork and assets for act 4.

I’ve also included some of the official showcases at Exilecon2023 that show some of the Act 4 Bosses and information shared by Mark Roberts on Act 4.

Act 4 Content:

1) Karui Islands + Kingsmarch

Act 4 has us traveling between different islands - Each Island has it’s own different Biome and theme with high diversity of different monsters and Bosses

Ngakanu

-Act 4 Tribal “Player Town/Hub” that later becomes corrupted and inhabitants infected by Blood Fever, a Karui-specific infectious disease caused by the use of virtue gems

(Ngakanu Artwork: https://imgur.com/i6Ebdyf)

Isle of Shrikes

-Where the current Trial of Chaos “Chicken Boss” is officially from (it is temporarily being used for Trial of Chaos)

(Boss in its intended environment: https://imgur.com/3DbJfPA)

-Karui tribe members have to prove their adulthood by going to this island and stealing a Shrike Egg

“Parasite” island

-A highly dangerous island overrun by mysterious and dangerous parasites

“Vanishing” island

-An Island where all the Karui tribes that lived there mysteriously vanished on a night of the full moon (Possible werewolves / druid shapeshifters?)

“Goblin” Island

-Mentioned by Mark Roberts, an Act 4 island is inhabited by goblins who worship “Krutog Lord of Kin” a Mid-Act Boss in the volcanic Barrens and bring him human sacrifices

-Mark Roberts also confirms the existence of Sulfite in this Island as the boss eats it

-The Blind Beast Boss is another boss showcased by Mark Roberts at Exilecon and is a boss in the area before you fight Krutog

Image of Krutog Lord of Kin: https://imgur.com/qQdajO

Imagine of the Blind Beast Boss: https://imgur.com/7l1oWFl

-Kingsmarch

-Unknown how much of PoE1s Kingsmarch is in Act 4, the artbook does not go into detail other than mentioning that Kingsmarch is in Act 4

Artbook: https://imgur.com/UmMfyDk

2) A New ascendancy trial - The Halls of the Dead

A new Ascendancy Trial “The Halls of the Dead / Trials of the Ancestors” that has origins from PoE1’s Trials of the Ancestors league (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJNGeWTO5_s)

Artbook: https://imgur.com/5eFSvJ6

3) Hinekora - The Mother of Death

We’re getting Hinekora from PoE1 and the Halls of the Dead / Trial of the Ancestors

Artbook: https://imgur.com/De2wUKm

4) Rakiata

Seems highly likely we are getting Rakiata and their signature unique weapon from PoE1 (https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Rakiata%27s_Dance)

Artbook: https://imgur.com/aoqKxXQ

5) We will fight corruption infecting the islands and corrupted Tribe members inflicted by Blood fever in Act 4

-If a Karui tribe member uses gems, they go mad and develop a highly infectious blood fever that infects any other Karui who come into contact

Artbook: https://imgur.com/5AUfsnd

Artbook: https://imgur.com/wGYPuqC
