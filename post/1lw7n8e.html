I took a few hours to collect all the information about Act 4 we currently have from official sources together into 1 place:

The 420$ Liberator of Wraeclast supporter pack includes a physical Artbook that includes some key information on Act 4, which is theorized (but not confirmed) to be released with 0.3 based on recent GGG comments about cruel going away “sooner than you think”.

I’ve attached pictures of some of the content in the book under the Act 4 chapter and summarized some of its other content. This content is likely within Act 4 since its an official product released by GGG and shows the artwork and assets for act 4.

I’ve also included some of the official showcases at Exilecon2023 that show some of the Act 4 Bosses and information shared by Mark Roberts on Act 4 Islands, content, monsters and bosses.

Act 4 Content:

1) Karui Islands + Kingsmarch

Act 4 has us traveling between many different islands - Each Island has it’s own different Biome and theme with high diversity of different monsters and Bosses. One of the areas in Act 4 also appears to be the PoE1 town of Kingsmarch

Kingsmarch

Unknown how much of PoE1s Kingsmarch is in Act 4, the artbook does not go into more detail outside mentioning Kingsmarch is within Act 4. However we also have loading screens and images from Mark Roberts at Exilecon showing Act 4 includes Kingsmarch (EDIT: Kingsmarch is confirmed in Act 4, see EDIT at the top of the post)

“Parasite” island

A highly dangerous island overrun by mysterious and dangerous parasites

“Vanishing” island

An Island where all the Karui tribes that lived there mysteriously vanished on a night of the full moon (Possible werewolves / druid shapeshifters?)

Isle of Kin + Volcanic Warrens + Krutog, Lord of Kin Boss

Mentioned by Mark Roberts, an Act 4 island is inhabited by goblins who worship “Krutog, Lord of Kin” a Mid-Act Boss in the volcanic Barrens and bring him human sacrifices

Mark Roberts also confirms the existence of Sulfite in this Island as the boss eats it

The Blind Beast is another boss showcased by Mark Roberts at Exilecon and is a boss in the area before you fight Krutog
