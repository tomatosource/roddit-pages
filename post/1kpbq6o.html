Which player had a better career - Charles Barkley or Chris Paul?

Charles Barkley (1984-2000):

  • 11× NBA All-Star (1987–1997)

  • 1x NBA MVP (1993)

  • 1x All-Star Game MVP (1991)

  • 5× All-NBA First Team (1988–1991, 1993)

  • 5× All-NBA Second Team (1986, 1987, 1992, 1994, 1995)

  • 1x All-NBA Third Team (1996)

  • 1x NBA rebounding leader (1987)

  • 1x NBA Finals appearance

  • 2x Olympic Gold

Chris Paul (2005-2025):

  • 12× NBA All-Star (2008–2016, 2020–2022)

  • 1x All-Star Game MVP (2013)

  • 4× All-NBA First Team (2008, 2012–2014)

  • 5× All-NBA Second Team (2009, 2015, 2016, 2020, 2021)

  • 2× All-NBA Third Team (2011, 2022)

  • 7× NBA All-Defensive First Team (2009, 2012–2017)

  • 2× NBA All-Defensive Second Team (2008, 2011)

  • 5× NBA assists leader (2008, 2009, 2014, 2015, 2022)

  • 6× NBA steals leader (2008, 2009, 2011–2014)

  • Rookie of the Year (2006)

  • 1x NBA Finals appearance

  • 2x Olympic Gold

Both have similar accolades as far as team achievements and nominations go, with CP3 having more league leading stats, and defensive nominations. Charles Barkley has an MVP and a more iconic namesake due to his personality and TV career. CP3 has played in 4 more seasons and counting.

Sir Charles’ peak was arguably second best player in the league (MVP season and behind Jordan) while CP3 arguably had a case for top 3 player at one point (came 2nd in MVP to Kobe in 2008, behind LeBron skill wise).

Who had the better career if comparing them today?
