Armour as a Concept vs Player Expectations

First off, I dont want this to be a discussion comparing the balance of the game and a war of fairness between ES vs. HP (or lack thereof) since that has been discussed ad nauseam…

I want to offer a conceptual challenge to PoE players who are frustrated with armour’s current role and offer a thought provoking defense of current Caster vs. Warrior defenses.

Currently, as many have been made painfully aware, Armour is insanely good at protecting and shrugging off small and even medium hits, while Energy Shield excels at absorbing big hits but suffers from constant pestering small hits…

While I do understand the desire for Warriors to be TANKS and be unkillable, I see Path of Exile’s implementation as quite thematic fantasy-wise.

I dont believe full plate mail armor was ever meant to protect a warrior from being crumpled into a bent soda can by a giant monkey slamming a pillar directly onto their skull. However, a warrior holding a shield will not even notice small hits and most medium hits have little chance of even bypassing his towering shield.

Contrast this with a Wizard channeling a shield of energy… the pestering hits of a dozen wasps from a dozen angles will break the concentration of a wizard and wear down his focus, while the large hit can be absorbed by the huge pool of sheer willpower and concentration from the wizard and then need to be recharged during a window of vulnerability.

Forgive me for the flowery descriptions, but this is what I have always imagined being the case and my experience with PoE has fit pretty much what I expect in my fantasy brain.

So my question to the community… let’s just assume this is thematically what GGG is going for - is that really so wrong given proper implementation? I can understand being frustrated that the balance doesn’t feel quite right and maybe ES regen is too quick and accessible, but I have never understood why warriors should be nearly invulnerable from all variety of damage? If so, what is their weakness meant to be then?

P.S. After following 2 leagues (0.2 and 0.3) of Carnarius play warrior, I just fail to see armour being anywhere near as trash as Reddit seems to believe… he achieve near immortality both times and 1 was even without a shield.
