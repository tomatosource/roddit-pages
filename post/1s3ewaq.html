Assuming health, what’s your playoff rotation?

I am sticking with the same starting 5 of Queta, Tatum, Hauser, Brown and White.

Minutes is a whole different thing. To me Tatum, Brown, White and Pritchard play big minutes every game. Beyond that there is another tier of Queta, Vooch, Garza, Hauser, Baylor, Hugo and Walsh whose minutes depend entirely on what the game requires.

To me it’s not that the team is deep with overall talent, but they are deep with guys that really good at specific things.
