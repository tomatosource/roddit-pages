Top-selling NBA jerseys for the 2025-26 Season

The NBA released its top 15 best-selling jerseys for the 2025-26 season.

Full list:

  1. Stephen Curry

  2. Luka Dončić

  3. Jalen Brunson

  4. Victor Wembanyama

  5. LeBron James

  6. Anthony Edwards

  7. Jayson Tatum

  8. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

  9. Cooper Flagg

  10. Nikola Jokić

  11. Kevin Durant

  12. Tyrese Maxey

  13. Devin Booker

  14. Cade Cunningham

  15. LaMelo Ball

Notable: Flagg cracks the top 10 as a rookie. Jokić lands at #10 despite back-to-back MVP seasons. Jalen Brunson at #3. No Giannis or Kyrie in the top 15. LaMelo Ball will always have the youth.
