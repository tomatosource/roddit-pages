Suggestions from a new player who wants to stick around.

I have played PoE2 now for about 250 hours SSF level 89 Deadeye. Never played PoE1.

Here are my suggestions to GGG from a newbie perspective.

  1. Make crafting a bit more deterministic. The essences help a bit but trying to get more improved items for me is pick up base gamble orbs drop item if it shitrolls.

  2. Make some drops like waystones and orbs a bit more common. I have done 500% t15 maps without even getting a t15 waystone. Wtf that doesn’t make sense to me.

  3. Buff some of the activities. Expedition feels worthless without getting a logbook. Rewards are all i60 from shops.

  4. Follow the endgame pattern of trials/pinnacle bosses. Keep making more pinnacle bosses. Honestly I am not one to restart characters for leagues so if they keep making more pinnacle bosses I would love that.

  5. Gear design variety. I don’t mind that much that all gear is the same except unique but it would be cool if the prefix/suffix modifiers changes the look of items.

Thats all.
