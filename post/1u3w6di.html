Crafting feels unsatisfying and frustrating.

Let me start with the fact I genuinely am enjoying the endgame changes. I also completely agree with the essence nerfs. That was the most deterministic crafting on earth. That being said I have a few grievances.

  1. Picking up bases as opposed to just picking up currency is not fun. After the last Zizaran podcast with GGG, I can tell, Mark wants you to spend a half hour in each map identifying bases. That sounds like my own personal hell.

  2. Nerfing perfect currencies seems like the opposite thing you should do if you gut essences. If GGG wants me to buy a 50 divine base with ONE T1 mod, how about you make it so I don’t blow every ounce of currency I have just to brick an item because RNJesus wasn’t feeling it.

  3. ”#+ to skill” is arguably the worst vector of scaling damage I’ve ever seen in an ARPG. Especially with the drastic increase in mana consumption.

  4. Crafting bench for the love of all that is holy. You guys added a, sort of, harvest reforge but that doesn’t change the fact runes don’t solve the majority of issues with dead affixes. The whole “Slam and pray” stuff is getting pretty old.

I know half of this group has never played POE1 but it easily has the most rewarding crafting mechanics of any game I’ve ever played. GGG seems to just not want to take the lessons from the game they have developed for 13 years.
