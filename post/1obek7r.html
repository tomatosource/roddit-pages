NBA Starting 5 needs to come out sooner

The content is too good to be vaulted and released until right before the season starts, it needs to be released as a refresher, like a “previously on the NBA” type of thing.

Gets storylines moving again, get buzz and momentum heading into the season. But releasing right before week 1 means most people don’t even watch the full show.

Need that Hali/Shai showdown, KD and Harden in their final chapters of their careers, and Jaylen Brown right before he takes on a #1 option role for the very first time content during the dead time of the offseason.

Releasing now is like turning on the heater right before the sun comes up, it doesn’t hit the same.
