Bismack Biyombo and Mason Plumlee now have a chance to join Dwight Howard, Jared Dudley, Ish Smith and Svi Mykhailiuk as the only former Charlotte Hornet players to win NBA title since 2020

The San Antonio Spurs backup centers Bismack Biyombo (2011-15, 2018-21) and Mason Plumlee (2021-23, 2025-26) will now have a chance to join a rare group of former Charlotte Hornet players that had won the championship since 2020.

  • 2020 - Jared Dudley (2007-09) & Dwight Howard (2017-18)
  • 2023 - Ish Smith (2021-22)
  • 2024 - Svi Mykhailiuk (2023)

Other former Hornets that played in the Finals since 2020 were Frank Kaminsky (2021, Phoenix Suns), Caleb Martin (2023, Miami Heat), Cody Zeller (2023, Miami Heat) and PJ Washington (Dallas, 2024).
