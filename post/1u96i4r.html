Last Sunday, Jackson Chourio became the 20th-youngest player (and youngest Brewer) to hit 50 career HR

He got there 6 days younger than Adrian Beltre. Junior Caminero is 19th, he hit his 50th last September.

It seems like a trivial milestone until you go through the list. All of the top 30 (besides the two young JCs) had excellent, memorable careers.

The two worst players by WAR in the top 30 are Tony Conigliaro and Bob Horner, whose promising starts were derailed by injuries. In the top 50, the only mediocrity is Rougned Odor.

Greatness is likely for Chourio, health permitting.
