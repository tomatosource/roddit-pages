Acolyte of Chayula ES Leech Oversight/Bug

In 0.2 they gave Chonk “mana can be leeched from any damage” which is huge. However they removed “mana leech is instant”. This had the effect of making ES never leech since mana leech stops working when mana is full. So your only solution in the entire game to this as far as I am aware is to wear a uniqe pair of armor base gloves that give you “leech on critical is instint”. This not only forces you to wear a terrible unique for chonk but also forces you to stack crit chance. I find it pretty much impossible to believe that this is intended and more that it is an oversight or bug.

There are two ways to fix this issue.

  1. Simply give Chonk “mana leech is instant” back somewhere in the acendency tree. This is obviously very strong but they had it before and chonk is considered the weakest acendency rn anyway.

  2. Give “mana can be leeched even when mana is full” to the acendency tree. This is much weaker and will still fix the issue.

I really hope they can see and fix this. I also posted this on the poe2 bug forum.
