In Game Price Checking is a huge W

With all of the content coming in .5, I actually forgot entirely about the in game price checker that was shown until an interview I caught last night!

We have EE2 and Awakened for POE1 but that involves making and maintaining an overlay. Those of us who use Linux, specifically with Wayland, have some issues using it more recently. We still can but it’s not exactly simple and is prone to breaking.

This addition solves that issue entirely for us. Further, new players don’t have to go and download an app to use that takes them away from the game. A friend of mine found both add-ons to be a little finicky for him and rough to get used to, he is not a technical user and hates when things get complex in the least.

Take it one more level, console gamers and those of us who also play on a handheld and don’t have easy access or any access to those tools in game, this opens a direct use case for them to really join the trade world at a high level!

It’s a huge W all around I feel like.
