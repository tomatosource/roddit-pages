In 2020, the Houston Rockets field a starting line-up with no player taller than 6’7”. Five years later, they will be opening with a starting line-up in which their shortest player is 6’7”

In 2020, the Houston Rockets traded injured center Clint Capela (6’10”) for forward Robert Covington (6’7”), giving them another 3-and-D wing to improve spacing for Russell Westbrook. Their starting line-up, dubbed the “Pocket Rockets”, was the following:

PG - Russell Westbrook 6’4”
SG - James Harden 6’5”
SF - Danuel House 6’7”
PF - PJ Tucker 6’4”
C - Robert Covington 6’7”

Thanks to a season-ending injury to starting point guard Fred VanVleet (6’0”), the Rockets head into the 2025-2026 season without a proven point guard. To start the season, head coach Ime Udoka has said that he will slide third-year Amen Thompson (6’7”) into the point guard spot, and move veteran center Steven Adams (6’11”) into the starting line-up, deploying a “two big” strategy that was very effective the previous season.

PG - Amen Thompson 6’7”
SG - Kevin Durant 6’11”
SF - Jabari Smith 6’11”
PF - Alperen Sengun 6’11”
C - Steven Adams 6’11”
