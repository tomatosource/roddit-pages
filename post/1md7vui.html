Datamined info on Einhar + Scourge leagues! Most likely cut content

Stumbled across a few things on poe2db.tw and saw barely anything about them. One thing you should know about the “lore” portion of the poe 2 database (poe2db.tw) is that there’s a lot of redundant information pulled from PoE 1. However, in general, it’s sorted roughly so that the PoE 2 stuff is at the bottom of the page. If you follow the links below, you’ll see what I mean.

You should also understand the formatting structure. For example, “FinnOnEinhar” will be the voiceline Finn (the NPC from Act 1) says when you click on the “Einhar” option in his dialogue option box. Generally, the first word is the speaker. Abbreviations or alternate names are used for certain characters, like WifeBoss is the ghost lady you fight in the crypt, or Risu (Act 2 NPC) is called Defector.

#Einhar in PoE 2, Act 1

Source (bottom ~6 entries): https://poe2db.tw/us/FlavourText?q=einhar

The implication here is that Einhar and the Beast Hunting mechanic was at one point intended to be an Act 1 quest, taking place in the hunting grounds. Additional support for this is the fact that Einhar is an Ezomyte (from a one-off voiceline in PoE 1), and the area is named “Hunting Grounds”, as well as the design of the beast with the bell.

It’s 100% possible that beast crafting was cut from the game (as it is somewhat annoying, even in PoE 1) and replaced with Ritual. I think it’s equally possible he’s intended to be introduced in a future update, once they animate and incorporate all the beasts.

#Scourge, Act 3

Source (bottom 5 entries): https://poe2db.tw/us/FlavourText?q=crucible

It seems that there are voicelines in the game where Oswald remarks on you finding the Blood Crucible, which was a PoE 1 artifact used in the Scourge League, where the Beyond monsters were introduced. The blood crucible item can also be found on the website, but it doesn’t seem to have anything new, note even updated art. I didn’t really play Scourge league so I don’t really have much else to say about it, but I do know that story-wise it would make sense for Alva to have it. This could simply be a small side quest that sort of closes the loop on how she got it, or it could be a way to introduce league content in act 3.

That’s it! I searched for literally every other league and a bunch of keywords related to them, but this is literally everything I could find.
