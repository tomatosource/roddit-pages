Endgame Improvements

Just wanted to toss out some suggestions that I think could help spice up the endgame a bit in POE 2.

Delirium: Distilled Emotions are cool, but I really miss the old variety in delirium rewards. Can we bring back the different types like jewels, currency, maps, and armor? I’m not saying we need to mix in essences or breach stuff if you’re trying to keep mechanics separated, but give us something else to chase besides just emotions. It’d make farming delirium way more interesting and less samey.

Expedition: When expedition shows up in maps, it’d be cool if the chest rewards matched their respective NPCs. Rog chests = armors, Tujen = belts/jewelry, etc; higher level map = higher tier drops. That kind of target farming would actually make it worth interacting with more often instead of just going through the motions.

As for recombinators, we need to raise the success floor a bit no? It feels like I’m constantly just picking 2 affixes and deleting items which makes me not want to engage unless I’m clearing out tabs. I agree that we should be able to select 3 affixes from each and have it work, but I think you have to give a little.

Also, can we please get like one or two more steps in the expedition crafting process? I don’t think it should make mirror-tier items or anything, but it feels like it just stops short of being satisfying.

Irradiated Maps: If the idea is exploring outwards, then it’d be cool if the further out you go from the starting point, the more challenging (and rewarding) things get. Higher monster levels, more loot, more risk/reward. Feels like a natural way to encourage full clear exploration rather than beelining nexuses, citadels and towers.

Anyway, just some ideas. Would love to hear what others think.
