Crunch time & buzzer beaters

I understand that Jayson Tatum is the #1 guy on the team and that the team is built around him. Him taking the final shot is the responsibility of being the #1 options. However, maybe we should switch it up? Each team knows that the Celtics team is built around Tatum being the #1 option, therefore, each time crunch time and buzzer beaters are about to happen the opponents defense is going to swarm Tatum. We have other guys like Brown, Porzingis, Pritchard, and White. There are plenty of other scorers on the team. Maybe it’s time to switch it up and have other plays that way we have an advantage against other teams and can trick them. I’m a huge Tatum fan and still firmly believe he’s an elite player and the #1 option, but he did take some very questionable last second shots during the game against Atlanta. The Celtics are the defending champs and an elite team, but switching things up and having alternative plays will give us the winning advantage because other teams won’t know what to expect.
