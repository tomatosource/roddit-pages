Pitcher K’s By Zone 2025

One identifying characteristic of pitchers that doesn’t get talked about a whole lot is strikeout pitch location. I decided to do a deep dive into this metric. Using Baseball Savant’s search metric, I looked at strikeout pitches in each of the nine attack zones (middle/middle, up/away, down/in, etc.), against both left handed and right handed hitters.

I’m not necessarily interested here in domination of each zone (I didn’t look at the strikeout rate among pitches in each zone). Instead, I looked at all of a pitchers strikeouts and calculated the proportion of those strikeouts in each zone. The idea is to get a sense, when scouting pitchers ahead of matchups, of where he tries to place his putaway pitches. I’ll call this metric K-location %, or K-loc %.

Below is a top-five list of pitchers in K-location % for each zone, against right handed hitters. (min 50 K vs R). If people are interested in seeing more, I can do the same for left handed hitters.
