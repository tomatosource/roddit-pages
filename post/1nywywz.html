Ritual Tablet drop rate seems incredibly low in 0.3.1

One of the first things I noticed post patch was that self-sustaining Ritual Tablets let alone having an abundance of them would be really difficult.

My Experience: After running a whole 3 tower setup + pack size atlas tree + 50%+ pack size map + 2 post patch Ritual tablets and irradiated tablet focussed on the suffixes, I only got 2 Ritual tablets back. Curious, I decided to do 3x pack size tablets, 1 ritual and any maps I could find that had old tower Pack size. I almost burn through the 10 uses before I find another Ritual Tablet.

This is such a stark contrast to Breach Tablets where you can just run a juice strat and be swimming in so much tablets that you can self sustain running only well rolled BIS mods on 2 breach tablets every map.

Seems really rough for players without any old Ritual tower setups/SSF players. Can’t even get into the mechanic let alone choose your desired tablet modifiers. This goes without saying how much worse Ritual became with tower overlaps removed. Was this an unintended consequence of towers making 30+ maps in range affected per tablet compared to the 10 uses now? and the open layout pack size nerfs as well?

Edit: After searching and asking around in different platforms, it seems it might be the result of a bug disproportionately affecting players? If so, let’s hope for a quick hotfix :)
