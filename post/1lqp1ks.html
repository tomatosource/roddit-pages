The narrative around him since the 2021 Brooklyn Nets days has been mostly negative and too many people seem to forget that he is (in my opinion) one of the 5 greatest pure scorers this game has ever seen. And he’s still efficient to this day. He just finished a season averaging 52/43/84 shooting splits in year freaking 17.
