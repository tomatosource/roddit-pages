PoE 2 is heavily inspired by Diablo 2

Just started playing Diablo 2 Resurrected (first time) after playing 1000+ hours in PoE 2 and cant get off from deja vu effect.

Acts/story structure are the same:

1 act - Forest/castle dark fantasy

2 act - Desert and arab culture motives (hell, we even collecting staff components same as horn components in poe)

3 act - Jungle and some old civilizations (with same enormous large areas lol)

And we are always late for destination, so moving forward.

UI looks really familiar with demons/angels holding orbs of mana/health.

Enormous amount of runes (well, poe 2 doesn’t combine them in words tho)

Some pretty close item modifiers like + to skill levels and of course magic find/light radius.

Even some skills looks familiar (fireballs, blinks, chains etc)

It feels especially the same at early levels (didn’t reach even nightmare difficulty, but from what I seen, it has less breaking the game combos in late, where you blow up few screens)

Just sharing, it seems oddly fun to me
