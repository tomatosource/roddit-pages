Is the fortress too long, so we leave it being too strong?

By the time I left the fortress, did breach and deli quest; I could already face normal pinnacles and any quest pinnacles were way too weak.

The fortress has around 65 mandatory maps. I could reach tier 15 maps inside the fortress by map ~25. That’s 30 tier 15s with deli/breach worth of loot before I can even interact with grand mirrors or breach hives in the atlas.

I don’t think the fortress is “long” as in “the time I spent inside”. I think it’s too big as in sixty five maps is too much loot and power, it’s not an introduction to the endgame, it’s just the endgame.

Now all maps without intensive content are traveling maps. I don’t need to farm breach and spawn hives and find them, I already have the breach items I wanted. I hadn’t done ONE delirius map (the ones with permafog) and I had already instilled my amulet with highest tier emotions that I dropped myself: in the fortress.

Every quest version of a pinnacle was trivial, and how strong the build was or not is non-important because: sixty five maps worth of currency make a lot of builds just work. Not oneshot pinnacles, but definitely face them.

The fortress should be cut by half as long and half as wide in dimensions , only need 1 fragment for arbiter of ash AND connect matriarch/patriarch halls. I should leave the fortress at lower than tier 15 maps and lower than level 85s-90s so I have a reason to go to the atlas and do/find cool things.

I feel like the character is “finished” (as in able to do all content) WAY before doing all content. And the culprit is the fortress.
