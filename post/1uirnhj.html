World Cup fans at MLB and the growth of the sport

I know that baseball as a sport is played in the Netherlands, Czechia etc. have small but strong baseball communities and fans. That said I do genuinely wonder if we will see any significant fandom develop in Europe as a result of all of the fans in the country for the FIFA World Cup hanging out at MLB games during their downtime. It’s been really fun to see the Norwegians, the Scots etc. all bring that type of energy to stadiums thus far. It’s made for an extremely unique and fun season..

Also I wish we as fans were as loud, sang songs the way soccer fans do. That shit rules.

Any EU fans or people experiencing MLB from Europe for the first time this summer? Would love to hear that perspective on things.
