I love PoE 2 and I wanted to share it with you

I had never played an ARPG before Path of Exile 2. I come from League of Legends and other battle royale games, and I’ve never been this invested in a video game before PoE2. I tried PoE1 and I liked it, but PoE2 feels different.

So I wanted to make this post to share my hype about the new season, even though this patch has disappointed a lot of people. The game is really good — sure, it gets criticized a lot — but I truly enjoy it and I think it’s worth playing. I encourage new players not to listen too much to the negativity around the game, but simply to play it themselves.

Also, don’t watch tons of videos about the endgame, how to progress fast, or how to farm divines quickly. Just play the game, and eventually you’ll start making profit. Once you’re in, you’ll understand.
