Western Conference Playoff Tiebreakers

With Minnesota’s win over Memphis last night, the Western conference standings now stand thus (source: https://www.nba.com/standings):

  1. Thunder (66-14)
  2. Rockets (52-28)
  3. Lakers (49-31)
  4. Nuggets (48-32)
  5. Clippers (48-32)
  6. Warriors (48-32)
  7. Grizzlies (47-33)
  8. Wolves (47-33)

Notably, the possibility of 6 teams (seeds 3 through 8) all ending up tied at 33 losses is still in play. This would require:

  • Lakers to lose out
  • Nuggets lose to Grizzlies on Friday, win vs. Rockets on Sunday
  • Clippers and Warriors going 1-1 (they play each other Sunday)
  • Grizzlies and Wolves winning out

This would result in a 6-way tie. The NBA’s tiebreaker procedures (https://ak-static-int.nba.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2017/06/NBA_Tiebreaker_Procedures.pdf) are as follows:

  1. Division winners
  2. Win % among tied teams
  3. Division win % (only if teams are in the same division)
  4. Conference win %
  5. Win % vs playoff teams in conference
  6. Total point differential

Once a team is taken out of the tie, the tiebreaker criteria starts over from the top with the remaining.

Now let’s apply this tiebreak procedure to this 6-way tie:

  1. First we need to figure out the Pacific division winner, since the 3 candidates (Lakers, Clippers, Warriors) are in this clump. Among them, the Lakers have the best H2H (6-2 vs. the other 2 teams), so they get the Pacific division crown and end up as the 3rd seed.

  2. Now let’s repeat with the remaining 5 teams. The Wolves have the best H2H record here, going a combined 9-5 vs. the others (the Clippers can also go 9-5 if they win vs. the Warriors, but they lose in the H2H tiebreaker against the Wolves, having gone 0-3 against them this year). This means they’re the 4th seed.

  3. Now let’s repeat with the remaining 4 teams. Here the Clippers get the nod, going either 9-2 or 8-3 vs. the others (depending on the vs. Warriors result on Sunday, which doesn’t end up mattering for this tiebreaker). They get the 5th seed.

  4. Rinse and repeat with the remaining 3 teams. The Warriors have the best H2H, at 4-3, so they get the 6th seed.

  5. Finally, our play-in teams are the Nuggets and the Grizzlies, who both have coincidentally fired their coaches recently. The Grizzlies end up 7th due to their 2-1 season series vs. the Nuggets. This means the Nuggets are 8th.

The end of season standings would then be:

  1. Thunder
  2. Rockets
  3. Lakers
  4. Wolves
  5. Clippers
  6. Warriors
  7. Grizzlies
  8. Nuggets
