In 1987 Nolan Ryan became the 2nd SP ever to have 34 starts and 0 CG

Ryan pitched a complete game on April 25, 1986, the 203rd of his career, but not another until May 13, 1988, a span that included his entire age 40 1987 season. (In fairness, he pitched 9 innings of a 14 inning game September 19.)

His uncustomary limit may have contributed to better command than ever. He achieved a league-leading 2.76 ERA, 6.5 H/9, and 270 K that gave him career-highs 11.5 K per 9 and 3.10 K/BB, the only time he led in that category.

In June he made consecutive starts with 0 walks allowed which had not happened since October 1980.

Despite an 8-16 record, he finished 5th in CYA voting, losing to recently converted reliever Steve Bedrosian who, 2 years earlier, captured GM imaginations as workload pioneer by becoming the first SP ever with 34+ starts (37) and 0 CG.

Three more SP joined the club in 1992 and then it’s off to the races. Only three pitchers have made 34 starts in a season this decade.
