Sort Button

While I understand that the inventory capacity limit is a design choice (though I still think it’s too small), the lack of sorting or one-click organization features is a concern.

These elements don’t add to the strategic depth; they simply increase the burden on the player. Since these are now standard quality-of-life features, I believe the developers should consider adding organization tools to improve the overall gameplay experience.

In fact, there are times when the hassle of organizing items makes me hesitate to play. I understand that monetizing the stash is part of the business model, but even if you pay for it, you still can’t sort your gear. If that makes the game harder to play, I think it defeats the whole purpose… friction? ah…
