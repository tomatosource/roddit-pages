Who would you “Moneyball” Nba equivalent be?

I just finished watching moneyball, a movie where back in the early 2000s, the Oakland A’s couldn’t afford to chase baseball’s biggest names. GM Billy Beane used a “Moneyball” approach, finding undervalued players who were elite at one thing (like getting on base) even if they weren’t stars in the traditional sense. It was all about maximizing value per dollar.

In the NBA, the same idea could work: skip the max contracts, load your roster with guys who are elite shooters, defenders, rebounders, or playmakers (players the market doesn’t price like stars but who make a big impact on winning). I was wondering what players would be the NBA equivalent of undervalued guys that contribute to a team that could potentially make a run in playoffs? Obviously the cap rules are different between MLB and NBA but its the offseason and I have too much time on my hands. For example: Westbrook is potentially a David Justice, older vet past his prime but can have productive bursts and lead a locker room. (maybe Bradley beal on his new contract as well)

My team would be as follows:

Westbrook - Reasons above Caruso - pesky defensive guard making &lt;10 million Sam Hauser - 3 pt specialist making around the same as Caruso Naji Marshall - wing defender and slasher making 9 million Luke Kornet/ Brook Lopez: cheap big men who can hit the the 3 and stretch the floor, but also provide rim protection

Does a team like this get to the play-in? Who would your starting 5 be?
