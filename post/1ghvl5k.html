Through six undefeated games Donovan Mitchell is averaging 28.8 minutes per game. Thats 5.3 minutes lower than his career average.

Source: https://www.statmuse.com/nba/player/donovan-mitchell-9592

Hopefully this lower burden translates to less injuries for the Cavs this season.
