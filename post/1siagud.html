The Phoenix Suns: The Ugliest Team in the NBA?

Watching the Lakers/Suns game tonight it really struck me: I’m pressed to find a single cutie on the Phoenix Suns. For a sport that has positions 1-5, I’m struggling to see anything beyond a bunch of 3’s fill the court.

Dillon Brooks (1⁄10), the de facto team leader with Booker out, may be the singular ugliest man in the league. But his supporting players are doing nothing to pick up the slack - Amir Coffey (2⁄10), Mark Williams (4⁄10), Ryan Dunn (3⁄10), Royce O’Neal (3⁄10), Grayson Ellis (looks elderly/10) - the list goes on and on. Even Booker skates by off talent/aura alone and would just be a kind of freaky 6’5 dude in the wild if not for his abilities.

I watch a fair amount of teams and I can’t recall seeing one so bereft of a single cutie, let alone multiple, and because of it am frankly astounded by their (albeit minor) success this season.

I will concede Jalen Green has a strong jawline and Oso has a kind smile. However, in my eyes, that alone is not enough to tip the scales in any meaningful direction.
