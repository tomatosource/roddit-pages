I have a solution to the infinite atlas problem

The main talking point to keep the infinite atlas is the sense of exploration and visual immersion but as you play the endgame the atlas becomes horrible to navigate. My solution to that is islands. Once you reach the endgame you start in the mainland/main island and follow the questline for arbitter and to t15 maps. After that you travel to one of the ports to sail to a different randomly generated island.

You can influence the generation with favorite maps, blocking the maps you dont like to run, more chance for unique maps or anomaly bosses etc whatever GGG decides to give us as tools. If you dont like the generation or done with that island you path to a port to sail and generate a different island for yourself. Once you leave the island, its gone. You can always return back to the main island to fight arbitter or open reliquary keys etc.

This way the atlas keeps being clean and easy to navigate no matter how long you have been playing the endgame. It keeps the exploration part of the infinite atlas without the downsides (atleast that I can think of). It lets us influence the generation to some extend and I believe performance would be much better than what we have now.

Let me know what you think.
