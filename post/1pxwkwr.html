Achieved my league goal, sharing thoughts on the current game state.

Finished my hardcore lv 95 ranger (lightning arrow still works!) and just wanted to share thoughts.

  • The game is fun!

  • Most fun farming for me was spamming abyss on maps. Abyss is a great combination of risk and reward. You really have to focus and pay attention (at least in hc) to win, and you get rewarded by drops that are high value in trade AND are good crafting tools for ssf.

  • Best feeling in the game: The rhoa mount is the best feeling in the game. Slap it on at level 56-ish and FINALLY you don’t have to dodge roll anymore!

  • Fate of the Vaal mechanic: I did not like or enjoy the vaal temple mechanic. Not for me. Not into the aztec aesthetic, don’t like oneshot slam bosses in small rooms, don’t like maze layouts, don’t want to play a puzzle game, I want to cruise through maps. Not sure whether other people agree.

  • Droprates : The droprates for most orbs feel out of balance. I consistently had way more regal, exalts and alchemy orbs than I could ever use or need, not to mention all the blue orbs. Lesser and greater jewelers orbs were also totally valueless since they dropped so often. Only divines and up were exciting drops. I think POE1’s orb system worked better overall.

Overall I enjoyed POE2 a lot and might play again when swords are added.
