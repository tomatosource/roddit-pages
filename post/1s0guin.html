The Los Angeles Lakers are appealing to the NBA to rescind Luka Doncic’s 16th technical foul which will otherwise result in a one-game suspension.

The Los Angeles Lakers have already appealed to the NBA to rescind Luka Doncic’s technical foul from L.A.’s 105-104 win on Saturday, a team source told ESPN, while Doncic and Orlando Magic center Goga Bitadze offered differing accounts of what led to the tech being called.

It was Doncic’s 16th technical of the season. If it is not rescinded, he will have to serve a one-game suspension when the Lakers play the Detroit Pistons on Monday for reaching the threshold.
