Notable Matchups and Playoff Implications for April 8th

Ahead of the 10-game slate tonight, here are the notable matchups with playoff seeding implications:

Atlanta Hawks vs. Orlando Magic | 7:00 p.m. ET

  • Atlanta can leap Orlando for the 7 seed with a win via the tiebreaker.

Chicago Bulls vs Cleveland Cavaliers | 7:00 p.m. ET

  • Cleveland can clinch the number 1 seed in the East with a win.
  • Chicago remains the 9 seed with a win OR loss.

Memphis Grizzlies vs Charlotte Hornets | 7:00 p.m. ET

Boston Celtics vs New York Knicks | 7:30 p.m. ET

Los Angeles Lakers vs Oklahoma City Thunder | 8:00 p.m. ET

Minnesota Timberwolves vs Milwaukee Bucks | 8:00 p.m. ET

Golden State Warriors vs Phoenix Suns | 10:00 p.m. ET
