About huge Curse nerfs.

So in the last mini patch there was a HUGE all-around nerf to Curse area, values in the Tree nodes and Jewels got 2x lower (halved) and the scaling got 3x lower in the gem after lvl 20.
And it seems like it specifically targeted Doedre’s Undoing, cause it was changed to scale with the Area instead of Int…
But of course because of these changes all the other builds that didn’t use Doedre’s Undoing but were still casually utilizing curses got hit quite hard and basically now using Cursed Ground as, say, Storm Weaver, is not a reasonable option. Blasphemy is also hit very hard.

Wouldn’t it be better to leave Curse Area scaling everywhere as it was and just let Doedre’s Undoing have 20-25% less Curse Area on a gem? The Doedre’s Undoing users would still min-max area and have big circle, but all other builds won’t be hit just cause of a single support gem they don’t use.
