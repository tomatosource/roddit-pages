Abyss feedback

Hello! I’ve ran countless maps with abyss this league so I thought about sharing some feedback about it, maybe some people can relate and who knows maybe GGG ends up finding it useful.

Things I like

  1. Amanamu void: this was extremely unfun to run until the latest changes, big W to GGG for the changes as it makes engaging with the void mechanic less tiresome;
  2. Abyss tree feels quite alright, feels like you can spec into different abyss strategies such as rogue exile farming, omen farming or coffer farming, not bad for a first iteration of the tree;
  3. Making abyssal echoes more common and light less common was a good change IMO - rerolling unveils is very nice early on whereas using lights is more high end;
  4. In general, nice league mechanic to run with maps and feels quite rewarding with raw currency drops and omen drops.

Things I hate

  1. Snake invincibility. This is EXTREMELY unfun, especially if you somehow slow their action speed, they just stay invincible for literal 5 seconds - I mean, that is if they stop chasing you around or don’t glitch their pathing to you. Please get rid of this - when paired with undying will and 200% deli maps it’s certain death, you get stuck in the mobs, don’t know what is going on and rip;
  2. Pits having collision. Easy to get stuck on and mobbed by the monsters. Please get rid of collision, it’s extremely hard to deal with this with undying will as well (maybe just get rid of undying will?);
  3. Abyssal depths kinda meh. I tend to skip it 95% of the time, hardly rewarding, sometimes I get abyssal echoes/lights from random mobs inside but the troves are underwhelming. Maybe make this similar to poe 1 mirage where you get down and you end up with the same number of pits you had in the regular map? Idk.;
  4. Lightless void - this one is a big skip, big waste of time;
  5. Maps being overrun by abyss should be more common - way infrequent imo;
  6. Pits spawning in boss room. This paired with 200% deli is very miserable, in general I don’t think it’s a good idea. Unless you make it so that the pit is empty and the boss is the only monster, when you kill it, it ressurects from the pit, kinda ligue rogue exiles. Opportunity to also grant exclusive rewards when map boss is influenced by the abyss.
  7. Ancient bones are too rare IMO, I’ve been running 150% rarity 8 moded jado + 200% deli and I barely see any bones at all. Maybe I drop like 1 ancient bone in a week.
