A little bit of sunshine pumping

The flat truth is we are spoiled as Celtics fans. A lot of fans watch their teams for decades and don’t sniff the success that we take for granted.

A team doesn’t have to win a title to be a fun team to watch and root for, and a team you can be proud of. Only one team is happy at the end of the year, and it doesn’t equal failure if you’re not that team. Winning titles is hard, and depends a lot on luck.

I had an absolute blast being a Celtics fan this year. We were supposed to be garbage. But we got the second seed in the east.

I had fun watching JB come into his own, have phenomenal season and make his way into the MVP conversation. Score 50 on the Clippers.

Tatum was out for en entire year with an Achilles tear. But then he did something unheard of, something amazing and not only came back to play, but came back to play at an extremely high level. Remember how it felt watching 0 come back out and playing?

I loved watching Baylor and Hugo and Walsh make strides to becoming legit NBA players.

I loved watching Queta turn into a monster in the middle.

I loved watching Prichard flummox much bigger players in the paint to get open shots, and bomb threes from the logo to beat the buzzer.

I have fun watching DWhite do his best Motumbo, getting block after block no guard has any right to get.

There is no front office in the league I would trust more than ours to make the right moves over the offseason.

I can’t wait till next year. I think we’ll have a shot at the title. But whether we win it or not, LETS GO CELTICS! I’m both lucky and proud this is my team.
