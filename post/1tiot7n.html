Spears deserve better than this

I’ve always had a soft spot for spears, so when they finally got announced I was genuinely excited. That excitement didn’t last long.

Trying to theorycraft a physical spear build right now basically means picking Rake and calling it a day. And Rake… is a bleed skill? On a spear? I get that any weapon can technically cause bleeding, but it feels completely disconnected from what a spear is. Spears thrust. They pierce. They don’t really… rake.

Lightning Spear is fine. Spear Field & Rapid Assault too, kind of. But half the skill kit plays like a bow build that forgot to equip a bow, and the melee skills are so generic you’d never guess they were spear-specific.

I just want skills that actually feel like spear skills. Distinct thrusting attacks, javelin throws, some kind of charge or impale mechanic. The Javazon fantasy is right there and it’s a start, but give me more! I want skills and animations that make the weapon feel distinct and dangerous, not just reskinned sword moves with a longer hitbox.

Between the underwhelming skills and a short spear, designed to be paired with a buckler, being the only available type, I am a sad panda.
