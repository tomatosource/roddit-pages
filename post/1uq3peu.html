Unpopular opinion: Why Brad chose Tatum over Brown

Other than for the luxury tax reason..

1.. IMO - Brown’s best season was when he suddenly played more like Magic Johnson than trying to be “the man”. During the Celts championship run, Brown was driving and passing like a machine. He would also pass up jump shots for easier ones. He made everyone else better and sacrificed/was more selective regarding his scoring output.

2.. After the championship season. And winning playoffs MVP. Brown slowly reverted back to his “You can’t stop me” ways. He would drive to the basket against mutiple defenders. Make some tough baskets. Also gain calls.

However - I’m not sure if this sat well with Mazulla or Stevens. They likely preferred “the share the scoring wealth/touches” system.

3.. Tatum on the other hand has become less of a “you can’t stop me” type player. And a more willing passer. His injury/recovery also forced him to place less stress on his game (difficult baskets) and rely more on his teammates (good passing game).

4.. From an eyetest - Brown’s defense looks like took a little bit of a hit also. He could be tired - trying to make difficult baskets.

Is the way Brown plays necessarily a bad thing? No. The league and especially the Celtics are too 3 pt trigger happy. Having someone like Brown who likes to drive to the basket - helps keep opposing Ds on their feet.

But I wonder, if opponents realized that whenever Brown got the ball. 3 out 4 touches meant he was going to head to the basket. Likely with no kickouts or passes.

People like Brad, Joe who seem like are obsessed with analytics - seem to double down on their 3 and D system. And Brown’s current game - didn’t seem to fit this narrative no longer.
