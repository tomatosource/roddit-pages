Stash Tabs need an overhaul too.

Currently there are a lot of stash tabs that have:

  • Overlapping associations (Breach splinters go where now?)
  • Inconsistent design layouts (compare map to gem tab)
  • Functionality gaps (Why can’t I instil in the Delirium tab and avoid a voyage?)
  • Activities split across multiple tabs. (Maps here, tablets there, splinters here, boss keys there)
  • Still some types that have no tab (Jewels)
  • Many opportunities to allow filtering, and preset searches are missing.
  • There is STILL no sort button

I think at some stage GGG should stand back and take a look holistically at what the tabs are for, and where players want to find their items.

The “Fragments” tab, while a needed addition, overlaps a lot of content type, breach splinters also go here AND the breach tab, and I have to move them to my inventory, de-stack a pile of 300, and rejoin them to get the merge to occur.

If I am mapping, I want to get my maps and tablets in one place.

If I am doing breach, I want all my Breach gear including the boss keys in one tab
