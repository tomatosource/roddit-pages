Performance issues

I want to start off by saying I absolutely enjoyed the game this patch (0.5) and the mew content that got added, also had my first try at SSF but I don’t think I’ll be able to go on like this.

I’ve got a Ryzen 5 5600X, a regular 3060 and 16GB of RAM, played during 0.3 and although it got kind of frame-drop-y at some points at the endgame, nothing was ever ever close to the performance issues I’m experiencing today, while having played on an HDD as well. The only thing I’ve changed during my time playing are getting an ssd and resetting windows to de-bloat my system.

It got to the point where I can’t play 3 maps without a deadlock coming up if I alt-tab from the game or it completely lagging out on bosses and resulting in me getting one-shot. I don’t even have a build that’d be particle heavy, plus I have all my settings as low as possible..

I really hope they can do something about this because it combined to all content disappearing when you die to a boss, it just makes the game unplayable for me.

Edit: Switching to DirectX12 seems to have done wonders. It still isn’t seamless by any means but looks a lot more promising.
