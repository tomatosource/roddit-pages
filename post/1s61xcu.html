Payton Pritchard is small Jamal Crawford…

Similarities:

Jamal Crawford and Payton Pritchard spent their first seasons establishing themselves as score-first guards who can alternate between starting and coming off the bench. While their scoring numbers scaled differently due to team context, Crawford being on a rebuilding Bulls and Knicks teams vs Pritchard on a championship-contending Celtics, their roles and immediate impact are very similar to each other over their first 5 years.

Their 5 year stretches respectively being:

Prichard (2020-25’).

MIN: 20.2 | PPG: 9.1 | AST: 2.5 | STL: .5 | TO: .8 | RPG: 2.7 | FG %: .454 | 3PT%: .399 | GP: 347 | GS: 17

Crawford(00’-05’).

MIN: 28.7 | PPG: 12.7 | AST: 3.9 | STL: 1.1 | TO: 1.9 | RPG: 2.5 | FG %: .397| 3PT%: .347 | GP: 317 | GS: 185

Even their early playstyles are very consistent in their reliance on elite ball-handling and high-volume perimeter shooting. Both players entered the league with deep range (respective era) and the confidence to take unscripted, difficult shots that most role players would avoid. Both players handles are very fast and twitchy, allowing them to get to almost any spot on the floor and have a decent look (not always). These abilities are the reason these two are so “boom or bust” while still being high impact players as the 4-5th best player.

A KEY DIFFERENCE…

In their play styles is their handles. Crawford’s handle is relaxed and fluid, uses lots of lengthy combos, uses a lot misdirection to create space, and is very creative/free. Pritchards handle is extremely low and fast to the ground, and uses alot of physical factors as acceleration, deceleration, strength to force the defender off balance to create space. The best way to describe it Crawford keeps his head up to find way around you while Prichard will stick his head down and will find a way through you.
