Why aren’t bullpen games the norm?

I’m a relatively casual fan, come in and out of the sport, recently got on board the Jays bandwagon (still sad) and playing a lot of The Show.

So with all that.

When I hear about “third time through the order” OPS jumps and the heat that bullpens are throwing these days, it gets me thinking.

Wouldn’t it make sense for a manager to have like 4 guys in various, mixed up orders throwing absolute STUFF on a regular basis, so batters never really get used to what’s coming? That’s the point of having specialists like middle relievers and closers, right?

Why leave one guy up on the mound for 5-6 innings when you can mix it up even more?

Is is just tradition, vibes and stats? I know it’s fun watching a duel like we got between Scherzer and Ohtani in game 7 but I wonder.
