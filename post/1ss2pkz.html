[Charania] Oklahoma City Thunder’s Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has won the 2025-26 NBA Clutch Player of the Year award.

Source: https://www.espn.com/contributor/shams-charania

Oklahoma City Thunder’s Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has won the 2025-26 NBA Clutch Player of the Year award.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the NBA with 175 total clutch points and 6.5 points per game in clutch time (games within five points in the final five minutes of the fourth quarter or overtime).

He also made an NBA-leading 16 go-ahead field goals in clutch time.
