Walker Kessler has a chance to set an NBA record tonight - Most offensive rebounds in a single game

As of halftime, Walker Kessler has 18 rebounds - 10 of which are offensive.

This isn’t the most in an NBA half - that record goes to Charles Barkley who grabbed 13 rebounds in 1987 - but it puts Kessler on pace to potentially break the record for most offensive rebounds in a single game.

Tthe current record for most offensive rebounds is a three-way tie between Zaza Pachulia, Dennis Rodman and Charles Oakley with 18. While it’s possible the Jazz pull him to secure another loss in their shameless tank against a Raptors team that is also shamelessly tanking, losing by 11 at half gives some leeway for Kessler to potentially play long enough to top this NBA record.
