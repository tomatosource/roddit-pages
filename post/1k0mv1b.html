Increased item rarity on gear is mandatory.

Increased item rarity is as mandatory as resistances or health on gear. True, you’re not gonna die if you don’t have sufficient increased rarity, but, unless you play hardcore, the only additional downside dying has at the moment is xp loss. Without item rarity you’re still going to lose the map (because you don’t get any significant drops), you’re still going to waste time and you won’t be closer to your next upgrade.

Get 100% combined increased rarity on gear and treat it like an additional requirement for any piece you buy or craft. It sucks, I hate this state of things and I wish the baseline for drop rarity was much higher, but doing otherwise means the game rolls on the garbage section of the loot tables. Everyone here complaining, me included, that the loot sucks is victim of this mechanic. Fix your health, fix your res, fix your luck.
