Draymond Green: “I’m gonna have less than 10 technical fouls this year”

Green made the seemingly impossible prediction on his podcast, which he now co-hosts alongside former Warrior Baron Davis. Green said he would get T’d up “less than 10” times, and even contemplated setting a lower goal than that.

“I’m gonna have less than 10 technical fouls this year,” Green said. “I might even make a decision to keep it even less than eight, but I’ve got to make a conscious decision and I don’t even know if I want to keep it at eight. But I’m definitely going to have less than 10.
