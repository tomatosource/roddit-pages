We’ve established that the Nike Kobe 5 and 6 are the most popular models among NBA players, but with hundreds of colorways released over the years, which one stood out this season?

That honor goes to the Kobe 6 Protro “Caitlin Clark,” which Nike describes as featuring a “light armory blue, white, and Baltic blue” colorway. Since its public release on Nov. 11, 2025, the colorway has emerged as the NBA’s most‑worn shoe by minutes played every month since December, logging a combined 12,625 minutes on court, according to Kickstats.
