Now it is time to explain how this works and add some caveats.

First, the caveat: this does not account for the strength of the opponent, playoff positioning, injuries, etc. It also doesn’t account for the pre-season expectations for each team. The only data used was just an in-order list of all wins and losses.

Here is the overview: For each team, iterated through every streak of games in the 2025 season (Starting games 1 to 162, Length 1 to 162, such that start game + length is less than or equal to 163). For each streak, I calculated the Cumulative Beta-Binomial odds that a team would do better or worse than their actual record during the streak. The Alpha and Beta values used were the teams’ wins and losses plus one, respectively, before the streak. The n was the streak length, and the x was the number of wins in the streak.

Let’s work on an example streak to explain, the Tigers’ Games 4 to 11 streak, where they went 7-1. Before the streak, the Tigers were 0-3 after being swept by the Dodgers. This allows us to calculate the Beta Distribution of their win probability by Game 4. The odds that the Tigers would go 0-3, given some underlying win probability, are (3 choose 0) * p^0 * (1-p)^3. Luckily, we can scale this up by some factor (which in this case is 4) to get a PDF of p such that the integral from p1 to p2 of the PDF(p) with respect to p is the odds that the underlying win probability in any given game for the Tigers is between p1 and p2. In this case, PDF(p) = -4p^3 + 12p^2 - 12p + 4 (such that p is between 0 and 1). This PDF(p) is also Beta(1,4).

Now, using this data, we can calculate the odds of the Tigers winning 7 or more of their next 8 games, which is the probability of winning 7 of 8 plus the odds of winning all 8. In the binomial distribution, this is given as (8 choose 7) * p^7 * (1-p)^1 + (8 choose 8) * p^8 * (1-p)^0. Unfortunately, p is not just a number we can plug in, but a random variable with a distribution Beta(1,4). Luckily, the math works out cleanly, where we can calculate the integral from 0 to 1 of PDF(p) * BinomialDist(x, 8, p) with respect to p to obtain a distribution for x. Calculating this for x = 7 (The odds of the Tigers winning 7 of 8) gives a result of 4 over 495, and doing the same for x = 8 gives 1 over 495. Combined, this tells us the odds of the Tigers going 7-1 or better in their next 8 games after starting 0-3 are 1 in 99, or 1.01%. Luckily, there is a formula to do this entire calculation, which is the Beta-Binomial Distribution. After a little bit of Python and patience, this table is generated.
