Yea or Nay: The Portland Trail Blazers are better off without Anfernee Simons and Deandre Ayton.

I personally do not think they are. I’m a fan of the Blazers, and I am loving, cherishing what we’re witnessing right now from some of their youngsters. Sharpe looks like a potential All Star. Camara is a defensive beast. Clingan is an elite shot blocker and offensive rebounder. Banton, Murray have improved substantially. Avdija is only 23 and has looked good in a point forward role off the bench.

The Blazers are 6-8 now, competitive in the West, on a three game winning streak, all without Simons and Ayton, which has led to the questions among fans on whether this team is better off without those two and whether the team should look to move those two out as soon as possible. Personally, I don’t think they’re better off, especially Simons. Ant hasn’t had the best start of the year and his defense is very exploitable but he’s still the best option to start at the PG position for this Blazers team as his combination of shooting, shotmaking, and playmaking is something this team lacks. Banton is shooting it really well right now but that’s against his norms. Banton and Henderson fit as a tandem off the bench, allowing for two guys to tilt the defense and taking some ballhandling responsibility off each other.

It’s a little more of a legit question with Ayton, I don’t think Clingan is better than Ayton at all right now even though he’s great at what he does well for a rookie, but I think an argument can be made Timelord is his equal as a player at minimum. Timelord has the defensive edge. Ayton is a good player to have in the PnR but Timelord has some real passing skill and is the better lob threat and much better screener. Ayton has been expanding his game outward and taking more jumpers to less-than-stellar results. But he is a walking double double and good enough, or shall I say, willing enough as a shooter where you have to check him, at least from midrange. You don’t find those easily. And the Blazers guards could use a more spaced floor to be able to attack and a PnR guy like Ayton to be able to play off of.

What do you all think? Blazers are 28th on offense. They haven’t actually been that great on offense during the win streak either but their defense has made up for it, they’re generating a lot of steals and making easy baskets and getting to the foul line a lot. I think the defense is sustainable to a large degree because all of these guys leading the charge are pretty high quality guys on that end. But it’s gonna be hard to continue winning is the offense isn’t more efficient on a possession to possession basis. I think they’re better with those two. Do you all agree?
