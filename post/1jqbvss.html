Blood Mage changes need to actually be about blood magic 🩸🩸🩸. Where’s the cool bleeding skills or Blood Golem or sacrificial YOLO buffs

With the cool new bleeding skills and bleeding supports introduced in path 0.2, I would have thought the changes to Blood Mage would make it more in line thematically with blood magic. As of now, its design is a little too generic, in my opinion. I think the blood mage rework should have incorporated more ascendancy node changes that revolve around the bleeding mechanic, or visually compelling blood skills, or blood based minion skills like Blood Golem from Diablo 2.

For example:

Blood Nova: Enemies whose bleeding damage exceeds their remaining life instantly explode in a blood nova that deals damage.

Blood Golem: Summon a golem of blood that takes 10% of your damage for you. (This makes more sense on a Blood Golem than the Infernal Hellhound) Blood Golem sucks out additional blood from bleeding enemies in its presence and increases in size the more blood it consumes. Damage to Blood Golem decreases its size. While Blood Golem is at maximum size, it takes 20% of your damage for you and any additional blood it consumes can heal or overflow heal you instead.

Blood Wave: Expend all overflowed maximum life to call forth a large wave of blood. Damage and size increased proportionally to the amount of overflowed maximum life spent.

Blood Offering: Link one of the other offering spells. This offering can now be used on a regular enemy, instantly killing it and using it as the offering. All bleeding effects in range of this offering are also aggravated. Long cooldown.

Sacrifice Blood: Skills sacrifice 5% of your life on cast to deal 20% more damage.

Enhanced Leech: Life and Mana leech effects are doubled on enemies that are bleeding.

Blood Orb: Some version of that super annoying blood orb that drops on our fucking heads in Ultimatum.
