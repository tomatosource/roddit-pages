GGG bring back Omens of Corruption but make them more rare

Who thought the game would be more fun with the removal of Omens of Corruption? Is this even debatable? They were awesome.

Now if GGG wants to make them rare I think that’s great. Another valuable omen to farm is more fun. Having to choose when to use the omen of corruption, doing a little cost benefit analysis is also fun. I don’t know that it was optimal for their cost to be negligible so we always used them for all our corrupts. But honestly corruptions doing nothing is just lame. Risking making an item worse to make it better is awesome. Getting another multiple div omen would be awesome too.

Who’s with me?
