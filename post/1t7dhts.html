Does PoE2 need more skill slots?

Topic.

I feel like in poe2, depending on the build, I’m always either full up on skills, and having to decide between skills I want to keep, or having absolutely no socket pressure whatsoever, and end up having like 4 sockets with nothing in them.

Part of this certainly comes to optimizing your build, and cutting out the unnecessary fat, but I feel like half the time I’m theorycrafting a more intricate build, I always end up running out of skill slots, and unlike in poe1, you can’t just cut a couple of supports due to the nature of the skill system.

There are things like Unset rings, but that’s obviously a big opportunity cost, and it feels extra EXTRA bad, because as previously mentioned, some builds are just swimming in free slots.

I feel like the skill design in poe2 is limited enough already, that even if you had unlimited skill slots, you couldn’t and wouldn’t reasonably use more than a dozen skills, so is there really a need for having such a limited amount of skill slots by default? Feels like it just punishes certain builds, and hampers creativity, and unlike in poe1, it’s not really even a fun optimization challenge of trying to fit everything in and trimming the fat, you either fit everything in or you don’t, and I think it’s very unfun.

I’ve felt this way since the very first time I played poe2, I ESPECIALLY felt it last league since bear druids just have a ton of skills they “need” or can use, and I imagine a lot more people are going to feel it next league with the new Rune Ward skills coming out, and I am making this post in the hopes that maybe it’ll be noticed and changed by the developers.

Thoughts, opinions?
