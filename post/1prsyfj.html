Wrapping up my league, and just wanted to show off the coolest build I found in the new fate of the vaal league. Temporalis Auto Bomber

Thanks to the new Oracle 100% crit node, plus temporalis, you can now infinitely loop choir of the storm’s Lightning Bolt!

100% crit from Oracle

Choir of the Storm + Temporalis + Mana Flare support socketed into the choir.
