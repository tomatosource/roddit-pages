Is Tatum Finally Starting To Reach His Full Potential?

I’ve been extremely impressed with how Tatum played the last 3 games of this Orlando series. Does anybody else feel like he is finally starting to put all the different facets of his game together?

I think Tatum finally showcased his elite ability to score and play-make out of the post/midrange and looked unstoppable at times during this series. He mixed up his game well. Attacked mismatches. Showed some mental toughness and got to the FT line. I can’t imagine what he would look like if he cut down on some of the tough 3’s he takes and starts getting 10+FT’s a game or some easy buckets in the paint.

As someone that’s been highly critical of his game and decision making in the past, I’m starting to think he might actually have a legitimate case to be a Top 3 player in the NBA. The only questions I have left about his game are his finishing in the paint/through contact, as well as his propensity to bail out defenders by taking tough shots or avoiding contact.

Somebody tell me if I’m being to optimistic or am out of line here.
