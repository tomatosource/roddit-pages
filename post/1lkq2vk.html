Brad Stevens on Hugo Gonzalez (to be updated)

Brad Stevens says the Celtics will bring Hugo Gonzalez over next season: “Just a big fan of how he plays. He’s tough, he’s hard playing, he cuts, he goes after the ball, he competes. He’s got all the intangibles of a winning basketball player.”

EDIT 1: Stevens on Gonzalez: “I think he’s a young guy, but a little bit of an old soul.”

EDIT 2: Stevens noted how he’s been playing on a Real Madrid team full of NBA veterans.

EDIT 3: Brad Stevens: “We were really excited Hugo was there at 28.”
