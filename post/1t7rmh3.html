The Knicks have tied their franchise record with 6 straight playoff wins

Their only other 6 game winning streak was in 1999, when they:

  • Won g5 (winner take all) vs the #1 seed Heat in the first round (1 game)
  • Swept the #4 seed Hawks (4 games)
  • Won g1 against the #2 Pacers in the ECF (1 game)

They lost g2 to the Pacers but won in 6, and then famously went onto the finals as an 8 seed where they lost in 5 to the #1 seed in the west, the Spurs.
