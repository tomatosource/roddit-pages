Hot take: The celtics are playing better recently

Since that embarrassing loss to the raptors, the celtics have gone 4-2. In that stretch, they have 3 blowout wins and a tight win against the g league clippers who couldn’t miss. I also think the 2 losses are easily explainable.

The lakers game was as scheduled a loss as I’ve ever seen. We were at the end of a 5 in 7, had gone to OT the night before, and were on the road. The lakers haven’t had a b2b since early january and had played the wizards before with a day off in between. They’ve also had multiple games cancelled this month due to the fires. We’d probably lose that game to 24⁄29 teams in the league. The hawks game was frustrating but the team itself had the game won. Jrue just kept messing up down the stretch and singlehandedly lost them the game. Refusing to take the layup, throwing the ball out of bounds, and then fouling up 2. My point is, unlike before, these games weren’t effort losses like we’d been seeing for the last couple months.

We’ve also seen individual players start playing better. DWhite and Jrue played well today, sam hauser has gotten hotter recently, and KP’s been great.
