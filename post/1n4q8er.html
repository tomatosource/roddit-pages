Sprint is Quicksilver, but better.

I hate Quicksilver in PoE 1, especially in the campaign. You just keep running out of charges, and its effect stops every 6 seconds, which is annoying as fuck. Like, yeah, press an extra button to enjoy this faster movement speed, but hey, you have to press it again every 6 seconds. Also, you have to constantly stop and kill mobs to recharge it.

With sprint, you just hold down a button for 1 second and then go full speed for as long as you want. People have trouble with corners, however all you really need to do is just dodge to take a sharp turn, and then your character can continue sprinting. Also, people struggle with falling, but just stay away from enemies, and if you can’t, again, dodge once, and you will go through them, and then you can continue sprinting.

I prefer to deal with sprinting’s downsides than dealing with Quicksilver’s downsides of having to take two Quicksilvers, so two extra buttons to press because one isn’t enough, and then still having to stop every 12-24 seconds to farm charges. And if you run out of charges in an empty location, then good luck, you will crawl like a snail until you reach a decent-sized group of enemies that can give you enough charges to enable your Quicksilver flasks again.

For me personally, this is way more annoying than the current downsides of sprint.
