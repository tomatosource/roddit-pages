HYPOTHETICALLY, Should We Have Tanked the 2025-26 NBA Season?

With how deep this years draft class is poised to be, should a dwindling celtics side, devoid of major contributors in Holiday, Porzingis, Kornet, Horford and Tatum, have written the season off early, in favour of tanking for one of the many highly talented prospects?

Whilst the side did observe great success in the regular season, boasting the second seed in the east (fourth overall), the power of hindsight in regards to how the playoffs turned out portray the existing roster as a team of overachievers, as opposed to having legitimate dominance. Furthermore, if this team had somehow fought its way to the finals, how would a thin, unexperienced center rotation of Queta and Garza hold up against the western conference heavy hitters in Jokic, Wembanyama or even Holmgren? Surely we should have seen this and realised our odds were slim to none, thus shutting down our title race.

Instead, by shifting goals early on, the C’s front office would have given themselves the chance to both bolster the existing team, and set up the long term future of the franchise with genuine star power. For example, drafting the young Darryn Peterson, a strong facilitator, could ease the roster concerns of lacking a “true point guard,” slotting into the roster and gameplan almost seamlessly.

That being said, tanking holds as many cons as it does pros, including that:

PROS CONS
Star Talent, Potentially the most valuable possession in the modern league, GMs have proved again and again that they are willing to trade anything and everything on potential alone Not all talent is immediately “league Ready,” think Grant Williams first two seasons
Allows the team to rest players, a full year of rest after contending for numerous consecutive years would replenish many players, For example, Tatum with months away from the game looks like a whole new player, smarter, more efficient and healthier Players may become disgruntled, who knows how the likes of Brown, White and Pritchard would have fared a decisive losing season

Therefore, the question I ask of other celtics fans is if you were In a position of power, with hindsight and complete knowledge of how the season turned out, would you have decided to tank?
