Can you identify the NBA player by just looking at their stats? I made a web game to answer this question: https://www.balldontcry.com/

In my college dorm (10 years ago…), I would pull up a random player’s Wikipedia article, scroll down to the stats section, and show it to my friend Avi, and he would try to guess who is was. Then we’d switch roles and repeat. We would do this for hours. I tried to make it into a web game back then, but my coding skills were nascent. This year, I took another crack at it, and you can play it for yourself at Ball Don’t Cry dot com!

It’s a daily game (like wordle or whatever), where you guess 3 players each day (the first one of each day is typically the easiest). For each player, you get 3 guesses. Initially you don’t see any of their teams, but they get revealed after each incorrect guess.

Please try it out and let me know what you think :) My one goal in life is to make this go viral, so share it with your friends if you enjoy it.
