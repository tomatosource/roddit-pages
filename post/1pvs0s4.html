This game has the same problem as POE1, defensive mechanics don’t make sense. One shot mobs or be one shot.

Defensive mechanics/systems need a complete overhaul.

You cannot have back and forth combat when everything in the game incentives killing things as fast as possible so it can’t fight back is the best defensive option.

Playing a slower, meatier build is worse on every measure.

You don’t farm as fast -&gt; Currency is slower -&gt; Gear isn’t as good -&gt; Why am I playing a slower or tankier build?

Have to pick a lane GGG, do you want meaty combat or just going as fast as possible?

Honestly, I love POE1 but I always thought this was the biggest problem in the game. Monsters don’t “chunk” you so you can react. I “chunk” mobs and a degen or bad line up of crits will kill me.

I don’t know what the solution is, but what’s the difference between this game and POE1 if the core concept is to build the most fast character that can do the most dps instantly?

FYI - Played plant druid this league which is pretty fast, but my god my ranger went 5x as fast and farmed 5x more currency dying less…

Edit: This is mainly a softcore complaint - I realize defense is very important in hardcore but even then most people don’t play hardcore because of random one shots.
