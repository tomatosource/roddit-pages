While you’re at it, please make the endgame also more multiplayer friendly!

I know this is potentially a personal take. The vast majority of players will likely play solo, and if they play with friends it’s just for a few maps, and for that the current system works well enough. But now is the time for feedback to be given for 0.4, so I might as well…

Problem
There is one big problem with playing in the endgame together with a friend in the current version: There is no shared progress, no shared sense of achievement. You can’t even see the party leader’s Atlas, so you can’t even make the decision of which map to run together – no shared agency.

I know many people will say that there is already little sense of progress in the endgame and this aspect doesn’t make much of a difference. But the perspective I am coming from is that I am hoping that GGG will add a lot more progression to the endgame with 0.4. And while they are conceptualizing their ideas I want to stir up some wind for this issue, so that it doesn’t get overlooked.

Solution (quick fix)
With Trial of the Sekhemas they already made a subtle but important change in 0.3 (afaik) which allowed the non-party-leader to see the floor map. This enabled the group to have a small discussion about which way to choose through the floor before every room. It’s fine that only the party leader gets to ultimately decide. But that little discussion creates a sense of shared agency, and that’s what ultimately makes multiplayer fun. Without that it’s just one player making decisions and a second player following along.

So at the very least they could do for the Atlas what they did for Sekhemas and allow the non-party-leaders to at least see the party leader’s Atlas. This wouldn’t give a sense of shared progression, but at least create a feeling of shared agency.

Solution (proper)
I realize that with the current Atlas system it’s close to impossible to create shared progression while also preventing players from hard carrying each other. So I don’t want to go into specific suggestions on how to fix this properly with the current Atlas – given that they will likely rework the Atlas anyway.

But as they say, “don’t criticize without offering solutions”, so I will at least provide a possible solution to this problem so that it doesn’t look like I am just criticizing without offering any ideas.

For this possible solution to work, I need another idea that I posted a few months ago. In this idea I proposed to adjust the Atlas so that it consists of individual regions (details here).

This regions idea offers a good solution to the multiplayer problem: They can give us the option to synchronize individual regions one at-a-time between the Atlases of two players.

  • You could lock this ability behind the use of a certain item that you can break apart and give the other half of the item to your friend. Then both players can use their half of the item on their region to synchronize it.
  • Once synchronized, the two individual regions become essentially the same. Every map cleared in the region is cleared in both Atlases. Once the region is completed, it is complete for both Atlases. All Atlas rewards are shared.
  • You can make it so that both players need to be present to complete maps in a synchronized region, so that this feature cannot be abused for carrying.
  • You can allow players to desynchronize regions, so that they are not locked out of the region when their friend stops playing.
  • (I am sure other tweaks are necessary to prevent the feature from being abused, but I want to focus on the concept here)
  • One region is roughly 20 maps - which is a good sized chunk of content you can do with a friend in one go.

This last solution I included is just an example how it could be done – to prove that it’s not impossible. It’s not meant to be the core of this post, however.

TLDR
I want to raise awareness for the issue of making the endgame more multiplayer friendly. The more attention this gets now, the more likely it is for GGG to notice it and include a solution in 0.4.
