0.5 Retrospective and hopes for 0.6

EDIT: oops, uploaded this to the wrong qdrive and I guess it entangled itself with a reddit post from april 2026. Those guys are gonna be so confused lmao

Having just hit 100 on my Duelist glad and finally landing my Zerphi’s ring double corrupt (only cost me three mirrors) I thought I’d jot some notes down on my experience in 0.5 and final thoughts before 0.6

Obviously, I’m thrilled with how glad turned out (I’m still getting used to the ascendency and base class getting their name swapped but oh well). Swords have felt incredible after playing maces for over a year. I hope they go back and rethink some of the mace pain points, but I also understand that swords being more agile helps give them some identity. While melee definitely feels better with swords as a more mobile option, they’ve basically made maces unplayably bad by comparison.

As you can see on the ladder and poeninja Duelist is the only viable ascendency. It’s just not worth taking the “tanky” gladiator when you could play a zero duration titan, or a duelist and delete the screen with blood explosions. I hope they remove some of the downsides on the big nodes, and maybe actually fix armor next league.

Speaking of armor, I definitely felt the changes but it’s still in a bad place. Being able to ES stack with it helps a bit. But now it feels extra bad because you’re forced into an armor hybrid and just playing blue life isn’t possible anymore either (especially starting from this side of the tree).

I thought the way they implemented legion was a good change. In poe1 you’d just screen delete all the mobs, whereas having them activate gradually over time time makes it feel more on-par with poe2’s pacing. But when you have to wet noodle like four mobs and run away and then lose everything else to the timer it feels terrible. I did drop my first div this league on a currency chest though.

Conflicts are really, really fun. I think uber conflicts are the single best end-game mechanic they’ve added yet. I do wish we could still do five-ways but even after investing multiple mirrors the four-ways are challenging enough. It was nice having a good leveling strat since I got spoiled by old temple, even if it’s pretty expensive. This is another one where I hope they rethink other mechanics (simulacrum) to make it actually challenging and not feel like waiting through a cut scene so it’s as good as conflicts.

Speaking of temple, I think GGG really untangled the Gordian Knot with that one. When I read that temple was literally unchanged except for removing lock medallions I thought “okay we’re doing temple league 2” but it ended up in a really good place. I was running some through campaign and early maps and it gave me some genuinely useful stuff without building a 77 room snake or whatever. (shout out to that guy who posted a 46 room snake even without locks what a gigachad)

So yeah overall I think the changes were really good. I hope they finally remove or cap +levels next league but at this point honestly I think I’ve acclimated to it. What was your guy’s favorite part of 0.5?
