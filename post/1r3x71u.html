The regular season is 40 days away and the Atlanta Braves are the only MLB team without concrete plans for in-market broadcasts. They have neither a TV provider nor a streaming option at the time of this writing.

The Braves were part of those final 9 MLB teams that recently left FanDuel Sports Networks.

The Angels are the only other team without a finalized TV provider arrangement, however, they were able to extend an olive branch to their fans and announced that in-market streaming would be available via a $100/season “Angels.tv” MLB package.
