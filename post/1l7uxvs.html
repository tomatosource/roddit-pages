Enjoying this game much more than PoE 1

I only have about 50 hours in PoE 1. I tried but I could never get into it, I don’t really like the skill gem system and the way you can just mindlessly mow down huge groups of mobs.

PoE 2 is a breath of fresh air in the action RPG genre. I like how it’s slower and more methodical and how it encourages you to use multiple skills all the time. The boss fights are challenging yet very fun and rewarding when you beat them. I also like how there are less loot drops, that way your dopamine is not constantly stimulated by loot, so when you actually get a good loot drop it feels like you earned it.

I really don’t understand some of the hate this game gets, is it from veteran PoE 1 players who expected this game to be a reskinned version of PoE 1?

I’m still playing the campaign so I’m sure I will have more positive things to say about the game, but for now I am just enjoying it very much and being thankful to the devs for creating such a gem.
