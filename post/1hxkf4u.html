While away from the team during yesterday’s game against Denver, Kawhi Leonard’s house burnt down.

Yesterday Kawhi Leonard left the Clippers after hopping on the flight to Denver with them in order to tend to his family and his home during the raging wildfires that are still destroying many parts of California.

It was learned through a member of the Locked On Clippers podcast, who also lost his home due to the raging fires, that Kawhi Leonard’s home was in a neighborhood that was engulfed in flames. He said “I know exactly where Kawhi’s house is and I’d be shocked if it’s still standing. Same with JJ Redick.” This was tweeted yesterday and Shams Charania just confirmed minutes ago that JJ Redick’s home did burn down.

A writer for People’s Magazine also confirmed that her friend who lives on same street as Kawhi Leonard “lost everything.”
