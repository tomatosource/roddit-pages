0.4 End of league build showcase

Hey everyone, with 0.5 hype staring up I figured its not a bad time to showcase the weird builds i did this league. Hopefully someone might find some inspiration for next league. My goal going in was to try off-meta ideas and avoid the usual meta skills/combos as much as possible. Most builds are relatively cheap because im broke re-rolling characters every league. These builds are by no means min maxed so they can 100% improved and made better. All of these builds are on the squishier side since tanking with a lot of uniques is kinda hard.

All of these are relatively cheap to put together, with the most expensive being the Spirit-stacking Dark Defiler.

1. Mirage Lightning Archer (Chronomancer) - Most ‘Meta’

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/bowboy686-0610/vaal/character/GameplayOptional

Built around Lightning Rod as the main damage source, with Chronomancer mechanics letting me lock enemies in time inside the rod.

The interesting part is using Mirage Archers to trigger the rod explosions. With cooldown reset + chance to not consume cooldown, it turns into a loop of spawning more mirages and chaining freezes. I initially tried dropping the cooldown as low as possible but i think for that you’d want a temporalis but not sure if you can get it down to 0.

Verdict: Great bosser, decent mapper. Amazing for Sekhemas.

Video: https://youtu.be/BSnkfmuRxJA
Im only showcasing the bossing since mapping is the same as any archer. I totally didnt die at the end, trust.

2. Quality Stacker “Froggy” (Gemling) - Most Fun to watch

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/bowboy686-0610/vaal/character/FeelinALilFroggy

This one abuses quality scaling to spawn frogs through Murkshaft Quiver.

Using Killjoy Linen Wraps, all the on-kill effects get triggered twice, and stacking Fox Idols pushes quality even higher. Damage ends up coming from a mix of poison and bleed while everything cascades. This is a fun build but sucks at bossing since there isnt a good way to trigger the on kill effects.

Verdict: Great mapper, terrible bosser. Very fun chains.

Video: https://youtu.be/vu3T-KvMaxU

3. Hysseg’s Claw – Hyena Boss Roleplay (Shaman) - Most boring

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/bowboy686-0610/vaal/character/FurryAficionado

A companion-focused setup using Hysseg’s Claw to run two full sets of companions.

Nothing super special i just wanted more dogs(ish). Uses the rage node from the druid line that buffs companions for damage. Potentially you could get the attack damage from the claw and not make this a minion build but i never really tried it. Could work with lich i think as well.

Verdict: Mid mapping, decent bossing. Kind of boring to play.

Video: https://youtu.be/6T29fVX-cpc

4. Dark Defiler – Bring My Own Enemies (Shaman) - Funniest

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/bowboy686-0610/vaal/character/IBringMyOwnEnemies

Built around Dark Defiler + Threaded Light for damage scaling.

Skeletons use Brutus Brain so they don’t die so you have constant damage. Then its just a regular plant guy. I couldnt figure out a better combo, storms was the closest. This builds can break some bosses like the one in the video or the ice variant of him. Really i just wanted to see how many skeletons i can have.

Verdict: Funny.

Video: https://youtu.be/pAC5g8bFmPo

5. Doomfletch Elemental Archer (Ritualist) - Most surprising

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/bowboy686-0610/vaal/character/DreamsButNoHopes

This is based on Doomfletch, with Rain of Arrows + Spiral Volley as the main skills.
I honestly didnt think this build would perform as well as it did. Does decent damage but comes short due to how bad rain of arros is. If they remove the max # of rains this would be very good i think.

Leans into Herald stacking from the armor to scale elemental damage plus ritualist rings.

Verdict: Good mapper, decent bosser.

Video: https://youtu.be/xFhaKuVoGnU
