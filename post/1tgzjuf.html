6 New York Yankees Yankees -2 I am not a doomer. I can generally look at a Yankees team and kvetch about this or that, but I know it’s a long season, and ultimately, a season is grueling, grinding, and much changes over time. That being said, Sunday’s loss to the Mets was the culmination of why the Yankees have continued to be good, but not great. I don’t like any saying of this, but the same issues that plagued the Yankees in their WS loss in 2024 and their playoff exit last year were on full display in the 3rd game at Citi Field. When you are consistently consistent about the wrong things and find ways to dodge them, you are playing with fire, and ultimately, you will be burned. This was May 17th, 2026, for the Yankees at Citi Field and the worst loss of the season for the Yanks so far. Can’t find the bats with RISP, the bullpen can’t produce clean 1-2-3 innings, and your defense, while improved since that infamous 5th inning, still haunts you; you are playing not to lose, and not to win. There are a lot of positives, Ben Rice continues to be a grand superstar, Cam Schlitter, the SP depth, Volpe has been patient and had wonderful ABs since his return, some actual small ball instead of relying on HRs (first sacrifice bunt of the whole season, (really)), and Jazz just starting to play instead of swinging at every single pitch, but in a weakened American League, you must win, and dominate. Road Trips inevitably may end up being .500, especially against division and subway rivals, but when you have the plate discipline of Austin Wells, and give away wins (and read all the above), you begin losing series consistently. This team is going to be alright, it typically always is, but that’s the problem, the team has the potential to be great, but often fails and settles for good. In a weak AL, this may and has worked, but against NL contenders, you hardly are in the same room with them. This is the most doomerish and negative post I will likely have about the Yankees all season. I had a whole different write-up before 2 outs, with 2 on in the 9th inning, and had to change my take immediately; I know many teams would kill to have these types of problems (I get it), but when you have the potential for greatness and come up “good enough” or ‘just fine’, that’s not baseball, Suzyn, that’s BS, Suzyn. 28-19
7 San Diego Padres Padres 0 The Padres have taken home the 29th edition of the Vedder cup, a contest they’ve played their natural rivals in every year since 1997 (besides 2017). The trophy is a guitar, in honor of a Cubs fan with ties to both cities. The Mariners lead in Cup wins (12-11, 6-ties), and the head-to-head record (73-70), in both cases it’s been really close. This was the first Cup win since it’s been made official by the MLB, the first for San Diego since 2021, breaking a streak of four straight by Seattle. A sweep to end the week feels good though. 28-18
