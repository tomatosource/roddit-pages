If a Giannis//Jaylen Brown exchanges goes through, it would be the first time two Finals MVPs have ever been traded for each other.

Even if the two aren’t directly traded for one another, it would still be the first time two Finals MVPs have been involed in the same trade. This also includes players that may have been traded before they won their fMVP, but the fact that both have actively won the award is interesting to note. Maybe not surprisingly, this is not the first first trade to involve an “active” finals MVP; there have been roughly 10 or so previous players (didn’t actually do a full count, plus AI is not accurate on this stuff). However there were four Finals MVPs that were traded squarely in their primes (Kareem, Shaq, Kawhi, Dennis Johnson) after having won the award. Fair to say Jaylen Brown and Giannis would be the 5th and 6th. Some interesting historical significance for a trade that I personally hope does not happen!!
