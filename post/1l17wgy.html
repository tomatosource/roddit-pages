New Suns general manager Brian Gregory was scheduled to interview Thunder assistant Dave Bliss over the weekend in Oklahoma City, league sources say. Bliss obviously still has playoff coaching responsibilities with the NBA Finals-bound Thunder and was not expected to travel to Phoenix like other top candidates for the vacancy.

Two (or maybe even three) of the candidates who had face-to-face interviews with Gregory over the past week are expected to be chosen soon to advance to face-to-face meetings with Suns owner Mat Ishbia.

No official word has emerged yet regarding who will progress, but Miami’s Chris Quinn and Cleveland’s Jordan Ott have been repeatedly forecasted in league coaching circles to advance to the Ishbia stage. Dallas’ Sean Sweeney and Ott’s fellow Cavaliers assistant coach Johnnie Bryant have likewise interviewed with Gregory in recent days.

Two of my longtime colleagues (ESPN’s Marc J. Spears and Chris Haynes) have reported that Suns holdover assistant coach David Fizdale remains in consideration for the post.

There have been strong indications during the search process, sources say, to reinforce the notion that Phoenix is likely to move on from Kevin Durant after Durant’s 2 1⁄2-season run in the desert. It has been anticipated for some time that Phoenix, no matter who is hired as the new coach, will explore its Durant trade options this offseason — in collaboration with the future Hall of Famer and his business manager Rich Kleiman — after Phoenix unilaterally pursued a Durant trade in February that the 36-year-old ultimately urged them not to make.

Suns star Devin Booker has been speaking to candidates as part of the interview process in this third round of the search for Mike Budenholzer’s successor. Phoenix is said to have interviewed more than 15 candidates in Round 1 before narrowing down to nine candidates for Round 2.
