The Celtics are capital ‘C’ Contenders

The C’s now have the the best point differential in the East and the 3rd best in the league. They also have the 3rd best net rating in the league. These are the hallmarks for a true title contender and the fact that they’ve done with for 34 means this isn’t just a phase or a fluke. These guys are legit, one of the best teams in the league, and not just a nice, cute story anymore.

The things they were doing to the clippers last night was just downright cruel - like the Clippers were flaming hot coming into the game, actually wanted that one and the Celtics straight up sonned them. Like the Clippers seemed legitimately shocked that they were getting their ass beat that bad.

I also think people have also been severly underrating their supporting cast. DWhite is a borderline all-star, Pritchard is clearly a staring level guard/creator in the league, the same true for Queta at center. Simons might be the most potent off the bench scorer on the team in years (even more than Pritch last couple years imo). And the rest of the bench offer two-way optionality and hustle that most teams would kill for. These aren’t some scrap heap guys Joe whipped into shape, as it often been framed (though there’s some truth to that) they’re just plain good talent at this point.

Jayson Tatum is going to come back this season (Stevens at his workouts basically confirmed this), and its time to start thinking of the C’s as a legit threat to make a run at the title this year. If Tatum can come back and produce at 80% of what he used to the Celtics I think the Celtics will become a true top tier contender that could make a run at OKC, the Nuggets, or any of the other top teams in the west. How far they’ll actually go, we’ll see, but I think its time to start accepting that we’ve got another potential title team on our hands (emphasis on potential)
