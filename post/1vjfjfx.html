Xesht bug solutions

Hello! I just had a little problem with this boss in two instances, so I thought maybe some people might have it too. Here’s how to solve it:

  1. Dying for it and being softlocked on the ressurect screen, and then not being able to rejoin Xesht’s realm

Go back to the character selection, you will spawn on your hideout. Then open another map that’s not the Monastery, any map! You don’t have to enter it, just open, and then open Monastery again and you will be able to join Xesht’s realm.

  1. Killing Xesht and being softlocked

I finally killed it and Ailith just left me there like bro?? teleport me too???

Anyways, you just have to go back to the character selection screen - no need to close it!

I hope this is helpful for future players :)
