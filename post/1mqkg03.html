The New York Knicks and England National Football Team are the Exact Same Team

So I watch a lot of soccer (football) and basketball and I noticed this, but the Knicks and the England squad are pretty perfectly paralleled. I am aware that I am probably not the first person to draw this comparison, but either way I think it’s interesting enough to share, and I love merging my two favorite sports.

  • Both teams won their last trophies a long time ago and cling to them because they have nothing else.
    • England’s 1966 World Cup
    • Knicks 1973 Championship
  • Every year both fanbases are famous for claiming every year to be “their year” to ultimately lead to disappointment.
    • Unironically though this season really is the Knick’s year with how open the east is.
    • England is pretty famous for the whole “It’s coming home thing” and with Tuchel in charge a World Cup win is not out of the question.
  • Both teams/fans are fairly hated by fans outside of their own teams in the rest of the sport.
  • Both teams are historic and considered to be pretty iconic/storied regardless of their lack of success
  • They both have iconic stadiums that are highly respected in the sport
    • MSG and Wembley
  • The media is always talking about both teams and is very harsh and scrutinizes failures. Players and coaches are immediately placed under a huge amount of pressure from the media just from being part of the team.
  • Both teams semi-recently got rid of their coach/managers that brought them the most success they have seen in a very long time, but just couldn’t ever get all the way
    • Southgate leaving after losing two Euro finals
    • Knicks just fired Thibs after making the Conference finals for the first time in decades.
  • Both of the women’s teams have actually had success recently but sadly people care way less because they are women
    • England women won back to back Euros
    • NY Liberty won the championship last year (I know they are more of a brooklyn nets team but I’m still considering them to fit my narrative)

I don’t know, I thought this was pretty interesting. You could probably make more of these types of comparisons if you look hard enough. This really made me realize that the Knicks should make their own “its coming home song” since it fits well with us.

Anyway, England could do well at the World Cup in 2026 and the Knicks are gonna be a big team this coming season too. It’ll be nice to see if both teams can finally end their droughts and the many years of hurt.
