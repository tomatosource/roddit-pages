At this point I think I can confidently say that GGG needs to stop trying to do Leagues until they can get the game itself to a stable state

This was a point of discussion during the initial announcement where they said that the endgame rework was delayed again till 0.5

There was a discussion that they should just not commit to 4 month update cycles and instead just do more aggressive balance updates periodically and release the actual missing core game content like classes, ascendancies and Acts as they are ready to go. Save dev resources for the actual important stuff.

The main argument against this was that “leagues need to happen” because the leagues are the core game content that will make the game better over time.

Well what now. The vaal temple has turned into such a waste of time for everyone. Is it actually in any state to be added to the core game as it is right now? And even if it is with how annoying, convoluted and time consuming it is, who is actually going to want to do this mechanic if it even does go core? At best its going to take a lot of work to rebalance and fix which you definitely can’t convince me isn’t going to eat away dev time from the devs and delay stuff like swords and Act 5 even more.

This might get push back but I genuinely think that they should just focus on getting the game to 1.0 with acts 5 and 6, the rest of the classes and an Atlas that doesn’t suck before they try and make leagues. Just have a regular ladder reset every 4 months to keep the economy healthy and add an ascendancy and people will still play the game.
