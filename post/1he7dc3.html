The way the games are called is a way bigger problem then “teams taking too many 3’s”

Recently, partly because of the rating decline, I’ve seen a lot of people argue some sort of version of “teams are taking too many 3’s, it’s making the product unwatchable”, and I admit I just don’t get it.

Statistically teams are taking as many jumpers as they used to do 20 years ago, long 2’s have just turned into 3’s. And mostly it’s not about stars transitionning from mid range to 3’s (it is in some case, but most on ball creators still rely on the mid range shot quite a bit), it’s about role players actually being stationned behind the line instead of the memorial Tayshaun Prince spot up from the baseline.

What really hurts the product IMO is the complete lack of balance in the way the game is called on both ends : offensive players are allowed to use their off arm as much as they want, to protect their dribble, to create separation while on the other end a defender simply can’t use his forearm unless an offensive player is posting up.

I could do a whole paragraphe on illegal screening, but I think the fact that basically every recent contender has a known illegal screener on the team basically says it all.

What makes no sense, is suddenly in the playoffs, the refs will allow defensive players to get more physical, and basically EVERYBODY agrees that the level of physicality in series like OKC/Dallas or Denver/Minny last year was what made those series fun, but why can’t it extend to the regular season ?
