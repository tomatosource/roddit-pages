Most of the talk around Jaylen Brown / Celtics is outside noise

For one, Brown has not publicly made any remarks about disliking his situation or wanting to leave. The most we’ve heard is the ‘favorite season’ remarks, which comes off a bit tone-deaf, but has also been blown out of proportion. Beyond that, he’s made no reference towards wanting to leave or being unhappy. After his comments, Brad Stevens publicly stated Brown has not expressed frustration and feels positive about the relationship with the Celtics. Brown essentially supported this the next day, when he said he wants to play for the Celtics for the rest of his career if possible. Most comments about Brown’s discontent basically come down hearsay or speculation.

Secondly, the only confirmed offer involving Brown was explicitly for Giannis - this is an extremely reasonable offer and shouldn’t necessarily be considered ‘shopping’ Brown. Most teams would be negligent not to consider a trade for a 2X MVP winner. Current reporting is that the Celtics are ‘listening’ to offers on JB - however both of these are numerous course of NBA business and don’t indicate the Celtics are looking to move Brown.

Third, numerous teams have an incentive to potentially hurt the Celtics by having Brown continually involved in trade talks - The Brown/Tatum due makes the Celtics one of the biggest threats in the East and the league at large, having Brown changes teams and/or increasing team strife all stands to benefit other contenders in the East. Miami was likely using Brown/The Celtics as leverage in their talks. Wannabe contenders have an incentive to increase trade rumors and potentially create a situation where they can poach Brown. There’s significant interest in having other teams to promote Jaylen Brown trade rumors.

This is all to say when you look at the facts, there’s little hard evidence that the Celtics want to move on from Jaylen Brown or vice versa. Not saying that these rumors aren’t true per se or that Brown won’t get traded, but when you consider the sources of these discussion it seems that not much of it is coming from the party’s that are actually involved in the situation. Basically take these discussions with a grain of salt, because there’s a lot of noise out there that seems to have little to do with the Celtics.
