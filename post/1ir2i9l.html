source: https://www.pbpstats.com/wowy-combos/nba?TeamId=1610612753&Season=2024-25,2023-24,2022-23&SeasonType=All&PlayerIds=1630532,1631094&OnlyCommonGames=true

In 1600 minutes with just Paolo on the court without Franz, the net rating drops drastically to -8.14. Opponents FG% and 3P% both go up compared to the both of them on the court.
