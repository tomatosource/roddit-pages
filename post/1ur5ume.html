Rudy Gobert is The Last Active NBA Player to Beat Team USA in the Olympics

With the news of Nicolas Batum and Guerschon Yabusele’s departures to Europe, Rudy Gobert is the last player from the French team that beat the USA 83-76 in the 2021 Olympic opener. Gobert notched 14 PTS and 9 REB and frustrated the US defensively, as they shot 40.5% inside the arc. FIBA box score.

While the last Olympic final between US and France was fabulously entertaining, the 2021 final was even closer (87-82, USA). Gobert totaled 16 PTS and 8 REB and was +3, although he struggled with fouls and FTs. If it weren’t for a virtuoso performance from Kevin Durant and a vital effort from Jayson Tatum off the bench, the USA would’ve lost. FIBA box score for the final.
