The game needs to do more to introduce end game

I am someone who loves POE 2, as a n00b, non-POE 1 player (Well I did a bit a decade ago), the way thte game transitions from campaign to end game is utterly overwhelming.

Yes, you get a few short sentences from Doryani, but then you open the Atlas, and hoo boy.

Does anyone else thinks that this is just waaay too involved?

All I am told is: “Go clear corruption” but I open a huge sprawling atlas and simply stare at it.

So you go and read some guides, watch some videos, try to get handle on what the game is actually doing in end game. Now in theory this is fine, don’t get me wrong, nothing wrong with learning about a complex game, but so many things change or ar added to the game from before you complete the campaign, to after.

We suddenly have to understand:

MAPPING

Suffixes, prefixes Vaals, Towers, corrupted, cleansed, citadels, towers, bosses, Delirium, Breaches, Expedition, Rituals, oh let’s not forget Distilled Delirium! (I didn’t even know these interacted with mapping until a random comment mentioned this in a video dedicated to explaining mapping!)

On top of that, as you get to T15, every single waystone needs exquisite attention to ‘not suck’, every tower needs exquisite attention to ‘not suck’ pathing wrong is bad, heading in the wrong direction is bad, and almost nothing exists in game to help you along, like, nothing! (occasional lines of dialogue AFTER your first Delirium or whatever in no way help you understand)

ATLAS
Oh wait, we still have an entire new passive tree, and additional smaller trees to flesh out!
Don’t spend your atlas points wrong if you don’t want to ‘suck’ too.

MATS

A butt load of new crafting materials drop, Omens, Distilled Delirium, various mats you collect to get a bigger mat you use to encounter a pinnacle boss, that I’m supposed to somehow just know about. YES the mat tells me what it’s for, kinda, but a tooltip is simply inefficient to explain entire systems to the player.

My inventory is filling up with things that I need to head off to a web site and research for half an hour before I even know what I’m supposed to do with them.

but this is just way too much, dropped on players all at once, requiring you to not just read and learn, (which is fine and dandy) but to deeply study the systems, or else you’ll be simply out of your depth.

If a non-POE player’s opinion is of an interest, the game needs to add in elements slowly, each few tiers a new system is unlocked, and explained, I dunno, something, ANYTHING But this.
