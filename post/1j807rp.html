Is Jeremy Lamb influencing decisions in the Kings organization?

I don’t know that I’ve ever seen this posted but this is the first time I’m hearing about this and it actually seems to have legs.

Sometime in 2023, former King, Jeremy Lamb, started dating the daughter of Kings owner Vivek Ranadive.

Anjali initially pursued a music career but then transitioned into the Kings organization after taking an assistant GM role with the Stockton Kings (Sacramento’s G League affiliate) in 2022. She eventually stepped down in 2023 but her and Lamb remained highly visible figures at Kings games, often sitting court side next to Vivek.

A Kings employee sarcastically remarked to the Athletic that “we’re all going to be working for Jeremy Lamb soon enough”. Tension escalated after the mid-season firing of Mike Brown, many of which in the organization believe was a decision influenced by Anjali and Lamb. Brown was a favorite of De’Aaron Fox and his dismissal only fueled Fox’s desire to leave the organization as it was reported that he no longer trusted the franchise’s stability.

Now, it’s also being reported that Sabonis is looking for clarity on the direction of the team and who is actually making decisions.

Is this a generational infiltration by Jeremy Lamb?
