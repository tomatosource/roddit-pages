Big man rotation

I’m not that alarmed by this 2 game skid, but the clear and obvious lesson should be that physical games need physical players. Specifically with the bigs, Kornet is not a physical player. The beginning of the 4th was a disaster in large part because Philly went small and played physical to keep Kornet away from the rim while simultaneously killing him in the high PnR. When Kornet can’t camp around the rim, he’s a liability. Queta on the other hand was great when he was out there, Philly couldn’t put smalls on him as he’s too athletic and physical. And this is not a knock on Kornet, when he can camp around the rim he’s wildly effective. But in these games where the other team is punching us in the mouth, we need guys who can punch back harder. This is the 1 thing that Joe needs to figure out, when to push the right buttons. For instance, Springer got a game recently and was ok, but this game he doesn’t get a shot even though we could’ve really used his defense on Maxey. Frustrating, but we’ll be fine in the long run.
