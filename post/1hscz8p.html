With tonight’s win, the Oklahoma City Thunder have now won 13 straight games, the longest streak in franchise history

With tonight’s 116 to 98 victory over the Clippers, the OKC Thunder have set a new franchise record with 13 wins in a row. This comes during a stretch where they have gone without their 2nd best player in their defensive anchor Chet Holmgren.

The Thunder will look to extend this streak tomorrow against the New York Knicks, who are on a very impressive 9 game win streak of their own.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander also continues his MVP campaign, putting up 29/3/8 with 2 steals on 53/40/100 shooting splits.
