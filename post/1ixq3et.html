My thoughts on currency trading after 6 weeks

After I got bored with the repetative gameplay I decided to try the trading feature since I figured it would let me take things easy while still getting enough orbs to gear myself up to see if the game would get more fun.

I started off with about 100 exalts that I had scrounged up through playing. The first couple of days I didn’t do any trading but looked at how prices would fluctuate and how different currencies could be converted. Once I felt like I had an idea of what I was doing I set off. My first purchase was something like 4 soul cores, which I bought for exalts and then flipped for chaos orbs, which I then converted back into exalts and kept buying things which were cheaper in exalts than in the converted value of chaos orbs. Within a couple of weeks I had made roughly 3000 chaos orbs in profit, cached in half of them to divs and bought myself some decent end-game gear, so I was back to some 1500 chaos orbs.

Playing was a bit more fun, but once I “beat the game” by smashing all the end game bosses (with my shockwave totem build) I felt done with the game. But trading was still giving me a kick so I kept trading.

Give or take another 3 - 4 weeks and I’m up to about 12000 - 15000 chaos orbs (which is a bit, but seeing as Divs are now nearing 500 exalts and good end game gear upgrades are like 200+ divs I don’t really feel accomplished). But since my trading started nearing 5000 chaos orbs per day I got another problem, the only way for me to handle the transaction costs (now 2 - 3 million gold per day) was to “recruit” a network of players that will sell me their waystones so I can vendor them.

So instead of playing the game I will spend maybe 1 hour trading, upgrading and selling maps so I can set up some deals for the day (which at this scale brings me about 5 - 10% daily interest). This is marginally more fun than doing maps (especially on my computer which tends to stutter frequently enough that I tend to die in super-juiced strongholds) but considering how much good endgame gear costs it feels like the best thing you can do is not play the game at all.

This is my experience with trading. Also with the gear I bought myself (SW totem builds seems not to be the best MF class but I guess they are at least ok) I could make maybe 1 - 3 divs per juiced map (with 300% MF) after spending way too much boring grinding time to clear shitty maps over and over again, as long as my PC didn’t stutter at an inappropriate time and kill me.
