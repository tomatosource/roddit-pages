Namelocking: why it sucks more in PoE2 than in PoE1

Note: This post is in response to Talkative Tri’s interview with Mr. Rogers, on the topic near the end about an option for turning off namelocking. This is mainly regarding mouse + keyboard movement.

Namelocking is an issue in PoE2 more than in PoE1 for mouse + keyboard because the movement system is fundamentally different. The “cast without moving” option removes namelock, as it did in PoE1. However, we don’t use “cast without moving” in PoE2 because we are able to cast/attack WHILE moving. We never had this in PoE1. People are getting namelocked every single mob in PoE2 because they play with this option disabled - and rightly so, as casting while moving is one of the big features of PoE2. “Cast without moving” is not needed in PoE2, but namelock removal is. Therefore, namelocking needs to be SEPARATED from “cast without moving”, or just give us the option to not use namelock.

If Mr. Rogers does not realize this point, it needs to be acknowledged before the problem can be solved. There is no need to make it more complicated than it is - just give us the option. We don’t need to discuss projectile trajectories. How do I know this? I have zero issues targeting in PoE2 when I click halfway on the other side of my screen just in order to prevent namelocking, then mouse over TO THE MOB I am trying to aim at. It makes no sense I have to do this in order to aim properly and move while casting. It seems like he fundamentally doesn’t understand the problem at hand.
