The Wizards hired GM Will Dawkins on June 8th, 2023. Since then he has completely gutted the roster, with only 2 players remaining from the pre-Dawkins era

And those two players are: Corey Kispert, a great role player who was signed to a 4-year, 54 million dollar extension at the beginning of this season, and Anthony Gil, a good locker room vet who costs $2 mil a year.

Looking at everyone else:

Khris Middleton: Acquired in February 2025 in a package for Kyle Kuzma

Jordan Poole: Acquired in July 2023 through a series of blockbuster moves which sent Beal to the Suns and Chris Paul to the Warriors

Malcolm Brogdon: Acquired just before the 2024 draft in a package for Deni Avdija

Marcus Smart: Acquired in February 2025 in a multi-team deal

Richuan Holmes: Acquired in February 2024 in a package for Daniel Gafford

Alex Sarr: Drafted with the #2 overall pick in the 2024 draft

Saddiq Bey: Signed in July 2024
