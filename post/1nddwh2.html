Under-discussed aspects of the Steve Ballmer and Aspiration situation

Since the Pablo Torre story about the potential cap circumvention broke, I have been following the news on it closely. I’ve been listening to podcasts like the Zach Lowe Show, and Dunc’d On, podcasts that I love, with people whos opinions I respect, but I have been surprised with how the takes from them have evolved over the last week.

When the story first broke, the consensus was that the Clippers and Steve Ballmer were in deep shit. That something fishy was definitely going on here. That Adam Silver doesn’t fuck around with tampering and cap circumvention. That the owners of the 29 other teams would be livid about the situation, and want Silver to use his power to make other teams think twice before trying some dumb shit.

But now? Now the discussion seems to be that there is not enough evidence. That there is no smoking gun. That if Ballmer is deemed guilty with no smoking gun, the NBA teams will have to police all players endorsement deals, which would be bad for the league. And the legitimacy of Torre’s “seven sources” from within Aspiration are being questioned.

Here are some things that I think are being forgotten or misconstrued by the media.

  1. Scammers gonna scam.

Media members are quick to point out that Aspiration was a scam, and that Ballmer just got scammed out of his money. That is Ballmer’s defense, and that was Mark Cuban’s defense of him as well. My question is, how often do scammers just willingly give away \(28 million (or \)50 million with the stock options) for no reason at all? Do you think these scammers, who were entirely money motivated, were in the business of giving free money to people? Why would they give all of this money for one of the quietest, most mysterious players in the NBA, to be their spokesperson while doing absolutely nothing? If your reasoning for Steve Ballmer’s involvement in this is just “He got scammed!”, you also need to provide reasoning for how giving away $28 million to Kawhi Leonard is a part of this scam.

  1. Kawhi endorsements.

Many people in the media have said that Aspiration would have used Kawhi to do endorsements if they weren’t in deep shit and going under, bankrupt. Don’t you think the first thing they would try, in an effort to avoid this, would be to use their highest paid celebrity to do…. something? Anything? It wouldn’t take a lot of effort to have Kawhi like a post on social media, wear one of their shirts, or mention them in an interview. But he likely didn’t. The only reason why he wouldn’t do these things is to keep this shady deal out of the public eye.

  1. Seven sources.

The “seven sources” will certainly play a pivotal role in the investigation going forward. They might be the most important part of the whole story. Pablo confirmed that none of the seven are going to jail, or committed any crimes while at Aspiration. So, then, why is their credibility being called into question? What do they gain from lying about the situation? There doesn’t seem to be any good reason for them to just make shit up about Steve Ballmer and cap circumvention just for fun. Would you want to pick a fight with the sixth richest guy in the world, without solid evidence?

  1. This isn’t Ballmer’s first time.

Just a quick mention that this is not Ballmer’s first time circumventing the cap. In 2015 it looked like Deandre Jordan was going to be leaving the Clippers for Dallas, then all of a sudden he re-ups with the Clippers. It later came out that Ballmer organized a deal between Lexus and Deandre which gave him an extra \(250,000. Ballmer was fined the same amount by the NBA. Steve Ballmer is an ultra competitive guy. He would do anything to win. Deandre Jordan was a great player in his prime, but not anywhere close to Kawhi Leonard, especially right after his FMVP in Toronto. And lets be honest, a \)250,000 fine for Ballmer is such an incredibly small sum of money for him. Why wouldn’t he try cap circumvention again for a much better player?

I know some of this shit is very obvious, but the media is just forgetting about these things very conveniently. I think there is already enough evidence to bring severe punishments to the Clippers, at least to the Joe Smith level from the 90s, where the Timberwolves and him circumvented the cap, and were initially docked 5 first round picks (later it ended up being 3). Pablo Torre and the NBA will leave no stone unturned. There will be more evidence.
