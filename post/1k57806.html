Getting Ascendancy Points Kills All Pacing

Nothing is more of a total buzzkill than having to run either trial in this game. I’m at the point in the game where I’m full clearing tier 15 maps with ease but still haven’t gotten half of the ascendancy points because nothing feels worse than having terrible rng and wasting a bunch of time only to die late in the trial process. The loop of having fun clearing maps, finally wanting to try a trial for the points, dying, and turning off the game has been awful.

What’s worse is that they said they alleviated this with a patch last week by lowering the level needed in Trials of Chaos from 75 to 65, but I guess this wasn’t implemented?

I specifically made my current character for fun and fast endgame mapping because my previous level 90 character was built around spectres and those are in a totally bugged state. But now even with almost 75 resist on everything I’m too squishy for Trials of Chaos because by the end of the trial there’s so much shit flying around between blood gobules, massive exploding rings, heart tethers on top of unfun trials like having to sit and guide a very slow statue while I can barely see what’s going on the screen. Throw in a debuff like less resistances or defenses and it’s just over and there’s nothing I can do. Sekemas is even more unfun to me and is more frustrating since it takes much longer to slog through and again rng can screw you over by the end. The “find the exit” rooms for example, what’s even the point of those? Traps are 10x more annoying than the monsters itself, I’m not playing an Indiana Jones game.

I just want to clear maps, kill bosses, and get loot in the typical end game conditions that I built my character around. When I’m building my character and thinking of the power fantasy of it all, I shouldn’t have to be worried about having to cater my build towards something separate from the end game loop
