My experience as a New POE2 player (never played POE 1)

I know the game is still in early access, and I must say, it’s pretty good i will mostly give the negative points i have in mind here because that’s what i remember, but the game is great in a lot of aspects, gameplay, look, endgame loop is fine. With that said, there are multiple things that makes the game clunkly.

What I loved in the first seconds of playing was the kiting possiblity. Being able to attack while moving is really cool. I was used to Shift+spell in D3/4 and Last Epoch, to make sure my spells goes through even if i miss aim the mobs. I still press Shift but the fact that the character moves is really nice.

I knew what was awaiting me in POE before launching the game. It’s a complex game which was why i’ve never played the first one. The new player experience is.. Mid, and sometimes miserable. The amount of information you need to get to understand the basics is hard. And I tried going without a guide on my first character and mid act 2, i rerolled, because i felt like i was going nowhere. It’s pretty much the same on all ARPGs you need to follow a class guide but in POE it’s even worse because the deeper you go the harder it gets to change stats spells etc. Now that i have a character level 85, It is way easier to start a new one, you get all the gems and stuff to speedup the process. But the first one is always really hard.

Now, there are some stuff that i really hate. I might be alone on this but it just feels horrible to me. When slowed by an ice patch or something else, you’re not only MS slowed, but also AS, this is atrocious. The things mobs put on the floor stays indefinetely. I was on a boss in chaos trial, there was fire, ice, poison everywhere and stayed for the whole fight. i couldn’t play the game.

Talking about trials. i’m going to stay calm, but holy this is not fun. Is it really meant to be a roguelike system ? Because i cannot see it beside selecting a room in sekhema, and having a loot in the end. Because in a roguelike you get stronger each room (more or less) and you choose things that changes how you play. Here, you just choose the affix that would least brick your key. I mean “you get 40% less defensive stats” For what ? Nothing, just F you. Or enemy hits also apply poison AND burn. Why not only one it would already be enough since there is no good counter part to it. And there are also all the environment stuff like lightning turrets or doom zones. Bro why are they so big and there a so much of them. This if for the chaos trial, this is not fun i’m sorry but if the run was longer but less punishing it would be a bit better. For sekhema, it’s just a pain, the honour system is just bad to play around. Maybe if it’s your 4th character and you’ve stacked relics you’re fine, but man as the first runs i really don’t want to go there again waste hours to just get 2 ascendency points.

The movement in the game feels clunky. I don’t know why but there on my monk, when i press tempest flurry, my character doesn’t know what to do and goes left right left right it’s so weird. There is no transparency between players, you shock each other, thats a bit annoying. The spacebar roll is nice, being able to dodge like that feels rewarding. Also the sprint after the roll is great to go faster. But while kitting mobs i ran into a problem. sometimes, I roll, I press Shift+spell to stop and cast, but its like it doesnt register and my guy starts to sprint where my cursor is which is literally inside the pack of mobs, i get stunned and die. I tried to replicate it but its random it’s not because i keep spacebar pressed or anything else.

It’s already long but i have one last thing. The in game shop is expensive as F. 30€ for 300 points meaning the map stash cost 15€ bro this is ia bit too much. I was about to spend to get more stash when i found the prices i was like well, maybe if i am desperately in need of space, because this is luxury QoL.
