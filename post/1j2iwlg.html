I hope GGG learn from this Idols PoE 1 event…

The mapping in PoE 1 is made significantly worse as an enormous amount of agency has been taken away from controlling the mapping experience. Understandably this was an event and a system they decided not to implement and for good reason.

The current actual PoE 1 mapping system has 2 major layers:

  1. atlas passives which you fully control

  2. Scarabs for “juice” rng drop and trade.

The event removed 1 and replaced it with another layer of rng and trade and it is a significantly worse experience.

In PoE 2:

  1. Atlas passives do literally nothing. They’re incredible weak and do not generate content in your maps.

  2. Tablets are rng and trade layers that have to be put into towers for further rng.

Tablets and towers in place of scarabs would be alright as extra juice. But #1 needs a full rework in this patch or it’s going to be abandoned by the bulk of PoE player base. The atlas passives need to be huge and strong again to control our endgame experience.

I love PoE 1 but if I had to use this idols system over the atlas passives for more than just an event I’d probably quit. Poe 2 suffers from the same problem, and is actually more tedious than the idols system.
