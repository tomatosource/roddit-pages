POE2 made me lost all interrest in POE1

So I bought poe2 about 2 weeks ago, and I’m having a blast. The campaign(except Viper, fk that guy), combat, graphics are so good, that made me lost all the interest on poe1. The art work is surreal, the details the skills, illumination, skills, and skills effects are already a masterpiece. I’m also really enjoying the simple crafting, before I need a PhD to do it, which will eventually come.

The only criticisms I have so far is the melee in classes in early game are way more harder than ranged, with the druid(talisman) being the only exception. The other one( I will be crucified for this) there should be a lower threshold for cast/attack speed, or at least a higher trade off.

Why the only solution for end game so far is casting the same skill fast as possible until 0 FPS? I would rather cast a single skill with higher dmg.

As for the mapping I have a mix feeling. I like the change, but I don’t like the endless delve. I hope that will be more changes in this part.

Overall, its amazing that this is only a beta game, I have paid more money to “finished” games that were not even close to this level of refinement, outstanding work by GGG, I’m really excited for what will come next, and goodbye poe1.
