Sprint Tip: If you’re using WASD controls, let go of WASD while sprinting and it’ll move towards your cursor instead

Keep holding dodge/space, but let go of WASD keys, and it’ll start targetting your mouse.

This gives more control, and follows the mouse pathfinding rules. Prevents me from bumping into all the walls as I sprint.

Edit: As people have said in the comments, you can also try “Keep sprinting while moving” under options &gt; interface &gt; gameplay if you don’t want to hold down dodge/space
