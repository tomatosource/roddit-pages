Lone Minion Builds

Hey all, I’ve been playing with a Disciple of Varashta build (basically Sandschorcher but pure Kelari/Phys) and very quickly realized that the whole “army of minions” thing isn’t for me.

Muster is almost required for damage though, since things (understandably) count as minion damage. I don’t want that to change in any way. It’s a really cool way to play and to have characters be built.

Instead, I’d love some (more?) support to be added for lone/low minion build players. Doesn’t have to be 700%° like Muster, but something that can compensate for the huge loss of damage in not using a horde. Maybe something like Supported Minions deal x% more damage. Limit 1 for Reviving Minions. Though a damage increase that quickly scales down based on minion count would be a more accurate reflection.

I dunno the specifics of what would be best, I just know it would be cool to have options for stuff like this. Even more so when they add in newer minions or minion related stuff.
