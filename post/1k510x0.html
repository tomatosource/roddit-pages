Suggestion: Guild Atlas Mapping and Passive tree

Let me start by saying that I think this is one of the most rewarding games in terms of single player progression (even with the current state of loot). But right now, the end-game is angled heavily toward solo progression, and playing with friends often feels like you’re just tagging along rather than progressing together. Sometimes you even get punished for playing with friends (only progressing in one atlas, no fragments unless you own the map etc).

One thing I’d love to see: Guild Atlas Mapping and Guild Passive tree – a separate atlas/passive tree with shared progression for guild members, unlocking content that only appears here.

  • Same loot pool as solo mapping, but with unique mechanics on how loot is earned.
  • A separate passive tree and progression tree that unlocks buffs or features for your guild Atlas
  • Requires minimum 3 players in a guild party for map progression.
  • Content and quests designed for true teamplay – think synchronized objectives, split-path challenges, maybe even group boss mechanics.
  • Guild-exclusive Events & League Mechanics

This would bring meaningful multiplayer progression to PoE2 without disrupting the core solo experience. Let guilds feel like guilds again – not just a chatroom with a stash tab. I am NOT a game designer so might be several problems with this, but if there is a world where we can reward team play in this masterpiece of a game I’d love to experience it.

Thoughts?
