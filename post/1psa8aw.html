As you progress through the campaign, the game feels less tactical

You go from slow moving mobs where combo gameplay feels possible, to Hyper- aggressive speedsters rushing you down while ranged mobs bombard you from off-screen. The Vaal and abyss enemies are the worst offenders of this. Makes me wonder whether they’ll just give up on the combo gameplay they keep talking about and just let go wild with screen clearing builds. I don’t see a way for them to really balance that.
