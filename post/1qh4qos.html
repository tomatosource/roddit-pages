What are your top 5 suggestions for the next league?

i really liked this league, i finished everything i wanted and i still play it.
The game still needs some work, but it’s clearly way better than S01.
This is my top 5 improvements I would like to see in the next league:

  1. Effect & Visibility.
  • The visibility in POE2 is not great. Sometimes I get killed because I can’t see what’s going on, there are just too many effects. It’s even worse in multiplayer. I’d like to see an “effect opacity mode” (up to -30⁄40%, for example) in solo mode, and an option to turn off companions/minions & other players effect in multiplayers.
  1. More information on mechanics / AOE zones / Boss fights.
  • Some bosses or monsters have AOE damage that’s impossible to see when they fall from the sky. Good examples: the lightning attack from the double spear Abyss monster, comets/rocks falling from the sky, the big green laser from the abyss monster or Geonor’s laser attack (you can see the atk but the AOE zone is invisible). I think a red AOE indicator would be nice to warn players that a big damage attack is incoming in that area.
  1. HP nodes, HP boost & more love for armor.
  • They made big improvements since Season 01, but compared to Energy Shield it’s still meh and way too hard to stack HP. I did my best balancing HP/defense/attack and right now, i have around 3000 HP (max reached 3700) with about 300 Energy Shield. I really tried to max my HP, maybe I did something wrong, but when I focus on it, I lose too many stats (I play Tactician). For armor, I was able to reach around 17k–22k with +400% defense armor (belt + Tactician passive), but it still doesn’t feel good enough.
  1. Overtuned bosses / Overtuned monsters (they already nerfed some, which is good).
  • Kill fast or get killed. One hit can one-shot you. Even with solid defense, HP, and crit reduction (i can go down to -140% crit if I want). I understand that big hits (with warning sounds) can one-shot you, but I don’t understand why some normal hits can delete you instantly.
  1. Change the one attempt for higher difficulty.
  • For me, one attempt, that’s fake difficulty. Running very high juiced maps is fun, but it becomes extremely unfair because you can’t see anything and even normal hits can one-shot you and the map is gone. I think they can find a better solution than this. Same for bosses with infinite spam/overlapping mechanics like XESHT or Uber Arbiter. This system encourage you to play super DPS bursts instead of balanced build & actually learning the mechanics (all-in DPS, zero defense).

Bonus: I also wanted to see a better endgame (i know they are working on it).
Love POE2!
