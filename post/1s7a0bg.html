The Toronto Raptors 31-0 run is the longest unanswered scoring run in the play-by-play era

Their 31-0 run beats out the previous record holder of 30-0, which occurred on December 3, 2023; in that game the Mavericks went on a 30-0 run to come from behind 111-87 to win 117-111 against the OKC Thunder…only to lose the game 126-120.

An absolutely astonishing accomplishment given how many players are out injured for the Raptors - including All-Star Brandon Ingram.
