Insanely Underrated Playoff Runs?

Now, this is a topic I used to bring up pretty much weekly here. But I’ll admit that I was insanely strict with what counted, mainly excluding players that got playoff awards. So this time, anything goes. So, what do you think is the most criminally underrated playoff run by a player in MLB history, and why?

My pick for hitters is definitely Scott Spiezio in 2002. Dude was raking, and racking up RBI’s like he was playing pinball. But unfortunately, he didn’t earn the ALCS or WS MVP, despite hitting just as good as Kennedy and Glaus in both series.

But my pick for pitchers is definitely a tougher pick. But Jon Lester in 2013 definitely comes to mind for me. Him, Koji, and Big Papi were like a 3-headed dragon for that Boston team.
