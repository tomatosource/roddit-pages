Unpopular opinions / wish list for 0.5 and on

To start with I’m a grinder and love actually playing the game not being a hideout warrior. That being said I’m a big fan of the temple and more mechanics like it to easily grind for currency and feel profit crafting needs to be nerfed. I’d say I’m an average to above average play who was able to put a few hundred hours in this season and am near the top of the leader board on Pathfinder.

The Temple -

Pros: best way to farm raw currency on game, best way to level in game, best way to farm majority of uniques, best way to power level your alts so you can blast through the campaign and get to end game(this has been a drag for a while now until temple). Best way to truly test your damage and strength of end game characters (after a certain point map clear speed become stagnant even though damage keeps improving on character.) has allowed the average player to dabble in crafting. Took away the stress and hoarding of [what little] currency I would normally acquire in a season. Has made all other farming option more viable.

Cons: loot and currency scaling with party (THIS is what broke the temple) additionally this also led to a very toxic experience as leeches for most of the season. Another option for this would be shared loot drops as in what ever drops can be picked up by everyone, so when a div drops everyone gets to pick up the div, etc. or some way to make it a public event where the fastest picker gets the drops and no one can complain about it being”their temple/their drops”. Temple needs a way to clean slate/ full wipe temple to start fresh.

And/or a shared temple per account or the ability to switch between 2-3 templates (this way alts can have access to your fully built temple instead of spending a few weeks to a month building a new one or access to different layout or one for currency, one for atziri farming, one for leveling, or any other layout you’d like to swap between).

Things not directly temple -

Because of the temple everything else in the game became more profitable. This is a big win for the game overall as you can do anything in game and to make currency.

Having end game mechanics to effectively farm raw currency as well as challenge your builds has made the game enjoyable throughout the entire length of the season. (Big win for actually playing the game instead of being a hideout warrior). You are able to min max a char in addition to trying out other builds during the season without being some master crafter. This makes grinding fun.

Having access to more raw currency mean your are less likely to be a hoarder and actually use some of it as intended instead of just trading it. Like rerolling jewels for max rolls(takes ALOT of divs), using chaos orbs to get decent rolls on your tablets for farming game mechanics (can take ALOT of chaos orbs). Trying out a couple mirror crafts (takes SOOO MUCH currency and time) I do have a better appreciation and understanding for crafting that I would have never experienced if not for the temple. That being said in most leagues it seems you have to be very fast and lucky to get to end game first and have some lucky crafts to get ahead of the market. After league dies down it’s almost impossible to get mirror items.

Currency

Getting enough currency to craft your own top tier items should be obtainable from grinding (10-20 hours for average grinder/mapper. Not all season with saving every last exalt orb you have). High end omens(ritual and abyss) need to be a little more common so the average player is more likely to use them thus driving demand (grinding them your becomes more obtainable and thus drives up tablet prices as well). Having T1 gear should be fairly obtainable goal for everyone and set you up for nicely end game mechanics. The real chase should come from things the Vaal Cultivation Orbs and corrupt/double corrupt items. We NEED more mechanics like this. Since obtaining T1 gear should become easier to obtain a lot more will also be bricked or poofed helping to curb supply. Also unique bases will carry a much higher premium since everyone will be trying to VCO them for the new stats.

Having mechanics to power level chars will also be an effective part of the market. Future leagues “temple runners” could host leveling parties and charge per run. Becoming a go to method for reaching lvl 100 or for your alts, driving play time.

Having season/league rewards for reaching level 100(first char- mirror/ second char-lock). This should also be accompanied by an in game vendor mechanic for mirror service. Say ange has a tab specific for this or some other way(I know similar things have been mentioned before so I can’t take credit for that idea). Use your mirror from reaching level 100 on said item at vendor and it’ll prompt you the crafters fee say 100div to mirror item. You instantly get your mirror item and crafter has divs waiting at ange for them.

Overall very fun season and hope for more grinding seasons like the temple
