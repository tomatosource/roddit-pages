The OKC Thunder with their “Championship Starting Lineup” are 0-2

Before a smart-ass says that they should trade everyone for first round picks, this post is not to say this lineup sucks.

I’m just pointing it out since while the Thunder are making their wild 24-1 run, the talk of the town is that “they’re doing it without their championship starting lineup” or that they’re doing it with missing pieces. Which is true, and is impressive.

I just find it funny that ironically, now that they’re complete, they’re starting to lose.

This lineup consists of:

  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander
  • Lu Dort
  • Jalen Williams
  • Chet Holmgren
  • Isaiah Hartenstein

They are 25-1 when one of those pieces did not play. They are 0-2 when they have the full lineup.

What do you guys think of this phenomenon?

Do you think it’s actually hurting the Thunder now that Ajay Mitchell is getting less playing time and less touches?
