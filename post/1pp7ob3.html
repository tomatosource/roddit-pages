Atziri’s Temple Tips & Tricks?

For those of us who still didn’t gave up on new league mechanic, I though it would be useful to share some knowledge on some elements of the mechanic that might be not so obvious at first glance, since it for sure has some depth to it.

I wouldn’t have created this post if i didn’t had anithing on my hands, so here’s what I have found. So at some point I noticed that, when you close the temple and it destabilizes, destroying some rooms and paths, it can’t destroy the rooms in such way, that some of remaining rooms left disconnected from paths. So for example, if you have a sequence of rooms A-B-C-D where only room A is connected to paths, game can’t destroy room A while B isn’t destroyed, room B can’t be destroyed while C isn’t destroyed etc.

Why is it useful? There is Medallion called “Juatalotli’s Medallion” which can be used on a room to prevent it from being destroyed next time you close the temple, so if you use this medallion on last room in a long sequence of rooms, you actually preserve the whole sequence.

Here’s screenshot of my temple after closing it with Architect defeated. To my regret I haven’t done screenshot of it prior to closing it, so I by memory painted places of rooms and paths that were destroyed. Blue lines are paths, red rhombs are rooms (only regular rooms, not architect exclusive), yellow rhomb is architect, and green rhomb highlights the room I used Juatalotli’s Medallion on before closing the temple. How you can see, I have preserved a 6 room sequence whole while all the other rooms got destroyed. So my strategy now is trying to build long sequences of rooms and storing Juatalotli’s Medallions before doing Architect, So I could defeat architect without losing all of my progress on temple.

I also noticed that after my regular runs (runs where I don’t kill architect) never more than one room (last) in sequence got destroyed, but I don’t have big enough sample of runs to confirm that it’s how it works, I might just have been lucky. There is also more things that I want to test out, but didn’t yet had opportunity to do.

So if you have figured something about temple mechanic that you think isn’t very obvious but also might be useful, please share it here.
