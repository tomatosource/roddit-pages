Endgame juicing is a massive chore

Juicing Strategy:

You absolutely need a 3 Tower overlap for multiplicative value, 8 7% Iradiation/Boss Quantity Tablets and 1 Unique Tablet that removes Irradiation on your maps after completing it so you can run your juiced 56% quantity Maps 2 times instead of 1.

With a good 3 Tower setup you get around 10-12 maps with 56% quant so 20-24 Runs.

For waystone mods you only care about Rarity, Rarity/Quant and Increased Rare monsters, distilled with 45% Increased Rare Monsters.

Really Juiced Maps will be like 120% Quant 300% Rarity and 120% increased Rare monsters.

Now for these 20-24 maps you are making 10-15 divs on average in raw drops, sales from Tier 4⁄5 Rares and exchange.

Now the juice is over and you need to look for another 3 Tower overlap and path to it only to realize between the towers are empty space and you can look for another tower setup and wasted 1-2 Hours on meaningless maps.

You spend multiple hours setting up your towers and rolling your maps only to enjoy 20 maps with good loot.

I just wands to log in, juice up 20 maps and run them and not plan my next pathing route for a possibly bricked 3 tower setup.

The endgame problem from 0.1 is basically the same in 0.2, juicing locked behind towers can and will never work and I hope GGG sees this issue and plans a overhaul of the endgame before the full release.
