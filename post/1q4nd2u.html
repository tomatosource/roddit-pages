In 1981, for the NBA’s 35th anniversary, the league writers put together an all-time team and voted for the GOAT player, coach, and team, the only such vote of its kind. Here were the results

https://www.basketball-reference.com/awards/nba_35th_anniversary.html

The GOAT: voted as Bill Russell (11x champ, 5x MVP)

GOAT coach: Red Auerbach (9x champ)

GOAT team: 1967 76ers (best offense ever when they played, wins record at that time, beat Celtics dynasty. Wilt, Hal Greer (arguably best SG of 60s), Billy Cunningham (future ABA MVP, nearly won an NBA MVP), 7x All-Star Chet Walker

All-time team (just 11 players, as that was how small rosters were. It would be expanded to 12 the very next season).

In alphabetical order:

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar (69-89)

Elgin Baylor (58-72)

Wilt Chamberlain (59-73)

Bob Cousy (50-63)

Julius Erving (70-86) (realistically they counted his ABA years to include him)

John Havlicek (62-78)

George Mikan (46-54) (56), counting the NBL

Bob Pettit (54-65)

Oscar Robertson (60-74)

Bill Russell (56-69)

Jerry West (60-74)

Some potential snubs: Rick Barry (only player to lead NBA, ABA, college basketball in scoring, carried 75 Warriors to a title) Dolph Schayes (all-time leading scorer until 1964, 12 time all-star, champ) Elvin Hayes (12x All-star, champ, retired top three all-time in points, rebounds and blocks a few years later)
