Proposed solution to the +lvl of spells “issue”

So, coming from the argument that +lvl to spells on weapons is the core issue - and not on other sources such as focus, jewels, etc. The issue being that its so uniquely powerful to the degree that it’s such a mandatory affix to have on your weapon that any other rolls/affixes practically only serve as quality separators between items with the same +lvl roll.

First off, I want to quickly also mention why this isn’t an issue for martial weapons: +Flat damage.
Staves and wands only have increased damage and gain damage. These have quickly diminishing returns, as gain stacks additively with itself and functions as another increased layer.
On the other hand, martial weapons come with an implicit +flat damage, and can roll additional flat damage. The increased% physical damage also scales this flat damage, rather than act as an actual increased%; making it actually be a %more source.
This flat implicit physical damage scales with the weapon “tier” (like dualstring -&gt; twin -&gt; gemini) having a higher baseline flat damage.
Spell weapons do not have this, and instead only come with a higher level implicit skill gem that no one actually uses (outside of spellslinger).

Now, its important to not just make spell weapons follow the same system as martial weapons.

My proposed idea:

Remove +Level of spells as an affix. Replace it with something maybe. But ideally to keep the overall balance intact, not something strong.

Add +Level of spells as an implicit to the weapon. Have this scale with the item iLvl. Since we don’t have a strict tier 1, 2, 3 version of staves and wands, we cannot have it based around this like martial weapons. But this would also prevent having to make as many balance changes and adding new items. Have the +Spell type be dependant on the item thematic. For example:
Ashen staff iLvl 1: +0 to fire spells
Ashen staff iLvl 2 +1 to fire spells
Ashen staff iLvl 18: +2 to fire spells
Withered wand iLvl 55: +4 to chaos spells.
For staves, some tiers have roll ranges. The items could either drop with the same roll range, or just the lower of the the range.
It would be up to GGG’s discretion for which items would have which spell type, as not all skill gems attached to the current wands/staves are explicitly of one element/generic.

On the same front, I would also like to propose a change to arcanist etcher:
Have it work like quality does for martial weapons and equipment: %more spell damage. Naturally this would require a -1lvl on the base implicits to keep the balance.
Instead, to increase quality of the skillgem attached to the wand/staff, let the user apply gemcutters prism to the weapon for +5% quality.

On the same front, I would also like to propose that movementspeed is moved to the implicit of boots; With different boot types being either +5% or a higher base defense, similar to body equipment either having a high roll range, or a special effect such as +mana/es regeneration/spirit.

This post isn’t an implementable balance suggestion, but an idea for a design direction.
