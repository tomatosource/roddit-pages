5 Jrue Holiday Trades

Its that time of year Celtics Nation! We’re going into the great unknown with next year having more question marks than we’ve had in a long time. The thing we are almost positive of, is Jrue Holiday is most likely getting moved for cap reasons. That’s even more likely given the Tatum injury, and the Celtics not being clear contenders walking into the season.

I’ve cobbled together 5 trades for you guys to dissect and complain about and make the usual “X GM hands up the phone”

&nbsp;

Sacramento gets:

Jrue Holiday, future 2nd

I’m ngl, i don’t know what the Kings are doing. I do know that Zsch Lavine and DeMar DeRozan aren’t it. Jrue is a ceiling raiser for whatever franchise he’s on, and could help organize a really discombobulated Sacramento roster. Jrue can be a bit more of a playmaker than he has the last few seasons, and brings defensive leadership and playoff experience. Sacramento has LaVine and Sabonis as their 1-2 scoring punch, like it or not thats what they’ve invested in. Bringing in defense and veteran leadership, especially someone with success, should be a priority.

&nbsp;

Boston gets:

DeMar DeRozan

Keon Ellis

Boston gets out of Jrue’s money a year early, and pick up DeRozan, who makes nearly 10m less whan Jrue. With Tatum out for the immediate future, and questions around Boston’s other core guys, DeRozan could fill this gap year where the C’s are figuring things out. While he doesn’t bring Boston back up to contender, this could be a fun playoff team. Keon Ellis is an emerging 3 and D guy who plays with solid energy. He’s in a similar mold to Torrey Craig, just younger.

&nbsp;

Detroit gets:

Jrue Holiday

Detroit is going to be aggressive looking for experience this offseason. They just had their best season in forever and should be looking at a ceiling raiser. Jrue Holiday is great in that role, and is able to play as a secondary ball handler opposite Cade, while helping their spacing off the ball.

&nbsp;

Washington gets:

Tobias Harris, 2 future 2nd round picks

Washington swaps out bad contracts and picks up 2nd round picks, which is a solid move for where they are.

&nbsp;

Boston gets:

Marcus Smart, Saddiq Bey

This one is almost purely to get out of Jrue’s contract. Smart’s contract is an expiring deal and can be moved again at the deadline with a pick, if the Celtics want to go for it in the 2nd half of next year. It also gives them a 2 year look at Saddiq Bey, who has been out for forever with an ACL tear. This roster has so many question marks next season and beyond, and giving a guy an opportunity to bounce back is a good use of our time next year. He’s also only making 6/7m a year on a 2 year deal, so this is pretty low impact high potential reward.

&nbsp;

LA Clippers get:

Jrue Holiday, Xavier Tillman, Jarace Walker

The Clippers continue their trend of acquiring past their prime guys to put together a quality team. Holiday’s contract lines up with Leonard’s and lets be real, this team isn’t going to be able to move Kawhi. They’re locked into this thing anyway, so getting Jrue fits that. Jrue is a great glue guy, and they add him without losing Zubac or Powell, who they really like. They also add Tillman who can play more to his natural role of being a strength based defender, passer, and clean up guy. They also pick up Jarace Walker who could use a change of scenery after being buried by Indiana.

&nbsp;

Boston gets:

Bogdan Bogdanvic

Bogdanovic had a meh season and honestly a bad playoffs. He might fit Joe Mazzula’s 3 point green light system better than the Clippers very heliocentric offense. There are definitely shots available in Boston with Tatum out. His contract is only 2 years so he’d have Boston’s potential down year next year, and then he’d be a movable expiring deal after that. He also makes half of what Jrue makes and in this deal he’s the only salary coming back.

&nbsp;

Indiana gets:

Derrick Jones Jr., future 2nd from Clippers

Indiana gets a guy who fits their fast paced offense and also has finals experience. The fit makes a lot of sense. They lose Walker, a former top 8 overall pick, who just hasn’t broken into their rotation. They have a handful of guys ahead of him and are even playing Thomas Bryant ahead of him at times.

&nbsp;

Dallas gets:

Jrue Holiday

Even though Dallas fell apart last season, they still have a roster of Kyrie Irving, Anthony Davis, Derrick Lively, PJ Washington, and they’re about to add the #1 overall pick to it. Dallas has 2 routes to go, strip the roster down for parts and do a full rebuild, or keep the roster together and go for a playoff run. Being competitive and setting a winning mentality early is how we ended up with Brown, Tatum, and a ring, and I can’t imagine a better vet than Jrue Holiday. Jrue and AD’s deals would run together for 3 years, having them as leaders and guides for this #1 overall pick, presumably Flagg.

&nbsp;

Brooklyn gets:

Klay Thompson, 2025 1st from Boston, future 2nd from Dallas

Brooklyn has cap space and not a lot to do with it. Eating a 2 year deal for a few draft picks is exactly the mode this team is in. They don’t have a young stud to build around either, and should be throwing darts at the draft.

&nbsp;

Boston gets:

Daniel Gafford, Cody Martin

Boston gets a couple of bruisers. With Tatum down, Jrue most likely traded, and huge question marks around Al Horford and Kristaps Porzingis, Boston needs to retool around Jaylen Brown and Derrick White. Gafford has 1 year on his current contract, and Martin makes less than 10m, so he’s super movable after this season. Cutting up Jrue’s deal into 2 smaller deals gives Brad flexibility for smaller trades at the deadline and beyond. Boston has lacked some toughness and have been out finessing people the last few years, these 2 balance that out a bit.

&nbsp;

LA Lakers get:

Jrue Holiday

This is exactly the type of signing Lebron tries to get his teams to do and could help them get Lebron to recommit to a 2⁄3 year deal that lines up with Jrue. This is a pretty massive upgrade over Gabe Vincent at PG, and they know Jrue can play on or off point based on whoever he is playing with. Hachimura and Vincent have been underwhelming in LA. The only real asset they’d be losing is Knecht, who they’ve already shown willing to trade to improve the roster. It doesn’t get them the C they’re looking for, but it improves the roster, and they dont lose Reeves, who is their best asset to get a big man.

&nbsp;

Boston gets:

Rui Hachimura, Gabe Vincent, Dalton Knecht

Celtics get long term cap relief with Hachimura and Vincent only being 1 year deals. They can either be 1 year transition guys, or get moved mid season, but either way this clears the long term Jrue deal. They are also able to pick up Knecht who fits well as a shooter who can do a little more. They could even rope in a 3rd team to absorb either Hachimura or Vincent for a 2nd round pick.
