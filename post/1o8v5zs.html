He did not assume the primary MLB PBP role w/ FOX until 2022, which they had the NLCS that year

2023: the ALCS that was infamous for its parallel to the 2019 WS in which all 7 games were won by the road team. Texas vs Houston

2025: Mariners vs Blue Jays. SEA wins the first two @ Toronto. Toronto wins the next 2 @ SEA, pushing the series to 2-2 (where we are now)

That is TWELVE games in a row that’ve been won by the ALCS road team when Joe Davis is the announcer. Does it mean anything? No. It’s just a very interesting circumstance.
