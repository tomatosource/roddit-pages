Most Underrated World Series Team?

Alright, I’m gonna try something different.

We hear every now and then about Murderer’s Row, The Core Four, ‘04 Miracle Red Sox, Big Red Machine, etc. But for every legendary championship team, there are some that kinda fly under the radar. They’re not totally forgotten, obviously, but they’re not as talked about as much. Which of these kinda teams do you think is the most underrated?

My pick would probably go to the 2014 Giants. There’s a lot of cool aspects about their run. 87-win season, 5th seed in the NL, and probably the biggest underdog out of all 10 playoff teams that year. But they dominated in October, going 12-5 overall and becoming the first 5 seed in MLB history to win the title. Most of the heavy lifting was thanks to a phenomenal run by Madison Bumgarner, but their bullpen, defense, and offense also came in clutch as well.
