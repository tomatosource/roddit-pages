PoE2 needs to be a more clearer, in-game, about snapshoting

Snapshot effects are oversights or bugs most of them time in many ARPGs, a way to game the mechanics to get the bonuses of another specialization without it costing anything from your primary spec. You don’t expect the game to signpost them, because they aren’t really meant to be a part of the game in the first place.

PoE2 is absolutely not like that. Between weapon sets and separate passive trees, you are clearly meant to use snapshots. The Druid is literally built around it, switch to a staff and spell tree, cast a long-lasting spell, and enjoy the snapshot as you switch back to animal form and attack. Jonathan even talked about this in his interview with Tri, noting—with some confusion, which felt a little out of touch, to me—that people feel uncomfortable with using weapon swaps this way. The reality is, in most games of the genre, it’s often kind of an abuse.

Given that it is a key, initiative part of PoE2, and also that not everything gets a snapshot, the game needs to be a lot, lot better about informing us when things retain a snapshot and when things don’t. It can’t just be looking it up on the wiki, hoping someone else has already done the trial and error and then actually took the time to document it. It absolutely cannot be “I know it when I see it,” since Jonathan and I are some things about the game very differently.

I should just be able to tell that a curse or a thunderstorm that I cast with my staff and spell tree will continue to enjoy the benefits of those things when I switch to a weapon and attack tree. I should also just know that, say, a persistent minion won’t, that its stats will always reflect my current weapon and tree. And I should be able to know where a non-persistent minion like zombie falls—because right now, I’m not sure.
