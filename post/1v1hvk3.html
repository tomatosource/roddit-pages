Bugs are really killing this game

https://preview.redd.it/ga4o4haz3deh1.png?width=613&format=png&auto=webp&s=8c67ab43009b05b5c57df082ac1e0e45ce3ef876

I really enjoy playing poe2 and love to sink hours of my valuable time into it. However, I have a feeling that the game suffers so many game breaking bugs, that I just hope they somehow tackle it. And they are really game breaking.

Simulacrum: The picture is from my last run. There is a single ecreep I cannot reach because it spawned behind the fence. So the run is - again - lost.

Expedition. The remants had been bugged the last on and off. But there are also UNKILLABLE rune combinations. I had one a few days ago, where a minion spawned other minions. Every time you killed one of those new spawns, the “mother” got healed to full. As I have an AOE build, I simply cannot do anything.

Hunt for the beast: This was bugged for me from the beginning. It looks like that some league mechanic “overwrote” my great beast map. Or it was some bugged multiplayer interaction. Anyways. I CANNOT do the first great beast. This means that I cannot get hilda buffs. Neither on my main char, nor on all alts, as they share the atlas. Basically, Hilda is out of bounds for me thanks to a bug.

A similar thing happened to a friend, but with the expedition quest… bugged out. Cannot be finished…

I chose my current build (corrupted blood fortifying cry gemling)for visual clarity. It looked great for a long time. Now? All killed minions still have their debuffs on them, littering the floor.

https://preview.redd.it/gcjqm2q65deh1.png?width=800&format=png&auto=webp&s=bd489de42aceb35b6a68d2202233fc0e55c9c474

And there are many more bugs, some of them break the game (ruin league mechanics), break all characters or just ruin the character you spend hours and hours to build… honestly… get those bugs out asap.
