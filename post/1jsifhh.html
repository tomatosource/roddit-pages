Meta builds MUST faceroll content, or casual builds cannot be viable

I think there is a fundamental logical philosophy that the POE2 developers have missed here with 0.2 They want a “challenging” game in which meta builds can’t faceroll the content, but they fail to understand that you can only achieve this at the cost of punishing the majority of players.

This logical concept should be intuitive, but clearly it has not been understood. There will always be meta builds that are better than the rest. If you balance your ARPG so that average players can make homebrew builds and reasonably complete the content, then by definition the meta builds will faceroll the content.

There is no getting around this tradeoff. I personally think it is bad design philosophy to punish the average player that doesn’t employ a meta build. In fact, this can make the problem worse: more players than ever will flock to the meta due to the 0.2 changes.
