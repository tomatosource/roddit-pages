Only a third of the way through their first season with TV rights, Prime has better leagu pass integration and streaming quality, than the NBA themselves

The NBA Leagu pass app has sucked for years. The price isn’t bad, but the app is so laggy. My biggest gripes with the NBA app

  1. Switching game to game takes forever on the NBA app. On Prime it’s almost instant. You can also seamlessly add multi-view in Prime, which the NBA app struggles to load, and constantly buffers

  2. The video quality just seems better. The NBA app takes forever to finally get into HD, but the Prime app does so right away

  3. There has been a glitch for the past three years now where audio is out of sync on the NBA app. Before you hit play now, it’ll load the audio, and then once you hit play, it’ll also load THAT audio too. Only way to fix is to quit the app and restart

Overall, it’s embarrassing that the NBA has ran leagu pass for over a decade, yet it’s filled with terrible UI. Shout out whoever at Amazon did all this.
