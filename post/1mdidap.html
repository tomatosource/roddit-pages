[Spoiler] New 0.3 Act Boss potentially teased in new GGG interview

In a recent series of GGG interviews by DarthMicrotransactions (poe2 content creator) who recently visited the GGG studio in New Zealand, Darth showed a recent interview with GGG Steven, the Lead level designer, that contained some small hints at an Act 4 boss that could be coming in 0.3.

In the interview GGG Steven comments on an upcoming boss in 0.3 where they designed a map for a boss fight with a spike trap system that the player can interact with “on a tactical basis”

Source (36:45): https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2526638215

From footage we currently have of Act 4, GGG Mark Roberts previously showcased the Izaro’s Prison Act 4 Boss fight with a boss called The Prisoner.

In this boss showcase Mark points out that there are 3 ballista traps that the player can interact with in the arena that shoot “A big metal spike” (quote) into the boss.

Source (35:09): https://youtu.be/5ji6yuWJnac?si=dsVI2_zUcTfQRmwa&t=2109

This hints at the Act 4 boss from Izaro’s prison potentially coming in 0.3, but it does not fully confirm it as they could have designed a different boss that uses a player-interactable trap system using spikes. Since the Act 4 boss is currently the only known boss with such a mechanic that we know of, it seems likely that the Lead Level Designer is referring to the same boss showcased in Act 4.
