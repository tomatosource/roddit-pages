2026 draft prospects

I absolutely love the draft and will talk about it all year. This is the first year in a while we have a pick thats not 25+ so I wanted to highlight some guys in our draft range that captured my interest.

&nbsp;

Chris Cenac - 6’10” Big

Projected: 7th - 10th

Cenac is a top 10 talent, but with this lottory system theres an outside chance the C’s sneak into the top 10 where Cenac will be available. Cenac is a springy jump shooting big, who even has some fade away nuance to his game. He has a very high release point and with just a little space is able to shoot over most defenders. Hes also solid in transition and cutting to the basket. While hes a decent passer hes not going to be a big assist guy. He also pretty much has no back to the basket game. Hes 100% face up, cutting, and shooting. Defensively hes a solid help shot blocker, but can absolutely get obliterated in the post by NBA bodies. While hes not crazy slow, he gets jumpy on the perimeter defending and can get blown by. He has great tools here but bad discipline.

Shades of: Kelel Ware, Brook Lopez, Lauri Markkanen

&nbsp;

Yaxel Lendeborg - 6’10” Big

Projected: 15th - 20th

Yaxel shows elite basketball IQ on both ends. He can find cutters and shooters out of the post on offense. He also has solid 3 point and FT shooting metrics meaning he has a potential for hitting 3’s at the NBA level. Defensively hes again smart, and stays in position well. Hes not an elite shot blocker and probably wont be able to switch at an elite level. Hes also a good rebounder who is comfortable bringing the ball up the floor for a fast break. Or finding the right outlet pass.

Shades of: Al Horford, Alperen Sengun, Naz Reid

&nbsp;

Matt Able - 6’6” Wing

Projected: 11th - 15th

Strong and smart are the name of the game for Able. Hes a strong midrange shooter and finisher, and is already comfortable playing within the flow of an offense, not forcing shots. While hes looked ok from 3, the volume to say hes a ready 3 point shooter isnt there. He can get crafty and use both hands to slam it home too. Defensively hes a willing and solid on ball defender, but sometimes loses track of cutters.

Shades of: Jaylen Brown, Aaron Nesmith

&nbsp;

Labaron Philon - 6’4” Guard

Protested - 11th - 20th

Philon is a fast ball handler adept to collapsing defenses, its just a matter of it he knows what to do once he does that. Hes more of a project than some of the other guys, but with grest size and atheltic tools, he could be a starting PG in the NBA. Hes made great progress as a passer, but still throws some risky or poorly timed passes. Hes streaky as a 3 point shooter. Defensively he has decent size for an NBA PG, but he is lean and can be bumped or muscled off his spot. He also gambles a bit too much for steals.

Shades of: DeArron Fox, Dennis Shroeder, Collin Sexton

&nbsp;

Tounde Yessoufou - 6’5” Wing

Projected - 15th - 25th

An absolute unit, Tounde Yessoufou projects as a plus defender right away. Hes an aggressive slasher who can body people out of his way. He excels at playing through contact and getting to the line. While he hasn’t been a great 3 point shooter, his high ft% suggests its in the cards to develop there. Yessoufou can violently attack the rim and finish with the best of them. Defensively, dude is an absolute animal who you’d look to defend 1-4. He can stay with ball handlers on the perimeter, most guys wont be able to body him in the post, and he can elevate quickly enough to contest shots.

Shades of: Lu Dort, Victor Oladipo, Marcus Smart
