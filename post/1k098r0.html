Devs, please don’t neglect console players this time.

Hi,

So yes, I am a peasant console player. However, I am not so different to you in that I absolutely love PoE2.

I have been a Diablo player since the OG D2. When PoE1 released, I ditched D3 for a while trying to get into it, but unfortunately the game ran horribly on the PS4 and this has remained the case to this day.

I’m really hoping PoE2 doesn’t fall into the same trap.

Now don’t get me wrong, the game runs pretty good on the PS5. Far better than PoE1 did on the PS4. However, there are still many problems that I am trying to ignore, telling myself to enjoy the game as is and that these problems are only temporary. Things like targeting and instability in the end-game are in my opinion, the biggest issues with the game on console. Playing any class besides a melee class is rough enough as it is, and even worse when you are running at 20 FPS in the late stages of the game.

Please don’t neglect us GGG. We love you too.
