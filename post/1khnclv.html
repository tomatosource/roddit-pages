Post your QoL improvement ideas for POE2

I’ve seen a lot of people suggest GGG should add x or y. I thought it might be a good idea to compile all those ideas in one place where it could possibly get some visibility and traction. With that in mind I’d like to ask everyone to post their QoL ideas, preferably with a bit of a reason for their inclusion and to upvote your favourites.

Here’s a few of mine.

  • Breach and Delirium should drop all loot at the end of the event. These events are timed and on juiced maps there’s a lot of loot so looting as you go isn’t very feasible. Currently you end up having to backtrack all over the map which is time consuming and tedious.
  • There should be an option to turn off the Delirium filter/music. The game is beautiful and colourful however if you want to juice your waystones you end up stuck with the same music with washed out colours whenever you instil waystones.
  • The rarity borders on vendors should be expanded to everything, inventory, stash tabs, the lot. Maybe include an option to turn it off for those who don’t like it. It helps with visibility and makes it easier to tell at a glance what rarity an item is.
  • The inventory and stash tabs should have a rearrange button that automatically rearranges loot to fit in the most optimal way to save space. There should also be a lock option to prevent some items from moving. When you pick up items its pretty common that loot is stacked in a very inefficient manner. Adding a rearrange button would prevent the busywork of rearranging things to make them fit.
  • The trials for both Sekhemas and Chaos should include a stash at the end of every level. Both of these events encourage you to collect loot yet its very tedious to do so. Chaos allows you to zip to town and back easily enough but Sekhemas requires teleporting out, waypointing back in, activating the pedestal and walking in. Having a stash would save a lot of time and frustration.
  • Events that cause massive loot dumps (Expeditions, Breaches, Delirium) should spawn a stash nearby, if only temporary. This would allow you to quickly transfer all loot to your stash without jumping back to town multiple times. This is mostly an issue when you’re stacking quantity and rarity on juiced maps.
  • Change the Atlas Map to stay at the last position with a jump to last completed node option. Time and again I’ll check the atlas decide what I want to do, then go fish a waystone out of the stash only to have to pan around trying to find where I was looking earlier.
