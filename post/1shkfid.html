Annual “Vault” Weekend for Legacy Supporter Packs

Dear GGG,

With *Path of Exile 2* bringing in a massive wave of new players, I’d like to propose a way to celebrate the game’s history while giving the art team’s older work a second life: A once-a-year “Legacy Weekend” where past Supporter Packs become available for a limited time.

I know the “Limited Edition” tag is a core part of the marketing, but hear me out on how this could work without ruining the prestige for veterans.

The Proposal:

  • Time-Limited: Only available for 48–72 hours once per year.
  • Purchase Cap: Limit accounts to one legacy pack purchase per event to keep them rare.
  • Cross-Game Support: Even if they aren’t fully ported to PoE2 yet, let us buy them for use in PoE1 now with the “future-proof” promise of the shared microtransaction system.

Why this is a Win-Win:

  1. For New Players: Many are seeing things like the Cosmic *Turtle Hideout* for the first time and are gutted they can’t ever get it. This gives them a “second chance” at a piece of PoE history.
  2. For Veterans: Even long-time players miss a pack here and there due to real-life timing. This lets us “complete the collection” without devaluing the original run.
  3. For GGG: It’s a massive revenue spike on assets that are already created. It lets the artists’ older work “come alive” for a brand-new generation of Exiles.
  4. Managing FOMO: By capping it to one pack per year, players still have a huge incentive to buy new packs *now* rather than waiting years for a specific “Vault” weekend. ( to address the concern of new packs not being purchased)

Addressing the Downsides:

  • The “Work” Argument: I 100% understand porting everything to PoE2 is a massive task. My suggestion is: Enable them in PoE1 first. We are adults; we can make a decision on buying a pack that will only work in POE1 for now and have to wait. ( since it will be reoccurring things everybody can make their mind when to purchase) Also pretty sure thy have already committed to this work anyway :D
  • Exclusivity: I know some will hate this because they bought packs specifically because they were “gone forever.” However, by limiting the window and the quantity, the “OG” status of those items stays mostly intact.

To the community: I’m not someone who buys everything in the shop—I have a budget like anyone else. I just genuinely enjoy supporting GGG and getting well-designed MTX in exchange. This system would give those of us who can’t buy *every* pack a fair chance to finally grab that one “masterpiece” we missed. If you never buy packs, this changes nothing for you—but for those of us who do, it’s a second chance to support the devs and own a bit of PoE history.

If the “Delirium Fog” guy taught us anything, it’s that GGG actually listens to feedback. If you’ve ever stared at a legacy set or hideout with regret, please upvote or comment so the devs can see there is a market for this!
