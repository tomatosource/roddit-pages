What was your best trading strategy this season

Hey all, I’ve probably had one of my best seasons ever this league and it was due to a few good trading strategies early on as well as grinding craftofexile.com. It’s unfortunate because most of the strategies where fairly simple but people still just assume if you have currency, you RMT.

I’ve posted a few strategies to Reddit already but my easiest and most lucrative trade strategy this season was early on with undying hates. I had actually been looking for a kurgal base with 2 increased tributes nodes which I thought was part of the seed. I had not div’d one yet but realized it changed the whole base rather than just the seed #.

Then I noticed that tecrod base was required to make bloodmage work. My single div had turned my kurgal base into a tecrod base which was selling for about 39d. The unid were selling for 6-7 and non corrupted, non tecrod base were selling for 4. So I started to buy up as many as I could unid’d or non tecrod then div slammed to tec and sold.

This actually worked for almost a month before the market adjusted. I think this was my best strategy in terms of ease and how lucrative it was. The other stuff was just mastering bloodmage crafting and making up dope wands and amulets and rings.

Please note that this strategy is no longer viable, while tecrod base continue to sell, it’s about 6 divs away from an unid base. At 1⁄5 options you’d have to be super lucky but it’s about break even mathematically. Just wanted to show people how simple the strategy can be to generate a ton of currency!
