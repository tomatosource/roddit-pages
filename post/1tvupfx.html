Where I come to terms with - and make the case for - rooting for the Knicks as a Celtics fan in NYC

I am at peace with the realization I am rooting for the Knicks. I cannot but see a mirror of the 2004 Sox.

We moved to NYC from Boston in 2012, months after the Giants beat the Pats for the 2nd time. Since then, only the NY Liberty have gotten a parade down the Canyon of Heroes. It’s like I cursed the city by moving here. I have wanted it to stay that way forever, but the Knicks have cracked my resolve.

Lest you think I’ve gone soft…

14 years in the city has not softened my hatred of the Yankees. I want every beefy, mouth-breathing Tony riding the subway to The Bronx on game day in his gold chains and Jeter jersey, every dentist from New Jersey leaving in the 8th inning to beat traffic to know that their best years were 30 years ago, when Rudolf Giuliani was mayor. Your glory days will never come back. On his deathbed, Tony will have three regrets: he lost his mother’s lasagna recipe, he was arrested at his daughter’s wedding and Aaron Judge failed to win a ring.

The Mets are on a 40 year run of suffering and I confess a soft spot for them. The fans represent more of the heart of the city. I’ll have a hard time not smiling if they finally win a title. And if they win, it will make Yankee fans suffer more than a 5th Red Sox title this century. But I’m not holding my breath.

I will never, ever tire of the Jets’ tripping on their own dicks. Year after year. Their futility sustains me. It’s like pleasure I get out of re-watching VEEP. The butt fumble might be the last thing I see as I draw my terminal breath.

The Giants, of course, have one - well, two - on us. I’ve quietly enjoyed their 14 years of failure and dysfunction since 2012. But quietly. I want Jaxson Dart to get caught with a male prostitute.

Because I live on the West side, on the subway line that runs to MSG, I see Rangers fans - whole families, couples - in jerseys going to games at rush hour and it’s hard not to have respect. They are an Original Six team. They have great uniforms. I can only respect and don’t know how I’d feel if they won the Cup.

The Nets are not a thing.

What is happening with the Knicks is special. It’s undeniable.  Mock the celebrities sitting courtside all you want, but that’s the reality of the city. Also basketball. It is a city game. And the Knicks have not won a title since Nixon was President. If they win, the city will pulse and shake across all five boroughs like a reverse 9⁄11. The team was assembled almost entirely by trades and free agents, but they did a remarkable job assembling a team with heart and grit. The vibes, I’m afraid to say, are 2003-2004 Red Sox and that strikes a chord.

I’m not disingenuous. I’m not going to wear a Knicks shirt. But the City needs this. And it will be better for the NBA.
