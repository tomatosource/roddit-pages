A story about Mike Gorman

Hey guys,

Long time lurker, 1st time poster :) I

So I was reading the comments on a post about that Twolves commentator being salty AF about JB making those five 3’s in the first quarter against the Wolves and it was filled with people whinging about how ‘biased’ the Celtics announcers are. Firstly, this isn’t even true. Drew is pretty damn neutral for a home commentator and Scal spends a lot of time praising guys on other teams. During that same Wolves game, he was defending Gobert multiple times and even said a few times ‘if you don’t like Ant, you don’t like basketball.’ Pretty damn friendly for an opposing team’s commentary team. Anyway, I digress as my story is about Mike, who the whole topic of Celtics commentators got me thinking about.

2 years ago, I was at the Garden watching a game. I come from Australia so this was no short trip, travelling nearly 24 hours door to door to be there. I love my Celtics - I’d do it every week if I could :)

Anyway, I was a few rows back of Mike’s table and I was early into the Garden as Brad had been so kind as to give me a really special experience (a story for another day but in short, he is absolutely a FIRST CLASS human too). Mike walked down and I thought about it for a sec, thinking ‘he doesn’t wanna waste time chatting to a random’ but I thought ‘who knows if I’ll ever get the chance again’ and just said ‘hey Mike’ when he was a few metres away. He smiled at me and said ‘hey how are ya?’ I said ‘yeah good thanks mate. Yourself?’ Evidently picking up on the odd accent, he stopped walking towards his table and walked up to greet me. He shook my hand and said ‘where are you from?’ We chatted for a few mins, with him asking me what Australia is like, how long I’m in Boston for, what got me into the Celtics etc.’ I was struck by just what a kind hearted and humble man he is. He took nearly 5 mins to just stand and chat with me, as well as take a pic together.

Anyway, a few days later the Celtics were home against OKC and I was back at the Garden. I was walking near the entrance to the stadium when suddenly I felt a tap on the shoulder after someone had just walked past me. I turned my head and who was it but Mike Gorman? He said ‘thats twice in a few days now. How are ya?’ Again, we chatted for a few mins and before he ran up the stairs into the Garden, I thanked him for being so kind as to take a few mins to chat with me and for being such a class act for the Celtics all these years. We shook hands and he said he hoped to see me back again one day.

That was the whole story but I just thought about him today, what a class act he is and how humble he is to take time to make someone’s day a little better.

Greatness throughout this organisation all through its history :)
