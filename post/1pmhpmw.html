Poe2 skill design

The release of druid cemented the fact that I won’t be enjoying making builds in poe2.

I’ll preface that I actually do enjoy the supposedly slower pace, ‘combo’ playstyles. I’ll play most janky, offmeta or even quin builds in poe1(&gt;!and in ssfhcbtw!&lt;). But the way GGG approached skill design, which is actually class design, is just so hamfisted and limiting. And dare I say even more so than D4.

The druid “build showcase” just showcased that they want the ““class”” play in a certain way and any deviation is just players finding an overtunned piece of the puzzle.

Wanna play plants? You gotta get that keystone(which is the laziest designed keystone GGG ever came up with yet)! Imagine if they finally make ignite good by making a keystone that is simply your hits deal 80% less damage and you have 1000% more ignite magnitude.

Hey we buffed the smith kitava trigger fire spells thingy! Hmmm, let me check which “fire spells” it can trigger, oh look there is like 3 fire spells total in the game, all of them have a limit, and all of energy nodes are on the opposite side of the tree, and most of mace attacks are awful at triggering anything in the first place.

Hey remember mjolner, it’s in the poe2! Luckily you can start in the correct part of the tree if you want this time, but then again mace skills still suck at triggering anything. There is like 4 lightning skills and all of them require infusions or some other conditional BS.

Playing quarterstaves? Remember to have the 3-4 mandatory skills every quarterstave build has socketed.

Now I get that in some distant future there hopefully will be a higher selection of skills and it might alleviate some of my gripes. But due GGG designing skills in a current fashion, where they must have some sort of interaction with other skill and be weapon/archetype locked it will result in skills coming out in snails pace and still suffering the same problems. Which fundamentally is if you want to use X you gotta do Y, Z unless we fucked up the numbers on X so it doesn’t need the Y and there is maybe an unique that automates the Z for you.

srry for rant
