A guy who can hit people with a metal bat joins the Washington Wizards. Can they win an NBA title?

Rules:

  1. The guy with a bat is an average American man–fairly sedentary 5’10, 200 lbs

  2. He’s played varsity basketball in high school, so assume a base level of familiarity with basketball mechanics and rules

  3. Any hit during live play is treated as a common foul, no matter how hard it is

  4. Any hit during other segments of the game (jump balls, free throws, etc.) is a Flagrant 2 and an ejection, no matter how hard it is

  5. Normal rules regarding common fouls apply, so he only gets six standard hits and opponents are rewarded with free throws if they’re in the middle of a shooting motion or if his team is in the bonus

  6. Normal rules regarding flagrant fouls apply, so he gets 1 point for a Flagrant 1 (regular unnecessary contact) and 2 points for a Flagrant 2 (regular unnecessary & excessive contact or every bat hit out of turn); in the playoffs, 3 points trigger a one-game ban, and points carry over; in the regular season, accumulating more points leads to suspensions (e.g., 5 points, then every 2 points after)

  7. Referees just treat this as a totally normal part of the game. If opposing players or coaches complain too vehemently, they’re liable to receive techs. The NBPA and owners are also okay with this, for some reason

  8. The Wizards have to have him on the court for at least 25 minutes a game. They can’t just selectively deploy this guy as a goon

Can Washington raise a championship banner for the first time since 1978?
