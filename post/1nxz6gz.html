WEMBY STATS

2024 - 21.4 ppg, 10.6 rpg, 3.9 apg, 3.6 bpg, 3.7 tov. (71 games)

2025 - 24.3 ppg, 11.0 rpg, 3.7 apg, 3.8 bpg, 3.2 tov. (46 games)

—

Wemby is being hyped by ESPN and other media. They are projecting him to be the 5th best player in the league. Which means Superstar/MVP Candidate.

Last 2 seasons, He had monster numbers. And even had many Triple Double, and Almost Quadruple Double stat games.

His Blocking and Rim Protecting are arguably the best in the league right now. Unfortunately, he did not win a DPOY yet. He was 2nd in his rookie year. And Ineligible for the award in his sophomore year. (Due to not achieving no. of games requirements

This upcoming season, He is expected to win DPOY. (Barring Injuries)

But do you think his offense and limiting turnovers will improve by much?

Is this the year where Wemby will be considered a clear cut Top 10 player in the league?

And one step further, is this the time where he will be knocking on the door of Top 5?
