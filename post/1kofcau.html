In the 12 games where the Nuggets have faced post-season elimination Jamal Murray has averaged 27/5/5 on 60% TS. Jokic has averaged 26/12/7 on 60% TS.

I have no idea how Sunday’s game 7 will go, and as a Nuggets fan, I am worried about Aaron Gordon’s hamstring. But it is absolutely wild how well the Nuggets two foundational pieces have played in elimination games during their career.

Of note, few of those games have come on the road and the OKC defense is probably the best one the Nuggets have faced in the playoffs during this era.

Also of note, Jamal was horrid for the vast majority of last year’s post season after coming back (in less than 2 weeks) from a calf injury that should have kept him out 2 months. Yet, he still put up 35/3/3 in the game 7 against Minnesota… and it wasn’t enough because the Nuggets role players didn’t have it. And that was a healthy MPJ and in a home game (where role players tend to play better).
