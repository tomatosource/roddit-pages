Game 7 will decide the first NBA champion team that starts with a vowel.

This obviously means that there has not been a Finals matchup between 2 teams starting with a vowel - a situation we now have for the first time. The 5 (current) teams that start with a vowel are Atlanta, Orlando, Indiana, Utah and OKC.
The “closest” we got to a Finals matchup between 2 of these teams was during a 6 year stretch from 1995 to 2000 when Orlando (1995) and Indiana (2000) made the Finals from the East while Utah (1997, 1998) made the Finals from the West.
Teams that start with a vowel are 0-6 in the Finals so far. That will become 1-7 after Game 7 either way.
