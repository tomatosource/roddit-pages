The “pull-out” mechanic for Abyss has got to go.

For Abyss rares, you want to drag them out of the fog they appear in to increase the chances of them dropping high-value omens like Omen of Abyssal Echoes or Omen of Light - otherwise you’re much more likely to get low-value ones like Omen of the Liege. The community has aptly named it the “pull-out tech”

Being effectively punished for having a build that can kill everything as it spawns feels bad, it’s against the entire fantasy of an ARPG, and when you really get into it: it just doesn’t make sense. Would it not be better and more interesting for the fog areas to be loot multipliers, so that I have a HIGHER chance of getting high-value drops if I kill them in that space before they move out of it, instead of breaking up the gameplay to try to kite them into a different area? Shouldn’t more build power equal more loot, instead of the reverse?

Nobody likes pulling out.
