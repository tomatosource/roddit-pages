I often feel like WAR is lying to me. When have you looked at someone’s WAR and thought “that doesn’t seem right..”?

I personally think WAR is a fantastic statistic that is great at comparing players from different eras. And for good reason, it’s been the number one statistic for judging how much value a player has for years now.

However, at the end of the day, it really is just a mathematical formula. It doesn’t account for everything a player provides to his team.

This conundrum is probably best expressed in how Baseball Reference evaluates catchers. The Toronto Blue Jays were heavily favored by umpires this year thanks to catcher Alejandro Kirk’s framing ability. Paired with his great defense, he has a knack for getting on base and making contact. Seems like a decently valuable player, right?

He was only worth… 2.3 bWAR. That is SHOCKINGLY average.

There’s just no way Kirk is average. Kirk is awesome, man. He’s a beast.

What are other examples of WAR lying to us?
