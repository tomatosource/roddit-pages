It’s such a bummer that the PS5 version runs so poorly

Let me just say that this has been such a banger of a season. My only feedback would be to drop more crafting and runes during the campaign. Other than that, this has really been a great league. I’m probably one of the few to say this, but I absolutely loved the interlude acts, too. They were the perfect length, and I enjoyed the quick little side stories.

Now I’m in maps, and I absolutely love the map system, but the performance on PS5 is so bad it’s really hard to enjoy. I’ve done a lot of messing with the settings, and I can’t seem to get anything that gives descent performance without killing the visuals.

Another bigger issue is the sound. It is constantly glitching and going out, which completely ruins my flow.

The biggest issue is that even with dynamic culling turned on, it can be really hard to see the ground effects thar kill me. This leads to a lot of deaths that just feel cheap and undeserved.

As much as I love this game, I’m not sure how much farther I can progress because I can’t imagine my system being able to handle juiced maps. This is just such a huge bummer because I’m really enjoying the end game.

Edit. I’m happy to hear any settings tips that worked for people.

I’ll give one. I got the horrible sound buzzing glitch to go away. This was occurring pretty much every 20 minutes like clock work.It wouldn’t go away until I reset back to character select. What I did to get this to stop happening was I went into filter blade and turned off the custom drop sounds.
