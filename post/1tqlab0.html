[Bug] Negative “Presence Area of Effect” applies Ally Buffs to the Player

​The Issue

​I am reporting a critical, game-breaking bug that is currently causing a massive controversy within the Korean community.

There is an exploit regarding the Presence Area of Effect stat that needs immediate attention before the 0.5.0 launch, as it has the potential to completely ruin the upcoming season’s balance and economy.

​How the Bug Works

​When a player uses items like Fixation of Yix in combination with passive nodes to reduce their “Presence Area of Effect” to a negative value, the game’s logic breaks.

​Instead of removing the Presence area entirely, this negative value causes effects intended strictly for “Allies in your Presence” to be applied directly to the player character.

​The Impact

​Because of this exploit, items with strong ally-specific buffs (such as Scepters and similar auras/nodes) grant ridiculously overpowered, unintended stats to the player. If this makes it to the live servers, it will result in completely broken builds that trivialize the game’s content.

​I am reporting this because I, along with many others, want to have a fair and enjoyable experience in this final early access phase before the official 1.0 release. Please look into this before 0.5.0 drops!

​Reference Item: Fixation of Yix (Stellar Amulet)

​For context, here is the item heavily used to trigger this interaction due to its massive presence reduction:

​Requires: Level 24

​+(5—7) to all Attributes

​+100 to maximum Life

​Allies in your Presence have (30—50)% increased Critical Hit Chance

​Allies in your Presence have (30—50)% increased Critical Damage Bonus

​Allies in your Presence have (10—20)% increased Attack Speed

​Allies in your Presence have (10—20)% increased Cast Speed

​50% reduced Presence Area of Effect
