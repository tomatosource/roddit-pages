De’Aaron Fox is playing some good ball. Even if his stats don’t show it

His stats aren’t eye-popping because Spurs have so many good guards and they split usage pretty evenly, but when I watch him it seems like he has complete control of the game. Fox has the 2nd best +/- on the team during playoffs only behind Wemby. He is a nice offensive hub for the team and plays like a vet even though he’s still on the younger side. He could easily chuck up shots to get his 25+ points but he and the Spurs are playing very oriented team ball. Fox has one or two complete playoff stinkers with bad shooting splits, but for the most part I think he’s the 2nd most important player on the Spurs even though there is very deserving hype on Castle and Harper.
