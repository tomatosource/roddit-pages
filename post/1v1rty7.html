How do we feel about the current state of crafting?

Because, honestly, I feel like it is pretty shit and has creeped very far from GGG’s initial vision for crafting in PoE2.

We are right back to where they DIDN’T want to be with currency spamming - this time it’s just chaos instead of alts.

The initial iteration was also pretty terrible but was in the right direction. Omens are also annoying and unfun, imo. I’m curious what the communities take is here - some ideas / thought starters:

  • Chaos orbs completely reworked or removed
  • Bench or some kind of crafting interface that lets you consume X amount of currency to slam with altered weights (aka “20 ex to add a non-physical modifier” or “30 ex to add a speed modifier”). Rarer crafts that use higher tier currency (“swap res, add a prefix, add a suffix)
  • Split currency that allows you to split one base into 3

The idea is to weigh RNG more in your favor, give a currency that allows more “at bats” on a good base (split), and spend less time clicking. Yes, changing chaos orbs would remove how deterministic crafting can be but that is boring and leads to way too much time in your stash.

Itemization is also in a not great spot (+skills) but that’s another topic altogether.
