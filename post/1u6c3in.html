The San Antonio Spurs finish the 2026 season with the Triple Silver Crown

After finishing 2nd in the NBA Cup (Winning against the Lakers in Quarters and Thunder in Semis, and losing against the Knicks in the Cup Final).

And finishing 2nd in the Regular Season (62-20 behind the Thunder(64-18) and in front of Detroit(60-22)).

They also finished 2nd of the 2026 Playoffs (Winning against the Blazers, Timberwolves and Thunder, and losing in The Finals against the Knicks).

Thus completing 3 second places (Unofficial Triple Silver Crown).

-

All three times, they exceeded expectations:

- Not supposed to make it out of the Cup group stages (against Denver)

- Over/under of 44.5 (they won 62)

- Predicted to lose against the Thunder in WCF

They can be proud of how much they overachieved, even if being always one step away from gold is frustrating.
