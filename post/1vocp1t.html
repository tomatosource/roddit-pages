Looking Back at 24 run. Soak the moments in, you don’t know when they’ll ever be back

Was fortunate enough to attend a conference finals game during the 24 championship run. what a team and what a run.

Had just met my now wife, and it was a surprise date going to the game. not only the game, but those moments, are something ill never forget and carry with me forever.

Married now, with a child. So much can change in a little over 2 years, both personally and for the teams you root for. thankful I was able to take it all in and have those experiences.

I guess having weird reflections today. when those moments come around, don’t take them for granted. I’m dissapointed in where the Celtics are today, and how much has changed for the worse in 2 years since, with the JB Trade and new ownership, and JT injury.

The Celtics went 17 years between titles and decades prior to 2008. I guess my advice would be, if a run like that happens again in anyone’s lifetime, if you are a diehard fan, soak it in, take advantage of it, enjoy it. there is no guarantee they’ll ever be back.
