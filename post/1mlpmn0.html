Overlooked/Forgotten Peak: 2018 Victor Oladipo

Although he wasn’t quite on the same level as guys like Penny Hardaway, Grant Hill, Brandon Roy, and Derrick Rose, I think Victor Oladipo also deserves to be in the conversation when talking about talented young players that were unfortunately derailed by serious injuries. It seems like people have already forgotten that he was the best all-around guard in the league during his last fully healthy season. 23.1 PPG, First Team All-Defense, and led the league in steals. There wasn’t a single other perimeter player that year that was as impactful as he was on defense while also averaging at least 20 points a game. Plus, he was just as good, if not better in the playoffs. That was one of LeBron’s best years of his career and Dipo almost single handedly pushed his Cavs team to a seventh game. In that series he was Indiana’s leader in points, rebounds, assists, and steals. It would have been a lot of fun to see how his career would have progressed if he managed to stay fully healthy. His natural athleticism was off the charts.
