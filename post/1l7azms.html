PSA: Aside from Temporal Chains you should probably vendor Elemental Weakness too.

It got the same rework as Temporal Chains and instead of lowering your res by 15% it’s now 30% which is even higher than the old elemental pen which GGG described as too strong.
Aspects of this update are really poorly thought out.

Almost died just now got hit by the curse while leveling from 99 to 100. From now on I will be vendoring every Elemental Weakness

EDIT: I want to emphasize you should do this if your main goal is not dying when you are trying to push for lvl 95+. As most death in this game is due to damage spike. It’s either damage spike being lower than your total HP and you survive or being higher than your total HP and you die. It may be easy to dodge it with enough familiarization but you will get hit by it eventually, especially in a pinch when you are spamming doge and in a panic.
