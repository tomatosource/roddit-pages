In that case, a neutral arbitrator would have to be jointly appointed by the NBA and NBPA. After a hearing, the arbitrator’s ruling would be subject to an appeals panel made up of three people – also jointly appointed by the NBA and NBPA. Their decision would be binding, and would determine whether Silver can actually dole out punishment.

The players’ union is believed to be ready to take the matter to arbitration if the league seeks to penalize Leonard without sufficient evidence proving wrongdoing.

As a reminder, punishments can include team fines up to $7.5 million, draft-pick forfeiture, voiding of the player’s contract, and suspensions of up to one year for any team personnel involved.

Bulletproof evidence or full agreement on the findings and proposed settlement are the two paths to a faster conclusion.
