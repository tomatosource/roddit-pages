How will Russell Westbrook be remembered?

There’s a good chance he does not sign with anyone this year, and that his NBA career is over. We’ve seen this before, but what we haven’t seen is such a bright spotlight on a guy’s dwindling twilight years. Bouncing from team to team didn’t help and he might have led the league for some time in most memorable turnovers (Nuggets vs Wolves OT will live in my memory forever, and I’m not a fan of either team). Not to mention his Laker years.

It’s an interesting pattern in the newer social media era. How you finish your career has become more important and your memes can outlive anything else you’ve ever done.

The thing is, we were calling it years ago. He was heading toward the Allen Iverson exit if he didn’t evolve his game. He must’ve heard it. Did he choose not to do it? Was he incapable? He’s made hundreds of millions so maybe he didn’t care, but for someone who plays so hard I would’ve thought he’d cared.

He’s the most interesting player in my lifetime, the pendulum swings so far either side with his game.

Which side of the pendulum will you remember most?
