The highest vs lowest it took to lead the league in each of the 5 major statistical categories

Basically, the highest PPG, APG, RPG, SPG, and BPG vs the lowest it took to lead the NBA in each of those categories.

Side note, I’m gonna be counting it from the 60s on, as in the late 40s and 50s they were still figuring out scoring and assisting (blocks and steals weren’t counted yet).

Points per game:

Highest - Wilt Chamberlain 1962, 50.4 PPG

Second in scoring that year was Walt Bellamy at 31.6 PPG

Lowest - Allen Iverson 1999, 26.8 PPG

Second in scoring that year was Shaq at 26.3 PPG

Assists per game:

Highest - John Stockton 1990, 14.5 APG

Second in assists that year was Magic at 11.5 APG

Lowest - Kevin Porter 1975, 8 APG

Second in assists that year was Dave Bing at 7.7 APG

Rebounds per game:

Highest - Wilt Chamberlain 1961, 27.2 RPG

Second in rebounds that year was Bill Russell at 23.9 RPG

Lowest - Domantas Sabonis 2023, 12.3 RPG

Second in rebounds that year was Nikola Jokic at 11.8 RPG

Blocks per game:

Highest - Mark Eaton 1985, 5.6 BPG

Second in blocks that year was Hakeem Olajuwon at 2.7 BPG

Lowest - Jaren Jackson Jr. 2022, 2.3 BPG

Second in blocks that year was Robert Williams at 2.2 BPG

Steals per game:

Highest - Alvin Robertson 1986, 3.7 SPG

Second in steals that year was Michael Ray Richardson at 2.7 SPG

Lowest - OG Anunoby 2023, 1.9 SPG

Second in steals that year was Jimmy Butler at 1.8 SPG

That’s every category. 2022 and 2023 are two quite recent years that account for 3 of the 5 categories for the lowest it took to lead the league in something.

Edit: Worded the title incorrectly, I wanted to compare the highest vs the lowest seasons a player led in each category, not the highest “it took”
