Why is “Jaylen’s gonna be pissed/now we HAVE to trade him” such a popular take? Professional basketball is (literally) a business first, sport second.

I love JB, but let’s be real here.

He was only offered in a deal for one of the top 5 players in the NBA. Somebody who, by every objective measurement, is a better player than he is.

He’s coming off a great season, and said at the end he’d love to be here for the rest of his career — implying that he understands that decision isn’t up to him.

He’s also dealt with being involved in trade rumors for most of his career. For one reason or another, Brad and Danny never gave him up for anything else to this point. As far as anybody knows, he was only offered by the Celtics in a trade for a top 5 player in the league.

He’s smart enough and been around long enough to know that everyone has a price. Tatum is the only player who’s as close to untouchable as it gets. For him to get into his feelings over this, at this stage of his career, would go against everything he’s shown to this point.

It’s Brad’s job to build the best possible roster to compete for a championship. If he feels that moving Jaylen Brown for Player X does that, then so be it. He shouldn’t have to “mend” anything with Jaylen and he doesn’t have to trade him now because of it either.
