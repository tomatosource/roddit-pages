Casual’s review of POE2

I play HC exclusively, and tonight I set my personal best with a lvl 86 Storm Wave Invoker. I beat Xesht, KotM, and finished the 4th ascendancy, all POE2 firsts. And my previous highest chars ever, POE1 or 2, were lvl 84

This game is so good. I don’t think much needs to be tweaked for POE2, but one thing that needs to be adjusted is boss difficulty.

In POE1, if you missed obvious cues or you stood on the wrong thing, you were dead. In POE2, there’s only one boss that penalizes mistakes and that’s Monkey.

I’m having lots of fun with POE2, but I also know I’m not very good at POE. I shouldn’t be getting this far tbh lol, and lots of things that should have killed me didn’t.

Maybe an unpopular opinion but bosses should be RIPpier
