How Many Players On Your Team Are Guaranteed Everyday Starters?

This is something I think about a lot whenever the Sox aren’t playing well and I see the lineup cards getting put out. I can’t follow every single team closely enough to know what the average is for the MLB, so asking here:

How many players in your team’s lineup own their position? As in, they are starting at that same position every single day except for rest days. That means never platooned, not bouncing around different OF or IF positions. Truly own their position (and give catchers some leeway here since they have lots of rest days).

For the 2026 Red Sox, it’s 4 (RF, CF, 3B, 1B).

And then DH, 2B, SS, LF, C are either platooned or shared in some way.
