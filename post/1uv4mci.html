Game Design - Why not a rarity tree to replace the general stat ?

Would be nice to have a tree for rarity to replace the stat.
For example we’d have the possibility of rolling a mod “+ # rarity power” on our items.
And then we’d use that “rarity power” to setup our rarity tree, specialising in some kind of drops or even all drops if we have enough rarity power (has to be mega pricy though).
Each node of the rarity tree requiring an amount of “rarity power” to be allocated.

Doing that avoid having too many different rarity mod line which would make crafting tedious, and the rarity tree would allow to quickly switch from a spec to another without having to change gear.
