Rant: season ticket prices and just another way the league is becoming inaccessible for regular people.

My tickets went up significantly last year, which I understood because we had a massive payroll and looked like contenders for the future (renewals are during the season the year before). Then they got rid of half the starters to cut payroll but we were still stuck with the higher ST prices, which is frustrating because there is no resale value this year. It’s been by far the hardest year to sell tickets. I figured at least we wouldn’t get another big increase this year because we already got hit hard the year before, but instead I got an 8% increase in a year where we gutted the team and our star player missed the season. That’s what pisses me off. We dropped all the way under the apron and I’m still being asked to pay more for an objectively less valuable team (literally less valuable in terms of salary value, regardless of how well they are playing). I just want it to be reasonable.

The league makes it near impossible to watch the games with all the different channels and packages you need, local blackouts, etc, and then your own team makes it impossible to afford to attend in-person unless you are also a PE-bro like the owners (not to mention the sky high prices for drinks, etc). Celtics love thinking we have the best fan noise/support at the game, but that will wither and die if most fans can’t afford to attend anymore.
