0.2.0h - Loot From 500 Twilight Reliquary Key

Earlier this week, when GGG announced they would introduce a new batch of submissions to the Twilight Reliquary Key, I started collecting Keys from the market. I did buy 1200+ keys and planning to open around 1000 (or until foiled HH). Yesterday, right after patch dropped I started opening them.

BUT, in short**,** loot was miserable and it took ages to open chests.

I don’t think all the people who bought the supporter pack submitted Widowhail or PoCG, but more than a quarter of the loot was either of them, and it was really frustrating to watch more and more of them drop. Aside from the loot, the layout is intentionally made long to prevent people from opening keys back to back. I’m adding a short section of my run here so you can understand how long it took to open a chest: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-C8NARMJAk&feature=youtu.be

Overall, that was the most boring gamble session that I had. I already gave up around 300 or so (took maybe 3-4 hours at that point), but decided to continue just for hitting even number, like 500.
