Should the NBA add an All-Defensive Third Team?

IMO, the defensive side of the NBA is kinda overlooked by the NBA community(fans, media and the NBA itself), specially by the casual fans and besides that, there’s a lot of good defensive players that never get praised enough, and will probably never be since there’s only 10 spots for the best defensive players. Disregarding the 65 game rule for a moment, we, just in the last season, had pretty good defensive seasons by Jarrett Allen, DWhite, SGA, Giannis, Scottie, Cason Wallace, Caruso, Kris Dunn, Bam, McDaniels, just name a few. Maybe adding an All-Defensive Third Team would validate the efford of some guys that in the future won’t be remembered as good/great defenders since we tend to look at accolades to estimate the value of a player. What do you think? Should an All-Defensive 3rd Team be implemented?
