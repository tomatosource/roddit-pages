Armour: One more time

Well, 5 major patches in and the preeminence of ES, somewhat diminished, is still there. No doubt, this has to do with the right side of the tree being more popular than the left side.

That said, the problem, stated simply, is that you can’t scale life high enough to defend against one-shot mechanics. Arguably, you can scale both armour and life enough at the end game to matter, but that’s a very extreme corner solution. Meanwhile, you can reasonably scale ES and EV high enough for most players without bankrupting yourself in the process.

The simplest design solution is then making sure that the normally attainable Armour/Life scaling reaches the same level of ES or ES/EV hybrid against commonly encountered one-shot mechanics. So, are we there yet in your view? If not, what’s holding GGG back from simply allowing better Life/Armour scaling?
