TIL: Half of the Delirium mechanic has been bugged for 11 months & straight up does not work

The Delirium mechanic (not the Distilled Emotions you anoint maps with) is still bugged 11 months after it was first reported that half of the mechanic straight up does not work additional reward types are not being generated.

EDIT for clarity: This is not talking about the regular reward of distilled emotions it’s talking about “additional reward types”

When doing the Delirium mechanic with the Delirium Mirror, it should generate and drop additional reward types alongside the standard reward of Distilled Emotions (it should also drop currency, jewelry, armor, weapons …), as “additional reward types” always referred to that even in Path of Exile 1. However, this functionality is currently not working. If you add “additional reward types” through the atlas tree or tablets the mechanic does not drop any other reward types besides the standard instilled emotions.

Furthermore, the Atlas passive points that are supposed to grant “additional reward types” for Delirium have been bugged for 11 months, you can pick them but they do nothing.

(as a commenter pointed out they don’t strictly do nothing, they give you an additional instilled emotion, but they do not generate additional reward types)

https://imgur.com/a/aFTfDd1

Similarly, the Delirium Tablet modifiers that state a “% chance for additional reward types” is also bugged, and has been for 11 months. You can roll them on your tablets, but these modifiers do nothing.

(as a commenter pointed out they don’t strictly do nothing, they give you an additional instilled emotion, but they do not generate additional reward types)

https://imgur.com/a/qngPJXA

The reason this issue has largely flown under the radar is that most players don’t even play or use the Delirium Mirror mechanic. Current endgame mapping strategies prioritize Instilled Emotions for juicing, and those maps never include a Delirium Mirror. As a result, very few players are actively engaging with this part of the Delirium mechanic/content and are unaware of the bugs and that half of the Delirium mechanic does not even function fully and hasn’t functioned since 0.1 and that the delirium extra rewards part of the atlas tree straight up does nothing. does not generate additional reward types.

TL;DR: The Delirium mechanic has been bugged for 11 months and currently still is - A quarter of the Delirium Atlas tree literally does nothing since the reward mechanics are completely bugged and non-functional. The rewards tied to the delirium mirror do not fully function, as they do not grant “additional reward types”, only an extra emotion. “Additional reward types” in delirium refers to reward types such as armor, weapons, currency, jewelry etc. which is currently not generated.
