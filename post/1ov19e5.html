TIL: Half of the Delirium mechanic has been bugged for 11 months & straight up does not work

The Delirium mechanic (not the Distilled Emotions you anoint maps with) is still bugged 11 months after it was first reported that half of the mechanic straight up does not work and a quarter of the delirium atlas tree actually does nothing (you can select the points, but they will have no effect in-game)

When doing the Delirium mechanic with the Delirium Mirror, it should generate and drop additional reward types alongside the standard Distilled Emotions (it should also drop currency, armor, weapons …) However, this functionality is currently broken. The mirror does not drop any other reward types nor does it drop additional emotions (besides the original 1 bar you see on the bottom left); in effect, it simply doesn’t work.

Furthermore, the Atlas passive points that are supposed to grant “additional reward types” for Delirium are also non-functional and have been bugged for 11 months, you can pick them but they do nothing.

https://imgur.com/a/aFTfDd1

Similarly, the Delirium Tablet modifiers that state a “% chance for additional reward types” is also bugged, and has been for 11 months. You can roll them on your tablets, but these modifiers do nothing.

https://imgur.com/a/qngPJXA

The reason this issue has largely flown under the radar is that most players don’t even play or use the Delirium Mirror mechanic. Current endgame mapping strategies prioritize Instilled Emotions for juicing, and those maps never include a Delirium Mirror. As a result, very few players are actively engaging with this part of the Delirium mechanic/content and are unaware of the bugs and that half of the Delirium mechanic does not even function and hasn’t functioned since 0.1 and that half the delirium atlas tree straight up does nothing.

TL;DR: The Delirium mechanic has been incredibly bugged for 11 months and currently still is - A quarter of the Delirium Atlas tree literally does nothing since the reward mechanics are completely bugged and non-functional. The rewards tied to the delirium mirror simply do not function. This severely undermines an already underwhelming mechanic (Referencing the mirror; not using distilled emotions on maps), making it even less appealing for players to engage with, that people already don’t play since the rewards are so much worse as half of the mechanic is completely non-functional due to the bug
