Idea: Fast Travel + Exalt Sink = two problems solved

With most everyone asking for a Grand Project-like ability to skip around to various maps, and also a clear need for more robust Exalt sinks, why not create some sort of fast travel system paid for with Exalts?

e.g. Have a mid-endgame questline to unlock a Caravan which you can hitch a ride on - something like 5-10 Exalts per map connection traveled, maxing out at three or four maps?

It would be a big QoL improvement, keep exalts more relevant, allow for additional fine-tuning as necessary by playing with the #s, and not be something game-breaking or easily exploitable.

Just my random morning coffee thought. Carry on.
