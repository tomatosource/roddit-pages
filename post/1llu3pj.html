[Media] The hotel owner did not recognize Jokić. Her husband: A sad, short story

NIKOLA JOKIĆ has returned to the Italian city of Ferrara, where he spent the summer four years ago. After a grueling season in the USA, the Serbian basketball player for the Denver Nuggets is using the summer to devote himself to his great love - horses. Already in 2021, trying to avoid attention, he visited equestrian clubs and racetracks, Italian media report.

Four years ago, he was seen in the center of Ferrara, and this time, a photo of him posing with the owner of a city hotel became a hit. Elda Filia hosted one of the best basketball players in the world, but she didn’t recognize him. Her husband admitted this on social media.

“A short, sad story. My wife runs a hotel in Ferrari. She sent me this photo this morning. She doesn’t know anything about basketball. She wrote to me: ‘A very tall man slept here last night, he came to look at some horses. Now he’s gone. I asked him for a photo to show you, do you know who he is?’ She asked me if I knew who the best basketball player in the world was. Never, never, never luck.”
