Incendiary shot feels really bad

I’m working on an ignite build. So, as far as my game understanding goes, a faster ignite deals the same damage in a shorter amount of time, therefore if you have enough attack speed, and the duration is short, you can apply a very strong ignite, many times.

That would be absurd, base duration of ignite is 4 so It should… What ? 16 seconds ? So it also has a conflicting “ignites you inflict with this skill deal damage 200% faster”, which brings us to a “simple” +100%, therefore 8 seconds. I had to use a few items to decrease it again to the average 4 seconds…. But why ? Why is it like this ?
