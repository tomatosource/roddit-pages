Some ideas to make Towers fun

Towers have steadily improved though patches, but they’re still not in a great place. I think it would be neat if the player could choose from several options when clearing a tower. The goal is for towers to provide a bunch of different potential utility, but allow the players to choose.

Some examples I’ve seen recommended:

  • Make all nodes in range traversable - Just Grand Project
  • Make the nearest undiscovered tower node visible and traversable - the player can travel further than Grand Project, but with some RNG. This might need some way to prevent players from chaining several of them back to back to traverse the Atlas too quickly.
  • Point toward the nearest citadel / corrupted nexus / unique zone / other interesting thing - the player would choose which thing they want to point toward, it would use a graphic similar to the compass in Khari Crossing but scaled down and permanent on the atlas, centered on the tower node itself.

Assuming towers are still in 0.5 at all, what other ideas have you seen that would make them more useful and fun?
