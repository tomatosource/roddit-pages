This game has gigantic multiplayer problem…

I own an optimized 77 rooms Temple. I’m good currency wise, so I decided I was going to take a few leechers.
Why? To share the goodies, allow them to gain a lot of XP, pick the items that drop in the Temple that are usually good, and have a cool multiplayer experience but…

I told the party that I was going to pick divs and up and pay 20 divs to each one of them. If mirror 400D and hinekora 100D. Everyone typed that they were cool with it, so we go in.
As soon as divines started dropping, I see this dude jumping into them very fast. He took like 3 very quickly. I paused for a sec and remided him what the deal was and telling him that if he takes the divs I wasn’t gonna be able to share with the group. He didn’t type, he started spinning around so I thought it was an “ok” answer.
We go into the next room and he starts doing the same picking every divine. I decided it was time to kick him… And here I found a gigantic problem with this game: you CAN NOT kick players from Temple, which seems to also apply for maps or any other activity.

I tried relog, joined again, and he was still there.
I tried ignore - He was still there.
Left the Temple and went inside again - He was still there.

So I thought “okay, he is not in the party anymore, maybe I should just keep going and since we are not in the same party he shouldn’t be able to pick anything…” Wrong! I entered the next room, he follows me because he knows what he’s doing, and despite not being in my party and being an ignored player: HE CAN STILL LOOT MY DROPS!!
I had to just go a few rooms until dropped the only thing he couldn’t steal, the lock medallions, leaving the Temple and close it.

Why is there an option to “kick from party” when it doesn’t kick from party? Maybe I am missing the meaning of party in terms of PoE, I don’t know.
Are we supposed to deal with players that just want to ruin the experience for the others?

GGG is so busy nerfing builds that they don’t realize how many problems the game has beyond people having fun, which seems to be the main problem for them. I see every patch “fixing” the fun, but I have read that this is a problem since day 1.
My bad for inviting unknown players, but come on…
