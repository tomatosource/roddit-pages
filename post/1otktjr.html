Potential KBO players to make the jump to MLB

Hi all! I made a post on the OOTP subreddit about international stars potentially coming to MLB, but I realized after that I hadnt heard a lot about the KBO stars, mostly just the NPB guys. I think this is because in recent years KBO competition has improved vastly, but in America, its remembered for its “busts”.

Im curious what baseball fans think of these guys, as ive only heard analysis! Ill include a KBO-&gt;MLB immediate comps (first season) for those who don’t follow Korean Baseball, to give some prespective. Some comps are not the best, as they happened almost a decade ago, but ill try my best. (For hitters I will only be including hitting stats, as idk how to find fielding in KBO)

-–
