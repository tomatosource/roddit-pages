The Knicks are the first champion of the modern era to have all their top 5 minute getters grade out as 80th percentile or above in playoff impact, per advanced metric EPM

EPM: https://dunksandthrees.com/epm/actual?season=2026 is considered the best one number publicly available metric, combining tracking, box score, and on-off data.

It dates back to 2002, and according to it the Knicks are the first team of the modern era to win a championship and have all their top 5 minute getters grade out 80th percentile or better in playoff impact.

Here were each of the Knicks’ players playoff ranks in per possession playoff impact:

KAT: +8 — 99th percentile
OG: +3.7 — 97th percentile
Brunson: +3.2 — 96th percentile
Mikal: +2.6 — 92nd percentile
Hart: +1.9 — 87th percentile

Ridiculously well balanced, every one of those can feel good about themselves that the Knicks would not have won without them.

Additionally, they are the first finals team in the entire database to have this distribution of impact.
