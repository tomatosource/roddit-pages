Has any team had a season end more tragically than the Pacers last year?

I’m a Brazilian fan and I’ve only been following NBA since the first finals between the Cavs and GSW, so some 10 years, but I can’t remember a playoff run end so genuinely tragically as the Pacers last year. All the vibes and charisma they built during the playoffs, the exciting basketball they’ve played, with comebacks, winners, the “redemption arc” involving Reggie Miller etc. Then going to the finals and taking it to game 7 against one of the most dominant teams in recent history, only to have Haliburton suffer a devastating injury that not only ended their season possibly steps away from one of the most epic championships ever, but also ended their winning window for years to come, and now they have to go into full rebuild mode.

From older fans and NBA “historians”, which teams also had tragic endings to their seasons that left fans with this painful “what if?” feeling? Since I’ve been a fan, I can think of the Rockets team that lost to GSW with Chris Paul getting injured, but still it doesn’t strike me as even close (maybe so to Rockets fans).

Ps. I really ask this out of curiosity for the history of the game. Much respect for the players and the fans, and I don’t mean at all to spark any antics or trolling. Cheers!
