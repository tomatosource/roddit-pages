I am actually amazed how much I enjoyed Act 4

In PoE 1 I’m usually speeding through the acts. I’d be deep in maps by Saturday night (damm EU launch times), but this time around due to having less time and trying to go off-meta (Tactician Minions are not a good league starter) I just finished act 4. And the whole time I was doing it I was thinking “I don’t want this to end”.

What a weird feeling. In PoE 1 and even in acts 2 and 3 from the start I liked the aesthetics but I couldn’t wait to get through them. But for act 4 I just got sucked in by amazing quality of evrrything. I talked to all the NPCs, listened to the banter, admired the bosses. It has so many epic moments that I sometimes watched videos of bosses I killed too fast to see them in full detail. I actually stopped attacking bosses to see them use their full set of abilities because my build was just too strong (Explosive Shot Witchhunter).

I don’t know how ot will hold up on multiple playthroughs but so far out of all the acts this felt the least like a slog. I’m hoping being able to do it in any order will make ot retain its charm longer.
