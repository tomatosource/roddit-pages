Patch 0.5.3 is likely dropping tomorrow.

Fun fact: Kakao Games (the Korean publisher for PoE 1&2) always posts server maintenance notices a day in advance.

They just announced a downtime scheduled for:

4:00 PM - 5:00 PM PDT (June 18) / 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM KST (June 19)

While they don’t explicitly call it a “patch,” this routine almost always means an update is coming. 

(I do remember at least one time the maintenance got canceled because GGG delayed a patch, but that’s very rare).

Looks like 0.5.3 is tomorrow!
