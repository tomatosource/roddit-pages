Thanks for the PS5 Bugfixes!

I don’t know since when, but PS5 experience became a lot, like: really a lot, better.

The most annoying bug, this terrible breakdown of the sound system, seems to be gone now. One had to restart the game when this thing occured. The general performance got a lot better. Only extreme situations lead to a state ong, visible downgrade of the rendered graphics. But then, although maybe a tid sluggish, the game keeps responsible and acts accordingly to controller inputs.

And finally, at least my game did not shut down since a week or two.

So, what can I say? POE2 on PS5 is a testament to game programming. It’s just a console at the cost of a third of a PC high-end gaming card. And it runs smoother than many offline games even with a lot of action on screen. AAA and: I really, really like the art style.

What can I say? Thank you, keep going!
