0.4 Needs to Massively Expand the Atlas Tree

With the promised endgame rework in 0.4 coming up soon the current Atlas tree needs to be seriously addressed.

The current tree feels small, relatively boring, and uninspired, with only one or two optimal setups that everyone gravitates toward. The absence of many full league mechanics isn’t a valid excuse for the Atlas tree being this lackluster, we already have content in the game that could be made more interesting by an expanded atlas tree.

There are ways to create interesting and unique atlas passives that interact with the map content we already have: Strongboxes, Wisps, Rogue Exiles … League mechanics could be folded into the main tree or kept in separate mini-trees like they are currently but would need to be expanded for more options.

When you compare the PoE 2 Atlas to the PoE 1 Atlas (Comparison), it’s honestly depressing how barebones it is and it’s not just because PoE 2 has less league mechanics; even with the content we do have, there’s tons of design space being left on the table, even if some of it would just be copy/pasted from designs that were huge hits in PoE 1 already.

PoE1’s Atlas and scarab designs offers a great example of what’s possible. There are so many cool atlas passives and scarabs, a scant few which even exist in PoE 2, that create real customization when combined with things like scarabs. We don’t have scarabs in PoE 2, but some of that functionality could easily be moved onto the Atlas tree, especially since many of these effects aren’t available on tablets anyway, even if you wanted them.

Let’s take strongboxes as an example, imagine how much more fun they could be if we had more options like:

  • All map monsters are trapped inside strongboxes, creating huge burst packs when opened (containment in PoE 1)

  • A chance to double currency items that a strongbox drops (Imagine double Tinks! A small chance for double divine orbs! …)

  • Other modifiers, like rare-corrupted boxes, more strongbox effect or functionality changes that aren’t even in PoE 1: Perhaps a chance for a strongbox rare to be influenced by a wisp, or higher chances for a Strongbox to spawn a Rogue Exile!

These are some of the biggest highs in PoE 1 (double mirror from a strongbox) and there’s no reason such fun design space or others couldn’t exist in PoE 2 as well.

(Existing strongbox features from PoE 1)

And this isn’t just about strongboxes, you could expand atlas passives for wisps (as a crude example see my very basic attempt here), rogue exiles, and other core features.

Please GGG expand the Atlas tree significantly and make it more engaging than simply pathing to a handful of explicit effect nodes. Right now, only one or two atlas routes really do anything and the system has so much more potential than that.
