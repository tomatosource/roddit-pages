[Chicago Bulls] Chicago Bulls guard Ayo Dosunmu will undergo surgery and be out for the remainder of the season.

“After experiencing left shoulder instability and further evaluation from training and medical professionals, Chicago Bulls guard Ayo Dosunmu will undergo surgery and be out for the remainder of the season. Updates will be provided as appropriate.”

Ayo missed 3 games with left shoulder instability and then played 23 minutes in the OT game against the Raptors on Friday, will now be out for the remainder of the season
