Rasheed Wallace’s 41 technical fouls alone were the difference between Portland finishing 4th and 6th in average margin of victory in the 2000-2001 season

Rasheed Wallace famously got 41 technical fouls in the 2000-2001 season, a record that will stand forever given the current suspension rules.

Assuming opposing teams shot 81% on the technical free throws (which is roughly the league average on such shots), he cost his team about 33 points on technical fouls alone. That’s about 0.4 additional opponent points per game, due solely to Sheed’s raging temper. On a WAR basis, this is the equivalent of roughly 1 win.

If Sheed had kept himself in check and not gotten any techs, that alone would have been enough for Portland to leapfrog the Dallas Mavericks and Philadelphia 76ers to go from 6th up to 4th on average margin of victory. Portland finished the year with an average of margin of 4.20 points, while Dallas and Philly both ended at 4.28.
