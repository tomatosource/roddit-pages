Idea that would solve the current problems of many ‘useless’ Unique items

I thought of an idea that would probably solve the problems of current low powered uniques and make like 90% of them actually important.

Solution: Make a new currency orb that is fairly common, that can be used to ‘sacrifice’ a unique item. It will destroy the unique and preserve its ‘spirit’ in the orb. This orb can then be used on any Rare item base of the same type, Corrupting it and replacing a random modifier on the item with a random modifier from said unique item.

Orb needs to be fairly common as the use case is highly randomised, but it would make a LOT of trash tier uniques suddenly very very useful.
