EDIT: as you can see in the replies you can CTRL + left click to directly open a portal to these areas. My mouse cursor actually hides half of the tiny explanation when hovering over the button (my bad). I think the rest of the post still stands though. Feels inconsistent, but at least the ctrl left click helps.

It feels inconsistent that locations like Caer Tarth, Monastery, Kingsmarch, and other “special” zones require panning the atlas and opening a map portal just to visit them.

The current system feels clunky because the atlas can be laggy (especially when you are exploring deep into it), you lose your place unless you remember to bookmark it (ive done this up a few times), and you have to physically open portals to travel to the node (can’t travel to spend hiveblood if in the middle of a map for example).

The well of souls has a direct travel option from the waypoint which is consistent with PoE 1 and also provides a way, way better experience. If others agree with this maybe we can get GGG to see the feedback and consider a change. I feel like they would agree with this
