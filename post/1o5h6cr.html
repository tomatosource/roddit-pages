MLB batters this year when swinging on the first 2 pitches: .331/.328/.554. MLB batters after that: .211/.303/.343

There’s been a lot of talk about quick innings and how the Jays should’ve taken more pitches (I also saw this after the Yankees losses, and plenty of other times).

Hitters do best when they swing at good pitches to hit, and the best pitches to hit often come early in the count. You would be doing the pitcher a favor by taking pitches.

It’s one of those items that only comes up when a team does poorly. Did anyone mind Springer swinging on the very first pitch of the game?

Of course, if it’s a ball you shouldn’t swing at it. But the Mariners were pounding the strike zone. They threw 11 first-pitch pitches out of the zone and Jays hitters only swung at 3 of them (and one was the Santander single).

The Jays got stifled by good pitching, good defense, and batted ball luck, and likely would’ve had the same level of failure if they decided to willingly fall behind in the count to “work the pitcher.”
