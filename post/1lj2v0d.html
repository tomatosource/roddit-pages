P.J. Washington will be traded soon… but to where?

The Mavs starting front court is set with Derek Lively, Anthony Davis, and Cooper Flagg. P.J. Washington is the odd man out, but he’s too good of a player to be coming off the bench.

The Mavs have too many backup forwards already (Naji Marshall, Klay Thompson, Caleb Martin). And they’ve got huge holes in the back court. Max Christie is the only serviceable guard on the roster. It makes a lot of sense to trade P.J. Washington for a starting caliber guard.

It will likely happen sometime between now and the start of free agency.
