A Handful of Questions on Our Cap Moving Forward

We currently have ~85% of our cap tied up in 3 players.

  1. Jayson Tatum
  2. Jaylen Brown
  3. Derrick White

I am not trying to be a reactionary or speak to any amount of his on court ability – JB is a finals MVP, multiple time all star, and can make an All-NBA third team every now and again (not to mention one of my favorite players). At a certain point math becomes math though and it seems, to my simple brain, to be untenable to build a championship roster with just 15% of your cap to play with in addition to the above guys. The only thing we have going our way is the ridiculously cheap deal we have Pritchard on currently.

So I have a few questions for folks who say “ABSOLUTELY NOT!!” to trading Brown.

  1. What are the player archetypes we can surround these guys with to contend in 2026 or 2027? Are we expecting insanely high production from our second round picks this year, Luka Garza, player to be named later that gets paid ~5% of the Cap (for reference this is about what Pritchard makes which is a STEAL for a player of his caliber)?
  2. Is there a time where we would get a better return for Jaylen? Next offseason or trade deadline maybe? Would you have him ride out his entire contact w/ the Celtics?
  3. Can we reasonably take on projects/future trade chips given our current salary cap situation? So far we have been able to land middling known quantities on expensive contracts (Simons + Niang). If we take the FO at their word, then we have to effectively trade these guys for nothing to accomplish their stated goal of getting under the tax.

All of this is to say, I think if Brad gets a godfather offer for Brown in the aftermath of FA (after teams inevitably miss on their FA targets) – I think its worth entertaining for the possibility of cap relief ALONE.
