Hand of Chayula is extremely good right now.

Hand got a nerf in that the curse magnitude is reduced but lasts longer, but more importantly it got a buff in increased mark effects.

Now that palm abilities use Quarterstaff damage, they can scale to the point of easily one shotting enemies.

This is awesome for HoC because you can socket it with voltaic or freezing mark and get a built in nova. Both of these marks can also proc their respective heralds for further damage around the enemy.

Shattering Palm into HoC can be a huge AoE clear. Run AoE and rapid attacks in both.

For shattering palm, + cold skills and some “more” damage to fill it out works great. Long shot is fantastic as anything outside the radius of the mark detonation will take extra damage being further from you.

For HoC, a curse + hexbloom to amplify whichever damage type you want (hexbloom will cascade just like the wave in this case) and the lightning or freezing mark.
