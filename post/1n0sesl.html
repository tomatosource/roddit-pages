[Feliks] Raznatovic: “They are planning, a certain group of investors, classic business, to create a new league that would somehow also compete with the NBA… I enjoyed it, and I never expected to find myself on a boat with LeBron James”

About a month ago, Serbian agent Misko Raznatovic, the most influential basketball agent in Europe by far, posted an intriguing photo with LeBron James on a yacht in Saint-Tropez. Almost immediately, fans and media lost their minds, with many initially concluding that plans were being made to pair Nikola Jokic with LeBron soon.

However, the agent himself, who also represents big NBA names like Ivica Zubac, Goga Bitadze, Nikola Jovic, and Nikola Topic, among others, revealed on the 6.75 Range podcast that it was an informal meeting at which Bron actually had plans to create a league in Europe to compete with the NBA.

“They are planning, a certain group of investors, classic business, to create a new league that would somehow also compete with the NBA,” Raznatovic said. “They don’t want it to be a U.S. league; they want it to have a European flavor, so to speak. And when they started working on it, everyone told them to call me.”

“I spoke a few times with Maverick Carter, where I got an official role as a consultant. He said it would be best to meet for lunch on a boat where LeBron would also attend. We had that meeting where we talked a lot about the new league, how it would be built and what would be done,” he added.

The league promotion ended up being much bigger

Raznatovic explained later how a simple yacht photo tweet sparked theories that the two might team up. Also, the league promotion ended up being much bigger than he had expected, as their conversation the next day left a strong impression on him.

“We talked for a long time the next day and he really surprised me. Very approachable and cool. Lots of questions about European basketball, how they travel, how many games are played, just a nice basketball talk without any arrogance. I enjoyed it, and I never expected to find myself on a boat with LeBron James,” he concluded.

Although Adam Silver plans to create NBA Europe in the future, a European league with two conferences that would directly compete with the EuroLeague.

The oldest and by many the best competition after the NBA, won multiple times by greats like Toni Kukoc and Drazen Petrovic, among others, and mostly played in major European cities, Raznatovic’s statement becomes particularly intriguing.
