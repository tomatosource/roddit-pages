The Cleveland Cavaliers have 5(!) players with a PER over 20. This has NEVER been done by any Championship team in NBA History

I posted this earlier in the year and people said that it was too early to call these stats real. Well we’re more than halfway now and the point still stands. The Cavs have 5 players with a PER over 20, which is stunning, since often a players PER production can come at the expense of other players production on the floor.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/CLE/2025.html

Evan Mobley, Jarrett Allen, Donovan Mitchell, Ty Jerome and Darius Garland all have over 20 PER.

I can’t say for sure no team has ever had more than 4 players with a PER of 20 because I didn’t look at every team in history, but I’m fairly certain about it. Something needs to be said that this really marks the new era of play, because something about pace and emphasis on 3 pointers. The cavaliers are 2nd in the league in 3 pointers made and first in points made, but this is possibly the most balanced team ever assembled.

EDIT: I know PER is not a great stat! There is a reason that people still keep track of it. Why haven’t they just gotten rid of it if its so bad.
