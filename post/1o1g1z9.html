Victor Wembanyama has a genuinely solid chance to be the youngest MVP in history this year

Putting it out there that I’m not a Spurs fan, and I’m not usually a “Donovan Mitchell could really win the MVP this year, just needs a few things to break his way” type of guy.

For those that remember, before information about his blood clot came out, Wembanyama was firmly in consideration for the fifth All-NBA first team spot despite his team sitting outside of the play-in.

Now the Spurs will have a healthy Fox (at least initially) alongside him, they drafted a highly touted prospect second overall that would have gone first most years, they now have a backup center.

Fox, Castle, Vassell, Barnes, Wembanyama along with Harper, Johnson, Sochan and Kortnet off the bench is significantly better than Wembanyama has had to work with in the past.

Last year, he averaged 24-11-4-4 blocks. He’s already an efficient scorer. He’s already considered the best defensive player in the game, and from a rim protection standpoint he’s on a different plane than everyone else in the league. He does things that are completely separate from the capabilities of any other MVP candidate already, and the last time he was on the court he had just turned 21.

He should have easier baskets with a much better team, but I actually don’t think he has to make that huge of a leap. If the Spurs are a top five seed, which I think is much more possible than most would predict, he should be right in the mix.

I’ve seen him put as a deep dark horse behind the usual four candidates and even players like ANT, Brunson or KD but I just don’t see it, because at this stage there’s much less that separates them in their strengths, and Wemby is already a top tier talent offensively.
