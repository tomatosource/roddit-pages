Nobody is talking about the Timberwolves

I feel like they’ve gone under the radar..? Everyone is talking about Detroit, OKC, San Antonio, but I think the Timberwolves can absolutely beat any team and win the championship.

They’ve got a solid lineup of DiVincenzo-Edwards-McDaniels-Randle-Gobert, plus Reid, Dosunmu, Hyland, and now Anderson. Edwards has taken a leap this year, and if Randle plays to his peak level I think this team has what it takes. Excellent defense, three point shooting, athleticism. Their bench is better than last year as well. And previous playoff exits gave them experience.

Don’t sleep on them, you don’t make back-to-back conference finals by accident. We slept on Indiana and they almost beat OKC.
