Do we have too much power as players?

This isn’t a post ment to be biased towards one direction or another. Something that crossed my mind a while ago.

Is it too easy to become powerful enough to roll over the game? Both builds i played this league (selfcast arc gemling/sunder martial artist hollow form) felt like they just went over the entire game without much of a hassle. Sure i died here and there, but my damage felt so high that it was trivial to do all the bosses. Yet at the same time they were incredibly fun to play, partially because i was so strong.

So here is the question i suppose. Are we too powerful? Or is the game fun because we are so powerful? Is it too easy to aquire gear? Or is the game more enjoyable because of it.

It’s easy to look in either direction if i’m honest. Friction makes for a more frustrating game where you might call quits early. But at the same time you might stop playing earlier then you would if you had to grind more for gear.

So where do we as a community think the balance of player power is in it’s current state, are we too strong, should we be even stronger or are we in a good spot?
