The sky isn’t falling

I think it’s important for everyone to calm down and not panic and try to theorize what’s coming. We don’t actually know what Brad offered. We don’t know if Jaylen was informed of this or what the team communicated to him. We don’t know if Jaylen wants out. It’s very clear from Jaylen’s time here that he loves the city. He’s been here for 10 years. This team gave him two huge contracts (including the largest of all time when it was signed) when everyone doubted him. I think he knows how highly regarded he is here.

There is no shame in only being offered for a top 25 player of all time. Shams and Fischer both said they believed Brown would only be traded for Giannis. So for now we move forward with the Jays until something happens. It’s not a bad place to be to have both Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown on your roster.
