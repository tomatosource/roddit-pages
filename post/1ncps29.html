Adam Silver is an employee of the owners, not the other way around.

When people do the whole “Stern was harsh, Silver is lenient, upvotes to the left” bit and start already blaming the league before the investigation has really started, they are missing that the commissioner’s opinion is just a small piece of the puzzle.

Adam Silver, like most people, likely wants to keep his job. If there are 29 owners calling for blood, he is going to try and deliver for them.

If the penalty is ultimately deemed too lenient, it means one of 2 things:

A) The outcry from the other owners is not as extreme as has been reported, or at least more of a mixed bag

B) The league (meaning the owners AND the commissioner) are trying to push through a ‘lesser’ penalty that they think the Clippers will accept without appeal, as bringing in an arbitrator would make things even messier

With that said, the fact that even if there is no direct evidence the Clippers are still likely to get penalized in some way says a lot about how upset other owners are. Normally a smoking gun would be needed for this type of punishment so I don’t think losing draft picks without a smoking gun should be seen as lenient, personally (I do think the Clips are likely guilty though, for the record)

The difference in temperament between Stern and Silver is very unlikely to be a factor here. Stern had a smoking gun for the Twolves situation so it’s easy to bring the hammer down. There is no situation where 29 owners are calling for heads to roll and Silver says ‘nah let’s go easy on them’
