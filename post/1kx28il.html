NBA L2M Report - Clock not reviewable on SGA pass out of bounds because time didn’t expire

https://official.nba.com/l2m/L2MReport.html?gameId=0042400314

People were questioning how the clock ended up at 0.3 rather than 0.1 (original stop time) or 0.6 (time the fan caught it). It turns out that 0.3 was an estimation by the refs, and that the play wasn’t reviewable at all.
