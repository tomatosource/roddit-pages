Tower overlaps are overrated & other misconceptions about mapping.

I’ve been playing a lot of SSF, and tried a lot of different stuff with juicing and what not. I feel like a lot of community sentiment just doesn’t match what I’ve experienced ingame regarding mapping. I think this is because most content creators moved on before the buff patches and theres been a lot less discussion about the post buff world. Let me know what your thoughts are, im curious if others who are juicing have similar experiences. Also at the end I wrote exactly how to roll good maps, since I think a lot of players aren’t abusing regex based on the current price of exalts.

  • Tower overlaps are overrated. People typically aim for 3 tower overlaps, but this isn’t that much better than doing 1 or 2 towers + a corrupted/cleansed area. A 3 tower overlap + the passive tree adds 83% quant and a 20% chance to double it, so about 100% on average. Thats only double the loot, which is obviously worth doing when you find them but isn’t make or break for if a map is worth running. In comparison, a cleansed area is similar to having 2 extra prefixes on a waystone, and a corrupted area is like 50-70% more mobs as long as it’s a somewhat juicy map. Those are definitely not as strong as 100% quant, but when you include how much more common it is to find a corrupted/cleansed area with a tower nearby I would say they’re roughly equal. The main downside of corrupted/cleansed areas is it’s harder to get every league mechanic you like onto the maps & some of the random mods suck for drops.

  • Suffixes on waystones significantly affect map juice. When you’re preparing maps to run, you should prepare a whole ton of maps, but only finish up the ones that have good prefixes. This means the more maps you own, the more picky you can be about only finishing super well rolled maps. this makes it so the amount of waystones you find significantly affects the quality of your future maps indirectly. Also you should be corrupting your maps, and without all the suffixes filled out you won’t be able to get 6 prefix maps which are the juiciest maps you can run (or sell). Especially with how cheap exalts are on trade you should be 6 modding everything and only running the super juicers (sorted via regex)

  • You should be juicing your pathing maps. I get the impression that people hate pathing due to not getting drops, but you should really just think of pathing as a place to use your juicing maps that didn’t land a good corruption. This doesn’t slow down your pathing due to the extra rares adding more headhunter buffs (aka movespeed) and many map layouts requiring you to run to the end for the last rare regardless of the total number of rares.

Here’s a step by step for how I prepare juicy maps relatively quickly (if you’re new to regex, its just putting the text including quotation marks into the search bar. You can use multiple quotes.)

  1. Augment, regal & alch all your maps (in trade you should probably also 6mod them all, since exalts are so cheap. In ssf it’s not sustainable though.)

  2. Use regex to pick out decent ones, afterwards you can vendor all the ones that weren’t good enough. In trade with pre 6modding you probably want to use something like this: "items" "!esse|shrine|gbox|chest|gold|tempo" That’ll only highlight waystones with no dead prefixes, without temp chains & they must have some rarity. Maybe you can exclude magic monster mods as well if you want even better maps or set a minimum rarity. Or you can loosen the requirements if you’re not getting enough good maps. In SSF I use this at the moment: "items|exper|r of r" "!magic mo"

  3. Instill them all with 45% more rare monsters. This is the best emotion, but if its too expensive doing packsize is ok too (idk the trade prices xd). Rarity is bad IMO, you should get that from the waystones affixes. This is the most annoying step by a lot.

  4. Corrupt them all. t16s can be sold or used. t14s I just vendor, or you can use them for pathing if you want. For the t15s you can sort them to find the 6 prefixes/suffix maps via regex. “!items” will highlight every all suffix map which you can vendor. “!Waystone drop chance” will highlight every 6 prefix map so you can set them aside for corrupted areas or tower overlaps. Do one last regex for brick mods like “temporal chains”, and vendor those.

  5. That’s a bunch of maps ready to go. I make sure to use my 6prefix & t16 maps in corruption/cleansed zones, and the rest either for pathing or in corruption/cleansed since you don’t get enough of the giga ones. You can also further sort the them if you want for only high rarity maps via "[8-9]\d.*ms|\d{3}.*ms" "quant" and set those ones aside with the 6 prefix maps.

So yea, hope this either helps others have more fun mapping, or helps newer players with how to juice well.
