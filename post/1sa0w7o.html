Grizzlies’ Brandon Clarke arrested in Arkansas on speeding, reckless driving, controlled substance allegations.

MEMPHIS, Tenn. (WMC) - Memphis Grizzlies big man Brandon Clarke was arrested Wednesday. Clarke, 29, was booked just after 1 p.m. in Cross County.

Clarke is charged with improper passing, possession of a controlled substance, fleeing/exceeding the speed limit and trafficking a controlled substance.

Clarke has been a member of the Grizzlies since he was drafted in 2019. He appeared in just two games this season due to injury, his seventh season in the NBA.
