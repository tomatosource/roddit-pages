350+ Hours in Last Epoch and 700+ in POE2…

I just had to get this out even though I know everyone and their mom has been posting their opinions on these games as of late.

I want to start by saying I have given Last Epoch three tries (1.0, 1.1, 1.2). Also, keep in mind 1.0 and 1.1 were before POE2 was playable, and I actually enjoyed the game quite a bit upon my first playthrough during 1.0 (especially the crafting system, which is obviously what most others enjoy about this game). Patch 1.1 was met with more frustration than fun based on glitches/clunkiness of builds I tried as well as a mind numbingly boring end-game.

Patch 1.2 of course is post POE2 launch, and getting back into LE after playing POE2 was quite eye-opening for me. I found myself actually missing POE2 campaign. Now is POE2 campaign perfect? hell no, but I actually don’t think it needs a complete overhaul either. Even something simple such as adding a fourth act and removing cruel difficulty to cut down on repetitiveness. In fact, my overall goal of this post is the hope that the campaign isn’t completely overhauled and turned into POE1 or LE campaign.

For me its quite apparent now that for moment-to-moment combat, impactful/engaging abilities, and challenging/interesting boss fights POE2 is absolutely the game for me. That doesn’t mean LE is terrible and shouldn’t exist, but only having a great crafting system and not much else going for it just isn’t enough for me.

I think its also important to remember that POE2 is still quite early in its, well, Early Access and we are currently the beta testers. I don’t mind that loot/endgame/crafting isn’t in a perfect place right now. I’m taking a break from the game for a bit and I know I’ll be back to check out 0.3!

Lastly, this isn’t a post meant to bash Last Epoch, but more so to appreciate what I like in POE2. I think getting over 300 hours in a $40 game is pretty great, but I don’t see myself playing it too much in the future just to be perfectly candid (as I’ve already called it quits on 1.2).
