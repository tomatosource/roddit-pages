Wake me up when they increase drop rates and make things fun again

I’ve been playing since the start, just like most of us. I got a nice 3.7second Temporalis in 0.1. I joined a guild, did TONS of runs on everything you could.

I don’t even play the game anymore. I’m sure ppl like me are showing up in the metrics, and I’m supposedly who the devs are designing the game for… I’m a 40y.o. cis white male that loves arpgs.

I wanted more content from 0.2, and expected the nerf bat to hit hard… but not this hard. The whole game is just too joyless. There are almost no drops, which destroys the majority of mechanics in the game.

They are so concerned about economic creep, they killed all the fun.

I know there are discord servers selling items for actual cash. Some of my guild mates take part in that and made decent money from it in 0.1.

It’s still going on in 0.2… but there is no way I’m going to slog through this experience with almost no reward, even if that means I’m missing out on the new content I wanted.

I wanted to enjoy the new content, but they designed the game to be far less rewarding.

I don’t get it.

And now, the nerf bat caused everyone to use a single skill, lightning javelin, instead of the many that were used before.

Everything is far too pigeon holed now, with no feeling of freedom.

They messed up their own game so badly.

We were having way more fun in 0.1 than now. It’s because of the drop rate, for sure.

If they increase the drop rates bigtime, not just a little bit, I’d love to come back and grind to endgame again. Let me know if that happens.

Otherwise, I’ll keep playing all the other games that are very enjoyable. These are good times for gamers, overall :)
