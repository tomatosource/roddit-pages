Everybody talking about only the FOG of Delirium but there is bigger issues with it

I do agree that FOG needs to go, but Delirium also needs huge changes. Currently it is the only mechanic in the game that everybody will do at the top endgame to get more juice. It’s not an opitional league mechanic like others are. It’s designed in a way that if u want more loot in your maps you add delirium onto your maps, does not matter what type of content u like/do.

And i think this is a big issue. These mechanics should be “optional” on what you wanna do or like but delirium sadly falls off on that.

You like ritual, essence, breach and want more loot at top endgame well add delirium!

You like strongboxes, breach and essence and want more loot at top endgame well add delirium!

Every road leads to god damn foggy Delirium. And even if fog goes away this issue will persists and i think it needs to get reworked.
