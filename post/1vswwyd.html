I think the most fun loot is the item that makes you temporarily abandon your original build plan

I usually start a character with at least a vague idea of what I’m trying to make.

Then something drops that clearly doesn’t belong in that plan, but is interesting enough that I immediately open my skills and start thinking, “Okay, but what if…”

Those moments are probably my favorite part of PoE2.

Not because the item is necessarily some perfect endgame piece. Half the time I eventually replace it and return to whatever I originally intended to do. But for a few hours it changes the character. I start testing things I wouldn’t have touched otherwise, taking a different direction, or trying to make some weird interaction worth keeping.

When every upgrade is just the same thing with slightly better numbers, I stop remembering individual drops pretty quickly.

But I absolutely remember the random item that derailed my entire evening because I suddenly had a terrible new idea.

For me, good loot isn’t always the thing my build already wanted. Sometimes it’s the thing that makes me question the build.
