The last major gripe

I’m so ready to fully endorse this as an amazing game. They fixed the freezing issue I was having, and I have a blast making new builds and discovering how to craft and all the different ways to play and generate currency.

However, the inventory clutter absolutely murders my game enjoyment. Even with super strict filters my inventory is full 1⁄2 way through a map when all I want to do is blast. There are 100’s of currency/crafting items that all take up space, and then half a dozen waypoints and tablets each. Why can’t I just move these to stash with the click of a button at any time like I can in Last Epoch? I want my inventory space for looting equipment, not for currency items that I then have to click 50x to move over to my stash.

Why does GGG insist upon this situation? Does the player base not feel the same way that I do?

Just let me blast, please!
