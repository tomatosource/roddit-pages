Am I the only one enjoying the game right now?

I started the new league with the new huntress and been loving it so far. The levelling has been way smoother than at EA launch. Now I did skip most of 0.1.0 so maybe I missed out on the POE2s peak/paradise time to play but compared to launch, the campaign is a lot easier.

Loot drops are not as much as I’d like but better than 1st launch - was able to gear well enough to 1 shot every boss I’ve encountered so far.

The mechanics of the new huntress are fun with tools to handle every situation whether it be crowds or single target. She is very mobile, can take a hit and clears a ton of mobs quickly. However, there is definitely more combos needed than some other builds. It’s not a ‘spam 1 skill’ class which I think is the intention. You are strongly incentivised to combine skills for the best results. This includes jumping in and out of melee and ranged esp to gain frenzy at the start.

I am still in early game and crown of thorns is helping with survivability a lot so may feel different later on. Currently on the desert caravan.
