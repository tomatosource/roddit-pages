GGG, sincerely, explosion radius on Snipe is a nearly useless Quality

Snipe, my dearly beloved, is incredible. I love to build it and take rares and bosses to task with this attack. I look forward to building it again with freezing as a heavy element of the mechanic. I want to see your vision!

BUT, Quality granting Explosion Radius does NOTHING for this ability. It is not an add clear attack, chaining it isn’t a viable strategy for dealing with whites, there’s no reason to make the boom a bigger radius. Please consider improved crit chance instead, now thst it cant crit automatically. It’s significantly difficult to get crit chance up with bow builds, please hear my plea.

Snipe: Explosion is no longer Guaranteed Critical Hit and doesn’t have increased Critical Damage Bonus. Explosion now deals 363-936% Attack Damage at Gem levels 3-20 (previously 133-343%), and has a radius of 2.5 metres (previously 1.6). Perfectly timed arrows that hit a Frozen or Heavy Stunned enemy now cause larger explosions that deal much more damage. Quality now provides +0-0.4 metres to Explosion radius.
