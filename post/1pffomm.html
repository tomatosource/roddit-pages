2026 Marlins

So this will be a lengthy post. I’ve been doing a lot thinking lately about Marlins. I saw them play last season and see a team with a ton of potential. They’re missing a few key pieces to make them a serious contender for the 2026 season. I made a mock roster for 2026. Before I share it, let me explain some of the things I believe they need to be a contender.

First, they need more veteran presence on the team. They have a great young core but no one really who’s been through the 162 grind plus playoff to guide these young players.

Tighten up the bullpen and defense. The bullpen wasnt strong last season. They need to add a top reliever to contend against the Mets and Phillies. Also, adding defense would turn some of those loses into win. They had an -89 run differential and rated a C- in defense last season.

Make a huge splash. This can come in the form of a trade. Marlins are pitching rich so trading a pitcher wouldn’t hurt them.

Add a true sparkplug on top of their lineup. Edwards is good but he’s not a true sparkplug on a contending team.

So here are my suggestions for the team to address these above issues:

  1. Trade Edward Cabrera and Marsee for Jarren Durren. Durren had somewhat of a down year last season but he has too much potential to not bounce back. Adding Durren would give them a sparkplug, tighten up the defense.

  2. Sign Harrison Bader. I know his underlying stats show signs of regression but his defense is still far above average. Even if Bader does his average offensively when you factor in his defense he’ll have a great season. Also, adding Bader will improve their defense dramatically and offer them veteran presence in the outfield. Two years at 24 million seems reasonable.

  3. Sign Justin Verlander and Michael King. Verlander offers them a veteran on the pitching staff who’s been through the 162 grind and a WS champion. He doesn’t have to be prime Verlander. He just has to perform like last season and his leadership in the clubhouse would be huge. He also lives in the area during the off-season so being close to his family could be a selling point to him. Michael King will give the Marlins a strong 1-2-3 punch with Alcantara, King and Perez. Alcantara, Perez and Meyer will benefit tremendously from Verlander and King in their rotation. One year at 15 million for Verlander and 5 years at 70 million for King.

  4. Sign Fairbanks. Fairbanks gives them a proven bullpen arm and makes their bullpen much stronger. Three years at 39 million

  5. Take a flyer on Adolis Garcia. He was just DFA by the Rangers but a change of scenery can do him a favor. He can add power to the lineup. He can DH. One year at 7 million plus incentives

  6. Sign Wilmar Flores. He’s not the best option but he had a solid season for the Giants and has been a decent first baseman for them. Aside from Alonso, first baseman market is slim this season (we know the fish aren’t spending on Alonso). Flores might be the best option. They can always trade for an upgrade at the trade deadline. One year for 5 million

  7. Sign Tyler Wade. He’s not much of a bat but he can all positions and an excellent pitch runner coming off the bench. One year 900k

  8. Lock up young talent. Marlins fanbase have ripped apart whenever they trade their young talent. Lock up Stowers, Edwards, Perez, and Norby and Meyer. When you look at the talent the Marlins have had throughout the years it’s such a shame they chose to tear down the team inside of building around them. That cause them to lose a fanbase. They shouldn’t let it happen again.

So the lineup would look like this:

  1. Durren-LF
  2. Edwards- 2B
  3. Stowers- RF
  4. Garica- DH
  5. Bader- CF
  6. Flores- 1B
  7. Norby- 3B
  8. Ramirez- C
  9. Lopez- SS

Rotation:

  1. Alcantara
  2. King
  3. Perez
  4. Verlander
  5. Meyer

Bullpen: Fairbanks- CL Bridge to Fairbanks: Bachar and Henriquez

I know all this will expended their payroll by a lot but they have a lot of money spend from MLB revenue sharing before they get investigated. These signing will show the fanbase the team is serious about winning and will increase fan attendance and engagement. It will make an automatic wild card team and possibly even a very strong contender for the NL East. I truly believe these moves will turn them from a 73 win team to a 90 win team. Thoughts?
