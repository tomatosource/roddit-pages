I would like to spend my 5 cent about the game in a positive way but..

This is a view of a humble casual player. I loved this season, compared to season 1, i did 2 charaters, one in Ssf and one “trade”. In ssf I played alone and i reached t3 t4.. while in standard with trade, I followed some build and got to T15 (obviously). For me the game is super difficult and no, it doesn’t help new players, but I love it.

If GGG thought to approach new players, they are wrong. Too much time to do anything. The loot does not exist ? well.. maybe it exists for people who know how to do and juicY everything to the max. But people who maybe in one season can barely put together 300 breach splinter…

I did 10 expeditions and played hours, only to see 4 of my best items disappear. Anyway, I REALLY hope the best for this game.

Pros - about second season, has nice maps, more variety, lots of bosses and mini quests. I loved the corrupted nexus and many more stuff, in my opinion the maps are more balanced, (the counterpart ? you MUST have a super build at t3 or you don’t move on unless you have 6k of ES) now they getting t15 much harder to reach, nice strongbox. I saw many different builds (nice ideas also) ignite, thorn, bleed, dot chaos, bone.. and many more. The exiles are pretty ok.

Cons

- Please..balance the campaign.

- Now when I die or leave the map, I can’t redo the layout again, why ?

- Checkpoints, some quite useless. Spike, lag and disconnection are terrible.

- Yeah, not enough loot, is tedious as ****

- Recombinator is really meh.. too clunky and too many artifacts to do something

- Spirits ? mmm.. i don’t know

- You have to grind like hell. Tons of new players will sure leave after the campaign.

I saw people play LE, and they have more sense of progression. To me this is a “double side of the coin” for POe2; because you really would to move forward and discover more and more, do Xesht, Olroth and many more.. but at the same time, the progression is toooo slow and not every player can do a season properly.

I really love this game, i discover a world thanks to poe, and met good people, and i hope Poe2 goes in the right direction
