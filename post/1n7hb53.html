[OC] NBA MVPs sorted and ranked by their western zodiac sign

Hello. Last week I made a thread sorting and ranking NBA MVPs by their Chinese zodiac sign (Rat, Tiger, Rabbit, etc) and people seemed to have liked it. So here I am again doing the same thing but for the western zodiac, which I bet more people on r/nba are familiar with.

As a reminder, the twelve astrological signs are, in order: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces. 

Firstly, how about some predictions? Since the western zodiac naturally has more granularity than the Chinese zodiac (cycles are 1 year long versus 12 years), I expect the distribution of players to be more even. But will that actually be the case? And which sign reigns supreme? Let’s find out together!

Did my prediction pan out? Sort of. In the last thread we ended up with the Ox sign having no selections, which I thought was surprising. In this exercise all twelve signs ended up being represented with at least two selections. Furthermore, we had two instances in the Chinese zodiac thread where a sign was entirely represented by one player (Bill Russell and Moses Malone). In this exercise that only happens once with LeBron. That would appear to support the case for more even distribution. However, last time no sign had more than 12 selections, but here we have one with 13, and the results are still skewed heavily by players who won the award multiple times. So once again let’s look at the list focusing on the number of unique players and not the selections themselves and see if anything changes.
