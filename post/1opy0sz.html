Endgame Tempest Flurry Titan Armour

https://preview.redd.it/m07rgqhxumzf1.png?width=785&format=png&auto=webp&s=f661a549147af60ea77b8c1dd15252682f93f406

So I had some money sitting around and wanted a break from PoE1 mapping, so I decided to try and craft an endgame armour for my zoomy titan boy. The build is based 100% on Palestrons, so all the credit goes to him!

I was super pleased with the outcome, and the divine is so damn crispy I just had to stop when I hit those numbers :D.

Crafting Process Steps:

  1. Purchase an ilvl 82 Swiftstalker coat with 3 holes.
  2. Perfect Transmute/Aug and hope for T1 flat evasion, % evasion, or hybrid flat+%. If none hit, then annul down until “clean” and hit with augs until you hit the mod.
  3. Perf regal+ regal omen in hopes of hitting a t1 prefix listed above or t1 deflection (I hit t1 light res, which was amazing for my build!).
  4. After getting 3 desirable mods, slap a preserved rib and pray for a good fracture (preyge). In my case, the light res was perfect, and onto chaos spamming we go!
  5. Chaos spam for any t1 prefix desirable mods (again, flat, hybrid flat+% or %).
  6. Homegnising exaltation omen + sinistral omen + either greater/perfect exalt for those desirable mods we want!
  7. After countless chaos and omen spamming, I finally hit the 3 t1 mods and onto the suffixes we went.
  8. This is where it got a bit painful; my method was to hit a random suffix, use an omen of dextral crystallisation along with an essence of the abyss, then hit it with an ancient rib (thankfully I got lucky after about 5 tries and hit the t1 fire res, which was absolutely insane for what I wanted!).
  9. At this point, the process was omen of light + annuls and ancient rib spam until I hit T1 deflection.
  10. And finally, divine it as high as possible, which I chose to stop even at 25% deflection because of how well everything else rolled.

(Hopefully I didn’t miss any steps, but feel free to ask me or add feedback!)

This was a super stressful project overall, but worth spending the money since I won’t be making more characters!

I know my process may not be the best route, but I had fun making it, and other than that, I feel like a giga-chad! Happy hunting, exiles! &lt;
