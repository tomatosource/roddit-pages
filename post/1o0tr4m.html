What NBA player had the longest and most varied international career?

But which NBA player has the most varied international playing career? Who has played in the most countries?

Rules are:

  1. Has to be a professional league, not just playing for their country’s national squad.

  2. Has to have some time on an NBA roster, even if it is just one season in a 12 season career.

  3. We can count the total amount of teams, the total amount of leagues, or the total amount of countries.
