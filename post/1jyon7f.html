0.2.0 Feedback from a Minion Player’s Perspective

This post is meant as feedback. It will sound like I’m complaining, because I probably am.

tl;dr Minions need more SPEED. Every design decision has significantly crippled the damage & overall archetype’s “feel.” Current state of minion is not fun. Majority of MinionAndy’s are playing something else and are sad.

(I just really want minions to be an enjoyable & comfy experience with a wide variety of minion types/builds to choose from. I’m mostly frustrated because all the “good” things about minions were not brought over from POE1 but instead reinvented).

Despite the recent buffs that minions received, I feel that Minions have moved from a 1⁄10 experience to play to a 4.5⁄10 experience to play. I’m sure that GGG is planning ™ stuff, but I just wanted to voice them out just in case it was not on the radar of the developers. I spend a lot of time interacting with the minion community on Global 6666 plus Discord and I believe that these are the main concerns/gripes of most players that want to play this archetype.

  • The majority of tested Spectres do not perform well in Maps or even well in the Campaign.
    • POE2 Summoner Community Spectre Compendium - Google Sheets
    • The community has now tested about ~150+ spectres (see above). About 2-4 of them are usable for end game by themselves with the current support gems available. Theres A LOT that are super interesting. Those most interesting ones tend to fall short in terms of SPEED. They either don’t attack or cast fast enough or there’s some super large cooldown for the abilities that matter. Spectres are in great need of more support gems or speed in general to function.
    • S/O to Truditoru & everyone else for putting in the work!
  • All the Tools & Power we had in POE1 is GONE! Why?!
    • Convocation
      • Minion positioning & movement speed are extremely important since minion AI is unreliable. Currently the majority of all minions are slow and will lag behind you. If you try to WALK at a normal pace, you will outrun your minions and will very likely die. This is not fun. A tool like Convocation would help tremendously.
    • Utility/Aura buffs being moved to Companions with a % based Spirit reservation
      • Auras, utility debuffs, & any power granted by them through Spectres have been moved over to Companions a separate type of minion. This is fine; however, Companions are % Spirit based which locks out minion players from using these reliably without hindering our already mediocre damage.
    • Command Skills
      • The community does not like these. I feel like a Spellcaster! I DO NOT want to play as a Spellcaster!
    • (lack of) Desecrate & Raise Zombie was turned into a minion with duration.
      • Out of all the minion skills that could’ve become Persistent and auto-revive, why was Raise Zombie not one? I cannot imagine a worse way to make this skill unusable since there is no Desecrate nor any reliable way to generate Power Charges as a pure Summoner.
