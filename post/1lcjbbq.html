People aren’t being reasonable regarding KP

So just about every post I’ve seen here suggests people want to get rid of KP no matter what. The chorus grew louder when some started suggesting that we actually resign him at a lower number. This actually makes sense if, like most seem to prefer, that we should not blow up the team. Obviously health is an issue but factoring that into a smaller contract seems reasonable.

Most people seem to want him gone no matter what. I can understand that, but those same people seem to want something back of value, predominately that they like better than keeping KP. How does that actually work? Take my trash and turn it into gold?

Obviously I’m exaggerating but you get the point. We don’t want him so give me something I do want. Now, if you want to dump him and save money by throwing something in, that at least is consistent with the idea that you don’t want him. Of curse, it isn’t consistent with the idea that you want to still contend.

So which is it?
