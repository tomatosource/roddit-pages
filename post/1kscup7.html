Why the Celtics defense isn’t equipped for the playoffs

A new style of play is needed to take advantage of the soft off-ball whistle in the playoffs. The Celtics have emphasized clean defense and that’s just not what plays. Look at MIN and OKC who have the most ferocious defenses because they’re willing to test the whistle. I’ve written this up in full here:  https://carmencliffs.substack.com/p/what-is-a-foul-in-the-nba

But I think the Celtics need to come back next year with an emphasis on testing the limits with physicality on the defensive end from the beginning of the season. They should never be last in FTA allowed again.
