The current direction of the game kills build diversity and promotes streamlined builds with little choice, as if they want to make a diablo clone.

I’ve been trying to figure out how we got here from PoE 1. They did say they want a game with more broad appeal. I thought this was going to come in the way of making things less obtuse and more user friendly, while keeping the thing everyone loves about PoE, the depth of player choice. Instead, the direction I’m seeing is killing player choice and forcing very distinct roles. It is very reminiscent of diablo 3 and 4 classes.

When I first heard about skills being locked to weapons I was concerned. It severely limits build variety by making weapon choice a bigger choice than class and ascendancy. It limits the pool of skills you can use to 1/6th of the entire pool of active skill gems right off the bat. Sure, the argument could be made about weapon swapping, but to that I say with my whole entire chest FUCK WEAPON SWAPPING. 1⁄6 will only get made even worse with more weapon types enabled. It is as bad as I had feared, but something else is making it even worse.

Combo. Based. Gameplay. This waters down choice even further. The game has basically fully gone diablo with this design decision. Now you have your main damage/ resource dump skill, a resource charge skill, a movement skill, a defense skill… you see where I’m going. It’s a stone throw away from having a primary, secondary, and 4 utility skills. The only way out of this gameplay currently is to be able to power up a one or two button build by using the most meta skill that does everything and for sure is going to get nerfed (lightning spear), get hyper expensive items that allow you to make a shittier skill work as a one button build, or go around relying on skills almost entirely, aka bleed/thorns.

I think the best example of this right now is chaos spells. Spell casting is by far the most diverse builds you can make, as you can use 1h or 2h, and all the elemental skills + chaos skills + minion builds…. but mixing them makes no sense. So, if you want to go chaos, you are stuck in the chaos tree. Then, there are just built in combos for chaos skills. You are stuck going ed contagion, with the other skills as supplements because they aren’t strong enough to carry a build themselves. There is no other viable choice unless you are a masochist that hates playing a good build.

Support gems are also crazy limiting at this point. Being able to only use one for each skill makes it so one skill is going to get the best damage ones, one skill is going to get the resource charging/ailment forcing ones, and everything else gets wtf ever is left. It feels like such a downgrade from PoE 1’s unique, genre revolutionizing skill support gem system everyone loves. Don’t get me started on how they sold us you won’t have to worry about fusings, a currency you could gradually farm, as a upside while hiding extra sockets behind hyper rare currency drops. I was running T15’s with 4 link skills until I decided to quit.

I find myself letting go of PoE 2 because I think this direction is only going to continue. I think it obviously isn’t for me, because it doesn’t have what I loved about PoE 1. It feels like I’m playing the hardcore rippy abusive megagrind version of Diablo 3, except skills have a much better balance in Diablo 3. I feel like once I’ve played a weapon once, I’ve played all the builds available for it. Ascendancy and class just feel like extra skill tree now. There’s just no room for crazy experimentation. The “oh look at this cool build I made” that could be made by tons of creators is now “oh look at this skill that outperforms the rest,” “this one unique that breaks the game,” or the one mechanic that let’s you transcend the skill system, like bleed and thorns.
