League Idea for GGG to consider

Hi All,

I love playing POE2 and async trade was a huge step in the right direction but I feel like the game lacks one thing; meaningful multiplayer. I feel little incentive to be part of a guild or do party play. Hence why I introduce my new league idea:

“The colloseum of Champions!”

After being captured as a prisoner on Oriath after a failed assault, you are pitted against the champions and beasts that have also fallen victim to their capture.

How it will work:

You start with no gear, no skills, no passive tree, nothing. But are given a weapon, a skill gem and a support gem to start with.

You are pitted against waves of increasingly difficult enemies. With every victory, you get the choice of 3 randomised awards; skill gems, support gems, spirit gems etc. You also recieve 1000 gold, which you can use in a shop to either buy gear, save it for something more expensive, or reroll the gear.

Every 5 waves you verse either a rogue exile or a player (depending on if you choose pvp or pve). If you beat them, you get their gear and you have been deemed worthy by the Queen of Oriath. You can chose to leave the colloseum with gear you’ve obtained, or you can choose to risk your life for increasingly rare gear and be pitted against stronger waves of foes and new opponents every 5 waves.

If you can manage to reach wave 100, you may challenge the champion of Oriath. An incredibly strong foes who has never known defeat.

This is where guilds come in aswell. Fight for the honour of your guild for rarer rewards as you Verse more difficult waves of enemies and other teams in 3 on 3 duels. The guild with the highest score at the end of the league are crowned as the new champions and earn uber rare uniques to befit a champion.

This is all just theoretical, I have no idea if any of this would be possible. But I think it would really breath more life into the community and encourage more multiplayer.
