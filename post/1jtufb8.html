The game they’re trying to make is a fantasy and will never exist

They want a hardcore combo heavy action game with “meaningful combat” and long intensive boss fights, but at the same time they want the build diversity of PoE and the same complex gem system with hundreds of build defining uniques and a massive passive tree.

The problem is that it is literally impossible to balance such a game and achieve that result.

If you want a game where top builds always have to interact with the boss mechanics, then the mid to low tier builds are going to be next to unplayable. If you want a game where all builds have a fair chance to beat the bosses, then everyone will be playing the top builds that melts everything in seconds. There are just too many variables for anyone to realistically balance while still keeping the stats relevant.

The only way they can achieve the fantasy they have in their minds is if they completely remove all the complex rpg mechanics, and instead focus on the action game aspect where they can actually balance the classes and force the players to interact with the monsters and bosses.
