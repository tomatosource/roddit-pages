[Gambadoro] The Toronto Raptors, similarly to the Clippers, made attempts in 2019 to entice Kawhi Leonard with off-the-court incentives to convince him to stay. Their efforts reportedly included three separate sponsorship deals that would have collectively netted him an additional $15 million.

Source: John Gambadoro of https://arizonasports.com/

This story is fascinating. From what I know from sources - when Kawhi signed with the Clippers the Toronto owner Larry Tannebaum was beside himself because he believed he bent over backwards for Kawhi - including calling three sponsors to meet Kawhi’s demand of an extra $15 million in endorsement money. This was brought to the attention of Adam Silver as a concern over the cheating going on in the league being out of control. But no action was taken as it was thought Tannebaum didn’t truly understand the rules - only a promise to get more serious about it in the future.

I was told that Toronto was very upset about losing Kawhi and felt they had gone above and beyond to keep him which included $15 million in endorsement money that was going to come from three sponsors.
