After 330 hours I think I finally figured it out

When I picked up the game I decided to learn how to play on HC SSF mode with no guides, just trial and error. After some painful lessons I think I’m ready to finally clear the campaign.

My top lesson is to turn on health bars on yourself. Most of my deaths have come from running into some deadly environment issue that kills you over 2 seconds. When my attention was split between the battlefield and the life bars on the corner, I would lose valuable time noticing the effect. Now my first job is keeping my eyeballs glued to that health bar, and being ready to do what I need to react immediately when I’m in trouble. As a last resort I’ve got the esc key bound to a mouse button, ready to pause and walk away if I need to.

Also, some bosses have patterns that I had to memorize. Mighty Silverfist, Viper Napuatzi, Yama the White, The Prisoner and Azmadi Prince all have positioning mechanics that initially caught me unprepared. Now I know how to handle them.

There’s only one campaign boss that I’m still worried about. Lythara. Even after a dozen practice fights, I still sometimes get caught in that chaos lightning storm, which is basically insta death. Definitely will need to put in extra time practicing on that one.

Also, I don’t know if any other HC players had a difficult time with the Vaal Pyramid, but I died several times to the bosses in there, and decided it wasn’t worth the risk of entering certain rooms.

I’m looking forward to the new expansion. Hoping to get my starter character into the new endgame. Fingers crossed.
