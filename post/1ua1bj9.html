Since we’re making mechanics more rewarding, lets tune up the trials!

Back in 0.1 I actually use to farm the Trial of Sekhemas because it was a great way to get jewels and raw currency. However, as they have buffed the base loot of the game and other league mechanics, the two trials are now kind of a joke other than farming for very specific uniques (Against the Darkness primarily). The Chaos trial is much the same, why are 90% of the rewards just really poorly rolled corrupted items? Why is getting a div so rare? Hell, I didn’t even know you could get divs in the Chaos Trial rewards until my friend posted a screenshot this league (we’ve both been playing since 0.1).

I feel like all of the chests at the end of the Trial need to be heavily buffed. Perhaps the Trial of Chaos should just not offer corrupted items and only offer currency and soul cores? Either way, I really do feel like these trials could use an update and make them viable end game farms.
