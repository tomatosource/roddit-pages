Women’s Pro Baseball League - Fyre Festival of baseball?

This startup league has been getting some attention so I looked under the hood at it. As an investor, I wouldn’t touch it. If I were a player, I am not sure I would show up.

This could set women’s pro baseball back. This is seeming like the Fyre Festival of baseball.

  • Four teams allegedly from different cities, all playing in Springfield, IL.

  • Four week season.

  • No broadcast deals yet. No sponsorships.

  • $95k salary cap per team.

  • They drafted players but the teams don’t even have names.

Are young women having their dreams exploited here? This is not at all how the WNBA or NWSL started. They had broadcast deals and sponsors.

All I see is red flags. Hope won’t fix this.
