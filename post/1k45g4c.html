The goal should never be for veterans to feel challenged by early campaign…

Sorry for piling on but when it was mentioned by lead development that he needs to feel challenged when restarting the campaign (again and again) made me go wide-eyed and say WTF?!

This is fine for a first playthrough in a new difficult league/event - think for example gauntlets or private leagues in PoE, sure. But if you have thousands and thousands of hours in an aRPG like the development team has, the goal shouldn’t be to have a hard (and tedious) time in the supposedly easiest part of the game. You should be challenged by endgame or new aspiring content.

Feeling forced to make early game and campaign super punishing and hard has so many downsides it’s crazy.

Wanna make an alt? Get ready to go back to the hardest and least fun part of the game. Feels like work. There is absolutely NOTHING wrong with the campaign being basically a speedrun for experienced/knowledgeable players. It’s actually pretty fun to try and go as quick as possible on new characters in most games, twink or not. It’s almost its own sidegame to learn new tricks and to practice.

Trying to go past monsters that are unrewarding is ALWAYS gonna happen, it’s just playing smart. It’s playing an aRPG. Making monsters (especially “trash” monsters) be too quick and unavoidable just makes it frustrating for the sake of it. You still need items and experience, the campaign would never ever be about avoiding every packs even if you were very quick. Just picking your battles that feel worth it.

The campaign was often cited as the least enjoyable part of PoE 1, and it seems like they thought the solution to this was to make the campaign slower, longer, harder, with less available crafting options. This is literally the Ruthless mentality, and the VAST majority of people did NOT like ruthless at all.

I can’t even imagine what frustrations PoE2 is putting new inexperienced players through (or imagine like disabled or older people who want to give it a try at all), when their main goal is to make the introducing parts of the game the most punishing.

I like the updates they are making recently and it seems headed in the right direction but this mentality explained a lot to me on why so many things went wrong from the start. Looking forward to more improvements.
