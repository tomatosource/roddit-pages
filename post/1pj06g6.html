Tutorial experience of POE2

Hey

Had a couch co-op session at my place with a friend who mostly plays that other big ARPG we all know. His main takeaway was actually very positive: he felt that Path of Exile is more thought through overall, and he had a lot more fun during boss fights in particular.

But he also bounced off some of the core mechanics. Even with me explaining things next to him, he struggled to understand how some systems work and said he really wished there were more dedicated in-game tutorials or guided explanations. For me who is very average players, have a ok english, i took a lot of time to understand how things work and have a vague ideas where i should upgrade my stuff for examples. Him not having a good english, the localized ressources are limited in France to be honest, there is some creators of course but not a lot doing french content.

Personally, I think that’s a fair point.

There’s probably a lot of room to improve with better onboarding directly in-game.

  • clearer explanations of core systems
  • more step-by-step guidance
  • maybe optional “learn more” prompts for players who want extra detail

I’m not saying the game needs to change its core systems or depth at all. It doesn’t. But for the upcoming free-to-play launch, I really feel the onboarding experience is something that could and should be tightened up so new players don’t bounce before they even see what makes PoE great. I don’t think it would make the game more casual to have a simplier onboarding experience, but it would help to make the game standout even more for his true essence.

Curious if anyone else has had similar experiences introducing friends to PoE. And if you have specific onboarding experience from other games that you feel are very good.
