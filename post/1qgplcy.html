[Duane Rankin] Suns’ Jalen Green Listed as Questionable for First Time Since Injury After Missing 32 Straight Games

Phoenix Suns guard Jalen Green is listed as questionable for the Jan. 19 game against the Brooklyn Nets at Barclays Center.

Green has missed 32 consecutive games with a right hamstring strain, and he’s only played in two games in his first year with the Suns (25-17).

“He’s been licking his chops getting ready for this moment,” Suns star guard Devin Booker said after a 106-99 victory on Jan. 17 over the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden. “I think we’ve overdone it with keeping him out. He’s going to be healthy and strong and ready to go.”

— Duane Rankin, Arizona Republic
