From 2019–2026, the MVP has always had the most Player of the Month awards… except 2024

I wanted to look at this specifically since Luka & SGA came into the league, and how MVP winners compare to Player of the Month (POTM) leaders.

They line up almost perfectly:

2019 — MVP: Giannis Antetokounmpo | POTM: Giannis Antetokounmpo

2020 — MVP: Giannis Antetokounmpo | POTM: Giannis Antetokounmpo

2021 — MVP: Nikola Jokic | POTM: Nikola Jokic / James Harden

2022 — MVP: Nikola Jokic | POTM: Nikola Jokic / Joel Embiid

2023 — MVP: Joel Embiid | POTM: Joel Embiid

2024 — MVP: Nikola Jokic | POTM: Luka Doncic / Jayson Tatum

2025 — MVP: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander | POTM: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

2026 — MVP: ??? | POTM: Luka Doncic

What stands out:

- In every completed season except 2024, the MVP also had the most or tied for most POTM awards.

- 2024 is the only clear break in the pattern - Luka finished 3rd, Tatum finished 6th in MVP voting.

- 2026 so far: Luka leads POTM but is ~3rd in MVP betting odds.

Sources:

MVP: https://www.basketball-reference.com/awards/mvp.html

Player of the Month: https://www.basketball-reference.com/awards/pom.html
