“Bench” Crafts - POE2 to Phrecia and back

I believe bench crafting was not included in POE 2 likely for some good reasons, but playing poe 2 in trade and ssf, then poe 1 in trade and ssf, and coming back… there are little things like bench crafting some movement speed onto boots, or adding the correct resistance, that doesn’t need to be like “S” tier crafting, but can really make finding your own items more accessible/enjoyable as it softens one degree of the RNG. “Wow, these boots would be an upgrade but are useless because they don’t have movement speed.” Becomes “Wow these boots are amazing, but don’t have any movement speed…I can put 15% on there for now…”

Maybe that’s not what the devs wanted but I can say I like finding recipes/unlocking them (at least the first time through) and then being able to slap a bandaid as I was going through the campaign or early maps.
