Why on earth are the Cavs fans wearing white Tshirts?

I spent the first quarter wondering why the Pistons suddenly had the biggest road fanbase in the league before realizing the Cavs crowd was also wearing white. Who approved this idea? Every wide shot looked like Detroit had taken over the arena. I get wanting a playoff “white out,” but maybe don’t do it on the exact night the opposing team is also dressed head to toe in white uniforms. At one point I genuinely couldn’t tell if a section was cheering for Cleveland or celebrating a Pistons three. Amazing atmosphere, terrible color coordination. Somebody in marketing definitely forgot to check the jersey schedule before printing 20,000 shirts.
