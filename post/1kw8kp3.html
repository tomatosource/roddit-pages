SupportGem nerf is a big reason for the boring growth

POE deep skill customization is, for me, a key source of its fun. You can take a basic fireball, for example, and attach a Scattershot gem to unleash multiple fireballs, or a Chain gem to make it bounce between enemies. Combine both, and your fireballs explode outwards, hitting multiple foes and then chaining amongst them. There’s immense satisfaction in seeing these effects layer, transforming a skill into something entirely new as more gem slots become available.

Unfortunately, this season has seen significant nerfs to many beloved support gems, drastically reducing the joy of skill evolution.

Let’s look at the gems for this season’s popular Lightning Spear:

Volt: Extra Lightning Damage and Chain.

Chain: Additional chains.

Primal Armament: More weapon Elemental damage.

Deliberation: More Damage, slower movement.

Overextend: More Critical Damage Bonus, gain Dazed.

Blindside: More Critical Damage Bonus vs. Blinded Enemies.

Ambush: More Critical Hit Chance vs. Full Life Enemies.

Pinpoint Critical: More Critical Hit Chance, less Critical Hit damage.

Considering that critical-related support gems primarily function as damage increases, your options narrow to just Chain support gems and penalty-driven damage support gems.

This leads to a dull progression. The Lightning Spear, beyond its damage numbers, essentially remains unchanged from its initial form (even with early access to level 3 support gems).

We attribute this stagnation to the prevalence of penalties on most support gems. While balancing without penalties is tough, it’s evident that these restrictions ultimately make the game less interesting.

Hopefully, GGG will adopt a more empowering philosophy.
