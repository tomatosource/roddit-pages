On this date in 1986, Alvin Robertson recorded one of the four quadruple-doubles in NBA history (20 points, 11 rebounds, 10 assists and 10 steals)

Box score to Alvin Robertson’s game: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/198602180SAS.html

He remains the only player to have a quadruple-double with 10+ steals. The other 3 players did it with blocks.

The others:

  • David Robinson in 1994: 34 points, 10 rebounds, 10 assists, 10 blocks: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/199402170SAS.html
  • Hakeem Olajuwon in 1990: 18 points, 16 rebounds, 10 assists, 11 blocks: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/199003290HOU.html
  • Nate Thurmond in 1974: 22 points, 14 rebounds, 13 assists, 12 blocks: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/197410180CHI.html

There are a few other players who came close and had a triple-double with 9 steals: Michael Ray Richardson, Larry Bird, Johnny Moore, and Rick Barry (Source)
