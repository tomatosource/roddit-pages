Bane Trade and the CBA.

I’ve seen a lot of ppl since the Bane trade indicate they think there’s a decent chance grizzlies blow it all up after the haul they received.

Personally i think it’s the opposite and really think this was the grizzlies making a choice between keeping JJJ and Bane. This choice was forced on them then by the new CBA.

You simply can’t pay 3 guys like JJJ, Ja, and Bane under this new CBA. It’s impossible to field a team with the proper depth year after year unless you absolutely nail the fringe roster moves all the time, and even then I’m not sure it’s realistic . Under the old CBA you can get away with this and still field good depth.

Teams constructed under the old CBa will have to make tough roster decisions similar to Memphis. Denver for example is a team suffering greatly from the CBA change. Their bench is basically useless because all their money is tied up to Jokic, MPJ, and Murray. They need to trade away Murray or MPJ for more depth. The timberwolves traded KAT partly for this reasoning as well.

TL;DR: grizzlies making this trade was a product of the CBA, and I don’t think it really indicates they’re blowing things up at all. I think this makes it much more likely they keep JJJ.
