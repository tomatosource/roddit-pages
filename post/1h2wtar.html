In-person experience at an NBA game vs NFL and NHL games

On Wednesday, I went to my first NBA game: it was the Spurs versus the Lakers—in San Antonio. We sat in the tenth row. I’ve been to Texans games and Hurricanes (NHL) games with similarly decent seats, and the experience was completely different. I don’t have ADHD, but with the NBA game, I couldn’t concentrate because there were so many distractions (e.g., music during play, courtside fans walking to get food during play). With the NHL and NFL, the loud noise and fan behavior seemed to be relevant to the game at hand.

My question is: Was this just me? I’m still trying to make sense of why the experience seemed so different. Perhaps it was that the tickets were more expensive?

Not trying at all to be judgmental here! I had fun and would go back. Just genuinely curious to know more about differences between experiences.
