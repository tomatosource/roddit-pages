Great Endgame Update But - Stop Making us do so many filler maps

I love the new endgame but I’m getting burnt out on the travel maps between content. I want to try it all, I want to look the 311 point atlas and all the 3 master missions and the new endgame trees. But when I think of how many maps I have to do just to get to the starting area for one of the new atlas tree quests….its just a burn out.

Few suggestions:

  1. Each atlas content master persons quest starting area should be closer to main starting area and or give a portal option or something.
  2. Once you get to quest area to start new atlas tree - don’t make us do 20+ maps to unlock it when including travel nodes - be a little more creative with it. Tougher fights or some kind of more nuanced way to unlock the tree.

Kind of the same with the new masters - a lot of travel between.

I shouldn’t be level 96 and have barely any of the endgame atlas unlocked.
