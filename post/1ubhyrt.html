Why farming feels so amazing right now

I am setting here with my coffee and thinking of my yesterday journey and now I know why 0.5 is so crazy right now.

Started with 65d in my stash and a lot of uncraftet tablets, endet with 273d and a Headhunter after 8 hours of “work” and I had an absolute blast. No hunting for biomes, just tablet, 6mod t15 and go.

​

Now I know why. For example: started with abyss, some really nice maps but after a dry streak I got pissed. Ragechanged to ritual and everything was fine again.

​

Today I realized, every mechanic has something really needed. Nothing is useless. Abyss and ritual for all the omens, deli for jewel crafting, brach for all the other stuff and so on. All give a decent amount of currency so there is no fear of missing something.

​

GGG nailed it. At least for me. Thank you so much. Love it. (Before 0.5 i did not liked poe2, but please tell no one)
