Are we at our best starting Pritchard or having him come off the bench?

Is anyone on this team capable of allowing us to keep Pritchard as the league’s best 6th man?

I keep seeing photos of the Celtics projected starting 5, and the more I look at it, the better I feel about this team.

Lots of scoring. Lots of ball handling. Lots of playmaking. Lots of shooting. Lots of rebounding. And enough defense and versatility to offer lots of different looks making us hard to game-plan for in the regular season.

Then add in Queta & Hauser and that’s a solid 7-man rotation. Add-in Hugo, Walsh, Baylor, and Cenac and we have the potential to roster a long list of capable players on this team.

We will win a lot of regular-season games. And unlike last year, we should be able to sneak in a decent number of wins against the top teams in the league.

But given our surplus of depth is at the wing (Hauser, Hugo, Baylor, Walsh), and our best depth is at center (Queta), we’re left with real question marks at PG and PF.

Of course at point, we can shift White to the one and then have Conley come in for limited minutes, but what about when White misses games? What about when our point-forward JT is out? We already know that George will miss a lot. The backup PG role is a real concern if Mike Conley (who turns 39 in October) is our only answer.

And at Power Forward… we essentially have zero proven depth there. Sure Queta could probably play the four a couple minutes if Tatum is on the court with him, but that gives us two centers who can’t shoot in the lineup. And sure, when Tatum sits we can have Hugo, Walsh, or Hauser play some time at the four, but that gives up A LOT of size at times when we probably need it most.

This makes me think that at our best, Pritchard is coming off the bench.

I don’t care what the rumors say, I don’t see a world where Pritchard becomes our version of Brunson. Yes, he put crazy numbers when starting without JB last season. Yes, he’s been very productive when given the ball more. But he’s also been less efficient when being relied on game after game. He also is much harder to hide on defense than anyone else on the team due to his size, and that costs a lot more come playoff time.

And the Celtics also clearly prefer him being their spark plug off the bench. He won 6MOY in that role. We won a championship with him in that role. And they even moved him back to that role last year PRIOR to Jayson Tatum coming back to the starting lineup.

I imagine the Celtics ideal role for him is a Ginobli-esque 6th man role. Perennial 6th man finalist. Starter minutes. Starts occasionally but closes most games. Same value as if he was a starter, but a little more responsibility as he’’ll spend more time with bench players. Less Brunson, more Crawford/Ginoblii

But in order to take Pritchard out of the starting lineup, somebody else needs to step up so we can slide White to pg.

And I’m not sure if Hauser starting makes us better even if it balances our rotations. Also not sure if Hugo, Walsh, or Baylor will be ready for that role by the end of the year.

Let me know your thoughts! Go C’s! ☘️
