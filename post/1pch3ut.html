NBA screens are probably the ugliest part of the modern game

Im not an old-head; I love a lot of the aesthetic of the post “3pt revolution” nba. I love the evolving chess match of the pnr, and the pacers/thunder finals last year was beautiful. But the universal tolerance for blatant moving screens on every single play is gross. It’s moderately unsafe, and it very visibly gives the offense a huge advantage on every screen for the ballhandler, unless the on-ball defender is Lu Dort. Aside: this is why popular opinion will never sway me on Lu Dort; the way he deals with the unfairness of modern ball screens is cathartic.

I just was watching steve nash highlights, and saw Amare set a normal screen; not a moving screen where he steps up to impede the defender, just a normal screen. It was prime Steve nash being guarded by a spurs joe shmoe, and I was thinking how much more boring the play would be if Amare got away with the ju jitsu they do today.

Edit: the reason i bring up screens and not the illegal shoulder checking that the ballhandler is allowed to do on every drive, is because i think the screens make that possible for most teams, although the heat are a counterexample. Even with modern spacing and the illegal dislodges on drives, i think if the ballhandler had to initiate this contact earlier in the play it would be a much more pleasant game to watch.

Rant over.
