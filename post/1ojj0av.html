“If they do win, I hope my calls match the moment” — Dan Shulman on calling the World Series with his son 🎙️🇨🇦

https://preview.redd.it/bmwg0uujp4yf1.jpg?width=1750&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=897420af19f9f85c51df021274af52507e508b8f

We had the chance to chat with Dan Shulman right before the World Series and he couldn’t have been more humble about it all.

He talked about how he didn’t even think about becoming a broadcaster until he was 23, how his family was half supportive and half worried when he switched into radio, and how surreal it feels to be calling games for his hometown team alongside his son Ben — the first father-son duo ever to call a World Series.

He also shared a great reminder about staying grounded: “You’re only as good as your last game.”

And when asked how he’ll celebrate if the Jays win, he said he just hopes his call “matches the moment.”
