Glass Half Full Outlook

As much as it sucks ghat we completed the most humiliating series loss in franchise history, I’m trying to balance optimism with pragmatism for next season.

Firstly, this is not the summer of 2025. Last year around this time was some of the darkest days of being a Celtic fan. Tatum’s Achilles ruptured , we got trounced in game 6 against the Knicks, and we had to ship out quality players in the name of shedding salary and getting under the second apron.

This is not that.

Tatum returned at a record time and looked like himself. Furthermore, this will be the longest offseason of his career and he will certainly use that time to get his injured side just as strong as his uninjured side and will be ready for next season.

Brad has more flexibility to improve the roster whereas last year, he was focused on cost cutting. MLE plus the Anfernee Simons trade exception will be two powerful tools to improve the roster.

The wildcard is really Mazzulla. I think it’s foolish to believe he will be fired given how Boston over achieved in the regular season. He has proven to be an excellent regular season coach. The post season flameouts 3 of the last 4 years are frustrating, no doubt. I’ll give some grace for 2023 as he was thrust into the position on short notice and did not have a full staff. 2025 in my opinion was more frustrating than 2026 given we had more talent on that team. I think the jury is still out on Mazzulla even with the ‘24 chip but if we make significant roster improvements and without injury, we have another postseason flameout, you cannot justify keeping him around. He needs to drastically improve chess matching in the playoffs.

As far as what I’d like to see for the roster next year… a facilitating point guard would be nice. I think perhaps Derrick White struggled this year since he was on ball more whereas during the Jeue Holiday years he was amazing. Queta is a great center but he cannot be starting on a championship team… curious to see how we address that need this summer .
