Item Rarity needs to be gone

Honestly, it feels terrible to be forced to build every character around item rarity, which purposely makes your character weaker. In PoE 1, magic find was already a complete disaster before the nerf, so I really don’t understand why they implemented it again in PoE 2.

And for those who claim it doesn’t matter: it matters a lot. The difference between 0% and 100%+ rarity is absurd. You go from seeing zero good currency drops for several maps in a row to seeing them drop almost every single map.

And the worst part that it’s a stat that once you play with it, it is basically obrigatory in every other char you make because of the difference, almost like resistance cap.

They really need to shovel down rarity and balance the game without it.
