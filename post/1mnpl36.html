The WNBA reset timeout

It’s my first season really watching the WNBA, and they sure have a lot of work to do to refine their product. However one aspect I really like about their game is the reset timeout. For those that dont know what this is, it’s like a no-huddle timeout just to advance the ball. End of game situations feel crisper, momentum is sustained, and the viewing experience is improved in my opinion. I’ve heard some suggest the NBA not want to do this due to commercial revenue from full timeouts, but outside of this argument, any thoughts on whether this would be a good rule change in the NBA? Are there any other things about WNBA the NBA can learn?
