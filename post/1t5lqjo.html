Trading Josh Minott Was a Mistake

The Celtics stumbled upon several promising young role players this regular season, but in my opinion, Minott was clearly the best. We ultimately chose the ones we drafted, but idk why we punted on a $2.4 M, 6’9, lockdown defending, highly switchable, 42% from 3 on several attempts wing who recently turned 23 at the time. We ultimately got under the tax with the Simons trade (also questionable), so why did we need to give Minott up for nothing? Would we have helped us beat the 76ers, probably not, but if he can give us potentially Hauser-level spot up shooting, with Walsh-level defense, at an extremely cheap contract, I just don’t see why we didn’t hold on to him for at least next season. He also could have given us some Tatum at the 5, small ball 4 minutes that we were ultimately starving for.
