Martial Artist VS Witchhunter for Cull Threshold stacking.

I want to preface this with I know any good build will one shot monsters anyways. That’s not what i want to look at. I just specifically want to look at which class is better for building a character that stacks increased Cull threshold.

Currently we have the following means to unconditionally increase cull threshold:

I should Note, that i am NOT including any items corrupted to be higher than base.

  1. Myris Uxor Helmet &gt; 100% increased Cull threshold. (global drop.)
  2. Heart of the well Unique Jewel &gt; 25% increased Cull Threshold. (abyss boss drop, high variance.)
  3. Hunting Companion &gt; Notable on tree. 20%. (this is likely to be the anoint for both classes to save on passives)
  4. Bounty Hunter &gt; Notable on tree. 25%
  5. 3 small passives leading to Bounty Hunter &gt; 15%
  6. Time Worn Jewel. Medium radius Socketed near Hunting Companion minimum 24% increased notable &gt; 6%
  7. Time Worn Jewel Any radius, socketed near Bounty hunter, 25% increased Notable, minimum 20% increased Small &gt; 5 for the notable and 3 for the smalls total: 8%

The Conditional increases:

  1. Horror’s Flight Unique Gloves. &gt; 100% increased cull threshold while at max Fear Incarnate stacks. (Deli boss drop) Also grants Crushing Fear skill. (for the martial artist this will likely be our best skill option.)
  2. Finish them &gt; notable on tree 40% vs immobilized. (only comes into play vs Rares/Uniques.)
  3. Cull the Hordes &gt; 40% vs Rare uniques.
  4. Culling strike II &gt; Support gem. 20% if we’ve culled with supported skill in last 20 seconds

So our totals look like this:
White: 319% increased cull threshold.
Magic: 319% increased Cull Threshold.
Rare: 399% increased Cull threshold. (359% if not immobilized.)
Unique: 399% increased Cull Threshold. (359% if not immobilized.)

What that translates to: (using the formula [Cull threshold] * (1+[Increased Cull Threshold]/100)
White Monsters Culled at: 35*(1+319⁄100) = 146.65% life. (culled at full life)
Magic Monsters: 20*4.19= 83.8%
Rare monsters: 10*4.99 = 49.9% … 10*4.59= 45.9% (if not immobilized)
Uniques Monsters: 5*4.99 = 24.95% … 5*4.59= 22.95% (if not immobilized)

Ok all of that is the same for both classes.

So we have to look at ascendancies to see which is actually better.

Witchhunter has the following notables:

  • Weapon Master (2pointer) &gt; useless
  • Pitiless Killer (2p)&gt; Culling Strike. (we take this one)
  • Judge Jury Executioner (4p locked behind Pitiless) &gt; Decimating Strike (this is the one we want from this class)
  • Witchbane / No Mercy &gt; Concentration based. Technically More damage, but we don’t care about damage we care about maximizing one shot kills. So we’re not taking these.
  • Zealous Inquisition &gt; 10% chance for explode. This benefits our Map Clear Speed but does nothing for bosses. It is one we Could take.
  • Obsessive Rituals &gt; Sorc Ward applying only to ele damage. 50% less AR/EV. (even with the big AR/EV loss its still extremely powerful as a defensive layer. Probably take it.)
  • Ceremonial Ablution &gt; Sorc Ward applies to Phys / Chaos as well. (If we don’t take the explode we take this one.)

Ok So Witchhunter has 1 notable that we really care about. Decimating Strike. What does decimating strike do? It makes Monsters who are on full life lose 5-30% of their life BEFORE DAMAGE BEFORE CULL. Effectively meaning that we only need a Cull Threshold of 95% to cull 100% of the time. Assuming each whole number between 5 and 30 has an equal probability of rolling. With the above thresholds we could NEVER get Rares/Uniques to cull in one hit. We already Cull White in one hit. So this only applies to magic monsters. we need to roll a 17 or higher to Cull them in one hit. 14⁄26 possible rolls are 17 or higher. giving us an estimated: 53.85% chance to cull Magic Monsters at full life with the above numbers. This is what makes Witch Hunter Powerful as a Cull Threshold Mapper. It does improve our time to kill on bosses/rares as well but we ain’t instantly culling them. Rares we only really have to worry 55% of their life at worst. Decimating strike brings this value to 50-25% Depending on its roll. This is HUGE for a cull threshold Stacker is why people do Witch Hunter Cull Stacking.

Next let’s look at Martial Artist:

For this class we’re going to want to use Crushing Fear from the gloves. As it Always culls, It triggers an AoE on cull (which also always culls). This makes up for not having the Explode passive from Witch Hunter. We could use Killing Palm instead. Which grants us a Power Charge when it culls. this is relevant below.

Notables

  • Hollow Form Technique &gt; Trash for us if we’re using Crushing Fear because skills granted by items cant be socketed in it, but we could use Killing Palm instead. If we’re opting for Killing Palm on Hollow Form. Hollow form will consume Power Charges granted from this skill to summon additional images. This might be better than using Crushing Fear. We lose the AoE but gain extra Images. which might be a net positive. Only in game testing will show. (So this is a Variable Maybe take, Either way its comparable to the Witch Hunter’s Explode for our purposes.)
  • Hollow Focus &gt; Summons a Bell that’s Primed for Stun, hits always crit, Primed for cull. Also does an AoE. This is our most important pick on the Martial artist. Two of our Cull Threshold increases require us to cull Frequently: Fear Incarnate and Culling Strike II. The Bell is summoned every 2.05 seconds. Fear Incarnate needs us to cull once every 10 seconds to maintain its stacks. Culling Strike II requires us to cull every 20 seconds. With this skill we can maintain both even during Boss fights. (witchhunter can’t do this, without being forced to use things like Tempest Bell OR Ice Crystals.) Which means VS Bosses Martial artist has 120% higher Increased Cull Threshold VS Witch hunter UNLESS the witch hunter is routinely using Ice Crystals / Tempest bell.
  • Hollow Resonance &gt; Attaches a bell to our back. Does AoE when we crit. We’re not crit focused, but its technically extra damage and has increased Stun Buildup which does support the Cull if Immobilized. (this is a fall back pick for one of our other options listed later)
  • Runic Meridan &gt; Extra Rune slots. We take it (we could sac this one if needed for another)
  • Martial Adept &gt; Gain Extra Combo, Gain ES recharge Rate based on combo lost. We dont care. we’re not taking.
  • Martial Master &gt; All hits build Combo, Don’t lose combo for weapon slot. Same as before We’re not particularly spending combo or caring about combo. We Skip
  • Way of the Mountain &gt; This gives Damage. This gives Defense. We take it. (bringing us to 3 picks)
  • Fist of Stone &gt; this is arguably the Strongest Ascendancy Node in the game. However, this is the one that we have unknowns on. Because we are using a Unique Pair of gloves. There is a high chance this Node will BRICK our build. If Horror’s Flight Loses the Fear Incarnate mod, we Can’t take this one. If it buffs it, then its a snap pick. but we HAVE to have the Fear incarnate Mod. Its our second largest Cull Threshold increase (technically the same amount as Myris, BUT its stack based)

So our outcome for martial artist is: Hollow Focus &gt; Runic Meridan &gt; Way of the Mountain and one of the following: Fist of Stone, Hollow Resonance, Hollow Form.

Conclusion:

I Explained in the Hollow Focus Section, but i want to reiterate it.
Witch Hunter Loses 120% increased Cull threshold against Bosses unless they bring something to the fight to cull. Martial Artist Just passively maintains the stacks. Which brings me to the conclusion that Martial Artist is better for a Cull Threshold Stacker than Witch Hunter. Witch Hunter provides 3 things that we might care about: Explode, Decimating strike, and the freedom to use WHATEVER attack/Spell we want with all attacks/Spells having “Culling Strike” However, They are forced to bring Ice Crystals or Tempest bell locking them into Crossbow OR Quarterstaffs OR Spells. Which pretty much negates the benefits of the culling strike node.
