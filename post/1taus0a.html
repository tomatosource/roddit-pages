Melee feels much weaker than ranged in early leveling…. am I missing something?

I’m pretty new to Path of Exile 2, and before patch 0.5 started, I wanted to get a better feel for the game and form my own opinion.

So I tried every class up to the end of Act 1.

What I noticed is that the “ranged” classes felt much easier for leveling , faster clear speed and noticeably higher damage overall.

This made me wonder…, shouldn’t melee classes have much better survivability, especially now in 0.5 when you can sometimes be fighting multiple bosses at once (2–6), on top of all the dangerous modifiers and mechanics?
