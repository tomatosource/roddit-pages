The problem with low level uniques

So im playing ssfhc as warrior, trying all kinds of funny mixes for campaign.

And i get: Brynhands Mark - unique wooden club in act 1, item level 11.

So the club is interesting for shield warriors because it has double stun buildup. But it has a downside of: 20% reduced attack speed.

This is a moment that would give a lot of dopamine in a game like d2, im low level and got a low level unique drop. Only issue is, not only am i going to replace this low level unique a few levels from here (with a rare), i possibly wont even equip it due to the downside.

I dont understand why the downside has to be so strong. Its taking away from the joys of low level adventures and small dopamine spikes.

Equipping a unique over a rare already comes as a downside because youre giving up affixes that come on rares in order to get unique affixes. But why do we have to have a downside on top of it?

D2 did this right imho
