(O.C) comparing EVERY US president to an NBA player

Look before I start, this will be problematic because, even more than nba players, presidents are ALL very problematic and represented a country that has been problematic since it’s founding. We are in an era now where a lot of these presidents are getting their legacies re-examined and I think that’s great. In my opinion, no nba player in history deserves to be compared to Ronald Reagan. nothing here is meant to be 1:1

George Washington: Magic Johnson

The boring answer is someone like George Mikan or maybe Wilt, but Magic is a face that will never be replaced. no one is arguing he’s the greatest, but his importance can’t be overstated.

John Adams: Draymond Green

combative, clearly intelligent, but clashes constantly with the people around them. legacy will always be complicated.

Thomas Jefferson: Kobe Bryant

Massive impact. influenced every president that came after him like kobe is talked about by players today. problematic sex stuff, to say the least.

James Madison: Chris Paul

Short in stature, but massive in influence. Master of laws and knows them inside out. overcame all that to be one of the most influential players of the last 20 years.

James Monroe: Tony Parker

proved reliable in big moments, helped extend that Spurs era and had big moments but still wasn’t flashy or the face of that period.

John Quincy Adams: Shane Battier

JQA is the non-stat all-star. his arc after his presidency defined his legacy much like people writing about Shane Battier helped define his.

Andrew Jackson: Paul Pierce

look I’m not going to say a lot of good things about Andrew Jackson, but he is the most likely president to get stabbed and still go play an NBA game.

Martin Van Buren: Mario Chalmers

was not a big guy(by nba standards) was not a great player, but was there with big names, had big time connections at least just like Van Buren

William Henry Harrison: Greg Oden

We’ll never get to see his full potential sadly.

John Tyler: Jeremy Lin

John Tyler became president on accident. he wasn’t meant to be the guy. Jeremy Lin’s run was not expected either. Tyler set the precedent for the vice president became the guy after the guy dies. crazy stuff.

James K. Polk: Kawhi Leonard

Polk came in, with 4 goals in mind did them all in one term and left. had more impact than almost every other president. Kawhi Leonard went to Toronto for one year, won the title and then left.

Zachary Taylor: Derrick Rose

Zach Taylor’s presidency was 16 months and we have to wonder what could have been. likewise with Derrick Rose, who won MVP and we were excited to see his time on top and then it sadly faded.

Millard Fillmore: Michael Carter-Williams

Another guy who wasn’t supposed to be the guy but looked reliable at first. America thought the compromise of 1850 was gonna be a big accomplishment. Sixers fans probably thought they had a guy who could be something on the eventual team they were building in the process. both somehow were let downs.

Franklin Pierce: Kwame Brown

Franklin Pierce is known for being a handsome disappointment. He looked the part but didn’t live up. Kwame Brown honestly looked like he could have been something. His draft comps compared him to KG and Chris Webber. He was neither of those players.

James Buchanan: D’lo Russell

Seems to be on teams just at the worst point and right before a star era. Buchanan was president right before the Civil war, and his passivity, much like D’lo’s snapchat with Nick Young, really divided the team.

Abraham Lincoln: Bill Russell

I know people will complain I didn’t say Lincoln’s grandson Chet, but Bill Russell just feels nice here, and it’s the only place I’d be willing to put him personally.

Andrew Johnson: Latrell Sprewell

Johnson was the first US President to be impeached because he was combattive and stubborn. Latrell Sprewell was also combative and stubborn but he was the one who tried to impeach his coach…by choking him.

Ulysses S. Grant: Russell Westbrook

Grant’s success as a general wasn’t built off being the best. He was just crazy and bold af. Russell Westbrook vibes for sure. definitely flawed as a long term option and didn’t age perfectly but the chaos was something we needed once upon a time.

Rutherford B. Hayes: Chauncey Billups

did Hayes deserve the presidency in 1876? it’s disputed. Is chauncey the best player on the 04 pistons? again disputed. did attempt to right the ship and was a calming presence after the scandals of the previous presidency. I’m sure Chauncey did a lot as a quarterback for the personalities of the 04 pistons.

James Garfield: Brandon Roy

James Garfield could have been special. underrated president. Destiny of the Republic is one of my favorite books on a president if you’re looking for a rec. Brandon Roy was something special too. Tragic.

Chester A. Arthur: Zach Randolph

Arthur was a part of the corrupt new york political machine but after he became president, he actually worked to clean up the system and dismantled it. Randolph was a guy who got into a lot of fights on the Blazers and was called immature, and he punched teammates but he was the heart of Grit & Grind.

Grover Cleveland: Lebron James

Much like Lebron, after 4 years away, America went back to Cleveland.

Benjamin Harrison: Khris Middleton

Efficient, not a bad president, but lacked charisma and was in the shadow of much bigger personalities. Middleton was overshadowed by Giannis like Harison was by Grover Cleveland.

William McKinley: Penny Hardaway

Had a run there where he looked like he would be the guy. Definitely achieved a lot. the spanish-american war, Penny went to the finals with shaq but was overshadowed by who shaq played with after Penny (Kobe) like Mckinley was by his vice president(teddy).

Theodore Roosevelt: Michael Jordan

Look, personality wise, was problematic by today’s standards but god was he everyone’s favorite for a long time. absolutely insane people with insane personalities who defined America.

William Howard Taft: Kevin Love

Taft is famously fat but he was also given depression by teddy Roosovelt like Love was by Lebron. Taft would also probably be good at memes.

Woodrow Wilson: Kyrie Irving

high minded folks who fancy themselves intellectuals but then they talk about their views and you cringe.

Warren G. Harding: Jontay Porter

look, Harding was corrupt and Porter was banned from the league. not all of these can be that deep.

Calvin Coolidge: Al Horford

Silent Cal and Horford, both no nonsense, low drama stars who aren’t there to be famous and are just there for their goals.

Herbert Hoover: CJ McCollum

look, it’s not Hoover’s fault the great depression happened but he deserves some blame for it. McCollum was NBPA president when the new CBA took place and that’s been a weird thing.

Franklin D. Roosevelt: Kareem Abdul-Jabbar

Was president from the dark ages of the great depression all the way til WW2. Kareem played against both Wilt and Michael Jordan. he was able to see two eras. Lebron would be here but the Cleveland joke was there so i took it.

Harry S. Truman: Kevin Durant

Harry Truman dropped the bomb and was unpopular for the destruction in his wake and the cold war he birthed. KD joined the warriors which was a nuclear level event for the NBA.

Dwight D. Eisenhower: Tim Duncan

Military discipline, kept the ship right in a hard time. played the game well and played to win, nothing flashy. Eisenhower wasn’t Patton or MacArthur. he was a heroic general who focused on success at all costs.

John F. Kennedy: Bill Walton

Walton was the top of the league even with his body failing him. Walton’s run at the top was cut short just like Kennedy.

Lyndon B. Johnson: Shaq

Both guys were absolute huge personalities who did jokes that seem extremely inappropriate now.

Richard M. Nixon: Gilbert Arenas

massive rise to the top, and scandals cut his career short. self-inflicted wounds.

Gerald R. Ford: Steve Kerr

stablizing force, not flashy, definitely not a star, but was there.

Jimmy Carter: Dirk

high character, respected globally. looked back on fondly.

Ronald Reagan: Steph Curry

Paradigm shifting players where everyone tried to emulate. splash man vs trickle down.

George H. W. Bush: Dame

in the image of steph but different. overshadowed.

Bill Clinton: Charles Barkley

Charismatic, loud, brash. southern guys who’s voices charmed a lot of people.

George W. Bush: James Harden

James Harden doesn’t deserve this but it’s really the only one i can think of. widely despised figures in texas with evil sidekicks(Morey, Cheney) who had a huge impact on the future of the nba/america.

Barack Obama: Blake Griffin

Came in with massive expectations. and it honestly felt like he lived up to them for a while but we were in denial. still not bad. we miss those days. was a lot more fun then what’s going on now. Wish we were in the era of lobs and dunks instead of an era of threes and efficiency.

Donald Trump: Evan Turner EDIT: Karl Malone

According to Evan Turner he was the best. according to history, he was not. personality bigger than production. people regret taking Turner so high in the draft.

EDIT: Actually it’s Karl Malone. for obvious reasons.

Joe Biden: Mike Conley

Long career, was steady. never the best in the league but was trustworthy and got the job done. Age is clearly showing but we respect him for it.
