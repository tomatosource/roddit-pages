Why later unlocked skills are not viable as main clear skills in endgame?

While we wait for the Druid, this is one of my main wishes.

At endgame, skills that become your main endgame skill always seems to be something thats unlocked by gem level 5 (3rd row on the gem cutting skill page). I haven’t played every weapon, but skills that have taken over - Arc, Spark, LA, LS, tempest flurry, ice strike, frost bolt, fireball (I’m sure I’m forgetting some).

It seems like later skills are generally used as applying debuffs, or single target damage skills like comet or wind serpents furry or lightning conduction. They never seem like main skills.

I used to feel very excited when I unlocked a new line of skills, only to be disappointed and then just upgrade my current main skill and move on. I know of course supports and lineage supports change the skills a lot, but why is it that later skills feel so lackluster?

Can we have skills that unlock at level 13 be game changing and not just for the incoming talisman, but also for all the existing weapons as well.

PS- if I made a mistake or a wrong assumption, please point it out, but I want the thread to be a fun discussion and not an argument.
