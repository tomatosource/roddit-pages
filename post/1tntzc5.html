The New York Knicks finish with the all-time biggest point differential in an Eastern Conference Finals sweep

New York finishes with a point differential of 77 over the Cavs, the highest in an ECF sweep all time, & the third highest point differential ever in an Eastern Conference Finals, with only the 15-16 Cavs (93 pt diff. over Toronto, won 4-2) & the 16-17 Cavs (100 pt diff. over Boston, won 4-1) ahead of them.
