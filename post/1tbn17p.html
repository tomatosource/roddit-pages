My feedback so far after playing warrior every season.

Maces vs. Shapeshifter Bear: Design Comparison and Suggestions

Maces

  • Full of noob traps, both in feel and in reality
  • No straightforward paths to power or relevancy
  • Effects and gems are hard to quantify (e.g. Aftershock, Ancestral)
  • Unnecessary complexity hidden in obscure multi-part interactions
  • Easy to invest heavily and get nothing in return
  • Hidden debuffs (e.g. dual-wield attack speed penalty) and strange negatives on nodes
  • Only viable at extreme wealth (mirrors) or with perfectly optimal pathing

Shapeshifter Bear

  • Almost no noob traps; everything feels like progress
  • Straightforward paths to power and relevancy
  • Effects and gems are intuitive (stacking Rage = good; Increased Duration = good; Walking Calamity = good)
  • Interactions are obvious and self-contained
  • Easy to invest and see massive returns; all nodes feel great
  • Essentially no frustration
  • Very strong with just one affordable unique; doesn’t scale much further with wealth or optimal pathing

Suggestions (Smith of Kitava Main)

1. Bring the Bear’s Passive Identity to Warrior

The bear’s passive bundle (Rage, Armour applying to Elemental Damage, passive Armour per level, Endurance Charges per Rage spent) was a huge success. Transpose this to Warrior via new skills:

  • Bear Heart (100 Spirit) - Grants all passive bear effects when using a mace, sword, axe or shield. Cannot use Bear skills.
  • Warrior Strength (30 Spirit) - If Strength is your highest attribute: +10 Armour per level, +0.05% max life regen per level, 30% Armour applies to Elemental Damage.
  • Enduring (30 Spirit) - If Strength is your highest attribute: gain 1 Endurance Charge per 15 Rage spent.
  • Against the Chaos (30 Spirit) - If Strength is your highest attribute: 33% of Armour applies to Chaos Damage, receive 33% less damage from DoTs.
  • Fire Combat (30 Spirit) - If Strength is your highest attribute: 25% of incoming Physical Damage taken as Fire Damage, gain +25% as extra Fire Damage.
  • Shapeshifter Heart (Keystone) - Always considered in Shapeshifter form without actually transforming. Cannot equip Talismans. +8 maximum Rage.

2. Item Changes for a Bear-Like Progression Feel

  • New endgame uniques targeting distinct playstyles: Glory, Fire, Fissure, Ancestral Cry, Slam, Jump, etc.
  • New meaningful damage sources that scale off base Armour and Strength requirements of gloves, helmet and boots.
  • Mace early skills buffed around immediate impact for the average player. Big slams, fast attacks, rage spending for sustained destruction.

3. Smith of Kitava Specific Changes

  • Allow socketing a unique body armour into the ascendancy tree to copy one of its lines onto your normal body armour. Only 1 line.
  • Rework Smith and Companion nodes around Ancestral Cry, Glory, Fire or Thorns.
  • Rework Fire Spell on Hit to trigger based on hit power rather than hit frequency. One powerful strike should proc it multiple times, not the current counterintuitive frequency-based model.

So far I think that with those changes a lot of the issues I observe would not exist anymore. There has never been a big patch in this game where armour was not buffed, and I feel Warrior was launched in such a strangely weak state that it surprised me when I saw how good it felt to play Bear compared to Mace. Everything just… fit together. Then I decided to try Mace again and caught myself in the same loop of buying a ton of items, building a bunch of support gems, and still not dealing the correct damage. Dying too much. Not having enough Strength for this or that, etc.

Its extremely EASY to waste your time with maces. Not so much with Bear. Thank you for reading! This is my feedback!
