[Fischer] The Hawks were offered a package of Immanuel Quickley and picks during last years trade deadline which was swiftly rejected. The Raptors still continued to maintain an interest in Trae Young this year but the Hawks value long term flexibility

Sources say that Toronto actually offered a package built around Quickley and draft capital for Young before last February’s trade deadline and maintained a level of interest in Young this season. Yet sources say that the Hawks had no interest in a Quickley-headlined package given the future money he’s owed. The Hawks staunchly prioritized the ample flexibility they now have before the the Feb. 5 trade buzzer and possibly into free agency next summer by taking back CJ McCollum’s expiring $30.7 million salary as the key return in their Young deal.
