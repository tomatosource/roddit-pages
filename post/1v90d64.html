[SPORT 24] Russell Westbrook: Reports in Europe for Panathinaikos, Hapoel, Dubai

Russell Westbrook has put in a lot of mileage in the NBA, competing for years as one of the league’s top players.

Now 37 years old, he is looking for the next stop in his career, and reports from Europe link him to EuroLeague teams, specifically Panathinaikos AKTOR, Hapoel Tel Aviv, and Dubai BC.
