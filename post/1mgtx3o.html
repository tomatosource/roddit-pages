Fenerbahce reportedly close to signing NBA swingman Brandon Boston

Source

Update: According to further reports released now by Bballmaniacs, Brandon Boston signed a one-year deal worth up to $2 million with Fenerbahce.

Fenerbahce Beko is very close to signing NBA swingman Brandon Boston Jr. according to Bballmaniacs.

Boston, 23, averaged 10.7 points, 3.2 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 42 games with the New Orleans Pelicans last season. It was his fourth year in the NBA after spending three seasons with the Los Angeles Clippers.

If the deal is finalized, then Boston will play in EuroLeague for the first time in his career by joining the reigning champions.

Boston may become the second new signing of Fenerbahce this summer after forward Mikael Jantunen. The Turkish powerhouse parted ways with Final Four MVP, forward Nigel Hayes-Davis, who moved to the Phoenix Suns and combo guard Marko Guduric who signed with Olimpia Milano.
