24 hrs of t9 juicing without a spreadsheet

  • pros

  • fast mapping for realistic or budget builds.

  • fast gold farming. Nearly as much in quantity as t15 but time spent in map is probably halved because you ignore gear drops and focus on currency and speed. So in a way gold per hour is higher. -it’s fun blasting -honestly feels like I’m getting more raw currency from exalts, chaos, anul, divine and fractures than at t15s.

-cons -lack of valuable base types and potential gear upgrades. -higher tier currency doesn’t drop(this is in some ways an upside as you get more frequent exalt and chaos) -xp gain is nearly non existent if you are mid 90s. -death feels worse than in t15s. (Only happened once because I was stupid)

I’ve been blasting and surprised myself with how much fun it is steamrolling everything. I heard people talking about t7’s in chat for farming and decided to try it. I didn’t have any t7’s so just grabbed the t9 waystones I had. 6 modded 3x paranoia or greed depending on my tablet setup or the % of iir or rm on the waystone. Usually 2 paranoia at least.

For tablets I went 3x rarity with breach and tried a few rare monster ones. Had very good results with any setup such as this.

167% character rarity on LA deadeye. Out of my first 17 maps I made more divine, total than I had in dozens of t15s the days before this. I enjoyed it more than occasionally bricking a map and it was so much faster. In terms of raw numbers I have no idea how much better it is as currency farm. With small sample size I can say it’s good, feels good and if you just want to farm some currency fast it’s very cheap entry. After my first 17 I bought another 10 t9 waystones on trade for 15-20ex each added delirium and still came out with many more times the currency I spent in profit. Highly recommended and am continuing t9 farming today because I like it and its working for me.

I’m sustaining enough waystones to prep 10 at a time and I’m also selling spares on trade for 20 ex a piece which is a nice bonus. I understand there might be a conflict of interest here but I am already selling all of my waystones very quickly and wanted to share what I’ve been doing differently with everyone else as I was originally struggling to get juice in this latest patch.
