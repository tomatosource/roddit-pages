Goal is to spam the new Brutus lead sprinkler with high enough STR to trigger its skill molten shower consistently.

This skill being a projectile means that it’s affected by Tactician’s pin.

With Irongrasp it’s eaasy to grab a lot of %dmg, and even more so with constricting command (4 fewer foes to be surrounded).

Off hand could be either chernobog for extra fire damage, or go all out with dance with death (25% more skill speed if off hand empty).

Defenses can be somewhat solved with black scythe training (1% increased energy shield per 2 STR) if we put a big ES helmet instead of constricting command, we’ll have to see. Pinning foes is also a very good defensive layer in itself though
