Exactly as stated in the title.

I’m curious if GGG could include bestiary as a mechanic, specifically to update it with animal companions/mounts.

Rhoa exists, but I feel like it could be its own league mechanic using the Boars and the rolling Lobsters, using both the bestiary league mechanic and wisps as unique modifiers.

I feel like you could utilize and hunt down beasts that can give you materials to ritually craft equipment for your mount/animal companion and allow a new/old style of crafting, beast crafting.

I could easily see attaching Resistance swapping by hunting specific beasts from specific regions (Fire/Desert, Mountain/lightning and water/cold, swamp/chaos etc) and allowing for target farming of specific beasts you couldn’t do in poe1 by region.

You can even allow for niche crafting via new beast bosses and move Rhoa from a specific weapon/attribute archetype to a multi-attribute puzzle for players to solve? Hell, you can make the equipment be ward-based to allow for some new defensive options.

You can even give Wisps a new use by giving captured mounts/beasts a unique modifier that can affect them while in use or add a specific modifier in the beastcrafting ritual.
