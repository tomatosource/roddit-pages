Increased Cast Speed should be a prefix modifier for gloves…

If there can be four different kinds of attack modifiers as prefixes on gloves for martial classes, there can at least be increased cast speed for casters. It makes sense: casters are waving their hands around to cast spells, or channeling energy through their wands/staves, which they are holding in their hands… better gloves should facilitate this process! Plus, it would make it less difficult to find gloves that have no wasted stats for casters, because right now it’s really difficult to find them without some kind of attack damage modifier.
