JT’s nickname should be The Standard, not The Anomaly

A lot of people online like to use “The Standard” as more of a jab than an actual nickname, but I think it makes for a far better, more accurate, and honestly cooler nickname than The Anomaly.

Here’s my pitch:

Tatum as a player is far from an “anomaly.” When I think of anomaly, I think of guys like Wemby, Shaq, KP, Giannis, freaks of nature that can be clearly set apart from their peers before you even dive into their skill set. Tatum is a 6’8”, versatile wing. He’s exactly what most teams look for in a wing in today’s nba.

And that’s exactly why I think “The Standard” fits. He’s literally the gold standard for the modern wing; an elite scorer who can pass like a 1, rebound like a 5, and defend everything in between. He’s guarded and played at every position on the floor, and although he’s had a bit of a down year in terms of 3-point shooting, he’s proven to be elite in this regard as well as his career has progressed.

Sure, you can say that the combination of these qualities is what makes him “anomalous,” but I think that rather than this setting him apart, it sets him above, and that makes “The Standard” the more fitting name.

Plus, “The Standard” just sounds so classy and badass, I love it.
