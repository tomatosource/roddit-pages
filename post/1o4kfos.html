Exceptional Items are fun, but does it hurt double-handled items in endgame?

With the introduction of Exceptional items, you can now potentially get 3 + 3 sockets on your mainhands and offhands, a total of 6 sockets. While Expetional double-handed items might give you 4 sockets, there exists a 2-socket differential.

I suppose the issue has been present since items were always able to vaal +1 socket, but instead of 4 vs 3 sockets, now it’s 6 vs 4, the differential is actually higher. Does this mean that double-handed items will always be worse than single-handed ones? 🤔

Would granting Exceptional double-handed weapons 4 sockets be okay (disregarding potential balance issue with dual-wielding two double-handed weapons for now)? Or is it okay for the gap to exist and have it be a trade-off about crafting cost? 🤔
