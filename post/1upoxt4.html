Some handy hotkeys on KBM

This is nothing fancy, but I realize many players don’t know some of these. Listing them below, hope you find some of them useful

Ctrl click on a league hub icon to quick open portal

In the atlas view if you hold ctrl while clicking on the monastery icon, it directly opens the portal on your map device. This means you don’t have to wait for the camera to pan over then click traverse.

Ctrl Shift click to bypass stash tab affinity.

While ctrl click on an item drops it into the stash tab with a corresponding affinity, you can override that by also holding shift. Most useful when managing maps or tablets.

Hold shift while applying a currency allow you to click repeatedly.

This is quite basic and it is helpful for things like maxing quality, alch-ing tablets or batch corrupting maps. Hopefully anyone who is chaos spamming knows this…

Ctrl Right click to take all stacks of an item.

Say you have 5 stacks of chaos orbs from Ange merchant tab sales. Instead of clicking the stacks one at a time, ctrl right click transfers all the chaos orbs to your backpack. You can do it again to transfer all into your stash too.

This also works when removing item from stash or when removing item from currency exchange.

Ctrl click on an NPC to quick identify / disenchant / currency exchange

On Dory/Hooded one, you can ctrl click them to identify items instead of opening up the dialog menu and clicking “identify” manually. With Ange, control click opens up currency exchange.

If you need to open up the buy / sell window, it is alt click.

Note that for Ange merchant tab, it is ctrl+alt+click opens
