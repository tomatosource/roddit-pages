My Dumb Brain: My picks for the last 5 drafts

I absolutely love the NBA draft and I pay attention to it year round. I’m always looking at college highlights and following along with where we may land in the draft. I wanted to look at the last 5 drafts and share my thinking, as well as see if any of the guys I liked at the time panned out. Please do the same, i want to see if you called it on anyone in their years!

&nbsp;

2020 NBA Draft

&nbsp;

14th pick

Who we took: Aaron Nesmith

Who I wanted: Aaron Nesmith, Cole Anthony, Josh Green, Kira Lewis Jr.

&nbsp;

Aaron Nesmith was the pick and he’s turned into a fine player in Indiana. He’s a rare combination of hard nosed athlete who can hit 3’s at a high clip. He’s been injured in Indy this season, but I expect a long career playing with the energy he plays with.

&nbsp;

This is less of a guessing game about who the Celtics draft, and more about did they become NBA level players. The 3 guys I wanted at this point all turned into NBA level guys which is awesome. I saw Cole Anthony as a 6th man, and I was a little higher on Josh Green than he’s been, but he’s absolutely an NBA player. I was dead wrong about Kira Lewis Jr. I liked how fast he was and thought he could really build into something.

&nbsp;

26th + 30th pick

Who we took: Payton Pritchard, traded #30

Who I wanted: Xavier Tillman, Desmond Bane, Leandro Balmaro, Tyrell Terry, Theo Maledon, Tyler Bey, Vernon Carey JR.

&nbsp;

There were a bunch of guys I liked at the end of the draft and would have been happy with any of these guys. Most mocks had Pritchard going 40+ and wasn’t someone I would have taken had he even been available at #47. Just wasn’t on my radar. I’m almost always opting towards size and feel like a shot blocker or a wing with a shakey 3 is a better 2nd round investment than an undersized guard with limited athleticism. Prichard was obviously the right choice. Bane hurts but you can’t be mad at all at how well this pick turned out.

&nbsp;

With 2 picks most expected one of these to get traded, which is what happened. But that didn’t stop me from getting excited about a bunch of different guys. Xavier Tillman actually ended up on the roster. I saw him as a more NBA ready pick with a strong body the C’s could use ASAP. Where he’s not a shot blocker, he really needs to start hitting 3’s to stay on the floor, and that was the concern coming in. I listed the players in order of how I would have drafted them, I did alright here.

&nbsp;

47th pick

Who we took: Yam Madar

Who I wanted: Killian Tillie

&nbsp;

Madar is a guy who I had never heard of pre draft. Closest NBA comp i could imagine is Tomas Satoransky, where he’s a bit undersized for a wing, has some PG skills but plays more like a 2, and makes up some ground with tenacity. C’s were definitely looking for someone to have sit overseas after already having multiple draft picks and not wanting to add 3-4 rookies to the roster.

&nbsp;

Kevin Love was a player I really appreciated at the time so stretch 4’s were something I thought the C’s could really use. He spent a few years in Memphis before leaving the league. I was super excited when he joined our summer league team. He hasn’t done much of anything in the league, but neither has Madar.

&nbsp;

2021

&nbsp;

45th pick

Who we took: Juhann Begarin

Who i wanted: Ayo Dosunmu, Luka Garza

&nbsp;

Juhann Begarin is another guy I hadn’t really heard of pre-draft. Stevens liked his athletic profile and saw a guy he could stash overseas. He’s still only 22 years old but it doesn’t seem like he’s developing much or lighting it up over there. The league is at the point where if you’re an athletic defender, but can’t do anything on offense, you’re unplayable. We currently have Jaden Springer on the roster who has a similar build, and I would say is better on both ends than Begarin, and we’re not playing him. I don’t see him coming over at this point. I will say, it’s not like this draft had crazy success after this pick. Aaron Wiggins is the only guy drafted after Begarin who is contributing to a good team 4 years later.

&nbsp;

Ayo Dosunmu was my guy here but it was super unlikely he was going to drop this far, and he didn’t. BUT HE CAME CLOSE and hit the middle of the 2nd round. He’s done great as a reserve in Chicago, also being a spot starter. The other guy I had my eyes on in the 2nd is Luka Garza, who has been ok. As far as 2nd round picks go, you can do a lot worse than a guy who puts in the work and stays ready. They’re good for practice, they’re good for morale, and they’re good for those stretches where you have a guy go down and need a minute eater.

Side note, this is the year an undrafted Sam Hauser fell into our laps. There’s shooting in the 2nd round if you can find it!

&nbsp;

2022

&nbsp;

53rd pick

Who we took: JD Davison

Who I wanted: Trevion Williams, Jordan Hall, Gabe Brown

&nbsp;

JD Davison was the pick here and I don’t hate the raw athlete pick this late in the game. I broke this down over summer league, I think he could be a solid player on a team that needs someone to feed their young star big man. I feel like he could have gotten minutes in San Antonio feeding Wemby, and he could honestly get minutes in Washington today, trying to set up Alexander Sarr. Boston just doesn’t have space or need for him, where we have multiple Guards and Wings who can handle. He’s a great athlete, and a passable pick and roll PG. That being said, we have White, Jrue, Brown, Tatum, and Pritchard who can all run the pick and roll, and then also launch a 3, JD can’t do that. He’s not bad on D, but because of his size he needs to bring insane Avery Bradley like tenacity. Guys like TJ McConnell, Hose Alverado, and Patrick Beverly are how guys like Davison can get on the court, and he just doesn’t bring that energy to the floor on D. I don’t see him making the main roster, and hope he gets another chance on another team next season.

&nbsp;

Trevion Williams would have been my pick here. He has done absolutely nothing in the league so thats an L for me here. He’s an offensive big who can pass and maybe hit a 3 occasionally. He went undrafted and i was hyped to see him on our summer league team. Jordan Hall I saw as an Evan Turner style guard. He’s a bit bigger, is a decent passer, and was crafty with it around the rim. He got some time with San Antonio but didn’t get much run. Gabe Brown was my standard, “Just take the 6’8’’ defender and hope the jumper is translates”. Brown signed with Toronto in undrafted but didn’t make the opening day roster.

&nbsp;

2023

&nbsp;

25th pick

Who we took: Traded back for Jordan Walsh and a handful and 2nds

Who i wanted: Olivier Maxence-Prosper

&nbsp;

I genuinely feel like Olivier Maxence-Prosper was the target here, and Dallas snagged him the pick before we drafted. This set off a cavalcade of trades that landed Boston Jordan Walsh at the 38th pick, and a handful of 2nd’s that the Celtics used to facilitate trades around the deadline involving Xavier Tillman, Jaden Springer, and Delano Banton. I had literally nobody else queued up for this pick. It’s year 2 for OMP and he’s not a regular rotation guy. He’s ironically in the same space as Jordan Walsh where he’s a young player on a contender and he’s not getting room to grow. He’s a solid versatile defender who needs to find some way to contribute on offense.

&nbsp;

We ended up taking Jordan Walsh who is a less heralded version of OMP so I was happy coming out of the draft with a similar player and a handful of 2nd round picks. Walsh is a little younger than OMP and I’m not surprised he’s not contributing yet.

&nbsp;

2024

&nbsp;

30th pick

Who we took: Baylor Scheierman

Who i wanted: DaRon Holmes, Ryan Dunn, Tyler Kolek

&nbsp;

This is definitely into “too early to tell” territory, but Scheierman wouldn’t have been my pick, I had a handful of guys I would have taken after him. So far he’s looked a bit overwhelmed by the speed of the NBA game. He’s rushing his 3 point shots instead of making a read. Supposedly, he’s a guy who can hit 3’s, but also run pick and roll a bit and make the smart pass. He just has seemed absolutely out of his league, even in summer league he didn’t seem like he was ready.

&nbsp;

My first 2 picks were not available when pick #30 rolled around. DaRon Holmes I was excited for, but got injured before the season started, which sucks. Holmes was drafted by Denver. He reminds me of a late career Al Horford, where he can shoot a bit, pass, and be versatile defensively. Ryan Dunn had a great start to the season but has faded a bit since opening night. Similar to Walsh he’s all D and no O, he’ll occasionally outhustle someone for a put back but he’s super limited offensively. Tyler Kolek also would have been on my radar here. He reminds me of Tomas Satoransky or TJ McConnell where he can play PG, hit the 3, and plays with a bit of an edge. He wouldn’t have played much this year, and he’s not playing much in New York where they already have Brunson, McBride, and Payne at PG. He’d be buried here too though, especially with PP blowing up this year.

&nbsp;

54th pick

Who we took: Anton Watson

Who i wanted: Kevin McCullar, Ulrich Chomche, Melvin Ajinca

&nbsp;

Anton Watson was nowhere on my radar this draft. I absolutely loved him in summer league and he was my pick for the 15th roster spot going into the season. Defensive versatility was great in summer league, where he guarded 3-5, and hit the occasional 3. I’m hopeful he’ll make the jump to the main roster, especially with the Celtics needing reinforcements. Horford, Holiday, and Porzingis won’t be on the roster 2-3 seasons from now and it’s unlikely the Celtics can maneuver cap space to trade for replacements. These guys play key minutes and of our farm system i like Watson more than most.

&nbsp;

Kevin McCullar was my NBA ready guy I thought would hit the ground running. Similar to Kolek McCullar also ended up on the Knicks and is on a 2 way. He’s a solid utility guy who can defend, shoot, and make decent reads. I’d say he’s probably more ready than Walsh right now. The Knicks have Hart, Bridges, and Onunoby eating any minutes McCullar would fill, and they’re content sitting on him in the G league. Ulrich Chomche is a guy I liked with his massive frame and coordination, but understood he’d be a few seasons away. He just turned 19 a few days ago, and is currently playing for Toronto on a 2 way contract. Melvin Ajinca is my “draft the 6’8’’ guy who can shoot a bit pick. Ajinca was a draft and stash for Dallas and I don’t have any notes here.

&nbsp;

Thats where I was at those years! Who did you guys like these drafts? Did any of your guys pan out?
