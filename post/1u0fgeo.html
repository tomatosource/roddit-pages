Boss-room Rituals resetting the map on death is awful

I get that Ritual spawning in the boss arena is part of the new 0.5 mechanic for progressing toward the final boss.

That part is fine.

What is not fine is dying during that boss-room Ritual and having the whole location reset.

You lose all the Tribute you built up, all the rerolls you were saving, and the entire Ritual chain for that map is basically gone.

This makes the new mechanic feel way more punishing than it needs to be.

Losing the boss-room encounter is fair. Losing the whole map’s Ritual progress because you died inside the required Ritual step is not.
