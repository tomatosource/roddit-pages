why is +3 comet prism of belief so expensive?

I asked the same question in g2 but couldnt get a good answer.

prism of belief for +3 comet costs 6 mirrors. This will increase comet damage by roughly 50%. Sounds good enough to justify the price right?

However the way coc or coa works is that its also based on the damage you do with whatever skill you use to activate either one (or both). I could instead buy the prism of belief that increases that main skill, in my case Ice nova and it would cost me only 1600 divs instead of 6 mirrors.

That would increase ice nova damage by 50%, which would cause coc or coa to proc 50% more often, which in the end gives the same comet damage increase. Its either 50% more comets or each comet doing 50% more damage depending on which prism you get - do you get the comet one or your main skill one? I would argue that its actually better in most cases to increase your main skill as opposed to comet for two reasons:

  1. If your main skill is doing enough damage to proc coc or coa often enough to carry you through whatever youre doing, a 50% boost to whatever that skill is would give you a big boost in that skill too in addition to comet making you much better at killing stuff without comet procs.

  2. 50% more comet procs would give more consistency spreading the damage out over smaller chunks rather that fewer and bigger booms. This is more true the more you are in endgame where speed is everything and you just want to “tag” each enemy for an insta kill as you run by them.

The only downside I see (with the limited knowledge that I have) is that its probably a bit more mana/hp intense to buff your main skill as opposed to buffing comet. When you upgrade a skill the mana cost increases although its slightly less than the damage increase it gets, for +3 skill points lets say 30% more mana cost for the sake of simplicity. That means that comparing the two, getting the main skill jewel youd have 30% cost increase for main skill and 50% cost increase for comet (due to comet proccing 50% more times) whereas the comet skill jewel would cause no cost increase to your main skill and only 30% cost increase to comet.

What do you think?
