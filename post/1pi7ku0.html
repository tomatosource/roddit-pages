Caster Perfect Essences are severely lacking

The only one used in 0.3 was +3 spell levels which was added to focus, but now its only for a wand/staff and should essentially never be used. Aside from that there is just mana cost efficiency.

The lack of powerful perfect essences makes caster weapon crafting significantly more rng than melee. And feels remarkably like 0.1 when caster weapons had no benefit from runes.

It’d be great if the gain damage as extra perfect essences also worked for caster weapons. If thats too strong then hybrid spell damage or regular spell damage as the perfect essences and gain as extra as the greater essences.

We need damage mods on perfect essences that are considered lower value than the best ones. The best ones: for attack builds thats flat dam and increased phys. For spells I’d say gain as extra and max + to spell levels are the equivalent. Hard to say since we generally go for double inc spell damage.
