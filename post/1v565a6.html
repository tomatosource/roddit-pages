New idea for the next potential league mechanic

Hey everyone,

​I’ve been thinking a lot about crafting mechanics, risk/reward loops, and how to make high-stakes itemization engaging for both casuals and endgame crafters. I wanted to pitch a League/Expansion design concept centered around Timeline Anchors and a deterministic Lock & Key system.

​1. The Core Loop: Locks, Keys, & Temporal Rifts

​Instead of purely random global drops, players interact with Time Rifts during mapping or the campaign:

​Time Rifts & Boss Variants: Stepping into a Rift triggers a brief atmospheric transition into an alternate arena featuring a timeline boss variant.

​Deterministic NPC Choice: Defeating the variant opens a portal where an NPC lets you choose one of two core crafting currencies:

​The Temporal Lock (The Anchor): Freezes an item’s current stats/modifiers in time, creating a saved snapshot.

​The Temporal Key (The Rewind): Allows a one-time-only reset on a locked item, wiping any failed craft, soft-brick, or bad corruption and returning it to the saved snapshot.

​Single-Use Safeguard: Once a Key is used to revert an item, the anchor shatters. That item can never be locked or rewound again.

​2. Modern UI & Quality of Life

​To make the system completely intuitive without relying on third-party spreadsheets or guesswork:

​The Ticking Clock Icon: Locked items display a subtle ticking clock icon on their item card/frame.

​Interactive “Ghost Preview”: Clicking or hovering over the clock icon opens a side-by-side Temporal Preview window. It shows your current item on the left and the saved snapshot on the right, making it crystal clear whether burning your single Key reset is worth the cost.

​3. Accessibility & Campaign Onboarding

​Rarity Tier: Drop rates for Locks and Keys (via global drops or Rifts) are balanced around Exalted/Divine Orb frequency. They are rare enough to maintain economic weight, but accessible enough that average players can actually experiment with them.

​Campaign Drops: Locks can drop early in the storyline (Acts 3–4). Because campaign gear is temporary, leveling acts as a zero-stress sandbox where players can test the mechanics and UI before hitting maps.

​Player Agency: Crafters can target-farm Rifts for guaranteed pieces, while speed-mappers can engage passively through global world drops or sell their Locks/Keys on the market.
