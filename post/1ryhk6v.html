How to fix “bad” modifiers!

We all have our least favorite modifiers, + minion skills, reduced attribute requirement, mana (puke) you get the drift!

What REALLY gets me though is 35% reduced attribute requirement IN THE REAL WORLD would be nearly the most desirable modifier, and these games not reflecting that is a HUGE fail! Let’s frame this up using a real world example.

30% reduced requirement would mean we have a car we can license an 11 year old to legally drive, No way we’d Annul orb that away (lets be kind here, no one needs to type it, we all feel the same about other peoples kids)

When it comes to equipment, logic would say an otherwise “strong enough” individual swinging a hammer that requires only 70% as much strength as the hammer he’s used to would gain signifigant improvement.

Here’s my long winded idea for how the mod should work… 30% reduced attribute requirements, meeting requirements adds 12-18% extra quality to item 😁. I feel this does 2 things at once makes an otherwise static modifiers dynamic, AND makes actual friggin’ sense
