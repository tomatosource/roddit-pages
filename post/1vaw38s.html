In the 1963-64 season, 39 of the Celtics’ 80 games were against Hall of Fame centers. They posted the best defensive rating in league history.

Because of the 9-team schedule format, Boston played 12 games against Eastern opponents and 8–10 games against Western teams:

  • Wayne Embry (CIN): 12 games
  • Walt Bellamy (BAL): 10 games
  • Zelmo Beaty (STL): 9 games
  • Wilt Chamberlain & Nate Thurmond (SFO): 8 games

That’s 39 of 80 regular-season games. Then the Celtics played all 10 playoff games against Hall of Fame centers (five against Cincy, then five more against Wilt & Thurmond).

Two years later the Celtics played 10 games each against Eastern rivals (Knicks, 76ers, Royals) and 8–10 games against Western teams. So here’s who Russell faced that year:

  • Wilt Chamberlain (PHI): 10 games
  • Willis Reed (NYK): 10 games
  • Wayne Embry (CIN): 10 games
  • Nate Thurmond (SFW): 9 games
  • Zelmo Beaty (STL): 9 games

That’s 48 out of 80 games against Hall of Fame centers. They then played 10 of 17 playoff games against Hall of Fame centers.

Data from basketball-reference and Backpicks Top 40.
