Yossi Gozlan came up with the idea of trading Simons to Chicago for Ayo Dosunmu and Jalen Smith. It fills the big man need, replaces Simons, basically gets the Celtics under the tax, and both Ayo and Jalen are having career seasons. But would it be worth it?

I’m torn on this one.

On one hand this trade makes a lot of sense if you’re moving Simons. You get a guard to replace him in Ayo, get a big who’s been pretty good in Jalen Smith, and nearly get under the tax to start the process of avoiding the repeater penalties (about $900k over). You can then do something like move Boucher for a 2nd and convert Amari, and you’re fully under.

But Ayo doesn’t have the scoring pop Simons has, so it’s a slight downgrade IMO. Is Jalen Smith going to help you in the playoffs too? You’d probably have to include second-rounders since Chicago is taking the salary. You could also re-route those picks to a team like the Nets, who could take Boucher and a Dalen Terry to make it work financially.

What are the thoughts of the board on this one?
