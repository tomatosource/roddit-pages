Favorite put outs? Reasonable offseason pap.

The strike-him-out-throw-him-out DP to end an inning is the sweetest, sleekest, momentum-shifting play in the game if you ask me. Love it when the catcher tucks his mask under his arm for the walk back to the dugout like a business man with his briefcase stepping off the Friday afternoon train. I’ll also never hate a lazy can o’ corn to center for a simple “F8” in the scorebook–especially when Trent Grisham is out there flicking open his glove like he’s annoyed he’ll have to put down his freshly cracked Sunday morning beer to get the ball back in during softball BP. Or a routine 6-3 with someone like Sally Perez dogging it down the line while Masyn Winn takes his time to check the seams and the stands before lasering a 100mph dart to the first baseman’s chest who doesn’t even bother making a stretch. What else you got?
