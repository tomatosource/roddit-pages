Some stats: The last pitcher to ever throw x innings….

In Honour of Max Scherzer reaching 3000IP this week and it likely being the last time we ever see that milestone reached given how pitchers are used now and nobody active today on course to come close to 3000, i thought i would list some of the last times a pitcher ever hit a milestone.

Last pitcher to throw 5000 IP: Greg Maddux.
Sounds insane that it happened this recently, but Dodgers legend Greg Maddux crept over the milestone in his penultimate career game in LA in 2008.

Last pitcher to throw 4000 IP: Jamie Moyer.
In 2010, just 2 years after the last 5000 IP, we saw the last 4000 IP milestone breached with Jamie Moyer, aged roughly 78 years old.

Last pitcher to throw 3000 IP: Max Scherzer.
Its possible someone else will have a Jamie Moyer-esque career and pitch well into their 40’s and reach this milestone but currently nobody is on pace to do so.

Last pitcher to throw 400 IP in a season: Ed Walsh 1908.
Walsh threw a 464 IP in 1908, and nobody has breached 400 since. Walsh was built differently even by the standards of the era, having thrown 424 the previous year. He would come close with 393 a few years later.

Last pitcher to throw over 350 IP in a season: Wilbur Wood 1973.
He last did so in 1973 at aged 31, a weird career that saw him not be a full timer until 25 and only as a reliever, transitioning to starter at 29.

Last pitcher to throw 300 IP in a season: Steve Carlton 1980.
No real surprise here, although Niekro was right there with him in the late 70’s.

Last pitcher to throw 250 IP in a season: Justin Verlander 2011.
Somewhat surprised this was already 15 years ago, when i first started i wasnt sure to include 250 because it seemed like active pitchers might well challenge it but nobody has even thrown 230 IP in a decade at this point.

Its possible the last pitcher to ever throw 2500 IP is already active, any thoughts on who might be the last?

Bonus fact: Only 1 player under 30 currently has over 1000 IP. Logan Webb. Next best with 980 IP is Logan Gilbert. Dont underestimate Logans.
