Some parallels between 2019 Toronto Raptors and 2025 Blue Jays.

In the NBA, the 2019 Raptors won it all. While the Blue Jays in baseball have not won it all just yet, here are some similarities:

  • Both teams were based in Toronto (obviously because that’s the point of this post)
  • Both teams faced really stacked teams throughout most of the run.
  • Both teams had at most 1-2 stars followed by solid role players. The Raptors were Lowry/Leonard but everyone else contributed. Blue Jays have Vladdy, Kirk, and maybe Springer. The rest of the lineup is composed of role players that contributed slowly.
  • Both teams had a really good player injured. Annunoby and Bichette.
  • Both teams will be facing the defending champions.
  • Both teams have home court advantage.
  • Both teams will be facing a California-based team. Golden State and LA.
  • Both teams had a history of playoff failures in recent years.
  • Both of the Toronto opponents swept their opponent in the conference finals.
  • Both conference finals series featured being down 2-0 by a Toronto team.
  • Both finals appearances will be the franchise first in the 21st century. Raptors never had one prior.
  • Both teams added a solid veteran presence. Gasol and Scherzer.

Any other similarities?
