So, about Deflect mechanic

Deflect description from the stream

I’ve made a post a while ago, talking about how Evasion is too much of a coinflip between being incredibly OP when it allows you to completely avoid all damage/ailments and completely useless when it doesn’t proc. I was proposing the “half-evade” mechanic, “Fumble”, that is significantly easier to achieve and maybe even able to hit 100% chance, but it only mitigates % of damage instead of avoiding it completely.

Deflect is pretty much it, no? I’m really excited to see how it actually works in-game. If you can reasonably hit 100% chance on it, permanent 40% mitigation on both hits and ailments should solve a lot of problems with low health pool of pure Evasion builds, it almost doubles your eHP.
