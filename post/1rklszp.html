Personal Opinion: If Tatum isn’t back by 3⁄14, then he’s not coming back this season

I’ve seen speculation from Bill Simmons that he’s coming back Friday, and I’ve seen people speculate 3⁄14 because that’s the St. Louis area code. We’re all hoping this isn’t 2009 when KG was supposed to be back within a week, within a month, and then he never came back. But just looking at the games situation, I can’t see the Celtics sanctioning his return any later than 3⁄14.

If he comes back 3⁄6, then he’ll have 17 games to ramp up (I took out B2Bs and the last game of the year where nobody plays). I think that’s a good amount of time for the team to get used to him.

If he comes back 3⁄14, then he’ll have 13 games. That’s cutting it reallllll close IMO, I think any less than that and the team might not get used to him in time, and the playoffs are right around the corner.

Given the adjustment period that will come with his return, I feel like anything past 3⁄14 might be too late. What do you guys think?
