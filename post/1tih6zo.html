I got completely hooked to this game (3rd try)

I have to say I was always a big diablo player and was always hesitant to try poe due to it’s complexity (what people are saying) and somehow I just never tried it. I bought EA poe2 at the end of 0.3, went to end of 1st act and left the game. I gave it another try at 0.4 with pathfinder, stopped immediately at maps and just a week ago I said hmm, let’s try it again.

And since this I have 40 hours in the game!!!! As I got to 90 lvl and seen and understood more of the game I can’t believe I could ever say that diablo is better than poe. Not trying to start fight between fans of the games but just objectively saying as diablo player since d2, poe2 is the best arpg I ever played! It just takes a lot of time to start understanding how to build a character, craft, upgrade everything and how to be quite efficient and have more fun with build. Can’t wait for 0.5 to try facebreakers on monk!
