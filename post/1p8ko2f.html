Is TD Garden a must visit or not ?

I see so many conflicting opinions on the bucket list status of TD Garden. It’s obviously a newer less historic arena unlike MSG. Most of the new arenas tend to be multi purpose somewhat generic feel.

Yet it also fits in a bucket with United Center and Staples Center as venues in iconic cities with iconic franchises that somewhat replaced an old historic venue.

When I read NBA arena rankings, TD Garden is normally a top 5 venue. But when I read NHL rankings, TD Garden is normally ranked somewhere between 15-20th place which is incredibly mediocre.

So is it worth visiting? Or is it just a generic arena?
