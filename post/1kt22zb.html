The Oklahoma City Thunder are the first franchise in NBA history with 3 different MVPs in a 12-year span.

Pretty much it.

Durant 2014, Westbrook 2017, Gilgeous-Alexander 2025.

They’re not the franchise with the most MVPs overall (Philadelphia is, with 5), but the only team in NBA history with 3 different MVPs in such a short span.

The second fastest franchise is Philly, with 3 different players to win the award in 16 years (1968 Chamberlain, 1981 Erving, 1983 Malone).
