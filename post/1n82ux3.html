Kawhi Leonard Secret $28M deal timeline

Here is a timeline of key events related to Aspiration, KL2 Aspire LLC, Kawhi Leonard’s contracts, and the Clippers:

  • 1997: Andre Cherney and Joe Sandberg, co-founders of Aspiration, first met at a Harvard networking event in D.C..
  • 2012: Andre Cherney lost a congressional race, despite an endorsement from former President Bill Clinton.
  • 2013: Andre Cherney and Joe Sandberg co-founded Aspiration.
  • 2014: Steve Ballmer purchased the Los Angeles Clippers.
  • 2019 (Summer): Kawhi Leonard and Serge Ibaka won the NBA title with the Raptors. Kawhi Leonard, the most sought-after free agent, chose to sign with the LA Clippers. NBA Commissioner Adam Silver agreed to investigate complaints from other teams about how the Clippers landed Leonard, focusing on whether Kawhi’s uncle, Dennis Robertson, requested improper benefits, such as “guaranteed off-court endorsement money”.
  • Early 2021: Internal paperwork from Aspiration, potentially examined by Steve Ballmer, listed 80-90% of its Letters of Intent (LOIs) as fake.
  • 2021: Aspiration was valued as a “green bank” at $2.3 billion, aiming to go public.
  • August 2021: Kawhi Leonard signed his first extension with the Clippers, a 4-year, $176.3 million deal. NBA insiders were surprised by its structure, as it appeared to be a favor to the Clippers, rather than maximizing his earnings.
  • September 2021: The Clippers announced Aspiration as the first founding partner of the Intuit Dome, making it the first arena to be 100% carbon neutral. This involved a sponsorship deal worth over $300 million over 23 years. Steve Ballmer also broke ground on the Intuit Dome, with Kawhi Leonard present.
  • October 29, 2021: Joe Sandberg sent an internal email, noting Steve Ballmer’s enthusiasm to help Aspiration and act as a reference.
  • November 1, 2021: Steve Ballmer personally sent an email connecting the CEO of Intuit with Andre Cherney and Joe Sandberg, suggesting they take in a Clippers game together due to their shared passion for the team and the Intuit Dome project.
  • November 22, 2021: KL2 Aspire LLC was registered with the state of California. Kawhi Leonard is listed as the manager or member, and the LLC’s name “Aspire” indicated its purpose was to receive money from Aspiration. Uncle Dennis Robertson is also listed in the endorsement contract as Kawhi’s designated representative.
  • December 2021: Steve Ballmer’s personal LLC, Polepat, invested \(50 million into Aspiration, as part of a larger \)315 million investment round from Ballmer and Oaktree Capital Management. This infusion of funds allegedly enabled Aspiration to pay Kawhi Leonard $28 million for what sources described as a “no-show job” or “sweetener”.
  • April 1, 2022: The $28 million endorsement agreement between KL2 Aspire LLC (Kawhi Leonard’s entity) and Aspiration Partners became effective. The contract specified payments of $7 million annually over four years. Internal sources indicated it was Aspiration’s single largest sponsorship deal, completely eclipsing all other celebrity endorsements. The contract notably included clauses allowing Kawhi Leonard to decline actions not consistent with his beliefs, effectively making him not in breach even if he did nothing. A unique termination clause stipulated the contract would end if Leonard was no longer a Clipper, even if still being paid by the team elsewhere.
  • October 2022: Andre Cherney resigned as CEO of Aspiration. Aspiration internally began prioritizing Kawhi Leonard’s contract payments, even amidst severe financial hardship, reportedly due to calls from Uncle Dennis. The company also falsely advertised Qatar as a customer.
  • 2022-23 NBA Season: The Clippers ended their sponsorship relationship with Aspiration because Aspiration defaulted on its obligations.
  • August 2023: Kawhi Leonard signed another extension with the Clippers.
  • March 2025: Joseph Sandberg was arrested for conspiring to defraud an investment fund of at least $145 million.
  • Weeks after Joe Sandberg’s arrest: Aspiration filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy.
  • July 4th (this summer): Pablo Torre encountered Andre Cherney’s signature in a New York Times article about “Project 2029,” a Democratic political initiative.
  • Last Month (relative to broadcast): Pablo Torre connected with Andre Cherney on LinkedIn to inquire about his time at Aspiration, his resignation, and the company’s tree planting claims, but Cherney did not respond.
  • August 21, 2025: Joseph Sandberg agreed to plead guilty to a \(248 million scheme to defraud investors and lenders (two counts of wire fraud). On the same day, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) charged him for raising over \)300 million from investors through a fraudulent scheme involving fake revenues for environmental services. The SEC’s investigation remains ongoing.

This timeline highlights how Aspiration, co-founded by politically ambitious individuals, attracted celebrity endorsements and significant investments, including from Clippers owner Steve Ballmer, leading to a controversial $28 million no-show deal with Kawhi Leonard, all while allegedly engaging in widespread fraud that ultimately led to bankruptcy and federal charges for one of its founders.
