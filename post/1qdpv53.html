Cohwheel freezing / crashing / stuttering, etc.

there has been a freezing cogwheel animation since the 0.2 release.
for nvidia, the only fix for a long time was downgrading to the 552.44 driver.

i found a new fix, and it’s probably the real issue.
just go into settings and set target framerate to 6, not 120 (or anything else).

this setting seems to force the gpu to do extra work every frame until it basically gives up. then the gpu disconnects and reconnects, which causes the freeze and cogwheel animation.

my guess is that when fps keeps dropping and jumping nonstop, the system keeps trying to correct it and ends up crashing the gpu instead.

these are the settings i’m running right now, with no stuttering or cogwheel freezes:

  • dx12
  • windowed fullscreen
  • dynamic resolution: off
  • upscale: dlss
  • image quality: quality
  • hdr: off
  • textures: medium
  • texture filtering: trilinear
  • lighting: shadows
  • shadow quality: low
  • number of lights: low
  • bloom: 25
  • water detail: low
  • nvidia reflex: off
  • foreground fps: off (144)
  • background fps: off (60)
  • triple buffering: off
  • dynamic culling: off
  • target framerate: 6
  • engine multithreading: on

if i change target framerate to 120 or any other value, the freezing cogwheel comes back immediately.

if anyone tries this and it fixes the issue, i’d really appreciate a confirmation — it fixed it for me and for a friend who was freezing almost every temple run.
