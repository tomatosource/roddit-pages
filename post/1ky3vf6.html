Are spectres never going to be fixed?

There’s a pretty egregious spectre bug that sometimes puts them in purgatory any time you move too far away from them. Instead of teleporting to you like they’re supposed to, they get stuck in the teleport animation but never move. The only way to get them out is to move close to them. This bug is so common that it happens about every other time you move far away from your spectres.

It should go without saying this bug massively limits how effective spectres can be since it’s constantly taking your minions out of fights. The bug has existed since day 1, is EXTREMELY obvious to anyone who plays spectres for more than an hour, and makes the skill frustrating and exhausting to play.

Has GGG never played spectres? Have they addressed the bug in any way and some reason why they can’t fix it? They’ve made bigfixes for spectres since the launch of season of the hunt, so I don’t understand why it’s gone unfixed for so long. I’m very disappointed as raise spectre was my favorite skill in poe 1, and it’s been so irritating to play that I’ve been avoiding it.
