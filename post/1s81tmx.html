Does Neemias Queta deserve more credit?

Going into this season the majority of writers/vegas/fans were expecting Boston to hover around .500 in large part due to their perceived weak frontcourt. ESPN for example put them at 40 wins listing their biggest weakness as the fact Garza was slotted in as starter.

Is Queta getting enough credit for not only becoming serviceable but one of our most essential players? 10 points, 8.3 rebounds, 1.3 blocks 64% shooting in 25mpg is solid (per-36 of 14 points, 12 rebounds, 2 blocks), but he’s also leading the team in defensive rating (whereas prior years it was players like Kornet and Holiday who are no longer with us).

Win shares is a stat I don’t personally put much stock into, but I’ve seen some heavily upvoted threads here using it do things like claim Pierce was better than Havlicek. For those who embrace that stat, it should be noted that Queta is #1 on the team in win shares with only Pritchard close. Last year, the top 5 in win shares was Tatum, Pritchard, White, Kornet and Porzingis. The year prior the top 5 in win shares was Tatum, White, Porzingis, Holiday and Horford.

Even if you go back to our first Tatum title run, the #1 in win shares was actually Robert Williams slightly edging him out. Williams, of course, was also 1st on that team in defensive rating. And that actually makes a lot of sense. The big difference between that year and the crappy season prior wasn’t the coach change from Brad to Udoka, it was that Tatum had reached the typical age (23) phenom prospects reach superstardom (first of his 4 straight 1st team all-nba selections), and that Timelord had suddenly evolved into a DPOY level big man. Unfortunately his body broke down before the playoffs ended.

But are we seeing a similar thing here with Queta? Yes, we’re getting far better play out of guys like Walsh, Hugo, and Garza than anyone reasonable expected. Yes the mainstays like Pritchard and White have stepped up. But should we be giving more kudos to the fact what was thought to be our biggest weakness (starting center/defensive anchor), might actually be one of our biggest strengths? Similar to what Timelord was doing briefly for us? Timelord in that lone great season averaged 10 points, 9.6 rebounds, 2.2 blocks with 74% shooting in 29.6mpg. It was genuinely the most impactful defense we had seen since KG (of course nowhere near the offensive versatility, though). Is Queta giving us like 80% of what peak Timelord was doing?

TL;DR: Can it be argued Queta is the major difference maker between expectations and reality with this team’s “gap year”?
