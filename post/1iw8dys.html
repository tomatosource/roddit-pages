Trade system improvements

From what I understand, the design philosophy behind the current trade system is to have “friction”, presumably to somewhat stabilise the economy. How does it affect the player?

  1. Spending 20 mins trying to get whispers back
  2. Getting scammed/wasting time
  3. Having to interrupt whatever you’re doing to make sales
  4. Having to be online to even be able to make sales
  5. Relying on the owner of the item you want being online at the same time, let alone responding

It is a huge waste of time, adds nothing of value, and is predatory FOMO to keep you playing only POE2, since you need to be online to even make sales. I genuinely find myself doing other things with the game open just to have a chance of selling valuable items.

How to improve this while maintaining “friction”?
Have a regular marketboard like many other games with large playerbases do (e.g. extraction games/MMOs). To stabilise the economy, and prevent rapid undercutting of prices etc, restrictions can be put in place.

The main restriction is that, once you’ve listed an item, you cannot change the price of it for 3 days. This way, you can’t constantly undercut other players etc. Players can just pop the item on the market, and then the other player simply buys it from the market. If a player made a mistake in pricing, they can remove it from the market, but they can’t relist it until the 3 days are finished. The timer is bound to the item itself, to prevent abuse such as a player manually trading it to another player, who could then relist it at a different price without the timer.

This solves all 5 of the above issues, and still maintains some friction to prevent abuse of the market. I really cannot think of any reason not to implement something like this, other than people who say “but it’s always been like this!” Yeah, it’s a brand new game, it should be different and try new things, and that’s why it’s in early access. Otherwise, POE2 might as well have been an extension to POE1.

To be tested:

  1. Once bought, the timer disappears, allowing the buyer to instantly relist at a higher price
  2. Once bought, the timer still continues, preventing the buyer from instantly relisting at a higher price for the remaining duration
  3. Once bought, a “recently purchased” timer is applied, preventing the buyer from instantly relisting for ___ amount of time.

Hope to hear feedback on this idea, and what people think about it, any possible problems I may have missed. The current trading system feels awful, it needs major changes.
