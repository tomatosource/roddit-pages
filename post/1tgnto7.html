0.5 Ritual Rite of the Nameless

I was going back through the 0.5 content reveal video, and one thing that stuck out to me that I didn’t really comprehend during the initial livestream was how insane the new Rite of the Nameless probably will be.

As a refresher, you do this after beating the Nameless King, and its how you access the new Ritual pinnacle boss. You start the first map as normal, but now the last ritual site is in the boss room, and you have to do the ritual (which includes all monsters slain in previous rituals after the new change) with the boss there. Then you pick from one of the options presented to you of adjacent maps and continue the ritual. You need to do five continuous maps this way to complete the rite and “lose your name” so you can challenge the pinnacle boss.

What makes this potentially ridiculous is that all of the previous monsters from rituals in the past maps also get summoned at each new ritual. Including the bosses. This sounds like chaos when you’re at the later maps and having to fight 2-3 bosses (on top of all the other monsters) every single ritual.

Many of the map bosses in this game have pretty massive attacks that give you plenty of warning and are fairly easy to dodge when fighting them 1:1. What happens when there are four of them in one small ritual zone? On top of waves of blood or the sapping degen? I can dodge monkey slam sure, but after doing so I’m right on top of the bone mastodon’s charge or filth queen’s big slam. On top of that, you’re in one of the ritual zones that is shoved against a wall or on a bridge so you have half the normal space. This is before even thinking about map modifiers.

The only clean solution seems to be to DPS race everything. Which isn’t new to this game by any means, sure. But this also feels like it will be head and shoulders more rippy than any other mapping content in the game so far. I’m interested to see what it’s like in practice when 0.5 goes live.
