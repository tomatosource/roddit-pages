Brief Guide on using the Two Chains for Atziri’s Temple building

I’ve seen a lot of theorycrafting around Temple mechanics already, so I won’t repeat how everything works. Just wanted to share some practical experience from actually building it, especially if you’re aiming for a more stable setup.

First thing I learned the hard way: don’t rush the start. The core of the whole setup is the Spymaster, since it’s your source of Juatalotli’s Medallions (locks). Ideally, you want to be in a position where you can protect your first Spymaster before you really commit. What worked best for me was starting only when I had at least one Juatalotli’s Medallion and one Quipolatl’s Medallion (upgrade), or a Temple layout that already showed Spymaster + Garrison + Armoury. That gives you a much better chance of securing your first Tier 3 Spymaster instead of watching it collapse immediately.

Second, running two single chains feels way more stable than trying to do everything at once. Your main chain should be:

Spymaster → Garrison → Armoury → Garrison → Spymaster.

At the beginning, basically all your resources should go into protecting this chain.

The second chain (Commander → Garrison → Commander) is optional at the start. I wouldn’t even think about locking it unless you’re sitting on 3+ spare locks.

Third, having a Generator + Workshop cluster near the center early is huge. Tier 3 Workshops generate Quipolatl’s Medallions, which are insanely flexible. One upgrade medallion is basically equivalent to creating a Spymaster: upgrade a fresh Spymaster once, let another room collapse, and you can get a tier 3 spy. Any extras can go straight into pushing Tier 3 Commanders later.

That’s basically the end goal for me this season: a Temple that can reliably sustain two locked chains without constantly bleeding progress. It takes patience, but once it clicks, the system actually feels pretty rewarding. Curious if anyone’s found cleaner or more efficient variations on this approach.
