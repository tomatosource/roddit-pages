This is peak art design!

Yes, it was a tad buggy at first, but ignoring that, the Derelict Mansion is simply one of the most beautiful, well designed levels in this or any other ARPG.

https://preview.redd.it/lddlb7ltoi2g1.png?width=2730&format=png&auto=webp&s=a9ea4d2b6c596c074712e7277d5f41d018cbb787

Two levels that are actually in 3D space, (you can see aura effects from the monsters on the lower level peaking through), actual artworks on display, a design that actually makes sense as a mansion, and let’s talk about the elephant in the room:

https://preview.redd.it/2nrsa9p7pi2g1.png?width=2151&format=png&auto=webp&s=5ef916fe898c6ea58c737ad4547b8ba52361d3e9

Yes, this is reskinned Asinia and Draven, but oh my, if you’re going to reskin, this is the way:
