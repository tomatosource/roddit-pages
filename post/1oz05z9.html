• level to skills is a problem, but there is a much easier solution here then removing it from items.

Everyone knows that + level to skills is absolutely mandatory on the weapons for your build due to its exponential scaling of damage. But the solution no seems to be considering is this.

Make it a prefix.

Now you have to trade your weapon’s base damage for that exponential bonus. Is it worth it? maybe. is it still BIS? possibly. But the biggest thing here is sacrificing base weapon damage is a lot bigger of a deal than sacrificing gain 5 life on hit.

There is a pattern with prefixes and suffixes on weapons.

Almost all prefixes on weapons directly influence your weapons damage. Some in visually obvious ways, others in less visually obvious ways. For example, +10 to 20 physical damage will apply directly to the weapon’s stats, but + 100% elemental damage will only apply to your skill gem’s damage. The only exception to this on most martial weapons is accuracy, but even in that case, if your build needs accuracy to hit, then it’s also essentially damage.

On the other hand, suffixes are primarily utility. For example, attributes, leech, reduced attribute requirements, life/mana on kill/hit. The pretty much universal exceptions here are crit chance, crit damage, and attack speed, but even in these cases it’s not literally just damage that’s being changed here. It’s “damage” that favors some builds more than others.

If you interpret what + to skill levels means, its essentially an additional 10-20% More damage with 10-20% More mana cost. I say more here because it’s a multiplier that gets factored separately from the rest of damage calculation like every other “More” effect in the game. You could also say the same for the stats that do directly impact the weapon’s base damage because those also are factored separately from external increases, so it even shares that in common with the other prefixes.

But what about spell weapons.

Even the spell weapons like wands and staves put all of the direct damage buffs like increased spell damage, increased elemental/physical damage, and gain as extra damage in the prefixes, so having + to level competing with those would most likely fix the same problem.

Conclusion / TL; DR

All this is just to say, keep anything that is an obvious direct damage increased in the category of prefixes and make suffixes more the utility or “situational damage” affixes.

+ to skill level will never be situational because even if you don’t have the mana to support it, the problem isn’t the skill level, it’s your mana.
