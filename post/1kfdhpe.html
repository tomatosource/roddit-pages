Ring of Friendship - XP Bonus Playing w/ Friends

I would like to share a fun idea for a unique ring that grants you an XP bonus while adventuring with your friends. This item encourages more people to play together, offer a fun ring for couch co-op, and help improve the social aspect of the game. However, in pure GGG fashion, this ring would have a negative affix of reducing light radius. I thought the flavor text was fitting as well…

Ring of Friendship

  • Unique Ring
  • Requires: Level 1
  • 10% Increased Experience Gain While In Party With Friends
  • 10% Reduced Light Radius

Flavor Text: “Walking with a friend in the dark is better than walking alone in the light.”
