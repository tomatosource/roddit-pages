With 21 Rebounds Tonight, Zach Edey has Set the Grizzlies Rookie Record for Rebounds in a Game

Edey was dominant on the glass against the Pistons. With Duren out, Edey was able to feast with 21 boards, 14 defensive and 7 offensive, in 29 minutes. The previous record (18) was set by Bryant Reeves in 1996. Edey also put up 6 assists, largely feeding from his rebounds
