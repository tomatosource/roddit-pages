Walsh: maybe tied with Hugo rn or at least up there with him in terms of highest upside, best defender of the bunch and probably the best player of the bunch right now too. Super valuable once Tatum concerns as someone we could start between the jays to soak up defensive matchups

Hugo: youngest by far, super high ceiling already shown a ton of promise this year. No brainer absolutely cannot trade with him being on a rookie deal for 3 more years and having so much room to grow- similar to Walsh in that he’d fit perfectly next to the jays going forward

Minott: probably the most expendable right now but idk man, he’s the only one of the 4 who is long and tall enough to comfortably play backup 4 which we kinda need especially against bigger matchups, and he actually showed a lot earlier his year and I still think he’s pretty promising, honestly not sure why he fell out of the rotation because I thought he played well before his injury.
