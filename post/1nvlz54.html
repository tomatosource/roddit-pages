Decided not to be a settler and actually craft myself an upgrade….

https://preview.redd.it/mj0rnuvjnksf1.png?width=392&format=png&auto=webp&s=e8148890297820c6ace161f923eabc8dd730d964

Step 1: Buy spirit fractured solar amulet

Step 2: Anull down to two total mods

Step 3: Chaos for +3

Step 4: Omen of Dextral Necromancy + Preserved Collarbone for desecrated suffix

Step 5: Unveil for any res (with or without Omen of Abyssal Echoes)

Step 6: Omen of Dextral Exaltation + Homogenising + Perfect Exalt for T1 res

Step 7: Omen of light to remove low unveiled res

Step 8: Omen of Sinestral Necromancy + Preserved Collarbone for desecrated prefix

Step 9: Unveil for any ES mod (thankfully hit T1 here)

Step 10: Homogenising+ Omen of Greater Exaltation + Perfect Exalt for T1 flat + T1 lightning. Got lucky here.

Was winging it a bit here but I needed specific mods on this upgrade. Pretty darn happy with the accessability of Abyss crafting as I’m not a big crafter and prefer to just blast.
