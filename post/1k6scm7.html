Wisp suggestion

Pretty simple rework that I believe would enhance this mechanic.

When a player finds a wisp it attaches itself to the player .

Afterwards monsters that face the player get the wisp effect it the same way a normal wisp would.

When the player finds a rare the Wisp leaves the player and possesses the rare instead.

Key points:

Wisps could work with a duration so you would need to find the rare in X seconds.

Multiple wisps could be possessing the player at the same time ( instead of as is now that if you find two wisps you need to choose one to follow).

Wisps could enhance mobs around the player (presence area) or perhaps a larger radius. Atlas could reflect this

To conclude. The wisps would remain very similar in mechanics but the control would remain with the player. No more following wisps through cleared content
