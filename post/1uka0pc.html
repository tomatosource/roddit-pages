Arguing for a Pelicans trade around Zion and Herb, not Trey

ETA: To be clear I’m **not arguing for a Jaylen trade**. I love him and I hope they keep him. It seems maybe inevitable he’s getting traded so I’m arguing for this return.

think if anyone can turn post-athleticism Zion into a two way star, it’s Boston. The frustration around him has been 3 things. 1) His fitness/availability 2) his defense 3) his refusal to develop a 3 point shot

  1. Ozempic Zion lost a ton of weight and played most of the year. He had one small injury and missed only two short stretches of games.
  2. He has elite strength and agility and there’s no reason he can’t be a plus defensively. I am confident Boston’s staff can bring it out of him
  3. His career high for 3 point attempts and makes is both 4. Which he set in his first game. He has good form. He is OK from the line. He must have the yips or something. I’m confident Joe can fix him

I know everyone is clamoring for Trey Murphy, but I don’t see their urgency to trade him. The only reason people think the Pels will move him is because their GM is incompetent. Trey’s value isn’t going down. No urgency to trade him unless they get a Godfather offer. Thats not Jaylen. I would argue that Trey on his contract is more valuable than Jaylen on his. So no Try Murphy.

That leaves Zion and Herb for Jaylen. Exact same salary. Hauser, Missi, and/or Bey could also be included to even things out. Picks/whatever. The main idea is Zion and Herb as far as next years roster goes.

Boston would add an elite POA defensive quarterback and another great ball handler and Boston with Zion is king of all drive-and-dish offenses. Center would be a problem for another day.
