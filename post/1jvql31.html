Disenchanting is a system designed to slow progression, ‘add value’ to otherwise undesired Ground loot and create the sense of a ‘player choice’ of taking either gold or crafting materials.

A system which revolves around picking up gear which is bad for your character, is a pretty poorly designed system.

Chris Wilson said it best, when it came to Flask charges…

“Players should want to go back to town, to ID items/craft, not to fill their flasks”.

I feel the same with disenchanting. I should want to return to town because I have found a potential upgrade…. Not because I want to haul back completely useless items.

A rare item should drop with a regal shard, rather than be the outcome of disenchantment.
