Tonight’s Grizzlies lead of 29 is the second largest blown lead in the playoffs of the play-by-play era. The previous second largest was…the Grizzlies, who blew a 27 point lead in 2012 to the Clippers.

The game in question was Game 1 of that series where the Lob City Clippers - a team not exactly known for clutch performances - eked out a win after being down 27 points to the Grit N’ Grind Grizzlies.

Believe it or not, Nick Young led the way with the most points scored by a Clipper with 19 in total. +28 in 24 minutes at that.

I’m honestly not sure what is worse: Blowing a bigger lead to a more talented team, or blowing a slightly smaller - but still notable - lead to Nick Young.
