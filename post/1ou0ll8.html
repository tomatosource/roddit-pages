With 0.4 looming around the corner. Here are some of my top niche asks for the upcoming update

  1. Abyss pits should not close until the rogue exile nearby is killed

- Hunting down a Heart of The Well diamond is extremely difficult as the chances of spawning a rogue exile near an abyss pit is extremely slim. On top of that, players are required to kill it before all other monsters around the pit before it closes makes it more difficult as they are often stronger/tankier than the rare mobs around it. More often than not, the monsters will die before the rogue exile is killed which makes the “Lichborn” version the rogue exile unspawnable. It is unfun having to spend an eternity to “lure” the rogue exile away from the pits and other monsters just to kill it first while simultaneously being surrounded by mobs from all sides.

  1. Total Attack Time stat should be removed from “certain” skills

- I am not against the idea of total attack time as a “lock” on certain skills that requires a skilled timing to pull off like perfect strike and gathering storm, but it absolutely makes no sense on many of other skills out there. The perfect example I have is Whirling Assualt: According to GGG’s own explanation of the skill, it is meant to apply many small on hit effects like Staggering Palm Projectiles and Charged Staff waves. However having total attack time tacked onto the skill makes it impossible to scale the application of these on hit effects to make it remotely viable, hence why people mainly use Tempest Flurry as a way better alternative instead. Other skills that I can’t wrap my head around are the ones on maces like rolling slam, sunder, etc…

  1. Let us build combo points for our second weapon set skills

- Not much to add here. I would love for my weapon set 1 skill that scales attack speed to build up combo points for my other weapon set skill regardless of the weapons as well. Could possibly be very OP, but it opens the doors for more weapon cross polination and build variaty

  1. Unset rings should not be a mandatory for many builds on a game that is focused on more combo gameplay

- I personally dislike how many skill slots that are available currently, especially with the game design focused more on combo gameplay with many button presses which I am a fan of. The problem lies with most builds usually run 3-4 spirit gems, and some up to 6-7 with weapon swap tech, taking up most of the available skill slots which as a result severely limits the amount of active skills a player have access to. My proposal is the following options:

- Having to run unset rings doesn’t feel good, give players atleast one additional skill slot.

- Separate spirit reservation skills into its own panel that doesn’t take up the active skill slots

  1. Balance aside, the skill unlock level of some weapon classes needs to be reworked for better campaign experience

- Having to run auto attack and glacial cascade until level 14 to get access to tempest flurry/ice strike is a huge turn off for a lot of monk players starting out with the class, and many caster classes having to run ED/Con until mid game to transition to the elemental spells is not a good feeling. A simple rearrangement of the current skill unlock levels of some of these weapon classes would significantly improve the campaign experience by a mile.

  1. Simulacrum needs a loot buff

- Simulacrum is possibly the hardest pinnacle content to complete compared to Xesht, KoTM, and Olroth. However the drops is abysmal in comparison where the other content all having pretty decent drops (jewels, crafting mats, lineage support gems, unique gear), especially taking in account the amount of time it takes to complete a run.

  1. Hideout Testing dummy

- It doesn’t matter how simple it is for the first iteration, or even if it’s a MTX that we can purchase. Please let us have a testing dummy in our hideout to test out our builds. There are so many things outside of damage numbers that we can benefit from experimenting with a testing dummy, and it’s a huge miss opportunity with not having one especially in an early access state.

  1. Death Recap

- Not really looking for anything complicated with an UI, just give us a list of all sources affecting the player’s HP for the last X seconds before death. Keep it as simple as possible and spit it into a log file or something. Anything is fine as a first iteration as long as it gives players a way to troubleshoot our death. There are definitly going to be outliers with certain builds that can’t be accurately represented through just the logs, but it will be leagues better than what we have currently.
