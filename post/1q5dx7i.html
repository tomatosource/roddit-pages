Opinion: Maybe bosses that get extended invincibility mid-fight to set up one shot moves is not the best fight design.

POE2 seems to have a fixation with ‘artsy’ boss fights, which is not bad in of itself. But the invincibility hack that some bosses abuse, some notable offenders like Digsite boss and Arbiter of Ash, feels like it’s really pushing the boundaries of fairness. POE2 seems to want to sprinkle elements of Soulslike into its gameplay but Elden Ring for example does not do this. If you decide to outdamage the moves, it’s a fair way to approach the fight. If you decide to stun them, paralyze them, put them to sleep, it’s a fair way to approach too. All of that adds myriad of ways to approach a fight. That feels like real interaction with the boss. What POE2 has doesn’t feel like interaction and just feels like the bosses cheating. I feel like I’d rather enjoy bosses being fully interactable during the fight with a larger HP pool than watch them dance around invincible for a whole minute.
