How often do great players miss 3s in an open gym?

This is more of a thought exercise than a request for actual numbers: Was shooting around (badly) by myself and it got me wondering what the theoretical upper limit is for elite shooters (think Steph, Dame, etc) when they don’t have to worry about defenders, etc.

My first thought was that obviously their percentages (assuming they’re actually locked in and not just casually shooting around) would have to be crazy, like the difference between FT% and 2PT%. On the other hand, if the whole point of working on your shot is to shoot it exactly the same way whether you’re warming up or you’ve got two guys with their hands in your face, then maybe the improvement wouldn’t be as dramatic as I might think. Curious what other people think
