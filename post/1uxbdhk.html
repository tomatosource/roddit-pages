As a casual player, this league has been fun for me

I’ve seen a lot of posts nitpicking different aspects of the league, and just wanted to offer a couple thoughts on why I’ve enjoyed it.

Usually I play for ~2 hours a day a few days per week. Maybe 30-45 minutes before work, and a hour or two after the kids are in bed. I’ve played steadily since the start of 0.5, and mainly played a single character (plant abyssal lich) following Deadrabbit’s guide.

Here are a few thoughts

- the campaign is good. I enjoyed the changes made this league. And while I’m not incredibly invested in the lore, I am very interested to play acts 5⁄6 to see what happens

- the endgame is very engaging. I’ve finished most of the mechanic atlas trees, but I still have many nodes left on the main atlas. I’ve killed the arbiter of divinity 3 times total, so that many nodes unlocked. I really like the overhaul on the atlas with the different mechanics and story lines

- the drop rates are okay, but I got lucky. I’ve killed Atziri 2 times, and both times she dropped the communion support gem. I use the first on on my build for that sweet CoC, and sold the other which funded my getting an armor piece that helped dramatically. My weapon is the next thing I need to upgrade, but an actually meaningful upgrade is several hundred divs, and I only have 60 right now. I also had a mageblood drop with some resistances which was very helpful

- for my build currently, a juiced 200% deli city map takes me about 20 minutes to clear. I can survive okay, but there are times my heart is pounding. Boss fights take me a couple minutes usually, and grand mirror fights scare me a little bit

- overall I’m still having fun several weeks into the league and I look forward to playing again tonight when the kids head to bed!

Other thoughts

- I still don’t understand crafting that much. I watch videos, I try things, but props to the people who really understand it

- I’m terrible at pricing things. I know, the in game price checker sucks, but it’s easy to use and doesn’t require more setup. What I don’t get is when I have something that I think the price is easily 100div, but it sits there. Over time I drop the price until it’s sitting at 1 div and still not moving. I don’t get it

- i don’t believe mirrors exist. I think they are a ruse that streamers use to get views. The spot in the currency tab is them conspiring with the devs and the posts on Reddit are paid propaganda!

- who the help is the guy on the dragon the arbiter of divinity smashes? I still don’t understand the good guys/bad guys

- verisium makes absolutely no sense to me. I have so much of it. I know how to infuse stuff. But I haven’t touched it since the campaign. I haven’t touched all the kalguuren gems either. They just sit there like the little knickknacks my grandmother used to have on her kitchen hutch

A couple ideas

- I can’t put in 8 hours a day every day, and likely will never be able to do so. But it would be kinda neat if there was like a temp buff or something that casual players could get for a map or two. Kind of a “hey, you played &lt;6 hours yesterday, your next 3 maps will have 10% increased rarity” or something

- something around late league economy prices. I know a lot of folks probably dip out from the league without passing on their gear in a Reddit giveaway. But maybe if someone hasn’t played in a couple weeks, any items they have listed in the store could start decreasing in price. Like 1% per week. Then people who are starting late, or casual players don’t have quite the same inflationary pressure

That’s all, thanks for reading.
