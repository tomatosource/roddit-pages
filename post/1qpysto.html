What does baseball mean to you? How did you get into it, and why is it your favorite sport (if it is)?

I recently came back from a trip to Japan, and while I was there, even though it was during the off-season, I could see firsthand just how passionate people there were about baseball, and how it has become woven into the DNA of modern Japanese culture.

That got me thinking: what does baseball mean to you? What is your story of how you got into the sport, and if it’s your favorite sport, why?

For me, growing up in LA I was mostly a fan of the Lakers and Dodgers. I did get into basketball first, but got into baseball shortly after. Even though this was during the period of 2010-2012, I still remember hearing Vin Scully’s voice and watching the Dodgers for so many nights. In addition, being Korean-American, I grew up with both cultures, and I sometimes had an identity crisis. But baseball was one of the biggest things that bridged my ancestral homeland’s culture with the United States’, given how popular baseball is in Korea and how many Korean MLB players there are. My mom’s favorite player growing up was Park Chan Ho, and my favorite was Ryu Hyun Jin. Over time, I kinda fell off of basketball (mostly because the Lakers were bad for most of the 2010s), but also because basketball just never had the same cultural bridge effect that baseball had to me. Shortly after following the Dodgers I started playing baseball, and even now baseball is far and away my favorite sport.

Nowadays I’d say football and the Rams are my second-favorite sport, but even if we had an NFL team growing up, it wouldn’t have had the same cultural appeal that baseball had for me.

What about you guys, though? How did you guys get into baseball, and why is it your favorite sport (if it is)?
