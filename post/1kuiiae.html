There has been a blown 2-0 lead in 8 of the last 9 Playoffs as well as each of the last four. With both teams up 2-0 in the Conference Finals, will this continue?

2024: #6 Pacers over #2 Knicks (4-3) in ECSF

2023: #6 Warriors over #3 Kings (4-3) in First Round

2022: #4 Mavericks over #1 Suns (4-3) in WCSF

2021: #4 Clippers over #5 Mavericks (4-3) in First Round, #3 Bucks over #2 Nets in ECSF (4-3), #4 Clippers over #1 Jazz in WCSF (4-2), #3 Bucks over #2 Suns in NBA Finals (4-2)

2020: None

2019: #2 Raptors over #1 Bucks (4-2) in ECF

2018: #4 Cavaliers over #2 Celtics (4-3) in ECF

2017: #1 Celtics over #8 Bulls (4-2) in First Round

2016: #5 Trail Blazers over #4 Clippers (4-2) in First Round, #1 Cavaliers over #1 Warriors (4-3) in NBA Finals

There has yet to be a blown 2-0 lead this postseason. Both the Thunder and Pacers are up 2-0 currently, leaving the opportunity open for this trend to continue.

Edit: For those wondering, the 2017 Celtics and 2021 Clippers both did this after losing the first two at home. Either way, going up 2-0 as the road team is something that prior to this year didn’t happen very often so there’s not a large sample size for it.
