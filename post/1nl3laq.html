Outdated Exchange Rates on Trade Site Are Misleading New Players

It’s been over 10 days now and the exchange rate displayed on the official trade site still shows 1 Divine = ~117 Exalted Orbs. Meanwhile, the real player-driven market is closer to 285+ Exalts per Divine.

This discrepancy creates a serious problem for newer players. When they check the official trade site — which is supposed to be the most trusted resource — they see items listed using the outdated ratio and assume it’s accurate. In reality, they often end up overpaying by a huge margin without realizing it.

Annulment Orbs are one of the worst offenders, since their listed Divine/Exalt ratios don’t reflect what’s actually happening in trade. The end result is that newer or less-experienced players are effectively walking into bad deals simply because the “official” numbers aren’t updated.

Could we get these exchange rates updated more frequently (daily, or even hourly)? Even a rough automated adjustment would be better than leaving things stuck for weeks. Keeping the official trade data closer to reality would go a long way toward protecting new players and making the economy healthier for everyone.
