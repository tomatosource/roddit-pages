Since the start of the 2025 playoffs, OKC has lost only 3 home playoff games. All 3 came in Game 1, and every series went to a Game 7.

OKC’s only home playoff losses since the start of the 2025 playoffs:

Game 1 vs Denver (2025 WCSF) - OKC won series in 7

Game 1 vs Indiana (2025 NBA Finals) - OKC won series in 7

Game 1 vs San Antonio (2026 WCF) - Series now headed to Game 7

In all three series, OKC entered Game 7 coming off a Game 6 loss by an average of 19 points.

Thought this was a cool stat. If the Spurs win in OKC tomorrow, they’ll become the first team since the start of the 2025 playoffs to eliminate the Thunder after stealing Game 1 in Oklahoma City.

Obviously Haliburton’s injury is an important piece of context when looking at the Indiana series, but I still thought the pattern was interesting, small sample size and all.
