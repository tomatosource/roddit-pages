Seems I was the only person to go for a dual 2H CI IronGrasp strength stacker this league (not to mention it’s a smith)

(I think there’s still stuff to improve but as league is ending I figured someone might be interested in the knowledge this build can work)

Wanted to have some fun at the end of the league and went for homebrew smith with the constraint of using IronGrasp + Crown of eyes uniques. (+1% increased projectile & +1% attack damage per 2 strength)

Went for CI as life scaling is disabled due to IronGrasp. Also grabbed Giants blood and dual 2H mace due to no downside.

Vorana jewel then makes strength add ES to further profit on strength stacking.

Switched from sunder to molten blast as it double dips on the increased projectile and attack damage (and sunder is just painfully slow)

Combo of ingenuity + kalandra ring + breach ring maxes out all resistances due to smith ascension. Managed to get it 85% res (90% can be easily reached by swapping STR runes for max res runes)

It doesn’t instamelt bosses in 2s but I did manage to kill all bosses including Uber Arbiter (didn’t try simulacrum T3). Used budget of about 150div (it can be a lot cheaper imo, most if it went to items with more sockets or correctuped for a bit more strength.)
