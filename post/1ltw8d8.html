As the Utah Jazz continue to trade away their best players for pennies on the dollar, a reminder: Austin Ainge said the Jazz were “done with tanking this year”.

Newly named President of Basketball Operations Austin Ainge said the Utah Jazz will not be tanking in the 2025-26 season.

When asked if the Jazz would prioritize losing games to increase their odds of winning the 2026 NBA Draft lottery, Ainge offered a quick response.

“You won’t see that this year,” Ainge said.
