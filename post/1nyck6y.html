Deandre Ayton was a good player during his first 4 seasons

According to Basketball Reference

Team Performance with Ayton (Per 100 Possessions)

Season Record Ayton On Court On vs Off
2018-19 17W-54L -8.2 +1.8
2019-20 20W-18L +3.4 +4.5
2020-21 48W-21L +6.0 -0.2
2021-22 46W-12L +9.8 +3.7

In 3 of the 4 years, the Suns performed better when Ayton was on-court than they did when he was off-court. The only season they didn’t was in 2020-21. Even then, the Suns improved by only 0.2 points per 100 possessions when he was off court. When he was on court, they still outscored their opponents by 6.0 points per 100 possessions.

People act like Ayton was always trash. But the reality is that he was a good player and a positive contributor for 4 years. His attitude became toxic only after the Suns tried to low-ball him out of a max contract in 2022.
