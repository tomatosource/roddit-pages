Great year but we weren’t as good as the hype made it out to be

So I was as frustrated as the next fan when we lost last night, but after sleeping it off and really reading people opinion on the matter. I feel a lot of people just based their opinion on a team off of recency only and doesn’t really pay attention to the underlying issues of the team they talking about. First off to anyone bashing the team just remember to start the season. Mainstream fans never expected us to be here nor did we even expect Tatum to even be available as soon as he was. so while is disappointing to see us loose so early I’m extremely proud for what we did for the majority of the season. Also, I want to be clear i’m not trying to make excuses for the team. I do still think our squad dropped the ball collectively and could’ve went a lot further. This post is simply to explain why I don’t think this team was really as dominant as the regular season made us look. So here’s my points

1.76ers is NOT are true play-in team: The team was floating around top playoffs to start the season, injuries, and a suspension dropped them down to play-in level Embiid missed 44 games, PG missed 45, Kelly missed 32, Maxey missed 12, even the VJ the rook missed games and if you really dive deep, you’ll notice pretty much the entire roster dealt with injuries that kept them out 10+ games

2.The Jays and the team, for that matter still struggle against physicality: Every loss in the past 5 years came from a team that just overwhelmed them physically 2022 Warriors 2023 Heat 2025 Knicks and 2026 76ers

3.Derrick White’s shooting: Not sure if he was dealing with injuries all season, or the pressure of playing a bigger role overwhelmed him, and he still provides pretty much everything else he’s giving us since he got here. But the regression on shooting is a little concerning. We all kept hoping all season that he will figure it out, but he struggled badly, and a good portion of his misses was open. In all honesty, White, Hauser, and Prich shooting is the biggest swing factor for our series going forward. So if any of those guys aren’t shooting well it makes our series a lot more difficult

4.Our role players are Inexperienced: For a lot of players this is their first year playing real playoff extended minutes (Prich and Nemi was bottom of rotation last year)( Baylor, Ron, Walsh, Garza DNP last year) (Hugos first year in the league)

5.Those role players were also just specialist: one of the issues I see with how we are developing our young player is, a lot of guys are taught to play a role and if one of the core players is out there’s more flexibility in that role. The problem with that in the playoffs when the stars get canceled out, nobody else knows how to or is too scared to create or play outside the system. this is the only year since 2021 that we didn’t have an option who could step up outside of Prich who struggled. Before we had guys like Jrue, Brogdon, Kristaps, and Horford who could have big games when we need it

6.Vucevic isn’t it: Considering Vucevic is also an expiring like Anfernee, he clearly doesn’t fit our roster, and Anfernee would’ve been a solution for the previous stated point. That trade was a rare bad trade for Brad. Although the extra cap space may be a blessing in disguise if he walks (expectations) or takes a big cut. Regardless never was a fan of the trade then, warmed up to it early on, and by the time Vucevic came back from injury I was back to being sour on the trade. A lot of times this season, including in the playoffs Garza just look like a better player for us.

7.We really need an Al replacement: not only was Al our best defender against big offensive player like Embiid but he was also extremely versatile on both end and can play just about any rotation. Nemi and Garza are like splitting Al’s versatility/role between two players. Vucevic as stated previously, I just don’t see in our future.

8.Joe is a great coach, but: He needs to diversify our offense. It’s been three years and we still don’t have a solution for when teams are physical with our guys other than shooting our way into getting blown out or possibly getting a win.
