Ben Simmons and his fishing team, The South Florida Sails, are champions of the Walker’s Cay Open fishing tournament

Ben Simmons has not officially retired from the NBA after his last season with the Clippers, but it doesn’t look like he’s coming back anytime soon. It’s really great to see Simmons succeed in another sport after all the injuries and difficulties he faced in the NBA. Simmons and his team caught six blue marlin and three sailfish to win it all.
