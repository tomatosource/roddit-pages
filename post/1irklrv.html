PoE 2 Does Not Feel Like A Multiplayer ARPG

First I want to start by saying I love PoE and so far PoE 2 has been a great experience as a player. When it comes to multiplayer content though, aside from the market, nothing in this game feel inherently multiplayer. The pinnacle boss may be the only gameplay content that can be considered for this but not likely since plenty of single players can almost one shot him.

I would love to see PoE 2 find a way to introduce more multiplayer specific content. Raids, Maps, mini missions, bosses, I know that’s not what PoE was but I don’t see why it’s sequel can’t expand more appropriately to fill this void? I find myself getting bored on PoE2 and if this is all it’ll be od rather go back to PoE since it’s already fully fleshed out and has plenty of content to stay busy
