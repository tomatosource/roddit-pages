Can we get an omen or something to make getting T16 maps easier or less tedious?

Someone in chat said that the chance to get downgraded to T14 is equal to the chance to upgrade to T16 but from what I’ve seen that’s not the case. I ALWAYS get more T14 than T16.

We need an omen or an orb that only works on waystones that reduces this process to one click instead of 6. Make it drop often or give us a way to target farm it.
