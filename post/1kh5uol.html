GGG why you don’t encourage build diversity but offer many options to pretend that you do? (Repost after correction)

The biggest flaw in POE2 right now is lack of FUN build diversity. I know what’s feeding it!

I Know someone will jump on here and say it’s plenty of ways to play. It’s not plenty of FUN ways to play. Nobody cares about a build that can’t clear content past Tier 10. Or the builds that can’t get you through 3rd or 4th ascendancy. (The opinion part )Those builds are booty and won’t take you to any of the true endgame content. (Back to the facts) GGG creates all these different gems and weapons to say “Ima leave it to you guys to figure some cool stuff out” How can you figure stuff out when each skill gem and unique has huge trade offs that discourage experimentation? There are several skill gems that make no sense to exist because the function of the gem is sooo NICHE that you will have to use POB and two calculators to to solve for the tradeoffs to have fun with it. I have so many uniques just sitting in my stash because I can’t use them to make a functional build that’s fun.

Builds have to be at LEAST capable of survival or high DPS to stand a chance of being used. Nobody wants to use a chaos gem to attempt a chaos build if the build won’t be able to kill anything past tier 3 map. What’s the point of a melee unique if the trade off is 20% reduced movement? What’s the point of a cold damage unique weapon if the starting DPS point is sooo low and has a stun enemies requirement to even trigger the good stuff out of it? Why have a gem that need 3 things to happen before the gem activates?

GGG you have too much complexity in the way of having fun and exploring all the gear that you create. You need more gems and gear that can be used without so many tradeoffs that make the unique trash out the gate. You need to release more gems that change your build potential like “Volt”. Almost all the gems have so many tradeoffs that’s discouraging.

Also lastly, attribute requirements for builds to function are still too high. Why do I have to focus on defeating the monsters and then have to figure out how can I use the reward I was given ALL THE TIME?
