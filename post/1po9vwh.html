Overdesign of skills, part 2 - Acolyte of Chayula build

I was posting here how skills are generally overdesigned and needlessly specific and complicated. Here is another example, a completely different one.

I am now focusing on Acolyte of Chayula and what the game has to offer for it. I am trying to make some things work which are not used very often (not Darkness, I am not that derranged)

Trying out a very basic Crit monk with some obvious interactions with the ascenancy. It’s a complete and utter mess. Here is some of the fun that overdesign brings along.

Timers you generally should pay attention to.

  1. Volatility 1: timer for the debuff, and refreshing it with crits - 6s

  2. Volatility 2: timer of the acutal buff after explosion, which you should pay attention to in order to refresh it when necessary - 10s

  3. Mantra of destruction - very important buff for refreshing extra choas damage (yes another buff with a timer) - 9s

  4. power charges - about 15s - important for charged staff

  5. charged staff - extra hits for volatility, damage, shock - 25s

  6. and 7) purple flames of chayula - since there are 2 sources of those - 10s and 20s

  7. misc - there are some other small buffs and timers - like a curse from hand of Ch 7s., main skill buff from Shattering Palm - 9s and some small support gems related one.

9)unholy might from a command skill - (later addition) - 15s

there you go. This basic, leveling build you can start mapping with has about 10-11 different timers, each with its own duration and starting and ending time. some you need to play attention more, some less, but overall, it is a MASSIVE pain to play.

There will be some improvements and QoL options later on, like charge generation which will be so simple you don’t have to pay attention much, but generally this is it.
