Rare monster modifiers should be limited to specific monster types.

TL;DR: Certain modifiers should be restricted from being put on certain monsters, because of the likelihood of those modifiers creating unfairly difficult rare monsters that people have to deal with in mapping early on.

Buddy of mine had one of those warthog guys (forget what the name is, the ones that charge at you that look like giant warthogs) as a rare last night with Haste and Shrouded as modifiers. He was playing as a lightning spear invoker monk and could not get away from that rare to do any of his skills because it kept teleporting to him stunlocking him and killing him. I watched him use up i think 5 revives to kill this monster.

It really feels unfair to have those modifiers on that specific monster type because of how quick it can attack already without any modifiers to start with. With Haste and its bleed potential, that bleed potential becomes a certainty to happen. Add in the teleport from shrouded, and that makes for a ridiculously hard monster to fight. He just got to mapping and is only probably 70 (i’d have to ask him), on his first character this league. But i feel like at that point you really should not have to deal with monsters like that, unless you have some ridiculous movement speed of your own, a really high amount of dps, or a high evasion rating.

I haven’t really run into many rares like the one my friend ran into but the fact that rng can spit out a monster like that, should be addressed by GGG.
