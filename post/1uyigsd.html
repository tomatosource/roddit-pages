Warriors’ Yaxel Lendeborg ‘a little worried’ about Draymond Green’s ‘rein in the rookie’ remark

SRC: https://www.sfchronicle.com/sports/warriors/article/warriors-yaxel-lendeborg-little-worried-22347177.php

The latest exchange occurred when Green, on his podcast released Thursday morning, called Lendeborg’s pitch to free agent LeBron James “pointless.” Green also cryptically referenced training camp as a time he could “rein in the rookie a little bit.”

Lendeborg heard Green’s comments, even if he wasn’t entirely sure how to interpret them. In a Chronicle interview later Thursday at the team hotel in Las Vegas, Lendeborg acknowledged the clip caught his attention.

“I got a little nervous,” he said, laughing. “I don’t know what he’s talking about, man. He could be hinting at a lot of different things. Hopefully it’s not the (rookie) hazing — I hope we keep that at a minimum.

“I hope it’s just like, ‘Yeah, I got you. This is what you should and shouldn’t do.’ So I’m a little worried, but hopefully everything goes well.”
