Build your own Mercenary/Follower “Metamorph” style League Mechanic.

GGG are great at reusing previous content, and I love the idea in PoE 2 of having a League Mechanic combining “Metamorph” and “Mercenaries”, to create your own Follower.

Thematically, Doryani seems great for this, with his dark and gritty experimentations. I would imagine it to be a bit like Qyburn in Game of Thrones, who essentially resurrects the Mountain.

The League Mechanic would work as follows….

You stumble upon Doryani in each zone, who is “Hunting” for the strongest/most powerful people to use for his experiments (Mercenaries).

Killing them allows you to ‘extract’ either a skill gem they use, or a support gem/aura gem, which Doryani will then infuse with his creation, allowing you to put together your ideal set of Skills/Supports/Auras. Doryani allows you to take the follower and use it, so that he can gather information to enhance his future creations. Over time you can return to make enhancements, changing skills, supports etc. Whilst you can equip gear at any time in the wild.

How does the Mechanic differ from PoE 1?

- You can’t take Currency/Gear from the Mercenary in each zone. Their interface has no Currency/Gear, they only have Skills/Supports/Auras.

- You simply kill them and “Extract” a Skill Gem, Support Gem or Aura Gem.

- Skill Gems and Aura Gems all go up to level 20. With each zone including at least 1 Gem with a minimum level of ‘X’, depending on the difficulty of the Zone.

- Support Gems work like the current Mercenary System in 3.26, where there are different tiers and a “Golden” tier.

- “Doryani’s Creations”, which are your ‘Metamorphed’ followers, do have an interface for Gear. Unlike with Mercenaries in 3.26, they start with no gear. Which means all Gear dropped in the world has an even greater value, even at the very beginning of the game.
