Armour Damage Reduction Table

Top row in red represents raw damage, while left italic column represents armour value, cross-section representing damage you are going to take

I wanted to make a small project to see effectiveness or armour on extended values (to also observe 200%+ armour applies to elemental damage). You can use it to compare how changes on armour values on patch notes affected your characters tankiness.

I believe problem with armour is low defensive pool amount, usually around 3.5k life or lower. To solve this problem, going hybrid to achieve 5-6k HP and Energy shield total is very much possible. When you are reducing the damage you take, every point in your life pool becomes much more important.

Problem with this approach is your energy shield getting constantly chipped away in combat, basicly making it non-existent especially if you are a melee character with low range and slow animations. To solve this, Zealoths Oath is a perfectly fine candidate and i will be utilising it this league.

How do i plan to amass regen you may ask, thing is i believe even around 200-300 regen would be perfectly fine. When you think es recharge can go upto “1.5 bowyer/sec” levels of recovery, it may seem low. But if we define our goals it makes perfect sense. We try to protect our ES againist chip damage, and armour againist chip damage does a really good job. In a scenario an ES character would lose 1500 shield by taking 500 dmg 3 times, even 10k armour (Which could be easily around 25k with some investment both in gear and skill tree) could reduce it upto 66% and result in 500 damage taken in total. In this scenario, it is very much possible to keep up the energy shield with 200-300 regen, especially with higher armour values.
