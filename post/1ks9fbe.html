We have two options this offseason: Try & compete or trade JB

Lots of posts about this subject, but these are the only two options I see. I am in the camp of we should probably keep JB unless a godfather offer comes in that gives us the opportunity to immediately retool. We obviously are going to try to get under the 2nd apron, but that doesn’t mean we can’t be competitive. I think a lot of what we do depends on the prognosis of JT and his recovery. If he is out the entirety of next season I think trading JB becomes a real idea, if he has any chance of returning next year it may change the ideology of ownership and management.
