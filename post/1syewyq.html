Killing off the most expensive uniques shows POE 2 is still lacking in depth

So, this late in the league, I decided after trying multiple times to find a non-bait build (and losing a ton of divs in the process of experimenting), to do a little thought experiment.

I went to POE.ninja, and decided to see how things stacked up once I removed the most popular and expensive ‘build definers’ in POE 2 (majority were lineage gems) - expensive being items ranking as more than 20 div.

I removed the following from the filter:

  • Atziri’s Splendour
  • Megalomaniac
  • Prism of Belief
  • Uhtred’s Omen/Exodus/Augury
  • Breach lineage gems (Pyre, Radiance, Stillness)
  • Rakiata’s flow
  • Atalui’s Bloodletting
  • Garukhan’s Resolve

Just those as a start.

Once you kill those, pretty much you are left with Pathfinder poison arrow, Plasma Blast Bloodmage, and Disciple of Varashta Comet.

Pathfinder makes natural sense, but the point is, you don’t see a variety. That Bloodmage in this case uses Plasma Blast speaks to the lack of a good spell skill or two for them to use that can be scaled and pushed, that don’t require equipment that costs an arm and a leg to obtain.

Same argument can be made for all the Comet builds, which oddly seem to mostly go away once you take all the expensive stuff away, with the exception of Disciple of Varashta.

Don’t get me wrong - I am NOT making an argument against these expensive items. But my point is, from a scalability perspective, the game is out of whack and feels constrictive, because there so many of those expensive items are required for a build to “feel good” to play.

I think a pass needs to be done on the skills in the game. Not making the argument for skills to be overpowered, but I do think that each weapon type needs at least 2 skills that a player can easily scale into T15 red maps and the initial round of bosses, and then have the harder content gated behind the more expensive items to really push your build.

The lack of viable variety is hurting the game IMHO - the game punishes you for exploring your skills and passive tree instead of rewarding it, and based on early interviews with the developers, I don’t think that’s what they originally intended (especially given we can see they pulled a lot of power and utility out of ascendencies in POE 2 compared to POE 1, and put them in other areas in the game…)
