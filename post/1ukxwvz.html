The Tatum of it all.

Since it’s the first of the month and it looks like free agency is in full swing, i do have a question that i have not seen discussed: If Brown is traded and we don’t make any more splash moves, do we think Tatum can carry this team? There were a lot of questions on whether Brown could in Tatum’s absence but can Tatum also do that?

My thought is yes because Tatum is at worst the 7th-8th best player in the league when healthy. However an Achilles injury is going to be a career long issue. Tatum is no longer 19 anymore so that will be a question as well. Brown proved that he is a serious force to be reckoned with but can Tatum do the same for this team?

I have found recently when talking to other fans that this whole Tatum and Brown debacle has a few more layers than just who is better and frankly I have not interest in that. But, do we believe that Tatum is good enough to take what is, save for a few pieces, essentially the same team to a similar or better position?
