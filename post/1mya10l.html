Keystone suggestion - cooldown spellcasting play-style - please GGG

Could either be implemented as a Keystone or as a support gem (support probably easier).

Keystone example:

  • Non-curse Spells you cast have 1000% of base cast time added as cooldown
  • 200% more Cost of spells
  • 30% less cast speed
  • 200% more damage with spells
  • 2 extra skill gem slots

This would enable a cooldown gated spellcasting playstyle where you are forced to have a wider selection of spells and cast then more carefully since you have less cast speed. But you do way higher dmg per spell and more dmg per time spent casting.

This is basically a implementation of Unleash staff/ascendancy that automatically triggers for every spell.
