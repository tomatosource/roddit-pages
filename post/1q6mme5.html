The process of what to do, I focused on sapphire jewels with 20% Max Energy Shield FRACTURED. This is the best stat I found to be fractured, I tried with all the different crit hit chance/damage bonus but I inevtibly always wanted energy shield anyway. A Couple Bases. Buying the base already fractured ranged from 5 Divines if I was lucky to (unfortunately) now 25⁄30 Divines! Average price was approximately 18 Divines for most of my fractured jewels. But with a selling price of 80+ div a lot of the time, the lowest it sold for was 40 divines normally.

The Method, starting with fractured 20% Max Energy Shield, you would annul down to 1 mod, and… you guessed it! Chaos orb spam! What we are looking for are any Critical mods so that is Crit hit chance/spell hit chance and Crit damage bonus/spell bonus. I would chaos till I hit and then exalt, hope to hit another crit mod or I found mana per kill (I mainly went with double crit mods, but some mana on kill ones started to sell for a fair bit). Side note, if you roll a low % on your crit values while chaos orbing, you can keep spamming till you hit a good one, I did not, if I hit a crit mod, I send my exalt and would then divine my jewels till both crit mods were close to max (probably more expensive my way but it worked out often enough).

Ideal world, you have Max ES % fractured, chaos orb till crit modifier, exalt another crit/mana on kill as your 3rd mod. Divined to a high number on both mods. You would now desecrate to get an abyss modifier on it. ALWAYS use the echos to be able to reroll this modifier. What I found to be some of the best mod to search for in the abyss are Spell dmg + Max ES together. Spell/Cold/Ele dmg, magnitude of ailments with crit hits, Ele dmg + ailment, any Max ES + ___ (could be att dmg ,minion dmg, etc) Spell dmg + evasion, cold penetration, dmg with plantskills.

A lot of good choices, aim to hit that Spell dmg + ES shield as it seems to be the one that sells the most. The others are good choices but sometimes you low roll the stat and will have to get lucky diving 3 mods now instead of 2.

If you decide to use a crit mod that has been fractured, you nearly always want to hit Max ES anyway so it makes it take a bit longer to hit this with chaos/annul orb spam.
