Spirit Gem Level Ups Feel Like A Punishment

Not always of course. But I feel like often times the attribute cost (if it’s a non primary attribute) is way too high for a marginal improvement.

I can’t be the only one who feels this way, but for normal Skill Gems I’m excited to find a higher level gem because I can do more damage.

But if I see higher level Spirit Gem often times I’m thinking:

Ah fuck, do I have enough STR/DEX/INT to increase my damage by 0.01%? I think I won’t bother, I’ll check again 5 levels later.
