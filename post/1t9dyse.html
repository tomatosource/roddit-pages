Five months ago, I made a post that Lauri Markkanen and Miles Bridges were on the precipice of reaching the top-10 all-time in career regular season games without a postseason appearance. Bridges has made the list. Markkanen has not.

Link to original post: Lauri Markkanen and Miles Bridges both have an opportunity this season to reach the top-10 all-time in career regular season games with zero playoff appearances. : r/nba)

Link to list: Most Games, No Playoffs | Basketball-Reference.com

Due to the Hornets losing steam in the final play-in game against the Orlando Magic, the Hornets have missed the playoffs for the eighth straight season that they have employed Miles Bridges. He is now eighth all-time in career regular season games with zero postseason appearances.

Markkanen did not make the cut this year as the Utah Jazz prioritized the health of the former All-Star, only playing in nine of the team’s final forty-three games, good to put him in twelfth.

Going into next season, another active name to keep an eye on is Collin Sexton, currently fifteenth and who is only 25 games from passing Doug Overton for tenth next season (assuming Lauri makes the list next season as well).
