Season Summary

First of all it was a pleasure and great fun watching so many new guys step up on the big stage and cover the gap to the top. There were times even in playoff we were looking forward to some of them getting more time.

The regular season was great fun, good games and so much energy and positivity by the team that it took the game to the league, as the team to beat.

JB stepped up big time and it was great to see him. There is still something in clutch games where he needs to figure out how to get us wins. Also when JT is on field, he needs to play his own brand of game more.

JT came back soon for us, the team and himself but he came back good. Whatever we saw from him was good and positive.

Playoff were disappointing especially after a promising season and a 3-1 start. Mis matches or bad shots are real reason but lack of energy or effort is not. Not playing our own brand of basketball (looking at you game 5) is not acceptable. Letting them keep the door open is not acceptable and they just stormed in and we started to get swept in their rhythm which is never a good thing especially when you are the Celtics.

We need to find a better starting 5 who can give us momentum in first 6 min and not play catch from beginning till the 4th. Building from the one we have is possible but if not we can look at Brad. Signing off with lot of expectation for next season.

Thank you to all the fans and plastic fans, we need you all.
Shout out to the entire Celtic team, staff and anyone who work in background.
