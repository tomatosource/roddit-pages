[Wright Gazaway] Internal emails reveal Blazers debated responding to Portland’s Moda Center questions

“Internal emails obtained by KATU show Portland Trail Blazers leaders debated whether to respond to the City of Portland’s questions for commitments on private investment, rent, labor agreements and other key pieces of a proposed Moda Center deal, with one executive saying the city’s requests felt like “a trap.”

… Blazers lawyer Zandira Conyers stating “[W]e are not looking for a legal response but more so positioning, anticipating that our responses will be public. Which brings me to my next point - I don’t think our public answers will be well-received. And I wonder how the councilors plan to use them. I want to think about that and welcome thoughts from you guys,”

She continued: “In the meantime, we can work on answers but the more I think about it - the less I like the idea. Maybe we should think about questions to pose back that paint us in a positive light. But that just leads to more distraction and inefficiency….soooo…I’m just thinking of different issues. The fact that they were so insistent on a written response was weird. But I question EVERYTHING.”

President of Blazers business operations Dewayne Hankins, responded shortly after to Conyers and said the team should discuss “whether we even respond to this.”

This exchange is notable as it occurred a month before city councilor Novick toyed with the idea of suing the team in the event they leave. The team has since stated Novick’s statement chilled negotiations, however these emails prove the Blazers front office refused to respond to the city a month prior to these statements.
