Ice Shot is the Best Skill

Hey everyone. My name is Redshift and I’ve been playing PoE1 and PoE2 for a long time.

I really, really like the skill Ice Shot. I don’t really want to play skills that aren’t Ice Shot.
Why?
I don’t really know. I can’t explain it. It is my skill. I loved it in PoE 1, and I love it in PoE 2.

So, I made a build for Ice Shot. So far, I’ve cleared the following content:

Xesht 4
Olroth 4
Ritual 4
Deli 1

Which is pretty good progress especially since most people think I’m insane. It really excels in mapping content, even T16 deli maps. Content that you can’t clear using Ice Shot probably sucks anyway and I don’t even want to do it.

There are no special gimmicks with the build. You press ice shot in the direction of enemies and they die. Sometimes you get the extreme pleasure of being able to press Ice Shot several times. This is probably the best part.

There aren’t any odd interactions or strange things going on. Everything is in service of making Ice Shot better. You have Acrobatics from Hyrri’s, a decent amount of life for a ranger (2k), and 85% dodge with 10 stacks of tailwind. I don’t bother with ES because I haven’t felt like I needed to.

I use Cadiro’s Gambit as a quiver because it makes Ice Shot really, really cool (heh).

The build could be better. Much better. Probably. I’m not smart. I am just a guy who likes Ice Shot.

Usually when I ask people what they think, they recommend I use a different skill in addition to Ice Shot, which is a problem because that additional skill is not Ice Shot. I do not want to use skills that aren’t called Ice Shot. Although you will occasionally catch me using Bow Shot as a storage device for gems or to recover mana in long fights with Soul Thief. But that is in service of using more Ice Shots, so it is acceptable.

It is not an efficient or incredibly fast way to play, and you will need to know boss mechanics, but it is extremely fun because you get to use Ice Shot. People always think one button skill users want to blow up whole screens. This is not the case. Some of us just want to use Ice Shot.

While rolling maps, I skip %monster life, temp chains and enfeeble because those make things feel awful.
