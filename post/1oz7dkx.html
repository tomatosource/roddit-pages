Create your all-time team for your franchise without using teammates

A late night thought experiment which may or may not work.

I’m a Blue Jays fan. If I wanted, say, Josh Donaldson as my all time 3rd baseman, I can’t use Edwin or Jose, as they were Donaldson’s teammates. So if I say John Olerud is my all-time 1st baseman, I can’t have Robbie Alomar or Joe Carter.

This exercise will benefit teams with a long history. I wanted to share this game before I forgot.

Thanks all. Looking forward to seeing everyone’s all-time, no-teammate team.

EDIT: Alomar not Alonso.
