My honest problems with POE 2

I know that this game is still EA and therefore will go under a lot of other developments, and I’ve actually enjoyed this game so far (or at least for the most part). However, I still want to address a few things that seriously worsen my experience with POE 2 and suggest some improvements (these are just my opinions, so feel free to agree or disagree with them). My character is an Amazon, 93, capped res, 2.5k ES, 1.6k HP, got ES leech and 4x LAEK, 100k DPS for LS at Hideout, so it’s definitely not a squishy one.

1. Unknown causes of death

One major issue that I believe a lot of people have raised is they just don’t know why the hell they die in a map. Dying is really frustrating even in softcore due to experience loss (if you’re mid-9x and mostly play solo you’ll see how painful it is to suddenly lose a bunch of exp for unknown reasons).

Sometimes I know it’s the volatile things, but many other times I just stepped into an area and then suddenly died. Even worse, sometimes the game just DID NOT load the mobs and effects immediately, and I died without even seeing anything.

Suggestion: Please at least add the Cause of death when a character dies, so that I know what the hell just killed me and avoid it next time.

2. Delayed images and effects

Whenever I start the game, I always experience the situation when it takes a while for the mobs and their effects in a map to load properly. In the first one or two maps after starting the game, it always feels like I’m fighting invisible mobs because I just throw spears and mobs die without me even seeing them. This also applies to whatever the hell mods they have and of course their telegraphed attacks.

Suggestion: Just fix this ASAP.

3. Temporal chains on waystones

I particularly have a problem with Temporal Chains debuff on a waystone. The game already feels kinda slow at some points already, so I don’t think we need something to make it even slower. I’m find with other waystone mods but TC is really a pain in the ass, especially for someone who doesn’t like taking Rhoa mount.

Suggestion: Just remove this please!

4. Ridiculous mods on unique tablets

I really like the idea of having unique tablets, but I find Wraeclast Besieged (Breach) and Vision of Paradise (Irradiate) to be particularly unpleasant to use rather than enjoyable.

First, WB forces you kill almost every breach mob, regardless of their types, in order to close the breach and get the splinters. This is really frustrating, especially after you have already finished the map (who would want to run around over and over in a badly designed map just to find a few white mobs?). I ended up skipping the breach close several times because of this.

Second, VoP literally makes you run a map TWICE just to complete it and move to another map because in the first time you just remove irradiation and then have to do it again without irradiation (which means -1 area level). This is really time-consuming when you want to quickly go through maps to reach a Citadel or unique map. This can be partly solved if you also have The Grand Project (which is a great unique tablet), but the problem is you don’t always have both of them.

Suggestion: change the mods of these tablets to something more enjoyable. For example, WB may allow players to close breach when they want, or VoP allows them to see an area beyond the tower’s range or even irradiate a unique map.

I won’t talk about Volatile Plants or some other stuff because people have been talking about that a lot already.

Just feel free to add any of your opinions in the comment section. And I hope GGG will read this post :D
