11 different champions in 14 years

11 different champions in 14 years and only one dynasty, we’re in the most wide-open era ever

The last 8 years being 8 different champs is brought up often, but if you cut off at 2013 it’s even crazier. Every NBA champion since then:

Heat, Spurs, Warriors, Cavs, Warriors, Warriors, Raptors, Lakers, Bucks, Warriors, Nuggets, Celtics, Thunder, Knicks.

That’s 14 seasons, 11 different franchises and the only repeat is the Warriors winning 4.

Think about how insane that is compared to the Celtics/Lakers 80s, the Jordan Bulls, the Duncan Spurs and 00-s Lakers.

For most of league history it’s been all about dynasties, but not anymore. Crazy how it coincides with Adam Silver taking over the league in 2014.

Who could continue the trend next year? Wolves, Rockets, Sixers, Pistons or Pacers are the likeliest candidates, but they are all underdogs against Spurs, Knicks, Thunder or Nuggets.
