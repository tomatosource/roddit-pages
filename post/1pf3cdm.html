Stats say we’ve never seen this many great foul drawers in a single year

If the season ended today, 2026 would have by far the biggest cluster of all-time foul-drawing greats we’ve ever seen.

Free-throw rate (FTr) = free throws attempted per field goal attempt.

League average usually jz between ~.22 and .30, depending largely on how often teams get to the rim. In a normal year, you might see one to three non role-playing, non–“hack this big man” with an FTr above .50,

Guys who score at real volume and whom defenses genuinely don’t want to foul.

In ** 2024** that group was basically Jimmy Butler (.58) and Giannis (.57).

Go back to 2012 : it’s only Harden, and he’s scoring under 20 PPG.

This year?

We’re at six or seven (don’t say it) of these guys: Butler (.67!), Avdija (.59), Giannis (.57), Reaves (.556), Luka (.522), Shai (.514), and Grant (.511 — you can quibble about his raw scoring volume, but per 100 possessions still solid).

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2026_advanced.html
