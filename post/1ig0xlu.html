How long have you been a C’s fan?

Basketball enthusiast and Celtics fan from Sweden here. Just realized next month (march) is my 10 year anniversary as a Celtics fan. I had gotten interested in basketball in 2014, at the time basketball culture was pretty much nonexistent in Sweden. A year later me and my dad went to visit some relatives in Boston and of course we had to attend a home game at the garden.

IT, Avery, Turner and the boys were facing the Toronto Raptors and unfortunately had a horrible game despite IT doing his magic. Then and there I decided that I was going to be a Celtics fan, seemed reasonable enough considering the family ties and who wouldn’t like to root for the little guy, something about him just made him feel very much more like a human being than most players due to his size, a true underdog. Started following everything Celtics after that and I remember streaming the 2017 draft with JT being selected at 3am right after midsummers eve.

Well that’s about it, how long have you been a c’s fan and what’s your story?
