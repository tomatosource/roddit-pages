Just started POE2 again and wanted to share my experience while also asking some questions and starting a discussion

Disclaimer: I am not a good poe1 player. Furthest I got was t6 maps with a whirlwind build. It was a build guide I was following, and if memory serves me correctly it was in hardcore. Since then I played poe1 here and there but it never stuck, and my interest in it was mostly due to having a hazy image of a power fantasy once my build comes together. This interest was always short lived and I would give up quite fast.

I have been thoroughly enjoying poe2 and while I still haven’t finished the campaign, I have no shame in saying that poe2 is a superior game, period. In all honesty the memes about pob in poe1 being the real game appears true. Get ready for a hot take, but I believe that poe1 is a glorified incremental clicker game, and while I enjoy a quick simple dopamine rush, that’s all it is.

The reason I am writing this is because I wish to know whether I have the wrong idea about the public reception poe2 got. From my understanding people wanted poe1 with additional content, and the reception of poe2 is generally pessimistic. This honestly hurts me personally. Not to sound overly dramatic but it also makes me lose hope in the gaming industry, or maybe even humanity in general.

Games are supposed to be worlds you experience and interact with directly. I don’t wish to get too philosophical as to what constitutes a world, or the experience of the world but the defining prerequisite seems to be meaningful interaction( abilities, movement…) and the ability to perceive the world. If I understand GGGs vision correctly they do appear to be moving in the direction of gameplay being in the game world itself, instead of in spreadsheets, and pob.

For clarity sake, in spite of sounding cliche, poe2 appears to be a soulslike. I don’t think people give them enough credit for this, and the Herculean task they set themselves up for. Getting the visual clarity necessary to make a soulslike work from an isometric perspective and not to mention having to balance around items that are generated and not firmly designed.

This is what I would love to hear GGGs opinion on. If anyone knows of a talk, interview or something similar where this is mentioned I would be grateful if you linked it. I have been playing poe2 exclusively on hardcore from the start, and despite dying I still see my self coming back, because that’s the world and that’s life. One thing I have noticed that I am missing, or at least I think I am missing is world information inside the world itself. Now they have done a lot about communicating info on conceptual information( keyword definitions, categories), but this once more pulls me out of the game and into pob, and once I am in the game makes my eyes unconsciously gravitate towards the minimap. The information I am speaking of here has to do with enemy information( dmg type, skills, behavior) at least in the campaign. Using the wiki or a video to learn of this feels wrong, yet dying to a boss knowing you could have done more before fighting him completely blind just leaves a sour taste. I would love to know if this preparation before a fight( darkest dungeon style) is something you would encourage, or is trial and error the intended experience. Your stance on death screen information is also something I would love to hear about.

I would also love to hear the stance of endgame poe1 players. Seeing endgame gameplay of poe1 makes me lose all interest in the game, and seeing that the endgame gameplay( I am not speaking about the content, I am speaking about the feel of the game, one click builds, and the ability to zoom through areas with your eyes glued to the minimap without dying) in poe2 hasn’t really changed that much also made me disappointed. Hearing that they are balancing around disincentivizing that kind of gameplay got me excited once more. Would you as an endgame player still play the game if they truly succeed in making that gameplay impossible?
