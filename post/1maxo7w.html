I’m a relatively new Celtics fan from the UK and I’m curious, what would a “soft tank” actually look like?

With Tatum likely to miss the entire season, Holiday, Porzingis and Kornet all gone from the roster, (and with Horford also almost certain to leave), there seems to be a consensus amongst the media that 2025-26 will be a bridge year for the Celtics, which means they won’t be a championship contender. I have seen some stuff about the team “soft-tanking”. To me tanking is when a team deliberately tries to lose games to secure a higher draft pick. I believe it’s common in the NFL, but I am not sure about how effective tanking is in the NBA.

My first question is, how is soft-tanking different to hard-tanking? Is the HC likely to be telling the players in the locker room something along the lines of, “Alright guys, let’s try to be competitive out there, but not bust a gut trying to win”? I assume hard tanking would be more egregious, with the team basically getting stomped night after night and not really making an effort to win. Is that actually a thing? Also, assuming the upcoming season will be a down year (or bridge year), do you think attendances at TD Garden and TV ratings are likely to be significantly impacted? Do you think there will be a lot of empty seats visible on TV if the team ends up sucking?
