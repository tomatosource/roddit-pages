There are only two centers in the history of the NBA to have multiple 60 point games. Wilt Chamberlain and…

Karl-Anthony Towns.

Regular season only to my knowledge. It’s surprising since there have been tons of incredible centers but also, scoring 60 is a rare thing with only 39 players ever notching a 60 point game.

Nikola Jokic and Joel Embiid (70 point game) both have one 60 point game so they could still join this short list.

Nowadays it feels like you need a 3 point shot to put up that many points. KAT made 7 and 10 3-pointers in each of his 60-point games respectively.

Jokic put up 6 3-pointers in his 60 point game but shockingly, Embiid had just a singular 3 pointer in his 70 point game so 3 pointers definitely aren’t necessary.

Just something I thought was interesting. The End.
