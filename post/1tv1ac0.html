Championship odds history will be made regardless of the result of the NBA Finals this year

If the San Antonio Spurs win the NBA Finals, they will have the longest pre-season odds of any team to win the Finals in NBA history at 66-to-1 , or at least since odds have been tracked which is around 1984. The current record is the 2015 Warriors at 28-to-1. It would also be the 4th longest odds of any eventual champion among all 4 major US sports, behind the 1999 Rams, 1991 Twins, and 2003 Marlins

However, if the NY Knicks win the finals, that would also be historic in a slightly different way. They were 22-to-1 to win the NBA Finals prior to the start of the playoffs, factoring in their regular season. That would be the longest odds any eventual champion had prior to the start of the playoffs in NBA history (or since the oldest record in 1976). The current record is a tie between the 2011 Mavericks and 1995 Rockets, both at 18-to-1. In fact, the Knicks’ odds of 22-to-1 would also be tied for the 3rd longest odds among any eventual champ in all 4 major US sports, with only the 2007 Giants (50-to-1) and the 1997 Marlins (25-to-1) having longer odds prior to the playoffs starting, and tied with the 2011 Giants and 2012 LA Kings.
