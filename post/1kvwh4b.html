Is it me or is Knicks v Pacers the most fun/entertaining playoff series we’ve had in years?

I genuinely cannot remember the last time I was glued into every game of a playoff series like this (where I was a neutral fan).

Two historic franchises, high intensity play on both sides of the ball, passionate fans living and dying with each make or miss, top tier characters in Brunson, Towns, Halliburton, etc that show their emotions on the court. Dramatic 4th quarters in every game so far.

I don’t know what it is, but the stakes feel so much higher in this series than we’ve had in a long time, and the emotion and intensity from the fans/players/coaches is insanely entertaining. You can feel it through the broadcast with Stan + Reggie too. As a basketball fan I can’t get enough!
