Leveraging Free Agency in a Jaylen Brown Deal

Hey all, I just wanted to discuss a concept that I think could help us maximize our return on Jaylen Brown: acquiring cap space.

Brown Trade Concept

Here are some examples of what a Brown trade could look like:

  • Brown to Blazers for Clingan + 2-3 Firsts
  • Brown to Hornets for Miller + 2-3 Firsts
  • Brown to Rockets for Sheppard + 2-3 Firsts
  • Brown to Hawks for Okongwu + 2-3 Firsts

The purpose in all of these trades would be to create cap space, in addition to acquiring these players. So, these teams would send these packages AND find 3rd team to take on the filler salary.

How Much Cap Space Could We Open UP?

If we trade for a player making at most like 15-20mil, we could open up almost 40mil in space by trading Hauser and declining 3 team options (Banton/Amari/Schulga). We can always bring these guys back after we use cap space. Breakdown:

  • Salary Cap: 165mil
  • Current Allocations: 187mil
  • Subtract Brown + Hauser + Decline 3 of our team options: 110.5mil
  • Add 17mil player: 125.5mil
  • Cap space: 39.5mil

That 39.5mil is just enough to give Duren/Kessler a 165⁄4 contract with 5% annual raises. Those would be the most expensive free agents, everyone else would be cheaper than that.

Follow-up Free Agency Targets

My favorite list of free agents are:

  • Jalen Duren
  • Walker Kessler
  • Norman Powell
  • Peyton Watson

3 of these guys are restricted free agents, but BOTH Kessler/Duren are frustrated with their teams and have hinted at wanting out. Nugs have some finagling to do to have space to keep Watson. If you acquired Clingan/Okongwu, Powell is probably the easiest guy to get because he is an unrestricted free agent.

Now, Duren/Kessler/Watson would require sign and trades because we have the cap space to just outright take them. However, we may need to do a sign and trade to prevent teams from matching our offer. Our offer is already something these teams haven’t wanted to pay, so we may be able to just take them. If a sign and trade was needed, it’d be pennies on the dollar in value.

After Free Agency

We now have a 27mil TPE and can use it in it’s entirely while staying under the tax. We also have draft assets from the Brown trade to add to the TPE. My favorite target is Trey Murphy III and we’d have 4 firsts to trade for him AND he fits into the TPE.

But there are some other TPE options that aren’t as exciting AND would cost a lotless assets:

  • Cam Johnson
  • FVV
  • Myles Turner

You end up saving draft picks for future moves.

What Could A Final Deal Look Like?

Here is what our offer would be:

  • Brown + Hauser + 0-2 firsts

Here are some variations on what we could get in return:

  • Powell(FA) / Murphy(TPE) / Clingan
  • Miller / Murphy(TPE) /Duren
  • Watson(FA) / Murphy(TPE) / Kessler
  • Miller / Watson(FA) / Turner(TPE)
  • Powell(FA) / Johnson(TPE) / Clingan
  • FVV (TPE) / Watson (FA) / Okongwu

Essentially, pick one player from the Brown trade, one player from free agency, and one player to take into the TPE.
