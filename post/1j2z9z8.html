Idea for Currency and Uniques

It’s no question that the market is straight fuh’d but I think I’ve come up with an idea that would shake things up and make many Uniques super useful.

The idea is “Orb of Imprinting”. The orb can be used on any item to capture 1 single mod from that item. When used on it, the item is consumed and the Orb becomes something like an Exalted that can be used on an uncorrupted six slot item to place the captured mod as a 7th.

This would create a currency sink as people chase mods to make the perfect armor. Unique mods could be weighted by how good they are (like Alpha hood with its +100 spirit would have like a 1% chance of nabbing that mod).

The mod could of course be erased by a Vaal orb when corrupting as well.
