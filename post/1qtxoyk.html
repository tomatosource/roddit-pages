Here’s why we’re shipping Hauser

The Celtics are overachieving. They already have a shot at the ECF. With some luck, and with JT back, they have a shot at winning it all. The East is historically weak, and the West is strong, but once you get to the Finals — again, with some luck — anything is possible.

Our most glaring need is another big. Specifically, someone who can protect the rim and rebound. Ideally, he should be able to pass and stretch the floor as well. The most obvious move everyone’s been talking about is to ship Simons and [filler] in exchange for said big.

There were always two problems with this scenario:

  1. Simons on a high and expiring contract is not very attractive to anyone with a big we want.

  2. So, to get what we want, we would have to pay in order to offload Simons, which would mean losing additional current and future assets.

That would mean we would be making a short-term bet about an unknown center that might not be enough to matter this year and definitely limit our options in the future.

There was a simple solution to this dilemma: Do nothing. Let this team achieve what it will, wait and see how JT is doing, let Simons expire (or switch to a cheaper contract), and then make our moves in the summer.

But then the team became too good. There is a truly historical opportunity to win the East this year, and doing nothing at the deadline would mean wasting an opportunity that simply does not come around very often. Even if we wait until the summer and get much better, our odds next year might be worse due to other teams’ moves and players recovering from injury.

In addition, Simons himself became too good. He has a lot of limitations, but his unique ability to get a bucket when you need one is one of the most valuable things when you’re trying to win a championship. This means Simons is suddenly more valuable to us, but he’s still not very valuable to other teams because he’s on a bad/expiring contract. (A team that wants to win now won’t give us their big for Simons. And a team that doesn’t want to win now will ask us to pay extra in order to offload Simons.)

In short: We can’t be buyers at the deadline, do what we thought we’d do (Simons for Big), and we can’t do what we thought we’d do instead (wait till summer).

So what should we do?

There is a way for the Celtics to be sellers at the deadline, get better, and not mortgage our future. We can trade Sam Hauser. He’s in demand, he’s on a good contract, and we can actually get something good in return for him.

The same is true for D White, but D White has unique skills that we’ll need during the playoffs and his contract is not as attractive to a trade partner. Meanwhile, Hauser’s loss could be offset by Simons and JT’s return.

This is not meant as a diss towards Sam Hauser. I love the guy. I’m just trying to make sense of our options, and this seems like a reasonable conclusion.
