It’s apparently the name of a bird in New Zealand.

The New Zealand fantail (Rhipidura fuliginosa) is a small insectivorous bird, the only species of fantail in New Zealand.

[…]

It is also known by its Māori names, pīwakawaka, tīwakawaka or piwaiwaka

[…]

In Māori mythology, the pīwakawaka is a messenger, bringing death or news of death from the gods to the people. The bulbous eyes and erratic flying behaviour of the bird is attributed to it being squeezed by Māui for not revealing the whereabouts of his ancestress Mahuika, the fire deity. Tiwakawaka is also the name of one of the first Māori settlers to New Zealand.
