Anthony Edwards recently passed Andrew Wiggins to become the Timberwolves 3rd all-time leading scorer

Link to Basketball Reference for proof

This is somehow equally impressive for Ant as it is kind of depressing for the Timberwolves. In his five seasons in the league, Ant has averaged 19 - 21 - 24.6 - 26.9 - 27.5 ppg and played 70+ games every year (tonight at Indiana will be game #70 this season), so he’s definitely racking up points fast.

Despite this, I still have no idea how the math works out that Kevin Love is not higher. He had three 20+ ppg seasons and spent 6 years there total (even though he only played 18 games in 2013). Memory is a fickle thing.
