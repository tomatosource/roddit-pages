Am I approaching the game from the wrong angle, or are ARPGs just not my genre?

I’ve been playing PoE2 since EA launch, for a month or so each league. Every time I go through the same internal process:

  1. I get motivated for the league, choose a class and plan a build (this is my prime motivator, creating something of my own, not following a trend or a build guide).

  2. During campaing and early levels, I don’t really enjoy the game because I need to level up to unlock the abilities that compose my planned build. The campaign is the same I already know so I don’t get to enjoy the story part either, after I played it the first time (new acts are intersesting when they first launch).

  3. The sweet spot: when my build is mostly in place, usually around reaching endgame or early waystone levels. The most important nodes on the passive tree are unlocked, my equipment is decent. I get to experience and enjoy it, getting the rewards of my time in points 1 and 2.

  4. Soon after reaching T15 waystones, I quickly fall off of interest with the game. I feel no motivation to grind for multi-div equipment and a few extra passive points that would just “enhance” my already working-as-intended build instead of transforming it. Also creating a new character feels too much like a chore and I get stong sunk cost anxiety just by thinking about it.
    I like the changes to endgame but using the same 4-5 skills over and over without any real objective in mind feels tedious and pointless (because even if I kill the arbiter or Xesht or whatever endgame boss, it doesn’t really change how the game plays (endgame doesn’t “end”, my build doesn’t change, I just got a check on the engame checklist and unlock better tablets).

Am I approaching the game from the wrong angle, or are ARPGs just not my genre?
