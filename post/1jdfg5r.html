Discussion: the trade system as it is creates friction for players, but it affects much less the blasters than the casuals

If I understood correctly the philosophy to not implement an AH is to maintain a certain level of friction in trading so that gear is harder to obtain. Although I think this system impacts much more people with 1-2h/day of play than blasters. Blasters who play 10+ h/day have much more opportunities to sell/buy items just by being more present on the market. Comparatively, casual players can’t even sell their items while offline. I understand the attempt to limit the power of trade, but wouldn’t the implementation of a AH tip the scale towards the casuals a little bit more. Probably it would become much easier for everyone, but comparatively casual players would benefit the most. Shouldn’t this decrease item prices and help with inflation btw if more stuff is permanently online?
