Giannis stats in the last decade

Over the last 10 seasons, 2016-17 (age 21 season) through to last season 2025-26, Giannis ranks:

- 1st in total points
- 1st in field goals made
- 1st in 20-point games
- 1st in 30-point games
- 1st in 20⁄10 games
- 1st in 30⁄10 games
- 1st in 25⁄15 games
- 1st in All-NBA selections (9)
- 1st in First-Team All-NBA selections (7, 2nd amongst active players behind LeBron)
- 1st in total 4th quarter points
- 1st in transition points
- 1st in points in the paint

Other marks during that span:

- All Star Starter in 10 straight seasons
- First player in 50 years to have over 3 consecutive unanimous First team All-NBA selections, he had 5 straight from 2018⁄19 to 2022⁄23
- 1st in 4th quarter PPG over the last 9 seasons
- 1st in transition PPG in the last 8 consecutive seasons
- NBA all-time leader in 30/10/5 games
- Most 25/10/5 seasons in NBA history
- Most 30/10/5 seasons in NBA history
- Only player in NBA history to average 30+ PPG on 60%+ FG in a season. He did it twice, in 2023-24 and 2024-25, and was on course for a third straight before his injuries in 2025⁄26
- First player in 25 years to win MVP and DPOY in the same season
- Most points in a championship-clinching Finals game in NBA history (tied with Bob Pettit)
