Dark Souls boss designs without DS progression mechanics

First time playing POE2, in the endgame, level 84. One thing that’s really starting to frustrate me is map bosses. They clearly took inspiration from Dark Souls to make bosses more of involved engagements with mechanics to deal with that can be difficult. That’s fine, cool even.

My issue is dying to one of these bosses is SO damn punishing. In DS you lose your souls, can still pick them back up again, and can manage it so you spend your current souls before a difficult boss so there’s no risk in losing them anyway. But here, you permanently lose a big chunk of XP, with XP gain already EXTREMELY slow at this point in the game, and maybe fail a one-try map outright all because you mistimed a dodge roll against a one-shot hit. “Your build sucks then, map bosses should be trivial” some might say I anticipate, but requiring an OP build seems like a bad response. Endgame being either trivial or extremely frustrating seems like bad game design to me. Making me consider quitting at this point and spending my time elsewhere.
