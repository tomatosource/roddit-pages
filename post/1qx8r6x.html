The one thing about Brad - he sure likes his stretch 5s

Going back about 13 years, starting with drafting KO (I’m sure Brad had a hand in it), then came Daniel Theis, Horford, Kristaps, and now Vucevic. Garza can also shoot.

Vucevic, like KO, can drive well with his left hand. He is a good shooter, but not as reliable on defense and is now in his mid-30s. The other guys on the floor will need to bail him out from time to time.

Even with better defensive center talents like Timelord and Queta, Brad has always had a soft spot for 5s who can stretch the floor. I wonder if Vucevic can integrate well and provide 80 percent of what big Al was able to in his final years as a Celtic.
