Everyone is talking trades, but the buyout market feels more our style this year

This isn’t a post with any legs behind in terms of sources…but I’ve been seeing a whole lot of trade buzz across the league, and nothing has been really substantiated or loud regarding the Celtics.

A majority of the Celtics “rumors” have been names we’ve heard before, names we “would” have interest in, and the ongoing theme of trading Simons. Nothing concrete and nothing we didn’t hear in the offseason or the previous trade deadline. Much of the same being regurgitated.

I can see a scenario where we stay quiet. If a reliable big becomes available at a good price, you make the deal…but I think we’re going to hold a roster spot and make a buyout pickup. It’s the easiest way to add to your postseason hopes without giving up assets.

Anyone have some names that they think are legitimate buyout candidates?

Last year we saw guys like Mo Bamba, Ben Simmons, Christian Wood, Josh Richardson bought out. Torrey Craig came here last year after a buyout.

Thoughts?
