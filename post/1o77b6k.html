Identifying good loot vs actually playing the game

I love PoE 2 (this is my third league) but there’s one thing that keeps bothering me: I spend way too much time in my hideout identifying items, hoping to find something valuable… only to realize that 99% of them are trash, especially rares.

Right now, that’s my biggest friction point with the game. It feels tedious to invest so much time checking items and price lists just to maybe get a Divine Orb once in a while.

I’m already using an Uber Strict loot filter and only pick up Tier 2–3 items at minimum, yet it still feels like too much. I’d honestly prefer less loot overall if it meant the drops were more meaningful — so I could spend more time actually playing.

Maybe it’s just me? Should I ignore certain bases entirely? Only pick Tier 5+? Stick to meta or popular items? Funny enough, magic items sometimes feel more valuable since they can be good crafting bases.

How do you guys handle your loot management?
