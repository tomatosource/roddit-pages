How to Craft a Tenebrous Ring for BM/Ritualist Life Stacker

Figured I’d share a full breakdown in case anyone wants to craft one of these before league end.

You’ll need a Tenebrous Ring with a minimum ilvl of 74, since that’s the highest ilvl required for the mods we’re targeting. These drop from Fractured Lake or can be bought on trade for ~10–15 divines.

The mods we are going for are:

P1: T1 Life

S1: T1 Cast Speed

S2: T1 Strength

S3: T1 All Attributes

S4: T2 Chaos Res [highest roll of Chaos Res we can get on a Tenebrous (or other +/- prefix/suffix jewelry) since you cannot irradiate Fractured Lake to 81 by using tablets]

S5: [Skill Speed / T1-T2 Intelligence on final reveal]

There is no chaos spamming or use of expensive purple omens with this method. We are purely relying on improving homogenising tag slam odds and resetting with regular annulls / omen of lights if we miss.

Below are the steps:

1. T1 Cast Speed

  • Desecrate prefix with Kurgal (Sinistral Necromancy + Omen of the Blackblooded) and reveal “Increased Spell Damage While on Full Energy Shield” to get the caster tag. This mod usually appears on the first reveal, so no Echoes needed.
  • Greater Ex + Dextral Exaltation + Homogenising → guarantees T1 Cast Speed.

2. Annul Off the ES Spell Damage Desecrate

  • Use a regular annul to remove the “Increased Spell Damage While on Full ES.”
  • If it hits your T1 Cast Speed instead, return to Step 1 and re-slam.

3. T1 Strength / All Attributes

3a. Set Up Attribute Tags

  • Desecrate suffix with Kurgal (Dextral Necromancy + Omen of the Blackblooded) and reveal hybrid Dex + Int. This blocks single-attribute Dex/Int mods and provides the Attributes tag. It usually shows up immediately, but bring Echoes just in case.

3b. Hit T1 All Attributes + Try for T1 Strength

  • Perfect Ex + Dextral Exaltation + Greater Exaltation + Homogenising → guarantees T1 All Attributes and gives a 1⁄3 chance at T1 Strength.
  • If you miss T1 Strength, annul and hope you only remove the T2/3 Strength. Otherwise, you may need to go back to Step 2.

4. T2/T3 Chaos Res Slam Setup

(The biggest RNG portion of the craft)

4a. Clear the Hybrid Attribute Blocker

  • Omen of Light off the hybrid Dex + Int.

4b. Reveal Hybrid Chaos/[Lightning] Res

  • Desecrate suffix with Ulaman (Dextral Necromancy + Omen of the Sovereign) and reveal hybrid Lightning + Chaos Resistance. Any hybrid chaos res works, but Ulaman is generally the cheapest omen.

4c. Catalyst

  • Apply 20% Chaos Catalyst.

4d. Chaos Damage Slam

  • Greater Ex + Sinistral Exaltation + Homogenising + Catalyst Omen → you want any tier of % increased Chaos Damage.

4e. If You Miss

  • If you hit flat ele damage or non-chaos % ele damage:
    1. Omen of Light off the hybrid chaos res.
    2. Sinistral Crystallisation + Essence of the Abyss to mark the prefix miss.
    3. If the mark lands as a suffix → Ulaman + collarbone to reveal hybrid chaos res again. If prefix → Omen of Light + re-desecrate.

4f. *IMPORTANT* Reset Attribute Blocker for a Clean Chaos Res Slam

  • Once you have % Chaos Damage on the ring:
    • Omen of Light off the hybrid chaos res.
    • Desecrate with Kurgal to reveal the Dex + Int hybrid blocker again. This ensures the homogenizing slam has only chaos-res-related tags in the pool.

4g. Slam Chaos Res

  • Perfect Ex + Homogenising → 50⁄50 to hit T3 or T2Chaos Res (T2 is the highest possible below ilvl 81).

5. T1 Max Life Slam Setup

5a. Clear Blockers

  • Omen of Light off the Dex + Int hybrid blocker.
  • Use Sinistral Crystallization + Essence of the Abyss on the % Chaos Damage mod to mark it.
    • If the mark lands as a suffix: Ulaman + collarbone to reveal “Recover % Life on Kill.”
    • If prefix: Omen of Light → Ulaman + collarbone.

5b. Prefix Prep

  • Your ring should now have 5 suffixes, 0 prefixes.

5c. Catalysts

  • Apply 20% Life Catalysts.

5d. Slam T1 Life

  • Perfect Ex + Homogenising + Catalyst Omen → aims for T1 Maximum Life. Life catalysts heavily weighs the slam toward T1 Life over % Chaos Damage (the only competing prefix tag).

6. Final Mod

  • Annul the Recover % Life on Kill mod.
  • Desecrate suffix to reveal your final choice:
    • Increased Skill Speed (Ulaman) — easy and instant reveal.
    • Hybrid Chaos Res (Ulaman) — easy and instant reveal. Allows you to basically max out Chaos Res on a Ritualist if you pair with Kalandra’s (or come close on a BM without the 25% ascendancy bonus).
    • Intelligence — great for attribute requirements and allows you to swap int to str on your tree, but more expensive to reveal.

Edit: Just in case asked, you can insert Int into earlier crafting steps, but from my experience, it gets insanely expensive with a lot of resets: you have to slam T1 Str (1⁄3 odds) and then annull off the blocker and hope to slam on T1 Int (1⁄6 odds with Dex not blocked off). Then you can use abyss marks and perfect mana essence to reveal for incr. chaos dmg and it’s still low odds you hit T2 Chaos Res on the next slam (1⁄5 odds when catalyst weighted against T1-T3 dex with Chaos Res being a 250 weight mod at each tier before 5x catalyst odds improvement).

You’re then also slamming T1 Life with just the catalyst and no homogenizing (since suffixes are full at that point), which adds even more cost.

Much less hassle to reveal it in the last step and you can commit to the cost with T2 Chaos Res. Otherwise it’s technically 1 in 90 the craft goes through but you may get some luck with annuls. With T1 Int being a pretty high weight mod and 4 suffix mods out of the pool at that point on a lower-than-82 ilvl ring, you’re better off just revealing it.

7. If you have the Vaals, corrupt for Crit Dmg
