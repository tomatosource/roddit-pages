Taking an early look at summoner options

First and foremost, I expect this discussion to evolve quite a bit as we see more new uniques, balance changes, specter options etc. That being said, we now know the new ascendancies and there are now a couple of interesting options for summoner builds.

Let’s take a look at what each brings to the table:

Infernalist

  • Hound summon typically used for easy application of exposure
  • Beidat’s Will typically grants 60-100 spirit (85 on my current summoner)
  • Quite a lot of extra ES, and tankiness from splitting damage

Balance changes that could make Infernalist more or less attractive include ES nerfs and/or life buffs.

Lich

  • Double curse - likely not useful for clearing but very good for bossing
  • “Free” corpse explosion - might help some with clearing
  • 30+% more damage. Not sure how high this can be stacked, but it only takes 2500 mana to double it.
  • Alternately, a free jewel socket at double strength

Any changes to MoM/Eldritch Battery could be impactful here, as Lich provides a way to scale damage through mana much like Archmage. Stacking mana on a summoner should be fairly easy. Even if one doesn’t go the mana stacker route, the double strength jewel socket is quite good for minions as minion builds get a lot of damage from jewels. It’s also possible that the best option will be to give up double curse and keep both the jewel socket and mana stack.

Tactician

  • Direct damage from Supporting Fire
  • +8% spirit (probably translates to 30-50 spirit at endgame)
  • 25% of weapon damage as added attack damage

Most of the minion stuff is up near Witch, so Tactician is a less natural fit, but passive tree changes could have a big impact here. It’s unclear how well supporting fire will scale, but it could be a good option for clearing. Clearspeed is the main downside of minion builds, so having a big aoe that you can charge forward and just trigger to clear stuff could actually be very good. The weapon damage bonus is potentially very good for attack minions (some specters, snipers, arsonists, reavers, maybe even warriors). The problem here is that you are trading off using Trenchtimbre. A good 1h weapon is going to give your minions something like 80-100 flat damage. I’m a bit skeptical about this competing with +3-4 minion levels, but again depending on other changes it’s not impossible that it could be good.

______________________________________

From what we know now, I think Infernalist could end up being overshadowed here. My summoner currently gets about 16% of her spirit from Beidat’s Will, so trading that for 30%+ more damage seems like a no brainer. Infernalist gets a lot of extra tankiness, but I’ve found that summoners can often avoid damage and may not need as much direct defense as other playstyles. Depending on how balance shakes out with MoM and Eldritch Battery, I could also imagine Lich gaining just as much tankiness as Infernalist offers just by stacking mana to scale Unholy Might.

Tactician is a bit of a wild card. I have a hard time imagining it being better than Lich in terms of raw power, but I could imagine supporting fire really speeding up map clear which is the most obvious weakness of summoner builds in general. There might also be a specter option that really breaks the added weapon damage bonus, so it’s worth keeping an eye on.

I’m really curious what your thoughts are about how the summoner meta might shape up, especially as more new information becomes available!
