In 2017, 22 players out-tripled the Blue Jays

The days of a team being out-homered by an individual player are over, Dave Kingman being the last serious challenger in 1979, but we can still find idiosyncrasies elsewhere like, hey, somehow the 2017 Blue Jays only hit 5 triples all season.

Imagine, a full season, 162 games, 5,499 atbats, and only five times did the batter reach third. Oddly, three of those came in the first 20 games, followed by one in June and one in August.

It’s the record by 1, ahead of the 2016 Orioles (excluding 2020 but including 1994 and 1981). Their star Josh Donaldson hit 5 in 2016 then never tripled again. Newcomer Teoscar Hernandez hit 0 after joining the team on July 31 but managed 7 in 2018. Obligated to mention Teoscar’s former teammate Evan Gattis once hit 5 in a span of 88 atbats.

A few other teams have scrounged single-digit seasons recently; low triples is not unusual anymore. The most by a team since 2020 is 47 by the 2024 Tigers, which would’ve only been 25th-best for 1995 to 2019.
