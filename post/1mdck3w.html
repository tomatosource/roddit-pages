In the 1993 NBA Playoffs Michael Jordan and Charles Barkley had the top 10 individual scoring games of the playoffs. This is the only time two players have comprised the entire top 10 going back to at least 1983.

https://www.landofbasketball.com/year_by_year_stats/1992_1993_most_points_pl.htm

Four of Jordan’s games were in the Finals, what a performance.
They also had 15 of the top 19 games (although 5 players were tied at 19).

Also shoutout to Bernard Kind in 1984 having 6 of the top 7 scoring games in just 12 playoff games!
