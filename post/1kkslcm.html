Private Leagues coming this week: People want a Fresh start / GSF feel or something more like Gucci Hobo?!

So …. we are getting Private Leagues in PoE 2 this week!? But with no custom modifiers just yet.

A few people have been floating the idea of simply fresh economies, some ‘group found’ type setups OR maybe even Gucci Hobo ( Uniques only rules )

If the idea of a fresh economy start is appealing to you which one of the ideas could be interesting?

- Simple : Fresh economy, usual trade
- Group Found : Sharing resources through 420vaal pricing == item is ‘free’ for others to have on trade.
