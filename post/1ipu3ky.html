Grim Famine became grim feast

Don’t be like me, new player to Poe, and forget to upgrade your support gems. LVL 93 and struggling to get to 94. Have been working super hard on playing better, learning mechanics, numerous upgrades, reading heaps, tweaking passives and I found out grim feast was level 10. Upgraded to 18 and maxed the quality and my es now sits on 11k and barely ticks down. Previously it would take 3⁄4 of a map to get max if I basically didn’t get hit. Now it recharges super fast. I have been spending so much time fixing everything else that I overlooked grim feast thinking it was sorted and it’s made the single biggest change to my build, monk invoker Ice strike.

I was always wondering why youtubers had max double es when I had to fight so perfectly to achieve the same. Really helps when your picking up 100s of es vs 10s at a time. Whole game has changed.
