The Sixers are the first team in NBA history to lose 3 home games by 40+ points in a single season

The 3 losses:

  • 41-point loss to the Magic in November
  • 49-point loss to the Knicks last month
  • 40-point loss to the Spurs last night

There are 10 other teams with two such losses in a single season:

  • 1967-68 Rockets (Box scores)
  • 1971-72 76ers (Box scores)
  • 1988-89 Heat (Box scores)
  • 1998-99 Bulls (Box scores)
