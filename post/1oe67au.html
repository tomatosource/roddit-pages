Have you ever wanted to be a major league GM? r/baseballoffseason2026 is looking for participants!

Hello /r/baseball!

I represent /r/baseballoffseason2026, our 14th annual offseason simulation, with users representing each organization over the course of the offseason. We’re currently looking for 3 new GMs, so if you’re interested and want to take a look at how the sim worked last year, check out /r/baseballoffseason2025.

As a GM you’d be in charge of trades, player signings, options, tendering contracts…pretty much everything real life GMs have to deal with (except the rule 5 draft). You’ll need to create and maintain a spreadsheet of your full roster, complete with players’ salaries. One major goal of the sim is realism - we don’t want to see what ridiculous trades you can convince people to make. We realize that ridiculous trades happen in real life, but that’s not a reason to make them in the sim.

The sim is a lot of fun, but I must stress that it is a significant time commitment, especially if you’re a GM. GMs are expected to be online in our Slack channel (just about) every day for at least two months.

Let us know here if you’re interested, as well as what teams you’d like. The following teams are available:

  • Angels
  • Diamondbacks
  • Pirates

List the teams you would prefer, and also, as a “get to know you” type of question: what is your first concrete baseball memory?
