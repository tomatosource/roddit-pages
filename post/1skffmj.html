The Athletic’s Anonymous Player Picks - Who’ll win 2026 NBA Finals; Top 3 - Thunder (50%), Spurs (25.3%), Celtics (8.2%)

The Athletic released the second part of their anonymous player poll, this one asking players who will win the NBA championship (besides your own team).

Out of the 161 players polled, 146 responded over the course of late February through the start of April. The results:

  • Oklahoma City Thunder - 50.0% (73 votes)

  • San Antonio Spurs - 25.3% (37 votes)

  • Boston Celtics - 8.2% (12 votes)

  • Denver Nuggets - 7.5% (11 votes)

  • Detroit Pistons - 4.1% (6 votes)

  • Cleveland Cavaliers - 1.4% (2 votes)

  • Los Angeles Lakers - 1.4% (2 votes)

  • Minnesota Timberwolves - 1.4% (2 votes)

  • Atlanta Hawks - 0.6% (1 vote)

Previous season poll results:

  • 2025 - Boston Celtics (57.7% of 137 votes)

  • 2024 - Denver Nuggets (55.9% of 127 votes)

  • 2023 - Milwaukee Bucks (62.2% of 90 votes)

  • 2019 - Golden State Warriors (79.5% of 110 votes)
