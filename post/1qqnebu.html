One thing I wish we could all admit is that there has been and always will be tension by Tatum and Jaylen.

I want to start by saying I love basketball. I coach high school, I go to college games in division 3, I’m currently watching FDU vs Stonehill because I love the sport. I don’t like talking about click bait or first take topics, however….. chemistry and competition between teammates is a real thing.

As Celtics fans, I think a lot of us have found comfort in looking back at 24 or KG and Pierce and their relationship. But a schism between the Jays, and growing tension is a real thing that will impact the team on the court. Its not the end of the world to discuss how they’ll co-exist go forward, especially considering Jaylen’s performance this year.

Historically, there have always been those questions. But I think they’ll ramp as Tatum makes his way back. Jaylen has clearly loved his role this season. He’s said multiple times this is his favorite season he’s had. How will things change?

It’s something that I’m sure the team has talked about internally. Clearly, through 3 coaches, the team has seen Tatum as the work horse that needs the majority of touches. I have never once thought they were wrong, and I don’t believe a super high usage and eh efficiency season from Jaylen should make anyone second guess what has happened in the past. However, there’s a good chance Tatum never returns to who he was. There’s a good chance the team see’s him as a number 2 going forward.

Guys like Karalis aren’t wrong in saying “can Tatum make it work?”

Tatum was one of the 5 best players in the world for half a decade, and now Jaylen is proving how good he is. How does that work *if* the team continues to run the majority of their offense through Jaylen? Does that mess up the locker room? Does Tatum react the right way?

Like most people here I’m really hoping that they work together like they always have. But they are also humans who will be dealing with a different dynamic. It’s not crazy to talk about how things will be different. And it’s not like toxic or whatever to openly discuss how the team operates moving forward.
