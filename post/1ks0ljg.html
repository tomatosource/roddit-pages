Ascendancy Trials

Trying to get my last ascendancy point and rerunning the last floor of sekhema has been the most frustrating and infuriating experience I’ve had in gaming in a very long time. Half the mobs in the last floor are AC-130 autocannon machine guns and the other half have near hitscan proj they shoot offscreen, I’ll have swift shrine with perma onslaught rolling around and still getting nuked with as much honor res and max honor physically possible. It is so oppressively unfun.

The jar guys that call in airstrikes, the orbs that full auto near instantly, and the wizards that shoot orbs feel so overtuned, I can’t imagine playing against that if afflicted with giving monsters inc. action speed.

I am not a casual ( which shouldn’t matter since this game is more marketed to such audience ) Multi mirror chars in diff leagues with 4k hours poe1 and 1k hours poe2 and I every time I try to get the trials done for hours over multiple days I just resort to getting a paid carry, and EVEN THEN its an absolute slog.

Its honestly impressive how unfun it is. As an avid gamer who enjoys all genres and always going for the most difficult runs when the games are fair its beyond me with how frustrating it is. for a decade I’ve put thousands of hours into hardcore shooters, milsims, fighting games, souls and soulslikes I can’t even think of a time where I’ve been this frustrated with a game or the game mechanics.

Thanks for reading my rant, as I see it Volatile plants are just now getting recognized as a problem I doubt throwing myself endlessly at the trials and failing will get picked up on a stat line and get looked at, and I don’t know of another place to put my feedback.

To conclude this is not a hate post, I love both games and will continue to play but fuck me if I’ll be going back into the trial, lastly I think most of the trial is fine, its just the last floor, and some of the afflictions / mobs are so overtuned. Honorable mention Trial of chaos is just a slog with over tuned mods. Things like the timer events are lame, waiting for mobs to spawn is lame. Tornado bird boss is lame.
