On Jan 4, 1995, Gary Payton scored 32 pts without missing a single shot, including free throws.

He shot 100% (14⁄14 including 1⁄1 3-pt) from the field and 100% (3⁄3) on free throws.

32 points is the most for a player who did not miss a shot (edit: in the regular season).

Tied 2nd: Thomas Bryant and Charles Barkley with 31.

Thanks to u/Additional-Ear-6871, who pointed out that Chris Paul dropped 33 without missing a shot in a playoff game in 2022.
