Robert Horry has 3 achievements we might not ever see again.

In honor of his 56th birthday I wanna present 3 impressive achievements by Horry (all team related) that we might not see again.

Horry definitely has one of the most unique careers in all of sports.

Achievement 1). Winning 7 championships in the post-merger NBA (since 1977).

Achievement 2). Winning at least 2 championships with 3 different teams.

Achievement 3). Not only making the playoffs in every season of his 16 year career but also winning a 1st round series in all 16 of them. He’s literally an Anti-McGrady.
