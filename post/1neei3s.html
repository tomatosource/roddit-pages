The Aspiration guy was already a liar

An important fact: the founder of Aspiration (Joseph Sanberg) was already lying about very large amounts of money.

That is why he pleaded guilty to federal fraud charges. https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/orange-county-man-and-aspiration-partners-co-founder-agrees-plead-guilty-248-million. He got fake letters from friends and family saying that they were purchasing his company’s products. He counted the money from their “purchases” as revenue. But the letters were always fake, and they never paid. Plus, to hide it, he sometimes sent in fake payments using his own money. And he used that fake “revenue” to get real investments and loans from other people. Those real investments and loans ended up being worthless. He caused other people and companies to lose hundreds of millions of dollars.

This is the same guy at the center of the Kawhi agreement. This fraud is related to the Kawhi situation in the sense that this fraud led to the company’s bankruptcy, which led to Kawhi’s claim on the bankruptcy estate, which led to this all becoming public. But this was an otherwise separate (and very bad) fraud.

We can’t just analyze the Kawhi-Clippers-Aspiration situation in a vacuum, focusing only on the sponsorship agreement and the basketball issues. We also have to think about what else was going on with the company. This makes it FAR more likely that the Kawhi sponsorship was fraudulent. Liars lie. And this guy was lying about a similar kind of financial issue with the same company at the same time.
