The mechanics of life and energy shield should be swapped.

Energy shield is something difficult to recover but has a high hit pool. Life is intended to have much easier recovery but has a lower hit pool.

These mechanics should be swapped.

If 10k life was normal but difficult to recover outside of flasks, then armor and evasion would be much better and you could have battles of attrition with bosses without having to rely on one shots. Thematically, a quick recovering shield barrier for your difficult to recover actual life makes much more sense. It would also make the flask more relevant.

If energy shield was much lower, 3-5k being normal, but much quicker to recover through very quick recharge rates and delay time, then it would function more similarly to evasion an armor as a defensive layer AND it would work more thematically in the trio of Mitigation/avoidance/recovery. It would ALSO work well going hybrid ES/armor or ES/Evasion.

It would make going CI a much bigger investment and much bigger trade-off.

I think ggg took the exact opposite to a correct approach by nerfing recovery but leaving the massive hit pool of ES.
