[NBA] Stephon Castle, Jalen Johnson named NBA Players of the Week

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/nba-players-week-stephon-castle-jalen-johnson

San Antonio Spurs guard Stephon Castle and Atlanta Hawks forward Jalen Johnson have been named the NBA Players of the Week for Week 16 of the 2025-26 season.

Stephon Castle (SAS): 24.0 PPG; 7.3 RPG; 7.3 APG; 3-0 team record (1st career POTW)

Jalen Johnson (ATL): 27.3 PPG; 12.0 RPG; 11.3 APG; 2-1 team record (2nd career POTW)
