My issues with 0.2 (long post)

Hello everyone,

I’d like to discuss some of the main issues I’ve faced playing 0.2, I’ve played 3 characters total (1 in trade (Level 94), 2 in SSF (Level 93 & 87 (both fresh SSF from scratch).

I’ll mostly talk from my SSF perspective, but most of the issues are same for trade as well, I’ll start from the beginning:

ACTS:

To put it simply, there is no loot. Vendor prices are insanely high, especially if you plan to disenchant all of magic/rare items for currency (which is pretty much mandatory), to top it off the vendor items are trash 99% of the time, most of the gambles/crafting with currency will lead to minor upgrades (if any upgrades at all). Fortunately for GGG, they tuned the acts down so much that you only need to be up to date/ahead with your weapon and it’s enough to clear the campaign (with a good build of course). Gold prices need to be reduced by a lot (both for vendor items & gambling, especially on rings/amulets), more raw currency drops (Alchemy orbs don’t exist), more rare items, more gold.

PLAYERS:

1) MOBILITY: It’s terrible, walking simulator, no dashes over ledges (Blink being a spirit gem is a crime), limited movement speed, movement speed penalty for wearing items from POE 1 still exists, if you’re not right side of tree (Ranger/Huntress/Monk) you’re just doomed to have no speed.

2) ATTRIBUTES: Another terrible system, the attribute requirements on Gems/Gear is way too high, completely unfun. Requirements need to be reduced by at least 40%. I understand the logic behind having attribute requirements in the game but they are too demanding and are dictating which items you can and cannot use.

ASCENDING:

Both trials feel terrible to do, compared to eternal labyrinth from POE 1. Only my trade character received their 4th ascendancy and only because I can buy all 3 fragments for Trialmaster. Doing 4 floors/30 waves of insufferable content is not something I will do unless I really have to (fortunately my SSF character was a deadeye so I sacrificed Wind Ward gladly), it’s not hard at all it’s just absolutely tedious.

MONSTERS & MONSTER MODIFIERS:

1) On-death Effects: I don’t know, why is this still a thing in 2025? When I enter Seepage/Decay/Augury and all of the maps that have on-death effects I know I’ll have a bad time, just remove them and buff monsters, this is not the way to deal with player power (which doesn’t exist compared to POE 1).

2) Charging Monsters: It just feels bad. I don’t want to be pushed by monsters, either remove the push or let us cancel it by dodge rolling, especially annoying in Rituals.

3) Volatiles & Volatile Plants: Terrible, especially the Volatile Plants that have a very low cooldown and follow for too long, screen clutter in maps is already high, having to focus on this as well feels bad.

4) Eternal Knight: These guys are insane, the return projectile is too fast, having multiple of them in 1 pack is extremely dangerous and most times they can just offscreen you with your own projectiles if you’re not careful (clutter in maps is already very high, hard to see).

5) Priest of the Sun: I don’t know who thought it’s a good idea to give a monster the ability to fire a offscreen locked to player beam, just nerf it.

MAPS & MAP MODIFIERS:

1) Progression: Progression feels both good and bad, while rushing to T15 is viable and very easy, I don’t understand why there is almost no difference in monster difficulty between T1 and T15. I would rather the monster difficulty be higher between tiers but also have the rewards be higher, meaning that T15 rare will award more base loot than a T1 rare (significantly more).

2) Rare Monsters: Main objective of every map is to kill an X amount of rare monsters, meaning that the main enemy in those maps are the rare monsters, those rare monsters dropping Blue items/Low amount of gold/No loot at all feels terrible. Rare monsters should base line drop at least X amount of rare items or X amount of currency instead of being able to just drop 800 gold and an orb of augmentation.

3) Suffixes not increasing rewards: I don’t really get this one, by adding suffixes to my map I’m not gaining any loot increase, I’m increasing monster power as well as reducing the amount of portals I can use, what is the logic here? Please use the same system as in POE 1 where both Prefixes and Suffixes increase how rewarding a map will be.

4) Temporal Chains & Burning Ground: Temporal chains is just terrible, remove it please. Burning ground does way too much damage (as is tradition from POE 1 it seems).

5) Size/Layouts: While much better than 0.1, there are still a lot of maps that could benefit from Vaal Factory/Augury treatment.

6) Atlas Tree: Not interesting at all, 99% of players will use the same generic tree, won’t critique this one too much as it’s early access.

LEAGUE MECHANICS:

1) Tujen/Expedition Vendors: Not sure what the thought process is here, from one of the best NPCs in POE 1, GGG turned Tujen to TEMU Rog that offers 3-4 items. Just move the Rings/Belts/Amulets to Rog and let Tujen sell us currency like in POE 1. Vendor item bases should scale with player level, vendors selling low level bases makes no sense.

2) Ritual: While it’s fast and pretty good to do, Ritual has a lot of problems, starting from spawning monsters that charge you and do a lot of area denial, if that was not enough Ritual also has its own version of Volatile plants (insanely low cooldown, same like the monster mod, if you get stunned once in Ritual you’re most likely dead). Rituals having monsters that won’t spawn until you come close enough feels terrible. 90% of offered loot is trash, good omens are way too rare, make new even more insane omens and reduce the rarity of the current best ones.

3) Essences: Way too rare. Smaller essences are fine, Greater essences should also be able to reroll rare items like they can in POE 1.

4) Wisps: Following a ball around the map for X amount of time for it to buff a rare feels bad, especially when the loot is bad as well.

ITEMS:

Corrupted Items: We need a way to add Quality, Sockets and Annoints to corrupted items, this already exists in POE 1, why not in POE 2?

Item Implicit: Having to use a Divine orb to change the roll of an implicit feels bad, add Blessing Orbs.

Charms: Still feel terrible, the mods need more buffs. (TOO MANY BELT MODS ARE TIED TO THIS).

Resistances: So many items become mandatory/replaceable by only similar items because they have +X% amount of a certain resist, we need a way to swap resistances like we can with Harvest in POE 1, runes cannot come close to this.

Vendors: Vendor gamble prices are way too high considering the fact that we can get low level bases, scale the bases from vendor with player level.

Runes: The numbers need to be higher to match the crafting band-aid from POE 1, the drop of runes needs to be doubled in acts.

CRAFTING: While 0.2 included Fracturing orbs and Recombinating, besides having good RNG what is the idea of getting good items? Fracture a good mod, annul, spam chaos until second mod and then what, spam exalts? Start dumping currency into omens of whittling until you have a good item? We need more deterministic crafting/actual currency dumping in the game.

THE VISION: Please just do what you did in POE 1, make RUTHLESS and have this level of drops/player mobility/tediousness there, let the rest of the player base enjoy the game. The game looks beautiful, plays beautiful and has potential, the vision needs to go.

Thank you for reading & sorry for making the post so long!
