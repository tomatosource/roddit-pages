Help r/baseball Choose the Greatest MLB Moments of All Time: An Offseason Time-Killer

With the season done and a long, cold off-season looming, I’d like to propose a time-killer: let’s rank the greatest MLB moments in the history of the game.

Is it the Shot Heard ‘round the World? Gibson’s pinch-hit walk-off? The Catch? Aaron passing the Babe? As a community, let’s decide!

My day job is creating and running surveys for a federal government that may or may not be open right now due to “reasons”. I’d like to propose a 2-part process to help build r/baseball’s all-time MLB moment rankings:

  1. In this thread, users can suggest great MLB moments that should be included in the survey. Comment with your suggestion! Feel free to include a link to a video or Wikipedia article of the moment if you’d like.

  2. Using a “Pairwise Comparison” survey, users can vote on moments head-to-head. For example, on your screen it might say “Which of the following do you think is a greater moment in MLB history? Option 1: Gibson’s homer. Option 2: The Catch.” You vote on which you prefer between those two options. If you’re committed, you could conceivably vote on every possible pair.

  3. Once the survey closes, I will compare the “win rates” for each moment. The higher the win rate, the higher a moment ranks. From that, I will release r/baseball’s top moments (aiming for at least 50, but maybe 100 depending on how many moments we have).

With that, please help out by suggesting the greatest MLB moments in history! I’ve got several dozen in mind, but want to make sure I’m not missing anything.

I’m planning on opening the official survey on Monday 11⁄10, so make sure your suggestions are in before then.

Happy nominating!
