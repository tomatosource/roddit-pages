So for the entire league I’ve died a lot, like I have died as much on my 93 sorceress that I have surpassed my total deaths for all other Leagues combined. I have like 130 deaths in this character so far or something. In 0.3 my 96 sorc has like 20.

And I’ve been getting one shot out of the blue for the entire league by like just running past a pack of white mobs, I was taking absurd amounts of DMG and been looking for others posting the same thinking they really ramped up the monster dmg by like 3x since last league.

I run a no limit sorceress with thunderstorm and the Shock Conduction II gem, which makes it so that thunderstorm always shocks. I also noticed that wrath scepters have a skill called fulmination. that does some lightning dmg to a monster whenever I do dmg to it, extra dps i thought as I was using scepters. Fulmination states “cannot shock”. However, combined with the Conduction gem, it turns out it does shock. It shocks everything, myself included. If I use thunderstorm On a monster that is close to me, I get my own shock debuff as a debuff. This never clicked until I got the ascendancy for double shock, as I was suddenly almost always running around with a double shock debuff. Tested it and found this interaction, I have swapped to a different scepter type and I die 90% less now.

TLDR shock conduction + fulmination causes you to apply shock to yourself. I was debuffing myself with an extra 20 - 56% extra damage taken for the entire league.
