[ESPN] Brad Stevens Shuts Down Indiana Coaching Rumors: ‘I Thoroughly Appreciate Being a Celtic’

Brad Stevens: “I thoroughly appreciate being a Celtic and love the people I get to work with every day… I am not a candidate [for Indiana’s coaching job].”

Stevens, the Celtics’ president of basketball operations, dismissed speculation linking him to Indiana’s vacancy despite his deep ties to the state (Butler HC, 2010-11 NCAA title runs). Indiana’s Mike Woodson will step down after a second straight season missing the NCAA tournament (15-11 record).
