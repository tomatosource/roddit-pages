The statistically most entertaining games of the 2025-26 regular season

There’s a cool website called inpredictable that gives every game in the play-by-play era a score based on how exciting it was, how close it was, and how big of a comeback there was. I thought it would be fun to see what their methods say were the top games this season in each of those categories.

It’s worth noting that these metrics lack context. They aren’t affected by who is playing in the game or how much the game means. For example, a game where a player has a crazy performance or passes some milestone may be exciting but isn’t taken into account for these statistics.

Excitement

From inpredictable themselves, “the excitement index measures how far the win probability graph “travels” over the course of the game”. So if both teams had periods of time where they were heavily favored in win probability, the excitement for that game would be very high. Here’s the top 5 games this season by excitement.

Tension

Games with a high tension score are close the entire way. Watch these if you don’t want to know who’s gonna win until the final buzzer.

Comeback

This stat finds the games ranks games by the winning team’s lowest win probability at some point during the game. That is, if a team has a 0.0001% chance of winning the game, but they end up pulling it off, they would have a ridiculously high comeback score.

5. January 23, 2026 - Celtics @ Nets

Result: &gt;!BOS 130-126!&lt;

!Claxton gets a dunk to go to tie it up at the end of regulation. Then in OT with just 8 seconds left, Ziaire Williams hits 2 FTs to put the Nets up 5, giving the Celtics a 0.2% win probability. Then Payton Pritchard hits the 3 on the other end with 3 seconds left. The Celtics foul Nolan Traore, he misses one, and Hugo Gonzalez ties it up with a 3 for the Celtics. Then a tight 2OT ends with the Celtics on top. Jaylen Brown with a triple double and Pritchard gets 34 points on 6-11 from 3.!&lt;

4. March 25, 2026 - Rockets @ Timberwolves

Result: &gt;!MIN 110-108!&lt;

!Rudy Gobert commits an away from the play foul up 1 with 9 seconds to go. KD promptly sinks the FT to tie it up. Regulation ends in a tie. In OT, the Rockets come out hot and with 3 minutes left, Sengun dunks it to put the Rockets up 13 giving the Wolves a 0.2% win probability. But some timely buckets by Donte DiVincenzo and Julius Randle amongst other Wolves pour in and the Rockets fail to score the entire rest of the game. Wolves win.!&lt;

3. November 16, 2025 - Hawks @ Suns

Result: &gt;!ATL 124-122!&lt;

!With 8 minutes left in the game, the Suns are up by 20 points, giving the Hawks a 0.2% win probability. Then the entire Hawks roster heats up and the Hawks go on a 19-0 run in 3 and a half minutes to reduce the Suns lead to just 1 point. Even then, Devin Booker hits a shot and some free throws to put the Suns back up 6. But the Hawks are able to come back and win thanks to NAW and Onyeka Okongwu.!&lt;

2. November 21, 2025 - Timberwolves @ Suns

Result: &gt;!PHX 114-113!&lt;

!Down 8 with 50 seconds left, Dillon Brooks misses a step-back 3 to put the Suns win probability at just 0.1%. Royce O’Neale collects the rebound and puts it back up. Then Ant turns it over and Jordan Goodwin hits a 3 to cut the lead to 3. Then Randle turns it over and Goodwin gets a 2 to fall. Joe Ingles comes in the game and gets subbed out. Ant misses 2 free throws and Collin Gillespie hits a floater to put the Suns up 1. Suns win.!&lt;

1. November 10, 2025 - Wizards @ Pistons

Result: &gt;!DET 137-135!&lt;

!A terrible 3rd quarter for the Pistons puts the Wizards up as much as 13, but the Pistons come back to make it close. With just 4 seconds left and the Wizards up 5, Cade misses two layups to put the Pistons at a 0.1% win probability, but Javonte Green gets the rebound and kicks it to Daniss Jenkins who hits a 3 with 3 seconds left. Then Kyshawn George makes 1 misses 1 on the other end to keep the Wizards up 3. But Daniss Jenkins again hits a 3 to send it to OT. The Pistons win in OT in a close one.!&lt;

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Obviously, these metrics miss some of what makes games exciting. For instance, a decently close game between two of the top teams in the league is probably more exciting to most than a Bulls vs. Jazz double overtime game. A game where Jokic puts up a 50-point triple double in a blowout is probably more exciting than a riveting Jazz vs. Kings game in October. But I think these metrics are interesting to look at nonetheless.
