Why not hold an auction for any item purchased in less than 5 minutes?

I’ve seen frequent complaints about how Chris Wilson was right and that trading is now more botted than ever before. Main problem being items being sniped before they even hit the public trade site.

So I’ve been wondering for a while now… why not make it so if you try to buy an item that’s less than 5 minutes old on trade, you are entered into an auction where the item ultimately goes to the highest bidder? If you lose your currency goes back to your remove only shop tab. This solves both sniping problem, and also helps players that accidentally toss their items in for exa instead of div.

Players that want instant trades simply have to settle on buying fairer prices by being restricted to items that have lived on the auction for more than 5 minutes.

I would like to hear what you think and also what downsides that I am not thinking of with such a system. I know one problem would be determining the relative value of all the currencies for the bidding mechanic, I think you could simply restrict the auction to the currency that was set by the trader, maybe with an exception for Ex/Div which could have a frequently updated rate based on market trends
