Choking tendencies?

For the second year in a row, going cold from three at the wrong time cost us our season in a series we were picked to win. Can we really just blame it on bad luck at this point? What are the chances of everyone on the team going cold while the Knicks are making a run to come back from 20 down in back to back games? What are the chances of it happening AGAIN the very next year when the Sixers are walking us down from behind 1-3?

I don’t buy it. These guys are STD. Scared to death. Particularly Sam Hauser, Derrick White, and Payton Pritchard. You can tell when their confidence is rattled out there. Jaylen Brown isn’t scared, but his over dribbling/poor court vision is also a killer in crunch time.

We aren’t just getting cold at the wrong time. We’re scared in the challenging times. Team has that choke gene.
