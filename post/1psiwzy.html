VJ Edgecombe leads the NBA in loose balls recovered per game (min 10 GP)

For the most part, this list is a who’s who of the best athletes/hustle guys in the league with a couple surprises (shoutout Austin Reaves and Steph). Still, it’s cool to see VJ at the top. The box score won’t jump off the page, but he’s such a winning player. The handle is a serious work in progress, but it’s no surprise that a guy who shoots 39% from 3 on decent volume, has a &gt;2 AST:TO, is an elite positional rebounder, and leads the league in loose balls recovered is an advanced stats darling. Despite being one of the least efficient 15+ PPG scorers, VJ is 2nd among rookies in EPM (Kon) and one of I believe only six rookies with a positive EPM (Kon, VJ, Will Richard, Harper, Jase Richardson, Noah Penda). I don’t have a dunksandthrees subscription to confirm that last part, so if anyone can do an official search I’d appreciate a correction if I’m wrong.
