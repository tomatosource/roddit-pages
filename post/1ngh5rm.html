Controller Feedback: Enable L3/R3 Joystick Clicks as normal buttons

Currently, the L3 left joystick click and R3 right joystick click on controllers have no function in the game outside menuing and interfacing.

I would like to suggest that these buttons be made usable, enabling us to bind actions to them like other buttons.

I’ve re-mapped these buttons through Steam’s controller settings to act as the “up” and “down” d-pad inputs.

By binding my health and mana potions to the joystick clicks, it feels much more intuitive and smoother. It allows me to aim, run, shoot, and use potions simultaneously.

Having to release either of the joystick to reach for the d-pad feels less fluid, and the shoulder buttons are prime real estate for other skills.

Enabling these buttons would be a nice quality-of-life improvement for controller.
