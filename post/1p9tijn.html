Hardcore felt great again (0.3), thank you

Now that the league is almost over, I have to say that the last time I enjoyed playing hardcore was around the Perandus league. Over time, Poe 1 accumulated a lot of mechanics and overtuned mobs that could one shot a mostly defensive character in the blink of an eye.

Now, besides a couple of exceptions that deal ridiculous damage when compared to anything else around their level (like the crappy ape from act 3 in maps that can sometimes off-screen one-shot you), there is mostly time to react, things that are happening on your screen are mostly clear (outside of del maps), and investing heavily in defenses (except for life ofc, life sucks) feels worthwhile.

Things are that good, I can even almost forget the utterly terrible idea of having a white mob that can insta-kill you in the game.

I really hope that things stay that way and don’t end up like they did in 1! Cheers!
