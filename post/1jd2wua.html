I Came Back and It Was So Worth it!

I was so hyped for Path of Exile 2 from all the trailers and all the ARPG players being excited for it. I’ve been playing the Diablo series since I was 7, especially Diablo 2.

I played PoE2 right at launch and within a week I was horribly disappointed. I tried making a witch thinking my minions would protect me but it was just way too hard. I tried watching guides but my character was broken by the end of act 2 and I was mentally defeated, so I went back to Diablo IV.

I tried again yesterday using a build Raxx recommended and oh man is this game amazing. My character finally feels powerful.

Here is the build guide for anyone interested. If the game was too punishing for you but your heart wants to love it, this guide is for you.

Happy Hunting Everyone!
