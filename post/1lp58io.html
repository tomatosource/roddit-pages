Revisiting the Damian Lillard-Jrue Holiday-Deandre Ayton-Robert Williams megatrade in 2023: what each team got overall, how each team performed in the 2 subsequent years

On September 27, 2023, the Portland Trailblazers, Milwaukee Bucks, and Phoenix Suns each partook in a 3 team megatrade that resulted in the following:

Bucks receive: Damian Lillard (via Blazers)

Trail Blazers receive: Deandre Ayton (via Suns), Toumani Camara (via Suns), Jrue Holiday (via Bucks), 2029 1st-round pick (via Bucks), Two first-round pick swaps with Bucks (2028, 2030)

Suns receive: Grayson Allen (via Bucks), Keon Johnson (via Blazers), Nassir Little (via Blazers) and Jusuf Nurkic (via Blazers)

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/damian-lillard-traded-to-bucks-2023

A few days later, on October 1, the Trailblazers traded Jrue Holiday to the Boston Celtics for the following:

Celtics receive: Jrue Holiday

Trailblazers receive: Robert Williams III, Malcolm Brogdon, 2024 1st-round pick (via Warriors) and a 2029 unprotected 1st-round pick (via Celtics)

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/jrue-holiday-traded-to-celtics-2023

As a result of this megatrade, this is how each team performed in that season + the subsequent season

2023-2024 season:

Boston Celtics: 1st seed in the East, Eastern Conference Champions, NBA Champions

Milwaukee Bucks: 3rd seed in the East, first round exit (lost to IND)

Portland Trailblazers: 15th seed in the West, no playoffs, picked 7th in the lottery (Donovan Clingan, C, University of Connecticut)

Phoenix Suns: 6th seed in the West, first round exit (lost to MIN) (fired coach Frank Vogel after season)

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2024_standings.html

2024-2025 season:

Boston Celtics: 2nd seed in the East, Eastern Conference Semifinals exit (loss to NYK)

Milwaukee Bucks: 5th seed in the East, NBA Cup Champions, First round exit (loss to IND)

Portland Trailblazers: 12th seed in the west, no playoffs

Phoenix Suns: 11th seed in the west, no playoffs (fired coach Mike Budenholzer after season)

All in all:

The Bucks gave up Jrue Holiday, Grayson Allen, a 2029 first-round pick and pick swaps in 2028 and 2030 for 2 seasons of Dame Lillard, 2 first round exits but an NBA cup championship.

The Celtics gave up Robert Williams III, Malcolm Brogdon, and 2 first round picks for 2 years of Holiday before trading him back to the Trailblazers for Anfernee Simons and 2 second-round picks, but were a top 2 seed in the east each year and won an NBA championship in 2024. Source: https://www.nba.com/news/celtics-blazers-trade-jrue-holiday-anfernee-simons

The Suns gave up Toumani Camara and Deandre Ayton but received Grayson Allen, Jusuf Nurkic, Naasir Little and Keon Johnson, making one playoff appearance.

The Trailblazers received all-defensive star Toumani Camara, Deandre Ayton (who was bought out last night- source: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/45612548/sources-blazers-ayton-become-free-agent-buyout), Robert Williams III, Malcolm Brogdon, a 2024 1st-round pick (via Warriors) and a 2029 unprotected 1st-round pick (via Celtics) while eventually re-acquiring Jrue Holiday 2 years later for Anfernee Simons.

To say the least, the clear winners out of all this were the Celtics and the Trailblazers, who won an NBA championship and are set up for future success, respectively.
