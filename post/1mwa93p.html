Patch 0.3 Valuable Item Refresher

I wanted to reach out to the community to assist myself and others with a quick refresher course. There are quite a bit of junk items the Filterblade loot filter helps to weed out. However, even with some of the highest filter levels, I remember on release being overwhelmed with all the loot.

Below are items I look out for and will update the list based on comments on this thread:

  • Stellar Amulet (White)
  • Sapphire Ring (White)
  • Spirit on Armor, Ring, or Amulet (Solar Amulet with High Spirit Roll is great)
  • Any Armor with 2-3 High Resistances (Chaos even better)
  • Boots with 30-35% Movement Speed (25% Early)
  • Wands with Spell Level
  • Amethyst, Breach, and Prismatic Rings for Self Crafting
  • Sapphire (Non-white), Ruby, and Topaz for Self Crafting
  • Heavy Belt (White) for Self Chance Orbing
  • Emerald Ring (White)
  • Utility Belt with 2-3 High Resistances
  • Bows with Additional Projectiles or High Crit
  • Quivers with Added Ice, Lightning, Physical Damage
  • Quivers with Projectile Speed, Projectile Skill Level, Attack Speed, Crit, Damage with Bow Skills
  • Most Uniques from End Game bosses
  • Any Piece required to access End Game bosses (can also be sold in currency exchange)
  • Alchemy Orbs, Orb of Chance, Vaal Orb, Exalted Orb, Orb of Annulment, Divine Orb, Mirror of Kalandra, Any Jeweler Orb

Another tip is to hold Alt and hover over a gear piece. You will be able to see gear tiers from T1-T5 (Tier 5 being the best). When you get to later end game, you can start dismantling items that have multiple low tiers right away. However, by then, the higher tier loot filters will usually handle this for you.
