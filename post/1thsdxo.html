Looking for SSF Community?

TLDR: Are there any groups of SSF players who all play the same build at league launch, who are welcoming to new players, and help guide them through the SSF experience?

————————————————–

I’ve been trying to enjoy PoE for almost a decade now- I stumble into a trade league maybe once a year, push a starter build into the early 90’s, farm red maps with minimal juicing, and  inevitably flame out; overwhelmed by the mechanics of efficient end-game farming and character progression. At my height I could pull together a few hundred Divine build, but I’ve never been a “Headhunter/Mageblood” level player.  

By the end of week 2 my character feels on par with veteran players after their second day. The market has passed me by and all the “get rich quick” tips have dried up. The economy is one of my favorite aspects of the game, and yet it feels like a sisyphean task I can never keep up with.

“Then play SSF”- is the advice I always hear.

I would love that. But I don’t know where to start. It got me thinking, are there any communities of solo self-found players who all decide to play the same build and help each other maximize their SSF play-through? 

And before you write me off with, “Just watch Zizarian, Steel Mage, or Pohx. Follow them.” Believe me, I’ve tried.

My other issue with PoE is watching content, and keeping up with the Joneses (Content Creators), becomes a larger task than just playing the game. These guys are doing 8-12 hours of content a day and I quickly lose track. I pick up some great tips for their YouTube clips, but as someone who dips in and out of the PoE universe- there’s a lot to digest when you’ve skipped the last 2, 3, 4+ leagues. A lot of guide content is based on old information and requires a fair bit of research to get up to speed. 

So my thought is, maybe there’s a helpful community of folks who like to shepherd the SSF newbies to the promised land. It would be fun to have a Discord group to bounce questions off of instead of combing through YouTube. I find almost any build fun to play (assuming it doesn’t die if I sneeze), and would be interested to see the progress of other SSF players on the same journey.
