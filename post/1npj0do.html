Kevin Durant and Jeff Green will break the record for longest duration between first becoming teammates (19 years), held by Stockton and Malone.

The record for longest streak of being teammates belongs to Karl Malone and John Stockton who were teammates on the Utah Jazz for 18 straight seasons (1985–2003), until Stockton retired and Malone played his last season for the Lakers. They also held the record for duration between their first and last seasons together (18).

KD and Jeff Green were both drafted in the 2007 Draft, and were teammates on the Seattle SuperSonics / Oklahoma City Thunder from 2007 to 2011. They teamed up again on Brooklyn in 2021, and now will suit up again as running mates in 2025⁄2026. This would give them 19 seasons in between their first and current season together.

Even through their own adversities (Jeff Green missing a whole season due to a life-threatening heart condition and KD tearing his Achilles in his 30’s), 19 seasons is quite the accomplishment.
