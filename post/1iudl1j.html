[Bobby Manning] Joe Mazzulla says Celtics have leaned into a double-big lineup this season after expecting to use it in last year’s playoffs.

Mazzulla just said on 98.5 that the #Celtics have been more of a double-big team this year after expecting to be that in the playoffs last year, but it didn’t happen. Said that was a story of being flexible. Of course, they didn’t have Porzingis for much of it.

Mazzulla added that the worst position you can be in late in the year is having to go to something you haven’t tried before. We’ve seen the Celtics try three-guard lineups, Kornet-Queta, and other experimental lineups throughout this season.
