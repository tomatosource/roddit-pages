What happened to the hop step?

This just dawned on me and sorry if it’s a “duh” moment for some of you guys, but the two-foot hop/gather into traffic was practically part of the standard superstar wing toolkit back in the 2000s as a way of splitting defenders, and it’s all but gone today.

Vince, T-Mac, Kobe, AI… they used it all the time as a way to get closer to the rim in traffic. It would open up all possibilities, from pull up jumpers to a continuation and finish. Can’t remember the last time I saw someone 6’6 or taller do it in today’s NBA. I’m talking swingmen with size similar to Kobe/Vince/Mac. But I guess today’s spacing has largely removed the problem this move was trying to solve, since the paint isn’t clogged anymore and players also tend to pass out of a double team rather than attack it.

I miss seeing it though. As a former hooper myself, growing up on that era of basketball, I remember how crazy effective of a move this was 😃
