Out of the 16 teams in the playoffs, OKC was ranked #13 for 3P% with 33.8%. The only teams that shot at a worse percentage were the Pistons, Grizzlies and Magic who were all eliminated in the first round with a combined 3-12 record.

Despite winning the Championship, OKC shot awfully from 3 over the entire playoffs at only 33.8% after being ranked #6 in the league over the regularly season at 37.4% For context in the regular season, 33.8% would have been ranked #28 in the league above only the Wizards and Magic.

  • Road games - 29.9% (5-5 record)
  • Home games - 37.5% (11-2 record)

This was largely caused by their shooting in road games while at home they shot their season average and only losses from last second buzzer beaters. It goes to show how good their defence and mid range shooting games are to be able to cover those numbers.

A very clear area of improvement for OKC looking to next season. Age and inexperience in these environments would be part of the reason along with possibly the fatigue of the long season, but other teams and players handled it better so it’s not just.

A change in the way the game is played?

In the age of NBA 3 point shooting, some of the stats that OKC put up are quite remarkable to have won a ring with. They are truly a unique and special team that you may look back at as one of those teams that changed how the rest of the league played with that strong focus on team defence moving away 3s + paint scoring and having the mid range game being a difference maker again.
