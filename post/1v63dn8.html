LeBron James is the first human in modern history to spend over 54.7% of his total time on Earth as an active NBA player

Since his total lifespan is around 15,180 days LeBron has officially crossed the 54.7% threshold of his total existence being under an NBA contract.

Which also means that he has now officially spent more of his life in the NBA than as a civilian.Which means if you encounter a LeBron James in the wild, there would be a higher mathematical probability that he is dressed in an NBA uniform on his way to a game than him being a regular member of society.

To put this into perspective, if he plays two more seasons, his civilian Ratio will drop below 42% making him legally and biologically classified as THE basketball guy.
