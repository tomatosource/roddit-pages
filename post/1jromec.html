The huntress feels like GGG saying play my way or don’t play.

Something I have always loved about PoE is the ability to put things together and make them work in unintended ways. But huntress (and I guess to a certain degree this patch) feels like it is just completely counter to that.

Damage bonuses for melee conditioned on using a projectile, damage bonuses for projectiles conditioned on using melee. Frenzy charged from parrying into a disengage, but if you don’t want to do that then have a lesser skill.

I saw there were some other things like that with other skills as well but was so excited about huntress I didn’t pay as much attention. Between all the crashes, this design just kinda boggles my mind.
