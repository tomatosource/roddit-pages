More Details on the Revised Contract Tom Dundon Offered Blazers Radio PBP Voice Travis Demers:

From Boston Globe Celtics and NBA columnist Gary Washburn, in his latest Sunday Basketball Notes column:

“Hearing the revised contract for Trail Blazers radio play-by-play voice Travis Demers offered by new owner Tom Dundon included a 60-plus percent pay cut, no benefits, no travel, and no analyst. Demers did not accept the offer. Longtime television play-by-play man Kevin Calabro turned down an offer to return while analyst Michael Holton was released from his contract.”
