Some key points -

since we can’t miss we can maximize leap slams AOE with both Magnified effect and reach.

Lots of herald of blood testing and I’ve fallen on Crystals, physical mastery, envenom and plague as the best combo for the gem.

Corrupting blood is really understated it seems. It clears maps like crazy as I leap slam away. It only scales with effect duration sadly but it is very strong.

Since I’m ssf I haven’t been able to just buy keys to test bosses but I have fought bosses and think this can one combo t0-t2 bosses. Two combo t3 and maybe 3-4 t4. Hard to say without reaching that higher level of content. Due to the nature of HOTG overwhelming presence and high stun build up is a must.

Combo for bossing - mace strike -&gt; bone shatter - infernal warcry - seismic warcry - HOTG (my HOTG deals 1m impact and 1.5m bleed atm)

Bone shatter needs to have incision and increased damage to bleed but not being able to bleed support tech. This allows bone shatter to apply 2 stacks of incision to give 20% more chance to bleed + 30% more magnitude on HOTG

Seismic cry HAS to have Malady or it’ll pop the incisions and nerf your HOTG.

I’m ssf obviously but this build could deal infinitely more damage on trade with access to some uniques. And just general better gear. This build can surpass 30k armor currently with scavenged plating and feels quite tanky. I am pretty sure once I manage to get chaos res gear it will feel very comfortable.

Weapon swap tech is pretty straight forward with map set up being shield/two hand with some aoe investment and boss set up being dual wield with more investment into magnitudes.

Hope this helps or inspires someone and good luck out there Exiles!
