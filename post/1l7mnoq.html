find the current stash tab underwhelming; it hardly seems better than a normal quad tab, here are my idea to improve this all-important tab:

HOW IT WORKS: (READ FIRST!)

The top three rows select either your map level or precursor tablet, YES, Tablets are part of mapping too GGG!

The Green tabs are preset searches (think Gem Tab) they are NOT filled by you but show any maps that have the attributes listed. You can select multiple with ctrl-click. (This means you don’t manually put maps in these green tabs, rather that maps that meet the search criteria show up from a global group.

The tabs shown are just want I threw together as an example.

Corrupted maps have a distinct marker (I just used an ugly red square, but you get the idea!)

Choosing Tablets would present a different set of preset search tabs of options, including Table specific affixes, Unique, etc
