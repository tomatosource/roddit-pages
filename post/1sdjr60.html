NBC Sports Boston: Bring Noa Back to Postgame

Kayla and Abby always bring tremendous energy. The guest commentators including Ashley were fun. But for the first time I can remember, Postgame Live and Postgame Plus were actually watchable, and it’s because Noa Dalzell was on it.

Every single thing she said was substantive, thoughtful, or genuinely interesting.

In a format where most commentary is filler designed to fill dead air, that stands out immediately. I like journalists and commentators and analysts who you can actually learn something from.

She reminds me of early Chris Forsberg on ESPN, the kind of reporter who outworks everyone, earns every insight, and never phones it in. Unfortunately, Forsberg has gotten comfortable. His analysis has gone from sharp to autopilot, which is a bummer for those of us who remember how good a writer he used to be.

And Eddie House? I don’t know who he’s phoning in his takes for, but it doesn’t feel like it’s for us. Half the time it feels like he’s waiting for someone else to say something interesting first.

NBC Sports Boston would be smart to give Noa a permanent seat on that desk. She’s clearly putting in the work, and she always has interesting things to say. Give her the platform that reflects it.

Make the product better. As fans of a championship contender, we deserve better from our local broadcast. Make the product worthy of the team.
