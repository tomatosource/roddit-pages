New Rune Suggestion - Greed Rune

As the developers already added Greed Shrine to the game, I would like to propose a new rune that provides gold find…

Lesser Greed Rune

  • Weapon: 15% Extra Gold from Monsters
  • Armour: 15% Extra Gold from Monsters

Greed Rune

  • Weapon: 30% Extra Gold from Monsters
  • Armour: 30% Extra Gold from Monsters

Greater Greed Rune

  • Weapon: 50% Extra Gold from Monsters
  • Armour: 50% Extra Gold from Monsters

As Gold is the main currency to perform several actions in PoE2, I think it would be fun to have the option to build a gold find character, especially when starting a new campaign where gold is relatively scarce. Who doesn’t want extra gold in their pockets?
