Temple is (probably) better for poe2 than you think

Putting this on top incase people feel shit about not having a full temple completed and are having fomo — It prints more raw currency and inflates the market but it is far from the best method of accumulating wealth in trade league because you can just do things that other people don’t want to do for more currency/hour - each temple takes a decent chunk of time to go through one temple even with builds that insta-clear rooms. It’s also rough for lower-end PCS.

Pros in order of importance:

  1. Gives a tangible end game goal to grind through as well as shows tangible progress (something more than just doing a pinnacle boss and a couple juiced maps and being done with it).
    *Side-note poe in general has a massive problem with boss design that allows you to just out-stat bosses so hard that there is no boss fight and grinding them becomes a chore rather than fun and engaging, so end game content can only really be enjoyed by massive loot goblins and not the classic video-game types.

  2. Gives end game builds a hill to climb up (increasing difficulty of monsters the more effectiveness you get) and therefore gives the game + league more longevity

  3. Gives access to a high quantity of the fun currencies (double corrupts)

  4. Gives reason to have multiple sets of gear (one for speed-clearing maps and one for power/speed-clearing temple)

  5. Allows the general userbase to use upper-end currencies

  6. Allows players to farm 100s of a specific unique to get a specific corruption (ie not reliant on trade)

Cons:

  1. Temples are too easy to brick and limited in customization (easily fixable)

  2. Makes generic high-rarity uniques/currencies outside of temple worse

  3. Exacerbates performance issues (poe2 problem, not a dev unsure if they’ll be able to fix it)

  4. Exacerbates the problem with ground loot in its current state (poe2 problem, not a dev unsure if they’ll be able to fix it)

  5. Exacerbates the problem with headhunter’s existence (poe2 problem, not a dev unsure if they’ll be able to fix it)

I’ve been playing on and off since nemesis, enjoyed ruthless and expedition league (notable since they are low-loot modes), stopped playing hardcore by the time crucible came out because I don’t have time to reset anymore.

The only other time I felt like I had comparable fun goals end-game that weren’t loot-centric was when they released the atlas tree in ritual.
