I don’t much like the current wisp mechanics. :(

I thought id post some ideas of mine to maybe fix wisps and see what others thought. Both of my ideas and to hear their own.

First, I would love it if it just shot off and left a trail to follow at my leisure. Because while I can more often than not kill everything fast enough, it isn’t exactly fun, just stressful. Especially if you find one at the very start of the game and because currently the loot from it isn’t any better than regular mobs 99.99% of the time

Second idea: The wisp randomly wanders around the map, not stopping at all, and for every second you’re in its aura effect, it boosts your chances of finding better loot by X%. Then, at the end of its timer, it pops an elite to fight that has the potential to drop the loot. That way, people can choose to try to keep up with it or not. And those that can’t keep up aren’t penalized by no wisp loot. And those that can and do are rewarded for doing so. The better you keep up, the higher the chance for better loot. And if you can clear the map first, and THEN get the wisp, you have a guaranteed ability to stay with the wisp and get better loot, assuming you kill the end mob it spawns. It would reward both those that can keep up no matter what and those with the patience and forethought to plan ahead, only punishing those who are impatient and unable to fight well enough to keep up if they start following in an enemy heavy area.

My third idea would be to have the wisp just stick to the player or stay in place for a set time period. And for the duration, it “attracts” mobs to keep spawning around them. At the end, it spawns the boss monster, who drops the loot. No jumping into tons of mobs that you almost have to not kill to get past fast enough to not get to far from the wisp. You just kill what comes to you.
