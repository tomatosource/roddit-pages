1960s: Bill Russell and Wilt Chamberlain

1970s: Kareem Abdul Jabbar

1980s: Magic Johnson and Larry Bird

1990s: Michael Jordan and Hakeem Olajuwon

2000s: Tim Duncan, Shaquille O’Neal and Kobe Bryant

This kind of makes sense since Kobe and Shaq played on the same team they both got the majority of their chips in.

2010s: LeBron James and Steph Curry

Obviously a lot of these players got achievement that made them top 10 potentially outside of their “decade” like Kareem getting 5 chips in the 80s and Steph getting himself in top 10 convos in 2022.
