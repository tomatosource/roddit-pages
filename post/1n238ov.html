This build centers around Infusion, a new mechanic that GGG is adding in 0.3, The Third Edict, while using Blood Mage, a class that received a sizable buff in 0.3. I also put great emphasis into making this build as SMOOTH as possible, minimizing the number of buttons you have to press at all costs.

Elaborate leveling setups included in the PoB to guide you through every phase of the game.

This is my personal build preparations for build 0.3, it may not be perfect but its good enough for now and I’ll improve on it whenever I can.
