Zone into map -&gt; IGNORE Deli -&gt; run through the WHOLE MAP for runes -&gt; Put up Portal near the Summoning Circle in case you bug out and have to relog (without this Deli will end before you can get back to the circle after relogging) -&gt; Checkpoint TP back to Start, start Deli -&gt; DO NOT TP instantly, as you want the bar to fill up first so it doesn’t end by you going too far ahead of it via Checkpoint TP -&gt; TP to Map boss to kill it, then TP to Circle and kill bosses after kiting them for 10-20 seconds or if you don’t and you bug out relog.
