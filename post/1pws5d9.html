Do you think a SP reevaluation is needed for the HOF?

I was having a discussion in another thread and the person said that we’re currently going through a re-evaluation of starting pitcher candidacy. And that guys like Hamels and Buerhle fit that re-evaluation.

Would love to hear people explain this.

To me, I don’t see how you justify a re-evaluation for players like Hamels and Buerhle when they were peers of Verlander, Scherzer, Kershaw, and Greinke.

Like you clearly had HOF-caliber, generational pitchers in this era. So why lower the bar when people still meet it?

It’s not like the outlook is bleak, either. Sale has a HOF case that’s much better than Hamels/Buerhle. We’re seeing potential HOFers blossom in Skubal and Skenes.

It’s not like modern Cy Young winners have ERAs of 3.80 and strike out 150 batters.

So why should we be lowering the barrier to entry already?
