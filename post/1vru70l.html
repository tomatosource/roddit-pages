Is this game just not for me?

I wanted to hear from others to maybe see if anyone has a similar experience or can offer some reassurance.

Context: I like ARPGs casually. I do not get super into the grind but I enjoy coming back for new seasons, leveling characters, making and trying new builds, etc. I have played a lot of the genre overall. I’ve played all entries of Diablo, Last Epoch, and a little bit of PoE1. I bought the early access to PoE2 back when it first released.

So my problem is one I can’t really nail down. It seems to be only with this game. I can’t seem to push myself to actually beat the game or get to end game. I wouldn’t say it’s because I get bored but more like I lose motivation. I have tried all the classes and a lot of the ascendancies. But I always seem to fall off once I get to Act 3 in the story. I have never gotten past it. I don’t have issues with the difficulty of the game or get stuck on anything really, I just like can’t seem to stick with it. I haven’t had this issue in any other ARPG I’ve played. Even if I don’t do much of the end game I still enjoy the story and leveling experience and at least get one character to max level. I like the way PoE2 feels to play, I like the visuals and it feels satisfying.

Does anyone else feel the same way? Can anyone offer words of encouragement to push through because maybe I’m really missing out by not doing so?
