Poe2 Nvidia Dlss / Smooth Motion

Just thought id share my experience.

When i started playing i was on a 1080ti and 9900k besides juiced up maps it wasn’t terrible, but i upgraded to a 5070ti (i know im cpu bottlenecked now). Im playing on a 1080p 240hz monitor. Iv been playing with settings with and without dlss i was getting around 100fps with 1%lows 50fps. In the Nvidia App I set dlss Override Model-Preset to latest and turned on Smooth Motion, In the game im using Vulkan. Now im getting almost 200fps with 1% lows 100fps and frametime around 11ms. Game feels alot smoother to me, No issues with juiced maps so far. level 86 Witch Hunter.
