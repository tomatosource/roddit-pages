One thing I never understood about part of the PoE1/2 playerbase (and especially the forums)

To me, it seems like there’s a lot of players who essentially hate the game and think it sucks. I get that there’s bad parts about the game but to many the whole game seems filled with them.

And they have hundreds of hours put to it, and are active on the forums.

I can see that forums are mostly used for venting negative experiences; as humans we have a bias for negativity and are more likely to give negative than positive feedback.

But many of those players have a post history of months or a full year full of telling how much the game sucks.

So.. Why they spend time playing the game and spend time in its community?

I just don’t get it. PoE1 and 2 have had some of the most negatively minded players I’ve seen. I haven’t seen this much negativity in GW2 or ESO or D2 or Warframe or CS or NWN or Overwatch (games that I’ve played more than a hundred hours).
