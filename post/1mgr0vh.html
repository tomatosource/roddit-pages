After getting lightly roasted on here and some time has passed in the offseason….I’m still a believer this Celtics team will finish at very minimum .500.

Maybe not a top 5 starting five in the East like I had said before and I get that saying fans should have optimism this season considering the expectations you all have of this franchise and JT’s injury being such a heartbreaker, I understand why fans are kinda down on the C’s this year. You all expect and demand greatness and this team isn’t going to be great. It’s why I respect Celtics fans…you expect to win.

But, man….I think Mazzulla is a fantastic coach and I just think you guys have enough in this version of the Eastern Conference to be a .500 or better team. I still think 40-42 wins is the minimum with good health. Maximum isn’t much higher than 42….like 46, but I feel this team has a pretty stable floor….I think JB/DWhite/PP is just too good of a trio. Whoever is left between Niang/Simons/Hauser offensively are too good a fit next to those former three. Celtics are under the second apron after waiving Davidson so there is a little less urgency there, and value can be considered a bit more in a deal vs just trading one of these three just to do it. There’s basically no pathway to dip under the first apron/tax unless Simons is moved in a deal that cuts salary.

If Simons is around Opening Night, Ant/DWhite/JB are gonna put points on the board. Shooting around them in either Hauser or Niang. Yes, the C position is a weakness (though they have the ability to play match-ups and optionality at the position) and this team is very young off the bench. If there’s any spot this team is gonna be found wanting beyond the C spot, it’s with the bench; Pritchard not withstanding and he’ll have to take on more playmaking this season and we’ll see how he handles that. Yes, there are question marks but fewer than I think narrative has engineered here. Jaylen and DWhite plug a lot of holes. Jaylen should actually be healthy going into the season. He showed some real playmaking chops this past season that suggests he’s got another gear to tap in to.

Think the C’s are in a decent spot to have a top 10 offense, and Mazzulla will get just enough out of the defense, perhaps through turnover generation, where this team outscores a lot of opponents throughout the season. C’s also have one of the very best home advantages in the association.

I’m not going to be insensitive and tell fans to pretend to be excited about the season ahead, and deleted the original post because I got past vs passed wrong lol, but I do think this team has more in it than a lot of fans seem to and some oddsmakers. Seems likely JT will return this season too….not saying this will be a chip season, but if you make the Big Dance, anything can happen. It’ll be to the benefit perhaps of these other C’s to be forced into higher leverage roles—grow and sharpen skills that’ll make them better basketball players in the long run.
