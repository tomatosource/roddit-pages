Teams/Players you don’t want to see win

What are teams or players you don’t want to see when as a Celtics fan.

Te first team I don’t want to see win is the Miami heat. They’ve been giving us trouble since the big three got together but it’s Jimmy Butler’s teams that really gave me nightmares 🙃. Caleb Martin and Gabe Vincent averaging like 35 or 40 points collectively should not have happened.Of course Jimmy would always punk us back then but it was the role players that chose to average career highs against us. All that to say I never want to see the Miami Heat win.

My second team that I don’t want to win is the Oklahoma City Thunder. I would always get frustrated as a Celtics fan when people used to say Shai was better than Tatum ,but that ended up being the case with how ALL TIME consistent that man is. I also don’t want OKC to be the first team to go back to back or win multiple times in a few years.

If I had to stop being a hater for a second, I honestly would not be mad at Joel Embiid getting a ring. We’ve been giving them “belt to ass” ever since he got into the league, and I feel bad that a talent like himself can’t even get to the finals.Partially due to him but his teammates have been very flaky over the years.
