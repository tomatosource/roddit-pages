Clippers coaches in training camp are wearing shirts designed by Jeff Van Gundy that say “Get the F.U.C.K. back”. F stands for Floor balance. U stands for Urgency. C stands for Communication. K stands for “Know we lost to Denver because of transition”

*C stands for Consistent Communication and Concentration but didn’t fit in the title.
