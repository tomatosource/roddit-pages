My anecdotal experience with the new Abyss Omen farming setup (103% monster rarity maps)

So I’ve pretty much only farmed abyss this league, mostly hand rolled tablets, usually with 1 good modifier, 30-40% monster rarity T15s and go.

I was getting a decent number of omens of light with 80-90 rarity % on gear, I would say maybe 2-3 per 10 maps doing this and the upside was I didn’t have to pay anything.

This eventually made me quite rich, improved my dps and clear speed and made me decide to optimize my time better and spend more to make more.

I started doing the 103% monster rarity maps (the ones you make activating the 3 map omens at once and chaos a 5 mod map) and getting slightly better tablets (none of the 5+ div ones mind you). Truthfully, I was getting considerably more Amanamu monsters on my pits, but my Omen of Light drop rate was not really improving. Now the problem was that I was making a bit more, but spending a lot more making these maps.

Today tho, I hit a breakthrough. I got my rarity up to 173% on my gear, buying 3x rarity ring and 2x rarity amulet. I realized by making these monster rarity maps, you are effectively taking all potential item rarity from them, decreasing your chance for the better omens, especially with low gear rarity.

So my little experiment was buying 50+ monster rarity maps instead of crafting so that they include some item rarity as well, while also having very high rarity in gear. Lo and behold, I am now dropping an Omen of Light once every 2 maps on average.

TLDR; by crafting 103% monster rarity maps and having lower rarity on gear you are dropping more omens, but less of them are the rare ones, light and abyssal echoes. By running more “normal” maps around 40-60% monster rarity and some item rarity and having better rarity on gear I have increased my omen of light drop significantly.
