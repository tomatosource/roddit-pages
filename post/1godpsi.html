The Bucks’ season will be determined by their record on Thanksgiving.

Starting with tonight’s loss against the Celtics, the Bucks play 7 of 8 games at home and 8 of 10. Of those 8 games, only one is against a winning team, the Houston Rockets.

The Bucks play the Hornets twice, the Raptors/Pacers/Wizards/Pistons/Bulls at home during this stretch. The Wizards are their last game in November, first game after Thanksgiving and they play a road game in Miami before Thanksgiving.

They play one team with a winning record currently the rest of the month. We’re gonna know what this team is truly gonna do this year through the rest of the month, but I think Thanksgiving is a date to watch. If they’re not around .500 going into that Wizards game, the calls for Giannis to be moved will grow loud.

I’m not encouraged with their game today because while I think they did a good job getting Giannis in the middle of the court and playing through him and utilized the PnR with Dame+Giannis a bit better today, it is unsustainable for Giannis to have to carry that much for this team to win games. Dame had a weak game tonight, and Giannis doesn’t have one guy you can consistently rely on every game, night in and out. We’ll see what happens but I don’t think they’re dramatically better than these other weak East teams anymore. I’m kinda sour on them now, but not burying them yet like others. Still only 10 games in.
