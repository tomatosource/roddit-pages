0.5 Campaign Feedback from a Merc

I finished my campaign starter yesterday on a Merc in 10-11 hours (1-ish hour act 1, 2-ish hour act2, 2.5 hour act 3, 3 hour act 4, and 2 to 3 hour interludes w/ a little AFKing and trading). I’ve been playing since 0.1 launch, 1.1K hours in POE2, and maybe 10-15 runs through campaign both from normal personal characters + with party. So that’s some context for my background.

Overall the campaign went really smooth. The changes that have been made in Act 3 to make some of the zones less “mazy” and a little more linear/logical in progression was much appreciate.

The changes to Waterways was really nice. Its actually a little shocking how big of a difference the switch to trigger pads makes for the entire zone’s progression feel. My pet theory is that the ancients somehow stole the waterway’s levers and stuffed them in their ancient gateway place though…

After the Waterways, the new layout on the Titan Valley felt really good as well and the level design change felt way cooler.

Maybe the biggest thing I noticed though was that the bosses all felt…. easy. Rings and amulets were rarer in Act 1 and it was hard to build up resist, so I was honestly expecting the Count to be a pain in the ass with basically no resist. I completely ROFL stomped him… I still ended up interacting with each of his mechanics at least once, but it was way less frustrating than in the past. Some of this is experience and some it might have been my build, but everyone I know equally agreed he felt way easier.

It wasn’t just the Count though, all of the bosses were much more… approachable. I know at least one content creator will have opinions on this, but I don’t care about the difficulty change personally. If the changes were made for completely new players to the game, then I suspect this works well to prevent bosses being barriers for progression for new players. If people want more challenging fights with the bosses, then there is the end game.

Bug check recommendation to GGG: Check the Dig site boss’s HP. I’m like 90% sure his HP is bugged. Most bosses were way easier, but that guy’s HP basically vanished with a sneeze and it felt like an outlier.

I wish the Rune quests were handled better in campaign though. I ended up having to almost full clear the Jungle Ruin to find the quest encounter for the Rune’s on that map. If the quest chain stays in after this league, then I recommend better “signaling” on the map on where to find those locations.

Final thing I’ll say, if GGG can manage to make Act 5 and 6 flow just as well as what we have now, then they’ll be real winners. Just stay away from meandering mazes and levers you have to stop and pull to progress, and they’ll be solid.
