Linas Kleiza on the Schroder incident and argument with German players

Dennis Schroder at halftime in response to the racist remarks said as team captain its Valanciunas’ responsibility to take action. Which caused an argument between the two teams in the tunnel at half time. Kleiza had the following statement.

“It’s not right to hold our players accountable for the actions of one fan. As a federation we have done everything possible to apologise to Dennis and try to make things right, but its not right to blame the actions of one fan on our players and staff and expect them to take responsibility. We have the best fans in the world and of course racism has no place in this sport. But its not up to us to supervise each individual fan and their behaviour”
