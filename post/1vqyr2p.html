New Player just starting the game!

And holy crap dude. Why is it so addictive?! I came from around 1k hours in Diablo 4, and stopped playing before this last expansion. So I tried POE2 on a whim, cause I heard it was really good. So I booted up POE2, and right off the bat, I liked it way more then D4. The beginning parts, (well, the only parts I’ve played so far), is way darker in tone and feeling then I was expecting. For example, in the character select screen, all the characters you didn’t choose are hanged, while the one you picked survives.

And I’m not gonna lie, at first, I was very confused about everything thrown at me in game. The only thing I really knew how to do was kill and loot and stare in confusion about what the loot did/was. However, I did see a beginner build for witch, (the class I chose), and after figuring out how to put it together, I just started blasting. And my god its so much fun. I can just hit my little skill rotation and my minions just kill everything in sight. How did I not notice this game for so long?

Anyways, I just wanted to say that I really look forward to sinking tons of time into this game, and really look forward to the endgame mass killing. Thanks for listening!

(For anyone curious, my witch build is just using Skeletal Arsonists, Unearth, Contagion, and Flame wall and spamming all my skills and letting my minions kill everything. If anyone has any fun builds I can try and work towards, lemme know!)
