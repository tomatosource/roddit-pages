High level tier 3 crafting bench in Vaal temple should have perfect currency effect

Everyone complains how Vaal temple is tedious and not rewarding and providing a lot feedback on how to improve that.

I think if temple crafting benches would upgrade currency that they apply to items would solve some of the cost-opportunity-reward loop problems.

For example:

Level 60+ area of temple in combination with level 2+ crafting bench should give effect of a greater crafting currency. Or at least have a chance to be such.

Level 79+ area in combination with level 3 crafting bench should give effect of a perfect crafting currency.

With this type of reward it will at least make sense to spend 6 maps in hope of improving your unfinished crafts. And I don’t think this would be game or balance breaking.
