The JB trade was inevitable

The more i’ve sit with the trade the more I’ve understood that this was pretty much inevitable when we signed the Jaylen to the supermax in 2023. With the new CBA, you can’t win with 2 supermax players unless they complement each other extremely well. As good as JB and JT were, they needed a lot of help to finally get over the hump and Brad was only able to give them that much help because JB and JT’s extensions hadn’t kicked in yet and they could afford Jrue and KP. But it also wasn’t a mistake to sign him to that contract because we ended up retaining JB for the 23-24 season and wouldn’t have won a championship without him. But the seemingly weak return was always gonna be the outcome unless JB became a consensus top 5 player and teams were willing to give a haul despite his contract(in that scenario I don’t think Brad trades him). I think in a couple years we’ll see the same thing with Mitchell if the Cavs try to trade him and get back what will look like a horrible return on paper because of the new contract he just signed.
