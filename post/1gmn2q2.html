Nikola Jokic could be on his way to join Lebron James, Michael Jordan, Bill Russel, and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar as the 5th person in history to win 4 MVPS

Jokic play has been overshadowed by the Nuggets rocky start to the year but after a revenge win against one of the leagues best team OKC, Jokic and with the help of stellar play from Russel Westbrook as of late the Denver Nuggets seem to be back on the right track.

Jokic this season is now 4th in PPG at 28.8 on 53.2% within an incredible 51.4% from 3, 1st in assists per game at 11, and 1st in rebounds per game with 13.5 which has him averaging a triple double to start the season .The Nuggets have also won 5 of their last 6 games including missing Jamal Murray and Aaron Gordon for some of these.

Its obviously far too early in the season to start picking a solid MVP candidate especially with the stellar play of Anthony Davis, SGA, Jayson Tatum, Trae Young and Donovan Mitchell but the horsey man is off to a fantastic start.
