Returning for the first time since 2024 - change to difficulty early game?

This is anecdotal, curious if others have experienced the same.

I’m playing for the first time since the beta launched at the end of 2024 and am noticing that the game feels significantly less challenging early on.

In my first run-through, I had to do more problem solving with my builds. For example, I had to use a cold resist/freeze charm to survive the act 1 boss. This time around I could rush in with the intent to do as much damage as possible without worrying about building for survival. In general, I find that rare mobs are generally less threatening as well as I steamroll through areas.

My guess is that the drops have been improved so my character is just stronger— but curious if anyone knows if this difficulty change was intentional or has noticed the same?

Overall I’m still enjoying the game and am happy to run through it a bit quicker the second time around— but I did like the aspect of being forced to change out items to solve different problems from the start of the game. Curious how others feel.

For added context my first run through in 2024 was a lightning rod ranger and this time around I am a werewolf/cold Druid.
