Ticket draw registration for the 2028 Olympics (including baseball and softball) begins at 7 AM pacific time

The 2028 Olympics will feature baseball at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, and softball at Devon Park in Oklahoma City

The Olympics and Major League Baseball have been in discussion to allow MLB players to play in the games, and the start of the baseball tournament was bumped up to the day before the Opening Ceremony so it can finish before MLB’s All Star Game, hinting that the two sides are close to an agreement allowing MLB players to play in the games

In order to purchase tickets later this year, you must be registered for the draw. Once your name is drawn, you will be sent a link to purchase tickets

How to register

  1. Go here
  2. In the crawler at the top of the page, click on “REGISTER NOW FOR THE LA28 TICKET DRAW”
  3. Registration will open at 7 AM pacific time
  4. At that time, you will be directed to either a registration page, or a virtual waiting room

Good luck to everyone trying to go, nothing sounds more epic than a gold medal game in front of 52,000 screaming fans at Dodger Stadium

EDIT: looks like there will be a special locals presale for those that live in Los Angeles and Oklahoma City
