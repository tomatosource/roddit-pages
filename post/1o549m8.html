[Chan] Devin Booker: “The game of basketball touches everywhere in the world, but especially in China. Just seeing the reaction of the fans, their faces lit up just upon our arrival…. I know I have tremendous respect for the country of China [and] they have the same for us.”

The Phoenix Suns’ star player Devin Booker has hailed the importance of the NBA’s return to China, as his team prepared to face the Brooklyn Nets in two pre-season games in Macau.

The pair of games, to be played at the casino hub’s Venetian Arena on Friday and Sunday, will be the league’s first matches in China since 2019, and the 10-year veteran acknowledged that it was a “big deal” for it to be back.

“It’s super-important – we have a big fan base out here in China, and we have Chinese players in the NBA,” the Suns shooting guard said.

“Being here on their homeland and letting them see us play in person, for the youths and the next generation of Chinese players getting this experience on their land, [can] open up their inspiration, you know, make them feel they have a chance [to play in the NBA].”

The four-time All-Star, an Olympic champion with Team USA in 2021 and 2024, added that he shared the excitement felt among Chinese fans about the resumption of NBA action in the country.

“The game of basketball touches everywhere in the world, but especially in China,” the 28-year-old said. “Just seeing the reaction of the fans, their faces lit up just upon our arrival.

“So it’s important for us and for the league to be here in person and doing this.”

There have been no NBA games in China since the reaction to a 2019 tweet by Daryl Morey, then general manager of the Houston Rockets, that expressed support for anti-government protesters in Hong Kong. It prompted China to halt televising of the NBA, and Chinese sponsors pulled millions of dollars in deals.

With the league welcomed back, Booker said he felt the enthusiasm was as great as ever.

“From my perspective, it’s always been an extreme amount of love – I can see that through the social platforms,” Booker said.

“I know I have tremendous respect for the country of China [and] they have the same for us.”
