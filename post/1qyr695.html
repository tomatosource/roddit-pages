What are some examples of trades that have flip-flopped in terms of the perception of which team got fleeced?

As a Phillies fan, the one I think of is Curtis Mead for Cristopher Sanchez. It was initially a nothing trade. Curtis Mead shot up to a top prospect while Sanchez was a nobody. It was seen as a patented Rays fleece. I know I and many other Phillies fans wished that trade could’ve been undone.

A coupe of years later, Mead has fizzled out while Sanchez is a top pitcher in baseball.

Diaz for Kelenic is another very good example of this.
