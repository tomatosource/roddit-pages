The one thing I miss about old weapon swap - swapping keybinds

The poe2 weapon swap is great and all and does a lot of things much better than the poe1 version, but the one thing it cannot do is swap keybinds.

For example I played spears in 0.4, Lightning Spear for clear and Explosive Spear Storm Lance combo for single target. And it was a lot of fun, except finding comfortable keybinds for three main skills, that part was awful. Especially for WASD, having a skill you use a lot bound to Q or E or whatever can be very uncomfortable. I do have sidebuttons on my mouse but that just didn’t feel good either.

Instead I would love to just press weapon swap so that my RMB and LMB keybinds swap. As in: if I do bosses I want Explosive Spear and Storm Lance on my RMB and LMB, but when I map I want Lightning Spear on RMB instead. And I don’t want to manually swap keybinds all the time, it’s annoying.

I know, if I press weapon swap then why don’t I just press a key for the skill? Because Explosive Spear Storm Lance combo for example is pretty spammy, so instead of clicking the skill bound on keyboard dozens of times I’d just press weapon swap once and have them on RMB and LMB.

I know that’s pretty incompatible with how weapon swap works right now, but I’d gladly give up whatever advantage it doesn’t work with. I just want to press weapon swap and have my keybinds swap, no matter how many funcionalities may be incompatible.
