Is chaos damage just gonna suck this league?

So I was planning on playing a chaos witch but I’m pretty worried about endgame with the 40% more HP / 40% less pack size changes.

There have been zero mentioned changes to chaos skills in the patch notes and Johnathan’s mention that chaos skills “need something” like a new status effect makes me very worried that chaos is testing like shit in their play tests but they haven’t got the time to give it anything for 0.4.

A reminder to why the changes to late game probably impact the chaos spell play style more than any other… it’s a play style which relies on mass DoT contagion spreading when it kills enemies and benefits from large pack sizes.

  • Chaos is entirely DoT with no real big instant damage spells.

  • 40% more HP means every individual enemy always takes 40% longer to kill than before due to chaos’s DoT nature. Not the case with other play styles where you can still reach a point of one shotting mobs and have the 40% hp buff mean nothing.

  • Taking longer to kill mobs means contagion spreads slower.. which again means mobs take longer to die, kind of a double whammy.

Honestly I think it’s pretty intentional that Witch and chaos nodes / spells have gotten basically zero changes in the patch notes. They see the problem and will probably fix it in 0.5.

Thoughts?
