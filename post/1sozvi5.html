Horford Departure

As someone who’s pro Al-Horford and was deeply saddened when he left us. I came to terms that his departure benefitted the team greatly. Queta had what I would call a Most improved player year. Hopefully Horford can came back as a Celtic Vet to give the young guys some advice before hanging the jersey up.
