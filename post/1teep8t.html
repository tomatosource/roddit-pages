Herald of Ash doesn’t actually “Ignite”

https://reddit.com/link/1teep8t/video/vpwdqluo9e1h1/player

I’ve always heard that Herald of Ash is the least useful of the herald skills; but I had no idea it doesn’t even do what it promises to, ignite enemies. Herald of Ash says that it ignites based on overkill damage. I see the ignite animation with a damage over time affect, but there’s no ignite status on the enemy. My Volcanic Eruption support is supposed to trigger when I hit an ignited enemy, but doesn’t seem to work with the “ignite” caused by Herald of Ash. I’m assuming this means that none of the “do x% more damage with hits to ignited enemies” nodes actually synergize with Herald of Ash. It also seems that none of the ignite support gems can be used with Herald Of Ash from what I can tell. I see a few posts in the support forum asking if this is a bug, but has there ever been any response from GGG? This seems like a significant oversight.
