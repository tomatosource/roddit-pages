I love this game

I have thousands of hours and Dollars spent on POE since 2012. They did such a good job with making POE modern with number 2. It looks and feels amazing. But….boy do I f***ing hate the skills and the skill system. I get so depressed when I go to plan a build. Being limited to a certain play style with weapons feels so trash.

“Oh you like two handed melee? Here, pick from these 3 skills that all focus around the same mechanic. Also, you must do 14 situational things to setup the skill for it to actually be effective. Why? Because it is engaging gameplay that everyone wants, trust us.” - This is obnoxious!

Having interesting interactions is great, but for me it feels like Poe2 is constantly trying to make you play your character the way someone else envisioned it. The skills feel more D4 like than POE like. They used to be a big part of build creativity and character fantasy fulfillment.
