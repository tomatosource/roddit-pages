I Reorganized the NBA into 25 Teams Based on the Watersheds of Players’ Birthplaces.

Recently I was bored, and I decided to sort the NBA into new teams based on which watershed they were born in. A couple of caveats beforehand: by “watershed” I generally mean the last freshwater waterway before hitting either the ocean or the endpoint of an endorheic basin, if you take the path that water would travel from a player’s birthplace. Secondly, there are some towns that are built on the border between two watersheds (this is EXTREMELY relevant). If I encountered a town built between two watersheds, I looked up where the local hospital was, and chose the watershed closer to the hospital. If a city was large enough to have multiple hospitals and multiple waterways in the area, I generally chose the most iconic or central waterway for that city. So without further ado, let’s look at the 25 new teams which can field at least a full starting lineup of current NBA players and/or first round draft picks.

Tier 1: The 2 Heavyweights

There are only 2 teams that are really in contention for this, and they are of course, the two teams that are on either side of Akron Ohio. Team St. Lawrence River, and Team Mississippi River. Team Mississippi River would have run away with this exercise, but unfortunately, the Akron City Hospital is on the North side of town, and therefore falls into the watershed of the St. Lawrence River.

Team Mississippi River

An absolute powerhouse with 123 total players, I landed on the following roster:

PG: Derrick White, Darryn Peterson, De’Aaron Fox

SG: Austin Reaves, Desmond Bane, Jalen Suggs

SF: Jalen Williams, Brandon Miller, Cam Johnson

PF: Jayson Tatum, Chet Holmgren, PJ Washington

C: Anthony Davis, Daniel Gafford, Robert Williams III

Notable players left off: Ayo Dosunmu, Dean Wade, Moses Moody, Keaton Wagler, Morez Johnson, Michael Porter Jr., Matas Buzelis, and many more

And their only real competitors with 67 players of their own:

Team St. Lawrence River

PG: Stephen Curry, Andrew Nembhard, Shaedon Sharpe

SG: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Devin Booker, Lu Dort

SF: Lebron James, Dillon Brooks, Andrew Wiggins

PF: Draymond Green, Jalen Johnson, Miles Bridges

C: Zach Edey, Isaiah Stewart, Jayden Quaintance

Notable players left off: NAW, RJ Barrett, Bennedict Mathurin, Tyler Herro, Kon Kneuppel, Darius Acuff, and many more.

Ultimately, these two teams are just a cut above the rest. They were the only two teams to have more than 25 players total, and it shows in the quality of the squad. St. Lawrence is really overloaded at SG and underloaded at C, but the raw star power of that Akron-based starting lineup makes it a pretty close battle for me. Meanwhile Team Mississippi might lack some of the top-end star power, but a more balanced team construction and elite talent from top to bottom puts them in this tier above the rest.
