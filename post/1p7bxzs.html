Yes this is Raptors propaganda 😅

I was curious what other teams had big leaps (or falls) and thought it would be helpful to visualize them like this, maybe others will find it interesting as well.

A few (unoriginal) thoughts:

  • OKC is absurd

  • San Antonio’s taken a big leap. Wemby has similar on/off splits as last year (~10 pts/100 better with him on the floor) but last year the Spurs were decent with him on the floor (+2.3) and absolutely terrible when he sat (-7.9). This year they’re elite when he plays (+9.5) and only slightly below average when he sits (-0.8).

  • Phoenix is cool — anyone have a good article or video going over what they’re doing this year to be so much better? I haven’t been paying enough attention to have a good understanding there

  • Poor Wizards

  • Miami’s taken a surprising (to me at least) jump — other teams have bigger increases but some degree of improvement is expected with young players improving (eg. Detroit) or new additions (eg. San Antonio). I don’t know if many people anticipated the Heat getting this much better, especially with Tyler Herro being injured. Erik Spoelstra for COTY in perpetuity.

Of course take everything with a grain of salt considering we’re not even 1⁄4 through the season. Still I find it interesting to take a look at these early season trends. What numbers stand out to you?
