PSA: Stop fracturing +5 melee/+4 proj skills on your crafts.

Every day there’s a new post of someone asking how to complete this quarterstaff craft and it has +5 melee fractured and they’re talking about how they either want tri elemental or pure phys with t1 crit and some attack speed.

If you want to craft a crit martial weapon and you dont have 12 mirrors at your disposal, only go for crit% fracture.

Yes +5 melee is rare and low weight, but it can NOT be whittled off at any point of the craft, crit can.

If you want to learn how to craft you gotta utilize https://poe2db.tw/us/Modifiers

This is your bible.

Knowing what mods have what itemlevel is vital information when you’re going for an expensive craft, so that you don’t waste 100’s of divs and then realize ”oh, this mod will never be safe from whittles so I’ll have to settle with the rest of the weapon being bad”.

Edit: Obviously I’m not saying everyone should be crafting mirror weapons, its not feasable and way too expensive.

But I’ve seen so many freaking posts of people looking for xyz suffixes and zyx prefixes and they have a mod that is un-whittleable fractured.
