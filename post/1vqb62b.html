How do you think Andre Drummond will do as KAT’s backup?

The Knicks were dealt a big blow as their backup Center Mitchell Robinson left the Knicks to sign with the Celtics and he was the perfect backup center for KAT as he was a great offensive rebounder, a lob threat, and a great rim protector off the bench. Unfortunately, his shooting woes made him unplayable at times last playoffs as teams often did the Hack-a-Mitch strategy on him and he often missed his free throws. While Drummond doesn’t have the defensive abilities like Robinson, he is a great rebounder as well and a slightly better offensive player as he can make his free throws and space the floor too.
