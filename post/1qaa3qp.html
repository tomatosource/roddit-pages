The Casual Player(s) and End Game - Incremental Progress Problem

POE2 plays really well up until the levels where likely they see rampant fall-off in casual player retention into end of normal acts and then the treadmill of mapping. If anything, right now it plays “oddly” with the mixed intent - from the first of EA where they were attempting to go for the same “heavy hitting” and “decisions matter in combat” feel the same that D4 attempted (and failed), and emulating a bit of the Souls-like combat systems. 

But you reach this point in the end game where your progress doesn’t feel impactful through your own effort. Through your own grind, to a point - but mostly so long as you buy it.

The challenge:

  • End game and the more progression into crafting and it’s complexity is still off/a miss.
  • You generally are (highly likely) to only have significant progress on your character via TRADE, not organic luck (after a certain point relevant to your build) or through even incremental random crafting luck the way the system is designed. Where you are then solely interacting with the game as a currency aggregation system; hoping to find ways to progress, but then realizing you have only one path - trade to someone who played more easily/templated than you.
  • You have limited to few deterministic crafting methods that really are intuitive to the average player load without significant external crafting guides (i.e., 2-4 min shorts on “how to go after an item like this” being mandatory).
  • You cannot “inch” your character along without trade. And while you do have one inch-along systems (i.e., Catalysts), they also have rigid caps that don’t let you feel like you are making headway over time. (Runes having tiers and scaling was a great step towards improvement, but those also progress so quickly towards irrelevance for ALL lower tiers that they are intrinsically ONLY valid for the max-tier as soon as you get past parts of the main campaign.)
    • Catalysts, and Quality systems are basic enough that they don’t scale past ‘defacto necessary’ and offer nothing after their fixed point. Since they’re mandatory for any build the are only a minor currency sink (even if prices have been going up) - which further erode people exploring/experimenting since it doesn’t pay to up-Quality every item.
  • Systems are designed at times for only a hyper-limited set of players to interact with. When your crafting system only works by relying on third party websites to explain any of it, and failure to use it correctly results in hours/days of lost game time and effort - something is off. The overall layers are not perhaps all bad, but their presentation, their clarity and then the entire lack of in-game support for consuming the crafting systems beyond “basic currency” really reduces a layer to a more niche community of users.
  • “Fragmented” concepts like “tier-X” items are well intentioned but are entire “tac on” inside POE2 that have: zero introduction to players, no KBs, and generally not visually represented at all after the item is identified (I think). This entire system needs retooled, or consumed and made relevant - or simplified / and icon-’d on items for any type of relevance after identification.

Some Fix Angles:

  • Crafting needs to take more cues from either crafting enablement “window-fication” to make it more approachable; and consider - give EVERYONE a crafting panel that helps this process, and don’t monetize it - as it may well help the entire game by making it more approachable.
    1. You can obviously still retain all the other “logistics” tabs for helping organize and the like, but seriously consider why you wouldn’t want to have a RECIPE or learning aide that helps people consume, participate and or engage in crafting in the game without having to rely on videos online to even approach it.
  • Non-tradeable luck stacks? - should we explore a concept where the solo-self-found and or “non-trading” route is encouraged for a playstyle? Where your loot grind could start giving you some either entry paths to more deterministic crafting or luck stack generation minimally? (ala a return of some “type” of POE1 crafting or POE1 Sacred Grove crafting methods). These would be some type of “sink” mechanic that a person either tosses gear, or craftable currency/other-X into to generate and give them a mini-game to play, attempt. (Similar to how recombination is not a valid mechanism for anyone to really use at this time except (maybe) some crazy high end only crafters).

Admittedly I don’t quite know what would fix this, but as POE2 EA is still improving - and skill design needs loads of work; we also need to focus on base elements like modernizing crafting. Perhaps looking at Last Epoch and others for inspiration that maybe didn’t start out in POE. We need to think that not-every system should be a new sellable stash tab, and it may make the game a better game by that fundamental truth.
