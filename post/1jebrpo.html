PSA: Console players, fix for broken loot filter!

Since the last hotfix my loot filters have been not working properly. I had ‘followed’ a few of them and they worked before but slowly they started to all just break, until not a single one of the first 3 pages would operate regardless of strictness.

The fix is simple… just close the game, open a new game and let it load in, then quit that game back to console home page. After doing these steps you can open PoE2 and it should just work, but you may have to go to options and unfollow/refollow the applicable filters.

I guess there is some sort of cache data that is not cleared if you don’t ever actually open new games, because I have for sure closed the app but since a few weeks I haven’t played anything but PoE2.
