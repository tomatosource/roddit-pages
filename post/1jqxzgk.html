Early game minion life needs to be fixed

I love minions, they’re my favorite play style; and SUPER popular among the general player base as well. I played a lot of minion builds in poe1, and decided to league start a minion infernalist on PoE2 launch and I just gotta say the experience was MISERABLE.

Even investing the first 20-ish levels into minion life they are still constantly oneshot by even boss basic attacks. I literally would not have minions for 90% of some fights. It only started to feel playable in the mid-30’s when I found out about SRS and that did all of the heavy lifting. In the new patch notes there are adjustments to minion life scaling, which aim to bring down the higher level minion health values(im assuming because of DD and Corpsewade shenanigans). I think the early game values should be rebalanced as well, even doubling their hp wouldn’t be enough with how frail they are. But one of the worst things was how minion respawning works, where if another minion dies it delays ALL of your minions from respawning and resets the countdown timer, I’m hoping will be fixed this next league.

I’ve had friends either quit or get very close to quitting because the leveling experience for such a popular archetype is terribly balanced. With all the QoL adjustments they feel pretty good after lvl 50-60 or so. Also I’m sad to see no changes to Zombies, they are terribly clunky and not worth the high APM it needs to have an army summoned.
