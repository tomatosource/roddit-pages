The ancestral warrior totem nerf feels like a bait and switch

When 0.3 was announced, they added a requirement of 3 endurance charges to use ancestral warrior totems, and the justification was that now they had no totem limit, so the idea was that you could summon a massive totem army if you could generate charges fast enough.

Except in 0.31, they hard capped the # of ancestral warrior totems to 10, which heavily nerfed the single target of builds that invested heavily into charge generation to make it work. They also did this without any buffs to the skill itself.

So now they still require a lot of investment in charge generation, but cap out pretty early. Did they even mention why they did this? Was it too much server load? Did they think it was too OP? ‘Cause I’m pretty sure it was still nowhere near as strong as the top meta builds, and it feels like they basically halved the single target damage of a mid tier build for no apparent reason.
