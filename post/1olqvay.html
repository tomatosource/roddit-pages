With the first game of the 2025 season being played in Tokyo on March 18 and the final World Series game being played on November 1 in Toronto, this appears to be both the first season that has started and ended outside the United States and also the longest season in MLB history.

The Blue Jays’ last World Series appearance was in 1993, and according to this article the MLB started doing regular season games in countries other than the US and Canada in 1996.

Baseball Almanac’s “History of Opening Day” article links to lists of each team’s opening day schedules over time.

This article from 2022 says there were 9 World Series played in November, though that obviously didn’t include the 2022 or 2023 ones. The first World Series in November was in 2001, and all the following ones were in years where the Opening Day was in April.
