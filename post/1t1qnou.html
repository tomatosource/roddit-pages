How I would make Azmeri Whisp work if I was a Dev at GGG

1- The Mechanic:

Upon encountering a Wisp, it possesses you giving a random buff alongside a movement speed one that lets you run through the map like the wind for period of time.

Once reaching an Elite monster and killing it, the Wisp detaches from you and possess the Elite Body to resurrect him into a Spirit empowered version, providing you with Azmeri loot chance upon killing him this second time.

2- The Atlas tree:

A- Generics:

A.1- Chances of finding Wisps/More Wisps count

A.2- Enhanced buffs from Wisp/ Buff type choices

B- Synergies with other leagues:

B.1- Upon encountering a Ritual circle, the Wisps leaves your body to possess the Ritual, making the Ritual Altar glow from Spirit force, running that Ritual will have Azmeri Spirits possessed one coming out.

Same for Breach, the Hand will glow, if you run it, you will have breached Azmeri possessed monsters.

B.2- Atlas trees: gives you the choice to block a league so the Wisps doesn’t leave to possess a Riutal for instance if you prefer doing Breach.
