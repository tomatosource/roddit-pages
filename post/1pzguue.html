Omen of Rarity maps having guaranteed Chilled Ground is actually criminal

When players reach endgame (and eventually everyone will), sooner or later they’ll need to use Omen of Chaotic Rarity on their Waystones to get 100%+ Item Rarity on their maps. Unfortunately, the Chilled Ground effect also grants Item Rarity, which means it is virtually guaranteed that these high-end, juiced maps will have Chilled Ground on them. It is actually CRIMINAL to force us to deal with that mind-blowingly awful effect.

In my opinion, ARPGs should be all about rampaging through hundreds of mobs, looting nice stuff, and feeling good while doing it. Chilled Ground completely kills this feeling. One moment you’re rampaging through mobs, the next moment your character switches into grandpa mode because you stepped into a pool of ice. Abyss Pit spawns 5+ Rares, but you’re standing in the ice pool? Good luck spamming your combo to kill them. Also, good luck on seeing these pools, because: 1.: It’s impossible to see them through the 10+ different layers of effects, and 2.: They’re EVERYWHERE. There is basically no point in trying to “dodge” them because there are so many of them, your characters hitbox will end up standing in one or two no matter where you go.

I believe GGG did a great job revamping the Temporal Chains effect. It is still pretty annoying if it hits us, but we have the chance to dodge it, and if we are experienced enough we can go through a whole map without getting hit by these Chains. If they are capable to understand the players’ perspective regarding these annoying debuffs, they should look into the Chilled Ground as well ASAP, because it’s not an “added difficulty”, it’s just an annoyance that makes their game feel like crap to play. The only difficulty it adds is how hard it becomes to find the motivation to keep playing when all of my maps have it.
