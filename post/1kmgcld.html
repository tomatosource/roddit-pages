In order to confirm my suspicion that I had no characters I attempted to make a new one with the same name as my old character and it went right through. In hind sight that was probably stupid but it worked out.

At that point I noticed my gold count was the same so I played thru til clearfell and found my stash intact. After exiting to character select screen I saw there was only one character available. At this point I just am kind of bewildered I guess because wtf happened… But I email support even though it’s 1 am NZT.

I asked support if there was any odd information or what could have happened and they said they didn’t see anything out of the ordinary and didn’t know what happened… Which again I found odd.

So just know if you login to find your characters deleted I guess just email support.
