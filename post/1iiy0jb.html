After the Brandon Ingram trade, every player & pick from the Anthony Davis is no longer on the New Orleans Pelicans. Essentially, they turned Anthony Davis into Dejounte Murray, Bruce Brown, Kelly Olynyk, 2026 Pacers 1st & 2031 Raptors 2nd.

For reference Lonzo Ball, Brandon Ingram, Josh Hart, De’Andre Hunter and 2 future 1sts were traded by the Lakers to the New Orleans Pelicans. What happened them? Well…

  • Lonzo Ball - Sign and traded to the Chicago Bulls in 2021
  • Brandon Ingram - Traded to the Toronto Raptors in 2025
  • Josh Hart - Traded to the Pelicans in the CJ McCollum deal in 2022
  • De’Andre Hunter - Traded in the same draft for Jaxson Hayes, Nickeil Alexander Walker, and Didi Louzada
    • None of those 3 are on the Pelicans any more
  • 2022 1st - Became Dyson Daniels, moved for Dejounte Murray
  • 2024⁄25 1st - Pick was deferred from 2024 to 2025, traded to the Hawks for Dejounte Murray
    • Quick note: Deferring that pick from 2024 to 2025 allowed the Lakers to draft Dalton Knecht 17th. He was used in the Mark Williams trade with the Charlotte Hornets.
