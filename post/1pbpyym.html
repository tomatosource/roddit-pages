Cast on X gems - Power needs adjustments

Trigger gems which consider the power of enemies (cast on crit, cast on elemental ailment) need an adjustment on power scaling. I am not sure if this is widely known but it is rather off at the moment. And I would argue in a direction which is not about whether it should be buffed or nerfed.

Atm, if you build a trigger build (especially in campaign, because later everything evaporates anyway), you struggle against a few white mobs and then you get to the boss and it gets absolutely obliterated.

I honestly think there are just numbers way off considering ailment threshold (which cast on crit is also based on). With a high increased shock chance and 1000% more chance to shock (Ball lightning + shock support) you can still kill white mobs without shocking them. But uniques are very easy to shock because they have a lower ailment threshold than their HP and then they generate much more power.

The end result is, when you play a build with its damage focused on triggered spells you struggle against easy mobs and harder enemies die instantly. Which both feels bad for me.

Is that a concern which the community shares? Have you had a different experience?
