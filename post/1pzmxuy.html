Hey exilew, I know the mood around this league mechanic been kind of a rollercoaster.

We started off with everyone abandoning the temple because of the rewards (yep, they sucked real), then all the temple-access shenanigans with the Holten bug. But credit where it’s due: GGG actually listened and made some solid tweaks.

They bumped up the crystal drop rates so it feels way less grindy to get into the temple now, and honestly, the whole loop just feels a lot better balanced. Sure, some people on Reddit are still grumbling, when are we not?, but I think they’ve actually hit a pretty good sweet spot now between making the league’s mechanics rewarding and not forcing weird workarounds.

That said, it’s probably now a bit too late to fully fix the economy since all that bug abusing did mess things up a lot.

But honestly, this change is a really great move for the longterm enjoyment of the game. I’m actually thinking the Vaal temple mechanic might end up being my favorite for a few leagues to come.
