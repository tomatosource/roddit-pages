Meaningful dropped loot is bad

Whether you like it or not, and whether GGG likes it or not, almost no items that drops will ever be of use to you or your character. What we all do is to pickup the ‘good’ stuff and then sell it, we get ex or div, and we go buy the right item for our character

With so many item types, so many stats, so many builds, so many skills the statistical chance of you getting something that is good for your build, and then getting mods that are actually good is impossibly small

In POE1 the same is true, but due to the craft bench and reasonable abundance of craft materials, we can pickup an OK item and make it usable, or pickup a half decent item and make it fit in with our other items. So we tend to use a lot more of the dropped items in POE1

As POE2 doesn’t have crafting to speak of it doesn’t make sense to waste a few exalts on an “OK” item to gamble for an extra OK mod or two - it’s better to spend that 2ex on trade and buy a pretty good item already matching our needs, or even just save that 2ex a few times and buy a good item

Nothing GGG can ever do (short of removing trade or adding proper crafting) can ever change this, and every attempt they make to “make ground loot meaningful” is doomed to fail - and every time they put blocks in our way to try and make us use ground loot they just annoy more and more players

We have a mode for players that love ground loot - it’s called SSF

You can even play in trade and choose not to buy items but use currency exchange if you like that challenge

But for the rest of us, saying “we want ground loot to matter” just means more slog and grind for zero “fun” reason
