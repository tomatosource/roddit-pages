Completing zones faster and faster is part of the power fantasy.

I have seen some people confused why there is any dislike of larger zones because theoretically if they are packed with mobs and loot it is the same as doing multiple smaller zones.

To me the issue is two fold.

#1 - There are certain rewards that are only a limited number of times per zone such as a boss or league mechanics. Which means that more zones mean more content. In 0.1 people hated having to rerun a map after dying and it losing all that extra content for this reason. Rewards also exist for completing waystones by being able to get skill points to get better rewards. If maps take longer rewards feel slower.

#2 - The completing of the maps quicker and quicker feels good just like DPS going up. In PoE we aren’t given visual damage indicators while blasting through a regular map but you can time it. You can feel your character getting better by how quickly they finish maps. Maps that are long or with dead ends really damage that feeling of progression. Adding more mobs to slow down maps instead of shrinking maps will feel like a nerf in practice.
