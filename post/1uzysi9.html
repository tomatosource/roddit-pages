[OC] The state of every NBA team’s local broadcasting rights

I saw post on here yesterday about the Hawk’s moving their games to a local antenna broadcast which inspired me to dive deeper into this. I don’t watch every NBA team in their local market obviously so some of this may be inaccurate and deals that seem good or okay may actually be worse. I did all this research while bored at work and may have made some mistakes when looking over things, but I did do this myself and not with AI so those mistakes are of the human variety. I also did not factor in bundled extra teams with some of these platforms and am just putting in the perspective of someone who would just want the cheapest and easiest way to watch their local NBA team.

S Tier: Antenna + Streaming service

Antenna TV in your local market is free, high quality, and probably how most fans would prefer to watch. A streaming platform is a nice option for super committed fans who may want to watch on the go or on any assortment of devices that don’t play well with an antenna. Mavs get top honors for offering an insanely cheap streaming platform.

  • Dallas Mavericks, KMPX or MavsTV ($14.99/season)
  • Portland Trailblazers, KATU KUNP or BlazerVision ($19.99/month)
  • New Orleans WVUE 7 or Pelicans+ (99.99/season)
  • Phoenix Suns, AZFamily 3 or Suns+ (109.99/year)
  • Utah Jazz, KJZZ 14 or Jazz+ ($124.99/season)

A Tier: Over-the-air antenna broadcasts

The gold standard and what every team should do considering how monetized every possible inch of the NBA is. These games are free to anyone within range and with a TV antenna.

  • Atlanta Hawks, PeachtreeTV WANF
  • Milwaukee Bucks, MY24
  • Detroit Pistons, WMYD TV 20
  • Miami Heat, WPLG 10

B Tier: Standalone streaming platform + Antenna TV Hybrid

While offering every single non-national TV game for free is preferred these teams at least gave fans a few games for free before putting the rest behind a paywalled streaming service.

FDSN, or FanDuel SportsNet, is something you are going to see a lot of here and in the next tier. They were a RSN that operated a large number of cable stations and sold those regional stations through a standalone app. They went bankrupt earlier this year and while they managed to finish out the season every team on FDSN last year will need to find a new home for 2026-2027 source. As of writing only a handful of teams (Pistons, Bucks, Hawks) have announced their new plans. For completeness I put their old FDSN deals on this list but these are all subject to change.

All three of these are $19.99/month

  • LA Clippers, FDSN or 10 games free on KTLA
  • Orando Magic, FDSN or 10 games free on WESH and CW18
  • Oklahoma City Thunder, FDSN or 9 games free on KWTV
  • Memphis Grizzlies FDSN or 7 free on WMC-TV

C Tier: Standalone streaming services

Like the above but with no free games. All are $19.99 a month except the New York teams and the Raptors

  • Washington Wizards, Monumental Sports+
  • Chicago Bulls, CHSN “Bulls Only” plan
  • Denver Nuggets, Altitude+
  • Charlotte Hornets, FDSN
  • Indiana Pacers, FDSN
  • Cleveland Cavaliers, FDSN
  • Minnesota Timberwolves, FDSN
  • San Antonio Spurs, FDSN
  • LA Lakers, Spectrum SportsNet+
  • Brooklyn Nets, YES via Gotham Sports App ($209.99/season)
  • New York Knicks, MSG+ via Gotham Sports App ($279.99/season)
  • Toronto Raptors, Sportsnet.ca+ ($29.99 CAD/month, Raptors also have Canadian national TV games on TSN not included in this)

D Tier: NBC Sports + Peacock

NBC Sports RSN is available through cable or as an add on for peacock, which is a much cheaper option although still a lot more than standalone. Peacock + the NBC Sports add on for your region costs $35/month

  • Boston Celtics, NBC Sports Boston
  • Philadelphia 76ers, NBC Sports Philadelphia
  • Golden State Warriors, NBC Sports Bay Area
  • Sacramento Kings, NBC Sports California

F Tier: Cable TV RSN

To my knowledge, only one team is truly left in the dark ages of having to pay for a cable package to watch your team.

  • Houston Rockets, Space City Home Network. Cheapest option appears to be FuboTV Sports at $55.99/month

Let me know what I got wrong here and I’ll try and update it.

EDITS: Formatting fixes, Blazers pricing fixed, OKC moved up a tier for having 9 OTA games.
