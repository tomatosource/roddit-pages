Anfernee Simons

Just wanted to make a discussion regarding Simons and how people feel about him after his preseason debut. Throughout the whole offseason, there seemed to be the general idea that Simons will come off the bench and I was just wondering if the majority of the fans here still think that. Just a friendly discussion among fans.

I’ll start. I do believe Simons has to be a starter. Since he got traded to the celtics, they have been openly trying to shop him so wouldn’t it be in the team’s best interest to drive that trade value up to get the best return for Simons? Having him off the bench limits his value as a trading piece, to truly optimize Simons’ trade value he needs to be viewed as a starter by the teams trading for him.

Production wise, I do think he’ll be productive either coming off the bench or as a starter. Simons was the first option in Portland since Dame left, so he was guarded by the other team’s best defender every game and still averaged 20/4/3. There is no reason to think he wouldn’t be able to replicate that (or even exceed it) EVEN if he played off the bench since he will be guarded by the other team’s bench unit instead of starters. It’s also important to consider we don’t have Jrue, Porzingis, Horford, and Tatum (for the majority of the year, maybe) so there is undeniably a whole lot of usage to share across the team and of course Simons will benefit off of that as well. If he starts, he may not have that same usage as he did in Portland, but I do believe he will still be quite productive and I still think he can average 20/4/3. I always thought that Simons is a better player when he’s not the main defensive focus, which is true as his shooting efficiency was the highest when he was playing behind Dame. Now think about that and realize that he also has very capable playmakers and passers in D White and JB on the starting unit who will also be higher on the other team’s defensive priority than Simons. I think the game will open up more to him as the team’s third offensive weapon and that D White and JB will get him the most open looks he’s ever got in his career, he’s a career 38% shooter from 3 and he’s definitely skilled and talented so I’m sure he can make the most out of the gravity that JB and D White give him that he hasn’t had since Dame left Portland.

This is just my opinion, I’m open to discuss but please be kind.
