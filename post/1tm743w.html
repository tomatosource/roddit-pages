Fire spells feel incredibly clunky and awkward to use

So I decided to give POE2 another shot after waiting a year after release to see if they ironed some of the kinks out, and boy howdy is Fire in a bad place. I’m really trying to make it work but between Ember Fusillade’s inconsistent aiming + having to slow to a crawl to build up a volley, combined with basically having to detonate that living bomb and hoping you click the right creature with the bomb on it with your auto attack before the Fusillade goes out, so that Fireball is empowered with ember shards and isn’t completely useless and can actually clear a room.

All of it just feels contrived, extremely dangerous in this kind of game, and just overall not very satisfying. Incinerate in particular was a huge letdown when I reached Act 3. It just takes so long to ramp up the damage, and you’re standing there completely exposed as a caster in order to use it. Meanwhile if the boss is melee you’re basically facetanking whatever he’s putting out unless that boss happens to have a 3-second grace period which certainly isn’t all of them, especially Rares. All of this whilst dealing almost no damage most of the time and still getting screwed by the Fusillade aiming issues.

And I guess in a broader sense, one thing I don’t like about POE2 compared to POE1 is that you can’t really build around one or two spells in particular. With Fire at least, you’re forced into one or two valid playstyles involving 70% of the fire-based abilities in these pre-defined rotations. These spells are balanced in order to function together as a set and are fairly useless in isolation, which kinda limits the extent to which you can specialize your build into any one thing. Channeling spell nodes for instance aren’t going to help you clear faster with Fireball, or scale Living Bomb’s damage, yet in order to activate Incinerate, you have to spend mana on these other skills in order to use it.

Also this is a minor gripe but another issue with Incincerate is that if you go Blood Mage and you take the nodes which convert mana cost into increased Life cost + damage, you actually have a harder time charging Incincerate since it needs mana specifically. Yes, there is built-in negative synergy with a core damaging spell for fire-based blood mage ascendents.

It’s disappointing because back in WoW I always played either a fire mage or a destruction warlock and I loved the power fantasy of just blowing everything up with raw carnage. You don’t really get that with PoE2’s fire elemental spells. I feel like I need to be “clever” and deal “chip” damage to whittle my enemies down while dodging things like a ninja, which is anachronistic to how most people view an inferno-summoning pyromaniac.

Hopefully they’ll decide to rework it at some point, but for now I guess I’ll have to find a different class to play.
