I don’t need MORE stash tabs. I need the stash tabs I HAVE to be better.

So, a wishlist for stash tabs.

Map tabs - I want to copy my tab names/colors to other tabs. In fact, just have a checkbox I have to uncheck myself in order to handle one of the tabs independently. Nobody wants to have to manually adjust 80 tabs just to make their map organization uniform.

Also, put the number indicator over each sub-tab, so I don’t have to shuffle through each one to figure out what’s where.

Please brighten up the selected subsection of map tabs so it’s clearer what section you’re looking at.

This would have been a perfect section to squirrel Precursor Tablets away in, too.

I upgraded normal tabs to premium, but can I please upgrade premiums to quads?

I want enough slots to fit runes and catalysts in the currency tab, and the option to give them an affinity for the currency tab. Soul cores too, while we’re at it. It’s no use setting the currency tab up as a workshop otherwise - the point of a workshop is that everything you’ll need is already at hand there.

Sorts. By item name (not including the prefix,), by type, by rarity, by gold value, modified first (rune slots, quality), by waystone drop chance on waystones, by number of affixes, by damage or armor values. And just to close up a crowded inventory without gaps (remove and replace all items, from largest to smallest, and you should be 90% of the way there.)

And the ability to set some tabs to auto-sort upon opening them.

For example, I would like my waystone stash to auto-sort by number of affixes. That’ll put the most potent stuff at the top automatically, even after I move things in and out of the tab, and I can use the sub-groups for more important things, like which ones have rarity, which ones have high waystone chance mods, and which are safe (6-prefix or safe suffixes)/hazardous (use on maps without special mods to farm waystones with less risk to losing rifts, etc). Things like my jewelry tab, for trying recrafts of potentially useful rings, amulets, and belts, I’d sort by the name of the item, to group them together so I could tell when I had three of anything.

The junk tab I keep for breachstones, expedition artifacts, runes, catalysts, etc would also benefit from sorting, for obvious reasons.

All stuff I’ve wished for at some point, all purely convenience, all things I’d pay a reasonable cost for.
