Ground Loot Equipment Doesn’t Feel Worth Looting Anymore

With the introduction of:

  • 40% quality
  • Alloys
  • Special mods from socketed runes such as “Marksman,” etc.
  • Special equipment from Breach
  • Breach-exclusive mods/jewelry
  • And many other crafting methods

The endgame has become almost entirely focused on crafting, even jewels.

I don’t think i identify an amazing piece of equipment from ground loot anymore and have it actually be worth something, like in previous leagues.

Suggestion

Why not add exclusive mods that can only be obtained by identifying items found on the ground?

This would make loot drops exciting again for players who actively play the game and pick up items. Crafters likely wouldn’t mind, since they can fracture almost anything anyway.
