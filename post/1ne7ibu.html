Kawhi played 26% of games pre-payment week in 2022, and 82% post-payment by Aspire. [repost with source]

https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/l/leonaka01/gamelog/2023/

Pablo Torre explained how Kawhi was paid his endorsement from Aspiration late in 3Q22. The payment was supposed to arrive in October/Nov and wasn’t paid until December 15th, after a Clippers owner invested \(2MM to pay Kawhi \)1.75MM. Going into the game logs for the 2022 season, you can see that Kawhi played 7 of 27 games before that December week (26%). During and after that week, when payment was finally arranged, Kawhi played in 45 of the remaining 55 games of the season (82%). I would theorize that his playing for the Clippers may have been tied into his endorsement deal, or else his injury healed exactly at the same time.
