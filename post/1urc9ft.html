JB and PG difference

Love JB. Don’t like the return. He’s obviously a better overall player. That said, I think we shouldn’t compare them 1 on 1 to understand the bigger picture of the new JT-led squad.

Set aside the basic box score stats, skills, age, 54M, injury history for a minute…

Based on last season, I think the major difference is JB’s franchise-high 36% usage rate vs. PG’s 23%. What we will miss in individual output will now be spread among PG aaaand everybody else.

PG is way more comfortable in a complementary role at this stage in his career. He has a better assist-turnover ratio as well as a result of his lower usage. I think we should view his role as part of a collective.

We already had a glimpse of last year’s fun and scrappy young depth. Now, they will have an offseason with added experience and a more defined role. They will next-man-up the hell outta next season.

When Brad took over, he emphasized building a contender around the Jays. It has successfully produced Banner 18! That time has come to an end. We are now in Season 19 with a new cast, and we will build a new contender around JT 💪☘️
