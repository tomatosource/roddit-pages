Opinion - Cast on Crit, Cast on Elemental, are too powerful

Just curious what the general opinion is on these spirit skills.

I have several builds all plenty capable of juiced up endgame content with full parties..but COC/COE are simply too easy to build around and essentially exploit. It turns entire builds into 1 or 2 button presses and theres hardly any build that doesnt benefit from using them.

Of course i know people will scream not to nerf them and to upscale other skills, but imo they are flawed in their design to begin with. There’s almost no way to make them functional without being overpowered, or decrease their power without making them useless. But lets be real, a single button press results in 20 meteors instantly lol.

Idk- it just feels too easy. Spirit skills imo are meant to supplement your build, not define it.
