PSA: Trinity can never reach maximum resonance

New patch, same issues, hoping to bring this to the attention before we get the patch notes so these get addressed.

As the title says, people often look at trinity and decide that if you work hard enough, with enough V I S I O N applied to your damage rotation of doing: cold, fire, lightning, etc. You can get to the magical 300 resonance number, thus getting the bonus of 10* more elemental damage.

However, each hit with a type reduces the other types, so you have the following resonances:

  1. 100 fire, 93 cold, 93 lightning –&gt; Now do a cold attack
  2. 97 fire, 100 cold, 90 lightning –&gt; Now do a lightning attack
  3. 94 fire, 97 cold, 100 lightning –&gt; Now do a fire attack
  4. 100 fire, 94 cold, 97 lightning –&gt; etc.

In a perfect world, you can have 100+97+94 resonance = 291

TBH, in its current implementation, trinity needs a full rework, but before that, if /u/Belakay_ggg/ any other ggg’ers could just raise the cap to 112 that would at least allow us to go up to 327, and not require a perfect rotation.

However, IMHO it should get a soft rework so that you only lose resonance of a certain type if you haven’t dealt damage with a skill where that is the primary damage factor for X seconds.

Compared to poe1 this still requires more investment as you’d need to still rotate 3 damage types regularly, but at least you don’t have to do it on a perfect rotation where you rotate them all picture perfect.

Otherwise this feels pretty crap for 100 spirit tbh.
