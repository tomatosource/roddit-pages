Is POE2 supposed to compile shaders every single zone change through campaign?

Title. I have a good rig, game runs well, but every single time I enter a zone it compiles shaders for 10-15 secs and blasts my CPU to 80c which sets off my rocket ship 100% CPU fan setting in my otherwise silent rig, so it’s a bit annoying. No other game I play compiles shaders like this. I would think it would compile on first load up and maybe once in a while but it’s start up and every single zone entrance. I don’t remember it doing this 8 months ago.

Running a 9800X3D on Vulkan. Edit and a 9070xt

Answer edit: It’s just how POE2 does shaders, as needed. Because I have a higher end multi thread CPU with a fairly high TDP, POE2 uses all available resources (threads and TDP) from the CPU, thus generating a lot of heat quickly. CPU heatsink/fan did its job well, it was just louder than I wanted. I didn’t mess with shader settings. Made more sense to adjust CPU settings to what I use it for.

Enabled ECO mode (lowers TDP from 120 to 105) and set -15 curve optimizer all cores (bios-&gt;advanced settings-&gt;PBO-&gt;Curve optimizer), tested stability with aida. ~14c lower on stress tests and during POE2 shader compiling, absolutely zero noticable real world performance loss from a few hours of play, nor does compiling take any more time, and quiet fans (!). Thanks all.

If you have a modern CPU, Would recommend the TDP limit (if you don’t need the max multi thread performance like in commercial video editing) and simple small undervolt negative 10-15 in the CO in bios (do not adjust clock speeds like YouTubers suggest, that’s really just a PITA for marginal gains with more work and risk for people who enjoy crashing their system repeatedly to see where they landed in the silicon lottery) and stability test with aida64 for 2-6 hours. Don’t skip the stability test. Neg 10-15 with no clock adjustments should work for almost all modern CPUs, but almost is not all. Lower it from -15 to -10 or -10 to -5 if it crashes and retest.

The bios tweaks took literally 30 seconds once I found them.

Oh and while you’re there, make sure expo/xmp is enabled so you actually get advertised timings and speeds out of your RAM.
