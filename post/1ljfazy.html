[Danielle Lerner] After giving up the 10th and 59th picks in the Kevin Durant trade, the Rockets are exploring trading back into this week’s NBA draft, sources tell.

https://www.houstonchronicle.com/sports/rockets/article/houston-nba-draft-intel-watch-20383440.php

The Rockets are exploring options for trading to get back into the draft in the scenario that a player they really like slides down and there is an opportunity to acquire the selection at a low enough price. Sources tell the Houston Chronicle that the Rockets have identified about five prospects, mostly projected first-round picks, who could fit that criteria. If that happens, the player drafted is likely to be a high-upside depth piece on a Houston roster set to return most of its playoff rotation.
