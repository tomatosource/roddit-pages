Taylor Swift Reportedly to Attend Knicks’ NBA Finals G4 vs. Spurs at MSG

Taylor Swift is reportedly joining Madison Square Garden’s celebrity row for Game 4 of the 2026 NBA Finals.
Swift will be attendance to watch the New York Knicks and San Antonio Spurs play Wednesday night, Page Six’s Riley Cardoza reported.
The singer-songwriter is attending the game “with friends,” a source told Cardoza.
