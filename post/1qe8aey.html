Why do “treat enemy elemental resistances as inverted” effects feel much weaker than they sound?

Please tell me which of these premises is wrong. ​What I assumed:

  1. Let’s say with all buffs, passives and Co., my character deals 100% of its damage as cold damage, i.e. 100% cold dps. None of it is physical, all of it is elemental (cold) damage.

  2. enemies have up to 75% fire/cold/lightning resistance each, depending on the enemy type. For simplicity sake, let’s say it’s 75% resistance against cold damage.

  3. unless explicitly stated otherwise, enemies don’t have other defence factors that apply in the damage calculation.

  4. the damage I deal to them without inversion is (100% - 75% enemy cold resistance) = 25% damage.

  5. inverted elemental resistance multiplies the 75% enemy cold resistance with (-1), “putting a minus infront of it”.

  6. ​the damage I deal to them WITH inversion is (100% - (-75%)) = 175% damage.

  7. 175 / 25 = 7. I *should* have been dealing 7 times the damage (600% MORE damage!) against an enemy whose cold resistance got treated as inverted compared to it not being converted.

  8. what happens in reality: maybe 30% more damage compared to the non-inverted situation.

And it really surprises me because I genuinely don’t know which of those premises is wrong. ​Is there an additional calculation factor enemies have? ​Specifically, are premises 4 and/or 6 wrong?
