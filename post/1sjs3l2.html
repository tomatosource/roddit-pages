I made my own MVP Tracker based on voting history

Basketball Reference has something called the MVP Tracker that, as opposed to the subjective KIA Ladder, gives probability to players’ chancing of winning the award based on their season averages and team win%, using the players who have won in previous years for precedent. I like the idea of this, though I don’t think it’s as well done as it could be. So I decided to make my own using the same idea. The idea wasn’t “who deserved MVP,” but: given what voters have historically valued, who were they most likely to pick?

  • I measured players in 3 categories: Box score, Advanced Stats, Team Win%
  • I looked at the top 5 vote-receivers for each of the last 45 years (when the NBA switched to a media vote)
  • I adjusted the model until its margin of error in predicting ranking and vote share was as low as I could get it (to a reasonable degree, this is mostly casual), and got it much lower than Basketball Reference’s.
  • I also valued team win% less to those who are not the first option on their team.

Here’s the results for 2026, with some ineligible players included:

Well, since my intention was to reflect the voting body, I definitely think I did a better job since I don’t have Jokić at 67% like Basketball Reference does.
