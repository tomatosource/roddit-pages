I was at the Raptors and Spurs game yesterday… couldn’t help but feel like there were TOO many bells and whistles

This isn’t my first NBA game, my first was during the 18-19 season, I honesty don’t remember if it was like this then, or if I wasn’t paying attention then, but being at the game yesterday, I couldn’t help but feel as though the arena relied too much on distractions, bells and whistles, etc

First of all, do we need an instrumental loop playing in every single possession? And it’s not like the music was there to add to the game. The PA didn’t try to push for “defense” chants or cheers, it was just music during offensive possessions, and the crowd carried the “defense” chants - which got hilariously worse as the game went by given the blow out.

To add to this, we can never let a time out or dead possession just be, and on the jumbotron we never see close ups of the teams, coaches, etc - its fan cam all the time. The mascot, cheerleaders and so on are dancing on the middle of the court, and we just get a fancam every time there is a dead ball. It happening every once in a while is fine, but at the end of every possession? I mean… when all you do is fans noticing themselves on camera, and then Snapchat filters multiple times… maybe tone it down a little?

And then I feel like there is no real connection with the tone of the game and what the PA/mascot/dance crew is doing. They’re basically two different entities existing on the same arena entirely separate from each other. This got especially hilarious when the raptors were down 30, and the “time for the 4th quarter” graphics with the “make some noise” meter comes in like we’re entering a death match of some kind. We’re down 30 in the 4th quarter, the scary cinematic “4th quarter” act does not make sense here.

Like I said, I don’t remember if it was like this before, cause while the basketball itself was fun (the spurs were fun to watch this game, the raps seemed to not know how to set screens), the bells and whistles and overall ‘distraction’ part felt extremely excessive and unnecessary. Like I don’t mind it existing to some extent, to be clear, but it felt both very by the books and excessive at the same time. Like they were following a script saying “remind people you’re fun and energetic. Do these steps” and it kept just insisting upon itself, for lack of a better term.
