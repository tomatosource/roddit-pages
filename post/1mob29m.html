Who are your predictions for Netflix’s Starting 5 Season 3? Who would you like to see?

The casting has been pretty good for the past two seasons. For reference, it was

  • Season 1: LeBron, Sabonis, Tatum, Jimmy Butler, Anthony Edwards

  • Season 2: SGA, Haliburton, James Harden, KD, Jaylen Brown

Here are who I think they would choose:

  • JDub: Second best player on the reigning champs’ and favorites, similar to the Brown pick in S2
  • Brunson: reached ECF on one of the projected top two teams in the East this year, New York
  • Giannis: One of the best players still in the league in his prime, probably an amazing interviewee in general, walking viral clip machine, also he probably has another kid coming along?
  • Steph: Obvious living legend, American, approaching tail end of his career (similar to the LeBron/KD picks)
  • Luka: Revenge tour season, skinny Luka narratives, Lakers

Steph and Giannis I think are obvious candidates. But the fact that they haven’t appeared yet does make me wonder if they have simply refused to appear in the past two seasons.

I left Jokic off the list because he doesn’t seem the type to want to appear in something like this. He didn’t do that many interviews in Court of Gold iirc even though Serbia was one of the teams they tailed.

I think Wemby should also be up there but I’m not sure who to take out.

Who I would like to see

  • Donovan Mitchell: mostly because of my flair, but also still currently the best player on a projected top two team in the East. He’s also more media-friendly than Mobley. (Plus he and CoCo Jones might have their wedding in the near future, just saying.)
  • Klay Thompson: mostly for Megan Thee Stallion tbh
  • Damian Lillard: Netflix would absolutely never do this, but I think the recovery and return to Portland would be such great stories. Portland can also have a very interesting season as a young team in the west.
  • Myles Turner: the Judas narrative
  • Cooper Flagg: would be interesting to just see a rookie’s perspective to be honest, and especially in the most volatile workplace in the league recently.
  • Trae Young: interesting Hawks team, every story needs a good heel, seems like a good interview.

I’m sure I’m missing a lot of players so feel free to add your own.
