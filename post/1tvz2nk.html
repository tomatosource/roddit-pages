One narrative I haven’t seen discussed: The Knicks have the chance to win the NBA’s first “double”

The NBA lifted its idea for the Emirates Cup from soccer’s domestic in-season tournaments. When a soccer team wins two competitions in the same season, it’s called a “double”– if they win three, it’s a “treble”. The Knicks have the chance to be the first team in NBA history to win both the Emirates Cup and the NBA Finals.
