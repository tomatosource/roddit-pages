Giant’s Blood is in a good spot.

The hate is really unwarranted. The strength requirement is very easy to satisfy now with little to no investement. Even when using the best 2-handed mace type in the game like Massive Greathammer, the strength requirement is only 489. And it can go down all the way to 315 with the reduced requirements mod, meaning that players can use it with Giant’s Blood with no investement at all- just choose weapon with the correct mod. Around 300 strength is what I assume high level Warrior players have anyways (I did).

Let’s assume that a 500 strength player chose the Giant’s Blood keystone. It would seem that they lose 500 HP, but… Shield’s can give over 200 HP with strength + maximum life increases. So a good shield neutralizes around half of the downside while also contributing to the strength requirement. Also around 30% block chance is enough to make the character ~40% more tanky in maps- which gets further increased by mods like life gained on block etc.

Not only that, shields now can also roll both “#% of armour also applies to elemental damage” and “#% of armour also applies to chaos damage” with Abyss crafting, both of which are insanely powerful mods.

And keep in mind that players don’t even have to use the best base type in order to make use out of this keystone. Bases like Heavy Greathammer with 312 strength requirement are still T15+ map viable.

I think that players care too much about what they lose by taking Giant’s Blood and don’t really value the upside of having a shield enough.

Warrior overall is in a very good spot endgame-wise and mine cost me only around 10 divines to get to a point where I’m never dying in T15+ maps, including Abysses. The only time I died recently was due to corrupting the boss from Silent Cave (don’t do it).
