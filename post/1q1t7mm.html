what i like about the temple

im still progressing my snake, and since im a casual dad im pretty much at the beginning. only 2 spymasters and like 10 rooms in total

- endgame progression (without stuff like Holten to speedrun it): every map i ffel like there is purpose. i want to run the temple again and try to enlarge my snake. Every room added matters and feels satysfying and you can feel the increase in rares/difficulty/loot

- way more endgame scaling: while those multipliers may look stupid, they finally show the flaw of base endgame mapping: missing scaling options. i now have a purpose to keep upgrading my char. While pinnacles are another reason, mapping besides the occasional overpowered whisped/essences/abyss/whatever mob, feels too easy and dull

- action! no fucking walking for ages with no mobs. each room already is filled with like 3-4 rares and someontimes a unique. i dont know how crazy this will get with more rooms, but damn, i love the multiple rares each room

- no fucking wasting my time with deadends! if build correctly, i can just follow my snake. no need to be perma “map gaming”. i can finally let the map in the top corner and enjoy the grafic

- player agency. i can more or less choose which rooms to add and how much i want my temple to be juiced (if RNG with room selection aligns…)

yes: there are many bad things with the temple. But i for myself like to concentrate on the good, not the bad. I hope at least some of the good aspects make it to 0.5, maybe they even take inspiration from the good things and integrate those aspects into the endgame revamp.
