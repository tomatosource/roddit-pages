[Stein & Fischer] sources say that the Cavaliers should be regarded as unlikely to factor into the trade chase for Antetokounmpo this summer

Source

While we’ve previously written about Cleveland’s exploratory trade interest in Milwaukee’s Giannis Antetokounmpo before the in-season trade deadline nearly four months ago, sources say that the Cavaliers should be regarded as unlikely to factor into the trade chase for Antetokounmpo this summer.

The skepticism alone about whether Antetokounmpo would want to extend in Cleveland beyond the one guaranteed season left on his current contract is one significant factor working against a swap headlined by Mobley heading to Milwaukee. How could the Cavaliers surrender valuable trade assets — even for a former two-time MVP — without assurances that Antetokounmpo would want to sign a contract extension?

Another obstacle for the Bucks: Mobley, at 24, is regarded in Cleveland as the sort of player who could offer a legitimate bridge to a successful post-Mitchell future. The Cavaliers, remembering how hard life was after James walked away from the franchise — twice — are obviously reluctant to surrender Mobley and the staying power he is projected to represent unless it’s a sure-thing trade.
