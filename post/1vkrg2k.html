This game will be huge for the genre when it goes F2P

I know people think about endgame when they think about PoE, but I want you to think about the campaign alone for a second.

The game offers a AAA-quality 20-30 hour (probably longer once we hit 1.0) crossplay multiplayer campaign with very fun boss fights. This will be accessible for completely free. Stash tabs are not necessary for the campaign.

The game’s systems are easier to get into compared to PoE1 and the gameplay is more action-based. There will eventually be 12 whole classes to try out and replay the campaign with.

The amount of people that will try out this game and discover a love for the aRPG genre will be enormous. Dungeon crawler aRPGs are normally seen as a nerdy niche genre, but this might push them into the mainstream.
