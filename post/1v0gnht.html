Still doubting the Ritual Farm? I gotchu fam

I have been farming Ritual almost exclusively for Omens over the past few days and have made thousands of div.

I’ve probably done around 200+ maps total, but I was getting bored and decided to start tracking every map individually. I know a sample size of 50 maps isn’t enough to be definitive, but it should at least give people an idea of the trend.

Setup:

  • City maps with Head of the King
  • Mostly ran 2x cheaper reroll / reroll x3 tablets, Omen Chance/Increased Tribute, and the Unique Ritual tablet (If you want to be real sweaty you can run cheaper reroll then swap it out if bounties have given you plenty)
  • Averaged around 20–34 rerolls per map with anywhere from 15-80k tribute
  • Jado - Partial Translations

Results from 50 tracked maps:

  • Each block of 10 maps cost around 90–105d
  • Average cost per map: ~10d
  • Average return per map: ~27d
  • Average profit per map: ~17d
  • Only 9⁄56 maps did not have a Purple Omen appear
  • Atlas procced 6 times giving me 6 more uses of my tablets
  • Did not include any chase unique drops in the calculations (4x Rathpith, 4x Shrine Sceptre)
  • Still no HH or MB :(

Extra observations:

I don’t think running more than one Omen Chance tablet is worth it. I was rarely noticing a difference, and when I did, the extra cost usually didn’t justify it.

Even though this is only a 56 map sample, across the full 200+ maps I’ve done I haven’t actually lost currency on a session. The worst run I had was still around +65d profit.

For Foretold Proliferation, I found the increased number of favours option was generally the strongest.

Ritual has become much more popular over the last few days, so Omen prices have dropped quite a bit. This data was collected around 24 hours ago.

I’m planning to track another 50 maps using non-city maps with only a single reroll x3 tablet, mainly because finding cities consistently is becoming a nightmare.
