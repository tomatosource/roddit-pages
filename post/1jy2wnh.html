If the Pistons beat the Bucks today they will do something they haven’t done since 2008: win a 45th game

Last time Pistons won a 45th game: March 7, 2008. This was a 101-97 victory against the Knicks. It has been 17 years, or 208 months, or 6,246 days.

For the Pistons: Tayshaun Prince led the Pistons in scoring with a respectable 28 points on 9⁄17 shooting (1⁄4 from 3). Chauncey Billups had a somewhat forgettable 12 points and 5 assists. Richard Hamilton had an off night with only 6 points on 3⁄8 shooting. The Pistons went on to have a great year, winning 59 games and losing a 6 game dogfight to the newly formed big 3 Celtics in the ECF.

For the Knicks: You might think that Thibs was coaching even back then, because Jamal Crawford played 43 minutes, Eddy Curry played 38 minutes, and David Lee played 35 minutes. Eddy Curry put up 23 points on 8⁄13 shooting. David Lee had a mean double double with 12 points and 15 boards. Jamal Crawford put up a forgettable 17 points on 6⁄15 from the field. Nate Robinson, who played 30 minutes off the bench, put up an empty 14 points on 5⁄13 from the field. The Knicks would go on to have an absolutely awful year, finishing 23-59. Head coach/president of basketball operations Isiah Thomas would be fired from both positions in the offseason.

What has happened since?: Nobody that played in this game is still in the NBA. There have been 3 new US presidents. David Stern retired and was replaced as NBA commissioner by Adam Silver
