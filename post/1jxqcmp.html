POE2 Is Losing Its Identity — Build Freedom Is Turning into Build Obligation

Title: POE2 Is Losing Its Core Identity: Build Freedom Is Being Replaced by Build Obligation

One of the things that made Path of Exile so special—something that set it apart from other ARPGs—was its unparalleled freedom to explore different builds.

This freedom wasn’t just theoretical. In POE1, it was possible to make almost any build work with enough investment, creativity, and planning. It wasn’t perfect, of course—but the sandbox was big enough to feel like you had real choices.

Now in POE2, that sense of freedom is disappearing fast.

The Problem

  • Certain builds (like Lightning Spear) are so overtuned that they invalidate most others.
  • The base difficulty of zones has also increased, which further punishes anyone who isn’t running those optimal builds.

This means:

  • If you choose something off-meta, you’re immediately at a significant disadvantage.
  • The game feels more like a test of how “correct” your build is, rather than how creative or fun it is.

My Personal Experience

I’ve been experimenting with Reap-based builds this league. I love the skill. It feels satisfying and thematic.

But no matter what I try—different support gems, gear setups, ascendancies—I constantly feel like I’m fighting an uphill battle, while Lightning Spear users are deleting screens with half the effort.

The build gap is just too large, and it’s exhausting.

What Needs to Happen

Balance should never come from nerfing the fun out of everything. Instead:

  • Keep strong builds strong, if they feel good to play.
  • But raise the floor for the underperforming ones.
  • Build diversity should come from the fact that many options feel viable, not just from theorycrafting that leads nowhere practical.

This is the only way POE2 can preserve the spirit of what made the game beloved in the first place.

The Bigger Picture

Right now, POE2 is starting to resemble the exact design philosophy we used to criticize in games like *Diablo*—where a small number of “correct” builds dominate the meta, and the rest feel like traps or challenges for masochists.

And when the game stops rewarding creative builds and starts demanding meta obedience, the fun fades too.

Please don’t let POE2 lose its soul.

TL;DR:
POE2 is punishing off-meta builds too much. We need to restore real build diversity by buffing the weak, not just nerfing the strong. Build freedom was what made POE great—don’t let it die.

One of the things that made Path of Exile truly stand out in the ARPG genre was its build freedom. The ability to pick a skill you like, theorycraft around it, and make it work—even if it wasn’t the most meta—was a huge part of what made the game fun and unique.

In POE2, that spirit feels like it’s fading.

The Core Issue

Right now, certain builds are far too strong, while most others feel noticeably underpowered. At the same time, the overall zone difficulty has been raised, making the gap even more punishing.

The result is simple:
Unless you pick one of the top-tier builds, your gameplay experience becomes frustratingly harder, and the game feels more like a chore than a challenge.

This turns build diversity into an illusion. Sure, technically you can play whatever you want—but if you do, you’ll be facing an uphill battle while others breeze through content with half the effort.

The Frustration

When players feel forced to follow the meta just to keep up, the game stops being about creativity and becomes a race to copy-paste whatever is overperforming. That’s not what POE is about.

Build freedom only exists when multiple builds are viable, not when one or two dominate while the rest are left behind.

A Better Direction

Instead of constantly nerfing what feels fun or strong, the focus should be on bringing underperforming builds up to a competitive level. The goal should be to let more skills feel satisfying and viable—not just mathematically possible.

We don’t want “Diablo-style balance” where 90% of builds feel like traps.
POE deserves better.

Final Thoughts

POE2 still has incredible potential.
But right now, it’s teetering dangerously close to a place where the core appeal of the game—freedom of choice—is being replaced by mandatory meta compliance.

Please don’t let this become the new norm. Build diversity should be celebrated, not punished.

TL;DR:
POE2 needs to restore real build freedom. Right now, certain builds are too strong and others too weak, and that imbalance—combined with high base difficulty—forces players into narrow choices. Buff the weak, don’t just nerf the strong.
