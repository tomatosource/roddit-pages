“Disenchanting” Skill and Support Gems, as is possible with the Spectre Skill Gem - How would people feel about this being a feature in the game?

This doesn’t impact me, but I feel as though it would be a significant improvement for newer players going through the game for the first time, or even experienced players trying new Classes/Builds for the first time. Especially when going through the Campaign where “Experimentation” is pretty much disincentivized.

I feel like the opposite should be true, and that players should be encouraged and incentivized whilst levelling to try new things.

Now sure, the addition of allowing us to respeccing is something which has improved the new player experience, however, the rarity of Skill Gems and especially Support Gems whilst levelling is a new player issue….Likewise as is selecting a Support Gem which may not function the way you believe.

E.g. The support gem may say that it synergises with Unearth, however, it only synergises with the Skill element, rather than the Minion element.

I think it would be a beneficial change to allow players to “Disenchant” Skill and Support Gems, returning it to an “Uncut Gem”, allowing players to try other Skills and Supports.

You could argue, with my example above, that the player would “Learn” that the Support Gem would synergise with the Skill part and not the Minion part, I totally agree they would “Learn”….But why then should that also be “Punishing”, by that player not having a synergising support gem equipped, before they find another?

Especially when logically, playing through PoE the first time with no experience of how these things work, it would be safe to assume that it would synergise with both parts.

I’d suggest a short tutorial when a player is shown how to select their first skill from their Uncut Skill Gem, to then also show they can “Disenchant” it and change their mind.
