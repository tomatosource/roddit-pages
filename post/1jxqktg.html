Day 8 of EU servers not working

Title sums it up. I absolutely love 0.2, I must’ve played at least 40 hours since it came out. Only problem is that I can barely play it as an EU player. At most times, but 90% of the time in the morning of every single day, the servers catch on fire. Crashes every 10 mins, rolling back your inventory but consuming your waystone, map etc.

It is genuinely unplayable. I have had to play on Washington servers for all of 0.2. I tried some EU today again, and yet again more crashes. Today I landed a Divine, Perfect Jeweller’s Orb, and Audience to King on Washington servers. If I had dropped those and experienced a rollback on EU, I’d probably have smashed my computer.

We need to bring awareness to this. It needs to be in Q&A’s, it needs to be the top post on the game’s Reddit, and frankly we may possibly need to start negatively reviewing the game if they don’t provide a response, not out of hatred, but a genuine review. How can I recommend that someone in the EU buys this game when they’re going to play through the (already slow) campaign and experience losing their progress and restarting areas constantly because of crashes? I can’t, that’s what.
