Temper Weapon from a Hobby Blacksmith

I’m a hobby blacksmith,, I love the look of this ascendancy and its relation to the character is awesome visually. I watched game play of this and it looks awful. Folks standing around getting beaten while they slowly hammer for a small number of charges of the ability.

My question is why isn’t this a useful interactive ability? Why don’t we have waves of energy that push back and damage, like in the image? Why is the number of charges so small?

The problems I see:

- Small number of charges

- No combo or interaction potential

- Danger of standing still

Solution:

Give the ability an AOE interaction the closer you are to the anvil while channeling

- Vulnerability to fire

- Knock Back

- Crowd Control or additional benefits (Daze, slow, hobble, destroying or reflecting projectiles)

- Damage the closer you get to the anvil during the channel

Potentially make it a buff rather than charge based, this may be less of a problem if you make the absurdly long animation actually valuable. You could also integrate weapon switching into ascendancy by allowing charges to apply to ALL weapons that are used by the smith. This could include the floating weapon.

Lastly you aren’t tempering by hitting it on an anvil… you’re work hardening, but that’s pretty minor in the fantastical nature of it.
