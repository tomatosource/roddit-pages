Can’t play PoE 1 anymore due to this game

I am a very long term player of PoE 1. I have at least 5k hours logged in it since open beta. I have enjoyed my time in it quite a bit. It is still a great game in many ways. But, eversince PoE 2 came out, I just can’t play it anymore. It feels clunky, unresponsive, and generally very boring due to the gameplay of mostly holding one button with no dodging.

I have really tried to get into the Merc league and the entire time all I can think is that I would rather be playing PoE 2. And during that time, the global chat is spamming “PoE 2 bad”, “D4 &gt; PoE 2” and so on. The whole thing makes me feel like I am taking crazy pills.

Does anybody else feel this way? Or am I just cooked? I would love to still enjoy PoE 1 like before. But I just can’t after having tasted the sweet fruit of the sequel.
