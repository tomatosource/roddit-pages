I’m at the end of act 3 and I’m exhausted

The game is fun, very fun actually. Mechanics are interesting (gems, crafting) and deep (passive tree, everything lol). But God damn do the campaign maps suck ass. There are many nooks and crannies that are just long enough to check out but turn out to be dead ends; the map layout doesn’t guide the player to any objective, which means that if you missed a small part 5 minutes ago exploring - you’re gonna have to come back there to see if it is the way forward (it may not be). The pacing is also pretty slow imo, yeah I do get stronger, but you can feel the grind in every map and it gets too repetitive. Maybe if the maps/levels weren’t so long and vast…

I heard that the developers want to add more acts in the final release? So more than 6? Please no, please no.

And I would run few trials of chaos or sekhamus, but 0 keys dropped so I can’t run those. I like those (run maybe 2 of them, but they were a nice change of pace)

Anyway, I hope I’ll have the will to push through to the end game. But pls tell me, do you feel the same? And is the end game, “maps”, any better?
