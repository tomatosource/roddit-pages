Cruel Mode Should Be a Permanent Option

I really think Cruel mode should become a permanent game mode — something that sits between Normal and Hardcore.

In my opinion, Cruel should increase overall difficulty in a meaningful way: more rare monsters, higher monster resistances, tougher encounters, and better loot to compensate. That would create a real challenge without relying on permadeath as the main difficulty lever.

I understand that Hardcore is beloved by a lot of players, but for me, it doesn’t necessarily mean “harder” — it just means you only get one life. The tension comes from the risk, not from deeper mechanical difficulty. Personally, I don’t find that appealing as a form of challenge.

Another idea that could make Cruel even more interesting would be allowing Atlas passive points to apply during the campaign when creating new characters. That would make campaign maps more engaging and customizable, especially when combined with a tougher Cruel mode.

I think this would add meaningful replayability and give players more control over how they want to experience difficulty.

What do you guys think?
