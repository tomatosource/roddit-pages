One well-placed league source insisted to me that the Heat should be scratched as a Morant suitor — even if the terms are favorable — if such a move jeopardizes a future trade run at Milwaukee’s Giannis Antetokounmpo. All indications are that Antetokounmpo will not be in trade play before the Feb. 5 deadline, but as we’ve often warned readers previously, that won’t stop interested teams from continuing to plot future pursuits of The Greek Freak. And word is that Miami believes it will be firmly in the mix as a legit trade destination for Antetokounmpo if he actually becomes available at season’s end — at last — as various rival teams project.
