Players who has the most top 5 mvp finishes in a row in nba history

I wanted to make it “in a row” rather than total because I wanted to see how many years in a row a player could be a durable and healthy, a top 5 player in the world, and their team have a sustainable record in order to be in mvp talks.

1st - LeBron James, 2006 - 2018 (13 years)

LeBron was top 5 in mvp voting for 13 years in a row, and has a total of 14 top 5 finishes, becoming an mvp candidate again in 2020. The only reason he doesn’t have 15 in a row is because in 2019 the lakers had a losing record and they failed to make the playoffs.

2nd - Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, 1970 - 1981 (12 years)

Kareem actually has the most top 5 mvp finishes in nba history with 15, he fell out of those talks in 1982 and 1983. The last time he was top 5 in mvp voting he was 38 years old.

3rd - Bill Russell, 1958 - 1967 (10 years)

Bill Russell in his sophomore year (just like Kareem) won mvp. He was all nba second team when he won 3 of his mvps, and he was all nba first team for 2 of them.

4th - A two way tie

Magic Johnson, 1982 - 1991 (9 years)

Larry Bird, 1980 - 1988 (9 years)

Magic and Bird were also both all-nba first team every single year that they were also top 5 in mvp voting

5th - A three way tie this time

Tim Duncan, 1998 - 2005 (8 years)

Kobe Bryant, 2006 - 2013 (8 years)

Oscar Robertson, 1961 - 1968 (8 years)

Time Duncan was top 5 in mvp voting one other time in 2007 and then never again.

Kobe Bryant was top 5 in mvp voting for 3 years before he missed it in 2005. This was a year he was below .500 and didn’t garner a single mvp vote.

Oscar Robertson surprised me the most, but also wasn’t very surprising. He was top 5 in mvp voting again in 1971 (the year he won a chip with Kareem and the bucks) and then never again.

The current player with the most top 5 finishes in a row (minus LeBron) is Giannis with 7, though he broke his streak this year.

That’s the list, I wasn’t very surprised by it but it is cool to see nonetheless. One thing I wanna mention is that Michael Jordan isn’t on this list because I wanted to show sustained excellence. No doubt he would’ve been top 5 in mvp voting if he stayed in the league in 1994 and ‘95, but he didn’t. If I don’t include those years he would be tied with Bill Russell, but I don’t want to be biased.

I think that Magic and Bird being tied is pretty cool, but that’s my off season stats post, hope you enjoyed the read.
