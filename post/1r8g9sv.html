Mildly Interesting: In the past 30 years there have only been 2 draft classes where each Top 3 pick was named All Star at some point in their careers

Just found myself thinking about RJ Barrett today, as I’m sure we all do. And his odds of becoming an All Star at some point in his career.

This started me down a path of looking up draft classes. Was interested to discover that it’s only happen twice in the past 30 years where all 3 guys made it at some point.

2016: Ben Simmons, Brandon Ingram and Jaylen Brown

1999: Elton Brand, Steve Francis, Baron Davis

Furthermore, 47% of the time the draft class will have 2 All Stars. 37% it will only be 1 all star.

In recent years, this top 3 class of Flagg, Edgecombe and Harper look promising to be fair. So maybe they can be the next one to buck the trend!
