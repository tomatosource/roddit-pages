I’m the Director of Baseball Operations for the Pittsburgh Pirates and author of Chasing Dreams: How to Land a Job in Sports. AMA about breaking into baseball.

Hey r/baseball! I’m Trey Rose. I’ve spent the last 10 years working in Baseball Operations with the Pittsburgh Pirates, starting through a fellowship and working my way through several roles before becoming a Director of Baseball Operations.

Earlier this summer, I published Chasing Dreams: How to Land a Job in Sports, a book designed to make the process of breaking into the sports industry a little less mysterious.

The book combines lessons from my own career with insights from 40+ people across the industry, including executives, coaches, scouts, analysts, agents, and others. Additionally, it provides practical advice on resumes, networking, interviews, internships, side projects, and actually getting your foot in the door.

One of the biggest reasons I wrote it was because I remember how difficult it was to figure out how you actually get one of these jobs when I was trying to break in.

So, AMA. I plan to be available to answer around 8 PM EST, so please send any questions in advance!

I’m especially happy to answer questions about:

  • Breaking into baseball or professional sports
  • Internships and entry-level jobs
  • Resumes and applications
  • Networking advice
  • Interviews
  • Skills teams are looking for
  • Baseball Operations career paths
  • Things I wish I knew when I was trying to get hired

I’ll do my best to get to as many questions as I can.

If you’re interested in the book, you can find it here (there will be an accompanying workbook published in the coming weeks): CHASING DREAMS: HOW TO LAND A JOB IN SPORTS

Looking forward to the questions!
