Which less-discussed changes/additions would you like included in version 0.4

With 3.27 coming soon and having played 0.3 to death, i was wondering about which changes/additions i hope GGG introduce in 0.4, that isnt the typical discussed stuff.

This disqualifies all the typical discussions about endgame being shallow, adding acts, new league mechanics, nerfing deadeye, passive tree movement speed/life stuff, performance, delirium fog etc.

Personally, after having played about 500 hours of 0.3, and more than 1000 hours of 0.1+0.2, there are a few things that has started to annoy me quite a bit:

  • Invulnerability in the endgame. Phasing and invulnerability in the campaign is fine because it’s a different game. However, i think that invulnerability in the endgame is a huge concern, especially with how much of it 0.3 added, in my opinion it is quite anti-ARPG. The whole point is get stronger -&gt; kill better mobs -&gt; get even stronger -&gt; kill even stronger mobs. Having to add invulnerability is a symptom of something wrong on a deeper level. Even worse is bosses that phases and damages in the meantime, while being invulnerable. You could have a mirror item in each slot and still die to a random lvl80 boss.
  • Stones and pebbles. Again, this is fine in the campaign as it’s a different game. However, in the endgame it gets extremely annoying to be body blocked constantly, being stuck etc. Especially considering performance is like 15 FPS in dire situations, and getting stuck on a pebble feels horrible in those situations. I also don’t think that altitude in maps works well. It feels bad, and seems to tank performance when a map has hills.
  • Special rewards from campaign too hidden. Just make a basic list in game to check if you have picked up all the stat bonuses you get from the campaign.
  • Loading screens everywhere. How and why do i even get a loading screen to enter my own hideout? I feel like the game would instantly get so much better to play, if GGG allowed players to opt-in to some general caching, where theyd store our Hideout and just load it instantly rather than having to load shaders constantly everywhere. Or if i have a 1TB SSD, just let me literally cache most maps etc.
  • Make the tradesite automatically track the currency ratio.
