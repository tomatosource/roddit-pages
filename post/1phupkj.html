From 1936 to 2019, the only Writers Ballots that did not contain at least 10 people currently in the Hall of Fame were 1988, 2006 and 2012, which all contain 9.

I had read a quote from a voter explaining why they used 10 votes this year saying that you would be hard pressed to find a ballot that did not contain 10 hall of famers, so I decided to check.

For each year I counted those inducted that year plus those who made the HOF eventually either on a future ballot or on a committee

1988 could get their 10th with Curt Flood entering for pioneer reasons.

2006 could get their 10th with Tommy John who may also get in for similar reasons, or if Don Mattingly/Dale Murphy eventually make it.

Either of Mattingly/Murphy making it would be the 10th for 2012 as well.

Manny Ramirez first appeared on the ballot in 2017 so numbers starting in 2017 are still more fluid, but there are already 12 HOFers on the 2017 ballot, 11 in 2018, and 10 in 2019

I just wonder if this trend will eventually die out in the future with the Vet Committee steadfast in keeping Steroid Era players out and standards being as high as they’ve ever been, and I thought it was interesting because you see so much discussion about how there are not 10 hall of famers on every ballot every year but the trends from the past indicate that there has been at least 10 hall of famers on nearly every ballot
