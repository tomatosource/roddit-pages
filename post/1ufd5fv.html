Justin Verlander has passed 20 years of MLB service time

Even for 20+ season guys, this is an extremely rare achievement.

By my count, he is the first to pass this mark since Pujols who retired with 21 years in 22 seasons.

Players who retired in the past decade & change who played in 20+ MLB seasons but did NOT pass the 20 year service time threshold:

Miggy, Greinke, Rich Hill, Jeter, Beltre, Beltran, Bartolo, Giambi, Hawkins.

Obviously this isn’t celebrated the way 10 years is, but IMO it’s still a pretty neat thing!
