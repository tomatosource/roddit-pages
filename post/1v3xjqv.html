Tanking is dead. The Bucks should tank

The new NBA lottery rules are there to discourage tanking, but having the worst record in the league lowering your odds at the number 1 pick doesn’t matter if you don’t control your own lottery picks.

The bucks should be trading away anyone with any value to stock up on lottery picks. At the deadline, they should be trading with desperate teams trying to get into the play in. Amass as many lottery picks as possible, and have a bad record in the mean time.

The bucks tanking into a bottom three spot will actually help their strategy. By taking one of the bottom three spots, they decrease the likelihood that another team falls to that level. That keeps their amassed picks more valuable.

Tanking is dead. Long live tanking.
