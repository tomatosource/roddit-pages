If you somehow missed this, since 0.5 one character per league (SC, HC, SSF) can sacrifice their level 100 character to grant every other player in the league a permanent skill point. All the normal leagues already had their martyrs long ago but private leagues have their own ladder and with that their own martyr and I happened to be playing in one. Once I realized this I started grinding to 100 even though I’ve never been close to it before in 10+ years of playing PoE. Now after playing for a month I’ve reached my first level 100 and my goal for the league. I can’t say that it was worth it but at least I can finally take a break now.
