It is with the deepest sadness that I say…it’s time to have the Derrick White conversation :(

We’ve established thus far in the thread:

  1. It’s time for a comprehensive re-tool. No one is safe except D white and Tatum

  2. Jrue and KP are the first to go

  3. Next year is a goner

It hurts me more to write this than it hurts you to read it, trust me. It’s time to seriously consider trading our beloved.

Trading KP+Jrue makes a world of sense, but they’re not going to get us much of anything on the open market. Porzingis is on the books for 30mil next year. Bad contract, maybe terrible. Let’s remember that the Wiz had to attach a first-round pick to get him off their books. All he’s done since is miss time (and win a ring). We need to trade him to open up roster flexibility, but we’re not going to get much for the guy.

Jrue is a more interesting piece because of his obvious championship pedigree. All 30 teams would love to have Jrue on their roster, but I wonder how they see his trade value at another 33mil a year for the next two. I think his market value is closer in the 10-20 range with guys like Caruso, Hart, Dort, and Naz, not the range with Bane, Fox and Dejounte. I think we still get a good piece for Jrue, but not what we’re hoping for in this “hard cap” era. I’d guess a top 15 protected first with a fringe rotation guy.

I think we all understand this, which is why there’s buzz about Jaylen. But seriously, we’re talking about trading Jaylen before White? The sentiment is that he’s not a super max player and that’s probably true. But this isn’t like Klay’s hometown supermax in 2023 (when he was 33), it’s more like his 2018 paycheck. Jaylen is a finals MVP in the prime of his career. He struggled shooting this year, but this guy has 5 more years of All-NBA level basketball in him. Not to mention his importance to the city.

I swear, the only ones who love D White as much as Celtics fans are the Tatum haters out there. He’s a great shooter, a plus defender, and a great teammate. Don’t we start looking to acquire a D White type the second we trade him? The truth of the matter is he’s a role player on a great deal. He doesn’t guard your best player, and he’s a spot-up guy on offense. I don’t mean to knock his play, the issue is that we need at least 3 (probably four) of these guys to pair with elite playmaking. I’m talking about guys worse than White we’re watching in the finals like Nembhard, Wallace, Joe, and Wiggins.

I think Jrue+KP gets us 1. White gets us two. Hopefully, we can grow one internally and Baylor or Davidson turns into another. Boom, banner 19.

P.S. I think we should look long and hard at Marcus Smart.

GO CELTICS!
