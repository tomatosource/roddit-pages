Rui Hachimura was a Catch & Shoot GOD in the 2026 playoffs

https://preview.redd.it/hxm3pb2k32ah1.jpg?width=881&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=9ecd3293e0597122bf5749684cbb704d63d550bd

Rui averaged 17.5 points over 10 playoff games while shooting 57% from deep this postseason. One of the most insane shooting stretches we’ve ever seen. It literally made him the all time leader in career playoff 3P%.

He was almost always on the weak side, stretching and splashing. Feet set, clean release, cash money.

Some say the Lakers not being C&S heavy helped him because they put so much rim pressure and collapse defense. Then why did the rest of the Lakers shoot around 30% on C&S meanwhile the midrange god shot 59%? At some point, you gotta admit he’s the one. There isn’t a better stationary rhythm shooter in the league now. He’s certainly a whole lot better than y’all ever gave him credit for.

Will he shoot 59% again? Will he shoot 67% on wide open again? Probably not. But it’ll still be cash only, every time he’s open.
