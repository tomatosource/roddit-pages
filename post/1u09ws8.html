For it being the bare minimum entry point for expedition content, logbooks drop rates are way too low. Especially considering its a consumable resource on top.

Currently logbooks are 150 exalts which is nearly 2 Divines. You basically cannot engage with expedition outside of them, as there is nothing on the atlas tree, and you cannot get tablets for them, meaning if you want to farm expedition you are hardlocked by logbooks.

This is the only content in the game currently like this, if i want to do abyss every map, i can just use a tablet. For expedition every map, i need to find a very rare drop, or fork out nearly 2 divines every hour.

For context, and im aware this is a very small sample of one person, i have explicitly only done expedition for pretty much a week, (30 logbooks worth of locations) and i have dropped 2 logbooks total. Meaning i’ve had to fork out for 28 logbooks.

I know the game is not balanced around SSF but i can only imagine how abysmal that is for SSF players lol.

I think they should improve the drop rates for logbooks as of right now theyre they only way to guarantee expedition content in maps.
