Could the Celtics possibly get Jayden Quaintance at 27?

Jonathan Givony said on the latest episode of the Zach Lowe show that this is a “Robert Williams situation” and that Jayden Quaintance could slip into the mid 20s because of his medicals, but that some top team is going to get him and be “very happy”

Quaintance’s #1 comp is Rob Williams. 6’9 with a 7’5 wingspan, can jump out the gym, elite shot blocker and lob threat. Has serious injury concerns (tore his ACL previously and only played 4 games this season with knee soreness). But he’s really young (will be 18 on draft night) and highly touted (the Ringer has him as their 10th overall prospect).

The Celtics drafted Robert Williams 27th overall in 2018. This year the Celtics are drafting…27th overall. Is this destiny?
