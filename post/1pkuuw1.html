Pseudo Self Found (zero to hero)

For anyone struggling to decide what to play this season, allow me to suggest something that will change your mindset.

Do not rush the campaign. You get to maps quickly and then what? What was your aim?

Ok, if it’s to make 100 div before anyone else, you do you. But for anyone else? Here’s what I find rewarding, just a suggestion.

Pseudo self found. The currency exchange is ok but trading for gear is not. Wear the items you make, there’s so much enjoyment to be had from that alone.

You must farm the fragments you need for arbiter yourself. Make mapping have a purpose other than just aimless node travelling.

Try not to get wrapped up in YouTubers. If you’ve played 0.1 onwards you must be bored of end game by now, right? I am. So don’t make the game about mindless mapping. Farm with purpose. Change your filter to highlight bases you need, and try crafting (exalt slamming) something useful that isn’t going to trivialise mapping.

Perfect trans and aug are really good. Better than most appreciate I think. Ok so Homogenising got removed but we roll with it.

If you’re brave, take on a new class or ascendency. What’ve you got to lose? A bit of a struggle because you made a poor choice? Now you have reason to get some gold…

The challenge is the fun. And seasons run out of challenge very quickly if you rush end game with the same old class as usual or a YouTuber Uber build.

One last thing. Loot drop dopamine. In pseudo self found, that come back too. Go to filter blade and figure out how to highlight the bases you need and cull everything else. Now when you get a drop, it’s decent, useful, rewarding. Screens of useless crap are gone!

We spend so much time trying to avoid the struggle when maybe it needs embracing a bit more :)
