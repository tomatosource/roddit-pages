Eurobasket 2025 Day 2 - Recap and NBA Player Stats

Hi, since you guys yesterday liked the recap I will be doing it too the next couple of days as long as nothing irl comes into the way. As mentioned yesterday I didnt have as much time today and was only able to actually watch a single game. But I dug into the stats and threads I could find aswell as the highlights available to find most important things.

We are starting Group C and D today and have some good matchups.

Group C:

Spain vs Georgia
When asked yesterday of what the best games today would be I mentioned that this game might be deceptively close. There are a lot of questions surrounding former powerhouse Spain right now and most people would not rank them as top contenders. Still they were favorites vs a fierce Georgian side who feature two NBA players in Orlandos Bitadze and Torontos Mamukelashvili.
And for once I can say my ball knowledge seemed to be correct as Georgia not only won the first quarter but also the third and the fourth quarter and stunned the defending Champions with a deserved 83:69 Win. The questions for Spain this Eurobasket seems to be whether they even can escape groups vs Bosnia, Greece and Italy now.

NBA Player Stats:
Bitadze: 15/4/2 no turnovers
Mamukelashvili: 19/7/6 with 3 Turnovers
Aldama: 12/3/2 with 1 Turnover

Bosnia and Herzigovina vs Cyprus
Cyprus has the home advantage. Thats about as positive as I can say about a small nation that qualified because they are the hosts. Like I mean that they would have not gotten through Round 1 of qualification where they went 1⁄5 in Games vs Basketball Powerhouses Ireland, Austria and Switzerland. In the second qualification phase they didnt win a Game and lost by -30 on average but still knocked out Croatia because the host autoqualified so third place in their group was knocked out. That included Games vs Bosnia in qualifying which they lost 99:66 and 108:62. We can expect similar or higher scores this tournament. While the first two quarters went as expected with Bosnia leading by 22 at half time in the third quarter Cyprus unexpectedly started a comeback getting into single digit score difference. However Bosnia managed to swiftly recover and score a decisive win.
91:64 Bosnia.

NBA Player Stats:
Nurkic: 18/6/1 with 3 Turnovers

Greece vs Italy
One of the Games that on paper is supposed to be close as these two are in the running for the Top 3 Spots in the group. Ultimately this game was about Giannis being unstoppable in two otherwise very shaky looking teams. Simone Fontecchio in contrast had a really bad game going 1⁄11 from the field. The game was close but overall still Greece with a good win. 75:66

NBA Player Stats:
Giannis: 31/7/2 with 2 Turnovers
Fontecchio: 4/5/1

Group D:

Israel vs Iceland
I am gonna admit I barely know anything about either team and wasnt able to watch this game. Most Icelandic players play in Iceland while Israel features Deni Avdija as their only NBA player and main man. Still people have Israel as the second best team of this group ahead of the one men team of Slovenia, so obviously they are very good. I just personally dont know much about them.
Their first game showed that they are good enough to compete as they beat Iceland, who played a decent game but ultimately were not good enough. 83:71 Israel.

NBA Player Stats:
Avdija: 20/9/2 with 2 Turnovers and 3 Blocks

Belgium vs France
This one would always be a tough game for Belgium as they were facing one of the contenders with France. Sporting a lot of younger NBA players + Yabusele while missing Wemby and Gobert, they were still considered a Top 3 Team while Belgium….well they got stomped by Finland not so long ago. And France showed their superiority in every single quarter of the match leading by 15 at Halftime and finishing it just as strong.
92:64 France

NBA Player Stats:
Yabusele: 11/3/2 no turnovers
Sarr: 7/4/1 with 1 Turnover
Risacher: 10/2/2 with 1 Turnover
Coulibaly: 12/7/0 with 1 Turnover

Slovenia vs Poland
Ok this one is really interesting. Its an open secret that Luka has absolutely negative help and this game showed it a lot. Slovenia was the favorite before this game but thats because of Luka. Poland meanwhile was riding a surprise 4th place at the last Eurobasket and are far from an easy opponent as a tense battle started. Along with Germany and Italy both of these teams live by offense rather than defense and here it shows as this was the game with the most points so far. Surprisingly Poland decided to Match Slovenia and Luka in scoring and that turned out to be the right choice as Monaco Player Jordan Loyd also scored 32 Points.
105:95 Poland

NBA Player Stats:
Luka: 34/4/9 with 4 Turnovers and 5 Steals.

Awards:

Positive Surprise of the Day: Georgia
Looked just way better than Spain.

HM: Cyprus (actually had a good quarter) and Polands offense looked lowkey really good.
