thoughts on the end of this league

At the end of the day, poe (both versions of it, which are basically the same at this point) might look complicated to a new player but it’s really not. It’s summarized by the following:

  • base crit is the only damage stat that matters, pick a build / class that can have a lot of it and you will be better than builds / classes that don’t.

  • beyond making your enemies dead with your 100% crit chance, you also need to keep yourself not-dead, and poe (1 or 2, doesn’t matter) is a game full of jump-slam one-shot mechanics, so a spec with enough spirit left over to run auto enfeeble or auto temporal chains means you win.

If (when) they nerf one of the above, the player base will half-disappear again, probably.
