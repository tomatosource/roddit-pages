I don’t want to keep replaying the campaign

I know this is a touchy issue and PoE players have given up on arguing, but being expected to replay the campaign over and over is my number one problem with PoE 2.

It’s weird how people have tolerated this. The campaign is long. It’s the same bosses, same zones, same order. The league system spice things up a little, but you get them once or twice an act.

The fixes are easy to imagine. The game has a ton of content, they could easy add a mode to swap out areas and bosses so each time you play you get a new experience.
