I haven’t seen it discussed on here, but the show “Coaches Corner” where Tim Leggler talks Xs & Os with various NBA head coaches is a very watchable and entertaining way to learn a little about about set-plays, strategy, and general coaching philosophies.

The show airs on NBA TV but also on YouTube.

The premise is little bite-size interviews, usually where Leggler shows the coach a specific play and then they discuss what makes the play work, what decisions the players made and why, and really just about basketball fundamentals and strategy in general.

It’s not like a super deep dive but you do learn some interesting stuff and I enjoy it.
