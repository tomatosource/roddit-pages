Gained fps and got stability

I just want to share my experience with the game and its performance for me.

My rig is just an R5 5600 with a base 4060, and 32gb RAM. The game is ultimately playable with capped 90 fps via Riva Tuner and dips to 20+ at t15s abyss content.

So my mobo apparently kills my left audio for some reason (a different post in reddit somewhere says it’s about too much power going into the headset). I thought maybe I need to remove Sonar from Steelseries (I Steelseries GG mainly for my mouse). So I removed sonar, and at the same time lowered the audio frequency available (16 bit in sound settings).

Then after that, everything felt smooth, never dipped below 40+ with abyss or expedition content. Idk if it was Sonar or the lowering of audio frequency. But I’m glad nonetheless.
