The ascendancy designs are amazing, but I wish they showed more on the actual character

First off, I just want to say that the visual design of this game is genuinely stunning. The art direction, the themes, everything just looks so good.

But one thing that stands out to me especially are the ascendancy designs. The splash arts on the passive tree are incredible. The Witch ascendancies in particular look so cool and the themes feel really well thought out.

Here’s my only gripe though: that awesome design only really shows up in those small splash arts. When you actually pick an ascendancy and level it up, nothing changes visually on your character. And that feels like such a missed opportunity to me. Like, if your character actually reflected the ascendancy’s theme and look in some way, that would be such a huge upgrade to the whole experience.

I’ve never played PoE1 so I have no idea if this was a thing there or if it’s something that’s already planned for PoE2 down the line. Maybe it’s coming and I just don’t know about it?

Either way, I just wanted to throw this out there, give some credit to whoever is doing the ascendancy art and theming because it’s really great work, and ask: does anyone else feel the same way?
