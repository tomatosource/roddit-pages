The four remaining teams in this year’s playoffs went 132-196 (.402) in the 2021-22 season and had a playoff record of 2-4 combined.

The Pacers went 25-57 in the 2021-22 season. The Knicks went 37-45. The Wolves went 46-36. The Thunder went 24-58.

The Wolves went on to lose to the Grizzlies in 6 games in the first round of the playoffs.

This season, those same four teams who were atrocious for the most part in 2021-22 went 218-110 (.665) in the regular season and currently have a combined playoff record (disregarding the current conference finals games) of 32-11.

None of these teams have ever won a title since 1979, when the Thunder did so branded as the SuperSonics.

These four teams have a combined total of 3 NBA titles between them. Two of them (IND, MIN) have zero.

No NBA Finals has ever had two teams who were not a top 3 seed in their conference. There’s still a chance of this year’s Finals becoming the first in that regard, if we get a Pacers-Wolves Finals matchup.

This NBA postseason has been nothing short of incredible, considering all of this.
