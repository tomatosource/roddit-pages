Jamal Murray so far this season 25 and 7 assists per game on 51/45/90. The Nuggets are off to their best start in franchise history despite being down their 3rd and 4th best players (and 2 best defenders)

I’ve spent years defending Jamal on r/NBA because it’s been clear that the inconsistency in his play was directly linked to his inability to stay healthy.

His breakout season came on the back of improving his handle during the pandemic break. Against the Lakers in the WCF, Jamal hurt his hammy in game 5 and then faced drastically shorted off-season.

He started off that season disappointing while accusations of “bubble Murray” before having a very strong January-March and then tore his ACL. This of course led to him missing two post seasons.

It took him the majority of his return season to look right and then his was elite while helping the Nuggets win a title… But the following season saw him deal with hamstring issues, shin splints and then, of course, the calf injury.

Point being, since the ACL tear, there have been a lot of justified questions about his ability to stay healthy, but also a lot of dismal of how the injuries were effecting him and the role they played in his ability to stay in the shape during the off-season.

This is the first time he has looked completely healthy in years and it’s the single biggest reason, imo, that the Nuggets have a chance of upsetting OKC. He is playing like a legitimate all-star. Now he just needs to stay healthy. Hopefully the Nuggets decision to not have a true back up behind him doesn’t backfire in that regard.
