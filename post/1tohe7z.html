BREAKING: Kenny Atkinson to remain Cavs coach despite Knicks sweep in conference finals: Sources

https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7308048/2026/05/26/kenny-atkinson-cavaliers-coach-status-2/?unlocked_article_code=1.lVA.4Rki.O6xAuLavB_J-&source=athletic_user_shared_gift_article_copylink&smid=url-share-ta

The Cavaliers plan to retain coach Kenny Atkinson even after the team was swept Monday night by the New York Knicks, two league sources told The Athletic.

Atkinson guided the franchise to its first appearance in the conference finals since 2018 and first without LeBron James on the roster since 1992, but there was some question coming out of the series about potential changes Cleveland might make. Instead, no major changes to the front office or coaching staffs are now expected, per those sources.
