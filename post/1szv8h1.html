Comet is a bad Skill, not a bad Design

Consider 2 things:

  1. Comet is the unambiguous best skill to use with any Cast On X effect (CoX) (Not counting Deodres Undoing, support gem).

  2. Absolutely no one is hard casting Comet, because Comet is actually a bad skill.

How can this be the case when CoX just cast spells for you? It is because Comet has a very specific spell effect:

https://preview.redd.it/5ojbmlw2obyg1.png?width=731&format=png&auto=webp&s=6a39ce1e3f92e2291131c4cbea47ca248bc9e7cc

+1 Second to Total Cast Time

Given Comets Base Cast Time of 1.00s, Comet has an effective cast time of 2.00s (before Cast Speed increases). 2 seconds to cast a spell is just bad, and furthermore Cast Speed increase suffer a 50% Less effect (not actually, but approximately). This makes Comet very clunky and just bad to cast, it is simply not worth the time nor mana.

But in CoX, Comet is getting a 50% discount on the Energy cost, and because it is cast for you it has an effective 0s cast time. So the single largest downside of Comet is entirely negated AND the cost of investment is discounted by 50%! (Energy).

So, since we are all expecting CoX and Comet nerfs in 5.0, I suggest the following:

Remove the +1 Second to Total Cast Time and make the Base Cast Time 2.00s. This immediately halves the damage of CoX-Comet by doubling the Energy cost. It also double the effective value (not actually, but approximately) of Cast Speed on Comet.

A Second change I would recommend would be to change the effect of Quality from Chance to Repeat to More Cast Speed (20%). Repeats are going to further contribute to Comet dominating CoX, but More Cast Speed would contribute to Comet being reasonably Castable. With 20% More Cast Speed, 84% Cast Speed results in a 1s Comet and everything greater than 84% results in an under 1.00s Comet (currently impossible), 250% Cast Speed would result in 0.5s Comets.

TL:DR

Remove +1 Second to Total Cast Time

Remove (0-10)% Chance to Repeat from Quality

Add 1.00 Seconds to Base Cast Time

Add (0-20)% More Cast Speed to Quality

While these changes do “solve” the CoX problem, I think they would greatly contribute to the end of CoX - Comet dominance and contribute to the actually usage of Comet as a primary Skill.
