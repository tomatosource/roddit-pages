Thoughts on this season being a tanking one?

I have mixed feelings on it.

On one hand, I really wanted to see what a decent/okay team with JB, Derrick, & PP leading the way would fare. I was getting kinda hopeful maybe we’d have a low-stakes season with some exciting games like in ‘17-‘18 after Kyrie & Hayward went down. Getting to the playoffs maybe?

But on the other, I also get why Brad, Zarren, and Bill would want to tank. Assuming the plan is to keep with a duo of The Jays (and as such will need a cheap, probably young group around them), what are the odds we ever get the opportunity again to potentially get a top 10 pick in a draft while we have them both here & in their primes?

What are everyone else’s thoughts?
