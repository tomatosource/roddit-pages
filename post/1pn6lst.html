It’s not you, it’s me

TL;DR: Game is good now, but it’s just not for me.

I came from POE 1, so there will no doubt be a bit of bias, but bear (heh) with me.

I jumped on the POE 2 hype train when it first released, and was absolutely flabbergasted and disgusted at some of the design decisions. The fluid combat, animations, boss fights, and beautiful art made it bearable, but gameplay-wise overall, it was absolutely miserable. I won’t go into specifics; this isn’t a rant post. So I dropped it.

Fast forward 1(?) year later, and most of those grievances have been addressed. Hopped on the new class, levelled as a bear, and had an absolute blast. Was getting loot, even got a raw divine drop, was able to upgrade my gear in the temple; I was having a good time.

Then I hit maps, and something clicked off. The temples weren’t giving goodies anymore, so I stopped doing them. My build, I realized, was basically where it would be for the rest of the game, owing to the restrictive nature of ability interaction. There were no sneaky interactions or build-changing chase pieces to work toward or save up for. I would just be playing to do higher map tiers and get more damage on my gear. That’s it.

And that’s just fine. I get it, I’ve been on the WoW treadmill. Number goes up, brain releases happy chemical. But I guess that’s just not how I want to game anymore. So, with that being said, POE 2 developers, you’ve done a great job turning this ship around, and you’ve created a beautiful game. You should absolutely be proud of what you’ve made. But it’s just not for me.
