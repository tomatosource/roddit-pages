What will the Thunder do with their stockpile of draft picks?

The Thunder, already with a young, contending core, also has famously amassed a ton of draft capital. Just in 2026 alone, they will own 3 first round picks, two of which are lightly protected.

Here’s the Thunder’s first round pick situation:

2025:

  • 15th pick (via Miami)
  • 24th pick (via LA Clippers)

2026:

  • Own FRP
  • Philadelphia FRP (Top-4 protected)
  • Utah FRP (Top-8 protected)

2027:

  • Own FRP or swap with LA Clippers
  • Denver FRP (Top-5 protected)

2028:

  • Own FRP or swap with Dallas

2029:

  • Own FRP
  • Denver FRP (Top-5 protected)

2030:

  • Own FRP

2031:

  • Own FRP

And this list doesn’t include the 17 second round picks that the Thunder possess in the next 7 drafts.

It’s already been acknowledged that they likely will not have the roster spots or minutes to realistically roster all these picks, so some of them will need to eventually go out in trades by necessity,.. right?

Curious what Thunder fans and the rest of r/nba think will be Presti’s strategy with what is probably the best problem for an NBA GM to have?
