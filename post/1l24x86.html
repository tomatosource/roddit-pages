Casual Gameplay Feedback

So far the game is absolutely a blast to play at somewhat endgame, trying to fight pinnacle bosses, however I do have some gripes about the game. This is from a casual lvl 91 Rhoa lightning arrow ranger.

The Good:

Precursors: amazing addition. I can finally somewhat define what type of content I want to do. Delirium, Expedition or Ritual, I can just make them more common.

Hooded Man/Doryani: Best boys. Free identifiers + somewhat free passive refunds(If comparing to POE1).

Decrafting Bench/Reconstructing Bench: Good to farm for materials, and if you have 3 of a kind, just merge it into a new unidentified item.

Guild Stash Tab: I play with my friends so its an absolute blast to have the ability to share stashtabs, with my friends.

The Bad:

Campaign: Its honestly a slog. Most of your skills that help you the most or stats that help you the most in terms of movement, is so late, it almost feels like a waste. Your killing potential is so low, before reaching map, it feels like I would like to pull my hair out.

Mercenary: I think he is an amazing class, late game. Early game, its such a horrible feeling to be constantly in a state of reloading, while other range classes just ignore the reload mechanic altogether. Bow, Wand, Staff, just have mana as its limiting factor, while Crossbows have mana, bolts (which take time to swap inbetween), the same slow movement speed as everybody else when attacking, and reloading.

Distilled (Emotions)/Oils: Want Perma delirium on your maps? I like it too. Did you know you can upgrade your OILS!? You can! However. One. By. One.

The Ugly:

Towns in general: How are they the worst places to lag + they take the longest time to load up. I can take half the time to load up a map with way more enemies + drops than the town. Heck, even when playing with my friends, I try to avoid the towns like the bubonic plague. I get that you have to load player data, but honestly this town lag is unbearable.

Dynamic Loading: I’m pretty sure the game uses some weird dynamic loading instead of preloading the assets, as opening a chest, starting delirium, opening breach, doing anything, that causes some sort of new effect to happen causes the game to try to fetch the asset on the fly. That is the only explanation I got, cause its so annoying to go from smooth to 10 spf when opening a strongbox, and no I did not write that wrongly. Seconds per Frame.

The Atlas: The Majority of your gameplay at endgame, other than sanctum or ultimatum. I have a love hate relationship with it. I love how it grows as you explore, however, I hate how it feels. The lag everytime you drag across the screen to load the “map”, the indicator of where you are feels so insigificant, sometimes if I have built my map in 5 directions when I drag it, I get lost where I was going, and the worst is pinnacle specific nodes. Make them like weird exclusive waystones like shaper and elder fragment bosses instead of, hey go in a random direction till you see a red pillar of light. It kinda kills my mood, when all I have to go off of, is from corruption to corruption, precursor tower to precursor tower and sometimes randomly maybe, a single unique map.

Last thing: The bugs. Nothing more to say about it. The crashes and the times enemies spawn off screen. Sadge.
