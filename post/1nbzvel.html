Each Biome on the Atlas Map should we a lot bigger

One thing that I find very frustrating is that the Atlas Map looks pretty much the same no matter where you are. You always see small mountainous areas, grassy areas, desert, forests and sometimes water. This makes it very easy for me to get lost and lose my sense of direction.

I would much rather have each biome cover almost one screen and be a lot bigger than right now. As well as being more realistic, I think this would make navigating the Atlas easier, help with orientation and overall feel better and more immersive. The game has such a wide variety of maps that they could easily fill larger biomes with lots of different above- and below-ground maps to keep things interesting.

What are your thoughts on this?
