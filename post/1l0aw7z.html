Pure Power vs Energise & IE – Which Node Setup Wins?

Hey folks!
I’m trying to figure out which of these passive nodes would be better for my build, but I haven’t been able to find any specific info about them.

I’m currently playing a crit-based Spark Mage with one CoC setup.
The question is whether Pure Power is better than the combination of Energise and Invocated Efficiency (IE).

Pure Power shows a higher DPS in PoB, but it looks like PoB doesn’t account for the bonuses from Energise and IE. Those two give increased damage to triggered spells and meta skills, plus a chance to refund energy and increased energy gained when used—so the benefits aren’t fully represented in the DPS numbers.

My thinking is:

  • With CoC constantly triggering spells, Energise and IE might give strong and consistent uptime on those buffs.
  • Pure Power, on the other hand, gives a flat, always-on DPS increase.

So it’s kind of a choice between consistent extra buffs (that aren’t reflected in PoB) vs. guaranteed raw damage.

What do you all think? Anyone tested this or have experience with these nodes?
