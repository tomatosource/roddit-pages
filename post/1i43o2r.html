New Substitution Pattern

I didn’t really see people mention it in the game or post-game threads. But, wasn’t that a very different substitution pattern for the Jays tonight. Tatum has been playing the whole 1st and 3rd and Brown the whole 4th. Tonight, more like last season, Tatum came out halfway through the first and then came in from Brown with just under three minutes left. Tatum then played the whole second and Brown the whole 3rd. If they didn’t have a big enough lead to come out with 4 minutes left. I would assume the both would have finished the 4th.

I noticed it when it happened, and I feel like it changed the energy for the team. Tatum was able to really take over in the 2nd quarter and the 21 consecutive minutes he played from just under three minutes in the first until about halfway through the third the Celtics lead went from 5 to 18.

If they had to play out the game, Tatum would have been over 2 1⁄2 minutes vs over his season average (39.0 vs 36.3) and Brown would have been a minute over his. I don’t know that Tatum playing 39 minutes a game is sustainable, but on the whole I prefer this method from last season.
