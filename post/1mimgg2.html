Plan for 26-27?

If the Celtics continue to reduce cap below the tax in 25-26, I would expect that there will also be some hard choices in 26-27 season. They would still be a repeater (since the check is 3 of last 4 years) so they would need to also stay under the tax in 26-27 to avoid $ penalties. However, since Tatum and Brown should both be healthy in their prime I would expect spending and the ownership group to pay for a year or two at a chance of a championship. Even then the window will be short and require getting under the cap again unless they are willing to continually pay.

They could also zag and throw 2 years away and spend huge in 27-28, but that seems crazy to waste even one year of Tatum/Brown. These new CBA rules really make it impossible to have two stars on the Max and contend without getting a lot of young talent.
