[OC] Which Summer League game has had the most “NBA-level” players?

A couple of days ago, Kyler1313 made this post asking what summer league team had the most NBA talent. The question intrigued me, and I wanted to take it further and see which game had the most NBA-level players.

Data Gathering

Using the nba_api Python package, I gathered summer league box scores and player regular season statistics.

  • I didn’t count any player who didn’t actually play in the game (did not dress, not with team, etc).
  • To lessen the effect of veterans who join summer league for “one last shot”, I calculated remaining career totals. For example, Awvee Storey played for the Bucks Summer League team in 2008. Rather than using his entire career of 60 games, his contribution is the 26 games he played after the 2008 Summer League.
  • I set the bar for an “NBA-level” player at 20 career games played after the summer league game, to weed out the one-game/silly-season heroes.

Results

First, let’s start off by attempting to answer the question of the previous post. Kyler1313 surmised that the 2017 Lakers were the answer.

By Number of Players (Team-Level)

The Portland Trail Blazers dressed 10 NBA players for their game against the Houston Rockets on June 7, 2023: Kris Murray, Jabari Walker, Ibou Badji, Shaedon Sharpe, Scoot Henderson, Rayan Rupert, Duop Reath, Keon Johnson, Justin Minaya & Nate Williams.

There are multiple teams with 8 NBA players in a Summer League game, I’ll break ties between different games by the same team in the same Summer League by number of games played.

  • 2017 Lakers on June 7: Brandon Ingram, Ivica Zubac, Lonzo Ball, Kyle Kuzma, Josh Hart, Thomas Bryant, David Nwaba, Alex Caruso
  • 2022 Thunder on June 11: Aaron Wiggins, Jeremiah Robinson-Earl, Tre Mann, Josh Giddey, Jalen Williams, Jaylin Williams, Eugene Omoruyi, Vít Krejčí
  • 2022 Grizzlies on June 9: Jake LaRavia, Santi Aldama, Xavier Tillman, Ziaire Williams, Kennedy Chandler, Vince Williams Jr., David Roddy, Kenneth Lofton Jr.
  • 2022 Pistons on June 8: Isaiah Livers, Isaiah Stewart, Jalen Duren, Jaden Ivey, Killian Hayes, Saben Lee, Braxton Key, Buddy Boeheim

By Number of Games Played (Team-Level)

This will favour older Summer League teams.

  • 2009 Thunder on June 12 (4221): James Harden, DJ White, Serge Ibaka, Russell Westbrook, Shaun Livingston, Byron Mullens
  • 2007 Sixers on June 6 (3516, and this is the first Summer League game for which we have data!): Lou Amundson, Bobby Jones, Jason Smith, Lou Williams, Rodney Carney, Thaddeus Young
  • 2007 Grizzlies on June 6 (3481): Alexander Johnson, Tarence Kinsey, Kyle Lowry, Rudy Gay, Mike Conley
  • The aforementioned 2017 Lakers are 4th at 3287!
