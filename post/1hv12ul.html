There has been a fair amount of discourse in Celtics circles on whether Tatum should play a more heliocentric role in the Celtics offense.

The chatter around this has picked up recently, as Jaylen has been more assertive and taken more shots than Tatum in many of the Celtics recent losses. When Jaylen is on, this can work well since hes great at scoring in bursts, but he’s not nearly as adept as Tatum at making plays for others (or dribbling and not turning the ball over)

This was especially evident in last night’s game against the Thunder where after a great first half, scoring 21 points, Jaylen went 0/0/0/0/0 on 0/7 shooting with 3 turnovers in the second half.

Jayson had a 26.9% USG yesterday and only took 17 shots vs the Thunder. Despite ending the game with only 1 assist, Tatum actually generated 15 potential assists, and the team shot a disgusting 6.7% on them.
