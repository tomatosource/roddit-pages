Why are we surprised we are losing games because of a lack of 48 mins of good center play?

Guys, that was *always* gonna be what held this team back aside from Tatum being out(obviously)

Queta was a hit, that alone is a huge win and actually helps us a ton. That solves half the puzzle, the issue is we weren’t able to solve the second half of the puzzle in house with any of the other centers we tried this year- that’s okay, it’s a very solvable problem.

We are going to lose games because of this, it’s gonna happen, ESPECIALLY to the pistons who are one of the biggest most big man/paint dominant teams in the league. It’s inevitable and it’s *okay*

The answer is simply to acquire a backup or starting level big by the deadline, and see what we can get out of Tatum this year. Most teams in the nba have wayyyy more holes in their team to fill than that, one of those holes is due to be plugged by a guy we already have in house, so it’s really just one thing we have to address.

How do we address it? In my eyes we can surely do so through one of these six guys I’m gonna list, one of them has to be available at the right price by the deadline, and all of them solve our issue in one way or another.

  1. Zubac: I’m gonna go most expensive to least, Zubac is a beast on a great contract and would immediately make us the best team in the east when Tatum comes back. White-brown-Walsh-Tatum-Zubac is one of the most disgusting defensive lineups I’ve ever seen on paper and we’d still be fielding 2 25-30 ppg scorers plus white and Zubac who are both 15-20 ppg scorers, that lineup is a monster. Simons would go in the trade and immediately be replaced off the bench by a better version of himself in Pritchard, bench also gets queta who is gonna be a monster backup 5. This makes us the best very easily by doing it but also costs the most; likely would take simons and two firsts, although I’d love to find a way to do it with simons/hauser/1 first, depends how the clips would value Hauser.

  2. Claxton: I think it’s pretty clear he’d be the next best option, he actually brings a couple things to the table that are interesting especially for us where he is a very good passer/ball handler for a 5, something we used to love using horford for in the past. More expensive contract but it’s descending, unclear how much he would cost but I can’t imagine it would be two firsts, simons/baylor/1 first feels pretty fair, whatever it is it’s cheaper than Zubac, just not quite as good of an option. Big reason I have him as a viable option this year and not in the past is because he seems to have fixed his free throw shooting; he was horrific earlier in his career from the line and is at 70% over 25 games, good signs

  3. Mark williams: I think mark would be the cutoff where he’s the last of the guys we could target who would still start over queta. The idea of mark williams has always been an amazing player, and he truly has been amazing for the suns- but we’ve got a few things going for us in regards to why they might give him up. Injury issues in the past, due to get paid this summer, and they’ve got 3 other centers who should be getting mins behind him including maluach, who they drafted to be their big of the future. I think we could probably get him for Hauser and our 2026 first, that feels possible and I don’t think that’s a terrible idea. Big long dunker shot blocker who is a decent free throw shooter, really only issue with him is the long term injury concern, but maybe queta looking so good insulates that?

  4. Mo Gueye: from here on out we are looking at backups for queta. this guy is an interesting player man, I would love to get him but it feels unlikely with how unreliable KP has been for Atlanta. He kind of does everything as a 6’9-10” 4⁄5 hybrid, definitely a Celtic dna big. I don’t think he would cost a first if he was available, either Baylor and 2nds or Hauser straight up feels fair to me, but like I said the price might be higher due to KP being unreliable, would leave them short on bigs

  5. Jalen smith: this one is super interesting simply because he gives us a serious stretch 5 option, and opens us up for more double big lineup. Not sure how motivated the bulls are to keep him, they have Collins/Vuc still but they’re a little light on center depth. Trade wise, Hauser for smith works perfectly straight up, Hauser would be a dynamite shooter next to giddey so maybe they’d like the long term fit? I like the idea of smith and think he could be an options

  6. Oso ighodoro: sorry if I misspelled the last name, I’m admittedly getting lazy because the post has been long. Oso is currently one of the 4 good playable centers in the phoenix rotation, and I think he’s a very interesting option. Could probably be had for something like Baylor/tillman/2 seconds, he hasn’t had a great year on paper but I think the potential is there, kinda like gueye without the shooting where he does a lot of the little things which is cool, I think he’s a serviceable backup option.

If we trade for a starting big:

white-brown-Walsh-Tatum-starter

Pritchard-Hauser(or simons)-Hugo-minott-queta

That’s a pretty damn good rotation, and I think that’s a real contender assuming we get most of a good version of Jayson Tatum. I’d be pretty damn confident in us to steamroll the easy with that rotation.

Thoughts?
