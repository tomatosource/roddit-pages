PSA: The Black Crow is still bugged in the Blooming Field map

He phases, goes invulnerable, and is supposed to break the door but something bugs him out (probably too much damage or bad timing on stun mechanics) so he goes invulnerable but doesn’t finish the phase. It bricks the map essentially, because you can’t portal out or log out (unless you wait for a good while so the instance times out). It’s either XP loss or waiting on instance reset.

There’s a post about this on the bug forum with quite a lot of people being affected by the bug for quite a while, just be careful if you’re running up against the Black Crow
