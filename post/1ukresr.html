Favorite Ballpark Of Another Team?

Alright, apparently, this one got removed by the mods of r/MLB for possible AI use. I clearly don’t use AI to write these questions down, so I have no idea why they thought I did. I’m hoping that won’t happen again, because the post was catching traction pretty quickly.

Now, I’m sure we all have a few ballparks that we’ve grown a soft spot for. And I’m sure we all have a favorite in the Major Leagues that our team doesn’t call home. So, what’s your favorite current MLB ballpark that your team doesn’t play at, and why?

For me, I’ve always loved the Juice Box. The place honestly looks more like an amusement park than a baseball stadium, and that’s definitely not a bad thing, I feel. Everything from the Crawford Boxes, the Archways, the Gas Pump, the big grass H wall in center, the party deck above it, the food court in right center, etc. It’s absolutely immaculate, and I’m honestly kinda jealous that the Astros get to play in such an awesome park (not that I don’t love Yankee Stadium too, though). I’d kill to see a game there, but I’d probably get flamed for repping the Yankees down in Houston.
