[OC] MVP & Awards Vote Tracker: SGA Leads Jokić with 16 of 24 public first place MVP votes

We’ve found nearly a quarter of the NBA’s first place votes for MVP and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has two-thirds of them: SGA 16 - Jokić 8

Each year I track the NBA MVP and awards by identifying the voters and sourcing their comments about the awards and their ballots. Thank you to all the crowdsourcing community members dishing out assists. I track all of this in a public spreadsheet:

**View the 2025 NBA MVP/Awards Tracker**

MVP Award

As of Thursday afternoon here’s the rundown of ballots we have found:

  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander: 16 first place votes
  • Nikola Jokić: 8 first place votes

A Trend: Every voter I have found is considering either SGA or Jokić for first and second place. There’s been no talk of another player occupying those spots on their ballots. This includes media members who have not stated their vote or preference, but discussed their deliberations.

Other Awards

  • Four guys look like a lock for All-NBA First Team across 15 public ballots: SGA, Jokić, Giannis, Tatum.
  • Lots of players look like they’ll make one of the three teams: LeBron James (say his name), Donovan Mitchell, Evan Mobley, Steph Curry, Anthony Edwards, Cade Cunningham. I don’t want to speculate more, but several others.
  • Defensive Player of the Year: This is really spread across 15 ballots. We need to see more. First place votes so far: Evan Mobley 5, Draymond Green 3, Dyson Daniels 3, Lu Dort 2, Amen Thompson 2. One small trend: Across 11 full ballots (votes 1 - 3) Mobley has only been left off one ballot. Getting left off is zero points and there’s only three spots on the ballot.
  • Most Improved: Ivica Zubac and Dyson Daniels each have 7 first place votes. Together they comprise 14 of the 16 identified first place votes.
  • Rookie of the Year: Stephon Castle has 13 of the 16 identified first place votes.
  • Sixth Man of the Year: Payton Pritchard has 13 of the 17 identified first place votes.
  • Coach of the Year: Kenny Atkinson has 13 of the 16 identified first place votes.

Voting Details

Votes are in. There was some confusion on when the deadline was going to be.

The NBA sent ballots to awards voters at 12:01 a.m. on Tuesday and they were due back at 6:00 p.m. (Eastern). Voting is done.

There’s no public info on when the awards will be announced (yet). In recent years the NBA has announced the three finalists for all the awards pretty quick. Then rolled out the awards day-by-day during the playoffs.

The league has to get some of the awards out because they have contract implications for teams to plan. Including possible implications for Jaren Jackson Jr., Cade Cunningham, and Evan Mobley (though I’m guessing teams have parsed this out by now).

See this from Bobby Marks in February:

Other players to watch include Memphis Grizzlies big man Jaren Jackson Jr. and Atlanta Hawks guard Trae Young. Both are supermax-eligible if either earns All-NBA or All-Defense. And, like Doncic, San Antonio Spurs guard De’Aaron Fox is ineligible because he was traded.

Detroit Pistons guard Cade Cunningham and Cleveland Cavaliers forward Evan Mobley could see their five-year, \(224 million extensions increase to \)269 million if either are named All-NBA or Defensive Player of the Year.

Methodology and some notes on how this works:

  • I do my best to identify who the media members with ballots are. This is either through their own statements or through an established voting history. Which I consider to be a media member who cast a vote for the 2025 All-Star Starters, 2024 NBA Awards, 2024 All-Star Starters, and are at the same publication/outlet as when they cast those ballots.
  • The voting pool will be 100 media members.
  • I source every vote. But a voter could have changed their mind between pre-voting public declaration and submitting their ballot. View the sources in the spreadsheet.
  • There’s a chance I get something wrong. I’m one guy listening to podcasts at 2x. My accuracy rate over the years is over 95% on identified voters and votes (better than that but I want to post this and not figure out the exact number). If you see something wrong, send me a note.
  • HELP ME: Send me any votes or possible votes you see. I keep this going until the awards are announced. Please include a timestamp or as much detail as you can. If Stan Van Gundy says during a playoff broadcast “I voted for PLAYER” send me a note like… Stan Van Gundy said he voted for PLAYER in the 3rd quarter of Rockets-Warriors with 8 minutes left.

A giant shout out to everyone who helps. I get so many tips. Please, send it all. I respond to everything I get. I’m never annoyed if its not actually a voter or vote.

This year lots of folks have sent me tips while saying they don’t want any credit or a shout out on the spreadsheet. You are appreciated and thank you.
