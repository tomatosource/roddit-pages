How would relegation impact the NBA?

In many sports leagues across the world, there is the top league, with all the best clubs, but also one or more leagues with weaker clubs. Unlike the NBA, however, the worst X clubs from the top league are actually relegated to the second league, while the best X teams from the second league fill in the empty spots in the top league.

Now, let us day that the cureent NBA expands to 32 teams, and plays the following season normally, with an added twist that 16 bottom teams would, starting from the next season, be a part of League 2, while the top 16 teams would be a part of League 1.

The NBA champion, of course, is the one who wins League 1.
The top (let us say, four) teams of League 2 are each year promoted to League 1, while the bottom four are relegated to League 2.

How would that impact the overall product that is the NBA?

Edit: League 2 would not have the playoffs – they would play a certain amount of regular season games, and the top 4 teams are promoted to League 1.

Edit 2: The draft would function similar to a normal one - a single draft for bot leagues.
