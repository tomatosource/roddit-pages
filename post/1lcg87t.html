Everyone knows who the most influential offensive players are in the last 20 years, but who do you think are the most influential defensive players?

The players that’ve influenced offense are pretty obvious. Players like Curry we can saw their impact on offenses, Harden we saw how his step-back three infiltrate the league, Dirk’s outside shooting on many bigs, and every other lottery pick says they emulated Paul George’s style.

Best ≠ influence, LeBron is the best of the generation, but players can’t replicate what he does so he hasn’t been as influential as those three. On the other hand tall SFs like Orlando’s Rashard Lewis & Hedo Turkoglu influenced how offenses used outside-creative 4-out lineups going forward.

But what about defenses? It’s harder to pinpoint because most players aren’t listing their defensive inspirations, and all-time defenders like Dikembe and Hakeem are more appreciated for their personal mentorship & offensive footwork rather than their place on defense.

Some picks that came to mind:

  1. Tony Allen: The small defensive specialist, extreme energy guy who stays in your chest all game and erratically jumps past screens and between assignments, reaching at the best time. Pat Bev, Lu Dort, Caruso, Smart, Bledsoe, Kris Dunn, Jalen Suggs come to mind. These have always existed, Nate McMillan is the prime example, but I don’t know many that weren’t also very good offensively (i.e. Mookie Blaylock, Moncrief, MJ, Payton, Stockton).
  2. Danny Green, Bruce Bowen, Shane Battier: This has more to do with changes in offenses, but the 3-and-D wing has been the most valued role player archetype in basketball for a decade now. Nothing specific but every team goes into the draft hoping to get Danny Green.
  3. Draymond Green: He’s lead some of the best defenses of the 21st century as a defense-focused small-ball 4⁄5 archetype in response to modern offenses. He lists Kurt Thomas (played until 2012) as his influence on defense and Boris Diaw as his offensive influence (Jokic also does). No team has really been able to replicate this super-small-ball defensive success, but a lot probably tried because of him. 2025 Jaylin Williams feels the closest.
