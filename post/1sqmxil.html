Yoav Modai reveals his ballots for end of season awards of the 2025-26 NBA season. He is one of 100 NBA journalists who get to vote for end of season awards.

Source

He is an Israeli reporter who is working for Sport5 (Israeli sports media)

MVP: Nikola Jokic

Most Improved player: Deni Avdija

Defensive Player of The Year: Victor Wembanyama

Coach of The Year: J.B. Bickerstaff

Rookie of The Year: Cooper Flagg

Sixth Man of The Year: Keldon Johnson

Clutch Player of The Year: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

All-NBA Teams:

All-NBA First Team:

Nikola Jokic, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Victor Wembanyama, Luka Doncic, and Cade Cunningham.

All-NBA Second Team:

Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, Kawhi Leonard, Kevin Durant, and Donovan Mitchell

All-NBA Third Team:

Jamal Murray, Deni Avdija, Jalen Johnson, Jalen Brunson, and Jalen Duren

NBA All-Defensive Teams:

All-NBA First Defensive Team:

Victor Wembanyama, Chet Holmgren, Ausar Thompson, Scottie Barnes, and Derrick White

All-NBA Second Defensive Team:

Stephon Castle, Cason Wallace, Rudy Gobert, Toumani Camara, and Dyson Daniels

NBA All-Rookie Teams:

All-Rookie First Team:

Cooper Flagg, Kon Knueppel, V.J. Edgecombe, Dylan Harper, and Maxime Raynaud

All-Rookie Second Team:

Derik Queen, Ace Bailey, Cedric Coward, Jeremiah Fears, and Egor Demin.
