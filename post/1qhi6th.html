For me, this league is so good, first time ever i have more than 300div, i know it nothing compare to the economy, but if you look at it in different angle, i have more than 300 rerolls to the stats.

  1. Temple has suck a high ceiling, stacking effectiveness gives the mob way stronger than in map.
