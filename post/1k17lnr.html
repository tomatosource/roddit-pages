Rare Items are unreliable in PoE2. This drives part of the ‘loot problem’.

In PoE1, there are some rough edges to start but despite this, starter items are more ‘reliable’.

Thanks to Scour + alt spam + regal + one crafted mod, in PoE1 you get a relatively reliable baseline.

The trade is fueled by the next ‘unreliable’ steps that make items rare and then finally the pinnacle of RNG is corrupting into something insane.

In PoE2, the baseline is unreliable. Essences are too rare to be reliable, despite technically offering reliability.

You find a good base, slap some currency onto it and congrats you have something with a stat profile that is almost always not the profile you need.

Trade is thus additionally fueled by the need to actually have some level of control over the stat profile you want which means it brings the ‘requirement’ to trade earlier into the game compared to PoE1.

This means that for the loop of gameplay, if you try to craft. You on average see more items in PoE2 than PoE1, because yes you do pick up more bases - but you also see more useless items.

PoE1 dropped too much loot to the point you ignored almost all of it, because it was bad - but in PoE2 you are checking almost all of it and manually acknowledging it is bad.

There is less loot dropping but a byproduct of the system is we see more useless loot than when we were just ignoring it. Even loot that is half decent can be not worth the risk of exalting rather than just saving up to trade with, so it can feel like another ‘bad’ drop.

Runes do help a bit, especially in the campaign but at some point you really want to build out the items themselves.
All the endgame crafting tools that would technically bring you close to a similar baseline as PoE1 require tons of much slower maps to get to a level of crafting reliability you generally have by Act 5 in PoE1.

I’m not saying this requires a revert to PoE1, alt spam isn’t really fun but I think PoE2 needs to look at how to bring in some control over the stat profile at an earlier point in the game. Ideally in the core game so we can roll into maps with some reliable options.

TL:DR; the baseline crafting in PoE1 has agency but in PoE2 only the grindy endgame crafting has agency, making crafting feel non-existent to most players.
