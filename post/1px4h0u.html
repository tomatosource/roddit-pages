PSA: “Map contains an additional Essence” and “Areas with Powerful Map Bosses contain an additional Essence” nullify your %Increased chance to have an Essence.

TL;DR
Do not mix and match “Map contains an additional Essence” tablet mods with “Map has %increased chance to contain an Essence”.
Either only use tablets with 1-2 guaranteed essences with the Boss atlas node, or tablets with increased Essence chance without the Boss atlas node.

Detailed bug report: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3897486

If you have the Crystal Realm Boss atlas passive point allocated or you are using a tablet with “Map contains an additional Essence” suffix, then your X% increased chance to contain Essence inside the map (from tablets and atlas passive tree) become worthless.

They seem to be coded as “Map contains X Essence/Shrine” instead of “an additional” where X now is an unscalable value.

This is also the case for Shrines, Azmeri spirits and Strongboxes.

10 maps with Crystal Realm (Areas with Powerful Map Bosses contain an additional Essence) allocated and 450% increased essence chance in map:
Essence found: min: 1; max: 1; average: 1

10 maps without Crystal Realm allocated and 460% increased Essence chance in map:
Essence found: min: 0; max: 4; average: 1,3

Read the bug report forum post for more info.

This is in no way a definitive test, but I have run over a hundred maps with similar setups without collecting data to say there is definitely an issue here.

Happy grinding Exiles.
