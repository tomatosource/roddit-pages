[Almanza] Ajay Mitchell to return from 20-game absence against Nuggets

source

Help has arrived. Ajay Mitchell is no longer listed on the injury report. He’ll be available for the Oklahoma City Thunder as they host the Denver Nuggets in a highly-anticipated matchup on Monday.

Mitchell had missed 20 consecutive games with an abdominal strain and ankle sprain. The month-plus-long absence had folks worried about his recovery process. Enjoying a breakout season, the Thunder have gained back one of their best scorers.

Mitchell has averaged 14.1 points on 48.7% shooting, 3.7 assists and 3.5 rebounds in 43 games. He’s shot 35% from 3 on 3.2 attempts.

Will be interesting to see how Mitchell slides back into OKC’s rotation. It’ll be the first time the Thunder will have Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Mitchell and Jared McCain suited up. He last played on Jan. 21 in a win over the Milwaukee Bucks.
