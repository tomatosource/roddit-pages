Why can’t unique items scale with content?

Uniques have a lot of issues in my opinion. 99% of them are practically unusable and I’m not even sure how useful they are in meme builds.

A simple fix (at least in my hare brain) would be to allow the stats (and drop rarity) to scale with your character.

This means during the campaign they might drop a bit more plentifully, and then at endgame the higher level ones that scale really well stat wise could be much harder to find but actually maybe be useful more often (I’m not saying BiS just saying maybe we don’t leave them on the ground every time.)

Tldr; I might not be explaining this with the proper terminology but basically uniques get much better at endgame and are harder to find.
