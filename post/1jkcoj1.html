Shai Gilgeous-Alexander joins Kareem Abdul-Jabbar (1972) as the only players to score 32+ PPG and win 60+ games in a season

There have been 8 other 30+ PPG & 60+ win seasons in NBA history

2018 James Harden 30.4 PPG 65-17

2016 Stephen Curry 30.1 PPG 73-9

1996 Michael Jordan 30.4 PPG 72-10

1992 Michael Jordan 30.1 PPG 67-15

1991 Michael Jordan 31.5 PPG 61-21

1973 Kareem Abdul-Jabbar PPG 30.2 60-22

1972 Kareem Abdul-Jabbar PPG 34.8 63-19

1971 Kareem Abdul-Jabbar PPG 31.7 66-16

7 out of 8 of these won MVP in the same season except for 1973 Kareem who finished 2nd.
