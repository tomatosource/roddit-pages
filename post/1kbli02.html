The new curse in basketball: winning the NBA Cup means a first-round exit in the playoffs

The Los Angeles Lakers defeated the Indiana Pacers to win the NBA Cup in 2023-24 but lost in the first round of the playoffs 4-1 to the Denver Nuggets.
