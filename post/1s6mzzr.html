Sam “Howitzer” Hauser vs Jordan “Half-Brother Jay W” Walsh

It’s the middle of a really hot afternoon here where I am right now, and I am so baked, that’s why I’m making a post.

Tough conversation, but it’s coming sooner or later.

If the NBA expands and teams are forced to lock in 8 players, we’re going to have some real decisions to make. As much as I respect what Sam Hauser has done, going from undrafted to a legit rotation shooter, this might be where things shift.

Jordan Walsh is still raw, but honestly not as raw as we think. He can knock down 3s, defend at a high level, and you can see how eager he is to attack the basket. It feels like he’s just waiting for more touches to really show his full bag.

On top of that, Hauser is 100% getting a bigger bag, especially from a newly contending team that needs elite shooting. He’s slightly above average defensively, very smart, and as a shooter he’s much better than most typical 3&D guys like Jimmy Neutron from the Pistons. That’s exactly why he’s going to be paid, and I am very much looking forward to that for him.

But with how stacked our wing rotation is, it’s going to be tough to justify keeping him over a younger, cheaper player with more upside.

It’s not about who’s better today, it’s about what gives us more value moving forward.

And that’s why this decision is so damn hard.
