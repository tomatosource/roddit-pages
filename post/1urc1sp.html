Suggestion: Make animal spirits work like the verisium sentries

As the tittle says, I believe spirits would be more fun if they worked the same way verisium sentries works.

They would latch on to you and orbit around you, maybe visibly as a tiny version of an animal. Maybe in a way similar to the Celestial Cat Pet

Then whenever you walk, the spirit buffs enemies around you and then merges together with either the first rare or boss you encounter. And if you have the strongbox node, it empowers the first strongbox you click.

If we don’t want to give players the ability to control which enemy it possess, maybe simply light up a pat with a glowing path towards the “chosen” enemy to guide you there if you want. If you don’t go there, the spirit runs out after empowering X power of enemies, exactly like the sentry.

This would of course need an entire rework of the mechanic, but I believe it would make spirits much more fun. Thoughts?
