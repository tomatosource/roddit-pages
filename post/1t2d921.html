Jaylen’s press conference was weird and a bad look.

A couple paraphrased quotes:

  1. I didn’t find out Tatum wasn’t playing until about 45 minutes before the game.

I, a person who doesn’t play for the Celtics, got the news a full two hours before tipoff. What do you mean you didn’t hear it until 45 minutes before? He just made it up to make it sound like there was poor communication? Bizarre

  1. This was one of my the favorites years I’ve had.

Brother, you got bounced in the first round for the first time in your career? You had a 3-1 lead on a 7 seed and couldn’t close it out. And this was one of your favorite years? A rational person would conclude this means Jaylen cares more about his numbers than winning. But idk if we can be rational right now.

  1. I like the style we played tonight. It’s faster and more free than we’ve played in the previous games.

You mean the games you won? The games where you didn’t just get to take 27 shots and lose? That’s the style you like? Again, he’s telling everyone he cares more about himself than he does winning.

  1. Embiid is a big body just flopping around

Im just sick of him complaining about flopping. He tries to use it as a get out of jail free card. In his head, he think he plays “ethically”. So he should get the benefit of the doubt.

I will 100% give him credit for playing his hardest this year, but you’re up 3-1 and you can’t be the guy to win a playoff game against the Sixers? And then you talk like this? Rough ending.
