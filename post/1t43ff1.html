Trade Proposal

Now that the offseason is upon us I have been thinking about what we could do to improve the team with the goals of:

  1. Addressing a significant concern that is getting a real center (and stop fuckin around with “raw” or “aged” talent)

  2. Staying under the luxury tax. There is no world in which this isn’t the guiding principal for Brad this offseason, otherwise we never would’ve gotten under it in the first place

  3. Keeping the core together. I know most of the casuals think something is wrong with White/Brown or both, but there is no meaningful return we can get for these guys that changes it for us. The reality is Brown had a down series and white has a down year, but assuming Tatum is healthy next year, I still believe in this core.

The salary cap is roughly \(201MM and we have \)181.7MM on the books so those are our constraints.

Looking across the league to find someone that falls within the \(20MM and represents a meaningful upgrade with upside is challenging, so let’s say we unload Hauser for a second rounder. That frees up about \)10.9MM giving us ~$30MM to play with as far as the cap is concerned.

The challenge is in order to use as much of the cap possible to bring in someone we need to aggregate salaries which gets pretty messy considering we would have to send out PP.

The best alternative with the goals (constraints) outlined above would be to look toward the Bucks who, for all intents and purposes, are going to be sellers.

I’m sure many of you have your hot takes on Myles Turner, but he could be the key to remaining competitive next year, keeping the core together (the Jays, White and PP), and retaining future flexibility by staying below the cap for a second year and resetting the repeater tax.

The caveats are:

  1. This requires Giannis getting traded. Otherwise the Bucks say fuck no. But it’s an inevitability at this point.

  2. Turner waives a portion of his 15% trade kicker. He is making ~\(26.6mm and we would need him to waive just enough to fit into the \)27.7MM TPE (which can be rounded up to 27.95MM)

  3. We send perhaps a 1st and a 2nd plus what we get from the Hauser trade. OR we include Hauser in the trade if the Bucks want him. You can’t aggregate contracts with the TPE but if the TPE is used for a single player I believe this still works.

This way, Milwaukee gets to continue resetting with picks (something they desperately need after the failed Dame experiment) and perhaps gets a great shooter in the process that they can flip for more pick(s). Or we could include a higher upside guy like Hugo to sweeten the deal.

The Bucks would be selling low on Turner but he had his worst season last year since he was a rookie. They would also get Turner off the books who probably doesn’t want to be there at this point anyway.

The Celtics get a legitimate center (at least compared to what we have) who can space the floor and open driving lanes for the Jays. And they stay well below the cap meanwhile preserving future flexibility. We would still have a few mil left over and could snag someone like Simons on the cheap (though I bet he gets a small bag this summer)

Side note: I remember when he torched us like two years ago and I still think that version of Turner exists.

Feel free to share better ideas but if this could workout all caveats considered it keeps our core, keeps us competitive next year, and preserves the future (aside from giving up a few picks in the process).
