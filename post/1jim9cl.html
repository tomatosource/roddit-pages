I’m working on a PoE 2 auction app – AMA about it

Hey everyone, I’ve been tinkering with a cross-platform mobile app for PoE 2 auction system in my free time. I love the game (with 4000 hours on Steam and God knows how many on PS) but generally dislike how trade is organized, so this is my shot at making it better. It’s still an early work in progress, but I figured I’d drop by and chat about it. The app already has features mirroring the trade site: search, filters, a watch list for tracking the lowest prices based on your filters, and direct in-game whispers if you’re logged into PoE 2 and authorized with the trade site. I’m planning to release it on the Windows Store (for PC and Xbox), Google Play, and hopefully iOS down the line.

Before release, I plan to include things like notifications when the price for specific items or filters hits your target, item price evaluation, finding similar items on the market, and maybe even a self-hosted option for offline trading, hot item price tracking, LFG, item requests, and other stuff. No promises on timelines yet, just seeing what’s doable.

I’m curious what you all think. What features would you actually use in an app like this? Anything you’d want to see added? I’ll stick around for a bit to answer questions if you’ve got any. Cheers!
