New player having a lot of fun with POE2 but Act 3 can absolutely suck one

Hopefully Act 4 is better because I’ve felt that the campaign took a nose dive with Act 2 and then self immolated with Act 3. I don’t think that any bosses I fought in Act 3 are well design either.
