Celtics fan from Madrid here: Dont be fooled by Hugo González stats

Celtics and Real Madrid fan here. Im making this post because I’ve seen a lot of criticism surrounding this pick.

Hugo is a pretty good prospect who didnt got many minutes because top Euro teams like Madrid play only with vets (mainly ex-NBA players) so unless you are generational like Doncic right of the gate, you dont see that many minutes. Specially if they know you’re leaving to the NBA

Like i said, Hugo has the potential to be a versatile point forward who can put the ball on the floor, with a high motor (he defends his ass off every possession), crafty finishing and great vision are there that give him a nice ceiling.

Im concerned about his shooting, it needs work, and its gonna dictate if he can stick around or not, up until this point he is been quite streaky outside the paint. He also has to speed up his decision making to be a playmaker in the next level.

He might pan out or not, but i dont think his stats or minutes tell anything relevant. Because 99% of Euroleague top teams young players get this treatment.
