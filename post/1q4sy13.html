Do we need to change Tiered loot again?

GGG is all about ground loot not being dead, however with us having better crafting options, Tier 1-5 random rares are being filtered out in most end game loot filters unless it is a really good item base.

What if Tiered dropped were reduced but buffed so it becomes a semi-chase item with yellow loot. What if tiered rare loot was changed to guaranteed a T1 roll on the item per level of the item.

So a T1 bow would have 1 random T1 Prefix or Suffix and could drop pretty commonly. A T5 Rare bow would have 5 T1 Rolls and could be very rare, like less rare than a T0 Unique but more rare than divines. I think this would bring back excitement to a mid to late season rare item drop and give players a reason to look forward to rare drops.
