Turning a dumb oldschool baseball game into a fun online league for those interested.

https://preview.redd.it/gcdnphssns9g1.jpg?width=3024&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=6d3f8458c7bb844fde9d0d42d8d38b6ba0c001c9

So I recently got this game (I used to play a version of it when I was a kid). I’m currently bored with the flu, but I also have a decent bit of free time throughout the day, because I work remote from home and make my own schedule.

Point being, I want to make individual players for this game, using the outcomes at the top of the actual board as a baseline. So I’ll alter the outcomes to make players better or worse, power or average hitters, etc. Then I’ll make up teams with these players.

If people are interested, I’d like to make a small league through discord, where you GM a team. You would submit your lineups and then I stream a few games a day for each team or something (in discord chat, not YouTube or anything). If people are really interested, I’m happy to put a little more effort into it and have team budgets with like free agency and stuff and really have some silly funny.

So let me know if this stupid endeavor is something that tickles your baseball nerd fancy.
