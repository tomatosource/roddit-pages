Still thinking about the 24 new boss in 0.3.0

The stream yesterday has been stuck in my head ever since. There was a ton of information packed in, and the boss showcase was easily the highlight for me.

Pretty sure I’d seen some of that concept art before in the artbook, the one with fangs, bone armor, and those tribal elements. It looked cool on the page, but seeing it fully realized in-game was something else. The boss in the cave especially gave off a really intimidating vibe, very similar to Tai Lung!

I really like the direction they’re taking with this mix of harsh and mysterious design. The visuals, the atmosphere, and the way the environment ties into the fights all felt on point. It’s exactly the kind of tribal aesthetic I was hoping they’d lean into.
