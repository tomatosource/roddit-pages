Movement speed on Boots idea

I believe that POE2 can do better in dealing with movement speed than it being a mandatory affix on boots. I’ve seen a lot of feedback about it being an implicit, but I think it is such an important stat in ARPGs that it should be managed differently.

What about boots having a mechanic more like quality. It could have special orbs that drop allowing you to increase it that GGG can tweak the drops rates on to balance speed throughout the game.

They could either increase speed by 1% each, or they could also tier them like they’ve done with other currency. So the lessor ones increase movement but cap out at 15% the normals at 25% and the highest tier up to 35%.

I’m sure it would add more flexibility to what they can do with movement speed in the game and make us look at all the boot affix to make decisions. Right now if no movement speed, or just speed less than what you have now, the rest doesn’t matter and you toss it. With this you could consider taking one with less speed knowing you can push it up over time.
