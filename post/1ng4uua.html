2016 was the Greatest All Around NBA Season of the Century

My mates and I were discussing the best NBA seasons of our lifetimes and came to the conclusion that 2016 stands alone.

Obviously, everyone remembers the playoffs. Warriors come back down 3-1 over KD and the Thunder, Cavs come back down 3-1 to the Warriors, Kyrie’s shot, LeBron’s block, etc. It was cinema, but when you go down the rabbit hole, the season just gets better and better.

  • Steph and the Warriors win 73 games— the most all time
  • Steph wins unanimous MVP— the only unanimous MVP ever
  • Kawhi led the Spurs to 67 wins and a 11.1 Net rating— the third best Net Rating ever (to that point)
  • KD in his prime goes up 3-1 on the legendary Warriors
  • Kobe & Duncan’s farewell tours
  • Aaron Gordon v Zach LeVine dunk contest
  • Dwyane Wade’s last real playoff runs. He had a few great throwback games
  • DeAndre Jordan first team All-NBA over Chis Paul (NBA2)— lol it’s just always funny to hear

2009 is also pretty awesome with LeBron v Kobe v Wade for MVP, but 2016 is the best, most entertaining and historic season of my lifetime. Is it the best of all time? Which other seasons deserve contention?
