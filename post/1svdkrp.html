The Rui Hachimura trade is one of the best smaller-medium trades in recent years

I was a certified Rui hater when he was in Washington and thought the Lakers trade for him was dumb and grasping at straws during what was another disaster season with Russ. Mostly because i didn’t see a mediocre defending, poor shooting, poor playmaking forward as a key piece on a serious playoff team. Since getting to the Lakers Rui has become a really solid (albeit lower volume) shooter, efficient overall scorer and has improved on defense. He’s also been automatic from 3 in the playoffs and has proven to be a good postseason piece.

The lakers traded Kendrick Nunn and a bunch of second rounders for someone that has been a key rotational piece in 4 straight postseasons.
