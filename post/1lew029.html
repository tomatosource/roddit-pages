With the Lakers selling for a record $10 billion today, what does this mean for future NBA expansion team valuations?

As we’re all aware, The Buss family just agreed to sell majority ownership of the Lakers to Dodgers owner Mark Walter for $10 billion - the largest sports franchise sale in history.

This got me thinking about expansion. The NBA has been talking about adding teams in cities like Las Vegas and Seattle. If an established franchise like the Lakers is worth $10B, what should we expect expansion teams to cost?

For context:

  • The last NBA expansion was Charlotte in 2004 for $300M
  • Recent expansion fees: NHL Seattle Kraken paid \(650M (2021), MLS teams now cost \)500M+
  • Vegas has been heavily rumored as the top expansion candidate

A few things came to mind:

  • Should expansion teams cost $5-6 billion given this Lakers valuation?
  • How much would you pay for a Vegas expansion team right now?
  • Does this $10B sale price indicate the NBA is more valuable than we thought, or is this Lakers-specific premium?

Curious what everyone thinks about how this record sale impacts expansion economics going forward.
