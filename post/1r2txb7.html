Poe/Poe 2 could really benefit from a Horadric Cube style interface for Crafting.

Watching the Diablo 4 reveal this actually caught my eye, as they’re adding Crafting for pretty much the first time in that game, but they’re doing it with a UI system which is pretty well Explained.

https://preview.redd.it/07oybv3aj2jg1.jpg?width=1920&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=d547fffe6f54b24fae40dcb100f2c1291180af3f

One of the major barriers to entry for both PoE games is the lack of Explanation when it comes to crafting.

I’d love to see a similar optional interface that players can open which helps to explain what different crafting currencies achieve.

I’d like it to contain two boxes like the one above and a “Currency Box”.

Box 1 - Shows the Item is where you place the item you want to Craft on

Box 2 - Shows the potential outcome of the Craft

“Currency Box” - Is where you place the Currency you’re trying to Craft with.

In a way I guess it would appear similar visually to the Recombinator, just with an extra box for the Currency to go into and a line or two of basic explanation.

For example.

You place your Rare ring with 4 affixes in Box 1.

You place your “Exalted Orb” in the “Currency Box”.

Box 2 shows your item with a “Adds New Affix” line on the item.

You can then Click the “Craft” button to go ahead with the Craft.

I need to make it clear that this obviously doesn’t stop you doing your usual Inventory crafting in any way, but I do feel that this would be HUGE for newer players to have this option.

In time I’d even like the system to be expanded to show all possible outcomes from the craft in game so we don’t need to tab out for 3rd party tools, that even us experienced players need to do every now and again.
