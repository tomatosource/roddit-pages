Do you think the 2025 Celtics are being held accountable?

This is a season that most, if not all, Celtics fans predicted and expected to end in a repeat and talk of a flourishing dynasty. It just ended in a loss to the New York Knicks. A team that most, if not all, Celtics fans believed to be vastly inferior. Yet the discourse locally, as soon as Tatum went down in Game 4, instantly shifted to next year and future considerations. As the discourse nationally, at the same time, pivoted to the Knicks and how they pulled off such an empathic upset.

But the reality is the Celtics were unequivocally outplayed by a significantly inferior team. Why? It’s not because of injuries. When Tatum went down they were about to go down 3-1 despite Tatum having a career game. That is a significant choke and it seems there’s been very little introspection among the team, media, and fanbase as to how that choke materialized and what should be done to improve this team.

It’s frustrating that the only solution that has materialized is some form of tanking. Did we not just watch the Knick steam roll through a superior opponent? Why is next year instantly a lost cause without Tatum? Yes, there are financial considerations for the new ownership group. Why are we, as fans, allowing ownership dismantling this supposedly Championship level team as the only option? Why is it only acceptable for this team to fluctuate between front running favorite that can do no wrong or bottom of the barrel strategic tanker?

There’s no reason this team shouldn’t be a contender next year. But that would require an analysis of their approach, their coaching, their franchise philosophies that just simply does not seem to be taking place. It seems so much easier and convenient to throw your hands up and retreat into a narrative of unavoidable, unfortunate loss and endlessly hopeful world building for the future. Where is the determination and resolve to overcome? Why does success need to line up perfectly at attention and wink at this iteration of the Celtics? Why is it admissible for them to double down on a philosophical approach that has no history of succeeding even as it predictably sinks them prematurely?

In short, why aren’t the 2025 Celtics being held accountable?
