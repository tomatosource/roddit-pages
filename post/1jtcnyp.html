Dear GGG, no one checks out new leagues to play the campaign. It doesn’t matter how entertaining you make the campaign. It’s still a chore. We tolerate it only to play our builds in the endgame.

The “Large Area Sizes” section on the latest “What We’re Working On” post showcases the devs’ deafness to the almost overwhelming amount of negative campaign feedback.

In POE 1, the most fun part of the game is a build finally coming together and being able to be played in the endgame. Most people do not find the campaign compelling or fun because the game isn’t about the campaign or lore, it’s literally about how the skills that you’ve chosen can clear content of the highest level.

No one is doing the campaign just to get the occasional rare ILVL 40 piece of gear. So why die on this hill for this 20 hour campaign, and take a WINNING formula for your company and completely warp it and distort it into this?

I’ll put it so equivocally that not a single GGG dev will be able to be confused. No one likes the campaign. It is a chore. The campaign isn’t fun in its current state. It is a chore. The campaign is not why people play POE or POE2. We want to play our builds.

Shorten zones, make normal-magic monster life almost trivial, buff player’s early game damage, leave bosses approximately the same, and let people get to end game as fast as they can so that they can start experimenting with fun builds to play.
