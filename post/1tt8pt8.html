Most end game changes are really good, not sold on the fully allocable main tree

(I am overall VERY pleased by the 0.5 update, kudos to GGG)

- Fully allocable mechanics tree is a good idea, I think. If I want to main Ritual, I’m expecting to max out all the points.

- Fully allocable shared tree: not a fan of this, it’s currently not very interesting, “choices” are mostly non-choices, and I do not want a bajillion rogue exiles in all my maps. I would prefer having to make choices in what I allocate, how I path around, and how I synergize with secondary mechanics.

- Also, no atlas respec is really rough for early trial and error

Why not pick the best of both worlds: fully allocable mechanics tree, and maybe 100ish points to allocate in the main tree (why not introduce loadouts like in PoE). Maybe beef up/make some passives more interesting/unique in compensation?
