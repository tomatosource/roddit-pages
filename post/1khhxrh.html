Is this the first documented case of team-wide yips?

Basketball is all about confidence. You gotta believe you’ll make the shot. Once that self doubt creeps in, you’re screwed. That’s the trouble with losing Game 1 in humiliating fashion where each missed shot compounds on itself and the weight of every subsequent attempt is just ever so slightly rattling that you throw off your shot.

This team has proven time and time again that when things start trending downward for them, it will get as bad as bad can get. Whether they miss 100 three point attempts trying to chase some single game record or let an inferior Heat team sneak past them in the ECF, they have consistently failed the mental challenges.

Once it happens once… it’s easy for that self-doubt and self-fulfilling failure prophecy to come true again. You could see it happening Game 2 in real-time. That simply does NOT happen if they didn’t also get humiliated in Game 1, but you know for certain that every single attempt as that lead started to dwindle, every single player was like “oh fuck here we go again” … And you gotta believe that’s going to carry over into each game.

Team-wide yips in back-to-back games. It’s actually remarkable. Books will be written about this mental collapse by prominent New York publishers for decades to come.

With the Miami series they carried that failure into 3 straight games until everyone wrote off the season as dead and they no longer had pressure to perform. The changed stakes allowed them to rattle off a few surprising wins and make it series. Maybe the same thing happens here. Or maybe the reality of that MIami series just causes them to fold this time around.
