Celtics are taking a gap year and that’s fine, good even

Boston has no chance at winning a title or making a competitive playoff run without a healthy Jayson Tatum. None, zero, zilch, nada. It’s not happening. Anfernee Simons is a no brainer acquisition, he will either be bad and Boston can let him walk and open up close to 30 Million in cap space or he will be good and they can re sign or trade him in February. This is a Danny Ainge style soft tank in motion, there are no frontcourt guys out there that Boston can get at anything close to a reasonable price that will make them competitive this year and not completely fuck their cap sheet long term. They are not gonna outright punt games, but Garza, Niang, and Queta will be a bad frontcourt and that is the point. They don’t have a competitive roster right now, but this is the best shot Boston will have to get back into the lottery for likely the majority of Tatum’s career. Coming into 2026 with a healthy Tatum, a solid pick in a loaded draft, and 30 ish million in cap room with Brown and White still on long term deals sets Boston up to get right back into the mix. Embrace the soft tank, let Neemias Queta blowing rotations and Jordan Walsh bricking threes wrap you up like a warm hug at the end of a hard day. bask in the glow of anfernee simons shooting 8⁄31 and know that soon all will be right again.
