Fred VanVleet was roasted for “throwing up a wild airball trying to draw a foul” while down 3 with 10 seconds left. The L2M report says he was fouled.

The NBA’s Last Two Minute report says that VanVleet was fouled before the 3PA:

Camara (POR) moves laterally into VanVleet’s (HOU) path and creates body contact that affects VanVleet’s SQBR on the perimeter. The contact occurs before VanVleet’s upward shooting motion.

This explains why he threw up the shot–getting 3 FTA instead of 2 would be huge in that situation.
