Criticism of Harden’s defense should not minimize Brunson’s offense

There’s been a huge amount posts and comments on ECF G1 blasting Harden’s poor defense and Atkinson’s coaching choices. As true as some of that might be, I would encourage people to watch the highlights more closely, particularly the possessions that started the Knicks comeback.

Brunson was making some tough contested buckets that few other players would. If it were most other players, Harden’s defense would almost have been just enough, forcing the ball handler into contested looks that could easily look like bad shots if they rimmed out.

But this is Jalen Brunson. He makes these tough buckets look like easy layups when he’s on. There was notably a sweet running layup that Brunson banked over Dean Wade where Harden wasn’t involved, and of course many later buckets came from a weak Harden-Merill trap where geriatric James was forced to run halfway across the court to cover Shamet.

The point is that we can point out Harden’s deficiencies all we want, but that should absolutely not overshadow Brunson’s efforts and the clutch shots from other Knicks.
