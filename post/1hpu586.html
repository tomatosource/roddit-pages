Today is the One-year anniversary of the Detroit Pistons ending their historic losing streak

On December 30th, 2023 the Detroit Pistons beat the Toronto Raptors 129-127 at home to end their all time terrible 28 game losing streak. In this game Cade Cunningham had 30/12/3 and Pascal Siakam had 35/5/4.

Also on this day, the raptors traded away OG Anunoby to the New York Knicks , so the Raptors were short handed in this game. If they made this trade just a day later, we could’ve potentially saw the first ever 30+ game losing streak in American professional sports history(the NFL team Chicago Cardinals currently hold the record with 29 straight losses over multiple seasons). This pistons have certainly gotten better since then, they fired famous terrorist Monty Williams and are currently 14-18, 11 games better than their pathetic 3-29 record at this point of the season last year.
