My personal experiance comparison from playing a melee class in POE1 compared to POE2

I played a boneshatter juggernaut barbarian in POE1 and I’m playing a warrior in POE2 and I’m enjoying POE2 A LOT MORE! The whole system of blocking with your sheild, dodging attacks and having much more skilled combat in POE2 is so much more fun for me. Weapon switching matters, there’s more skill, there’s a lot less spam and it’s a lot more fun. In POE1 I felt like I only had two choices for juggernaut builds, either a boneshatter one ability spammy or a righteous fire juggernaut which is just running around and auto killing everything in a fire aura not not using my weapons at all. I didn’t like either of them at all. I had to sacrifice some damage to add some fire abilities to the boneshatter build to make it slightly fun.

I also didn’t like that weapon switching in POE1 was completely pointless and useless. In POE2 weapon switching is a lot more useful and you can have two skill trees, one for each weapon slot. Also shields in POE1 just added an armor stat, but shields in POE2 can block damage from the front, which is so awesome. I feel like I’m playing a top down Dark Souls when playing the warrior.
