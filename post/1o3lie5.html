Reforging is insanely good, why only act 3 ?

Yesterday I’ve noticed the reforging bench, so I’ve decided to give it a try. I had before collected some magic weapons with good modifiers, upgraded some with regal orbs to see if they were worth it, but stopped there because exalted orbs are very uncommon in the campaign. So I upgraded the to gold to reforge them, and the result was insane. A gold weapon with 6 modifiers, combination of the best ones.

It’s so much cheaper than using 3 exalted orbs, only need orbs I can get through disenchanting, and is less random because I can just buy or collect magic weapons with good modifiers, even upgrade normal ones, instead of praying that the gold modifiers will match what I need. Now the merchant that sells items with randomized characteristics makes much more sense

It feels weird that the game waits for the end of act 3 to drop this insane crafting tools, and without any explanation. I had to check on internet to even know how it appeared.
