So it’s me the guy who chance white belts

Hey everyone! It’s me again.

Yesterday evening was honestly pretty rough. I had to stay up way longer after work just to farm enough divines for 120 belts and chance orbs, which is what I’ve been doing every evening. Belt prices went up, and on top of that I died on a boss that dropped 2 fragments worth around 400 exalts each, and I couldn’t pick them up. So yeah, it was a painful one.

Another thing is that I haven’t upgraded my build at all since day one of the league, because everything I farmed went straight into chancing belts. I’m still playing in gear from day two of the league. Here’s my current setup, in case anyone is interested:

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/Shiftzxc-7053/runesofaldur/character/shiftzxcmanjiro

Also, the Twister gameplay itself is pretty specific, especially in Abyss, because the little tornadoes don’t go through the cracks, and it gets really annoying.

So what am I trying to say? I guess yesterday was the last straw. I’m really tired of doing this, and I don’t really feel like upgrading this build either. So I’ll probably either stop playing, or try to make a new build and actually start improving it.

Yeah, I give up. I admit it. I’m giving up.

Thank you to everyone who followed my tiny little story and wrote funny or supportive comments. I tried to answer every question I could, and I’ll keep replying when I can.

And one last thing. I was planning to tell this story when I finally chanced a HH and gave it away, but I guess that’s not happening, so I’ll say it now.

This whole time, I was playing on a Steam Deck with a mouse and keyboard connected to it, averaging around 35 FPS because i don’t have a PC. With my story, I wanted to show that anyone who loves the game and keep going  can farm a lot of currency, and maybe even chance a HH, even with pretty bad hardware.

That’s it.

Once again, thank you to the few people who actually read everything I wrote, told their wives about me — yes, apparently that happened — or thought about me whenever a belt dropped for them. All of that honestly warms my soul and gives me nothing but good emotions.

That’s all.

Stay safe, never gamble.

Your belt guy
