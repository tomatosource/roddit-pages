The NBA’s Golden Eras for each position

The 1990s was the “Golden Era for Centers” because it featured Hakeem, David Robinson, Shaq, Ewing, Alonzo Mourning and Mutombo on a nightly basis.

Duncan, KG & Dirk battled for the title of the GOAT Power Forward during the “Golden Era of Power Forwards” of the 2000s. This trio also had to play against Chris Webber, Pau Gasol, Amar’e & Rasheed Wallace.

The 2000s was the “Golden Era of Shooting Guards” with Kobe, Iverson, D-Wade, T-Mac, Vince Carter & Ray Allen.

The 2010s was the Golden Era for playmaking forwards and guards.

The 2010s was the “Golden Era of Small Forwards” with LeBron, Durant, Melo, Kawhi & Paul George.

The 2010s was the “Golden Era of Point Guards” with Curry, CP3, Lillard, Westbrook, Harden & Kryie.

To clarify what I meant by “Golden Era” is the most competitive era with Hall-of-Fame caliber players in their prime.

If you disagree then pick a different “Golden Era” in the NBA for said position.
