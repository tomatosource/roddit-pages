My 2 cents on units and game difficulty from level 92 bleed amazon

Colossus units are horribly overtuned as white mobs. They deal excessive damage, cover huge areas with AOE, take up too much space, and leave you with no real defensive options. They charge up abilities from halfway across the screen and constantly send shockwaves, making melee playstyles nearly unviable.

Explode-on-death minions often leave DOT floors that deal far too much damage — especially some of the fanatic units.

Enemies begin prepping abilities the instant you enter their range, which feels unfair and leaves no room for reaction.

No unit should have over 50% block chance. I spent 20 seconds trying to blink behind a Faridon shield unit just to land a single hit.

Doedre’s exploding circles detonate way too fast.

The explosions from periodic effects shouldn’t persist for an extra second after the timer ends. It completely breaks player trust and spikes stress because you assume you’re safe — then boom, you’re dead.

Fighting transcendent units makes me want to twist my balls.

Unearthed Urchins ignore evasion with their jump attack. I will not touch Expedition until that’s fixed.

Rubberbanding is also a real problem. For example, the Queen of Filth—a massive unit—will rubberband you back into danger when she moves at high speeds. Even if you dodge her properly, the game will still reposition you to where you should have been hit.

If your build is based on blocking or evading, forget it. Many attacks outright ignore these defenses, rendering half your items pointless. Defensive mechanics are fundamentally flawed, and instead of improving them, the devs just add more replacement gimmicks.

Armor went from doing nothing to being the only thing keeping you alive—because everything now does 10,000 physical damage. Without armor, you get instantly deleted.
