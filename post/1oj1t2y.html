Currently preparing for the upcoming PoE1 league and immediately notice a major difference I hope PoE2 adapts: Portals open instantly.

In PoE1, you can press the portal button (or use a portal scroll if you’re felling nostalgic today) in maps and immediately see the portal opening. No cast time. Just press and go.

And this is A LOT more satisfying, especially with all the portal MTX that have good opening animations. Those just hit different when appearing immediately instead of having to wait a few seconds for the portal to appear.

I really hope PoE2 either gets rid of the cast time, or makes it so that the portal opening animation starts the moment one casts the portal.
