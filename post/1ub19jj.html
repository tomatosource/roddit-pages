Quality of Life I’d love to see before 1.0

Here is a list of QoL things.
I’m trying to avoid balance changes, just like UI/UX stuff.

  • Allow access to your stash when viewing the map device inside your hideout.
  • When bosses do unblockable attacks, have their life bar indicate it (Sometimes can’t see them if my build is obscuring the boss)
  • Allow teleporting from towns without the Waypoint. (Can already do this for buying items, it can be faster in Act3 to teleport to buy something, not buy it, exit to hideout and then select the place you want to go than it is to walk to the WP over there on its own)
  • On the atlas add a tracker for ‘last area’ where if you complete a map too far from your current map you can quickly get back to where you were before you changed strats. (even if you complete a few maps in the new area like doing a rite)
  • Trade filter saving on the ingame search
  • Add a button to save your tab renaming/ordering to the parent league so next league it will match what you’ve setup this league
  • Add an option [entirely optional party setting] for multiplayer to respect pause requests from a single party member.
