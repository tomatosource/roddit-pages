Now that the post trade dust has settled, let’s break down where we are at now and where we are going roster wise

So currently we are at about 222 mill in guaranteed money with simons instead of jrue, second apron is supposed to be around 207, so we’ve got some work to do still

The avenue to achieve this now is pretty clear, it basically has to be a KP deal unless you feel we should trade Simon’s. I personally am very very strongly against trading simons, he’s exactly the kind of guy I highlighted going into this summer as a potentially undervalued guy who could fit and thrive in this Boston ecosystem and really flourish with the defensive stability we could provide here. So I’m going to work around the idea that it’s KP. Along with KP, I also strongly believe we should salary dump hauser before the draft into a TPE- he’s currently only making like 2 mill till July 1st, so if you trade him before the draft he will fit into a ton of TPEs and we can use him to move up for a better pick.

I’ll start with Hauser, I won’t get too far into this because nothing has really changed in his scenario, if you want more detailed trade scenarios I’ve posted them before. The structure is basically sending Hauser and one or both of our picks this year to move up in the draft in range for one of sorber or coward. The only thing that’s changed there is beyond sorber who would be the absolute best case scenario, we should be trying to get a young big- meaning reynaud or beringer should also be in play.

With KP, I’ve personally narrowed it down to 3 teams I feel are most likely to want to do business, I’m gonna assume the KP trade happens officially once the new season starts July 1 even if it’s announced earlier, so I’ll work from those numbers. I’m gonna lay out 3 deals to those 3 teams I think would work and shave the last 15-20 mill we would need to shed to get under the second apron.

Suns scenario:

Atlanta receives: Cody martin, Royce O’Neal, and Grayson Allen via 2 TPEs and part of their MLE

Phoenix receives: porzingis

Boston receives: nick Richards and mo gueye

Basically Atlanta adds solid depth guys for essentially free into their exceptions and cap space, phoenix frees up a lot of salary and gets under the second apron, and gets porzingis, Boston gets Richard’s and gueye, big man depth and cuts about 23 mill

Warriors scenario:

Golden state receives: porzingis and pick 32 from Boston

Boston receives: moody, TJD, and pick 45 from Chicago

Chicago receives: hield into their TPE

So Chicago basically gets hield for free, they clearly want to be good next year so he helps them with shooting next to giddey, golden state swaps moody/TJD for a perfect fit starting center next to draymond and adds pick 32 in the draft as some compensation for giving up moody. Boston takes back about 14 mill and gets under the apron, and adds a couple young prospects in positions of need

New Orleans scenario:

Celtics receive: Olynyk

New Orleans receives: KP

Atlanta receives: Alvarado, Brandon Boston, reeves all into TPE’s and 3 future seconds, one from Boston and 2 from New Orleans

Least enticing one imo, not really a good scenario for this trade that I found in limited digging, but regardless it’s still a salary dump that gets us under the apron.

So this is where we stand and these are the directions I see us heading, thoughts?
