What are a couple “what if” takes that you disagree with?

For me I always see Pacers fans saying “If the Malice in the Palace didn’t happen we would have won the championship”, I disagree with this for several reasons. Second reason is I don’t think they beat the Spurs, they were a good defensive team but would have been completely overwhelmed by Duncan inside. I don’t think it was a good match up for them.
