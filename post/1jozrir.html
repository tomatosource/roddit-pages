Deni Avdija in March: 23.4 ppg / 9.8 rpg / 5.2 apg / 65.9% TS & 45.7% from 3

A huge breakout month for the 24 year old, with notable performances:

  • 30/12/10 at CLE (March 2nd, first career triple double)
  • 34/16/6 at GSW (March 10th)
  • 27/15/5 vs. NYK (March 12th)
  • 31/16/8 vs. MEM (March 19th)
  • 36/8/7 vs. DEN (March 21st)
  • 33/7/4 at NYK (March 30th)

Deni is closing the season on an absolute tear & the emergence of his outside shot has been a huge boon for the rest of his offensive game. It will be exciting to see if he can finish the season strong and bring the same momentum into next year. At 6’9 he has shown a great ability to play-make and find his teammates for his size, and also use his frame to pull down boards at a high level.

His future appears to be bright and also seems to have sped up Portlands rebuild.
