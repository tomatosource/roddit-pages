Vecenie’s latest mock - Aday Mara

He’s got Aday Mara to BOS at #27.

2026 NBA mock draft: Absurdly deep class is why so many teams are tanking - The Athletic

After a disappointing first two years at UCLA, Mara has worked his way back into draft consideration by being one of the best shot blockers in college basketball while also being arguably the best passing 7-footer. He’s swatting nearly three attempts per game, contesting everything on the interior when he’s set. Michigan uses his creativity and intelligence on offense well by having him sometimes initiate sets out high or on the wing as a passer…

I like the write-up, but it is so tough to complete mock drafts with the Cs. They pick late, and it seems each of the last few years or so bigs were mocked to them at a high rate. Renaud and Kalkbrenner a year ago, Filipowski and Bona the year prior. Venecnie has Krivas to CLE after Maday, and other mocks suggest Veesaar, Johnson and Cenac.

It always seems that Brad keeps his options open - trading back, trading out completely, I don’t know that he’s pulled off a trade up, yet. He weighs a bunch of factors - will they go to the G-League (Scheireman), sign a two-way (Walsh), contribute immediately (Gonzalez).

It stands to reason that sooner or later he’ll select a big. I just don’t know how anyone can predict when that will happen.
