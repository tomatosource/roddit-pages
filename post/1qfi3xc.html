Hauser for a big makes the most sense

I keep hearing Simons name come up in trade ideas for the Cs, but when you actually look at the roster construction it doesn’t make a lot of sense. Sure, if you’re taking a big swing, Simons is the guy, but my money is on something based around Hauser.

Don’t get me wrong, I love Hauser. He is a very valuable player and is on a great contract. But that also makes him valuable in a trade. He brings more value to the team receiving him because he is cost controlled and should be able to step into just about anyone’s rotation and provide shooting.

The real reason I think we move him though is for roster balance. With the emergence of Walsh, Hugo and Minot, we have a glut of long wings, and we have seen the value you get from having guys who can defend at those positions. If Tatum is really coming back, the most reasonable role for him to fill in the short term is as a souped up 3 and D wing. Have him come in and take Hauser’s minutes. He will have similar gravity because he’s, you know, Tatum, and he will balance off any downgrade in shooting with his other skills.

More importantly though, we are much thinner at the guard spot. As it stands we really only have 3 guards who can handle the ball, and those guys have been a key to our offense this whole year. If you give one away to strengthen the front court, you will be in real danger in the playoffs, especially if White or Pritchard get hurt or in foul trouble. But if you keep Simons, you maintain that strength.

Of course if we are trading Hauser instead of Simons, it changes the targets a bit, but there still are great options. Here are a few:

Hauser and Boucher for Gafford - honestly I think this is win/win and may not need picks on either side. Both teams trade from strength to fill gaps with guys who are on solid contracts moving forward.

Hauser, Boucher, Tillman and a first for Zubac. I think Hauser is basically worth a first himself, so the Clippers can say they get 2 firsts value for Zu, and they also create a little more space below the first apron where they are hard capped if they want to make another move. Hauser also gives them much needed shooting, after they traded away Powell.

Hauser for Mark Williams. Williams is doing pretty well this year, so this may be a stretch, but he also is expiring and will want to get paid, and they have Maluach as well. This could allow them to add a cost controlled core piece for a guy who may walk in the summer.

Hauser and a first for Wendell Carter - Orlando forever needs shooting. Carter always seems to be better on paper for them than the impact he actually has (I think he would be better on a team like the Cs who have more versatile offensive guards).
