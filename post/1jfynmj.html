Kevin Durant is a dumb idea that could plausibly happen

Just in light of all the talk about new ownership and their willingness to spend, I got to thinking about Kevin Durant this afternoon even if it seems wildly unlikely.

Cavs and Thunder have both been outstanding this season. Let’s say that’s who actually makes the Finals and Boston falls short. Side note - kinda crazy to think of a LeBronless Cavs facing a Durantless Thunder, but it’s very much in play.

I’m operating under the idea that with or without this new ownership group, a failed playoff run probably results in Boston cutting cost. In my mind, that probably looks like attaching a pick or two with Jrue Holiday as incentive for a team with cap space to swallow his 30 mil per year contract. I love Jrue, but he’s looked mildly washed this season and it’s a lot to pay if you’re not repeating as a champion.

But let’s flip it in the other direction. What if new ownership was like, “cost be damned, let’s win at all cost”… Is it insane to image Boston getting into the Durant conversation?

I don’t personally see Durant’s trade value as remarkably high given his age (37 next season) and only 1 year remaining on his contract. Allegedly Suns will look for 3 picks and a young player:

“Sources informed The Republic the most ideal return on a Durant trade is regaining three first-round picks and a young player as part of a multi-team deal tied to getting under the second apron,” Rankin writes.”

I’m just throwing this out there that by including Jrue’s 32.4 mil, Hauser’s 10 mil and Pritchard’s 7.2 mil you’re sending out 49.6 mil in salary which is in range of Durant’s 54 mil next season. Boston still has tradable picks and selling high on Pritchard would probably qualify as “a young player”. We could get in the discussion if we really wanted to. Durant came close to signing with us years ago so there may be mutual interest.

I suppose we’d march out White, Brown, Tatum, Durant, Porzingis as our primary line-up which looks good on paper if they are healthy. Horford’s 9.5 mil is coming off the book this Summer so he’s either retiring or signing for less. Our bench takes a hit, but maybe some youth steps up or they find some serviceable veteran min guys to fill out the rotation.
