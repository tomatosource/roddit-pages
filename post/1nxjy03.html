Warrior Attack Speed When?

I understand the goal of warrior being this slow attack big aoe hitter with ancestral and aftershocks. It works well for map clear. However, there is a reason attack speed has been a staple stat for every arpg ever. By the time builds reach end game if they attack 10X faster they are going to do more damage because single big hits will just never actually keep up. With the bosses now receiving map mods and especially the powerful bosses its pretty evident you are not going to be melee range and trade for very long no matter your defenses.

Right now the best way to beat powerful bosses as warrior is to basically play ranged. Every actual melee ability besides maybe mace strike is too slow to avoid being hit by the boss. Also stun build up is completely unbalanced and at least to me seems based off just dps which doesn’t make a lot of sense.

TLDR: GGG fighting a losing battle trying to make a melee class with 0 attack speed and in a lot cases passive nodes, skills, gems actually subtracting attack speed
