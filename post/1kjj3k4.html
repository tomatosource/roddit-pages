Diablo 2 Themed Loot Filter

Hey peoples. So I’ve been working on a diablo 2 themed filter since launch. I think its now in a state worth sharing. I repurposed ~40 diablo 2 sound effects. If you love diablo 2 then its a must have. I have it tuned for monk but you can copy over the filter and edit it to your liking.

Here is the filter.

Here are the sound effects to put in your filter folder.

It will not work without the sound effects. Don’t worry, it does not effect performance at all.

Some of the Diablo 2 sounds:

  • Gold drop sound
  • Flip loot drop sound for gear
  • Portal sound for jewels
  • Custom sound for essences
  • Level up sound for exalts
  • And the best one is the waystones. Each tier has its own sound. I repurposed several shrine activation sounds. It’s much more pleasant to hear than the default filterblade sound.

I also removed nearly every instance of background colors and outlines on loot drops, so it also appears more like diablo 2. You wont have an issue seeing stuff on your screen. Here’s an example:

Please let me know if you like it.
