Idea for league/core mechanic for boss loot

TLDR: Quickly reset boss fight - enables target farming with increasing difficulty/cost

Building on the presence of time travel in the game, it would be cool to have some kind of “Temporal zone” you can activate on a boss arena. On killing the boss, it would allow you to activate the mechanic to quickly reset the boss and fight them again. It could be some kind of Doriyani/Harbinger/Chaos/Order device in your hideout that can be upgraded later.

To balance this, every reset could increase the difficulty in some way - rolling from a table of map modifiers or just gaining a flat scaling buff every time. The zone ends when you pick up an item dropped by the boss - putting the mechanic on cool down for that particular boss. Could be made as a currency/gold sink as a tool to help balance the economy too. Progression with this mechanic could allow players to have control on what modifiers the zone applies or let players bypass the item limit at the cost of difficulty and chaos.

This would play into the “floor gear is good gear” direction they want to take the game into, and be an amazing boon to SSF players. It could even be made available on things other than bosses, a less powerful in map version that could target strongboxes or unique enemies spawned by other content (breach, deli, etc.).
