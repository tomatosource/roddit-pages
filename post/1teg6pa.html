QOL suggestion for runes during campaign

So my suggestion is basically to combine resistance and attribute runes into single base types. For example instead of dropping a glacial rune for cold resistance you would just drop a resistance rune and you would choose which resistance you need when socketing the rune. Same goes for attribute runes being combined into one.

That way instead of having 3 runes for each element and 3 runes for each attribute we would have:

- Lesser Resistance Rune

- Resistance Rune

- Greater Resistance Rune

- Lesser Attribute Rune

- Attribute Rune

- Greater Attribute Rune

What do you guys think?
