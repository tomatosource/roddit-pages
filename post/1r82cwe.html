Easiest league to make money or am I just getting better?

Last league I finished with a tiny stash and honestly thought that was just normal for me. I played a lot, mapped, did the usual, but I never really understood how people were consistently making serious currency outside of lucky drops.

This league started the same way, but I decided to do one thing differently. Instead of grinding harder, I tried to learn why things sell.

Early league I focused on crafting helmets and quivers because they felt approachable. It wasn’t glamorous, but it taught me the basics of demand, pricing, and why “good enough” gear actually moves faster than chasing perfect rolls.

I still mapped a bunch too, spent a lot of time in Jade Isles, and that helped bankroll the experimenting. I also tried to min max Temple early on because everyone said it was insane, but I bricked my setup after a couple of weeks. It wasn’t worth rebuilding for me and I never ended up making anything meaningful from Temple, so everything I earned came from crafting, mapping, and trying other ideas.

Then I stumbled into the thing that surprised me the most this league. Uniques.

Crafting was steady income, but nothing moved value faster than buying unidentified uniques and pushing them with corruptions. I started with Alpha’s Howl just to test the waters, then switched into From Nothing jewels because they’re cheap enough to buy in bulk and the upside feels real.

My loop is pretty simple now. Identify and sell the good hits immediately. Anything that doesn’t hit gets pushed further and corrupted anyway. Some obviously brick, but the hits show up often enough that the whole process keeps moving forward.

The weird part is I’ve actually been playing less the last couple of weeks, but learning more about the economy than I ever have before. I also like what Temple did to the league economy overall, even if it didn’t work out for me personally.

I don’t know if the league is just generous or if something finally clicked for me, but it feels like I stopped playing scared. Currency stopped feeling emotional, and once that happened I started making better decisions.

I’m reinvesting into more of the same and I’m away for a week, so I’m genuinely curious what things look like when I’m back.

Did anyone else have a moment this league where everything suddenly made sense
