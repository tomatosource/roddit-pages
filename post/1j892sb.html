With the Luka Doncic trade getting much of noise, the Knicks trading 5 first round picks(4 unprotected) for Mikal Bridges is quietly going down as one of the worst transactions in modern NBA history so far.

So obviously much of the talk lately has been around the Luka trade and how disastrous that was, and its understandable why because it might have been one of worst trades in sports history and it was completely self-inflicted. But the Mikal Bridges trade was also bad, and the initial reaction was that the Knicks overpayed, but so far just saying “they overpayed” might be an understatement.

The trade up to this point has been a disastrous transaction for the Knicks and for some reason it does not get as talked about nearly as much as like the Rudy Gobert trade maybe because Mikal Bridges is just not a controversial player. But the Knicks gave the Nets 5 first round picks(including 4 unprotected) for Mikal Bridges, and usually for that amount of picks you can get a player like Giannis or KD. And the Knicks might have been able to get that type of superstar if they waited, but instead they used up a majority of their assets to get Mikal Bridges who was never a star, but has been particularly disappointing even up to his standards this season. This season he is averaging 17 points on 34.9% from three which is the lowest since his rookie year, his defense has also slipped, and he cannot create on ball at all for himself and has to rely on Jalen Brunson to always set him up. Overall, he is playing at the level of players like an Austin Reaves/Derrick White, and you just cannot imagine that 5 first round picks would ever be traded for those players.

It seems like the Knicks front office just got greedy to soon without negotiations or waiting, and just acquired Mikal Bridges way above market value. And with that they gave up a majority of their trade assets which might be very damaging and costly in their Jalen Brunson era. Now if they win a championship it can be justified but right now they don’t look like a contender and they look like they solely rely on Brunson.
