The Heat need to trade Bam and Herro and try to rebuild

The Heat are stuck in mediocrity with these two, they’ll just continue to be a play-in team and never improve. It seems like Bam Adebayo is getting worse from season to season even though he’s 28 and supposed to be hitting his prime. A few years ago he would consistently make all-star teams and now he’s not even in consideration. This year he’s averaging 18 points and 10 rebounds on 44% shooting, which is bad efficiency for a big man. I get that he’s shooting more threes but his field goal percentage shouldn’t be that low. Bam is making over 50 million a year while not even being able to make the all-star team in the weak East, one of the most overpaid players in the NBA.

Herro is just very injury prone, in the Jimmy Butler years he would constantly be out for the playoffs and now this year he’s barely played. I don’t think he’s going to improve much as a player from here. He’ll never be a good defender he’ll just be a player who mainly scores. If he’s constantly getting injured and not being available what’s the point of keeping him around and paying him 30 mil a year?
