Former Magic guard Evan Fournier wins the Euroleague Final Four MVP award, as Olympiacos beat Real Madrid 92:85 in the final

https://basketnews.com/news-248191-evan-fournier-2026-euroleague-final-four-mvp.html

Fournier scored 20 points in the final on 70% TS, with five rebounds and four assists, whilst in the semi-final win against Fenerbahce he had 10 points, four rebounds and four assists.

Besides Fournier, former NBA players Cory Joseph, Alec Peters, Sasha Vezenkov, Tyler Dorsey and Donta Hall also played for Olympiacos in the final.

As for Real Madrid, Mario Hezonja, Facundo Campazzo, Trey Lyles, Chuma Okeke and Gabriel Deck made up their cast of ex-NBA players
