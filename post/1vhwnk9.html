Chaos rolling needs QoL: Keep the RNG, remove the thousands of clicks.

Chaos rolling is a necessary part of crafting in PoE2, but manually clicking and checking an item thousands of times is an incredibly tedious player experience.

Yesterday I used nearly 3,000 Chaos Orbs just trying to hit +3 Projectile Skill Levels. I don’t think the mod should be easier to hit — I just don’t think physically performing thousands of identical rolls adds anything meaningful to crafting. So, here are my solutions. I’m sure people have had ideas like these before but I’ve never seen any discussed, so I came up with some myself.

  1. The Chaos Table

Add a crafting table where you can:

Deposit however many Chaos Orbs you’re willing to spend.

Select a modifier and minimum tier you’re looking for.

Press roll.

The table performs normal Chaos rolls using the exact same RNG and weighting as manually rolling.

It automatically stops when the selected mod/tier is hit or you run out of Chaos.

Any remaining Chaos is returned.

There is no improvement to your odds and no reduction in currency cost. If it would have taken 2,847 Chaos manually, it consumes 2,847 Chaos.

It simply removes 2,847 clicks and the need to inspect every single roll.

  1. Omen of Chaotic Excess

If full automation is considered too much, an Omen could allow Chaos Orbs to be rolled in batches of perhaps 20.

Select the desired mod/tier, use up to 20 Chaos at once, and have it automatically stop within the batch if the target is hit. Same RNG, same currency cost, dramatically fewer repetitive clicks.

  1. Mod-targeting Omens

Omen of Projectile Excellence / Melee Excellence / Spell Excellence / Minion Excellence could significantly increase the weighting of modifiers with their respective tags when using a Chaos Orb.

An Omen of Higher Order could instead bias Chaos rolls toward higher modifier tiers.

Unlike the first two ideas, these would actually alter the RNG and become additional crafting resources with their own economic value. They could also potentially be combined, allowing players to spend additional resources to target a particular category of higher-tier modifiers.

“Just use Greater/Perfect Chaos Orbs”

Greater and Perfect Chaos Orbs don’t really solve this problem. They provide better crafting odds in exchange for using substantially more valuable currency.

Whether that’s worthwhile depends heavily on the league economy, the item being crafted and the player’s budget. There will inevitably be situations where ordinary Chaos Orbs are the economically sensible way to target a mod, particularly for regular players who can’t justify burning expensive premium currency.

The Chaos Table isn’t intended to make Chaos Orbs stronger or compete with Greater/Perfect Chaos. It keeps ordinary Chaos exactly as inefficient as it already is — it just removes the requirement to manually perform that inefficiency thousands of times.

The main point

Rarity and tedium don’t need to be the same thing.

I’m completely fine with a desirable modifier taking thousands of Chaos Orbs to hit. The currency investment and RNG are part of crafting.

Once I’ve acquired the currency, understood the craft and accepted the risk, physically clicking an item thousands of times isn’t additional difficulty or meaningful gameplay.

If the expected cost of my craft is 3,000 Chaos, I’m happy to pay 3,000 Chaos. I just don’t think 3,000 mouse clicks should be part of that cost.

Thank-you for coming to my TED Talk. I never played Poe1, so I’d love to hear other more experienced players opinions about these ideas. I love this game. I will play probably over 5-10k hours over the next few years, but this is something that really puts me off crafting my own gears personally.
