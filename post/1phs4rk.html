GGG, please change Mirage Archer. It’s virtually unusable due to sprint requiring a dodge roll.

The mirage archer spirit gem creates a mirage that uses a socketed ranged bow/spear/xbow skill for a few seconds. The mirage is created when you dodge roll. However, the skill gem has the line:

Cannot create a new mirage if a mirage has expired or you have dodge rolled in the past 6 seconds

This line makes the skill almost entirely useless. Sprinting requires a dodge roll to start using, and also requires frequent dodge rolls to change direction at tight angles. As a result, each time you roll to start a sprint between packs it delays your mirage from being summoned for another 6 seconds. This effectively means your mirage is never active and the skill is useless.

This really needs a functionality change, as it could be an interesting skill to play around with but right now its just awful.
