Feedback after finishing the league

I’ve had my eye on the game for a while so I decided I would try it out when 0.5 launched and, as mentioned, I liked it enough to complete all of the league challenges. Overall I really enjoy it and think that it’s pretty much everything I wanted POE1 to be, but I still wanted to document the issues I had with the game as I experienced it for the first time.

1. Nothing stops you from upgrading Kalguuran gems past your current level

After familiarizing myself with the game I wanted to try going the distance with a build focused on one of the new skill gems and I settled on Bitter Dead as my main damage source. It was a decent build IMO, but at one point when I was level 82 I found a level 19 Thaumaturgic Flux. I used it on my Bitter Dead gem without thinking and suddenly I couldn’t use my main damage skill because I was 2 levels under its level requirement. I did manage to get myself back on track eventually by switching to another character and farming for a lower level Bitter Dead, but my build had still basically had its kneecaps broken and it took two days of miserably slow mapping to get those two levels.

2. Map resets un-complete secondary objectives

I like exploring maps and playing the game somewhat casually so I rarely have a problem with the map resetting, but I did play a couple of maps where a requirement to complete it was doing a league encounter in addition to the boss and when I died to the boss the map reset un-completed the league encounter while also deleting the league content from the area, making the map impossible to complete even if I managed to kill the boss. As you can imagine, it was extremely frustrating.

3. Lythara, Benedictus, and Phya are poorly designed

The three aforementioned fights stuck out as being extremely frustrating every time I fought them. The common thread between them is that they all have one attack that you can’t reliably avoid so instead of their fights being tests of your skill and knowledge it turns into a test of “Do you have enough DPS to kill them before they use That Attack?”. Lythara is the most egregious to me because of how quickly she can turn the entire arena purple. Phya’s attack where you have to stand in the circles is fine in theory but when you pair the random distance and time between them with the bugginess of the attack and the waves of projectiles that can chill or freeze you it becomes incredibly difficult to consistently avoid it even when you know what’s coming. Benedictus’ lasers are the least problematic and I’ll probably memorize the dodge timing with more practice but having high damage, hitscan speed, infinite range, and good tracking on a single attack is too much imo.

4. Vessel of Kulemak is confusing

Overall, the Pinnacle bosses are peak and definitely my favorite content. I didn’t fight all of them but I think that their visuals, mechanics, and sound design are leagues above anything else in the game. However, the Vessel of Kulemak stood out as being pretty disappointing. I wouldn’t say that it’s a bad fight, just that it’s really bad at communicating what it’s doing and how I can avoid it. It was the only time in the whole game I had to play with one eye on the wiki just to figure out what was going on.

5. Trial of Chaos is annoying

Put simply: I think that there are too many traps in the modifiers, most of the non-trap modifiers are boring, and you are forced into picking the non-traps at almost every turn because adding more than two things that restrict your movement to the boss fights is basically a death sentence. I much prefer the Trial of Sekhemas.

6. Bugs

  • Something caused my max spirit to decrease at seemingly random times during gameplay, turning my spirit skills off. Because I relied heavily on the Sacrifice skill for my build to work, it was a big problem when it happened and I had to navigate menus in the middle of combat to turn it back on
  • Lightless Moray enemies have long invincibility periods for seemingly no reason
  • Bitter Dead cores are supposed to detonate when they touch an enemy but they struggle to do so for a lot of bigger ones and instead just huddle up to them and only explode when their duration expires. It wasn’t a dealbreaker for my build but it slowed my clear speed a fair bit and it gave enemies a lot more time to attack or regenerate life
