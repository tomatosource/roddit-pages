The small-ball lineup is starting to get exposed

Don’t have the exact lineup data on this, but since the Raptors second half, it feels like the small-ball lineup is getting torched and not providing offense on the other end. Best data point I have for this is Minott being -12,-3 and -11 in the last 3 games.

Teams are starting to guard Minott differently in particular, as they’re switching on defense and closing out hard to not let him shoot the 3 and put the ball on the floor.

That lineup as a whole lived off outgunning the other teams’ slower lineups, but it feels like the switching has neutralized it offensively. And on the other end, they can’t rebound the ball.

This team really needs another big, and one that can take advantage of switching defenses (tbh, say what you want about him, but like Porzingis used to in his prime).
