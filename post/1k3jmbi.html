Feedback after 250 hours

i wanna give my feedback after playing 250 hours. i played poe 1 for 300 hours in boatlague so i knew a little bit what i was doing . these are some of the problems i encountered

for context i currently play minion infernalist and im at t9 maps.

Campaign :

- need more exalts. what ends up happening every time is that you have a couple of magic items that you would want to exalt and you got like 2-5 ex at any given time if you are conservative with them. with the amount of mods that dont do anything for you if you end up not doing enough dmg / are too squishy is too high and they also can roll low. the low amount of ex makes you always hesitate to just slam your gear and you are far better of just using trade to buy a piece that has 1-2 good stats + 1-2 mildly usefull stats than slamming your own piece that has 2 or 3 mods and pray. Unless you get really lucky with drops. I could imagine for a completly new player or ssf players its even worse.

- sometimes i see an upgrade at a vendor but the amount of time / farming that it would take me to farm enough gold to buy it would make me level up and removing it from the shop( except when im in act 1⁄4 and can abbuse forgotten riches ).this happens mostly in first playthrouhs as you have enough gold /items later. it would be nice if we could pin /defer items at the shop so they stay available for a couple of levels.

minions :

- minions feel really squishy right now. every league mechanic just obliterates my army even if the map otherwise is ok to run. im not really sure if thats a minion problem or a monster problem with the league mechanics.

- when using command skills, sometimes you lose mana and get locked into the animation but nothing happens. i gues this happens because im commanding a minion thats dead ( when using multiple stormmage / skelli sniper ). this feels terribe. sometimes you cast 6 sec fo nothing while your last 3 minions die and furthr extend the death timer. if i cant choose which minion to command, please just make me smart command any living minion of this skill.

- Add more interesting nodes / interactions with minions. Its really sad that the heralds are not working with minions . Even if you would give up your weapon and use gravebind, the buff would be granted to you and i dont think there is a way to extend the buff to your minions. Even the node that gives your amulet to your minions will still apply instilled modifiers to the player which guts creativity.

The general design choice that heralds/ wind dancer / rhoa mount … are locked behind weapontypes or that i dont like either.

mapping :

- make me able to fight the same mapboss again but with no xp gain or loot. It would make comparing gem + gear setups at any given point in the progression or just add a target dummy for the hideout. The work i would have to do to get a accurate comparrison is too much.

what do you think about this ? did you encounter some of these problems as well ?
