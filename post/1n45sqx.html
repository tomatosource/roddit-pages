Poison should be rebalanced to how it works in PoE 1

The current way poison operates (on being able to only stack 1-10 poisons depending on investment) does not interact well with the skill design of poison, which encourages rapid application of poisons.

Since poison concoction got reworked, there is not a single skill that acts to apply a single, strong poison. Instead, the skills encourage application of multiple small poisons, but the limitation of poison having up to 10 stacks makes it feel like these skills only do majority hit damage with a pitiful poison on top.

GGG, I really think you should consider allowing poisons to reach 100 stacks as they do in PoE1, especially considering that all of the DoT ailments in PoE2 (bleed, poison, ignite) currently scale very similar in that you want a large hit. This would allow poisons skills to scale based on attack speed/multiple projectiles/rapid application versus scaling a larger hit which does not even exist for poison right now.
