Made a vendor regex for bow builds in preparation for 0.4

!cros|taff|mace”add|ed ph|nt sp|ck sp|ile sk|sis”

Just type that into the vendor and it should give you:

excludes anything on the first page that’s not a bow

excludes staffs at Una (or the likes), in case you find a good wand to sell or save (HC / ssf stuff)

includes anything that gives added damage on weapons or gloves

includes the “increased physical damage” affix

includes the “movement speed” affix

includes the “attack speed” affix

includes the “plus to projectile skills” affix.

includes anything with resistances. You could remove this if it’s too much highlighting at the vendor. It’s the “sis” part of the regex
