Visual Clarity and the contradiction that is POE2

POE2 is at contradiction with itself. It has a split personality so to speak.

On the one hand, it is clear as day that the developers aim to make this game skill/combo based. They want us to read mechanics and movements of bosses and avoid them and make most of what we press on our skill bars deliberate and calculated.

This playstyle lasts mid way through end game. Then it’s ugly brother comes out to play.

The POE1 syndrome. The one to two button, screen vomit obliterators. Most builds in end game are like this. COC comet, minions, spark, herald skills, ect. All turn into screen garbling monsters that mask the machinations of bosses.

In ritual, on all three of my characters, I simply cannot see anything anymore. Same with Abyss, deli…. it’s just lighting, frost, minion spam. And the reason for this is simple. When we Juice things we need builds to demolish them fast because there is an incredibly jump where defenses just stop mattering.

I feel like this game is having an identity crisis and I’m not sure it will be addressed prior to 1.0….if ever.
