Huge Thanks for Patch 0.3 – Best Experience Yet!

Thank you so much for releasing patch 0.3 — it’s absolutely amazing! I’m extremely happy with it. The new adjustments have allowed me to design my own build and succeed with it. Honestly, I feel like my Titan is even stronger than some of the popular YouTube builds, and that makes me feel so much better than just copying others.

The crafting system (especially with the top-tier Essences, which honestly feel a bit overpowered and probably should be delete 2 mods for add 1 mod) has helped my character tackle boss content that I never played much during my 1200 hours in patches 0.1–0.2. Even though I can’t one-shot them, being able to fight and actually defeat those bosses has been an incredible experience.

Despite the rough launch with lots of bugs and overtuned Abyss monsters, you fixed things very quickly. I really appreciate your hard work!
