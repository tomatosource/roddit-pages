First things I notice right off the bat:

  • Only 24 (25 counting Ohtani) out of 100 players are starting pitchers (only 27 pitchers total). I feel like there are a number of good pitchers around the league that a lot of organizations would want for the 2026 season over plenty of the positional players on this list

  • 1B is generally considered the least valuable/important position on the diamond - and yet 11 of the 73 positional players on this list are first basemen. Compare that to only 4 CF, 7 3B, and 3 2B. LF is the second-least valuable/important position on the diamond and they have 10 players listed. WAR definitely goes out the window in order to justify a ranking system like this.

  • SS gets a lot of love - rightfully so, that is generally where the best ballplayers end up. But it is interesting that there are so few CF by comparison.

Starting pitchers put up 336 rWAR in 25,212 IP while relief pitchers put up 94 rWAR in 17,863 IP.

Nothing too surprising - the up-the-middle positions provide the most value, and 1B/LF/DH provide the least value.
