Kawhi Leonard is the only player in NBA history to have multiple finals MVPs but not a single regular season MVP

Kawhi has won 2 fmvps in both 2014 and 2019. He never won a regular season mvp though (probably due to load managing in his prime).

His best chance was 2016 when the spurs went 67-15… but Steph Curry exists.

Other players have won 1 finals mvp and no regular season mvps like Andre Iguadola, Jerry West and Tony Parker. But Kawhi is the only one to do it multiple times and not have a regular reason mvp.

Another cool thing. Kawhi, LeBron and Kareem are the only players to win fmvps on at least 2 different teams.
