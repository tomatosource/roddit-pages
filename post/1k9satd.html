Lower tier crafting omens need a rework

Omen’s of Coronation, Exaltation, and Alchemy. I honestly don’t understand why these exist. I tried using some of these today, but you get the exact same effect as just slamming without the omens active. I have never heard or seen of anyone actually using them. They should just be removed. Because currently the only thing they do is take up a favor slot which could be a useful like an omen of whittling.

I think a better idea for these lower tier omens would be to tie them to affix tiers. I.e. “omen of fire” - the next fire modifier is guaranteed to be at least tier 4. “Omen of greater fire” - the next fire modifier is guaranteed to be at least tier 6. And this would also fix essences as well, because theres nothing more frustrating then having 2 decent rolls on a magic item, using a greater essence, and having it give a tier 1 modifier.
