I am fine with a slower game, but monsters need to be slower and we need more loot to compensate.

I love PoE1, I have over 3.5k hours in it, but I absolutely see the appeal of an ARPG that is slower, more methodical, and is based around combos. I think parry into disengage into throwing an empowered spear into rushing back in is cool. However, the game is currently built around clearing masses of content and clearing the entire screen in one click because monsters are so fast and dangerous that they simply swarm and kill you and the average monster drops nothing.

I am totally fine with maps taking 10x as long if they drop 10x as much loot (and of a higher quality). But right now the players feel like they’re in one game and monsters are another.
