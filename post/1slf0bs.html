A few interesting things I noticed

  • Kobe Bryant would have 4 scoring titles, currently has 2

  • Kevin Durant took Carmelo Anthony’s only scoring title, would have had 5 in a row

  • Chris Paul goes from 5 assist titles and 6 steals titles to 3 assist titles and 5 steals titles

  • Tracy McGrady ends up with zero scoring titles, they go to Kevin Garnett and Kobe Bryant

  • Allen Iverson would have just one scoring title, currently has 4

  • Dikembe Mutombo goes from 2 rebounding titles and 3 block titles to 4 rebounding titles and 5 block titles

  • Shawn Marion takes both of Baron Davis’ steals titles

  • Michael Jordan somehow goes from 10 scoring titles to 11, because why not
