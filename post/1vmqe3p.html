What if GGG decided one day to make part of the skill tree paid?

I recently saw the news that the skill tree in Diablo 4 was going to be expanded, i thought, “Wow, that’s cool, maybe they learned from PoE, there should be more build diversity”, But Blizzard is unbelievable! I opened the game, leveled up, went to the skill tree, and discovered…The skill tree is partially locked, I thought, do I need a level? Do I need to advance the story? No guys, you have to PAY, pay for the skill tree of a base class which already existed since the game’s launch.

PoE2 has many problems, but I’m very grateful to GGG for making such a good game.
