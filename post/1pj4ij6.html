Rob Mahoney on Bill Simmons’ pod made two really good points why the Celtics are 15-9: the Celtics win the possession battle every night and the Walsh/Minott/Hugo minutes hold the rotation together

Mahoney made the point that having all those extra possessions from turnovers, not turning the ball over and getting offensive rebounds gives the Celtics a huge math advantage, and that taking so many threes basically stretches that advantage out. That’s a big reason why a lot of teams need to destroy us on the boards to beat us, basically if not, they’re going to have less chances to score than the C’s.

He also added that the Walsh/Minott/Hugo minutes all providing similar but slightly different characteristics allows for no drop-off when one of those three wings come out, which is big for the rotation that we have.

I thought Bill made a good point too when he said the entire rotation has something to prove for different reasons, which allows for Joe to get a major buy-in from the team.
