Rarity should be tied to character level. That’s it.

It’s that simple and there’s no need to overcomplicate it. Why is it still an unsolved issue (and devs agree that it’s an issue) I have no idea.

You have no rarity bonus when you start (actually 1%) and it grows as you level up to 100% bonus at lvl100. Makes total sense to me and there’s no way to mess it up.

And before anybody say that lvl100 is not achievable by a casual player, you’ll never notice the difference between 95% rarity and 100%, so it’s more like placebo for anybody who wanna push to 100 than an actual cure.

Anyway, 100% is maximum you can get. And it’s plenty, tbh. They don’t even need to change anything else. All the drop chance numbers can stay the same. And rarity from gear can be removed altogether, or swapped to something else (if they want to keep stat pool diluted).
