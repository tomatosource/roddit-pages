Lakers center Jaxson Hayes has received his Slovenian passport, meaning he will soon be eligible to play for the Slovenian National Team

https://www.kzs.si/novica/jaxson-hayes-podal-prisego-in-prejel-slovenski-potni-list

&gt; Washington / Ljubljana – After the competent authorities recently issued all the necessary approvals and documentation for extraordinary naturalization and acquisition of Slovenian citizenship, Jaxson Hayes took the oath at the Slovenian Embassy in Washington.

&gt; The 26-year-old American center is 213 centimeters tall. Last season, he played 75 games for the Los Angeles Lakers - 65 in the regular season and 10 in the playoffs. He averaged 18 minutes on the court and contributed 7.4 points and 4.1 rebounds per game.

Hayes will not be eligible to represent Slovenia in the upcoming FIBA World Cup qualifying window, but thereafter will be eligible to play for the team.
