Clinching scenarios for the East at play tonight

The top 4 seeds in the East could be determined tonight with the result of 3 games

The Cavs are 62-16 and can clinch the 1 seed with a win or a Celtics loss

The Celtics are 58-20 and can only catch the Cavs if they win out AND Cavs lose out, otherwise they are the 2 seed

The Knicks are 50-28 can clinch the 3 seed with a win or a Pacers loss

The Pacers are 47-31 and can clinch home court in the first round with a win AND a Bucks loss. They can only reach the 3 seed if they win out AND Knicks lose out because Knicks have the tiebreaker.

Bucks are 44-34 and have the tiebreaker over the Pacers, meaning the magic number here is still 2

A Knicks win over the Celtics, a Pacers win over the Wizards, and a Wolves win over the Bucks will lock the top 4 seeds in the East into place with nothing left to play for in the regular season for these teams.
