Uniques have an even more of a distribution than variety problem

Not so much the frequency of the drops, but rather the fact how some unique are tremendously more likely than others. On top of that, some of the most interesting drops, such as jewels that allow you to manipulate your skill tree, are super rare: Fact, I know literally multiple players with 250~300h playtime who haven’t found a single one of these.

It’s not really encouraging or fun if literally every unique that drops is more or less a “leveling unique”.

I have to say, at this point I even enjoyed D3 more than PoE2 in this regard, there I would very often think of trying new characters or builds since so much interesting stuff dropped.

I am well aware that PoE2’s distribution pool is yet to be extended, but it’s truly not just a problem of not having enough interesting uniques, but possibly even more than that - a problem of DISTRIBUTION, with some of the most interesting and build encouraging uniques not dropping within hundreds of hours of play.

I understand that some people like to have a “premium feel” to their items, but frankly - this should apply in terms of high and low stat roles, not by 99,9% of the playerbase being locked of literally gameplay enabling components.
