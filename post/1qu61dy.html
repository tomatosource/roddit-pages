Hugo Gonzales leads all rookies in total +/-, and as of today leads all rookies ever in +/- per game

I pulled these lists from StatMuse after seeing a post on the Celtics instagram.

Top 5 for total +\- for all current rookies:

H. González: +236

D. Harper: +117

VJ Edgecombe: +69

K. Knueppel: +59

J. Beringer: +45

Top 5 +\- per game by rookies all time (min 40 games played):

H. González: 5.36

T. Duncan: 5.23

C. Holmgren: 5.15

B. Simmons: 4.78

D. Mitchell: 4.52

J. Tatum: 4.3

Plus/minus in a vacuum doesn’t mean much, and the fact that the Celtics bench has been beating up on other teams benches makes it very easy to write this off as an aberration. But I think that ignores the reality that Hugo is a major reason why the Celtics bench has been beating up on other teams. He isn’t flashy, he’s certainly no Cooper or Kon, but he’s regularly making the kind of high-IQ and winning basketball plays that are rare for a 19 year old rookie. And he’s shown flashes of some real self creation.

That said, there are a lot of rookies out there absolutely lighting up the league that absolutely deserve their flowers and a whole ton of credit. I just wanted to shoutout this 19 year old kid for finding ways to seriously contribute to winning basketball in a relatively established system with a coach that demands a lot out of his players defensively and mentally.
