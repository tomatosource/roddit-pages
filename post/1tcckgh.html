Why Not Miles Turner

The Celtics main issue with the Big situation isn’t really a rebounding issue as the team as a whole can rebound, vs not having a defensive anchor of a center that can get his own bucket without relying on plays or set up.

Giannis is the hot name but Giannis is just as expensive as JB, older, and there’s no guarantee Tatum and Giannis mesh as you want both to have the ball in their hand.

Throwing stars together just because of name value hasn’t been working well, how the team is constructed and the needs around that make more sense without having to possible pay much.

Miles turner short tenure on the bucks has been lack luster with an expensive contract, the contract is a negative but can be a positive on price tag.

If you send Vuc and something else maybe a second to match salaries, you don’t give up much and you get a younger center that can defend and score.

Giannis doesn’t really cover any holes but more so adds more punch, but why add a little more when you can upgrade the center position to the need of the team while also keeping the Jays together in their primes
