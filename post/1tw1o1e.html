List of all Unique Beasts and what they gain as Minions.

So, in case you don’t know, GGG gave 12 of the Unique Bosses Minion Bonuses (they forgot about Morvak, sadge). They are as follows:

Act 1:

The Devourer: 200% More Poison Magnitude. Yes, MORE, not INCREASED. Boss doesn’t fucking move though.

The Crowbell: 300% More Stun.

Act 2:

Rathbreaker: Breaks Armor = 30% Physical Damage. 20% More Damage.

Act 3:

Mighty Silverfist (A.K.A. Best Boy): +19% to Critical Chance, +150% to Critical Damage Bonus.

Xyclucian, the Chimera: Gain 25% of Damage as Extra Damage of a random Element. 200% Increased Elemental Ailment Magnitude. Basically, insane for Shock and Chill. Or even Burn. Infernal Legion anyone?

Act 4:

Great White One: 25% chance to inflict Bleeding on Hit, +200% Magnitude of Bleed.

Interlude:

Rakkar, the Frozen Talon: Gain 50% of Damage as Extra Cold Damage. 50% of damage converted to Cold.

The Abominable Yeti: All Damage from Hits Contributes to Freeze Buildup. 50% of damage converted to Cold. 100% Increased Freeze Multiplier.

Trial of the Sekhemahballs:

Ashar, the Sand Mother: 50% increased Impale Magnitude, 50% chance to Impale Enemies on Hit, Cannot consume impale.

Trial of Chaos (who I’ve come to kill):

Uxmal, the Beastlord: Gain 50% of Damage as Extra Damage of a random Element

Chetza, the Feathered Plague: Corrupted blood on hit % average damage to deal per minute per stack [600] (no idea what the fuck any of this means. I’m guessing it deals 10% of average damage per second and has a duration of 4000 ticks)

Corrupted blood on hit duration [4000]

Corrupted blood on hit num stacks [1]

Bahlak, the Sky Seer: Gain 50% of Damage as Extra Lightning Damage.
