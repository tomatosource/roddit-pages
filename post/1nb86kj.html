A glaring issue with crafting

I’m a big crafting enjoyer in PoE1 and I have very much enjoyed the additions to crafting this league in PoE2. There’s however one thing that really nags me.

Why do we not have some sort of UI that shows available affixes? This feels like one of the most glaring issues from PoE1 being carried over to the sequel. It seems like a major oversight to start building a new crafting system but rely on 3rd party sites to fill out the gaps as they did with PoE1.

Right now a player can look at a currency that reads “guarantees an Ulaman modifier” and have no way to figure out what that actually means inside the game. I think this needs to be remedied.
