Two Games, One Company, Two Different Cases on Build Variety

Thanks to ladder info and poeningka, it’s not hard to see the state of build diversity in two POE games. Look at this:

POE1: https://poe.ninja/builds/mercenaries

POE2: https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds/abyss

POE2 has more ascendancy options than POE1 yet (currently) 40% of the top ladder is playing Deadeye. Not only that, this number was about 25% on launch and rocketed up to 40% in five days. By the time the league ends, more than half of the top ladder will be Deadeyes (especially after “bugfixes” today which destroyed about 3 unique and niche builds).

Compare that to POE1, which had a recent league launch and the top entry has only 11% representation. In fact, top 5 entries (ascendancies, not even skills mind you) are neck and neck in representation.

But wait, it’s actually worse than that for POE2. Because not only the majority of the playerbase is playing Deadeye, those who don’t play Deadeye are also using LA or any other lightning skills. Here is the top sill distribution for POE2:

https://preview.redd.it/awpy0ejdy1nf1.png?width=207&format=png&auto=webp&s=2228cb1893ebadcb28ef34613d1c4d3bdff3465e

and compare that to POE1:

https://preview.redd.it/l7huovsfy1nf1.png?width=207&format=png&auto=webp&s=366eef672674734caa31c74c96425d0222b17b1a

Why is the gap between top-performing and other skills are so large and why are people who are not playing the highest represented build being punished with “bugfixes” which completely destroy builds that only a handful people play?
