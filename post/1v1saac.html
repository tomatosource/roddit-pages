Has Baseball become the least Ref/Umpire affected major sport?

Coming off the world cup where half the matches seemed to end with some sort of controversy (causing mass conspiracy theories) it made me think about the current state of baseball. Hockey, soccer, football, basketball also have tons of important, subjective calls every game where the losing team fans complain about refs after every loss.

I am not saying baseball is immune to controversy, but with replay the calls in the field are overwhelmingly correct now. Balls/strikes still makes a clear difference but with ABS challenge system there is at least a system for you to do something. Like if you lose your 2 challenges and get a bad call later in the game….then it can sort of be viewed as a byproduct of losing your challenges. Im absolutely not saying its perfect….but it seems like there is way less “ref/umpire” complaining now vs other sports that have a lot more subjective calls.
