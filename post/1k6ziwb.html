Paolo Banchero Playoff Career (9 games) 28.6 PPG 55.9 TS% 41.5 3P%—5.9 3PA/g 8.9 RPG 4.3 APG

Imperfect, but very impressive start to a playoff career for a 6’10, 21-22 year old running an offense surrounded by the poorest shooting in the NBA. There are efficiency warts in his game right now. Obvious over-reliance on long 2s. FTs can be ugly. Some of the finishing is mediocre for his size/strength. But we need to grade him on a curve. To be this young & this productive with his responsibilities bodes well for prime P5. The tape is there. He has craft & feel that is unusual for a guy his size/age. Defense is solid, not great, but he’s huge, can move, and (usually) tries which puts him in front of most young players defensively. Has a game that can eventually transcend tough playoff defense.
