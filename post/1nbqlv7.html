I bought a T16 Waystone for 15ex. It was one of the best gaming experiences of my life.

My little Lich minion build is not a meta, I don’t think. (I found the magyk ant before it became a meta, so that doesn’t count.) The basic gestalt is tagging infinite-duration Despair and Vulnerability on every damned thing in proximity, then cleaning up the corpses with exploding fire unearthed xenomorphs. I like it when dead things explode, so I loaded up on that just for the vibe.

I got to maps, climbed rather arduously through T4, getting waystones as they came, but then said: I’m just gonna run a T16 and see what happens. So I find the lowest-priced Normal T16 Waystone available - 15ex, which represents 70% of my net worth - and set sail.

Me and the minionboyz are in some subterranean jail thing. Everything is exceedingly deadly. And we CRAWL and I mean fucking CRAWL through the thing. Every turn, terrifying. Every out-of-nowhere ground effect sends us all into full retreat. Panic and dread and adrenaline without relief. 15ex on the line, there was no way it was going to in failure.

Took probably 30 minutes to clear the thing, but it was the most tense 30 gaming minutes of my long, old life. (The only thing CLOSE is my first playthrough of one of the Ashes of Malmouth maps, when I was high enough on a very good sativa that it became transcendent, but that’s a different thing altogether.)

A++++++ 10⁄10 would recommend. thanks ggg.
