The Spiritborn problem

I’m calling this the spiritborn problem, because D4 went through this with their new class release. You can call it the lightning spear problem. But in both cases the devs decided to not nerf builds mid league and that basically trivialized all content in the game. I never played D4 after that season, cause I was 1 tapping all bosses. It’s happened here in poe2 as well with all LS builds.

Now ppl will say, play off meta builds. It’s a fair thought, but there’s a bunch of problems with this

  1. Games that have any MP component (economy in poe, and helltides and world bosses in D4) means ur getting left behind. In D4 u couldn’t even get hits out if u weren’t playing spiritborn.

  2. Once ppl experience the OP build, playing anything else seems cumbersome, especially when u know it’s available if u want it.

I also think it’s too late to nerf now. Nerfs need to happen right away, not even wait for a couple of days. Once more and more ppl get into gearing towards an OP build, then it’s hard to take it away from the masses.
