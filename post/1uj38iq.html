Elemental Infusions and Spells. The 2 Halves of the same puzzle. Feedback/Rant.

Loooong rant incoming, but I’m curious to see just how many people agree that the elemental infusion mechanic needs a major rework. Right now it feels like GGG put a band-aid over a massive shark bite.

The main issue is that most of the spells simply don’t feel good without infusions. That’s the REAL core of the problem. I don’t expect GGG to completely scrap infusions at this point, but the current way they’re implemented creates an immensely exhausting gameplay loop.

And the really annoying part is that infusions actually do fix the core problem and make most of the spells feel good. They transform a clunky or otherwise underwhelming spell into something I’m happy to play around with. But another part of the problem isn’t what the infusions fix. It’s how it also introduces a new problem in the form of how you engage with the system.

  1. Read the spell tooltip so you know which infusion you need.
  2. Figure out a way to inflict that specific ailment (you’re using a fire spell that needs a lightning infusion because hehe xd more problems to solve lmao)
  3. Extract the infusion into a remnant.
  4. Pick up the remnant.
  5. Oh look at this. Your next cast gets to feel like how the spell should have felt in the first place.

Except… During the early-mid campaign there isn’t a reliable way to create infusions consistently that doesn’t require you to awkwardly weave multiple spells together. And you have to do this during the portion of the game when it’s at it’s slowest. All of that just to unlock the version of your spell that feels like the complete version of it. For the first like 40+ levels, it feels less like interesting gameplay and more like you’re constantly doing chores before you’re allowed to play. Abso-fucking-lutely NO thank you.

So… Here’s a couple of ideas I think could make it more interesting/better overall.

  1. Make elemental infusions into a mechanic that’s unique to 1 ascendancy (e.g. stormweaver) that works somewhat similarly to the new Gemling “additional gem quality” node. But instead it just grants your elemental spells additional effects. That would give both the infusions and ascendancy a unique identity instead of making every caster jump through the same hoops.

  2. Turn infusions into Support Gems. You want a fire infusion for your spell? Socket the fire infusion support that gives “Supported Spells deal X% less damage, but gain the Fire Infusion effect” or whatever. Sky is the limit.

  3. Just bake these effects directly into the spells. A lot of these infusion effects aren’t just about damage. They just make the spells FEEL complete. If a spell only becomes fun once it’s infused, then maybe that’s how the spell should work always. Then redesign infusions to do something else entirely.
