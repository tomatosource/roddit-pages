Vendor recipes would be the solution to a lot of our gear problems

One thing I truly do miss from PoE1 is the ability to easily target that very first affix on an item, such as movement speed on boots. Especially at the beginning of a league.

I personally think the current implementation of vendors are great - especially with buyback. However it comes with a problem that Diablo 2 solved 25 years ago. They fixed that problem by allowing players to use the Horadric Cube to transmute items much like how vendor recipes work in PoE1. The annoying part of the Horadric Cube was that it took up inventory space and charm space.

Instead of a Horadric Cube, the Titan ascendancy passive Colossal Capacity which gives you an extra inventory with 20 slots could be available on every class from the start of the Renly quest. It could have a Salvage/Transmute button that turns items into scraps or does recipes/crafting. Imagine being able to salvage items right from your inventory.

The very first recipe I would suggest is Silver Charm + Orb of Augmentation + Normal Boots which would create boots with the highest tier movement speed it is able to receive based on the drop level of the boots.

There could be other recipes such as Topaz Charm + Orb of Augmentation + Normal Wand which gives you a random tier of +# level of all lightning spell gems, up to the highest it is able to receive and so on. This would really make use of the excess charms and there are so many possibilities. This format is taken directly from PoE1’s recipes (Topaz Flask + Orb of Augmentation + Normal Item). And since charms are literally the new utility flasks, it only makes sense.

I recall the reason GGG gave for not implementing the extra inventory in PoE1 is that they wanted the items to have “weight”. That no longer makes sense in PoE2 when we can enter portals many times and collect as much of the loot as we want. It also doesn’t make sense to lock it behind a single ascendancy.
