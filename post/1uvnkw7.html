My first attempt at a Hexblast build! (Infernalist Witch)

So, I’ve been reading around and noticing the general consensus was that this skill was just not good. Some might even say that it is complete cheeks. So, I decided to come here and see what people think of my setup since I fell in love with this curse! Been enjoying and have completed every single pinnacle boss. I’m very much up for questions!

This is my skill tree!

https://imgur.com/a/U3sWXw7

Important Tree Nodes

% Increased Chaos damage without skill effect duration. Only grab the ones that do not give skill effect.
Black Flame Covenant is a MUST! It works amazingly well with Bringer of Flame.
Grab every reduced skill effect duration. I have my Despair sitting at a 0.6 duration.
Curse Magnitude is super important.
You need just a little bit of Freeze build up for Chaotic Freeze to really be felt. Between Ignites and Freeze, you’ll be hitting comets a lot.
+3 Hexblast is NOT expensive AT ALL. No one likes this spell, so it’ll be easy to bump your damage sky high!

Ascendency is as followed:

Pyromaniac Pact
Bringer of Flame
Demonic Form
Altered Flesh

Important Gear Notes:

Wand: Dueling Wand (Prioritize +Spell Levels and Gain % of Damage as Chaos Damage)
Body Armour: Reverie Shaman Mantle (Cut’s flask regeneration rate by 40%, lower if corrupted and applies it even second)
Belt: Shavronne’s Satchel Fine Belt (Applies life flask regeneration to energy shield)
Everything Else: % Chaos Damage, % Spell Damage, Cast Speed, Mana, Mana Cost Efficiency

My spell setup is as followed:

Demon Form: Atziri’s Impatience
Spellslinger: Detonate Dead with Energy Capacitor, Boundless Energy II. Magnified Area II, Spell Cascade
Rite of Restoration: Cooldown Recovery II. Uhtred’s Rite
Essence Drain: Chaotic Freeze, Prolonged Duration II, Chain III, Dominus Grasp, Chaos Mastery
Despair: Impending Doom, Compressed Duration II, Heightened Curse, Considered Casting, Chaos Mastery
Hexblast: Controlled Destruction, Considered Casting, Chaos Attunement, Withering Touch, Chaos Mastery
Living Bomb: Considered Casting, Controlled Destruction, Concentrated Area, Chaos Attunement, Fire Mastery
Cast on Elemental Ailment: Comet, Controlled Destruction, Spell Cascade, Boundless Energy II, Fire Mastery
Withering Presence: Prolonged Duration II, Herbalism I, Chaos Mastery

Just a quick post! I might have missed something, but hopefully not! I’m all for questions and potentially suggestions!
