[Stein] The Portland Trail Blazers have no plans to buyout Jrue Holiday. The same no-go applies to the notion of a Zach LaVine buyout in Sacramento.

Portland is not currently pursuing free agent swingman DeMar DeRozan, league sources say, despite reports to the contrary.

I’m likewise told that recent speculation about a Jrue Holiday buyout from the Trail Blazers is erroneous. It has become a popular online pastime to suggest that such an arrangement is percolating that helps route Holiday back to the team that drafted him — which obviously fancies itself as a Superteam now after the Sixers acquired Brown and James — but league sources say Portland is not about to just usher Holiday onto the open market. The Blazers have been messaging for some time that they would like to keep Holiday … even after trading for Ja Morant and with Damian Lillard poised to make a comeback after losing a full season to an Achilles tear.

The same no-go applies for the notion of a Zach LaVine buyout in Sacramento. One source close to the process told The Stein Line that the Kings are operating under the premise that LaVine will start the season as a King and added that “no buyout” is being discussed.
