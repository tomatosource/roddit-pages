Looking for advice/critique on my first build

Link to build

TL;DR Me want more damage and survivability without excessive amounts of farming.

Hey what’s up everyone, this is my first time playing PoE (or any ARPG of this style) and I decided to play Warrior. The build above is what I came up with and I’ve been having a lot of fun, but I recognize that it’s VERY unoptimized and I’m looking for input from people who actually know what they’re doing. I’m pretty comfortably clearing T15 maps that are kinda juiced up (rare with 2-3 revives), but haven’t really dipped my toes into fully juiced maps yet. I give a lot of detail below for those who are interested, but the TL;DR is above.

The main thing that gets me killed right now is rare enemies with the modifier that make me take dot damage by being near them or the one that drains mana if I’m near them. For these, I run away and spam Sunder and Forge Hammer until one of us dies. I’ve tried a bunch of things to deal with them and haven’t found anything consistent unless Hammer of the Gods is ready.

Note that Perfect Strike isn’t part of the build, I just had it because that skill used to be Leap Slam, but I really wasn’t using that since it gets rid of broken armor. I’ve been trying a ton of different skills in it’s place and haven’t found anything that synergizes well. I was thinking of going with the basic totems with Ancestral Spirits from the Warbringer tree (which I used a ton when leveling but dropped in endgame) with my final ascendency points. However, I’m open to other ideas, especially if it will allow for easily killing the rare monsters mentioned above.

Speaking of ascendency, I’ll be going for those last 2 ascendency points this weekend, and I’m not sure which direction to go with this. Ancestral Spirits are fun, but I don’t know if it makes sense without a dedicated totem build. Encased in Jade sounds cool, but I have no idea how it actually works or if it’s worth it. I’ve also been thinking of switching from Warbringer to Titan. I like breaking a shit ton of armor, but I have no idea how much value it’s actually bringing. Similarly, exploding corpses with warcries is fun and I enjoy ignoring cool downs, but it’s not really doing anything for me that my other map clearing abilities don’t take care of. It seems like Titan would allow for more overall damage and survivability, though it might not be as interesting as Warbringer. I would love to hear ideas for how to spend those last two points and if it would make sense to switch to Titan.

Regarding playstyle, the build really relies on utilizing all these skills in conjunction with each other. Stampede is the bread and butter for mapping, and I like to combine it with Forge Hammer + Internal Cry as well as loops of exploding corpses with Infernal Cry followed by Boneshatter. Sunder is nice for ranged enemies, and Hammer of the Gods is good for rare enemies. Mapping is admittedly kinda slow, but it’s fun and I don’t mind the speed.

Bosses have been pretty smooth so far. The general strategy is to cycle between Forge Hammer+Infernal Cry, Sunder, and Stampede until they’re ready for heavy stun, then Boneshatter+Hammer of the Gods+Sunder. If I time it right, the Sunder hits right before the hammer lands, which generally deletes half the boss’s health bar. I’ll then repeat this until they’re dead, though truthfully I wouldn’t mind getting the damage high enough to one shot consistently with this combo.

One last thing to note is this build has been EXTREMELY cheap. All the gear is the result of trades, and I’ve only spent 1 Divine on the weapon and a handful of Exalts and Chaos on everything else. I don’t have a ton of time to play so I’d like to keep it pretty cheap, but it doesn’t have to be dirt cheap like what I currently have.

Alright, I think that’s everything. Thanks anyone who read this far. Looking forward to hearing your input!
