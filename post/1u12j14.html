I like sprinting

Usually the people who hate a mechanic are the ones that talk about it the most. So I just wanted to say that sprinting is almost perfect for me.

I HATE the fact that it’s hard to know what is a wall in PoE2 so I just hit walls a lot.

But besides that, I love having to think if it’s safe to sprint, I love trying to sprint through mobs in the trial of Sekemas, tapping the space bar as mobs queue up an attack so it does not knock me.

I like that the game slows down when you need to combat ( I do think the slowdown while casting is a bit much)

I know some people hate having to roll first, but if they remove it, it might just be optimal to tap sprint or some other balance issue.

But yah, I just wanted to let people know sprint enjoyers are here.
