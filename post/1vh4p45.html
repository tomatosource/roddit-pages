State of the NBA with new CBA

So we all know the reason Jalen Brown is gone is because of penalties and tax with the CBA right? Made me question Brad saying 2 players can’t take up 70% of the cap. It really felt like plenty of teams are able to do this. Upon further research, there are only 12 super-max players.

Steph Curry

Nikola Jokic

Joel Embid

Giannis Antetokounmpo

Jalen Brown

Jayson Tatum

Luca Doncic

Shea Gilgeous-Alexander

Karl Anthony Towns

Devin Booker

Kevin Durant(not supermax but 35% of cap)

Bradley Beal(not supermax but 35% of cap)

3 more look to hit next year

Anthony Edward’s

Tyrese Maxey

Jalen Johnson

Let’s face it, Steph, Beal and Durant are coming off that list soon. Which essentially brings it back to 12 players. As a Celtics fan, this makes me feel better. To fill out the remaining 13 spots with only 30% of the money left is crazy!

I want to make it clear, I don’t care about the owner’s money. I do understand the thought process though. You are killing yourself if you go over the cap more than once.

My question to everyone is, do you like the new CBA? Do you think it’s fair for teams like Oklahoma City that built their team the right way, have to dismantle it before it gets going. They had to trade off multiple pieces they probably wouldn’t have. The Celtics had to trade Porzingus and Holiday before they wanted. I think last year goes much differently if those guys are here. Then again, maybe we don’t see the jump of Gonzalez or Sheierman if they are here.

I think I like the idea of Tatum being the main big dog. I really want to see what he can accomplish. I feel like Brown was a safeguard. Tatum has a much better shot at MVP now. Now does that make us better? Time will tell. I like the idea of the cast that surrounds him being compliments to him. I do think Paul George is a better compliment at this stage. Not a better player than Brown. Just a better compliment. Also a steading influence for Tatum. We need someone to fill that Hortford role. I think the Hortford plan might keep him healthy. No back to backs. Maybe 40-50 games played. I think someone makes a huge jump this year. Whether it’s Sheierman, Gonzalez, Harper Jr. or Walsh? Or does Pritchard make one more jump? Maybe White rediscovers his shot and averages 21 a game? It will be fun to watch. I think we are in every game. I think we are a top 4 seed. I think Tatum gets his accolades this year. I think he averages 32ppg, 8rpg and 7apg. Multiple triple doubles. And maybe even an MVP. A rediscovered White shot, someone making a big jump, someone else just emerging and a little bit more from Pritchard and maybe we make even more noise. Am I crazy? Thoughts?
