Roller coaster of a year!

It has been a whirlwind of emotions this season for the Celtics.

Aside from JT going down to ruptured Achilles from the playoffs, we started the season losing 4 key players to trade/free agency (Jrue, Porzingis, Big Al and Kornet).

New players joined (and some left) during the off season and before the trade deadline. Basically, it’s almost like a whole new team with the stating lineup, rotation and overall usage of players,

Even before the season started, projections were all leaning to a “gap year”, with records hovering only slightly above 50% that. Started the season 0-3, it seems to head that way.

But eventually, they found their footing and slowly surged forward. JB played like the MVP he really is, Pritchard still is Pritchard and Sam is still Sam. White arguably has more of an off season, but still dependable on the defensive end and hustle plays. Quetta shined as the new starting center. And then you have the supporting cast making their own mark (Baylor, Garza, Walsh, RHJ and rookie Hugo)

The whole season was mostly positive. They shined (mostly) in games we won. But there are losses that are also surprising (and frustrating) - but overall, they over performed beyond expectations.

And now here we are. Blew a 3-1 lead, utterly dominated (at least the last 3 games) by an unfairly ranked 7th seed 76ers.

I get the disappointment, I get the frustration, I get all the anger. But what I can’t accept is all the blaming.

We blame the team for not being hungry enough - but can’t we also see the inexperience and fatigue?

We blame Joe for the coaching - but isn’t it the same coaching that got them the 2nd seed?

We blame them for shooting too many threes - ok this I agree, but also, if the shots go in and we win, do we complain?

Yes there are a lot of lessons to be learned. A lot of what ifs. But we never should hate the management, coaching staff and players. Who doesn’t want to win?!

So to all the real Cs fans, let’s give them the flowers they deserve. We will be back stronger next season!

Let’s go Celtics! ☘️
