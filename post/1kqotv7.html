I’m a Kings fan, How realistic would a Demar DeRozan trade for Porzingis or Holiday be?

I was watching Bobby Marks’s offseason guide for the Boston Celtics and he mentioned that the Celtics are unwilling to be in the second apron due to the luxury tax implications plus various other restrictions that will be placed on them for being repeat offenders in the second apron. Later in the video he mentioned that to get out of the second apron, they would need to clear 24 million in salary for next season.

The only way to do that would be to get one of their starters off their books and logically, I would think Jrue or Porzingis would be the most likely candidates. But due to no team having the cap space to just absorb 30 million in contract, Bobby Marks mentioned how they would most likely need to do various trades to go down in salary, for example 30 million contract for a 25 million contract, 25 million contract for a 20 million contract, etc. And Demar seems like a logical first contract for a process like that as his contract would be going down in salary and Kings can absorb the extra money and Demar might have some interest from other teams especially since in 26-27 his contract isn’t fully guaranteed.

If they were to trade Demar for Holiday, Kings would also have to add a minimum contract to make it work but celtics would still be going down about 6 million in a trade like this.

I’m not saying any trade for Demar or Porzingis would be 1 for 1, maybe there would be some draft compensation as well.
