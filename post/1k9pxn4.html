Some Ideas with Endgame Content

While waiting on the 0.2.0.G patch notes to drop, I’m sharing some ‘shower-thought’ endgame features that I think would be fun to play if added. (Probably super unbalanced)

The baseline assumption would be that you will always be able to 100% target farm (get at least one of the corresponding content every map) a certain mechanic if you allocate all the atlas passive points in that mechanic

Rogue Exiles

1. Anti-Social

Rogue Exiles in maps are accompanied by at least 2 of their party members, up to 4 accompanied partners (6 maximum).

2. All That Glitters

Rogue Exiles always spawn with at least 1 unique item. Rogue Exiles now gain 100% more life and deal 30% more damage per unique item they equip.

3. High Risk, High Returns

Rogue Exiles have 50% increased attack, cast, and movement speed. Rogue exiles drop currency items instead of gear and the drops are lucky.

Essences

1. Crystallized Threats

All naturally spawned rare monsters in your maps have 25% chance to be imprisoned. 50% less rare monsters in your maps.

2. Predicted Outcome

Corrupting imprisoned monsters will now only result in adding an essence or upgrading. 30% increased toughness of imprisoned rare monsters.

3. Superior Genes

Imprisoned monsters have 25% chance to contain a greater essence. Cannot corrupt imprisoned monsters.

Strongboxes

1. Ambushed

Strongboxes in your maps are at least rare. All monster packs will instantly spawn upon activation.

2. Ventor’s Cunning

Each strongbox in your maps has a 1% chance to be upgraded to a Ventor’s Contraption. Base gold cost of the initial activation of the strongbox now has a range of 3000 gold to 5000 gold.

3. Boxaggedon

Your maps have 50% chance to contain 3 additional strongboxes.
Your maps have 15% chance to contain 10 additional strongboxes.
Your maps have 3% chance to contain 20 additional strongboxes.
Your maps have an unknown chance to contain 10 Ventor’s Contraptions.

Sacred Wisps

1. Repossession

Wisps in your maps have infinite duration and can possess an enemy three times before dissipating.
Wisps are freed upon slaying the possessed enemy if they have remaining possession counters.
Possessed wisps now grant 20% increased toughness and will always drop their corresponding socketables.

2. Sacred Automation

Wisps have quadruple movement speed and possession speed.
Wisps automatically travel to the nearest targetable enemy and possess them.

3. The Touch of God

Wisps now possess players instead of the enemy.
Each wisp lasts 1 minute and grants the following:

30% increased attack, cast, and movement speed.
10% increased quantity of items found.
30% increased rarity of items found.
30% increased Damage
