Jaylen Brown

First, I understand this has to be a tank year so the C’s can get a top pick in a loaded draft.

But man… the future looks very bleak. The team as currently constructed has zero shot of winning a title, and I would argue that if JB is still on the team in the next few years it’s practically over for contention.

I appreciate JB and have been an avid supporter of him since he was drafted. However, there are TWO glaring issues that everyone is aware of but for some reason choose to ignore as if it isn’t a huge elephant in the room.

  1. Jaylen’s contract. It’s clearly an issue for any future contention whatsoever. Obviously not his fault, but with the new CBA having two supermaxes on your team makes it rather hard to add legitimate talent.

  2. Jaylen as a player. He statistically and visually has been declining since he peaked in the ‘24 playoffs. Part of that may be his knee injuries from last season and consequently his most recent surgery. His athleticism is shot and he just doesn’t have the burst anymore (I understand he’s getting older, but that doesn’t help his case).

    But, you also have to realize that Brown has a player hasn’t really changed all that much. His issues and weaknesses since 2023 ECF G7 have never actually been fixed and or improved all that much, they just haven’t been magnified to that degree until he’s actually put in a position as the #1 option by the opposing defense. Also known as this season. It’s only been two games into the season, but today’s Knicks game is literally a carbon copy of JB in G6 against the Knicks last playoffs. Just poor shot selection, and decision-making, which in turn leads to his constant turnovers. Also, his defense is vastly overrated as well. Off-ball defense and awareness continues to be a weak point for him.

Maybe it’s another loss to the Knicks that’s got me feeling a certain way, but man I hate to keep biting my tongue on this.

It would suck to have to trade JB and I honestly don’t know what you can even get in return for his contract. But the two above issues I pointed out are unfortunately the harsh truth about this team’s future.

This is going to be a rough season to watch, but it’s C’s forever.
