Golf courses, farms, and even suburban lawns burn through wayyy more water than AI data centers but somehow the outrage is pointed at compute.

This should be a crime!!!!!! Cigarettes and alcohol are legally have to slap on health warnings on packages. But the chemicals sprayed on our food… chemicals on our food. How does that even make sense cmon now.
