BBBB: Beasts, Bolts, Banners and Ballista! The intended way of playing Tactician?

Gamer dad here (about 1.5 hrs per day). I wanna share my self-made approach to tactician in a way, GGG might have thought about it. I did not research anything just tried it and I breeze through T15+ Maps. Recently started to do Pinnacles; Lvl 89 now.

1.Beasts

Why Beasts? After I saw the Keystone “Trusted Kinship” I wanted to try it out, but not in that classic MM kind of fashion. I wanted to be the main Damage Dealer. The Notable “Inspiring Ally” (Companion Damage also affects You) was the main selling point to start the char.

What’s the benefit?

What’s the benefit? Armour Breaker is mandatory because of the combination of the “Exploit Weakness” Support and the Buff “Shard Scavanger”. The beasts will constantly break armour and you will have not only 40% more dmg but also 4s without reloading which is huge damage wise and for sustaining mana.

Ignite with Exposure and shock and haste are other main damage sources for us as a crossbow guy.

The tree has many “12% inc. companion damage; 8% inc. damage while in the presence of your companion” nodes, which are 20% nodes in most cases. “Watch how we do it” gives raw damage to our companions. That means that the beasts themselves do a substantial amount of damage.

How does it work?

I looked for the following affixes on my beasts in that order:

  • Armour Breaker
  • All dmg ignites
  • Haste Aura
  • All dmg shocks
  • Other Utility
  • Other Damage

2.Bolts

Why Bolts? The beasts are the frontline, so we shoot. I wanted artillery style gameplay. The buffs to crossbows and especially “Siege Cascade” made me choose this. And it is also on the picture of the ascendancy. :-)

What’s the benefit? The pinning nodes on Tactician are incredibly good with “Siege Cascade”. 280% more damage vs. pinned enemies! And all the fire damage can pin now and they can’t act! No brainer!

How does it work? But SC is soooo clunky? Yes, it is, when You don’t overcome the following problems:

  • Projectile Speed
  • AoE for Attacks
  • Reloading

The reload is solved with the beasts as mentioned.
Projectile Speed is mandatory because the mobs will outrun the explosion otherwise. I felt comfy with 120%+ PS. Look for a “flexed crossbow” base type, take “Acceleration” support and some nodes on the tree!
The AoE is the best part of the skill because it shotguns! The bigger the packsize (atlas tree!) the better! You need to invest in AoE until you reach about 1.3 metres to the explosion part. The clear is amazing due to the seeking missiles. One of the most satisfying skills I played in ARPGs.

Addendum: Got a crossbow with “Loads 2 additional Bolts” which allows for almost no reloading (+“15% chance to not consume a bolt” on tree): 4s. Scavange, 2s. downtime.

3. Banners

Why Banners? I saw the new Armour “The Fallen Formation” and wanted to try it. There is a Banner on the picture of the tactician again.

What’s the benefit? War-Banner-Double-Dip with the beasts! The unique armour “The Fallen Formation” is mandatory.
It is a massive 50%+ more multiplier for you and for them.

How does it work? Banners are moaa clunky and the I don’t wanna grab the banner nodes!
No probs. Weapon swap is our friend. Everything snapshots: Take “Arvil’s Wheel” in weapon set II. It gives up to 50% inc. duration and you can use shield charge. I took the 3 “Weapon Swap Speed” nodes, too, so there is almost no delay between shield charging, placing banner and shooting. Keep in mind that your banner lingers for 4s. thanks to “Bannerman” notable. The linger also works if you are NOT in weapon set II! It seems to give that banner a modifier to linger on you while placing. Place the banner while bossing or on a highground, just like a tactician would do it! :-)

4. Ballista

Why Ballista? You know it. It is on that fckn pic.

What’s the benefit? Faster pinning and Rage for you and the beasts thanks to “Font of Rage”. With the boar and its innate 25% chance to bleed, you can aggravate on pin oder do more damage with the “Ripwire Ballista”

How does it work? The Ballista is just utility but can do a significant amount of damage and pin buildup.

-–

TL;DR

Clear: Press “Siege Cascade” and the screen explodes. More monsters = more damage!

Bossing: Banner into Ballista; The beasts will ignite (exposure), shock (inc. damage) and break armour; Siege Cascade will consume the armour and trigger the Scavanging-Buff and the boss will most likely be pinned. Then you fire for 4s. straight with maximum damage (398% base+280% more).
