[Jason Collins] on his Stage 4 Brain Cancer diagnosis: “To me it’s like, ‘Shut up and go play against Shaq.’ You want the challenge? This is the challenge. And there is no bigger challenge in basketball than going up against prime Shaquille O’Neal.”

I know we usually come here for highlights and trade rumors, but Jason Collins just penned one of the rawest, most warrior-mentality letters I have ever read.

He revealed today that he has Stage 4 Glioblastoma (the same aggressive brain cancer that took his mental clarity and short-term memory in a matter of hours). But instead of folding, he is treating it exactly like an NBA defensive assignment.

He is currently in Singapore undergoing an experimental chemotherapy because standard treatments don’t work on his specific mutation. He knows the prognosis is usually 11-14 months, but he says he wants to be the first person through the wall (referencing Moneyball) to help find a cure for others, even if it doesn’t save him.

Absolute legend. Whether you watched him play or not, that “Shut up and play against Shaq” mindset is something we can all learn from.
