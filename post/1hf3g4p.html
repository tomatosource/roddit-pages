The Wizards have only played 1 game against teams with 10 or less wins

Make no mistake, the Wizards are a bad basketball team and are likely to finish with the worst record in the league (by design). But their schedule so far this year has been absolutely brutal. As a fan, it’s felt like they have played the best teams in the league night after night so far so I decided to check their schedule.

As it turns out they have not played a single team with less than 10 wins so far this year (they have one game against a 10 win team in Detroit so far and none against the other 10 win team, Brooklyn).

Zero games against: Portland, Philly, Charlotte, Toronto, Utah, New Orleans.

By contrast, they have played Cleveland 3X, Boston 3X (after tonight). In total, they’ve played 13 games against the top 10 teams in the league record-wise as of December 15th. Obviously playing the Wizards inflates a teams record, but I don’t think anyone would argue these teams are only good because they faced the Wizards multiple times.
