The best players of the 2020’s if we used average MVP shares

First of all, I just want to point out the methodology is flawed since MVP is a narrative award, and decades are an arbitrary cutoff, MVP is a regular season award, and we still have 4 years left in this decade. This is more just a fun statistic than meant to be a “top 15 debate” or anything like that

This is a list of a players average MVP shares for the past 6 seasons
