Just a FYI on getting Rakiata Flow.

You dont need a fully upgraded waystone. Ive done probably 20 Jade isles, and never got it. Ran a normal rarity waystone, and got my first one. Fast forward 2 hours did the same thing but magic rarity, and got my 2nd one.

So, for those of you like me that dont have a nuke build, and struggle with some bosses, do yourself a favor…. run a normal 15 waystone. Its much easier, and seems the drop rate is fine.
