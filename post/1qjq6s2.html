I’d like to share my happiness (game progression)!

Hello there,

I just like to share my happiness over my personal journey through POE2 which started mid-v0.2. Since then, I play POE2 exclusively and excessively. Today, I want to share my happiness concerning my personal progression through the game. Knowing a lot from my on and off relationship with POE1 I already was able to reach a lot of the games content in V0.2, but over the last few days I just reached it all.

This is my story:

As usual (for me), I started 0.4 with a frost monk, my formerly most loved build. I then played druid (did not like it very much) and then turned to a very aggressive storm weaver which was/is a lot of fun.

Then I started the character that changed my experience with the game forever: my bleed-based pew-pew-bulletstorm mercenary. He was extremely fun and nice to play and progressed up to t15-maps. Where the magic happened: my first fracturing orb that I was able to sell for several divines. These allowed me to push my equipment way further which then allowed me to kill arbiter, so I was able to juice my tablets so much more.

Now I am sitting deep in endgame. I run maps (temple not so much) in no time. Yesterday I had my first map dropping two divines. And over the course of the last few days there is so much stuff dropping that I was able to buy equipment for every slot possible. The pew-pew got so much stronger that I don’t even look at the modifiers of my maps anymore - I am just able to kill fast and survive long enough (usually two abyss and one irradiated tablets).

Yes, one needs some luck. But once you set your foot in the doorstep and realize that you sit on lots and lots of currency you can just trade for, there is no stopping. I am not ashamed to express that I am not able to craft myself. But since the introduction of the market place and all I would say that every player has the chance to reach endgame.

Yesterday I switched to headhunter. Not only was I able to buy one, I was able to buy two new rings to adjust my resistances. Both of them were really expensive, but I was able to just buy them. Now the game has totally changed to something where I currently don’t know, if I like it. I just can’t see what’s happening anymore while I rush through the maps like never before. It’s extremely fun but feels somewhat wrong at the same time. Can’t wait for the weekend to sink another way too many hours in this game.

Over the course of the last two weeks or so and without playing temple I was able to (rough estimate) change currency worth maybe 250 divines to update my gear. It was basically one very, very happy drop that put me into the lane and from there and basically through maps alone there was no stopping. I even got another fracturing orb, several high value omens and so on.

Happy hunting and good loot!
