Forget Advanced Stats: Jaylen Brown doesn’t look great according to the simplest stat ever

Since Jaylen Brown was traded most people have been saying the Celtics got screwed and alot of those same people are also saying the league is hating on Jaylen Brown because they overvalue advanced stats.

So instead of advanced stats why don’t we use the most basic and inclusive stat possible.

Celtics with Jaylen Brown

440-234 - 65% win-rate

Celtics without Jaylen Brown

90-36 - 70% win-rate

Bonus, here’s a season by season breakdown:
What Is The Celtics Record With And Without Jaylen Brown By Season | StatMuse

As we can see, in most seasons, in what are largely decent samples, the Celtics are not significantly better with Jaylen Brown than without over 82 games.

Of course his defenders will make excuses like “weaker oppoistion when he doesn’t play” and “he turned it up in the playoffs” but Actual Superstars almost never need these excuses because they’re actually great.

Cue people citing an eyetest (without ever breaking down film) or one finals performance (where he wasn’t even the best player on his team) where he clamped one-leg Luka, or media votes (wow he was 6th in MVP!!!!).

These are obviously just lame deflections from people who aren’t actually able to come up with explanations for why the supposed top 20 player basically never shows top 20 impact. So instead of pretending the Earth is flat, I’m going to explain why it’s round and Jaylen Brown is indeed, not worth more than 2 first round picks plus Paul George

Scoring a Bunch is actually, not, underrated

“High PPG is underappreciated!”

Nope:

(10 years)
Hakeem takes 33-win teams to 48 wins, 15 win lift
Jordan takes 38-win teams to 53.5 wins, 15 win lift
Magic takes 44-win teams to 59, 15 wins lift

(12 years)

Magic Johnson(3x MVP) 1980-1991
Lakers are +0.8 without, +7.5 with

Micheal Jordan(5x MVP) 1985-1998
Bulls are +1.3 without, +6.1 with

Hakeem(1x MVP) 1985-1999
Rockets are -2.8 without. +2.5 with

One of these three players averaged 30+ points. One averaged around 25. And one averaged under 20. Yet, they all provide similar value to their teams.

Enter Jaylen Brown who, unlike any of these 3 players,

-&gt; is a negative or nuetral effeciency scorer

despite playing

-&gt; on one of the most spaced out teams in nba history

Jaylen Brown being able to chuck 25 plus points doesn’t make him a superstar. On that effeciency he needs to bring oher things to the table like…

Can’t run an offense

Playmaking is actually more important than scoring historically at least if you go by the best offenses and the best offensive signals (how much a team improves with a player offensively) and unlike actual offensive engines Brown struggles at being the lead initator or a creator for a team unless he’s playing second units**.**

Despite not being a high-usage ball-handler, he turns the ball nearly as much as he assists and despite not creating much or scoring effeciently he likes to have the ball alot.

Overhyped Defense

Brown offers basically no rim protection (most important part of defense), and is a middling team defender who doesn’t provide significant help for teammates

He is a good man defender….which is less valuable than any attribute i just said

Overall

-&gt; While pretty much every all-star sees their teams win percentage improve with them over the course of 82 games, his team’s generally don’t making him a massively negative outlier.

-&gt; He’s not effecient at his best skill and offers very little outside of it.

-&gt; Is approaching the wrong side of 30

-&gt; Is one of the top 10 biggest cap hits in the league

Losing him for multiple first round picks and an all-star calibre player is a steal. The Celtics are, despite all the criticism from box-watchers and hesi-tween fanatics, going to

-&gt; win 50+ games

-&gt; make it as far or further in the playoffs as they did with Jaylen Brown last season
