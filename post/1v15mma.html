PSA: Low Level Trials Let You Fully Change Ascendancy

In previous patches, you would have to do the highest-tier trials to switch ascendancies if you had all 8 points.

It now appears you can swap ascendancies in the lowest-level trials, even if you have all 8 points!

Edit for clarity: you can change your ascendancy (even with all 8 points) after the rock boss in Sekhimas or the first boss in Chaos (make sure you have a 4-room inscribed ultimatum)
