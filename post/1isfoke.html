Modifiers need to be phrased more clearly.

I’m a newish POE player (played POE1 briefly), so it’s possible that there’s context I’m missing, but I find it really difficult to interpret some of the modifiers on items. For instance, “Original Sin Amethyst Ring” says “X% of elemental damage converted to chaos damage” - and maybe there’s a standard phrasing for each effect that I’m not familiar with, but I have no idea if this means damage *taken* or damage *dealt*. After some googling, my understanding is that this applies to damage *dealt*, but I’m still not 100% certain.

There are numerous other instances, such as the simple “25% reduced damage” on “Leer Cast Hooded Mask” which, based on context clues, I *assume* means 25% reduced damage *dealt* - but again there’s no way for me to confirm this *in the actual game*.

Even in the passive tree - I recall several frustrated players posting here about the Blood Mage ascendancy nodes not working the way they expected (I think it was the leech mechanic only working on hits, which is never clarified).

This game already has a high barrier for entry, just due to the sheer number of mechanics and terminology new players are required to familiarize themselves with, but the phrasing of these modifiers is something that still trips me up after 300+ hours.

This all may seem like a minor inconvenience, and I’m sure more seasoned players can better interpret these based on experience, but when I’m considering trading (and have traded) currency for items, spending skill points on passives, even just theory crafting builds, that may not work as expected; - it really emphasizes the lack of clarity.
