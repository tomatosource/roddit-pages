People here are sleeping on the CBA

I see all the posts about trading for Giannis and adding this or that piece. One thing to remember: Celtics ducked the tax by trading away assets last year, and they need to remain under the tax this year in order to reset the related clock and all the restrictions imposed on them by blowing through the second apron in previous years.

This isn’t about saving money. In order to go all in and build a monster roster, the Celtics must first remain under the tax and reset.

So, where does that leave us?

Brad does want to make major moves this summer (and he does), one way to do so is to trade some pieces in exchange for assets that save us salary this year and give us options for the future. That means trading up in the draft, getting more picks, getting younger cheaper developing players, and creating or rolling over some Trade and Mid Level Exceptions where possible.

The new CBA rewards patience and sequencing. The Celtics may need one transition year before they can realistically build another superteam.
