Say Tatum comes back by March 1st, what’s your best “screw it we’re going for it” trade to swing us into a real contender?

I, like 99% of you, constantly go back and forth between “we’re tanking and that’s okay I like high picks” and “we are gonna be around .500 till Tatum comes back then we’re contending again”

Currently im leaning more towards the latter, and you want to know why? There’s one huge reason why that I think everyone tends to skip over or not think about- you never fucking know man

You never know if someone(s) on okc get injured, or they get knocked out by Denver, or any number of things happen that lead us to another real shot at a chip. For that reason, I cannot vote for tanking unless we actually have no punchers chance. We have championship pedigree and these guys will absolutely jump on the smell of blood from another ring.

Now that that’s out of the way, I came up with three “screw it we’re going for it” trades, curious to hear what you guys come up with

Trade 1: Anthony Davis for white/simons(Dallas sends exum into brooklyns cap space for a 2nd rounder

-mixed reviews on this one, but say AD comes back in a week and plays for the next 2 months no issue, looks like a beast putting up 26/12/4 or whatever. Whatever this is a “screw it” trade anyways

Trade 2: mark williams for Sam Hauser

-this one is very simple; phoenix has 4 centers that all need minutes, Williams is having a great year but is due to get paid. We take that risk and pay him this summer, nothing CRAZY but maybe we have him for 3 years 40 or something. When healthy he’s a really good player and it’s a straight swap for Hauser, who actually helps phoenix a ton, they’re pretty solid defensively but they could use more shooting

Trade 3: simons/hauser/2026 and 2028(worst of ours or San Antonio’s) firsts for zubac/bogdan/brown

-this scenario is very fun, basically the clippers continue losing, Zubac continues to underperform, and they clippers see the bleak future ahead of them, this trade nets them 2 firsts to bring in some young talent/assets, plus a young scorer in simons and a really good long term role player who could start with kawhi and harden in Hauser. Boston gives up the first this year and the 2028 one which is swapped with San Antonio for a franchise center in zu

Which one do you prefer? Do you have better ideas?
