[HC SSF] You have to fall deep to climb high. My grind to lvl 100

Hey fellow redditors

tldr: I reached lvl 100 in hc ssf after dying at 99 last season. In this post I would like to share some of my experiences during this journey.

A few days ago I reached a personal milestone by hitting lvl 100 in hc ssf. I am playing PoE 2 since January 2025 and my steam tells me that I played for 2081 hours (minus some time afk in hideout) since then. Because of this hefty amount of playtime I would like to share my opinion about different aspects of the game. My perspective is only about the SSF mode since I didnt get into trade yet (but I really want to since it adds a whole economy system and groupplay to the game).

Quick Review of my death in 0.2:
Molten Vault -&gt; Headhunter Belt -&gt; Shroud walker Modifier -&gt; Teleported to boss standing in Lava -&gt; More than 600 hours played time deleted in a blink of an eye.

0.2 Experiences:
First I would like to review quick my experiences in 0.2. As you know the deterministic crafting options were very limited before this season. During my journey to 99 it felt like expedition is by far the most effective way to craft good gear as a SSF player. Recombinating allowed you to create good bases and the expedition vendors got some incredibly good items if you bothered with it. Also lush isle logbooks lvl 82 provided great experience even at lvl 99 and you got a lot of itemlvl 82 bases to craft on. Upside you didnt have to set anything up at all compared to mapjuicing. Downside, you had to place all the explosions every time which can be really annoying if you spam a lot of logbooks.
Essences were completely useless and corrupted Essences were non existent. I didnt found a single one in more than 600 hours played time.
I also did a lot of breachruns for breachrings. Since it was incredibly hard to craft a sick breachring even with recombinate start, I just farmed multiple stashtabs of breachrings. Many of the t4 or t5 rings rolled very decent naturally.
I almost didnt bother at all with rituals last season because the omen just didnt feel worth in SSF.

Changes in 0.3:
Now what did change with 0.3 for me? Because of the abyss mechanic, we got so much more ways to craft, that expedition went from super important to basically irelevant except recombinate which is still an awesome tool to craft good bases. On the other side rituals became much more important even for ssf and i realised very quickly that i cant ignore them anymore if I want to craft really good items. Breachruns are still super important for rings and catalysts. It was absolutely astonishing for me in what ways you are now able to craft in SSF with all the changes and it feels you get rewarded for crafting properly.

Performance:
I love the aesthetics of the game and the overall look but performance is in my opinion the number one thing where something has to be done. Delirium always caused performance issues but now with the abyss mechanic added it makes some superjuiced maps nearly unplayable. This happened sometimes to a point where I was annoyed doing super juiced delirium overrun maps and almost felt happy when i had to do travelnodes.

Mapjuice:
At first it felt like the direct mapjuice with tablets is a downgrade to the system before. Even though it was super annoying to find good tower setups, the juice potential was way higher before the towerchanges. It looks for me like tripple tablet in a map is now the equivalent of a two tower setup. On the bright side, the new system gives you much more freedom and allows you to progress the atlas instead of farming clusters. Downside of this, your atlas becomes way too big way too fast. When I was lvl 99 my atlas took extremely long to load and it was a pain to navigate even with bookmarks. If I had to progress my atlas again I would probably go straight only in one direction all the time to avoid this problem to an extent.

Some facts and stats about my 0.3 adventure:

I died twice before hitting 100 this league.
First time at 96 in chaos trials. I am still not sure what killed me but it looked like it was a stormcaller rune which bursted me down.
Second time at lvl 88. Rolled like a champ straight in the spearvolley from savannahboss and got deleted.

My playedtime when I hit 99: 13days18hours
My playedtime when I hit 100: 23days11hours
Monsters killed when I hit 100: 3’128’945
Money invested in the game: I dont know how i feel to admit that but its more than 700 dollars.
