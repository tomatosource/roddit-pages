JJ Redick said in a recent interview that “there’s guys who have won championships that I wouldn’t say are winning players”. Do you agree with this concept and if so, which players?

Full context of Redick’s quote was talking about these types of players and how they contrast with Chris Paul

He’s the ultimate winner. There’s a guy who works in the NBA that I’ve known for almost 20 years now and we talk about this all the time. There’s guys who have won championships that I wouldn’t say are winning players and there’s guys who never won a championship who are ultimate winners. That’s who Chris Paul is.

Source: https://youtu.be/__AEqviZ_Wc?si=2nrvYpwauPvEg6SR&t=176

The list of good players who didn’t win a championship is well known so I’m interested in this other kind of player.

Suppose we exclude benchwarmers like Adam Morrison and such who were on the roster but weren’t real rotation players on championship teams.

Two examples I thought of:

  • Nick Young, 2017 Warriors. Played 80 regular season games including 8 starts at 17 minutes a game. Minutes dipped in the playoffs but he still had 2 starts. Generally known for memes as much as basketball, I don’t think he comes to mind for anybody with the phrase “winning player”. Believe he came into camp out of shape that year too.

  • Antoine Walker, 2006 Heat. By some measures one of the least efficient high volume players ever. Also a bad defender. He played 27 minutes a game and all 82 regular season games for the Heat, and was a starter for all 23 playoff games. Honorable mention might go to his teammate on this team, Jason “White Chocolate” Williams.
