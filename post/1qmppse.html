Are we about to see the end of an era with the 2000s players retiring?

Even though it’s 2026, we still have some players left in the big leagues who played “way back” in the 2000s (yes, everyone is getting old).

Last season we had Hill (05), Morton (08), Scherzer (08), Carrasco (09), McCutchen (09), Robertson (08), Turner (09), Chavez (08), Verlander (05), Kershaw (08) as the players who debuted in the MLB in the 00s who were still playing (might be more I’m missing?)

Of those 10 players, I don’t believe any of them are currently signed to a contract? I’m sure someone might offer a guy a ST invite or sign them later on, but who could wind up being the last player from the 00s, and do you think 2025 was that year, could 2026 be that year?

For reference, Adrian Beltre was the last active 90s player, playing his last game in 2018. Omar Vizquel was the last of the 80s retiring in 2012.
