What is the greatest single game regular season or playoff game performance of all time?

There’s multiple ways to interpret this imo. You can go off of a purely statistical narrative, or you can take into account stakes or legacy. Statistically compared to leave average stats Wilt’s 100 point 25 rebound game is probably the best combination of offense and defense, while Luka’s 73/10/7 game is probably the greatest offensive game, and you could make a case for either being better overall. Going off of narrative something like Kobe out scoring the opposing team through 3 quarters, 81 points, Klay’s 60 in 11 dribbles or 37 point quarter, David Robinson’s quadruple double, Luka’s 60⁄2010 or Jokic’s 31/21/22 all have solid cases.

In the playoffs something like Dame Illard’s game with 55/6/10 on 96.8 true shooting has a very good argument for the top spot. Others like Durants 47/17/10, Jordan’s 55pt finals game, LeBron’s 41/8/11/4/3, Barkley’s 56⁄14, Hakeem’s 49⁄25, and Butler’s 56 pt performance all have cases, In terms of narrative or legacy, LeBron’s Boston game 6, Jordan’s Flu game, or Tim Duncan’s near quadruple double all have good cases to be the greatest performance ever.

What would you say is the greatest single game performance ever?
