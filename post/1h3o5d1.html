Every 3-man iteration of Bron + AD + [Lakers rotation player] has a negative net rating this season except for Bron + AD + DLo.

Source: Lakers analytics expert Cranjis McBasketball https://x.com/Tim_NBA/status/1862888510891454664

The common theme the past 2 Lakers regular seasons is that Lebron+AD minutes are positive when they share the court with D’angelo Russell. Last season the trio had a +4.8 net rating. Nearly 20 games into this season every Lakers starter has a negative plus minus except D’angelo Russell.

Redick’s thought process in benching DLo was to reduce defensive liabilities in the starting lineup and improve the bench. While the bench improved, the starters are performing worse particularly Lebron. At this stage of his career, Bron needs a true PG to run the offense and get him easier looks. Reaves isn’t a playmaker and tends to force the issue and it hurts the overall Lakers offense.

In order to maximize the Lakers offense, Dlo must start at PG and Reaves should be benched. The current Lakers roster is flawed with no 2-way players except AD, but the team needs to find 30+ minutes for their most impactful guard DLo. My choice for the optimal starting lineup for the Lakers is Dlo/Reddish/Rui/Lebron/AD. Let’s see how long it takes supposed analytics nerd JJ to figure this out.
