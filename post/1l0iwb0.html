SSF Viability - Simple Solutions to Complex Problems

TLDR: To make SSF significantly more viable with minimal effort, make all items identified on the ground and allow the loot filter to filter by prefix/suffix + Tiers in SSF only

Finding gear in SSF is severely limited by what you pick up off the floor. Due to the limitations of identified gear, you can only pick up so much gear, and the amount of gear you check is limited by your patience. By exposing prefixes and suffixes to the loot filter, player are able to specifically target what their build needs. This also improves the flow of gear progression by allowing players to slowly tighten their loot filter as high quality gear is required for upgrades. For builds that need a specific set of attributes, this would reduce the frustration of picking up entirely useless items 90% of the time. This also keeps the value of high level crafting omens and mechanic in tact by giving end game players the option to refine those last few modifiers that they didn’t get lucky on.

Example:

Imagine a SSF LS Deadeye looking for rings. They would be looking for flat damage and attributes. In the current system, their only option would be to pick up all rings you see, slam currency into them and pray. In this new proposed system they could do the following at different stages of the build:

(Tiers are in PoE 1 style)

Campaign: Filter for rings that have any resistances at all, recolor if they have any flat modifiers

Early Mapping: Filter for rings that have at least 1 Tier 5 resistance, 1 attribute of Tier 4+, and at least 1 flat damage modifier.

Late Mapping: Filter for rings that have Int Tier 2+, Dex Tier 2+, and at least 1 flat damage modifier.

Etc etc, adjusting based on what they need.

This would also add even more value to the rare fractured orbs you find in SSF, where you can filter for specific T1’s that you’d want to build off of, giving another avenue of crafting. (T1 added lighting damage, T1 +Skills, T1 Crit)

We know this is viable way to do filtering since other games have successfully implemented similar filtering systems, but it would be a matter of if it would be simple to implement for PoE. I believe that this would solve the issues with SSF without having to revamp an entirely new set of systems, or adjust drop rates of anything. As someone who has finds themselves looking at trade more than actually playing the game because of upgrade paralysis, I would love to find simple ways to make SSF more viable. I would love to hear ya’ll thoughts &lt;3

Note: I am specifying specifically SSF for this as this might be too powerful for trade, as access to omens is much more common to create mirror level items. Maybe a Unique Tablet could be added that has everything identified so that it is useful for both.
