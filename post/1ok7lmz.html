FYI: Easy way to convert exalts to gold. Easy & cheap millions of gold per hour

Use the Currency exchange to turn exalts into Broken Circle Artifacts & Exotic Coins

Go to Gwennen in your hideout and use the filter at the bottom of the shop screen to only highlight rare items.

Purchase the rare items and immediately sell them back to the NPC for 1.5-4k gold each. Average ~4 items per page.

Use the Exotic Coins to refresh the shop.

Personally, I ignore quivers since they often sell for under 1k gold so it’s a little inefficient but not a big deal either way.

So that’s about 20k/gold per 10 seconds of work at the most. 120k gold per minute, ~7million gold/hr.

I get bored well before that, but I’ll have PoE2 in a window and mute it with a youtube video or podcast going and just go to town until I have a few million gold saved up.

Also a quick tip, gold cost in the currency exchange is based on the number of items you’re purchasing so if you’re selling something take a minute to covert the exalt value to chaos/annul/div. Assuming it’s similar/better, you’re better off selling for fewer higher value currencies vs a ton of exalts.

This method is much cheaper and faster than buying runs of the gold farming map. It’s not as fast as something like buying a quad tab of bulk rares for a div, so it’s good for those middle-class income levels where you’ve got a good income stream but nothing godly.
