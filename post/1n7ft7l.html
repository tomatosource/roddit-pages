this may be a good time to remember the Clippers’ suspicious Blake Griffin / Kawhi Leonard timeline

On July 19, 2017, the Los Angeles Clippers re-signed their superstar Blake Griffin to a five-year, $173M contract. They talked about how his name would be in the rafters one day. It was a major commitment between the two parties to keep their star in tow.

But during that 2017-18 season, the Spurs were having friction with their own star, Kawhi Leonard. He played some, missed some games, played some more. Ultimately, his last game in a Spurs uniform was January 13th, 2018. He’d sit out after that.

Two weeks later, the Clippers decided to reverse course in a major way and trade Blake Griffin, only a few months into his new massive extension. The move would enable them to have cap space for Kawhi Leonard’s free agency (in summer 2019).

Was it simply buyer’s remorse? A logical way to bide their time for someone like Kawhi Leonard in the future?

Or was there something more finite and tangible under the table there?
