Eurobasket Day 8 Recap and NBA Player Stats [Last Game of Group A nad B]

Again, wasnt able to do a recap yesterday but today I am back with the final day of Group A and B and the first quarterfinal matchups.

Group A:
Estonia vs Portugal
First game was already big as the winner of thsi amtch would qualify for the knockouts. Thats why its no surprise that this was a nervous game from both teams but Estonia secured the first Lead of the game and lead at half time. The game never felt like out of grasp from either team as it went back and fourth with lots of misses. With a minute left Rafael Lisboa hit a shot from way beyond the Ard to tie the game for Portugal and shift the momentum towards them. Kristian Kullamae missed the last Estonian chance much to the joy of Portuguese players who reach the next round for the first time.
68:65 Portugal

Turkey vs Serbia:
Now here come the big boys,Who would take the first Seed potentially avoiding what would be most liekly (more on that later in Group B) Germany in their half of the match. Serbia without Bogdanovic and Vukcevic who was out with a minor injury or a really well playing Turkey? Well it was a thrilling game and I really recommend actually checking it out. Turkey narrowly took the first quarter 19:18 but Serbia responded with a way more offense focused quarter to bring the game to a 49:46 score at half time. Turkey took back the lead as they won the third quarter by 4 (28:24). The fourth turned into Nikola Jokic vs Sengun and Shane Larkin but in the end Turkey was the one with a small upset taking the first seed and looking strong so far. Sengun was the MVP of not just this match but also the day.
95:90 Turkey

Group B:
Montenegro vs Great Britain
This game was deciding the 4th Seed of Group A. Montenegro needed a win to be safe and would be eliminated on a loss. Meanwhile the Brits needed a win by 33 or higher to get through the Group Stage AND hope that Sweden loses to Lithuania. Any win between 0 and 33 would put Sweden through the group stage. Now Montenegro was the big favorite coming into that game. Surprisingly they struggled a LOT in here and at half time they were down by 6. This lead was something the UK held on for the rest of the game in nervewrecking last 3 Minutes but in the end they win their first match since 2013 and eliminate Montenegro. They did not clear 33 Points however, meaning that they send Sweden through. MVP of the Game was Myles Hesson with 25 Points. Vucevic now also retired from the national team after this.
89:83 Great Britain.

NBA Player Stats:
Vucevic: 31/11/7 with 1 Turnover.

Sweden vs Lithuania
Like I said I was impressed by Sweden throughout the tournament and after the Montenegro result they could have just chilled out. Lithuania needed a win to force a potential tiebreaker with Germany and Finland aswell as securing second place. But the Swedes fought hard once again leading by 6 at half time before Lithuania narrowly came back and managed to close the game out when Pelle Larssons 3 Pointer just missed the mark at the buzzer. Jokubaitis being injured is a big problem for them but with this win they secured their Top 3 Seed. Meanwhile Sweden can be happy to advance from Groups.
74:71 Lithuania

NBA Player Stats:
Valanciunas: 18/9/2 with 4 Turnovers
Larsson: 18/5/2 with 1 Turnover

Germany vs Finland
Could anyone stop Markkanen? Lauri has been the standout player so far this tournament and Germany has been the standout team. However the answer became clear quite fast, that the Germans wont be getting upset today, as Isaac Bonga showed exactly how to stop Lauri, while Franz Wagner was unguardable for the Fins ain the first quarter. Up 14 at half Germany decided to Rest Franz a bit while Finland also didnt play Lauri as much as the other games preserving some Stamina for the rest of the tournament. Germany finish with an insane +164 differential aswell as the best offense this tournament but missed being the first team ever with 4 consecutive 100 Points Games at Eurobasket.
91:61 Germany

Since Group A is crossing Group B here are the confirmed quarterfinals:

Turkey vs Sweden
I think Sweden will fight but the quality difference is just too big. Turkey has Sengun and a lot of Euroleague talent and showed team cohesion so far. Should be a more or less easy win.

Serbia vs Finland
Serbia was the big favortie coming in but with Bogdanovic out of the tournament and now Vukcevic being a small question mark, it got harder. Germany should currently be ranked ahead of them and probably Turkey aswell. Finland only has 1 Player and as evident from this year and also the World Cup if he cant dominate thy gonane lose. Serbia still has Jokic, so this round should be fine.

Latvia vs Lithuania
Interesting game. I give Latvia the slight edge because Jokubaitis is not playing anymore. Porzingis and BErtans have the quality to win them the game. But with Valanciunas this could go either way.

Portugal vs Germany
Portugal has scored 63 Points per game which is the second worst of the tournament ahead of Cyprus. They face the team with 106 Points per game which is 15 Points better than the second best offense at 91 Points per Game from Turkey and Slovenia respectively. Yeah that might be a tough ask.
