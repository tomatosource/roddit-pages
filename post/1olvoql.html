Hali’s injury last year overshadowed the Celtics’ tragedy

I’m a Pacers fan, and I was heartbroken for legit entire months after Hali’s injury, both for Hali himself and for this organization which no matter suffers from one tragic event after the other. I 100% believe with no injuries the Pacers beat OKC.

But deep down, I know that if we’re playing the “no injuries” game, the real winners of the 24-25 NBA Finals would be the Boston Celtics. There was no way the Pacers could beat them last season.

That said. I’m still sad to this day. This injury, on a game 7, is tragic. But it doesn’t change the fact that the Celtics suffered from just as tragic circumstances
