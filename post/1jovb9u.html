Active block suggestion

Active block is pretty garbage, and it’s even more obvious with how unique and strong parry looks.

Active block needs something else to feel good and I think that could be in the form of an additional effect.

At the end of your block, release a shockwave that applies stun buildup and pushes enemies back (maybe some damage too?) depending on the armour value of the shield and the amount of stagger bar build up from that block.

This would have synergies with basically every mace build and helps with the slow moving weakness of that archetype, in addition to being a very fitting thematic addition.
