A Plea for Trade

As a console player, one of the main highlights of playing PoE2 after a couple leagues playing PoE1 was being able to trade with PC players. The market is way more active and there’s way more gear available to choose from to find the piece just right for my build (as well as cheaper prices). Also as a casual player who only plays 10-20 hours a week, this has made scaling my build feel a lot more doable.

But trade is very hit or miss, and I’ve seen lots of complaints on here of either a) people never responding, or b) scammers. Why not fix both and have fixed price buyouts?

They already built so much of the architecture with the trade site, being able to list items for specific prices, and even the PoE1 console trade experience where you can browse and make offers on items in game. Why not just allow players to buy and sell items without having to meet in game to trade? It doesn’t need to be as complicated as an auction house, just allow people to immediately buy items at the prices they’re currently listed.

This would get rid of a lot of the scamming. This would allow you to buy items from people that are unresponsive or offline. This would allow players playing late in the league or at odd hours to still trade easily.

I recognize it might make it easier for certain bad actors to rig market prices, but that could be addressed through some sort of ceiling on number of trades per hour or day. Honestly it might also improve that issue as it becomes more of a free market than it is today, with less need to always be online in order to trade.
