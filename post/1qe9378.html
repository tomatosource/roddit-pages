I don’t understand Delirium Mirrors and Breaches.

I get how they work, but no matter how much I do them, no matter how much I fine tune my build, they never seem to work for me the way they work for everyone else.

I get that the principal behind both mechanics is killing everything on screen as fast as possible and passing through whatever their point of interest is, but no matter what I do, mine always seem to time out and end somewhere around 30 seconds, getting me a piddly 2 or 3 currency each. I’ve seen other people do these with similar if not even less impressive clear times and drag the encounter on for minutes at a time, eventually getting dozens of the respective currency.

I don’t get it.

At first they were just genuinely too difficult for me and I would time out trying to kill enemies. That I get. But this season playing a C/ED lich and a LA Deadeye, I’ve been wiping the screen with faster and faster kill times, even to the point where there are just straight up no more enemies on screen for a few seconds, and I have to go look for them.

What gives? Are the mechanics not meant to be fully usable until you have 4+ points in their respective endgame trees? Because that’s kind of the impression I’m getting.
