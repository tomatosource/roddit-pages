Mark williams: Hauser+2 seconds for Williams

Claxton: simons/‘26 first/2 seconds for Claxton

\-nets get off his significant contract, open up their center rotation for Wolf/sharpe/clowney and give them significant cap space this summer to spend, they also get an extra first in this draft. His playmaking and switch defense is very intriguing for our system as he’d be able to emulate a lot of the Al horford stuff we used to have

Zubac: simons/hauser/2026 first/2 seconds for zubac/bogdan/brown

\-zubac would cost the most of the three, super reliable defensive rebounding beast, probably the best fit/most valuable guy we could get. I think replacing the 2nd first with Hauser and a couple seconds in practice kinda works, if the clips would jump on this I think that would be a great trade
