Runes/Socketables vs Crafting Bench

The more I’ve thought about this debate, the more I see the reasoning for the change, but I also feel as though there needs to be more of a middle ground.

I think the Socketable system is better for new players.

I think the fact a Socketable is essentially an “Additional affix”, rather than an imprinted affix which takes a slot, results in new players not needing to know about Prefixes/Suffixes early on, they can essentially “Fix” issues, like resistances, with any item. They’re encouraged to make a logical decision like using a 6 mod item rather than a 5 mod item with a specific empty Prefix/Suffix slot to solve their problem.

There’s two core problems I see with the system, especially for new players.

  1. A fear of using them, in case you find an “Upgrade”, but you don’t want to equip the upgrade, because you lose the Res from the Socketable on your previous item….I thought this problem was something that led to Essence changes in 0.3, for example. This problem also still exists with Runes in the Campaign.

  2. The other issue is the availability of finding the one you want. If you’re not finding Resistance runes, or only ones which help with Fire, for example, then you can’t access the system to solve your Lightning/Cold issues, like you could with the Crafting Bench in PoE 1.

Is the solution, not to find a happy medium, where we as players can “Craft” runes?

I wouldn’t advocate another currency(s) to drop as that would be needless bloat, but maybe it’s something that can also be fixed with Gold?

- A Gold cost to ‘Extract’ Runes using Renly’s bench, so you can imprint them again on your upgrades.

- A Gold cost to ‘Craft’ Runes using Renly’s bench, e.g. Change one Res to another. Like a simple form of Harvest crafting.

As a Core system, I think the fact Socketables are a “Additional Affix” make it easier, at least in theory, for new players as opposed to the crafting bench in PoE 1.

However, the lack of availability to find the ones you want, and also the fear new players have of using them because they’re one time uses only, ends in a scenario where new players especially, likely “Hang on” to them, waiting for an item upgrade, before using them.

I think it’s a good idea to encourage players to engage with the Socketables system and to encourage them to equip upgrades when they drop and extract/imprint Socketables, rather than hang on to them out of fear.

I also think a tutorial at the beginning of the game, explaining how they can “Craft” their runes (Very basically), would help when looking at creating more complex crafting systems in the future.

“Use Renly’s bench to extract a Rune”

“Use Renly’s bench to change resistance type”

etc

Maybe even allow “Gambling” for Runes too, as a way to combat bad RNG?
