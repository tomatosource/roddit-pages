The Knicks had the second-biggest sweep in a Conference Finals ever

So, we’re counting sweeps as the biggest victory. Lose a game, doesn’t count. Then I tried to find the biggest point differential. Basketball Reference had this data but it stops in 2010, so I counted the rest myself, and if I did my math right (feel free to check it) the Knicks have the 2nd biggest conference sweep of all time

Biggest sweeps in the Conference Finals or NBA Finals

  1. 2001 Lakers over Spurs, +89
  2. 2026 Knicks over Cavs, +77
  3. 1996 Bulls over Magic, +67
  4. 2017 Warriors over Spurs, +64
  5. (Tie) 1986 Celtics over Bucks, +60
  6. (Tie) 2018 Warriors over Cavaliers, +60 (FINALS)
  7. 1974 Bucks over Bulls, +57
  8. 1998 Jazz over Lakers, +54
  9. 2015 Cavaliers over Hawks, +53
  10. 1971 Bucks over Bullets. +49 (FINALS)
  11. 1991 Bulls over Pistons, +46
  12. 1987 Lakers vs. Sonics, +45
