Is Pure Life+Evasion actually pretty good?

I’ve been led to believe since Huntress came out that both parry/combo gameplay felt awful and that stacking ES is the only way to play an evasion character.

For fun, I decided to try one last build and see how far an ignite-based explosive spear Amazon would take me if I went full vision gameplay with Parry+Disengage combos for frenzy charges and frenzy-charged explosive spears.

I had a well-rolled Perfidy from another character in my stash so I figured, lets try utilizing Stalking Panther with it. If I’m gonna go evasion, I’ll try stacking some deflection and get an Atziri’s Step. I’ll get a little deflection on gear but otherwise obtain what I can from the tree. 2400 HP, about 30-35k evasion and 85% deflect chance later and I feel way more immortal than I was anticipating.

I’m constantly getting out of juiced abyss situations I thought I’d be totally hosed on. Having access to Parry+Disengage with some stun investment has been huge. Parry feels quite good in 0.4 with the latest changes combined with the new nodes that were added. I literally don’t die, or really even come close. And having the parry debuff on bosses leads to massive ignites from a (buffed in 0.4) Explosive Spear.

Am I saying this is better than ES? Not necessarily.. certainly ES is easier to build conceptually and its values are probably overtuned so it makes sense why its popular. Good evasion gear is also probably harder to come by and evasion only starts to feel good once you have enough of it. But I caution writing off the archetype entirely, especially if we start to see ES nerfs eventually - we’re not that far off from the other defenses having parity.
