I messed up.

I’ve been playing since launch and have really enjoyed the game. My first character was a monk in Season 1, I played a bit of a warrior in Season 2, and now I’m a ranger (Deadeye, daring I know). Having two kids hasn’t given me much time to play, but I was able to dedicate some time over the last two days to finally progress my character. I’ve been stuck on gearing my Deadeye for what felt like a while and wanted to get some divs for much-needed upgrades. I’m not really sure how to craft and am just an all-right player, so I decided to try flipping items on the market. It. Went. Well. I made about 40-ish divs in two days, playing pretty passively. After my last round of sales, I went to exchange my final stack of exalts for some divs. I was going to head to the market, but first, I took a break. When I came back, I sat down, inadvertently set Divine Orbs as “Owned” in the currency exchange, and mashed the place order button. Since I had originally set exalted to owned before I got up, it auto set exalted to “want”. I had not noticed… My. Heart. Sank. I didn’t understand what happened at first and thought I had just misplaced them somewhere in my inventory or stash. I looked everywhere. Nope. Restarted. Nothing. Then, it dawned on me what had actually just happened. I sat back for about a minute and explained to my buddy Chobo on Discord what happened. He tried to console me and told me to contact support, but I knew it was pointless, I messed up. Bad. I tried to get back into flipping on the market, but that experience really knocked the wind out of me. I think I might be done for this season. Just wanted to share. Don’t be like me. Be better. Check your currency exchanges before you mash buttons. I’m gonna go take a shower.
