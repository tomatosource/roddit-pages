My problem with wands/staves

I really dislike the current way skills are locked on a weapon with no way to increase it besides recrafting the whole thing.

Normal skills we can upgrade through uncut gems but the innate skills on wands/staves and some other weapons are stuck as is. It would be nice if we were able to use something similar to the salvage bench or the recombinator to increase the level of skill on your existing weapon.
Having your already crafted weapon in 1 slot and adding 1 or 2 bases with a higher skill level and a small % chance to increase the skill on your crafted one.

For example I’ve crafted my Reap staff on a base that gives me level 18 reap. I’m pushing juiced t15s and cruising through getting higher bases drop. Instead of having to recraft my staff on a level 20 base, I collect some bases with level 19 or 20 to try and upgrade it.

This will keep a demand for the higher bases for the sake of upgrading or using it to craft outright.
