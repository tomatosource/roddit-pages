Warrior Leap Slam

Been playing titan warrior and really enjoying it. It feels like there are 2 main options for clearing: stampede (probably just better) and leap slam (ability feels better to play imo). The issue is leap slam consuming fully broken armor to cause more stun buildup just overall seems like a negative synergy.

  1. You don’t get scavenged plating stacks from using the ability. Sacrificing 40% armor because you don’t want to use stampede feels pretty bad.

  2. Most people are using break armor on stun node. When you stun a boss or elite you don’t want the fully broken armor consumed. Increased stun does nothing for you. You want to use your big damage abilities on the boss who is stunned with fully broken armor.

  3. A support gem was specifically added because too much stun prevents you from using boneshatter. The leap + boneshatter combo was awkward because leap does too much stun damage and you have to sacrifice a support slot just to use it. Obviously more stun buildup doesnt have a lot of synergy with not wanting to fully stun.

So overall just delete leap slam consumes fully broken armor to cause more stun buildup and it would be a better ability. The 500% stun really adds very little with the warrior actually plays imo and what should be a positive is in reality just overall negative for the skill
