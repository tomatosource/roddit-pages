Dear networks and sports leagues: Please offer a “nat audio” option in your streaming packages. I would happily PAY MORE for that option. No additional cost to you, and more income, too. I know you can do it, because YTTV 4K has some “nat audio” games, and the CFP SkyCam option was stadium sound

This post yesterday reminded me about this ongoing gripe of mine. Sometimes ( a lot of the time) I just want the sounds of the game and stadium/arena and not a bunch of announcers.

Networks have been doing *nat sound* or *nat audio* feeds of games for decades, and sending in the raw video to networks.

I don’t want to sync to the radio crew on my phone, or use an app to do it. Just offer it.

No hate to a lot of broadcast teams (KB, Cakes and Ben; Joe B. and Locker; Tyler and Chris; ESPN if they ever want to bring Blackledge and McDonough back together…) but most of the time I just want the sounds and not announcers.

(Possible side benefit to networks and leagues is potential savings next time you negotiate your announcers’ contracts…)
