Idea for rework of the map system

I’ve been thinking about a possible rework for the map system in Path of Exile 2, and I wanted to share the concept and see what the community thinks.

The core idea is to turn maps into a level-based tower system. Here’s how it could work:

  • Each tower level would randomly generate a circle of 50+ maps.
  • You start on Tower Level 1, and once you complete all the maps or finish a “citadel” equivalent (or unlock all key objectives), you unlock Tower Level 2, which gives you a brand new set of random maps to progress through.
  • This progression would continue upward—each level offering fresh content, increasing difficulty, and new challenges.

As someone with OCD who enjoys clearing everything and seeing visual completion, this idea really appeals to me. The current map system can sometimes feel scattered or incomplete, and I think a tower-like structure could give a clearer sense of progression and accomplishment.

This is just a rough sketch of the idea, so any thoughts or improvements are more than welcome!
