[Serious Discussion] The Los Angeles Clippers are historically a worse franchise than the Cleveland Browns

  1. The Base Level of Competence: The Clippers have an all-time winning percentage of .425. Over 56 seasons, they have been a historically losing franchise. The Browns, despite the decades of agony, actually have a positive all-time winning percentage at .511. This means the Browns have, over 76 years, won more games than they’ve lost. The Clippers have not. The baseline is worse.

  2. Playoff Records: Their playoff performance is nearly identical. Despite being an NBA team where the playoffs are much easier to make (19 appearances in 56 seasons) versus the NFL (30 appearances in 76 seasons), the Clippers’ .431 playoff W% is matched by the Browns’ .436. The heartbreak ratio is the same.

  3. Championship misery: Since the NBA-ABA merger and the start of the Super Bowl era, both teams are true members of the Major Championship Zero Club. The Browns are rightfully mocked for their futility in the modern era, but the Clippers match that goose egg perfectly.

  4. Peaks of success: the Browns have made the AFC championship 3 times, which is two more times than the Clippers have EVER made it to the Western Conference Finals.

Yes, the Browns have had some truly abysmal stretches (0-16, the post-hiatus era), but the Clippers have spent the better part of 40 years as the laughingstock of the NBA, and look to be headed right back to it. The Browns’ misery might be louder, but the Clippers’ failure is deeper and more consistent over the long haul. If they had a fan base we might even know more about how bad they have been.

TL;DR: The Browns (.511 W%) have historically won more games than they’ve lost. The Clippers (.425 W%) have lost way more games than they’ve won. Both have 0 modern championships. The browns have had more playoff success (3 semifinals) than LAC (1 semifinal appearance). The Clippers are worse.
