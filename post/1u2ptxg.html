Temple needs to be fixed before it’s nerfed again.

Of all the league mechanics in the game currently Temple is my favorite. I love the idea of building my own dungeon and choosing to juice the temple or rush atziri. Last league I didn’t play it much because I hated the snake strat so this league I took a deep dive. Only to discover this is the most frustrating mechanic in the game.

My biggest issue with it is that it fails at giving the fantasy of building the temple the way I want. Even after figuring out that rooms can only be followed by rooms that upgrade it, restricted rooms stack with themselves, how to lock chains of rooms, etc. It randomly stops following its own rules or has rules which makes no sense. Why do Paths not just let me place them wherever I want? I’m already losing a room a.k.a juice to use it. There’s no reason a Paths can’t just work with any room other than to frustrate the player.

Just today I bricked the Atziri rush in my temple. It was 100% my fault because I locked the wrong room. That’s fine and not where the problem lies. The problem is that the process to rebuild my temple is a jumbled, frustrating mess. There’s no reset button so I’m stuck with bad room chains, the aforementioned rooms don’t work as they should, and it takes a least two maps just to do a temple run. Why can’t we get two energy for every beacon like we did in 0.4?

Needless to say I’m done with Temple this league and will be doing another mechanic until some major changes are made. It’s a shame because it’s my favorite mechanic but I felt that I was fighting the game to enjoy it. There’s plenty of bugs too but I understand it’s early access so I only care about the core mechanics for how it works. Just making Paths work with any room and adding a reset feature would go miles with lowering the frustration. Is there anything else I missed? I know tons of people have more experience with temple from last league so I interested in your thoughts.
