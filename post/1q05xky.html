Lebron James should have 4 years of college eligibility

LeBron James skipped college and went straight to the NBA in 2003. He never enrolled anywhere, so his eligibility clock never started. Just look at James Nnaji: drafted in 2023, played pro overseas, but never signed an NBA contract or attended college. The NCAA just gave him four full years at Baylor because his clock only kicks in when he enrolls. LeBron’s situation is the same: no prior college enrollment. College ball isn’t amateur anymore with NIL deals worth millions. Punishing him for going pro back when that was the smart move feels outdated. Precedents like Nnaji show the NCAA bends rules for guys who waited overseas. As LeBron’s NBA run winds down with the Lakers struggling, this is prime time for a switch. He could enroll, get four years, team up with son Bryce at Arizona for a title run, or go home to Ohio State and pack the house every night. It would break ratings, boost the sport, and let the GOAT chase NCAA glory he never had. No competitive downside. Come on, NCAA: give LeBron his four years.
