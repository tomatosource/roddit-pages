In Korea, fans are building a Kobe exhibition with their own memorabilia — and you can vote on it

Hey everyone, just wanted to share something cool happening here in Korea 🇰🇷🏀

There are so many Kobe fans here who have been collecting jerseys, sneakers, tickets, photos, and cards for years. Now, a museum project is inviting fans to upload their Kobe items + personal stories — and the community votes with likes to decide which ones will be shown in a real exhibition this December. 💜💛

You can check it out too:
👉 elandmuseum.com/g2/bbs/board.php?bo_table=contest

Tip: if you use Google Chrome’s auto-translate, you’ll be able to read all the stories in English.

It’s pretty amazing to see how Kobe’s legacy lives on, not just in the U.S., but all the way in Korea too 🌍🔥 Would love to hear what you think!
