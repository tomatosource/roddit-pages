Feedback from a somewhat casual dad gamer

For context, I’ve leveled 1 character to level 80 this league. I started a couple days late, and was enjoying Amazon while many streamers and reddit were melting down about how terrible Amazon was. I don’t follow build guides, don’t watch a lot of poe2 content, and I don’t buy gear. I’m currently running t9 maps and i have 12 atlas passive points. Much of my gear is still from the campaign or only marginally better than what I was wearing there, but it’s enough to keep pushing on for now.

Common Misconceptions I see:

  1. I see a lot of complaints about 1-button builds not being viable; however, there are many 1-button skills you can use for clearing trash mobs. Similarly, skills like Perfect Strike exist where you can just use one for single-target and be fine.
  2. The word “combo” gets thrown around a lot, but oftentimes it’s just situational power. Big hit throwing a spear with a frenzy charge, big hit when enemy is primed for stun, etc. Sure, you could use a different skill to set those up, but you don’t have to. Additionally, while GGG wants us to use multiple skills, that doesn’t mean that they have to be used at the same time… They’ve designed the monsters around players using multiple skills – use an Unblockable skill vs enemy with a shield, use a fast skill vs weak enemies that charge you, etc. For skills that actually do combo, like ED + Contagion or whirlwind into twister, there was already precedence for those kinds of skills in POE1, and you don’t have to play them if you don’t like it.
  3. There are a lot of complaints about loot… I haven’t ran into any issues, and in terms of sheer numbers of drops, I’m generally seeing a plenty of currency and loot. Maybe it’s because I don’t buy gear, but I had no reservations using regals and exalts whenever i had them and at least rolling magic mods on every spear I saw. But I can appreciate there are unlucky players, and even with similar drops, you might get unlucky when crafting. That said, you can go farm if you get stuck during the campaign. I just resign to having bad resists until the end of the campaign, and just make sure the one I need at the moment is high enough.

Actual problems in 0.2:

  1. mana costs, especially on the bottom half of the tree. There’s only 1 node on the bottom half of the tree that gives mana regen, with the rest being leech or flask related… but leech is limited to physical damage, while a bulk of attack skills deal heavy elemental damage. And even with some physical damage, 8% mana leech feels like it does nothing. Mana costs were manageable with level 16 gems, but since moving up to 17⁄18, I’ve been chugging mana flasks every couple attacks… and that’s with leech and some mana on my gear. I’m afraid leveling my gems any further would completely brick my character – and this is with just 3 support gems on my skills.
  2. lack of jewelers orbs. i found a single perfect jeweler’s orb in 0.1 leveling 3 characters into the 90s and not a single greater orb. I haven’t had a ton of time this league, but I’m sitting at 0/0 and that seems like a very common complaint.
  3. defenses don’t feel worth scaling compared to damage or recovery on the passive tree. Spending your first 20 points on damage makes the game incomparably easier than spending even half of those on defensive nodes.. and that trend continues with utility passives once your damage is good enough.
  4. Situational power shouldn’t depend on damage. For ex: needing to apply shock in order to use primal strikes, but shock application depends on damage dealt. Tanky mob means you do less damage by default, can’t apply shock as easily so you lose damage there, and then can’t get the extra damage from primal strikes on top of it. Maybe this is an issue with me still being on a 4L and using a low-tier weapon, but it feels bad when I melt most rares in seconds, and then I run across a super tank rare that i can’t skip to complete the map and it takes over a minute to kill it.
  5. Damage is too spiky compared to life, especially early on in the campaign. Walking into Clearfell and taking 90% of my life to a single hit from a mob feels like a lot of damage to take at the start of the game.. and elemental damage especially feels overtuned for so early in the game while you have very little access to life and resists. But even when mapping, requiring large life rolls on all of your gear to survive getting 2-tapped by trash mobs makes it very difficult to upgrade gear.
  6. Attribute requirements are high. While not insurmountable, I’ve found myself both in 0.1 and 0.2 taking travel nodes just to get attribute points when I’d really want to take one of several notables I was already next to that would have been useful at the time. I haven’t leveled since they added attribute runes, and I’m sure those help, but the numbers are still pretty steep and the runes don’t really grant that much. This stifles build creativity, as you need so much investment in your primary stat that you often can’t afford to branch out until much later in the game unless you’re running gems at a lower level, and that’s not always an option while leveling. Huntress gets this a little without even branching out, as spears are hybrid dex/str or dex/int/str weapons, but spear gems have full dex requirements

Other places that could use improvement:

  1. Right now there’s limited synergy between skills from different weapon types, which stifles build creativity. For ex, parried debuff is only consumed by 2 skills – and both are for spears. This could easily be a generic off-balance debuff instead and open up for mixing more ways to apply the debuff and consumer skills with other weapon types or even spells. Similar arguments have been made about charges.
  2. As an extension to the above, I think there should be more incentives to use multiple weapon types on a single build. In 0.1, I found Lightning Rod + Galvanic Shards let me swap between a high damage bow+tree with a fast attack speed crossbow+tree… but more often than not, I don’t even see a reason to use multiple weapon sets, let alone multiple weapon types.
  3. Being locked in place while attacking with Melee feels incredibly punishing. Most rare mob mechanics either surround the monster or chase the player, but while projectile attackers and spell casters can move quick enough to dodge both the monsters chasing them, attacks flying at them, and those rare mob mechanics, melee users are don’t get those luxuries. I’m not sure what the answer is here – I’m hopeful that melee having access to faster skills via swords or axes will help, and maybe defense buffs would help, but it definitely feels biased against melee right now.
