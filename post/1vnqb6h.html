A useful interaction on blood mage for those who don’t know.

I never noticed this because I love using Wind Dancer on attack builds for utility, but the on hit proc for remnant generation can be automated with any amount of thorns. With enough mitigation, the sustain from spawning remnants every 3 seconds when you’re hit can make you nearly unkillable while mobbing. Like I said, Wind Dancer can do this too, but one thorns suffix on a piece of armor can let you save the 30 spirit. This is especially useful if you focus on armor instead of evasion. Then, with some armour break in your build, you can slot Scavenged Plating for the armour buff AND it’s thorns buff for a functionally similar result.

Edit: Also, I assume it works on spell builds, too.

I haven’t seen any else mention this, so I thought I should post it. Cheers.
