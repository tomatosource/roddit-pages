Since the CS expanded to 7 games in 1985, there have been 4 out of 40 years where the WS matchup featured one team that won in 4 games and one that won in 7. The team that won in 7 has won all four of those matchups, with no series going longer than 5 games.

This year, the Dodgers swept the Brewers while the Blue Jays needed 7 games to dispatch the Mariners. That means the Dodgers have one week off while the Blue Jays have 4 days between their Monday clinch and Friday WS game 1.

This situation has occurred 4 times in the prior 39 playoffs since MLB expanded the CS to 7 games in 1985. In all four years, the team that won in 7 won the World Series:

1. 2012 World Series: Giants defeat Tigers in 4 games

The surprising Tigers swept the Yankees in the ALCS. They were rewarded with the Giants, who had rallied from a 3-1 deficit in the NLCS to knock off the defending champion Cardinals. The Giants dominated the Tigers, sweeping the series. The Tigers only led for 3 innings in the entire series.

2. 2007 World Series: Red Sox defeat Rockies in 4 games

The Rockies went on a furious end of season run to make the playoffs as the wild card, defeating the Padres in a controversial tiebreaker game finish. They followed that up by sweeping the DS and the CS, entering their first WS at 7-0 in the playoffs. The Red Sox had come back from 3-1 down to the Indians to win their second pennant in four years. The Red Sox completely owned the Rockies from start to finish, outscoring them 29-10.

3. 2006 World Series: Cardinals defeat Tigers in 5 games

The Tigers stunned the world by going from 119 losses to an AL Pennant 3 season later, sweeping the A’s in the CS. The Cardinals snuck in to the playoffs with just 83 wins, the second worst playoff qualifying team of all time. They defeated the Mets in 7 games in the NLCS in one of the best game 7s of all time. In the World Series, the plucky Cardinals won their first title since 1982 by limiting the Tigers to just 11 runs in 5 games. The Tigers only win was the “Kenny Rogers game”, where Rogers pitched 8 shutout innings while having “something sticky” on his hand.

4. 1988 World Series: Dodgers defeat A’s in 5 games

The Dodgers won a weak NL West with 94 wins before upsetting the 100 win Mets in 7 games in the NLCS to win the pennant. The juggernaut A’s won 104 games and swept the Red Sox to win their 2nd straight pennant. In the World Series, the Dodgers won one of the most memorable game 1s of all time with Kirk Gibson’s pinch hit walk-off homer. The Dodgers followed by winning 3 of the next 4 to clinch the series in 5, with the only A’s win on a Mark McGwire walk-off homer in game 3.

While history is made to be broken, this does not bode well for my Boys in Blue going for the first repeat title since 2000. Maybe it’s the extra time off, maybe it’s momentum, maybe it’s a fluke of a small sample size. But either way, the 7 game CS winner facing a 4 game winner is undefeated in MLB history.
