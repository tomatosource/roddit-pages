Quality of Life improvements that we need

  • Atlas Map: Zoom much much further out.
  • Atlas Map: Filter for map specifications (e.g. “Show maps with Breach modifier; show maps with %50 Quantity modifier”)
  • Atlas Map: Put bookmarks for any maps that you will do later
  • Atlas Map: Display Inventory AND personal stash and guild stash when looking for a waystone for a Map
  • Atlas Map: Ability to put more than 1 waystones to a map. (There are already available slots for 4. This will help make use of some useless waystones)
  • Trade: Ability to trade items without the need to be next to each other (Trade window can be opened with two people who have messaged in the past 5 minutes regardless of their locations)
  • Trade: Chat window visibility during trades (Not available on consoles)
  • Item crafting: Show what stats are changed (removed/ added)
  • Item comparing: Display the differences of the same stats next to them (e.g. Strength +15; Armor -20)
  • Gear sets: Option to save/load different gear loadouts
  • Gem sets: Option to save/load different Skill and Support gem loadouts
  • Chat: Quick responses of Yes, No, Wait, etc. (A huge help for console players)
  • Passive skill tree: Option to save/load different skill trees
  • Passive skill tree: Better highlight nodes after searching keywords in tree
  • Loot filter: In game simple loot filter (Show/ Hide selected items)
  • Loot filter: Highlight gear with chosen stats (e.g. “Highlight quivers with 200+ Lightning Dmg; Highlight rings with 30+ Cold Res”)
  • Stash: One click inventory to stash dumping and better sorting system (Put currencies in currency tab, maps in maps tab, rest in dump tab with one click)

Please add your contributions. I know GGG gives value to player feedback. Hopefully we will see these in the final version.
