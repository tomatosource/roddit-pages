Sorceress struggling

I am not someone who tried sorceress once, i have 500 hours of sorceress and I am very confident that what I’m saying can help the class.

This post isn’t for anyone who is levelling or doing low tier content.

Nerfed: -Maelstrom flask -Eldritch battery -Archmage -Everlasting Gaze

Playing the class right now feels like having both hands tied behind ur back and being tossed into the middle of the ocean with a metal ball tied to your legs.

Give sorceress a way to replenish mana.Remove the absurd EB nerf. archmage needs to go to at least 5% per 100 mana. Buff the ascendancy nodes they are uber useless right now. Stormweaver “storms” are absolutely irrelevant screen clutter at this point. Chronomancer can timefreeze but whats the point when you’re gonna do 3 milion damage maximum and then run around the boss which has 35 milion HP and every second ability 1shots you. Time snap ability has almost 0 use-cases. No abilities with a cooldown are really relevant.

Temporal rift is irrelevant because most Sorceress builds peak at around the average 2k mana (with insane gear) which gets insta-drained after 3 COC comets. That’s absurdly small damage.

Speaks volumes when the best sorceress build is a whirling slash ES stacking recoup build. That’s basically a warrior build.

Give sorceress ways to stack more mana with ascendencies since the abilties are so absurdly expensive.

The new Whispering staff isn’t happening. Neither is the Adognia’s Ego staff. They’re both irrelevant items. It’s like multiplying with 0.

Create actual mana synergy armour pieces because right now the only mandatory unique on sorc is a dex item “Maligaro’s virt. gloves”.

Indigon is clunky, slow, weak for pinaccle content.

Cheers to everyone.

All hail mana stacking.
