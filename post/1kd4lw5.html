Erik Spoelstra is now the longest-tenured coach in the NBA

With Gregg Popovich officially stepping away from coaching to focus on his role as Spurs team president, Erik Spoelstra now holds the title of longest-tenured head coach in the league.

Here’s the current top 5 in coaching tenure as of May 2025:

1). Erik Spoelstra (Heat) – 17 years (since April 2008)

2). Steve Kerr (Warriors) – 11 years (since May 2014)

3). Tom Thibodeau (Knicks) – Almost 5 years (since July 2020)

4). Billy Donovan (Bulls) – 4 years, 7 months (since Sept 2020)

5). Tyronn Lue (Clippers) – 4 years, 6 months (since Oct 2020)

Kind of puts into perspective how rare it is for a coach to stick around in one spot these days.
