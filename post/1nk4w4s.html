Rule tweak to make the game better?

I don’t know about what anyone thinks, but I hate when teams foul on purpose and the last 90 seconds of a game takes 15 minutes to finish and it turns into a free throw shooting parade. It’s just not entertaining to watch and is a disservice to the competition that goes on for the other 46 minutes of the game. I also hate the practice of hacking a player who shoots a low percentage to keep them from playing. It’s not fun at all. Plus, I think a team should not be rewarded for deliberately fouling on every play. No other sport gives a team the advantage for playing intentionally terrible defense.

To address that I would like to see a rule where a team has the right to decline free throws and elect for the ball side out under the other team’s basket. This would get rid of that strategy which plays out like waterboarding at the end of every game.

Would this make the game better?
