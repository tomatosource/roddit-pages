Some key points not really being mentioned but worth considering about endgame

As we’ve all seen, most of the changes to the game are positive (or hugely positive like the new trade system) but the endgame is where the game is currently lacking the most. Besides of the usual “lol delete towers” feedback, here are some points I’ve observed after a lot of endgame farming:

  1. Lack of specialized farm identity. This is a MAJOR reason of poe1’s success and I feel that’s the direction they will move forward for poe2 as well. A single, specialized farm strategy into a single mechanic should always be more rewarding than just slapping random stuff everywhere. People want to farm only breach, or farm only ritual, or maybe farm only expedition, but the economy balance isn’t really there and bosses are just very unrewarding in general.

  2. This one is big and ties into the first one: WE NEED JUICERS TO DROP SO WE CAN HAVE PEOPLE SPECIALIZE IN FARMING THEM. One of the reasons (besides high divine drop of course) sanctum was so extremely popular was because of sextants, being able to specialize into a farm identity of providing juice to the community not only feeds the economy but also gives you an actual option to play without sacrificing a lot of div/hr. Bonus points if the juicer farm (scarabs or whatever they come up with) can be done in an alch&go manner without a lot of setup so it will be super popular which the endgame juicers will love to buy in bulk with the now EXTREMELY AMAZING TRADE SYSTEM (seriously, the combination of both trade systems we have now is almost perfection, imagine just a few years back we had NEITHER of them)

  3. we NEED QoL elements urgently to improve map flow. Currently we have basically no flow during the map experience; you have to constantly review pathing on tower setup phase, double check your maps are under tower influence, you lose track of where you are, where you wanna go, it’s chaos. PoE1 nailed this to perfection with the inclusion of the small 20 map pouch on the map device - you just prepare 20 maps, run one, dump, run another, it’s a clean, smooth and delightful brain off experience after you dedicate all the time preparing all 20 maps. We need something similar in PoE2.
