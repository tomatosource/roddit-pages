Playoff clinching/seeding scenarios for the final day of the season

- Hawks clinch the 5th seed with a win, or an Orlando win. Hawks win the H2H tiebreaker with the Magic, and a 3-way tie with Hawks, Raptors and Magic would go to the Hawks due to being the leader in their division

- Hawks will be the 6th seed with a loss AND Raptors win + Magic loss

- Raptors clinch the 5 seed with a win AND a Hawks loss + Magic loss. This is due to them beating the Hawks H2H, but a 3-way tie would result in the Hawks in the 5 seed as laid out above.

- Raptors clinch the 6 seed with a win AND a Hawks win, or a Magic loss. Raptors owns the H2H tiebreaker with the 76ers so them winning would not matter in a direct tie

- Magic clinch the 6 seed with a win and a Raptors loss

- 76ers clinch the 6 seed with a win AND losses from both Raptors and Magic. The 76ers need a 3-way tie, as the tiebreakers are as follows: Division leader (none lead their division), better winning percentage among tied teams (76ers 4-3, Raptors 4-3, Magic 2-4), Division W-L if in same division (76ers 9-7, Raptors 4-13)

- Raptors will be the 7th seed with a loss AND Magic win + 76ers loss

- Magic will be the 7th seed with a Raptors win OR a 76ers loss. They do not have tiebreakers over either team and need to finish ahead of the 76ers

- 76ers will be the 7th seed with a win AND a Magic loss + Raptors win, or a win AND Raptors loss + Magic win. They essentially need to tie in the 7th/8th position to be the 7th seed.

- Raptors will be the 8th seed with a loss AND Magic win + 76ers win

- Magic will be the 8th seed with a loss and 76ers win. 76ers own the H2H, and a 3-way tie with the Raptors as explained above would result in the Magic being bumped down to 8th, meaning the Raptors win or lose would not matter

- 76ers will be the 8th seed with a loss, or Raptors + Magic wins

- Hornets will clinch the 9th seed with a win

- Heat clinch the 9th seed with a win and a Hornets loss. Heat own the H2H

- Nuggets clinch the 3 seed with a win

- Lakers clinch the 3 seed with a win and a Nuggets loss. Lakers own the H2H

- Trail Blazers clinch the 8 seed with a win or a Clippers loss. Trail Blazers own the tiebreaker due to conference record

- Clippers clinch the 8 seed with a win AND a Trail Blazers loss
