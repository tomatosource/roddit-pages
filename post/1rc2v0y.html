Eugenio Suarez has (so far) won every trade he’s been in.

Geno has been traded 4 times in his career: from the Tigers to the Reds, from the Reds to the Mariners, from the Mariners to the Diamondbacks, and from the Diamondbacks back to the Mariners. So far, he’s been worth more bWAR on his new team than any other player involved in the deal was for either team after the trade. Let’s take a look at them all.

Format is Player (#of seasons, bWAR for team)

Trade 1: Tigers &gt; Reds

Tigers receive: Alfredo Simón (1 season, -0.4bWAR)

Reds receiver: Jonathon Crawford (0 seasons, bWAR) and Eugenio Suarez (7 seasons, 12.8bWAR)

Trade 2: Reds &gt; Mariners

Reds receive: Justin Dunn (1 season, -0.1bWAR), Jake Fraley (3 seasons, 2.0bWAR), Brandon Williamson (2 seasons, 1.9bWAR so far), and Connor Phillips (2 seasons, 0.5bWAR so far)

Mariners receive: Jesse Winker (1 season, -0.1bWAR), Eugenio Suarez (2 seasons, 6.5bWAR)

Trade 3: Mariners &gt; Diamondbacks

Mariners receive: Seby Zavala (1 season, -0.3bWAR) and Carlos Vargas (1 season, -0.1bWAR so far)

Diamondbacks receive: Eugenio Suarez (2 seasons, 6.4bWAR)

Trade 4: Diamondbacks &gt; Mariners

Diamondbacks receive: Hunter Cranton (0 seasons, 0bWAR so far), Juan Burgos (1 season, -0.5bWAR so far), and Tyler Locklear (1 season, -1.1bWAR so far)

Mariners receive: Eugenio Suarez (1 season, 0.3bWAR)

As I was looking at these trades, I kept expecting the outcome to change, but each time the return seemed to lessen. He managed to be very productive after his first 3 trades while no one else involved really did by comparison. Even the last one, where Geno was merely pedestrian down the stretch for Seattle, the players Seattle traded were even worse (Hunter Cranton has yet to play a minor league game in Arizona’s system).

It think it’s likely that this is no longer the case sometime in the future, probably as a result of the last trade to Seattle (it should take one good year from any of them). Even without that 3 players from older trades are still on the team that traded for them: Vargas, Williamson, and Phillips, though I doubt any of them will since relievers are fucky and they all need 4-6bWAR to get there.

What’s the moral of all this? Every team should trade for Geno Suarez, he’ll always be worth it.
