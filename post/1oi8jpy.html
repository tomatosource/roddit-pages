4 games in, if you could decide our full rotation for the rest of the season, what would it be?

Personally I’ve seen enough and I think I know exactly what I would have the rotation as

Starters-Pritchard/white/brown/minott/queta

I truly think one of minott or Hugo should be starting. They do very similar things; but Minott is more of the wing who guards up 2-4 vs Hugo as wing that guards more down 1-3. To me, that’s where I landed on minott starting because I think you need more help in the starting 5 guarding bigger guys and more help rebounding than you need Hugo taking tougher guard/wing assignments. The rest of the starting 5 is pretty chalk and I assume will be agreed on by most.

Bench- Simon’s/hugo/hauser/boucher/tillman(or Garza depending on the game)

The first 4 names should all be getting 15-25 mpg every single game, Hauser/simons should be getting closer to 25, Hugo and Boucher closer to 15. Tillman or Garza depending solely on the matchup, Garza if you can get away with playing Garza, Tillman if you can’t
