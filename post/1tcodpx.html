The worst thing about Path of Exile, is that is does an incredible job of hiding the “ARPG Treadmill”.

You might think this is a blessing, and to an extent, whilst playing PoE/PoE 2, it is.

But I can’t be the only one who logs into another ARPG, either one I have previously enjoyed, or one that I haven’t even played before, and then straight away feel like I’m staring right at that treadmill?

I will never forget Chris Wilson stating that the most important feeling for an ARPG gamer to have, is that the very next monster you kill can be the one which drops the holy grail, there’s always something around the corner.

I don’t ever feel as though I’m going in a circle until hundreds of hours into a League. There’s just progression after progression after progression.

Diablo 2 WAS great. Rarity of items meant that you also had that feeling of the next drop could be the one you want….But it never had the other piece of the progression puzzle, the content.

I can’t play D2 now, because all I’m doing is finding extra gear to fight the same fights I’ve already completed, just with extra numbers.

It’s a completely different experience to finding a drop in PoE that makes me capable of clearing T16 Maps….Which then drop me an item which allows me to complete a pinnacle boss….Which then drops me an item which allows me to complete a pinnacle Uber boss, with completely different mechanics and a Unique loot table.

And then each league they add new skills or other content, which adds completely new builds….And the cycle begins again.
