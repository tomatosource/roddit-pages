2025-26 Too-Early Award Predictions

Due to the universal praise and incredible engagement of my “2025-26 Too-Early NBA Standings Predictions” (which received a sneaky athletic 0 upvotes and 74 comments!) I realized I have a calling. So, to satiate my adoring fans - I’ve decided it’s time for another feature in the “Too-Early” predictions: awards!

I’ll preface by saying that I am going to make a few leaps of faith - I think making a list that copy and pastes the betting odds favourites is a little too ESPN for my tastes. Instead, I’ll go with the classy Bill Simmons approach of using vibes, feels, and incomplete, unresearched opinions for my picks.

Without further adieu

Coach Of The Year (COTY)

Winner - Mitch Johnson
Runner(s) up - Quinn Snyder, Ime Udoka

All my picks are children of the Pop Father, but only one of them was handpicked to take over his role for the Spurs head coach. Was it because he had a heart attack and the other two had head coach positions already? We may never know. But what I do know is students of the school of Pop have a high hit rate as head coaches. The Spurs are set to have a very good year, signing megastar Fox to a team friendly $60 million a year deal (great move guys!), adding NBA champion Luke Kornet and Gonzaga Bulldogs legend Kelly Olynyk adds a much-needed depth to their center rotation, and they’ve got a 3rd year Ice Vein Thrombosis. If the real MJ can turn them into a ~47 win team, and make their offense and defense, uh, good - then I think he’ll have earned the pick.

Clutch Player Of The Year (CPOTY)

Winner - Trae Young
Runner(s) up - Kevin Durant, Anthony Edwards

Trae Young might be the poster-child of underrated players at this point in his career. Is he worse than many of you reading this at defense? No, but it’s funnier to say he is so actually yes! But despite his defensive SHORT comings (I’m 5’8), he’s consistently shown throughout his career to be a clutch player. One of the things that’s held him back at times in the clutch was a lack of depth, but this Hawks team is deeper than Kyrie Irving on a late night stream. To be clutch, you need to win - but not by too much! I think this Hawks team will do exactly that. Net-rating +2.5 with 52 wins (East boost baby!).

Most Improved Player (MIP)

Winner - Scoot Henderson
Runner(s) up - Trey Murphy III, Jaden Ivey, Cam Whitmore

Full disclosure - I hate this award. I think the way it’s awarded often feels arbitrary (not like the other awards that are always super objective), and it feels like a backhanded compliment in the form of a trophy. But I’m diamond hands on Scoot, I’ll hold this bag until he’s either out of the league or top 20. I think Scoot has so many of the tools in his arsenal to be great, and I think Portland is going to take a leap this year - well, maybe more of a small hop than a leap, but still. I think under the tutelage and leadership of some real vets in Dame and Jrue he can really flourish into a, I don’t know, .600 TS% guy? For like, 18-20 ppg? Diamond hands.

Sixth Man Of The Year (6MOY)

Winner - Jordan Clarkson
Runner(s) up - TJ McConnel, Dorian Finney-Smith

If you told me this time last season I would pick a New York Knick for 6MOY, I would’ve laughed in Thibs. But this season? Clarkson might not have played much last season but in his defense, he was on the Jazz. He’s gonna fill up the stat sheet and give you 15+ a night off the bench. Last year the best scorer off the bench for the Knicks was Deuce McBride. New York is a media darling, and the award voters just look at box score anyway.

Defensive Player Of The Year (DPOY)

Winner - Amen Thompson (jk it’s wemby)
Runner(s) up - AD, Mobley

Call me a box score watcher, but I think the guy getting 17 blocks a game and making player piss and shit themselves in the paint is pretty safe bet. Does this solidify the last 10 years as the era of the defensive elite french? Does this make up for WW2? Oui oui mon ami! (I don’t have much to say here, this guy is just really fucking good - the only way he doesn’t win it is if he gets injured at some point).

Rookie Of The Year (ROY)

Winner - Ace Bailey
Runner(s) up - Cooper Flagg, VJ Edgecombe

“Fuck you, you stupid ignorant wrong man” I hear you say. “Stopped reading after Ace” I see you type. Let me start off by saying you are valid king/queen - but hear me out! Will Ace be better than Coop? No, no he will not. Will he be better than Harper or Edgecombe? No I don’t think so. But he will have both hands on the reins in a way that no one else in this rookie class will. Even if the Jazz keep Markkanen (they won’t), he’s going to be THE guy in Utah. If he puts up 22+ a game I wouldn’t be the least bit surprised. This is one of the rare awards that team success doesn’t usually impact - that said, if the Mavs are actually good this year then you can bet your sweet, presumably pink ass that Coop’s going to be a major part of the reason why. But I don’t think they’ll be good so I’m going to give Ace the nod first.

Or when Kobe got dinged for… well anyway - Luka is skinny which NEVER happens in the off-season, motivated, and on a team that MIGHT actually be good enough for top 4 (cope cope cope cope cope cope).
