JJ Redick in 2016 podcast - “I’d be a GM, not coach or commentator”

Funny to see how his perspective changed from this point in his career with the Clippers: https://blamopod.com/blamo-podcast/2016/12/31/jj-redick

38:20: “There’s a few things that interest me in terms of what I’d like to do post career. Number one, if I was to do something within basketball, it could not be coaching. It’d definitely be the front office. I would love to be a GM… There’s tangible results. Can we put out a winning product? Can we draft well? …Commentating to me, I’m not sure if I’m built for. I went to school so I could get my competitive outlet out. I am not sure there’s a way to measure your ability as a commentator. Sitting behind a desk and talking about Cleveland’s weak side defense? I just don’t see it in the cards for me. It’s not a money thing. If ESPN or Turner offered, I’d be flattered, but it’s not on my radar right now.”
