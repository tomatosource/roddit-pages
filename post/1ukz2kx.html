The Mike Conley Effect

Mike Conley is ‘washed’. He is entering his 20th NBA season. He’s old. He has lost several steps. Physically, he is past his prime.

Mike Conley’s value is not going to come on the court. If he can play a few minutes per game, terrific.

As an insurance policy, he is rock steady: if there’s an injury crisis, Conley is gonna step up and give 100%. But he’s not gonna be Prime Mike Conley. Legs are gone…

On the bench and off the court is where Mike Conley helps this team tremendously.

First, the team BBIQ went up by a measurable amount: if Mike Conley isn’t the smartest player in the League, he’s in the starting five. Him, LeBron, Jrue Holiday, Steph, and pick ‘em. Mike Conley has forgotten more about basketball than Chris Cenac knows. Literally.

Second, Mike Conley has no ring and presumably wants one. A well respected veteran who wants a ring is a good thing in the locker room, at practice, and in general.

Third, the Celtics have a pair of yutes - Hugo and Cenac - who can learn a lot by sitting next to Conley on the bench, in practice, etc. Conley is a 4x ‘Sportsmanship’ award winner: he is a high character professional who loves basketball. He’s a veteran mentor and leader who is gonna help the yutes, Walsh, Harper, and everyone on the bench.

Culture matters. Mike Conley is culture and a statement about what the organization values.

Let’s hope he’s not a traffic cone and can perform on the court when needed.
