An issue with NBA media: instead of hyping matchups they just set teams up to be dragged if they lose. I know this gets ratings, but why though?

This is mostly a rant.

This game 7 is great. This series has been great, we all wanted it to go to 7. Both teams have played their hearts out and it’s come down to the wire. However, there’s already this focus about how it sets up a team to be dragged if they lose game 7. I just heard someone say that “oh jeez, if Jokic loses a game 7 at home in back-to-back years, that doesn’t look good for him!” even despite his insane showings and apparently coaching his team this whole series. and then there’s already the set up to drag Harden for choking game 7, even after Harden just played 47 minutes and won in a game facing elimination.

In my opinion, neither of these teams deserve to be dragged too hard. Like sure, drag teams for losing. Have fun with that. but there should be love both ways for giving us such great games. Neither team blew a big series lead, games have been insanely close. Both teams have the same record.

You know what I haven’t heard? is hype for how these teams will play against OKC. There are so many storylines there. SGA playing the team that drafted him, or Russ coming back and playing a series against the team he’s the most beloved player for. Or even the manifestation of this MVP debate between Jokic and SGA. There’s so much there. And the focus is instead going to be shifted to dragging whoever loses.

Even if you don’t want to hype up a match up before it’s set in stone (which…it starts Monday. maybe want to cut your inhibitions there imo and just hype both), why can’t we just love that both these teams played their asses off? why do we focus on losers more than we praise the winners?

This Cleveland Indiana series has been set in stone, and I think will be very fun. In my prediction, this will be the best series of round 2, no matter what the other matchups are. Is it being treated like it’s going to be the must watch tv I think it’s going to be on ESPN? To be honest I only watch ESPN on the treadmill so I might miss these great basketball discussions breaking down the sheer offensive firepower on both sides. but I doubt it?

I get teams have haters and thus a great way to mine content is to feed the vultures looking to poopoo them. It’s crazy to me how much this overshadows the actual play on the court. It sucks that it feels like even the media likes dragging people more than they like basketball when their job is to get us excited to watch basketball games.
