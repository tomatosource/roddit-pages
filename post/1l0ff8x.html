TNT SPORTS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED FROM NBA STREAMING CONTENTION

The NBA has aired on TNT for 36 years, starting with the 1988–89 season and concluded at the end of the 2024–25 season. This makes it TNT’s longest-running regular program and sporting event. “Inside the NBA” studio show will move to ESPN after TNT’s NBA broadcast rights end. For real THANK YOU Ernie, Kenny, Charles, Shaq, Kevin, Reggie, and the entire TNT Sports crew for giving us the best NBA coverage. RIP Craig Sager.
