This game is difficult, but not in a very satisfying

I have about 500 hours with EA and I gotta say, this game makes me struggle and not in a satisfying way.

Coming from Diablo I don’t understand why the drops and RNG in this game feel so awful. I’m trading for better items using the currency that be using to make better items from. Only issue is the scarcity of the drop rate for those currencies, and when I use them I almost never get a combination that’s even good enough to replace what I have.

I don’t know why they’re marketing this game as slow paced when enemies come charging at you fresh off the cocaine binge. Even worse are the ranged enemies constantly shooting from off screen. I hate the open maps in the endgame because I can’t take two steps without getting swarmed from off screen. It doesn’t help that I can’t push them out the way so I end up body-blocked into a mosh-pit of claws, teeth, and after-death hazards. The bosses are another issue, or rather the tracking on their moves are.

And those ascendancy trials are just…..bad. I don’t if there’s anything that can be done that’ll get me to participate in those because I just hate them in their entirety.

Much of this wouldn’t feel so bad or would feel more satisfying if the loot and rng were tweaked.
