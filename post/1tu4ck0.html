NYC Mayor, Zohran Mamdani signs executive order ‘repealing bedtimes’ for duration of Knicks NBA Finals run

NEW YORK (WABC) – Mayor Zohran Mamdani is making sure that New York City schools students can stay up late to watch the Knicks play in the NBA Finals.

The mayor was joined by a group of students Monday morning to sign an executive order that repeals bedtime so the kids can stay up late to watch the Knicks.

“Bedtime is repealed! All of you can watch the Finals,” the mayor declared.
