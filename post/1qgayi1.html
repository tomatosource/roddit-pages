Looks like medaillon lock drop rate bug and diminishing return are correlated

From my observation, the medaillon lock drop rate in the temple only seems to be bugged once you reach a certain amount of spymasters which leads me to believe the issue has something to do with diminishing return.

Like many people, I started noticing abyssal medaillon lock drop rate, and noticed that I got most of them from garrison/alchemy labs instead of spymasters.

I sort of thought I was paranoid, and that what I was readin was basically confirmation bias from other paranoid/unlucky people.

Anyway, I started building a second temple (with russian tech and stuff unlike my first one) and noticed really good lock drop rate. I thought: “I knew I was paranoid”. But then somewhere around my 8th to 10th spymasters I sarting getting the most terrible lock drop rates once again.

So yeah, to me it looks like the issue only happens once you have more than X spymasters, which is “similar” to the diminishing returns they added in recent patches.

Anyway, given that streamers are off to other games, and that GGG basically doesn’t play their own game, I don’t expect the issue to will ever get resolved…
