Moving the Blazers out of Portland is a Bridge Too Far

Portland can afford to keep an NBA basketball team and shouldn’t have to bow down to a billionaire to keep the Trailblazers. I am not even a Blazers fan but I am so sick of all of this “austerity” during a period of unprecedented prosperity in the Western world.

Productivity keeps rising, costs keep going up, and yet only the people at the top make any money. If the Blazers leave it will kill my NBA fandom for at least a couple years. I know this is true because the MLB has already done this to me and the only thing that brought me back (not even all the way) was the Blue Jays’ recent World Series run (I’m a Toronto fan). The world is on fire, yes, it’s true, but I watch the NBA to escape those problems for a little while and what Dundon is doing to Portland is such an obvious injustice that it is seriously turning me off. I’ll find another way to enjoy basketball.

I know that a lot of people will comment on this and say “Well, that’s just how it is,” but what if… that isn’t how it has to be? I’m also curious—how would you feel about the league if the Blazers left?
