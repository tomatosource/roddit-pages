Pre 0.4.0 is the Perfect Time to Jump In

Picked up PoE2 about three weeks ago after my buddy wouldn’t stop talking about it. Day one I opened the skill tree and… froze. It honestly looked like alien hieroglyphs.

I lurked here for a bit, then jumped into some Discord guilds, and honestly they are awesome. No gatekeeping, just people genuinely explaining why a node or mechanic works instead of just “copy this.” One master even walked me through my first 3-link in DMs like it was nothing. The wiki’s still growing, but the New Beginner Help page already saved me from a few gem sockets problems.

Point is, if you’re thinking about waiting for 0.4.0 to “start fresh”, start now. The community is in full “teach the newbies” mode before the patch hits. You’ll learn the ropes while everything’s still manageable, then roll into the update with everyone else instead of scrambling to catch up.

Kinda wild to say as a total rookie, but this is the first ARPG crowd that’s made me genuinely excited to experiment and theorycraft my own builds instead of just following guides, and I genuinely started to enjoy the game now thanks to all of you.
