Interesting NBA cup quirk that might be relevant this season - while the final doesn’t count towards regular season stats, it does count towards the 65 games played minimum for awards like DPOY

Reported by Matt Guzman SASonSI.

Since Wembanyama has only played 13 games out of a possible 25 this season, usually he would only be able to miss 5 more games the rest of the way and still be eligible for major awards. It’s might be significant because it will also affect his the max contract extension he can get.

The NBA Cup final gives him a extra game played to hit the 65 game minimum which might be relevant if Wemby has to miss a couple of games later in the season or skip a back-to-back
