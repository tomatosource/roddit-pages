small poe2 content creators still cranking!

i know many of our favourite big time cc’s have moved on, but i wanted to highlight some smaller creators that ive found on youtube that put out content daily and have really helped me to fall in love with poe 2 all over again. not a full list, so please share your recommendations below!

  • Carnarius - warrior guy, stopped me from quitting 0.2 with his smith
  • FatbellyWW - top ssf pathfinder
  • Xilareth - made a build for rank 1 ssf
  • KallTorak - off meta guy, budget jungroan rofl
  • Struth Gaming - incin shot guy
  • ronarray - poe2s empy
  • kingpinuwu - poe2 emiracles
  • Jorgen - coindweller rec

talkative tri recs:

  • Lupi - farming guides, build guides, leveling guides, livestreams of everything, too.
  • LazyExile - mostly build guides
  • Off-Meta Michael - doing a good bit of feedback videos on PoE2

there are probably so many more small creators im missing, but honestly when you intentionally seek out these smaller names you see that creativity and fun are still very much a part of poe 2. i was originally anti poe 2, but nowadays i could never go back to an “easy” ARPG
