Giannis Antetokounmpo averages in April: 32 PTS, 12 REB, 12 AST, +20 on 61/42/73 shooting splits (67% TS)

Giannis led the Bucks to 6 wins in a row in April to cement 5th seed in the Eastern Conference, the first season below 3rd seed since 2018. In April, he is averaging 32/12/12/1/1 on 67% TS without Dame Lillard.

Giannis becomes the second player in NBA history to average at least 30 points on 60% shooting in a season — the first being himself in 2024.

Giannis also becomes the third player to average atleast 30 PTS 10 REB in three consecutive seasons (after Wilt Chamberlain and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar).

If Giannis is included for First Team All-NBA this season, he will be second amongst active players in First team All-NBA selections (overtaking Durant, behind LeBron). He will also be one of only 13 players in NBA history to make All-NBA First Team in seven consecutive seasons. Rest of the list includes Michael Jordan, Shaquille O’Neal, LeBron James, Kobe Bryant, Tim Duncan, Karl Malone, Larry Bird, Magic Johnson, Oscar Robertson, Bob Cousy, Elgin Baylor and Bill Russell.
