Yet another ssf casual checking out

There’s a lot of things I really like about poe2, but the endgame mechanics are just too off-putting. Forcing you to avoid potentially rewarding content for fear of not being sufficiently overpowered / overlevelled is just not good game design, unless it’s an optional challenge (like hardcore is). Ssf progression is a joke, and I just don’t care about trading. Making nearly all uniques have major drawbacks removes the excitement of finding them. Mapping all the way to my first citadel and then getting one-shotted with no visual indication of the incoming attack is not my idea of fun.

I really enjoyed playing the campaign with 4 classes out of the initial 6 (actually 3.5 as I dropped my warrior at act 3 normal), and I will probably come back when the full campaign is available to check it out, but I just can’t force myself to endure the endgame unless ggg addresses the issues above, which seems very unlikely right now. And it’s a damn shame, because this game has tons of potential and all of the pain points could be easily addressed while keeping the current playstyle for those who prefer it that way.
