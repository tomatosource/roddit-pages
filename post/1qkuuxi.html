The inconsistency of death

I know lots of discussions have happened on the death penalty of xp and retries in maps, but I haven’t seen the discussion centered around how where u die matters as to how the game reacts. Below is a small list of how the game behaves.

Campaign death

- All enemies reset, have to redo full area. No XP penalty.

Maps (not boss)

- Everything stays the same (enemies not reset), limited retries, XP penalty.

Map boss

- Everything is deleted, limited retries, XP penalty.

Abyss Pit

- No retry, XP penalty, death doesn’t count to map deaths.

Temple (not boss)

- Unlimited retries, XP penalty, enemies not reset, deaths don’t count towards map deaths.

Temple Bosses

- Unlimited retries, map deleted, XP penalty. Deaths don’t count towards map deaths.

Pinnacle Content T1

- Unlimited retries, no XP or XP penalty

Pinnacle Content T2 or higher

- limited retries, no XP or XP penalty

Expeditions

- Unlimited retries, enemies don’t reset, XP penalty.

Like can we get some consistency?

I wish GGG sit down and iron out the whole thing instead of one off fixing each scenario and they all look so disjointed. Specifically before they add another new mechanic that behaves in a completely different way.
