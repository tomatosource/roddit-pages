Is Michael Adams the worst 25ppg scorer in NBA history?

The 1990-91 Denver Nuggets are known for one thing: Being THEE worst defensive unit of all time. They gave up 130.8ppg(!!). The worst defense in the league this year, the 17-win Utah Jazz, gave up 121.2ppg. While this was largely due to horrific effort and poor defensive philosophy, there was another major factor in play: Pace.

The Denver Nuggets led the league in pace at 113.7, an all-time record that stills stand today, and a whopping 10.1 above the 2nd place “Run TMC” Warriors. This enabled the 20-win Nugs to lead the league in points scored per game at 119.9; a mark that had been surpassed before, but wouldn’t be matched again until the 2020 Milwaukee Bucks would do it 3 decades later with MVP Giannis and Coach Mike Buldenholzer.

All this pace demanded some all time chucking, and nobody stepped up to the plate quite like Michael Adams. Standing just 5’10, the 28 year old would average 26.5ppg and 10.5apg, 6th and 3rd place respectively, a stat-line fit for one of the league’s brightest talents. In fact, his assist per game mark fell only behind legends Magic Johnson and John Stockton. However, Adams wouldn’t even be named to the all-star team. How was this possible?

Well, Adams starry stat-line couldn’t hide his atrocius shooting splits. Adams would take 21.5fga and 8.5(!!)3pa a night, on alarming .394/.296/.879 splits from the field. These numbers, along with the Nuggets poor season and inflated numbers due to pace of play, would lead to Adams lack of recognition despite his stellar counting stats.

His .394fg% is the worst mark ever for a 25ppg scorer (minimum 50 GP).

Of the 1,038 times a player has shot over 5 threes per game in a season (minimum 50 GP), Michael Adams ranks 1,027th in 3pt% at .296. He shot 8.5 threes a night.

(Funny enough, Russ, Kobe, and D Rose rank 1,035th, 1,037th, and 1,038th here respectively 😳.)

All this talk begs the question, was Michael Adams the worst player to ever average 25ppg in a season? On the contrary to this point, Adams would be named to the all-star game after being traded to the Washington Bullets the following season, averaging roughly 18ppg and 8apg.

Who are some players who put up elite numbers on terrible splits, or just had terrible advanced stats?

TLDR: 5’10 PG averages 26 a game on 39/29/88 shooting splits.

Edit: All stats according to Bball Ref, Stathead, NBA.com, and Statmuse.
