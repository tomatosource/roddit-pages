How to make chonk volatility fun

Just make the buff from volatility start its duration on the player’s next attack. Right now, you finish a monster pack, your volatility explodes, and the buff is usually gone before you reach the next pack. Or, you are fighting a boss, the boss transitions to phase 2, and your volatility has already exploded and expired before the phase even starts. With this change, you can strategically stack volatility in phase 1 of a boss encounter and then use the full duration of the buff in phase 2.

This would be so much more fun.

If this post gets 25 or more upvotes, it is likely that almost every chonk player has upvoted the idea.
