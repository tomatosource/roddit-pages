Unofficial PoE 2 Crafting Bench Concept — deterministic recipes with success chances (fan project)

Hey exiles,

I built a small unofficial concept for what a Path of Exile 2 crafting bench *could* feel like if existing currencies were combined into recipes with success chances.

Try it here:
https://poe2-crafting-bench.vercel.app

https://preview.redd.it/64dffenhz6hh1.jpg?width=1420&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=11266dd47f641ea28ae43e71b20d77481470e604

What it is

  • A fan-made hypothetical crafting UI (Item / Want / Inscribe)
  • Pick a modifier you want, choose a tier, optionally add catalysts, then craft
  • Success chance is shown up front (capped for the concept)
  • Styled to feel closer to the in-game market / exchange UI

What it is not

  • Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Grinding Gear Games
  • Not a real in-game feature
  • Not a profit project — no ads, no donations, no monetization intended
  • Balance / odds are conceptual approximations, not datamined truth

Feedback welcome — especially on UX, recipe flow, and what you’d want from a real bench if GGG ever explored something like this.
