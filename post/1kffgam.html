Tips on dealing with one-hit or “burst” kills?

Context

I’m playing Merc, lvl 88 with 4168 armour and 6427 evasion with all resistances capped. All my gear is worth at least 50 exalts if not 1+ Divine. I usually have no problem doing tier 15 maps and I’m fine with the rate I’m killing enemies.

I basically do poison/explosive grenades, and combine it with Artillery Ballista and a Quadrilla minion to act as tank. I have a couple high damage resources, Plague Nova and HV rounds (I have the armor piercing ones loaded by default and poison also breaks armour).

The issue

I might go for several maps just fine but sometimes I enter a map where I just… can’t stop dying, can’t even wipe half the rares. Those dog-bird like beasts with sickle-like front limbs 1-hit kill me. Those fat putrid witches that use curses, 1-hit kill me. There are a bunch of enemies that just 1-hit kill me or others that are grouped together and my life goes from full to zero faster than I can click space bar to dodge.

Is it just a skill issue, should I slow down? Am I over-extending? Or am I missing something?

Mini rant

It’s frustrating because ok, I know if I slow down and play more like Dark Souls I can avoid those deaths, but the game is not Dark Souls and if I go slow all the time I won’t get anything done. I feel like the game wants me to know when to switch pace but gives me no warning for it.

I’m all in for increased difficulty but this is not the right approach. Difficulty should be consistent and engaging, not a “surprise you’re dead”.
