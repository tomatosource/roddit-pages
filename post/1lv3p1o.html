Feesback for the Team on issues leading into 0.3 from a console gamer dad

So I’m looking to give my feedback to the team based on my experience as a console player for poe2 and an old poe1 blaster , some context here .

Last league I super played was archnem , kids and life had led me to be a console only gamer, leading up to poe2 I was exclusively playing D4 on my series S , I fell off release around act 3 I think , come dawn of the hunt I’m now doing LS and a second character pushing into T4 content the game feels really good imo on console but definitely has some issues like textures not loading or disappearing but I’m sure those will be fixed in time , my main feedback will be from the perspective of someone who used to blast poe1 to Ubers and maps for hours knowing the depths of that endgame and looking to reflect and see how to make poe2 better but not the same .

I’m going to format this how I used to read the patch notes when I remember the game from a community legend.

Problem: Mapping feels bad without towers , towers can be inconsistent with placement and coverage and overlap, moving away from a good setup feels really bad , moving to a set up feels bad .

Solution: Remove towers but keep the endless atlas system , have tablets be a scarab like mechanic that can focus league mechanics for us and scale certain modifiers how they do now .

Problem: The current atlas is to simple and lacks the progression feeling and reward of making your own set up for the content you enjoy .

Solution: Combine all atlas trees and add more options to scale specific content that actually feel rewarding , have the T1-T4 system stay I think it gives poe2 a uniqueness but everything together in one single tree.

Problem kind of ?: League start was rough but the nerfs I think where necessary but community outrage spiraled and content creators had nothing to put out for “league start” .

Solution: When balancing keep somethings from DoTH intact to have as our “base” through EA to consistently have a decent say 3-5 builds as the league start staples IE how ed , mines , ls and so on where staples people knew worked and could rely on . This way content creators can feel like they aren’t going in blind and community members can follow there builds.

Closing thoughts : The game as it stands imo feels really good on console definitely are bugs and things but that’s expected but the endgame needs love , with I assume the general idea of poe2 being cross save and play is to capture the D4 target audience with how accessible the game there still needs to be some of that poe complexity in the atlas tree to also give players a feeling of reward for pushing maps and content .

The endless atlas idea is honestly really cool and I want to see it stay there is so much design space their to add different poi’s that the game can generate to give that “one more map” feeling of what could be over the next mountain , But towers hamper that feeling , makes mapping without them feel negative and leaving a solid set up also feels terrible , make tablets like a scarab possible let us roll them to 3 mods at rare and have uniques be uniques .

Removing towers also allows for full exploration, you can have random encounters on the atlas that spawn periodically as you progress further away or only in certain biomes that aren’t about min max efficiency but fun cool random shit that we could stumble into like a map where you get a fishing pole to catch fish in that map .

All in all the game is really damn good and going in a positive direction after watching the last interview I felt like making my case on why towers should go but the endless atlas should stay , giving poe2 its own endgame that is noticeable different from poe1 is key for both to thrive otherwise why have both . People might say they want the same endgame but after 2 leagues of the same endgame just with different launchers back to back people would burn out, I think the endless atlas system gives so much opportunity for the team to have the space to just go wild with some ideas to add .

Thank you for reading if you do and thanks you giving Xbox gamer dads something to play that’s not shit.
