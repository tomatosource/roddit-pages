[Tom Friend] Report: PSG and its minority owner Kevin Durant preparing bid for NBA Europe franchise

Sources tell SBJ more than one team could end up in Paris

Paris Saint-Germain is “positively and actively” preparing a bid for an NBA Europe franchise, the French newspaper L’Equipe reported Thursday, which means Rockets F Kevin Durant – who holds a minority stake in PSG – could wind up with a vested interest in the startup league.

But sources told SBJ Thursday afternoon that PSG is not the only entity pursuing an NBA Europe franchise in the city and that it could be outbid, supplanted or even joined by a second team in Paris.

With Tony Parker’s EuroLeague club ASVEL – based in Villeurbanne – also a frontrunner for an NBA Europe franchise, sources told SBJ it is now conceivable that as many as three teams could be based in the country.

Although PSG might end up starting an NBA Europe basketball product from scratch, L’Equipe reported it could also partner with an undisclosed existing franchise. One possibility could be Spurs C Victor Wembanyama’s hometown team Nanterre – just outside Paris, with L’Equipe speculating that PSG could align or merge with Nanterre due to an agreement between the NBA and FIBA that NBA Europe franchises are required to play in their domestic leagues.

Wembanyama actually just invested in Nanterre, and, if the franchise aligned with PSG, it would conceivably make he and Durant part of the same team.

During his All-Star Game press conference in February, Commissioner Adam Silver said active players might be able to invest in NBA Europe clubs, pending ongoing talks with the NBPA – so both Durant and Wembanyama presumably would be able to proceed.

Asked at the All-Star Game about PSG’s NBA Europe pursuit, the 37-year-old Durant said, “I wouldn’t say I’m involved in the process. I’m prepared to talk about it if it presents itself. For now, I haven’t heard too much, just rumors and narratives. Once it starts to form, hopefully I can be a part of it, pushing the game all around the world.”

At this month’s upcoming Board of Governors meeting, the NBA will update the roomful of owners about the prospective league, but likely will not stage a vote to greenlight it at that time– because bids are not formally due until later in the month. As it stands, it is almost a foregone conclusion the BOG will approve the venture, especially if the bids are what they league expects –somewhere in the \(500M-to-\)1B range.
