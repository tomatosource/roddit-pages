Am I a bad Celtics fan?

I’ve been a Celtics fan since the 2010 Finals when they faced the Lakers. From Rondo/Pierce/KG to Smart/Avery/Crowder to Tatum/Brown, this team has been my life. I can’t even put into words what they have meant to me all these years. When they won the 2024 championship I broke down into tears, the feeling was beyond what I thought was possible to feel. Euphoria comes up short.

After the title, I promised myself that 24-25 wasn’t going to mean as much as far as results. Obviously I wanted them to repeat bad, but after going down 2-0 vs the Knicks I kind of accepted it. Then the Tatum injury happened. Just as I never felt anything like the 2024 title, it was the same when he went down and grabbed his lower leg. We all knew what happened even before the news was official. The Celtics suddenly went from repeat hopefuls to… uncertainty.

Fast forward to this season, I thought they were going to be bad (not top 5 pick bad because of the East, but play-in bad). So, for the first time since I became a fan, I lost interest in the team. I’m not sure why and as I have finally been watching games again (I have followed almost every box score and sporadically watched games) I feel like a fake fan. I’ve never been a ‘player fan’ or ‘player follower’. Rondo was my second favorite Celtic of all time and I sticked with the team after his trade. Tom Brady is my favorite player of all time and I stuck with New England. I think atp Tatum is my favorite Celtic all time or right there with KG. But why did I suddenly lose interest in the team? They’ve been AWESOME this year and I’ve known it.

How could I go from being a crazy fanatic that would miss social events to watch games - to a fan that didn’t even bother turning on the TV even when having all the time in the world. I watched the Bucks game tonight and I felt very happy again, but a part of me felt fake. I don’t know what to feel to be honest but I felt like I had to talk to you all and get it out of my chest. Am I being a bad Celtics fan? When Tatum comes back and I start watching every game again with the level of detail I used to, does that make me a player follower and not a real fan?
