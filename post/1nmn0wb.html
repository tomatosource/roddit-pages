One way to get minion skill + 3⁄4 with 150 spirit on a skeletal warrior scepter

  • find a skeletal warrior scepter (ilvl 78), make it a rare, or just magical with a mod that has high weightage. (mana regen, int, presence regenerate life, max mana)

  • find any scepter with %spirit 51 (tier 3 seems easiest, you can try higher tier but lower the chances of success)

  • use the recombinator to try to bring the (% spirit 51) onto the skeletal warrior scepter, you might fail a few times. or it become success but its on the wrong base. (only choose 1 mod from each scepter, and check the % success. you should be able to eventually manage over 50% success rate, and if you use omen of recombination, it will increase higher because of lucky rolls

  • repeat the same process but for this mod (minions has increased life, again you don’t need high tier to make it easier to succeed, I usually go for tier 3⁄4)

  • repeat until you get 2 skeletal warrior scepters with (spirit% tier 3) and (minion tier 3⁄4)

  • do the final recombination of this 2 mods (make sure you use the omen of recombination for guaranteed success, or you can YOLO)

  • use the below omens

  • Omen of Homogenising Exaltation (this is expensive for a reason)

  • Omen of Greater Exaltation

and then Greater Exalted Orb the scepter,

Now you have a +3 or +4 minion scepter with skeletal warriors. Why does this work? the recombined scepter has only 2 mods, %spirit and minion life, which means the only tags it has are minion and life, so when you homogenization x2 exalt, it give only the 2 mods (Allies regenerate life and minion skill). Now you also know why so many scepter with minion skill have this 2 mods.
