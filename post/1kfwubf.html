Lets not forget that the campaign is still way too long, takes too much time and the changes to it barely made a difference

Right now the sub is not up in arms about the length of the campaign anymore, and that is mostly because people have completed the campaign or quit 0.2. And nobody has the desire to go though the campaign again.

GGGs changes to some map layouts during campaign were good changes. But the truth is that these changes also made basically no difference to the length of the campaign overall. To the time it takes to complete the campaign.

When 0.3. comes around, the sub will be filled with complaints about the length of the campaign again, rightfully so.

My personal opinion on this is rather radical. I believe no matter how amazing the campaign is and how beautiful its zones are, the campaign will allways just be the thing you have to overcome in order to play the endgame. Campaign will never be more than that.

And i personally truely believe that we would have to cut about 30% of the campaign in order for people to go through it multiple times per league.

I dont think GGG will do drastic cuts to the campaign anytime soon. But i also believe that the length and the time it takes to complete the campaign will be a hindrance for the game in the future.

Poe is a game that is more alive and more diverse when people are making their own builds, try new things out and take something and elevate it through different means of scaling.

But right now people are hesitant trying new things out and rather stick to the meta simply because nobody wants to go through that slog of a campaign again (and also because balance is very bad right now).

As someone who loves to experiment with his own builds and usually plays about 5-10 different characters per POE1 season, I really wish GGG would acknowledge that the campaign, especially on a fresh season, simply takes too much time. I really wish they would cut some zones entirely and make the layouts drastically smaller in size.
