I think the success of the early access really shows why the game should be design around Solo play

The incredibly high influx of players shows how quickly the game economy can change and be saturated with good and cheap items. You can find good 1ex rares for your build that perhaps you would never be able to get playing solo due to so little crafting options, and most of them being gated behind heavy RNG

Now you might think that the amount of players in a economy doesn’t matter, its perfectly scalable since you have more items but also more buyers, and I think that doesn’t work in the context of heavy RNG based itemization, here’s my theory: Each character will eventually drop/roll a few very good rares that aren’t for their build, and they will sell a few of these rares to then buy one good item for their build, so in short the average player will sell more rares than he buys, willing to sell low and buy high.

A lot of streamers that usually play Solo have been playing trade, and I think that’s a strong sign that a lot people want to play Solo and get their own items but there just isn’t enough incentive to do so.

Balancing the game around Solo might seem that it would make trade league awful and always have a shitty economy but I think it would just make people rely less on trading items (currency exchange is still awesome and needed) while still maintaining a competitive economy for high end items.

My specific feedback as a Solo player is that I wish more tiered rares would drop, I have done several T15 maps and saw 1 or 2 Tier 5 drops.

TL;DR: RNG based economy doesn’t scale, balancing progress and item acquisition around Solo would make people need to trade less and be more encouraged to “just play”
