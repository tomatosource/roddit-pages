The Celtics Podcasts I’ve Listened to, Best and Worst

Best:

-The Garden Report: great group of personalities. A variety of takes. Nice mix of real analysis and goofy comic relief. Episodes after every game satisfy the Celtics junkie appetite.

-First to the Floor: infectious Celtics enthusiasm and homer-ism. Australian accents are pleasant to listen to 😂

-You Got Boston: Noah gets in the weeds in a good way and shines light on lesser discussed or developing players.

Good:

-Big Three Podcast: Solid. Gary Washburn is a straight shooter.

-Celtics Beat: it’s okay. Not a top choice for me but generally listenable.

Worst:

-How ‘Bout Them Celtics: Jack Simone is far less listenable….

-Locked on Celtics: we get it Karalis, you once briefly played in a professional league overseas.
