People are prematurely crowning the Rockets as an elite team

Most people seem to have Houston as a top 3 team this upcoming season along with the Nuggets and Thunder. But to me, it seems a bit premature. We saw their offensive limitations in the playoffs. People are basically saying all of those problems will disappear with the addition of KD, but at the end of the day KD is 37 years old, injury prone, and has not had a major impact on the court in years now. Yes he’ll put up numbers but it overstates his actual impact on his teams. They still lack shooting outside of KD, and have very poor guard depth, it’s basically just FVV, Amen, and then the unproven Reed Sheppard.

I think people might be jumping the gun on them. They’ll still be great in the regular season because defense, youth and rebounding can carry you there but in a playoff setting I’m not convinced they’re better than teams like the Wolves, Clippers, Lakers etc
