Feedback after ~50hrs in endgame: the one thing that infuriates me the most

As stated in the title, I am now ~50 hrs in the endgame, which I define as T15 mapping with 3 tablets. I am an quiet experienced PoE1 gamer, playing for many years and farming my occasional mirror in SF trade. I usually know how to play juiced content and what my build can and cannot do. In 0.4 I am playing a freeze CI werewolf, sitting currently on around 9k ES and ~400k shred DPS.

I am fully aware of the potential endgame renewal in 0.5, however due to the experience with GGG in the last 12-18 months, they stick to weird design decisions, when they are convinced of them, even if the community disagrees (whoever this is). So I want to give input into this endgame renewal process, although my opinion does not really matter.

The number 1 thing that drives me nuts on a regular basis: 1 portal policy. There is nothing, and I really mean nothing, that is so punishing than this. One slight mistake, one mod you overlooked, one attack (especially as a melee player) you did not see winding up, one ground degen you recognize too late, and you get punished in the worst possible way by a game designer: complete progress nuke without recovery possibility.

The reason for this frustration is easy to recognize:

Triple punishment (I think this doesn’t exist in any other ARPG):

- minus 10% XP: hurts massively on high levels but everyone who plays ARPGs is aware of this.
- minus 1 tablet use x3, map destroyed, all rewards gone for this map node: my god, without words. Financial loss can be between a 10-20 exalts to multiple divines but emotional frustration is ten times worse
- In many cases you have to rerun the map node without any rewards, taking away time and since you are aware of an “empty” map it just feels like a waste of time (objectively speaking the smallest of the three due to 2-3 minutes tops time sink).

I cannot tell you the feeling of finally making my way again to a citadel, maybe even a copper, and getting owned by one of the many, MANY mechanics in the game that wants to kill you within 1 second.

Of course you can always say: do not roll the maps so hard (more portals with less mods), scan all maps for deadly mods, play safe, etc. And also of course, we do not know how the endgame philosophy will change in 0.5.

But for the love of god: we are ARPG gamer. And although we all have different paces, we all have different goals: please rethink this design.

There is for sure not a small community who WANTS to juice, who WANTS to create tough content, who WANTS to farm on the highest difficulty level. But please, 6 portals work also in PoE1. If I fail 6 times in PoE1, I am NEVER angry at the game, only at myself. In PoE2 it is always the opposite.

In PoE1 one of the biggest complaints in 3.27 was the lack of aspirational content due to scarab nerfs without replacements. The 1 portal strategy is more or less the same in PoE2: hard content is almost impossible to farm due to the triple punishment. Also, because our characters have to be good in all content that exists in the game, while in PoE1 we can specialize much more.

Please reconsider this in 0.5. Out of all potential critics in the current game state, I would be so freaking happy if I can juice my content without biting into my keyboard.
