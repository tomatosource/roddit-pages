Trade to SSF pipeline

Dunno about you guys, but I started 0.5 as a martial artist and got to around 96 or 97 and decided to reroll to softcore SSF. Don’t get me wrong, MA was super fun and after I got past some of the issues with my build early it was cranking out maps…but even with all my complaints about having to run the campaign over and over and it being kind of a slog I was drawn to SSF once again.

Maybe it’s trade reducing chase items or the focus being more on how can I use all of my stuff to better my character rather than hoarding currency to play the market, but I feel like I just interact with more content on SSF.

Have ~1.5k hours on PoE1 and each time a new league comes out I always feel like trade is the way to go at first and then I get bored with having too much power and decide it’s time to get put through the ringer on a semi-self-cooked build and SSF.

Anyone else feel this way? Checking out companions on SSF at the moment, but wondering if this is standard for people with a decent amount of hours or if people generally pick one type of league and stick with it…

Also if you have any interesting interactions with companion nodes or anything majorly useful for a new-ish (~100-200hr) SSF player lmk!
