GGG please reconsider the bugfix to liquid verisium

0.5.4b Hotfix 2 includes this change: Fixed a bug where you could interact with Verisium Remnants after applying Liquid Verisium to them in Expeditions and Grand Expeditions.

This completely kills the main source of skill ceiling in grand expeditions: proper routing to max rewards. Making the reroll now completely unknown means you have no idea what to path to.

Please reconsider this change, it frankly kills the mechanic in terms of enjoyability.

The bug essentially showed us what the reward would be, it didn’t allow us to change it. That functionality should be baked into liquid verisium rerolls.

I spent a lot of time exploring expedition after 0.5.4, it was skill intensive and rewarded deep understanding of its mechanics. The reroll was a big reason for this, knowing what to reroll and figuring out pathing on the fly as you reroll was rewarding and fun. All of that is now gone, it’s just a terrible crapshoot at this point. You don’t get to know if your remnants have a good rune to propagate, you don’t get to know which rewards are worth pathing to.
