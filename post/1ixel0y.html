Loot from 100 normal T1 maps

https://imgur.com/a/dvwIrCy

ETA: I did this out of curiosity, not to make 400 div/hour.

I’ve been running juiced T15/16, with tablets setup up, etc. etc., and have been getting crap from it. Then I randomly ran a T1 and got a divine. So I decided to try just running T1s for a while, purchased fresh and used as-is from Doryani. Here are the results.

  • Lvl 88 deadeye, +52.3% MS, ~5 div build
  • Filter all except uniques, currency, fragments, and extra content (essences, omens, etc.)
  • Roughly 50% magic find (45% from char, 5% average from running a few tablet-enhanced maps)
  • Atlas skills (30⁄30) focused on rare mods, magic find, and item quantity
  • Clear speed was about 10 maps per hour (includes time organizing my stash tab and getting/drinking tea)

Results

  • Roughly 3.5 div equivalent total (0.35 div per hour)
  • 2 div
  • 150 ex (exactly)
  • 26 chaos
  • 5 chance (mostly from fragments, but I did get one actual chance orb to drop)
  • 1 greater essence (electricity, which was nice)
  • 1 greater jeweller
  • Heaps of semi-precious junk (catalysts, essence, low-tier distilled, etc.)
  • A bunch of tablets
  • I converted 120 augments to 60 transmutes via Alva to free up stash space, so if you’re interested in the stats, take that into account

Observations:

  • While this isn’t showcase-worthy loot, I was surprised to see how closely it came to the loot I’ve been getting with juiced waystones (not to mention the cost to fully juice those waystones in the first place). Maybe I’m just unlucky… now I want to run 100 juiced maps to compare.
  • I died once by drowning in that magic drowning water those a3 casters use. Whoops.
  • More fun than I thought. Zero chance of dying (unless you’re me), everything gets 1-shot (with any cheap, casual-accessible build), and can make progress toward citadels/good atlas setups quickly.
  • Definitely want to do the same with tablet-enhanced T1s, as well as fully juiced T15/16/tablets. But I’m a noob casual, so it’ll take me another month and the realm will probably reset before I finish the next batch.
