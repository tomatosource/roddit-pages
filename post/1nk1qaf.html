Towers - Why don’t they let You move around map freely in the ring around them, after completing them?

Like the title says - Why don’t they let You teleport around map freely in the ring around the towers after completing them? I’ mean we scouted the map, we should be able to move pass dangerous sites if we want to and strategize our travels on the map.

Why isn’t this inherent aspect of how towers work? (I know there’s uniqe tablet for it, but it feels bad when I plug it in tower, because I’m getting less juiced maps, when I use it) Imagine. You go through 3-4 maps to one tower, unlock it, juice it and then pick any map in the ring around the tower as You please. Maybe even jump to other tower and travel around the map like that and go through the content however You like, it gives so much freedom, to do exactly what You want. Now Your only problem is gear level and your own abilities etc. which should be the case always in my opinion in those type of games.

It would be so much fun, it would feel rewarding to complete a tower if You got bogged down in an area where there are none of them and would give us much more agency on the map, which I certainly feel I’m lacking while doing maps now.
