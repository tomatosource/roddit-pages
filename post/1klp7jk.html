Trial of Wisdom (Act 4 Trial of Ascendancy Idea)

While it has been revealed there will be a third Trial of Ascendancy in Act 4, little is known about it as Early Access only contains Acts 1-3. I would like to propose an idea for a “Trial of Wisdom,” but instead of combat-focused challenges it would contain puzzle challenges as follows:

Act 4: Trial of Wisdom

  • Final Boss: Black King & Black Queen (chess-themed duo)

Mirror Puzzle

  • Room has a light beam and several mirrors. You can spin the mirrors around along its axis. Your goal is to reflect the light beam to a specific spot in the room.

Statue Puzzle

  • Room is full of statues. Some of them can be rotated, some can not. Your goal is to make all the statues point in the same direction.

Clockwork Puzzle

  • Room has 12 platforms in a circle forming a giant clock. Your goal is to set the correct time with hour, minute and second hands. Clues are hidden throughout the room.

Fountain Puzzle

  • Room has a large fountain in the center with a vase that pours water onto a tiled floor. The tiles have channels cut into them. Each tile can be rotated. Your goal is rotate the tiles so the water flows the correct path.

Lever Puzzle

  • Room has multiple levers on the wall. Your goal is to figure out the correct sequence of lever pulls. A hidden clue will be provided on the wall.

Candle Puzzle

  • Room has a large 7 candle candelabra. Currently candle 3 and 6 are lit. The problem is, the candles are all connected. Lighting or extinguishing a candle will make the two candles next to it change state. Your goal is to light all the candles.

Chess Puzzle

  • Room is a massive chessboard. You play as White in a pre-arranged setup. Your goal is to checkmate the Black King in 3 moves.
