PSA: Tactician’s gain armour as deflection works on TOTAL armour not base.

It’s due to the wording. When we have gain as extra- it uses base. But apparently gain Deflection Rating equal to 20% of armour works on total armour. I just tested it in game, had 5000 armour total, was giving me 1000 deflection rating. That means stacking 30k armour with iron reflexes, defiance banner, scavenged plating and then taking glancing blows should make tactician extremely tanky.
