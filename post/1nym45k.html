This (might) be the best game, i’ve ever played

So basically:

I arrived now after 350h of gametime in the uber endgame, where i only have to min/maxx my build to the perfection.

The crafting system, the trading system, the economy, everything feels so fckn perfect. Dropping currency is fun, its worth something. The builds have much variety (lightning meta yes, but if it doesnt have to be Tier 1 Meta, you have a lot of options).

The way you get so many different ways of making money in the game, how the game can be played in the endgame (i.e. mapping, breach/xesht, simulacrum, just hideoutwarrior crafting) its just insane.

Cons tho..:

  1. Remove (and i beg you to do it GGG), remove maps, which have bad performance

I have friends, whos PC system is at least 10x better than mine, worth thousands and they optimized the game with every trick they can and still get lags on certain builds Diablo 4 is way more stable than this game unfortunately Lower the graphics, give us more options to get a better performance, remove those shtty gras maps. Yes overall it will lower the diversity of maps, but in long term it feels better to play every map at least on 50-60 FPS than having 9 FPS stutters during a flicker strike or lightning warp.

  1. Make self crafted items either harder to do, or not sellable.

Its just too easy for people never leaving their house to farm thousands of divine orbs, while basically not playing the game. They playing a trading simulator. I get it, they re having fun doing that and thats also a kind of way to play the game, but it shouldnt be THAT easy.

  1. Not a con anymore:

Keep up the good work on trying to change the system and seasons every 3 months. For me as a new player, it just holds me in knowing, next season i will get the abyssal idea 2.0 on my maps, something new and worth farming. New builds, meta shifts and stuff, i just like those things to stay in the game longterm.

Hope that helps a bit, i know most of it is commonly known, but maybe i could clarify what a new player thinks of a game.

Sincerly
