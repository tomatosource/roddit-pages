WBC on 3⁄9: KOR-AUS, COL-PAN, GB-BRA, CUB-PR, DR-IS, and MEX-US all have important outcomes

KOR-AUS: There’s a whole tiebreaker table for this one, AUS wins they go to the quarter if they lose there’s a whole table about it

COL-PAN: If Panama wins, they can advance to the quarter if PR or CUB loses their last 2 and the 3rd tiebreaker goes their way. The loser of this game is relegated.

GB-BRA: Loser is relegated

CUB-PR: Winner clinches. Whomever loses has to beat Canada to advance, and each will have a chance.

DR-IS: IS stays alive if they win, but are eliminated if they lose due to the tiebreaker

MEX-US: 2 of the 3 2-0 teams in Pool B face off, and one is going to have to go out by the end

I made this post because I was trying to look at it and there was way too much going on. My main criticism of the WBC this year is that it has been hard to follow what’s going on, I’ve had to put in extra effort for it. Sorry if I got any tiebreaker wrong.
