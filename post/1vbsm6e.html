From Keith Smith of Spotrac:

After spending 11 days in Las Vegas for NBA Summer League, my notebook was overflowing. I succeeded in my annual goal of talking to at least one front office executive (in addition to coaches and scouts) from all 30 teams, and often times multiple of each. In exchange for anonymity, I was able to get a lot of interesting information from these executives. In addition, several agents were willing to share thoughts as well.

“It’s crazy that the Kawhi (Leonard and LA Clippers) investigation has taken so long,” a Western Conference GM said. “If this was just the Clippers and Kawhi, that would be one thing. But it’s now spread to the Raptors (from the proposed trade of Kawhi Leonard for Brandon Ingram and Gradey Dick and draft capital) to other players and teams, because LA has put some other stuff on hold. It’s pretty aggravating.”

“Look, the (Gary) Trent Jr. contract is probably circumvention, or at least agreeing to a future contract well in advance,” an Eastern Conference GM said. “But, it’s not like it doesn’t happen around the league elsewhere. And, now Milwaukee is stuck with a contract that doesn’t look great. Who really got hurt by this one?”

“If the Clippers were paying Kawhi (Leonard) outside of the cap, that’s a huge issue. They need to be hammered with the stiffest of possible penalties,” an Eastern Conference front office executive said. “But that’s very different from the Trent situation to me. I think we’ve probably seen a lot of deals like Trent’s over the years. It’s part of cap management, even if it was maybe agreed to in advance. Paying a guy off books? That can’t happen.”
