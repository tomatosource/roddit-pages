Knicks vs Celtics and Boston’s “Easy Path” last year

In retrospect it’s kinda hilarious all the shit Boston took last year about their “easy path”. So much was made about injuries and Boston’s good luck as if it wasn’t Boston’s own injuries and bad luck that held them back from winning titles in 2022 and 2023. It’s part of the game. You remove injuries and luck from the game, Boston is probably working on a 4-peat right now.

But you had people like Joel Embiid running his mouth during the Finals about how Boston only won “cuz injuries” as if Boston didn’t knock his ass out of the playoffs in all 3 head-to-head matchups and win 3 out of 4 regular season games against him last season. Now look at Philly and their big scary 20-36 record.

You had clowns like Jimmy Butler run his mouth about how “things woulda been different” had he been healthy after his team got cooked in 5 games (mostly blowouts) as if Boston didn’t beat his team’s ass all 3 regular season meetings last year including one by 33 points with him healthy. Pat Riley had enough good sense to tell Jimmy to shut the fuck up, Jimmy got mad, and now he’s playing elsewhere with the big scary Heat 3 games under .500.

So much was made of the West being the “better” conference in spite of Boston having a winning record against every Western conference team last year. The Mavericks coasted through the big bad West, then faced off against the Celtics, while completely healthy, and got taken behind the woodshed. Just an absolute epic beat down so much so that Boston decided to mail one of those games in so they could win the title at home. One of the most brutal gentleman’s sweeps I’ve ever seen that was so astronomically humiliating for the healthy Mavs and the Western Conference, that the Mavs ended up dumping their star “Designated Hitter” mere months later.

Maybe the most hilarious of all of these is how much bellyaching the New York based media made about the big nasty Knicks and how they could have threatened Boston had they been healthy and not gotten knocked off in Round 2. As if Boston didn’t finish 14 games ahead of them last season (largest gap between 1st and 2nd seed since 1976). As if Boston didn’t beat their ass 4 out of 5 regular season meetings last season. And now you have the new and improved healthy Knicks this season who have now been blown out by Boston in every matchup by 23, 27 and 13 points.

Cleveland deserves some credit, I suppose, but Mitchell was healthy for 3 of those playoff games and Boston won 2 of them. Porzingis didn’t play a single game that series. Boston also beat them 2 out of 3 times in the regular season last year and have beat them 2 out of 3 times this season despite the Cavs dominating run.

Really the only team that you have to tip your hat to is the Pacers who were by far the most challenging playoff series Boston had last year (despite Boston sweeping them). Indiana genuinely had one of the greatest offenses ever last season and each game was a fight. At the moment, they have actually won 2 out of 3 matchups this season, but one of those games was Boston blowing them out by 37. Boston beat them in 3 out of 5 meetings last regular season (one of which by 51 points), but if any team has an injury complaint, I suppose Pacers fans could make the argument their team would have lost in 5 or 6 instead of a sweep last season had they been 100%. Unfortunately, Porzingis missed all 4 of those games too so it’s a total wash and that argument wouldn’t hold up in basketball court.

Obligatory mention that this is not a suggestion Boston will coast through the playoffs again this year. Cavs, Knicks and Pacers all are legitimate teams and a couple of those Western teams look great - even the potentially improved Mavs (on paper) if healthy. But it is really funny looking back on idiots like Embiid and Butler shit-talking the 2024 championship run given everything we’ve seen over the past two years suggesting the 2023-24 Celtics were an all-time great team with no team remotely close that season.

TL;DR: Boston beat everyone’s ass last year and all the talk about the “healthy Knicks” being their biggest threat looks hilarious in retrospect.
