Bow builds have Rhoa, Caster builds have Blink, what do Melee builds have for mobilty?

Melee mobility is just bad.

Bow/Spear builds get Rhoa which lets them shoot with no movement speed penalty, casters get Blink for quickly teleporting around the map.

And melee?

Melee gets… walking.

Or worse: slow jogging while being pelted by 18 projectiles per second.

And no Leap Slam doesn’t count, it’s not a mobility skill because it gets slower the farther you’re traveling with it.

In a game where everything is faster, deadlier, and more ranged than ever, melee’s mobility tools are nonexistent. Just animations that root you in place and hope you can get your attack off without dying.

PoE 2 clearly wants combat to be deliberate, okay cool, but why does “deliberate” only apply to melee, while ranged gets to zip around like caffeinated monkeys? Don’t even get me started on how bottom right of the tree has all the movement speed nodes, they get to be the fastest while other builds feels like walking on mud.
