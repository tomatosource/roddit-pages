With LeBron as probable to play tonight he will tie Kareem in regular seasons games played

With LeBron now listed as probable in tonight’s game against OKC LeBron James in his 22nd season will tie Kareem in 2nd place for regular season games played at 1,560 games played only behind Robert Parish at 1,611 games and he is already the all time leader in Playoff games played. So far this season in 67 games LeBron has passed John Stockton, Dirk Nowitzki, and Vince Carter in games played and he has now tied Kareem. Due to how packed the Western Conference is right now, barring injury, LeBron will likely play all the remaining games on the Lakers schedule and finish this season at 1,563 games played. Assuming this next season LeBron will need to play 49 of 82 games to pass Robert Parish and become the all time leader in games played which is a feat he has accomplished every year of his career except 2020-2021 season.

Source: https://www.nba.com/stats/alltime?SeasonType=Regular+Season
