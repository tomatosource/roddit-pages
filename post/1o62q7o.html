The Philadelphia 76ers have announced that Hip-Hop, their mascot during the Allen Iverson era, will return on November 8th

The Sixers made the announcement tonight on their social media accounts.

Hip-Hop was the Sixers mascot from 1998 to 2011. His return coincides with the return of the AI-era Sixers logo, jersey, and court as part of the Sixers’ celebration of the 25th anniversary of the 2000-01 team that won the eastern conference finals.
