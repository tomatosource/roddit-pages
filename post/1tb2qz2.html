Runic ward should be a big buff to life builds

I haven’t seen the new mechanic talked about much here… since the ward mechanic converts some of the armour mods to ward, investing in ward will feel worse on ES/EV based builds as you will have to give up some of your ES/EV.

However, this downside may be significantly reduced for life builds (particularly those that use armour) because of the armour formula, you’ll be trading a relatively small amount of phys max hit for a larger overall hit pool. Life prefixes won’t be affected by stacking ward, presenting a good synergy with bottom and left builds.

Thoughts?

Edit: A lot of people in the thread seem to think that I’m stating life and ward will use the same scalars (%life, life regen, +max life). That is obviously not the case. I’m talking about scaling life and ward separately with ward as a complimentary defense to life in order to get a larger max hit pool while still leveraging life’s strong recovery mechanics.
