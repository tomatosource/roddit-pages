Druid hype for 0.3.0

I made a post in another thread “what i was hoping for in 0.3” and wanted to talk about it more here; obviously its druid. Why? Because of this video : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VwbCqQnWqog
(druid playthrough in act 4 from 2023)

Druid will enable sooo many builds :
- adding str/int spirit skills for warriors and spellcasters (cast on channel with incirenate will actually make incirenate playable, cast on melee stun will enable lots of builds)
- every feral druid ability will be playable with warrior
- every spell druid ability will be playable with sorc/witch
- a few more spells for each element will actually make elementalist sorc/witch way more fun (a volcano infernalist? hell ye! a chilling tornado stormweaver? icy hell ye!)
- spear-feral druid with a bear companion? you can recreate that scene from Baldur’s Gate 3 with 2 bears! or 3 with the right gear!

As for act 4 in general, new item bases, new spectres/companions to test out. Hell ye.

This is a hype thread for druids in 0.3, which surely will make it in this time, right? Right?
Please GGG, delay 0.3 if you need to, but make sure we get our hairy boy
