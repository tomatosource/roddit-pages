I hope PoE2 can adapt a thing or two of the recent titanquest 2 defense systems

After playing the EA of TQ2 for quite a bit, I must say it’s straight up a lot more fun to me during leveling, and the defenses actually work there too.

small rundown: you also have a dodge skill, agility gear all have implicits that improve dodge/its cooldown, you can modify the dodge skill to be better by spending skill points, and the dodge is not a zero sum game in terms of movement, but it actually can be used to move in and out of danger, and having agility gear is making it your primary defense. the overall combat allows for actually manually dodging stuff and being able to decide if you want to engange in melee fight or not. unlike poe2, where you need to spend alot of time pumping those evasion numbers up until it finally magically feels good

the mage gear has implicits that improve barrier, its like energy shield but instead of being permanent, its a button you press after which for a few seconds, you get a decaying shield, and mage gear just gives you the highest numbers for it (and it costs mana/is based on how much mana you have, so also heavily favors mage stuff) because dangerous attacks are telegraphed and you have a chance to react before monsters attack you, you have this button you can press that gives you the energy shield when you actually need it. in poe2 you need to first craft alot of ES gear to have a huge pool and get some recovery before its useful, making early levels really painful. in TQ2 its solved via implicits that works even if you have trash gear.

heavy armor gear also comes with an implicit giving you a chance to take less damage from a hit, its weak if you dont scale it (baseline 10% to take 50% less for all characters) but you can quickly stack up numbers if you wear heavy gear, on top of it having the highest armor values

this feels much better to play and makes poe2’s defenses feel very outdated and archaic. I really hope the devs can get the systems in poe2 better than they currently are
