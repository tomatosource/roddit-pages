A statistical comparison of the top 5 DPOY candidates for 2024-2025

The 2024-2025 NBA season is nearing a close and we are again entering awards season. The DPOY award consistently draws debate, particularly because of the lack of reliable statistical measures that can accurately delineate one player’s defensive impact. We therefore often rely on the ‘eye test’ and team defensive impact.

This season, the eye test told us that this DPOY award was locked for Victor Wembanyama, and as you’ll see below, the few stats we do have available back that up. Historically, the DPOY award has generally gone to the best defensive player (usually a rim protecting big, or ‘defensive anchor’), on one of the best defensive teams. Indeed, the last 10 DPOY winners have all been on a top 3 team defensive rating:

Unfortunately, Wemby suffered an upper extremity DVT that has ruled him out for the remainder of the season, leaving the DPOY field wide open. However, none of the main remaining candidates that have been mentioned meet prior team defense criteria, namely: Evan Mobley (Team DRTG #8), Jaren Jackson Jr (Team DRTG #11), Dyson Daniels (Team DRTG #17), and now Draymond Green (Team DRTG #7). Outside of these main candidates, the only players mentioned on a top 3 defense include Lu Dort (Team DRTG #1) and Ivica Zubac (Team DRTG #3).

For that reason, I’m going to make an attempt at drawing an individual defensive statistical comparison between the leading candidates. Please note, these defensive statistics are meant as a fun exercise and to guide the discussion, and are notably not individually reliable indicators of defensive impact.
