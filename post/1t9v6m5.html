Questions about the game from a D4 player

Hi everyone, I’m almost finished with D4’s new season/xpac, and since I find myself with a lot of time to play, I really wanted to try out poe2.

However I had a few questions to understand if it’s my kind of game.

I really like class fantasy. I know that Poe allows you to have somewhat unintuitive builds, like say a ranger using wands and spells rather than bows/crossbows. Is this an edge case, and most “meta” builds follow class fantasy, or is it the norm? I understand you can make whatever kind of build you want, but I’m specifically talking about competitive builds, capable of clearing higher tier endgame content.

In terms of complexity, I like build complexity and skill-based complexity (attacks to avoid, boss patterns, etc), but I get easily overwhelmed by wider game design complexity, like very inaccessible season mechanics, or just so many of them layered on top of each other with interconnected systems that they become unapproachable without external help (like, I imagine, is the case with Poe 1).

Finally, how unforgiving is the game? Say I start on 0.5 release, but being my first time, I take my time with the story and campaign, say 10 days to get through it all.

Is trading so essential to clearing endgame content that by then I’ll have meaningfully gimped myself, or is that more of a concern for the top 1% of players?

Thanks for any help!
