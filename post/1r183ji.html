On this date in 1949, Joe Fulks recorded the NBA’s first ever 50-point game, going off for 63 points on 56 attempts

On February 10 in 1949, Joe Fulks went 27-for-56 from the field and scored 63 points in a 108-87 win over the Jets. Box score: https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/194902100PHW.html

It was the first 50-point game in NBA history. George Mikan then rattled off the next four, from 1949-1952.
