Should a Hardcore league death send you to it’s Softcore equivalent ? A 0.2 prospective.

With 0.2 coming closer and the first economy reset of POE2 alongside it, I wanted to bring (back?) this topic.

For clarification for those unaware, upon a “normal” economy reset and thus a league release, you now have the option to create a new character either in the most recent league or in the permanent parent league.
For example, let’s call the next league the Javazon league. We’d be prompted with 4 choices when creating a character : Javazon SC, Javazon HC, SC and HC.

How it currently works in POE1 if you want to create a character in Javazon HC, is that your death would send your character directly to the SC realm, not the Javazon SC realm. I’ve never quite understood the reasoning behind that and would love to hear what everyone has to say about this. My obvious proposal is to send a dead Javazon HC character to Javazon SC rather than directly to SC.

NB : There’s no telling either system is exactly what’s going to happen on 0.2 release since it is an early access and this version of SC/HC could be largely broken and not be an actual playable realm due to X or Y things becoming broken. But it is fairly certain that something at the very least similar to POE1 will eventually happen to POE2.

Downsides : The permanent SC realm would have to wait for the entire duration of the league to receive league-specific items. The counter argument being that it is the case for the permanent HC realm anyway.
The second downside would be for Javazon HC items would flood the market in Javazon SC. The counter argument there being that at least it means there are Javazon HC items in the first place, and secondly that the Javazon SC market is so obnoxious anyway, I’d be interested to see if it has actually reached its peak or if it can, in fact, get worse.

The upside is very simple. An incentive for more people to start Javazon HC (even if they end up dying and keep going in Javazon SC) rather than having to wait for the community “as a whole” to look themselves in the eyes and ask “Guys ? Do we go HC Trade this league ? Anyone ?”
