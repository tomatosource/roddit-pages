I also leveled my first lvl 100 on poe1 3.27 and in my opinion its much harder to hit lvl 100 on POE2.

Tips:

  1. Spend the time first to try out builds that you like. lvl 100 is going to take a lot of time so atleast play a build you really like.
  2. Defensive Layers
  3. Regen, Effective Health Pool
  4. Always check the map modifiers. You should know which one’s are capable of killing you or going through your defences.
  5. Omen of Amelioration. I recently just knew this but you can only use 1 Omen per map.
  6. If you get tilted, take a walk for a while. Dont force it. There was a time when I was lvl 99 I went from 62% to 42% just because like I was trying to power through and was frustrated
