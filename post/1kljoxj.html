This feels like the KG injury all over again

In 2009, Celtics were rolling and poised to win their second consecutive title. Then KG got injured and the team was bounced in the semis. Last night, it was a bit of deja vu. Yeah, let’s cheer the guys to extend the series and even go for it all. But reality is, next season Tatum is probably out and either JB takes us to the playoffs or Brad decides to tank. Regardless of the path we take, it sucks that this iteration of the Celtics may have played it last game. An unceremonious end to an unforgettable run.
