The Washington Nationals enter play on Monday in 2nd place in the NL East, with a positive run differential (+1)

The Nats enter the month of June with a winning record, which considering they’re in the midst of a restarted rebuild is pretty shocking. The vibes have been great in DC, Blake Butera has been a revelation for the clubhouse, and they’re doing it all without a competent pitching staff (25th-best ERA in MLB).

James Wood and CJ Abrams are studs. Curtis Mead has a 141 wRC+ in 45 games with the Nats. Cade Cavalli has shown flashes of his ace upside. Hell, even Miles Mikolas and Zach Littell have been better lately.

Nats fans have been starved for entertaining baseball in recent years. I was at Saturday’s game and it was the most fun I’ve had at the ballpark since 2019. Maybe it all falls apart down the stretch, but I’m going to enjoy this ride while it lasts.
