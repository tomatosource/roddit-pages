Our expectations from last weekend

We should all remember our expectations when we found out we were going to play the Knicks this series.

A lot of people thought we matched up well against the Knicks. The Magic took away the 3-point line, and we attacked the basket. We all knew that the Knicks were going to sag off, giving us a lot of opportunities for three-point shots.

That’s why we were excited for this match up. We thought we could feast on the 3s. Most people who predicted the Celtics in 5 probably pictured the Celtics winning by scoring a lot of 3s.

In these two games, we got exactly what we expected. They sagged off. We shot a bunch of threes, a lot of them were good looks. Unfortunately, we shot much lower than our average. I believe this is due to variance, and not because of the Knicks 3pt defense.

Because of this, I don’t think we should stray away from the 3-point shot. In game 3, I would still want them to take the 3 if it’s there.
