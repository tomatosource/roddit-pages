Detroit locks in top seed in the Eastern Conference

The Detroit Pistons (57-21) secure the top seed in the Eastern Conference after a 116-93 win against the Philadelphia 76ers on Saturday.

The Boston Celtics (52-25) hold a 2.5 game lead over the New York Knicks (50-28) in the battle for the second spot.

• With 5 games left, the Celtics magic number is 3 to clinch the #2 seed.

• Toronto moves back into 6th as they arrive in Boston for Sunday’s game.
