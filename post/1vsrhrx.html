How often does a first baseman have to make a competitive throw?

I noticed that first basemen basically never have to throw the baseball. I’m not talking about the little underhand lobs on pitcher-cover-first plays, I mean a THROW, whipping it in a situation where arm strength actually matters.

The best thing I found is this table, if you look at team averages at the bottom you can see that in 2025, the average team’s 1B had 17 assists to 2B, 2 to home, and 3 relay throws from the outfield, or 22 all year, around once every 7 games. Three 3-6-3 double plays a year, and 2 throwing errors. Some of the assists 2B are shovel throws going to their right, but no idea how many.

This does ignore non-error unsuccessful attempts, but I am not sure how to figure out how often those happen, but even if you say it’s 50⁄50, a 162-game 1B throws the baseball 44 times all year, about once, maybe twice a WEEK. Kind of nuts. Does this sound right, what do you think?
