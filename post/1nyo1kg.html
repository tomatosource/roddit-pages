Kyle Tucker has posted a negative Win Probability Added (WPA) in 9 straight postseason series. The Brewers series could be #10.

Win Probability Added (WPA) shows the change in probability of a team winning that game, based on the actual results of each AB. A negative WPA means that player hurt his team’s chances of winning more than he improved his team’s chances of winning.

Kyle Tucker has been a net negative to his team in 9 straight postseason series now, and already has a -2% WPA to begin the Brewers series. In the 16 postseason series his team(s) have completed (not counting the current series), he has had a positive WPA 4 times; and none since 2021, despite playing in the postseason every year.
