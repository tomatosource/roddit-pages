On a team in a slump

I’m a newer fan born out of last year’s playoff run. While the team is obviously going through it right now, I attended my first C’s game recently and man, what a wicked atmosphere, and what wicked athletes these fellas are. The jerseys, the crowd reactions, the physicality of the game – the whole thing was honestly stunning, and I would never have experienced any of it if it wasn’t for the work this team has put in.

I don’t know if the C’s are going to repeat this year. It’s a historic undertaking no matter how well they play. But I do know that however this year turns out, I’ll be there watching and enjoying the Celtics culture.
