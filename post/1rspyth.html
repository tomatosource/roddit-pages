Last night was the best regular season game of Jaylen’s career, and his run since the All-Star Break has been him at his peak.

Let me start by saying this: I’ve been a lot lower on Jaylen this year than most on the sub. I think his defense is overrated, the efficiency is not where you want it to be, and the TOs are still absolutely killer.

I’ve been a guy who’s constantly said “I’m not surprised by his numbers” because the added volume and responsibility were going to lead to higher counting numbers. I’ve also been a guy (and I still am) who thinks having the worst on/off on the team is cause for concern.

But with all that being said, these past couple weeks, culminating with last night, have been amazing to watch. And it’s also changed my opinion a bit on who he is. Part of it is having Tatum back, part of it is opportunity, but the passing has been ridiculous, and his efficiency has been so much better than was in January or February.

Last night, Jaylen controlled the game from start to finish offensively, making read after read that got his guys open shots. I know it might be an overreaction considering the loss and the TOs, but that was my favorite game of Jaylen’s RS career. (Game 3 of the 24 finals was my playoff favorite).

I know it’s not analytic to say this, but there haven’t been many times Jaylen has gone shot for shot with another teams superstar. Sure there have been times he couldn’t miss, but not in tight game situation like that. He was calm and collected all night. Even with the minutes, he still had the legs to make the shot over Dort to tie the game.

On top of last night, the past 10 have been his playmaking peak.

Against SA, he could have had 15 assists easily if he doesn’t get ejected.

Against Cleveland he allowed Tatum to get going, and then dominated off the dribble finding wide open guy everywhere, and made maybe the best pass of his career to Sam in the corner with his left.

7 straight of 7 assists. Never EVER did I expect him to make this type of jump.

IF Tatum is fully healthy, the Celtics look like they might have 3 guys who can run the offense effectively. It’s a huge recipe for success in the future.
