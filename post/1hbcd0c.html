NBA players have missed 40% more games due to injuries so far this season compared to the same point last season…

This season has been brutal for injuries. Most teams have stars injured, or stars who have already been injured.
In the east:

  • Celtics: KP has only played 5 games
  • Magic: Both Paolo and Franz got the same injury and won’t be playing for a while
  • Bucks: They have a bunch of older players so they will probably get some injuries
  • 76ers: Joel Embiid and Paul George keep getting injured, Tyreese was out 2 weeks
  • Hornets: LaMelo Ball just got injured
  • Raptors: Scottie Barnes just sprained his ankle

In the west:

  • OKC: Chet Holmgren literally fractured his pelvis
  • Grizzlies: Players keep rotating in and out of starting positions due to minor injuries
  • Mavs: Luka had an injury
  • Warriors: Steph Curry isn’t playing all games
  • Clippers: Kawhi Leonard hasn’t played this season
  • Nuggets: Jamal Murray has an injury
  • Lakers: Lebron got injured
  • Suns: KD sprained his ankle
  • Spurs: The Alien has back issues
  • Pelicans: Practically decimated

I’m probably missing a lot but injuries are really affecting the league. If the magic weren’t injured they would literally have 2 all stars. 76ers “may” have been way better. OKC would be more likely to win the in season tourney with Chet. The Clippers would be higher than they are right now.

It’s hard to see teams falling short of their potential because of injuries. The league is so stacked with talent right now, but we’re being robbed of seeing what these players and teams can actually do. If even half of these players were healthy, the standings would probably look completely different
