What if we seperate mod weights for Crafting and Ground Rares

Currently most people dont care about ground rares because its highly improbable to get combination of high tier good mods on a good base. But what if we had a system where rares had seperate mod weights (or maybe even tiers) for dropped items vs crafted items. The tiered rares do something similar, however i dont think they are good enough to solve the problem of people not caring about dropped rares. Maybe something along the lines of what i suggest would make people be excited to pick up and identify rares.
