I’d really like an SSF mode with some advantages.

I enjoy playing SSF, but I don’t want to play 300 hours per league just to have the resources to craft a good weapon or to find a unique that allows me to make the build I have in mind. And sometimes I’m not around during the first weeks of a league, which makes me not want to play Trade League because of the economy.

And honestly, I don’t really see the point of SSF league. Most of the time, I play Trade League without actually trading on the market, because at least I can be in the guild hideout and trade uniques with my friends who also play what I call “Guild SSF”.

For example, omens are way too rare for me to do late-game crafting. I’m going to stop playing the league long before I get what I need. Or maybe I manage to craft my late-game weapon, and that’s it.
I already have 600 hours in PoE2, so I play the leagues a lot, but I still feel this way.

I also have to choose between saving all my crafting materials for my super late-game item, which means I basically can’t craft anything along the way during my progression. And that feeling sucks.

I understand the loot balance in Trade League so that you don’t get everything right away.
But in SSF, it feels like if you don’t nolife the league, you can forget about getting a really optimized character.

I’d like something like Last Epoch an actual advantage for SSF league. Maybe a tree where you can specialize in uniques, omens, or other drops.

Am I the only one who feels this way?
