Falling Thunder Auto Proc Power Charges

Not sure how well known this is, but if you put ailiths chimes and culmination 2 on falling thunder skill gem and build combo with another ability (I use whirling assault), it will automatically use the charges when activating the skill (it doesn’t show an indicator that you have power charges). This makes it to where you dont need to use several skills to first get the power charges then use Falling Thunder, you can just freely activate the skill and it does the work for you.

You can also pair this with Fist of War support gem to empower your attacks. I used the nodes to the right of Subterfuge Mask in the passive tree to make my empowered attacks do 16% of dmg as extra cold damage. And because the ability already has used 2 slots of support gems i paired it with heightened charges (optional) for the chance of getting the power of 12 power charges (i full sent on maxing out for all 6 power charges. This is a very powerful option for bossing as it can be hard to make a solid way of getting power charges without trash mobs in my experience.
