Where in the world is Lucas Giolito?

Lucas Giolito remains unsigned, a week into the regular season. Has there been literally any reporting about this? He was a 2 win player last year and has average projections. He is unspectacular at this point of his career, but is definitely a solid number 5 in the worst case scenario. He is also only 31, way too young to even entertain hanging up the spikes.

He doesn’t really fit the profile of a guy who might hold out deep into the season. He didn’t get a qualifying offer. While he pitched 145 innings last year, he is coming off of recent elbow surgery, so he doesn’t make a ton of sense to sign as an early season injury replacement. Fangraph’s ‘We Tried’ tracker did list that he was having some elbow concerns, but was planning to pitch in 2026. If anything, he fits the profile of a guy who should sign very early in the offseason.

Perhaps he is holding out for his one big deal for some reason, and teams aren’t biting because of health. At this point, it is unimaginable to see him getting a multi year deal. But I think a majority of the teams in the league would definitley love to have him on a 1 year $12 million deal. Not to mention that he has had great strikeout stuff at points in his career, and could be a good lump of clay for a pitching lab to work with.

Other notable unsigned free agents have at least some reasons to remain teamless, but I would still have expected at least minor league deals at this point:

  • Michael Kopech was injury prone and ineffective last year, but has insane stuff metrics.
  • Nestor Cortes and Frankie Montas were both atrocious last year and underwent elbow surgery. They might rehab solo and sign small deals next year.
  • Jessie Winker was bad last year, but should make sense for a rebuilding team minor league contract
  • Patrick Corbin is 36, but had 2 fWAR (and -.3 bWAR lol) last year. Retirement makes sense but last year was arguably one of his best recent years.
  • Max Kranick was sneaky good last year too, but I believe he had an arm injury.

What do you think is going on with these guys, especially Giolito?
