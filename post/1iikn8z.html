De’Aaron Fox-“We definitely want to win,” he said of the Spurs’ game against the Hawks on Wednesday night. “We still have their picks, so that’s definitely a thing.”

De’Aaron Fox may not know the Spurs playbook from cover to cover yet, but he sure was up to speed on the importance of Wednesday night’s game against the Atlanta Hawks.

“We definitely want to win,” he said. “We still have their picks, so that’s definitely a thing.”

Fox, who the Spurs acquired from Sacramento in a three-team trade completed Monday, was referring to Atlanta sending the Spurs’ three first-round picks, including their unprotected 2025 choice, in June 2022 in exchange for Dejounte Murray.
