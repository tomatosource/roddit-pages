NBA viewership up 60% from last years Opening Week, highest since 2017

The NBA’s Opening Week games averaged 2.8 million viewers across NBC Sports, ESPN and Amazon Prime Video, per Nielsen (and Adobe Analytics for NBC games) — up 60% from last year and the highest average for a traditional Opening Week since 2017.

While Nielsen has in the past year expanded its out-of-home viewing sample and shifted to a new methodology that adds “Big Data” from smart TVs and set-top boxes to its traditional panel, those changes would not account for an increase of such size.
