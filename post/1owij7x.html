The Theme of the ‘25 MLB Season: Back-to-Back

For the first time in 25 years we had Back-to-Back WS champions, but that’s only the tip of the ice berg.

Now that the post season award cycle has ended we have also seen:

Back-to-Back AL & NL MVPs
Back-to-Back AL Cy Young
Back-to-Back AL & NL Managers of the Year

5 Back-to-Back Gold Glove winners
8 Back-to-Back Silver Slugger winners

Out of 50 Post Season Awards (Not counting RoY because Back-to-Back is impossible) 18 went to who won it last year. 36% of the awards were repeat winners.

I don’t have the capacity to manually go back and check all the seasons to confirm if this is a record, but seems like a VERY high percent, even more so when you just look at the major individual awards where only Paul Skenes didn’t win the same award last year… and even he won RoY so he is still Back-to-Back in terms of winning one of the major awards.

Seems like one of those weird statistical quirks that means next to nothing, but is interesting when you think about it.
