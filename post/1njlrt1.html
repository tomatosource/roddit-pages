The Celtics need to prioritize getting young players more minutes this year

I think in a gap year, this teams’ young players need to play a LOT. You need to see what you have with Scheierman, Walsh, Minott, Garza, Queta, Hugo, Amari, Luis, etc. This the perfect team to do it. On the other end, it makes sense to limit Jaylen and Derrick’s minutes.

Simons and Boucher are here to be traded at the deadline IMO. Take any positive value you can get for them. Simons is a bit challenging due to his salary, but as long as Boucher is healthy and productive, he will 100% have a market due to being on a minimum and for a bunch of teams that will need a backup PF/center. A team like Denver would make a ton of sense for him.

Simons I could see the Bucks? Heat? It’ll be tricky, and they probably won’t want long-term salary unless it’s a guy they like (they want to get under the tax). I could see Simons getting bought out at the deadline if there’s no trade out there for him (and he’s healthy).
