Campaign Support Drop rates need to be addressed

After doing a few 1-maps test runs in the past few weeks, and watching multiple runs from streamers, its pretty clear the drop rates are still too stingy.

There is 0 room for experimenting, say you start out with bolt based skill on xbows, but then want to test out some grenade skills later, the game just doesnt support that.

Its already pretty lame that skills are gated by such late levels, but the fact that you likely wont have enough support gems until 20-25 maps deeps into endgame kinda blows
