On the changes to Rapid Shot

I’d like to applaud whichever dev it was who decided to change rapid shot into an ability that requires me to keep my eyes on the top left of the screen AT ALL TIMES, effectively taking me out of the game. Which, by the way, requires you to LOOK at the enemies because you need to dodge their attacks.

This might seem like a small issue but I assure you it is not, with how fast heat builds up with the new changes, you HAVE to keep track of your heat stacks before you overheat and have to wait 10 whole seconds to shoot again, which is fucking ridiculous, they may as well make me wait 10 whole hours before I can shoot again at this rate.

No other ability in the game (to my knowledge) has this sort of bullshit to deal with, and if GGG absolutely insists on the current changes to Rapid Shot, PLEASE add a heat counter near where all the action is going on so I can LOOK at the enemies I’m fighting while keeping track of my heat stacks.
