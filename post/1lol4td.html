If you have Cats, check your screenshots folder!

I was wondering why and where a 1GB of space went but wasn’t too concerned because it was only a 1GB and figured it was an update file or something. Nope. My Cat likes to sleep next to me while I’m gaming and will lay on the keyboard when I’m not around.

Well I went to the bathroom, and came back to him sleeping on it, spamming F12. I had 1.36GB of screenshots in only a few minutes, and the pop-up saying “Screenshot Taken” keeps popping up because of how many were spammed.

TLDR; Check your screenshot folder -&gt; My Computer -&gt; C: -&gt; Program Files (x86) -&gt; Steam -&gt; UserData. Right-Click and see how much space is used from accidental screenshots
