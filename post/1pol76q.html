Constructive feedback to GGG

First of all, I want to praise the Delirium fog adjustments — they are fantastic. The transformation system is also very fun. With over 1,500 hours played since version 0.1, I’m really enjoying this patch, but I’d like to share some constructive feedback.

This league I started as a Druid instead of my usual Warrior, and my elemental defenses felt noticeably weaker. A key example was the fight against Lythara, the Wayward Spear. I assumed her purple ground effect dealt Chaos damage so I invested heavily into Chaos resistance, only to later realize it was actually Lightning. This highlights an issue: it’s unclear what type of damage kills the player, making it mistakes to fix defensive .

I also encountered a monster that transforms into a bird and becomes untargetable. It reappears briefly, then immediately transforms again, which feels similar to the old invisible ghost issue and could use a similar fix. Another frustrating enemy type is a shield-bearing monster that blocks all damage indefinitely. Early on, when player damage is low, this feels especially punishing, and its frequent, hard-hitting green projectile attack makes encounters feel unfair.

Leveling from 97 to 100 feels extremely difficult, especially for players without PoE 1 knowledge. Even with heavy effort, alternating farm between T15 and T16 maps, I couldn’t reach level 100.

Compared to the previous patch, character power and clear speed feel lower, making this grind even harsher. At the very least, I wish a T15 map could reliably give around 1% EXP.

Lastly, I like the Temple mechanic overall, but the limited and often destroyed paths make reaching desired rooms frustrating. I also haven’t encountered the body modification/surgery mechanic at all and am unsure if it’s currently in the game.

Despite all this, I’m still having fun and will continue playing. Thanks to GGG for continuing to improve the game.
