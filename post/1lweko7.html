The best we could hope for is Williams and Gonzalez become our starting 3 and 5 on opening night. (Yes this is far fetched). This would let us start rookies all season who will improve dramatically and give us the most on court asset generation. But it would cause us to lose games for sure.

The 2nd best thing we could hope for is Simons breaking out into his prime in a contract year, us extending him and him becoming a really valuable trade piece. (Then maybe using his contract to help us get a 2 way player that fits our model better)

No one has to be salary dumped until the trade deadline. That will likely be in the form of Tillman being traded for nothing and then waived by his new team or something similar.

There is no reason to asset dump Simons or Niang to get under the 2nd apron. And Simons stands as one of our best hopes to create some value next season outside of a rookie or Red Claw breaking out.

The goal is to find enough value that we can trade for a Jrue Holiday archetype again. Elite 2 way veteran looking for a new situation.
