Why Twisters and Snipe haven’t been nerfed

There has been a lot of griping in the community about how every cool build is getting nerfed (Like totems just did), but Twister and Snipe are avoiding this nerf, despite being demonstrably very powerful.

My 2 cents on this is that they aren’t being nerfed, because they are working as intended. Twister and Snipe are both supposed to be powerful playful skills that you get to use for doing a bunch of set up, and having a decent amount of skill. Both builds can be rather Finnicky and troublesome to play, with snipe having an in built timing requirement as well as obvious aiming Requirement, and twisters also has time windows and setup requirements to achieve the mlperfect outcomes.

The only of the two builds I have actually played is twister, as I used it as my twink leveling set up in 0.4. I am relatively familiar with the build. I am still not league starting it.

The Twink leveling version uses 5 different uniques, 6 gems (Tabula Rasa my beloved), and some annoying techs to make it work. The League start verison replaces those uniques with about a dozen more button clicks that have to be sequenced and timed correctly. I ain’t got time for that.

So despite their obvious power, they have a high skill floor, and I think GGG is fine with that.

The things we have seen nerfed in this last week or so have been unintended interactions that would have caused a toxic environment where something was both the best thing out there, and very easy to play.
