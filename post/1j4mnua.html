Uncut Support Gem 1, 2, 3 and Uncut Skill Gem 1-20 Should Stack.

The logic behind things that cannot stack is that they can be either:

-Unidentified

-Have multiple different stat on them

-They took more than 1 slot in inventory/stash

-They can be corrupted

None of these things are true for uncut skill/support gem. They should stack.

This would make the gem stash tab WAY cleaner without having to scroll all the way up and down as the level can be put next to each other and separate the already cut skill gem into their own section at the bottom.

Edit: Forgot to mention, Uncut spirit gem should also work the exact same way.

The final result of the change would be 43 slots of all types of uncut skill and support gem and the same tab at the bottom with all the already cut skill gems.
