Memphis Grizzlies 2025-26 Hardwood Classic Shorts Leak On Instagram

Based on a leak from a familiar source, the Memphis Grizzlies will celebrate their 25th season in Memphis by wearing Hardwood Classic uniforms during the 2025-26 season.

There’s an asymmetrical turquoise and red striping pattern down the left side of the shorts and around the leg holes, as well as their secondary logo on the right leg, There’s also their paw print mark on the waistband, which is primary turquoise with a smaller red stripe.

The jerseys, meanwhile, prominently feature a large “Memphis” wordmark across the chest, classic trim with the Grizzlies’ ball-in-claw logo at the base of the collar and a turquoise and red stripping pattern down both sides.
