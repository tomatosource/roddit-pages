The Denver Nuggets are a 1st round exit waiting to implode.

So a lot of people consider them contenders just because of Jokic, but they just have too many flaws like horrible defense and bad spacing that are time-ticking bombs that will make them inevitably collapse in the playoffs.

Their defense is so bad that when they score 120-130 points they are in dog-fights against lottery teams because they also give up the same amount of points. And their record of 43-25 has been heavily carried by a lot of comebacks/4th quarter heroics against mediocre teams. Maybe its an effort issue, but by this point in the season they just have’t shown they can defend. Jokic is to blame because his defense has slipped, but other guys like Murray/MPJ have also been horrible defenders and they lost a lot of good vet defenders from past years. Secondly, their offense often runs into droughts against good teams because they don’t have shooters and teams dare their non-shooters like Westbrook, Watson, Braun to shoot. And lastly, they are running all their starters into the ground by playing them 35-40 minutes a night and it is eventually going to catch up with Gordon already being injured, Jokic also hampered, and Murray injuring his ankle in the latest game.

Overall, there is just no good reasoning for them to be contenders despite of how good “Jokic” is on offense. And it is very likely that they will lose in the 1st or maybe 2nd round and blow parts of their team up like MPJ or their coach Michael Malone during the summer.
