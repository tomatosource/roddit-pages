MLB.tv IS NOT behind a double paywall.

I was hoping the comment section of that post regarding this would simply POINT OUT WHAT WAS IN THE ARTICLE. (The article by “Awful Announcing” which should already tell you it’s click bait garbage.)

FROM THE ARTICLE:

&gt; MLB.tv offers a free month of ESPN Unlimited upon signup. MLB.tv subscribers can cancel the ESPN Unlimited subscription during their free trial and still retain access to MLB.tv throughout the entire season.

All they’re doing is trying to sell ESPN Unlimited when you sign up for MLB.tv. The annoying part being you have to take the free trial and cancel it. But once you do that, you’re only paying whatever you have to pay for MLB.tv. That’s it. There isn’t another mandatory monthly charge on top of your MLB.tv subscription price.

Yes, it sucks and is just another annoying tactic to suck more money out of people who might forget to cancel. But no, it’s not a double paywall. Lets keep our heads screwed on.

EDIT: I am in no way defending MLB, ESPN, or any other of these greedy goons for how they are doing business. I am simply pointing out that it was VERY misleading in how it was presented and making people think they had to pay monthly subscription fees on top of their MLB.tv subscription.
