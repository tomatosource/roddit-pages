Kawhi Leonard has not been featured in recent New Balance commercials

From watching the NBA regular season and postseason earlier this year, as well as other sporting events where New Balance commercials are shown on TV, I’ve noticed a lack of Kawhi Leonard being in the ads.

Find that interesting considering he’s still a New Balance athlete and has a signature shoe.

Not sure if this is related to his Aspiration investigation, Kawhi’s request not to be in the commercials, both, or something else entirely.

As reference:

  • 2025 ‘We Got Now’ commercial featuring multiple NB athletes across different sports. (Featuring Kawhi.)
  • 2026 ‘We Got Now’ commercial featuring multiple NB athletes across different sports. (Kawhi is not featured, even though six NBA players and one WNBA player are in the commercial.)
  • 2026 New Balance Hoops commerical. (The same six NBA players and one WNBA player noted above are in the commercial, but no Kawhi.)
