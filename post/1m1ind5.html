NBA afterlife & mythmaking thesis

Hi r/nba! My name is Lev and I’m a grad student at NYU (and also a huge NBA fan). You might also know me as lisco_2000/the Dario Šarić girl, and I mention that to just give you a little context as to who I am and how involved in NBA fandom/media I am – I promise this post is not self promo!!!!

My grad school thesis is centering on afterlife, mythmaking, and grief in the NBA. It’s honestly pretty hard to put into words, but here is a mind map summing up the major events and concepts I’m trying to connect, and here is a presentation I did while scouting advisors for my project.

The main subjects and topics I’m dicsussing:

- digital reanimation, what-if culture, and simulation as a coping mechanism – i.e. simulating Len Bias’ career in 2k, Dražen Petrović haunting NBA Jam 93 (and Reggie Lewis not haunting NBA Jam 93), simulating different outcomes in 2k (what if Haliburton didn’t get injured in game 7? what if the Sixers didn’t trade Mikal Bridges on draft night?), etc

- Kobe numerology and Mamba Mentality as a spiritual practice formed out of grief

- how fans and media define and create afterlives for players’ careers, whether or not they actually have died. i.e. how Warriors fans make sad edits about Jordan Poole as if he had to medically retire after getting punched by Draymond, LeBron fans worrying about his legacy while he’s still actively playing, fans labeling players as busts (and how the entire label of being a ‘bust’ is harmful), etc

I was wondering if you guys knew of any resources I should check out, people I should talk to, etc. It’s been pretty hard to find writing about these concepts, and it’s been even harder to find people who are willing to talk to me. Obviously, I get a lot of fans willing to chat, and I’m working with creators like CliqueProductions, but finding people who actually have personal connections to the league and have experienced these phenomena from an inside perspective has been pretty difficult. This post is mostly just an open call for discussion and for anyone who’s interested to reach out! I’m super excited to be working on this project, and I hope y’all can provide me with some insights and resources :) Thanks!!!
