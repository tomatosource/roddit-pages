A true Spellsword (mace) build. Smith of Kitava 0.4 auto attacker

Disclaimer: If you enjoy 1 button clears where you don’t even have to think about the fight, this build is not for you. I know many builds can clear better and are safer but I like to be engaged so I build characters like this.

Cheap - only uniques Im using are Choir of Storms which really is just for fun and 2 Mjolnir.

I wanted to make Elemental Conflux work with a melee build and with the buffs to Smith and combustion this was perfect. My final product came out really sweet and is a lot of fun to play. Its half physical half elemental damage (fire and lightning) as well as reflecting self ignites. Conflux keeps me rotating weapons to take advantage of the damage boosts.

Both damage types are buffed by Elemental Conflux, plus it looks so sweet. Ice is wasted more or less, but still 66% uptime on a large damage buff.

The fire side of the build comes from: Auto attack + Temper + Herald of Fire + Fire Spell on Hit (Detonate Dead, Snap, Firestorm). Snap picks up any remnants and buffs firestorm with extra fire and lightning damage.

The Lightning side of the build comes from Mana Tempest, Choir of Storms which rains down lightning on any crit and 2xMjolnir with added lightning damage. For the Mjolnir spells Im running lightning cascade, Thunderstorm, and Lightning Warp. I know warp is the strongest but that builds been done to death plus Thunderstorm is good for increasing shock chance. I try to always pop mana tempest when I swap weapons to get a few seconds of increased spell damage. 20% gem gives 2 sec even after you leave the storm.

Round out the build with the utility of Infernal Cry - Armor break - Boneshatter - Hammer of the Gods

Some times it’s a little slow to get started, but once procs start going off and lightning is cracking all over the map and its raining fire and lightning, damn is it fun.

I’m on console so can’t link a poeninnja but Im just running all the fire and mace damage nodes and basic defensive stuff. Damage is high, defenses are mid. You’ll see I almost die from a big lightning blast in the video.
