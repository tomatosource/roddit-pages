Mistery Solved: The use of the “DNT Incursion Gemcutter’s Prism (not visible to players)”

After some thinking, I used one of mine “DNT Incursion Gemcutter’s Prism (not visible to players)” over a lvl 20 spirit gem to understand the outcome of that use. The result is quite simple:

  1. The “DNT Incursion Gemcutter’s Prism (not visible to players)” gives u a flat 10% quality (instead of the flat 5% that provides the normal version). During this process the gem got oviously destroyed.

  2. After that, I took another “DNT Incursion Gemcutter’s Prism (not visible to players)” and applied it over a 20% and 5 sockets lvl 20 skill gem to see I could overcap the quality or something. Nothing happened.

Conclusion: It only provides a flat 10% quality.
