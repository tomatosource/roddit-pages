Bind Spectre + Blood Cretin and Courtesan

An interesting interaction: if you bind spectre a Courtesan from Ogham Manor and one of their blood cretin minions, the courtesan will cast a spell on the cretin to cause it to explode into a pool of corrupted blood (just like the real enemies do). The damage doesn’t seem anything special, but I think it’s cool that they maintain this interaction as spectres.

Not sure if having a minion that consistently kills your other minions is useful for anything lol, but it makes me wonder if there are other unique interactions like this.
