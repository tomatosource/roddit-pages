I was surprised not to see more interesting affixes added to charms

By adding the charm branch on the Ritualist, it wouldn’t have been strange for GGG to add some new affixes into the pool for charms, as non-unique charms have a very minimal affix pool. With only four prefix options and five suffix options, charms feel very lackluster compared to their analog, utility flasks, in POE1. In their current state, most players even forgo having them altogether, especially in pre-0.20, where the sheer utility of vanilla ingenuity far outweighed having two or three charms. Outside of having near-permanent uptime on the unique charms, the Ritualist charm branch seems so lacking because charms are lacking as items, with the most interesting option for non-unique charms being guard stacking. I don’t think it would be egregious to see the return of utility flask suffix mods such as:

#% additional Elemental Resistances during Effect
#% chance to Avoid being Stunned during Effect
#% chance to Freeze, Shock, and Ignite during Effect
#% increased Armour during Effect
#% increased Evasion Rating during Effect
#% increased Critical Strike Chance during Effect
#% increased Movement Speed during Effect
#% of Attack Damage Leeched as Life during Effect (would probably be changed to physical damage in consideration of PoE2 balance)
#% of Spell Damage Leeched as Energy Shield during Effect
#% reduced Effect of Curses on you during Effect
Immunity to Corrupted Blood during Effect

I think that adding some diversity to the mods on these items would make them a more interesting point of itemization and make the charm branch on Ritualist a better option overall. What do you think?
