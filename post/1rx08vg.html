My first high end body armour craft

Hey all, here is my first high end body armour craft. This season is great for crafting such stuff thanks to abundand currency. probably never see that again.

So here is what I did:

Buy 3 socket normal bases. They cost close to 400 div. I basically bricked 5 of them going for quality… so with the last one I decided to stop at 27% and maybe go for lock quality if everything fits….

So now with a normal 27% qual 3 socket lvl 82 base I perfect aug/anul until I had 3 desired T1 modifiers. Spammed divs until all 3 affixes were perfect roll. Desecrated without revealing, fractured and hit lightning res.

Suffixes first… anulled down to two mods and greater chaos spammed (had so many lying around…) until I hit second T1 res. Then dextral exalt. If I did not hit, anul and repeat. At some point I had all 3 T1 res. So suffixes done.

Then prefixes. Desecrate, reveal with abyssal echo to try and hit T1 life. Omen of light anul if not hit. Cost me about 10 Omens of light… brrrrrr 800 div down the drain. Finally hit T1 life. Yay!

Then perfect ex for adding two more prefixes. And start whittling. Suffixes are safe with iLvl 82, and with T1 life out of the way, you must end up with spirit and flat es. at some point. Spent maybe 20 Whittles. 1200 div down the drain. Then I hit T1 %ES and T1 Flat ES, so had to go for spirit. Removed life with omen of light and desecrated again. No T1 spirit. Remove, desecrate again: Yay! T1 spirit!

I then spent a considerable amount div rolling all stuff until i got a nearly perfect roll. Only flat ES is 2 short, so 94 instead of 96. I considered rerolling, but as the chance of hitting everything perfectly is probably about 1⁄7500 i settled with that.

Then, the lock. First hovered another vaal infuser, but it would have bricked. Then hovered the vaal orb, would have scrambled everything and ruined the craft. Then the last chance: activate omen of corruption and hover vaal. Yay! 4th socket. I was not sure whether 4th socket or a good sanctification would have been better, but I decided to go for the socket.

Only thing which bothers me is that ES is JUST below 1000. One more max ES or one more % qual would have done the trick… so if I find another mirror, I might go for it. Or not.

Oh, and as I write this, I realized that I probably wasted about 800 div with trying to hit T1 life. Any life would have blocked the life prefix. Darn. Well, learned something.

Spend maybe around a mirror or a bit more on this craft. Might have saved 1k div or more on the craft. But who cares… Current reasonable price would be about 2.5 mirrors. But I am not selling. I wear that beauty!
