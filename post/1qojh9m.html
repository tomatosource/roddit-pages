Derrick White - Shooting Woes

DWhite has been a fantastic player since arriving to Boston in 2022 - he’s been an All NBA defender, had huge moments in the playoffs, and been generally steady and reliable.

This year he is definitely struggling being a higher level option for the offense, and both his shooting and at times his once pristine decision making/ball security has suffered.

I think this is something that self corrects if/when JT returns to a large role, but its worth nothing that not only is he being asked to be a higher order option (2nd/3rd versus 4th/5th depending on who was in the lineup) he is also being asked to lead the point of attack.

This was decidedly not his role the first 3 years he played in Boston - in terms of initiating offense and being the primary ball handler, he was almost always behind:

Marcus Smart (gross)

Malcom Brogdon

Jrue Holiday

Jayson Tatum

and last season PP led the bench unit for sure.

So, his FG is way down, his shot selection mix probably has more isolation in it (haven’t checked stats) and more “grenade” shots he has to take this year. And he is now leading sets trying to make the first read that teams are keyed on taking away, even in the regular season.

All that to say a player is best when they are a star in their role, and dont try to be more than that. DWhite is a STAR (all caps) when his role is what it was from 2022-2025. This year he is not, and thats OK, not everyone in the NBA can be what he is being asked to be.

Looking forward to getting past the deadline and AS break so we can see what this team is made of when the games start to count more.
