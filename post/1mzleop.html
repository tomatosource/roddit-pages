Thoughts on the Boss Rush after Gamescom

yes Gamescom is wrapped up now, and I wanted to share some impressions from watching the Boss Rush event on site.

Seeing the Act 4 bosses in action was honestly one of the highlights for me. These fights aren’t just about dumping damage anymore. There were traps, shifting terrain, and interactive mechanics that could completely swing the outcome if players reacted the right way. It felt less like a static encounter and more like a real duel, where awareness and positioning mattered as much as raw stats.

but the difficulty was no joke either because a couple of mistakes and players were out. But that’s exactly what made it so fun to watch. The way people adapted mid-fight made the whole thing much more immersive, and it really looked like a test of skill rather than just button mashing.

For me, this is the kind of boss design that makes PoE2 exciting. Can’t wait to see how it feels to play through these fights myself when 0.3.0 goes live.
