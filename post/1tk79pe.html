The New York Knicks are the 13th team in NBA history to have at least a 9 game winning streak in a single postseason. 7 went on the win the championship.

  • 2017 Warriors: Swept all the way through until Game 4 of the Finals, then won the championship in 5

  • 1999 Spurs: Lost Game 2 of the first round (Best of 3), then swept up to Game 3 of the Finals vs the Knicks before finishing out the series in 5

  • 2001 Lakers: Swept through the conference (Best of 3), lost Game 1 to the Sixers in OT, then closed the series

  • 1989 Lakers: Swept the first round (Best of 3), second round, and Conference Finals just to get swept by the Pistons in the Finals

  • 2024 Celtics: Lost Game 2 of the first round, Lost Game 2 of the second round, then swept the Conference Finals, lost Game 4 of the Finals, and closed out in 5

  • 2017 Cavaliers: Swept the first two rounds, lost Game 3 of the ECF, then only won Game 4 of the Finals

  • 2016 Cavaliers: Swept the first two rounds, then lost 3 and 4 in Toronto. 3-1 comeback in the Finals to win the championship

  • 2012 Spurs: Swept the first and second round, then won first to of the WCF at home before OKC won 4 straight. Only team on this list to not make the Finals.

  • 2003 Nets: Traded 1,2,3 and 4 with the Bucks in the first round, before winning two straight and sweeping their way through the conference. Lost in 6 to the Spurs in the Finals

  • 2026 Knicks: TBD

  • 1996 Bulls: Swept first round (best of 3), dropped Game 3 of Semifinals to Knicks, then swept the ECF and won three straight in the Finals (3-2-3 format) before closing in 6

  • 1982 Lakers: Got a bye in the first round, swept the Semifinals, swept the WCF, before losing Game 2 in the Finals. Eventually won in 6 over Philly.
