Bucks interested in Hugo Gonzalez in possible Giannis trade

The Celtics offer is presumed to center around Jaylen Brown, with the Bucks said to be interested in Hugo Gonzalez.

The Bucks reportedly want a first-round pick higher than what the Celtics have at No. 27, leading to rumblings that Boston is looking at other trade possibilities.
