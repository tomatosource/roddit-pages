I’ve never gone from completely addicted to logging out so quickly. Bad vibes GGG.

This is a wild experience for me. This was by far the most fun I have had in PoE2 since release. I chose not to despair for past week as people reset Holten and also prepared giga temples and printed mirrors.

Instead, I was learning from what others were doing, getting excited about applying it to my own temple. Then the map crystal buff came. Rejoice. Running maps quickly to farm crystals was fun. Struggling to get my chain going, finally persevering, getting it up to 10-15 rooms. Wow this is coming together but jeez, its hard as hell.

So now, I start farming harder and am invested into further gearing my character as the temple content is greatly out-scaling the T15 maps which my build was already trivializing. I haven’t felt this motivation to min max a character in PoE2 to date. The agency behind building my temple, it getting harder, me having to invest my build, and the rewards starting to get better, seeing the community do the same and share layouts and strategies. It. Was. So. Fun.

Earlier this evening, I had been waiting for a sac chamber and corruption room so I could start adding some rarity with alchemy labs as my 30 room temple was only netting me 3⁄4 divs per run (FINALLY, after grinding all week long). It finally comes, I get excited, ended up placing 3 rooms and locking a longer chain after grinding for a bit. Feels so good.

Then, it was like delirium fog appeared as the streamers, forums and reddit started erupting from the patch notes. I could not believe my eyes. I log out. Log back in. First temple run, 10 exalts. Next run, I already have lost 4-5 rooms in my chain. Poof. Can’t keep up the chain, chain drops no loot. I am just staring at my screen blankly in disbelief.

Sorry for the wall of text but this was an absurd experience. I was using my winter break absolutely loving this game, grinding it day in day out. I could not stop thinking about it, could not wait to log in.

Now, I can’t imagine logging back in. Just crazy

GGG, poor decisions are one thing, but the vibes are just off with this one.
