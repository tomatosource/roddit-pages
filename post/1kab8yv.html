Hello!

I recently created my first self-made build for POE2, utilizing the Painter’s Gloves’ unique and Trinity skill, to become a triple elemental tactician. At first, the build was underwhelming, but after several iterations and ~2-3 divines, I managed to significantly improve the damage output, which now allows me to clear maps, including T15s, efficiently. Then I encountered a wall with boss damage. To address this, I added the Attrition skill. I made some tweaks, which improved my boss damage to comfortably handle T15 bosses with +4 difficulty in about 30 seconds to 1 minute and 30 seconds (for example, Balbala can be tricky due to high mobility).

I also faced issues with survivability due to my low eHP (~1.6k), so I worked on raising my total to around 3.4k HP and ES. I also experimented with the Eternal Youth node a bit, which helped recharge ES when taking big damage. Overall, the build clears T15 maps effectively, deals decently with bosses, and is extremely fun to play.

Now, here’s my request: since this is my first time creating a build, what improvements do you suggest, and why? In my opinion, the most significant opportunities for improvement lie in survivability and damage per second (DPS) against bosses.
