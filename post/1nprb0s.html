I’m playing the Huntress playstyle, but I’m not getting the benefits.

The Huntress, as indicated by things like her area of the tree and her intended weapon, is designed around a hit and run, frequent melee engagement and disengagement playstyle. I have actually ended up using exactly this playstyle, but I can’t make use of any of the passives or gems that they released with her to support it, because I’m using Boneshatter as my melee attack, and Wind Blast as my ranged attack (which is classified as melee). I’m even using a weapon swap for my ranged and melee abilities, but neither is classified as projectile so it doesn’t count. Meanwhile, I could be exclusively using a mace (not even a spear) and it would work if I were using something like Molten Blast and Forge Hammer…

I think it’s really silly that there are so many ranged “melee” abilities, but since they don’t actually shoot projectiles they’re not tagged with “projectile”, and thus can’t be used to trigger all the melee-to-projectile back and forth mechanics. Especially now that we have Brink support, it’s really effective (at least in the early game where I don’t need to delete the screen in a split second) to pepper a swarm of enemies with Wind Blast, and then engage with a Boneshatter and annihilate huge chunks of enemies.

Every ability that is a strike or a slam is also melee, and just about every “melee” ability that is NOT a strike or a slam is essentially just ranged. I think they need to do something like create a ranged tag, and make the melee-to-projectile back and forth mechanic reference the ranged tag, instead of the projectile tag. If not exactly this, then there still should be some accommodation for hit and run tactics to make use of the hit and run infrastructure, even if they’re not using an ability that is specifically a projectile attack.
