Kawhi Leonard is not getting the level of criticism other star players would get.

I feel like I’m taking a crazy pill reading through all of the posts about the Kawhi deal. Seems like most users are lashing out at other parties way more than Kawhi. I want to point out that if this was about Lebron/Steph/KD/Tatum/etc. the player would be catching wayyyy more flack.

Is it me or does anyone else feel like the criticism is focused on:

  1. Adam Silver (comments assuming he’ll take no action and his actions so far led us to this point)

  2. Steve Ballmer (he should be first)

  3. Other team owners (alleging that all owners do it)

  4. Uncle Dennis

  5. Kawhi

  6. Brunson

Imagine Embiid doing this. Imagine other stars doing the same. It’s like, since Kawhi’s a quiet dude, he’s actually getting a pass in terms of criticism.

I’ve seen some users argue that Kawhi needs to be banned a season but honestly he’s otherwise getting an unfair pass for his role in all of this. There’d be posts about legacies for other players.
