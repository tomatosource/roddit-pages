For giggles, let’s imagine what a masochist mode in POE2 would be like

My ideas include:

  • You cannot drink flasks while knocked down, sprinting, or attacking. Consuming flasks now has a 1 second drinking animation.
  • Desert biome maps make you thirstier, doubling flask charge consumption.
  • When you die, in addition to losing EXP, you lose all items in your bag. This can be mitigated by having an omen active in your inventory that prevents the loss of half the items at random.
  • When you’re poisoned, you must use a medicine consumable item to cure the poison. Otherwise your HP keeps draining until you die. Poison resistance passives are added accordingly, boosting your immunity.
  • When you use too many warcries in a short amount of time, you gain the debuff “Laryngitis” and you can’t use cries anymore for a duration. The duration can be reduced by using herbal consumables.
  • When you use Leap Slam to jump off a cliff edge to lower ground, you gain “Strained ankle” debuff, reducing your movement speed for a duration.

What would you add to this list? Use your imagination!
