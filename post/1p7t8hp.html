The Even Season Jaylen Hypothesis

Hypothesis: Jaylen Brown is better in seasons that end in even numbers

This hypothesis will use an advanced stat: Win Shares per 48 Minutes (WS/48). Is it a perfect stats? No. Does it support my agenda? Yes! WS/48 rewards helping teams win on a rate basis. League average of qualified players for WS/48 is ~0.100. 

  • 2018: Jaylen follows up a solid rookie season by becoming a starter and improves his advanced stats to near league averages at 21. Ends the season DPOY-10 but misses an All-Defense team. WS/48 0.100. Celtics make ECF.
  • 2019: Jaylen goes back to a bench role. His advanced stats regress. WS/48 0.076. Celtics get gentlemen swept in round two.  
  • 2020: Jaylen has his first big leap as a player to all star level but teammate Kemba gets the nod. WS/48 0.123. Jaylen definitively becomes the #2 guy in the playoffs. Celtics make ECF. 
  • 2021: Jaylen increases his volume and becomes an all star but many of his other stats plateau or regress. WS/48 0.115. JB gets injured in May and misses the playoffs. Celtics lose in round one.
  • 2022: Jaylen improves his all around game and maintains his volume. WS/48 0.124. Celtics go to the finals.
  • 2023: Jaylen’s offensive game is stretched to its (then) limits. He is rewarded with an All-NBA selection but his advanced numbers go down. WS/48 0.100. Jaylen cuts his hand before the playoffs and plays through it. Celtics lose in ECF. 
  • 2024: Jaylen’s most complete full season to date. Playmaking and efficiency took a leap while volume took a step back. WS/48 0.121. NBA writers don’t reward JB with an all-NBA selection so he wins fMVP instead 👑
  • 2025: Jaylen’s efficiency and playmaking take a step back. WS/48 0.115. JB works through a bone bruise on his leg in the playoffs. Celtics lose in round two. 
  • 2026: Putting the team on his back without JT. Highest volume of his career on good efficiency. WS/48 0.124

Two takeaways

  1. Jaylen Brown and the Celtics are better in even numbered seasons
  2. The media is constantly wrong about JB. It’s up to us to spread the good word about our king
