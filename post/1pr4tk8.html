Devon White is the only player in MLB history to be the batter for the last out of two different World Series in which he was a member of the winning team

With the Blue Jays Devo flew out to Pete Incaviglia two at-bats prior to Joe Carter’s walk-off home run that ended the 1993 series, and with the Marlins he grounded out to his former teammate on those Blue Jays Tony Fernández who proceeded to throw Bobby Bonilla out at home in the play before Edgar Rentería’s walk-off hit to end the 1997 series.
