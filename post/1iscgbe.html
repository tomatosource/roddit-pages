5 simple tips to improve early Trial of Sekhemas success rate

The Trial of Sekhemas are difficult in the beginning, sometimes it screws you over but most of the time you screw yourself. What follows are 5 things I think can help improve your success rate.

  1. The most important one, the affliction you can choose is always better than the one you can not. Avoid “random afflictions” and anything that prevents you from being able to see what coming afflictions are.

  2. The same is true for boons, the boons you can choose are better than a random one. So if the choice is between a random Boon or Balbala, pick Balbala.

  3. Plan ahead! You don’t really need the ability to see more rooms ahead (although it will make planning easier), what’s important is to keep your options open. Try to avoid rooms with only one exit arrow unless you know what the room after is.

  4. Your first Djinn Braya is the most important one! You can try as many times as you like without it being consumed. Use this to get a couple of helpful relics (I like %maximum honour and %honour resist) and find what trials suits your playstyle and build the best.

  5. You do not have to take room rewards. This is especially useful if the Pledge of Kochai gives you poop options or you are forced to take the maraketh shrine which restored a large amount of honour in exchange for a random affliction. This took me waaaay too long to realise and made things alot easier once I figured it out.

The trials do screw you over sometimes but following these four things I have found that I can reliably finish all my ascendancies solo with whatever build I am using and the Trials of Sekhemas have become one of my favorite area to farm.

Best of luck to you all!
