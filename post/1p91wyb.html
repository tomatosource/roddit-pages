Due to Several Injuries, the Grizzlies are Starting Vince Williams Jr at PG. Over His Last 5 Games, He is Averaging 11 Assists on Just 27 MPG

One of the most bizarre developments this season is Vince Williams randomly becoming a point god. He is our 5th string PG and normally plays a 2⁄3 hybrid, but apparently he figured out how to move like prime Chris Paul. He has the defense too, but still needs to work on his shot consistency. He was a tank year darling in the 2023-2024 season but struggled with injuries himself last year.

He tied Ja Morant’s team record with SEVENTEEN assists in Wednesday against the Pelicans.
