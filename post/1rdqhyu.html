MLB ballparks and the heads groundkeeper

Dan Kiermaier is the heads groundkeeper of Wrigley Field, and he happens to be the older brother of Kevin, which sent me down a google hole….

using the ballpark names listed in u/baseball-reference, here are the types by their names:

  • park: 14
  • field: 10
  • stadium: 5
  • yard: 1
  • centre: 1

two quick notes:

  • Great American Ball Park is the only actual “ball park”
  • Oriole Park at Camden Yards is technically a park and a yard

only nine stadiums are not corporately named:

  • Angel Stadium of Anaheim
  • Dodger Stadium
  • Fenway Park
  • Kauffman Stadium
  • Nationals Park
  • Oriole Park at Camden Yards
  • Steinbrenner Field
  • Yankee Stadium III
  • Wrigley Field (technically, it’s named after William Wrigley Jr who owned the wrigley gum company…. different than Busch Stadium because now Anheuser-Busch pays…)
