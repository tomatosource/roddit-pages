Thorns should be a suffix.

Thorns, the build that functions by taking hits and returning damage. A super niche but fun playstyle that gets better each patch.

Outside of the usual complaints about the restrictiveness of the required uniques, one of the biggest issues with thorns is requiring the mod on body armour, shield and belt.

Worse than that, the mod is a PREFIX.

On a body armour you have to sacrifice life, armour or spirit to fit this mod. On a shield you need to sacrifice life, armour or block to fit this mod And on a belt it’s not a huge loss as a prefix or suffix tbh.

However since you are playing a build specifically looking to tank hits, the restriction on your prefixes on body armour and shield make this an enormous opportunity cost.

Given that we are expected to tank hits using armour, life and block as a thorns build; taking thorns mods actually greatly reduces how tanky you can make your character. This coupled with having a couple quite weak defensively required uniques really hurts the thorns playstyle.

Thorns mods being changed to a suffix greatly improves this.
