In Cooper Flagg’s last 8 games, he is averaging 25/6/4 on 52% from the field and 83% from the line. The Mavs are 6-2 in this stretch.

This includes real quality wins over the Nuggets, Heat, and Rockets by double digits, and a win over the Pistons last night. The only “bad game” Coop had during that stretch was against OKC, which join the club.

The mavericks may end up being too good to tank. Which, by next year with a bounce back Kyrie, an AD who can be a 2nd/3rd option, an improved Cooper, and a supporting cast of PJ/Gaff/Naji/Klay/Christie, the Mavs might end up being decent.

The best news in all of this is Flagg looks really good. He’s getting to his spots, controlling the flow of the game, and passing well even if he isn’t a point guard, while shooting 7.5 free throws a game. The Kawhi/Pippen/Tatum comparisons seem to be holding, and he’s better than all of those guys at this age.
