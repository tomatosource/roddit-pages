Tatum, Brown, and good role players are enough to compete, especially in the east

I think there’s a misconception going around in our fanbase that we need some unstoppable super team to compete in the weaker eastern conference. People are quick to forget we made tons of deep runs before we traded for KP and Jrue. Those teams weren’t unstoppable superteams but rather Tatum, Brown, and good role players. Thats kind of what we have now but tatum and brown may just need to score more points than they have the last two seasons. I mean like if we added back a non-injured Tatum to this team right now, I’d still put them as a top 3 team in the east. We’re fine as long as we have the jays and we dont need some record-breaking team to win a title or compete.
