E3, E4, E6, E5: mets had a game where every infielder made an error

mendoza is gone.

one of his lasting legacies with this expensive mets team will be his penultimate game - second game of cubs double header.

in this game, the mets managed 6 errors including all around the infield. the mets still were only down two runs going into the 9th but ultimately lost.

so, this sent me to retrosheet and their play-by-play files. the PBP files only go back to like 1920’s.

how many times has a team committed errors by all infielders in a single game: 76 times.

  • in those 76 games, the E-team managed to win 20% of those games which is astounding - 15 out of 76 total games
  • the most recent of those games where the E-team won: 2017 cards @ nationals
  • one of those 15 victories was the yankees in 1940 where the yankees infielders all committed an error, but cleveland actually had more errors (including cleveland errors by all three outfielders)
  • the A’s have the most such E-games with 7.
  • six teams have never committed this heinous act, and those six are all expansion teams.
