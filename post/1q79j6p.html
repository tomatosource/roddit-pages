Game crashes driving me nuts… here is how to secure your inventory

Currently, the game crashes regularly thanks to server issues (GGG pls fix!).

This usually means: You run around, your char begins to stutter and so forth. Then you lose server connection. You WILL lose:

- The map you are currently running with Waystone and Tablet count. There will be no portals anymore after logging back in.

- The map will be counted as attempted. So you will have to put in another Waystone but you cannot put in tablets anymore.

- If the server has crashed while your char was in the map you will also lose all inventory you have picked up during mapping. (I found that out after I picked up a div and it was gone after relogging…)

What to do if you start to see realy high spikey lags:

- DON’T try to make a portal and go back to your hideout. You will not be able to take that portal. Save your time.

- Just press “Esc” and log off game. You can log in and you will be back in your hideout. You will still lose the map with Waystone and Tablet count. You will still have no portals anymore. The map will still be counted as attempted. BUT: You will have all items in your inventory you have picked up!
