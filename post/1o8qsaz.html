Mariners just became the 3rd ALCS team to start 2–0 on the road and 0–2 at home — the other two won it all

After starting the ALCS with two road wins in Toronto, the Mariners have now dropped the next two games at home, evening the series 2–2. That specific sequence — 2–0 on the road followed by 0–2 at home — has only happened twice before in ALCS history (since it became a best-of-seven in 1985).

In both previous cases, the team that began 2–0 on the road went on to win the pennant and the World Series:

1993 Blue Jays: Won the first two in Chicago, lost the next two at home, then won Games 5 and 6 to take the ALCS and later repeated as World Series champions.

•	G1: TOR 7–3 at CHI

•	G2: TOR 3–1 at CHI

•	G3: CWS 6–1 at TOR

•	G4: CWS 7–4 at TOR

•	G5: TOR 5–3 at TOR

•	G6: TOR 6–3 at CHI

2023 Rangers: Won the first two in Houston, lost the next three at home, then won Games 6 and 7 in Houston to clinch the ALCS — and went on to capture their first World Series title.

•	G1: TEX 2–0 at HOU

•	G2: TEX 5–4 at HOU

•	G3: HOU 8–5 at TEX

•	G4: HOU 10–3 at TEX

•	G5: HOU 5–4 at TEX

•	G6: TEX 9–2 at HOU

•	G7: TEX 11–4 at HOU

So the Mariners are now the third team to follow this exact path. The first two ended up winning it all.
