One portal maps are bad game design

This whole concept of poe2 being filled with so many scenarios where deaths are extremely punishing - while also having an endless amount of unfair feeling deaths is extremely lame.

Dying in the new temples or a abyssal depths to a bs random one shot and just losing that whole mechanic is so frustrating

Imagine we also get tower removal but are forced to run 1 portal maps just to use all 3 of our tablets..

A big issue with one portal gameplay as well, is that many players will barely get to enjoy endgame mapping because they are constantly on high alert making sure to not die to the countless amounts of unfair one shots that this game is full of since release, or end up playing on builds that they truly don’t want to play but gravitate towards making a build that feels worse to play but is a lot more tanky..

Some players actually enjoy this, and I understand that. For people that enjoy this level of gameplay they should have maps that accommodate that urge, a great example of how we could solve that is have content like one portal valdo maps, to make the potential reward worth the perfect gameplay.

Just give us 6 portals back.. losing experience is punishing enough, especially on this game where leveling is as slow as it is.
