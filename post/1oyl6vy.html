An oddity about MVP, Cy Young, and the WS

This is just something I’ve been following for a while because I noticed at some point that the only Cy winner who had won a ring the same year since Maddux in 95 was Unit in 2001, and obviously only barely. Since then, Verlander in 2022, and that’s it.

Whereas MVP has happened in 06 (Pujols), 12 (Posey), 16 (Bryant), 17 (it’s fine now that Judge has 3), 18 (Betts), and Ohtani the last two years.

Obviously they are likelier to give a Cy to a pitcher on a bad team so that’s ‘why.’ I just found it interesting that it’s so relatively lopsided.

It’s actually been likekier for a team to win a WS featuring the previous year’s Cy (Unit himself, Carpenter, Lincecum, Arrieta).

It’s mostly random (Bonds almost won by his damn self in 02, Bryant and Altuve and Ohtani this year barely won, Verlander barely lost in 19, etc). But I do think it’s funny to look at.
