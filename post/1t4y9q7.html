A realistic take on the season

Its interesting to me to see the entire narrative in the media and on this board of this loss being some inflection point that we need to break up tatum/brown and blow up the team or get rid of Joe mazulla etc. Its as if everyone is acting like the celtics are some juggernaut that got upset.

But if you take a step back. Brad traded 5 of our top 7 rotation players to get under the 2nd apron, we lost Jrue/KP/horford/kornet. Put together a disjointed team with no centers other than queta who was sitting on the bench last year and garza. Also made zero effort to improve the team, even trading anfernee simons for a stiff in vucevic to save more money. and Tatum was recovering from an achilles. this team was not built by Brad to compete.

Then by a miracle the east has a very weak year. young guys develop and we end up as the #2 seed. Tatum miraculously comes back and looks about 80%. but it doesnt change that this was a very disjointed roster and Tatum is not near 100%. the fact that Boston shut tatum down for game 7 after his other leg was getting calf/knee soreness shows they honestly didn’t view this season as a true competitive one. do you think if they felt we could win a chip he would be shut down? they basically said “this experiment is over, lets see how game 7 plays out”

losing to the sixers was a big disappointment. but the reality is, White playing like garbage and the celtics having no reliable #3 scorer or an nba calibar big rotation to support tatum/brown is what doomed them. Embiid destroyed our center rotation where in the past horford tire him out. and Tatum/Brown vs. maxey/george/embiid was just too much.

I highly doubt Brad is looking at this season the same way this board or the media is. its a run that wasn’t supposed to happen. what we can take from it is queta/baylor/walsh/RJ can be roleplayers and that Tatum looks like he will return to 100% back to who he was prior with a full offseason. and Brown has improved as a player. we need to upgrade our bigs rotation. Also hopefully white can return to form as a solid #3 guy.
