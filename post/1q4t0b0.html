General POE2 Campaign Feedback

I have played through the campaign six times now. I share many of the same thoughts as others where some zones still feel way too long and maze-like. But I wanted to list a few ideas to make it more fun.

It seems like the vision is a campaign skip isn’t going to happen, and playing through the campaign multiple times is a big part of playing POE2.

Support gems are in a great spot but skill gems need some love. We need many more skill gem drops or a way to buy them from a vendor. Also the threshold to unlock a new tier of skills should be much lower. By gem level 10 I should have access to all skill gems in the game. Unlocking my final core ability in interludes feels bad if GGG want us to enjoy the campaign. Let us use the campaign as a way to test out builds and see what works.

Lastly, we need more crafting tools through the campaign such as gnawed bones and lower level essences. Maybe we can start unlocking campaign atlas points throughout campaign to get higher odds of some mechanics.

The atlas isn’t inherently more fun than the campaign, its the fact that we have access to the full game at that point and most things are locked or extremely rare to find throughout campaign.

As it is now, I can’t help but feel like the campaign is a waste of time. Because I am rushing to endgame to have access to all my skills and many more crafting resources to craft my items.
