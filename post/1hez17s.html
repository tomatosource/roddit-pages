The Ringer updated their top 100 NBA players list a few days ago. Joel Embiid has dropped to 22nd.

The Ringer’s Top 100 players in the NBA list was updated 12⁄11 after last being updated 11⁄20. Everyone already knows about Embiid’s fall from grace, but going from 4th best in the NBA last season to 22nd is pretty jarring.

Outside of Embiid, some other notable moves were:

  • Lebron James: 11 -&gt; 16
  • Karl-Anthony Towns: 28 -&gt; 19
  • Jalen Williams: 29 -&gt; 21
  • Franz Wagner: 48 -&gt; 24
  • Kawhi Leonard: 23 -&gt; 29
  • Bam Adebayo: 22 -&gt; 31
  • James Harden: 40 -&gt; 32
  • Tyrese Haliburton: 18 -&gt; 33
  • Zion Williamson: 36 -&gt; 49
  • Giannis remains at 3 while Luka remains at 2
