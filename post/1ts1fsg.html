The Red Sox have a unique and baffling issue with pitcher Brayan Bello

Bello has been seen as a player with great potential over the past few years, and last year he had a promising season overall (29 games/28 starts, 166.2 IP, 3.35 ERA, 1.236 WHIP). He figured to be a good middle-to-end of the rotation starter with the potential to make a leap and be a solid starter for years to come.

Instead, this year has gotten off to a disastrous start. He’s sporting a 5.65 ERA, 1.643 WHIP, and often looking lost on the mound. The Sox decided on a solution: pitch him in the 2nd inning. They put a bullpen “starter” out for the 1st as an opener, then put Bello in.

This has worked incredibly well for Bello. Per Tyler Miliken, in 4 2nd inning “relief appearances”/late starts, he has a 0.71 ERA in 25.1 innings and great numbers by pretty much every metric (7.1 K/9, .215 BAA, 0 HRs).

Problem solved? Nope!

Because in those 4 games, the openers have given up a combined 9 runs. The Sox are basically spotting the opposing team 2+ runs before their actual starter even takes the mound!

A while ago they tried starting Bello again, but he only lasted 5 innings and gave up 7 runs on 8 hits (including 2 HR) and 3 walks with only 1 K. So, they went back to the opener situation, where in 2 “relief appearances” he went 6.1 and 7 innings with only 1 ER in each game.

Some people have pointed to his pitch mix as a possible culprit; specifically, he uses his cutter way more as a start than as a “reliever.” Some people attribute his better numbers to not having to see the top of the lineup the first time, which certainly helps. Others think it’s a mental issue that he needs to resolve, or maybe a different way of preparation that ends up benefitting him on 2nd inning “starts.”

But overall, the Sox are in a weird bind. Either they have to find a reliever who can reliably “start,” which they have not yet found, or they have to hope that Bello figures things out. If they get “reliever Bello” as a starter he could potentially be a huge weapon. But right now, they’re getting an absolutely awful first followed by 7 innings of ‘00 Pedro incarnate (which, TBF, the first inning was often Pedro’s problem as well). You can’t win games by giving your opponent early runs, and whether Bello is a starter or a “reliever,” that’s what’s happening.
