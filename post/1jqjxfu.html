Blood Mage Waited This Long For This?

I’m sorry but this makes me irrationally mad in several ways.

I’m still at a loss as to how GGG saw the ascendancy, deemed it finished and be rolled out as a first wave of available to be played.

The fact that you need four points in total to use at once in order to “break even” while also introducing a second resource to juggle with no upside should be an immediate red flag not only for how the ascendancy penalizes the player but also how it essentially puts half the choices on rail.

It’s only with the sixth point when you actually gain something and it’s a mishmash of choices where only one is within the context of blood magic. Six points to essentially feel like other ascendancies do with two.

We’re told that big changes can only happen during a reset event (because this is a live game, apparently) so we patiently wait months in hopes that GGG sees this glaring issue with Blood Mage and why it has, like, 5% representation.

So the big reset gets here and we get… a tweaked passive for aggravated bleeding and a new physical spell buff…

But surely they made changes to how the Blood Mage sacrifices her life force for a huge benefit in return? You know, the fantasy of blood magic?

Nope.

But surely they condensed the early ascendancy points to be less mandatory and provide additional choices?

Nope.

But surely they…

Nope.

They did the bare minimum which could have been done at any point over the last several months and instead of doing so they decided to scoop it to throw in to the reset patch because it was simply too big to do any other time since it would have really made players upset.

So I guess in three months we’ll have to wait for them to buff blood orbs because blood orbs means blood magic which is a theme they totally understand.

By live, we might even see the Blood Magic keystone interact uniquely with a Blood Mage other than just double the cost of life to cast.
