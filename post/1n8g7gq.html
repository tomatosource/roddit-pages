[OC] I looked up Kawhi’s business filings — there are a ton of “KL2” LLCs in California 👀

After seeing the recent news about Kawhi’s Aspiration deal, I got curious and searched the California Secretary of State site. Found KL2 Aspire LLC (where Kawhi is listed as manager), plus a whole cluster of other KL2 entities with the same agent.

I checked some of the filings, and they list Kawhi and his uncle Dennis Robertson. So at least a few are definitely theirs.

Crazy thought, could each of these LLCs be a potential “no-show” job like the Aspiration deal?😱

Source: https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business
