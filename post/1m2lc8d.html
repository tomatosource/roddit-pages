Bobby Marks explains how Damian Lillard’s stretched salary on the Bucks cap sheet now drops from \(22.5m over 5 years  \)20.2m over 5 years, given his new $42m/3 year contract signed with Portland.

https://preview.redd.it/qjl9wzbgfidf1.png?width=960&format=png&auto=webp&s=8172e779733acaa05f6a43a547b7c37f2fcb6977

\(113m/2 years was waived and stretched over 5 years  \)22.5m over 5 years.

formula for calculating set-off in the CBA is (new salary - 1 year of service vet. min deal) x 50%

so Dame’s set-off formula is (\(14.1m - 2m) x 50% = \)6.05m for the 1st year; $5.6m for the 2nd year

set-off only calculated for 2 years because that’s the remaining length of his original contract that got waived and stretched.

so \(6.05m + \)5.6m = \(11.65m. \)11.65m/5 years  $2.3m

so new stretched salary is \(22.5m - \)2.3m  $20.2m over 5 years.
