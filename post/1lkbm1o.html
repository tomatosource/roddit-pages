Do you think this is an accurate assessment of the dynamics surrounding a potential Jaylen Brown trade?

I’m seeing a lot of people posting trades involving Jaylen Brown and a draft pick that could net Boston Jakucionis, but they all seem to be missing a couple of facts:

  1. Boston will try to hang on to Brown as much as possible. Brown and White are far and away the most core players on that team, and any interested teams are going to have to overpay to get them.

  2. Boston is targeting Jakucionis, who is projected to draft around #11 to #14. They have no need for a Top 10 pick, and you’re not going to convince them to give up Brown or White just to secure, say, #9 over #14.

  3. Boston has the assets to trade directly for some of the Jakucionis picks. Their roster includes players like Payton Pritchard, Sam Hauser, and George Niang. They can absolutely package these with picks to make a straight play for #9-#14.

  4. Brown is effectively 95% of the potential of most of these picks realized in the form of a 4x All-Star and recent FMVP. He has championship experience and a proven track record of being willing to give up the ball. In order to convince Boston to give him up, you’re going to have to manage the risk associated with draftees developing into a Brown-level player. This means that you’re not going to get Brown if the package is effectively a pick + trade assets. It will have to be either a pick + a proven performer or a pick + a promising young talent, so that the development threshold that the younger player would need to cross to make the trade worthwhile is lowered. Think Castle + #14 over Vassell + #2.

(From my thread here).

Thoughts on these points? Do you agree or disagree with any of this?
