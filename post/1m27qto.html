Has there ever been a 3-player alley-oop?

A lot of alley-oops involve a two players. You have the player that throws it in the air then the player that dunks it. Then you have the single-player alley-oop where a player throws it off the backboard and dunks it. I’m not sure if that’s even considered an alley-oop but you get the idea. It’s a player catching a ball mid-air and dunking it.

What about an alley-oop that involves 3 players? So it could go something like this; Player A throws ball in mid-air -&gt; Player B tips it to player C -&gt; Player C dunks it mid-air. Or another instance: Player A throws it off the backboard -&gt; Player B instead of dunking it decides to throw it off the backboard -&gt; Player C finally dunks it.

Has this ever happened in the NBA?
