Who are Two Players Whose Stats Compare in Surprising Ways?

If you go into BRef and look at career/season stat lines, sometimes you can find facts that really belie players reputations or performance in a season. Most of them are just statistical quirks, but still cool. For example:

  • Barry Larkin has a higher career OPS+ than Derek Jeter.
  • When Andruw Jones hit 51 homers, he had a SLG of .575. When Wade Boggs hit 24 homers, he had a SLG of .588.
  • In Pedro Martinez’s legendary 2000 campaign, he allowed 17 homers, but had a 1.74 ERA. In the same year, Andy Pettitte allowed 17 homers, but had a 4.35 ERA.

All of these things are explainable. Jeter stuck around a little too long. Boggs had a super high batting average pumping up his slug. Pedro’s homers were mostly solo home runs. But it’s still cool to see the comparisons, I think.
