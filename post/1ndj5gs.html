Current Pathfiner problems as Poison enthusiast

Before we start i would just love to say this is just my opinion from playing pathfinder since 0.1 so im hoping people will comment here with your thoughts about why i would be wrong

First of all pathfinder poison spread is a horrible ascendancy and let me explain why. The base radius of poison spread is 1.5 m while herald of plague provides 1.8m and applies hinder on enemies when it spreads poison to them. People will say “but it costs spirit” you can get additional spirit on your amulet and body armor if your body armor is either hybrid es or pure es while you CANT have ascendancy points on your gear so its really horrible. Im not saying herald of plague needs to be nerfed because if they do that will kill every poison build that is not pf, but what i mean is that poison spread on pf needs to be buffed or reworked for that point to be worth it.

Second is that her “double poison stacks” make it so that you would go more into poison stacks instead of big initial impact which reduces your clear speed which is okay if you lack single target damage because it is a really good boost to it but its kinda bad when it comes to clear, also double poison stacks grief your acidic concotion because how acidic concotion works is that it consumes the poison to deal that poison damage per second x duration of it and that double poison makes it so that you get 35% LESS poison duration which if you know how the game works there is a really big impact of statistic when its reduced vs less

acidic concotion is currently lets say “bugged” because when you consume the poison on the enemy you cant reapply poisons on that enemy for around ~1s which is probably just the problem of how this skill is coded but it reduces your boss dps if you dont one shot them with it

Running assault felt okay to use in 0.1 and 0.2 because there were not that many sources of reduced penalty of using skills but in 0.3 we got both support gems and points on the tree that provide it making that ascendancy kinda obsolete

50% of evasion rating as additional elemental damage reduction is okay but then again you need to use 4 points (2 trials) to get to it and you cant really stack evasion rn beause of the new formula that makes it really hard to reach 80~evasion and deflection is an absolute joke because its impossible to cap it without spending 80% of your tree on it

i feel like the only good points on pf tree rn are “6 points” to spend on your tree and slow immune because of how much temporal chains and chilled ground you are getting on your maps thanks to ggg making every mod on maps debuff and because we dont have a big pool of negative mods on maps you will be getting either of them every second/third map and you know its getting bad when literally the best ascendancy point on your character is “6 points” to spend
