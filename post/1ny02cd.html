PSA: Dying in Boss Arena’s in any maps resets everything on respawn at checkpoint, including any “extra content”, map Fog of War, even Map orientation.

Maybe someone already posted this but just in case - Basically title (btw, this is on a map with multiple respawns).

Happened on various maps for me and specifically ONLY when you activate the boss of the map.

Originally I noticed it was resetting the map so wiped ground effects across the whole map. After a few more deaths, I picked up on the lighting in the map changing, the map Fog of War resetting, the orientation of the map changing and even the “Extra content” icons on the right UI and the content itself disappeared.

The map remained intact if I died anywhere else except the boss arena.

So, just a fair warning. Do all your content first, then kill the boss.

I wouldn’t be surprised if all the mods affecting the boss were also wiped too. I tend to die the first time and kill much faster on the reset.
