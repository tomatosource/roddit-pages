The Cleveland Cavaliers are on the verge of breaking the 5 year “Curse of Miami”

Since the 2019⁄2020 season, the team that has eliminated the Miami Heat from the playoffs has either won the championship or made the finals.

With Cleveland down 3-1 against Indiana in the Eastern Conference Semis, one is left with the question of whether the Curse of Miami will come through in Cleveland’s favor, or will they be the first team to break the curse?

Jokes aside though, it’s a pretty fitting curse to show just how close, yet frustratingly far, the Heat were in the Jimmy Butler era.
