Tactician Changes: thoughts?

Hello everyone,
I was going through the 0.3 patch notes and noticed some tweaks for the Tactician ascendancy.
Here’s what stood out to me:

-Cannons, Ready! (Notable):
Now lets you summon one extra totem.

-Right Where We Want Them (Notable):
Provides a bonus to projectile damage.

-Polish That Gear (Notable):
Grants Deflection Rating and updates the stun mechanics.

-Supporting Fire / Unleash Hell! (Ascendancy ability):
Minions now scale AoE with Dexterity instead of Accuracy and Death from Above now deals increased damage.

-The Whoever Pays Best (Notable):
Banners are now unlimited and generate Glory over time.

-Strategic Embankments (New Notable)
Your totems grant small Auras depending on their type.

For those who’ve played Tactician or built around totems and ballista do these changes feel like they add real value?

Have a nice day
