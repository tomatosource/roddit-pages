Home-Only Player: A Hypothetical

Here’s an interesting player thought experiment. Consider an elite left-haded 5-tool player, who consistency puts up 162-game-paces of 40 HR, .300 average, 25 SB, and plays above average center field. The kicker: due to a chronic lung condition, he is unable to fly on airplanes. Also, he gets debilitating motion sickness in buses or cars, a sickness that knocks him out for days on end.

As a result, this elite player is only capable of playing home games. He lives a couple blocks from the stadium and always walks to work.

With a maximum of 81 regular season games per year (and potentially less due to injury), how much would you be willing to pay this player?
