Tools you should be aware of - Craft of Exile

After replying to one of the many crafting posts from newer crafters I figured maybe it’s worth a post of it’s own. If you want to learn how to craft you should be using this amazing site.

https://www.craftofexile.com/?game=poe2

You can import real items direct from your game into it and then simulate crafting or create an item from scratch or with particular mods, it gives you a rough count of how much you are spending while doing so, will open your eyes to exactly what items can and can’t be used on an item depending on factors like rarity and open affixes and types. Messing about on this site will teach you a lot more than any of the shortcuts people are trying to get from people explaining the process in reddit.

Crafting isn’t just about making items you can sell for 20 div profit, it also helps you as you play the game to make minor upgrades to your own gear.

As a starting point I would suggest people learn to take one magic item with 2 good rolls and figure out how to turn that into something usable or sellable. Mess around with what essences you can use while it’s still at magic rarity, When the item is rare check back in the essences section to see what you can then use. You’ll start to understand a lot more about what those items do.

Exalt slam some items in there, try some perfect exalts. Now look at the omens to see how you can control if the slam will use a prefix or suffix, same with annul orbs. Same with desecrating the item.

A simple formula for how to craft x is what people want but there’s really not much point in it if people dont understand the process itself, what and why certain things are being done and what to do once the craft goes bad. The answer to that might be a costly fix or it might be close eyes slam, vendor item and start on a new base. There’s just too many different outcomes for everything to be explained in the succinct way people want.

By all means I think crafting talk is good on here, but there’s a huge amount of tools out there and this one in particular is used by basically everyone that crafts.

The emulator section is where you can simulate crafting
Simulator takes some getting used to but is a valuable tool for figuring out the expected chances and cost of something.
Calculator is mainly just information only but you can get an idea of all of the mods available for a given item and how they are weighted.
