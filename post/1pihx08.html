Early leveling with elemental spells (for Act 2)

Has anyone tried leveling with elemental spells? Here is my experience and it is not very amazing, it’s more about suffering than anything else.

At the start I tried to take Ice Nova - It helps a lot in dealing with the surroundings, providing crowd control. Then, when it becomes possible, I’d take Voltaic Staff with Lightning Bolt. If you can find it with +2 lightning spells, increased spell damage - then this is an excellent weapon almost until the wolf. If you will reach level 14 and take a staff with the current ability level, then the wolf can be killed without any problems.

But in the second act troubles begin. Ice Nova stops slowing enemies because it doesn’t break the ailment threshold.

Infusions don’t really work. You have to dance ten seconds in front of a crowd of mobs to gain a single infusion and then spend it to boost some skill once.

I tried Fireball + Living Bomb (to get infusion). Fireball doesn’t get any damage boost from infusion at all, it just throws small projectiles in a circle.

And I also tried Ark + Orb of Storm (supported by Overabundance + Less Duration to get infusions faster). Overall, it does decent damage occasionally when Ark rolls with a good damage. But it’s still bad, it’s just 2 empowered Arc cast in several seconds.

By the end of act 2 I dropped this amazing staff, it’s a very good for its level. I tried to kill Jamanra with the Sun Orb (Overabundance + Considered Casting supports), Fireball and Elemental weakness - аnd overall, you can kill the boss with this combination. But for a good leveling staff, the damage is still very low.
