On Liam McNeeley’s play in Las Vegas: “He showed why he was a first-round pick. Liam’s college year got messed up by a bad ankle injury. He made no excuses, but it wasn’t right for a lot of his rookie year either. This kid is a big-time shooter. There’s no shot he can’t make: off the dribble, on the move, spot-up, going to his right or left. He’s just a pure shooter and scorer. Big leap coming for Liam in Year 2,” a Hornets assistant coach said.

On the big man rotation: “Last year we went into the season knowing Moussa (Diabate) was better than anyone else knew. We hoped Ryan Kalkbrenner would give us something. We hoped Grant Williams would come back and give us a small-ball option at the five. All three happened,” a Hornets front office executive said. “Now, we add Naz Reid and Hannes Steinbach to that mix? That’s real depth and they’re all different kinds of players. That’ll give (head coach) Charles (Lee) a lot to work with this season.”
