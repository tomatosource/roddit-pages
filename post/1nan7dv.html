Suggestion for Azmeri Whisps

Azmeri Wisps attach to the player when approached, granting a temporary buff (for example, a speed boost similar to shrine effects) while also enchanting any white monsters the player passes by.

To maintain balance, this effect will last only for a limited duration (exact timing to be determined by the developers).

Phase 2 – Upon reaching a rare monster, the Azmeri Wisps detach from the player and instead possess the rare, empowering it with additional buffs.

This will remove the clunkiness and slowness of the mechanic and provide us with dopamine every time we see one because of the buff and how it will help us clear faster until next rare.
