Potential tower fixes

In the interview last night one of the things that was brought up about towers and why they feel bad is that you can see what the map has ahead of time as far as major mechanics go.

For instance if you have a map on your atlas that is corrupted, radiated, breached, and 30% increased experience, but you have to do three unjuiced maps in order to get there… all three of those maps are going to feel like tedious busy work.

I propose three possible solutions to this:

  1. If the main complaint is having to run map TYPES that you despise ( looking at you, Vaal Factory), implement the Orb of Horizons from Poe 1. In Poe 2 you could use it on the Atlas map and it would just reroll the type of map that it is, guaranteeing a different outcome. If this was going to be the “solution” to the tower problem, you would probably need to make them pretty common. Like finding one every couple maps at least… but I don’t think that’s major problem here so that leads me to..

  2. Instead of a tablet guaranteeing that certain maps will have a league mechanic (ie 14 maps in radius have breach encounters), I propose it would read something like “the next 14 maps you do in Tower radius will have a Breach encounter”. This means that you can “choose”which maps you are running with your preferred mechanic.

Another option that I don’t like as much would be something along the lines of “all maps in tower radius have a 20% chance to spawn a breach”, scaling up as you have more tablets. The reason I don’t like this very much is let’s say that it capped out at 60% or even like 80%, you would be heavily in incentivize to run EVERY SINGLE MAP in a towers radius which… ew. Especially if the you get unlucky and the last 10 maps within the tower radius that you do happen to not spawn but one breach or ritual or whatever… No thank you. Antidopamine. I don’t want every single tower to be a 60+ map commitment. But I think this COULD work if….

  1. Tablets instead were along the lines of “the next 14 maps you enter in tower radius have a 60% chance to contain a breach”. NOW THAT I could get behind. That way every map would feel potentially rewarding. You have a good chance (scaling up with number of tablet both in the percentage chance and the duration of the buff) to get the thing that you want on any map that you run for a duration. This would enable your grinding to have a natural end. That way you’re not completing every map in a radius even the ones that you don’t care about, and once it runs out, it’s time to move on. You could even make it to where the last map that has the buff is GUARANTEED to have the league mechanic, or even a league boss.

What do you guys think of my approach to “solving” towers? I haven’t put TOO much time into these “solutions” so they can probably be ironed out a lot more, but I think I did a good job explaining why I think they could work in principle.
