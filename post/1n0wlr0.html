For my Dreamer friends (Acolyte of Chayula)

Remember that your gain as extra mods will not scale with elemental damage!

Scaling occurs in five stages, always in this order:

  • Base (+levels, attack and cast speed, extra projectiles, add damage to attacks)

  • Conversion (% converted to cold, % gained as extra chaos)

  • Increased (% increased physical)

  • More (% more damage, % less skill effect duration)

  • Multi (% additional crit bonus)

The two camps of Acolyte players seem to be either Blackflame Covenant or Hollow Palm. (Claim your membership badges on your way out!)

Blackflame gets 100% conversion, so they can scale chaos directly, and the Increased and More steps will apply to all of their damage. This is usually how spells work anyway, but for these purposes, they effectively get an Original Sin for free. Remember, however, that any infusions or DoTs won’t benefit from Volatility or Purple Flames.

Hollow Palm folks will need to focus on either the first step or the last. If you use elemental skills, like Ice Strike, 80% of your physical damage is converted to cold. Let’s say 70% of your damage is gained as extra chaos, and your base skill does 100 damage. Thus, 100 (base) -&gt; 20 (phys) + 80 (cold) + 70 (chaos).

Increasing cold by 20% would give you 16 extra damage, increasing chaos by 20% would give you 14 extra damage, etc. However, increasing your attack speed by 20% would increase the base, giving you 34 extra damage (4 phys, 16 cold, 14 chaos).

Because crit is after all of these conversions and scalings, it also benefits the whole of your damage. Every successful crit is a 50% more calc, or more with crit bonus modifier. This last step, or anything from the first step, will affect Acolyte more than % increased cold damage, etc.

Even though wind skills are bad, I personally feel that they have a potential leg up due to ignoring this loss in converted damage. (Scaling cold affects 80% of Ice Strike, scaling physical affects 100% of Wind Blast). They also scale with increased projectiles, which is in the base category. Unfortunately… they also scale worse with attack speed than elemental skills do. The passive tree will decide everyone’s fate on Friday.

Anyway! Remember that Chonk’s main source of damage, gain as extra, benefits from either 100% conversion, or base modifiers and crit modifers. Scale Hollow Palm with gem levels, attack speed, and crit chance as indicated, and wait to scale chaos until you can consistently get at least 80% gained as extra.
