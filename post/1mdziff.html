Pathfinder is My Favorite Beginner Friendly Ascendancy

This is just a love letter for the pathfinder ascendancy.

Overall:

Pathfinder is easy to build around and allows for a ton of flexibility with its wide range of defensive options. While there isnt a whole lot of damage related nodes on the ascendancy tree, I think it more than makes up for it with its immunity and damage reduction options. Allowing for more passives on the skill tree to be allocated for damage scaling.

Defenses:

  1. Can grant immunity to any slows, ignite, chill, freeze, and shock
  2. Grants 30% elemental damage reduction. Which when paired with max ele res is HUGE.
  3. 6 extra passive points for more tree flexibility.

Offenses:

  1. Pretty nice poison nodes
  2. More passive points
  3. Nothing too crazy

I HATE CRIT

Yep, I said it. I hate crit.

Its ridiculously expensive to get on your gear, takes up half your passive points, and is annoying to scale. So I just avoid it entirely.

Playing Pathfinder allowed me to scale my defensive and offensive layers REALLY nicely for my explosive gas arrow build. I can take on all pinnacle content for cheap and it feels great.

Thats all.
