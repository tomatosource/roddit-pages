How to change the pinned icon on your taskbar for PoE2.

If you play both PoE and PoE2, and you pin both games to your taskbar, they utilize the same exact icon for both. This makes it really annoying when trying to launch, as I am constantly launching the wrong one.

If you want to change the PoE2 icon, you can follow these steps:

Download the ico file from this URL: https://cdn2.steamgriddb.com/icon/87f939f0f0c74a8481743d6d5ddefb0e.ico

I just saved this in my PoE2 installation directory - Program Files (x86)\Grinding Gear Games\Path of Exile 2

Right click your pinned PoE2 icon on your taskbar, then right click “Path of Exile 2”, then click Properties.

Click the Shortcut tab, then click the Change Icon button. Click the Browse button on the Change Icon screen and navigate to the directory where you saved the ico file in the step above. Click Open, Ok, Ok.

The icon won’t change until you restart your computer or restart Explorer (which can be done through Task Manager if you know how).
