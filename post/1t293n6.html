Great Season

I’ve been reading the sub for many years and what i’ve read since our season ending is definitely a bit disappointing from our fans.

Guys, we had to make trades last year to mitigate the tax and we lost four rotation players (Zingus, Holiday, Al and Kornet) in the process. We lost Tatum for 75% of the season. And he came BACK! The game he returned was my championship full stop.

And do I wish we played better and advanced? Absolutely - but this rendition of the team is flawed and flawed for a reason. Shedding tens of millions doesn’t come without consequences.

We still have the Jays, White, PP, and so many players who got incredible experience this year. And we will absolutely make changes this offseason to address the center rotation.

We blew a 3-1 lead, yes, but sometimes, shit happens. The ball didn’t go in the hoop as often as we would’ve all liked but would you have wanted us to advance and put even more mileage on the Jays just to face the same reality that we aren’t there yet?

Good series for Philly. They are so talented, wanted it more, and are simply more athletic than we are.

I trust Joe implicitly and don’t want there to be any significant changes but with the way the cap works now, there are bound to be great buying opportunities and I trust Brad to make the right decisions to capitalize.

This was a super fun season. The offseason will get us all excited for next year and we will all be celebrating the chance to win again.

I love this team and y’all should remember that you do too.
