Small thoughts on Biomes and how under-utilized they feel in the Infinite atlas.

Biomes, in the infinite atlas, feel so.. dead. They feel like they are a static determination of annoyance or fun in an area.

In my opinion, the biggest detractor is that biomes should affect more than just loot type if you have and if the atlas nodes continue to have local knowledge, and should have some variety.

One thought I had that simulates nature a bit better while not sacrificing fun for realism is Weather systems and Natural disasters that move and change around the map as a built in feature of the atlas.

Example: say you have a desert tile and there is currently a typhoon or torrential rain happening along the borders of the desert biome and a Mountain biome. The desert could then effectively have both a *Desert* and a *Water* tag to utilize. The mountain has the same overlap with water, or it could overlap with grasslands as well.

What this opens up is currency like many suggest regarding terraforming a node; and it now has a plausible reason. The desert node could now randomize to another desert tile or a water map-tile.

This also allows for a new orb as well: Engineering orb. The engineering orb could bridge unconnected nodes or change a node to be something different, it could be the same orb for terraforming and bridging or separate.

Both these new orbs can probably be introduced to expedition content to give more reason to interact with Gwennen and other expedition encounters other than logbooks and Dannig.

Other thoughts that have been brought up are things like chasing a beast for Beastiary league.

If the infinite atlas stays, it needs more engaging content and dynamic ways to alter gameplay that’s not just Grand Project and skip, but it should still be an option.

I’m sure there are faults with my thoughts feel free to poke holes in them. I’m sure there’s going to be “delete the atlas” comments sooner or later.
