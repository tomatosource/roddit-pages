Advantage of Stephon Castle’s hairstyle

We all know and love Castle’s hairstyle but did you ever think there was a tactical advantage to it?

It’s a helmet. A cushion, if you will.

Imagine a dude like Caruso (bald) getting hard fouled on a fast break layup attempt. Accidental elbow to the top of the head. bone contact straight to the skull. owie. He’s writhing on the floor. stoppage in play. concussion. out for a week.

Now imagine if you’re Castle. There’s a hair tie horizontal to your head spreading your dreads out over a flat surface area. it’s a bouncy cushion. That same elbow is bouncing off the top layer of his hair, maybe even smacking the defender back in their face. he feels nothing. contact layup and 1. Wemby is proud.

You can’t wear helmets in basketball but you can have hairstyles that protect your head area. It’s not just a fashion statement, it’s a legitimate tactical advantage and I look forward to seeing more players realize and use it
