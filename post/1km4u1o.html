What can we do about price fixing?

While I think the general sentiment is that we need a better way to trade, until that day comes what can we do about the “1 ex, but what’s your offer” items.

As a less intense player of POE 2, I am not across the prices of various items and so will approach the trade site knowing to ignore the first 3 items listed.

But for one item I had a whole page of 1 ex listings, yet not a single one was valid, 90% ignored my whisper the ones that did both asked me for my offer.

Dude, you listed it for an ex, therefore that’s my offer!

And while we can talk about ‘friction’ all we like, this one single aspect is the most egregious aspect of trading.

What can we do about it?

The most ideal solution is to move to a verified sale system, right now, if someone lists for an ex, and sells for a Divine, there’s no way to know that.

So, the sale needs to be governed, with currency and item locked in and the trade verified and recorded, able to eb searched.

Sure, people could still work their way around it, but at least it’s a start.

Maybe this is a silly idea, so what’s your solution?
