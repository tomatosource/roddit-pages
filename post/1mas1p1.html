If you have micro-stuttering every 5-10 seconds check your RAM. Mine is now 100% gone after this.

*This is for PC only, IDK about console*

I have had micro-stutters on and off since the release. I checked my RAM settings in BIOS and found they were set to weird timings and voltage, as well as having XMP disabled from when I was messing around before. If you disable XMP your motherboard will sometimes put RAM on voltages/timings that it just feels like doing. It caused very tiny stutters that were hardly noticable and less frequent in some games but massive stuttering in POE2. I have 2 16gb Corsairs.

I took out the RAM sticks and gave them and the slots a quick clean with some compressed air, put them back in, went to BIOS, turned XMP back on and just set everything (voltages/timing etc) to “Auto” and it worked - no more stutters in any game, POE2 smooth as butter now. Also used CCleaner to clean up the registry just before starting POE2 but I don’t think that had anything to do with it.

What worked for me probably won’t work for you, because we likely have different RAM, different motherboards, different settings etc but I’m certain that the micro-stuttering many complain about is some kind of RAM optimization issue with the game itself. It has nothing to do with internet connection, the latency graph spiking when you stutter is the network/game engine catching up to where everything is supposed to be after the hiccup.

So just check out your ram, use https://www.memtest86.com/ for finding errors and diagnostics, check that your voltages and timings are correct for your RAM model or on “Auto” for the PC to figure out, check that the RAM is slotted in properly (they can come loose with time/moving your tower a lot), see if any weird things are enabled/diabled like DRAM timing mode Link/Unlink, try things out and see if they work. I think this is fundamentally an optimization issue and the game has trouble with various RAM settings.
