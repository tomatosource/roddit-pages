De’Aaron Fox has by far the most impressive turnover rate in the playoffs

Fox has played 13 playoff games this post-season and he has just 26 turnovers. That’s 2.0 turnovers a game for a point guard. Fox’s assist/turnover ratio of 2.9 ranks best in the NBA this post season across all positions who qualify

If you filter all playoff PG’s who play at least 25 minutes a game, the only player with less turnovers per game is Anthony Black at 0.7 a game, although Black has just played 7 playoff games compared to Fox’s 13. And Black has a far different guard role than Fox who more of a pure point guard.
