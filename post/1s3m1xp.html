The Boston Celtics are 6-1 in games without Jaylen Brown, and they have a +11.5 net rating in 1229 minutes without Brown on the floor. That’s a better net rating than OKC, and it’s better than Denver’s net rating with Jokic on the floor.

Jaylen Brown appears poised to make 1st Team All NBA because he’s seen as carrying the Celtics to the second seed in the East without Tatum and after losing other key parts from last year.

But the Celtics are in second place because they’ve been destroying teams in the minutes that Brown has been on the bench:

Celtics Net Rating:

  • Brown ON: +5.1 in 2189 minutes

  • Brown OFF: +11.5 in 1229 minutes

In the non-Brown minutes, the Celtics have a better net rating than OKC this season (+11.2). They’re also better than Nuggets in the minutes that Jokic is on the court (+10.1).

And this isn’t because the Celtics are running up the score in garbage time. If you filter out garbage time minutes, as Cleaning the Glass does, the Celtics’ net rating without Brown goes up to +13.1. For context, only the 1996 Chicago Bulls have a better net rating over a full season.

It’s also not a 3P shooting fluke. The Celtics are shooting 35.9% with Brown on and 35.3% with him off. They’re allowing 35.1% with him on and 35.3% with him off.

Brown is obviously a very good player, and the Celtics are obviously a better team when he’s playing. But he hasn’t taken a huge leap this year.

  • 2025: +1.1
  • 2026: +2.0
