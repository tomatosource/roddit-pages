What obscure rule have you always loved or hated?

As a kid I was outraged upon reading that a batted ball in fair territory that hits a bird is considered a live ball, so a routine fly ball can become a free base hit if you happen to strike a pigeon flying about. As an adult I’ve changed my opinion, if you actually manage to hit a bird off the bat on the fly, it should be an automatic home run, could turn into an interesting home field advantage for some teams if Hitchock comes to life.
