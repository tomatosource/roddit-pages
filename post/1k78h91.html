Please Put +Life Nodes in Bottom Left of Tree

Last season I played a warrior, which is typically the type of character I like to roll in ARPGS- beefy melee. However, it never felt particularly beefy despite stacking a ton of armor and life.

This league I rolled a HC SSF Sorceress and started speccing into ES right away for survivability. I’m nearly finished with the campaign now and I can say this character feels far, far tankier than by warrior ever did, mostly due to the +ES modifiers on the top part of the tree. It seems crazy to me that the tree allows for so much juicing of ES but practically gives no benefits whatsoever to Life. I think a few +Life modifiers in the bottom left of the tree would help warrior feel way less squishy.
