Fracturing orb farm

With the price of fracturing orbs continuing to go up I thought I would do some farming and figured out a strat that has been netting me about 1⁄2 an orb every map. With my build this has turned into almost 60 divs per hour including hunting the cleansed areas. Not including the anomaly maps you find and boss fragments.

Maps- t15 high item rarity rolls

Atlas just make sure you have the summoning circle nodes selected

Master -Hilda multiple tablet node and the chance to duplicate domains node and the chance to have boss mob instead of rare mob

Tablets- irradiated with rarity and summoning circle

Ritual with summoning circle chance and chance for omens rarity good

Deli with summoning circle, rarity, chance for unique

Very low effort strat and very cheap to run. Basically you speed run the map make sure to find the additional summoning circle interactable. Some maps if you proc you can get 5 bosses from the circle. Most Ive gotten was 3 orbs from one map with lucky ritual proc and Hilda boss mob procs. I ran this for 4 hours today so could have just been really lucky RNG but thought I would share
