278 Desperate Alliance Runs

Around the end of the season, I was close to lvl 99
I decided to get the rest of my level with Desperate Alliance Sekhemas and log the data.

Here is that data

Total Runs: 278
Total Spectrums: 15 (4 Ruby, 5 Emerald, 6 Sapphire)
Total Vases: 97
Total Zarohk Twilight Keys 2
Total Profit: 3600 Divines (2934 of that from ATDs)

Relic Setup:
4 MS/Quant Relics
2 Shop Choice / Resistance Relics
1 Desperate Alliance

Vase Drop Rate
I have 79% relic quant from my setup
That comes out to that comes out to 102% total relic quant with the 30% boon
Let’s just say I had 100% relic quant overall, between the few runs where I whiffed the boon
I would say this puts the drop rate of vases at roughly 17% per unique relic dropped

Value of Average ATD
including reforges, my average random ATD was worth 10.55 divines
I wouldn’t use that number as gospel, 278 runs took ages but it’s still a limited data set
Probably better off looking up ATD prices, calculating the odds, and summing the values
Pretty sure the average random ATD was worth around 12 divines most of the season by that metric
Think I got a tad unlucky by that metric, but only a tad. It was still big money.

Income Volatility
ATD runs and reforging is very volatile
If you look at the chart, I made as much in my first 50 ish runs as the last 228 combined
I guess, just be mentally prepared to make very little money for 50-100 run dry streaks now and then

Rate of Profit
Spectrums and Vase drops contributed about 2.5 div per run on average for my setup
Desperate Alliance contributed about 7.5 div per run on average
Other drops weren’t significant but I’d value them at +2 div per run on average
(quiver effect time lost emeralds were big this league)
All in all I think I clocked around 35 divines per hour

Notes about Profit Number

I bought all my vases for 3 div each, and the profit number reflects that
It wasn’t always easy to get them at that price though

All runs in this dataset were within a month of this posting
IE pretty late into the league
Pricing reflects that

All ATDs I could not sell for 4 divines were reforged

this profit metric does not include any other drops
definitely made a few hundred divines outside of the above
But it was too much of a pain to track all that

Closing Comments

Anyways, hope some of you find this information useful
I’m mostly done with this league
Cheers everyone
