I built a free currency arbitrage scanner for PoE2 — scans the exchange every hour and shows you what’s printing

Got tired of manually checking exchange rates and doing napkin math on whether routes were actually profitable. So I built a scanner that does it automatically.

What it does:

  • Scans the Currency Exchange every hour across 13 item categories
  • Finds triangular arbitrage (Divine → Item → Exalted → Divine loops that net you more Div than you started with)
  • Shows bid-ask spreads (items where buy/sell rates are way off)
  • Detects profitable 3-to-1 vendor recipes
  • Gives you a step-by-step playbook — exact amounts to buy and sell, expected profit, capital needed

Right now it’s finding routes with 20-45% edges on Fate of the Vaal. Some are low volume garbage, some are legit.

It’s free, no login required. I use it myself before every trading session — just check what’s live, go execute, close it.

Link: https://divine-tendies.vercel.app

Built by one dude who got tired of spreadsheets.
Happy to answer questions about the math or how any of this works.
