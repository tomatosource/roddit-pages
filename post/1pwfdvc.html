Critical Home Run Stat

I’ve begun developing a new advanced stat, what I like to call the Critical Home Run or CHr.

Basically it applies value to home runs, and home run hitters. Through a point system we can apply value to home runs and determine whether a big home run hitter, say Kyle Schwarber, is actually making an impact on the outcome of the game with base clearing hits.

No home runs are worth negative points, but home runs that have little or no impact on the game are assigned 0 points.

Solo home runs hit with a lead or deficit of 5 runs are given 0 points.

Solo home runs hit with a lead of 1 are given 4 points, 2 are given 3, 3 are given 2 and 4 run leads are given 1 point.

Solo home runs that tie the game are given 4 points, take the lead of a tie game is 5 points. Down 2-4 runs is 3 points.

Additional runners on each add 1 point to over all home run.

Home runs off of same handed pitcher add 1 point to score.
