This god is an unfortunate god for good

Not sure how much of this to spoiler because it’s technically part of the story for PoE 2, but also lore….so I’m just going to spoiler tag the whole thing.

!I love this game in part because of the lore and mechanics of the universe. How gods are made. I just re-listened to Sin’s reveal in Act 2 and his lore drops about Garukahn, his daughter, and the shrine. It gave me a thought that has shifted my view on things. !&lt;

!For a while in PoE 1 you weren’t really sure if Sin was a force for good or evil. Certainly, even with the name, there had to be *some* question as to whether he was helping you or using you. Especially as he is having you off all the other gods. Still, by the end of the main story I think most of us are left trusting him and seeing him as a force for good, at least I was. Still, there is that…hint that he can’t be trusted as a god. !&lt;

!Well, I think he just put another stone on the pile in support of him as a trustful character looking to do good. In the lore drop in A2 he spells out how worship leads to godhood, and that worship further shapes gods to lose their soul and become the character the worshippers believe to gain their power (Love that about the lore). But here is the thing: Sin never wanted that power. He never desired conquest, worship, or power. He was forced into it by his brother for his own benefit. That makes him fundamentally different from the other gods and I think it explains why he is so different.!&lt;

!He isn’t ever-changing into a more abstract icon of his worshippers desires. He doesn’t seek that at all. In fact he hides in the shadows, trying to be unseen. Because of this, he is almost immune to that same “corrupting” force that turned gods into monsters, less human with every passing moment. For that, I think, is what keeps him bound to care for humanity and having a stable soul or character. !&lt;
