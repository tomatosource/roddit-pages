[King] Al Horford says he left the Cs because it was “deeper than just the basketball stuff of it”

Al Horford said his decision to leave the Celtics for Golden State was “something that’s deeper than just the basketball stuff of it.”

“And it’s something that at some point I’ll share with people,” Horford said, “but for me it felt like it was the time for me to go elsewhere.”

Horford still expressed nothing but love for the Celtics.

“I just think that the relationships and the bonds that we’ve built there over the past few years are things that go beyond the court. It’s a great thing because basketball has allowed us to all be together and I end up having much more than just basketball.”
