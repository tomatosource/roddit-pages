Some crafting concepts I thought of and some positive feedback about crafting in PoE2 (for once)

i really really like the omen system. i don’t really think they need to make these omens cheaper, cuz crafters will still buy insane amounts of them with their budget unless they’re way too common to the point the supply beats the demand (which would not be a good thing, because they’re actually pretty powerful).

in poe1 we had beast crafting, synthesis crsfting, meta crafting, grave crafting, eldritch crafting, harvest crafting, expedition crafting, influence crafting, fossil crafting, etc list goes on endlessly. ultimately it was still just a lot of ways to reach the same goal, and what made you valuable was your ability to find the cheapest or lowest ev cost ratio to get there. this meant individual players had the opportunity to capitalise and profit off crsfting knowledge, there were so many avenues to achieve things that you would be able to sell items for profit with low risk investments to ppl who could craft it themselves, but it’s cheaper for them to buy it from you because the way they know how was more expensive.

i will say this right now - the omen system in poe2 is incredibly powerful. the only item in poe1 that compares is Hinekora’s Locks, which were like 60+ divine each (triple the price of any omen rn)

so i don’t think the problem is the omens or their drop rate. I’m a solo player. I know I’m not gonna make the best bow in the world and I’m probably not gonna profit off investing 500 divines into a craft because my mirror bow has one or two suboptimal rolls.

that doesn’t matter. i’ve played this endgame A LOT man, and i’ll tell you, having a bow to dump every divine i earn into as a project over months is what i’m having the most fun doing at this stage in EA.

when i finish it i’ll probably do a few free mirror services and maybe save up for a mirror myself if it bricks, but ultimately i wanna corrupt it because there’s a corrupt implicit / enchant that can give a +4 arrow dualstring, and if i hit that, i don’t care if i can’t mirror it. I’ll have the best bow in the game by far, even if it’s only one that i can use.

so what’s my solution to the lack of depth in crafting right now? obviously more mechanics would be nice. a market for knowledge and not a market based on selling shares of items to craft them and dividing up mirror fees to shareholders.

beast crsfting, harvest crsfting, something where you being good and smart alone is a profitable trait to have.

i’m not sure how or if ggg intends to go that way though and i do like omens, so i made a list of some conceptual ones i thought would really patch some holes we are missing right now:

Omen of Sinistral Divinity

  • While active your next Divine Orb will reroll only prefix modifiers

Omen of Dextral Divinity

  • While active your next Divine Orb will reroll only suffix modifiers

Omen of Chaotic Divinity

  • While active your next Divine Orb will not reroll the numerical value of any modifiers and has a small chance to reroll the tier of a single random modifier, or do nothing

Omen of Harmonious Divinity

  • While active your next Divine Orb will swap the numerical values of two randomly selected modifiers. No modifier is removed or added, only their magnitudes exchange places

Omen of Pristine Essence

  • While active your next Essence will replace an additional existing modifier of the same type

Omen of Chaotic Essence

  • While active your next Essence will reroll the tiers of any existing modifiers of the same type

Omen of Unbound Essence

  • While active your next Greater Essence can be used on a Rare item

Omen of Inverse Essence

  • While active, your next Essence will instead add a modifier that is of a different type

Omen of Balanced Annulment

  • While active, your next Orb of Annulment will not remove any modifiers that an item has two or more of the same type of (eg. Lightning, Attack)

Omen of Preserved Annulment

  • While active, your next Orb of Annulment will be prevented from removing the lowest level modifier

Omen of Rewound Corruption

  • While active your next Vaal Orb has a chance to reset an item to normal rarity and remove all modifiers without Corrupting the item

Omen of Influenced Forging

  • While active your next usage of the Reforge bench will combine the magnitudes of any explicit modifiers shared between all three items within the weight pool of the reforged item, and the increased magnitude of the explicit modifiers will additively stack with other individual items with influenced weighting pools

Omen of Revered Exaltation

  • Your next Exalted orb will not add a modifier within a weighting range of the most recently added modifier

Omen of Inverse Annulment

  • Your next Annulment orb will replace a modifier with a modifier recently removed by an Annulment orb

Omen of Corrupted Essence

  • Your next Greater Essence will instead corrupt the item and influence an enchant / implicit modifier, if any at all, is added

Omen of Basic Annulment

  • Your next Annulment orb will instead of removing a modifier, downgrade a rare item to a magic item and remove all excess affixes at random

Omen of Careful Erasure

  • Your next Chaos Orb will not add any modifiers equal to the item level

Omen of Calculated Corruption

  • Your next Vaal orb has a small chance to corrupt an item twice

Omen of Dextral Identification

  • Your next Scroll of Wisdom will identify only suffix modifiers

Omen of Sinistral Identification

  • Your next Scroll of Wisdom will identify only prefix modifiers

Omen of Alchemical Empowerment

  • While active, your next Orb of Alchemy will treat each added modifier as if it was identified on a Tiered rare item ranging from 2 to 5.

Omen of Twisted Chance

  • While active, your next Orb of Chance can be used on a Corrupted item and has a small chance to add, remove or replace a corrupted enchant, or delete the item

Omen of Last Resort

  • While active, your next Orb of Chance can be used on a Corrupted item, and has a chance to purge any corruption and restore the item, or delete the item.

Omen of Unbound Divinity

  • While active, your next Divine Orb can be used on a corrupted item and has a small chance reroll only the numerical value of Enchanted implicit modifiers

Omen of Ultimatum

  • While active, your next Mirror of Kalandra can be used on a Corrupted item, but has a chance to delete the item instead of copy the item

maybe these ideas are stupid, but I think they patch out some of the missing things in crafting and add some cool interactions when combined. there’s obviously a lot more to be done in terms of crafting but I think some more omens would be nice. I don’t want to touch on the drop rates too much because that issue is way too complex. obviously these should be appropriately rare and expensive, but they give players a lot of ways to reach similar results when you combine 3 omens at once.

additional feedback: PLEASE remove omens from the currency exchange. let the mirror crafters organise buying them in bulk. having more friction between trading individual omens will be good for the everyday player!

BUYING THESE ITEMS IN BULK NEEDS TO BE HARD. this is the one thing u should max trade friction on. Make it so the individual player can buy one when they want one, but the mirror crafter can’t set 1000 buy orders and instant buy out any listing under 11 div.

make the mirror crafters, buy from dedicated omen farmers, and let the players buy from players, and this will feel better. it doesn’t need to be easy or cheap to get them, it needs to be hard to get 1000 of them at once. i suspect the impact this might have is as follows: mirror teams begin ramping up ritual juicing, which already has an absurd barrier of entry. yes it doesn’t scale with rarity, but omen farming with tablet setups is much more expensive than building rarity anyway.

so the average player is still not seeing much benefit here, but the average player does actually do rituals in their maps, and the average player will now find themselves divines richer from the 0.1%ers buying all their ritual tablets they drop for absurd prices. it feeds back to the average player. currently wealth is being aggregated and there’s not a lot from this wealth going backward. this way, it would, they need to buy these tablets from average players, and they need a lot of them if they are selling bulk
