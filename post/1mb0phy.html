Marcus Morris taken into custody on fraud charges

“NBA veteran Marcus Morris was arrested on July 27, 2025, and is currently being held without bond following an out-of-state felony warrant, according to public arrest records. The charges stem from an alleged incident involving a fraudulent check written with insufficient funds.

Court documents reveal the charge is classified as a felony under the statute “HLD-ST FEL,” with the description reading “OUT OF ST HOLD – FEL” and further comments citing “FRAUD – INSUFF FUNDS CHECK.” Morris is awaiting trial, which remains pending, and no bond has been set in the case.”
