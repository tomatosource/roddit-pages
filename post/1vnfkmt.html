Game Feedback: Make Special Encounters Their Own Off-Atlas Content

GGG, thank you for a banger 0.5! I’m really enjoying the league and can’t wait for the 0.5.5 reset.

I do have one wishlist item I’d love to see eventually: turn special encounters like Simulacrums and boss fights into standalone, off-Atlas encounters.

What I mean is something similar to how Simulacrum worked in PoE 1, where you could go to a specific location and open the encounter as long as you had the Simulacrum item with you.

Another example would be the Xesth fight, where you have the required Breach splinter/wombgift and can go to the specific off-Atlas area to open the encounter directly.

For the most part, I just dislike having to run additional maps and build up Delirium % before I can actually run a Simulacrum (maybe thats just me though). It would be much nicer if I could simply take a Simulacrum, place it somewhere, and immediately run the encounter.

It would also make bulk farming these encounters feel much better. If someone wants to buy 20 Simulacrums and run them back-to-back, they could just do that without having to constantly return to the Atlas, run maps, and build up the mechanic first.

I really enjoyed how PoE 1 had dedicated places for certain bosses and mechanics. If you wanted to specifically farm/focus on something, you could go to that encounter and just do it. I’d love to see PoE 2 lean further into that philosophy.

I know there’s already a bit of an iteration of this with things like Delirium and Ritual bosses, but I’d love to see the concept expanded to more special encounters.

Basically: if I already have the item required to run the encounter, let me run the encounter directly.

I think it would make farming specific content much more convenient while still preserving the value of the items themselves.
