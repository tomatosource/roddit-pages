TL;DR - Freeze → Black Screen → Reload is killing my joy for the season and I’m at my breaking point

I want to start by saying the league itself is genuinely great. The content is amazing, my progression feels rewarding, and I’m having a blast with the build. But the client stability is completely undermining all of that.

What started as a freeze every 5 maps has now escalated to twice per map — and it always seems to strike at the worst possible moments. Mid-ritual, with a full board of monsters and modifiers? That’s where it loves to hit. When it happens, I get a 1-in-3 lottery: disconnect, full crash, or the game reloads and I somehow get back in. In the first two cases, I lose the ritual entirely. And even when I do get back in, the mental momentum is gone and I start playing sloppily.

The cruelest part? The juicier the content, the more frequent the crashes. So the better I get at the game, the more punished I am.

I’ve gone through every community fix I could find — clearing cache, increasing cache size, switching to Vulkan, revert driver version. Nothing has worked, except disabling multithreading, but when i do, fps drops as low as 10 when there is some action and game becomes unplayabe (and i end up dying from lack of visual clarity). And honestly, we shouldn’t have to rely on player-discovered workarounds in the first place. These are band-aids, not fixes.

What I’d like from GGG:

  • A proper acknowledgment that this is a known, ongoing issue affecting a subset of players
  • An official workaround while a fix is in development
  • An ETA or update on where a driver stability fix stands

This isn’t just a rant. This is a long-standing issue that’s been quietly eroding enjoyment for a lot of players. I’m genuinely considering stepping away from the league, not because the game is bad, but because the more invested I get, the more the crashes hurt. And been lasting for 3 leagues now for me.

Has anyone actually found a fix that works? And has GGG ever commented on this specific issue?
