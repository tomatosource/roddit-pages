Atlas Passive Suggestion: Make Wisps Follow the Player

I think the wisp mechanic would feel much better if the wisp followed the player for a limited duration instead of pathing unpredictably across the whole map. While following the player, it would still apply its effect to anything it touches before eventually possessing the closest rare or boss.

This would make the mechanic more enjoyable and give players greater control over which enemies are affected, allowing them to influence which rare or boss the wisp eventually possesses.

Even if it’s not considered for a full rework, it could work well as a choice on the Atlas passive tree. Players who prefer the current style could leave it unchanged, while those who want something different can invest in the passive.

Currently, I don’t like how they are implemented, even using the atlas passive that keeps wisps active when you aren’t near. I often find they just aren’t as meaningful as they could be. Typically, without the passive they either disappear immediately or remain alive in random areas of the map without contributing much.

This idea was partly inspired by comments made during one of the recent livestreams.
