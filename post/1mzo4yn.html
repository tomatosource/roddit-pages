Trade Gold Costs are Not Enough to Stop Bots & High-Frequency Trading Programs

TL:DR:

Getting hundreds of millions of gold is really easy for “Bad Actors” to get in 5 minutes. The game will be overrun by high frequency trader “automated systems” on day 2+. Automatic trade needs to require an account bound currency that you can only get by playing the game. In current proposed system bad actors can just acquire billions of gold through “alternative means” in 5 minutes and entire trade system will controlled by “automated systems”.

Major Issue 1: Bad Actors bulk getting and Vendoring Waystones Generate Practically Unlimited Gold

Every league you can acquire hundreds or thousands of low-value waystones for a few divines via the usual 3 letter servers. In just a few minutes, a bad actor can get them and exchange them with Doryani for tens of millions of gold.

This supply is widely available from both in-game sources and certain third-party trade hubs. Some players operating at large scale can get1,000 low-tier waystones for about 10 divines which is pocket change for those moving large volumes of currency daily. Once vendored, that turns into 14 million gold in under 10 minutes with minimal effort.

Gold costs on trade are laughably easy to sidestep for those leveraging “external” economies or “automated systems.”

Major Issue 2: Castaway Carries produce infinite gold for bad actors

A single Castaway carry run can yield 3 to 8 million gold in just a few minutes. Some participants can easily string together 10 of these runs for 10–20 divines and walk away with 30 to 120+ million gold in half an hour. These runs are advertised constantly in certain communities, especially when a league is active and Hundreds of them are ran daily which will fuel bad actors.

Once again gold costs on trade are entirely ineffective at slowing down this kind of large scale bad actor activity.

While there’s no easy fix, Castaway rewards need adjustment if the goal is to make gold a meaningful gate to trade.

As it stands Bad Actors can secure hundreds of millions of gold in 1 hour and then use “automated trade systems and programs” and effectively bypass all gold requirements in trade: Forever opening up high-frequency trade “programs” and other methods
