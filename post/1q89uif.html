My Path to Level 100 in PoE2

Just wanted to share my experience with the league.

This is my first time reaching level 100 in PoE2, and also my first ARPG ever. I started playing since 0.1, and after around 490 hours this league, I finally hit level 100 using Disciple of Varashta.

POB: poe.ninja

https://preview.redd.it/w9bzsx583ccg1.jpg?width=1914&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=ddb8e86ecb10f17352cfe69b70c3acd788eb2505

What made this run special for me was playing the character blind. I didn’t follow guides or copy builds. I just theorycrafted, tested a lot of variations, failed plenty of times, and learned along the way. That alone made the whole experience much more fun and rewarding.

It wasn’t all smooth sailing. The league had its share of issues like exploits, unintended mechanics, and the ups and downs of the temple, but despite all that, I genuinely enjoyed the league. I know my progress was a bit slow, but I made sure to try almost all the content and experiment with every farming strategy I could think of.

One thing I’m personally proud of is that I didn’t abuse any exploits or bugs. No Abyss abuse, no temple tricks, and definitely no “russian snake tech,” which honestly shouldn’t exist in the first place.

Early League Struggles and Farming

During the first week, I tried running 100 Strongboxes & Essences. Not gonna lie, it was somewhat profitable early on if you didn’t have much gear yet, but the real standout strategy early game was Essence farming.

Abyss absolutely destroyed me in week one. That was probably due to my early build being weak and some skill issues on my part, so I ended up running T7 maps until I managed to farm my Headhunter, which I got about one week into the league. I mostly crafted my own gear early on, so my main focus was farming Headhunter as fast as possible.

I really enjoy crafting, so that part of the league was honestly fun for me.

Farming Style (Not the Most Efficient, but Fun)

When it comes to farming, what I really enjoy running is Abyss, Delirium, and Breach tabs with 11% effectiveness. I know it’s not the most efficient setup out there, but I like it because it has a lot of variety and keeps maps feeling fresh. I’m definitely not an efficiency-focused player, so as long as the content feels fun, I’m happy running it.

Keeping Up With the Economy

To keep up with the economy, I somehow turned into a boots merchant. I bought a ton of bases worth 1–30 ex on discount through live search, then crafted and sold a bunch of boots for cheap divines. The margins weren’t huge, but it worked when done in bulk. I also sold plenty of level 21 gems and 23% quality gems just to slowly improve my own gear.

Setting up the temple was one of the most frustrating parts of the league for me. I’ll be honest, it’s not very intuitive, at least in my opinion.

The Real Grind: Levels 96 to 100

From level 96 onward, I was finally able to run my temple properly.
However, level 99 to 100 was where the real burnout hit.

The last 20% experience was brutal. I almost quit multiple times. At one point, I even corrupted all my items out of burnout. In the end, I took a one to two day break, came back refreshed, and finally pushed through to level 100.

Loot-wise, no Mirror, no Hinekora, and somehow not a single level 20 Spirit Gem dropped the entire time. Honestly impressive in its own way. The biggest drop I did get was Rakiata, which I sold for 95 div, though that was pretty late into the league.

For some perspective on how scuffed the grind was, I ended up killing over a million monsters and dying around 450 times along the way. Definitely not clean gameplay, but it got the job done.

Realistically, I don’t think I’ll ever do this again.

Final Thoughts

Overall, I think the league is great, the most I’ve played since launch. I just hope that in the future, exploit abusers are actually punished imo, because the majority of players end up suffering from the impact on the economy. Buying truly GG gear feels extremely difficult unless you get very lucky.

Now that I’ve reached level 100, I honestly don’t know what to do next. Curious to hear what other people did after hitting 100.

Thanks for reading. Happy grinding!
