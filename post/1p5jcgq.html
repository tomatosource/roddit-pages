Am I the only one to feel guilty with some of the killing ?

For the most part, the game asks you to massacre undeads and corrupted beings, so that’s probably a mercy. But few maps made me question who the hell am I killing 😆

  • in act 3, the Azak bog, you’re basically a conquistador genociding an indigenous village. OK they’re mean and they’re killing other villages, but wow.

  • in act 3 final, you’re a time traveler arriving in the Vaal capital and killing anybody you meet in street. I can’t not imagine families terrified in their homes as the streets get filled with corpses. At least those corpses had less painful death.

  • in act 4, Shrike island, you come extinguishing endemic species of birds that were quietly living on their little island. You can even accidentally crush chicks, which would be sad if they weren’t doomed after you killed their mom in front of their eyes.

  • in act 4, trial of the ancestors, OK they’re the souls of past warriors kept around to test you, but my character crushing them while shouting “Weak!” at their face was very disrespectful.
