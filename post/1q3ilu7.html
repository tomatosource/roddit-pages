Jaylen player comp (a look back) and a thought

back in the day before we drafted Jaylen i remember an article that said Jimmy Butler had been sort of mentoring him a bit and I believe he said he saw a lot of himself in JB when he was that age. I also like the fact that jaylens nickname was “old man” because of his play style. these things all point to him being able to do this for a long time since he’s not a hyper athlete in the fast twitch sense of the term, but instead is powerful and explosive…that shit ages well, especially when you’re staying below the rim mostly and playing conservative bully ball.
