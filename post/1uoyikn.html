The 2010 NBA draft class had shockingly low longevity.

I was just looking through old draft classes on basketball-reference and it caught my eye that the 2010 draft class only has one guy that played in the NBA last season (2025-26).

Paul George

It’s weird because the few draft classes before 2010 all have severa guys still playing.

09: Harden, Curry, DeRozan, Jrue Holiday, Taj Gibson

09: Westbrook, Love, Eric Gordon, Brook Lopez, Batum, DeAndre Jordan

07: Horford, Conley, Durant, Jeff Green

You have to go all the way back to 06 to find another draft class with only one guy that played last year: Kyle Lowry.

ETA: Another point of reference is the 2010 class had 2 guys play &gt;12 seasons PG and Hayward.

Every class from 2000-2009 had 8-17 guys play &gt; 12 seasons and the 11 and 12 classes are at 14 and 8 guys respectively. So it’s not just bad injury luck at the top of the class. It’s guys washing out early all along the class.
