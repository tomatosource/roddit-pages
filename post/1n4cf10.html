I need to speak or this is going to get worse

I don’t normally post negative things in community reddits, other than bugs, because contributing to the rage is never a good thing, but there are some really obvious paths that are completely flawed in POE2 and I have no idea what the directors are thinking with their design choices. I’m scared that my favorite IP is going in a really bad direction.

As a disclaimer, so that you know I’m not a “fake fan”:

  • &gt;!I have over 16,000 hours on POE1!&lt;
  • &gt;!I have 2,000 hours in POE2!&lt;
  • &gt;!I avidly build and theorycraft; releasing a few of my builds to the community with positive reception (see my last POE2 build on Hexblast, before it was nerfed. I made that build before any major creator released anything about it).!&lt;
  • &gt;!I have nearly every supporter pack. I am invested and truly love this game and company.!&lt;
  • &gt;!I got my wife into the game and we play it together. I build all of my friend’s characters, every single league, along with my own.!&lt;
  • &gt;!I am a game developer myself, small and primarily with little side-projects next to my actual job as a senior network architect, but I do invest a lot of time into understanding game development.!&lt;

All that said: You built your game on FIVE fundamental pillars and sprouted an incredible ARPG from it (POE1), that took the entire community and genre by storm. Now, you’re trying to re-invent the wheel to fix problems that don’t exist… Why?

Please explain to me why:

  1. You decided to add negatives to passives on the tree? The negative is the fact that you spent one of your finite skill-points to obtain a specific passive, NOT the passive itself! Keystones were designed to do this exact thing: provide a unique and powerful passive, while also having a unique and powerful downside. Why did you stray from this simple idea and start adding negative bonuses to normal passives? It also makes building your tree even more confusing for new players, just like weapon-swapping specific passives do, but I can deal with that.
  2. Again, another point from the skill tree: POE1 had Life, ES, Armour, Evasion, Suppression, Block, and various other defensive mechanics on the tree. You had a CHOICE to spend, again one of your precious finite skill-points, on going into one of these nodes. Why did you neglect proper life nodes on the tree? It’s a defensive mechanic (mostly), like anything else (eHP)! Why the choice to, again, re-invent the wheel and add Deflection when you came up with Spell Suppression (an awesome solution to Evasion’s issue in POE1, while maintaining the actual downside of evasion being an RNG-based defensive layer)?! Why are you trying to create solutions for problems that have already been fixed in your flagship game? A game that, I might need to remind you, everyone loves! POE2 is not the reason everyone says, “poe-like”–it was POE1 that did that for you. Just like how D2 did that for the Diablo franchise.
  3. I don’t mind Sprint and it’s downsides. I’m just going to say that here. Though, I don’t think the game needed it and having some kind of skill gem(s) that support movement, more in-line with POE2’s philosophies, would have probably been preferred, but I don’t mind it. That said… It feels like when they added Dash to POE1, which sucked comparatively, and there was no real reason for it; just fix the core problem: don’t keep throwing stuff at the wall, hoping whatever you throw will plug the hole.
  4. Elemental curses merging to Elemental Weakness. Lightning Archon. The choice to not have a specific Ascendancy that is specialized at any specific thing. MMOs that ditched the trinity for “every class can do everything”. The blurring of lines between specializing and generalizing. This is a massive issue, at least in my opinion, for “modern” game development. When you take away the identity of a archetype, then you take away the power fantasy of someone who enjoys that thing. For example: it was NEVER an issue to have a minion specific ascendancy in POE1; in fact, many times you’d forego it for a more specific mechanic to build around (e.g. Champion, Occultist, Guardian, Elementalist, Scion). but it gave players an identity to anchor themselves to that they won’t feel “weird” for picking. Why did you decide this was a bad thing? Honestly, I think some of the Ascendancies are really well made, but others… I just really question what the thought process was. In trying to pursue an identity other than a weapon, element, or skill-based archetype, you alienate all of them.

These are just a few of my issues and I know it was pretty wordy, but it just feels like I’m running into a wall over and over. The last 2 leagues, I’ve pushed through the campaign on like 8 or 9 characters, only to delete them after getting to maps because I just didn’t find them fun.

That said, I do tend to make builds that are off-meta because my favorite part of POE has always been build diversity and combining really cool mechanics to make something interesting (this league, I was trying the Blood Magic keystone with Necromantic Conduit, to avoid the downsides of mana-degen and essentially give my minions +30% added chaos damage with little downside). Read-on if you want to know more, otherwise, I’ve said my piece.

I tried about 5~7 different Spectres through the campaign, and the other skeletal minions (because I didn’t play minions much in 0.2.0 and wanted to re-test). They felt terrible and died in a single hit on any given boss or mini-boss, even with minion defensive nodes; while still dealing significantly less damage than any of the builds I created for my friends. All the while, I have to use damaging skills at the same time, even though my tree is fully spec’d to minions. Why?!

I use the minion command button to guide them away from mechanics, but they are WAY too slow to avoid any real mechanics. Just give us convocation back. It’s literally the only solution to this. Maybe it was created in POE1 to be a “patch” for bad minion AI and slow movement, but in POE2 it serves a different purpose and is literally a necessary dodge mechanic. Hell, I wouldn’t mind if you just gave us a skill that said “Minions are immune to damage for 2s on skill use”, or something similar instead, so we can actually save them from stupidly obvious boss attacks.

Sorry. I started to ramble for a bit.

Anyone feel the same way?
