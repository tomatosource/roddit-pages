The ideal JB trade - is it realistic?

Lot of talk about the Blazers seeking JB. I’d be happy with this return as I think it would fill the Giannis/JB sized hole in my heart. I think this is a competitive “upgrade” for the team:

Portland sends: Clingan + Camara + Grant + 1 or 2 FRP

Boston sends: Jaylen Brown

We could take the Grant + 2 FRP and kick the tires on Trey Murphy from New Orleans. Potentially (effectively) landing us Clingan, Camara, and Trey Murphy in place of Jaylen Brown.

I think that is an overall upgrade for the Celtics roster. I’ve always been high on Clingan since he played at Uconn, he’s a local kid which is always fun, but he’s got great size, shot blocking ability, and he really started to stretch the floor this year for the Trailblazers. His rookie year he shot 14⁄49 from 3. This year he shot 84⁄246 (34%). When Chauncey was still the coach, they never really played Clingan as much as they should have. Seems he got more unleashed after the scandal went down and Tiago Splitter stepped up as the interim coach.

I’ve seen the reports that Portland really doesn’t want to move Clingan. They also don’t really want to move Camara. But obviously they have to give up something of value to get a player like JB. I think it could be done if they get desperate enough. We wouldn’t be making them move Deni, Scoot, or Sharpe.

Even if we couldn’t land Trey Murphy, Grant is still a great wing player that can fill in as a mini JB to help us compete this year. Odds are he is more tradable next season when his contract is expiring.

We could possibly still use the Vuc TPE as well. Any thoughts on who we could add on top of the Clingan/Camara/Grant (hopefully Murphy instead of Grant) additions?

If any cap experts know something that I don’t that makes this scenario not possible, definitely correct me. But I’d personally be pretty happy with this even though we’d be moving on from our boy JB. What do you all think?
