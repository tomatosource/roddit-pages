grouping the last plays of every world series

the 1926 world series went to game seven and ended with this play:

Babe Ruth Caught Stealing 2B (C-2B)

which got me meandering over to the glorious play by play logs of retrosheet.

this is a tally of every last play of the 121 world series:

play category runs? count perc
flyout 39 32%
groundout 37 31%
strikeout 25 21%
single run scored 5 4%

Grand Total 121 100%

and some sub-category level details:

  • flyout: 7 of 39 were foul ball flyouts
  • groundout: 6 were force outs, 3 were unassisted
  • K: 14 swinging, 5 looking, 5 unknown, 1 was dropped third strike (c-1b)

in the table are 11 world series where the team clinched on a walk-off. THIS ARTICLE from mlb gives details.
