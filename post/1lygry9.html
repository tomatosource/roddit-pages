Summer League Game 1 Grades

Baylor Scheierman

This full court summer league defense is fucking Baylor up. Not that he shouldn’t be expected to know how to deal with a full court press, but not having a PG is making the full court extra difficult and offense was sloppy as fuck. Scheierman was pretty much the PG for stretches, which isn’t going to be his role. He was absolutely forcing the issue with mixed and mostly negative results. He did have some nice passes, but he was also just jacking up shots. Thats not his game and I hope it slows down for him a bit. They need someone else initiating the offense and then kicking it to him as a secondary playmaker. Same shit happened last summer league.

&nbsp;

Grade: C-

&nbsp;

Max Shulga

I have mixed opinions on Shulga after game 1. He actually kind of thrived in this tough, full court sprint to 40 minutes. Shulga was applying pressure to the Grizzlies almost full court to help match the Grizzlies intensity. While it definitely helped win the game, I don’t think it’s indicative of what he’s going to do at the NBA level. I would have liked to see the shooting that put him on Brad’s radar in the first place.

&nbsp;

Grade: B

&nbsp;

Hugo Gonzales

Similar to Shulga, Gonzalez’s frantic energy was chaotic and disruptive in ways the Grizzlies didn’t know how to respond to. He’s physical as fuck. He also hit 3 of his 5 3’s which was impressive. He also had a great putback to start the game. I think Gonzales is kind of a utility guy offensively, and his role will be defined by the guys around him. He was doing a bit of everything here and didn’t look bad. Defense was great too. He had a big role in matching Memphis’s full court press.

&nbsp;

Grade: A

&nbsp;

Jordan Walsh

I don’t want to overreact to 1 game, but I really liked what I saw out of Walsh. He took the shots that organically came and he looked like he knew what he was doing on offense. Even has passing was solid, quickly moving the ball out of his hands. Year 1 he looked like a deer in the headlights, year 2 he was chucking like crazy, year 3 for game 1 at least hes playing within the flow of the offense. Ironically it’s his defense that wasn’t so great. He let a few guys blow by him and got out of position a bit going to steals. Some of this is probably other guys not rotating right, but the eye test said Walsh just got out of position a few too many times. Encouraging game for Walsh though!

&nbsp;

Grade: A-

&nbsp;

Amari Williams

Amari Williams by no means had a bad game, but he also just didn’t shine. My hope was that they would run some sets out of the high post for him to facilitate, which didn’t really happen. He hit all 3 of his shots and got a pair of blocks. Honestly we’d be talking more about Williams if Bassey didn’t come in and get a double double. Williams did help break up the full court press by being able to bring the ball up the court himself. Having a C with some ball skills really messes with that.

&nbsp;

Grade: B

&nbsp;

Charles Bassey

I missed the Bassey signing completely with my last post, which I guess I started writing well before he was signed. He had a great game doing all the things you want a big man to do. He set picks and got some lobs, he rebounded, she shot at a high %. If the Celtics are looking for a minute eater next season, Bassey being on the roster might not be so bad. He is definitely making a case for himself doing all the big man things at an NBA level.

&nbsp;

Grade: A+

&nbsp;

Kenny Lofton Jr.

Kenny Lofton Jr. is a fuckin’ anomoly. While looking like the love child of Maya Rudolph and Rachel Dratch, he barrels through bodies in the paint and after clearing 2-3 grown men he pulls the trigger on some janky shots. He had 11 points going 1-8 from the field. 9 of his points came from free throws, many of which he generated by getting fouled after offensive rebounds. Nothing about this is sustainable. After several of the rebounds he absolutely just ate shit and got destroyed by the defense. It’s honestly just the pure volume of touches he forced for himself. I was expecting a bit more finesse from the International MVP of the Chinese league (Which I think might just be for best international player). He is a position rebounder who could be great if he just reset the offense after securing the ball instead of pulling the trigger.

&nbsp;

Grade: C-

&nbsp;

Miles Norris

I’ll be the first to say I’m not the highest on Miles Norris. He only went 1⁄4 from 3 today, which is supposed to be his strength. 1 game for a shooter doesn’t say much. Same Hauser and Payton Pritchard have had plenty of 1⁄4 or 1⁄8 games. He shot 39% from 3 in Maine last year. He also needs to bring it on the defensive end. He had a nice block tonight, but shot blocking has never been a strong suit of his. He needs to really get after shots, not just blocking but using his length to bother them.

&nbsp;

Grade: C+

&nbsp;

Isaiah Wong

Wong was in super briefly, and he’s the closest thing this team has to a PG. He was handy in getting the ball up the court, but just didn’t get a lot of time to do anything of note.

&nbsp;

Grade: C

&nbsp;

Kendall Brown

Despite Kendall Brown only playing like 7-8 minutes, he made an immediate impact hitting a 3, a transition layup, and a free throw. Brown is slated as a great athlete with limited basketball skills. If he can manage to maintain being a transition threat, while shooting 33-37% from 3 and playing D, he might be it. He’s 1 of like 4 guys all in the same mold trying to show they can play. He might not play every night, or he might play more as the standouts sit in games 3 and 4.

&nbsp;

Grade: B+
