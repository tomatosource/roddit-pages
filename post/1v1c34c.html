Yaxel on double-digit comeback against the Grizzlies: “Man, their butts got tight. They puckered up, you know what I mean? Some of us are built for the moment and some aren’t.”

SRC: https://larrybrownsports.com/basketball/yaxel-lendeborg-throws-shade-cameron-boozer-summer-league/762511

Lendeborg outperformed his rival Cameron Boozer, who beat him in each of the last two times their teams faced off — one in college and another just five days prior in summer league play.

“I think about [revenge] every time I play him,” Lendeborg said of Boozer, via Ben Golliver. “Every game we play, them refs be saving him. … It is a competitive nature [with] everybody that gets drafted ahead of me.”

Lendeborg took things a step further when he claimed his Warriors were ready for the moment and Boozer’s Grizzlies weren’t.

“Man, their butts got tight,” Lendeborg continued. “They puckered up, you know what I mean? Some of us are built for the moment and some aren’t.”
