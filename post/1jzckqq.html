Feedback on why PoE 2 builds aren’t very fun to make:

Disclaimer: This is not yet another “PoE2 bad” post. I love this game, and have a lot of confidence that it will be great. I don’t care about the things that I would consider “growing pains”, like lack of endgame content or the early game being a bit too hard. These will be ironed out in time. I also don’t have a negative opinion of the heavily overstated issues with “combo gameplay”, as anyone who has played the game past like level 30 would know that the concept of having to combo abilities just to kill packs of white monsters isn’t really a thing past that point, and skill combos are really just reserved for rares and bosses, which I think is a good place to be and the design intention.

Instead, this is intended to be specific pain points with fundamental design choices that seriously impact how fun it has been for me to make builds.

&nbsp;

1. Charges are lame

Builder spender gameplay is, generally, boring as fuck. Charges have been regressed into different colored combo points. I don’t mind the concept of consuming charges to empower abilities, but I strongly think that they should also go back to having some set buff amount that each charge gives you. That makes charges a more interesting facet of a build and presents you with a question for your character, for example: Do you value the extra skill/movement speed of maintaining high frenzy charges, or do you value buffing specific abilities more?

As it is, there is no choice. Charge Infusion as an aura isn’t worth it for most characters. So you just pick an ability, and if it needs charges to work, you slap one of the like 3 automatic charge generation engines and then never think about them again.

&nbsp;

2. Giant’s Blood

Giant’s Blood has put a complete stranglehold on mace builds, and it will only get worse with split attribute requirement weapons like swords. One handed weapons simply can’t compete. Part of the problem is that attribute scaling and gem level scaling are a bit too powerful, so stacking strength to be able to use Giant’s Blood doesn’t come with a trade-off, it is all positives all around, at least once you get over the initial hurdle of getting enough strength. While I would suggest the keystone get outright removed, at minimum I would like to see it be reworded into the similar node coming in LE’s next update: 2 handed weapons can be used alongside a shield. This is important for disallowing the dual wielding of 2 handers, which is just so absurdly powerful for the 14 gem levels, and evens the playing field a bit. It wouldn’t be enough on its own to make Giant’s Blood balanced, I don’t think, but it would be a start.

I tried playing a 1h mace Amazon, thinking that the extra added flat phys and the crit (normally extremely difficult to scale for maces) would be enough: it still was not. Tbf, could’ve put more currency and effort into it and maybe it could’ve gotten there, but that’s a project for someone else.

&nbsp;

3. Ignite

Ignite sucks. Not as bad as this sub would have you believe, I played a full ignite stormweaver up to T15 maps this league and it was fine (not great, mind you, but functional and fine), but it has two major issues:

  1. Ignite chance. This seriously needs to be changed to work like Bleed, reach 100% chance to apply and have it just always apply. Despite getting every % increased chance to Ignite and % increased chance to apply ailments within a reasonable distance on the tree, and using the support gem that gives +100% chance to ignite, AND using the candlemaker unique gloves that double ignite chance, I STILL had casts failing to ignite fairly often. This did get mostly solved once I had scaled crit enough to increase my hit damage, but it took far too long considering the amount of investment my character already had in chance to ignite. The only other big mechanism I could’ve had to help with ignite chance was Overwhelming Presence, but considering the strength requirements on the aura, it was too difficult to fit in with my build.

  2. Ignite proliferation. The buffs to proliferation were nice, they were the reason I started playing an ignite character, but I still don’t understand why there’s a 1 second delay and why the range is so short. It’s not quite as bad as it seems at first glance, because the ignites can prolif off of corpses, but it’s still bad. A big part of why ED/Contagion is so fun is the moment the first monster dies your big DoTs just instantly prolif throughout the whole pack, and it’s awesome. Let ignite have that.

The whole big hit making big ignites thing, I don’t really mind how that works currently. Once again a bit of an overstated issue imo, as once you get enough magnitude, you quickly get the desired result of the damage of the hit being relatively small but still putting up a big ignite.

&nbsp;

4. Energy system for trigger skills

I’m looking at you Mjolner. The system works reasonably well for the general “Cast on Ignite” type spells, I suppose, but it completely ruins the fun of something like Mjolner. I suppose the point I’m making here is that I don’t believe the energy system should be universally applied to all trigger skills. Let things like Cast on Block, Cast Lightning Spell on Melee Hit, Cast Fire Spell on Hit, break the rules a little bit and just…trigger on hit, with an ICD. I love these kind of effects and just have not managed to make them feel remotely good at all.

&nbsp;

I do have some other issues, but these are a few of the worst offenders in my opinion, and I really hope these fundamental design choices get revisited.

TL;DR: Charges suck, ignite sucks, Giant’s Blood is cool but too good (or maybe 1h maces too bad), energy system works well in some cases but awful in others and shouldn’t be universal for all trigger skills.
