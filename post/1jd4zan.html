Dirk Talks about the Luka Trade and his feelings on the Mavs organization

Dirk Nowitzki had a German podcast with his sister Silke for his foundation. He - numerous times - calls the trade “insanity”. Says he was about going to lunch when he heard about it and skipped lunch to sit in his hotel room for 1 hour.

Silke specifically asks Dirk, if by now he has heard of any reasons:

Dirk emphazises that he hasn´t beeninvolved with the Mavs for the last 2 years.

Dirk says it only can be about Luka´s preparation, not about his play.

Dirk talks about watching Luka playing in a yellow jersey in Crypto.

He calls it “strange” and “bizarre”

He calls the current situation with all the injuries “the new reality as a Mavs fan”

“It will take time” to live with this.

Says Cuban has little to no influence on anything.

Interestingly he implies criticism on Cuban:

Says Cuban had to expect what would happen, when he sold the majority share.

Says “last year things developed into that direction” and VERY BITTERLY he says:

“now we see, how everything panned out….”
