After a few years of watching the NBA, Amazon’s broadcast of the NBA Cup is head and shoulders above what I’ve seen during the last few actual postseason broadcasts.

Looks like Disney/ESPN have the rights to the Finals for the foreseeable future and that’s a huge shame. Jeff Bezos can go fuck himself but this Prime broadcast was excellent.

+ Image quality is exceptional and looks like something you should actually expect from the last decade

+ Audio quality is decent and Prime’s NBA theme is decent

+ SVG and DWade are plenty good on commentary even if they’re not the most dynamic

+ Taylor, Blake, Dirk, Nash, UD. Actually a fantastic panel. Insightful, entertaining, funny, complementary, sexy as fuck. I’d let any of the five sit on my face

+ Graphics and studio gimmicks are like… actually pretty good?

I don’t necessarily want Amazon to have rights to all broadcasts. I just want all broadcasts to meet this easily attainable standard. I’ll need to check out the new version of NBA on NBC, so far I’ve mostly caught local and Amazon for regular season games this year.

Eat my ass, ESPN
