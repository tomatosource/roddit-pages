Turns out 1h Mace is a blast

I’m one of those guys who likes to build around the gimpiest shit you can find. I want to find the diamonds in the rough, the stuff the meta won’t even look at. I spent a week or so perfecting an all chaos Chayula that was a blast to play. I got pretty bored of Monk and was looking for my next project.

It’s no secret Witchhunter looks pretty bad on paper, and one handed mace looks worse. Also, GGG said fuck Paladins and gave us hardly shit for melee combat. Warriors look slow and methodic, Monk is insanely OP, stick and board just seemed out of the picture.

My theory was if there was an ascendency that was like a Paladin, Witchhunter is the closest. I mean it’s a witch hunter. I thought the Culling/Decimating combo had some potential, so I figured I’d roll a Witchhunter 1h Mace build.

At first I thought I’d run around Giants Blood. Problem is you rely on the slow slams due to warrior not having a quick gap closing attack like the 15 in Monks Arsenal. Did some theory crafting and so far I’m actually pretty impressed- 1h mace DOES have some fast and fancy moves if you optimize right.

With Merc you are in an ideal spot to go towards 2 high value areas: Falcon Technique (and that spear tree next to it that increases strike range- HUGE for 1h Mace), somewhat close to the would-be Templar tree (I wanted to focus on Armour/ES combo), and also have quick access to Jack of All Trades area which is very high value even for moderate stat stacking.

The idea is to max out attack speed with HowA, Falcon Technique, and some decent stats. A good chunk of damage will come from HowA. Here are some things I learned. Expert Slim Mace has inherently faster attack speed. A max roll IAS Expert Slim Mace can hit quick. Olorovasara or whatever it’s called is not as good as it looks. Turns out a good Mahuxtol roll solves resistance in one fell swoop. I have a 600% Mahuxtol with 3 Ele resist soul cores leaving a lot of space for other value mods.

Some tricks I learned: grab those unique boots that make shocked ground. Leap Slam + Herald of Thunder + these boots = cruel done in 2 hours. You can get topaz brotherhood and herald of ice for extra clear.

You don’t need herald of ice or thunder to clear in maps. The ability Boneshatter scales well and can single handedly fill the role of dual herald. Equip it with magnified effect, devastate, armour explosion, and any two other value gems. Boneshatter heavy stuns anyone primed for stun. That’s the little yellow glowy ball above the enemy. This can be obtained in a single leap slam or 1-2 attacks (just one of your mace has increased stun buildup). As soon as you Boneshatter someone primed for stun it sets off a chain reaction. Devastate breaks their armour, Boneshatter damage is TRIPLED (over 600%!!!), and Armour explosion does a huge AOE explosion equal to the tripled explosion Boneshatter did. These explosions also do enough to prime OTHER enemies for stun allowing you to chain Boneshatter. It’s wild, I’m clearing screens with an ability that looked like absolute garbage.

Long story short 1h mace is actually fun. I am still on 2 ascendencies, currently 80 but will hit 90 and get my last two over the weekend. Looking at the concentration leg to help take down bosses and beefy rares. Anyways exiles, if you just ignore how insanely OP howa pillar builds are, you can still have a lot of fun. We may not kill bosses in 4 hits but explore the game, find the cool things that work and have a gas.

Also that keystone that makes life regen effect ES is absolutely bananas. You can stack so much regen on every slot I regen 450 ES/Sec at all times.
