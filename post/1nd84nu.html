Clip Request: Recent Carrying Fouls Called

Hi r/nba, it’s offseason and I got curious about what actually constitutes a carrying foul in today’s nba. Carrying and traveling not being called are frequent complaints of the modern game, but as I watch more games, I realize that traveling actually does get called from time to time. Usually when people complain about the lack of travelling calls, they’re referring to drives to the basket, which can be easily explained away by the gather step rule. Actual travels that I’ve seen however, are mostly from post ups and jump catches.

Carrys on the other hand, don’t seem to ever get called. Hesi moves are not carries, because I guess if the ball is spining and your hand is palm down it doesn’t count. Thinking basketball had a while video where they discuss how dribbling used to be strictly palm down only, and is now usually done palm up without a carry being called. Then of course players can just straight up hold the ball, and won’t get called unless they take too many steps (this has always been true and is good).

Thoughts?
