Fans and media

I would not be mad if brown is traded this offseason, but I don’t want fans to complain ever again about the big bad media that always apparently wants to trade Tatum and brown. Fans are eating up basically every sound bite and interpreting it in the most dramatic way possible. It’s not the Boston media that’s responsible for narratives around the Celtics, it’s almost exclusively the fans.

Separately idk about a JB for Giannis trade. It’s a big swing that narrows the title window and probably has to include a bunch of draft picks since you can’t afford to give up depth for just two superstars. My preference if a move happens is Donovan Mitchell. Downhill scoring and playmaking guard.
