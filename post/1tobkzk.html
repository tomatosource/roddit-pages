Does anybody care about the gap between Charge generation availability?

Since PoE2 has come out I have seen a largely undiscussed/ ignored issue with Endurance Charge generation options compared to Frenzy and Power charges.

Endurance charges have 2 accessible options for generation, Magma barrier and a support gem for Perfect Timing skills. Everything else is a node on the tree for a chance at generation a charge when stunning a rare or boss. A chance at generation for stunning and breaking armour on two support gems and a Timeless jewel that from what I can tell is locked behind the Pinnacle boss.

Mean while Power and Frenzy charges have 2 to 3 skills that guarantee a charge gets generated, around the same amount of probability options on the tree as endurance but they also have unique pieces of gear that give more ways to get a shot at generating charges.

Everytime I look various builds, it seems the optimal Endurance charge generation method is to path over into the Monk tree, take resonance so power charges are converted into endurance charges and slap on a unique chest that grants a chance at charges on crit

The fact this has been a persistent issue I am not sure if this is just oversight from the dev’s or if it’s intentional to prevent potentially overpowered Ancestral Warrior Totems builds.

I have made posts about this on the feedback forum but I either get completely dismissive responses, or they go largely ignored. I have looked up similar posts and it’s the same trend either no engagement at all and they get buried or comments are dismissive about the current methods are fine.

Is there something I have been missing this whole time, or is there actually an issue that should be addressed here?
