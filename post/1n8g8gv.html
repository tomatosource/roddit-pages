Superstars and their impact on the evolution of the game

I was reading a few pieces about Bill Russell and there seems to be a consensus that he was the first truly great defender in the history of the league, pretty much setting the template for defensive centers and rim protectors.

What can we say about how our favorite stars affect the way the game of basketball is played, at least in the NBA? I’ve always been interested in this question. It probably warrants a very very long analysis, but nonetheless I thought we can talk a little bit about that.

I guess the first thing that’s worth mentioning is… not many superstars and all time greats actually had a tangible impact in that sense? They are great winners with amazing careers full of heroics, but they hadn’t actually “changed” the game that much, sometimes less than their lesser peers.

I think about, for example, MJ and Pippen. Pippen is widely regarded as a pioneer of point forward, which imo is a big deal for the modern game. MJ’s legacy is of course unparalleled, but I can’t quite say the same about his game? New templates for half court offenses? Emphasis on the mid range game? No clear answer imo.

Looking around today’s game, the biggest trend setter is imo Steph, who should be credited for the modern emphasis on 3 point shooting and perhaps also the small ball in general. Lebron and his ability to excel as 1-5 is said to inpsire a whole crop of well-rounded talents thriving in positionless plays.

Who else you got? Which superstars moulded the game and which ones less than expected, despite their excellence? Excited to hear more and learn a few things from y’all.
