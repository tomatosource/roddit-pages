POE2 game feedback from a veteran player. GGG please take seriously.

I’ve played POE1 since beta and most leagues since then. I have more than twenty 40⁄40 league totems in my H/O. Apologies for the long post but these are all points I feel are necessary to get across.

Like many other players have posted, I dislike POE2 for many reasons.

The campaign is unnecessarily difficult and tedious. Player progression is too slow. Boss fights seem correctly balanced with some exceptions but everything else is just bad. The loot is very bad. I am not crafting anything because I would need regals (which I don’t have) or scours (which don’t exist). I’ve gotten one alch which bricked.

I tried playing Huntress and immediately stopped at level 10 because I realized it was way too frustrating. My skills did almost no damage and there was no way for me to really change that. I would get a small upgrade only to be pounded on twice as hard in the next zone. Classes that rely on weapons are too hard done by in the game. They need in game ways to upgrade them much easier like in POE1 with more alch and essence drops, or quest rewards. Just a nice yellow weapon would make the game so much better. Why not give a stack of crafting resources early on as a quest reward - 5 alchs, 5 exalts, w/e.

I reverted to my usual minion build which I am currently playing as I wanted to try out Spectres, with a combination of chaos skills because just minions is impossible to play at the start. I’m currently around level 28 and I have two warrior minions and just enough Spirit to summon…one spectre. It hits like a wet noodle. So much for my epic spectre build plans. I reverted to frost mages which at least hit a little harder. Big disapointment for me so far. I don’t really understand why the experience can’t more closely match POE1 where by level 30 I can summon an army of minions that deal relatively good damage. I am not forced to change one minion type for another…I can have skels, zombies and spectres if I want. The spirit requirements need to be drastically reduced or there needs to be more sources of it early game. I don’t want to wait until level 80 to have more than 3 minions. On top of this I’m foregoing any other auras to to get these minions.

My other major complaint with POE2 is the massive lack of defenses. I feel like there are no defensive options for most builds. In POE1 from level 16 most of my characters have some combination of Arctic Armour, Tempest Shield and Granite/JADE or some other defensive flask. A little bit of armour can go a long way at the start to reduce small hits. In POE2 you have basically nothing for the entire campaign. Bone cage and raise shield are a joke - if you stop at all you get surrounded and killled, everytime. All you can do is kite monsters and hope. Monsters move just as fast if not faster in POE1 but you have WAY less defenses. Usually I like playing high life regeneration builds but this is generally impossible in POE2, another big disappointment. Skills do so little damage that if you put half your points into something like block, you’re going to deal very , very low damage.

The skills system has potential but there is this weird aspect where if I want one skill to be decent, I have to put all my best gems in that skill, leaving the others to be sub-par. The skills already deal so little damage that if you put something like LMP it becomes even worse generally. In POE1 I had one skill that deals great damage…in POE2 I have five that deal very little. Feels like a lot of effort for same or less result. Late game jewelers are too rare and hard to come by.

The attribute requirements are unnecessarily restrictive, making it so that when you do find a nice upgrade, chances are you can’t use it because you’re lacking in a secondary stat. If finding myself having to choose from unspeccing valuable damage nodes into STR or DEX to to get a bit more life. Why is this so much more punishing than POE1?

I don’t understand the reasoning of putting negative stats on more and more things in the game. Shouldn’t the balance come from encounters and enemy mechanics, not negative stats? We now have unique items, passive tree nodes and skill gems for which many having negative stats. It seems like the list keeps growing. The game is challenging enough to fill out resists, life, damage, accuracy, crit and a zillion other things without being negatively hit but what is supposed to be an upgrade. I HATE this probably more than anything else.

I won’t go into an in-depth ascendancy review because it isn’t the place for it and I haven’t tried them all, but I will say that in general I find them less interesting than POE1. Even Phrecia had more interesting choices. The choice to make them less powerful overall has had the net effect of making them less interesting for me personally. Many of them feel like quick fillers that haven’t really been well thought out. In POE1 we get these huge, cool upgrades. In POE2 we get things like….a jewel socket.

The POE2 ascendancy system is a complete mess. Very challenging for a new player without gear. Needs a total rehaul IMO. I’ve spoken to no one that likes this. I can’t imagine trying to do these on a Huntress for the first time. I will probably stop playing for this reason alone.

I understand the goal of wanting to slow down the game in late stages but the campaign is NOT the place to do it. Make harder end game challenges or increase monster stats toward the final tier of maps. Players have very little control over their stats/characters early on because of a lack of crafting materials, gear, gems. If the game is too hard, this just leaves us feeling powerless and then there is only one thing left to do - quit. I strongly suggest reducing monsters speed , damage and life in the entire campaign. Make skills that actually hit hard. Put in more quest rewards to give crafting materials and weapon choices. THEN balance for end game after once it feels right. You can’t have a game like this that feels terrible in the first 10 hours. It will not succeed.

I am not a player to jump on a negative bandwagon. I legitimately want POE2 to succeed. I want to like it. But it’s hard to comprehend a lot of these changes that take the game so far away from the formula than made POE1 a massive hit. I think a lot of players wanted POE2 to be the same base game as POE1, just with better graphics, interesting skills with effective fast combos, cool new ascendancies, new loot to discover, new and better boss fights. We got almost none of that aside from better boss fights. I thought that much of the feedback from last launch would have been listened to and I think the community is shocked that actually the game went even deeper into the wrong direction.

My final strong suggestion for GGG is to stop making decisions in a development vacuum and start involving actual players earlier. Ask them how certain skills feel given starting equipment and really listen to the feedback without bias. Things like Against the Anvil for Smith of Kitava should never make it into the game. After just an hour of playing I could tell things were off. It’s so clear to me that you need more and earlier player feedback. Your player base is your most valuable resource - use it!
