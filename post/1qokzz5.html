The Mazzulla System is Working

Let’s just appreciate how well this team is running. As Jaylen mentioned post-game, we lost 5 players, including an MVP-caliber player, and we’re still 2nd in the East (as we were last season).

Easily the thing that shows the strength of the system and team culture is our ability to get great, effective performances out of back-ups. No Kornet, Horford, or KP this year? Queta steps up and looks fantastic. When Queta is out? Garza comes in and goes beast mode on the glass. And last night, we plucked Amari Williams from the Maine Celtics, played him 26 minutes, and he put up 9 pts, 7 rebs and 2 blocks. Even among guards and wings, we are getting meaningful performances from a teenager who was not on most teams’ draft boards (Hugo).

That’s not to say that these guys aren’t solid players in their own right. It just shows that Mazzulla is getting the most out of his guys, and that’s the sign of a very good team. I remember the Spurs and Warriors dynasties could play guys you’d never heard of before (Splitter, Festus Ezeli, etc.), and next thing you know they’re making key plays in the playoffs and inching you toward another banner
