Net Ratings (Adjusted) based on Opp. Net Rank - Who has been playing up/down to their competition?

https://preview.redd.it/op3vmiuqrtae1.png?width=953&format=png&auto=webp&s=91854667d7dbdd0a3c25619e632a360bde6ddc7a

I took Cleaning The Glass’s Net Ratings that filter out garbage time and broke them out into two samples: games against the Top-10 teams and games against the rest of the league (or Bottom-20). I find it valuable to view a team’s performance in this way because you can see which team’s are playing up/down to their competition and, if you place more value on a team’s performance against better competition (which you probably should, as the playoffs are full of the best teams), then you can find this more informative than simply sorting by season-long net rating to determine the best team. This plot can be read as follows: the further a team is above the gray dashed trend line, the better they play *than expected* against the best teams given how they fared against the rest of the league.

Additionally, I adjusted the net ratings for two factors: opponent uncontested shooting variance and home-court advantage. Both adjustments were done seperately on each of the two samples: games against Top-10 opponents and games against the rest of the league (or Bottom-20). For the first adjustment, I regressed each team’s wide open (closest defender &gt;= 6 Ft away) 3-point shooting and free throw shooting to the opponent’s respective season averages. This adjustment was made because opponent uncontested shooting is notoriously volatile and once an open shot is surrendered, inherently, it’s out of the defense’s hands. The second adjustment was for home-court advantage. This was only done due to the small sample size when selecting only the games against the Top-10 teams. Most teams have only played 10 games so far against the best teams, with Cleveland only playing in 6 such games. This adjustment helps teams such as the New York Knicks, who have played against Top-10 teams 9 times so far, but only 2 of those games were played at home.
