What do you think we should do at the draft?

We really have 5 general options, just curious where we all think Brad should be come draft time and where prioritize should lie.

  1. Stay put, draft at 27 and 40.

-The draft notoriously falls off pretty hard at this range, probably my least favorite scenario, I would honestly rather trade out completely for futures vs make both selections.

  1. Trade one of the picks, select at the other.

-I like this one a little more, probably my second favorite path overall. I would select at 27 and try to get a future 2nd for pick 40 simply because I question the level of talent at pick 40 range.

  1. Trade up.

-I think this would be pretty worthwhile depending on cost in this particular draft. I said it falls off pretty hard by pick 27, but it’s very potent between picks 14-22 range which should be pretty achievable trade up range. Guys in that range I would be after are morez Johnson, Bennett stirtz, hannes steinbach, Chris cenac, labaron philon, and honestly the list goes on- it’s a good range to be in. In terms of what we could give up, I think any of Hauser/pick 27/‘27 first should be on the table… unless we want to make a bigger splash to get into the top 10 and then more stuff is on the board. The pick I’d be targetting is either of the OKC picks in the teens, they can’t make both and will definitely trade one or both.

  1. Trade out for a player.

-Probably my favorite option, I think it makes the most sense to trade out of pick 27 and add further value(Hauser/future firsts) to grab a player. Orlando needs to shed salary and get cheaper guys on the books, maybe dangle Hauser/27/‘27 first for black? Same to the mavs for lively for less? Maybe even Orlando for a deal around Suggs? Atlanta for okongwu if they select Mara at 8? Tons of options, I prefer this option, maybe keep pick 40 and grab some cheap bench guy to fill the roster.

  1. Trade out completely for future assets.

-very simply, trade 27⁄40 for future picks.

What do we prefer of these options? I’ve followed the draft pretty heavily and I really don’t love our options at pick 27, im probably above all else against just staying at 27⁄40 and making the selections, I just don’t love what we likely have to work with. I think if we could move up a bit we strike oil in that late teens early 20s range where stirtz/steinbach/anderson/etc likely will be.
