2020s #1 overall draft picks are really good

LIST

2020 - Anthony Edwards

2021 - Cade Cunningham

2022 - Paolo Banchero

2023- Victor Wembanyama

2024 - Zaccharie Risacher

—-

Anthony Edwards is a low tier superstar right now. Not Jokic/Giannis/SGA/Luka level yet. But just outside. He had multiple 40+ pt playoff games. And even lead Minny to two WCF run.

Cade turned around the Pistons from Worst team in the Nba to a 6th seed in the East.(Albeit, some veteran additions helped)

Paolo was hit by injuries. But he had 40 pt, and even 50 pt games. He was starting to have a leap last season before getting hurt. He also had good 2 playoff series in his career. (Individual stats).

Wemby is Wemby. The most hyped prospect since Lebron. And he is showing signs of winning multiple DPOY and All Nba awards.

Risacher is probably the worst player here. But he is a solid starting level player for a competing team.

Next will be Flagg, But I need to see this upcoming season.
