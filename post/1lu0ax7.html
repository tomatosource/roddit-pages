One build to rule them all

Is there one? Or does it take the best in slot on the meta to claim that throne?

I rolled a late season LS Deadeye and she’s sitting at level 91 2 weeks after I realized I wouldn’t be able to stomach getting my Smith up to 100.

She clears maps like nothing I’ve played before but struggles against map bosses. My Smith is a slow but sure map clearer who eats the bosses for dessert.

She was terrible in a Logbook encounter; probably because I triggered an effect my Smith never worries about but still downed Olroth fairly easily. I won’t even bother with her in a Sim because those are harder than Expeditions and even gets dicey on the Smith at times.

She hasn’t run Trials yet but my Smith is so good at those that I won’t bother. She hasn’t seen a breach outside of a map yet but that’s next and here’s why.

Last night, she waltzed through all 4 floors of Sekhemas (my first time ever). It was never a challenge all the way to Zarokh. Zarokh was a little bit, but, it was my first time and I learned from the encounter. I made 10 div in that one run after selling a couple of things plus the 1 raw that dropped. I thought, “what if I was experienced and confident enough to advertise carries and filled out a group to add even more divs on top?”

My lesson from all this is that I will likely be starting every season with whatever the meta ranged class will be. I resisted being that guy all season long and now I regret it. My Smith is great and I’ll run one again but he’ll be a trust fund baby when that time comes.

Looking forward to August for sure.
