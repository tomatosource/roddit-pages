[Stein/Fischer] Daniss Jenkins turned down a two-year minimum contract from the Detroit Pistons.

Source: https://marcstein.substack.com/p/saturday-morning-confidential-the

Detroit Pistons guard Daniss Jenkins turned down a contract that would have run for the remainder of this season and covered next season. Jenkins has been a regular rotation player for the Pistons this season while on a two-way contract.

After playing against the Golden State Warriors on Friday night, the second-year guard has just three games of NBA eligibility remaining this season. If Jenkins exhausts that eligibility, he’ll be unable to be active in NBA games for Detroit for the remainder of the regular season. In addition, two-way players aren’t eligible to play in the postseason.

The Pistons have the ability to convert Jenkins to a rest-of-season contract, which would make the guard eligible to play in remaining games, as well as the postseason. Jenkins would then be a restricted free agent this offseason.

Detroit also has their $5.1 million Bi-Annual Exception, which could be used to offer Jenkins more money. However, using the BAE this season means that it would not be able to be used next season.
