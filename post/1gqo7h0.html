Chris Paul’s averages in 11 games as a Spur: 10.5 points on 47/37/100 shooting (62 TS%), 8.4 assists with only 2.1 turnovers

Source

CP3 won’t be making any more all star teams but he’s having another career resurgence, and is quietly putting up his best season since he turned 37.

Paul’s IQ, passing, and pesky defense never went away. But he was less impactful in the last two seasons because he didn’t give teams a big reason to respect his scoring. This season, his middie is back and he’s burning defenses (albiet on low volume).

He has also changed his shot profile to maximize his impact by helping spacing - he’s shooting more threes per game (5.4 attempts) than he has in any season outside of his Houston tenure. He’s currently shooting twice as many threes as twos even though he’s never shot more threes than twos in any previous season.

You can partially credit Wemby’s paint gravity and improved screen setting, but a young team with a good culture is a natural fit for Chris Paul at this stage of his career.
