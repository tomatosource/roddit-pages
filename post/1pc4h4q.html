The “+x Level to Skill Gems” modifier is just as ‘Mandatory’ as PoE 1 - But the multiple levels of modifier makes gearing in PoE 2 more tedious.

Much is made of the “Importance” placed on the “+5 to Skill Gems” modifiers in PoE 2 compared to PoE 1….But isn’t the modifier in PoE 1, just as “Mandatory”?

Take a build like Righteous Fire, you’re still always looking for the “+1 to Skill Gems” modifier on your Sceptre in PoE 1.

Both games have the same reliance on the modifier imo.

The greater issue in PoE 2, is that it appears as though having up to a “+5 to Skill Gem” modifier, results in this almost artificially introduced need to continuously change your weapon, throughout the campaign, which results in the Campaign being artificially more difficult, at least on your first playthrough.

PoE 1’s campaign is without doubt much easier, and people claim this is because of the “Borrowed Power” introduced after 10+ years, but that isn’t really true. The reality is, that PoE 2’s Campaign, ignoring the fact we’ve not fully established the correct balance also, appears to be balanced around reaching the breakpoints of “+2 to Skill Gems” and “+3 to Skill Gems”, when these modifiers begin to appear. Sure, for a build like ED Contagion which is very strong, this is less necessary, but for most builds, it becomes really obvious when your gear doesn’t have these skill ranks when they begin to appear.

In PoE 1, if you find this modifier on your weapon for example, you pretty much constantly feel “Ahead” of the curve during the Campaign.

In PoE 2, unless you’re constantly able to find a weapon with the increased gem levels as the modifiers begin appearing as you level up, then you always feel “Behind” the curve.

This makes gearing during the Campaign more tedious than necessary imo, rather than making it more fun and engaging….And also results in “League Start” builds becoming even more popular than they’re in PoE 1.
