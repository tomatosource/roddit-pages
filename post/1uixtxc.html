[How Long The 30 HR Streak Lasted]

Rodriguez: 13

(SEA 1998-00 -&gt; TEX 2001-03 -&gt; NYY 2004-10)

Bonds: 13 (PIT 1992 -&gt; SFG 1993-2004)

Pujols: 12 (STL 2001-11 -&gt; LAA 2012)

Foxx: 12 (PHA 1929-35 -&gt; BOS 1936-40)

Ramirez: 9 (CLE 1998-00 -&gt; BOS 2001-06)

Cabrera: 7 (FLA 2007 -&gt; 2008-2013)

Schwarber: 7 excluding shortened season

(CHC 2019 -&gt; WSH/BOS 2021 -&gt; PHI 2022-26)

Ruth: 6 (NYY 1919 -&gt; BOS 1920-24)

Ortiz: 5 (BOS 2003-07)

Note: Bonds has the longest streak with said new team at 12. Pujols debuted for Cardinals with 11.

[Led League In HR During 5 Year Span]

Ruth: (AL) 1920, 1921, 1923, 1924 (Led AL 12 times overall)

Rodriguez: (AL) 2005, 2007 (5 overall)

Cabrera: (AL) 2008, 2012 (Twice)

Schwarber: (NL) 2022, 2025 (Twice, currently leading 2026 by +5)

Foxx: (AL) 1939 (4 overall)

Bonds: (NL) 1993 (Twice)

Ortiz: (AL) 2006 (Once)

Ramirez: (AL) 2004 (Once)

Pujols: (NL) T2 in 2004 (Led NL Twice)

Note: Ruth, Foxx, Rodriguez, all did 35+. When Schwarber hits 36, he’ll be the first ever to have 5 seasons of 36+ with a new team (46, 47, 38, 56, 30*)

[Highest MVP Finish During Span]

Rodriguez: 1st in 2005, 2007 (3 MVPs overall)

Bonds: 1993 (SEVEN MVPs)

Pujols: 2005 (3 MVPs & 2001 ROY)

Foxx: 1938 (3 MVPs)

Cabrera: 2012 (MVP & 2012 Triple Crown)

Ruth: 1923 (Once)

Schwarber: 2nd in 2025 (None)

Ortiz: 2nd in 2005 (None)

Ramirez: 3rd in 2004 (None)

Note: Repeat MVPs weren’t allowed yet 1911-1914 & 1922-1929, hence Ruth only won once in his prime.
