Looks like the 76ers Kelly Oubre Jr. will also be returning from injury vs the Hornets: “I feel ready to play basketball tomorrow.”

Kelly Oubre Jr., who has not played since March 10 due to a left elbow sprain, appears that he will return against the Hornets.
