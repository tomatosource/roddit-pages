I see a lot of people have been asking about a potential 2nd round matchup with the Knicks so here is a brief insight into what some of those tactics would look like.

A lot of people have been asking questions about the potential 2nd round matchup with the Knicks. I’ll give some insight into tactics and how to gameplan for something like the Brunson and Kat PnR.

I’ll tell you what the one weakness is; our bigs can only really defend in drop coverage. Asking Queta/Vuc to come out of drop or deep drop to guard up at level is asking a lot of them. When Kat is on his A game the Brunson and Kat PnR is difficult to guard for any team in the league. Now add two bigs that you don’t want to put on an island up on the perimeter and you have a problem. As old as Horford was he was able to hold his own on the perimeter and could defend well enough on an island against mismatches.

It’s all about personnel. Yes you can put Tatum on the other team’s 5 but because of the team’s different lineup(s) that won’t bring the same result as the team that won the finals a couple years ago. The championship team basically had 5 defenders with no weak points. Teams are smart nowadays. They will just run multiple actions until they get the mismatch they want. There’s no hiding it. You can only mitigate it. Even if you put Tatum on the other team’s 5 they’ll just roll off ball screens or a weak side pin down for example to force a switch.

So what you want to do is control the Brunson KAT PnR, not stop it. You want to try and force Brunson into uncomfortable decisions and limit where Kat gets the ball. So start the game by having Queta/Vuc play a high drop/up at level then quick recover to prevent Kat from getting any open quick pops/short rolls. The key is to you want to control and prevent defensive collapses.

As far as Brunson goes our defenders have to top-lock and trail Brunson HARD. Remember our bigs will drop inevitably so we need Brunson to be under pressure. Being physical and keeping a hand on him by fighting over every screen puts pressure on Brunson and prevents him from turning the corner for Rhythm 3s.

This is just the basics. The game planning these teams will have is much more in depth. Playing the gap, peel switching, weak side low man help when Kat rolls; etc, etc. If Celtics and Knicks make it to the 2nd round healthy this will be the first time all year the Knicks get the full strength Celtics. Should be fun. Celtics in 6!
