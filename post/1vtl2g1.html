What contract will Mathurin end up with?

Now that Peyton Watson is off the board, Mathurin is arguably the most notable RFA that’s still a complete question mark (I think we’re all assuming that Duren will re-sign with the Pistons).

Honestly, I’m glad the Pacers aren’t team that’s paying Mathurin and I think Oubre will replicate Mathurin’s production at half of the cost that Mathurin is looking for anyways. The league is really down on Mathurin’s archetype of player. Look at what Colin Sexton and Anfernee Simons got this year (two players of a similar style) - both got 2-year deals in the 20M-ish range. No team wants to commit huge money to a score-first iso guard in today’s league. I guess those deals allow those players to get back onto the market quicker, but does that really matter if the market for your type of play is dry to begin with?

I think the best case for Mathurin to lock-in a decent second contract is a 3-year deal @ MLE money (~16M annually), with a team option in the third year. If I had to guess, he wants the same contract that Shaedon Sharpe got on the Blazers, but I’ve always contended that Sharpe is a bad contract.

I think there’s a non-zero chance he signs the QO, but see my comments about getting on the market quicker as a guard with no market.

What do you think?
