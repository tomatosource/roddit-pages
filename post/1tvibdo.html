Why must we bump and stumble into literally everything!

I have 10year in POE1 and while initially was not super happy with poe2 I am slowlyg gathering some momentum in liking it. This new release is definetly a step in the right direction.

The biggest issue I still have in PoE 2 is that it feels like I’m constantly colliding with the environment.

Tiny rocks. Small map decorations. Random terrain edges. Narrow passages. Invisible-looking corners.

Instead of flowing through a map, you’re frequently getting caught on little bits of scenery that abruptly stop your character. Maps seem filled with small edges and obstacles that interrupt movement, turning what should be smooth traversal into a series of stumbles.

The roll to run also feel just janky af.

The result isn’t that the game feels more challenging—it feels less responsive.

Difficulty should come from monsters, mechanics, and decision-making. It shouldn’t come from fighting the geometry of the map.

PoE 2 has made huge strides in combat visuals, animations, and boss encounters, but movement often feels like a step backward. When every little environmental detail can halt your character, the game loses some of the satisfying flow that made PoE 1 mapping so addictive.
