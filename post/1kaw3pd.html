Is PoE 2 secretly nerfing your loot the more you play?

After 900+ hours in PoE 2, my friends and I can’t shake this feeling anymore. At first we thought we were just imagining things, but it’s happened too many times to ignore.

Loot feels great during the first hour or two of the day. We get good drops, decent rolls, sometimes even real upgrades. But if we keep playing, it all goes downhill. Loot just gets worse. Bad rolls, barely any currency, and maps stop feeling rewarding at all.

The weirdest part? If we take a break and come back the next day, loot feels good again, especially in those first few maps. It’s like the game rewards short sessions and punishes you for playing too long.

Is this just in our heads, or is there actually some hidden system messing with drop rates based on playtime? Anyone else noticing this?
