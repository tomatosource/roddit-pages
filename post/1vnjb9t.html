WPBL data is now on Baseball Reference, along with a new dedicated page for women’s baseball

We’re very excited to announce that Baseball Reference now has coverage of the Women’s Pro Baseball League! We’ll have pitching and batting stats for both teams and players, updating daily, and pages for individual players and teams.

Additionally, we’ve reorganized our women’s baseball coverage in one central place: https://www.baseball-reference.com/register/womens-baseball-stats.shtml

There, you will be able to find both WPBL coverage, as well as historic data coverage for all 12 seasons of the AAGPBL.

Hope you enjoy! Please let us know if you have any questions.
