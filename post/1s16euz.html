Josh Hart is the weirdest ‘dare him to shoot’ guy in the league

Hart is the most confusing ‘not good’ shooter in the league. Teams vacillate between guarding him like a normal perimeter wing or putting their center on him. Hart himself goes from catching and shooting true with a hand in his face and taking tough shot clock dwindling bailouts to hesitating with a significant amount of space.

He’s a completely different caliber of shooter from someone also dared to shoot like Amen Thompson, and is shooting above 40% from 3 this year, but will definitely continue to get this coverage. It seems like a complete confidence related thing with Hart, and not at all related to his ability to actually shoot the ball.
