[Holmes] MPJ, Cam Thomas, and the purest form of basketball you’ve seen

Source

Not a fan of analytics? Don’t like return on investment short selection? Think the NBA is too heavily guided by shot maps? Well, have I got the team for you. 

Michael Porter Jr and Cam Thomas appear intent on making the Brooklyn Nets analysts redundant. The mere suggestion of shooting a high percentage shot is offensive to them, or at least they are playing like it. 

During his time with the Denver Nuggets, Porter Jr made a reputation for himself for only making contested shots. Having been traded to the Nets for Cam Johnson and a 2032 first round pick, he has now made it his sole meaning in life to only shoot contested shots. 

Give Porter Jr an inch and he will make the shot, stay too tight he will blow by for a midrange jumper. No wonder Jokic got all those assists. In Brooklyn, 91% of his made threes had been assisted. 

Opposition sides will be thankful the Nets pose no real threat of winning games this season, otherwise they may have to find ways to limit Porter Jr’s production. Looking at the Nets roster, I don’t think it can be considered speculation to say they’re tanking. 

…

Cam Thomas is just having fun. He plays NBA games like pickup runs. If there is a God, and he helped design basketball, this is the way he would have intended it to be played. 

Against the Spurs, Thomas hit several floaters over Wembanyama. Yes, you read that right, the 7’6” man who lead the league in total blocks last season, despite only playing half the campaign. 

Thomas’ 40 point scotching of the San Antonio Spurs was still not enough to win the game, despite the Nets best defensive efforts during the third quarter. 

Twelve-year-old NBA2K players would blush as some of the moves Thomas has been stringing together. Has anyone told Cam you can’t actually unlock skill points in real life? If not, please don’t. 

Three points down with 4:35 left in the fourth quarter, Claxton set a hand off for Thomas to shoot from the elbow. Instead he pivoted, drew an isolation and went for a between crossover into a step back contested three. 

He made the shot over an outstretched Devin Vassell. Ordinarily it would be a ridiculous shot selection, but with Wembenyama lurking in the paint, I do understand Thomas’ desire to not take the traditional route.  

…

Lets face it, the Nets are bad. The nets are so bad the only mayoral candidate to have not distanced himself from the team is Andrew Cuomo.

ESPN has projected the Nets will get 17.6 wins, and with a 0-4 start to the season, its difficult to argue they will be any better than a lottery pick. Nets fans may be better served scouting the NCAA to discuss the 2026 draft pick. 

Treating the Barclays Center as a homage to Rucker Park style basketball may be the best way to get bums on seats this season, in a league where style has evaporated in lieu of winning analytics, being fun may be what brings the fans in.
