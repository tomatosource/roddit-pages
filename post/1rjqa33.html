Enjoy this

There are very few regular seasons like this one, when the team exceeds expectations, plays hard, loves each other, and is really entertaining to watch. These Celtics are accomplishing the rare feat of making this season fun without a championship-or-bust mentality.

In recent years, we’ve had this maybe 4 times (2015-18, 2022). Before then, 2002 may have been the last time the C’s felt this way.

It’s not common you get to love a great team full of good people like these guys. When JT comes back, it’ll be exciting. But expectations are the enemy of fun. Let’s appreciate what we have now; many franchises haven’t felt this way in ages
