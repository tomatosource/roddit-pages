No Virginia, the temple did not tank the economy.

CAVEATS:

  1. Yes, the temple was far, far too rewarding, even given the intense effort required to set one up
  2. Yes, all end game content should be more closely aligned in terms of risk/reward.
  3. No, I am not advocating for massive increases in drop rate across the board.

But time and again I read comments stating: “Temple killed the economy” or the even more extreme: “Killed the league”

But how? What does that mean? What does a “dead” economy even look like?

Firstly, I want to dispense the myth that the retention was lower this league.

https://preview.redd.it/fdgm2ctlf9vg1.png?width=1044&format=png&auto=webp&s=2a5d4c35e253b3c67ba7e3fe97f51b0b70b5f1f6

There was an initial slight drop off, but that quickly recovered and was better overall than DotH.

Whatever you may interpret from the graphs, retention was not significantly worse or better than previous leagues.

Secondly, the idea that the surge of currency was totally bad for everyone NOT running temples is highly debatable.

“I saw that XXX was 100 Div and quit the league” was a common sentiment.

I did not snake my temple; I reran Atziri using a straight(ish) line to her chambers and made very good money selling Regalia’s.

I also ran all other content, and found that a Visions run on a Citadel could easily net 80 Div.

I believe that at least some of the claims that the economy was ruined come from people who saw currency dropping like never before, wanted in on the action, but couldn’t (or gave up) getting the temple set up.

But in reality, that income flowed down, rich players chose to buy keys, rather than farm them, buy crafting mats instead of farming them, and the players like me who did that farming made 1,000’s of Divs, without once snaking a temple.

The economy of a game is NOT the same as real world economics, money only serves one purpose, whereas currency in POE 2 is actually crafting material masquerading as currency.

Inflated currency means that now Divs can be used to; wait for it, reroll item affixes!

In other leagues you might well get to mapping and not see a single Divine, an item that by its very design is needed in the dozens to achieve its stated purpose.

More currency means more average players can actually use them to craft their own gear and not rely solely on trade.
