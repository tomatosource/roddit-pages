I don’t mind the Mystery Boxes, however…

Putting Set Items in a loot box system, that don’t do anything on their own but need the entire set to fully “unlock” their style is a bad idea.

For those of you who don’t know what I’m talking about, the Last of the Druids Mystery Box has an armor set called the Stormborne set, that comes in 4 variations. (cold, lightning, fire, and void).

Here’s the link to the video: https://youtu.be/EM5FqUDBHjo?si=S0o6v2dz0wsv217W&t=28

But here’s the catch: The glow of the armor is tied to the chest pieces, so the Gloves, Boots and Helmet look boring as hell on their own because they don’t glow unless you have the matching body armor equipped.

In isolation that doesn’t sound too bad of a concept, however, the problem is that all of these items are only available in the Mystery Box so far. This is designed specifically in this way to have people buy more boxes until they complete the entire set.

Now, I know that all of the previous Boxes had matching armor sets too, but they never specifically nerfed individual pieces until you had a full set…

Please, GGG, never put items into a gamble system again that need other items to work properly.
