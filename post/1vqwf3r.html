Hot take: Tatum is the first player to return from an achilles injury BETTER than before.

Not a Celtics fan but Tatum’s always been one of my more favorite players in the league, I think he’s the first player I’ve seen that came back from an achilles tear better than he was before. Everything is the same, the athleticism has barely gotten worse if it all, but now his passing is just fucking crazy. He’s obviously always been a good passer but idk how many passers in the league are better rn

Cade, Harden, Scottie, Jalen Johnson (maybe), Bron, Giddey, Egor, Hali, Trae, Jokic, Jamal, Luka, LaMelo, Garland, Keyonte, and maybe Keaton Wagler can be better. These are the only players in the league I think pass better than him. Idk how his passing just got so much better after his return maybe it’s just being injured you can only sit in a room and watch film all day, but I’ve never seen Tatum play as good as he has since he came back.

I know y’all lost JB, but just know Tatum is still here and the best he’s ever been.
