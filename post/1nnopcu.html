Sorceress identity?

What is the identity of the sorceress and her ascendancies? Personally I feel that out of all of the classes we have right now sorceress still feels like the one with an identity crisis.

Chronomancer lacks the most identity imo since most (if not all) of the elemental spells are just better on witch ascendancies, sure there are some niche things you can do with chronomancer but that ascendancy also really lacks any damage increase.

-Heck ggg just add a node that gives 5% more damage per 1 second of cooldown or something

-let chronomancer have + 2 charges to spells or something that reduces cooldowns of spells when you use other spells

-give a choice node where it let’s you pick one of the following: 70% reduced cooldowns, 20% reduced cooldowns and 100% increased damage of spells with cooldowns, 100% increased cooldown and 100% MORE damage of spells with cooldowns.

Stormweaver feels fine but arcane surge is in a rough spot and infusions arent class based (nor should they be) and they are so abundant that it doesnt feel like you gaining much with this ascendancy either.

-similar to gemling let stormweaver generate infusion charges overtime or maybe a percentage based on intelligence to gain an infusion on spell cast on like a 3 or 4 second cd.

-add in archmage to the ascendancy so you get the full buff for say half the mana cost.

-some meta nodes would be good too. (It would also be cool if the third ascendancy is based on meta triggers) right now if you want to trigger spells using spells its best to just go blood mage for cast on crit.

Besides the changes to the starting nodes on the passive tree it makes sense to just roll witch every time if you want to be a spell caster and that just doesnt feel great from a design philosophy. Im hoping that blood mage and infernalist dont get nerfed next league and instead sorceress gets some sizable buffs somewhere but im not holding onto hope at this point.

Id love to hear some thoughts from you guys as the community to how you would make sorceress have her own identity like I did or heck if you disagree with me let me know as well!
