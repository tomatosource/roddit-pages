Why do you like baseball?

I was never into any sports growing up, and I never paid any attention to them in adulthood. And one day I walked past a game on tv and they did a slow motion replay of a pitch and the trajectory of the ball was the coolest thing I had ever seen. It was like an optical illusion or something. The physics of the pitches were amazing to me. I then got into it in 2025 with the playoffs - I’m from toronto and it was baseball fever - and I find the rhythmic nature of the game so enjoyable, with extremely high moments of tension and release. And then learning about its history in Japan, where the sport is seen as “soul forming” was just so fascinating to me. Baseball has something other sports don’t have. It’s very special. It’s almost mythological. So why do you like baseball? What is it about the game that you find compelling?
