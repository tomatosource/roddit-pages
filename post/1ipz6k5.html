Rarity find

A little thing.. I have like 600 hours in poe1. That entire time, I didn’t care about rarity find. Didn’t know why it was so important. If I had an item with rarity find, cool, but I didn’t seek it out.

Playing this beta, I’ve started to get into the importance. If you’re new, and you can clear maps pretty consistently.. I urge you please please please get into rarity find.

I play 2 maps this morning, before work. Between my gear, map mods and atlas points, I still only have like 175% rarity find. In those two maps (breach and delirious for more monster density) i found 3 divine, 1 perfect jewelers orb, like 15 exalt, a handful of vaal and a handful of chaos orbs.

Now a 75% ingenuity belt doesn’t seem miles away. It gave me more will to play the game. If it decreases your overall dps, doesn’t matter what your dps is if you can still clear maps. My main tempest flurry is only 40k dps. I’m still clearing maps in sub 5 minutes. I just wanted people to know my experience.
