RFA Slog: Who Signs First?

Restricted Free Agency has been unbelievably slow. Actually, not even slow. It’s just not moving at all.

The biggest RFA’s are Kuminga, Grimes, Thomas and Giddey. And all of them are basically having the same conversation with their respective teams.

Player: “Gimme my money.”

Team: “No.”

Player: “Fine, then another team will.”

Team: “Like who? No one else can afford you.”

Player: “… Oh, right.”

And it’s basically that same conversation over and over again.

Who do you think blinks first, and just gives up trying to have any leverage?
