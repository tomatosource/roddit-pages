Requiem HC private hardcore league is live now!

RequiemHC is a Hardcore Private League heading into Patch 0.5: Return of the Ancients on May 29th at 10 PM CEST / 1 PM PDT. We’re ~15–20 players looking to grow. 

Why Hardcore? 

In Softcore, deaths are cheap and defenses don’t matter. You zerg content until it falls over. Hardcore flips that — every defensive layer counts, every boss mechanic counts, sloppy play gets punished. Death is permanent (character moves to Standard), which gives your run a natural arc: clear beginning, clear end, fresh reroll. No aimless grinding. 

Why Private League? 

No market to compete with. Your build doesn’t revolve around generating currency — you play the game to play the game. Orbs become actual crafting tools instead of coins. You’re forced to learn the crafting system and build around what you find rather than following a shopping list. 

A fixed group instead of an anonymous ocean. Sharing drops, helping someone get their Ascendancy back after a rip, pushing endgame together. When someone drops something valuable, the whole group gets hyped. Host can kick disruptors, no bots, no price fixers. SSF feel with people who give a shit. 

What we’re looking for 

Everyone’s welcome — especially players who don’t quit after their first rip. Reroll, regroup, keep going.
