PSA: If you’re going to corrupt valuable low level uniques, please Runeforge first.

On high level bases, Runeforging has upsides and downsides.

On low level bases, it just straight up makes the item significantly better. Bones of Ullr for example gains movespeed, increases its total energy shield, and gains runic ward.

In many cases on a low level base, not Runeforging it is as big a deal or even a bigger deal than not quality-ing it.

If you’re fishing for an extremely valuable corruption, Runeforge first!
