Our performance these playoffs/struggles against the Knicks

Let me first just say, and I think I speak for everyone here, I’m gutted right now and feel horrible for Tatum. Assuming it’s an Achilles tear, I’m optimistic he can come back after a year of rehab (and perhaps get that wrist fixed too) and return to MVP level. If there’s anyone who can do it, it’s Tatum (with the help of modern medicine).

On a separate note, why do people think the Knicks have been beating up on us so bad? I mean they seriously have our number this series - even before the Tatum injury - and I was not expecting to be down 3-1 against a team that we 4-0’d handedly in the regular season.

Can anyone with some reasonably high level basketball analysis give their opinion on this? I mean we were literally the best team in basketball last season by a wide margin - and that was without a healthy KP for most of our playoff run. It seemed like even when KP didn’t play, we had a deep enough roster to run other teams off the floor. The roster/coaching is currently the same as it was, and we even benefited this year from the development of guys like PP/Hauser. We have more championship experience, cohesion, and less pressure on us. Plus, we were a 60-win team this year while resting guys and coasting for most of the year. Everything looked promising for another championship run.

So what changed? Is it just that the Celtics are now a year older, and thus more tired/injury-prone than your replacement level Eastern conference contender? I’m aware how competitive the modern NBA is - and how short title windows are - especially with a veteran team. I wasn’t expecting this team to compete for a title for years to come. But this roster was in as good a place to repeat as any team has been in the last 10 years. We didn’t lose any role players like the Nuggets/Bucks did, and our core is still pretty young & in their physical primes. But in the modern NBA, if a team isn’t improving, they’re getting worse. Is what we’re seeing really just the natural result of 1 year of our own guys aging and other teams’ development?

Or can you chalk most of our struggles up to unlucky injuries? Does JB’s nagging hamstring and Hauser’s ankle really worsen our team dramatically compared to last year/this regular season? I feel like KP’s health issues aren’t the cause of our decline, because he’s consistently been a nonfactor for most of this and last year’s playoffs anyways. Last year’s team, even without KP, would have run the Knicks off the floor in 4-5 games, like they did to the Pacers/Mavs.

Is it a coaching/gameplan thing? Are we just not optimizing our personnel with the offensive/defensive sets we’re running?

Or maybe the Knicks really do just have our number, and are perfectly built to beat this Celtics team? And if that’s the case, why did we take the season series 4-0 by an average margin of 16 points? Does it just come down to our injuries/fatigue in that case? Like what’s making the difference here?

As someone who’s followed this team religiously for the last 10 years, I was pretty optimistic about our chances to repeat this year, given how historically dominant we have been the last 1-2 years. At a minimum I thought we’d handle the Knicks, and perhaps take the Pacers to 6 or 7. So I’m a bit surprised we seemingly fell off as quickly as we did, and are getting exposed by the Knicks. If anyone has any thoughts, I’m searching for answers myself, and would be curious what people think of the team’s struggles this series.
