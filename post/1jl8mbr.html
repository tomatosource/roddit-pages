Chayula Monk flames are shared

The ascendancy skill “Into the Breach” generates flames on the ground which can be walked over to gain a temporary buff. If you party with another Chayula monk, both generate flames and can pick up each other’s flames. I’ve never seen this since I’ve never seen two Chayula monks in a party before.
