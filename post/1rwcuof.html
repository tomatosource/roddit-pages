I think this game might be too complicated for me

I really enjoy this game, and love the visuals in it more than any other ARPG I’ve played but every time I get to the endgame, I am just so confused about what to do. Crafting feels incredibly difficult, I have loot items that seem important but probably aren’t (looking at you Essences), and I just get overwhelmed trying to figure out what to do next. I start a new character, and play to endgame again. Rinse and repeat. I don’t even know why I can’t use more than one Waystone when I click on the endgame map.

I truly envy those of you who understand all the ins and outs of this game. I’m sure it’s amazing.
