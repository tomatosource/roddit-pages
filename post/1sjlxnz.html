A look at the NBA playoff seeding scenarios entering the regular-season finale.

With all 30 teams in action today, there are 10 postseason spots still to be decided.

Eastern Conference Scenarios:

Locked-in Seeds: No. 1 Detroit, No. 2 Boston, No. 3 New York, No. 4 Cleveland.

Atlanta Hawks: Currently the No. 5 Seed. (vs. Miami Heat at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 5 Seed:
    • A win will lock in the No. 5 Seed for Atlanta.
    • A loss AND Orlando win OR Toronto loss.
  • No. 6 Seed: A loss AND Orlando loss AND Toronto win.

Toronto Raptors: Currently the No. 6 Seed. (vs. Brooklyn Nets at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 5 Seed: A win AND losses by Atlanta and Orlando.
  • No. 6 Seed: A loss AND losses by Orlando and Philadelphia.
  • No. 7 Seed: A loss AND wins by either Orlando or Philadelphia (but not both).
  • No. 8 Seed: A loss AND wins by both Orlando and Philadelphia.

Orlando Magic: Currently the No. 7 seed (vs. Boston Celtics at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 6 Seed: A win AND Toronto loss.
  • No. 7 Seed: A win AND Toronto win OR a loss and Philadelphia loss.
  • No. 8 Seed: A loss AND Philadelphia win.

Philadelphia 76ers: Currently the No. 8 Seed. (vs. Milwaukee Bucks at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 6 Seed: A win AND losses by Toronto and Orlando.
  • No. 7 Seed: A win AND a loss by either Toronto or Orlando (but not both).
  • No. 8 Seed: A loss OR with wins by Toronto and Orlando.

Charlotte Hornets: Currently the No. 9 Seed (vs. New York Knicks at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 9 Seed: A win OR Miami loss.
  • No. 10 Seed: A loss AND Miami win.

Miami Heat: Currently the No. 10 Seed. (vs. Atlanta Hawks at 6 PM ET)

  • No. 9 Seed: A win AND Charlotte loss.
  • No. 10 Seed: A loss OR Charlotte win.

Western Conference Scenarios:

Locked-in Seeds: No. 1 Oklahoma City, No. 2 San Antonio, No. 5 Houston, No. 6 Minnesota, No. 7 Phoenix, No. 10 Golden State.

Denver Nuggets: Currently the No. 3 Seed. (vs. San Antonio Spurs at 8:30 PM ET)

  • No. 3 Seed: A win OR Lakers loss.
  • No. 4 Seed: A loss AND Lakers win.

Los Angeles Lakers: Currently the No. 4 Seed. (vs. Utah Jazz at 8:30 PM ET)

  • No. 3 Seed: A win AND Nuggets loss. 
  • No. 4 Seed: A loss OR Nuggets win.

Portland Trail Blazers: Currently the No. 8 Seed. (vs. Sacramento Kings at 8:30 PM ET)

  • No. 8 Seed: A win OR Clippers loss.
  • No. 9 Seed: A loss AND Clippers win.

Los Angeles Clippers: Currently the No. 9 Seed. (vs. Golden State at 8:30 PM ET)

  • No. 8 Seed: A win AND Blazers loss. 
  • No. 9 Seed: A loss OR Blazers win.
