’If You Write a Check for $400 Million…’ - Celtics Star Jaylen Brown Teases Move to Chinese Basketball Association

Despite an early exit in the playoffs, Brown appeared to enjoy his trip to China thoroughly.

He drew attention earlier in June with his “龙布朗” tweet, a Chinese phrase roughly translating to “Long Brown.”

In a recent social media post, Brown jokingly expressed his interest in playing for a Chinese basketball team, such as the Shanghai Sharks.

“Listen, y’all cut the check?” Brown joked when asked how many points he would average for the Sharks.

“Shanghai, I’m outta here. You write a check, $400 million.”
