I filmed a year long behind-the-scenes doc about a British baseball club

Hey! I play for the Birmingham Metalheads, the first team of Birmingham Baseball Club. We’re based right in the middle of England. We’ve existed since 2003, but effectively became a whole new club in 2021. We have three senior teams; The Metalheads, the Bats and (soon) The Warpigs (We’re based in the village where Ozzy Osbourne was born…). We started a women’s program this year, and we also have three age-group teams under the Birmingham GOATS youth program.

We’ve had insane growth since 2021 and I wanted to show some of the work and effort that goes into building a club like ours, so I embarked on filming some of the activities we take part in, and interviewing some of the figures at the club.

It’s called What We Do: A Year With Birmingham Baseball Club (after our motto We Are What We Do). It’s split into four episodes, by season and they’re all up on YouTube.
