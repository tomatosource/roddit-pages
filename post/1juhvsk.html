The jewel socket change gutted off-class/unconventional builds

Jewels have mainly one purpose, facilitate build making by providing bonuses one can’t easily get enough of, or can’t get at all from the tree, due to either being too far away from the desired mods, or the mod not existing on the tree as much as one needs.

Thus, jewels were the primary way to get something like lightning/cold penetration on a titan, or crossbow reload speed on a chronomancer, or shock magnitude on a warbringer without spending 30 points to travel. On top of that, the jewel mod pool is significantly broader than what’s available on gear or the passive tree, making them basically essential for fine-tuning a build or enabling niche mechanics. Nearly every single build I wanted to go for became pretty much impossible to make because I can’t get what I need from the tree, and I don’t have enough jewel sockets to get what I can’t access on the tree.

I know this change was made to reduce player power, I’m not going to argue whether this was necessary after all the nerfs but please reconsider this change for the sake of build diversity. Don’t turn this into Diablo 3 where people are only allowed to play dev-sanctioned builds. It will get very stale, very quickly.

One more thing, jewels were one of the only resources we had access to in order to scale our builds into endgame, and they’re mostly gone. What do I even do after I have a good weapon and good jewelries to scale my damage? A slow paced game where getting to max level is relatively difficult should provide many more ways to scale our damage and defenses, but we barely have any, while Path of Exile 1 has tons of chase uniques, eldritch implicits, influenced and elevated gear, synth gear, cluster jewels, more jewel sockets, better corruption implicits, tons of build defining uniques, etc.

I know it’s still EA, but this decision makes an already shallow game even duller from a theorycrafting perspective.

tldr,

The jewel socket nerf severely limits build diversity, especially for off-class or unconventional setups, by restricting access to critical mods and scaling options that can’t be obtained from the passive tree or gear. This change also makes endgame progression feel stale, as there are fewer ways to scale damage, defenses and utility without the flexibility jewels provided.
