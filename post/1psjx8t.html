The Chicago Bulls are the only team in the NBA that has: a winning record vs teams .500 and above + a losing record vs teams below .500

After tonight’s game vs the Hawks, the Bulls are 9-6 against teams .500 and above while they are 4-9 against teams below .500.

A weird stat for the Bulls who have lost to the Pelicans twice, Pacers twice, Nets, and Hornets in the past weeks while also having wins against teams like Nuggets, Knicks, Pistons, Magic in the season.
