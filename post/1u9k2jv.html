Born to play basketball, Jalen Brunson.

Brunson has pretty much won every major American basketball reward that matters since highschool. He is already one of the most decorated basketball players of all time accounting for separate awards. Guy is the ultimate winner and should never be doubted again. Of course he will always be small and un-athletic, but as a basketball player there are few who can measure up to him. And considering how great the EU players are now, dominating the league, I think American basketball enjoyers should really get behind the guy.

Oh and if you haven’t noticed yet, he’s a great fucking guy. Professional, high IQ, funny & a loving father. He is the working mans basketball player and if the NBA was a genuin organization who cared about the future of the league, they would make him the face and ambassador of the NBA. Not Anthony Edwards, not Victor Wembanyama and not Shai.

Brunson embodies what an athlete should be: Works harder than anyone and everyone. Has one goal in mind and never gives up. If he gets knocked down he comes back better. If you doubt him he works harder. Brunson is HIM.

Everyone is better than Jalen Brunson until its time to better than Jalen Brunson.
