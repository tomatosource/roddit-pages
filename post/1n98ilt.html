[BasketNews] Valanciunas on a conversation he had with Jokic: “We talked about horses”

Jonas Valanciunas and Nikola Jokic will be teammates in Denver from next season, and both of them currently compete in the EuroBasket.

Although their paths haven’t crossed this summer at EuroBasket, Valanciunas revealed during his media availability before the start of the Round of 16 that he has already spoken with Jokic about the upcoming season.

“Yeah, we talked about horses,” Valanciunas joked. “He gave me some advice.”
