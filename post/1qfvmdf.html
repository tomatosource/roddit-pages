A simple recommendation for how to enjoy PoE2 much more

Make each season about 1 craft.

I don’t even play seasons, I’m just a tired dad playing on standard.

Regardless, the seasonal updates rebalance the game and sometimes brick my characters. I’ve learned to remake when a season changes. it’s more fun than salvaging an old character. It only takes a couple days to level, but a few months before the next rebalance. What makes this process WAY MORE FUN is going into this cyclical experience deciding that each time you are going to invest your eventual resources into 1 craft. Usually a weapon for your class, but eventually it could be something which is more easily shared across characters.

Top results from this process:

- Mastering crafting as a whole is too complicated, but just learning to craft 1 thing isn’t. There aren’t a ton of mods, so you figure out the decision tree, edit your item filter for good bases, and repeat it dozens of times over the months. The odds are basically guaranteed that you will end up with a pretty solid craft.

- Selling even the failed crafts end up becoming the highest income you make in a season

- Learning good item bases and the meta eventually empowers you to make good market decisions, I’ve sold magic items for 20div

- A silly amount of pride in your showcase of crafted items you accumulate over time

- Last but not least: If you feel particularly bold, you may decide to vaal the end result. If you spent months making one best item it probably is valued at a hundred+ div, and if you end up hitting an extra socket you can launch yourself into super wealth territory
