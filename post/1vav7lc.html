I think many people fail to properly appreciate Bill Russell’s dominance because the way he dominated basketball can not be understood through the modern lens of the NBA.

The impact a generational rim protector could have in the 1960s simply isn’t replicable in the modern game, where spacing and outside shooting have dramatically reduced the influence of any single defender. As a result many people see Russell’s meager offensive numbers in comparison to other ATG’s and overlook the fact that his defense was the greatest individual advantage any player has ever provided.

I don’t think anything sums that up better than Russell leading his teams to 3 titles in 3 years they were the worst offensive teams in the NBA.
