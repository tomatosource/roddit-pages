It feels like we should disable Abyss VFX

I know this has been brought up a lot, but squeaky wheel and all that.

Abyss should have all of the shader VFX disabled until they can sort out the performance issues.

When Abyss mixes with basically any other effect, it turns the game into a slideshow. I can’t move, I can’t use abilities, I can’t use potions. I sit and watch my character die at 5 FPS unless I feel like ALT+F4’ing. The vast majority of my end game deaths are to this.

I have never had issues like this in past leagues, and I don’t have this issue without Abyss nearby. I know it was probably exciting to hit “Select All” on the Abyss shader effects, but it has made the league mechanic game breakingly unplayable any time it is on screen.
