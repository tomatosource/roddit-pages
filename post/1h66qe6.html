Prediction: One Of Our Unscheduled Games Is A Home Game Vs. Detroit

Edit: Discussion started on this post by u/johnnybarbs92

Now that we are eliminated from the NBA Cup, I think a lot of us might be wondering who we are going to play in those 2 unscheduled games. Unfortunately, the scheduling for teams that don’t make it to the elimination rounds is unclear and not straightforward.

If you are curious here’s kind of a breakdown on how they make the schedule.

Why do I think the game is a home game vs. Detroit?

In theory, you are supposed to play every team outside of your division, (and in your conference) 4 times in 3 different seasons in a 5 year span. In 2 seasons of those 5, you are supposed to play them 3 times.

Past 5 years, we have played Detroit 4 times in 2 seasons and are scheduled to play them 3 times this year. This means that one of those unscheduled games is one taken from our typical rotation vs. Detroit.

We are scheduled to play Detroit twice on the road and once at home, which is why my guess is that we have a home game against them.

So what about the 2nd unscheduled game?

Well, by the same logic we would be scheduled to play Orlando, HOWEVER, they advanced and it’s quite literally impossible for it to be them.

There are only 2 other teams we play 3 times in our conference that didn’t advance: Indy and Washington. If Detroit is to be a home game, then it’d make sense that the other game is an away game. To balance the schedule then, the 2nd team might be Indy on the road, as we play them twice at home and Washington twice on the road already.

Idk though, playing either Indy or Washington might throw another team’s schedule out of wack.

Detroit seems to be the one team where we have the spot basically allotted for them already. My guess is the other team is Indy, but idk
