Is it only me who hates the campaign?

I have played 3 seasons in poe 2 reaching 92 level on all of them and I love the endgame and the economy sim feeling, I generally love economy sims. However, I hate the campaign, this will probably sound noobish but it’s a bit too hard, it’s too tense for someone who wants to chill until max level. Too much mechanic dodging and so on. I don’t understand what’s the point of this. Poe 2 must be the only game that I have played where the campaign is harder than the endgame. Once you reach max level with a proper build you delete everything in a instant, I don’t even know if there is a challenge to overcome since I usually don’t stick around for too long to see it.

I hate the rolling mechanic, I don’t know if poe 1 was like this, I never played it, I don’t like action games and there is too much action element on poe2 early on.
