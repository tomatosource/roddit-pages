How Chonk’s Darkness Actually Works (the details may be better than you’d think for all the hate it gets)

How Acolyte of Chayula’s Darkness actually works:

  • Each hit or dot tick you take becomes a unique darkness reservation instance for that amount of damage taken.

  • Every darkness reservation instance falls off on its own 5-second timer.

  • Because of how these reservation instances work, Darkness effectively regens itself in combat. It allows you to absorb ~2000 damage taken over any 5-second rolling window before life or es or MoM are touched at all.

  • Energy shield recharge can start while darkness is absorbing damage. In any situation where you have darkness available but your es is damaged, es recharge will start and not be interrupted as long as darkness is taking the damage. Which can happen really often.

  • For taking large hits, clearly the popular alternative to darkness is high es, with or without ci. However es recharge delay was heavily nerfed in 0.5, giving darkness a relative buff since it regens in combat.

  • EDIT: as others said, in 0.5, the new absent amulet base type can allows one spirit buff that uses no spirit. So chonks can have a single spirit skill. The 100-spirit buffs like CoC or trinity will be chase items, but lower spirit ones might be cheap.

So why did GGG make darkness such a huge investment?

Because darkness gives you ~2000 extra life that regens in combat, lets you recharge es while taking damage, and stacks directly on top of life+es+MoM+runic ward.
