Fixing Attack time on Maces

A lot of people have been asking for attack times on maces to be removed or scalable with attack speed for quite a while now. It’s definitely very annoying to deal with, but I thought there was a better way to fix this issue. It’s technically already somewhere in GGG’s build of the game, so it definitely seems possible to implement. If you remember the Druid showcase from a while back with animation weaving of skills. Essentially, for a slam, the bear raises its body up and then slams down (that skill likely has an attack time like all other slams). But you can use a claw swipe skill to attack quickly, which will leave the bear in a standing position, and you can follow up a slam from there with a much lower attack time as your position is in an intermediate stance of the slam animation.

I think mace skills should be able to use the same kind of animation weaving. Think of a fast strike skill that finishes with you being in the air for a short interval. What if you could immediately follow up with Sunder with a much lower attack time (maybe even none), as jumping takes up most of that animation time? Now, what if you could instead follow it up with the second slam of Rolling Slam or the big slam of Stampede? This keeps the identity of slams individually as these slow, chunky skills with long windups but also heavily incentivizes combo gameplay. I think this will feel a lot better than just cutting down attack times on slams, making them like just slower strike skills with an AoE.

One problem I can see happening here is that you need to further classify slams into which positions they can be started from (in the air, on the ground with hands raised up, etc.) and communicate that to the players. But I don’t think this will be too hard, considering the limited number of slams right now and the skill tutorials they have with videos and everything. Reaching further into this idea, another thing that they might do is have support gems that cause certain skills to finish up in those positions. This might be harder as you would need to change the skill animation, but in my opinion, it sounds like the perfect kind of support gem for PoE2 with no actual damage multipliers but a very transformative effect. You can afford to use it as you have a lot of gem slots, and the payoff is worth it through combos.

Now that I’m thinking about it, animation weaving also seems like something that melee could benefit from in general. So far, GGG has tried to incentivize combos through mechanical interactions, but combo-based melee gameplay already exists in many games, and you really only need the feeling of one skill naturally flowing into another to make it feel good. PoE2 melee skills, even on the monk, do feel slightly disconnected at the end of one and the start of another, even when they are obviously intended to combo together due to their mechanics. This might be too much to do, considering how many skills there are (and will be), but the more of this we have, the better melee will feel to play.
