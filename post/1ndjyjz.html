Reddit Investigators, I need your help: A missing book from Uncle Dennis

So with all of the Kawhi drama I’ve been so fascinated with the character of Uncle Dennis.

What I found interesting was I legitimiately had no clue what he looked like, for some reason I was imagining a dude in a hoodie making all the demands, but after many differenet google searches I finally was able to find some pictures and reverse searches on him.

I came across this website called Protocol International which Dennis Roberston (Uncle Dennis) co-founded with his wife. What I find funny is there is very little on what the “Why” of the program is, but it seems that they offer many different training programs and even a “Protocol Academy” that trains what I think are aspiring agents and clients to learn how to better negotiate? I honestly don’t know, but it reeks of those schemes that are done across hotel lobbies that all of our uncles have fallen for at one point in their lives.

https://protocolinternational.online/

Here is a great excerpt about Unclde Dennis on the website:

“Dennis’ involvement in an athlete’s career and ‘Off-Court’ development, brand and strategy have become pricelimperative to their business success. Dennis’ designing of programs equip athletes with the necessary skills and strategies to meet the opportunities and challenges his clients face in a variety of social and business situations. His experience, training, expertise as an athlete, banker, business manager, negotiator and coach equips his clients with a priceless advantage of having a business manager and an ‘Uncle’ in their corner.”

Here is where it gets really interesting, I clicked on the “books” tab and there is a book that apparently he wrote called “Rules of Engagement: How to Win at Dining with Clients and Other Important People”. The image of the book says its written by Dennis R. Robertson

https://preview.redd.it/401izjhebdof1.png?width=920&format=png&auto=webp&s=56f27b2d57005e374fc683b0afb622e326c90a6b

HOWEVER:

I cannot find this version of the book anywhere. In fact, the link right next to to the book leads you to this amazon link for a book very similar named, but written by his wife:

https://www.amazon.com/Rules-Engagement-Dining-Clients-Important/dp/0984447555/ref=sr_1_3?ie=UTF8&qid=1514051306&sr=8-3&keywords=cheryl+walker+robertson

What is hilarious is that the 4 images of the book are replaced with equally generic stock images, but it seems like its less about Sport Atheletes and more about general “Mastering the art of the deal”

I of course ordered the book, but very quickly realized that a Kindle version does not exist. I looked at the sample of the book and it such clear noncense, I also find it hilarious that under the author Cheryl Walker-Robertson, it says “Certified Protocol and Etiquette Expert”. I wonder where she earned that certification.

Anyway, I really really want to read the original version of “Rules and Engagement” that was written by Uncle Dennis.

I truly believe this could be a hugely important piece to understanding how Uncle Dennis masters the art of the deal, and whether we can find the smoking gun in all of this.
