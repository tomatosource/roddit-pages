NBA draft prediction (if we do draft and not trade)

First round:

- Christian Anderson - Texas Tech Red Raiders (6’2 165): 18.5⁄3.6⁄7.4⁄1.5 Really good on both ends and can run an offense. I’m sure he has his flaws, but nothing concerning. NBA Comps: Tyus Jones, Mike Conley, Kyle Lowry

- Dailyn Swain - Texas Longhorns (6’7 200): 17⁄7.5⁄3.6⁄1.6 I think for someone of his age he has a high IQ. He’s good at collapsing defenses, getting into the paint and finishing through contact. He can run PnR as a wing and is a good passer for a wing. Elite defender with a 6’10 wingspan. Shooting is decent. He can be a little turnover prone and has an inconsistent jumper. NBA Comps: Josh Hart, Naji Marshall, Andre Iguodala

Second round:

- Jaden Bradley - Arizona Wildcats (6’3 185): 13.3⁄3.4⁄4.4⁄1.4. Good at running an offense. He has good bursts and strength. Smart at offense, takes good shots and prefers 2’s over 3’s. Very strong defender who can play make. Can’t create separation like NBA guards, not a good rim finisher or consistent shooter. I don’t know if he’s really an NBA level player. NBA Comps: TJ McConnell, Tre Jones

- Otega Oweh - Kentucky Wildcats (6’5 210): 18.6⁄4.8⁄2.7⁄1.8 Really good downhill attacker whose elite in transition. A true defensive stopper with a “dawg” mentality. Offensively his jumper has improved, outside shooting is a question and being a playmaker. NBA Comps: Josh Okogie, Norman Powell or like a mini Jimmy Butler

- Trevon Brazile - Arkansas Razorbacks (6’9 215): 13⁄7.3⁄1.6⁄1.6⁄1.6. Crazy lob threat who can run the floor. Great help defender which is how he gets a lot of steals and blocks. He can shoot but not a sniper. He can’t create much offense on his own and passing is something he needs to work on. He has a ACL injury way back in 2022 (not sure if that matters since it was a while ago, but he performs well) NBA Comp: Jonathan Isaac with a jumper

Undrafted:

- William Kyle III - Syracuse Orange (6’9 215): 8.4⁄7.1/1/1/2.5blks. He has a 44 inch vertical, great shot blocker, rebounder, versatile defender, rim protector, he can score around the rim. Weakness is that he doesn’t have much offense, just scores around the rim. NBA Comp: Rob Williams

- Ernest Udeh Jr. - Miami Hurricanes (6’11 250): 6.7⁄9.2/1stl/1.4blks. A defensive anchor, lob threat and very physical. He has no offense. NBA Comp: Bismack Biyombo

- Aaron Nkrumah - TSU Tigers (6’6 175): 17.7⁄5.5/3/2.8 Great at attacking the rim, floor runner, strong versatile defender who can also rebound. Can’t shoot. NBA Comp: Dyson Daniels or Herb Jones

- Quadir Copeland - NC State Wolfpack (6’6 203): 14⁄3.6⁄6.5⁄1.8 NBA. A big PG who can see over others. Elite downhill player, great defender whose also a great playmaker and energetic. Not the best shooter. Comp: Andre Miller but a Shaun Livingston frame

(Feel free for suggestions/comments or to add anyone. Btw I know Jayden Quaintance will get drafted, but he’s more of a wildcard just due to his injury people have him all over from 20 - 30)
