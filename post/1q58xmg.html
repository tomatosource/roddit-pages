Were there any mid ranked, super physically tooled, high-strikeout, “all or nothing” prospects that actually had sustained success?

I’m not talking about prospects like Nick Kurtz who have a lot of strikeouts in their hitting profile but still undoubtedly have the power to overcome it. I’m thinking of prospects like Lazaro Montes, who have 80-grade physical tools but sky-high strikeout rates even in the low minors. Prospects where scouts are always saying “if he can shorten the swing, he has the potential to be an all star level DH”.

This might be a really narrow criteria but I can’t really think of anyone.
