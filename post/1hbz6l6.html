Devin Booker is averaging his highest 3PA of his career and lowest 2PA since his rookie season

It’s no secret that with KD out, the Suns and Booker have been struggling. The Suns have only won 3 of their last 10 games. But the change in Booker’s play has been very interesting.

Booker is shooting 7.7 threes per game (highest ever) at a 33.5% clip, which is his lowest since 2018-19, when the Suns finished last in the West.

He’s attempting 10.7 twos per game, his lowest since his rookie season in 2015-16, when the Suns finished second-to-last in the West.

He’s also at 24.9 PPG, 43.2 FG%, and 50.2% 2P%, his lowest in each of those stats since 2017-18, when the Suns also finished last in the West.

He’s doing all of this in 37 MPG, his highest ever. In Points per 36, he’s fallen from 10th last season to 46th.

Here’s hoping things turn around for him and the Suns.
