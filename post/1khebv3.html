Not a Celtics Fan, But Tatum and Brown Settling for Threes Is Killing Them

I’m not a Celtics fan, I’m a Lakers fan—but I was watching the game and I just don’t understand why the Celtics shoot and attempt so many threes when they have two of the best players in Tatum and Brown who are so good at getting to the rim at will. I mean, you saw it at the end of the game—Tatum just blew by Mitchell Robinson like it was nothing. So why don’t they do that all the time?

Jason Tatum has the skills to be the best player in the world. He’s 6’9”, can defend, pass, rebound, he’s a great shot-maker with high basketball IQ—and yet he doesn’t fully take advantage of that. Instead, he consistently settles for contested threes.

It’s honestly frustrating to watch, even as someone who doesn’t root for them. These last two games? It’s on them. They’re living and dying by the three, and they’re dying right now. When you have players who can collapse a defense anytime they want, why keep chucking jumpers?

This feels like something the coaching staff needs to address, but at the same time, Tatum and Brown have to recognize it themselves too. The potential is right there—they just have to stop settling.
