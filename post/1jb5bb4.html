Gaming Laptop futureproof for PoE2 for 5+ carefree years?

Exile! My gaming laptop died, probably from heat. It was nice for PoE1 back then, but now I need something new.

What I learned from my previous Laptop is that heat is your enemy, and it shows you what is truly nightmare.

I’m looking for a Laptop as described in the title, and here is what I would like to have to it:

  • around 17” (+/-)

  • good contrast display (because I don’t see very well and i wear glasses… contrast is more important than brightness bc the light gives me red eyes if too high.)

  • nice colors (i also play games like Genshin every now and then to have something cute in between going through hell, so i want vibrant colors. I looked on the screen of an Asus model recently, and I found it too cold to look at.)

  • Cooling should be good enough to not get heat related problems, like burn on my “w” middlefinger or dying while playing in the 10th hour nonstop in coming summer…

  • The case should be sturdy enough where the keyboard is embedded and the area around it.

  • The Laptop should run for some years without problems… hopefully.

  • My budget is around 1k - 2.2k tops.
