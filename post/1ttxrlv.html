Not being able to unallocate Atlas points causes there to be some weird traps. It feels bad.

The problem here is that some Atlas nodes are not always desirable effects to have, but if they don’t have a choice attached, you’re just stuck with it. You can never unallocate them.

I’ll give some examples:

  1. Breach - Frantic Invasion. “Your Breaches have 100% increased chance to contain Vruun.” Sounds fine, yeah? Well guess what - if Vruun doesn’t spawn, you instead get 4+ rares to finish out the Breach. You can get tablets that add even more - with 3 breach tablets, you can literally get up to 10 rares spawning at the end of every breach. All juiced to hell by the atlas tree. And if Vruun spawns, none of those rares spawn, even the ones added by the tablets.

Every time Vruun spawns in my map, I lose a ton of value. I wish I could unallocate this node.

  1. Specialized Containers - I’ve been avoiding this node because I worry it’s a trap. “Strongboxes can roll additional prefixes that change the types of items they contain.” Right now, the pool of prefix modifiers on strongboxes is tiny - basically always I hit quant + rarity which is… what you want on a strongbox. I don’t want to clutter up my prefix pool with “drops an extra barya” or whatever.

Sure maybe the prefixes are awesome but what if they’re not? If this node is “bad” and I allocate it - keeping in mind it’s literally 4 nodes away from the very start of the atlas tree, easy mistake to make - I’m just literally stuck with it for the whole league?

I should be able to turn off atlas nodes whenever I want.

I shouldn’t have to be this careful about allocating atlas points, especially not when I have to farm five fucking hundred of them, mostly one point at a time.
