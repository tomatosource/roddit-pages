A really, really great breakdown of the Ace Bailey situation from Sarah Todd, one of Utah’s best local beat reporters. Don’t believe every narrative from ESPN

https://www.deseret.com/sports/2025/06/27/ace-bailey-utah-jazz-rookie-omar-cooper-issues/

By all accounts, Ace is a great kid who’s excited to play in the NBA. He probably hasn’t had dreams about putting on a Jazz jersey since he was 5, but like… who does? If the Jazz only drafted those players, we’d probably have like six dudes on our roster.

People seem so ready and willing to look for negativity, which is terribly unfair to Ace on what should be one of the best times of his life. Regardless of what his career looks like moving forward, Ace, like all other 58 picks, should be celebrated for making it this far in the first place.

EDIT: Looking at the responses in the comments, I feel the need to clarify. Ace’s pre-draft strategy of attempting to steer toward a preferred set of destinations did not work. That is clear as day, and perfectly worthy of criticism. I’m sure that Ace’s camp would also agree that this backfired–no way that these conversations were how he wanted to have his client talked about after the draft.

What concerns me more than anything is the people who are so quick to judge his character. I keep seeing these side-by-side pictures of Khaman Maluach and Ace shaking hands with Adam Silver. Maluach was clearly emotional, with tears in his eyes. Dude is a stud, and a great story (hyped for the Suns btw). Ace did not have that look.

Now, people are using a few seconds of footage and combining it with his camp’s strategy to paint Ace as an entitled, arrogant young man. But based on feedback from all of those who are closest to him, and the statements that actually come out of his mouth, that appears to be simply untrue. His teammates, coaches, and executives that he’s met with have pretty much all agreed that Ace Bailey the person is an energetic, fun, humble dude, who also comes with the natural immaturities of an 18-year old.

Criticize Ace Bailey’s camp all you want. It’s deserved. But please don’t confuse Ace Bailey’s camp with Ace Bailey the human being.
