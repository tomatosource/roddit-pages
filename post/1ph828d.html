Staves (staff) still worse than wand/focus for spell casters.

Staves got buffs:

  • increased spell/elemental dmg affix for about ~25-30%
  • perfect sorcery essence can’t roll +3 on foci (not a direct buff, but now has same amount of levels as wand/foci)
  • magnitude/desecrate affixes buff

But with wands/foci you still have:

  • energy shield (which helpful for early endgame and for mana stacking providing more raw mana with EB)
  • more cast speed
  • more critical hit chance for spells
  • more critical spell damage bonus
  • better desecrate prefix mods for wands making it easier to get double increases
  • resistances can be useful for early game
  • sanctify potential getting more +lvls

Ultimately, by choosing a staff, you get a slightly higher increases damage as an advantage, which is why it is still very difficult to find reasons to use it instead of a wand and focus.

I think we need more buffs, because losing survivability for a conditional increased additional damage is not worth it.

This doesn’t necessarily mean that staves should have significantly more damage, but perhaps they need additional mods that will differentiate the staff for the better, for example, having +level of all spells (or elemental) instead of separately for each element, so that by the time the Druid comes out, it would be possible to be an elemental sorcerer without choosing something specific.
