[NBA] Dillon Brooks, Brandon Miller named NBA Players of the Week

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/nba-players-week-dillon-brooks-brandon-miller

Phoenix Suns guard-forward Dillon Brooks and Charlotte Hornets guard-forward Brandon Miller have been named the NBA Players of the Week for Week 15 of the 2025-26 season.

Dillon Brooks (PHX): 28.8 PPG; 4.5 RPG; 2.8 APG; 54.5 3FG%; 3-1 team record

Brandon Miller (CHA): 26.3 PPG; 5.8 RPG; 2.3 APG; 50.0 3FG%; 4-0 team record

This is the first time both Brooks and Miller have earned Player of the Week honors.
