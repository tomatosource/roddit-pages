Crafting tutorial for perfect Talisman

Hi,

I think i figured out how to craft a perfect Talisman for Druid:

Crit Talisman Steps:

  1. Get a Talisman with Crit Chance
  2. Add 2 random Mods
  3. Add desecrate mod unrevealed to increase chance for crit fracture
  4. Fracture and hope for crit (rinse reapeat until hit)
  5. Remove all mods until 2
  6. Chaos spam until t1 phys % (highest level and it blocks omen)
  7. Add random prefix
  8. Omen chaos spam until t1 flat phys
  9. Add 2 random suffix
  10. Omen + essence spam until you get both +5 skill ans onslaught (if failed remove suffix mods and repeat)
  11. Desecrate for Fire Damage

If you dont wat crit chance You can fracture Fire damage and later desecrate for Attack speed.

Maybe not the best Method. Feedback would be great :)
