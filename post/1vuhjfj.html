[OC] Which NBA Players Feast on Bad Defenses and Fall Off Against Good Ones?

I compared every 2025–26 player averaging at least 15 points per game against the league’s top-10 and bottom-10 defenses.

Teams were classified using their final full-season defensive rating. The middle ten defenses were excluded, and players needed at least eight games against both groups.

Top-10 defenses: OKC, DET, SAS, BOS, TOR, HOU, NYK, MIN, ATL, PHX
Bottom-10 defenses: WAS, UTA, SAC, MIL, BKN, NOP, IND, MEM, CHI, DEN

Source for team defensive ratings: https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2026.html
