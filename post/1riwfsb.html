[OC] After Ted Williams: The 16 Players Who Hit .400 Across an 80‑Game Stretch

Since Ted Williams last hit .400 in 1941, these are the 16 instances where someone hit at least .400 in an 80-game stretch at any point in a single regular season. Any surprises? A lot of these guys are legendary batting average hitters, with the exception of two that really surprise me. None are really that recent which makes it a little more fun to look back on.

EDIT: I was accidentally removing duplicates. We can add another: Tony Gwynn from 4⁄21-7⁄22 in 1994 hit .401!!!
