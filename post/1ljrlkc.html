Something Crazy is Coming

So we lost two of our top 7 guys with basically nothing coming back. Additionally, by the time Tatum is playing meaningful games again, Horford will probably be gone or not impactful.

So we are down 3 of our top 7… and we have no meaningful way to replace them except internal growth or getting lucky with a late first or second round pick this year or next.

But does anyone really believe that Brad’s plan is to be mediocre in a year and a half when Tatum is fully back?

I wasn’t an advocate of this before now, but looking at the return on KP and Holiday, and the landscape of the league… I don’t see how we are competing for titles in two years unless we do something drastic.
