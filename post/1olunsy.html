Road teams have clinched the World Series 10 of the 11 years

Interesting stat here, but since 2014, only the 2022 Astros have clinched the WS title on their home field whereas every other winner clinched it on the road.

2014 - Giants clinched WS in Kansas City (game 7)

2015 - Royals clinched WS in Queens (game 5)

2016 - Cubs clinched WS in Cleveland (game 7)

2017 - Astros clinched WS in LA (game 7)

2018 - Red Sox clinched WS in LA (game 5)

2019 - Nats clinched WS in Houston (game 7)

2020 - Dodgers clinched WS in Arlington (game 6*)

2021 - Braves clinched WS in Houston (game 6)

2022 - Astros clinched WS at home (game 6)

2023 - Rangers clinched WS in Arizona (game 5)

2024 - Dodgers clinched WS in the Bronx (game 5)

*= bubble year

I wonder what the reason behind this is? Is it because there’s less pressure to clinch when you’re playing on the road? Is it because the 2-3-2 format has allowed the better team to clinch in game 5 on the road which seems to have happened a few times over the past decade? Is this just a random coincidence?

We’ll see tonight if the Dodgers continue this streak of road teams clinching the WS or if the Blue Jays can become only the second team to win it at home since 2014.
