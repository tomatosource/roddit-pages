[OC] EuroBasket Semi Finals Preview & Storylines To Watch For NBA fans this Friday - Giannis Chases Greek Legacy, Franz & Dennis Schroeder cement Germany as a Basketball Superpower, Sengun’s Incoming Breakout Season, and Lauri Markkanen’s push for Finnish history with a future NBA phenom

#Intro + Thoughts On The Quarter Finals:

The Quarter-Finals have come and gone and we are set up for what may be one of the greatest semi finals ever in EuroBasket with some JUICY storylines that I’ve outlined below. Before we examine those, here are thoughts on some of the teams who lost the quarterfinals:

  • If you are a Luka fan you may be walking away sad that Slovenia couldn’t get the W over Germany but you have to hang your head high knowing that he looks to be back in MVP shape. His offense is second to none in the world and the Lakers could be a real title dark horse this season. This man is basically the greatest international scorer ever. Slovenia easily could have beat Germany today and they will be back with a better team soon. Be scared world.

  • Same should be said for Georgia as they should hold their head high after going down big to Finland and then putting up a fight to nearly comeback today. Raptors fans should be excited for whats to come for Mamu as a versatile wing player in the NBA. This was the greatest run of theirs in the history of the tournament and they should be proud of it!

  • Lithuania had a great gameplan and coaching for Giannis, Jonas Valanciunas had a beast of a game and fought hard to come close at one point but Greece looks great with their renewed shooting around Giannis. We may see the best to come from Giannis as he takes on Sengun next on Friday.

#Semi-Final Storylines:

Giannis Antetokounmpo chases his first ever medal for Greece.

  • The fit with the roster around him has always been clunky due its lack of shooting but this year seems to be a little different! For context Greece is historically a very good international team and Giannis is haunted to some degree by the bar those old legends set. Though none of them have reached the heights Giannis has reached as an NBA champion and MVP and one of the 20-30 greatest NBA players of all time. So Giannis is kind of fighting history here and NBA fans should realize this means so much to Giannis and this will be his last shot at a medal in his prime as a player. Right now he needs one more win in 2 games to at least get a bronze medal but he really wants gold and he has to go through Alperen Sengun and Türkiye to do it on Friday at 2pm EST, don’t miss it!

Franz Wagner and Dennis Schroeder look to cement Germany as the top dog in Europe & Dennis as one of the great international players of all time

  • After winning World Cup MVP and gold, if Dennis gets the Gold again ESPN’s Tim Bontemps said he will make the Hall of Fame on the Hoop Collective Podcast this week. That sounds pretty crazy on the surface but Dennis is in rare air as an international player. If he wins tournament MVP he would be the only person to do that in both EuroBasket and the Fiba World Cup in the 21st century besides international legends Dirk Nowitzki and Pau Gasol. After losing to Serbia in the Bronze medal game at the Olympics last year and narrowly escaping Slovenia today in a game where Dennis played poorly they are going to be motivated to come back blazing and finish this tourney strong. Finland is gonna have their work cut out for them.

Alperen Sengun’s tournament MVP run & Incoming NBA Leap.

  • Turkey has a history of great basketball players and teams but has had a down period the last decade until now. Sengun has reversed course and is putting them back on the map as a true threat. He looks to be the favorite for tournament MVP and is the youngest player ever to drop a triple double in the HISTORY of EuroBasket. He looks improved as a jump shooter, defender, his athleticism and endurance is improved and as a result he has fresher legs in the post at the end of games even though they run most of their offense through him. At only 23 years old this guy has already outplayed Porzingis & Jokic head to head in this tournament, is Giannis next on his hit list? If I’m a Rockets fan im paying very close attention to how he plays against Greece and Giannis Friday as they have several NBA caliber defenders inside including the Greek Freak himself.

Finland Chases Their Greatest Win in History

  • These guys are the biggest underdog left and it has truly been a magical Cinderella story. Lauri Markkanen is an international all timer already. Miikka Murrinnen is a young phenomenon nicknamed “Slim Jesus” who will be drafted in 2027 likely in the top 5. It’s hard not to root for these guys. If they can overcome a titan like Germany on Friday, this will be arguably the greatest win in the history of Finnish Sports and would increase visibility for the sport in a pretty unprecedented manner. They’ve already done the impossible beating Serbia to get here, can they do it again?
