[OC] Ranking The Top Games & Players To Watch Today In Summer League

Summer League is in full swing and we have some fun games Incoming today! Here is a ranking and overview for any of my hoops junkies that wants a rundown for the games. A few of them promise to have real NBA ripple effects:

1. Pelicans - T Wolves (3:30pm ET)

Pelicans: Jeremiah Fears / Derik Queen - Pelicans need these guys to be really good or there is going to be so much league wide slander around them for months into the season really after trading an unprotected 2026 pick. They need to make a statement early.

Yves missi needs to show something too or the Pelicans center rotation is going to be Kevon Looney and a prayer.

Wolves: This is a Sneaky good team with Terrence Shannon, Rob Dillingham, Joan Beringer, and Jaylen Clark. If these guys play well it will be a really good sign for Minnesota’s season as they need these guys for the rotation. Jamal Cain too should give them more former nba firepower too. Fun Dark horse tourney winner.

2. OKC - Brooklyn (5:30pm ET)

OKC: Nikola Topic / Ajay Mitchell - which of these two will add to the thunders embarrassment of riches? They could be potential legit swing pieces on a dynasty which fascinates me as right now the main Thunder weakness is a third ball handler off the bench which both of these guys have already flashed that they could be capable of real things there.

Nets: The whole nets 1st round of the draft - I’m high on every single player the nets took besides Egor Demin and have Danny Wolf and Nolan Traore in my top 10 on my big board and want to see them kick butt. I am extremely low on Demin so curious to see how it works with all these strange passer types on the floor too. Are the nets geniuses or idiots? Time to decide based off this one game!

3. Lakers - Dallas (8pm ET)

Dallas: Some might put this #1 but I think the above teams have much more variability in terms of the players on them for their careers so that is why I’m curious to see them perform more. Cooper Flagg can go 0-18 today and I’ll still see him as a future all star. Still it goes to show it’s a fun day of hoops if this is third, Flagg is gonna be a hall of famer and this will be fun to see him win over the laker crowd in Vegas.

Lakers: For Knecht and Bronny I’m rooting for them to carve out roles in the league. Both are looking iffy and this game can change a lot in their perception league wide.

4. Sacramento - Orlando (7:30pm ET)

Orlando: Jase Richardson - can he show that he shouldn’t have slid and can he out skill guys in the summer league? If he doesn’t I’ll be worried about him a ton with his size.

Tristan De Silva also needs to prove he can play as an off ball shooter this season in the rotation to help cement the magic offense.

Sacramento: Devin Carter & Nique Clifford should run things for the kings. Devin Carter should be on the radar for tournament mvp. If both of them disappoint the Kangz slander will be on the menu.

5. Spurs - 76ers (10pm ET)

No Dylan Harper or VJ Edgecomb today so checkout Carter Bryant on the Spurs for a potential OG Anunoby type player. Otherwise I don’t blame you for going to bed early on the east coast. Major bummer but apparently Dylan will play Saturday against Cooper Flagg so mark your calendar for that!

Games I wouldn’t really recommend unless you’re a fan of either team:

Denver - Milwaukee

Cleveland - pacers
