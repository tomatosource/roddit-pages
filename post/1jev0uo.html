Temporalis After 1000hours grinding

After 1057 hours grinding this absolutely magnificent game, i acquired the holy grail of this game.
Having 6 chars leveled to 96-97lvl in order to keep the freshnesh of the grinding alive i must admit that it has been a blast. Its time for a break till the next patch hits.

All that said, i have come to a conclusion. The speed and fun that Temporalis brings to the table, combined with the breathtaking worlds and animations that the game offers should be obtainable at a way earlier stage at one’s progression. And i am not talking about temporalis in specific or any other specific piece of gear.

Its all about the pacing of the game. Its simply a different game on a different gear when movement and backtracking moves out the window.

The developers must really consider uping the base speed of every class to at least the speed of having the movement shrine. It will multiplicative increase the fun and incentive to play.

Just a piece of feedback from someone with countless hours on ARPGS for over 20years.

Amazing game, HUGE ROOM for improvement.
