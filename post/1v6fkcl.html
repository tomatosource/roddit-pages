In 1989 Clyde Drexler averaged 27/8/6 with 3 steals a game and didn’t make an all NBA team.

By most advanced metrics, Drexler was a top 15 player that year. He was 6th in win shares, 5th in VORP, 4th in offensive box plus minus.

And while Jordan and Magic had the 1st team spots locked down, Dale Ellis made 3rd team on 28/4/2, and Mark Price made it on 19/3/8.

Sort of an egregious omission looking back.
