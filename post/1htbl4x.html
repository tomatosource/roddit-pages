After last night’s game, Jayson Tatum is now this decade’s leading scorer with 9738 points. He also leads the decade in playoff scoring with 2223. The only other players to lead a decade in both regular season & playoff points: Dolph Schayes (50’s), Kareem Abdul-Jabbar (70’s), Kobe Bryant (2000’s).

per Statmuse (regular season / playoffs)

After scoring 20 last night, JT has taken the #1 spot in total regular season points in the 2020’s with 9,738 points, surpassing Luka Doncic who was the previous leader with 9,722. The only other players with 9,000+ points scored this decade are Giannis Antetokounmpo (9,629) and Nikola Jokic (9,466). Pretty elite company.

In terms of playoff points he’s #1 by a mile with 2,223. Next highest would be Nikola Jokic at 1,861, followed closely by our very own Jaylen Brown (1,831), Jimmy Butler (1,583), and Luka Doncic (1,546).

The last time the same player led a decade in both total regular season and playoff points was in the 2000’s when Kobe achieved the feat with 21,671 and 4,053 points respectively. Before him, Kareem managed it in the 1970’s with 22,102 and 2,382 points, and before him Dolph Schayes did it in the 1950’s with 13,538 and 1,541 points. Again, talk about elite company. Generational stuff from JT (literally, seeing as this only seems to happen once every 20-30 years). He’s done it for the first half of this decade, let’s hope he can sustain it for the next half.
