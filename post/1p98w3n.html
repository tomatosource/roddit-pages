NBA Group Stage final day cheat sheet; what scenarios does each remaining team have to root for (11/28/25)

With one day to go and 14 teams still in the mix, here’s a cheat sheet for all of the clinching scenarios that are in the air tonight:

#Winner Takes All (win and you win the group, lose and there’s a high likelihood you’re going home)

  • Magic at Pistons (7:30pm EST; LP) - With the Magic at 3-0 and the Pistons at 2-1, the winner of this matchup will guaranteed lock up East Group B. If Orlando wins, they will secure a home game in the elimination stages with a 4-0 record (Toronto being the only other 4-0 record). If the Pistons win, the Magic will be in the wild card selection of the bracket with a current +61 point differential

  • Suns at Thunder (9:30pm EST; LP) - The Suns and Thunder both clast with a 3-0 record and a chance to win West Group A. Jalen Williams is set to return with the Thunder sporting an 18-1 record. Loser of this matchup will be heading to the wild card bracket with the winner of Clippers/Grizzlies.

  • Spurs at Nuggets (9:30pm EST; LP) - Simple; winner of this matchup wins West Group C and the loser goes home. The Spurs and Nuggets are tied with a 2-1 record and the loser will have no chance of catching the winner of Grizzlies/Clippers or the loser of Suns/Thunder. Unfortunately it seems that Wemby will still be missing the game, so we will not get the potential Wemby vs Jokic elimination game we could have gotten. But it should still be tough with the Nuggets at 13-4 and the Spurs at 12-5.

#Clinching Scenario (one team is fighting to win the group or secure potential homecourt):

  • Bucks at Knicks (7:30pm EST; Prime Video) - Giannis is expected to return tonight with a chance to keep Milwaukee alive in the cup. It’s simple, if the Knicks win they’re in. If they lose, they’re out. For the Bucks, if they win then Miami is guaranteed to win East Group C (having already beat Milwaukee in the head to head). The Bucks with a win will head to the wild card bracket with Orlando (if they lose) or Cleveland (if they win).

  • Mavericks at Lakers (10:00pm EST; Prime Video) - The Lakers have already secured West Group B, but there is still potential for them to lock up homecourt in the West completely if the Thunder lose tonight.

#Fighting for a Wild Card spot

  • Cavaliers at Hawks (7:30pm EST; LP) - The Hawks have already been eliminated from contention in East Group A, but the Cavaliers are in a must win to keep hope alive. The Raptors have locked up Group A with a 4-0 record, meaning the Cavaliers will have to win and scoreboard watch. With a win the Cavs will lock up a 3-1 record in group stage and second place in the group. They can potentially be fighting for wild card position with the Bucks (if they beat the Knicks) and the Magic (if they lose to the Pistons). If the Knicks beat the Bucks and win East Group C, they will also have to worry about the Heat and their +49 point differential. But none of this matters if the Cavaliers don’t win.

  • Grizzlies at Clippers (10:00pm EST; LP) - With the Lakers already securing West Group B, the Grizzlies and Clippers both need to win to even have a chance of keeping hope alive. Winner of this matchup will end with a 3-1 record, which will put them into a pool with the loser of Thunder/Suns. Unfortunately for the winner of this matchup, they are both well behind the Thunder and Suns in point differential. The Thunder have a +71 and the Suns have a +35 in the group stages. The Grizzlies currently sit at a +9 and the Clippers sit at a -15. Best hope for either of these teams is that the Thunder blow out Phoenix with a returning Jdub and the winner blows out the loser of this matchup.

#Rooting Interests for Remaining East Teams

  • Toronto - You will host game 1 of the Group Stage no matter what. If Detroit wins, you are the 1 seed throughout all of the East. If Orlando wins, Orlando has a higher point differential and you will fall to the 2 seed. This would put you up against the winner of East Group C (either the Heat or the Knicks).

  • Cleveland - Must win for Cleveland; nothing else comes into play without handling that first. Other than that, you will also need a bit of luck on your side. With Orlando currently sitting at a +61 point differential, you really do not want them to be in the wild card bracket. So you should root for Orlando to beat Detroit and knock them out of the wild card. If the Knicks win, you will need to clear Miami’s +49 point differential. If the Bucks win, you want them to win narrowly with the Bucks sitting at a current +13 point differential compared to your +33. You should probably be cheering for Milwaukee and Orlando tonight to have a chance.

  • Orlando - You are probably safe no matter what tonight, having handled your business and locking up a +61 point differential so far (just don’t get blown out). If you win, you will be the 1 seed throughout all of the East and will face the winner of East Group C (Miami or New York). If you lose, you will be playing at Toronto.

  • Detroit - Must win. Nothing else really matters in this scenario. For seeding purposes, you are currently sitting at a +24. You will either have to clear Miami’s +49 (if the Bucks win) or you will have to keep pace with the Knicks’ +26 (if the Knicks win). If you want homecourt in the first game of the elimination stage, you want the Knicks to win and you want to beat Orlando by 3 or more points more than the Knicks beat the Bucks by. Odds are you will not catch Miami.

  • Miami - You have no chance of getting the one seed with Toronto’s +55 point differential locked in, but you can still get knocked out of winning East Group C if the Knicks win tonight. If you want to secure Group C, you should be rooting for the Bucks who you have the matchup win over. If the Knicks win, you will be playing at Toronto in the elimination stage. If the Bucks win, you will either be playing at Orlando (if Orlando wins) or most likely at home against Detroit (short of the Pistons blowing out Orlando).

  • New York - Just win. You win East Group C with a win, you head home in a loss. For seeding, you will be playing at Orlando if Orlando wins. Or you can potentially play at home if Detroit wins and you beat the Bucks by a slightly larger margin than the Pistons beat Orlando

  • Milwaukee - Must win to keep hope alive. You have no chance of winning East Group C, but you can hunt for the wild card spot. High likelihood that you need Atlanta to beat Cleveland (very little chance of catching their +33 point differential unless you blow out New York). And you need Orlando to beat Detroit to keep Orlando and their +61 out of the wild card hunt.

#Rooting Interests for Remaining West Teams

  • Oklahoma City - Win and you lock up homecourt in the West. Lose and you are just facing Phoenix again but Phoenix will have homecourt.

  • Phoenix - Win and you have a chance of locking up homecourt in the West. Lose and you are the wild card (barring historic circumstances) and will have to play OKC again in the first knockout game. You should also be rooting for the Lakers to lose to Dallas tonight (which will knock them to 3-1 and secure them as the 2 or 3 seed). Or you will need to beat the Thunder by 2 points extra what the Lakers beat the Mavericks by. The Lakers sit at a +36 point differential while you sit at a +35.

  • Lakers - You have already secured West Group B, but you still have a chance to lock up homecourt in the West completely. If the Thunder win, you have no chance with the Thunder’s +71 point differential. If the Suns win, you will be playing down to the wire trying to maintain your one point lead in point differential. You should probably root for a Suns win.

  • Memphis - If the Suns win you have zero chance of catching the Thunder in the wild card. If the Thunder win, you probably still have no chance at catching the Suns in the wild card sitting at a +9 point differential currently to their +35. But if the Thunder blow out the Suns by 15-20 or more, things get more interesting and you might have a chance in a win over the Clippers.

  • Clippers - Win by 40 and hope the Thunder blow out Phoenix by double digits. Good luck.

  • Nuggets - Win and you’re in, lose and you’re going home. You will be facing the Lakers in LA if they win tonight, or facing the Suns in Phoenix if the Suns win and the Lakers lose. If you win and the Lakers lose you could potentially get homecourt over them in the first game depending on point differential.

  • Spurs - Win and you’re in, lose and you’re going home. You will be facing the Lakers in LA if they win tonight, or facing the Suns in Phoenix if the Suns win and the Lakers lose. If you win and the Lakers lose you could potentially get homecourt over them in the first game depending on point differential.
