In terms of crafting, is this league the Harvest league equivalent for PoE2? A Pandora’s box that the community will want back forever?

Relative to the amount of effort/currency it takes to make a good item in PoE 1, the current PoE 2 system is extremely easy to make amazing gear with. This is reminiscint of Harvest league in Path of Exile 1, where crafting became almost 5x easier step-wise, and 10x easier investment wise for the duration of the league.

Everyone loved it and it got a lot of my friends into actually crafting their own gear even after the league ended, but Harvest was almost completely gutted relative to the power it had in-league. The reason for this being that, obviously, every single league has the “borrowed power” mindset GGG plans around and it couldn’t remain that powerful as a core mechanic (same with Recombinators, etc).

So my question is, when Abyss crafting is heavily nerfed after the league ends for it to go core, do you think that the reaction will be as negative as the nerf Harvest got going core in PoE1, seeing as people STILL bring it up every week in crafting threads? Or will people be happier to go back to a more nerfed/simple crafting system?

Personally I think it will need to be tuned down quite a bit, but crafting was absolutely terrible before this patch and lacked any interesting components, so GGG will have to strike an interesting balance post-league.
