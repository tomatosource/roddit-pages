Had my “I get it now” moment

So I’ve had a very off and on relationship with PoE since about 2014. I would play it, hit about act 4 or so and Peter out due to how restrictive gems made your gearing options while casually playing through. Fast forward to now, I’ve bought PoE2 because despite my on and off again, I did enjoy it and I really liked what I saw of 2 visually and mechanically.

I finally got my 2nd ascension trial (shaman) done after a few extra levels and how super smooth the trade website is. I have unlocked apocalypse in my build. All playthrough I’ve struggled with damage output. Bosses taking forever or unable to beat the molten lava guy in time. But I saved some support gems for when I got it. And boy, I am a walking calamity. This shit is so fun to charge up and let rip as I do my tornado, lightning storm, bear, wyvern loop to finally have a super powerful spell that just cleans the clocks of anything around me both bosses and trash. I finally get it. I feel powerful but not excessively so, just finally got a build to click that I really like.

That’s all. Game is fun and good even to a much more casual enjoyer.
