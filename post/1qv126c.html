The 2018 Draft is one of the best in history. The top 11 players have all been traded during their careers.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/draft/NBA_2018.htmlhttps://www.basketball-reference.com/draft/NBA_2018.html

I only listed their first trade. Draft day trades don’t count.

  1. Deandre Ayton: traded from PHX to POR
  2. Marvin Bagley: traded from SAC to DET
  3. Luka Doncic: traded from DAL to LAL
  4. Jaren Jackson Jr: traded from MEM to UTA
  5. Trae Young: traded from ATL to WAS
  6. Mo Bamba: traded from ORL to LAL
  7. Wendell Carter Jr: traded from CHI to ORL
  8. Collin Sexton: traded from CLE to UTA
  9. Kevin Knox: traded from NY to ATL
  10. Mikal Bridges: traded from PHX to BKN
  11. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander: traded from LAC to OKC

Number 12, Miles Bridges, is the only one-team player (Hornets) remaining in the first round. Ironically, he was traded on draft day for Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

Mitchell Robinson is the only player who’s stayed on the team (Knicks) he was drafted by. He was taken 36th overall.
