Every playoff team’s biggest need(Western Conference).

OKC Thunder: If this team has any needs, it’s minor. But another, more consistent shot creator off the bench would help for when Shai is taking a breather, if he’s injured, or when they can’t get any offense going through Shai or J Dub.

Solution: Not popular, but bring back CP3 on a 1yr minimum, he’s still a great passer and playmaker and can help w/the development of guys like Ajay Mitchell.

As I’m typing this: CP3 joined the NBA retirement home, the Unction, the Steelers of the NBA, or just the Clippers.

Denver Nuggets: A team that addressed it’s depth need, and traded MPJ for a better, more consistent and cheaper version of MPJ. However, they still need some big man/forward depth behind Jokic and AG, as Jonas might be playing in Europe, or even if he is staying in the NBA this year to back-up Jokic, I’d like a back-up big/forward to eat up some of the non AG minutes at the 4.

Solution: Go get Chris Boucher on a minimum for like 15 mpg, as he’s still a high energy guy off the bench who can knock down 3s, and provide some good defensive hustle. They will have to buy out Zeke Nnaji but I’d say the Nuggets are ok with that.

Houston Rockets: They traded for KD who is a significant upgrade over Jalen Green, as their halfcourt iso scorer and shooter. He’s also a better defender than Jalen, even at his advanced age and more consistent, at literally everything. They got their Brooks replacement in DFS who’s arguably a better defender and significantly better 3pt shooter, just not as loud or vocal as Brooks. They also re-signed Adams, brought back Capela who they can use when one of their bigs is out, as he’s still a solid drop big and rim protector on defense, and still is a decent rim runner on offense. However, they still need a shooter off the bench who they can draw certain plays up for or if they need another shooter on the floor to punish teams cheating over to be in one of H-Town’s stars operating space. They could also use some ball handling.

Solution: Go get either Seth Curry or Malcolm Brogdon on a minimum. If they want a pure sniper for shooting and are putting faith in Reed and Amen, then Seth is their guy. But if they want a bigger guard who’s also a good shooter and can handle the ball a little bit, in case Reed needs to develop or doesn’t pan out, I’d go with Malcolm Brogdon.

Los Angeles Lakers: They got their big in Deandre Ayton, who can be a solid big for cheap on both ends of the floor if he gives an effort, which he most likely will as this is basically a do-or-die year for his career and potential next paycheck. However, Ayton isn’t a AD, Wemby, or Gobert to anchor a good defense by himself, which is why they need some 3&D wings or guards who can stay attached to their man over screens and funnel the ball to DA, as currently their best poa defender in the starting lineup is a 40.5yr old LBJ.

Solution: Hope and pray Jrue gets bought out and signs with them for cheap, as he’s at this point essentially a pretty damn good 3&D guard with championship pedigree, who can also offer decent extra passing. Or if the Blazers keep him/trade him elsewhere, go get KCP back for cheap, something like 2 of Gabe, Maxi, or Vando as salary dumps. Both are elite at staying attached and connected to their man through screens, and their perimeter iso defense in general is very good, albeit not elite anymore. Offensively, they both are good shooters, even though they both had a down year shooting wise.

Minnesota Timberwolves: They’ve re-signed Naz Reid and Julius Randle but lost NAW to Atlanta which a lot of people saw coming to be fair. They still need another ball handler to take pressure off of Ant.

Solution: Honestly just give Rob Dillingham and some of the other guys some goddamn minutes, please.

Golden State Warriors: They did nothing so far in FA, they’re the favorites to sign Al Horford, which I believe they should. They most likely will lose Kuminga in free agency so they need a replacement for him.

Solution: Sign big Al to the vet min and do a S&T sending Kuminga to Philly for Quentin Grimes. Grimes is a wing(SG/SF) who broke out in Philly and has been a decent 3&D rotation piece in on the Knicks, Pistons, and Mavs. He gives them some some scoring punch as a starter or off the bench, solid defense, lineup flexibility where 2 of him, Moody, Buddy, or Podz can compliment Steph when going small with Dray at the 5 and Jimmy at the 4. He’s also a good shooter so there’s that.

Los Angeles Clippers: This is tough to evaluate, as in theory, they have everything you need to be make a decent playoff run, a star duo in Kawhi and Harden who fit well, a double-double machine and a great young big in Zubac, solid defensive guards and wings like DJJ, Dunn, Batum’s still serviceable, and even Kawhi in spurts is a solid matchup vs big wings and forwards. Add in JC who can catch lobs and shoot 3s which is nice for a big 4/pseudo 5, and Beal, who while his reputation(earned tbh) is a empty scorer who doesn’t impact winning ball as #1 or #2, he’s still a great shooter who can put the ball on the floor a little.

Solution: Tell James Harden the playoffs is just the regular season but with weird schedule formatting, and hope that Kawhi’s knees aren’t cooked come playoff time.

Dallas Mavericks: Again, a weird team to evaluate, but hope that Kyrie comes back looking like Kyrie, Cooper Flagg pans out, and AD doesn’t turn into glass. They only need some minor stuff, as they added D’Lo to be the bridge guard they need. However, they have a ton of bigs in general and need guard depth, so I’d say try to trade one of; Gafford, Lively, or PJ for a guard, or not.
