The Dallas Mavericks have become the 7th team since 1980 to achieve a losing record the season after making the Finals

1980 Washington Bullets: Lost #2 scorer Bob Dandridge to injury before the playoffs.

1999 Chicago Bulls: Michael Jordan retired, Phil Jackson resigned, and Pippen and Rodman were moved for the rebuild. Also a lockout-shortened season.

2005 Los Angeles Lakers: Shaq demanded a trade, parted ways with Phil Jackson, veteran Karl Malone retired and Gary Payton was traded.

2015 Miami Heat: Ray Allen retired. Also lost a guy named LeBron James who returned home in free agency.

2019 Cleveland Cavaliers: Also lost LeBron James to the Lakers in free agency. Kevin Love was injured for most of the season. Parted ways with supporting vets JR Smith and George Hill.

2020 Golden State Warriors: Lost Kevin Durant in free agency, Stephen Curry played only 5 games due to injury, Klay missed the entire season with a torn ACL, Draymond regressed. Season was shortened due to COVID.

2025 Dallas Mavericks: Traded Luka Doncic for no reason, immediately lost primary return Anthony Davis to injury for around 20 games, followed by a season-ending ACL tear for Kyrie Irving.
