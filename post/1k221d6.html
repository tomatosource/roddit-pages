An Appreciation Post

I just want to say thank you.

I had plenty of fun since day 1 of the league, played for 20 hours straight (including disconnects in the first ~3 hours). I didn’t realize something is wrong before on April 7 my friend texted me “Why are you playing this? The reviews in Steam are horrible”. I answered I had no problems and sent a screenshot of my level 82 ranger. I had zero problems with the campaign, killed most of the bosses on first try. It even felt better than the first time. Personally, I enjoy Souls-level bossing, meaningful character progression and gear optimization (trading, in particular), so the game nearly perfectly fits my needs.

Then I decided to check some streams and saw an avalanche of hate. Streamers were complaining that bosses are hard, the character is slow, the water is wet and so on. It quickly escalated to an uncontrollable chain reaction, so even barely gaming-related channels were “hyped” to release “don’t play PoE 2” videos. At this point, it is difficult to say if even 5% of total toxicity was deserved. Yes, there is no excuse to what happened in the first hours, but after that there were little to no problems. Then, the streamers led their minions to all of us know where.

I will not let the crowd guide me. I will not let them damage my IRL friendships or make me regret my choice. I will make the decision myself, and I am ready to suffer any consequences of this decision.

I will upgrade my supporter pack today. Thank you, Grinding Gear Games.
