Feedback on Spell Totems: Skills should not be designed around endgame + 100% investment to begin being useful

As folks have been discussing the last couple of days, Spell Totems are in a weird place.

As you level a character in PoE2, for the most part GGG makes a good attempt at matching the resources a skill requires with the output and performance of that skill.

For Spell Totems, their baseline cost and balance makes zero sense in the context of any build that doesn’t fully invest 100% of their resources into making them usable. Furthermore, they don’t feel worth using until you hit that endgame breakpoint where you can nullify that generation of power charges completely.

The baseline downsides of spell totem are:

  • 3 endurance/power charge requirement
  • incredibly slow base placement speed
  • incredibly slow cast time (may be bugged?)
  • very high mana cost (may be bugged?)
  • buggy targeting logic when surrounded by enemies that delays casting
  • Squishy. Subject to dying after one performing 1-2 casts
  • Stationary.
  • Inherit loss of multiple gem sockets. Socketing the spell you want and a support for the totem itself (placement speed…) leaves you with a totem that simply casts a baseline spell in early game.
  • Can’t use infusions or skills with cooldowns

To be clear, you can optimize all of these things with a level 80+ character, but it takes building your entire character around it to even become useful. Ascendency, passive tree, uniques, + spell gem level. Jeweler’s orbs.

Tl;dr — Spell Totems are baseline useless and feel like they have their breakpoints for becoming useful far too late into the game (80+).
