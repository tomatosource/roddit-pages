After 14 Years, I Finally Found a Grind I Truly Hate

I’ve been playing PoE since 2012. I’ve put in thousands of hours over the years, and for the last 4–6 years I’ve played exclusively SSF.

I’ve seen a lot. I’ve farmed just about everything. No grind ever stopped me. I always finished what I started, and in a weird way I enjoyed it.

This league is my first experience with PoE2.

On SSF I’ve loved almost everything. It felt like a brand-new game, a breath of fresh air. The new crafting, the new Atlas, the reworked endgame mechanics…ans so on and so on & I genuinely think it’s fantastic.

But farming Omen of Light in Abyss…

This is, without question, the worst thing I’ve ever farmed in either PoE or PoE2.

I literally sit there hating every second of it.

I just finished a SSF 340-hour Ritual grind for a Headhunter, and honestly I would’ve happily farmed another 340 hours if I had to.
Back in PoE1 I did SSF alch-and-go Burial Chambers until I completed 3,000 maps for 8 Doctors. I ran 5,000 Labyrinths just to get the enchant I wanted on my helmet. I’m actually proud that I had the patience to finish those projects.

But this Abyss…

Who thought it was a good idea to design snake mobs with 5–10 seconds of invulnerability in an ACTION RPG?

You wait for the chest.
You wait for the monsters to crawl out of the cloud.
Or you wait for the cloud to disappear.
Then you wait for the immortal snake to finish slithering around before it drops another Amanamu cloud.

Treir cloud appears before he even fully comes out of the Abyss, so once again… you’re just standing there waiting.

The worst part is that this item is incredibly important for crafting. On SSF you can’t just ignore it. Sooner or later, everyone has to face this farm.

It’s not even that it’s difficult. I get the drops. I juice my maps and tablets, and I’m farming them efficiently enough for my SSF needs.

But the feeling afterwards… the sheer frustration and disgust…

I’ve honestly never felt this way about any grind in PoE. I can’t even decide what I hate more: the cloud mechanic or the snakes.

I think PoE2 is an amazing game. Sure, there are texture bugs, monsters spawning in weird places, bosses occasionally becoming immortal because of bugs—but those are accidents. Those are small issues that get fixed.

This, on the other hand, was a deliberate design decision.

Someone intentionally created… THIS.

Thanks to everyone who actually read all of this. I really needed to vent.

Good luck with your farming, everyone.
