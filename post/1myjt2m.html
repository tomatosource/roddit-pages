Atlas rework idea since we’re doing these: Towers apply to your next x maps regardless of range, and also grant a resource that lets you skip maps you don’t want to run

Going to keep changes minimal, but the general idea can be remixed in any number of ways; I only use towers here, but it could be split between towers and some other structure, or you could replace them entirely with normal, juiceable maps with a tower inside.

The problem: No one likes running unjuiced maps, and no one likes running bad layouts.

The solution:

  • Towers instead apply their effects to the next X maps you run. Towers, the Realmgate, and some other encounters are unaffected. The effects of up to 3? towers may stack. This alone would force you to sometimes waste runs on bad layouts, so,
  • Clearing a tower grants you 4? skips that are used to skip maps. These maps then act as if you had cleared them. These skips stack to some limit, let’s say 10?.
  • Since all this would result in stockpiling towers, completed towers can have tablets inserted via a separate UI so that we wouldn’t have to bookmark/remember their locations.
  • Numbers are subject to balance.
