Interview of recently posted NPB player, RHP Tatsuya Imai on pitching in the dead ball era in NPB.

Per Nikkan Sports, English translated by Yakyu Cosmopolitan.

“I didn’t always dream of going to MLB. But over the past 2 or 3 years […], I felt like there weren’t many hitters who were even trying to hit a homer off me anymore.

A lot of lineups would just foul pitches off and run up my pitch count. I get that’s a valid part of strategy, but if I were a fan, I don’t think that kind of Baseball is very fun to watch. Personally, I love power-versus-power matchups.”
