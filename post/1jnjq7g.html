Ritualist or Amazon (pre patch notes discussion for fun)

I’m enjoying debating which one to league start. Obviously patch notes aren’t out yet. But here is my thinking.

Amazon is a stronger league start. I’m thinking about building an accuracy crit stacking league start that uses an old weapon cuz I’m rushing the campaign and I want a weapon I’m familiar with. The quarterstaff has higher base crit, this could be really strong. I might go crossbow because I like how it feels like a gun and we know it got two serious buffs. Bow could be good. Depending on how many people go Amazon accuracy stacking, it may be pretty affordable, or not to get accuracy on decent gear. Player power will ramp faster through maps I think making it a logical league start.

I think the Ritualist will get stronger the deeper it gets into end game, for players who know how to make currency (and have the time, which I do right now). I think power will ramp slower because the rings and amulets won’t be that strong pushing through early maps. However once you’re efficiently Farming currency and can get some excellent jewellery the power is really going to ramp. I feel confident there will be a Ritualist in the meta (I want to be strong given all the nerfs). Also the Ritualist looks like the most flexible class to use a meta weapon, like the Gemling was this current league. The jewelry buff can work with a lot of different builds.

Keep on mind too, ingenuity will be seriously nerfed and possibly reworked. And this is happening when we are going to have access to better rings! This is because we have way better crafting options and because we also have a new base that allows us to have more prefixes or suffixes on our jewelry. This gives great build flexibility.

I’m leaning towards doing Ritualist and white knuckling through the walls I’ll inevitably hit. I like league starting my main for the season. Going to also roll either a support Lich or Tactician later on. Both look incredibly powerful for support classes and I’m part of a guild that really focuses on efficient and strategic group play.
