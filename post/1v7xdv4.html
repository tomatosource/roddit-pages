What do you guys feel about “finding” endgame content?

I will start by saying i’m a casual gamer. I have enough experience in PoE 1 and 2 that i generally know what im doing, but i have like 5 hours a week max to play and i progress very slowly.

So i got to endgame very late and it took me close to a week to finish the Arbiter of divinity for the first time.

What i found was a bit disheartening. There is the entire 300+ point endgame passive skill tree and after killing the arbiter i got to 145 points..

Only then i realized that inorder for me to get all the points i will have to either do all the maps in the big fort, or keep exploring the map until i find those 2 sub bosses again and kil them and the arbiter again. 4 more time!

I said ok whatever i will try to do some other endgame quest in the meantime. So i went for Breach because i wanted those minion affixes for my jewelery.

After finishing the quest and filling up the Breach tree (which also felt a bit grindy as i needed to do like 20 maps around the area to flll it) i got enough shards to create the breachstone, only to realize that i cant place it anywhere because i need to find a specific place on the atlas for it.. so you cant really “farm” bosses because you need to keep running through the maps looking for these specific spots where they spawn. Is it like that for any endgame activity? You cannot just farm or save up resources to “bulk” farm certain things until you find the right place on the atlas for them?

It already takes a long while to even finish these endgame quests. I havent even touched Abyss, Delirium, Grand Expeditions (didnt even continue the quest of this league mechanic) or Ritual. I still have only 1 point on Doriani because he too requires a quest line to finish, not to mention the other mentors that i didnt even get to yet..

What i’m trying to say is, this all feels way too tedious and long just to finish them. And there is this “feelsbad” kind of feeling that you run like 100 maps to finish your first major endgame quest and you encounter all these cool mechaincs that you *know* they are in a “less juiced” state until you finish their quest lines, and there are mentor bonuses that i’m missing out on because i couldnt get to them at all with all the other stuff i’m trying to do. And it all feels to someone like me like it will take me a month just to get to everything.

And i know this is kind of the idea of endgame activity, to give you something to do, but if i want to lets say farm breaches to try and get some upgrades i will naturally encountet other types of content that i enjoy doing but i will want to have them at the peak of their form too. Which means before i get to actually “finish” all the setup and start farming i will need to grind for like a month and a half just to finish all the quests.

I feel like what GGG has done in the endgame is amazing and definitely the right direction. But i also think it mighve need some adjustments to how long it takes to finish everything up.

Sorry for all the rambling, but i would like to know how other people feel about the current endgame and its pacing. Is it too tedious like i feel, or is it too lacking in other aspects or is it just perfect for you. Feel free to share your thoughts and experiences.
