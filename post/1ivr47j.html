Too much Rarity killing raw Divine drops

So, we know that rarity upgrades currency based on the drop pools you saturate into from your player IIR * area IIR * monster IIR.

I’ve noticed some posts and anecdotes about people getting LESS divines with high rarity values, and I think here’s what’s happening:

  1. The final currency upgrades go from Divine &gt; Annulment &gt; Chance &gt; Mirror

  2. At some rarity breakpoint or drop pool. your Divines are being upgraded to the more rare Annulment orbs or pure Chance orbs

Chances are only worthless because you can also get them from shards, and Annuls are just less used, but both are rarer. From Midir’s video, his tests with extreme area rarity and player rarity (although not enough of a sample size) yielded less Divines and more Annuls/Chances at super high area rarity, but significantly more tiered items

So my question to you is:

  1. Do you get more Divines when your rarity is low enough NOT to saturate into the point where you start dropping tiered rares (which are drop pools 6-9)?

  2. Do you get more Annuls/Pure chances? These are supposed to be rarer than Divines, so if you’re getting a lot of them it implies your Divines are being upgraded

The key here would be to find the breakpoint at which Divines are being “upgraded”.
