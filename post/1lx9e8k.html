Quick rehash of trading out of 32

We traded out of the 32nd pick for financial reasons (not wanting to give out the multi year guarantee) for the 46th-57th and 2 future 2nd rounders.

Obviously picking up 2 future second rounders is an added bonus for trade filler or just fliers on prospects or older plug and play, deep bench college guys is nice to have.

We made the trade to keep the financial commitment off the books and keep our cap as low as possible to stay below the 2nd apron and possibly move below the tax overall later in the season if we move off Niang and Simons.

However, we signed Garza to a two year deal with a cap hit of 2.4mil a year, while Raynauds is only 1.98mil. I would much much rather have the 22 year old Raynaud locked up than Garza, who at best, is a deep bench center. Even with the additional 3 picks we received (feel like we could have signed Shulga as an UDFA though), I would have taken Raynaud.

I know its absurd to think this much about end of bench contracts but its the offseason so why not?
