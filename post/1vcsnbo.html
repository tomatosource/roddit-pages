An underrated facet of Kobe’s triple Finals Run is that he also only missed 9 games total in the regular season

The Kobe run has been getting a lot of love on this sub recently, and one thing I want to add to the conversation beyond standard glazing is that Kobe didn’t take it easy during the regular season despite having championship aspirations.

Everyone points to MJ playing all 82 games during the second threepeat, but Kobe tried to do the same too – his MVP year he became the (as of 2026) last MVP to play all 82, and repeated the endeavor in 2010 and 2011.

2009 was the only year he missed games, and that was due to an ankle injury that took him out during ASB for 9 games. Still, 9 games in 4 seasons, three of those involving extensive playoff time, is impressive.
