Just a small PSA for people having performance issues on NVidia cards

Downgrade to driver version 560.70.

First, download DDU: https://www.wagnardsoft.com/forums/viewtopic.php?t=5594

Use it to completely remove your current driver. Next, download driver version 560.70: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/details/230093/

Install it, don’t forget to clear your PoE2 cache folders, run the game with DX12.

I can’t give a 100% guarantee that all your problems will be magically fixed, but it fixed all the issues that I had. Here are those issues:

  • Complete game freezes every ~15-20 minutes, the game freezes for about 30-40 seconds, then the cogwheels appear, and the game resumes (the game is paused during this time, so I usually don’t die even in a pack of monsters)

  • Heavy stutters during certain scenarios, most prominent near Abyss cracks

  • Random FPS drops after about an hour of the game, which require game restart

I am at awe right now. I have never had such good PoE2 performance. If you are plagued with the issues I have described above, do give it a try - it just might be the cure for you.
