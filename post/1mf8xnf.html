Worst award votes of the 21st century

Non-exhaustive list, add more in the comments

  • Allen Iverson 2001 DPOY (1st place vote)
    • Over Duncan, Mutombo, Garnett, Ben Wallace, etc.
  • Michael Olowakandi 2002 All NBA
    • 11⁄9 on 43% shooting for a 39 win Clippers team
  • Raef LaFrentz 2002 All NBA
    • 15⁄7 for a terrible Nuggets team, traded to a good Mavericks team but only averaged 11⁄7 after the trade
  • Brian Grant All NBA 2003
    • 10⁄10 for a 25 win Miami team
  • Marquis Daniels 2004 ROY
    • Somehow got votes in the same year as LeBron/Wade/Bosh/Melo while averaging 8/3/2
  • PJ Brown 2005 MVP
    • 11⁄9 on 45% shooting for a ghastly 18 win Hornets team
  • Nenad Kristic 2006 All NBA
    • 14⁄6 on 50% shooting; somehow gets a vote in a packed center race including Shaq, Ben Wallace, Yao, Dwight, Pau
  • Quinton Ross 2007 DPOY (1st place vote)
    • Don’t remember Ross’ game too well but he only played 21 MPG for a good but not great 10th ranked Clippers defense, somehow gets 1st place vote over Ben Wallace, Camby, Duncan, Bowen, Battier, etc.
  • Robin Lopez 2009 ROY
    • 3⁄2 in 10 MPG
  • David Lee 2010 DPOY 
    • Guess they thought rebounding = defense?
  • Keith Bogans 2011 DPOY (1st place)
    • Solid defender but insane pick in a year with Dwight Howard, Garnett, Tyson Chandler, Tony Allen, etc.
  • Kendrick Perkins 2011 All NBA
    • 6⁄8 in 26 MPG
  • Josh Selby 2012 ROY
    • 2/1/1 in 9 MPG
  • John Jenkins 2013 ROY
    • 6/2/1 in 15 MPG
  • Isaiah Thomas 2017 All Defense
    • Didn’t go through all the All Defense teams but this one deserves inclusion
  • Marvin Bagley 2019 All NBA
    • Rookie Bagley had ok stats but nowhere near All NBA levels
  • Domantas Sabonis 2024 DPOY
    • Almost as bad as the Isaiah Thomas vote
