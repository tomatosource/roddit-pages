TIL: You can now put your snake through the Royal Access Chamber to do Atziri every time (On Patch 0.4d)

Atziri’s Chambers now correctly connects to any room placed next to it.

With this change in the upcoming 0.4d patch you can now construct your temple in a way that allows your snake to go through the Royal Access Chamber to do Atziri every run and get all the usual mega juicy rewards from long temple snakes.

If you can lock 2 snakes with one of them going through the Royal Access Chamber you can do Atziri every run, as your snake will now always connect to her and the royal access chamber will not destabilize if your snake goes through it and you lock the snake.
