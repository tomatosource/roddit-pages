Can’t double-bell anymore

Has anyone noticed that since the new update we can’t place tempest bell on top of itself anymore?

Used to be with Overabundance (+1 to limit) gem socketed into tempest bell, you could drop the second bell directly on top of the first one. Now it appears that the bell has a “hurtbox” in a way where you need to place the second bell into empty space; it can’t go directly on top of the first bell anymore.

Functionally this means that I can’t just turbo DPS bosses anymore — have to move around the boss so that the second bell has an empty spot to land. This also makes it much harder to hit both bells at the same time.

Anyone else notice this? Any workarounds? Is it still worth overabundance gem on tempest bell??
