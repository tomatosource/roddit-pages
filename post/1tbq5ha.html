Regarding +levels for skills

I was wondering, as many people here feel like +levels for skill gems is a very strong and almost unskippable item mod that wraps many late-game items around it (be it on main weapon, sceptre, amulet etc..)

What would a good balancing approach you think would work to reduce the impact of them?

Some thoughts i had regarding that are mostly in the area of maybe reduce the maximum level of skills to 30 (and ofc balance skill damage/effectiveness around it)

And lower all +levels on items by a lot

Like 2H items getting max +3levels, 1H max +2, amulets having max +2, etc..

That way the difference between level 20 and “max level” gems could be lower, and thus less mandatory to scale damage. This could also help with affix pressures because for a 2H weapon maybe getting a better damage affix could work better than +3Levels, or maybe after getting some levels elsewhere those +3levels could be less meaningful.

Just throwing some thoughts overall. But i’m curious if people here got other ideas to what could be a good solution to balance between skill levels and other affixes.
