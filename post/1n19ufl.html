From one newbie to another newbie. Think twice!

Since GGG is making this weekend F2P, I think a lot of new exiles might be joining the game. And I just wanted to let fellow new players to be careful of all the Best builds for 0.3, Best league starters, Best this guide, Best that guide videos, posts, etc. There are many unknowns and nothing is 100%. The experienced players have general idea of what is going to be good and bad, but that’s it. And many of them are posting their predictions, plans, ideas and thoughts and it’s titled properly. But recently I’m seeing a lot of clickbait titles. I’m not saying don’t watch these videos, I’m saying just before you fully invest in their guides, think twice, do some research and compare other people’s takes and then make a judgement.

When the EA was released back in December, I followed a guide and had a terrible time. I just don’t want another new players repeat my mistake and have a hard time and gets discouraged from playing POE2. Specially since we have limited time to play and try out the game, I wish everyone can have a blast and experience the game as much as possible.

Happy gaming and see you Wraeclast exiles.
