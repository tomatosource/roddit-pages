Reaching the end-game just to quit

We’re now at 0.3. the endgame was tweaked multiple times. Quests are created for each “collect X shards” type of activity.

And yet, it feels purposeless.

I need the illusion of freedom and infinity. Not it’s raw essence.

Skyrim is a good example. No, it doesn’t have infinite amount of dungeons. It has 198 clearable dungeons. But it feels infinite as you never get there. You feel whole in your run way before you get to all of them.

Next time, you can tackle the game from a different angle (I never do, but that’s my style)

Sure, poe1 couldn’t be “tackled differently” every time. You can’t skip map tiers. But that’s why they shuffle the layouts. And moreover, it’s finite - yet long enough that us, the average players - feel that there’s something to aspire to. Even if we never reach it.

The whole point of the “atlas” was to give the maps system a sense of tracked progression. An “end-game story”. It’s the same revolution Hades brought to the table. A rogue-like where the story revolves around the mechanic. Genius!

Why oh why, then - did poe2 decide to go for this empty void of endless maps as far as the eye can see?

Our search for loot and stronger builds is endless. Don’t make the backdrop be endless as well. You break the illusion or advancement. Of nearing the end.

Put boundaries on the map. Make it so the further you are from the middle, it gets more dangerous. Give us real missions. All the end-game systems are, in my opinion, fine! Just integrate them in a way that doesn’t counter our sense of progression.

Learn from POE1. Take a step back. Fast please. There’s only so much the campaign can carry on it’s back.

Scrap the current.. atlas. Don’t let this be like monoliths from LE which were supposed to be placeholder yet became a keepsake. Infinite is the build. Make our journey worthwhile. Let it end. Be brave. Survey why the atlas in POE1 was so good.

That’s it. I’m sad, but can’t stomach poe2’s endgame.
