Campaign Feedback

After playing the campaign a lot in the last week. I want to share my thoughts on some of the systems mostly from a fresh character perspective. I started with Huntress in sc ssf and later playing Warrior, Ranger and Huntress in hc and hc ssf. This post focuses on parts to improve, which makes it seem like i had a bad time, but that’s not the case at all. It was a lot of fun testing the new patch.

  • Campaign
    • Act 3 feels way better after the patch
    • Trials of Sekhemas
      • Feels great on fast characters with spear or ranged weapons
      • Slow melee mace characters have a harder time especially in hourglass rooms, where you might take resolve damage to kill rares in time or you get noticeably less loot
    • Trial of Chaos
      • Feels good in general
      • Corrupted loot without sockets feels bad
      • Sky seer boss seems way harder than the others
    • Quest and side objective rewards
      • Feel way better since the patch adding artificer orbs and runes
      • Level 1 support gems feel useless after act 1. I get more than i need unless i want to experiment a ton. Maybe just sell them on a vendor in act 2 onward and replace some of the rewards with regal orbs or essences
      • Some guarantied rewards like the lesser jeweller’s orb in Sandswept Marsh act 3 could be better communicated
      • Troubled Camps in act 3 never sell anything good
      • Gold amount from quest items at the end of act 3 feels low
  • Smith
    • Body Armour nodes feel great
      • Its weird that i can use a transmutation orb on my white Body Armour while it is equipped
    • Resistance nodes feel powerful
    • Temper Weapon was bad and haven’t tried it after the patch
  • Deadeye
    • Fast is fun =&gt; Deadeye is fun :) play Deadeye
  • Ritualist
    • Feels terrible to ascend into, because every node is weak in campaign
      • Extra Ring slot
      • Ritual Sacrifice
      • Blood Boil
      • Intricate Sigils
  • Amazon
    • Critical Strike
      • Allows for early crit scaling and feels awesome
      • Penetrate (the node after) makes it way easier to find a usable weapon
    • Predetory Instinct
      • Good dps increased against rares and bosses
      • In for the Kill (the node after) feels bad, because it is barely active
    • Elemental Infusion feels good once you have some charge generation, but not as first node
  • Spear
    • Mobility feels great with Rake and/or Disengage
      • Would be nice to have a modifier key that changes the direction of Disengage
    • Whirling slash and Twisters
      • Combo feels good and rewarding to setup with Frostbolt and Barrage, but its weird that Frostbolt is the easiest way to get a ground effect especially having to keep it low level for int requirements
      • Using Twister without the combo feels weak
      • Whirling slash targeting feels bad. Often i target lock on enemies i don’t want to. The main use case for the skill is to build up whirlwind and explode them or empower Twister
    • Melee skills (Fangs of Frost, Rapid Assault, Primal Strikes and Elemental sundering)
      • Felt weak and clunky when i tried them, but didn’t put much work in
    • Bleed skills
      • Feel great and powerful
      • Bloodloss on my overhead lifebar is annoying
      • Stomping ground…
  • Stomping Ground
    • Far too strong with Rake and Disengage
    • No melee tag feels unintuitive
    • Hard to figure out how it works without a details page
  • Defences
    • Armour
      • Feels weak and punishing to get with high movement penalties on body armour and shield
      • No payoff for getting a lot like Ghost Dance on evasion/es builds. Need something like molten shell from poe1
      • Only works on one dmg type and is weak against big hits
      • Calculation order makes combining armour with damage taken as elemental modifiers less rewarding than in poe1
      • Scaling seems off. I feel stronger against physical by pathing through more strength nodes than getting armour
    • Resistances
      • Feel good after understanding how much I am expected to have
      • Swapping a ring to get specific resistances makes some bosses way easier and feels rewarding for knowledge, which i like
    • Block
      • Very strong
      • Reliant on increased block chance mod on the shield
      • Actually feels good investing into, towards the end of the campaign
    • Early defence investment on the passive skill tree
      • Investing into armour, evasion, stun threshold, ailment threshold feels like a waste
      • Just pickup some strength for early defence
  • WASD
    • Feels great on every character i have played in poe2
    • Options I would like to have while using WASD
      • Force Move on left click
      • Interact with nearest object key
      • Loot nearest visible item key
  • Movement speed
    • Feels like the most powerful stat by far
    • Hard to invest into unless you are at the bottom right of the tree
    • Mandatory stat on boots
      • Makes it difficult to regularly upgrade
      • Would love to see a higher weighting for movement speed on boots
    • Penalties on body armours and shields
      • Feel terrible when equipping
      • badly communicated
  • Crafting and Vendor
    • Salvaging
      • Feels good
    • Disenchanting
      • Magic items feel useless
      • Rares feels good early, selling seems better after act 3
    • Vendor
      • Seem strong, try to check every level
      • Feels weird that spears are at the end of the window, when every other weapon is at the start
    • Gambler
      • Feels weak early on
      • Usually start trying to make some gold for gambling around act 3
    • Crafting
      • Exalts feel too rare to craft during campaign
      • I usually have transmutes and augments in my bags to craft on slots that seem weak
      • Would like to see more regal and exalt drops during campaign
