Please add new “Gold Auras” similar to Rarity Auras — to increase gold drop rate

Right now, crafting feels prohibitively expensive. Even when farming efficiently, gold tends to vanish extremely quickly, especially when trying to push for good rares or experiment with builds. Because of this, most players end up avoiding crafting altogether or sticking to a single viable path instead of experimenting with new setups.

That’s why I think it would be awesome to introduce “Gold Auras” — similar to the existing Rarity Auras. These could grant increased gold drop rate or multiplicative bonuses to gold found. It would make gold-based builds more interesting and add an extra layer of itemization and meta depth.

Just like Rarity Auras allow you to focus on better loot quality, Gold Auras would let players specialize in wealth farming and crafting economy. It would also open up new build archetypes (for example, a support who boosts gold drops in party play, or a solo farmer focused on sustaining expensive crafts).

It doesn’t have to be overpowered — just something that makes gold feel like a viable, scalable resource rather than a hard cap on creativity.
