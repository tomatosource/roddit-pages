LeBron James has become the only NBA player Top 25 in 5 of the 6 major statistical categories

Last night, LeBron James passed Patrick Ewing for 25th All Time in career rebounds after accumulating 17 rebounds against the T-Wolves.

Earlier this month, LeBron James became the only player Top 75 in all 6 major statistical categories after he passed Horace Grant for 75th in career blocks.

LeBron is currently:

1st in points

25th in rebounds

4th in assists (most ever by a frontcourt player)

6th in steals (most ever by a frontcourt player)

75th in blocks

7th in 3PM (most ever by a frontcourt player)

LeBron also remains the only player Top 10 in 4 of the 6 major statistical categories.

Excluding 3PM, Hakeem Olajuwon remains the only player Top 15 in 4 of the 5 major statistical categories as he’s 1st in blocks, 14th in points, 14th in rebounds, and 10th in steals.

John Stockton remains the only player 1st in two categories- assists and steals (CP3 is 2nd in both)

Blocks/steals weren’t recorded until the 1973-1974 season

This happened on the same night LeBron became the 5th NBA player with 3,000 3PM (including playoffs) and the 9th NBA player with 600 double-doubles
