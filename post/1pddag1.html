Thoughts on uniques?

ever since EA launched, i was kinda confused with the purpose of uniques, because everytime i picked up a unique they either had crazy downsides / weak suboptimal stats, while on a rare you can get 6 deterministic stats (more or less).

very rarely have i used uniques in this game, because rares are just better 99% of the time, unless theres a mechanic in the unique that enables a type of synergy with the build. it’s usually one line of text that makes or break them, while the stats usually still just blows

i have around 200 hours on PoE1 and 700 hours on PoE2

in PoE1 uniques felt exciting and strong despite me not having a full understanding of game mechanics and knowledge

in PoE2 uniques very rarely feels rewarding, because there are only a few that would be considered useable, therefor almost everything sells for 1 exalt and the useable stuff is crazy expensive in comparison (and those ofcourse rarely drops)

i went on PoEdb and compared uniques from both games, and it’s quite mind numbing

i understand they don’t want uniques to just overshadow all rares. but right now the balance of the two is wayy out of touch, and a big touch up is needed imo.

to give an example, HoWA gloves from xesth got nerfed into the ground because it was used by everyone i 0.1

HoWA got nerfed so hard that it’s just a joke of a item, while still dropping from endgame boss.

it used to be 3% increased AS per 25 dex, and 1-10 lightning damage to attacks per 10 intelligence

now it’s 1% increased AS per 25 dex and 1-10 lightning damage to attacks per 25 int, it already just has bad stats with only having dex / int (15-25)

this makes it about 1⁄3 of the value it was before, which is just an insane nerf to me

why is something that is considered a endgame unique nerfed into the ground like this? why not just a middleground, and then see how powerful the item is next league. even if it kept it’s old stats in 0.3 league, they would still not be considered the best, and would honestly just be a decent unique drop from one of the endgame bosses.
