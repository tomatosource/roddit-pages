Make the Atlas Map a real open world to explore

I like the concept of the Atlas map, but find it interesting that the world map as presented, has very little to do with the underlying maps you run.

Example, I am in the middle of a mountain range but fighting through a Savanah.

It would be cool if the Atlas map was more literal. Oh, there’s a poison swamp region, the maps would reflect that, having only bog type maps of different designs. Maybe I should path around poison if my build is weak to that. You would then also be incented and really go out of your way path to a big open Savanah region that you could load up with Breach’s etc. Or more simply, path to region types you enjoy playing.

Also, GGG could lean into the corruption aspect. Imagine a big portion of map is corrupted. When you run a map at the edge of the corruption, the enemies are +1, but if you keep pathing deeper into the region, the enemies scale +2, then +3, etc. This would give some real risk reward in that you could push very deep into a corrupted region which would enhance drops and experience gain, but at the risk of losing to much harder mobs and bricking access to that region. This could also serve a bit like delve which would allow much higher scaling for builds that are melting T15. Perhaps deep into the corrupted regions there are special bosses as well that can only be found there.

Just some ideas, I believe things like this would really enhance the player engagement with the map overall.
