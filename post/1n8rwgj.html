A big problem with PoE 2 is that fast movement is restricted to a certain archetype (bows and spears)

The Rhoa mount specifically is such an insanely good skill for mapping mobility, but only available for bows and spears. Similarly, movement speed nodes are only present in the Ranger area of the tree.

Compare that with PoE 1 where all builds have access to movement skills and can get a good amount of move speed from things like quicksilver flask and onslaught.
