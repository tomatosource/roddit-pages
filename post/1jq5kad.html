Attribute Stacking Nerfs

(Based on the most popular attribute stacking builds on mobalytics, which were pillar howa gemlings).
Might have missed a few (i.e didn’t really look at supports, but we’re also getting 100+ new ones)
Uniques:
Morior Invictus no longer rolls % increased Attributes (it’s also been reworked a bunch).
Pillar of the Caged God attribute scaling is halved.
Hand of Wisdom and Action’s attribute scaling is halved for intelligence, and 60% worse for dexterity.
Astramentis gains a wider roll range.
Ingenuity is now 40% worse, and more difficult to get perfect rolls of.
Temporalis’ cooldown reduction is halved, and also much more difficult to obtain.
Adorned is indirectly nerfed by the removal of 12 jewel sockets.
Against the Darkness rolls 2-3% attributes, down from 2-4%
Melting Maelstrom no longer recovers all mana when used.

Skills:
Tempest Bell’s impact now has higher scaling, but now requires higher combo to use, is destroyed faster, and its shockwave’s elemental ailment synergy now grants phys as ele instead of more damage, and also has ~62.5% worse scaling.
Herald skill on-kills no longer proc themselves.
Grim Feast is disabled.
Combat Frenzy generates charges about 60% slower.
Cast on Shock gains half of the energy per shock.
Elemental Invocation gains 33% less energy from ignites, and 66% less from shocks.
Wind Dancer’s more % evasion is roughly halved, is consumed by all hits, but regenerates more consistently and faster, and has more damaging potential.

Passive Tree:
Enhanced Effectiveness grants 20% less attributes (Gemling Ascendancy).
Polymathy grants 7% attributes down from 10%.
Spaghettification grants 13 all attributes instead of 7% attributes.
12 jewel sockets are removed.
Jewel corrupts that grant attributes roll 4-6 down from 5-10.

Others:
Breach rings now have a maximum quality of 40%, and are also rarer.
