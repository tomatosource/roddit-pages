LeBron James’ 32 points sets a record for most points in a game by a player in his 22nd season. The previous record was set by LeBron James last night, at 21 points.

Vince Carter is the only other player in the history of the NBA to play 22 seasons. Source

Vince Carter averaged 5.0 PPG in his year 22 season through 60 games. His season high was 17 points. Next most was 15. Source

LeBron James put up 16, 21, and 32 points respectively in the first three games of the season. Source1 Source2

Thus, the leaderboard for most points in a game by a player in their 22nd season:

  1. LeBron James - 32 (tonight)
  2. LeBron James - 21 (yesterday)
  3. Vince Carter - 17
  4. LeBron James - 16 (four days ago)
  5. Vince Carter - 15

With spots 6 through 63 all filled by Carter.
