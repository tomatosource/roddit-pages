If you were able to force your favorite team’s GM to do one move before the 2026 transaction deadline, what would it be?

So assume you’ve brainwashed your gm and they have to listen to you about one single deal. You can structure this a number of different ways and there are things to consider.

  1. If your GM doesn’t have enough trade assets to make the move, you get nothing. Meaning if you’re the hornets you can’t just trade for Jokic, you lose

  2. Your GM will fundamentally try to get the deal done for the least amount possible, but will continually add assets up until they reach the limit you set for them or whenthe other GM accepts

  3. You have to consider the other side of this and understand there’s a range of trade packages, so maybe in the comments if you’re proposing what trade you’d want to make someone from the other teams fan base could tell you what it would take
