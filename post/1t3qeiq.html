Who should the Celtics target this offseason? Signings/trades

Personally I think we should sign Timelord for a good deal and then slot Simons into the MLE - Simons expressed some interest in returning to the team, think those guys would help with are two biggest needs to big men and shooting.

If we’re going to make a trade think we need to target another ballhandler type - to me Pritch and White were just a little too inconsistent in that role this year. I think Jrue was the biggest reason we won the Chip in ‘24 because he provided a reliable third option next to JT and JB, who can struggle to establish the offense in late games sometimes. I think another Jrue-level player will put the team over the top, though that’s easier said than done of course - someone like Colby White, Immanuel Quickely, even Trey Murphy (not really a guard but whatever) would be perfect for this team - actually getting those guys is another issue, but slotting a true third scoring option into the team that would balance the roster perfectly, taking some load of the Jays and letting White and Pritch slot into a slightly less heavy usage role.

Lastly, and I’m sure most people in this sub agree, but I don’t buy into the Giannis/Brown trade idea being worth it - Giannis has had some injury issues and Brown has been one of the healthier guys in the 2020s. He’s also a couple years younger, and probably not wildly far off in terms of talent. To me the risk reward isn’t there, especially considering we’d likely give up other assets too - maybe the Tatum/Giannis combo is simply so powerful its worth it, but as a fan, the combo of risk and emotional pain of trading JB are not.

Who do you guys think the C’s should realistically target? Feels like we definitely need to a move this summer. Seems like the core of a championship team is there we just need a few more pieces to fill the roster out.
