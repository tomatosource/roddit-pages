The MLB Ballpark App is better than it has any right to be

As someone who attends 10+ games a year and has goals to visit every ballpark, I wanted to give a shoutout to the Ballpark App and point out some things that maybe people miss.

First off, I love that you can check-in to games. It automatically does this when a ticket is scanned, but you can also check in at the park, or go back and check in to past games that pre-date the app. These check-ins keep track of when the game was and who was playing. Additionally, it logs all the teams you’ve seen and parks you’ve been at. It can show you the wins and loses of each team you’ve seen too.

Second, after a game you checked into is complete, you can revisit the check in, see stats and highlights from that game. *I do think that the “Game Wrap” & “Game Story” are only available for games post 2020.

Third, you can add pictures to specific games! I love this feature, because I can take pictures of the people I’m with or specific players on the field I see play.

Finally, the go ahead entry. Great if it’s just me a buddy or me and my wife attending a game, easy entry into the games.

The app isn’t perfect. Sometimes it’s slow, and I struggle to buy parking specifically through the app. It is also clear that different teams have different portals to buy tickets, which is annoying. However, I love the system it sets up and being able to keep track of all my games.

Side note, while I have attended many more games that aren’t on my app (especially pre Covid), my home team record is 21-14 if anyone wants to employ my services.
