2025 NBA Christmas Day Games Predictions

Was talking about NBA schedule predictions with my dad the other day and we were debating what we think the Christmas Day slate could look like. It gets hard to choose considering star power, market size and if the team made a playoff run the previous season.

Here’s what I settled on as of now (Central Time):

11 AM CST - Cavs at Knicks (likely top 3 seeds in the east with multiple stars on each team. If Haliburton wasn’t injured, would’ve bet my life savings that the morning game would be Knicks/Pacers.)

1:30 PM CST - Spurs at Rockets (Uncertain on this one, but it is a lone star state battle between the up and coming Spurs led by Wemby and the highly anticipated Rockets with KD, Sengun and Amen)

4 PM CST - Nuggets at Thunder (Top 3 most entertaining playoff series this past year, Jokic and Shai MVP battle, OKC defending champs, crowd atmosphere)

7 PM CST - Mavs at Lakers (Speaks for itself.)

9:30 PM CST - Timberwolves at Golden State (Not sure on this one either, but it is a semi-finals rematch featuring an OG guard superstar and an up and coming guard superstar.)

There’s some clear guaranteed teams such as Knicks, Lakers and Thunder. I don’t want to neglect the eastern conference but I had a hard time justifying those teams over others. First off my list was Celtics, you could have them play Golden State, Houston, San Antonio or Minnesota. I have a hard time seeing the league snubbing Golden State and Houston because of Curry and KD respectively. Hard to drop Minnesota because Ant is one of the most popular up and coming stars and they just made it to another conference finals. Similarly with San Antonio and Wemby, as the league knows he’s gonna be one of the faces of the league anytime soon.

Other Eastern conference considerations would be of course Indy and they deserve a slot but I think the league won’t give it to a medium/small market team with the star of the team out. I would like them to promote Detroit and Orlando, as they’re both playoff ready teams lead by number one pick stars in Cade Cunningham and Paolo Banchero, but let’s be real, it won’t happen unfortunately. I don’t think they’ll take the risk of putting Philly in the line up because of Embiid’s uncertainty. The one I thought about as well was Milwaukee, cause Giannis is still top guy in the league but man the rest of the roster leaves a lot to be desired, they were first round exits and they didn’t get a Christmas Day game last year.

Not trying to stir arguments, but just curious what other people think the other possibilities are.
