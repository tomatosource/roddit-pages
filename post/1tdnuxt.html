Asymmetrical defenses and item affixes

Outside of the obvious difference in the strength level of defences, one of the biggest problems is affix pressure.

This makes life based builds actually require better gear overall to achieve a similar end power.

For a ci character

Most items are simply double or triple es rolls on prefixes and res on suffixes and they don’t need chaos res.

For a life armour character

Prefixes need

Life, Armour, Armour

Suffix

Res, chaos res, strength, armor applied to ele

This gets worse for some archtypes, like thorns characters needing to choose a prefix to give up to make room for thorns and giants blood characters needing a ton of strength from gear for end game weapons.

Tldr even if life and Armour builds can reach a similar end game power as ci in some cases, they need way better gear to reach it
