How player movement in seasonal games works

There’s a lot of posts being made about the reduction in player counts and questions about if the game is dead etc. I thought it might be worth posting this simple graph for some of the newer players who aren’t used to seasonal games and the flow of the playerbase in and out of the game.

This is for POE1 obviously, top graph is showing the year 2022 as an example, longer term graph is below.

i labeled the 4 leagues that kicked off that year, you can see that once a new league kicks off the player count surges then trails off before surging again for the next league.

https://preview.redd.it/3s4k3ufjq3ke1.png?width=1448&format=png&auto=webp&s=ef87a4844463465d80cadcd7989bdcaa8cef9ed7

https://preview.redd.it/2m6t4zslq3ke1.png?width=1435&format=png&auto=webp&s=16476b53e3e73625424b0273ab778b3eac644f55

The second image is a closeup of the Archnemesis league period itself.

You’ll see that the player base only really stays very high for approximately a month. Some leagues die quicker than others, some of the really good ones stretch out a bit but generally speaking the bulk of players are done within 4-6 weeks.

We still don’t really know what they will do with POE2 at this point, but hopefully just trying to give an understanding of how these player waves move. Long term POE players tend to flood to a new league and play a lot for in particular the first couple of weeks, then drop off before eventually taking a break. It’s relatively common for POE players to do things like line up their holidays from work with leagues. A league that syncs with breaks from school and university will be widely celebrated. For a lot of players the game is very feast or famine.

Right now most of us are well past the use by date of a new league. Burnout has set in. There’s limited end game content and even if three was a lot of experienced players would still have been done with it anyway. A new POE1 league is on the way and people are preparing or having a break before that.

Hopefully we get some POE2 news soon so everyone floods back soon after the POE1 event wraps
