Performance issues as of 0.3.1 make endgame mapping unbearable.

Adding a boss fight to every map is an interesting experiment and has potential. However, the game constantly drops frames and lags out during these fights. The game’s performance was totally fine before 0.3.1, but has become a major problem for me as of that patch.

If most bosses didn’t have one-shot mechanics, it might not be so frustrating. Or if you had the option to run one or two revives without sacrificing a huge portion of your map rewards.
But because most bosses are designed to instantly kill you if you don’t avoid certain attacks and maps require you to run 0 revives to use 3 tablets, you’re stuck in a lose-lose situation of either taking on significantly more risk of having to waste time re-running the map with nothing on it after clearing the rest of it because you lagged while silverfist was using his giant pillar slam, or just taking much less reward at a baseline without as high a risk of having to re-run an ‘attempted’ map.

Imo the system already overly punishing, and the constant lag just removes any agency you had to avoid that punishment with skill.

Abyss has also started to run into similar frame drops as of the recent patch, but abyss modifiers and packs are usually easier to play and position around to compensate for not being able to react to stuff.
