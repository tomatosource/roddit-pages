The last pitcher to have 2 CGs in the same World Series was Tom Glavine in 1992, against the Blue Jays

In the 1992 World Series, Tom Glavine pitched a complete game at home in Game 1, allowing 1 run, leading the Braves to a 3-1 win.

He started again on short rest for game 4, and pitched another CG (8 innings) on the road, but allowed 2 runs and took a 2-1 loss.

Glavine did not appear again in the 1992 World Series, and the Blue Jays won in 6 games.

Before Glavine, the prior 4 pitchers to have 2 CGs in the same world series did so in winning efforts, in that all 4 of them won both games and their teams won the World Series.
