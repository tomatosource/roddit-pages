Juicing maps and currency drops feel unsatisfying and pointless

I ran about 75 giga-juiced maps (desecrated, instilled, etc) with 2-3 tower setups giving extra rarity and quantity and so on.

Not on a single map did I get a raw divine orb drop. Sure, I get a few drops of other stuff that I can eventually trade in for a divine orb, but my average earnings per map are far below 1 div, let alone multiple.

To me, it feels unsatisfying to run 30 maps and then eventually scrape together a few divs from all the other stuff that dropped.

I would much rather have consistent, small drops of divines. To me, this keeps up the fun since you get some currency in raw form every couple of maps rather than a ton of loot you have to sell in the currency exchange after hours of playing. In my opinion, this feels unsatisfying.

I got lucky a couple of times and I did get a lot of currency overall, but to me, getting a skill gem worth 24 divines after 30 hours of playing is way less fun than getting 1 divine per hour. But maybe that is just me?

Also, I ran 900 splinters worth of breach maps and got 0 currency from it. It just feels ass.
