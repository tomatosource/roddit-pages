Herald of Thunder & Ice Warrior Titan Meme Build

My first character in Path of Exile 2 was the warrior. I somehow got to Act 3. But was full stuck there. That took all the fun. So I played with other characters. I got really far with multiple characters and currently am doing Tier 15+ with them. But my Warrior was kinda stuck in Act 3.

Until this item dropped https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/489920 It is called Blueflame Bracers Goldcast Cuffs and it made the warrior fun again, admittably for the wrong reason. 100% of your Fire Damage is converted to Cold Damage. You can use Molten Blast and Volcanic Fissure, a Weapon with high Fire Damage, your skill tree that has multiple “increase fire damage”, the usual Herald of Thunder with cold infusion and increased freeze chance and Herald of Ice with lightning infusion and increased shock chance. I now did my ascension and am in Act 1 Crual, without dying so far. For Bosses, I use the Break Armor/Sunder-Tech. Works, although as the title says, it is a meme build.
