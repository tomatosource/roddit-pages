According to multiple NBA sources, the Heat have yet to receive clarity from the league about whether Rozier’s salary can be utilized to balance a potential trade for salary-cap purposes.

Contacted by the Sun Sentinel, the NBA declined to say whether the Heat are banned from including Rozier’s salary in a trade, with the Heat yet to receive any clarity from the league, according to a source.

Rozier is due \(26.6 million this season, with \)24.9 million guaranteed.

According to an NBA party familiar with the process, the status of Rozier’s salary in regard to a trade remains “a hypothetical,” with the league to take a more immediate position regarding salary-cap implications if and/or when approached in regard to such a potential trade.

The constraint of such an approach would be the Heat first having to enter trade negotiations, structure a deal, and then await NBA clearance, with all parties involved unclear until a league ruling about whether a deal involving Rozier’s salary would be approved.
