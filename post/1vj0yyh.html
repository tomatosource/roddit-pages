PSA: Audience With The King Must Be In Your Inventory to See Crux of Nothingness on Atlas

Unlike unrevealed Breach Strongholds (which show up on your Atlas naturally), unrevealed Cruxes of Nothingness will only show if you have an Audience With The King in your inventory. It cannot be in your stash, it must be in your inventory.

As soon as I grabbed one from my stash, 11 Cruxes showed up on my Atlas.

I hope GGG changes this so we have some consistency and that fragments in your stash are good enough to show you what content is ACTUALLY available on your Atlas.
