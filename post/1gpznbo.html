The nation’s largest owner of regional sports networks will offer single-game pricing for NBA games beginning next month.

DSG, which owns 16 RSNs, announced Tuesday that the option will be available on its direct-to-consumer streaming package starting Dec. 5.

Viewers will have the option for single games at $6.99, as well as the chance to sign up for monthly or season pass subscriptions.
