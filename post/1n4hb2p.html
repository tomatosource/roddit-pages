Loving the patch so far, but low tier runes are way too rare during the campaign

Why are low tier support gems and runes so rare so early on? I’m currently in the interludes and I’ve had like maybe 6 of the low tier iron runes drop and a handful each of the elemental ones. If we are meant to be freely using the lower tier socketables to upgrade our gear while leveling it needs to be more plentiful during the campaign. I feel like I’ve gotten exalts more consistently throughout the campaign then low tier runes which is wild. Maybe they need to give out during the main story quests. I have plenty of artifice orbs to add sockets but barely any low tier runes to socket in my gear.

Support gems as well I didn’t start accumulating a decent amount of them until Act 4 when Support lvl 4 started dropping. Before that I was starving for them.

Spirit Gems also seem to be quite rare with my only sources for them being the 3 from quests and 2 drops and I’m in the interludes.

Edit: I’ve now made it to maps and the problem has somehow gotten even worse. The first tiers of maps are barely dropping any runes or support gems. I literally have gear with open sockets because ive traded for better gear but dont have the runes to socket into them. I guess they want us to burn currency in buying runes? Feels bad tbh. Feels like we are back to before the loot buffs in terms of runes and gems.
