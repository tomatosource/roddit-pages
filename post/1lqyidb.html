One of the most hilariously bizarre and embarrassing ‘big 3’ performance in a playoff game: Jazz vs Thunder Game 6 in the first round of the 2018 playoffs

In an elimination game against the Utah Jazz, the Oklahoma City Thunder 2018 ‘big 3’ of Russell Westbrook, Paul George and Carmelo Anthony have very interesting stat lines to end up losing the series to the Jazz by 5 points

Russell Westbrook: 46 PTS 10 REBS 5 ASTS on 18-43 FG 7-19 3PT 3-6 FT in 44 mins

Paul George: 5 PTS 3 REBS 8 ASTS on 2-16 FG 0-6 3PT 1-2 FT in 45 mins

Carmelo Anthony: 7 PTS 3 REBS 0 ASTS on 3-7 FG 0-0 3PT 0-0 FT in 27 mins. Benched in most of the second half

Shoutout to Steven Adams, who had 19 PTS 16 REBS on 9-11 FG as the only starter with a positive +/-

Jazz win the game behind an efficient 38 points from Donovan Mitchell. Final score 96-91
