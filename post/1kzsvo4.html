Potential fix for your stutter/lag spike in game

So I built a decent PC two years ago—nothing crazy but definitely solid for gaming (64GB RAM ddr5, i9-13th gen, xt6800, Samsung SSD, etc.). I still get fps drop when play froze build but still bareable. But lately, after around 30 minutes of playing Path of Exile 2, I start getting insane stutter and lag spikes. I mean full freezes during fights, stuttering every 10 seconds, and even lag just standing in the hideout. It’s honestly becoming unplayable.

At first, I thought it was just my rig acting up. But after doing some digging, it turns out PoE2 optimization is still pretty rough. Luckily, I found a few things that helped reduce the stutter and improve performance. Hopefully, these tips help someone else too:

Fixes & Optimizations I Found:

  1. Global/Trade Chat Lag : The game can lag due to message spam or sync buildup in active chat channels. Use /global &lt;random number&gt; and /trade &lt;random number&gt; to switch to low/no-traffic channels. This fixs the problem above.
  2. Lower Channel Count: in Sound Settings Go to in-game settings and reduce the Channel Count to Low. This noticeably improved performance for me.
  3. Turn Off V-Sync: PoE1 had a “Predictive” desync mode, but PoE2 doesn’t. For now, turning off V-Sync reduces input lag and stutter but If you get desyncs, your best bet is to restart the game.
  4. Set Texture Quality to Medium: PoE2 currently doesn’t expose a texture_quality=4 setting like PoE1. “Medium” is the lowest available and can help with memory usage/stutters.
  5. Use Fullscreen Mode: Fullscreen provides better FPS than borderless/windowed. If you’re FPS-sensitive like me, use Fullscreen for a boost.
  6. Delete Shader Cache Clearing corrupted/outdated shaders can help: C:\Users\&lt;YourName&gt;\AppData\Roaming\Path of Exile 2\ShaderCache
  7. Disable Sound Settings: Manually Open: C:\Users\&lt;YourName&gt;\Documents\My Games\Path of Exile 2\poe2_production_Config.ini Find any sound values set 0 and change them to false.
  8. Mouse DPI Can Cause Lag. When I used high DPI in PoE2, the game became super laggy. Try lowering your mouse DPI or go to the game .exe → Properties &gt; Compatibility &gt; Change High DPI Settings
