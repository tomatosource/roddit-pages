Which active NBA vets Have Peaked in the general All-Time Rankings?

As ridiculous as I think all-time rankings may be, it is still something I will admit that I think about anytime I try to evaluate a player’s career and accomplishments relative to all players throughout the history of the NBA.

Like the title notes, what NBA vets have likely peaked in terms of where most fans will perceive them in an all time rankings? For example:

- Lebron: Top 2. A large majority of NBA fans either have Lebron at #1 or #2 all time. And regardless of what Lebron does in Philly, the fans who still have Jordan #1 will not accept Lebron as #1 for the same reasons they currently hold (i.e., Jordan had a higher peak, perfect 6-0 finals record). Therefore, his ranking is pretty cemented at this point.

- Jokic: Top 12-15. Despite his 3 MVPs, most fans I see on reddit and online have Jokic as a top 12 to 15 player, right outside of that top group of players that cover 1-11. Though Jokic’s stats and analytics are amazing in addition to the MVPs, cracking that top 11 usually requires being a face of the league or consistently winning or on the cusp of championships. (for reference I have listed the fairly consensus top 11 below):

General Top 11 (in no particular order): MJ, Lebron, Kareem, Wilt, Magic, Russell, Bird, Duncan, Shaq, Kobe, Curry

Is there anyone I’m missing?
