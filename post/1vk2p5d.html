Was Kawhi leaving Toronto in 2019 one of the worst mistakes from a superstar in nba history?

The team was literally perfect around him. He had floor spacing, secondary perimeter defense, and shot creation everywhere. The year after he left the Raptors won 53 games, and players like Pascal, VanVleet, OG, and Norman Powell improved significantly.
Obviously, we’ll never know for sure, but that team with Kawhi could’ve gone back to back. The butterfly effect could’ve played a factor, but Toronto could very well still had a trio of Kawhi, Pascal, and OG today. They wouldn’t have had much reason to trade OG or Pascal if they were consistently competitive with Kawhi.
They legitimately could’ve had a chance for multiple rings after 2019. It’s all hypothetical, and we’ll never know what actually would’ve happened, but Kawhi’s decision to leave Toronto in 2019 may have costed him multiple accolades and been one of the worst mistakes in NBA history.
