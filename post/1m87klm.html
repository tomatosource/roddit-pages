Projectiles should come out where you point, not click

I don’t know if this has already been said, but we know there is an “aiming problem” in PoE2, and frankly, it’s so bad that it makes the game feel like garbage to play.

We’ve addressed the needing to click on feet instead of bodies issue.

There’s the soft lock on issue.

But I was playing today and I was noticing something.

If I double click an area, my character will fire both projectiles where I clicked, even if I move my cursor away before the second projectile even comes out

Frankly, that makes no sense. I want my projectile to go where my cursor is pointing in the present, not where I clicked 1 second or even half a second ago. The buffering system is terrible.
