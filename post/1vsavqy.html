Lore compilation update for POE 3.29 & POE II 0.5

This is an update to the complete lore compilation for POE & POE II. For those unfamiliar with the lore compilation, the original post can be found here.

#What’s in the Update

  • Added lore from Mirage league (POE).
  • Added lore from Curse of the Allflame league (POE).
  • Added lore from Return of the Ancients (POE II).

#Major Changes

  • The early sections have been reorganized to fit the new information revealed in the POE II endgame update.
  • Moved the First Ones out of the gods section, as there’s now plenty of evidence that they aren’t gods in the sense of those who ascended from humans.
  • Updated the Azmeri gods section to include the new lore on Tangmazu, Viridi, Solaris, and Lunaris and take out old lore that was removed from the game.
  • Updated Garukhan’s section to add the new lore about Saresh.
  • Added a section on the history of Middengard to the Kalguur section. Also added some new lore to the end of the Kalguur section.
  • Added a new section called Dawn of the Ezomytes after the Kalguur section, which includes the new lore on Ezomyte history, including the integration of the Kalguur into their culture and the founding of Ezomyr.
  • Added some new lore under the Occupation & Liberation of Ogham section.
  • Added a new subsection on King Cadigan under Kingsmarch & the Second Kalguuran Colonisation.
  • Added a bit of new lore to the Viridian Wildwood section, including new information about its first entrance and the Bodach.
  • Added new lore to the Breaches & the Hiveborn section, particularly about what has occurred following the events of Keepers of the Flame league.
  • Moved the Titan lore from the Miscellaneous Lore section to its proper chronological places earlier in the compilation.
  • About 40 new characters were introduced and most of these were added to the appendix. Other miscellaneous updates were made to the appendix as well.
  • Updated a few of the illustrations that were not so good.

Hope you enjoy the update!
