Infusion Cheat Sheet

Avid Sorceress enjoyer here. Made a cheat sheet about the Infusion mechanic to have a better overview of what builds I should try. Thought I’d share it with my fellow exiles. I hope GGG expands on this mechanic to more abilities in the future.

There is also a Keystone and Stormweaver Passive related to infusions:
* Stormweaver passive - Refracted Infusion : Provides 50% chance when collecting an Elemental Infusion to gain another different Elemental Infusion.

* Keystone - Elemental Equilibrium : Causes Infusion Remnants you create to be random elements.

**Disclaimer - Only used what is in the patch notes as of the livestream reveal date for 0.3.
