Refusing to let TNT’s Allie LaForce interview Chris Paul at the Skills Challenge was a very bad look for the NBA.

Much has already been said about Wemby/CP3’s disqualification in the Skills Challenge tonight over a rules violation, and rightfully so. It’s quite interesting, but does not need to be rehashed here. Right or wrong, the incident itself will likely blow over pretty quickly.

More concerning to me is the NBA running off Allie LaForce from trying to learn what happened and from interviewing Chris Paul.

Adam Silver needs to explain that, and so far I have not seen any official league response.

The “Heisman-ing” (as LaForce called it in real time) of a reporter doing her job is the worst look for the image-conscience NBA in a long time.

Incidentally, LaForce seemed to want to know if her mic was live before explaining on the broadcast that it was a specific person (“Dana”?) that told her to stand down. Did I hear that right? If so, who is Dana?
