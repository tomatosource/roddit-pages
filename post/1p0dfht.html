Hornets rookie Kon Kneuppel in his last 8 games: 21.6ppg/7.0rpg/3.4apg on 63% TS (3.4 triples on 39% as well)

Kon has been excellent to start out his NBA career. Had to shout him out because he was already killing it for me in fantasy - but even more than that, I was at the Raptors game last night and was beyond impressed at what I saw. He plays with poise and composure beyond that of a rookie, and made smart plays across the entire game. In fact, I was more worried when he had the ball and was getting a shot off than even Lamelo or Bridges. I have no idea why they looked him off so much down the stretch - he really could’ve made something happen.

I thought the guy was gonna be just a Duncan Robinson type stand-in-the-corner spot-up shooter but he actually reminds me more of Gordon Hayward - a great shooter who can actually do a little bit of everything.

Bright future ahead for The Big Areola.
