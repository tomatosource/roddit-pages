Towers still need to do something

So, with tower juicing gone, towers are currently worse maps that drop an extra tablet. Overall, pretty underwhelming. Here’s my idea on how to remedy this while keeping them thematic:

  1. Make them rarer, no need for overlapping towers anymore
  2. On completing a tower you get to choose to “terraform” a few of the surrounding maps with some the spicier options we got - unique maps, deadly bosses, moments of zen, abyssal horde, etc
  3. Options are dependent on the biome the tower covers - can’t get Castaway or Jade Island without water, etc

I think this would offer a way for players to tailor their maps to chase specific content while still giving purpose to atlas exporation, you could hunt for essences with Silent Caves, or chase the coastline rolling for Jade Isles for Rakiata, etc
