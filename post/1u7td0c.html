Is Powered by Verisium being slept on? Endless Infusion generation with Cast on Crit

Powered by Verisium is a new ward spell that at level 25 gives 5 Verisium Infusions at the cost of 130(with efficiency) ward. Verisium Infusions *c*an be used instead of any elemental infusion. Which means that at the press of a button you can send 4 fully infused comets at the boss with no additional setup. But the good part is that you can put it into Cast on Crit or any other “Cast on” spell, and have a 1-button build that passively generates 5 infusions every 3-4 sec.

Example with my guy: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr2h0d5WInA
Comet blasting the boss at 4:30
The guy: https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/Oberr-7192/runesofaldur/character/OberRage

I’m using shaman’s bonded runes for ward regen, but ward regen can be solved by many different ways: bigger ward; new ward remnants; 2 runes that boost ward regen for caster weapons/wand
