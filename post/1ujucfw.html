Feedback About Dying on Maps

Hello,

I joined PoE years ago way back when it first came out in beta. I have played off and on ever since. I have been particularly excited about PoE2 and I am going to end up putting even more hours into it than I have PoE in the long run. Over the years, I have put a decent amount of funds into support packs as well.

During this time, I have never submitted a complaint of any kind. I haven’t even submitted any feedback, and it is extremely uncommon for me to do so for any game I have ever played.

I can appreciate that you lose XP when you die. I don’t have any issues with losing the ability to use tablets on maps you’ve failed and that you get almost no XP for re-running them. It makes sense that the map clears content and monsters when you die to the last boss, and it incentivizes making sure you have cleared all the content that you would like to do/is required before facing the final boss.

However, one very strong opinion I have about something that needs to change with this system, is that dying to the boss doesn’t just clear the map content. It clears your map progress, meaning that dying even once, no matter how many revives you have, to the map boss, will undo requirements for map completion that you already met. Then, the otherwise fair enough clearing of map content prevents you from completing the map at all. But then, you can re-run the map with a white tier 1 waystone, skip everything but the one required mechanic, then one-shot the boss and complete the map. I don’t see how that is somehow better for anyone or the health of this system, or how doing this makes me more deserving of completing the map than if I am running a tier 16 waystone with two revives and beat the boss after dying to it once, having no other deaths.

The idea that something I did, something I had already accomplished and locked in, being taken away is, well, sort of offensive. It is sort of like not just losing XP but actually de-leveling. It also calls into question why there are revives on maps at all, if dying even once bricks the map no matter how many revives are listed on your waystone.

I understand this is most likely to combat some kind of cheese strat. Which is fine. But the map quest progress needs to be de-coupled from the map state such that quest progress isn’t rolled back on death, unless that specific mechanic calls for it, such as breach stronghold.

I hope this isn’t a bit much. But I feel quite strongly about it, moreso than anything else I have encountered in either of these games. It doesn’t feel fair. It also doesn’t feel like this is explained, described or indicated at any point. For awhile I thought it was a bug, in fact.

I hope you guys can find a good solution to this issue. A lot of people talking about dying to boss once clearing the map being the problem. But that’s not the problem they are referring to, they just aren’t being precise with their meaning. What they mean is that it is clearing/resetting your progress on the map objective and then taking away your ability to re-do it that people are upset about, including myself.
