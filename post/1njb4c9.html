If you’re having lag spikes like I was, change this in your config! This stopped them for me

In my previous post I deleted, I was asking for any tips; and had found this config tip and was about to try it out before I posted. Well, this changed everything. I haven’t had. Slag spoke ever since I did this one change in the config.

Change this setting from “auto” to “predictive” Networking_type=predictive

*to get to your config, go in to documents folder &gt; my games &gt; path of exile 2 &gt; then open “poe2_production_Config”

Scroll or search for networking_type=auto and change auto to predictive. Then click ctrl+s or click save from the file drop down menu, and Bob’s your uncle…
