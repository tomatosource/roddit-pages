A Simple Life: Warrior

For years in ARPGs I’ve been ranger or sorc, and decided to try out a bonk build. Old school visceral action, and heavy hits really makes this game shine HARD with immersion and overall POE2 experience; it just works! No crazy button rotation, or worried about dishing damage in campaign for the most part; just dual macing monsters to the face. Finally…warriors voice lines, and comments are golden. Currently at the end of act 3 so far.

Anyways, new bonk boy believer here, can’t believe it took me so long, but at least we’re here. Cheers to warrior, a damn fun and simple class. Be the bonk. Embrace the bonk.
