Ten players have helped win the first-ever World Series championship of two different franchises.

Patsy Dougherty - 1903 Americans (Red Sox) and 1906 White Sox
Johnny Evers - 1907 Cubs and 1914 Braves
Goose Goslin - 1924 Senators and 1935 Tigers
George Sweatt - 1924 Monarchs and 1926 American Giants
Rube Curry - 1925 Hilldale and 1926 American Giants
Biz Mackey - 1925 Hilldale and 1946 Eagles
Lonnie Smith - 1980 Phillies and 1985 Royals
Devon White - 1992 Blue Jays and 1997 Marlins
Craig Counsell - 1997 Marlins and 2001 Diamondbacks
Max Scherzer - 2019 Nationals and 2023 Rangers

If you were to count city-specific first championships, you can add Edgar Renteria (1997 Marlins and 2010 San Francisco Giants) as well as eight players who were with the Dodgers in both 1955 Brooklyn and 1959 Los Angeles (Roger Craig, Carl Furillo, Jim Gilliam, Gil Hodges, Clem Labine, Johnny Podres, Duke Snider, and Don Zimmer).

Two active players, Mitch Garver and Victor Robles, helped win a franchise’s first World Series (Rangers and Nationals respectively) and are currently playing for a team that has never won the World Series (Mariners).
