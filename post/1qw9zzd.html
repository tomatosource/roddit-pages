I’m sitting here stunned. I think Joe might be the best Celtics coach in 40 years.

Yes part of the success is Jaylen derrick and Payton.

Yes some of it is Brad bringing in the right pieces to complement them.

I also think Jaylen really wasn’t great in January. Not that he wasn’t good but, Super inefficient turned the ball over a ton, etc. (I know I might be cherry picking, but his TS was 53.1% and 50.6 if you remove the 50 point game).

And they’re 16-5 over that run. Winning almost everything they had to.

this team is going to win 55 games after losing 80ish points from the championship team.

This. Is. Joe.

  1. Garza who played 3 seconds a game and was a total after thought in Minnie

  3. Queta, WHO CURRENTLY IS HAVING A BETTER WS SEASON THAN EVEN TATUM HAS EVER HAD.

  4. Simons (RIP)

  5. HUGO LOOKING LIKE MANU

Joe has found it man. I’m not sure if he’ll win it, but coach of the year should be a lock.
