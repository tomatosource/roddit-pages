Tablet precursor drop rates nuked by a spelling error?

I noticed two different spellings for tablet precursor while I was browsing through the atlas today. The “Tablet Effect” nodes were the only places I saw it, though I didn’t spend too long searching for others. The general consensus seems to be that tablet drop rates (especially ritual) were absolutely nuked with the most recent tower changes. The current market seems to support that theory, even considering late league market instability / inflation / spec buyouts.

I don’t have much coding experience beyond a couple of C++ classes many years ago, and I’d assume a tooltip typo doesn’t have any connection to the actual variables or calculations. But maybe the error goes deeper and is quietly sabotaging our tablet drops. A single missing letter cascading into massive inflation and countless internet arguments. Or maybe… it really is just a typo.

Now I feel obligated to min-max when farming rituals, as the lost value is now much more apparent. Oh well…
