Looking back, David Stern got super lucky with timing when he became the Commissioner

He first became commissioner in February of 1984. This was basically at the start of peak Bird-Magic rivalry. The two of them were already making the NBA popular. They played against each other in the finals for the first time a few months later. Then MJ comes into the league the next season. David really came at the perfect time.

David was known amazing commissioner, better than Adam by a lot. However looking back, he got super lucky at the timing.
