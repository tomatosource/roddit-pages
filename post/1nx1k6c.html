Looking back, David Stern got super lucky with timing when he became the Commissioner

They played against each other in the finals for the first time a few months later. Then MJ comes into the league the next season. David really came at the perfect time.

David was known amazing commissioner, better than Adam by a lot. However looking back, he got super lucky at the timing.
