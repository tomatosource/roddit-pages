Haliburton is the showman the NBA didn’t know it needed - at least, I didn’t

Haliburton is obviously a student of the game both present and past. This in combination with his quick twitch mind, allowed him the wherewithal to pretty cleanly re-enact the Reggie Miller choke job in a moment that probably elicited the same feeling in Knicks fans of wanting to find the tallest nearby building when the game was over. Haliburton is taking pride in his franchise’s history and past. He shares a lot of similarities with Reggie imo with the two biggest being elevating their games in the playoffs and having an unorthodox jumper that is somehow just cash. IMO, he’s been the breakout star of this postseason so far.
