Feedback from a Returning Player

I played a little over 300 hours in 0.1, skipped 0.2, touched 0.3 for maybe 20 hours, and I’m closing in on 80 hours in 0.4. I think the game is shaping up amazingly and want to leave some feedback about specific things I’m noticing:

  • Loot is in a MUCH better spot than it was in 0.1 – feels good without being overdone
  • OMG async trade is a god-send. I finally feel like I can participate in the economy
  • Abyss feels great now. If every league mechanic is this polished once it goes core, the game is going to be amazing. I have very high hopes for Vaal Temple in 0.5 and it’s cool to try the “half-baked” version of a league mechanic first then see it in its final form
  • Talismans are incredible because they enable so many different kinds of builds for almost all classes and really encourage weapon swapping
  • Act 4 is awesome. Kingsmarch, the islands, the lore, and the pacing are all excellent. Easily my favorite full act (formerly act 1). Bonus points for the NPCs basically calling you rude for walking away while they’re still talking, I laugh every time
  • I really hope we see more interlude content. All 3 interludes are cool, engaging, rewarding, and don’t feel like recycled content at all (even though you go back to the first 3 acts essentially). I think Act 3 feels so long because going back in time is basically a separate unannounced interlude.
  • Bear skills are SO satisfying. The visual shattering of the ground when you slam it, the audible crunch of the terrain, the feeling of weightiness as you move around – you guys really cooked on that one. This kind of visual/audio identity really helps set it apart from, say, 2h mace warrior

Big picture, I think GGG is doing a lot of things right and it’s clear that whatever they do get “wrong” (e.g. Vaal Temple issues) is more about getting lots of data and then finishing the feature, exactly what early access is all about.
