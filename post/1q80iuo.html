Best offensive seasons cut short by injuries/strikes in the post WWII era

1980 George Brett (injury), 117 games, .390 BA, 1.118 OPS, 203 OPS+, on pace 242 hits, 163 RBI, 411 TB, 13.0 bWAR

1981 Mike Schmidt (strike): 109 games, 1.080 OPS, 198 OPS+, stellar defense, on pace for 50 HR and 146 RBI, and 12.3 bWAR

1994 Tony Gwynn (strike): 110 games, .394 BA, 169 OPS+, on pace for 241 hits, (only) 6.2 bWAR (awful defense)

1994 Jeff Bagwell (strike): 119 games, .368 BA, 1.201 OPS, 213 OPS+, on pace for 57 HR, 169 RBI, 438 TB, 12.0 bWAR
