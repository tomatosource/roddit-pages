Gameplay vs Vision

GGG said before release that they were aiming for a slower, more methodical style of gameplay where combos would actually feel rewarding. But right now, that doesn’t really line up with how the game plays. Most of the time I still just Leap Slam into Boneshater, or hold down Ice Strike, drink a mana flask, and maybe throw in a bell which isn’t really that much of combo gameplay.

It feels like the core design of our characters, the way skills are structured and how they’re supposed to flow, was meant for that slower, deliberate pace. But the enemies, especially in endgame or mapping, are designed as nonstop swarms. Take a yellow map with a few modifiers, stack that with the map’s own mods and something like an Abyss, and suddenly you’re in a situation where you’re spamming heals and dodge rolling just to stay alive in certain areas (especially around abyss craters)

It really does seem like they wanted to build the game around slower, thoughtful combat, but the balance doesn’t hold up. Path of Exile 1 was the pinnacle of ARPGs for a reason, it had a formula that worked, even if it wasn’t very beginner-friendly.

The frustrating part is that whenever I try to set up actual combos, I end up getting punished for it. Enemies swarm too fast and hit too hard, which just forces me back into the old 1–2 skill playstyle. That still feels like the only viable approach, but it’s awkward, because the game doesn’t feel like it was actually built for that. Instead, it feels like two competing visions: a foundation designed for slower gameplay, and an enemy design that constantly pushes you into frantic spamming.
