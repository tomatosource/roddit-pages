Thoughts of Towers and Tablets, an Idea.

Just sitting here after work, catching up on the Tower Changes and reading the posts about towers being mid.

Reading the reason why Towers are getting hate is because it’s one of the two ways to juice our maps. The other being delirium.

Noting that Players arnt enjoying having to path through non-juicy map nodes looking for a cluster of towers, then pathing to each tower so you can juice the REMAINING maps in that cluster.

After reading a few posts, an idea strikes me.

What if tablets had a second use? What if we could use tablets like a consumable that had charges? And what if we could place those consumables directly into our mapping device, when we wanted to use them?

Imagine, you start mapping and complete the introductory sequence of quests and you unlock a “slot” on your map device to place a tablet. As you complete more end game related tasks you could unlock more slots. Up to a limit, I’m not a game dev so I’ll leave the specifics to others.

So, a recap.

Tablets already have a rolled quantity on them. Use tablets as map device modifiers for X maps. Unlock more slots as you complete end game content. Freely insert or remove tablets as you see fit.

Just a thought. Good luck out there exiles.
