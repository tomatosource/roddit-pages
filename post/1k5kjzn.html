Endgame feels joyless due to lack of drops

I hate to add to the general consensus but something feels off in this game given the total lack of expensive currency drops and frankly how shit and expensive non-meta classes are.

I can craft and flip items from the AH but it all adds to the work. I can speed run maps for Citadels to sell the keys but even there only keys drop. I’ve killed the King a dozen times without a single valuable drop.

Income is there but when it comes from a thousand small pieces it feels joyless.

Uniques are largely worthless, unique strongboxes on average worse than normal ones and rogue exiles naked. Unique maps besides the mirror lake are a waste of time.

Fragments are so expensive I don’t dare to attempt +1 ubers myself, still having a shopping list for something as weak as sorc that’s competing with meta classes for gear.

With the lack of valuable drops no matter the map mods the endgame feels shit and utterly boring.

I’m now at 92 / 550k kills and just yesterday and today I’ve run over 80 full juiced T15/16 maps without a single div drop, the most expensive currency being a single fracturing orb. Most of the valuable omens and such I haven’t seen once.

This feels like a daytime job rather than a game that ought to bring you occasional dopamine.
