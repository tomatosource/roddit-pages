EA Support Pack coin received in steam account

Not seeing many people mention this, but I am aware that there are plenty of players like me having issues with the initial purchase.

Basically, what happened was:

  • Purchased in Steam
  • Got directed to the website to create an PoE account
  • Connected Steam and PoE accounts
  • This is where the situation happened: the coin went to the PoE account, not the Steam account ( for some reason, we got 2 separate accounts)
  • Players like me were not aware and played on the Steam account
  • Coins stuck at the PoE account

After so many methods of reaching out through email, support messages, and no response for so many weeks , YTD night was a shock: there were coins and a notice that the coin was transferred.

Thank you, GGG, it’s a long wait, but I appreciate the support.
