About that skill speed bug, you can get rid of it in long temples if you find a shield bubble mob. Entering and exiting the bubble resets your action speed back to normal.

I get inflicted by that stupid action speed bug in literally every single temple. It drives me insane, because it forces you to portal out in order to fix it.

But I recently discovered that if you walk in and out of a rare mobs shield bubble, the bug will disappear. While this isn’t useful in maps (not many rare mobs in them), this is very useful inside long snake temples. Juiced temples have tons of rare mobs in them and they all have extra monster mods so your chance of finding a shield bubble guy is pretty high.

..Or you could just play a ranged character and one-shot everything off-screen. I’m pretty sure the bug only occurs if you get hit while you’re dodge rolling or something.
