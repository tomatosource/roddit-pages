Leading up to the trade deadline, on Kalshi “Giannis Antetokounmpo’s next team” had a monthly trade volume of $23,318,658. There’s a very real chance him or his agent could’ve profited immensely from this.

Every year around the trade deadline, there’s always a major star at the center of rumors. This year was focused on Giannis. However, rumor of whether Giannis would leave the Bucks caused people to bet a total of $23M as to whether/where he would go on Kalshi, an unregulated prediction market/insider trading platform.

He stays in Milwaukee, and the very next day comes out as a Kalshi stakeholder. Since the beginning of the season Giannis and his agent have fueled rumors of a trade that had drastic effects on a prediction market he was — at best — actively seeking to become an investor in? and at worst already an investor in? It just makes the NBA seem absolutely complacent with this being encouraged.

In a statement, Kalshi said “Antetokounmpo, a two-time MVP, will be the company’s first basketball star to join as a shareholder, and will partner with the company at live events, in marketing, and more.”

An active professional player who is a shareholder in a sports betting/prediction company should be considered a HUGE conflict of interest.
