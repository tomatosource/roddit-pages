A brief history of Devin Booker’s coaches in Phoenix

List of Booker’s coaches

  1. Jeff Hornacek: Went 14-35 in 2016 before getting fired

  2. Earl Watson: Took over for Hornacek and went 9-24 the rest of the season, won 24 games the next season and was fired after an 0-3 start in 2018

  3. Jay Triano: Won 21 games the rest of the season before being fired

  4. Igor Kokoskov: Won 19 games in 2019 before being fired

  5. Monty Williams: By far the most successful coaches in the Booker era. Won 34 games in 2020 as the young core of Ayton, Bridges, Oubre Can Johnson around 23 year old Booker looked like the next big team and they capitalized with their first playoff berth since Steve Nash was a Sun back in 2010. Not only that but they made the finals (First time this century). They followed it up with a franchise record 64 wins in 2022. However, after one of the most brutal playoff collapses in modern history and a down year with just 45 wins in 2023, Monty was sacked

  6. Frank Vogel: Vogel was tasked with leading the newly assembled big 3 with Booker, KD, and Beal. However, he was fired after just one year after being swept by the Wolves

  7. Mike Budenholzer: Bud was even less successful than Vogel as the Suns continued to collapse even further, this time not even winning 40 games or making the play in much less playoffs. He was unsurprisingly fired

Jordan Ott is about to be Booker’s 8th head coach (7th non interim which is actually an NBA record for a player on a single team). For now it seems Booker is content with staying in Phoenix but with yet another coach and roster shakeup (As KD looks to be all but gone) how long before enough is enough ?
