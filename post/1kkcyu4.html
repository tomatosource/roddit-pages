Pros:
100 spirit and some fire resistance
Cons:
20+ divines for a lowest roll corrupted version

^^ If this is incorrect and chaos res actually is considered, disregard this post, it might be worth running.

If you have the amount of money to buy a sacred flame anyway, you could use that to buy a spirit against the darkness jewel, a threaded light with &gt;12% corruption, and a crown of eyes with a spirit implicit instead. Then you’d have almost as much spirit as the scepter would give you, and way more damage. You can even get a diamond with spirit and fire resistance to match the scepter.
