If GGG wants to make PoE 2 a skill combo focused game, then they need to add more mechanics that reward slow gameplay and engagement time length

I don’t know if this idea has been suggested previously, but I would love it if GGG introduced mechanics that directly reward length of time for engaging with mobs. Some of these are in the game already. For example there are rare monsters with a proximity bubble, and you can move objects towards a goal by staying inside a proximity bubble.

What if some rare monsters had proximity bubbles that charge up a rarity meter the longer you stand near it? If you stand nearby for 5-10 seconds, the meter would reach full charge and increase the chances of dropping rewards? Maybe the monster also has low damage projectiles that could knock you back and reset the meter, if you don’t dodge roll them.

I think this could be a cool mechanic and change your tactics for how you work through enemy monsters.
