Who is/are your favorite Celtics team(s) that you wish would have won a title?

Inspired by this fun throwback moment from JT’s Instagram back then. The massive bag of popcorn kills me. And I forgot Luke was on this team :(

Whether the team was in contention for it or not, was there a Celtics team, aside from this season, that you wish would have won?

I only started watching the Celtics in high school, so that’s my frame of reference in case you’re wondering why there’s no ‘80s/‘90s/etc. teams on my list:

  • 2009. That team was even better than 2008. They came out of the gate like a juggernaut and gave me one of the most fun playoff series I’ve seen versus the Bulls. So many overtimes. So many huge shots.
  • 2010. Obvious, I know, but I will go to my grave being mad that we. were. up. at. the half. I distinctly remember the surprise and morose from the halftime show that we were up lmao.
  • 2018. I never expected us to get as far as we had. It was fun to have something to be excited about after Gordon’s leg got destroyed and with Kyrie being out. (And even more so when looking back because 2019 was such a dumpster fire season. That playoff series versus the Bucks 😭
  • 2022. We finally, finally (!!!!) got past the hump and won the ECF even if it was by a hair lmao. One of my favorite teams because the defense was unreal and it was our most homegrown team which I have a huge soft spot for. We could have been up 3-1 but Steph went nuclear on us. It also helped that they were a perfect “villain” team to me because I’ve found Klay & Draymond annoying since that 2016 season (Klay and his I guess his feelings just got hurt comment in the finals…I have never rooted for a LeBron team in my life, but that moment had me being like I really need the Warriors to get taken down. Needless to say, I never rooted for a non-Celtics team in the finals harder than the Cavs that year. The Pacers from this finals probably ties it for me.) and I wanted us to end their reign. But alas. I hope Al goes to Denver.
