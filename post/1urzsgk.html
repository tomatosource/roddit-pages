Here’s how to craft a +13 staff + a “cheap” way to craft +12 staffs

EDIT: RIP staffs overtaking wands, i did not realise Legacy of Runeseekers Call was exclusive to wands, therefore these crafts do not work. Sorry guys.
Also half the crafting steps got deleted, reddit seems to dislike long lists, but since its dead i cant be bothered to retype them

Feel free to instead use the thread for any crafting questions you have about wands/staffs and ill answer them tomorrow

Staffs may have finally overtaken wands in spell levels again.

Here’s how:

  1. Slam a regal orb to make it rare and pray it adds a prefix (cant currently think of a way to guaranteed add a prefix, if it adds a suffix you just have to pray that your desecration will hit the suffix added by this)
  2. If you don’t have another prefix use an Omen of Sinistral Exaltation together with an exalted orb to slam one (if you get max mana its a bit annoying as you have to annul it off or something)
  3. Socket an Astrids Creativity
  4. Use an Omen of Dextral Necromancy together with a jawbone to add a suffix desecration
  5. Socket a Thruds Might

Estimated cost for this is likely a decent bit cheaper than the +12 Wand craft which was around 40k div, since you dont rely on corruption first hitting a socket and then double corruption hitting the +1 to levels.

In case you want something a bit more reasonable you can instead craft a +12 staff:

  1. We follow the same steps as above up until Step
  2. Instead of vaaling we use an Omen of Sanctification together with a divine Orb to sanctify the item

Id estimate the cost for this craft at around 1000 div + another 1000 for the legacy, which for a +12 staff is quite reasonable and a LOT cheaper than going the corruption route. Especially since sanctify can never fully brick the item so you can always sell it on the market for a decent chunk.

If you do actually want to take the +13 craft to the next level you can socket the legacy early, add a Serles Triumph, which will grant you +2 to suffixes (Credit to Belton on YouTube for this tech). This will require you to hit an extra socket from corruption and THEN hit the +1 to spell skills from double corruption. If you don’t hit the socket you can use an Orb of Extraction to get the legacy back. If you double corrupt and it doesn’t poof you can also recover the legacy. If you hit, this would give you a +13 Staff with 7 modifiers (4 of which used for the levels, so only 3 open for other stuff). EDIT: This will not work for the sanctification route, as we would be one socket short.

Id estimate this pushes the cost somewhere near 100k div tho, so i doubt we will ever see this.

Good luck crafting!
