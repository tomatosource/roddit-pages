Analysis of Concentrated effect on Spearfield

Hi, please take this analysis with a grain of salt as the skill is not out yet and there can be a thousand little things that I didn’t account for. Still, this should be an insightful overview on the skill that let’s you look at it from another light.

There’s a very interesting line in how Spearfield creates the spears: “Creates 1 spear per 0.2 metres of radius”. As you know, area scales as the square of radius, and conversely radius scales as the square root of area. Therefore, reducing the area of Spearfield reduces the number of spears by taking a square root of the factor. For example, Conc. effect reduces the area to half, but reduces the number of spears by only 29%. This means that the spears are more densely packed.

But here comes the hard part: Explosion radius. As this is also affected by area modifiers, we now need to find out if the denser spears outweigh the smaller explosions. And yes, I assume that all the spears will explode as I think that’s what the new Delayed Reaction support gem does. At this point, I will throw math out of the window and start simulating. How many hits do you get per cast and how does the enemy size ® affect it? I made the assumption that the angle of the sector is 75 degrees and that position of the spears in the sector is random. The visualization shows only one example cast but the average hits are from 1000 samples. First for lvl 1 Spearfield, with skill radius of 4m:

Fig 1. Visualization and averages for Spearfield lvl 1

In case it’s not clear: Red circle is enemy, dots are spear spawns, green circles are explosions that hit, grey circles are missed explosions. As we see, these numbers are quite close to each other for with and without conc effect. Next I plotted it for smaller increments of r (again 1000 samples per datapoint):

Fig 2. Average Hits vs Target Radius for Spearfield lvl 1

In Fig 2 we can see that they are quite close to each other until at around r = 0.8 where the unsupported starts to gain the lead. This is because the target is so big, you hit with almost every spear anyways. Then, more spears = better.

Next I did the same plots but for skill radius of 6.9 which is the default for lvl 20 Spearfield:

Fig 3. Visualization and averages for Spearfield lvl 20

It’s quite shocking how much the number of hits reduce with the larger skill area (Fig 3 vs Fig 1). I guess the takeaway here is that don’t be surprised if you level up your Spearfield and notice no difference in damage. As you can see though, now the Conc. effect version has much more spears and is nowhere near reaching the limit at target size = 0.9.

Fig 4. Average Hits vs Target Radius for Spearfield lvl 20

In Fig 4 we can see a similar shape as in Fig 2 where the Conc. version overtakes the original and then later plateaus again, but here Conc gets noticeably better and for a longer range.

To conclude, I would say the skill is quite well balanced, and the practical choice for the user is as simple as it sounds: If you want more damage at the price of AoE, use Conc. effect.
