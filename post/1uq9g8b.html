Amulet Crafting: 20 Clicks = 1 Divine

https://preview.redd.it/y7ervw5gnvbh1.png?width=2746&format=png&auto=webp&s=9120912b070746d1236ff30bb0e3c0109468aecc

With the current amulet market, crafting +3 skill amulets is very profitable. On average, you’re making about 1 div every 20 Chaos orb clicks.

If you can click around 30 times per minute, that’s roughly 90 div/hour worth of expected value (purely from clicking), lol.

Here’s the method:

  • Buy a Gold Amulet with +50 Spirit (fractured) and annul to 2 mods. They’re currently selling for around 40 div.
  • Then simply Chaos spam until you hit one of these:
    • +3 to all Melee Skills
    • +3 to all Projectile Skills
    • +3 to all Minion Skills
    • +3 to all Spell Skills

According to poe2db, it takes about 420 Chaos Orbs on average to hit one of those four outcomes.

Current market prices are roughly:

  • +3 Melee: 80 div
  • +3 Projectile: 198 div
  • +3 Minion: 69 div
  • +3 Spell: 150 div

That gives an average sale price of ~124 div.

Assuming a 40 div base and 60 div chaos cost (1:7 ratio), your expected profit is:

  • 124 − 40 - 60 = 24 div per successful craft.

Since the expected cost is about 420 Chaos, that works out to approximately:

  • 0.05 div profit per Chaos click
  • ~1 div every 20 clicks

One might argue that the market is close to saturation, but I don’t think that’s the case.

The demand for these amulets is still very high because people are buying them as bases to Sanctify into +5 or +6 amulets. In reality, you’re selling the next person’s crafting base. The crafting chain just keeps going, which helps maintain demand.

Another nice bonus is that if you happen to hit the +3 you’re actually looking for, you can simply stop chaos spamming and start investing into your own amulet instead.

From there, you can keep crafting it with Essences, Catalyzing Omens, Purple/Light Omens, or any other upgrades you want. So even if your goal isn’t to just make currency, this strategy lets you continuously sink your profits back into improving your own endgame amulet over time, with the chance of eventually ending up with a +5 or even +6.
