Temporary minions in PoE2 / druid ascendancy

Hi GGG. Love your game!

Here is an idea for a Druid Ascendancy Class - the Bone Seer. A temporary minion/ spell caster ascendancy. I think temporary minions feel quite niche atm, especially in boss fights.

First the nodes with prerequesites:

Temporary minions create +1 minion and last 50% longer, +4 to maximum number of temporary minions - PREREQUISITE: Temporary minions do not need a corpse to be created

Maximum 20 Demonflame, Minions have empowered Version of Minion Instability - PREREQUISITE: Temporary minions have demon form. Minion demon form also affects attack damage and speed.

Double the number of corpses created, spells that use corpses have 30% more AoE and automatically use nearby corpses - PREREQUISITE: Create 3 corpses near you every second, Corpses have 30% more life and give minions created of those corpses 30% more life.

Now to the rest of the nodes, which dont have prerequesites:

Aura that takes 10% of minions life each second to give you one automated spell projectile per minion life taken. Scales with spell damage, not minion dmg.

If you have more than 20 temporary minions or you consumed a corpse recently you have 20% more life, more movement speed, ES, more armour, faster start ES

Your offerings have 50% more effect, can be created by targeting a corpse, have 50% increased AoE and duration and follow you. You are limited to one offering.

These three nodes have no prerequesites.

Finally there is one node, where you can choose from 5 options (like pathfinder concoction skill), also no prerequesites:

  1. New temporary minion skill: Gravetorn: Stationary ranged minion that shoots exploding projectile. Lasts for 10 seconds, Max 3 minions
  2. Melee Spectres become temporary minions, +40% minion speed and attack speed, 40% less damage
  3. Zombies get new ability: Zombies jump at enemies from great distance sacrificing 30% of life for 3 blood explosions/ can overlap. Damage per life sarificed.
  4. Constructs/ Unearth new ability: can throw corpses near them at enemies
  5. Wolves become temporary minions. Gain new ability: Death Howl. When a Wolf gets hit, Wolves howls and gives you and other wolves more attack and spell damage. 5 seconds cooldown. Lasts for 8 seconds and can stack up to 5 times.

I was thinking about a penalty for permanent minions if you choose this ascendancy. Something like XX% more spirit cost for permanent minion skills. I just thought about the big nodes.

Hope you like some ideas! It’s not so much about the exact numbers, but more about the general ideas. Feel free to post your own ideas for temporary minions.
