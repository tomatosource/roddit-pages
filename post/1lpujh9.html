There are two things that I absolutely hate in this game

First one I think affects most of the players. It’s the unnecessary obstacles in maps, where you can’t avoid while using wasd layout bur have no trouble when you’re using mouse. wasd is such a convenient way to play, but you’re always stuck in some kind of stupid obstacle that it takes you a couple of seconds to understand when there are a lot going on like ground effects etc. And there are so many maps with these ocstacles. It doesn’t add any difficulty, but it gives an immense frustration, at least to me. What do you think? Do you also find it frustrating?

Second one affects only a minority I think. Headhunter is such a blessing while mapping, but the moment you get the teleport buff (which is like 70% in my experience), it becomes (again) frustrating. In the middle of attack it just teleports you randomly, and sometimes it even lands you somewhere really dangerous. Sure you can just equip, unequip it to remove the buffs, but then what’s the point if using it. And also you become vulnerable while you equip unequip. I think giving the player the option to remove the debuff by right clicking at the specific buff (like you can do the shrines) would be an enormous improvement.
