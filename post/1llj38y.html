Azmerian Wisps need some love

I think this is not a controversial take, so I’ll skip the analysis, and get straight into some quick suggestions:

  • Wisps should simply take over the nearest monster, and convert it to a rare, powering up by absorbing the mobs around it. Having to follow one for 3⁄4’s of a map, trying to NOT kill the rare when you clear the white trash out of your way is a tad annoying. YES, they power up as they pass through monsters, but, umm, what if you find it when the maps already 90% cleared?
  • The drop rate of the Talismans is far too low, I have killed hundreds of possessed monsters, and have a small handful of talismans
  • There is apparently a special type of wisp: I am level 95, I have either never seen one or never noticed the upgrade. I have certainly never seen a unique conversion.
    • Sacred Wisps
    • Upgrades Normal Monsters into Magic Monsters
    • Upgrades Magic Monsters into Rare Monsters
    • Upgrades Rare Monsters into Unique Bosses
  • The atlas points and occasional wisp modifiers are interesting, but until the rewards are increased, you’re better off heading in another direction,
  • There should be some sort of pinnacle map/encounter you work towards (unless there is and I missed it!)
