[The Athletic] Adam Silver suggests Kawhi Leonard investigation would need to yield substantial evidence for NBA to act

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6616959/2025/09/10/adam-silver-kawhi-leonard-nba-investigation/?source=user_shared_article

Silver told reporters after a board of governors meeting Wednesday that he would be “reluctant to act” without substantial proof.

Silver said the burden of proof lies with the league if it is to punish the Clippers, Ballmer or Leonard.

While the NBA’s collective bargaining agreement gives Silver the ability to rely on circumstantial evidence to discipline the Clippers and Ballmer if he sees fit, Silver said he would want more than that before levying a punishment.
