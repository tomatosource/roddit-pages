Poe2 and seizures

Hey y’all, not sure if it’s been mentioned but there’s no warning at the start up of the game so I just wanted to take this time and share my experiences.

I’ve been diagnosed with epilepsy since 2019. One of my triggers is bright, flashing lights, colors usually don’t matter but some make a difference compared to others on intensity.

Games like vampire survivor, death must die and unfortunately even poe, can trigger my seizures depending on what’s going on in the screen/map.

I’ve noticed over the last week, while playing my autobomber and or spark gem, 2 builds that utilize a bunch of bright blue skills that “flash”(spark, frost wall, greater lightning bolt, comet, freeze effects, character teleporting everywhere for auto) tends to cause my aura to flare up. If I push thru, I realize I seize out within an hour, where if I turn the game off and get into complete darkness im usually ok.

I’ve tried other builds, such as poison ranger, and a mace warrior, both are utilizing darker skills, that aren’t as “flashy” that just POP in and out the screen in a millisecond and I’ve noticed that the above mentioned happens far more less frequent, if at all.

Not saying poe in and if itself can cause seizures but people with epilepsy such as myself should consider certain builds or rather skills? as it could affect us IRL and poe should at least put some kind of warning at the start of the game every time u boot it up(or if there is one, make it bigger/more noticeable)

I just wanted to make sure other players are safe while playing. There’s a lot of things to make an exile go mad between the 2 versions, we don’t want nor need seizures on that list 😆

Yours truly, a fellow epileptic exile
