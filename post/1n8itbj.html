[BasketNews] Trey Lyles finalizing a move to Real Madrid

Trey Lyles is set to bring his NBA experience to Europe as he is finalizing his move to Real Madrid, BasketNews sources confirm. The 29-year-old forward played 650 NBA games and averaged 7.6 points.

Trey Lyles, a 10-year NBA veteran, is finalizing an agreement with EuroLeague powerhouse Real Madrid, sources tell BasketNews.

The 29-year-old Canadian power forward is set to make his first move to Europe after a decade-long NBA career that began in 2015 when he was selected 12th overall in the NBA Draft.

Encestando was the first to report that Lyles is on his way to Madrid.

Lyles played in 650 NBA games (131 starts) for the Utah Jazz, Denver Nuggets, San Antonio Spurs, Detroit Pistons, and, most recently, the Sacramento Kings.

Over his NBA career, Lyles averaged 7.6 points, 4.3 rebounds, and 1.1 assists in 18.4 minutes per game, while shooting 34.7% from beyond the arc.

In the 2024-25 season with Sacramento, he contributed 6.5 points, 4.6 rebounds, and 1.2 assists in under 20 minutes of action.

His most productive NBA campaign came in 2021-22, when he split the season between Detroit and Sacramento, averaging a career-best 10.5 points and 5.1 rebounds per game.

Lyles also represented Team Canada at the 2024 Paris Olympics, posting 2.0 points and 1.8 rebounds en route to a quarterfinal finish.
