GGG, please let me reiterate my suggestion to really fix towers, as most people seem to like it.

Three weeks ago I suggested two changes to improve the tower system. The first was somewhat controversial, but the second was universally well received.

In light of the conversation on towers, please let me reiterate and insist that it’s a change that carries only upsides, and should be extremely easy to implement:

Rather than apply to a random subset of maps, make the tablet effects apply to all maps in range until a counter on the tower runs out. Keep the league mechanic icon on top of the tower rather than each map, with a 3x, 4x, etc counter next to it.

This change is totally compatible with today’s tower improvement: Towers would normally be affected by other towers. This change achieves two very positive things at a negligible dev cost:

  • This removes the need to run bad maps first to ensure effects are randomly allocated to the desired ones, and actually rewards the player for efficient pathing. No more pre-running unjuiced, horrible layouts so your randomly allocated icons can land on the good maps.
  • Also removes a lot of visual clutter by centralizing the icons on a few places. You could still see the number of elon “things” on each map by hovering over it, it just doesn’t need to clutter the map screen.

Other players are suggesting towers to simply apply to all maps, which is a fair idea. However, I think the counter solution doesn’t affect the power level as much, and more importantly allows players more flexibility in ignoring map layouts they don’t like, by mitigating the FOMO they would get by otherwise leaving juiced maps unopened, which would happen if towers affected every map with no limit.
