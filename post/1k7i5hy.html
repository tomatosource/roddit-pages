The Trial of Chaos is more based on luck than anything else

The two ascension methods are different, but I always do the 3rd and 4th via The Trial of the Sekhemas

With Sekhemas, I really feel that my ascensions are down to my experience and the knowledge gained doing them. What relics to use, how to plan out a path, how to be cautious with rooms that only have one next-room choice, how to complete the various room types, how to beat the bosses.

The Trial of Chaos is simply too much hoping that you don’t get three bad tribulations, or that darn tornado vulture instead of the dopey other bird that’s about 100 times easier.

I truly believe that an experienced player who knows the pitfalls (never choose “a random affliction!”) will not get beaten via bad luck, but Chaos is just a never ending addition of things that only make it harder, there’s no reprieve, no chance to remove any, and no other engagement like the vendor, or the treasure room, just variations of “don’t die”

EDIT:

I honestly think a lot of players tried it, found it too hard/slow, and gave up. When you actually learn the systems, Sekhemas is as rewarding and (OK, nearly) as fast as Chaos.
