Ailment identity change ideas

Currently ailments are tied to the damage of the original hit and generally aren’t all that interesting.

None of the DoTs below have their damage calculated from the base hit, except Bleed.

To shake things up, how about these ideas:

- **Ignite** Short duration damage over time that doesn’t stack but can be reapplied, resetting the timer. Each consecutive second an enemy remains ignited causes the target to take increased fire damage from all sources. Stacks indefinitely if you are able to consistently keep the enemy ignited.

- **Ignite (melee):** if the source of an ignite is melee, the base duration is doubled.

- **Poison:** Deals physical and chaos damage over time. Stacks indefinitely. Certain skills and weapon attacks can add more than one stack at a time.

-*Plagued*: If an enemy has lost 30% or more of their maximum health as poison damage in the last X seconds, they become plagued and their healing regeneration is reduced by a percentage per poison active on the enemy for a moderate duration.

- **Poison (melee):** Strikes with melee weapons inflict extra poison stacks based on your attack speed.

- **Charged (lightning)**: The next ranged hit will consume the charge and restore mana and energy shield. Enemies can only become charged once per X seconds.

-**Charged (melee)**: The next melee hit will consume the charge, restore mana and increase movement and attack speed for a duration. Enemies can only become charged once per X seconds.

-**Chilled**: Slows enemy movement and attack speeds per stack of chilled. As long as an enemy remains continuously chilled, their stacks of chilled will automatically increase every 4 seconds, up to a maximum of 6 stacks.

**Chilled (melee)**: Melee hits against chilled enemies grant the frostveil debuff, reducing all damage taken but increasing cooldowns and mana costs.

**Frozen**: Each time you deal cold damage to a chilled enemy, there is a chance to freeze them for 4 seconds. Enemies must have at least two stacks of chilled before they are able to be Frozen.

**Frozen (melee)**: Melee hits against frozen enemies will shatter them, dealing physical and cold damage in a large AOE. Each enemy shattered has a chance to grant an endurance charge.

**Bleeding**: Kept as currently working in POE 2, based on the hit damage rather than its own separate DoT.

**Bleeding (melee)**: Melee hits against bleeding enemies have significantly increased critical strike chance. Killing a bleeding enemy with a melee critical strike will cause a gory explosion and heal the attacker for a percentage of their maximum life.
