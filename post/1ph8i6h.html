Concerned about spell totems

I want to preface this by saying that we absolutely do not have all the information yet, but based on what we do know, the current itteration looks DoA from a stand-alone archetype perspective.

They appear to be aimed at self casters to provide a bit of burst damage for bosses. They have a short duration, significant less damage multipliers, and require charge generation. If that’s the objective, then they look like a decent addition.

However, there would have to be some major as yet unknown information, to make them even remotely viable as as a stand alone archetype, ie pizza totems from poe1.

Maybe there is some sort of astral bond-like notable that turns off your damage, but boosts your totems, maybe increasing the cap and removing the charge requirements?

Balancing totems is difficult, I do get that, the damage uptime compared to self cast means totems do need to be kept in check someway, otherwise nobody would selfcast. Poe1 is close to having it right, they are at least viable, just not particularly great (hence why the Play-share is always less than 1%). They have to give up any defence if they want to compete on the damage front, maybe a small buff would help there.

Not dooming, im just hoping that there is either more support for totems to make them viable as a standalone archetype, or that maybe in 0.4 they are focusing on them as a support, learning from that, before they look to implement spell totems as an archetype in the future.
