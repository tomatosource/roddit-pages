The Oklahoma City Thunder are the first team in NBA history to have double digit win increases in 3 straight seasons

The Thunder increased their win total every year by double digits every year since the 21-22 season.

21-22: 24 wins

22-23: 40 wins

23-24: 57 wins

24-25: 67 wins (and counting)

Really hard to articulate how impressive their rebuild has been orchestrated at an organizational level from the top down.

Nailed the Paul George trade, hit on a lot of their draft picks (JDub, Chet, Wiggins, Wallace), developed their bench guys into valuable rotational players (Joe, Kenrich), signed key players in the offseason (Caruso, Hartenstein), hired the right coach and built perfectly around their best players’ strengths.

OKC is poised to be competitive for years to come.
