New scam alert? Idol of Estazunti

TLDR at end

Hey, this has probably been going around for a while, but it was my first time running into it so I figured I’d share.

So I was looking to buy a castaway map for 1 divine. I usually get my gold from TFT, but service providers are harder to find now, so I decided to ask in global/trade chat instead. Right away, someone whispers me.

I think, “cool, that was fast,” and join their party. They tell me there’s still a couple maps before the castaway, but they can host the castaway for “free.” I tell them that’s fine, I can still pay the 1 div since that’s what my post was for, and to let me know when they’re ready to run it.

Then they say there’s a sealed vault map right before castaway and they don’t have an idol of estazunti.

I don’t map often (I mostly farm trial of sekhemas), so I didn’t have one on me. I figure, “ok, no big deal, idols were only a few chaos last time I checked.” I look it up and see they’re like 60chaos now. I tell the guy they’re expensive and ask if he has other maps.

Link to idol of estazuntis: https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2/search/poe2/Rise%20of%20the%20Abyssal/R4gp7agi7

He says he can route around it, but it’ll take a long time, and that he needs an Idol if I want to do Castaway. I tell him to just farm the maps around sealed vault and to let me know when he’s ready.

Immediately after, I get kicked from the party. I think, “eh, whatever, maybe he’ll find one later.” About 20 minutes later I whisper him again and find out I’m blocked.

So yeah, this is probably common sense, but don’t go buying idols for randoms, especially if they try to bait you with something like a “free castaway.” There’s no guarantee they actually have what they say they do, or that they’ll share anything from the sealed vault after you cover the cost of the idol.

Just a heads up. Cheers everyone!

On the bright side, I just passed my final :)

TLDR: Got whispered after posting to buy a castaway map. The guy said he’d host it “for free” but needed an idol of estazunti for a sealed vault map first. When I didn’t buy the idol for him, he kicked me and blocked me. Don’t buy idols (or anything) for randoms promising “free” maps/service — it’s a scam.
