Since 2016-17, One of these four NBA players has led the NBA in moving the least distance per time played while on defense: Dirk Nowitzki, James Harden, Luka Doncic, Chris Paul

The stats is average defensive speed, tracked by nba.com at https://www.nba.com/stats/players/speed-distance?Season=2025-26&dir=A&sort=AVG_SPEED_DEF

The rankings among qualified players:

2016-17: James Harden 1st (Dirk DNQ, but would be 3rd, Paul 7th)

2017-18: Dirk Nowitzki 1st (Harden 2nd, Paul 7th)

2018-19: James Harden 1st (Dirk would be 1st with 7 more games played, Chris Paul 4th, Luka 28th)

2019-20: James Harden 1st (Paul 2nd, Luka 6th)

2020-21: Chris Paul 1st (Harden DNQ but would have been 1st with 6 more games, Luka 3rd)

2021-22: Chris Paul 1st (Harden 2nd, Luka 3rd)

2022-23: Chris Paul 1st (Harden 2nd, Luka 3rd)

2023-24: Luka Doncic 1st (Chris Paul DNQ but would be 2nd with 1 more game played, Harden 2nd)

2024-25: James Harden 1st (Paul 2nd, Luka 5th)

2025-26 currently: Luka Doncic 1st (Harden 2nd, CP DNQ)

Stat is mostly dominated by first options with high usage, which makes a lot of sense, as they need to save energy on Defense. The crazier stat here is that not would one of these 4 led, but 2 of these 4 would have made up the top 2 every year since 2017-18 if Harden/Dirk could have met the minimum game requirements.
