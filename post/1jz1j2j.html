You don’t even need a loot filter…

So, I am currently in tier 10 maps SSF. I’ve customized my loot filter to show what I want and recently I decided to turn it off for a few maps cause I felt like almost nothing was dropping. Turns out, it still feels that way without the filter.

I remember last season I felt the need for a loot filter before I even hit maps. Now in tier 10 with a map that has 6 affixes and multiple league mechanics I did not even need a loot filter.

I’m sure this gets better at T15, but I really feel like something has changed.
