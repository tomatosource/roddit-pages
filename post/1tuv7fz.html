Sprinting is fine how it is

Pretty much the title. GGG obviously put sprinting into the game as a quality of life to back track or when you die and need to get from a checkpoint back to the fight. If you want to use it in-between mob packs then it becomes a high risk high reward mechanic where the reward is faster clear time. The skill is being able to identify when it’s safe to start and animation cancling with a dodge roll before the next pack.
