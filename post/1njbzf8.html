Spells on items need a rework

While many spell builds are good now, I myself play one, there is one big problem with spells: The +level to spells modifiers are too strong comparing to every other mod.

Think about it like this: On Wands:
Prefix:
- Spell damage t1: 119% increased Spell Damage. If you have 0 spell damage node allocated, this mod will give you 119% more damage. Cool
- Gain damage as elemental: 30% gain, if you have 0 gain source, this mod will give you 30% more damage only if it is the main type of damage you’re getting increased/more damage from

Suffix:
- Cast speed: 24% increased Cast Speed: Maximum of 24% more damage, if you have 0 cast speed nodes allocated
- Levels of all spells: level 5: This gives you 76% more damage, no matter what you have allocated, and raise mana cost

This mod is absurdly strong comparing to everything else, in PoE 1, the mod gets to maximum +1 level and give 12% more damage for you. In order to balance this, there are added damage to spells mod to give you more base damage, acting basically just like the +level mod, with the caveat that fast hitting spells benefit less from this modifier. This, however really complicate things and I like the fact that GGG tries to remove redundant stuff from the game.

This is why items without the maximum +level mods on them are useless to spell casters. You NEED +5 on wand, NEED +3 on amulet, NEED +3 on focus, everything else is secondary, and then casters need to compensate with getting mana. This results in every caster weapon looking kind of the same, having the same +x level to a specific kind of spell, same on amulet & focus. Kind of boring

Easiest solution:
- Reduce the power of the +level mod to +3 or something, raise the power of other mods.
- Mana should be easier to come by, and synergies between certain stuff reduced, like Stormweaver’s double shock literally x2 the damage of Lightning Conduit, or Bloodmage x2 the crit power of fire skills.
- On that node, base crit chance on spells should be brought to roughly the same value, some might have bigger synergy with crit, but having Cold spells having 13% crit while Fire spells have 7% crit is certainly a weird choice.
- An alternate way to scale damage should be made. In PoE1 you have Elemental Overload, which makes you deal 40% more damage for 8 seconds after criting, but makes it so that your crit damage does not deal extra damage (removing the power of crit damage bonus). It’s a bit weak, but earlier on it’s a good way to grab extra damage. I’d like to see it in PoE 2, maybe deal 80% more damage for 8 seconds after dealing a critical strike. Your critical strikes do not deal damage. (Kind of like the line on the Shade of Solaris unique wand)
