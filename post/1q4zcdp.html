Why doesn’t Isaiah Stewart get more love for 6MOY award?

The 6th man of the year award almost always goes to an offensive spark plug, which is because its just easier to convey their impact, but is it possible for a defensive player to win?

Stewart is:

— 1st in bench blocks

— 2nd in total blocks

— 3rd in blocks per game

People have been pushing for consideration for DPOY, but given his MPG there’s near 0% chance he wins, but he truly is one of the best defensive centers in the league, and is top 5 in opp rim FG%.

Is this a case where a player like this should be pushed as the 6MOY instead of the DPOY?
