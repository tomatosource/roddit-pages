Teaser2 for 0.3 has a Hidden Teaser: A Lightless Creature in the Shadows

I was looking at the teaser 2 with some video editing software to try to get some more information and by playing around with light, contrast and visual noise levels of the video I was able to illuminate some of the surroundings that are otherwise harder to see.

We already know the teaser 100% spoils that in 0.3 we’re getting “The Lightless” that appeared in PoE lore during the Winter of the World and were eventually defeated and driven underground by Solaris and Lunaris and the Third Pact (An alliance between humans, dog creatures, snake creatures, the titans and the breachlord Chayula) this is confirmed lore.

Besides teasing The Lightless on the right of the unlocking bone door in the shadows there appears to he a Lightless creature with 2 horns (horns are a signature design for The Lightless) holding a scepter and wearing a robe hidden in the shadows.

It could be a statue, but the creature is wearing clothes. The clothes are also made from a different material than the “stone looking” body. If it was a sculpted statue it would all be one material instead of wearing clothes “over” its body.
