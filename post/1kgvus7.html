Expect The Knicks To Come Out Strong

The Knicks must have watched that game last night like the cartoon dog with the eyes popping out & the tongue flopping all over the floor. The way the Pacers stole that game is very much aligned with the blueprint the Knicks want to follow, and I’m worried the Celtics are in danger. Even putting aside the off-kilter 3-attempts from Tatum and the lack of set-plays from Joe, the Celtics have just been playing slow, lethargic, low-energy basketball these whole playoffs. I’m sure the physical intensity of the defense that’s allowed — and how that can disrupt clean perimeter passing — is having an impact on their shooting and their rhythm. But still, it’s hard not to watch these Celtics so far & not see a team that is playing with the stiffness and slowness of a much older team. If they can’t conjure a real playoff intensity, they might be looking at a 15 or 20 point deficit early in the game tonight.
