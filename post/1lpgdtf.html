The masterful offseason for the Denver Nuggets has come to them courtesy of an interim GM

Ben Tenzer - the recently named interim GM-turned-VP of Basketball Operations of the Denver Nuggets - has been making moves this offseason in stark contrast to the Calvin Booth front office of old.

Here’s a quick recap of all the changes made to the roster thus far, be it through trade or signings:

In:

  • Cameron Johnson
  • Bruce Brown
  • The option to use most of the MLE now that MPJ’s contract is off the books
  • Jonas Valančiūnas 
  • Tim Hardaway Jr.

Out:

  • 2032 Unprotected 1st
  • Michael Porter Jr.
  • Dario Saric

More moves may be on the way with roster slots in need of filling and with the freed up money for the MLE that Denver is able to throw around.

Shout out to Ben 10.
