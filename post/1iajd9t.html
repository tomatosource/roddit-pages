Tyrese Maxey’s last 10 games: 31/8/4 on 58 TS%

This is probably the best stretch of Tyrese Maxey’s career as a #1 option. Maxey has been scoring efficiently, and even creating for others, since Joel Embiid’s last game 1⁄4.

I believe a large part of Maxey’s offensive resurgence is the insertion of Guerschon Yabusele at center over Andre Drummond. When Maxey plays with Yabu at C, the Sixers have an OFFRTG of 119.2 and a NETRTG of -0.6 (596 min). When Maxey plays with Andre Drummond at C, the Sixers have an OFFRTG of 104.1 and NETRTG of -14.7 (279 min).

Notably, Drummond has only played 47 min over Maxey’s last 10 games. For the Sixers, finding a lineup in which they can at least tread water without Embiid is a huge deal. Playing Maxey with a legitimate stretch 5 (Yabu has shot 39.4% on 5.4 3PA/36 min) seems to be the key to unlocking the offense.

Of course, this hasn’t translated into winning. They’re 3-7 over Maxey’s last 10 games, but it’s worth noting that all of their losses have been to teams in the top 8 of each conference. The Sixers just can’t beat good teams with Embiid, McCain, Caleb Martin, and many more rotation players out. Still, it’s been nice to see Maxey playing well with superstar usage (8th in USG% over the last 10 games and 15th on the season). With Paul George now injured again, Maxey might have to take on an even bigger role.
