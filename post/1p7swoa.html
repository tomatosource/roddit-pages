Erik Spoelstra joins Popovich and Sloan as the only coaches with 800+ wins for one franchise

Erik Spoelstra just picked up career win No. 800 tonight.

He’s now:

  • The 17th coach in NBA history to reach 800 wins, and
  • Only the 3rd coach ever to win 800+ games with a single team, joining: 
    • Gregg Popovich – Spurs
    • Jerry Sloan – Jazz
    • Erik Spoelstra – Heat
