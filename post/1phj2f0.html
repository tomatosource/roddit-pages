Ascendancies for Wolf (/Bear?)

I am tempted to league start wolf but i don’t know which ascendancy to pick.

Since I will be mainly on melee range, I will need strong defensives.

As a given we have that both talismans and skills require str+intel, about 86 each for lvl20 gems. The way I see it, this sort of excludes the monk & ranger?

I wanted to find out the best choice and I was leaning into smith or witchhunter or druid shaman.

So smith offers the best defensive toolkit, crit immunity, phys 25% migitation, extra life, more spirit and potentially easier reach to 80%+ resistance caps. No good offensive power though i think(havent played it ever), plus you are giving up your chest (though you do get some armor this time!). It’s highly unlikely a rare chest can beat this, but i dont know about uniques and some are buffed quite a bit!

Witch hunter now gives sorcery ward that also covers physical & chaos along with elemental damage, however the recharge time now will be like 6 seconds and i think that we need proper evasion too in order to buy time for ward to recover because 6 seconds is an eternity for non boss fights. Good thing about WH is that there’s also offensive power choices, either take the explody or buffed no mercy.
Can also fall back to Xbows if this does not work!

Druid shaman is also a mix, you get 15% phys into elemental, plus a complicated elemental mitigation, like 4% for the dmg type you got hit stacking and lasting for 10 seconds. No protection from chaos and the elemental one is amazing for small hits but not so much for large ones. As for dmg, you get more spirit by sacrificing charms, or rage buffs or the one side we dont know about.

Any ideas?? I also suspect that druid will start on the warrior point and related passive skills will be on that side easier to reach, though it’s not as if you cant reach as WH.
