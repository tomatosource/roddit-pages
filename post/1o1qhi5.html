A look at Michael Jordan’s 107 preseason games

Jordan played 95 of 99 possible preseason games for the Bulls, and 11 of 16 for the Wizards, for a total of 107 of 115 possible preseason games.

AFAIK, there aren’t any cumulative preseason stats easily available anywhere, but bballref does have a good page for Jordan’s High School / Amateur / Exhibition stats. It doesn’t show minutes played or anything but basic scoring stats, but since he consistently scored 20+, one can assume it was for more than a shift or two. (Neither Magic nor Bird have a high school/exhibition page; Lebron does.)

Notable MJ preseason stuff:

- 41 pts on 15⁄24 shooting in a loss to the Nets in 2001 (age 38 season, after 3 years of his second retirement

- 35 pts, also on 15⁄24 shooting in a loss to the Sonics in South Bend, IN in 1995

- 34 pts, again on 15⁄24 shooting in a loss to PHX in 1990

- 37 pts, on 16⁄22 in a win vs DEN in 1989

- 5 pts on 1⁄10 shooting vs ATL in 1996

- 28 pts on 11⁄30 shooting vs NYN in Lansing, MI, 1991

- 36 pts on 12⁄19 shooting vs LAL in Springfield, MO, 1987 (noted as “HOF Game”)

- 44 pts on 14⁄20 shooting (16⁄18 FTA) vs LAL in 1986

- He was on a tear that whole 1986 preseason, averaging 32.4 ppg across 9 games

- The first 1986 preseason game was Bulls (red) vs Bulls (white) at Beloit College in Wisconsin. It apparently counted as an official preseason game.

- 32 pts on 10⁄11 shooting (12⁄13 FTA) vs the Kansas City Kings in St Louis, 1984 (rookie season)

- In 1997, the Bulls played PSG Racing and Olympiakos in Paris (I believe this trip was shown in the Last Dance)

- Most preseasons lasted between 7-10 games

- Some small “markets” in which Jordan played preseason games: Uncasville CT; Grand Rapids MI; Lawrence KS; Albuquerque NM; Lincoln NE; Moline IL; Peoria IL; Dayton OH; Knoxville TN; Iowa City IA; Hamilton CAN; Richmond VA; Bloomington MN; Gary IN; Glen Falls NY
