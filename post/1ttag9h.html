The top of the 2022 draft is looking rough

  1. Paolo Banchero - Heavily flawed player who still has a bunch of questions about whether or not he can be a franchise cornerstone

  2. Chet Holmgren - Elite defender but role player on offense, looking like his ceiling is more limited than we thought (and he constantly chokes against his biggest rival)

  3. Jabari Smith Jr - Role player. Nothing else to really say.

  4. Keegan Murray - Lowkey one of the worst contracts in the league (5 years, 140m). His jumpshot has continuously gotten worse (41% from 3 as a rookie, then 36%, 34%, 28% the last 3 seasons). He also turns 26 in a couple of months so he’s not exactly a young prospect anymore.

  5. Jaden Ivey - His career might already be over for off-court issues.

The next 5 are Bennedict Mathurin and Shaedon Sharpe (both of who have stans who will tell you they are future superstars, but haven’t shown much), and then Dyson Daniels (good player but also heavily flawed), Jeremy Sochan and Johnny Davis (lol)
