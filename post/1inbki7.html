Giving up on SSF

This is more of a rant rather than feedback. Unfortunately i don’t think anything will change, because anything that makes solo play better will end up affecting trade lagues’ economy in a negative way and GGG will never balance for SSF.

I played on HCSSF since launch, and a few years before that in PoE1. I was really excited for the new atlas as i thought it would give us new opportunities for target farming, like there will be special nodes we could chase and find etc, but the reality is, there’s no such thing. Biomes are a wasted opportunity due to tower juicing - there’s no reason to chase specific biomes when you can just influence an area with 3-4 towers and then strip farm them. If there’s a few nodes with the needed biome, cool, if not well those juiced maps in the wrong biome will drop infinitely more of what i need than the correct biome, but not juiced.

I don’t care about drop rates in general, i’m ok with increased difficulty thought it was never the reason for me to choose SSF. But without target farming, the gameplay loop is not that much different from trade. You need something, you juice your maps and hope the item you need drops, that’s it. Well in a trade league you juice your maps and hope for divines. What’s the big difference then?

We don’t even have divination cards now. Honestly those weren’t that good in PoE1 either. Like yeah you could target farm those but the drop rates were balanced with global market in mind. So sometimes you could spend an entire league farming for a specific item because otherwise it would negatively affect the economy. And most uniques never had a div card anyway.
