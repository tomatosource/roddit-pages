Having more currency is good for the game

Please hear me out before responding.

Two scenarios

  1. Currency drops at rate Y.
  2. Currency drops at rate A x Y. (A being some integer)

In both cases, doing any specific content generates currency that allow you to buy gear with more or less the same time investment.

Higher availability means higher prices, but you have more to buy with.

The wash up is simply that the numbers are bigger, your stacks are larger, but the time taken to farm enough to purchase a given item is the same.

Scenario 1, that currency orb is more precious, rarer, and seen primarily as trade currency

Scenario 2, Currency orbs are much more common and can be used to craft without feeling like it’s a waste.

And what does this mean?

People often site the word: “Inflation” and immediately associate it with a negative connotation, because out here in the real world, it is.

But in the real world, money is ONLY used to exchange goods

In the game, Currency is the term used to describe crafting orbs. They are not only used to trade goods.

Having more crafting material be more available means more players would feel comfortable crafting their own gear.

If you disagree, please do me the courtesy to explain why, what about my argument is wrong?
