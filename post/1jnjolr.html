Lich Soulless Form + Necromantic Conduit + Enfolding Dawn

I’ve noticed a potential interaction that could turn Enfolding Dawn’s biggest drawback into a benefit or at least a mixed blessing.

The Lich’s Necromantic Conduit causes you to lose 5% of your Maximum Mana per second as a penalty for it’s sizable benefit. Soulless Form then decouples your mana recovery from your max mana completely, causing it to scale instead off of your max health, netting you 6% of your max health as your base mana recovery.

That got me to thinking. With these two ascendency nodes, you might actually want your maximum mana to be as low as you can comfortably get away with, because the lower your max mana is compared to your max HP the smaller the bite that Necromantic Conduit takes out of your recovery is going to be.

That’s where Enfolding Dawn comes in. Enfolding Dawn states that your max mana no longer scales off of INT, that’s normally a pretty raw deal if your planning to use that beefy 100 spirit to do minion related things, as you’ll need high INT. With these two ascendency nodes however that decoupling potentially serves you well as although it’s still removing INT’s ability to scale up the size of your resource pool, it’s also effectively removing the penalty that INT would otherwise apply to your recovery rate.

Still not a particularly great unique maybe, but I thought it sounded like pretty cool interaction. Im actually thinking about creating a martial Lich as my starter, but I wanted to have a few specters to fight alongside me, so being able to get some spirit off my chest piece instead of a scepter could be a big help.

Edit: All of this, of course, assumes you aren’t using Blackened Heart to scale up Necromantic Conduit. It’s also worth bearing in mind though that using Soulless Form with Blackened Heart may not be advisable in the first place as it will mean you have to be scaling up your max HP in tandem with your max Mana if you want your regen to keep up with the drain of Necromantic Conduit.
