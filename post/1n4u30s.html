Is poe2 new player unfriendly?

This game seems just very unfriendly to new players. I know it’s supposed to be difficult but I think they overdo it a little. It’s not that bosses are very difficult, they have visible patterns for the most part. As it stands, it has to deal with builds and understanding what exactly works and how it works. There’s so many choices to make along the way and once you do make these choices, you have your build and that’s what I am trying to understand how to overcome this gap of knowledge that I lack.

If down the road you find the abilities underwhelming then you need to respec out of them. How am I supposed to know what is good for leveling besides just copying from a guide? Am I supposed to just reroll and call it a day, play most op build and roll the content without learning the game? I want to play with my own build, my own gear, I don’t want to get leveling gear to roll the campaign. But I feel like that is what I should do.

I wish goodluck to everyone enjoying the league.
