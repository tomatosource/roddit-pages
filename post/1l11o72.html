100 T4 Breachstone results

I’ve loved farming breachstones this season for currency, especially on my BM spark where i throw 1 spark and my coc chains for half the breachstone. Since we are getting an update later this week I decided to run 100 breachstones so that I can compare times and drops to the updated version. Also I did die in 1 run so technically this is the results from 99. I only included the prices for items that i could sell within 15 min of running the farm, I have the unidentified HoWa’s for sale at 1 annul and the diamonds at 2 chaos, and am keeping the choirs for myself to corrupt, although they can easily be sold for 2 chaos each. Im not adding this to the total but if you were to run yourself and sell for similar amounts its an extra 4-5 div. Also got tons of tiered rares and ilvl 82 normal bases which can be sold/ id and slammed and potentially add lots of profit, but since they are harder to price and sell I omitted them fully(at the end of the run I had about 1.7 quad tabs full of tiered rares and normal bases).
