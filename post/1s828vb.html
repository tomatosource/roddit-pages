Is there a series that you think of where we lost and really should have won?

  1. 2012 ECF
  • No Avery Bradley
  • No Jeff Green
  • No Jermaine O’Neal
  • No Chris Wilcox

I don’t think this series gets talked about enough in the NBA. We took Miami to 7 games without these key players. Both of our backup bigs were not playing and our backup wing Jeff Green and starting guard Avery Bradley were not playing either. We would have easily, easily won in my opinion and played OKC in the finals which would have been an amazing finals between a young upcoming team and a veteran team.

What if we win? What happens to Lebron in Miami? He just came off losing to Dallas and now the ECF to Boston? What’s next? Does Ray Allen even leave the next season and saves Lebron in the 2013 finals? That would make it 3 years in a row Lebron doesn’t win and it’s his first 3 years in Miami. The fourth year we all saw him damn near get swept by San Antonio. Does he stay there? Or does he still go back to Cleveland? That would mean 0 rings in Miami. So many questions.

  1. 2009 ECSF/2010 Finals
  • No KG
  • No Powe
  • No Perkins

We would have easily won against Orlando in 2009. The Magic beat us in 7 games and we didn’t have KG nor Leon Powe. Again, another team we took to 7 games and Orlando actually went to the finals this season. I can honestly say we would have went to the finals again and ran through Kobe and the Lakers again.

The same thing with 2010. Perkins went down for the last and final game of the season. We lost the game 7 due to rebounding and he was our best rebounder. We lost by only 4 as Kobe had 15 rebounds and Gasol had 18 smfh.

Do we 3 peat? What happens to Kobe’s legacy? Can he win without Shaq?

  1. 2023 NBA ECF
  • JT gets hurt first play of game 7.

Crazy series. We were down 3-0 and came back to tie the series. Only a few other teams in sports history to come back from 3-0 and win, one was the Boston Red Sox and the 4 others were NHL teams, no NBA team has. This would have went down in history. If Tatum doesn’t get hurt I honestly think that we win as we had all the momentum and home court and move on to play Denver which would have been a great series. Murray/Jokic vs Jays. Damn.

  1. 2002 NBA ECF
  • Paul Pierce missed free throws

We were up 2-1 in the ECF and even had the greatest comeback in playoffs history we were down 26 in game 3 and won. In game 4 Paul went 10⁄15 from the ft line and with a second left to tie the game, Paul Pierce went to the line to shoot 2 to the tie the game and he missed both. He himself admits that it was probably his worst moment.

To the Nets credit their strategy was to make him get to the line because they knew they had no chance to stop him. We had a chance to go up 3-1. Also, we would have went the finals if we win this series to play the Lakers and we played them twice that regular season and beat them both times. Granted, Shaq didn’t play one of those games. Now, I don’t think we beat Kobe and Shaq in the finals but still a NBA finals series to consider.

  1. 1987 NBA Finals
  • Bill Walton goes down

This was the only series that didn’t happen in my time but I do think of it because Bird vs Magic is one of the greatest rivalries in sports between 2 players. We beat them in 84 but they came back and beat us in 85 and in 87 Bill Walton was hurt. Yes, we won the rebound battle even with Bill Walton playing only 5 mpg, but I think having a post player who could score would have given us the edge in this series. I remember watching highlights of how big he was for us in the 86 run as he even got the sixth man award.

What if Bird goes up 2-1 against Magic? Does this create any changes in LA? What if we had Bill Walton, does he make a difference in the outcome?
