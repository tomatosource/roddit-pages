Staggering league development between PoE1 and PoE2:EA: A good idea, or counter-productive?

Lately I’ve felt like this perhaps needs to be discussed more. GGG has essentially committed to alternating development resources between PoE2 major patches, and continuing to release leagues on a regular schedule for PoE1.

To those that have high hopes for a strong PoE2 release, whatever the time-frame might be, what thoughts do you have on this? The player-base can’t see what goes on behind studio doors on a daily basis or the resources they have to work with, of course, but I hazard a guess that this is a bad idea that may already be backfiring.

I hold no ill thoughts toward PoE1 and don’t think it should be in complete and total stasis during PoE2 EA, but committing to regular releases of PoE1 content feels like it might hamstring PoE2 development more than intended.

  • PoE2 had major hype and press behind it during 0.1/back in December, which seems to have really dried up. New players to the franchise are largely going to go with PoE2 and those interested in the game come to expect regular, noteworthy amounts of progress.
  • PoE2 has a lot of work that needs done. Stagnating development for 2-3 months has the potential to really push back the timeline, if it hasn’t already.
  • From a business standpoint, I recall reading there was a higher player count in PoE2 at peak than PoE1 ever enjoyed. Keeping the revenue stream and level of excitement going seems critical to the success of both games. GGG needs to be able to bring on additional resources and expand these teams to an extent with added talent. Staying in the spotlight, in turn, attracts that talent.
  • I understand that there was a sense of dejection when no updates were coming down the pipe, or plans for content in PoE1. That said… PoE1 veterans are going to literally chew through any amount of content the studio can throw at it in a matter of weeks, each league. Spending months to develop “keep-alive” content for PoE1 takes up a lot of resources when the team is split, and it only seems good for a few weeks of “recognition” and hype.

To me it seems like a strange philosophy that robs the developers of momentum. Going back and forth between two games has to make it somewhat hard to maintain concentration and oomph.

That said, one point that was brought up that makes sense, is the hope that the devs will remember and re-realize what made PoE1 popular and fun to begin with, and perhaps those good traits will trickle down to PoE2. Ideally.

Again, I don’t think PoE1 should just be ignored outright. But committing to a full 3-4month on-again, off-again rotation seems like it could create problems.

Curious to hear what others think.
