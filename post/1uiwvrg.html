On this date in 1990, Dave Stewart and Fernando Valenzuela became the first pitchers in the Modern Era to throw no-hitters on the same day

Fernando Valenzuela allowed 3 walks and struck out 7 Cardinals in a 6-0 win.

Box score: https://www.baseball-reference.com/boxes/LAN/LAN199006290.shtml

Dave Stewart also allowed 3 walks but struck out 12 batters in a 5-0 win over the Blue Jays.

Box score: https://www.baseball-reference.com/boxes/TOR/TOR199006290.shtml

It was the only career no-hitters for both pitchers.
