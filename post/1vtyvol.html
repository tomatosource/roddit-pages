The Braves have become the third franchise to reach 200,000 base hits thanks to Austin Riley’s 7th-inning RBI single today.

Baseball-Reference’s teams list lists the Braves as having 199,994 hits going into today. Riley’s single was their sixth hit today.

Both the Cubs and Cardinals reached the 200,000 mark last season.
