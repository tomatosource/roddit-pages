Increased pace is making the best teams a lot younger. This is the least experienced top 4 teams the league has ever had.

The Thunder, Spurs, Pistons, and Celtics all have an average roster experience of 4.5-5 years.

The only way to explain this is the increased pace, and just as important, the increased mileage from closing out 3 pointers on defense.

This combination of having more possessions and having to move more during them has made older players a lot less effective unless they are ex All-NBA level.

  • 2011 Mavs won with an average experience of 10 years
  • 2014 Spurs 8.7 Years
  • 2016 Cavs 8.1 Years
  • 2018 Warriors 7.1 Years
  • 2019 Raptors 6.3 Years
  • 2020-2024 all between 6-7 years
  • 2025 Thunder: 4.2 Years
  • Now this year we have the top 4 teams at 5 years.
  • During this time the pace has gone from 90 to 100 (roughly)
