Celtics front court concerns

Hello I’m a new poster that has been a Celtics fan since 2001 and I’ve stuck with the team through rebuilds and championships. Brad Stevens is usually a good GM when it comes to trades, most of his draft picks have been late but only Baylor gives rotation player vibes. I was cool with the Holiday for Simons trade because we need additional scoring while Tatum is out and I was cool to dump KP since he was constantly injured and regressed in the playoffs. I just despise the front court Queta is a good offensive rebounder but awful on the defensive glass, Tillman looked like his knee had him losing a step. Garza might be a sleeper like Kornet and we have Niang and minott at the 4. I don’t feel like that’s enough, a lot of the bigs are getting picked up we need to flip Hauser to get under the 2nd apron for a cheaper big. I think Bol Bol is still a free agent, Kai Jones had a good stint in Dallas and is available for the low. Seeing jay huff get traded for 2 second rounders there might be a few guys like Jalen Smith or Zach collins that’s available. Who do you guys want us to target as far as frontcourt players?. #nba #celtics
