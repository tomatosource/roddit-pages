You guys play a different game than me :)

Hi there,

browsing through this sub made me realize that I probably play a completely different game than most of you. Profit crafting, currency investments, corrupting and juicing maps, deleting T4 pinnacle bosses, … doesn’t concern me at all :)

This isn’t to throw shade at these activities, far from it. Rather I find it amazing that the game has so much to offer and seemingly supports such a variety of play-styles. I enjoy playing SSF, slowly gathering resources and advancing my builds into endgame. I’m happy if I reach T15 maps and can find and defeat the various pinnacle boss encounters at their base tier … for most of you, this is not the end but the beginning of the journey.

However, I’m also grateful for the “die hard” community that figures out every small detail about the inner workings of the game, providing useful info even to me as a more casual player.

So shoutout to everyone enjoying PoE 2!
