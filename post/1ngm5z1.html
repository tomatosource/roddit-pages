Warrior, narratively speaking, feels like the hero of PoE2’s story.

Out of every class available right now in the game, Warrior’s character feels the most like it was set up to be the canonical “hero” type for the game’s main story.

His entire reason for embarking on the quest that he does is because of the destruction of his home at the hands of the Count and the Madness.
He doesn’t do the things he does out of a lust for power, riches, or fame, but out of a sense for the greater good.
Warrior actively laments and shows an uncommon level of empathy (compared to the other classes) for the people he comes across and even a few of the bosses you kill.

I cannot tell you how good a job the voice actor does. He absolutely nails the the emotion through all of his dialogue every. single. time.
You can hear the rage filled sorrow when he discovers Ogham village was set ablaze and his father’s workshop ransacked, and you can hear his intense drive to continue through the campaign in search and in opposition of The Beast.

I dunno, all I can say is that this class’ background is just such a 10⁄10, and that is feels like it was written to be the defacto “good guy”. A humble man who was adopted into a smith’s family as a child, and raised like he was one of their own then stands up to defend it all when he has to. Warbringer’s Ascendancy quote really nails home the entire theme of warrior: “Your home has been threatened. A good man must be roused to war.”

\“My life has been a forge. I am ready for war.\”
