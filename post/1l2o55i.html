0 PTS, 0 REB, 0 AST, 0 STL, 0 BLK in 22 Minutes – Kyle Kuzma’s ‘Snell Game 2.0’ Jersey Hits the Auction Block

Catalogue note

This away ‘Statement Edition’ jersey was worn by Kyle Kuzma on April 19, 2025, during Game 1 of the First Round of the NBA Playoffs, when the Milwaukee Bucks played the Indiana Pacers.

Kuzma did not record any stats. The Bucks lost to the Pacers, 117-98. 

This jersey was also worn by Kuzma on April 22, 2025, during Game 2 of the First Round of the NBA Playoffs, when the Bucks played the Pacers. Kuzma finished with 12 points, 3 rebounds, and 1 assist. The Bucks lost to the Pacers, 123-115. 

Kyle Kuzma is known for his scoring ability and athleticism. At 6’9”, he can play both forward positions and has shown proficiency in scoring at multiple levels. Drafted with the 27th pick of the 1st round in the 2017 NBA Draft from the University of Utah, Kuzma quickly became a key contributor for the Lakers and played a significant role in the Lakers’ 2020 NBA Finals run.  

Kuzma has taken his game to the next level with the Wizards, averaging career-highs in points and assists in the 2023-2024 season. At the time of cataloging, he has career averages of 17.3 points per game, 6.4 rebounds per game, and 2.7 assists per game.

Kuzma is a 2017-18 All-Rookie 1st Team.
