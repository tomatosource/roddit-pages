Can we just appreciate how goated the ritual change is? Everything about the new ritual is fantastic.

Rewards are now only uniques or omens. This gives a way to players in SSF to target farm uniques. It also makes sifting through the rewards much less tiresome. Now you can just look at the reward window and instantly know if you’ve hit it big.

The change to fuel the invitation with spare tribute is also a really nice change. Makes every ritual feel worth it, even the ones where you didn’t get any rewards.

And we get an awesome looking pinacle boss on top, that shadow thing was probably the coolest boss they’ve shown
