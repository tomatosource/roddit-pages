Another loot suggestion…

Loot not feeling good right now, covered by many others in many words.

Personal opinion; T1 and T2 modifiers on loot are very weak and make gear that rolls them feel terrible.

Second opinion; the way lesser and standard runes stop rolling as you level feels good (I like that only greater runes drop in endgame): Can we get the same thing where gear stops rolling with T1-T2 mods as you progress into the upper levels?

Example; at level 70, no loot drops with T1 modifiers. At level 85, no loot drops with T2 mods. At level 90, T3 mods stop appearing. Same with adding mods via currency orbs at each of those breakpoints.

Ps: Raising the floor on gear like that would make me feel like progression through endgame is more valuable, and would reduce the amount of floor drops that are instantly worthless. Might even get people playing longer each season as levelling would be more appealing to the average player with more benefit to grinding into the later levels.
