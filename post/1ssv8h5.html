Defensive Approach in Game 2

I just heard a hilarious advanced stat that validates what many of us were saying during and after the game last night regarding the Celtics defensive approach. The Celtics ran drop coverage on D 69.7% of the time last night. That was the third highest In a playoff game going back to when it was tracked on 2013.

So yes, the approach was drastic and there were zero adjustments. Something tells me that’s going to change going forward, but I found that statistic a bit funny and maybe slightly alarming.
