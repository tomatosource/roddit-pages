Chet on playing Steven Adams for the first time: “He’s not really trying to clash with you. Just kinda moves you super gently. Like there’s nothing you can do about it, really. It’s a different type of strong. So … yeah, he strong.”

Last night was Chet’s first time playing against Steven Adams (since Adams missed all of last season with an injury). His full quote:

“Obviously he’s really strong. But it was different than what I expected. He’s not really trying to clash with you. Just kinda moves you super gently. Like there’s nothing you can do about it, really. It’s a different type of strong I guess, and he’s also got like all the tricks. So … yeah, he strong.”
