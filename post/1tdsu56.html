A year ago I asked GGG to “stick to the road” instead of shrinking zones… and now it’s happening

About a year ago, when a lot of players were asking GGG to shrink campaign zones, I made a forum post arguing for a different approach:

Instead of making areas smaller, guide players naturally through the world with environmental cues.

Roads. Rivers. Trails. Signposts. Blood stains. Monster tracks. Landmarks. NPC hints.

Basically: let speedrunners find objectives quickly if they know what to look for, while still preserving the feeling of exploration for players who actually enjoy wandering through the campaign.

Watching the new 0.5 videos felt surreal.

Trails of locusts leading to Freythorn.
Blood trails leading to bosses.
Rivers guiding players upstream.
Explorer NPCs revealing directions.
Signposts in the Hunting Grounds.

This is exactly the kind of “soft navigation” solution I hoped for.

And honestly, I think it’s the perfect compromise:
- explorers keep large immersive zones
- experienced players get smoother replays
- the world still feels like a world instead of a corridor

I don’t care whether GGG ever saw my post or not. I’m just genuinely happy they’re solving the problem this way instead of simply shrinking everything.

Really excited for 0.5.
