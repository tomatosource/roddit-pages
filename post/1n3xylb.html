PoE2 is absolutely stunning

I’ve been with PoE since closed beta, and I finally decided to jump into Path of Exile 2 after skipping the first release. I honestly can’t believe how good it feels. The biomes, skill animations, voice acting, bosses — everything is at such an incredible level, and I know it’s only going to get better with time.

I went in completely blind, just exploring and trying new things, and it feels so fresh. There will always be things people dislike, but I’ve come to appreciate every little detail. I’ve realised that efficiency doesn’t always equal fun. I love the slower pace, the sense of struggle, and the thrill of finding that rare gear upgrade. Slamming every currency orb I find and inching forward through the story has been its own kind of reward.

A huge thank you to GGG. Thirteen years ago, PoE changed my life, and while I no longer have the time to grind like I used to, PoE2 somehow brings back my old self — and the joy I’m feeling is immeasurable.
