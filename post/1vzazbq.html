Jeanie Buss petitions court to block siblings from selling Lakers stake

Jeanie Buss has petitioned a Los Angeles Superior Court asking it to block her five siblings from selling the family’s remaining stake in the Los Angeles Lakers, while accusing them of secretly orchestrating a deal to try to remove her as the team’s governor.

“Jeanie never agreed to any sale, was never consulted, and was never even informed,” her petition states.
