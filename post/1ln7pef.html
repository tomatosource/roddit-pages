Headhunter PSA for new players

Recently I finally acquired a headhunter for my build. I know I’m very late to the party but as a casual and new player I was very excited to try this item out. Immediately I noticed while mapping my character was jumping about and for a few days I thought it was related to ping and the recent poe1 league. Turns out one of the monster mods you steal from the headhunter will just teleport you randomly at whatever monster is on your screen.

Not only is this jarring and jumpy and I imagine terrible for performance for players playing on struggling hardware but also terrible in general as it makes your evasion less valuable and eats wind dancer/ghost shroud/tailwind stacks. It goes against everything poe2 stands for in terms of slower paced combo oriented gameplay and just encourages more Insta kill screen wiping builds.

From my research even in poe1 this headhunter interaction is bothersome and just makes sense for it to be removed in the spirit of the type of gameplay poe2 is reaching to achieve

Tldr; if you experience random jitters with the headhunter unique equipped it’s not your internet or pc

Also to GGG it would be nice if they could display what affixes we’re actually stealing from monsters with headhunter as I literally thought there was a problem with my Pc when I suddenly was teleporting around the screen. There is no way of telling ingame what’s causing this and it would be nice if we could hover over the HH buff and see what we’re actually gaining. Also please do something about the enemy visuals and the headhunter visuals looking identical and impossible distinguish what’s enemy and ally ground effects
