Build Damage vs Boss Health/Design vs Game Design

Was perusing in the builds subreddit and it’s kinda funny that in many threads people say “boss died too slowly”, “terrible boss damage” etc, when the boss lives for about ~5 seconds.

It just feels odd that in theory an optimal build with good boss damage is one where you don’t have to engage with the boss and it’s mechanics at all; the boss is basically a target dummy that you melt in 0.5 seconds and move on to the next one; every boss ends up being the same.

I guess this just musing on the state of the game than anything. Don’t get me wrong, melting a boss instantly can be fun, but you get to a certain point where well, that’s it? In a way you aren’t really “playing the game” anymore once your build hits a certain level.

Boss design almost seems diametrically opposed to mapping too. If you have really low mapping damage it drastically increases the risk of you dying due to getting swarmed etc. It almost seems like bosses still fit the originally intended “slower” pace of PoE2 where mapping is getting closer and closer to PoE1 style.

Edit: Let me be clear, I’ve been very much enjoying the league and am not trashing anyone who’s preferred playstyle is to go fast. I just think it’s a shame to go into a fight like Arbiter of Divinity or other pinnacles and see all these cool animations and music and voicelines and clearly there were designed mechanics there to play around and figure out; they clearly put a lot of work into the fight, and he dies in 0.5 seconds. Part of it is what people mention below, the design of the game being supper rippy and defenses being pretty hard to balance properly, etc.
