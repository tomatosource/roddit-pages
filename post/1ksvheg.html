The Pacers did not have a first round pick in the 2020 NBA Draft.

That year, the Kings took Haliburton 12th overall, and the Celtics took Nesmith 14th overall.

Just a reminder how fortunes can change in this league from trades.

Celtics traded Nesmith to the Pacers in a deal for Malcolm Brogdon, who they eventually used in a package to land Jrue Holiday, and we know how that turned out for them.

Kings did Kings things and now the Pacers are set up to compete for years. But I wanna give the Pacers credit here, its truly insane to get two of the best players from one draft class without a first round pick from that class.
