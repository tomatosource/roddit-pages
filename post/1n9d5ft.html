A brief summary of the PTFO Cuban interview.

Cuban’s points are as follows:

  1. There is no direct evidence we currently know of that Balmer was aware of or directed the payments to Kawhi.

  2. The indirect evidence that he knew (Pablo’s interviews with 7 Aspiration employees who all said it was common knowledge the payments were given and prioritized for Balmer) is hearsay and could be the employees repeating lies they were told by their scammer CEO.

  3. Balmer was just an investor who got scammed, which can happen to anyone.

  4. Aspiration may have independently made the deal with Kawhi to give him 28 mil (or 48 based on other reporting) without the clippers knowing because keeping Kawhi a clipper raises the value of their 300 mil sponsorship deal with the intuit dome.

  5. Balmer is too smart to have done something thjs stupid.

My take on this:

For 1 and 3? Sure. Will see if the investigation turns up the true smoking gun there.

For #2? This makes no sense. Why would the employees all be told the deal was for Balmer if it wasn’t? What possible motivation could the scammers have to put out there something that if known could sink one of their primary partners?

For #4? Completely ridiculous. Sure it’s better to be the sponsor of a good team. But to pay twice as much per year for the sponsorship (their sponsorship deal was $300 mil but spread out over many years) for a no-show endorsement with Kawhi would be complete insanity. It fails any semblance of a fair market value test.

For #5? This is not a believable defense. Smart people do dumb things all the time, especially when outside their primary field.
