Are there too many breaks in games?

Hey all,

With many people weighing in recently on NBA ratings and all that I’ve been thinking about what I like/dislike about watching NBA games and there’s an elephant in the room that I haven’t seen many people talk about. Of course, it has been mentioned, but many conversations seem to centre around 3PA, lack of regular season urgency and load management instead of the actual broadcast.

Now, I am European and tend to watch football, where games have a (more or less) uninterrupted flow, but I absolutely can’t stand how many stoppages there are in the NBA. Between games regularly starting 15-20mins late and all of the timeouts, free throws, quarterly breaks etc. a 48min game will finish hours and hours after it starts. It just feels like your time as a consumer is being wasted when the game stops literally once every minute or less and an ad starts playing, to the point that I’d prefer to just check the gamecast every 15 mins or so and do something else rather than actually watch the game. There are numerous examples that showcase the issue I have, such as the Nets/Bucks game in which the final 20s took 17mins to finish. This is AWFUL to watch and makes me far more likely to consume games via highlights and a box score rather than sit through the full game.

Again, this may just be a cultural thing, but if I’m going to sit and watch a game, I want to actually see the sport being played relatively uninterrupted, not ads. Do you think there’s a way for the NBA to reduce these breaks, reduce the number of timeouts, reduce the number of fouls or the length of time it takes for free throws to be set-up so that broadcasters will force fewer ads down our throats during the game? Maybe it’s a silly suggestion given how much money is at play, but I think it would go a long way to maintaining viewers’ attention and respecting their time, thus getting more eyes onto the actual game.
