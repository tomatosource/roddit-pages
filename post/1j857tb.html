  1. 2016 Golden State Warriors - 1788

  4. 2015 Golden State Warriors - 1772

  5. 1986 Boston Celtics - 1770

  6. 2017 Golden State Warriors - 1770

  7. 1992 Chicago Bulls - 1769

  8. 2016 San Antonio Spurs - 1767

The teams they would be kicking out of that Top 10 are the 2013 Miami Heat and then a tie between the 1997 Utah Jazz, who lost to the Bulls who are on this list, and the 2024 Boston Celtics.

Teams in bold won the title.

Interestingly enough, the only other season to feature 2 teams in the Top 10 was the very infamous 2015-16 season where neither of the teams listed won the championship.
