A question on J Walsh

First off, I really appreciate what he brought this season and how he stepped up when it mattered. There’s something genuinely exciting about watching a rookie develop, especially one who plays with that kind of relentless hustle. That said, I can’t shake the feeling that he’s lacking confidence, and his decision making is a bit concerning.

too often it feels like he picks the wrong play or forces a bad shot, and that becomes hard to ignore in tighter moments. I’m not sure a scrappy defender alone gives us enough down the stretch unless he’s already disrupted the game early. I’d like to see him stick around, but when you compare him to Baylor, Hugo, or even some of the bench guys, they just seem more composed and make smarter reads. Curious how others see it.
