General Information

  • Two ascendancy classes for the druid: The Shaman and The Oracle
  • Over 30 new support Gems
  • Substantial balance changes on Unique Items, Lineage Support gems, and more
  • New pinnicle boss of Atziri, the Queen of the Vaal
  • New league mechanic to allow double corruptions and other crafting options
  • Abyss mechanic, introduced in 0.3.0, is going core
  • Delirium Fog is less visually annoying and is toned down
  • And much more!
