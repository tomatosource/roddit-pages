LeBron finished in the top 15 for xRAPM and EPM for 20 straight seasons.

LeBron had an insane run as measured by these metrics starting with his sophomore season in 2004-05 and ending in his age 39 season in 2023-24.

His xRAPM in 2005 was 4.6 good for 11th in the league and in 2024 it was 5.1 good for 8th in the league.

His EPM in 05 was +4.3 again 11th and in 2024 it was +4.5 10th in the NBA.

In none of the intervening seasons did he drop out of the top 15.

Interestingly, he has seen a sharp decline in both metrics the last two years.

His xRAPM fell to .7 (82nd percentile) outside the top 100 in 25 and all the way to .5 (73rd percentile) in 26.

His EPM fell to +2.1 (56th in NBA) in 25 and to +1.5 (87th in NBA) in 2026.
