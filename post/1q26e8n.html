I am having a blast this league because I started actually playing the league mechanic

I used to be a hater. I thought this league was a total let down. I was angry about the exploiters ruining the economy. But now…. after actually trying the mechanic earnestly. I love it. I have never had more fun. I might not be swimming in mirrors… but I can buy literally anything on the market outside of the giga chase items like temporalis (which lets be real, even in normal leagues no one is getting that without considerable time investment). And the biggest perk, crafting mats are dirt cheap. I can buy chaos orbs and annuls and anything super cheap which has made crafting more accessible to me and more enjoyable. I no longer cry over bricked items and lost crafting mats. AND leveling up in the temples is insane. I never had a character reach 100 before, even with 100s of hours played. And now with like 80 hours played I have over a thousand div and i’m about to hit level 100. It’s insanely fun.

This is my current temple. It has taken me 30-35 hours to get this (not using the exploit), which I believe is a very reasonable amount for a seasonal game. (I don’t want the season to be over in 2 days like D4)

Temple

These are my rewards for said temple after running all 60 charges. 107 divines for like 2 hours of farming start to finish.

Loot

It’s pretty easy and it’s fun to learn how to build the temple.

Some quick tips:

  • Headhunter is dirt Cheap. you can run a few juiced abyss maps or rituals or breaches and get enough divs in one day to buy it. And it helps with farming and raising your power, especially in the temple, tremendously.

HH

  • When you build the temple, look at a planner, like sulozor’s on github. Read up on how the temple works (fubgun made a short video on it that is super helpful).
  • When you first start off… just go to the architect and get your 60 charges and 6 medallions. Then…never touch him again. he wrecks rooms and snakes.
  • Always build your middle rooms to help with destabilization. The way I have it in my pic is the way you should build them as “unprotected” one off rooms. This way if you don’t have a destabilization token, it will limit the amount of damage done to your snake.
  • Don’t get cocky like me, focus on one snake at a time even if you feel like you are overflowing with lock medallions.
  • Prioritize these medallions
  1. Destabilization lock - Always prio these. you need like 10+ spymasters to sustain

Lock Room

  1. Reroll medallion. This helps with rerolling before you place rooms down to get 6 whole new rooms if your rng is crap.

RR

  1. Upgrade tokens to upgrade rooms. You can only upgrade a room one time with these and the second upgrade has to come form the room being connected to a room that upgrades it.

Upgrade Room

Tips on farming maps: It takes me like 20-30 minutes to get 60 charges.

  • Use the grand project tablets in towers. They go for like 60EX right now and once you get a good chain going you can easily sustain them yourself.
  • Go into a map that was unlocked via the tower and find the alter, then leave. It’s counter intuitive but its fast. You will sustain maps from the temple. I get like 100 T15 maps from the temple every 60 charges and you only need 30 of them per 60 charges.
  • if you can, hop between towers. If you reveal a tower with your current tower, just keep opening up maps. Tower maps are the fastest to fully complete.
  • Get some light radius gear. I use two rings that give like 20-30% light radius each. Head hunter makes up for the lost stats.
  • Always search around the start of the map and around the boss. 90% of the time the Vaal sacrifice circle is around the start.

All in all. I am having a blast. I have already spent like 800 div on several characters builds I wanted to try. I’ve been buying up crafting mats and crafting good bases and selling them for 100s of additional div.. it’s so fun. Most rewarding league I have experienced once I started doing the league mechanic.
