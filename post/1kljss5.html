Don’t write Tatum’s epitaph yet

I’m seeing way too many posts declaring Tatum’s career all but over. Let’s try to be optimist here (which I realize is a difficult concept for most Celtics fans and with some reason). A lot of the guys who didn’t come back from an Achilles injury were already near the end of their careers. Others came at a time in which medicine wasn’t what it is today. Tatum is still young and it’s 2025. KD, I believe, was a few years older and he bounced back. You might point to Cousins as the antithesis of this, but he is much bigger than Tatum, therefore more stress on the lower body. Let’s see how the man bounces back folks.
