Joel Embiid returns from injury with a strong showing in Chicago: 31 pts, 12 reb, 4 ast, 2 blk on 13-28 FG

Embiid began slow, starting 0-7 from the field in the first quarter. However, he picked up his game from then on, shooting 13-21 after the first quarter with 31 points, 12 rebounds, 4 assists, 2 blocks. All in all, a strong showing after not playing since November 20th due to knee swelling. All that’s left to be seen is how his knee responds, but he’ll have time to heal up by the Sixers’ next game on Friday. Source
