Incursion feels like a true spiritual successor to Last of the Druids, and I’d just like to commend GGG for fixing the Temple.

In Last of the Druids/Fate of the Vaal, building a temple was arcane and punishing. You were forced to run the Temple immediately after 6 maps or lose your progress, which was incredibly tedious. It felt like a prison: once you were in, you couldn’t leave. You literally couldn’t port out! Forgot the item you wanted to craft on that rare Exalt bench? Too bad. If you tried to portal out to your stash by leaving through the entrance, the entire instance would just collapse and vanish without warning. It was never clear why we couldn’t just access our stash or why leaving through the front door destroyed the entire building.

Now, in Incursion, GGG has finally listened. They introduced this revolutionary item called the “Chronicle of Atzoatl.” You can actually store the temple and run it whenever you want! No more being forced to interrupt your mapping flow. Even better: inside the temple, you can use portals! You can go to your hideout, trade, grab a crafting base, and come back. It’s so intuitive and respectful of the player’s time compared to the “do or die” prison system of Druids.

In Druids, we spent half our time backtracking through empty “Generator Rooms” or dealing with glitching doors that wouldn’t open even after clearing the connection rooms. And let’s not forget the “Road Tiles” – hoping you got a crossroad instead of a dead end was pure RNG frustration. In Incursion, they streamlined this perfectly. You kill a specific architect to swap the room, or you kill the other to upgrade it. Simple. You pick up a Stone of Passage and open the door you want. No guessing games, no glitching hitboxes. It’s simple and intuitive. Furthermore, the loot situation is fixed. We all remember clearing 20 rooms in Druids just to get… nothing. Mobs dropped no loot, and half the chests were empty. It felt unrewarding to kill anything, and regal orb benchcrafts that you couldn’t take with you were just frustrating. In Incursion, the monsters actually drop items! And when you finish a room in a map, in many rooms you get a literal “lootsplosion” at your feet. It feels so much better than running a 30-minute dungeon for a single Regal Orb craft. And Atziri? Did anyone ever see her during the druids league? Well in Incursion, you can do the boss basically every single time unless you mess up building your temple before.

I know I’m bashing Last of the Druids a lot here. It had great atmosphere and the visual design was cool. But Incursion really feels like “Druids 2.0”. Being able to see exactly what a room does, change the temple layout deliberately, and actually get loot for killing monsters? It’s just good game design. I’m really looking forward to farming Alva for the next few months!
