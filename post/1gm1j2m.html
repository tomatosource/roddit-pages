Two weeks into the season, the Lakers have the 28th ranked defense in the NBA.

That is with AD having been a monster when active. Even in their 4-1 start, Lakers were middling defensively. They’re not shooting as well from the outside, turning it over a bit more, and that’s stressing their defense, and they haven’t been able to stem the tide. Obviously last night was a real low without AD.

Digging into the numbers, the real culprit is their bench. Christie is a trainwreck; Knecht obviously as a rookie with questionable lateral mobility and lower body strength has his question marks. Jaxson Hayes good for some energy plays but not great in terms of displaying awareness and focus within a team defensive scheme and gets pushed around. Vincent is giving the Lakers essentially nothing, and looks to be a somewhat costly mistake as a free agent signing the offseason before last.

LeBron’s defensive numbers are poor but I think that’s more a reflection of who he plays around vs his individual performance, but we know he isn’t a 48 minute defender anymore.

You think the Lakers can turn this defense around into one that’s at least passable?
