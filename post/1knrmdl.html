A reminder: The Denver Nuggets - who just forced a Game 7 against a 68-win team - fired their head coach mere days before the playoffs began.

It’s truly wild how little this has been talked about given the unprecedented season Denver has been having this postseason. Not only was Mike Malone given a pink slip but the GM he so often clashed with was fired alongside him for what felt like the first steps of a significant shapeup in the offseason.

Now? The Nuggets are one game away from their 3rd Conference Finals appearance in the past 5 seasons (Bubble year & 2023 when they won were the two prior appearances). How much one wants to credit interim coach David Adelman for his coaching decisions or Jokic for being Jokic for this postseason is up to you but the 180 on defense alone is quite the paradigm shift - and this isn’t even to account for the fact that Michael Porter Jr. isn’t even fully healthy at the moment.

If the OKC Thunder - who are perceived to have one of the better coaches in the league via Mark Daigneault - lose at home then it will be a historic upset in the best and worst ways possible.

Denver will see OKC for a “win or go home” Game 7. The MVP for the season has yet to be announced.
