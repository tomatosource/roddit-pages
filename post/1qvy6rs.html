BHM: Ken Griffey Jr. switched from No. 24 to No. 42 on the inaugural Jackie Robinson Day, April 15, 1997.

Ten years later, he asked for permission to wear the number again.

That led to a Jackie Robinson Day tradition since 2009 – every Major Leaguer wears No. 42 on that day.
