SSF Feedback

Lvl 97 with 2 magebloods to my name (Character), game feels amazing right now, and I’m ready to keep voting with my wallet.

That said, everything can be improved. Here are the remaining pain points for me:

1) Getting the first mod on an item is tedious process.

Usually requires Filter editing/ID-ing/Transmut/Aug dozens or hundreds of bases. Having a way to isolate a given mod from high-tier drops would be more enjoyable endgame gameplay.

2) Mandatory mods are no fun

Main offender is +gem, but spirit and MS on boots are also up there. Those mods are just far too strong compared to the rest of the mod pool.

3) Reforging bench is bad design

Requires tedious hoarding of hundreds of bad items to see 40% more rolls. Save your players from optimizing the fun out of the game and just get rid of it.

4) QoL for tablet swapping while mapping.

Maybe allow 2 different sets or something.

5) No campaign shortcuts for 2nd characters

Already discussed at lenght here. Being able to skip secondary questlines and boss-rushing acts bosses would already be a win.
