Market analysis tool for currency pairs across seasons, unique prices and more!

This is a repost from a few days earlier. To comply with the subs rules I made the account creation optional.

Hi all,

I wanted to share a tool I built to monitor currency pairs (like DIV:EX) using GGG’s currency exchange API.

The tool is using lightweight-charts from Trading-View, a popular open source framework for financial charting.

  1. You can follow currency pair movements
  2. cross compare different pairs relative or absolute performance over time
  3. or compare a currency pair movement between seasons as the days progress
  4. you can also follow unique prices tracing poe.ninjas published prices
  5. and see volume of a certain currency traded per hour across all its markets

Like many others I have a second monitor open with trade searches when I play or just for browsing on the side, and I always wanted to be able to chart and follow currency pair movements for certain markets without having to check the in-game vendor or go to poe.ninja.

There is also a suite of widgets built in to record, time and analyze speedruns you upload. These were actually the first things I built this site for, and later expanded with more data coverage.
