What players should the Celtics target this offseason?

Hey everyone. I’m pretty new to this community but I have been a Celtics fan since i was really little. I just wanted to get you guys’ opinions on what should happen this offseason after a brutal first round exit to the 76ers. I would love to hear all of you guys’ opinions.

In my personal opinion, the Celtics should look at players who fit next to Brown and Tatum. They need a secondary playmaker next to Tatum. They should go after players like Andrew Nembhard, Ayo Dosunmu, Quentin Grimes, Nickeil Alexander-Walker, Davion Mitchell, T.J. McConell, and Dyson Daniels. They should also go after centers who can strengthen the defense with rim protection and rebounding that can still function next to Tatum and Brown. Some realistic targets that fit this are Isaiah Hartenstein, Walker Kessler, Daniel Gafford, or Naz Reid.
