The Ferryman should be moved to one of the earlier acts as a way to deterministically get runes earlier on without trivialising progression of the campaign

For anyone who doesnt know, in the interlude acts you will encounter The Ferryman who sells near a dozen or so higher tier runes for gold - its one of the nicest new encounters added in this patch, but i cant seem to think that the game would massively improve with his addition earlier on

When you encounter him youve likely spent hours in Act 4 and are close to the endgame, youve likely solved a lot of the problems youve encountered and spent a lot of the gold you had doing so and youve already gone through 90% of the obstacles in the campaign - aka you encounter him in a position where you need him the least throughout the entire campaign, and not only that runes sell for a good amount of Exalts to the point where most people would benefit more from just selling his items on the currency exchange

If he was moved to act 2⁄3 it would give a lot more urgency to the players and allow them to partially fix the glaring holes in their builds when they need to - the gold cost will make it so it doesnt trivialise the campaign since most people would need to decide between spending it on him or another vendor + its a limited stock so there would still be weight to the players decisions on buying/using the runes

Imagine fighting Doryani and needing a little more lightning resistance only to remember “oh yeah that guy selling runes was selling a storm rune, ive got a few thousand gold lets try that.” Id imagine it would be a massively well received change without ruining the flow of or complicating the campaign

For a system thats been said to partially exist as a way to patch your build during the campaign Rune drops are too RNG to rely on. We need a better way to get them and i believe the ferryman is it
