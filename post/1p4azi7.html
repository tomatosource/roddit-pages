Random Internet Man’s Predictions and Ramblings for .4 to kill time before the launch.

Hello Exiles,

During my commutes since .3 launch I keep thinking of random things I’d like to see in POE. So here’s a extremely full time job having man’s predictions/thoughts/hopes for .4 and beyond. Purely because I want to see how much hopium I’m on and maybe look cool if I get things right.

Some of this info comes from datamined stuff so potential spoilers?

# 0.4 Dawn of the rise of the wolfboy

● Druid

•So, Talismans seem to be the most likely “weapon” to be paired with the druid. I haven’t quite nailed down what to expect. But, I think the Hollow Palm notable being added seemingly out of nowhere may mean that the attacks meant to be paired up with shapeshifting will be considered unarmed and some of the druid passive tree sections(particularly the obvious claw shaped one) will be changed to provide unarmed benefits.

This means talismans could do a few different/unique things.

  1. They provide shapeshift skills. I feel this is unlikely and a bit too restrictive.
  2. More likely to me they will influence shapeshifting skills used. I’m actually close to 50⁄50 on this but I guess smarter people will have a better idea. Either application applies to my below shapeshifting thoughts.

•Shapeshifting forms we all but know are confirmed are, Bear & Wolf, pretty bold to assume those aren’t a given. Datamining heavily suggests a Wyvern form(fucking sick)and there was the recent post that showed the reference to the Weretree(hilarious)

My big stretch prediction is that each form will have options, given or influenced by talismans.

🞗Bear: 1. Physical: Slams

  1. Fire, it just feels right to me, and the idea is it would play like slams but use volcano/fissure skills

🞗Wolf: 1. Physical: Seems straightforward enough, likely bleed heavy is my guess

  1. Lightning: Feels kinda “wolfy” (think enhancement shaman from wow I guess) and would fit the stormy side of the druid theme.

  2. Cold, at first I didn’t think of it, but we all know a fairly cool ice themed wolf in POE so it could be a neat twist.

🞗Wyvern : Not sure really, I would sort of just bet we’ll see it either be a caster form or be shockingly melee focused. But either way, easy Cold and Lightning focused.

● Updates

•Crafting

🞗I’m predicting(hoping let’s be real) a huge buff to crafting in terms of available approaches and modifiers but it’s going to come at a cost of the high end stuff being nerfed down situationally as needed to compensate(looking at you +gem skill levels) I do actually want them to leave +gem levels on caster staves quite high to offer a trade off of pure damage to defenses(imagine that)

🞗Abyss was a pretty huge success but between that and essences we have some layers of extremely deterministic crafting on the POE scale. So, I think more modifier options through influence is inevitable to bring some chaos to that process. If the new league provides crafting, I’m expecting super high risk/reward.

•Ascendancies

🞗I feel it’s a fair bet we’ll get at least one or two. I know martial artist was sort of leaked a bit so that’s kinda likely and would line up with the unarmed stuff from above.

🞗Bigger hope here, some of that lost power can be moved not to just the skill tree but ascendancies. Meaningful choices here would be great. The easy way is just adding power to the nodes but a cooler way would be to add maybe 2 extra sets of nodes per ascendency and give us another set of 2 points. Would differentiate from POE1 a bit and I don’t think it would be crazy complicated, the Forge Master has a million nodes already so they could be open to the idea. This would of course, be quite a large rebalance.

● Endgame
^(These predictions are… unlikely)

🞗 Delve & Blight(wild thoughts warning)

Delve, I think this is coming, maybe now but not how it is in POE1 at all (Sorry Jorgen) My idea for it, it will be directly implemented into the endgame infinite atlas system as an alternate travel method. You would spend currency to begin a delve and it would create an entrance to the underground half of the map, which will be swapped between by using that silly little button during the campaign. Extra wild thoughts, mechanics from above such as abyss and expedition that involve underground stuff(possibly harvest in the future) would influence what’s happening in delve.

Blight, bigger stretch here by far. The loose idea is blight will be a mechanic that is applied to the towers(blight towers duh) on the atlas and do some sort of spreading in a line of connected maps type deal with increasing difficulty and rewards.

🞗Atlas reset, pretty common request I think it would be likely the implement this to be able to limit the atlas getting overwhelmingly large over time. I thought of two interesting ideas

  1. Most likely to me would be a storyline approach of causing another or a mini cataclysm that would reset the landscape. Possibly something along the lines of, kill uber Arbiter of Ash and Frost(above thought) and the Arbiter of Lightning would do some sort of wipe and the Atlas would reset with more lightning modifiers, you get the gist.
  2. Bit wilder, they swap out the map device and you travel the atlas via caravan. Then (inhales copium) the implement boat league and we travel to a new continent/atlas via boat.

🞗If they cook and really stretch out the endgame in .4 I think it could be nice to see some influence style crafting mods similar to POE 1. I figured the easy & obvious route is Arbiter of Ash having a cold & lightning counterpart(possibly some druid lore thing ties into it?)

🞗

● The end and final thoughts.

Yeah I love POE 2 and trust that GGG will make good decisions but I really want them to take some more risks. Let them go nuts and accept nerfs when they go too crazy.

Didn’t really fit into anything but a big wish I have for POE2 is for the game to be able to breathe a bit more. Things that I’ve ran into from trying to make builds along the way that I’d like to see.

🞗Generic skill gems that work for multiple weapons. Think Cleave for axes/swords and Multishot for bows/crossbows

🞗Either remove or indicate weird restrictions/conversions that are build destroying. Got pumped up theory crafting two ideas for a build using Voll’s Protector and Redflare Conduit with rain of arrows and firestorm respectively to generate tons of power charges to boost a different skill. Would love to have seen a line of text saying only one power charge per cast/attack not per hit as stated(if this is a bug please fix)

🞗Adjust the mana formula for melee attacks. Another personal complaint, I really reallllly tried to make Mind of the Council work for a hollow palm build that also used Eldritch battery. I realize that’s an absolute ton of increased mana cost but since it seems Eldritch applies last, it made it impossible even with as much investment as I could manage into mana efficiency and instant ele leech it was just not possible and overall not even a big damage increase. It’s obviously another very niche issue but I’d love to see some… love for weird builds to become more competitive.

Anyway, if you read this(I’m sorry) but you’ve now been inside my mind during my groggy morning commutes. See you all in .4
