I finally got POE2 to click for me

I never really got into POE, and the first time i tried POE2 in EA i only made it to mid Act 2 before i just kinda lost interest.

I tried again this weekend and initially had the same result. I wanted to do a lightning sorc but the skills and setup just weren’t really landing with me. I didn’t really like using spark, something about arc felt awkward even though i love chain lightning style spells, and i just felt like i had to do too much setup with orbs and buffs and prefight stuff etc. I got to Act 2 again and just kinda lost interest.

So then i decided to try a ranger build because i love bow characters if i’m not playing a caster. The problem was that i wanted to do a more standard “physical” build and not use all the elemental stuff.

Honestly, i didn’t think it was gonna work at all, i decided to go with two versions of just the basic shot from the bow. I used pin and rapid supports for my single target attack, then used multishot and pierce for my aoe version. The nice thing about this was i didn’t use mana at all. It basically plays like a multishot demon hunter from Diablo.

I’m not a speed clearer or anything, i like to explore everywhere and the build worked well enough and was simple enough that i kept going with it. I skipped snipe since i didn’t want to try and time the skill and just picked up barrage instead.

I’m basically going full evade + move speed while attacking which lets me just kite, picking up attack speed etc bonuses. It’s not super fast, but i don’t struggle on bosses so its not outright terrible, and campaign is going fine with very rare deaths because everything is new to me.

I keep expecting it to fail at anytime, but for now im enjoying it and this is the first time i’ve really managed to get hooked on POE even though i don’t really know what im doing.
