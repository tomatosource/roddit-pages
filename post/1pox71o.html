Ways to make the campaign fun without shortening it

I actually agree with the devs; if the campaign was fun to play nobody would be talking about how long it takes. The problem is that it’s not the fun part of the game, thats the “endgame.”

Currency-I dropped 8 exalts throughout the campaign this league. By the time you get to the endgame you can make this much on a single map; so players are naturally going to want to rush to the endgame where currency is “flowing.” So drastically increase the currency that drops throughout the campaign. If that makes the game too easy then scale the bosses to match the power increase, but having more currency=more enjoyment for most people. It also allows more experimentation because you can actually make different items to try things out.

Rarity-just why. Again with only 8 exalts, you have the choice of damage/defensives on your gear, or gear with rarity, for the most part you can’t have both in the campaign. Which leads to the annoying chicken and the egg problem of getting rarity on gear with no currency. Just get rid of it please, at least in the campaign.

Skill gems-unlock all the skills at lvl 1, obviously the damage would scale down. If I want to make a build thats all about trampling people under a bear, why do I have to wait until act 4 to do that. That just makes players want to rush through acts 1-3.

Runes-make them drop blank or be buyable please. I have a stash full of mind, vision, and stone runes, thanks. I would really like to get the runes that I need, instead of waiting 2 acts for another iron rune for my weapon.

Random engame content- can we get endgame content sprinkled into the campaign. I liked the abyss extras that they added. But it would me more fun if they appeared often and things like breach and expedition appeared as well.

For the most part I don’t even slow down to enjoy acts 1-3. Like most people I just rush to act 4 because the currency exchange alleviates some of these issues.
