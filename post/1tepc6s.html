We are all hyped?! So lets get prepared and share your thoughts for early progression!

As the title says i wanna collect some “simple things” we all can consider early on or even prepare right now to make a better start or to simplify the progression a little bit.

Here are some things everyone can do:

Guaranteed area rewards:
This did not change from 0.3 to 0.4 and will stay with 0.5. Consider doing the league mechanic in your desired areas for guaranteed rewards to boost up your progress.
https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/campaign-league-mechanic-rewards-cheat-sheet
For example: There is a 100% lesser jewellers orb in the Buried Shrines.

Invest some time in your loot filter configuration:
You absolutely can declutter your screen a LOT by using NeverSinks newly introduced customization options for your build. Simply click “Auto Adjust” at the front page. Go to “Campaign” and limit your filter to: your class, your weapon type, your off-hand and the item bases you wanna see through the campaign (ES, hybrid, armour…).
https://www.filterblade.xyz/?game=Poe2

Remember shapeshifting from the beginning:
Either wolf, bear or wyvern are an insanely huge addition to your early progress. Gap closing skills like “pounce” or running in wolf form do have a lot of value. Try to buy a white talisman in the first city (prefered “Changeling Talisman”, cause it doesnt have a STR requirement).

New and old layouts:
As far as we know most zones will change in their appearance. But to close the gap in time until league start consider a testrun especially for early leveling. Zone with guaranteed rewards will not change. Learn your early progression in your support gems. Try to remember yourself in checking vendors after a level up when revisiting the city. Try to make your own useful regex and keep it open in a word pad to have a quick copy and paste for your vendors.
PoE2 Regex Builder: https://poe2.re/

Please share your knowledge to make the time fly by faster! 😄
