Making currency

Good morning everyone I wanted to share how I make currency. I’m sure it’s nothing new however I’m a casual player and I’m probably making close 3-4 divines per hour.

I’m finding that waystones are a great way to make consistent money. The strategy is to use tier 15 maps. Add all the mods you can until you only have 1 revive left. The goal is to have an open prefix. If you end up with 3 suffix and 2 prefix desecrate with vertebrae. You can take it to the well. The reveal you are looking for is adds # if pits and area is overrun. The more pits it adds the more money you can sell it for. Check what current prices are prior to list. Range is 14-18 pits I believe.

Other modifiers I like always leads to the abyss instance and abyss always gives rewards.

If you end up with 3 prefix slots OR don’t get the desecrate you want exalt slam and Vaal the map in the hopes to get a t16. These also sell pretty decently for like 35 ex.

All the maps that are left over t 14 or t 15 I typically run. I don’t juice maps really. If I find a tower I usually will throw in 3 boss tablets with some quantity. I find boss maps are great because they very consistently drop multiple t15 waystones.

Within the maps I always add Vaal orbs to essences unless it says essence of battle. Same goes with researchers strongboxes. I will get it to max modifiers and Vaal or before opening. I quite often get a raw divine just doing these. Lastly I always run the abyss instance. I have probably been lucky but I haven gotten a few omen of lights which sell for like 2 divine right now.
