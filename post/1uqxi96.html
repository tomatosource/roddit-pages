Mazzulla and Mitch: a free throw redemption arc

Last year, Joe used every chance he could to send Mitchell Robinson to the free throw line. Now, he’s his coach.

So as we transition from Hack-a-Mitch to Backing-a-Mitch-up, I’d like to speculate. Say we wake up 6 months from now, and the headline reads “Joe Mazzulla teaches Mitchell Robinson to shoot 80% from the line”. What was his secret to achieve this?

Knowing Joe, I doubt he will just hire a shooting coach and call it a day. I can almost see it now… Mitchell in his bed at 3am, snoring away in a blissful slumber, and rudely awakened by a megaphone. And who is that shouting into it, dangling from the fire escape? None other than his coach, asking why he isn’t in the gym.

What are the funniest/ most unconventional Mazzulla methods you can think of for fixing Mitch’s free throw percentage?

A few others I thought of:
- Complete darkness. Can’t leave until he makes 10 in a row.
- Shoot on an outdoor court in the middle of a nor’easter.
- Hires a hypnotist to convince him that when he gets to the line, he is prime Steph Curry.
