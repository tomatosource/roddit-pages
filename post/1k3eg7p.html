Will the “stay ready crew” get many minutes these playoffs?

The thing I’ve noticed about the Celtics versus other teams in the playoffs, which I feel is a Mazzulla thing, is a lack of trust in bench players. Last season, the Celtics only played 8 players in 8 out of the 19 playoff games* (several games were 8 players + bench garbage time, but I’m not going that deep).

Did the “stay ready” crew, as House and Scalabrine call them, do enough during the regular season to earn some minutes in the post season? Or is Mazzulla going to lose trust in them and keep them out of a close game?

Baylor Scheierman showed up in a few key games and Torrey Craig was picked up as a free agent for his “experience”. J.D. Davison was handed an NBA contract after 3 G League seasons and 1 MVP. Will the three of them be good enough this year for Mazzulla to look deeper to the bench?

My thinking is no, when the going gets tough I doubt many games will go past the big 9 of starters plus Al, Pritchard, Kornet, Hauser. But I could be wrong. Perhaps Mazzulla needed this whole season to test the bench players where last season they weren’t able to tap into their abilities.

*Heat Game 2, Cavs Game 4+5, Pacers Game 1+4, Mavs Game 2+3
