Cooper Flagg will have a 1-of-1 rookie card expected to have more value than the Paul Skenes baseball card that auctioned for $1.11 million

Flagg will wear a “debut” patch on his opening night jersey. It will be removed, authenticated, and later put on a Topps Rookie Debut Patch Autograph card. The concept is modeled after the Paul Skenes baseball card that auctioned for $1.11 million.
