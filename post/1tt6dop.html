We are almost certainly going to see either the tallest or shortest* FMVP in history

The current tallest Finals MVP in NBA history is Kareem Abdul Jabbar at 7 foot 2 inches tall, and the shortest is Isaiah Thomas at 6 foot 1 inches tall.

If the Spurs win the series, Wembanyama is the heavy favorite to win FMVP, and he is reportedly 7 foot 4 inches, although I think he’s actually taller than that. So if he wins FMVP, he would be the tallest to do so in NBA history.

On the other side, if the Knicks win Jalen Brunson is the favorite to win FMVP. While google claims he’s 6 foot 2, almost everyone I can find who has met him in person says he’s closer to 6 foot even or potentially 5’11, which is also backed up by photos of him next to other players that are listed at 6’2 or around there.

At worst I think he would be tied with Isiah Thomas at 6’1 for shortest FMVP ever.

Kind of a fun coincidence

Edit: “almost certainly” is definitely an exaggeration, but I would say it’s most likely going to happen.
