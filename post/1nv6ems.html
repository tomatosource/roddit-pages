Skill point atlas needs extra love (imho)

I have read someones comment just now and realised how right he was. The gearing in PoE2 is absolutely painful in sense of upgrading pieces compared to PoE1 and not in a good way.

I get a new ring , nice! more rarity but im lacking max life now on prefix, but wait I cant get max life through tree anymore, so I have to get life on my belt, but wait! Now I dont have enough mana so I have to upgrade another item but now I dont have enough fire resist!!

In PoE1 you could just move few points in your skill tree around to fix your build quickly for minor amounts of lacking said stat and getting upgrades slowly by 1 item felt great, in PoE2 it feels like you need to switch your whole gear if you move one item or just get exactly same affixes just with bigger numbers.

I loved getting new upgrades, it waas major part of my enjoyment fromt he game. Basically played few hours and at end of session spent my divines I aquired to get minor or bigger upgrades. Now In PoE2 I am sitting at 120 divines raw currrency that I accumulated in past 2 weeks and just cant get myself to buy a single upgrade just from the knowledge that when I do, I have to switch most of my gear at same time. I have same gear that I bought entering t15 maps two weeks ago and still farm 0 revive, T15 full delirium 3 tablet maps and only upgraded my bow to 580 dps 2 additional arrow one that I crafted myself because weapon doesnt brick your build.

tldr: Would be great to see some change to atlas variety of prefix/suffix choices even if not mathematically efficient just to fix your build quickly if you lack some smaller amount of stats instead having to line up all gear piece choices and switch whole build everytime u want to upgrade.
