Wand Bases are vastly inequal

In this I’d like to pay attention to the implicits. Which I really hope get looked at in the future.

Galvanic Wand: I think this actually holds a lot of potential, if the buff is made to not be consumed when the orb is spawned. More chance to shock is solid, but more importantly, the orb from this buff gets its limit scaled with stormweaver, and could be a really appealing base for them. At the moment spawning an orb then having to recast the buff makes this very tedious and not worth it.

Withered, Volatile, Bone Wands: I’m of the opinion implicit weapon skills are bad. They cant be accessed as gems, and thus can’t be triggered nor generally used with weapon uniques. We aren’t working with a large range of skills in each category already, and I think its a mistake to have skills locked in such a manner. Plus this does absolutely nothing for any build that doesn’t use the ability as their main skill usually.

Spellslinger Wand: what everyone thinks about, dreams about, and wants. Undoubtably an amazing implicit and very fun. This feels great, and likely should always remain the pinnacle of wand implicits. Even if it is ludicrously op.

Attuned & Siphoning Wands: next to useless. I know mana drain can have some uses, but both these skills are just far too niche. Especially power siphon, power charges have very little uses for casters, and will always be solved by Infusion of Power while it exists anyway.
