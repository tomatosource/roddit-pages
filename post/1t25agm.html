A worthwhile reminder

Look, by no means am I a Celtics die hard. I am someone who lurks, follows results and watches games when I can from over here in Scotland.

At the start of this season of basketball, a good percentage of Celtics fans would have admitted it was a year where they expected nothing. Tatum was down and looking likely out for the whole season, we’d lost key stars who had been crucial to previous success.

This year has been a testament to what Celtics basketball should be. “Identity” is harder to come by in sport than what we’re led to believe. Fans can talk about it, but unless it is actually displayed it doesn’t really mean anything, does it?

We’re in the business end of the season, and when I look across the other sports I follow, those I’m more invested in, it’s clear that something predictable arrives across them all. That is to say, the unpredictability of it. Clear favourites fall at the first hurdle all the time. Shocked by plucky underdogs whose stars are overlooked because of a myriad of reasons. Perhaps key decisions fall a certain way or injuries occur at the worst time.

If the Celtics lose tonight and we all begin that frustrating analysis of where it’s all gone wrong, remember where that team was way back on opening night. Remember what you felt coming into the season. It’s been such a fantastic year and possibly (who knows what happens in sport!) a super stepping stone towards success moving forwards.

Having said all that…

Celtics by 30!
