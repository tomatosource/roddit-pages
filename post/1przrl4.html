Nine Innings: Universal Baseball Drinking Game

Objective:

Survive all nine innings (or more!) while enjoying your beverage of choice. Pace yourself, cheer your team, and have fun!

Setup:

Beverages: Any drink works—beer, wine, cider, soda, juice, sparkling water, or anything else.

Glasses/Bottles: One per inning (more for extra innings).

Optional: Pen and paper to track innings and drinks.

Basic Rules:

  1. Inning Drinks:

Each inning has two halves: top (away team) and bottom (home team).

Take half a glass for each half-inning.

Example: Top of the 1st → drink half your 1st glass. Bottom of the 1st → drink the other half.

  1. Extra Innings:

If the game goes into extra innings, prepare one new glass per inning.

Continue the half-glass rule for each half-inning.

  1. Pacing:

Take small sips throughout the half-inning to finish your portion.

Hydrate (with water) if needed, especially with alcoholic beverages.

Optional Baseball-Themed Rules:

Hits and Runs:

Home run → extra sip.

Team scores a run → optional tiny sip per run.

Strike/Out Penalties:

Every strikeout by the batter → one sip.

Three strikeouts in a row by a pitcher → two sips.

Pitching Drama:

Pitching change → optional cheer sip.

Double play → everyone takes half a sip to mourn the runners.

Special Plays:

Grand slam → finish your current half-glass immediately (optional challenge).

Walk-off win → finish your remaining cup in celebration.

Non-Alcoholic Twists:

Flavor innings → use different drinks each inning for variety.

Temperature challenge → alternate between hot and cold beverages.

Speed round → quick sips if a team scores 3+ runs in an inning.

Winning / Finishing:

The game ends after the last inning (or extra innings).

Finish all glasses → you’re the MVP of Nine Innings.

Behind on drinks → finish the remainder to “catch up.”

Safety & Fun Tips:

Drink responsibly. Pace is key.

Alternate sips of water to stay hydrated.

Snacks are encouraged.

Feel free to make house rules to fit your group.
