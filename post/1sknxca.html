Luka will be the first scoring champ to miss All-NBA since Bob McAdoo in 1976. Cade will be the first 23/9/5 statline ever to miss All-NBA. Ant will be the first player to score 28ppg on 57% eFG shooting to miss All-NBA since Adrian Dantley in 1982.

  • 1. Luka

Every scoring champ in the last 50 years made an All-NBA team. Luka will be the first in the last 50 years to miss out on All-NBA. The only other time it happened was Bob McAdoo in 1976. (Curiously McAdoo finished 2nd in MVP voting but did not make one of the two all-nba teams.)

  • 2. Cade

23 times in the history of the league a player has put up a 23/9/5 statline (and qualified for leaderboard). Cade x2, Luka, Harden, LeBron x2, Magic, Jokic x4, Robertson x9, & Westbrook x3. Every single one of those seasons, the player made an All-NBA team. Until now.

  • 3. Ant

23 times in the history of the league a player has put up 28 ppg while shooting 57 or better efg. Kareem x3, Giannis x6, Barkley, Curry x2, Dantley, Luka, KD, Ant, Embiid, Shai, Jokic, Donovan Mitchell, Shaq x4. In 4 of these instances the player played less than 65 games (Giannis x3, Curry). It hasn’t happened since Adrian Dantley, 44 years ago, who is the only ever to miss an All-NBA team (back when there were only two All-NBA teams). Until Now.
