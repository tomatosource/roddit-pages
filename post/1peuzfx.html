Which play from your favourite team completely changes the outlook of that opposition in the long run?

In NBA terms, Lillard’s buzzer beater off Paul George in the 2018 Western Conference 1st round signalled the end of the Westbrook era and began a 7 year long rebuild towards NBA title

In the MLB, Big Papi’s Grand Slam off Benoit in 2013 ALCS game 2 started the slow decline of Tigers into mediocrity as the Tigers would got humiliated by the Orioles the following season with more or less the same roster as the previous season but with a new manager by the name of Brad Ausmus (Boone’s bench coach). As a result, Scherzer would depart for the Nats by the offseason, Porcello traded to the Red Sox, Verlander gone by 2017 and so on.
