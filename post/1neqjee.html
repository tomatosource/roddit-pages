Monta Ellis was the last player to average 40+ mpg in a season, will we see it again any time soon?

Monta Ellis lead the league in back to back seasons in ‘10-‘11 in mpg averaging 40.34 and 41.36 mpg. No player has done gone above 40mpg since then with Josh Hart being last years’s minutes leader at 37.62.

Prior to Ellis there were only 2 seasons since 1987 where we didn’t have at least one guy averaging 40+ minutes.

As recently as 2006 we had 9 guys above the 40 mark.

Is this a permanent trend or will we ever go back to the old days?
