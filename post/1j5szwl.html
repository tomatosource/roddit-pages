[Unpopular opinion] Gems should drop with a name

Hi everyone,

I feel like I’m in the minority here but I need to get it out of my system: I miss the PoE1 gem system.

I find PoE2 gameplay superior in almost every aspect, especially due to having an actual combat system (which PoE1 lacked), but I miss the joy of finding a new support gem in the floor and trying to fit it somewhere in my build. Ok, tbh, the links and sockets, not so much. Links and sockets can go to hell. What I miss is the fact that skill and specially support gems come with a name so I have a clear opportunity instead of a list of 100+ options. I don’t want to make decisions all the time while playing.

Ok, you caught me, I admit it: I come from Ruthless. I like making my build from scraps. I was not fond of Siosa and Lily either but, at least, you came to them when your build was starting to roll, in order to fill in the gaps. It was a decision you made consciously once or twice. Now I have to make this decision every time I drop an uncarved skill or support gem, and spend some minutes scrolling up and down a huge catalog or stick with the ‘go-to’ suggestions and make the most boring build ever.

I don’t know if there’s much room for improvement here, but I wanted to make my point, specially now that 0.2.0 comes with more support gems and all I can think of is ‘great, more scrolling’.

As I don’t think this is going to be addressed anytime soon (again, I know I’m in the minority), I also propose a small fix: skill and support gems should drop less (I don’t think it’s normal that only playing the first 3 acts of the campaign I can unlock every skill two or three times) and be more valuable for vendoring (something like 10g*level) so we are at least happy to find them. I know we’re in EA and we’re supposed to test stuff but right now it feels like they are everywhere, and selling them for 25g (or 1g!) makes them almost not worth to pick.

Glad to know your opinions.

TL;DR: Skill, support and spirit gems should drop with a name, less often, and sell for much more to vendors.
