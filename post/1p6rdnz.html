A way to make Ignite special: make it the growing/refreshing DoT.

With the current conversations on DoT scaling, here are my two cents on how DoTs (particularly ignite) could be made unique in a thematically appropriate way.

Bleed: Keep the hit scaling. If any DoT can scale off the hit damage, Bleed would be the one where it makes thematic sense. Big chop = more blood. It helps that bleed is in a good state mechanically, so if it ain’t broke don’t fix it.

Poison: Remove the hit scaling (make it scale from base damage like in PoE1) but preserve the ability to scale by number of hits. This also makes thematic sense.

So what about ignite? I have always thought that ignite lacks identity, but it’s easy to turn it into its own unique thing by just taking, again, the thematic angle.

Make ignite the DoT that “grows” through its duration.

  • Keep scaling fixed to base damage, like poison.
  • Unlike poison, it doesn’t scale from number of hits.
  • Uniquely for ignite, add ways to increase the damage of an already existing ignite, and to refresh ignite duration (think fanning the flames).

It makes a lot of thematic sense that the initial hit doesn’t matter as much for ignite (a single spark can start a blaze) but that you’d be able to feed into the already existing fire through other mechanics. This lends itself pretty well to combo gameplay. For example, wind skills could get synergy with fire: imagine Wind Blast increasing magnitude of ignite by 20% per hit, adding one second to duration, and proliferating the ignite backwards, for example, or spear whirlwinds “carrying” the same ignite between enemies.

The gameplay of an ignite build could look like getting one fire started and feeding into it, propagating it through the map (maybe bridging over burning ground?), transferring it between enemies and keeping the magnitude increasing. Or, of course, you could opt out of the combo aspect of it and simply scale DoT magnitude and duration.

Anyway, just an idea to give ignite some unique identity in the same vein as the unique scaling angle from poison.
