I Think I’m GGG’s Target Player, I Don’t Get It Either

Long story short, I found myself with a lot more spare time after being laid off; I had purchased PoE2 before I was laid off (some friends said they wanted to play and backed out) and decided to play through the game with all my free time. The 0.2 patch hit while I was midway through the cruel difficulty. I was playing a physical based blood mage, which almost certainly isn’t meta, but I had been bopping merrily along towards the end of the cruel difficulty before the patch hit. After reading up on the patch, and the dev’s reactions, I think I’m the guy that GGG is trying to appeal to…and I have no clue why they think people like me would want this.

As background, I’ve played a lot of “Soulslike” games, I’ve played action RPGs since the original Diablo came out and I play a lot of other action RPGs that are somewhere between the two genres like Monster Hunter. I also didn’t like PoE1’s endgame; the focus on full clearing maps as quickly as possible did not appeal to me, that felt pretty trivial and uninteresting. Probably my most played ARPG in the Path of Exile style is the much slower Grim Dawn; so, GGG, I am your target player and…I still don’t get it, here’s why:

This Combat is Not “Meaningful”

Slowing down the pace of combat does not mean it is meaningful; what makes combat meaningful is when your skill at combat determines how well you do. In Elden Ring or Monster Hunter you can kill things with basic skills and no armor if you’re good enough, your skill can carry you through any fight; yes it’s way easier if you properly gear and/or level your character, but if you want to make the game harder for yourself you can and still win. When I perfectly dodge the Waterfowl Dance and don’t take a hit, that isn’t meaningful because it’s hard, but because that means there is an opening for me to do big damage on Malenia; Malenia is fast, but if you can get some hits in, she’s easy to stagger, and dodging the Waterfowl Dance puts me in a position to deal out some punishment. Similarly, if I hit an offset attack in Monster Hunter Wilds, I have used my skill and timing (or maybe luck) to knock over a giant monster and I get a high damage follow up combo attack. The reward for properly dodging in PoE2 is typically…more dodging. What’s more, the big attacks don’t feel meaningful here; if I get off a fully charged Bonestorm attack my reward is watching that white monster walk right through it like it’s nothing. Combat being meaningful isn’t just about speed, it’s about IMPACT; each attack or dodge matters when the impact matters.

Mixed Signals on My Build

When I play this sort of game, I want to be able to tell if my build is good or not and make tweaks accordingly; but when I play PoE 2, particularly after patch 0.2, I get a whole lot of mixed signals. I will take, as an example, the final area of Act 2, so SPOILERS if you haven’t played that yet. The fight across the dreadnought felt like an absolute slog for my physical damage minion blood mage, I’m sure I’m not playing the meta and I’m doing something wrong, but the entire time I fought through the dreadnought area, it felt like I would walk onto a platform, immediately get attacked, have to just frantically dodge and weave, getting off little attacks and chugging potions until my minions and/or my quick attacks could finally bring the enemy down. The biggest moment of “Oh fuck” came when I got to a large platform piled with bodies and saw at least a dozen enemies literally leaping towards me; at this point I felt my build was trash and was thinking about how to redo it from scratch. Then I got to the boss, and I absolutely bodied him and made him my bitch. The giant magic mech created by the greatest technomancer Wraeclast has ever seen? No problem! A bunch of naked guys with sticks? Absolutely beating my ass. Is my build good or not? Fuck if I know. The bosses are trivial for me and certain mobs are absolutely insanely fast, what exactly am I supposed to take away from this?

…And Finally, Maps

…or waystones, whatever you want to call them. So I beat the game and I tried a waystone, starting with a nice clean white one at tier 1. I was really curious to see if the map/waystone system was better than PoE1, where I found it pretty boring. I loaded up the waystone and it was….largely empty. The goal was just to kill a couple of rare monsters and it took a good 30 minutes because it took so long to kill the white monsters and when I got to the rare monsters they were no different than the other rare monsters I have killed. This just feels like the maps from PoE1, but slower; sure I’m not trying to full clear in 30 seconds, but this is somehow worse. It does kind of look like there is something interesting here, there are areas on the new map system that look different and maybe they do cool stuff, but I’m not willing to slog through more of what I just experienced.

In Conclusion

Even as someone who wanted something different from PoE2 compared to PoE1….I don’t get the goal or the aim here. This does not feel fun, this does not feel interesting, so many decisions seem to be to give good things major downsides rather than to just make good things be good. I understand wanting to slow down gameplay a bit, but if you do that, then individual spells, attacks and dodges have to feel like they actually matter. I’ll probably come back after 1.0 and see if things are better, but for now, I’m out.
