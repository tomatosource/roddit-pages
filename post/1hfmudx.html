Here’s my annual trade for Torrey Craig post

Just about every year I see Torrey Craig somewhere maybe he shouldn’t be and i want him in Boston.

I’ve long called him Spicy Ojeleye.

My purposed trade:

Boston gets: Torrey Craig

Boston gets a veteran wing with defensive ability and plenty of playoff experience. Craig has had playoff stints with the Nikola Jokic Nuggets and the Devin Booker Suns. He plays with an edge i feel our team lacks. Hes kind of an asshole and will be the first to get in someone’s face after a hard foul. I think we could really use that fire. On defense hes got energy and decent length. On offense he hits his wide open 3’s, he just doesn’t take many shots. The last 2 seasons + this seasons hes hit 39% of his 3’s.

Chicago may be incensed to get a deal done, after years of not getting off their ass and moving players to create value. Last year they held onto Andre Drummond and got bounced in the play-in, and then lost him for nothing, instead of picking up 2 2nd round picks.

Chicago gets: Jaden Springer, 2030 2nd round pick, unprotected

Chicago gets a 2nd for an expiring role player before they lose him for nothing. They also get to take a peek at Springer, who can’t get on the floor for this histroically deep championship roster I’m Boston.

Boston has a handful of better 2nd round picks, so because this one is so far out it doesn’t get protections.
