Jaylen is having the weirdest season of his career

Am I the only one who feels like Jaylen is having a weird season? I’ve watched every game, and it just doesn’t feel right. I do love his reliance on the mid range jumper. He’s good at it, has always been, and it’s clearly been the driving force behind his career high in efficiency so far (61% TS).

I also think his passing has been much better this year, just from an eye test perspective.

Per 36 min Jaylen is through the roof scoring, at 31.7, and I have loved his aggression.

But his advanced stats are weird. Per cleaning the glass, his on/off rating is -14.2(!!!!!) which ranks in the 13th percentile.

Of the starting 5 (White, Pritchard, Brown, Queta, Minott) Jaylen is the only player with a negative plus minus.

I still think he’s having a good season. I just can’t wrap my head around his advanced numbers looking like this when it feels like he’s been the teams biggest over achiever.
