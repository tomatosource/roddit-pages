Will a hitter ever challenge Ball 4?

In Dodgers @ Jays (04-08-26), Vlad Guerrero Jr takes ball 4 on a 3-0 count. Tie game in the bottom of the 7th. Runners on 2nd and 3rd. One out. Jesus Sanchez was on deck. When he takes the walk, the situation changes from 3-1 count with no real double play opportunity to 0-0 count where a ground ball ends the inning. The Jays did not get another run. Sanchez struck out and Okamoto grounded into a fielders choice.

If you’re Guerrero Jr., you have to think you’re a better hitter than Sanchez. In this very high leverage situation, don’t you want one more shot at a big RBI opportunity?

I know there’s a famous instance of Albert Belle trying to refuse to go to first on a HBP, but now hitters actually have a way to potentially overturn a walk in high leverage.
