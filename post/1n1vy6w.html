Eurobasket 2025 - Day 1 Overview and NBA Player

Eurobasket 2025 has started with Group A and B at Day 1. Several NBA players playing and if you are an NA fan you finally feel the same pain as us Europeans trying to catch the NBA.

Heres a quick overview at how it went and how the NBA stars did with relevant stats.

Group A

Portugal vs Czech Republic
The first match of Group A was the one of Portugal vs the Czech Republic. Not many NBA players on either side, the Czech have Vit Krejci from the Hawks while Portugal has Neemias Queta from Boston. The game was a really nervous one with a lot of offensive mistakes on either side with good defense. However the Czech could not contain the best player on the field which was Neemias Queta and in the end Portugal managed to get an 62:50 win.

NBA Player Stats:
Neemias Queta: 23/18/1 Rebounds along with 4 Blocks on 4 Turnovers
Vit Krejci: 10/5/0

Latvia vs Turkey
With the first game being more of the underdogs, this game was between the teams that are supposed to be second and third. And there arent many more NBA players, however the quality increases aswell as some EX-NBA players like Bertans being in the mix.
Latvia starting Porzingis (Atlanta) while Turkey playing Sengun (Houston) and Bona (Philly). This game was supposed to be close but really wasnt as Turkey dominated the play winning every quarter and in the end beating Latvia 93:73, Porzingis had a rough showing while Sengun was really good but overall it was a team effort by Turkey,

NBA Player Stats:
Porzingis: 10/4/1 with 6 Turnovers.
Sengun: 16/8/7 with 5 Turnovers
Bona: 4/3/0 with 2 Turnovers.

Serbia vs Estonia
Yeah Serbia is one of the best teams in the world even without NBA attending. Now they have Jokic, Meanwhile on the other side of the court is the small baltic nation Estonia. And this one wasnt really close. Serbia dominated start to third quarter and then coasted to their 98:64 Win vs the underdogs who have no NBA players.

NBA Player Stats:
Jokic: 11/10/7 with 3 Turnovers
Bogdanonic: 11/2/7 with 5 Turnovers
Jovic: 18/4/6 with 1 Turnovers
Vukcevic: 9/7/1 with 1 Turnover

Group B:

Great Britain vs Lithuania
On potential european NBA expansions London is often a city mentioned. Well currently it is not known for their good Basketball (not for their good soccer either as you United showed today). And their game vs a solid Lithuanian side was no different. Valanciunas was the only NBA player on the court and with his 9 Rebounds he moves to 4th all time Rebounds at Eurobasket. Ahead of him are Boris Diaw, Dirk Nowitzki and Paul Gasol.
Still Great Britain had a decent showing despite being outmatched in every regard and losing by 24 at the end.
94:70 Lithuania.

NBA Player Stat:
Valanciunas: 18/9/1 on 2 Turnovers.

Germany vs Montenegro
Even though three of the German NBA players not playing for different reasons (Hartenstein, Kleber and Mo Wagner) Germany is still considered an absolute powerhouse with FIBA Schröder and Franz Wagner. Montenegro however showed that with Vucevic they can keep up for a while. At half time the Germans were only ahead by three. However that quickly changed as in the third European Curry Andy Obst arrived making 4 Three Pointers to push Germany away and let them score an impressive win. Schröder and Franz also played well and taking their part to set the shooter up while the War on Theis continued.
Still Montenegro looks dangerous for the second/third position in the group. 106:76 Germany.

Schröder: 21/2/4 with 1 Turnover.
Franz Wagner: 22/8/1 with 2 Turnovers
Tristan da Silva: 4/1/0 no Turnovers.
Vucevic: 23/10/5 with 2Turnovers

Sweden vs Finland
Northern Faceoff with Lauri Markannens Finland vs a Swedish Team that has Pelle Larsson from Miami as their resident NBA player but is lead by Ludvig Hakanson who plays in Spain. Sweden was a big udnerdog while Finland wanted to prove that they belong into the conversation for second or even first with Germany and Lithuania. The game however was absolutely wild. While the Swedish Players had absolutely no answer to Lauri and Finland was leading for most of the match they just quite couldnt shake off persistend Swedish players, who seemed to run on pure willpower alone. The third quarter ended tied and with 13 Seconds left Hakanson made two free throws to have the game within one point.

However that wasnt enough for Sweden who can still be proud for an electric game while for Finland more questions arise than being answered.

NBA Player stats:
Markkanen: 28/6/3 with 1 Turnover.
Pelle Larsson: 10/2/1 no Turnovers

Overall awards from me:

Player of the Day: Lauri Markkanen
Without Markkanen Finland loses by 20. Enough said he was simply better than the rest. Other teams either had too easy games or had a team effort.
Honorable Mentions: Queta and Sengun

Best Game of the Day: Sweden vs Finland
It was close and it was tense. And basically the only tense game for four quarters today.
HM: Germany vs Montengro

Worst Game of the Day: Czech Republic vs Portugal
That was dreadful and apart from Queta being a beast I took nothing of it.
HM: Serbia vs Estonia just felt like Garbage time after 6 Minutes.

Disappointment of the Day: Latvia
Expected way more, got steamrolled by Turkey and Porzingis wasnt good either.
HM: Czech Republic

Positive Surprise of the Day: Sweden
Kept it close and fought hard.
HM: Montenegros first two quarters and Turkey.
