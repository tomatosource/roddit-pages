853.6 hours later, I don’t think PoE2 is the game for me

I was super excited about PoE2 after several thousand hours in PoE1.

Dove hard into 0.1 and 0.2. enjoyed 0.3, and 0.4 I never even finished the campaign, despite 3 attempts with 3 different builds.

This isn’t a criticism of the game, nor a complaint. The game is fantastic in almost every possible way.

This is introspection and the realisation that I simply don’t have the skills necessary to play this game.

I decided to try another campaign run because I’m bored and have nothing better to do. Had a super smooth run through act 1. Chill and easy. Only 3 deaths, all because I was watching something on my 2nd screen and got distracted and stood in a degen. Doesn’t matter.

Get to Geonor. It’s necessary to have absolutely perfect positioning, movement, timing, and dodging for the entire fight, while maintaining godly APM to execute all the skill combos perfectly to kill him, twice.

After a crapload of getting absolutely annihilated by him over and over again, I realised that I just don’t have what it takes to play this game. I’ll never have the mechanical skill required. My wrist hurts like hell. I know that I should grind it out to get 10 more levels, then go try the fight again and die 20 more times. But I just don’t have any more desire or motivation to do it, especially with the pain.

I really want to be hyped up about 0.5, but I feel none of the excitement I usually feel leading up to a new league. Instead I just feel dread at having to do this again, and fail over and over again.

I don’t want to do comparisons with PoE1 or why I don’t have the same problems there, it’s not fair to either game.

TLDR: I suck at the mechanical skills necessary to play this game.
