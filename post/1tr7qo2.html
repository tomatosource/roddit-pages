She’s the keeper of time — so why can’t Chronomancer stop her own hourglass?

Time Freeze stops the entire battlefield. Enemies, projectiles, everything frozen mid-air. Everything except the one thing on the whole class that’s literally a clock: Quicksand Hourglass.

Sands of Time just keeps sliding — skill speed running 60% down to 1% and back, AoE doing the inverse — completely ignoring the fact that you just froze time. The keeper of time can stop the world but not her own hourglass. That feels backwards.

Proposal: while Time Freeze is active, Sands of Time freezes with it. The buff locks at whatever value it’s at for the duration.

We already have the blueprint for this kind of interaction — Phased Form stores delayed damage, and Temporal Rift wipes it. A passive and a time skill that talk to each other and reward knowing your kit. Quicksand Hourglass and Time Freeze should do the same.

Why it’s good design, not just a buff:

• It rewards mastery instead of handing out power. Lock the 60% skill-speed peak for burst, or the 60% AoE peak for clear — same ceiling, you just have to earn the timing.
• It sells the fantasy. Stopping time should stop her time too, not just the enemies.
• It fits the class’s whole identity: windows, timing, control.

Same philosophy as Phased Form + Temporal Rift. Let the hourglass obey the woman who controls time.
