So I gotta ask.. Why does no one talk about Runecrafting?

To anyone coming in thinking RuneScape, sorry. Not RuneScape related.

I’m talking about the concept of turning socket-able runes (specifically the original ones, not empowered and soul cores or Idols) and making them consumable when currency is used on the item to function as the harvest craft equivalent of “more likely” reforges except it’s not a complete reforge (unless you use an alch) but more often then not a slam of some sort.

Example:

You have a body armour with life, fire resistance and flat defense on it with two sockets. You need cold res and you need % defenses so you socket a glacial rune and an Iron rune in it, and omen to double exalt an item. You could scale this modifier by tier of rune, 30% more likely with lesser upwards to 60-70% more likely at the highest tier of rune to slam onto your gear.

This doesn’t guarantee an affix but it’s a level of increased odds alteration that is more scaled towards Tag crafting and gives sockets more of a place in the crafting scheme as well as fulfilling more of that promise of “runes will take the place of the crafting bench” feel in crafting.

Downside I see is early league launch but after that when runes are typically a dime a dozen this feels like a fantastic way to utilize existing content to give BASE stats a helping hand in the crafting process, leaving sockets to both fill its intended early niche for filling holes in your build, and to make way for filling with more powerful options later once you’ve fixed it via affixes.

I see very few downsides except for early game but maybe y’all can come up with fringe cases I’m not thinking about. Every podcast or content take I hear about reintroducing removed sources like homogenization but never new ideas.
