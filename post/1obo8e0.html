Hey everyone! I’m sure you’ve seen all of the sites ranking players for the 2025-26 season. I figured I’d create a consensus of all the lists I could find.

For each No. 1 ranking, a player was awarded 100 points. For each No. 2 ranking, a player was awarded 99 points. If a player was not ranked, he received 0 points.

If a player was not included on any of the top 100 rankings, they did not make it on this chart. Injured players like Jayson Tatum, Tyrese Haliburton, Damian Lillard, Fred VanVleet were excluded from this list. Other injuries like Tyler Herro and Kyrie Irving were also factored.

The composite score indicates what percentage of all possible votes (total score divided by 1,200) that a player earned from these surveys. This chart is color-coded, searchable, and sortable for your convenience. There are three pages on the chart. The data is downloadable.
