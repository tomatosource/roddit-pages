Jeff Teague admitted he only went to the Timberwolves for the money: “Minnesota was a place to go die”

“I did not want to go to Minnesota. When I was in the league, Minnesota was a place to go die. I literally went for the money. I cried. I wanted to stay with the Pacers, right? But Thibs called me that morning, before the free agency really got going, ‘We’re gonna offer you three years, $63 million. My options were Sacramento, Utah, Timberwolves, or Pacers. It’s some bada-options. So I’m like I’m a gona f—k with Indiana. My agent was like, ‘They gonna lowball you,’” Teague recalled.

“My agent was like, ‘Alright, we gonna sign. We signed with the Timberwolves.’ I’m like, ‘F—k we didn’t.’ He like, ‘I never met somebody that’s pissed about $57 million,‘” Jeff narrated.
