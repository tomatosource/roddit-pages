Future identity of this team

I had a thought/realization that seems very prevalent toward the end of this season. Jaylen really seemed to form a leadership identity around this young new team, and it appears like he took a lot of pride in that throughout the season (I watched at least 75 games this year out of 82 plus the playoffs).

Ever since JT came back, it seemed like JB became more dramatic and emotional. He also severely underperformed compared to his 30ppg average & regular season dominance.

I am not a JT vs JB guy, I love them both and they are clearly brothers for life. There’s no concrete evidence of them personally clashing like the media loves to present, however I can really see how Jaylen feels neglected, especially after the leadership role with these young dudes and how surprising of a successful season they had this year.

This team’s recent playoff collapse further adds to this case, as they resorted to classic Celtics ball of trading off hero ball & desperation shots, a complete opposite of what made this team so good this year. Scal was talking about this and took the words out of my mouth.

Curious to see what type of dynamic exists next year, as JT & JB have shared the court successfully for years, but I do think with every thing JB has said and done this year, he wants to be the number #1 option
