Path of Exile 2 : Elemental Expression: Useless Acendancy and why

Hi Exiles,

So today i was pretty disappointed with the Elemental Expression Acendancy skill, as it is not doing alot at all.
Honestly dont understand why it does so low dps, and why it is a spell. (Being a spell could be cool if you would go spark Monk for example, yet it does only trigger on Melee crit, so a big fail).

These are my findings:

There arent any real good support gems for this skill other then:

-Elemental discharge (which still sucks because Monk is the shatter king due the 10% cold dps acendancy. Triggering elemental discharge will remove the freeze, and screw up your Herald of Ice).

-Blind; this support gem is i believe the ONLY thing that actually does something for elemental expression, yet a full acendancy point for only blind is bullshit as you can get it on your attacks too.

-Lightning Exposer; which will never proc cus its only 200-300 dps.

-Fiery Death; This was another option that could work, but as the fire DPS is also idiotic low, it wont ignite any enemies, thus skill will not trigger.

All other support modifiers which add + DPS are useless, wont even remove 10% life from a white mob.

My suggestion to GGG:

-Preferable let it trigger on any crit, to create more build diversity + Up the DPS by ALOT.

-Let the DPS be for what it is, but make it an ailnment skill, so let it trigger Shock + Ignite + Chill at a 500X rate or something (i still wouldnt think its that fun, but it would be most usefull).

For now this skill could be completly ignored, ignoring one of the coolest looking acendancy nodes ingame……
