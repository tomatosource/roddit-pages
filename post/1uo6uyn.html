Questions Media Members SHOULD be asking tomorrow, but likely won’t.

I have almost zero expectations for tomorrow’s press conference. There are likely very few questions that will be asked tomorrow that will make these guys uncomfortable. like the the patriots, after the Vrabel debacle, you got very few real, hard hitting questions because the reporters and people who cover the team just strangely don’t go there anymore.

I don’t want to see 8 questions about Paul George’s fit etc etc.

  1. Asking broke Bill how much of an influence private equity and external forces have on basketball decisions, given his well documented issues coming up with enough cash to finance this sale 2 years ago.

  2. If they say this decision wasn’t morivated by money, what was it? because it’s very difficult to make a coherent case that the Celtics are better now than a week ago.

  3. Is the goal still championships at all costs? the evidence directly contradicts this.

  4. Are the Celtics now just another portfolio item where streamlining operations and reducing costs is the #1 goal?

  5. With some of, if not the highest ticket prices in the NBA, how to you reconcile the moves of the last year +, especially this one, with that. Should fans be paying these prices, and still sell out the building with the product that is being put out and developed?

The last one especially, needs to be asked. This seems like a classic FU to fans, while prices continue to rise to attend games and events.
