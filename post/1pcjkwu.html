[NBA PR] Cason Wallace, Scottie Barnes earn Defensive Player of the Month honors

Source: https://www.hoopshype.com/story/sports/nba/rumors/2025/12/02/cason-wallace-scottie-barnes-earn-defensive-player-of-the-month-honors/87571707007/

NBA Communications: Oklahoma City Thunder guard Cason Wallace and Toronto Raptors guard-forward Scottie Barnes have been named the Kia NBA Western and Eastern Conference Defensive Players of the Month, respectively, for games played in October/November.

Cason Wallace: Leading NBA in steals per game (2.2); leading west in deflections per game (5.0); Thunder lead NBA in defensive rating (103.6)

Scottie Barnes: Only player in NBA this season with 30 steals and 30 blocks; 4th in east in deflections per game (4.0); Raptors are 3rd in east in defensive rating (111.5)
