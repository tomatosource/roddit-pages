LeBron James has now been in the NBA so long that the time he accidentally flashed everyone in the 2015 finals will be closer to his debut than the present day by the end of this year

https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/nba-finals-abc-accidentally-flashes-802084/

You can criticize if you want but I thought this was an important observation that puts things into perspective. I remember in that moment, I wasn’t at all thinking that he might not even be half way through his career. I was more thinking “wait, did we all just see LeBron’s dick on live TV.”

4,244 days passed between LeBron’s debut (https://www.espn.com/nba/game/_/gameId/231029023/cavaliers-kings) and that time ESPN accidentally caught a full on video of his dick in the finals (https://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/201506110CLE.html). It has been 4,062 days since. By the end of the next NBA season, it will have been not even at the midpoint of his career.

To recap, LeBron played 12 years, then accidentally showed a worldwide audience his dick, and then played even longer, which is insane longevity.
