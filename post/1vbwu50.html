What’s your favorite Celtics 2026-2027 season subplot?

I know a lot of fans hate these kind of conversations, as there’s a big subsection of fans (for all teams) that believe that the only thing that matters is winning, and any conversations outside of that are pointless.

Some fans believe rooting for one individual player over others means you’re not a real fan of the team. I don’t think that way. I’m a fan of the team and the individual players. And my enjoyment watching and rooting for this team doesn’t solely revolve around them winning a title. It’s much bigger than that for me.

With that said, there are a ton of things that I’m really excited for outside this team competing for a title, and I’m curious what’s exciting everyone else?

I’m sure lots of people are pumped to see Tatum’s level-up/MVP campaign year will be like this year now that he is the undisputed guy here in Boston.

I’ve already seen a ton of people who clearly are expecting a BIG year from Pritchard and probably excited to see him average 20+, contend for allstar, etc etc etc

But for me personally, there are 3 things that stand out most to me:

  1. What will JT do this year with more responsibility and a team that better suits his playstyle? And what will the reaction his play be? This has the potential to be one of the bigger stories in the NBA, so no surprise it’s top of mind for me
  2. Will Hugo take a leap and become an undisputed key member of our rotation? Our top 4 guys are clear: JT, White, PP, & PG13. Then there’s Robinson, Queta, & Hauser. Hugo has a chance to not only join this second group, but climb towards the top of it. I’m personally very excited to see if he’s ready and able to do that
  3. Will Cenac crack our rotation by end of season? Cenac was at one point an elite prospect with limitless potential. The length and athleticism that led to him being the #1 center in his high school class are still there. His potential for NBA defensive versatility is still there. There are legit questions about whether his NCAA production was limited due to his role or if it was limited due to his need for more experience.

Cenac has the potential be a very solid starter in this league and he addresses two of our biggest needs: back-up PF & a stretch big. We literally don’t have a backup PF or a big who can shoot in our rotation. As of now our only options are Garza and Walsh. Celtics love going double big and if Cenac can develop into a rotation guy, he would give us the ability to do just that. Him playing meaningful minutes this year will be a stretch, but I’m super excited to see if we get any glimpses that show us Cenac will be our starting 4 in the future.

Let me know what you guys are excited for. Would love to talk about it.

And if these types of convos aren’t for you, don’t worry, most of my posts will be more analysis and less fan fiction.

Go C’s! ☘️
