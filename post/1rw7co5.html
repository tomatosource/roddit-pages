Mapping LOTR Characters to Baseball Positions Because Why Not

So I was listening to some baseball podcast the other day and they had this wild segment about what positions the Fellowship members would play. Got me thinking way too hard about this nonsense but here’s my lineup

Catcher - Gimli

Pretty obvious choice here. All the great backstops have been stocky dudes - you want someone built low to the ground who can handle getting beat up behind the plate

Pitcher - Legolas

Dude’s got that perfect accuracy thing going for him, probably throws strikes all day. Would be like having Greg Maddux but with better hair

1st Base - Gandalf

At his age the old wizard’s lost a step or two, gotta park him somewhere he won’t get exposed. Still probably rakes though

2nd Base - Pippin

3rd Base - Sam

Shortstop - Merry

Left Field - Frodo

Look, the hobbits are gonna struggle out there, no way around it. They’re just not built for this game. Frodo gets hidden in left where balls don’t come his way as much. Sam seems like the type who’d grind in the cage until he figures it out. Merry and Pippin have that twin telepathy thing going which might help up the middle

Center Field - Aragorn

Perfect five-tool guy, reminds me of those classic center fielders from back in the day

Right Field - Boromir

Strong arm for gunning down runners, doesn’t need crazy speed out there

This lineup’s got some holes but honestly what else you gonna do with a bunch of fantasy characters
