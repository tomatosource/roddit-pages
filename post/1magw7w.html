roster construction has gotten a lot better across the league since 2020

Like the championship teams of the past were very complete teams and had complete lineups. Teams like the raptors, the warriors(obviously) and the cavs. They weren’t playing any bad players. However, the other playoff teams…like heavily flawed. There’s less glaring holes in rosters these days.

There are some teams with glaring holes in their roster. the lakers are a notable example, starting a center in Jaxson Hayes that probably wouldn’t even get playing time on most of the other playoff teams. But, two things, the Lakers roster was like that because they made a massive trade in the middle of the season, it wasn’t how the roster was going to stay(even tried to make a trade to fix it), and 2. the Lakers still had probably 6 good players and three REALLY good players.

a lot of the playoff teams in the 2010s were flawed. It feels like a lot of teams back in the 2010s were actually constructed like the suns were last year.

The Dame and CJ Blazers? Even in the conference finals in 2019, were starting Al Farouq Aminu, was probably the best defender on the team. him and Mo Harkless had a lot on their plate. but those aren’t all defense level defenders either.

The 2019 thunder had Russ and Paul George, but then were starting Terrence Furgeson. young jerami grant might not be getting minutes on other teams. that thunder roster actually had talented players. They had PG, Russ, Steven Adams, Dennis Schroeder and (maybe) a young Jerami Grant. that’s 4 or 5 good players.

Today, you have rosters like the Clippers last year had 8 good players: Kawhi, Harden, Norm Powell, Zubac, Derrick Jones Jr. Bogdanovic, Batum, Kris Dunn. The biggest flaw there is Kris Dunn not being able to shoot but he’s incredible defensively.

The Denver Nuggets of the last two years, who are often considered flawed with not a lot of depth around Jokic, had 6 or 7 good players depending on how you feel about Peyton Watson.

Tl;dr: rosters now = good
