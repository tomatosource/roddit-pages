Isaiah Stewart Spends Offseason Farming Dates In California

Two Detroit Pistons that are polar opposites from an on-court perspective, Isaiah Stewart and Duncan Robinson sat down for a pretty lengthy interview on the Old / Young Man and the Three podcast. During their chat, Beef Stew spoke about the citrus farm that he’s been working on out in Cali, and Duncan said he’d like to send his kids out there to learn what hard work really means someday.

This article about their podcast chat covered the extent of Stewart’s farm, and it sounds like the big time shot blocker has a budding passion for growing his own food as well. The duality of man.
