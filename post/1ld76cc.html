I built a system called FRPE (First-Round Pick Equivalent) to measure what teams actually paid vs. what they should’ve paid based on player tier. Here’s what it told us:

🛠 Methodology: How FRPE Works

We wanted a better way to evaluate whether a team overpaid or got a steal in a star trade. Here’s the system we built:

🔢 Step 1: Assign FRPE to All Trade Assets

Then we assigned values to everything in a trade:

Asset Type FRPE Estimate
Unprotected 1st 1.0
Lightly Protected / Late 1st 0.5 – 0.9
Pick Swap ~0.3-0.5
Second-Round Pick ~0.1 – 0.3
Young Starter / Rookie Deal 0.5 – 1.0
Fringe Star 1.5+
Veteran Starter / Rotation Guy 0.5 – 1.0

We added up all the FRPE paid in each trade.

🌟 Step 2: Define “Expected” FRPE by Star Tier

This is what stars typically cost in the current trade market:

Player Tier Expected FRPE
Generational MVP 8.0 – 8.5
MVP Caliber (Prime) 7.0 – 7.5
All-NBA Superstar 6.5 – 7.0
Aging All-NBA / Star ~6.0
Young All-Star ~5.5
Fringe All-Star ~5.0 – 5.5
Elite Role / Upside Player ~5.0
Expiring All-Star ~4.5 – 5.0
Volatile Star (e.g., Kyrie, this is an outlier) ~4.0 – 5.0

We based this on dozens of real trades since 2020.

💥 Step 3: Subtract Paid FRPE – Expected FRPE = Bargain or Overpay

Example:
Rudy Gobert → Expected 5.5, Paid 7.2 → +1.7 Overpay
Luka Dončić → Expected 8.5, Paid 7.0 → –1.5 Bargain

🧪 APPLICATION: What If the Spurs Traded for Giannis?

Here’s a mock trade proposal based on the FRPE system:

Spurs receive:

Giannis Antetokounmpo (FRPE Expected: 8.0)

Bucks receive:

Stephon Castle (2.0) (Reigning ROTY)

Devin Vassell (1.0)

3 First-Round Picks (3.0)

3 Pick Swaps (0.9)

Total Paid FRPE = 8.2

📉 Delta: Around Fair Value

This is a clean, MVP-caliber offer that lands right in the expected range for a superstar of Giannis’ tier. It’s expensive, but that’s what it costs to land a top-5 player still in his prime. A deal like this is very realistic if Giannis were ever made available — and the Spurs, with Wemby, are one of the few teams that could justify it.

💬 What This Shows

  • The market overpays for defense (Gobert)
  • Stars with contract or personality risk (Kyrie, Butler) go cheaper
  • Young players on team control inflate value (Bridges, Bane)
  • Truly generational stars like Luka or Giannis are almost priceless
  • Donovan Mitchell? Surprisingly one of the cleanest value trades

Of course, this is all subjective. I evaluated the price of first rounder using my own jurisdiction. Context is difficult to quantify, and all of these trades were done by teams under different circumstances. However, with all the discourse on what’s an overpay and underpay, I tried my best to create a system that answers this question

TL;DR

I built a model that converts all trade pieces into a unified pick value system (FRPE = First-Round Pick Equivalent). Then I compared what teams paid vs what they should have paid. It turns out a lot of GMs overpaid — and a few (like Dallas for Kyrie or LA for Luka) got incredible bargains.
