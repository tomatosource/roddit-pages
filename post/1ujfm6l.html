It cannot be overstated how much performance needs to be an absolute priority next couple patches.

4070
i7-12700k
1080p at 90fps
DLSS Quality, shadows, lights, etc all low

I have a very modest rig and but also very (I feel) modest settings. All I want to do is play this game at its endgame pinnacle level with some degree of smoothness with my preferred settings.

I have spent an inordinate amount of time doing every kind of backend fix you can think of – rebar, shader deletion, xmp profile tweaks, ddu driver re-installs, the list goes on.

No matter what every t15-t16 map I load into has stutter and massive frame drops during the beginning or just seemingly random throughout. Every other map I do will either crash or freeze. I want to juice my maps and stack all the league mechanics, and my build for once can actually handle the content, but not my PC or my game.

The freezes/crashes always happen when many things happen on screen, e.g. delirium on top of an abyss whilst next to a boss. This screams a shader issue of some form. My rig is very well cooled and ventilated so my temps are not my issue.

The in game graph almost never spikes on the cpu and gpu lines, only on the shader and server lines.

The stutters are completely random. I can be walking through a tunnel with little to no enemies and get a stutter. And this is not even addressing the horrible occasional server rubberbanding.

No other game I play has this issue.

In 0.3 and 0.4 I also had this issue but I also had a 3060, so I thought my GPU upgrade would help alleviate this, but frankly the performance has somehow gotten worse.
