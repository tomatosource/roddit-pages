The unique strongbox situation….again

During 99% of 2.0, and some of 2.1, I had ran strongboxes as my atlas strategy. I didn’t really see many unique strongboxes. I have ran around 500 T15/16 maps and saw 10-20 unique strongboxes.

There had been discussions here with people stating the atlas node for unique strongboxes was bugged. And if you disabled it you’d start seeing them more. Well I switched my atlas points over to essences with the recent patch….ive had 5 unique strongboxes in my first 25 maps. Just wanted to try to bring more attention to this issue as I’m almost certain the atlas node for unique strongboxes is bugged somehow.

Sadly I haven’t kept track of all of my runs and I dont have actual hard data on this. Stay sane Exiles.
