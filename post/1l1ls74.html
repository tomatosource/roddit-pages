Would you rather lose all XP you gain in a map if you die but keep it all if you complete the map or keep it the way it is? (10% xp loss capped at lvl requirement)

No idea whether it would be feasible to implement in the game or if it’s something anyone else would want, but it feels to me like if I complete a map, I should keep the XP. I like there being a penalty for dying, but in the 90+ levels, 10% of XP can be a pretty big time investment for someone who doesn’t have a lot of time to play and juice maps and towers with increased %XP gain mods, and losing it because of a random one-shot death always feels harsh. Would it make it too easy to progress if any XP gained was permanently kept upon completing a map?
