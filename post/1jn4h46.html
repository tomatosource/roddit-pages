Something Jonathan said worries me

During the ziggy d interview while talking about recombination being added Jonathan talked about crafting and how he didn’t want that to overshadow ground loot. This struck me as odd because other than like the first 4 acts I rarely pick anything up off the ground.

While I don’t think ground loot is necessarily bad, in last epoch I love getting good ground loot, there is a fatal flaw with that in poe.

Having to identify every ground item before knowing if it’s good or bad is a terrible system for this. It makes it so you can’t even filter ground loot, so when you make it to end game there is next to no reason to pick things up. I mean you run 20 maps and 300 rares will drop are you really going to pick up identify and check out the affixes for every item? Is that the expectation?

I hope ggg rethinks this design philosophy.
