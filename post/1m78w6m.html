Allow us to trade through a “Player Vendor” NPC in the sellers hideout

Under such a system you just join a player’s hideout directly from the tradesite. No whispers, no waiting for a party invite. You click on the trade website, the game sends you a hideout invite and you trade with a vendor NPC that has the listed items inside a simple shop exchange window/tab (like those in campaign).

You click the vendor and there it is: the exact bow/spear/wand/mace/jewel you’ve been searching for. Hovering over it it’s listed for the same price as listed on the tradesite - No surprise messages from the seller: “give me an offer” or “Sorry, already gone” and then seeing the seller relist it for 5x higher 10 seconds later, no pricefixers listing expensive uniques for 1c and never trading, no waiting for someone to get online that has the perfect item you want but was “last seen online 2 months ago”.

You just click the item you want in their vendor NPC trade window, the exchange happens instantly and the divs get taken from your inventory.

While looking at the vendors trade tab, you spot a cool crit jewel right next to the item you saw, you weren’t even looking for it but it’s perfect for your build. One more click, divs taken from your inventory and now you have a jewel as well. Quick and efficient while still having some “friction” of having to go to hideout and trading “manually” - The Vendor NPC becomes like a shop storefront where you can see their other items for sale.

In the meantime the hideout owner returned from a map they were running, they didn’t even have to leave it or leave their 4 man party of friends while in the middle of a breach, to trade you their item, they could just continue. When they are ready the seller just clicks the NPC vendor and claim the divines.

For you two item upgrades secured and zero frustration to be had and for the seller it’s convenient and easy so they can list even lower value items late into the league without having to leave for 10ex trades.

This… this is at the minimum how trading should feel.
