Simple QoL option for ES builds

While playing the game, I rely in peripheral vision to notice changes to my life, as my eyes are focused on my cursor.

However, when 90% of my EHP is represented by a thin sliver on the side, while the 10% of my EHP, which is expended after ES is exhausted, is a very obvious red ball.

Similarly, the tiny bars above my character are too small for changes to draw my attention.

Yes, I am older, and wear glasses, I need all the help I can get.

The ability to swap the visual is already in the game, simply choosing CI will change the ball to ES, which is VASTLY easier for my poor old eyes to notice.
