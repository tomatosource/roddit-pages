Lakers defense pre vs post LeBron

Pre-LeBron, the Lakers’ defense was actually middle of the pack if not solid across the board:

• 14th in DRTG

• 13th in opponent PPG

• 15th in opponent 3P%

• 1st in opponent offensive rebounds

• 11th in turnovers forced

Post-LeBron, those defensive numbers cratered:

• 29th in DRTG

• 20th in opponent PPG

• 30th in opponent 3P%

• 7th in opponent offensive rebounds

• 14th in turnovers forced
