PSA for the new .build files

Hello just a fyi.
If you download a .build file on lets say mobalytics you have to download every section of the guide seperately, the thing is, if you add all of them into your gamefiles, ingame it will show every single one of them with the same name.
To change this you can simply open the .build file with your editor, ctrl+f for „name“, and then change it to whatever you desire for example Act 1 Martial Artist, Act 2 Martial Artist, Endgame Martial Artist etc.
Hope you all have fun this league!
