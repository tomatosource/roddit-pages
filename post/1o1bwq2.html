  1. Start with your desired amulet base ilvl 75 or higher with T1 cast speed
  2. Get any other base with bottom tier spell damage I.e 7% or less (non-corrupted)
  3. recombination them together selecting the T1 cast speed and T7 spell damage; repeat until you succeed (50-57% ; 75-80% with recomb omen)
  4. use homogenising omen of exaltation and omen of greater exaltation with a perfect exalted orb (this will give you +3 spells and T1/T2 crit damage bonus; about a 1⁄3 for T1 here)
  5. use an omen of sinistral crystallization and an essence of the abyss to remove the T7 spell damage and add “bear’s the mark of the abyssal lord”
  6. use a preserved collarbone to give you a desecrated prefix
  7. use an omen of abyssal echoes and reveal aiming to hit life or % life of your desired tier (not that hard to get T1)
  8. If you missed select any random mod that is NOT global defences.
  9. use an omen of sinistral crystallization and a perfect essence of enhancement to remove the desecrated mod and add global defences
  10. Go back to step 5 to remove the desecrated mod and repeat steps 5-9 until you hit life or %life.
  11. use an omen of homogenising exaltation and an omen of greater exaltation with a perfect exalted orb to slam T1/T2 spell damage and the other T1/T2 life mod (if you desecrated flat life you can use a greater exalted instead; it will still be T1/T2 % life but will be T1/T2/T3 spell damage but it’s much cheaper. Do not do this if you desecrate % life as flat life with a greater exalt rolls as low as T4)

Lastly use omen of sanctification and hit plus 4 to spell level
