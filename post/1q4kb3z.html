PoE2 SSF problem with Drenched

Currently the Drenched mechanic can only be (realistically) achieved by using Thunderstorm skill. There is no other reliable source of Drenched as far as I know.

Due to how the gem works, there is a limit to enemy level that can be drenched using the skill. Only from level 18 base gem you can drench all enemies, before that the hard cap sits at lvl78 enemies.

In SSF a lot of things relies on RNG, I know that. But all of them can be mitigated with either skill, knowledge, good build or just playing slower. However, I have been sitting on tier14 maps for quite some time - due to lack of Uncut 18 drops. I literally cannot progress, even thou I could as I melt everything on even the most juiced up Tier14 maps.

I’m blocked from progression due to the fact that i cannot drench my opponents that are above lvl 78 - and my self found / made build - heavily relies on this to do damage.

As someone who plays only SSF in all aRPGs (probably because when i started playing aRPGs online play was a dream) - I think I encountered a hard blocker like this for the first time.

This is also true for all hexes, and multiple other things (exposure sources etc) - where SSF is just simply blocked from progressing due to lack of Uncut Gem drop.
