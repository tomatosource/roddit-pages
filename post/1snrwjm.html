KAT’s ‘difficult’ season: Narratives in the media versus actual performance : does he deserve All-NBA?

KAT this year:

  • 20/12/3 on 62% TS
  • 1st in the NBA in total rebounds
  • 2nd in NBA in rebounding %
  • 7th in EFF
  • 8th in Win Shares
  • 10th in PER
  • 10th in defensive rating / best individual rating on the #7 defense in the league (besides Robinson whose mpg exclude him from qualifying)
  • Played 75 games as a modern big through multiple minor injuries

Obviously KAT had some major ups and downs this year, with the most difficulty adjusting to the new-look Mike Brown offense

But it seems the media did not really cover his turnaround or steady progress since January.

I don’t see him on almost any year-end All-NBA prediction lists.

What does everyone think about this? Is KAT more deserving of All-NBA this year than the media is letting on?
