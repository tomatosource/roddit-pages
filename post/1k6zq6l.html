A tale as old as time… Sekhemas afflictions…

Today I decided to run a trail of the Sekhemas with The Burden of Leadership relic. (Room types unknown). Not sure why I decided to do this, maybe just because I hadn’t done it before, but I know it would be failure. To nobody’s surprise, somewhere on the second level I landed up getting the No Energy Shield “minor” affliction. At this point I’ll add that I’m playing a Lich with Eldritch Enhancement (requires energy shield to cast spells).

I go from having no problem at all running this, to instantly bricked. I lose about 6k honor instantly, due to the loss of energy shield, and I lose the ability to even cast spells. At this point, you think, surely you just quit right? Nah, not me. I refuse to believe that anyone at GGG could be so catastrophically stupid that they designed a system that would require someone to just immediately quit. Surely, this is just the “vision” of a game that is difficult and that the difficulty can be overcome. So I respec out of Eldritch Enhancement so that I can cast spells again (at a cost of 50k gold, “great design”), and for some reason I can’t use Contagion anymore (not sure why, didn’t care to look). I’m thinking, maybe I will get lucky and hit a shrine that will allow me to remove the affliction so I can continue my run.

Amazingly, I manage to not only finish the second floor, but the entire 3rd floor as well, before wiping on the second room of the fourth floor. Now I’m on the fence. Could I have made it? Perhaps it’s not out of the realm of possibility, but is at least one person at GGG catastrophically stupid for the design? Yeah I think so.
