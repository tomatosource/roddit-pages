EV Life stacking is a horrible experience

With the change in Evasion Rating formula which basically lowers the amount of Evasion Rating in the early game to mid game to non existent for the ability to dodge everything, pure Evasion Rating with life stacking character is basically unplayable. The amount of hits that you can evade is lowered significantly comparing to the same character in the previous patch, the health pool is basically unchanged, The Great Boar notable only gives 1 health per 4 Dex which is insignificant, and due to low evasion rating and the Deflection clusters are in awkward positions, stacking Deflection is also not effective. There’s basically no point in playing pure Evasion life stacking, where I have to endure all the hardship and punishment if I misplay, comparing to a hybrid EV/ES character or even warrior with armour and health stacking. GGG said that they are trying to address the pure Evasion playstyle with deflection, my verdict is that they have failed to achieve this objective. What are you guys’ thoughts? Let me know in the comments.

P/S: one of the suggestions that I think that GGG should do is to divide the life mod in half, one half should be added directly to the explicit of pure Evasion/Armour/Energy Shield base armour depending on the item levels. This will alleviate the health problems in the early to mid game and smooth out the ability to play a life stacking character.
