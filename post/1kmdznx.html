Refreshing trade interaction

Just thought I’d shine a light on the nicer side of trade.

Last night I brought a focus on trade that was listed for 1 div. When trading the guy, I accidentally put up 8 div instead and when I tried to remove them, I held A on my controller whilst hovering over the stack to try and remove 7. Obviously on console when in trade this just accepts the trade… the other guy had already accepted and it went through…

I thought I’d lost 8 div just like that and I sat in the guys hideout as he proceeded to disappear back into his map. I tried to message him asking kindly for the divs back as it was an accident. He was ‘offline’. I sat in his hideout for the next 10 minutes just sighing and staring at the screen saddened I had lost 8 div so quickly. That was until…

The guy returned to the hideout. I messaged him again quickly and said he could keep half of the divs if he just gave me half back. Lo and behold, he offered a trade to me, put back all of the divs and accepted. I was eternally relieved. It’s easier than you think to make a mistake on trade. I tried to offer the guy at least 1 div for his honesty but he wouldn’t accept. Had to befriend the dude there and then.

Be careful when you are trading folks, just have the currency you need in your inventory and stash the rest!!

Things worked out in the end for me though.
