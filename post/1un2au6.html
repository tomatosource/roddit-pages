Heads up for Facebreaker users leveling alts!

It seems that the facebreaker bonus is more tied tro picking up a bosses reward than it is about killing them.

I was in a2 and noticed I hadn’t gotten completion on brambleghast and executiner. So I killed them and went back to a2. Noticed I STILL hadn’t had it. Went back and killed them again and picked up the gems they dropped.

When I got to balbala in a2 I killed her anc checked my map. No completion. Picked up the barya and her gem 6 she drops and my FB went up and the skull on map got the X.

So kind of a weird thing you may not know. Especially on an alt where you may have too many gems already!
