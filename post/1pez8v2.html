GGG: Please reconsider charges for Spell Totems (a suggestion)

For us spell totem enjoyers, hearing that they require 3 charges to activate a totem takes away from the classic spell totem play style.

Instead of them requiring 3 charges to create one, why not give them shorter durations or less dmg, but you can expend a charge to increase the duration and damage? This way instead you’re using charges to “empower” the totems, which is similar and inline with many other skills that get empowered by charges without the architype requiring 3 for usage.

With this change, you enable spell totem enjoyers to make it their primary focus for their build, but while still staying close to your new vision of requiring charges.
