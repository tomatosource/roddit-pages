An idea for making the skill tree more impactful

Each passive cluster needs to be stronger, especially ones that don’t directly give any damage.

There are a lot of different stats to get in PoE2–probably even more than in 1, as they add things like incision–and so there’s a lot of different stuff to cram into the skill tree. It’s fine to make them more sparse on the skill tree, and more spread out, if they have more of an impact when you get to a cluster of passives for something you want. Feeling like you need to path to every defense or crit cluster you can possibly reach with your limited points just doesn’t feel great, and stops you from getting other stats that would feel more fun to get.

Things like “% reduced slowing potency of debuffs on you,” “increased stun threshold,” “%increased mana cost efficiency,” “% increased damage (or crit chance, etc) against blinded (or hindered, etc) enemies,” and plenty more. People don’t get these kinds of stats nearly as much because they’re just not as impactful to get on the tree, and are better solved elsewhere instead of wasting skill points for them.
