Exploring the possibilities of physicality in PoE 2’s “Atlas” system

With this post, I’d like to explore with you the possibilities offered by PoE2’s endgame ”Atlas”.

First, let’s discuss 2 crucial terms.

Physicality?? Here I’ll define physicality as the quality of making something tangible, realistic, real. Interestingly enough, this interest for physicality has been mentioned first in a PoE 1 reveal, specifically the Mirage content reveal https://youtu.be/zFzHf3etCTA?si=Uvgd22adLefomgQ-&t=1191 and https://youtu.be/zFzHf3etCTA?si=TKvrncFAP2qMqDMK&t=1330 With Mark working on both games, this relatively recent interest for physicality likely comes from work on PoE 2’s endgame “Atlas”.

“Atlas”??? It’s important to note that PoE 2’s “Atlas” is not an atlas at all. It is more simply an aerial view of the world. And this changes everything when it comes to physicality. It is the crucial element upon which every other aspect of physicality can be built. We are quite literally observing the world, its places, its hills and valleys, its giant abysses, its towns, its landmarks, and so on.

I’d like to put forward several ideas, organised in these categories: building, collecting resources, exploring, layers, troops on the map, and evolving threats.

(re)Building

Here the idea is that you’d start with rubble on the map, and you’d gradually rebuild a city/camp/building/whatever. To help push physicality further, you’d see the representation of these rubble slowly being built over time, taking shape as objectives are met (bring 1000 stone and 200 wood). Here is an example of rubble turning into a town, all visible on the atlas.

I have a whole mock league where you’d rebuild Oriath by gathering resources all over the atlas, recruiting workers/soldiers by liberating camps and castles, it’d be pretty epic.

Collecting resources on the map

The idea is to disseminate resources all over the map in a natural way. You’d find sulfite in mountains, wood in forests, recruit villagers/workers by liberating enemy camps, and so on. It’d be relatively easy to find some use for these resources (sulfite for delve, wood for boatpedition, etc). The goal is to make exploring rewarding, make players anticipate the finding of resources and make advancing in the atlas more exciting, and make the world feel more real.

Exploring the map

I have 2 suggestions here : make exploring more exciting and rewarding, and give players tools to explore more efficiently.

Some suggestions: towns occupied by bandits/evil entities, liberating them could upgrade vendors or provide you with explorers/eradicators for the guilds presented below. Castles occupied by bandits/evil entities. Large landmarks visible in the fog of war: a large dragon flying in the sky, a big pirate ship, red smoke, a spooky lonely tower, a giant, etc.

Tools to make exploring more efficient: the Explorers’ Guild and the Eradicator’s Guild. The Explorers’ Guild would give you control of Explorers (shocking I know) which you could send on the atlas at the border of the fog of war. They’d take some time to reach that spot (ideally you would show the group moving on the atlas), and upon arriving, they’d reveal a big part of the fog of war. The idea is to send a couple explorers to some areas of the atlas while you are progressing somewhere else. The Eradicator’s Guild works similarly to mappers from Settlers in Poe 1. You send them to a map on the atlas, and they clear it after a while, you get rewards, and the map is marked as cleared. You could potentially make it possible for Eradicating groups to chain maps on the atlas. Here the idea is to progress further into the atlas, particularly around points on the map where nothing exciting/interesting is happening, and the player would rather focus somewhere else at the moment, but wants to go further past that “boring” set of maps. You could also let eradicators do layouts you hate.

Layers

Currently we only see the surface. Adding layers could help develop the atlas in a meaningful way.

The most obvious one is the underground layer, in which Delve would take place. Possibilities are endless in terms of environment. Who knows what’s going on underground, it could be absolutely anything, it would be modular, it would accommodate future leagues very easily. Moving in Delve could be done by going from point to point just like at the surface, but at the cost of sulfite/coal/whatever (found in maps, more likely found in mountains). To make it especially interesting, Delve could make you move several times faster than at the surface. You’d be basically covering long distances very quickly underground, then pop out back to the surface. This could reduce the tediousness of traveling long distances, which opens the way for objectives put quite far away from the player.

The other obvious layer is the sky, with sky islands for example. The Moon has also been mentioned in a Q&A before, we’ll get there once the atlas has been powercreeped xD.

I would like to add something a bit different: sub-layers. These are condensed layers tied to small structures, like a castle or a town. These sub-layers are opened by clicking on the map circle on the atlas. They would open a window which displays an aerial view of the building in question. On this map, you would see several map points linked by dotted-lines. Clicking on the map point would open a map, and the assets used in the map would reflect the assets used on the sub-layer map. You’d have to do these maps in order. For example, you’d have to clear 2 maps inside a castle before fighting against a General.

Troops on the map

In general, adding moving figures on the atlas would go a long way to make it feel more realistic and interesting. But here I’ll focus on more league-based moving elements on the map. These could be tied to Legion, and you’d see troops on the atlas. Opening maps around these troops would guarantee legion fights. Once they are defeated, they disappear (or better yet, you see them retreating in the direction of their general’s castle). A similar thing could be done with Bestiary, you’d see beasts on the atlas. Bonus points for the beast models on the atlas corresponding to beast models in maps. You could also have bigger targets like castles (easy to incorporate to something like Legion again).

Evolving threats

This one is inspired by Factorio, specifically the way aliens evolve over time. They become stronger, more advanced, as you play the game. Something similar could be done here, with a badass threat far away on the map slowly growing. Rather than being time based, the evolution would be map completion based. This could go from “woah that’s a cool alien hive growing in the distance, that’s crazy bro i can’t wait to go kick their ass” to “i’m so tired boss these guys are too strong i’ve taken too long to go there” very quickly so balancing this would be tough and scary. We could also imagine an evil empire slowly expanding in the distance, starting with a puny little castle, developing the castle into a megastructure, and building camps farther and farther away over time.

And that’s it, it’s possibly too long already xD I’d be very curious to hear what you would like to see on/above/under the atlas, I’m sure people have some crazy cool ideas.
