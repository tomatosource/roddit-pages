I would totally come back to this game if they had a season pass with at least one cosmetic set in it

I really truly hate the \(50 a pop armor sets. I would pay \)15 for a season pass with a cool looking cosmetic armor set that I have to unlock through gameplay— but not the insane grind fest you have to go crazy for within one season for the free one they offer you each season. I need something I can take at my own pace rather than racing before a deadline passes. The game itself is incredible. I do have my issues with crafting and wishing it had some more deterministic elements, but other than that, the biggest deterrent to me is a very expensive micro transaction for visuals. I realize this game is an upgrade from the previous one in terms of the visuals you can earn, but it’s still few and far between the cool stuff to collect for your character visually. And I’m somebody who’s probably gonna be playing standard if I play at all… not starting from scratch every season and trying to race to the finish to earn cosmetics and try some new mechanics… I just don’t have that kind of time unfortunately… more power to anyone who does have it and by all means you guys enjoy the game the way you’d like to… but I do wish there was an alternative for someone like me who needs a little bit of a longer pace and enjoys the cosmetic chase… a reasonably priced season pass which allows me to earn an aspirational chase cosmetic set would fit perfectly for someone in my place style needs… probably a longshot… but I thought I would say my peace and see what comes of it. Best wishes to everyone.
