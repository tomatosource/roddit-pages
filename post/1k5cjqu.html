Defensive discrepancies and imbalance between melee / ranged

I would like to give some feedback on *my take* and *my personal experience* on defensives so far

So what kind of defensives can we go for?

  1. Play ranged: as you optimize your build, mobs die before they get the chance to touch you, you avoid on death / ground effects and that is the most optimal defensive. It also evolves into optimal speed in farming since you don’t have to chase mobs around
  2. Play cold dmg: mobs are frozen and thus they can’t do dmg to you. Bosses are frozen EASILY and there’s no better defense than that. I play crossbow glacial bolt, everything is frozen or there is a ton of ice walls between me and my enemies, projectiles hit my walls and not me. Mobs can’t even reach me through those walls. I feel super super safe and i can jump over the walls if they are annoying me.
  3. Play high evasion: you can reach 70% evasion relatively easily. You can reach 85% with actual investment without much trouble, but it comes down to not using too many uniques. Evasion is good, however it’s not good enough. Eventually, you *will* get hit and you *will* die without other forms of mitigation. Moreover, it doesn’t work on the dangerous stuff and Acrobatics is nerfed SO hard it’s not worth picking - there’s almost no reason to even have it there to taunt you.
  4. Play armor: Armor has its limitations, you need a ton of recovery and again it does not protect from hard hitting mobs / bosses. Plus for some reason you get slower (even though you are strong enough to use the armor)!!!
  5. Play block: block is like evasion, maybe a bit better but still, you will eventually get hit and if you get hit and die once every 2 maps you can’t level past 92 or so.
  6. Play ES: well, you can get a ton of ES that will make you survive any hit. Much much higher than life. How do you recover it? It’s not as easy this season, but hey - *Eternal Youth* smiles at you!
  7. Combine ES and Evasion - that’s maybe the best combination, there are talents on the tree that mix those, Amazon also has the panther ascendancy point where you can use an ES chest. The downside is that you might need to invest on intelligence stat, but you get hit rarely and when you do, ES covers you. It will recharge but you don’t get because you don’t get hit often.

Of course there is more, but again, it’s *my take* from the builds that I have played.

Melees are not favored. Let’s say you play bleed.

You got to touch mobs, so you put yourself into harm’s way much more than ranged and it takes some time for mobs to die. You will get hit much more often and you can’t freeze since you don’t play cold.

It’s also super slower than just pounding mobs from afar when you want to clear a breach for instance and mobs are not close to eachother.

Life does not scale well, not well at all in comparison to ES and leech does not protect you vs one shots. There should be some normalization there, a way to increase life - it makes no sense to have ES talents on passive tree and not life talents. It’s totally skewed.

In my eyes, there should be an easy way to play fortify for melees. It’s more or less mandatory.

Speed should also be higher for melees - so a mandatory fortify supports needs to combine speed, dmg reduction, and dmg (so that it does not penalize us using it). Yes, that’s a MANDATORY support for all melees, but what else?

Alternatively, fortify from the rarer runes/talismans, NOT on weapon and fortify granting speed and dmg reduction for melees.

In my opinion, you need to die from being:

  • overwhelmed, like you take dmg, spam flask, but it’s not enough and you die after a few seconds - but you got time to react. Maybe your flask charged are over,
  • too slow to react in 2-3 seconds time, not through one-shots
  • you failed to avoid major boss abilities / mechanics

But again, eventually your goal is to kill stuff too fast, so your end-game build needs to be able to prevent one-shots through some reasonable investment..
