List of players to score 60+ points in a game

I found out there are only 39 players who have ever scored at least 60 points in a game and was kind of surprised by that. So I thought it would be fun to list everyone who has ever done it. Here’s the list, in order by the first time a player accomplished it.

Player, Season (Total number of 60 point games)

  1. Joe Fulks, 1949 (1)
  2. George Mikan, 1952 (1)
  3. Elgin Baylor, 1960 (3)
  4. Wilt Chamberlain, 1961 (32)
  5. Jerry West, 1962 (1)
  6. Rick Barry, 1974 (1)
  7. Pete Maravich, 1977 (1)
  8. George Gervin, 1978 (1)
  9. David Thompson, 1978 (1) - Gervin and Thompson did it on the same day, so I don’t know who actually did it first
  10. Bernard King, 1985 (1)
  11. Larry Bird, 1985 (1)
  12. Michael Jordan, 1987 (4)
  13. Karl Malone, 1990 (1)
  14. Tom Chambers, 1990 (1)
  15. David Robinson, 1994 (1)
  16. Shaquille O’Neal, 2000 (1)
  17. Tracy McGrady, 2004 (1)
  18. Allen Iverson, 2005 (1)
  19. Kobe Bryant, 2006 (6)
  20. Gilbert Arenas, 2007 (1)
  21. Carmelo Anthony, 2014 (1)
  22. LeBron James, 2014 (1)
  23. Klay Thompson, 2017 (1)
  24. Devin Booker, 2017 (2)
  25. James Harden, 2018 (4)
  26. Kemba Walker, 2019 (1)
  27. Damian Lillard, 2020 (5)
  28. Steph Curry, 2021 (2)
  29. Bradley Beal, 2021 (1)
  30. Jayson Tatum, 2021 (1)
  31. Karl-Anthony Towns, 2022 (2)
  32. Kyrie Irving, 2022 (1)
  33. Luka Doncic, 2023 (2)
  34. Donovan Mitchell, 2023 (1)
  35. Giannis Antetokounmpo, 2024 (1)
  36. Joel Embiid, 2024 (1)
  37. Jalen Brunson, 2024 (1)
  38. De’Aaron Fox, 2025 (1)
  39. Nikola Jokic, 2025 (1)

Elgin Baylor (1962) and Michael Jordan (1986) have the only 60+ point playoff games. Those aren’t included in the totals.

Kind of crazy this list was only at 22 players just a decade ago.

Edit: the season is the first season the player joined the “60-point game” club. The number in the parentheses is how many times they did it in their career. Sorry if that was confusing initially.
