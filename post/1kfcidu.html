Feedback: HCSSF Warrior

I’d love to hear other’s thoughts.

What do you think of the identified problems and my suggestions?

PS - I spent 45 minutes writing a very long, in-depth review breaking everything down, and right before I hit submit, my power went out and it was all lost (lesson learned, save drafts!). So I’m just going to write a shorter version here.

–

Good:

Warrior big aoe, chunky damage. Boneshatter feels good. Shockwave totem + Earthquake (EQ) feels good. Big perfect strike feels good. The core gameplay itself in poe2 feels great.

Recent artificer orb increase is nice. But I didn’t have the runes I needed, which leads to the bad of this experience.

Bad:

The game feels designed for SSF, balanced around trade. This felt really bad to play as every craft felt guaranteed to roll dogwater garbage mods. I picked up and disenchanted everything and still felt I didn’t have enough to craft anything useful. I constantly crafted, used everything I had all the time, and it felt bad because nothing useful came from it.

  • I tried selling everything for a bit to buy from vendors/gambling, but this left no currency (naturally). This also did not work to solve gear issues.
  • Yes I constantly checked vendors for upgrades.
  • I got almost 0 jewelry throughout normal, outside of the deterministic ones like in the graveyard/under Keth.
  • I used an act 1 maul into mid act 3 because I COULD NOT get anything better, despite trying my hardest.

Armour is too unpredictable for damage mitigation. It makes block feel mandatory.

Leap slam makes you eat damage as you jump in, then damage while there’s a delay when you land.

WOW do you move slowly with armour. The movespeed penalty on armour is awful.

EQ delay is too long, even with fast forward.

Rolling slam feels slow and clunky. Similar problem to leap slam with eating damage that is not mitigated by armour (because it doesn’t do diddly squat).

Earthshatter, at least when you only have 1 cry - which is the case when you unlock it, feels bad because I can’t explode it readily.

1h maces are not strong enough. Giant’s blood feels mandatory.

Most of the warrior skills did not feel useful.

When forced into high strength builds, weapon set passives do nothing. I hope this is fixed when more weapons are added to the game, but it felt like I was forced into maces and nothing else. My weapon swap was a shield for shield charge.

In boss fights, warrior feels forced to stand and eat damage to get anything off. I tried many strats, and the best was just stand there, instant flask through damage, while slamming perfect strikes.

Suggestions:

Address mod weighting to be more favourable for desired mods. Seriously. Crafting feels awful and punishing right now when it COULD be really fun and satisfying. I don’t know what the odds are, but it felt like a 1⁄50 chance of getting anything remotely useful, then an even smaller chance the next mod will be useful too.

Drop more transmutes and exalts in normal. More rares too for more regal shards. More runes. Let me craft more abundantly.

More jewelry drops in normal.

Armour should work like a physical version of elemental resistances until a better system is provided. Right now it’s just bad, but this feels like a better solution.

Strength over armour requirements should mitigate armour movement speed penalty. Maybe give a bit more life too. Why does big overly strong man get weighed down by puny armour?

Damage mitigation on leap slam animation would be nice, or allow us to jump over the projectiles (but that’s probably broken).

Buff 1h maces so I can use shields before giant’s blood.

The non-meta warrior skills are not used for a reason. They could use some love.

  • EQ delay should be made shorter + given more options for reducing further.
  • Would love if I could shield charge more. A little less cooldown.

--

That’s all I can remember for now, will update if I remember more :)
