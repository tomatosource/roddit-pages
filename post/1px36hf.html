Coaches always get the short end of the stick

This is partly inspired by a lot of Hawks fans wanting Quin Snyder fired. Every time Atlanta looks average, the first instinct is to blame the coach. It didn’t matter if it was Budenholzer, Lloyd Pierce, Nate McMillan, or now Quin Snyder, the results stayed roughly the same and the fan reaction never changed. When the outcome is identical across all those different coaches, that’s usually a sign the problem runs deeper than whoever’s on the sideline. At some point the conversation has to shift toward the roster construction, the front office, and the culture of the organization instead of just recycling head coaches.

Same thing with the Cavs and Kenny Atkinson. Last season fans loved him and he won COTY. Now a rough start and suddenly some of the fanbase wants him fired. He didn’t magically forget how to coach over the summer. Maybe look at the players who’ve underperformed all year, or the front office moves (like swapping Ty Jerome for a washed Lonzo Ball), before blaming the head coach.
