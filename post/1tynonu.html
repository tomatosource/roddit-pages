I highly encourage you to start a public party for group mapping with randoms

The last few leagues I’ve tried an experiment.

I don’t know anyone who plays PoE, but my builds always become too strong for solo mapping to be challenging. So I started creating open public parties that anyone could join. I use my own maps, juice them as much as I can, and anyone who can hang can run them with me.

I use permanent allocation (i.e. your drop = your loot) and disable level scaling (important or you’ll hit like a noodle). I say what I’m running, my region, and number of portals (1) in the group description. The groups fill almost immediately, and we just fucking go.

Now, I can’t say this is much more profitable than solo mapping. A group of randoms mostly have shitty builds, can’t hang, and die in 30 seconds with the player scaling. But those guys will leave and more people will join. The people who can hang will stick around and filter out the shitters. It’s also not good XP for leechers with level scaling disabled.

In this way I’ve built a friends list of chill people who like doing the same thing as me and have builds at about my power level. Now I can just form parties by asking my friends list.

It’s also a lot of fun to see other people’s builds. The meta ones, the shitty ones, the ones that turbo carry, and the ones that die to blue mobs. It’s also a lot of fun to just hear all the tinks, even if they’re not mine. And most of all, it’s just fun to engage socially in a thing I already love doing.
