Boss hitbox still active during Time Freeze

I just lost a level 59 HC Chronomancer to Siora, Blade of the Mists in a truly unexpected fashion. I hit her with Time Freeze while she was using her dash attack, and she ended up frozen pretty much on top of me. My character model must have been in contact with one of her blades or something, because even though she was frozen, the damage from the charge kept triggering over and over.

If I had been expecting this, I might have had the time to Temporal Rift out. But it was so bizarre and unexpected that my brain just froze for a couple seconds, and that was all it took for the hits to eat through my ES and Health.

Just wanted to offer a heads up so others don’t lose characters to something similar. Now I’m curious if there are other bosses that have “hot” hitbox animations even while frozen.
