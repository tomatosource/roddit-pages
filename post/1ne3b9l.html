Weapon swap into low life is possible

Hi all, not sure if anybody realized (probably but anyway worth sharing). When you weapon swap into a higher max life setting, you life is at the value of the lower weapon set intitally. That means with cowards legacy (high. Corrupt goe up to 92) and a few % life and or strength nodes (or life on shield, foci if used) you are initially on low life in your furst skill use after weapon swap. I am using it with a crossbow and execute 3 to gurantee the 30% more damage when shooting frag rounds against frozen enemies but there are for sure other usefull interactions. Happy building
