POE2 is amazing

So I’ll start off by saying I’m not a POE 1 fan. I played a little but never got hooked. I played a lot of D1 and D2, not a fan of D3 and disliked D4.

I gave POE2 a shot and man… this game is freaking amazing. The overall pacing of the game, the difficulty level, the itemization, the skills, the graphics and the atmosphere, I like almost everything about it. I’m not being showered with loot all the time and upgrades feel actually meaningful and rewarding.

Anyway I thought I would share. Amazing game. Hope the devs keeps in this direction and not lean toward the Diablo tendencies.
