The recent classes feel more fluid and their identities are stronger/emphasized

Sorry if the title is confusing, but my point is that the Druid ascendancy classes, Djin, Martial Artist, and Spiritwalker, all feel not only strong but also fluid and coherent. I don’t know if I’m the only person who feels this way.

The earlier classes, pre-0.4, are interesting, but it feels like they lack something that I cannot really put into words. They just feel rushed, and many of them seriously need an entire rework, whereas post-0.4 classes, including Druid, are strong but by no means unfair. They’re just better designed. The day-and-night contrast between MA and Invoker tells us a lot.

Do you think it’s because GGG has understood their game better as time goes by, and the designs of the later classes also improve along the way?
