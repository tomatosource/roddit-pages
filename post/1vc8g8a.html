Tsais Say Pending Divorce Won’t Impact Nets, Liberty Ownership

Src:https://www.sportico.com/business/team-sales/2026/joe-clara-tsai-divorce-nets-liberty-ownership-1234940715/

Joe Tsai and Clara Wu Tsai said Friday that their pending divorce won’t impact the ownership of their sports empire, which includes the NBA’s Brooklyn Nets, the WNBA’s New York Liberty and the economics of the Barclays Center.

Brooklyn Sports & Entertainment (BSE), the parent company, also includes the Nets’ G League affiliate, a training center and a handful of other real estate investments.

“Joe will remain chairman and Clara will remain vice chair of [BSE],” the couple said in a statement. “Joe will remain governor of the Brooklyn Nets and Clara will remain governor of the New York Liberty. They will continue to own and be stewards of the teams with professional management in place as they always have.”
