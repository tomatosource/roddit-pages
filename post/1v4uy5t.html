They really should add a training dummy

I’ve heard the argument, they want players to do what feels good. Valid.

Problem is, there are bugs, unclear interactions etc. I do want to play what feels and looks good. Thats the joy of an ARPG. You dont have to compete with others, you can create for the sake of creativity.

Unfortunately sometimes interactions for example from support runes are unclear, misleading or worse, bugged. With a training dummy we could see if you had in mind is actually working as intended. I and I bet others too derive great joy from that.

When there is no easy testing is becomes hard to test whats actually working and it starts to feel like you are driving a car with a toy steering wheel in the 2nd seat as in I have no idea if my decisions are actually impacting the game.

I started to really like this game but this is really bothering me. Hopefully the Devs will add this.
