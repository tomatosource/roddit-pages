[Stein] The Chicago Bulls are a team, alongside the New Orleans Pelicans, that has expressed interest in Bennedict Mathurin.

Source: https://open.substack.com/pub/marcstein/p/we-repeat-more-late-night-nba-intel?r=nuq3a&utm_medium=ios

Late Monday night I reported that the Pelicans have indeed renewed their in-season interest in then-Pacer Bennedict Mathurin by pursuing the LA Clippers’ swingman in restricted free agency.

And now for more on Mathurin:

The Stein Line on Tuesday learned that Chicago is Mathurin’s other current suitor.

You’ll recall that The Stein Line also revealed recently (July 23 to be exact) that New Orleans has been trying to shed Jordan Hawkins’ contract via trade to move further away from the first apron. Among the options available to the Pelicans, if they succeed in those efforts, would be opening up the use of a healthy portion of its midlevel exception to assemble an offer sheet to Mathurin.
