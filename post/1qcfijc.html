The Lakers have a record of 20-8 when Rui Hachimura plays this season and when he doesn’t play they have a record of 4-6.

The Lakers since acquiring Rui are 117-70 when Rui plays and 55-74 when he doesn’t play.

Very interesting to see how important he is to the Lakers success. Not saying he is the sole reason for their success but he is a high level role player and glue guy who Lakers fans love making out to be the scapegoat for when things go wrong.
