The Lakers’ league-leading free throw differential is adding to their enormous advantage since 2020

The Los Angeles Lakers are currently leading the league in free throw attempt differential for the 3rd time in the last 4 years, after finishing only 2nd in 2024-25.

This adds on to a towering lead the Lakers have over the rest of the league in the last 5 seasons, spanning multiple player and coach changes.

Over this timespan the Lakers have nearly double the free throw differential of the next closest team, the Milwaukee Bucks. And their differential is nearly 50% higher than the inverse of the last place team, the Golden State Warriors.
