Please rethink the nerfs to secondary effects like armour explosion!

With the update to patch notes giving things like Armour Explosion, Plague Burst, Quill Burst, etc., the ability to be used with cooldown support gems, they are most definitely getting an internal CD to limit the number of times they can occur.

I understand this was problematic in combination with Herald skills, but that was a BUG and they should not have infinitely chained Herald skills in the first place because Heralds should not reproc Heralds.

Secondary effects like Armour Explosion taking kill credit despite being caused by a Herald, and then chaining the Herald shouldn’t work that way and made AOE clear OP in some cases, but Armour Explosion on its own was necessary on other builds like on most Warbringer and many melee Titan setups, that weren’t even using the bugged interaction.

This kills the clearing speeds of what were already really slow builds and makes it much more difficult to map without. If this is the direction we are going, all it does is make the difference between the slowest of builds and the fastest builds even bigger.

This is a HUGE nerf to many Warrior builds which were already struggling. I’ve played Warrior since 0.1, and this would be the final nail in the coffin for me to just play a Pathfinder.
