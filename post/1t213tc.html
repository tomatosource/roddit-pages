The Lakers won the series against the Rockets without any individual player scoring 30 points or more in any game

Their highest scorer in any game was LeBron James with 29 in G3, followed by LeBron James with 28 in G2 and G6, then Luke Kennard with 27 in G1. The Rockets also only had one player who scored above 30 points: Alperen Sengun with 33, also in G3. So the only person who broke 30 points in a game was Sengun and he did it in OT.

I just found this pretty interesting because this has got to be pretty rare in today’s game. I can’t find the stats for it, but I can’t think off the top of my head a won series with your highest individual scorer never touching 30. I expected LeBron to break 30 at some point but he never did.
