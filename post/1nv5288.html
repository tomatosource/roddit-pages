How are Hollow Palm monk players faring after this update?

For context I play a Hollow Palm Ice Strike build and before the new update I was able to clear juiced up t16 maps with no issues, basically never dying or in real danger of dying, and I went from that to getting one shot by random mobs and leaving me confused of how is that even possible.

Playing a build with Chaos inoculation, now I have to run away every time I take a bit of damage to my ES (until it recharges) as I’m afraid of being one shotted again. During the few maps I played I was very close to dying and just barely made it alive playing very very defensively.

On top of that it feels like my DPS went to shit, and the whole build feels clunky now. I have issues with mana leech that I didn’t have before, I’m constantly out of mana. Everything feels very very clunky and definitely not fun.

As a bonus, as this is not monk related, I feel that while the Tower meta was annoying, at least then I would set the towers up and just focus on mapping. Now I have to make a decision every time i do a new map, on what waystone to choose which is perfectly fine, but I also need to think with is the perfect tablet combo to go with it and the preexisting attributes on the node. And that is tiring to me, it’s not fun. I don’t think that this is the change to fix the mapping issues, but it’s a good try (and I think it’s a good thing they experiment with this until they get it right).

All in all I think this might be a sign to leave and wait for the next league as I had my fun, but I am curious if other monk players were affected the same.
