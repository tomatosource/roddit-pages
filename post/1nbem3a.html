Spectres need way more time in the oven

TL;DR: Spectres in PoE2 feel pre-nerfed at every turn (with some exceptions): tiny pool of viable choices, punishing Spirit costs, gutted aura synergy, and wonder-bread scaling. If GGG wants spectres to thrive, they need lower costs, clearer scaling, and systems that reward building a “team,” not just picking the one or two that dominate.

I’ve been deep in spectres this patch (Frozen Mandibles enjoyer), and it really feels like GGG is terrified of letting spectres be good. Every time a combo looks promising, it’s either pre-emptively toned down or quietly constrained. The result: we’re basically forced into 1 of 3 functional options, and even those require awkward compromises other archetypes don’t.

• Spirit costs choke variety So many spectres have premium Spirit costs that don’t match their output or utility. A simple pass that reduces Spirit on most spectres (especially utility/auras/ranged) would unlock builds without power-creeping the game.

• Aura removal kneecaps the archetype’s identity Half the fantasy is assembling monsters that buff and play off each other. With spectre auras removed or neutered, we lost a whole layer of squad-building. Bring back spectre auras (even at lower base effect) and make them interact with Presence, Muster, and support categories so there’s a real reason to mix types.

• Blind scaling = can’t learn = I’m trash We still can’t reliably calculate what makes a given spectre strong: DoT vs. hit conversion, innate ailment values, internal cooldowns, aura magnitudes, etc. Expose key stats in-game (or on gem tooltips) so players can actually iterate instead of guessing.

• AI whiplash Minions swing from laser-focused to brain-AFK: idle stalling, random target swaps, wandering out of Presence. Tighten target priority, improve leashing around the player, and/or bake in Presence-aware positioning so uptime isn’t a coin flip.

• Reward the “team comp” fantasy Add lightweight synergy tags (Commander, Reaver, Elementalist, Warden, etc.) that grant small bonuses when you field complementary tags. Give us multiple viable roles (ranged clear, aura support, single-target burst, CC) with at least 2–3 competitive spectres in each.

Right now spectres feel balanced around fear of what might happen rather than what is happening. We don’t need broken, just options. Muster is a step in the right direction on where spectres should go…team comps combining similar tags, possibly auras or other synergies.

*PS: Game still gud but will be better
