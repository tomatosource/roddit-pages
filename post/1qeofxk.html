Michael Jordan joining the NBA On NBC as a “special contributor” helped launch the network’s highly anticipated relaunch of broadcasting games during the 2025-26 season.

But many fans are wondering if NBC will have an additional one-on-one sitdown between Jordan and NBC Sports’ top play-by-play announcer, Mike Tirico, for the “MJ: Insights To Excellence” series.

There was just one long-form interview with Jordan recorded during the Ryder Cup in September, and NBC aired three truncated segments from that same interview during halftime of multiple games.

“Nothing scheduled as of now that I know of, but it is a possibility,“ Tirico said during his appearance on the S.I. Media With Jimmy Traina podcast this week.  ”I hope there’s something else after it. Michael was awesome.”

Tirico was asked about Jordan’s vaguely titled role as a special contributor for NBA On NBC and if there’s more to come with the Hall of Famer.

“Was it what everyone wanted? Probably not,” Tirico admitted of Jordan’s special contributor role. “Was it better than not hearing from Michael Jordan? You’re damn right it was. And if we get another shot at it, will I be more than excited to be a part of it? You bet ya.

“In our world right now, all you want to do is make the final answer before you know everything. I love that Michael trusted us enough to sit and do something that he hasn’t done in a long, long, long time,” Tirico added.

Back in May 2025, Jordan said in a press release that he was “excited to see the NBA back on NBC” as a special contributor, adding, “the NBA On NBC was a meaningful part of my career.”
