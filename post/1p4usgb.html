Favorite Baseball TV Episode?

It seems most long running American TV shows have at least one episode where the plot is “it’s a baseball episode.” No doubt countless examples.

I’m going to have to go with “The Gang Beats Boggs” from *It’s Always Sunny In Philadelphia”. Some of the most ridiculous moments of that show come from that episode. All the characters are at their worst here, spurred on by their love of baseball- just like going to an actual MLB game. The actual playing of baseball in the episode is minimal, it is a loving tribute to one of the most incredible feats ever pulled off by a professional athlete.Rest In Peace Boss Hogg

The obvious (no pun intended) softball answer involves a certain animated family and a helluva lot of guest stars, I’ll let someone else in the comments claim that one.
