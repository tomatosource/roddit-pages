🇯🇵 NPB’s Hiromi Itoh of the Nippon-Ham Fighters has won the Sawamura Award, the Japanese equivalent of the Cy Young Award. Itoh met three of the seven criteria for the Sawamura Award — recording 195 strikeouts (150 required), making 27 starts (25), and posting a .636 winning percentage (min .600).
