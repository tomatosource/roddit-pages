Tried POE1 after 1000 hours of POE2 while waiting for 0.3 patch and my personal hopes.

After playing over a thousand hours in POE2 and finally trying POE1, the difference is huge. And it’s not just “POE1 is better because there’s more content” - the games feel very different.

UI & UX

  • POE2’s interface is cleaner and more intuitive, even in alpha.
  • Tooltips and explanations are everywhere - hover a keyword with Alt to instantly see what it does.

What can be done for both POE1 and POE2? Would be nice to see tooltips in POE1. Both games should show detailed info about class + ascendancy options during character creation.

Combat feel

  • POE2 combat feels more meaningful: positioning, timing, and skill combos (till endgame) matter. Now I see what GGG are talking about. Combat in POE2 is so much better.
  • POE1 often boils down to hit-and-run or hold one button until the screen is empty.
  • POE1 lets you spam movements tech endlessly, dash, quicksilver flask, etc.. while POE2 has longer cooldowns or lacking that movement tech at all, making movement choices more deliberate. My hope for POE2 0.3 Hopes: more movement options but with cooldowns to keep combat thoughtful.
  • Imagine WASD movement in POE1. So much better.

Progression differences

  • In POE2 (current 0.2 patch), getting STR/DEX/INT or extra jewel sockets on the passive tree is much harder. Some skill gems should nerf their mana/stat requirement, like “cast on X” or high lvl skill gems (like arc/spark) when u have +level 5 levels here, +3 here, +2 here….. Also heralds on spells in POE1 is an option to get a power spike. U know, there is more flexibility in everything and I like it in POE1. Some mechanics should also be available for spells, not only for attacks. Would like to see at least more jewel sockets like it was in 0.1 patch (is anyone using adorned diamond rn?). Hope for 0.3 patch, can’t say anything more. Stats also easier in POE1. Sometimes there is like +40 to stat and + 8% increased stat at the same moment, wow, and so easy to reach. Nodes like +10% increased mana and multiples of them. Small stat nodes are +10, not +5.
  • In POE1 sometimes it’s hard to change rare, cause it’s 4-link for example, and u have a much better gear, but it’s 3-link or wrong colors, I don’t know. This feels so bad compared to POE2 skill gems.
  • POE1 showers you with powerful low-level uniques, while POE2 uniques feels garbage. Examples I found and used within a first 3 acts as my first playthrough in this game (lvl32 character and my blind ramdom build feels stonger than most of poe2 builds):
    • Gloves that give random power/endurance/frenzy charges on kill. (Also there is charges nodes in passive tree that are doing the same. It’s so much easier to get charges in POE1, no need to “just do a combo”, parry, jump, then use something. Or hit until low, then use this, to get 1 charge, lol)
    • Boots that double my damage while any flask is active (it just give me additional chaos damage, don’t remember how much, but stat’s shows my dps is almost doubles). Also they have +20% movespeed and other bonuses.
    • Belt with a permanent 50 chaos-damage aura.
    • Gloves with minus small amount of cast speed but +100% spell damage. (din’t used that one, cause not a caster build, just found it and it looks strong)
    • And many more…

In POE1, even low-level uniques can make your build explode in power instantly in campaign, which is fun but can trivialize combat. In POE2, uniques are more situational, so power spikes require more planning and synergy. Like this new infinite poison low lvl unique spear, it requires ton of dmg on rings, unqiue gloves, unique belt from king of the mist, 4-5 socket skill gems and ton of spirit to do any dmg with it. And I can use only spear skills. Low-tier uniques should be a bit better in POE2. That my hope for 0.3 patch.

Mechanics & economy

  • POE1 has tons of overlapping or outdated mechanics that feel redundant.
  • The variety and volume of currency in POE1 is overwhelming compared to POE2’s system.
  • POE2 needs some currency sink, to avoid 2500 exalted to 1 div situation for longer.
  • Many T0 uniques in POE2 cost nothing now.

POE2 feels tighter and more deliberate in almost every aspect, while POE1 gives you absurd amounts of power and flexibility very early on. Both approaches have their charm - POE1’s chaos is fun, but POE2’s pacing makes each decision feel more impactful for me.

My hopes for POE2 0.3 Patch

  1. Bring back more build variety - Stat stacking, Temporalis, Spark Mage, minions… right now in 0.2 the meta feels narrow (LS) and a lot of iconic 0.1 archetypes are dead. That impacts the economy, cause everyone is chasing the same items.
  2. Rework or buff pinnacle/delirium/other encounters??? uniques - drops from Arbiter, Trialmaster, Olroth, Delirium encounters should all feel meaningful. If it’s a pinnacle boss unique, it should always be worth building around. It’s kinda bad design when boss always drops unique, but it’s trash.
  3. Un-nerf iconic items – Items like HoWA, Pillar of the Caged God and Temporalis were nerfed to much in 0.2.
  4. Nefr Lighting Spear and buff other gems. But don’t overneft it, don’t make it unplayable like it was with everything in 0.2.
  5. As I said before - more jewel sockets in passive tree.
  6. Some rework of passive tree, cause a lot of nodes are just garbage and can’t be used in any build, so people are just using the same nodes in every build.
  7. Better Evasion, Armor, HP, Manna defence, cause Energy shield is so much better than everything else.

Thanks for attention.
