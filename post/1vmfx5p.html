Statistical Arbitrage Trading on the Currency Market

Goooooood day everyone,

Long time lurker, first time poster.

I am a big fan of PoE, been playing since I got my first PC at like age 12.

Recently (past 3 years) have really gotten into software dev and want to work in fintech in the future.

I decided that a cool project might be to try and run a small amount of finance math on the currency market and see if I can make some decent currency gains.

It works and I have made good trades but I am still kinda unsure about my math, however, this is something that I think some of the nerds here might find it slightly interesting.

It pulls hourly data from the poe2scout API (GGG emailed me that they have a currency API released probably a day after I finally deployed this) and sends images to discord every few hours with currency analytics.

Feel free to test it yourself and ask any questions

https://github.com/F0RG-2142/kingsmarch-koinery

Edit: not a service or website being promoted, just a repo you can clone yourself and play around with
