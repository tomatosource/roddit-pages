NBA MVP Celebrations Ranked

The premise is simple. I’m ranking every NBA MVP since 2000 by their performance in the game where they receive the trophy. I split the list into tiers, placing heavy priority on victory. After all, these nights should be a tremendous memory for the MVP, the team, and their fans, and the game result shouldn’t ruin it.

TIER ONE: MASTERPIECES

  1. Iverson 2001: 52 PTS, 7 AST, 4 STL, 1 TOV. 79 TS%, 45.3 GmSc, +30, W in ECSF G5 (TOR)

  2. Jokic 2024: 40 PTS, 7 REB, 13 AST, 2 STL, 0 TOV. 77 TS%, 43.3 GmSc, +21, W in WCSF G5 (MIN)

TIER TWO: BRILLIANT

  1. Shaq 2000: 38 PTS, 20 REB, 5 BLK. 56 TS%, 34.6 GmSc, +3, W in WCSF G2 (PHX)

  2. LeBron 2009: 34 PTS, 10 REB, 4 STL. 71 TS%, 31.7 GmSc, +13, W in ECSF G1 (ATL)

  3. Nash 2006: 31 PTS, 12 AST, 4 TOV. 82 TS%, 29.0 GmSc, +14, W in WCSF G1 (LAC)

  4. SGA 2025: 38 PTS, 8 AST, 3 STL, 1 TOV. 69 TS%, 33.8 GmSc, +25, W in WCF G2 (MIN)

  5. Kobe 2008: 34 PTS, 8 REB, 6 AST, 5 TOV. 73 TS%, 26.4 GmSc, +18, W in WCSF G2 (UTA)

  6. LeBron 2012: 32 PTS, 15 REB, 5 AST, 2 STL, 1 TOV. 53 TS%, 29.5 GmSc, +12, W in ECSF G1 (IND)

  7. Durant 2014: 32 PTS, 12 REB, 9 AST. 60 TS%, 27.3 GmSc, +14, W in WCSF G2 (LAC)

  8. Curry 2016: 29 PTS, 5 REB, 11 AST. 67 TS%, 24.4 GmSc, +10, W in WCSF G5 (POR, closeout)

TIER THREE: SOLID IN VICTORY

  1. Duncan 2003: 28 PTS, 8 REB, 7 AST, 3 BLK. 50 TS%, 19.8 GmSc, +7, W in WCSF G1 (LAL)

  2. Nash 2005: 11 PTS, 6 REB, 13 AST. 51 TS%, 11.3 GmSc, +21, W in WCSF G1 (DAL)

  3. Rose 2011: 25 PTS, 6 REB, 10 AST, 2 BLK, 8 TOV. 42 TS%, 11.5 GmSc, +14, W in ECSF G2 (ATL)

TIER FOUR: EXCELLENT IN DEFEAT

  1. Jokic 2021: 32 PTS, 20 REB, 10 AST, 2 TOV. 48 TS%, 30.2 GmSc, -10, L in WCSF G3 (PHX)

  2. Embiid 2023: 30 PTS, 13 REB, 4 BLK. 62 TS%, 27.5 GmSc, -8, L in ECSF G3 (BOS)

TIER FIVE: GOOD GAMES SPOILED

  1. LeBron 2013: 24 PTS, 8 REB, 7 AST, 2 STL. 57 TS%, 22.5 GmSc, -15, L in ECSF G1 (CHI)

  2. Duncan 2002: 28 PTS, 12 REB, 3 AST. 46 TS%, 18.4 GmSc, -10, L in WCSF G3 (LAL)

  3. LeBron 2010: 24 PTS, 7 REB, 3 STL, 2 BLK, 5 TOV. 56 TS%, 18.2 GmSc, -16, L in ECSF G2 (BOS)

TIER SIX: CLUNKERS

  1. Curry 2015: 19 PTS, 5 REB, 6 AST. 46 TS%, 10.8 GmSc, -7, L in WCSF G2 (MEM)

  2. Garnett 2004: 16 PTS, 18 REB, 7 AST, 6 TOV. 35 TS%, 10.6 GmSc, -12, L in WCSF G1 (SAC)

TIER SEVEN: NBA AWARDS CEREMONY (after the playoffs)

  1. Westbrook 2017, Harden 2018, Giannis 2019

TIER EIGHT: ELIMINATED, OUT OF THE COUNTRY

  1. Jokic 2022 (at a stable in Serbia), Giannis 2020 (at home in Greece)

TIER NINE: HUMILIATION

  1. Dirk 2007: press conference in Dallas

All stats are from Basketball Reference. I used Getty Images to track down the presentation dates. MVPs went 13-7 when they had a game.
