Retired NBA star Carlos Boozer to star in Bravo dating show ‘Kings Court’. Per the show press release, Boozer says “I have met so many women who will recite my stats — like, ‘You averaged 21 points a game last year!’ But I want somebody that gets to know me.”

Show details

The show serves as a spin-off of the Peacock original series Queens Court, and will follow three celebrity kings — supermodel Tyson Beckford, NBA All-Star Carlos Boozer, and WWE legend Thaddeus “Titus O’Neil” Bullard — as they attempt to find “the one,” aka their queen.

Adds Carlos, “I have met so many women who will recite my stats — like, ‘You averaged 21 points a game last year!’ But I want somebody that gets to know me.”

Kings Court premieres Sunday, July 13 at 9 p.m. ET/PT on Bravo. Episodes will stream next day on Peacock.
