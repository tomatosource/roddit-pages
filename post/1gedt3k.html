[OC] NBA Week 1: Winners & Losers

The first week of the NBA season is in the books and I’m here to overreact over a 2-3 game sample size. Let’s jump in and see some of the biggest winners and losers throughout the first week.

​

Winner: The Memphis Grizzlies Bench

After what was seen as a lost season last year due to injuries, the Grizzlies have bounced back and seem to have found some quality rotation players. Scotty Pippen Jr. has emerged after getting ample playing time last season and is now contributing quality minutes. Jay Huff & John Konchar are hitting 3’s at an absurd rate, and Santi Aldama has filled in admirably for Jaren Jackson Jr. who returned after missing 2 games to start the season. They’re also still missing players like Luke Kennard & GG Jackson. If the Grizzlies bench can keep this up and the starters can stay healthy, the Grizzlies will be very competitive in the West this year.

​

Loser: Denver Nuggets

The rerun of the experiment where a team replaces Kentavious Caldwell Pope with Russell Westbrook has gotten off to a shaky start. The 3PT shooting has taken a huge dip in efficiency which is troublesome especially when you have a generational playmaker that creates open shots for his teammates. Michael Porter Jr. & Jamal Murray both haven’t been playing well. Christian Braun, a role player that they needed to take the next step, has not done enough to offset the losses they’ve sustained over the past two seasons (Bruce Brown & KCP). Who could have seen this coming?

​

Winner: Jordan Poole

Jordan Poole is….good again? Poole has looked like the Wizards best player so far this season, even if the competition for that title isn’t exactly competitive. He’s averaging 20 points per game, shooting 50% from the field, 55% from 3, and averaging 4 steals a game. Seems like the Poole party is back, even if it’s being held in a swamp where Kyle Kuzma lurks in the shadows.

​

Loser: The Washington Wizards (and their fans)

Watching basketball being played like this is borderline inhumane.

​

Winner: JJ Redick

JJ Redick has the Lakers 3-0 to start the season in what feels like forever. The Lakers won their first home opener since 2016, and Redick hasn’t put his hands in his pockets once this season. Laker fans rejoice, your next generation Pat Riley is here (until he loses). Jokes aside, Redick has Anthony Davis playing at an MVP level while Lebron is quietly averaging his lowest points per game (23ppg) since his rookie season. The Lakers have pulled off quality wins against 3 top-tier opponents (Minnesota, Phoenix, & Sacramento).The biggest question might now be how D’Angelo Russell fits in all of this.

​

Loser: Tobias Harris

Tobias Harris was brought in to be the second option and help relieve some of the scoring burden that franchise player Cade Cunningham carried with him on a game to game basis last year. So far, the results have been…disappointing to say the least. Tobias has been inefficient from the field and ineffective everywhere else. (10.3 ppg, 33% FG, 21% 3PT FG) The Pistons have been in every game this season, and one could argue that if Tobias played just a tad better they would already have their first win of the season (if not more).

​

Winner: Brooklyn Nets & Jordi Fernandez

I don’t know if the Brooklyn Nets are a good basketball team, but they are certainly competitive. Jordi Fernandez has his team playing hard, and that’s the least you could ask for from a team that has essentially shipped out all of their talent in an attempt to rebuild. The Nets were floated as potentially the worst team in the league this season but they haven’t looked the part, even knocking off the Bucks for their first win of the season. Ben Simmons however, is still Ben Simmons.

​

Loser: Tyrese Haliburton

The Indiana Pacers may need to issue a missing persons report for Tyrese Haliburton, or figure out how they ended up with his less skilled doppelganger. After making it to the Eastern Conference Finals, winning a gold medal in Paris, and getting rewarded with a huge contract, Haliburton is playing like he’s on a hangover. Through 3 games, he’s shooting 31% from the field, 20% from 3, averaging only 3.7 assists per game (after leading the league last year).

​

Winner: Los Angeles Clippers

The Clippers new arena is absolutely stunning. The Wall is proving to be a huge distraction for opposing teams at the free throw line. (Opponents are shooting 73.8% from the FT line) James Harden & company are competitive even without Kawhi Leonard, and the Clippers are 2-1 so far this season with big wins against the Nuggets and the Golden State New Orleans Saints. Zubac, who used to be worth 1 Mike Muscalas is a 20⁄10 player playing with James Harden this year. All eyes are now on the status of Kawhi’s knees. If Kawhi can come back, this team might be a contender.

​

Loser: The Philadelphia 76ers

The 76ers are not a good basketball team without Paul George and Joel Embiid. Tyrese Maxey through the first week is being asked to do everything for this team, and it’s come with mixed results. The Sixers desperately need one of their two stars back to shoulder the load, because you can’t expect Maxey to be playing 43 minutes per game for the entire season. They’ve looked pedestrian against the Bucks and the Raptors, and managed to eke out a win against a Pacers team that’s performing well below their ceiling. Not only that, Embiid came out and stated he won’t be playing back to backs anymore which has also prompted an investigation by the NBA.

​

Loser: The Milwaukee Bucks

Are the Milwaukee Bucks cooked? After winning their first game against an undermanned Sixers team, they’ve proceeded to lose to the Bulls and the Nets by double digits. Doc Rivers has gone 19-22 after taking over a 31-14 team last year. Khris Middleton has started the season hurt again, and players like Brook Lopez, Pat Connaughton, and Taurean Prince have been hot and cold. They’re also underperforming on defense, as they’re in the bottom 10 in the NBA in DRTG so far. While Damian Lillard and Giannis are still a deadly combo on offense, everything else around them has been questionable.
