LeBron James is 16 points away from becoming the 10th all-time leading scorer for the Lakers, making him the only player in NBA history to crack the top 10 for three different teams.

After scoring 24 points last night against Utah, LeBron now has 10,585 points as a Laker, ranking 11th on the franchise’s all-time scoring list.
Jamaal Wilkes sits at 10th with 10,601 total points—just 16 points ahead.

By breaking into the top 10, LeBron will become the only player in NBA history to rank in the top 10 all-time scorers for three different teams:

  • Cleveland Cavaliers 23,119 (#1)
  • Miami Heat 7,919 (#7)
  • Los Angeles Lakers
