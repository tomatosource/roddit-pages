Worst I’ve felt since Lewis died

The Hayward injury sucked but it wasn’t really clear how serious that team’s title chances were.

This team was a legitimate title contender with a real shot of winning this year and/or next. In one game, that ended.

I was just a young kid when Reggie Lewis died but it was obviously devastating. This might be the worst day in Celtic history since then.

Tatum missing all of next season when there had been a contender around him - devastating. Unclear if he will ever return to being a top 5 player.

We head into an offseason of major uncertainty. With no real hope of contending, Horford might retire. Unlikely we hold onto aging Jrue and constantly injured Zingis until 2026-27 when Tatum might return. Even Browns future might be in jeopardy given our lotto prospects next season.

It’s terrible. And that doesn’t even touch on how this team’s failure and 2nd round exit to the Knicks impacts the legacy of their championship.

Legit in the ranking for worst days in Celtic history. Call me hyperbolic or emotional, but it’s clear that’s the last game we will ever see of that team play together. I loved them and they will nevet get the credit for being as great as they were.
