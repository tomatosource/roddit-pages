Try a controller if you find PoE2 more tiring than PoE1

PoE1 player from near the beginning here. I tried out PoE2 when the early access started and as I’ve seen others mention several times, I found it tiring to have to be focused on reacting all the time. I sat the game aside until this patch. I have been following the patches since then and decided to try again. I had played a bit with a controller (I prefer PS5 Dual Sense) on Settlers league on PoE1 and I noticed it was a lot more relaxed. I decided to go controller on PoE2 for 0.3 and it has made a huge difference for me. I wouldn’t recommend it for trading, crafting, etc. But when you’re blasting the campaign or maps, give it a go. It takes some getting use to, but for me it cut down on the fatigue a lot.
