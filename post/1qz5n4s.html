Yankees - 41 Red Sox - 12 Dodgers - 6 Giants - 5 Tigers - 4 Padres - 3 Mets - 3 Cubs - 3 A’s - 2 Braves - 2 Blue jays - 2 White Sox - 2 Reds - 1
