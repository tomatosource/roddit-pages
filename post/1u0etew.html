Playing Bloodmage has become pure torture

For those unaware, after bloodmage was dominating the meta in 0.4 GGG has heavily nerfed the leech available to bloodmage. This has resulted in the ascendancy feeling truly awful to play. This is not to say that reaching endgame or „beating“ the game isn’t possible anymore, but the ascendancy, whose power fantasy is leeching and sustaining yourself, has turned into a life flask spam fiesta and is straight up locked out of certain endgame content now.

Even after taking all (life) cost efficiency augments/passives available your leech cannot possibly sustain ur life cost - and I’m not talking about spamming 100 comets per second. As a vampire-esque class you end up not leeching your enemies‘ life force but instead are required to run around/hug your enemies so you can lick up the blood puddles they leave behind on the ground - which even with ridiculous investment into some of the remnant nodes on the passive tree cannot sustain your life cost either in some cases.

And the worst of it all: It is literally almost impossible to run certain late game content without being constantly OOM (OOL?). For some reason GGG has put insane leech resistances on high monster effectiveness, which in turn means that you can cast one spell every 2 seconds in party play or when doing juiced abyss. And god forbid you roll „less life recovery“ (which is also an item rarity mod and thus desirable) on any of your maps! Have fun spending 20 seconds to kill one rare because GGG thought it’d be cool to disable the mana regeneration of an entire ascendancy with one single map mod.

I understand that instant leech was problematic to some degree as it allowed for seemingly „invincible“ builds, but there is no way that there’s no middle ground between invincibility and totally bricked „mana“ regeneration in half of the end game content. I really don’t understand why the ascendancy which inherently doubles the cost of your spells and is dependent on stacking spell levels as one of the only ways of damage scaling *also* gets hit with so many different mechanics which obliterate your life recovery down to a minimum. On the other hand any attack builds have a convenient way of slapping a mana leech mod on a ring and can call it a day.

To repeat, this isn’t about bloodmage being strong or weak, but rather about the QOL that comes with the ascendancy. The obstacles the game puts in your way regarding life recovery are out of proportion to what the ascendancy has to offer and the game does not give you any options to overcome them besides „just“ avoiding juicing/party play/less recovery mod on maps. This was honestly an issue in 0.3 and 0.4 as well - for example taking the „more effectiveness per abyss pit closed“ mod on tablets was a death sentence to bloodmages even back then - but now even normal mapping feels awful.

I hope GGG will not forget to take another look at the ascendancy in the next league and make some fundamental changes to leech resistance and leech in general. I don’t wish to be immortal or not having to care about spell cost at all, but being forced to flask spam and not being able to play with friends on a 500d build is ridiculous and sad.
