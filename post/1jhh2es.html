Should Coach K.C Jones Be Inducted Into The Hall of Fame?

I recently heard someone point out K.C Jones is the only head coach after 1970 to win multiple championships, and hasn’t been inducted in the Basketball Hall of Fame. His time in Washington and Boston being his most successful stints.

A 155-91 record in Washington, and a Finals appearance. Then a 308-102 mark in Boston, two championships, 4 finals appearances, and 5 conference finals appearances.

Coached probably the greatest team in league history in the ‘86 team. Beat the Lakers.

To put into context for K.C, here are the other nine coaches who’ve won multiple championships since 1970:

Red Holzman

Tommy Heihnson

Pat Riley

K.C Jones

Chuck Daly

Phil Jackson

Rudy Tomjanovich

Greg Popovich

Erik Spolstra

Steve Kerr
