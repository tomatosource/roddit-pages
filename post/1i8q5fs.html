Coming into this game, Jokic was 0-9 on “heaves,” which is the only reason his 3-point percentage was below 50%.

(ICYMI, here’s the shot)

The rest of the top 10 scorers in the league have combined for 10 attempts on “heaves.” Superstars (and players in general) are super-reluctant to take these shots, it’s not uncommon at all for a top scorer to go a full season without taking one. Steph Curry is one of the few who does; he’s 5-106 on them in his career, and his career-high for a season is 18, which I think is the current high mark for any player since they started tracking these shots. (Sadly, you have to check player pages individually to find this data.)

If he hadn’t shot any “heaves” this season, his 3-point percentage would be a league-leading 50.3%. Neither Sabonis nor Luke Kennard, the only players currently shooting better than Jokic from 3-point range this season, have attempted a “heave” yet this year.

This is Jokic’s first make on a “heave” – before this game, he was 0-37 on those shots.
