Brad Stevens & Jaylen Brown figuring out what an appropriate salary for Brown is?

A “Make this make sense” random thought:

When two people are trying to set a price for an object, it is often useful to look to third-party sources for pricing guidance. A house or car are obvious examples, and people also do this when negotiating salaries.

In 2023, Brown was eligible for an extension (it became a supermax extension when he was named to the All-NBA 2nd team in 2023).

In 2026, Brown will be eligible for a two-year extension on his supermax contract.

Perhaps the conversation is

  • Stevens: Should we pay you as a ‘Number 1 Option’ when we have another ‘Number 1 Option’ on the roster?
  • Brown: I’m worth it.
  • Stevens: Can we test that in the market?
  • Brown: Sure, check on Indeed and ZipRecruiter. 😂
  • Stevens: They don’t go into the hundreds of million range. I’ll see what the trade options are.

I know, wishful thinking, But it makes more sense than the Celtics trying to trade him even after Giannis got traded.
