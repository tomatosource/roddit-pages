A Short New Years Eve Story

New Years Eve, me and a buddy were playing, just cruising around some maps. We are casuals of PoE. We can smash T16 maps but will never have 100s of divines in a league or have the rarest top end gear.

Just before midnight, we were talking about how nice it would be to have a Headhunter and yada yada.

I said “F#$k it, how much are twilight reliquary keys? I saw a guy pull one from it before.” obviously being sarcastic, knowing I will never get as lucky. “Might as well get some dupes and chance shards”

2EX each, so I bought 50 of them.

Just after midnight now…

We start joking about how I should make a YouTube video “ I did 50 Twilights so you didn’t have to” (I don’t make vids) lol

First pull…crude bow…second pull…crude bow

5 more pulls go by and just more duplicates

and then finally, after praying to sweet Baby RNJesus……Headhunter!!

Proof or no proof, I have the first Headhunter out of a Twilight box in 2026 lol hooray me

My greatest moment of PoE so far, so just wanted to share. Would’ve been an all time clip if I could’ve recorded it.
