I want to make a build, not assemble yours

Let’s take Wolf Form – Lunar Assault as an example.

On its own, this is a frontal slashing ability that does cold damage and has a high chance to freeze. Mechanically it feels great, heck, its amazing actually. So what’s the issue? It’s a skill designed to be part of the wolf kit and not designed to be a skill in the overall game.

The entire wolf kit clearly works well together, but it was designed with that intent, and not with the intent to be part of the game as a whole.

So here’s a thought, the skill should simply be a frontal slash, period. That’s it. And I, the player, should decide if I want it to do Cold damage, lightning damage, poison damage or even better… Bleed damage.

This skill would actually be sick if I wanted to try and make a wolf bleed build, but it clearly shows it wasn’t built with that intention.

And there are more examples of this, wolfs autoattacks are designed to create small ice shards when hitting frozen enemies. This could be a support gem and be socketed and expanded so that we, the players, can explore your game.

The whole point of this game is for me to decide if I want to be a dragon pumping out ice breath and transforming into a dual wielding gnome that farts rainbows.

I don’t want to play your factory build. I want to build my own.
