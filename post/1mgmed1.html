Different enemy types feeling too similar

When playing the game, I just noticed that a lof of enemy types feel very similar. They do have different 3d models and animations. And maybe they also behave a little differently. But the way you deal with a lot of them is basically the same. With my Ranger i just used my AoE skills, lightning, or poison, and try to run away if enemies come too close. It does feel like a lot of wasted potential that different enemies feel the same as the way you deal with them is the same.

What do you think? Should enemies require more different responses from players? Or would that make things too complicated and it’s fine to have many enemies that you deal with in the same way, and just 3d model and animation variety is enough to keep things feeling different?

Of course there are some enemy types that feel different because they require a different response, e.g. range attacks, AoE spells, etc. It just seems like there are a lot of enemy types that are a basic zombie moving towards you, trying to melee attack you.
