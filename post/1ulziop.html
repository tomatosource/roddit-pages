Jaylen Brown comments since his trade on Twitch

Updating as this goes- Stream completed 8:55 PM EST

Says of Boston, “they packed me up” (…) pack your bags, Sayonara buddy” (re-hashed this multiple times in the interview, at least 5+ times)

He’s still processing the trade and how everything went down, just rehashed/repeated some of the comments in his statement on Instagram/twitter

Says he’ll take The connection he has to the fans with him, and it definitely feels like a heartbreak

To the people of Boston- thank you, to the community- I love you

As one chapter closes, another begins - grateful for opportunity to join Philly. Every city has its own identity, etc

He will say: there was definitely a message being sent and said message was being received. He wasn’t thrilled with the amount of respect shown (lack of) during this process, Brad is getting a lot of criticism (wasn’t thrilled with the way he facilitated a lot of the conversations, but there was more to it- JB wishes he was told abt this specifically because he’s earned the respect to be told). Wishes that people had the respect to tell JB as it was, but it never went down/was facilitated like that and JB is most upset at this part because he thought he earned the respect. Says life is like that

One thing he wishes- that his integrity and character haven’t been attacked so much during the process, and he’s responded/keep responding because people tried to create narratives based on anonymous comments

One thing that he say that bothered him as well- Colin Cowherd from earlier today- the one calling JB a “disease” (JB calls this an “interesting” word to use). He says everything is so PR, so scripted/fictional that people want to know what’s real over what’s political propaganda that we get delivered in different industries like sport/music. JB wants to stop all these “damn anonymous sources/executives” stuff because he thinks Cowherd/SAS themselves are the sources to hide how they really feel or are examples of bigotry. Says it’s not the first time that SAS has said stuff like this, also calls out Bobby Marks as well and criticising the “anonymous source” and how JB has to now answer questions about himself.

JB says critique my performance all you want, but a line was crossed multiple times by multiple people.

JB’s excited for the future and what comes next, it’s bittersweet. Says Philly has a history of being a hard/tough nosed city, and doesn’t want any extra privileges and handouts. Is looking forward to getting in the gym and getting to work.

Hard part- for the last 10 years, he’s been “programmed” to hate Philadelphia- the history, the rivalry, specifically says “Fuck the Process” and now has to reverse engineer this in his brain, and that it’ll take a while

Mentions he saw Allen Iverson’s post on his Instagram page, mentions how the city fell in love with him through his journey. Doesn’t wanna be part of any internal politics and wants to let his work do the talking, wants to improve his mindset and authenticity this summer.

“It’s business and how they operate business, it is what it is (…) stuff in life happens that you can’t control (…) it’s unfortunate but don’t let it be disheartening- lead with your authenticity and be yourself and you won’t lose sleep at night”

Said the process overall happened very suddenly.

To the people seeing him personally get attacked and getting discouraged from showing up authentically- don’t be discouraged and don’t lose sleep.

Calls this an outdated system that needs to be updated- and not to shoot the messenger. It’s not to do with journalism or journalistic integrity, there’s a lot of gaslighting, and if you call it out, they attack you and call you arrogant.

Says it’s time to get back to work, he’s already talked to some of the guys from the Celtics and Sixers, including the young guys.

Still says this past season was his favorite, mentions Luka and Queta were great to play with. Mentions he’s grateful.

Mentions that we had a better seed/record in the regular season than the team that won the championship and that despite losing so many guys, Boston was still the second seed.

Calls the entire process an honor, enjoyed playing every night. Says this was likely the result no matter what happened this season. Is looking forward to meeting Sixers fans and having a Philly cheesesteak. Is looking forward to building camaraderie and chemistry with his new teammates and is looking forward to being his authentic self there.

Takes uncomfortable conversations, brotherhood, and chemistry to win a championship.

Mentions again how his brain is still processing everything and doing a 360, now it’s smoke against Boston instead of against Philly.

The world right now can use him as an example with the level of authenticity that someone might bring. He streams but he’s not a streamer, as it’s a way to be more authentic and tell others about himself.

Reason he started streaming- not to make money, but to call out people and not allow people to call him out citing the same anonymous sources.

Calls him Colin Coward, a play on his name.

He gets the whole intelligence thing, and says intelligence is relative and mentions the different types/forms of intelligence. Each person has different values that they value.

Says the bar in sports isn’t that high - how high is the bar really to being smart in sports? Says a lot of people who do different things with their time and is aware of that.

Once again- he and Cowherd have to disagree about athletes live-streaming. Repeats his argument about authenticity and how important it is. Says the world is programmed and cultivated that we’ve got normalized to fake being the new norm- being PR appropriate instead of being authentic through the fuckups and all.

Not gonna comment on Cowherd but still is against the anonymous sources/executive- calls out Perkins as well. Days it’s like someone spreading a rumor to millions of people without realizing if it’s true or not. Says these sources aren’t basketball related and is mad about them coming at his character, calling his intelligence a disease and SAS saying JB isn’t marketable- asks what this has to do about basketball and crosses a line here. JB is not here for that and won’t stand for it.

Spent a third of his life in Boston and will keep moving forward- there’s nothing else he can do. Mentions how he’s been able to build with entrepreneurs in the community, talk with people in power (like the governor) and steer conversations in the way. He puts millions of dollars into Boston and still plans to do it despite being traded. Says he enjoyed every bit of it.

VJ Edgecombe called him earlier or during the stream, and JB calls him back during the stream- mentions how he’s processing everything and says he’s a fan of VJ’s game.

JB Called Maxey as well- who picked up, is jumping on the stream right now. Maxey is in the gym whenever and mentions they’re both locked in now.

JB says the energy is great and the Sixers and him are now moving forward.

Will always love the city of Boston but that they “packed him up”, and his friend says he never thought he’d be rooting for another team

Says “The Process is back M*****F*****”

Mentions about where he’s from and how he’s come out the mud and is hoping forward to him and the city succeeding

Mentions the other stuff going on around the league like Giannis to Miami and Kawhi back to Toronto (and more, mentions he doesn’t know what Ant and Melo are going to do off the court jokingly and recommends supervision, mentions Melo driving 100mph to the arena, says this will be entertaining to watch), mentions he has secretly loved the black Iverson-era jersey

Mentions PG looked like vintage PG, was hooping in the Boston series and that he turned back the clock

Mentions how the Randle and Naz reid trades came out of nowhere, mentions the Kessler trade and is more of a chill/cool guy and wonders hiw he’ll adjust to the bright lights of LA.

Is looking forward to reuniting with Anfernee Simons on the Sixers, calls him a dawg and loved their time together in Boston

Says Boston packed him out the facility and his key card got rejected when he tried to enter, trying to see if the trade was actually happening

Is really looking forward to working and playing with the young talent in Boston

Mentions he got a gift from the community

Also wants to say- he grew up in Boston, spent 10 years here maturing and learning and spending time with people in the community and doing whatever he could, put his heart into this city

We got a championship, felt like we could’ve gotten more, and he doesn’t understand - he overstands what’s happened.

Calls the anonymous sources super unethical and reminds everyone to just ignore them. It doesn’t have ethics and integrity and damages other people’s character.

Needs to start checking people like SAS, Colin cowherd, and more.

Says the Villain has emerged and now, you got me.

Mentions how he and Donovan Mitchell are also streamers, says he’s close with IT from day 1

Shows a custom blue Cape Verde futbol jersey with his name on it, shouts out the Cape Verde community and the person who made the jersey

Repeats he plans on using his money to help the community and will re-visit Boston going forward and wants to give back to how the community adopted him.

His friend mentions the trade isn’t about basketball, and says stars like KG and Antoine Walker weren’t really accessible to the city as JB was

Is excited about having a Philly Cheesesteak

End stream: 8:55 PM
