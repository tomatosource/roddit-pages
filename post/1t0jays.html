I’m just glad we won in ‘24

I love that the Jay’s got at least one championship. No one can take that away from us. And although it’d be nice, just like it would have been with Pierce / KG / and Allen, to get another, I think the current incarnation of the team is a success having just gotten one.

I think it matters with this team too cause I’ve never seen a team as good as the Jay’s Celtics teams sporadically collapse for seemingly random games in the playoffs. Like they were so bad tonight they didn’t even look like they belonged there and I’m convinced it’s a mental issue. Put all the strategy aside and there’s clearly an underlying issue of them getting in their own heads. It’s been going on for too long too consistently at this point. So I’m glad we at least got one

I have no idea what’s going to happen in game 7, but assuming Tatum plays, I think it’s going to come down to effort. I hate to simplify it that much, but I think as basketball fans who are invested in this team we overthink it too much, and understandably so. I’d like to see Mazzulla return to his vision from the regular season and there’s only so much you can do with a lack of talent but at the same time I’ve seen this team when they’re at their best and this has been going on for years. It’s going to come down to effort. If they show up and Tatum’s playing I truly believe we win this game easy and that’s so frustrating to admit but I really think it’s true. I’d love to see them lose a close game that they give their all in, but if they lose to this 76ers team, I think we all know what it’s going to look like… anyway regardless what happens Saturday night I’m glad we at least got one. Hope for the best
