Only thing I want from 0.5 is to remove “mediumcore”

It just feels bad having this forced “mediumcore” gameplay. I want to chill and not lose juiced maps I have invested time and currency into. At this point it feels like the original vision has drifted so far that it would be better to just go back to 6 portals. I am completely fine with the XP loss on death, but losing the map on top of that feels awful.

As for HC players save your breath. I already know the arguments about running easier content or not juicing as much, but that is not how I enjoy the game. Even though I play SC it doesn’t mean I like dying either. With how limited defensive options are you end up getting pushed into very specific builds just to survive or over gear to trivialize the content.

If there is a strong case for this direction I would like to hear it, because right now it just feels like unnecessary punishment. SC players chose SC for a reason.

edit// Guys, suggesting to just go play D4 is not really constructive. The point I am trying to make is that having 6 portals actually enables better game design. It gives room to create much harder and more rewarding content without making every mistake feel punishing to the point of killing the experience.

POE1 handles this well by offering extremely difficult content for the top end of players while still keeping the core experience accessible. That kind of design works because 6 portals exist as a buffer. I would love to see POE2 lean into that approach, where difficulty is pushed higher but feels fair, and 6 portals help support it rather than limit it.
