How to get Temporalis

Hi!

I want to share my experience doing the hardest challenge of this game (I think so, at least).

I started playing this game the second day of this game, december 2024. I was new because I didn’t play POE 1 too much, just I reached endgame with one character. And even I’ve never played a map.

I have learnt everything I know since then, reading guides, watching Youtube, failing in the game… you know.

In this season I have learnt about Trial of Sekhemas, and I’ve always thought the most difficult thing anyone can do is to get the Temporalis. If you don’t know why, just take a look to The Last Flame, second and third line of its description.

But I am a little noob, so I ignored completly the second line and I created an Acolyte of Chayula to do this challenge. I trained as hell, I got the skills and I overcame the challenge without The Last Flame multiple times.

So, with nobody in my house, I tried, pretty sure that if I get touched doesn’t matter because Darkness will save me… ouch, that is not true, or yes… I really don’t know because I got the best try in my life and I got a 0 hit run.

I’m very proud because I think there is no challenge as hard as this one. If you want to know how to overcome the challenge follow this rules:

  1. Practice, practice, practice.
  2. Practice a little more.
  3. Find a quiet moment for yourself.
  4. Think you are capable and nothing can be wrong, but be alert all the time anyway.
  5. Stack evasion.
  6. Memorize almost all rooms, where the monsters are placed, and how to avoid traps (no effort, just practice).
  7. Have a little good luck. Feel the luck. You are the luck :)
