Infernalist and some general feedback.

I’ve been playing quite a few versions of Infernalist over the past couple of patches and have built up a couple of opinions.

### Phys

While I feel overall that the phys spells that we have are in a decent state, I do wish we could have a couple more that don’t need minions to reap the full rewards. Even just changing an interaction around like making the Reaping Staff tagged as both an Attack and a Spell, or at least allowing it to extract impale, could allow for interesting new interactions that would be unique to Phys Spell Builds.

Bonestorm could also use a bit of love since it feels a bit clunky to use. Changing to ground targeting helped a bit, but I still find myself missing in instances where I feel I shouldn’t have. I would also love to see some “less movement speed penalty while channeling” nodes somewhere on the top side of the passive tree.

### Fire

While flammability did go some way to fixing the issue with stacking ignite sources, it still remains a problem that multiple sources of ignite don’t actually help you do more damage. If the design going forward is going to remain “ignite as though”, I feel we need some way for stacking to feel impactful.

Another problem with flammability magnitude is that you currently just find the amount you need to ignite fast enough and then it’s usefulness drops off sharply. A solution could be changing flammability to pull double duty both as “chance to ignite”, and “increased fire/ignite damage taken”. Then changing flammability magnitude nodes to instead raise the threshold of max flammability. That way flammability magnitude will always feel valuable to add rather than being an exercise in finding the exact right amount for your QoL. It doesn’t fix the problem of multiple sources not stacking, but maybe if the drop-off time is harsh enough they can feel less bad (though it might make multiple sources mandatory).

While I understand the need for tri-elemental spells, I’m sad that firestorm is no longer viable in fire only builds.

Incinerate not dealing damage immediately feels off, but other than that I like this iteration more than the old one, with a few exceptions that I’ll get to in the Infernalist section (also would like some “less movement penalty while channeling” nodes for this).

### DoTs

DoT scaling is very unintuitive, and I feel like it needs a rethink. It might be the form of damage with the least amount of options for scaling (except thorns maybe?), and I’m not sure why that is the case. Just let me have “Gain” modifiers work with DoTs. In terms of the fiction, Contagion dealing a bit of lighting damage really doesn’t feel that different to me compared to a fireball dealing cold damage because I equipped a certain wand.

It also hurts that a bunch of Witch ascendancy nodes are Gain or Crit modifiers precluding them from being used in DoT builds.

### Infernalist

I really like Infernalist because it feels like the extreme of the “power at a price” philosophy that seems to be the something that GGG wants to include more of, but which I feel is absent in the top side of the tree. I like to problem solve the downsides, but that might not be for everyone so having a more extreme version of that in an ascendancy for people like me is great.

The Dog has problems in terms of Ignite stacking, which if that is changed that will probably make the dog feel like a cool option rather than just the no downside option you pick because your build can’t sustain the other passives yet.

Altered body, Grinning Immolation and the Beidat nodes are great.

Demon Form, I think it’s good that they swapped the cap to be on the first node, but I think “lose x percent of your health” was a way cooler restriction since it forced the build into a low max life path that was unique to demon form builds.

Bringer of Flame has the ignite stacking problem. In general I find the choice to make 3 out of 6 “elemental ailment” ascendancy nodes not matter if you are only dealing the damage of the associated ailment kind of weird, at least in the Stormweaver tree they are behind something that improves the ailment. Also please let it work with Incinerate if nothing else, Incinerate not being able to take advantage extra non-fire damage when you have this node feels so bad.

Pyromantic Pact has so much potential, only to be used as a recoup machine. If people are to use it as intended they need incentive to do so, and to be “punished” when they try to use it for something else.

I would like to see grinning immolation be built into the base passive, and instead of being a modifier that was given whenever you were on high infernal flame, it scaled with how much you had. “Gain 1% of fire damage for each 5% of infernal flame” or something like that, it could even scale off flat infernal flame so you are still incentivized to stack mana/int. Then have a debuff preventing you from gaining the damage if you have taken damage from infernal flame recently. That way it can still be used to recoup, but that comes at a cost of its damage functionality.

Also a QoL thing but they should really replace the mana bar with an infernal flame bar over you character’s head rather than just removing it. Putting UI elements down in the corner that I might have built around keeping track of shouldn’t be a thing. If there is anything preventing me from using infernal flame as an alternate resource rather than to recoup it is this.

Finally let me use infernal flame as replacement for fuel for incinerate, that is such an obviously cool interaction that I’m surprised that it wasn’t what they had done (or at least let me still gain fuel while I have Pyromantic Pact).

### Misc Stuff

Sacrifice should be a support and not a spirit gem (or it should swap minimum levels with cast on minion death). Not being able to use DD and Profane Ritual on bosses for hours after you get the skills, makes those builds feel like something you swap into rather than being a natural evolution of your characters journey.

Raging Spirits are amazing, but they got hit a bit too hard by the nerf to their interaction with flame wall. I’d like to see the restriction instead be something like “Maximum 1 spirits summoned per second per spell cast” so that Solar Orb and Firestorm can become the more convenient ways to summon them rather than flame wall and ember fusillade.
