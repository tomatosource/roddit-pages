Ulrich Chomche has been waived by Toronto and referred to league’s fitness to play panel, similar to Chrisitan Koloko two seasons ago

Per Blake Murphy:

Chomche has been referred to the league’s fitness to play panel, per sources.
No specifics at this point but similar, process wise, to Koloko the other year.

The fanbase was surprised to see Chomche waived as he has shown lots of promise and the Raptors don’t have any other big men in their development pipeline. However, we now know that he is being referred to the league’s fitness to play panel, which is the same thing that happened to Christian Koloko two seasons ago when it was discovered that he had developed blood clots. Hopefully Chomche is cleared and is able to return to the NBA soon.
