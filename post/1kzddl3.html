Stutter fix (for some)

So everyone knows the stutter issue. This does NOT work for my friends. This works for me every single time the stutters begen. So I want to share this if it helps anyone else at all.

Just type /clear in the chat box in game hen the stutter begins and it should flatline. I’ve even went as far as adding a hotkey in Exile Exchange 2 and put it on my mouse. It actually saved me in a Pinnacle boss fight because I didn’t have to stop and type it out.

I hope this helps at least one other person out there. Additional fixes(but may not work for you) Include deleting your graphics cards shaders as well as PoE’s. And if you are a steam player, go into steam settings turn off preshader cache loading stuff. I’ve always had a not so good gaming PC. I’ve min maxed PCs my whole life. But there are a lot more solutions out there to make PoE run smooth until it gets optimized. I didn’t want to make this a guide but I at least wanted to throw out one stuttering fix.

I play on the client also and not steam like most PoE players. Not sure if that’s an issue either. Don’t wanna redownload the game on steam just to test lol.
