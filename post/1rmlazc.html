In 2020, Vince Carter became the only player to have played across 4 decades. Will there be another player to accomplish this in 2030?

Vince Carter was drafted in 1998, and is the first and only NBA player to play in four different decades (90s/00s/10s/20s), His last season ended in March 2020 due to the pandemic.

Looking ahead to 2030, only a few players drafted in the 2000s remain active in the NBA.

  • 2003: LeBron James
  • 2006: Kyle Lowry
  • 2007: Kevin Durant, Jeff Green, Al Horford, Mike Conley
  • 2008: Russell Westbrook, Kevin Love, Eric Gordon, Brook Lopez, Nicolas Batum, DeAndre Jordan
  • 2009: Stephen Curry, James Harden, DeMar DeRozan, Jrue Holiday, Garrett Temple, Joe Ingles 

KD seems to be the safest bet, but would that be it?
