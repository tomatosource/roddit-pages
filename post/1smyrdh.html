Something I really hope GGG doesn’t change because of constant complaints

Feel free to disagree with me (politely) but here’s what I think:

Whenever combat comes up in discussions I see a vast majority of players agreeing on the fact that a “one button game” can make you feel “accomplished” (you broke the system over your knee) but very boring pretty fast for most of us. Something else that keeps popping up is the game’s overall speed. It seems that a lot of people are unhappy with how “slow” and “deliberate” (especially) endgame feels. This is one point I’m so baffled by because what those people are basically asking for - at least from my understanding - is PoE1? Because if you’re tanking the entire screen and thousands of monsters at the same time why even bother playing PoE2? It looks like a gorgeous blur anyways?! Or why not play D4?! That game looks shiny and is VERY fast (from the moment you skip the campaign you can face-tank hundreds of monsters at once. I even got the feeling (coming back to D4 for the latest expansion) that they dialed up the carnage because of fan feedback (the horse is a racing car now to compliment this and just rushes you along).

I really really hope GGG stays firm and tries to deliver a game that even in endgame is a bit “slower” and less “Dynasty Warriors”/Musou and needs more than 2-3 buttons (preferably until way into lategame). I’m not saying “fast carnage is bad” (it’s a matter of opinion) but basically every ARPG - maybe aside from Titan Quest - ends up like this very fast (and I don’t mean like 50 hours into a character, I mean in the span of like 1-2 hours).

What do you think?
