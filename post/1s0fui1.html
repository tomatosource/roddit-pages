The Pacers are Quietly on a 16 Game Losing Streak, Tying the Kings for Longest Streak of the Season

The Indiana Pacers have broken their franchise losing streak record yet again; their previous record happening from Dec 12th - Jan 8th at 13 games. They may break the league record for the season when they play the Magic on Monday.

On a related note the Wizards are on a 15-game losing streak and may tie four other streaks for franchise losing streak record when they play the Knicks.
