Zizaran’s idea of a high-level character paving the way for alts is genius

Hey everyone,

I was watching a recent clip from Zizaran (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/AX7Jp9RcV0U?feature=share), and I just wanted to strongly support the idea he brought up regarding leveling alternate characters in PoE 2.

The idea of your high-level character paving the way for alts is awesome. Modifying the campaign after your first playthrough is actually genius. Instead of a boring, raw “campaign skip,” having your endgame character make permanent changes to the world that speed up the process for your alts fits the game thematically and sounds incredibly fun.

It feels like the absolute perfect compromise between people who want to skip the campaign entirely and GGG’s vision of playing through the game.

I really hope the devs see this and consider something along these lines!
