Casual player experience

I am a very casual player, and moreover I am new to these kind of games. Poe2 is the first isometric, loot-based action rpg that I have played. I bought the game in february, and had good time blasting through the campaign with my warrior. I had so much fun, that I reached the endgame, which for me was mapping, and I didn’t get far with that when the 0.2. dropped.

Now somethong has changed. Maybe it is the feeling of novelty that has disappeared, maybe it’s the loot, I don’t know. All I am saying that I couldn’t be bothered to do the campaign again. It’s all so tedious. The hype I had at the beginning of this version dried up really quick, even though I changed class to easier one (lich).

I do get that the game is still in beta. But gor me, a casual 33 year old dad, it seems better to stand out and come back after the remaining acts have been released.

Am I right with this conclusion?
