Rockies prospect Braylen Wimmer diagnosed with brain tumor

Understandably, I can’t post the Go Fund Me directly, but here’s what it says.

“On November 1st, 24-year-old Braylen received news that completely changed his life. Doctors discovered a brain tumor that will require an awake craniotomy to remove as much as possible.

He now faces a challenging road ahead with ongoing MRIs several times a year, specialist follow ups, and post op care to ensure a safe and full recovery. Through it all, Braylen’s goal remains the same, to heal fully and get back to doing what he loves most, playing baseball.

We are raising funds to help cover medical and recovery costs including surgery expenses, scans, and care during this time. Our hope is to take some of the financial stress off Braylen so he can focus on recovery and getting back to the game and life he loves.

Anything helps, whether it’s a donation, a share, or simply keeping him in your thoughts and prayers. Please don’t feel obligated to give, your love, encouragement, and prayers mean just as much and truly make a difference.

Thank you for supporting Braylen through this journey.”

Braylen was drafted by the Rockies in the 8th round of the 2023 draft and made it to AA this year, hitting a combined .296/.366/.466 with 17 HR and 37 SB in A+ and AA.
