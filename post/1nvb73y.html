Better Buzzer Beater Win: Aaron Gordon put back dunk vs Derrick White offensive rebound layup?

Context on these two plays.

The Denver Nuggets in the first round of the 2025 playoffs faced off against the red hot LA Clippers and were down 2-1 in the series on the road. Led by Nikola Jokic, the Denver Nuggets held a 22 point lead in the 4th Quarter only for the Clippers to lead a 32-10 run that tied the game. It was for sure going to over time and a likely Nuggets loss until Jokic heaved a desperation 3 after an inbound, and Aaron Gordon dunked it with less than 0.1 seconds in game time. Videos surfacing the ball left Gordon’s hands with one 1 frame left before the signal light. Denver proceeds to tie the series 2-2 and win game 7.

In the 2nd play, in the 2023 Eastern Conferenve finals The 57 win Boston Celtis faced off against the red hot 8th seed Miami Heat who knocked out the no.1 seed in the Milwakee Bucks before eliminating the New York Knicks for a 2022 Conference Finals rematch.

After going down 3-0, the Boston Celtics clawed back up, winning game 4 at Miani and Game 5 at home to make it 3-2. Like Denver, The Celtics held a massive lead in the 4th but Miami clawed back to take the lead with Al Horford fouling Jimmy Butler at the 3pt line, making all three freethrows with only 3 seconds left.

Forcing a desperation inbound play, Marcus Smart recieved the ball and shot a 3pt pointer. It bounced off the rim, and Derrick White on the left side corner ran, getting the offensive rebound lay up over Kevin Love with 0.1 seconds left on the clock to force a game 7 at home to tie it 3-3 becoming the third team in NBA history to force a game 7 after going down 3-0.

Unfortunately, the Miami Heat tean defeated the heavily favored Boston Celtics in the TD garden, despite the heroics of Jaylen Brown with a thunderous dunk to cut the lead to 21 with 3 minutes left remaining and avoided being the first team ever to blow a 3-0 lead.
