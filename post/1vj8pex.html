Jon Lester on “ruining baseball”

Baseball is Dead released a pretty great interview with Jon Lester yesterday. On the subject of the Dodgers “ruining baseball,” he noted that we don’t see the owners of the Royals /small market teams lining up to bail out and sell their franchises. They’re pretty smart billionaires and know to bail out of bad investments. He recognizes the fans are frustrated but notes players generally think the sport is in a good place. His view on the Dodgers is they aren’t “scared” to take risk to win. And scared money doesn’t make money.
