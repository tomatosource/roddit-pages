Jordan Walsh needs to be in MIP talks.

There might be more obvious candidates but Jordan Walsh went from a guy with no apparent future to one of the best defensive players in the league. His stat line today was not super impressive, but this guy dominated the boards, generated many second chance opportunities, and absolutely HOUNDED Kawhi. Kawhi did not really have an off game, He was just unable to get it going because Walsh didn’t give him an inch of room. White’s scorching first half and JB’s 50 point game were absolutely phenomenal, but Jordan Walsh single handedly prevented the Clippers’ offensive engine from the get-go.

This is an incredibly dramatic jump, am surprised no one in the league has put him in MIP talk despite Walsh getting considerable coverage.
