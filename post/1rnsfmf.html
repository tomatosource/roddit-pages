LA Lakers are the only team that hasn’t played in overtime this season

With 19 games left in the season, the Lakers still haven’t played a single overtime game.

The closest they came was on November 5 against the Spurs. LA was up by 2 with under a second left, but fouled Champagnie on a last-second attempt. OT looked inevitable…until he missed both free throws.

Meanwhile, the Sixers (4–3) and Rockets (1–6, yikes) lead the league in OT games. The Thunder (4-0) are the most dominant, while the Cavs have played the most OT games without a win (3).

Side note: Champagnie and clutch free throws vs LA teams? Not exactly a great combo.
