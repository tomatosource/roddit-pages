Give me your best mapping tips and tricks

This is my first league, I’m having a lot of fun but am slowly learning a lot of things I wish I knew earlier. For example:

  • Ratiakas flow drops from jade isles.
  • use visions of paradise to run valuable maps twice (eg jade isles or abyssal overrun maps).
  • fractured orbs drop from cleansed areas.
  • can run deadly maps as white as they always drop a support gem regardless of rarity
  • Drag the abyss mobs out of their cloud thingy.

So, asking veteran players, what other mapping tips can you give a new player?

Edit: Thanks everyone for all the tips, i’ve tried to consolidate them into a comprehensive list below:

General Mapping

  1. Omen of Resurgence can save your life
  2. Omen of Amelioration can save your XP
  3. Shrines OP
  4. Care -max resist and/or monsters penetrate ele res maps
  5. If you have an ailment/curse/fall down going into character select will cleanse it
  6. Make sure your enemies life gets down to 0 before yours do.
  7. If you have a white or blue tablet, use aug and transmute to try to find good mods before using an alchemy orb. If you don’t hit anything good, the alchemy orb will reroll everything and give you a second chance. 

Precursor Towers

  1. Use tablet of grand project to tower hop looking for what you want

Corrupted/Cleansed Areas

  1. Can leave nexus to end if value corruption mods or rush nexus to focus fracturing orbs
  2. Avoid immured fury maps for now since its bugged and doesn’t spawn

Mechanics

  1. Abyss/Deli is a good combo
  2. Drag Amamumu mobs out of their cloud (well documented at this point)
  3. Use “rares have 1 additional modifier” in combination with abyss for them to be more likely to be loyal to Amamumu
  4. Abyss overflow/unique tablet with rogue exiles is good for farming heart

Citadels

  1. When hunting for citadels, just keep uncovering the map in one direction going outward, instead of in circles
  2. They spawn in groups
  3. Rune them with run high waystone drop chance maps + precursors

Deadly maps

  1. Can run as white, they always drop a support gem
  2. Use visions of paradise to get 2 attempts (and hence 2 drops)
  3. (unconfirmed) unique overseer tablet (wisp affects the map boss) makes it more likely for the boss to drop a good lineage support
