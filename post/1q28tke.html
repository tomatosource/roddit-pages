Alex Sarr is Learning to be a Center

Alex Sarr’s development on offense has many pieces, but it’s striking how much better his shot diet has been this year for a big.

2024-25 within 5 ft: 3.4 FGA at 61%

2025-26 within 5 ft: 6 FGA at 71%

This change has increased his EFG% from an abysmal 45.7% to a respectable 54.1% while increasing his ppg from 13.0 to 17.6. Even more simply, he’s reduced his average shot distance from 14.4 ft to 10.0 ft and is dunking almost 50% more often than last year. It’s an interesting contrast to the trend that Wemby embodies, where Sarr has significantly improved his offense overall by playing more like a big and less like a guard.
