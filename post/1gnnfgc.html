ESPN broadcast and media product have been disappointing across the board

ESPN media quality has deteriorated across the board. I know people like Stephen A. or Perkins aren’t popular on this sub but it goes beyond those TV talking heads. Their game broadcasts used to be and was their saving grace. But now it feels flat and doesn’t give national TV vibe at all. Now I wish we still had Mark Jackson and JVG - despite their irrelevant jokes and rants.

And their analysts have become 100% ESPNified. Bob Myers looks so out of place on the analyst desk as they ‘analyze’ the game for 30 seconds during halftime. Nobody has time to talk about what is happening on the court. Its all big picture hot takes or game highlights.

Now might be a good time to talk about this because Windhorst recently talked about how NBA media is suffering this season. Pretty ironic because the producers and executives at ESPN are partially responsible.

NBA media right now, we’re not in a great place. Because I think we’re devaluing things … that help build the league up. Like one of the things [is] storytelling. Everything is too short right now. People are too focused on tweets, too focused on guys getting crossed-over, guys getting dunked on, guys getting embarrassed, social media posts. Not as much on storytelling, learning about players and learning their backgrounds, what I call chronicling the season.

So what is the point of doing reactionary takes and negative commentary if that is not working anymore? I might be asking for a lot but they could start treating NBA as a sport.
