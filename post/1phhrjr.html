Pennant-Winning Teams from the 2000s with No Hall of Fame Players

Basically a sequel to this post I made a few weeks ago. I wanted to wait to see how the VC elections turned out, and sure enough, Jeff Kent got elected, knocking the 2002 Giants off the list, so I’ll only give them a cursory acknowledgment.

Also, since there seems to be some early confusion: this period is from 2000 - 2009. I’m not including any team from 2010 onwards, as I feel that’s a post for another time with how many there are (and additionally, Carlos Beltran likely getting elected this year will knock two teams off the list, and like with Kent I don’t want that post to be instantly outdated).

Without further ado:

  • 2002 Anaheim Angels - won in seven against the San Francisco Giants who had Jeff Kent. Most worthy HOFers: Kevin Appier, Francisco Rodriguez
  • 2005 Chicago White Sox - won in four against the Houston Astros who had Jeff Bagwell and Craig Biggio. Does not count Frank Thomas as he was not on the postseason roster. Coaches Harold Baines and Tim Raines elected as players. Most worthy HOFer: Mark Buehrle
  • 2008 Philadelphia Phillies - won in five against the Tampa Bay Rays (see below). General manager Pat Gillick elected. Most worthy HOFers: Cole Hamels, Chase Utley
  • 2008 Tampa Bay Rays - lost in five to the Philadelphia Phillies (see above). Most worthy HOFers: Evan Longoria, Ben Zobrist

This makes 4 out of 20 (anyone got a blunt?) teams from this decade left. Of course, unlike the 20th century teams, a lot of these are simply the symptom of there not being much time to elect them. Based on current voting trends, I could see all of these teams getting at least one rep in the next ten years.
