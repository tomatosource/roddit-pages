Frustrating expedition experience - Aldur’s Saga

I had an absolute blast during the 0.5 launch running standard Expeditions. The loot felt rewarding, and it wasn’t uncommon to hit remnants yielding 5 to 10 Divines.

​After saving up 50 Divines, I decided to step out of my comfort zone, push my build, and invest all of it into running Aldur’s Saga. Unfortunately, the experience left me feeling completely scammed. Out of a 50 Divine investment, my total return was a meager 16 Divines—a massive net loss.

​To make matters worse, my very first Grand Expedition (with +7 remnant slots) completely bricked. A mandatory mob glitched and became entirely invincible. I cleared and searched the entire map to find a workaround, but there was nothing I could do.

​Between the devastatingly low returns and game-breaking bugs, this high-tier content feels incredibly unreliable and unprofitable. When investing 50 Divines into endgame content, a player should reasonably expect a strong return on investment—not to lose more than half of it due to poor balancing and technical glitches.
