After starting the season 0-2, the Denver Nuggets have gone 7-1

This stretch has included impressive wins against what was at the time the undefeated Thunder and the reigning Western Conference Champion Dallas Mavericks. They’ve been missing Aaron Gordon and Jamal Murray during some of these games as well.

Everyone is well aware how incredible Jokic has been during this stretch. However, in addition to him, the Nuggets younger players have all stepped up. In particular, Christian Braun has been playing fantastic basketball since becoming the starting shooting guard. He’s been averaging 16ppg on 50% from the field and 50% from 3.

Was the Nuggets demise greatly exaggerated?
