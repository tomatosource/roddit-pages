People are massively underrating the buff crafting got.

I’ll explain here how I see it.

  1. Fracturing. This is by far the strongest and most core crafting mechanic in PoE 1. Endgame crafting (outside recombination) is still mostly essence harvest and fossil spam on fractured items then Meta crafting methods.

Fracturing orbs won’t be that rare. They are a deterministic guaranteed drop from a cleansed boss according to the q and an and drop from mobs around it. You can absolutely target farm fracturing orbs.

  1. Recombinations + greater essences.

Not only have greater essences been made way more common AND with maps that guarantee them, but recombinations have been introduced.

Making a 4 mod item just won’t be that hard with the enhanced ability to SELECT your mods from the recombination.

You don’t need to alt spam. Pick up a rare with 1 mod you want. Slam it together with another rare with 1 mod you want. 35% you get both (per the stream, on average). You’re already at 2 mods. Do it again for feeders. You can probably make dozens of these to slam for a 4 mod.

  1. Fractured Item Crafting.

Poe 2 already gives you plenty of exalts and chaos, which you can freely and safely use on fractured items. Alongside annuls having an actual purpose now.

Then the prevalence of essences, greater essences, and omens being significantly enhanced.

TL;DR You can make a fairly deterministic path towards getting fractured items with mods you want that are much much easier to craft on than before.

Recombinations as well can easily get you very strong early to mid game deterministic items as well.

Significant buffs to meta crafting and greater essences will also have a big effect.
