Complete National Broadcast Teams for the 2025 AL and NL Division Series

For those interested. Not including regional radio teams because they don’t change in the same way; for the most (or whole) part, it’s the regulars on regional radio.

Los Angeles Dodgers vs. Philadelphia Phillies
TBS/TruTV/HBO Max - Brian Anderson, Jeff Francoeur with Lauren Shehadi
ESPN Radio - Dave O’Brien, Jessica Mendoza

Chicago Cubs vs. Milwaukee Brewers
TBS/TruTv/HBO Max - Alex Faust, Ron Darling with Lauren Jbara
ESPN Radio - Jon Sciambi, Doug Glanville

Detroit Tigers vs. Seattle Mariners
FOX/FS1/Fox Sports App/Fox One - Adam Amin, AJ Pierzynski, Adam Wainwright with Tom Verducci

New York Yankees vs. Toronto Blue Jays

Sportsnet (Canada) - Dan Shulman, Buck Martinez with Hazel Mae, Kevin Pillar, and Caleb Joseph

ESPN Radio coverage is free on the ESPN App if you need free, radio, or both, Enjoy the games!
