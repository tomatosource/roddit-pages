Could uniques get an overhaul please?

As someone who has a level 94, level 92, and level 87 character, I feel like I’ve sunk enough hours to get a broad feel for the game and itemization diversity (stopped playing due to endgame having no infinite climb / reward system). With very few exceptions (the Headhunter belt being one), the vast majority of uniques in this game have no real value or unique identity. I’d be fine with toning down how often we see a unique drop, but IMO, the person in charge of thinking through the itemization characteristics needs to take a long hard look at each item and ask if said item would motivate them to make a build for it. Every unique should motivate the finder to create a build around it.

Here’s some random examples I just came up with:

  • Armor (Trail of Spheres): The more you are physically attacked, the more health globes spawn within X radius of you for Y seconds.
  • Boots (Slow on the Go): The slower your movement speed is below base movement speed, the more frequently you randomly teleport to unexplored locations on the map.
  • Ring (The Many are the Few): Equipping this ring will allow you to equip a third ring, but dampen the effects of all equipped rings by 33%.
  • Bow (Balls of Steel): Every time your health drops below 10%, your critical strike chance increases by 100% for X seconds.

I’m not saying these ideas are fully fleshed out, but they have personality and get me thinking about build ideas a lot more than most of the uniques I ever come across.
