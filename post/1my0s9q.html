Serbia announces final 12-man roster for this year’s EuroBasket, Topic left out

Source

Point guards: Vasilije Micic (Hapoel Tel Aviv), Aleksa Avramovic (Dubai), Stefan Jovic (Valencia)

Shooting guards: Bogdan Bogdanovic (Los Angeles Clippers), Vanja Marinkovic (Partizan Belgrade)

Small forwards: Marko Guduric (Olimpia Milano), Ognjen Dobric (Crvena zvezda Belgrade)

Power forwards: Nikola Jovic (Miami Heat), Filip Petrusev (Dubai), Tristan Vukcevic (Washington Wizards)

Centers: Nikola Jokic (Denver Nuggets), Nikola Milutinov (Olympiacos)
