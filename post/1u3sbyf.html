I love the new Atlas, but drastically increasing the sizes of Biomes would still be a big improvement imo

My biggest wish is still to make the biomes in the atlas much bigger. Currently, you see pretty much everything on your screen at once (mountains, woods, deserts, water, etc.), so the atlas looks pretty similar most of the time, and it’s hard to tell where you are. Sometimes it even looks silly because there’s a tiny desert with only one map next to a large mountain area.

I think larger biomes would help a lot with navigation and orientation because they would serve as fixed points, similar to the fortress. The fortress helps with orientation, but I still get lost and dizzy as soon as I go deeper into the infinite atlas.

From an immersion standpoint, it would also be great to have large biomes, such as a large mountain pass or desert, that you can cross or avoid if you don’t like it. This would make sense, especially with all the biome-specific nodes on the Atlas tree.
