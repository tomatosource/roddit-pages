[NBA.com] The Basketball Hall of Fame is opening an exhibit for Steph Curry. It is the first time in its nearly 75-year history that the Hall of Fame will feature a dedicated experience honoring an active NBA player.

SPRINGFIELD, MASS. – On Friday, July 24, the Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame will make history by launching its newest exhibition dedicated entirely to 12-time NBA All-Star, two-time MVP, Olympic gold medalist, four-time NBA champion, and the undisputed greatest shooter of all time: Stephen Curry.

This project represents the first time in the Hall’s nearly 75-year history that a current, active player will be honored with a dedicated feature experience.

Developed as the next iteration of the Hall of Fame’s acclaimed gallery, “The Vault,” the immersive exhibit titled Stephen Curry: Beyond the Arc goes far beyond Curry’s legendary on-court accolades. Created in direct partnership with Curry’s Thirty Ink business team, the exhibit is designed to explore his multifaceted legacy as a basketball icon, entrepreneur, philanthropist, and family man.

Visitors can expect a captivating, curated journey through Curry’s life and career, featuring:

  • Exclusive Memorabilia: Rare game-worn gear, championship artifacts, and Olympic ephemera.

  • Rare Footage: Archival video tracing his journey from an overlooked college prospect to a global superstar.

  • First-Hand Storytelling: Immersive audio and video components that capture the emotion and heart of a true champion.

  • The exhibit is scheduled to open to the public July 24 at the Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame in Springfield, Massachusetts.
