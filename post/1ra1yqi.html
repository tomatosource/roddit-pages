The woes of ticket prices and fandom

I know this is a pretty well exhausted topic, but ticket prices are getting insane. I’m a Cubs fan from a family of multiple generations of Cubs fans, but there’s a real chance that my two young boys could become White Sox fans. As much as I want to raise them to be Cubs fans, I can take them to multiple Sox games with decent seats for the price of one Cubs game. Even if I take them to a Cubs game, the seats would be so far away that they likely wouldn’t even enjoy it. We are down to one income, so if I’m taking the family to a ballgame, price matters more than ever. I expect pro sports to be somewhat pricy, but not one game per year stretching my budget pricy. It just sucks that we as fans are to a point that we can’t even afford to root for the team we grew up loving.

I don’t know…maybe I’m venting. Maybe I’m looking for validation to be a casual fan of another team for the sake of making core memories with my kids. If players can leave a team for more money, can’t we fans root for teams that we can afford? Mostly, I’m just sad about it all. I really want to enjoy the sport with my kids.
