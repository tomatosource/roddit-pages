The Portland Trail Blazers have won eight of their last 9 games

The Portland Trailblazers have won 8 of their last 9 games. Their only loss being a competitive game(10 point loss) against the number one seeded Oklahoma City Thunder.

“I’m just happy for our guys,” said Trail Blazers head coach Chauncey Billups. “That was a tough one, but we, we thought it would be, you know, the way that we beat them the other day, we knew that they were gonna come in scrapping and fighting and not allow that to happen again. I mean, you got Hall of Famers on that team so we prepared for a dogfight and that’s exactly what it was we kept scrapping the entire night.”

The Blazers are now 21-29 this season, 13-13 at home, have now won four-straight and eight of their last nine. And with the win, the Trail Blazers split the season series versus the Suns 2-2 after getting both games at Moda Center.

Though Portland had a chance to win in regulation, Phoenix forward Royce O’Neale’s three with 2.1 seconds to play sent the game to overtime

But in a game that featured the likes of Kevin Durant, Ant Simons, Devin Booker and Deni Avdija, it was Ayton who came through in the clutch. The 7-0 center grabbed six rebounds, including four on the offensive glass, went 5-of-6 from the line and 2-of-2 from the field in overtime to lift the scales in Portland’s favor.

Ayton finished the game with a team-high 25 points on 9-of-22 shooting from the field and 7-of-8 shooting from the free throw line to go with a game-high 20 rebounds, nine of which came on the offensive glass, two assists, a steal and a block in 42 minutes.

“I think this may have been DA’s best game all around game, because he’s had some really, really good ones since he’s been in our uniform,” said Billups. “But in terms of his activity and just everything all across the board and to be able to close it out with the big free throws, at the end was, was a really big thing.”

Deni Avdija went 8-of-12 from the field, 3-of-6 from three and 5-of-5 from the line for 24 points to go with eight rebounds, five assists and a steal before fouling out in 43 minutes.

Toumani Camara, who was acquired from the Suns as a part of the deal that brought Ayton to Portland, made five of his seven three-point attempts to finish with 17 points along with five rebounds, an assist, a steal and a block in 45 minutes.

Scoot Henderson came off the bench on his 21st birthday to post 15 points, six assists and a steal in 22 minutes. Anfernee Simons posted a double-double with 13 points, 10 assists while also getting two rebounds and two steals.

Jerami Grant added 14 appoints, four assists and five rebounds, there last of which sealed the victory for Portland.

Next up, the Trail Blazers continue their longest homestand of the season by hosting the Indiana Pacers Tuesday in the second game of a back-to-back for both teams.
