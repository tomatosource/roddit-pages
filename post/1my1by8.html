you can get more fps by swapping to xess 2

since poe 2 0.3 is coming out soon, i wanted to give a tip to those of us without 3 thousand dollar battlemachines. there is a program called dlss-swapper that allows you to ‘swap’ the XeSS version that PoE 2 utilizes from version 1 to version 2. the difference, at least on my computer, (GTX 1060 6GB) is significant. make sure you also use the --nopatch launch option after swapping versions otherwise the launcher will keep reverting it back.
