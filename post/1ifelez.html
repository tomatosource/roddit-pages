What is a niche NBA archetype that has died down over the years?

One I think of is the short chunky roleplayer to borderline all star point guards from the 2000’s and 2010’s. Raymond Felton, Jameer Nelson, Andre Miller, Ty Lawson, and the best of them all, Kyle Lowry. The modern versions that come to mind are Fred Vanvleet and Jamal Shead. Most of them weren’t great athletes and didn’t have any elite skills but they were methodical and gritty.
