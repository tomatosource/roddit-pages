Celtic Pride

I have been a fan of the Boston Celtics for my 42 years on this earth. I inherited this blessing from my father and his father before him. It’s easy to see why my elders were fans of this amazing team and passed their fandom onto me when watching the series Celtics City. While aware of the greatness of this franchise, to see the story unfold is truly amazing. Greatest sports franchise in sports history, hands down.

I could go on and on about how fantastic this team is from a strategical business standpoint to what they’ve accomplished in the name of social equality - no matter what anyone wants to say. While having Luck on our side, we’ve experienced our deal of feeling cursed. From deaths to untimely injuries, this franchise has continued to overcome adversity and persevere.

While I wish the series would have delved deeper into how our young studs in Jaylen and Jayson were thrust into the spotlight; accomplishing more in their young careers than most, if not all, I encourage everyone to watch. Fans, non fans, and especially current players. If I’m psycho Joe, I’m spamming the series to all current Celtics or at least making them watch the final episode. A great reminder of why I love this fucking team and how fucking great we are and how great we can be!

Jayson, Jaylen - while notching one on the belt, the climb is not over. The pinnacle peak awaits. Do not be satiated. Stay Humble and Hungry. So much more is still ahead, your destinies have yet to be told. Embrace the challenge, overcome the critics and know greatness is on your side.

This in not a plug for the series but more in the name of Celtic Pride. Belief in our boys. I am guilty of getting overly frustrated knowing we are capable of being that next Celtics dynasty while stumbling, but I am grateful for what has been accomplished and KNOW there is so much more to come. Let’s get this shit! Fuck the Knicks. Banner 19 awaits. Not if, but win!
