Pursuant to Magic Johnson’s claim of what defines a superstar: A 2018 study comparing road game attendance for MJ, Lebron, Kobe and Shaq.

Here’s a graphic showing some of the results.

https://www.thescore.com/nba/news/1558139-study-how-lebron-compares-to-kobe-shaq-jordan-at-filling-up-road-games

Magic Johnson’s statement that a superstar is someone who you pay to see at a road game was very popular, which made it pretty clear that someone must have compiled those types of numbers in the past. Here’s one that came up from 2018 that measured the average percentage boost in attendance for MJ, Lebron, Kobe and Shaq. Finding that MJ was at 16.86%, which was more than Lebron, Kobe or Shaq by themselves. Though Kobe and Shaq together in the early 00’s equaled or even slightly surpassed MJ’s boost with 16.9%.

I tried to look up individual game attendance on bball reference but couldn’t find the numbers there. Wherever those numbers are available, they make it possible to add Steph Curry, Kevin Durant, and other players to this ranking too. It would take some number crunching if it wasn’t already done, but I have a feeling that’s not a problem here.
