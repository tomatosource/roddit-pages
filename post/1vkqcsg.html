[EuroHoops] Giannakopoulos sets his sights on Nikola Jokic for Panathinaikos next summer

*I always wanted Milos Teodosic. However, there is one player who remains a dream of mine, whom I haven’t signed yet, but I will sign next year,”* he teased.

When the hosts questioned who it could be, he boldly proclaimed: “It’s Jokic, the best player in the world!”

Faced with immediate skepticism, Giannakopoulos doubled down on his claim. *“Don’t laugh at all. If I don’t sign Jokic, I’m not going anywhere,”* he stressed.

*“People are going to say, ‘OK, now he’s joking’. I did make a very serious proposal this year, both to his team and to him. I got a rejection. I know that he has one more year on his contract,”* he admitted.
