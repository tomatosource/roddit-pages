[OC] What NBA players names have the highest value in Roman Numerals

I’m using two different scoring methods:

  • Simple sum: every roman numeral letter (I=1, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, D=500, M=1000) adds its face value regardless of position
  • Evaluated: the roman numeral letters extracted from the name are read as an actual roman numeral using subtractive notation (so IV = 4, not 6)

All other letters count as zero.

Source for numeral value: https://www.rapidtables.com/math/symbols/roman_numerals.html

Key Findings Before The Table

Andre Drummond wins with 3,500 simple points. His name contains D, D, M, M, D, three D’s (500 each) and two M’s (1000 each).

32 players score zero — their names contain no I, V, X, L, C, D, or M whatsoever. Trae Young. Josh Hart. OG Anunoby. Johnny Furphy. Johnny Juzang. Yang Hansen

CJ McCollum is 70% roman numeral letters (7 of 10), the highest ratio of any player. His name CMCCLLM evaluated = 2,100. He is the most Roman player in the NBA.

No player has a name made entirely of Roman numeral letters. The closest is E.J. Liddell (LIDDLL) at 67%, but the double-D and the missing vowels prevent it.

David Roddy contains four D’s, the most D’s in the league. 4 × 500 = 2,000 from D’s alone. He came, he saw, he had four D’s.
