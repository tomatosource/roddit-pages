Pin needs some reworking.

Ok so, I love me some Pin. It is integral to my build. But…. there are some issues. Not huge ones but… when I pin most small mobs they stay put. For melee mobs they’re just SOL. For ranged they can still attack but not move or dodge. But anything with teleport, jumping skills, or any other movement skill, can still use them to gap close to me. The teleport I can kinda understand but not the jumping and rush tackling type stuff. They are chained to the ground, they shouldn’t be able to leap 7 meters to get to me. Bosses also seem to keep chugging along when pinned but… they’re bosses so… oh well.

But can we please fix the pin so it works as it should. You can keep the teleports even but no more jumping and running while pinned please.

And to the eventual “just use stuns, its better” group. I do. I use pins AND stuns for my CC. The stuns are better but the pins proc faster/easier. So I use them for quick stopping most enemies while stun builds up.
