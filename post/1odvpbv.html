Cosmetic Color Customization and Community-Designed Skins (Inspired by Warframe’s TennoGen)

Hey GGG team and Exiles,

I’ve been playing the Amazon lightning spear build in the beta and really enjoying it. Right now I’m using the Valkyrie cosmetic set, and it looks great. The blue and orange lightning effects with feathered details looks really good!

While I love how it looks, it made me think of something that could make cosmetics in Path of Exile 2 even better: color customization.

1. Color Palette System for Cosmetics

In Warframe, players can recolor cosmetics using different palettes. It doesn’t replace the item, it just lets you personalize it.

Imagine being able to:

  • Adjust the Valkyrie’s feathers to purple or red
  • Match armor hues to your skill colors or aura effects
  • Reuse store cosmetics across multiple builds without them all looking the same

It would make every Exile look more personal while increasing the long-term value of MTX items players already own.

2. Community-Created Cosmetic Program

Another idea inspired by Warframe’s TennoGen:

  • Artists could design armor sets, weapon skins, or effects that fit Path of Exile’s style
  • GGG could review and polish selected designs
  • The community votes on the best designs each league, and winners get released officially in the store
  • The creator earns a share (for example, 30%) of the cosmetic’s revenue

Each league could even have a theme like. Artists could submit designs based on the theme, and the best entries get featured.

This would give players fresh cosmetics every league while encouraging creativity within the community. It would also make the cosmetic system feel more alive and connected to the players who love the game’s art style.

I think this would be a great way to make PoE 2’s cosmetics more meaningful, giving players a chance to personalize what they’ve bought and even contribute to what comes next.

Would love to hear others’ thoughts on this idea and whether a color palette or community cosmetic program sounds appealing.
