Felix Hernandez will make the Hall of Fame eventually…

He won’t make it this year, and it’s possible he won’t make it next year either. But more and more I get the feeling that he’ll eventually get in.

He started his first year on the ballot with a surprising 20.6%. This year he’s already gaining new votes, and I estimate he can make a big jump on his second ballot. I think the historical standard of what a Hall of Fame pitcher is “supposed” to look like is changing drastically, and voters are building that foundation for the future, rewarding pitchers like Felix. So whether it’s through his ten years on the ballot or through a Veterans Committee later, I think he’ll eventually get in.

Cy Young (should’ve been two), a perfect game, one of the best primes of the modern baseball era, and the icon of a franchise that gave him no offensive support.

There are only six pitchers in the 21st century with more than 2,500 innings, 2,500 strikeouts, 165 wins, and an ERA under 3.50:
Verlander, Scherzer, CC, Kershaw, Greinke… all clear Hall of Famers—and King Felix.
