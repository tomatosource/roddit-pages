+4 amulets “disappeared” from the market

https://preview.redd.it/vafan11boo8h1.png?width=1668&format=png&auto=webp&s=11636ab6d321951262326db8256220a5cb94700e

They changed the way the search engine works.
Before it would consider the quality of the amulet, so if you searched for +4, no matter how it was achieved you would find them.

Now it ignores the quality and only takes in account the raw modifier, so +3 is +3, even if the quality makes it +4, so when you search for +4 only +5 and +6 appears, no because those are the only ones in the market but because those reached +4 by sanctification.

For the system, +5 and +6 doesn’t exist neither

As a result you can’t search for +4 amulets anymore, when searching for at least +4 only +5 and +6 appear and when searching for +3 you can’t click on the modifier because for the system +3 can only be +3, so even those +4 are considered +3.

Right now, the only way to see them is to sort them by price, in which case you will only see those overpriced.
edit: As comments mention, you can still search them by adding a minimum of 34% quality at Item Quality.
