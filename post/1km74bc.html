Imagine if Jaylen Brown led the Celtics to a comeback and repeated as champions

You know, I was just thinking, can you imagine if Jaylen Brown somehow goes crazy, and the Celtics come back from being down 3–1? They win the series, go on to the Eastern Conference Finals, win that too, and then go back to the Finals and repeat as champions. I don’t know how likely it is, but if it happened, the legacy talks around him would be insane.

But honestly, what matters most right now is Tatum’s health, not just the possibility of some miracle run. Still, if it happened, it would be one of the wildest playoff stories ever.
