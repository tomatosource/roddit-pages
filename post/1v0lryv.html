Since 2000, 2 players have been an All-Star in their rookie year. Before 2000 there were 43 rookies who were an All-Star in their rookie year

I found this really interesting.

The two players since 2000 to be an All-Star in their rookie year are Blake Griffin in 2011 when he missed his first year in the NBA. And Yao Ming who averaged 13.5 PTs in his rookie year and placed 2nd in ROTY voting.

Before 2000, notable players like Duncan, Hakeem and MJ were all stars in their rookie year, along with Wes Unseld who also won MVP his rookie year.

I just thought it was interesting such disparity. Shows how voters changed their criteria for being an all star and (maybe) the talent in the league has gotten a tiny bit better? Idk if this points to that but it’s a thought I had. Anyways what are your thoughts.
