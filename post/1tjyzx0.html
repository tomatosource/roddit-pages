What the fuck happened to the Mariners in 2004?

I know that sounds like a weirdly specific question, but I was just looking at their list of seasons on Wikipedia and it jumped out at me. For a long time there was the narrative around their drought and how it started immediately after their record-setting 2001 season, but it seems they were actually quite good in 2002-03, and there is no indication of a hidden decline from underlying metrics: in 2003, they actually had the best X-W/L in MLB, and won two more games than the eventual World Series champions. Obviously I didn’t watch them, but I’d assume it could be argued they were genuinely elite still, just unlucky (no other team in the wild card era has missed the playoffs in consecutive seasons of over 90 wins).

And then in 2004, Ichiro shattered the hits record and lead the league in bWAR (9.2), but you wouldn’t know it looking at their record: *63-99*. Essentially, without their star player, they’d have lost over 105 games? That’s an absurd drop-off and I can’t think of anything that measures up to it in recent memory. Maybe the Nationals, but they were at least mediocre for a year and half and *then* had a fire sale. What’s the explanation for the ‘04 Mariners?
