The Hornets just finished January tied with the 2010-11 Heat for the best road differential in a calendar month in NBA history (+151)

Source via Stathead.

In the month of January, the Hornets went 8-3 on the road, and were +151 in those 11 games.

https://preview.redd.it/a7f8jgp1hkgg1.png?width=588&format=png&auto=webp&s=46b21d226cec20fc3973c4f9548c866ff8ed9d84

Highest road differential in NBA history per month:

  • January: 2025-26 Hornets (+151)
  • February: 2021-22 Celtics (+136)
  • March: 2013-14 Spurs (+131)
  • April: 1995-96 Bulls (+111)
  • May: 2020-21 Pacers: (+65)
  • October: 1971-72 Lakers (+84)
  • November: 2025-26 Thunder (+132)
  • December: 2010-11 Heat (+151)

Note: regular season only*
