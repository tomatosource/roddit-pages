Thorns and 0.4

Thorns has been a fun, albeit fairly niche addition to POE2.

It has some glaring issues though that should be looked at prior to 0.4 to improve the archetype as a whole.

-Absolutely mandatory unique items- whilst these items are cheap and super common, it’s very weird to have a build with specific passive tree support absolutely require specific unique pieces to function at a reasonable level.

-crown of the pale king- completely mandatory unique to enable the thorns playstyle. This item should be changed to be something else and replaced by either a notable or keystone or just make thorns work on all hits implicitly.

It just completely stifles build craft to have a mandatory unique item for a build to work at all.

Second to this is briarpatch.

You could in theory not use these boots, but they provide such an enormous amount of base crit scaling that they too are all but mandatory for every thorns build. The power of these boots should definitely be reduced and shifted more to the passive tree so that they are not absolutely mandatory no matter how you build your character.

Support gems- Both barbs and quill burst should be available earlier in the game so that people can choose to level with thorns for a first character

There isn’t really any drawbacks to this.

Also the way these support gems function really holds back the entire thorns playstyle.

There are several monsters and bosses that do not trigger thorns with any attacks whatsoever, you can actually get soft locked on a boss and need to sit there till you die because they have 0 attacks that trigger thorns and thus you can’t kill them.

If they want to support this archetype, either every boss at minimum, but ideally every monster as well needs to have some attack that triggers thorns

OR

And this is much more preferable. Barbs and quill burst should just be replaced with a support that adds thorns to attacks with some trivial cooldown such that it can’t be abused and is much less clunky than the current playstyle.

Ideally you’d be able to play thorns as purely a reflect build, but currently thorns is too weak to achieve this and would need quite a bit more power for this to work.

-Thornskin support stacking needs to be fixed and removed. This is clearly unintended since no other spirit support gem is stackable, but this bug is propping up the entire thorns playstyle right now and it has devolved into stacking as much spirit as possible to stack this support as the easiest way to improve damage. This should be fixed and thorns improved slightly to increase build diversity.

There’s many other issues with the playstyle but these things stand out as far and away the most glaring issues from my perspective and fixing these things would go super far in making the niche thorns playstyle more viable, more flexible and more fun.

As it stands thorns is completely viable but it’s fair to say the build sits at a at best C or D tier in terms of power compared to the rest of the game. Which is a shame because it is a super fun build to play as a whole and very new player friendly in a lot of ways.

Really looking forward to 0.4 and the potential additions to the playstyle!
