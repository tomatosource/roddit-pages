Props to the Pioneers

Props to the Celtics for taking the inaugural Pioneers Classic seriously. I thought they came out tight like it was a meaningful game then opened it up once they settled in. Cooper, Clifton, and Lloyd deserve all the credit they can get for being the first to integrate the NBA. Hopefully at the next Classic another pioneer with Boston ties, “the Original” Bucky Lew, gets his due. The man who integrated every conceivable role in the pro game over 100 years ago deserves some recognition too!
