Once in a Lifetime (Now in English ;D)

I’m just going to write a little story about me and my two friends who play PoE2.

I’ve now put in about 1,000 hours of the game. I’m having a lot of fun with it and have also spent a lot of time with the subject matter. I was very hyped when PoE2 launched and got a friend hooked, who had never played an ARPG before, and he just wanted to try out the new game with me. He’s also been playing for 700 hours now. Our other friend just started playing this League (0.3) and is completely dedicated to it. He’d also never had any contact with the genre before and is now at around 150 hours. And this is the “wunderkind” that we’re going to talk about here…

My alarm goes off at 6:30 a.m. because that’s when I let my sheep out of the barn. The alarm also goes off at the same time, so I check my phone and turn it off. I immediately see a new WhatsApp message that reached me at 3:30 a.m.

He had reached the endgame with his Warrior and was slowly but surely and with great joy playing through it. At the time, he was on a T8 map.

This image hit me full force in the morning, and I immediately jumped out of bed in a rush of adrenaline. I started sending him voice messages about how awesome and rare the whole thing was. Since he had already gone to bed, my messages remained unanswered for a while, and I had to practice patience.

Well, he got up in the afternoon (after sending me a voice message the morning before saying, “Let me get some more sleep.”)

My plan for that day was actually to spend the whole day anticipating the Battlefield 6 release and then actually follow through with it when it happened. But that was all secondary :D

Okay, he’ll be online and we’ll all meet up on Discord. Totally freaking out, how awesome and rare and so on and so forth. He sold the mirror and, as previously discussed, we shared the Divine Orbs he’d received.

Overjoyed and completely delirious, my mate, who’s been around for a while, and I build our gear. We buy a bow here, some awesome gloves there, and so on and so forth.

The “wunderkind” just continues his endgame and rejoices with us at how much fun we’re having min-maxing our characters. He’s now on a T11 map.

*TINK*

The two of them are freaking out….. At first, I don’t know what’s going on. I look at my second screen in the “wunderkind’s” stream and I can’t believe what I see there…

He just finds another mirror! 12 hours later, a few levels further on in a T11 map. How crazy is that? How wild are the chances of something like that happening? In such a short second space, items drop twice that you could only ever guess at in the distance. He sells it again, we share, and we’re overjoyed to have been so blessed.

What happened as described above will never happen again, and I can’t imagine anyone in this league, or in the entire PoE2 era, has experienced anything similar.
