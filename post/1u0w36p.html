Trey Mancini goes 3-for-4 with 1 RBI and a run scored in his first MLB game in nearly 3 years

In what is surely the second biggest personal comeback in his professional baseball career, Trey Mancini made a surprise return to the big leagues with the Angles tonight. Before this, his last Major League action came on July 31st with the Cubs. Chicago released him shortly after, and he’s bounced around baseball on minor league deals ever since.

He spent a month in the Reds’ organization to finish 2023, then spent Spring Training with the Marlins in 2024, but does not appear to have played anywhere during the regular season, and then he was with Arizona in AAA for half the season last year.

Mancini’s always been one of the easiest players to root for in MLB, so I was delighted to see his name in a lineup tonight, and even more delighted to see him do well, as the Angles lost 5-4.
