My (SSF Player) idea for next the next League mechanic.

I’m an SSF player who has 4 characters above 90 this season and I’ve killed the pinnacle bosses with all of them. As of right now SSF isn’t in a terrible place outside of periodic dry spells in things like fracturing orbs and Grand Mirrors spawning….

HOWEVER, Rings and amulets are still a huge nightmare to craft. Breach was a fantastic move forward in ring, belt, and amulet availability. It still leaves a ton of RNG results for most of the affixes. I’d even go as far as saying the fluxes from expedition remnants helps a ton with suffixes. However there are so many prefixes that it’s almost impossible to get what you are looking for (within a fairly reasonable cost).

With that in mind, here is my idea for a new league.

League: Bestiary Rewards: Trophies

The Bestiary could be as simple as the mechanic in PoE1, but maybe with the beasts able to roam from atlas node to atlas node each time the player finishes a map. This lets the player “hunt” beasts across the atlas. Maybe even some beasts travel in packs where you have to kill a number of pack members before you can directly engage the “main beast” (think of it like a roaming anomaly map). Obviously there would be some sort of pinnacle boss and different biomes spawn different types of beasts.

Rewards: The rewards would be called “trophies” and they would be things like teeth, paws, ears, and fins. (You get the idea). These trophies could act like perfect essences or alloys, but only for rings and amulets. (Maybe gloves too they have a similar mod pool). This works thematically as many hunters in real life would wear things like claw blades or teeth from their kills as a necklace. This would be like we are attaching these trophies to our necklace(amulet) or rings.

This would help plug a hole in crafting, hits all the “modern league” activities and could have very interesting interactions with things like breach, rogue exiles, abyss and essences. (Maybe even make wild things available as favours in Ritual if killed in a ritual ring.)
