The Celtics now have 0 second round picks from 2027-2030

I know most people are going to laugh and say “who cares about second round picks, they hardly ever work out anyways” but that’s kind of missing the point. Second round picks are good for making small deals happen, and when you run out you have no choice but to start using first round picks instead. There’s also a lot of players on this team that were drafted late in the 1st round or in the 2nd, which proves that this front office is great at utilizing later draft picks when they have the chance. Second round picks also provide a way to fill out an expensive roster with the smallest contracts possible.

It’s a little bit of a bummer that we had to give up the NO pick just to extend the TPE and we couldn’t even keep the Denver 2027 pick because ownership wanted to save money. Not anything to be super upset about, but we should call it like it is. Wouldn’t be surprised if we see Brad trade back out of the first round again this year to try to refill the coffers.
