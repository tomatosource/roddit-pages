I made a simple, clean Stash Tab Sale Timer & Campaign Tracker

Hey Exiles,

I always found myself googling “when is the next PoE stash sale” or realizing halfway through maps that I missed a few passive point quests during the campaign.

I wanted a single, clean place to check both, so I built: https://poestash.pages.dev/

What’s inside:

  • Stash Sale Timer: A simple countdown based on the 3-week rotation. No more guessing if it’s this weekend or the next.
  • Campaign Tracker: A checklist for all the important quests and labs (Passives) so you don’t have to /passives at level 80 to find out what you missed.
  • Tab Calculator: Quickly check how many points you need for the tabs you want.
  • Useful Links: Quick access to other community tools I use daily.

The site is ad-free, mobile-friendly, and designed in a dark theme that won’t flashbang you at 3 AM.

It’s my first tool, so let me know what you think! I’d be grateful for any feedback or suggestions. Stay safe, Exiles!
