[Fix] Constant crashes in POE2 solved after VBIOS update. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop

Hi all.

Just wanted to share this in case it helps someone else.

I’ve been dealing with constant crashes in PoE2 and many other games on my Lenovo Legion Pro 5 laptop with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070. After extensive troubleshooting, the only thing that finally resolved the issue was updating the GPU VBIOS.

Since the update, I haven’t experienced a single crash in PoE2 or any other game. So if you’re encountering similar issues with this setup, I highly recommend checking if there’s a VBIOS update available for your laptop GPU. You can find the official VBIOS update for the Legion Pro 5 16IRX8 (Intel-based) on Lenovo’s support site: VBIOS (RTX 4070) for Windows 11 (64-bit) - Legion Pro 5 16IRX8, Legion Pro 7 16IRX8

Please note that the VBIOS update needs to be installed manually, as Lenovo Vantage may not pull it automatically through system updates.

Hope this helps someone!
