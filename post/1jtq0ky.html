I don’t believe GGG is testing Path of Exile 2 anymore.

As an individual who spent almost 2K hours with GGG games;

After typing and giving up on posting this post several times, I have finally decided to click “Post” this time.

They say “We tested and like the current situation about …….” but then after a couple of days they release another statement saying

  • We found out the …. was a bit harsh. (Fill in the blank however you like)
  • Monster life was too much. So we nerfed monster life.
  • Areas were bigger then we expected so we added checkpoint teleport system.
  • Minion life were getting out of hand so we lowered their life. (while I am trying to figure out how the hell i am supposed to keep my minions alive)
  • We shrank the colliders of player while dodging because we found out that it was not working properly. (??? What? You have never tried pressing space bar?)

and many more I can not think of at the moment.

Some are understandable but some are not. If you have played your game properly, you would know that rare monster minions fights take more than 10 seconds.

I genuinely do not believe that they are playing their game or even look at the numbers anymore because numbers do not make sense. My skill with 2 supports should not have lower DPS then my zero-supported auto attack.

I am not the best player out there to find which option is best for me in a single step so I like trying different skills, different supports, different weapons even. But the game is too hard and trying out new stuff is expensive, the loot drop has literally never been worse, I can not even find another Uncut level 10 to test the other skills so I try to guess and imagine “what would this skill look like if it was level 10 and with proper supports” because I can not afford to try another.

The point of early access in my opinion should be “Testing out everything and giving feedback” but GGG is literally doing the opposite and punish people with good game understanding because they have found something fun.

Last night I have died countless times to Act 2 boss (probably more than 20 times and another 10 in the map), but after, even though I have not updated my gear in any way or I have not cut any other gems (supports or others) and I have not speced any other passives in the tree, I had super easy game play in the first couple of Act 3 maps until the Bog Witch Fight and still managed to kill her after my third try. This clearly shows the game is out of balance, some areas are incredibly harder than others, but instead of finding more about these problems or listening players who are trying to help, they nerf heralds because we had a bit of fun without damaging the economy. I am saying that because I did not forget that almost for 10 days they ignored people duping items and disrupting the economy.

I kindly ask GGG to provide images/videos of “The ….. was out of hand we had to nerf” moments without several mirror tier builds. Or do a stream of playing their game with a clean account without the problems 90% of players have.

I want to say more but I do not want to take more of your time. I just wanted to share my feelings here. I hope I have not hurt anyone’s feelings. Thanks for reading.

PS: Also I am still mad because they have done nothing about Armour and Evasion problem, and I still have no idea why there is no Life nodes in tree.
