It’s low priority but GGG could we please get a quick QA pass on the Ritual Omen icons to make them consistent with their name & function

First of all, there are actually existing rules to the omen icons but a few seem to have been missed so it’s not consistent across the board. Practically, it’s just needlessly confusing trying to remember which is which when glancing at the icons. On a more trivial note, it also triggers the absolute hell out of the neat freak in me. I am sure this is a simple oversight and not at all intended, but please could these be fixed!

There are 3 basic icons used for omens:

A) Sinistral (prefix) = the half-disc/crescent shaped one

B) Dextral (suffix) = the V shaped one

C) Misc = the one shaped like a voodoo doll or something

  • the exalt, chaos and annul omen icons are all fine
  • the two Alchemy omens are wrong and need to be swapped (i.e. the disc shaped icon is being incorrectly used for the Dextral omen and vice versa)
  • the three red ones (2 regal and 1 Vaal) are also all mixed up. Sinistral Coronation should use A), Dextral Coronation should use B) and the Omen of Corruption should use C)
  • for the three blue non-currency omens (Amelioration etc.) they don’t apply to items so it would be nice if they got their own unique artwork like the 4 new omens, but not a huge deal either way

In addition to the icon shapes being partially mixed up, the colours of the icons themselves also need to be cleaned up. Current logic is:

Gold = Exalts

Green = Alchemy

Red = Regals + Vaals

Dark Purple = Chaos

Light purple = Annuls

Blue = misc non-currency types like the XP omen

I propose that each colour should be associated with only ONE currency type whenever possible for clarity:

  • Since alchemy orbs and exalts are both gold, it’s fine to keep exalt as gold and alchemy as green, no change required here.
  • Vaal orbs are red so keep the ‘Omen of Corruption’ red, simple.
  • Regals orbs are blue and yellow and since blue is already taken, a new pair of yellow icons could be used for the two regal ‘Omens of Coronation’. Maybe orange - either way it just needs to be distinct from the gold used in exalt omens
  • Dark purple can stay as is for the 3 chaos omens. However the two shades of purple are too similar so I propose a white recolour for the 3 annul omens to actually match Annulment orbs, it should look really nice. Or at least a more distinctive lighter shade of purple/pink.

That’s it for now, but with the 4 new omens and likely more coming over time I hope some kind of stricter standard could be implemented, for the more let’s say visually challenged among us. Despite running hundreds of rituals I still find myself having to hover and read the tool tip often. It should be more intuitive. Thanks.
