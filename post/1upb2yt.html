Corporate speak doesn’t work in professional sports.

If there’s any takeaway from that press conference….that is what it is.

This isn’t a boardroom and CNBC reporters. Constantly saying you’re not trying to defend yourself or be dismissive of people’s feelings eventually makes people feel like you’re trying to convince yourself of that. Like you’re reading from a proverbial script.

I think the general point and message is clear on why they traded Jaylen, but there was such an effort to not come off as insensitive to Jaylen or have a viral quote that they came off quite hokey. It came off quite hokey and insincere.

Sometimes trying to be or thinking you are—or being perceived as—the smartest guy in the room is going to turn people off. It’s going to feel abrasive. Talking to press, to the public, in a relatable and human way is the way to engender a more positive response and get your points across in a way that’s going to be digestible.

This organization hasn’t handled this off-season well and all the talk about optionality and PG’s contract doesn’t really lend one to believe their spin that winning is the first and predominant priority.
