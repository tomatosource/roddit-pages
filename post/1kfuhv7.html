Endgame lacks purpose

For the first time since playing D3 (which I deleted immediately) have I found myself thinking “why am I playing this?” and I think it comes down to lack of purpose in the game. There are no chase items, no power locked behind quests, unique drops are unusable low level base types, and mostly RNG crafting disappointment. It is a grind with no purpose. Never felt that way in the Ruthless grind because there was always something to chase whether it be finding new maps, completing the Atlas for passive points to spec into favourite content or completing Incursions for gems.

I’m still playing because I set my own goal but finding it harder and harder to login.

Endgame, “crafting” and unique drops needs a rethink. The gameplay loop needs more interaction, more quests and rewards and unique bosses to keep us engaged on the journey to the pinnacle bosses. And the pinnacle bosses need to be tied into the story somehow, they just feel tacked on.

Just spit balling some ideas that I think could add a bit of interest…

The Atlas starts corrupted and its our job to cleanse it or claim the corruption for ourselves and roleplay as an evil sort of conqueror of the Atlas with passive points to suit. Completing a tower as either a corrupted nexus (in the present time) or pre-corruption using Alva in the past as an incursion clears the corruption in the area for us to add our own mechanic and rewards Atlas points.

Renly the Smith could send us out to collect craft items, runes etc which he can forge into unique and powerful items. They could be in corrupted or cleansed maps with the reward depending on the state of the corruption.

Doryani gives you quests to search and locate items and people or hunt a boss that you can use to improve your character power.

Little milestones on the journey of the Atlas to keep us entertained and engaged and motivated. At the moment its disappointment after disappointment with the way uniques drop and crafting works and nothing really to keep motivated and push on.

Just my two cents not expecting it to get any airtime. I see the potential in PoE2 and want to no-life it and appreciate it’s EA and things are going to change.
