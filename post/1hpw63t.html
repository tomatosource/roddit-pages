Who’s a player you didn’t like but later learned to appreciate

Patrick Ewing. As a preteen I thought he was the standard slow ass lump of a man, the token basic 7 footer every team had who’s decent at basketball but owes his spot 80% to his size because he lived in the paint and close to the basket. I caught him late in his career. I eventually started watching him when younger, and thought he’d just be a 20% faster/springier version of his slow late 90’s self. Man do yourself a favor as a lover of this sport, check out some games if you can or bits on YouTube, from the early 90’s. No wonder he’s widely considered a Top 10 center (and there have been MANY great ones in 75 years).
