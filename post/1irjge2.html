The NBA does not understand their fanbase

After watching this weekend, here is all I think they know are our interests:

  • YouTube (Mr Beast, Kai Cenat, Jesser)
  • Rap music (the one thing they get right)
  • Soda
  • Kevin Hart?

Seems like their only concern is new fan acquisition. Hey NBA! Millions of people love basketball! If it wasn’t so painful to watch, more people would tune in. Just feels so out of touch with the reality of the NBA fans I know.

Instead, all they succeeded to do was piss the players off and get some 12 year olds to tune in to watch Mr Beast (maybe). I wish they would lean into their current fans that have supported the NBA over its history. We didn’t die!

All of us know the ratings problem is more of an access problem. And then they piss those of us who still manage to watch by extracting everything they can with sponsors.
