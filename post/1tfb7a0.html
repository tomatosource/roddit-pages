[Dwight Jaynes] What’s next for Terry Stotts? ‘I would love to return to the Blazers and Portland’

Terry Stotts has notified the Golden State Warriors he will. not be back as Steve Kerr’s No. 1 assistant coach next season. That opens the door to some speculation.

Would he be interested in returning to the Trail Blazers as the head coach?

“I would love to come back to the Blazers and Portland,” he told me via text Friday.

And not only that, Stotts confirmed that his agent, Warran LeGarie, has contacted the Trail Blazers.

New Trail Blazer owner Tom Dundon has made it known his preference is to pay in the neighborhood of $1-2 million a year for a coach, which is likely less than Stotts made as Kerr’s top assistant.

When I mentioned Dundon’s intentions to Stotts, he answered, “So I’ve heard.”
