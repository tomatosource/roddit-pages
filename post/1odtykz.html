If you like this game, do not farm trial of sekhemas…

Being a newborn dad, I don’t have much time to play, but man — I’ve been loving what 0.3.1 brought us. Farming maps for an hour at night, hearing that “ding” when a divine dropped… that alone could make my day. Sometimes I’d even hit something big — a drop worth 20 divines or more — and I’d be buzzing for days. I still remember when I got my first rare rune drop and ran to my wife yelling, “I’M RICH, BABY!”

I took my time and saved up to buy upgrades piece by piece. Each improvement felt earned and meaningful.

Then one night, I stumbled on a Reddit post saying Trial of Sekhemas was insanely profitable. I didn’t have many resources left, but I could afford a Desperate Alliance — so I gave it a shot.

At first, I barely broke even. But by the fifth run, I dropped a gem worth 90 divines. A few more runs later, a spectrum for 60 d. Then on my tenth run — a gem sold for 190 d and a Spectrum Gem worth another 57 d. Just like that, I was rich beyond anything I’d imagined. I could buy almost every best-in-slot item that originally I never dreamt to ever have.

But that’s also when I realized… I wasn’t having fun anymore. Map runs didn’t matter — they could never come close to the profit from Sekhemas. The excitement was gone.

So maybe that’s my cue to call it a season. Hopefully in future leagues, the loot balance between different content types will feel a bit closer.

Anyway… guess it’s time to fire up Hades 2 or that new Jurassic Park game.
