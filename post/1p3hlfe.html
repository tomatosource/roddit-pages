There needs to be a solution to the abomination that is NBA/WNBA reffing.

Sometimes what is and isn’t a foul changes mid-game. Sometimes calls are uneven. Apparently tackling JB isn’t a foul.

Suspect reffing affects everything. Player stats—and therefore compensation. Injuries. Watchability. And now, with the impact of sports betting, integrity of games.

I had an idea, and thought if was worth a damn, the NBPA could push for it. Standards enforcement for refs. Take it out of the league’s hands. No more Adam Silver seemingly making entertainment-driven decisions at half time. No more wondering if refs are corrupt.

(1) Every team designates a reviewer. A former player. A former ref. A former coach. A fan with autistic-like adherence to the Rules of Basketball (I volunteer as tribute).

(2) Every game is reviewed by 5 reviewers from teams other than those that played the game. Not just the last two minutes. The whole game. Score the refs on accuracy.

(3) Each season, the coaches set minimums: (a) to ref a game, (b) to be a crew chief, and © to ref in a post-season game. Maybe 65% is good enough to ref, but 75% for post-season and 80% to crew chief. Refs that fall below average are not eligible for that role.

This review would ensure that the rules agreed on are adhered to are enforced. Flopping techs? They’d start getting called. Landing zones? Protected. Refs literally on the take? They’d get caught.

I’m sure the NBRA would fight it. But ostensibly, if the league has integrity, it would be on board with something like this: transparency without additional burdens on league staff.

Thoughts?
