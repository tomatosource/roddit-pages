Jokic is the third player in NBA history to finish top two in MVP voting for five straight seasons

Jokic has been the winner or runner-up for the MVP award every year since 2021. Bill Russell (1958-1963) and Larry Bird (1981-1986) did it for six straight seasons each. (Russell had four wins, Bird had three).

Jordan, LeBron, and Kareem each were close but had one third place finish mixed in.
