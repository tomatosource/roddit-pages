The hot-cold delirium mini-game has to go

I can farm 200% deli all day with 4 corrupted tabs but I still fail the normal delirium maps constantly because this mechanic simply does not work.

You mixed some of the most convoluted and annoying maps in ARPG history with a forced speedrun mechanic that doesn’t even function. Any map that isn’t a straight line to the boss is a nightmare to navigate and stay inside the fog. The compass sends you into dead ends constantly and then going the right way requires leaving the fog, screwing your whole map since delirium is straight up worthless unless you can kill the boss for a grand mirror.

If you stop paying attention for literally 5 seconds while grinding you can accidentally leave the fog and brick your map as well. Like we really only get 4 seconds outside of the fog which has no visible perimeter at all before we fail? Did anyone even try playing this?

And I thought the whole idea with the map wipes on boss death revives was that we were supposed to full clear maps before we kill bosses, but now we have TWO different mechanics (ritual and delirium) that want you to always kill the boss first to get proper rewards. Which is it?

On top of all of that, the timer is bugged and only stops on content and bosses when it feels like it.

Please just give us a normal, working timer without a forced zone and make the mirror breaks extend it or something. The current incarnation makes zero sense at all with the layouts of the maps and leads to so many frustrating issues.
