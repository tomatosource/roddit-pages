Adam Silver is a terrible Commissioner for the NBA

The regular season feels less important than it used to. Star players sit out games more often because of load management, even during big matchups. Fans pay a lot of money to see these players, but the league rarely steps in.

Discipline and punishments do not always seem equal, and similar actions can lead to different consequences. Officiating is another ongoing problem, with missed calls and confusing decisions happening often. The league’s explanations usually do not satisfy fans, which makes people trust the system less.

The quality of basketball has declined as well. The game now relies heavily on three-point shots and drawing fouls, while defense and physical play are limited. Many games feel repetitive and less intense. While Adam Silver is good at managing the NBA’s image cough cough “Kawhi” “Cooper Flagg”, these issues suggest that the league has lost some of its competitive spirit under his leadership.

Promoting NBA betting. Many fans, especially young ones, are pushed to see betting as a normal part of watching games, even though it can lead to addiction, financial problems, and stress.
