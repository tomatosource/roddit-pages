Guild gameplay

ENG:

I’ve been wondering for a while now: what’s even the point of guilds in PoE2 if the devs don’t want to lean into multiplayer and PvP? Objectively speaking, things like guild wars, side quests, and actual guild gameplay would bring in a ton of players. But the devs are openly saying they won’t do it. So, what are guilds even for?

If it’s just for trading, you can easily find people in chat. And guild coins feel completely pointless — they’re literally 1:1 with regular points, and you can’t get them any other way except by converting your own money, so why do they even exist? Don’t get me wrong, I love the game and how the development is going, but I just don’t get why they refuse to give guilds some real love
