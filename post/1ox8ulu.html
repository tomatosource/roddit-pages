[Boudway -Bloomberg] NBA Reviews Timberwolves Owners’ Ties to Kalshi Sports Trading

SRC (paywall): https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-14/nba-reviews-timberwolves-owners-ties-to-kalshi-sports-trading?srnd=homepage-americas

A startup’s entry into prediction market trading is raising fresh questions about the involvement of NBA owners in sports betting.

The startup, Mojo Interactive Inc., was co-founded by Alex Rodriguez and Marc Lore, the owners of the NBA’s Minnesota Timberwolves and the WNBA’s Lynx. Mojo has started trading on the outcome of sports games on the prediction market exchange Kalshi, an arrangement that is now being reviewed by the NBA.

Mojo began trading event contracts tied to sports during the NFL season this fall, according to Mojo’s chief executive, Vinit Bharara. Mojo has yet to trade on any NBA games, Bharara said in an interview with Bloomberg.

Mojo was originally a “stock market but players instead of companies” then seem to go more on actual betting and now is just a backend service helping betting apps price their bets
