Limit testing - how atziri made pushing build limits necessary

The temple snake mechanic has been the first time I’ve enjoyed solo playing. Usually I rush league end and group play to get loot and it all feels a bit mundane, eventually I just get bored and stop league.

With the new temple mechanic I’ve been challenged in end game as I HAVE to upgrade my gear to be able to clear rooms easily which has actually been hard and as a result fun. Usually at end game I can easily blast t16 and arbiter content but the challenge in the temple is unmatched. Also the RNG progression of temple building adds a fun twist rather than spamming t15s and the same maps over and over.

It seems to me that the reward is commensurate with the effort it takes to build the temple, and with the build needed to clear it efficiently.

Really disappointed with this nerf as it was the most fun I’ve had in game yet.
