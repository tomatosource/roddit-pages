Scroll of Amnesia - Curreny Item for Free Respec

I would like to suggest a new currency item that makes a character forget their past, allowing a free respec. The Scroll of Amnesia can be found randomly throughout the world and relatively rare. This would give players added flexibility for encouraging different builds later in the game without costing a fortune to respec.

Scroll of Amnesia

  • Currency Item

  • Stack Size:1⁄40

  • Right click item to refund character’s passive skill points for free.
