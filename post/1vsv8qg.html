Determining waystone value easy mode

I make a lot of waystones trying to get T16s. I don’t usually use ritual omens to target stats due to cost. One thing I’ve always struggled with is sorting through them and determining which to keep, sell, or pass off to Doryani. It’s tedious and a lot of people don’t bother… but here’s my approach.

I dump them into a stash tab and search for the stats I want to run or maybe sell. For example:

waystone chance 120%+

monster rarity 60%+

effectiveness 40%+

Item rarity 60%+

After that I load up my inventory with what’s left and go to doryani and check the price. If the waystone price is under 3k gold it’s probably junk so sell to doryani. If it’s above 3.5k gold it’s going to be 8 affix. Anything in between is probably usually 7 affix and could be decent (hybrid effectiveness/packsize for example) so I’ll usually list those with ange at 150ex or run them depending on what content I’m doing.
