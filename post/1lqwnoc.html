What summer league team had the most NBA talent

With the summer league coming up soon I was thinking about some of my favorite summer league teams, and it got me thinking which Summer League teams had the most NBA talent. Most summer league teams probably have 3-4 rotational NBA guys and maybe a few guys who are in and out of the league. The team didn’t necessarily have to be a good summer league team, but just a team that later went on to have a bunch of successful NBA players.

The first one I thought of was the 2017 Lakers summer league team with Brandon Ingram, Ivica Zubac, Kyle Kuzma, Lonzo, Josh Hart, Alex Caruso, Thomas Bryant, David Nwaba, and PJ Dozier.
