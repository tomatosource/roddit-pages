The Celtics lost every “finals preview” game last year as well

I’m not really sure where this sentiment is coming from involving us not being as good as OKC or whoever over a regular season loss. So far, the teams that people generally consider to be competition for the celtics are the cavs and thunder. We’re 1-2 against those teams this season while missing JB and DWhite in that second cavs game, one in which we blew late. However, the celtics didn’t exactly win these types of “finals preview” games last year either. Last year, the nuggets, twolves, and thunder were the teams that people thought had a chance against us and could potentially beat us in a series. There were no eastern conference teams people thought had a chance after it became evident the bucks couldn’t defend YMCA players. Against those opponents, we got swept by the nuggets, split with the twolves, and lost our one real game to the thunder (the win late in the season without SGA doesn’t count) combining for a 1-4 record.

The only difference between the celtics and cavs/thunder is the record. We’ve simply lost to more garbage teams than they have because our guys are coasting and those two teams have a chip on their shoulder against the league’s trash. Like seriously, does blowing out a bunch of garbage teams in january do anything to prove we would fare better against the elite ones come playoff time?
