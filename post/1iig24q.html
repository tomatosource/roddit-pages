Something Crazy We “Technically” Can Do

Imagine getting the best player we possibly could get this trade deadline AND get under the 2nd apron, without losing a single rotation player?

Technically, we could do all of that. Probably not realistic, but it’s also not out of the realm of possibility either. Is it worth it? Well, also probably not, but hear me out.

The 2nd Apron

With Springer gone, we are 3.625 mil above the 2nd apron. I think the cheapest pro-rated min player we could sign would be 2 weeks from now and we could sign them at $380,997. That means for every player we trade, we have to tack on that extra amount in order to sign a rookie replacement player, (Davison, Peterson, Watson, etc.)

Scheierman + Tillman = 4.6mil. That’s a 1 mil difference between the 3.625mil we are over the 2nd apron and covers the 2 players we’d need to sign to replace them. Can we dump Scheierman + Tillman?

Incomplete roster charges don’t come into play unless we dip below 12 players. So, sign a replacement for Springer now and then dump these 2.

Boom!!! Below the 2nd apron and can aggregate players.

Most Coveted Player: Walker Kessler

Since we are below the 2nd apron now, we can aggregate players. Not only that, but you can aggregate 2 min players, as long as they are the only players being traded.

Kessler = 2.9 mil

Walsh + Queta = 3.9mil

Another 1 million is saved and you can safely acquire 2 replacement players. We are sending out more than we receive, so we stay below the 2nd apron.

Money Overview

  • Total outgoing: 8.5mil

  • Total incoming: 4.43mil

  • Total saved: 4.07mil

  • Room below the 2nd apron: 0.47mil

How much is it worth to get Walker Kessler and get below the 2nd apron:

  • Scheierman + Tillman + Queta + Walsh + 2 firsts, (likely the draft cost) + 2 2nds (likely the cost to dump Scheierman + Tillman)

Conclusion

Technically we can get under the 2nd apron and acquire Walker Kessler. Should we? Probably not, but it was a fun exercise for me at least

Edit:

I don’t think it matters what players are sent in what order. So you can dump Tillman + Queta instead of Scheierman and you can make Shceierman part of the trade. The only important piece is getting under the 2nd apron AFTER the trade. You can rearrange the money how you want, as long as that is true.
