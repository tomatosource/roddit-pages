Draymond Green has passed Anthony Mason for the 7th most technical fouls in NBA history

After getting two quick techs in the second quarter against the Jazz tonight Draymond Green has passed Anthony Mason for 7th on the all time technical foul leaderboards.

Draymond has just recently passed Russell Westbrook to become the active leader in technical fouls and he has now gotten his 207th and 208th career technical fouls taking him all the way to 7th.

If Draymond wants any chance of moving up the list he’s going to need 3 more to pass Dwight Howard and 60 more techs after that to pass Rodman and enter the top 5.
