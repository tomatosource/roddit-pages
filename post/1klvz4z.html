Let Solo Players Scale Content Like Parties. The Current Loot System Punishes Us and Encourages Exploits.

Path of Exile 2 heavily rewards party play with more loot and xp. But if you play solo, there’s no way to scale content to match that. Even if you’re capable of handling party-level difficulty, you get stuck with worse returns.

The system is so skewed that people are running public parties asking for leechers. Players who join, do nothing, don’t loot, don’t attack, just inflate the scaling so the host can farm better rewards. It’s not just cheesy, it’s broken.

This isn’t a complaint about people grouping up. It’s about locking the most rewarding content behind an arbitrary party requirement. If a solo player wants to take on party-level content, they should be able to opt into it and earn the same loot.

Do others feel this way? Is GGG ever going to address it?
