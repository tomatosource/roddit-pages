Why armor often ends up low priority in builds (with some quick math)

Armor’s mitigation formula in PoE2 is:

0.1 × armor / (0.1 × armor + damage)

At first glance, it seems simple—stack more armor, take less damage. But it leads to some weird scaling behavior when enemy damage goes up.

Let’s say an enemy deals physical damage and gets a +40% more modifier. If you have 0 armor, you just take 40% more damage—straightforward.

But if you had armor equal to 30x the original hit, that same modifier now results in a 78% increase in actual damage taken. Why? Because increasing the incoming damage reduces the effectiveness of your armor non-linearly.

These “more” damage multipliers show up frequently—on rare mob mods, map mods, and so on. Which means the more you try to scale armor to survive, the less payoff you get when the damage spikes.

That’s one of the big reasons armor often ends up being an afterthought in build planning. It’s not useless—but the return on investment can drop off fast in real content.
