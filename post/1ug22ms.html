What keeps you playing after your character can already clear all content?

So right now I’m at the point where my character can pretty easily clear basically everything I’m doing in the game. And honestly, that’s where I’m starting to lose motivation a bit. It’s not really that I hate the game or anything. I actually want to keep playing, especially since the league is still fresh. But I’m struggling to find a real goal now. Like, I can upgrade my gear more, make my character even stronger, get more damage, more defenses, more efficiency etc… but for what exactly?

If the result is just clearing the same content slightly faster, or farming more loot so I can buy better gear to again clear the same content faster, it starts to feel kind of meaningless. I usually enjoy the grind when there is something difficult ahead of me. Some boss, some content wall, some chase goal where I feel like “okay, I need to improve my character to beat this.” But right now I don’t really feel that. There isn’t anything left that makes me think I need to push my build further. So I’m curious, what do you guys do at this stage? Do you just keep farming for currency? Try new builds? Push for perfect gear? Chase rare drops? Or do you normally take a break once there is no content left that feels like a real goal?
