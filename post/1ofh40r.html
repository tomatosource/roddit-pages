Thoughts on Celtics through two games

Both Queta and Tillman are doing a solid job rebounding and on offense. The younger players are showing a lot of grit too and I’m excited for us to develop Gonzales and Minott. Both games were against higher tier eastern conference teams and there were moments in both games where we had an upper hand before our offense went dry. I think we need Tatum and another piece(likely through an anfernee Simon trade) that can give quality offensive production to prevent dry periods in our scoring attack. Defense still looks really good btw except for a few stretches.
