Rikuu Nishida: The Chicago White Sox’ Red Headed Stepchild.

For the life of me, I cannot figure out why the Chicago White Sox denied Rikuu Nishida a Spring Training invite yet again this year. He’s a 24 year old 2B/OF who has a decent glove.

In 2024 Nishida started the season in A ball. He hit .290 with a .414 OBP while leading the Carolina League in runs (84) despite only playing in 90 games. In those 90 games he stole 35 bases and got caught 8 times. More importantly, he walked 66 times and struck out 51 times.

He moved up to A+ ball for 26 games and AA for 11 games. At A+ he hit .337 with a .436 OBP. In that span he scored 20 R, stole 10 bases (caught 3 times) and walked 16 times compared to 13 strikeouts. At AA he hit .333 with a .400 OBP. In that span he scored 10 R, stole 4 bases (caught 1 time) and walked 4 times compared to 0 strikeouts.

In 2025 he played a full season at AA in the Southern League where he hit .273 with a .403 OBP. He finished 2nd in the league with 72 R and once again had more BB (75) than K (69). He was a little less stealthy on the basepaths with 40 SH and 12 CS.

Is this not someone you want to even TRY and create a spark at the top of your lineup? The Sox are a shit team and their leadoff hitters combined for 79 runs, .240 AVG, .325 OBP last year. Why not at least TRY it?
