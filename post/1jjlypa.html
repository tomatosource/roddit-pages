The NBA regular season is even more indicative of playoff success than people say - in all of NBA history, the 4-8 seeds (and I guess we can now include 9 and 10) have won a combined two championships. Those occurred in 1969 and 1995.

For all of the talk about coasting in the regular season for the last 10-15 years, teams that don’t win in the regular season don’t win with basically no exceptions.

The only teams to do it happened to both be defending champions, the 4 seeded 1969 Celtics (led by Russell in his last year) and the 6 seeded 1995 Rockets (led by Hakeem)
