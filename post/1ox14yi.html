Baseball by the Rules: A Website

About a year ago, I started on a little project. The project started with a question: “how would you explain baseball to someone who was completely unfamiliar with it?” As I mulled it over, I realized that you can break a lot of what happens in the game itself into a kind of decision tree. The pitcher throws a pitch: does the batter swing, or not? If they don’t, is the ball a strike or a ball? If it’s a strike, is the batter out? Or, maybe the batter does swing: do they hit the ball, or not? If they hit the ball, where does it wind up?

The end result of all of this mapping? Baseball By the Rules, a website where you walk through a game, chooseable-path-style, and learn about how everything works along the way. Along the way, you’ll see examples of individual plays or outcomes, served up by MLB’s extremely robust (yet, strangely balk-free) video site. The site covers a good 99% of what you’ll see in the average baseball game. I’ve seen a lot of “hey, I’m new to baseball, what’s the deal with _____?” posts in the past few weeks, so my hope is that anyone who needs an intro course on baseball can dig into this site. Even if you know ball, you might learn something, here! Or, maybe you can just kill time during a slow work day. That’s cool, too.

Enjoy!
