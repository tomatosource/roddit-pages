3 Pointer complaints have reached a fever pitch because it’s the most convenient ratings scapegoat for the NBA

The top 5 most discussed reason for ratings decline in no particular order:

  • Too many 3s

  • Too many ads and stoppages in play

  • Inconsistent and poor refs

  • Bad and disjointed viewing options (platforms, times, cost, blackouts)

  • Ineffective marketing

4 of those 5 can be blamed primarily on NBA leadership. The increase in 3PM, on the other hand, deflects blame away from the league office onto teams, tactics, and the vague evolution of the game.

As a result, whenever announcers, half-time shows, or other NBA affiliates discuss ratings, the focus is overwhelmingly on 3 pointers to avoid biting the hand that feeds them. People rarely hear the game feed discussing blackouts or incessant ads or league wide poor officiating, they hear about 3s. Any existing perception viewers have of 3s as the root of the ratings problem then becomes reinforced and amplified. Millions of viewers then carry that effect over to social media where echo chambers can further the momentum
