Is this the crafting strategy everyone uses?

I watched a lot of youtube videos and this is the main crafting strategy that is basically used for most of the crafts.

  • buy blue bases with a hard to get T1 mod - 20 div per base, you need 3 in avg
  • (you can also get white bases and perfect aug/transmute/annul)
  • 2 x exalt, 1 x desecration
  • fracture and try to hit your mod - 12 div per fracture orb, you need 3 in avg

–&gt; milestone 1: fractured base (around 100 div until here)

  • chaos spam until you get another desired mod (around 40 div usually)

–&gt; milestone 2: 2 mod item (around 140 div until here)

  • use an essence for third (medicore) mod
  • desecrate with ancient materials + annul with omen of lights (15 div per try, let’s say you need 4 tries here)

–&gt; milestone 3: 4 mod item (around 200 div until here)

Now you can either just exalt and pray, or work with whittling/sinistral annulment/dextral annulment. This costs insane amounts of money, since each of this omens is 10-15 divs.

So in my calculation, I calculate around 200 div for 4 good/perfect mods and somewhere around 300-400 div for 5-6 good/perfect mods. This is also what I see in the market (for bows at least).

What do you think about these numbers?

I personally would wish that some of the omens just would be a lot cheaper or that we get more crafting materials for mid-budget crafting.
