PSA for late starters that need currency

You can sell level 1 normal wands (such as the one witch starts with) for 100ex, level 3 wands (if there are any at the vendor) for 50 ex, and level 1 uncut gems (from the chest in clearfell) for 100ex. You can’t sell the green uncut gem that you get for entering town, but if you cut it into a movement skill (boneshatter or whirling slash) you might be able to sell that gem. If you farm up enough stock it might be possible to sell it from a non-instant tab while leveling (its usually easier to sell stuff like this in bulk), otherwise you can list it in an merchant tab once you get to act 4. Also if you happen to find a level 8 or 9 uncut spirit gem (it’s unlikely, but it could happen) those are worth over a div.
