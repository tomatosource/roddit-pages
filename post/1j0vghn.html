The Lakers are 15-3 in their last 18 games and are legit championship contenders

I went back and looked to see if it had anything to do with schedule as in season sample size can be a bit misleading

The fact is that the 3 losses are to Jazz, Hornets, and 76ers (without Embiid)

In the meantime, during this stretch, they beat Celtics, Nuggets, Knicks and several other playoff caliber teams

The rosters have changed, but AD had also missed a good number of the games and since the trade, Luka has been off to a slow start (shooting wise).

The Lakers keep on winning however and ALOT has to do with their massive improvements on defense. They have been the best defensive team during this stretch and with Vando returning, trade for DFS, and other solid defenders contributing, the team looks great.

I have to give a lot of credit to JJ Reddick for his emphasis on defensive schemes and they looked solid even when AD was out. Heck, even Luka has been pretty solid on defense in his short Lakers stint thus far.

If they can keep up the defensive intensity and Luka improves his shooting (which would be expected), I don’t see why they aren’t championship contenders.

There is only 1 team in the West that has potential to beat Lakers in a 7 game series and that’s OKC but even then, other than SGA, I think Lakers lock the rest of the team down. Boston would be a tough matchup but Luka will have LeBron as his partner this time around. Kyrie is great but really struggled in the finals against the best perimeter defense. JT, JB, DW, and JH is just unfair and as great as Kyrie is, there were no favorable matchups for him to exploit.
