Playing Warrior in endgame as a ‘casual’

I’ve never used meta builds and I just go with the flow, find some fun skills, abilities or uniques and try to build around them. Theorycrafting is the best part of the game for me. Last league I mostly played as a Lich with some kind of minion build and it was fun and smooth. I easily did some T3 bosses and rarely died on T15 juiced maps – it was simple for me: spirit for minions, ES and res for defenses. With 10k ES you don’t need to be very careful with your gameplay.

This league I started as a Warrior…

And I really don’t know what devil gave me this idea. I spent 25 hours on the campaign but that’s fine; the horror started on T13–T15 maps. I was just dying before I could even swing my mace. I had no idea what to do, because I did the normal stuff and it was not working.

Of course, after this I read a lot of posts about armor in the game and I have no idea why it is implemented like this. I saw that I had like 83% armor and max res and it gave me nothing – now it’s clear, because 10–12k armor is basically glass cannon – but you still invest a lot of passive nodes into defense and you have no damage…

Anyway, I wanted to thank this community – after some of your posts and comments plus my “casual” testing, right now I ended up with 93lvl Smith of Kitava:

  • 32k armor (approx 36k with buffs)
  • 53k applying to elemental damage
  • 32k (100%) applying to chaos damage
  • 89% max res (overcapped elemental to 120–130%)
  • 157 life regen (approximately 300 with consecrated ground)
  • immune to damaging ailments
  • 30% of physical damage taken as fire
  • defend with 200% of armor against critical strikes

Now I can finally play a tank and not some squishy glass cannon without damage.

I can tank this awful kamehameha, stand in front of it, and bonk the abyss monster on the head.
I can tank the big Gorilla hit with Fortifying Cry.
I can jump into every group of monsters and survive long enough to kill them.
I can even stand in those ground AOE effects for a few seconds and still have time to react.

I’m literally not dying. But it cost me a lot of frustration and hours of testing and reading about the game outside the game.

So, I’m in no position as a casual to suggest how to improve armor; I can only ask that the game doesn’t lie to players on the character sheet. If I have 10k armor, just show the real percentage of damage reduction. Or, if you want to go for some soulslike/roguelite design, at least give me some logs from my deaths – we need to know what to improve before the next map, instead of blindly trying every possible solution.

I love melee characters and I’m waiting for Templar and Duelist (Gladiator?), but I’m afraid that stacking armor + ES/evasion will be awful and not work at all. I still don’t know how people play Druid and don’t get wiped on T15 juiced maps.

If you have some stories about your armor characters, feel free to share them. Thanks again for all your tips.
