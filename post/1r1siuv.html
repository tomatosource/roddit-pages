Poe2 Has a Light Radius Problem

It’s so much more apparent at lower levels. Act 1 is the worst offender, but it’s kind of unbelievable that my character needs to hug every point of interest just to see it.

Why do I have to almost step on a waypoint to activate it? Why do I need to be so close to an entrance for it to be marked on the minimap? It doesn’t make any sense in-game that my character is as blind as a mole and can’t see an entrance to a new zone unless his nose is practically touching it.

The (beautiful) cluster of assets makes it so hard to see any important points of interest, and the huge maps don’t help.

TLDR: GGG needs to triple the player base light radius.

edit: it’s so small that the checkpoints at the beginning of the temple can fail to activate if you walk past it xdd
