Unusual shooting splits for the season: Amen Thompson is currently shooting 80.3% from the line (106-132) yet only 19.7% from 3 (13-66).

He’s bumped his FT percentage 12 points from the last two years (he shot 68.4% both years) and usually it’s agreed that a player’s FT’s percentage is a good indicator of he is currently or will become a good shooter in the future.

I can’t recall a player that’s had such a drastic drop off between three and free throw percentage and don’t know how to interpret it. Yes the season is still relatively early but I’m encouraged to see Amen not only improve his FT percentage, but also not shy away from taking threes during games, even if they don’t go in because they’re still winning.
