Invulnerable Amanamu monsters need to go

I honestly don’t understand what these mobs are supposed to add to the game.

Abyss encounters are already chaotic enough, but whenever the invulnerable Abyss snake monsters show up, the whole thing goes from “challenging” to “why am I even bothering?”. One of them is annoying. Several spawning at once is just absurd.

I keep trying to understand what the intended gameplay is here, because I’m clearly missing something. Is there some mechanic I’m supposed to interact with? Some counterplay? Some decision I’m expected to make? Because from my perspective, they just sit there, spam damage, clutter the screen, and refuse to die for what feels like an eternity.

Difficulty is fine. Dangerous enemies are fine. But enemies that are actively attacking you while being effectively immortal don’t really create engaging gameplay. They just create downtime and frustration.

What makes it even stranger is that Abyss encounters are designed around keeping up momentum. You’re clearing waves, chasing the cracks, trying to maintain the pace. Then these things show up and suddenly you’re stuck running circles around immortal mobs waiting for the game to decide they’re allowed to die. That’s not difficulty. That’s just waiting.

Maybe there’s some brilliant design philosophy behind this that I’m failing to appreciate, but right now they feel less like an interesting mechanic and more like a reminder that “invulnerable enemies are annoying” has been a fairly universal lesson in ARPGs for a long time.

I’d genuinely like to know what purpose they’re supposed to serve, because at the moment they seem to exist solely to turn otherwise enjoyable Abyss encounters into a tedious exercise in patience.

Please either redesign them or remove the invulnerability entirely. Challenging enemies are fun. Enemies that spend half the encounter refusing to participate in the “kill monsters” part of an ARPG are not.
