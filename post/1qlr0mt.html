Ja Morant will miss the next 3 weeks with a UCL Sprain

Link: https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/basketball/news/grizzlies-ja-morant-will-miss-at-least-three-weeks/

Morant sustained a UCL sprain in his left elbow in the Jan. 21 game against the Hawks, and while he’s expected to make a full recovery, he’ll be re-evaluated in approximately three weeks. This means Morant won’t return to action until probably after the All-Star break, impacting his potential trade value ahead of the Feb. 5 deadline. With Morant on the sidelines, the Grizzlies should turn to Cam Spencer and Vince Williams, although Ty Jerome (calf) should also see minutes at the point once he’s fully healthy and ready to play.
