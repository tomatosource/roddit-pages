Keep limited respawns for bosses/pinnacle, but remove the XP loss

As the title says, removing XP loss from pinnacle bosses will allow people to not have to ‘wait’ to start a boss fight after leveling up, which becomes especially terrible at level 95+.

Keep the limited respawns, it is enough of a loss to lose your resources to enter the fight. Losing your experience after 3 deaths in a row is terrible. And I do not mean remove experience loss alltogether, just during the pinnacle content with respawns.
