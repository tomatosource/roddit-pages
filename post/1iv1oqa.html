PS5 purchase issues - still having problems

The night that the patch went live for the Map Stash Tab, I went to the Playstation Store and purchased 100 points to get enough currency to buy the tab. After 30 or so minutes, it had not arrived in-game. I waited a couple of hours, restarted the game multiple times, and it still never showed up. I contacted Sony and got a refund for the purchase.

I then tried to purchase the same 100 point pack again, thinking that maybe it was a one time error with Sony, and it did the same thing. Money came out of the account, got the e-mail showing the transaction happened, but I never got the points.

From checking here on Reddit, I tried the following things:

  • Going to the Points tab in-game, which forced the client to check against the server
  • restarting the game multiple times
  • uninstalling and reinstalling the game
  • installing the original Path of Exile and going to the Points menu there
  • restoring licenses for both PoE1 and Poe2

Finally, after 5 days of troubleshooting and trying different things, I contacted Sony again and asked for another refund. They were more than happy to issue it since it had only been a few days and they confirmed that it wasn’t working.

I had also e-mailed GGG and asked them if they knew what was going on, and I received a response that just said “I can confirm that this is a known issue that is currently being investigated.”

Has anyone else had purchase issues through Playstation Store since the 16th? Or is this just an issue isolated to me? Are there any other suggestions for how to purchase points and get the Maps Stash Tab?
