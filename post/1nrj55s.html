A lot of big assumptions are being made about how the new tablet mechanics will interact with previously-juiced nodes

I have seen at least a half-dozen posts here and on the official forums from people who are investing what seems like an awful lot of time, effort, and in some cases currency to “prepare” for 0.3.1 by pre-buffing Atlas nodes so they can buff them again after the patch using the new tablet mechanics.

Unless I have missed a follow-up from GGG, These are the facts they have communicated so far:

  • Existing Tablets that have been applied to your Atlas will remain and you will not lose any value due to these changes
  • ...the ceiling for the amount of content you can apply to a single Map has been lowered

Neither of these indicates that we will be able to re-apply precursor buffs to nodes that already have them. All this says is that nodes with precursor buffs in place prior to the patch will not lose those buffs, even if they exceed the new lower ceiling. Yet I’ve already seen people claiming that if GGG doesn’t allow us to buff those nodes again that they will have “gone back on their word.”

As I’m reading these two bullets they’re saying to balance the fact that you can now apply 3 tablets to 100% of maps, the total precursor buffs capable of existing on any given map has been capped below where it was previously. To me that says that at best we can expect to be able to buff nodes that are below the new ceiling, but if people are currently “prepping” nodes with 12-15 tablets each that’s very unlikely to be the case and I’m afraid they’re going to be pretty unhappy (if I’m right) when the patch drops and they realize that all they’ve done is locked in those previous buffs.
