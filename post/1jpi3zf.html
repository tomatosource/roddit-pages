Amazon - Penetrate Ascendancy Node Math’s

Hi All,

Looking at the info on POE2DB and trying to decide how much value Penetrate has, as its one of the only ascendancy nodes in the game that actually require you to craft a specific mod on your weapon - accuracy.

The node takes 25% of weapons local accuracy rating and gives it as added physical dmg.

So taking POE2DB info on modifiers on a helix spear base, and comparing a perfect rolled Phys spear with hybrid vs Phys spear with Accuracy, I noted the following:
* +-30% increase in minimum dmg
* 5% increase in maximum dmg
* 15% increase in average dmg

Note this is subject to change when the patch comes out, but can give us some general guidelines.
Some things to consider for the patch too:
Runes are getting tiers, so depending how much increase higher tier iron and vision runes get if the % increase is different between them it could change results.

Corruptions:
% Phys corruption can roll 15-25% while accuracy can roll 50-100.
% Phy corruption can get 25% increase value to iron rune (25% vs 20% = 5% which is 25% increase) while accuracy roll is equal to a vision rune - This means that the % increase on a perfect corrupted item will drop slightly lower than 15%
