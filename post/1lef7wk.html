Patch 0.2.1 - Improved Quality of Life Changes. Thoughts?

Patch 0.2.1 has been out for a few days now, and frankly, it feels pretty solid so far. The gameplay actually satisfies me.

First off, the passive tree now has a one-click reset button for all passive points. Combined with the new search function (looking for key words like “strength”, those relevant nodes will be highlighted), respeccing and tweaking BDs is now literally like a breeze. This function was previously only for controller players, but now keyboard&mouse users like me also get it. Props GGG for eventually hearing us out!!

And another significant change is definitely the Unique drops. After grinding for over 10 hours straight recently, I’ve found way fewer trash Uniques, while the rare ones are more WILLING to drop. Just based on my own experience, it feels every boss or rogue can potentially drop 2 Uniques now (that 3x buff of rogue seems legit).

All in all, this patch delivers a solid sense of achievement - definitely worth the wait for polishing the game.
