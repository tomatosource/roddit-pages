Why is the SORC gameplay so forced? Infusions are boring

I want to start off by saying the act 4 / interludes were great. The offline trading is easily the best thing about this patch.

I’d love to hear how other sorc players are doing, but for me, my fingers hurt trying to play the Sorc in endgame.

We have to do combo after combo to generate infusions, get that on our ascendancy too (4 points btw), walk in to pick up infusions so we can use them for 1 cast per infusion? What?

I’m very bored already, and I see no light at the end of this tunnel. It demotivates me to keep grinding for currency knowing I just have to play this piano the whole game.

Definitely going to just play deadeye or some simple meta build next league, probably wont experiment again.
