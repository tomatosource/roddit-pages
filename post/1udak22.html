We have no right to be mad if JB requests a trade

If Jaylen Brown requests a trade, I honestly have no reason to be mad at him.

If you’re Jaylen and you hear that you were going to be traded away from the franchise you literally dropped stats that put you in top 5 MVP ladder conversations, what message are you supposed to take away from it? That you’re the cornerstone of the franchise until a bigger name becomes available?

What makes this even crazier is that if Jaylen ends up getting traded now, there’s a real chance the return is worse than what Celtics fans would have accepted in a Giannis deal. That’s going to age horribly. We spent years saying the championship window was built around Tatum and Brown. They finally win a title together, make multiple deep playoff runs, and now we’re talking about splitting them up instead of riding it out and letting them define the next era of Celtics basketball.

And can we talk about how hypocritical this subreddit is being right now? Yesterday half the comments were basically begging Brad Stevens to pull the trigger if Giannis became available. People were posting mock trades every five minutes and talking about how “you have to do it for a top-3 player.” Now suddenly everyone has always loved Jaylen Brown, would never trade him, and can’t believe his name was mentioned.

Pick a side. Either trading Brown for Giannis was a reasonable basketball discussion or it wasn’t. But acting outraged today after spending all week fantasizing about the trade is ridiculous.

If Brown feels disrespected by all this, I can’t really blame him. The second his name hit the rumor mill, Celtics fans proved just how conditional that love can be.
