How could I even play any other arpg after this?

I’m only in act 1, but I’m in awe. Everything in this game is so, so much higher quality than any other in the genre. Skill feel and looks, the twitch stick style wasd movement, the enemy variety and design, holy shit how many cool unique enemies are only in the first few zones?! And don’t get me started on the bosses, there’s just no comparison with other games.

I bought Grim Dawn on the sale with Poe 2 because I was hyped for the expansion but I might just refund it, I found peak arpg and I don’t think there’s a way back. I’m not even sure if I can go back to Project Diablo 2.

The only thing that I could probably stomack is Poe 1 if they ever add wasd, but still everything is just so, so high quality in Poe 2 even compared to 1, which I played for hundreds of hours.
