Jalen Brunson is having a terrible series

13 assists and 13 turnovers, got into foul trouble and had to sit a long time last night, and shooting a dreadful 46.3% True Shooting with his 82 points on 81 shots.

For context of how bad that is, Jayson Tatum, who has notoriously been referred to as inefficient and carried by his super team, has never had a single playoff series with a TS% that low. His lowest ever came in 2019 against Milwaukee where he shot 46.4% when he was 20 years old in his 2nd season. In his 2 finals appearances he had 48.1% in 2022 against Golden State and 50.5% in 2024 against Dallas. Both not great, but not as bad as Brunson has been despite the Knicks having a 2-1 lead in the series.
