RJ Luis

So, everyone knows the story. Luis was the best player in the Big East last year leading St. John’s to the tourney and one of their best seasons in a long, long time. Then in the tourney against Arkansas he shit the bed hard, 3-17 shooting which obviously get in his head causing defensive flubs and a general defensive malaise which was so evident Pitino benched his in crunch time while the game was still winnable. One can’t help but think this performance, the benching and Luis blaming Pitino for not coaching him up contributed greatly to him going undrafted. So, the question is did we pick up a slob or an unpolished gem who’s best basketball is ahead of him?
