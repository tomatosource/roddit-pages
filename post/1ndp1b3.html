The Hornets have removed “Sports Betting Night” from their promotional calendar on their website. It had been scheduled for the March 17 appearance of Terry Rozier and the Heat. It no longer is happening on that date.

Previously, “Sports Betting Night” was scheduled on their website for March 17, but now that entire date has been removed. They are set to play Terry Rozier and the Miami Heat that day.

Source: https://www.nba.com/hornets/news/hornets-unveil-theme-nights-for-2025-26-season

For those unaware, Terry Rozier famously had a betting scandal, only uncovered earlier this year:

In March 2023, a professional bettor placed 30 wagered “unders” on Terry Rozier’s performance in a Hornets game—each bet won after Rozier exited early due to a foot issue—sparking a U.S. federal investigation. The NBA found no rule violation and Rozier has not been charged or accused of wrongdoing.
