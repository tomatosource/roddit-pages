Garzilla Numbers last 9 games(re-insertion into rotation)

Since Joe put Garza back into the rotation the Celtics are #3 in OFF REB%, #4 in DEF REB%, and #3 in REB%.

The Celtics are rebounding a pretty staggering 59% of available rebounds with Garza on the court in this stretch which includes about 42% of offensive rebounds(Stevens Adams territory).

Garza is also only allowing about 60% at the rim during this stretch which is not an elite number but he was around 70% by the time he first got benched. 70% is a layup line, 60% is “good job, good effort”.

Seems like Cs are also doing a better job of protecting that weak spot on defense. Hugo and White are blanketing guys when they are out there and when Garza gets put in the action, there seems to be a Bat Signal that alerts help defenders.

His screen setting, rolling, shooting touch have all been really awesome. Wasn’t a fan to start the season but consider me a Garza fan now.

He’s got good hands to catch the ball on the move, keep it high and finish with his momentum going towards the basket. His 3Ball is a nice value add, defenses don’t guard him so it’s not bringing an absurd amount of value but some found money 3 pt FGs are never bad. His touch inside and at the line tells me he’s at least a decent shooter.
