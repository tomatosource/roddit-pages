We now know with very high likelihood when 0.5 teasers, reveal stream & launch will happen

GGG has always been very consistent in their marketing for new leagues, it almost always follows the same 3 week pattern of: 2 weeks before launch week we get a teaser, 1 week before launch we get a full stream reveal and then the week afterwards is the league launch. The only time this hasn’t been true is when leagues have gotten delayed.

A few weeks ago GGG posted the following post stating that the 0.5 full reveal will happen at the end of April and since we received no teaser yesterday (Thursday 16th) we now approximately know when teasers will start and when the launch will happen.

GGG’s statement:

Towards the end of April we will be announcing our full plans for the 0.5.0 update including all the changes we will be making to the endgame as well as the new league! It’s a pretty huge update, so stay tuned.

This leaves only 2 more possible dates in April for the reveal stream: the 23rd and 30th of April. Since we didn’t get a teaser yesterday on the 16th this means the 23rd of April is not the reveal stream date.

This means the only valid date left based on GGG’s official post and usual marketing schedule is then April 30th as the 0.5 reveal date & stream per the previous GGG statement. This also means that teasers should start on the 23rd of April and the launch of 0.5 would fall on the 7th of May (unless the league gets delayed).

TL:DR: Based on the recent end of april announcement and how GGG usually conducts their marketing for new leagues & teaser season the following has a very high likelihood of applying for 0.5 unless the entire league gets delayed:

23rd of April - Teasers start

30th of April - 0.5 Reveal Stream

8th of May - 0.5 League Launch
