It’s not just that so many maps have maze-like layouts, it’s also that you can’t choose your maps in any way so you can’t pick the ones you memorized the best

Many maps with mazes still often have pretty decent traversals to navigate the map with minimal hassle, to avoid dead ends and minimize retracing steps.

In poe2 there’s no way to select your maps at all - besides expanding the atlas and simply not running the ones you don’t like, which is a very weak form of “map selection”.

I get that full-on map selection in poe1, so you can do 1000 strands in a row, is not supposed to exist in poe2 and tbh i support that.

But maybe there could be some kind of psuedo/stronger map selection, like terraforming a tower area into a specific biomes to get a chance at number of maps you like, or a tablet that terraforms a map into one of a list of maps built into the affix “terraforms the map into a Bastille, Sump, Vaal City, or Cenotes”.

As a sidenote i agree with the ppl saying that biomes are fucked right now. Biomes are not meaningful when the biome changes every since map. There needs to be vast mountain ranges, endless swamps, deep forests - biomes should at random be at least half the size of tower area or something.

Stronger map choice =&gt; more useful becoming an expert in some particular maps =&gt; less negative impact of mazes as you can learn them for 10-20 maps but probably not 100s. At least not with my unc brain
