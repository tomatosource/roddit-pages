Why did Washington trade Daniel Gafford?

I know I’m about ten months late with this question, but the more I watch this guy dominate in Dallas and see whatever the Wizards are doing right now, why did they trade him?

I know they’re “rebuilding”, but he’s 24, filled a need the wizards had at center, and he’s steadily improved almost every season in his career.

He was also only traded for a washed up Richaun Holmes and a low first rounder who turned to be Kyshawn George (44 TS%) after a couple trades, so you can’t even argue they got a large haul.
