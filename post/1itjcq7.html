Are Suns done for? What can they realistically do to improve?

I was thinking about which underperforming teams should be able to make a run post trade deadline with their recent improvements. Bucks deadline wasn’t held in high regard when it happened, but looking at their play afterwards you could definitely see the front office’s vision of a more athletic, faster team coming into fruition. Sixers are in the play in race, the teams above them are in tank mode and Embiid is coming along at the right time. I have both ended the season relatively strong.

But out of all the underperforming contenders, there’s one whose situation seems utterly hopeless no matter what they do.. the phoenix Suns. Second apron team, no flexibility, terrible depth, the worst contract in the league, a toxic locker room after an attempt to trade two of their “Big 3”, Bud hasn’t been able to save this roster as he done in the past with other teams. The Suns have the most bleak future in the NBA and I don’t think they have a run in them like Bucks and Sixers do.

What are your impressions of the Suns? Will they finish the season in the playoff hunt or will they sink completely? I’m of the latter opinion.
