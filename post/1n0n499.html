  • Gold costs will be really high on buyer end (in the video it was 33,000 gold for an amulet)
  • Many buyers will prefer not to pay the gold tax, and will defer to classic trading

So you want to keep some classic tabs as well if you REALLY want to sell your items fast.

Basically:

  • Old tabs = Seller pays (with time)
  • New tabs = Buyer pays (with gold)

You will eventually need both
