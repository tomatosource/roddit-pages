Have we seen the last 300 win pitcher?

With Verlander hanging it up at the end of this season, he’ll likely finish with 270-something career wins and come up well short of 300. Next on the wins list among active pitchers is Scherzer with 222, but he’s 41 and at the end of his rope too. Cole (35) and Sale (37) are next up, with 156 and 154 wins, respectively, but they’ll be lucky to sniff 200 let alone 300 wins.

The first pitcher under 30 on the career wins list is Logan Webb, who has 75 career wins at age. It seems very likely we may have seen the last 300 win pitcher (Randy Johnson, 2009) and they are now an extinct species.
