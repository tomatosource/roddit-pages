I have enjoyed this league more than I expected, mostly due to having a really fun build. But, I find myself quitting the league because of the lack of portals for deaths.

This wasnt an issue last league. It was nice that I only had to ensure my towers were run with a 6 mod map, and then I could play the game more safely with 5 mod maps without massive penalty to rewards/league mechanics.

I approve of the QoL change of moving tablets to the maps, but it has forced me into playing uncomfortably, and getting game-rage when I die, lose all my ritual rewards etc, and then cant even juice that node again (which I may HAVE to run in order to get to a special boss encounter).

I understand that GGG wants POE2 to be more challenging than poe1, and death to “mean something” in it. But, I absolutely hate this system of being forced into playing zero death/6 mod maps, or else have Massive FOMO.

I would be thrilled if either they could move three tablet slots to 5 mods, or give us one grace death portal at 6 mods if they want us to run them fully juiced for more difficulty.
