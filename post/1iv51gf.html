Mapping does not feel rewarding in Endgame

Basically titel. I ran juiced T15-T18 Maps for 2 days, breaches, instilled, bosses… It is just exhausting and not rewarding. I “crafted” and identified like 1000 Items and sold maybe 4-6 items for 8 divine all together. I would say maybe even less and this was mostly one lucky Amulet I sold for 6 Div. If I had spend the same time in TOC I would have gotten a lot more in comparrison (it would have been more boring though).

Some deaths from bosses included (the boss at the forge is kinda… wtf?) I didnt really progress at all after this playtime. I am level 93 now and sure I gained some exp and the 8 div in currency but I cant even farm for specific items because I need to clear 3 different Citadels (and all the maps on the way) or like 15 breach maps (good ones, planned ones) or maybe 20 instilled Maps or 100 Rituals to get the chance to fight a specific/instance boss ONCE. This just doesnt feel good.

The fact that a death in endgame means you lose your waystone and the map modifiers makes it even worse - I cant learn to fight the forge boss with these mods he had because I died once and next time he/the map will have different mods. I dont even now why someone thought this is a good game design. The feeling is just bad.

It is much more progress to just brainless farm TOC and buy Items from trade page instead of farming them in high juiced maps, which is much more demanding and I think it would be the way the game should be played. I rolled a level 81 chest with +9 life. Why is this roll on a level 80 Item even possible. This makes a meaby mediocre good endgame item instantly trash. It feels I cant even try to get an Ingenuity by myself just because it is time consuming as hell. One chance to get an Ingeniuty is like… uncountable hours of hardcore gameplay - meanwhile they are to buy for 2 div on the trading page. My time invested in mapping feels just wasted.

Sorry for rant.
