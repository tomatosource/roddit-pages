Prized Pain scaling with Ingenuity (for non-ritualists)

I made a sheet calculating the effective chance to trigger thorns using 2x the updated Prized Pain with Ingenuity in 0.5.0, considering the change:

  • The Prized Pain Unique Ring no longer has Deal your Thorns Damage to Enemies you Stun with Melee Attacks. It now has 15-25% chance to deal your Thorns damage to enemies you Hit with Melee Attacks.

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dF9rApDBlLW-mSmyIzM5ezH13jITLDnGqDEIDXlGNWg/edit?usp=sharing

TL;DR: Using two perfectly corrupted Prized Pain (one can replace one with Kalandra’s Touch) you end up with 62% chance to trigger. On top of that, using a perfectly corrupted Ingenuity, you get a total of 84% chance to trigger (+22% chance).

This is not too existing and might be expensive next league. And given that the only other relevant mod to scale is the flat thorns (which uncorrupted is roughly equal to a Greater Tempered Rune), using Ingenuity might not be worth it.

If ~100 % chance to trigger turns out to be “required” by this new archetype, Ritualist might be mandatory, since we can use 3x perfectly (25%) rolled Prized Pain to achieve 3x 31% = 93 % chance. To achieve 100%, you would will still need some corruption outcomes though - as a minimum 2x 27%-rolls, and 1x 28%, which after multiplying and rounding becomes 2x 33% + 1x 34% = 100% (or some other combination).
