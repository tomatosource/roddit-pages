Let’s talk about the upcoming Derrick White season

Most of the discussion about next season thus far has been about how Jaylen Brown is going to bear more burden to play as the main guy on a one all star team. I think something that needs to be included in this discussion is how Derrick White also has to make the leap and increase his offensive load.

We know he can put up a very high volume of 3s and still remain efficient. We’ve seen high assist stretches from him. And we’ve seen games were he’s determined to get downhill and hit 30 pts. All he needs to do is put all three together and he can be the real leader of this offense.

The main is a stupidly hard worker, always shows up (with a smile), and I believe that he has what it takes to step into this role.
