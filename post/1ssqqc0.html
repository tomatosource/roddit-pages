Is Oracles “Unseen path” uniqueness important that we can’t use it in small quantities for the other ascendancies?

Title says it all honestly. But to expound a bit, is the unique identity of ascendancy specific hidden passive nodes so intrinsically tied to the identity that “Unseen path” cannot be used by all ascendancies to give some ascendancy related nodes built into the build decision process?

I’m not thinking 100+ nodes per ascendancy, but something more reasonable like 20-40 nodes tied to class identity.

Example: Pathfinder could have a node that allowed crossbows and thematic realistic options to play a roll in damage calculation and delivery style. Giving it the ability to take 30% of the damage from crossbow and apply it to the overall damage, and to convert the flasks in a separate continuation node into grenades (still using flask charges) would be a step in the right direction for fixing it, and would be tied exclusively to pathfinder due to its ascendancy quirks.

You can tie more life nodes into strength based classes and ascendancies that isn’t accessible by everyone, and potentially keep classes inside their normal niche such as a dex character not venturing excessively out into Int or str zones.

Another Tie-in might be the ability to tie a portion of the lost power from reducing gem levels into currently less required options like the passive tree and ascendancy choices.

I’m sure there’s flaws here, it will take a lot of work since even 20 would require once everything is all done 700 passive nodes, 1,400 if 40 (oracle already has it) but it would be an interesting way to bridge the gap that allows power rebalancing in the build dynamic away from equipment and back to ascendancy and passive trees.

Hit me with your thoughts.
