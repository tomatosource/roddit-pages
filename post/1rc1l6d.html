Greater and perfect orbs are bloat

I believe it’s a band-aid fix that shouldn’t stay in the game. To me it feels like it violates some of the core concepts of PoE. Why don’t we have some kind of an omen that culls low-level mods when any currency is applied? Or what if high iLvL bases would be ineligible to roll lowest tier mods?

It feels weird that many types of currencies are becoming obsolete so soon. All currencies shall be relevant and useful no matter how far into the game you’ve progressed.

I’m not a game designer, but can’t there be a more elegant solution for culling low tier mods beside introducing a ton of tiered currency?

I would say the same about tiered runes. It’s a convoluted mess. But, at least, low tier ones do not drop at higher levels, so it’s not as bad.
