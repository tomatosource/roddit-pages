“New” Rage Tech/Bug I Found with Projectiles

https://reddit.com/link/1ohu1ph/video/3am39tk8mqxf1/player

In this video I was using the unique “Hateforge” so that is why you never actually see the rage icon. More on that down below in the “Notes/Thoughts” section.

The reason I put new in quotations is the fact that I actually found this while playing Amazon at the start of 0.2 but ultimately never shared it online because I was lazy and I thought someone would also eventually find out and post a video or how to for it. It seems that never happened though from what ive seen so I’d thought I’d share it now with 0.3.

Tech/Bug Explanation: 

It’s pretty much due to infusions or well “Surges” as they’re called now in 0.3. When a projectile attack that has a Surge attached to it is thrown that surge explosion at the end of its flight activates “Rage on Melee Hit” when it hits a monster.

Notes/Thoughts:

Not all projectile attacks works for whatever reason. For example lightning arrow and Fragmentation Rounds does not. I haven’t bothered to test every projectile attack skill but I do know that mostly every spear skill works.

Ofcourse you can also do this on non amazon characters however the only things that allow you to get surges is “Spire of Ire” and “Double Vision”. 

I incorporated this into numerous builds over the past months with the Unique “Hateforge”. Like in the video above, with 20 rage on melee hit you can get 3 charges every projectile throw. Which means you can sustain whatever charge you want while mapping or bossing without using any charge generation gems or gear besides you know Hateforge itself. Also you can do all of this while on rhoa as well but ofcourse you’d have to start the loop with a actual melee skill first 

Like let’s take Explosive Spear for instance. Lets say you have 3 frenzies, you throw an explosive spear, explosive spear consumes a frenzy to explode immediately, that consumed frenzy also generates a surge, which then sets up your next explosive spear throw to activate your rage which then activates Hateforge giving you more frenzies and so on and so forth. Add in some Charge Regulation and you’re golden. 

Anyways, I can talk about this forever on the things I’ve done with this but I’ll stop yapping since the post is getting kinda long anyway and I explained the gist of it. Hopefully this post doesn’t get lost in the sea and reaches people. 

TLDR: You can activate rage on melee hit on certain projectile attacks that has a surge infused with it.
