Tech Explanation: 

Okay so as you know, rage on melee hit only activates via melee. That’s just how it works and is worded that way. However, for some odd reason there’s a way for you activate it via projectiles as well.

It’s pretty much due to infusions or well “Surges” as they’re called now in 0.3. When a projectile that has a Surge attached to it is thrown that surge explosion at the end of its flight activates rage on melee hit when it hits a monster.

Notes/Thoughts:

Every projectile attack doesn’t work for whatever reason. For example lightning arrow and Fragmentation Rounds does not. I haven’t bothered to test every projectile attack skill but I do know that mostly every spear skill works.

Ofcourse you can also do this on non amazon characters however the only things that allow you to get surges is “Spire of Ire” and “Double Vision”. 

I incorporated this into numerous builds over the past months with the Unique “Hateforge”. Like in the video above, with 20 rage on melee hit you can get 3 surges every projectile throw. Which means you can sustain whatever charge you want while mapping or bossing without using any charge generation gems or gear besides you know Hateforge itself. Also you can do all of this while rhoa as well but ofcourse you’d have to start the loop with a actual melee skill first 

Like let’s take Explosive Spear for instance. Lets say you have 3 frenzies, you throw an explosive spear, explosive spear consumes a frenzy to explode immediately, that consumed frenzy also generates a surge, which then sets up your next explosive spear throw to activate your rage which then activates Hateforge giving you more frenzies and so on and so forth. Add in some Charge Regulation and you’re golden.
