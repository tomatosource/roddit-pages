I miss Omen of Corruption

Crafting was cracked in 0.4 and you could make a pretty good 5 mod item easily and awesome 6 mod gear was not too far further. I think they tapped that pretty good with some of the updates.

What I really miss is OMEN OF CORRUPTION.

That was a fun item to use when you upgraded and wanted to see what you could do with your former gear.

It was fun to use on uniques you needed.

It was a good way to go for extra sockets. For midrange budgets corruption goalposts got moved way out in the distance.

I was actually excitedly anticipating it’s introduction in POE1.

This league, getting extra sockets is much more difficult. It used to be a worthy attempt and removing the “do nothing” part of it tipped the scale into your favor but it was still a risk.

I think with the other changes we can safely have it back? Not that I NEED the extra sockets for power but it was still a fun way to make use of all the cool new stuff to play with this season.

What do you all think?
