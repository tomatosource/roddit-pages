What’s your favorite trade idea if Boston tried to add another impact player?

The team is hovering around .500 which will probably be the case all season. If Tatum returns and is 100%, they could conceivably be a dangerous team come playoff time.

I’ve said all season that I’d like to either see Boston make moves to increase their ability to bottom out or make moves to increase their ability to win if Tatum is coming back. The buyer’s market is starting to take shape so I’m wondering what everyone’s favorite “win-now” trade idea is.

Thoughts on our trade assets:

Simons remains an awkward fit being relegated to bench duty with his 27.6 mil expiring contract being the focal-point of any pivot to “win now” trade.

While some of the reaction is exaggerated, we have seen some positive signs from guys like Neemias, Garza, Minott, and Walsh to suggest these guys, who previously had no trade value whatsoever, could either factor in as long term rotation players for the Celtics or pieces other teams might place some value on in trade packages. Hugo, likewise, is someone I’d say hasn’t necessarily blown anyone away, but given his age and solid signs of defense also hasn’t lost trade value and still probably is seen as the equivalent of a fringe 1st rounder/2nd rounder.

In the case of Minott, Walsh and Hugo, their growing competence also suggests Boston could withstand to lose Hauser (10 mil).

Simons + Hauser combine for 38 mil in outgoing salary.

Once you start throwing in guys like Scheierman, Tillman, Boucher (each around 2.5 mil) and some of our C+ level prospects like Walsh/Hugo, and 1st round picks, Boston can throw together a package capable of matching salaries for guys well into the 40 mil price range without hurting their core roster too much.

Combine this with the fact this draft is being talked about more and more as a massive one with maybe 6 franchise-level guys at the top (I’ve now seen multiple people say Cooper Flagg would go 3rd if he was in this draft class), there market for tanking teams looking to dump real talent for pennies on the dollar is starting to shape.

Guys like Zion (39.4 mil), Lauri (46.3 mil), Sabonis (42.3 mil), Morant (39.4 mil), LaMelo (37.9 mil) could all hit the market at a discount. You might even see someone like Trae (46 mil with player option next season) become available.

I would say all of those guys could become reasonably attainable for a package Boston could put together.

With Dallas’ future questionable, AD is another guy who could hit the market but at 54 mil he becomes nearly impossible to match salary for without also giving up Derrick White. Once you start including White + Simons together in deals (55 mil combined), you now open up to even more crazy possibilities.

Side question… what trade hypothetical would disgust you more? Simons + Hauser (38 mil) + picks for Kyrie (36 mil)… or alternatively White + Simons (55 mil) + picks for LeBron and Bronny (55 mil)?
