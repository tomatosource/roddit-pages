Thought’s on this season

I’ve played this season (and all previous) quite a bit. I main really just witch. Overall I think this is the best season so far. My witch is putting out around 300-700k damage depending, and I clear most maps in ~5mins. Looting takes longer imo.

What I like

  • Crafting - so much greatly improved
  • Build variety - so much better with the gem changes
  • Campaign is greatly improved with the new act

What I don’t like

  • The recent changes with the towers. I never had a problem with them before. I thought this change could be quite good, but it kinda takes away the fun of dropping tablets in towers and then having to do all those maps. Overlapping towers made maps so nice. With tablets now, I am getting way less mobs and XP even with maxed tablets and a maxed atlas.
  • I’ve played probably atleast 3-4 hours a day during weeknights and 15 odd during the weekends. I am still yet to have a level 20 skill gem or spirit gem drop on level 82 maps. I am 100% rarity.
  • Looting is ok, but not enough is dropping to have real fun for a lot of my mates who are not playing it as much as me. A few of them are buying currency because it’s so cheap vs the grind.
  • Level 95+ is still a drag - noticed this way more with the tower changes.
