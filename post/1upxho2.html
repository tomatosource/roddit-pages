Friendly reminder: Claim the Mote of the Martyrs power on all new characters.

Now that’s it’s been a month of so since the Martyr of the First Edict discovery happened, I imagine quite a few people have forgotten to claim the free Passive Skill Point that you get at the “Site of the Chosen”. I know I did on all my new characters after my first one. I didn’t claim it on my most recent character until level 92 😆

For those who weren’t around when it was discovered a month ago, or need a reminder. For each character you get to endgame, you can travel to an area called “The Site of the Chosen”, located directly behind the area you fight the Arbiter of Divinity. There, you can travel up the elevator and claim a free Passive Skill Point. Just click the “Mote of the Martyrs Power”.

You can do this on EVERY character. I, and everyone else at the time, did it on our main characters and it was epic. Then I, and many other people, promptly forgot to claim it on every character they made there after. I know this for a fact as yesterday I messages Global Chats 1 to 14 reminding people it existed, and at least 12 people thanked me for reminding them. Two of them were all the way at level 97. I’m sure a passive point at that level felt amazing.

I imagine that as time goes on, it will be second nature for everyone to take any new character to claim the free passive, but I’ll probably forget next league as well lol
