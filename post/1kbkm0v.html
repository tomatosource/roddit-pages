If Orlando ever gets some shooters to surround Banchero/Wagner, they’re going to be a problem.

First off, I’m glad we stepped on the gas in the second half and put these guys away last night, every extra minute you need to play against this team only increases the chance of further injuries.

That said, despite Orlando’s borderline level of physicality - really, past the border at times - they were able to do what no other team did all year, and that’s run us off the 3pt line. I look at guys like Wagner and Banchero and see that they are a matchup problem for us. If the Magic FO surrounded them with 3 and D guys instead of the cast of bricklayers they had out there in this series, they would have given us a real challenge.

What I find strange is that Orlando has had this problem for a few years now, and they still haven’t addressed it. And I wonder why that is. Seems like a huge whiff from their FO to not take advantage of surrounding their two very talented bigs with solid complementary pieces. They really aren’t far off from being a contender, hell they should have probably been the 4- or 5- seed if they didn’t get decimated by injury.
