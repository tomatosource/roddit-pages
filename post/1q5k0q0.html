Anfernee Simons=Josh Richardson 2.0 (and I mean that in a very good way)

The Celtics seem to have this amazing ability to take bad assets that other teams don’t want, like Simons and J-Rich, and get the most out of them. Simons had a bad reputation when we got him; he was known as a guy who dribbled the air out of the ball, played no defense and was inefficient. Nobody else in the league really wanted him. J-Rich was considered a negative contract and bad offensive player when we got him, Dallas handed him to us for free.

It was a struggle at first, but Simons has really adapted well to being an excellent off-ball shooter and is trying harder on defense than he ever has. He’s been excellent in the last few games, him and Garza (who were both terrible to start the season).

That being said…while I wouldn’t be shocked if we kept Simons, I think we all know that Brad is the master of selling high. He turned J-Rich into DWhite. If he sees the right deal out there for Simons, I think he’ll do it.

Still, good for Simons really turning into this.
