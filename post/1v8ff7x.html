Kobe 2008-2010 Playoff Stats: 30/5/5 on 46/34/85, 57% TS en route to 3 consecutive finals and back to back championships

I feel like this run has become underrated over time.
The Lakers had a real good team/roster and had as much success as the Steph-KD Warriors, and big 3 Heat with Pau and Odom as the 2nd and 3rd options. 2 games away from a 3peat!

Kobe got compared to MJ throughout his entire prime but this run was the height of it.

Motivated by Shaq winning in Miami and 3 years of mediocrity with Kwame and Smush, Kobe and the Lakers destroyed the West including the defending champion spurs in 08, the best version of the Melo Nuggets in 09, the Nash Suns & Baby Thunder.

Then in one of the greatest Finals series of all the. the Lakers beat the Celtics in 7 games in 2010.

Despite revisionist history because of people looking at Kobe’s 6⁄24 shooting in game 7 as if several greats like Duncan, LeBron, Hakeem, Bird and others haven’t also had bad shooting Game 7s, Kobe played great in that Finals overall(30/7/4 on 43/36/93 splits in the first 6 games) and the Lakers avenged that 08 loss.

Anybody else feel like this run gets underrated now?
