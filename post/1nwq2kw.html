Does anyone else feel the new T15 bosses are a bit overtuned?

So let me first say thar I think .3.1 is overall a very good patch. I’m really enjoying the new changes to mapping. That being said the new boss maps feel a bit overtuned. I have 90% armor and 90% armor applies to elemental and 5k EHP and I’m still getting one spotted constantly by the new bosses. My issue with this is that it will just drive players to be even more forced into Meta builds. If builds can’t one shot the bosses people just won’t play them.

Once again I think overall everything feels a lot better. I would just like to see the new bosses HP gets halved.
