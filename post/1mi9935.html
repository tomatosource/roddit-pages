Hauser is extremely valuable.

Hauser is going to be ages 28-31 through the length of his 4 year/ 45m deal that just kicked to start this season.

  • 99th Percentile in Shot Quality
  • 97th Percentile in 3 Point %
  • 90th Percentile in True Shoot %
  • 61st Percentile in Crafted DPM
  • 99th Percentile in Turnover Rate
  • Championship Proven shooting a blistering 48% from 3 in the NBA Finals

We have what is one of the most Elite shooters on the planet who also is a plus defender in his prime for 4 seasons at 6% of the cap. He also never turns the ball over.

This pretty much makes Hauser untradable. He does not make enough money to bring back equal level player of the same cost. The only way you would even trade a player like this is if you were somehow getting back an All-star level player in return and you need to attach Hauser to a larger contract to facilitate that.

And on our team the only players you could attach Hauser to are Simons and White, since we arent trading the Jays.

Unless you see Simons + Hauser trade or a White + Hauser trade out there, I dont foresee Hauser going anywhere for the next 4 seasons. He is simply too good to be traded alone or salary dumped.

Conclusion: I think Hauser fits the model for type of role player that can stay with a franchise for his entire career.
