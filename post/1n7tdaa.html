Campaign bonus objectives need better signposting.

I have felt that while the campaign has been the best part of PoE2 there has been this problem where the size of the maps makes exploration feel like you are just wandering around looking for the bonus objectives. I think some kind of sign posting would help so much in exploration. Like there are times when they do sign post things really well. Like Crowbell has an area dedicated to it that you find way before the actual boss. But a lot of the times it feels like if you want to do the bonus objectives you are just hugging the walls like a hall of mirrors waiting to stumble across the things worth engaging in.
