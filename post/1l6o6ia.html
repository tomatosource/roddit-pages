How good was prime Antawn Jamison?

In the 2000’s, Jamison could shoot 3’s and get you around 20⁄10. He made 2x All Star teams, and won 6th man. He ended his career with 20k points and notable moments including back to back 50 point games, one against the Lakers.

Some say his numbers were way inflated because his Warrior teams were awful and someone had to score/rebound. He was also too short to guard bigs and too slow to guard wings, so he got exploited on defense a lot. On Washington they did not have much team success even with 2 other scoring All Stars.

How is he rated looking back and would he have better luck today?
