[Timberwolves PR] Anthony Edwards diagnosed with left knee hyperextension injury and bone bruise, will be considered week-to-week

www.nba.com/timberwolves/news/anthony-edwards-injury-update-5

Minneapolis/St. Paul – The Minnesota Timberwolves today announced the following injury update on guard Anthony Edwards who suffered a left knee injury on Saturday, April 25 in the second quarter of Game 4 against the Denver Nuggets: 

An MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging) conducted by Dr. Craig Bennett at Mayo Clinic Sports Medicine on Edwards revealed a left knee hyperextension injury and bone bruise. Edwards will be considered week-to-week, and further updates of his progress will be provided when available.
