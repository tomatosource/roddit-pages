Request to buff Glacial Cascade + Frozen Locus combo

With the new Monk ascendancy coming, I’m tempted to league start the class. Unfortunately, the best way to do that right now is to immediately switch to a different weapon.

Unlike Glacial Cascade, xbow doesn’t require positioning to consume freeze and deals more damage on top of that. Same thing with snipe on bows.

Talisman (wyvern) gets power charges from stunning and can do both cull/corpse charge acquisition. Why does the quarterstaff, a weapon all about charges, get the most awkward skill to get them?

Essence drain/contagion (wands/spell staves) straight up deal more damage at range with none of the clunk and can also support freezing later on. Hell, even profane ritual feels better to get charges because it doesn’t discourage me from killing mobs.

I feel like this is very easy to fix. Allow either of the cold quarterstaff skills to generate charges from freeze or when shattering ice crystals and remove the bizarre “final burst” mechanic from glacial cascade. Thank you.
