What is wrong with Jayson Tatum’s minutes?

https://preview.redd.it/v4b45peesarg1.jpg?width=660&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=85c5bc7a1cd877224e42544658948a1afa49bbb1

It’s not about how many minutes he’s getting, it’s about how those minutes are being used. He was going off all the way until the third quarter, then we bench him for almost double digit minutes and decide to play JB, who wasn’t even having a good game. Then we bring him back in after he’s already cooled down. Like what is that?

It’s a pattern at this point. If we’re giving JB all those minutes so he can get into his groove, why aren’t we doing the same for JT? Why give him these on-and-off minutes? Every time Tatum starts going off, Joe decides to bench him for long stretches and then brings him back when he’s already cooled off. And then there’s JT standing in the corner when hes hot, while someone else starts ball-hogging.

We’re prioritizing JB a lot more now and just messing with Tatum’s rhythm and momentum. Things have definitely felt different since Tatum’s return.

Yea JB was having an MVP season, but that shouldn’t impact how we utilize our All-NBA caliber player. We still have to focus on bringing him back to who Jayson Tatum is, then just using him as a piece for winning games.
