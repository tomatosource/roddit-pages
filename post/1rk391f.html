Wemby becomes the first NBA player to win Player of the Month and Defensive Player of the Month in the same month.

Since the defense player of the month award was introduced last season

Victor Wembanyama won the very first Defensive player of the month award in November 2024.

This is Wemby’s 3rd defensive player of the month award and second in a row. No other player has more than 2.

Wemby becomes the first player in NBA history to own a Player of the Month award and Defensive Player of the Month award
