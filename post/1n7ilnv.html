Hopium/copium Take: I hope Interludes stay.

But Acts 5-6 come and replace them. I hope that Interludes become an alternate leveling path after completing the first play through per league that get expanded to about 5 interludes that cover all the normal quest rewards you’d usually get condensed into 6-7 zone chunks at anywhere from 1.5-3 levels per zone (starting with 3 max per interlude 1, teetering down to 1.5-2 levels per zone at interlude 5.) ending your leveling progression at around 60-65.

It still gives loose story-based quest progression and allows elaboration of the overall story while giving optional leveling that many have asked for.

My own personal take? This is a better option than some of the “skip everything let me innn let me iiinnnnn to endgame” takes I’ve seen on the Reddit and forums. It also gives GGG free reign to create a story element while also giving lore junkies and story enjoyers some leeway as well while still keeping the campaign esthetics in a meet-you-half-way kind of middle ground with build creators/testers.
