Watchtower idea pitch

We all have read the posts ad nauseam about how watchtower mechanics suck so I’ll just dive into my idea to incentivize players completing maps within tower range vs rushing towers or multi tower setups.

Map mechanics:

-Keep the random spawn watchtowers and corrupted patches

-Buff range of towers and nerf their spawn again making overlaps uncommon

-Remove citadel, unique map, and hideout random spawns.

-Allow citadels/unique maps to convert an uncleared map in tower range when clearing a watchtower, chances scaling up with waystone discovery % and effect, citadels only spawn when clearing t14+ maps on watchtowers, unique maps retaining current tiering like untainted paradise at any levels and phyrll at watchtower t11 maps or higher and so on

-Citadel bad luck prevention via t14+ map boss fragment drops or invitation drop. These can be used on any non unique maps

-New map location called “vaal beacon” can convert your current location when fully clearing a map

-Vaal beacons essentially are reduced radius watchtowers with a single tablet socket to allow juicing with no more than 3 vaal beacons allowed within 1 watchtower and can spawn out of tower range

Atlas progression:

-Make atlas points tied to clearing higher tiered watchtowers instead of corrupted nexus

-Add atlas passives that increase chances to unveil citadels, corruption or unique maps separately

-Add unique tablets that can force citadel/unique/corruption spawns in tower range

-Add tablet suffix/prefix that increase effects more maps in range completed

-Add atlas passive and tablet suffix/prefix for chance for completed maps to be clearable again keeping all additional map features and bonuses

-Create a corruption tree on map atlas passive with points obtained through completing higher tiers nexus

-Make hideouts spawns rare again and like in poe1 and the default ones obtainable through campaign.
