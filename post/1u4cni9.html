What is your favorite team’s most devastating playoff moment?

With Game 4 of the finals still being fresh in everyone’s mind, it got me thinking that most franchises have at least a couple of playoff moments that were just utterly heartbreaking. Maybe not to the extent of what happened Wednesday night, but still undoubtedly painful to think about.

I’ll go first. I live in Portland, and the most devastating playoff moment of my lifetime has to be the Game 7 choke against the Lakers in 2000. Up 15 with about 10 minutes left in the game, they had just gone on a 25-9 scoring run over the previous ~7 game minutes and it looked like the NBA finals was in the bag… until it wasn’t. The wounds of blowing that game 7 to the Lakers still hurt people whenever I hear coworkers or people out and about talking about the Jailblazers.

Now I’ve dumped a little trauma, your turn. What is your favorite team’s most devastating playoff moment?
