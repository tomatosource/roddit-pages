You can do 6 mod, 3 tablet maps without sweating balls while your build is going off the ground because you now have double the tries per map (2 revives is way more than 1)

Previously I was only doing 4 mod, 2 tablet maps, to have 3 revives from alch and go

But Doriyani master can be unlocked early by simply doing one corrupted tower

He has a power “+1 revives each map”

Super useful early as you getting your footing in the end-game
