[NBA] The Kia NBA Players of the Month for December: Shai Gilgeous Alexander and Jalen Brunson

Oklahoma City Thunder guard Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson have been named the Kia NBA Players of the Month for games played in December.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — 31.4 ppg, 6.1 apg, 59.4 FG%, 9-4 W/L Record

Jalen Brunson — 30.6 ppg, 7.1 apg, 40.5 3FG%, 10-4 W/L Record

Source: https://www.nba.com/news/monthly-nba-awards-december-2025
