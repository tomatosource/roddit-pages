50+ Hours in PoE2 Patch 0.2 – Zero Divine Orbs, 122 Exalts. Where’s the Loot?

https://preview.redd.it/jbgjwl1ffvue1.png?width=508&format=png&auto=webp&s=7d539331908cf373ab7a8a5f9dba9ac027940f9f

Just wanted to share my honest feedback after spending over 50 hours in Patch 0.2. Here’s a screenshot of my playtime as proof. Despite investing this much time, I’ve had zero meaningful loot drops. 122 Exalts, not a single Divine Orb. Not one.

This has been, hands down, the least dopamine patch I’ve played in the history of PoE – and I’ve played a lot, both PoE1 and now PoE2. It honestly feels like a loot filter sim with no pay-off.

I don’t know if this can still be salvaged. Player retention is already looking rough, most major streamers have stopped playing, and many are preparing for Last Epoch instead. It feels like the community is losing confidence, fast.

GGG has so much experience from Path of Exile 1. Why not bring 80% of that successful formula into PoE2? Why reinvent every wheel when you’ve already built such a strong base?

Ever since Chris Wilson stepped back, it feels like GGG’s vision has gotten lost. I really hope there’s still time to turn this ship around, but right now? It’s not looking good.

Just my 50 cents from the perspective of a 5,000+ hour PoE player.
