To the People complaining about the economy; SSF story

Hello,

I’m rather thick headed and I decided I would complete the game SSF before even looking at trading or guides because I wanted to learn the game the proper way.

Said and done in roughly 8 days of play time I had reached level 88 gotten my 4 ascendency points and was running basically any T15 maps (not delirious, didn’t know about that mechanic) no problems. I had one REALLY lucky drop in my first T15 map a rattling sceptre +200 spirit and +5 minions with damage and life modifiers, but was a so so upgrade from my other sceptre. I considered my SSF journey done

Sold the Sceptre a couple of hundred divines to cheap and began a new journey.

Now the game is a lot bigger than farming T15s but it’s in no way an impossible task to do in SSF and it’s outright ridiculously easy to do in trade league but it takes time, without guides I’ve done some testing on how expensive it is to start farming true endgame content (Audience T4, Breach T4, delirious maps max level bosses and citadels). Being smart about what you buy it’s very possible to do all this for a cost under 10 divines, the problem is people buying +5 skill wands only you can get a top tier staff with +6 for exalts while the cheapest wand is divines. Same problem with focuses there are 350es +30 int +30 cast speed +60 mana regen for pennies but people only look for the +skill and +damage ones.

My point being the economy is not the problem your lack of knowledge is your bottle neck, don’t blindly follow guides experiment. Your DPS number is not as important as you might think and anyone saying they’ve got a cheap spark guide to reach 300k+ dps is a bad actor.
