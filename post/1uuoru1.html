This bug hasn’t been fixed since 2024, and now it’s gotten worse (Cast on / Missing Socketed Skill)

This game-breaking bug has been present and ignored by the devs since 2024 (detailed breakdown of updates can be read on this thread: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3971178/page/1).

Almost every “Cast on” skill gem has the chance to permanently stop working on one or both weapon sets every time changes are made to spirit, gem levels, passive tree, attributes, and other misc. tweaks in a build.

I believe that this is due to having different spirit values in a character’s weapon sets. I am not a coder or game designer, and I cannot explain why this is happening. The only way to get it to work again is to cut another spirit gem or buy it from another player (as far as I know, if you buy a cut Cast on ____ gem from another player, the system recognizes it as “new” and it will work again). But if you change anything in your build, the bug will happen again.

This is probably the worst bug I’ve experienced in POE2 because of how tedious and limiting it is. I am in awe of how this has not been addressed since launch and has only gotten worse every patch. I am posting here to bring more attention to this, as the main thread hasn’t received a reply from a community manager. Please give some attention to this.
