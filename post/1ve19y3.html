Can an Older Team Win a Championship in Today’s NBA?

Winning 4 NBA playoff rounds has always been insanely difficult but winning it all in today’s NBA appears to be more physically demanding than ever and we’re seeing that evidenced not only by younger and deeper teams making the finals the last 3 years but by so many talented teams with key older or unhealthy players wearing out in the second round every season. Having one key starter with durability problems seems like a death sentence now. The Sixers having TWO of these players, will they have ANY real chance of winning FOUR playoff rounds?

Just look at the last 3 seasons:

2024: Mavs: top 9 all 31 or younger, outside of Kyrie & THJ, everyone 26 or younger. SO young

2024: Celtics: top 3 29 or younger with best player only 25 but Jrue (33), Al (37) & KP (durability) all had questions. This might be the one to point to. KP only played 2 series and scored 12ppg but was able to contribute ENOUGH. Jrue was still a really good player at that point and the Celts got a bit of a lucky draw and just got insanely hot from 3.

2025: Pacers & OKC both VERY young and deep with Siakam at 31, they only player over 29

2026: Knicks oldest rotation player only 30 years old. Spurs insanely young

Is this coincidence or trend. Some of the things pointing to this being a trend:

  1. The refereeing, beginning in the 2025 playoffs began to allow WAY more physicality.

  2. Pace and space offenses have forced defenses to come out of drop coverages and need to defend further up the court. Three-point shooting forces defenders to sprint massive distances from the paint to the perimeter on closeouts.

  3. Constant Switching: Defending higher pick-and-rolls means players must rapidly switch assignments and guard elite playmakers one-on-one in open space.

  4. No Hiding Spots: Offenses actively hunt out weaker defenders on every possession, leaving no place to rest on the court.

Do you expect this trend to continue? How does a team like Philly make it through 4 rounds of this relentless physicality when we saw them get wiped off the court in the second round last season. Do they need Lebron and Embiid to play well for 4 rounds or do they have enough talent to get 1.5 rounds of good Embiid, 2.5 round of good Lebron and cover for the rest of the time?
