My Thoughts on PoE2 0.5

Overall, I think 0.5 is a step in the right direction. There are a lot of things I enjoy about the patch, but there are also some fundamental issues that are making me lose interest faster than I expected.

Goods

Divine drops feel great

Divines are dropping at a healthy rate, especially once you reach the endgame. It feels much better than previous versions where progression could feel overly dependent on getting lucky.

Waystone and Tablet sustain feels good

I haven’t had any major issues sustaining waystones or tablets. The drop rates seem to be in a good spot and I rarely feel blocked from progressing because I’m missing mapping resources.

League mechanic introduction is good

I like how the current league mechanic is introduced. Completing the quest chain the first time feels rewarding and gives players a proper introduction to the system.

My only concern is replayability. If future leagues require us to repeat the same introductory process every single time, it could become tedious very quickly.

Bads

Infinite Atlas isn’t the problem — the setup is

I actually don’t mind the Infinite Atlas itself.

The issue is how much preparation is required before I can do the content I actually want to farm.

For example:

  • Path through the Atlas to find a city.
  • Get Grand Mirrors in my city
  • Buy the necessary tablets.
  • Buy the maps I need.

Only after all that can I finally start running my strategy.

As someone who only plays around 3-4 hours a day, I often spend my Saturday setting everything up, then spend Sunday actually farming. It feels like too much time is spent preparing to play instead of playing.

Boss maps have the same issue

The boss encounters themselves are fine.

The problem is that I need to clear a large amount of surrounding content before I can access the boss map I want to run. Again, it feels like unnecessary setup before reaching the content I’m interested in.

The progression from T1-T14 feels empty

Maybe other players feel differently, but for me there’s a huge void between early maps and true endgame mapping.

T1-T14 progression feels relatively uninteresting. The excitement only really starts once I reach T15/T16 and begin engaging with proper endgame farming strategies.

I have no motivation to make multiple characters

This is probably my biggest issue with PoE2.

In PoE1, one of my favorite parts of every league was creating multiple characters and trying different builds, whether meta or off-meta.

Leveling was fast enough that rerolling felt exciting.

In PoE2, whenever I think about making another character, I immediately lose motivation because I know I have to go through the campaign again. Even with twink gear, the campaign is too boring

For the first time in years, rerolling characters feels like a chore rather than something I’m excited about.

Tablets feel like Sextants 2.0

Personally, I’m not a fan of tablets.

They remind me of old sextants where I’m constantly managing another layer of maintenance before I can actually run content. I understand why they exist, but I don’t find them particularly fun.

The Atlas Tree feels boring

The Atlas Tree currently feels very linear.

Most players are farming the same content in roughly the same way, which makes both the endgame and economy feel less interesting.

One thing I loved about PoE1 was how much agency players had.

If you wanted to farm Breach, you could fully specialize into Breach.

If you wanted to farm Alva, you could invest heavily into Alva and run hundreds of maps around that strategy.

Different players could pursue completely different goals and create their own niches in the economy.

In PoE2, the Atlas Tree doesn’t currently offer that same level of specialization, so everyone ends up doing very similar things.

Final Thoughts

I am not here to spread hatred among the players or to start any drama. this is just my opinion on the current patch. 0.5 is definitely the most enjoyable version of PoE2 I’ve played so far.

The game has improved significantly, loot feels better, and endgame progression is more rewarding.

However, a lot of the friction still comes from the amount of setup required before you’re allowed to engage with the content you actually want to play. Combined with the long campaign and relatively shallow Atlas specialization, it’s becoming harder for me to stay motivated long-term.

Curious to hear if other people feel the same way or if I’m completely off the mark.
