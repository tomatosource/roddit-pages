Critical failure: Living Bomb + Cast on Criticial — Reported Since January

In short: Cast on Critical applies Living Bomb to enemies that are already dead, completely preventing its explosion and Fire Infusion generation while at the same time it still consumes all Energy from CoC.

I do not use the termm “Critical failure” lightly… Actually, it is quite challenging to come up with a hypothetical scenario in which another pair of skills in combination could be so broken as CoC + LB are in the current version of the game - and how it has been like this even after bug reports for more than 6 months in official GGG platforms, raising some additional questions on what exactly is the practicality of “Early Access Bug Reports” or similar channels…

It starts breaking Living Bomb even at is conceputal form and premises: Living Bomb’s practical purpose is indirect area clearing, it is planted in a living enemy so that subsequent damage or the enemy’s death detonates the bomb, damaging the surrounding pack and generating Fire Infusion. Mid-to-longterm return on investments if you will or delayed benefits.

Cast on Critical breaks that entire LB function:
* Living Bomb never explodes;
* surrounding enemies receive no explosion damage;
* no Fire Infusion is generated;
* all accumulated Cast on Critical Energy is consumed;
* all 100 spirit is still reserved

The behaviour also violates Living Bomb’s own targeting rules. When cast manually, Living Bomb cannot be placed on a dead corpse (and I do not beleive I am typing this at this very moment…). Cast on Critical is therefore forcing the skill to target something that the active skill itself correctly treats as invalid.

This failure was reported more than six months ago: An official forum report dated 17 January 2026 already describes the same triggered Living Bomb failure: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3906822

In that report, Living Bomb triggered through Fire Spell on Hit remains visible on the corpse, never detonates, generates no Fire Infusion, and works normally when cast manually. The triggering mechanism differs, but the defect is the same: triggered Living Bomb is applied only after its target has died.

A separate report from 9 July 2026 documents an additional Cast on Critical integration defect in which Living Bomb detonates but the resulting Fire Infusion cannot be collected: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3983454

The corpse-targeting failure remains directly reproducible in patch 0.5.4e after more than six months and numerous patches and hotfixes. I found no documented correction and no public response confirming that the January report was reproduced, acknowledged, or assigned for investigation.

This is not an edge case or a minor loss of efficiency. The interaction fails whenever the triggering critical kills the selected enemy—which is precisely the normal clearing scenario in which Living Bomb should provide value.

The exception from being 100% broken: It appears functional only against bosses and sufficiently durable rare enemies because those targets survive the triggering hit. That does not preserve the skill’s primary clearing function; it merely avoids the condition that exposes the bug. In fact, the bug ends up completely inverting the very own premise of Living Bomb use in practical gameplay: now instead of putting the seed in a weak opponent so it can be put down quickly and used for wider damage into more and stronger enemies around it, your only hope for clearance with CoC + Living Bomb is accepting eating multiple hits for longer time while chasing elites around and hoping that they can at least serve like a repeatable piñata for 2-3 times (while we pretend that the rest of the mob has not been hitting us from all angles during the extra seconds neeeded to take down the elite in comparison the the weaker link)…

To wrap things up: This creates a serious question about the purpose of the official bug-report forum: was the January report reviewed and left unresolved, or was it never meaningfully triaged?

Suggested resolution - in layers of how quick they are to implement as a patch to the game:

  1. Living Bomb should be made temporarily incompatible with Cast on Critical and other affected triggers - quick to program, saves people from the embarrassment at least.
  2. Redirect it to a nearby living enemy. If no valid target exists, Cast on Critical should preserve its Energy rather than spending the complete trigger on an impossible cast.
  3. No enemies around and the CoC energy is more volatile that treated Uranium? WELL THEN PUT THE LIVING BOMB SEED IN THE PLAYER SO IT CAN AT LEAST HAVE A CHANCE AT RUNNING TOWARDS THE NEXT MOB BEFORE IT EXPLODES IN VAIN - maintaining ailment tresholds of the true enemy killed of course, to avoid exploits right darling.

At minimum, GGG should publicly acknowledge whether this long-standing defect has been reproduced and is being investigated. Leaving a permitted skill combination in the live game when it systematically consumes its full trigger resource + reserved spirit, bypasses the skill’s own targeting restrictions, eliminates its defining area-clearing function, and produces no Fire Infusion is unacceptable.
