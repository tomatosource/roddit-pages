You were right (lessons learned)

I caved in and used the trade website. I hate it still and an AH would be better but I got a few pieces of gear that changed the game for my Monk.

The biggest change was picking up a Quarterstaff for 5 ex which doubled my DPS instantly. I started clearing maps I was struggling on with ease. I decided to strip down the whole build.

I spent hours swapping out gear and passives until I felt I had the most balance I could get. My EHP went from ~3200 to ~6800 (thanks to Ghostwrithe) even with the addition of Chaos Inoculation/Eternal Youth.

My inner Thanos felt the power shift in a couple of test runs. I farmed a few good relics and went for my third ascendency after failing so many times before in the Trial of Sekhemas. Walking in with 14k honour, 75% honour resist and my retooled Monk made me lol at my former self as I shredded everything in the Trial and my goal was accomplished.

I credit the community here for pushing me to be better even though I didn’t see it that way at the time. Thanks … jerks ;)
