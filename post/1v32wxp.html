GGG, please bring Recombinators back. SSF is becoming exhausting.

I genuinely think removing Recombinator has made SSF significantly less enjoyable.

One of the best parts of Path of Exile has always been taking mediocre items and slowly turning them into something great through effort and smart crafting. Recombinators made that possible. Every rare had potential, and every crafting session felt like progress, even when you failed.

Without it, crafting in SSF feels incredibly restrictive. Finding a good crafting base is much harder, and every rare you throw away feels like a missed opportunity because you know it could have been useful. You still had to farm bases, find good modifiers, and accept the risk of failure. The difference was that progress came from knowledge and persistence, not just waiting for the perfect drop.

Players who didn’t enjoy this mechanic could simply ignore it. Many people barely interacted with it at all, while for SSF players it was one of the most satisfying crafting tools in the game.

Please consider bringing Recombinator back, at least for SSF. SSF should be challenging because of resource management and decision-making - not because players have no realistic way to improve their gear.
