35 games into the season, the OKC Thunder are still on pace to be the best defense since 1996-97

The Thunder are 10.5 points below the league average defensive rating, which is the highest margin since defensive rating has been available as a stat. The best defensive rating over a full season is the 2003-04 San Antonio Spurs with a defensive rating 9.8 points below league average. Here is a table of the top 5 in league history with the Thunder in there as well:

Team DRtg below league average
2024-25 Thunder (through 35 games) 10.5
2003-04 Spurs 9.8
2007-08 Celtics 9.4
2003-04 Pistons 9.0
1998-99 Spurs 8.6
2004-05 Spurs 8.3

Defense usually gets worse as the season progresses, so we probably shouldn’t expect OKC to stay above 10, but if all goes right, they could hold on to the best defense in recorded NBA history.
