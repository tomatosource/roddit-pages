Some anecdotes from end-league solo testing various Instilled Emotion juicing

Intro

First of all, remember this is all speculative in nature and the best sources of data we have are from PoE1, Prohibited Library Discord, dev interviews, Slipperyjim and Midir on YouTube. Beyond that, this is my own anecdotal opinion supplemented by these sources.

So for a quick run down on rarity in maps, we assume it goes like:

Player IIR (heavy diminishing returns) * Party Bonus IIR * Area IIR * Monster IIR

The same formula goes for IIQ. There is actually one source of Player IIQ in the game, but only for gold, and that is the Perandus unique ring.

As far as I’m aware, whether an item is gold or not is determined prior to IIQ calculations, so this ring should not increase the chances for gold to upgrade to rare currencies in the same way player IIQ.

Anyway, high diminishing returns appears to only impact Player rarity, and it seems to have breakpoints at around 100%, 150%, 200%. Stacking rarity beyond this will still increase rarity, but not significantly and at an increasing steep rate.

I won’t be going over party bonuses here but they are obviously substantial, just not relevant to the topic.

Optimising Juice

We have a list of things we should check off first:

  1. 200% Player IIR - This is where I’m currently at while I’m collecting my anecdotes.

  2. Area Level - This seems to increase the chances for rare items to drop directly by weighting (as in, area level 82 seems to have a higher weighting for items in higher rarity drop pools than area level 81). All my testing is done in 80, 81 and 82 area levels.

  3. Full Tablet / Waystone effect Atlas. We can get a ton of increased precursor effect, and increased Waystone effect. Increased Precursor effect is the only viable way to get high area IIQ right now, and combined with increased Waystone effect this seems to be the only way to get both high area quantity and area rarity together.

  4. We go full 20% quant on every tower. Other than this, your other area juicers are mostly able to be rolled higher on Waystone affixes. The value for quant on tablets is just unmet by other modifiers.

  5. Monster IIR. There’s a few distinctions to be made here, including one that is new in PoE2. Unique monsters have a MUCH HIGHER base monster IIR and IIQ than rare monsters do, and rare monsters than magic monsters, so forth.

The baseline rarity and quant for rares seems to be around 2300 / 750 and double that for uniques. Each additional modifier a monster has seems to be multiplicative with this base Monster IIR and Monster IIQ. Unique monsters can also have modifiers that act the same way.

  1. For Breach we take the point that gives all rares in your breach Soul Eater. This is a powerful rare modifier that guarantees all your rares have a ton of additional monster IIR and IIQ in every breach you run.

Deliriousness % and Monster IIR / IIQ formula

This is where things get interesting. After a ton of testing running 150% delirious maps (using triple isolation, yes this is expensive as hell to do), I’m pretty confident the following is happening in PoE2:

Monster IIR and Monster IIQ seems to have a few multiplicative factors in its own calculation, that goes as follows:

Base IIQ / IIR * Number of additional modifiers * Any additional reward modifiers (from Atlas nodes or league mechanics) * Deliriousness Modifier

Yes, Deliriousness definitely DOES impact Monster IIR and Monster IIQ significantly, and seemingly adds an additional multiplier on top of the modifier count and reward extras. It does not increase area rarity or IIQ and we know this because things like Breach splinters are not impacted.

New in PoE2 juicing, boss juicing

Distilled Suffering seems to add a strange modifier to your maps.

“Unique monsters have 1 additional modifier”

At first I didn’t take much notice of this, but after testing, it is insanely strong. Running 3x Distilled Suffering with Deadly Evolutions means you guarantee 5 or 6 modifiers on all Delirious bosses spawned, coalesced corruption bosses spawned, and map bosses including Citadel bosses

The new Overseer tablets also have a higher roll range for quantity on items dropped by map bosses than normal tablets. I did some testing on 6-modifier Doryani’s with some Citadel runs and it really seems like we are scaling monster IIR and IIQ to well over 10000% with a triple suffering map..

TLDR

Deliriousness acts as an additional multiplier to monster IIR and monster IIQ. There is currently a method to achieve an absolutely absurd amount of Monster IIQ and Monster IIR by juicing Overseer quant tablets, using Deadly Evolutions with triple suffering to guarantee 5-6 modifier Unique monsters, and farming Delirious bosses, Coalesced Corruption and Map Bosses with high modifiers.

They are also extremely hard to kill and will absolutely one shot you if you are not used to fighting 6 mod bosses on high deli numbers, so keep that in mind.

Anyway. Maybe someone finds this speculation useful.
