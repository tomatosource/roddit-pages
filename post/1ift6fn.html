Mavs Fans: Boycott the team, boycott the NBA

There’s some shady, corrupt shit going on. I’m not even a conspiracy theorist, but there’s no way this trade goes down without corruption. No other bidders, randomly trading a generational superstar entering his prime for pennies on the dollar, the whole thing done in secret. With the new ownership, their ties to gambling and Vegas, and their general ethically reprehensible positions and influence, this just doesn’t add up as a trade you make for basketball reasons. By extension, boycott the entire NBA for sustaining this shit. We’re all being gaslit. Don’t believe all those talking heads who are now trying to convince you why the Mavs wanted to make this trade, Luka lacked conditioning, lacked focus in the biggest moments?! Are you fucking kidding me? The latter is a complete lie, and the former is not a reason you trade him for AD and a 1st. We are being lied to and gaslit. Not another penny spent on Mavs games or the NBA. Speak with your purses. Don’t attend the games, don’t buy the merchandise, unsubscribe from all the social media accounts. This is a corrupt team and a corrupt league, exploiting the loyalties and love of fanbases to enrich the pockets of billionaires. Fuck them. Free yourselves. 

Signed,
