My issue with “high-end” crafting.

First. I have a disgusting amount of hours in poe 1 and 2. My gameplay loop each league is generally to clear all content and try to get into the dps top of my build on poe.ninja (while keeping it playable) despite knowing full-well it counts for nothing because yadayada.

That said, I am arguably (but not really) casual in my way of playing. I invest a lot of hours, but not necessarily efficiently. I don’t look up farming strats, I don’t research profit crafting popular builds, etc.

I generally just go with vibes. I juice whatever content I prefer, and it usually works out. I craft pretty much solely for my build, selling off “bricks” or pieces I replace. Occasionally I’ll stumble over something easy and profitable to craft and craft a couple.

And in PoE1 this is enough. I can get myself a mageblood. I can afford to gamblecraft cool stuff. I can reach the top of flicker and be surrounded by profiles that have “mirrored” on every other item.

This is because when it comes to high-end items and omega-end items, there’s a vast difference in cost but a relatively minor difference in power.

For example, when it came to flicker strike a few leagues back you wanted the whatever heist sword with double-influence and a bunch of shenanigan gem rolls. Looking at the finished mirror-tier product, that shit is intimidating.

But in reality, with a LOT of luck, you can do it for 50 div. And even if you fail you can get a usable one (more often than not in my possibly blessed experience) which could be sold in hopes of getting your money back, or even make a profit. Because people are too scared to try for the finished product.

The point is that the currency used in the initial 80% of the steps have a chance at succeeding at the mirror-tier despite being “affordable.” The bases, the influence orbs, the alt orbs, the annul orbs, the awakener orb, the beast shenanigans, the dominance orbs, the etcetera. It’s more about time, knowledge, and gamble addiction than raw currency.

Yes, it is scary to invest 50 div if you don’t have hundreds or thousands, but the odds of success aren’t non-existent, and you almost always end up with something of value. It’s when you get to all the hinekora, harvest reroll, or heavy-duty fossil shit it gets REAL expensive. But it is reasonably achievable to reach the same result for much cheaper. And that’s the crux.

Hell, even synth rerolling can be attempted with a budget.

THIS FINALLY BRINGS ME TO POE 2

It is, in my opinion, all about the raw currency. I can’t imagine what it’s like trying to scale up while playing genuinely casually.

In the very first patch, there was basically no crafting. But there was bases and chaos orbs. But you know what? There was a lot of chaos orbs. It was worth picking up bases, hitting a few chaos and annuls, and maybe getting something cool. Now 7 or 8 chaos orbs are a divine, and 1 annul is basically also a divine. So that’s gone.

The only truly accessible currency is exalted orbs, which is useless without annuls on a low-end scale.

So, okay, I want to craft a high-end weapon.

First, I need a base. Preferably an exceptional one, but that’s way too expensive if I also want the right pre/suff on it. So I get the cheaper one for like 6-10div, depending on what it is.

(I would get an exceptional and try to roll them myself, but then I’d have to either hit my pre/suff on trans/aug or make it rare, annul, and then spend 1 div per every 8 roll for each line.)

Then, if I’m lucky, I want an essence, in which case yay, because otherwise I yolo a t3 regal. If it’s good, great, if not. I guess I annul it? But then I’d prob have to omen so that’s maybe 8 div all-in. So maybe try to perf exalt slam something better, but that’s also a couple divs.

Maybe I need a perfect essence or an alloy, in which case I can target either suff/pre and if it’s isolated, yay, if not. Well, maybe I brick it and that’s my 6-10 div gone.

Oh well, lets say I hit it. Now I have my 3 lines, and can target slam a desecrated mod on either pre/suff. That’s maybe a div or two. That’s fine.

Now I can fracture it, fantastic. Which is maybe 5 div or so… but why should I? So I can safely chaos reroll my prefixes or suffixes? That’s like 6-10 div per roll. I can’t afford that. Especially not with perfect or even greater chaos orbs.

Screw it. I’m greater exalt slamming the last two lines. Maybe I’ll even whittle a bit.

Holy shit, they’re decent. We’re actually good. Now for the desecrated mod. Echo is actually pretty cheap so that’s fine. Buuut the rolls are trash anyways. Now I need an annul and an omen of light, so that’s 9 div. Then the rolls are trash again. And again, and again.

Ugh, I’d rather start over than try to keep light rolling it. Odds are, I am not making my money back, and it’s going to take a long time to sell even if correctly or even undercut priced.

SUMMARY:

Every step is heavy in raw currency, and is short-lived. That is to say, the path is already laid out before you, you just have to pay to step on it. And should you accidentally step off of it, at almost any point, pay up and go back to the beginning.

In PoE1 you can spend your time rolling with transmutes, augs, alts, and annuls, and save tons of currency on the first step. You can beastcraft in order to save yourself should you step off the path. You can yolo some annuls or chaos orbs and possibly save yourself should you step off the path.

You can, in essence, lay your own path. Be it winding or longer. You can surely lose it all, but you could also turn 10 div into 100.

But in PoE2, as far as I can tell, the buy-in is so much higher. And laying your own path isn’t for the poor, it’s for the rich.

Most importantly, it’s boring and short. Crafts aren’t long-lived. You either win or you lose in 5 minutes flat. You can’t roll, roll, roll, and roll unless you’re made of gold (I mean divs but gold rhymes). You can’t run out of some base currencies and farm some of your own. You can’t run out of all of your currency and farm up a couple of div to continue.

You have to put it down and farm 20 div or even way way way more. Alternatively wait for some other craft to sell, which for whatever reason takes much longer in PoE2 than 1, despite always selling in the end (might be a me thing).

And even if you should succeed in gamblecrafting an “almost” high-end item, the difference in power between yours and the mirror-tier is going to be VAST.

TLDR: I think base currencies need to be cheaper. Give me 20ex annuls, 2ex chaos orbs, cheaper greater/perfect, 1-2div reroll related omens. Put the stupid expensive part of crafting in the omega-high-end which differentiates 15-30% of power except in some omega stupid giga cases.
