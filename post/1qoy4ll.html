After receiving 53% of voteshare in the 2025 AL MVP Race, Jose Ramirez now has the all-time highest MVP voteshare received of someone who has never won an MVP with 3.61 shares

Voteshare basically means the percentage of vote points that a player received in each MVP race. Ramirez has received: 0%, 56%, 50%, 72%, 32%, 44%, 10%, 44%, 53% in each of the 9 seasons he has received MVP votes. So you add them together he has received about 361% worth of votes total, so he has 3.61 MVP Voteshares, good for 26th all time.

The other 25 names above him all have won at least 1 MVP. Hank Aaron still has the highest gap, winning 1 MVP despite having a 5.45 MVP Voteshare, with Ted Williams right behind him (2 MVPs, 6.43 MVP Voteshare). Barry Bonds has the most voteshares at 9.30 but has 7 MVPs to show for it.

The names who have at least 3 MVP Voteshares but never won it:

Al Simmons (3.53)

Bill Terry (3.45)

Eddie Murray (3.33)

Mike Piazza (3.16)

Manny Ramirez (3.06)

Simmons and Terry also benefited a bit from the pre-modern MVP voting system, and it also counts the unofficial ballots in 1929 (AL) and 1930 (both), so theirs may be slightly inflated. So in the modern MVP era, the distinction was held by Murray until now. Plus at least Murray and Piazza won ROY, and Manny finished 2nd. J-Ram wasn’t a full time player at the time so he never had a shot at it.
