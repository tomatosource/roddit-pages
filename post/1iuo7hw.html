Everything is hardcore mode and it’s not fun.

Trial of Sekhemas: go through this long ass gauntlet of challenges to finish out your character and unlock your final builds, also none of your defenses matter because we’ve given you an artificial health bar, also if you die you’re out and your ticket to entry is RNG.

Trial of Chaos: go through this insane gauntlet of RIDICULOUSLY hard challenges to finish out your character and unlock your final builds, also if you die you’re out and your ticket to entry is RNG.

Waystone maps: go through these things looking for random doodads like tablets that are pure RNG to progress the endgame (seriously I’ve done like 40 maps, not a single precursor tablet), also your ticket into the maps is pure RNG, also if you die you’re out.

I really want to sit down and play PoE for an hour or two and the game is like… actively pushing me out and making it not fun. I just got to Trial 5 of my third ascendancy challenge and washed out because the only choice I could take at one point in the run was a debuff that took away 150 honor after every room.

So no matter how cleanly I played, I just got chipped away until I got dead from some random lightning strike that did like 1/20th of my energy shield. I’m 6 levels above the monsters and they really can’t threaten my ACTUAL health bar… but none of that matters, because Honor Bar.

This doesn’t feel good or fun. Probably putting the game away until this changes, which sucks, because it really does scratch the Diablo/ARPG itch. There’s so much to like about this.

But having the game punch you in the gut and send you back to zero all the time is a major bummer.
