TIL the Angels have the longest active drought in MLB without a player hitting three home runs in a single game.

The last time an Angels player hit three home runs in a game was Torii Hunter on June 13, 2009. Since then, every other MLB team has done it at least twice in the regular season.

Here’s how many times each team has done it since the Angels last did it.

New York Yankees — 15

Cincinnati Reds — 13

Colorado Rockies — 11

Obviously, hitting 3 home runs in a game is a difficult thing to do, but a drought this long is pretty bizarre considering the Angels haven’t lacked for power hitters during that time.
