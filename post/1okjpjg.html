Ryan Rollins signed with the Bucks last season on a two-way deal, played around 14 mins per game as a backup to Damian Lillard. The Bucks converted his contract to a standard NBA contract after Ryan Rollins showed his upside on defence and catch and shoot ability, knocking down over 40% from 3.

When Dame went down towards the end of the season, the Bucks previewed their guard rotation without Dame, as KPJ and Rollins earned more minutes and playing time down the stretch of games. Although KPJ was the starter due to his experience and great two man game with Giannis, Rollins increased to around 24 mins per game and excelled in his role, as the Bucks won 10 of their last 14 games. On starts he also showed towards the end of last season he can be an efficient player next to Giannis, shooting over 45% from 3.

The Bucks resigned him this offseason on a 3 year $12 million dollar deal, which could look like a steal. So far this season he has showcased his defensive skills, guarding the best guards and being top 10 in deflections this season. With KPJ out he has also made the starting point guard spot his own, with back to back career nights against the Knicks and Warriors. Couple that with his improved playmaking so far this season (maybe thanks to working with Rondo) and his 3pt shooting (spot up and on ball), he can maybe replicate another defensive guard in Jrue Holiday for the Bucks.
