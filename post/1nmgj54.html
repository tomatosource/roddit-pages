Why are jump shots made with the same front foot as the hand?

In baseball, when you’re throwing, your lead foot is the opposite foot to your throwing hand. If you throw right-handed, the lead foot is the left foot.

In basketball, I’ve noticed that NBA players make jump shots (if there’s a lead foot) with the same lead foot as their shooting. If you are a left handed jump shot, you would lead with the left foot.

Why is that the case? I made a jump shot with the opposite lead foot because that’s what I learned with baseball.
