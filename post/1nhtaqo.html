Who has the better NBA career through 7 seasons - Shai Gilgeous-Alexander or Dwyane Wade (pre-Heatles)?

Dwyane Wade played 7 seasons with the Heat, winning a title, before the Big 3 happened. SGA just wrapped up his 7th season, and is on top of the NBA world right now.

Both seem like intriguing comparisons so I compiled their resumes. Keep in mind that their roles, team contexts, and eras differ.

Dwyane Wade (2003-2010)

  • 1x NBA champion (2006)

  • 1x NBA Finals MVP (2006)

  • 6× NBA All-Star (2005-2010)

  • 2× All-NBA First Team (2009, 2010)

  • 2× All-NBA Second Team (2005, 2006)

  • 1× All-NBA Third Team (2007)

  • 3× NBA All-Defensive Second Team (2005, 2009, 2010)

  • 1x NBA scoring champion (2009)

  • NBA All-Rookie First Team (2004)

  • 25.4 points, 6.6 assists, and 5.1 rebounds per game (with 1.8 steals)

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (2018-2025)

  • 1x NBA champion (2025)

  • 1x NBA Finals MVP (2025)

  • 1x NBA Most Valuable Player (2025)

  • 3× NBA All-Star (2023–2025)

  • 3× All-NBA First Team (2023–2025)

  • 1x NBA scoring champion (2025)

  • NBA All-Rookie Second Team (2019)

  • 24.4 points, 5.1 assists, and 4.8 rebounds per game (with 1.4 steals)

SGA’s stats are backed by consistent development and team growth, peaking right now (where he is the reigning champion and MVP with more success on the outlook).

Wade exploded early and started peaking in his third season, and again through 2008-10. Although his team’s success was brief, he was consistently a top 3 shooting guard since his second season. He arguably enjoyed the last of his peak after this period.

Which basketball career has been more successful, given the two summaries of their careers through 7 seasons? Keep in mind SGA would have been 19-26 years old, and Wade 21-28 years old.
