Warrior feels amazing so far, but there are a few problems.

I’m close to finishing act 2 with Warrior and here are my thoughts so far:

  1. The Abyss mechanic is super hard before getting Herald of Ash, since Warrior doesn’t have any real AoE abilities other than Boneshatter around this point. Boneshatter works well for a while, but good luck dealing with 5 monsters up close while 30 other attack from the back. I feel like something with bigger AoE than Rolling Slam would have been a great addition to make early warrioring feel more complete.
  2. After getting Volcanic Fissure, Abyss is a breeze. It feels suuuper satisfying to clear Abysses with Volcanic Fissure.
  3. New ancestrally boosted slams look and feel magnificent.
  4. I feel like some early zones like the Consort’s Tomb were incredibly hard, due to the lack of sustain at this point in the game. I really think that melee characters, especially Warrior, should have some sort of sustain early on, before even getting to the HP regen nodes. Perhaps it could be an implicit on maces or something, I don’t know.
  5. Rolling Slam should have 2 modes. A single tap only performs the first hit, while holding performs the full ability. Otherwise there is this wacky gameplay loop of Rolling Slam -&gt; Cancel with dodge -&gt; Boneshatter.
  6. The difference in survivability between 1 and 2-handed maces feels too big already and it’s only going to get worse later on. So far I don’t think that anyone sane would ever want to use 2-handed maces without Giant’s Blood + shield.
  7. Mana problems- why does Rolling Slam without any +skill gem levels take 1/3rd of the mana bar per cast? Luckily I dropped Everlasting Gaze and it solved the problem completely, but I feel like mana costs could be halved and Warrior would still run out of mana frequently.
  8. My main issue is still the terrible super-early game, I think that it can get a lot of people disappointed with the class or even the game way too early.

EDIT: 5. is a non issue, ever since I learned that Rolling Slam can be cancelled by other abilities too.
