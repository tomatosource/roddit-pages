I made my first actual interesting build!

I’m sure this has already been done by someone, but discovering it on my own feels great. For the past two years, I’ve been using the most ethical, basic bitch builds possible. It’s been super fun, but I’ve never really tried to play with the inner mechanics of some skills until now.

This build is a Warbringer minion damage/strength stacking Ancestral Totem build.

Ancestral Totem: Every 1 strength applies 1% increased damage for minions from this skill

Choker Chaber Great Hammer: +2 levels to minion skills and Increases and reductions to minion damage also affect your character

Jarngreipr Gauntlets: Stength can satisfy other attribute requirements of melee weapons (this makes the 100 intelligence requirement of Choker Chaber unnecessary, allowing me to go all in on strength)

On the passive skill tree, I stack as much strength as possible. I’m still working on it, but right now I have 344 strength. I also allocated in the Sentinels branch, with 5 passives giving my minions and me each 10% damage, and one passive giving my minions and me 20% damage each. In total from what I can tell, I have 464% increased damage.

I know I can push it even further, but I’m still thinking through how atm. Even though I had to drop a mace with +3 attack skills and gloves with +2 melee skills, I’m still doing the same (if not more) damage. If I can get an amulet going with +skills and a different notable attached to it, I can push it so much further.

I also spec into armor break support of Warbringer passives, allowing me extra physical damage and permanent extra physical damage (thanks to the Uruk’s Smelting lineage support)

Edit: After some more tweaks, I’ve brought it to 577% damage and my Ancestral Spirits are Gigantic. If I go all in, I might be able to get it to 800% increased damage without any +attack/melee skill modifiers.

Also, I forgot to mention that most of my damage is coming from the totems detonating Jagged Ground
