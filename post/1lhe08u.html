Favorite “overqualified” players?

Thought of this after Bill Simmons used the term to describe Kevin Love on the Cavs in his 2008 redraft. Basically, who are some players you respect for taking a lesser or simpler role on a new team in order to contribute to winning? Some of mine:

- Chris Bosh going from a 24⁄11 star to small ball C and tertiary scorer

- Bill Walton reinventing himself as a title-winning sixth man following a litany of injuries

- Ray Allen becoming the ultimate catch-and-shoot role player in Miami

- Al Horford remaining valuable late into his thirties by turning into a stretch five and defensive sparkplug off the bench

Who else fits this?
