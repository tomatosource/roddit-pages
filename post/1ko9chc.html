This series is eerily similar to ‘23 Miami, but now we’re the ones playing with house money. And this team loves being underdogs.

This is why I feel oddly confident about winning game 6, and at least getting it back to game 7. Maybe it’s a mix of post-Tatum-grief/denial and a little mental illness, but what’s the point of watching sports if all optimism is lost?

23 Miami: An inferior team that we were clearly favored to beat, with a mental toughness edge on us. They went up 0-3 in their series and we rattled off 3 straight wins. We were the 2 seed that season, the Bucks were already out of the playoffs and most people had us favored to go to the finals that year. Except Tatum gets injured in game 7, and we tighten up and lose in game 7 due to the Heat honing in on a lesser version of Jaylen. Brogdon was also out that game too.

25 Knicks: We swept them 4 times in the regular season, are clearly the more talented team all year, and were favored in the series. Similar to the first few 23 Miami games, we had leads in those first halves too and then shat the bed in the 4th. The Knicks are mentally tough team, and they do not go away - similar to the Heat. Both led by a hard nosed, tough leader in Brunson and Butler. We blew the first 2 games, won the 3rd, lost the 4th in tragic fashion due to a Tatum injury, and then won the “moral victory/Tatum/heart of a champion” game in game 5. Game 6 is back in New York.

A few things about this team under Joe:

-They love having their backs against the wall, for some unknown reason. Even in the Miami series, they damn near got excited when they were down 0-3. Remember the whole “don’t let us get 1” thing? There’s very similar vibes here.

-Similarly to the players, Joe seems to not be at his best until it’s win or go home. Game 5 was his best coached game by a long shot. It’s almost a fucking shame that he didn’t make those adjustments. Who knows, if he played more Luke/PP in games 1-4 instead of KP, maybe we win and Tatum doesn’t get hurt. But no point in thinking that now. But I say this to say I think Joe will be super locked in for game 6.

-This team is a bunch of masochists and they really love being underdogs. Anyone who remembers the 2018 team (Jaylen/Al were on that team too), I feel like that run really solidified something within the Celtics that they really love playing in high pressure situations.

-We’re road dogs. The Celtics have largely played better on the road during RS and payoffs this year. And what better place to shut a crowd up than MSG.

-Derrick White. That’s all. He’s going to be an all star next season. He’s pretty easily a 20ppg guy with a heavier shot diet.

-Jaylen Brown/Al/Jrue - our vets. They will be ready. So will PP and Luke. Porzingis… godspeed. He was a good dude.

All of that being said, I think the series gets extremely interesting if this comes to a game 7 because we will be home, we will still be underdogs without Tatum (Celtics get a +10 energy boost from being underdogs), and we are largely playing with house money considering most of the media and bettors literally started looking at Knicks vs. Pacers odds. The Knicks aren’t exactly battle tested, and they will be extremely tense in game 7 if it gets there. However I greatly respect Brunson and Thibs and those guys are tough, so I absolutely do not expect either team to run away with IF it gets to a game 7. But man will it be fucking intense.
