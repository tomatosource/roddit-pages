Do you feel like playing SSF for the first time as well?

This. Title. I’ve been playing PoE1 for around 3 years and never I felt like touching SSF as it looked like a chore. We finally have async trade, that’s a real blessing, and I thought it would make me happier forever! But after reaching pinnacle content with my league starter in 0.3 and gathering a lot of currency, for the first time I didn’t want to level another character just for the sake of gearing it up with trade stuff. Somehow my guts are screaming to progress alone. It seems that we were given some amazing tools that make the total solo experience actually enjoyable to go through: greater and perfect essences are really meaningful, greater jewellers start dropping not so late, desecration alongside various omens open a world of many good possibilities, recomb and reforge… this list goes on. Playing SSF in PoE2 for the first time showed me HOW FUN it is to give meaning to every single drop, and somehow GGG is progressing towards a sustainable solo experience. How do you feel about it?
