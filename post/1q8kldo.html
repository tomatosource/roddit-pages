New Player feedback

Played monk a bit during 0.1, but really went hard into the game in 0.4 because DRAGON. Had tried PoE1 some years back but dropped it quickly for reasons i forget. But I have thoroughly loved PoE2, and wanted to give some feedback as a relatively new player.

  1. The overall fantasy of the druid is perfection. The transition between forms is smooth and feels QoL in itself. The forms feel unique and different, with dragon being easily my favorite. It doesn’t feel like a 1-button wonder, but that I can make him do what I want when I want it. Wnat to clear a room with fire? easy. Want to clear it with lightning? smooth. Want to jump in and start swinging? Mouth watering. Even the fact it takes time to get into the air to begin breathing fire is annoying, but still adds to the fantasy, which helps me enjoy the game. But the fact I can adapt to the situation and tackle it in different ways is just fun. Apparently Herald of Fire makes everything explode, which is just icing on the cake at this point.

  2. The story and acts were a blast, easily competing with Diablo. Much more digestable when the attempt I made on PoE1. Could use cinametics to compete with Lost Ark in that area, but still good. Esspecially Act 1, end of Act 3, and everything after that were my favorite. Act 2 and most of Act 3 were fun, but I get the sense it will be annoying doing more than 1 character back to back. Act 2 is basically a desert biome followed by a sand biome followed by rock followed by a cave underneath sand in a desert, while the bosses just don’t have the same degree of cinametic epicness as the wolf dude or ax-swinging sailor tribesman. on reflection I think its because their design was clearer and cooler, there were multiple phases with brief downtime to just sit and be epic and aura farm, and I found them more challenging. The desert final boss was cool but outside of his willingness to sacrifice himself for the mission he was a 30second speedrun, and the robot was really cool, easily best part of act 3, but also treated like a speedrun. On the other hand I loved the act1 and act4 boss were both multiphase aura farmers I had to learn with pure cinema. I remember being the wolf boss gave me chills and made me stand like my team just won a touchdown.

  3. Going into the interlude, maps, lategame, though feels like a small downgrade. I’m not expecting story in what is clearly a grind-fantasy of exploring builds. I am enjoying my build and building it up, but it feels like there is… like a handicap? Build’s are feeling more constrained then free flowing. For example I tried stacking heralds for a multi-color explosion, but that doesn’t work. I tried combining bleed, poison, fire to get a ton of DoTs everywhere but that didn’t work. Most abilities take power charges to be worth anything after tier1 which are difficult to pull off against bosses, which I have learned requires very special gear or gems to go around or force me into a single linear playstyle of building stun. and its at the point where I feel forced to play a linear playstyle to make the tools given to me work where I wonder if I suck at understanding or if I am being put in a cage, and I cant tell which. For example I stacked every stun passive in the druid area of the tree, still wasn’t building a single bit of stun, which made the experience difficult, and it wasn’t until I reached the end of my rope and begged for help that someone told me a piece of my gear prevented it called Prisoners Manacles. As someone who played League for years, the words ‘stun’ and ‘immobilized’ do NOT mean the same thing, so I feel stupid.

I get the sense crafting is not what the developers want you to do either. The crafting system goes out of its way to maximize randomness within itself, which, sure. I don’t really even mind that trying to understand crafting is like studying advanced algebra. But the amount of tools I am given to craft with feel handicapped, leading me to wonder if its something the developers want me to be doing to begin with. Now I feel locked in behind SSF where I am spending what few minutes I have after work trying to find the piece of the puzzle I need to be able to craft something youtubers treat as basic and extremely common craft pieces. Its starting to feel less like a chef experimenting, or a mathematicion working out the puzzle, and more like an office worker putting in the dailies hoping the stars align to get, not even godly tier items, but just basic pieces I can use to craft what I have already puzzled out in my head. The worst part is that I don’t even know if I am ‘grinding’ correctly because when I research where to find items websites are SURPRISINGLY blank. I’ve had to go to many many youtubers, and I am left wondering where they are getting their information from, which makes me feel dumb like its supposed to be intuitive for some reason, while they also boil crafting piece farming down to “just buy on the market”. Which… okay… but I am on SSF.

  1. The activities have nice variety, with some definitely being preferable depending on taste. Some slow down the game and keep you in place, like ritual, while others make the map NOTICABLY DENSE with more enemies to EXPLOOOODE! Lots of fun moments I wasn’t expecting. Like an abyss opened up after a boss to unleash 6 centaur-spiders that were each as powerful as a boss in themselves (that was 10 minutes of panic to work down one at a time). Or another abyss that released 3 centaur-spiders, each with charge, each with stun, in a small room of some mansion. That was 45 seconds of being shoved in a corner and meme’d on while I panic-laughed and died.

  2. Holy bugs! Yes, I can confirm I’ve been stuck in more trees than a dragon should be. What can I say, I wanted to perch apparently lol

  3. Temple of Vaal… I like that atmosphere. I like idea of crafting my map in what is typically a random-based game. I get the sense there is a lot of drama around it. Overall I enjoy it and feel much more positive than negative, but again its a crafted-map in a game that feels like crafting is a big-no-no where once again I am restrained on how many pieces I am given, and restrained on where I can put them, without a reset button, and with large swathes of it being wiped each time I run it. (Yes, I know there is ways of locking it and snaking it, but my point is more just not being given much to play with. I have yet to be able to build a snake to lock in with medallions I haven’t found.) And I cant just put anything anywhere and experiment, I am only able to place pieces in very specific orders which I can only ask… why? Why can’t I plug a corruption room next to a poison room or a robotics next to a commander? I look forward to where the devs take this because I think it has the potential to be one of my favorite activities, but it feels handicapped right now in a similar way to what I said before.

Overall I have been THUROUGHLY enjoying the game and will continue to play it, but wanted to express some thoughts of things I enjoyed and not as much.
