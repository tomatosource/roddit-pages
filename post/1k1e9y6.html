Loving the Communication Lately, But Something’s Getting Lost

Appreciate the dev communication lately, it’s been a big step forward and it really does make a difference knowing feedback is being looked at and responded to.

That said, there’s been a recurring thing that feels a bit off. When the community points out a clear issue, the response often ends up addressing something adjacent to it, but not the actual core of the problem. And the thing that gets changed tends to be something with way less impact than what was originally raised (Recent loot updates).

I don’t want this to come across as disrespectful. I know the devs are smart and clearly care about the game, which is why this is a bit confusing. It just feels like something’s getting lost along the way, whether that’s prioritization, interpretation, or just how the feedback is being processed.

Not trying to start drama, just hoping this can be acknowledged or maybe explained a bit more. If there’s a reason why this is happening, it’d help to understand it. That kind of transparency would go a long way in keeping people on board.
