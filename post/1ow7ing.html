Which players have been traded for the most WAR over their careers?

Jesse Chavez retired earlier this year and it got me thinking about all the players he was traded for over his career. Jesse only accumulated 6.4 career bWAR, but he was traded for 10 players over that time (according to Baseball Reference). I got to wondering how much total bWAR those players were worth. I’ve decided to call the sum of these players’ WAR, TradeWAR or tWAR.

&nbsp;

I’ve broken down Jesse’s numbers below. The “Trade Pieces” column lists the number of players Jesse was a part of as part of the trade. For example, Jesse Chavez was traded with Tucker Davidson for Raisel Iglesias. So, the number of trade pieces is 2, and Raisel’s 18.60 bWAR is divided by two for a tWAR of 9.30.

Player Trade Pieces bWAR TradeWAR
Kip Wells 1 8.000 8.000
Akinori Iwamura 1 4.5 4.5
Rafael Soriani 1 13.800 13.800
Rick Ankiel 3 9.100 3.033
Kyle Farnsworth 3 6.200 2.067
Liam Hendricks 1 9.000 9.000
Mike Bolsinger 1 -1.300 -1.300
Tyler Thomas 1 0.000 0.000
Sean Newcomb 1 4.000 4.000
Raisel Iglesias 2 18.600 9.300

As can be seen in the table above, Jesse Chavez has been traded for 52.4 tWAR. This is a full 46 WAR, or one Thurman Munson, more than Jesse’s career bWAR.

&nbsp;

What other players might have a significant amount of TradeWAR? They don’t even need to have been traded that many times. Just need to have been traded once for the right player. Charlie Chech, for instance, gets 81.8 tWAR just for having been one of two players traded for Cy Young.
