A couple changes I would love in PoE2.

Two large changes I would love to see in PoE2.

  1. Gem level change.

So, I was thinking about it. Whats one of the largest problems we have with Gem levels? It’s the main way to increase power, but because of this you need to have gem level+ on gear, to the point that if the piece of gear can have it, but doesn’t it’s essentially a dead piece of equipment.

So, what I think should happen. Is GGG removes the +Level gems from gear completely, no more on anything at all.

So how do we scale?

Once a gem hits level 20, after finding it in maps. Gems should switch to the PoE1 system and start to gain experience, upwards to level 30. The experience for gems should be MASSIVE, so that it takes a while to do it.

What this does, is move the requirement from gear to the gems themselves. Not only that, but this creates a new trade system for people who need a way to make some currency, have bad luck with drops? No problem, slap on the gem level it up and then sell it to someone who doesn’t want to level it up.

This also helps GGG in balance, and this would immediately turn all unique weapons and gear suddenly better because now +level to skill gems is a dead and gone stat.

And to get an even higher skill level from 30 to 40, could be put onto some super hard to farm and ultra rare chase gems you can slot into your skill tree. something like. “This skill gem slotted into the passive tree increases your skill levels by 3+ for Gems at level 30.”

This turns into super chase items for players. As for power creep, its all at the very top end so it wouldn’t ruin the progress curve.

As for my second change that I want to see.

  1. Change the Rune system.

What do I mean change the rune system? I mean that we should take the best of both worlds for PoE1 and PoE2.

Instead of having 100 different runes already, with another 160+ runes on the way, we’re talking about a massive bloat of rune collectables. We’re talking about a lot of work to know which ones will be good and which ones will be trash to add to filter lists.

Rune page in storage is going to end up having its own darn filtering system soon. Lmao.

So whats the fix to this?

Simple, We change the runes system into a book. You find a rune, you pick it up and its added to your character, but instead of it being an item you pick up and use. It’s an item you pick up and is added to a rune glossary that its bound to your account.

The actual runes you find is something similar to the blacksmith tools in Act 1, when you find a “blank” rune.

But now instead of 266 different runes. The only runes that drop are.

Low, Mid, High, Rare, blank runes.

Just those four. And then when you want to use a specific rune, you take a blank rune of varying quality and then from your glossary you etch the specific rune that you’ve discovered while playing. Then you slot that rune into your gear.

What does this do? Less bloat, less things to filter through on item lists. A simplified system, and QoL for people that want to customize their gear how they want.

Blank Runes themselves can still be traded on the market, however its stream lined.

We already have.

  1. The glossary can be copied over from our current Rune storage page.
  2. The Blank Runes already exist in the game from act 1 quest.
  3. The essence of crafting on blank runes FITS THE THEME, of the new league.
  4. This is a massive QoL for filter makers, and for players in general.
  5. This would essentially quench the players demanding crafting bench from PoE1.
  6. Keeps the aesthetics and system of PoE2 without compromising on it.
  7. Keeps the discovery of finding new runes still the same, you pick them up with RNG, however once you pick them up, that one specific rune doesn’t drop anymore since you’ve learned it.

I honestly think these two changes would massively increase the playbility, remove bloat, and make massive leaps for balance in current PoE2 format. They are also simple to do, most of the systems already exist in PoE2 already. And would make balancing and bloat easier to manage further down the line.
