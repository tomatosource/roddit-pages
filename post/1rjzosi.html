Kevin Durant thankful for second apron bringing parity to NBA: ‘It’s a fun time to be in the league’

What Durant Actually Said:

Kevin Durant said he’s grateful for the salary cap first and second aprons under the NBA’s current collective bargaining agreement.

He believes these rules are helping create real parity in the league, meaning more teams have a legitimate chance to compete for a championship this season instead of a few dominant favorites.

Durant said this season feels wide open and fun for fans because teams are more balanced, and you can’t predict who will be at the top late in the year.

He also noted that the restrictions give teams confidence, even if they aren’t playing their best at any given point, nobody seems clearly superior.
