By losing to the Orlando Magic, the Charlotte will receive a worse first-round pick in the 2026 NBA Draft from the Suns.

TL;DR - With the Orlando Magic advancing to the postseason, the pick the Charlotte Hornets acquired from the Phoenix Suns has moved out of the lottery while the Hornets own pick is now at the very bottom of the lottery. The former’s status is the result of a triple swapping and subsequent trading away of the pick a la the Jusuf Nurkic salary dump last year and this result is irrespective of whether the Suns win or lose as the Hornets will, to keep this as simple as possible, get the worst of the Magic/Suns pick.

Due to their elimination via a wholly unexpected blowout by an Orlando Magic whose coach’s firing is a matter of when and not if, the 44-38 Charlotte Hornets own first-round pick will be in the lottery, receiving the 13 or 14th best odds in the lottery and thus the 13th/14th pick if they do not move in to the Top 4 or if the team below them at 14 jumps in the standings should they be 13th.

Whether their pick will start at the 13th or 14th spot in the lottery is contingent on who wins tonight’s Suns/Warriors matchup; the Suns winning pushes them to 13th as the Warriors possess a much worse record of 37-45 (As of right now, Golden State would have the 11th best odds, the Warriors would place them 14th as the Suns won one less game than the Hornets.

To clarify one more time, wherever the Suns pick ends up, it will be swapped with the 2nd 1st round pick the Grizzlies acquired via the Desmond Bane trade and the worst of the Magic/Suns 1st will go to Charlotte so regardless of the lottery outcomes, Charlotte is getting the Magic’s 2026 1st.

As for where the pick they’ll be receiving from the Magic ends up, that is also dependent on if the Suns make the postseason as they share the exact same record with them and the 76ers, and thus if they both make it the order will be determined via a drawing at a later time. If they make it, the pick could land anywhere from 16-18. If they don’t, it’s anywhere from 16-17 due to the swap rights the Grizzlies hold and the tiebreaker is done via a coin flip instead.

So in short, the Hornets are definitively not getting a lottery pick regardless of tonight’s outcome in the Warriors/Suns matchup. They are looking at some combination of 13 + 16-18 or 14 +16⁄17, depending on what happens in Phoenix tonight.
