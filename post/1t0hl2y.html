For those of you unfamiliar with the disaster draft, the following is a summary:

The NBA disaster draft is a contingency plan activated if five or more players on a team die or are dismembered. Affected teams can select players from other teams, which can protect only five players, ensuring quality talent is available to rebuild. Only one player can be taken from any single team.
Key Rules and Details
Trigger: The Commissioner determines if a “disaster” (e.g., plane crash, catastrophe) has resulted in the loss of 5+ players.
Protection List: All other NBA teams can protect five players (starters/core) from their roster.
Selection Process: The affected team can draft available players from other teams to replace their losses.
Restrictions: Only one player can be drafted from each team.
Salary/Rebuilding: The team likely cannot exceed the salary cap, focusing on taking contracts to rebuild.
Draft Lottery: The affected team may also receive the first overall pick in the next NBA draft

Now let’s just say the Celtics decide to kill White, Vucevic and at least 3 other players after this series or before game 7. They will then be able to take the best sixth men in the league on other terms as long as the contracts work. They could maybe secure bigs such as Naz Reid, Isiah Stewart or Mitchell Robinson to improve depth. Or take guards like Jared McCain or Dylan Harper or THJ. This can improve the rotation and make the Celtics the most deep team in the league.

Furthermore the Celtics would get the first pick in the draft and could secure a generational prospect like AJ Dybansta or Cameron Boozer or whoever. They could also trade down the pick like with Tatum if there is a specific player they want who won’t go first.

These moves would improve the team both short term and long term. It would surely make the Celtics the favourites for the title after this season.
