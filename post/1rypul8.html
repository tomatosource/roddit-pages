Ever feel like, thematically, the two Atlases should be switched?

Be aware that I’m not talking gameplay-wise. As much as I like the idea, the infinite Atlas should, if not go entirely, be heavily reworked into giving actual adgency. Agency? Player-friendly.

With that out of the way:

PoE1 Atlas is supposed to be an infinite real of awake dream worlds, shaped by memory, emotions and/or other soul doodads, where gods and beyond feast and fight and warriors of mythical proportion guard the real world from them.

Meanwhile, PoE2 Atlas is supposed to be actual Wraeclast, like, bombarded (for now) by Corruption, but a finite and definite real place.

But gameplay wise, PoE1 Atlas is fixed and explorable to completion, while PoE2’s is infinite and random.

Feels turned around, don’t you think?

Edit: I know the narrative of PoE2’s Atlas is not set in stone. I don’t believe they’d actually let us lose the fight against Oriana/the Beast. So maybe it’s groundwork for Zana’s Atlas after her PoE1 storyline completion, idk
