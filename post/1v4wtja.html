Tablet Effect Information patch 0.5.4

#Masters:

  • Jado: 0-40% increased Tablet Effect
  • Hilda: 8% increased Tablet Effect per type of Tablet used
  • Doryani: None

#Atlas Passive Tree Sources:

If you have all 336 points of the Atlas Passive Tree, you will receive:

  • 15% tablet effect in general (5% effect x 3 nodes)
  • 5% tablet effect per tablet used in any biome
  • 24% tablet effect if your biome is a city

At a baseline, this nets us 30% effect from using 3 tablets. Cities increase this to 59% effect with 4 tablets.

#Total:

  • Jado —– 3 tablets = 30 to 70% effect = 15% + 15% + 0-40%
  • Jado —– 4 tab city = 59 to 99% effect = 15% + 20% + 24% + 0-40%
  • Hilda —- 3 tablets = 54% effect = 15% + 15% + 24%, &lt;————cannot use same tablet twice
  • Hilda —- 4 tab city = 91% effect = 15% + 20% + 24% + 32%, &lt;—cannot use same tablet twice
  • Doryani - 3 tablet = 30% effect = 15% + 15%
  • Doryani - 4 tab city = 59% effect = 15% + 20% + 24%

#Useful Breakpoints:

  • +2 mods cannot go to +4 under any circumstances. Hilda can guarantee that +2 mods such as “Map has 2 additional random modifiers” will always go to 3 without needing to use a City Map. Doryani and Jado guarantee this on City maps.

Fun side note, if you run a corrupted waystone with Jado, Jado adds an extra mod since the map is corrupted. If you run an 8-mod corrupted waystone in a city biome with Jado, and you use 4x +2 map mod tablets, they will all be upped to +3 from the tablet effect, giving +12 total. Jado makes your 8-mod waystone into a 9-mod, and now you can run a hilariously difficult and rewarding 21-mod waystone. If you do this with Doryani, you will get a 20-mod map but you also get 40% increased monster effectiveness, rares have 60% chance of monster mods, and 20% item rarity.

  • +3 mods such as +3 Ritual Altar Rerolls can only go to +5 using Hilda or Jado. Hilda can guarantee this in a City map. 67% effect is the minimum threshold for +5, thus he has a 9.76% chance of getting +5 in a non-city, and an 80.5% chance of getting +5 in a city map.

Jado Effect math: Jado’s random effect roll varies from 0% to 40%, giving us 41 possible outcomes. For the +5 in a non-city calculation, I took the range 30-70% and found that 4 of those 41 values are 67 or higher. (4 / 41) = 9.76%

  • +4 mods such a Four Additional Abysses will go to +7 on Hilda’s city maps, and have a 61% chance to go +7 for Jado’s city maps.
  • +5 mods such as “Breaches spawn 5 additional Rare Monsters when Stabilized” on Unique Breach Tablets will go to +9 in Hilda’s city maps, and have a 49% chance to go +9 in Jado’s city maps.
