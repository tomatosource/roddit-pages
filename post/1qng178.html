DeAndre Ayton future in the NBA

Sometimes he is DominAyton

Most times he’s not

However, he’s still a good value big for $8 million/year. I think the problem really is the high expectations by people. At this stage, he is who he is, mostly a low motor big that can have a solid outing every now and then. What he is not, is a starting caliber center anymore, especially for a team that has aspirations to contend for a title.

At \(8million/year, we just need to accept that he’s a role player. He will be off on most nights but has a decent enough skill set as an NBA player, he is best suited to be backup rotation big that can give some scoring off the bench. His defense is not terrible as he’s mobile enough (mostly depends on his effort level). With a more defined role as a bench player on a salary in the range of \)8-$10 million, he could be more appropriately utilized.
