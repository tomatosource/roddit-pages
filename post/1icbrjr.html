The Boston Celtics are severely underperforming and it’s a coaching problem

The roster isn’t the problem. And trading for some 9th man isn’t going to fix anything if it was anyways.

The team is playing significantly below their capabilities. They aren’t doing what worked for them last season and the coaching staff isn’t emphasizing it. They turnover the ball too much in key moments, they don’t defend the 3 and honestly the defense in general is atrocious, they don’t rebound, they miss their FTs, they don’t score in transition, poor late game execution, they don’t utilize Tatum as much as they should, etc. It’s just seems like they’re doing everything wrong. Like the complete opposite of what worked last year. It’s a clear failure by the coaching staff for not enforcing these things.

They should be LAPPING the field and on a 70 win pace. They have BY FAR the most talented team in league. It’s insane they’re at risk of not getting the 40⁄20 rule. I’ve never seen a team underperform this much since….the 2022 Celtics! It’s embarrassing and ridiculous what I’m watching right now.

If they go out sad in the playoffs, it’ll be because of the bad habits they’re continuing to build up and the coaching staff not doing anything about it. NOW IS THE TIME TO ADDRESS THESE PROBLEMS. Don’t wait until you’re down 3-1 in the Conference Finals. FIX IT NOW, Joe Mazzulla.
