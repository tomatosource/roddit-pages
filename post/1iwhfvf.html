Report: Kevin Garnett to Join Timberwolves Immediately Upon Ownership Change

Source

According to Walters, Wolves legend Kevin Garnett will be hired to work in the Wolves’ front office immediately upon Lore’s majority ownership being made official. This, after he has mostly avoided Target Center for the last decade due to his unyielding hatred for Glen Taylor.

As many know, KG has had an ongoing feud with Glen Taylor for years. This mainly stems from Taylor promising Flip Saunders and Garnett some sort of minority ownership opportunity. Then Flip passed and Taylor apparently didn’t follow through on his promise.

This is highlighted by KG’s famous line “I don’t do business with snake mutha fuckas”

For Wolves fans, Kevin Garnett is the best player in franchise history and one of the best players of all-time. For it to be 2025 and we have yet to retire his number has been nothing short of a slap in the face to Wolves fans. Seeing this report is awesome and I know many of us are looking forward to him being associated with the franchise again
