POE2 Support Gems are Anti Fun

Remember the days where you slot in a skill, Arc or Venom Gyre for example. Where every single support gem you slot in was going to be a banger, you couldn’t wait to put in that next support because you knew, it was going to be bigger, faster, more projectiles, faster clear, decimation! Adrenaline, Dopamine! ARPG!

In POE 2, I am currently playing Poisonburst Arrow, and I’m at my 3rd support gem, and I am unexcited and depressed. No support is simple and exciting and fun. No support is making my skill faster, stronger, more badass. Every single support I could put in comes with over complicated conditionals and downsides. And then I will still get owned by white mobs after I slot it in.

If you want extra damage, You have to take debilitating downsides such as “30% Reduced movement speed on use”, or the HILARIOUS “Attack in the random indicated direction, OMEGALUL?”. You want your curse bigger? Ok but you will have to wait 3 business days before it activates.

And you can only use each support gem 1 time, so you better have a spreadsheet and a POE2 degree to figure out what support gems should go into which build.

I really prefer simple supports we can use multiple times, that are intuitive, I can slot in and get back to playing the game. And not “Browsing” supports like I’ve been doing and then settling on “I mean I guess…”.

Thanks for coming to my support rant.
