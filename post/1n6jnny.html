Missing that “one more map” feeling…

In all of my favorite PC games… across all categories of ARPG, FPS, RTS, turn based etc…one of the defining characteristics is that emotional hook for one more map. One more fight. One more mission.

The most successful games would all keep pulling me back in and giving me some sort of payoff for clicking the go button one more time. And they respected my time, or at least it felt like they did.

Last night, after my 3rd completely ridiculous abyss in a row where I just kited around the same place for several minutes and killed a never-ending stream of identical abyss monsters without seeing any other portion of the map…I realized I had no desire to hit that go button again.

I like so much about the patch, act 4 was amazing and the abyss mechanic is a good idea, but the execution of huge maps and grindy slog mechanics are killing it for me.
