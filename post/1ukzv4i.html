Best Farming strat for noobs 20 Div/Hour guaranteed (not clickbait)

This one is for some of the casuals / noobs still struggling to make currency and maybe overwhelmed when it comes to mapping… this isn’t bait jump into Chaos Trials!

Currently all the fragments for completing the trial are between 6.5 / 7 Divs each, literally zero investment just slap a 10 floor Inscribed Ultimatum and away you go. Most of the soul cores also sell for 100-200 ex so on average over 10 completed runs i’d say you should average 75 Div minimum.

Now is this glamorous? certainly not. Unless you want to try and use 3 fragments to challenge the Trialmaster at the end for a miniscule chance of dropping “The Adorned” jewel (which i wouldn’t recommend) you won’t be getting any big drops.

I can’t imagine the market will tank either (if for some reason this post blows up) Chaos Trials is by far the most boring content in the game, hence why no-one wants to waste any time doing it.

If you are still reading this and want to know some of the worst modifiers to avoid my top 3 would be

1 - Escalating Damage taken : In each encounter room, damage taken will slowly increase by 1% per second, up to 50%

2 - Impending Doom : Rings of Doom appear on the ground which grow over time, exploding for Physical damage once they reach a maximum area

3 - Volatile Fiends : Monsters release deadly Volatiles on death. Rare monsters leave larger ones

If you get unlucky and you have to choose between all 3, probably take Volatile Fiends but be careful if you get stunned and multiple of these orbs fly at you you will get oneshot.

One final note I am not sure if Chaos Trials is melee build friendly so keep that in mind, for everyone else as long as you are Elemental Res capped and maybe 30% minimum Chaos Res you should be fine.
