What is everyone hoping for today?

I’m excited to see what GGG has been cooking up! I’m curious what is your bucket list for the stream?

Personally I’m looking for a few things.

  1. More crafting. I don’t want Poe1 crafting. I actually like the current base of crafting a lot. I just want more ways to fix my loot as someone who doesn’t like trading.

  2. Multiple classes. The Huntress is gonna be great but it’d be so sick if they released 2 classes. I’m not expecting this to happen at all just hoping!

  3. New league mechanic! Either something from Poe1 but changed or something brand new.

  4. Something done with mapping. Not sure what they could do but right now I spend 5 minutes running a map and get like 30 seconds of a league mechanic which is the only time I’m excited about a map. Hoping for some surprises added.

  5. I like the slower gameplay and no traversal skills. But I want the monsters to reflect that. They’re so fast that you can’t play how they originally intended you to play so I’d love for them to slow enemies down a little bit.

That’s really all. I’m curious what you guys want to see!
