I think I discovered a niche trick with crafting with Astrid’s Creativity.

Was trying to craft myself a Grasping Ring, and when working on prefixes I noticed that I could basically guarantee remove desecrated mods without having to Omen of Light/Annul.

Basically, if you have only 1 mod on one side (e.g. prefixes) and is desecrated, if you don’t like the revealed mod you can use an Omen of crystallisation with a cheap essence that gives a mod on the same side to overwrite the revealed modifier. then you use that same omen to put on a Mark of the Abyssal Lord and try again.

you could do this with 2 mods on the same side as well, but it’s only a 50⁄50 chance of overwriting the desecrated mod, unless you had a second socket with another Astrid’s Creativity.
