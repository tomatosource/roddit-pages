Why do the late 2000s and early 2010s draft classes seem to be so plagued by injuries?

It seems that a large proportion of the best players from those draft plagues have just had their careers plagued by injuries.

I am specifically talking about all-star/superstar players who entered the league between 2007 and 2014.

Examples:
Derrick Rose (ACL), Kevin Durant (Achilles, Jones Fracture), Kyrie Irving (Knee Fracture, Torn ACL), Anthony Davis (nagging injuries throughout his career), John Wall (knee, achillies), Klay Thompson (achillles, ACL), Kawhi Leonard (Knee Injuries), Joel Embiid (Knee Injuries), Demarcus Cousins (Knee, Achilles), Blake Griffin (Knee), Kemba Walker (knee)

This is basically a list of the best players who entered the league during that time, with the only notable emissions being Steph Curry, Lillard, Russell Westbrook, and Paul George (worth noting that Curry had severe ankle issues, and that Westbrook and PG both suffered injuries from freak accidents that mostly did not seem to sap their athelticsim or change their style of play, and that Lillard recently had a torn achilles, though near the end of his career)

Why could that be the case? And why hasn’t it seemed to affect the earlier generation of stars (Lebron, CP3, Melo, Dwight) or the recent generation (Shai, Luka, Brunson, Jokic, Giannis)? It is worth noting that the recent generation does still suffer from it (Haliburton and Tatum this year).
