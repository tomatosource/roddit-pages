Why do you think Joe’s played so many double big lineups this year?

The simple answer is we have three good bigs (and a fourth who’s playable) and not as much depth at the wing position. I’m wondering if that’s the the only reason, or if it’s something deeper than that. Do you think there’s another reason?

My theory is that he’s anticipating certain playoff matchups where he knows we’ll need a ton of size on the floor, so he wanted everyone to get used to it over the course of the season. Another possible theory might be a little out there, but perhaps Joe is intentionally sacrificing speed on offense and defense, forcing the team to work harder to create ball movement on offense and rotate on defense, so when the playoffs roll around he can unleash the single-big lineups and it feels that much easier on both sides of the ball. Kinda like how baseball players put a donut on their bat during warmups. That’s probably some B.S. fanfic on my part, but if anyone would do something like that, it’s Joe.

What do you think? Do you expect this many double big minutes in the playoffs?
