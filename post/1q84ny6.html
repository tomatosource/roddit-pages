Essence, Citadel, Bossing, Atziri rush, random BS go mapping strat (25Div per hour without jackpots)

EDIT: I guess a youtuber made this strat a few days ago, my apologies I dont watch all content creators so I didn’t know haha. Ill credit AP for this I guess lol.

This strategy is mainly a bossing strategy that has an essence/fragment side hustle for passive income while you hunt for Jackpot drops.

Using the Grand Project tablet, you tower hop, looking for citadels, temporal sandstorms, and jade isles. When you get these maps, use Visions of Paradise tablet to run them twice.

While hunting for these maps, you are farming essences using 3x overseer tablets with 2 additional essences, and you are farming temple crystals to rush Atziri.

The atlas tree is simple: all essence nodes.

This strategy nets you around 25Div an hour WITHOUT the jackpot drops.

After around 50 maps of this, I dropped 1 Atziris Splendour, and 2 rakiatas flow, which all sold for a total of 320 Div. I wouldn’t say this was lucky, because I ran quite a few jade isles and fought atziri 5 times. Which I think is about right for the drop rates.

Oh and if anyone is worried about tablet prices for essences just roll them yourself, it isnt hard to get essences. Right now 2 additional essence overseer tablets is about 250ex, visions of paradise is 25c, and grand project is 7c.

Also I think I got unlucky with my essence drops as well so if you get more than 1 essence of horror like I did your div per hour goes up quite a bit.

Just wanted to share.
