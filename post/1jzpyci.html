Martial weapon prefixes need a redesign

Most of the time when looking for a martial weapon for an attack build it’s almost like the only thing that matters is physical DPS.

It doesn’t matter whether or not you convert your damage to something else, Phys practically always comes out on top, and as an added injury it’s also way more versatile. Doesn’t matter if you’re going for Lightning projectile build, melee bleed or fire slams. You can’t go wrong with a weapon with high Phys DPS. Meanwhile elemental affixes feel too weak and restrictive.

In my opinion a weapon with fire damage prefix should be much better than a similar weapon with physical prefix if you are using fire-based skill.

Weapon bases which don’t have physical base damage (e.g. Cultist bow, Smithing Hammer, Strinking Spear, Crackling Qstaff) are significantly worse, if not completely useless.

Some suggested solutions:

  • Turn these mods into “weapon damage increase” mods which apply to all local damage
  • Remove these mods, rebalance flat damage mods, and add interesting new mods like “Ailment magnitude increase”
