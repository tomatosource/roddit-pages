Suggestion for bringing back long/epic boss fights and concoction builds

One of the things that became a big talk in the very first impressions of PoE 2, in the start of Early access, were the epic almost dark souls’esc boss fights. I think we still have this to some extent in the campaign on league start, when people are still leveling with subpar gear.

However, it feels like it has become very normalized to kill bosses in less than a few seconds. Its almost like I won’t even attempt a boss fight in the endgame if I see that it will take me longer than 1 minute to kill it. So instead of doing the early pinnacle boss fight, I will just wait until I have enought DPS to one shot it.

I guess this will always exist to some extent, due to the temptation of just one shotting stuff. However I think what feels especially bad with trying to take on a boss fight when it might take me 15 minutes or more to beat it because my damage isn’t that high yet, is that I will end up running out of mana flask charges when the boss has 30% hp left. So at this point I have to make a decision if I give up on the fight, or keep going for another 15 mins, just to kill the boss with my weak base attack. The only exception to this if you have leech and mana regen to out-regen the mana cost of using your skills consistnetly, but it sucks that our only 2 choices are “kill the boss with enough damage before you run out of mana flasks” or “have enough mana sustain so you never have to use mana flask”.

I think they could make this alot better by adding a way for us to gain flask charges during boss fights. Right now you can only gain flask charges from killing stuff, but that won’t work in boss fights (unless you’re fighting a boss that spawns minions).

I was trying out some concoction this league because I heard so much bad stuff about it and wanted to see if it was true. I was surprised how okay it worked for clearing, maybe not the best damage but I could atleast run t15 maps and my mana flask charges were constantly sitting at max. However in boss fights I would get the boss down to like 30% life, relatively quickly, but then I would just be out of mana flask charges and there is literally nothing I can do.

So I wonder if they can solve 2 birds with 1 stone here, both making concoction builds valid and make it more tempting to attempt tanky bosses when your damage can’t “one shot” them. Give us some way of gaining flask charges during boss fights, I don’t know how, but there must be something better than “gain 0.2 flask charges per second”.

TLDR

Give us some better way for generating flask charges (especially mana flask charges) during boss fights, both to make concoction builds more valid, but also to make it more tempting to attempt endgame boss fights before you have enough DPS to just one-shot it.
