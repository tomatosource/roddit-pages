What big takeaways have you compiled after 3 preseason games?

Personally I’ve learned a ton, and some of it im curious to hear other opinions on because it feels lesser mentioned.

  1. This is my biggest one, I think the reason Joe is prioritizing rebounding so much is because he knows the only way we stay competitive is if we have a monster offense, only way we have a monster offense is if we go small(Pritchard-white-Hauser-brown-queta/boucher). If we go big with Boucher or Tillman at the 4 and queta at the 5, we solve the rebounding and maybe get a little better defensively but our offense becomes plain and average. This dawned on me today and im almost certain this is where Joe’s mind is at; he’s trying to justify going small by forcing our guys to be the best defensive rebounders they possibly can be.

  2. I truly don’t think Baylor can play and if we could dump his contract for free or for a 2nd I would do it in a heartbeat and directly replace his potential minutes this year with Hugo. This feels wrong because im sure Baylor works his ass off and tries, but it just doesn’t work. He has lead feet and can’t move well defensively, he’s a good but not great playmaker, can’t really score anywhere but from 3 and even from 3 he’s shooting horribly, his form is all arm and looks unnatural. Hugo on the other hand just looks ready to me; super clean looking spot up 3, does all the role player things you want, super strong nba ready body who will be a good defender from day 1, also gives you a cutter/dunker alongside the catch and shoot stuff offensively, I genuinely see no reason he can’t get minutes in the regular season.

  3. I am nowhere near closer than I was before preseason to knowing what our center rotation will be. Genuinely have no clue, Boucher looked the best but he’s also the smallest/skinniest and least center-y center of the bunch. Garza provides energy, great screens and rebounding but he’s a ball stopper and has the slowest feet I’ve maybe ever seen. Tillman is just all around solid but gives up a lot of height so probably doesn’t help our rebounding enough, kind of same sort of player as Boucher functionally. I feel like I’d lean queta especially if we go small? Just go Pritchard/white/brown/hauser and set them on fire on offense and throw queta as the 5 to hold down the paint/rebounding.

What are your takeaways? Agree with mine?
