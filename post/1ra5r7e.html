Kon Knueppel being 1 three away from the league lead as a rookie is insane

At this point in the season, Kon Knueppel being just one made three away from leading the league in 3PM is actually crazy.

He’s a rookie. Not a second year jump. Not some random hot month. A rookie keeping pace with the best shooters in the league.

And it’s not like he’s just standing in the corner waiting for kick outs. He’s running off screens, relocating, taking tough ones when the clock is low. Teams know he can shoot and he’s still getting them up.

Most rookies struggle once defenses adjust. He’s doing the opposite. The volume is real, the minutes are real, and the confidence is obvious.

If he actually ends up leading the league in made threes, that’s ridiculous territory for a first year player. Even being this close right now says alot about how legit his shot is.

We might be watching one of the best shooting rookies ever and it doesn’t even feel that loud yet.
