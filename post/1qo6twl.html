The Economy and me

Hi guys! As a long time poe1 player as well as having played poe2 since launch I’ve found this league very peculiar, between a more limited time to play and not jumping on the temple tech quickly enough.

I genuinely believe this type of economy is great for a more casual player based mostly on the trickle down effect making mid to higher end gear, failed corrupts and sanctifies and rare uniques considerably cheaper. Having said that though I’ve always been able to somewhat succeed in past leagues through a lucky drop or two pushing me to a point where I can begin crafting for profit, whereas this league I’ve kept afloat mostly by small trade arbitrage which is incredibly dull and I’ve only done enough to work toward a next upgrade when I’m within striking distance.

So my question how are my fellow semi casual poe enjoyers finding the current economy? Do you prefer it?
