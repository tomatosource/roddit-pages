Some observations after watching some full ganes from the 80s.

I’m a newer NBA fan, started watching during the 2018 season as a celtics fan and have watched 60+ full games every year since.

Bored one night, i decided to watch some full 80s games, focusing on the mid 80s Celtics. Celtics vs Bulls in 86 and game 6 of the 84 finals.

Some instant differences i immediately took note of was the interior passing, standing contested finishes, rules, pace, and commentating.

  1. The interior passing skill of nearly every player on the court was incredible. Bird, Jordan, Dennis Johnson, Magic and Kareem in particular made incredible interior passes in super crowded paints between multiple defenders. Passes that i rarely see in todays game. Dennis Johnson to Larry Bird faking a floppy right under the basket with McHale and Parish on each block really stood out. 3 offensive players in the paint, yet the pass was pinpoint on target, on time. Magic to Karewm back to a cutting Magic was also cool to see

  2. Rarely in the NBA today do players put themselves in the positions to catch and immediately finish while standing in the paint. If you caught the ball within 5 feet, you would be expected to put up a shot. There were tons of double clutch, tough standstill finishes between multiple defenders in packed paints where players got shots off that i would never expect to be taken today. It was really cool.

  3. Rules. Obviously the driving layup traveling call and illegal defense rule has changed, but I’ll talk about some things otherwise that stood out. Any handoff or catch with steps into a jumper were immediately ruled traveling, which is one traveling change i have not seen talked abiut as often. Also the offensive foul. Any drive that dislodged a defender was immediately whistled an offensive foul. I did still see players talking to and complaining to the refs about calls, but genuinely saw no flops in the games i watched. Refs also still made obviously wrong calls, this hasnt changed.

  4. The pace and style of play was very interesting. I know the teams i watched were known for playing well in the fastbreak, but the rate they pushed the ball in transition was fun to see. Tons of long outlet passes and early shots, including long jumpers. However, in the half court, play was methodical with very little dribbling, lots of set plays and passing. Players had specific spots or shots they would shoot. Other than the stars of these teams, the bulls center shot quite a few elbow jumpers, the lakers backup center also had a hookshot and guards shot a lot of 12-19 foot jumpers.

  5. Commentating was very similar to today, with lots of talk about how the rules have changed, players get away with more rule infractions, etc.
