“Meaningfull combat” in poe2 takes place during the campaign

The game is set up in such a way where scaling of damage becomes exponential the futher you get into the game. I’m not really sure why so many people are hung up on this meaningfull combat narrative when its clear that it doesnt happen in late game the same way as during the campaign and its by design, im pretty sure devs know how the game and math works. THIS IS A GOOD THING.

If you have a damage skill which has 500% scaling at lvl 20, you then add a huge weapon base to it, a bunch of more damage sources, hundreds of increased damage % points, shock, DOTs and other sources which just give you more scaling, there is no way you mean to have a game be centered around meaningfull combat late game. If they changed the game to work in such a way where the monsters infinitelly scale with your damage it would become just a pointless runaround which is always feels the same. The point of poe is go from the beach with no gear to becoming a god killing loot machine. There is no way to change this, the moment they change this, they will kill their game.

Meaningfull combat is restricted to the campaign, and tbh just the first run on normal. By the time you get to cruel you already scale so hard with just some ascendancy points that it becomes a joke. This is in my opinnion good even because i dont want to replay the campaign again and have it be a huge drag.

Combos are in the game since poe1. If you have to set up before a boss with lightning rod or orb, cast -resistance, do stack rage or whatever and then boink the boss, then it’s a rotation, its a fucking combo, what ever you wanna call that shit, point is it already exists.
