Act 4 is coming in 0.3 - 99% confirmed

Yesterday we got new information that pretty much confirm that Act 4 is coming in 0.3.

DarthMicrotransaction (a content creator) was invited by GGG to visit their offices in New Zealand and interview employees there and is now releasing some content from that trip. He is not allowed to give spoilers, but there were some comments by GGG employees and background info that pretty much confirm Act 4 is coming in 0.3.

  1. The Music Director Kamil Orman-Janowski was asked in his interview to provide a keyword teaser for 0.3 boss fights and he said its: Tribal

We know from the official poe2 artbook that act 4 is the Karui islands which are islands inhabited by the different Karui tribes and we know from the artbook we will be fighting (among other things) corruption infected tribal leaders (and a boss worshiped by a tribe of goblins).

The comment from the music director pretty much 100% confirms Act 4 is coming in 0.3 combined with the info from the official Poe2 artbook.

Source for Music director comment
