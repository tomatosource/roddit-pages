Act 4 is coming in 0.3 - 99% confirmed

Yesterday we got new information that pretty much confirm that Act 4 is coming in 0.3.

DarthMicrotransaction (a content creator) was invited by GGG to visit their offices in New Zealand and interview employees there and is now releasing some content from that trip. He is not allowed to give spoilers, but there were some comments by GGG employees and background info that pretty much confirm Act 4 is coming in 0.3.

The Music Director Kamil Orman-Janowski was asked in his interview to provide a keyword teaser for 0.3 boss fights and he said its: Tribal

We know from the official poe2 artbook that act 4 is the Karui islands which are islands inhabited by the different Karui tribes and we know from the artbook we will be fighting (among other things) corruption infected tribal leader(s) and a mid-act boss worshiped by a tribe of goblins.

The comment from the music director pretty much 100% confirms Bosses from Act 4 are coming in 0.3 combined with the info from the official Poe2 artbook.

Source for Music director comment

There’s also a few other things that further increase the likelyhood we’re getting Act 4:

In the background footage of the GGG office tour you can for a brief few seconds see a GGG employee playing Act 4 (beach/island area) before noticing that they’re recording something at the desk next to theirs and they quickly exit the zone and go to the character selection screen which shows Act 4 loading screen and character select idle screen, which we know is Act 4 since we saw it during exilecon2023

(Source: Darth stream, I don’t have the exact timestamp, it was during the studio tour)

Darth said on his stream that the only thing he’ll say is (since he promised GGG not to leak things) that 0.3 is bigger than 0.2. (which GGG also mentioned previously)

(Source: Darth stream, I don’t have the exact timestamp, it was during monster balance interview)
