If GGG wants me to care about ground loot more, they need to make it more legible

Dipped back into PoE 1 and it really shows how much barebones the “crafting” system is that we currently have. A ton has been said about crafting, so I won’t linger on the subject for long, but the ground loot in that game is 99% worthless garbage, and it’s just not worth picking anything up because of that. They’ve said they want PoE 2 to make you care more about the loot you pick up off the ground, but they’ve barely changed how loot generation works, they just turned off alts and scours and the bench. They took away our main way to re-roll for good loot and did not provide the tools to adequately replace it.

I’m not opposed to their core principle of ground loot being a larger source of gear, and us spending less time at a crafting bench outside of making perfect endgame items. But anytime I click on something and put it where it takes up space in my inventory or stash, I want to know it has value before I ferry it back home (currency takes up 1 space, is stackable, and is supposed to be equivalent to gaining rolls on better gear). My inventory is the chokepoint, and there’s so much garbage being generated that it’s easier just to not allow anything into it. I would propose two things to make it so we can actually interact with ground loot better:

  1. No more unidentified items, and make it so item filters can help you search for specific affixes. I’m not really annoyed by just ctrl+clicking on Doryani or The Hooded One whenever I come back to town, but just imagine what loot would look like if we were able to instantly highlight drops with triple resistances or all T1 rolls. Picture the all white background and gong sound you’d hear from “Rare Bow (600 DPS)”. I would be stoked to find an awesome sword mace off the ground, I just can’t be bothered to bring them all back to Doryani to ID them and then read through them.

  2. Hand-in hand with this, we absolutely need a customizable-in-game item filter if we want to be able to read our ground loot. I use filterblade of course, but you can literally see half the posts on this sub are people who don’t use any kind of loot filter. Why? Because it’s literally not in the game. I imagine this would be extremely difficult from a UI perspective, but I think it’s not just helpful but necessary for a good experience.

The combination of these things is kind of present in the tiered item system. You know a T5 unidentified item is likely to roll well, and your loot filter will certainly highlight it. But the odds are still fairly low it will actually be good, and the above system accomplishes the same exact thing but cuts out the middle man.

Again, I’m happy for rares off the ground to be the source of my upgrades. But I simply cannot and will not pick up everything that drops that might be useful. If this is the system we’re going with, where we devalue currency and pick up more loot drops, I need to know it’s good before I need to click on it.
