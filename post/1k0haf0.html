My opinion on the patch last night

Last night we had a new patch and here is what i think about it.
Finally we can change our ascendancy! TY GGG
Atlas bookmarks are great QOL, we really needed that
It’s amazing that we only get greater runes now from high level maps.
Infinite tries on tier zero pinnacle bosses is pretty cool i love that.
Ty for looking into light stun and changing it a bit &lt;3 It feels much better now

Now what i don’t like is the stash tab affinities. It’s amazing that we now have breach, expedition, ritual, socketables and fragments affinities, but im not a fan of how they work. Here is an explanation why:

When you put an affinity for fragments on your stash tab, it will be for Baryas/Ultimatums, tablets, AND boss summon fragments like Breach Splinters. I would love if those were actually separated. Tablets and Baryas/Ultimatums should have separate affinities for sure, and boss summon fragments should go to their respective affinity. Breach splinters should always go to Breach affinity stash tab instead of Fragments stash tab.

Besides that everything else is great, ty for working on the game to make it more enjoyable

P.S. Pretty please remove Vaal Factory from mapping :(
