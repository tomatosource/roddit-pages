Expedition Feedback and thoughts

All the leagues have had attention for 0.5, and they are all in a really good spot, (yes, even Vaal Temple) in terms of risk/reward (except Delirium, I still dislike Delirium, I have always disliked Delirium).

With Expedition, the move to island exploration is a solid concept, the risk/reward ratio is pretty close to perfect, push too hard and you’re gonna struggle, but the drops are great, the process is smooth, and the idea fresh.

However, there are some items I think still need tweaking (and this so true for all content of course)

  • Grand Expedition Map choice

I don’t know why, when we have sooo maaany map types, were the one we have chosen.

Sloughed Gullies is almost non-compatible with Expedition, GGG you HAVE island Maps!

I, like a lot of players I guess, simply place a few bombs to grab some gold chests and move on.

Can we get a revisit of the maps pool for the Grand Expeditions.

  • Underground areas

What is the point of these? One in particular stretches on for ages, to give you a bunch of chests that drop rewards NOT boosted by the expedition! You have no choice but to walk all the way back to the entrance to go back, meaning you either leave this one to last, or, more likely, skip them altogether.

These areas could have been SO interesting, offering random encounters, new rewards, but a bunch if basic chests? Ugh. Hey, how about stuffing them with Verisium? I mean, there’s even a Gilded version for gold, that drops, ooh 10,000 gold? WOW!

  • Invulnerability

OK, can we please have a look at this? I spent 20 minutes beating a double remnant on one map. Yes, it was challenging, yes, I did complete it successfully, and yes, it was a PITB.

I don’t know what to do, but having an invulnerable foe with mana drain, that moves at Mach 3, or worse teleports directly on you, that you cannot avoid, or kill, while you run around with 60% speed in werewolf mode to try and get some breathing room to kill the other monsters that you have to kill first, racing around the entire sodding map…is not peek gameplay.

  • Remnants rewards rarely match the effort

I said that the overall risk/reward was great, with one exception. Why are so many high rune remnants utter garbage? I know GGG can’t predict the market, but no one could have thought Flux’s were worth 8 runes, or those useless rune spells that are almost universally unused, and don’t get me started on the alloys.

When I see an 8+ rune remnant, I want to be excited, then you click on it and: “ahh well, let’s hope I get a Div”

Look, I know this is all sounds like negative Nancy stuff, I DO love Expedition, but it just needs some tweaking to get it perfect.
