Two unusual NBA scoring records were set by Spurs players, both New Yorkers, against the Knicks in the last 15 months.

I wanted to give you all a fun fact regarding the upcoming Finals matchup between the Spurs and the Knicks.

These two games:

  • Most points in a game in under 20 minutes played

  • Most points in a game without a 2-point attempt

were both by New Yorkers playing for the Spurs against the Knicks within the last 15 months.

Here’s the info on the games:

Sandro Mamukelashvili, born in New York City and raised in Tbilisi, Georgia, scored 34 points against the Knicks on March 19, 2025. He shot 13⁄14 from the floor (including 7⁄7 from three) off the bench in 19 minutes and 27 seconds, for the most points in an NBA game in under 20 minutes. He also had 9 rebounds, 3 assists, and 1 steal.

Julian Champagnie, born in Staten Island and raised in Brooklyn, scored 36 points against the Knicks on New Year’s Eve, 2025. He was 11⁄17 in shooting and from three (plus 3⁄3 from the free throw line). He did not attempt a 2-point shot. This is the most points that anyone has scored without attempting a 2-point field goal. He also had 6 rebounds and 1 block.
