What Can The Celtics Do To Better Protect Al Horford?

Last season the Celtics were enjoying the splendor of Kristaps Porzingis being healthy for the majority of the season and consistent production from Luke Kornet, and later Xavier Tillman, which helped keep Al Horford fresh for the playoffs run where he held down the fort heroically when KP was sidelined for all but 5 games during the title run.

But this year, Kornet and Queta have been up and down and KP’s been in and out of games to avoid injury. This led to more Horford minutes when last year he came off the bench and played sparingly.

Is there anything the Celtics can do to better protect Horford? Is there a trade candidate?
