My MLB Expansion/Realignment Plan - 8 Team Divisions

Someone else posted their idea of what MLB will look like with 32 teams, 8 divisions of 4 teams each. I’m not a fan of 4 team divisions. Do you want a playoff team with a losing record? Because that’s how you get a playoff team with a losing record. Why not 4 divisions of 8 teams instead?

AL East: BOS, NYY, TB, TOR, BAL, CHI SOX, CLE, DET /// AL West: MIN, KC, POR*, ANA, SEA, HOU, TEX, LV

NL East: NYM, PHI, MIA, ATL, WAS, CIN, PIT, NASH* /// NL West: MIL, CHC, STL, COL, LAD, ARI, SF, SD

Interleague: 3 games x 16 teams = 48 games Intraleague: 6 games x 8 teams = 48 games Divisional: 9-10 games x 7 teams = 66 games

For the playoffs, each division winner gets a 1 or 2 seed and bye. The 2nd place finishers host wildcard series against the two best remaining wild card teams from either division.

My only issue is that the AL seems very imbalanced in terms of how teams are today, but I’m guessing that would change over time. Some regional rivalries are broken up, but an extra 3 game rivalry series could make up for it (playing 9 divisional games x 7 teams = 63 games, leaving an extra 3).
