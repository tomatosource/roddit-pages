An underappreciated thing about PoE2’s current endgame: the simple waystone mod pool.

What I genuinely dislike about PoE1’s end-game are the large amount of annoying map (waystone) mods that you constantly have to roll over (requiring a regex so you can roll maps in bulk). There are a lot of map mods which brick your build entirely or make the area extremely tedious to run. And let’s not even get started on those horrendous T17-exclusive mods.

By comparison PoE2’s waystone mods mostly make mobs deal more damage or more likely to inflict ailments/stun. I don’t know if this was by design or because they didn’t have enough time to add more mods. But it’s one of those things that goes a long way to improving the overall feel of the game in my opinion. I hate affixes which cripple your character rather than making the enemies stronger. And yes I’m aware Abyss had some of the more PoE1-style annoying mods but those were not part of the core pool and they were offset by a major upside if you rolled one of the good mods (also some of those bad mods could be completely ignored, such as on summoners vs “marked for death”).

In PoE1 it’s like: lol your auras are disabled, you have no regen lmao, your curses do nothing git gud, you instantly died to reflect (what’s the matter, can’t read?), here’s a statue that pops up everywhere and freezes you in place while you stare at the screen and reflect on your life’s choices. God they’re so awful.
