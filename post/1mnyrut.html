Belts and Charms get no love from you guys

Everyone so set on charms sucking and I see you all using Goldens and other unique charms, but the real power is in some belts and some charm prefixes and suffixes.

All ascendencies have their weakness. For me, a Chayula not using CI and using Eternal Youth, stun and bleed are my banes.

Getting charms with .25 charges/second and extended duration mixed with some really cool belts covers my weak areas really well.

For instance, Long belt with a t1 Duration Prefix and T1 duration charms has these suckers lasting 7 seconds. I managed to snag a megalomaniac that has Voracious, Critical Exploit, and Primal Protection. Primal with some belt mods and the act 2⁄4 buffs have my charms refilling very fast, and also they last like 9 seconds a piece now.

Tl;dr

Belts are more than a resist slot and charms are actually really useful right now.
