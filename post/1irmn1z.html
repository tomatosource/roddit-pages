Adam Silver ignoring concerns and blaming European players for flopping shows everything wrong with the current NBA

Last week, Adam Silver did an interview on PMT. It’s mainly a football podcast, so the questions aren’t super informed, but they’re sports fans with a working knowledge of the league. As someone who spent years watching basketball on a daily basis, I’ve found myself watching less and less over time to the point I rarely check in outside of the playoffs these days.

I’m sure it’s been said a million times, but it’s insane how tone-deaf the league is. For the amount of money and time they pour into social media and fan engagement, it really feels like the people in charge either aren’t getting the memo or think they’re smarter than all of us. The NBA has become an embarrassment at this point, reaching for gimmick after gimmick instead of fixing the core gameplay issues that lead to the massive decline in viewership.

The problems with the league are common knowledge at this point. Fans have spent the past decade complaining about the foul-baiting tactics that Harden and CP3 seemed to glorify on a nightly basis. After years of empty promises from the league to look into that, nothing has changed. “Verticality” is just a buzz word at this point and driving to the rim feels like a popularity contest. If you have an All-Star coming at you, it’s going to be a foul 10 times out of 10.

And how does Adam Silver react on a national sports podcast after spending his whole career hearing this? He blames flopping on European players (soccer style) and says that things have greatly improved. That’s it. End of story. Onto the next gimmick.

It’s seriously infuriating. I want to love NBA basketball again. The regular season is a joke. The All-Star Game is a joke. And at every turn, the geniuses in charge try to think “outside of the box” and come up with some BS new tournament to bring viewers instead of, I don’t know, listening to the fans?

The MLB saw viewership declining and added a pitch clock, which greatly improves the viewers experience. Those are the type of changes the NBA should be embracing. Make people want to watch. Don’t have the commissioner come out and completely disregard everyone’s complaints. It’s a bad look and makes me sad about the future of the league.
