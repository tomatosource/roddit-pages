Saw Dwight Powell But Accidentally Called Him Norman Powell

This happened yesterday on Sunday. I saw him walking out of the TMU athletic centre on Carlton here in Toronto, I assume for the national team. The awkward thing was that I didn’t say it to him, but to the friend next to me. I think I said it too loud, because he had a weird look on his face. We were also only like 7m apart so he likely heard me.

I quickly realized my mistake. I was too awkward to apologize, and it felt wrong to ask for a picture.

If Dwight somehow finds this, I’m sorry for getting you mixed up with Norm. Playing in the NBA for so long is an incredible feat, and you deserve to be properly recognized. I think since he was pretty big on the Raptors, you two took up the same “Powell-Canada related” nook in my brain. I’m not going to lie and say I am a super fan, but I always remembered you since you were a fellow Canadian. I also remember picking you up on the waiver wire some weeks to stream some extra rebounds and FG%, so thank you!
