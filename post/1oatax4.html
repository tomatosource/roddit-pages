With the Milwaukee Bucks reportedly waiving Tyler Smith, they have gone eleven consecutive draft classes without signing one of their own picks to a second contract. The last Bucks draft pick to sign a second contract with the team was Giannis Antetokounmpo.

Antetokounmpo was drafted in 2013, since then the Bucks have selected:

2014: Jabari Parker (Left in free agency after four years), Damien Inglis (waived after three years), Johnny O’Bryant III (waived after three years), and Lamar Patterson (rights traded to Atlanta on draft night)

2015: Rashad Vaughn (traded after three years for Tyler Zeller), and Norman Powell (rights traded to Toronto for Greivis Vásquez on draft night)

2016: Thon Maker (traded after three years in a deal that brought back Nikola Mirotic), Patrick McCaw (rights traded to Golden State on draft night), and Malcolm Brogdon (sign-and-traded after three years to Indiana)

2017: D.J. Wilson (traded after four years for P.J. Tucker), and Sindarius Thornwell (rights traded to the Clippers on draft night)

2018: Donte DiVincenzo (traded after four years for Serge Ibaka)

2019: Kevin Porter Jr. (traded on draft night to Detroit, later traded to Cleveland, now back with the Bucks)

2020: R. J. Hampton (rights traded to Denver on draft night) and Jordan Nwora (traded after 2.5 years to Indiana)

2021: Isaiah Todd (rights traded on draft night to Washington)

2022: MarJon Beauchamp (traded after 2.5 years to the Clippers)

2023: Chris Livingston (waived after two years)

2024: AJ Johnson (traded during rookie season to Washington) and Tyler Smith (waived after a year)
