GGG Please add an Invisible/Offline mode for friends

I know this is not the highest-priority feature, but I’d really love to see an option to appear invisible/offline.
Right now, people can always see when you’re online, even if you don’t accept a friend request.

Sometimes after work I just want to focus on my own progress, relax, and play quietly without feeling pressured to reply, help, join, or explain why I’m not responding. An invisible mode would be a small feature, but it would make a huge difference for privacy and personal space.

P.S. I know DND exists, but it can create awkward situations, because people see that you are online and in-game anyway. Then it just turns into having to explain why you are not replying, which kind of defeats the point.
