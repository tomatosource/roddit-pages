Poe 2 needs to be 50% more like Poe 1

The whole combo idea was worth a shot. I was excited about it for sure as someone who was bored with Poe 1 skill system (only one six link skill, only one primary damage skill).

THE PROBLEM IS that rather than opening up and expanding on Poe 1, this system has made the game feel more restrictive and here’s why: rather than having 2 even 3 damage dealing skills, YOU GET ONE COMBO OF ALL OF YOUR SKILLS THAT DOES DAMAGE.

And it’s not just bossing! You need to do combos to clear WHITE AND BLUE MOBS. This isn’t good.

The game legitimately needs to allow you to press one button and kill everything but bosses and rares (or gigantic packs). They need to dial UP the mindlessness, NOT dial it down. Legitimately.

Additional spells and attacks should definitely do more damage for sure! But they should be used SITUATIONALLY not CONSTANTLY. Big windup skills for bosses. Huge screen clearing for giant packs. Maybe even some just for fun? To change the color of explosion on the screen

Am I crazy for thinking this?
