You want to know how long ago that was? Antonio McDyess led the team in points and rebounds with a 21⁄16 double-double. The man was a post-championship rotation player who averaged less than points that season for the Pistons.

The Pistons play at home in a couple more days so hopefully fans won’t have to wait much longer to see their team win in their arena rather than on the road.
