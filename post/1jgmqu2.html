A player on every NBA team who is arguably a better MIP player candidate than Cade Cunningham

https://www.covers.com/nba/most-improved-player-odds

Currently Cade Cunningham is the favorite for the MIP player award with Detroit’s dramatic improvement from bottom of the league to the sixth seed in the East.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/c/cunnica01.html

On an individual level, he’s averaging three more points and two more assists, while improving his efficiency very slightly - it continues a trend of incremental improvement for the first overall pick.

It’s understandable - the way things are going the Pistons probably won’t be represented by another award, and he might end up being a proxy for most improved team. Still, I wanted to illustrate that him winning would be a little silly by making a dumb post, feel free to follow along.

  1. Detroit Pistons

Malik Beasley - Increased his scoring average by five while playing less and not sacrificing efficiency, has been as good as any bench player in the league https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/beaslma01.html

  1. Cleveland Cavaliers

Ty Jerome - He only played two games last year due to injury (where he did nothing), but he’s probably the best candidate for this award in spirit. A 27-year-old who had previously been seen as a third stringer will earn himself a significant raise this offseason https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/j/jeromty01.html

D’Andre Hunter and Mobley are also arguably better candidates.

  1. Boston Celtics

Payton Prichard- made a huge jump this year going from averaging 9 points to 14, and is the favorite for sixth man of the year on one of the top teams in the league https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/p/pritcpa01.html

  1. New York Knicks

Josh Hart- has gone a little under the radar, but he’s averaging five more points this year over last year while improving his true shooting percentage by 10% and having better other numbers across the board https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/hartjo01.html

  1. Indiana Pacers

Alright, I’ll be real. There’s no candidate here, I lied to you

  1. Milwaukee Bucks

AJ Green - Reaching, but he emerged from the wide receiver’s shadow this year, not only solidifying himself as a legitimate rotation player, but arguably the Bucks’ best option at his position and one of the league’s premier outside shooters. Also made tremendous strides defensively https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/g/greenaj01.html

Atlanta Hawks-

Dyson Daniels-

Another guy who definitely has a better argument. Went from averaging 5 points and one steal to 14 and 3, improving all of his numbers across the board.

Orlando Magic-

Anthony Black- pretty much all of their best candidates were taken down by injury, so this is what we get. Quietly doubled his scoring output, becoming a very promising two way wing after a rough start in his rookie year https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/blackan01.html

Chicago Bulls-

Zach Lavine - Not even kidding. Yeah, he was traded so maybe this shouldn’t count, but at 30-years-old he’s having the most efficient season of his career and improved his scoring average by 4 while playing at a borderline all-star level after it seemed as if his value was shot https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/l/lavinza01.html. Giddey is also a solid choice

Miami Heat-

Tyler Herro- Similar jump to Cade this season on an individual level in his first all-star campaign, significant improvement across the board https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/herroty01.html

Toronto Raptors-

Gradey Dick - improved his scoring average by six and seamlessly stepped into a bigger role for the team after early rookie year struggles https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/d/dickgr01.html

76ers-

Quentin Grimes- on a ridiculous run recently, between the Mavs and Sixers he’s improved his scoring average by six and other numbers across the board https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/g/grimequ01.html

Brooklyn Nets-

Cam Johnson- pretty significant jump for Johnson, improving his scoring by six and efficiency significantly. Big name in trade talks https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/j/johnsca02.html

Charlotte Hornets-

Moussa Diabate- Another very significant injury casualty, but Diabate went from being a fringe g league player to earning a long-term contract with the Hornets, showcasing major defensive upside https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/d/diabamo01.html Alright, I don’t want to see him win the MIP

Washington Wizards-

Bilal Coulibaly- significant improvement across the board, looks like there’s a very solid player in there https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/c/coulibi01.html

Improved ppg by 4 and in every other volume category

OKC Thunder-

Aaron Wiggins- No longer a meme, Wiggins is averaging 5 points more than last season, and genuinely looks like a cornerstone for this OKC team going forward of late https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/wiggiaa01.html

Houston Rockets-

Amen Thompson- huge jump for Amen across the board, averaging five more points and looking like a future dominant all-around player

https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/thompam01.html

Denver Nuggets-

Christian Braun- another guy who maybe just should win this award, improved his scoring by eight points and his efficiency leaps and bounds. https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/braunch01.html Westbrook is another shout

Austin Reaves- his jump has been talked about quite a bit, looks every bit of an elite NBA playmaker https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/r/reaveau01.html

Memphis Grizzlies-

Jay Huff- Yeah, Jay Huff. A 26-year-old with about 100 NBA minutes coming into this year, Huff improved his scoring average by six and has been extremely productive in relatively limited minutes off the bench https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/huffja01.html
