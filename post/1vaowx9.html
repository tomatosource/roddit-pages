I really wish old Supporter Packs would come back

As a relatively new Path of Exile 2 player, I’ve already bought pretty much everything that’s currently available because I genuinely want to support GGG.

But one thing I just can’t understand is why all the old Supporter Packs are gone forever.

Some of those packs have incredible armor sets, portals, weapon effects, and cosmetics. GGG clearly spent a huge amount of time and effort designing them, yet they’re only sold for a limited time and then disappear permanently.

Why?

I understand the idea of exclusivity, but would it really hurt to bring them back once a year? Maybe during the Christmas holidays or another special annual event, players could choose one retired Supporter Pack to purchase.

As someone who started later, it’s honestly frustrating to know there are dozens of amazing cosmetics that I will simply never have the opportunity to buy, no matter how much I want to support the game.

Am I the only one who feels this way?
