Runic Ward is great but the downside is too big

Trading so much from your other defensive layers for what is essentially ghetto energy shield just feels horrible.

Runic Ward was honestly what armor and evasion needed to fix their survivability against massive hits, just extra effective health buffer that you can’t just instantly refill with leech, but the armor/evasion defensive cost of runic ward is simply obscene and turns it from a great solution to the problem, to a trap that bricks otherwise good items.

Please reconsider even having the downside to Runic Ward at all.

Edit: The downside I’m talking of only begins at ilvl 55 and up. It converts a percentage of the defensive stats into runic ward. The penalty increases with ilvl and supposedly caps at 65% (That is, a reduction of 65%, leaving you only with 35% of the defensive stat left) at ilvl 80.
