New player thoughts on PoE 1 vs PoE 2

Hey all,

I started with PoE 2 and have around 50 hours in it now, with two characters just getting into low tier maps. Recently I tried PoE 1 as well, about 8 hours in and currently in Act 4, and I just wanted to share how it feels jumping between the two as someone completely new.

The biggest thing I noticed is how different the difficulty feels. In PoE 2 I died a lot. Like… a lot. My Blood Mage had around 100 deaths after the campaign and my Pathfinder around 50. Going into PoE 1, I’ve died twice so far and I’m already clearing whole screens. It kind of surprised me. PoE 1’s campaign feels way easier so far, almost like I’m just rolling through it without really being challenged. I’m also constantly overleveled by like 4–6 levels without even trying, which takes away a bit of the tension.

At the same time, PoE 1 is way faster. There’s so much movement speed that I’m basically always running with my map open just to figure out where I’m going. It can be fun to zoom like that, but it also gets a bit tiring mentally. In PoE 2 everything feels slower and more deliberate, and I actually notice what’s happening in fights instead of just deleting everything instantly.

Controls are a big one for me too. I learned PoE 2 with WASD and it just feels natural. When I switched to PoE 1 it took me a while to get used to mouse movement, and even now I can’t really find keybinds that feel comfortable. In PoE 2 I don’t really think about controls at all, it just clicks.

Combat also feels different in a big way. In PoE 2, at least during the campaign, I’m not just one-shotting everything, so fights feel more meaningful. In PoE 1 I’m already at the point where most things die instantly, which is fun in its own way but also makes it feel a bit mindless at times.

One thing I do prefer in PoE 2 is the gem system. Having it more separated and clear just makes it easier to understand as a new player.

Overall, PoE 2 feels more challenging and immersive to me, while PoE 1 feels faster and more about power and speed. I can see why people enjoy both, but right now I’m definitely leaning more toward PoE 2.

Since I did not experience poe 1 endgame yet, I know I miss alot of stuff, but can’t miss what you dont know I assume.

Curious how it felt for others who started fresh and tried both.
