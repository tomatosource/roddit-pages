PSA: Atziri’s Communion will brick your character if you have CI allocated

As the title said. You can put Atziri’s Communion on but It doesn’t do anything (because you don’t have enough life to be reserved) which is fine except you will unable to use ALL of your spirit skill on your current weapon set. No matter if you already unsocketed the Atziri’s Communion support or not.

My weapon set 1 stay bugged until I uncut a new spirit skill that I put the Atziri’s Communion on (Cast on Minion Death), and then all my spirit skill weirdly become usable again except the one spirit skill I socketed in will stay bugged forever.

In my case it’s fixable but I believe you can lose your hard earned companion this way.
