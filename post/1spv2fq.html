Support Gems in PoE2 are lame

I’ve been having the same discussion with friends multiple times now and we always come to the same conclusion. Support gems as they currently exist in PoE2 are extremely lame.

The main reason is that a lot of the META support gems are simple % damage increases without any downsides that directly compete with support gems that have interesting effects but also have excessively strong downsides. And I think support gems should be about customizing an ability and not giving it extreme amounts of blanket damage increases.

Like why would I ever replace a support gem that gives me 25% extra damage without any downsides for something that changes the ability in a really interesting way, but also reduces my attack speed by 35% and my damage by 20%? The META since the EA launch has always been to stuff as many damage increasing support gems into your sockets as possible, which leaves very little to no room for creativity.

I honestly feel like this stuff just needs to be revisited in a major way at some point. Excessive downsides are just not fun and I really hope most of us can agree on that. Even though these are not the topic of this thread, this also includes things like adding unnecessary charge requirements on certain skills, having hidden MS reduction on armor pieces or making some baseline skills so laughably slow by default that they feel completely unplayable without adding a ridiculous amount of attack speed.

Just to clarify, I don’t think downsides are bad by default, but they need to be deliberately balanced against the upside that is provided. And to me it honestly feels like a lot of downsides are just arbitrarily extreme to adhere to the original vision of the game, completely disregarding overall balance.

I think no matter what they ultimately decide to do regarding the intended speed of the game, which has been a very divisive topic lately, allowing players to express their creativity should always be a goal when designing systems like support gems. And sadly the current balance of gems seems to have the opposite effect, leading to a lot of homogenization of builds due to the same handful of support gems being absolute no-brainers to slot in.
