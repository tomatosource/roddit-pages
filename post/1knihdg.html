D White full potential

Not enough people are talking about this, but I think one of the most interesting storylines next season is going to be Derrick White. With how things are shaping up, this could be his real breakout year, like 25+ points per game is actually on the table.

He’s always played within the system, made the right reads, played defense, hit shots, etc. But if he gets more usage and freedom? Whole different player. The bag is deeper than people think. He’s got the midrange, he can create off the dribble, he can finish, hit spot up threes, hit threes off the dribble. he just hasn’t had to be “that guy” consistently. I wouldn’t be surprised if he ends up being our number 1 option and lands on an all NBA team. Probably the most underrated player in the league.
