Character identity is important

I am not the most experienced player; In only have about 450 hours in poe and 200 in poe 2, but i strongly support the idea that character visual identity is quite a strong feature in an ARPG.

Might be my lack of game time, but ATM i have an hard time recognizing one class from another just by looking at the ingame model.

If you take games like D2, for example, with a bit of experience is really easy to understand if the player is a sorc, an amazon or something else, while i find it impossible in poe 1 and 2.

What i mean is that even the standard idle animation gives away the class in other games, while i find idle, casting animations etc all too similar in poe 2 and i think that this lack of character identity takes away a lot from the game.

I do understand the huge work behind creating and animating different animations for each class, but honestly, being able to recognize a character right away is something i’d love to be able to do.

On a side note, an ascendency icon, in the top left corner of the screen, maybe even with the character level, is another feature i’d love to see. It would make so easy to understand what class and ascend is getting played, when whatching streams etc.
