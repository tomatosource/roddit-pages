In 2024 the Charlotte Hornets selected Tidjane Salaün 6th overall From France, He would play 60 games averaging 5.9⁄4.7⁄1.2 on 45% TS

Coming into the draft Salaun was seen as a raw prospect. Now raw prospects taken high in the draft is nothing new with players like Jaren Jackson Jr having this label in the 2018 draft as a defensive player with a limited but high potential offensive game. Contrary to JJJ, Salaun looks to be in the the category of “this brother cannot play” after NBA action instead of limited with potential to grow group. The pick is somewhat justifiable as the 2024 draft was rated low compared to other years and severely lacking potential franchise changing prospects so a shot in the dark is more warranted.

Looking back at the 2024 draft though they would have easily been able to find better players to surround their star Lamelo Ball legitimately right around their pick with Clingan, Dillingham, Edey, and Buzelis. It seems going to a pretty poor developmental franchise at the moment in the Hornets and with his lack of skills that he already has that Salaun’s almost immediate future in the league will be in question already going his 2nd season in the NBA if he doesn’t drastically improve this offseason somehow.
