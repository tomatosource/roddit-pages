What were your favorite things about 0.3?

With 0.3 coming to an end let’s reflect on how much 0.3 actually brought us.

It introduced a lot of highly request changes to Path of Exile 2. It’s honestly kind of hard to fathom how many things they’ve actually added to the game in 0.3, so I’ve made a small list below of just some of the things they’ve added in 0.3 to jog our memory:

  • Async Trade

  • Ingame trade window

  • Act 4

  • 3 Interludes

  • Removal of Towers

  • Crafting improvements

    • New Omens
    • Essence Rework 
       * Corrupted essences
    • Abyss crafting
    • Sanctified items

  • Waystones rework: Quantity, Rarity and pack size now tied to modifier difficulty

  • Large Passive Tree expansion

  • Abyss League Mechanic

  • Sprint

  • Exceptional Items and Uniques

  • Greater and perfect Currencies (Perfect Exalt Orbs, Perfect Chaos Orbs… )

  • Splinters for Pinnacle Boss content (King in the Mist, Olroth… )

  • New defensive layers: Armor to elemental damage and Deflection

  • New 3 tier support gem system

  • Lineage support gems

  • Skill changes

  • Underpowered Ascendancy reworks (unfortunately they’ve been mostly unsuccessful)

  • Uber Arbiter

  • New Unique Atlas maps

  • Instant Weapon Swap

What were your favorite things about 0.3?
