If Rick Carlisle wins the NBA Finals with the Pacers, he’ll join an exclusive club of head coaches that have won titles with multiple teams.

That club currently consists of 3 coaches:

  1. Phil Jackson - 6 championships with the Bulls, 5 with the Lakers

  2. Pat Riley - 4 championships with the Lakers, 1 with the Heat

  3. Alex Hannum - 1 championship with the Hawks, 1 with the 76ers

Also shoutout to Alex Hannum - the only coach to have ever beat Bill Russell’s Celtics in a playoff series.
