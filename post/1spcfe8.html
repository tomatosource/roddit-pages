Path of exile 2 for VERY casual player, path of exile 1 for players who realy love ARGP. change my mind

The more I play both games, the more this thought keeps bugging me.

PoE 2 feels way more “friendly” and easy to get into. Less overwhelming, cleaner, you can hop in after work and just play without feeling completely lost.

But at the same time, it kinda feels like a different game that just happens to be called Path of Exile. Like the identity of the original isn’t really there anymore.

And I’m not sure I like the direction. It feels like they’re clearly aiming at a more casual audience that’s willing to spend money. I get it from a business standpoint, but as a player… not sure.

I actually started with PoE 2, and it pushed me to try PoE 1. And when I did, it just clicked how different they are - and honestly, how much deeper PoE 1 feels.

Even something like speed. In PoE 1 you eventually zoom like crazy, and I love that. But I see a lot of people getting turned off by it, which is weird because you don’t start that fast - you grow into it.

And yeah, I’m part of that same “paying audience” they’re targeting.

To me, PoE 1 really only has one big issue - the entry barrier. It’s rough. But PoE 2 could’ve been a perfect bridge into it, since a lot of mechanics are basically simplified versions of PoE 1 systems.

So right now my take is:

PoE 2 feels like a casual version / entry point

PoE 1 is for people who actually want depth

Curious what others think — am I off here?
