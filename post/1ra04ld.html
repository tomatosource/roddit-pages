Russell Westbrook’s wife shared an email showing a death threat she received on Instagram

Nina Westbrook posted an email she received after her husband (Russell Westbrook) scored 5 points last night against the Orlando Magic.

The email was titled “F*** You” and read: “Your piece of f***in sh*t husband sucks to f***n bad can’t even get 1o points is pathetic. I hope you both die in a car crash dumb b*tch.”

Nina blamed sports betting for the death threat, writing “The negative effects of sports betting. Brings out the worst in ppl smh.”
