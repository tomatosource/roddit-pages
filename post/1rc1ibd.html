The Vault of Kasama, Moment of Zen, and Reliquary Key are all pointless wastes of time

I don’t get the thought process behind these.

Vaults are about as rare as Immured Fury maps, one is a full map will experience, mods, and can carry extra content, as well as dropping a nice item, while the other is a good opportunity to waste 5 minutes to get almost nothing.

Moment of Zen should be way less common, and way more likely to actually have something decent. Over 100, and not one had anything better than a Grand Project.

But what takes the cake is the Reliquary Vault…what was the idea here??

Well, a unique could drop in a map with a rare chance to be coloured, OR, there could be an item that you need to take to a specific area on the Atlas, and walk through an empty couple of rooms, WITH A DOOR IN BETWEEN, and wait for…absolute junk, but it’s green?

Oh, what if you want to do a few at once, to save time?

Uhh, no, each and every one is back to Hideout, click the map, navigate home, select the vault, place the reliquary, portal, walk, open door, walk, click…wait…repeat.

These things should be super rare, and an absolute OMFG moment, right now, I simply skip them altogether. I have enough chance shards…thanks.
