I think Kevin Durant not wanting to return to the Warriors has a lot more to do with how they handled his injury in the 2019 playoffs than people are willing to admit.

I am just speculating, so I could be talking out of my ass. But, I do have some supporting arguments.

It’s been so long that I think people have forgotten just how contentious Durant returning to the finals actually was at the time. I am not talking about the injury itself, but how his return was speculated, rumored upon by his own team, the org and the warriors media.

Publicly, Durant says no one in the Warriors told him to return early from injury. (and I believe him)

“How can you blame [them]? Hell, no,” he said to a suggestion from Yahoo Sports that Golden State mishandled the calf injury that sidelined him for several weeks before Game 5 against the Toronto Raptors. “I heard the Warriors pressured me into getting back. Nobody never said a word to me during rehab as I was coming back.

However, many people inside the Warriors organization along with the Golden State media at the time tried to make it seem like he wasn’t trying hard enough or wasn’t tough enough or just didn’t want to return in the finals.

That was publicly talked about by Warriors media at the time. Those types of talk don’t get out unless someone wanted that message out.

As we learned from the Luka trade, If there are certain things that shouldn’t leak, than it’s very easy to not leak. So the feeling inside the Warriors camp was definitely leaked on purpose to send a message.

A few excerpts from a few different publications at the time of injury once it was clear that Golden State couldn’t win without Durant

Tim Kawakami - June 7th

There are hints that something changed somewhere in the last few days, maybe involving the recovery or the doctors or Durant’s decision-making process. … The larger question: Has Durant fought to get back into action the way Thompson and Looney did for Game 4? It’s unclear. …

It’s probably a bit cynical to even attempt to analyze. But just ask yourself this: If Thompson or Looney or Curry or Green or Andre Iguodala had this injury 30 days ago, would the Warriors still be waiting for them?

Sam Amick - June 9th

At the very least, Durant’s absence that began back on May 8 is causing a mixture of confusion and angst among several of his teammates that simply can’t be helpful to their overall cause. Sources say there was a very real hope that Durant would be able to play in Game 4, to push through in much the same way that Thompson, Cousins, Iguodala and Looney have done of late. When that didn’t happen, and when they saw their season compromised more than ever without him after they’d grown hopeful of his return after seeing him on the court, the irritation grew in large part because they simply didn’t understand why he wasn’t there.

From The Washington Post After the fact

Following a Golden State loss in Game 1, Yahoo Sports cited league sources in reporting that Durant was “expected to return” from his calf strain “at some point midway” through the Finals. Members of the Warriors’ organization were described as having “some optimism” that he could play in Game 3 but sensing “a stronger possibility that Game 4 is the most logical option.”

“The franchise, sources said, does not foresee a scenario in which Durant would be completely unavailable during the NBA Finals,” Yahoo Sports reported.

So while Durant was hoping for a return in Game 5, as he claimed Wednesday, some other members of Golden State apparently thought he might be able to come back sooner, and his failure to do so led to questions about his toughness

And when the guy did end up returning game 5, he immediately tore his Achilles.

Of course the org is going to deny that they ever forced him back. And Durant, even though the most contentious split of the time, has been a pro and been pretty quiet on the full reasoning for his departure.

But if you were there during those finals, you remember how bad the KD injury discourse was before he returened. And I think that really turned him off to coming back to the org. Because he felt betrayed by the org doubting his injury and his willingness to return.

TLDR: While recovering from injury, the speculating from the organization, and his team questioning his injury, his toughness and his willningness to return probably caused him to distrust the entire org at a fundamental level.
