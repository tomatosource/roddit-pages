First time hitting act 3, I really over complicated this game for so long.

I’ve had the game since it launched in EA. Usually not a big ARPG fan but just really like the aesthetic and animations in POE2. Im not good at keeping track of what stat is boosted and by how much on each item equipped, im bad at building incremental stat increases with characters and if you give me abunch of skill gems and support stuff I feel compelled to use it and thought it was just natural progression of the game.

So basically most of the time ive had this game ive been equipped with just the highest item level that had stats I thought wrere good and a busted skill bar were im trying to juggle 8 skills every encounter. I would watch “new player guides” but most them just overload you on concepts and mechanics, telling you to complete campaign and then what to do for end game. Most build guides would just throw a slill tree at you then focus on getting uniques and crafting stuff. All kinda presuming you may be new to POE2 but I guess with a general understanding if ARPG loops, even if they spend 8 min intro constantly saying “no knowledge needed”.

Anyway, after getting into a loop grind game Arc Raiders and understanding the progression there and with .5 coming out I gave POE2 another shot and just kinda get it now. You just kinda focus on one thing you wanna do and build around it. Im a frost nova sorceress because exploding things with Ice is really cool. Saw frost bomb gave the things to make frost nova stronger. Just focused on that till it got weak in act 2, then I looked at guides and builds to tweek and find better synergies. Like just dropping most of my other fire and elctro skills for more frost stuff. Now getting new items I know what is better because I know what stats are important for what skills I use.

Im sure im gonna be completely lost again here soon and I don’t even think about trading or how to best craft what on what but im just glad to finally be close to seeing my hideout lol. Game really isn’t as complex as it seems just to get going. All the things are kinda just to help give variety im seeing now.

It’s all been said before, just another slow stubborn player who finally figured it out for myself. The boss fights are alot more fun when you know you can win, it’s just your own timing and positioning that’s losing.
