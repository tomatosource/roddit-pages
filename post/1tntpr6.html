##HOUSTON ASTROS NO-HITTER FUN FACTS

  • The Astros are the only baseball team to lose a Complete Game No-Hitter (1964, Johnson)

  • Don Wilson’s 1969 No-Hitter against the Reds was thrown the day after the Reds’ Jim Maloney threw a No-Hitter against the Astros. Both No-Hitters were Wilson & Maloney’s 2nd.

  • Nolan Ryan’s 1981 No-Hitter was the only one of his seven he threw for the Astros, but he broke Koufax’s record with it.

  • Mike Scott clinched the NL West and the Cy Young with his 1986 No-Hitter

  • The 2003 No-Hitter was the final No-Hitter throw at Old Yankee Stadium & the 2022 No-Hitter was the first No-Hitter thrown at New Yankee Stadium

  • Mike Fiers 2015 No-Hitter was the first thrown at Minute Maid Park/Daikin Park by an Astro

  • 2019 and 2022 are the years there have been multiple Astros No-Hitters.

  • The 2022 WS No-Hitter is the latest No-Hitter thrown in a calendar year in MLB History and the 2024 No-Hitter was the earliest thrown in the calendar year in MLB History.

  • Framber Valdez is the only Astros pitcher to face the minimum in a No-Hitter, and the only Lefty to throw one.

  • The 2024 Blanco No-Hitter was Joe Espada’s first managerial win, making him the first MLB manager to get his first win that way.
