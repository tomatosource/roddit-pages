Considering the “parent” threads recently, I just wanted to share my thanks

TLDR: I’m out of a job, have two small kids, and have really enjoyed this season. It’s been something I look forward to each night.

Short story: I was laid off a couple months ago and have been actively applying to jobs (over 180 so far). My second child was born shortly after being laid off, and my other child is just past 2 years old. Though my family is doing okay, it’s been hard on me being out of work and wanting to provide for my family.

PoE2 is my first ever aarpg, I bought it soon after launch because I thought the skill tree looked amazingly fun. I hopped back in this season for the Druid, and because I was feeling down about the job search. I’ve had a great time, the economy is much easier for me since I only get to play a couple hours a day when the kids are asleep.

I just wanted to say thank you to the community and GGG for making it a fun season. I currently have a L89 bear that smashes things. I die a lot on t15 and t16 maps, but I’m trying to make my build better a little at a time.

So to the person who bought that thing from me for a hundred exalts, thank you. So the guys who helped me get the 4th ascendency, thank you. To the people in chat who helped answer numerous questions I had, thank you. To GGG, thank you! You helped make a tired, jobless dad a little happier over the past couple weeks. I’m excited to hop on again tonight!
