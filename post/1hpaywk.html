[Russo] “The Wall” at the Clippers’ Intuit Dome has been paying dividends: Opponents are shooting a shocking 72.3% on FTs (30th in the league) facing The Wall and 33.3% on 3s (27th in the league).

Source: https://x.com/flybyknite/status/1873512930341531699?s=46

The Clippers’ new home, Intuit Dome, features The Wall—a section of die-hard fans near the visitor’s bench—and it’s already proving to be a nightmare for opposing teams.

  • Opponents are shooting 72.3% on free throws against The Wall, which would rank 30th in the NBA if it were a team.
  • Opponents are hitting just 33.3% from three, equivalent to 27th in the league.
  • In 17 home games, only 6 visiting teams have dared to shoot against The Wall in the second half.
  • On 11⁄22 vs. the Kings, Sacramento shot 24.4% from the field and scored just 29 points in the second half—the lowest scoring half by any NBA team this season.
  • Overall, teams are shooting 73.0% on free throws at Intuit Dome, the second-lowest FT% for road teams in any arena this year.

Early signs suggest The Wall might be a real factor for the Clippers’ home-court dominance. Is this the best new fan setup in the NBA?
