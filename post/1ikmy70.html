Sean Grande’s call is so hard to listen to I got cable again.

To save a buck in this ruthless world, I thought I would try to cancel my cable/streaming subscription for a bit. I don’t watch a lot of TV - mainly just Celtics games and occasionally other live sports.

Since I often have games on in the background while doing projects, I thought, maybe I can just listen to games on the radio. I’d still be able to catch the games, and I wouldn’t have a monthly cable bill. Win win.

LOSE.

I just resubscribed to cable. I couldn’t do it. I listened to maybe three games on the radio and Sean Grande was just too hard to stomach.

The overly verbose monologues; pained, forced stat narratives; and complete lack of chemistry with Cedric Maxwell were just too much. Not to mention the overbearing pessimism and anxious tone he piled on to games during losses. It was unbearable. I literally couldn’t tolerate it. He somehow made something I have always loved - following Celtics games - into something I realized I would dread doing.

So, congratulations to the cable company, congratulations to Grande (the best salesman the could have) and congratulations to me for spending the bag just so I don’t have to hear that terrible product again.
