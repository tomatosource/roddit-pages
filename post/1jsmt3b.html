Post Monster HP nerf: there’s a lingering issue with the ‘minion’ tagged monsters accompanying rares

The monster packs that currently accompany rares (a white or blue pack with the “minion” tag applied) are incredibly tanky right now, sometimes being almost as tanky individually as their rare master/parent monster.

The way these minions scale was increased in the patch notes, but right now it’s not sitting right: and, I suspect, is causing a lot of the continued complaints, post HP nerf hotfix.

Rare Monsters’ minions have been made stronger defensively and now scale far more appropriately in party-play.

The “Powerful Minions” modifier has been reduced in power to compensate for the above.

It’s almost always the cause of the sensation of “why on earth is this white mob such an HP sponge?!”.

This needs to be dialed back a fair amount: the patch note implies that it was done to help group scaling, but it really feels like group scaling is in effect even while trying to solo them, and there’s really no reason for a white monster to be that tanky.
