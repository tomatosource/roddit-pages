Marks: How about we replace the word tanking with: Cap flexibility, short-term contracts, player development (evaluation of young players) and minute restrictions for veteran players in the last 25 games. The T word is such a negative word.

Marks: You think that would fit on a t-shirt? Could maybe trademark it.

2019 he tweets at Tankathon:

Marks: Can we please get rid of the word “Tanking” in the NBA vocabulary. That word means that players/coaching staff are trying to lose on purpose. Now if you want to say management has put together an inferior roster with the focus of player development, that is fine.

Marks: Or in the case of Golden State, injuries has decimated a roster that it now shifts gears to Player Development 101, that is fine. But the word tanking is such a demeaning word. Nobody chooses to lose. Sorry @tankathon

Tankathon: Organizations do choose to lose though, with the goal of winning later. Obviously Golden State wanted to win this year, but they’ve been dealt a horrible hand (ooh that is a pun) and “player development” and extended rests for injured stars for the future is a form of tanking.
