Protip: If you want to see your tooltip dps for a conditional skill (power charges, buffs, recent kill) open the passive tree mid-combat and then the skills menu

Basically the title. Opening the passive tree (hotkey is P) pauses the game, even on the keyboard and mouse interface (my controller homies already know they can pause the game with any menu). Once paused, you can then pull up your skills (hotkey is G) or inventory on top of passive tree menu at your leisure and compare things. This also obviously works for when you want to compare tooltip impact for passive tree nodes, but that’s a little less generally helpful.
