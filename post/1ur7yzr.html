PSA: Always double check relevant corrupted / sanctified / crafted status when buying items!

Mostly a vent post. Been farming like crazy to afford a new amulet. Finally got enough divines earlier this morning, found my amulet, bought it…

It’s sanctified. It’s anointed with Augmented Flesh, something that I certainly don’t need, and even worse, as far as I’m aware, no meta builds run. I obviously was just assuming I’d put my new anoint on myself…

I have it re-listed now but I think at best I’m probably going to get 50% back, maybe less. I basically just deleted 200d.

It’s on me for not paying attention, but I’m pretty bummed out about it. I wish the “Sanctified” label was as obvious as “Corrupted” or there was some kind of currency that would let you re-anoint a sanctified / corrupted item.

tl;dr always check the status of corrupted / sanctified / crafted as relevant when buying expensive items
