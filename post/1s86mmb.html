[Popper] Karl-Anthony Towns on only getting up 9 shots against OKC despite being guarded by smaller players like Alex Caruso: “I would like to be in the post, yes… I would like to utilize my talent and my size. But we decided to do something different… Just executing our offensive game plan.”

Karl-Anthony Towns had some choice words about the gameplan the Knicks put into play against OKC last night that resulted in him only getting up 9 shots while guarded by smaller players like Jalen Williams and Alex Caruso:

Source: Steve Popper - Newsday

Asked about having a smaller defender on him in the Knicks loss to the Thunder, Towns said: “I would like to be in the post, yes.”

Asked why that wasn’t happening enough, he said, “I don’t know. I don’t know. I’ll watch the film of tonight I’ll have a better idea after.”

After drawing fouls on Alex Caruso in the second quarter and then taking over briefly offensively in the fourth, he was asked his mentality in that situation. “Of course, I would like to do it again. I would like to utilize my talent and my size. But we decided to do something different.”

What’s forbidding you from getting those post possessions? “Just executing our offensive game plan, what our game plan and what we drew up and what we want to accomplish. I don’t know. I’m just trying to do whatever I can to impact winning honestly.”

But they’re doubling you?

“That’s fine.”

This is not the first time this season that KAT has publicly shown reservations for his usage in Mike Brown’s system, especially in moments where Brunson is not on the floor. KAT still finished the night with 15 points and 18 rebounds after scoring a team leading 9 in the 4th quarter.
