Every 4 years, Seattle Sounders season ticket holders vote in a “GM Vote of Confidence”. If a majority vote “no confidence,” the GM is removed. NBA teams, especially those with institutional ownership (vs individual ownership) and/or ownership with a “fan-first” philosophy, should do the same.

The Seattle Sounders introduced the GM Vote of Confidence when the club was founded in 2009.

Since then, they’ve:

  • been to the Conference Finals 7 times (2012, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2024)
  • been to the Finals 4 times (2016, 2017, 2019, 2020)
  • won the MLS Cup twice (2016, 2019)
  • missed the Playoffs once (2022)

The club’s first-ever General Manager Vote in 2012 saw 41.6 percent of all eligible voters cast a ballot, retaining then-General Manager Adrian Hanauer with a 96.3 percent vote of retention.

In 2018, with the franchise’s second vote and updated bylaws, Lagerwey was retained with 37.2 percent of eligible voters casting ballots and a 87.3 percent vote of retention.

In 2022, of the 31.4 percent of Alliance Members that participated in the third vote, 90 percent cast a vote of retention for General Manager & President of Soccer Garth Lagerwey.

For a vote of no confidence to move forward, it must be chosen by the super-majority of no less than 67% of all votes cast.

Honestly, I can see an especially young/progressive owner adopting this GM Vote of Confidence approach. Maybe even someone like Steve Ballmer. Anyone who wants the fans to feel like he’s doing everything in his power to run the team for the fans and not for himself. I wish The Raptors allowed their fans to do this. I don’t think any of us understood what Masai was doing, but I honestly think a lot of us still trusted him (a trust that was earned after the Bargnani and Kawhi trades, and after drafting Scottie over Suggs).
