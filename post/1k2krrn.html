Most uniques could become a lot more interesting and viable had they three individual versions:

  1. Version (build-enabling, early campaign): Lower values of the mods, no downside mods like reduced attack speed, low lvl requirements. The unique in its purest form that’s just focused on the defining mods that only this kind of unique offers.

  2. Version (late campaign): Stronger values of the mods, small downside, medium lvl and attribute requirements. It’s an upgrade of the first version if you’re okay with the downside. If not, keep using the first version or swap to a rare item.

  3. Version (endgame, potential chase item): The strongest version of the unique, but also with the strongest downside. Requires your character to be build around it to utilize it best.

With this, weak uniques with interesting mods only they have could also become relevant in later stages of your character’s progression. And unique designs don’t become immediately useless upon release just because they have a major downside, since you now have the option to use a weaker version that, in turn, doesn’t come with said downside. Or only a smaller one.
