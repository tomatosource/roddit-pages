My humble opinion on trading

I always used to defend the trading system thru the homepage when friends coming from other games complained about the missing auction house and how tedious trading is. My take coming from Poe 1 was that the system there works quite well. In the meantime I have to admit, just cause it works for Poe 1 it does not necessarily work for Poe 2. but why is that? I feel the system is reliant on a somewhat active and big player base. Not just in terms of buying but also selling. 3-4 hours playtime in Poe 1 allowed me to buy and sell quite a lot of stuff. In Poe 2 I do hardly any trades at all. I thought my prices might just be too high but even selling them way underpriced did not work at all. I feel the number on players is just lower and in conjunction with the limited play time per day which is not that low either it just does not work out. I don’t need an auction house, but if players could just enter my hideout thru a link on the trade site, open my stash with all the items I am selling and simply pay and take the item would be a game changer for me. I understand this is a tricky topic and hard to satisfy everyone, but right now I essentially feel like playing ssf although in trade league. Any thoughts on that or advice what I could do myself?
