Interesting (at least to me) Last Lament weapon swap interaction

If you lock your Last Lament into both weapon sets, they both charge Compose Requiem % separately. This means that you can get 24 (12x2) seconds of Requiem if you swap weapon sets between casts. This does require you to have crossbow shot (weapon set 1) and crossbow shot (weapon set 2) on your bar and you do have to “load” Requiem once for each set. But if you were using plasma shot in a second set to apply shock already, it just ends up being easily utilized free extended single target damage. Even though Requiem is such high dps that you rarely need 24 seconds of it to kill something, it’s quite helpful if there are multiple bosses or multiple phases to a boss with a delay.
