Knicks Have a Chance to be the First “Double Crown” Winners (and Other Slang) – Sports Chat With the Boys

A topic came up recently during some local sports chat with the boys: Why isn’t the NBA pushing the Knicks and their NBA Cup win from earlier this season harder in marketing? What title(s) should we give it to denote its importance?

This season’s NBA Cup was a Finals preview (Knicks/Spurs in both), and last season’s NBA Cup final featured the eventual champions, OKC, but the marketing doesn’t denote those benchmarks.

We came up with: The Double Crown

This denotes an NBA team that wins the NBA Cup + NBA Finals in the same season.

Also, the NBA Cup introduced a new individual trophy – NBA Cup MVP.

This led to some other accolades/instances that should be named:

We came up with: The Grand Slam

This denotes a player who has won:

  1. Regular Season MVP
  2. NBA Cup MVP
  3. Eastern (or Western) Conference Finals MVP
  4. Finals MVP

For example:

  • SGA has 1, 3, and 4, and only needs 2 to complete the Grand Slam
  • Brunson has 2 and 3, and needs 1 and 4 to complete the Grand Slam
  • Jokic has 1, 3, and 4, and only needs 2 to complete the Grand Slam
  • LeBron has 1, 2, and 4, and only needs 3 to complete the Grand Slam

Lastly, this made me think of a retroactive accolade that should be named:

We came up with: The Trifecta

This denotes a player who has won:

  1. Regular Season MVP
  2. Defensive Player of the Year
  3. Finals MVP

For example:

  • Jordan, Hakeem, and Giannis are the only players to achieve the Trifecta.
  • Kawhi only needs 1 (Regular Season MVP) to complete the Trifecta.
