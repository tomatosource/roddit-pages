Jayson Tatum suffers bone bruise, status in the air for Game 2

As reported by Shams on ESPN and confirmed in other sources, Jayson Tatum has been diagnosed with a bone bruise on his shooting hand’s wrist following his mid-air collision with the Magic’s Kentavious Caldwell-Pope in the fourth quarter of the game last night.

Celtics coach Joe Mazzulla said after Tuesday’s practice that Tatum is day-to-day

On a related note, the team’s other star Jaylen Brown, has also been playing with a bone bruise, this time on his knee, for the past few weeks.
