[Vardon] NBA won’t punish Bam Adebayo for punching Tyler Herro in Las Vegas

Article

The NBA will not punish Miami Heat star Bam Adebayo for punching former teammate Tyler Herro at a Las Vegas hotel and gym last week, a league spokesperson told The Athletic.

“After discussing with the players involved and the NBPA, everyone would prefer to move on from this unfortunate circumstance, and no further action will be taken by the league,” the spokesperson said.
