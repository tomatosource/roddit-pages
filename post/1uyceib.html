What if every NBA team moved to the least populated town in their respective state? I did the research and renamed every team accordingly.

For states with more than one team, I took the second smallest town, or the third, or fourth depending on how many teams the state had.

Eastern Conference

Atlanta Hawks become Tate City Rubies

(Tate City, Georgia. Population: 16)

Tate City is a small town in Georgia that used to be a ruby mining town, now it’s a lumber town. The place wasn’t electrified until the 1970’s.

Boston Celtics become Gosnold Ferries

(Gosnold, Massachusetts. Population: 75)

Gosnold is a group of islands off of Cape Cod. The only way to get to the islands is by ferry that only runs a few times a week. There are more golf carts then people.

Brooklyn Nets become Oil Spring Gushers

(Oil Spring Reservation, New York. Population: 1)

Oil Spring is a small town located on a Native American reservation. The town is located on a spring that produces oil. The residents had to fight for their land.

Charlotte Hornets become Dellview Mongrels

(Dellview, North Carolina. Population:13)

Dellview is a small town that was founded so a family could legalize shooting dogs that were bugging their chickens.

Chicago Bulls become Moonshine Griddles

(Moonshine, Illinois. Population:1)

Moonshine is a small town with a population of one. They have a store and a house. The owner of the store makes burgers for the traveler that passes through.

Cleveland Cavaliers become Rendville Pioneers

(Rendville, Ohio. Population: 36)

Rendville is a small coal mining village. It was owned by a man that employed white miners to work together. It was known for it’s unusual government system. It had a mayor and a black postmaster.

Detroit Pistons become Pointe Aux Barques Prows

(Pointe Aux Barques, Michigan. Population:10)

Pointe Aux Barques is a small town on a rocky shore. It was named by a French priest that thought it looked like the front of a ship. Millionaires used to vacation there.

Indiana Pacers become New Amsterdam Floods

(New Amsterdam, Indiana. Population:27)

New Amsterdam is a small town that was flooded years ago. Most buildings and people were gone. The ones that stayed were strong. They celebrated their 200th anniversary.

Miami Heat become Weeki Wachee Manatees

(Weeki Wachee, Florida. Population:5)

Weeki Wachee is a small town with an attraction that has mermaids. The theater there has been around since 1947. It’s populated with manatees instead of people.

Milwaukee Bucks become Odanah Watertowers

(Odanah, Wisconsin. Population:13)

Odanah is a small town that houses the tribal government. They had to relocate some years ago and many died. They got some land back and have relocated to Odanah where they stay.

New York Knicks become Saltaire Offseasons

(Saltaire, New York. Population:2)

Saltaire is a small town on an island connected by ferries. It’s largely deserted in the winter when the summer people go away.

Orlando Magic become Marineland Aquanauts

(Marineland, Florida. Population:15)

Marineland is a small city that used to be a marine park and underwater film studio. It now serves as a research and conservation area.

Philadelphia 76ers become Centralia Embers

(Centralia, Pennsylvania. Population:5)

Centralia is a small town with a big problem. There’s an underground fire that has been going since at least May 27, 1962. Some residents still live there even though the government told them to leave.

Toronto Raptors become Cockburn Island Castaways

(Cockburn Island, Ontario. Population:16)

Cockburn Island is a small town on an island in Lake Huron. It’s a remote area with only a few people living there.

Washington Wizards become Washington Wizards

(Washington DC is not a state.)

Western Conference

Dallas Mavericks become Guerra Ranchers

(Guerra, Texas. Population:3)

Guerra is a small town in Texas. It was founded a long time ago and has a post office.

Denver Nuggets become Bonanza Hotshots

(Bonanza, Colorado. Population:1)

Bonanza is a ghost town that used to be a mining town.

Golden State Warriors become Amador City Prospectors

(Amador City, California. Population:200)

Amador City is a small city in California. It has a rich Gold Rush history.

Houston Rockets become Tulsita Wildcatters

(Tulsita, Texas. Population:14)

Tulsita is a small town that was named after a nearby town in Oklahoma. It was bought by an oil company and has been quiet since then.

LA Clippers become Industry Warehouses

(City of Industry, California. Population:264)

City of Industry is a small city that is almost completely warehouses and factories. It doesn’t have many residents.

LA Lakers become Vernon Meatpackers

(Vernon, California. Population:222)

Vernon is a small city that used to be a place for manufacturing and warehousing. It has many meat packing plants. It’s been a relatively quiet place as well.

Memphis Grizzlies become Saulsbury Sandmen

(Saulsbury, Tennessee. Population:112)

Saulsbury, Tennessee is a small city that used to be a sand mining town.

Minnesota Timberwolves become Funkley Bartenders

(Funkley, Minnesota. Population:10)

Funkley is a small town with a bar. The owner of the bar makes profit and gives it to the funke visitors as tips.

New Orleans Pelicans become Mound Cottons

(Mound, Louisiana. Population:18)

Mound is a small town named after an Indian burial mound that was nearby. It’s a fairly quite town with plenty of history.

Oklahoma City Thunder become Lotsee Scouts

(Lotsee, Oklahoma. Population:2)

Lotsee is a small town that was founded when a rancher bought land to keep it away from being annexed.

Phoenix Suns become Jerome Copperheads

(Jerome, Arizona. Population:455)

Jerome is America’s largest ghost town. I’m sure by now you could guess that it used to be a mining town.

Portland Trail Blazers become Greenhorn Highlanders

(Greenhorn, Oregon. Population:2)

Greenhorn is a small mountain town. It doesn’t have any sewer systems or power.

Sacramento Kings become Bradbury Estates

(Bradbury, California. Population:921)

Bradbury is a small mountain town nestled in the mountains. It’s a very expensive private area with no commercial development.

San Antonio Spurs become Quail Coveys

(Quail, Texas. Population:19)

Quail is a small town that is famous for, you guessed it, Quail.

Utah Jazz become Bonanza Inkwells

(Bonanza, Utah. Population:1)

Bonanza is a small town owned by a mining company. They mine salt that is used in road sealing and ink production.

It is ironic that both Denver and Utah had a city named Bonanza. This could cause an interesting rivalry between the Nuggets and Jazz, although it might be confusing
