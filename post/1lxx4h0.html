Please please do a Passover on Uniques for 0.3

There are oh…so many Uniques that simply don’t have any place, in any build.

Yes, many are fantastic for levelling, some have uses in niche builds, some are Godly.

But most…ehh…a chance shard in disguise.

There are far too many to go into specifics, you all know the type, a unique that wants to take over an item spot spot, that has 4 modifiers, one of which is: “You thought this unique might be OK, so let me add a massive downside to it”

The negative modifiers are often:

  1. Ridiculously bad
  2. On a unique that is only mildly powerful

Or ones that are just…baffling, sure I get a moderate amount of mana, and Oh! Mana flask charges…and…that’s it… FOR AN AMULET!
