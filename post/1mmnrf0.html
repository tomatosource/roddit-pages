Interesting fact: Former Spurs Player and 2x NBA Champion Matt Bonner Had a 3.96 GPA in Business while Playing for Florida

Not many NBA players finish their degrees, let alone do it with almost perfect grades. Matt Bonner might be best remembered as the “Red Mamba” and as a two-time NBA champ with the Spurs, but his college career at Florida was just as impressive off the court as on it. Bonner graduated from the University of Florida in 2003 with a bachelor’s in business administration and high honors (3.96 GPA), all while being one of the best players in the SEC. He was also Academic All-American of the Year for basketball in both 2002 and 2003.

Does anyone else have examples of NBA players who were just as strong academically? Where would you rank Bonner in terms of the smartest players to ever make it to the league?
