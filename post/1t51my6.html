JB = Zach Lavine?

This is my hot take of the day, playing devil’s advocate.
I’m interested in how other people think about it.

Is JB just a similar type of player to Zach Lavine? In that he’s a high volume scorer that doesn’t contribute to winning due to deficiencies in their game(in Zach Lavine’s case horrible defense, for JB bad court vision and ball-hogging isolations shown in bad on/off numbers)
What if the only reason our perception of the two is different is because JB played on a well managed Celtics team his whole career, that won despite having JB on the team thanks to IT, JT, Horford, great bench players, etc. Replace Lavine on the Bulls with JB, do you see the team getting better?

Throughout NBA history, there are always wings that can score but never lead their team to playoff wins, due to shaky playmaking and defense. Demar DeRozan, Brandon Ingram, Rudy Gay, Twolves Wiggins etc. come to mind, with the Ewing Effect taking place after they leave. Isn’t JB in the same mold as these guys with mid efficiency volume scoring, shaky playmaking and defense? (1 on 1 defense is good, but team defense not so much; I see him miss rotations and cutters all the time, and his on/off numbers are always putrid)

Edit: I mean, Zach Lavine’s bulls in 21-22 were on pace to be the #1 seed before injuries decimated the team, Lonzo-Caruso-Lavine-Derozan-Vuc was the lineup then with a 27-13 record in 40 games (55+ win pace). So even Lavine can put up good numbers on a winning team with the right players around him, but we just don’t see him as THE GUY to win a championship around. So many people seem opposed to trading JB for Giannis, but if JB is a similar type of player to Zach Lavine, wouldn’t you do it in a heartbeat? My point is you don’t normally win championships with flawed wings like JB/Lavine/Gay/Ingram as your 1A or B unless you have a completely busted/stacked 5 man lineup… which is no longer the case for us with the current salary cap.

Edit2: ok maybe Andrew Wiggins would be a better comparison than Zach Lavine, with decent efficiency scoring, good 1 on 1 defense but bad off ball defense, and bad playmaking. With him taking the 1A/B burden on your team and scoring 20+ points, you only go as far as the rest of the team takes you (which was not very far with the Twolves). Wiggins only won a chip when he became the 3~4 option on the team, and focused on only the things he does best.
