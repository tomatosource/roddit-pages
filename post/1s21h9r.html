Ballers and Beakers

I thought of this randomly when Jaylen Brown had Bill Nye participating during NBA media day and I’m reminded of it after Derrick White’s recent comments about not believing the Moon Landing.

Pairing science-ignorant athletes like Kyrie Irving, Derrick White, Jaylen Brown, etc with Bill Nye in a Saturday morning style kids show (could stream on one of the NBA’s partners) would make for some quality TV. Call it “Ballers and Beakers”. They can do experiments to show Kyrie why his flat earth theory is wrong. They can do demonstrations with magnets to show Jaylen Brown why the magic snakeoil necklace he wore throughout the 2024 FInals run can’t actually improve your mood with an app. They can take Derrick White on a tour of NASA and have him do a Space camp with other children.

Honestly I’m only 10% joking. This could be a really positive use of their platform to educate kids and themselves.
