Got my Depleted Mana Rune, here’s my setup

I’ve been hunting Depleted Mana Rune for a week now. Frustrated with not getting any results, I asked my friends to tag along. Before they came I had prepared 10 Secluded Temple maps ahead of time. But surprisingly I got it on the first try with 2 other friends. Here’s what I did before we went in:

1 - Talked to Farrow in Hideout and greyed out all his convos
2 - Went to ruined kingsmarch and greyed out all the convos there
3 - Talked to Marou and traveled to cater
4 - From cater, TP to hideout, then Secluded Temple
5 - killed boss, got the rune

I highly doubt the steps above helped but just adding that just in case.

One thing that stood out from all my previous tries though; this Secluded Map didn’t use Uhtred’s Saga. When hovering the rumors interface on the atlas, if I see “Stardrinker” I skip the saga since its guaranteed to have a Secluded Map, so I did. I didn’t remember the other mods, just Stardrinker. So I’m thinking THAT might be a contributing factor. Perhaps Stardrinker + some other line.

Rarity on me: 43%
Waystone Mods: 40% Item Rarity, 30% Monster Effectiveness, Waystone 100%
Tablets: 3x Irradiated =&gt; Unique Monsters have +1 Modifier, Item Rarity Found, Effectiveness
Master: Jado =&gt; Tablet, Exception, Lineage

We continued on my alt hoping to replicate the results, unfortunately it did not drop for me and it did not drop for my party mates also. The only difference was that the remaining temples were accessed using sagas.

Hoping the other hunters out there try noting their logbook rumors before running to see if there’s a special combo needed. Goodluck!!
