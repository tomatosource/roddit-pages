5% into the Season here are my general thoughts

Pros

  • We’re absolutely killing teams despite missing Porzingis and Hauser. This is a big deal for a lot of reasons.
  • I hope refs don’t try this with us all year, but it says something that we beat one of the chief eastern conference teams despite extremely one-sided reffing. Easily one of the worst refed games I’ve ever seen.
  • Pritchard is stepping up big time, dudes a baller. Somehow have to find touches for him even when Hauser returns to the lineup.
  • Porzingis could be weeks out rather than months out. Depends on what the ORg wants, but he looks good already, if he sits til Christmas or after New Year’s its simply because we don’t need him.
  • Tatum and Brown are absolutely positioning us for a repeat. They have to carry a lot of the offensive load right now, but this will change when Porzingis returns.

Cons

  • Our defense isn’t quite where we need it to be, but it will come, especially when Porzingis returns.
  • Tilman and Kornet are simply not real answers. They can be 8s and 9s, but they’re not 6s or 7s…
  • Jordan Springer is playing himself off this roster.

Extra: Giannis is terribly overrated. Not because he’s not a great player, but because so much of his dominance comes from how the refs call the game around him. He travels constantly, he charges constantly.
