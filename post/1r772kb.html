How did the top free agent contracts this winter line up with November industry predictions? An in-depth review.

In this post, we’ll look at the top free agent contracts this winter (all with a guarantee above $10,000,000) and how they line up with predictions from various baseball outlets back in November. The purpose of this post isn’t to mock an outlet for a bad guess. In fact, many of the predictions were very good. I was just curious how things looked here at the end of free agency. (Note that Lucas Giolito and Zack Littell appeared on many of these Top 50 Free Agent lists but haven’t signed yet.)

TL:DR Summary

  • In general, the baseball media industry struggled to accurately predict contracts for: the very top free agents (who settled for mega-AAV deals), players coming over from Japan, QO guys, and players coming off injured seasons.
  • Players who signed early, signed deals in line with November predictions. Players who signed late, did not.
  • The biggest overestimations by total guarantee were: Kyle Tucker, Munetaka Murakami, and Tatsuya Imai.
  • The biggest underestimations by total guarantee were: Dylan Cease, Kyle Schwarber, and Pete Alonso.
  • Considering the sum, average, and median of the total guarantee prediction misses, I’d rank the outlets from most to least accurate this winter as: 1. FanGraphs, 2. ESPN, 3. MLBTR, 4. The Athletic, and 5. Baseball America. There are some individual misses that skew the numbers, so keep that in mind. The top 3 outlets were very close.
