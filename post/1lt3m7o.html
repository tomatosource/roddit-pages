The Suns and Knicks are interested in adding Darvin Ham to their coaching staff:

“Phoenix and now New York — after the hiring of Mike Brown as the Knicks’ new head coach — have expressed interest in trying to hire Darvin Ham away from the Bucks, The Stein Line has learned

The Knicks are interested in bringing in Darvin Ham for an assistant coaching role on their bench. Ham and Mike Brown served together on the 2011-12 Lakers squad.

Phil Handy has also been mentioned as a possible target for Mike Brown’s staff.
