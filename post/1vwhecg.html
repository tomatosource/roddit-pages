So…who wants Jalen Duren or Ben Mathurin in a Sign and Trade for their team?

We’re fast approaching September and thus the start of training camp with not much of note regarding the two remaining notable RFAs: Jalen Duren of the Detroit Pistons and Bennedict Mathurin of the Los Angeles Clippers.

Both remained unsigned for various reasons. Duren had one of the worst playoff performances for an All-NBA player in recent memory and while he is still one of the youngest players in the league, his asking price is pretty hard to justify unless one really believes in their ability to develop him. There’s also the fact that the aforementioned All-NBA nod bumped up the max he could receive but again, the regular max is hard enough to justify for Jalen because of how bad he was.

Mathurin, on the other hand, is part of an archetype that’s been dying out for some time ie the “score only” player who doesn’t defend well enough to justify their lack of playmaking/court vision though Ben does still remain elite as ever at one specific thing: Drawing fouls and getting to the line as a result of those drawn fouls.
