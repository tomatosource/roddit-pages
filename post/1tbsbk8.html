[idea]Let us reset the atlas world

So currently I do not see the problem of an infinite atlas causing infinite bloat being adressed, other than the new search. I have an idea on how to solve this bloat, and allow further player agency.

Allow our first clear of the main questline to unlock a world reset. You reset the world, and choose benefits (in the form of specialisations) for the next world. For example:

  • Your next world will contain 200% more abyss biomes.
  • Your next world will not contain the “desert biome”
  • Your next world will not contain the following maps: (Pick 5).
  • Citadels will spawn closer to your starting point in the next world.

Doing this for the first time will unlock more ways to reset. This could for example be clear 40 maps, defeat 3 citadels.

I don’t think these should be in the form of “increased quantity in next world” or “the next world contains boxes in every map” as they would just lead to a reset meta. I think it should be aimed at reducing bloat and making the world more “your own”.
