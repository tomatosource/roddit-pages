Do we even care if we get the first seed?

Despite a frustrating night all around, one of the things lost in discussion is the fact that the cavs have now pulled 3 games clear of us for the number one seed. I honestly don’t think the one seed is that big of a deal and, despite my anger following tonight’s loss and the poor play over the last couple weeks, the celtics are still the best team in the east.

Thinking of this topic on a broader scale, the celtics have rarely even been the first seed in the tatum/brown era. As a matter of fact, they’ve only been the one seed once and that was last year. This team clearly doesn’t give a fuck about playing big games on the road. They’ve won elimination game 6’s in philly and milwaukee in the 2nd round and a game 7 in miami in the ECF. I don’t think going into cleveland is that big of a deal. Also, except for literally last year, they’ve been a significantly better road playoff team than home during this era for whatever reason.

As a small aside, the celtics remind me of the 18 warriors and the cavs remind me of the 18 rockets. The rockets had the better record that year but nobody believed they were the better team. This was due to the warriors coasting for large portions of the 17⁄18 season and dropping games (like we have) to teams they shouldn’t have. Honestly, this cleveland team isn’t even as good as that rockets team. They’re more like the 2021 jazz, also led by mitchell. Just my thoughts.
