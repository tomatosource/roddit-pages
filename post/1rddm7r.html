Troubleshooting a temple problem where a room doesn’t finish even after you’ve cleaned it completely.

Basically, it’s what the title says… a solution to this problem has been found, and it’s called Entagle.

You basically walk around with an Entagle equipped with gems in its skills, and allways have Ball Lightning + Lightning Warp on your inventory. Having trouble? You put Ball Lightning and Lightning Warp on cast on critical and throw your Entagle under the door. This will hit the enemies in the next room hard and teleport you to them when they’re about to die. This way you can not only fix a room you can’t complete, but you can also advance through numerous rooms even without completing them first, since GGG refuses to fix the temple bugs… and apparently loves to see a temple destroyed.
