My personal experience after “completing” the game on SSF for the first time on a completely homebrew build.

I’m not an english native, sorry if I write something wrong.

I had a little over 1000 hours on PoE 1, but I never liked zooming throught content and the more I played the more I feel like I am playing more of a build calculator than actual game. I know why many players likes PoE 1 and it’s a great game, but not for me because of a few reasons.

Five years ago I thought PoE 2 solve all of my issues with PoE 1 and will be my favoruite game for long if not forever, and now I feel that it actually becomes true as development go closer to full release.

I played 300 hours in 0.1.0 and actually done nothing of endgame content, except killed Xesht for once. With the realease of 0.2.0 I set myself a goal to play SSF for the first time and to reach mapping, lately I add another goal of kill every end game boss at least for once on T1. Here’s my little feedback of that journey that took me almost another 100 hours.

I picked huntress and after the first trials chose ritualist, it seemed more intresting and suited to me. Despite started on day one of 0.2.0 with all of it’s problems campaing was alright, except few bosses like giant in grotto, I had like 10-15 deaths in total as I reached maps. During the campaing I’ve spent almost all my gold to respec a few times, more defense here, more offens there, trying different abilities. I’ve end up making my own totaly homebrew bleed build, with focus on standart spear sthrow, rake, blood hunt and herald of blood, and I’m far from good at making builds. No guides, no meta, I just tried to play what I like aestethically and was trying to make it work.

I progress map tiers slowly, from 1 to 15, playing at least 10 maps of every tier, it took me some time to craft somewhat decent spear for red maps. I had to pick up every spear I found, pile them up in stash and later identidie, aug, regal, reforge or recomb if possible, and so with rings and amulet. Almost every item upgrade feels really important, I actually value items a lot more then while I played trade.

As I reached T15 my surviability was a big trouble. I tried different layers of defence based on evasion, evasion+armour, evasion+block, but end up stacking ES on top of really good evasion like %85 and capped double block from keystone, plus aliment and stun thresholds, when I managed to craft 2k+ evasion body mapping actually becomes easy. I was lucky to get an audience with king relatively early, and other endgame bosses was just a little grinding. Here some feed back on every boss so far.

  • Zarokh

I started from him cause I already knew mechanics, despite I don’t really needed my last 2 points of ascendancy and managed to complete the trial in 3 tries. The boss itself was a lot more easy than in 0.1.0 and I managed to kill him from the first attempt reaching boss room.

  • King of the mist

This one was rough even on T1. I know its intentional but the mess on arena is really disturbing. I died 3 times and I wasn’t even sure why. But after killing him I got unique bleed ring suited for my build.

  • Xesht

Easy on T1, I died once because of my own fault. The boss is okay, fight seemed fair and good overall.

  • Olroth

I died 2 times on T1 and lately managed to kill him on T2 and T3 in a row without too much trouble, but it took some time because of lack of damage.

  • Delirium

Because Herald of blood doesn’t work on delirium mobs it was somewhat painful. I managed to clear all the waves with something like 4 deaths and it can be done a lot more easy If I complete my def layers before the fight. But overall drop in loot was one of the best amongst all bosses.

  • Trialmaster

I’ve grinded him late on lvl92, the fight itself was easy and also a little boring. He is so slow that every other trial boss feels more dangerous.

  • Arbiter of ash

It’s awfull. I knew there was some troubles and bugs with his abilities from 0.1.0 but was hoping they fixed him. Hell no. I died 5 times and only one was actually my fault. Every death was from oneshot phases and it’s actually a mess. He can cast deadly fire beam in the whole safe circle or safe zones during flame walls will ocasionally spawn almost one screen away. It seems still broken and unfair.

And here I am, my goal is completed and it was actually great time. Maybe next time I will try self restricted trade league, cause It was a little sad to drop perfect jeweller obrs or divines, knowing I just don’t need them. Here’s some of my stats. Thanks for reading and stay sane, exiles, feel free to ask anything.
