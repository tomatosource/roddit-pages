Can the Modern Era break this record? It’s stood for 35 years.

This is an interesting record.

  • In 1990, Round 1, Game 2, The Celtics scored 157 points on the Knicks.
  • This is the most points by any team in playoff history.
  • It still stands 35 years later through the pace and space and 3 point era

I find it interesting that this record hasnt been broken since 2015. I would have assumed the highest scoring playoff game had come in the most recent of times.

Looking into the details of this game and I start to realize why maybe no other team has accomplished this since.

Larry Bird in 1990 was older, but his vision and IQ was still peaking. He had 16 assists this game, and had his team playing on a level literally no other team had ever played before.

Im simply not sure this record can be broken because a player like Larry Bird is what fueled this record and he is 1 of a kind.
