Cherry-Picked Accolade: Derrick White is the best shot-blocking guard with above 36% three-point shooting on at least 5 attempts in NBA History

Derrick White averages a career 0.9 blocks per game and 36.9% from beyond the arc. Compare to Dwyane Wade who some regard as the best shot blocking guard in NBA history, but never shot over 33% from three in a season.

Other shot-blocking guard candidates: Roleplayer Mattisse Thybulle averages the same 0.9 BPG, but only 2.5 3PA per game and a 34% three point percentage throughout his career. Klay Thompson exceeds Derrick’s 3PA and %, but averages only 0.5 BPG.
