Dana barros’s tenure with the Celtics….

people that were there at the time did you have a favorable memory of him?

I was young at the time so I did not pay close attention and just like the fact that he was a good shooter. I vaguely remember him sitting a record for the most consecutive games with a 3-pointer against Minnesota in 95… but I was extremely young

looking at the statistics though he was an All-Star for the 76ers and average over 20 points per game before going to Boston. and he never came close to scoring like that in Boston and it’s not like we were loaded with offensive talent.

Dino Radja was the hub of the offense. but Barros seem to hover between 9 and 13 PPG in his tenure.

I assume some of this was the Sherman Douglas situation in his rookie year but they moved him along (to the dismay of then Celtics vice President / executive Larry Bird who mentioned it in his book as one reason why he left the team).

I know he was definitely a combo guard of sort which was harder thing to be in 1995 than it is now. so him being in point guard might have been a little out of position but due to size it was where he was pigeonholed. I can’t recall what his defense was like at all.

tl,:Dr Dana Barros was an All-Star the year before he signed with the Celtics but has middling statistics at best with the team in hindsight. anyone have any thoughts?
