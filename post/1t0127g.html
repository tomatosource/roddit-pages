The NBA has a serious playoff injury problem

Out of the 16 playoff teams, 13 have dealt with injuries to star players or starters.

Stars injured: OKC, Lakers, Rockets, Timberwolves, Spurs, Magic, Raptors, Celtics, 76ers

Just Starters injured: Nuggets (Gordon), Pistons(Huerter), Cavs(strus, wade), Hawks (Hart).

The 3 who have no injuries are all relatively young teams with little playoff milage under their belt. Suns, Hawks, Blazers

Its clear that the modern game is much more strenuous on the human body and changes need to be made. This is a bad product. The rockets lakers series is an absolute farce and is embarrassing playoff basketball.

Its only a matter of time until something is done. I can’t wait for the “whoever is left uninjured wins” finals AGAIN this year.
