Sunder the Flesh

For the love of all things Unholy can we please get a rework to Sunder the Flesh in 0.4!

I have played blood mage across 4 “leagues” at this point and I am very aware of how good this notable is, and arguably it is the only thing keeping Blood Mage as a strong Ascendancy.

The issue I have is its affect on other spell casters primarily. StF is so good at making low crit spells strong that those low crit spells need to be balanced around StF, thereby making other Ascendancy classes have less viable spells to work with. This notably exempts a lot of what the Lich is doing with its DoT based gameplay where skills cant crit and therefor aren’t shadow-nerfed by StF, or the Infernalist with summons which again aren’t crit compatible.

According to PoeNinja, the top ranking spellcaster that is not built for Summons, DoTs or StF is the stormweaver at about 4% usage (admittedly there aren’t a lot of casters classes to work with, and there are casters in other Ascendancies that wouldn’t be themselves considered casters). And it is in my humble opinion that it is, in at least significant part, the spell selection for crit applicable spells (elemental) being weaker in comparison to other playstyle options and that this weakness is a consequence of what Blood Mage would be like if they weren’t weak.

Consider how much of a buff the Elemental skills would need to compare to ED+C or Summons or any reasonable Herald build (not LA, different issue). They can’t even be compared to StF builds because any buff to those skills to make them comparable would simply give the Blood Mage even stronger skills to work with!

While I can appreciate the elegance of flat 15% base crit, the trade off is that all spells need to effectively be balanced as if they were 15% base crit because this node exists. StF is stealing Power from those spells in its current implementation.

There is a secondary consideration that the Bone Spells have a 2 Ascendancy point tax on Goring, and otherwise are ignored by StF but very few people (other than myself) care about that.

Quick fixes to StF are to change it to 3% more chance to crit which maintains the feel of the current StF while still allowing those skills to be given more room to shine with other Ascendancies. Alternatively, crits applying Expose Weakness creates a balancing act between having enough crit to get the exposure stacking, and not too much crit to get wasteful with it modulated by Cast Speed. Although it would prefer multi-hitting spells over one shots which is where late endgame typically ends up anyways.

With the Druid on the cusp on introducing new crit compatible skills, which one do you think the Blood Mage will use in 0.4 to be the strongest caster?
