In-Game Glossary Menu

Might not be a popular take - or might come back with “read patch notes” or something.

But I’d love to see a Glossary Menu, similar to what we have in PoE1. Where it’s a dedicated menu, updated by the GGG team for accuracy to explain league mechanics, etc. To more granularity in defining key terms, or core game mechanics, etc for more beginner level support.

We have many of the definitions built into the game in tool tips, etc. but it would be great to have all of those housed in one menu where some of us who are less knowledgeable to every detail can just peruse and get lost in a web of information.

Edit to mention: Of course since it’s in PoE1 I would expect something to the same effect to eventually be in PoE2. I think it’s felt needed more right now because things are changing very very regularly league to league.
