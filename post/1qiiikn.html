Bill Kennedy & Rick Welts have been inducted into the 2026 class of the LGBTQ Sports Hall of Fame

The LGBTQ Sports Hall of Fame has announced its inductees for this year, including 10 people from the Olympics, WNBA, NBA, NFL, high school sports, the sports media and more.

In addition, Billie Jean King and Jason Collins will each be honored with the Glenn Burke Award, given to “individuals whose courage and authenticity have transformed the sports landscape.”

“These individuals embody the very spirit of our mission of the Hall of Fame,” Scott Bova, Chair of the LGBTQ Sports Hall of Fame, said in a statement. “Their contributions to sports and advocacy have paved the way for greater inclusion and have inspired countless athletes and fans worldwide. We are honored to recognize their legacy.”

The 2026 class of the LGBTQ Sports Hall of Fame are:

  • Nikki Hiltz – Olympic distance runner (trans and nonbinary trailblazer for Team USA).
  • Bill Kennedy – Longtime NBA referee (the first male NBA official to come out publicly).
  • Maybelle Blair – Former All-American Girls Professional Baseball League player (featured in “A League of Their Own” history).
  • Rick Welts – CEO of the Dallas Mavericks and former NBA executive (credited with creating the “Dream Team”).
  • Phaidra Knight – Legendary rugby player and World Rugby Hall of Fame member.
  • Robin Roberts – Renowned broadcaster (ESPN and Good Morning America) and Women’s Basketball Hall of Fame member.
  • Sue Wicks – WNBA All-Star and one of the first active WNBA players to come out.
  • Anthony Nicodemo – High school basketball coach and athletic director known for LGBTQ advocacy.
  • Dan Woog – Successful high school soccer coach and author of “Jocks,” one of the first books on gay male athletes.
  • Kirk Walker – Legendary softball coach (UCLA and Oregon State) and a major advocate for inclusion in college sports.

Source: Outsports
