Baseball Rules Quiz

Hey y’all, I created this fun baseball rules quiz to test your knowledge of the game! It should only take about 10 minutes. This is all based on the OBR (official baseball rules) ruleset, the same one used by MLB and most travel ball associations.

This quiz is totally anonymous, I don’t see anyone’s name or email.
