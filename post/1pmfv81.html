GGG if you are going to keep the current length of the campaign, please introduce an interlude-style leveling alternative

At the current rate, I’m honestly not sure how you will make people happy about campaign length without seriously compromising on your vision for that experience.

It’s not that there isn’t enough content, it’s not that the content isn’t engaging enough, it’s that maps are too big, there are too many zones in a given act, and it makes multiple characters prohibitive in a genre where that is a very common player choice.

The interludes are great, there should be an abridged shorter campaign option once you complete the campaign once. It preserves a semblance of your vision (engaging tutorial vs aimless grind in delve leveling) while not burning people out.
