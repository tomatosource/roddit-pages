Philly “won” the trade but there are a lot of ways this could go wrong.

I was as surprised as anyone by the trade. On paper, Boston got fleeced and the Sixers should likely be the Eastern Conference favorites. It’s a little crazy Brad would strengthen a rival. And the return is not at all what I thought JB would bring. I have to imagine this had to be based on concern about how disgruntled JB was and about the looming contract extension this summer which will be an overpay.

But I think the Sixers may have a looming issue they need to carefully address. Over the last few years, Brown has been causing unrest in Boston because of his clear belief that he’s the number one option, is better than Tatum, should be leading the team, and is unappreciated. He got a taste of being the number one option this year and it seems to be what pushed him over the edge to the point the relationship with the team was no longer sustainable.

As such, in Philadelphia there’s a strong chance he clashes with others. Maxey is the Tatum of Philadelphia and the clear young leader. When healthy, Embiid is a high touch player and often the best offensive force on the court. And Edgecomb is a young star who will need touches and emphasis to develop. When Embiid is healthy, Brown may sometimes be the team’s third option. His touches will go down. And the likelihood that he assumes the “team leader” role in Philly is low. If he was clashing with the team competing for time and attention with one star, how will he respond when it’s four stars?

Maybe he will work it out. Maybe he’ll be so motivated to prove himself it won’t matter. Maybe Philly has more moves around Embiid, Edgecomb, and Maxey to make. But man…if they extend him for $70m per year this summer and he blows up the team dynamic there are a lot of ways this could go wrong in Philly (even if there are also a lot of ways this could end up a super team). And if things go south his contract could end up as hard to move as Embiid’s at precisely the time they are going to need to pay Edgecomb and some of the other guys.
