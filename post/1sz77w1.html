Can we do it again, few points of concern.

Can we win it all this year?

As much as I’d like to see the Celtics go the full distance, I can’t help but wonder if that’s going to be possible this time around. We are a great SHOOTING team and we are great on defense, but there are a few points of concern.

We don’t seem to have an inside game. Our inside game consists of Jalen Brown or Jayson Tatum going off a pick and roll/screen and driving in the open lane. If the lane gets clogged, kick out to the perimeter. In fact, it is quite obvious this is our approach because when JB or JT drive, you see 3 or 4 guys on the perimeter waiting for a kick out, and no one else goes in the paint to help. Rarely, you see Queta big guy in the paint and we dish out to him for a quick layup, but only if he is open. And Queta averages only ~20-25 min a game.

When we had the championship run in 24, we had Luke Kornet 7-2, Porzingis 7-2, and Jrue Holiday 6-4. Those guys could do the job in the paint and lay it up.

We dont have those guys anymore. Relying solely on 3s and fadeaway jumpers works best in the 1st and 2nd quarters, but when we get tired by the late 3rd of 4th, those shots start missing. You see that.

And also, on the defense, although solid, we dont have anyone big enough to match up against a Joker or a Wemby. Queta is our only 7 footer and again, he cannot go 48 minutes. How are we going to guard those guys without getting slaughtered on the glass?
