The crawl speed of experience gain, level 90-100 needs to be addressed

First off, I love PoE2 even more than I loved PoE1. I am no hater. I am a huge supporter of this game and I know for sure it will only continue to get better. I love that the devs are taking the opportunity in early access to try things out and make big changes even if they don’t work out and end up changing them. I love that they actually listen to the community and are making new changes all the time.

With that being said however, the amount of xp you get in the endgame is utterly demoralizing, especially when combined with the death penalty. The death penalty was always the one part I hated about PoE1 and was always the reason why I would quite a league somewhere around level 92-94. Then come back a few leagues later to play it again when I wanted to roll a new character.

In PoE2, while I still hate the death experience penalty mechanic and firmly believe that it should be replaced with a death gold penalty mechanic (xp only if you have no gold) at least there is an omen to negate 75% of the experience loss. However, the amount of experience you gain from a map is FAR too low.

This map I posted below had built in % increased experience gain, % increased monster packs, % increased magic monsters, % increased number of rare monsters and was Irradiated and Corrupted and had Delerium. The only modifier that was kind of low was Increased Pack Size at 9%.

And I literally only got 3% of my experience for clearing the map. Sure it was Creek, so if had been a bigger map like savannah I could have gotten a bit more, but still. This is absolutely ridiculous. I’m only level 92 and this is the most difficult high experience gain map I could possibly create, and all I got was 3%.

I didn’t die. All my atlas points so far have been focused on increasing the number of monsters and the power of mods to level as fast as possible. No investments in special occurrences.

I mean how the heck am I ever supposed to reach level 100 when I need 20-30 of these maps just to get from 92-93 and the experience gain will be even less as my level goes up?

I men it seems like I would need to run a few hundred maps like this to hit level 100.

Am I missing something here?
