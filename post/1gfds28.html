Derozan averages through 4 games: 23/4/4 on 64.2% True Shooting

There were some people, Kings fans and non-Kings fans, who believed that Derozan would have his most efficient season yet due to the high talent that’s around him on this Kings squad. So far, so good! Derozan’s on pace to surpass his most efficient season (2019-2020 when he averaged 60.3% true shooting).

Obviously though, it’s only 4 regular season games out of 82, and he’s got a lot to prove if the Kings make it to the postseason. But Deebo’s been averaging over 20 for almost a decade, so there’s a good chance that he can keep the streak up. With these numbers, he might even be sneaking into the All-Star game.

You might say that Derozan doesn’t play defense, but then I’d point you to the guy he replaced - Harrison Barnes. After 4 games, Derozan is currently averaging 2 steals and 0.25 blocks per game, and that’s compared to Harrison Barnes’ averages of 0.7 steals and 0.1 blocks per game last year.
