Thought exercise: if we DONT acquire giannis, come up with your realistic most ideal/best offseason scenario that would make this team a true contender next year

I’ve seen a *lot* of “if we don’t get giannis we aren’t serious and no way we can contend everything is ruined” lately, and find it way over the top and unnecessary. The playoffs matter, they matter a lot, but they matter differently in terms of team context. It showed us we have the bones of a championship team still in that our core guys were able to carry a weak on paper roster to a top 2 seed and create new role players out of thin air, it showed our top end is still as good as its ever been in brown/tatum(brown was clearly tired and Tatum was a little rusty off his injury, they’ll be better). In terms of the things it showed we need to fix, it showed us we lack bench creation and scoring spark, and it showed we lack *options* in our big rotation.

How do we fix this? In my eyes, that’s two problems, meaning I think we have answers and check marks for every other question/box that needed to be addressed, just need a bench/fringe starter shot creator and another really good big. I’m gonna give my perfect offseason vision, and im curious to hear yours. To be clear, you don’t have to agree with my idea of what we need to fix, if yours is different or more involved then by all means, tell me how you fix that too.

Our warchest(not including white/pritchard/most of the young guys but feel free to trade them if you wish):
-Hauser
-Walsh
-pick 27⁄40
-3 future firsts(27, 31, 33)
-27 mill TPE
-full MLE
-BAE

My perfect offseason:

First thing im doing is trading up in the draft with Hauser for a long term big solution. I chose to do this via draft because all of the bigs that fit what we need are too expensive/not getting moved/don’t feel realistic. If I do it via draft im getting a cost controlled healthy young energy guy, young bigs also tend to be ready earlier.

The pick im trading for is 14 from charlotte, and the player im getting is either morez Johnson or hannes steinbach; for this I’ll take morez Johnson who’s my ideal pick. Charlotte has another pick at 18 so they agree to trade this one, we send them Hauser/walsh/2027 first/2 future 2nds/pick 40.

Next thing I’d do is take either Sergio de larrea with pick 27 in the draft.

-I chose to do this because we need to continue getting young, cheap, and I feel it’s important to get rookies in this system early and mold them into exactly what we need. Switchable 4⁄5 who’s got real potential to be a good shooter for a big, I think he’d pop in this system. Really strong all around defender and motor, the ideal outcome for him is his 3 becomes real and he ends up being that 4⁄5 who can play really big at the 4 or let us go small without giving up shooting as a 5.

Next I’d do is sign Coby white outright to the MLE, or sign and trade/throw charlotte a future pick if he needs more than the MLE. Like I said, this is my “ideal” offseason so in this scenario Coby wants to play for a chance at a chip with us and signs, but consolation prize would be bringing in sexton or bringing Simon’s back, each for 12-14 mill

Last thing I’d do, since I saved us money swapping Walsh and Hauser for two rookies, is spend our biannual exception(about 5 mill) on Rob or KP, whoever has a weaker market and would come for a one year 5 mill deal. For this, we will assume that’s KP and joins as a chance to win another title and prove he’s still got it. If you decide these are both too unrealistic, then I spend it on brook Lopez.

Starters: Pritchard-white-brown-Tatum-queta

Bench: Coby-Hugo-Baylor-morez-amari

Extended bench: de larrea-Harper-Garza-Lopez

That’s my offseason, I think that fixes pretty much all that ails us and fixes a lot long term. What would you do?
