serious questions… i find that we already had an amazing tool in POB and it was forked for POE2. so why all the sudden the change to Mobalytics for every build? are they paying content creators for it? does the community genuinely believe that it’s better?

i personally am not a fan. POB is the perfect tool for path of exile. if you need to provide notes for the build there is a notes section specifically for that. and while i will admit that mobalytics is more aesthetically pleasing…. it just frustrates me that in order to follow someone’s build i have to have a page open that has weird banner ads all over the place. i strayed from maxxroll when i found POB. and while i appreciate everyone who contributes builds… i just don’t think that mobalytics has what i need to make decisions on a build.

i fully admit that i am relatively a novice when it comes to path of exile 1 and 2 with a combined 1500 hours between the two… but i don’t like to run anyone’s build 1:1 for the most part. i always like to put my own little flair on things. i use build guides as a basis and not a hard and fast rule. and the fact that i have to import everything manually into POB to figure out what is legitimately better for me and my playstyle that i envision makes the process unnecessarily tedious. i also understand that this is more of a gripe than something that is truly wrong with anyone’s preference.
