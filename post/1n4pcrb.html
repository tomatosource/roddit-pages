[BasketNews] Bogdan Bogdanovic will miss the rest of EuroBasket 2025 due to a hamstring injury sustained vs. Portugal

Source

Serbia suffered a massive blow as its team’s captain, Bogdan Bogdanovic, will be forced to miss the remainder of the EuroBasket 2025 due to a hamstring injury.

Meridian Sports reports that the additional tests showed a tear in Bogdanovic’s hamstring, and he won’t be available to represent his country.

Bogdanovic suffered the injury against Portugal after playing 15 minutes.

In two EuroBasket 2025 appearances, the 33-year-old averaged 20 minutes, 9.0 points, 3.5 rebounds, 4.0 assists, and 12.5 efficiency points per game.

Serbia has a 3-0 record in Group A, although its last two wins, against Portugal (80-69) and Latvia (84-80), came after intense efforts.

Nikola Jokic finished with 39 points and 10 rebounds against Latvia, setting his career-high for the national team.
