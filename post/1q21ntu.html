Proposal for Vaal Temple Changes

I have a somewhat major but well-reasoned (I think) change I would make to the design of the vaal temple.

Most of our current issues with the temple stem from its semi-permanent nature. You put rooms down with the hope that they stay, but are disappointed when they get randomly deleted. Some people are taking advantage of how the temple deletes rooms by making one giant snake and protecting the very end of it so the whole thing can’t ever be deleted. This leads to very static and uninteresting temples with only a few room types. This is a design problem regardless of how you feel about the exact quantity of loot being given by doing this. If you aren’t doing the snake strat, or even just trying to get it started, you will often find yourself fighting the system and getting frustrated when you don’t get the rooms you want.

My proposal is to change the temple to be a completely run-based affair. You build your entire temple each time using the (larger) pool of rooms you’re given. I would also probably make the temple slightly smaller in both dimensions as well, making it easier to get to Atziri or the architect.

Instead of the Architect giving you a room that THEN unlocks Atziri, beating the architect in a run immediately lets you fight Atziri on that run (assuming you pathed to her).

The entire temple would wipe after each run, no punitive RNG, only the RNG of what rooms you’re given initially (remember we’re getting more rooms and the temple is smaller now).

This would make every run an independent and unique little puzzle trying to maximize the benefits of the pool of rooms you get each time. It’s the fun part of temple, minus the unfun parts and tedious RNG setup. It also conveniently avoids all fringe temple setups that would be a problem if you could do them reliably, instead maybe you only get to do the giga-juiced divine printing layouts occasionally. (If you get lucky).

My main point, I suppose, is that temple currently is like a bad city builder that’s constantly (randomly) deleting your buildings, when it really should feel more rogue-like.
