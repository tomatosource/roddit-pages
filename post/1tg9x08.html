The Cleveland Cavaliers have made it to their first Eastern Conference finals without LeBron James since he got drafted to the NBA in 2003.

Their last Eastern Conference finals before LeBron came to the league was in 1992 and since LeBron got drafted, they made the Eastern Conference finals in 2007, 2009, 2015, 2016, 2017 and 2018 all with LeBron. This is the first time they’ve done it without LeBron James during his tenure in the NBA.
