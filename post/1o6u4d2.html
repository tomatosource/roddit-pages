Minott was the 44th ranked HS player in the country back in 2021. Some ranked him as low as 77th (247) He played for powerhouse St. Anthony’s in FL.

At Memphis (college) he played less than 15 mpg. Averaging 6+ ppg. He had good efficiency with a .522 FGP. His 3 ball was almost nonexistent shooting 2-14 for the year. After being drafted 44nd overall, he had a good PER with the T-Wolves averaging 17 over 3 seasons in limited time. He had a per of 20+ in 23-24. He fell off a bit last year, so the Celtics were able to get him signed to a 2-yr/$5M contract Monday, July 7, 2025. This was another great pickup by Brad Stevens- the NBA’s best GM.
