Why is the team so much better on the road vs at home in the playoffs?

The Celtics had *Another* Successful road trip in the playoffs. It feels like this happens every year, and I looked up a couple stats to confirm what I thought I saw.

At home since 2022, the Celtics are 25-17 at home. A .595 winning percentage. On the road, the Celtics are 25-11. A .694 winning percentage. That is drastic. They play like a 49 win team at home, and a 57 win team on the road.

Tatum’s numbers follow this pattern. At home, Tatum is averaging 25/9/6 on 44/32/84. On the road Tatum is at 28/9/6 on 45/37/90. This makes sense based on their record.

But Jaylen at home is averaging 24/6/4 on 49/39/70. On the road, Jaylen is averaging 22/6/3 on the road on 48/33/71

So what is? Even though Tatum’s numbers are better on the road, it’s not like they are drastic. Plus Jaylen’s production goes down on the road.

This is a sports radio take, but part of me thinks it’s them being anxious. When they’re in their home building, I think they feel the pressure of letting people down. For those of you who go to games, i feel it. When Tatum lines up a pull up 3, the whole building kind of groans. But this feels like lazy analysis. So what do you guys think it is?
