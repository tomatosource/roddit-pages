76ers vs Bulls flexed into Amazon Prime on December 26th, replacing Celtics vs Pacers

The Chicago Bulls will now have two nationally televised games before the new year. Following their matchup with the Magic on Peacock next week, they will now be part of Amazon Prime’s post-Christmas lineup. The team’s battle with the Philadelphia 76ers at the United Center will appear on Amazon Prime, making that two games this season that will be aired by one of the league’s newest associates.

The game will replace the Celtics vs. Pacers game that was initially scheduled for 6:30 p.m. CT. This will also be the first of an Amazon Prime double-header, which currently ends with Clippers-Trail Blazers.
