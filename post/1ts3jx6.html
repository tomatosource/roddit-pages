Victor Wembanyama message to the Spurs before their elimination game against the Thunder

Before Game 6, Victor Wembanyama gathered his team on the court and gave them a passionate speech about no longer having “life after.” They had to leave it all out on the floor, because all that was waiting for them on the other side was the afterlife of a season full of promise.

Then he went out there and made it clear the Spurs season would not be ending on this night. (Via/@JaredWeissNBA)
