SGA’s Historic Ball Security

SGA is one of the most ball secure scorers in NBA history.

Since the NBA first started tracking individual turnovers per game back in the 1977⁄1978 season, there has been 62 total 30 point per game seasons. Of those 62 total seasons, SGA holds three of the top 5 most ball secure seasons. The other two? None other than Michael Jordan.

  1. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, 2025⁄2026, 32.6 ppg, 1.6 tpg

  2. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, 2023⁄2024, 30.1 ppg, 2.2 tpg

  3. Michael Jordan, 1995⁄1996, 30.4 ppg, 2.4 tpg

  4. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, 2024⁄2025, 32.7 ppg, 2.4 tpg

  5. Michael Jordan, 1990⁄1991, 31.5 ppg, 2.5 tpg

Should Shai sustain his level of ball security this season, he’ll be the first player in NBA history to average less than 2 turnovers per game while scoring over 30 points per game. Surprisingly, Lauri Markkanen (1.4 tpg this season) can hit that milestone too if he increases his scoring average from 29.3 to 30.0.

What makes Shai so impressive though is just how much he has the ball in his hands. He is currently ninth in the league for time of possession per game (6.8) and when you combine that with his historically low turnover rates, the rate at which OKC force turnovers, and how good of a rebounding team OKC is, you basically win the possession game every single time.

If you were to use turnover% over turnovers per game, then Shai is still the most ball secure 30 point per game scorer in NBA history, claiming both 1st (6.3% 2025⁄2026) and 2nd (8.4% 2023⁄2024) on that list. Interestingly, the gap between Shai at 1st (6.3%) is larger than the gap between Shai at 2nd (8.4%) and Tatum at 21st (10.4% 2022⁄2023). Although, once again, Lauri Markkanen (5.7%) can top that list should he increasing his scoring average and sustain.

All this to say, Shai is straight up one of the most ball secure primary ball handlers in NBA history and is having a historic season this year in that regard.
