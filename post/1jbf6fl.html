My thoughts on an endgame upgrade

TLDR - Towers for favorite maps, more atlas points and Nodes, any points can be used in league mechanic trees, tablets used with waystones, and add orbs to change/link maps.

In general, I really like POE2 and given it’s state in early access i think it is in a great position for the long term. At the moment the end game is lacking but i believe that will expand greatly. Once acts 4-6 are released that should add a significant amount of content and maps to the end game. There are 44 map layouts in the endgame now, and after acts 4-6, i think it is reasonable that jumps into the 80ish range. below are some ideas for the end game:

  1. Use the same system from POE1 to grant atlas points. Instead of “complete 10 T1 maps” just grant 1 point per map completed. Assuming we are up to 80 maps, thats 80 atlas points, on a tree of ~400 nodes, plus league trees (ritual, breach, etc trees.)
  2. Allow atlas points to be used on league trees until you can unlock their specific atlas points by beating the boss for that mechanic. So after, 8 maps you can farm fully juiced breach if you are strong enough, but you wont have any points in the main atlas and you will probably die. once you beat the boss 4 times, you can put those points in breach, and move the other 8 back into the main atlas.
  3. Inverse towers and use them for favored maps. You start the tower by picking you favorite map and upon completion of the map requirements (a random boss at the end preferably, not a bunch of rares) all maps are “rerolled” with a boosted chance to change each map to your favorite. 1 favorite slot for each tier of map (1-15) for 15 slots or you could do 3 per biome. To change it later on simply enter a tower with a t16 and re allocate with no need to finish the tower. That said, you might want to finish the tower, because in this verison each of the 5 tower types would have a unique boss at T16. This would come with a reduction in the number of towers that appear in the map, call it 25% of current.
  4. Use the extra 3 slots when putting in a Waystone as a source to juice maps with tablets. Increase the tablet drop rate and just put them in individual maps. So if you want to add a boss, ritual, and breach map just toss in those tablets. With this, and a complete 80 point atlas, there should be plenty of juice and a way to pick the mechanic you want to focus on. This also helps with finding more citadels since your not hunting groups of towers and weaving around crap maps
  5. add an Horizon orb to change a map and a fusion orb to add a link between to maps with in a radius. so if you hate a map, you can just change it. If you don’t want to path around 8 maps to get around water, you can build a bridge.

Obviously there are other things that need fixed like balance issues, one-shot or be one-shot on bosses etc but i believe ggg will address a large portion of that in the 0.2.0 patch and tweak it as needed. let me know what you think.
