This patch is incredible. Once you get to like level 40.

Sprinting, all the crafting changes, and the support gem changes have made the game feel incredible once you get past like level 40 or so. Before level 40ish the progression curve feels brutal if your not playing one of “the chosen skills” like lightning rod or essence drain. Mostly you feel both starved on damage and tankiness so you end up spending a decent amount of time sacrificing one for the other with no real tools to fix either. This usually means damage wins out so that you don’t also suffer mana issues and 15 minute boss fights. Outside of the skill balance issues that have been talked about a lot here I think this is the biggest problem for the game right now. There either needs to be a little bit more implicit power to the gearing, skills or passive tree starting around level 10ish till level 40 or so. Right now low level doesn’t feel challenging, but punishing. Mid to endgame feels much better in my opinion with plenty of options to improve where your character might be lacking.
