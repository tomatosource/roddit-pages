The NBA needs to cut down on the length of timeouts at the end of games

I think it’s ridiculous that 1 minute of game time left can be dragged out to 30 minutes thanks to all these timeouts and ad-breaks.

Whilst the basketball aspect of the Spurs-Thunder game were fantastic, the end of that game was borderline unwatchable.

The NBA needs to enforce shortening the length of timeouts at the end of games, or lower the number of timeouts each team can get at the end of games.

The NBA is a fast paced high action sports league, why kill all the hype and momentum in the last minute, when you’d want a close game to be as hype as possible?

Also shorter timeouts means less ads, NBA already makes bucket loads of money, there’s no need for all this filler that completely kills the watchability from a fans perspective.
