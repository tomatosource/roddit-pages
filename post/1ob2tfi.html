It has been a thrilling race season, with players really respecting that 2.5 min death timer penalty. Gas grenades have utterly dominated the bosses and there are many upsets this round.

lightspeedcake managed a ~32min Act I completion with the Sorceress.

wholenewsociety cleared Act III in under the 2 hour mark, at about 1:58, with the Mercenary. We haven’t seen a &lt;2hr Doryani since last season with Rake + Stomping Ground, and that was only better by ~20 minutes or so.

wholenewsociety started the Act IV Tavakai fight at a time of ~2:22, but unfortunately failed the 1st attempt during the 3rd phase.
