Why is PoE2 combo gameplay so restrictive?

PoE2’s combo gameplay is largely built around the classic “builder” and “spender” (or “primer” and “detonator”) principle. You build up resources with one skill — Remnants/Infusions, Charges, Combo stacks, or elemental ailments — and consume them with another. Many skill gems are clearly designed to work together in sequence.

The problem is that most “builders” can barely fulfill their function because they either have a condition attached, feel clunky, have too much delay, have a cooldown, or some combination of all of the above.

For example, almost all Elemental Infusion generators like Living Bomb, Frost Bomb, and Orb of Storms have a limit and don’t generate their Remnants immediately. These are actually among the better examples — Frost Bomb applies Exposure and Orb of Storms can deal decent damage with Spark — but that also highlights the issue: you’re not using these skills as builders, you’re using them for their other functions and the Remnants are just a bonus. Then there’s Runic Extraction added in 0.5, which grants a Viridium Infusion and is effectively an omni-infusion, but it’s locked behind an on-kill effect, making it useless in boss fights.

Charge generators aren’t much better. Many only generate charges on kill — Killing Palm, Power Siphon, Cull the Weak — or require you to parry/block, like Disengage or Magma Barrier.

With elemental ailments, Freeze has the specific problem that after you freeze an enemy, they gain an extremely high freeze resistance that only decays over time. This means any combo built around consuming Freeze effectively stops working after the first time you freeze a enemy and you need ~15 seconds to freeze the boss again. I get that GGG doesn’t want enemies in permanent CC, but they still need some solution for combo skills that rely on this — whatever that solution looks like.

I could keep going and list more examples of how restrictive many of these combos feel. And don’t get me wrong — there are some good combo skills and workarounds that combine passive nodes, uniques, and active/support gems to create genuinely smooth combo gameplay, so it’s not all bad. But overall, I think the overly restrictive combo design leaves a lot of skills dead on arrival.

What I also don’t understand is why GGG designed PoE2 this way. Are they that afraid of players abusing these skills? But why should that even matter in a PvE game? And despite all this restrictive design, there are still builds that can basically one-shot endgame encounters — so what’s the hesitation?

That’s my take — what do you think?
