Why do Strongboxes suck so much?

https://preview.redd.it/379fbl8o7yuf1.png?width=503&format=png&auto=webp&s=e9fae56f8a62602d224730ea3085c94b578a68e1

Okay, so today I sat down and hit peak hatred for Delirium on maps, which made me check out some other stuff. I went with strongboxes, added points to them on the skill tree, got the tablets and omens for double-opening, and I’m like-let’s go.

And oh my god, you cannot farm this. I wanted an alternative to that stupid Delirium, and what I got was nothing, namely - no matter how many “extra strongboxes” you have from tablets or the mod “area with powerful boss contains additional strongbox” - the number of strongboxes on the map is always 2, even though you can clearly tell from map mods that there should be at least three (from the powerful boss mod and from tablets). Nope, always two.

And that’s not even the worst part: the Omen that lets you reopen a strongbox only works once per instance. Why?

Why am I forced to run maps with Delirium, why does nothing else make sense, PEOPLE.

EDIT: And why can’t they spawn all monsters at once like in POE1, not spawn them in waves with some fog that OBVIOUSLY hurts performance.

EDIT2: Did some testing, and it seems “additional X” tablets are kinda bricking the map. It applies to essences too. You can have 1000% increased chance for essences or strongboxes on map, if you have “additional strongox/essence on your map” on in your tablet then you’ll have exactly that number of strongbox or essence on your map. So 1 tablet = 1 box, 2 tablets = 2 boxes etc, it completely ignores your tree chance and all

So TL:DR - it’s not ‘additional’ its ‘fixed’ amount
