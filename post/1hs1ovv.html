NBA All Star fan voting through the first fan returns has decreased nearly 50% in the past two years

I was looking at the numbers for this year’s All Star fan voting and they seemed pretty low, so I decided to count up each section.

All numbers below are indicative of first fan returns each year, not the final number when starters are announced.

#2025 First Fan Returns

Total: 21,453,173

West/East Total: 11,270,469 / 10,182,704

West Bigs/Guards: 6,792,049 / 4,541,420

East Bigs/Guards: 6,077,523 / 4,105,181

#2024 Total First Fan Returns

Total: 27,566,972

West/East Total: 14,190,647 / 13,376,325

West Bigs/Guards: 8,412,790 / 5,777,857

East Bigs/Guards: 7,957,137 / 5,419,188

#2023 Total First Fan Returns

Total: 41,847,180

West/East Total: 21,611,160 / 20,236,020

West Bigs/Guards: 12,895,498 / 8,715,662

East Bigs/Guards: 12,051,974 / 8,184,046

It’s 48.7%, to be more exact.

Coincidentally, Adam Silver first instituted the NBA Cup two years ago, a move designed to increase fan viewership and participation. The NBA Cup championship also takes place days before All Star voting begins.

Is this an indicator of the NBA’s declining popularity or general apathy towards the All Star Game itself?
