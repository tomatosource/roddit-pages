Up until 2023, the LA Clippers intended to become exclusive partners with Aspiration before the company filed for bankruptcy and was subsequently liquidated.

It is possible that Kawhi Leonard never delivered on his promotional/partnership agreement because the Clippers terminated their partnership with Aspiration after the company came under federal investigation and slid toward bankruptcy. If the broader deal between Aspiration and the Clippers collapsed, there’s a good chance that Kawhi’s individual obligations were halted or voided along with it.

It is also possible that Kawhi Leonard never delivered on his promotional/partnership agreement because his contract with Aspiration might have included conditional activation clauses. If certain milestones — like Clippers-Aspiration partnership activations — weren’t met, he may never have been required to promote them at all.
