Since 1989 (when all-nba third team was added), there are 5 teams in nba history that have had 3 all nba players on them that year.

1994-1995 Seattle Supersonics:

Gary Payton - 2nd Team

Shawn Kemp - 2nd Team

Detlef Schrempf - 3rd Team

This team had a record of 57-25, but the next year they had an even better record of 64-18 but Detlef Schrempf didn’t make an all nba team that year

2004-2005 Phoenix Suns:

Steve Nash - 1st Team

Amar’e Stoudemire - 2nd Team

Shawn Marion - 3rd Team

This suns team had a record of 62-20, the previous year they were 29-53. An insane 33 game turn around.

2014-2015 Los Angeles Clippers:

Chris Paul - 2nd Team

Blake Griffin - 3rd Team

Deandre Jordan - 3rd Team

This team had a record of 56-26. They also had the player that led the league in assists (Chris Paul 10.2 APG) and rebounds (Deandre Jordan 15 RPG).

Ok, the interesting teams are out of the way.

2015-2016 Golden State Warriors:

Stephen Curry - 1st Team

Draymond Green - 2nd Team

Klay Thompson - 3rd Team

This team had a record of 73-9. Nothing else to say, they were really fucking good.

2016-2017 Golden State Warriors:

Stephen Curry - 2nd Team

Kevin Durant - 2nd Team

Draymond Green - 3rd Team

This team had a record of 67-15. If any team on this list could’ve had 4, it would’ve been this one, but Klay didn’t make an all nba team this year.

Thats the list. If I included pre-1989 then there would’ve been like 7 Russell Celtics years and I’m not tryna do all that work. I’m honestly surprised there aren’t more modern teams on here though, there aren’t any heat years on here or Spurs dynasty years. Not even a bulls dynasty year on here. The team that surprised me the most is the clippers, but that team had the leading apg and rpg player so not too surprising. As always, I hope you enjoyed the read.
