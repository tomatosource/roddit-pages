DiVincenzo included in Italy preliminary roster for EuroBasket 2025

Donte DiVincenzo has been included in Italy’s preliminary roster for EuroBasket 2025. Head coach Gianmarco Pozzecco announced the squad ahead of the training camp.

Italy has officially tipped off its EuroBasket 2025 journey, announcing the preliminary squad that will gather for training camp in Folgaria (Trento) from July 23 to 31.

Among the most notable names on the list is NBA guard Donte DiVincenzo, who has been invited to join for practices starting July 30.

The camp serves as the starting point for Italy’s preparation ahead of FIBA EuroBasket 2025, which runs from August 28 to September 14.

Head coach Gianmarco Pozzecco will count on several key contributors. Simone Fontecchio is scheduled to join on July 30, while veteran Danilo Gallinari will arrive later after wrapping up club duties.

Despite being unavailable for the training camp, Achille Polonara was still included in the player pool in the reserve roster. His presence, even from a distance, underlines his ongoing importance to the team. Darius Thompson, currently with Valencia, has also made his intentions clear: he wants to wear the Italy jersey as soon as he’s eligible.

Full roster includes:

Donte DiVincenzo (Minnesota Timberwolves) Marco Spissu (Casademont Saragozza) Dame Sarr (Duke University NCAA) Stefano Tonut (EA7 Emporio Armani Milano) Danilo Gallinari (Vaqueros de Bayamon) Nicolo Melli (Fenerbahce Istanbul) Simone Fontecchio (Miami Heat) Giampaolo Ricci (EA7 Emporio Armani Milano) Matteo Spagnolo (Alba Berlin) Gabriele Procida (Free Agent) Saliou Niang (Virtus Segafredo Bologna) Giordano Bortolani (Acqua San Bernardo Cantu) Guglielmo Caruso (Napoli Basket) Momo Diouf (Segafredo Virtus Bologna) Riccardo Rossato (Trapani Shark) Luca Severini (Unahotels Reggio Emilia) Nikola Akele (Segafredo Virtus Bologna) Alessandro Pajola (Segafredo Virtus Bologna) Reserve roster:

Diego Flaccadori (EA7 Emporio Armani) Darius Thompson (Valencia Basket) Amedeo Tessitori (Umana Reyer Venezia) Tommaso Baldasso (Bertram Tortona) Michele Vitali (Unahotels Reggio Emilia) Achille Polonara (Segafredo Virtus Bologna)
