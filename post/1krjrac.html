A little speed goes a long way

I am playing Amazon, Deadeye, and Lich spear builds using the exact same gear. It is weird that a total of 2 extra 3% movement speed nodes on Amazon and Deadeye make them feel much more responsive than the lich. The Deadeye gets tailwind, so it is not just the nodes. No wonder people pay extra divines to get that 5% from 35% boots.

I also play a SoK without any movement speed nodes at all using the same skills and gears. it feels awful in comparison. I played a Titan through most of campaign last season, but could stomach the thought of putting him in map.

This gets me thinking. The big thing about Mace skills is that they all have added time to attack of varying degrees. The longest is obviously the HotG. I know that GGG is going for a lumbering hulks wading slowly through enemies vibes. All two-handed maces have roughly 50% more base damage and is supposed to be a compensation.

But this comes at a cost of the feeling of clunkiness and unresponsiveness. in reality, especially in boss fights, if you have to cancel animation to dodge boss mechanics, you can’t even get the attack off. Being a lumbering hulks means that you are much more likely to lose DPS if you have to dodge. The window where you can use skills is also incredibly narrow.

Just as with movement speed, a little reduction in Mace attack time should improve the responsiveness of the gameplay. How about dropping the added attack time in exchange for the lower base damage? You could even do this as a Keystone to test out how the class plays.
