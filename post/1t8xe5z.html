Only 2 coaches in NBA history have a better record than Mark Daigneault through their first 40 playoff games

Daigneault hit exactly 40 career playoff games tonight at the end of Game 3 vs. the Lakers, finishing at 29-11. This ties him with Tyronn Lue and Pat Riley.

The Thunder lead that series 3-0, so a Game 4 win Monday pushes him to 30-11. He would own the #3 record solo if that happens. As it stands he’s already part of the rarest air in NBA coaching history.

  • Phil Jackson — 30-10 (.750)

  • Tyronn Lue — 29-11 (.725)
  • Mark Daigneault — 29-11 (.725, active)
  • Gregg Popovich — 27-13 (.675)
  • Erik Spoelstra — 23-17 (.575)
