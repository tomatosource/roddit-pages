My Experience as a new player to POE

I bought the game on December 28 and had a really bad Time. Before POE 2 i played Sacred and Diablo, there was Arpg Experience but nothing could prepare me for this Game. All started with the second Boss, that Worm killed me like 30 times until i gave up and leveled in the forest. I killed that Worm and died my way to Ogham Manor. That was where i started hating the Game. Mobs die, blood kills me and mobs respawn at Checkpoint. Uninstalled the Game and forgot about it.

Came Back like 3 weeks ago, got to Geonor and didn’t try one Time. I asked for help and realized the POE Community is Something different, i got Help instantly and pulled up a Guide for my witch. Got through the Normal campaign with Help and even almost got scammed, my helper for doryani tried to sell me stuff worth a couple of augments for my first divine 😂. Cruel felt way easier than Normal, i got to Endgame, farmed currency and rerolled to a Spark sorc. Twink Gear made it easy and now i’m blasting Maps with my 3m DPS archmage Spark.

This Game instantly tells you it’s not Diablo and that’s good, i first gave up and now i have 200 hours in 3 weeks.

TL:DR got my a.. kicked by the Game, hated it, uninstalled. Reinstalled got help by the Community and having now the best time when i boot up POE.

Just my 2 Cents, still hate the Worm 😂
