Ballplayers: DId you play a game called “Bobble” growing up, or a game matching this description?

Growing up in the 90s, my friends and kids we know from out local league would head to the fields and play a game called “Bobble.”

Bobble was a great way to play baseball with as few as four people. We didn’t quite have the Sandlot experience of having 10 dudes show up all the time so we had to make due.

The rules:

  • Pitcher throws batting practice or “lob” pitches.

  • No balls or strikes

  • Any pop fly, fair or foul, caught cleanly, was an out. A fumbled catch, even if it would count as a catch in a game, was a “bobble” and a ghost base runner would be awarded.

  • Any ball that hit the ground, a fielder would go pick up. They’d then throw the ball to the pitcher.

  • The pitcher must keep their foot on the rubber and catch the ball cleanly. If they “bobble” it or are pulled off the rubber, a “ghost runner” was awarded.

    • In the event a fly ball is caught with a ghost runner on, the fielder can throw to the pitcher. A clean catch by the pitcher records a second out, with the lead ghost runner being called out.

  • Ghost runners advance one base at a time unless a home run is hit.

  • 3 outs ends an inning, switch sides

So basically I’m just wondering if this game was widespread or regional or unique to the friends who taught it to me.

And feel free to tell me all about whatever baseball style mini game you and your friends played in the old days.
