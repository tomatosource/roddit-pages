PoE2 Campaign Feedback: It’s Too Long and Boring on Repeats

I gave the PoE2 campaign a fair shot. The first playthrough was decent. It had some cool moments, new areas, and felt fresh. But doing it again after that? Honestly, it feels like a chore.

The campaign is long. Not just in terms of content, but in how slow progress feels. Even when you know what you’re doing, it still drags. It kills the excitement.

For me, ARPGs really start at the endgame. Maps, builds coming together, pushing limits. That’s where the fun is. The campaign just feels like a barrier to the real game. I don’t want to spend ten plus hours slogging through it every time I want to try something new.

It’s frustrating to feel stuck in slow progress when I just want to get to the part of the game that actually keeps me coming back. I hope GGG finds a way to make this better. Either speed it up, offer a skip for alts, or streamline it somehow.

Anyone else feel the same way?
