For the first time in NBA history, three 60+ win teams lost Game 1 of the same playoff round

With the Nuggets’ comeback win over the Thunder, this marks the first time ever that three teams that won 60 or more games in the regular season lost their first game of the same playoff round:

  • Thunder (68–14) lost to the Nuggets
  • Cavaliers (64–18) lost to the Pacers
  • Celtics (61–21) lost to the Knicks

Unreal. These playoffs have been nothing but entertainment.
