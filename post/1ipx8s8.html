Endgame hit difficulty sweet spot for me

I am playing lighting ranger build, and am not great min maxer or someone with huge POE knowledge. But hear me out :)

I am not fan of zooming and was greatly excited with promise that POE2 will be more active and more in direction of “hard but fair” Darksouls games

So first why Ranger really “clicked” for me ( compared to Monk, Summoner witch I endgame earlier ).

Unlike Witch that makes all screen burn, and you can not even see ( or care ) who you are fighting, Or monk that has to kill everything in 1 second - Ranger actually gets to see who they are fighting and react accordingly. Also dodge is super important, Ranger is squishy and one mistake could result in death. This makes Ranger gameplay immensely satisfying and really feels like action game - what I think GGG was going for.

Now about mapping.

- Die and you lose map mechanic is great. It really ups the stakes without being hardcore. It makes you be on your toes entire time
- Monsters matter. As ranger I am very squishy so I have to look every group of monsters cause some can easily one shot me with special abilities
- Limited portals. Really makes you conserve the flasks. Yes I can spam my top abilities but I don’t want to drain mana to fast.

This gameplay results in loop of maps that are difficult. I often die if I get too greedy or make mistake. And this results in fun factor much greater than just zooming mindlessly with zero challenge.

I really hope GGG expands the game with such gameplay in mind. The game should feel challenging, players should be forced to play reactively ( dodge, block, position ).
