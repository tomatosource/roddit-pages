New Laker Adou Thiero on who he models his game after “I watch a lot of defenders. Trying to pick up on what they do in the league, that’s a different level. It’s a lot of different stuff offensive players do in the league, so trying to pick up off of OG Anunoby, Jrue Holiday, he’s a great defender”

Source: https://lakersnation.com/adou-thiero-dream-come-true-to-be-drafted-by-lakers/

Shortly after being drafted by the Lakers, Thiero expressed how happy he was.

“It feels good, dream come true. Just happy to be here,” Thiero said. “Playing for the Lakers too, that’s a blessing for sure.”

Adou Thiero discussed who he models his game after in his interview with Spectrum Sportsnet:

“I watch a lot of defenders. Trying to pick up on what they do in the league, that’s a different level. It’s a lot of different stuff offensive players do in the league, so trying to pick up off of OG Anunoby, Jrue Holiday, he’s a great defender. Just picking up on what they do, watching how they play defense.”

On playing alongside LeBron and Luka:

“It’s definitely crazy because it was like damn, I was just watching them on TV. I was at a couple of Laker games in the playoffs. It’s still crazy to just think about right now. I still haven’t made peace and I don’t think I will anytime soon.”
