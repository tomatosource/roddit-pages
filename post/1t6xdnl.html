If you’ve played the new POE 1 league, you should know what to expect in this new endgame.

The revamp offers something that we had very little of prior to 5.0: goals. And it’s more important that it seems.

In PoE 1 once you finished the campaign you aim for the four corners to get all the void stones and scarab slots so that the “true” grind can actually begin. For many exiles (I’d argue most), the journey to get your build strong enough to accomplish all those goals are the fun part of the endgame.

PoE 1 Atlas

Now in 5.0, you head to the fortress to unlock all the atlas points and then aim for the direction of the league mechanics you enjoy the most and start the quest lines there, only then our maps will be juiced enough for the grind. And how you get strong enough to kill all the bosses will be the challenge of the endgame.

PoE 2 Atlas

The details might be a bit different but the endgame philosophies are finally starting to align between PoE 1 and 2. We are no longer headless chicken roaming aimlessly on the infinite atlas just to get to T15 after the campaign. Instead we now have clear goals at the start of the endgame, the atlas map is no longer meaningless, and that’s an absolute win in my eyes.

As for how the game will feel when you start the grind after finishing all the goals, I’m seeing some similarities between astrolabes and how you open/change certain sections of the map in PoE 2. I guess we’ll see if it’s fun enough to last a month or two.
