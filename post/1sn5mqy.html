The Announcement Before the Announcement

It was the night before the announcement of the announcement,
and every stash tab told lies we believed,
gear with “potential” carefully hoarded,
and rares we swore we’d fix, then never retrieved.

A quad tab of bases, all almost upgrades,
with tiers that looked close, but never quite hit.
Affixes that teased something nearly coherent,
then bricked at the last and we still kept the kit.

Currencies stacked in uneven intentions,
too much of one, and none of the rest.
Enough to begin something overly hopeful,
never enough to finish it best.

But none of it mattered; the rhythm was changing,
a current beneath all the clutter and doubt.
The noise of old leagues grew suddenly distant,
as something more urgent was phasing it out.

For Jonathan’s calm, that measured explaining,
where systems unfold like they always made sense,
and Mark’s sharper edge, that quick spark of humor,
that turns every detail a little more tense.

Not the reveal, not the systems, the numbers,
but hearing it spoken, alive and made real.
The moment the silence gives way to their voices,
and suddenly everything clicks into feel.

And when it arrives, that quiet beginning,
we won’t need the full weight of what’s yet to come,
just one line, one post, one hint of them speaking,
and already the next league has begun.
