What the Hell Am I Doing Wrong with Spellslinger?!

Howdy all,

I’m having a weird issue with Spellslinger and Google/Global Chat haven’t had an answer for me so I figured I’d ask the experts here.

I’ve got Spellslinger bound to one of my mouse buttons, and I can see “charges” building up because 1 and then 2 appear over Spellslinger on my hotbar, but when I go to actually use one of those charges by clicking the mouse button I’ve assigned to Spellslinger while my cursor is over an enemy… Nothing happens. I’ve also tried hovering the mouse button over my own character and over a random patch of ground, and as long as there are enemies on screen, nothing happens when I try to use Spellslinger.

BUT as soon as the last enemy on screen dies, if I then hover my cursor over a random patch of ground and press the Spellslinger hotkey, it activates.

I feel like there must be something I’m misunderstanding or doing wrong, but I can’t for the life of me figure out what it is.

In case it’s relevant, my Spellslinger is slotted with (in order): Living Bomb, Comet, Lifetap, Arbiter’s Ignition, and Boundless Energy II.

Any idea why I can’t seem to use Spellslinger unless there’s nothing on screen to damage with it? Please let me know if I can provide any additional info.

Any help greatly appreciated!

EDITING TO ADD WHAT SOLVED THIS FOR ME IN CASE ANYONE FINDS THIS THREAD IN THE FUTURE

Not sure exactly which of the things I tried did the trick, but it’s finally working correctly! The steps I took were:

  1. Removed everything from the support slots on Spellslinger.

  2. Reequipped (in order) Living Bomb, Lifetap, Arbiter’s Ignition, and Boundless Energy.

  3. Went into a map, built up a bunch of charges of, and used Spellslinger.

  4. Confirmed that Spellslinger was casting Living Bomb by A) Observing total charges tick down on my hotbar after activating skill, and B) Checking Buff bar for Elemental Archon (granted by igniting an enemy using a skill supported by Arbiter’s Ignition).

  5. Equipped Comet again, this time in the final support slot on Spellslinger rather than the second slot.

  6. Repeated step 3 and 4 to confirm it was still working with Comet slotted.
