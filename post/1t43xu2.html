The problems with spells in PoE 2

Outside of the obvious +skill gem level that outpower all other affixes on a staff combined, there are some other problems with spells that I’d want to highlight:

- There are only a few spells in the game that can clear maps: Spark, Arc, ED-Cont are the only ones that you should be using for clear (baseline). All other spells either cannot clear, or need an unique item to help with clear like Reap & Bone Cage (need Trampletoe), Frostbolt (needs snakepit).

- Triggered Comet deals way too much damage comparing to everything else.

- Spells have vastly different crit chance, too much differences and scaling crit is the only way to scale damage now. Fire spells have 5%, Lightning spells have 9%, Cold spells have 13%.

- On top of that, Bloodmage is a bullshit ascendancy with Sunder The Flesh, putting your crit to 15%, meaning you almost triple your damage output with any fire spells if you play Bloodmage. It’s just much, much stronger than any other ascendancies.

- Mana cost is huge, and all good builds have to use life to cast instead. Investing into mana feels bad if you’re not playing Archmage, and after the Archmage nerf, it’s also bad.
