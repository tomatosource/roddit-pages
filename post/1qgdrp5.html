The inflated economy is great for a new(ish) player

I wanted to toss in my experience as a fairly average player this league. Around 500 hours, played a few leagues now, played POE1 for a few leagues, so I’ve got a bit of experience, but still don’t understand a ton of the endgame stuff.

I know the economy is in a weird spot with all the crazy temple stuff, but honestly, I haven’t really messed with temples at all and have been making money just fine. It’s the same as always, farm the currency people use to run endgame content, basically just farm splinters and citadels of any kind you’ll make tons of money. It always scales with the economy so you can always keep up with inflation pretty well.

Personally, I’ve found this league super encouraging to try and learn how to craft because of the inflation, there’s tons of currency around to use up and I’ve been having a ton of fun trying my hand at rolling a high end weapon. I’m normally pretty cautious of this because endgame crafting mats are very expensive, but they seem to be fairly abundant right now.

I’m not making hundreds of divs an hour or anything like that, but I can consistently farm a div or two every map and have been constantly seeing upgrades. If you’ve been wanting to jump in but are worried with the economy posts and videos, I’d say give it a shot, I’ve been having a ton of fun.
