Two big things that most don’t know about Magic’s rookie season finals feats

Magic was amazing in game 6 to help the lakers win the championship, but there are 2 big misconceptions about it

  1. CBS/NBA got voters to change their mind after initially voting for Kareem to be finals MVP because he wasn’t present to receive the trophy. Kareem got injured in game 5. This is something that has been widely documented, with reporters talking about it and the lakers drama series winning time talking about it as well
  2. Magic Johnson didn’t actually play center in the game. Yes he did the tip, but it was actually 6’11 Jim Chones who played 41 minutes at the 5
