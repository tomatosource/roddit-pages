[OC] Basketball Triple Crown Candidates

To achieve a Triple Crown, a player must win an NCAA championship, NBA championship, and the Olympic gold in their careers. Only 8 players have done so: Clyde Lovellette, Bill Russell, K.C. Jones, Jerry Lucas, Quinn Buckner, Magic Johnson, Michael Jordan, and Anthony Davis.

Here are the candidates with 2⁄3 trophies, ranked in order of Triple Crown likelihood:

  1. Christian Braun (NCAA 22, NBA 23). Can Braun ever make an Olympic team? It might be more possible than you think. Last year, he averaged 15 ppg on 66 TS%. If he continues improving his 3 pointer in particular, he would fit well as a role player. He has two shots at age 27 and 31. I’d optimistically give Braun a 20%.
  2. Donte DiVincenzo (NCAA 16⁄18, NBA 21). DDV at his best would fit as a bench shooter, but he’d be happy to make a select team. 1%.
  3. Al Horford (NCAA 06/07, NBA 24). It’s unfortunate Horford hasn’t played in an Olympics for the D.R. 0%.
  4. Collin Gillespie (NCAA 18, NBA 23). 0%

The candidates with 1⁄3 trophies:

  1. Jalen Brunson (NCAA 16⁄18). Easily the best candidate. He should make the 2028 team at age 31, although 2032 is dicey. While gold may be in his future, an NBA ring has proven elusive so far. If you give Brunson an 80% chance of a gold, and a 5% chance of a ring for 6 more seasons, his Triple Crown probability is approximately 20%.
  2. Mikal Bridges (NCAA 16⁄18). His 3&D profile would fit well in an Olympic setting, and he was a starter on Team USA for the 23 FIBA World Cup. He’s just 1 day older than Brunson. I’d say he’s about half as likely to make the 28 squad as JB, so his probability is about 10%.
  3. Josh Hart (NCAA 16). The last of the Villanova crew. Hart also contributed to the 23 World Cup team, but his game is a far more clunkier fit for an Olympic team. He’d be 33 by the time 28 rolls around, and he showed some slippage last season. 4%.
  4. Donovan Clingan (NCAA 23). He’s an intriguing Olympic candidate in the future considering Team USA’s lackluster big man depth. 3%.
  5. DeAndre Hunter (NCAA 19). Another 3&D candidate who’s fairly close to an NBA ring. Doubtful he makes Team USA at age 30. 2.5%.
  6. Stephon Castle (NCAA 24). The ROY deserves a mention, and he played U18 ball for USA. He’s pretty far from a ring or the senior team at this moment. 2%.
  7. Selected NCAA champs: Walter Clayton Jr., Davion Mitchell, Ty Jerome, Grayson Allen.

BONUS:

Luka Doncic: It is ridiculous how decorated his European career is. Doncic won the Next Generation Tournament, 3 Spanish Leagues, 2 Domestic Cups, an Intercontinental Cup (aka the Club World Cup), and a Euroleague for Real Madrid. For Slovenia, he famously won the Eurobasket in 2017. All of this as a teenager.

What are his chances of winning an NBA title? LAL is battling in a stacked West right now, but as veterans age, a re-tooled LAL with a (hopefully) peak Doncic would have a good shot. If he has a 5% chance of a ring for 8 seasons, his probability of winning an NBA title is about 35%.

That would put him alongside Manu Ginobili and Bill Bradley as the only players with a Euroleague and an NBA championship. At this point, with titles at the amateur, pro, and international levels, that would be enough in my mind for a Triple Crown.

Joining Ginobili and Bradley as Olympic gold medalists, however, might be impossible. I can’t see Slovenia winning an Olympic gold during his career. It would be insane if they did.

Summary: the top Triple Crown candidates are Doncic, Braun, Brunson, and Bridges.
