What exactly is it that makes PoE2’s visuals and “game feel” look so much more high quality and polished than its competitors?

I’ve used the ~4 months downtime between 0.2 and 0.3 to give other ARPGs a shot. D4, LE, LA. I’ve even played GD and NRftW for a bit (acronyms because I don’t know if naming other games is allowed). Gameplay-wise, they all have their upsides and downsides, but what really got my attention is the noticeable difference in visual and “game feel” quality PoE2 has above them.

And while budget difference is certainly the main factor, that doesn’t really answer what exactly PoE2/the artists did to achieve this. The dev/artist talks of past video streams (e.g. Exilecon) couldn’t answer it either.

One major example is the ‘floatiness’ that many character movements have in other ARPGs; or RPGs in general. PoE2 movement has a lot more physically to it. At first, I thought that this can be attributed to an exaggerated “slow out” animation when characters stop moving (e.g. clothes having an inertia that keeps them moving for just a few milliseconds longer). However, other games like D4 do this as well and characters there feel a bit less rigid.

So I wonder:

  • Is the lack of floating damage numbers?

  • Does PoE2 use noticeably higher quality textures and meshes where light shaders can bounce off?

  • Is is mainly “just” the sound engineering and not the visuals?

  • Is it the high number of light sources? Seriously, once you notice how many small light sources are in every part of the game you can’t unsee it. No wonder stuff like rigid frost particles from cold skills burn my GPU, lol. It even affects the player UI and health/mana globes!

Of course, the answer is most likely a combination of all of these, but I would like to get a more definitive answer. Maybe there are some artists here who have some insights or guesses how this quality result gets achieved.
