PSA: level 38 arctic armour + trampletoe is strong and fun

I just wanted to get this out on the record somewhere in case GGG nerfs it somehow:

I am currently using a level 38 arctic armour with 34% quality. It has a maximum of 9 stacks.

Combined with trampletoe and +aoe, the level 38 arctic armour one shots entire screens, always. Any rare, any pack, always instant dead the moment one monster hits you. You just walk through maps and everything dies, always.

Endgame bosses lose about 20% of their life each time they hit me. That’s with still only using a level 20 4-socket arctic armour. I think it could do 30%+ of endgame boss life per hit with a level 21 5-socket and q40%+ (see below), very expensive.

The only downside is some monsters won’t melee attack you even if you stand right next to them. I use frost nova + cast on freeze + cold snap for purely ranged monsters. The hardest encounter are purely ranged bosses.

It’s possible to get arctic armour up to 40%+ quality using fox talismans + my build, but very costly. This would give it 10 stacks which is 11% more damage.

I don’t think anybody on the ladder is using level 38 arctic armour: https://poe2.ninja/builds/dawn?skills=Arctic+Armour&items=Trampletoe&weaponmode=Wand+/+Focus

In ten years of Path of Exile, I have never stumbled upon such a powerful combo that you can create with inexpensive gear. Enjoy - I am.
