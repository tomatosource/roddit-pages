Historic Baseball Broadcasts Re-recorded

I have an idea!

You know how there’s a ton of old baseball game radio broadcasts available, but the audio is kind of hard to listen to?

What if a bored father of three used podcast and editing skills to meticulously re-record the broadcast with voice actors and high-fidelity recording techniques, and either before or after added context for the game/season to make it interesting?

I am floating the idea of making this my new project and am wondering if any fans want to hear the first cut, be involved, etc. There’s no money here - I am not selling a product and do not intend to pay anyone involved. Just a labor of love!
