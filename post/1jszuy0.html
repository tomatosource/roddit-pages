GGG needs a Project Manager

Jonathan has said before that they’re all backs to the wall, burning the candle at both ends to make this game good, and after playing this slop of 0.2.0 release, I cannot understand how they manage their time.

Jonathan has also said that he took the most experienced devs from POE1 to work on POE2. Based on the current state of the game, they were either burning daylight, or they had no coordination.

The difference from 0.1.0 to 0.2.0 is abysmal, sure there’s a new class and 3 new ascendancies, but really? 100 uniques, which most of them are the usual 1c uniques we’re used to, and some could be build defining (i haven’t found one yet),

As a project manager myself on my day to day job, I would love to see the schedules of the teams and departments, how they communicate, because let’s be real, the balancing teams don’t really talk to each other, based on the multiple nerfs on the same skill.. it’s like their playing tug of war on who can nerf it in the worst way possible.

What are they prioritizing? Because it sure as hell aint testing the game… ascendancies not working or not being able to ascend, MTX bricking your game, 1FPS gameplay due to a render and so on and so forth.

Taking devs away from one game to ensure that the other game succeeds was the main goal, and it failed, miserably.

The way the game is currently, it shows a poor management of time, resources and talent.

Please GGG, get a proper project manager, don’t let your game directs be your project managers, they just don’t get it.
