Which player’s legacy would be affected the most if today’s rules were retroactively applied?

Was browsing through Basketball Reference earlier and randomly arrived on Kawhi’s page. I knew he was injury prone, but looking through his page, I didn’t realize just how many games he missed. He’s so lucky the NBA didn’t institute their 65 game minimum for awards earlier in his career. If they had, he would’ve lost a legit 4 seasons worth of honors including: DPOY, All-NBA x3, All-Defensive Team x4. He’d still have his 2 Finals MVPs, but the rest of his career would look more borderline.

Also, with the rule changes in regards to All Star Game Starters voting, I think Yao Ming would get hit hard. Combine that with the 65 game rule and he’d automatically lose at least 3 All-NBA teams, and probably a similar number of All Star appearances. He might even lose an additional All-NBA team just from it being position-less now.

From a play style rule change standpoint, I think a lot of on-ball defenders who relied on hand checking would also likely be viewed a bit differently.
