The 1986 76ers hold the record for the most former or future MVPs on a team at one time

There are a 3 teams in nba history who have had 3 former or future mvps on them (2012 thunder, 2004 lakers, 1982-1985 lakers). But only one team has had 4 former or future MVPs on it.

The 1986 76ers had:

Julius Erving (MVP in the NBA in 1981, won 3 MVPs in a row in the ABA)

Moses Malone (MVP in 1979, 1982 and 1983)

Charles Barkley (MVP in 1993)

Bob McAdoo (MVP in 1975)

Each of them are in the twilight of their career (Julius Erving and Bob McAdoo). Early in their career (Charles Barkley) or in the middle of still being really good but not MVP level anymore (Moses Malone).

Either way I thought it was interesting that because Bob McAdoo didn’t resign with the lakers and instead spent his last season in the NBA in Philly, there’s a team that (albeit none in their prime) had 4 mvps at one time in their career all on the same team.
