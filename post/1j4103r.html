Endgame Builds are just travelNodes + Jewels. Is that fine?

It makes sense, Statstackers like travel nodes and good jewels are worth 3 to 5 passive points (or up to 6 with the adorned).

Is that fine? I dont think so, because thinking about builds is uninteresting. So lets all think of solutions:

  1. Make other nodes Stronger (to make them more attractive)

  2. Make travel nodes weaker (the closer to jewels the less stats they give) to make the other nodes gain relative strength

  3. Make jewels more interesting. Jewels have areas like a triangle, and suffixes to rotate that area. Affixes to enhance nearby nodes. Maybe even negative areas to make placement even more interesting.

So what do you think. Not a problem? If not, do you have an idea for a fix?
