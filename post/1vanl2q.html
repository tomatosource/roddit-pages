Celtics as dark horse for Kuminga?

Celtics have an MLE, plus a $27MM trade exception and seem to be able to offer a lot more than other teams who covet him (eg the Lakers), and a better situation than other teams who can pay him (Clippers).

And without Jaylen Brown as the primary rim attacker, Kuminga offers a facsimile of that skill and is still young enough that you could dream on continued growth from him, and maybe even him fitting in to a Mazzulla system that focuses on athleticism and effort. And with the Celtics already having spacing, they wouldn’t have to ask Kuminga to be something he’s not, which maybe was part of the issue in Golden State.

It’s possible the money would not fit and maybe there’s an overrated factor and/or he could be a bad fit with the offensive system, but fresh off the loss of Jaylen Brown I would at least feel like this partially fills the gap.

Edit: I see there’s no realistic way to do this unless it’s a sign and trade and the Celtics abandon their goal of getting under the tax and resetting it for next year. I still think talent wise he would be an intriguing addition but financially there’s probably no way to make it work that wouldn’t involve sending out players you don’t and to send out.
