Hugo/Simons in rotation

Just a couple thoughts about both of them in the past week or so with the differing rotations Joe has had.

  1. Should they consider sending Hugo to Maine, just to get more live reps? He had a DNP today and 5 games with less than 10 minutes played. Obviously as a rookie it’ll take time to develop, but I just wonder why not have him sent to Maine to at least get him more practice vs different schemes instead of minimal minutes.

  2. Simons is a very gifted scorer when he gets hot, but sometimes lacks effort and hustle that Joe takes pride in. I assume that’s why he might have got his minutes cut today. I just wonder that since we’re in a gap year, and Simons is on an expiring contract, wouldn’t we prefer him to get more minutes just to improve his stock? His trade value is not very high right now, and it feels like he’s the only player that Joe takes out immediately when he makes a mistake. Just think that benching a ~20 PPG scorer the last few years won’t help his confidence/shot, and in turn hurts our trade goals
