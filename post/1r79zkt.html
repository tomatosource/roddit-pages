Which of these offseason free agency scenarios involving our roughly 20 mill of cap space do you like most?

Also, feel free to present your own scenarios with the 20 mill we should have this summer based on my projections. This basically ends up being the full MLE and the biannual exception(about 15 mill and 5 mill). If you kind of like one of these but would change part of it, that’s fine too. Just curious to see where the subs priorities lie in regards to the summer(plus no basketball till Thursday so im bored out of my mind)

Scenario 1: Coby white(full MLE), Rob Williams(BAE or vet min)

-for this scenario I’ll say another option is giving vuc the BAE instead of rob, basically it’s prioritizing Coby white with our big money and playing the margins for a big with the idea that the queta/garza/Amari combo is rapidly improving by the year and it would be a waste to spend MLE money on a guy to play over those 3, so get a vet for cheap. I actually really love this scenario and it might be my favorite, I think Coby white would legit turn this offense all time all time great, like I think we’d be ridiculous offensively. He’s just a better anfernee, worse pure shooter but better at scoring everywhere else on the floor and way better passer.

Scenario 2: John Collin’s(full MLE), melton(BAE)

-really like the John Collin’s fit, I think 15 mill is pretty fair money for him and how much flexibility he would offer. I think he’s a safe long term bet because of his ability to play big at the 4 or small at the 5 and really stretch the floor well. Melton is a perfectly serviceable 3&d third guard.

Scenario 3: Quentin Post(10-15 mill), Ivey/sexton/simons(use the other half of that 20 mill minus the post contract), precious achuwa(min)

-this is a sneaky underrated one to me; basically it’s a cheaper version of the snatch and grab RFA big man situation im gonna have for the next scenario. I think post is a legit big who would fit us like a glove, and I wonder if there’s a number where seeing how expensive the warriors are and how they just traded for KP, they’d be willing to pass over him for. Maybe even a sign and trade for a minor asset, regardless of this you bring him in for 10-15 mill and then spend your remaining money on a 2nd tier scoring guard like sexton/simons/ivey/etc, then grab precious achuwa as a buy low 4⁄5 bench guy.

Scenario 4: mark williams/walker Kessler/peyton Watson for the full 15-20 mill, and either a guard or a big at the minimum depending on who is signed from the first group

-this is the most aggressive option of them all. Here you’re almost certainly facilitating a sign and trade of sorts unless somehow you’re able to outright outbid one of these 3 guys. This is a big swing for a real long term piece(mark williams the weakest of that criteria). I think this is my least favorite idea so far, just threw it out there as an option. From the three I think Watson makes the most sense; little more gettable than Kessler, little safer long term than Williams, team he plays for is insanely expensive also and might let him go.

Scenario 5: minor move like simply sign a rookie in the draft, and bring in Vuc or Rob at a low number and maybe a depth guard like melton

- the idea here is simply keep your cap space into the season and weaponize it via trade using the TPE for an okongwu, smith, etc
