There are some systems in PoE2 that I now like more than PoE1

Probably gonna get big hate here, because right now it’s popular to shit on PoE2, but I’m starting to see the thing coming together:

1: Early on I was confused by the lack of Alteration, but with Async now buying bases is good, and picking up bases feel good 2: Crafting is so much better for the poor in PoE2, yes, even now. Slamming to create bases feels good, crating with only omens and leveraging those omens instead of several separate system is so much better 3: Not having added damage to spells: The old system in PoE1 was so convoluted with added damage, and then spells having different effectiveness due to hit rate, etc. While the mods on spell items are pure shit right now, I do understand the idea behind Gain as Extra, Increase Spell Damage, and Levels only. 4: I like tablets more than scarabs. We only need more unique tablets, more weird shit mods on rare tablets and it would be much better than scarabs. 5: Mechanics drop only the thing for that mechanic makes mechanics more unique and feels better to do. 6: Classes have unique skills instead of being generic stats stick. Also Ascendancies 4 is unlocked in t12 maps feels more gradual than PoE 1 where the distance between Asc 2 and Asc 3 is literally 2 acts.

Things I still don’t like:

  • Buy in Atlas tree: Buy the thing and run it to do it better is just wrong. It feels backwards
  • Levels mods on items have too much of the power budget of items, especially for spells.
  • Infusions are pure shit. Spells should have higher base damage, and infusions add some, not Infusion x3 the damage of a spell, and non-infused spell does no damage.
  • Difference in movement speed between each class is too high
