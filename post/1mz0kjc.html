Better “player” vs better “fit”

Yesterday someone asked, “Who is better, Julius Randle or OG Anunoby?” The consensus in the thread was that Randle is the better player, but that most good NBA teams would rather have OG.

So I want to pose the question: how does that make Randle the “better” player? If the ultimate goal of any team is to win a championship, wouldn’t you rather have the player who contributes more meaningfully to a contending team?

Randle is the better volume scorer and rebounder, but ideally he should never be your best player on a championship-caliber team. OG, on the other hand, fits seamlessly on any winning roster.

Put simply: OG is S-tier at what he does, while Randle is more like B-tier at what he does. Doesn’t that make OG the more valuable player if the ultimate goal is winning at the highest level? Randle may be capable of being the first option on a playoff team, but that’s meaningless if the team isn’t good enough to contend. OG would never be mistaken for a first option, so he doesn’t run into that problem.

I’m genuinely curious why most people seem to agree that Randle is the better player in this context.
