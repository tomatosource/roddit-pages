Thunder were 18 regular season wins better than the Pacers, the largest win gap in the Finals since 1981

The Thunder finished the season 68-14 and the Pacers finished 50-32, giving the Thunder an 18-win regular season lead. A disparity this large hasn’t happened since the 1981 Finals when the 62-20 Celtics beat the 40-42 Rockets. The team that won more regular season games has won 69% of the finals, and the team with less has won 25% (3 years the teams had the same record).

The expected win (via Basketball Reference) difference between the Pacers and Thunder is the largest ever at 22.
