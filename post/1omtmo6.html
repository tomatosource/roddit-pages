My ritual precursor tablet farming loop

Hello,

After doing some research, I found the content creators weren’t doing a great job, in my opinion discussing getting ritual tablets versus how to farm them. After experimenting, I’ve been able to go net positive SSF for farming ritual tablets.

Note: I am specced into CARTOGRAPHER STRONGBOXES in the atlas tree. I used grok to check if ritual tablets drop from strongbox. It says no. This is untrue, the “STRANGE TABLET” mod on cartographer strongboxes drops ritual tablets. The rest is just rarity, quantity, pack size, precusor drop rate

For ritual tablets farming, I run wildwood monsters.

Step 1: Disgust farming Here we use a delirium tablet, breach tablet, and irradiated tablet with strongbox chance to obvious reasons. We 3-1 (reforge) all the delirium liquids into disgusts. Keep running maps until you over sustain resources: breach should help waystone over-sustain, delirium should help get a huge stockpile of disgusts, strongboxes will help maintain tablets periodically plus waystone over sustain.

Note: ritual can naturally spawn during this process. You can run some disgusts to try to nail a natural map. The consolation price will be breach and delirium tablets.

Step 2: Cashing in disgusts Because the tablets drop from very few monsters at the ritual, the goal is to make sure they are rare and magic as much as possible. You may choose to run chaotic monsters but I find additional rare mod to be as good or better. I try to use exclusively additional mod maps. I’m assuming desecrated maps can be better.

Run the 90% precursor distills until empty.

My tablets are 1 ritual, plus any tablets which gives more rare or magic monsters.

I don’t drop a precursor tablet every ritual map but over the course of the 10, I will get on average one every other map, not including lucky streaks . But due to the disgusts, I will end up with many tablets in aggregate. Don’t get discouraged if they don’t immediately drop.

Step 3: Reset everything for normal farming once resources are abundant. Loop back to 1 if it changes.
