I wish Support Gems would get disabled instead of entire skills if your weapon swap has less Spirit.

Example:

My crossbow has “10% increased reservation efficiency” on it.

This gives me an extra 20 Spirit with my crossbow.

I would like to be able to run Vitality on that weapon slot, but instead of disabling the support gem that requires the extra 20 spirit, it disables the entire Skill it’s stuck in.

However, if you are lacking stats, the game disables specific support gems in non-spirit skills—not the entire skill itself.

Spirit support gems should behave the same way—as it is now, it forces dead stats, and that sucks. I have absolutely no way to utilize that extra spirit without pausing and manually removing/replacing gems every time my weapon swaps, which is functionally impossible.
