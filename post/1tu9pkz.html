[PSA] Evasion/Deflection in 0.5 is better for everyone, Ghost Shroud change is BIG buff for high Evasion characters.

Armour and Evasion base values were buffed in patch 0.5. This is good for all classes that use Evasion, but Amazon really turns it up to 11. I’d like to quickly explain why:

###Evasion Changes

  • Amazon has a node that gives 0.5x body armour evasion rating, and 2x non-body armour evasion rating. This makes Amazon unusually efficient at stacking pure Evasion (and thus Deflection) on non-body armour, and pure Energy Shield on body armour.
  • My 0.4 Amazon had 32k Evasion (80% evade) and 35k Deflect (86% deflect). In 0.5 this increased to 38.3k Evasion (82% evade) and 41k Deflect (95% deflect, way overcapped)
  • We only need ~27k Deflection rating to hit the 95% cap, thus with so much Evasion I was able to unspec many %evasion –&gt; %Deflect conversion nodes and remove %evasion –&gt; %Deflect suffixes from gear.
  • With some passive tree optimization, I’m at 44.5k evasion, or about 39% more evasion rating than in 0.4.
  • Deflect is now at ~28k, which as mentioned above reduces my chance to fail deflection from 14% to 5%, or ~3x better than before.

###Ghost Shroud Changes

  • Initial responses to the patch notes were that Ghost Shroud was nerfed in 0.5 by losing its instant recovery. In my experience this is incorrect.
  • Old Ghost Shroud was 5% of evasion as ES recovered instantly.
  • New Ghost Shroud is 2% of evasion as ES regenerated if you have been hit recently. Recently means 4 seconds. This actually means you get 8% of evasion total as ES over 4 seconds, and “regenerated” means even if you are hit while you are out of Ghost Shroud stacks, you will continue regenerating from your previous stack if the new hit was in the last 4 seconds since the previous hit.

Thus:

  • Old Ghost Shroud with my old 32k evasion = 1600 instant ES recovery. If I only took 800 damage, the rest of the recovery is wasted.
  • New Ghost Shroud with my new 44.5k evasion = 3560 ES regenerated over 4 seconds. If I get hit again during those 4 seconds and have no Ghost Shroud stacks left, the regeneration keeps topping me off.

Finally:

  • Compressed Duration no longer works with Ghost Shroud, however…
  • Cooldown Recovery I and II do work with Ghost Shroud. CDR II reduces the time between charges to ~7.7 seconds with a lvl 21 Ghost Dance.
