One of the things that really bothers me as a Celtics fan

I became a fan of the Celtics as a young kid from Massachusetts in the early 2000s. While we had a pretty lackluster roster for most of 2000s, it sort of made sense that we didn’t get a lot of media love, even with the team’s storied history and guys like Paul Pierce being a perennial All Star. But as I’ve gotten older and we’ve had more success and players that are going to be sure fire hall of famers, it seems as if the media isnt just forgetting the Celtics, they are blatantly averting their gaze from them.

I constantly see players who only play one side of the floor or are lawyer players (guys who abuse the rules to find success) placed over people like Tatum and Brown who are elite on both sides of the floor and posses more versatile skill sets than the guys getting all the love. I see the media contorting itself to praise every breathe a Lakers player takes but yet they cant even put one of the most storied franchises with the most passionate fan base and 2 of the best players in the league in any of their ads or segments.

Tatum is easily a top 5 player in the league in terms of actual basketball skill. Hes elite at shooting, dribbling, post moves, passing, and defense. And Brown proved this past season hes an MVP level player with a similar level of versatility but then they pick the dude who gets half his points from flopping and plays no defense.

I feel like the league and media both conspire to prevent Celtics success and its pretty irritating. Like looking back at the Finals vs the Warriors where they were playing illegal defense the entire series, Draymond pantsing and tackling JB and Brown getting the foul for it, the ticky tac fouls on Steph putting him at the line while Warriors players were hanging off Celtics the entire time. Same thing with this recent Philly series. Embiid just wrapping arms with Queta the whole time and jumping into defenders when he has getting shutdown.

Idk why this is at the forefront of my mind now. Maybe its because JB got snubbed from first team when he clearly deserved it just so they could put Mr. Cone on Defense Doncic there? Either way, I maintain that Tatum and Brown are both top 10 players in the league, I just wish they got the flowers they clearly deserve.
