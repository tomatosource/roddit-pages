Who do you want round 1?

I keep seeing people acting like the hawks would give us more trouble than the magic, especially after that round 1 series two seasons ago where they took 2 wins.

I don’t see the reasoning behind wanting to play the magic though. Magic always play us tough and I’d have to check but I believe since paolo got there we have at best a .500 record against them. They’re a great defensive team with two star wings. That team composition is always tougher for our team.

Hawks on the other hand are missing jalen johnson and possibly clint capella. They have so-so defense, mostly hinging on dyson daniels getting steals, and it would be easy to switch onto trae for some easy buckets by literally anyone on our squad.

Maybe I’m crazy but I’d rather play the team that challenges us offensively than the one that tires us out early with physical defense that also means a higher injury risk.

Celtics in 5 either way
