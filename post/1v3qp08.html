Edit: Some collected information from the below posts and global.

The connection across all of these is definitely the oath rune, or with spawning in general.

1.) There are cases where mobs will spawn outside of reachable geometry in the map. This can be caused by knockback, effects that spawn entities (ex. Rite of Passage) and nearby area objectives you didn’t open (ex. Rogue Exiles prison, Animal cages or far off unreachable zones).

2.) There are reports of the scorpion increasing the odds of the bug occurring or hugging a remnant decreasing the odds. From what I remember as having it for a tame the Scorpion shoved other mobs AND has knockback type moves. Additionally, hugging a remnant may make your build kill the enemies instantly before this has a chance to occur.

3.) It may be platform, or instance, based but there isn’t enough data on this particular instance.

4.) Sometimes, mobs spawn up to a full screen or teo away due to this rune. With the above knowledge CHECK YOUR MAPS and make sure you path by any objectives within the map that are an enclosed space in order to perhaps avoid this bug.
