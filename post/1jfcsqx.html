Dear GGG, make low tier map farming more popular next patch

Back in PoE 1, people farmed Piety, Dominus, Dried Lake, low tier syndicate, essences etc for years. These lower difficulty contents had decent rewards and made people feel like they are not wasting time. If you are new to the game, if your gear is utter trash, if you are afraid of dying, if you are playing a 2k hp tshot with full rarity, you could’ve stayed in these contents forever and still had a sense of progression.

In PoE 2, everyone gets streamlined into T15s rather quickly. And everyday there are posts blaming game balance, build balance, item balance etc, just because T15 mobs oneshoted yet another inadequate character.

In the current patch, getting to lv100 takes 5 million+ /kills. If that goal is out of reach for most players, what’s the point of funneling everyone into T15s? Why not give T6 breaches good rewards and make casual players happy?

Trials are gated by its roguelike mechanics. And people complain about the the gauntlet, the bird, or picking the wrong debuff all the time. Maybe dumbing down one of the trials to Heist-level difficulty could a good alternative.

Lastly, bots are strong enough to clear the hardest content in the game; and Melting Maelstrom has been mass-printed(scripted) for months now. So I would imagine making easy contents more popular wouldn’t further exacerbate that issue. But that’s just my guess.
