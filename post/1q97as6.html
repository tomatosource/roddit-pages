Crafting Interface

The actual theory behind PoE crafting isn’t all that difficult to understand. Certain affixes can roll, and you’re using various tools to try and narrow down the list of those affixes to make it more likely to roll the one you want.

And yet it’s still treated as an opaque process by most of the player base - just look at the fact that this sub’s rules actually require you to spell out the cracting process you used for any item showcase.

In my view, the barrier to entry isn’t really complexity, but rather the way that all of these mechanics have been spread out across different parts of the game - making it difficult to get a bird’s eye view of how crafting works, so to speak.

This is infinitely worse in PoE 1 of course, but even in PoE 2 the mechanics of crafting are spread out over the various currency, hidden affix lists, the recombinator, the abyss crafter, and a variety of different omens (omens being further complicated by having crafting and non-crafting varieties intermixed).

I think that all of this needs to be more centralized in some sort of UI or universal crafting mechanism - something where a player can see all of the options available to them, to better understand what is and isn’t possible.
