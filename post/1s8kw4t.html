Idea for game mode : Less enemies - but that are more powerful

Without a doubt, most players want zoom-zoom power trip gameplay where everything explodes as they mow down hundreds of enemies on the screen.

But I am also convinced lot of players are looking for more methodical tactical feeling. I am sure the game as it now can offer both - and make both camps happy.

Simply there should be a game mode that reduces numbers of enemies ( especially makes swarms less likely ) - But in same time makes enemies much more dangerous and powerful.

- So we basically have same game but playing distinctly different.
