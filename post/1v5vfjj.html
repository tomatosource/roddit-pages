Brad’s move gets more masterful by the day

I like Jaylen. He was probably my favorite player on the roster. But Brad made a good move. I won’t recap why because it’s been hashed out everywhere if you look and listen.

What’s cool to me is that the Brown trade stuffed VJ Edgecomb. Note that we tried to trade for him before PG. Now, Philly’s chance at a long term, star caliber guard is up in smoke. Why? He’s going to get about half the touches he had last season. That’s a savage dent in experience at this stage in his career. And if Brown wasn’t already going to take the ball out of his hands, you can trust that LeBron will. I respected JB’s ability to play nice with crazy Kyrie when Kyrie was here—he is neither a fool nor above playing nice—but 76ers are the new Clippers. I await the shade and carnage with baited breath. 🍿

I get that people are pissed. I, too, want to see grit rewarded and put on a pedestal. At the same time, we did Isaiah Thomas far more dirty. If anything, we should have traded JB one year ago today to get higher in the draft and help our young studs develop. Guess I have to Stan for Queta, now.

And then there’s the fact that we gave them our car problem. Unless they have a Popovich level coach, that team is going to be a circus spinning into the stands for the next five years. Maybe Edgecomb will sign with us when the time comes.
