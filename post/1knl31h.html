Is Payton Pritchard Ready to Be a Starter?

I love PP. Want more PP. Need PP. Love when I get more PP. I wanna believe that PP has it in him to be a starting guard for a contender, but I know I’m coming at it from a biased, hopeful side. But let me break it down: curry-lite volume shooter, nasty handle, undersized but strong and quick, competitive as fuck, surprisingly effective rebounder, hard-nosed presser and defender. I love watching him play, but I recognize that his game also has limits. His center of gravity is extraordinary, but his finishing and passing can’t help but be affected and disrupted by bigger defenders. And I don’t think he’s shown much of a middy game, like a good runner or floater to use against rim protectors. What concerns me most is the passing. With switching and matchup zoning as prolific as is in modern NBA defenses, I’m earnestly asking: Can PP realistically develop into the kind of two-way playmaker standard for a starting guard in today’s league? Because I have anxiety about transitioning from Jrue Holiday to Pritchard. Holiday is not only a defensive menace with the strength and mobiltiy to play way above his size but also a pretty damn good passer who can shoot and bully smaller guards. Compared to Holiday, Payton Pritchard feels like a big swing away from the Cs defensive identity, and defense wins championships; in fact, it has. Pairing the Jays with two-way guards like Holiday and White is what makes the Celtics such a imperious squad. Does the next Cs championship squad include Payton Pritchard in a starting role, a sixth man, or not at all?
