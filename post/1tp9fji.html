Be Cautious of New applications, extensions,etc.

Greetings POE 2 community,

I’m making this post to remind the community to be cautious of linking your account to new applications, extensions, and software that will be coming to the POE community.

I feel like as a Cybersecurity guy in the industry I should put this out to the POE community.

The AI hype is real and people without development expertise can use Claude /ai to make a website or an extension etc. Citizen developers have more freedom and agency than ever before.

Which in some ways is great because it allows for more community tools and overall improvement for the community.

The down side of this is that security holes/vulnerable code may be used which may put your session tokens at risk if they’re not properly managed by the application.

Before adopting something as omg amazing ask some basic questions:

Does this use my session token? If so, how are they vaulted/leveraged in the application? Where is my data being stored? (Local or cloud) Is my data being sent to an AI model? Are you using version control?
