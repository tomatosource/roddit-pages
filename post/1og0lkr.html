The next championship roster

I think I’m using this season and seeing which players can carve out a roll for themselves on the next championship contending roster.

For the most part we pretty much can include, Tatum, Brown, White, Pritchard being on that roster.

I’ve been saying this for a while, but I think we have to find a way to keep Hauser. Championship rosters need a player like him off the bench and he’s on a great contract. I hope we don’t move him for tax/apron purposes.

I’ve liked what I’ve see from Tillman thus far (granted it’s only 2 game) but if he can take a step up this season AND I think he’s a player that won’t even cost much, I’d like to see him on the roster for a bit longer.

I think only one of Hugo, Baylor and Jordan will be in the next roster build. I like Hugo, I think he has that defensive talent which will get him on the floor and feel like over time his offensive game will improve. I’m over Walsh at this point and tbh don’t have high hopes for Baylor at this point.

So as of right now, that roster is looking like:

  • Tatum
  • Brown
  • White
  • Pritchard
  • Hauser
  • Tillman
  • Hugo

No bigs yet. No idea where those are going to come from. Does KP come back on a cheaper contract but even if he does, he won’t be reliable for the entire season. I like Queta and sure have him in the roster too but would have to be as a backup/bench big role. I think this is who Queta is. Can’t see him drastically improving.

My guess our draft pick in this draft will land somewhere between 12-18 and we HAVE to hit on that pick. Or maybe it becomes a valuable trade asset and we can bring in a very good role player or big that will fit around the Jays and White.

But yeah, overall, I think I’m using this season to see how the role players play rather than our stars.
