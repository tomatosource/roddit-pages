Options for getting under the 2nd apron

I think we all kinda knew this day would come. The new cba really screwed us over (thanks Grant Williams for the parting gift) and now we have to find ways to get under the 2nd apron without sacrificing a chance at a title. I see 2 probable paths and 1 much less likely one and just wanted to see what everyone else thought.

Option 1: Trade Jrue.

The most straightforward path, if we traded Jrue we would be under the 2nd apron by about \(14 million I believe. I don’t see many landing spots for him, but maybe Houston could be one if they decide to trade for KD. They would probably trade FVV to do that and would need a pg. Maybe we could find a way to get a guy like Eason (only making \)5 mil) from them in a larger 3 or 4 team deal.

Option 2: Trade Porzingis and Hauser.

This would leave us with roughly $21 million I believe. If the team feels like Baylor is ready for a larger role, this might make the most sense. He can take Sam’s minutes off the bench. We would obviously have a need at center, but I also think we would get assets back for KP and Sam that might make this option actually more attractive than the Jrue trade.

Option 3: Trade Jaylen.

To be clear, I don’t expect this at all, nor do I want this. By all accounts the new ownership group considers Jaylen to be a franchise player, as do Wyc, Brad, and co. But we all know JT isn’t going anywhere so this would be the only other path to getting under the cap. But I think we can safely disregard this option.
