Yesterday I made a post about players to have hit .400 over an 80-game stretch in a single season. A couple comments pointed out that Boggs and Gwynn hit .400 over 162 game spans broken out over multiple seasons, and it looks like it’s true that only those two were able to accomplish it.

Rod Carew hit .399 from 5/14/1977-5/20/1978, so he just missed the list, although he did hit .401 if we expand that stretch one more game to 5/21/1978 for 163 total games.

What I’d like to look at next is the longest stretches that someone hit .400 and not keep the games played a fixed number. Carew did it for 163 games. Did someone else do it longer? Also, to bat .400 in a single season, a player just needs to qualify with 502 plate appearances in a 162 game season. Of course they don’t actually have to play all 162 games. I’m curious too to see who has batted .400 over a 502 plate appearance stretch.
