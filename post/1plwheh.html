There are too many narrow corridors everywhere.

The campaign and maps are full of them, but the new Vaal temple has a lot of them as well. So many times I’ve gotten stuck in a doorway because mobs are trying to rush towards me the moment they agro. Trying to squeeze through tight hallways, doorways or other small areas where obstacles are constantly blocking free movement for both me and mobs. Some of the mini-boss rooms have no space to fight the boss whatsoever. There will be like a table to his left, a chair to his right, some annoying elevation behind him. Hardly any room to move around. You’re forced to just sort of stand there and burst him down and good luck trying to dodge his attacks (you’ll just run into an obstacle).

It doesn’t help that I rolled a Shield Wall build, but I can’t be the only one whose noticing this annoying level design. Details can make an environment look better but they also hurt gameplay when the zone is too small. My favorite temple rooms to actually play in are the basic sand arena ones where I can actually move around freely.
