Idle Poe? To old or just weird?

I just realized i would love a game with depth of systems and potential of experiments, optimization, synergies, eye candy and loot pr0n of path of exile games, but that would have automated map clearing where you would just spectate your build and possibly some scripted behaviors you could tweak.

I just find the manual, both hands glued to peripherals 100% time grind tiring as hell. I like the game, i like the systems, but after clearing a map or two i’m to tired to continue having fun that way.

More and more im being pulled to the games, where i just tweak stuff and watch how it works while vaping and taking a sips of drink. Mostly spectating the results.

I have tried stuff like autobattlers, but sadly they are all build around having very limited time for thinking and tweaking stuff. And they are quick games with RNG and reset each time. More like a slot machines than nice tweaking experiments material. So its not it.

But to the point: Does anyone of you close to 40s feel the same, or I am just weird?

I’m not sure how would PoE like that look. An idle game? Would you play idle PoE?
