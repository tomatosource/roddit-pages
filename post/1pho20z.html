Move Power Charges/Buffs From Top-Left to Center/Bottom

I’ve been really enjoying Path of Exile 2 so far, but there’s one thing that has been a consistent struggle for me: the current UI layout makes it very hard to track charges and certain buffs during combat.

Right now, power charges and other charge types are stuck in the top-left corner of the screen. Meanwhile, life is in the bottom-left, mana is in the bottom-right, and the full map (TAB) appears in the center. Even if I move my life and mana to the center — which helps a bit — I still find myself constantly having to glance at the top-left corner just to see the status of my charges. This becomes especially problematic for builds that rely on maintaining or consuming charges, like charged staff setups or abilities that heavily interact with charge uptime.

To make it worse, I’m playing on a 27-inch monitor. The bigger the screen, the harder it becomes to quickly look at the corners during intense gameplay. My eyes constantly dart away from the action just to check something that should be visible at a glance.

And just to mention it: I posted about this already back in patch 0.2, hoping UI customization would be revisited. Since nothing seems to have changed yet, I wanted to bring the topic back again as more players share feedback.

My request:
Please consider adding an option to use a more centered, controller-style UI layout while playing with keyboard and mouse. The controller UI is already much easier to read in the heat of combat, and having access to that layout (or something similar) on PC would solve a lot of visibility issues.

Alternatively, another solution would be allowing buffs and charges to be displayed near the center of the screen — for example, above the life/mana or skill bar — similar to how addons like WeakAuras work in WoW. Being able to place important status effects where players naturally focus would massively improve gameplay clarity.

I think giving players more UI customization options would help many of us who struggle with tracking small, peripheral UI elements during fast-paced combat.

Thanks for reading, and I really hope the team considers something like this!
