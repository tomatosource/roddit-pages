Deadly Boss - took me 12 maps to find the beacon

12 maps later

A quick twenty minute adventure is what I thought, but oh boy was I wrong. I took me twelve, yes - TWELVE maps I had to do to find the damn beacon. I thought I might be missing something, asked friends if there’s any sort of indicator, and whether the beacon can spawn two maps away or not. The first couple Deadly Bosses I’ve done were great, did an adjacent map or two, cute experience, but this was a big wakeup call. And of course the boss dropped a worthless Lineage support and literally nothing else of any actual value, because that’s how most of them are, unfortunately.
I didn’t even find the beacon by the way, I finished this last map, there was no boss, no beacon to interact with, (and yes, I did 100% explore every single one of those twelve maps. aware) - there was nothing telling me I finally did “the thing”, at least as far as my blind arse could tell. I was already dooming how the heck is this not it, it’s the last map anywhere near the boss, until I opened my atlas to check and the tornado was just gone. It was indeed the very last map that unlocked it.

Well, what are your experiences with this mechanic? I was honestly loving the addition until today.
