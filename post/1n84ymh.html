Never expected to love POE2’s leveling process this much

Been grinding through POE2’s campaign acts recently and honestly it feels incredible, haven’t had this much fun in ages. I remember when I first got into POE1, the early game and transitional acts were super tedious, both the monster farming and leveling felt like boring repetitive chores. But this time around in patch 0.3.0, the campaign chapters are genuinely interesting.

The most noticeable difference is the improved pacing and combat feel. Before I felt like the campaign was mainly about “doing quests,” where farming was usually a set up to the main storyline, but now it’s different. The monster design and level layouts give you this “combat + exploration” vibe. Especially those random events and environmental interactions, like campfires, mechanical traps, even how monster special abilities combined with the battlefield environments, makes every single fight feel unique when I’m farming.

Plus the monster design this time around is way more interesting than before. Those mobs with new mechanics, like the ones that can teleport or call for backup, give me genuinely challenging combat experiences. The old “run through areas spamming skills” hasn’t given me this kind of rush since forever.

Overall, POE2’s campaign acts brought back that joy of farming for me. Before I’d often rush through progress and ignore the satisfying combat, but now I can actually enjoy every single kill. I feel like this update didn’t just make the game more “fun,” it also lets players appreciate POE2’s depth through these finer details.
