How to farm 1-2m+ gold a map

Map Setup

  • Get a map with abyssal overrun and about 70% increased rare monsters and no Delirium on it. You can also search once with Delirium at 100-110%.
  • Add three Liquid Paranoia for +45% increased gold, bringing it to around 115%.
  • Use three tablets: Either ones with a shrine and increased gold, plus one with two additional rare monsters for breaches and increased gold. Or rare ones with increased gold and rare monsters, or increased gold and quantity (these are expensive and rare, so shrines are recommended).

Skill Tree

  • If using shrines, grab the shrine nodes (these might be good anyway).
  • You can also grab increased explicit modifiers if skipping shrines.
  • Shrines are great because the Greed Shrine has a chance to give more gold and convert drops.
  • For breaches, take nodes for more monsters; everything else doesn’t matter much.
  • For the rest, quantity, rare monsters, and effect nodes.

Gear

You need as much quantity of gold dropped from monsters as possible on gear, as well as rarity:

  • Gloves: 75% from essence and implicit.
  • Belt: 44% from as socketed as though it was gloves.
  • Rings: 18% per ring slot using Perandus Seal (corrupted ideally; you can get 3 with Ritualist; 16-17% is also fine).
  • Add rarity on helmet, gloves (if possible), etc. It’s hard to fit due to resists, but prioritize getting to like 100-150 since maps wont have any.

Running the Map

  • Use Cleansed Zones with increased gold, or normal ones if you don’t have any Cleansed Areas. Even normal maps work.
  • In the map, run and try to find the shrines without killing too many monsters (especially rares if possible).
  • There will be 2-3 shrines per map. If there’s no Greed or Covetous Shrine, just kill the rest of the monsters.

Good luck!
