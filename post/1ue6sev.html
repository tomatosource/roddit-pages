Since getting a Mageblood, I feel invincible

So yesterday I bought a Mageblood with double Quicksilver, Silver and Diamond and I have gone through lvl 95-97 in a couple of hours.

My Falling Thunder/Flicker Monk feels invincible (died once to combo of ignited ground and poison) but other than that fully juiced T15 maps feel like campaign.

So it is not some ultra defense Mageblood but I bought it so that any future char can use it with skill speed, movement speed and crit chance.

I zoom around the map 1-shotting everything with Falling Thunder, then 1 shot with Staggering Palm get my 6 power charges and repeat.

I am playing the game since release, and I have never felt so powerful. Even my Spark Pathfinder and Spark Witch that dealt millions of comet DMG didn’t feel nearly as good as a char that has 50k DMG on tooltip for Falling Thunder.

My only Uber item on Monk is Mageblood. Quarterstaff is mid ~5 div, armor ~1 div, everything else is maybe combined 10-15 div.
