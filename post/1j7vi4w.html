Who do we want Cleveland to play?

I’d ask this as “who do WE want to play,” but I think the answer is obvious.

There’s a huge gap between each of the top 3 seeds, so they’re pretty much locked into place barring something crazy.

There’s a logjam at 4-5-6 (MIL, IND, DET), but as the 2-seed, we wouldn’t face any of these teams in the first two rounds unless one of them upsets the Knicks.

Then after another very wide gap, there’s another logjam at 7-8-9 (ATL-ORL-MIA).

The Bulls are fairly close to being locked in at 10 (they’re 3.5 games below the 9 seed and everyone behind them is either actively tanking or simply terrible), and we likely won’t face them either, since a 10-seed cannot face a 2-seed even if they survive the playin tournament.

So given that our very likely potential opponents are the Hawks, Magic and Heat, there’s not much drama there: Hawks have our number. Healthy Magic are pesky. Butler-less Heat are garbage. Door Number 3, Monty. And then I guess we just root for the Knicks to beat whoever so that they can face us. Easy.

--

So let’s talk about Cleveland’s path. I know, I know. “Who cares, as long as we’re better than they are?” We may be, but I’d rather face them when they’re tired than when they’re fresh. “Worry about ourselves first.” I just did in the last paragraph. Besides, I’m not a Celtics player, I’m a random fan, so this post isn’t going to affect their concentration. Let’s go.

I’m assuming the Hawks are also CLE’s toughest first-round matchup of the 3, having beaten them twice. (!) Do the Magic give the Cavs any difficulty at all? At least extend them to a Game 5?

Then there are those 3 teams in the middle: the Bucks, Pacers and Pistons. The Pacers are a fast, long team who made it to the conference final last year and should have won at least 2 games. The Pistons look very hungry, play hard, are deep, and have a legitimate top 10 player on their roster. The Bucks have all of the same flaws they always have, but they also have two players that CAN get 40 on any given night.

Since after Wednesday night, the Celtics are basically going to play 16 irrelevant games, we should probably start thinking about something fun, something other than the Lakers and Warriors meeting in the first round and deciding that the proper hype quotient for the matchup is that it gets played on an epic tricked-out party barge 18 miles off the coast of Montevideo, which then sinks. Let’s instead start picking a team to root for in the second round against the 69-win Cavs. Who can give them some probllems?
