Mark Cuban states it plainly, regarding the Adelson family: “I regret who I sold to.”

The previous Mavericks majority owner made his opinions very clear. He knows that there are a lot of things that could have been better during his tenure. One of it was hiring Nico Harrison. However, this is the first time that Cuban has outlined disappointment towards the Adelson family and how they are running the Flagg-led team.

He got real on why that regret has lingered for about two years, via Intersections The Podcast.

“I do not regret selling. I regret who I sold to. I made a lot of mistakes in the process, and I’ll leave it at that,” he declared.
