I analyzed all 5,416 NBA player names since 1946 (character lengths, most common names, and a Michael Jordan affect). Graphs in post.

So I decided to pull every NBA player name from Basketball-Reference.

Here is the first and last name length distribution

The longest first name is 15 characters:

Olivier-Maxence (Prosper)

Names are getting longer over time.

Here are name lengths by decade

On average, names were shortest during the 1950s and longest during the 2010s.

Full name lengths hold pretty steady from the 1940s through the 1990s, then start creeping up in both median and spread from the 2000s onward, with a lot more long-name outliers in recent decades.

John is the most common first name of all time. Williams is the most common last name.

The Michael Jordan effect?

There have been 23 players with the first name Jordan in NBA history. All of them debuted after Michael Jordan entered the NBA in 1984. And none debuted before 2000.
