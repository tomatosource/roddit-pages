The Phillies’ eight runs with two outs in the ninth inning is tied for the second most scored by a team that was trailing prior to the rally.

The record is nine runs scored by the Indians against the Yankees on August 4, 1929, who were losing 5-6 when the rally started.

Three other teams have scored nine runs with 2 outs in the ninth, but they all already held the lead.
