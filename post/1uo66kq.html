4 PG Trades for the Celtics

Im sitting out ofnthe trampoline park because I hurt my back yesterday, so I decided to workshop some PG trade possibilities and give my reasoning. I wont go into exactly which 1sts or how many picks exactly, as thats going to get picked apart. Im really just trying to provide the bones and motivation of these teams who may deal.

&nbsp;

Paul George to Golden State for Jimmy Butler and future 1st

Reasoning: Golden State needs to make good use of Steph Curry’s twilight, and this PG contract is only for 2 years. They absolutely can’t sit on Jimmy Butler’s dead weight contract, followed by rehabbing the rest of the season. AD is their preferred choice, but it looks like the Wizards have plans and dont intend on sitting on dead money this season either. There is a lot of pressure on GS to make this season count, and they could give up a solid pick 3 drafts from now, where they no longer have Steph.

&nbsp;

Paul George to Sacramento for Zach LaVine/DeRozan + Hunter/Monk + filler and future 1st

Reasoning: Here is the weird thing when breaking down the Kings, they can’t operate like a lot of other teams. They have to either overpay for 2nd tier talent, or trade for their roster upgrades. They arent a free agent destination even when they have capspace. This means PG13’s PO isnt as big of a deal to them, because they weren’t going to do shit with the cap space and they know it. They have Sabonis as a vet and Acuff who they’re clearly giving the keys to the franchise to. They need to get rid of malcontents like LaVine who might not compliment Acuff. Instead of LaVine, PG could be Sacramento’s get out of jail free card for DeRozan’s essentially dead contract.

&nbsp;

Paul George to Houston for Fred VanVleet + filler + picks

Reasoning: Houston currently have KD and it looks like they’re ready to run it back with his snarky ass undermining the locker room. Not the best plan imo. This puts them in obvious win now mode. Houston needs a PG + shooting. Paul George is often called PG, and hes a good shooter. Jokes aside, George can still pass better than most F’s, and is a great shooter. Houston has a potential dead weight contract in Fred VanVleet, as he absolutely destroyed his knee last year. Hes a 32 year old undersized guard with a bad leg, its unlikely he comes back this year and fixes Houston. As for the filler, Steve Adams and Clint Capela are both on the roster. This guts Houston’s big man depth, but neither of these guys can play next to Sengun and have a functional offense. We might send Garza back, or find a 3rd team to send Adams/Capela to who need a C.

&nbsp;

Paul George + every asset not named Tatum to Denver for Nikola Jokic + Filler

Reasoning: Paul George for Jokic pretty much exatly as it sounds. They get all the picks we can offer, and any/all of the young guys. If they ask us to flip PP to a 3rd team for another 1st we do it. Luckily, its unlikely theyre interested in Derrick White, Sam Hauser, Queta, or Robinson. If they want us to use Robinson/Hauser to eat Aaron Gordon’s/Christian Braun’s multi year deals we do that too. We blow our load here. This obviously doesnt happen unless Jokic expresses its time to move. Could be a year before something like this formulates.
