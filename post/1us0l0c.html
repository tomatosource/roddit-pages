Hunting Companions is both fun an annoying

Finding a beast with great mods and low reservation is a great feeling but man is it an annoying task.

The constant need to pause the game and search for the mobs you can barely see because of everything else that is going on. That is if you even have time to pause before the monkey slaps them to the next dimension. Or you reset low level zones over and over, that’s bad gameplay tbh. I’ve found a few good ones from Essences and it would be really cool if we could add two options to the Atlas multi choice nodes for essences that increases or makes every essence a beast. That or maybe a new currency that can be used to extract a random mod from a beast destroying the gem that you can then use to craft on another gem. But I dunno how I feel about adding a currency that is only for one skill gem, it feels wonky. Or maybe when they add more atlas masters Hilda can be dedicated to hunting beast and have her nodes give more control over what beast/mods can roll on them.
