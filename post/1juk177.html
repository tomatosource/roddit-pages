NBA Ownership More Impulsive than NFL Owners?

Might be some recency bias, but it feels like NBA ownership is way more impulsive than NFL ownership. You’ve got guys like Mike Tomlin in the NFL who can go years without a playoff win and still have full support. Meanwhile, in the NBA, you’ll see star players getting traded — players on the level of Luka — over relatively short-term frustrations. I can’t imagine an NFL team trading a young franchise quarterback in his prime the way NBA teams move superstar talent. Ownership styles just seem very different between the two leagues. Valid take, or am I completely off base? Maybe it also depends team to team
