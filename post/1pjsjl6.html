If you have orders stuck in the Currency Exchange from Hardcore, but no longer have a character high enough level to access the Exchange, you can visit someone else’s hideout and use their Exchange to cancel the orders.

FYI, if you have orders stuck in the Currency Exchange from Hardcore, but no longer have a character high enough level to access the Exchange, you can visit someone else’s hideout and use their Exchange to cancel the orders.

You should be able to either group with someone and go to their hideout or just use the trade website and click ‘Travel to Hideout’ on any trade (no need to have the currency or actually intend on buying the item).

I had a hard time finding out how to cancel some older trades I had, and stumbled on this while trying to level another character in hardcore past Act 3, so passing the info along in case anyone else was in the same situation.
