Give me your strategy for theorycrafting your own builds!

For all the theorycrafting / homebrew build enjoyers, I thought it’d be fun to compile everyone’s approach for creating a build for any class, ascendancy, skill or combination of skills, etc.

I’m still fairly new to this but my approach is:

  1. Find a skill I find very appealing. Something I want to scale to millions of dps and use for hundreds of hours in the current league

  2. Study the skill description. Where does its main damage come from? How do I scale that damage? What is the biggest multiplier for scaling it? Is it attack speed? Movement speed? elemental damage? Maybe it scales with a defensive stat somehow (e.g. life)?

  3. Think about an ascendancy / ascendancy points I like that would align with whatever scales this skill, while providing good defensive layers. For example blood mage can make almost any skill scale damage with max life in 0.3.

  4. Defensive layers for the class / ascendancy. Does it gel well with my chosen skill? What are some of the best notables and keystone passives near its starting point that I can path to and how do they impact my skill?

That’s about what I do, and I’m still very much a noob. Some of the steps I did not list because I still feel overwhelmed by it frankly is choosing / thinking about support gems and uniques that could take my idea to the next level. There is just an overwhelming amount of both so for this I typically succumb to just looking at Poe ninja profiles of people scaling the same skill.

That’s it! Share your own style !
