This game has a recovery problem

Hello everyone.

Youtuber Dreamcore has made an excellent video today, going over the defensive meta in 0.5 ( https://youtu.be/vaIsy-78Qmw?si=YMAdXrgYhf038z2O ). In it he goes into a bit of a rant about how OP energy shield is compared to life, even after the “nerfs” to ES that we got. And while I think he makes many good points, imo he’s missing one of the most important points to why pure life builds are so unpopular right now, and that is recovery.

Energy shield recovery was directly nerfed this patch, but as Dreamcore correctly says, it mostly made it more annoying, not actually worse.

So what options for recovery do you have in PoE2?

Leech

Well, leech was very directly nerfed in 0.5, and frankly, it’s in an absolutely atrocious state right now. It is straight up impossible to sustain your lifepool via leech on a build that gets into the thick of things in endgame juiced maps. Prior to 0.5 it was absolutely possible, through avenues like instant leech, and multiple leech instances stacking together. Instant leech was completely removed from the game, and also we can only have one leech instance affecting us at any time now. That is simply not enough. It takes AGES for that one leech instances to fill up the HP pool of an endgame character.

Regeneration

The access to life regen in poe2 is SEVERELY limited, to the point where it’s basically unviable to rely on it as a recovery layer. It’s a cool idea to heal over time constantly, but in practice it just doesn’t work that way. Even with a character heavily invested into regen, it can take like 10 seconds to fill up the life pool. That might not sound like much, but in this game you often get hit like 20 times per second in a juiced map. What is the idea here? Say a hit takes 75% of the characters HP. Is that character just supposed to jump back out of the battle and run away for 10 seconds straight, while waiting for their regen to heal them back up? That is often impossible, because in juicy maps, enemies come at you from every direction, so you straight up don’t have the space to run away and wait often enough. And even IF you can do that…that is just really unfun.

Flat regen rolls on gear are also laughably low, with a T1 regen roll on a helmet providing 23 life per second for example.

Life flasks

Flasks are simply not good enough in PoE2 in my opinion. Instant flasks heal way too little, non-instant flasks take too long. Same problem as with regen above. You could jump out of harms way, hide for like 10 seconds, while waiting for your flask to heal you up. But who actually wants to do that in an ARPG?

Life on hit

This stat is a straight up joke in this game. A t1 life on hit roll on a 2h weapon gives you 5 life on hit. That’s nothing!

Yeah it might sustain you in a big pack of white monsters, where you hit 200 times all at once. But those are not the situations you need healing in. You need it against bosses and dangerous rares. Even skills that hit extremely fast like Tempest Flurry don’t heal you up in those situations. This stat is a straight up joke.

Life on kill

Yeah this is a pretty good one. Against trash monsters. As stated above, that is not the dangerous part of the game. Life on kill is very, very good while leveling, and for lower juice content. But it straight up does nothing in the actually dangerous situations in this game.

So yeah, basically all of these layers range from mediocre to straight up non-functional. The leech situation is especially dire in my opinion. The nerf it got in 0.5 was total overkill. And it didn’t even kill the obnoxious coc comet setups it was targeting. They just found other workarounds.

Speaking of which:

Energy shield

Compared to life, this resource (and mana below) is extremely easy to recover. Which is super backwards in my opinion. You can get so much more of them, so the drawback SHOULD be the recovery aspect. But it’s not.

Energy Shield recharge rate has been nerfed, yes. So did Ghost Dance. But especially when coupled with Evasion those still tower leagues above anything that life builds have access to.

Mana

MoM is as popular as it never was before, and it’s clear to see why. Mana regen is what life regen wishes it was. You can easily scale your mana pool to be upwards of 5000, and then get an absurd amount of regen, both from the tree and from gear.

Remnants make this even stronger, providing instant recovery for mana, including overflow.

Avoidance

This is only tangentially relevant to the recovery topic, but it’s very relevant to how the game is played. With recovery options being as limited as they are right now, many players just opt to play builds that don’t get hit in the first place. If you don’t lose life, you don’t have to recover it. This disproportionately favors Evasion builds and ranged builds. Preferably both at the same time. You just stack enough eHP to not get oneshot, and then make sure you don’t get hit basically ever. A pseudo-glasscannon playstyle.

While this is a valid way to play the game, I don’t feel like it should be this dominant. Hitting and getting hit are both aspects of combat, and should be considered in equal measure. But PoE2 currently doesn’t reward that balance very well.

So what is the solution here? Frankly I don’t know. I don’t want GGG to just put instant leech back the way it was. This leads to players recovering their entire health pool in a single server tick. We’ve seen that both in poe1 and poe2. This is not healthy for the game. But just not being able to recover life at all is also not great. I’d like to see leech buffed back at least a little bit, alongside some buffs to regen and endgame life flasks for a start.

Oh, and some avenues to scale the lifepool in general of course. Aside from just stacking strength…

I’m curious to hear what other people have to say on the matter.

Thanks for reading
