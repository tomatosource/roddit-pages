Leech is Bad and Probably Bugged

Lets say you do 40000 pure physical damage on hit, and have 8% leech as physical to leech 3200 HP. That is fine. Now you add an heart of the well to add 15% extra lightning and 15% extra cold damage. You will deal 40000 physical damage, 6000 lightning damage and 6000 cold damage, for a total of 52000 total damage. There is however a 40000 damage cap on leech. It sounds like nothing should change but this isn’t how it’s currently working. Your damage is now only 76.9% physical. This means instead of leeching off of a 40000 hit you are now leeching off of a 30769 damage hit. By adding damage you are lowering your leech to 2461 HP. This becomes worse the more non-physical your damage becomes, even if you are dealing 40,000 physical damage. Please note that this has no effect on blood mage, the worse offender of leech and the main reason leech got nerfed in the first place as it will leech from all damage types. This does not seem to be intentional.
