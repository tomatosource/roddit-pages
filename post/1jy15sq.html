Opinion: Risacher and Sarr will be very good all star nba players

These two french rookies were drafted #1 and #2. Everyone is saying it was a weak 2024 draft. That these two will be just in bottom lottery picks in recent previous stronger drafts. Now, they might even lose to Castle for ROY award this season.

But imo, they will be very good all star nba players.

Risacher showed some high scoring games like 38 and 36 pts outburst.

Sarr had a 34 pts career high games against no other than Jokic.

Risacher is a Solid 6’8 Combo Forward who seemed to be a Furure elite scorer.

Sarr, on the other hand, is a legit Mobile 7 Footer who has a deadly Outside Shooting ability. I think his 3 pt shooting will be his ultimate weapon in Nba for the future.

France already produced Wemby this decade. It is scary that they might have produce 2 players that are at least all star level players this decade.

What do you think of the two?
