Thought on the defenses problem

I’ve been thinking about the armor vs es vs evasion issue and had a thought.

What if instead of armor v es v evasion, it’s instead es v life and armor v evasion? If evasion and armor had access to either life OR es both would be fine. The issue is that without a bigger battery for the larger hits, they end up getting one shot too often. So divide the passive tree a little differently.

https://preview.redd.it/0yzu5mqabvxe1.png?width=914&format=png&auto=webp&s=d8086ca74a736770e027eecb56c5aa6d3fd3e903

We already see evasion/es builds as pretty tanky, if you gave armor a similar choice between cross building both armor/es or armor/life a lot of the issues with armor stop being as prevalent.

Would have to be a bit careful so that you couldn’t comfortably spec into three. A build with a ton of life/x/es would probably be too much, so would need to maintain pressure to take enough dps nodes. But you could also argue that would give people a way of building tanks if they wanted to.

Anyway, off hand thought. Figured I’d put it out there in the world and see if I’m being crazy.
