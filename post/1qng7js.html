Justin Russo on the social media platform also said

The best record any team who started 6-21 or worse through 27 games (of which there’s been 116 teams to do that before this season) has ever achieved is 36-46. No team who started 6-21 or worse has clawed their way back to .500 at any point during that same season.

The closest turnaround in recent memory was the 2017 heat who started 11-30 and stormed back to finish the season 41-41

So it has been a historic turnaround for this team this season
