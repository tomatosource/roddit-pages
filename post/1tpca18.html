If you’re looking at people running Twisters and see them getting to T15 maps in sub 5 hours, don’t expect an insanely OP build for YOU… it’s good because they have TONS of practice.

These guys have practiced the absolute shit out of the campaign for weeks or months some non stop. It’s not just about the campaign though, it’s knowing your build.

As an example, I am a pretty good PoE1 player, KF totem campaign was 4.5 hours and I was able to kill 7⁄10 Ubers in SSF on day 2.5, but that’s because I had done a week of practice runs before league start. But if I had started a build I had never played before with zero practice and started on league start, it wouldn’t have been nearly as smooth/fast.

Even zizaran said in one of the posts here that people are going to basically have a rude awakening starting twisters.

Personally I am either going Martial Artist or Spell totems and I still think I am going to have a hard time because I haven’t played either of those builds in poe2.
