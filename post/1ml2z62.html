Lauri Markkanen against Belgium: 48 PTS │ 9 REB │ 3 STL │ 17-24 FG │ 7-13 3P

Source: https://www.fiba.basketball/en/events/fiba-eurobasket-2025/news/unstoppable-markkanen-errupts-for-career-high-48-points

Playing his first game for Finland since the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2023, the Utah Jazz superstar set a new national team career-high after going 17-of-24 from the field, including 7-of-13 from range, while also adding 9 rebounds, 3 steals and a block in 25 minutes of action.

The 48-point performance set a new Finnish national team record, breaking his own previous best of 43 against Croatia at FIBA EuroBasket 2022.
