Lauri Markkanen against Belgium: 48 PTS │ 9 REB │ 3 STL │ 17-24 FG │ 7-13 3P

Source: https://www.fiba.basketball/en/events/fiba-eurobasket-2025/news/unstoppable-markkanen-errupts-for-career-high-48-points
