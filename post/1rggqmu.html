Just got the game three days ago

After hesitating for a long time because I never got into the first one I jumped the gun three days agoy got the game and I have to say, I am glad I did! Started out as Druid, had a bit of a hard time against some bosses in the first act ( although now I think it was more me not knowing what to do character and gearing wise ). Started again as Mercenary and I have to say I have a blast! Bosses seem to be way more manageable ( dont know if its just me getting better or a difference in class power) and running around blasting things with the equivalent of a shotgun is just plain awesome. Graphics are phenomenal for an Arpg as well as the art direction. But one of the biggest things that makes it fun for me is the way they changed the skillsystem in comparison to the first part. Aa someone who excusivly plays ssf, getting equipment with the right amount of sockets in the right colours was way more tedious than fun. It seems to me with the new system you can experiment way more. I am still early in the game, but I am already looking forward to trying out other classes and see how far I can get into the endgame.
