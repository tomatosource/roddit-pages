How long has each team been without a good player at a specific position?

On the Gio Urshela retirement post, I saw someone comment that he was the Yankees best 3B since A-Rod, which surprised me. It got me thinking, what’s each team’s longest active gap of being without a good player at a specific position? I was extremely bored at work so I researched it. I decided to make the cutoff of “good player” at a round 3.0 rWAR, and my “research” was done simply by browsing the bbref page for year-by-year starters at each position - so if there was a player who amassed 3 WAR, but didn’t get the most starts at any one position on their team that year, they didn’t show up on that list, but I was not browsing through every team and every season to manually hunt for those guys, so i probably missed someone. Sorry. Deal with it. 

Tier 1: The Annoyingly Well-Rounded Teams

*These teams and their spoiled fanbases haven’t had any recent significant gaps in productivity at any one position. They have no idea how good they have it.* 

Cardinals - LF - their last 3 WAR player was Tyler O’Neill in 2021. 

Blue Jays - 2B - Marcus Semien in 2021

Rays - C & 2B & CF - a 3-for-1 deal, with Mike Zunino, Brandon Lowe, and Kevin Kiermaier all in 2021

Cubs - these sick fucks had 3+ WAR at EVERY SINGLE POSITION in 2025. 

Tier 2: It Hasn’t Even Been That Long, Stop Complaining

*These teams haven’t had to wait more than a decade to have a good player at every position. Yes, they’ve had some gaps, but they haven’t been bad, it’s not worth getting all riled up over.* 

Braves - LF - Ronald Acuna in 2018

Brewers - 1B & CF - Jesus Aguilar and Lorenzo Cain, both in 2018

Diamondbacks - LF - David Peralta in 2018

Nationals - C - Wilson Ramos in 2016

Twins - 1B - Joe Mauer in 2017

Athletics - CF - Ramon Laureano in 2019

Mariners - 2B & DH - Robinson Cano & Nelson Cruz in 2018 (a pair of PED guys….really makes you think)

Tier 3: Damn, It’s Been A Minute

*These teams have had to wait quite some time without ever seeing a good player at a given position. It’s tough. They’re suffering. But it could be worse.* 

Phillies - 3B - Placid Polanco in 2010. They’ve only even had one 2-WAR season since then, Alec Bohm in 2024. And with the current state of their depth at that position, it could easily be another 10 years.

Reds - RF - Jay Bruce in 2013. 

Dodgers - 2B - Dee Strange-Gordon in 2014. What a failure of a franchise. (Also, another PED guy…)

Padres - C - Nick Hundley in 2011

Giants - LF & CF - Melky Cabrera & Angel Pagan in 2012 (yet ANOTHER PED guy…..it’s making me think!!!)

Orioles - DH - Nelson Cruz in 2014 (yeah, a repeat, but cmon, it’s getting weird now)

Yankees - 1B - Mark Teixeira in 2015 (not a PED guy, but now a weird wannabe far-right politician, so the moral dubiousness of this list continues to grow)

Red Sox - C - Victor Martinez in 2010 - to their credit though, they’ve had a lot of 2 WAR seasons from catchers since then.

Guardians - CF & RF - Michael Brantley and Shin-Soo Choo in 2012

Tigers - SS & CF - Jhonny Peralta (HMMMMMM) and Austin Jackson in 2013 - although Kevin McGonigle is highly likely to break the streak for SS this year, already at 2.5

White Sox - DH - Jim Thome in 2007

Royals - DH - Billy Butler in 2012

Angels - C - Mike Napoli in 2009

Astros - CF - Michael Bourn in 2010

Rangers - DH - Milton Bradley in 2008. They almost made it to the top tier of this list, as every other position has seen a 3+ WAR season in the past five years, except for DH, which has been empty for seventeen seasons now.

Tier 4: I Was There, Gandalf, 3000 Years Ago…

These teams are the teams of true black holes. Gaping chasms in their franchise histories. Positions that may as well be unmanned, where it’s been so long that children have been born and grew up and bore their own children before their favorite team could find a halfway decent third baseman or something. A stain on the sport. I sure hope *my* favorite team isn’t on here.

Mets - C - Mike Piazza in 2002. They’ve only had two 2-WAR seasons since then - Wilson Ramos in 2019, and Paul Lo Duca in 2006. Save us, Francisco Alvarez, you’re our only hope.

Marlins - 1B - the last (and so far, only) 3 WAR first baseman in Marlins history was Derrek Lee in 2002.

Rockies - RF - it hasn’t been since 2003 Larry Walker that the Rockies have had anyone halfway decent patrolling the cavernous right field corner in Coors.

Pirates - 1B - the kings of this list, the Pirates, have not had a good first baseman since all the way back in 1999, when Kevin Young had a 5.6 WAR season. However, they could technically be on this part of the list twice, as they also haven’t had a good right fielder since Reggie Sanders in 2003. Unsurprisingly, it is tough to be a Pirates fan.
