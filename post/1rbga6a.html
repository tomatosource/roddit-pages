The Celtics are the last team eligible for “Phil Jackson’s Contender Criteria” Threshold

With the Spurs win against the Kings, the Celtics are the final team eligible for Phil Jackson’s Contender Criteria (40 wins before 20 losses). The team would have to win all of its next 4 games, which are against the Lakers, Suns, Nuggets and Nets, respectively. I know the criteria isn’t the best indicator, but I think it would be hilarious if we made the cut.
