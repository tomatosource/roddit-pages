Predicting the MLB Postseason based on tarot cards

I was advised to make this a separate post by commenters, so here it is. As an obligatory warning this is not scientific, it’s just for fun. Without further ado, here are what the mystical energies of the cards told me.

National League West - Reversed Sun

This is a bad omen, one which will be massively disappointing for a large group of baseball fans. Unfortunately, I suspect the Los Angeles Dodgers will ultimately win, but not only that- I suspect that no National League West team will clinch a Wild Card spot. This will be very sad for the Padres, Diamondbacks, Giants and Rockies, so I wish to extend sympathies.

National League Central - Upright Knight of Pentacles

This reading suggests that a team that’s fallen on hard times will manage to get a division win and playoff spot through sheer determination and a willingness to persevere, and although this sounds hard to believe, I think that might be the Pittsburgh Pirates for the first time ever. Looks like Okamoto, Skenes and O’Hearn put in the work!

National League East - Reversed Ace of Cups

This doesn’t look like a good omen for the Mets- in fact, it looks sluggish for the NL East as a whole. A repeat of last year’s Philadelphia Phillies division victory seems likely, much to the disappointment of all other teams involved.

American League West - Upright Eight of Wands

This is a good card for me in particular, since the Seattle Mariners completed their victory over the division by the skin of their teeth in 2025. I could conceive that they could snatch the playoff spot this time again- though it might not be as conclusive as last time.

American League Central - Upright Ten of Swords

I sincerely apologize to Detroit Tigers fans in the chat, this is a bad portent for them- in fact, this could be a major collapse compared to previous years. I’m going to guess that the Guardians don’t manage it either because of the ill omen of failure, so the Kansas City Royals seem to have won their division again.

American League East - Reversed Page of Wands

This is a major shakeup- the already competitive America League East seems to have produced an unexpected winner. This might actually be a Red Sox victory for Boston- but don’t celebrate too quickly, this looks thinner than they might like.

Wild Card Slots - Reversed Two of Wands and Reversed Chariot

The Reversed Two of Wands suggests a sort of stagnation, one which will keep certain things the same. As such, I suspect the American League is going to look pretty similar to last year, with the Toronto Blue Jays and New York Yankees making it through to the playoffs on wildcard slots.

A reversed chariot suggests that raw power and aggression is going to carry the National League wildcard through, so I’m going to guess that the Milwaukee Brewers, furious at their spot being taken by the Pirates of all teams, are going to surge into a wildcard spot with unrelenting fury.

World Series AL Contender: Upright Ace of Swords

World Series NL Contender: Upright Ten of Swords

This is a massive upset: I don’t actually think the Dodgers are going win the World Series. Will they make it? Yes! However, over time a new candidate will surprise everyone and surge through the ranks of the American League to battle a giant, and this candidate will be emerging from a hotly contested field with an extremely skilled strategy on their side. Good job Alex Cora, the Boston Red Sox will win the World Series in 2026.
