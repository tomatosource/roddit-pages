People are not excited about loot, they are excited about currency that they can convert into Divine Orbs… to buy items

The fundamental issue with this game. It feels like a gambling simulator. You have a rush of dopamine when you see a shiny currency drop on your screen. It’s like those Las Vegas slot machines. An ARPG is only as good as its loot as the saying goes. Most of the loot in PoE2 is garbage.

It would be already a huge improvement, if, at the very least, all dropped items by enemies would be already identified on the ground (meaning there would be no need to identify them) so a loot filter could filter out all the garbage and show you gear that actually have potential so you wouldn’t have to pick up millions of pieces and identify them one-by-one or via teleporting to the Hooded One to find out whether they are good or not.

So that way maybe players would actually start picking up loot instead of only focusing on chasing currency.
