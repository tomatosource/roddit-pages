Pacing is my biggest issue currently

Every zone feels the exact same despite being very thematically different. Theyre all very open and sprawling zones with convoluted routing.

Poe 1 pacing felt better because each zone had a unique layout and direction to its pathing. Poe 2 does not have any real distinction in routing between zones. You have to wander around in random directions always just hoping to stumble into a POI. The game world feels like it never directs you in any way, and because of this the games pacing feels off because its always just a single pace: wandering confusion.

Please give different zones a different flavor of movement through the zone instead of this constant droning large map layout.
