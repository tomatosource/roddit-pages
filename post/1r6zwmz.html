Why do attribute requirements exist?

I feel like each league, I’ve encountered the “not enough attributes issue” in some form and I’m now questioning whether they need to exist at all. Unless you have some gear that scales off of attributes (e.g. HoWA, Pillar of the Caged God) or you really really want to stack mana/life, they really don’t feel meaningful at all to have. I’ve played ranger every league, and each time I’ve had to take some +attributes affixes not because those affixes offer me some kind of benefit to the build but because I literally cannot use my gear otherwise. At least with resistances, you can still play the game if you’re not capped, you just take some more damage. Attribute requirements have no forgiveness if you don’t meet them.

Part of why I think attribute requirements are strange is because it is possible for your build to only use items that are concerned about one stat, in which case it’s pretty easy to meet those requirements. But if you want to mix stuff together, should you be taxed by being forced to get more attributes just to use your gear? If I want to be a spellcaster that uses armor, why should I need to have loads of both strength and intelligence? It feels like a punishment for straying from the intended path.

The presence of attribute requirements and resists makes gear choices totally suffocating. I’d like to have some mana regeneration as a suffix on my ring, but I can’t justify sacrificing 30 or 40 elemental resist to make that happen. If you add rarity into the mix, which isn’t required this league but was hugely impactful in previous ones, the variety in gear goes down even further (e.g. gold amulets being so good because of their implicit rarity). And having unique gear makes your remaining rare slots more restricted too, since many of those items don’t offer the same amount of resists that a rare item would.

Personally I would be ok with removing the concept of attributes entirely, but that’s probably too extreme. I just think attributes themselves have little to no value for many builds, and end up as the worst form of “required stat” where they hardly provide value on their own, but the game demands that you have them or your character will have a massive disadvantage. If each attribute point had more value maybe it wouldn’t so bad to be forced to have some amount of it, but it’s still forced.
