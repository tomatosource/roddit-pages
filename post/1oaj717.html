Giga Physical Wand Craft - Steps I took

Wanted to share this insane wand I crafted. As a relatively new player, I had to sink all my currency to create this, and I’m pretty satisfied. Not sure how to price it tho lol

Steps I took (probably sub-optimal):

  1. Buy a normal lvl 81 bone wand with two sockets
  2. Perfect transmutation + perfect augmentation and hope for good modifiers
  3. Get bad modifiers and perfect regal
  4. Annul until you have one modifier so you can start chaos spamming
  5. Chaos spam until you get a good caster modifier in the suffix (crit, cast speed, or + spell skills)
  6. Once you get a t1 caster suffix modifier, use Homogenizing exalt + omen of double exalt + omen of suffix + perfect exalt and hope for two other strong caster modifiers in the suffix (t1 roll of +spell skills of some kind is a must)
  7. desecrate with omen of amanamu and hope for % increased elemental damage in the prefix (use omen of light and re-desecrate as needed)
  8. Once you get % increased elemental damage on prefix (or even +extra lightning/cold/fire damage), homogenizing exalt + omen of double exalt + perfect exalt (no need for prefix omen) and hope for extra spell damage/spell damage
  9. extra tip: if you want to remove a prefix or suffix that you don’t like, you can use the mark of the abyssal essence + prefix/suffix essence omen

I think I did omen of light + step 9 a few times in between here and there, I don’t quite remember
