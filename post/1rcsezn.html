The All-Time MLB All-Chess Team

The premise of this post is simple: Find the best team we can with players who have names or nicknames that are chess pieces or contain a chess piece in them (so like, “Rooker” or “Kingman” is acceptable)

To start off I’d like to find the best 6 players who have one of the pieces in their actual names, not nicknames. Well, I’d like too, but there’s as far as I can tell no player named or nicknamed “Pawn” in major league history. We can get each of the other five pieces, with the best of the bunch probably being Heinie Groh (his middle name is Knight- or if you don’t think that counts, we can go with Ray Knight), Max Bishop, Jim Rooker, Mel Queen Jr (he didn’t have much of a career, but a slightly better one than his dad), and 19th century hurler Charles Frederick “Silver” King. But no Pawns to be found, at least not in English- but if we allow for names of chess pieces in foreign languages, the Germans call the piece a Bauer, and there have been a few players named Bauer or Bauers, with Hank Bauer being probably the best of the bunch- of course if we allow this, then players named Springer are also allowed to count as Knights (George is the best of the bunch, but it’s hard to say whether he’s actually better than Heinie Groh or not)

Expanding it to include nicknames, we can absolutely get a ton of mileage out of King, which a baseball reference search gives as being used for dozens of nicknames. I’m going to narrow the scope a bit and say that if it’s not part of a player’s actual name (King, Kingman, Kingery, Kingsley, anything of this nature is acceptable), then I will only allow a nickname that is just a standlone King (like hall-of-famer Michael Joseph “King” Kelly, or… apparently Joc Pederson?) or “The King” (like Jim Leyritz) or if the nickname was “King &lt;player name&gt;” (like “King Felix” Hernandez or “King Carl” Hubbell). But something like Tim Salmon’s nickname (Kingfish) or Charlie Keller’s (King Kong) or, the biggest reason for this rule, one of Babe Ruth’s many nicknames (The King of Crash) are too much of a stretch.

Anyway, best overall roster we can, chess names or nicknames, sticking with only English ones. I’m not going to require we find one name for every piece this time, because again there’s no Pawns, and the only Queens are like, not good:

Well there’s… a few really good players here, but that’s still got some really rough parts. Okay let’s take the suggestion of “allow foreign piece names” from before and see where that gets us. I will allow the name itself or something close (like Bauers counts for Bauer) but nicknames are out- no, Rogers Hornsby, your nickname of “Rajah” isn’t allowed here, not unless it was your actual name. I already mentioned Bauer and Springer from German, but the Spanish names (Rey for King, Torre for Rook) are also helping us a lot here:
