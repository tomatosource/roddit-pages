please change how skills granted by items behave. its almost impossible to use something like lifesprig for leveling

probably too late for this feedback but for next league, i think they need to change how skills provided by items work.

https://i.imgur.com/hexmhOH.png

https://i.imgur.com/DUhUSr1.png

theres a lot of ostensible leveling items that are kind of just ruined by the fact that their requirements get set by the skill level of the skill they provide, so the only possible way a copy of the unique can ever actually be used as a leveling unique is if it drops in the campaign. every single lifesprig that drops past act 1 (which is 99.9999% of them) is a dead item with no real utility

they just buffed sands of silk to have 3 charges. sounds like a premier speedleveling chest. except there are essentially none available for the leveling range. so ultimately its pointless.

solution: just have all item-provided skills work the same way as default attacks from weapons and ascendancy skills already do. they scale their level and socket-count with your player level, hitting 6s and level 20 when you hit player level 90. if you want a certain unique to provide a hardcoded skill level, then you keep it like that.

this would also fix the issue of 99% of high level caster uniques being kind of cooked because you can never 6L them. doesn’t matter that your whispering ice has godlike rolls its a level 19 weapon so thats -1 level and -1 socket

would this be broken? no i don’t think so. it would still only scale its skill level with you, so you can’t dumpster the campaign with it. a shitty act 1 caster weapon that scaled its mediocre spell along with you in the campaign is still a bad caster weapon
