PSA: Don’t use Doomfletch

TL;DR:
Doomfletch gives Attacks with this Weapon gain 50% of Physical Damage as Extra Damage of each Element, which sounds great. Until you realize that skill conversion happens before gain. Most elemental bow skills convert physical to elemental, meaning you gain nothing. Leveling sucks, gear support is limited, and unless you’re rich or a masochist, it’s not worth the effort.

To preface i loved playing Doomfletch in poe1

🚧 How Damage Mechanics Screw Doomfletch

The core issue is this:

skill conversion happens before gain

If a skill converts 100% of your physical to elemental (which a lot of bow skills do), then there’s 0 physical left for Doomfletch to gain extra damage from.

Example 1: Lightning Rod

  • Converts 100% physical → lightning
  • Result: 0% physical left
  • Gain = 0% × 50% × 3 elements = 0% gained damage

Example 2: Lightning Arrow

  • Converts 40% physical → lightning
  • 60% physical remains
  • Gain = 60% × 50% × 3 = 90% gained damage

To get the full 150% extra damage, you need a skill that doesn’t convert any physical. Sounds easy, right? Well…

🎯 Skills That Actually Work

Only 10 skills avoid converting physical altogether. But:

  • 4 of them are meant to scale with poison, not elemental:
    • Poisonburst Arrow (1 / level 0)
    • Vine Arrow (3 / level 6)
    • Toxic Growth (5 / level 14)
    • Gas Arrow (7 / level 22) — which also doesn’t benefit from the fire explosion

Now you’re down to 6 usable skills, and none of them are good for early wave clear.

The first decent one? Theoretically Spiral Volley at gem level 13 / character level 52.
Also Herald of Thunder/Ice convert physical to their elements so you deal no damage there either

So what are you using until then?

🏹 Bow Shot.

You’re stuck using Bow Shot for everything:

  • Wave clear? Bow Shot.
  • Bosses? Still Bow Shot.
  • Why not Snip or Detonating Arrow?
    • they felt great, but against quite a few bosses and rare’s, you just dont get the time needed to use them.

💸 Gearing is a Nightmare Too

Even if you wanted to make this work:

  • You must constantly upgrade rings, gloves, and quiver for better “Added Physical Damage to Attacks”
  • These mods gain new tiers every ~9 levels. Miss an upgrade? You fall behind fast
  • If you have the currency, congrats. now you’re in Trade Chat Simulator 2025
    • Whispering +20 people to buy a ring
    • Spending more time trading than playing

🗒️ Usable Skill List (That Actually Works with Doomfletch):

  • Bow Shot (0 / level 0)
  • Snipe (3 / level 6)
  • Detonating Arrow (9 / level 31)
  • Rain of Arrows (9 / level 31)
  • Tornado Shot (11 / level 41)
  • Spiral Volley (13 / level 52)

I abandoned the build at level 37. Maybe it gets better later, but based on what I’ve experienced and the time I’ve wasted. I seriously doubt it.

If 3-4 shotting white mobs for 3 levels, followed by an hour and a half of trading every 9 levels sounds fun, go for it. Otherwise just play something else.
