To everyone blitzing the campaign - remember that they’re including guaranteed drops as quest rewards and at fixed locations

Over the course of the 0.2 patch life cycle, GGG added several fixed drops for runes and currency like Artificer’s Orbs to add sockets to gear. These are not always given as specific quests or quest rewards, but can be found in some of the unique encounters throughout the campaign. An old example that was even in 0.1 is the rare witch in the Ogham Woods (third or fourth area of Act 1 I think) who always drops a health/mana flask.

There are some guides that include some of this information (Act 1 example), but I’m not sure they’re up to date or not. They also made it so rare/magic chests guarantee a rare/magic drop, in case you missed that change.

Stay sane exiles!
