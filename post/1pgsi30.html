Are Walsh and Queta Our Next Big Signings?

There has been a lot of talk about possibly getting under the tax for this season, as well as possibly next season. This is a divisive issue since fans generally don’t care about how much ownership spends, as long as their team attempts to be successful.

If the team is able to trade Simons for lesser salaries and possibly Tillman/Boucher for nothing, getting under the tax is actually quite simple. It’s next season when we are really supposed to get back to competing that it gets questionable.

With the Simons money and the KP TPE, we are poised to be able to add some high quality impact players to the roster, but with the surprising development of Queta and Walsh, it’s possible that we might already have our impact players on the roster now.

One of the biggest concerns coming into the season was our lack of depth, but it seems like we are hitting on all of our young guys right now. Hugo has shown some real flashes and looks to be a player in this league; Minott and Baylor look to be rotation players; and Queta and Walsh appear to be possible long-term starters.

Luckily, we have everybody signed through at least 2027, but after that it gets dicey. What if Queta and Walsh continue to improve? They are both extension eligible next summer and we hold their Bird rights. Minott is only on a 1+1 minimum, but I believe he could be re-signed with part of the MLE.

So what do you think, should Brad look to extend Queta and Walsh before they become FAs? Would you be okay staying under the tax this season and next season in order to reset the clock? Or would you rather spend above the tax this season, spend above the tax next season, and then hope to re-sign Queta and Walsh no matter the cost?
