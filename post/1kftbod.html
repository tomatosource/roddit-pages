What’s the harm in creating an Alternate Levelling system for Alts, like Delve, to test the response during Early Access?

I’ve been walking Alts through the Campaign for 10 years….. And a “Better campaign” does not make levelling Alts more desirable than in PoE 1.

In fact it does the opposite. PoE 2’s campaign is a significant improvement, but the changes they’ve added to improve replayability, like random layouts, actually does the opposite.

Don’t get me started on Twink Gear. The very existence of Twink Gear is an admittance that Alt Levelling is tedious. Twink Gear feels like a bandaid that should have a solution in 2025.

Why not just let us test Alternate Levelling in EA?

Delve wouldn’t need too much of a rework for PoE 2, and balance would simply be copied from the respective area levels of campaign zones.

If it works it works… If it doesn’t it doesn’t. What’s the harm?
