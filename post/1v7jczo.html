Should party play be balanced around casuals or super juicing parties with extra leachers?

Would love to hear others thoughts and what they personally experienced this league on this. I’ll open with mine but happy to have a change of heart if I see lots of people had a different experience than me and my group.

-I think GGG drastically missed the mark on party play this league. My group of buddies is fairly casual, all dads and ranged between 2-4 people a handful of nights a week. In previous leagues if we were all on discord we would just without a second thought jump into maps together and split the loot, and it has always been a good time. We always just put free for all pickup on and dump it in a guild stash tab and then after a couple hours just divide it by 3 or 4 of us and take our loot and say goodnight fellas good work.

Well this league things changed… the loot just completely sucks running in a group compared to all of us running solo!!!! Now for starters none of us are super juicing up our maps or anything. But basically this league we started figuring out the math: if the 3 of us run together for an hour, we only earn as a GROUP about 1.25x-1.5x currency of what an individual would earn. So basically if we take what we each can go make in a night (obviously RNG happens so this is just conclusions over the course of like a month) we would be WAAAYYYY better off just running maps by ourselves. It started to feel really blatantly obvious a few weeks ago when we would jump on and be like, “you guys wanna run maps?” And all of us are kinda like ehhhh i really don’t want to have to share the loot.

My theory is that GGG drastically overcorrected for last season with having temple farmers running 6 leachers and creating giga loot. But now we are in a situation where I can fairly confidently say that if a casual player parties up with 1-2 other buddies to map and chill, they are totally screwing themselves over on loot.

Is this because we see the big 6 man parties buying x4 20div tablets and finding ways to print currency?

Well if so… i don’t believe casual party play should be balanced around that small percentage of the player base that is grinding nonstop trying to max out all the juice possible from the game. Let them print. Because if the alternative is scaling back party play drops so much that a group of dads that want to alch and go together have to suffer 50% or more less loot than running solo… i don’t think you have a proper party play balance at that point.

I think the majority of people crying over party play balance in previous leagues have been solo players that are comparing selves to 6 man juicing parties… but the reality is I think GGG is nerfing party play for casual players to the point that they will lose players. Like my group isn’t gonna play this game if we are incentivized to all play separately while hanging out on discord. We want to play it TOGETHER, but without a 50-75% penalty on total loot drop
