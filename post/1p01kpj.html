First Game Memory

Does everyone remember their first game?

Mine was July 1, 1992. I was 5 years old. A weekday afternoon game against the Kansas City Royals. The Royals got out to a quick 6-0 lead by the 2nd but the Yankees kept clawing back. I was so depressed in the bottom of the 7th when Showalter pinched hit Matt Nokes for my favorite player Mike Stanley but Nokes deposited a 2 run homer into the upper deck in right to take the lead. The Royals got a few on in the 8th but the Yanks got out of it. George Brett smoke a ball down the line in the 9th but Mattingly made a diving stop. A few popups later and I was 1-0 in attendance.
