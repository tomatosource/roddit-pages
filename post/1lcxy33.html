What is the value of a first round pick now? And how does it affect the Celtics’ rebuild?

I’m sure I’m not alone in expressing my surprise at how little teams seem to be valuing first round picks. The Desmond Bane and Mikael Bridges trades seem like such silly overplays for role players (albeit very good role players), I am wondering how to interpret it.

This is not what should have happened in the era of the luxury tax, and especially with the punitive 2nd apron. I would have expected the opposite: teams concluding that the only way they could compete – especially if they had multiple players on max or near-max contracts – was to fill out the roster with cheap, young role players on rookie contracts. Instead, they seem to be dumping those future picks to win in a narrow window.

Keeping first round picks was the way I expected the Celtics to try and compete over the coming years, assuming they retained Brown and Tatum. And, to a certain extent, that is what Brad Stevens (and Ainge before him) have done. Brad famously did trade near-term picks, but only until recently – the Jrue Holiday trade – was he willing to trade a pick in the future, and never has he dumped multiple picks in a “win now” trade.

What do people think is going on with the league? Why these types of trades? And do you think the Celtics would be wise to follow suit? I, for one, don’t; I think the Celtics should be looking to add draft capital if possible as they try and trim the roster, because I think some teams are eager to compete in the near-term and those picks will be available, and valuable to a team like the Celtics given their salary realities.
