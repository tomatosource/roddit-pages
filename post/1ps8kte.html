Pathfinder and Werewolf should be the direction mobility is taken.

Sprinting

The Werewolf movement should be what sprinting is. After a second of moving without casting or taking damage you get a movespeed buff.

  • No heavy stun, no space bar.
  • The pathfinder Ascendancy should be something like Sprint speed or sprint evasion, both mods already exist on the tree.
  • And then reduce the effectiveness of the “While Sprinting” mods.

Movement Speed Penalty

Add ranks to Mobility support:

  • I: 25% less penalty
  • II: 50% less penalty, 120% cost multiplier.
  • III: 50% less penalty, movespeed on kill, movespeed reduction if haven’t killed recently.

This would let other classes a means to be mobile as Mobility in the game right now doesn’t do enough. Reworking Rhoa with this in mind would also help. There is no reason at 100 spirit and not having the movespeed increased, that the heavy stun should be a thing. Just make Rhoa a 50% less reduction and “You are always sprinting” and then look at the cost based on that.
