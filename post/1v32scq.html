[OC] 2026 NBA All-Adjective Team. I went through each NBA roster to find every player whose name is, or should be, an adjective (plus more)

I did not use AI to write this. It took me a week, on and off. This project began as a lighthearted aggregation of every name on an NBA roster, first or last, that is also an adjective. I found plenty, but I also found a lot of names that I felt should be adjectives. I’ve proposed definitions with example sentences. You’ll notice that my standards were not necessarily consistent throughout. Join me in this adventure, as we collectively await LeDecision.

However, numbers generally don’t fit neatly into conventional lexical buckets like nouns and adjectives, and are better conceptualized as a standalone specie, “numerals.” Unlike most nouns, numbers are definite and can’t be modified by adjectives, and unlike most adjectives, they can’t be graded or otherwise modified by adverbs. For instance, if a waiter asks how many people are joining for dinner, you’d say “there are 4 of us.” But in French, it is syntactically valid to say “we are 4.” Or consider “Louie XVI;” while it literally conveys “Louie Sixteen,” we understand it to mean “Louis the Sixteenth,” where the inclusion of the numeral has meaningfully modified the noun.
