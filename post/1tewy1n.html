POE2 still needs a LOT of QOL early on in acts, especially for melee classes

As I’m sure many people are, I’m a bit excited for the next league, so I’ve been trying out the different classes again, running them through at least act 1, and trying to decide what I feel like playing when the new league drops, and to put it simply, some of the older classes are just miserably behind the newer classes.

Druid is the super obvious, super egregious offender with Devour on their Wyvern which makes Killing Palm look like a freaking joke, but then there’s also the mana issues which just don’t even exist for Druid but Warrior and Monk constantly have to worry about mana throughout at least the first half of the campaign. Then the basic attacks on druid are SO good and Warrior gets Mace Strike, which I’ll just leave that as is, and lets not even talk about the basic attack for Quarterstaff, Monk feels barely even playable throughout most of Act 1. This attack is SO bad, you should legitimately just play another weapon type on monk until you get at least Frost Strike and preferably Hollow Palm, even then, it’s just playable, and far from actually being good.

We could turn around and talk about Druid spells, but I think we get the point, Druid has TONS of QoL that the other classes don’t have, but we also don’t even need to stop there. We can go back a bit further into Huntress and look into her skills. She gets a full combo of Explosive Spear, Whirling Slash, and Twister all available right there at level 1, as soon as you can find the skill gems. The only issue she has early on is sustaining mana, beyond that, all her buttons feel good and CLEARLY have room to grow. Then at level 3 she gets Lightning Spear, even in it’s nerfed form, it’s still a 1 button build, and while it isn’t particularly good, it’s something other classes SORELY wish they had to get them through the early stages until you have a density of resources to build up the multiple skills and supports you need to enable dealing real damage with all these combos.

I believe there is a great game somewhere in this game, but we SORELY need a QoL pass through on the release classes and PLEASE give us combos that ACTUALLY WORK in act 1, what are we doing here with non-functional pieces of builds for 13 levels and the entire first act of the game before we get to start having fun on many of the release classes, but especially melee. Why?
