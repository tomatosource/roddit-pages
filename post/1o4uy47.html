A’s Baseball Reference stats are still being adjusted to the run scoring environment in Oakland

As an example:

Brent Rooker, 2023: .817 OPS, 127 OPS+

Brent Rooker, 2025 .814 OPS, 123 OPS+

Statcast park factor for Oakland, 2022-24 (standard 2 year window): 97, 24th in the league

Statcast park factor for Sacramento, 2025: 108, 2nd in the league

Obviously not the biggest deal ever but keep this in mind when seeing OPS+ stats about the Athletics as a whole. The offense is still strong but not that great.

Same goes for the pitching staff - obviously not that good but slightly better than it seems.
