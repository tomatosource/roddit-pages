Where will Brandon Ingram end up?

A BI trade is pretty much imminent at this point but all the destinations have issues

Utah Jazz: Have the money to pay him and the assets to trade for him but Ingram said he valued a competitive environment and the Jazz are even further than the Pelicans from that. Also I doubt he wants to live in Utah

Los Angeles Lakers: I would bet my life savings this is where he wants to end up with signing to Klutch and getting to play next to LeBron and AD. However, the Lakers obviously don’t have the assets to get this done and the money is fishy as well

San Antonio Spurs: On paper, this is really the perfect destination. They need a SF and a true 2nd star to put next to Wemby, they’re competitive now, and they have the money and assets to get a deal done. However, I don’t know if they have interest in Ingram. If I were them I wouldn’t do it and would continue to build through the draft
