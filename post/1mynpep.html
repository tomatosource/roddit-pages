Upcoming trade system is going to be awesome.

I’m genuinely seeing this upcoming update as a very good change for poe2.

I’ve noticed, though, a lot of people mentioning trading bots and such. While I do think that some of it will be somewhat of an issue (such as bots always being first to the trade to grab the underpriced item) and as always, the game is for people so the ggg will have to worry about anti bot systems…

Overall, this will be going to be a way more convenient way of trading and actually getting what you want. Right, you might not be getting the heavily underpriced item first as most of us will be playing the game and not sitting 24⁄7 at the AH trying to get a better deal… But to be able to actually purchase an item for a decent price and do it conveniently, without having to agree to trade via whispers and then meet up? Instead, you find the item on a trading system, decide to buy it, go to their hideout and buy it from their npc?

That’s awesome. That’s really good. Saves my time as a player selling off items, saves your time and makes a gaming experience way smoother.
