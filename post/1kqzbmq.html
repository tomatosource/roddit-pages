My experience so far, did i reach a dead end?

I started out with huntress, you know, a new character with no previous builds and all, wanted to give it a shot. Maybe a bleed build? And you guessed it, bleed was never a viable option, even early on against low levels.

I tried a few other spear skills and switched up a few stuff in my skill tree but nothing really worked.

Switched to bow and arrow for a good while, did explosive gas arrow, then it actually became a thing and others were doing it and when looking at their builds, the uniques they used would lead to capping my dmg at somepoint though some were necessary for the build to work as intended.

So i switched again early on in endgame to lightning arrow, it was very good and very fun untill I’d say t12+ i started to feel it’s lacking in damage. I went for the crazies possible lightning dmg , on all the items that could roll it, and i mean the craziest, some of that stuff sold for 20+div and that’s early on in the game. Crazy crit and attack speed. Went well untill t15+, i couldn’t do shit to bosses and it wasn’t a steady rise of diff. And i promise you, i tried it all, all kinds of shit to make it work and in the end i just gave up.

A week later after taking a break a friend sent me a fork spear lightning build and told me that this was the shit and the only thing that would work, and what do you know, kept the same damn items i had before just changed my bow and quiver to spear and buckler, i was wiping even the unique bosses like they were minions. But i still stopped playing because i hatted the fact that i couldn’t make my own build for this character and i hate to play something everybody else plays even though i did tweak it to my liking.

Got back to playing almost a week or so ago and wanted to give warrior a try. Now that I’m in endgame, i got lucky to craft my own weapon in cruel 3 though i has to wait a few levels to use it because i didn’t want to spend my currency on market loot that early.

When it was finally time to buy some loot in endgame, i go to the market the first weapon i see listed that is barely and i mean barely better than the one i have crafted is priced at 300div. I thought that must be a joke or maybe i messed up the search, but what do you know, it really was like that, all prices were crazy. for example, people were selling rings at 50ex that were better than the ones sold with a divine or more.

I was flabbergasted, i didn’t see the market in a state of chaos like that in 0.1.

Warrior is fun, but if there are so few options to upgrade my loot, then what can a casual player do, since the market has no chaos resistance and is currently going crazy.
