[Robbins & Aldridge] The Washington Wizards are expected to meet with AJ Dybantsa this week and have already met with Caleb Wilson.

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7348455/2026/06/10/aj-dybantsa-wizards-visit-washington/?source=user_shared_article&unlocked_article_code=1.pFA.bKyS.9XBqHc0OC9O1

As the Washington Wizards prepare for a huge moment for the franchise — having the first pick in the 2026 NBA Draft on June 23 — multiple league sources told The Athletic that the team will host AJ Dybantsa, one of the prospects the team is considering to take at No. 1, for a visit with team officials in Washington in the next few days, perhaps as soon as Thursday.

The Wizards are also likely considering taking Kansas guard Darryn Peterson with the top pick. The 6-foot-5 Peterson averaged 20.2 points for the Jayhawks and shot 38 percent on 3-point attempts. His ability to create and shoot off the dribble, along with improved decision-making as a primary ballhandler and his potential as a plus defender at the pro level, make him an intriguing prospect.

Peterson is still in the mix for the Wizards at No. 1. If the Wizards opt for Dybantsa, the Utah Jazz, who own the second pick, are expected by most around the league to take Peterson.

A league source indicated this week that Washington has already met with North Carolina wing Caleb Wilson, another likely top four pick. Wilson did not work out for the team but met with team officials.
