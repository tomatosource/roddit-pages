My problem with Wisps

I’m posting this only in hopes that the right eyes see it, and address it. Upvote if you’ve been having a similar experience.

I’ve been trying to enjoy the new wisp mechanic in PoE 2’s endgame, but there’s one aspect of it that’s really messing with the flow of mapping — the way wisps decide which rare enemy to follow.

From what I understand, they’re supposed to trace toward a nearby rare, but I’ve had so many instances where I’m standing literally next to a rare enemy, and the wisp just takes off in the opposite direction — across the map — to some other rare. I’m then forced to chase after it or lose the wisp entirely. The worst part is that after dealing with the random rare it picked, I often have to backtrack all the way to where I started, just to kill the original rare I was right next to in the first place.

This really breaks the rhythm of a map. It kills any kind of efficient pacing and feels like I’m being punished for trying to engage with the mechanic. I can’t imagine I’m the only one noticing this?

I’ve also seen plenty of people complain about how underwhelming the wisp loot is, which only makes it feel less worth chasing them around. But even if the rewards were better, the tracking behavior is just frustrating. I really hope GGG takes a look at this — even a small tweak to prioritize the nearest rare would go a long way in improving the experience.

Is anyone else running into this issue, or found any workarounds?
