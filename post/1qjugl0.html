Is is me or is the temple slowly getting worse as the economy further inflates?

Mirror is now up to ~6,700 div.
Last weekend it was 4.2k, Monday 5.6k (I actually bought one for 5.5k that day…), and I can only imagine it going higher.

Meanwhile, Temple (fully juiced like Milky hard or fubgun layout) farming has a currency ceiling.

Previously, you roughly needed ~40 runs if we assume ~100 div per run to reach a Mirror benchmark.
Now, at 6.7k, you need ~67 runs for the same result.

Let’s quantify that:

Assume:

  • 3 Temple runs per hour
  • ~100 div per run
  • 60 crystals needed per cycle
  • ~40 minutes to farm a new set of crystals

So:

  • 10 runs = ~200 minutes
  • +40 min crystal farming = 240 minutes
  • That’s 4 hours for ~1,000 div → ~250 div/hour

That’s effectively the Temple ceiling.

There are other ways to farm Mirrors, such as:

  • Sekhamas (Last Flame)
  • Trial of Chaos (Unid’d Adorned)

And this is where things get interesting.

As Mirror price climbs, at what point does Temple become worse relative to other content?
For Chaos Temple or Sekhamas, a rising Mirror price actually raises your ceiling — but Temple doesn’t scale in the same way.

Sure, a Mirror can drop in Temple, but personally I’ve had 10 Hinekora’s Locks there and zero Mirrors so far.

Sekhamas example

Let’s say you farm Last Flame in Sekhamas:

Assume:

  • 20 min per run
  • 3 runs per hour
  • Mirror = 6.7k div
  • 4 Mirrors ≈ 26.8k div

If Last Flame drops within:

  • 500 runs → ~53.6 div per run → ~160 div/hour
  • 200 runs → ~134 div per run → ~402 div/hour

Now consider inflation:
If Mirror jumps to 10k div…

Temple remains roughly capped due to its fixed ceiling.
But Sekhamas scales upward — your div/hour can increase with Mirror price.

Same logic applies to Trial of Chaos.

With the right setup — e.g.:

  • Temporalis or very fast Ranger like PF or Deadeye (pick your poison)
  • Full MS relics
  • Onslaught helmet

Personally, Sekhamas feels both more engaging and than Temple of Chaos.

Just some thoughts.
