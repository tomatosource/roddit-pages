I was once a Tatum Defender

After several post seasons of very mediocre or flat out bad shooting splits. I’ve defended him, once it was. He’s young give him time to figure it out. My next defense especially last year. That he did the little things to help his team win and he did. This year, there’s no room for excuses. Averaged 30 and 11 on a bad wrist against a good defense. I figured ok here in the version of Tatum us Celtics fans have been waiting on.

The Knicks who are a good team, but have mismatches we can exploit. All I could think about is how, Tatum should thrive this series. Here we are 2 games in. Yes 2 abnormal shooting nights, but all decided by a couple of points. In the back of my mind I was thinking to myself. Damn we still could have won these games if Tatum at least gave us an AVERAGE performance. Went back watched some of the game. I don’t understand, he’s getting the mismatches, has length or a speed advantage. Easily gets discouraged driving to the lane when he feels a body on him. Bails Knicks out with a very bad and tough shot selection.

I can’t defend Tatum anymore, think he’s an amazing player. Just shrinks in the playoffs and I’m unsure why.
