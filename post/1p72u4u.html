Just an Opinion

Hey Guys. I just wanted to say im super excited for the new league and have lowkey been counting down and trying to find leaks about the game. However, i just wanted to share some thoughts so far from a newbie in the ARPG community (i come from LOL).

So i started POE2 in dawn of the hunt (0.2) and got to a max of level 72 because i didn’t know what i was doing (didn’t know resistances were that important). After looking at some guides from mobalytics from (scorpius, woolie and a few others ) detailing the basics and mechanics needed for you character to succeed. Which thankfully i did as most of my characters are over level 90 and clocked over 1500 hours.

But just wanted to share my opinion on what could be coming in POE2 or what i would like to see. Thank for coming to my TED talk and please leave any comments or suggestions and man the community compared to the LOL one is vastly different

  • SWORDS (like how is an ARPG not having swords and its been a year since it released)
  • Performance fixes which can include delirium (i don’t mind it but i can understand why people hate it)
  • SORC is one of my favourite classes but i feel like BM is just a better caster or elementalist class to use especially if u want to go a lightning build. (the other spells are pretty weak compared to lightning) Cold can work infact my chronomancer is only using cold spells and is level 92. Getting new ascendancy for the other classess would be very cool too.
  • Trial of Suckmeass needs to go and that bring me to another point of another way to ascend ultimatum is fine since you know what to stack but boy the honour system sucks for melee.
  • Another big thing i would like to see is a sense of progression, because right it is basically mindless mapping and i have no motivation to keep going, a lot of people say taking inspiration from POE1 atlas would be good idea but i like the layout of the atlas but maybe the tree itself would need a rework.
  • The tree itself is a bit unbalanced im sure it will get better as more class and items are added. (i heard something about tattoos ). I still think energy shield is kinda nuts compared to armour or even evasion
