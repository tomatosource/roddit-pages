My dead dad loved baseball and now I want to too

My dad passed 7 years ago when I was 16. It was understandably the absolute end of the world. I was his only kid and he was my best friend. And then Covid happened like a year after he died. Almost comically tragic adolescence I had!!

Anyways, he loved baseball so much like lifelong Tigers fan, played throughout school and then in rec leagues all the way up to 1 year before he died. He played while undergoing cancer treatment. He LOVED baseball.

Despite his best efforts, I was never able to get into it. I was an athletic child and liked other sports, but baseball felt different for some reason. He took it pretty well because we had plenty to bond over otherwise. I went to most of his games and I would always listen to him talk about baseball, but it was almost like he was speaking a different language.

Since his death, I’ve had some signs pulling me towards getting into it. The biggest was from my favorite band, Fall Out Boy, who themed a lot of their imagery for their most recent album around baseball and specifically Field of Dreams. I’m obsessed with fob and I always told my dad that they were my baseball. So it felt kinda crazy that they themed after the dead dad baseball movie!

The other day I was on a walk and passed a sign for a school field that said in huge letters “FIELD OF DREAMS” and for some reason everything clicked and I was like oh my god I need to become obsessed with baseball. I haven’t even seen Field of Dreams just because it felt too close to home. I think I’m gonna try to watch it tonight.

So I’m gonna start watching the Tigers next season and check out some local games (I’m in the PNW so can’t easily go to an actual Tigers game lol). I might even try to get into a rec league, like probably slow pitch softball.

But in the meantime I’d like to learn and prep for the season. I’m a big reader so I’d love any recommendations on good baseball nonfiction, especially if it’s about the Tigers! I feel like I’m going into this with minimal working knowledge and feel like winter is a good time to learn a bit about it.

Anyways, that’s my story. I tried to keep this light but of course it’s a heavy topic. I’m excited to start this journey and connect with my dad.

Build it and he will come.
