Dream of dual-wielding two-handers Berserker build

Hello everyone,

I’m fairly new to this genre of games and recently got into Path of Exile 2 after trying Grim Dawn. What really hooked me was the feeling of smashing through hordes of enemies with a club the size of a person.

Without a second thought, I picked the Warrior class and started exploring its possibilities. In the skill constellations two perks immediately caught my eye: “Convert all damage to Fire” and the one that allows you to wield a two-handed weapon in one hand. I became obsessed with the idea of creating a raging berserker who wades into the thick of battle, wildly swinging two two-handed maces and sustaining himself through lifesteal.

However, after looking more closely at the active skills available to the Warrior, I started to doubt whether it’s possible to create something close to this fantasy without completely gimping the character.

With these negative thoughts, I decided to play through the campaign as a more conventional shield-based Warrior, dealing damage through retaliation and attacks during stun windows. But sometimes, I just want to throw defense to the wind, leap into a crowd of enemies, and start fearlessly smashing them to pulp. The berserker dream keeps calling me back.

So, to the Warrior class experts out there: what are your thoughts on this? Is a viable dual-wielding, two-handed berserker build a possibility, or is it more of a late-game / theorycrafting dream? Any advice on skills, devotions, or gear direction would be immensely appreciated!
