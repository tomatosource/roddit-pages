The NBA already solved flopping. They just refuse to enforce the rule.

The solution to flopping is already in the rulebook. The NBA just refuses to enforce it.

There were 84 flopping technicals called the entire regular season of 1,230 games league-wide. With multiple ones obvious each game, that’s basically the league admitting they don’t actually care about enforcing it.

People always talk about how hard flopping is to officiate, but refs already make subjective calls all game long. They decide landing zone violations, continuation, marginal contact, charges vs blocks, verticality, all of it in real time. The issue isn’t whether they can call flops. It’s whether the league wants them to.

If players knew obvious flops would actually get punished every night and affect winning, they’d adjust immediately.
