Boston Celtics fans — especially the ones in Boston — are insufferable sometimes.

We won the championship less than two years ago, and it’s ridiculous how many people are already complaining about Jaylen and Derrick White. In just two games, we’ve stayed relatively competitive with a broken squad.

If you’ve been watching since the early 2000s, you know there have been plenty of bad years. This is a good thing, we can’t win every year, and that’s not a bad thing. Teams go through cycles. A lot of players are going to get opportunities to shine on this roster who otherwise would be stuck on the bench, and that’s okay.

We need seasons like this. It’s nice to finally see player progression for once. It’d be great if this sub didn’t overreact to one loss or a rough stretch. I’ve seen the Celtics play much worse. As long as Jaylen gets fully healthy and they figure out a system as the season goes on, that’s a win. We can have good and bad days without someone crying about Jaylen Brown and the team.
