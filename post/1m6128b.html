One of the most underrated seasons of all time!

James Harden in 2018–19 is one of the most dominant players I have ever seen. He averaged 36.1 points, 7.5 assists, and 6.6 rebounds in 78 games! For context, the only players to average more are MJ and the mythical Wilt. It is crazy to me how little this season is talked about when people reference the all-time great seasons. I think this is one of the best examples of a player single-handedly terrorizing the league, and this is coming from a non-Rockets/Harden fan. I think not only should he have been MVP, I think this is one of the best offensive seasons of all time!
