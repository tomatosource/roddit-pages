I never thought I’d say this, but PoE 2 largely feels like a themepark game to me

The devs have been making a lot of good community-requested changes over the last week or so, but it feels like it’s all a bit for naught at the end of the day because the core game systems just don’t really facilitate depth very well. The slow streamer exodus from PoE diehards seems to reflect this as well, since they got a lot of what they were asking for, but they still aren’t really motivated to keep playing, and many are slowly getting bored and dropping off.

And to me, the reason is that while PoE 2 is a much better first playthrough than PoE 1 was, but it doesn’t feel like it’s deep enough for the long haul at endgame. And this isn’t something that can be fixed by just adding more stuff. It’s the way the core systems are designed to sort of railroad you into a much more dev-directed experience. It feels like I’m painting by numbers rather than just being given free reign with a blank canvas and an endless amount of crazy new colors every few months.

This is something I felt nagging at me as early as 0.10 after I beat the campaign, but it’s been exacerbated in 0.20 now that everything is no longer shiny and new, and I don’t have that voice in my head telling me everything’s going to be fine anymore.

Right now, it feels like a great one and done game, but not a particularly compelling log in everyday kind of game. Ironically, pretty much how I felt about Diablo 4.
