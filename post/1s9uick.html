Celtics defense

So guys playoffs are right around the corner and usually top defensive teams are the ones who make deep runs.

let’s break down some of the defensive stats before and since the all-star game to count in JT’s return and impact on the team.

to start things off, Celtics are #5 in the defensive rating (108.7) significant increase from their pre all-star number 112.6(#9)

another important defensive stat that often gets overlooked is the defensive rebounding. C’s averaged 32.6 defensive rebounds per game before the all star break, which ranked #12 in the league. after the break C’s completely crash their glass with 36.9 DREB per game, which grants them undisputed number one spot. coincidentally, Boston allows fewest second chance points(11.3) during this span, when they were merely #18 before the all star weekend. Jayson Tatum effect?

Celtics have noticeably upped their paint protection this season. before the break we were second in the league in opponents points in the paint, and guess what, we overtook OKC in this quite important stat as well. shout-out Neemias Queta.

And lastly, another key factor in the modern basketball is the three point shooting. Teams shot 36% from beyond the arc before the break, which put The Celtics on the mediocre 17th place. however after the ASB Celts clamped down on the perimeter and only allow opponents to shoot unimpressive 34.4% from three which bumps the Greens to number 8 spot.
