By winning 6 of their last 8 games, the Pirates clawed back to a .500 record all time

For reference, I’m using the Wikipedia all-time regular season win-loss table. If it’s wrong, I blame Rob Manfred.

On another thread, someone mentioned the Cubs all time record not being all that bad (“Psh, you’re not even a thousand games over .500” - Yankees, Giants, and Dodgers), so that led me down the rabbit hole.

After 144 seasons playing in MLB, a grand total of 21,960 games, the Pirates own an all time record of 10,910-10,910-140.

There are all sorts of fun facts on there. The Pirates have 38 more regular season wins than the Yankees, but there are 6 teams ahead of them in total wins. They’re 125 losses behind the Braves and a whopping 482 losses behind the Phillies. The Phillies are the only team over 1000 games under .500, though the Orioles are 24 away from that mark at 976 games under.
