Made a PoE2 league start build randomizer for my friend group - Vetology

Made this as something we can do over discord together when season starts, you roll a class/ascendancy and a flavor archetype (Palpatine, The Lich King, Bearpocalypse etc.), and can veto each other’s rolls with a proper ruleset. Also built a companion build guide with gem unlock order, ascendancy node picker and PDF export. There’s 118 Archetypes so far, some of them more fleshed out than others. This build was AI-Assisted and the site is not hugely intuitive/readable cos I have a life during the day -it’s really stupid but will be fun with friends.. Have fun vetoing and I hope you enjoy Vetology!

https://jo3al3x.github.io/Vetology/
