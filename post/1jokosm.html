After the loss tonight, the Wizards will NOT finish the season with 60 wins

Sorry to any Wizards fans holding out hope, but for the 50th season in a row, the Wizards will once again finish the season under 60 wins.

The last time the Wizards (as the Bullets) achieved this feat was in the 1974-75 season, where they set a franchise record 60 wins, but unfortunately that record will stand for at least another year after tonight’s loss.
