Congratulations to the winner of the Third Edict Race 2, with an Act IV Tavakai kill in 2 hr 36 min 15 sec. Now 6⁄7 classes in under 3 hours!

!…wholenewsociety, with the Mercenary class!!&lt;

Approximate completion times per act, not necessarily by the same person:

  • Act I: 35 min (-5 from last week)
  • Act II: 1 hr 25 min (50 min) (-0 from last week)
  • Act III: 2 hr 05 min (40 min) (-0 from last week)
  • Act IV: 2 hr 35 min (30 min) (-5 from last week)

There isn’t much to say about Act I other than it has clearly become hyper optimized - half hour completions seem unrealistic. Some players consider respawning at checkpoint within the first zone to give themselves multiple gems to sell to a vendor for an attempt at an early purchase, however this does not translate to an obvious advantage.

Act II remains the most time consuming zone while seemingly having the least opportunity for improvement.

Act III is the most lethal zone, with Abysses taking out multiple players. Silverfist continues to be a threat, even in spite of the active block changes which highlight his moves bright red. The Queen of Filth also improved her KDR. Multiple deaths hit a player in the lead who remained competitive afterwards, almost shrugging off the 2 min 30 sec death penalty, in some cases multiple times. It seems plausible that with no deaths Act III could be completed in under 2 hours.

Between Race 1 and Race 2, Hotfix 0.3.1b changed spectres to prevent them from repeatedly triggering minion instability on weapon swap, where these effects ignored the minion death timer; Sorceress & Witch racers had used this to contribute to their speed. Some found workarounds post-patch with spectres and infernal legion, repeatedly summoning them with weapon swap and once again, ignoring all minion death timers.

Of the Act IV islands—Shrike, Whakapanu, Prison—there still seems to be no consensus on a necessary order. With the 0.3 additions of sprint and instant swaps, most top players have taken to the combination of sprint + dodge roll + leap slam to close out Act III and complete all of Act IV with very few monster kills.

The level spread against Tavakai (L53) tends to be similar but with a high variance on class. L33 - L34 is the sweet spot for many. Sorceresses in both weeks are around L31, while Rangers in both weeks finish around L41.
