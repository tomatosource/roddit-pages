Unique Jewel idea: Walk a single Path Not Taken

I really want to see a unique jewel with

Limit: 1
Walk the Path Not Taken to &lt;random unseen notable&gt;

Between the limit, the cost of the jewel socket, and needing to actually get this unique jewel, I feel like the Oracle’s uniqueness is protected (particularly with Entwined Realities allowing the Oracle to jump around to a bunch of different notables). It’s just very frustrating to me that so many different things are unavailable to other builds. Most of them aren’t even all that unique (though they are somewhat stronger than regular nodes), but the few that are unique, are especially frustrating.

Alternatively, instead of a unique jewel, it could be a special distill option, replacing your usual instilled notable with opening up a path, but you still have to actually spend the passive points. That becomes a huge cost and I’m not sure anyone would ever want to do it, though. Just being able to straight-up instill an unseen notable seems probably too strong, though.
