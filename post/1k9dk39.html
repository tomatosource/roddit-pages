I have figured out why “uniques” are “bad”

The truth is, they aren’t. There are a lot that are just bad design yes, but not all, and I’m not talking meta. The problem is defenses. Think about it. Oh cool unique chest, got a unique interaction, could be a fun build. But at what sacrifice? The fact we rely heavily on defenses, looking at you resistances, I’m not wearing that cool unique because a rare out beats it not only in base defense (Eva,es,armor) but we NEED those resistances/life/spirit.

Look, to really make uniques useful, to make crazy cool builds, us players need less of a stress on defense. If resistances needed were 50% cap along with other defenses not needed to be so high, less spirit required and a buff to our health pool in general, we could get resistances easier on pieces, have more health, beefier base defenses and wear these uniques GGG works hard on. 98% of uniques that drop I shard. Even if hey, this could be cool to build around. Because I know I’ll take a hit in resistance, def, life, mana, attributes…. You get it. Imagine having to stress less on all of that, letting you experiment with uniques. As it stands, rares are just always better 98% of the time.
