PSA :For you Struggling with performance, try this before giving up

TL&DR

close chrome, and turn off (power button no need to disable it) your second monitor, then come back here and tell me if its smoother now :) [i really hope that mix as many people performance issuse ]

Hello, since the Tower patch , pretty much nothing changed on my computer, but the game feel very very laggy, so FPS instead of a rock solid 60, rubberbanding 20 to 40 in juicy maps, even in Hideout without doing anything 56-60 fps (that nailed it)

I had reduced everything while i could play and stream in full quality, was my PC dying, did GGG screw up again (many tend to flag them as the usual suspect), NO!

It was way easier, first i killed Chrome ( mine wasn’t upto date but i had to many things to preserve there)

And miraculously, FPS became stable in HO

(i play windowed full screen), then before going juicy maps, i just turned off my second monitor ( power button of monitor) and i gained in stability.

This put an end tothat full month where i wasnt even to enjoy the game due to poor performance, and was unable to stream and share the poe love.

Hope it solve your performance issues too
