What is the current story for the Power Fantasy in this game?

While I’m having fun with the game, I’m not feeling the Fantasy part of the Power Fantasy.

In PoE1 our Exile is The Godslayer, and that is what made Kirac and Zana to ask us to help them fight horrors larger than gods in the Atlas. Even the starry Atlas tree is thematic to this Power Fantasy, with Cosmic Eldritch beings behind is effects.

PoE2 is still in EA, so while things will change, critique is allowed. I’m feeling the growth of Power, but not the Fantasy behind it.

As a suggestion, could our Exile be “The Conqueror”? This is a throwback to Conquerors Of The Atlas, where players grinding the Atlas too much becoming bosses became canon, and it was glorious. In EA, we already defeat Count Geonor, Jamanra The Risen King and Doryani The Royal Thaumaturgist in the first 3 acts. They are all big figure heads, so if we are not The Conqueror, then what are we?

Our inherent objective in PoE2 Atlas could be to explore, fight and conquer the lands, and this is a matter of storytelling. Even the Atlas tree could be made to fit this thematic, as in different sections of the tree are about different types of governances, but mechanically everything would still work the same.

As for the governances, we have the 5 Conquerors from PoE1, and each of them are different. Sirus could be the chaos, and is effectively alch and go governance. I’m sure we could come with other ideas as well.

Wraeclasts history of full of Conquerors, with glorious battles, drama and betrayals. Would it be cool, if it was finally our turn?
