[Mike Vorkunov] Sixers, Rockets among ownership groups bidding for WNBA expansion teams

Paywalled Article: Mike Vorkunov - Sixers, Rockets among ownership groups bidding for WNBA expansion teams

  • The ownership group behind the Philadelphia 76ers has submitted an official bid for a WNBA expansion team.

  • Houston Rockets owner Tilman Fertitta has also submitted a bid, aiming to bring a team back to Houston, home of the former WNBA champion Comets.

  • Detroit Pistons owner Tom Gores is leading a bid to bring a WNBA team back to Detroit, with support from Grant Hill, Chris Webber, and others.

  • Nashville’s bid, led by Bill Haslam, would name the team the Tennessee Summitt in honor of Pat Summitt, with Candace Parker and Peyton Manning involved.

  • Cleveland, Charlotte, Austin, and Denver have also expressed interest in expansion teams.

  • The WNBA has recently added teams in the Bay Area, Toronto, and Portland.
