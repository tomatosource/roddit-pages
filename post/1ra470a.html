With his retirement announcement, I am reminded of one of my favorite what-if players in Juan Lagares.

His peak Gold-Glovery defense in CF was truly something to behold. Flashes of competence at the plate really made you think he could ascend to stardom. However, he was hampered by injuries relatively frequently and there were simply too many times when he was a near automatic out at the plate or generally a non-factor offensively.

If he had been able to muster a career slash line of just something in the realm of .250/.325/.450 (of course he is evading the injury bug in this hypothetical timeline) we would be talking about him within an inner circle of outfielders that is hard to reach - his defense was simply that great. Iykyk.
