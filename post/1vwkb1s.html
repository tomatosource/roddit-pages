[BasketNews] Victor Wembanyama: “Of course you always learn when you play against Jokic. You always catch some stuff and smarter moves on the court in those duels with him.”

“It’s weird to play in France again, but honestly, there is nothing easier than to return and play games like this. The atmosphere in Orleans was great, it was really beautiful to play,” Wembanyama said during a post-game media scrum.

The San Antonio Spurs star started at center, having previously played significant minutes at power forward in Belgrade.

“I felt more comfortable on the court [as a center] from the start. We are a new team, and we don’t know each other so well yet, so it was important to start from the basics. I think that we have made a step forward.”

“My words before the game were that we shouldn’t take anything for granted. It is an exhibition game, but we have to learn how to win. Today we learnt a few lessons.”

Wemby’s honest take about matchups with Jokic

Speaking about the lessons learned, Wembanyama admitted that he gains insight from every matchup against Nikola Jokic.

“Of course you always learn when you play against Jokic. You always catch some stuff and smarter moves on the court in those duels with him.”

“Our plan is simple: we are going to Paris in a day or two, continue with our preparations, and then we play the Qualifiers games,” he concluded.
