Silver will certainly be asked to weigh in at today’s post-BOG news conference.

“Oh my God, these guys are animals,” said one NBA team executive about NBA-led investigations. “It’s a league of lawyers. They investigate. You’d rather be investigated by the DOJ than the NBA.”

According to CBA by-laws, Silver has the ability to void Leonard’s Clippers contract, take away draft picks, fine the team up to \(7.5M, fine Leonard up to \)350,000 or suspend Clippers personnel who were complicit. None of that will be figured out today, but Clippers-gate is the obvious cloud hanging over today’s BOG session.
