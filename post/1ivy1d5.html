The most fun 2 hours of gaming I’ve had since the early days of WoW raiding.

I started a blind HCSSF. No videos, no guides, almost no background on PoE2. I’ve played Diablo series since the early days of Diablo 1 (anyone else play beyond naked mages in the 90s?) and played PoE1 a few years ago but went back to D2 HC.

The first 2 hours have been so much fun and this is with almost zero progress in the game! A few notefull interactions.

  • Near death 1 happened right away at the bloated miller. No big deal though, just getting used to the buttons.

  • Near death 2 happened out of nowhere one of those ‘hags’ exploded a ring around me. Okay don’t stand in stuff! Easy enough.

  • Spent a looong time (hour+) planning out my minion strengths. Not even sure what attributes are but I’ll grab those nodes when I need them. For now, stronger minions!

  • I still can’t figure out how to get a skeleton sniper to actually appear!! Bah, next time for sure because I’m sure I’m missing a ton of early dps.

  • Beat the devourer at level 3 but it was a multi minute fight and I won with 0 flask and almost no health. My witch continually mentioned something about a tail and I still have no idea what she was talking about b/c my only goal was move shoot move shoot move shoot…

So much fun!! I fully expect to die soon (and likely many times to come given the early boss fights so far) and come back with a little more knowledge each time. This has been awesome.
