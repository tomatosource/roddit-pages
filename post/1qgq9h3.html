As the title suggests, is Kareem the only guy to be the best at all 3 levels (HS, college, NBA)?

His total Basketball career is arguably the best ever.

In college, his side went 88-2. His first year they went 30-0! In his 3 years in college he averaged 26ppg and 15 rebounds. he won 3 College players of the year, 3 time all American, 3 time most outstanding player in the tournament and won 3 titles

In the NBA he has 6 rings, 6 MVPS, 19 All stars, 10 first teams, 5 all Defense first teams.

When you look at other guys in that top 10 argument, does anyone have a resume close to this?
