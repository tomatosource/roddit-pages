The Mets are 0-15 in Game 3 at Yankee Stadium since 2001. I scanned 61,634 MLB games to find it.

I built a system that scans every MLB game since 2000 using Retrosheet game logs. 61,634 games total. It automatically flags statistical anomalies. This one stopped me cold.

The Mets have never won a Game 3 at Yankee Stadium. Not once in 24 years.

15 series. 15 Game 3s. 15 Yankees wins.

The Yankees have outscored the Mets 110 to 47 in these games. Average final: 7.3 to 3.1. These aren’t nail-biters. The 2009 game was 15-0. The 2006 game was 16-7.

If you assume a coin flip, 15 straight is a 1 in 32,768 probability. Even accounting for home field advantage, it’s roughly 1 in 6,600.

Honestly I go back and forth on whether this means anything or if it’s just the kind of thing that happens when you have 60,000+ games worth of data. Flip a coin enough times and you’ll hit 15 heads in a row eventually.

But tell me this. If you’re a Mets fan sitting down for Game 3 at Yankee Stadium this year, is this in the back of your head or not? Watch them finally break it in 2026 now that I’ve put this out there.
