No visible record after killing Uber Arbiter of Ash? Lack of progression info in the endgame feels strange

Pretty new to POE myself. I just killed the Uber version of the Arbiter of Ash, and I find it a bit odd that there’s no visible record anywhere showing that you’ve done it — unlike with the Pinnacle bosses from other mechanics, where there’s a clear interface that shows if you’ve cleared 1, 2 or 3 of the difficulty levels.

Maybe it’s intentional, so that progression feels more organic and less checklist-based — but it still leaves a strange sense of “did I really do that, or did the game forget to mark it?”

Would anyone know if this is a bug, a design choice, or something still not implemented in 0.3?
