From 2000 onwards, the finals mvp award has been given to the player who averaged the most ppg on the championship team except for 4 instances

There are 4 instances of a player who didn’t average the most ppg on the championship team winning fmvp.

2004:

Rip Hamilton - 21.4 PPG on 50% TS

Chauncey Billups - 21 PPG on 69.6% TS

Chauncey Billups won fmvp

2014:

Tony Parker - 18 PPG on 55% TS

Kawhi Leonard - 17.8 PPG on 75.3% TS

Kawhi Leonard won fmvp

2015:

Steph Curry - 26 PPG on 58.5% TS

Andre Iguodala - 16 PPG on 58.8% TS

Andre Iguodala won fmvp

2024:

Jason Tatum: 22.2 PPG on 50.5% TS

Jaylen Brown: 20.8 PPG on 53.5% TS

Jaylen Brown won fmvp

The PPG difference between fmvp winner and the leading ppg scorer is not high at all, the only outlier of a substantial difference is the 2015 fmvp where Curry averaged 10ppg higher than Iguodala and Iguodala won fmvp.

If the knicks win the finals, the two favorites for fmvp would be Jalen Brunson and Karl-Anthony Towns.

Jalen Brunson is currently averaging:

25 PPG on 41.6% TS

While Karl-Anthony Towns is currently averaging:

19.5 PPG on 65.8% TS

I personally think that if the Knicks win from their current level of play, KAT should win fmvp. But Brunson has also somewhat made a case for it. If KAT did win it would be the 2nd most point differential between the fmvp and leading ppg scorer since 2000 onwards.

BUT PLEASE READ - PPG isn’t the only important metric for fmvp.
