GGG please explain systems to your players.

This game has countless systems and just drops you into them with no warning or information. Pyramid was probably the first thing that you get thrown into during the campaign with no explanation of what the purpose of the mechanic is, how the rooms work or your objective.

Hive fortress introduces a new mechanic to the breach system with waves of enemies (pretty self explanatory) but then appears to be bugged if you don’t know there is only one way out and you have to find a way out.

Its the same with atlas skill points etc at end game, NPC’s pop up within maps and tell you to go to specific POI’s with 0 information about the importance of those POI’s or what you will unlock there.

I don’t expect to have my hand held completely but it feels like the whole game is an escape room that 2% of the player base understands and then teaches others on reddit and youtube (which is usually information on how to maximise the mechanics not the basics of how everything is unlocked and actually works)

All im asking for is a couple of lines of info in the quest text and a splash screen here and there explaining how things work.

For context I haven’t played POE1 and played POE2 during the launch of EA.
