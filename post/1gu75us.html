How the 2024 Cleveland Cavaliers Stole the 13-0 Power from the 1948 Washington Capitols

Last week the Cleveland Cavaliers became the 6th NBA team to start a season 13-0. After looking across the six teams, it seems the power of the 13-0 start has been stolen, Monstars-style, from the 1948-49 Washington Capitols down to the 2024-25 Cleveland Cavaliers.

1. 1948-49 Washington Capitols: The first team to go 13-0 to start a regular season, the Capitols were coached by none other than Red Auerbach. This would be Auerbach’s final year coaching the Capitols and in 1950 he would go on to coach the Boston Celtics, who just so happen to be the second team in NBA history to start 13-0.

2. 1957-58 Boston Celtics: It took a few years, but Auerbach revived his 13-0 power in ‘57-58. Although the Celtics would start this season 13-0, they lost in the NBA Finals. However, they would immediately bounce back and win 10 of the next 11 NBA Finals, with Auerbach at the helm in either a coaching or executive capacity. Ironically, though, none of those championship seasons would start off as well as it did in 1957-58.

3. 1993-94 Houston Rockets: Auerbach’s last season as the Celtics head executive was in 1983-84. One of the few games Auerbach’s Celtics lost in ‘83-84 was against the Houston Rockets, who had a rookie assistant coach by the name of Rudy Tomjanovich. It seems Tomjanovich stole Auerbach’s 13-0 power, as he would go on to become the head coach of the Rockets in 1991⁄92 and then lead the 1993-94 Houston Rockets to a 13-0 start.

4. 2002-03 Dallas Mavericks: During the Rockets’ 13-0 season in 1993-94, there were a few teams they went 4-0 against in the regular season, one of those teams being the Golden State Warriors. In ‘93-94, the Warriors were coached by Don Nelson. Nelson learned a lot from the multiple losses at the hands of the 13-0 Rockets team and bring those lessons with him as the head coach of the Dallas Mavericks. He would lead them to a 13-0 start in 2002-03, and to further the 13-0 connection, Nelson played for the Celtics while Auerbach was head coach.

5. 2015-16 Golden State Warriors: After coaching the Dallas Mavericks, Don Nelson would return to Golden State to coach for four more seasons. His last season with Golden State was in 2009-10, when he coached a rookie by the name of Wardell Stephen Curry. The power of the 13-0 start was then transferred from Nelson to Curry, and Curry would go on to help lead the Warriors to a 13-0 start in 2015-16.

6. 2024-25 Cleveland Cavaliers: And now we get to the Cavs. In the offseason, the Cavs hired a new head coach, Kenny Atkinson. Before coming to Cleveland, Atkinson was an assistant coach for the past three seasons with Steph Curry and the Golden State Warriors.
