Do not fall for orb of annulment scams

There are many items being sold for annuls that are worth far less but since annuls are calculated as much cheaper than they actually are when sorting for lowest price people selling for annulment orbs show up first despite being multiple times the worth of the items. I started to see this more which means people are probably buying the items. So instead do like I do and in the trade filter make sure to filter for the actual standard trade currency you are expecting to pay for an item so this doesn’t work, people do it with transmutes and things as well since they are valued lower than normal by the trade filters.
