Movement on skills should be optional

After watching the trailer for the druid, I got a bit disappointed that GGG is still pushing for “jump back” attacks. Yes they look cool in the trailers, but actually using them in-game either gets old really fast, as most of the time you wanna get the damage of the skill, jump back, and then re-engage with the enemies; at the end, the backflip is a side-effect of you wanting to use the skill, and not for actual repositioning.

I feel the cleanest solution is that skills that allow you to backflip could get a special tag like “Jumping”, and you can either use them as normal, or if you hold spacebar while using the skill you perform the backflip, and could potentially have “Does X% more while backflipping” if they want to incentivize this form of gameplay.
