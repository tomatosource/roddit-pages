Skill levels - let it grow!

It seems to be common agreement that +level of skills is kind of mandatory on all gear where it is applicable.

I would suggest a new way to handle skill levels. Just remove the affix completely, but let your weapon grow!

You could link +level of skill with the experience you gained WITH THE WEAPON. So the longer you play with that weapon, the better it gets. You basically get used to the weapon and become more skillful.

It would also be a very interesting mechanic, because when you have a nice weapon with a lot of +skill levels you would have to balance on changing to another weapon. The new weapon would have better stats but would start with +0 skill level. So in the beginning, it would be a trade off, maybe even worse damage but a higher potential with increased experience.

Further, I would make the skill level bound to player+weapon. This means that when you sell, the skill level is lost and the buyer needs to “get used to” the weapon and improve skill level.

Finally, leveling weapons would be faster at higher tier content due to the increased exp gained.

What do you think?
