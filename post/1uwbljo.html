Low-Life Tech

I decided to try Atziri’s Communion. Before building around it, I was wondering if gameplay would be more stressful, since losing a good chunk of my energy shield would pose a bigger threat. And I was wondering about bleeding and poison bypassing my ES and killing me.

So, I tried a couple of maps, and, yes, I was less relaxed playing, since big hits to my ES freaked me out more… but I run convalescence, so I have a counter, as long as I pay attention to my ES.

Then, I played another map and got hit with a red projectile from an abyss monster that one-shot me while I was on full ES. This must have been high bleed damage, and it was partially my bad, since I’d swapped out my bleed and poison charms and forgot to put them back in.

Still, a big one-shot that wipes out most of my ES creates more of a problem, and, even with replacing the charms, I now have to worry about getting hit twice-in-a-row by bleed damage.

Sure, I managed to boost my overall damage by around 50% with the low-life tech, but I’m wondering if killing a boss in .75 seconds instead of 1 second is worth being less comfortable overall.

I’m not positive the extra damage is worth the extra vulnerability, especially since I was handling all content before the damage boost.

I was wondering what people think about the trade off, or if there’s other mitigation that might help. I’m at 8,500 ES and 60 Evasion. I literally didn’t die for the longest time doing 200% deli maps mixed with abysses and breaches and rituals, or doing super-juiced grand expeditions, so maybe I shouldn’t bother with the low-life tech.

On the other hand, at this stage of endgame, seeing just how far you can push your build is one of the few motivations left.
