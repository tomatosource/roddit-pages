Who else has screwed up this badly?…

So still mostly new to POE2 but been having a blast with it. I’m finally in the endgame and getting into crafting and markets to ensure I keep moving forward. The community has been great and supportive so I finally took a chance and heavily invested into crafting a new crossbow, you know something worth using or at least selling.

I ended up crafting two solid ones, one Ill use and one that just wasnt quite as good as mine but very close so it was to selel to hopefully make back some of the investment into this. I was super happy that I built a solid base to use and another that it was actually worth something to help negate the cost.

Based on comparables on the market I saw (Ruins of Aldur) a similar crossbow would go for 300-350 Divines, so I listed it, and figured I would lower it day by day if it didnt sell until I found the price sweet spot.

So I listed it thinking it would take time to sell… but then it sold instantly…. and it was at that moment I knew I messed up…. I accidentally listed it for Chaos orbs instead of Divines…. so someone got insanely lucky and has an insane crossbow, and Im broke and with a small stack of chaos orbs to show for it…. *Sighs*

Just wanted to share my tale to see if anyone else has suffered this, and as a warning to be careful when listing your stuff!
