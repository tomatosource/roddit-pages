Relative Room and Medallion Drop Chances

Hi everyone.

I spent a few hundred temples recording the relative weighting for temple rooms and medallions. THe method is as follows:

Open temple and record room tiles. Record how many rooms can drop “High” or “Low” chance medallions. Record medallions dropped during temple. Repeat.

This game three sets of data.

The first was the relative chance for any particular room to appear as a tile available for placement in the temple. These were only recorded when the Sacrifice Chamber had not been placed, as you may only have one of these.

The second was the generic chance for a “High” chnace or a “Low” chance to give a medallion. This could include a Level 3 Spymaster giving a room tile placement medallion instead of a Lock medallion.

Room Chance Descriptor Chance (%)
High 19.9
Low 3.7

The Third was the specific chance that a “High” chance room or a “Low” chance room would give their named medallion. For example, the chance that a Level 1 Alchemy Lab (low) would give a Quipolatl’s Medallion, or a Level 3 Spymaster (high) would give a Lock Medallion.

Room Chance Descriptor Chance (%)
High 12.7
Low 2.3
