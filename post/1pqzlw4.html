Is Kenley Jansen a Hall of Famer?

I was looking at reliever HOF bona fides the other day and saw that there are basically four current relievers that are in the discussion for the Hall. Chapman, Kimbrell, Diaz and Jansen. To me Kimbrell is a no doubt HOF. Depending on where Jansen ends up Kimbrell will either be 3rd or 4th all time in Saves, he’s made 9 All-Star Teams, 5 top-10 Cy Young finishes and 3 top-25 MVP’s.

Chapman is closer, but I still lean yes based on 8 All-Star Teams and a good chance at 400+ Saves by the time he’s done. Diaz has work to do, but he’s got time left to turn in 5-6 more good seasons.

Interestingly, there is a very clear line for relievers getting into the Hall in that all relief pitchers with 7 or more All-Star appearances are in (Rivera 13, Hoffman 7, Smith 7, Wagner 7, Fingers 7 and Gossage 7). Two more Relievers have 6 appearences (Eckersley and Sutter), but they also have at least 5 seasons in which they finished in the top-10 of CY voting. The 6 vs 7 line is fascinating to me because it leaves out K-Rod, Papelbon, and Nathan who all have a case, but K-Rod had more ups and downs and Pappelbon and Nathan just don’t have the longevity.

This brings me to Jansen. Barring injury, Jansen is going to end up with 500+ Saves and his ERA+ & WAR/IP are both better than Hoffman and Sutter. The problem is that he’s only made 4 All-Star teams and finished top-10 in CY voting just once. I don’t think he’s on the level of other HOF relievers, but I’m wondering what number of Saves will be high enough to not ignore?
