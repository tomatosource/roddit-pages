GIANNIS’ all-time ranks before turning 30

With the Greek Freak turning 30 last Thursday, one of the greatest young careers in NBA history has come to a close. He debuted in 2013 at age 18, the youngest player of the one and done era to that point (he’s now the 4th youngest since 2006). All stats are from the regular season and are from Statmuse.

GAMES – 811 (7TH)

MINUTES – 26606 (18TH)

POINTS – 19121 (7TH)

FREE THROWS – 4642 (10TH) / 6643 (7TH)

FIELD GOALS – 6974 (10TH) / 12742 (11TH)

-—–since 1982——–

REBOUNDS – 7953 (7TH)

TURNOVERS – 2446 (6TH)

DOUBLE-DOUBLES – 423 (9TH)

  • He’s the only player with 19000 points, 7900 rebounds, and 3900 assists before turning 30 since Statmuse tracked rebounds and assists.
  • Only 5 other players in history have accumulated those numbers plus 900 steals and 1000 blocks for their whole careers since blocks and steals became official.
  • No other player in history has matched Giannis’ career averages across the 5 stats (23.6⁄9.8⁄4.9⁄1.1⁄1.3). Larry Bird exceeds him in every measure except blocks (0.8).
  • No other player in history has averaged 25 ppg, 10 rpg, and 5 apg for more seasons (7, including this one). Jokic has 4 and Oscar had 3.
  • He leads the Bucks in all 5 categories except steals, where he trails Quinn Buckner by around 70. That record is falling in short order.
