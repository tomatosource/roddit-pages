The Golden State Warriors have advanced to the NBA Cup knockout stage as a result of winning West Group C

With Denver’s loss to Dallas, they now have 2 losses, which locks them at 2 wins maximum. New Orleans and Memphis also have 2 losses. Golden State has 3 wins, so they’re automatically above all three even if they lose their next game to Denver. Dallas only has 1 loss, but even if they were both tied in win count, Golden State would win the tiebreaker due to head to head.

I think Golden State is now the first team to advance to the NBA Cup knockout stage. Great recovery from last season when they nearly advanced but managed to clutch defeat from the jaws of victory against the Kings.
