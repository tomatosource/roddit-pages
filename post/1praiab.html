Why I keep coming back

I’ve been playing Path of Exile 2 for about a year now, and one of the main reasons I keep coming back is how much thinking it asks from you.

The problem solving around resistances, gear stats, and making skills actually work is what hooks me. You can’t just equip random upgrades and steamroll. You have to figure out why something feels bad and fix it.

This league is the first time I’m actually taking the time to properly learn and fix my character’s defenses. In previous leagues I’d always make sure my resistances were capped, but that was basically it. Everything else was just full glass cannon and hoping offense would carry me.

A good example is my Druid this league. He felt insanely squishy and fixing that turned into a huge undertaking. I had to dig into defensive layers and gear choices instead of just chasing damage. Last night I finally made some real progress and it felt great.

I also really enjoy starting with some guidance from an online build and then experimenting from there. I like tweaking things and making the build feel like it’s my own, for better or worse.

When things click in PoE2, it’s because you actually learned the systems and earned the power.

Just wanted to share my experience as it’s been mostly positive. The devs are doing a good job and I can’t wait to see how this game unfolds!
