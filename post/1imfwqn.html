The Hoop Collective Fans: Windy Tanks an Episode

(Pretext) I love listening to Windhorst, McMahon, and Bontemps get deep in the weeds of talking hoops. Bickering is there but usually in an entertaining way.

Today’s episode (2/10/2025) started off fiery from Windy for no reason and he TANKED THE FIRST 15 minutes being salty about.. people being too mean to the Mavericks for trading Luka?

The guys rightfully talked about how important AD getting injured this quickly is. It’s huge! Nobody blamed the Mavs for this injury but they added context of AD having injury issues. Windy basically said the injury is a non-topic in relation to the trade.. which is ridiculous! It’s bad luck, but obviously not unprecedented, and hugely impactful to how the 24-25 Mavs’ season goes. Rather than getting into the interesting dynamics here, Windy was SO quick to become whiny and defensive of the Mavs front office. He was called out on it quickly by Bontemps but kept finding ways to frame it in a combative and frankly childish way.

I found it weird and unenjoyable to listen to, and I typically love listening. Even if someone were to say, “let’s wash out AD’s injury history entirely bc of him being healthy last year, start fresh and analyze the trade from here..” this injury is massive!! By itself and in relation to the trade, whether you view it as a clear factor of hand-picking AD or purely bad luck, injuries happen. Now Luka (if he comes back from this calf injury right) is going to have a chance to quickly play weeks if not a month+ of games more than AD and the Mavs are going to have to find a way to scrap games together without 2 of their best 3 players.

Anyways, I wanted to use this post to engage in banter about the injury, about the Hoop Collective and anyone having a similar reaction, any stories of peoples’ favorite sports medial folks crashing out, and sports media interest in general.
