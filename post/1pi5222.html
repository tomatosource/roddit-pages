With Weapon Sets becoming more and more important, i think the onboarding and interface needs to be better

With druid seemingly being expected to alternate between shapeshifting and casting spells in human form and lots of QoL over the past patches (like Weapon locking), Weapon Sets seem to finally have matured.

But while the functionality is there, i don’t think the feature is obvious, intuitive or accessible enough.

A few problems:

  1. Problem: The weapon set points on the tree are insufficiently distinguished from each other and the “normal” points. Possible Solution: Stronger color contrast + different texture/ornamentation of the lines.

  2. Problem: Functionality is split between three menus, and sometimes hidden in submenus (Inventory, Skill Menu, Skill Tree).
    Possible Solution: Better inventory-like overview with intuitive visual structure where you assign skills left (Set 1), right (Set 2) or center (either)

  3. Problem: Respeccing Weapon Set Points can be awkward.
    Possible Solution: Option to “transform” an allocated normal skill point into a weapon set point, so you don’t have to respect the entire branch.

Overall i think the Skill menu needs an overhaul in general, and honestly should be full screen (or at least full screen minus inventory screen) that includes my proposed visual aid for allocating skills to weapon sets.
