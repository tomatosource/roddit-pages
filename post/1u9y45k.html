Melee improvement: Special dodge rolls?

Melee combat in POE2 needs improvement.
It is not that melee isnt viable - it just doesnt feel nice.
Getting ministunned in your attack animation, getting surrounded by too many mobs, so many anti-melee modifier and boss mechanics - holy 90% of the “The King in the Mists” fight was me waiting for the tornado to move away and since it follows you it was constantly on the boss you are trying to hit (visual clarity was also horrible in this fight)

Im sure that there might be a lot coming in future patches, as there are only melee weapons missing.

But perhaps one suggestion from my side for the surround issue:
Can we have special dodge rolls bound to melee weapons? I imagine something like a “Break through” for str melee weapons - Pushing through enemies and knocking them back.
Or for dex melee weapon “Slip through” - like a dodge but without collision.

In general, I like the idea to have special dodges for the individual weapon types. But let us first begin with melee :)
