JJ Redick does not get enough credit for being a great Head Coach.

we know Redick from his days as a star at Duke, as an elite shooter for a long time in the association, and from his podcasting and commentating days.

I hated him at Duke, gained respect for him in the NBA, and became a fan during his podcast days.

what he has done as head coach of the Lakers is nothing short of remarkable. he’s coaching a team with an aging LeBron, and with injuries to his other two best players in Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves.

he led the Lakers to 53 wins in the regular season despite only having a +1.7 difference in points per game. every other team that won that many had at least a +4 differential. earning the 4 seed in the tough Western conference is so small feat.

apparently I have to post a link to prove I am not AI - espn.com/nba/standings

he led his team to a first-round playoff victory despite not having his best player in Doncic, and missing Reaves for the first few games. the Lakers were substantial underdogs in the series, and were dogs in every game against Houston.

the Lakers are drawing dead against OKC, but he has his team playing hard and competitive ball despite getting a rough whistle from the refs.

a tip of the cap to Redick and his staff. give the man his flowers.

edit : to everyone saying he should be discredited for having LeBron on his team, this is a 41-year-old LeBron who played 60 games. he also gets the extra bonus of dealing with the Bronny attention.

Luka is hurt, Reaves was hurt in round one. take those two away and this is a tank-level roster.
