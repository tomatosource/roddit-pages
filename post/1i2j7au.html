Interesting Notes on Opponent Shooting the Last 15 Games…

I was curious where we’ve been giving up points in this stretch of the season, where the eye test gives the impression that the defense has been struggling. So I went to look at team stats. Particularly, I was interested in shooting from 2, seeing as that was the major gap between the two teams tonight.

https://www.nba.com/stats/teams/opponent-shooting?LastNGames=15&dir=D&sort=25-29%20ft.%20FG%20PCT

This may come as a surprise to some, but in the last 15 games, the Celtics have allowed literally the lowest opponent FG% from within 5 feet (57.7%; median 64.7%) in the league.

They’ve also allowed the lowest opponent FG% from 5-9 feet in this stretch, by three full percentage points (35.6%; median 44.7%).

From 15-19 feet, they’ve allowed the 4th lowest FG% in the league (32.5%; median 43.1%). Again, way ahead of the curve. Beyond that, it’s mostly all three pointers and those numbers are middling for us, not remarkable.

But from 10-14 feet, the Celtics have allowed literally the highest opponent FG% in the league the last 15 games (53.7%; median 43.1%). 2nd highest is the Blazers at 51.1, 3rd highest is the Warriors at 49.2, after that no one over 48. We’re a legitimate outlier.

I found that to be an insanely glaring difference between ranges. I wonder if its down to scheme in some way. But we are getting absolutely COOKED in the close midrange (for reference, free throw line is 15 feet). While locking down seemingly everything else inside the 3 point line.
