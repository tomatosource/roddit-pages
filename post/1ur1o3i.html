Similarities between Jaylen Brown trade and Russell Westbrook Trade

I can’t help but notice that Jaylen Brown Trade and Russell Westbrook( to the Rockets from OKC) have many similarities between them.’

Sixth, their teams became more efficient with them off the floor.
