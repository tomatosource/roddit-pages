Jaylen Brown literally plays like Kobe and I can’t be the only one noticing this.

Now first off I will say that Jaylen Brown is a miles better playmaker than Kobe and is also a much better teammate, but I am talking play style-wise, in many aspects, they are effectively identical.

I first realized that both of them are elite mid-range shot creators. Then I thought about the nasty dunk highlights which JB has, not dissimilar to Kobe, (Both are elite slashing guard-forwards). Also the killer mentality which they have, and the pride they take in guarding the best player on the opposite team.

Even if you want to argue based on narratives, Jaylen was at most considered a 1a 1b option until he got the chance and now he has been exceptional at it.

If you look at it through the eye test, it gives a lot of similarities. The way they speed past bigger, slower players and bully their way through smaller ones is very similar. Jaylen often puts his head down and tries to score over multiple defenders, (not saying it’s a good thing) which is not dissimilar to Kobe. This year we have seen plenty of games where JB alone kept the score afloat shooting whatever difficult shot it is until the rest of the team got their groove back. This head down “I am going to carry until y’all start scoring” gives me Kobe vibes.

In short they have:

  • Basically the same build (Kobe was a little lighter)
  • Elite midrange jumpers (practically AND aesthetically)
  • Excellent two-way impact
  • Fantastic slashers and very athletic
  • Decent three-point shooters who can make tough threes when needed
  • Get to the free throw line a lot (Something JB has been getting only since this year)

The more I think about it the more I feel JB is the closest player in this era, or for that matter in any era, to Kobe. I’m surprised people don’t talk about this. That’s probably because everyone downplays JB and hates the Celtics, and I have to hear BS where people claim that Shai is the closest player to Kobe smh.

Anyway would love to hear ya’lls thoughts. Go Celtics!
