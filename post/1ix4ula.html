Jayson Tatum in February: 27-8-6.8 on 60% TS (8-1 record).

I know that Tatum’s numbers this season aren’t jumping off the screen in a way that SGA, Jokic and Giannis’ are but the mini leap he has taken this season has been tangible and the eye test fully confirms it. Beyond improvements to his jumper/release and ability to drive and kick, his overall vision and playmaking from the top of the key has reached a near-elite level.

Further context: Between 2019-2024, Tatum had 7 games of double digit assists. In this season alone, he already has 6. And anyone who has watched these games can attest that he could have had even more because he creates a lot of assist opportunities. (Source: Tatum game logs https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/tatumja01/gamelog/2025/)

Playmaking is not the easiest skill to improve because it involves so many different variables - handle, vision, timing, etc. Tatum came into the league as a slightly below average passer and watching him improve over the years has been incredible to behold. I think he’s confidently reached a level as a high level playmaker. If you compare him to other playmaking wings (excluding Bron/Luka who are in the top tier of passers int he league), Tatum stands at the top. Adding in his defensive versatility, improved scoring at the rim and 3 point volume and rebounding ability, he is a very unique player. There’s not another guy in the league doing exactly what he does.

Since the mini slump that the Celtics had, Tatum’s usage has increased to &gt;30% and has yielded positive results. The offense looks extremely efficient when the offense is centered around Tatum PnRs from the top of the key - yielding high assist games with good Ast:TO ratios in the last month as well.

On a selfish level, Tatum’s numbers would definitely look better and closer to the top MVP guys if they played through him as much as the top guys, but the team is looking damn near as good as last season, so who really cares as long as they win. Fans gravitate towards the shit talk and outward charisma of Ant, Luka, KD, etc. (although they hate Haliburton a little irrationally too and he talks shit). He had a streak of shit talking back when Ime was coach, but Mazzulla has turned him into more of a silent killer.
