If Bismack Biyombo does not return to the Spurs/another team, that will be the end of any former Charlotte Bobcats in the NBA

Biyonbo joined the Spurs this season, and became their starting Center after Victor Wembanyama went out with pulmonary issues.

While he is currently set to be a free agent again, he is unlikely to be retained by the Spurs.

11 years after their conversion to the Charlotte Hornets, he is the last remaining NBA player with a full season played for the Charlotte Bobcats, who ceased to exist after 2014.

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/CHA/2014.html

Note: Garrett Temple is a 39 year old free agent and likely done (he played 11 games for Charlotte in 2011). Cody Zeller (2013⁄2014 rookie) was waived by the Houston Rockets to create roster space this season, but did not play in any games in 2024⁄2025.
