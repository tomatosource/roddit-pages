There have been 19 Game 7s in the NBA finals. The team to force a game 7 (meaning they won game 6) has only won the championing 9⁄19 times. However, in the past 40 years, they’ve won 5⁄6 times.

After reading this NBA article, I started wondering whether it was the team forcing the game 7 that had “momentum”, or if the team that “choked” the series ended up winning. To my surprise, it’s a little less than a coin flip (granted half these games took place before the NBA-ABA merger), with the team leading the series having a slight advantage.
