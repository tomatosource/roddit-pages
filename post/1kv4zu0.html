Infinity isn’t fun

Learn from what wow taught the devs in legion: infinite grinds are not fun.

I’m looking at you, atlas map. Poe1 has the benefit of immediate feedback to the player: 1 map is 1 point. There’s a web of maps that you can tick off. 10 maps is 1 maven challenge. X Maps for a pinnacle. While you can run maps infinitely, the actual game tells you how far you gotta go. Finite system for infinite gameplay.

Poe2 does not succeed in giving players control over their grind. There is no immediate feedback for progress, and progress feels unsatisying / distant. On top of this, I don’t enjoy trudging towards towers to setup temporary milkers. This is chore gameplay and it doesn’t go away. I want immediate fun /juice. Infinite system for infinite gameplay.

You know what would be fun? The atlas is blank. You slot a crafted, juicer tablet into it and it reveals a finite amount of map nodes. You can craft the tablet, alter its topography, major influence, and your atlas passive tree affects what you see. At the start, 1 tablet is 1 point. Let me run what I want, ggg. Give me immediate feedback for my time. Give me control to see the content I enjoy.
