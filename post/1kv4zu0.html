Infinity isn’t fun

Learn from what wow taught the devs in legion: infinite grinds are not fun.

I’m looking at you, atlas map. Poe1 has the benefit of immediate feedback to the player: 1 map is 1 point. There’s a web of maps that you can tick off. 10 maps is 1 maven challenge. X Maps for a pinnacle. While you can run maps infinitely, the actual game tells you how far you gotta go. Finite system for infinite gameplay.

Poe2 atlas does not succeed in giving players a finite system for an infinite grind. There is no immediate feedback for progress, and progress feels unsatisying / distant. Towers dont quite hit the same niche as waystones; I view them as chore gameplay and it doesn’t go away. I want immediate fun / juice. Infinite system for infinite gameplay.

You know what would be fun? The atlas is blank. You slot a crafted, juicer tablet into it and it reveals a finite amount of map nodes. You can craft the tablet, alter its topography, major influence, and your atlas passive tree affects what you see. At the start, 1 tablet is 1 point. Let me run what I want, ggg. Give me immediate feedback for my time. Give me control to see the content I enjoy. Please cut down on travel time between towers at least. I spend more time annoyed finding a farming spot than i do enjoying the farm.
