Teams who were the 1 seed for 3 or more consecutive seasons (since 1976 NBA merger)

  • 1980-1982 Boston Celtics. In these 3 seasons, the Celtics nabbed the East’s top seed all 3 years, winning 61, 62 and 63 games. They lost 2 Eastern Conference Finals to the Philadelphia 76ers and won the championship in 1981.
  • 1984-1988 Boston Celtics. With the only gap year being 1983 when Philadelphia had the 1 seed, Boston was on top for 5 more straight seasons, averaging 61.6 wins. They made 4 finals and won 2 championships in this stretch.
  • 1982-1990 Los Angeles Lakers. For 9 straight seasons, the Lakers held the top spot in the Western Conference. They averaging 60 wins during this stretch, made 7 finals and won 4 of them.
  • 1996-1998 Chicago Bulls. The Bulls second 3peat dominated with 72-10, 69-13 and 62-20 records, capping off a historic run.
  • 2008-2010 Los Angeles Lakers. Kobe and Pau’s Lakers didn’t win less than 57 games any year in this stretch, went to 3 straight finals and won back-to-back in 2009 and 2010.
  • 2015-2017 Golden State Warriors. With 2 MVP seasons from Steph Curry and later adding MVP Kevin Durant, the Warriors absolutely dominated going 207-39 during this 3 year period, making 3 straight finals and winning 2 of them.

The next team with the opportunity to accomplish this task is the current Oklahoma City Thunder. If they are the 1 seed in the Western Conference next year, they will have done so for 3 straight years posting 57-25 and 68-14 records in 2024 and 2025 respectively, with a championship in 2025.

The only seasons on this list in which these teams did not make at least the conference finals are the 1990 Lakers and 2024 Thunder.

The teams on this list are considered some of the greatest teams of all-time. Bird Celtics, Showtime Lakers, Jordan’s Bulls, Kobe’s Lakers, Warriors dynasty. If the Thunder are at the top of the Western Conference next year and go back-to-back, do you think we will look back on them in a similar way?

Also, in case anyone was curious, no the Spurs never were the 1 seed 3 years in a row. They almost had it 2011-2014 but in 2013 were 2 games back from the Thunder, and from 2001-2006 they were 3 games behind the Kings (2002), 1 game behind the Wolves (2004) and 3 games behind the Suns (2005).

The 2000-2002 Lakers won 3 titles, but in 2001 were 2 games back from the Spurs and in 2002 were 3 games back from the Kings. Shaq and Kobe Lakers are also considered one of the greatest teams of all-time.

The 2019-2021 Bucks almost pulled it off as well, with the 1 seed in 2019 and 2020, and 3 games behind the 76ers in 2021 the year they won the title. Between this title and Giannis’ 2 MVP seasons and DPOY, this stretch by the Bucks was extremely dominant but their losses in the playoffs to the Raptors and Heat really hurts the discourse of this being considered an all-time team. Had they had more postseason success, I think they would be talked about that way.

If you have any other interesting pulls from 3 year stretches I’d love to hear them. This is an extremely hard thing for a team to accomplish where so much can change in the NBA in 3 years.
