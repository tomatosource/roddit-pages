Collecting Old Radio Broadcasts Of Games

Hi there,

I’m curious to know whether anybody else out there is interested in collecting old radio broadcasts of games.

I know a few people who have somewhat large collections, but I’m always interested in meeting more.

I’ve got quite a few, including a little over 2,000 unique games in MP3 format, as well as an ever growing collection of high quality transfers in FLAC format (currently 410 files and growing; we’re talking over 390 GB of those alone).

I also collect old video, though it’s generally easier to find those games on places like YouTube.

I know that quite a few games were recorded, especially when trading circles started up sometime in the 1970s. However, it’s not always that easy to find others interested in the old broadcasts.
