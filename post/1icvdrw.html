Not exactly a basketball hotbed but from 1952 to the end of the 2023⁄24 season, Icelandic leagues had had a total of two former NBA players play there, and one of them was Icelandic. This season four American former NBA players are playing in the country.

Pétur Guðmundsson, of 2K fame, became the first after playing in the NBA in the 80’s. Next up was former Nets Kurk Lee who played in Iceland in 1999. Took 25 years for the next to arrive and then they arrived in a bundle. Ty-Shon Alexander (went to the Finals with Suns in 21), Justin Taylor James, Jeremy Pargo and former “Trust the Process” player Tony Wroten all showed up in the last couple of month. While Pargo and Wroten are still waiting for their Letter Of Clearance, Alexander and James, though decent, haven’t exactly set the world on fire with their play. Wroten might though, considering he’s playing with one of the worst teams in the second-tier league.
