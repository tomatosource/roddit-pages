Why does the Smithing Ascendency, Smith of Kitava, not have any effects that play around with Weapon and Armour quality?

Quality

Gemling grants all gems +10% quality.

I think Smith could afford to give all Weapon and Armour +10% quality as well. It’s called Blacksmith’s Whetstone, after all. Armourer’s Scrap too, if we’re willing to stretch it a little.

Also consider alt quality for Weapons and Armours too, with possible similar effects to poe1’s alt quality on weapons and armour. Could be made like Pathfinder’s concoction node where you choose one of three choices to give to your gear.

Temper Weapon could be like Resonating Shield

And while we’re at it, let Temper Weapon release shockwaves too, both to make it less clunky to use in-combat and for the Rule-Of-Cool as well.

Like Resonating Shield, you hammer the weapon and things explode around you. The animation doesn’t even need to change.

Smith’s Masterwork was made for a previous era

And personally I feel like the White Armour requirement for Smith’s Masterwork is too restrictive. Without taking a bunch of points (and thus preventing us from experimenting with the rest of the ascendency), the node is extremely weak, which leads to a feast or famine type of choice. Either heavily invest into it, or don’t take it at all.

Honestly, with things like Fist of Stone in the game now, I don’t really see too much of a negative just removing the +200 armour effect and allowing people to wear rare armours, would it? Would be cool to allow uniques too, but idk about the balancing for that.

Even if this doesn’t happen, this +200 armour is still quite outdated after the changes to base types in 0.5. Other items got an overall 20-30% increase in max armour in the endgame, while Smith’s value remains unchanged.

Anyway that was my thoughts on Smith. There’s other ascendencies that need a balance pass as well, but I just played Smith so I gave my thoughts on it.
