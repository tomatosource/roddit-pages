My first week flipping currency has helped me re-engage with endgame

I didn’t understand it and while some videos helped, there wasn’t a solid, fast way to learn, so I’m going to explain it as simply as possible:

how to flip currency

You pick one or more currency. I’m going to use Perfect Jeweler’s Orb.

Let’s say the current exchange rate is 33ex for one PJ Orb.

  1. Put a listing for 30ex per, so buy 3 for 90ex.

  2. The exchange rate will fluctuate, and eventually the price will go down to 30ex and your orbs will sell.

  3. Put a listing selling them for 34ex per, so those 3 would be 102.

  4. The exchanges rate will fluctuate, and sell when it hits 34:1.

If you do this with all 10 slots, you’ll make 40ex for exactly 30k gold. There are better options, I’m sure, but I’ve stuck to one or two currencies. The closer you get to market value, the faster they flip, but the more gold it costs. But if you bet too high or too low, you’re not going to be making any profit.

You’re providing a service of sort (microscopically) but it allows people to exchange currency fast for slightly lower than market value.

My first day, I turned 120ex to ~800, and only by checking in every now and again, with a total time online of about 45 minutes, but I also made mistakes as it was my first day.

I’m a dad gamer, and so I play only a few hours after the kids go down. It can be hard to keep up with the leagues, but currency flipping changed that for me. I play at night to earn gold, and then check in a few times a day to relist currency exchanges, and while it’s about 1 Div/day at the start, it’s more than I usually can get otherwise!
