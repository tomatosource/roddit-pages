Not the same team

So we know we lost talent when Jrue, Al, Luke and KP left, we certainly lost experience. i see posts bemoaning that the replacements aren’t cutting it. I too notice Vooch is butt cheeks and Hauser and White are no shows. Here’s the thing though, this wasn’t a problem during the regular season. yeah playoff basketball yadda yadda. What I notice is that this is not the same team. These are not the rotations that were working during the season. This was not the minutes distribution. Yes teams shorten the bench in the playoffs, but considering this team’s regular season success was predicated on our bench getting experience and deeper this might have been the worst strategy for this team. They look like a bad version of a previous Celtic’s team with worse personnel rather than the team they were all year because that is what Joe decided to run. I was quite excited when Tatum returned and was facilitating for the team that had played together all year and vooch was injured not forcing a fit because he was traded. Instead they decided to pretend they could shorten the bench and run the old offense and expect the same results. It’s not the same team we watched all year and it is not the team of yesteryears. We are losing because the team doesn’t trust young players that improved faster than expected? WTF? This square peg round hole team strategy is not working. Listen I love me some crazy joe memes but this is a lot on him being stubborn about doing things the way they have always been done and not trusting what he did all year. The fact he doesn’t adjust does not bode well for fixing what ails them.
