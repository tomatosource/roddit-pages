Jewels just have way too many affixes.

Looking at rolling a 4-affix jewel for your build?

Well, I hope you are very, very lucky, or very, very patient, or very very rich.

Comparing the modifiers list for Jewels to other items slots reveals an interesting observation.

Amulets, a traditionally ‘diverse’ item, has 12 Prefixes, and 22 Suffixes.

Rings have 13 Prefixes and 18 Suffixes

Jewels have 27 Prefixes and 39 Suffixes!!

Is it any wonder rolling, rerolling, combing 3 into one, rerolling, leads to 99.9% bad jewels?

Now, it may be that someone is thinking:

“Yeah, that’s because jewels are powerful., and need to be mitigated.”

To which I would argue:

“Control the power of an item via the values of the affixes, not by RNG-bloating the options available.”

We only have 3 base jewel types, Ruby Emerald and Sapphire, for Strength, Dex, and Int builds.

Why not split the jewels up, like every other item slot, into the same dual attributes,

Emerald: Dex

Ruby: Strength

Sapphire: Intelligence

Amethyst: Dex/Str

Topaz: Dex/Int

Agate: Str/Int

Garnet: Str/Dex/Int

And break down the affixes to a more manageable number of choices?

IF, as I side-barred earlier, the argument is that jewels would be too powerful if you could more easily (but still not really) get 4 good affixes, balance the affixes!
