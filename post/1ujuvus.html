Lebron James

Sitting here thinking about it now, we could be real contenders again if LeBron signed with the Celtics on a Vet Minimum and we decided to keep Jaylen Brown. How likely is it? Not really what so ever, but a White, Jaylen, Jason, and James team would be hard to beat thinking from a defensive aspect. I know LeBron is the defender he used to be, but is still an above average defender.

On the other side of the ball, he can get to the rim at will which is going to open everything up for the Celtics for shooting from the outside. We would be able to get way better looks as Jaylen, Jayson, White, Prichard, Hauser would be able to spot up almost everywhere playing off of the drives.

All in all, never been a big fan of Lebron but could help us out tremendously. Likely….. no, I think he’s going to the Warriors or back to Cleveland.
