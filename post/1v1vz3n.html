2026 r/baseball Power Rankings – All-Star Break / Week 17: 5th Annual Bias Analysis, Come Find Out How We Really Feel About Your Favorite Team!

Hey Sportsfans — it’s time for Week 17 of r/baseball Power Rankings: Please enjoy these different baseball numbers as the voters go on their own break.

This week – for the fifth year in a row – we ranked the 30 MLB teams based off how much we liked them in an attempt to identify, in an unserious manner, the collective voter bias.

We do this for four reasons:

  • It’s fun

  • To give the proper ranks a chance to shake up

  • Ranking based off a mere 3 games would otherwise be a waste of time

  • To identify our “bias” and see how it changes year to year.

If you take this overly seriously, you will be asked to sit in the corner.

TRANSPARENCY: This link will show you who voted each team where and has added neat statistics!

The delta change refers to last year’s bias vote.

If something is a little messed up, feel free to pester me let me know.
