The 10 biggest 2026 FA contracts so far

https://preview.redd.it/3dfpufuts09h1.png?width=848&format=png&auto=webp&s=fbe4fcd8dd25090750387f827faf77761434e685

I had started feeling like last year’s big FAs were cursed. Like they were mostly doing worse than expected. After pulling the numbers, I think it’s just Bichette and Tucker coloring my view (and to a lesser extent, Valdez). Everyone else has more or less performed as projected, or better. Schwarber, Cease, Bellinger and Suarez especially are doing great so far.

Of course on pace projections don’t mean much (if anything), there’s still ~half a season to go, you can’t judge a 5 (or even 3) year contract after a few months, etc etc etc. I just wanted to see if there was anything to my gut feeling. I went back and forth on using top 10 by AAV for this, but I landed on total salary because big numbers are more fun to work with.

I know that Bichette switching to third (and then back again? I’m confused) influences his fWAR somehow, since shortstops are more valuable than 3Bs by default. I don’t know how to untangle that and I should get back to work, so I just made a note of it. Even with that in mind, I think it’s safe to say he has grossly underperformed so far.

You could quibble about Schwarber being more of an extension than an FA, but keeping him in the table lets me avoid including Josh Naylor. As a Mariners fan, I really want to avoid including Josh Naylor in this exercise.

Source: various Fangraphs tables. I’m using preseason ZIPS projections.
