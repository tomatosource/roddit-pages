The most consistent high quality drops available in the game right now (in my opinion)

Out of everything I’ve tried including maps, pinnacle t4’s, Sekhamas, trials etc the best loot is definitely from expeditions, and ONLY from frigid bluffs, the other two are very hit and miss. Lush isle can be good for farming xp and barren Attoll can be okay if you’ve got enough modifiers but the most consistent by far is the frigid bluffs.

This is even from farming the absolute shit out of maps. Like 60-100% quant build up from precursors and lvl 80/81s with hybrid quant rarity. All these done with all various delirium setups ranging from maxing out to 150% deliriousness with suffering/isolation. The best of these was from the 50% emotion plus 2 fears. Basically the more modifiers on rares the better.

TLDR: If you’re over the shitty and ridiculously priced crafting in this game and you’re just getting really crappy drops while still seemingly juicing the crap out of maps then you should try running some frigid bluffs expedition. The currency drops arnt that good but the rolls on rares are the best in the game I’ve found and the most consistent.

Be aware though you’ll have to be pretty tanky and high damage if you don’t wanna keep dying but it’s worth it.
