Among the Top 3 seeds of East and West last year, only the OKC Thunder and Milwaukee Bucks didn’t have a Christmas game scheduled

Usually reserved for the top teams and the marquee teams, the Christmas day slate of the NBA is a gift that fans look forward to unwrap. Playing on Christmas is considered an accomplishment to some, as it means you’re hot shit if you’re playing on Christmas.

2024 Christmas day schedule:

  • Spurs @ Knicks (East #2 last year)
  • Wolves (West #3 last year) @ Mavericks
  • Sixers @ Celtics (East #1 last year)
  • Lakers @ Warriors
  • Nuggets (West #2 last year) @ Suns

The obvious omissions are the West #1 OKC Thunder and East #3 Milwaukee Bucks.

Giannis and the Bucks have been a fixture for years since he has established himself as a superstar in the league. He was approaching the level of LeBron, Steph and KD as fixtures for the Xmas day games. His streak is broken this year because of back-to-back first round exits. Though I would reckon that is due to Giannis’ injuries.

OKC Thunder on the other hand didn’t get a spot because they don’t have the aura or the rizz as kids call it this day. They simply don’t have the it factor. Market is a factor, but KD and Russ played in the same market. They were always playing Christmas. Shai simply don’t have “it”.

&nbsp;

Poetic justice though, these two teams that were left off eventually played in the final of the NBA Cup. Two All-NBA first teamers and MVP candidates not playing in Christmas? The writers say, put them in the NBA Cup final.
