Jaylen Brown’s impact on Boston can’t be measured by stats alone

As a Black Celtics fan from Dorchester, this trade hurts for reasons beyond basketball.

I know at the end of the day it’s a business. Brad Stevens has a job to do, and maybe a few years from now we’ll look back and say this was the right move (highly doubt, especially for perc P) But today, this one just hurts.

People outside of Boston love to reduce our city to its history with race. They bring up busing, redlining, and joke that there are “no Black people in Boston” or that Celtics fans are all racist.

That’s why Jaylen Brown meant so much to me.
He didn’t just play for Boston. He embraced communities like Dorchester, Roxbury, and Mattapan. He showed up at schools, basketball clinics, community events, and youth programs. He spoke about educational equity, invested in young people, and consistently used his platform to support Black youth in Boston. It reminded me of the kind of community connection that made Reggie Lewis so beloved here.

When I worked at the Roxbury YMCA, JB came to spend time with the kids in our youth basketball league. He wasn’t just checking a box. He stayed, talked with them, answered every question, took pictures, encouraged them, and made every kid feel like they mattered. For a lot of those kids, seeing someone who looked like them, believed in them, and chose to spend time with them meant more than they’ll probably ever realize.
That’s what people outside of Boston don’t always see.

I know it probably sounds ridiculous to some people to be this emotional over a sports trade, but this feels like losing a close loved one. When I saw the headline, I honestly was emotionally brought back to some of the sadness I felt when we lost Terrence Clarke. I knew Terrence personally, and seeing another person who inspired so many young Black athletes from Boston leave our city’s basketball story is hard to put into words. I know the situations are completely different, but the feeling of loss is similar.

Last night I burned my custom “In Brad We Trust” hat. I haven’t eaten in almost 12 hours. I’ll probably laugh at myself next week, but
today I’m just sad.

Thank you for everything, No. 7.
Boston won’t forget you. ❤️
