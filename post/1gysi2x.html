LaMelo Ball is currently on pace for the FGA per 100 possessions single season record (since 1973)

This is a post I absolutely never thought I’d write, but last night LaMelo Ball’s 50-piece on 38 FGA moved him up to 35.02 FGA per 100 possessions on the season, barely surpassing 2005-06 Kobe Bryant’s 35.01 FGA/100p.

Ball’s USG% this season is currently 5th all time behind 2016-17 Russ, 2018-19 Harden, 2022-23 Giannis, and 2005-06 Kobe… sitting at 38.52%

On the season, Melo is averaging 30.2ppg on 24.0 FGA and just 5.8 FTA, leading to a 56.9 TS% that is a fraction of a percent behind league average, on a team where he is often being asked to do everything for his team.

Is it sustainable? Probably not, but it is insanely fun to watch the guy blossom into an offensive superstar this year.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/leaders/fga_per_poss_season.html This stats was not tracked, at least on basketball - reference, prior to 1973-74, so undoubtedly there are some Wilt seasons in the mix, maybe another from that era.
