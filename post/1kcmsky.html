What are your most unique half-baked trade ideas this summer if the Shams report turns out to be true?

Since we don’t play till Monday I’m gonna start a little offseason style post just for the sake of fun discussion, basically if the news is true, and we can assume the trade candidates are the obvious 3 guys we’ve kinda known all along(Jrue/KP/Sam), what are some of your trade scenarios you wouldn’t mind playing out?

I’ll list our trade assets for reference

Players: Jrue, KP, Hauser

Picks: 3 firsts(2025/2027/2031 own), 5 seconds

Okay, I’ll list my favorite 3 scenarios, curious to see what yall come up with

Scenario 1:

Celtics send Hauser and our 2025 first unprotected to the kings for Keon Ellis

Honestly I have a hard time gauging Ellis’s value, all I know is I absolutely love him and think he’d be an amazing Celtic. Maybe since the kings have so many guards now they might be motivated to move him? Idk

Scenario 2:

Celtics trade Jrue plus a 2025 and a 2027 first to the mavs. Celtics send 2 seconds to the magic

Magic send Anthony black and Caleb Houston to Boston

Mavs send klay to the magic and Pj Washington to Boston

So basically the Celtics turn jrue, 2 firsts, and 2 seconds into PJ Washington and Anthony black. This is great for us imo as we get a little cheaper, add black as a future jrue replacement, and could immediately start PJ and move brown back to the 2. Magic add a first and klay which vastly improves their shooting, and the mavs turn klay and PJ into jrue and a first, which actually helps them quite a bit when fully healthy

Scenario 3: porzingis and a 2027 first for Jalen duren

Pistons made a huge step, but they still need shooting and I’ve heard from a lot of fans they aren’t actually thrilled with duren. We add a young promising big we can craft into what we want, Detroit fixes their shooting in a huge way and turns into a more serious playoff team

Thoughts? What are some of your half baked trade ideas?
