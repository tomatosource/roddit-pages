Opinion: Why we should build similar to the 17-18 Celtics

Let me preface by saying that I trust Brad Stevens when it comes to roster construction. This is purely my opinion on how we should take lessons from a previously well-constructed team for the next complete iteration of the Celtics.

If you go back to the 17-18 Celtics roster, there was a lot of promise. The year before, the Celtics finished with 51 wins and a 1st seed in the Eastern Conference led by Isaiah Thomas before being gentlemen’s swept in the ECF by the LeBron led Cavs the following year. Despite this, Boston brought back just 4 guys from that roster to the following year (Horford, Smart, Rozier, and Brown). That eventful offseason led to the signing of Gordon Hayward, a small forward in his prime fresh off an all star appearance in the loaded West. The drafting of Jayson Tatum. The trading of Avery Bradley for Marcus Morris, and the trading of Isaiah Thomas and Jae Crowder for Kyrie Irving.

The starting lineup on opening night was Kyrie Irving, Jaylen Brown, Jayson Tatum, Gordon Hayward, and Al Horford. The notable bench pieces were Smart, Rozier, Morris, and Baynes. To me, this was such an ideal roster for both the short term and long term. You had the “win-now” players like Kyrie, Hayward, and Horford along with the future stars (Brown and Tatum) combined with studs off the bench Smart and Rozier. Combine this with great coaching and team management, the Celtics were well on their way to a dynasty in my opinion. (We all know what happened next :(

I believe Brad Stevens should take a similar approach to constructing the next iteration of the Celtics. When Tatum gets back our “win-now” guys are Tatum, Brown, and White. TBD who the future stars are but honestly it might be difficult to get those without a lottery pick. Hugo seems very promising with his NBA ready defense but he is raw offensively ( perhaps his ceiling is another D White while his floor is Marcus Smart). I feel like you could justify tanking this year since you cannot contend even in a weak east. And yes, I believe there is no justifiable reason to bring Tatum back this season. This could yield us a top 3 pick in a loaded draft class (Peterson and Boozer) can be franchise altering pieces. You will also have greater flexibility to address front court issues given we are under the second apron and when Simon’s’ contract expires we will be under the tax.
