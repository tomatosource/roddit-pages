GGG needs to rethink how punishment works in POE 2

As per title I think GGG is operating on a wrong philosophy. If we take games traditionally considered punishing, difficult or rewarding but still fun like Soulsborne, they operate on a simple premise.

The game mechanics you face are HARD and COMPLEX requiring attention and timing, that’s it, that’s the main difficulty. What they also do is punish, traditionally in two ways, you lose the accumulated resource​ used to level up, and you lose time to run back to the bosses. The first loss however is virtual, because you level ups stay after death meaning you just need to spend it before going into a Dangerous situation. If you do lose is it’s almost always easy to farm.

When I see Jonathan and Mark Talking about the need to have costs to losses they often do so to justify mechanics such as heavy stun on sprint, loss of everything that goes on a map, loss of currency when crafting, risk in interacting with crafting. Which also are all essentially losses of time.

What GGG misses on my estimation is that their game has TOO MANY moving parts ​for punishment to feel rewarding to overcome.

If I die to a boss in a soulsborne game I lost maximum 4⁄5 minutes on a boss attempt plus whatever my resource amount for level up was.

In POE 2 we have SO MANY systems where loss is experienced that a bad streak feels like bullying.

I can fail a craft, lose a map, get shitty drops from a pinnacle encounter, die again cause of a fat finger on spacebar and sprint kills me, bad temple RNG.

In a game with so many moving parts experiencing 4 or 5 of those feels like bullying.

I think GGG needs to make content more challenging AND rewarding while giving WAY more freedom to the player.

Give us more portals on maps, not 6 maybe in T15 but two? Same with pinnacle encounters at higer level, if I can only see the arbiter once after 5 hours of gameplay, and I haven’t seen the fight before and get oneshot and now I lost TIME, FRAGMENTS, POSSIBLE DROPS.

That is not difficulty it’s insult to injury.

THIS IS AN ENTERTAINMENT PRODUCT. GGG IS IN THE ENTERTAINMENT BUSINESS.

We need better challenges we want to get a weekend off for not 15 layers of getting kicked in the nards.
