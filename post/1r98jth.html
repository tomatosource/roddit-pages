+Skill Levels should not show up until the end of campaign or at least be tied to a special currency or endgame mechanic.

So I have played every league since the EA release and a very consistent problem with the itemization in this game has been +Skill Levels. That affix is so disgustingly strong that it makes itemization in the game feel completely annoying in so many ways. This becomes very apparent during the campaign but also the worst part of gearing in maps. Its the only real thing that matters and the difference in power is so strong you basically have to forgo actual base upgrades because skill levels is the best modifier in the game. You can have an amazing new weapon but it doesnt matter because if it doesnt out pace +2 gem levels than you are basically nerfing yourself by upgrading. And the downside of the affix is just things cost more mana which isnt a hard problem to fix. They need to do something with this affix because it hurts the progression of a character so much especially at the start of a league where resources are scarce.

The best solution in my opinion would be to tied the affix to an endgame mechanic like a Trial or special map mechanic. I dont want it removed because I think having the ability to juice your gems is important but that should be something to help the ceiling of progression and not be a consistent thing to worry about throughout your progression.
