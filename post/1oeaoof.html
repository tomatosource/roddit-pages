More Info from Porter-Billups Leadership Academy’s website:

In 2006, NBA star — and now Portland Trail Blazers Head Coach — Chauncey Billups joined forces with PBLA. In 2024, Coach Ricardo Patton joined PBLA as an executive director and Coach Lonnie Porter moved into the Founder Emeritus role. Coach Porter, Coach Billups and Coach Patton credit their success to the people who challenged them to work hard and get an education at a young age. The Academy is a way for both men to do for low-income youth what others did for them.
