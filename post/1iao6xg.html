If Norman Powell is named an All-Star, he would join Pascal Siakam, Fred VanVleet as the sixth All-Star to come from the 2019 Raptors championship team.

Norman Powell is having a career-year, averaging 23.9 points, 3.5 rebounds, 2.2 assists, 1.3 steals, and 3.5 made threes per game.

After the Raptors 2019 championship season, Pascal Siakam (2020) and Fred VanVleet (2022) became first-time All Stars. The roster already had 2 current (Lowry, Leonard) and 1 former (Gasol) All-Star on their roster. If Powell is named an NBA All-Star, he would be the sixth overall and third first-timer from that roster.

Here is a look at their depth chart looking back (in terms of their accolades throughout their careers):

Kyle Lowry (All-Star, All-NBA)

Danny Green (All-Defence)

Kawhi Leonard (FMVP, DPOY, Steals Leader, All-Defence, All-Star, All-NBA)

Pascal Siakam (MIP, All-Star, All-NBA)

Marc Gasol (DPOY, All-Defence, All-Star, All-NBA)

Fred VanVleet (All-Star)

Serge Ibaka (Blocks Leader, All-Defence)

OG Anunoby (Steals Leader, All-Defence)

Norman Powell

That kind of depth is remarkable.
