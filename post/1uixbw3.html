The possibility of trading Jaylen Brown and putting all the chips on Tatum and why that could be a franchise altering mistake.

I have been on the “keep Jaylen Brown” side since the beginning of this storyline. I didn’t want them to trade for Giannis for the same reasons this community has already discussed (Age, durability issues, etc.) At the same time I have thought that if you really HAVE to trade Brown then fine trade him for Giannis. Now what concerns me is all of these rumors of different trade packages we keep hearing about. All of these packages fall short of what a trade for Giannis could have given us.

What I feel hasn’t been discussed enough is the ramifications of trading Brown and putting all the chips on Tatum. First off, I am not a Tatum hater by any means. He is a good person and an excellent player. My issue with all of this nonsense is the idea that we could be trading Brown and having to rely solely on Tatum.

I think moving forward Tatum is going to have questionable durability, I think he an excellent player but a poor leader, he is too passive, and he lacks tenacity and grit. I feel that in the biggest moments he has disappeared in the playoffs at least in terms of scoring that of which Brown has almost always made up for. I think both Brown and Tatum are awful clutch players but Brown has been more clutch of the two.

With that being said we have seen what this team looks with Brown AND Tatum. We can win with both. We can contend with both. We have just witnessed this season what this team looks like with just Brown. We got 2nd seed and were favorites to come out of the east and our poor shooting and lack of a quality big man is what lead to our downfall. We have NOT seen this team with just Tatum. However with my concerns listed above i do not think Tatum would have been capable of leading this team to a 2nd seed like Brown did. Both players play their best together but I think Brown is far more capable of leading a team than Tatum.

I am not saying trade Tatum or anything like that. But we are going to miss Jaylen Brown a lot more than I think Brad Stevens or the owners expect. There are too many question marks with Tatum and I think we should at least see if Tatum returns for a full season and can be as good or better than he was pre injury and if his durability holds up before we even consider trading Brown. Bonus points if Tatum can find some tenacity, grit, and maybe lead a huddle every once in a while.

TLDR: I predict Tatum will have durability issues moving forward and his lack of leadership skills and playoff consistency will set this team back far more than just keeping Brown and Tatum together and paying to have them both.
