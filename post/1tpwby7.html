Witchhunter Culling Strike.

The game slowly becomes oversaturated with a ways to get Culling Strike. There will be at least few new skills in 0.5 that do that. And with one rune made from unique gloves, you will be able to get Culling Strike with just 1 socket on the gloves.

And all that makes the Witchunter Culling Strike ascendancy node worthless. Right now it becomes no more than a travel node to get to Decimating Strike.

In my opinion one of two things should be done.

  1. Swap position of Culling Strike and Decimating Strike on the ascendancy tree. So you don’t have to spend 2 points to get something you can get with 1 rune…

  2. Buff the node. Maybe give it additional 20% culling strike threshold or something, to make it at least slightly better and maybe worth those 2 ascendancy points.

And in addition to that, on the boss’ health bar, there should be indicator of your culling strike threshold. There is so maybe different effects that they already show on the health bar, so why not this?
