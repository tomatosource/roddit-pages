Houston Rockets don’t list any rotation players on most recent injury report. They will apparently play their starters against Denver despite having 2 seed locked up

The only player listed as out is Jae’Sean Tate. There’s not even anyone else listed as questionable or probable so unless the Rockets are late in filing paperwork all their players will be active.

This doesn’t necessarily mean they will play to win the game, as they could just play their starters for a half and use the game as a warmup.

The other thing that this does is that it potentially gives them some more control over who the 7th seed is and likely first round matchup. Assuming Minnesota beats Utah, the game that decides the 7th seed is Warriors-Clippers and the game they play. If the Clippers win or look likely to win, Warriors will be the 7th regardless of the outcome of HOU-DEN.

However, if the Warriors win or are up big, the Rockets would have some control over which team would be their first round opponent. Lose the game and the Clippers are the 7th seed. Win the game and Denver would be the 7th seed. The play-in means this doesn’t guarantee the matchup, but if for some reason they really want to avoid the Clippers or prefer the Denver matchup, they could push hard to win the game in front of them.

In the very unlikely but theoretically possible event that Minnesota loses against Utah, then the 7th seed will be Memphis or Minnesota, but the outcome of the Rockets-Nuggets game will not affect it.

With that all laid out, there’s still very little upside in going for a win compared to Denver who is trying to avoid the play-in so the most likely scenario is the Rockets playing all their guys limited minutes.
