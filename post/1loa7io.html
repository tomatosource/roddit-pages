[Windhorst] Something like ‘Satan’s Jail’ or ‘Adam Silver’s Dungeon’ conveys the devastation the Second Apron means for teams financially and with ability to change rosters. Celtics lowered payroll by $200m by trading just 2 players. So many transactions are focused on this reality of modern NBA

“I’m workshopping a new name for the second apron because it’s so legalese and so nondescriptive and so, you know, annoying. I got to find something else to try to convey what that means. It’s something like Satan’s jail or something like that. It needs some sort of something with, you know, Adam Silver’s dungeon. You know, like there’s got to be something that illustrates that this is not something that your grandmother would have after her first apron got dirty.

The reality is, is that being in that zone is devastating for teams, both financially and with their ability to change their rosters. This was illustrated yesterday by a faux pas that the owner of the Denver Nuggets made. He was introducing his new front office and was basically explaining why the Nuggets will never be a second apron team. They will never be a “Satan’s jail” team because he said if we get in there and then something bad happens to us, we might have to trade Joker. Everybody in his PR department slapped their head. Every Nuggets fan who was only marginally interested in the team right now, all like had their phones blow up with notifications. And now I think that Josh Kroenke will not do another media appearance for 7 to 12 years.

Getting into that zone is extremely unappealing for teams and that is just what’s happened to the Boston Celtics. So 10 days ago, the Boston Celtics were faced with having a payroll this year, including taxes, of \(520 million, \)540 million. They traded two players, Holiday and Porzingis, and now it’s 280. They saved $260 million by trading two players. And obviously those two players don’t make 100 whatever twenty-five between each. But that is an illustration of how penal this is.

The purpose of “Satan’s jail” is so that you can’t have super teams, that you can’t have teams win 4 championships in five years. It is forcing you to break your team up. In the case of the Celtics, it is exacerbated because Tatum got hurt and they don’t want to do anything like that because they don’t think they can win. It’s one thing to think about spending four or 500 million if they think you’re going to win. If you don’t think you’re going to win, you can’t do that.

They’re trying to get away from this and so many of the transactions are focused on this. That’s the reality of the modern NBA.”
