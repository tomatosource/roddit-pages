How is POE2 now?

I had never played POE1 but, enjoyed Diablo 4 so, I got excited for POE2 and played at launch. I really enjoyed it then, so much I paid for a pack that was way too expensive (don’t ask, I’m not telling you), thinking this could have finally been my “forever game” if you know what I mean.

Then I realised, this games kind of a slog in a way… I still really enjoyed everything about it; graphics, classes, combat, story everything… except the difficulty. Not that it was “too hard” but, I felt like I needed to search “best POE2 build” on Youtube to have any hope of actually clearing maps fast and melting packs. You know, the whole reason anyone plays these games.

Since, I’ve tried a bunch of other ARPGs and (this is entirely my own doing and my own weird mentality - I fully accept that this is a stupid, stupid thought process) I cannot help but think, ALWAYS in the back of my mind, “I spent so much money on POE2… PLAY THAT ONE”.

So, is POE2 any different? I’m aware Grinding Gear plan on operating POE1 and 2. POE1 even has a new expansion soon? Maybe? I don’t know but, point is; I am aware that POE2 will ALWAYS be slower than POE1. I know there goal was not to replace 1 but, provide something a little different. I don’t mind a slower pace, I just don’t want to feel like I have to play with a levelling guide/build on my second monitor. I want to play what I think is cool and not be put through hell because it’s less than optimal..

I know I could just play the game and see but, last time it took like 40 hours to realise “My build sucks, respeccing is hell and my only choice is starting again…” I don’t want to do that again so, I’m asking you guys. Any and all discussion is welcome, I just wanna know what the games like these days.

Little side note too, is Titan Quest 2 any good? Could that scratch my itch instead, what’s it like?
