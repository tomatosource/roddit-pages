What’s the future of shorts?

We’ve seen the league dominated by the very short shorts. Then it was like the league overcorrected and the long ones dominated. Now we’re in a middle ground, which seems normal your our eyes but only because that’s the current fashion—in the 50s you’d be wearing short shorts too.

So what’s next? Are we more likely to get shorter or longer? Or is there a new dimension of short style we can’t even comprehend at this point? Like maybe tassels or sequins? Or same length but hiked up like George Forman?

And who decides? And can each team choose their own style of short?
