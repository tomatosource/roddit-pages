Playing without guide or premade built in PoE 2 is a frustrating and punishing experience

In its current state, the game is seriously punishing for people who want to explore and experiment (which by necessity has to include failure/wasted attempts), rather than following a perfect leveling guide.

(For context, i’m in interlude 1, lvl 57 Warrior.)

&nbsp;

Let’s look at tools that i desperately need, but don’t have:

  • Support gems only really start to drop in sufficient numbers in Act 4
  • Low and mid tier runes that you can use are fairly rare, largely useless (like flask mana recovery) and feel like something you want to save for the next gear upgrade (especially %phys for weapons, got 4 of those total until now)
  • Low tier Essences might as well not exist, because i have only dropped &lt;10 of them (haven’t skipped a single encounter), and way over half of them are nothing that i would ever use (added lightning/cold damage, minion damage for example)
  • Artificer orbs are rare, and require religiously salvaging every socketed item from ground loot and vendors in order to even hope to keep up with normal gear progression

&nbsp;

Now let’s look at my current stash:

  • I have scraps of Delirium stuff, but i have no great use for them, and if i do i won’t have any for my next amulet.
  • I have one Omen
  • I had like 4 gnawed ribs and jawbones respectively, and used them all up, but most of them had bad outcomes
  • I have 4 Chaos and 3 Vaal
  • After salvaging stuff i was able to quality up a grand total of 2 weapons and armor pieces. Meanwhile i have 4 Prisms and 1 Baubles.
  • Exalt quantity feels fine-ish, but i’m mostly using them to fill up resistances
  • (Jeweller’s feel fine; i’ve just been saving them because damage isn’t my issue atm - enjoyment is, and i want to be able and swap build soon)

Did i mention that i’m running decent rarity on 3 gear pieces?

&nbsp;

I’m just left wondering:

###Why have all of these low level crafting tools when you can’t use them in quantities that will actually affect gameplay?

&nbsp;

They feel like the occasional goodie alright, except that most of the time they are just plain useless at best and a waste of time interacting with them at worst.
It would be so much more enjoyable to just cut down the variety, and amp up the quantity.
Just give me plenty of Essences tbh, that’s all i need.
But enough so i will have multiple physical essences on a physical build, instead of MAYBE one of them if i’m lucky.
