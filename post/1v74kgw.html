What is the most annoying ailment/damage type to mitigate?

It feels like this patch ggg has made an effort to make ailments do significant impact on players, and it requires players to add things in their arsenal to mitigate them or else they become extremely squishy. I wanted to see if my personal experience lines up with others and what tricks they have used to avoid some of these.

My winner is definitely Ignite. It’s the most overturned imo. My biggest issue with ignite is, that the only way to see the ailment is on the small box that shows up at the bottom of the screen with all ur buffs/debuffs. Most of the times I don’t know I’m lit up before it’s too late. In contrast, chill/slow is noticeable on the character. Shock and poison is visible on the health globe. I wish they would give ignite this treatment to make it more visible.
I don’t have a great mitigation for it, charms are what I’m using, but it’s a very ok solution.

Honorable mention is the expedition monsters reflect/chill as the second most annoying thing to mitigate. It does have multiple solutions to it. The legacy of wunderlust is the most ideal. You can also anoint crystallized immunity, and that works. Charms are another half baked solution.

What are other ailments/damage types ppl have problems with, and how are you mitigating them?
