No other ARPG will feel the same now.

I am still a huge fan of the action RPG I’ve spent so much time with (A game I’m not going to name). I love its lore, world, and characters, and I enjoy the experience as a whole despite its many flaws. The recent arrival of the Paladin class had the whole community excited—myself included. The seasonal mechanic of sanctifying items was fantastic, allowing you to empower gear with incredible, game-changing modifiers. The Paladin itself felt like a beautifully designed machine—powerful, yes, but the fun came from its seamless flow. That enjoyment lasted through multiple characters and builds, played consistently but not excessively.

Recently, however, I decided to revisit another action RPG, path of exile 2, obviously—one I had been highly critical of in the past—largely out of curiosity about its take on the Druid, my favorite fantasy class. What I expected to be a brief trial run has completely captivated me for days. I’m genuinely stunned by its quality. The systems, like an abyssal sanctification mechanic where you modify items at a mysterious well, feel incredibly rewarding to discover. The boss fights, even early on, delivered a greater sense of accomplishment than the hardest content in my previous game. The skill tree is phenomenal, classes feel distinct and satisfying, and the seasonal mechanic of crafting your own dungeons is mind-blowingly creative—especially before I even realized the added layers of time travel and other secrets. It also features elements I’ve longed for elsewhere: deep Druid cosmetics, a seamless in-game trading market, and even a form of base building.

This isn’t meant to disparage the other title, which is moving in a positive direction and whose expansion I’m still eagerly awaiting. But this experience has been nothing short of a revelation—one of the most perfectly crafted action RPGs I’ve ever played. I feel fortunate to have had the chance to dive into it and am genuinely excited that two such engaging worlds exist to enjoy. Happy New Year to everyone.
