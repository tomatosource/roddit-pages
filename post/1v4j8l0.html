Some Honest Feedback After Playing the New League

I have been thinking about why mapping and rerolling characters have started to feel more exhausting than fun for me. I enjoy grinding. That is a huge part of why I play Path of Exile. I can farm the same content for hours when the process feels rewarding or when I am working toward something meaningful. What bothers me is repetitive setup that feels like busywork.

Tablets should be more permanent or bring back Scarabs

Tablets feel too temporary for something that has such a large effect on mapping. I would much rather see a system closer to the Phrecia idols from PoE 1. Let us collect them, improve them, organize them, and build a mapping setup that lasts. Right now it feels like I am constantly managing consumables just to keep my maps worth running. The setup disappears, then I have to rebuild it again.

City maps take too much monotonous setup

City maps can be rewarding, but getting them ready feels tedious. Finding the right city areas, preparing the maps, organizing tablets, and lining everything up takes a lot of time. After running the setup, you eventually have to go through the same process again.I am fine with a grind. I just want the grind itself to be engaging. City/hunting setup feels like administration before I am allowed to start playing the farming strategy and it diminishes other non-city maps significantly.

Atlas biomes make the Atlas tree feel worse

I am also not a fan of tying major Atlas bonuses to biomes. The Atlas tree should let me build toward the content I enjoy and keep that setup active while I map. Biome-specific passives make that progression feel conditional. I can invest into a mechanic, then reach a different part of the Atlas and lose the benefits because the maps are in the wrong biome. That pushes players toward hunting for specific areas instead of simply running maps and following the content they enjoy. It also adds another layer of setup on top of tablets and city maps. I would rather remove biome-specific Atlas bonuses and move those effects into permanent Atlas passives. Biomes can still influence visuals, monsters, bosses, or natural encounter frequency, but they should not determine whether major parts of my Atlas tree actually work. The Atlas tree should represent the mapping strategy I chose, not a temporary bonus that only applies when I happen to be standing in the correct region.

Let us save item search filters in Market Panel

The built-in item search needs saved searches. I should be able to create a filter, name it, and use it again later. Recreating the same search every time adds nothing to the game. This would be useful for map modifiers, tablets, item bases, defenses, sockets, resistances, and anything else players regularly search for.

Add mirror shops

Players should be able to list a high-end item for mirror service without unequipping it. The shop could save a verified copy of the item and allow other players to mirror it while the owner is offline. The owner could choose a fixed fee or allow free copies. The game could handle the Mirror of Kalandra, the copying fee, and the mirrored item automatically. This would allow people to keep using their own items while offering mirror services. It would also remove a lot of the trust issues, impersonation, item swapping, and scams surrounding mirror trades. If the original item is changed, the listing could simply become inactive until it is verified again.

Rolling alternate characters feels much worse than PoE 1

This one will probably be more opinionated and divisive, I am sure. I have no problem with this in PoE 1, and I struggle to pinpoint my issues with this in PoE 2 but it just feels bad to me. I rolled 4 characters this league. In PoE 2, I finish Act 2 on an alternate character and immediately think about how much campaign is still left before maps. The zones themselves might be what wear me down. Many of them feel huge, and there is so much running through areas that I have already explored several times. I am not calling for a full campaign skip. Smaller zones for repeat characters, more waypoints, faster movement, account wide objective completion, or another form of accelerated progression would help a lot. PoE 1 made rerolling feel like I was racing toward a new build. PoE 2 often feels like a long walk before I can start playing it.

I still enjoy the game and want to keep experimenting with builds. These systems just create a lot of friction between deciding what I want to do and actually getting to do it.
