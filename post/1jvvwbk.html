I think a monster power is not the biggest issue

I don’t think the biggest issue with PoE2 right now is monster tuning. That can likely be fixed with simple numerical adjustments. The real problem lies in the direction the developers are taking the game.

I believe this is closely tied to the combo system. Currently, the game doesn’t allow for creative builds like PoE1 did. It feels like GGG is tightly controlling what builds are viable, and players are limited to playing only the ones that GGG explicitly allows. In PoE1, there was never this kind of restrictive feeling, but in PoE2, stereotypes seem to matter a lot—Huntress seems to use spears, warriors seems to use maces, and so on.

On top of that, GGG’s usual “player tuning” approach makes it feel like there are even fewer build options left for players.

Since this is still Early Access, I think they should go the opposite direction: overtune the players first, then fine-tune the numbers later. That way, players will have room to be creative with their builds
