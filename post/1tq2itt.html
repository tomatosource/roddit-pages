Totems, mana cost and efficency

There’s been a bit of confusion in posts so I just wanted to create a simple post that explains what exactly affects totem base cost. With the changes to AB, totem base cost will still be there and there’s some nuances on what changes this and what is considered added cost.

Spell totem on its own has a base cost which does not get affected by what spell is in it but on some of the support gems socketed in it. F.e. Projectile Acceleration has a 1.2x cost multiplier and can be socketed into spell totem + spark but it does not change the base cost. This is due to spark having the projectile tag while spell totem does not.

But f.e. Prolonged Duration, which is also a gem sometimes used with spark, WILL change the cost of spell totem as it also affects the spell totem gem which has the duration tag.

Essentially if the support gem only affects the spell the totem is casting and not the totem itself, it does not cost extra.

To set the baseline:

A level 14 spell totem costs 74 mana. A level 23 spell totem costs 152 mana. Say you’re lucky and you have it 6L in both scenarios. Into this you socket Spark, Rakiata’s Flow, Projectile acceleration III, Rapid casting II and Urgent totems III. Your totem still only costs 74 mana or 152 mana.

So what actually changes this cost besides the support gems like prolonged duration?

x to spell levels - This is the obvious one but it’s worth to note. + to spell on a neck is often a staple, but you have to remember that it now also affects your totem cost. This is in contrast to a wand where you can have + to lightning spells which does not affect the base cost of the totem.

% increased (general/mana/life) cost efficiency - This can be found on the tree and gear and this lowers the mana cost. F.e just the 3 nodes near witch start with Efficient Casting notable lowers the 152 mana cost of a level 23 totem down to 111 mana.

% increased (general/mana/life) cost of skills - this can be found on gear and tree and is the inverse of above. Less common but does affect the base cost.

% of skill (specifically just skill) mana costs convert to life costs - this can be seen on the tree and does change a % of the base cost to life.

Blood mage’s Sanguimancy - This is a funky one, it basically takes totem mana cost after applying support gems (in our example above 152 mana) but before efficiency and other things like Eldritch Battery. There’s generic and life mana cost increases/reductions as well as noted above, so this can also be increased/reduced that way but separate from mana cost reduction.

Eldritch Battery - as mentioned above this basically takes the cost and doubles it. Mana cost increases and reductions will give the same % reduction but now reduce the mana costs more in points of mana per %.

What doesn’t affect it:

Archmage - This only modifies what Spark would cost and as such doesn’t do anything to mana cost.

% of spell (specifically just spell) mana costs convert to life costs

speculation but likely Kalguraan skills - since these are just what the skill costs, they should not add any cost to the totem cast.

Now for some end-game examples:

Shaman - You’re a shaman and you are running an end-game setup with EB and have your spell totem at level 23 for the 3 (6) limit. Your baseline costs is 310 mana and 5 rage per totem since none of your support gems are affecting it. Since you are a Shaman and want to get spirit efficiency and have Idol of Eeshta socketed in your helm, this also gives 15% increased Cost Efficiency. On top of this you’re running a Heart of the Well with a 12% mana cost efficency affix.

Your spell totem costs 239 mana and 4 rage.

Blood Mage - You’re a blood mage and you are running an end-game setup with EB and have your spell totem at level 23 for the 3 (6) limit. Your baseline costs is 310 mana and 152 life per totem since none of your support gems are affecting it. Since you are a witch you are pathing to Efficient Casting cluster, giving you 36% mana cost efficiency. On top of this you have crafted your wand with a Pefect Essence of Alacrity for 20% efficiency and you’re also running a Heart of the Well for 7% efficiency this time.

Your totem costs 186 mana and 152 life.

Overall there’s quite a few things you can do to mitigate the cost of your totems, like cost efficiency, choosing what support gems to use with it and the gem level of spell totem itself. Totem mana costs are not going to balloon to 700+ like some people are saying (this only happens with really high + to spell gems and not managing cost at all with supports, rip tending people).

Edit: added the rage cost to Shaman example. With even more cost efficiency reductions like Dialla’s Desire, you can get rage down to 3 per totem which is nice.
