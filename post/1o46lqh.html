Controversial take: Crafting has killed the ARPG drop joy

Its a controversial take, but as a long term PoE and PoE2 player, who never really enjoyed the crafting aspect of the game, I honestly feel like the whole item drop enjoyment has been completed neutered this season. I respect that tons of people are so happy about the crafting being so improved, but its honestly killed the enjoyment of getting a good drop or a discovering a good item.

Its like everything is now pointless unless you have the currency to craft it to the oblivion heights of perfection. As one example, market is dead for gloves and boots unless you craft on the 100% socket improvement so any drop becomes a ‘meh’ event as nothing will compare.

The abyss mechanic has become the defacto method for achieving anything worth selling on the market, which means anyone who isn’t overly into the crafting side can only cheer on from the sidelines to everyone else who is studying videos on crafting the perfect item.

I am not sure what the best solution is, but it would be lovely if Tier 5 items that dropped had a chance at being that perfect item, or close to it, it may kill the market as well, but I honestly miss the enjoyment of items dropping, identifying them and being blown away at it being such a good roll.
