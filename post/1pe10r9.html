[Stat] The 2025-26 Denver Nuggets have become only the 3rd team in NBA history to feature three different 50-point scorers in a single season, joining the ‘62 Lakers and ‘19 Warriors.

With Jamal Murray’s last 50-point game, they are now the only team other than the 1961-62 Lakers (Elgin Baylor, Jerry West, Rudy LaRusso) and the 2018-19 Golden State Warriors (Steph Curry, Kevin Durant, Klay Thompson) to accomplish this feat.
