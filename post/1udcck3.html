The Diversity of Gemling builds shows that many Skills severely lack baseline Power

The additional Quality Effects on Gemling are an amazing addition to the 0.5 Patch and opened up a wide variety of new builds that we havent seen before.

Almost every day a new Gemling build pops up and I think thats absolutely amazing.

But it also shows that many skills that are otherwise unpopular severely lack baseline power.

I think its great to have a class that can specify in all kinds of Builds. Gemling can literally build into all weapon types and all Skill types. But I also think that therin lies a bigger issue.

If almost every Skill feels better to play on one Ascendancy, or if Skills that see no play only see play on one ascendancy, a natural conclusion would be that these Skills should come with these powers, or at least similar Power, as a Baseline.

I personally would like to see sweeping buffs to skills across the board to get a little closer to what Gemling does right now so that the Ascendancy choice is not allways gonna be “Oh, this only works on Gemling, why bother with anything else?”.

What do you think of the current state of Gemling?
