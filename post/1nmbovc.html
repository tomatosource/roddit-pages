Learning crafting

Picked up this Focus for 3 exalts because it looked a bit underpriced — fractured 30% cast speed and a big chaos damage roll. Figured it would just be a fun side project.

First step was cleaning up the suffixes. Went in with Dextral + Abyssal Echoes, and RNG actually cooperated: Lightning res got deleted, exactly what I wanted gone. Already felt like a win.

Next came the scary part. I slammed an Exalt to fill the last prefix slot (as “bait”), then went for a Perfect Essence of Sorcery. That meant a 1⁄3 chance to nuke my chaos damage… and somehow, chaos stayed while the junk disappeared. End result: +3 of all spell skills stacked onto fractured cast speed and 118% chaos damage.

Now I’ve got capped resists, extra ES, and a Focus that feels straight up BiS for my build. Total cost was basically 3 ex + 2 ex + the Essence. Crafting really is the true endgame — I get why people love it.
