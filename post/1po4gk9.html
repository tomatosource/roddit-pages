Players hate league mechanics that takes them out of maps, unless…

Hear me out. I’ve noticed a pattern since PoE1. People generally hate league mechanics that take them out of maps. That’s understandable since it takes you out of the gaming loop and flow, UNLESS the mechanic is as interesting, deep and rewarding as doing maps! Superficial and boring mechanics like Prophecy, Anarchy (Rogue Exiles), Bestiary are bearable because, besides being meh, they just live inside of the mapping experience and is just a detail among the entire experience. It’s OK to have filler leagues that add some mid-low tiered stuff. But please, GGG, when you decide to make a league mechanics that forces you to leave your maps to another reality, you better ALWAYS make something interesting, rewarding and engaging like Delve and Heist. Every major disappointment with leagues come from these shitty league mechanics that takes forever to complete without rewards, so people always think it’s better to just stay inside maps. It’s Kalandra 2.0.
