Reveal the map content once the boss is dead, not with all checkpoints please

Pretty much the title,

Now that we’ve moved on from the 0.1 way of “sometimes there’s a boss, sometimes kill all rares” in maps, isn’t it time to tie the reveal of the map content to the boss rather than the checkpoints ?

In my opinion, checkpoints are more a kind of failure on GGG part, a band aid if you will, rather than a real gameplay aspect, core to the experience.

However, killing the boss is now the main goal and the condition to get a map to completion, so once it’s done, why not give us the location of all content on the map so we can choose to either leave or keep searching ?

I’ve been doing temple runs these last few days and searching for checkpoints isn’t something that gives any fun and adds anything to the experience, I usualy find the boss way before I find all checkpoints.

Any thoughts on that ?
