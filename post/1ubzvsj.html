Some tablet farming tips for 10+ tabs per map

This has been my best farm so far. You must be strong enough to run maps with 12-16 mods. This means temp chains and chilled ground are common and sometimes together. Monsters end up with 100-150+ effectiveness and get super tanky.

https://preview.redd.it/fqha0vl1uo8h1.png?width=827&format=png&auto=webp&s=5f1be684801789e90c29fef3bd8dee8e685a7d1e

These are the tablets you want to run. The fourth mod doesn’t matter. Increased effectiveness can be swapped for rarity of items, number of rares, rarity of monsters, whatever. You can buy them for less than 1d (edit) a few div and div them until “map has 2 additional random modifiers.” The waystone quantity and +2 random mods are the most important for dropping tablets. Irradiated tablets are best, but you can run up to one league mechanic tablet with these mods. Be sure not to run a league mechanic tablet for a tablet type you want to farm. For example, don’t run delirium tablet for farming delirium tablets in fog.

Break a grand mirror and spawn fog in 5-20 maps of mountains, any city (Faridun is best), or breach stronghold. Use head of the king to spawn rite of the nameless in the deli fog.

https://preview.redd.it/arf27imswo8h1.png?width=596&format=png&auto=webp&s=cdb08503aa44c4397169548009aa9fae44a404f5

Top left Doryani node is the most important. On a 16 mod map it gives +32% monster effectiveness, 16% rarity of items dropped, and 48% increased chance of rare monster mods. Row 2 column 2 node is second most important for further juicing high mod maps. The last two are up to preference. You can run the revive node if your survivability is low. Only run t15 maps with 8 mods or t16 maps. Run the worst fog maps first to juice up to 200% deli.

For example, the ideal map would be a faridun city t16 6-mod with +8 random mods from 4 irradiated tablets, 250%+ waystone find (+50% from faridun mountain atlas node), 200% deli fog, and rite of the nameless. You’ll know you’re doing it right if you hit tab and the list of map modifiers is so long is goes behind your mana bar. If you have full atlas and 100%+ magic find, expect to get a tablet every third monster pack.
