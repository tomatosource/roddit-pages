AMA with Thomas Nestico of TJStats at 3:00 PM ET on May 21

Hey everyone, Thomas Nestico of TJStats here to answer any questions about my work and how I got into the public baseball sphere. The mods of r/baseball reached out to me about being an approved user on the subreddit and encouraged me to do an AMA to introduce myself officially.

For those that are not familiar with my work, I am a freelance writer/analyst and the creator of https://tjstats.ca/, a website which hosts my MLB and MiLB articles, leaderboards, and tools. I gear my writing to MLB as a whole. with a heavy lean into prospect coverage, including a regularly updated Top 100 MLB Prospect list. My initial goal for starting this project was to eventually work for an MLB team, however, my direction has changed as I focus on growing my brand and proving myself as a trusted source in the industry.

Outside of my articles, the site hosts a variety of tools such as my MLB and MiLB player pages (the latter which include Statcast data where applicable). You may have also seen graphics of mine floating around, with the most notable being my pitching summaries which synthesize a pitcher’s start/season into a digestible card chock full of information such as pitch usage, results, movement, and quality. While I have not focused on data modelling recently, I created my own pitch model called tjStuff+, which aims to capture the effectiveness of a pitch based off its physical characteristics alone.

So ask me questions about TJStats, my journey in the public baseball sphere, or anything at all (I am a massive Pokemon fan). I’m excited to engage with best and largest baseball community on Reddit!
