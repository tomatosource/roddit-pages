Can Lakers win 1st round without Luka? KG has some thoughts: “We’re not talking about the intangibles that LeBron comes with… I’ve seen the man turn it on and take it to another level, seen him control the game… When Bron hit that button, you really got to be on your s—t, bro… I Like the Lakers.”

When fans talk about the Los Angeles Lakers, the conversation usually circles back to how far the team can go depending on what Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves can deliver. That storyline has followed them all year, but Kevin Garnett stepped in to remind the league that overlooking LeBron James because of his age is a huge mistake.

Opponents may be getting comfortable thinking the Lakers live or die by their young backcourt stars. As a result, KG highlighted LBJ’s ability to flip a game on its head in multiple ways, making the Purple & Gold a tougher playoff opponent than most can assume.

“We’re not talking about the intangibles that LeBron James comes with. I’ve been a testament to it. I’ve seen the man turn it on and take it to another level, seen him control the game. That’s what I’m banking on. When we get to the playoffs, guess who’s going to be able to get to the hole and out of five drives get three calls? When Bron hit that button, you really got to be on your s—t, bro,” Garnett said. “I like the Lakers, they’re trending high.”
