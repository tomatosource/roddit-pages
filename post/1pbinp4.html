What is an extremely niche thing your team or front office seems to be very good at?

I don’t mean big, important things for a team to do like “developing hitters” or “international scouting” - I’m talking little things that fans of other teams would never notice themselves.

I’d say the Mariners are oddly good at avoiding TJS, especially for their starting pitchers. The last two to go under the knife were Robbie Ray in 2023 and James Paxton in 2021, who were both on the wrong side of 30. I believe the only one of their current homegrown SPs that had TJS was Bryan Woo while he was in college. The lack of extended absences from the rotation means the M’s have only used 10 starting pitchers in the past two years, the lowest number in the league.
