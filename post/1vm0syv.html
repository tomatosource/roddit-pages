Which League Mechanic Do You Want Next?

I’m a completely new POE player who tried out POE 2 for the first time on 0.5 launch. Had a blast, no life’d it for 2 months, then checked out the POE 1 for the first time as well with 3.29 AllFlame league launch. And I gotta say that while I prefer POE2 overall, POE 1 has been incredibly fun and one thing I’ve been loving was checking out all of the other league mechanics that hadn’t made it to 2 yet. Some of these mechanics really feel deep and mix up the moment-to-moment gameplay in fun & refreshing ways.

So wanted to ask you all who played both games to see which league mechanics you’d love to see ported over next?

Personally i’d love to see Blight added with a revamped tower defense mechanic. I also really want to see POE 2’s take on Legion and Delve. Also apparently it’s controversial, but as a new player I like the Harvest crafting and wouldn’t mind seeing something like that ported to 2
