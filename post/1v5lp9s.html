It wouldn’t surprise me if the 76ers win it all… which means it wouldn’t surprise me if Jaylen Brown wins another Finals MVP.

Let me start out by saying that this post is more to highlight Jaylen’s opportunity this season than it is say the 76ers are going to win it all.

But on paper, the 76ers starting lineup is absolutely loaded. They are going to have a mismatch somewhere on the court every single game - probably two most nights and sometimes 3 or more.

They’re biggest questions before LeBron were:

They’re playmaking:
Who’s going to settle the offense? Who’s going to be their primary playmaker? How are Maxey and Brown going to handle sharing that role? How is Brown going to do owning a big piece of that responsibility with a lot higher expectations and a lot more people not only watching but watching very closely and constantly evaluating given all the discussions that have taken place about him recently.

Embid’s availability:
How often will he be available? What seed will they get if he misses so many games as he usually does? Will he be healthy come playoffs? Is Embid willing to give up some limelight and usage to Maxey and Brown given he won’t be there every game? Will Brown and Maxey be willing to hand over the keys if he doesnt? Does Philly have the right people leading this to help the team navigate these obstacles?

LeBron’s arrival makes many of those things questions of the past.

-He addresses the playmaking and facilitating a sound offense.
-Maxey, Embid, Brown, & VJ will now have even more space when operating at ball. And when they receive a pass the opponents will get killed if they send a double team.
-All the pressure is now off Brown. It’s LeBron’s fault if they don’t win.
-Maxey has no pressure to develop into some elite playmaker anymore.
-and if Brown or Maxey are going to comfortably defer primary playmaking decisions to anyone, it’s probably LeBron

The sixers now have the type of depth in their starting lineup that makes Embid’s availability a non-factor in the regular season

-they already had enough talent to make the playoffs if Embid barely played, and just got more.
-now LeBron & Embid could theoretically alternate taking off every other game to stay fresh and they still probably end up a decent see in the east.
-the chances of Embid being healthy come playoff time just went up significantly, and LeBron has proven time and time again that he will.

Now to the main point: Jaylen Brown might be the biggest winner of this whole thing.

Like I’ve already stated, there is no longer this mountain of pressure on him. Whether the 76ers don’t win a title or even fail, LeBron will be the one blamed. The microscope that was about to be on Brown has been removed from him and placed on LeBron.

That alone is going to do numbers for Brown, but in addition to that:
-he now will be setup by one of the best playmakers all time, allowing Brown to do what he does best
-he’s going to have even easier matchups defensively as teams may decide to put a better defender on Bron to get the ball out of his hands
-the space he’s going to have is going to possibly at an all time high. Teams have to stay attached to Maxey, LeBron, and Embid. And it certainly wouldn’t be smart to leave Maxey. He just averaged 28 with defenses all over him. With way less attention and a lighter responsibility, this season is going to feel easy for him.

But that’s not even why he’s the winner of all this. Cuz honestly, he might see a drop in regular-season recognition. But come playoff time, he’s got another chance to take his legacy to new heights.

If the 76ers were to win, Jaylen Brown would be my bet to win Finals MVP

Let’s go with the guarantees first:

1. He’s going to be the defender on the team.
That we already know. If they make the finals, his previous Finals MVP and his defense on Luka is going to be a major talking point. If they play OKC, it’ll be Jaylen who covers SGA. If they play the Spurs, it’ll be Jaylen who is responsible for shutting down Castle or Harper. He’d be the one to shut down Jamal Murray or Ant. The only way the defensive story of a 76ers title isn’t about Jaylen is if Embid just complicate erases Wemby. Any other situation most likely results in Jaylen being highlighted for his work on defense.

2. He’s going to score.
I’ve already highlighted the several ways that scoring is going to come easier for him this season. Sure, he’s probably not going to be dropping 25+ game in the finals. But probably nobody on the 76ers will. And Jaylen’s already won finals MVP before by being a close second in scoring for his team. He is going to get easy buckets. He is going to get scrappy buckets. He’s going to hit tough shots off-script when the offense breaks down. He’s just going to score. He’ll end up averaging 20+ a game on good efficiency just like last time he won.

3. His presence will be felt.
Jaylen lives with a chip on his shoulder. If he makes the finals this season it will be bigger than ever. And he’s going to make his presence be seen and felt. His plays will be timely. His dunks will be loud. His defensive stops will be felt. For years, that’s how he’s made his impact. The analytics might not capture those moments accurately, but the fans and media will. There will be no missing Jaylen and what he provides.

I’m not here to say the 76ers are going to win it all. Given their bench, I still believe it would need to know Embid was going to be healthy to make that claim.

But with LeBron’s arrival, I think there’s a very good chance he is. And if they do, I would expect Jaylen Brown to be MVP.

Now LeBron could be LeBron and be so good that Jaylen doesn’t even receive a vote. But at this stage of his career, at this age, I’d expect him to be very comfortable handing the mvp trophy to someone else while he takes all the credit. And I think he’d be especially comfortable giving it to someone like JB.

Is this a crazy take?

Or does it make sense?

Would JB have a good shot or would it most likely go to LeBron or Embid?

What happens to Brown’s legacy if he gets another finals MVP?

Who gets the blame if they lose in the finals or don’t even make it?

Let me know your thoughts.
