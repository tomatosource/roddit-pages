How to get the Twitch Drops for TGA

Since the official announcement reads like a novel, here’s a simpler version of the Twitch Drops guide.

Link your account:

  1. Go to Twitch Settings → Connections → Other Connections → Path of Exile
  2. If successful, the page will say: “Your Path of Exile account is currently linked to your Twitch account.”

Watch the stream to get rewards during The Game Awards (Dec 11, 4:00 PM → 8:30 PM PST):

Drop #1 — Druids of Maji Portal (Free 30-min Drop)
Watch 30 minutes on any Twitch channel streaming The Game Awards with Drops enabled.

Drop #2 — Battle-Hardened Warbear Shapeshift (Sub Drop)
Purchase or gift 1 Twitch subscription to any channel streaming The Game Awards.

Claim: Go to your Twitch Inventory and hit Claim for each reward.
