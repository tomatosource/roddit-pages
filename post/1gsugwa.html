In Orlando’s five game winning streak, Franz Wagner is averaging 28.4 PPG and 6 AST’s. He’s going to the line 6 times a game, averaging 2 steals, and is a +61. Has he made the Leap?

Re-posting because I got in a silly exchange in the last thread and got the %’s wrong on the 3% Franz has had the last five games, just calculated it wrong. It’s 34%, not 37%, but that’s still above his season average prior to the homestand, and better than last season. He’s bumped up the volume and bumped up the difficulty of the 3’s he’s taking and while 34% isn’t anything to write home about, the fact he’s increased his offensive load significantly during this stretch and his efficiency has not taken any kind of dip at all as a scorer is very notable, and that’s suggestive of he taking a real leap in his play.

Made five 3’s last night, attempted 11 of them. He’s taking more pull up 3’s, step back 3’s even. More assists, he’s at a career high AST% by far, over 30% AST rate, without a turnover increase…his turnover rate is at a career low currently. He’s a talented passer and very capable playmaker for others, and we’re seeing that part of his game highlighted in a lead creator role. Playing with a level of aggression as a scorer we’ve never seen, added strength is seeing him shed guys on his way to the rim.

Franz’ game is kinda simplistic in the sense there’s nothing otherworldly notable about how he applies his game but he’s doing it in a very effective way. He’s been good as a PnR handler, which was something you wanted to see him get more reps in doing. He’s tough to stop getting downhill.

He’s getting to the line more than ever during this stretch. His season numbers are in line with where he’s been consistently, in that 4-ish range, over the course of the last few seasons. With his ability to get in the paint, being better able and more willing to play into and draw contact was a big part of Franz’ game that you thought could level up. He’s always had the craft but adding more power to his game around the basket to pair with it only makes him even more versatile a scoring force.

We’ll see if he keeps this up when Paolo gets back but these are All Star numbers. Considering how long Paolo is expected to be out, there is a good chance Franz is the Magic’s All Star rep. They’re the East’s #3 seed right now and if that remains true I think a great chance he makes it, but beyond that, do you think Franz has made the Leap to stardom?
