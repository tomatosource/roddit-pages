Maces (slams) are terribly designed

Maces should be the weapon of choice for slams, attacks that hit the ground and explode an area around you. After playing maces every league since EA started, this is simply not the case.

&nbsp;

Of the 23 skills requiring maces, only 6 (earthquake, rolling slam, sunder, stampede, supercharged slam, and tangentially hammer of the gods) can be considered similar to this base concept. There are some slams like earthshatter, volcanic fissure, or forge hammer, which are not so much attacks on their own but support skills to set up slams.

&nbsp;

The other skills consist of 4 warcries, 2 totems, 3 shield skills, and some strike attacks. So about 25% of the skills are direct-damaging slams. How many of them are actually relevant in an endleague environment?

Fate of the Vaal featured our first truly endgame scaling content with the temple snake. Looking at POEninja, the only ‘mace’ skill being used to clear temples is shield wall (3% of all reported builds, tied with shockwave totem), a skill that is nearly independent of mace stats. In reality the strongest method of playing mace skills are just using better shields and warcries.

&nbsp;

Mace slams are hardly ever used after a certain point of progression. Total attack time places a ceiling on how much dps a skill can output. Not only that, but support skills like earthshatter or volcanic fissure scale with the number of destructibles/fissure procs, only to be contradicted by the slowest slams to proc them.

Simple brutality (if not crudeness) should be thematic to the mace skillset, not having 75% of my kit for warcries, fissures, and stun/armor break conditionals to place before slamming.

I just want to slam the ground and have it explode around me, something that is possible just after killing Hillock in POE1 (groundslam).

&nbsp;

Patch notes for 0.5 are unknown but this is the current state of maces: a place for shield skills and warcries.

This isn’t a discussion of how melee suffers the most from mob modifiers/attacks or how armor and armor hybrids are the weakest defense layers, or how maces are the only warrior martial weapon there is, but these tremendously exacerbate the experience.
