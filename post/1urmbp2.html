[Theory] The airpod Charania lost outside Madison Square Garden prior to Game 3 of the finals is now Taylor Swift memorabilia

Game 3 of the finals involved the secret service locking down the entire block so that Trump could attend and curse the Knicks.

On his way to his pregame bit, Shams dropped an airpod in an intersection outside the Garden. When he bent down to grab it, a secret service agent who was not at all profiling told him to keep moving. He lost the airpods.

Various updates were provided on ESPN over the course of the evening. Shams said “we’re still on the hunt.” Later, he provided a screenshot from the Find My Device page, locating the airpods between Penn Station and MSG

No report has emerged of Shams recovering the airpods.

Fast forward to the Taylor Swift wedding at MSG. Artist Justin Gignac, who’s been selling NYC garbage for 25 years, walks around the perimeter of MSG in a tuxedo picking up trash which he then seals in small plastic cubes and sells.

From Gignac’s interview: “I found a lot of bottle caps, cigarette butts, and one left airpod on the crosswalk - that was a first.”

Photos of the airpod cube show it to be a non-silicone model, which Shams has stated he prefers.

“But it could be anyone’s airpod, thousands of people walk there.” Maybe. But: (a) in 25 years of this somewhat strange man professionally collecting garbage off NYC streets, the one and only airpod he ever finds happens to be at the exact spot where Shams loses his; and (b) ESPN has yet to report on any other lost airpods.

It’s widely accepted that the Knicks championship run was deeply imbued with actual magic (including, relevantly, the Knicks going undefeated in all games Taylor Swift personally attended, beginning in Cleveland). I believe this to be another, clear example of this. I believe that somewhere out there, on a shelf beside a friendship bracelet, is Shams Charania’s left airpod. I’ll point out also, this is an incredibly rare piece of NBA memorabilia because it commemorates the only game the Spurs managed to win in the finals (there is, for example, four times as much memorabilia from games won by the Knicks, who won four).

To my knowledge, Shams has yet to deny the airpod is his.
