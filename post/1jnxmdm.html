Poe2 intended gameplay can’t exist along this much recovery

I have seen several people mentioning how damage is king, because there are lot of things that one shot you. How the campaign has a completely different feeling than endgame maps. Campaign feels like a proper rpg, while endgame is just poe1 lite. People are tired of oneshot mechanics, but the truth is it is the only way to challenge the player, because if a hit does 99% of your life, you’ll just heal up in seconds.

If what the devs want is a slower gameplay, they don’t neet to gut the damage to wet noodle levels. What they need to do is redesign recovery. If recovery was severely limited (For example, life flasks and maybe very few skills with lifesteal), then hits could be threatening without outright oneshotting you. An elite could hit you for 40-60% of your life, and without killing you it would be a bad blow to receive, cause you have limited recovery. Of course, this is not even close to possible when you have things like grim feast, or heal on kill, and popping a 30 monsters pack will pop you back from 1 health to full health/ES.

Even if you have 2 billion dps, doesn’t matter, because putting yourself in the middle of packs will mean you will inevitably get hit. That means it will end in death by a thousand cuts, probably less if you neglect your defenses. This would also promote the slower gameplay devs wanted to do with poe 2. In fact, this is also what makes games like dark souls slow and methodic. You can build really high defenses, and really high damage in those games. It is the limited recovery what is keeping players in check of just going full damage and survive one hit.

I understand that this is very unlikely to happen due to several very deep mechanics ingrained with this level of recovery (For example, ES couldn’t even exist) and ascendancies like the bloodmancer would be a challenge to balance properly. Nonetheless, I don’t think a slower gameplay will ever be viable without this kind of change.
