The last four Eastern Conference champions lost in the Eastern Conference Finals the previous year. If the pattern continues, the Cavaliers are making the 2027 NBA Finals. Perhaps one more reason for LeBron to come home.

There has been a weirdly specific succession pattern in the Eastern Conference over the last five years:

Season Eastern champion What happened the previous year
2023 Miami Heat Lost the 2022 ECF to Boston
2024 Boston Celtics Lost the 2023 ECF to Miami
2025 Indiana Pacers Lost the 2024 ECF to Boston
2026 New York Knicks Lost the 2025 ECF to Indiana
2027? Cleveland Cavaliers? Lost the 2026 ECF to New York

Miami lost a seven-game conference final in 2022, returned the next season and beat Boston in seven in the Zombie Heat year. Boston then did the same thing: after losing to Miami in 2023, it came back and won the East in 2024. Indiana was swept by Boston in that 2024 conference final, in one of the closest sweeps ever, but returned one year later and beat New York. Then New York recovered from its 2025 loss to Indiana by sweeping Cleveland and ultimately winning the 2026 championship.
