How to make your own budget +4 minion staff with 160 spirit.

This is for skeleton warrior sceptre (rattling scepter)

Initial investment cost

  1. 3 to 6 omen of homogenizing exaltation
  2. 1 or 2 omen of recombination

from the trade site, try to find sceptres with the below settings.

  • item level 78 or higher
  • -skeleton warrior

look thru the list and pick up items around 1-2 exalted

  • (spirit% only) at tiers 4 to 7.
  • (spirit% + mana) &lt;&lt;= this is a single mod! (tier 6, 7 is better)
  • minion life (Tier 6).&lt;&lt;&lt;= this might be expensive, because crafters are sniping this mod. There is an alternative Allies in your Presence Regenerate # Life per second. Get Tiers 8 to 10

use the recombinator to combine either spirit% with minion life/regen (aim for 70% percent) so it has lesser chance to fail.

then use the recombinator to combine( spirit% + minion life/regen) with (spirit%+mana)
you should eventually get to a point where the recombination of the 3 mods get to 40+

YOLO combine or use omen of recombination (for lucky chance)

My easiest combines without using omen of combination are

  • T5 spirit%,
  • T7 spirit%+mana, and
  • T9 regen

the chance of failure is pretty high but since the input cost is so little, you usually get 1 good scepter out of 2-3 tries

now you have a 150-160+ spirit wand with minion or life regen.

If you have minion life suffix, use homogenizing omen + greater exaltation omen + greater exalt, you will get minion skill +3⁄4

if you have life regen as the suffix, use homogenizing omen + greater exalt (to force out the minion life mod), then do it again to get the minion skill +3⁄4 mod.

the chances of a +4 is roughly 1⁄3, that’s why u need at least 3-6 homogenising omens.

now you own a +4 minion sceptre with 160 spirit at material cost investment!

I’ve placed mine for sale at 3 div to break even (failures of minion skill+3) included.
