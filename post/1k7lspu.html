As the titles said, I believe runic casters are overtuned. I am a SSF Avatar of Fire Smith of Kitava running a tier 10 - 11 maps with 82 fire res, 77 cold and lightning res and 2k4 health (not yet getting the 3rd ascendency due to substantially lack of damage because of the big strength requirement for 2H mace,… but it should be another story). Whenever I tried expedition, I always had problems with those casters that stayed afar, casting huge ass AoE spell dealing cold or cold/fire dmg that deleted from at least 1⁄3 of my health in one go if white mobs, to straight out unalived me if they are magic with absurd mods like Extra Chaos Damage or a Rare mob. And they spawned about 2 to 3 mobs in a detonation charge, which if I cannot clear the screen I will end up facing 10 to 15 mobs, staying behind a bunch of runic monsters, keeping casting their bullshit spells till I run away in shame or die. This is a bit too much since I am only in tier 10. It makes me feel like capping my resistance gives me nothing, and I should have invested everything into stats and dmg to just delete all of them instead, which is not good at all.
