Monk (Quarterstaff) Early Game Experience

Except for Bell (which is conditional), all of the Tier I and III skills use 75% of base attack speed, for a Class that:

A) has the lowest starting health and stun threshold

  1. has the least access to early damage nodes (speed is just not the same until damage is online)

  2. is aligned with the worst starting defenses (armour being best, and hybrid being generally worse than pure)*

  3. is a melee class with the increased damage received that accompanies that

If GGG want Monk (Quarterstaff) to be quick and nimble as a trade off for being enduring and tanky they really need to take away these attack speed nerfs to the ONLY available skills early game. Granted Quarterstaff attack is good enough, but getting stunlocked while trying to use Killing Palm (which fails to path) to get charges to get stunlocked while using Falling Thunder is rough. Where getting stunned is easier too, because of the low starting health (7 strength). Would love a stun threshold buff (maybe as part of Quarterstaff base).

*I acknowledge that the Devs have stated that Armour/ES has a 300% increased death count, but that’s likely at endgame and buffed up by new class popularity.

After level 6, Monk is fine for campaign. Could use some extra flavour for endgame, but Falling Thunder, Staggering Palm, Ice Strike, Charged Staff, Whirling Assault, Flicker Strike and Gathering Storm are all pretty good.

Made a similar post recently while tilted, took some advice from it and tweaked my gameplay (Wingblast into Hollow Palm). Monk is totally playable as is, it is just very frustrating for the first 6 levels (compared to other classes).
