Plus/Minus & NetRTG watching

I’m starting to see a massive problem crop up among media and fans, where they look at box scores and advanced stats to ascertain what’s going on, but +/- and NetRTG can be extremely deceiving.

If a guy you’re playing with misses all their shots and they get a ton of shots, that’s going to impact your numbers.

Tonight Porzingis was 63 percent from the field and 80 percent from 3. He impacted the ball at the rim. He played lights out and he’s been playing pretty well the last few games, but what has been the reframe? He should be benched for Horford because of +/-? You can’t be serious. He’s probably the starter playing the best right now.

Derrick White and Jaylen Brown combined for 6 of 25… 24% from the field… Why Joe doesn’t recognize players with the hot hand and get them more involved is beyond me, but these guys missing shots should not be somehow a referendum on Porzingis.

Overall there is a lack of critical thinking going on that conflates correlation with causation… Players started slumping when Porzingis came back, so Porzingis is the problem. It’s simply not true.

Derrick White has been slumping for some time now and Jaylen Brown has always been inconsistent; I’m not sure I’ve ever seen such a hot and cold player. He has to be smarter with his shot selection and his handling, but what’s more alarming is his poor defense as of late. I’m not sure what’s going on with Derrick White but his mind isn’t on the game. He made two major boneheaded plays in the previous game that almost cost us the game and he’s just playing like shit. If anyone should be benched it should be him for Pritchard.
