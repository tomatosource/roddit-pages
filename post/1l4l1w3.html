About 4 hours after the 0.2.1 patch dropped, so did this ring! I hadnt played PoE1 before playing PoE2 (that has since changed with the Legacy of Phrecia event), but after almost 1300 hours played one of the rarest rings in the game finally dropped. As of the time of writing this, trade lists only 66 of these being sold with the minimum price being 278 divines. I have no doubt that price will go down though now that rings and amulets have been added to the general loot pool.

While I could use the divs, its a pretty powerful ring and as my first mythic in a PoE game ever, I think I’ll keep it.
