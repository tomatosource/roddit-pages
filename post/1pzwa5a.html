Any examples of guys whose “Final Year on HOF ballot” push failed?

In terms of Hall of Fame voting there seems to be a pattern where for guys who are on their final year of the ballot & who are “close to threshold” in terms of percentage they get a major boost in their final year. For instance: Tim Raines went from 69.8% to 86% (a 16.2% increase); Edgar Martinez went from 70.4% to 85.4% (a 15% increase); Billy Wagner went from 73.8% to 82.5% (a 8.7% increase). Heck Larry Walker went from 54.6% all the way to 76.5% (a whopping 21.9% increase).

Therefore, with the voting for the 2026 Hall of Fame in full swing I’m very curious: Are there any examples of guys who were in a Tim Raines, Edgar Martinez, Billy Wagner situation where they had a close threshold but ended up failing to get enough “final year on ballot” push to be inducted? And just to clarify guys with “major problems” like Bonds, Clemens, and Schilling don’t count.
