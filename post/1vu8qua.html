Creating NBA starting 5s that match the city/franchise identity: Atlantic Division Edition

I created this league about 4 years ago where each year I do a free agency where up to one starter and two bench player can switch their team each year and then I do a draft also to keep up with the league, so these are the rosters that are most up to date. Not every team is equal because of drafted players not living up to potential or others exceeding expectations and messing with the balance.

Will post Central tomorrow night

Boston Celtics: The Celtics always have elite defense, championship contending starting 5, some gym rat white guys, and historically the personality of the players have a focused intensity like a Bird/Garnett/Russell, Cooper Flagg being the face of the C’s is a perfect fit here

Starting Lineup: Collin Gillespie, Dyson Daniels, Cooper Flagg, Jayson Tatum, Jaren Jackson Jr.

Key Bench players: Miles McBride, Ron Harper Jr. Justin Champaigne, Dominick Barlow, Moe Wagner

Brooklyn Nets: The Nets never had a real identity, so I went with some hometown players and used Brooklyn’s city identity of a cultural melting pot so some of these guys have cool international last names. With the color scheme and sleek look of the Nets I think the original vision for the Nets was to have a no nonsense/tough mindset about them as well which never happened in reality lol

Starting Lineup: Fred VanVleet, Donte DiVincenzo, Quentin Grimes, Paolo Banchero, Bam Adebayo

Key Bench Players: Jose Alvarado, Dailyn Swain, Royce O’Neale, Mo Gueye, Sandro Mamukelashvili

New York Knicks: The Knicks run NYC and the national media, so it would be fitting to put the 1 draft pick and the most polarizing personalities of the league on one team, but playing style wise when the Knicks are good they tend to impose their will with their physicality so some of that spirit is incorporated too into this roster

Starting Lineup: Kyrie Irving, AJ Dybantsa, Kevin Durant, Kyle Kuzma, Naz Reid

Key Bench Players: Ty Jerome, Klay Thompson, Keon Ellis, Georges Niang, Derik Queen

Philadelphia 76ers: To me the 76ers fans always valued toughness and the team always have had a divisive star like a Wilt/Barkley/AI/Embiid. Zion never amounted to be at that level, but he is polarizing and fits in well with the love/hate culture of Philly fans along with a blue collar team built around him to give the team a chance (not a good one though)

Starting Lineup: Jrue Holiday, Jalen Green, Mikal Bridges, Zion Williamson, Walker Kessler

Key Bench Players: Darius Acuff Jr., Luke Kennard, Sam Hauser, GG Jackson, Isaiah Stewart

Toronto Raptors: The Raptors since the mid 2010s have relied on international talent, a defensive first mentality, length at all positions, and good culture fits from Kyle Lowry/DeMar DeRozan to Pascal Siakam/Scottie Barnes. So this roster comprises of all of that here with a few home country heroes

Starting Lineup: Jalen Suggs, Josh Giddey, RJ Barrett, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Jakob Poeltl

Key Bench Players: Dennis Schroder, Andrew Nembhard, Javonte Green, Alpha Diallo, Goga Bitadze
