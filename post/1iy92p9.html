The 2017-18 New Orleans Pelicans played 3 straight games in 3 straight days, and managed to win them all

I don’t know if many people remember this from this season, but the Pelicans managed to win 3 games in 3 days (back-to-back-to-back).

The reason why this occurred in the first place was because a game on February 6th, 2018 between the Pelicans and Pacers was postponed due to a waterleak in Smoothie King Center and rescheduled to a different date.

This match against the Pacers was rescheduled to March 21st, inbetween matches against the Mavericks (3⁄20) and Lakers (3⁄22)

Prior to this, the Pelicans came off a big win against the Boston Celtics at home at a record of 40-30.

On March 20th, the Pelicans faced off against the Mavericks (lottery team, Dennis Smith Jr. was on the rise) and won 115-105.

On March 21st, the Pelicans won 96-92 against the Pacers (Oladipo 😢, and Myles Turner is still on the team 7 years later).

On March 22nd, the Pelicans faced off against the Lakers (still had Julius Randle and Lonzo Ball), and won 128-125.

The Pelicans finished with a record of 43-30 after these games.

Notable names on this roster at the time included Anthony Davis, Jrue Holiday, Rajon Rondo, DeMarcus Cousins (out for injury at this point of the season), E’Twaun Moore, Ian Clark, and Nikola Mirotic
