Just stopped playing mid map while listening to the GGG interview

Listened in a bit and all I am hearing so far isn’t making me particularly hopeful that the game is going in a direction I expected it to go in. Things are going to be incrementally improved as time goes on, but after like 10 years of experience with PoE1, I expected PoE2 to build on the learnings of the first game, which isn’t really happening.

I am currently just before maps and bought a nice crossbow - mobs and bosses melt - the campaign is trivial now. Then I realized I had pivoted back to shock burst rounds just like last patch because nothing else felt particularly great.

I guess what makes me a bit apathetic so early in this “league” is already see the road ahead, way earlier than usual: My own builds aren’t going to pay off and I’ll be copying other people’s stuff like last time. 80% of the fun of PoE for me was to come up with my own build ideas, but the variety and depth that I could get in PoE1 is not here - the skills are too narrow and prerequisitional, the skill tree is not build-enabling. You can see it in every creator’s guides as well - its all super similar stuff. That’s boring to me…

Thinking on this, I just stood still in my map a bit and made that “shit, I don’t really care” face from Always Sunny and closed the game. I could easily reach endgame from here, but what’s the point?

Thankfully, the current ARPG space is super saturated, so I’ll check what other options are out there - See Y’all!
