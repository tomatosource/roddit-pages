Where would the best fits be for AD, Sabonis, and Ja if they end up being dealt by the deadline?

I think the way this season is shaking out suggests these are gonna be the three stars up for grabs between now and the deadline. I’ve got ideas of where I think they make sense but let me know your favorite landing spots.

Sabonis: i actually am kind of sold on Washington, they don’t have a leading man or a high end playmaker on the roster. They’re flush with young play finishes and more off ball centric guys, simply using the McCollum or Middleton contract and attaching coulibaly and AJ Johnson for example, feels like a pretty good talent grab for a guy who actually fits kind of perfect, I would argue playing sarr next to him is exactly the kind of second big youd want next to sabonis, a spacing switchable rim protector. Love the fit, makes total sense to me, shoutout Sam Vecenie for coming up with the idea.

Ja: had a hard time with Ja finding somewhere that made a lot of sense to me, I just ended up with Minnesota for him, I think Toronto also kind of works but minny just makes a bit more sense to me, they’d send Naz/conley/dilly/shannon for Ja. Memphis adds an interesting 3rd big in Naz and a couple young pieces with dillingham and Shannon

AD: Orlando feels the most interesting to me for AD, you send Suggs/Carter jr for AD(you have to add some filler on either side but the structure of that is close) and Dallas adds a good fit next to kyrie or in place of kyrie in Suggs, Orlando starts Anthony black in place of Suggs and upgrades Carter jr to AD
