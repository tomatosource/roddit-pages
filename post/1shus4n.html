I’ve Noticed the Knicks have the Worst Sports Fanbase I’ve Seen

I’m an avid Basketball and Football watcher, and I generally enjoy debating rival teams’ fans. Usually, its all in good fun and I personally don’t really mind minor jabs or roasts.

In the football world, I’m a Commanders fan, so I’m no stranger to the main stars of my team getting injured, but I have noticed different reactions. Cowboys fans? Nothing but well wishes when Jayden Daniels went down. Eagles fans? Same story. Every one of our rivals was still wishing JD5 the best.

When Tatum went down, Lakers fans wished him the best. Mavs fans didn’t cheer his injury. Knicks fans? They cheered when he went down, and while I’ve seen a few Knicks fans wishing him well, the majority didn’t wish him well/actively laughed at or cheered at his injury.

This whole thing was prompted when I looked at the postgame discussion on r/NYKnicks and instead of seeing what I’d see on this sub, minor jabs, glazing our own players, or criticizing the other franchise as a whole or the opposing players’ playstyle, their comments were more personally hateful insults. I saw comments saying that Scheierman was a hillybilly and probably inbred, insulting Tatum’s looks, etc.. This isn’t normal postgame banter, it’s outright personal hatred against our Franchise and players.

Have yall noticed this too? It seems the Knicks are specifically more hateful and disrespectful than other fanbases.
