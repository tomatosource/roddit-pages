Kevin Durant to Houston trade breakdown

According to Bobby Marks:

The biggest thing is Houston triggers the second apron next season

To Houston

*⃣Kevin Durant: ($54.7M, UFA 2026)

✍️Note: Durant is eligible to sign a two-year \(122 million extension as of 7/6. If he waits 6-months, he can add an additional \)2 million.

To Phoenix

*⃣Jalen Green (\(33.3, \)36 and $36 million, Player 2027)

✍️Note: 10% trade kicker of his combined salary in 2025-26 and 2026-27. The $6.9M bonus (spread out over 2 years) can be amended or eliminated (will likely have to).

*⃣Dillon Brooks (\(21.2, \)19.9 million, UFA 2027)

✍️Note: Contract includes a $1M bonus for reaching the first round of the playoffs. The bonus is now considered unlikely but will apply to the apron. Brooks is eligible to sign a two-year extension as of July 8.

The Brooks likely bonus (reaching the 1st round) to unlikely is key in this trade.

Houston will aggregate contracts and as a result, the 2nd apron in 2025-26 gets triggered.

I would expect this trade gets expanded for Houston to avoid the first apron (they are currently taking back more $$).
