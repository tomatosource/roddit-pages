The last time the Detroit Pistons won at least 30 of their first 40 games was 20 years ago, when they started the 05/06 season with a 35-5 record through January 25th, 2006!

The Detroit Pistons are the story of the NBA season for me and it’s not even remotely close. A team that only reached the Playoffs in only 6 of the last 20 seasons, not making it past the 1st round since 2007⁄08.

They are on pace to win 61+ games and I think it’s entirely possible they reach that mark, assuming the players stay healthy and the team stays focused.

Coach of the Year will definitely be a lock by the All-Star break if they continue on this run.

Cade is doing a great job being a leader of the team, while everyone else contributes in their own way and it’s such a breath of fresh air to see them rise up to the challenge.
