Is shock close to being mandatory?

Building new characters, I always find myself asking why I shouldn’t be using some lightning skill to inflict it. Then it becomes a question of class identity.
I hate nothing more in these games than meta being amorphous class that could essentially be called “damage multiplier stacker”. While some people enjoy it, I find lack of distinct character in such cases to be incredibly lame, causing me to lose interest.
I started SSF warrior because I wanted to play a bit of PoE2 again, and I ended up thinking about it while playing. While there’s armor break and some other stuff for physical builds, you could always add shock on top and benefit from it. I’m not saying it should be removed or anything, but perhaps there should be a mutually exclusive counterpart for physical damage that’s accessible for non-elemental builds?
