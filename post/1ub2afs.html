There are 9 teams in NBA history that have had 4 All-stars on them, the Celtics have 3 of them.

1962 Celtics:

Bill Russell, Sam Jones, Tom Heinsohn and Bob Cousy were all named all stars.

This team had the mvp (Bill Russell) on it and including him, two other players were also named All-NBA second team.

1975 Celtics:

Dave Cowens, Jo Jo White, John Havlicek, Paul Silas were all named all stars.

This team had 2 all defensive 1st team players and 2 all defensive 2nd team players. 3 of them were also named All-NBA second team

1982-1983 76ers:

Moses Malone, Julius Erving, Maurice Cheeks and Andrew Toney were all named all stars.

This team had 3 all defensive 1st team players and also the 6MOY in Bobby Jones.

1997-1998 Lakers:

Shaquille O’Neal, Eddie Jones, Nick Van Exel, and Kobe Bryant were all named All stars

I have nothing extraordinary to say about this team.

2005-2006 Pistons:

Chauncey Billups, Richard Hamilton, Ben Wallace and Rasheed Wallace were all named All stars.

This team had the DPOY (Ben Wallace) and 3 all defensive team players on it.

2010-2011 Celtics:

Rajon Rondo, Ray Allen, Paul Pierce, and Kevin Garnett were all named All stars.

Not a single player on this team averaged 20 points or above.

2014-2015 Hawks:

Paul Millsap, Kyle Korver, Al Horford and Jeff Teague were all named All stars.

This team also didn’t have a player to average above 20 points. But along with that, the reason this team has 4 all stars is because Dwyane Wade was hurt, and withdrew from the game. His replacement was Kyle Korver.

2016-2017 Warriors:

Klay Thompson, Stephen Curry, Kevin Durant and Draymond Green were named All stars.

This team (and the next one) are the only teams on this list to have 2 of their players average 25 points (Kevin Durant and Stephen Curry). The closest was the 76ers team. This team also had the DPOY (Draymond Green).

2017-2018 Warriors:

Klay Thompson, Stephen Curry, Kevin Durant and Draymond Green were named All stars.

Of all of these teams, only 2 of them didn’t win 60 games or more.

2011 Celtics: 56 - 26
2018 Warriors: 58 - 24

Besides them, every other team won 60 or more games.
