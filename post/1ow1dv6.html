The Denver Nuggets are currently the 2nd best defense in the NBA: Legit or smoke and mirrors?

The Denver Nuggets is no.1 in offense and no.2 in defense right now in the NBA. But do you think their defense is legit or no? Or are they just benefiting from a rather light schedule (though facing elite offenses) . Their worse defensive performance right now over the last 8 games has been the Clippers first half where they allowed 68 points, but then they got shut down in the 2nd half only scoring 22 points in the 3rd and basically garbage time in 4th quarter.

Jamal Murray’s defensive improvement along with Jokic is anchoring this defense. Denver surrounds themselves with big and strong wings alike Peyton Watson and Aaron Gordon while having quick perimeter players like Cam Johnson/Bruce Brown and Braun.
