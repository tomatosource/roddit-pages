[Ganglani] “Keep putting him in trade rumors for second string centers” - Austin Reaves’ agent issues warning amid constant trade rumors

Los Angeles Lakers guard Austin Reaves’ agent, Aaron Reilly of AMR Agency, rightfully doesn’t seem happy about how his client has been dragged into trade dialogues since the team was eliminated by the Minnesota Timberwolves in the first round of the playoffs. Reilly took to X to send a pointed message to his client’s critics and doubters, aiming to silence the noise, but the post has since been deleted, possibly to avoid further backlash or unnecessary attention.

“Next year is going to be a show,” Reilly initially wrote. “People have no idea what’s to come. Keep putting him in trade rumors for second string centers.”

What is Austin’s value?

Reaves keeps popping up in trade scenarios — whether by fans or analysts — because it’s clear the Lakers have major roster reshuffling to do this summer. The undrafted guard also happens to be the team’s most attractive asset, aside from LeBron James and Luka Doncic, who are obviously deemed untouchable.

The 26-year-old is also up for a contract extension this summer, which will be a challenging negotiation process for both the Lakers and Reaves’ camp regarding his value. But for a player earning $12 million this season and putting up 20.2 points and 5.8 assists across 73 regular-season games, the 6’5” guard has certainly delivered on the contract he signed back in the summer of 2023.

Perhaps the only hiccup in Reaves’ time with the Lakers thus far was his performance in this year’s playoffs against the Wolves, where his numbers dropped to 16.2 points and 3.6 assists per game. The three-year guard was clearly bothered by Minnesota’s suffocating perimeter defense and it didn’t help that he was reportedly playing with a left toe sprain during the postseason.

Will the Lakers sacrifice AR for a center?

Reaves’ playoff performance this year will be assessed hard by the Lakers brass, especially when deciding whether he can fit alongside James and Doncic, two ball-dominant superstars who are higher in the hierarchy than him. Even if the Arkansas native has shown signs that he can fit alongside the duo, there are still concerns about the team’s perimeter defense, athleticism and height — something the Lakers must address over the summer.

One way the Lakers are addressing that is by targeting a much-needed center — something that won’t come easily in free agency. That’s why all eyes are on Rob Pelinka to see how he plans to upgrade the roster and which pieces might be moved. And if another superstar suddenly hits the market, things could get even more interesting.

Only time will tell if “AR15” will remain with the Lakers and be awarded another handsome extension. But for now, while he continues being put in trade machines and involved in topics about being shopped around, his camp wants the world to know that it’s only fueling Reaves to get better.

With the offseason looming and speculation swirling, Reaves is using all the noise as motivation. Whether he stays or goes, one thing’s certain — he’s far from finished proving himself. The undrafted guard knows that in this league, growth never stops, and neither will he.
