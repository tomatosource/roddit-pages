The Map design in this game is the worst I’ve ever seen out of any ARPG

I have farmed so many maps this season for the temple charges and I must say, I hate mapping. Too many Maps just feel so bad to run. I have never felt that making you have the tab map open 100% of the time so you can navigate maps was ever a great idea.

My solution to maps listed in order of importance

-Get rid of small path winding maps like Trenches and Mire

-Get rid of all the useless dead ends

-Fire whoever thought it was a good idea to add doors into maps and then remove all the doors.

-If you make a map like Rugosa- where it is just one big ass circle… please for the love of god make sure checkpoints are evenly spread out. Nothing sucks more than walking my slow bear ass down to the bottom only to realize the boss entrance is at the top and there’s no checkpoints

-I don’t gaf about trees and rocks. get rid of all these useless props that do absolutely nothing but make you stuck. Some of the forest maps are just atrocious to navigate.

-Make maps 30%-40% smaller

-Do something with Unique maps. they are utterly useless. I actively avoid them.

Mapping is the endgame. I doubt they will revamp it away. Please make Mapping fun again.
