SSF guide v2.0: How to target farm specific things and what each piece of content drops in 0.5

I am lvl 97 in SC SSF, and I am still compiling this list, please give a hand

SSF is great now, you have a lot of direction for target farming specific loot drops if you are knowledgeable enough

  • Do Fortress to get:
    • Atlas passive points which buffs all your maps with: essences, wisps, strongboxes, shrines, rogue exiles, +2 mods on tablets, and many others
    • +1 permanent level from Martyr
    • Tip: Explore for Matriarch/Patriarch citadels later (red beams in the fog) to mass complete the fortress. Reminder: +waystone chance makes you drop two fragments.
  • Do Abyss to get:
    • Abyss crafting currency
    • Good General currency
    • Abyss unique desecrated jewel from Kulemak
  • Do Ritual to get:
    • Specific Unique items
    • Omens
    • Ritual only idols
  • Do Breach to get:
    • Amulets, rings, belts with minion mods
    • Amulets, rings, belts with caster mods
    • Breach rings
    • Catalysts for ring and amulet quality
    • Unique amulet bases that reserve a spirit skill without costing spirit (but with -1 suffix, -1 prefix)
    • Altered collarbone (can add otherworldly breach mods to an item without needing to craft it from genesis tree)
    • Good General Currency
  • Do Temple to get:
    • Vaal currency
    • Irradiated tablets
    • General Currency
    • Vaal orbs
    • Atziri special supports
    • Double corruption
    • Soul Cores & Ancient Soul Cores (core destabilizer room)
    • Mechanical limbs (lost on death)
    • Lineage skillgems
  • Do Delirium to get:
    • Amulet instilling
    • Very Large Radius on a timeless jewel
    • Emotion crafting for jewels
    • Double instilled amulets
    • Effect of Prefix / Suffix mod liquids
    • Grand Mirror map juicing
    • Megalomania jewel
  • Do Sekhema to get:
    • Ascendancy
    • Jewels
    • Time-lost jewels
    • Against the Darkness jewel
    • Tip: You can combine one of each color jewel to make a diamond (including time-lost variants). But be careful, this will eat up your exalts, only do it if rich.
  • Do Trial of Chaos to get:
    • Ascendancy
    • Chaos orbs
    • Soul cores
    • Adorned jewel (2% chance)
  • Do Expedition to get:
    • Runestones
    • Alloys (also needed for final league challenge to unlock cosmetics)
    • Fluxes (res swap on gear)
    • Runic ward skillgems
    • +1 permanent passive unlock
    • Secret wand quest
    • Masterwork runes (upgrades greater runes in a socket to perfect runes)
    • Expedition Logbooks
    • Expedition Sagas
    • Currency
  • Spend your gold for:
    • +3 amulets (gambling amulets at Ange NPC)
  • Do wisps to get:
    • Idols
    • Rite of Passage charm
  • Do rogue exiles to get:
    • Tons of rings and amulets and belts
    • Specifc DEX/STR/INT gear
    • (if abyss in map) Abyss influenced rogue exiles drop the Kulemak desecrated jewel very commonly
  • Do shrines
    • For extra mapping speed/power
    • To get ilvl82 rare drop loot explosions from “bosses get buffed by shrine instead” atlas node
    • Examples:
      • Enlightened = Jewels explosion (No equipement)
      • Shock = Lightning tag loot explosion (lot of shock duration)
      • Crit shrine = Critical tag loot explosion
      • etc. (PLEASE TELL ME MORE IN COMMENTS)
  • Do boss rush maps (with irradiated tabs, or overseer tabs, or no tablets)
    • To get way more tablet drops
    • How it works: This is because each non-league mechanic tablet slot gives increased tablet drop chance from random content appearing in map. If for example you fill all 3 tablet slots with 3x abyss tablets, you will only see abyss mechanic, and it will drop way less abyss tabs.
  • Do unique maps for:
    • Untainted paradise: XP
    • Mirror lake: For -1 prefix, +1 suffix amulet and ring bases
    • Anomaly maps: For rare lineage support gems
  • Do cleansed maps for:
    • Fracturing orbs
    • Tip: Summoning circles also help you get fracture orbs (get tablets with summon circles)
  • Do specific biomes to get:
    • Cities: Juiced 4 tablet maps
    • Forest: Lineage supports
    • Swamp: Target farm chaos/exalts
    • Desert: Target farm regals/alchs
    • Mountain: Target farm tablets/runes
    • Grass: Target farm perfect currencies
    • Water: Currency
  • Enable Jado to get:
    • More exceptional items and unique strongboxes
    • More lineage skillgems
    • Buffing multiple tablets of the same kind
  • Enable Doriany to get:
    • More revives and juice
    • Chance to shortcut via exploration
    • Chance to find citadels (and teleport to them)
    • Terraforming maps to a specific biome
  • Enable Hilda to get:
    • More rares and uniques
    • More tablet effects for each different tablet
    • Bossing boosts
