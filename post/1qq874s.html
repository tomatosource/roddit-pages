This game has me in a strangle hold and I can’t escape it.

This is going to be a long one so be prepared!!

I know this game is far from perfect, especially for those of you coming from POE 1, but man I can’t put this game down. I got heavily invested in it when it first dropped, and put around 220 hours into it before any league mechanics hit. I purchased up to the thaumaturge supporter pack, bought the game on Steam as well as Xbox for when I didn’t feel like going downstairs and playing at my PC setup, and I had an amazing time. eventually I put the game down and actually ended up not picking it back up until this league, so I took nearly a year break. I picked it back up probably 3-4 weeks into the new league, and my steam hours went from 220 to just over 400, and that doesn’t include the probably 30-50 hours I’ve also dropped into it on the xbox. I’ve also spent another 300$ or so on supporter packs and gotten nearly everything out of the loot box.

I want to add that I have a job, working 3 12’s and having 4 days off, so I end up not playing much if at all for the 3 days, and save my time for those 4 days. I’m not making this post as a “ Please help me, I want out “, but its pretty bad. Just yesterday, I decided to gamble / give away all of my wealth in the game ( roughly 5k div’s, and all of my gear that didn’t poof on the double corrupt coming out to another couple thousand div ), I just did like a mass give away in chat, cleared most of my stashes and deleted my 2 characters, I thought ” I think I’m officially done with the league, let me give away my stuff and finally rest.. “. We’ll, this is where it gets really fun. I then went up stairs, hung out with my family ( Believe it or not I still did that anyways, i work nights so when I’m off work, I play through the night while they sleep ) and then sat down to throw on some youtube. What videos come up? POE 2 is nearly every other video in my feed, so I start watching and after an hour or so, I immediately have that urge to play again. I told myself, man I never got around to actually building a temple, my wealth came from Arbiter farming and Citadel hopping, I should really go in on the league mechanic at least once, so I launched the game up on the Xbox ( it being upstairs in my bedroom ) and started my new character. I made it to the end of act 2 before going to bed, and I have no regrets. I fully intend on continuing to play, this time focusing on mechanics I didn’t on my other characters.

TLDR: I play this game a lot, gave away everything I owned thinking I was taking a break, proceeded to instantly start up a new character the same day. I think I may have a problem.

Thanks for sticking around, hopefully all of the gear I gave away helped out rather it was sold or used, and I’m excited to get back to that end game grind :D
