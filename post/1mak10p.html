Lich ED/Contagion overview

Hey, We are towards the end of league now, I played my Lich for the whole league 0.2.

0.1 I played minions.

So I wanted to give a overview of pros and cons for people looking to make this build (Lich ED/Contagion) now or in 0.3.

Cons: Leveling- Acts 1-3 can feel a bit sluggish especially until you get Dark Effigy Totems spell They provide good Direct Damage.

You will want to have spell/chaos damage armor/weapons, having a early +1 to chaos spells wand is the trick.

Bosses- You need to have the proper resistances per each boss fight or you will fail HARD. Lich is squishy in act 1-3. Janmara and doryami will continue to slaughter you until you have the exact resistance to negate them. I found it hard to try to outplay the bosses without the resistances

Gearing- Very Niche stats needed, You need what you need and nothing else. Chaos dmg ,cast speed, energy shield- Also chaos resistance is as important as fire,cold,lightning due to this build being a high focused Energy Shield build. (Chaos dmg does double damage to energy shield)

Endgame- You will need to avoid certain Mods in maps. Some Nuke the build. 60%slower energy shield recharge is one of those nukes. (Good thing we can sell maps for decent gold) not much of a problem but something to watch out for.

Don’t rush in! Drop that totem first and throw contagion / ED. I’ve had deaths I could of avoided due to thinking I can just rush in and get stunned to death / locked in a group of mobs because my ED missed.

Cast speed & AOE on your spells is important. Without both you will find yourself getting stunned A LOT.

Arbiter t4 you need to be capped in your gear- But still have to play all his mechanics out. Cool but a learning curve, not for nothing I avoid him.

Not a CRIT build, in 0.2 crit builds are top meta. This ain’t one of them.

Can’t 1 shot bosses

Delirium- Not worth it lol…. Build can’t handle T4.

Pros: Great mapper Tanky Great at all trials - trials are a breeze for this build. (bulk of my money comes from running trials , expeditions,breach) Does all content (delirium a bit scary) Consistent farmer of trials. Great loot.

Unique play style with some good options in passive tree to tweak to your liking.

Pretty cheap to get geared. You can craft all the get for pretty low. Chaos essences are not expensive really. I’d say 2Div and you can get to feeling good.

I don’t have my char uploaded on POB: Maybe I figure it out later.

I do think In 0.3 il be doing the same build, I love the play style / smoothness and think I can min/max it further.
