What is your biggest nitpick about the NBA?

Hot Take Culture: Pundits and fans feel the need to create sensationalist arguments to generate clicks and engagement. Simply praising a player isn’t as interesting as dissecting their every mistake.

The “GOAT” Debate: The obsession with ranking players and constantly comparing them to all-time greats means that current players are often held to an impossible standard. Anything short of perfection is viewed as a strike against their legacy.

Analytics Overload: While advanced stats are valuable, sometimes they are used to diminish a player’s impact. A player could be making the right passes and creating open looks for teammates, but if their own shooting percentage is down, they’re “underperforming.”
