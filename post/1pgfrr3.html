Don’t sleep on Infernalist

Infernalist was already pretty good although it wasn’t a prime choice due to not being the fastest class.

Ignite applying to every damage type is a straight damage buff and I used it to great effect in 0.3 with arc - lightning conduit.

That being said, the real juice is demon form. It’s classified as a Shapeshift. Considering we are getting the druid side of the tree fleshed out, there might be some insane nodes for shapeshifting which will indirectly buff demon form.
