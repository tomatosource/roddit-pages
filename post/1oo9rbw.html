Does Shota Imanaga have the Yusei Kikuchi problem?

One thing that stood out looking at Imanaga’s profile is the 4 degrees lower arm angle and the increased sweeper usage. Because this is something us Angels fans saw play out with Kikuchi.

During the off season Kikuchi worked on developing a new sweeper. But he needed to lower his arm angle to get the right break on that pitch. But this carried over when he started the season and he found himself still unintentionally pitching with that lower arm slot. Which he felt hurt the snap on his other usual pitches. So he worked hard to get his arm angle back to norm. And when asked said “no more sweepers” after that experiment went awry.

https://www.mlb.com/news/why-yusei-kikuchi-stopped-throwing-sweeper

Back to Imanaga, his “rising” fastball has had worse vertical drop this year. And more horizontal run. Same with the splitter having more cut action. So it seems like the arm slot is definitely playing a role here, and perhaps why his fastball has not been nearly as effective. Perhaps his increased sweeper usage has been hurting him like it did to Kikuchi.
