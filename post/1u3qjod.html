0.5 is the best league in PoE 2

I’ve been playing the 0.5 league in PoE 2 and honestly… I’m having a great time.

Yeah, there are bugs. But they don’t ruin the experience for me. The new mechanics are solid overall – some things take time to figure out, but that’s just part of PoE.

Currency is also in a good spot. You don’t need huge investment to start making divines, and it feels pretty easy to keep progressing and min-maxing your character.

GGG is also doing a really good job this league. They’re reacting fast, pushing a lot of patches and bug fixes, and the response time has been genuinely impressive. That deserves credit.

For context – I started in Necropolis league in PoE 1. I’ve got ~1500h in PoE 1 and 600+ in PoE 2. And for the first time, I actually feel really optimistic about PoE 2. I can’t wait for 1.0.

I’m also thinking about going HC soon, since in PoE 1 I mostly play HC/HCSSF.

About the Atlas – it’s good, but definitely still needs work. There are a lot of points, but some of them feel kind of pointless (like small % gold bonuses in specific biomes). It could use some tightening.

I know Zizaran talked about a lot of important issues in his recent interview, so I’m hopeful things will keep improving.

Right now I’m kinda split – I want to try new builds, but I’m still enjoying my level 97 too much to drop it.
I’ve already maxed all Atlas trees, only Temple left, and I’ve just started it.

I see a lot of negativity around this league, but to me it honestly feels like a big step forward.

What do you think?
