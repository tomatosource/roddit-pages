Currency drops should be more generous

Have you found nicely rolled magics, or 4 affix rares, and just sold them for gold? Have you considered slamming those exalts only to realize you can trade a better item for juste 1ex, without the risk?

Crafting is in a bad state, even if things are better than 0.1 we are still miles away from a good system.

I think the randomness is a design choice by now and I will focus on how GGG can improve crafting without making it more deterministic.

One obvious thing that comes to mind is regal exalt and chaos drop rate. With today’s drop rates, I can transmute, augment and maybe regal. But if these 3 affixes are not great I will never exalt, just because a bad affix means you have a very small chance of making something better than what you can buy.

This creates an experience where 99% of ground loot is instantly shit after I ID it.

If exalts dropped 5x more often or so, I could give a shot to maybe 5% of my items which already would make things more exciting.

This would impact prices of course where 1 div would be worth way more exalts but I dont think it would affect buying power, so why not do it?

Also this is my personal feedback with 5-10 div of gear to my name, so I have no idea of how good crafting becomes when you reach the true endgame?
