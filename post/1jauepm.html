I Played POE2 the Way Chris Intended

I picked up every dang rare that my main and alt can possibly use. I even picked up every magic and normal on high level bases I can use and craft on the spot with crafting currency I carry. Yet, most of my gears are traded.

To be fair, I only got into T14+ and running juiced maps in the last 4 weeks. But that’s still 100s of high level maps I have gone through. So far, I have got 3 divines, 1 L20 gem, 1 greater essence. In contrast, I got 3 divines , 1 perfect jeweller’s orb, and 1 greater essence running 3 floor Sekhemas and campaign on the way to ascending 3 sorcs, 2 pathfinders, 2 witches, and 1 deadeye. I got all other classes into cruel but couldn’t stand their play style.

It is thoroughly a waste of time. Most of my wealth is from farming 4-floor Sekhemas, 10L Ultimatum, and farming boss keys with 1k waystone drop chance. This isn’t how the end game should be, is it? I would have sold Breach keys, but the fight is pretty fun and rewarding. So, I didn’t.

The way I see it, either a. random drop algorithm should be tweaked to make end game random more useable, or b. up crafting currency drops to overcome slam randomness, or c. scaling drop more sharply to map tiers, or d. introduce more controlled crafting, or e. a combination thereof.

For the love of god, given that trade is here to stay, put in a decent in-game trading and listing already. Don’t get me wrong. POE2 is still great despite its shortcomings. I would still play 0.2.0 even if none of the above is done.
