Armour and why GGG is wrong about it

Armour as a defence has a fundamental problem that isn’t just numerical and adding %of Armour applies to elemental damage just adds to it.

You can make a character with 50k Armour with some serious investment. You can even get that character to 5-6k hp.

This will make your character super tanky in maps, but you’ll have 9k ish effective hp to a slam give or take.

With 100% of armour applies to elemental damage and 75% all res, this will let you take a max hit of 25k ish in the elemental.

This all sounds amazing, but the problem is two-fold.

  • A character can relatively easily reach 10k es and 75% all res and is not only tankier, but with ci is immune to chaos as well

-but mostly, to reach 5.5k hp and 50k armour and 100% of armour applies to elemental damage, you have to have a very large investment on the passive tree, but worse, you need absolutely insane levels of gear. You need 3 perfect prefixes on shield, body armour, boots and gloves As well as strength and armour applies to elemental damage in your suffixes

And you have to cap elemental res and you have to cap chaos res.

Meanwhile you can reach 10k es with absolutely mid tier gear and it’s instantly better than almost perfect armour based gear.

The amount of affixes you need to have on gear to make a tanky life/armour based is just so much greater than an es character that needs just 2 prefixes and capping resses to be better already.
