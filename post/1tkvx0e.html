PSA: plant skills cast by totems/proxies cannot be overgrown/trigger accelerated growth

Considering Ancestral Bond rework and the possible totem popularity increase, I thought it was worth mentioning. (They even showcased their own totem plant build last league)

When your totems cast entangle/thrashing vines, watering the plants won’t make them overgrow/trigger accelerated growth, and neither will the passive notable Boundless growth, which gives the plants a 20% chance to immediately overgrow. It hasn’t worked at the 0.4 league start and I couldn’t find it in any patch notes.

I found some mentions that Abyssal Apparition apparently has some issues with it too, but the topic seems pretty obscure overall, so I’m making the post hoping it at least shows up when someone googles it, or maybe they even fix it.
