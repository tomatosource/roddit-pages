A little bit of history: the Clippers and Lakers are playing home games tomorrow simultaneously for the first time in over 25 years

When the Lakers tip-off tomorrow against the Blazers, it will be the first time both LA teams play simultaneous home games since May 5, 1999. At the LA Sports Arena, the Clippers lost to the Sonics 107-105, while the Lakers beat the Blazers at Staples Center, 119-91.
