Jaylen Brown may be the most coddled player in this subs history

If you point out that JB grades out universally poorly in advanced metrics there’s something wrong with those metrics. If you point out his negative on/off then lineup data is simply flawed. And the funniest part is JB is the only “star” that is so bad in all these metrics consistently throughout his career. Now don’t forget if you bring these stats up you’re either a nerd or have something against him personally lol.

What kills me the most though is the lack of expectation. JB is on a supermax the conversation should be that he needs to be more impactful; not that these metrics are simply inherently flawed and woefully unimportant. You can’t be untouchable in trade talks whilst having fringe All Star level impact.

Now I also don’t understand why this sub views him as untradeable. JB realistically is a top 25 or so guy and is paid like a top 5 guy. He still can’t carry lineups without Tatum on the floor; he had a negative on/off again this year. His best attributes are probably some of the least valuable a star can have. He doesn’t make anyone on the team better.

This is why I pushback on the Giannis or bust mentality some people have. I genuinely think keeping JB at his current price point effectively closes our window. Even replacing him with impactful roleplayers that fit JT better would be a better option than keeping him here on that absurd contract.
