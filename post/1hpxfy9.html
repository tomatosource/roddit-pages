Competing executives around the league were surprised to learn that the Sacramento Kings fired head coach Mike Brown. The Kings (13-19) were underperforming, but the franchise recently extended his multiyear contract.

Those same executives now wonder how long De’Aaron Fox will remain in Sacramento.

Fox will be extension-eligible this offseason with hopes of earning All-NBA honors and a supermax contract starting at approximately \(59.5 million in 2026-27. That seems out of reach, and his max extension would start at \)51 million (which is still a massive payday)—a number he can get from any team (unlike the supermax, which could only be from Sacramento).

Grumblings in NBA circles suggest Fox will push for a trade before the February 6 deadline and that the Kings will likely entertain all options.

One team stands out as their best potential trade partner: the Houston Rockets
