Big Queen of the Forest Buff this league. Don’t sleep on it.

Queen of the Forest has a massive amount of % Evasion, but it’s a low level base item. The good thing is that it’s Level 51.

This means we can use Unique Verisium Runeforging on it, since:

After unlocking Unique Verisium Runeforging in Act 3 you can upgrade the base type of Unique Weapons and Armours that drop at levels lower than 55. Upgraded Unique Weapons will have higher damage, and Upgraded Armours will have increased base defences as well as Runic Ward. Allowing them to be more competitive at higher levels. Unique Armours above level 55 can still be Runeforged to modify their defences to include Runic Ward at the cost of reducing their other defenses similar to the base types.

This means we can not only get a lot more Base Evasion on our QoF, it also comes with Ward.

But is that all? Nope. Take a look at this:

The amount of Evasion granted by items and modifiers has been increased as you get to higher levels. At level 65, you should have approximately 33% more evasion, tapering down to 15% at level 80+.

Now, it says “Evasion granted by items”, which I’m pretty sure is the base amount. I’m not going to say we are getting 33% more evasion on it, but we are getting some extra from it.

Over all, this is going to be great for Queen of the Forest.

Deflect is also now something that works. With 30k Evasion, we only need around 70% of Evasion as Deflect to reach 95% Deflect.
