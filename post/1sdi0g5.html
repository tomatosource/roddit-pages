Alex Antetokounmpo is probably actually in the running for the worst/least qualified NBA player of all time

Alex Antetokounmpo played in garbage time for the Bucks today, actually scoring and dishing out an assist in the last two minutes to Thanasis.

If that sounds like unnecessary slander, of course it is, but it’s also not an exaggeration.

https://www.basketball-reference.com/gleague/players/a/antetal01d.html

In Europe as a developing young player, he shot 20% from the field https://www.basketball-reference.com/international/players/alex-antetokounmpo-1.html
