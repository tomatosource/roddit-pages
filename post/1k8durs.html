If you were to run some quick statistical analysis on this, the dropoff would be highly statistically significant. A quick method of looking at how expected an outcome is statistically is called a z-score, which is calculated off of some simple sample statistics, in this case based off of Ant’s free throw attempts on drives. A z-score of 0 more or less means an outcome is normal or expected in a statistical sense. A z-score of around 2 is considered pretty abnormal (in statistics, this is a common threshold for statistical significance). The z-score when calculated on Ant’s drive free throw stats is roughly 5.8, suggesting something highly abnormal is going on.
