I feel like its one of the most weirdly if not annoying extra content to deal with, especially with how hard they are to control.

There MANY many instances where the wisp is basically a few feet aways from the targeted mob, while some people may like this as they get to kill the targeted rare immediately, then it completely minimizes the number of influenced mobs you kill then you end up most likely not getting the full benefit of the spirit mechanic itself. Does anyone know if the Omen of the Hunt alleviates this issue? Does the tracked influenced mobs counter continue from the last slayed rare or it resets and doesnt really solve the issue?
