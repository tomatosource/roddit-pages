5 Chicago Cubs Cubs +1 The Cubs roared out of the blocks after the trade deadline with a 6-1 week headlined by a sweep of the Dodgers. Javier Assad outdueled Tarik Skubal on Tuesday, ruining his Dodger debut and the offense scored at least 5 runs in each of the 3 games. They followed that up with a dramatic comeback against the Blue Jays, with Alex Bregman hitting a game tying home run off of Louis Varland with the Cubs down to their last strike after being shut out by Dylan Cease. Over the weekend, the trade deadline headliners made their debut against the Royals with mixed results. Kevin Gausman pitched 7 inning allowing only 2 runs, but Clay Holmes was understandably rusty in his first MLB appearance following a 3 month injury absence. The Cubs have played at a 109 win pace for the past 2 months, but are still 5.5 games back on the Brewers and have only made up 2.5 games in that span. They will look to maintain their momentum with series against the Nationals and then the Cardinals over the weekend. 69-50
6 Boston Red Sox Red Sox +1 Suffering our first series loss in over a month to the A’s may not have been the best way to end the streak, but I can’t be bothered to care at this point. This past month has been such a fun ride, and it really does seem like this squad has the bones to make a deep run. Any pen where Weissert and Slaten are your bottom of the barrel is doing well, our starters have been the best in the league despite a season functionally without Crochet, and our lineup has excelled with some unproven, low expectation guys establishing themselves when put in a regular starting spot. Oh, and shoutouts to Raymond Burgos, six innings over two days to reset the pen was invaluable amidst a 16 game stretch with no off days. 64-53
7 New York Yankees Yankees -2 It’s a prototypical week for the Yankees, which means I don’t know what to think of this team: there’s some bad, there’s some good, and then there are just damn perplexing things happening. Offense has been anemic! I don’t know, it seems as mid as my Gen Z lingo, but at least they aren’t drowning yet. But with a surging, firing-hot AL East, mid may just not do it. There is a whole lot of season left in these last two months, including the arrival of George Lombard Jr, who provided some hope and a bunch of joy to this team and fanbase. The defense upgrade alone has me calming my nerves a bit, but the power is sure to make me smile wide too. That said, since Judge has been on the IL, they have been nothing but mid, downright sour, but considering where they could be, maybe there’s some copium involved here. That said, this team perplexes me, and surprises me, let’s hope it continues to be the ones that create joy and hope. 66-52
