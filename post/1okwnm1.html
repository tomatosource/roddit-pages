List of every NBA team that has gone at least 5-0 to start the season

There have been exactly 27 franchises that have had at least one 5-0 start to an NBA season. Here’s a list of all of those franchises in order of most consecutive wins to start a season:

Golden State Warriors: 24-0 (2015-16) [Lost in the NBA Finals in Game 7]

Houston Rockets: 15-0 (1993-94) [NBA Championship Winner]

Cleveland Cavaliers: 15-0 (2024-25)

Dallas Mavericks: 14-0 (2002-03)

Boston Celtics: 14-0 (1957-58) [Lost in the NBA Finals in Game 6]

Chicago Bulls: 12-0 (1996-97) [NBA Championship Winner]

Oklahoma City Thunder/Seattle SuperSonics: 12-0 (1982-83)

Los Angeles Lakers: 11-0 (1997-98)

Atlanta Hawks: 11-0 (1997-98)

Portland Trail Blazers: 11-0 (1990-91)

Philadelphia 76ers: 10-0 (2000-01) [Lost in the NBA Finals in Game 5]

Milwaukee Bucks: 9-0 (2022-23)

Indiana Pacers: 9-0 (2013-14)

Detroit Pistons: 9-0 (1970-71)

New Orleans Pelicans: 8-0 (2010-11)

Denver Nuggets: 8-0 (1976-77)

New York Knicks: 7-0 (1993-94) [Lost in the NBA Finals in Game 7]

Washington Wizards: 7-0 (1974-75) [Lost in the NBA Finals in Game 4]

Toronto Raptors: 6-0 (2018-19) [NBA Championship Winner]

Memphis Grizzlies: 6-0 (2014-15)

Minnesota Timberwolves: 6-0 (2001-02)

Utah Jazz: 6-0 (1998-99)

Miami Heat: 5-0 (2011-12) [NBA Championship Winner]

Phoenix Suns: 5-0 (1984-85)

Sacramento Kings: 5-0 (1995-96)

Los Angeles Clippers: 5-0 (1985-86)

San Antonio Spurs: 5-0 (2025-26) [TBD]

The three NBA franchises that have never had a 5-0 start in their entire history as an NBA team are the Brooklyn Nets, the Charlotte Hornets and the Orlando Magic.

Edit: I’ve updated the list and added a tag for the teams that made it to at least the NBA Finals during their franchise’s best start. For the OKC Thunder, their best start to a season was the 7-0 start last year which lead to an NBA Championship.

Fun Fact: Despite starting the 1970-71 NBA season with a franchise best 9-0 record, the Pistons didn’t qualify for the playoffs.

Fun Fact #2: Despite starting the 1985-86 NBA season with a franchise best 5-0 record, the Clippers ended the season with an 32-50 record.
