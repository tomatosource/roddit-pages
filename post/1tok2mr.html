New Players Avoid These Traps

Id like to create a thread to divert new players from falling into bad build guides and other traps. Specifically creators and websites.

Off the top of my head i can think of 3 things to avoid.

Kaidgames is a “creator” who rips other players builds but also just puts out complete utter garbage thats not accurate or up to date

Fextralife their wiki is garbage please never click the link

Anything on youtube that says Uber Dan basically because its all just ai garbage thats mostly a joke but can bait new players

Any others?

Edit: More submissions

“Path of Exile Builds” youtube channel. It’s clickbaity, ai thumbnails and the builds I saw recommended for league starting are by far what you’d consider a league starter. Apparently there’s paywalled builds too. Thanks to u/Queen_Natalie for the submission

Spud The King on youtube posts guides that are seemingly fine but have a few misleading pitfalls. First off in his guides he always seems to start with gold and buys items for the campaign which you will not have. In the video i reviewed he mentioned having minion skill gem levels on scepters starting at level 2 in 0.5 which is not possible as far as i know. It’s also all AI thumbnails and pretty clickbaity garbage.
