QOL: Can We PLEASE Always See Item Levels on Items?

Hey Exiles,

There’s a quality-of-life feature that I believe would significantly improve the player experience in Path of Exile 2, both for veterans and especially for newcomers: always displaying the item level on items, whether they’re on the ground, in your inventory, or at a vendor. This means no needing to pick them up, hover, or spam ‘Alt’ to check this crucial stat.

I know that you can currently pick up an item and hold ‘Alt’ to see its item level without identifying it. That’s a helpful existing feature! And yes, loot filters can indeed filter by item level. However, I’m arguing for something even more streamlined and immediate that benefits a wider range of gameplay scenarios.

Why Always Showing Item Level Matters:

  • Instant Crafting Potential Assessment for Specific Projects: So much of PoE’s endgame revolves around crafting, and item level dictates the highest tiers of affixes an item can roll. Seeing that item level directly on the ground, without any extra steps, immediately tells you if a base has the potential for specific high-tier mods you’re hunting. For example, if you’re looking for boots with the highest possible movement speed roll (35%), you know you’ll need an item level 82 base or higher. Seeing that number instantly tells you if a pair of boots is even worth considering for such a craft.
  • Streamlined Inventory Management & Vendor Interactions:
    • No More ‘Alt’-Spamming & Visual Discomfort: It’s genuinely annoying to constantly spam the ‘Alt’ key when inspecting a full inventory tab of items you’ve picked up, or when gambling at a vendor (e.g., for rings or belts). The repeated toggling of the detailed item description on and off is also visually jarring and uneasy on the eyes, disrupting flow and causing unnecessary strain. Having item levels always visible would be a huge relief for quick sorting and decision-making.
    • Reforging Bench Efficiency & Avoiding Costly Mistakes: When using crafting stations like the reforging bench, you often want to use items within a very specific item level range. A critical mechanic here is that the reforging bench always outputs an item with the item level of the lowest input item. Accidentally including a single low-ilvl item can downgrade your entire crafted base, potentially ruining a high-value project. Always seeing the item level would prevent these costly mistakes and streamline your workflow.
  • Targeted Crafting & Base Management: Even though loot filters can hide items below a certain item level, there are often cases where you want to keep lower item level bases for different crafting projects (e.g., specific delve mods, lower-tier essence spamming, or different meta crafting strategies where high ilvls might dilute the mod pool). Always seeing the item level allows for quick, visual confirmation of which item level range a drop belongs to, letting you decide on the fly if it fits your current specific crafting needs without interrupting your flow.
  • Hyper-Efficient Loot Sifting: While filters are powerful, manually sifting through drops for specific properties still happens. Not having to pick up, hold ‘Alt’, then potentially drop if it’s not the right ilvl (for this specific project) saves precious time over the course of hundreds of maps. It removes a small, but persistent, friction point.
  • Trading Clarity: Item level is a fundamental part of an item’s value, especially for crafting bases. Knowing it instantly on the ground or in your inventory means less time spent evaluating potential pickups that might end up being worthless due to being the wrong ilvl for a common trade target.
  • Lowering the Learning Curve (Significantly!): While ‘Alt’ exists and filters are customizable, many new players don’t immediately grasp the importance of item level or the ‘Alt’ key’s function in revealing it. Crucially, new players frequently confuse “Item Level” with “Required Level”, leading them to think a level 68 item has high-tier potential just because it requires a character level 68 to equip. Making item level a constantly displayed piece of information helps them intuitively understand why certain items are valuable and others aren’t, directly from seeing the drop or reviewing their inventory. It demystifies a core aspect of item potential and helps prevent common early-game misunderstandings. You might even say, it helps prevent one from “getting the wrong ‘level’ of understanding” about an item’s true potential!
  • Zero Downside: This isn’t a hidden mechanic or a ‘discovery’ element. It’s pure statistical data. Displaying it adds minimal UI clutter (it’s just a number!) and significantly enhances clarity. We already see sockets, links, quality, implicits… why not item level, which is arguably more important for an item’s potential for endgame crafting?

This isn’t about making the game “easier” in terms of mechanics, but about making it more transparent and less frustrating for players trying to engage with its core systems, particularly when it comes to the constant stream of loot and complex crafting.

What do you all think? Is this something you’d like to see as a default display in PoE2? Let’s discuss!

TL;DR: Even though you can see item level with ‘Alt’ after picking up, and loot filters can use it, I’m advocating for always showing item levels on all items (ground, inventory, vendors) in PoE2. It would greatly improve specific crafting assessment (e.g., needing ilvl 82+ for 35% MS on boots), targeted base management, eliminate ‘Alt’-spamming (and the associated visual annoyance), prevent costly mistakes at reforging benches (where the output ilvl is the lowest input), and help new players understand the crucial difference between item level and required level, enhancing overall player clarity with no real downsides.
