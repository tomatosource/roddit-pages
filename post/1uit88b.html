Canada Basketball has announced that NBA MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander will be among those playing for Canada this coming weekend in a FIBA Americas qualification window.

Atlanta’s Nickeil Alexander-Walker, Phoenix’s Dillon Brooks, Indiana’s Andrew Nembhard and Dallas’s Ryan Nembhard are among those who will play in the window.

Several players are with the team but won’t play, including Luguentz Dort, Bennedict Mathurin, RJ Barrett, Zach Edey, Dwight Powell and Kelly Olynyk.
