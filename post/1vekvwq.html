I am more excited than ever for this upcoming season.

I’d like to preface this by saying I recognize this is a hot take, and after all, it is just my opinion.

I am more excited than ever to watch the Celtics this year. Maybe it’s just that I’m a Tatum stan, but I’m pumped to see how he holds up as the clear cut #1 option. I’m sure most of you have seen the stats regarding the C’s winning percentage when he exceeds a certain usage threshold, I want to see whether or not that holds weight. This will be JT’s chance to prove to the association that he is in fact an all timer.

Furthermore, the pressure that the entire franchise has experienced over the last half decade (disregard last year) has subsided. That matters.

This is why, as much as I love seeing the Curry in green posts, I don’t know if I’d want to see him take this upcoming chance from Tatum.

All love for JB btw, I hope the 76ers end in catastrophe.
