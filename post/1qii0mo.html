Percentages calculated from the franchise’s first year of ML play through most recent year of HOF eligibility (2020). Team is credited with a HOFer for that season if the player made at least one appearance during the regular season.

Only individuals inducted as players are considered; managers/executives/umpires are not, even if they once played in the majors.
