I am going to create a video for all specters followup

Around 10 days ago i posted a discussion thread here to gauge if the community would be interested in having an intro/basic video for each spectre in the game.

In the end I’ve ended up creating a spreadsheet and sharing it with a few well known minion content creators. GhazzyTV, Balormage, Macrobioboi to name a few. They are all on board with it and we have basically got this turned into a real compendium that can be found here: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1oadXSCHczpyCgRxzTk3nRBeeOLlefZAzKM3ijwmWevY/edit?gid=0#gid=0

Anyone with the link can comment and a select few can edit so we do not create chaos in the spreadsheet. If you wish to contribute, feel free to do so via comments in the spreadsheet.

We have around 80-100 people using the spreadsheet daily and it already contains a lot of info on the spectres.

In terms of videos, my aim is to create 10 videos of 1-2 min every day and post them (there is link in the spreadsheet). There is also separate column for in-depth guide or video for spectres which can be filled by anyone in the community if this meets a decent quality standard, regardless of your channel size. Feel free to comment in the spreadsheet or contact me if you would want to contribute with in depth spectre videos specifically aimed on a spectre.

Currently, i have created around 80-90 videos for spectres and they were very well received.

I am glad and humbled by the community feedback and early encouragements that started this project and I welcome all of you to contribute or just make use of this resource if you find it useful.

Huge shout to GhazzyTV for initially bringing attention to this spreadsheet and the work that we’re doing here. It basically started a very productive snowball and the community is very engaged to find all the good spectres and all the good combinations

Let the theorycrafting begin
