My opinion of the campaign and creating more than one character in this game.

The campaign is too long; you spend too many hours playing a campaign that, to make matters worse, you have to repeat every season. And don’t even think about creating another character, because you’ll likely spend more time playing the campaign than the endgame. Not to mention the free trial periods designed to attract new players; it’s a miracle if those poor souls even make it past Act 3. In my opinion, subsequent characters shouldn’t have to play the campaign every season; it’s very annoying, and that’s why many players only play their strongest character and don’t try anything else because it takes too much time to complete the campaign.
