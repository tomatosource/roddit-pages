Someone else never did get into PoE 1 but has a blast with 2 ?

I played PoE 1 last time in 2019, did bring a character into endgame, did maps until last t15 or something and never touched it ever again.

I don’t know what it was but it just didn’t felt good to play for me.

With PoE 2 I just can’t stop playing since 0.1, how smooth moving feels, the gem system, sound design, how good it feels to kill stuff, like enemies feel like you really hit them, the gameplay has just more crunsh or something.

I don’t remember everything from my PoE 1 time but there where a few reasons why I never touched it again after one character and 140 hours.

I know the endgame in PoE 2 isn’t done or perfect but for me its enough to put 100-300 hours into it per season which is more than enough tbh.
