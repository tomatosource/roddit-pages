Mookie Betts has the chance to become the first player in MLB history to play in and win 5 World Series without playing for the Yankees

There are 31 players who have played in and won at least 5 World Series, and all 31 of them won at least two rings with the Yankees. The only player on the list who did not win the majority of their 5+ titles with the Yankees is Catfish Hunter, who won 3 titles with the Athletics and 2 with the Yankees. Babe Ruth won 3 titles with the Red Sox but 4 with the Yankees. No other player on the list won more than 1 title with a team that wasn’t the Yankees

Eddie Collins has the most rings of a player who never played on the Yankees with 6, but he did not play in the postseason with the Athletics when they won in both 1929 and 1930 and he only played a combined 12 games those 2 seasons, so for this purpose he only played in and won the World Series 4 times

He can also join Collins, Jim Gilliam and Harry Hooper as the only players to win at least 4 rings (regardless of playing status) with a team that is not the Yankees

List of players who have played in and won 4 World Series that did not win one with the Yankees:

  • Eddie Collins (3 with Athletics, 1 with White Sox) (plus 2 with Athletics where he did not play in postseason)

  • Jack Barry (3 with Athletics, 1 with Red Sox) (plus 1 with Red Sox where he did not appear in postseason)

  • Stuffy McInnis (2 with Athletics, 1 with Red Sox, 1 with Pirates) (plus 1 with Athletics where he did not appear in postseason)

  • Mookie Betts (1 with Red Sox, 3 with Dodgers)

  • Frankie Frisch (2 with Giants, 2 with Cardinals)

  • Larry Gardner (3 with Red Sox, 1 with Indians)

  • Jim Gilliam (4 with Dodgers)

  • Harry Hooper (4 with Red Sox)

  • Amos Strunk (3 with Athletics, 1 with Red Sox)

  • Gene Tenace (3 with Athletics, 1 with Cardinals)

  • Mike Timlin (2 with Blue Jays. 2 with Red Sox)
