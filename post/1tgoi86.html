The Cavaliers Now Have the Best Game 7 Win Percentage in NBA History (Minimum 5 Games)

With their Game 7 win over Detroit, the Cleveland Cavaliers are now 8–2 all-time in Game 7s, giving them an .800 winning percentage.

Using a minimum cutoff of 5 Game 7s played, that is now the best Game 7 win percentage by any franchise in NBA history.

The only team above them overall is Minnesota, but the Timberwolves are only 2–0, so they do not meet the 5-game minimum.

Top Game 7 win percentages, minimum 5 games:

  1. Cavaliers: 8–2 (.800)
  2. Celtics: 27–11 (.711)
  3. Lakers: 16–8 (.667)
  4. Bullets: 6–3 (.667)
  5. Thunder: 4-2 (.667)
  6. Mavericks: 5–3 (.625)
  7. Heat: 7–5 (.583)
  8. Warriors: 7–5 (.583)
  9. Rockets: 7–6 (.538)
  10. Jazz: 3–3 (.500)
  11. Nuggets: 5–5 (.500)
  12. Pistons: 6–6 (.500)

Over the past decade (2016-2026), the Cavaliers are 6–0 in Game 7s, tied with the Celtics (6-3) for the most Game 7 wins in that span, but Cleveland is the only franchise with 5+ Game 7 appearances and a perfect record.

Edit: I’ve changed the Thunder/Sonics record to reflect just the OKC Thunder and did the same with the Wizards/Bullets.
