Saw the change to Bow Attacks fire X additional Arrows and this question popped up in my head: what is the projectile behavior for additional arrows?

Correct me if I’m wrong but in POE1, firing 1 additional arrow will cause the attack projectiles to spread evenly toward your aim, leaving a gap in the middle of the attack. So going from 1 to 2 projectiles may cause you to misses the enemies in the middle of your aim. I assume that this is the behavior in POE2 as well. However, it can be fixed by adjusting your aim.

However, with the newest patch in POE2, a new modifier has been added to Bows: “ +X% Surpassing Chance to fire an additional arrow”. This modifier is interesting because if my understanding of the mechanic is correct above, then if I have 50% Surpassing chance to fire an additional arrow, will I also have 50% chance to miss an enemy right in front of me?

(Of course with 5-6 arrows, everything will be eventually hit but I’m just curious about the additional arrows behavior)
