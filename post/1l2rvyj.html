A way to make Twilight Reliquary Keys more valuable and interesting - make the foil uniques itemlevel 100

The foil uniques that drop from the reliquary are currently set at itemlevel 65. This is even 10 levels lower than foils from PoE1.

The itemlevel matters even more here in PoE2 because skills that are inherently granted from item bases are capped depending on the itemlevel.

If the itemlevel was set at 100, you can potentially have the best version of the unique you’re after. I believe itemlevel 90 is required to have a level 20 skill granted by an item, which I don’t even think is possible yet.
