With Wemby on the court, OKC’s ORtg is a pitiful 102. When Kornet is on, it explodes to 134

Much has been made of Wemby’s impact in this series and rightfully so, but the gap in the Spurs defense is where it couldn’t be any more clear.

When Wemby is on the court, OKC only produces an ORtg of 102. The worst mark of any team in the regular season - Brooklyn - was significantly higher at 108. The only team to perform worse offensively in these playoffs is the Orlando Magic.

Even if you ensure OKC has SGA on the floor, their ORtg remains a pitiful 105 against Wemby.

On the contrary, the second Wemby hits the bench their offense becomes all-time quality. The best regular season and playoff offenses have both peaked at ~122, nowhere close to how well OKC is performing in these minutes.

Cycling from one of the strongest defensive showings of all-time to an incredibly weak defense is astounding to see. Kornet lineups aren’t even filled with scrubs, his four most frequent teammates were the starters for at least half of this series - Vassell, Champagnie, Castle, and Harper. The only major difference in the lineup is Wemby vs Kornet.

This isn’t an anti-Kornet post, he’s just a poor matchup with the Thunder and the Spurs lack another reliable option. Kornet is useful in series like against Minnesota.
