Having nearly all of our depth hit in some way really helps simplify the team building process going forward

Hugo, Walsh, Baylor, garza, Harper, Amari and queta have all hit or shown real flashes of being a hit this year and I think there’s a whole other dimension to what that actually means for this team looking ahead.

This summer we are building a brand new team, a team around brown AND Tatum that will have real expectations to immediately enter another top end contender window for the foreseeable future. Brad will be tasked with filling out a roster, on the margins with limited spending per position due to our big 3 costing nearly 150 mill combined.

The fact that we will have all of Hugo, queta, walsh, Baylor, Garza, Amari, and probably Harper making a combined 17 mill next year means we’ve got like 20-30 mill to spend this summer. The rotation as it would stand pre draft this summer is

Starters: white-insert any wing-brown-Tatum-queta

Bench: Pritchard-Harper-Hugo-Hauser-Walsh-Garza-Amari

That bench alone is nearly a competent championship quality bench, and so are the starters. To me, this means we can spend big on 1-2 really high end impactful guys instead of spreading our cap space around since we have so much depth already.

What does this team look like with an okongwu(via trade into cap space w/ picks), collins(MLE), Coby white(MLE- 20 mill), or one of the high end RFA guys? Or even big fish hunting for a Jalen Suggs, or a big contract center on the trade market?

To summarize, there are now endless names in the 15-30 mill range we can be real players for, we have picks and financial flexibility we can use all on one guy because of how strong our depth has become. We are in a *great* position.
