The Seattle Mariners have had a different pitcher lead in bWAR for every year of the 2020s so far.

2020 - Marco Gonzalez - 1.6 WAR

2021 - Chris Flexen - 3.4 WAR

2022 - Logan Gilbert - 3.2 WAR

2023 - George Kirby - 3.8 WAR

2024 - Bryce Miller - 3.5 WAR

2025 - Bryan Woo - 4.3 WAR

2026 (so far) - Emerson Hancock - 1.5 WAR

The most consistent asset of this era of Mariners baseball has been in it’s starting pitching. And unlike the previous three decades where one pitcher has been the primary ace (Randy, Moyer, Felix), it’s been a share of the rotation.

Everyone and anyone’s gotten a chance to shine, except for Luis Castillo, sadly. (Still a rock for most of his time in Seattle though)
