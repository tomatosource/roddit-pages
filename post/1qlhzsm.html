Having a large influx of currency into the economy has improved the experience for every player.

I believe that the early-league Temple shenanigans were not intentional, however, I would ask for a careful assessment by the powers that be to realise that, actually, this should be considered a benefit.

The min-maxers are going to push the 99% items to 99.9%, or 99.99% no matter what, leaving the detritus of discarded 99.9%/99% gear for less financially affluent players.

The players flush with currency would rather buy the various keys and fragments then run the content needed to acquire them providing the less well-off players a means to generate significant currency.

More currency means more players of all types can experiment with this ‘crafting’ thing, and see benefits directly for their own builds.

When players were quitting early league, they were in fact missing out on the benefits of early inflation in a sandboxed economy. The rising tide lifts all boats.

Your lucky drops are worth more.

Base items are more affordable, while double corrupted items are still available for the more risky gamble for higher returns.

More players can afford to acquire gear to enable more content to be more easily overcome.

Please consider this when deciding future content rewards.
