Omens need to be more accessible (“6x more likely” being a lie)

I farmed/traded enough currency this season to buy a mirror at 1800d. I then converted to divine when it hit 2000d with the intention of crafting a 6, T1 spear with phys/flat phys/hybrid/crit/crit multi and either attack speed or proj skills. I knew going into it that probability was not on my side, and that I’d have to get incredibly lucky to ladder craft a mirror-tier spear with a single mirror worth of currency.

Steps:

  • Buy ilvl 82 spears with T1 crit%

  • Divine to 5% and fracture (did this 3x before it hit)

  • chaos spam until T1 phys%/merciless

  • Exalt and chaos spam with whittles until T1 flat phys (this is where I ran out of whittles)

  • Instead, hit T1 EDTA and decided to keep it, praying for flat phys still.

  • exalt once more and chaos spam with whittles praying for T1 flat phys

  • Run out of currency

  • Sadness

Even with a mirror worth of currency, the spear only ever hit 3⁄6 (and one of them was me settling).

Crafting is in a very sad state that even a mirror worth of currency won’t give you enough crafting materials to hit 4-6 T1 mods unless you’re incredibly lucky.

It’s currently limited to the ultra rich. And having had a mirror, over 90% of the player base would still consider that rich beyond their expectations for a league.

Omens need to be more accessible. The “6x more likely” was either a lie or never implemented correctly. Please fix.
