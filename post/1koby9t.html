The Weight of it all

Just finished the article on ESPN by Wright Thompson. That was an incredibly well written article that emphasizes so much about the Celtics culture and how hard it is to live up to. As fans we tend to think of everything in terms of “win or it sucks and we’re losers”. But what that article showed me is that no team, NO TEAM Boston or otherwise should ever be mentioned in the same breath as the Celtics of old when they were winning title after title. And that’s okay.

For those of us that have been watching and cheering for the Celtics for our entire lives we know that no matter what happens tonight, the legacy will continue on and those Championship banners (19, 20 and on and on) will continue to hang. Maybe not this year but this is a franchise that is only (somewhat) matched by the Lakers and will never be outdone in terms of history, but most importantly, FAMILY. Celtics are family- and the spirits of Red, Russ, Hondo, Tommy, Reggie, DJ, Walt, …and all the others that are gone now that once wore the Celtics jersey will be there tonight WIN or LOSE. And win or lose I’m so damn proud of this team and SO DAMN PROUD to call myself a Celtics fan, and let’s be honest- if the Celts force a game seven no way are they losing that one in Boston. LETS GO CELTICS!!
