I did 100 Min-Maxed Vaal temples and here are the results

I built fubgun min maxed temple post the temple nerf with the mod that allows me to run atziri every alternate run and keep my temple. I then ran the temple 100 times on its full min maxed state across from solo to full party (6pax) and heres some key stats:

  • My build is BM Spark COC ARC w Archmage Mana stacking - I prefer Arc as its more widescreen coverage and instantaneous dps vs comet; dps with full HH buff is around 2m and I slot 2 Arc into COC; Arc has insanely low energy trigger and chains so its perfect for temple
  • POENINJA&gt;&gt; https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds/vaal/character/ryanrtjh-6263/Atipaii?i=0&search=name%3Datipa
  • My gear rarity is at 112% + 15% (ROP which is active almost all the time); I reached that mostly through running almost perfect andvarius isntead of kalandras touch (not accurately reflected on poeninja)
  • I did try running rarity bot setups to test but I found flow and speed of clear just too choppy without HH buffs even if my dmg could comfortably carry 6 pax, so for these 100 runs I had 0 rarity bot
  • Most of my parties were strangers (found from global chat and open party); more often than not I got someone in the party dedicated to help pick loot (hence my tracking ring is lesser than actual divs). Its extremely hard to set up a fully maxed party of 6 without friends so more often than not I just went ahead with 4⁄5.
  • The raw divs here were all before giving leechers their share during the temple (5-10div each)
  • I tracked most of the valuable loot here; I act picked up alot more smaller loot like chaos orbs and HH and shrine sceptres to chance into dreaming
  • Unidentified Atziri splendour chance to drop was ~ 42% across 50 runs (annectodally, seems quite high)
  • Ultra lucked out with mirrors lmao 2 in 100 runs??? One of the mirror runs one of the party guys name was actually [pleasedropmirror] or something and he actually blessed me lol. I shared the mirror equally with the rest of the party
  • The most painful part was actually the 600 crystals needed for this whole run &gt; I put on sands of time and have now sort of gotten a good feel of where crystals spawn in maps. I take around an average of 30s to get 2 crystals and get out of map (without tempo) For eg, in bastille, it only spawns in one of two outer rooms in the main area/
  • IMO, if you want to get consistent raw div always try and partner up or more. Just 2 pax and you bring your average raw div drop to &gt;100
