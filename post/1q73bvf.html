First (Half) Mirror Craft & Recipe

Wow!

I’m a High School Math Teacher and have finally found a good way to use my math skills online lol.

I just started crafting this week and have found it to be the best way to make 100s of divine a day reliably.

Tools

https://poe2db.tw/us/Wands#ModifiersCalc

https://homepage.divms.uiowa.edu/~mbognar/applets/geo1.html

Steps

  1. Fracture +5 cold (or +4 to all spells). This took 5 attempts but should on average take 3. The idea is to have a 3 mod item with one unrevealed desecrated mod. You cannot fracture an unrevealed desecrated mod.
  2. Chaos spam for T1 Spell Damage or T1 Cold Damage. This follows a Geometric Distribution (in layman’s terms: the number of failures (Chaos Slams) until you get the desired mod (T1 Spell Damage or T1 Cold Damage) . The probability of success is 100⁄100900 or 0.000991080 or 0.0991080%. Using this calculator (https://homepage.divms.uiowa.edu/~mbognar/applets/geo1.html) we can find that the average number of chaos to reach this outcome is 1008 Chaos Orbs which is roughly 18 Divine.
  3. Block mana using desecrate. Omen of Sinistral Necromancy + Jawbone + Abyssal Echoes. This should hit first try but if you miss use an Omen of Light and repeat. The reason we do this is Mana has an insanely high weighting.
  4. Omen of Sinistral Exaltation + Perfect Exalted Orb. This will hopefully apply T1 Gain (Fire, Lightning, or Cold). If not don’t worry we can Whittle that off later.
  5. Omen of Light to remove desecrate. Omen of Sinistral Exaltation + Perfect Exalted Orb. This will probably hit mana now but it’s ok.
  6. Desecrate to add a suffix. Pick the highest ilevel suffix you can to block. Bonus points if it hits something good like T1 #% increased Critical Hit Chance for Spells, or #% increased Cast Speed.
  7. Omen of Whittling + Perfect Chaos orb until you get another T1 Gain (Fire, Lightning, or Cold). If you hit T1 #% increased Critical Hit Chance for Spells or T1 #% increased Cast Speed, apply another Perfect Exalted ORB
  8. Repeat until satisfied. I stopped because I hit T2 Cast Speed. I could have kept going but I didn’t want to risk removing my T1 #% increased Critical Hit Chance for Spells for something like T1 Mana Regen, mana on kill, life on kill etc.

Let me know if there is anyway to make this more efficient!!!!
