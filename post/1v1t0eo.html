“I don’t know. I really don’t,” Riley said when asked what he expects James to decide in free agency this summer. “I have nothing, as I think probably everybody else that had an opportunity to talk with him or to sort of wait him from that standpoint. So this is a man’s decision and you let him make that decision. He has the right to do that. And so I’m not calling [agent Rich Paul] every day and bugging him. He’ll make a decision.”

This is from an interview with Pat Riley that was taped on Friday.
