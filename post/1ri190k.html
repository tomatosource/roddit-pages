NBA Stars Have Massive Pace Splits This Season — Some Guys Are Completely Different Players Depending on Game Tempo

I ran a stat deep-dive comparing how the top 12 NBA scorers perform against the 5 fastest-paced teams (MIA, UTA, ATL, DAL, CHI) vs. the 5 slowest (BOS, HOU, LAC, BKN, CHA) this season. Some of these splits are wild.

🔥 TRACK MEET MONSTERS — players who go off in fast-paced games

Anthony Edwards (+11.8 PPG in fast games) This is the most dramatic split in the entire dataset. Ant is averaging 28.8 PPG against fast teams and 17.0 PPG against slow ones. His FG% drops from 48.6% to 26.5% in slugfests. He’s a rhythm scorer who needs chaos — when teams like Boston or Houston slow it to a crawl and pack the paint, he basically disappears. His plus-minus swings 20 points between the two contexts (+11.8 vs fast, -8.2 vs slow). That’s not a small sample fluke, that’s a real thing.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (+8.6 PPG in fast games) 30.0 PPG against fast teams, 21.4 against slow ones. FG% goes from 55.1% to 40.1%. SGA’s whole game is built around getting downhill and into the paint — slow the game down and suddenly he’s not the unguardable monster we’ve been watching all season. Still good, obviously. But noticeably more human.

Steph Curry (+4.1 PPG in fast games) Fewer possessions = fewer Steph threes. His efficiency actually holds up fine in slow games (63.6% TS vs 57.4%) but the counting stats take a hit. Less chaos = less Steph.

🧊 HALF-COURT ASSASSINS — players who actually prefer the grind

Donovan Mitchell (better FG% in slow games by 13.7 percentage points) This one surprised me. Mitchell shoots 48.0% FG against slow teams and only 34.3% against fast ones. His pull-up mid-range game and isolation scoring are built for half-court settings. Basically the anti-Ant Edwards.

Cade Cunningham (better across the board in slow games) 23.5 PPG and 63.4% TS against slow teams vs. 20.7 PPG and 52.5% TS against fast ones. He’s a methodical playmaker and the slow-it-down style just suits him perfectly.

Jalen Brunson (basically neutral PPG but 17 pp more efficient slow) The scoring gap is tiny (25.3 vs 24.8) but his TS% is 69.1% in slow games vs 51.8% in fast ones. He is genuinely a different level of efficient in half-court sets. The Knicks running slow-paced offense is not an accident.

⚖️ THE TRULY PACE-NEUTRAL GUYS

Nikola Jokic — PPG is almost identical in both contexts (22.4 vs 22.0). But his TS% against slow teams is 92.9%. That number is basically a rounding error away from impossible. He was making everything and drawing fouls on top of it. The man does not care about your game plan.

Kevin Durant — Barely a PPG difference (+2.8 for fast games) but somehow posting 80.5% TS in up-tempo games. KD in transition getting open looks is apparently a cheat code.

Luka Doncic — Slight preference for fast games (+3.1 PPG) but the bigger story is his plus-minus: +6.9 vs fast teams, -4.3 vs slow ones. Luka still scores in slugfests but the Lakers as a whole struggle when the game slows down.

Note: Data from the 2025-26 NBA regular season. Fast/slow pace groups based on each team’s season pace rating (possessions per 48 min), top 5 and bottom 5 in the league. Min. 7 games per split to be included. TS% = Points / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)).
