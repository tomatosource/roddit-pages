I still can’t get over how historically great Vlad Guerrero Jr.’s 2025 playoffs were

18 games, 1.289 OPS, .397 BA, .494 OBP, 8 HRs, 14 BB, only 7 Ks. All this with an all-time record of 89 at-bats, in spite of not playing the wildcard games due to a bye. His glove was exceptional, highlighted by the incredible double-play he started to kill a Dodgers rally in game 7.

Every one of his at-bats from his first at-bat against the Yankees to his double in the 11th inning of Game 7 was must watch TV.

I don’t know if we’ll see a complete playoffs performance like this again any time soon.
