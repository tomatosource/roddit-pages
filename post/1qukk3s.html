The Clippers currently have a James Harden Bobblehead Night scheduled for their February 22nd home game against the Orlando Magic. They have already had to cancel their plans for a Chris Paul Bobblehead Night on February 2nd.

The Clippers entered the season with 6 planned bobblehead nights, the most in the NBA according to SBNation. Here is the original schedule:

- January 12: Kris Dunn Bobblehead

- February 2: Chris Paul Bobblehead

- February 22: James Harden Bobblehead

- March 4: Bradley Beal Bobblehead

- March 16: Nicolas Batum Bobblehead

- March 25: Kawhi Leonard Bobblehead

If Harden gets traded, the only active player with a bobblehead night before March would be Kris Dunn.
