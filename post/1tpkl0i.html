Give it a try

Hey, fellow gamers!

Recently I’ve seen more and more messages on streams, Reddit posts or even sometimes posts on Discord about things such as “Should I even start Poe2 at this point or is it too late?”, “I have not played X build, but I’m not sure if I should give it a try” and “I have not crafted/played solo-self-found yet, I don’t know if I should even attempt it, I have no clue how to”.

And to all of this I want to say- if your heart tells you that it wants to give it a try and it sounds potentially exciting and rewarding, please do it.

I genuinely understand the concerns about being a new player to X thing and feeling like you might struggle because of it. It’s fair and there’s a chance that you will face certain obstacles, that will be related to the lack of knowledge or skill. But that’s literally like anything else in life. To be somewhere, you have to start from 0.

And trust me when I say this- sometimes the journey is sooooooo worth it, that you wouldn’t have even expected that in the first place. Honestly, I really want to encourage you to try playing POE 2 if you haven’t yet- you might just have tons and tons of fun, and learn a lot along the way. Haven’t crafted anything more complicated besides slamming a couple of blue currencies? Try going beyond that and make a bit of a better item. SSF sounds like a cool constraint and that I could be very rewarding, but maybe too challenging? Give it a go, honestly. Same goes for character building. Heck, even speedrunning is a thing or collecting uniquely interesting items, how about that?

A lot of things can seem overwhelming at first, but as long as you focus just on the next step, the challenge is manageable and you grow one step at a time. And on top of that, this can be a lot of fun. And the whole point of this is having fun, isn’t it?

I don’t have a long standing POE experience prior to POE2 coming out. And let me tell you… I played every single league and with every league, I love this game more and more. And I genuinely mean it.

This is going to be my first league where I start learning to put a build together on my own. I have not done it in the past. I followed build guides because it was too much, and I wanted to focus on the other stuff. Basically, one challenge at a time. This league, I’m going to try a very exciting build, but the kind that I put together for the most part (with input of other people + various guides or tools to compare things, take inspiration from and such).

And despite that, I have a few goals related to breach, rituals, exceptional farming and FINALLY breaking the threshold of acquiring mirror+ in liquid wealth (because I love crafting and gathering wealth in a game like POE 2).

So, Exile, what’s going to be the challenge that you’re going to take on this league?

If you’re still on the fence whether you should try something- honestly, give it a try, and at worst you’ll have tried something and will know that it’s not for you, no shame in that.

I’ll see you on Friday and it’s going to be loads of fun!

(Just wanted to share and encourage the excitement for the upcoming League launch)
