A look at (slightly) off-ball Luka, a quarter into the season:

TL;DR- The Mavericks have made it a point to develop Luka’s off-ball this season, and after a rough start he’s back to averaging MVP numbers despite his reduced role. Most interesting stat is at the bottom of the post imo.

An interesting discussion that was happening in the Mavs fanbase during our rough start to the season, where we went 5-7 including four clutch losses in a row, was that many people felt Luka and the team were struggling due to him being played out of position. It seems like the team was making an effort to let players like Kyrie, Naji Marshall, or Klay take a bit more responsibility bringing the ball up the court and initiating the offenses, and Luka was clearly struggling to get into rythm.

Before the season Jason Kidd mentioned this being a goal he set for Luka for the year. On 11⁄13, after the loss to the Warriors in Klay’s return to Golden State, Luka literally said he was struggling “Just overall. Just playing without the ball. Just all of it.”

The stats to back this up:

-Last year Luka lead the league in usage rate at 35.9%.

-This year so far, Luka is fourth in the league behind Lamelo, Giannis, SGA at 32.8%

-According to @NBA_University on Twitter when looking at % of shots unassisted this season:

“Most notably, Luka is absent from this list. 65.8% of his baskets are unassisted this season. He led the league in each of the previous 4 seasons:

‘24: 78.0%

‘23: 84.7%

‘22: 85.8%

‘21: 86.4%

He is scoring WAY more of his buckets off ball this year. Intriguing development!”

But maybe the most important stat in this entire post:

In the specific 4 games since Luka came back from injury recently, he’s been averaging 31/11/10. With many people saying he’s back to MVP form. Luka’s usage rate during this (admittedly small) stretch is 30.7% which is his lowest since his rookie year and is notably below players like Jokic, Tatum, Cade Cunningham, and Wembanyama this season.
