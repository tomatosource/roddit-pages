The west finals from every year from 2019 to 2024 have been

2019: Blazers vs Warriors

2020: Nuggets vs Lakers

2021: Clippers vs Suns

2022: Mavs vs Warriors

2023: Lakers vs Nuggets

2024: Wolves vs Mavs

Nobody was able to go back to back until the Timberwolves. The parity in the west has been insane.
