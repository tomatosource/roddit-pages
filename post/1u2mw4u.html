Tablets are just Phrecia Idols Reskinned

I want to open by saying I absolutely adored all the endgame changes that GGG have made. The bosses are incredibly fun and progression felt quite good. But this will be another season where I stop using my character around level 94 and it had me wondering why.

Its tablets. Once you get to the point where you are doing juicing content, I truly just don’t enjoy searching for the right set of tablets. I’m sure I have like 50D worth of tablets sitting in my stash just because I don’t know what is optimal for each strategy. This is most likely what 90% of the player base is doing. Nobody lists what they don’t understand, so even though tablets are dropping constantly, the actual tradeable supply of usable ones collapses. That’s the scarcity. From there, the cost of running these strategies becomes prohibitively expensive.

I wanted to see if my character could give the high end Ritual a shot. I invested a ton of currency to get this set up. It was just a little too strong for me when all the bosses came in. It was incredibly fun but it wasn’t a strategy I was ready for yet. If this was run with the POE1 scarab system, it would have been no big deal. I would have blown a set of scarabs and would have moved on, very low cost overall. But with tablets it feels so much more deflating. Tablets make the cost of failure so high that experimenting with new strategies feels punished, and that’s exactly the kind of thing that pushes average players out of juicing entirely

I think this is very similar to Phrecia. The first time it happened it was a fun novelty, but eventually the issues with this system became a little more clear in round 2. And idols at least had one thing going for them. Once you bought your setup, it was permanent. Tablets have the same random-roll problem idols did, except they’re also consumable. You pay the idol tax, get 10 runs, and you’re back on the trade website.

Also, trying to figure out what strategy to run feels more complicated. Now I need to ensure I am running 4 tablets with at least 6 specific affixes running at the same time. I need to make sure I’m in the right biome. And I haven’t even gotten there yet, but the current delirium meta apparently involves finding a grand mirror and making sure it’s placed in a city biome. That’s just tower setup from older patches with a new coat of paint, the exact chore loop they already patched out once

Anyways, I do absolutely love the state of the game from Act 1 to the final endgame boss kill. I just think things need to be patched up for the giga juicers in a way thats a little more accessible. I’ll assuredly be moving to HCSSF because the knowledge burden of figuring out which affixes matter for which strategy is high enough that I’d rather just play the version of the game where tablets barely exist. Even just a baseline tier of fixed-effect tablets that anyone can farm, with the random chase rolls kept on top for the no lifers, would fix most of this. I feel like scarabs already proved this out
