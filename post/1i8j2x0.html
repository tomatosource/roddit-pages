Here is your Western Conference Starters Pool for the 2025 NBA All-Star Game

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander:

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is making his 3rd NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 11th overall pick in the 2018 NBA Draft out of Kentucky, Shai has two All-NBA First Team Selections. This season, he is averaging 32.0 PPG, 5.4 RPG and 6.1 APG for the Thunder.

Stephen Curry:

Stephen Curry is making his 11th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 7th overall pick in the 2009 NBA Draft out of Davidson, Curry is a 4x NBA Champion and 2x MVP. This season, he is averaging 22.6 PPG, 4.9 RPG and 6.3 APG for the Warriors.

LeBron James:

LeBron James is making his 21st NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 1st overall pick in the 2003 NBA Draft out of Akron, Ohio, LeBron is a 4x NBA Champion and 4x MVP. This season, he is averaging 23.7 PPG, 7.5 RPG and 9.0 APG for the Lakers.

Nikola Jokić:

Nikola Jokić is making his 7th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 41st overall pick in the 2014 NBA Draft out of Serbia, Jokić is an NBA Champion and a 3x MVP. This season, he is averaging 30.1 PPG, 13.2 RPG and 9.9 APG for the Nuggets.

Kevin Durant:

Kevin Durant is making his 15th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 2nd overall pick in the 2007 NBA Draft out of Texas, Durant is a 2x NBA Champion and was named MVP for the 2013-14 season. This season, he is averaging 27.2 PPG, 6.5 RPG and 4.1 APG for the Suns.
