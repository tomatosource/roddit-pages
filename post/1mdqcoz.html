Rewatching some 2018 playoff games

I was feeling nostalgic tonight so I watched game 7 of the Bucks series and game 3 of the Philly series from 2018 on league pass. 18 thoughts and observations I had:

  1. You can see how the Bucks and Celtics had the cores of teams that would run the East over the next 7 years, but on the other hand Semi Ojeleye and Thon Maker started the game.

  2. Speaking of Semi, he attacks a close out with a nice sidestep finish??! What in the world is going on?

  3. Banger quote from Brad via Abby Chin on the young Celtics having no fear of a game 7: “There is power in naivety.” Also, Abby and Scal look so freaking young on the sideline.

  4. Mike Gorman has been traumatized by Khris Middleton. “Do NOT let him get loose” when he gets a layup after a quiet start.

  5. Smart and Al are magic

  6. I remembered JB pulled his hamstring in this game, but I thought it had happened earlier and I forgot how routine the play where he tweaked it was. He spent the whole second half on the exercise bike and stretching, hoping to get back in the game. Dog.

  7. Horford is the best player on the floor by far. As he scores 10 points in the first 6 minutes of the third quarter Tommy the GOAT proclaims, “I tell you he’s the horse tonight. He’s going to carry this team on his shoulders.”

  8. Monster Tatum sequence late in the third to just about seal it.

  9. The lineup of Larkin-Smart-Ojeleye-Morris-Baynes to start the fourth quarter is terrifying though. The cojones on Brad. Shane Larkin was a sick player. It’s cool he went to be the man in Europe but I don’t see why he couldn’t have had an NBA career to equal TJ McConnell. He hits a 3 with 52 seconds left to put the Celtics up 14, “Good night” says Mike.

On to the confetti game:

  1. There’s an old Sixers fan courtside violently waving a towel at Celtic players. He annoys me greatly.

  2. Tatum is so cold. This game single handedly caused me to disregard Robert Covington’s sterling defensive stats.

  3. Brown is a defensive demon, even though I’m not sure he’s able to sprint because of the hamstring injury. I feel like his three pointer looks way smoother than it does today

  4. A Sixers fan behind the broadcast table has a, “Kawhi Leonard will complete the process” sign with Kawhi photoshopped in a Sixers jersey. Hilarious given the incoming 4 bouncer in a year’s time.

  5. I would need to go to therapy about the Aron Baynes 3s if I was a Sixers fan.

  6. Terry Rozier mic’d up is gold. “It don’t matter!” he yells after he hits a tough fadeway over long 2 aficionado Marco Bellinelli.

  7. Oh Ben Simmons, you poor bugger. He actually wasn’t bad for most of the game before this moment, but it all came crashing down after.

  8. Sequence I’d completely forgotten about: Big offensive rebound by Brown leads to Tatum almost ending Embiid’s life, but he misses both free throws so the highlight is completely nullified.

  9. Playoff Al Baby!
