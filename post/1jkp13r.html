Making the Atlas maps replay-able would improve Mapping 10 fold, right?

EDIT: ATLAS NODES REPLAY-ABLE*

Investment into specific areas of the Atlas is such a huge part of the endgame identity, that I don’t think it can be completely deleted unless it comes with a complete endgame rework(Which I don’t think people would mind, though I think that will come after a few patch cycles, if it’s still this disliked). But a huge part of what makes juicing and trying to find valuable Map points, is that the investment it takes to get to those rewards has to be repeated monotonously without a change of pace. Travel around, remove bad areas with bad maps, activate towers with bad maps, use tablets to activate 5~10 good map areas, and 2~3 great ones, play through the randomly determined map layouts, and repeat. Granted this is just the cycle for Mapping, not including finding Citadels or playing through the early endgame, just Juicing or currency farming. And this would be a fine setup if 80% of the playtime wasn’t in absolutely unrewarding content, but it is. Which makes most of the gameplay quite boring, and is leading to the complaints about rare mobs not being on the map, and the towers being a nuisance instead of an exciting prelude to a lot of fun, challenging gameplay.

However, if the Atlas points were able to be replayed, then people could spend much more time in a given tower setup, while playing the maps that they actually enjoy. Whether that means giving it a limit to make it an exhaustible resource (6 portals???) or perhaps the only reason to expand your atlas becomes finding citadels, other unique maps, or better tower setups around the RNG atlas. Combining this with a waypoint system to allow teleporting around the Atlas, and easier navigation because of this, makes it a much more rewarding system for finding an optimal location, instead of locking content behind a time sink like the current iteration of Mapping.
