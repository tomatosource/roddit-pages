Ricky Rubio back in action in the Spanish League, for his teenage club Joventut Badalona. First game of the year : 18 points, 3 rebounds, 4 assists

Video

Ricky Rubio (34) returned to Spain’s Liga ACB with his original club, Badalona, having last played in spring 2024 for Barcelona. In his first game back, he scored 18 points (including 9⁄9 free throws), grabbed 3 rebounds and dished 4 assists, helping Badalona beat Granada 87–75.

Rubio said his return isn’t just about playing, but about helping the club grow. He has previously shared publicly that during his break, he battled depression and even had suicidal thoughts.
