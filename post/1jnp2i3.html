Path Masters: Dawn of the Hunt Bounty event (for fresh characters) with REAL Prizes!

Hey yall, I’m happy to announce a fun new way to compete with each other in Path of Exile 2’s 0.2 patch! Path Masters bounties!

PATH MASTERS: Dawn of the Hunt- Start a fresh character April 4th in the new league and compete with others for REAL prizes! Prove your skill and speed. Just record yourself doing a bounty (we suggest OBS), upload it somewhere (on youtube, twitch, etc), and submit your link to our judges by clicking the ‘submit’ button on this page next to the bounty you did to win! You may also submit a time-stamped VOD. The first to complete each bounty wins a prize!

Path Masters is a community event for PoE2. The team behind this event have experience organizing events like BPL and EOE and we were looking for challenges to do in PoE2. The cash prizes are sponsored. (bc we’re poor LOL). We’re also looking for help coming up with new bounties for future events, so feel free to comment suggestions or message me if you want to join the team!

One challenge per prize level per person. Each player may only claim one \(5, one \)10, and one $15 prize. Cash prizes will be sent through Paypal. The List of Bounties and additional rules can be found here: https://www.thecrimsonmarket.com/bounties
