virisium remnants are fundamentally broken for melee characters

the difficulty difference for a melee character and a ranged character is a little insane.

played druid most of the league and it’s been fairly easy, switched to martial artist and have been running expedition and ive died more in these two days than the entire league on my druid.

the amount of cc on floor, cc being shot from enemies, getting hit once and being stun locked in place, being body blocked by enemies, cc spawning underneath you as tornadoes etc. is just ridiculous.

this should get looked at to have a more diverse variety of difficulty besides “the floor is lava and you can’t move!”

ranged characters can stand 30 feet away and throw shit at enemies while melee is forced to engage with the enemies face to face with no added advantage. no added defenses, no added reward for playing the game in what’s an objectively more difficult manner.

i dont know the solution to this, just adding my feedback to the list im sure is already long but i can assure you i will not be playing melee anymore in path of exile because its from the ground up a flawed design.
