Well, now I understand the hype

I wasn’t planning to get attached to Path of Exile 2 during the Free-to-Play weekend, but here we are.

I downloaded it mostly because it was free and figured I’d give it a few hours. Fast forward to now, and I’ve spent most of my free time thinking about what character I want to build next.

One thing that really surprised me was how rewarding progression feels. Every new skill gem, support, piece of gear, or passive point felt meaningful. There were several moments where a small upgrade completely changed how my character played, and that’s something I haven’t felt in an ARPG for a long time.

The boss fights were another highlight. A few of them absolutely destroyed me at first, but instead of feeling frustrating, they made me want to jump right back in and figure out what I did wrong. Finally winning felt genuinely satisfying.

I also love how much personality the classes have. The Martial Artist immediately caught my attention and ended up being way more fun than I expected. The animations alone make every fight look awesome.

The biggest problem now is that the weekend is ending. I didn’t expect to enjoy the game this much, and unfortunately an Early Access key isn’t really in my budget at the moment. Looks like I’ll be living vicariously through streams and Reddit posts until I can play again.

Either way, the weekend completely sold me on the game. Curious to hear what surprised everyone else the most when they first tried PoE 2.
