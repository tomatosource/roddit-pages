0.5 was great for testing other classes

I’m the type of player who plays one or two total classes the whole league and barely plays anything more. I usually play my league starter (Acolyte) as my main then pick something as a secondary to maybe get to maps.

this league I hated the temple, the forced Snake to keep up with the economy felt bad for me as I just don’t enjoy that type of gameplay. Other mechanics helped bridge the gap in currency a little but not to the extent of a couple snake runs.

Due to this, I bounced off endgame rather fast this time around as the gear I needed far exceeded what I could buy with my currency count. So I did what I always do when I get a bit bored and roll my alt, settling for a smith of kitava this time and enjoying it.

I quickly got him through the campaign with twink gear and had him in endgame before I knew it. He got to T15 quickly and then the same feeling came in… I didn’t want to quit because I was still having fun, just hated the way the endgame felt with things being out of reach for me.

So I did what I never do and rolled a Djinn Sorc. she flew threw the campaign faster than my Smith did and even eclipsed my Acolyte in endgame by level. As I needed to upgrade gear to get to pinnacle fights the same set in…

I’ve made a Ranger and Merc since and while my ranger is slow leveling (girlfriend started to play so I only do ranger with her) my merc has been going quite smooth.

all of this to say, even though I consider this league a letdown, it did allow me to break out of my normal play style and try other styles of play which I enjoyed very very much. it opened a lot of doors for me for .5 and what I want to do, even allowed me to experiment with builds a bit with the gear I had.

As a fun note, as someone who has mained Acolyte for every single league including 0.1, this may have been my favorite design for it. full EV, no ES, all Physical/Chaos build with quarterstaff. using Bleed and Poison to AOE clear screens of mobs in lightning speed and use deflection with evasion to ensure my small Health pool isn’t a hindrance to me. using Original Sin to convert my elemental abilities to chaos to give me more in my toolkit was a blast and my natural mana leech from Chaos made for a fun time.

looking forward to 0.5 and the endgame rework to push this kind of character far. Even more excited to see a Acolyte rebuild and maybe the third ascension class to monk to try out!
