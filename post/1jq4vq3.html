Direct Minions Cooldown in Patch notes is a terrible change

In the new patch, direct minions will have a 0.5 second cooldown.

If you scale attack speed, wanting to use this to target multiple things means your minions aren’t able to interact meaningfully.

I don’t understand the purpose of this change. It’s going to make builds with minions borderline unplayable at higher levels. Directing minions was one of the only ways to get deter enemies coming at you at mach 5.
