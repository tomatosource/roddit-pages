The longest streak of series without losing

The Braves have now gone 11 straight series to start the season without losing. It’s always a question in the post game threads/generally “What’s the longest streak to start the season? How close are these Braves to the record?”

tl;dr Not close. The 1970 Reds were insane to start the season and went 32 (or 31 depending on how you count series) series in a row without losing. This is the also the record for any time in a season.

Note: These numbers are counting single game series as full series. Some of the older teams will have a series or two less if you do not want to count those.
