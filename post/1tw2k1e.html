0.5 is a big step up, but stop making me pick between unlocking league mechanics and the big atlas.

0.5 is genuinely good. way better than where we were. moving through the atlas feels smoother and the pacing is in a much better spot. no notes there.

but every map i drop into hits the same wall… go unlock the league mechanic, or do the fortress. one or the other. delayed gratification across 311 maps is a bit much.

i want to explore the sea. instead i keep getting pushed into a bazillion fortress maps just to unlock the full atlas.

so why can’t the two just get along? a map is a map. let me run and unlock the league atlas and chip away at the big ass fortress atlas at the same time. maybe not every map rewards an atlas point, fine, balance it however you want. nerf warriors again and give more uniques to twisters for all i care.

happy to be told i’m missing something. but right now it reads like an artificial choice where there doesn’t need to be one.
