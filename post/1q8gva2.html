With Ja Morant officially on the trade bloc, predict who will net the higher return: The Hawks for Trae or the Grizzlies for Ja

Obviously the Grizzlies have the significantly better track record eking out trade returns (Ignore the 3-teamer with the Pelicans and Hornets in 2021) but while Trae Young is a flawed player, he at least provides spacing despite some recent down shooting years from outside and in general doesn’t cause issues for his team due to unforced errors.

Ja Morant is…well he has significantly more red flags despite being on MVP ballots a few seasons ago.

He’s played less than 80 games total across the past 3 seasons with only 18 games played this year.

His athleticism seems to be on the decline with severely diminished play and even at his peak his efficiency was dubious compared to other high volume scorers. And it’s worth noting the archetype of hyper athletic guards have a history of aging like milk left out in the sun.

He’s not as bad a defender as Trae but that’s a low bar to clear.

And of course, there’s the laundry list of off-court controversies.
