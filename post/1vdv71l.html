Undying Hate literally breaks the game

A word of warning to anyone playing around with Undying Hate as this jewel will literally break your game leaving you unable to even log in.

When undying hate randomizes your passive tree one of the nodes it can give you is Derogatory Gaze which provides “3% reduced Accuracy Rating per 25 Tribute, 5% increased Attack Critical Hit Chance per 10 Tribute”

I had 3 Derogatory Gaze notables allocated on my Oracle along with 628 tribute and received 5*62*3=930% increased critical strike chance, along with the base 100% a 9.6% crit chance weapon equipped results in a 98.88% crit chance, pretty nice right? well the -225% accuracy penalty causes a bit of a problem but I figured the Pinpoint Shot notable “Attacks gain increased Accuracy Rating equal to their Critical Hit Chance” along with some Cat idols was a pretty good solution, better than the torture of having to use an Akoyan club and being locked into using only mace skills .

Well it turns out there’s a bug where if you exceed 100% crit chance with pinpoint shot allocated you get immediately disconnected from the game and restored to an earlier state, If you’re really unlucky though you won’t get rolled back to before the crash and so you’ll be left unable to log in at all which is unfortunately why I’m writing this now.
