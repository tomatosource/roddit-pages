In Defense of Paolo Banchero: A Look into his 2024-25 Season

Yesterday there was a post about Paolo Banchero and a lot of the discourse was about how inefficient he has been, and while this is true it does not tell the whole story, particularly with regards to last season.

We can break down Paolo’s 2023-24 seasonm into three parts

Pre Injury (5 Games, which is an admittedly tiny sample size)

29.0 PPG 8.8 REB 5.6 AST .495/.344/.644 2.2 TOV 3-2 W/L record

Return from Oblique Injury Pre-All Star Break (17 Games)

20.6 PPG 6.6 REB 4.8 AST .406/.289/.657 3.4 TOV 5-12 W/L record

Post ASB (24 Games)

29.0 PPG 7.8 REB 4.5 AST .473/.333/.783 2.8 TOV 13-11 W/L record

Any Magic fan can tell you he was bad during that post injury stretch. He was clearly not in basketball shape and was more than likely rushed back from injury way too early due to all of the other injuries to the top Magic scorers. However once he got a week off for the ASB he looked like a completely different player and helped the Magic secure the 7th seed after a terrible January.

To conclude, Paolo Banchero did take a leap last year but a great season was interrupted by an unfortunate injury and a longer than expected recovery period. While he needs to improve his shot diet and continue developing his outside shot, there is a lot to like about a 22 year old who put up 29 PPG for a sustained period, particularly with his poor supporting cast.
