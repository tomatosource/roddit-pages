Via Brian Windhorst and rsalgm

Ace Bailey was a candidate to be picked as high as No. 2 in the 2025 NBA draft, but fell to the Utah Jazz at No. 5 and the team is ecstatic with his development since joining their program. Bailey reportedly didn’t want to be drafted by the Jazz as his previous representation tried to get him to the Washington Wizards.

“I spent some time with the Jazz folks this week in L.A.,” said Brian Windhorst. “They were raving about Ace Bailey. Up and down. They love his make up. They raved about his basketball intelligence. They said he’s just now beginning to work on his body. They haven’t really done much of that. They are convinced that they were so lucky to get him at five.

“They tanked last year and then had egg all over his face when they had a horrible lottery night. They were so pleased he fell. They consider themselves lucky.”

Bailey is averaging 8.7 points, 3.4 rebounds and 1.5 assists in 20.3 minutes over 14 games as a rookie with the Jazz.

Bailey was drafted after Cooper Flagg, Dylan Harper, VJ Edgecombe and Kon Knueppel.
