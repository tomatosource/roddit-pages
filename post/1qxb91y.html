Background

Just hit Lv100 on Stormweaver (Rank #2) in HCSSF. Learned a lot of new crafting tech along the way and thought I should share it.

Obtaining Bases

Breakpoints

  • INT armour: item level 79 for T1 Defence
    • Example ES bases: Vile Robe (i79+), Ancestral Tiara (i80+), Sirenscale Gloves (i80+)
  • DEX and STR armour: item level 81 (for Deflection and Armour to Elemental)
  • Boots: item level 82 for 35% Movement Speed
    • Example ES base: Sekhema Sandals
    • Do not try to Desecrate the 35% MS mod later; it is extremely rare.

Gambling (Ange and Rog)

Ange and Rog can sell up to item level 80 bases (at Character Level 90). You generally prefer magic items on a good base, but will also take rares if Rog offers them with a T1 defense prefix and the correct ilvl. This is a surprisingly fast way to get bases for 3xT1 prefix crafting.

In order of precedence, stash bases with:

  1. Magics with T1 prefix
    • Example T1% or flat defense prefix (not hybrid) regex:
      "(9[1-9].*m|1(0[1-9]|[1-9]\\d).*d) ene"
  2. Magics with no prefix
    • Example ES no‑prefix regex:
      "^(Ances|Siren|Vile).*of"
  3. Other magics
  4. Rares with T1 prefix
  5. Other rares

Recommended stash setup:

  • Sort stash tabs into these categories.
  • If you can use premium tabs, set the tab to public and put the regex in a note on an item inside of it.
    • Copy/paste the regex into the vendor window search.

Vendor tips:

  • Ange: spam buy and spam sell items, then check the Buyback window for hits with regex.
  • Rog: you can use his prefix crafts/rerolls, but you’ll likely fill all affixes, which reduces later recombination chances (still often worth it for strong T1s).

Farming

For bases you cannot get via gambling:

  • Irradiated / Overseer T15 maps will suffice for most bases.
  • For higher ilvl (up to 83), run Atziri’s Temple.

Pick up:

  • All Normal/Magic/Rare items of the desired base, regardless of unidentified tier or mods.

Preparing Bases

Normal Items

  • Use Perfect Transmutation.
  • If you did not get a T1 prefix, use Perfect Augmentation.

Magics with T1 Prefix

  • Hold these for recombination.

Magics with No Prefix

  • Use Perfect Augmentation and hope for a T1 prefix.

Other Magics

  • Use the Reforging Bench.
  • If it has no prefix, use Perfect Augmentation and hope for a T1 prefix.
  • Otherwise, treat the item based on the result (e.g., if it now has a T1 prefix, move it to the “Magics with T1 Prefix” bucket).

Rares with T1 Prefix

  • Hold these for recombination.

Other Rares

  • If the item has 1 prefix + 3 suffixes, double Exalt slam.
  • Otherwise, use the Reforging Bench.

Crafting

Fracturing (Important Note)

You mainly fracture when:

  • You want 35% Movement Speed.
  • Or you want T1 Deflection / Armour to Elemental locked in.

Process:

  • Slam (or Annul) until the item has exactly 3 total mods.
  • Then Desecrate → Fracture:
    • Desecrated mods cannot be fractured, so your fracture odds effectively improve (from 1⁄4 to 1⁄3 on an otherwise 3‑mod item).

Recombination

Goal: get 2x T1 prefixes on one rare.

Best case:

  • Recombine magic items where each has a single T1 prefix (one mod each).
    • These have the highest chance of combining into a rare with both T1s.

If that’s not possible:

  • Combine items with T1s of the same rarity (magic+magic, rare+rare) and aim to merge the prefixes.

Desecration

Once you have a rare with 2x T1 prefixes and a free prefix:

  • Use Desecrate with Omen of Abyssal Echoes and Omen of Sinistral Desecration.
  • Use Perfect Exalt slams on the remaining suffixes to fill them.

Body Armour Specific Note

  • Perfect Slam the remaining suffixes.
  • Use Perfect Essence of Seeking (Crit Damage Reduction).
    • This will only remove suffixes (since they’re full), preserving your prefixes.

Final Item

At this point you should have:

  • At least a 2x T1 prefix rare (with the option of using Omen of Light to fix the Desecration).
  • Hopefully some decent suffixes.

If you dislike the suffixes, you can:

  • Spam Omen of Whittling.
  • Use Dextral Chaos / Annul.
  • Abandon the craft and recombinate with another item that has your desired third prefix (this can reopen Desecrate/Fracture suffix plays if the result hits).
  • (Double) corrupt with Omen of Corruption and Architect’s Orb.
  • Sell the item if you’re in Trade.
