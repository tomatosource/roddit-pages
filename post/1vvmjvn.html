for all my fellow warrior/mace players, what’s your 0.5.5 wishlist?

I obviously want more balance, not that Warrior/maces are unbalanced. I actually think mace skills are some of the most balanced weapon/skill gems. They just need some QoL improvements, like animation/skill speed, more combat fluidity, and also some new skills.

I also wish we had easier ways to scale life. Armour and Armour applying to Elemental Damage is fine and provides a nice defensive layer. Maybe we could get a little more to make it more competitive with ES. Either that, or nerf ES again.

And finally, not directly related to maces, but it would still be a huge QoL improvement if map layouts and map obstacles were “fixed.” Some maps have a ridiculous (and actually unnecessary) number of obstacles like trees, rocks, vessels, roots, etc.
