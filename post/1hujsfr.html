The Boston Celtics just had their third lowest scoring 4th quarter in franchise history by scoring 12 total points as a team

In a loss against the 1st seed in the west, The Oklahoma City Thunder, who were down 13 at one point, the Boston Celtics just set their third lowest points scored in the final quarter (first is 11 points) with 12 total points as a team.

With this game, OKC are now 30-5 and extend their winning streak to 15.

Jayson Tatum (9 PTS) and Al Horford (3 PTS) were the only players to score in the 4th

The Celtics went 3-16 from the field and 2-10 from behind the arc
