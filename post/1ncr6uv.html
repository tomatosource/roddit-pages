Can anybody explain to me how the Joe Smith situation made sense for anybody at the time?

With the whole Kawhi and Clippers situation the Joe Smith contract that cost the Wolves so much in the 2000’s has come up a bunch, so it gives me the chance to ask a question that has been on my mind for years. Who did this deal benefit and why was it made?

From Joe Smith’s perspective, you are taking a way below market value deal to get more money on the back end? Wouldn’t he have made roughly the same amount if he just got market value for the first 3 years and market value after that without taking on huge risk? If he thinks he is gonna do great, he will get his money anyways and is just costing him money in the meantime. If he is unsure about his future contracts why is he taking minimum deals? Even if he thought there was zero chance of getting caught (which is dumb to think) he has almost no security against injury or just Minnesota reneging on the deal if he sucks or declines for 3 years.

From the Minnesota perspective I don’t understand the benefit either unless you planned on reneging on the deal. You get a 6th man for 3 years who is underpriced? Maybe you think he is going to outperform and be worth the the max later you are getting a discounted player in the meantime, but that’s an insane gamble. If he sucks or gets hurt you then have to pay him way beyond his market value? They just came off a .500 season and I do not remember them being one piece away form a championship or anything. Also how would it look if he declined in performance for 3 years and then you give him a max contract, you don’t think it would be obvious what happened? There just doesn’t seem like any value other than an extremely short signed plan to get over the hump for a ring, which Joe Smith was not the player to do that and to take such an insane gamble on.

I don’t understand how anybody (even the agent) was all good with this plan. I feel like it would be similar to if the Lakers signed Deandre Ayton to a minimum deal while telling him they will max him in 3 years no matter what, but much, much worse, more like Sacramento or Atlanta who has lest hopes of a ring and being at the top of the league doing it.
