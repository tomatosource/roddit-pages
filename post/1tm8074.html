Tavern Talk notes

  • Not sure if mentioned before the tavern talk, but in A3 after the Viper boss fight, there is no longer a secondary zone to get lost in (which could be especially annoying if you already took a while to find the boss). The exit to the next zone appears when you kill the boss.

  • Absent Amulets (from Ornate Wombgifts) will likely be similar to Simplex amulets in their chase item status, as they can roll any spirit reservation skill in their implicit

  • Not new info, but they talked about how much better it felt to have some direction in the endgame. For me personally, I enjoy that a lot. Unlocking the general atlas passives and the league trees is something I look forward to in 0.5. They mentioned that until recently, only the general passives (from the Fortress) had the “shortcut” (extra points) from killing the pinnacle boss, but now you get points this way from the league mechs as well.

  • Unsure if it will make it to 0.5, but they are looking to modulate the ability for rares/essences to spam abilities when affected by several/many extra mods.

  • Curse mods were very frequent in maps as each curse was a different mod and weighted normally. Felt bad to feel like you’re dodging curse circles every map, so curse mod weighting is lowered.

  • A pass was done to map mods so you always feel rewarded for adding more mods (compared to before when some mods were useless). Increased monster effectiveness is now another bonus vector (like quant/rarity/pack size etc).

  • Atlas Masters fulfill the role of reactive bonuses (swapping depending on what content shows up on the infinite atlas), as opposed to the more static setup of the atlas tree. Allows for more risk/reward as there’s no friction to swapping/removing them. Allows for more experimental/ambitious bonuses (new masters, etc) that don’t require reworking the base atlas tree.

  • League trees are like the general atlas – you can allocate every point and there are choice nodes.

  • There’s a lot of stuff not revealed or intended to be secret for this patch, maybe more than any before.

  • Biomes are more relevant now – more Atlas passives that relate to Biomes, and Doryani has biome related node(s) in his atlas master tree.

  • It was a discussed, conscious decision to make leagues have items that are theoretically BiS compared to world drop items, but they want the BiS status to be build specific, not general power. The items will be rare and not for every item slot (Genesis tree is jewelry for example, amulets most obviously). Personally I like this direction, but like Mark says, it’s a delicate and dangerous balance to not trivialize world drops.

  • TotA unlikely to make it for 1.0 (likely coming with a Karui themed league). Quality of the game has gone far past what the assets of poe1 TotA has to offer, so it’s a lot of dev/gfx work from the ground up.

  • Pretty interesting discussion about unique design. Hard/impossible to port poe1 uniques to poe2. Want uniques to have an identity/fulfill a fantasy. They want to add mid-game uniques (some coming in the economy resets between 0.5 and 1.0) that fill the void between early game uniques and endgame uniques. They very much want to make it so you can use a perfect jeweler orb to give max sockets to a non-lvl 20 unique (“hopefully” gets done in time for 0.5).

  • Economy resets between 0.5 and 1.0: General balance/bug fixes, passive trees, base types, chaos dmg/ignite, more campaign replayability improvements, new ranger/druid ascendancy, Phrecia type shakeups each reset.

  • Rest in píss recombinator (good enough reason for me)

  • Divines more common so people whíne less about drops compared to 0.4
