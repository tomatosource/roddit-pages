Are bosses too difficult? (with atlas points)

It could be that just my build is having really hard time with bosses, which it does. I know my build struggles with things I have hard time freezing.

But still… I can clear T15 fully juiced maps and I did probably hundred of them by now and maybe died once.
So I was overconfident and allocated the points to boss atlat tree. Only to realise that I die like every 3 maps. So I am putting them away for now. Until my character becomes much stronger, if ever.

  1. Difficulty vs reward is just not worth it, and you could say that with any content that you scale outside of capabality of your character. If it’s too hard or it is too dangerous to do or it takes so much time that it is not worth doing beside challanging yourself.
  2. I die to mechanics I know very well because of screen clutter of my own skills and I don’t play something crazy. I play Werewolf. But when there are cold meteors flying down, boss is surrounded by my wolfs, ground is fronzen around him I have hard time seeing the mechanics.

I think what confuses me is that… in current game there is never reason to not apply your atlas points. Sure, other mechanics can increase in difficulty. But not to do point where you can’t do it anymore. I guess the bosses are unique in that regard.

Maybe it just comes down to the rewards.
