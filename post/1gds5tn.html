The Thunder are the first team in NBA history to block 30 shots and record 35 steals in the first 3 games of a season.

The Thunder racked up 30 blocks and 37 steals in their first three games this year, which has never been done. They are also the 4th team in NBA history to get 30 blocks and force 60 turnovers in their first 3 games. If you reduce the first 3 games filter to just any 3-game span, then the Thunder are just the 4th team since 2000 to force 60 turnovers and 30 blocks in 3 games.
