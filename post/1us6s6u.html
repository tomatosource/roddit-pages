The skill tree can feel disappointing at times

PoE2 has an incredibly expansive skill tree that (in theory) should allow you to make any kind of build and play style.

However by the time you reach the end game, I find that if I’m not picking the skills that directly buff damage or defenses it massively hampers my ability to clear maps out.

There are so many different skills that I would describe as quality of life, like remnant production and gathering range, are just not good choices. Sure you can make them work, but selecting that over say, 10% increased elemental damage feels so bad.

I almost wish there was like, two separate skills trees with utility separated so there’s not so much fomo.
