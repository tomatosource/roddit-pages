DeMar DeRozan could finish this season top 15 all time in points, and yet there’s a decent chance he doesn’t make the hall of fame.

DeRozan currently sits at 25,435 points, 25th all time. He’s scoring 20 a game so far this season. If he keeps that up, he should land just under 27k points at the end of this season, which should land him around 15th all time. By the end of his career, he could have 30k points. However his hall of fame resume is relatively shaky. He’s a 3X all NBA, 0X 1st team. No awards, and just a 6 time all star, all in the weak east. He’s never mad a finals, certainly never won a ring, and yet he was a relatively prolific scorer. That’s in part due to the fact that he is one of the most healthy players of the 21st century; he’s missed 8 games or less in a season 14 times, and missed just 11 during the COVID season.

For reference, the highest player on the all time scoring list that isn’t in the hall of fame and isn’t a shoe in (Lebron, KD, curry, Westbrook etc) is Joe Johnson and Tom chambers, both with barely 20k points.
