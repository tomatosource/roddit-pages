While we are discussing “+Level to Skills”, please don’t forget the problem with rarity still exists and needs to be adressed

In 0.4, they heavily reduced the amount of rarity each modifier, which in theory reduced the power of each rarity affixes.

However, players are still either aiming for +100% to +150% sweetspot for rarity in solo or using rarity bot for group play. It’s just that ppl need to invest more resources/more modifiers into rarity. This issue is counteracted heavily by how strong temple is in 0.4 and therefore kinda burried underground a little bit this league.

It didn’t solve the issue that rarity is still needed and is limiting the gearing and builds, since weaker skills/builds need more modifiers to make it become endgame viable and can’t have both damage and rarity at the same time.

In a way, it’s the same problem with +level to skills and by just reducing the power of the affixes, it can’t be solved.

What really needs to be done is to buff the scaling curve early and nerf the scaling curve later. In that way the investment into rarity/+level to skills is still justified and worth it, but we don’t need to stack these modifiers on every piece of gear in every possible way anymore.
