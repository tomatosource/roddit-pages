Should boss rewards scale with time?

An interesting take I thought of.

What if bosses give better loot the longer you take to kill them? This would mean that if your build falls short, it wont be a waste of your time to face the boss mechanics and challenge it brings.

This would also mean blasting bosses from 100 to 0 wouldn’t provide you the best benefit either. Im not saying if you have one of those builds you won’t get loot, but there would be a baseline and the scaling would improve to be geared towards actually interacting with all the boss mechanics making it overall more rewarding. Maybe its not “time” but an internal counter on how many actions the boss performs (weighted) to scale loot.

What do you guys think?
