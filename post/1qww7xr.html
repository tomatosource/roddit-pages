A’s just sent out surveys to prospective seat purchasers

Just got an email with a survey about upcoming sale of A’s ticket packages. I live in Vegas so I guess I’m placed in some sort of priority status, but really I’m just looking to get consistent decent seats for Angels games.

Email doesn’t say much

The A’s new ballpark in Las Vegas is taking shape, and we’re preparing to go on sale with seating options. Seating will go on sale in phases, and fans will be contacted based on their responses and availability. We’d like to hear what matters most to you.

Please take a moment to complete the short six-question, survey here so we can better understand your seating preferences and connect you with the right options as they become available.

Take Survey

A member of our sales team may follow up with you on next steps.

Thank you for being part of this exciting journey!

The options are just “suites, small groups, club seats, regular season tickets, and partial season ticket packages”. I picked club seats since watching the Angels lose in a suite is not really any better than watching them lose at home except it cost a bunch more. At least from the club seats I get to heckle.

Then there were questions about my season ticket status with local teams (which is none, Raider tickets + seat licenses cost tons and the team is a contender for 1st overall pick year after year, the Golden Knights are much better but also expensive and don’t play when weather is hottest, and my address is not just in Clark County but literally in the city of Las Vegas so not sure why any of the other stuff matters that much since I watch baseball if it’s around and the weather isn’t going to roast my ass). Finally it asked how many tickets I’m looking to purchase and since there’s no option for “1”, I picked “1-2” even though to fill that plus one on a regular basis I’d have to find a new partner since in 12 years I managed to drag this lady to exactly one game just to watch the Angels blow it in extras.

Current status of ballpark: not enclosed yet, the first and second concourses are partially built, no word as to how they’ll fit a hotel and casino in on the piece of property that wouldn’t obstruct LAS’ landing path and also not butt into the circle designated as the ballpark that is not allowed to include any part of a hotel/casino, and with an additional kicker that the clause cannot be changed. Good luck with fitting in anything of scale right at the end of one of the LAS runways, I guess.

Also: The priority list, which the team is still soliciting $19.01 per person just to get on, is ostensibly set so that current season ticket holders in W. Sac get top proority, followed by Aviators (AAA) season ticket holders, followed by Clark County residents. Group seating is an option alongside suites, buit not premium seating between club seats and suite. The small groups are not specified in terms of size and section but my guess is probably blocks of 10-40 seats like one’d see at most ballparks. There’s also a question in regards to whether the tickets are for businses or pleasure and since nobody knows if congress is able to get their shit together and allow for 100% deduction on wagers that didn’t win any money. and there’s no option for “masochism”, I picked pleasure, even though it feels wrong.

And no date or price info anywhere yet. Reading the tealeaves, my guess is that the wait list isn’t at 30k yet. Vegas is full of vaporware projects so I’ll believe it when I see it actually finished Anyone else get the email?
