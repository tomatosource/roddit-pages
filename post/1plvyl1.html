There is a really nasty crash bug that can delete all your progress (items/XP) from that instance, when transforming back into a human. Is this happening to anyone else?

Over a dozen times today I have crashed when transforming back into a human on Whakapanu and Isle of Kin. The crash deletes all unsaved character progress, so I have lost over a level of XP and many drops. PS5 Pro

https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3886699/page/1#p26478311

UPDATE: I was able to determine root cause and workaround. When you transform back into human with barkskin buff active and barkskin is only in your transformed weapon set, it crashes every time. Workaround is to use barkskin in both weapon sets, or not use barkskin, or don’t use transforming.
