The Future of the Temple

I believe most of us would agree that the temple isn’t going to be integrated into the main game the way it is now. It’s just too much better than anything else for the game to continue like this and just too hard to balance new league mechanics against it.

What I don’t think we all agree on is how they will change it to fit into the game among the other mechanics of the game.

I wanted to start this discussion to share my thoughts on how it could be changed in 0.5 and to hear other people’s guesses and theories.

I think it’s almost guaranteed that it will be added to the main game, I think the times of GGG just letting a mechanic rest and not immediately using it are bygone.

I also feel like this one’s a bit different than others as it’s not a mechanic where getting it in a map is the rewarding part, but running it separately from your maps. In this aspect it’s a bit like PoE 1’s Delve: you get your crystals in the map, but the outcome is not bound to map mods and ‘juice’. So I think a tablet for it is very unlikely. But I’m guessing tablet mods like we have for Spirits or Essence.

I think GGG can go two routes: either making it much harder to maintain a temple (reducing lock drops for example) or making it scale much less with the number of rooms.

I really hope they will implement some QoL and make it a bit less unforgiving (by adding a undo button).

My fear is that they will instead completely gut it by needing it from two end (as they have often done), making it barely profitable until they patch it again.

What do you guys think will happen?
