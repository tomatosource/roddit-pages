PSA: Neversink Strict and above is hiding Normal (White) Utility Belts

I was wondering why I was getting so many Heavy Belts and no Utility Belts. It turns out that Utility Belts are classified as inferior chancing bases, along with Silver Charms.
At the time of writing a Heavy Belt is 15Ex, and a Utility Belt is 10Ex, while a Silver Charm is only 1Ex (if it even sells).

It’s up to you to decide if you will edit your filters, I just wanted to let you know you might be missing some Ex every now and then… or you could be like me and throw Chance Orbs at belts hoping to hit it lmao. Stay sane Exiles
