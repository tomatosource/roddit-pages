XP loss, can we at least get escalating amounts?

Look, honestly, I don’t even think we need an XP loss at all, but I got no hope of winning that argument, so, can we at least have incrementing loss?

Start at a lower %, and increase slowly per level? Something such that one random death is minimal, but a build truly not capable of surviving can’t brute force through.

Yes, you lower the amount by 75% with the Amelioration omen but even that loss is hours of lost gameplay ay high levels, and if you die again in the same instance, tough luck boy, you lose a day’s progress.

Yes, level 100 is aspirational, but a quick glance to Ninja shows you that it is clearly achievable, so why the need to gate it so hard? Would the game change in anyway if 20% more players got to 100?

Yes, I should just “not die”, thanks, but between my build choice (not insta screen clearing) and occasional “huh?” deaths to hidden ground effects, realistically, this attitude furthers the “build to screen clear” concept that stifles build freedom
