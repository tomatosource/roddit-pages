While the Jazz blatant tank strategy is in the news, here’s a funny look into other teams that did similar..

Golden State in 2012

They owed a protected pick to Utah and managed to keep it, blatantly resting guys on certain days at certain times. It ends up working out for them pretty well seeing as they landed Harrison Barnes. They traded Monte Ellis for an injured Andrew Bogut to lose 17 of 20. Some might call this ethical, but their methods were obvious in hindsight.

Golden State also tanked 2020 and their 2020 tank wasn’t necessarily built on the same methods we see these days and this example is how you do a single season tank I guess.

Raptors 2025 “ethical tank more or less the exact same thing as the Jazz are doing now where Toronto sat guys as needed. I was surprised to not hear more about this one.

Wizards right now.. not even gonna link an article or anything, Washington is sitting Trae and AD the whole year. We all know how badly injured they actually are though so I guess this is just ethical young player development.

Indiana has been a respectable tank team but it’s worth mentioning that they’ve handicapped Siakam all year.

Mavs blatantly throw game to keep pick 2023 pretty sure this one was investigated by the league but this seems to be the model Utah follows now where their guys still start games but rest the end, truly inspiring.

I could list more examples, but yeah tanking is a huge issue. I think teams like Sacramento and New Orleans (who don’t even own their pick) are actually pretty ethical with their process since, they’re just really bad, but I’ve seen a lot of overlooked tank jobs that really deserve their shine.

I’m a Jazz fan, obviously I’m going to be biased and want to defend my team, and the pick is top 8 protected, they’ve done some diabolical stuff this year, but it definitely blows my mind how it’s all over the news when other teams have gotten away with it before, and it really isn’t even the worst tank Utah has pulled off, where in 2023 they had Juan Toscano Anderson, Luka Samanaic and other NBA legends finishing games.

All that being said, yeah something is probably going to have to be done about this, but for the people saying Utah should be punished, maybe we should look at all of the other blatant examples of teams who got away with it, or are doing the exact same thing.
