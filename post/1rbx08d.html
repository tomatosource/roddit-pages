Crafting Is Already Too Bloated And Needs Changes ASAP

First off, I absolutely love this game. I have over 500 hours in and I’m providing this feedback because I want this game to succeed for years to come:

Normal, greater, and perfect versions of transmutation, augmentation, regal, and exalt orbs – 12 different orbs that all do the same thing (add 1 more modifier) except under different conditions that are hidden to the player, since tiers and tier LEVELS are different things.

Same thing with Abyss bones (gnawed, preserved, and ancient versions of jawbones, ribs and collarbones) – all to do the same thing (desecrate) but for different item types. Why?

Armourer’s Scraps, Blacksmith’s Whetstones, Arcanist’s Etchers, Gemcutter’s Prisms and Glassblower’s Baubles – all do the same thing (add quality) but for different item types. Why? Also, why are catalysts (another quality item) limited to jewelry?

Lesser, Normal, and Greater versions of 19 different essences and 13 different runes.

30 different soul cores. 27 different omens. 18 different idols. 8 new Vaal currencies (that are under “Incursion” in the currency exchange for some reason?)

In my opinion, this is complexity for complexity’s sake. I think it’d be much better if GGG:

  • Got rid of the lesser / normal / greater system everywhere
  • Combined currencies that do the same thing (e.g. abyss bones, normal quality items)
  • Allowed catalysts (breach) and bones (abyss) to be applied to all item types
  • Added a currency exchange tab for ALL socketables (so we can search all)
  • Added a currency exchange tab for ALL crafting currency (so we can search all)

Or just do the obvious and bring back the crafting bench.
