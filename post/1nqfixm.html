Anyone agree that this is Make or Break season for Jordan Walsh?

Been a few posts over the summer about Walsh, with varying opinions basically asking the Celtics community how much you all like him as a prospect.

To me, this is a Make or Break year for Walsh. The opportunity he necessarily hasn’t had his first two seasons, he will this year. Offensively, outside of crashing the boards, we’re still not sure what he can actually do at an NBA level. He had some moments in Summer League, but the question is whether he can do that in a structured environment. His decision making is still pretty wild based on what we saw in Vegas, so the jury is out.

I am expecting the Celtics to play faster this season than they have been which will benefit Walsh. He’s still so young, he’ll have his opportunities going forward, but if he can’t really prove himself to be a positive rotation contributor this season, he could get lost in the shuffle. Would you all agree this is a Make or Break year for Walsh?
