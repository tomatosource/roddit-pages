Is Queta the long-term solution at center?

Before the season started, the one position that I dreaded was the center spot. Losing KP, Al, and Kornet meant it was obvious Queta was going to be starting center but equally obvious he was not the long term solution.

Now with the regular season almost wrapped up, the Celtics being where they are, and how much of a leap Queta has made this season especially recently, are we fine if he is the long term starter? Props to Garza as well as he struggled early on but got better as the season progressed (so much so that I probably can argue giving Garza minutes in the playoffs over Vuch)

Regardless of what happens in the post season, Cs have much more flexibility to improve the roster. If Queta is not the answer, who will be?
