Spoiled by grand expeditions and the state of Difficulty

For a while I felt that the game had no “very difficult” content, until I started juicing grand expeditions a few weeks ago. Additional random modifiers plus max effectiveness on tablet and map mods coupled with rune stacking makes grand expeditions, in my opinion, the ceiling of difficulty in the game.

As such, while it fully satisfied my craving for difficult content, it does make the rest of the game feel like a breeze. Back then, 200% deli maps were buildmakers’ measure of a good build that can handle hard content. Now, if a build doesn’t mention or show juiced expeditions, I won’t know if the build is effective for hard content. I fully appreciate creators that mention if a build can’t do expeditions.

The point of my post is that, compared to POE1 where there are T17s and ubers, in POE2 the hardest content is a rare mosquito with siphon mana and 20 other modifiers. My hope is that GGG has a roadmap to introduce harder content like uber pinnacles or make all the other league content (abyss, ritual, deli, especially breach) difficult *and* rewarding in their own ways.

My question is: what kind of difficulty should POE2 have that makes it different from POE1? POE2 sort of lost its direction on mechanically challenging content (ie timing, dodging, etc) and became more similar to POE1 where its entirely a numbers game getting more defense and offense.

My ideal state of difficulty is that bosses should be more difficult than mapping, it doesn’t make sense that a rare mob is more difficult than a pinnacle boss at least lore-wise. Ideally difficulty is directly commensurate with rewards but I don’t quite believe in that exactly; it reinforces an economy where the rich get richer. There should be an obvious progression in difficulty from T1-T15 (which imo is trivial right now), corrupted T16s and effectiveness tablets, then something else that isn’t only grand expeditions.
