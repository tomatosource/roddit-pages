PSA to any newer players: Don’t leave the Trial of Sekhemas reward room before you’re completely sure you’re done in there.

Learned this the hard way last night after struggling through the trials for the first time. My duo and I finally finished and I was looting the chests, my inventory filled up quickly, so I thought I’d port out to town, empty my bag and jump back in to collect more loot and choose my ascendency. Sadly once you’ve left you are unable to port back into the reward room. My portal could not be clicked on and I was unable to travel to my duo (who was still in the room) via his portrait. There is no warning. This is the first instance of this having happened that I’ve encountered so far. Every other time I’ve ported back to town I could jump straight back in to where I left off so it felt really unintuitive that the game was suddenly restricting this. I’m not sure whether this is intended behaviour or a bug, but thought it might be worth mentioning to anyone who’s new to the game like I am.
