[OC] The Warriors Nightmare Season Injuries Thus Far

- Background -

After seeing Moses Moody’s injury last game, I wanted to compile a list of every GSW player and their injury history this season. Hopefully this post will bring extra clarity to their nightmare season.

Note: This post isn’t AI, I painstakingly checked every roster member before this post.

Also, the current Warriors record is 34-38, meaning 72 games have been played in total.

- Injured Players -

Stephen Curry: He has played 39 games this season averaging 27 PTS. After the game against Detroit on January 30th, 2026, he developed runner’s knee and has been out ever since. Technically, he might be able to play again before this season ends. However, even with him on the court, the Warriors don’t have a good enough lineup to compete thanks to injuries.

Jimmy Butler: He played 38 games this season averaging 20 PTS before tearing his ACL on February 9th, 2026. He is out for the season.

Draymond Green: He has played 59 games this season. To be honest, he hasn’t been injured all season, but his impact has severely diminished without Stephen Curry. He currently has a -125 plus-minus: His Stats.

Al Horford: He has played 43 games this season and has been out since March 13th, 2026 with a right calf strain. As a 39 year old, he frequently sat on back-to-backs throughout the season and is now sidelined for the time being.

De’Anthony Melton: He has played 41 games this season averaging 13 PTS. Although he hasn’t been injured this season, he returned from a repaired ACL during December 2025.

Seth Curry: He has played 4 games this season, 2 in December and 2 in March. He suffers from a ton of injuries that you can check here: Seth’s Injury History. After rehabilitating his injuries all season, he returned in March and immediately strained his groin in his second game back. Who knows when he’ll return.

Moses Moody: He played 60 games this season. At the start of March, he went out with with a wrist injury during early March. Then in his first game back, he suffered a torn patellar tendon in his left knee when trying to dunk. As such, he will be out for the entire season, and possibly the entire 2026-2027 season as well.

Kristaps Porzingus: He played 8 games for the Warriors this season, averaging 17 PTS. Before getting traded from the Hawks, he played with them for 17 games. Thus, he has played 25 games total this season. He is almost always out with any injury under the sun.

- Healthy Players -

Quinten Post: He will miss his second consecutive game due to foot injury management and is currently out. However, he was largely healthy all season, playing 66 games.

Gary Payton (64 games), Will Richard (62 games), Brandon Podziemsky (72 games: He played every game), and Gui Santos (61 Games) have not been out for any notable period this season.

Pat Spencer has also not been injured (56 games). He was signed on a two-way in early January and has since been upgraded to a full contract.

Omar Yurtseven, Nate Williams, Malevy Leons, and LJ Cryer are 2-way contract players. For obvious reasons, they will be cut if they get injured.

- Traded Players -

Jonathan Kuminga played 20 games for the Warriors before getting traded to the Hawks. Since then, he has played 8 games. He has suffered from persistent knee issues all season, and trade-itis also contributed to his lack of games.

Buddy Hield: He played 44 games for the Warriors before getting traded to the Hawks. Since then, he largely rides the bench, only playing 5 games. He has been healthy all season, but he doesn’t play every game because he’s no longer good (no offense).

- TLDR -

Every key Warriors player (Steph, Jimmy, Kristaps) has missed substantial time this season. Additionally, injuries have impacted the rest of the roster as well. As a result, the Warriors are now the 10th seed in the West led by Podz.

My own personal opinion is that their top-end injuries are largely due to having many veteran players. However, their other injuries are unfortunate. Practically speaking, their season is basically over now. Hopefully they can rebound next season.
