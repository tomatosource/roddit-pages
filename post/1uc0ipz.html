Despite Owning the 9th Pick in Tuesday’s Draft, the Dallas Mavericks Have Yet to Hire a Head Coach

Someone made a post earlier about the Blazers not hiring a head coach before the draft. But, the Blazers currently have no picks in this year’s draft, so not having a head coach is probably not a huge concern.

However, that isn’t the case for the Dallas Mavericks. Dallas has TWO first round picks and one second rounder on Tuesday, in a draft that many front offices expect to be very fruitful.

Here’s who they have on roster for next year as of now:

Kyrie Irving
Cooper Flagg
Klay Thompson
Daniel Gafford
PJ Washington
Caleb Martin
Naji Marshall
Max Christie

Team Options: AJ Johnson, Dereck Lively

RFA: Ryan Nembhard, Moussa Cisse

Aside from Flagg, Dallas is lacking young talent to develop at the moment. Instead, they are a team full of veteran role players, basically a blank slate in terms of team identity. Kyrie has just one year left on his contract before it becomes a Player Option next summer, so a trade could be on the horizon.

With the vision for the future unclear, this could be an intriguing team to watch on draft night. But, with no coach expected to be hired by Tuesday evening, it will be up to 2 new hires, Team President Masai Ujiri and GM Mike Schmitz, to decide what direction to take going forward.
