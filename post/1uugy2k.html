I can finally enjoy POE2 without Crashing

For the longest time, I’ve been trying to get my system to stop crashing mid fight or even having performance issues when big clutter fights happens on mapping.

All I know is Screw DX12.
I’ve tested Vulkan in the past and It did not perform as expected or even worse than DX12.

I don’t know if its this patch or something they fixed with Vulkan, but man its looks great and performs soo smooth now.

Just to start out. I have a good system.
AMD 9950x3D (PBO / CO configured)
Nvidia 5090 Astral
64GB DDR5 6000 cl 26 fclk 2200 tweaked

NVidia Global Settings
Low Latency - Ultra
Power mgmt mode- ether one Max or Normal.
Shader cache- Size 100GB

This part is the Change needed to see the crash disappear.
Everything else is optional.
Make sure you dump your Shader cache in your POE2 APPDATA folder when switching to Vulkan.
In the POE2 game. Switch to Vulkan.

You can still utilize DLSS to make it look pretty (I tested both DLAA and DLSS quality and both looks almost the same with better latency and frames with DLSS quality.
Depending on your system.
Choose your graphic settings as fit to your system.
I have everything on Max on mine.
Sharpening is set to 90.
Bloom to 100.
Dynamic Resolution off
Triple Buffering on
Nvidia Reflex on/boost

Let the shader compile for a bit. you can do this by turning on your network meter in the options on the bottom of graphics.
Once enabled you will see a bar called Shader. wait till the green bar drops down to 0. then you can game out.

Hope this helps for the rest of you.
If not.. keep searching. All I know, I’m clearing maps crazy fast now.
Good luck and God speed.
