Positivity Post (for those sick of the media telling us how to think and feel)

Reading all of everything about all the stuff that has been happening just makes me want to hold out for Flagg in green. That’s so boring and glass half empty.

I just want the Celtics to do well and JT to be healthy. We don’t have to win all the time but we should be competitive. I’m ready to be surprised by the next few years before we get another banner. JT is PP and JB is AW. History repeats itself. 19 will be here sooner than we all think, and thank the lord we bleed green. I’m 33 and I can’t imagine being a lifelong fan of any other team. I feel bad for anyone who isn’t a Celtics fan honestly.

We’re the Celtics, what other franchise is ready to hang 19 in their original city? I’m ready to blindly support the team, even if I have to claw and scratch my way to a silver lining. If that silver lining is just that we’re the Celtics… that’s awesome. I don’t care what we do I’m just going to blindly support it and be happy that I’m not a Pacers or Pelicans fan. Boston is beautiful and so are all of you, no one needs to freak out, it’s all gonna be good.

I’m obv pretty wasted, but it just needs to be said. We’re the Celtics.. am I wrong? So if anyone feels the need to comment it should be a ‘remember when’ of their favorite Celtics moment.

There’s certainly flaws to this take, but if you point them out you must be rooting for the lakers. Time to move on down the road, and if you want to look back. Just jump off the wagon. Otherwise, grab a cigar and give a nod to Red bc we ain’t going nowhere ☘️
