Second+ Characters

I’ve played 4 of the leagues so far in PoE2 and every league I only get a single character to maps. Twice I’ve tried starting a second character but by the time I hit act 2 my interest completely evaporates.

I think the vast majority of players are like this. We would love to try other builds/characters but the 20-30 hour(?) grind to get to maps on a second character, let alone a third, is just so freaking dull - and I actually enjoy the first campaign play through every league.

Other games offer various solutions for this.

Borderlands 4 allows your second character to start at level 50.

Diablo 4 doesn’t make you tag all the passive point statues and tedious account progression stuff on additional play throughs

Etc

Even something as simple as autocompleting the side quests to get spirit/passive points would be a god send.

If they allowed this then the wave of people quitting and giving away their stuff wouldn’t happen just a month into a league and would keep their player numbers high for much longer than they currently are.

That in turns helps keep the trading economy buying equipment much longer. I mean if you are trying to sell anything other than a unique that vaal’d over max stats or a failed mirror tier item right now, you’re lucky to get a div for it. I can only imagine how bad it gets 3 months into a league, but I’ve never made it beyond 2 months.

The end game has become pretty fun. Being able to access other characters to keep the excitement going would be good for every aspect of the game.
