Critical hits should be rolled on hit, not on ability cast

For this league I decided to create a build for myself and I ended up settling on Invoker with Cold Twisters due to the notables of creating Chilled Ground on freeze and ignoring resistances on critical hit, which sounded really good on paper combined with Tangletongue.

However, it turned out to be really unsatisfying to play because critical hits are rolled once when I cast Twister and that determines if every Twister hit from that cast is a crit or not, making it feel really inconsistent at times. I have around 80% effective crit chance, and spending a fully charged Salvo + all my Frenzy Charges for Barrage and dealing almost no damage because I got unlucky feels terrible because now I have to wait another 6 seconds and generate more charges to get another attempt at dealing any meaningful damage.

Changing the way crits are rolled wouldn’t have any effect on average DPS, but it would greatly improve the consistency of that DPS, allowing builds that have a longer prep time for their damage or slower attack speed to not feel punished for getting unlucky.
