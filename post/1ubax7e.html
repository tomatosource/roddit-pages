You can mass birth wombgifts in the tree

I just discovered this accidentally and haven’t seen anyone else mention it before, so here it goes.

In the genesis tree, take a wombgift as you’d usually do, and put that into the slot. Then instead of a regular click, use ctrl+click on the purple hand. This gives you a purple cursor and then you can click a bunch of wombgifts in your stash or inventory to birth them all really fast.
