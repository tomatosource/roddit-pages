If you haven’t liked the Celtics 3 point strategy of the past few seasons I have bad news for you..they’ll be shooting way more without Brown.

Brown’s game has always been less than ideal for the Celtics math/analytics strategy.

Brad and Mazulla want to shoot more threes than anyone. They want more shots than the other team. They want those shots to be threes. Brown is great at getting buckets but those buckets are often contested at the rim or they are mid range jumpshots- which i guarantee you Mazulla and Brad would not be fans of.

Was the return less than ideal? Yeah probably. But I’m not sure there was too much else to be had with his looming extension situation.

I will say that get ready for a team that shoots more threes than we’ve ever seen. Paul George is good at it- that’s where he fits in at the short term JB stop gap. He won’t be taking as many mid range jumpers- but he’ll be given the green light to let it fly from deep at any opportunity.

Same with Mike Conley in the corners.

Mitch Robinson will be eating up offensive boards and getting either an easy put back or looking for a shooter from deep.

If you haven’t liked the Celtics three point shooting style of the past few seasons I have bad news for you. It’s going to another level this season. The majority of those JB midrange jump shots will be three point attempts.

I actually think we’ll again attempt to break the record for most 3pt attempts in a season.

Is this a good thing? Not if you hate three point spam- but to beat OKC, Spurs, New York, Miami it’s basically our best shot.

So at least there’s a plan here. That being:

  1. easy transition baskets- especially catch and shoot threes.
  2. creating good 3 point shots in the half court.
  3. minimising turnovers
  4. defending the three and coaxing our opponents to take tough two point shots.
  5. rebounding to create more 3 point opportunities on offense and defensive rebounding to create more transition opportunities.

I’m a Celtic for life so I’m behind it. How else can you beat this Spurs team that’s only gonna get better?
