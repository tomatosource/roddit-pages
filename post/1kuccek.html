Stats from 50 Ultimatum tries

So i saw an upcoming patch with new soul cores and decided to stop my project at the point of 50 runs. From start i planned to make 100–150 runs… Probably, now my stats are kind of worthless.

Google sheet with summary of drop

Disclaimer: If u want to test your own drop and record stats, here is a blank version with a little guide. You can copy to your drive and use it. If you have any questions about how this sheet works, i will try to answer as soon as i have time. Sorry for my low English, btw.

Interesting numbers:

  1. The most valuable income (exclude Adorned) was from Ticaba core.
  2. Random drops from monsters and bosses generate a noticeable part of the profit, BUT i ran with 100% irr. Even i got 3 divines from rooms, i still generate the same amount of currency from the floor.
  3. ~3% spawn of unique corrupted on 1st “floor”. Only one item got something for corruption (3 sockets on warstaff) but still cost 1 ex.
  4. 1st boss room has a strict pool of 4 cores, and it doesn’t depend on the boss.
  5. 2nd floor can only have x1 multiplier for room reward. At the same time, you can see x3 only in room 9.
  6. It looks like the 2nd boss also has some “excluded” cores. We can assume we can find the most rare cores in boss3.
  7. I don’t understand market prices on fragments. I made a small formula to calculate the price for the same cost of a full Trialmaster try based on drop, and numbers are really different. Just interesting.
  8. 2% adorned confirmed :D The reason why i decided to do this record is adorned drop(68%) from 1st ever Trialmaster when i just tried this content. Made 14 test runs for build adjustment before record. So got 2 adorned in 65 runs but didn’t put it cause rooms wasn’t recorded.
  9. I calculated “jackpots” as something above average profit per run. Number was 14. Basically this number should have meaning: “every 3rd run will increase your average drop”. Decided to leave “jackpot” as number above Divine Price.

Thought about content overall:

  1. A possible tradable valuable reward you can receive from floor 1 is some good corrupted unique. But chances of it… I check every item, and from 50 runs i got only one decent rare item that can be sold - a ring with skill speed enchant but the stats on it are not worth buying. Got a couple there and here items that can be sold in theory but only during the start of the league. In poe2 there is still no currency to use on corrupted items, so at this moment this floor is all about finding 1 in million.
  2. Overall, drop rate feels pretty ok - not big in long distance compared to meta farms, but very stable. If your build is VERY fast, your profit per hour will be extremely high, because you don’t need any preparation to rush it.
  3. However, it becomes very repetitive pretty fast. In poe1 players have the opportunity to choose their own way what they want to see as rewards: currency/ins ultimatums/catalysts. In poe2 all you want is to skip deadly affliction. Yeah, Trial of Chaos is still content with something about stat-checking of your build, and fundamentals works fine at this point, but it feels too bony. I think adding something like global modificators on runs can refresh Trial. I mean the choice of how Trail going to reward you and which challenge you will accept for yourself. Even if it might look like Sekhems, I think it will still save the concept of “test your limit” for a bigger reward. For example, if Ultimatum has 10 rooms, 1st boss room can contain a choice (instead of core) from something like:
    • Double currency drop from the final reward room, but remove all drop from monsters during whole run
    • Trialmaster drops an additional exclusive unique reward but receives 30% skill speed or something else challenging enough.
    • All rewards from rooms become cores, but minimum tier of affliction becomes maximum tier.
    • Anything C
  4. Corridors take 20-30% of the run in time, depends on your ms. It’s time when all you need is to just run without any action. Sometimes generation gives you a really long way between rooms. Feels a bit off in game where you always want to be in the middle of action.
  5. Inscribed Ultimatums from Trialmaster feel not exclusive. It’s the same run, actually. Not a big problem, but in theory it may contain some rule changing mechanics: take medium soul core which slowly builds “hex” on you and carry fast it thought 3 floors + 1 boss, after every floor “refresh” hex with fountain. Or something like: survive for 5 minutes of hordes, in last 30 seconds 3 bosses jumps on you. Some sort of combination between unique relics in Sekhems and Inscrtibed Ultimatums from poe1.

I enjoy Trial of Chaos, overall. I did not enjoy at all this content in poe1, but feels in love in POE2. All i wrote was just thoughts and ideas i got during this little project about what can be more fun and interesting to challenge. Definition of my playstyle in poe2, for you understanding, is 2 hours after work here and there, no rush, no guide, my own build just playing what i feel interesting.

What’s next? Going to updating the blank sheet, after next patch, will make it collect more interesting info. Will comeback in next season if i will have time for at least 300+ runs, because collecting statistics is everything! At the moment, i want to rest and start poe1 in June.

Thanks for reading, and stay sane, Exile!
