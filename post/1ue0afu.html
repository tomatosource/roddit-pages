Why wasn’t there a three-team deal for JB/Giannis?

The Bucks deciding between two starkly different pathways never made sense. Typically, a Giannis level star becomes available, and then the bidding war revolves around similar packages of picks and youth.

Here, the Bucks decided between wanting to try and remain relevant with a proven star in Jaylen, or hit reset with Miami’s (below average) package of picks/players.

There was reporting that Portland, Atlanta, and Houston all had interest in Jaylen, and they all have more compelling players/packages than Miami.

The one I can’t get over is Portland. Primarily, they own Milwaukee’s own picks, which is the most valuable thing you can have when you’re tanking/rebuilding. In addition, I find Scoot/Sharpe to be vastly more interesting than Herro & co.

(On a side note, I think some mix of Jrue/JB/Deni/Rob/Camara is REALLY good)

Considering reports that these same teams are still expressing interest in JB after the fact tells me that this was impatience due to a self imposed draft day deadline by Milwaukee, which is malpractice. You’ve waited a year to move Giannis, what’s a few more days to maximize your return?

I could be making too much of this, and ultimately I’m glad that Jaylen remains in Boston. However, I am just dumbfounded that Milwaukee talked themselves into a reality of keeping Jaylen when they so clearly need to rebuild.
