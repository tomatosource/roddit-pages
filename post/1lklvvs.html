Will PoOE1 Crafting come to POE 2?

More or less a discussion but also game feedback because of what ive learned recently.

With 3.26 released and multiple end game characters in poe2 made, i decided to run a new poe1 toon. And wow, crafting is infinitely better in poe1!

I know it sounds silly and obvious, but I had no idea. I knew 2’s crafting was not really ideal at all, but 1’s crafting is everything poe2 needs right now.

Currently, there’s very limited options on how to get geared for higher end content. I refuse to buy currency from 3rd party sites, so ive literally only killed abberoth once since the game came out….which probably doesnt sound like I play much but I have probably 400 hours in the game since the beginning. As it stands its nearly useless to “craft” items until whittles etc because youre far more likely to find what you need on trade with a quarter of the cost of time and currency spent to obtain it.

In poe1 you can have shit gear, and literally add whatever you need to the items for easy currency, whether its resistances, damage, health, defenses etc. I honestly dont know how it didnt come shipped with the same crafting system…..
