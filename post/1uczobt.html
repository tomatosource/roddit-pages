Data from 1,000 Lavish Wombgifts (Catalyst Weights)

Hi all,

I spent some time running Breach to harvest approximately 1,000,000 Hiveblood, and used it on 1,000 Lavish Wombgifts (ilvl 79-80) with the “Only drop catalysts” node allocated and all other points spent on surpassing chance to drop additional items, totalling 240% additional chance, meaning 1 womb gift gives 3.4 catalysts on average. I recieved 3411 catalysts total, aligning with that math.
