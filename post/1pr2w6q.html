Victor Wembanyama reaches 100 straight games with at least 1 block, extending the third longest streak in NBA history

With his 2 blocks against the Atlanta Hawks tonight, Victor Wembanyama has now recorded 100 straight games with at least 1 blocked shot; extending the third longest streak in NBA history.

He gets closer to Dikembe Mutombo at 2nd place with 116 consecutive games and Patrick Ewing who owns the record with 145 consecutive games.

With 55 games remaining in the Spurs’ regular season, Wembanyama could pass both players before the end of the 25-26 season at just 22 years old.
