Francisco Lindor has played the same number of games for the Guardians and Mets (777). Here are his stats (regular season only).

Guardians: .285/.346/.488, .833 OPS (118 OPS+), 896 H, 138 HR, 99 SB (78.6 SB%), 3510 PA, 43 DRS, 23.9 oWAR, 9.0 dWAR, 28.3 WAR

Mets: .259/.337/.458, .795 OPS (122 OPS+), 782 H, 142 HR, 119 SB (82.6 SB%), 3425 PA, 9 DRS, 26.6 oWAR, 5.4 dWAR, 27.5 WAR

The OPS+ figure might catch people by surprise. It’s a reflection of how hostile the offensive environment has become in recent years.

Overall, Lindor has maintained remarkable consistency for over a decade, despite peaks and valleys within seasons. He’s only had two full seasons below a 115 OPS+, all while playing tremendous defense at SS and hardly ever missing games. Newer fielding metrics such as OAA are even higher than DRS on his Mets tenure.

Sitting at 1678 hits, 280 HR, 219 SB, and 55.7 bWAR at age 32, he has a chance to hit some big milestones and solidify a place in Cooperstown. He’s more likely than not to break Cal Ripken Jr’s SS record for HR. He might be slumping to start the year, but if his track record is any indication, he’ll snap out of it soon enough.
