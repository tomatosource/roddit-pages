Julian Champagnie vs Danny Green: A Closer Look

Danny Green (25 years old), 2012-13 regular season:

10.5 PPG

3.1 RPG

1.8 APG

1.2 STL

0.7 BLK

42.9% from three on 5.2 attempts

58.1 eFG%

27.5 MPG

Julian Champagnie (24 years old), 2025-26 regular season:

11.1 PPG

5.8 RPG

1.5 APG

0.8 STL

0.5 BLK

38.1% from three on 6.2 attempts

57.8 eFG%

27.6 MPG

Their minutes are basically identical. Their scoring is basically identical. Their eFG% is almost identical. Both are high-volume spot-up wings who do not need the ball to be useful.

The big difference is how they get their value.

Danny was the better pure 3&D archetype. He shot a higher percentage from three, created more defensive events, and was much more trusted defensively. That part matters a lot. Danny’s value was not just his shooting; it was that he could stay on the floor deep into playoff series because of his defense.

Julian’s case is different. He is bigger, rebounds way better, takes more threes, and turns it over less. He is not the same kind of guard/wing defender Danny was, but as a forward-sized floor spacer, the production is honestly pretty similar.

The playoff comparison is interesting too.

Danny Green, 2012-13 playoffs:

11.1 PPG

4.1 RPG

1.5 APG

1.0 STL

1.1 BLK

48.2% from three

59.4 eFG%

31.9 MPG

Julian Champagnie, 2025-26 playoffs:

10.9 PPG

5.5 RPG

1.5 APG

1.5 STL

0.4 BLK

45.2% from three

66.7 eFG%

28.2 MPG

Julian Champagnie’s age-24 season is statistically in the same neighborhood as Danny Green’s early Spurs breakout. Danny was the better defender and more proven shooter, but Julian is a better rebounder, has more forward size, takes more threes, and is already giving similar low-usage offensive value.
