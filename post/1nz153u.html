Anyone else just really enjoying the campaign and experimenting with different builds?

I’ve seen many posts or comments about people saying once you get to T15s that’s when you’re “really playing the game” or it “really starts”.

Like what? Don’t get me wrong, I’m having a blast with my ice machine gun merc at that level but I’m also enjoying crafting my build for my lightning damage fissure warrior. Or last league with my ice slam warrior, or my lightning monk. I think builds can easily be settled in by Act 4, and in my opinion it’s the best act in the game right now.

I’m really looking forward to when we have all the acts and it feeling like a fully fleshed out story that leads into end game as more of bonus extra content as opposed to what people reach for. I think part of the fun of a game like this is figuring out the puzzle for how to make that specific or niche build you want to work from start to finish.

Edit: Case in point, I just chanced an amythest ring on a whim and got a Blackflame ring, so now my lightning fisurre warrior using Valako’s Vice just changed into a Smith of Chayula build, at least for now. I freaking love this game.
