Is GGG Blaming DDoS when the issue is actually a noisy neighbor? (EU server stability)

Let’s talk about these constant EU server disconnects in POE, especially during prime time.

GGG recently said it was due to a “DDoS attack,” but honestly, does that explanation really hold up?

Here’s my theory: this isn’t DDoS at all—it’s a “noisy neighbor” issue on their cloud infrastructure.

When you share servers with other companies, someone else running massive backups or data syncs can hog all the bandwidth, leaving everyone else to deal with lag and disconnects.

It’s a known problem in cloud computing, but nobody likes to talk about it.

Think about it:

•	The disconnects are like clockwork—always at peak hours.

•	DDoS attacks don’t usually follow a perfect daily schedule.

•	And if this really was a DDoS issue, why is it always just the EU servers getting targeted? Why not US or other regions?

•	If GGG’s on shared infrastructure, all it takes is one heavy customer to screw us all over at the same time every night.

GGG utilizes cloud vendors for their server infrastructure. While specific providers aren’t publicly confirmed, discussions suggest they might be using services like IBM/SoftLayer or similar cloud platforms.

This setup means their servers could be sharing resources with other clients, making them susceptible to the noisy neighbor effect.

Honestly, I’m tired of hearing the “DDoS” excuse for every issue. Maybe it’s just easier to blame mysterious attackers than to admit their server resources aren’t as isolated as we’d expect.

What do you all think? Anyone else feeling like we’re not being told the whole story here?
