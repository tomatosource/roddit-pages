Give Hardcore SSF A Try

For all the POE2 players not going back to POE1 for the new league gimic. I wanted to give my recommendation to give HC SSF a try.

-Its pretty challenging

-No late league trade bs

-They’ve fixed most of the overtuned mobs(one shot bosses are your biggest worry)

-I think the lack of crafting(while it does need to be improved) doesn’t really come into play until you get to 90+ and gear progression really stalls

-Gear upgrades are really felt

TLDR- Fun, mobs no longer overtuned, lack of finding gear overblown.

Thanks for reading my presentation on the hidden joys of HCSSF. Please enjoy the rest of your day.
