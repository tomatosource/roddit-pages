Some ideas for the Atlas system

  1. Allow us to zoom out way more, I mean way way more. Something like the Civilization map could be implemented and that could bring exploration to the next level. (For Example)
    Imagine sharing a screenshot where you explored more km2 than we have on our planet.

  2. Add different points of interest to that map, Towers for vision for example, maybe some bigger Towers for larger scale scouting (more towers lmao), something like the Warp Gates which could teleport you to the random location on the world map, there really a lot of things can be added, those are just a few from the top of my head.

  3. Make first Citadels visible in some area and add quest markers to them (For Example). Citadel can be located in any point of that area, not only in the center because that would be too obvious. Maybe show them not right away but after some milestone which will make sense in the progression.

  4. Make finding Citadels more deterministic by adding certain item or currency which you can apply to the map device or somehow use it, so you can see an area in which Citadel can be found. Ancient Locator or something like that.. Think of some different ways to obtain that, that might be not just the random drop. (Maybe only Empowered Bosses drop)

  5. Make endgame on that Atlas more scalable, for example if you go really far from the startinng location you can encounter new biomes with specific modifiers already applied (which buff difficulty and loot). Something with the corruption theme. Add some tiers of that, so you start lightly, then you go deeper and deeper (pause). You encounter another layer of corruption but to proceed you may need to meet certain requirements or complete an objective. In the first tier there might be just 1-2 mods which will be present on all maps (chosen randomly, same can be rolled on waystones), in the second tier add more mods or make em more difficult. And in the last some unique mods can be added (no volatile cores please). The further you go, the more world crumbles and the final destination could be the Hell itself, heart of the Corruption. Unique tilesets for those bioms will be required. That could be implemented in many ways. Right now engame become kinda stale after some point, there is really no reason to explore the Atlas.

  6. With map like that master missions can be implemented very easily. You talk with the NPC and he asks you to go to some area and find certain item or any other objective. Rewards should worth the hassle.

That sums up my thoughts, lmk what do you think guys
