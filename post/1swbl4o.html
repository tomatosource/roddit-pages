League Start Builds For Each Class (from 0.4.0 for 0.5.0)

This post is to give newish players some ideas for builds to start with for the upcoming league. The campaign can feel smooth as butter with the right starting build and I would like to discuss those builds here. Starting with a good build can give a great head-start in a trade economy. If you are the type of gamer who loves to figure out their build for themselves, this post is NOT for you. If you are an SSF player, this post could be for you but probably not.

I also want to preface this all by saying the following:

This is all from my experience, therefore the information below is NOT objective and it is heavily biased.

Please comment any criticisms or suggestions for better starting builds and I will do my best to update this post with that information. Patch Notes for upcoming league may change the builds on this post as well.

This is not a promotion for content creation. If I link a video or build, and it was YOUR video or YOUR build and you don’t want it referenced here, please let me know.

————————————————————————————

GuyThatDies’ guide on how to quickly navigate through the campaign (I recommend looking at this even if you are a veteran player, he has some great tips): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6SLMvMJb4s

Builds:

DRUID: Ice based druid skills with the oracle ascendancy. I started this on day 1 last league and went in blind, it felt incredibly good and probably feels better now with the updates that have happened since. The build is fairly squishy until later, but has big damage and even bigger ‘pops’ when it comes to clear.
Woolie’s Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/woolie-wolfman

WARRIOR: Cluster grenade mortar cannons with the warbringer ascendancy. Are you are dad gamer who takes a sip of his miller lite between every monster kill? This build is for you, its tanky, afk, and high damage with basically no investment. I played this build for 2 leagues now and will probably play again in 0.5.0.
Tatiantel’s Guide: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npXaIO0keUY

MONK: Hollow palm. don’t want to worry about upgrading you weapon while leveling, do you not want to have to think at all and just button mash and win? Well try this build. This is a build you will have to swap off of later tho for something that has better scaling. Incredibly fun build though and it is easy enough to transition off of it later.
MisoxShiru’s Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/misoxshiru-hollow-palm-monk-league-starter

RANGER/HUNTRESS/MERCENARY: Grenade Grenade Grenades and a crossbow…. then transfer into your desired late game build or just stick with grenades, they are powerful. I have tried league starting each of these classes before with skills other than grenades, grenades were simply better, especially for a speedy campaign.
GuyThatDies’ Guides:
Mercenary: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/guythatdies/builds/0-4-mercenary-grenades-leaguestarter
Huntress/Ranger: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/guythatdies/builds/0-4-huntress-ranger-grenades-leveling

WITCH: Essence drain contagion with the lich ascendancy. if you want something smooth for witch that feels good right out of the gate, try essence drain + contagion with the lich ascendancy, I’ve heard really good things but haven’t played it myself. I’m sure people in the comments will have some insights.
DEADRABB1T’S Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/deadrabbit-crossbow-lich

(honorable mention) WITCH: Spark with the bloodmage ascendancy. Suffer through the campaign with spark and orb of storms and then become a god later. This is a 1 button build essentially, it clears off screen, has crazy defense, and deletes bosses. While this build is more of a late game build, I had so much fun with it even though the campaign on league start is a bit rough, the pay-off is worth it.
ImJustAnthony’s Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/anthonys-vampire-queen

SORCERESS: Thunderstorm with the stormweaver ascendancy. this build is incredibly well-thought out and uses game mechanics in a very clever way. while this build may take a bit more research for a new player to understand, it is worth it to learn. the build has a very strong early game, capable of giving you a very big head start. If you are lazy and don’t want to understand how the build works, find a spark guide somewhere for sorceress.
Jungroan’s Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/jungroan-limitless-stormweaver
