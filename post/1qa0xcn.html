JB continues to struggle in the 4th

As a lead option, and without a peer or someone else initiating the sets and offense in crunch time, JB really is going through some learning pain right now.

He’s had a number of very poor 4th quarters, turnovers, bad shot selection and bad reads

That’s just where he’s at in his development, which is quite late in his career because of his pairing with JT, among other lead guards obviously since he arrived in the league

I do think he’ll figure it out, but it is for sure one of the things that you can’t cover up with effort and hustle - late game poise and execution comes from reps, and learned lessons from failing.

Dude is having a great year, but I think everyone should be objective about his struggles right now.
