PoE 2 Could Really Benefit From a Bestiary / 3D Monster Viewer

One thing I’d love to see added to PoE 2 is a Bestiary or Codex that lets players revisit enemies and bosses they’ve encountered, complete with a 3D model viewer.

PoE 2 has some of the most impressive creature and boss designs I’ve seen in an ARPG. The problem is that during actual gameplay, we’re usually focused on surviving, dodging mechanics, and clearing content as efficiently as possible. Many enemies are only seen for a few seconds before they explode into loot.

A Bestiary could include:

  • 3D models that can be rotated and zoomed in
  • Enemy animations (idle, attacks, death animations, etc.)
  • Basic lore and flavor text
  • Regions or acts where the enemy appears
  • Boss encounter summaries
  • Statistics such as kills, deaths, and first encounter

Beyond being a cool collection feature, it could also serve as a learning tool. Sometimes you die to a boss or rare monster ability and barely understand what happened. Being able to review enemies and their abilities afterward would help players learn encounters while also appreciating the incredible art and animation work that went into creating them.

I think PoE 2’s monster roster is diverse enough that a feature like this would have real value, both for gameplay and for world-building.

Would anyone else use something like this? What information would you want included in a Bestiary?
