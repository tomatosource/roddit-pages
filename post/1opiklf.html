After I made my first one I decided to craft another mirror wand for myself. The plan was to mirror my wand to create the truest value of +3 os for the wand. You cannot even Vaal or sanctify a mirrored item.

I realized then that I do not understand the value of the mirror. The absolute best value you can get from an item is the equivalent of a vaaled extra socket regular base or a unvaaled unsanctified base. So essentially the best you can get with a ~1700 div item is missing a whole socket or a sanctify roll. If mirrors could be vaaled or sanctified after I would understand the value. Especially with the base being unvaalable If wanting to profit and the bricks from failed vaals or sanctified keeping yhe item from being everyone’s main weapon.

I just can’t see with the new runes and socket strength how anyone spending 1700 divs would want something unfinished wi the no chance to finish it?
