PSA: You might be missing 5% max life from Interlude 2

I don’t know if this was due to the server issues last night or what, but when I was doing “The Khani Crossing” zone in Interlude 2, the side objective on the map for “Molten Shrine”, 5% max life, got crossed out without me doing anything. When I found what I thought was the Molten Shrine, a titan skull with a checkpoint and a spoken line from my character, I could not interact with or enter it. I didn’t think much of it.

This morning, I was thinking a bit more clearly, and I got naked and calculated what my max life should be without the 5% max life…and confirmed I did NOT, in fact, have the 5% max life, despite the map marker saying I did.

I went back into “The Khani Crossing” and, in the titan skull, there was now an entrance to a short side zone, at the end of which was a titan statue/altar with a green quest item pick up I clicked and actually got the 5% max life.

If you did not pick up this green item from the titan statue in the short lava side zone, you might be missing 5% max life despite the map saying you have it.
