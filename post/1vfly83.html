I made a super simple app to help slow learners (like me). Dad gamers, this one’s for you!

I was struggling to make good profit from Expeditions until I realized that Rumors are what really matter. After watching a bunch of videos and reading recent posts, I got tired of constantly alt-tabbing to check rumor spreadsheets.

So, with Claude’s help (because I’m definitely not a programmer), I made a very simple app for my own use.

You just select the Rumors you have, and the app tells you what the best decision is. I’m still testing it, but everything seems to be working correctly so far. It’s based on information from recent Expedition videos and community posts.

If you find any mistakes, I apologize in advance. Like I said, I’m not a programmer—I was just tired of alt-tabbing all the time.

It even works in your phone’s browser.
