One shots are a plague

Casual vet with 500hrs played, beat all content on multiple chars. The thing that pisses me off like nothing else and makes me quit the league are one shots. Nothing in this beautiful game makes me want to choke myself to death with skittles more than one shots. I built a glass cannon LA deadeye, sure, one shots are gonna happen dude, don’t worry about it. You’ve got great gear but your build is set up to die sometimes. I built a warrior, armour w ele stacked, gets one shot. Okay man… maybe it’s the gear or build? Proceed to research for hours, adapt, get one shot. Okay, I’ve heard ES is tanky, let’s invest 150divs in a djinn minion Sorc with 7k ES. One shot.

I love this game, but one shots are the absolute counterpoint to my enjoyment with it. It makes me quit every league and play something else.

Thanks for listening to my TED talk 🤙
