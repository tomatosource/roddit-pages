Ascendancy idea - Heir of Perandus

I quite enjoy conjuring ideas for ascendancies as a thought experiment. Obviously not balanced, just thinking what kind of concepts would be fun. This is an idea for a dex/str ascendency.

This ascendancy would be themed around unique items, item rarity and quality.

Notables 1-3:

  1. +15% quality on all equipped armor and weapons
  2. Quality on equipped body armor instead grants increased maximum life.
  3. Quality on main hand weapon instead grants +3 spirit per 1% quality

Small passives grant: +2% quality on all equipped armor and weapons

The \“skill tree\”

Notables 4-6

  1. Increases and reductions to rarity of items found and also apply to damage. 30% less rarity of items found

  2. Modifiers to the rarity of items found only affects the amount of gold found. +1% increased movement speed per 3% rarity of items found 

  3. Grants skill: Weight of Wealth. Leave a trail of gold in your wake that hinders enemies and taunts normal and magic enemies in your presence. Hitting an enemy causes the trail to explode, dealing cold and physical damage. Dexterity increases trail duration, strength increases trail area of effect

Small passives grant: +6% increased rarity of items found

Notables 7-10

  1. Cannot equip non-unique items. 3% increased attributes per equipped unique item

  2. Cannot equip helmets. 10% quality to all equipped items per empty equipment slot. (This passive is free to allocate and has no small passive leading up to it)

  3. Cannot equip gloves. 20% increased rarity of items found per empty equipment slot 

  4. Cannot wear equipment in the off-hand slot. 20% more evasion

Small passives grant: +5% to all resistances
