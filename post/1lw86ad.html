James Harden closing in on 10th All-Time scoring spot, surpassing Carmelo Anthony

With 27,687 career points, The Beard is just 602 points shy of surpassing Carmelo Anthony, who holds the 10th spot with 28,289 points.

What makes this even more impressive is that Harden is on track to achieve it in 2 fewer seasons than Melo. Carmelo played 19 seasons from 2003 to 2022, while Harden is entering his 17th season.

Assuming Harden plays around 70 games this upcoming season, he only needs to average 8.6 points per game to surpass Melo.

Also, he has a real chance of passing Shaq’s 9th place /28,596 points/. Harden would need to average about 13 points over 70 games to pass Shaq.
