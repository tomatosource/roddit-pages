Ryududeplays mid league build compilation/summary!

I’ve been saying for awhile that I would start making legitimate guides but its hard to pull away from playing when i still have so many builds i want to try so I figured instead I’d put together this compilation of all the builds of ran/tried this league and a short summary of the game plan and its pros/cons

I hope you all enjoy the catalogue and feel free to dm me if you want more specifics about any of these builds.

League start: Oracle (Raise zombies) - I’ve done a zombie league start in some form or another basically every league now. They just keep buffing this skill and they really gave it some love this patch. Normally I would play lich and I would recommend lich over oracle but I promised myself I’d league start druid even if I had to shoe horn zombies into it.

The game plan is simple. Run sacrifice with 4 - 6 skeletal clerics and use profane ritual to generate power charges. Use the power charges to summon buffed up zombies.

Pros - Great league start and all rounder build. No must have items and as a zombie build you don’t rely/scale with spirit so all you want is +minion levels. Safe playstyle and crazy single target makes for a great early bosser.

Cons - While power charges let you summon zombies inside packs its still just a generally slow play style. If you want a league start that is focused on getting ahead of the pack this build falls off at maps due to the generally slow play. Lot of buffs/upkeep make for a very active/busy minion build which may put some off.

This character was converted to another build but captured a PoB before I took it apart. https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/18891

Stormweaver (Infusion/storm limit stacking) - Didn’t quite finish this one out. I played something similar in 0.3 and coming into 0.4 i was excited for the storm limit stuff but in the end it felt too much of the same and it didn’t gain much but heres what i had so far.

Use snap and orbs to generate infusions and use infusions to do ??? whatever you like the most really.

I don’t remember much as this was my 2nd character but generally the build does just fine and if you haven’t had a chance to play elemental caster with infusions I recommend trying it out as its actually alot of fun but very hectic/active

Amazon (Talisman/Wyvern) - I love making amazons so I had to try amazon out with shapeshifting. Wyvern felt the best but power wise but its a bit wonky and certainly not my favorite style of amazon that I’ve played.

Just get a talisman with big flat Acc and %phys then go to town with wyvern skills (wolf would be fine as well.)

Pros - Classic amazon of massive damage with very little investment due to Acc dmg node. Wyvern skills are really cool/flashy and have some crazy scaling. elemental leech node fits well with shapeshifting.

Cons - Man shapeshifting is just a little wonky to me. Sure there are some really cool powerful skills but in practice they feel bad and pretty clunky especially after you get some hours in and the skills stop being that cool and are just plain annoying.

Still a reliable build but definitely next in line for the recycle bin

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/ryudude-1408/character/ryududeo

Tactician (Ancestral Warrior Totems) - This one is pretty cool but ultimately I hit a point in the build where I realized how much tinkering I would need to do to get it in a really nice spot and I didn’t want to put in that effort when the end result would be a totem centered build that in my experience has similar issues/drawbacks as raise zombies which I had recently scrapped… The foundations are here but it still needs some work.

So after trying to work in the vaal cultivated redflare into zombies I realized that you could have the gain power charge each second but use resonance to gain endurance charges instead thus never hitting max charges or “consuming a power charge” With unlimited endurance charges we summon ancestral warrior totems that use earthshatter and we explode the spikes using warcries that again have no cooldown due to unlimited endurance.

As tactician we can play further into the totem playstyle grabbing banners to buff the totems and the all important watch how I do it passive to add marohi erqi massive flat damage to the totems damage.

Lot of moving parts but alot of cool interactions. I may revisit the build at some point and hammer out the details but for now this is all I have

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/ryudude-1408/character/ryududeu

Shaman (Adonia elemental caster) - I’ve been shouting from the rooftops about how good adonia is and after the buffs I knew it was a matter of time before everyone else caught on. This build leverages adonias down side to hit the negative resist cap of -200% in tandem with doryanis prototype to lower their lightning resist and with sacred flame in the off hand that in turn makes enemies in your presence have -200% to all elemental resists.

Pair that with adonia give an absurd more ele dmg modifier and you have crazy damage output. With shaman you get an added layer of defense to counter doryani in the form of adaptation making for a surprisingly smooth build thats runs around with -200% lightning resist.

As is my MO the build is currently in a serviceable spot but has plenty of room for improvement but I felt my work was complete and left it in this state.

Pros - DAMAGE. Elemental caster playstyle is just fun as well as the shaman skill apocalypse. Its just freaking cool man what more do you want?

Cons - Getting the resists just right is tough and still playing around with the correct armour values to feel tanky. Mana is an issue. A solvable issue but the build hasn’t implemented it at this point. Another multi button active playstyle.

Ritualist (Bloodbarrier tank) - This build has been around since 0.2 but I finally gave it a shot this league and I enjoy having it as an option when I want to work on a PoB or watch a video.

The build just goes into max difficulty simulacrum and stands in the center afk. 100% don’t even have to use the flask. Just start a wave and walk away and come back to your loot at your leisure.

Pros - Having something this hands off to make passive currency is actually surprisingly useful. (Like while I’m writing this…) Very low investment for near top end gear.

Cons - Not very useful outside of simulacrum

Yeah not much more to say simple straight forward and useful.

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/ryudude-1408/character/ryududen

Warbringer (Explosive spear/Spear of solaris) - This may be the biggest surprise of the league for me and also one of the most fun to play builds I’ve done this league.

Turns out the buffs to explosive spear in tandem with buffs to warbringer are actually really good. Explosive spears AoE is unreal and makes for buttery smooth mapping. Paired with warbringers easy armour break and resonance converting endurance to frenzy charges you can have explosive sustain its own frenzy charges for one killer build.

Ill be the first to admit… Solaris is rough. I’ve tried my best to make it work and the build feels perfect for it but man it still just feels really bad. when I go to make improvements its likely the first skill i’ll cut.

Pros - essentially 1 button gameplay with extremely good clear. Just a fun skill to use. Warbringer giving 75% block makes for easy solid defenses for no investment. Armour break ascendancy gives a massive damage multiplier to anything you want to add into the build.

Cons - Not getting charges out of bad luck can be frustrating. Bossing is an issue that solaris was supposed to solve but i havent been able to make work. I have ideas how to resolve the issue but it isnt in the current build.

https://poe.ninja/poe2/profile/ryudude-1408/character/ryududec

Gemmling (Hollow palm) - The latest build is pretty straight forward if you’re familiar with the build from last league. I never played with hollow palm last league but realized with the addition of +4 levels on vertex and +1 level from splendor that maybe there was something there.

Whatever quarterstaff playstyle you prefer will work.

Pros - Just a fun playstyle particularly with the buffs the palm/strike skills. so far fairly well rounded build.

Cons - Expensive items. Mana costs are a real legitimate problem I’m still working on. Nothing new added from previous league builds aside from more +level scaling.

This one is pretty new so still early stages but I think its still a fun build so far if you have the spare currency.
