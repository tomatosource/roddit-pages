Changes that need to be implemented

So I would first like to say that I am thoroughly enjoying POE2, albeit more content is needed for endgame.

There is just a couple of points I would like to make about some changes that really should be implemented to make the game have a smoother experience.

Interact animations - I know it might not seem like a fuss to some people but after going back to POE1 and seeing how much faster interacting with the environment is (there is no animations as far as I know) compared to POE2 where you have to watch the animations of pulling a leaver for what feels like forever. What’s even worse is going down the abyssal depths having to watch the animation of jumping in, landing on the floor and then getting up. If they made the change to be the same as POE1, I’m sure this will save a lot of people the tedious waiting around for roughly 15 seconds.

Falling over when sprinting - Now this isn’t as much of an issue as the first point but this can still be a bit of an annoyance at times. It doesn’t happen very often but when I do end up clipping a wall or getting hit by an enemy, why does it take 7 years for your character to then get back up? What’s even worse is when you end up falling over, I have no chance to survive and end up getting killed because of this. If they could remove the falling over part then this would be a perfect QOL change.

What’s everyone’s thoughts on this?
