I FIXED MY MASSIVE NETWORK STUTTER/LAG!In a way that made me feel stupid….

I kept intermitantly getting these long network hangs(10 seconds or more). The ones where, when the game catches up, you’re dead because mobs have been beating you the entire time.

I can’t recall how many times I have gotten elbow deep in both my computer and the game’s settings while pouring over forums, trying to fix it. Regedit, command prompts, config files, you name it.

It turns out that the game forces lockstep mode, and, while trying to force predictive mode in the settings, I learned that poe2 is extremely vulnerable to packet loss. My Internet is decent, so I never tried to troubleshoot it. On a whim, I wondered if it could be my Wi-Fi. I used an Ethernet cord, and boom. Buttery smooth. I wish there was a word for the happy/pissed off that I feel right now.

TLDR: Direct connect to your router with an Ethernet cable. Minute packet loss from your wifi makes the game trip and fall on its face. :-)
