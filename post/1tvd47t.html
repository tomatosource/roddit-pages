Why aren’t the Celtics as successful as they were in the 80s?

I’m asking older fans, what happened? I know we’re spoiled as Boston fans. The Celtics have 2 titles in the 21st century, I know that’s more than most of other teams, but that’s below our standards. Even before the Title Town era from 2001 to 2018, Boston has always fallen back on the Celtics to rep the city.

The Patriots in the 21st century are 6-4 in Super Bowls. Two of those super bowls are arguably the best super bowls ever played 49 and 51

The Red Sox didn’t reach a dynasty level, honestly I thought they had a chance for 2004, 2005 and 2007, but we settled for 2004 and 2007. Sox are 4-0 this century, we’ve dominated all of those wins.

The Celtics underachieved with the big three in 2008. We got one title, but we shouldn’t have lost 2010 and the group was aging in 2010. I still get mad about Ray Allen leaving to join LeBron, when LeBron started the super team trend. Anyone who says the 2008 Celtics were a super teams is a moron.

We lost to an older Warriors team in 2022. We did beat the Mavericks in 2024, but they were an overrated team. So the 2008 title is weighted more than 2024 one. I will gladly take beating Kobe than beating an aging Kyrie Irving and a fat Luka.

I hope the Celtics fan figure something out next season.
