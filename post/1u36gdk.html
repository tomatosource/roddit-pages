+6 skill rare amulet

You can make a +6 spell or melee skill rare amulet this league. I’m too poor at the moment to make the whole craft happen end to end, but I’m surprised to see none on the market. It uses a similar idea to fireflower, where corrupting makes +4 skills -&gt; +5 skills, and the quality brings it to +6 overall.

Here are the rough steps to get +6 spell skills for example:

  1. Get any rare amulet
  2. Use breach essence to get +20 max quality, then caster quality to 40%
  3. Use vaal catalyzing infusers to 50% without corrupting it. This has about 10% overall chance to succeed if it works the same as last league.
  4. Craft on it and get unfractured +3 spell skills.
  5. Finish other affixes (you can remove the breach essence affix, it’ll keep the quality)
  6. Sanctify and get it to +4 spell skills (which gets bumped to +6 with quality). This has about 13% chance to succeed. Don’t forget to annoint, perfect flux, etc. before sanctifying

You can use locks for step 3⁄6. You can also move step 4 before step 2 or 3.
