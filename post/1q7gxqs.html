Will we ever see another 300-strikeout season?

I was talking with my coworker about it, and the last 300 strikeout seasons were Cole and Verlander in 2019. You would need 13.5 K/9 to get it in 200 innings (only Strider has been qualified with 13.5 K/9 in the last 5 years), or 11.85 K/9 in 228 innings (the most in the last 5 years, 2022 Sandy Alcantara). 11.85 K/9 is certainly achievable but the guys that can get there aren’t pitching 228 innings. What do you all think? Will we ever see 300 K’s again?
