GGG, do we really need disenchant?

It feels like the choice, whether to disenchant an item or to sell it, is never real. You always want to disenchant a unique item, you might consider disenchanting rare/socketed/quality items early in the campaign, but it loses relevance very fast and you just sell them.
Why do we even need to make this choice? Why not combine the two, so that once you sell an item, you get gold and a shard that you would get from disenchanting. That would be a small change, hardly any impact on economy, especially now, when the normal regal orbs are dirt cheap, but it would significantly improve the handling of loot, both if you handle it after every map or use a dump tab.
