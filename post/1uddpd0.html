I couldn’t be more disgusted by the way parts of the fanbase treat Jaylen Brown

First of all, let’s start with the obvious.

Jaylen is not a perfect player. He definitely has flaws in his game. But the guy always gives everything he has.

He is one of our homegrown superstars, along with Tatum. He has been there since the start of his NBA carreer. He has seen the Isaiah Thomas era, the failed Kyrie-Gordon superteam, and he was there during that incredible 2018 ECF run with rookie Tatum. He helped bring us that beautiful title in 2024 and won FMVP.

This year, with Tatum out, everybody was saying the team would suck, that we should play for the draft and tank for A.J. or Peterson.

Yet, Jaylen showed up and DELIVERED big time.

He could have sat out the whole season, invented an injury, taken nights off like Giannis this year, but no. He was 1000% professional, played very, very well, was 6th in the MVP race, 2nb team all-NBA (borderline 1st team) and acted as a mentor to the young guys on the team.

During the playoffs, with Tatum still hurt and out for some games, with a healthy Embiid for three playoff games, something that literally happens once in a decade, and with NO real center, the Sixers still needed seven games to beat Boston.

If the Celtics had an average big and a healthy Tatum, the Sixers would have been swept easily.

And yet, some of you wanted to send our homegrown superstar, all our FRPs and Hugo for a diva, injury-prone, clearly physically declining player ( 4 calf injuries in less that two years) who doesn’t want to screen, can’t protect the rim that well, can’t shoot, and has questionable ethics (the guy invested in a fucking betting app).

Fuck Giannis. I’m so happy it was the Heat that got fleeced, not Boston.

Run it with our homegrown superstar duo. Let them retire as Celtics. Put their jerseys in the rafters.

This team is still close to a title. Find a real 5 and go again.
