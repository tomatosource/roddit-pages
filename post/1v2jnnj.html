For the first time in 5 years, Yordan Alvarez’s career batting average is over .300

He burst onto the scene in 2019 slashing .313/.412/.655 in 87 games, enough to win unanimous ROY.

He only played 2 games in 2020, and maintained a .300 AVG into July 2021 before a mid-month slump put him below. He ended 2021 with a .277 season and a .290 career AVG.

Yordan chipped away a bit over the next 4 years, with a sluggish 2025 leaving him at .29993 over that span and .297 career. His 2026 renaissance has seen him bat .324 over 100 games and 364 AB.

https://www.baseball-reference.com/players/a/alvaryo01.shtml

Is it sustainable? Well, his BABIP sits at .328, just 7 points above his career average and 30th among qualifiers this season. His Statcast xBA is .339, 100th percentile (xSLG: .723).

https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/savant-player/yordan-alvarez-670541?stats=statcast-r-hitting-mlb

Not only could he win the traditional Triple Crown this year, he could also win the slash-line Triple Crown. We are witnessing greatness.
