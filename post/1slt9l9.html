The NBA implementing the play-in tournament has improved the league greatly

Charlotte vs Miami was a fantastic game but I’ve felt excited for the play in every year. And Miami showed in 2023 that it is in fact possible for a play in team to make the finals. Obviously uncommon but we can’t act like they never mean anything.

With all the new ideas the NBA has brought to the league over the past 5-10 years, the play-in has to be at the top of the list and I look forward to continuing watching it.
