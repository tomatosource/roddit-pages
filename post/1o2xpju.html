Suggestion: auto- unlock waypoints of connected areas when finding the connection

As a casual, not sure if it’s just me, but when playing through the campaign when finding a connection to a new area, what typically ends up happening is that

  • I travel to the new area
  • I get the waypoint
  • I travel back to the previous area to further explore it

This feels like a chore with unnecessary load times and is imo also immersion breaking when you get a special audio dialogue when first entering the area whilst your focus is still on the previous are

if you don’t do this you risk having to explore the same area multiple times if the instance resets

Suggestion: Id be quite a nice QOL feature to just auto-unlock the waypoint. There’s a checkpoint before every connection point, so unlocking the checkpoint could also make you unlock the waypoint of the connected areas
