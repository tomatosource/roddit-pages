For the second straight year, the 1 and 2 seed have failed to make the ECF

Assuming Cleveland closes this out, this is now back-to-back years where the top two seeds in the East both failed to make the conference finals.

2025:
- 1 seed Cavaliers lost to the 4 seed Pacers
- 2 seed Celtics lost to the 3 seed Knicks
- ECF ended up being Knicks vs Pacers

2026:
- 2 seed Celtics lost to the 7 seed 76ers
- 1 seed Pistons, a 60-win team, are currently getting absolutely buried at home in Game 7 by the 4 seed Cavaliers

And for Detroit specifically, this is an absolutely sickening way to go out. A 60-win No. 1 seed losing Game 7 at home is already brutal. Doing it like this is the kind of performance people remember for years for all the wrong reasons.

Worst contender exit since the 2022 Suns? It’s at least in the conversation.
