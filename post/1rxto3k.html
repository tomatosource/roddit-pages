Tatum is basically Tatum

Just some early observations 6 games into Tatum’s return.

The eye test shows he’s still working out the kinks. You can kinda look at it like him being in preseason form. Some dribbles get away from him. He is missing some layups and his efficiency is down.

But that said, early signs even with the minute restriction seem pretty great.

His field goal percentage is trending up each game. 37.5%, 37.5%, 41.7%, 42.1%, 44.4% and 46.7%

He’s leading the team in +/-. He’s leading the team in defensive rating.

His stats across the games: 20.7 points, 8.5 rebounds, 4.0 assists, 1.2 steals, with 41.7%/30.5%/88.9% shooting in 29.4mpg

Lift that minute restriction and expand that to Per-36 (he generally averages 36mpg) and you get: 25.4 points, 10.4 rebounds, 5 assists, 1.5 steals with 22fga

That’s basically Tatum give or take (last couple seasons with 36mpg = 26.8 points, 8.3 rebounds, 5.5 assists, 1 steal with 20fga.

We’re also seeing a small sample size positive impact on his teammates.

Jaylen Brown in the two months prior to Tatum’s return had been shooting 44%/32% from the field. In the game’s played with Tatum, his FG% is up to 49.4%. His least efficient game recently was the 40% shooting game against OKC without Tatum.

Likewise, Derrick White shot 37.8% in the two months prior to Tatum’s return and so far he’s shot 44% since JT arrived.

And of course, we’re 5-1 with the lone loss being the Spurs game Pritchard sat and Brown got ejected (we were winning at the time of ejection).

Gotta love it.
