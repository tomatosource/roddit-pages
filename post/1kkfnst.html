Lightning Spear Amazon, how do you boss?

So I switched to LS because I, too, want to melt screens and use a meta build for once.

I thought I’d treat myself to some easy playstyle and follow a LS guide.

Oh

Boy

Where do I even begin.

Obviously, mobs and busy areas are easy, no one needs help with that, but it’s when you ask the: “So how do I kill bosses?” question that things get…complicated.

Every guide I read seemed to have a totally different approach, which would be fine, if they worked for me!

(And YES, this is a ME git gud issue, I realise, but with so many different approaches, which ones actually work reliably)

First I was told to ue Primal Strikes

Another said no no, Sniper’s Mark is the key

One told me to use barrage, then snipers, then lance, then strike…

“For single target you want to make sure you’re always using Barrage with Storm Lance on cooldown. If you have a spare frenzy from Sniper’s Mark Profusion, then throw another non-barraged storm lance. It should look something like this.”

“On bosses Conductivity -&gt; Orb of Storms -&gt; Sniper’s Mark -&gt; Storm Lance . Refresh Sniper’s Mark when it falls, otherwise spam Sniper’s Mark. Can weave in 1 Lightning Spear if it’s supported with Lightning Exposure otherwise don’t worry about it.”

“Cast one spear stabs in between every two Primal Strikes to maintain the DPS”

“Dance with Death which massively improves skill speed and lets you go full on offence with Lightning Spear”

I watch them attack a boss, and the hit points fly off, but when I do it, it doesn’t.

Can someone please help a poor levelling Amazon with 30 ex to kill bosses.
