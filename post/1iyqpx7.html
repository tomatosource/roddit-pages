The fundamental problem with the POE2 Atlas is you don’t design your own endgame anymore.

If we set aside specifics like towers and dead maps, and look at the problem with mapping at a higher level (why mapping just “feels bad”) it’s because we are missing the most interesting part of the POE1 endgame experience: Player agency in what the endgame actually looks like.

When I got to maps for the first time in POE1 and figured out how they worked, I realized that GGG had done something incredible:

The POE1 atlas tree system allows you to choose your own endgame, and match it to character build.

You can build a character designed to do legion, and spec hard into legion and other peripheral mechanics that work with your character build and currency strategy.

Now you are playing both a character and endgame you pieced together slowly over time that all works together… and it feels incredible.

Everything in POE2 is randomly at the mercy of Precursor tablets and mechanics randomly spawning on maps.

You get points for Ritual by doing ritual, Bosses from doing Bosses etc. There isn’t actually even any specialization or customization, just a checklist to check off boxes.

Love POE2, realize it’s EA, but endgame currently is fundamentally flawed, and missing the part that made it so exciting in POE1.
