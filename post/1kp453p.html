This Celtics team is one of only 20 teams ever to have back-to-back 60 win seasons

The 2023-24 & 2024-25 seasons the Celtics won 64 & 61 regular season games, respectively. That is one of only 20 instances a team won 60 games in back-to-back 60 seasons. Only 13 of those 20 teams won a championship during those seasons.

Bummer how the season ends, but a great run these last 2 seasons that few teams have matched.
