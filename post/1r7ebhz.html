The best way for MLB players to optimize on-field performance? Tell them to play like it’s their birthdays (Proof from Joc Pederson, Mike Trout, and more MLB players)

(Written by the great Sarah Langs)

What if there was a way to optimize player performance and it was as simple as: tell him to play every day like it’s his … birthday??

For some players, it just might work. The Birthday Index compares a player’s stats on his birthday to those on every other day of the season while also considering how often he has played on his special day. Sorry, February babies — this is for those born in late March through early November only, the regular season and postseason calendar.

Based on this index, here are some players who might be or have been even better if they could play every game on their birthdays. 

The top birthday index among active hitters belongs to Joc Pederson. In seven games on his April 21 birthday, Pederson has a 1.418 OPS. He’s basically 2004 Barry Bonds (1.422) on his birthday. Think about that. 

Let’s also consider Mike Trout. He has five career birthday home runs in his seven games on August 7. Over a 162-game season, that’s a casual 115-homer pace. Imagine. For someone already bound for the Hall of Fame with just one birthday per year. 

Birthday index data goes back to 1969. In that span, the leading pitcher is Max Scherzer. On his 41st this past July 27, he became the oldest pitcher with a 10-strikeout birthday performance in the last 125 years and just the second pitcher with multiple 10-strikeout birthday celebrations (Stan Williams). 

Let’s also look at Adam Wainwright, who’s second on the birthday index list. In four outings on his August 30 birthday, he allowed just a .134 batting average. Can you fathom that across 30 starts for a season? Or hundreds for a career? That’s like the incredible 2021 Jacob deGrom season that was cut short in July due to injury (.128 BA). 

Gerrit Cole has only made two birthday starts, but on Sept. 8, 2019 he set a record with 15 birthday strikeouts. He has 22 birthday strikeouts total in the two starts. He has a 45.8% strikeout rate. The only pitcher with a strikeout rate that high in a season with at least 80 innings was 2018 Josh Hader (46.7%). But imagine that over 30 starts and 200 innings. That’s 366 strikeouts in 800 batters faced. That number has been reached just four times since the mound was set at 60 ft 6 inches in 1893 — 2001 Randy Johnson, 1973-74 Nolan Ryan and 1965 Sandy Koufax. 

Some other fun birthday stats:

  • Freddie Freeman is the current birthday hit leader with 23, with 4 homers! And is batting .397 all-time on his b-day
  • Andrew Benintendi is the current 2nd-best player on his birthday after Joc Pederson, with 14 total birthday hits and a .412 average on his birthday
  • Jeff McNeil has an astounding 1.730 OPS in 4 total birthday games, +.958 his career OPS
  • Nick Martinez is the 2nd best b-day pitcher behind Cole and has made 3 appearances on his b-day.
