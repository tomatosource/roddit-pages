Spell Ascendancies having Anti-Syngergy with their Identity

Several of the spell-caster ascendancies (Stormweaver, Blood Mage, Infernalist) have weird anti-synergies with their identities. Before I get into the details I want to say I’m aware this is by no means a priority to address in the current state of the game, but I want to get it off my heart nontheless. Without further ado here are some examples:

  • Stormweaver: Probably the lesser offender, esp with the most recent patch making Stormweaver lean more into infusions, but at least previously Stormweaver leans towards Lightning and Cold spells as a part of their identity. However the Ascendancy Notables “Shaper of Storms” and “Shaper of Winter” which allows all damage to hits contribute to Shock/Chill chance respectably aren’t very useful if using Lightning/Cold spells because those can already apply Shock/Chill, pushing you towards other damage types instead to take advantage of this nodes. Lightning Stormweaver still gets away with plenty of other decent options for the Lightning fantasy however, as “Constant Gale” and “Force of Will” have obvious synergy with supplying mana for the Archmage Spirit Gem which adds Lightning damage, but I digresss.
  • Blood Mage: Probably the greatest offender, being depicted in its art as a caster using Osteomancy (aka the physical bone spells) has the Ascendancy Notables “Sunder the Flesh” and “Blood Barbs”. Sunder the Flesh causes all spells to have a base crit chance of 15% Unearth, Bone Cage and Bonestorm already have 15% base crit, making it worthless for those spells, to make matters worse “Gore Spike” is locked behind this node. Then there’s Blood Barbs which allows all Elemental Damage to contribute to Bleed Magnitudes, which once again doesn’t benefit the Osteomancy identity because those spells already do Physical damage and contribute to Bleed magnitude naturally.
  • Infernalist: Most commonly seen and used as the “minion” ascendancy, it still has a strong identity with fire. However this Ascendancy also has the typical “all damage contributes to Flammability and Ignite magnitude” which discourages the use of Fire spells.

I’m aware that GGG stated they don’t want they’re to be an obvious “best ascendancy” for XYZ. (This statement was made specifically regarding them not wanting a “best minion” Ascendancy and that being the reason why there’s no traditional “Necromancer”. However I believe it is still important for classes/ascendancies to have an identity that it’s possible to build around, while not necessarily being enforced. Personally I don’t want to see these ascendancy notables removed, as they allow for some really interested creative builds (e.g Plasma Blast Bleed Blood Mage) however I would like to see some nodes that lean more into the class fantasy, perhaps creating “choice nodes” like the flask node in the Pathfinder ascendancy where one choice leans into the identity while the other choice remains as is, allowing the class to do more creative things.

Apologies for the wall of text, anyone else have some thoughts on this or minor complaints to get off their heart related to class fantasies in this game?

TL;DR Stormweaver, Blood Mage, Infernalist having anti-synergy with Lightning/Cold, Physical and Fire spells respectively makes me sad ;_;
