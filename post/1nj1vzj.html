DoT Damage immediately reserves all of Acolyte’s Darkness

Yeah so I know not many people play Acolyte, much less run Embrace the Darkness (losing spirit is rough), but there’s a pretty ridiculous build-killing bug.

Any DoT damage seems to first hit your Energy Shield (this defies the functionality-as-described on the tooltip, Darkness should be reserved before Life or ES), and the moment ES is gone, the Darkness pool immediately drops to 0. This also works the same way if you have 0 ES to begin with, so it’s a more general bug with Darkness.

I’ve tested this with the Ogham Manor (level 15) blood pools, as a level 40 Acolyte. I had some delusions of making a build around Darkness but it’s absolutely not going to work with it being bugged like this.

I’ve made an official forum bug report about this, but just wanted to get more visibility here - and maybe save the time of other people who want to build around Darkness.
