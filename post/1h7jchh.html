NBA fines Rockets’ coach Udoka, center Aengun and forward Eason

The NBA has fined Houston Rockets head coach Ime Udoka, center Alperen Sengun, and forward Tari Eason following their actions during and after Tuesday’s 120-111 loss to Sacramento. Udoka was fined \(50,000 for confronting an official, using profanity, failing to leave the court after his ejection, and criticizing refereeing in a post-game press conference. Sengun received a \)15,000 fine for inappropriate language directed at an official, while Eason was fined $35,000 for throwing a towel and using inappropriate language at a spectator after the game. The Rockets, 15-7 on the season, sit 2nd in the Western Conference, just 1.5 games behind Oklahoma City.
