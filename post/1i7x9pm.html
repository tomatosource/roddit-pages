Jaden Springer

In a game where the Celtics played in the mud against a team without their stars, Jaden Springer emerged.

Joe’s been experimenting with Queta a ton this year so I was surprised that Springer wasn’t getting minutes. We need young energy on this team during the next couple months and Springer brought it tonight.

I know people have been clamoring for Brad to ship out Springer and bring in someone like Lonnie Walker, but this game might tip things in Springer’s favor…

Edit: As long as the dude can hit some open corner 3s, I think he’s definitely earned some minutes moving forward.
