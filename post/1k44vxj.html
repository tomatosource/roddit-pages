With a road Game 1 win tonight, the Warriors have won a road game in 29 of 30 playoff series dating back to Steph Curry’s playoff debut in 2013.

This includes a playoff record 28* straight playoff series with a win on the road. The lone series in this run that the Warriors did not win on the road was the 2023 2nd Round series against the Lakers, when they went 0-3 at the Lakers’ home court.

Also a fun fact: The Warriors since 2015 have won a road playoff game against 12 of the 15 western conference teams: the Lakers, Timberwolves, and Suns are the only exceptions.
