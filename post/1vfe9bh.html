(Quick guide) How I earn Divines by selling Waystones

Inspired by this post, I want to explain how I am making easy and guaranteed money by slamming and selling my waystones.

&nbsp;

First save up a lot of T15 Waystones, personally I run “increased quantity of waystones” on my tablets such that each map boss drops 3-4 waystones. Btw this also increases your tablet drop rate, which is free extra money.

Alch/Exalted orb each waystone to 6 modifiers (0 revives). Then corrupt them using a Vaal orb.

&nbsp;

How to decide if a Waystone might be valuable:

Mouseover each corrupted waystone, we are looking for two things:

  • Tier 16 waystones (mouseover not needed, just look at the icon).
  • Tier 15 waystones with 8 modifiers.

Hint: Do not count the number of modifiers; it’s way faster to check the Waystone Drop Chance. 120% or higher = 8 mods, 100% or lower = 6 mods. This trick works because each waystone mod adds 10-20% Waystone Drop Chance, and on average it will result in a noticeable gap between 8 and 6 mods.

&nbsp;

Throw away all the waystones that didn’t become T16 or 8-mod, a few of them are worth maybe 1 chaos orb but most will not even sell for 1 exalt. Rich people pay for the best, and 6-mod waystones do not have enough stat budget to be the best.

&nbsp;

The T16s and 8-mod T15s are the money makers.

  • T16s are worth half a divine, maybe more if mods are good.
  • T15 8-mods might not be worth anything, but some sell for 1-10 divines, or more.

&nbsp;

I could write a long and complicated essay about how to price-check each waystone, but ultimately the fastest way to sell them is to list em’ all for:
9 Divs -&gt; 3 Divs -&gt; 1 Div -&gt; 3 Chaos -&gt; 1 Chaos
(Ctrl+click many Waystones to sell them for the same price, then come back and reduce the price after a few hours).

Do smaller or bigger price jumps based on how much you value your time. Expect only 1 in 50 waystones to sell for 9 div, and only 1 in 10 waystones to sell for 3 div. Most will sell for 1 div. But you risk losing a lot of profit if you skip these steps and start the price at 1 divine.

&nbsp;

Generally, people pay big money for good Monster Effectiveness and/or extremely high-rolled Item Rarity / Monster Rarity / Pack Size / Waystone Drop Chance. Or some complex mix of the above. It’s basic supply and demand, with rich people paying for a juiced mapping experience (it’s not about profitability for them). Do not try to understand it, just sell and see if the market accepts your price.
