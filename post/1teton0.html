Modular GSearch query to find Unique (Boss) Beasts

Here’s a modular search query in google, filtering out the duplicate/translated pages for PoeDB’s languages (Cn, Jp, Ru, Kr, Pt, Th, Fr, De, Sp, Tw):

&nbsp;

site:"poe2db.tw" "categorybeasts" "Boss is alive" -"categoryhumanoid" -"categorydemon" -intitle:"wiki cn" -intitle:"wiki jp" -intitle:"wiki ru" -intitle:"wiki kr" -intitle:"wiki pt" -intitle:"wiki th" -intitle:"wiki fr" -intitle:"wiki de" -intitle:"wiki sp" -intitle:"wiki tw"

&nbsp;

The final search results displayed by GoogleSearch will also depend on how fast the new PoeDB pages are updated/indexed with the new data extracted from datamining/etc.

Relevant TAGS to tweak (don’t ignore quotes), add in the search query above:

&nbsp;

  • “categorybeasts” &lt;- relevant tag, this is the category we’re aiming for the spiritwalker ascendancy;

  • “i am boss of tier”&lt;- mostly a boss tag, will apply to unique monsters that have a boss variant;

  • “MonsterActBoss” &lt;- generic boss tag, can apply for unique monsters that spawn in campaign areas;

  • “Boss is dead” or “Boss is alive” or “Boss Rush Area” &lt;- status tag for boss only areas (mapping bosses, tied to map tier)

  • “MeleeAtAnimationSpeedUnique” &lt;- generic melee tag attributed to unique monsters, I’d say that 90% of said monsters have a default melee attack, except some burrower beasts;

  • “very_fast_movement” &lt;- highly impactful tag, better spectre/companion movement speed is a great QoL;

&nbsp;

Sadly, some filters such as “monstersize” can’t be targeted properly. For example, MightySilverFist boss (https://poe2db.tw/us/Mighty_Silverfist) monster size is 100%, but we all know that miniboss is huge, compared to the quadrilla beast group that have ‘spectrable’ corpses.
