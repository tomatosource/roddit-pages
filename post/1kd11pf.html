AURA: is this the qualification to becoming the face of the NBA?

Why isn’t Jayson Tatum considered the face of the NBA?

Just because he doesn’t have the “aura” that everyone keeps talking about doesn’t mean he shouldn’t be in that conversation. Anthony Edwards, who hasn’t even had multiple deep playoff runs, is suddenly being labeled the face of the NBA just because he has a personality or an “aura”?

When Ant copies Jordan’s celebration, people go crazy and call it iconic. But when Tatum honors Kobe, his idol, and talks about him, people call it corny or say he has no aura.

The hate towards Tatum is crazy. He’s a consistent top performer, leads one of the best teams in the league every year, and plays both ends of the floor. But just because he doesn’t fit a flashy, loud persona, he gets overlooked?

It’s wild how much narrative and personality bias affect how players are viewed, even when their basketball resume speaks for itself.

I just don’t get it.
