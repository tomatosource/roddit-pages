Pelicans: Kevon Looney out at least 2-3 weeks with knee sprain

NEW ORLEANS — The New Orleans Pelicans announced Wednesday that Kevon Looney will miss at least the next two to three weeks after suffering a knee injury during the team’s preseason game in Australia earlier this month. Team officials said Looney was diagnosed with a proximal tibiofibular ligament sprain in his left knee.

He will be re-evaluated in two to three weeks, according to the team.
