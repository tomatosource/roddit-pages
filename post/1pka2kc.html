Inevitable Critical expected damage increase

I was wondering how much expected more damage multiplier the new inevitable critical node gives based on 2 variables: crit chance and crit damage bonus. Here’s the result:

  • For most crit chances, the greater the crit damage bonus, the greater damage increase the node gives (so I limited my search to 5, aka 500% crit damage bonus).
  • For crit chance, the damage increase peaks at around 24%.

The greatest damage increase is 44.02% given by 24.40% crit chance and 500% crit bonus (although a larger crit bonus would give even more damage increase).

Formula for calculation:

Expected damage with node / expected damage without node

where:

Expected damage with node = sum over k = 0 to 4 of (probability of needing exactly k rerolls * (1 + effective crit damage bonus after k rerolls)), with the probability of k rerolls given by a geometric distribution truncated at 4 (all probability for k ≥ 5 added to k = 4) and effective crit damage bonus for each k calculated as max(0, crit damage bonus * (1 - 0.3 * k))

Expected damage without node = 1 + (crit chance * crit damage bonus)
