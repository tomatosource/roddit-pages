I took a liberty to disect Armour vs ES again

Here and there, I do a post about game mechanics. Recently I have seen that post where OP got killed by Jamanra.

I got some digging and find out Jamanra probably does 8k phys damage (actually 8k phys and 2,7k lightning), well if I’m reading the poe2db skill right:

https://preview.redd.it/2gwhzkqqwbcg1.png?width=418&format=png&auto=webp&s=972d8465b48bba731a39b99abab0f5da200d022b

So I was thinking, if small hits does as little as 350-500 damage and big slams do up to 10k damage (without map mods), lets do compare Armour to ES again, This community favorite topic! :)

But this time, I will try to do some useful tables and numbers, so you have a clear idea how much is your defense effective. I hope to cast some light on not just Armour/ES but also hybrid builds.

How much % of damage does Armour reduce

You can do some useful observations. Such as

  • 100k armour can reduce 90% damage from regular monsters, but only 50% damage from boss slams
  • 40k armour will still reduce 80% damage from regular monsters, but roughly 30% from boss slams
  • 30k armour, often reached by players, reduce 75% damage from monsters, still amazing, but only about 23% damage from boss slams
  • 10k armour is half damage reduction from monsters, yet 10% only for boss slams
  • anything below 10k armour is still quite useful against monsters, but hardly noticable against boss slams

This is a classic red vs blue.

I picked two classes which seems roughly equal and good representants of each color and I have tried to invest 65 nodes into defensives as well as I could. I didn’t get any regeneration or ES recharge. For the gear I went with full rare equip with mid of T2 mods. So the gear should be easily accessible on SSF. The boots with 35ms. For titan shield (and giants blood), for lich focus. No amulet nor jewels. Titan has extra 50% from small nodes passives. But the lich takes 15% less damage naturaly. https://pobb.in/WOsXXkW_odXi
