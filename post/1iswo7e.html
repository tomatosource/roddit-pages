The current Kings are extremely similar to the Bulls last season.

Point guard: Monk - White

Undersized point guard who’s a pretty good scorer and shooter although sometimes inconsistent, relatively bad defender

Shooting guard and small forward: Lavine and DeRozan

The exact same players, Zach is just healthy this year.

Power forward: Murray and Patrick Williams

Two somewhat underwhelming high picks who specialize on defense and have glaring holes in their offensive game, basically the last option on the floor to score

Center: Sabonis and Vucevic

Sabonis is a clearly better player and playmaker but both are consistent offensively and weak rim protectors which limits the defense of both teams

6th man: Alex Caruso and Keon Ellis

Two elite guard defenders that can hit a three or 2, not really an offensive threat but a pretty reliable role player

Backup center: Drummond and JV

Two guys who can amass numbers in a short period of a time but are past their prime and net negatives on the floor at this point in their careers

Backup forwards: Torrey Craig and Jake LaRavia

Whatever guys

The only guy I’m missing here is Ayo, but the Kings just signed Markelle Fultz, and although their games aren’t that similar, it is remarkable how similar these two teams are.

Of course they have the same guys starting at the 2 and 3 but the rest of the teams have a ton of similarities too
