Did Frozen Mandibles get a mid-season nerf?

I know GGG re-enabled it on 0.3.0b Hotfix #2 and the tooltips are still bugged (and if you visit a town, their spirit cost reduces to the value of a normal Nettle Ant, strange bug), but even so, they just feel significantly worse than before.

Below are some strange things I noticed after patch:

  1. Frozen Mandibles just stop working (not moving, not responding to minion move command; just totally broken) when I enter maps or change zones.

  2. Frozen Mandible’s hitbox (or collision box) became significantly bigger? Or so it seems. They get stuck on corridors and seem to collide with each other and other skeletal minions much more. This is a HUGE nerf if true because they are melee minions. They won’t be able to reach bosses and mobs like before and thus lose a significant portion of their DPS.

  3. Frozen Mandible became less aggressive? This could be due to losing the 25% movement speed buff from Fleshpierce, but I did play without Fleshpierce for few hours before the patch (to pre-adjust spirit costs) and it was still very smooth. It does feel like Frozen Mandibles lost some aggro range.

Is it just me? Or do they actually feel off?
