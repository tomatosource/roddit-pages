What are some (potential) MVP seasons that were robbed by players on steroids

My list:

- 1988 Kirby Puckett (came in 3rd but he was better than Mike Greenwell as well, led MLB in hits and total bases while slashing .356/.375/.545/.920 and winning a gold glove)

- 1996 Ellis Burks (played in Coors so if you want to you can use that to say he still shouldn’t have gotten it — but he slashed .291/.367/.535/.903 on the road with a 139 OPS+ and 17 HR, 49 RBI, and 16 SB in 75 games, so I’ll “give it to him”)

- 2001 Luis Gonzalez (He may have *also* done roids although I can’t find any concrete proof; My “backup” pick would be Lance Berkman)

- 2002 Vlad Sr (Led NL in hits and total bases and finished 1 SB shy of 40⁄40 while slashing .336/.417/.593⁄1.010 with a 160 OPS+)

- 2003 Albert Pujols (led NL in hits and MLB in runs and total bases while slashing .359/.439/.667⁄1.106 with a 187 OPS+) *and* Carlos Delgado (led MLB in total bases and RBI’s while leading AL in OPS and OPS+)

- 2004 Albert Pujols (led MLB in runs and total bases with a 173 OPS+)

- 2007 Magglio Ordonez - MLB allowed Arod to use testorene this season due to a “deficiency” (Led MLB with a .363 BA as well as 54 doubles which also led the MLB, finished with a 166 OPS+ and 7.3 bWAR)

- 2011 Matt Kemp (Led NL in bWAR, runs, HR, and OPS+ while leading MLB in total bases and RBI, also won a gold glove)
