“The Limb Mechanic is just a cool thing I wanted for One League”

Just wanted to put this out there (two talking points) - this mechanic should not be “one league” this is one of the most interesting mechanics I’ve ever seen put into an ARPG (outside of it existing in The Ascent but that’s technically not an ARPG).

The concept of using your character limbs as additional resources to build modification is an incredible idea. I’d say go further and make it even cooler than just base item affixes and make them actually alter how skills work. Just +Movespeed on both Legs and +ES on both Arms will be boring.

But something where like “You gain rocket boots, and can shoot forward at rapid speed for X seconds” or “Your first can slam the ground and cause Aftershocks” etc.

Also, and because I just want to put my two cents in on the “lose your limbs on death in SC” topic because I have an opinion here for SC specifically.

SC by default is meant to be the area where new players can “learn” the game and while there can obviously be punishment for dying, I think when that punishment can (potentially) drastically hurt your player power for the “time invested” in the character that is a big issue.

Let me paint you a picture as an example:

SC player has “a mid-decent build” but struggles with learning pinnacle bosses and spends 20-50hrs in the new mechanic Vaal Temple to get all 4 limbs juiced up to help their mid-decent build have the best chance to do damage/survive. They go into their first Arbiter fight don’t know mechanics and get 1-shot by one of his many 1-shots.

Now that players 20-50hr investment into the Vaal Temple getting those limbs immediately evaporates and his player power (damage and survivability) drop down considerably. Now his chances of beating Arbiter drop down proportionally and that player doesn’t want to go back into the Temple 4x times to get new limbs / fish the right limbs again for their build just to try Arbiter again. So instead, they quit.

Because for me, I imagine losing all 4 limbs at once after obtaining 4 limbs would be a quit moment for a LOT of SC players. It’s just a drastic loss in time investment which is the reason a ton of people don’t play HC to begin with. And some people will say “limbs are a nice to have don’t rely on them.” but people want to do the “cool new things” not that’s kind of the point of seasons. I just think it’s an awful idea to penalize player power that harshly in SC.

TLDR:&gt; Limb Mechanic should absolutely remain core and not just be a 1 season mechanic, but it should be way more impactful than just Affixes. And, SC should not be penalized with the Limb Mechanic losses as set currently. It’s too damning for SC to lose that time investment. To large of a quit moment.
