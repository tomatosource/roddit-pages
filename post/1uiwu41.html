[Men’s Health] Ben Simmons: “Maybe I’ll go back to Philly. Miami would be nice. And not because it’s Miami—I like Erik Spoelstra, I like the Heat, I like their organization, I like the culture.”

Source: https://www.menshealth.com/fitness/a71654161/ben-simmons-interview/
