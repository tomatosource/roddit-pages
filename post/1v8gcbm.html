Farmed for a mirror of divs in about 2 weeks.

About 2 weeks ago I bought a bunch of upgrades and had about 100div to my name. Today I have just enough to buy a mirror (about 5k divs). I didn’t follow some streamers “Easy divs/hour” video. I just played what I enjoyed and did my own thing.

I did some ritual, some breach, some tablet farming, and some cleansed maps and then later some Expedition. I rarely bothered with 200% delirium because spawning mirrors annoys me and again, it’s all about speed and spawning the mirrors cut into my other farms. If I got a mirror organically I’d spawn it, otherwise I didn’t bother. Abyss, Delirium, Ritual, and Breach spawn often during the tablet farm and I got a bunch of omen of light, abyss currency, and ritual omens from that without even using those tablets. I haven’t touched Temple at all. I don’t do any target farming but ran Jade Isle and citadels as they showed up using max waystones to get more fragments. It all just adds up.

The last few days I did some Expedition and since Aldur’s wasn’t giving me the return I wanted I quit using it. I think I have about 6 in my stash. The most expensive tablets I used were for ritual and came from my own farming/crafting. The rest of the tablets I used were 1-2div max. I got a few lucky drops of exceptional crafting bases (I always run T16 irradiated maps), 2 magebloods (1 from ritual and 1 drop in a GE but the price had already dropped from 600div to what it is now). I got a HH too but haven’t sold it. I did find a lock in one of the maps between GEs and got 1105div from it today. The only thing I crafted with intention to get specific stats in all of this was tablets. I often pick up gear with good stats, hit it with a perfect regal if its magic, then blindly slam some greater exalts. Then I corrupt it and pretty often I’d get a double socket which sometimes made a 5div item into a 30-50div one. If it didn’t turn out great I’d just vendor it. I play about 3-6 hours in the evening where I actually run maps/GEs then I craft up and sell the tablets on my work laptop between tasks.

What I think a lot of people do is see streamers and people talk about the latest “best way to earn fast divs” and they just run with it, buying up expensive tablets and such, and becoming frustrated when they don’t get the divs/hour others claim to. They think they need to follow the streamer strategy and use things like Aldur’s to make fast currency. I just take the cheapest and most fun method I can find and run as fast as I can until I get bored, then I switch. Breach farming was and still is great for reliable div earnings. Absent amulets are easy money. Catalysts always sell. Ritual is also great once you can either farm/craft the 25 div 3x reroll tablets or can spare divs to buy them. I made 30-50div per nameless run from the omens. Overall though I think tablet farming is just one of the best and easiest ways to make divs right now. I’ve sold maybe a dozen for 30-50div. Anyone can do it with any build that can clear T15 content. The only currency you need are dirt cheap. Using irradiated/overseer tablets that cost 50ex you get a ton of free abyss, delirium, ritual, and breech without any real investment. You just gotta learn how to craft the tablets so they sell and learn what mods are desired.

Oh yeah, I have 35% rarity on my items but I do use a quiver that gives 600% rarity now and then. I just never bothered with boosting my rarity. Maybe after I get my bow. I don’t mean for this post to come off as a brag. I know 5k divs in two weeks and playing as much as I do is not a lot to some people but it was my league goal to own a mirror and use it it to get a mirror quality bow. All I’m really saying is that following streamers and other people’s methods tends to be risky because nearly all of them are heavily dependent on rng and investing in tablets/waystones/etc to boost rng in your favor and since everyone is picking up on it those tablets skyrocket in price and for the average player (such as myself) the rng often doesn’t yield the same returns and that cost cuts into your gains and is discouraging.

I didn’t buy the pillaging ring until just before I started the push from 100 to 5k divs and right now it says I have 989 divs looted, some fracturing orbs, annuls, and the lock so 3k from learning to play the market, which a lot of people say is dead. I’ve never crafted expensive gear and just stuck to items I knew would probably stay in demand.
