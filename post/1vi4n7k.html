Random thoughts from a new player

I’m playing completely blind (no guide but I do look things up occasionally…like that one djinn boss who poisons the room lol). I’ve reached about level 45 as a plant/lightning druid guy and just wanted to share my thoughts on the game so far.

IT’S. AMAZING.

The boss fights are some of the coolest I’ve ever seen in a game. The boss fight with the molten gold guy was awesome! Now I’m in the Kopec temple. The whole mechanic of avoiding the sun is so cool!

I am constantly surprised and amazed at how much quality this game oozes. The art, the animations, the gameplay. I’m obsessed with the support gem system. I can’t believe how flexible it is.

The difficulty has been perfect so far. It’s not anywhere near as hard or “overwhelming” as people make it out to be. I’m sure at endgame it can get super complex if you want it to, but the campaign has been really smooth for me.

Anyway, just wanted to share how much fun I’ve found this game to be!
