Inspiring Quotes by Katsuya Nomura (The only professional baseball player to have won the Triple Crown as a catcher in Japan)

 

•    ‘There are mysterious victories, but no mysterious defeats.’

(There is always a reason for the outcome. Defeats, in particular, have a clear cause.)

•    ‘Leaving behind wealth is the lowest; leaving behind work is the middle; leaving behind people is the highest.’

(What one leaves behind determines how one is judged.)

•    “When the mind changes, attitude changes. When attitude changes, behavior changes. When behavior changes, habits change. When habits change, character changes. When character changes, destiny changes. When destiny changes, life changes.”

(A saying regarding the transformation of one’s own behaviour and life)

•    “Fixed ideas are sins; preconceptions are evils.”

(The importance of keeping one’s thinking flexible)

•    “‘Thinking’ is the greatest weapon of the untalented.”

(A method for the weak to defeat the strong)

•    “To leave behind wealth is third-rate; to leave behind work is second-rate; but to leave behind people is first-rate.”

(This aligns better with the “First-rate/Third-rate” terminology he often used)

•    “The third-rate are ignored, the second-rate are praised, and the first-rate are criticized.”

(Only when criticized does one become a true professional)

•    “Effort is important. However, it does not always guarantee great results. The most important thing is the direction of that effort.” (This highlights his focus on “correct” thinking over blind hard work.)

•    “The moment one overestimates oneself, one’s thinking begins to become inflexible.

(A warning against arrogance)

•    “If you seek ease, only suffering awaits you.”

(The consequences of neglecting effort)
