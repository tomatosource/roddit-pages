Omen Potential

 

 I think Omens are really cool. Really hope they introduce a bunch. Here are some i made for fun that would be really cool to use when crafting. 

 What are your thoughts on them and if you had to create your own omen, what would it be?

Omen of Carnage: While this item is active in your inventory your next Divine orb changes the tier of one random modifier. Requires 6 mods.

Omen of Arcane: While this item is active in your inventory your next chaos orb will remove a non-caster mod, then add a caster mod. Has a chance to turn the item into a unique.

Omen of Resilience: While this item is active in your inventory your next exalted orb guarantees a resistance modifier. Requires 4 mods.

Omen of Misfortune: While this item is active in your inventory your next Chaos orb rerolls all mods on a rare, guaranteed to have one random modifier of one it has.

Omen of Truth: While this item is active in your inventory your next Alchemy orb guarantees one mod to be the highest tier.

Omen of Sacrifice: While this item is active in your inventory your next Annulment turns the item to normal rarity. Must be rare.

Omen of Betrayal: While this item is active in your inventory your next Annulment orb removes one prefix and one suffix. Has a chance to remove all prefixes or all suffixes.

This was fun to do, looking forward to what kind of omens you guys make.
