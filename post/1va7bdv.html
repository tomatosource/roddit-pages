Jewel crafting is great for beginner crafters!

https://preview.redd.it/1jcfsw0848gh1.png?width=1002&format=png&auto=webp&s=135af97a4ea684e94eb38195db558fa50bd65726

Process:

  • Start with Jewel with desired ES prefixes.
  • Use Potent Liquid Contempt, looking for +1 prefix limit.
  • Use Preserved Cranium / Omen of Abyssal Echo / Omen of Light / Orb of Annulment
  • Hit ES / Spell Damage Hybrid (In this case, nine rounds of desecrating).
  • Use Omen of Dextral Annulment / Orb of Annulment
  • If +1 prefix limit not removed, use Dextral / Annulment again.
  • Exalt once (or twice) to fill both suffix slots.
  • Chaos spam until you have 2 desirable suffixes (prefixes cannot be re-rolled because we removed +1 prefix limit).
  • Use Potent Liquid Ferocity granting % effect of prefixes.
  • Divine to desired stats.
  • Use twenty defensive modifier catalysts.
  • Vaal Orb?
