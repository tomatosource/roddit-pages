All we need for ailment builds is a scaling stat and more skills

First, what if we had a stat on skills that controls what percent of the hit is used to scale the different ailments, much in the same way that we already have weapon damage scaling? This probably only needs to be shown when you hold down alt for most skills.

I agree with Jonathan that it probably makes sense to apply some ailments at least with a magnitude that is based upon the hit, and it also makes sense to me that some skills should be better at applying certain ailments than others. So a scaling stat is the obvious answer, right? It doesn’t make a whole lot of sense for hammers to apply poison or bleed as easily as a spear, except for some of the skills that explode rocks maybe, and to make up for that we could simply get more weapon scaling for such skills.

Second, it seems to me that to make ailment-based builds more suitable for the style of PoE 2, we need more abilities that combine with ailments in interesting ways. (Potentially this is already on the way to some degree with 0.2.0?) Consuming ailments is one thing, but I’d also like to see abilities that boost ailments instead of consuming them, amplifying the magnitude further or potentially adding an extra debuff for the duration of the ailment, similar to corrosion, but more as a separate ability. Also with bow skills we already have skills that interact with poison in ways that many ailment enthusiasts would enjoy, and I think applying similar concepts to other ailments but with different flavour could work.

Of course, I don’t expect any such ideas to be ready for 0.2.0, but perhaps this is something for future updates.

Hmm… come to think of it, it’d be interesting to have a combo that goes like… you apply a poison based on the magnitude of an enemy’s current bleed, and then you can apply a bleed based on the magnitude of an enemy’s current poison, and so once you’ve applied the initial ailment you can go back and forth between the two.
