Rui Hachimura might be the most consistent wide-open 3-point shooter currently in the NBA

There are plenty of players who excel at hitting wide-open threes in the regular season. But most of them tank their efficiency in the playoffs. Luke Kennard went 0 for 5 on wide-open 3s, Ty Jerome shot just 29%, Taurean Prince also went 0%, and Donovan Mitchell hit only 33.3% on 24 attempts, a 15% drop from his regular season percentage. The list on and on.

Then there’s Rui Hachimura. He’s quietly developing into one of the most reliable shotmakers in the league. His wide-open 3P% has improved a ton over the years. What truly sets him apart tho is his playoff performance. Not only does he maintain his high wide-open 3P% in the postseason, he actually elevates it, year after year. An absolutely rare and valuable trait that makes him a major anomaly.
