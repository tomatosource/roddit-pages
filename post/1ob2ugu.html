Our Vegas odds changed slightly

Just something I noticed. The odds didn’t shift much at all after Tatum’s videos and rumors surfaced of him potentially returning this season. In fact, immediately following the hype videos our odds had actually gotten slightly worse and fell below the Spurs. Almost like Vegas looked at those training videos and took them as seriously as watching Mike Tyson’s social clips gearing up for his Jake Paul fight. Earlier this month a \(100 bet on Boston winning the finals would have paid out \)6700. A \(100 bet on them getting out of the East would have paid out \)2300.

However, as of today that’s shifted slightly. A \(100 bet on a Boston title now pays \)6100. Now Spurs have fallen below them again. So maybe there’s ever so slightly more optimism after the preseason?

\(100 on them getting out of the East still pays out \)2300. We still have the 8th best odds in the East. Their over/under set at 41.5 wins.

I still think that making the playoffs with a .500 record, getting swept in the 1st round, and getting stuck with the a mid 1st pick is the absolute worst-case scenario. This team either needs to make moves in-season to fall well below 41.5 wins and end up with a much higher/valuable draft pick (by far their best path towards retooling), or Tatum miraculously returns at 100% and they make moves in-season to push them into actually contending. I think staying in that current expected also-ran path is a total failure of a season.
