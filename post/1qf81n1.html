Hope temple doesn’t go core

…or if it does, I hope it will be a completely different/revamped league mechanic.

Even though I’ve built the temple “the right way” with some of my characters, even though I can earn multiple random divines per run because of the stacking bonuses, every time a temple portal opens I sigh.

It interrupts gameplay in an annoying way Abyss doesn’t and there are no “multiple ways to build the temple” since at its current state room bonuses and medallions will always guarantee a single optimal way. It’s boring because besides random drops rooms and rewards are too predictable. It can easily get out of line from the rest of the content. The effect of killing its bosses is counter-intuitive to designing it since the one thing that shouldn’t be completely RNG, is (decay).

In general, it is an attempt to a roguelike “build your own dungeon” and while this concept is amazing and worked in various other games I’ve played, its current execution shoots itself in the foot and doesn’t apply the basic principles that make this concept work in other games.
