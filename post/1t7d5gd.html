Spirit Walker - List of Beast Bosses

With the Spirit Walker able to tame Beast-Type Bosses, I keep trying to find a list of tamable options. Unfortunatley I can’t find any page for this, so I’m going to try and be the change I want to see. Bellow is a list of the options I found, though PLEASE comment any I missed and I’ll add them to the list.

Spirit Walker Ascendancy - The Natural Order lets you tame Beast-Type Bosses!

  • Act 1
    • Devourer (poison super-maggot)
    • Brambleghast (chaos plant monster)
    • The Rotten Druid (fungus guy. that might change.)
    • Crowbell (the crow… with a bell…)
    • Count Geonor - Wolf/hybrid form (yup.)
  • Act 2
    • Rathbreaker (Hyena Centaur)
    • Kabala, Constrictor Queen (snake that gives you the weapon skill points)
    • Ashar, the Sand Mother (trial version of Akthi)
  • Act 3
    • Silverfist (harambe the hardcore run ender)
    • Noxious Behemout (poison flower boss)
    • Xyclucian, the Chimera (first chimera you fight)
    • Bahlak, The Sky Seer (trial version of Rakkar)
    • Chetza, the Feathered Plague (trial version of Scourge of the Skies)
    • Uxmal, the Beastlord (trial version of Xycucian)
  • Act 4
    • Blind Beast (thing in the goblin areana)
    • Scourge of the Skies (blood bird)
    • The Great White One (landshark)
    • Yama the White (that crazy spirit monkey)
  • Interludes
    • Akthi, the Final Sting (Giant Scorpion)
    • Anundur, the Sandworm (super version of Devourer)
    • Vornas, the Fell Flame (fire dude at the gate)
    • Rakkar, the Frozen Talon (Ice owl/bear thing)
    • Abominable Yeti (Harambe mk2)
    • Frozen Mandibles (the big bug with a really big mouth)
  • Map Bosses
    • Gorian, The Moving Earth (super-deduper version of the Devourer)
    • Gressor-Kul, the Apex (map version of Xycucian)
    • Karash, Dune Dweller (map version of Akthi)
    • Morvak, the Infernal (Map version of Vornas)
    • The Black Crow (Crowbell… with black feathers…)
    • Ravenous Fang (map version of Blind Beast)
    • Sandstrider (map version of The Great White One)
    • Thraeven, Wing of Winter (map version of Rakkar)
    • Rain Festival Beetle Strongbox Boss (probably just finding it on maps)

Gotta Catch ‘em All!!!!
