Checkpoints were a temporary fix in 0.1 and now they’re going core ? wtf

Title,

Checkpoints were never meant to be a permanent solution to a feedback given since day one about the size of the maps and the respawn of monsters. It felt okay at the time because huge update mid league is hard and it made some stuff more manageable but it SHOULD NOT be a permanent solution.

It makes no sense that monsters respawn, yes they do in Souls game but this isn’t one, POE 2 is a bit better for it but in the tug of war GGG is playing between dark souls and path of exile, they’re leaning too much toward dark souls for my taste (and my job IS making video about souls like games since 2010).

I get that the staff is probably under a lot of pressure, tired and burned out from the early access release but the “what we’re working on” is piling on the bandaid we saw in 0.1 and that’s not a good look for future updates.

GGG should review the scope of the game before it’s too late, also why not add a PTR system where they can experiment and “fail quick” instead of this confusion between early access and us being lab rats. I’m okay with their trial and error, I’m looking forward to them cracking the code on how to mix poe and dark souls, heck I’m even rooting for them to succeed.

But mistaking bandaid fix for permanent fix feels like denial, it’s okay to have real issue, GGG it’s okay to admit you’re shooting in the dark and you need more time.
