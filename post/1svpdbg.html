Shai’s playmaking is incredibly underrated

When people compare Shai’s offensive game to prime LeBron, the common refrain is ‘Ok he may be a comparable/better scorer, but the playmaking gap is huge’

I disagree.

It’s true that Shai can’t make the passes that prime LeBron could, but being more conservative also has serious benefits. Shai’s turnover economy is absolutely insane. It’s straight up one of the most impactful parts of his game.

The guy has a 3:1 assist/TO ratio and basically never gets stripped.

It’s not sexy, but being incredibly solid with the ball should be viewed as part of playmaking. And Shai is maybe the 2nd best ever at it, behind prime CP3
