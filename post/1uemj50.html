Vladimir Guerrero, Jr. has 0 HRs at home this year, which follows a trend in his career

Much has been made of Vladdy’s performance in the first year of his mega contract and how he’s not living up to it. In addition to his overall power outage, he has 0 HRs at Rogers Centre, despite 160 PAs at home. Compare this with recently acquired Luis Urías, who has 1 HR at Rogers Centre in only 5 (yes, 5) PAs.

Vladdy is putting up the worst offensive numbers of his career and as of this post is a below-average Major League hitter with an OPS+ of 99.

But what’s most interesting to me is that his career production has been noticeably worse at Rogers Centre than anywhere else. It’s almost like Rogers Centre has an anti-Coors Field like effect on him. Consider:

  • Vladdy’s career OPS is .852, but only .811 at Rogers Centre
  • His tOPS+ at Rogers is 91
  • His career ISO drops from .199 overall to .182 at Rogers

This is really fascinating to me, especially when you consider the Jays committed to 14 years of having him play most his games at a place where he underperforms relative to his career numbers.

It got me thinking, have any other mega/ long-term deals been signed where a player performs worse at his home ballpark? Judge’s career OPS is higher at Yankee Stadium and Trout’s is better at Angel Stadium than on the road. Beyond the larger question of why the Jays outbid themselves for Vladdy’s services, it’s really interesting that they seem to have done so despite the metrics saying he wouldn’t be playing in his best environment for the majority of the contract.
