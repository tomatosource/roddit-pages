I think this POE 2 update is not for me

Straight to the point: I’m leveling up, trying out the spear and the new wisp mechanic in the campaign. I’m almost at the end of Act 1.

The spear feels too static and globally weak right now. The auto melee attack and parry don’t feel worth the cost of being static for so long. The disengage is quite nice to use, but trying the combo piece with the whirlwind attack feels clunky and takes too long to charge up to be worth using. I ended up using my auto ranged attack for the first bosses, dealing much more damage overall and giving me enough mobility to survive.

The wisp mechanic is quite nice and engaging at first, but after doing it 4 times, it didn’t feel exciting anymore — very redundant and overly pushy when it comes to not losing its track. I died after the 3rd times because I was surrounded by monsters too fast, cornering me in front of a wall. This mechanic is just a “rush B” event, hit and run. I felt like I was chasing the last rare enemy while mapping in endgame to finish the map, but in a more colorful and messy event. It felt weird, not original enough for me— probably just not my cup of tea.

I feel this update is not for me at all, unfortunately. My first playthrough in EA was much easier to play, with the discovery of the game as a whole, but the reset in this update feels heavy and harder than I expected. Cruel and early endgame is what I’m trying to zoom through as fast as possible, but I just don’t have the energy overall to redo everything again until I get to the fun part.

Right now, I’m quite disappointed with everything overall. I feel the chore of playing through the campaign and anticipating endgame all over again, and I’m very, very discouraged. I just don’t feel that this update is worth my time to create a new character again and experience a new economy. I don’t even think it’s worth the time at all. I’ll pass on this league, and strangely, I don’t feel bad at all about missing a league in POE 2.

Hope you’re enjoying your league start though.
Good luck, exile.
