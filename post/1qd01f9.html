Gemling Legionnaire is so close to actually being good.

I think only one node needs a legit rework: Advanced Thaumaturgy.

The other just need to be shuffled around with some stat balancing.

  • Adaptive Capability should be free like Sanguimancy.
  • Swap +2 Level and 10% quality positions, and cut it to +1 level and increase it to 20 or 25% quality.
  • Integrated Efficiency should be “more/less” modifiers and should be damage, skill speed, less cost. Then just adjust the numbers.
  • A node needs to be added behind Enhanced Effectiveness that is directed at attribute stacking. Like % Damage leeched as Life if you have 500 strength, % Damage leeched as Mana if you have 500 intelligence, half of both if you have 500 dex.
  • Remove Thaumaturgical Infusion like holy fuck lets just require filling up unused slots with bullshit and need a stupid amount of perfect jewelers.
  • The attribute requirement small nodes are ass.

Gem Studded maybe has the same problem as Thaumaturgical Infusion in that it encourages stacking random skills up, but you will kind of already do that to an extent with Gem Enthusiast and that at least caps out at 30 supports.

Gem Studded is also just extremely good QoL, the crit immunity is nice for rolling maps, though if you didn’t have that, the less cost and movement bonus just feels awesome without it being a raw offensive/defensive power gain.

So even though currently the +2 Skill Level is the most raw power out of the tree, Gem Studded it probably the overall best balanced and thematically nice nodes.
