Rare Currency Suggestion: Scroll of Omniscience

One of the issues with late game farming is that whites and blues tend to be much higher value pick-ups than floor rares due to the current potency of crafting currency. I love that crafting is stronger, and I dont want nerfs to that style of gearing, so I propose an idea to make the floor rares better: the Scroll of Omniscience, a stronger, rare variant of the Scroll of Wisdom that lets you strip/reroll the lowest-value mods from an item during the ID. It could automatically target the lowest value mod, or maybe it lets you pick, or maybe it lets you reroll one mod, etc.

In general I think there needs to be something to make floor rares something you actually want to pick up though without ruining the value of good crafting bases, and making the act of ID’ing more impactful seems like the best angle to accomplish that
