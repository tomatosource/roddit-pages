Excitement inversion

I am in shock and that’s why I want to ask whether someone else had a similar feeling yet.

Coming from PoE1 there are certain items that got me instantly hyped and excited when I dropped them. For a moment I had this exact feeling today, dropping a unique Champion Cuirass. The image looked familiar and I thought “nice, maybe something I can use in a build on another character” until I identified it. Checking the stats I had one big question - what in Zana’s name is the intention behind those item “reworks”.

Path of Exile 1

Path of Exile 2

In my humble - maybe uneducated - opinion this unique was not reworked it was butchered. Could it decide for itself it would rather not have dropped at all. Maybe I miss the use case, but which build given the current state of the game would even consider using this? Armour is mediocre and -4 all maximum elemental resistance!? I just hope that GGG decided to just make some uniques a shadow of their former selfes until they come up with time to show them some love. Cause this is sad to watch :D
