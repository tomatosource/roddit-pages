So where are the Armor/Evasion node changes in Patch Notes? Did Witchhunter really needed a nerf?

In the q&a Mark said ( https://youtu.be/P2-BLJQCeWo?t=4385 ) armor/evasion hybrid nodes on tree are super weak and give random bonuses that dont exactly allign with what armor/evasion builds would want.

Now the patch is out and all i see is they are buffing base ar and base ev values of armor items by 33% earlygame, 15% lategame.

I assume because of this they preemptively nerfed witchhunter node from %35 less to 50% less armor/evasion.

So witchhunter goes from 65 armor/evasion to 50*1.15 = 57 armor/evasion. Other ar/ev users go from 100 to 115, and thats it? No tree changes? I think i read 100 lines of energy shield related changes and no love for armor/evasion hybrid?

Also did witchhunter really needed a nerf?

You spend 4 ascendancy points to get an energy shield that has 6 second recharge time that scales off-of your armor and evasion but also cuts it by half. Any amount of damage in that 6 second window interrupts the recharge and you start over from 6 seconds. Because of this most people go evasion so you dont get constantly interrupted. It was also basically useless until engame where you actually got meaningful amount of armor and evasion.

It also has bunch of problems with any conditional source of armor/evasion. For example evasion rating while moving sources (they are adding another one of these clusters) would lower your shield the moment you stopped, and would put it on 6 second cooldown to get back up to expected value.

Most people i see on poe.ninja has around 10k evasion (20k with hyrri bonus), which means 3k shield from sorcery ward. Is that really that strong? Did that really need a nerf to “balance the buff”?

All i want is to invest into defence and make a comfy and tanky character that might not go as fast or might not deal as much damage as the most meta zoom zoom characters, but still manages to do most of the content.

Edit: My initial assumption about interaction of Hyrri’s Ire and Sorcery Ward interaction was incorrect, as i didnt get the chest while i played this build in SSF, now that i understand the interaction i think it was fair enough to nerf it a bit. I still feel like armor/evasion hybrid style needs a lot of love on tree though.
