Also did witchhunter really needed a nerf?

You spend 4 ascendancy points to get an energy shield that has 6 second recharge time that scales off-of your armor and evasion but also cuts it by half. Any amount of damage in that 6 second window interrupts the recharge and you start over from 6 seconds. Because of this most people go evasion so you dont get constantly interrupted. It was also basically useless until engame where you actually got meaningful amount of armor and evasion.

It also has bunch of problems with any conditional source of armor/evasion. For example evasion rating while moving sources (they are adding another one of these clusters) would lower your shield the moment you stopped, and would put it on 6 second cooldown to get back up to expected value.

Most people i see on poe.ninja has around 10k evasion (20k with hyrri bonus), which means 3k shield from sorcery ward. Is that really that strong? Did that really need a nerf to “balance the buff”?
