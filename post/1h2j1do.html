What is it you love about basketball?

Over the last couple of years I’ve been trying to make a proper effort to get into basketball - it’s quickly become possibly my favourite sport. Guess I’m just interested to hear about the feelings other people associate with the game (specifically the sport - I’m a Pels fan so fully aware of the frustration that comes with a cursed team). Should also say, the community is easily the best of any sport I follow.
