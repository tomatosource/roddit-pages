What is it you love about basketball?

Over the last couple of years I’ve been trying to make a proper effort to get into basketball - it’s quickly become possibly my favourite sport. It probably sounds weird but the game feels like Christmas as a kid - it’s exciting and gives a warm, comforting feeling. It’s hard to explain why, given I’m from the UK, but watching a rerun of the 98 finals lately, I felt nostalgic for a time in a sport that wasn’t even on my radar (I was 5 at the time). Guess I’m just interested to hear about the feelings other people associate with the game (specifically the sport - I’m a Pels fan so fully aware of the frustration that comes with a cursed team). Should also say, the community is easily the best of any sport I follow.
