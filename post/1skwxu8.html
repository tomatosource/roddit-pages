Vaal Siphoner (The item that nobody talks about)

There really isn’t any information on this item anywhere so I thought I’d just share some of my findings… The description says [paraphrased] that after the kill threshold is met, it removes a random modifier and can increase other modifiers. THIS IS WRONG! It does not remove a modifier at all, but it simply rolls down a random modifier by ~20% and randomly increases a random number of other modifiers on the ring or amulet. I have not seen an instance where it can increase the “tier” of any modifier, however it can lower a tier of the unlucky modifier. What is interesting is that you can apply a vaal siphoner and complete the kill threshold on the ring or amulet multiple times. I do not know if there is a true cap on T1 modifiers as of yet, which is the most interesting thing about this item. For example lets use a breach ring with T1 flat lightning damage capped at 71 dmg. Add 40% quality you have 99 damage. Corrupt it for a perfect roll to increase that by 1.22 to give you 122 damage. Now a perfect roll with a siphon would be ANOTHER 1.22 to give the ring 148 flat lightning damage. Is it possible to siphon again and get another increase??? Or is 148 damage the ultimate cap???
