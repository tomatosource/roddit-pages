Can we please get a “Stash Access” button directly in the Map Device UI?

It is just a small addition, but I believe it would help a lot with the end-game loop.

In the end-game, there are so many different things to do, and even if you are only farming a single piece of content, you constantly find yourself switching between different tablet and waystone setups.

Right now, the process is pretty tedious: you have to open your stash, grab the right setup, close it, run over to the map device—or alternatively, carry multiple pre-prepared waystones and tablets in your already limited inventory space. It’s very easy to accidentally dump your expensive tablets back into the stash by mistake, or simply forget your pre-crafted waystones entirely.

I initially thought about having pre-set inventories for the map device, specifically for the tablet menu, but that might add unnecessary complexity for newer players. Instead, could we at least get an “Open Stash” button directly inside the map device interface?

https://preview.redd.it/ri01t4jgzmdh1.png?width=2559&format=png&auto=webp&s=819f361042234f94047ee5434f75180e209b5dc1

We already have a similar mechanic with the Genesis Tree interface allowing us to open our stash directly. Having this on the map device would make the end-game mapping flow so much smoother.
