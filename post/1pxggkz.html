Delete Rarity on Gear, Make Currency a Map Juicer

This economy is just brutal and two of the big reasons is the lack of use for a lot of orbs and rarity/group play just raining currency.

Delete Rarity on gear and transfer some of that into map mods.

On top of that make the 3 extra slots on your map device be able top consume currency to give a scarab like effect.

  • Exalted Orb could +1/3/5 player scaling for normal/greater/perfect.
  • Alchemy could give Rarity.
  • Chaos could give Rare Monster Effectiveness.
  • Transmute could give X/Y/Z Quantity.
  • Augments could give X/Y/Z Magic Monster Effectiveness.
  • Annulments could increase Exceptional Drops
  • Chance could increase unique chance drops.
  • Regal could transform X/Y/Z magic packs into rare packs.
  • Divine Orbs could increase monster Effectiveness.
  • Gemcutters could increase chances of higher level uncut.
