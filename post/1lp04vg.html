Fans that want us to shoot for a 7-8 seed…..why?

DISCLAIMER: I am not trying to demean any one. This is a legitimate question.

I don’t see how pushing for a 7-8 seems helps us win banner 19. If we are a bottom 8 team we have a 25% chance of getting a Top 4 pick. This is subject to change but as it stands there are 7 teams that are trying to tank. There’s probably 2-3 more that aren’t trying to tank but will be dog shit. If we can get into the bottom 6 it’s roughly 40% chance of getting a top 4 pick. After this year we will have Tatum, Brown, White, Pritchard, and Hauser(assuming we keep him) under contract in 27. We could extend Simons if he plays well. By my reckoning we’d have around 20 mil below the salary cap, and 70 mil below the second apron. By my estimation we need a good pick to either play for us or use as a trade chip. I don’t see a path back to contention without tanking.

Love to hear your thoughts on the what the 27’ roster should look like and how we get there.
