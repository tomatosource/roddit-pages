You can make a low life build with Chaos innoculence and berserk aura.

I have been messing around with a 1 life no ES all mana character and found a very interesting interaction. Berserk drains a % of your maximum life and the only way you counter it is with a % regeneration per second. A flask or life on kill will not do the trick. But having Vitality support actually counters it. It takes quite a long time before you actually die from it since and can safely be used with CI.

If you put Vitality on a weapon swap you regenerate the lost life and keep you steadily on low life treshold. If balanced properly you can even use the corrupted belt which puts you at low life at 90%.

I am going to further dive in how to utilize this and if anything comes up i will post some more info.
