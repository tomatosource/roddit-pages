Quarterstaff is just not very fun in the early game

Quarterstaff doesn’t actually become viable untill ice strike is unlocked which feels mandatary to start having fun. This is less of a damage issue and more of a clunky consistency issue.

Here are some thoughts on what GGG could do to massively improve the fun of the staff in the early game by buffing the other skills.

Falling Thunder - Cone length of melee attack increased by 30%.

This skill is aweful when you first pick it up. This buff at least makes makes it safer without power charges.

Killing Palm - Targeting prioritises low health enemies if possible. When culling an enemy, create a small shockwave that knocks back enemies in a small radius.

Adds increased defense and consistency.

New support gem - pre-emptive strike (1) - Deal 15% more damage against an enemy at full health. Cannot reduce an enemies health below 1hp.

Stun consistency has the brink support gem so Cull consistency can have this.

Glacial Cascade - Fissure length is no longer fixed. Maximum length is the same as before.

You can now more reliably attack close range targets such as a newly created frozen locus or close range enemies.

Frozen Locus - Creating a locus object knocks back small enemies preventing the locus from being destroyed immediately. Moving in a direction when using this skill forward vaults the player in that direction, similar to a dodge roll.

This massively prevents premature object explosions and allows for much greater defensive movement flexibility.

Staggering Palm - Targeting prioritises primed for stun enemies if possible.

Better consistency.

Vaulting Impact - You can now free aim the skill rather than aiming in the direction of the momentum.

Much more consistent for landing on enemies.
