Disciple of Varashta is an incredible ascendency for SSF

Hi there fellow ssf enjoyers!

I wasn’t super stoked for the new sorc ascendency initially and was more keen to try out the improved elemental infusions, but decided to level with the new ascendency and give it a try before switching over to Stormweaver. To say I was impressed would be quite an understatement :) (and so far, I didn’t switch to Stormweaver ….)

DoV is an incredible strong ascendency in ssf, due to a couple of reasons:

- we get lvl20 gems + 5links “for free” on our main damage skills. This is super strong imo, as finding a lvl20 uncut gem has been super, super rare for me (I got 1 in all leagues combined so far)

- Muster is an easy way to scale the Djins damage. Even with a base Rattling Sceptre you can get like 7 different perma minions, i.e. about 50% more damage. With more and more spirit this scales further.

- DoV is incredibly tanky! You get 40% increased ES, -1 sec on recharge delay and 60% of your ES as armor for PDR. This adds up to the most tankiest build I’ve played so far. With about 12k ES I can even tank Abyss elites (in 4-mod maps with ~25% monster effectiveness).

All in all, I think this is one of the best packages to get in SSF, and if you want to give ssf a try, I’d encourage you to go with the Disciple.

Cheers and happy holidays!
