Surprisingly the Maine Celtics don’t have their own sub so posting this here. Looking on Google there are only a few token spots at the Expo Center and the Maine Celtics page recommends that everyone first tries to park at Congress Street Medical Garage at 887 Congress Street, a 4-minute walk to the center.
