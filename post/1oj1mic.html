Stop with the tank talk

I am 100% against the tank talk, especially after 4 games. This teams problems are not as significant as their record and the overreactions are bordering on insane.

The team needs rebounding so they can play at the pace they want to. Considering the fact they lost 3 centers and the All-world forward who would also be the best rebounder on this current team, that’s not surprising.

My point is that with Tatum on the floor this should be a 3-1 team right now and we’d all be saying how Brad is a genius. We wouldn’t be talking about how our centers look like wacky inflatable arm flaming tube men on the court.

We’re a Jayson Tatum and a serviceable center away from being a 50 win team so let’s cool it with the tank for picks thing. Not only should this organization be able to put together competitive teams without tanking, you risk losing buy in long term.

This season is a test for Joe to get the team to buy in, and for JB to prove he’s the 1a he thinks he is.
