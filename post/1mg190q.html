Casual Dad Gamer Here: Is PoE2’s Endgame Too Time-Intensive?

Got into ARPGs (D4, LE, PoE) just last year and honestly, I’m hooked—it’s easily my favorite genre now. Out of the three, PoE2 is my top pick, with D4 coming in second. The story and endgame in PoE2 are both solid, and as someone who likes organizing, those stash tabs are heaven sent (even if GGG charges a small fortune for them lol).

I really enjoy the endgame systems too, but as a dad with maybe 2 hours max to play most days, I worry about actually getting to do the big bosses. In D4, it felt a lot more accessible—I could hit endgame bosses pretty easily, plus there were world events allowing to engage is fun content without grinding. Kinda hoping PoE2 makes boss content a bit more approachable, hard to do but easier to reach.

Gotta admit, I’m only about 15-20 maps in so far and I started halfway through this league, so maybe my perspective will change—but that’s just how I’m feeling right now. Really enjoying this game though! 🙌🏼
