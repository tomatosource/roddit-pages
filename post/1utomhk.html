KY’s Arc totems - When did it click for you?

I have taken KY’s arc totems all the way up to level 96, cleared some insane expedition maps and just about every other content I could possibly think off.

Still have more pieces of gear I could get but im now able to run all the auras I want, double curse, 6 totems, the whole shibang basically.

The leveling guide was a bit rough compared to the oil barrage build I started the league with but once I ended up switching over to 4 totems and running Blasphemy with temp chains instead of a 5th totem suddenly everything fell into place.

Probably at the point now that this has to be one of the strongest builds I have ever played and thanks to that totem life no matter how juiced up the opponent is, running in circles while they kill it is always a viable solution.

So for people who also ran this build / are getting into it, when did it start to click for you?
