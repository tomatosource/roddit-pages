How much small ball do you expect to see this season?

The Celtics have had the ability to play up consistently over the years, either with Double Big lineups or with Tatum who is a big forward in size/strength while having wing skills and fluidity.

Positional size has been a real strength for Boston. There is the ability to continue that this season. I think Boucher is probably going to start at the 4 for defensive purposes. It obviously sure looks like Queta will start, but I think his minutes get stagged with Garza a lot.

This Celtics team is going to be one of a totally different identity than we’ve seen of recent iterations. The roster more or less ensures that. Joe Mazzulla will have to find different ways to win and the this ballclub’s best talent is at the guard/wing. Their best shooting forward is Hauser and best shooting big man is Garza. It won’t be easy to find lineup combos that are sufficiently balanced offensively and defensively but it’s not impossible. That said, Mazzulla will lean on what this team does best, and that could end up being small ball this year.

How much small ball do you all envision the Celtics playing this year?
