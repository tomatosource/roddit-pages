I am having an absolute blast (Bleed Bloodmage)

I don’t want to bury the lede here, I’m a dad gamer still in campaign, not farming maps. Don’t @ me with hate.

I’m playing SSF spear bloodmage witch. I am doing all the things GGG wants me to be doing. I am getting frenzy charges, bouncing around all over the place, bleeding stuff, exploding spears. Whoever designed the spear toolkit did an awesome job. It feels strong, but not broken or anything. It feels like I have to stay engaged to play the game, but I can explode some shit when I get going.

I played blood mage last season and tried to make dots work (they didnt work) and ended up spamming hexblast all season. I tried dots again this time with spear/bleed/fire synergies and it didn’t work either. I think chaos needs endgame scaling or something to work, or i’m just not smart enough to figure it out.

I got stuck at jamanra act 2, and decided to go full bleed spear build with just enough int to do use my chaos dots for clear purposes, but i eventually came around to building full str/dex spear for now, to get through the campaign.

Blood mage LOOKS like it has good potential to synergize with my build, but as most things in this game, I have no idea what i’m doing until I actually build it and try.

Just to make sure any potential reader knows what I’m talking about if they want to try:

rake+stomping ground = scales with str

explosive spear+martial tempo+expedite

disengage

cull the weak for frenzy charges

blood hunt for bosses

herald of blood for clear.

I’m missing a ton of support gems and my gear is actually decent for my level, but clear speed is excellent and bosses feel long, but to a point where they’re still fair, but challenging. You have to play smart to avoid burning through all your flask charges before jamanra dies.
