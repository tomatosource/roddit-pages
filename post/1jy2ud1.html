My experience as a new player to PoE

Just like movies in the same genre, there is no need to pick one only and I enjoy all three ARPG games and have a blast playing all of them.

D4: I have fun slashing through the campaign, and it is amazing visual and animation overall. No endgame but we already know it.

Last Epoch: Truly incredible gameplay and progression, I think I can find meaning upgrades in every room. Boss fight is fun and the mechanic is clear. The crafting system will be a reference to all APRG going forward. I play different characters with different skills and they all work. Only that the game feels a bit lack of “wow” in the world building vs the other two.

PoE2: As a new player to PoE, it takes me to a very impressive atmosphere especially in Act 2. The worlds are simply jaw dropping. The peak dopamine hit is when you beat a challenging boss, both in solo and group setting. However, I played the least time among the three, and here’s why,

  1. It feels like a dark soul game, but mechanics complexity is lacking. So it would be infinite dodge rolling around, and there are little rewards to focus on parry or stun vs. DS. Large map, high HP mobs just make the whole experience a bit tiring.

  2. Loots are just not there. The only way to upgrade is to trade.

  3. Skill diversity and balance. I always play the first character in APRG without guide. And in season 1, I can’t really progress after mid act 3 without reading a guide. There are very few skill combos that work. Experiments with different builds are prohibitedly expensive. And no Loots with unqiue skills to test out new skill. So you end up with a weak build, dodge rolling around, and spam a single skills for 1k times to kill a boss.

  4. Too much chore to reach endgame. I didn’t finish the normal campaign in s1, I revisited s2 and found it much improved and completed the normal campaign. But then it brought me to cruel and I just don’t feel any motivation to complete the same campaign again.

I think one huge differentiation is that the PoE community is great, and I have never been in a game that people are so willing to help when I am stuck. Devs are also actively listening to feedbacks so I am definitely staying!
