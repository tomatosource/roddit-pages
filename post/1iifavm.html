Getting off of Springer for a 2030 second was good value

The Celtics only had 4 second round picks and they were able to trade the least valuable one to get rid of Springer. It would have been a bummer if they had to move the 2025 Washington pick.

This move will save around $15 million. Normally I don’t care whatsoever about saving ownership money, but considering how much luxury tax they have agreed to pay for this amazing team I’m cool with it.

And before people get mad about losing Springer they have to realize that he is not good, was not going to help us this year, and will likely be out of the league in 2 years. Don’t stress yourselves out about losing our 13th man.
