Tatum’s rim defense

I’ve noticed that JT has made zero effort this year to contest defensively at the rim. In previous years, he has contested at times or at least slapped down at the ball to try and get a steal/block before the offensive player brings the ball up. This year, there’s nothing. Are the coaches telling him to play this way? The problem is, when JT is defending the opposing center, he is getting put in this situation more and more this year. It’s one of the reasons why Boston’s paint defense numbers are so bad.

I can think of only three reasons why he doesn’t challenge (because I know he’s able to do so).

  1. He doesn’t want to pick up a foul - this argument only works for the early part of the game. I’ve seen him not challenge in the 3rd/4th quarters when he has very few fouls on him.

  2. He doesn’t want to get injured - a high impact play at the rim is more likely to cause injury. This is possible. It would also explain why his drives per game are down. If this is the reason, I suspect that his rim contests and drives will pick up noticeably in the playoffs. Still, you can’t play scared.

  3. He doesn’t want to get dunked on/look bad - I REALLY hope that this isn’t the reason.
