What is the motive for the Celtics to trade Jaylen Brown?

Recently, there’s been rumors that the Celtics are heavily shopping Jaylen Brown, and he might be traded this offseason. We know that they attempt to trade him for Giannis before losing out to Miami, and although I didn’t agree with that, Giannis is Giannis.

However, even after the Giannis trade, it seems like Boston is overtly willing to move on from Jaylen, who’s coming off a 2nd Team All-NBA season in the midst of his prime.

Unless he’s asked for a trade that has not been publicly reported to my knowledge…why?

- He’s coming off his best season as a pro, putting up 29/5/7 and carrying the Celtics to the second seed without JT missing most of the year and the surrounding cast being role players.

- He has proven to be a great playoff performer, and has literally won a Finals MVP for the Celtics.

- He’s never played less than 60 games outside the two shortened seasons (where he played 57-58 games), and he’s never missed a playoff game, so health isn’t the clear issue.

- While the Celtics did have a disappointing finish, there are PLENTY of reasons outside of Jaylen that you could fall back to (JT missing game 7, Derrick White’s horrific shooting, the Celtics poor interior defense)

- He is in his prime (will be 30 when the season starts) and has been an all star for 5 of the last 6 seasons.

With all of that information presented…..why would Boston be so hard-pressed to give up an elite talent? Maybe someone else can explain it better than I can.
