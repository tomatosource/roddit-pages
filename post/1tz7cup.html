First Impressions from someone playing for the 1st time

So, I started playing the game when it became available during the free weekend.

I’m enjoying the hell out of it.
I just started Act 3 and already have more than 20 hours played 😂
I know, that’s a lot, but as a 1st time player who has to discover everything about the game, I’m trying understand the game according the campaign.

One complaint that I have is: the game is not intuitive for new players.
I always have to search for things on external websites or watch videos to clarify doubts (and most of theses videos are from people talking about much more advanced stuff 😅).

If the game were more intuitive, it would easily be a 10⁄10 for me hahaha.

Honestly, I have no idea what to expect from the endgame or what players usually do in this stage (I’m trying to learn things according my progress through the campaign).

But so far, aside from having to constantly research things outside the game, I’m having a great experience 🙂
