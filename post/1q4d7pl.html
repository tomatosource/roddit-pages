Vaal Temple Energy & T15 Map Party Play Experiment

Hi guys!

My friends and I have been trying to build our temples and have been testing out how energy accumulates in party play and here are our results:

For Map Owner:

- Fully modded T15s with 0-3 tablets will give at least 2 energy crystals, sometimes 3. Tablets do not appear to guarantee an extra energy. Corruption and +1 level does not seem to guarantee extra energy as well.

- T15 7 or 8 modded have similar results as 6 modded, granting just 2, sometimes 3 energy crystals

- Extra tests: T14s and T16s regardless of mods or tablets appear to only give 2, sometimes 3 energy as well but only on the T16

For Party Members:

- T15 6 modded with 0-3 tablets only grant ONE energy to party members, dead or alive, occasionally granting 2.

- T15 7-8 modded and T14s/T16s have similar results. Per our tests, there may be a slightly higher change for party members to get 2 energy slightly more often from T16s. Tablets, Corruption, and +1 level do not seem to guarantee extra energy.

- Note: per our testing, distance doesn’t matter when it comes to generating energy for party members. You just have to be in the map to get it. You can on top of, be the one to complete it, or very far away from the Vaal mechanic and still get at least 1 energy. Unsure where extra energy occasionally comes from.

TLDR: In parties, map owners get 2-3 energy in T14-16 fully modded maps, party members always just get 1 energy, sometimes 2. Number/type of tablets, corruption, and +1 do not appear to guarantee extra energy. Also increased light radius is an amazing stat to find the Vaal mechanic quickly.

Hope this was helpful! Please let me know y’all’s thoughts and findings as well! GL & have fun for the rest of the league y’all! :3
