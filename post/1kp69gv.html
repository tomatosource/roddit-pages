The Curious Case of Torrey Craig: The NBA’s Most Forgotten Role Player

When I watch him play, he reminds me of the Holy Roman Empire. For those of you that don’t know, the Holy Roman Empire was neither holy, nor Roman, nor an actual empire. Similarity, Torrey Craig is supposed to be a 3 and d guy yet he’s not a good 3 point shooter nor is he a good defender. Yet despite his limitations, he was an effective player for the Suns and Nuggets once upon a time. He tirelessly hustled, made smart passes, and hit timely shots.

Nowadays, he sits on the Celtics bench, with an epic 36/29/63 shooting split.
