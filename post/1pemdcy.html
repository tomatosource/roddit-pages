[Cross-Sport Analysis] Does anyone remember when Stan Ross bunted in his final AB instead of going for his 3,000th hit in the film Mr. 3000? That just happened to LeBron James, hear me out.

https://youtu.be/_JhJj4MSCVw?t=80

In the 2004 film Mr. 3000, Stan Ross (played by Bernie Mac RIP) had one more chance to record his 3,000th career hit in the final inning of a tied game. The team’s young superstar Rex Pennebaker was on second, and signaled to Stan that he was taking off for third.

Seeing that, Stan knew that he no longer needed a knock to drive in the winning run, so he squared around to bunt in a quasi-suicide squeeze situation.

Stan was thrown out at first, but Pennebaker ended up scoring. No 3,000th hit, and Stan paid the ultimate sacrifice to give his team the win.

https://streamable.com/ut3m08

An hour ago, LeBron James had a chance to take matters in his own hands to win the game with the games tied 120-120 against the Raptors with only 10 seconds left. At that point in the game, he had recorded 8 total points in the game. He was trying to preserve a 1,297 game streak in which he scored 10 or greater points.

https://v.redd.it/dew6j7vm2b5g1

However, apparently he had devised a smart play with Rui Hachimura, the team’s [non-Luka] phenom to secure the winning basket. LeBron followed through, sacrificing his streak to grant his team the win.

^(Huge Caveats) I don’t watch basketball much at all, and I don’t know [basket]ball like I know [base]ball. Stan Ross was notorious for being a selfish player, so that moment meant more for his character arc than tonight does for LeBron. If I’m not mistaken, LeBron has never really been a ball-hog.

Also, 3,000 hits as an accolade would mean nothing - comparatively - for LeBron’s career. He’s [one of] the greatest ever. Stan Ross would be a borderline HOF case.

And as I alluded to above, the parallels would have been a bit more uncanny if it was Luka who got the final shot off, but I blame his newborn child for that.
