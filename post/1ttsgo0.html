Awesome campaign! Wish I could play more!

Hey everyone! Just wanted to gush about loving at least the first 2 acts as it’s what I’ve been able to play so far thanks to this Free to Play weekend. I usually explore the full map to make sure I don’t miss any quests or mini-boss.

Coming from Diablo, I feel like the builds/characters play way better and the presentation feels more impressive. It also helps that some of the bosses feel slightly difficult.

The Martial Artist ascendancy also looks and feels so badass. I wish I could afford to get the Early Access key to finish it, but I’ll have to wait for full release.

The only thing I wish was that names didn’t have to be unique. I like to use specific names for each role and had to retry naming more than all my boss fights! Anyway, hope everyone’s having a blast!
