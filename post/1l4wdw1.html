Quick sell auction house idea

Heard something similar in a stream and it inspired me to rethink an idea a bit - I could imagine a world in which we have an NPC where we give him an item for high gold price (so we don’t do it with EVERY item and it inflates the market too much) and it goes for bidding for let’s say 30mins.

It’s a win for people who don’t want to bother with trading too much, and win for bidders who can grab something good at a discounted price. No messaging needed, you just give it to merchant, the timer starts and people can bid currency.

For me this would be perfect as even after 700+ hours I’m struggling to set good prices and sometimes I just don’t care and don’t want to waste time on it.

Fully optional so if you have something truly amazing, you still set your own price.

Thoughts?
