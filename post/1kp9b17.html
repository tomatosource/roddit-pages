Off-Meta Titan!

TLDR, Titan played Black Myth Wukong and got jealous of Wukong’s big staff.

Hey everyone, next paragraph will be the start of the explanation if you want to skip the fluff. I’ve been trying to homebrew something for a WHILE now, as following the meta isn’t really fun for me, unfortunately, everything I’ve tried sucks, until now. I was playing the titan class with a more “normal” build, but i got very bored as it didn’t feel fresh, there was no excitement or intrigue. I looked at my ascendancy tree and realised 2 things, Hulking form (small nodes in tree are 50% stronger) and all of the aftershock and ancestral boosting nodes are really strong, and aren’t tied to using more “theme fitting” weapons/ skills. I got my thinking cap on, and I got to work. QUICK WARNING : I don’t have any recording software on my pc, so its all words just now, but if anyone wants me to make a video, I’m more then happy too! I used to do video editing in college so it would be cool to dust off my skills.

-Vaulting Impact is a quarterstaff skill that counts as a slam( aftershocks and ancestral boosting), is a travel skill (momentum is a DISGUSTING support for more damage) and most importantly, feels very silly and fun to use on a titan. The whole idea of this build is to get a quarterstaff with as much raw phys dmg and melee levels as possible, get a TON of AOE, and as much attack speed as possible to make traversal quicker. With my current setup, I have over 100% chance for aftershocks, aka every attack I do hits twice, I do this by using AFTERSHOCK and REVERBERATE support gems, the latter is good because VI has a fixed 1s attack time, meaning even if you have 100000000% attack speed, you will always gain good use out of it. my tooltip right now says I’m at roughly 19.5k overall per use, meaning 39k per slam with aftershocks, AND THEN you multiply all of that by 40% because of momentum, this build explodes packs. Better yet, my current setup has 0 levels on my quarterstaff, and I can make upgrades elsewhere so that 19.5k can easily reach 25-6k. For bossing we simply use rolling slam, and we use a weapon set swap 2h mace alongside swapping to deepest tower, making sure to swap our 2 aftershock supports from VI to rolling slam. We need a from nothing diamond (unwavering stance) if we want to minmax our dps as there is a disgusting cluster of dmg there, 210% in total for 3 set points. for our other set points we path off to the right of our start area to the low life cluster for a ridiculous amount more damage. rolling slam with deepest and all of the low life nodes is ridiculous. we are just using 1 2H mace, as we cant take giants blood otherwise we cant wield quarterstaffs without serious dex investment, but we dont care anyway, this build can firm t4 pinnacles. Dont forget about herald of ash for some more clear.

-The only pain point of this build is stat requirements. They are quite honestly a pain in the ass, and they take up so many of your passive points, and you have to path awkwardly to get to big clusters… but what if I told you there was a helmet that god rid of all of those issues and allowed you to use int/ dex requirement gems and gear with next to 0 investment. Enter THE VERTEX. Stats are mid, has some ok Chaos res, but the HUGE upside is “Equipment and Skill Gems have 50% reduced Attribute Requirements”, and, you can corrupt it up, mine is currently at 59% reduction. with only a few travel nodes here and there, and the big cluster at the top of the tree for int ( we travel there for a specific reason, ill talk on that next paragraph) we can satisfy any quarterstaff, and any level gems. as of right now, this is the only mandatory unique, everything else is just rare gear.

-Defences now, ill try and keep it short. we stack as much strength and max life on gear as humanly possible, right now I’m sitting at 3.1k hp, I reckon I can get it to 3.4k with time and currency, I’m only level 92, so I can grab a health cluster in later levels if I decide I want to, we cap all res, and we can also cap chaos res too with a few sockets. we have a super high armour chest, gloves and boots, realistically we can sit around 35-45% armour with no nodes at all in the tree. in terms of gaining life back, we have cannibalism aura support, and due to our huge aoe clearing and nicely sized hp pool, we’re getting a constant and fast stream of heals, this support alone makes me feel very safe. Our last layer of defence can be credited to Ulfhednar , cheers mate. we take the two clusters in the tree that give reduced skill duration, 1 is very close to warrior start, and the other is ALLLLLLL the way at the top of the tree, so we need to path up to the 25 int node and its sitting cosy right next to it. where the tech lies is, titan gains hulking form, and paired with that, the -skill duration is amplified way more then it usually would be, and when we put the 30% less duration into Time of need ( a skill that heals and dispels on a cooldown) we can bring it down to 1 second intervals, a 1 second 500-600 heal and cleanse is insane. Lastly, we want a huge HP flask, and we take the support gems that boost our flasks (forget the names) to get as much hp as quick as possible. all of that combined together leads to a pretty tanky chap indeed. A last pseudo layer of defence is VI has really high stun build-up, so more often then not enemies you don’t kill in 1 hit wont even be able to hit you back before you slam again

-If you made it this far, cheers, If you have any questions at all fire away, I’m more then happy to reply, and also, again, if anyone at all wants a video, I’m more then happy to make one, I lowkey want to, It’s been years since I deleted all my software, It would be fun to take part in an old hobby again. Stay sane, exiles.
