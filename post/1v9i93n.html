Peyton Watson Seeking Over \(25M/year For His New Deal - Meanwhile The Nuggets Current Offer For Him is a 5 Year/\)70M contract

Tony Jones of The Athletic reports that the Nuggets offered restricted free agent Peyton Watson a five-year, \(70 million contract. Watson on the other hand is seeking over \)25 million per year on his new deal.

The Nuggets are looking to get back under the second apron, hence the figure being offered to Watson. Regardless of the contract’s length, the annual value is a far cry from the $25 million that Watson was reportedly seeking. And with teams that have reportedly expressed interest in signing Watson needing to execute a sign-and-trade to get something done, the Nuggets have leverage. Watson has the option to accept his qualifying offer, which would make him an unrestricted free agent next summer, but that path carries significant risk. If he were to either play poorly or suffer an injury, Watson could stand to lose even more money next summer

Watson finished the 2025-2026 season averaging 14.6 Points, 4.9 Rebounds and 2.1 Assists per game for the Nuggets.
