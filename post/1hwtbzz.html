NBA Starter Net Rating of Champions since 1997

Here is a list of the NBA’s champions since 1997 and where their starters net rating ranked relative to the league

  • 1997 Bulls: 2nd

  • 1998 Bulls: 4th

  • 1999 Spurs: 2nd

  • 2000 Lakers: 1st

  • 2001 Lakers: 2nd-tied

  • 2002 Lakers: 2nd-tied

  • 2003 Spurs: 3rd-tied

  • 2004 Pistons: 3rd

  • 2005 Spurs: 1st

  • 2006 Heat: 5th

  • 2007 Spurs: 1st

  • 2008 Celtics: 1st

  • 2009 Lakers: 2nd-tied

  • 2010 Lakers: 2nd-tied

  • 2011 Mavericks: 9th (1st defense)

  • 2012 Heat: 1st

  • 2013 Heat: 2nd

  • 2014 Spurs: 4th-tied

  • 2015 Warriors: 1st

  • 2016 Cavaliers: 5th

  • 2017 Warriors: 1st

  • 2018 Warriors: 4th

  • 2019 Raptors: 1st

  • 2020 Lakers: 5th

  • 2021 Bucks: 4th-tied

  • 2022 Warriors: 6th-tied (1st defense)

  • 2023 Nuggets: 1st

  • 2024 Celtics: 1st

As you can see, every NBA champion had a starting lineup in the top 5 of Net Rating as far back as this data goes except for the 2011 Mavs and 2022 Warriors whose starters had the best defensive rating. If we use this as the qualifier for the current year as a predictor of who will win the NBA championship, the current top 5 are

  1. Thunder

  2. Cavaliers

  3. Celtics

  4. Knicks

  5. Nuggets

And the Grizzlies are 7th with the best defense

These 6 teams are the top 3 seeds in the East and 3 of the top 4 seeds in the West.

Other notable teams within the top 10 include the Rockets (6th, 2nd in West), Mavericks (8th, 5th in West) and Magic (9th, 4th in East) which are also all of the league’s current 9 best records.

If these hold through the end of the year, the 3 contenders from the East are the Cavaliers, Celtics and Knicks with the Magic being a dark horse, and from the West the Thunder, Nuggets and Grizzlies with the Rockets and Mavericks being a dark horse.

Source: https://www.nba.com/stats/teams/advanced?Season=2024-25&StarterBench=Starters&dir=A&sort=NET_RATING
