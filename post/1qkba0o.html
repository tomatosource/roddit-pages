Ja Morant who suffered a UCL Sprain in his Left Elbow Wednesday Night vs Atlanta is out Tomorrow vs New Orleans

Link: https://www.commercialappeal.com/story/sports/nba/grizzlies/2026/01/22/ja-morant-injury-trade-update-grizzlies-pelicans-basketball/88308115007/

“Memphis Grizzlies guard Ja Morant is listed at out for an upcoming game against the New Orleans Pelicans on Jan. 23 at FedExForum (7 p.m. CT, FanDuel Sports Network) due to a left elbow UCL sprain.

Morant played in the last two games after missing the previous six due to a right calf contusion. He recorded at least 20 points and 10 assists in each game.

Morant appeared to hurt his left elbow when he hit it on the backboard after a chase down block against the Atlanta Hawks with 8:12 left in the third quarter. After that play, Morant was favoring his left arm and not moving it much on the next possession.

He didn’t seem too concerned about the injury after the game.

“It was just like a little sting, funny bone,” Morant said after the Grizzlies’ lost to the Hawks. “I was good though.“”
