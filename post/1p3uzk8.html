Jordi Fernandez exploited the Celtics bigs when he trapped the ball-handler on pick-and-rolls last night

The Nets were the first team I’ve seen all year completely trap the ball-handler on every Celtics PnR last night, and it was really effective for them. The idea is that you force Queta and Garza to make reads and handle the ball like Al used to, but those guys really struggled at it (Garza in particular was abysmal).

All year, teams have played drop coverage against us and let Jaylen/DWhite/PP/Simons just walk into wide-open pull ups from 3 or mid-range. The Nets are the first team I’ve seen that trapped every play, and if coaches are smart, more teams are going to do this. I mean, who would you rather beat you, Jaylen Brown…or Luka Garza?
