Here is your Eastern Conference Starters Pool for the 2025 NBA All-Star Game

Donovan Mitchell:

Donovan Mitchell is making his 6th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 13th overall pick in the 2017 NBA Draft out of Louisville, Mitchell is averaging 23.2 PPG, 4.5 RPG and 4.5 APG for the Cavs this season.

Jalen Brunson:

Jalen Brunson is making his 2nd NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 33rd overall pick in the 2018 NBA Draft out of Villanova, Brunson is averaging 26.0 PPG, 3.0 RPG and 7.3 APG for the Knicks this season.

Jayson Tatum:

Jayson Tatum is making his 6th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 3rd overall pick in the 2017 NBA Draft out of Duke, Tatum is an NBA Champion and has three All-NBA First Team Selections. This season, he is averaging 27.4 PPG, 9.2 RPG and 5.5 APG for the Celtics.

Giannis Antetokounmpo:

Giannis Antetokounmpo is making his 9th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 15th overall pick in the 2013 NBA Draft out of Greece,  Antetokounmpo is an NBA Champion and 2x MVP. This season, he is averaging 31.5 PPG, 12.0 RPG and 6.0 APG for the Bucks.

Karl-Anthony Towns:

Karl-Anthony Towns is making his 5th NBA All Star appearance. Drafted as the 1st overall pick in the 2015 NBA Draft out of Kentucky, Towns was named ROY for the 2015-16 season. This season, he is averaging 25.1 PPG, 13.9 RPG and 3.5 APG for the Knicks
