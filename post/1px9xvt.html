50⁄50 Players

I was thinking the other day about how many players in MLB history have hit 50 HRs in a season and also had 50 SBs in a season.

After some research I found that there are a grand total of 3 players in the entirety of MLB to have achieved this:

Barry Bonds: Stole 52 bags in 1990 and hit 73 home runs in 2001. Each one being the only time he achieved above 50 in his career.

Shohei Ohtani: Famously has the only 50⁄50 season on record in 2024. He repeated his 50+ homer performance in 2025 but is unlikely to have another season with 50 swipes as he hasn’t cracked the 30 mark in any other season of his career.

Brady Anderson: Known more as a speed/defense guy, in 1992 at age 28, Anderson learned how to hit and get on base, allowing for him to attempt to steal frequently, leading to a season in which he stole 53 bases. Then with certainly the most anomalous season of any mentioned in this post, he smacked 50 home runs in 1996 at age 32 without having hit more than 21 previously or 24 since.

This is a very exclusive club that seems to take a change in rules (Ohtani) or a change in play style (Anderson and Bonds) to achieve (I will leave you to your imagination as to why someone’s play style might change).

All this being said, who do you think the next member of the club will be? Will we see a 50⁄50 single season again? Other thoughts?
