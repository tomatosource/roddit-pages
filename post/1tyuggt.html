People need to stop talking about Wemby like he’s Shaq

It’s really annoying when fans expect Victor to play like a traditional big when he is incapable of doing so consistently.

He is skinny and has a really high center of gravity so bigs can move him pretty easily so instead of just standing in the lane doing nothing, he moves to the perimeter to create spacing for his teammates. Also because he’s so big, he needs space in order to move around and with the Knicks chipping him every time he rolls to the basket, it’s hard to even get him lobs or open touches in the post.

Very frustrating watch for sure when he’s being bullied like that but let’s credit the Knicks for executing a great game plan.
