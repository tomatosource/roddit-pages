Roles taken over by AI over the years

In 1996 the 76ers drafted Allen Iverson #1 overall who immediately became the starting point guard, a role he took for a few years before being moved to SG and back to PG and so forth.

He would replace Philly’s previous year’s point guard Vernon Maxwell who would go on to play for the Spurs.

In the ‘06-‘07 season Iverson was traded to Denver to pair up with Carmelo Anthony. Andre Miller was previously the starting PG and as part of this trade was sent to Philly.

Not satisfied with with one AI on their roster, the 76ers drafted Andre Iguodala in 2004 to pair up with Iverson in the backcourt.

This moved Iverson back into a PG role with Iguodala into SG, so we have AI shifting AI’s role.

In the ‘12-‘13 season the 76ers again would trade an AI to Denver, this time Iguodala would take over Arron Affalo’s role as SG..and finally AI would end up in GSW where he take over Harrison Barnes starting role before famously and selflessly took on a bench role.
