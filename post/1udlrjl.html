I love the patch but the bugs are ruining the fun almost all the time now

I just want to preface by saying that I love the new patch and the endgame overhaul. I have had a blast pathing through many of the maps and trying to get the chase endgame items. Initially, it was a lot of fun moving through all the new mechanics and learning how to progress and completing the challenges.

However, the more I progressed, the more one thing became clearer and clearer. The number of game breaking bugs this patch has is a little out of hand. I am not talking about small things such as your minion stops moving or the enemy glitches out.

Rather, it was mechanic ruining bugs. One such example was simulacrum where I had cleared the wave and it did not allow me to progress to the next wave when there were no monsters bricking my simulacrum and forcing me to forfeit the map. Another is the grand mirror bug which for some reason refuses to be acknowledged even though there have been a ton of outcry about it (it has happened to all of my 10 grand mirrors so far.) If the mirror is places at the corners or next to a boss corpse, you lose the mirror entirely and it is bricked with no way to activate it. Another bug is from expedition where I faced invisible and invincible monsters.

These bugs are not minor and actively ruin the experience as many of these are either through RNG which can be rough (grand mirror) or actively cost your divines (simulacrum). I understand with big patches, there may be bugs that slip through the testing. I am just a little disappointed in how this many mechanic breaking bugs were able to slip through testing. I do hope the 6 months to the 1.0 release will have many of these bugs fixed and there is more rigorous play testing to identify such bugs in the future.
