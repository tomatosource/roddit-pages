Towers need to not be random (RNG)

I don’t hate towers as a concept, specifically juicing multiple maps at a time, but I don’t like RNG elements on top of RNG elements in the endgame.

I think towers should either let you select nodes to boost or at least select a path of connected nodes to boost. Honestly I’m actually much more against at last restriction in the endgame I think you should be able to jump around to unconnected nodes within tower range. This would also fix the issue of unconnected citadels and alleviate the frustration of shitty terrain obstacles.
